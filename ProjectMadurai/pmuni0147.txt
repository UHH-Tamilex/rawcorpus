0. ciṟappuppāyiram

malartali ulakiṉ malku iruḷ akala
ilaku oḷi parappi yāviyum viḷakkum
paritiyiṉ orutāṉ āki mutal īṟu
oppu aḷavu āci muṉivuikantu uyarnta
aṟputa mūrttitaṉ alartaru taṉmiyiṉ
maṉa iruḷ iriya māṇporuḷ muḻuvatum
muṉivuaṟa aruḷiya mūaṟu moḻiyuḷum
kuṇakaṭal kumari kuṭakam vēṅkaṭam
eṉum nāṉku elliyiṉ irun tamiḻk kaṭaluḷ
arumporuḷ aintiyum yāvarum uṇarat
teākivaki viriyiṉ tarukeṉat tuṉṉār
ikal aṟa nūṟi irunilam muḻuvatum
taṉatu eṉak kōlit taṉmata vāraṇam
ticiteāṟum niṟuviya tiṟal uṟu teālcīrk
karuṅkaḻal veṇkuṭik kārnikar vaṇkit
tiruntiya ceṅkōl cīya kaṅkaṉ
aruṅkali viṉōtaṉ amarā paraṇaṉ
moḻintaṉaṉ āka muṉṉōr nūliṉ
vaḻiyē naṉṉūl peyariṉ vakuttaṉaṉ
poṉmatil caṉakic caṉmati muṉi aruḷ
paṉṉaruñ ciṟappiṉ pavaṇanti
eṉṉum nāmattu iruntavat tōṉē
------------------

potup pāyiram (1-55)

1.0 potup pāyiram (1-3)

1. mukavuri patikam aṇinturi nūṉmukam
puṟavuri tanturi puṉinturi pāyiram
2. pāyiram potuc ciṟappu eṉa iru pāṟṟē
3. nūlē nuvalvōṉ nuvalum tiṟaṉē
koḷvōṉ kōṭal kūṟṟām aintum
ellānūṟkum ivi potup pāyiram

1.1 nūliṉatu varalāṟu (4-25)

4. nūliṉ iyalpē nuvaliṉ ōr iru
pāyiram tōṟṟi mummiyiṉ oṉṟāy
nālporuḷ payattōṭu eḻumatam taḻuvi
ai iru kuṟṟamum akaṟṟi am māṭciyōṭu
eṇ nāṉku uttiyiṉ ōttup paṭalam
eṉṉum uṟuppiṉil cūttiram kāṇṭiki
virutti ākum vikaṟpa naṭi peṟumē
5. mutal vaḻi cārpu eṉa nūl mūṉṟu ākum
6. avaṟṟuḷ,
viṉiyiṉ nīṅki viḷaṅkiya aṟiviṉ
muṉivaṉ kaṇṭatu mutalnūl ākum
7. muṉṉōr nūliṉ muṭipu oruṅku ottup
piṉṉōṉ vēṇṭum vikaṟpam kūṟi
aḻiyā marapiṉatu vaḻinūl ākum
8. iruvar nūṟkum oruciṟi teāṭaṅkit
tiripu vēṟu uṭiyatu puṭinūl ākum
9. muṉṉōr moḻiporuḷē aṉṟi avarmoḻiyum
poṉṉēpōl pōṟṟuvam eṉpataṟkum-muṉṉōriṉ
vēṟunūl ceytum eṉum mēṟkōḷ il eṉpataṟkum
kūṟupaḻam cūttirattiṉ kōḷ
10. aṟam poruḷ iṉpam vīṭu aṭital nūl payaṉē
11. eḻu vaki matamē uṭaṉpaṭal maṟuttal
piṟartam matamēṟ koṇṭu kaḷivē
tāaṉ nāṭṭit taṉātu niṟuppē
iruvar māṟukōḷ orutali tuṇivē
piṟarnūl kuṟṟam kāṭṭal ēṉip
piṟiteāṭu paṭāaṉ taṉmatam koḷalē
12. kuṉṟak kūṟal mikipaṭak kūṟal
kūṟiyatu kūṟal māṟukoḷak kūṟal
vaḻūuccol puṇarttal mayaṅka vittal
veṟṟeṉat teāṭuttal maṟṟoṉṟu virittal
ceṉṟu tēyntu iṟutal niṉṟu payaṉ iṉmi
eṉṟivi īr aiṅ kuṟṟam nūṟkē
13. curuṅkac collal viḷaṅka vittal
naviṉṟōrkku iṉimi nalmoḻi puṇarttal
ōci uṭimi āḻamuṭittu ātal
muṟiyiṉ vippē ulakam maliyāmi
viḻumiyatu payattal viḷaṅku utāraṇattatu
ākutal nūliṟku aḻaku eṉum pattē
14. nutalip pukutal ōttumuṟi vippē
teākuttuc cuṭṭal vakuttuk kāṭṭal
muṭittuk kāṭṭal muṭiviṭam kūṟal
tāṉeṭuttu moḻital piṟaṉkōḷ kūṟal
colporuḷ virittal teāṭarccol puṇarttal
iraṭṭuṟa moḻital ētuviṉ muṭittal
oppiṉ muṭittal māṭṭeṟintu oḻukal
iṟantatu vilakkal etiratu pōṟṟal
muṉmoḻintu kōṭal piṉṉatu niṟuttal
vikaṟpattiṉ muṭittal muṭintatu muṭittal
urittum eṉṟal urittām eṉṟal
orutali tuṇital eṭuttuk kāṭṭal
eṭutta moḻiyiṉ eyta vittal
iṉṉatu allatu itueṉa moḻital
eñciya colliṉ eytak kūṟal
piṟanūl muṭintatu tāṉuṭaṉ paṭutal
taṉkuṟi vaḻakkam mikeṭuttu urittal
colliṉ muṭiviṉ apporuḷ muṭittal
oṉṟiṉam muṭittal taṉṉiṉam muṭittal
uyttuṇara vippueṉa uttieṇ nāṉkē
15. nūlporuḷ vaḻakkoṭu vāyppak kāṭṭi
ēṟpuḻi aṟintu itaṟku ivvaki yāmeṉat
takum vaki celuttutal tantira utti
16. nēriṉa maṇiyi niralpaṭa vittāṅku
ōriṉap poruḷi oruvaḻi vippatu
ōttu eṉa moḻipa uyarmoḻip pulavar
17. oruneṟi iṉṟi viraviya poruḷāl
potumoḻi teāṭariṉ atupaṭalam ākum
18. cilvaki eḻuttil palvakip poruḷic
cevvaṉ āṭiyiṉ ceṟittu iṉitu viḷakkit
tiṭpam nuṭpam ciṟantaṉa cūttiram
19. āṟṟu oḻukku arimā nōkkam tavaḷip pāyttu
paruntiṉ vīḻvu aṉṉa cūttira nili
20. piṇṭam teāki vaki kuṟiyē ceyki
koṇṭu iyal puṟaṉaṭik kūṟṟaṉa cūttiram
21. pāṭam karuttē colvaki colporuḷ
teākutturi utāraṇam viṉāviṭi vicēṭam
virivu atikāram tuṇivu payaṉōṭu
āciriya vacaṉam eṉṟu īrēḻ uriyē
22. karuttup patapporuḷ kāṭṭu mūṉṟiṉum
avaṟṟoṭu viṉāviṭi ākka lāṉum
cūttirattuḷ poruḷ tōṟṟuva kāṇṭiki
23. cūttirattuḷ poruḷ aṉṟiyum āṇṭikku
iṉṟi yamiyā yāviyum viḷaṅkat
taṉ uriyāṉum piṟa nūlāṉum
aiyam akala aiṅ kāṇṭiki uṟuppoṭu
meyyiṉi eñcātu icippatu virutti
24. pañcitaṉ collāp paṉuval iḻiyākac
ceñcol pulavaṉē cēyiḻiyā-eñcāta
kiyē vāyākak katirē matiyāka
miyilā nūl muṭiyum āṟu
25. urattiṉ vaḷam perukki uḷḷiya tīmi
purattiṉ vaḷam murukkip pollā-marattiṉ
kaṉakkōṭṭam tīrkkumnūl aḵtēpōl māntar
maṉakkōṭṭam tīrkkumnūl māṇpu

1.2 āciriyaṉatu varalāṟu (26-35)

26. kulaṉ aruḷ teyvam koḷki mēṉmi
kali payil teḷivu kaṭṭuri vaṉmi
nilam mali niṟikōl malar nikar māṭciyum
ulakiyal aṟivōṭu uyarkuṇam iṉiyavum
amipavaṉ nūl uri āciri yaṉṉē
27. terivarum perumiyum tiṇmiyum poṟiyum
paruva muyaṟci aḷavil payattalum
maruviya nal nila māṇpu ākumē
28. aḷakkal ākā aḷavum poruḷum
tuḷakkal ākā niliyum tōṟṟamum
vaṟappiṉum vaḷamtarum vaṇmiyum malikkē
29. aiyam tīrap poruḷi uṇarttalum
meynnaṭu niliyum mikumniṟi kōṟkē
30. maṅkalam āki iṉṟi amiyātu
yāvarum makiḻntu mēṟkoḷa melkip
poḻutiṉ mukam malarvu uṭiyatu pūvē
31. moḻikuṇam iṉmiyum iḻikuṇa iyalpum
aḻukkāṟu avā vañcam accam āṭalum
kaḻalkuṭam maṭalpaṉi paruttik kuṇṭiki
muṭatteṅku oppeṉa muraṇkoḷ cintiyum
uṭiyōr ilar āciriyar ākutalē
32. peytamuṟi aṉṟip piṟaḻa uṭaṉ tarum
ceyti kaḻalpey kuṭattiṉ cīrē
33. tāṉē tarakkoḷiṉ aṉṟit taṉpāl
mēvik koḷakkoṭā iṭattatu maṭalpaṉi
34. aritiṉ peyakkoṇṭu apporuḷ tāṉpiṟarkku
eḷitu īvu illatu paruttik kuṇṭiki
35. palvaki utavi vaḻipaṭu paṇpiṉ
allōrkku aḷikkum matu muṭatteṅkē

1.3 pāṭañ collaliṉatu varalāṟu (36-37)

36. ītal iyalpē iyampum kāli
kālamum iṭaṉum vālitiṉ nōkkic
ciṟantuḻi iruntutaṉ teyvam vāḻtti
urikkappaṭum poruḷ uḷḷattu amittu
viriyāṉ vekuḷāṉ virumpi mukam malarntu
koḷvōṉ koḷvaki aṟintuavaṉ uḷamkoḷak
kōṭṭam il maṉattiṉ nūl koṭuttal eṉpa
37. taṉmakaṉ ācāṉ makaṉē maṉmakaṉ
poruḷnaṉi koṭuppōṉ vaḻipaṭu vōṉē
urikō ḷāḷaṟku urippatu nūlē

1.4 māṇākkaṉatu varalāṟu (38-39)

38. aṉṉam āvē maṇṇoṭu kiḷiyē
illik kuṭam āṭu erumi neyyari
aṉṉar tali iṭi kaṭi māṇākkar
39. kaḷimaṭi māṉi kāmi kaḷvaṉ
piṇiyaṉ ēḻi piṇakkaṉ ciṉattaṉ
tuyilvōṉ mantaṉ teāṉṉūṟku añcit
taṭumāṟu uḷattaṉ taṟukaṇaṉ pāvi
paṭiṟaṉ iṉṉōrkkup pakarār nūlē

1.5 pāṭaṅ kēṭṭaliṉ varalāṟu (40-46)

40. kōṭal marapē kūṟum kāli
poḻuteāṭu ceṉṟu vaḻipaṭal muṉiyāṉ
kuṇatteāṭu paḻaki avaṉkuṟippiṉ cārntu
iru eṉa iruntu col eṉac collip
parukuvaṉ aṉṉa ārvattaṉ ākic
cittirap pāviyiṉ attakavu aṭaṅkic
cevi vāy āka neñcu kaḷaṉ ākak
kēṭṭavi kēṭṭu avi viṭātu uḷattu amittup
pō eṉap pōtal eṉmaṉār pulavar
41. nūlpayil iyalpē nuvaliṉ vaḻakku aṟital
pāṭam pōṟṟal kēṭṭavi niṉittal
ācāṉ cārntu avi amivarak kēṭṭal
am māṇpu uṭiyōr tammoṭu payiṟal
viṉātal viṉāyavi viṭuttal eṉṟu ivi
kaṭaṉāk koḷiṉē maṭam naṉi ikakkum
42. orukuṟi kēṭpōṉ irukāl kēṭpiṉ
peruka nūlil piḻipāṭu ilaṉē
43. mukkāl kēṭpiṉ muṟiyaṟintu urikkum
44. ācāṉ urittatu amivarak koḷiṉum
kālkūṟu allatu paṟṟalaṉ ākum
45. avviṉi yāḷaroṭu payilvaki orukāl
cevvitiṉ urippa avviru kālum
mi aṟu pulami māṇpu uṭittu ākum
46. aḻaliṉ nīṅkāṉ aṇukāṉ añci
niḻaliṉ nīṅkāṉ niṟinta neñcamōṭu
ettiṟattu ācāṉ uvakkum attiṟam
aṟattiṉ tiriyāp paṭarcci vaḻipāṭē

1.6 ciṟappuppāyira ilakkaṇam (47-55)

47. ākkiyōṉ peyarē vaḻiyē elli
nūlpeyar yāppē nutaliya poruḷē
kēṭpōr payaṉōṭu āy eṇ poruḷum
vāyppak kāṭṭal pāyirattu iyalpē
48. kālam kaḷaṉē kāraṇam eṉṟuim
mūvaki ēṟṟi moḻinarum uḷarē
49. mutalnūl karuttaṉ aḷavu mikuti
poruḷ ceyvittōṉ taṉmi mutal nimittiṉum
iṭukuṟi yāṉum nūṟku eytum peyarē
50. teākuttal virittal teākiviri moḻipeyarppu
eṉattakum nūlyāppu īr iraṇṭu eṉpa
51. taṉ āciriyaṉ taṉṉoṭu kaṟṟōṉ
taṉ māṇākkaṉ takum urikāraṉ eṉṟu
iṉṉōr pāyiram iyamputal kaṭaṉē
52. tōṉṟā tōṟṟit tuṟipala muṭippiṉum
tāṉ taṉ pukaḻtal takuti aṉṟē
53. maṉṉuṭi maṉṟattu ōlit tūkkiṉum
taṉṉuṭi āṟṟal uṇarār iṭiyiṉum
maṉṉiya aviyiṭi velluṟu poḻutiṉum
taṉṉi maṟutali paḻitta kāliyum
taṉṉip pukaḻtalum takum pulavōṟkē
54. āyiram mukattāṉ akaṉṟatu āyiṉum
pāyiram illatu paṉuval aṉṟē
55. māṭakkuc cittiramum mānakarkkuk kōpuramum
āṭuamittōḷ nallārkku aṇiyumpōl-nāṭimuṉ
aituriyā niṉṟa aṇinturiyi ennūṟkum
peyturiyā vittār peritu
------------------

2. eḻuttatikāram 56 - 257

2.1 eḻuttu iyal 56 - 127

56. pūmali acōkiṉ puṉiniḻal amarnta
nāṉmukaṉ teāḻutu naṉku iyampuvaṉ eḻuttē
57. eṇ peyar muṟi piṟappu uruvam māttiri
mutal īṟu iṭinili pōli eṉṟā
patam puṇarpu eṉap paṉṉiru pāṟṟu atuvē
eṇ
58. moḻimutal kāraṇam ām aṇut tiraḷ oli
eḻuttu atu mutal cārpu eṉa iru vakittē
59. uyirum uṭampum ām muppatu mutalē
60. uyirmey āytam uyiraḷapu oṟṟaḷapu
aḵkiya i u ai oḷa maḵkāṉ
taṉinili pattum cārpeḻuttu ākum
61. uyirmey iraṭṭu nūṟṟeṭṭu uyar āytam
eṭṭu uyiraḷapu eḻu mūṉṟu oṟṟaḷapeṭi
āṟu ēḻ aḵkum i muppāṉ ēḻ
ukaram āṟu āṟu aikāṉ mūṉṟē
oḷakāṉ oṉṟē maḵkāṉ mūṉṟē
āytam iraṇṭoṭu cārpeḻuttu uṟu viri
oṉṟu oḻi munnūṟṟu eḻupāṉ eṉpa
peyar
62. iṭukuṟi kāraṇap peyarpotuc ciṟappiṉa
63. ammutal īrāṟu āvi kammutal
mey mū āṟu eṉa viḷampiṉar pulavar
64. avaṟṟuḷ,
a i u e o kuṟil aintē
65. ā ī ū ē ai ō oḷa neṭil
66. a i u mutal taṉi variṉ cuṭṭē
67. e yā mutalum ā ō īṟṟum
ē iru vaḻiyum viṉā ākummē
68. valliṉam ka ca ṭa ta pa ṟa eṉa āṟē
69. melliṉam ṅa ña ṇa na ma ṉa eṉa āṟē
70. iṭiyiṉam ya ra la va ḻa ḷa eṉa āṟē
71. ai oḷa i uc ceṟiya mutaleḻuttu
ivviraṇṭu ōriṉamāy varal muṟiyē
72. tāṉam muyaṟci aḷavu poruḷ vaṭivu
āṉa oṉṟu āti ōr puṭi oppu iṉamē
piṟappu
74. niṟi uyir muyaṟciyiṉ uḷvaḷi turappa
eḻum aṇuttiraḷ uram kaṇṭam ucci
mūkku uṟṟu itaḻ nāp pal aṇat teāḻiliṉ
vevvēṟu eḻuttu oliyāy varal piṟappē
75. avvaḻi,
āvi iṭimi iṭam miṭaṟu ākum
mēvum meṉmi mūkku uram peṟum vaṉmi
76. avaṟṟuḷ,
muyaṟciyuḷ a ā aṅkāppu uṭiya
77. i ī e ē ai aṅkāppōṭu
aṇ pal mutal nā viḷimpu uṟa varumē
78. u ū o ō oḷa itaḻ kuvivē
79. kaṅavum cañavum ṭaṇavum mutaliṭi
nuṉi nā aṇṇam uṟa muṟi varumē
80. aṇpal aṭinā muṭiyuṟat tanavarum
81. mīkīḻ itaḻuṟap pama piṟakkum
82. aṭinā aṭiyaṇam uṟa ya tōṉṟum
83. aṇṇam nuṉinā varuṭa ra ḻa varum
84. aṇpal mutalum aṇṇamum muṟiyiṉ
nā viḷimpu vīṅki oṟṟavum varuṭavum
lakāram ḷakāram āy iraṇṭum piṟakkum
85. mēlpal itaḻuṟa mēviṭum vavvē
86. aṇṇam nuṉinā naṉiyuṟiṉ ṟaṉa varum
87. āytakku iṭam tali aṅkā muyaṟci
cārpeḻuttu ēṉavum tammutal aṉiya
88. eṭuttal paṭuttal nalital uḻappiṉ
tiripum tattamiṉ ciṟituḷa ākum

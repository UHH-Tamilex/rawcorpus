tirukkuṟaḷ : kuṟaṭpākkaḷ tamiḻilum
kaviyōki makariṣi
cuttāṉanta pāratiyāriṉ āṅkila moḻiyākkamum

1. aṟattuppāl - Part I - VIRTUE

1.1 pāyiram - PREFACE

1.2 illaṟaviyal - DOMESTIC VIRTUE

1.3 tuṟavaṟaviyal - ASCETIC VIRTUE

-------

1. kaṭavuḷ vāḻttu - The Prise of God

1. akara mutala eḻuttellām āti
pakavaṉ mutaṟṟē ulaku
'A' leads letters; the Ancient Lord
Leads and lords the entire world.         1
2. kaṟṟataṉā lāya payaṉeṉkol vālaṟivaṉ
naṟṟāḷ toḻāar eṉiṉ
That lore is vin which does not fall
At His good feet who knoweth all.         2
3. malarmici ēkiṉāṉ māṇaṭi cērntār
nilamici nīṭuvāḻ vār
Long they live on earth who gin
The feet of God in florid brin.         3
4. vēṇṭutal vēṇṭāmi ilarṉaṭi cērntārkku
yāṇṭum iṭumpi ila
Who hold His feet who likes nor loathes
Are free from woes of human births.         4
5. iruḷcēr iruviṉiyum cērā iṟivaṉ
poruḷcēr pukaḻpurintār māṭṭu
God's prise who tell, are free from right
And wrong, the twins of dreaming night.         5
6. poṟivāyil aintavittāṉ poytīr oḻukka
neṟiniṉṟār nīṭuvāḻ vār
They prosper long who walk His way
Who has the senses signed away.         6
7. taṉakkuuvami illātāṉ tāḷcērntārkku kallāl
maṉakkavali māṟṟal aritu
His feet, whose likeness none can find,
Alone can ease the anxious mind.         7
8. aṟavāḻi antaṇaṉ tāḷcērntārk kallāl
piṟavāḻi nīntal aritu
Who swims the sea of vice is he
Who clasps the feet of Virtue's sea.         8
9. kōḷil poṟiyil kuṇamilavē eṇkuṇattāṉ
tāḷi vaṇaṅkāt tali.
Like senses stale that head is vin
Which bows not to Eight-Virtued Divine.         9
10. piṟavip peruṅkaṭal nīntuvar nīntār
iṟivaṉ aṭicērā tār
The sea of births they alone swim
Who clench His feet and cleave to Him.        10
---------

2. vāṉ ciṟappu. - The blessing of Rain

1. vāṉniṉṟu ulakam vaḻaṅki varutalāl
tāṉamiḻtam eṉṟuṇaraṟ pāṟṟu
The genial rin ambrosia call:
The world but lasts while rin shall fall.         11
2. tuppārkkut tuppāya tuppākkit tuppārkkut
tuppāya tūum maḻi
The rin begets the food we eat
And forms a food and drink concrete.         12
3. viṇiṉṟu poyppiṉ virinīr viyaṉulakattu
uḷniṉṟu uṭaṟṟum paci
Let clouds their visits stay, and dearth
Distresses all the sea-girt earth.         13
4. ēriṉ uḻāar uḻavar puyaleṉṉum
vāri vaḷaṅkuṉṟik kāl
Unless the fruitful shower descend,
The ploughman's sacred toil must end.         14
5. keṭuppatūuṅ keṭṭārkkuc cārvāymaṟ ṟāṅkē
eṭuppatūum ellām maḻi
Destruction it may sometimes pour,
But only rin can life restore.         15
6. vicumpiṉ tuḷivīḻiṉ allālmaṟ ṟāṅkē
pacumpul talikāṇpu aritu
No grassy blade its head will rear,
If from the cloud no drop appear.         16
7. neṭuṅkaṭalum taṉnīrmi kuṉṟum taṭinteḻili
tāṉnalkā tāki viṭiṉ
The ocean's wealth will waste away,
Except the cloud its stores repay.         17
8. ciṟappoṭu pūcaṉi cellātu vāṉam
vaṟakkumēl vāṉōrkkum īṇṭu
The earth, beneath a barren sky,
Would offerings for the gods deny.         18
9. tāṉam tavamiraṇṭum taṅkā viyaṉulakam
vāṉam vaḻaṅkā teṉiṉ.
Were heaven above to fail below
Nor alms nor penance earth would show.         19
10. nīriṉṟu amiyātu ulakeṉiṉ yāryārkkum
vāṉiṉṟu amiyātu oḻukku
Water is life that comes from rin
Sans rin our duties go in vin.         20
--------

3. nīttār perumi - The merit of Ascetics

1. oḻukkattu nīttār perumi viḻuppattu
vēṇṭum paṉuval tuṇivu
No merit can be held so high
As theirs who sense and self deny.         21
2. tuṟantār perumi tuṇikkūṟiṉ viyattu
iṟantāri eṇṇikkoṇ ṭaṟṟu
To con ascetic glory here
Is to count the dead upon the sphere.         22
3. irumi vakiterintu īṇṭuaṟam pūṇṭār
perumi piṟaṅkiṟṟu ulaku
No lustre can with theirs compare
Who know the right and virtue wear.         23
4. uraṉeṉṉum tōṭṭiyāṉ ōrintum kāppāṉ
varaṉeṉṉum vippiṟkōr vittu
With hook of firmness to restrin
The senses five, is heaven to gin.         24
5. aintavittāṉ āṟṟal akalvicumpu ḷārkōmāṉ
intiraṉē cāluṅ kari
Indra himself has cuse to say
How great the power ascetics' sway.         25
6. ceyaṟkariya ceyvār periyar ciṟiyar
ceyaṟkariya ceykalā tār
The small the paths of ease pursue
The great achieve things rare to do.         26
7. cuvioḷi ūṟuōci nāṟṟameṉṟu aintiṉ
vakiterivāṉ kaṭṭē ulaku
They gin the world, who grasp and tell
Of taste, sight, hearing, touch and smell.         27
8. niṟimoḻi māntar perumi nilattu
maṟimoḻi kāṭṭi viṭum
Full-worded men by what they say,
Their greatness to the world display.         28
9. kuṇameṉṉum kuṉṟēṟi niṉṟār vekuḷi
kaṇamēyuṅ kāttal aritu.
Their wrath, who've climb'd the mount of good,
Though transient, cannot be withstood.         29
10. antaṇar eṉpōr aṟavōrmaṟ ṟevvuyirkkum
centaṇmi pūṇṭoḻuka lāṉ
With gentle mercy towards all,
The sage fulfils the vitue's call.         30
------------

4. aṟaṉvaliyuṟuttal - The power of virtue

1. ciṟappuīṉum; celvamum īṉum; aṟattiṉuuṅku
ākkam evaṉō uyirkku
From virtue weal and wealth outflow;
What greater good can mankind know?         31
2. aṟattiṉ ūṅku ākkamum illi; ataṉi
maṟattaliṉ ūṅkuilli kēṭu
Virtue enhances joy and gin;
Forsaking it is fall and pin.         32
3. ollum vakiyāṉ aṟaviṉi ōvātē
cellumvā yellāñ ceyal
Perform good deeds as much you can
Always and everywhere, o man!         33
4. maṉattukkaṇ mācilaṉ ātal; aṉittuaṟaṉ;
ākula nīra piṟa
In spotless mind virtue is found
And not in show and swelling sound.         34
5. aḻukkāṟu avāvekuḷi iṉṉāccol nāṉkum
iḻukkā iyaṉṟatu aṟam
Four ills eschew and virtue reach,
Lust, anger, envy, evil-speech.         35
6. aṉṟaṟivām eṉṉātu aṟañceyka maṟṟatu
poṉṟuṅkāl poṉṟāt tuṇi
Do good enow; defer it not
A deathless aid in death if sought.         36
7. aṟattāṟu itueṉa vēṇṭā civiki
poṟuttāṉōṭu ūrntāṉ iṭi
Litter-bearer and rider say
Without a word, the fortune's way.         37
8. vīḻnāḷ paṭāami naṉṟāṟṟiṉ aḵtoruvaṉ
vāḻnāḷ vaḻiyaṭikkuṅ kal
Like stones that block rebirth and pin
Are doing good and good agin.         38
9. aṟattāṉ varuvatē iṉpam:maṟ ṟellām
puṟatta pukaḻum ila
Weal flows only from virtue done
The rest is rue and renown gone.         39
10. ceyaṟpāla tōrum aṟaṉē oruvaṟku
uyaṟpāla tōrum paḻi.
Worthy act is virtue done
Vice is what we ought to shun.         40
-------------

1.2 illaṟaviyal - DOMESTIC VIRTUE 5. ilvāḻkki - Married Life

1. ilvāḻvāṉ eṉpāṉ iyalpuṭiya mūvarkkum
nallāṟṟiṉ niṉṟa tuṇi
The ideal householder is he
Who aids the natural orders there.         41
2. tuṟantārkkum tuvvā tavarkkum iṟantārkkum
ilvāḻvāṉ eṉpāṉ tuṇi
His help the monk and retired share,
And celebrate students are his care.         42
3. teṉpulattār teyvam viruntokkal tāṉeṉṟāṅku
aimpulattāṟu ōmpal tali
By dutiful householder's aid
God, manes, kin, self and guests are served.         43
4. paḻiyañcip pāttūṇ uṭittāyiṉ vāḻkki
vaḻiyeñcal eññāṉṟum il
Sin he shuns and food he shares
His home is bright and brighter fares.         44
5. aṉpum aṟaṉum uṭittāyiṉ ilvāḻkki
paṇpum payaṉum atu
In grace and gin the home excels,
Where love with virtue sweetly dwells.         45
6. aṟattāṟṟiṉ ilvāḻkki yāṟṟiṉ puṟattāṟṟiṟ
pōoyp peṟuvatu evaṉ.
Who turns from righteous family
To be a monk, what profits he?         46
7. iyalpiṉāṉ ilvāḻkki vāḻpavaṉ eṉpāṉ
muyalvāruḷ ellām tali
Of all who strive for bliss, the great
Is he who leads the married state.         47
8. āṟṟiṉ oḻukki aṟaṉiḻukkā ilvāḻkki
nōṟpāriṉ nōṉmi uṭittu
Stright in virtue, right in living
Make men brighter than monks praying.         48
9. aṟaṉeṉap paṭṭatē ilvāḻkki aḵtum
piṟaṉpaḻippatu illāyiṉ naṉṟu
Home-life and virtue, are the same;
Which spotless monkhood too can clim.         49
10. viyattuḷ vāḻvāṅku vāḻpavaṉ vāṉuṟiyum
teyvattuḷ vikkap paṭum
He is a man of divine worth
Who lives in ideal home on earth.         50
---------

6. vāḻkkit tuṇinalam - The worth of a wife

1. maṉittakka māṇpuṭiyaḷ ākittaṟ koṇṭāṉ
vaḷattakkāḷ vāḻkkit tuṇi
A good housewife befits the house,
Spending with thrift the mate's resource.         51
2. maṉimāṭci illāḷkaṇ illāyiṉ vāḻkki
eṉimāṭcit tāyiṉum il
Bright is home when wife is chaste.
If not all greatness is but waste.         52
3. illateṉ illavaḷ māṇpāṉāl; uḷḷateṉ
illavaḷ māṇāk kaṭi
What is rare when wife is good.
What can be there when she is bad?         53
4. peṇṇiṉ peruntakka yāvuḷa kaṟpeṉṉum
tiṇmiyuṇ ṭākap peṟiṉ
What greater fortune is for men
Than a constant chaste woman?         54
5. teyvan toḻāaḷ koḻunaṉ toḻuteḻuvāḷ
peyyeṉap peyyum maḻi
Her spouse before God who adores,
Is like rin that at request pours.         55
6. taṟkāttut taṟkoṇṭāṟ pēṇit tacicāṉṟa
coṟkāttuc cōrvilāḷ peṇ
The good wife guards herself from blame,
She tends her spouse and brings him fame.         56
7. ciṟikākkuṅ kāppuevaṉ ceyyum makaḷir
niṟikākkuṅ kāppē tali
Of what avil are watch and ward?
Their purity is women's guard.         57
8. peṟṟāṟ peṟiṉpeṟuvar peṇṭir peruñciṟappup
puttēḷir vāḻum ulaku
Women who win their husbands' heart
Shall flourish where the gods resort.         58
9. pukaḻpurinta illilōrkku illi ikaḻvavārmuṉ
ēṟupōl pīṭu naṭi
A cuckold has not the lion-like git
Before his detractors aright.         59
10. maṅkalam eṉpa maṉimāṭci maṟṟuataṉ
naṉkalam naṉmakkaṭ pēṟu
An honest wife is home's delight
And children good are jewels abright.         60
-----------

7. makkaṭpēṟu - The wealth of children

1. peṟumavaṟṟuḷ yāmaṟivatu illi aṟivaṟinta
makkaṭpēṟu alla piṟa
The world no higher bliss bestows
Than children virtuous and wise.         61
2. eḻupiṟappum tīyavi tīṇṭā paḻipiṟaṅkāp
paṇpuṭi makkaṭ peṟiṉ
No evil comes and no blemish;
Noble sons bring all we wish.         62
3. tamporuḷ eṉpatam makkaḷ avarporuḷ
tamtam viṉiyāṉ varum
Children are one's wealth indeed
Their wealth is measured by their deed.         63
4. amirtiṉum āṟṟa iṉitētam makkaḷ
ciṟuki aḷāviya kūḻ
The food is more than nectar sweet
In which one's children hands insert.         64
5. makkaḷmey tīṇṭal uṭaṟkiṉpam maṟṟuavar
coṟkēṭṭal iṉpam cevikku
Children's touch delights the body
Sweet to ears are their words lovely.         65
6. kuḻaliṉitu yāḻiṉitu eṉpatam makkaḷ
maḻaliccol kēḷā tavar
The flute and lute are sweet they say
Deaf to baby's babble's lay!         66
7. tanti makaṟkuāṟṟum naṉṟi aviyattu
munti yiruppac ceyal
A father's duty to his son is
To seat him in front of the wise.         67
8. tammiṉtam makkaḷ aṟivuṭami mānilattu
maṉṉuyirk kellām iṉitu
With joy the hearts of parents swell
To see their children themselves excel.         68
9. īṉṟa poḻutiṟ perituvakkum taṉmakaṉic
cāṉṟōṉ eṉakkēṭṭa tāy
The mother, hearing her son's merit
Delights more than when she begot.         69
10. makaṉtantikku āṟṟum utavi ivaṉtanti
eṉṉōṟṟāṉ koleṉuñ col
The son to sire this word is debt
"What penance such a son begot!"         70
-------------

8. aṉpuṭimi - Loving-kindness

1. aṉpiṟkum uṇṭō aṭikkuntāḻ ārvalar
puṉkaṇnīr pūcal tarum
What bolt can bar true love in fact
The trickling tears reveal the heart.         71
2. aṉpilār ellān tamakkuriyar: aṉpuṭiyār
eṉpum uriyar piṟarkku
To selves belong the loveless ones;
To oth'rs the loving e'en to bones.         72
3. aṉpōṭu iyinta vaḻakkeṉpa āruyirkku
eṉpōṭu iyinta toṭarpu
Soul is encased in frame of bone
To taste the life of love alone.         73
4. aṉpuīṉum ārvam uṭami: atuīṉum
naṇpeṉṉum nāṭāc ciṟappu
Love yields aspiration and thence
Friendship springs up in excellence.         74
5. aṉpuṟṟu amarnta vaḻakkeṉpa viyakattu
iṉpuṟṟār eytuñ ciṟappu
The crowning joy of home life flows
From peaceful psychic love always.         75
6. aṟattiṟkē aṉpucārpu yeṉpa aṟiyār
maṟattiṟkum aḵtē tuṇi
"Love is virtue's friend" say know-nots
It helps us aginst evil plots.         76
7. eṉpi lataṉi veyilpōlak kāyumē
aṉpilataṉi aṟam
Justice burns the loveless form
Like solar blaze the boneless worm.         77
8. aṉpakattillā uyirvāḻkki vaṉpaṟkaṇ
vaṟṟal marantaḷirt taṟṟu
Life bereft of love is gloom
Can sapless tree in desert bloom?         78
9. puṟattuṟup pellām evaṉceyyum yākki
akattuṟuppu aṉpi lavarkku
Love is the heart which limbs must move,
Or vin the outer parts will prove.         79
10. aṉpiṉ vaḻiyatu uyirnili aḵtilārkku
eṉputōl pōrtta uṭampu
The seat of life is love alone;
Or beings are but skin and bone!         80
------------

9. viruntōmpal - Hospitality

1. iruntōmpi ilvāḻva tellām viruntōmpi
vēḷāṇmi ceytaṟ poruṭṭu
Men set up home, toil and earn
To tend the guests and do good turn.         81
2. viruntu puṟattatāt tāṉuṇṭal cāvā
marunteṉiṉum vēṇṭaṟpāṟ ṟaṉṟu
To keep out guests cannot be good
Albeit you eat nectar-like food.         82
3. varuviruntu vikalum ōmpuvāṉ vāḻkki
paruvantu pāḻpaṭutal iṉṟu
Who tends his guests day in and out
His life in want never wears out.         83
4. akaṉamarntu ceyyāḷ uṟiyum mukaṉamarntu
nalviruntu ōmpuvāṉ il.
The goddess of wealth will gladly rest
Where smiles welcome the worthy guest.         84
5. vittum iṭalvēṇṭum kollō viruntōmpi
miccil micivāṉ pulam.
Should his field be sown who first
Feeds the guests and eats the rest?         85
6. celviruntu ōmpi varuviruntu pārttiruppāṉ
nalviruntu vāṉat tavarkku
Who tends a guest and looks for next
Is a welcome guest in heaven's feast.         86
7. iṉittuṇit teṉpatoṉ ṟilli: viruntiṉ
tuṇittuṇi vēḷvip payaṉ
Worth of the guest of quality
Is worth of hospitality.         87
8. parintōmpip paṟṟaṟṟēm eṉpar viruntōmpi
vēḷvi talippaṭā tār
Who loathe guest-service one day cry:
"We toil and store; but life is dry".         88
9. uṭamiyuḷ iṉmi viruntōmpal ōmpā
maṭami maṭavārkaṇ uṇṭu
The man of wealth is poor indeed
Whose folly fails the guest to feed.         89
10. mōppak kuḻiyum aṉiccam mukantirintu
nōkkak kuḻiyum viruntu
Anicham smelt withers: like that
A wry-faced look withers the guest.         90
-----------

10. iṉiyavi kūṟal - Sweet words

1. iṉcolāl īram aḷiip paṭiṟuilavāñ
cemporuḷ kaṇṭārvāyc col
The words of Seers are lovely sweet
Merciful and free from deceit.         91
2. akaṉamarntu ītaliṉ naṉṟē mukaṉamarntu
iṉcol ṉākap peṟiṉ
Sweet words from smiling lips dispense
More joys than heart's beneficence.         92
3. mukattāṉ amarntuiṉitu nōkki akattāṉām
iṉco liṉatē aṟam
Calm face, sweet look, kind words from heart
Such is the gracious virtue's part.         93
4. tuṉpuṟūum tuvvāmi illākum yārmāṭṭum
iṉpuṟūum iṉco lavarkku
Whose loving words delight each one
The woe of want from them is gone.         94
5. paṇivuṭiyaṉ iṉcolaṉ ātal oruvaṟku
aṇiyalla maṟṟup piṟa
To be humble and sweet words speak
No other jewel do wise men seek.         95
6. allavi tēya aṟamperukum nallavi
nāṭi iṉiya coliṉ
His sins vanish, his virtues grow
Whose fruitful words with sweetness flow.         96
7. nayaṉīṉṟu naṉṟi payakkum payaṉīṉṟu
paṇpiṉ talippiriyāc col
The fruitful courteous kindly words
Lead to goodness and graceful deeds.         97
8. ciṟumiyuḷ nīṅkiya iṉcol maṟumiyum
immiyum iṉpan tarum
Kind words free from meanness delight
This life on earth and life the next.         98
9. iṉ col iṉitīṉṟal kāṇpāṉ evaṉkolō
vaṉcol vaḻaṅku vatu
Who sees the sweets of sweetness here
To use harsh words how can he dare?         99
10. iṉiya uḷavāka iṉṉāta kūṟal
kaṉiyiruppak kāykavarn taṟṟu
Leaving ripe fruits the raw he eats
Who speaks harsh words when sweet word suits.         100

11. ceynnaṉṟi aṟital - Gratitude

1. ceyyāmaṟ ceyta utavikku viyakamum
vāṉakamum āṟṟa laritu
Unhelped in turn good help given
Exceeds in worth earth and heaven.         101
2. kālatti ṉāṟceyta naṉṟi ciṟiteṉiṉum
ñālattiṉ māṇap peritu.
A help rendered in hour of need
Though small is greater than the world.         102
3. payaṉtūkkār ceyta utavi nayaṉtūkkiṉ
naṉmi kaṭaliṟ peritu
Help rendered without weighing fruits
Outweighs the sea in grand effects.         103
4. tiṉittuṇi naṉṟi ceyiṉum paṉittuṇiyāk
koḷvar payaṉteri vār.
Help given though millet- small
Knowers count its good palm- tree tall.         104
5. utavi varittaṉṟu utavi: utavi
ceyappaṭṭār cālpiṉ varittu
A help is not the help's measure
It is giner's worth and pleasure.         105
6. maṟavaṟka mācaṟṟār kēṇmi: tuṟavaṟka
tuṉpattuḷ tuppāyār naṭpu
Forget not friendship of the pure
Forsake not timely helpers sure.         106
7. eḻumi eḻupiṟappum uḷḷuvar taṅkaṇ
viḻumam tuṭittavar naṭpu
Through sevenfold births, in memory fares
The willing friend who wiped one's tears.         107
8. naṉṟi maṟappatu naṉṟaṉṟu: naṉṟallatu
aṉṟē maṟappatu naṉṟu.
To forget good turns is not good
Good it is over wrong not to brood.         108
9. koṉṟaṉṉa iṉṉā ceyiṉum avarceyta
oṉṟunaṉṟu uḷḷak keṭum
Let deadly harms be forgotten
While remembering one good-turn.         109
10. ennaṉṟi koṉṟārkkum uyvuṇṭām: uyvilli
ceynnaṉṟi koṉṟa makaṟku
The virtue-killer may be saved
Not benefit-killer who is damned.         110

12. naṭuvunilimi - Equity

1. takuti eṉavoṉṟu naṉṟē pakutiyāl
pāṟpaṭṭu oḻukap peṟiṉ
Equity is supreme virtue
It is to give each man his due.         111
2. ceppam uṭiyavaṉ ākkañ citiviṉṟi
eccattiṟ kēmāppu uṭittu
Wealth of the man of equity
Grows and lasts to posterity.         112
3. naṉṟē tariṉum naṭuvikantām ākkatti
aṉṟē oḻiya viṭal
Though profitable, turn away
From unjust gins without delay.         113
4. takkār takavilar eṉpatu avaravar
eccattāṟ kāṇap paṭum
The worthy and the unworthy
Are seen in their posterity.         114
5. kēṭum perukkamum illalla neñcattuk
kōṭāmi cāṉṟōrk kaṇi
Loss and gin by cuse arise;
Equal mind adorns the wise.         115
6. keṭuvalyāṉ eṉpatu aṟikataṉ neñcam
naṭuorīi alla ceyiṉ.
Of perdition let him be sure
Who leaves justice to sinful lure.         116
7. keṭuvāka viyātu ulakam naṭuvāka
naṉṟikkaṇ taṅkiyāṉ tāḻvu
The just reduced to poverty
Is not held down by equity.         117
8. camaṉceytu cīrtūkkuṅ kōlpōl amintorupāl
kōṭāmi cāṉṟōrk kaṇi
Like balance holding equal scales
A well poised mind is jewel of the wise.         118
9. coṟkōṭṭam illatu ceppam orutaliyā
uṭkōṭṭam iṉmi peṟiṉ
Justice is upright, unbending
And free from crooked word-twisting.         119
10. vāṇikañ ceyvārkku vāṇikam pēṇip
piṟavum tamapōṟ ceyiṉ
A trader's trade prospers fairly
When his dealings are neighbourly.         120

13. aṭakkamuṭimi - Self-control

1. aṭakkam amararuḷ uykkum: aṭaṅkāmi
āriruḷ uyttu viṭum
Self-rule leads to realms of gods
Indulgence leads to gloomy hades.         121
2. kākka poruḷā aṭakkatti ākkam
ataṉiṉūuṅ killi uyirkku
No gins with self-control measure
Guard with care this great treasure.         122
3. ceṟivaṟintu cīrmi payakkum aṟivaṟintu
āṟṟiṉ aṭaṅkap peṟiṉ
Knowing wisdom who lives controlled
Name and fame seek him untold.         123
4. niliyil tiriyātu aṭaṅkiyāṉ tōṟṟam
maliyiṉum māṇap peritu
Firmly fixed in self serene
The sage looks grander than mountin.         124
5. ellārkkum naṉṟām paṇital avaruḷḷum
celvarkkē celvan takittu.
Humility is good for all
To the rich it adds a wealth special.         125
6. orumiyuḷ āmipōl aintaṭakkal āṟṟiṉ
eḻumiyum ēmāp puṭittu.
Who senses five like tortoise hold
Their joy prolongs to births sevenfold.         126
7. yākāvā rāyiṉum nākākka kāvākkāl
cōkāppar colliḻukkup paṭṭu
Rein the tongue if nothing else
Or slips of tongue bring all the woes.         127
8. oṉṟāṉum tīccol poruṭpayaṉ uṇṭāyiṉ
naṉṟākā tāki viṭum
Even a single evil word
Will turn all good results to bad.         128
9. tīyiṉāṟ cuṭṭapuṇ uḷḷāṟum āṟātē
nāviṉāṟ cuṭṭa vaṭu
The fire-burnt wounds do find a cure
Tongue-burnt wound rests a running sore.         129
10. kataṅkāttu kaṟṟaṭaṅkal āṟṟuvāṉ cevvi
aṟampārkkum āṟṟiṉ nuḻintu
Virtue seeks and peeps to see
Self-controlled savant anger free.         130

14. oḻukkamuṭimi - Good decorum

1. oḻukkam viḻuppam taralāṉ oḻukkam
uyiriṉum ōmpap paṭum
Decorum does one dignity
More than life guard its purity.         131
2. parintōmpik kākka oḻukkam terintōmpit
tēriṉum aḵtē tuṇi
Virtues of conduct all excel;
The soul aid should be guarded well.         132
3. oḻukkam uṭami kuṭimi iḻukkam
iḻinta piṟappāy viṭum
Good conduct shows good family
Low manners mark anomaly.         133
4. maṟappiṉum ottuk koḷalākum pārppāṉ
piṟappoḻukkaṅ kuṉṟak keṭum
Readers recall forgotten lore,

5. aḻukkā ṟuṭiyāṉkaṇ ākkampōṉṟu illi
oḻukka milāṉkaṇ uyarvu
The envious prosper but ill
The ill-behaved sinks lower still.         135
6. oḻukkattiṉ olkār uravōr iḻukkattiṉ
ētam paṭupāk kaṟintu

7. oḻukkattiṉ eytuvar mēṉmi iḻukkattiṉ
eytuvar eytāp paḻi
Conduct good ennobles man,

8. naṉṟikku vittākum nalloḻukkam tīyoḻukkam
eṉṟum iṭumpi tarum
Good conduct sows seeds of blessings
Bad conduct endless evil brings.         138
9. oḻukka muṭiyavarkku ollāvē tīya
vaḻukkiyum vāyāṟ colal
Foul words will never fall from lips
Of righteous men even by slips.         139
10. ulakattōṭu oṭṭa oḻukal palakaṟṟum
kallār aṟivilā tār
Though read much they are ignorant
Whose life is not world-accordant.         140

15. piṟaṉil viḻiyāmi - Aginst coveting another's wife

1. piṟaṉporuḷāḷ peṭṭoḻukum pētimi ñālattu
aṟamporuḷ kaṇṭārkaṇ il
Who know the wealth and virtue's way
After other's wife do not stray.         141
2. aṟaṉkaṭi niṉṟāruḷ ellām piṟaṉkaṭi
niṉṟāriṉ pētiyār il
He is the worst law breaking boor
Who hunts around his neighbour's door.         142
3. viḷintāriṉ vēṟallar maṉṟa teḷintāril
tīmi purintuoḻuku vār
The vile are dead who evil aim
And put faithful friends' wives to shame.         143
4. eṉittuṇiyar āyiṉum eṉṉām tiṉittuṇiyum
tērāṉ piṟaṉil pukal

5. eḷitueṉa illiṟappāṉ eytumeñ ñāṉṟum
viḷiyātu niṟkum paḻi
Who trifles with another's wife

6. pakipāvam accam paḻiyeṉa nāṉkum
ikavāvām illiṟappāṉ kaṇ
Hatred, sin, fear, and shame-these four
Stin adulterers ever more.         146
7. aṟaṉiyalāṉ ilvāḻvāṉ eṉpāṉ piṟaṉiyalāṉ
peṇmi nayavā tavaṉ
He is the righteous householder
His neighbour's wife who covets never.         147
8. piṟaṉmaṉi nōkkāta pērāṇmi cāṉṟōrkku
aṟaṉoṉṟō āṉṟa oḻukku
They lead a high-souled manly life
The pure who eye not another's wife.         148
9. nalakkuriyār yāreṉiṉ nāmanīr vippiṉ
piṟaṟkuuriyāḷ tōḷtōyā tār
Good in storm bound earth is with those
Who clasp not arms of another's spouse.         149
10. aṟaṉvariyāṉ alla ceyiṉum piṟaṉvariyāḷ
peṇmi nayavāmi naṉṟu.
Sinners breaking virtue's behest
Lust not for another's wife at least.         150

16. poṟiyuṭimi - Forgiveness

1. akaḻvārit tāṅkum nilampōlat tammi
ikaḻvārp poṟuttal tali
As earth bears up with diggers too
To bear revilers is prime virtue.         151
2. poṟuttal iṟappiṉi eṉṟum ataṉi
maṟattal ataṉiṉum naṉṟu
Forgive insults is a good habit
Better it is to forget it.         152
3. iṉmiyuḷ iṉmi viruntorāl vaṉmiyuḷ
vaṉmi maṭavārp poṟi
Neglect the guest is dearth of dearth
To bear with fools is strength of strength.         153
4. niṟiyuṭami nīṅkāmi vēṇṭiṉ poṟiyuṭimi
pōṟṟi oḻukap paṭum
Practice of patient quality
Retins intact integrity.         154
5. oṟuttāri oṉṟāka viyārē vippar
poṟuttārip poṉpōṟ potintu
Vengeance is not in esteem held
Patience is prised as hidden gold.         155
6. oṟuttārkku orunāḷi iṉpam poṟuttārkkup
poṉṟun tuṇiyum pukaḻ
Revenge accords but one day's joy
Patience carries its prise for aye.         156
7. tiṟaṉalla taṟpiṟar ceyyiṉum nōnontu
aṟaṉalla ceyyāmi naṉṟu
Though others cuse you wanton pin
Grieve not; from unjust harm refrin.         157
8. mikutiyāṉ mikkavi ceytārit tāntam
takutiyāṉ veṉṟu viṭal
By noble forbearance vanquish
The proud that have cused you anguish.         158
9. tuṟantāriṉ tūymi uṭiyar iṟantārvāy
iṉṉāccol nōṟkiṟ pavar
More than ascetics they are pure
Who bitter tongues meekly endure.         159
10. uṇṇātu nōṟpār periyar piṟarcollum
iṉṉāccol nōṟpāriṉ piṉ
Who fast are great to do penance
Greater are they who bear offence.         160

17. aḻukkāṟāmi - Avoid envy

1. oḻukkāṟāk koḷka oruvaṉtaṉ neñcattu
aḻukkāṟu illāta iyalpu
Deem your heart as virtuous
When your nature is not jealous.         161
2. viḻuppēṟṟiṉ aḵtoppatu illiyār māṭṭum
aḻukkāṟṟiṉ aṉmi peṟiṉ
No excellence excels the one
That by nature envies none.         162
3. aṟaṉākkam vēṇṭātāṉ eṉpāṉ piṟaṉākkam
pēṇātu aḻukkaṟup pāṉ
Who envies others' good fortune
Can't prosper in virtue of his own.         163
4. aḻukkāṟṟiṉ allavi ceyyār iḻukkāṟṟiṉ
ētam paṭupākku aṟintu
The wise through envy don't others wrong
Knowing that woes from evils throng.         164
5. aḻukkāṟa uṭiyārkku atucālum oṉṉār
vaḻukkiyum kēṭīṉ patu
Man shall be wrecked by envy's whim
Even if enemies spare him.         165
6. koṭuppatu aḻukkaṟuppāṉ cuṟṟam uṭuppatūum
uṇpatūum iṉṟik keṭum.
Who envies gifts shall suffer ruin
Without food and clothes with his kin.         166
7. avvittu aḻukkāṟu uṭiyāṉic ceyyavaḷ
tavviyik kāṭṭi viṭum.
Fortune deserts the envious
Leaving misfortune omnious.         167
8. aḻukkāṟu eṉoru pāvi tirucceṟṟut
tīyuḻi uyttu viṭum
Caitiff envy despoils wealth
And drags one into evil path.         168
9. avviya neñcattāṉ ākkamuñ cevviyāṉ
kēṭum niṉikkap paṭum
Why is envy rich, goodmen poor
People with surprise think over.         169
10. aḻukkaṟṟu akaṉṟārum illiaḵtu illār
perukkattil tīrntārum il
The envious prosper never
The envyless prosper ever.         170

18. veḵkāmi - Aginst covetousness

1. naṭuviṉṟi naṉporuḷ veḵkiṉ kuṭipoṉṟik
kuṟṟamum āṅkē tarum
Who covets others' honest wealth
That greed ruins his house forthwith.         171
2. paṭupayaṉ veḵkip paḻippaṭuva ceyyār
naṭuvaṉmi nāṇuvar pavar
Who shrink with shame from sin, refrin
From coveting which brings ruin.         172
3. ciṟṟiṉpam veḵki aṟaṉalla ceyyārē
maṟṟiṉpam vēṇṭu pavar
For spiritual bliss who long
For fleeting joy commit no wrong.         173
4. ilameṉṟu veḵkutal ceyyār pulamveṉṟa
puṉmiyil kāṭci yavar
The truth-knowers of sense-control
Though in want covet not at all.         174
5. aḵki akaṉṟa aṟiveṉṉām yārmāṭṭum
veḵki veṟiya ceyiṉ
What is one's subtle wisdom worth
If it deals ill with all on earth.         175
6. aruḷveḵki āṟṟiṉkaṇ niṉṟāṉ poruḷveḵkip
pollāta cūḻak keṭum
Who seeks for grace on righteous path
Suffers by evil covetous wealth.         176
7. vēṇṭaṟka veḵkiyām ākkam viḷivayiṉ
māṇṭaṟ karitām payaṉ
Shun the fruit of covetousness
All its yield is inglorious.         177
8. aḵkāmi celvattiṟku yāteṉiṉ veḵkāmi
vēṇṭum piṟaṉkip poruḷ.
Aginst covetousness - The mark of lasting wealth is shown
By not coveting others' own.         178
9. aṟaṉaṟintu veḵkā aṟivuṭiyārc cērum
tiṟaṉaṟin tāṅkē tiru.
Fortune seeks the just and wise
Who are free from coveting vice.         179
10. iṟalīṉum eṇṇātu veḵkiṉ viṟalīṉum
vēṇṭāmi eṉṉuñ cerukku
Desireless, greatness conquers all;
Coveting misers ruined fall.         180

19. puṟaṅkūṟāmi - Aginst slander

1. aṟaṅkūṟāṉ alla ceyiṉum oruvaṉ
puṟaṅkūṟāṉ eṉṟal iṉitu
Though a man from virtue strays,
To keep from slander brings him prise.         181
2. aṟaṉaḻīi allavi ceytaliṉ tītē
puṟaṉaḻīip poyttu naki

3. puṟaṅkūṟip poyttuyir vāḻtaliṉ cātal
aṟaṅkūṟum ākkam tarum
Virtue thinks it better to die,
Than live to backbite and to lie.         183
4. kaṇniṉṟu kaṇṇaṟac colliṉum collaṟka
muṉiṉṟu piṉnōkkāc col

6. piṟaṉpaḻi kūṟuvāṉ taṉpaḻi yuḷḷum
tiṟaṉterintu kūṟap paṭum
His failings will be found and shown,
Who makes another's failings known.         186
7. pakaccollik kēḷirp pirippar nakaccolli
naṭpāṭal tēṟṟā tavar
By pleasing words who make not friends
Sever their hearts by hostile trends.         187
8. tuṉṉiyār kuṟṟamum tūṟṟum marapiṉār
eṉṉikol ētilār māṭṭu
What will they not to strangers do
Who bring their friends' defects to view?         188
9. aṟaṉnōkki āṟṟuṅkol viyam puṟaṉnōkkip
puṉcol urippāṉ poṟi.
The world in mercy bears his load
Who rants behind words untoward         189
10. ētilār kuṟṟampōl taṅkuṟṟaṅ kāṇkiṟpiṉ
tītuṇṭō maṉṉum uyirkku
No harm would fall to any man
If each his own defect could scan.         190

20. payaṉila collāmi - Aginst vin speaking

1. pallār muṉiyap payaṉila colluvāṉ

Is held in contempt as banal.         191
2. payaṉila pallārmuṉ collal nayaṉila

3. nayaṉilaṉ eṉpatu collum payaṉila

4. nayaṉcārā naṉmiyiṉ nīkkum payaṉcārāp

5. cīrmi ciṟappoṭu nīṅkum payaṉila
nīrmi yuṭiyār coliṉ

8. arumpayaṉ āyum aṟiviṉār collār
perumpayaṉ illāta col

9. poruḷtīrnta poccāntuñ collār maruḷtīrnta
mācaṟu kāṭci yavar

10. colluka colliṟ payaṉuṭiya collaṟka
colliṟ payaṉilāc col

21. tīviṉiyaccam - Fear of sin

1. tīviṉiyār añcār viḻumiyār añcuvar
tīviṉi eṉṉuñ cerukku
Sinners fear not the pride of sin.
The worthy dread the ill within.        201
2. tīyavi tīya payattalāl tīyavi
tīyiṉum añcap paṭum
Since evil begets evil dire
Fear ye evil more than fire.        202
3. aṟiviṉuḷ ellān taliyeṉpa tīya
ceṟuvārkkum ceyyā viṭal
The wisest of the wise are those
Who injure not even their foes.        203
4. maṟantum piṟaṉkēṭu cūḻaṟka cūḻiṉ
aṟañcūḻum cūḻntavaṉ kēṭu
His ruin virtue plots who plans
The ruin of another man's.        204
5. ilaṉeṉṟu tīyavi ceyyaṟka ceyyiṉ
ilaṉākum maṟṟum peyarttu
Who makes poverty plea for ill
Shall reduce himself poorer still.        205
6. tīppāla tāṉpiṟarkaṇ ceyyaṟka nōyppāla
taṉṉi aṭalvēṇṭā tāṉ
From wounding others let him refrin
Who would from harm himself remin.        206
7. eṉippaki yuṟṟārum uyvar viṉippaki
vīyātu piṉceṉṟu aṭum
Men may escape other foes and live
But sin its deadly blow will give.        207
8. tīyavi ceytār keṭutal niḻaltaṉṉi
vīyātu aṭiurin taṟṟu
Ruin follows who evil do
As shadow follows as they go.        208
9. taṉṉittāṉ kātala ṉāyiṉ eṉittoṉṟum
tuṉṉaṟka tīviṉip pāl
Let none who loves himself at all
Think of evil however small.        209
10. aruṅkēṭaṉ eṉpatu aṟika maruṅkōṭit
tīviṉi ceyyāṉ eṉiṉ
He is secure, know ye, from ills
Who slips not right path to do evils.        210

22. oppuravaṟital - Duty to society

1. kimmāṟu vēṇṭā kaṭappāṭu mārimāṭṭu
eṉāṟṟuṅ kollō ulaku
Duty demands nothing in turn;
How can the world recompense rin?        211
2. tāḷāṟṟit tanta poruḷellām takkārkku
vēḷāṇmi ceytaṟ poruṭṭu
All the wealth that toils give
Is meant to serve those who deserve.        212
3. puttē ḷulakattum īṇṭum peṟalaritē
oppuraviṉ nalla piṟa
In heav'n and earth 'tis hard to find
A greater good than being kind.        213
4. otta taṟivāṉ uyirvāḻvāṉ maṟṟiyāṉ
cettāruḷ vikkap paṭum
He lives who knows befitting act
Others are deemed as dead in fact.        214
5. ūruṇi nīrniṟin taṟṟē ulakavām
pēraṟi vāḷaṉ tiru
The wealth that wise and kind do make
Is like water that fills a lake.        215
6. payaṉmaram uḷḷūrp paḻuttaṟṟāl celvam
nayaṉuṭi yāṉkaṇ paṭiṉ
Who plenty gets and plenty gives
Is like town-tree teeming with fruits.        216
7. maruntākit tappā marattaṟṟāl celvam
peruntaki yāṉkaṇ paṭiṉ
The wealth of a wide-hearted soul
Is a herbal tree that healeth all.        217
8. iṭaṉil paruvattum oppuraviṟku olkār
kaṭaṉaṟi kāṭci yavar
Though seers may fall on evil days
Their sense of duty never strays.        218
9. nayaṉuṭiyāṉ nalkūrntā ṉātal ceyumnīra
ceyyātu amikalā vāṟu
The good man's poverty and grief
Is want of means to give relief.        219
10. oppuravi ṉālvarum kēṭeṉiṉ aḵtoruvaṉ
viṟṟukkōḷ takka tuṭittu
By good if ruin comes across
Sell yourself to save that loss.        220

23. īki - Charity

1. vaṟiyārkkoṉṟu īvatē īkimaṟ ṟellām
kuṟiyetirppi nīra tuṭittu
To give the poor is charity
The rest is loan and vanity.        221
2. nallāṟu eṉiṉum koḷaltītu mēlulakam
illeṉiṉum ītalē naṉṟu
To beg is bad e'en from the good
To give is good, were heaven forbid.        222
3. ilaṉeṉṉum evvam uriyāmi ītal
kulaṉuṭiyāṉ kaṇṇē uḷa
No pleading, "I am nothing worth,"
But giving marks a noble birth.        223
4. iṉṉātu irakkap paṭutal irantavar
iṉmukaṅ kāṇum aḷavu
The cry for alms is pinful sight
Until the giver sees him bright.        224
5. āṟṟuvār āṟṟal paciāṟṟal appaciyi
māṟṟuvār āṟṟaliṉ piṉ
Higher's power which hunger cures
Than that of penance which endures.        225
6. aṟṟār aḻipaci tīrttal aḵtoruvaṉ
peṟṟāṉ poruḷvip puḻi
Drive from the poor their gnawing pins
If room you seek to store your gins.        226
7. pāttūṇ marīi yavaṉip pacieṉṉum
tīppiṇi tīṇṭal aritu
Who shares his food with those who need
Hunger shall not harm his creed.        227
8. īttuvakkum iṉpam aṟiyārkol tāmuṭimi
vittiḻakkum vaṉka ṇavar
The joy of give and take they lose
Hard-hearted rich whose hoarding fails.        228
9. irattaliṉ iṉṉātu maṉṟa nirappiya
tāmē tamiyar uṇal
Worse than begging is that boarding
Alone what one's greed is hoarding.        229
10. cātaliṉ iṉṉāta tilli iṉitatūum
ītal iyiyāk kaṭi
Nothing is more pinful than death
Yet more is pin of giftless dearth.        230

24. pukaḻ - Renown

1. ītal icipaṭa vāḻtal atuvallatu
ūtiyam illi uyirkku
They gather fame who freely give
The greatest gin for all that live.        231
2. urippār urippavi ellām irappārkkoṉṟu
īvārmēl niṟkum pukaḻ
The glory of the alms-giver
Is prised aloud as popular.        232
3. oṉṟā ulakattu uyarnta pukaḻallāl
poṉṟātu niṟpatoṉṟu il
Nothing else lasts on earth for e'er
Saving high fame of the giver!        233
4. nilavari nīḷpukaḻ āṟṟiṉ pulavarip
pōṟṟātu puttēḷ ulaku
From hiling gods heavens will cease
To hil the men of lasting prise        234
5. nattampōl kēṭum uḷatākum cākkāṭum
vittakark kallāl aritu
Fame in fall and life in death
Are rare but for the soulful worth.        235
6. tōṉṟiṉ pukaḻōṭu tōṉṟuka aḵtilār
tōṉṟaliṉ tōṉṟāmi naṉṟu
Be born with fame if birth you want
If not of birth you must not vunt.        236
7. pukaḻpaṭa vāḻātār tannōvār tammi
ikaḻvāri nōvatu evaṉ.
Why grieve at those who blame the shame
Of those who cannot live in fame?        237
8. vaciyeṉpa viyattārk kellām iciyeṉṉum
eccam peṟā viṭiṉ
To men on earth it is a shame
Not to beget the child of fame.        238
9. vaciyilā vaṇpayaṉ kuṉṟum iciyilā
yākki poṟutta nilam
The land will shrink in yield if men
O'erburden it without renown.        239
10. vacioḻiya vāḻvārē vāḻvār iciyoḻiya
vāḻvārē vāḻā tavar
They live who live without blemish
The blameful ones do not flourish.        240

1.3 tuṟavaṟaviyal - ASCETIC VIRTUE 25. aruḷuṭimi - Compassion

1. aruṭcelvam celvattuḷ celvam poruṭcelvam
pūriyār kaṇṇum uḷa
The wealth of wealth is wealth of grace
Earthly wealth e'en the basest has.        241
2. nallāṟṟāl nāṭi aruḷāḷka pallāṟṟāl
tēriṉum aḵtē tuṇi.
Seek by sound ways good compassion;
All faiths mark that for-salvation.        242
3. aruḷcērnta neñciṉārk killi iruḷcērnta
iṉṉā ulakam pukal.
The hearts of mercy shall not go
Into dark worlds of gruesome woe.        243
4. maṉṉuyir ōmpi aruḷāḷvāṟku illeṉpa
taṉṉuyir añcum viṉi.
His soul is free from dread of sins
Whose mercy serveth all beings.        244
5. allal aruḷāḷvārkku illi vaḷivaḻaṅkum
mallalmā ñālam kari
The wide wind-fed world witness bears:
Men of mercy meet not sorrows.        245
6. poruḷ nīṅkip poccāntār eṉpar aruḷnīṅki
allavi ceytoḻuku vār
Who grace forsake and graceless act
The former loss and woes forget.        246
7. aruḷillārkku avvulakam illi poruḷillārkku
ivvulakam illāki yāṅku.
This world is not for weathless ones
That world is not for graceless swines.        247
8. poruḷaṟṟār pūppar orukāl aruḷaṟṟār
aṟṟārmaṟ ṟātal aritu.
The wealthless may prosper one day;
The graceless never bloom agay.        248
9. teruḷātāṉ meypporuḷ kaṇṭaṟṟāl tēriṉ
aruḷātāṉ ceyyum aṟam
Like Truth twisted by confused mind
Wisdom is vin in hearts unkind.        249
10. valiyārmuṉ taṉṉi niṉikkatāṉ taṉṉiṉ
meliyārmēl cellum iṭattu.
Think how you feel before the strong
When to the feeble you do wrong.        250

26. pulāl maṟuttal - Abstinence from flesh

1. taṉṉūṉ perukkaṟkut tāṉpiṟitu ūṉuṇpāṉ
eṅṅaṉam āḷum aruḷ
What graciousness can one command
who feeds his flesh by flesh gourmand.        251
2. poruḷāṭci pōṟṟātārkku illi aruḷāṭci
āṅkilli ūṉtiṉ pavarkku
The thriftless have no property
And flesh-eaters have no pity.        252
3. paṭikoṇṭār neñcampōl naṉṟūkkātu oṉṟaṉ
uṭalcuvi uṇṭār maṉam.
Who wields a steel is steel-hearted
Who tastes body is hard-hearted.        253
4. aruḷallatu yāteṉil kollāmi kōṟal
poruḷallatu avvūṉ tiṉal.
If merciless it is to kill,
To kill and eat is disgraceful.        254
5. uṇṇāmi uḷḷatu uyirnili ūṉuṇṇa
aṇṇāttal ceyyātu aḷaṟu
Off with flesh; a life you save
The eater hell's mouth shall not waive!        255
6. tiṉaṟporuṭṭāl kollātu ulakeṉiṉ yārum
vilipporuṭṭāl ūṉtaruvār il
None would kill and sell the flesh
For eating it if they don't wish.        256
7. uṇṇāmi vēṇṭum pulāal piṟitoṉṟaṉ
puṇṇatu uṇarvārp peṟiṉ
From eating flesh men must abstin
If they but feel the being's pin.        257
8. ceyiriṉ talippirinta kāṭciyār uṇṇār
uyiriṉ talippirinta ūṉ.
Whose mind from illusion is freed
Refuse on lifeless flesh to feed.        258
9. avicorin tāyiram vēṭṭaliṉ oṉṟaṉ
uyircekut tuṇṇāmi naṉṟu.
Not to-kill-and-eat, truly
Excels thousand pourings of ghee!        259
10. kollāṉ pulāli maṟuttāṉik kikūppi
ellā uyirum toḻum
All lives shall lift their palms to him
Who eats not flesh nor kills with whim.        260

27. tavam - Penance

1. uṟṟanōy nōṉṟal uyirkkuṟukaṇ ceyyāmi
aṟṟē tavattiṟ kuru.
Pains endure; pin not beings
This is the type of true penance.        261
2. tavamum tavamuṭiyārkku ākum avam ataṉi
aḵtilār mēṟkoḷ vatu.
Penance is fit for penitents
Not for him who in vin pretends.        262
3. tuṟantārkkut tuppuravu vēṇṭi maṟantārkol
maṟṟi yavarkaḷ tavama
Is it to true penitent's aid,
That others austere path avoid?        263
4. oṉṉārt teṟalum uvantāri ākkalum
eṇṇiṉ tavattāṉ varum
In penance lies the power to save
The friends and foil the foe and knave.        264
5. vēṇṭiya vēṇṭiyāṅ keytalāl ceytavam
īṇṭu muyalap paṭum
What they wish as they wish is won
Here hence by men penance is done.        265
6. tavañceyvār taṅkamarumañ ceyvārmaṟ ṟallār
avañceyvār āciyuṭ paṭṭu
Who do penance achieve their aim
Others desire-rid themselves harm.        266
7. cuṭaccuṭarum poṉpōl oḷiviṭum tuṉpañ
cuṭakcuṭa nōṟkiṟ pavarkku
Pure and bright gets the gold in fire;
and so the life by pin austere.        267
8. taṉṉuyir tāṉ aṟap peṟṟāṉi ēṉiya
maṉṉuyi rellām toḻum.
He worship wins from every soul
Who Master is by soul control.        268
9. kūṟṟam kutittalum kikūṭum nōṟṟaliṉ
āṟṟal talippaṭ ṭavarkku.
They can even defy death
Who get by penance godly strength.        269
10. ilarpala rākiya kāraṇam nōṟpār
cilarpalar nōlā tavar.
Many are poor and few are rich
For they care not for penance much.        270

28. kūṭāvoḻukkam - Imposture

1. vañca maṉattāṉ paṭiṟṟoḻukkam pūtaṅkaḷ
aintum akattē nakum.
Elements five of feigned life
Of a sly hypocrite within lugh.        271
2. vāṉuyar tōṟṟam evaṉceyyum taṉneñcam
tāṉaṟi kuṟṟap paṭiṉ
Of what avil are sky-high shows
When guild the conscience gnaws and knows.        272
3. valiyil nilimiyāṉ valluruvam peṟṟam
puliyiṉtōl pōrttumēyn taṟṟu.
Vaunting sinthood while weak within
Seems a grazer with tiger skin.        273
4. tavamaṟintu allavi ceytal putalmaṟintu
vēṭṭuvaṉ puḷcimiḻt taṟṟu.
Sinning in sintly show is like
Fowlers in ambush birds to strike.        274
5. paṟṟaṟṟēm eṉpār paṭiṟṟoḻukkam eṟṟeṟṟeṉṟu
ētam palavun tarum.
Who false within but freedom feign
Shall moan "What have we done" with pin.        275
6. neñcil tuṟavār tuṟantārpōl vañcittu
vāḻvāriṉ vaṉkaṇār il
Vilest is he who seems a sint
Cheating the world without restrint.        276
7. puṟaṅkuṉṟi kaṇṭaṉiya rēṉum akaṅkuṉṟi
mūkkiṟ kariyār uṭittu
Berry-red is his outward view,
Black like its nose his inward hue.        277
8. maṉattatu mācāka māṇṭārnī rāṭi
maṟintoḻukum māntar palar.
Filthy in mind some bathe in streams
Hiding sins in showy extremes.        278
9. kaṇikoṭitu yāḻkōṭu cevvituāṅ kaṉṉa
viṉipaṭu pālāl koḷal.
Know men by acts and not by forms
Strit arrow kills, bent lute but charms.        279
10. maḻittalum nīṭṭalum vēṇṭā ulakam
paḻittatu oḻittu viṭiṉ.
No balding nor tangling the hir!
Abstin from condemned acts with care.        280

29. kaḷḷāmi - The absence of frud

1. eḷḷāmi vēṇṭuvāṉ eṉpāṉ eṉittoṉṟum
kaḷḷāmi kākkataṉ neñcu.
Let him who would reproachless be
From all fruds guard his conscience free.        281
2. uḷḷattāl uḷḷalum tītē piṟaṉporuḷik
kaḷḷattāl kaḷvēm eṉal.
"We will by frud win other's wealth"
Even this thought is sin and stealth.        282
3. kaḷaviṉāl ākiya ākkam aḷaviṟantu
āvatu pōlak keṭum.
The gin by frud may overflow
But swift to ruin it shall go.        283
4. kaḷaviṉkaṇ kaṉṟiya kātal viḷiviṉkaṇ
vīyā viḻumam tarum.
The fruit that frud and greed obtin
Shall end in endless grief and pin.        284
5. aruḷkaruti aṉpuṭiya rātal poruḷkarutip
poccāppup pārppārkaṇ il.
Love and Grace are not their worth
Who watch to waylay dozer's wealth.        285
6. aḷaviṉkaṇ niṉṟoḻukal āṟṟār kaḷaviṉkaṇ
kaṉṟiya kāta lavar
They cannot walk in measured bounds
who crave and have covetous ends.        286
7. kaḷaveṉṉum kāraṟi vāṇmi aḷaveṉṉum
āṟṟal purintārkaṇ il.
Men of measured wisdom shun
Black art of frud and what it won.        287
8. aḷavaṟintār neñcat taṟampōl niṟkum
kaḷavaṟintār neñcil karavu.
Virtue abides in righteous hearts
Into minds of fruds deceit darts.        288
9. aḷavalla ceytāṅkē vīvar kaḷavalla
maṟṟiya tēṟṟā tavar.
They perish in their perfidy
Who know nothing but pilfery.        289
10. kaḷvārkkut taḷḷum uyirnili kaḷḷārkkut
taḷḷātu puttē ḷulaku
Even the body rejects thieves;
The honest men, heaven receives.        290

30. vāymi - Veracity

1. vāymi eṉappaṭuvatu yāteṉiṉ yātoṉṟum
tīmi ilāta colal
If "What is truth"? the question be,
It is to speak out evil-free.        291
2. poymmiyum vāymi yiṭatta puritīrnta
naṉmi payakkum eṉiṉ.
E'en falsehood may for truth suffice,
When good it brings removing vice.        292
3. taṉṉeñ caṟivatu poyyaṟka poyttapiṉ
taṉṉeñcē taṉṉic cuṭum.
Let not a man knowingly lie;
Conscience will scorch and make him sigh.        293
4. uḷḷattāl poyyā toḻukiṉ ulakattār
uḷḷattu ḷellām uḷaṉ.
He lives in loving hearts of all
Who serves the Truth serene in soul.        294
5. maṉattoṭu vāymi moḻiyiṉ tavattoṭu
tāṉañcey vāriṉ tali.
To speak the truth from heart sincere
Is more than giving and living austere.        295
6. poyyāmi aṉṉa pukaḻilli eyyāmi
ellā aṟamum tarum.
Not to lie brings all the prise
All virtues from Truth arise.        296
7. poyyāmi poyyāmi āṟṟiṉ aṟampiṟa
ceyyāmi ceyyāmi naṉṟu.
Lie not lie not. Naught else you need
All virtues are in Truth indeed.        297
8. puṟantūymi nīrāṉ amiyum akantūymi
vāymiyāl kāṇap paṭum.
Water makes you pure outward
Truth renders you pure inward.        298
9. ellā viḷakkum viḷakkalla cāṉṟōrkkup
poyyā viḷakkē viḷakku.
All lights are not lights for the wise;
Truth light is light bright like sun-light        299
10. yāmeyyāk kaṇṭavaṟṟuḷ illi eṉittoṉṟum
vāymiyiṉ nalla piṟa.
Of all the things we here have seen
Nothing surpasses Truth serene !        300

31. vekuḷāmi - Restrining anger

1. celliṭattuk kāppāṉ ciṉaṅkāppāṉ alliṭattuk
kākkiṉeṉ kāvākkāl eṉ
Anger aginst the weak is wrong
It is futile aginst the strong.        301
2. cellā iṭattuc ciṉantītu celliṭattum
ilataṉiṉ tīya piṟa
Vain is wrath aginst men of force
Aginst the meek it is still worse.        302
3. maṟattal vekuḷiyi yārmāṭṭum tīya
piṟattal ataṉāṉ varum
Off with wrath with any one.
It is the source of sin and pin.        303
4. nakiyum uvakiyum kollum ciṉattiṉ
pakiyum uḷavō piṟa.
Is there a foe like harmful ire
Which kills the smile and joyful cheer?        304
5. taṉṉittāṉ kākkiṉ ciṉamkākka kāvākkāl
taṉṉiyē kollum ciṉam.
Thyself to save, from wrath away!
If not thyself the wrath will slay.        305
6. ciṉameṉṉum cērntārik kolli iṉameṉṉum
ēmap puṇiyic cuṭum.
Friend-killer is the fatal rage
It burns the helpful kinship-barge.        306
7. ciṉattip poruḷeṉṟu koṇṭavaṉ kēṭu
nilattaṟintāṉ kipiḻiyā taṟṟu.
The wrath-lover to doom is bound
Like failless-hand that strikes the ground.        307
8. iṇareri tōyvaṉṉa iṉṉā ceyiṉum
puṇariṉ vekuḷāmi naṉṟu.
Save thy soul from burning ire
Though tortured like the touch of fire.        308
9. uḷḷiya tellām uṭaṉeytum uḷḷattāl
uḷḷāṉ vekuḷi eṉiṉ.
Wishes he gins as he wishes
If man refrins from rage vicious!        309
10. iṟantār iṟantār aṉiyar ciṉattit
tuṟantār tuṟantār tuṇi.
Dead are they who are anger-fed
Saints are they from whom wrath has fled.        310

32. iṉṉā ceyyāmi - non-violence

1. ciṟappuīṉum celvam peṟiṉum piṟarkkuiṉṉā
ceyyāmi mācaṟṟār kōḷ
The pure by faith mean pin to none
Though princely wealth by that is won.        311
2. kaṟuttuiṉṉā ceytavak kaṇṇum maṟuttuiṉṉā
ceyyāmi mācaṟṟār kōḷ.
The spotless hearts seek not revenge
Though Malice does the worst in rage.        312
3. ceyyāmal ceṟṟārkkum iṉṉāta ceytapiṉ
uyyā viḻumam tarum.
Revenging even cuseless hate
Bad-blood breeds and baneful heat.        313
4. iṉṉācey tāri oṟuttal avar nāṇa
naṉṉayam ceytu viṭal.
Doing good-turns, put them to shame
Thus chide the evil who do harm.        314
5. aṟiviṉāṉ ākuva tuṇṭō piṟitiṉnōy
tannōypōl pōṟṟāk kaṭi.
What does a man from wisdom gin
If he pines not at other's pin?        315
6. iṉṉā eṉattāṉ uṇarntavi tuṉṉāmi
vēṇṭum piṟaṉkaṇ ceyal.
What you feel as 'pin' to yourself
Do it not to the other-self        316
7. eṉittāṉum eññāṉṟum yārkkum maṉattāṉām
māṇācey yāmi tali.
Any, anywhere injure not
At any time even in thought.        317
8. taṉṉuyirkku iṉṉāmi tāṉaṟivāṉ eṉkolō
maṉṉuyirkku iṉṉā ceyal.
How can he injure other souls
Who in his life injury feels.        318
9. piṟarkkuiṉṉā muṟpakal ceyyiṉ tamakkuiṉṉā
piṟpakal tāmē varum.
Harm others in the forenoon
Harm seeks thee in afternoon.        319
10. nōyellām nōyceytār mēlavām nōyceyyār
nōyiṉmi vēṇṭu pavar.
No harm is done by peace-lovers
For pins rebound on pin-givers.        320
-----------

33. kollāmi - Non-killing

1. aṟaviṉi yāteṉil kollāmi kōṟal
piṟaviṉi ellām tarum
What is Virtue? 'Tis not to kill
For killing cuses every ill.        321
2. pakuttuṇṭu palluyir ōmputal nūlōr
tokuttavaṟṟuḷ ellān tali.
Share the food and serve all lives
This is the law of all the laws.        322
3. oṉṟāka nallatu kollāmi maṟṟuataṉ
piṉcārap poyyāmi naṉṟu.
Not to kill is unique good
The next, not to utter falsehood.        323
4. nallāṟu eṉappaṭuvatu yāteṉiṉ yātoṉṟum
kollāmi cūḻum neṟi.
What way is good? That we can say
The way away from heat to slay.        324
5. niliañci nīttāruḷ ellām koliañcik
kollāmi cūḻvāṉ tali
Of sints who renounce birth-fearing
The head is he who dreads killing.        325
6. kollāmi mēṟkoṇ ṭoḻukuvāṉ vāḻnāḷmēl
cellātu uyiruṇṇuṅ kūṟṟu.
Life-eating-Death shall spare the breath
Of him who no life puts to death.        326
7. taṉṉuyir nīppiṉum ceyyaṟka tāṉpiṟitu
iṉṉuyir nīkkum viṉi.
Kill not life that others cherish
Even when your life must perish.        327
8. naṉṟākum ākkam periteṉiṉum cāṉṟōrkkuk
koṉṟākum ākkam kaṭi.
The gin of slughter is a vice
Though deemed good in sacrifice.        328
9. koliviṉiya rākiya mākkaḷ puliviṉiyar
puṉmi terivā rakattu.
Those who live by slaying are
Eaters of carrion bizarre!        329
10. uyiruṭampiṉ nīkkiyār eṉpa ceyiruṭampiṉ
cellāttī vāḻkki yavar
The loathsome poor sickly and sore
Are killers stined by blood before        330
-------

34. niliyāmi - Instability

1. nillāta vaṟṟi niliyiṉa eṉṟuṇarum
pullaṟi vāṇmi kaṭi.
The worst of follies it is told
The fleeting as lasting to hold.        331
2. kūttāṭṭu avikkuḻāt taṟṟē peruñcelvam
pōkkum atuviḷin taṟṟu.
Like a drama-crowd wealth gathers
Like passing show its pride too goes.        332
3. aṟkā iyalpiṟṟuc celvam atupeṟṟāl
aṟkupa āṅkē ceyal.
Wealth wanes away; but when it comes
Take care to do enduring things.        333
4. nāḷeṉa oṉṟupōl kāṭṭi uyirīrum
vāḷatu uṇarvārp peṟiṉ
The showy day is but a saw
Your life, know that, to file and gnaw.        334
5. nācceṟṟu vikkuḷmēl vārāmuṉ nalviṉi
mēṟceṉṟu ceyyap paṭum
Ere tongue benumbs and hiccough comes
Rise up to do good deeds betimes.        335
6. nerunal uḷaṉoruvaṉ iṉṟilli eṉṉum
perumi uṭittuiv vulaku.
One was yesterday; not today!
The wonder of the world's way!        336
7. orupoḻutum vāḻvatu aṟiyār karutupa
kōṭiyum alla pala.
Man knows not his next moment
On crores of things he is intent.        337
8. kuṭampi taṉittuoḻiyap puḷpaṟan taṟṟē
uṭampōṭu uyiriṭi naṭpu.
The soul from body any day
Like bird from egg-shell flies away.        338
9. uṟaṅku vatupōlum cākkāṭu uṟaṅki
viḻippatu pōlum piṟappu.
Death is like a slumber deep
And birth like waking from that sleep.        339
10. pukkil amintiṉṟu kollō uṭampiṉuḷ
tuccil irunta uyirkku
The life berthed in this body shows
A fixed home it never knows.        340
--------

35. tuṟavu - Renunciation

1. yātaṉiṉ yātaṉiṉ nīṅkiyāṉ nōtal
ataṉiṉ ataṉiṉ ilaṉ
From what from what a man is free
From that, from that his torments flee.        341
2. vēṇṭiṉuṇ ṭākat tuṟakka tuṟantapiṉ
īṇṭuiyaṟ pāla pala
Give up all to gin the True
And endless joys shall hence seek you.        342
3. aṭalvēṇṭum aintaṉ pulatti viṭalvēṇṭum
vēṇṭiya ellām oruṅku
Curb the senses five and renounce
The craving desires all at once.        343
4. iyalpākum nōṉpiṟkoṉṟu iṉmi uṭimi
mayalākum maṟṟum peyarttu.
To have nothing is law of vows
Having the least deludes and snares.        344
5. maṟṟum toṭarppāṭu evaṉkol piṟappaṟukkal
uṟṟārkku uṭampum miki
Why add to bonds while this body
Is too much for sints to be birth-free.        345
6. yāṉeṉatu eṉṉum cerukkuaṟuppāṉ vāṉōrkku
uyarnta ulakam pukum
Who curbs the pride of I and mine
Gets a world rare for gods to gin.        346
7. paṟṟi viṭāa iṭumpikaḷ paṟṟiṉip
paṟṟi viṭāa tavarkku
Grief clings on and on to those
Who cling to bonds without release.        347
8. talippaṭṭār tīrat tuṟantār mayaṅki
valippaṭṭār maṟṟi yavar
Who renounce all are free from care
Others suffer delusive snare.        348
9. paṟṟaṟṟa kaṇṇē piṟappaṟukkum maṟṟu
niliyāmi kāṇap paṭum
Bondage cut off, rebirth is off
The world then seems instable stuff.        349
10. paṟṟuka paṟṟaṟṟāṉ paṟṟiṉi appaṟṟip
paṟṟuka paṟṟu viṭaṟku
Bind Thyself to the unbound one
That binding breaks all bonds anon.        350
-------

36. meyyuṇartal - Truth consciousness

1. poruḷalla vaṟṟip poruḷeṉṟu uṇarum
maruḷāṉām māṇāp piṟappu.
That error entils ignoble birth
Which deems vin things as things of worth.        351
2. iruḷnīṅki iṉpam payakkum maruḷ nīṅki
mācaṟu kāṭci yavarkku
Men of spotless pure insight
Enjoy delight devoid of night.        352
3. aiyattiṉ nīṅkit teḷintārkku viyattiṉ
vāṉam naṇiya tuṭittu
To doubtless minds whose heart is clear
More than earth heaven is near.        353
4. aiyuṇarvu eytiyak kaṇṇum payamiṉṟē
meyyuṇarvu illā tavarkku.
Knowledge of five senses is vin
Without knowing the Truth within.        354
5. epporuḷ ettaṉmit tāyiṉum apporuḷ
meypporuḷ kāṇpatu aṟivu.
Knowledge is Truth of things to find
In every case of every kind.        355
6. kaṟṟīṇṭu meypporuḷ kaṇṭār talippaṭuvar
maṟṟīṇṭu vārā neṟi
Who learn and here the Truth discern
Enter the path of non-return.        356
7. ōrttuḷḷam uḷḷatu uṇariṉ orutaliyāp
pērttuḷḷa vēṇṭā piṟappu.
One-minded sage sees inner-truth
He is free from thoughts of rebirth.        357
8. piṟappeṉṉum pētimi nīṅkac ciṟappeṉṉum
cemporuḷ kāṇpatu aṟivu.
It is knowledge to know Self-Truth
And remove the folly of birth.        358
9. cārpuṇarntu cārpu keṭoḻukiṉ maṟṟaḻittuc
cārtarā cārtaru nōy.
Know the Refuge; off with bondage
Be free from ills of thraldom, O sage.        359
10. kāmam vekuḷi mayakkam ivi mūṉṟaṉ
nāmam keṭakkeṭum nōy
Woes expire when lust, wrath, folly
Expire even to name, fully.        360
---------

37. avā aṟuttal - Curbing of desire

1. avāeṉpa ellā uyirkkum eññāṉṟum
tavāap piṟappīṉum vittu.
Desire to all, always is seed
From which ceaseless births proceed.        361
2. vēṇṭuṅkāl vēṇṭum piṟavāmi maṟṟatu
vēṇṭāmi vēṇṭa varum.
If long thou must, long for non-birth
It comes by longing no more for earth.        362
3. vēṇṭāmi aṉṉa viḻuccelvam īṇṭilli
yāṇṭum aḵtoppatu il.
No such wealth is here and there
As peerless wealth of non-desire.        363
4. tūuymi eṉpatu avāviṉmi maṟṟatu
vāaymi vēṇṭa varum
To nothing crave is purity
That is the fruit of verity.        364
5. aṟṟavar eṉpār avāaṟṟār maṟṟiyār
aṟṟāka aṟṟatu ilar
The free are those who desire not
The rest not free in bonds are cught.        365
6. añcuva tōrum aṟaṉē oruvaṉi
vañcippa tōrum avā
Dread desire; Virtue is there
To every soul desire is snare!        366
7. avāviṉi āṟṟa aṟuppiṉ tavāviṉi
tāṉvēṇṭu māṟṟāṉ varum
Destroy desire; deliverance
Comes as much as you aspire hence.        367
8. avāillārk killākun tuṉpam aḵtuṇṭēl
tavāatu mēṉmēl varum.
Desire extinct no sorrow-tints
Grief comes on grief where it pretends.        368
9. iṉpam iṭiyaṟā tīṇṭum avāveṉṉum
tuṉpattuḷ tuṉpam keṭiṉ.
Desire, the woe of woes destroy
Joy of joys here you enjoy.        369
10. ārā iyaṟki avānīppiṉ anniliyē
pērā iyaṟki tarum.
Off with desire insatiate
You gin the native blissful state.        370
------

38. ūḻ - Destiny

1. ākūḻāl tōṉṟum aciviṉmi kipporuḷ
pōkūḻāl tōṉṟum maṭi
Efforts succeed by waxing star
Wealth-losing brings waning star.        371
2. pētip paṭukkum iḻavūḻ aṟivakaṟṟum
ākalūḻ uṟṟak kaṭi
Loss-fate makes a dull fool of us
Gain-fate makes us prosperous, wise!        372
3. nuṇṇiya nūlpala kaṟpiṉum maṟṟuntaṉ
uṇmi aṟivē mikum
What matters subtle study deep?
Levels of innate wisdom-keep.        373
4. iruvēṟu ulakattu iyaṟki tiruvēṟu
teḷḷiya rātalum vēṟu
Two natures in the world obtin
Some wealth and others wisdom gin.        374
5. nallavi ellāan tīyavām tīyavum
nallavām celvam ceyaṟku.
In making wealth fate changes mood;
The good as bad and bad as good.        375
6. pariyiṉum ākāvām pālalla uyttuc
coriyiṉum pōkā tama.
Things not thine never remin
Things destined are surely thine.        376
7. vakuttāṉ vakutta vakiyallāl kōṭi
tokuttārkkum tuyttal aritu.
Who crores amass enjoy but what
The Dispenser's decrees allot.        377
8. tuṟappārmaṉ tuppura villār uṟaṟpāla
ūṭṭā kaḻiyum eṉiṉ.
The destitute desire will quit
If fate with ills visit them not.        378
9. naṉṟāṅkāl nallavāk kāṇpavar aṉṟāṅkāl
allaṟ paṭuva tevaṉ
Who good in time of good perceive
In evil time why should they grieve?        379
10. ūḻiṟ peruvali yāvuḷa maṟṟoṉṟu
cūḻiṉum tāṉmun tuṟum.
What power surpasses fate? Its will
Persists aginst the human skill.        380

39. iṟimāṭci - The grandeur of monarchy

1. paṭikuṭi kūḻamiccu naṭparaṇ āṟum
uṭiyāṉ aracaruḷ ēṟu.
People, troops, wealth, forts, council, friends
Who owns these six is lion of kings.        381
2. añcāmi īki aṟivūkkam innāṉkum
eñcāmi vēntark kiyalpu.
Courage, giving, knowledge and zeal
Are four failless features royal.        382
3. tūṅkāmi kalvi tuṇivuṭimi immūṉṟum
nīṅkā nilaṉāḷ pavarkku.
Alertness, learning, bravery
Are adjuncts three of monarchy.        383
4. aṟaṉiḻukkā tallavi nīkki maṟaṉiḻukkā
māṉam uṭiya taracu.
A brave noble king refrins from vice
Full of virtue and enterprise.        384
5. iyaṟṟalum īṭṭalum kāttalum kātta
vakuttalum valla taracu.
The able king gets, stores and guards
And spends them for people's safeguards.        385
6. kāṭcik keḷiyaṉ kaṭuñcollaṉ allaṉēl
mīkkūṟum maṉṉaṉ nilam.
That land prospers where the king is
Easy to see, not harsh of words.        386
7. iṉcolāl īttaḷikka vallārkkut taṉcolāl
tāṉkaṇ ṭaṉittiv vulaku.
The world commends and acts his phrase
Who sweetly speaks and gives with grace.        387
8. muṟiceytu kāppāṟṟum maṉṉavaṉ makkaṭku
iṟiyeṉṟu vikkap paṭum.
He is the Lord of men who does
Sound justice and saves his race.        388
9. cevikippac coṟpoṟukkum paṇpuṭi vēntaṉ
kavikikkīḻt taṅkum ulaku
Under his shelter thrives the world
Who bears remarks bitter and bold.        389
10. koṭiyaḷi ceṅkōl kuṭiyōmpal nāṉkum
uṭiyāṉām vēntark koḷi.
He is the Light of Kings who has
Bounty, justice, care and grace.        390
------

40. kalvi - Education

1. kaṟka kacaṭaṟak kaṟpavi kaṟṟapiṉ
niṟka ataṟkut taka.
Lore worth learning, learn flawlessly
Live by that learning thoroughly.        391
2. eṇṇeṉpa ēṉi eḻutteṉpa ivviraṇṭum
kaṇṇeṉpa vāḻum uyirkku.
Letter, number, art and science
Of living kind both are the eyes.        392
3. kaṇṇuṭiyar eṉpavar kaṟṟōr mukattiraṇṭu
puṇṇuṭiyar kallā tavar.
The learned alone have eyes on face
The ignorant two sores of disgrace.        393
4. uvappat talikkūṭi uḷḷap pirital
aṉittē pulavar toḻil
To meet with joy and part with thought
Of learned men this is the art.        394
5. uṭiyārmuṉ illārpōl ēkkaṟṟuṅ kaṟṟār
kaṭiyarē kallā tavar
Like poor before rich they yearn:
For knowledge: the low never learn.        395
6. toṭṭaṉit tūṟum maṇaṟkēṇi māntarkkuk
kaṟṟaṉit tūṟum aṟivu.
As deep you dig the sand spring flows
As deep you learn the knowledge grows.        396
7. yātāṉum nāṭāmāl ūrāmāl eṉṉoruvaṉ
cāntuṇiyuṅ kallāta vāṟu.
All lands and towns are learner's own
Why not till death learning go on!        397
8. orumikkaṇ tāṉkaṟṟa kalvi oruvaṟku
eḻumiyum ēmāp puṭittu
The joy of learning in one birth
Exalts man upto his seventh.        398
9. tāmiṉ puṟuvatu ulakiṉ puṟakkaṇṭu
kāmuṟuvar kaṟṟaṟin tār.
The learned foster learning more
On seeing the world enjoy their lore.        399
10. kēṭil viḻuccelvam kalvi oruvaṟku
māṭalla maṟṟi yavi.
Learning is wealth none could destroy
Nothing else gives genuine joy.        400
--------

41. kallāmi - Non-learning

1. araṅkiṉṟi vaṭṭāṭi yaṟṟē nirampiya
nūliṉṟik kōṭṭi koḷal.
Like play of chess on squareless board
Vain is imperfect loreless word.        401
2. kallātāṉ coṟkā muṟutal muliyiraṇṭum
illātāḷ peṇkāmuṟ ṟaṟṟu.
Unlearned man aspiring speech
Is breastless lady's love-approach.        402
3. kallā tavarum naṉinallar kaṟṟārmuṉ
collā tirukkap peṟiṉ.
Ev'n unread men are good and wise
If before the wise, they hold their peace.        403
4. kallātāṉ ōṭpam kaḻiyanaṉ ṟāyiṉum
koḷḷār aṟivuṭi yār.
The unread's wit though excellent
Is not valued by the savant.        404
5. kallā oruvaṉ takimi talippeytu
collāṭac cōrvu paṭum.
A man untught when speech he vunts
Sadly fails before savants.        405
6. uḷareṉṉum māttiriyar allāl payavāk
kaḷaraṉiyar kallā tavar.
People speak of untught minds
"They just exist like barren lands".        406
7. nuṇmāṇ nuḻipulam illāṉ eḻilnalam
maṇmāṇ puṉipāvi yaṟṟu
Like pinted clay-doll is his show
Grand subtle lore who fails to know.        407
8. nallārkaṇ paṭṭa vaṟumiyiṉ iṉṉātē
kallārkaṇ paṭṭa tiru
Wealth in the hand of fools is worse
Than a learned man's empty purse.        408
9. mēṟpiṟantā rāyiṉum kallātār kīḻppiṟantum
kaṟṟār aṉittilar pāṭu.
Lower are fools of higher birth
Than low-born men of learning's worth.        409
10. vilaṅkoṭu makkaḷ aṉiyar ilaṅkunūl
kaṟṟārōṭu ēṉi yavar.
Like beasts before men, dunces are
Before scholars of shining lore.        410
----------

42. kēḷvi - Listening

1. celvattuḷ celvañ ceviccelvam accelvam
celvattu ḷellān tali.
Wealth of wealths is listening's wealth
It is the best of wealths on earth.        411
2. cevikkuṇa villāta pōḻtu ciṟitu
vayiṟṟukkum īyap paṭum.
Some food for the stomach is brought
When the ear gets no food for thought.        412
3. ceviyuṇaviṟ kēḷvi yuṭiyār aviyuṇaviṉ
āṉṟārō ṭoppar nilattu.
Whose ears get lots of wisdom-food
Equal gods on oblations fed.        413
4. kaṟṟila ṉāyiṉum kēṭka aḵtoruvaṟku
oṟkattiṉ ūṟṟān tuṇi.
Though not learned, hear and heed
That serves a staff and stay in need.        414
5. iḻukkal uṭiyuḻi ūṟṟukkōl aṟṟē
oḻukka muṭiyārvāyc col.
Virtuous men's wisdom is found
A strong staff on slippery ground.        415
6. eṉittāṉum nallavi kēṭka aṉittāṉum
āṉṟa perumi tarum.
Lend ear to good words however few
That much will highly exalt you.        416
7. piḻittuṇarntum pētimi collār iḻittuṇarn
tīṇṭiya kēḷvi yavar.
Who listen well and learn sharply
Not ev'n by slip speak foolishly.        417
8. kēṭpiṉuṅ kēḷāt takiyavē kēḷviyāl
tōṭkap paṭāta cevi.
That ear though hearing is dulled
Which is not by wisdom drilled.        418
9. nuṇaṅkiya kēḷviyar rallār vaṇaṅkiya
vāyiṉa rātal aritu
A modest mouth is hard for those
Who care little to counsels wise.        419
10. ceviyiṟ cuviyuṇarā vāyuṇarviṉ mākkaḷ
aviyiṉum vāḻiṉum eṉ.
Who taste by mouth and not by ear
What if they fare ill or well here?        420
----------

43. aṟivuṭimi - The possession of knowledge

1. aṟivaṟṟaṅ kākkuṅ karuvi ceṟuvārkkum
uḷḷaḻikka lākā araṇ.
Wisdom's weapon wards off all woes
It is a fort defying foes.        421
2. ceṉṟa iṭattāl celaviṭā tītorīi
naṉṟiṉpāl uyppa taṟivu.
Wisdom checks the straying senses
Expels evils, impels goodness.        422
3. epporuḷ yāryārvāyk kēṭpiṉum apporuḷ
meypporuḷ kāṇpatu aṟivu.
To grasp the Truth from everywhere
From everyone is wisdom fair.        423
4. eṇporuḷa vākac celaccollit tāṉ piṟarvāy
nuṇporuḷ kāṇpatu aṟivu.
Speaking out thoughts in clear trends
Wisdom subtle sense comprehends.        424
5. ulakam taḻīiya toṭpam malartalum
kūmpalum illa taṟivu.
The wise-world the wise befriend
They bloom nor gloom, equal in mind.        425
6. evva tuṟivatu ulakam ulakattōṭu
avva tuṟivatu aṟivu.
As moves the world so move the wise
In tune with changing times and ways.        426
7. aṟivuṭiyār āva taṟivār aṟivilār
aḵtaṟi kallā tavar.
The wise foresee what is to come
The unwise lack in that wisdom.        427
8. añcuvatu añcāmi pētimi añcuvatu
añcal aṟivār toḻil.
Fear the frightful and act wisely
Not to fear the frightful's folly.        428
9. etiratāk kākkum aṟiviṉārk killi
atira varuvatōr nōy.
No frightful evil shocks the wise
Who guard themselves aginst surprise.        429
10. aṟivuṭiyār ellām uṭiyār aṟivilār
eṉṉuṭiya rēṉum ilar.
Who have wisdom they are all full
Whatev'r they own, misfits are nil.        430

44. kuṟṟaṅkaṭital - Avoiding faults

1. cerukkuñ ciṉamum ciṟumiyum illār
perukkam perumita nīrttu.
Plenty is their prosperity
Who're free from wrath pride lust petty.        431
2. ivaṟalum māṇpiṟanta māṉamum māṇā
uvakiyum ētam iṟikku.
Mean pride, low pleasure, avarice
These add blemishes to a prince.        432
3. tiṉittuṇiyāṅ kuṟṟam variṉum paṉittuṇiyāk
koḷvar paḻināṇu vār.
Though millet-small their faults might seem
Men fearing disgrace, Palm-tall deem.        433
4. kuṟṟamē kākka poruḷākak kuṟṟamē
aṟṟam tarūum paki.
Watch like treasure freedom from fault
Our fatal foe is that default.        434
5. varumuṉṉark kāvātāṉ vāḻkki erimuṉṉar
vittūṟu pōlak keṭum.
Who fails to guard himself from flaw
Loses his life like flame-lit straw.        435
6. taṉkuṟṟam nīkkip piṟarkuṟṟam kāṇpiṟpiṉ
eṉkuṟṟa mākum iṟikku
What fault can be the king's who cures
First his faults, then scans others.        436
7. ceyaṟpāla ceyyā tivaṟiyāṉ celvam
uyaṟpāla taṉṟik keṭum.
That miser's wealth shall waste and end
Who would not for a good cuse spend.        437
8. paṟṟuḷḷam eṉṉum ivaṟaṉmi eṟṟuḷḷum
eṇṇap paṭuvatoṉ ṟaṉṟu.
The gripping greed of miser's heart
Is more than fault the worst apart.        438
9. viyavaṟka eññāṉṟum taṉṉi nayavaṟka
naṉṟi payavā viṉi.
Never boast yourself in any mood
Nor do a deed that does no good.        439
10. kātala kātal aṟiyāmi uykkiṟpiṉ
ētila ētilār nūl.
All designs of the foes shall fail
If one his wishes guards in veil.        440

45. periyārit tuṇikkōṭal - Gaining great men's help

1. aṟaṉaṟintu mūtta aṟivuṭiyār kēṇmi
tiṟaṉaṟintu tērntu koḷal.
Weigh their worth and friendship gin
Of men of virtue and mature brin.        441
2. uṟṟanōy nīkki uṟāami muṟkākkum
peṟṟiyārp pēṇik koḷal.
Cherish the help of men of skill
Who ward and safe-guard you from ill.        442
3. ariyavaṟṟu ḷellām aritē periyārip
pēṇit tamarāk koḷal.
Honour and have the great your own
Is rarest of the rare things known.        443
4. tammiṟ periyār tamarā oḻukutal
vaṉmiyu ḷellān tali.
To have betters as intimates
Power of all powers promotes.        444
5. cūḻvārkaṇ ṇāka oḻukalāṉ maṉṉavaṉ
cūḻvāric cūḻntu koḷal.
Ministers are the monarch's eyes
Round him should be the right and wise.        445
6. takkā riṉattaṉāyt tāṉoḻuka vallāṉic
ceṟṟār ceyakkiṭanta til.
To move with worthy friends who knows
Has none to fear from frightful foes.        446
7. iṭikkun tuṇiyāri āḷvāri yārē
keṭukkun takimi yavar
No foe can foil his powers
whose friends reprove him when he errs.        447
8. iṭippāri illāta ēmarā maṉṉaṉ
keṭuppā rilāṉuṅ keṭum.
The careless king whom none reproves
Ruins himself sans harmful foes.        448
9. mutalilārkku ūtiyam illi mataliyāñ
cārpilārkku illi nili.
No capital, no gin in trade
No prop secure sans good comrade.        449
10. pallār pakikoḷaliṟ pattaṭutta tīmittē
nallār toṭarki viṭal.
To give up good friends is ten times worse
Than being hated by countless foes.        450

46. ciṟṟiṉañ cērāmi - Avoiding mean company

1. ciṟṟiṉam añcum perumi ciṟumitāṉ
cuṟṟamāc cūḻntu viṭum.
The ignoble the noble fear
The mean hold them as kinsmen dear.        451
2. nilattiyalpāl nīrtirin taṟṟākum māntarkku
iṉattiyalpa tākum aṟivu.
With soil changes water's taste
With mates changes the mental state.        452
3. maṉattāṉām māntark kuṇarcci iṉattāṉām
iṉṉāṉ eṉappaṭuñ col.
Wisdom depends upon the mind
The worth of man upon his friend.        453
4. maṉattu ḷatupōlak kāṭṭi oruvaṟku
iṉattuḷa tākum aṟivu.
Wisdom seems to come from mind
But it truly flows from the kind.        454
5. maṉantūymi ceyviṉi tūymi iraṇṭum
iṉamtūymi tūvā varum.
Purity of the thought and deed
Comes from good company indeed.        455
6. maṉamtūyārk keccamnaṉ ṟākum iṉamtūyārkku
illinaṉ ṟākā viṉi.
Pure-hearted get good progeny
Pure friendship acts with victory.        456
7. maṉanalam maṉṉuyirk kākkam iṉanalam
ellāp pukaḻum tarum.
Goodness of mind increases gin
Good friendship fosters fame agin.        457
8. maṉanalam naṉkuṭiya rāyiṉum cāṉṟōrkku
iṉanalam ēmāp puṭittu.
Men of wisdom, though good in mind
In friends of worth a new strength find.        458
9. maṉanalattiṉ ākum maṟumimaṟ ṟaḵtum
iṉanalattiṉ ēmāp puṭittu.
Good mind decides the future bliss
Good company gins strength to this.        459
10. nalliṉatti ṉūṅkun tuṇiyilli tīyiṉattiṉ
allaṟ paṭuppatūum il.
No help good company exeeds;
The bad to untold anguish leads.        460

47. terintu ceyalvaki - Deliberation before action

1. aḻivatūum āvatūum āki vaḻipayakkum
ūtiyamum cūḻntu ceyal.
Weigh well output the loss and gin
And proper action ascertin.        461
2. terinta iṉattoṭu tērnteṇṇic ceyvārkku
arumporuḷ yātoṉṟum il.
Nothing is hard for him who acts
With worthy counsels weighing facts.        462
3. ākkam karuti mutaliḻakkum ceyviṉi
ūkkār aṟivuṭi yār,
The wise risk not their capital
In doubtful gins and lose their all.        463
4. teḷivi lataṉit toṭaṅkār iḷiveṉṉum
ētappāṭu añcu pavar.
They who scornful reproach fear
Commence no work not made clear.        464
5. vakiyaṟac cūḻā teḻutal pakivarip
pāttip paṭuppatō rāṟu.
Who marches without plans and ways
His field is sure to foster foes.        465
6. ceytakka alla ceyakkeṭum ceytakka
ceyyāmi yāṉum keṭum.
Doing unfit action ruins
Failing fit-act also ruins.        466
7. eṇṇit tuṇika karumam tuṇintapiṉ
eṇṇuvam eṉpatu iḻukku.
Think and dare a proper deed
Dare and think is bad in need.        467
8. āṟṟiṉ varuntā varuttam palarniṉṟu
pōṟṟiṉum pottup paṭum.
Toil without a plan ahead
Is doomed to fall though supported.        468
9. naṉṟāṟṟa luḷḷum tavaṟuṇṭu avaravar
paṇpaṟin tāṟṟāk kaṭi.
Attune the deeds to habitude
Or ev'n good leads to evil feud.        469
10. eḷḷāta eṇṇic ceyalvēṇṭum tammoṭu
koḷḷāta koḷḷātu ulaku.
Do deeds above reproachfulness
The world refutes uncomely mess.        470

48. valiyaṟital - Judging strength

1. viṉivaliyum taṉvaliyum māṟṟāṉ valiyum
tuṇivaliyum tūkkic ceyal.
Judge act and might and foeman's strength
The allies' strength and go at length.        471
2. olva taṟivatu aṟintataṉ kaṇtaṅkic
celvārkkuc cellātatu il.
Nothing hampers the firm who know
What they can and how to go.        472
3. uṭittam valiyaṟiyār ūkkattiṉ ūkki
iṭikkaṇ murintār palar.
Many know not their meagre might
Their pride breaks up in boastful fight.        473
4. amintāṅ koḻukāṉ aḷavaṟiyāṉ taṉṉi
viyantāṉ virintu keṭum.
Who adapts not, outsteps measure
And brags himself-his fall is sure.        474
5. pīlipey cākāṭum acciṟum appaṇṭañ
cāla mikuttup peyiṉ.
Even the gentle peacock's plume
Cart's axle breaks by gross volume.        475
6. nuṉikkompar ēṟiṉār aḵtiṟan tūkkiṉ
uyirkkiṟuti yāki viṭum.
Beyond the branches' tip who skips
Ends the life as his body rips.        476
7. āṟṟiṉ aḷavaṟintu īka atuporuḷ
pōṟṟi vaḻaṅkum neṟi.
Know the limit; grant with measure
This way give and guard your treasure.        477
8. ākāṟu aḷaviṭṭi tāyiṉum kēṭilli
pōkāṟu akalāk kaṭi.
The outflow must not be excess
No matter how small income is.        478
9. aḷavaṟintu vāḻātāṉ vāḻkki uḷapōla
illākit tōṉṟāk keṭum.
Who does not know to live in bounds
His life seems rich but thins and ends.        479
10. uḷavari tūkkāta oppura vāṇmi
vaḷavari vallik keṭum.
Wealth amassed quickly vanishes
Sans level if one lavishes.        480

49. kālamaṟital - Knowing proper time

1. pakalvellum kūkiyik kākki ikalvellum
vēntarkku vēṇṭum poḻutu.
By day the crow defeats the owl
Kings need right time their foes to quell.        481
2. paruvattōṭu oṭṭa oḻukal tiruviṉit
tīrāmi ārkkum kayiṟu.
Well-ordered seasoned act is cord
That fortune binds in bon accord.        482
3. aruviṉi eṉpa uḷavō karuviyāṉ
kālam aṟintu ceyiṉ
What is hard for him who acts
With proper means and time and tacts?        483
4. ñālam karutiṉuṅ kikūṭum kālam
karuti iṭattāṟ ceyiṉ.
Choose proper time and act and place
Even the world you win with ease.        484
5. kālam karuti iruppar kalaṅkātu
ñālam karutu pavar.
Who want to win the world sublime
Wait unruffled biding their time.        485
6. ūkka muṭiyāṉ oṭukkam porutakar
tākkaṟkup pērun takittu.
By self-restrint stalwarts keep fit
Like rams retreating but to butt.        486
7. poḷḷeṉa āṅkē puṟamvērār kālampārttu
uḷvērppar oḷḷi yavar.
The wise jut not their vital fire
They watch their time with hidden ire.        487
8. ceṟunarik kāṇiṉ cumakka iṟuvari
kāṇiṉ kiḻakkām tali.
Bear with hostiles when you meet them
Fell down their head in fateful time.        488
9. eytaṟ kariyatu iyintakkāl anniliyē
ceytaṟ kariya ceyal.
When comes the season ripe and rare
Dare and do hard things then and there.        489
10. kokkokka kūmpum paruvattu maṟṟataṉ
kuttokka cīrtta iṭattu.
In waiting time feign peace like stork
In fighting time strike like its peck.        490

50. iṭaṉaṟital - Judging the place

1. toṭaṅkaṟka evviṉiyum eḷḷaṟka muṟṟum
iṭaṅkaṇṭa piṉal latu
No action take, no foe despise
Until you have surveyed the place.        491
2. muraṇcērnta moympi ṉavarkkum araṇcērntām
ākkam palavun tarum
Many are gins of fortresses
Ev'n to kings of power and prowess.        492
3. āṟṟārum āṟṟi aṭupa iṭaṉaṟintu
pōṟṟārkaṇ pōṟṟic ceyiṉ.
Weaklings too withstand foe's offence
In proper fields of strong defence.        493
4. eṇṇiyār eṇṇam iḻappar iṭaṉaṟintu
tuṉṉiyār tuṉṉic ceyiṉ
If fighters fight in vantage field
The plans of foes shall be baffled.        494
5. neṭumpuṉaluḷ vellum mutali aṭumpuṉaliṉ
nīṅkiṉ ataṉip piṟa.
In water crocodile previls
In land before others it fails.        495
6. kaṭalōṭā kālval neṭuntēr kaṭalōṭum
nāvāyum ōṭā nilattu
Sea-going ship goes not on shore
Nor on sea the strong-wheeled car.        496
7. añcāmi allāl tuṇivēṇṭā eñcāmi
eṇṇi iṭattāl ceyiṉ
No aid but daring dash they need
When field is chosen right for deed.        497
8. ciṟupaṭiyāṉ celliṭam cēriṉ uṟupaṭiyāṉ
ūkkam aḻintu viṭum
Though force is small, if place is right
One quells a foe of well-armed might.        498
9. ciṟinalaṉum cīrum ilareṉiṉum māntar
uṟinilattōṭu oṭṭal aritu
To face a foe at home is vin
Though fort and status are not fine.        499
10. kālāḻ kaḷaril nariyaṭum kaṇṇañcā
vēḷāḷ mukatta kaḷiṟu.
A fox can kill a war tusker
Fearless with feet in deep quagmire.        500

51. terintu teḷital - Testing of men for confidence

1. aṟamporuḷ iṉpam uyireccam nāṉkiṉ
tiṟanterintu tēṟap paṭum
Pleasure, gold, fear of life Virtue-
Test by these four and trust the true.        501
2. kuṭippiṟantu kuṟṟattiṉ nīṅki vaṭuppariyum
nāṇuṭiyāṉ kaṭṭē teḷivu
Spotless name of noble birth
Shamed of stin-that choice is worth.        502
3. ariyakaṟṟu ācaṟṟār kaṇṇum teriyuṅkāl
iṉmi aritē veḷiṟu
Though deep scholars of stinless sense
Rare is freedom from ignorance.        503
4. kuṇamnāṭik kuṟṟamum nāṭi avaṟṟuḷ
mikināṭi mikka koḷal
Good and evil in man weigh well
Judge him by virtues which previl.        504
5. perumikkum ēṉic ciṟumikkum tattam
karumamē kaṭṭaḷik kal
By the touchstone of deeds is seen
If any one is great or mean.        505
6. aṟṟārit tēṟutal ōmpuka maṟṟavar
paṟṟilar nāṇār paḻi
Choose not those men without kinsmen
Without affine or shame of sin.        506
7. kātaṉmi kantā aṟivaṟiyārt tēṟutal
pētimi yellām tarum
On favour leaning fools you choose;
Folly in all its forms ensues.        507
8. tērāṉ piṟaṉit teḷintāṉ vaḻimuṟi
tīrā iṭumpi tarum
To trust an untried stranger brings
Endless troubles on all our kins.        508
9. tēṟaṟka yāriyum tērātu tērntapiṉ
tēṟuka tēṟum poruḷ
Trust not without testing and then
Find proper work for trusted men.        509
10. tērāṉ teḷivum teḷintāṉkaṇ aiyuṟavum
tīrā iṭumpi tarum.
Trust without test; The trusted doubt;
Both entil troubles in and out.        510

52. terintu viṉiyāṭal - Testing and entrusting

1. naṉmiyum tīmiyum nāṭi nalampurinta
taṉmiyāṉ āḷap paṭum.
Employ the wise who will discern
The good and bad and do good turn.        511
2. vāri perukki vaḷampaṭuttu uṟṟavi
ārāyvāṉ ceyka viṉi
Let him act who resource swells;
Fosters wealth and prevents ills.        512
3. aṉpaṟivu tēṟṟam avāviṉmi innāṉkum
naṉkuṭiyāṉ kaṭṭē teḷivu
Trust him in whom these four you see:
Love, wit, non-craving, clarity.        513
4. eṉivakiyāṉ tēṟiyak kaṇṇum viṉivakiyāṉ
vēṟākum māntar palar
Though tried and found fit, yet we see
Many differ before duty.        514
5. aṟintāṟṟic ceykiṟpāṟku allāl viṉitāṉ
ciṟantāṉeṉṟu ēvaṟpāṟ ṟaṉṟu.
Wise able men with power invest
Not by fondness but by hard test.        515
6. ceyvāṉi nāṭi viṉināṭik kālattōṭu
eyta uṇarntu ceyal
Discern the agent and the deed
And just in proper time proceed.        516
7. itaṉi itaṉāl ivaṉmuṭikkum eṉṟāyntu
ataṉi avaṉkaṇ viṭal.
This work, by this, this man can do
Like this entrust the duty due.        517
8. viṉikkurimi nāṭiya piṉṟi avaṉi
ataṟkuriya ṉākac ceyal.
His fitness for the duty scan
Leave him to do the best he can.        518
9. viṉikkaṇ viṉiyuṭiyāṉ kēṇmivē ṟāka
niṉippāṉi nīṅkum tiru.
Who do duty for duty's sake
Doubt them; and fortune departs quick.        519
10. nāṭōṟum nāṭuka maṉṉaṉ viṉiceyvāṉ
kōṭāmi kōṭā tulaku
Worker stright the world is stright
The king must look to this aright.        520

53. cuṟṟantaḻāl - Cherishing kinsmen

1. paṟṟaṟṟa kaṇṇum paḻamipā rāṭṭutal
cuṟṟattār kaṇṇē yuḷa
Let fortunes go; yet kinsmen know
The old accustomed love to show.        521
2. viruppaṟāc cuṟṟam iyiyiṉ aruppaṟā
ākkam palavun tarum.
The gift of loving Kins bestows
Fadeless fortune's fresh flowers.        522
3. aḷavaḷā villātāṉ vāḻkki kuḷavaḷāk
kōṭiṉṟi nīrniṟin taṟṟu.
A kinless wealth is like a tank
Which overflows without a bank.        523
4. cuṟṟattāl cuṟṟap paṭoḻukal celvantāṉ
peṟṟattāl peṟṟa payaṉ.
The fruit of growing wealth is gined
When kith and kin are happy found.        524
5. koṭuttalum iṉcolum āṟṟiṉ aṭukkiya
cuṟṟattāl cuṟṟap paṭum.
Loving words and liberal hand
Encircle kith and kin around.        525
6. perumkoṭiyāṉ pēṇāṉ vekuḷi avaṉiṉ
maruṅkuuṭiyār mānilattu il.
Large giver and wrathless man
Commands on earth countless kinsmen.        526
7. kākki karavā karintuṇṇum ākkamum
aṉṉanī rārkkē uḷa.
The crows hide not; thy call and eat
Welfare abides a man of heart.        527
8. potunōkkāṉ vēntaṉ variciyā nōkkiṉ
atunōkki vāḻvār palar.
From public gaze when kings perceive
Each one's merits so many thrive.        528
9. tamarākit taṟṟuṟantār cuṟṟam amarāmik
kāraṇam iṉṟi varum.
Forsaken friends will come and stay
When cuse for discord goes away.        529
10. uḻippirintu kāraṇattiṉ vantāṉi vēntaṉ
iḻittiruntu eṇṇik koḷal.
Who leaves and returns with motive
The king should test him and receive.        530

54. poccāvāmi - Unforgetfulness

1. iṟanta vekuḷiyiṉ tītē ciṟanta
uvaki makiḻcciyiṟ cōrvu.
Worse than wrath in excess is
Forgetfulness in joy-excess.        531
2. poccāppuk kollum pukaḻi aṟiviṉi
nicca nirappukkoṉ ṟāṅku.
Negligence kills renown just as
Ceaseless want wisdom destroys.        532
3. poccāppārkku illi pukaḻmi atuvulakattu
eppālnū lōrkkum tuṇivu.
Forgetful nature fails of fame
All schools of thinkers say the same.        533
4. acca muṭiyārkku araṇilli āṅkilli
poccāp puṭiyārkku naṉku.
The fearful find no fortress here
The forgetful find good never.        534
5. muṉṉuṟak kāvātu iḻukkiyāṉ taṉpiḻi
piṉṉūṟu iraṅki viṭum.
Failing foresight the guardless man
Shall rue his folly later on.        535
6. iḻukkāmi yārmāṭṭum eṉṟum vaḻukkāmi
vāyiṉ atuvoppatu il.
Forget none; watch with wakeful care
Miss none; the gin is sans compare.        536
7. ariyeṉṟu ākāta illipoc cāvāk
karuviyāl pōṟṟic ceyiṉ.
With cutious care pursue a thing
Impossible there is nothing.        537
8. pukaḻntavi pōṟṟic ceyalvēṇṭum ceyyātu
ikaḻntārkku eḻumiyum il.
Do what the wise commend as worth
If not, for seven births no mirth.        538
9. ikaḻcciyiṉ keṭṭāri uḷḷuka tāntam
makiḻcciyiṉ mintuṟum pōḻtu.
When joy deludes, their fate recall
Whom negligence has made to fall.        539
10. uḷḷiyatu eytal eḷitumaṉ maṟṟuntāṉ
uḷḷiyatu uḷḷap peṟiṉ.
Easy it is a thing to get
When the mind on it is set.        540

55. ceṅkōṉmi - Just government

1. ōrntukaṇ ṇōṭātu iṟipurintu yārmāṭṭum
tērntucey vaḵtē muṟi.
Test and attest impartially
Consult and act the laws justly.        541
2. vāṉōkki vāḻum ulakellām maṉṉavaṉ
kōlnōkki vāḻum kuṭi.
The earth looks up to sky and thrives
And mankind to king's rod of justice.        542
3. antaṇar nūṟkum aṟattiṟkum ātiyāy
niṉṟatu maṉṉavaṉ kōl.
The Sage's scripture and virtue spring
From the sceptre of a stately king.        543
4. kuṭitaḻīik kōlōccum mānila maṉṉaṉ
aṭitaḻīi niṟkum ulaku.
The world clings to the ruler's feet
Whose sceptre clasps the people's heart.        544
5. iyalpuḷik kōlōccum maṉṉavaṉ nāṭṭa
peyalum viḷiyuḷum tokku
Full rins and yields enrich the land
Which is ruled by a righteous hand.        545
6. vēlaṉṟu veṉṟi taruvatu maṉṉavaṉ
kōlatūum kōṭā teṉiṉ
Not the spear but the sceptre stright
That brings success to monarch's might.        546
7. iṟikākkum viyaka mellām avaṉi
muṟikākkum muṭṭāc ceyiṉ.
The king protects the entire earth
And justice protects his royal worth.        547
8. eṇpatattāṉ ōrā muṟiceyyā maṉṉavaṉ
taṇpatattāṉ tāṉē keṭum.
Hard of access, the unjust king
He shall himself his ruin bring.        548
9. kuṭipuṟaṅ kāttōmpik kuṟṟam kaṭital
vaṭuvaṉṟu vēntaṉ toḻil.
Save his subjects and chide the wrong
Is flawless duty of a king.        549
10. koliyiṟ koṭiyāri vēntoṟuttal piṅkūḻ
kaḷikaṭ ṭataṉoṭu nēr.
Killing killers, the king, behold
Weeds removes from cropful field.        550

56. koṭuṅkōṉmi - The cruel tyranny

1. kolimēṟkoṇ ṭāriṟ koṭitē alimēṟkoṇṭu
allavi ceytuoḻukum vēntu.
The unjust tyrant oppressor
Is worse than cruel murderer.        551
2. vēloṭu niṉṟāṉ iṭueṉ ṟatupōlum
kōloṭu niṉṟāṉ iravu.
Sceptered tyrant exacting gold
Is "give" of lanced robber bold.        552
3. nāṭoṟum nāṭi muṟiceyyā maṉṉavaṉ
nāṭoṟum nāṭu keṭum.
Spy wrongs dily and do justice
Or day by day the realm decays.        553
4. kūḻum kuṭiyum oruṅkiḻakkum kōlkōṭic
cūḻātu ceyyum aracu.
The king shall wealth and subjects lose
If his sceptre he dares abuse.        554
5. allaṟpaṭṭu āṟṟātu aḻutakaṇ ṇīraṉṟē
celvattit tēykkum paṭi.
Groaning tears cused by tyrant's sway
File the royal wealth away.        555
6. maṉṉarkku maṉṉutal ceṅkōṉmi aḵtiṉṟēl
maṉṉāvām maṉṉark koḷi.
Glory endures by sceptre right
Without it wanes the royal light.        556
7. tuḷiyiṉmi ñālattiṟku eṟṟaṟṟē vēntaṉ
aḷiyiṉmi vāḻum uyirkku.
Dry like the earth without rinfall
Is graceless king to creatures all.        557
8. iṉmiyiṉ iṉṉātu uṭimi muṟiceyyā
maṉṉavaṉ kōṟkīḻp paṭiṉ.
To have is worse than having not
If ruler is unjust despot.        558
9. muṟikōṭi maṉṉavaṉ ceyyiṉ uṟikōṭi
ollātu vāṉam peyal.
The sky withdraws season's shower
If the king misuses his power.        559
10. āpayaṉ kuṉṟum aṟutoḻilōr nūlmaṟappar
kāvalaṉ kāvāṉ eṉiṉ.
The *six-functioned forget their lore
Cows give less if kings guard no more.         560
---
* the six functions are : learning, teaching, giving,
getting, sacrificing, kindling sacrifice - These are duties of Vedic
savants.

57. veruvanta ceyyāmi - Avoiding terrorism

1. takkāṅku nāṭit taliccellā vaṇṇattāl
ottāṅku oṟuppatu vēntu.
A king enquires and gives sentence
Just to prevent future offence.        561
2. kaṭitōcci mella eṟika neṭitākkam
nīṅkāmi vēṇṭu pavar.
Wield fast the rod but gently lay
This strict mildness prolongs the sway.        562
3. veruvanta ceytuoḻukum veṅkōla ṉāyiṉ
oruvantam ollik keṭum.
His cruel rod of dreadful deed
Brings king's ruin quick indeed.        563
4. iṟikaṭiyaṉ eṉṟurikkum iṉṉāccol vēntaṉ
uṟikaṭuki ollik keṭum.
As men the king a tyrant call
His days dwindled, hasten his fall.        564
5. aruñcevvi iṉṉā mukattāṉ peruñcelvam
pēeykaṇ ṭaṉṉatu uṭittu.
Whose sight is scarce, whose face is foul
His wealth seems watched by a ghoul.        565
6. kaṭuñcollaṉ kaṇṇila ṉāyiṉ neṭuñcelvam
nīṭiṉṟi āṅkē keṭum.
Whose word is harsh, whose sight is rude
His wealth and power quickly fade.        566
7. kaṭumoḻiyum kiyikanta taṇṭamum vēntaṉ
aṭumuraṇ tēykkum aram.
Reproofs rough and punishments rude
Like files conquering power corrode.        567
8. iṉattāṟṟi eṇṇāta vēntaṉ ciṉattāṟṟic
cīṟiṉ ciṟukum tiru
The king who would not take counsels
Rages with wrath-his fortune fails.        568
9. ceruvanta pōḻtiṟ ciṟiceyyā vēntaṉ
veruvantu veytu keṭum.
The king who builds not fort betimes
Fears his foes in wars and dies.        569
10. kallārp piṇikkum kaṭuṅkōl atuvallatu
illi nilakkup poṟi.
The crushing burden borne by earth
Is tyrants bound to fools uncouth.        570

58. kaṇṇōṭṭam - Benign looks

1. kaṇṇōṭṭam eṉṉum kaḻiperuṅ kāriki
uṇmiyāṉ uṇṭiv vulaku.
Living in the world implies
The bounteous dame of benign eyes.        571
2. kaṇṇōṭṭat tuḷḷatu ulakiyal aḵtilār
uṇmi nilakkup poṟi.
World lives by looks of lovely worth
Who lack them are burdens of earth.        572
3. paṇeṉṉām pāṭaṟku iyipiṉṟēl kaṇeṉṉām
kaṇṇōṭṭam illāta kaṇ
Of tuneless song what is the use?
Without gracious looks what are eyes?        573
4. uḷapōl mukattevaṉ ceyyum aḷaviṉāl
kaṇṇēṭṭam illāta kaṇ.
Except that they are on the face
What for are eyes sans measured grace.        574
5. kaṇṇiṟku aṇikalam kaṇṇōṭṭam aḵtiṉṟēl
puṇṇeṉṟu uṇarap paṭum.
Kind looks are jewels for eyes to wear
Without them they are felt as sore.        575
6. maṇṇō ṭiyinta marattaṉiyar kaṇṇō
ṭiyintukaṇ ṇōṭā tavar.
Like trees on inert earth they grow
Who don't eye to eye kindness show.        576
7. kaṇṇōṭṭam illavar kaṇṇilar kaṇṇuṭiyār
kaṇṇōṭṭam iṉmiyum il.
Ungracious men lack real eyes
Men of real eyes show benign grace.        577
8. karumañ citiyāmal kaṇṇōṭa vallārkku
urimi uṭittiv vulaku.
Who gracious are but dutiful
Have right for this earth beautiful.        578
9. oṟuttāṟṟum paṇpiṉār kaṇṇumkaṇ ṇōṭip
poṟuttāṟṟum paṇpē tali.
To be benign and bear with foes
Who vex us is true virtue's phase.        579
10. peyakkaṇṭum nañcuṇ ṭamivar nayattakka
nākarikam vēṇṭu pavar.
Men of graceful courtesy
Take hemlock and look cheerfully.        580

59. oṟṟāṭal - Espionage

1. oṟṟum uricāṉṟa nūlum iviyiraṇṭum
teṟṟeṉka maṉṉavaṉ kaṇ.
A king should treat these two as eyes
The code of laws and careful spies.        581
2. ellārkkum ellām nikaḻpavi eññāṉṟum
vallaṟital vēntaṉ toḻil.
All that happens, always, to all
The king should know in full detil.        582
3. oṟṟiṉāṉ oṟṟi poruḷteriyā maṉṉavaṉ
koṟṟaṅ koḷakkiṭantatu il.
Conquests are not for the monarch
Who cares not for the Spy's remark.        583
4. viṉiceyvār tamcuṟṟam vēṇṭātār eṉṟāṅku
aṉivariyum ārāyvatu oṟṟu.
His officers, kinsmen and foes
Who watch keenly are worthy spies.        584
5. kaṭāa uruvoṭu kaṇṇañcātu yāṇṭum
ukāami vallatē oṟṟu.
Fearless gaze, suspectless guise
Guarding secrets mark the spies.        585
6. tuṟantār paṭivatta rāki iṟantārāyntu
eṉceyiṉum cōrvilatu oṟṟu.
Guised as monks they gather secrets
They betray them not under threats.        586
7. maṟintavi kēṭkavaṟ ṟāki aṟintavi
aiyappāṭu illatē oṟṟu.
A spy draws out other's secrets
Beyond a doubt he clears his facts.        587
8. oṟṟoṟṟit tanta poruḷiyum maṟṟumōr
oṟṟiṉāl oṟṟik koḷal.
The reports given by one spy
By another spy verify.        588
9. oṟṟoṟ ṟuṇarāmi āḷka uṭaṉmūvar
coltokka tēṟap paṭum.
Engage the spies alone, apart
When three agree confirm report.        589
10. ciṟappaṟiya oṟṟiṉkaṇ ceyyaṟka ceyyiṉ
puṟappaṭuttā ṉākum maṟi
Give not the spy open reward
It would divulge the secret heard!        590

60. ūkkamuṭimi - Energy

1. uṭiya reṉappaṭuvatu ūkkam aḵtillār
uṭiyatu uṭiyarō maṟṟu.
To own is to own energy
All others own but lethargy.        591
2. uḷḷam uṭimi uṭimi poruḷuṭimi
nillātu nīṅki viṭum.
Psychic heart is wealth indeed
Worldly wealth departs in speed.        592
3. ākkam iḻantēmeṉṟu allāvār ūkkam
oruvantam kittuṭi yār.
he strong in will do not complin
The loss of worldly wealth and gin.        593
4. ākkam atarviṉāyc cellum acivilā
ūkk muṭiyā ṉuḻi.
Fortune enquires, enters with boom
Where tireless strivers have their home.        594
5. veḷḷat taṉiya malarnīṭṭam māntartam
uḷḷat taṉiyatu uyarvu.
Water depth is lotus height
Mental strength is men's merit.        595
6. uḷḷuva tellām uyarvuḷḷal maṟṟatu
taḷḷiṉum taḷḷāmi nīrttu.
Let thoughts be always great and grand
Though they fail their virtues stand.        596
7. citiviṭattu olkār uravōr putiyampiṟ
paṭṭuppā ṭūṉṟum kaḷiṟu.
Elephants are firm when arrows hit
Great minds keep fit ev'n in defeat.        597
8. uḷḷam ilātavar eytār ulakattu
vaḷḷiyam eṉṉum cerukku.
Heartless persons cannot boast
"We are liberal to our best".        598
9. pariyatu kūrṅkōṭṭatu āyiṉum yāṉi
verūum pulitāk kuṟiṉ.
Huge elephant sharp in tusk quails
When tiger, less in form, assils.        599
10. uramoruvaṟku uḷḷa veṟukki aḵtillār
marammakka ḷātalē vēṟu.
Mental courage is true manhood
Lacking that man is like a wood        600

61. maṭiyiṉmi - Freedom from sloth

1. kuṭiyeṉṉum kuṉṟā viḷakkam maṭiyeṉṉum
mācūra māyntu keṭum.
Quenchless lamp of ancestry goes
When foul idleness encloses.        601
2. maṭiyi maṭiyā oḻukal kuṭiyik
kuṭiyāka vēṇṭu pavar.
To make your home an ideal home
Loath sloth as sloth; refuse it room.        602
3. maṭimaṭik koṇṭoḻukum pēti piṟanta
kuṭimaṭiyum taṉṉiṉum muntu.
The fool who fosters sluggishness
Before he dies ruins his house.        603
4. kuṭimaṭintu kuṟṟam perukum maṭimaṭintu
māṇṭa uñaṟṟila varkku.
Who strive not high, sunk deep in sloth
Ruin their house by evil growth.        604
5. neṭunīr maṟavi maṭituyil nāṉkum
keṭunīrār kāmak kalaṉ.
To lag, forget, idle and doze
These four are pleasure boats of loss.        605
6. paṭiyuṭiyār paṟṟamintak kaṇṇum maṭiyuṭiyār
māṇpayaṉ eytal aritu.
With all the wealth of lords of earth
The slothful gin nothing of worth.        606
7. iṭipurintu eḷḷuñcol kēṭpar maṭipurintu
māṇṭa uñaṟṟi lavar.
The slothful lacking noble deeds
Subject themselves to scornful words.        607
8. maṭimi kuṭimikkaṇ taṅkiṉtaṉ oṉṉārkku
aṭimi pukutti viṭum.
If sloth invades a noble house
It will become a slave of foes.        608
9. kuṭiyāṇmi yuḷvanta kuṟṟam oruvaṉ
maṭiyāṇmi māṟṟak keṭum.
The blots on race and rule shall cease
When one from sloth gets his release.        609
10. maṭiyilā maṉṉavaṉ eytum aṭiyaḷantāṉ
tāaya tellām oruṅku.
The slothless king shall gin en masse
*All regions trod by Lord apace.        610
*Hindu mythology holds that Lord Vishnu
measured with his feet the three worlds.

62. āḷviṉiyuṭimi - Manly effort

1. arumi uṭitteṉṟu acāvāmi vēṇṭum
perumi muyaṟci tarum.
Feel not frustrate saying "Tis hard".
Who tries attins striving's reward.        611
2. viṉikkaṇ viṉikeṭal ōmpal viṉikkuṟi
tīrntāriṉ tīrntaṉṟu ulaku.
In doing work don't break and shirk
The world will quit who quits his work.        612
3. tāḷāṇmi eṉṉum takimikkaṇ taṅkiṟṟē
vēḷāṇmi eṉṉuñ cerukku.
On excellence of industry
Depends magnanimous bounty.        613
4. tāḷāṇmi illātāṉ vēḷāṇmi pēṭiki
vāḷāṇmi pōlak keṭum.
Bounty of man who never strives
Like sword in eunuch's hand it fails.        614
5. iṉpam viḻiyāṉ viṉiviḻivāṉ taṉkēḷir
tuṉpam tuṭittūṉṟum tūṇ.
Work who likes and not pleasure
Wipes grief of friends, pillar secure.        615
6. muyaṟci tiruviṉi yākkum muyaṟṟiṉmi
iṉmi pukutti viṭum.
Industry adds prosperity
Indolence brings but poverty.        616
7. maṭiyuḷāḷ māmukaṭi eṉpa maṭiyilāṉ
tāḷuḷāḷ tāmariyi ṉāḷ.
Illuck abides with sloth they say
*Laxmi's gifts with labourers stay.        617
8. poṟiyiṉmi yārkkum paḻiyaṉṟu aṟivaṟintu
āḷviṉi iṉmi paḻi.
Misfortune is disgrace to none
The shame is nothing learnt or done.        618
9. teyvattāṉ ākātu eṉiṉum muyaṟcitaṉ
meyvaruttak kūli tarum.
Though fate is aginst fulfilment
Hard labour has ready payment.        619
10. ūḻiyum uppakkam kāṇpar uliviṉṟit
tāḻātu uñaṟṟu pavar
Tireless Toiler's striving hand
Shall leave even the fate behind.        620
*Laxmi the Goddes of wealth and prosperity.

63. iṭukkaṇaḻiyāmi - Hope in mishap

1. iṭukkaṇ varuṅkāl nakuka ataṉi
aṭuttūrvatu aḵtoppatu il.
Laugh away troubles; there is
No other way to conquer woes.        621
2. veḷḷat taṉiya iṭumpi aṟivuṭiyāṉ
uḷḷattiṉ uḷḷak keṭum.
Deluging sorrows come to nought
When wise men face them with firm thought.        622
3. iṭumpikku iṭumpi paṭuppar iṭumpikku
iṭumpi paṭāa tavar.
Grief they face and put to grief
Who grieve not grief by mind's relief.        623
4. maṭuttavā yellām pakaṭaṉṉāṉ uṟṟa
iṭukkaṇ iṭarppāṭu uṭittu.
Who pulls like bulls patiently on
Causes grief to grieve anon.        624
5. aṭukki variṉum aḻivilāṉ uṟṟa
iṭukkaṇ iṭukkaṭ paṭum.
Before the brave grief grieves and goes
Who dare a host of pressing woes.        625
6. aṟṟēmeṉṟu allaṟpaṭupavō peṟṟēmeṉṟu
ōmputal tēṟṟā tavar
The wise that never gloat in gin
Do not fret in fateful ruin.        626
7. ilakkam uṭampiṭumpik keṉṟu kalakkattik
kiyāṟāk koḷḷātām mēl.
The wise worry no more of woes
Knowing body's butt of sorrows.        627
8. iṉpam viḻiyāṉ iṭumpi iyalpeṉpāṉ
tuṉpam uṟutal ilaṉ.
Who seek not joy, deem grief norm
By sorrows do not come to harm.        628
9. iṉpattuḷ iṉpam viḻiyātāṉ tuṉpattuḷ
tuṉpam uṟutal ilaṉ.
In joy to joy who is not bound
In grief he grieves not dual round!        629
10. iṉṉāmi iṉpam eṉakkoḷiṉ ākuntaṉ
oṉṉār viḻiyuñ ciṟappu.
His glory is esteemed by foes
Who sees weal in wanton woes!        630

2.2 amicciyal - STATE CABINET
64. amiccu - Ministers

1. karuviyum kālamum ceykiyum ceyyum
aruviṉiyum māṇṭatu amiccu.
He is minister who chooses
Right means, time, mode and rare ventures.        631
2. vaṉkaṇ kuṭikāttal kaṟṟaṟital āḷviṉiyōṭu
aintuṭaṉ māṇṭatu amiccu.
With these he guards people, - by his
Knowledge, firmness and manliness.        632
3. pirittalum pēṇik koḷalum pirintārp
poruttalum vallatu amiccu.
A minister cherishes friends
Divides foes and the parted blends.        633
4. teritalum tērntu ceyalum orutaliyāc
collalum vallatu amiccu.
A minister must sift reflect
Select and say surely one fact.        634
5. aṟaṉaṟintu āṉṟaminta collāṉeñ ñāṉṟum
tiṟaṉaṟintāṉ tērccit tuṇi.
Have him for help who virtue knows
Right wisdom speaks, ever apt in acts.        635
6. matinuṭpam nūlōṭu uṭiyārkku atinuṭpam
yāuḷa muṉṉiṟ pavi.
Which subtler brin can stand before
The keen in brin with learned love?        636
7. ceyaṟki aṟintak kaṭittum ulakattu
iyaṟki aṟintu ceyal.
Albeit you know to act from books
Act after knowing world's outlooks.        637
8. aṟikoṉṟu aṟiyāṉ eṉiṉum uṟuti
uḻiyiruntāṉ kūṟal kaṭaṉ.
The man in place must tell the facts
Though the ignorant king refutes.        638
9. paḻuteṇṇum mantiriyiṉ pakkattuḷ tevvōr
eḻupatu kōṭi uṟum.
Seventy crores of foes are better
Than a minister with mind bitter.        639
10. muṟippaṭac cūḻntum muṭivilavē ceyvar
tiṟappāṭu ilāa tavar.
The unresolved, though well designed
To fulfil an act they have no mind.        640

65. colvaṉmi - Power of speech

1. nānalam eṉṉum nalaṉuṭimi annalam
yānalattu uḷḷatūum aṉṟu.
The goodness called goodness of speech
Is goodness which nothing can reach.        641
2. ākkamuṅ kēṭum ataṉāl varutalāl
kāttōmpal colliṉkaṇ cōrvu.
Since gin or ruin speeches bring
Guard aginst the slips of tongue.        642
3. kēṭṭārp piṇikkun takiyavāyk kōḷārum
vēṭpa moḻivatāñ col.
A speech is speech that holds ears
And attracts ev'n those that are averse.        643
4. tiṟaṉaṟintu colluka colli aṟaṉum
poruḷum ataṉiṉūuṅku il.
Weigh thy words and speak; becuse
No wealth or virtue words surpass.        644
5. colluka collip piṟitōrcol accolli
velluñcol iṉmi aṟintu
Speak out thy world so that no word
Can win it and say untoward.        645
6. vēṭpattāñ collip piṟarcol payaṉkōṭal
māṭciyiṉ mācaṟṟār kōḷ.
Spotless men speak what is sweet
And grasp in others what is meet.        646
7. colalvallaṉ cōrvilaṉ añcāṉ avaṉi
ikalvellal yārkkum aritu.
No foe defies the speaker clear
Flawless, puissant, and free from fear.        647
8. virintu toḻilkēṭkum ñālam nirantiṉitu
collutal vallārp peṟiṉ.
The world will quickly carry out
The words of counsellors astute.        648
9. palacollak kāmuṟuvar maṉṟamā caṟṟa
cilacollal tēṟṟā tavar.
They overspeak who do not seek
A few and flawless words to speak.        649
10. iṇarūḻttum nāṟā malaraṉiyar kaṟṟatu
uṇara viritturiyā tār.
Who can't express what they have learnt
Are bunch of flowers not fragrant.        650
--------

66. viṉittuymi - Purity of action

1. tuṇinalam ākkam tarūum viṉinalam
vēṇṭiya ellām tarum.
Friendship brings gin; but action pure
Does every good thing we desire.        651
2. eṉṟum oruvutal vēṇṭum pukaḻoṭu
naṉṟi payavā viṉi.
Eschew always acts that do not
Bring good nor glory on their part.        652
3. ōotal vēṇṭum oḷimāḻkum ceyviṉi
āatum eṉṉu mavar.
Those in the world desire for fame
Should shun the deed that dims their name.        653
4. iṭukkaṇ paṭiṉum iḷivanta ceyyār
naṭukkaṟṟa kāṭci yavar.
Though perils press the faultless wise
Shun deeds of mean, shameful device.        654
5. eṟṟeṉṟu iraṅkuva ceyyaṟka ceyvāṉēl
maṟṟaṉṉa ceyyāmi naṉṟu.
Do not wrong act and grieve, "Alas"
If done, do not repeat it twice.        655
6. īṉṟāṉ pacikāṇpāṉ āyiṉuñ ceyyaṟka
cāṉṟōr paḻikkum viṉi.
Though she who begot thee hungers
Shun acts denounced by ancient seers.        656
7. paḻimalintu eytiya ākkattiṉ cāṉṟōr
kaḻinal kuravē tali.
Pinching poverty of the wise
Is more than wealth hoarded by Vice.        657
8. kaṭinta kaṭintorār ceytārkku avitām
muṭintālum pīḻi tarum.
Those who dare a forbidden deed
Suffer troubles though they succeed.        658
9. aḻakkoṇṭa ellām aḻappōm iḻappiṉum
piṟpayakkum naṟpā lavi.
Gains from weeping, weeping go
Though lost, from good deeds blessings flow.        659
10. calattāl poruḷceytē mārttal pacumaṭ
kalattuḷnīr peytirīi yaṟṟu
The wealth gathered in guilty ways
Is water poured in wet clay vase.        660
--------

67. viṉittiṭpam - Powerful acts

1. viṉittiṭpam eṉpatu oruvaṉ maṉattiṭpam
maṟṟiya ellām piṟa.
A powerful mind does powerful act
And all the rest are imperfect.        661
2. ūṟorāv uṟṟapiṉ olkāmi ivviraṇṭiṉ
āṟeṉpar āyntavar kōḷ.
Shun failing fuss; fail not purpose
These two are maxims of the wise.        662
3. kaṭikoṭkac ceytakka tāṇmi iṭikkoṭkiṉ
eṟṟā viḻuman tarum.
The strong achieve and then display
Woe unto work displayed midway.        663
4. collutal yārkkum eḷiya ariyavām
colliya vaṇṇam ceyal.
Easy it is to tell a fact
But hard it is to know and act.        664
5. vīṟeyti māṇṭār viṉitiṭpam vēntaṉkaṇ
ūṟeyti uḷḷap paṭum.
Dynamic deeds of a doughty soul
Shall win the prise of king and all.        665
6. eṇṇiya eṇṇiyāṅku eytupa eṇṇiyār
tiṇṇiya rākap peṟiṉ.
The will-to-do achieves the deed
When mind that wills is strong indeed.        666
7. uruvukaṇṭu eḷḷāmi vēṇṭum uruḷperuntērkku
accāṇi aṉṉār uṭittu.
Scorn not the form: for men there are
Like linchpin of big rolling car.        667
8. kalaṅkātu kaṇṭa viṉikkaṇ tuḷaṅkātu
tūkkam kaṭintu ceyal.
Waver not; do wakefully
The deed resolved purposefully.        668
9. tuṉpam uṟavariṉum ceyka tuṇivāṟṟi
iṉpam payakkum viṉi.
Do with firm will though pins beset
The deed that brings delight at last.        669
10. eṉittiṭpam eytiyak kaṇṇum viṉittiṭpam
vēṇṭāri vēṇṭātu ulaku.
The world merits no other strength
But strength of will-to-do at length.        670
-------

68. viṉiceyalvaki - Modes of action

1. cūḻcci muṭivu tuṇiveytal attuṇivu
tāḻcciyuḷ taṅkutal tītu.
When counsel takes a resolve strong
Weak delay of action is wrong.        671
2. tūṅkuka tūṅkic ceyaṟpāla tūṅkaṟka
tūṅkātu ceyyum viṉi.
Delay such acts as need delay
Delay not acts that need display.        672
3. ollumvā yellām viṉinaṉṟē ollākkāl
cellumvāy nōkkic ceyal.
It's best to act when feasible
If not see what is possible.        673
4. viṉipaki eṉṟiraṇṭiṉ eccam niṉiyuṅkāl
tīyeccam pōlat teṟum.
Work or foe left unfinished
Flare up like fire unextinguished.        674
5. poruḷkaruvi kālam viṉiyiṭaṉoṭu aintum
iruḷtīra eṇṇic ceyal.
Money and means, time, place and deed
Decide these five and then proceed.        675
6. muṭivum iṭiyūṟum muṟṟiyāṅku eytum
paṭupayaṉum pārttuc ceyal.
Weigh well the end, hindrance, profit
And then pursue a fitting act.        676
7. ceyviṉi ceyvāṉ ceyalmuṟi avviṉi
uḷḷaṟivāṉ uḷḷam koḷal.
Know first the secret from experts
That is the way of fruitful acts.        677
8. viṉiyāl viṉiyākkik kōṭal naṉikavuḷ
yāṉiyāl yāṉiyāt taṟṟu.
Lure a tusker by a tusker
Achieve a deed by deed better.        678
9. naṭṭārkku nalla ceyaliṉ virintatē
oṭṭāri oṭṭik koḷal.
Than doing good to friends it is
More urgent to befriend the foes.        679
10. uṟiciṟiyār uḷnaṭuṅkal añcik kuṟipeṟiṉ
koḷvar periyārp paṇintu.
Small statesmen fearing people's fear
Submit to foes superior.        680
--------

69. tūtu - The embassy

1. aṉpuṭimi āṉṟa kuṭippiṟattal vēntavām
paṇpuṭimi tūturippāṉ paṇpu.
Love, noble birth, good courtesy
Pleasing kings mark true embassy.        681
2. aṉpaṟivu ārāynta colvaṉmi tūturippārkku
iṉṟi yamiyāta mūṉṟu.
Envoys must bear love for their prince
Knowledge and learned eloquence.        682
3. nūlāruḷ nūlvallaṉ ākutal vēlāruḷ
veṉṟi viṉiyurippāṉ paṇpu.
Savant among savants, he pleads
Before lanced king, triumphant words.        683
4. aṟivuru ārāynta kalviim mūṉṟaṉ
ceṟivuṭiyāṉ celka viṉikku.
Who has these three: good form, sense, lore
Can act as bold ambassador.        684
5. tokaccollit tūvāta nīkki nakaccolli
naṉṟi payappatām tūtu.
Not harsh, the envoy's winsome ways
Does good by pleasant words concise.        685
6. kaṟṟukkaṇ añcāṉ celaccollik kālattāl
takkatu aṟivatām tūtu.
Learned; fearless, the envoy tends
Convincing words which time demands.        686
7. kaṭaṉaṟintu kālam karuti iṭaṉaṟintu
eṇṇi urippāṉ tali.
Knowing duty time and place
The envoy employs mature phrase.        687
8. tūymi tuṇimi tuṇivuṭimi immūṉṟiṉ
vāymi vaḻiyurippāṉ paṇpu.
The true envoy of three virtues
Is pure helpful and bold in views.        688
9. viṭumāṟṟam vēntarkku urippāṉ vaṭumāṟṟam -
vāycōrā vaṉka ṇavaṉ.
The envoy who ports the king's message
Has flawless words and heart's courage.        689
10. iṟuti payappiṉum eñcātu iṟivarkku
uṟuti payappatām tūtu.
Braving death the bold envoy
Assures his king's safety and joy.        690
---------

70. maṉṉaric cērntoḻukal - Walk with kings

1. akalātu aṇukātu tīkkāyvār pōlka
ikalvēntarc cērntoḻuku vār.
Move with hostile kings as with fire
Not coming close nor going far.        691
2. maṉṉar viḻipa viḻiyāmi maṉṉarāl
maṉṉiya ākkam tarum.
Crave not for things which kings desire
This brings thee their fruitful favour.        692
3. pōṟṟiṉ ariyavi pōṟṟal kaṭuttapiṉ
tēṟṟutal yārkkum aritu.
Guard thyself from petty excess
Suspected least, there's no redress.        693
4. ceviccollum cērnta nakiyum avittoḻukal
āṉṟa periyā rakattu.
Whisper not; nor smile exchange
Amidst august men's assemblage.        694
5. epporuḷum ōrār toṭarārmaṟ ṟapporuḷi
viṭṭakkāl kēṭka maṟi.
Hear not, ask not the king's secret
Hear only when he lets it out.        695
6. kuṟippaṟintu kālam karuti veṟuppil
vēṇṭupa vēṭpac colal.
Discern his mood and time and tell
No dislikes but what king likes well.        696
7. vēṭpaṉa colli viṉiyila eññāṉṟum
kēṭpiṉum collā viṭal.
Tell pleasing things; and never tell
Even if pressed what is futile.        697
8. iḷiyar iṉamuṟiyar eṉṟikaḻār niṉṟa
oḷiyōṭu oḻukap paṭum.
As young and kinsman do not slight;
Look with awe king's light and might.        698
9. koḷappaṭṭēm eṉṟeṇṇik koḷḷāta ceyyār
tuḷakkaṟṟa kāṭci yavar.
The clear-visioned do nothing base
Deeming they have the monarch's grace.        699
10. paḻiyam eṉakkarutip paṇpalla ceyyum
keḻutakimi kēṭu tarum.
Worthless acts based on friendship old
Shall spell ruin and woe untold.        700
------------

71. kuṟippaṟital - Divining the mind

1. kūṟāmi nōkkik kuṟippaṟivāṉ eññāṉṟum
māṟānīr viyakku aṇi
Who reads the mind by look, untold
Adorns the changeless sea-girt world.        701
2. aiyap paṭāatu akattatu uṇarvāṉit
teyvattō ṭoppak koḷal.
Take him as God who reads the thought
Of another man without a doubt.        702
3. kuṟippiṟ kuṟippuṇar vāri uṟuppiṉuḷ
yātu koṭuttum koḷal.
By sign who scans the sign admit
At any cost in cabinet.        703
4. kuṟittatu kūṟāmik koḷvārō ṭēṉi
uṟuppō raṉiyarāl vēṟu.
Untold, he who divines the thought
Though same in form is quite apart.        704
5. kuṟippiṟ kuṟippuṇarā vāyiṉ uṟuppiṉuḷ
eṉṉa payattavō kaṇ.
Among senses what for is eye
If thought by thought one can't descry?        705
6. aṭuttatu kāṭṭum paḷiṅkupōl neñcam
kaṭuttatu kāṭṭum mukam.
What throbs in mind the face reflects
Just as mirror nearby objects.        706
7. mukattiṉ mutukkuṟintatu uṇṭō uvappiṉum
kāyiṉum tāṉmun tuṟum.
Than face what is subtler to tell
First if the mind feels well or ill.        707
8. mukamnōkki niṟka amiyum akamnōkki
uṟṟa tuṇarvārp peṟiṉ.
Just standing in front would suffice
For those who read the mind on face.        708
9. pakimiyum kēṇmiyum kaṇṇurikkum kaṇṇiṉ
vakimi uṇarvārp peṟiṉ.
Friend or foe the eyes will show
To those who changing outlooks know.        709
10. nuṇṇiyam eṉpār aḷakkumkōl kāṇumkāl
kaṇṇallatu illi piṟa.
The scale of keen discerning minds
Is eye and eye that secrets finds.        710
----------

72. aviyaṟital - Judging the audience

1. aviyaṟintu ārāyntu colluka colliṉ
tokiyaṟinta tūymi yavar.
The pure in thought and eloquence
Adapt their words to audience.        711
2. iṭiterintu naṉkuṇarntu colluka colliṉ
naṭiterinta naṉmi yavar.
Who know the art of speech shall suit
Their chosen words to time in fact.        712
3. aviyaṟiyār collalmēṟ koḷpavar colliṉ
vakiyaṟiyār vallatūum il.
They speak in vin at length who talk
Words unversed which ears don't take.        713
4. ōḷiyārmuṉ oḷḷiya rātal veḷiyārmuṉ
vāṉcuti vaṇṇam koḷal.
Before the bright be brilliant light
Before the muff be mortar white.        714
5. naṉṟeṉṟa vaṟṟuḷḷum naṉṟē mutuvaruḷ
muntu kiḷavāc ceṟivu.
Modest restrint all good excels
Which argues not before elders.        715
6. āṟṟiṉ nilitaḷarn taṟṟē viyaṉpulam
ēṟṟuṇarvār muṉṉar iḻukku
Tongue-slip before the talented wise
Is like slipping from righteous ways.        716
7. kaṟṟaṟintār kalvi viḷaṅkum kacaṭaṟac
colterital vallā rakattu.
The learning of the learned shines
Valued by flawless scholar-minds.        717
8. uṇarva tuṭiyārmuṉ collal vaḷarvataṉ
pāttiyuḷ nīrcorin taṟṟu.
To address understanding ones
Is to water beds of growing grins.        718
9. pullaviyuḷ poccāntum collaṟka nallaviyuḷ
naṉku celaccollu vār.
O ye who speak before the keen
Forgetful, address not the mean.        719
10. aṅkaṇattuḷ ukka amiḻtaṟṟāl tamkaṇattār
allārmuṉ kōṭṭi koḷal.
To hostiles who wise words utters
Pours ambrosia into gutters.        720
--------

73. aviyañcāmi - Courage before councils

1. vakiyaṟintu vallavi vāycōrār colliṉ
tokiyaṟinta tūymi yavar
The pure fail not in power of words
Knowing grand council's moods and modes.        721
2. kaṟṟāruḷ kaṟṟār eṉappaṭuvar kaṟṟārmuṉ
kaṟṟa celaccollu vār
Among scholars he is scholar
Who holds scholars with learned lore.        722
3. pakiyakattuc cāvār eḷiyar ariyar
aviyakattu añcā tavar.
Many brave foes and die in fields
The fearless few face wise councils.        723
4. kaṟṟārmuṉ kaṟṟa celaccollit tāmkaṟṟa
mikkāruḷ mikka koḷal.
Impress the learned with your lore
From greater savants learn still more.        724
5. āṟṟiṉ aḷavaṟintu kaṟka aviyañcā
māṟṟam koṭuttaṟ poruṭṭu
Grammar and logic learn so that
Foes you can boldly retort.        725
6. vāḷoṭeṉ vaṉkaṇṇar allārkku nūloṭeṉ
nuṇṇavi añcu pavarkku.
To cowards what can sword avil
And books to those who councils fail?        726
7. pakiyakattup pēṭiki oḷvāḷ aviyakattu
añcu mavaṉkaṟṟa nūl.
Like eunuch's sword in field, is vin
His lore who fears men of brin.        727
8. pallavi kaṟṟum payamilarē nallaviyuḷ
naṉku celaccollā tār.
Though learned much his lore is dead
Who says no good before the good.        728
9. kallā tavariṉ kaṭiyeṉpa kaṟṟaṟintum
nallār aviyañcu vār.
Who fear to face good assembly
Are learned idiots, certinly.        729
10. uḷareṉiṉum illāroṭu oppar kaḷaṉañcik
kaṟṟa celaccollā tār.
They are breathing dead who dare not
Empress before the wise their art.        730

2.3 araṇiyal, kūḻiyal, paṭiyiyal – POLITICS 74. nāṭu - The country

1. taḷḷā viḷiyuḷum takkārum tāḻvilāc
celvarum cērvatu nāṭu.
It's country which has souls of worth
Unfailing yields and ample wealth.        731
2. perumporuḷāl peṭṭakka tāki aruṅkēṭṭāl
āṟṟa viḷivatu nāṭu.
The Land has large luring treasure
Where pests are nil and yields are sure.        732
3. poṟiyoruṅku mēlvaruṅkāl tāṅki iṟivaṟku
iṟiyoruṅku nērvatu nāṭu.
It's land that bears pressing burdens
And pays its tax which king demands.        733
4. uṟupaciyum ōvāp piṇiyum ceṟupakiyum
cērā tiyalvatu nāṭu
It is country which is free from
Fierce famine, plague and foemen's harm.        734
5. palkuḻuvum pāḻceyyum uṭpakiyum vēntalikkum
kolkuṟumpum illatu nāṭu.
Sects and ruinous foes are nil
No tritors in a land tranquil.        735
6. kēṭaṟiyāk keṭṭa viṭattum vaḷaṅkuṉṟā
nāṭeṉpa nāṭṭiṉ tali
The land of lands no ruin knows
Even in grief its wealth yet grows.        736
7. irupuṉalum vāynta maliyum varupuṉalum
vallaraṇum nāṭṭiṟku uṟuppu.
Waters up and down, hills and streams
With strong forts as limbs country beams.        737
8. piṇiyiṉmi celvam viḷivuiṉpam ēmam
aṇiyeṉpa nāṭṭiṟkiv vintu.
Rich yield, delight, defence and wealth
Are jewels of lands with blooming health.        738
9. nāṭeṉpa nāṭā vaḷattaṉa nāṭalla
nāṭa vaḷantaru nāṭu
A land is land which yields unsought
Needing hard work the land is nought.        739
10. āṅkami veytiyak kaṇṇum payamiṉṟē
vēntami villāta nāṭu
Though a land has thus every thing
It is worthless without a king.        740
-----------

75. araṇ - Fortress

1. āṟṟu pavarkkum araṇporuḷ añcittaṟ
pōṟṟu pavarkkum poruḷ.
The fort is vital for offence
Who fear the foes has its defence.        741
2. maṇinīrum maṇṇum maliyum aṇiniḻaṟ
kāṭum uṭiyatu araṇ.
A crystal fount, a space a mount
Thick woods form a fort paramount.        742
3. uyarvakalam tiṇmi arumiin nāṉkiṉ
amivaraṇ eṉṟurikkum nūl
An ideal fort's so says science:
High, broad, strong and hard for access.        743
4. ciṟukāppiṟ pēriṭatta tāki uṟupaki
ūkkam aḻippatu araṇ.
Ample in space, easy to hold
The fort foils enemies bold.        744
5. koḷaṟkaritāyk koṇṭakūḻt tāki akattār
nilikkeḷitām nīratu araṇ.
Impregnable with stores of food
Cosy to live-That fort is good.        745
6. ellāp poruḷum uṭittāy iṭattutavum
nallāḷ uṭiyatu araṇ.
A fort is full of stores and arms
And brave heroes to meet alarms.        746
7. muṟṟiyum muṟṟā teṟintum aṟippaṭuttum
paṟṟaṟ kariyatu araṇ
Besieging foes a fort withstands
Darts and mines of treacherous hands.        747
8. muṟṟāṟṟi muṟṟi yavariyum paṟṟāṟṟip
paṟṟiyār velvatu araṇ
A fort holds itself and defies
The attacks of encircling foes.        748
9. muṉimukattu māṟṟalar cāya viṉimukattu
vīṟeyti māṇṭatu araṇ.
A fort it is that fells the foes
And gins by deeds a name glorious.        749
10. eṉimāṭcit tākiyak kaṇṇum viṉimāṭci
illārkaṇ illatu araṇ.
But a fort however grand
Is nil if heroes do not stand.        750
-----------

76. poruḷ ceyalvaki - Way of making wealth

1. poruḷal lavarip poruḷākac ceyyum
poruḷallatu illi poruḷ.
Naught exists that can, save wealth
Make the worthless as men of worth.        751
2. illāri ellōrum eḷḷuvar celvari
ellārum ceyvar ciṟappu.
The have-nothing poor all despise
The men of wealth all rise and prise.        752
3. poruḷeṉṉum poyyā viḷakkam iruḷaṟukkum
eṇṇiya tēyattuc ceṉṟu.
Waneless wealth is light that goes
To every land and gloom removes.        753
4. aṟaṉīṉum iṉpamum īṉum tiṟaṉaṟintu
tītiṉṟi vanta poruḷ.
The blameless wealth from fairest means
Brings good virtue and also bliss.        754
5. aruḷoṭum aṉpoṭum vārāp poruḷākkam
pullār puraḷa viṭal.
Riches devoid of love and grace
Off with it; it is disgrace!        755
6. uṟuporuḷum ulku poruḷumtaṉ oṉṉārt
teṟuporuḷum vēntaṉ poruḷ.
Escheats, derelicts; spoils of war
Taxes duties are king's treasure.        756
7. aruḷeṉṉum aṉpīṉ kuḻavi poruḷeṉṉum
celvac ceviliyāl uṇṭu
Grace the child of love is nourished
By the wet-nurse of wealth cherished.        757
8. kuṉṟēṟi yāṉippōr kaṇṭaṟṟāl taṉkittoṉṟu
uṇṭākac ceyvāṉ viṉi.
Treasures in hand fulfil all things
Like hill-tuskers the wars of kings.        758
9. ceyka poruḷi ceṟunar cerukkaṟukkum
eḵkataṉiṟ kūriyatu il
Make wealth; there is no sharper steel
The insolence of foes to quell.        759
10. oṇporuḷ kāḻppa iyaṟṟiyārkku eṇporuḷ
ēṉi iraṇṭum oruṅku        
They have joy and virtue at hand
Who acquire treasures abundant.        760
-----------

77. paṭimāṭci - The glory of army

1. uṟuppamintu ūṟañcā velpaṭi vēntaṉ
veṟukkiyu ḷellām tali
The daring well-armed winning force
Is king's treasure and min resource.        761
2. uliviṭattu ūṟañcā vaṉkaṇ toliviṭattut
tolpaṭik kallāl aritu
Through shots and wounds brave heroes hold
Quailing not in fall, the field.        762
3. olittakkāl eṉṉām uvari elippaki
nākam uyirppak keṭum
Sea-like ratfoes roar ... What if?
They perish at a cobra's whiff.        763
4. aḻiviṉṟu aṟipōkā tāki vaḻivanta
vaṉka ṇatuvē paṭi.
The army guards its genial flame
Not crushed, routed nor marred in name.        764
5. kūṟṟuṭaṉṟu mēlvariṉum kūṭi etirniṟkum
āṟṟa latuvē paṭi.
The real army with rallied force
Resists even Death-God fierce.        765
6. maṟamāṉam māṇṭa vaḻiccelavu tēṟṟam
eṉanāṉkē ēmam paṭikku.
Manly army has merits four:-
Stately-march, faith, honour, valour.        766
7. tārtāṅkic celvatu tāṉi talivanta
pōrtāṅkum taṉmi aṟintu.
Army sets on to face the foes
Knowing how the trend of war goes.        767
8. aṭaṟṟakiyum āṟṟalum illeṉiṉum tāṉi
paṭittakiyāl pāṭu peṟum.
Army gins force by grand array
Lacking in stay or dash in fray.        768
9. ciṟumiyum cellāt tuṉiyum vaṟumiyum
illāyiṉ vellum paṭi.
Army shall win if it is free
From weakness, aversion, poverty.        769
10. nilimakkaḷ cāla uṭitteṉiṉum tāṉi
talimakkaḷ ilvaḻi il.
With troops in large numbers on rolls
Army can't march missing gen'rals.        770
---------

78. paṭiccerukku - Military pride

1. eṉṉimuṉ nillaṉmiṉ tevvir palareṉṉi
muṉniṉṟu kalniṉ ṟavar.
Stand not before my chief, O foes!
Many who stood, in stones repose.        771
2. kāṉa muyaleyta ampiṉil yāṉi
piḻittavēl ēntal iṉitu.
To lift a lance that missed a tusker
Is prouder than shaft that hit a hare.        772
3. pērāṇmi eṉpa taṟukaṇoṉ ṟuṟṟakkāl
ūrāṇmi maṟṟataṉ eḵku.
Valour is fight with fierce courage
Mercy to the fallen is its edge.        773
4. kivēl kaḷiṟṟoṭu pōkki varupavaṉ
meyvēl paṟiyā nakum.
At the tusker he flings his lance
One in body smiles another chance.        774
5. viḻittakaṇ vēlkoṇ ṭeṟiya aḻittimippiṉ
oṭṭaṉṟō vaṉka ṇavarkku.
When lances dart if heroes wink
"It is a rout" the world will think.        775
6. viḻuppuṇ paṭātanāḷ ellām vaḻukkiṉuḷ
vikkumtaṉ nāḷi eṭuttu.
The brave shall deem the days as vin
Which did not battle-wounds sustin.        776
7. cuḻalum icivēṇṭi vēṇṭā uyirār
kaḻalyāppuk kāriki nīrttu.
Their anklets aloud jingle their name
Who sacrifice their life for fame.        777
8. uṟiṉuyir añcā maṟavar iṟivaṉ
ceṟiṉumcīr kuṉṟal ilar.
The king may chide, they pursue strife;
They fear loss of glory; not life.        778
9. iḻittatu ikavāmic cāvāri yārē
piḻittatu oṟukkiṟ pavar.
Who will blame the heroes that lose
Their lives in war to keep their vows?        779
10. purantārkaṇ nīrmalkac cākiṟpiṉ cākkāṭu
irantukōḷ takkatu uṭittu.
Such a death shall be prayed for
Which draws the tears of the ruler.        780

2.4 naṭpiyal – ALLIANCE 79. naṭpu - Friendship

1. ceyaṟkariya yāvuḷa naṭpiṉ atupōl
viṉikkariya yāvuḷa kāppu.
Like friendship what's so hard to gin?
That guards one aginst acts villin?        781
2. niṟinīra nīravar kēṇmi piṟimatip
piṉnīra pētiyār naṭpu - Friendship .
Good friendship shines like waxing moon,
The bad withers like waning moon.        782
3. naviltoṟum nūlnayam pōlum payiltoṟum
paṇpuṭi yāḷar toṭarpu.
Like taste in books good friendship grows
The more one moves the more he knows.        783
4. nakutaṟ poruṭṭaṉṟu naṭṭal mikutikkaṇ
mēṟceṉṟu iṭittaṟ poruṭṭu
Not to lugh is friendship made
But to hit when faults exceed.        784
5. puṇarcci paḻakutal vēṇṭā uṇarccitāṉ
naṭpām kiḻami tarum.
No close living nor clasping grip
Friendship's feeling heart's fellowship.        785
6. mukanaka naṭpatu naṭpaṉṟu neñcattu
akanaka naṭpatu naṭpu.
Friendship is not more smile on face
It is the smiling heart's embrace.        786
7. aḻivi ṉavinīkki āṟuyttu aḻiviṉkaṇ
allal uḻappatām naṭpu.
From ruin friendship saves and shares
The load of pin and right path shows.        787
8. uṭukki iḻantavaṉ kipōla āṅkē
iṭukkaṇ kaḷivatuām naṭpu.
Friendship hastens help in mishaps
Like hands picking up dress that slips.        788
9. naṭpiṟku vīṟṟirukki yāteṉil koṭpiṉṟi
ollumvāy ūṉṟum nili.
Friendship is enthroned on the strength
That always helps with utmost warmth.        789
10. iṉiyar ivaremakku iṉṉamyām eṉṟu
puṉiyiṉum pulleṉṉum naṭpu.
"Such we are and such they are"
Ev'n this boast will friendship mar.        790
-------

80. naṭpārāytal - Testing friendship

1. nāṭātu naṭṭaliṟ kēṭilli naṭṭapiṉ
vīṭilli naṭpāḷ pavarkku.
Than testless friendship nought is worse
For contacts formed will scarcely cease.        791
2. āyntāyntu koḷḷātāṉ kēṇmi kaṭimuṟi
tāṉcān tuyaram tarum.
Friendship made without frequent test
Shall end in grief and death at last.        792
3. kuṇaṉuṅ kuṭimiyum kuṟṟamuṅ kuṉṟā
viṉaṉu maṟintiyākka naṭpu.
Temper, descent, defects and kins
Trace well and take companions.        793
4. kuṭippiṟantu taṉkaṭ paḻināṇu vāṉik
koṭuttuṅ koḷalvēṇṭum naṭpu.
Take as good friend at any price
The nobly born who shun disgrace.        794
5. aḻaccolli yalla tiṭittu vaḻakkaṟiya
vallārnaṭ pāyntu koḷal.
Who make you weep and chide wrong trends
And lead you right are worthy friends.        795
6. kēṭṭiṉu muṇṭō ruṟuti kiḷiñari
nīṭṭi yaḷappatōr kōl.
Is there a test like misfortune
A rod to measure out kinsmen?        796
7. ūtiya meṉpa toruvaṟkup pētiyār
kēṇmi yorīi viṭal.
Keep off contacts with fools; that is
The greatest gin so say the wise.        797
8. uḷḷaṟka vuḷḷañ ciṟukuva koḷḷaṟka
allaṟkaṇ ṇāṟṟaṟuppār naṭpu.
Off with thoughts that depress the heart
Off with friends that in woe depart.        798
9. keṭuṅkālik kiviṭuvār kēṇmi yaṭuṅkāli
yuḷḷiṉu muḷḷañ cuṭum.
Friends who betray at ruin's brink
Burn our mind ev'n at death to think.        799
10. maruvuka mācaṟṟār kēṇmiyoṉ ṟīntum
oruvuka voppilār naṭpu.
The blameless ones as friends embarace;
Give something and give up the base.        800
-----------

81. paḻimi - Intimacy

1. paḻimi eṉappaṭuvatu yāteṉiṉ yātum
kiḻamiyik kīḻntiṭā naṭpu.
That friendship is good amity
Which restrins not one's liberty.        801
2. naṭpiṟ kuṟuppuk keḻutakimi maṟṟataṟku
uppātal cāṉṟōr kaṭaṉ.
Friendship's heart is freedom close;
Wise men's duty is such to please.        802
3. paḻakiya naṭpevaṉ ceyyum keḻutakimi
ceytāṅku amiyāk kaṭi.
Of long friendship what is the use
Righteous freedom if men refuse?        803
4. viḻitakiyāṉ vēṇṭi yiruppar keḻutakiyāṟ
kēḷātu naṭṭār ceyiṉ.
Things done unasked by loving friends
Please the wise as familiar trends!        804
5. pētimi oṉṟō peruṅkiḻami eṉṟuṇarka
nōtakka naṭṭār ceyiṉ.
Offence of friends feel it easy
As folloy or close intimacy.        805
6. ellikkaṇ niṉṟār tuṟavār toliviṭattum
tollikkaṇ niṉṟār toṭarpu.
They forsake not but continue
In friendship's bounds though loss ensue.        806
7. aḻivanta ceyyiṉum aṉpaṟār aṉpiṉ
vaḻivanta kēṇmi yavar.
Comrades established in firm love
Though ruin comes waive not their vow.        807
8. kēḷiḻukkam kēḷāk keḻutakimi vallārkku
nāḷiḻukkam naṭṭār ceyiṉ.
Fast friends who list not tales of ill
Though wronged they say "that day is well".        808
9. keṭāa vaḻivanta kēṇmiyār kēṇmi
viṭāar viḻiyum ulaku
To love such friends the world desires
Whose friendship has unbroken ties.        809
10. viḻiyār viḻiyap paṭupa paḻiyārkaṇ
paṇpiṉ talippiriyā tār.
Even foes love for better ends
Those who leave not long-standing friends.        810
---------

82. tīnaṭpu - Bad friendship

1. parukuvār pōliṉum paṇpilār kēṇmi
perukaliṟ kuṉṟal iṉitu.
Swallowing love of soulless men
Had better wane than wax anon.        811
2. uṟiṉnaṭṭu aṟiṉorūum oppilār kēṇmi
peṟiṉum iḻappiṉum eṉ.
Who fawn in wealth and fail in dearth
Gain or lose; such friends have no worth.        812
3. uṟuvatu cīrtūkkum naṭpum peṟuvatu
koḷvārum kaḷvarum nēr.
Cunning friends who calculate
Are like thieves and whores wicked.        813
4. amarakattu āṟ ṟaṟukkum kallāmā aṉṉār
tamariṉ taṉimi tali.
Better be alone than trust in those
That throw in field like faithless horse.        814
5. ceytēmañ cārāc ciṟiyavar puṉkēṇmi
eytaliṉ eytāmi naṉṟu.
Friends low and mean that give no help-
Leave them is better than to keep.        815
6. pēti peruṅkeḻīi naṭpiṉ aṟivuṭiyār
ētiṉmi kōṭi uṟum.
Million times the wise man's hate
Is better than a fool intimate.        816
7. nakivakiya rākiya naṭpiṉ pakivarāl
pattaṭutta kōṭi uṟum.
Ten-fold crore you gin from foes
Than from friends who are vin lughers.        817
8. ollum karumam uṭaṟṟu pavarkēṇmi
collāṭār cōra viṭal.
Without a word those friends eschew
Who spoil deeds which they can do.        818
9. kaṉaviṉum iṉṉātu maṉṉō viṉivēṟu
colvēṟu paṭṭār toṭarpu.
Even in dreams the tie is bad
With those whose deed is far from word.        819
10. eṉittum kuṟukutal ōmpal maṉikkeḻīi
maṉṟil paḻippār toṭarpu.
Keep aloof from those that smile
At home and in public revile.        820
------------

83. kūṭānaṭpu - False friendship

1. cīriṭam kāṇiṉ eṟitaṟkup paṭṭaṭi
nērā nirantavar naṭpu.
The friendship by an enemy shown
Is anvil in time, to strike you down.        821
2. iṉampōṉṟu iṉamallār kēṇmi makaḷir
maṉampōla vēṟu paṭum.
Who pretend kinship but are not
Their friendship's fickle like woman's heart.        822
3. palanalla kaṟṟak kaṭittum maṉamnallar
ākutal māṇārkku aritu.
They may be vast in good studies
But heartfelt-love is hard for foes.        823
4. mukattiṉ iṉiya nakāa akattiṉṉā
vañcari añcap paṭum.
Fear foes whose face has winning smiles
Whose heart is full of cunning guiles.        824
5. maṉattiṉ amiyā tavari eṉittoṉṟum
colliṉāl tēṟaṟpāṟṟu aṉṟu.
Do not trust in what they tell
Whose mind with your mind goes ill.        825
6. naṭṭārpōl nallavi colliṉum oṭṭārcol
olli uṇarap paṭum.
The words of foes is quickly seen
Though they speak like friends in fine.        826
7. colvaṇakkam oṉṉārkaṇ koḷḷaṟka vilvaṇakkam
tīṅku kuṟittami yāṉ.
Trust not the humble words of foes
Danger darts from bending bows.        827
8. toḻutaki yuḷḷum paṭiyoṭuṅkum oṉṉār
aḻutakaṇ ṇīrum aṉittu.
Adoring hands of foes hide arms
Their sobbing tears have lurking harms.        828
9. mikacceytu tameḷḷu vāri nakacceytu
naṭpiṉuḷ cāppullaṟ pāṟṟu.
In open who prise, at heart despise
Cajole and crush them in friendly guise.        829
10. pakinaṭpām kālam varuṅkāl mukanaṭṭu
akanaṭpu orīi viṭal
When foes, in time, play friendship's part
Feign love on face but not in heart.        830
----------

84. pētimi - Folly

1. pētimi eṉpatoṉṟu yāteṉiṉ ētaṅkoṇṭu
ūtiyam pōka viṭal.
This is folly's prominent vein
To favour loss and forego gin.        831
2. pētimiyu ḷellām pētimi kātaṉmi
kiyalla taṉkaṭ ceyal.
Folly of follies is to lead
A lewd and lawless life so bad.        832
3. nāṇāmi nāṭāmi nāriṉmi yātoṉṟum
pēṇāmi pēti toḻil.
Shameless, aimless, callous, listless
Such are the marks of foolishness.        833
4. ōti uṇarntum piṟarkkurittum tāṉaṭaṅkāp
pētiyiṉ pētiyār il.
No fool equals the fool who learns
Knows, teaches, but self-control spurns.        834
5. orumic ceyalāṟṟum pēti eḻumiyum
tāṉpuk kaḻuntum aḷaṟu.
The fool suffers seven fold hells
In single birth of hellish ills.        835
6. poypaṭum oṉṟo puṉipūṇum kiyaṟiyāp
pēti viṉimēṟ koḷiṉ.
A know-nothing fool daring a deed
Not only fails but feels fettered.        836
7. ētilār ārat tamarpacippar pēti
peruñcelvam uṟṟak kaṭi.
Strangers feast and kinsmen fast
When fools mishandle fortunes vast.        837
8. miyal oruvaṉ kaḷittaṟṟāl pētitaṉ
kiyoṉṟu uṭimi peṟiṉ.
Fools possessing something on hand
Like dazed and drunken stupids stand.        838
9. peritiṉitu pētiyār kēṇmi piriviṉkaṇ
pīḻi taruvatoṉṟu il.
Friendship with fools is highly sweet
For without a groan we part.        839
10. kaḻāakkāl paḷḷiyuḷ vittaṟṟāl cāṉṟōr
kuḻāattup pēti pukal.
Entrance of fools where Savants meet
Looks like couch trod by unclean feet.        840
---------

85. pullaṟivāṇmi - Petty conceit

1. aṟiviṉmi iṉmiyuḷ iṉmi piṟitiṉmi
iṉmiyā viyātu ulaku.
Want of wisdom is want of wants
Want of aught else the world nev'r counts.        841
2. aṟivilāṉ neñcuvantu ītal piṟituyātum
illi peṟuvāṉ tavam.
When fool bestows with glee a gift
It comes but by getter's merit.        842
3. aṟivilār tāmtammip pīḻikkum pīḻi
ceṟuvārkkum ceytal aritu.
The self-torments of fools exceed
Ev'n tortures of their foes indeed.        843
4. veṇmi eṉappaṭuvatu yāteṉiṉ oṇmi
uṭiyamyām eṉṉuñ cerukku.
Stupidity is vanity
That cries "We have sagacity"        844
5. kallāta mēṟkoṇṭu oḻukal kacaṭaṟa
vallatūum aiyam tarum.
Feigning knowledge that one has not
Leads to doubt ev'n that he has got.        845
6. aṟṟam maṟittalō pullaṟivu tamvayiṉ
kuṟṟam maṟiyā vaḻi.
Fools their nakedness conceal
And yet their glaring faults reveal.        846
7. arumaṟi cōrum aṟivilāṉ ceyyum
perumiṟi tāṉē taṉakku.
The fool that slights sacred counsels
Upon himself great harm entils.        847
8. ēvavum ceykalāṉ tāṉtēṟāṉ avvuyir
pōom aḷavumōr nōy.
He listens not nor himself knows
Plague is his life until it goes.        848
9. kāṇātāṉ kāṭṭuvāṉ tāṉkāṇāṉ kāṇātāṉ
kaṇṭāṉām tāṉkaṇṭa vāṟu.
Sans Self-sight in vin one opens Sight
To the blind who bet their sight as right.        849
10. ulakattār uṇṭeṉpatu illeṉpāṉ viyattu
alakiyā vikkap paṭum.
To people's "Yes" who proffer "No"
Deemed as ghouls on earth they go.        850
-----------

86. ikal - Hatred

1. ikaleṉpa ellā uyirkkum pakaleṉṉum
paṇpiṉmi pārikkum nōy.
Hatred is a plague that divides
And rouses illwill on all sides.        851
2. pakalkarutip paṟṟā ceyiṉum ikalkaruti
iṉṉācey yāmi tali.
Rouse not hatred and confusion
Though foes provoke disunion        852
3. ikaleṉṉum evvanōy nīkkiṉ tavavillāt
tāvil viḷakkam tarum.
Shun the plague of enmity
And win everlasting glory.        853
4. iṉpattuḷ iṉpam payakkum ikaleṉṉum
tuṉpattuḷ tuṉpam keṭiṉ.
Hate-the woe of woes destroy;
Then joy of joys you can enjoy.        854
5. ikaletir cāyntoḻuka vallāri yārē
mikalūkkum taṉmi yavar.
Who can overcome them in glory
That are free from enmity?        855
6. ikaliṉ mikaliṉitu eṉpavaṉ vāḻkki
tavalum keṭalum naṇittu.
His fall and ruin are quite near
Who holds enmity sweet and dear.        856
7. mikalmēval meypporuḷ kāṇār ikalmēval
iṉṉā aṟivi ṉavar.
They cannot see the supreme Truth
Who hate and injure without ruth.        857
8. ikaliṟku etircāytal ākkam ataṉi
mikalūkkiṉ ūkkumām kēṭu.
To turn from enmity is gin
Fomenting it brings fast ruin.        858
9. ikalkāṇāṉ ākkam varuṅkāl ataṉi
mikalkāṇum kēṭu taraṟku.
Fortune favours when hate recedes
Hatred exceeding ruin breeds.        859
10. ikalāṉām iṉṉāta ellām nakalāṉām
naṉṉayam eṉṉum cerukku.
All evils come from enmity
All goodness flow from amity.        860
-------------

87. pakimāṭci - Noble hostility

1. valiyārkku māṟēṟṟal ōmpuka ōmpā
meliyārmēl mēka paki.
Turn from strife with foes too strong
With the feeble for battle long.        861
2. aṉpilaṉ āṉṟa tuṇiyilaṉ tāṉtuvvāṉ
eṉpariyum ētilāṉ tuppu.
Loveless, aidless, powerless king
Can he withstand an enemy strong?        862
3. añcum aṟiyāṉ amivilaṉ īkalāṉ
tañcam eḷiyaṉ pakikku.
Unskilled, timid, miser, misfit
He is easy for foes to hit.        863
4. nīṅkāṉ vekuḷi niṟiyilaṉ eññāṉṟum
yāṅkaṇum yārkkum eḷitu.
The wrathful restive man is prey
To any, anywhere any day.        864
5. vaḻinōkkāṉ vāyppaṉa ceyyāṉ paḻinōkkāṉ
paṇpilaṉ paṟṟārkku iṉitu.
Crooked, cruel, tactless and base
Any foe can fell him with ease.        865
6. kāṇāc ciṉattāṉ kaḻiperuṅ kāmattāṉ
pēṇāmi pēṇap paṭum.
Blind in rage and mad in lust
To have his hatred is but just.        866
7. koṭuttum koḷalvēṇṭum maṉṟa aṭuttiruntu
māṇāta ceyvāṉ paki.
Pay and buy his enmity
Who muddles chance with oddity.        867
8. kuṇaṉilaṉāyk kuṟṟam palavāyiṉ māṟṟārkku
iṉaṉilaṉām ēmāppu uṭittu.
With no virtue but full of vice
He loses friends and delights foes.        868
9. ceṟuvārkkuc cēṇikavā iṉpam aṟivilā
añcum pakivarp peṟiṉ.
The joy of heroes knows no bounds
When timid fools are opponents.        869
10. kallāṉ vekuḷum ciṟuporuḷ eññāṉṟum
ollāṉi ollātu oḷi.
Glory's light he will not gin
Who fails to fight a fool and win.        870

88. pakittiṟan terital - Apparising enemies

1. pakieṉṉum paṇpi lataṉi oruvaṉ
nakiyēyum vēṇṭaṟpāṟṟu aṉṟu.
Let not one even as a sport

2. villē ruḻavar pakikoḷiṉum koḷḷaṟka
collē ruḻavar paki.
Incur the hate of bow-ploughers
But not the hate of word-ploughers.        872
3. ēmuṟ ṟavariṉum ēḻi tamiyaṉāyp
pallār pakikoḷ pavaṉ.
Forlorn, who rouses many foes

takimikkaṇ taṅkiṟṟu ulaku.
This world goes safely in his grace
Whose heart makes friends even of foes.        874

iṉtuṇiyāk koḷkavaṟṟiṉ oṉṟu.
Alone, if two foes you oppose
Make one of them your ally close.        875
6. tēṟiṉum tēṟā viṭiṉum aḻiviṉkaṇ
tēṟāṉ pakāaṉ viṭal.
Trust or distrust; during distress
Keep aloof; don't mix with foes.        876
7. nōvaṟka nontatu aṟiyārkku mēvaṟka
meṉmi pakiva rakattu
To those who know not, tell not your pin
Nor your weakness to foes explin.        877
8. vakiyaṟintu taṟceytu taṟkāppa māyum
pakivarkaṇ paṭṭa cerukku.
Know how and act and defend well
The pride of enemies shall fall.        878
9. iḷitāka muḷmaram kolka kaḷiyunar
kikollum kāḻtta viṭattu
Cut off thorn-trees when young they are;
Grown hard, they cut your hands beware.        879
10. uyirppa uḷarallar maṉṟa ceyirppavar
cemmal citikkalā tār.
To breathe on earth they are not fit
Defying foes who don't defeat.        880

89. uṭpaki - Secret foe

1. niḻalnīrum iṉṉāta iṉṉā tamarnīrum
iṉṉāvām iṉṉā ceyiṉ.
Tritorous kinsmen will make you sad
As water and shade do harm when bad.        881
2. vāḷpōl pakivari añcaṟka añcuka
kēḷpōl pakivar toṭarpu.
You need not sword-like kinsmen fear
Fear foes who feign as kinsmen dear.        882
3. uṭpaki añcittaṟ kākka ulivuiṭattu
maṭpakiyiṉ māṇat teṟum.
The secret foe in days evil
Will cut you, beware, like potters' steel.        883
4. maṉamāṇā uṭpaki tōṉṟiṉ iṉamāṇā
ētam palavun tarum.
The evil-minded foe within
Foments trouble, spoils kinsmen!        884
5. uṟalmuṟiyāṉ uṭpaki tōṉṟiṉ iṟalmuṟiyāṉ
ētam palavun tarum.
A tritor among kinsmen will
Bring life-endangering evil.        885
6. oṉṟāmi oṉṟiyār kaṭpaṭiṉ eññāṉṟum
poṉṟāmi oṉṟal aritu.
Discord in kings' circle entils
Life-destroying deadly evils.        886
7. ceppiṉ puṇarccipōl kūṭiṉum kūṭātē
uṭpaki uṟṟa kuṭi.
A house hiding hostiles in core
Just seems on like the lid in jar.        887
8. aramporuta poṉpōlat tēyum uramporutu
uṭpaki uṟṟa kuṭi.
By secret spite the house wears out
Like gold crumbling by file's contact.        888
9. eṭpaka vaṉṉa ciṟumittē āyiṉum
uṭpaki uḷḷatām kēṭu.
Ruin lurks in enmity
As slit in sesame though it be.        889
10. uṭampāṭu ilātavar vāḻkki kuṭaṅkaruḷ
pāmpōṭu uṭaṉuṟin taṟṟu.
Dwell with tritors that hate in heart
Is dwelling with snake in selfsame hut.        890

90. periyārip piḻiyāmi - Offend not the great

1. āṟṟuvār āṟṟal ikaḻāmi pōṟṟuvār
pōṟṟalu ḷellām tali.
Not to spite the mighty ones
Safest safeguard to living brings.        891
2. periyārip pēṇātu oḻukiṉ periyārāl
pērā iṭumpi tarum.
To walk unmindful of the great
Shall great troubles ceaseless create.        892
3. keṭalvēṇṭiṉ kēḷātu ceyka aṭalvēṇṭiṉ
āṟṟu pavarkaṇ iḻukku.
Heed not and do, if ruin you want
Offence aginst the mighty great.        893
4. kūṟṟattik kiyāl viḷittaṟṟāl āṟṟuvārkku
āṟṟātār iṉṉā ceyal
The weak who insult men of might
Death with their own hands invite.        894
5. yāṇṭucceṉṟu yāṇṭum uḷarākār ventuppiṉ
vēntu ceṟappaṭ ṭavar.
Where can they go and thrive where
Pursued by powerful monarch's ire?        895
6. eriyāl cuṭappaṭiṉum uyvuṇṭām uyyār
periyārp piḻittoḻuku vār.
One can escape in fire cught
The great who offends escapes not.        896
7. vakimāṇṭa vāḻkkiyum vāṉporuḷum eṉṉām
takimāṇṭa takkār ceṟiṉ.
If holy mighty sages frown
Stately gifts and stores who can own?        897
8. kuṉṟaṉṉār kuṉṟa matippiṉ kuṭiyoṭu
niṉṟaṉṉār māyvar nilattu.
When hill-like sages are held small
The firm on earth lose home and all.        898
9. ēntiya koḷkiyār cīṟiṉ iṭimurintu
vēntaṉum vēntu keṭum.
Before the holy sage's rage
Ev'n Indra's empire meets damage.        899
10. iṟantaminta cārpuṭiya rā yiṉum uyyār
ciṟantaminta cīrār ceṟiṉ.
Even mighty aided men shall quail
If the enraged holy seers will.        900

91. peṇvaḻiccēṟal - Being led by women

1. maṉiviḻivār māṇpayaṉ eytār viṉiviḻivār
vēṇṭāp poruḷum atu.
Who dote on wives lose mighty gin
That lust, dynamic men disdin.        901
2. pēṇātu peṇviḻivāṉ ākkam periyatōr
nāṇāka nāṇut tarum.
Who dotes, unmanly, on his dame
His wealth to him and all is shame.        902
3. illāḷkaṇ tāḻnta iyalpiṉmi eññāṉṟum
nallāruḷ nāṇut tarum.
Who's servile to his wife always
Shy he feels before the wise.        903
4. maṉiyāḷi añcum maṟumiyi lāḷaṉ
viṉiyāṇmi vīṟeytal iṉṟu.
Fearing his wife salvationless
The weaklings' action has no grace.        904
5. illāḷi añcuvāṉ añcumaṟ ṟeññāṉṟum
nallārkku nalla ceyal.
Who fears his wife fears always
Good to do to the good and wise.        905
6. imiyāriṉ vāḻiṉum pāṭilarē illāḷ
amiyārtōḷ añcu pavar.
Who fear douce arms of their wives
Look petty even with god-like lives.        906
7. peṇṇēval ceytoḻukum āṇmiyiṉ nāṇuṭip
peṇṇē perumi uṭittu.
Esteemed more is women bashful
Than man servile unto her will.        907
8. naṭṭār kuṟimuṭiyār naṉṟāṟṟār naṉṉutalāḷ
peṭṭāṅku oḻuku pavar.
By fair-browed wives who are governed
Help no friends nor goodness tend.        908
9. aṟaviṉiyum āṉṟa poruḷum piṟaviṉiyum
peṇēval ceyvārkaṇ il.
No virtue riches nor joy is seen
In those who submit to women        909
10. eṇcērnta neñcat tiṭaṉuṭiyārkku eññāṉṟum
peṇcērntām pētimi il.
Thinkers strong and broad of heart
By folly on fair sex do not dote.        910

93. kaḷḷuṇṇāmi - Not drinking liquor

1. uṭkap paṭāar oḷiyiḻappar eññāṉṟum
kaṭkātal koṇṭoḻuku vār.
Foes fear not who for toddy craze
The addicts dily their glory lose.        921
2. uṇṇaṟka kaḷḷi uṇiluṇka cāṉṟōrāṉ
eṇṇap paṭavēṇṭā tār.
Drink not liquor; but let them drink

3. īṉṟāḷ mukattēyum iṉṉātāl eṉmaṟṟuc
cāṉṟōr mukattuk kaḷi.
The drunkard's joy pins ev'n mother's face

Good shame turns back from him ashamed
Who is guilty of wine condemned.        924

To pay and drink and lose the sense

They take poison who take toddy

kaḷḷoṟṟik kaṇcāy pavar.

Can torch search one in water sunk?
Can reason reach the raving drunk?        929
10. kaḷḷuṇṇāp pōḻtil kaḷittāṉik kāṇuṅkāl

The sober seeing the drunkard's plight

94. cūtu - Gambling

1. vēṇṭaṟka veṉṟiṭiṉum cūtiṉi veṉṟatūum
tūṇṭiṟpoṉ mīṉviḻuṅki yaṟṟu.
Avoid gambling, albeit you win
Gulping bit-hook what does fish gin?        931
2. oṉṟeyti nūṟiḻakkum cūtarkkum uṇṭāṅkol
naṉṟeyti vāḻvatōr āṟu.
Can gamblers in life good obtin
Who lose a hundred one to gin?        932
3. uruḷāyam ōvātu kūṟiṉ poruḷāyam
pōoyp puṟamē paṭum.
If kings indulge in casting dice
All their fortune will flow to foes.        933
4. ciṟumi palaceytu cīraḻikkum cūtiṉ
vaṟumi taruvatoṉṟu il.
Nothing will make you poor like game
Which adds to woes and ruins fame.        934
5. kavaṟum kaḻakamum kiyum tarukki
ivaṟiyār illāki yār.
The game, game-hall and gambler's art
Who sought with glee have come to nought.        935
6. akaṭārār allal uḻappar cūteṉṉum
mukaṭiyāl mūṭappaṭṭa ṭār.
Men swallowed by the ogress, dice
Suffer grief and want by that vice.        936
7. paḻakiya celvamum paṇpum keṭukkum
kaḻakattuk kāli pukiṉ.
If men their time in game-den spend
Ancestral wealth and virtues end.        937
8. poruḷkeṭuttup poymēṟ koḷīi aruḷkeṭuttu
allal uḻappikkum cūtu.
Game ruins wealth and spoils grace
Leads to lies and wretched woes.        938
9. uṭicelvam ūṇoḷi kalvieṉṟu aintum
aṭiyāvām āyam koḷiṉ.
Dress, wealth, food, fame, learning-these five
In gambler's hand will never thrive.        939
10. iḻattoṟūum kātalikkum cūtēpōl tuṉpam
uḻattoṟūum kātaṟṟu uyir.
Love for game grows with every loss
As love for life with sorrows grows.        940

95. maruntu - Medicine

1. mikiṉum kuṟiyiṉum nōyceyyum nūlōr
vaḷimutalā eṇṇiya mūṉṟu.
Wind, bile and phlegm three cuse disease
So doctors deem it more or less.        941
2. marunteṉa vēṇṭāvām yākkikku aruntiyatu
aṟṟatu pōṟṟi uṇiṉ.
After digestion one who feeds
His body no medicine needs.        942
3. aṟṟāl aḷavaṟintu uṇka aḵtuṭampu
peṟṟāṉ neṭituuykku māṟu.
Eat food to digestive measure
Life in body lasts with pleasure.        943
4. aṟṟatu aṟintu kaṭippiṭittu māṟalla
tuykka tuvarap pacittu.
Know digestion; with keen appetite
Eat what is suitable and right.        944
5. māṟupāṭu illāta uṇṭi maṟuttuṇṇiṉ
ūṟupāṭu illi uyirkku.
With fasting adjusted food right
Cures ills of life and makes you bright        945
6. iḻivaṟintu uṇpāṉkaṇ iṉpampōl niṟkum
kaḻipē ririyāṉkaṇ nōy.
Who eats with clean stomach gets health
With greedy glutton abides ill-health.        946
7. tīyaḷa vaṉṟit teriyāṉ perituṇṇiṉ
nōyaḷa viṉṟip paṭum.
who glut beyond the hunger's fire
Suffer from untold diseases here.        947
8. nōynāṭi nōymutal nāṭi atutaṇikkum
vāynāṭi vāyppac ceyal.
Test disease, its cuse and cure
And apply remedy that is sure.        948
9. uṟṟāṉ aḷavum piṇiyaḷavum kālamum
kaṟṟāṉ karutic ceyal.
Let the skilful doctor note
The sickmen, sickness, season and treat.        949
10. uṟṟavaṉ tīrppāṉ maruntu - Medicine uḻic celvāṉeṉṟu
appālnāṟ kūṟṟē maruntu.
Patient, doctor, medicine and nurse
Are four-fold codes of treating course.        950

96. kuṭi - Nobility

1. iṟpiṟantār kaṇallatu illi iyalpākac
ceppamum nāṇum oruṅku.
Right-sense and bashfulness adorn
By nature only the noble-born.        951
2. oḻukkamum vāymiyum nāṇumim mūṉṟum
iḻukkār kuṭippiṟan tār.
The noble-born lack not these three:
Good conduct, truth and modesty.        952
3. nakiīki iṉcol ikaḻāmi nāṉkum
vakieṉpa vāymik kuṭikku.
Smile, gift, sweet words and courtesy
These four mark true nobility.        953
4. aṭukkiya kōṭi peṟiṉum kuṭippiṟantār
kuṉṟuva ceytal ilar.
Even for crores, the noble mood
Cannot bend to degrading deed.        954
5. vaḻaṅkuva tuḷvīḻntak kaṇṇum paḻaṅkuṭi
paṇpil talippirital iṉṟu.
The means of gift may dwindle; yet
Ancient homes guard their noble trit.        955
6. calampaṟṟic cālpila ceyyārmā caṟṟa
kulampaṟṟi vāḻtumeṉ pār.
Who guard their family prestige pure
Stoop not to acts of cunning lure.        956
7. kuṭippiṟantār kaṇviḷaṅkum kuṟṟam vicumpiṉ
matikkaṇ maṟuppōl uyarntu.
The faults of nobly-born are seen
Like on the sky the spots of moon.        957
8. nalattiṉkaṇ nāriṉmi tōṉṟiṉ avaṉik
kulattiṉkaṇ aiyap paṭum.
If manners of the good are rude
People deem their pedigree crude.        958
9. nilattil kiṭantami kālkāṭṭum kāṭṭum
kulattil piṟantārvāyc col.
Soil's nature is seen in sprout
The worth of birth from words flow out.        959
10. nalamvēṇṭiṉ nāṇuṭimi vēṇṭum kulamvēṇṭiṉ
vēṇṭuka yārkkum paṇivu.
All gin good name by modesty
Nobility by humility.        960

2.5 oḻipiyal – MISCELLANEOUS 97. māṉam - Honour

1. iṉṟi yamiyāc ciṟappiṉa āyiṉum
kuṉṟa varupa viṭal.
Though needed for your life in min,
From mean degrading acts refrin.        961
2. cīriṉum cīralla ceyyārē cīroṭu
pērāṇmi vēṇṭu pavar.
Who seek honour and manly fame
Don't do mean deeds even for name.        962
3. perukkattu vēṇṭum paṇital ciṟiya
curukkattu vēṇṭum uyarvu.
Be humble in prosperity
In decline uphold dignity.        963
4. taliyiṉ iḻinta mayiraṉiyar māntar
niliyiṉ iḻintak kaṭi.
Like hir fallen from head are those
Who fall down from their high status.        964
5. kuṉṟiṉ aṉiyārum kuṉṟuvar kuṉṟuva
kuṉṟi aṉiya ceyiṉ.
Even hill-like men will sink to nought
With abrus-grin-like small default.        965
6. pukaḻiṉṟāl puttēḷnāṭṭu uyyātāl eṉmaṟṟu
ikaḻvārpiṉ ceṉṟu nili.
Why fawn on men that scorn you here
It yields no fame, heaven's bliss neither.        966
7. oṭṭārpiṉ ceṉṟoruvaṉ vāḻtaliṉ anniliyē
keṭṭāṉ eṉappaṭutal naṉṟu.
Better it is to die forlorn
Than live as slaves of those who scorn.        967
8. maruntōmaṟṟu ūṉōmpum vāḻkki peruntakimi
pīṭaḻiya vanta iṭattu.
Is nursing body nectar sweet
Even when one's honour is lost?        968
9. mayirnīppiṉ vāḻāk kavarimā aṉṉār
uyirnīppar māṉam variṉ.
Honour lost, the noble expire
Like a yak that loses its hir.        969
10. iḷivariṉ vāḻāta māṉam uṭiyār
oḷitoḻutu ēttum ulaku.
Their light the world adores and hils
Who will not live when honour fails.        970
----------

98. perumi - Greatness

1. oḷioruvaṟku uḷḷa veṟukki iḷioruvaṟku
aḵtiṟantu vāḻtum eṉal.
A heart of courage lives in light
Devoid of that one's life is night.        971
2. piṟappokkum ellā uyirkkum ciṟappovvā
ceytoḻil vēṟṟumi yāṉ.
All beings are the same in birth
But work decides their varied worth.        972
3. mēliruntum mēlallār mēlallar kīḻiruntum
kīḻallār kīḻal lavar.
Ignoble high not high they are
The noble low not low they fare.        973
4. orumi makaḷirē pōlap perumiyum
taṉṉittāṉ koṇṭoḻukiṉ uṇṭu.
Greatness like woman's chastity
Is guarded by self-varacity.        974
5. perumi uṭiyavar āṟṟuvār āṟṟiṉ
arumi uṭiya ceyal.
Great souls when their will is active
Do mighty deeds rare to achieve.        975
6. ciṟiyār uṇarcciyuḷ illi periyārip
pēṇikkoḷ vēmeṉṉum nōkku.
The petty-natured ones have not
The mind to seek and befriend the great.        976
7. iṟappē purinta toḻiṟṟām ciṟappuntāṉ
cīral lavarkaṭ paṭiṉ.
The base with power and opulence
Wax with deeds of insolence.        977
8. paṇiyumām eṉṟum perumi ciṟumi
aṇiyumām taṉṉi viyantu.
Greatness bends with modesty
Meanness vunts with vanity        978
9. perumi perumitam iṉmi ciṟumi
perumitam ūrntu viṭal.
Greatness is free from insolence
Littleness swells with that offence.        979
10. aṟṟam maṟikkum perumi ciṟumitāṉ
kuṟṟamē kūṟi viṭum.
Weakness of others greatness screens
Smallness defects alone proclims.        980
----------

99. cāṉṟāṇmi - Sublimity

1. kaṭaṉeṉpa nallavi ellām kaṭaṉaṟintu
cāṉṟāṇmi mēṟkoḷ pavarkku.
All goodness is duty to them
Who are dutiful and sublime.        981
2. kuṇanalam cāṉṟōr nalaṉē piṟanalam
ennalattu uḷḷatūum aṉṟu.
Good in the great is character
Than that there is nothing better.        982
3. aṉpunāṇ ōppuravu kaṇṇōṭṭam vāymiyoṭu
aintucālpu ūṉṟiya tūṇ.
Love, truth, regard, modesty, grace
These five are virtue's resting place.        983
4. kollā nalattatu nōṉmi piṟartīmi
collā nalattatu cālpu.
Not to kill is penance pure
Not to slander virtue sure.        984
5. āṟṟuvār āṟṟal paṇital atucāṉṟōr
māṟṟāri māṟṟum paṭi.
Humility is valour's strength
A force that averts foes at length.        985
6. cālpiṟkuk kaṭṭaḷi yāteṉil tōlvi
tuliyallār kaṇṇum koḷal.
To bear repulse e'en from the mean
Is the touch-stone of worthy men.        986
7. iṉṉācey tārkkum iṉiyavē ceyyākkāl
eṉṉa payattatō cālpu.
Of perfection what is the gin
If it returns not joy for pin?        987
8. iṉmi oruvaṟku iḷivaṉṟu cālpueṉṉum
tiṇmiuṇ ṭākap peṟiṉ.
No shame there is in poverty
To one strong in good quality.        988
9. ūḻi peyariṉum tāmpeyarār cāṉṟāṇmikku
āḻi eṉappaṭu vār.
Aeons may change but not the seer
Who is a sea of virtue pure.        989
10. cāṉṟavar cāṉṟāṇmi kuṉṟiṉ irunilamtāṉ
tāṅkātu maṉṉō poṟi.
The world will not more bear its weight
If from high virtue fall the great.        990
----------

100. paṇpuṭimi - Courtesy

1. eṇpatattāl eytal eḷiteṉpa yārmāṭṭum
paṇpuṭimi eṉṉum vaḻakku.
To the polite free of access
Easily comes courteousness.        991
2. aṉpuṭimi āṉṟa kuṭippiṟattal ivviraṇṭum
paṇpuṭimi eṉṉum vaḻakku.
Humanity and noble birth
Develop courtesy and moral worth.        992
3. uṟuppottal makkaḷoppu aṉṟāl veṟuttakka
paṇpottal oppatām oppu.
Likeness in limbs is not likeness
It's likeness in kind courteousness.        993
4. nayaṉoṭu naṉṟi purinta payaṉuṭiyār
paṇpupā rāṭṭum ulaku.
The world appluds those helpful men
Whose actions are just and benign.        994
5. nakiyuḷḷum iṉṉātu ikaḻcci pakiyuḷḷum
paṇpuuḷa pāṭaṟivār māṭṭu.
The courteous don't even foes detest
For contempt offends even in jest.        995
6. paṇpuṭiyārp paṭṭuṇṭu ulakam atuiṉṟēl
maṇpukku māyvatu maṉ.
The world rests with the mannered best
Or it crumbles and falls to dust.        996
7. arampōlum kūrmiya rēṉum marampōlvar
makkaṭpaṇpu illā tavar.
The mannerless though sharp like file
Are like wooden blocks indocile.        997
8. naṇpāṟṟā rāki nayamila ceyvārkkum
paṇpāṟṟā rātal kaṭi.
Discourtesy is mean indeed
E'en to a base unfriendly breed.        998
9. nakalvallar allārkku māyiru ñālam
pakalumpāṟ paṭṭaṉṟu iruḷ.
To those bereft of smiling light
Even in day the earth is night.        999
10. paṇpilāṉ peṟṟa peruñ celvam naṉpāl
kalantīmi yāltirinta taṟṟu.
The wealth heaped by the churlish base
Is pure milk soured by impure vase.        1000
------------

101. naṉṟiyil celvam - Futile wealth

1. vittāṉvāy cāṉṟa perumporuḷ aḵtuṇṇāṉ
cettāṉ ceyakkiṭantatu il.
Dead is he with wealth in pile
Unenjoyed, it is futile.        1001
2. poruḷāṉām ellāmeṉṟu īyātu ivaṟum
maruḷāṉām māṇāp piṟappu.
The niggard miser thinks wealth is all
He hoards, gives not is born devil.        1002
3. īṭṭam ivaṟi icivēṇṭā āṭavar
tōṟṟam nilakkup poṟi.
A burden he is to earth indeed
Who hoards without a worthy deed.        1003
4. eccameṉṟu eṉeṇṇuṅ kollō oruvarāl
naccap paṭāa tavaṉ.
What legacy can he leave behind
Who is for approach too unkind.        1004
5. koṭuppatūum tuyppatūum illārkku aṭukkiya
kōṭiuṇ ṭāyiṉum il.
What is the good of crores they hoard
To give and enjoy whose heart is hard.        1005
6. ētam peruñcelvam tāṉṟuvvāṉ takkārkkoṉṟu
ītal iyalpilā tāṉ.
Great wealth unused for oneself nor
To worthy men is but a slur.        1006
7. aṟṟārkkoṉṟu āṟṟātāṉ celvam mikanalam
peṟṟāḷ tamiyaḷmūt taṟṟu.
Who loaths to help have-nots, his gold
Is like a spinster-belle grown old.        1007
8. naccap paṭātavaṉ celvam naṭuvūruḷ
naccu marampaḻut taṟṟu.
The idle wealth of unsought men
Is poison-fruit-tree amidst a town.        1008
9. aṉporīit taṟceṟṟu aṟanōkkātu īṭṭiya
oṇporuḷ koḷvār piṟar.
Others usurp the shining gold
In loveless, stingy, vicious hold.        1009
10. cīruṭic celvar ciṟutuṉi māri
vaṟaṅkūrn taṉiyatu uṭittu.
The brief want of the rich benign
Is like rinclouds growing thin.        1010
-----------

102. nāṇuṭimi - Sensitiveness to shame

1. karumattāl nāṇutal nāṇut; tirunutal
nallavar nāṇup piṟa.
To shrink from evil deed is shame
The rest is blush of fair-faced dame.        1011
2. ūṇuṭi eccam uyirkkellām vēṟalla
nāṇuṭimi māntar ciṟappu.
Food, dress and such are one for all
Modesty marks the higher soul.        1012
3. ūṉik kuṟitta uyirellām nāṇeṉṉum
naṉmi kuṟittatu cālpu.
All lives have their lodge in flesh
Perfection has its home in blush.        1013
4. aṇiaṉṟō nāṇuṭimi cāṉṟōrkku aḵtiṉṟēl
piṇiaṉṟō pīṭu naṭi.
Shame is the jewel of dignity
Shameless swagger is vanity.        1014
5. piṟarpaḻiyum tampaḻiyum nāṇuvār nāṇukku
uṟipati eṉṉum ulaku.
In them resides the sense of shame
Who blush for their and other's blame.        1015
6. nāṇvēli koḷḷātu maṉṉō viyaṉñālam
pēṇalar mēlā yavar.
The great refuse the wonder-world
Without modesty's hedge and shield.        1016
7. nāṇāl uyirit tuṟappar uyirpporuṭṭāl
nāṇtuṟavār nāṇāḷ pavar.
For shame their life the shame-sensed give
Loss of shame they won't outlive.        1017
8. piṟarnāṇat takkatu tāṉnāṇā ṉāyiṉ
aṟamnāṇat takkatu uṭittu.
Virtue is much ashamed of him
Who shameless does what others shame.        1018
9. kulañcuṭum koḷki piḻippiṉ nalañcuṭum
nāṇiṉmi niṉṟak kaṭi.
Lapse in manners injures the race
Want of shame harms every good grace.        1019
10. nāṇakat tillār iyakkam marappāvi
nāṇāl uyirmaruṭṭi yaṟṟu.
Movements of the shameless in heart
Are string-led puppet show in fact.        1020
------------

103. kuṭiceyalvaki - Promoting family welfare

1. karumam ceyoruvaṉ kitūvēṉ eṉṉum
perumiyiṉ pīṭuṭiyatu il.
No greatness is grander like
Saying "I shall work without slack".        1021
2. āḷviṉiyum āṉṟa aṟivum eṉiraṇṭiṉ
nīḷviṉiyāl nīḷum kuṭi.
These two exalt a noble home
Ardent effort and ripe wisdom.        1022
3. kuṭiceyval eṉṉum oruvaṟkut teyvam
maṭitaṟṟut tāṉmun tuṟum.
When one resolves to rise his race
Loin girt up God leads his ways.        1023
4. cūḻāmal tāṉē muṭiveytum tamkuṭiyit
tāḻātu uñaṟṟu pavarkku.
Who rise their races with ceaseless pin
No need for plan; their ends will gin.        1024
5. kuṟṟam ilaṉāyk kuṭiceytu vāḻvāṉic
cuṟṟamāc cuṟṟum ulaku.
Who keeps his house without a blame
People around, his kinship clim.        1025
6. nallāṇmi eṉpatu oruvaṟkut tāṉpiṟanta
illāṇmi ākkik koḷal.
Who rise their race which gave them birth
Are deemed as men of manly worth.        1026
7. amarakattu vaṉkaṇṇār pōlat tamarakattum
āṟṟuvār mēṟṟē poṟi.
Like duntless heroes in battle field
The home-burden rests on the bold.        1027
8. kuṭiceyvārkku illi paruvam maṭiceytu
māṉam karutak keṭum.
No season have they who rise their race
Sloth and pride will honour efface.        1028
9. iṭumpikkē koḷkalaṅ kollō kuṭumpattik
kuṟṟam maṟippāṉ uṭampu.
Is not his frame a vase for woes
Who from mishaps shields his house?        1029
10. iṭukkaṇkāl koṉṟiṭa vīḻum aṭuttūṉṟum
nallāḷ ilāta kuṭi.
A house will fall by a mishap
With no good man to prop it up.         1030
------------

104. uḻavu - Farming

1. cuḻaṉṟumērp piṉṉatu ulakam ataṉāl
uḻantum uḻavē tali.
Farming though hard is foremost trade
Men ply at will but ploughmen lead.        1031
2. uḻuvār ulakattārkku āṇiaḵtāṟṟātu
eḻuvāri ellām poṟuttu.
Tillers are linch-pin of mankind
Bearing the rest who cannot tend.        1032
3. uḻutuṇṭu vāḻvārē vāḻvārmaṟ ṟellām
toḻutuṇṭu piṉcel pavar.
They live who live to plough and eat
The rest behind them bow and eat.        1033
4. palakuṭi nīḻalum taṅkuṭikkīḻk kāṇpar
alakuṭi nīḻa lavar.
Who have the shade of cornful crest
Under their umbra umbrellas rest.        1034
5. iravār irappārkkoṉṟu īvar karavātu
kiceytūṇ māli yavar.
Who till and eat, beg not; nought hide
But give to those who are in need.        1035
6. uḻaviṉār kimmaṭaṅkiṉ illi viḻivatūum
viṭṭēmeṉ pārkkum nili.
Should ploughmen sit folding their hands
Desire-free monks too suffer wants.        1036
7. toṭippuḻuti kaḵcā uṇakkiṉ piṭitteruvum
vēṇṭātu cālap paṭum.
Moulds dried to quarter-dust ensure
Rich crops without handful manure.        1037
8. ēriṉum naṉṟāl eruiṭutal kaṭṭapiṉ
nīriṉum naṉṟataṉ kāppu.
Better manure than plough; then weed;
Than irrigating, better guard.        1038
9. cellāṉ kiḻavaṉ iruppiṉ nilampulantu
illāḷiṉ ūṭi viṭum.
If landsmen sit sans moving about
The field like wife will sulk and pout.        1039
10. ilameṉṟu acii iruppārik kāṇiṉ
nilameṉṉum nallāḷ nakum.
Fair good earth will lugh to see
Idlers pleading poverty.        1040
----------

105. nalkuravu - Poverty

1. iṉmiyiṉ iṉṉātatu yāteṉiṉ iṉmiyiṉ
iṉmiyē iṉṉā tatu.
What gives more pin than scarcity?
No pin pinches like poverty.        1041
2. iṉmi eṉoru pāvi maṟumiyum
immiyum iṉṟi varum.
The sinner Want is enemy dire
Of joys of earth and heaven there.        1042
3. tolvaravum tōlum keṭukkum tokiyāka
nalkuravu eṉṉum naci.
The craving itch of poverty
Kills graceful words and ancestry.        1043
4. iṟpiṟantār kaṇṇēyum iṉmi iḷivanta
coṟpiṟakkum cōrvu tarum.
Want makes even good familymen
Utter words that are low and mean.        1044
5. nalkuravu eṉṉum iṭumpiyuḷ palkurit
tuṉpaṅkaḷ ceṉṟu paṭum.
The pest of wanton poverty
Brings a trin of misery.        1045
6. naṟporuḷ naṉkuṇarntu colliṉum nalkūrntār
coṟporuḷ cōrvu paṭum.
The poor men's words are thrown away
Though from heart good things they say.        1046
7. aṟañcārā nalkuravu īṉṟatā yāṉum
piṟaṉpōla nōkkap paṭum.
Even the mother looks as stranger
The poor devoid of character.        1047
8. iṉṟum varuvatu kollō nerunalum
koṉṟatu pōlum nirappu.
The killing Want of yesterday
Will it pester me even to-day?        1048
9. neruppiṉuḷ tuñcalum ākum nirappiṉuḷ
yātoṉṟum kaṇpāṭu aritu.
One may sleep in the midst of fire
In want a wink of sleep is rare.        1049
10. tuppuravu illār tuvarat tuṟavāmi
uppiṟkum kāṭikkum kūṟṟu.
Renounce their lives the poor must
Or salt and gruel go to waste.        1050
------------

106. iravu - Asking

1. irakka irattakkārk kāṇiṉ karappiṉ
avarpaḻi tampaḻi yaṉṟu.
Demand from those who can supply
Default is theirs when they deny.        1051
2. iṉpam oruvaṟku irattal irantavi
tuṉpam uṟāa variṉ.
Even demand becomes a joy
When the things comes without annoy.        1052
3. karappilā neñciṉ kaṭaṉaṟivār muṉniṉṟu
irappumō rēer uṭittu.
Request has charm form open hearts
Who know the duty on their part.        1053
4. irattalum ītalē pōlum karattal
kaṉavilum tēṟṟātār māṭṭu.
Like giving even asking seems
From those who hide not even in dreams.        1054
5. karappilār viyakattu uṇmiyāl kaṇṇiṉṟu
irappavar mēṟkoḷ vatu.
The needy demand for help becuse
The world has men who don't refuse.        1055
6. karappiṭumpi illārik kāṇiṉ nirappiṭumpi
ellām oruṅku keṭum.
The pin of poverty shall die
Before the free who don't deny.        1056
7. ikaḻnteḷḷātu īvārik kāṇiṉ makiḻntuḷḷam
uḷḷuḷ uṭippatu uṭittu.
When givers without scorn impart
A thrill of delight fills the heart.        1057
8. irappāri illāyiṉ īrṅkaṇmā ñālam
marappāvi ceṉṟuvan taṟṟu.
This grand cool world shall move to and fro
Sans Askers like a puppet show.        1058
9. īvārkaṇ eṉṉuṇṭām tōṟṟam irantukōḷ
mēvār ilāak kaṭi.
Where stands the glory of givers
Without obligation seekers?        1059
10. irappāṉ vekuḷāmi vēṇṭum nirappuiṭumpi
tāṉēyum cālum kari.
The needy should not scowl at "No"
His need anothers' need must show.        1060
* Saint valluvar talks of two kinds of Asking:-
(1) Asking help for public cuses or enterprises.
(2) Begging when one is able to work and this is condemned.

107. iravaccam - Dread of beggary

1. karavātu uvantīyum kaṇṇaṉṉār kaṇṇum
iravāmi kōṭi yuṟum.
Not to beg is billions worth
E'en from eye-like friends who give with mirth.        1061
2. irantum uyirvāḻtal vēṇṭiṉ parantu
keṭuka ulakiyaṟṟi yāṉ.
Let World-Maker loiter and rot
If "beg and live" be human fate.        1062
3. iṉmi iṭumpi irantutīr vāmeṉṉum
vaṉmiyiṉ vaṉpāṭṭatu il.
Nothing is hard like hard saying
"We end poverty by begging".        1063
4. iṭamellām koḷḷāt takittē iṭamillāk
kālum iravollāc cālpu.
All space is small before the great
Who beg not e'en in want acute.        1064
5. teṇṇīr aṭupuṟki yāyiṉum tāḷtantatu
uṇṇali ṉūṅkiṉiyatu il.
Though gruel thin, nothing is sweet
Like the food earned by labour's sweat.        1065
6. āviṟku nīreṉṟu irappiṉum nāviṟku
iraviṉ iḷivantatu il.
It may be water for the cow
Begging tongue is mean anyhow.        1066
7. irappaṉ irappāri ellām irappiṉ
karappār iravaṉmiṉ eṉṟu.
If beg they must I beg beggers
Not to beg from shrinking misers.        1067
8. iraveṉṉum ēmāppil tōṇi karaveṉṉum
pārtākkap pakku viṭum.
The hapless bark of beggary splits
On the rock of refusing hits.        1068
9. iravuḷḷa uḷḷam urukum karavuḷḷa
uḷḷatūum iṉṟik keṭum.
The heart at thought of beggars melts;
It dies at repulsing insults.        1069
10. karappavarkku yāṅakoḷikkuṅ kollō irappavar
collāṭap pōom uyir.
The word "No" kills the begger's life
Where can the niggard's life be safe?        1070
----------

108. kayami - Meanness

1. makkaḷē pōlvar kayavar avaraṉṉa
oppāri yāṅkaṇṭatu il.
The mean seem men only in form
We have never seen such a sham.        1071
2. naṉṟaṟi vāriṟ kayavar tiruvuṭiyar
neñcattu avalam ilar.
The base seem richer than the good
For no care enters their heart or head.        1072
3. tēvar aṉiyar kayavar avaruntām
mēvaṉa ceytoḻuka lāṉ.
The base are like gods; for they too
As prompted by their desire do.        1073
4. akappaṭṭi āvārik kāṇiṉ avariṉ
mikappaṭṭuc cemmākkum kīḻ.
When the base meets a rake so vile
Him he will exceed, exult and smile.        1074
5. accamē kīḻkaḷatu ācāram eccam
avāuṇṭēl uṇṭām ciṟitu.
Fear forms the conduct of the low
Craving avils a bit below.        1075
6. aṟipaṟi aṉṉar kayavartām kēṭṭa
maṟipiṟarkku uytturikka lāṉ.
The base are like the beaten drum

109. takiyaṇaṅkuṟuttal - Beauty's dart

112. nalam puṉinturittal - Beauty extolled

113. kātaṟ ciṟappurittal - Love's excellence

1. pāloṭu tēṉkalan taṟṟē paṇimoḻi

tirunutaṟku illi iṭam.
Depart image in my pupil

ataṟkaṉṉaḷ nīṅku miṭattu.

114. nāṇut tuṟavurittal - Decorum defied

1. kāmam uḻantu varuntiṉārkku ēmam
maṭalallatu illi vali.

Except riding *'palmyra horse'.        1131

nāṇiṉi nīkki niṟuttu.
Pining body and mind lose shame

3. nāṇoṭu nallāṇmi paṇṭuṭiyēṉ iṉṟuṭiyēṉ
kāmuṟṟōr ēṟum maṭal.
Once I was modest and manly
My love has now Madal only.        1133
4. kāmak kaṭumpuṉal uykkumē nāṇoṭu
nallāṇmi eṉṉum puṇi.
Rushing flood of love sweeps away

māli uḻakkum tuyar.
Palm-ride and pangs of eventide

Her sea-like lust seeks not Madal!

Fools lugh at me before my eyes

115. alaraṟivuṟuttal - Public clamour

1. alareḻa āruyir niṟkum ataṉip

Rumour sustins my existence

116. pirivāṟṟāmi - Pangs of separation

1. cellāmi uṇṭēl eṉakkuri maṟṟuniṉ
valvaravu vāḻvārkku uri.
Tell me if you but do not leave,
Your quick return to those who live.        1151
2. iṉkaṇ uṭittavar pārval pirivañcum

4. aḷittañcal eṉṟavar nīppiṉ teḷittacol

Many survive pangs of parting

117. paṭarmelintiraṅkal - Wailing of pining love

118. kaṇvituppaḻital - Wasteful look for wistful love

1. kaṇtām kaluḻva tevaṉkolō taṇṭānōy

My lover's parting, I allowed

Climing it is begot through him

121. niṉintavar pulampal - Sad memories

122. kaṉavunili yurittal - Dream visions

uyaluṇmi cāṟṟuvēṉ maṉ.
I beg these fish-like dark eyes sleep

123. poḻutukaṇṭiraṅkal - Eventide sigh

124. uṟuppu nalaṉaḻital - Limbs languish

1. ciṟumi namakkuoḻiyac cēṭceṉṟār uḷḷi

My pale tearful eyes betray

125. neñcoṭu kiḷattal - Soliloquy

1. niṉittoṉṟu collāyō neñcē eṉittoṉṟum

Think of, O heart, some remedy
To cure this chronic malady.        1241
2. kātal avarila rākanī nōvatu
pētimi vāḻieṉ neñcu.
Bless O mind! you pine in vin

3. iruntuḷḷi eṉparital neñcē parintuḷḷal
pitalnōy ceytārkaṇ il.
O mind, why pine and sit moody?
Who made you so pale lacks pity.        1243
4. kaṇṇum koḷaccēṟi neñcē iviyeṉṉit
tiṉṉum avarkāṇa luṟṟu.
Take these eyes and meet him, O heart

Wrath is false, O heart, face-to face.

yāṉō poṟēṉiv viraṇṭu.
Off with love O mind, or shame

8. parintavar nalkāreṉṟu ēṅkip pirintavar

Without pity he would depart!
You sigh and seek his favour, poor heart!        1248
9. uḷḷattār kāta lavarāka uḷḷinī

Whom you think, mind to whom you go?        1249

126. niṟiyaḻital - Reserve lost

127. avarvayiṉ vitumpal - Mutual yearning

Will as guide he went to win

128. kuṟippaṟivuṟuttal - Feeling surmised

kāṇēṉ tavaṟal lavi.
When he's with me I see not fault
And nought but fault when he is not.        1286
7. uyttal aṟintu puṉalpāy pavarēpōl
poyttal aṟinteṉ pulantu.
To leap in stream which carries off
When lord is close to feign a huff.        1287
8. iḷittakka iṉṉā ceyiṉum kaḷittārkkuk
kaḷḷaṟṟē kaḷvaniṉ mārpu.
Like wine to addicts that does disgrace
Your breast, O thief, is for my embrace!        1288
9. malariṉum mellitu kāmam cilarataṉ
cevvi talippaṭu vār.
Flower-soft is love; a few alone
Know its delicacy so fine.        1289
10. kaṇṇiṉ tuṉittē kalaṅkiṉāḷ pullutal
eṉṉiṉum tāṉvitup puṟṟu.
She feigned dislike awhile but flew
Faster for embrace than I do.        1290
---------

130. neñcoṭupulattal - Chiding the heart

1. avarneñcu avarkkātal kaṇṭum evaṉneñcē
nīemakku ākā tatu.
You see, his heart is his alone;
Why not my heart be all my own?        1291
2. uṟāa tavarkaṇṭa kaṇṇum avaric
ceṟāareṉac cēṟieṉ neñcu.
O heart, you see how he slights me
Yet you clasp him as if friendly.        1292
3. keṭṭārkku naṭṭāril eṉpatō neñcēnī
peṭṭāṅku avarpiṉ celal.
You follow him at will. Is it
"The fallen have no friends" my heart?        1293
4. iṉiaṉṉa niṉṉōṭu cūḻvāryār neñcē
tuṉiceytu tuvvāykāṇ maṟṟu.
You won't sulk first and then submit
Who will then consult you, my heart?        1294
5. peṟāami añcum peṟiṉpirivu añcum
aṟāa iṭumpitteṉ neñcu.
Frets to gin and fears loss in gin
O my heart suffers ceaseless pin.        1295
6. taṉiyē iruntu niṉittakkāl eṉṉit
tiṉiya iruntatueṉ neñcu.
My itching mind eats me anon
As I muse on him all alone.        1296
7. nāṇum maṟantēṉ avarmaṟak kallāeṉ
māṇā maṭaneñciṟ paṭṭu.
forget shame but not his thought
In mean foolish mind I'm cught.        1297
8. eḷḷiṉ iḷivāmeṉṟu eṇṇi avartiṟam
uḷḷum uyirkkātal neñcu.
My heart living in love of him
Hails his glory ignoring blame.        1298
9. tuṉpattiṟku yārē tuṇiyāvār tāmuṭiya
neñcam tuṇiyal vaḻi.
Who support a man in grief
If lover's heart denies relief?        1299
10. tañcam tamarallar ētilār tāmuṭiya
neñcam tamaral vaḻi.
Why wonder if strangers disown
When one's own heart is not his own?        1300
-----------

131. pulavi - Bouderic

1. pullā tirāap pulatti avaruṟum
allalnōy kāṇkam ciṟitu.
Feign sulk; embrace him not so that
We can see his distress a bit.        1301
2. uppamin taṟṟāl pulavi atuciṟitu
mikkaṟṟāl nīḷa viṭal.
Sulking is the salt of love; but
Too much of it spoils the taste.        1302
3. alantāri allalnōy ceytaṟṟāl tammip
pulantārip pullā viṭal.
To leave the sulker unembraced
Is to grieve the one sorely grieved.        1303
4. ūṭi yavari uṇarāmi vāṭiya
vaḷḷi mutalarin taṟṟu.
To comfort not lady in pout
Is to cut the fading plant at root.        1304
5. nalattaki nallavarkku ēer pulattaki
pūaṉṉa kaṇṇā rakattu.
Pouting of flower-eyed has
To pure good mates a lovely grace.        1305
6. tuṉiyum pulaviyum illāyiṉ kāmam
kaṉiyum karukkāyum aṟṟu.
Love devoid of frowns and pets
Misses its ripe and unripe fruits.        1306
7. ūṭaliṉ uṇṭāṅkōr tuṉpam puṇarvatu
nīṭuvatu taṉṟukol eṉṟu.
"Will union take place soon or late?"
In lover's pout this leaves a doubt.        1307
8. nōtal evaṉmaṟṟu nontāreṉṟu aḵtaṟiyum
kātalar illā vaḻi.
What's the good of grieving lament
When concious lover is not present?        1308
9. nīrum niḻalatu iṉitē pulaviyum
vīḻunar kaṇṇē iṉitu.
Water delights in a shady grove
And sulking in souls of psychic love.        1309
10. ūṭal uṇaṅka viṭuvārōṭu eṉneñcam
kūṭuvēm eṉpatu avā.
My heart athirst would still unite
With her who me in sulking left!        1310
--------

132. pulavinuṇukkam - Feigned anger

1. peṇṇiyalār ellōrum kaṇṇiṉ potuuṇpar
naṇṇēṉ parantaniṉ mārpu.
I shrink to clasp you bosom lewd
To the gaze of all ladies exposed.        1311
2. ūṭi yiruntēmāt tummiṉār yāmtammi
nīṭuvāḻ keṉpākku aṟintu.
He sneezed while we went on sulking
Expecting me to say "live long".        1312
3. kōṭṭuppūc cūṭiṉum kāyum oruttiyik
kāṭṭiya cūṭiṉīr eṉṟu.
"For which lady?" she widely cries
While I adorn myself with flowers.        1313
4. yāriṉum kātalam eṉṟēṉā ūṭiṉāḷ
yāriṉum yāriṉum eṉṟu.
"I love you more than all" I sid
"Than whom, than whom?" she sulked and chid.        1314
5. immip piṟappil piriyalam eṉṟēṉāk
kaṇṇiṟi nīrkoṇ ṭaṉaḷ.
"In this life we won't part" I told
Her eyes at once with tears were filled.        1315
6. uḷḷiṉēṉ eṉṟēṉmaṟṟu eṉmaṟantīr eṉṟeṉṉip
pullāḷ pulattak kaṉaḷ.
I sid "I thought of you". She left
Her embrace crying "Oft you forget".        1316
7. vaḻuttiṉāḷ tummiṉē ṉāka aḻittaḻutāḷ
yāruḷḷit tummiṉīr eṉṟu.
I sneezed; she blessed; then changed and wept
"You sneezed now at which lady's thought?"        1317
8. tummuc ceṟuppa aḻutāḷ numaruḷḷal
emmi maṟittirō eṉṟu.
I repressed sneeze; she wept crying
"Your thoughts from me you are hiding".        1318
9. taṉṉi uṇarttiṉum kāyum piṟarkkunīr
innīrar ākutir eṉṟu.
I try to coax her and she remarks
"Your coaxing others thus this marks".        1319
10. niṉittiruntu nōkkiṉum kāyum aṉittunīr
yāruḷḷi nōkkiṉīr eṉṟu.
I think and gaze at her; she chides:
"On whom your thought just now abides?"
1320

133. ūṭaluvaki - Sulking charm

1. illi tavaṟavarkku āyiṉum ūṭutal
vallatu avaraḷikku māṟu.
He is flawless; but I do pout.
So that his loving ways show out.        1321
2. ūṭalil tōṉṟum ciṟutuṉi nallaḷi
vāṭiṉum pāṭu peṟum.
Fading first, love blooms and outlives
The petty pricks that pouting gives.        1322
3. pulattaliṉ puttēḷnāṭu uṇṭō nilattoṭu
nīriyin taṉṉā rakattu.
Is there a heaven like sulk beneath
Of hearts that join like water and earth?        1323
4. pulli viṭāap pulaviyuḷ tōṉṟumeṉ
uḷḷam uṭikkum paṭi.
In long pout after embrace sweet
A weapon is up to break my heart.        1324
5. tavaṟila rāyiṉum tāmvīḻvār meṉṟōḷ
akaṟali ṉāṅkoṉṟu uṭittu.
Though free form faults, one feels the charms
Of feigned release from lover's arms.        1325
6. uṇaliṉum uṇṭatu aṟaliṉitu kāmam
puṇartaliṉ ūṭal iṉitu.
Sweeter than meal is digestion
And sulk in love than union.        1326
7. ūṭalil tōṟṟavar veṉṟār atumaṉṉum
kūṭalil kāṇap paṭum.
The yielder wins in lover's pout
Reunited joy brings it out.        1327
8. ūṭip peṟukuvaṅ kollō nutalveyarppak
kūṭalil tōṉṟiya uppu.
Shall not our pouting agin give
The dew-browed joy of joint love?        1328
9. ūṭuka maṉṉō oḷiyiḻi yāmirappa
nīṭuka maṉṉō irā.
Sulk on O belle of shining jewels!
Prolong O night! our delight swells!        1329
10. ūṭutal kāmattiṟku iṉpam ataṟkiṉpam
kūṭi muyaṅkap peṟiṉ.
Bouderie is lovers' delight
Its delight grows when they unite        1330

                                Introduction

   This work presents in an easy style the life of MANIKKAVACAKAR alias
   VADAVURAR or THENNAVAR. He is the son of Sambupadacariyar an ardent
   leader of Saivism and Sivajnanavatiyar.
   The history of Manikkavacakar is also given in chapters 58-61 of the
   Thiruvilaiyadal Puranam of Paranjoti and in chapters 27-30 of
   Tiruvalavayudaiyar Tiruvilaiyadaj Puranam of Perumparra Puliyur Nambi.
   It is also contained in the Purana of Tiruvadavurar etc.
   This work mainly follows the Purana of Tiruvadavurar with some
   alterations, for example the well known incident that the Pandiya king
   caned the Lord is stated as though Vadavurar himself caned the Lord.
   The fact that Manikkavacakar cured the dumbness of the daughter of the
   Chola king is stated as though the daughter of Mechari, king of Madras,
   was cured of the same defect.
   This work is also conspicuous for the ommissions of some very famous
   incidents or events. The fact that Manikkavacakar defeated the
   Buddhists is not at all mentioned in this. But strange information is
   given that Manikkavacakar assumed the form of a deer.
   As could be seen from the above instances, this gives a slightly
   different account of the life of Manikkavacakar.
   The work is titled “Ammanai” merely due to the fact that the word
   “Ammanai” occurs frequently even though it does not conform to the
   definition of Ammanai in respect of contents or composition.
   AUTHOR.
   This work is said to be composed or written by one Sankaramurti, son of
   Nallur Nalla Thambi and the younger brother of Namaccivayar. Besides
   this, no information is found about the author, his literary and
   religious merits or qualifications.

   pēriṭṭup piḷḷaitaṉaip piriyamuṭa ṉēvaḷarttār :
   makaviṉ vaḷarcci
   pōṟṟi vaḷarttārkaḷ putumaiyuḷḷa maintaṉaiyum
   70. kātukutti vāḷiyiṭṭuk kaikkuveḷḷik kāppumiṭṭuk
   kālukkut taṇṭaiyiṭṭuk kaṇṇukku maiyumiṭṭuk
   kaṇṇēṟu vārāmaṟ kattūrip poṭṭumiṭṭut
   tūṅkumañcat toṭṭil taṉil cukamāy vaḷarttārkaḷ
   vaḷarukiṉṟa maintaṉ vayatiraṇ ṭāṉatumē
   75. tavaḻntu viḷaiyāṭit taraiyiṟ ṟirikiṟataik
   kaṇṭu talaivaṉavaṉ karuṇai mikapperuki
   maintaṉukkut tiruṭṭicuṟṟi maṭimītu vaittuvaittut
   talaivaṉ maṉamakiḻntu cantōṣa māyiruntāṉ
   cantōṣa māyirukkat talaivaṉ makaṉṟaṉakku
   80. vayatu morumūṉṟu vantatukāṇ ammāṉai
   tatti yaṭiyiṭṭut taṭumāṟit taḷḷāṭi
   āvaluṭaṉ pālaṉava ṉayyarmēṟ ṟāṉviḻavē
   vāri yeṭuttaṇaittu maḻalaiccoṟ ṟāṉkēṭṭup
   puḷakit tirupuyamum pūrittāṉ ammāṉai:
   cempoṉṉait tēviyiṭamītal
   85. ippaṭiyē yivarka ḷirukkiṉṟa nāḷaiyilē
   caivat talaivarukkut taṉṉaṟivu tāṉtōṉṟit
   taṉṟēvi miṉṉaṉaiyait tāṉē yarukaḻaittuc
   canniyāci yaṉṟoruvar tāṉaruḷip pōṉatoru
   cellāp paṇameṉṟu cempoṉ ṉirukkutukāṇ
   90. unta ṉuṭaiyamainta ṉuṟṟa āḷāṉataṉpiṉ
   intac ceḻumpoṉṉai yavarkkīyu meṉṟuraittār
   pattiramāy vaittiruntu pālaṉukku nīkoṭeṉṟu
   taṉṟēvi kaiyiṟ ṟāṉkoṭuttār ammāṉai
   taṉkaṇavaṉ ṟāṉkoṭukkat taiyalnallāḷ tāṉvāṅkip
   95. pattira māyppēṇip pataṉamāy vaittiruntāḷ
   vaittē yiruntavarkaḷ vāḻkiṉṟa nāḷaiyilē;
   tēviyiṉ kaṉavu
   kaṉṉiyavaḷ miṉṉaṉaiyāḷ kaṇtūṅkum vēḷaiyilē
   kaṇṭāḷ kaṉavatoṉṟu kārikaiyu mannēram
   ūrukku mēṟkuniṉṟa vuyarntatō rālamaram
   100. vēruṭaṉē cāyntu viḻakkaṇṭāḷ ammāṉai
   kaṇṭavuṭa ṉēyavaḷuṅ kalakkamuṟṟut tāṉeḻuntu
   taṉkaṇava ṉōṭētāṉuraittā ḷannēram
   kēṭṭut talaivaṉavaṉ kilēca mikapperukik
   kilēcattai viṭṭuk kiḷimoḻikku īturaittāṉ
   105. kōṭṭu mulaimātē kūṟakkēḷippoḻutu
   kaṇṭakaṉā meyyākāk kārikaiyē nīkēḷu
   kaḷavukaṇṭa poṉṉukkuc cavuṭiyuṇṭō kāciṉiyil
   maṉakkavalai vaiyātē maṟantuviṭu ippoḻutē
   ippaṭi yēyivarkaḷirukkiṉṟa nāḷaiyilē;
   caivat talaivaṉ maṟaivu
   110. caivat talaivaṉukkut tāṉcuramuṅ kaṇṭatuvē
   curamumatu pārāmal tōṣamikak kaṇṭatuvē
   vākkumatu kuḷaṟi vāṅkiyatē cīvaṉuntāṉ
   ceṉṟāṉ kāṇcaivat talaivaṉ civalōkam
   annēram miṉṉaṉaiyu malaṟi viḻuntaḻutu
   115. kutti viḻuntu kōveṉṟāḷ kompaṉaiyum
   mayirai virittalaṟi maṇmēl viḻuntaḻutu
   ayyō civaṉēnī yaniyāyañ ceytāyē
   maintaṉ ṟēṭṭuṇṇa makāppiriyamā yiruntīrē
   maintaṉ vaḷarumuṉṉē vāṉulakam pōṉīrē
   120. pātināḷ vāḻap pakavāṉ poṟukkalaiyē
   eṉṉa ceyvēṉeṉ ṟēṅkiviḻi nīrcoriya
   kutti viḻuntaḻutu kūveṉṟāḷ kompaṉaiyum :
   īmakkaṭaṉ
   annēram vētiyarka ḷaṉaivarun tāṅkūṭi
   miṉṉaṉaiyait tāṉamartti vētiyarka ḷellōrum
   125. caivat talaivaṉaiyum tāmē yeṭuttaṭakkic
   ceyyuñ caṭaṅkuñ ciṟappāy muṭittārkaḷ
   paṉireṇṭu nāṭcaṭaṅkaip pāṅkākat tāṉmuṭittuk
   karumāti yellāṅ kaṇakkuṭaṉē tāṉmuṭittār
   paṇṇuñ caṭaṅkellām paṇṇi muṭittapiṉpu
   130. miṉṉaṉaiyāḷ taṉmakaṉai virumpi vaḷarttiruntāḷ:
   makaṉ paḷḷi cērtal
   vaḷarukiṉṟa maintaṉukku vayataintu māṉatuvē
   aintām vayatutaṉṉi laḻakuḷḷa maintaṉukkuc
   ciṟukuṭumi vaikkaveṉṟu cintaitaṉi lēniṉaintu
   vētiyarait tāṉaḻaittu mikkamukūrt taṅkēṭṭu
   135. naṉmukūrtta vēḷaiyilē naṉṟāyk kuṭumivaittu
   paḷḷikku vaittārkaḷ paṉṉun tamiḻaṟiyat
   tāyārai yuntoḻutu taṉkuruvait teṇṭaṉiṭṭup
   paḷḷiyilē tāṉiruntu paṭittāṉkā ṇammāṉai
   paḷḷikkuc ceṉṟu paṭittuvarum nāḷaiyilē
   140. piḷḷaikaḷun tāṅkūṭip piramarā yaṉaippārttu
   takappaṉ ṟaṉaiyaṟiyāṉ taṟutalaiya ṉeṉṟu colli
   koṭṭi yaṭikkavavaṉ kūveṉ ṟaḻutukoṇṭu
   tāyāri ṭattil vantu tāṉītu ceppaluṟṟāṉ
   eṉṟaṉ ṟakappaṉeṅkē yirukkiṉṟār mātāvē!
   145. eṉṟaṉakku nīrītai yitamā yuraiyumeṉṟāṉ
   mainta ṉuraittavitai mātāvun tāṉkēṭṭu
   innāḷ varaiyilnī yippaṭiyē kēṭṭaṟiyāy
   iṉṟaikku nītāṉu mippaṭittāṉ kēṭṭateṉṉa
   colvāy makaṉēyeṉṟu tuyaramuṭa ṉēkēṭṭāḷ
   150. tāyā ravaḷkēṭkat tāṉētu colluvaṉām
   iṉṟaikku nāṉita mākap paḷḷiyilē
   pālarkaḷ tammuṭaṉē paṭittu yirukkaiyilē
   entaṉaiyu mavarpārttu ellōrun tāṅkūṭi
   takappaṉ ṟaṉaiyaṟiyāṉ taṟutalaiya ṉeṉṟucolli
   15. paiyaṉka ḷeṉṟalaiyil paṇpuṭaṉē tāṉaṭittār
   ākaiyiṉā litaiyaṟiya vēṇumeṉak kēṭṭēṉnāṉ
   eṉṟumakaṉ colla veḻuntiruntu mātāvum
   maintaṉait tāṉeṭuttu maṭimītiṟ ṟāṉirutti
   uccitaṉai mukarntu vuṭampellām tāṉṟaṭavi
   160. metta maṉamuṭainta melliyaḷu mappoḻutu
   inta vacaṉameṉ kātiṉilē kēṭkaveṉṟu
   īcuvaraṉu meṉ ṟalaiyi liṭṭaviti yippaṭiyō
   eṉṟu maṉatunontu yēntiḻaiyu mannēram
   maintaṉ mukantaṭavi maintaṉuk kīturaippāḷ
   165. uṉṟakappa ṉārceyti yuraikkakkē ḷeṉmakaṉē
   cāti maṟaiyōriṟ caivat talaivaṉeṉṟu
   perum pukaḻuṭaiyāṉ pirapalaṉava ṉuṉṟakappaṉ
   pāti vayataḷavum pālakaru millāmal
   mettat tavaciruntu meynōṉpum tāṉiruntu
   170. peruntavattiṉ payaṉākap pettārkā ṇuntaṉaiyum
   uṉṟēṭṭuk kāṇumuṉṉē untaṉayyar tāṉumavar
   ceṉṟār civalōkañ cērntārkā ṇuṉṟakappaṉ
   eṉṟucolla mātāvu miyalpuṭaṉē tāṉkēṭṭu
   eṉṟalaiyi liṭṭaveḻut titukāṇ mātāvē
   175. nīrcalikkak kāraṇameṉ niṉputalva ṉāṉirukkac
   calippataṉai viṭṭuviṭum tāyēnī reṉṟucolli
   metta maṉamnontu mīṇṭupōyp paḷḷiyilē
   carittu mukamvāṭit tāṉum paṭittuvantāṉ
   paṭikkum payalkaḷukkuḷ pattiraṭṭi puttiyuṭaṉ
   180. cakalakalai vallavaṉāyt tāṉpaṭittāṉ ammāṉai
   paṭittuvarum pālaṉukkup paṇpāyvaya tēḻākat
   tāyā ratuvaṟintu taṉmakaṉār pālaṉukkup
   pūṇūṟ kaliyāṇam poruttamuṭaṉ ceyvataṟku
   nāḷ mukūrttam pārttu nalamā yuraiyumeṉṟāḷ
   185. mukūrttamavar pārtturaikka moykuḻalā ḷappoḻutu
   pūṇūṟ kaliyāṇam poruttamuṭaṉ ceytaṉaḷām
   paṉireṇṭu vayataḷavum paṭittāṉkāṇ paḷḷiyilē
   vētam purāṇaṅkaḷum mikkanalla cāstiramum
   kāviyaṅka ḷuḷḷatellāṅ kaṟṟaṟintā ṉammāṉai
   190. cakalakalai vallavaṉāyt tāṉpaṭittāṉ pālakaṉum:
   tāyiṭam cempoṉ peṟutal
   patiṉaintām vayatutaṉṉiṟ pālaka ṉavaṉṟaṉatu
   tāyār taṉaittoḻutu tāṉītu kēṭkaluṟṟāṉ
   eṉṟakappa ṉārtāmu miṟantuviṭṭār mātāvē
   avartēṭun tiraviyaṅka ḷuṇṭō veṉakkēṭṭāṉ
   195. mainta ṉavaṉkēṭka mātāvu mīturaippāḷ
   uṉṟakappa ṉārtāṉu muyirpōkum vēḷaiyilē
   cellāp paṇamoṉṟu cikkeṉavē tāṉkoṭuttu
   uṉṟaṉ makaṉārkku uṟṟaporu ḷituveṉavē
   200. antap poruḷoṉṟu aṭaivā yirukkutappā
   uṉṟakappa ṉārtā ṉuṉakkuvaittup pōṉatoru
   cellāp paṇamoḻiyat tiraviyaṅkaḷ vēṟilaiyē
   yeṉṟu calippā yeṭutturaittāḷ mātāvum
   antamoḻi kēṭṭavuṭa ṉaṟivu mikattōṉṟi
   205. yoṉṟu maṟiyāma luraittāyē mātāvē
   cekamuḻutu mintaccem poṉṉukkut tāṉcariyō
   veṉṟu colittāṉu miṉpamuṭaṉ ṟāṉvāṅki
   yaṉpāy muṭintukoṇṭu maṟṟumava ṉīturaippāṉ:
   tīrttayāttirai
   aṉṉaiyarē nīrumeṅka ḷaiyarumāy vāḻkkaiyilē
   210. puttiraṉai vēṇṭip puricaṭaiyō ṉainōkki
   mettat tavamiruntu meynōṉpun tāṉiruntu
   eṉṉaiyun tāṉpeṟṟu iṉpamuṭaṉ ṟāṉirukka
   eṉṟēṭṭuk kāṇumuṉṉē yiṟantārē yeṅkaḷaiyar
   eṅkaḷaiyaṉ tāṉumippō yīcar patampeṟavē
   215. pāṇṭit talaṅkaḷ patinālu nāṉpārttuk

   tīṭcaiyun tāṉpeṟṟuc cellāp paṇañceluttic
   cokkaraiyun tāṉṟoḻutu cukamāy varuvataṟku
   eṉakku viṭaitantu iṉpamuṭaṉ tāṉaṉuppu
   220 meṉṟucollit tāyā riṇaiyaṭiyaip pūṇṭukoṇṭu
   eṉṉuṭaiya mātāvē yeṉakkuviṭai tārumeṉṟāṉ
   mainta ṉuraittamoḻi mātāvun tāṉkēṭṭu
   makaṉuṭaiya vallamaiyai maṉattil mikavaṟintu
   īcaṉ malaraṭikku yicaintiruppā yeṉmakaṉē
   225 pūlōkat tārtoḻavē puṇṇiyaṉāy nīyiruppāy
   pōyvaruka veṉṟu porunta viṭaikoṭuttāḷ.
   tāyiṉ viṭaivāṅkit tāṉ naṭantāṉ ammāṉai
   ceṉṟāṉ kāṇappoḻutu cīkkiramāyk kāḷainakar
   kāḷainā yakaraiyum karuttāṉa cokkaraiyum
   230 cōmanā yakaraiyun toḻutumikac cēvaiceytu
   āṅkumō rācāṉai yaṭivaṇaṅkit teṇṭaṉiṭṭuk
   kurutaṭciṇaiyum vaittuk kumpiṭṭāṉaṭi vaṇaṅki
   kēṭṭā ṉupatēcaṅ kirupaiyuṭaṉ collumeṉṟu
   taṭciṇaiyaip pārttavavar cellā teṉavētāṉ
   235 vāṅki muṭintukoṇṭu maṟṟumava ṉītuceytāṉ
   uttara kōcamaṅkai yuṭaṉē yavaṉumvantu
   maṅkaipāka nātaraiyum malarppātañ cēvaiceytu
   aṅkumō rācāṉai yaṭivaṇaṅkit teṇṭaṉiṭṭut
   taṭciṇaiyum vaittuc ceppumupa tēcameṉṟāṉ
   240 upatēcañ colla vuṟṟaceḻum poṉṉatutāṉ

   ivar namakku ācā ṉillaiyeṉṟu tāṉniṉaintu
   vāṅki muṭintukoṇṭu maṟṟumava ṉītuceytāṉ
   vantāṉkāṇ āvuṭaiyār vāḻuñciva ṉāriṭattil
   245 civaṉār taṉaiyuntaṉ cintaitaṉi lēniṉaintu

   taṭciṇaiyai vaittuc ceppumupa tēcameṉṟār

   250 nelvēli nātaraiyum neñcil mikaviruttik

   255 upatēcañ colla vuṟṟa ceḻum poṉṉatuvum

   tiruvēṭa katti lurai civaṉāraip pūcaipaṇṇi
   yaṅkumoru ācāṉai yaṭivaṇaṅkit taṇṭaṉiṭṭut
   taṭciṇaiyai vaittuc ceppumupa tēcameṉṟāṉ
   260 upatēcañ ceyya vuṟṟaceḻum poṉṉatutāṉ
   cellāteṉa vēyācāṉ tirumpak koṭuttu viṭṭār
   ācāṉ koṭukkavavaṉ vāṅki muṭintukoṇṭu
   yivar namakkā cāṉillai yeṉṟumikat tāṉnaṭantu
   āṭāṉai nātaraiyu maparañci yammaṉaiyum
   265 iruvartam pātamatai yiṉpamuṭaṉ pūcaipaṇṇi
   taṭciṇaiyum vaittuc ceppumupa tēcameṉṟāṉ
   aṅkumavar cempoṉṉaic cellā teṉakkoṭuttār
   tirumpa vataivāṅkit tīramāy muṭintukoṇṭu
   270 vantāṉpuṉal vācalmakā vīcāṉaṉ cannitikkē
   paḻampōti nātaraiyum pāṅkuṭaṉē pūcaipaṇṇi
   aṅkumoru ācāṉai yaṭivaṇaṅkit teṇṭaṉiṭṭut

   aṅkumavar cempoṉṉaic cellā teṉakkoṭukka
   275 vāṅki muṭintukoṇṭu maṟṟumava ṉītu ceytāṉ

   280 irāma nāyakaraiyum paruvata varttaṉiyaiyum

   taṭciṇaiyum vaittuc ceppumupa tēcameṉṟāṉ

   285 cellā teṉavē tirumpa vavarkoṭukka

   pūvāṇa liṅkattiṉ poṟpātañ cēvai ceytu
   aṅkumoru ācāṉai yaṭi vaṇaṅkit taṇṭaṉiṭṭut
   290 taṭciṇaiyai vaittuc ceppumupa tēcameṉṟāṉ

   ēkiṉār varuvārō veṉṟumantap paṭṭaṇattil
   1770 collukiṟa pērka ḷuṇṭō veṉakkēṭṭār:
   āvuṭaiyār maturaicceyti yuraittal
   antamoḻi kēṭṭa vētiyaru mappoḻutu
   teṉṉavaṉaip pārttuc cirittītu ceppaluṟṟār
   paṭṭaṇattiluḷḷa palacaṉaṅka ḷellōrum
   pāṇṭiyaṉār vācaṟ parivāra mattaṉaiyum
   1775 iṉṟu piṟanta viḷaṅkuḻantai tāṉmutalāyt
   teṉṉavaṉār tammaippōl tēcattiṟ kiṭṭātu .
   pōṉārvaru vārōveṉap pulampuvā rellōrum
   iṉṟu tavikkutukāṇ niṉaivu taṭumāṟip,
   āvuṭaiyār teṉṉavaṉai maturaikkaḻaittal
   pōṉālnī yippoḻutu putumaiyuḷḷa pāṇṭiyaru
   1780 tēcavati kārañ celuttumeṉṟu tāmuraippār
   vārīrō nāmpōvōm maturaitaṉak kippoḻutu;
   teṉṉavaṉ maṟuppu
   eṉṟucollak kēṭṭa teṉṉavaṉu mīturaippāṉ
   oṉṟu maṟiyāma luraittīrē vētiyarē
   civañāṉi yentaṉukkuc ciṟṟācai vēṇṭāṅkāṇ
   1785 kuruntaṭiyi līcuvararuṅ koṭutta varappaṭiyē
   poṉṉam palattaiyar poṟpātam viṭṭorunāḷ
   varuvēṉō vārēṉnāṉ vārttaiyiṉi yuraiyātīr
   intamoḻi taṉṉai yippoḻutē viṭṭuviṭum.
   eṉṟucollat teṉṉavaṉā rīturaippār vētiyarum:
   āvuṭaiyār teṉṉavaṉaik koṇṭu kōvaipāṭuvittal
   1790 īcar malaraṭiyai yitayattil tāṉvaittu
   īcarmēṟ kōvai eḻutanīr vallavarō
   eṉṟē yavarkēṭka yiyampuvār teṉṉavarum
   ummaippōl vētiyarka ḷeḻutavē yuṇṭāṉāl
   kōvainā mippoḻutē colluvō meṉṟuraittār
   1795 uraitta vataikkēṭṭa veḻiṉmaṟaiyōṉ tāṉumappō
   ippoḻutē nīrcoṉṉā leḻutuvō meṉṟuraittāṉ
   antamoḻi kēṭṭut teṉṉavaṉu mappoḻutu
   āṉanta māccariya meṉavē maṉamakiḻntu
   īcarmalarp pātamatai yitayatti lēniṉaintu
   1800 kōvaiyavar pāṭak kōmā ṉeḻutiṉarām
   āvuṭaiyār tirukkōvaiyaic ciṟṟampalattuḷ vaittal
   eḻuti muṭittavaru meṇṇilakkan tāṉpōṭṭup
   puttakamuñ cērttup peruṅkayiṟun tāṉpōṭṭut
   tillaic civaṉārtam kaiyeḻut tīteṉavē
   kaṭaiyēṭṭiṟ ṟāṉeḻuti vaittār citamparaṉār
   1805 eḻutiṉa puttakattai yītuceytā rammāṉai
   pañca kēntirap paṭitaṉilē puttakattai
   vaittuk katavaṭaittu vantārkā ṇammāṉai.
   āvuṭaiyār maṟaivu
   vanta maṟaiyōṉum māyamāyt tāṉmaṟaintār
   teṉṉavaṉu mappoḻutu tēṭi yaluttavaṉāy
   īcaratu māya mituveṉavē tāṉaṟintu
   1810 eṉṉainīr cōtaṉaikaḷ ceyyaveṉa vantīrō
   īcuvararē ummuṭaiya māyaiyī teṉṟucollip
   pōṉaviṭaṅ kāṇāmal pōtap paritavittu
   niṉṟuneṭu mūcceṟintu niṉṟārkā ṇammāṉai
   eṉṉai mayaṅkavait tēkiṉī rīcuvararē
   1815 ummuṭaiya cīrpāta morunāḷum nāṉmaṟavēṉ
   eṉṉuṭaiya caṉaṉamatai yīṭēṟṟik koḷḷumaiyā
   eṉṟu pulampi yivarirukkum vēḷaiyilē;
   mūvāyiravar tirukkōvaiyaik kaṇṭu tikaittal
   tillai mūvāyiram pēri loruvaṉavaṉ
   tillaic citampararkkuc ciṟappākap pūcaipaṇṇa
   1820 vēṇumeṉṟu colli viraivākat tāṉumvantāṉ
   vantu katavutaṉai vākāyt tiṟantiṭavē
   pañca kēntirap paṭitaṉilē puttakattaik
   kaṇṭa vuṭaṉēyavaṉ kalakkamuṟ ṟītucolvāṉ
   kaḷavāṇi vantiṅkē kaḷavupō yirukkuteṉṟu
   1825 eṇṇi yavaṉmaṉati lippuṟattē vantavaṉum
   tiṟanta katavaṭaittut tīvaramā yeṇṇiyavaṉ
   tillaimū vāyirarkkuc cēticoṉṉā ṉappoḻutu
   antamoḻi kēṭṭu aṉaivōru maṅkuceṉṟu
   katavutaṉait tiṟantu kaṇṭārkaḷ puttakattai
   1830 inta nalla puttakantā ṉivviṭatti luḷḷatalla
   māyamā yituviṅkē vantirukku teṉṟucollip
   puttakattait tāṉeṭuttup pēṇiyavar pārttapiṉpu
   avaiyaṭakka meṉpataṟ karttamveḷi yākāmal
   niṉṟu mayaṅki niṉaivuvan tīturaippār
   1835 teṉṉavaṉā rippoḻutu civaṉā raṭumaiyallō
   avarainām pōykkēṭṭā larttamveḷi yāmeṉavē,
   mūvāyiravar māṇikkavācakaraik kāṇutal
   ellōrun teṉṉavarai vantukaṇṭā rammāṉai
   kaṇṭumavar tammōṭu karuma muraikkaluṟṟār
   kaṇṇārak kaṇṭataiyuṅ karuṇaiyuḷḷa teṉṉavaṉār
   1840 īcuvaraṉār māyamitai yēteṉ ṟeṭutturaippōm
   vētiyaṉār tammiṭatti liruntatoru puttakantāṉ
   ivarkaiyiṟ ṟāṉuḷatu vitumāya meṉṟeṇṇi
   tillaimū vāyiraraic ciṟappākat tāṉpārttu

   tillai mūvāyirava rellām varavaḻaittuc

   poruḷituvē nīrkāṇum puṇṇiyarē yeṉṟucollic
   capaiyaiyavar kaikāṭṭic ceṉṟār capaiyiṉuḷḷē
   capaitaṉilē tāṉceṉṟu cāyucciya patamaṭaintār
   teṉṉavaṉaik kāṇāmal tillai mūvāyiravar
   1855 tēṭi yaluttavarkaḷ cintai yatukalaṅki
   civaṉāraip pōṟṟic cintai teḷivuṟavē
   īcuvararē nāṅka ḷaṉaivōru mippoḻutu
   cēraviṟan tiṭuvōñ cīkkiramāyk kāṭṭumeṉṟār
   appoḻutu civaṉāru mavarkaṭamaip pārttu

   vīṇukku nīṅka ḷiṟakkavanta kāriyameṉ
   eṉṟuciva ṉāraruḷa vēkiṉar tampatikku.

   AN APPEAL.
   The Government Oriental Manuscripts Library, Madras, is unique in point
   of variety and vastness of collections covering a very wide field of
   Indian Literature in Sanskrit, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam,
   Mahratti, Arabic, Persian and Urdu. The dates of several of the
   manuscripts go back to many hundreds of years. From an academic point
   of view, these collections contain some very interesting specimens of
   Literary, Scientific, Historical and Artistic importance. A large
   number of them have not yet been printed and some of them are available
   only in this Library. With a view to placing these manuscripts in the
   hands of the public in a printed form, the Government of Madras were
   pleased to sanction the starting of a BULLETIN under the auspices of
   the Government Oriental Manuscripts Library. For the present only two
   numbers are issued in a year. They contain critical editions of
   original works in Sanskrit, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam,
   Mahratti, Persian and Urdu. The annual subscription is only Rs. 4
   inland and 8 shillings foreign. Scholars, Patrons of Culture,
   Universities, Public Libraries and Educational Institutions are
   requested to enrol themselves as susbscribers for this Bulletin and
   thus encourage the cause of Oriental Studies and Culture.

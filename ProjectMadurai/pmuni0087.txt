uricciṟappup pāyiram
nilimaṇṭila āciriyappā
kaṇṇutaṟ kaṭavu ḷaṇṇalaṅ kuṟumuṉi
muṉivēṉ muruka ṉeṉavivar mutaliya
tiruntumoḻip pulava raruntami ḻāynta

kōtil coṉmaka ṇōtakak kiṭattaliṟ
pāṭiya cāṉṟavar pīṭunaṉ kuṇara
mikipaṭu poruḷi nakipaṭu puṉcoliṟ
ṟantiṭi maṭutta kantitaṉ piḻippum       10
eḻutiṉar piḻippu meḻutturu vokkum
pakutiyiṉ vanta pāṭakar piḻippum
oruṅkuṭaṉ kiṭanta vovvāp pāṭam
tiruntiya kāṭciyōr cevimutal vetuppaliṟ
ciṟṟaṟi viṉarkkun teṟṟeṉat tōṉṟa       15

nīṇilaṅ kaṭantōṉ ṟāṭoḻu marapiṟ

1. tirumāl
aru maṟip poruḷ
āyiram viritta aṇaṅkuṭi arun tali
tī umiḻ tiṟaloṭu muṭimici aṇavara,
māyuṭi malar mārpiṉ, mi il vāl vaḷi mēṉic
cēy uyar paṇimici eḻil vēḻam ēntiya,
vāy vāṅkum vaḷi nāñcil, oru kuḻi oruvaṉi;       5

ēval uḻantami kūṟum,
nā val antaṇar aru maṟip poruḷē.
amar veṉṟa kaṇi
iṇipiri aṇi tuṇi paṇi eri puri
viṭar iṭu cuṭar paṭar polam puṉi viṉimalar       15
neri tiraṭeri puri taṉa miku taṉa muraṇ miku
kaṭaṟaru maṇiyaṭum muttu yātta nēraṇi
neṟi ceṟi veṟi uṟu muṟal viṟal vaṇaṅku aṇaṅku vil
tār aṇi tuṇi maṇi veyil uṟaḻ eḻil pukaḻ alar mārpiṉ
eri vayira nuti eṟi paṭi eruttu mali ivar naviyiṉiṟ       20
ṟuṇi paṭal iṉa maṇi veyil uṟaḻ eḻil nakku
imi iruḷ akala muṟu kiṟuku puri oru puri nāḷmalar
malar ilakiṉa vaḷar paritiyiṉ oḷi maṇi mārpu aṇi
maṇam mika nāṟu uruviṉa viri vaḷi miku kaṭu vici
uṭuvuṟu tali niri itaḻ aṇi vayiṟiriya amararip       25
pōreḻuntu uṭaṉṟu irittu uriiya tāṉavar
ciram umiḻ puṉal poḻipu iḻintu uram
utirpu atirpu alam toṭā amar veṉṟa kaṇi.

ceru mēmpaṭṭa ceyir tīr aṇṇal!       30

aṉṉa marapiṉ aṉiyōy! niṉṉi
iṉṉaṉ eṉṟu urittal emakku evaṉ eḷitu?       35
arumi naṟku aṟiyiṉum, ārvam niṉvayiṉ
perumiyiṉ vallā yām ivaṇ moḻipavi
melliya eṉāa veṟāatu, alli am
tiru maṟu mārpa! nī aruḷal vēṇṭum.
tuti moḻikaḷ
viṟal miku viḻuc cīr antaṇar kākkum       40
aṟaṉum, ārvalarkku aḷiyum, nī;
tiṟaṉ ilōrt tiruttiya tītu tīr ciṟappiṉ
maṟaṉum, māṟṟalarkku aṇaṅkum, nī;
am kaṇ ēr vāṉattu aṇi nilāt tikaḻtarum
tiṅkaḷum, teṟu katirk kaṉaliyum, nī;       45
ain tali uyiriya aṇaṅkuṭi arun tiṟal
mintuṭi oruvaṉum, maṭaṅkalum, nī;
nalam muḻutu aḷiiya pukar aṟu kāṭcip
pulamum, pūvaṉum, nāṟṟamum, nī;
valaṉ uyar eḻiliyum, māka vicumpum,       50

ataṉāl,

2.9 tacik kaṭṭiyip peṇṇuruvamitta paṭalam (699 - 733)

699

marumalar cumantu tēṉvaḻin toḻuku

maṇippuya mukammatu napiyun
teritarun tīṉi ṉeṟimuṟi yavaruñ

cintiyiṟ kaḷippoṭuñ ciṟappa
variyameyp poruḷi muṟimuṟi vaṇaṅki

yaṟṟiyiṟ kaṭaṉkaḻit tamarar
tiruvaṭi paravat tammuyi raṉiya

celvarō ṭuṟintiṭuṅ kāli.

2.9.1

700

pūraṇak kaḷapa kaṉataṉa maṭavār

porutirik kavarikā lacippa
vāraṇi muraca matirtarac cīṟu

maṭaṅkaliṉ koṭimuṉaṅ kulava
vāraṇat talivar maruṅkiṉiṟ piriyā

taracarka ḷuṭaṉvarat toliyāk
kāraṇak kuriciṉ mukammati ṉiṭattil

vantaṉaṉ hapīpeṉu maracaṉ.

2.9.2

701

cemmalarp patattil veṇkatir kulavuñ

ceḻumaṇi muṭicirañ cērttit
tammiṉat tavarka ḷūṭaṉcalā murittut

takkatō riṭattuniṉ ṟavaṉi
vammeṉat tiruvā yuriyaruḷ koṭuttu

mukammatu maruṅkiṉi lirutti
vemmiyi ṉamutak kaṉiyeṉuṅ kalimā

viḷampuka veṉavirit turittār.

2.9.3

702

naṉṟeṉap pukaḻntu maṉaṅkaḷit teḻuntu

narapati timaṣkiṉuk karacaṉ
veṉṟiko ḷaracē yiṉamoru vacaṉam

viṉavutal vēṇṭumeṉ ṉiṭatti
leṉṟava ṉurippa mukammatu napiyu

miṉpuṟu muṟuvalkoṇ ṭiṉitāyt
tuṉṟumeṉ maṉattiṟ ṟerintuṉ makaṭaṉ

ṟolviṉi tuippataṟ keṉṟār.

2.9.4

703

āṇṭaki yuritta putumoḻi naṟuntē

ṉakattiṉiṟ pukuntuṭal kaḷittu
vēṇṭunaṟ patavi paṭittaṉaṉ ciṟiyēṉ

viḷittiṭum pavakkaṭa ṟolittēṉ
kāṇṭakāp putumi yaṉittiyun terintēṉ

kaṭikama ḻaṇimalarp patattit
tīṇṭavum peṟṟē ṉiṉiyarum poruḷoṉ

ṟiliyeṉa vurittaṉaṉ ṟiṟalōṉ.

2.9.5

704

putiyavaṉ ṟūtar mukammatun timaṣkip

purantiṭu maruntavat tavaṉu
maturameṉ moḻiyā laḷavaḷā yuḷaṅkaṇ

makiḻntiṉi tirukkumak kāli
kakaṉiḻin tariya peruñciṟi yoṭukkik

kaṭitiṉiṟ kaṇṇimit tiṭumuṉ
cekatalat tuṟinta napiyiṭat tuvantār

terimaṟi koṭujipu rīlē.

2.9.6

705

maruṅkiṉi levarkkun tōṉṟiṭā tuṟintu

vallavaṉ calāmeṭut tiyampip
peruṅkulam viḷakku mukammati nōkkip

piṟaḻnturut tōṉṟilāt taciyi
neruṅkuveṇ koṭikkaḵ pāviṭat tēki

nirimayirp pōrviyāṉ mūṭi
yaruṅkatirkkala cat tāpusam satti

ṉariyanīr karaṅkoṭu tuittē.

2.9.7

706

iṟivaṉi nōkkit tuāviran tiṉirē

lilaṅkurut tōṉṟumeṉ ṟicittuc
ciṟiniṟan tōṉṟā tamarula kataṉil

jipuṟayī lēkiya piṉṉar
kaṟiniṟaṅ kulavuñ ceḻuṅkatir vaṭivēṟ

karatala mukammatu nayiṉā
raṟimura catirat timaṣkiṟi yavaṉu

meḻuntaṉa rariyakaḵ pāvil.

2.9.8

707

vāṉava riṟiyō ṉaruṭpaṭi yamitta

makkamā nakariyi ṉāppaṇ
kāṉalar potuḷuṅ kakupattul lāviṟ

kaṭimatiṭ puṟattoru pālil
tīṉava ruṭaṉu maṇipeṟa viruntu

cevviyaṉ hapīpiṉi nōkki
yīṉamaṟ ṟuṉatu makaveṉun taciyi

yivaṇkoṭu varukaveṉ ṟicittār.

2.9.9

708

antanaṉ moḻikēṭ ṭaṭaṟpaṭi mālik

karuḷiya hapīpeṉu maracaṉ
cintiyiṟ kaḷittu maruṅkuniṉ ṟavarit

tiṇṇiya taciyiṉik koṇarkeṉ
ṟuntiṭa vurippa vōṭiṉar cilava

ruṟuporuṭ potinteṉap potintu
tantapeṭ ṭakatti ṉoṭumeṭut tuvantār

taṉuviṭu carattiṉuṅ kaṭitiṉ.

2.9.10

709

muṉṉuṟap paṇitta peṭṭakat tirunta

mutirtacik kaṭṭiyi yeṭuttu
maṉṉiya kuriciṉ mukammatu napimuṉ

vittaṉar curikuḻaṟ karuṅkaṇ
miṉṉeṉap piṟaḻu maṭantiya ruṭaṉum

viritirip pacuṅkaṭa laṉiya
vannakar mākka ḷaṉivarum virivi

ṉaṭuttati cayittiṭa vaṉṟē.

2.9.11

710

kaṭalkiṭan tuṭutta perumpuvi yiṭattiṟ

kaṇṭaṟi yāpperuṅ kāṭci
maṭalaviḻ kamala vāvicūḻ timaṣku

maṉṉavaṉ makaveṉap piṟantu
miṭaluṟuṅ katirvāṇ mukammatu poruṭṭāl

viḷikkuṅkā raṇattiṉuk kirunta
vuṭaleṉun tacita ṉuyiralā tiyintō

ruṟuppeṉum vaṭivupeṟ ṟilavē.

2.9.12

711

poruntuṟuṅ kuṟippoṉ ṟaṇuviṉu milatāyp

poruvaṟum putumiyi ṉetiri
ṉiruntavat taciyi mukammatu nōkki

yiṟiva ṉaruḷeṉak kuṟittut
tiruntuṟa mayiriṉ pōrviyiṟ pōrttuc

ceṅkarat tariyanī rēnti
maruntiṉu mamara ramutiṉuñ ciṟappa

makiḻvoṭun tuittuniṉ ṟaṉarāl.

2.9.13

712

maṇiyeṉac ciṟantu malariṉmeṉ miyavā

maḻicceḻuṅ karaṅkaḷi yēnti
yaṇitarap pōṟṟik kaṉintaṟa nekiḻnta

vakattiṉi lariyanā yakaṉit
taṇivilā tuyarttip palapala pukaḻāṟ

cāṟṟinaṉ ṉeṟimuṟi tavaṟāp
paṇipaṇin tirantār tīṉili niṟuttum

patumameṉ patamukam matuvē.

2.9.14

713

pūtalat tevarkku maṟineṟi putukkip

poruvaruñ cuvaṉanā ṭaḷippat
tūteṉa vutitta mukammatiṉ tuāvit

tuyyava ṉuṟakkapū lākkap
pātakak kupirar maṉampati patippap

palaṉpaṭāp peruntacit tiraṭci
tītaṟat tōṉṟu mavayavañ ciṟappat

teriviyiṉ ṟiruvuru veṭutta.

2.9.15

714

variyiḻi mayirppō ruviyeṉuṅ kariya

valliru ḷiṭiyeḻu matipōl
virikaṭaṟ perunī ruṇṭucū luḷinta

vicumpiṭi yutittamiṉ kulampō
lariyiṉa naṟavuṇ ṭalampukuṅ kumattā

raṇipuya mukammatiṉ kalimāt
teritarap pavaḷa vitaḻtiṟan tōtic

ceṟitaru maviyiṭat teḻuntāḷ.

2.9.16

715

viṇṇakat tarampik kulattiṉum vaṭivāy

virikaṭaṉ makaḷiṉum viyappāy
maṇṇakat tuṟiyu meḻuvakip paruva

maṭantiya raṇintiṭu maṇiyāyk
kaṇṇiṉuk kaṭaṅkā taḻakiṉic cumanta

kaṉiyuru veṭuttakāṭ ciyatāyp
peṇṇalaṅ kaṉintu nalaṉeḻil piṟaṅkap

perunilat teḻuntuniṉ ṟaṉaḷē.

2.9.17

716

maṅkuliṟ peruki viṭattiṉuṅ karuki

variyaṟa liṉumiṉu miṉuttut
taṅkiya yitaḻit tiraḷiṉun tiraṇṭu

civalat toṭariṉun taḻittuk
koṅkuṟak kuḻaṉṟu neṟittuvārt toḻukik

kuvalayat tiḷiñarkaṇ vaḻukka
veṅkaṇaṉ ṉayiṉār muṉṉinā ḷaḻitta

viruḷiṉu miruṇṭamik kuḻalāḷ.

2.9.18

717

kīṟṟiḷam piṟiyuṅ kaṇicciyiṉ vaḷivuṅ

kiḷarntacev vakattiṉmeṉ malarun
tōṟṟiṭat tōṟṟi viḷaṅkunaṉ malarun tōṟṟiṭat

tōṟṟi viḷaṅkunaṉ ṉutalāḷ cuṭarumuḷ vāraṇat talaku
māṟṟarun taṉucuṅ karuṅkoṭi yetirvu

māṟṟimik kaṭkaṭaṟ kariyiṉ
mēṟṟikaḻ kariya pavaḷameṉ koḻuntāy

viḷaṅkiya ceḻumpuru vattāḷ.

2.9.19

718

maṭaṟkuḻi kiḻittut taṭakkuḻal kuḻittu

variyaḷi yiṉacciṟip paṭuttik
kaṭaṟkuḷan tēṟā talitarac ceytu

kaṇiyayil kaṭipaṭak kaṟuvi
viṭattiṉi yaravap paṭattiṭi paṭutti

mīṉiṉam payappaṭat tāḻttit
tiṭakkatir vaṭivā ḷeṉakkoli paḻakic

cevantari paṭarntami viḻiyāḷ.

2.9.20

719

vaḷḷiyi vāṭṭi yūcali yacittu

maṇṇeḻi lāṭava ruyirik
koḷḷikoṇ ṭuṭalaṅ kuḻippataṟ kaṉṟō

kuḻiyeṉum peyariṭuṅ kuḻiyā
ḷeḷḷiyuñ ciṟanta kumiḻiyum vācat

tiṉiyacaṇ pakamalar taṉiyum
viḷḷaruṅ kāṉat tiṭiyalar paṭutti

vilaṅkiṭa vilaṅkiya kumiḻāḷ.

2.9.21

720

veṇṉilāt taraḷa nakiniri potintu

virintacem pavaḷamō vilavō
vaṇṇavāyc ceḻuñcē tāmpaliṉ malarō

vaṭivuṟu toṇṭiyaṅ kaṉiyō
veṇṇinōk kiṉaruk kuvamiyi ṉaṭaṅkā

teḻilkuṭi yiruntamu toḻukip
paṇṇelāñ cuvaṟṟi yāṭava rirukaṭ

pārviyiṟ cevantamel litaḻāḷ.

2.9.22

721

mulliyu muruntu nirittaṉa pōṉṟu

mutteṉat tikaḻntaṟa neruṅki
melleṉac cevanta maṇiyiṉiṟ pirittu

viḷakkiyop pittuvit taṉapōl
villiṭak kaviṉkoṇ ṭirupuṟat toḻuṅkum

virintapūṅ kāvikaḷ paṭarntu
collaru maṉattā ṭavarmaya liruḷit

tuṇittiṭa nakikkumeṉ ṉakiyāḷ.

2.9.23

722

pāleṉa vuiṟāk kaṉiyeṉa vaḻiyāp

pacumaṭaṟ ṟēṉeṉac citaṟā
vēlavār kuḻalār ceḻuṅkarat tēntu

miḷaṅkiḷi moḻiyeṉak kuḻaṟā
vēlivā ḻamutam piṟanteṉa vulakam

viḷaṅkiṭap poṉmaḻi poḻiyac
cālavu miṟanta taruviṉan taḻippat

taravaru miṉiyameṉ moḻiyāḷ.

2.9.24

723

veyyava ṉalartta vikacitam porunti

virinaṟik kamalameṉ malariṟ
ceyyava ḷiruppa teṉaveḻil ciṟantu

ceḻuṅkaḷik katirkaḷkāṉ ṟoḻuka
viyaka matippat timaṣkiṟi yuritta

vaḻimuṟi mukammataṅ kaḻitta
tuyyaveṇ matiya nikareṉa vulakiṟ

columpaṭic ciṟantamā mukattāḷ.

2.9.25

724

tiraḷiṉiṉ maṇiyāy muralviṉiṉ vaḷiyāyc

cevvineyp piṉiṟkamu keṉalāy
virikatir maṇippūṇ ṭāṅkumeṉ kaḻuttāḷ

vēyiṉik karumpimel laṇiyac
caruviṭap pacantu tiraṇṭumeṉ miyavāyt

taḻitteḻil piṟaṅkiya tōḷāḷ
varivaḷi cumanti yāḻiṉum viyantu

mayirnirin toḷirumuṉ kiyiṉāḷ.

2.9.26

725

kulikamārn taṉapō larakkiṉuñ civanta

koḻumaṭaṟ kāntaḷaṅ karattāḷ
maliciṉik kuiṟṟiṉ vaṉappiṉum vaṉappāy

maṇiyaṇi cumantamel viralāḷ
polivuṟac civantīn tiliyeṉak kiḷarntu

puṉakkiḷi nāciyiṉ vaṭivāy
nalivilā tiḷitta vayiravoṇ katirāy

nalaṅkiṭan tilaṅkiya vukirāḷ.

2.9.27

726

taṭittaṭi parantiṭ ṭeḻuntapū rittut

taḷataḷat toṉṟoṭoṉ ṟamiyā
taṭarttimi yāta kaṟuttakaṇ ṇataṉā

laruntavat tavaruyir kuṭittu
vaṭattiṉu ḷaṭaṅkā tiṇittakac caṟuttu

matakarik kōṭṭiṉuṅ katittup
paṭattiṉum piṟaṅkuñ cuṇaṅkaṇi paṭarnta

parumitat tuṇikkaṉa taṉattāḷ.

2.9.28

727

parimaḷac cimiḻō kulikaccep piṉamō

pacumatuk kalacamō vamirtam
perukiya kuṭamō kāmanī ruṟinta

pēriḷaṅ kurumpiyō katiriṉ
murukukop paḷikkum vaṉacameṉ mukiyō

muḻumaṇi patittameṉ muṭiyō
kariyilā vaḻakā ṟoḻukiya variyō

kavalutaṟ kariteṉun taṉattāḷ.

2.9.29

728

taṉameṉu mirukōṭ ṭattiyō rāliṟ

ṟaḷipaṭap piṇittacaṅ kiliyō
maṉanili kavaruṅ kaṭitaṭa varaviṉ

vālaṇi kiṭantatō valatu
ciṉavuviṟ kāmaṉ malikkuntaṉ maṉikkuñ

cērttiya mayanaṭu nūlō
viṉaṉuṭa ṉaḻaku niṟikuṭi yirunta

vivaḷvayi ṟaṇimayi roḻuṅkē.

2.9.30

729

peruvari yiṭatti ṉaṭiyuṟin tilaṅkum

pēreḻiṟ cumantapoṟ koṭiyō
virikatir maṇimē kalinaṭuk kōttu

viḷaṅkiṭa nuṭaṅkumel liḻiyō
kurumukam matunaṉ moḻivaḻi yaṭaṅkāk

kupirkkulan tēynteṉat tēyntu
terivari teṉalā yuvamiyiṟ poruvāc

cēyiḻi maṭanticiṟ ṟiṭiyē.

2.9.31

730

katiroḷi vaḻukki ṉarampiyip paḻittuk

kaviṉuṟun tiraṭciyiṟ katatta
matamalik karattiṉ vaṉappiṉi yaḻittu

māṟaru mirutumeṉ miyiṉi
litamuṟac civanta vilaviṉik kaṭintiṭ

ṭiṇiyaṭi yaṇiyeṉap paṭuttip
putumiyiṉ viḷaṅkit tavattuṟi yavarum

pukaḻntiṭac ciṟantapoṟ kuṟaṅkāḷ.

2.9.32

731

aṇimukaṭ ṭalavaṉ ṟaṉimukan taṭutta

varivaric ciṉivarāṟ pōṉṟu
maṇiyiṉiṟ ceṟitta tūṇiyum poruvā

vaṭivatāy veṟṟimaṉ ṉavarmuṉ
ṟaṇivilā ticikkuṅ kāḷamum poruvāt

taṉmiya vākimeṉ miyavāyp
paṇipala cumantu ciṟumayir neruṅkāp

paṇpuṟu miṇikkaṇik kālāḷ.

2.9.33

732

niṟitaru tarāciṉ vaṭivaṟum paraṭāḷ

niṟimaṇip panteṉuṅ kutiyāḷ
poṟiyoṭuṅ kamaṭat tiṉamvaṉam pukuntu

poruvaṟā tintiyu moṭukki
maṟipaṭat tavañcey tiṇipaṭaṟ karitāṉ

matittiṭaṟ kuṟumpuṟan tāḷāḷ
kaṟitarā maṇiyiṉ kulameṉa viralkaḷ

kaviṉkoḷac civantameṉ patattāḷ.

2.9.34

733

vaṉamayiṟ cāyaṟ kulameṉa veḻuntu

marimala ritaḻiṉmēṟ kulavu
maṉameṉa naṭantu napimukam matuta

maṭimalarp patattiṉi liṟiñci
yiṉiyaṉa pukaḻntu palarati cayippa

viṉamukiṟ karuṅkuḻa ṉekiḻap
puṉimaṇi piṟaḻa miṉṉeṉa nuṭaṅkip

putumiyiṟ ṟōṉṟaniṉ ṟaṉaḷāl.

2.9.35

tacik kaṭṭiyip peṇṇuruvamitta paṭalam muṟṟiṟṟu.

2.10 hapīpu rājā varici varaviṭutta paṭalam (734 - 774) 

734

pūṅkoṭi yeṉamuṉa niṉṟa pūviyit
tēṅkama ḻamutavāy tiṟantu nannapi
vāṅkuteṇ ṭirittaṭak timaṣku maṉṉavaṉ
pāṅkiṉi luṟikeṉap pariviṟ kūṟiṉār.

2.10.1

735

variviḻic ciṟunutaṉ maṭanti naṉṉeṟik
kuruviṭam viṭutteḻil kulavac ceṉṟaṇi
virikati rilaṅkili vēṟki maṉṉavaṉ
ṟiruvaṭi karuṅkuḻaṟ ceṉṉi cērttiṉāṉ.

2.10.2

736

teṇṭaṉiṭ ṭeḻuntapoṉ mayilic cīrpeṟak

viṇṭalat tiṉilāp patavi veṟṟiyik
koṇṭaṉa ṉeṉamaṉak kuṟivu nīkkiṉāṉ.

2.10.3

737

terivipiṉ varattimaṣ kiṟivar cevviya
kuruciṉan napikoḻuṅ kamala mellaṭi
paravinaṟ pukaḻcila pakarntu vākkoṭum
variciyi ṉeṟikkali māvi yōtiṉār.

2.10.4

738

kurimiyi ṉoṭuṅkali māvi yōtiṉār.

2.10.5

739

īṉamoṉ ṟillatō riṟiva ṉākiya
tāṉavaṉ ṟaṉiyuḷat tiruttit takkatōr
vāṉavar pukaḻmukam matuvi vāḻttinaṟ
ṟēṉeṉuṅ kaṭaṟperun tīṉi lāyiṉār.

2.10.6

tiṟivaṉik toḻuticu lātti ṉērvaḻi
kuṟivaṟap paṭittaruṅ kupiri nīkkiyē
muṟitava ṟāpperu muculi māyiṉār.

2.10.7

741

māṉakar timaṣkumaṉ ṉavarun tamperuñ
cēṉiyu mukammatiṉ ṟirumu ṉākicceṅ
kāṉmala raṭiyiṇi yiṟiñcik kikoṭut
tāṉanal laṟivarāyp puṟappaṭ ṭārarō.

2.10.8

742

2.10.9

puntiyiṟ pukaḻvarpoṉ ṉulakam pōtuvār.

2.10.10

744

palatara murittuttam patiyi nōkkiṉār.

2.10.11

745

kataḻvoṭuṅ katiyoṭuṅ kaṉittu muṉcelap

natikaḷuṅ kaṭantaya ṉaṭantu pōyiṉār.

2.10.12

746

karittira ḷolittakam paliyuṅ kāvaḷar

vurittiṭun toṉikkaṭa luṭittuk kāṭṭumāl.

2.10.13

747

kaṉiyiṉun tēṉiṉuṅ kāynta pākiṉu
miṉiyaṉa putumaṟi yiyaṟṟu nāviṉar
naṉipala cūḻvara nakari naṇṇiṉār
paṉivari yiṉumpukaḻ paritta pāṉmiyār.

2.10.14

748

viṇṇuṟi koṭimatiḷ timaṣku mēviya
vaṇṇalum patimuti yavaruk kaṉpoṭu
paṇṇarun tīṉmoḻi payiṟṟi naṉṉeṟi
yeṇṇili peṟavicu lātti lākkiṉār.

2.10.15

749

tīṉmuṟi naṭattiya timaṣku maṉṉavar
māṉmataṅ kamaḻntamey napikku mācilāp
pāṉmatik kalikali paṇipoṉ paṭṭivi
kūṉverin toṟuviṉiṟ koṭutta ṉuppiṉār.

2.10.16

750

nitimaṇi paṇipala niṟinta voṭṭakap
potipatiṟ ṟoṭuparaṟ puṭavi nīntivāṉ
matinaṭan tulaviya makka mākiya
patiyiṉuk kaṭuttoru pāluṟ ṟāravar.

2.10.17

kurumaṇi yoṭuniti timaṣkiṟ koṟṟavar
varaviṭut taṉareṉa vaḻaṅkum vācakan
teritara apūjakil ceviyiṟ cārntatē.

2.10.18

752

maṭittacin tiyiṉeḻun tēki maṉṉavaṉ
koṭuttaṉup piyanitik kuviyum paṇṭamum
viṭuttatiṅ kemakkeṉa vekuṇṭu veñcolāṟ

napitiru muṉañcilar naviṉṟiṭ ṭārarō.

varuḷki ṭantakaṭ kaṭicivap puṇṭavap pōtil.

2.10.21

ciṉakka vantivaṇ maṟippatu takuvatō celuniṉ
maṉikke ṉaccilar kūṟalu maṉattiṭik kotittāṉ.

2.10.22

peṭṭa kattiyum poṉṉiyum piṇakkaṟak kariyāyp
paṭṭa ṟattuin tumakkemak keṉappala raṟiya
voṭṭi vāytiṟan turikkumeṉ ṟapūjaki lurittāṉ.

2.10.23

virittuk kēṭṭaru ḷeṉṟaṉa rapūjakil virivi
ṉirutti yipporu ṇāḷiyiv vūrava raṟiyap
paritta voṭṭakaṅ karipakarn tiṭumeṉap pakarntāṉ.

2.10.24

758

poṉṟi kaḻnteḻil kulaviya maṉiyiṭip pukuntār
kaṉṟu puṉmaṉat tapūjakil kiḷiyuṭaṉ kaṭitiṟ
ceṉṟu veṇmalar ceṟitaru mālayañ cērntāṉ.

2.10.25

cāla meṉmalart toṭiyoṭum palapaṇi tarittuk
kōla mārnteḻat tīpamun tūpamuṅ koṭuttut
tāla mītiṉiṟ cirampaṭa virukaran tāḻttāṉ.

2.10.26

760

poḻinta nīrviḻi taraviran tēttiya pukaḻāṉ
moḻintu valviṉi toṭuttiṭu mukammatiṉ vāymi
yaḻinteṉ coṟpaḻu taṟavara maruḷkaveṉ ṟaṟintāṉ.

2.10.27

761

taritta lampukaḻn tiṭavapū jakiltaṉak keṉavē
nurittut tūṅkita ḻoṭṭivāy tiṟanteṉi nōkki

2.10.29

763

cuṟṟa mōṭaṭin tāṉṟuṇit tōḻarka ḷōṭum
veṟṟi naṉṉeṟi mukammatum viriviṉi lēkik
koṟṟa maṉvara viṭuttava riṭattiṉiṟ kūṇṭār.

2.10.30

764

kaṭṭu rippaṭik kariyiṉi yevarkaḷuṅ kaḷippa
viṭṭu rittiṭeṉ ṟurippati yāṉeṉa virittāṉ.

2.10.31

765

niṉṟu nīvirkaḷ cumantiva ṇiṟakkiya nitiyam
veṉṟi maṉṉava ṉevarkkaṉup piyateṉa virittāṉ.

nāṭṭi vittiṭuñ ciliyeṉa naviṉṟila maṟuki
mīṭṭuṅ kēṭṭalu naviṉṟila vīramum valiyum
pōṭṭuk kēṭṭaṉaṉ piṟṟiyum pukaṉṟicit tilavē.

mikuvi tapputu mikaṭara voṭṭiyi viḷittup
pukalu meṉṟaṉa rapūjakil keṭumaṉam puḻuṅka.

2.10.35

769

uritta colluḷan tarittiṭak kiṭantavoṭ ṭakaṅka
ṇiritte ḻuntaṟa vaḷineṭuṅ kaḻuttiṉi nīṭṭi
viritta vālacit tuvantiru viḻikaḷi viḻittup
perutta vāytiṟan taṟapeṉu moḻiyiṉiṟ pēcum.

yaracar kēcari hapīpeṉun timaṣkiṉuk karaca
riraci tampaṇi maṇitama ṉiyamivi yaṉittum
parica ṉattoṭu numakkaṉup piṉareṉap pakarnta.

2.10.37

771

terita rāpperum putumiko leṉaccira macittu
virita rāniṟi peruṅkaṭaṉ mētiṉi yaṉittum
purita rātipa rivareṉap pukaḻntayal pōṉār.

2.10.38

772

marunti lāpperum piṇivaḷit teṉamati mayaṅkik
karintu māmukam vāyveḷut taṟattali kaviḻntu
tirunti lāmaṉat toṭuñciṉat toṭuñceya laḻintu
porunti lātutaṉ kiḷiyoṭu mapūjakil pōṉāṉ.

2.10.39

maṉṉar maṉṉavar varaviṭu nitiyamu maṇiyu
naṉṉa yampeṟun tōḻarkaḷ cūḻvara nayiṉār
tanna kattiṉiṟ ceṟittaṉar ceḻumpukaḻ taḻippa.

2.11 īmāṉ koṇṭavarkaḷ hapaṣā rācciyattukkup pōnta paṭalam (775- 811) 

775

māci lāṉaruḷ perukiya makkamā nakari
lāci lānapi tīṉiṉi niṟuttuman nāḷiṟ
pāca maṟṟava ṉapūjakil kiḷipala pakuppāyp
pūca liṭṭaṉar perumpaḻi naṭunili pukuntē.

2.11.1

776

cikiyi ṉīṇmuṭi kuyiṟṟiveṇ katinaṭut tīṟṟi
nakini lāttaru mēṉili makkamā nakariṉ
mikima ṉattoṭu kāpirka ṭiṉantoṟum viḷikkum
pakiyi ṉōṭarum pañcamu muṭaṉparan tatuvē.

2.11.2

777

veṟutta kālamuṅ kāpirka ṭoṭuttaval viṉiyu
maṟutti lāmiyum pīsapī lāṟkaḷi māyttu
niṟuttun tīṉeṉa vēvalu milliyā ṉitamum
porutti ruppatev vaḻiyeṉa vakattiṭip poṟutta.

2.11.3

778

colatta kāpperum pakitoṭut tiṉattoṭuñ cūḻntu
kulatti ṉumpirit taṟaneṟi tīṉilik kuriyō
rilatti ṉumvarap poruntilā namariṉā liṉiyin
nilatti ruppatu paḻuteṉa maṉattiṭi niṉittē.

2.11.4

779

maṟuvi lātanaṉ ṉeṟimaṟi tērutu māṉi
yaṟivi ṉāyntaku matutaṉit taḻittaru kiruttip
piṟavu muṟṟatum varuvatu nikaḻvatum pēci
yuṟiyu mippati perumpakik kāvaṉa vurittār.

2.11.5

780

uṟṟa nummaṉat tuṭaṉpaṭa vuṟipava ruṭaṉum
veṟṟi maṉṉajā ciyyuṟi tirunakar mēvik
kuṟṟa miṉṟiyaṅ kuṟivatu karutteṉak kuṟippa
maṟṟu vēṟuri yātutu māṉuñcam matittār.

2.11.6

781

ṣaraki ṟaṅkinaṉ ṉapiyeṉum perumpeyar taritta
varuṭa minteṉa varavaru miṟajapu mātan
taruma nērutu māṉoṭu ṟukkiyā tamiyu
miruḷum pōtaṉup piṉarhapa ṣāveṉun tēyam.

2.11.7

782

tiruttun tiṇpuya napitiru makaḷuṭaṉ ciṟappa
virinta pūṅkuḻaṉ maṭantiyar mūvarum viyappap
poruntun tīṉavar patiṉmarum pukaḻutu māṉum
pirinti ṭātuceṉ ṟantanā ṭaṭintataṟ piṉṉar.

2.11.8

783

canta ṉantikaḻ puyavapit tālipu tavattāl
vanta jaḵpaṟu mavaruṭaṉ mintarkaḷ cilaru
nantu veṇṭara ḷantikaḻ natihapa ṣāpāṟ
pinti ṭātoru muṟimaṟi taṉittaṉup piṉarē.

2.11.9

784

maṉṉu māṭava reṇṇoru paḵtiru varumāyp
poṉṉu lāhapa ṣāvaḷa nāṭaṇi puratti
liṉṉa lilleṉac cērntavaṉ makiḻvoṭu miruntār.

2.11.10

785

uṟinta māntaruk kapaṣiya raceṉu muravōra
ṉiṟinta naṉkali yoṭumpala variciyu nitiyuṅ
kuṟinti ṭāteṭut taruḷinaṉ moḻipala koṭuttuc

2.11.11

matittu naṉkoṭu muyarttiṉa ṉeṉumvara lāṟṟik
kotitta cintiya ṉapūjakil kuḻuvoṭuṅ kēṭṭuk
katitta cūḻcciyiṉ vēṟoru viṉikaru tiṉaṉē.

2.11.12

787

villu miḻntacem maṇittoṭi tiraṇṭaveṇ ṭaraḷam
palla vamporu vāttama ṉiyattukil palavuñ

mallu ṟumpuyaṉ karattiṉmut tiriyoṭum vittāṉ.

2.11.13

788

yāta rattoṭu cērttanal variciyu maḷittuk
kōta ṟattuin toḻutupat tirattiyuṅ koṭuttāṉ.

2.11.14

789

koṭuttu naṉmoḻi koṭuttuna jāciyyāṅ kōvuk
kaṭuttu niṉṟaḷit tiṭumvari cikaḷivi yavaṉcoṟ
paṭutti ṭāmati mantirark kiviyeṉap pakuttu
viṭutta ṉaṉperu vañcamum paṭiṟum viṭātāṉ.

2.11.15

790

aṟuma ṉattiṉa ṉapūjakil koṭuttavi yaṉittu
meṟuḻiṉ mikkuya roṭṭaka mītiṉi lēṟṟit
taṟuki lāteḻun tiruvaru maritiṉiṟ cārntār
naṟavu yirttataṇ ṭalitikaḻ hapaṣinaṉ ṉāṭṭil.

2.11.16

791

ceṉṟa tūtuvar varicika ḷaṉittiyun tiruntak
kuṉṟe ṉattiraḷ puyaṉajā ciyiṉmuṉaṅ kuvittu
muṉṟi liṟṟaṉit teḻutiya muṭaṅkali yeṭuttu
niṉṟu nīṭṭiṉar niruparka ṇeruṅkiya capiyil.

2.11.17

792

varinta pattirap pācura makkamā nakari
lirunta hāṣimā kulattoru vaṉṟali yeṭuttu
virinta mantira vañcaka māyaṅkaḷ viḷattut
terinta vētamuñ camayamu nilikeṭac citittum.

2.11.18

793

kuṭipo runtilā tinnakark kulampaḻu tākkip
paṭipa kuttiṭak koliyoṭu pātakam viḷittu
muṭivi lāpperun tēvata mālaya muḻutu
maṭiya ṟuttiṭat tuṇintaṉaṉ mukammateṉ pavaṉē.

2.11.19

794

aṅka vaṉmoḻik koḻukiṉa navaṉiṉuṅ koṭiyōr
poṅku mavvuḻi pukuntaṉar hapaṣimā purattip
paṅka mākkamu ṉavarkaḷit taṇṭaṉip paṭutti
yeṅki rukkiṉu mirukkoṇā takaṟṟiṭu meṉavē.

2.11.20

795

iṉṉa vācaka maṉittiṉuṅ kēṭṭava revarun
tuṉṉa lārkolō ciṭṭarō veṉacciran tūkkip
paṉṉu vāratiṉ mantirar pakittavā cakattāṟ
coṉṉa vāṟṟiṉiṉ muṭippatu tuṇiveṉat tuṇintār.

2.11.21

796

aracar nāyakar hapaṣina jāciyā maracaṉ
parica ṉattavar moḻiyiṉum maṟiviṉum pārttē
yurica marppaka mukammatiṉ vaḻiyiṉuk kuriya
varici ceytiva ṇiruttalē kaṭaṉeṉa vakuttāṉ.

2.11.22

797

malima ṉattapū jakilaṉup piyaveku māṉat
taliva rittaṉa tirumpati yiṭiyiṉiṟ cārāk
kulaṉu ṭaṉumar patikkaṭin tiṭumeṉak kuṟittu
vilaki yaṅkavar koṇrntapal poruḷiyum veṟuttāṉ.

2.11.23

798

catuma ṟipporuṇ mukammatiṉ vaḻiyavar taminal
litama ṉattoṭu maṉucarit tapūjaki liṭattiṟ
putiya rippuṟam pōkkiṉa ṉeṉumoḻip pukaḻi
matuki vēntapit tālipu kēṭṭuḷa makiḻntār.

2.11.24

799

vaṇakka vācakat toṭumhapa caracaṉi vāḻtti
yiṇakki naṟporuḷ peṟappala payitteṭut teḻuti
yuṇakkum puṉmaṉat tapūjaki leḻutiya vōlip
piṇakka ṟuttapit tālipu koṭuttaṉup piṉarāl.

2.11.25

800

putiya naṟporuḷ peṟatteri kavitiyiṉ pukaḻāṉ
matura vācaka meḻutiya tuṇarntuḷa makiḻntu
tutice yuṅkali māneṟip paṭumaṟit tūyōrk
kativi tappala varicicey tapaṣā ciruntār.

2.11.26

801

anta nāḷiyiṉ makkamā nakarava revarum
vantu naṟkali māvurit taṉareṉum vacaṉam
punti kūrtarak kēṭṭaṉar cilaratiṟ poruvāc
cinti yāyiṉar nakarkkeṉat tirumpiṉar cilarē.

2.11.27

802

paṭikki vēntutu māṉuṭaṉ maṉiviyum palarun
toṭikki ṇaṅkiya puyattavar cūḻvara neṟiyi
ṉaṭakka muṉmoḻi paḻuteṉa naviṟara naṭuṅki
yaṭikka lattiṉuṅ karaviṉum patiyivan taṭintār.

2.11.28

803

putitiṉ mūvoru patiṉmaru mūvarum puṟattiṟ
catiya ṟattiṉi yavaravar cārpiṉiṟ cārntā
ratila pūcalmā veṉpava raṟiviṉi luyarnta
matiyiṉ mikkapit tālipi yaṭuttuvan tiruntār.

2.11.29

804

ciṉattu vaṉkolik kāpirka ṭiraṇṭikal cekukku
maṉattiṉ mikkapit tālipu matimuka nōkki
yiṉatti ṉittavirt tapūcalmā veṉpavaṉ ṟaṉinum
maṉitta lattilvit tiruppatu paḻuteṉa vakuttār.

2.11.30

805

poytta māmaṟi mukammati maṉiyiṭip pukutti
vitti runtaṉi yapūcalmā taṉiyunum maṉikku
ḷeyttu vittirup patupaḻu teṉavika liṭarāy
moytta ṭarntaṉa rapūjaki loṭumuraṇ matattār.

2.11.31

806

eṭukkum vāḷayil paṭikkalam palakarat tēntit
toṭukkuṅ pūcaliṭ ṭaṭalapit tālipit turatti
viṭukku meṉpatu maṉiyuṭa ṉakariyum veṟuppak
keṭukku meṉpatu mapūlaku peṉumavaṉ kēṭṭāṉ.

2.11.32

807

eṉakku muṉṉavaṉ ṟaṉiyiṭar viḷittiṭa leṉatu
maṉakku ṟippaṭa rivitavirt tiṭīreṉiṉ matiyi
niṉikku muṉpaki rakumatu neṟinili niṟuvik
kaṉakka vittaliyā ṉalatili yeṉakkaḻa ṟiṉaṉāl.

2.11.33

808

mōtum vāymiyi ṉapūlaku peṉumavaṉ muraṇi
yōtum vācakaṅ kupirava ruḷaṅkaḷi yuruvip
pōtu kiṉṟateṉ ṟaṭarntuniṉ ṟavarpoṟi kalaṅkit
tīta kaṟṟiyaṅ kavaravar maṉivayiṟ cērntār.

2.11.34

809

kaṟita virntiṭā maṉakkuṟi ṣiyaṅkulak kāpi
raṟavu nontakat taṭaṅkiṉa reṉavaṟa mutirntu
niṟiyun tīṉilik kuriyavar makiḻntaneñ ciṉarāy
maṟipa ṭāmukam matiṉvaḻi vaḷarttirun taṉarāl.

2.11.35

810

ariya nāyakaṉ ṟūtuvā ṉavarkkiṟi yaṇukik
kiriyiṉ mītuniṉ ṟarumpeyar napiyeṉak kiḷattum
varuṭa māṟiṉiṉ māṟukoṇ ṭavarmaṉaṅ kaliyat
teruḷu mēṉmiyiṉ mukammatuñ ciṟantirun taṉarāl.

2.11.36

811

porunta māṉilat tulaviya pukaḻuci ṉayiṉā
rarunta vattiṉuṭ poruḷeṉa varumapul kācim
virinta meyneṟic cintiyi ṉaṭuvuṟa viḷaṅki
yirunta meṉmalarp patamukam matumiṉi tiruntār.

2.11.37

īmāṉ koṇṭavarkaḷ hapaṣā rācciyattukkup pōnta paṭalam muṟṟiṟṟu.
ākap paṭalam 11kkut tiruviruttam...811
uḷḷuṟi aṭṭavaṇikkut tirumpa

2.12 māṉukkup piṇi niṉṟa paṭalam (812 - 883 ) 

812

kuyiṉiḻal parappac cevvik koḻuntaṭi naṟavañ cintum
vayiravoṇ variyiṉ vimmi vaḷarntatiṇ puyattu vaḷḷal
ceyiraṟu maṟiyiṉ ṟīñcoṟ ceḻumaḻi poḻintu tīṉiṉ
payirvaḷan tēṟac ceytu parivuṭa ṉirukku nāḷil.

2.12.1

813

ariyiṉañ ceṟinta pōṉṟa aṟapikaḷ kuḻuvi ṉāppa
ṇorutaṉic cīya moppa vuṭiyavaṉ ṟūtar celvat
tirunakarp puṟattu nīṅkic ceḻumukiṉ muṭiyiṟ ṟāṅki
marumalar ceṟiyuñ cōli cūḻntatōr variyic cārntār.

2.12.2

814

koṉṟiyuṅ kuruntuṅ kārkkōṟ kuṟiñciyum vēyun taṟṟit
tuṉṟiya niḻalu naṉṉīr coritaru miṭamuñ centēṉ
maṉṟaloṇ malaru nīṅkā vaṉantikaḻ variyiṉ kaṇ ṇa
ceṉṟaṉa neṟikkuṅ kānti cevvimey mukamma taṉṟē.

2.12.3

815

vaṉantari vilaṅku māyttu vaṉṟaci vakirntu vārit
tiṉantoṟuṅ kōliṟ kōlit tīyiṭi yamiḻttik kāyttit
taṉantaṉi yiruntu niṉṟu taṉṟacik perukka laṉṟi
yaṉantaliṟ poḻutum vēṟō raṟiveṉpa taṟinti lāṉē.

2.12.4

816

kāliṉiṟ kaḻalu nīṇṭa kariyakā ḷattiṉ vīkkum
pāliṉil valiyuṅ kiyiṟ paruvarit taṉuvuṅ kūruṅ
kōlveṟi naṇiyun tōḷiṟ kūṉpiṟi vāḷu meṉmi
vāluṭip paṟavi cērttuṅ kaṇṇiyu maruṅkiṟ koṇṭōṉ.

2.12.5

817

kuṟuveyarp putitta meyyuṅ koḻuntaci maṇatta vāyum
paṟitali virippuṅ kūrnta paṭukoli viḻiyu māka
vaṟapiṉi laṟapi vēṭa ṉaṭaviyiṟ ṟoṭarntōr māṉik
kaṟuvoṭum valiyiṟ cērttik kaṭṭivit tiruppak kaṇṭār.

2.12.6

818

kuḻikuḻit teriyuñ centēṉ koḻumalark kāvi nōkkār
poḻimali yaruvi nōkkār puṟattunaṉ ṉiḻali nōkkār
ceḻumukiṟ kaviki vaḷḷal ceṟitaru mīntiṉ ceṅkāy
maṟiyeṉac coriva nōkkār māṉiyē nōkkic ceṉṟār.

2.12.7

819

aruḷaṭi kiṭanta kaṇṇu maḻakoḷir mukamuñ cōti
corinaṟi kamaḻnta meyyuñ cūṉmukiṟ kaviki yōṭum
varuvatu tūyōṉ ṟūtar mukammatu veṉṉat tēṟip
paruvara luḻakku muḷḷat toṭumpiṇi pakaru maṉṟē.

2.12.8

820

neṭiyavaṉ ṟūtar vantār vēṭaṉā ṉilatti ṉanta
muṭaluyirk kiṟuti yilli yuḻiyiṉat tōṭuñ cērntu
kaṭitiṉiṟ kaṉṟuṅ kāṇpō meṉamukam matuvik kaṇṇā
ṉoṭivari yimimū ṭāma ṉōkkiyē kiṭanta taṉṟē.

2.12.9

821

poruppiṭit tuṟukaṟ cārpiṟ poriyārit taruvi ṉīḻaṉ
maruppuṭip paṭalit tiṇṭōṉ maṉṉava ruṭaṉum pukku
neruppiṭit tacivā yārntu niṉṟavē ṭaṉiyuñ cemmāṉ
ṟirupputaṟ karuṅkaṭ ṭuṇṭu kiṭappatuñ ciṟappak kaṇṭār.

2.12.10

822

iṭinilat turukki viṭṭa viracitam paranta teṉṉa
maṭicuti yamutañ cinta vaṭikkaṇīr paṉippat tēṅku
muṭalakan turutti yoppa neṭṭuyirp puyirttuk kāliṟ
ṟuṭaroṭuṅ kiṭappat tūyōṉ ṟūtuva raṭuttu niṉṟār.

2.12.11

823

koṭiyaṭam piliyi māṉum kuḷampiṉmēṟ curukkum puḷḷip
poṭiyuṭaṟ patippum vīṅkip putiyuneṭ ṭuyirppu nōkki
neṭiyava ṉiṟacū lullā neñcunek kurukik kāṉiṉ
piṭipaṭu māṉiṉ ṟaṉpāṟ pēraruḷ curappa niṉṟār.

2.12.12

824

katirviri hapīpu niṟpak kāṉakat tarukka ḷiyāvum
putumala ralarttic centēṉ poḻivamāṉ varutta nōkki
vitirciṉik karaṅkaḷ cāyttu meṉṟaḻik kūntal cōra
matiyaḻin tiraṅkik kaṇṇīr vaṭippaṉa pōṉṟa taṉṟē.

2.12.13

825

kulattoṭum paṟavi tattaṅ kuṭampiyiṟ pukutaṉ māṉi
nilattiṭik kiṭattik kaṭṭi niṉṟavēṭ ṭuvaṉik kaṇṇā
ṉalattoṭuṅ kāṇpa tākā teṉanaṭu naṭuṅki yuḷḷa
mulittaṟap peṭiyi ṉōṭu moḷippaṉa pōṉṟa taṉṟē.

2.12.14

826

ēṭṭalar naṟava mānti yiruñcurum picikkun tōṟṟam
vāṭṭamiṉ mukamma tiṅṅaṉ vantaṉar varuntu māṉi
mīṭṭaṉar vēṭa ṉīmāṉ virumpiṉaṉ payaṅka ṭīrttār
kūṭṭuṟin toḷittaṉ māṟṟu meṉappala kūya pōlum.

2.12.15

827

niṟivaḷañ curanta kāṉi ṉiṉṟanan napiyi nōkkik
kuṟiyavā lacittu nīṇṭa koḻuṅkaḻut tuyartti nīṭṭi
maṟipaṭā matiyē vaṇmi mukammatē yeṉṉap pōṟṟit
taṟukiṭā tevarkkuṅ kēṭpac calāmeṭut turittuk kūṟum.

2.12.16

828

vallava ṉuṇmit tūtē maṉṉumā nilattiṉ mānta
rallali yakaṟṟi vētat taṟaneṟi payiṟṟic corkkat
tilliṭip pukuttap pūvi ṉiṭattiṉi lutitta kōvē
yolliyi ṉeṉatu coṟkēṭ ṭuvantaru ḷaḷikka vēṇṭum.

2.12.17

829

eṉṉuyi reṉanīṅ kāta viṉamumeṉ kaliyuṅ kaṉṟun
tuṉṉiṭat tiraṇṭu pimpuṟ ṟuṟitoṟu mēyntu nāḷu
muṉṉiya pacika ṭīrttōr mirukaṅkaṭ kuyirko ṭāmaṉ
maṉṉiya maliyiṉ cārpu maṉappaya makaṟṟi vāḻntēm.

2.12.18

830

irunilat tācik kāyō riḷaṅkaṉṟeṉ vayiṟṟu ṟātāṉ
maruviya kaliyu nāṉum varuttamuṟ ṟirukkuṅ kālam
perukutīṉ mukamma tēnum peyariṉip pōṟṟal ceytē
ṉuruvamin tiḷañcūṉ muṟṟi yutaramum vaḷarnta taṉṟē.

2.12.19

831

taṉiyaṉeṉ ṉuyiruṅ kākkuṅ kaliyuyir tāṉu moṉṟā
yiṉitiṉoṉ ṟāya teṉṉa viḷaṅkaṉṟoṉ ṟīṉṟē ṉiṉpa
naṉikaḷi kaṭali lāḻntu naṟumali yiṭattiṟ cērntu
tuṉipala vakaṟṟi ṉēṉmuṉ cūḻviṉi yaṟikē lēṉē.

2.12.20

832

uḷḷuyi raṉiya kaṉṟu moruttalu miyāṉu mōrnāḷ
veḷḷamot taṉiya māṉi ṉiṉamōr veṟpiṉ cārpi
ṉaḷḷili yaḷḷi vāykoṇ ṭarumpaci taṭintu nīruṇ
ṭeḷḷaḷa veṉiṉu macca miṉṟiniṉ ṟulavu nēram.

2.12.21

833

atticik ketiriṉ mēlpā laṭuttoru kuvaṭṭiṉ kaṇṇē
mattakak kariyu māykkum varippuli muḻakka nīṇṭa
kuttirat tacaṉit tākkiṉ kuvalaya matirak kēṭṭut
tattiyet ticiyun tikkun taṉittaṉic citaṟi ṉēmāl.

2.12.22

834

kūṭiya tūṟum pāriṟ kuḷittiṭak kutittu vallē
yōṭiya ticiyi ṉoṉṟi yoṉṟukāṇ kilatā yāṉum
vāṭiya maṉatti ṉōṭu maṟiyiyu nōkkā tākki
yāṭiyiṟ ṟurumpāy vēṟō raṭaviyi ṉaṭintiṭ ṭēṉāl.

2.12.23

835

aṭaviyi ṉaṭiyuṅ kāli yavvuḻik karantiv vēṭaṉ
ṟuṭariṭum valiyic cuṟṟic curukkiṭap pulivāyt tappi
miṭalari yuḻiyiṟ cikki miṭinteṉa miṭintu cevvi
yuṭaluyir patippat tēmpi yuṇarvaḻin toṭuṅkā niṉṟēṉ.

2.12.24

836

valiyiṭat tuṟinta teṉṉa makiḻnteḻun tōṭi vantu
nilipeṟa vaṭuttuc cāyttu niṉṟeṉi nōkki yākat
tulivuṟum pacikkiṉ ṟeṉpā luṟṟaṉi yeṉṉak kūṟic
cilakaṇi nilattiṟ cērttit terintoru pācan toṭṭāṉ.

2.12.25

837

tirukkaṟa nālu tāḷuñ cevvitiṟ kūṭṭi yaṅki
varikkayi ṟataṉāṟ cuṟṟi māṟukoṇ ṭīḻttuk kaṭṭik
carikkara meṉṉa nīṇṭa karattiṉāṟ ṟāṅki muṉṉarc
curukkiya valiyi nīkkit tōḷiṉi leṭuttuk koṇṭāṉ.

2.12.26

838

cuvimuṉik kōṭṭuc cevvik kaliyuṭa luyiru mīṉṟa
naviyuṭa luyiru mōrmā ṉuṭaṉkoṇṭu naṭappa tottuc
cavipuṟan tavaḻuṅ kōṭṭuc cārpiliv vaṉattiṉ kaṇṇē
cuviyaṟu moḻiyā ṉeṉṉic cumantiva ṇiṟakki vittāṉ.

2.12.27

839

kaṭṭuṭaṉ kiṭantu neñciṟ kavaliyuḷ ḷaḻintu māṟā
neṭṭuyirp peṟintu cōrntu nilattiṭik kiṭakku nēram
vaṭṭaveṇ kaviki vaḷḷal mukammatu napiyē yummit
tiṭṭiyiṟ ṟeriyak kaṇṭēṉ ṟiṭukkamun tīrnta taṉṟē.

2.12.28

840

eṉaviṉi yurittup piṉṉu meḻiṉapi mukatti nōkki
maṉanili vākki ṉōṭu mukammatē yeṉṉap pōṟṟip
puṉamuṟi vilaṅkiṉ cāti yāyiṉun tamiyēṉ puṉcoṟ
ṟaṉiyaruṭ paṭuttik kēṭpī reṉṟuri cāṟṟic cāṟṟum.

2.12.29

841

iccili vēṭaṉ kiyi ṉiṟattali yuḷatti leṇṇi
yaccamuṟ ṟurippa taṉṟiv vavaṉiyiṟ cīva ṉiyāvu
niccaya miṟatta lallā liruppavi nilatti luṇṭō
muccakam viḷaṅkun tīṉiṉ mutaṉmaṟi muṟimic collōy.

2.12.30

842

kaliyeṉap pirivi lātu kaṇṇimi kāppa teṉṉa
valivaṟak kāppac ciṉṉā ḷavaṉiyiṟ kalantu vāḻntēṉ
kulaviya maṟiyu mīṉṟēṉ kuṟittiṉi yiruppa teṉko
lilinuṉip paṉiyi ṉākki yiṟattalē nalattaṉ maṉṉō.

2.12.32

843

aṭaviyiṟ kiriyil vīṇi lavatiyuṟ ṟiṟanti ṭāmal
vaṭivuṭik kurici lēnum malarppatac cevvi nōkkip
paṭuparaṟ kāṉil vēṭaṉ pacippiṇi tīrppa tāka
vuṭaliṟat tiṭuta levvev viṟappiṉu muyarcci mēlōy.

2.12.33

844

varippuli muḻakkaṅ kēṭṭu māṉiṉañ citaṟit tattan
tarippiṭa maṟiyā toṉṟuk koṉṟuṭaṉ cārā teṅku
murippari teṉṉap pōnta tāleṉa torutta ṟēṭi
yirippaṟā neṭikkāṉ pōyppō yiruntatō viṟanta tēyō.

2.12.33

845

olliyi ṉōṭi nīṅkā toruttaliṉ ṟaḷavu mōntu
pulliṉik kaṟiyā nīrum pucittiṭā tiruntu tēṭi
yallaluṟ ṟaḻuṅkik kaṇṇi ṉaruvinīr coriya vāṭip
pallava meriyiṟ pukka teṉavuṭal patikku maṉṟē.

2.12.34

846

piṭipaṭu mitaṟku muṉṉē mūṉṟunāṭ piṟantu pulliṉ
koṭinuṉi mēyntu nīruṅ kuṭittaṟi yātu pāvi
maṭimuli yiṟaṅkip pālum vaḻintatu kuḻavi cōrntu
paṭimici kiṭanteṉ pāṭu paṭuvatō vaṟiki lēṉē.

2.12.35

847

kōṭṭuṭik kaliyi ṉōṭuṅ kūṭiṟṟō valatōr pāliṉ
mīṭṭatō viṉattic cērntu vimminiṉ ṟēṅkiṟ ṟōkāṉ
kāṭṭiṭip pulivāyp paṭṭuk kaḻintatō veṉṉit tēṭi
vāṭṭamuṟ ṟalaṟi yōṭi maṟukiṟṟō vaṟiki lēṉē.

2.12.36

848

tēṅkiya paciyāl vāṭit tirintatō viṟanta tōveṉ
ṟēṅkiya varutta mallā livviṭa rataṉi lāvi
nīṅkumeṉ ṟuḷḷat tuḷḷē ṉeṭṭuṭa luṭuppi ṉāvi
tāṅkiya taruma vēntē tavaṟaṉṟu cāta maṉṟē.

2.12.37

849

maṉṉiya kalimā veṉṉum vaḻinili mānta riyārum
poṉṉilam pukutac ceyyum puṇṇiyap pukaḻiṉ mikkōy
koṉṉilic cilikki vēṭaṉ koṭumpaci taṇippē ṉeṉṟāṭ
piṉṉiya piṇippu nīkkip piṇiyeṉa viṭuttal vēṇṭum.

2.12.38

850

viṭuttirēṟ kaliyic cērntu viḻivuṟuṅ kavali tīrap
paṭuttiyeṉ ṉiṉattuk kōtip paṟaḻiṉuk kiṉiya tīmpāl
koṭuttarum paciyi māṟṟik kulattoṭuñ cērttu vallē
yaṭuttoru kaṭikip pōtilaṭiva ṉeṉ ṟinta taṉṟē.

2.12.39

851

māṉuri vaḻaṅkak kēṭṭu maṉattiṉiṟ karuṇi poṅkik
kāṉavēṭ ṭuvaṉi nōkkik kaṉṟiṭi varuttan tīrttut
tāṉvaru maḷavu miyāṉē piṇiyeṉac cāṟri niṉṟār
tīṉeṉum payirik kāttuc ceḻumpukaḻ viḷakkuñ cemmal.

2.12.40

852

piriyamuṟ ṟiraṅkik kāṭṭiṉ piṇakkiyāṉ piṇiyeṉ ṟōtu
muriyiṉik kēṭṭu vēṭa ṉoṇpuyaṅ kuluṅka nakkit
teritaru maṟivi ṉōṭuñ ciṉattoṭuṅ kalantu tērntu
karumukiṟ kaviki vaḷḷal kaviṉmuka nōkkic colvāṉ.

2.12.41

853

muḷḷuṭik kāṉi lēki mukamaḻin tucci vērvi
yuḷḷaṅkā ṉaṉippa vōṭi yuṭalulin toṉṟuṅ kāṇā
viḷḷarum paciyāṉ mīḷum vēḷiyip piṇiyi nōkki
yoḷḷiḻi valiyiṟ ṟākkip piṭittiva ṇoruṅku cārntēṉ.

2.12.42

854

peruttamāṉ ṟaciyā liṟṟip perumpaci tavirnta teṉṟu
ḷiruttiyiṅ kiruntē ṉanta virumaṉak kaḷippi nīkki
varuttamuṟ ṟiṭuñcoṟ coṉṉīr mukammatē yevarkku miccoṟ
poruttama taṉṟu viṇṇu maṇṇilum pukaḻiṉ mikkōy.

2.12.43

855

kāṉiṭip piṭitta māṉik kaṭṭaviḻt tavaṇiṟ pōkkiṉ
māṉiṭar pāliṉ mīṭṭum varuvatu muṉṉa ruṇṭō
ñāṉamu maṟiyun tērntōr ceyyuḷu nāṭṭiṟ ṟuṇṭō
vūṉamip piṇicco liyā vōtuva toḻika veṉṟāṉ.

2.12.44

856

eṉṉuṟu piṇiyāyp pōṉa virumpiṉi kaṭikip pōti
ṉuṉṉiṭat tuṟumvā rātē luṉpaci tīrppa tākap
piṉṉiraṇ ṭoṉṟuk kaṉpāyt tarukuvaṉ pētu ṟēleṉ
ṟaṉṉavaṉ ṟaṉakkuc coṉṉā rāraṇat tamirtac collār.

2.12.45

857

kāraṇak kuricil kūṟuṅ kaṭṭuri ceviyi ṉōrntu
pāriṉi levarkkun tōṉṟāp putumipārt taṟivō mallāṟ
cārpiṉiṟ cārā loṉṟuk kiraṇṭumē tarutu meṉṟār
pēriṉiṟ piṇayāyk koḷḷal karutteṉap perituṭ koṇṭōṉ.

2.12.46

858

kaḷḷamuṅ karappu māṟāk karuttiṉa ṉuyirkaṭ keṉṟu
meḷḷaḷa virakka millā vēṭṭuva riṉatti ṉuḷḷē
ṉuḷḷama taṟintuṅ kēṭṭī rurippateṉ ṇuyarnta mēṉmi
vaḷḷalnum matura vāymi maṟuttilēṉ viṭutti reṉṟāṉ.

2.12.47

859

vēṭṭuva ṉurippak kēṭṭu mukammatu viruppa muṟṟu
vāṭṭamuṟ ṟirunta puḷḷi māṉiṭat tiruntu pāri
ṉīṭṭiya kāliṟ cērtta tuṭariṉi nekiḻttuk kāṉiṟ
kūṭṭuṟāk kuḻavik kuppāl koṭuttivaṇ varuka veṉṟār.

2.12.48

860

iruntukāṉ maṭakki nīṭṭi yeḻuntuṭaṉ muṟukku nīkki
marunteṉu mamutat tīñcoṉ mukammatiṉ vataṉa nōkkip
poruntiya kalimā vōtip pukaḻntuṭaṟ pūrip pōṭun
tiruntavē ṭaṉiyum pārttuc ceṉṟatu kāṉiṉ māṉē.

2.12.49

861

veṇṇilāk katirkāṉ ṟeṉṉa meṉmuli curanta tīmpāṉ
maṇṇelā naṉippac cūḻnta vaṉamelān tirintu tēṭik
kaṇṇiṉi liṉaṅkā ṇātu kalaṅkiyōr vaṉattiṉ kaṇṇē
yeṇṇarum piṇiyuṅ kaṉṟuṅ kaliyuṭa ṉiṉitu kaṇṭa.

2.12.50

862

malivaṟa viṉattu ḷāki maṉattiṉuṭ kavali nīkkik
kaliyiṉuḷ varuttan tīrttuk kaṉṟiṉi yaṇittu vimmu
muliyiṉi yūṭṭi meṉmi mutukuvā laṭinā nīṭṭi
yalitara vaḷittu mōntu vēṭkiyi yakaṟṟiṟ ṟaṉṟē.

2.12.51

863

kaṉṟatu vayiṟu vīṅkak katirmuli yamuta mūṭṭi
niṉṟataṉ ṉiṉattuk kellā neṭipaṭuṅ kāṉi lōṭi
vaṉṟiṟal vēṭaṉ kiyiṟ paṭumvara vāṟun tūtar
veṉṟikoḷ piṇiyiṉ mīṭṭu viṭṭatu mōtiṟ ṟaṉṟē.

2.12.52

864

piṇiyeṉa vuritta māṟṟam piṇikkula maṉittuṅ kēṭṭup
paṇipaṭu kāṉi luḷḷap patippoṭun tuṇukki niṟpat
tuṇiyeṉuṅ kaliyi ṉaṅkañ cōrntuneṭ ṭuyirppu vīṅki
yaṇitara vaṭuttu nōkki yāṟṟuvāṉ ṟoṭaṅkiṟ ṟaṉṟē.

2.12.53

865

māṟukoṇ ṭavarki tappi vantamā ṉiṉattiṉ cāti
kōṟali virumpi muṉṉu nararkiyiṟ kūṭiṟ ṟuṇṭō
vēṟuri pakarēl pārppi veṟuttumuṉ ṉiṉatti nīttu
mīṟeṉap pōtal vēṇṭā meṉumuri yiyampiṟ ṟaṉṟē.

2.12.54

866

iṇitteṉip piṇitta vēṭa ṉitayattuk kiyiyap pēcip
piṉitaṉip porutti niṉṟōr periyavaṉ ṟūta rintat
tiṇittalat taṟivi lāta cētaṉac cāti yaṉṟē
yaṇittuyi raṉittuṅ kāttaṟ kavarala tilli yaṉṟē.

2.12.55

867

eṉṉuyi rataṉi vēṭa ṉirumpacik kiyiya vīntu
naṉṉapi piṇiyi mīṭpa naṉmaṉam porunti lēṉāṟ
poṉṉula kiḻantu tīyu narakiṉiṟ pukuva tallāṟ
piṉṉoru katiyu muṇṭō piḻiyaṉṟip perumi yaṉṟē.

2.12.56

868

ciṟappuṭik kuriciṉ muṉṉañ ceppiya māṟṟa māṟi
maṟappoṭu miruntē ṉākil varippuli yiṉattiṉ vāyppaṭ
ṭiṟappatē carata mallā liruppataṟ kiṭamaṟ ṟuṇṭō
vuṟapperum viruppa meṉmē liruttali yoḻittal vēṇṭum.

2.12.57

869

natiyiṭip perukkiṉ muṉṉōr navvipi ṉaṭakku nāḷiṉ
matiyili yoruttaṉ vaḷḷaṉ mukammatiṉ vacaṉa māṟip
putiyanaṉ ṉīru ḷāḻntu noṭiyiṉil vīḻntu pōya
vaticaya mulakil viṇṇi liyāvarē yaṟiki lātār.

2.12.58

870

ītelā maṟintu meṉṉi yivaṇiṭi yiruttal vēṇṭi
yōtutal paḻuteṉ ṟōti yuḻiyiṉa maṉittun tēṟṟik
kātaliṟ kaliyip pōṟṟik kaṉṟiṉi yataṉpāṟ cērttip
pētuṟa leṉappā lūṭṭi yeḻuntatu piṇiyu maṉṟē.

2.12.59

871

iṉattiṉi viṭuttu nīṅki yiruṅkaḷip pitayam pūppa
vaṉattiṉi lēkuṅ kāli maṟimuṉa maṟippac cīṟic
ciṉattatu taṭuppa vōṭic cevvimāṉ mukatti nōkki
yiṉittavāy puṟṟīṇ ṭāta viḷamaṟi yurikku maṉṟē.

2.12.60

872

mātavam peṟṟu niṉpōṉ mukammatu napitañ ceyya
pātapaṅ kayattik kaṇṭu parivuṭa ṉīmāṉ koṇṭu
pōtalē yaṉṟi niṉṉip puṟattiṉi lakaṟṟi vāḻē
ṉītumut tiriyeṉ ṟōti yeḻuntumuṉ kutitta taṉṟē.

2.12.61

873

iṟiyavaṉ ṟūtik kaṇṭa vaticaya mituko leṉṉa
maṟimaṉa maṟuki lātu vatitaṉip poruntic cēṟa
liṟutiyiṟ ṟiṉpa nampā leytumeṉ ṟakatti ṉeṇṇic
ceṟivaṉaṅ kaṭantu vēṭaṉ ṟicitaṉi yaṭutta taṉṟē.

2.12.62

874

kuruḷiyum piṇiyuṅ kūṭi varuvatu kuṟittu nōkki
murukalar puyattār vaḷḷaṉ mukammatu makiḻntaṉ pāka
viruḷuṟu maṉatta ṉāṉa vēṭaṉi yiṉitu kūvi
yorupiṇik kiraṇṭuṉ pālil varuvateṉ ṟurittiṭ ṭārāl.

2.12.63

875

aṉṉatu kēṭṭu vēṭa ṉōkkiyaṉ puṟṟa kāli
muṉṉiya kaṉṟu māṉu mukammati ṉaṭiyiṟ ṟāḻntu
paṉṉiya calāmuṅ kūṟip pāviyeṟ kāka vēṭṭu
maṉṉiya piṇiyi mīṭṭu meṉumuri vaḻaṅkiṟ ṟaṉṟē.

2.12.64

876

māṭuṟin tivimāṉ kūṟa mukammatu napiyum viṟki
vēṭaṉi viḷittu nantam piṇiyiṉi viṭuttu niṉṟaṉ
pīṭuṭip paciyi māṟṟip perumpatik kaṭika veṉṟār
vīṭupeṟ ṟuyarntu vāḻntē ṉeṉamalarp patattiṉ vīḻntāṉ.

2.12.65

877

pātapaṅ kayattip pōṟṟip paruvara lakaṟṟi yāti
tūtuva rivarē yallā liliyeṉa maṉattiṟ ṟūkki
vētanā yakamē yeṉpāl viruppuṟuṅ kalimāt taṉṉi
yōtumeṉ ṟiruki yēnti yuvantuniṉ ṟurippa tāṉāṉ.

2.12.66

878

karumukiṟ kaviki vēntē kāṉaka vēṭa ṉeṉṉu
muruviṉaṉ vilaṅkō ṭoppē ṉuḷḷaṟi vuṇarvu millēṉ
ṟeruḷuṟap pāvi yeṉṉi tīṉilik kuriya ṉeṉṉap
peritaḷit tiṭutal nunnam perumiyiṟ perumi yeṉṟāṉ.

2.12.67

879

matimuka makiḻcci kūra mukammatu kalimāc colla
vitayamuṟ ṟōti vēṭa ṉiṉitiṉi ṉīmāṉ koṇṭu
putiyiṉi vaṇaṅkic ceyyuñ ceytoḻil poruntak kēṭṭu
nitimaṉik kuriya ṉāki tīṉili neṟiniṉ ṟāṉē.

2.12.68

880

peṟukati niṉṉāṟ peṟṟēṉ perumpavaṅ kaḷintēṉ māṟāt
teṟukoli viḷittu muṉṉañ ceytoḻi ṟaviḻttē ṉīyu
maṟukali yeṟintu tēṟu mṉakkali yoṭukaṉ ṟōṭu
muṟukali yiṭattiṟ pōyccērn toḻukali muyalti yeṉṟāṉ.

2.12.69

881

vāṉavar paravuṅ kōmāṉ mukammatu māṉi nōkkik
kāṉakañ ceṉṉī yeṉṟār kamalameṉ patattiṟ ṟāḻntu
tīṉilik kuriya vēṭaṉ ṟaṉṉiyun tiruntap pōṟṟi
nāṉilam pukalap pāri ṉaṭantiṉañ cērnta taṉṟē.

2.12.70

882

tēṉikkuṅ kumaṅkaḷ cintac ceḻittatiṇ puyattu vaḷḷal
kāṉakkuv viṭattiṟ kāṭṭuṅ kamalameṉ patattip pōṟṟit
tāṉikkum patikku miyāṉē talivaṉeṉ pavarpōl vēṭaṉ
māṉikkoṇ ṭuvarap pōyī māṉikkoṇ ṭakattiṟ pukkāṉ.

2.12.71

883

tuṭavinaṉ malarit tūṟṟun tūyniḻa liṭatti nīntip
paṭarmukiṟ kaviki yōṅkap pāruḷa teviyum vāḻtta
vaṭavari yaṉiya tiṇṭōḷ vayavarka ḷiṉitu cūḻak
kaṭimaṉi yiṭattiṟ pukkār hapīpiṟa cūlū maṉṟē.

2.12.72

māṉukku piṇi niṉṟa paṭalam muṟṟiṟṟu.

uḷḷuṟi aṭṭavaṇikkut tirumpa

2.13 īttaṅkuli varavaḻitta paṭalam (884 - 900 ) 

884

curutiyiṉ muṟiivaḻit tuṇivar cūḻtarak
karumuki ṉiḻaloṭuṅ karuṇi poṅkiṭa
maruvumeṇ ṭicikkumāṉ mataṅka maḻntiṭa
virunilam pukaḻnapi yirukkum pōtiṉil.

2.13.1

885

kāliṉiṟ kapucumōr kiyiṟ kuntamu
mēliṭuñ caṭṭiyum vicitta kacciyun
tōloru tōḷiṉun tūkki vantava
ṉālanan napitami yaṭuttu nōkkiṉāṉ.

2.13.2

886

tariyalark kaṉpuṟuñ cārpi ṉāṉmaṟik
kuruneṟi mukammatik kuṟitte tirntunī
rirunilat tevareṉa viyampi ṉāṉpara
ruranerit tiṭaccevi yuḻukkuñ colliṉāl.

2.13.3

887

accamoṉ ṟiṉṟiniṉ ṟaṟapi kūṟalum
vaccirap puyamukam matutam vāytiṟan
ticcakam pukaḻtaṉi yiṟivaṉ ṟūtiyā
ṉiccaya mituveṉa nikaḻtti ṉārarō.

2.13.4

888

ātitaṉ ṟūteṉa vaṟiva taṟkarum
pūtalat teṉmaṉam porunti yaṉpoṭuñ
cātamuṟ ṟiṭapperuñ cāṭci yāmeṉuṅ
kōtaṟu kuṟippevi kūṟuvī reṉṟāṉ.

2.13.5

889

kāraṇak kariyuṉak kiyiyak kāṇkileṉ
ṉāraṇat tuṟuṅkali māvi yaṉpoṭum
pūraṇa maṉattoṭum pukalvi yōveṉac
cīrtaru mamutavāy tiṟantu ceppiṉār.

2.13.6

890

avaṉiyi levarkkunaṉ kaṟiya veṉmaṉak
kavaraṟak kāraṇak kariyuṇ ṭāmeṉi
ṉapiyumi yalatili yeṉṉa naṇpoṭum
pavamaṟa numvaḻip paṭuva ṉiyāṉeṉṟāṉ.

2.13.7

891

nilattiṉil viṇṇiṉī ṭicikku ṇiṉmaṉa
nalattatu kariyevi nāṭṭu vāyeṉak
kulattuṟu mukammatu kūṟak kēṭṭunaṟ
cilittaḻum piruntō ḷaṟapi ceppuvāṉ.

2.13.8

892

iruvaruk ketirtara niṉṟa vīntatiṉ
viritalik kulimalar vīḻnti ṭātivaṇ
varavaḻit tiṭuvirēṉ maṉamum vākkumot
tarumaṟi moḻivaḻi yāva ṉiyāṉeṉṟāṉ.

2.13.9

893

īntiṉi nōkkiniṉ ṟiṟivaṉ ṟūtuvar
vāyntaniṉ kuliyivaṇ varuka vēṇṭumeṉ
ṟāyntanaṉ maṟiteri yamuta naṉkaṉi
yēyntavāk kiṉittiṟan tiyampi ṉārarō.

2.13.10

894

ātitaṉ ṟūtuva raṟiyak kēṭṭaluñ
cōtiveṇ kuruttoṭun tōṉṟa mēleḻun
tētiṉuñ citikilā tiḻintu mānila
mītiṉil viritali viḷaṅki niṉṟatē.

2.13.11

895

viritalit taruvaṭi niṉṟa meṉkulip
parivoṭum payappayak kutittup pāriṭi
yiruviḻi teripava revarkku miṉpuṟat
tirumukam matumuṉañ ciṟantu niṉṟatē.

2.13.12

896

antara viritalik kaminta pūṅkuli
yintamā nilattiṭi yiṟaṅki yivvuḻi
vantatiṟ putumiyu maṟuttuṇ ṭōveṉac
cintiyuṟ ṟaṟa pinan napiyic cintittāṉ.

2.13.13

897

paṭittalam pukaḻnapi pātam pōṟṟiniṉ
ṟaṭikkaṭip putumiyuṟ ṟaṟapi yīntiṉa
tiṭattiṉiṟ kuliporun tiṭaccey vīreṉat
tiṭattoṭum payattoṭuñ ceppi ṉāṉarō.

2.13.14

898

maḻimukiṟ kavikiyiṉ vaḷḷa ṉaṉkeṉak
kuḻitarum viritalik kuliyip pārttuniṉ
ṉuḻiyiṉiṟ celkeṉa vurippa vōṭimuṟ
puḻivaḻi nuḻintatu porunti niṉṟatē.

2.13.15

899

mētiyac camumuḷa vilaṅki ṉāyatōr
cātiyaṉ ṟītoru tarumuṉ ṉātalum
pōtalum paṭittavar putiya nāyakaṉ
ṟūtuva ruṇmiyeṉ ṟaṭiyic cūṭiṉāṉ.

2.13.16

900

pātapaṅ kayamalar ciraciṟ paṟṟiniṉ
ṟātiyiṟ coluṅkali māvi yaṉpoṭu
mōtiṉaṉ ṟuintaṉa ṉuriya nāyakaṉ
ṟūtuvark kivaṉoru tuṇiva ṉāyiṉaṉ.

2.13.17

īttaṅkuli varavaḻitta paṭalam muṟṟiṟṟu.

2.14 oppeḻutit tīrnta paṭalam (901- 939) 

901

parivuṟu napiyeṉum paṭṭa mākiya
varuṭamē ḻiṉiṟṟiṉa muharra māttiyiṟ
ṟeritarum piṟimuta liraviṟ cēralark
kuriyavar kuṟiṣika ḷoruṅku kūṭiṉār.

2.14.1

902

kaṟikeḻu maṉakkoṭuṅ kāpi rākiya
kuṟiṣiyan talivarkaḷ palaruṅ kūṭṭamiṭ
ṭaṟapika ṭammuṭa ṉāyntu vāymiyāṉ
muṟitava ṟiṭumoru karuma muṉṉiṉār.

2.14.2

903

hāṣimut talipeṉa vaṭuttuk kūṭiya
mācaṟu mirukulat tavariṉ vāṇikam
pēcutal campantam piṟavu nīkkiviṭ
ṭēcaṟu cātiyiṉ vilakkiṭ ṭārarō.

2.14.3

904

neruppunī rivimuta ṉīkki nīṇilat
tiruppava revarumaṅ kavarkki ṭaṅkoṭā
turuppamō ṭikalvatē yevarkku mūḻeṉa
varippuṟa vorumuṟi varintiṭ ṭārkaḷāl.

2.14.4

905

cātiyiṉ vilakkeṉat tavaṟi lāteṭut
tōtiya voppeṉu muṟiyi yūravar
mātira maṭarkaku pāviṉ vāyiliṟ
ṟūtaru maṟiyaveṉ ṟeṭuttuk tūkkiṉār.

2.14.5

906

koṭumaṉak kuṟiṣiyaṅ kāpir kūṭiyap
paṭinaṭat tiṭumannāṭ palaṉkoṇ māmaṟi
piṭipaṭun tīṉava riyārum pētuṟā
tuṭaluyi reṉavuvan toruṅku kūṭiṉār.

2.14.6

907

teritarun tīṉeṟi yavaruñ cērtaru
miruvakik kiḷiñaru micinta pērkaḷun
taruveṉat tarumaput tālip tampuya
variyeṉa vaḷintavar vāḻu nāḷiṉil.

2.14.7

908

pōtalar matīṉamā puratti ṉāṭoṟuṅ
kōtuṟā tavuceṉuṅ kūṭṭat tārkaṭkuṅ
kātiya kacuṟacuk kiḷikkuṅ kaṭṭaṟā
tōtiya perumpaki poḻinta tilliyāl.

2.14.8

909

iciyunūṟ ṟirupatu varuṭa mumkacu
ṟacuveṉuṅ kūṭṭattā ramitta veṟṟiyē
ticipukaḻn taṉaavu ceṉṉun tiṇmiyōr
vicayamō rāṇṭiṉum vēynta tilliyāl.

2.14.9

910

intaval viṉiyiṉā liṭintav vucuḷar
tantama roṭumpalar taṉittu cāviyē
cintiyiṟ ṟuivoṭun terintu pārttunaṉ
mantira mīteṉa vakuttuk kāṭṭiyē.

2.14.10

911

makkamā nakaruṟi maṉṉar tamminam
mokkali liṉpuṟa vuvantu cērttuvan
tikkaṇam kacuṟaci yetirva tallatu
pukkiṭa miliyeṉap poruntak kūṟiṉār.

2.14.11

912

peṟumuṟi yīteṉap pēci nālvarit

2.15 puttu pēciya paṭalam (940 - 951 ) 

940

yeṉacciṟappu niṟimak kāviṟ
kulattaraca riṉituvappak kalimāveṇ

ṭicimuḻutuṅ kulavi yōṅkac
cilittaṭakki vayavēnta riṉitucūḻn

kaṉittatirik kaṭaṉilattiṟ palarpukaḻa

coṉṉatili yōrmoḻiman tirattaṭaṅkit

vurippateṉko luṟutit taṉṟē.

2.15.5

turikkurinērn tiyampu mēyāṟ
paṉṉumaṟi vaḻiyoḻukip paṭitīṇṭā

malaraṭiyip paravi vāḻtti
maṉṉumicu lāmākik kupirakaṟṟit

tīṉilami vaḷarppa ṉeṉṟāṉ.

2.15.6

946

ticimuḻutu morupuṭiyiṟ kiṭattuṅkaru

vicitarumvaṇ ṭimirtoṭiyal puraṇṭaciya

varittaṭattiṇ puyattunaṟi kamaḻntamukam

matutaṇmati vataṉa nērā
yiruttiyoru pāliruntāṉ mummaṟiyun

terintumaṉat tirutti ṉōṉē.

2.15.8

yiruntōṉṟaṉ maṉatu kūra

meyttavuri maṟippērum viṇṇiṉumaṇ

ṇiṉumaṟiya viḷampu vāyeṉ
ṟuttamacaṟ kuṇanayiṉā ramutamalar

vāytiṟantaṅ kōti ṉārāl.

2.15.9

949

2.15.10

2.16 pirāṭṭiyār poṉṉulaku pukka paṭalam (952- 967)

2.16.6

2.16.8

riṟanta kāliyiṟ kaṭaluṭin teṉanaka riraṅkac

yaṟaṅki ṭantaneñ cavaroṭu maḻutiraṅ kiṉarāl.

2.16.13

2.17 paruppata rājaṉik kaṇṇuṟṟa paṭalam (968-1002)

2.19 jiṉkaḷ īmāṉ koṇṭa paṭalam (1015 - 1057)

ṟaṅkiṉar paṟavi tattaṅ kuṭampiyiṟ cāra vavavip

2.19.2

1017

nalaṉuṟu nakulā veṉṉa nāṭṭiya talatti ṉōrpāṟ

2.19.3

1018

yulakelāṅ koṭuṅkō lōcci yorukuṭi niḻaliṟ ṟāṅkip
palakali maruvalārkkup paṭiṟeṉum paṭina ṭāttun
talimiyaṉ ciṟumi kīḻmi taṉipperu miyatāyk koṇṭōṉ.

2.19.4

1019

koliyiṉuk kuriya tanti kōḷuyirt tuṇivaṉ māṟā
nilikeṭuṅ karavuk kaṉpa ṉintaṉik kuṟṟa tampi
yilipiḻi matuvuk kīṉṟa cēyiṉu miṉiya ṉīṇṭa
vulakiṉiṉ māyamellā mōruru veṭuttu niṉṟōṉ.

2.19.5

1020

eṇṇiṟan taṉiya kālamirun tiṟi yēvaṉ māṟi
viṇṇila kiḻantu meymi vitimaṟi taṉakku nāṇi
maṇṇilat tiruntu vāḻu māṉuṭa revarkkum veyya
taṇṇiya ṉipulī ceṉṉun taṉipperu nāmat tāṉē.

2.19.6

1021

makkaṭaṅ kuḻuviṉ viki mantiran talivar cūḻa

pakkali lurippa nōkki vammeṉap pariviṟ coṉṉāṉ.

2.19.7

1022

aruntavan tavaṟi niṉṟa varacaṉī turippak kēṭṭup
peruntokik kuḻuvi ṉōṭum peri teḻun tāḻicūḻa
viruntavi yakatti kānta meṭṭiṉun tēṭic ceṉṟu
pirintati loṉpāṉ jiṉkaḷ pēraṟa paṭinta vaṉṟē.

2.19.8

1023

aṟṟiyiṟ poḻuti rāvil rakaciyat toḻuki yaṉpāy
muṟṟuṟa muṭittu vaḷḷaṉ mutalavaṉ ṟaṉṉi yēttik
kuṟṟamaṟ ṟirantu niṉṟa vacaṉattiṉ kuṟippuk kāti
ṉuṟṟaṭut toruṅku nōkki yōriṭat tuṟinta vaṉṟē.

2.19.9

1024

tarippoṭun tuvāvi yōtit taṉinaku lāvi ṉōrparl
viruppoṭu mirappak kēṭṭu mikamakiḻn titaya nōkki
yiruppatu napiyē vāykoṇ ṭicippatu puṟukkā ṉeṉṉat
tirupputaṟ karitāy niṉṟu jiṉkaṇ meycilirtta vaṉṟē.

2.19.10

1025

ittiṉat tiṉilaṉ pāka veḻiṉapi kamala pāta
muttipeṟ ṟīmāṉ koṇṭu mutaṟpavan tuṭippō meṉṉa
vottita mittut tammi loṉṟukkoṉ ṟuṟuti kūṟip
pattiyuḷ ḷirutti nāṭṭat tuṭaṉ vuippaṭṭa vaṉṟē.

2.19.11

1026

paṉiyoṭu timira mūṭap paṭavaru miraviṉ kaṇṇē
taṉiyirun teḻiṉmeyc cōti tayaṅkiya napimuṉ pāka
viṉiyavar pōlac ceṉṟu vantavā ṟeṭuttuk kūṟik
kaṉiyeṉa nekiḻnta neñciṟ karuttiyuṅ kūṟa luṟṟa.

2.19.12

1027

piṉṉaṇit tāti tūtar piṟappareṉ ṟāti nūlkaḷ
paṉṉiya tuḷatiṉ ṟeṅkaṭ pārvikaḷ kuḷirak kaṇṭē
muṉṉināṭ pavaṅka ṭīrttē mukammatē yeṉṉap pōṟṟic
coṉṉayak kalimā vōtic cuṭarppatan toḻutu pōṉa

2.19.13

1028

naṉkali māvi yōti naṟumaṉak kaḷippi ṉōṭuñ
jiṉkaṭam miṉattic cērntu ceṉṟatu maṟapu nāṭṭiṉ
miṉkaṭan tilaṅkuñ cōti virintamey mukamma teṉṉuṅ
koṉkatir vēlārk kīmāṉ koṇṭatu murittuk kūṟum.

2.19.14

1029

pūtalat tiṭattiṉ makkā purattiṉiṉ mukamma teṉpōrk
kākita ṉaruḷāṟ ṟūteṉ ṟarunapip paṭṭam vantu
vētamu miṟaṅkit tiṉpat tīṉeṟi viḷakkañ ceytār
pētama taṉṟu kāṇā tiruppatum piḻiya taṉṟē.

2.19.15

1030

vañcaka ṉipulī ceṉpōṉ vārttiyu ḷaṭaṅkip pēti
neñciṉa rākit tīyō reṉanili niṉṟōm vēṟoṉ
ṟañcalit taṟiyōm nallōrk kavamviḷit tōmī tellām
nañcuṟi narakam pukku neṟiyalā ṉalaṉu muṇṭō.

2.19.16

1031

napitirup pāta naṇṇi naṉṉeṟi muṟiva ḻātōr
puviyiṭat tiṉitu vāḻntu poṉṉula kāḷva reṉṟār
kaviṉuṟum periyōr vētaṅ kāṭṭiya neṟiyu mītē
tavirkilā teḻuka veṉṉac cāṟṟiṉa jiṉka ḷaṉṟē.

2.19.17

1032

kūṟiya moḻiyik kēṭṭuk kuḻuvuṭa ṉirunta jiṉka
ṭēṟiya karuttu ḷottut tērnteḻun taviṭa nīntip
pēṟuṭi makka meṉṉum perumpati yaṭuttōr jiṉṉi
yīṟilāṉ ṟūtark kaṉpāyt tūtuviṭ ṭirutta vaṉṟē.

2.19.18

10331

naṟikoḷuñ cevvit tiṇṭō ṉapinaku lāvi nīnti
yiṟivaṉē valiṉāl vāṉō reṇṇilar cūḻac celvaṅ
kuṟivaṟā makka meṉṉuṅ koḻunaka rataṉiṉ vantār
maṟipaṭa viruntu jiṉkaḷ varaviṭun tūtum vanta.

2.19.19

1034

vantatū tirunta cevvi matimukam matuvik kaṇṭu
kantameṉ malarttāḷ vīḻntu kikuvit teḻuntu pōṟṟic
cintiyiṉ makiḻntaṉ pākac ciṉkaḷāl viṭukka vanta
cantiyā ṉeṉṉac cāṟṟip piṉṉaruñ cāṟṟu maṉṟē.

2.19.20

1035

eṅkaṭaṅ kulatti ṉuḷḷā reṇṇilar nakarkka ṇittāy
maṅkuliṉ kaviki yōynum malarpataṅ kaṇṭu tīṉi
ṉiṅkitat toṭumī māṉkoṇ ṭēkutaṟ kicintu niṉṟā
raṅkeḻun taruḷa vēṇṭu meṉṟiṉi taṟinta taṉṟē.

2.19.21

1036

jiṉṉivi yurippak kañcac ceḻumuka malarntu vētam
paṉṉiya picumi lōtip paṇpuṭa ṉeḻuntu vaḷḷal
poṉṉaṇi māṭa vīti nakarppuṟat taṭuttuk kūṇṭu
maṉṉiya kuḻuviṉ vantār mānilan taḻikka vantār.

2.19.22

1037

karumuki ṉiḻaṟṟak kañcak katantari paṭātu nāṉam
poruvaṟak kamaḻavanta puṇṇiyap poruḷik kaṇṭu
tirumukat tetirntu jiṉka ṭiraḷoṭu miṟiñci vāḻttip
paruvara lakaṟṟit tēṟac cilamoḻi pakaru maṉṟē.

2.19.23

1038

vāṉakat tamarar ceyya malaraṭi paravi yētta
nāṉilat tariya vēta napiyeṉum paṭṭa numpā
lāṉataṟ kurittā yeṅka ḷakattiṉiṟ kaḷaṅka meṉṉu
mūṉamaṟ ṟiṭavē ṟuṇmi yuṟutiyoṉ ṟaṟiya vēṇṭum.

2.19.24

1039

vaṉkaḷaṅ kakaṟṟit tīṉiṉ vaḻinili kuṟittu vanta
jiṉkaḷiṟ ṟalami yāṉa jiṉkaḷiv vuriyit tēṟṟap
poṉkaṭan toḷirun tiṇṭōṭ puravala riṟacū lullā
viṉkaḷip poḻuka nōkki yeṭutturi koṭuppa tāṉār.

2.19.25

1040

kuvaṭuṟi vilaṅki ṉālō koḻuñciṟip paṟavi yālō
tavaḻtaru muyiri ṉālō tarukkaḷi ṉālō vuṅkaḷ
ceviyaṟin titayaṅ kūrntu teritara veṉṉi yinta
vavaṉiyi luṇmit tūteṉ ṟaṟiyavēṇ ṭuvatē teṉṟār.

2.19.26

1041

pētamaṟ ṟurittīr cōti perukutīṉ viḷakkē yintap
pātiyiṟ ṟaruvan teṅkaḷ pārviyiṟ kaṇittāy niṉṟu
tūtuva reṉṟōr māṟṟañ collumēṟ kalimā vōtik
kōtaṟa maṉattu ḷīmāṉ koḷvatu tiṇṇameṉṟa.

2.19.27

1042

kaṭattiṉmā ṉurippa niṉṟa kāraṇak kurici ṟūrat
tiṭattiṉi ṉiṉṟa vañcit taruviṉi yetirntu nōkki
paṭittalat tuṟinta vēriṉ paṟṟaṟā teḻuntu veṟpi
ṉaṭuttivaṇ vāveṉ ṟiṉpa vamutavāy tiṟantu coṉṉār.

2.19.28

1043

pācaṭi kuḻitta vañcit tarupaṭi tuḷitutuḷ ḷōṭi
vīiciya kavaṭṭuc cilli vēriloṉ ṟaṟāta vaṇṇa
mācaṟa veḻuntu cevvi mukammatiṉ pāta nōkkik
kāciṉi yiṭattiṟ ṟōyak kaviṉpeṟap paṭinta taṉṟē.

2.19.29

1044

palaṉuṟuṅ kalimāt taṉṉip paṇareṉum palaki yāra
nilipeṟu maṟapi ṉāṉaṉ ṉeṭunilat teḻutic ciṉkaḷ
kulaṉoṭu miṉitu kāṇak koḻuntaḻi kuḻiya vūrntu
kalimukam matutam muṉkaṇ kaḷittiṭa niṉṟa taṉṟē.

2.19.30

1045

niṉṟamā maratti nōkki neṟipaṭa vevaruṅ kēṭpa
viṉṟeṉi yivarkaṭ kiṉṉā reṉaveṭut tiyampu keṉṉa
maṉṟalaṅ kuricil kūṟa malarili kuluṅka vāṭā
veṉṟiko ḷiṟiyō ṇuṇmit tūteṉa viḷampiṟ ṟaṉṟē.

2.19.31

1046

cāṭiṉi teḻuntu vantu tavaṟilā turitta māṟṟaṅ
kūṭiya jiṉka ḷellāñ cevimaṉaṅ kuḷirak kēṭṭu
nīṭiya vuvaki yeṉṉu neṭuṅkaṭa ṉīnti nīntit
tēṭiya poruḷi teṉṉac cēvaṭi ciraciṟ koṇṭa.

2.19.32

1047

cītameṉ kaviki nīḻaṟ ṟirunapi yiṟacū lullā
pātapaṅ kayatti muttik kaṇmalar pariviṟ cāttik
kōtaṟuṅ kalimā vōtik kuḻuvoṭu mīmāṉ koṇṭu
vētanaṉ ṉilimi nīṅkā meynneṟi mēvi niṉṟa.

2.19.33

1048

kalineṟi pakarum vaḷḷal kāṉakat taruvi nōkki
yulivilā tuṉatu tāṉat tuṟikeṉa vurippat tīṉi
ṉalaṉuṟu mapulkā cīmtam nallici ticika ṭōṟu
nilipeṟa niṉṟa teṉṉa neṟicceṉṟu niṉṟa taṉṟē.

2.19.34

1049

kaṇattoṭum jiṉkaḷ vaḷḷal kamalameṉ mukatti nōkki
yiṇittanaṉ ṉeṟiyi ṉiṉṟō miṉṟutoṭ ṭiṉimē luṅkaṭ
kuṇattaka vuṇavī tiṉṉa teṉaveṭut turiyu meṉṉap
paṇittuvāy putittu niṉṟu paṇpuṟap pakarnta vaṉṟē.

2.19.35

1050

paṟaviyil vilaṅki luḷḷa paṭippiṉiṟ ṟakku pīri
liṟiyavaṉ vititta vaṇṇat tiṟantateṉ paviyu nuṅkaṭ
kuṟipacik kuṇaveṉ ṟaṉpā yōtiṉar kēṭṭu mīṭṭu
maṟuvaṟa veṅkaṭ kuṟṟa vākaṉat tuṇavē teṉṟa.

2.19.36

1051

tēṟiya maṟiyiṉ ṟīñcol tīṉilik kuriya tūyō
rēṟuvā kaṉantiṉ ṟaṟṟa teviyuḷa vavika ḷellā
māṟupā ṭaṉṟi nuṅkaḷ vākaṉat tuṇavē yeṉṉak
kūṟiṉar piṇikki yāṉē piṇiyeṉak kūṟuṅ koṇṭal.

2.19.37

1052

viritaru mamutac cevvāy tiṟantivi viḷampak kēṭṭut
tirumukat tetirnta paṉṉī rāyirañ jiṉka ṭaṅkaḷ
ciramaṭi malariṟ cērttit tīṉavar tamiyum vāḻttip
parivoṭu makiḻntu tattan ticiyiṉiṟ paṭarnta vaṉṟē.

2.19.38

1053

paraviyum vicumpum pārum paṭarntiruḷ ceṟintu tōṉṟu
miraviṉiṟ ṟiraṇṭa jiṉka ḷiṉattiṉi yīmāṉ koḷvit
tariyaṭa lēṟa teṉṉa vaḻakoḷi virittuk kāṭṭa
marumalark kariya kūntaṉ mayiluṟi maṉiyiṉ vantār.

2.19.39

1054

neruṅkiya kaṅkuṟ pōti ṉiṟintaval liruḷi mōti
yiruṅkatir karaṅka ḷāra veṭutteṭut teṟintu cinti
yaruṅkaṇa maṉittu nāṇi yakalvicum poḷippa nōkkik
karuṅkaṭaṉ mukaṭṭil veyya katiravaṉ ṟōṉṟi ṉāṉē.

2.19.40

1055

kāciṉi yiṭatti ṉaṟṟik kāliyiṉ kaṭaṉ ṭīrttu
nēcamuṟ ṟuvantu tīṉō ruṭaṉiṉi tuṟintu jiṉkaḷ
mūcivan tīmāṉ koṇṭu pōyatu muṟi vaḻāmaṟ
pēcinal luṇavu mīnta ceytiyum piṟakkac colvār.

2.19.41

1056

maṟaccilik karattīr tīṉiṉ māṉuṭar vāyiṉ micci
liṟicciyeṉ paṉittuñ jiṉkaṭ kuṇaveṉa vīntēṉmēluṅ
kaṟittaveṉ piṟicci micci leṉpatik kaḷaṅka millāp
puṟattiṉil vīca liyārkkuṅ kaṭaṉeṉap poruntac coṉṉār.

2.19.42

1057

pūmaṇam poruntak kāṭṭum putumikaṇ ṭariya jiṉka
ṭāmati yātu kūṭit taḷattoṭun tiraṇṭu vantī
māṉmaṉam poruntiṟ ṟeṉṟa vārttiyiṟ puḷakaṅ koṇṭu
tēmalarp puyattār pōṟṟat tirunapi yiruntā rippāl.

2.19.43

jiṉka ḷīmāṉ koṇṭa paṭalam muṟṟiṟṟu.
ākap paṭalam 19kkut tiruviruttam...1057
uḷḷuṟi aṭṭavaṇikkut tirumpa

2.20 kāmmāp paṭalam (1058 - 1086) 

1058

nariyoḷi piṟaṅka vuṭampelān tirintu

narampuka ṭerintiṭa vaṟantu
teritaruṅ kaṭpā viyiṉoḷi maḻuṅkit

tiraḷpaṭap pīḻiyuñ cāṭit
taripaṭā nācit tuḷiyiṉīr tatumpat

tittaṟak kiḻintatōr tuṇiyu
mariyiṭik kiṭantu carintaṭik kaṭivīḻn

taviḻntiṭa vorukaran tāṅka.

2.20.1

1059

uṭaṟkuṟi kūṉuñ cevittuḷi yaṭippu

morukiyiṟ raṭikkuḷā taravi
ṉaṭakkiyi ṉaṭakkun talikkiṭu kiṭuppu

naṉitara vacintu taḷḷāṭi
yaṭikkaṭi yiḷippiṟ kulukkiya kaṉippu

maṟattavit teḻuntakō lamumā
yiṭukkaṇuṟ ṟoruvaṉ mukammatu nayiṉā

riruntiṭu maviyakat tetirntāṉ.

2.20.2

1060

uḷḷuṟak kiṭanta pallaṟap peyarnta

vutaṭṭiṉil vāyiṉī roḻuka
viḷḷutaṟ karitā yorucalāṅ kuḻaṟi

viḷampiniṉ ṟaṉaṉmuka nōkki
vaḷḷalum piratti yurittivaṉ cūmaṉ

vaṅkiṣat tuḷaṉoru vēṭat
teḷḷaruṅ kuṇattā laṭintaṉa ṉivaṉkū

ṟīteṉa maṉattirut tiṉarē.

2.20.3

1061

ūṉṟiya taṭiyiṟ kiṭantuḻaṉ ṟotuṅki

niṉṟava ṉuḻiyiṉi nōkki
vāṉṟikaḻ pukaḻār tirumoḻi koṭuttu

varavaḻit torumaruṅ kirutti
yīṉṟava riyāva revvaḻik kuḷaniṉ

ṉiruṅkulap peyariyā tuṉakkut
tōṉṟiya nāma mētivi viṭuttuc

colleṉa mīḷavu murittār.

2.20.4

1062

napiyeṉum peyarpeṟ ṟavarkkevar karuttu

naṉkuṟat terintiṭum vicumpi
ṉavariṉum putiyōṉ ṟūtariṉ mutalō

ravaṉiyiṟ piṉvarum nayiṉā
rivarkarut taṟiyat terintiṭāp poruḷka

ḷiliyeṉak karuttiṉi liruttit
tavaṟuvan tatuntaṉ talimuṟip peyarun

taṉittaṉi viṭutteṭut turippāṉ.

2.20.5

1063

vāṉula kaṭaṅkat taṉvacap paṭutti

maṟuvaṟum peyarkkiṭar viḷittup
pāṉiṟa vaḷiveṇ ṭirikkaṭaṟ parappiṟ

pakiyaṟa vorutaṉik kōlāṟ
ṟāṉeṉac celutti yaracuvīṟ ṟiruntōṉ

ṟaṇappilāp perumpaṭi yuṭiyō
ṉīṉamuṟ ṟoḻiyā māyikaḷ viḷikku

miyalipu līceṉum peyarōṉ

2.20.6

1064

apperum pukaḻōṉ ṟarutiru matali

yavaṉiṉu mummaṭaṅ kāki
mupperu nilattun taṉpeyar niṟuttu

muṟimiya ṉāḷiyā caṉattā
ṉopparun tiṟalā ṉilākkiceṉ ṟōṅki

yuṟumpeya riṉaṉpeṟum putalvaṉ
kippaṭuṅ katirvāṭ perumpaṭik kiṟivaṉ

kāyimeṉ ṟurittakā valavaṉ.

2.20.7

1065

kāyimeṉ pavaṉṟaṉ kaṇṇiṉi maṇiyāyk

karuttiṉuḷ ḷuṟintameyp poruḷāyc
cēyeṉap piṟantē ṉicipeṟak kāmmā

veṉṉumap peyariṉaṉ ciṟiyē
ṉāyiran tiruppērk kuriyavaṉ ṟūtē

yamararuk kariyanā yakamē
māyirum puvimā ṉiṭariṭar kaḷiyu

mukammatē yeṉappukaḻn ticittāṉ.

2.20.8

1066

maruṅkiṉi liruntu pakarntakām mātaṉ

vārttikēṭ ṭakattiṉiṟ kaḷittut
taruṅkatirt taraḷa nakiyiṟpuṉ muṟuva

ṟaravaru viruttaṉi nōkki
neruṅkiṭa vaṟanta kāṟaṭu māṟa

neṭitucañ calattoṭum varunti
yiruṅkaṇa maṭutteṉ ṉiṭattiṉi luṟinta

teṉṉiṉi veṉaveṭut ticittār.

2.20.9

1067

muṉṉeṭuṅ kālat tipperum puvaṉa

muḻutiṉu morukuṭi nīḻa
ṟaṉṉiṭip paṭutti nālvakik katatta

taḷattoṭu morutaṉik kōlāṉ
maṉṉiya ticikaḷ potuvaṟap purantu

maruvala riliyeṉat taṭintiṭ
ṭeṉṉiyop pavarin nilattiṉi liliyeṉ

riruntaṉa ṉāḷiyā caṉattil.

2.20.10

1068

orutaṉit tikiri celuttiyen nilamu

muḷḷaṭip paṭuttiṭu nāḷiṟ
poruvaru matattāṟ ṟavaṅkuṇa mirakkam

poṟiniṟi puṇṇiyam piṟavun
terivarā tikaḻntu pavampaḻi toṭarac

ceyvaḻi muṟimiyiṟ ceytēṉ
perukiya valiyuñ cīrttiyu muṭiyōy

piṉvarun tuṉpamoṉ ṟaṟiyēṉ.

2.20.11

1069

tīviṉik kurittāy varuntoḻi laṉittuñ

ceytara cirukkuman nāḷiṟ
pāviyeṉ ṉuṭalu mitayamu naṭuṅkap

pārtteṉik kaṭintuvaṟ puṟuttik
kūvimuṉ ṉiruttit tāṭperu viralka

ḷiraṇṭiyuṅ kūṭṭuṟa nerukki
nōvara viṟukak kaṭṭivit teḻuntu

pōyiṉa ṉorunotu malaṉē

2.20.12

1070

kāliṉiṟ piṇitta piṇippiṉi valitiṟ

kaḻaṟṟiṉa ṉōkkiṉaṉ kaḻalā
tōliṭuṅ kaṭakak karattiṉā laviḻttē

ṉaviḻntiṭā toruṅkuniṉ ṟavarkaḷ
mēluṟa vakirntuiṅ karuviyā laṟuttum

virintacen neruppiṭi koṭuttu
nūlaḷa veṉiṉu nekiḻntila vataṉiṉ

valiyiṉi nuvalutaṟ karitē

2.20.13

1071

kaṭṭiṉāṉ mikuti varuttamuṟ ṟoṭuṅkik

kalaṅkiṉaṉ malaṅkiṉa ṉeṭunāḷ
viṭṭiṭā tiḻitta pāvaṅka ṭiraṇṭu

vekuṇṭoru kayiṟṟuru veṭukkap
paṭṭatō valateṉ ṉūḻvitip payaṉō

paṭipuran tiṭumperum palaṉō
kiṭṭiya tavattōr muṉintiṭu muṉivō

veṉak kiṭantaṉaṉmati yiliyēṉ.

2.20.14

1072

araciḻan teṉatu kiḷiyiṉiṟ periyō

riṭattiṉu maṭuttaṉa ṉavarā
lorutiruk keṭuttu nekiḻkkavum payamuṟ

ṟoṭuṅkiṉar peruvari yiṭattuṅ
kurikaṭaliṭattu meṇṭici purakkuṅ

koṟṟava riṭattiṉu maṭintē
ṉirumeṉa virutti nōkkuva ralateṉ

ṉiṭartavirt tiṭupava riliyē.

2.20.15

1073

antaram puvimaṭ ṭulaviyuṅ kāṟkaṭ

ṭaviḻkkaval lamiyiṉa riliyeṉ
ṟintaṉa meriyiṟ kiṭanteṉa vitaya

miṭintiṭa vuṭintaṉa ṉuiyēṉ
puntiyaṟ ṟoṭuṅki yaḷavaṟuṅ kālam

pōyapi ṉavaṉiyiṉ maṉuviṟ
cuntarat toṭumpē raṟattoṭu muruvāyt

tōṉṟiṉa rātameṉ ṟoruvar.

2.20.16

1074

avaṉi yilāta napiyeṉum pērpeṟ

ṟiruntaṉa ravariṭat tēkit
tavirkilā tiṭaruṟ ṟaṉaṉeṉa vuiyēṉ

kāṟṟiṉaṉ vīkkiṉi nōkki
kavaraṟap piṇitta kāvala ṉalatu

kaṭṭaṟup pavareva reṉṉak
kuvitarun tiruvāy viritara vurittār

koṭiyaṉeṉ valikuṟin tiṭavē

2.20.17

1075

ivvuri pakarntā rātanaṉ ṉapiyeṉ

ṟiruntaṉaṉ varuntiṉa ṉataṟpiṉ
kuvviṉa ṉapimā reṉṉumap peyarpeṟ

ṟiruntava riṭantoruṅ kuṟukic
cevviti ṉurittē ṉavvava revarun

tirunapi mukammateṉ pavarāl
vavviya taḷiviṭ ṭakaṉṟiṭu malatu

maṟuttevar tavirppareṉ ricittār.

2.20.18

1076

aṉṉava ruritta moḻimaṉat taṭakki

yiruntaṉa ṉaṟiveṉun tuṇiyā
lenneṭuṅ kālat teppuvi yiṭatti

ṉiṉitoṭum piṟappareṉ ṟeṇṇip
paṉṉeṭuṅ kāla mituniṉi valatu

vēṟuri pakarntirun taṟiyēṉ
maṉṉiya pukaḻār mukammatu piṟantā

reṉumuri maṟikaḷcoṟ ṟaṉavē.

2.20.19

1077

puviyiṉi laṟattiṉ makkamā purattiṟ

potuvaṟa vētamu miṟaṅki
napiyeṉun tiruppaṭ ṭamuntarit tariya

naṉṉilit tīṉeṟi naṭattik
kuvikupi rakaṟṟi yiruntaṉa reṉṉak

kōtilā maṉamakiḻ vuṭaṉē
yivaṇilvan taṭintē ṉiṉiviṉip pavaṅka

ḷiyāviyu muitiṉveṉ ṟaṉaṉē.

2.20.20

1078

patatti ṉilaṭinta paviyeṉ maṉattiṟ

paruvaraṟ kaḷaṅkaṟat tuṭittuk
katattoṭu miṟukki vittapā takaṉṟaṉ

kaṭṭaṟak karuṇiyiṟ paṭutti
yitattoṭu mumatu tīṉvaḻik kuriya

ṉivaṉeṉa niṟuttimē liyiṉum
vitittacoṟ kaṭavāp paṭinaṭat tiṭuka

vēṇṭumeṉ ṟurittiṭi vīḻntāṉ.

2.20.21

1079

iraṅkiniṉ ṟiṟiñci yurittavā cakatti

yirucevi kuḷirtarak kēṭṭu
neruṅkiṭa viṟukki vittavar peyari

niṉittaru ḷoṭumuṟu valittu
maruṅkiṉi loṭuṅki yiruntakām māvi

viḻittaṇi maturavāy tiṟantuṉ
ṉuraṅkeṭa viṭukkaṇ viḷitta variyāva

ruriyeṉa mukammatu murittār.

2.20.22

1080

kaṭṭivit takaṉṟa nāṭṭoṭut tavaṉṟaṉ

peyariṉik karuttiṉi laṟiyē
ṉeṭṭiyet talattun tirintaṉa ṉivvū

ruḷaṉeṉa vaṟikilēṉ puviyiṉ
maṭṭaṟuṅ kulatti likkulat tiṉṉāṉ

makaveṉṟu maṟikilē ṉetirntu
kiṭṭiṭi lurukkaṇ ṭuitiṉi laṟivē

ṉeṉakkiḷat tiṉaṉperuṅ kiḷiyōṉ.

2.20.23

1081

karumukiṟ kaviki nannapi kāmmā

vurittacoṟ karuttiṉi liruttik
kurutiyun taciyuñ citaṟuceṅ katirvēṟ

koḻuntaṭak karatta pittāli
puriyakaṇ maṇiyāy varumali tamiyeṉ

ṉuḻiyiṉiṟ koṭuvaru keṉṉap
pariviṉiṟ ṟūti viṭuttaṉa ravarum

paṇpuṟa virivoṭu meḻuntār.

2.20.24

1082

ciṅkavē ṟaṉiya alitirukka rattiṟ

ceṅkatir vāṭkiṭan tilaṅkat
taṅkiya maravat toṭipuraṇ ṭaciyat

tāṉavaṉ pulivara ṉōkki
yaṅkamu maṉamum veruvarat tiṭukkiṭ

ṭalamvara veḻuntuvāy kuḻaṟip
paṅkamuṟ ṟayarntiṭ ṭaṭikkaṭi nōkki

pataṅkara naṉinaṭu naṭuṅki.

2.20.25

1083

maṉṉarmaṉ ṉavari mukammati nōkki

vāyvui ṟiṭaviḻi cuḻala
venniṭi yoḷittiṭ ṭotuṅkuṟa voṭuṅki

viṟaṟpuli yalitamit tūṇṭi
eṉṉiyuṅ keṭutteṉ ṉaraciyu maḻittiṭ

ṭittaṉik kiyaṟṟiya cīmā
ṉaṉṉava ṉalatu vēṟili yiṉamvan

taṭukkileṉ viḷiyumō vaṟiyēṉ.

2.20.26

1084

meypporuṇ maṟikku nāyakap poruḷē

viṇṇava ruyiriṉuk kuyirē
yippuvi yiṭatti laṭikkala maṭiyē

ṉeṉippiṇit taṭalvali yeṟinta
tuppiṉa ṉītō vaṭuttaṉaṉ caṟṟē

tūraniṉ ṟiṭavaruḷ paṇitteṉ
kippaṭa nuntaṅ karaṅkoṭut tuyirik

kāppatu kaṭaṉeṉak karintāṉ.

2.20.27

1085

veruvuṟēl kāmmā veṉakkara macittu

viṟaṟpuli yalitami nōkki
yerikatir vēlōy nammiṭat taṭintō

ṉīṅkiva ṉiṭaruṟu miṇittāṭ
peruviraṟ ṟoṭuppi viṭuppiyeṉ ṟiṉitiṉ

periyavaṉ ṟūtuva rurippa
variyeṉa makiḻntu nōkkaluṅ kāṟkaṭ

ṭaṟṟiṭat tuṉpamu maṟutta.

2.20.28

1086

kāṟṟaḷi yakalap payaṅkara makaṟṟik

kāvalar mukammati yiṟiñcip
pōṟṟiniṉ ṟamuta meṉuṅkali māvi

yurittunal vaḻiyiṉiṟ pukuntu
tēṟṟunaṉ maṟiyiṉ mutiyarip pukaḻntu

cevviya ralipatam vaḻutti
māṟṟarum vēṭan taṉiyumviṭ ṭoḻintu

mativalā ṉeṉattaṉi niṉṟāṉ.

2.20.29

1087

cītavoṇ katircey mukammati ṉaṭiyiṟ

ceṉṉivit taṭikkaṭi pukaḻntu
kōtaṟa veḻuntu tīṉva revarkkuṅ

kuṟivaṟac calāmeṭut turittuk
kātaliṉ tīṉtīṉ viḷaṅkaveṉ ṟēttik

kaṭimalarc cōliyu nīnti
mātavam peruku maṉattiṉaṉ kāmmā

maṉṉutaṉ ṟiciyiṉiṟ pōṉāṉ.

2.20.30

kāmmāp paṭalam muṟṟiṟṟu.
ākap paṭalam 20kkut tiruviruttam....1086
uḷḷuṟi aṭṭavaṇikkut tirumpa

2.21 viruntūṭṭup paṭalam (1087-1104) 

1087

puviyi liṉpam poruntip pukaḻpeṟu
napimu kammatu naṇpoṭun taṅkiḷi
yavarkaṭ kaṉpuṟ ṟariya virunteṉak
kavalu meṉṟalik kōtiṉar kāmuṟṟē.

2.21.1

1088

tūya tūtuva rōtiya coṉmaṟā
nēya muṟṟeḻun taṅkavar nīṇmaṉi
vāyi ṟōṟu naṭantunal vākkoṭum
pōyi runtu viruntu pukaṉṟaṉar

2.21.2

1089

maṟuvi nāṟu mukammatuk kaṉpuṟu
maṟiva rummava riyarkku muṉṉaruñ
ciṟiya tantiya rummavar cēykaḷu
muṟavi ṉuṟṟava rummava rokkalum

2.21.3

1090

vaṉṟi ṟaṟpuli vāḷali muṉvara
miṉṟa vaḻntaṇi yāraṅkaḷ vīciṭat
toṉṟu tōṉṟiya tūtuvar māmaṉi
muṉṟi leṅkaṇu moyttirun tārkaḷāl.

2.21.4

1091

ciṟiyar pētiyar tīīyppaci tīṇṭiya
vaṟiya rallatu vantavar nāṟpaḵ
taṟivar hāṣim kiḷikkuyi rāyiṉō
riṟivaṉ ṟūtuvark kiṉpuṟu māntarē.

2.21.5

1092

eṭutti ṟātta leṉumpati ṉāṟeṭi
koṭutta pāluṅ kuṭittoru mēḻakat
taṭitta ciccuṭu kōliṉiṟ ṟaḷḷumu
ṉeṭuttut tiṉpa rivarciṟi yōrkaḷē.

2.21.6

1093

varici vaḷḷaṉ mukammatu vantuniṉ
ṟuriya kēḷi ruṭaṉuḻi yōriyum
viriyuṅ kānti viritta virippiṉ mēṟ
parivi ṉīṇavi pattivit tārarō.

2.21.7

1094

māṟṟa larkkari yēṟeṉum vaḷḷalār
tīṟṟu veṇcuti māṭattuṭ ceṉṟaṭa
vūṟṟup pāliyu mūṟṟikkoṇ ṭiṅṅaṉañ
cōṟṟi yuṅkoṭu vāvaṉac colliṉār.

2.21.8

1095

maruṅku niṉṟavar māma ṉiyuṭpukun
toruṅki runta vorupaṭic cōṟṟiyuṅ
karuṅka leṉṉuṅ kalacattiṟ pāliyun
taruṅki vaḷḷa liṭaṅkoṭu cārntaṉar.

2.21.9

1096

aruntuñ cōṟṟiyum pāliyu maṅkiyi
lorutta rēnti yulāvukiṉ ṟārivar
varutta miṉṟi varavaḻit tāḷelā
miruttu kiṉṟaṉa reṉṉeṉak kūṟuvār.

2.21.10

1097

iṟṟi nāḷvirun teṉṉaviv vūriṉiṟ
coṟṟa tillittoṉ māmaṟik kāraṇat
tuṟṟa ceyti yaṟiyaveṉ ṟuṉṉiyō
paṟṟi ṉālivar pālaḻit tāreṉpār

2.21.11

1098

iṉṟu vantivar muṉṟi liruntaṉam
veṉṟi yāka viruntu vaḻaṅkiṭuñ
coṉṟi yummivar kāraṇat tōṟṟamu
naṉaṟu kaṇṭaṟi vōmiṉi nāmeṉpār.

2.21.12

1099

inta vaṇṇa mivarka ḷiyampiṭa
vanta nāyakaṉ ṟūtuva raṉpoṭu
vanta cōṟṟiyum pāliyu maṉṉavar
cinti kūrac ciṟantaḷit tārarō.

2.21.13

1100

cēruñ cīṉiyun tēṉumot tāleṉa
mūra lummavi mūḻkiya pāliyum
vīrar taṅkaḷ vilāppuṟam vīṅkiṭa
vāra vuṇṭeṉa raṅkiyil vāriyē.

2.21.14

1101

māta ruñciṟu mintaru māntarum
pēta maṟṟatam milluṟi pērkaḷuṅ
kōti lātuṇṭu pāluṅ kuṭittiṉip
pōtum pōtu meṉappukaṉ ṟārkaḷāl.

2.21.15

1102

urucik kumpaṭi pākañcey yōrpaṭi
yaricik cōṟu marippaṭip pālunal
varici yāka vaḻaṅka vaḻaṅkavē
perukiṟ ṟallatu piṉkuṟi villiyāl.

2.21.16

1103

ākaṅ kūrtara vuṇṭava riyāvarum
vāyki pūci makiḻntiṉi tuṟṟapiṉ
pāku veḷḷili pāḷitañ cantaṉa
mōki kūra vuvantaḷit tarararō.

2.21.17

1104

cōti nāyakaṉ ṟūteṉum vaḷḷaluk
kīte lāmari tōveṉa vēttiniṉ
ṟōtik kiyeṭut tuṟṟaca lāmurit
tāta rattoṭu maṅkavar pōyiṉār.

2.21.18

viruntūṭṭup paṭalam muṟṟiṟṟu.

uḷḷuṟi aṭṭavaṇikkut tirumpa

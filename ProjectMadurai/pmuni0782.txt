       kaṭaiyam (vaḷaicai) kaliyāṇiyammai piḷḷaittamiḻ
       (piḷḷaittamiḻk kaḷañciyam 2)
       irācarācacōḻar, nātamuṉikaḷ, u.vē.cāmināta aiyar, mu.rā. aruṇācalak
       kavirāyar, puṭparata ceṭṭiyār - tirumuṟaiyai, tivviya
       pirapantattai, kāppiyaṅkaḷai, ciṟṟilakkiyaṅkaḷai, ceppēṭṭilum,
       paṭṭōlaiyilum, acciṭṭum paintamiḻ celvaṅkaḷai ulakiṟku aḷitta
       cāṉṟōr ivarkaḷ tiruvaṭikaḷukku tuṟaicai ātīṉa 23vatu cannitāṉam
       civappirakāca tēcika mūrttikaḷ avarkaḷ āṇaiyiṭṭapaṭi innūlai
       patippittu paṭimakkalamāka paṭaikkiṉṟēṉ. -
             kampapātacēkaraṉ
       veḷiyiṭṭōr : kampaṉ ilakkiyappaṇṇai
       piṭṭāpurattammaṉ kōvilteru, tirunelvēli nakar.
       ________________

   kaliyāṇiyammai piḷḷaittamiḻ āciriyariṉ ūrum, pērum kūṟum pāṭal
       tirumakaḷ naṭaṉa miṭupukaḻ vaḷaicait tecarata rāmavīc curaṉuk
       kirukaṇiṉ maṇiyā mumaikali yāṇik kiḷamaicēr tiruviḷai yāṭaṟ
       paruvamī raintum viruttanaṟ pāviṟ pappattup pāṭinaṉ kaḷittāṉ
       aruḷviḷain tirukkun tiruppuli yūrvāḻ aḻakiya tirucciṟṟam palamē.

   ippāṭalil nūlāciriyariṉ ūr tiruppuliyūr eṉpatum peyar tirucciṟṟampalam
   eṉpatum teriyavarukiṟatu. inta tiruppuliyūr ciṟṟampalam eṉpavar tāṉ
   teṉkāci talapurāṇattai pāṭi āciriyarāka iruppār eṉa karutappaṭukiṟatu.

      tiruppuliyūr aḻakiya tirucciṟṟampala tēcikar iyaṟṟiya
     tirunelvēli māvaṭṭam ampai vaṭṭam kaṭaiyam (vaḷaicai)
                    kaliyāṇiyammai piḷḷaittamiḻ
                              1. kāppupparuvam

   tirumāl
   pārkoṇṭa tiṇṭiṟal palpuvaṉa pantip
           parappaip purakkavēṇṭip
   pāṇṭippi rāṭṭipeṟu celviyaic celvap
           paḻantēvaṉaip puṇarnta
   vārkoṇṭa kumpak katampap paṭīramulai
           vañciyaic ceñcolvaḷarum
   vaḷaicai mānakar maruvukali yāṇacuntara
           maṉōṉmaṇi taṉaippurakka
   tārkoṇṭa paintuḷava marumatti ṭaittaṅku
           tavamaṟait taiyalīṉṟa
   caturmukaṉai mutaliṭṭa puttēḷir yārum
           taṉittaṉi virumpiniṟpac
   cīrkoṇṭa mantarac cikariyil paḵṟalaic
           cēṭaṉuṭa laippiṇittut
   teṇṭiraik kuraikaṭal kaṭaintamu taḷittiṭṭa
           ceṅkaṭkaruṅ koṇṭalē!         (1)
   _________
   civaperumāṉ
   variyaḷi kumuṟiya maṭalviri yitaḻiyiṉ
   matumaḻai māṟāta vārcaṭaik kāṭṭiṉar
   maḻaiyiruḷ eṉaviṭam umiḻtaru kaṇapaṇa
   maṇiveyil kālvīcu mācuṇap pūṭṭiṉar
   mataṉeri paṭanuta laḻalpoḻi viḻiyiṉar
   malaraṭi mēlēṟu māmaṟaip pāṭṭiṉar
   malainikar puyamicai yorupuṭai yaṇikuḻai
   yorupuṭai mēṉīṭu vālvaḷait tōṭṭiṉar
   caruveṉa muṉivar maṉaipuku muṉamavar
   taḷiriya lārtēṭu tātakic cūṭṭiṉar
   tamaṉiya varaiporu makaliya vuramicai
   piṟaivaṭa māyāṭu cūkarak kōṭṭiṉar
   tavaḷaveṇ nilavumiḻ poṭiyaṇi nutaṉmicai
   taruṇanilā vīcutāḻ piṟaik kīṟṟiṉar
   caturmaṟai paraviya tamiḻvaḷai caiyiluṟai
   tacarata rāmīcar tāḷkaḷaip pōṟṟutum
   porumiya kavuṇiya maḻamukil parukiya
   puṇaricey pālūṟu pūṇmulaip pārppati
   puvaṉai kavuritiri puraiyeṉa vaṟivoṭu
   pukalpavar nāvēṟu pūvaiyaip pūttoḷir
   polaṉmuka ṭaḷaviya paṉivarai yatipati
   puṇartaru pērācai mēṉaimut tāṭṭiya
   putalviyai mutalviyai maḻakaḷi ṟeṉavaru
   putalvaṉai yīrāṟu tōḷvaraic cūrppakai
   aracaṉai makaveṉa aruḷiya vaṉitaiyai
   alaikaṭal pōlārum ākamac cūḻcciyai
   aruveṉa uruveṉa uḷateṉa ilateṉa
   akilamu māṉāḷai yākamak kāṭṭiṉil
   aṉutiṉa naṭamiṭu marakata mayilaiyeṉ
   aṟiviṉi lūṭāṭu nīḷoḷik kāṭciyai
   aṭiyavar maṉatiṉil niṉaiyumuṉ ṉutaviya
   aruḷniti pōlvāḷ kalyāṇiyaik kākkavē!         (2)
   _____________
   vināyakar
   iṉituṟa vāyttanaṉ kataiyaip poruppiṉil
           eḻutiya kōṭṭuveṅ karaṭak kaḷiṟṟiṉai
   vaṉamalar pūttatiṇ puyaveṟ paṉaippukaḻ
           vaḷaicaiyiṉ mūrttiyaiṅ karaṉaip paḻiccutum
   kaṉitami ḻāṟceṟin tavark karuṭkaṭal
           kalaivaḷar kīṟṟiḷam piṟaiyait tarittiṭu
   taṉimutal pālpuṇarn tavaḷaik karuttoṭu
           tacarataṉ vāḻttu paiṅkiḷiyaip purakkavē!         (3)
   _____________
   murukaṉ
   kōtaṟu malarkkuvaḷai pūttoḷi nirampumoru
           kōkaṉakam viṇṭalarntu
   kumiḻmukiḻ virintoḻuku tamiḻamu tarumpumiru
           kōṅkalar polintumeḷḷap
   pātava raiyilkoṭu muṭittalai vaṇaṅkavum
           paimpoṉ varaiyaikkiḻittup
   pavaḷā calattil paṭarntēṟu paccaip
           pacuṅkoṭi taṉaippurakka
   vētaṉai yaḻaittoru cirattiṭai puṭaittumaṟai
           vittakaṉe ṉattaḻaittu
   mikkakama lattaraci ruppiṉil viruppiṉoṭu
           mēvippa tākaipeṟṟi
   ōtimamu yarttuyi raṉaittum paṭaittiṟaitaṉ
           ucciyi ṉuvantukēṭpa
   orupiraṇavat tiluṟumuṭ poruḷiṉ meypporuḷ
           uṇarttu kumarakkaṭavuḷē!         (4)
   ______________________
   piramaṉ
   ilakic cuṭarkāl veṇciṟai yekiṉap pariyūrti neñciṉil
           iḻaimup purinūl viḷaṅkiya iṟaimaip paḻaiyōṉ vacuntarai
           mulaiyil tuyilkūr karumpuyal mukaiviṭ ṭalarnā piyañcuḻi
   muḷarip perumāḷ pataṅkaḷil muṭivait tiṉitāva ṇaṅkuvatum
           kalaikaṟ ṟavarnā vuṟaintavaḷ karuṇaik kaṭalāy niṟaintavaḷ
   kamaḻat tarutār puṉaintavaḷ kayilaik kirimē liruntavaḷ
           malaiyat tuvacamī ṉavaṉpeṟu maturait tamiḻnā ṭukantavaḷ
   vaḷaicaik kaliyāṇa cuntara mayilaippari vāymai taṅkavē!         (5)
   __________________________
   ilakkumi
   cikarā calattil paṇāmuṭip paḵṟalaic
           cēṭaṉuṭalaip piṇittut
   teṇtirai neṭuṅkaṭalai yaṇṭar kaṭaiyattuḷḷu
           teḷḷamu tuṭaṉpiṟanta
   makarālayat tainikar maippuyal kavuttuva
           maṇiccuṭa reṟittamārpil
   mākaṉaka neṭṭitaḻk kōkaṉakavīṭṭiṉil
           vacippavaḷai yañcalippām
   pukarmā mukakkaḷiṟu kaṉṟukku veṉṟip
           puḻaikkara meṭuttunīṭṭip
   poṉṉāṭu cuṟṟum poḻil pacuṅkaṟpakap
           pūntaḻai paṟittaruttum
   cakalāca ṉattukku mēlāṉa veḷḷit
           taṭaṅkiri neṭuñcāralil
   tacarataṉ vaḻuttumoru taṉimuta liṭattiṟaivi
           taṉṉaip purakkaveṉṟē.         (6)
   _______________________
   caracuvati
   aṇṭar toḻutiṭumāla kaṇṭaṉiṭat tamarnta
           aruṭkaṭalai yimayavarai yaḷittaru ḷumpāla
   tuṇṭamati nutaṟkoṭiyai yaṇṭaraṇ ṭampuranta
           turantariyaik kaliyāṇa cuntari yaippurakka
   vaṇṭamiḻiṉ vaṭakalaiyiṉ vaṭivuṟu māñāṉa
           maṭuvukutta pālarunti mālmaka ṉāṉmukattum
   viṇṭamalarp puṇṭarika maṇṭapattu miruntu
           viḷaiyāṭu morupiḷḷai veḷḷai yōtimamē!         (7)
   ______________________________
   cattamātar
   pukalaru marumaṟai yeṇṇit terittavaḷ
           purameri paṭanakai muṉṉic cirittavaḷ
   puraiyaṟu mariyuṟa vaṇṇip parittavaḷ
           porutirai pukaiyavil luṉṉik kuṉittavaḷ
   pakupaṭai pakaivarai pammap paṇittavaḷ
           paliyiṭa vuyartiri taṇṭak karattiṉaḷ
   paruvata meṉavaru veṇmaik kayattiṉaḷ
           pakariva reḻuvarkaḷ tammaip paḻiccutum
   makaraca lativiṭa muṇṇap paṭittava
           racurar paṇiceya viṇṇaip purakkumōr
   makapati pakaiteṟu kaṇṇaip paṭaittavar
           malarayaṉ viṟalari kaṇṇuk koḷittavar
   cakatala muḻumuta leṉṉat taḻaittavar
           taḻuviya kaṉataṉa miṉṉaip puṉaṟkarai
   tavaḻkuṭa vaḷaicori veṇṇit tilattoḷir
           tamiḻvaḷai caiyilumai taṉṉaip purakkavē.         (8)
   ________________________
   tikkuppālakar
   mūtaṇṭakūṭak kulakkiri kaḷaicciṟai
           muṟittatiṟal vaccirapāṇi
   mōtuntiraikkaṭal curuṭṭumoru teṉkīḻai
           mūlaiyil cittirapāṉu
   mātaṇṭaṉaikkuriya taṇṭāyutak kaṭavuḷ
           vātiṉil tiṟalarakkaṉ
   makaravākaṉamuṭaiya vāruṇa ticaikkaṭavuḷ
           maṇṇula kelāmparitta
   vētaṇṭakoṭumuṭi naṭuṅkap piṭuṅkumuyar
           vēkappira caṇṭavāyu
   veṟpaṉaiyapuṭpaka micaippoli kupēraṉavaṉ
           veṇmarup piṭapavūrti
   tītoṉṟilātaviva reṇmarun tāmpeṟṟa
           ticaitoṟuñ ceṉṟuniṉṟu
   cīrvaḷaicaiyūrkātta kalyāṇa cuntarac
           celviyaik kāttaruḷavē!         (9)
   ___________________________
   muppattu mukkōṭi tēvar
   marakatamaṇik kaṟkaḷ pācoḷi piṟaṅkavaruḷ
           vaṇmaik kuṇattiṉoḷiyai
   maṉṉavarkaḷ maṉṉaṉān teṉṉaṉmaka ḷeṉṉavaru
           vaṉṉappacuṅ kiḷḷaiyai
   iracitacapā naṭuvil empirāṉ ceynnaṭaṉam
           eytukāl māṟṟuyarnta
   teṉṟukaṇ ṭuvakaiyuṟum vaḷaicainaka ramartarum
           emmaṉaiyai yiṉitukākka
   tiraitirait teṟiyum kaṭaṟpuvi yiruṭṭaṟac
           ceṅkatir parappiyoṟṟait
   tiṇcakaṭ ṭeḻupuravi caṇṭā nilamporat
           tiṇtēr naṭāttiniṟkum
   paritikaḷ maruttuvar vacukkaṇa ruruttirarkaḷ
           paṉṉiruva riruvareṇmar
   patiṉoruva reṉavaitta muppattu mukkōṭi
           paṇṇavar makācamukamē!         (10)

                           2. ceṅkīraip paruvam

   naṟumpuṉa leṭuttāṭṭi yīrampu larttiyicai
           nāvacait taḻukaimāṟṟi
   nāvēṟu kaṇṇēṟu vārāmal veṇṇīṟum
           naṉṉilap poṭṭumiṭṭuk
   kuṟuntoṭi cilampukiṇ kiṇitaṇṭai pāṭakam
           kuḷirmaṇik kāñcikārai
   kuḻaiyil kutampaimutal yāvaiyum aṇintukaṭ
           kuvalayaṅ kaḷiciṟappap
   peṟuṅkaṉaka valliyār kaikoṇṭe ṭuttulaku
           peṟṟaciṟ ṟutaraniṟaiyap
   perukumulai yamutūṭṭi vaittumut tāṭṭimakiḻ
           piḷḷaimaip paruvamellām
   ciṟantamala yattuvacaṉ vārak kumāratti
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!
   cellār karuṅkuḻal kalyāṇa valliyumai
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!         (11)
   ___________________________
   nelvantu kaṉṉalai nikarppap pacuṅkaṉṉal
           neṭṭilaik kataliyēyppa
   neṭṭilaip pūṅkatali marakata maṭaṟkamuku
           nikarākanīḷ kamukelām
   alvanta veṇkatirc cantiramaṇ ṭalamaḷavi
           yārkalip puviyaṉaittu
   māyiram paṇamuṭip pantiyil tāṅkurakaṉ
           ampoṉ nāṭumparikka
   vilvan tarittacaṭai mukkaṭpirā ṉaruḷiṉ
           mēliṭṭu eḻuntateṉṉa
   veṇkoṭu muṭikkōpurak koṭikoḷ māṭaṅkaḷ
           mēvumaḷakā purameṉac
   celvan taḻaikkat taḻaittavaḷai caikkiṟaivi
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!
   cellār karuṅkuḻal kalyāṇa valliyumai
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!         (12)
   _________________________
   maṅkāta poṉmalark kaṟpakā ṭavicūḻ
           malarppūṅ kaṭampavaṉamu
   maṇikoḻit teṟiyumamu tōtatiyumo ḷirkiṉṟa
           māpuṭpa rākatīvum
   koṅkār malarttiraḷu mēvumuyar kayilāya
           kōlaveṇ kiriyiṉmītu
   koṉṟaiyan toṭaiyalaṇi ceñcōti yāyoru
           kōyiluḷ kuḷirmatuppūm
   paiṅkāvi yottaviḻi mātaṅki yātiyar
           paṇiviṭai yiyaṟṟiniṟpap
   pattarā kiyamōṉa cittaryō kilkaṇṭu
           paravivaḻi paṭṭumakiḻac
   ciṅkāra mākkolu virukkum pacuñcōti
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!
   cellār karuṅkuḻal kalyāṇa valliyumai
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!         (13)
   ___________________________
   kampāca lappakaṭu rittuṭuttu raiyaṟṟa
           kakaṉavaṭ ṭattilāṭik
   kāṇarun turiya turiyātīta mokkak
           kaṭantupara yōkameyti
   ampāṉa paṉmalar eṭuttamar toṭuttacilai
           aṅkacaṉ erintuciṟutēr
   ākavaru teṉṟalaṅ kaṉṟuṅ kiṭantalaṟa
           aḻalviḻi tiṟantukarutār
   moympāṉa muppuramu mokkaveri paṭṭaḻiya
           mukiḻnakai tiṟantuñāṉa
   muttirai yaḷittariya mōṉam purintatiru
           mukkaṇāṉ cekkarmēṉic
   cempātiyuṅ koṇṭa marakatak kalāpamayil
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!
   cellār karuṅkuḻal kalyāṇa valliyumai
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!         (14)
   ______________________
   vaḷḷat taṭañcuṉai kuṭaintāṭa añcumiṭai
           vañcik kuṟiñcimaṭavār
   vaḷarcen tiṉaippuṉaṅ kākkumitaṇ mītēṟi
           maikkaruṅ kūntalcāyat
   tuḷḷit tuṭittakayal maikkaṇ kuṟikkoḷat
           tuṅkameṉ koṅkaipoṅkat
   toṭṭiṭṭa vaḷḷaip polaṅkuḻai yilaṅkac
           cuṭarkkānta ḷaṅkaimalarāl
   aḷḷik katirppaṟpa rākamaṇiyaip putaittu
           āṭalkavaṇ kalvīci
   amutaṅ kaṉintūṟu kumutaṅkaḷait tiṟantu
           āvāyeṉap puḷḷōccum
   teḷḷit teḷintatamiḻ veḷḷikkirik kiṟaivi
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!
   cellār karuṅkuḻaṟ kalyāṇa valliyumai
           ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!         (15)

                           8. ammāṉaip paruvam

   oḷitūṅku cekkarmaṇi yammāṉai yāṭṭoḻintu

   ovvoṉṟum viḷaiyāṭu mavvēlai ceṅkāntaḷ
           oppala veṉaccivanta
   kiḷitūṅku kaikkiṭai pukakkatire ṟikkiṉṟa
           kiḷarmaṇiyammā ṉaiporuvak
   kiraṇap paṉippakai yeṉakkaṇṭu viṭutalil
           keṇṭaikke tirntakaruṇait
   tuḷitūṅku maikkaṇ ṇoḷipāyat tuḷaṅkit
           tuḷaṅkuṅ kaḷaṅkavaṭṭac
   cōmakkuḻā meṉak kaṇṭōr tikaippat
           tokaippaṭa niṟaintunaṟavuṇṭu
   aḷitūṅku pūṅkuḻal celviyuṭa ṉeṉṉammai
           ammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē!
   ayirāṇi yayirāṇi vāṇipaṇi kaliyāṇi
           ammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē! (71)
   _______________________________
   emmāti rattiruḷu mokkac curuṭṭiviṭum

                          9. nīrāṭaṟ paruvam

           vārkuḻaikaḷ vaḷḷaiyoppa
   vāḷmukam paṅkērukam poruva maikkaṇkaḷ
           maṭalneṭuṅ kuvaḷaimāṉap

   kōlak kuṟuñcuḻi yoḻuṅkupaṭu urōmanirai

           kūṭṭameṉum avarkanaṭuvil
   kālaic ceḻuṅkamala vāḷmukat taṉṉamē
           kaṅkai nīrāṭiyaruḷē!
   kaliyāṇa cuntarak kaṭavuṭ peṇamutamē!
           kaṅkai nīrāṭiyaruḷē! (81)

           kaṇṭupiṭit turalōṭu piṇittuk kaṭṭik kaḷvaṉeṉat
   tiṉamāṭak kuṭamaṅkaiyar ceṅkaiyiṉ mattāl mottuṇṭu
           ciṟṟāyp pāṭiyil neymmaṇa māṟāccem pavaḷakkaṉivāy
   vaṉamālait tirumāluk kiḷaiyavaḷ varanati yāṭukavē!
           vaḷaicainakark kaliyāṇa cavuntari varanati yāṭukavē! (88)

   kāṉavinō tariṭampiri yātakaruṅ kuyilē! mayilē!
           kaikkaḷi yāṉaimukattaṉai yīṉṟatōr kaṉṉimaṭap piṭiyē!
   pīṉapayō tarapūtara mēntiya peṇmaiyi ḷampiṭiyē!
           piraṇavapañ caramaṉṉiya vaṉṉappiḷ ḷaiyiḷaṅ kiḷiyē!
   mōṉatiyāṉa camāticey mātava muṟṟu tavappēṟē!
           mutumaṟai muṟṟiya upaniṭatat tumuḻutta viḻupporuḷē!
   vāṉatiyāka muḻaṅku neṭuntirai varanati yāṭukavē!
           vaḷaicainakark kaliyāṇa cavuntari varanati yāṭukavē! (89)

   aṇṭarkaḷ koṇṭavi ṭāytavirap parukāramu takkaṭalē!

           vaḷaicainakark kaliyāṇa cavuntari varanati yāṭukavē! (90)

                             10. ūcaṟ paruvam

   virikiṉṟa teḷḷoḷip pavaḷak koḻuṅkāliṉ
           mēliṭṭa kōlavayira
   viṭṭatti liṭṭavaṭa meṇṇilā veṇṇilā
           viḻutuviṭ ṭeṉṉavīkkic
   corikiṉṟa kuḻaviveyil maḻakatire ṟikkiṉṟa
           cuṭarmaṇip palakaimītil
   tōṟṟuniṉ tōṟṟamap paritimaṇ ṭalamicait
           tōṉṟavaruñ cōtiyēyppat
   tirikiṉṟa kayaleṉap piṟaḻkiṉṟa tuṇaiviḻic
           cilainutal kariyakūntal
   centāmaraip poṉniṉ ṟāṭṭa veṇṭāmaraic
           cēyiḻaitutip payimavāṉ
   purikiṉṟa meyttava meṉappeṟṟa kaṉṉimayil
           poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē!
   poṟpuruk koṇṭakal yāṇacun taravalli
           poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē! (91)

           oḷikoḷpari vēṭamoppa

           paṉimatik kaṭavuḷoppap
   pattartañ cittānta kārañcu ruṭṭimeyp
           paramānupūti viḷaiyap
   puḻukoḻuku kumpakka tampamulai yaṉṉamē
   poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē!

           poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē! (92)

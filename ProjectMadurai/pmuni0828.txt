piḷḷit tamiḻk kottu (kuṟippuriyuṭaṉ)
PILLAI-T-TAMIL-K-KOTTU (WITH NOTES)
Edited by T. CHANDRASEKHARAN. M.A., L.T.,
Curator, Government Oriental Manuscripts Library, Madras
AND THE STAFF OF THE LIBRARY

"muttup piṟappatoru teṉkaṭalilē'' eṉṟu pāratiyum, - pāṇṭi nāṭu muttuṭittu"
eṉṟu oḷaviyārum, “teṉkaṭal muttum kuṇakaṭal tukirum'' (paṭ - pāli. 189) eṉṟu
kaṭiyalūr uruttiraṅkaṇṇaṉārum pārāṭṭak kāraṇamāyuḷḷatu tūttukkuṭiyē. rōm nāṭṭiṉ
irāṇiyāṉa kiḷāṭiyasiṉ maṉivi pāṇṭi nāṭṭiṉ muttiṉmītu mikka mōkaṅ
koṇṭiruntāḷām. ittaki nakaril kōyil koṇṭuḷḷavarē pākampiriyāvammi.
īcuvarar peyar:-"caṅkararā mīcura riciyip paḻiccutum.” (539) “muṉṉatāy niṉṟa civa
caṅkarēcurar taḻiya” (633) eṉpatāl īcuvarar peyar caṅkarēcuvarar eṉṟu terikiṟatu.
talavināyakar: -"karuṇicey cuntara pāṇṭiyac cuṭarit tutippām....'' (540),
''cinturac cuntarap pāṇṭiyak kaḷiṟōṭu ...” (638) eṉpataṉāl cuntarap pāṇṭip
piḷḷiyār eṉṟu terikiṟatu.
aiyaṉar peyar:- "tiyal maruvu paccimukiliyaṉ...'' eṉpatāl paccip perumāḷ aiyaṉār
eṉpatu pōtarukiṟatu.
tīrttam:- “tiriceyā makōtatit tīrttamum” (638) eṉpatāl makōtatiyē (kaṭalē) tīrttameṉa
viḷaṅkukiṟatu.
mutal pāṭṭil ''taḷavāmpal' eṉpatit taḷavu + āmpaṉ eṉap pirittut taḷavu eṉpatu mulli
malarpōṉṟa paṟkaḷiyum āmpal eṉpatu alli malarpōṉṟa vāyiṉiyum kuṟippatākak koḷḷalām.
''kāmpum kuḻiyum" eṉpatu pirati ceytavar "kōm -pum kuḻiyum'' eṉa
eḻutiyiruntataṉi mōṉi, poruḷ ākiyavaṟṟiṟkēṟpa māṟṟappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itaṉālēyē pōlum
eṉa uriyil kūṟappaṭṭatu. ivvāciriyar upaya, upa eṉpavikaḷi upiya, upi eṉṟē
āṇṭuḷḷaṉar. 2-vatu pāṭṭil "muṇṭaka vētāmaṟi" eṉṟirukkalām.
tūttukkuṭi nakaraṇmiyil kaṭal tiri ceyāteṉpatu aka -nāṉūṟṟil “koṭuntimiṟ paratavar
vēṭṭam" eṉṟu toṭaṅkum pāṭṭil, “veṉvēṟ kavuriyar toṉmutu kōṭi muḻaṅkiṭum peḷavam
iraṅkum muṉṟuṟi velpōr irāmaṉ arumaṟik kavitta palvīḻalām pōla oliyavintaṉṟu
ivvaḻuṅkalūrē.'' (70) eṉa varutal kāraṇamāyuḷḷatu. itil pāṇṭiyarukkuriyatāyt
tiruvaṇik kariyiṉarukil muḻaṅku miyalpiṉavāṉa periya kaṭaliṉ olikkiṉṟa tuṟimuṟṟam
eṉpatu tūttukkuṭiyik kuṟippittal kāṇka. 549, 587-ām pāṭṭukkaḷil icceytiyik
kāṇalām.
''vāyiṟkuḷḷakappaṭṭa taṇṇīr viṭam ... mantiram paṭikka” (608) eṉṟa aṭiyiṉ karuttu
''vallaravuṅ kēṭku mantiraṅ kaḷikkiṉṟōyi yaṭukku...'' (niṅ. paṭa. 59.) eṉṟa
kamparāmāyaṇap pāṭali ottuḷatu kāṇka. nīrāṭaṟ paruvattil tāmiraparṇiyiyum am miyiyum
camappaṭuttik kūṟi nīr viḷiyāṭuka eṉpatu putiya amcam. 536- ām pāṭṭil “cittam
palattiletir vātāṭi'' eṉpatum “pōrāṭum viḻiyāṭa varaṉāṭa vātāṭi....'' 637-
eṉpatum ottuḷḷaṉa. ippiḷḷittamiḻil "ēṉ karuṭā cukamā'' ''civaṉē yeṉakkiṭanta" eṉṟa
vaḻakkukaḷit takkavā ṟamittuḷḷār.
"tattun tāṅka muḻaṅkāvuṉ'' eṉṟa pāṭṭil mutteṭukkum pala karuvikaḷum, tarampirikkum
pala karuvikaḷum carivara aṟiyac cāttiyap paṭātatāl kuṟippuriyil viḷakka muṭiyavilli.
628-ām pāṭṭil "naṭuvuyar mutukoru kamaṭameṉa tikaḻ amaṭam" eṉa varuvatu yāteṉak
kāṇaviyalavilli. itu karppaṅkoṇṭa caṅkukaḷā yirukkalā meṉpatu tañcivāṇaṉ kōvi 104-ām
pāṭṭālaṟiyalām. pirati ceytavar carivarak kavaṉiyātatāl nērnta piḻi itu.
tūttukkuṭi pāṇṭiya nāṭṭil tirunelvēli māvaṭṭattiluḷḷa ōr tuṟimuka nakaram.
ivviṭattil araciyalārāl muttukkaḷ ippōtum eṭukkappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. itaṉ paḻiya peyar
tirumantiranakar eṉpatām. innakar vaḷamikka teṉpati 567, 568, 584-ām pāṭṭukkaḷil
kūṟi ( eḻupaṉiyōṭali kuyvaru millāta taṉṟi maṟṟillāta toṉṟilli yellāc ciṟappu
muṇṭu,'' eṉṟu - muṭikkiṟār.
innūliṉ āciriyar yāvar eṉa viḷaṅkāviṭiṉum ciṟcila kuṟippukkaḷ kiṭikkiṉṟaṉa.
573-ām pāṭaliṉiṉṟu ammikku āciriyar toṇṭareṉṟum, pēparakkuṭiyeṉpatu avar
kuṭippeyar eṉṟum, talimuṟi talimuṟiyāka ammi taṉ tirumuṉ vittuvāṉā yirukka
vēṇṭumeṉṟu kūṟiyatāyum pulavarit taṉ kōyililēyē ammi kuṭiyēṟṟuvittār eṉavum,
594-ām pāṭaliṉiṉṟu āciriyarkku maturamāṉa vākkip pāṭa ammi aruḷ purintār
eṉṟum, avaratu pāmāliyi ammi cūṭiṉa naṉmiyāl puttira, peḷattira pākkiyaṅkaḷ tara
vēṇṭumeṉṟu āciriyar kēṭṭatākavum terikiṟatu. ippiḷḷit tamiḻ akkālattil mikavum
uyarnta nili peṟṟu viḷaṅkiyatu eṉpatu "... upiyarttamum tōṉṟaniṉṟa ikkavi
poruṭkiṭam peṟṟatāl ...'' (633) eṉpatālaṟiyalām. āciriyar taṉṉi ammi aruḷuṭaṉ
āṭkoṇṭaṉar eṉpataṉi, "kaṇṇar tuṇi yeṉṉi yaruḷaṉṉi ...'' (631) "eṇāṉkaṟam vaḷartteṉi
vaḷartta." (632) “contamā yeṉiyāḷ kirupāliyē...” (577) "eṉi āṇṭa ruḷuñ
cakkariyē ....'' (583) eṉṟa aṭikaḷāl uṇaralām. murukaṉ mītu kāppil nīṇṭa
pāṭṭiyaṟṟiyatōṭu ceṅkīriyiloru nīṇṭa pāṭṭum 592, 615- pāṭṭukkaḷil avarippaṟṟiyum
kūṟiyiruppatāl murukaṉ mītum mikka īṭupāṭuṭiyar eṉa aṟiyalām.
kāppup paruvattil, "tirumālaraṉē ... kaṭavuḷarē.'', ''avaruḷ kāppiṉṉākum.''
"avaṉṟāṉ ... ṉeṉpa" eṉṟa paṉṉiru pāṭṭiyaṟ cūttiraṅkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.
ippiḷḷittamiḻ eṭṭiyāpuram makārājā camastāṉattiṟkuriya ōlic cuvaṭiyiṉiṉṟu 1924-
25-il pirati ceytu vitta mūvaruṭak kāṭalākku 590 (b) eṇṇuḷḷa kākitap
piratiyiṉiṉṟu veḷiyiṭappaṭukiṟatu. akkālattil caripārttalillātatāl cila aṭikaḷ
viṭupaṭṭum cila poruḷ purintukoḷḷāmal eḻutiyumuḷḷatāl tavaṟukaḷ ivvaccup
piratiyilum nērntirukkalām. paṭippavarkaḷ tiruttikkoḷḷak kēṭṭuk koḷḷap
paṭukiṉṟaṉar.

tirumantira nakaramākiya tūttukkuṭi pākampiriyāvammip piḷḷittamiḻ kāppup paruvam

tirumāl
538. kārkoṇ ṭakaṉṟu kaṉṉaṅkaṟē
      reṉṉat tiraṇṭu curuṇṭakuḻaṟ
kāṭun tilaka paṅkayamuṅ
      kāmpuṅ kuḻiyuṅ kaḻunīrum
pārkoṇ ṭalarāk kumiḻtaḷavām
      palumpūt tuḷampūt talarntupaccip
pacēleṉ ṟirunta kaṟpakappūm
      pākam piriyā ṭaṉikkākka
pērkoṇ ṭirunta vakalikikal
      luruvāk kupiya tāmariyum
piramaṉ ceṉitta tāmariyum
      piḷḷip pirāṭṭi tāmariyuñ
cīrkoṇ ṭirunta nayaṉameṉac
      cekkac civanta tāmariyuñ
ceṅkip paruvat tāmariyuñ
      ceḻitta corūpak karumpuyalē. (1)
vēṟu
caṅkararāmīcuvarar
539. mantiravāḻ vāṉavar vaṭatik kiruppavar
      vaṅkaṇarā lācaṉak kaṉakac caturppuya
mantararē ṟēṟiya pavaṉic ciṟappiṉar
      maṉṟiṉilvā tāṭiya ciramap patattiṉar
cantirakalā māliyar kayilip poruppiṉar
      taṇkeḻupā kīrati talivit tiruppavar
caṅkorukā tāṭiṭa viciyit tarippavar
      caṅkararā mīcurariciyip paḻiccutum
muntiyavē tākamak kavuṇiyak kulappayir
      muṉpatiṉā ṟāyira maturit tirukkaṭi
muṇṭakavē kāmaṟi yeṉavuc carittu
      muraṇkaḻumē lēṟiṭa vamaṇic ceyittaruḷ
pintamiḻā kāracaṇ mukaṉuk kitattoṭu
      pintaṉamī tūṟiya vamutip pukaṭṭiya
piṅkoṭiyāḷ pākami ṉiciyip paṭippaṭi
      paṇpuṭaṉē pāṭumip paṉuvaṟ ṟaḻikkavē. (2)
vēṟu
cuntarapāṇṭiyap piḷḷiyār
540. kontaviḻ pūntoṭip paraṉik kavaḷak
      koḻuṅkaṉi tāṅkukit talaṉit tikaḻoru
kompiṉi yūṉṟukit talaṉik kayiṟu
      koṭumpuri pūṇṭakit talaṉip parumit
tontiyu mintukit talaṉip pirapi
      tokaṅkuca mēntukit talaṉik kavuṇmata
tumpi mukāmpuyat taracik karuṇicey
      cuntara pāṇṭiyac cuṭarit tutippām
mantira pūntirup patiyiṟ caravaṇa
      mintaṉi yīṉṟiṭap paruvak kuḷirpaṉi
mañcamar kāñcaṉat timiyak kiritaru
      maṅkala nāṇkaḻut tamutak kaḷimayil
cantira kāntiyiṟ katirviṭ ṭiḷaveyil
      taṅkoḷi kāṉalpōṟ paracuk karatala
caṅkaṇi pūṅkuḻi karuṇi poruntiya
      caṅkarar tōyntavut tamiyip pukaḻavē. (3)
murukaṉ
541. makarameṟi tirikataṟu kaṭaliṭi kavaṭupaṭu
      ciṉicella naṭcattira
malarvateṉa malarmalaru malartali kaviḻumā
      maramalla nirppākkiya
maramiteṉa varipiramar mutaliyavar piramikoḷa
      varumvalla miccūrkkula
maṟukamaka patikirupi peṟavimi yavarka
      ṇilipeṟa veḷḷiveṟpāṟ
pakarakuka raṟukaramu muṭiyaporu ḷeṉamakuṭa
      kaṇapaṇakal lutirttākkiram
koṭiyavura kamumuṭal neṟuneṟu neṟeṉa
      vatiratici cellavaṟṟāk
kaṭalkumuṟa maṭamaṭeṉa naṭaṉamiṭu mayiliṉvaru
      kuṟavaḷḷi kaṟpāṟpuṇar
kumarakuru paramuruka caravaṇavu tiyavaṟu
      mukaccelva ṉippōṟṟutum
nikarilparu mitacikara koṭumuṭi kakaṉaveḷi
      mukaṭuḷ ḷuṟattākkiṭa
neṭiyaveti rakilpirama tarumakiḻa curarkaṭaru
      malarcille ṉappūttoḷi
nilavumaṭa viyuḷatika parakati yaṇiyakuṟu
      muṉiceyta vattāccirama
niṟiyumuyar tavamuṉivar ceyaceya ceyeṉa
      vulaviyanal (la) caṟpāttira
takaranaṟa vumiḻpotiya malikumu kumeṉumaru
      vikaḷkal leṉappāṟkaṭal
carivateṉa varuporuni varanati parukivaḷi
      yumiḻveḷḷi muttāyttikaḻ
taraḷamaṇi yoṭucakala vaḷamumuḷa cakarapira
      puṭarkallu mikkārkkaṭa
ṟavaḻumali yolitaviru muyartuṟi nakarilvaru
      malivalli yikkākkavē. (4)
vēṟu
tirumakaḷ
542. vaḷivāyp puḷimēl virumpātu
      maṇpiṭ ṭaruntā tiḷamuliyiṉ
maṇikkaṇ kaṟutti ṭātutiru
      maḻalic cevvāy veḷiṟātu
tiḷiyāc cūṟkoḷ ḷātupacē
      leṉṟa narampe ḻātutavañ
ceytu tiruppāṟ kaṭalpayanta
      centā marippeṇ caraṇpaṇivām
aḷivāy maṭutta kaḷvāyu
      macōti tayirmat teṭuttaṭippa
vāvā veṉṟu kuḻipiṭittē
      yaṅkān taḻuta mukiṟkiḷiya
piḷavāk kiṭantuṭ kuḻinteḻuveṇ
      piḷḷi mativāṇutaṟciṟupeṇ
piḷḷi pākam piriyāṇmēṟ
      piḷḷik kaviti taḻikavē. (5)
kalimakaḷ
543. kuṭili ṉuṟiyip piṭitturaliṟ
      kuntik kālvit tekkimukaṅ
kōṭṭik kaṭikkaṇ pōṭṭiṭiyar
      kumpat tatiyik kuṭittuviṭum
neṭunī laṉukkō riḷiyaneṭu
      nīli pākam piriyāta
nimali mōkam piriyātu
      nilikku niṟuttun tamiḻkākka
muṭiviṉ muṉiyiṉ muṉikkaḻuttiṉ
      muṭitta piṟikkū ṉirukōṭṭiṉ
muṉiyuñ cūla mutaṟṟēva
      reṉattāṉ kaviṉ muṭikavikkum
paṭika niṟatti paṭikavaṭam
      paṭika paṭāmpūm pintaṉatti
paṭika varuṇa patumakeṟpa
      paripū raṇatti pāratiyē. (6)
cattamātarkaḷ
544. taḻiyuṅ kaṭukkip pūvumpiñcu
      matiyuṅ kāyuñ carppamumveṇ
ṭaraṅka meṟikaṅ kiyaṅkaṉiyun
      taritta caṭilap pemmāṉpāṟ
paḻiyapuratti pārpātti
      paṇṇam paṇatti yeṇkuṇatti
pākam piriyāp parācatti
      paṉuvaṟ ṟaḻikkum paṭikākka
maḻavañ ciṟiyō timamviṭimā
      yūramuvaṇa matayāṉi
makiṭa malaki maṟivilvēl
      vaṇcak karamvac ciramērcūl
kuḻiyan taṇiyē yuruttiriyē
      kumari nārā yaṇiyēcaṟ
kuṇavin tiriyē varākiyē
      kulacā muṇṭi tēviyē. (7)
ātitta cantirar
545. kampa maṭukim purikkōṭṭuk

      payantu curantu mulikoṭutta

vempi rāṉuk kiṭakkaṇṇē
      yiṭakkaṇ ṇiṉukku valakkaṇṇē

      yiravuk karacē pakaṟkaracē

tuḷḷum pacuntakaṭṭu āral ukaḷ poruni - pacuntakaṭṭippōṉṟa āral mīṉkaḷ tuḷḷik
kutikkiṉṟa tāmiraparaṇi; ukaḷ - kuti. (5)
624. kamaṭam - āmi; ikaḻāp poruni nati pākīratikkup piratipimpa nati - ikaḻappaṭāta
tāmiraparṇi kaṅkikkup pirati pimpamāṉa natiyākum. (piratipimpam - atēvaṭivu);
pārkku ituvē pāvanācamum eṉṟu - pūmiyiluḷḷa uyirkaṭku ituvē pāvaṅkaḷi
nācañceyvatu eṉṟu niṉittu; tāmiraparaṇiyil calap(jalap) piravākattil mīṉ kūṭṭaṅkaḷum,
āmik kūṭṭaṅkaḷum aliyil eḻuntu viḷiyāṭuvatu kutikkum mīṉ avatārattiyum, kūrma
(āmi) avatārattiyum orucēra eṭuttup poruninati kaṅkikku piratipimpamākiya
natiyeṉṟum puṇṇiya natiyeṉṟum, pūmiyiluḷḷa uyirkaṭkup pāvatti nācam ceyyum nati
yeṉṟum eṇṇi tirumāl tīrttamāṭi viḷiyāṭuvati yoppap pukaḻnturikkum tirumantira
nakar eṉṟu poruḷ koḷka; purāri - civaṉ; piriyātāy - piriyātavaḷē; piri+ ā+t+
āy eṉṟu pirikka; ā etirmaṟi yiṭinili; t eḻuta tuppēṟu; āy muṉṉili vikuti.
(6)
625. ukaṭṭum cakaṭṭi uti caraṇa ukaḷa patum nayaṉa paṭa vuraka cayaṉar - kutittu varum cakkara
vaṇṭiyi (cakaṭācūraṉ eṉpavaṉi) utitta pātamum, iraṇṭu tāmariyotta kaṇkaḷum, paṭamuṭiya
pāmpākiya cayaṉattiyumuṭiya tirumāl; potiyavari - potiya mali; kaṭal maṭutta - kaṭalil
kalanta; pacumpoṉ maliyaraci - imayamalikku araciyē; taṅkak koṭuṅkikku uḷḷāy - taṅka
mayamāṉa vīṭṭiṉuḷḷāy; koṭuṅki - vīṭṭiṉ oruṟuppu, iṅku vīṭṭik kuṟittatāl
ākupeyar. caṅka mukattāl - caṅkup pālaṭiyiṉiṭamāka. (7)
626. tattāḻ tirikkārk kaṭal mukaṭṭuc cakattil katirc cūryōtayam - tattum āḻnta
aliyiyuṭiya kariya kaṭaliṉ ōrattil ulakiṉarukkāka varum kiraṇattiyuṭiya cūriya utayamum;
'vicumpaṉittum tayaṅkum tārākaṇa rācik kottu utiyamum - ākāyam pūrāvum viḷaṅkum
naṭcattirak kūṭṭattiṉ utayamum; utitta mutal kuṇatikku utittup potiyavarik kuṭumi
kaṭakkum variyum vaḷam koṭukkum tāmpirapaṉṉi - tōṉṟiya mutaṉmiyāṉa kiḻakkut
tikkilutittup potiya malic cikaraṅkaḷik kaṭakkum variyum vaḷam koṭukkiṉṟa
tāmpiraparṇi; cutta ākirutit tēcu uṭittāy - māciṉmiyāṉa vaṭivuṭaṉ oḷi uṭittāka;
cūriyōtayam, cantirōtayam, naṭcattira utayam ākiyaṉa tāmpiraparṇiyil cuttavaṭivōṭu
oḷipeṟṟut tōṉṟum piratipimpaṅkaḷ ellām paricuttamāṉa natiyil mūḻki mūḻki cērnta
patavikaḷip peṟuvati ottirukkumpaṭiyāṉa putunīr eṉap poruḷ koḷka. (8)
627. kāntaḷ ceṅkiyiṉ māntaḷir viral veṇkamalap perumāṭṭi - kāntaḷ malarpōlum
civanta kiyum māntaḷirpōlum viralumuṭiya veḷḷit tāmariyilirukkum caracuvati; ki
virivākac cevvari - citaṟu uṇkaṇ eḻutu añcaṉamum - kivēkamāka civanta kōṭukaḷ
citaṟiṉāṟpōlirukka miyuṇṭa kaṇṇil eḻutukiṉṟa añcaṉamum; nutal tāṉeṉa villic
cērttiya vataṉantīṭṭiya cinturam - neṟṟiyeṉṟaṟiya villotta puruvam cērnta mukattil
tīṭṭiya poṭṭum; kūntalil ampuya maṅki muṭittalar kottuc caramum - tirumakaḷ kūntalil
muṭitta malarum, “kottu carappaḷi'' eṉum āparaṇamum; vaḷaṅkoṇṭu iru kariyum vaṇṭaliṭat
tiṭarkuvital kamakameṉap pūntiri - vaḷappam koṇṭu iraṇṭu karikaḷilum vaṇṭaliṭavum maṇal
tiṭṭuk kuvintu kamakamaveṉum vācaṉi vīcum pūkkaḷiyuṭiya alikaḷ; ampuya maṅki
tirumakaḷ. (9)
628. naḷikaṇ puṉal niṟi kaṭal akaḻat taṭuppaṭukki yamukkiyeḻu naṭuvuyar mutuku oru
kamaṭameṉat tikaḻ - kuḷirnta iṭamāṉa nīr niṟinta kaṭalit tōṇṭa taṭippaṭṭu aḻunti
yeḻuntirukkiṉṟa naṭuppākam uyarnta mutukiyuṭiya oppaṟṟa āmipōṉṟu viḷaṅkum;
tuḷicintiya tiripuraḷuva piraḷayattu ūṭuruvit turuvi - tuḷikaḷi vīcum alikaḷ
puraḷum piraḷayam pōṉṟa nīriṉ mattiyil ceṉṟu tēṭi; cūṉmutir vayiṟu uḷiyak kuḻiyak
karitōṟum kariyēṟi - karuppam mutirnta vayiṟu piracava vētaṉiyāl varutta melintu
kariyiṭamellām ēṟi; aritu tavaḻntu - tavaḻa muṭiyāmal tavaḻntu; kiveṭṭāl
tāḻkuḻiyāy veṭṭiyatil cori muṭṭikaḷiṭṭati mīṭṭu nirappiya veṇpuḷiṉameṉa -
kiveṭṭu eṉum karuviyāl tāḻnta kuḻiyāka veṭṭiyatil corinta muṭṭikaḷ iṭṭati
maṟupaṭi akkuḻikaḷi nirappiya veḷḷiya maṇal pōṉṟu. (10)

629. aṇṭamukaṭu aṇavu katir iḷanilavu eṟitta paṭikak kāliṉmici marakatattāl
viṭṭamiṭṭu - ākāya ucciyiyum toṭum kiraṇamākiya iḷiya nilavi vīciya paṭikak
kāliṉmītu marakatak kallāl uttiramiṭṭu; taraḷavaṭam - muttiṉālākiya kayiṟu; atō tāri
māriyumiḻum koṇṭali nikarppa - kīḻtāriyākap peyyum maḻiyi veḷippaṭuttum mēkatti
otta; nīlniṟa maṇippalaki - nīlaniṟamāṉa aḻakiya palaki; koḻuvi - mēṉmi poruntac ceytu;
paṭām virittu - āṭiyi virittu; malar parappu amaḷi - malarparappiṉa metti; uṉ kōlam
irukālum nilava - uṉṉaḻaku irutaṭavi atikamāka viḷaṅka; tuṇṭa veṇ tūṇattiṉuḷ
pacuntuḷava māl tōṉṟuvatu māṉum - orupirivāṉa veḷḷiya tūṇil pacuntuḷapa
māliyiyuṭiya tirumāl tōṉṟuvati yottirukkum; oṉṟil tuḻāy mukil cakōtariyeṉap
piṉ utikkum uṉ tōṟṟamum poruvum - maṟṟoru pirivil tirumāliṉ cakōtariyeṉṟu
piṉpu utitta uṉ aḻaki ottirukkum ; aruṇa puṇṭarikam - centāmari. (1)
630. muruku viri makaranta muṭi viḷivuṟu naṟā muṭṭi maṭṭitaḻ vimmi nāḷ mukip
piṇi murukkaviḻum ampuya ācaṉam makuṭa muṭi aṇaṅku - vācaṉi virinta makarantap
poṭikaḷ, nuṉiyil viḷikiṉṟa tēṉ muṭṭi attēṉ poruntiya itaḻ vimmutalaṭintu aṉṟu
viṭṭa arumpu malarum tāmariyi ācaṉamākak koṇṭuḷḷa kirīṭattit taliyiṟ cūṭiya ilakkumi;
āciri varum - avaḷic cārntavarum, āciri tam - cārntirukki; oruniṉ aruku -
oppaṟṟa uṉatu arukāka; iḷanteṉṟal maṇṭu upiya cāmariyiraṭṭa - iḷiya teṉṟaṟkāṟṟu
neruṅki iru cāmarikaḷi vīca; "ūruvaci .............. ēnti niṟpa" - ūrvaci,
mēṉaki, arampi, tilōttami, intirāṇi, irati ākiyōr civanta kiraṇa oḷi tikaḻa
karuppūra ālātti ēnti niṟkavum; āṭci uṉ cannitāṉam viḻi koḷātu aiyaṉ aticayittu
eṉap pirikka; aiyaṉ - civaṉ; porupuṉal aṭakkuñ cirakkampamum ceya - mōtukiṉṟa kaṅki
nīrit taliyilē aṭakkum cirattiṉāl āṭṭi makiḻcciyit terivikka. (2)
631. kayal mīṉa maṇṭalam muttu aṇi paṭaṅku iṭṭa kalyāṇa pantar ēyppa - kayal mīṉ pōṉṟa
naṭcattira maṇṭalam muttiṉāl aṇinta kūriyiṭṭa kalyāṇa pantali ottirukka; kakaṉa veḷi
ākāya veḷi; kaṭṭu poṉ taṭṭu āṭavē - oṉṟiṉ mēloṉṟākak kaṭṭappaṭṭa aḻakiya ulakat
taṭṭukkaḷ āṭa; ayal vaṇṇa maṇṭalam veḷiyēyuḷḷa niṟam poruntiya naṭcattira maṇṭalam;
pūmaṇṭalāti - yantamum muḻutum āṭum avaḷ tāṉ aciyāmal aṇumutalum aciyātu eṉṟu
pirikka; aiyam - cantēkam; muyal vaṇṇa maṇṭala matic caṭāmaṇi makuṭa muttar maṉiyāṭṭi -
kaḷaṅkamākiya karuniṟa maṇṭalattiyuṭiya cantiraṉiyaṇinta caṭiyākiya aḻakiya makuṭam uḷḷa civaṉ
maṉivi puyal vaṇṇam - mēkakiṟam; tuṇi - cakōtari. (3)
632. eṇṇariya - kaṇakkillāta; iyampariya - colla muṭiyāta, aṉantam - pala; arampiyar
kaṉṉiki - tēvap peṇkaḷ, mēṉakiyōṭu + yāvarum eṉṟu pirikka; palaki nāppaṇ nī

633. akkarattāl peritu rūpikarittalāl aḻakiya pataccuvaṭṭāl aṟivākarark kelām

iṭam; paṉuval - pāṭṭu; pikkaṇāṭavi viṭap pāntaḷaṅ kuḻuviṉip pāramaṇi valiyamē

māṇikka valiyam pōṉṟākki; mukkavar ayil paṭi cumantu - mūṉṟākap pirinta kūrmiyāṉa

634. kayattampa māmakara cākarap piraḷayam - āḻamāṉa niliyil periya cuṟāmīṉkaḷiyuṭiya kaṭal
veḷḷam; kaṭṭami marakkalam naṭukkuḻi niṟutti maṉ - kaṭṭiyaminta kappali naṭuviṭattil
nilipeṟa niṟutti; kāl āṟṟiṉil piṇittu - tūṇkaḷiṟ kaṭṭi; kāl - tūṇ;
ceyattampameṉṉat tokum pāymarat tokuti yucciyil cērntu - veṟṟik kampameṉac cērum
pāymarat tokutiyucciyiṟ cērntu, kitic ceṅkāl aṉat tiraḷkaḷ - tāḻiyil irukkiṉṟa

mūṅkil; tūyniṟak kañcukam - tūya niṟattiyuṭiya caṭṭi, vēḻampar - kaḻik kūttāṭikaḷ;
kaṭalil puyal kāṟṟiṉāl kari tatumpa kappalkaḷi naṭuvil niṟutti naṅkūram pāycca
tāḻiyiluḷḷa civanta kālkaḷi yuṭiya aṉṉat tiraḷkaḷ veṟṟik kampam pōṉṟu cērum pāymarat
tokutiyiṉ ucciyil cērntu kampam kāṟṟiṉāl aciyum pōtellām ciṟakukaḷ virittu
acippatu periya kampam pōṉṟu kayiṟṟālitattoṭu cuṟṟik kaṭṭiya mūṅkil mēl nalla niṟac
caṭṭi pōrttuk kaḻikkūttāṭikaḷ taṅkaḷ vittikaḷ ceyvati yottirukkum. (6)
635. kāṉṟa umiḻnta; takaṭupaṭu mippi iriyumiḻ muttum - takaṭupōṉṟu poruntiya

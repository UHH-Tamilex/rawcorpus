Source:
nallurikkōvi (mutaṟ pākam)
makāmakōpāttiyāya tākṣiṇātya kalāniti
ṭākṭar u. vē. cāminātiyaravarkaḷ eḻutiyatu
urimippativu, 1958
ēḻām patippu – 1958, vili rū. 1-25
srī tiyākarāca vilāca veḷiyīṭu
Kabeer Printign Works, Madras.
---------------
kuṟippu
innūliṉ iraṇṭām patippu 1941-m varuṣam* eṉ tantiyārākiya makāmakōpāttiyāya
tākṣiṇātya kalāniti ṭākṭar aiyaravarkaḷāl veḷivantatu. avarkaḷ eḻutiyuḷḷa mukavuriyāl
itaṉippaṟṟiya vivaraṅkaḷ naṉku viḷaṅkum. sarvakalācāli atikārikaḷum āṅkāṅkuḷḷa
pāṭacālit talivarkaḷum apimāṉi kaḷum ipputtakattit taṅkaḷ taṅkaḷukkut terinta
iṭaṅkaḷil paravac ceytu eṉakku ūkkamaḷittu varum viṣayattil avarkaḷpāl mikka naṉṟi
pārāṭṭukiṟēṉ.
ceṉṉi iṅṅaṉam
16-9-1950 S. kaliyāṇacuntariyar
-------------------------

mukavuri

avvappoḻutu pattirikikaḷil nāṉ eḻutivanta varalāṟukaḷiyum, nūtaṉamāka eḻutiyavaṟṟiyum
tokuttu, "nāṉ kaṇṭatum kēṭṭatum" "putiyatum paḻiyatum" eṉṉum iraṇṭu puttakaṅkaḷāka
muṉpu veḷiyiṭṭēṉ. avaṟṟip paṭitta tamiḻaṉparkaḷ palar, "ippaṭiyē nīṅkaḷ eḻutiya
piṟavaṟṟiyum ñāpakattiluḷḷavaṟṟiyum cērttut taṉipputtakaṅkaḷāka veḷiyiṭavēṇṭum" eṉṟu
kūṟi ūkkam aḷittārkaḷ. cila aṉparkaḷ avaṟṟiṟ cilavaṟṟi āṅkilattilum, hintiyilum
moḻipeyarttup pattirikikaḷil veḷiyiṭṭaṉar. cilar tāṅkaḷ tokutta pāṭa puttakaṅkaḷil
eṉ aṉumati peṟṟuc cila kaṭṭurikaḷic cērttuk koṇṭaṉar. eṉṉuṭiya paḻiya ñāpakaṅkaḷ
eṉakku iṉpam taruvatu pōlavē piṟarukkum iṉpam taruvati aṟintu iṟi vaṉ tiruvaruḷi
vāḻttukiṉṟēṉ.
nāṉ eḻutivanta varalāṟukaḷil mēlē kuṟippiṭṭa iraṇṭu puttakaṅkaḷilum
cērkkappaṭātavaṟṟiyum, avvappōtu eḻutiya kaṭṭurikaḷiyum ceyta piracaṅkaṅkaḷiyum
tokuttu, "nallurikkōvi" eṉṉum peyarōṭu nāṉku pākaṅkaḷāka veḷiyiṭṭēṉ. avaṟṟuḷ
itu mutaṟ pākam. itaṉ mutaṟ patippu 12-2-37l veḷiyākiyatu. ita ṉuḷ eṭṭu viṣayaṅkaḷ
aṭaṅkiyuḷḷaṉa. "tarumam talikāk kum", aṟiyilūr, pūṇṭi araṅkanāta mutaliyār,
"appaṭic collalāmā?" eṉpavi kalimakaḷilum, vaṉṟoṇṭareṉ patu raṅkūṉ taṉavaṇikaṉ poṅkal
malarilum, āṭal pāṭaleṉpatu āṭalpāṭa leṉṉum pattirikiyiṉ mutal āṇṭu malarilum,
poṉkātta kiḻavi yeṉpatu āṉantavikaṭaṉ tīpāvaḷi malarilum veḷivantavi. paṇṭikkālattup
paḷḷikkūṭaṅkaḷeṉpatu puracapākkam sar. em. si. ṭi. muttiya ceṭṭiyāravarkaḷ uyartarap
paḷḷikkūṭattiṟ ceyta piracaṅkam.
ipputtakattip patippippataṟku ūkkamuṇṭākkiya aṉparkaḷukkum,
pattirikāciriyarkaḷukkum, itaṉ patippiṟ kup palavakiyāṉa utavi ceytavarkaḷukkum eṉ
naṉṟiyit terivittuk koḷkiṟēṉ.
'tiyākarāca vilācam' tiruvēṭṭīcuvaraṉpēṭṭi iṅṅaṉam
ceṉṉi, 15-2-'41 vē. cāminātiyar

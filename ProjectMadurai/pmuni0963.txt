vaṭatirumullivāyil koṭiyiṭi ammi piḷḷittamiḻ

tirucciṟṟampalam
vaṭatirumullivāyil koṭiyiṭi ammi piḷḷittamiḻ
    vināyakak kaṭavuḷ
cīrpūtta cempū tarattil pacuṅkoṇṭal
        cērpumiḷirkiṉṟatu ēyppat
    teḷivuṟa urukkuarak kaṉiyatiru mēṉiyil
        ceṟiyak kaṭāṅ kaviḻkum
vārpūtta ciṟukaṇ peruṅkaḷiṟ ṟurimūṭum
        vaḷḷal pirāṉaḷitta
    maḻimatat taḻicevip puḻineṭum kikkaruṇi
        maḻakaḷiṟṟit tutippām
ērpūtta tavaḷap putuppoṭi aṇinteṉa
        ilaṅkip paṉippaṭaliyāṉ
    iḷanilavu poḻiveḷḷi veṟpeṉa imittiṭṭa
        imayā calattil utiyāk
kārpūtta tirumulli mānakar amarntu oru
        kalāpa mayil vīṟṟirunta
    kāṭcitaru vaṭivuṭip piṭinaṭik koṭiyiṭik
        kavuri coltamiḻ taḻiyavē.     (1)

1. kāppup paruvam

    tirumāl
nīrkoṇṭa caṭilattar vētaṉ paṭittaṟku
        nirumitta vippu itu eṉṉa
    nilavumaṇi māṭamici nīlak kalāpamayil
        neṭitu niṉṟu āṭi amartal
kūrkoṇṭa kulicioru mukil ēṟi āyiram
        kōvum kaḷippa vīti
    kulavum eḻil kāṇtaṉikara mullivaḷar
        koṭiyiṭik kompiṉit taṉipurakka
tārkoṇṭa tirumakaḷi marumattu iruttiyavaḷ
        taṉi oruvar aṟiyāvaṇam
    taṇ naṟavu koppaḷikkum tuḻāyt toṭi eṉum
        taḻipacum tiriyiṉ maṟiyāc
cīrkoṇṭaceṅkēḻk kavuttuvac cuṭarmaṇit
        tiruviḷakkiṭṭu vittu
    teṇtirimicip paḷḷikoṇṭu ulakaḷikkum oru
        tikirip pacum koṇṭalē! (2)
    mācilāmaṇiyīcar
    (vēṟu)
kurumaṇittiraḷi alikoḻittu eṟiyum nīḷ vēli yūṭuṟu naḷḷiruṭṭu ākkiyar
    kulamaṟuttu amarar ciṟiviṭuttu aruḷ ceyvāṉ ātināḷiyil uḷḷam vittu ēttupu
    kukaṉ aruccaṉicey tiruvaṭik kamala mūvāta cōtiyi aiya, muṉkāttaruḷ
    kucalavarkku ariya periya muttitaru tēvāti tēvaṉi vēḷ aḷippārttipar
tirumakaṭku iṟivar, piramar, arccaṉi cey nāṉācorūpaṉi meyyaṉic cīrttikaḻ
    tiṉakarakkaṭavuḷ vaḻipaṭutta maṉi mānāvalūrvaru teḷḷucol pāṭṭiṉar
    'tiruvum meypporuḷum' eṉeṭuttu uricey pāmāli cūṭiya aiyaṉip pālpoli
    tirunatikkuvaṭakari mulippatiyuḷ vāḻmācilāmaṇi vaḷḷalip pōṟṟutum
orumatikkuṭiyum iruḷ matakkaḷiṟum ōrkīravāciyum miy olittu ārttu eḻu
    muvaraḷakkar neṭu muracamikka eḻil māṉār patātiyum malliṉil cūḻcciyiṉ
    oḻivaṟapporutu varukayalkoṭiyum mālāṉatēr koḷum vallamik kīrttiyum
    uṭiyakoṟṟamuṟum oruvaṉ nekkuruka īrēḻulōkamum miyalip pūṭṭiṭum
marumalarkkaṇiyum varukaruppu vilum māṟātu nīṭiya kiyiṉil tūkkiya
    mataṉ urukkeṭavum iratikaṭku iṉiya mārūpam ākiṭa miyiṉit tīṭṭiya
    vaḷarkaṭikkaṇ aruḷ ciritu aḷittaruḷcey tāyāṉa ērpeṟu tiyalic cēṭṭiyal
    vaṭiviṉukku iṇi il koṭiyiṭippiṭiyi vāṭāta kōmaḷa valliyik kākkavē. ( 3)
    mulli vināyakar
    (vēṟu)
veyyapa ṇāṭavi aravap patakkarpoṉ
    villiṭi ōr ulaviyiṉip piṭittu muṉ
moyyuṟu pārata katiyip poṟittaruḷ
    mulli vināyakaṉ aṭiyip paḻiccutu
miyuṟa nīṭiya tuvacak karattiṉiṉ
    malimiyōr taru malarip paṟittu aṇi
cey eyil cūḻ tirumuliyil ceḻippuṟu
    teḷ eḻil kūr koṭiyiṭiyip purakkavē. (4)
    murukavēḷ
    (vēṟu)
takkanal kaṭamvaru tāpataṟ kōttuṟu
    cattiyap poruḷpakar vāyaṉit tākkuṟu
kokkiṉic cuṭarvaṭi vēliṉil cāyttu aruḷ
    kukkiṭak koṭiyuṭi nīraṉip pōṟṟutum;
mukkaṉik koḻunaṟavār vayal tēkkiṭa
    moyttaṭit teḻucenel vāṉnatip pālcela
akkulac curapikaḷār mulip pārppati
    aṟputak koṭiyiṭiyāḷ taṉik kākkavē. (5)
    piramatēvar
    (vēṟu)
cantamuṟu curutip peruṅkaṭal taṉātu nāttaṭa mattiṉāl kaṭinta
    taṭipaṭāp poruḷamuta neñcak kaṭattiṉil tāṉaṭit tuṇṭutēkkic
cinturac cūṭṭuveḷ aṉṉam paṭittu ēṟu cēvaṭikkuravaṉ naṟiyār
    cītaḷat tōṭṭuṭip patumātaṉattil uṟiticimukak kaṭavuḷ kākka
kantamalar kaṟpakak kāṭṭuṭip puttēḷ kaṭāvum payōtaram eṉak
    kārakil pukiyūṭṭu kōtiyit tōkimayil kāṇupu kaḷittāṭavē
kontalar alaṅkal kuḻalkaṟṟi aramakaḷir kūṭṭattoṭu impar maṭavār
    kūṭiviḷiyāṭum maṇimāṭam malimullivāḻ koṭiyiṭi maṭappiṭiyiyē. (6)
    tēvēntiraṉ
    (vēṟu)
vaṭikkum naṟumpāl orukōvum, matikkum iraṇṭu mānitiyum,
    vaḻimummatattil, nāṉkupiṟi maruppup poruppum, aintaruvum,
iṭikkum āṟu, viṇkoḷ puyal ēḻum, katir vaccirap paṭiyum,
    eytik kaṉaka nāṭu aḷikkum eṇ āyiram kaṇṇavaṉ kākka!
noṭikkum aḷavil puram mūṉṟum nutivem kaṇiyāl poṭipaṭutta
    nuntāc cuṭar muccuṭariṉ irunōkkum kuḷirap pūkkaḷ matuk
kuṭikkum kaḷivaṇṭu aṉiya taṭam kuṭaṅkikku aṭaṅkā viḻiparappum
    kūntal piṭiyi aṭiyavarkaḷ kuṭivāḻ mullikkoṭiyē! (7)
    tirumakaḷ
    (vēṟu)
mākattiṉ mītulavu mēkak kuḻanti peruvayiṟu kuḷirap payam uṇum
    vārioru tāḷiṉiṉṟirupōtum orupōtum mātavam iḻikka utiyā
nākappaṭam kiḻiya vāṭik kaṭaṅka viḻunākam purantu ilakupoṉ
    ākatti aṇinalla nākattiṉ ākamvaḷar nāyakiyi añcalippōm
mōkap perumcalati mūḻkit tuyarttiri kaṇmōtak kaṭantu alamaṟum
    mūṭakkurampiyuṟu vāḻvik kaṭantu uyirkaḷ muttikkarikkaṇ ēṟap
pūkattaṭam poḻil uṭutta tirumulliyil puttēḷ iṭattiṉi marīip
    puvaṉaṅkaḷ yāviyum uyirtta koṭiyiṭi iḷam pūviyik kākka eṉṟē (8)
    kalimakaḷ
varikkum kalikkaṭal ulāvippaṭikkum oru makiṇaṉ tiruttāl uṟīi
    maturam paḻuttu oḻukum ici vīṇi puttaka malark kuṭaṅkikkuḷ ēnti,
virikkum varicciṟittumpi matuuṇṭu mural veṇtāmarit taḷiyiṉmēl
    vīṟṟirukkiṉṟa oruveḷ ōmattiṉ aṭi meyyaṉpuṟat tutippām
nerikkum karumpu kaṇveṭittu ukuttiṭṭa veṇnittila nilā eṟippa
    nikaḻum irupoḻutum malarkumutat taṭaṅkaḷ toṟum nilavum polamcūṭṭu aṉam
terikkum tamiḻccuviyoṭu amutoḻukum pālinīrt tīñcuviyum māntivaḷarum
    tirumullivāyil uṟi ciṟparak koṭiyiṭit tiruvip purakka eṉṟē (9)
    catta mātarkaḷ
    (vēṟu)
veṇ naki ilavu oḷip pūniṟat tōṭṭamum
    miṉnikariṭu kiṭippāl kuṟik kōṭṭamum
    viṇṉuṟu karumukiṟkēy kuḻal kāṭṭiyum
    vimmiya maṇivaṭattār mulik kōṭṭiyum
kaṇ eṉum aḻalviḻik kāruruk kūṟṟamum
    kaṉṉali uṟaḻumat tōḷiṇit tōṟṟamum
    kaṇṇiṭum aṭiyarik kāvalil kāttiṭum
    kaṉṉiyar eḻuvar poṉtāḷ muṭiccūṭṭutum
taṇṇaṟa virivirit tāmaric cēkkiyil
    taṉmiyil amaru naṟpālniṟap pēṭṭaṉam
    taṉniḻal aṟaluṟat tāṉ atip pārttu, itu
    taṉmamil kaṉavueṉac cēvalic cīttiṭum
paṇṇiyiṉ vaḷamulit tēviyit tētteṉap
    paṇaḷi muralmaṇat tātuitaḻt tēttoṭip
    paṇṇavar muṟimiyil kātaliṉ tōttiram
    paṇṇiṭu koṭiyiṭip pāviyik kākkavē. (10)
    muppattu mūvar
    (vēṟu)
naripputu malarttavicu ayaṟkuoru naṭukkamatu
    naṭṭamāka nakattil koytu āḷpavar
    nakippoṭu paḻittatoru takkaṉ naviliṭṭitaṉi
    naṭpu ilātu citittiṭṭa cēvakar
    naṭittaruḷ patattiṉil etirttiṭum paṭiccamaṉ
    naṭukkam mēva utittiṭṭa nītiyar
    nakattiṉi eṭuttavaṉ muṭittali patittu alaṟa
    naccutāḷiṉ etirttiṭṭa kāraṇar,
irippoḻutu uḷattiṉil iṇippatam niṉippavari
    eṭṭiṭā iru patmattiṉ mūṭuvar
    iṭikkural matakkaḷiṟu uritta kavacattu iṟivar
    icci kūra, vaṉappuṟṟa pūtari
    emakku maturittiṭa aruḷkaṭi viḻikkaruṇi
    iṭṭamāka maṭuppitta pūraṇi
    ilikkatir ayilpaṭi eṭuttu varukit talivaṉ
    eccamāka aḷittiṭṭa kāriki
muṟippaṭi vakutta puvaṉattiṉi muḻakkavumōr
    muṭṭi vātu ceḻippitta māṟukaḷ
    murukku paritip paṭi taritta kiyarikku iḷiya
    muttuvāḷ naki oppaṟṟa māṉiṉi
    muḷitta piṟiyip porunutal tuṭiiṭikkamala
    mokkunēr muli maṭṭuṟṟa kōtiyar
    murukku alar itaḻk karuviṭakkaṇviṇiṭat tiyalar
    muṟṟu mēval ceyappeṟṟa kōkila
maṟipporuḷ ciṟikkiḷi urittiṭa viruppiṉmaṉam
    vitta pūvi taliccuṟṟi āṭiyil
    vaṭittiṭu matuppuṉal kuṭittiṭu kaḷippiṉoṭu
    matta mēvu curuppuk kuḻāmural
    varappoḻil mulippati maṭakkoṭi yiṭikkavuri
    maṭṭil pēraḻakukku uṟṟa kāvalar
    maruttuvar uruttirar vacukkatir eṉappakarum
    matta vāṉavar muppattu mūvarē. (11)

2. ceṅkīrip paruvam

vaṇṭāṭu ceṅkamala mukamaṇṭalattu aḻaku marakata niṟattiṉ vaṭivu
    matura āṉantac ceḻuntēṉ vaḻintu oḻukumaṇi vāyiṉilaveṟippum
viṇṭāṭu cili nutal kaṭṭiyum poṭṭaḻakum veṇṇittilap paṭṭamum
    viḷariya palāyiram kōṭi aṇṭamum viritta ciṟṟāliṉ iliyuḷ
kaṇṭāṭu moḻiyuṭip puṉal maṅki ceñcaṭik kāṭṭūṭu mey viḷarppak
    karuṇimaḻi poḻi kaṭikkaṇkaḷ orumūṉṟum kaḷittiṭak kaṇṭu kaṇṭu
ceṇṭāṭum viṭi aṇṇal koṇṭāṭum maṭamaññi ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē!
    tirumulli mānakar amarnta koṭiyiṭiyammi ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē! (12)
pūntoṇṭi maṇṭalam viḷaṅkap perumpukaḻ polintiṭap pūṇtikaḷ teṟum
    poṅkoḷip paritiyum tiṅkaḷ veṇkuṭiyum poṟuttu aracaḷikkum neṟiyē
āntoṇṭimāṉ kaṭavu poṉ ōṭi āmpali aḻutti mullik koṭiyiṉāl
    alamarakkaṭṭiṭṭu avaṉpeṟa veḷippaṭṭu aḷappil iṉpatti aruḷi
ēntoṇṭi alarkaṇmaṭal eḻutum mataṉ ākatti eriviḻik kūṭṭi aruḷum
    eḻilmulli nāyakar eṉum teviṭṭākkaṉi iṭappāliṉik kavarnta
tīntoṇṭi aḻakutaru cevvāyp pacuṅkiḷḷi ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē!
    tirumulli mānakar amarnta koṭiyiṭiyammi ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē! (13)
kampak kaṭācalap piḷḷiyit tantu vaṭakayilip poruppil orunāḷ
    karuṭap patāki viṭṭuṇu perum pāntaḷ kaṭum cāpa maṭipaṭa orīi
vempik koṭumpiṟavi veḷḷam tiḷittu uḻalum veṟṟuyirkaḷip paṟṟiyē
    vevviṉip poṟiyakipaṭam virittu āṭāmal viṇṇuṟum eṉatturantē
ampoṉpaṇa aṭavic cēṭaṉ muṭikoṭpuṟa orimpaṭap pāmpum uruka
    arumaṟikaḷiṉṉum ivvaṇṇam eṉṟu uṇarāta aṟputap poṉpotuviṉil
cempoṉ ceḻuṅkoṇṭal āṭak kaḷikkum mayil ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē!
    tirumulli mānakar amarnta koṭiyiṭiyammi ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē! (14)
vāṅku ceñcili nutal kuṟuveyar poṭippa iru malar aṭi tuṭippa niṉṟu
    vaṇṭu uṟāk kaṟpakap pāricātattiṉ malarmāri kālap paṟittut
tūṅku kikkari maruppu iṇimuli arampiyarkaḷ tūyp paṇintu ēval ceyya
    tūtuḷaṅkaṉivāy malarntu iṉi taḻittunal cūṭakac ceṅki malarāḷ
ēṅkumaṇi nūpuram iraṅkumaṇimēkali ilaṅkuceñcīṟaṭiyiṉum
    iṭṭiṭiyiṉum tiruttu ayirāṇi paṉmuṟi eṭuttu aṇittu ucci mōnta
tēṅkuḻal piṭiyi marumakaḷ eṉap peṟṟapiṭi ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē!
    tirumulli mānakar amarnta koṭiyiṭiyammi ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē! (15)
ilakkāṉa pakiraṇṭamuṟṟum ceṟukkaḷ ceytu eṇṇil uyir varkkam muḻutum
    īriru karuppattil vittiṭṭu iruttiyavi eḻiluṟa muḷitta piṉṉar
malakkōṭi veppattil vāṭātu aruṭkaruṇi maṭi tiṟantu amutu oḻukki
    valviṉik kaḷikaṭṭu muṭṭuṟupul āti eṉum mākkaḷi aṭātu aṭakkic
calippāṟum vaṇṇam taḻittu ōṅka nīṅkātu tāṅki muṉṉīṭu peṟṟut
    tākkaṟṟa civapōka mutiraviḷivikkum taṉitteyvamāki niṉṟa
cilikkōla naṟunutal pacciḷam peṇṇaraci ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē !
    tirumulli mānakar amarnta koṭiyiṭi yammi ceṅkīri āṭi aruḷē! (16)
    (vēṟu)
poṉ aṭi taṅkiya kiṇkiṇiyōṭu pulampu cilampu āṭap
    pūraṇa mukamatiyūṭu eḻu kiraṇapputunaki nilavu āṭa
miṉ oḷir kātaṇi kuḻiyoṭu koppu viḷaṅki virintāṭa
    veṇtaraḷacciṟu mucciyum uccimītiṉ acintāṭak
kaṉṉaliṉ mecciya pacciḷamēṉik katir oḷi viṇṭāṭak
    kamalam malarnta viḻikkaṭi tōṟum karuṇi kutittāṭa
vaṉṉamalarttaṭa mulli iḷaṅkiḷi āṭuka ceṅkīri!
    aṭiyavarukku aruḷ koṭiyiṭi yampiki āṭuka ceṅkīri! (17)
veḷḷiya muttiṉi ottu mukattiṉil vērvu mutittāṭa
    veyil umiḻ cuṭṭiyu maṇikiḷarpaṭṭamummiṉṉutal mēlāṭat
teḷoḷi vaḷarkuru maṇivayirac cariceṅkiyil mītāṭat
    teyva maṇam kamaḻ āliliyip porutiru utaramum āṭak
kaḷḷaviṉip pacupācam aṟuttaruḷ kaṇiṇi vaṇṭāṭa
    kaṭṭaḻakuṟṟa karumpuruvac cilikatirviṭa maṇimārpi
ṉaḷ aḻakāṭiṭa mulli iḷaṅkiḷi āṭuka ceṅkīri!
    aṭiyavarukku aruḷ koṭiyiṭi yampiki āṭuka ceṅkīri! (18)
vaṇtukil āṭi vilvīci nuṭaṅku maruṅku tuvaṇṭāṭa
    vāyamutūṟi vaḻintu naṉinta vaḷkāñci malintāṭak
kaṇṭavar uḷḷam uṭintu nekiḻntu kaṉintu kaḷippāṭak
    kāmar tatumpu kutampiyiṉōṭu irukātum acintāṭat
taṇtaraḷam taru muṇṭaka vampakam taṇtuḷi koḷḷāmē
    tāvaṭi pōl avir toṭṭil utittu irutāḷkaḷum nōvāmē
aṇṭamuṭaṉ pakiraṇṭamum āṭiṭa āṭuka ceṅkīri!
    aṭiyavarukku aruḷ koṭiyiṭi yampiki āṭuka ceṅkīri! (19)
    (vēṟu)
tantelum iṭṭiṭiyāḷ iratic catitaṉṉō ṭikkākun
    taṉittaṉuvik koṭualark kaṇipeytu camarkkōlattōṭum
vantuetir aṅkacaṉitteṟum vaḷḷal makiḻntārum tēṉeṉ
    maṉattuṟu miyal akaṟṟiṭavanta maruntē! pintōkāy!
ninti iḻukkaṟumāl ayaṉ umparkaḷ niṉpāl iṉpārum
    nilippuṟu celvam aḷittaruḷka eṉṟu neruṅkā niṉṟu ōtum
centaḷirip porucīṟaṭi aṅkaṉi ceṅkō ceṅkīri!
    ceḻittiṭum mulli taḻitta pacuṅkoṭi ceṅkō ceṅkīri! (20)
paṅka malattuyar pōy akalumpaṭi paṇpōṭu aṉpākip
    paḻiccuṟu ceyya malarppatam vantu paṇintē niṉṟāri
amkamalattu ayaṉ nāraṇaṉum toḻa añcēl eṉṟu ōti
    aruṭperu veḷḷam aḻuttiṭukiṉṟa aṇaṅkē nantāta
caṅkamalappaṭi cārpaṇiyūṭu tavaḻntēṟum pālit
    taṉittuṟi malku maṉappeṭi pōluṟu tattāy muttārum
tiṅkaḷ mukak kaliyāṇa cavuntari ceṅkō ceṅkīri!
    ceḻittiṭum mulli taḻitta pacuṅkoṭi ceṅkō ceṅkīri! (21)

3. tālap paruvam

kaḷḷak karuṅkaṇ kaṭiciyarkaḷ kaḷikaṭṭiṭa nīr kaḻaṉivarak
    kaṇṭam kavarkāṉaṭik kuṭinta kaviṉār cūṭṭuc ciṟiyaṉaṅkaḷ
tuḷḷit taḻalil tōyvatupōl cuṭarkkōkaṉakattalar oṭuṅkit
    turuvi iviyum kaḷivar eṉat tuṇintaṅku iriyat tuṉṉiyavar
muḷḷil polinta nāḷamuṟu muḷari arintu nāciyiṭi
    mōntu murukuvāy maṭuttu mukiltaṇ kuḻal kāṭṭiṭic ceruki
maḷḷarkku aḻaku kāṭṭum mulli vaṉattāy tālō, tālēlō
    malimēl muḷitta malarkkoṭiyē maṇiyē tālō tālēlō. ( 22)
kāril polinta karuṅkuvaḷi kañalik kiṭakkum puṉal taṭattil
    karuṅkōṭṭu erumi pāyntu uḻakkakkaṇṭu veruvik kaṉakaniṟa
mūripparu neṭṭiḷavāḷi mutirākkantit taṇṭalimēl
    mōtip paḻakkāy utirntiṭap paṉmuṟiyu mīṇṭu varum kāṭci
cīrilpolinta nakar maṭanti ceḻumpūṅkuḻalil cikkaṟukkum
    ceyyapavaḷak kaṅkam eṉum ciṟappil polintu taḻittu ōṅkum
mārippoḻilcūḻ tirumulli vaṉattāy tālō, tālēlō
    malimēl muḷitta malarkkoṭiyē maṇiyē tālō, tālēlō. (23)
toṭṭa akaḻi naṭuppukuntu tuṭum eṉṟu olippap piṭikaḷoṭu
    tutikkip pakaṭuviḷiyāṭat tōṭṭik kuṭaṅkik kaṭumpākar
iṭṭavirupput toṭaryāttu aṅku īrttiṭṭatti eṉa iḻiyum
    eḻilitaṉiyum piṇippar eṉa viriyakkūṭat ticittiṭutal
aṭṭa ticiyum pukaḻparappum aḻakārkūṭal ceḻiyaṉ muṉa
    matiruṅkaruṅ koṇṭalic ciṟiyuḷākkum perumi ēykkum uyar
vaṭṭa matilcūḻ tirumulli vaṉattāy tālō, tālēlō
    malimēl muḷitta malarkkoṭiyē maṇiyē tālō, tālēlō. (24)
teḷḷik kuḻitta veṇcutiyāl tīṟṟappolinta māḷikiya
    teruvil tavaḷat turaṅkaniri ceṟintu kaḷippōṭalamaravē
uḷḷattu aṭaṅkā viyappaḷikkum oḷikūr ammāḷiki mukappil
    oḻuṅkil patitta nīlamaṇi oḷiruntōṟṟa naḷirpūtta
paḷḷap perumpāṟkaṭalum atil parakkum taraṅkap palaniriyum
    paṇimēl kiṭantu kaṇvaḷarum pacci niṟattu aṇṇalum ēykum
vaḷḷal koṭiyār vāḻ mulli vaṉattāy tālō, tālēlō
    malimēl muḷitta malarkkoṭiyē, maṇiyē, tālō, tālēlō. (25)
patikkum aruṇam maṇimakuṭam parittumuṭimītu aracar iḷam
    pariti aṉiya kumarar kuḻām pacumpoṉkoṭiñcit taṭamtērmēl
tutikkumpiṭi nīḷmaṇimaṟukil culavip pavaṉi varuṅkāli
    tuṇikkuṉṟu aṉiya mulikkaḷiṟu cumanta nitampattēr maṭavār
kotikkum nīlakkaruṅkaṇ neṭum kūrvēl eṟiya avar piṭitta
    kolivēl vallinili taḷarntu kuṟṟēvalukku āṭkoḷḷum eṉa
matikkum kāmappōrcey mulli vaṉattāy tālō, tālēlō
    malimēl muḷitta malarkkoṭiyē maṇiyē tālō, tālēlō. (26)
    (vēṟu)
nirkkuṇa niṉmala nittiya pūraṇa niraticayattu aḻiyā
    niṭkaḷa acala nirañcaṉa puṉita nirūpa akaṇṭitamār
ciṟpara varata vicintita nirumala timiram irittaruḷum
    tiṉakara utaya aṉātiparampara ciṉmaya citturuvām
aṟputa meyppara tattuva uttama aṭṭakuṇap poruḷām
    attuvitat tiṟi ettiṟam uṟṟaṉar attiṟamāy amarvāy
caṟkuṇar paraviya poṟpuramuli umi tālō, tālēlō
    taṟpara marakata viṟkoṭiyiṭi umi tālō tālēlō (27)
attiyiluṟu vaṭavittaḻal puriyum arakkartamakku iṟiyām
    attam ōr irupatu peṟṟavaṉ alaṟi aḻuṅkiṭa maṇimuṭikaḷ
pattuaṟa, orukaṇi toṭṭavaṉ, eruvaṭapattira mici vaḷarum
    patmavilōcaṉaṉukku oru taṅki eṉap pakar piṅkiḷiyē!
cittira maturiyiṉ muttaṉam eḻa vaḻutikku oru makavākit
    tikkoru vicayamuṟat taṉiyaracu celuttiya peṇṇamutē!
cattiyar teritaru muttamiḻ mulicili tālō, tālēlō
    taṟpara marakata viṟkoṭiyiṭi umi tālō, tālēlō (28)
veṇtiri akaṭu neruppeḻa ñāṅkar viṟalpaṭiyit tiriyā
    viṭṭu oru cūrmutal aṭṭu imiyōr ciṟiviṭṭu aṇi mayilūrāk
koṇṭal uṟaṅku piṟaṅkaliṉēṉal kuralkaḷi vavvāmal
    kurumaṇikavaṇvit tiṟaṇiliruntu palkuruku kaṭintōppum
vaṇtaḻi āṭikkuṟavar vaḷarttiṭum vaḷḷitaṉ vaḷḷam eṉum
    maṇikiḷar kaḷapakkuvimuli taḻuviya makaṉit tarumaṭavāy
taṇṭali maḻitavaḻmulli vaṉakkiḷi tālō, tālēlō
    taṟpara marakata viṟkoṭiyiṭi umi tālō, tālēlō (29)
    (vēṟu)
kavaliyuṟṟa pavamāṟātu āṟātu ēṟātē
    kaḻiuyirt tokikaḷ tēyā māyā tōyātē
navamatuṟṟa aruḷ īvāy, pāvāy ōvāmē
    naḷiṉa meccumoru vētā ōtā mātāvē
avam aṟukkum oru kōmāṉē māpūmātē
    amutupeṟṟa peruvāṉāṭu āṉātē nāḷum
kuvalayattiṉ muliyāḷvāy tālō tālēlō
    koṭiyiṭik karuṇi vāḻvē tālō, tālēlō (30)
kulavu aṉattiṉ naṭiyāḷē! tālō, tālēlō
    kulavaṉattiṉ naṭiyāḷē tālō tālēlō
malivu ilarkku iṭamatu āvāy tālō tālēlō
    mali vilarkku iṭamatu āvāy tālō tālēlō
cili yirukkum nutal īṭāy tālō tālēlō
    ciliyirukku nutal īṭāy tālō tālēlō
kali virikkum muliyāyē tālō tālēlō
    kalivirikku muliyāyē tālō tālēlō (31)

4. cappāṇip paruvam

cakkarattu āyiram cuṭaruṭaṉ eḻuntamārttāṇṭaṉip paricikkum ōr
    tāmariyi maṟṟor tāmari muraṇimōtuṟum cālpu eṉavum iravil prakā
cakkarattāl taṉi mukam kūmpiṭac ceyum tārakāpatiyi ikalic
    calamēvum muḷariyi namakku itu takātu eṉac cār muḷari takital eṉavum
cakkarattāl oru calantaraṉ iraṇṭu kūṟāmpaṭi taṭinta koḻunaṉ
    taṉkaṇ kaḷām cuṭar iraṇṭum putittut taḻittuc civantirunta
cakkarat tāmaric cuntarac ceṅkikoṭu cappāṇi koṭṭi aruḷē!
    taṭamulli kuṭikoṇṭa koṭiyiṭip perumāṭṭi cappāṇi koṭṭi yaruḷē! (32)
eṅkaṇum pūttem irukaṇ putittalāl īṭṭumā vaṭu uṉakku iṉṟu
    eytiyatu eṉakkēṭṭu umakku eṉṟum māvaṭu viruttuvaṉ eṉac ceytal pōl
aṅkaṇ neṭuñālattiṉ āruyir taḷirttiṭa aṟaṅkaḷ eṇṇāṅku ceyyā
    arumātavattiṉāl orumā aṭuttavari arccittuḻik kampi nīr
poṅkum vaki varavaḻittu accam koṭukkap porukkeṉavuṟat taḻuviyē
    puttēḷir kāṇāta pūraṇattukkum pututtaḻumpuṟa viyaṟṟum
taṅkavaḷi taṅkum irupaṅkayac ceṅkiyil cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē!
    taṭamulli kuṭikoṇṭa koṭiyiṭip perumāṭṭi cappāṇi koṭṭi yaruḷē! (33)
poṅkāṭum aravaṇip puttēḷ tarukkuṟāp porucūkarattu uruvamāyp
    pūppiḷantu aṟiyātu teru marappaḻamaṟipulampi ōliṭṭu ayarvuṟak
koṅkāṭu koṉṟi, taṇkūviḷaṅ, kōḷaravu, kuḷirtiṅkaḷ, kokkiṟakuṭaṉ
    kuripuṉal, ceḻumattam, veḷerukku, aṟukātti, kōtil veṇṭaliyum, aliyum
ceṅkāṭu koṇṭapoṟ kōṭīramāṭac ceḻum poṉṉiṉ maṉṟuḷ āṭum
    cēvaṭi varuntātu tivantu tivantu tiṉamum civappūṟum niṉ
caṅkāṭu ceṅkoṇṭal amkit talam koṇṭu cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē !
    taṭamulli kuṭikoṇṭa koṭiyiṭip perumāṭṭi cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē ! (34)
miyōti miyaṇi kuṭaṅkikku aṭaṅkā matarttu ariparanta oṇkaṇ
    malarvāṇi ayirāṇi peṇ āṇi muttaṉiya māvāḷ nilāmatimukap
poyyōtu ciṟṟiṭip poṟṟoṭik kaṟpuṭip pūviyarkaḷ nāyakaroṭum
    pūrippoṭu aḷavaḷāy iṉpaṅkaḷ tuyttiṭap pōkam koṭuppatu oṉṟu
meyyōka cātakattāl iruviṉip pantam vēraṟa aṭittoṇṭu cey
    viḻumiyari nāḷtoṟum eṭuttu eṭuttu āṉantaveḷi vīṭṭiruttal oṉṟum
ciyōkamākaniṉ irupāṇiyum koṇṭu cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē!
    taṭamulli kuṭikoṇṭa koṭiyiṭip perumāṭṭi cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē! (35)
pōkkumāṟu ariyoru poyiruḷ aḻuntivaru pulliya uyirkku iṭamatāp
    puvaṉap parappu elām paṉmuṟiyum īṉṟavikaḷ pōṟṟa muttēvar tamiyum
ākkumāṟum aṉṟi avaravar maṉakkaruttu aṟiyumāṟum karuṇiyāl
    akilamum taṉmayam eṉumpaṭi kalantaruḷi aivakic cattiyākik
kākkumāṟum viṉi kaḻikkumāṟum taṉatu kivalyamāy
    kāṭciyi niṉintu irukkum nī caṟṟu vimmitamōṭu kimmalarkaḷ kūṭṭi orukāl
kākkumāṟu oppa irupoṉṉam kuṭaṅkiyāl cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē!
    taṭamulli kuṭikoṇṭa koṭiyiṭip perumāṭṭi cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē! (36)
    (vēṟu)
pañcaṭi nōva naṭantu orutoṭṭil paḷḷikoḷ puṇṭarikap
    paṉṉiru kaṇ irumūṉṟu mukattup pālakaṉip parivāl
koñci eṭuttuk koṅki aṇittuk kumutam ceṅkaṉivāyk
    kutali moḻic cuvi amutam maṭuttuk kukaṉukku kuḷiru
mañcaṉam āṭṭi, maṇikkalaṉ iṭṭu, aṇi miyum viḻikku eḻuti
    mañcaḷ tarittu ceñcaraṇattiṭi malinuṇ tukaḷ nīvik
kuñci tiruttic cinturam iṭṭavaḷ koṭṭuka cappāṇi!
    koṭimatil mullik koṭiyiṭi nāyaki koṭṭuka cappāṇi! (37)
tavaḷat tiricuricaṅkam eṭuttueṟi calati mukaṭṭūṭē
    tamaram mikuttiṭa mantara veṟpit taṉimattāka niṟīit
tuvaḷap paṭukayiṟu aravu piṇittut tuṇiyum maṉattiṉarāyc
    curaroṭum acurarkaḷ kaṭiya utittuc cuṭṭiṭa varunañcip
pavaḷappaṭiva maṇāḷaṉ eṭuttup parukiṭum amiyattē
    paṟpalarūpa vicittiram nalkum pārviyiṉ amutākkum
kuvaḷik kaṇkaḷiṉ añcaṉam iṭṭavaḷ koṭṭuka cappāṇi !
    koṭimatil mullik koṭiyiṭi nāyaki koṭṭuka cappāṇi! (38)
vaḻuttiya poytal pētiyarōṭu malarkkāvūṭu ēki
    vaṇṭalar kuṟṟu varippantu eṟṟupu maṇi ammaṉi āṭā
viḻippuṟu kaṇ pottiyum viḷiyāṭi viḷaṅku ceyaṟkiyalāl
    meymmi iyaṟkit taṉmiyum muṟṟum mikutta civappūṟic
ceḻittiṭu teyvattāmari aḻaku tiṟampac cevvipeṟum
    tīṅkovvik kaṉivāyil peruviral tittikkac cuviyāk
koḻittiṭu tēṟal virippoli kikkoṭu koṭṭuka cappāṇi!
    koṭimatil mullik koṭiyiṭi nāyaki koṭṭuka cappāṇi! (39)
    (vēṟu)
kayili varikkuḷ irukkum viruppiṉarkaṭ pāvāy
    karuṇiyiṉ veḷḷattu amutam vaṭitta cuvittēṉē!
mayili maruṭṭum vaṉappiṉi uṟṟamel nalcāyal
    malarmakaḷ celvak kalimakaḷ peṟṟa tavappēṟē!
ayiluṟum mattaṉi attiyi vittu vaḷarttāḷē
    aruviṉi vemmic ciṟumi akaṟṟum mayilpēṭē!
kuyiliṉam veṭka miḻaṟṟuvaḷ koṭṭuka cappāṇi!
    koḻitamiḻ mullik koṭiyiṭi koṭṭuka cappāṇi! (40)
viḷari muḻaṅkum aḷikkulam moyttavirik kōtāy!
    vituvuṭaṉ ellukku eḻili aḷikkum veyil pāvāy!
oḷivaḷar patmamalarppatam vitta karuttoṭu
    murukiya uḷḷattavarkaḷ kuḷitta perukkāṟē !
puḷaka mulik kalacat tamutattiṉ vayal tōṇi
    puramvaru piḷḷikkutara niṟittaruḷ naṟṟāyē!
kuḷir imayattu maṭappiṭi koṭṭuka cappāṇi!
    koḻitamiḻ mullik koṭiyiṭi koṭṭuka cappāṇi! (41)

5. muttap paruvam

    (vēṟu)
cekkac civanta pavaḷam eṟitirup pāṟkaṭalil piṟavāmal
    celvak kaṉaka nāṭṭil iru teyvanitiyil piṟavāmal
okkap puvaṉam paṭittāṉi untik kamalattiṭi uyirtta
    uvaṇak koṭiyōṉ karattu ēntum oḷir veṇcaṅkattu utiyāmal
kakkak kuruti camparaṉik kāynta malariṅkaṇi vīraṉ
    kiyil piṭitta māl vicayak kaṉṉaliṭattu naṇṇāmal
mukkaṇ karumpiṉ iṭattu utitta muttē muttan tarukavē!
    mullip pativāḻ koṭiyiṭiniṉ murukār muttam tarukavē! (42)
urukka muṟakkuñ caramukattiṉ oruttaṉtaṉi muṉīṉṟeṭutta
    ōmeṉ piṭiyē! aṭiyēṅkaḷ uḷḷam teviṭṭāt teḷḷamutē!
tirukkit tirukkaṇṇāl akaṟṟum teyvak koḻuntē peruntavattāl
    tēṭi eṭukkum aḻiyāta cēmanitiyē ūḻitoṟu
mirukku mirukkum mukil aṟiyā iṉpakkaṭalē emperumāṉ
    ellām uṇarnta tiruvuḷattil eḻutiyamitta ōviyamē!
murukki urukkuñ ceṅkaṉivāy muttam taruka muttamē!
    mullip pativāḻ koṭiyiṭi niṉmurukār muttam tarukavē! (43)
poṟṟāḷ iṇikkum tōḷ iṇikkum puṇarmeṉ mulikkum maṅkalanāṇ
    pūṭṭumpacum kantarattiṉukkum puṉitat tiruvāy moḻiyiṉukkum
vaṟṟāk karuṇik kaṇkaḷukkum maruvār ñāṉakkarum kuḻaṟkum
    maṟuki uṭinta muṇṭakamum variyār variyum karimaruppum
naṟṟāṟ ṟilaṅkum maṭal kamuku naralum kamañcūl valampuriyu
    naṟukkum karumpum kaṭalu mañcu nalkum muttaṅkaḷi nayavēm
muṟṟāk kurumpikkaḷapa iḷamuliyāy muttam tarukavē!
    mullip pativāḻ koṭiyiṭiniṉ murukār muttam tarukavē! (44)
tuṭikku nikaru maṭikkayaṅkaḷ tuḷikki mukantu piṭikku ūṭṭum
    tūveḷ aruvi niriniriyāc corinta tikiric cuṭar muttum
paṭikkum pulavar kavikku aṭaṅkāp pāli āṟṟil paṭumuttum
    pakitīr toṇṭi vaḷanāṭṭil paṇṇic cennel katirmuttum
veṭikkum vēḻattiraḷ muttum vēṟupala veṇ muttamum niṉ
    viḷiyāṭṭiṉukku vittaruḷi vēṇṭum emakkuk kaṟpakap pū
muṭikkum kuḻalāy niṉkaṉivāy muttam taruka muttamē!
    mullip pativāḻ koṭiyiṭi niṉmurukār muttam tarukavē! (45)
nākamuṭiyil cuṭarmaṇiyum ñālattu ilaṅku navamaṇiyum
    naṟumpūn taruvil curumpūtā nāṭṭil uṟucin tāmaṇiyum
ākam civanta malarppīṭattu ayal nāṭṭuḷḷa tirumaṇiyum
    antaṇ tuḷavatteriyal aṇi ammāṉ marumattu aṇimaṇiyum
cēku paṭiyāk kuruvintac ceyya maṇi eṉṟu eṇṇi viṭum
    ciṟiyēṅkaḷukkuc civāṉantam tittittu arumpattiṉam tiṉamum
mōkam koṭukkum ceṅkaṉivāy muttam taruka muttamē!
    mullip pativāḻ koṭiyiṭi niṉmurukār muttam tarukavē! (46)
    (vēṟu)
marukkiḷar taḷirttanaṟu māniḻalkīḻ viriyum mallikit taviciṉ naṭuvaṇ
    mañcaḷaṭi tivarattuñcu ceṅkaṭ kavarimaṇi alarp poyki aḻiyā
urukkiḷar vaṉattiṉi uḻakkiṭa neṭum keṇṭi ūṟupāl maṭiyi muṭṭa
    oḻukum veḷ aruviyil poḻikiṉṟa tīmpālu vaṭṭueṭut tamutamayamāka
kurukkiḷarum aṇi kaṭantu eṅkum vaḻikāṭci orukōṭi mati utayamākik
    koḷḷi nilavu eṇtikkiṉum kaṟṟi kaṟṟiyāk koṭṭik kiṭattal māṉum
tirukkiḷarum mulliyuḷ irukkum oru cuntari tirukkōvi muttam aruḷē!
    cinturappiṟinutal kuntaḷak koṭiyiṭi tirukkōvi muttam aruḷē! (47)
vāṉāṭu maṭṭum vaḷar nāḷikērattiṉ mutir vaṇṇap paḻaṅkaḷ palaviṉ
    vacimuṭpuṟak kaṉiyaṟak kīṇṭu tūṅkukuli māṅkaṉi uṭittataṉ kīḻk
kāṉāṭu kataliyiṉ tāṟṟup paḻattik kaṟuttuvantu ālavālak
    kaṇṇaka nirampac corinta accāṟu pōyk kaṭimaṭi uṭittiṭakkaṇ
mīṉāṭu uḻattiyar viyappuṟak kaḷamar atil viravivarum appaḻam elām
    vēṟeṭuttu ataṉāl uṭippaṭittap paṅkamēṉāṟu naṭum vaḷami kūr
tēṉāṭu taṇṭalit tirumuli nāyaki tirukkōvi muttam aruḷē!
    cinturap piṟinutal kuntaḷak koṭiyiṭi tirukkōvi muttam aruḷē! (48)
toṉṉūl maṟikkaṭaṉ mukanta cennāvōṭu tōṉṟum miṉeṉṉa munnūl
    tuvaḷum maṇi mārpuṭip pūcurarkaḷ mukilil toṉittiṭum
munnūlu muṟiyil payiṉṟu aiyam aṇuvēṉum muṉṉattuṟāmal uyttu
    muḻuvatum uṇarnta mutukuravarkaḷum akkuravar moḻikaḷ tavaṟātu niṉṟē
ennūlum uṇarum nalcētaṉac caṟkuṇat teṇṇiṉ māṇākkar kuḻuvum
    iruntōtum muttamiḻk kaḻakaṅkaḷ tōṟum nalicivāṇi kāṇikoṇṭa
cennūl piṟanta tirumullivaḷar kāriki tirukkōvi muttam aruḷē!
    cinturap piṟinutal kuntaḷak koṭiyiṭi tirukkōvi muttam aruḷē! (49)
    (vēṟu)
ulipuku meḻukueṉa urukiṭum irutayar ucciyiṭat tuṟiyum
    olikeḻuparipura caraṇamum vakiriḻi ottiṭum ciṟṟiṭiyum
alieṟi calatiyil nilaviya nayaṉamum accam akaṟṟiyiṭum
    aruḷpoḻi vataṉamum iruḷceṟi aḷakamum akkaṇutaṟ kaṇavar
kaliyoṭu maḻuvalar karatalam kuviya kaccu mulit taṭamum
    kaṉavilum naṉavilum niṉipavar viṉitaru kaṭṭam aṟuttaruḷvōy!
mulinakar nilipeṟu cilinutal malimakaḷ muttam aḷittaruḷē!
    mutumaṟi muṭivaḷar koṭiyiṭi umiyavaḷ muttam aḷittaruḷē! (50)
kurakata niriyumiḻ aruviyum maruviya kuṟṟi muṟittu ikalik
    kumuṟoli perukiya kaṟiyaṭi iyamavi koṭṭu matattiraḷum
viraviya natieṉa oḻukiṭa ataṉiṭi vittakam uṟṟiṭunal
    viciyoṭu varumaṇi kiḷar kaviṉuṟu koṭi mecciratat tokikaḷ
tiriceṟi uvariyiṉiṭi varupala timil cettuviyapputavum
    ticitoṟum iciyuṟu taṉamuṭi aracarkaḷ cepparum veṟṟikoḷum
muracatir tirumaṟuku aṇimuli imiyavaḷ muttam aḷittaruḷē!
    mutumaṟi muṭivaḷar koṭiyiṭi umiyavaḷ muttam aḷittaruḷē! (51)

6. varukip paruvam

veṇtaraḷam malkuciṟu taṇṭiyoṭum erimaṇi viḷaṅkucempoṉ cilampu
    meṉkural kiṇkiṇiyum ōliṭṭu araṟṟiṭa viruppuṭaṉ aḻuttu cempañcu
uṇṭaaravintat tiruttāḷ peyarttali uṭukkip peruṅkuvalayam
    uṉaṭikaḷ cūṭap peruntavam iḻittatāl utavum perumpayaṉuṟat
taṇtaraḷaveṇ toṭitarikkum taṉakkuvaṭu tāṅkariya tiṭukiṭi eṉat
    tamaṉiyak kurumaṇip parumam pulampiṭat tammiaṟi toṇṭaritaya
muṇṭaka malarkkōyil kuṭikoṇṭirukkum orumulli nāyaki varukavē!
    muḻumutal poruḷ mācilāmaṇiyar aṇikiṉṟa mōkaṉappeṇ varukavē (52)
oppaṟṟa cīṟaṭi naṭikkuiṭintu ōtimam oḷittiṭap piṇimukam elām
    uṉcāyalik kaṇṭu arum kāṉakattuṟa uḻikkaṇam viḻikku otuṅkac
ceppotta mulikaṇṭu nēmippuḷ iriya niṉ cevvāy moḻikku uṭintu
    tittitta colkiḷiyum māṅkuyilum ēṅkiṭat teyvap piṇākkaḷuṭiya
appotta kaṇiṇik kaḷivaṇṭiṉaṅkaḷ mataṉ attam toṭutta ciliyil
    aḷivantu aṭintaṉṉa amminiṉ tirumēṉi avvaḷavum vavvikkoḷa
muppattu iraṇṭu aṟam vaḷarttu ulaku aḷitta tirumulli nāyaki varukavē
    muḻumutal poruḷ mācilāmaṇiyar aṇikiṉṟa mōkaṉap peṇ varukavē! (53)
vētaṇṭam otta puyavīrap perumpaṭiñar vempaṭi payiṟṟum oliyum
    viritirikkaṭal kuḻiya vāymaṭūu varukiṉṟa viṇ kuḻāttiyum vilakku
mātaṇṭam nālum puḻikkarattu ēntivarum matakaḷiṟu piḷiṟu oliyum
    maṉṉukati aintiṉoṭu cāripatiṉeṭṭum valavāmpari kaṉikkum oliyum
kōtaṇṭa nāṇ teṟittiṭa nīṭum ōtiyum koḷḷāmal uḷ aṭakkik
    koṟṟaveṇ kuṭi maṉṉar kōyil kaṭittali kulāvip piṭitta caṅkam
mūtaṇṭa kūṭamum veṭittiṭa muḻaṅkutiru mulli nāyaki varukavē!
    muḻumutal poruḷ mācilāmaṇiyar aṇikiṉṟa mōkaṉap peṇ varukavē! (54)
kaḷaviḻ maruppoḻil varukkip paḻaṅkaḷik karumanti muḻavu koṭṭak
    kaṇṭumiku vāṉaraṅ kokkiṉutir kaṉikaḷik kiyil ammāṉi āṭa
naḷamar curumparici pāṭak kalāpamayil nāṭakam puriya aḻakār
    naṟumalarkkōṭaraṅ kittali acittiṭa nayattit terintu aḷikkum
vaḷḷaliṉ cempoṉ ceruntikaḷ corintiṭa malark kāntaḷaṅki ēṟkum
    vaṇmiyi arampiyar naṭittiṭum araṅkeṉa matittiṭu mataṟ kuḻiyelām
muḷḷarit tāmariyil aṉamēvumaṇi vāvi mulli nāyaki varukavē!
    muḻumutal poruḷ mācilāmaṇiyar aṇikiṉṟa mōkaṉap peṇ varukavē! (55)
tattum veḷaruvit taṭañcāral nantiyam cilapparappil viḷiyuṟṟa
    tamaṉiyak kuppiyum tantiveṇ kōṭu nalcantaṉak kantaniripūṅ
kottuṭaṉ kōḻarik kār akil kuṟaṭum kurūuniṟap pīlipalavum
    kuṅkumat toḻutiyoṭu caṇpakap pōtum kuvāṉmaṇit tiraḷ varkkamum
mattuṟum kāntaḷiṉ arumpum ceḻum tēttiṟalum cumantu untiyē
    alampum tirikkarattāl irupuṟattum veḷḷamutam tuḷippatēpōl
muttam tiraṭṭivaru pāliyiṉ vaṭātu kari mulli nāyaki varukavē!
    muḻumutal poruḷ mācilāmaṇiyar aṇikiṉṟa mōkaṉap peṇ varukavē (56)
    vēṟu
caratkālattu mativataṉat tiyal maṭavār ūṭaliṉāl
    taṭamcōpāṉat taramiyattil taṉiniṉṟu ukutta kalaṉ maṇiyi
uralkāl yāṉi neruppeṉak kaṇṭu otuṅki naṭakkum vītielām
    oḷirum taraḷaṅkaḷik koḻittu aṅku ulavum paruvap pētiyarkaḷ
tirakkātaliṉāl paṇṇiyoṭum ciṟṟil iḻikkac cēyiṉaṅkaḷ
    ciṟutēr uruṭṭic citittiṭap piṉ tiraṭṭi muttaṅkaḷiṟ kuyiṟṟu
marakkāmpalampūntaṭa mullikku aracē varuka varukavē!
    aruḷpūttu ilaṅku koṭiyiṭip peṇ amutē varuka varukavē (57)
paṭikkum kayaṟkum karuviḷikkum paravikkaṭaṟkum pāṉalukkum
    pāyum piṇikku nikar taṭaṅkaṇ pavaḷattiruvāyp piṟikkoḻuntiṉ
iṭikkuṅkumap poṉ taṉamaṭavār eḻilārpaḷikku māḷikimēl
    eṟikantukaṅkaḷ alaripacci eḻukantukattip pōyp puṭikkat
tuṭikkum urimiyāḷarip pōṟṟuvaḷu neṭuṅkāṟ kētaṉamat
    turaṅkat takaṭuvaruṭiyavaṉ cuṭarttērk koṭiyi kīṟi viṇṇi
aṭikkum puricit tirumullik karacē varuka varukavē!
    aruḷpūttu ilaṅku koṭiyiṭip peṇ amutē varuka varukavē! (58)
paṇṭic curutip paṉuvaltaṉil patiyum poruḷi aṟatterintu
    payilum irumuttoḻiṟku uriyār pacumpoṉ yūkattaṟi niṟuvik
kuṇṭattu iyaṟṟum vēḷvitoṟum koṇṭal paṭalipōl eḻunta
    kuyeṉṟu urikkum naṟumpukipōyk kuḻumivicumpip putittaliṉāl
caṇṭaṟku iratantaṉi iḻuttut tāṇṭum eḻumā uṭal karumi
    taṅkik kiṭappataṉi ulakōr taḻikkum paccik kurakatameṉ
aṇṭattaṟiyac cāṟṟum mullikku aracē varuka varukavē!
    aruḷpūttu ilaṅku koṭiyiṭip peṇ amutē varuka varukavē! (59)
kaṇiyā aṇṭap parappu aṉittum kāvum oru tūṇ eṉa niṟuttum
    kaḻaṟaṟku ariya matukiyuṭik kaṉakap poruppām vaṭamēru
kuṇiyāk kālam poṟuttataṉāl koḷḷum paramāl tātuvali
    kuṟintu peruṅkāl taḷarntiṭiṉum kolēṟṟu aṇṇal iṉiorukkāl
paṇiyāp puriyōr tami aṭuvāṉ parumātavarāk kuḻittiṭiṉum
    parikka ataṟku oppāka oṉṟu paṭittatu eṉa vāṉ mukaṭu uriñcum
aṇiyār tirukkōpura mullikku aracē varuka varukavē!
    aruḷpūttu ilaṅku koṭiyiṭip peṇ amutē varuka varukavē! (60)
maṇamkūr ēlanaṟum kūntal mayilē varuka ! eṅkaḷ peru
    vāḻvē varuka! maliyaracaṉ makaḷē varuka! māṉa aruḷ
kuṇamkūr iṉpaccivañāṉakkompē varuka! ampalavar
    kūttiṉ payaṉi uyirkku aruḷum koḻuntē varuka! niṉintu uruki
iṇaṅkātavarukku eppoḻutum eṭṭāy varuka! aṭiyavarukku
    eḷiyāy varuka! uyirkku uyirāy iruntāy varuka! emi āṇṭa
aṇaṅkē varuka! tirumullikku aracē varuka varukavē!
    aruḷpūttu ilaṅku koṭiyiṭip peṇ amutē varuka varukavē ( 61)

7. ampulip paruvam

vaḷamaruvum attimuṉ peṟṟatāl irukōṭu māliyiṭi maruvi eḻalāl
    māṉam uṭkoḷvatāl imiyavari makiḻac cey māṭciyāl paṇiārtalāl
iḷamaruta vātāra mēvalāl vāṉpeṟṟiruntāri aṇikiṉṟatāl
    iṟivaṉa tiṭattirukki yuṟṟatāl puri irāvil carikkiyālum
taḷava muṟuval kayaṟ kaṇṇāḷ uṉakkunikar tāṉām avaṭku nīyum
    cariyātalāl uṉṉi 'vam' eṉak kūviṉāḷ taṇṇaḷiyiṉāl kāṇṭiyāl
aḷakinakarukku nikar mulli pacumpiṇiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē!
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē (62)
pāṭukoṇṭu ilakuvāṉ pālāṟu varutalāl paciya mañcaḷ tarum ataṉāl
    pakkam maṟi uṟṟalāl paṉṉilavu koḷvatāl paritiyāl tamkiyālum
nīṭukāl taṅkac cilampu valam varutalāl nēmi aravam tuṉṉalāl
    niṟikalāniti eṉṟu urippatāl taṉṉiyā ṉeytalukkuvaki
tōṭukoṇṭu āṭum iruvaḷḷiyit tāvic culāy kumiḻ maṟintu marimēl
    tuḷaṅkaluṟu keṇṭiyil piṟaḻunīḷ iruviḻic curikuḻal kottal ēyntā
āṭukoṭi māḷikit tirumulli nāyakiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē!
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē.! (63)
nīrkoṇṭa puṇṭarika malar kāmpuṟac ceyvi nīivaṇ mukattiṅkaḷ tāṉ
    nittamuṟu cattiyarkaḷ uḷḷac carōruka nekiḻntu viriyac ceykumāl
ērkoṇṭa niṉkaṇ orumāṉ uḷiyeṉ ammi kaṇirumāṉ ulāvi varumāl
    īṇṭu nīōr aṇṭa muḻitantu tapaṉaṉil ilaṅkiṭa viḷakkam ceyvāy
pērkoṇṭa akilāṇṭa muṟṟum vaḷarmey aruṭpēroḷi parappalālē
    perumāṭṭiyāṉavaṭku oppunī alliyāl pēraruḷiṉāl viḷikku
mārkoṇṭa tērvīti mulli apirāmiyuṭaṉ ampulī āṭavāvē!
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē.! (64)
orukarum pāmpiṉ vāyuḷpaṭṭu umiḻnta piṉṉucciṭṭa māyalivi nī
    uyirkaḷukku eṉṉammi orukarum pāmpiṉ vāyuṟṟa tuppuravu ceyumāl
varum amarar uṇpāṉ amutam koṭuttē varuntuvi peruntakikku
    māṟāta itaḻamutam eṉṟum koṭuttē makiḻcciyiṉ vaḷarntaruḷumāl
kurumataṟkuk kaviki yāvi yāvarkkumivaḷ kuṟivaṟak kaviki aruḷvāḷ
    kūṟumūr kōḷvaḷiya niṟṟiyūr kōḷ ivaṭkuṟuvatili oppu nīyē
arumaṟi muḻaṅku tirumulli mātaṅki uṭaṉ ampulī āṭavāvē!
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē.! (65)
vempip poṟip puyaṅkattiṉukku añcinī veḷḷi matiyāyiṉi atāl
    virakap peruṅkāma vēṇavāk koṇṭu uḷam viḻuṅku malkuṟa paṇikkāk
kumpap paṭāmalik kuruviṉ kiḻattiyik kūcātu toṭṭa tīmik
    koṭum pātakattiṉāl iṉṉamum tēyntu uṭal kūṉumuṟṟu aliti antō!
cempoṉ ceḻuṅkamala malku cuppiramaṇiya tīrttat tor tivali paṭiṉum
    ciṟitu aḷḷi uṇṇiṉum kayarōka mākiya cimiḻppaṟuttu iṉitu uytiyāl
ampoṟṟaṭam purici cūḻmulli valliyuṭaṉ ampulī āṭavāvē!
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē! (66)
poṟpuṟu muyal kaḷaṅkattiṉāl uṭalam putittuk kiṭantaṉi atu pōkkum oru
    pukaluṉakku uriceyval kēṭṭi: ippuvaṉat tuḷārkaṭ kelā(m )
naṟpayaṉ tarukiṉṟa puṇṇiyap 'pāli' nīḷnatiyil paṭintu orutiṉa(m )
    naṟumullivaṉam maruvi varukiṉṟa kāṟṟiṉiyu naṇṇik kaḷaṅkamakaṟṟi
viṟpuruva eṉ ammi kaṇavaṉip pūcittuṉ mētaku kulattil oruvaṉ
    vīṭṭiṉpa muṟṟatuṅ kēṭṭiruppāy; nīyum vēṇṭiya varaṅkaḷ peṟalā(m)
aṟputat tirumulli alliyam kōtiyuṭaṉ ampulī āṭavāvē!
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē! (67)
veḷḷip pacuṅkuḻavi uṟṟa paruvattaṉṉi miṉṉutal cevvi vavvi
    mēlum īreṇ kali nirampu paruvattivaḷ viḷaṅku vataṉat taḻakelām
kaḷḷak karuttiṉāṉ meḷḷak kavarntu nī kakaṉattil ōṭiviṭṭa
    kāriyattiṉi ivaḷ maṇāḷaṉ tiruccaṭik kāṭṭil orupaṇi kaṇṭatēl
eḷḷip piṭittuk kuṭittuk kuṭitta amutam eḷḷaḷavu pōtiluṉṉi piri
    koḷḷumātalāl uyyavēṇṭuti eṉil, iṉip porukkeṉa eḻuntē
aḷḷal ceḻum paḻaṉa mullip pirāṭṭiyuṭaṉ ampulī āṭavāvē !
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē ! (68)
maṭṭuṇṭu piramaraṅkāmaram pāṭumalar māmaṟik kiḻavaṉīṉṟa
    matikeṭṭa ciṟuviti makaccāli pukka nī vanta cayavīraṉ muṉivāl
taṭṭuṇṭu teḷḷamutu koṭṭuṇṭu muṭṭuṇṭu tāḷiṉāl tēyppuṇṭatum
    caṟṟalla uṉṉi attaṇṭam purintatum tiyalivaḷ taṉiyaṉāmāl
oṭṭuṇṭu kollukikku eṅkaḷ peṇṇaraci tiruuḷḷam kaṭukkilataṉāl
    oyyeṉat tāṉ varuti maṟukelām ciṟuvīṭu uñaṟṟi iḷamātar ciṟucō(ṟu)
aṭṭuṇṭu viḷiyāṭum mulli pacuṅkiḷiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē !
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē ! (69)
maṇṭiya ciṉappoṟi viḻikkaṭi teṟittiṭa vaṭikkaṇi toṭuttu etirttu
    varuperun tuṭṭak kuṟumpaṉik kāntaṉi vaḷittup piṭittuk karuṅ
koṇṭaliṉ uruṭṭip puraṭṭic cavaṭṭi veṅkuṭar kuḻampat tutittuk
    koṉṟu viṟal koṇṭiṭat toṇṭimāṉukkuk kuṟittoru paṭittalivaṉāyp
paṇṭu vantu amar ceyta veṟuḻ valik kaṭunaṭip piṅkaṇēṟṟiṭap pirāṉ
    pakivariṉi yāreṉṟu nī varun tikki vaḻipārttiruppatum aṟitiyāl
aṇṭarum vaṇaṅku tirumullik kaṉaṅkuḻiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē!
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē ! (70)
vāṭ koṇṭa nōkkamuṟu piḷḷi nāyakaṉ muṉṉar vāriyil cuti vāriya
    maliyiṭat tuṉatukali ellām takarntum uyir maṉṉutal uṟappeṟṟatun
tāṭkoṇṭa malaya muṉikaṭal kuṭittiṭṭa nāṭṭaṉi yakaṭupukku mīṇṭu
    taṅkutalum ammi niṉṉitayamuṟi vāliṉit tiyalivaḷ nūpurattāḷ
tūṭkoṇṭu eṟintiṭiṉum oruvar katiyiṉṟiyē cōrvuṟṟu maṭivi ataṉāl
    tuṇṇeṉa virumputaṉi makaṉeṉap peṟṟa nī turicaṟak koṭiyēṉiyum
āṭkoṇṭa tirumulli marakatak kompiṉuṭaṉ ampulī āṭavāvē!
    aṭimuṭi kaṭanta oru koṭiyiṭi iḷampiṭiyoṭu ampulī āṭavāvē! (71)

8. ammāṉip paruvam

cēlip puṟaṅkaṇṭa nāṭṭattiṉ aiṅkarac cempōtakattukku muṉ
    tittikku(m) mōtakak kavaḷamum veḷḷic ceḻuṅkaṉiyum īvateṉavum
mālik kaṭukkiyaṇi kōlac caṭātāri mintarip pārttu "ulakelām
    valam vantavarkku ippalam tarutum" eṉṟu tami vanta tantikku aḷikka
vēlikkuḷ vantu acurarkula muḻutaṟuttiṭṭa veṅkūruṭam piṭikki
    vīrarkku vārattiṉīcuṉit tīṅkaṉikaḷ vēṟu aḷittu aruḷvateṉavu
mālik koḻuñcāṟupāy mulliyūr ammi ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē !
    ammāṉi vittakip pemmāṉi aṇi valli ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē ! (72)
muṭitta ceñcaṭi uṭi paramaṉantaramēṉi muṟṟum ceṟintu eḻil peṟa
    moympiṉil palvaṇa aṭukkut toṭit teriyal muṟi muṟiyil aṇivateṉavu(m)
kaṭittaṭam kamalattiṉ aḻakip paḻitta ceṅkit taḷiriṭattiṉiṉṟum
    kaṉaka aṇṭaṅkaḷ viḷiyāṭṭil camittēvu kāṭciyi nikarppateṉavu(m)
vaṭitta añcaṉamalar kaṇkaḷ imiyātu navamaṇikaḷ iḻitta palavum
    vaḷami peṟum intiravileṉṉa oḷi kulavi eḻiṉ mākattiṉ mītulāva
vaṭittoḻum pāṟṟumavar vāḻ mulliyūr ammi ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē !
    ammāṉi vittakip pemmāṉi aṇi valli ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē! (73)
tiruttamuṟuvitturuma oṇkaṭiki ciṟcila ceṟinteṉat tiru viralkaḷiṉ
    cevvoḷi oḻuṅkutara avvoḷi maṟintiṭat tirumuṟuval nilavu kāṭṭap
perutta maṇi ammaṉi piṭikkum toṟum toṟum peṟṟi vevvēṟu kāṇap
    peṇṇamuta niṉṉuṭiya kaṇṇaḻaku kaṇṭempirāṉum imiyātirukkak
karutturi kaṭantatu akantik kiḻaṅkik kaḷintiṭṭa meyk kāṭciyar tamik
    kātaloṭu niṉṟu vaḻipārttaḻitta kaṉmaṉik kaṇkoṇṭavark kōtaṉa
maruttiyiṭumā celvar vāḻ mulliyūr ammi ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē!
    ammāṉi vittakip pemmāṉi aṇivalli ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē! (74)
maṉṉalaṅ koṇṭa piṅkāṟpaḻukkāyp pavaḷavaṇ kulikkamu kiruḷelā
    māyntiṭak kaṟṟi veṇcōti iḷanilaviṉi vaḻaṅku muttaṅkaḷīṉak
kaṉṉalaṅ kāṭṭiṭi neruṅkuvaḷi ārkkak karuṅkuvaḷi vāytiṟakkak
    kaviṉoḻuku patti pattiyiṉiṉṟu nīṭuṅkatirc cennel talivaṇaṅkap
poṉṉayam kamalap potaṭṭiṉ mēl karuñimiṟu pōṟṟu cevvaḻi payilvuṟap
    puṭināri, imiḻicip pīliyūtac ciṟiya puḷḷaṉṉam vīṟṟirukku(m)
annalaṅkaḻaṉi puṭicūḻ mulliyūr ammi ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē!
    ammāṉi vittakip pemmāṉi aṇivalli ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē ! (75)
muppuṭiya kāykkulit teṉṉam poḻiṟkuḷuṟu muṉimaṭaṟ pāḷi vāḷiṉ
    mutirum parārip palāmuḷ puṟakkaṉi muḻuccuḷi kiḻitteṭuttu
veppuṭiya ūṭalil piriyum maṭamantikaḷ micintiṭak kaṭuvaṉalki
    viḷiyāṭu parimaḷac cōliyūṭu ēki varu veṇṇilāk kaṟṟi matiya
meppuṭiya neṟiyiṉṟi māḷikic cāḷarat teṇṇilā vaḻiyiṉuḻiyū
    viṉittuṭal vaḷintatik kūṉuṟṟu varukiṉṟa teṉṟuki miṉṟuñcolu
mappuṭiya nīṇmaṉit tirumulliyūr ammi ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē!
    ammāṉi vittakip pemmāṉi aṇivalli ammāṉi āṭi aruḷē! (76)
    (vēṟu)
pañcuram ōtiya añciṟi vaṇṭukaḷ tuñciṭu piṅkuḻalāy
    pañcamalak koṭuvēri akaḻntu paṟikkum aruṭkaṭalē
nañcu piliṟṟum eyiṟṟara vintu nakittali ceṉṉiyiṉ mēlnaṇṇa
    muṭittiṭi eṉṉa vulampu nakatti yuritta vataṭ
kañcuka mēṉiyar kaṇmaṇi yākiya kaṉṉimaṭap piṇiyē
    katir vaḷarammaṉi ammaṉi uṉkik kāntaḷ maṇaṅkamaḻa
añcuka meṉmoḻi kiñcuka vāyavaḷ āṭuka ammaṉiyē !
    alakil peruṅkaruṇik koṭiyiṭiyavaḷ āṭuka ammaṉiyē! (77)
vāraṇi kuṅkuma mūḻkiya koṅkiyiṉ maṅkiyarukkaracē
    vaṇṇa valattaka naṇṇiya cempoṉ malarpata viṇmaṭavāy
kāraṇi malkiya tāraṇikiṉṟa karuṅkuḻal aṅkaṉiyē!
    kaṇṇiya aṉparkaḷ iṉṉal akaṟṟupu kāṭci aḷittaruḷvōy!
tēraṇi vīti viḻā oḻiyāta ciṟappu mikutta peruñ
    celvam viḷaṅkiya mulli vaḷampati tikaḻa iruntaruḷum
āraṇi nāraṇi, kāraṇi, pūraṇi, āṭuka ammaṉiyē!
    alakil peruṅkaruṇik koṭiyiṭiyavaḷ āṭuka ammaṉiyē! (78)
villip porunutal vallit tarumuli vēymaru tōḷ maṭavār
    viripaṉi nīril kuṅkuma mikku virāvit tēyvi ceyā
mallal teruviṭi aḷḷi iṟikka malarkkāl vaḻuvāmi
    vaṇṇap paṇimaṇi erioḷi kakkupu vāṉmaṇi naṇpukoḷa
ollip poḻutil ularttiyiṭak kaṇṭoppaṟu maṭavārkaḷ
    uḷḷam iṟumpūtu uṟṟu makiḻcciyiṉ ulaviṭum vaḷamaruvu
mallitaru meḻiṉ mullip pati mayilāṭuka ammaṉiyē!
    alakil peruṅkaruṇik koṭiyiṭiyavaḷ āṭuka ammaṉiyē ! (79)
muttani rittiṭum īrintammaṉi muṟimuṟi niṉṉiru kim
    muḷari aḻuntic cenniṟamāki mukattiṭi villumiḻa
vittaka malkiya niṉkaṇ kuvaḷi virinta nalamkāṇā
    viri aḷakattuṟu toṅkaliṉ vaṇṭu veruṇṭu cuḻaṉṟāṭap
puttamu tattiṉi vitta kaṭamporu puḷakita iḷamuliyāy!
    pōkamaḷikku neṭuṅkaḻi villoṭu pūvala riṅkaṇiya
mattame ṭuttavaḷattaṉiṭattavaḷ āṭuka ammaṉiyē!
    alakil peruṅkaruṇik koṭiyiṭiyavaḷ āṭuka ammaṉiyē! (80)
mūlaviṉit toki aṉpari moyttu muṉin tetir ōṭāmē
    mūṭiya aimpula vēṭarkaḷ aṟivi muṟik koṇṭāṭāmē
cīlamil eṅkaḷi nuṅkupu valliruḷ ciṟṟaravu āṭāmē
    ceppam ilāta maṉappēy pavuri tiraṇṭiṉi tāṭāmē
māluṟu kāma mutaṟpakiyāṉum valintuṭaṉ āṭāmē
    vaṉmiya paracamayak kuṟumpōrkaṇ malintiṭa nāṭāmē
āliya pāli natittuṟi yāṭuvaḷ āṭuka ammaṉiyē!
    alakil peruṅkaruṇik koṭiyiṭiyavaḷ āṭuka ammaṉiyē ! ( 81)

9. nīrāṭal paruvam

taṇṇamar kaṭukkip pacuṅkāyiṉ meṉ kuḻal civalam kāṭṭi arukē
    tamaṉiyak koppeṉak kaṉṉikārattiṉ malarcār vaḷḷi ūṭaṇittup
paṇṇamarumaṇi mūcu kāviyaṅkaṇ kaḷum piṅkumiḻp pōtiṉ mūkkum
    patumāṉa ṉattiṉoṭu cevvīḻi vāyum veṇpaṉi muruntāṉa nakiyum
vaṇṇa naṟkāntaḷiṉ malarkkiyum tumpiyiṉ maruppāntaṭaṅ koṅkiyu(m)
    maruvumāliliya kaṭumaracilik kaṭitaṭamu māṇpuṟak kāṭṭi oḻuki
viṇṇavar viyappa oru peṇṇil varutaṇpāli veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē!
    viṭiyuṭik kaṭavuḷ puṇar koṭiyiṭik kumāri veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē! (82)
vaṇṭirikkuṅ karuṅkuntaḷac cevvāy maṭantiyar kuṭintāṭavē
    vaḷarum iḷamuli veṟpil appikkiṭanta cevvaṇṇac ceḻuṅkuṅkumam
koṇṭirittōṭi orupāl cemmiyum taṉatu kuṇameṉum veṇmi oḷiyiṉ
    kōlam oru pāliṉuṅkāṭṭi parantuvaru koḷkiyāl aṉivarukkum
teṇṭirik kaṭalvanta celvitaṉṟavaḷat tiruppaṭiva marumakaḷoṭum
    cērntu varukāṭciyit tantiru ciṟikkaṇun tiraṇ muttamaḷḷi aḷḷi
veṇṭirik kikkoṇṭu vīci oḻukum pāli veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē!
    viṭiyuṭik kaṭavuḷ puṇar koṭiyiṭik kumāri veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē! (83)
kaṇṇār aṇaṅkiṉ oru kaṟpuṭiya maṅkitaṉ kātalaṉ cēṭcelamikum
    kavaliyuṟṟu ariya kāmattuyariṉ mūḻki naṟ kaviṉuru vaḷartta teṉṉat
taṇṇār cuvētat taṉāturuk kāṭṭit taṭañcikara maruvu nantit taṉipporup
    peṉṉum oru tāy maṉiyiṉiṉṟu moṇṭari eṉuntōḻi tuṇiyā
uṇṇāriṉōṭum karuṅkaṭal tuṇivaṉi ukantu aṇintu iṉpamuṟupāk
    kulappil variyuḷḷaṉa taraṅkak karaṅkaḷāl unti vantanti pukalum
viṇṇār eṉapperuki oḻukum tirippāli veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē!
    viṭiyuṭik kaṭavuḷ puṇar koṭiyiṭik kumāri veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē! (84)
naṭpulavu uḷḷat tiruccēṭi mātaruṭaṉ naṅkaḷkulat teyvamāṉa
    naṅkinī veṇṇīrt taṭantuṟi kuṭintiṭa nayappaṟṟa naṭaliyuṭiyār
kaṭpulam katuvāp pacuṅkatirt tiruuruk kānti pōyppāyntu aṇivatāl
    kāḷinti natiyoṭu kaṅki vantatu eṉum kāṭci kaṇṭu aticayamuṟap
puṭpula varuntap poruntiyiṭu muttalip porumuṉic cūṟpaṭiyuṭip
    puṅkavaṉeṭuñ caṭiyiṉ māṟṟavaḷuruk koṇṭu pōntateṉṟu eḷḷāmalē
viṭpulattamutil parantoḻuki varupāli veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē!
    viṭiyuṭik kaṭavuḷ puṇar koṭiyiṭik kumāri veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē! (85)
maṉṉarkku maṉṉaṉāy innilattu aracurimi maṉṉiyatirut toṇṭimāṉ
    matipparum varampaṟu perumpukaḻ tiraṇṭoru vaḻikkoṇṭu vantateṉavum
kaṉṉal carācaṉak karatalattoruvaṉik kāṉmuḷi eṉappaṭitta
    kamalat taṭaṅkaṇṇar kikkoṇṭa nēmiyāl kāyirākkataṉi mēṉāḷ
piṉṉappaṭak koṉṟa kāruṭaṟ pōrviyāl pērpeṟṟa mētiṉi elām
    peṭpuṟum pālāl viḷakkip peruñcuci piṟaṅkiṭap pōnta teṉavu(m)
miṉṉal pacumpoṉ koḻittuvaru taṇpāli veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē!
    viṭiyuṭik kaṭavuḷpuṇar koṭiyiṭik kumāri veḷḷa nīrāṭi aruḷē! (86)
taṅkum cuvaṇat takaṭṭakaṭṭum taṭañcēr kaṇaṅkaḷ nuṭaṅkeḻuntu
    cārumiraṇṭu puṟattum vaḷar taṇṇam poḻil pūntēṉ kaviḻttu
maṅkul paṭalattu ūṭuruvi matiyiṉ akaṭu kiḻittaṅkaṇ
    vāṉyāṟṟulavi iḷippāṟi vaḷaṅkūr uṭumaṇṭalat taṇintu
naṅkaḷ kulameṉṟu avi taḻuvi nāḷum paḻakum keḻutakimi
    nayam pārāṭṭi mīṇṭu aṇintu nāppaṇ ukittuk kutittu ulavum
poṅkum perumit taṭam pāli putu nīrāṭi aruḷukavē!
    poṟpār mullik koṭiyiṭiyāḷ putu nīrāṭi aruḷukavē! (87)
miyār ciṟivaṇṭu aṭikiṭakkumalark kāvaṉiyaniṉ kuḻaliṉ
    maṇṇippūcumāṉ matamu maṇakkum puḻukuntukilikiyāl
aiyār toyyil poṟitta kuyat taṇinta kaḷapac cantaṉamu
    māṭak karinta aḷaṟṟiṉ uvarāḻik kaṭalum kumukum eṉa
neyyār kulappaṭiyāḷi niṟiyum karuṇip piraḷayatti
    nikaḻāvikaḷukku aḷippateṉa neṭṭāvikaḷiṉ akanirappip
poyyatu oḻukum cuvippālip putu nīrāṭi aruḷukavē!
    poṟpār mullik koṭiyiṭiyāḷ putu nīrāṭi aruḷukavē! (88)
kālappuyal kaṟṟik kūntaṟ katirtta muṭiyiṉ aṭiparantu kaṉattup
    puṭittup paruttu vimmik kaccukku aṭaṅkāk kaṉataṉattār
cālac ceḻuṅkuṅkumam citaṟit taḷir pōl niṟattaṅ kaluḻmēṉi
    tām kaṇṭaṟiyā vaki muḻutum taḻi cēppāy oṟṟumippaṭalāl
vālap pacuṅkōkilam aṉiyār maṉiyōr māmi nōkki mati
    maruḷāmuṉṉam avarōṭum vañcik koṭiyē niṉṉaruḷip
pōlap parantu varumpālip putu nīrāṭi aruḷukavē!
    poṟpār mullik koṭiyiṭiyāḷ putu nīrāṭi aruḷukavē! (89)
    (vēṟu)
mammarp piṇiyuṟu ceṉmattuḻitaruvāri eṭuttaruḷu
    malarpuri pāṇik kuruku kaṟaṅkiṭa varu pāṇikkuruku
mammuṟ ṟiṉamuṭaṉaruku cilampiṭa valar kaṭatintaṇi kū(r )
    aḷakattuṟi vaṇṭuṭaṉ aḷakattuṟi vaṇṭumamarntāṭa
vemmik karicaṟa eṉṉi aḷikku niṉ viḻiyiṇi civavāmē
    veṇṇaki viritaru cempavaḷam poru mellitaḻ veḷiṟāmē
pommaṟ katirmaṇi kuvitaru pālipputu nīrāṭukavē!
    pullak koṭiyuṭi mullik koṭiyiṭi putu nīrāṭukavē! (90)
paṟṟalar cāya varikkaṇi toṭṭa paramporuḷuk kamutē
    pāṟkaṭalūṭoru pacci aṭap peṭi pāyntu kuṭintiṭalpōl
teṟṟeṉa vantaruvippuṉal kaṇṭu ceḻumpiṭi yāṭutalpōl
    tēcuviḷaṅkum iḷaṅkuyil oṉṟu tirippuṉal tōyvatupōl
vaṟṟi aṟāta taṭattoru kalava mayiṟpē ṭāṭutalpōl
    vācaṉi yōcaṉi kamaḻ kaṅkā calamaṇṭu pacuṅkiḷipōl
pōṟṟiraḷ vāri varaṉṟiya pālip putu nīrāṭukavē!
    pullak koṭiyuṭi mullik koṭiyiṭi putu nīrāṭukavē! (91)

10. poṉṉūcal paruvam

cītaḷac cekkark kuruccuṭarp pavaḷat tirukkāl urukkoḷa niṟīit
    teḷḷoḷiya vaccirat tiraḷ viṭṭa maḻakuṟac cērtti vayiṭūriyamuṟuṅ
kōtilāc cempoṉ koḻumpalaki iṭṭuk kuṭinta veṇṭaraḷaṅkaḷāṟ
    kōmaḷak katirvaṭa maṇittoppilāmal kuyiṟṟu maṇiyuñcaṉ miciniṉ
cōtivaḷar nīṉiṟattirumēṉi aḻaku ilaku cuntarac cāyi paṭiyāt
    tukaḷ tīru marakatak kuṉṟiloru tōki mayiṟuyal varuṅkāṭci yuṟaḻap
pūtalattavar paravu mullivaḷar kalyāṇi poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (92)
takaṭṭitaḻp paṅkayac catumukattēvum oru tāmōtarak kaṭavuḷum
    cārvariya taṇṭāc civānanta mayamāṉa tāparap pariyaṅkamēl
pakaṭṭuṭip paramēcaṉāṉa maṇi mañcamēl paramapīṭattiṉaṭuvaṇ
    parampu paripūraṇak karuṇip piḻampāyp patintu iṉitu vīṟṟiruppōy!
mukaṭṭuṭip palakōṭi aṇṭamum palluyiru muṟimuṟi uyartteṭuttu mūvāta
    kaṉṉinī ṇaṉṉākiṉantiṉa muṟukkaviḻ naṟic cantaṉap
pokuṭṭamaru mullit tiruppati purakku mayil poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (93)
nattēyu moṇ kaḷattiṭukiṭi maṭantiyarkaḷ naki nilāmuṉṟilēṟi
    nakumāṭakattit tirutti ici vīṇiyiṉ narampuḷarn tamutoḻukka
muttēy muruntaṉiya mūraliṉu mannalār mukamatik kāntitaṉiṉu
    moyttavav varamiyat taṇṇantu kāntameṉumuḻu maṇikaḷ nīrpilkiṭak
kottēṟu vīmaruvu kuñci oṇ kāḷiyar kuḻukkaḷattaṉmi kāṇūk
    kūṟumavar iciyiyu mukamaṇṭalattiyuṅ koṇṭāṭi mika viyakkum
puttēḷi rumparavu mullipatik karaci poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē ! (94)
teḷḷoḷiya veḷ nittilam cuḻit teṟikiṉṟa teṇ tirik kuṇṭu akaḻicūḻ
    cīrkoṇṭa pūkaṇṭa matiluḷḷa patikaḷil cīkarap pūṅkōyil cē(r)
oḷḷiya viṭaṅkattiṉ eṇmaṭaṅku aṟputattu oppilāc ciṉakaram eṭuttu
    orumuṭi kavittāṇṭa toṇṭimāṉ kaṇṭavarkaḷ uṟutiṭṭi paṭṭiṭāmal
veḷḷiya erukkiṉ irutūṇāṭi nāṭṭupu viḷakkiya tirukkōyilil
    vīṟṟiruntu aruḷpuriyum āṉanta veḷḷamē vēlip paṭappi naṭuvē
puḷḷi maṟituḷḷi vaḷar mullipatikku iṟivi poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (95)
viruppoṭu veṟuppi aṟaviṭṭa aṭiyavar uḷavirit tāmarik kōyilil
    vīṟṟirukkiṉṟa oru tōṟṟamatupōṉ meṉavum meyppōta vaḻiterikkuṉ
tirupoliyum āraṇac ceṉṉi mici maṉṉivaḷar tēciṉippōṉmeṉavu(m) it
    tiruūcal mici iṉitu eḻuntaruḷi eḻutariya ciṟṟiṭip pēralkulā
uruppaciyi mēṉakiyi uḷkiṭac ceyumāmi ōviyap pāviyaṉiyār
    ōvāmal ūṭṭucempañca ṭiyiṉoṭu cilampoliyarāyt tirimarukukuḻ
poruppaṉiya māḷikit tirumulli vaṭivaḻaki poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (96)
cāṟṟariya palkalik kēḷvi āyntu uḷḷa tarukkoṭu tarukkamiṭṭum
    catumaṟi terintu muṟucāttiram uṇarntum oru caṅkaraṟku aṉpu ceyyār
kāṟṟiṉic carukiṉi nukarntu eṇṇil kālaṅkaḷ kaṇmūṭi yōkiruntum
    kāṇṭariya tūṇṭā viḷakkiṉi aruḷkaṇāl kaṇṭu taṇṭāmal viḻinīr
ūṟṟiruntu ōṭac civāṉantamatu uṇṭu uṟaṅkum arumātavariṉum
    uṇmip peruntavac cīruṭiya aṭiyavarkaḷ orumittu iṟiñci nāḷum
pōṟṟunal tirumullivāyil parācatti poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (97)
kaṟpaṉiya veṇṇakic ciṟunutal teyvak kalikkaṭal teḷḷamutamum
    kamaliyum cuntariyum uṇṭāṭṭu makaḷirum kātalil koṇṭāṭavē
ceṟporu koṭikkiyiṉ aruccuṉaṉ aruccaṉi ceyappācupatam aḷittē
    civaparamcuṭar eṅkaḷ mācilāmaṇi varatar cevvitaru mukkaṇ kaḷum
aṟputat tiruviruntu ayarac civāṉanta aḻivilā veḷḷattiṉūṭu aṭiyavar
    tuḷintāṭuṟac carācaramāṉa akilamum acintāṭavē
poṟpuvaḷar mullip patikkuḷ peruñcelvi poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (98)
ūkkamuṟu kūrukirk karuviral perumantiyumpar vāṉaḷavu nākat
    turukēḻ karumpaṇiyiṉ veṇmalar tiraḷ parittu ōṅku mantara nilattil
nīkkamaṟum vaṇṇam corintukaḷavappōtu nilamici oruṅku varutal
    nīṉeṭum ciṉipōya viciyiṉāl kiḻipaṭṭa niṟi katirkkaṟṟi matiyiṉ
tēkkamuṟum amutam carintu oḻukiṉāl eṉac cevvi terivikka varukē
    tēṅkoṉṟi poṉ corital poṉṉāṭu mittalam cērnteṉak kāṭṭa vakuḷam
pūkkaṇ maṇanāṟu naṟṟirumulli varuvalli poṉṉūcal āṭi āruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (99)
kaṉmaruvum eṉṉuṭiya neñcik kuḻittu aruṭkaruṇiyāl eṉṉi, uṉṉik
    kavipāṭum vaṇṇam uṉ amutat tirukkaṭik kaṇ aruḷpurintu āṇṭa pim
puṉmaruvu nuṇtuḷikaḷ ātittaṉik kaṇṭapōtu aṟṟa taṉmi eṉa eṉ
    puṉ kaṇṭuṭit taruḷi aṉṟumutal eṉṉuḷ pukuntu āṭṭiniṉṟa tuṇiyē
teṉmaruvu pimpūṇ ciṟār viṭu maṇippaṭam cēṇ antarattiṉ ulavac
    ceñcāṟu ceyyuṅ koṭippaṭa meṉa tiṉam tēvar toḻa vantaṇavuṟum
poṉ maruvu tirumulli pūtta cuntaravalli poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (100)
ceṅkatirp pirapitaṉi vellu(m) māṇikkac cilampum tiruttaṇṭiyum,
    cillarik kiṇkiṇiyum āṭakap pāṭakac ceyyatiru āparaṇamum
caṅkiyuṟu miṭṭiṭip paṭṭāṭiyum, katir taḻitta tiru oṭṭiyāṇamum
    cantira kalāpamum, cakravākam poru taṉaṅkaḷum, ceṅki malaru(m)
maṅkaḷattiṅkaḷ mukamaṇṭalamum ampavaḷa vāyum vaḷar karuṇi veḷḷam
    vaṟṟāta kaṇṇiṇiyum vaḷḷik kuḻikkātum, vācak karuṅkēcamum
poṅkaḻaku pūtta vaḷar mulli marakata valli poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē!
    poṟcaraṇa paṅkērukak koṭiyiṭik kuri poṉṉūcal āṭi aruḷē! (101)
vaṭatirumullivāyil koṭiyiṭi ammi piḷḷittamiḻ muṟṟum.
tirucciṟṟampalam

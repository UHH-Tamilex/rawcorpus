       Preface.
       A t the present day, when books are multiplying without even a
       shadow of satisfying the well-known Leibnitzian Principle of
       Sufficient Reason for their coming into existence, a few words of
       mine by way of preface will not be out of place heie. My object in
       this little Manual of Tamil Prosody is to give a clear and
       comprehensive explanation of the rules and priociples of Tamil
       Poetic Diction for the use of beginners in the study. The Treatises
       on Tamil Prosody of ancient date, which are the ground works upon
       which all later Tamil poetical productions are based, are in the
       form of sutras, i e., blank or rhyming verses. Manuals of Tamil
       Prosody of recent recent date, written in prose, are either too
       compendious or sadly wanting in right order and classification.
       Hence, the author intended, that, by paying due attention to order
       and classification and to clearness and simplicity of style in
       explanation, be will be enabled to place in the hands of beginners
       in Tamil Prosody what is usually styled by the ordinary role of
       book makers, a long felt wanted real and not imaginary long-felt
       want. The points of improvement which the author modestly claims
       for this little book are among many, chiefly the following : (1) a
       new and intelligible classification of the Tamil Metrical forms,
       (2) a simpler and more accurate explanation of the Principles of
       Tamil versification and (3) an explanation of the most difficult
       form of Tamil Poetic composition vaṇṇam which is for the first time
       to be found in prose in this little book.
       With these words of preface, I leave the book to the judgmeut of
       the learned and the learners, with the hope that it will meet with
       indulgent treatment from my critics. I shall consider myself amply
       repaid for what little trouble I may have taken in the preparation
       of this book, if I find that to any the least extent it proves a
       useful guide in the hands of the students of Tamil Prosody.
       MADRAS,
       15th Feb. 1893.         P. CULLEAUNASUNDROM.

                               1. uṟuppiyal.

                                  ceyyuḷ.

   eḻuttu, acai, cīr, taḷai, ōcai, aṭi, etukai, mōṉaiyeṉṉumuṟup pukkaḷāṟ
   ceyyappaṭuvatu.

                                1.1 eḻuttu

   uyir, kuṟil, neṭil, kurriyalikaram, kuṟṟiyalukaram, aikārakkuṟukkam,
   āytam , valliṉam, melaṉam , iṭayiṉam, uyiramey, uyiraḷapeṭai,
   oṟṟaḷapeṭai yeṉapaṉavām.
   ------

                                  1.2 acai.

   eḻuttāṉatu taṉirōm, iṇaintēṉuriṟpatu. itu nēracai, nirai yacaiyeṉa
   viruvaṉakappaṭum.
   1.2.1 nēracai.
   kuṟṟeḻutatuk caṉittētum oṟṟaṭuttēṉum, neṭṭeḻuttut taṉittēṉum
   oṟṟaṭutcēṉum karuvatu.
   ka = kuṟṟeḻuttu taṉittu vanta rōcai.
   maṉa = ñāyiṟaḻa ka ittuvanta nēracai.
   tā = neṭṭeḻu kaṉat tayār nēracai
   pāl = neṭṭeḻut oṟṟaṭuttuvanta nōcai.
   1.2.2 niraiyacai
   kuṟiliṇaintēṉum ottaṭuttēṉum, kuyiṉeṭi liṇaintēṉum oṟṟa ṭuttēṉum
   varuvatu.
   ari = kuṟilṇaintu vanta niraiyacai
   mām - kuṟiliṇaintu oṟṟaṭuttuvanta niraiyacai
   nilā = kuṟiṉeṭi vīṇaintuvanta niraiyacai
   varāl - kuṟiṉeṭi viṇaintu oṟṟaṭuttu vanta niraiyacai
   ------------------

                                  1.3. cīr

   acaiyuṭaṉ acai cērntu varuvatu. itu iyaṟcīr, veṇcīr , vañcic cīr,
   potuccīr, acaiccīr eṉa vaintuvakaippaṭum.
   1.3.1. iyaṟcīr.
   iraṇṭacaikaḷā vākkappaṭuvatu. itu āciriya variccireṉṟum mutaṟ ōreṉṟum
   collappaṭum.
   vāypāṭu.
   nēr nēr = tēmā
   nirainēr = puḷimā.
   nirainirai karuviḷam
   nērnirai = kūviḷam
   (u-m.) kāṇṭaṟ kariya kaṭavuḷaip pōṟṟiṭa
   tēmā puḷimā karuviḷam kūviḷam
   vēṇṭiya yāvum viḷaiyum
   īṇṭiya valaki vemma nōkkē.
   1.3.2 veṇcīr.
   iyaṟcīr nāṉkiṉukkum ovvoṉṟiṟkuppiṉ kāy eṉṉu nēracai yaikkūṭṭa mūṉṟu
   acaikaḷāki veṇcīr eṉavum, kāyccīreṉavum, iṭaic cīreṉavum paṭum.
   vāypāṭu.
   nēr nēr nēr = tēmāṅkāy
   nirai nērkō = parimāṅkāy.
   nirai nirai nēr = karuviḷaṅkāy.
   nēr nirai nēr = kūviḷaṅkāy,
   (u-m.) kavaiyākik kompākik kāṭṭakattē niṟkum
   puḷimāṅkāy tēmāṅkāy kūṭayiḷaṅkāy tēmā
   avaiyalla nalla maraṅkaḷ - camaiāṭuvē
   puḷimāṅkāy tēmā puḷima karuviḷaṅkāy
   nīṭṭōlai vāciyā nāṉ kuṟippaṟiya
   tēmāṅkāy kūviḷam tēmā karuviḷaṅkāy
   māṭṭā tavaṉē maram.
   tēmā puḷimā malar.
   1.3.3. vañciccīr
   iyaṟcīr nāṉkiṉukkum ovvoṉṟukkuppiṉ kaṉi eṉtu niraiyacai yaikkūṭṭa mūṉṟu
   acaikaḷāka vañciccā, kaṉiccīr, kaṭaiccareṉavum vaḻaṅkum.
   vāypāṭu.
   nēr nēr nirai= tēmāṅkaṉi.
   nirai nēr nirai = puḷimāṅkaṉi
   nirainirainirai = karuviḷaṅkaṉi
   nērnirainirai = kūviḷaṅkaṉi
   (u-m.) pūntāmaraip pōtalamarat
   tēmāṅkaṉi kūviḷaṅkaṉi
   tēmpuṉaliṭai maṉṟiritarum
   kūviḷaṅkaṉi kūviḷaṅkaṉi
   vaḷavayaliṭaik kaḷavayiṉ makiḻ
   karuviḷaṅkaṉi karuviḷaṅkaṉi
   viṉaikkampalai maṉaic cilampavum
   puḷimāṅkaṉi karuviḷaṅkaṉi
   1.3.4. potuccīr
   iyaṟcīr nāṉkiṉum oṉṟoṉṟiṟkuppiṉ taṇpū naṟumpū , taṇṇiḻal naṟuniḻal eṉṟu
   miraṇṭacaikūṭi varuvaṉa.
   vāypāṭu
   tēmāntaṇpū tēmāntaṇṇiḻal,
   tēmā kaṟampū tēmānaṟunital.
   parimān taṇpū puḷimān taṇṇiḻal
   puḷimā naṟumpū puḷimānaṟuniḻal.
   karuviḷantaṇpū karuviḷantaṇṇiḻil,
   karuviḷanaṟumpū karuviḷanaṟuniḻal.
   kūviḷantaṇpū kūviḷantaṇṇiḻal.
   kūviḷanaṟumpū kūviḷanaṟuniḻal.
   (u-m.) innūliṉ ceyyuḷiyalil kāṭṭiyuḷḷa kuṟaḷaṭi vañcippāviṟ kāṇka.
   1.3.5 ōriccaicīr
   nēracaiyum, niraiyacaiyum, taṉittaṉiyē cirāki , nāḷ malar eṉṟu
   vaḻaṅkuvatuṭaṉ, ivaṟṟuṭaṉ kuṟṟiyalukāñcērttu kācu , piṟappu eṉavum
   varum ivai veṇpāviṉ kaṭaiyil vaḻaṅkum.
   (u-m.) īṉṟa poḻutiṟ perituvakkun taṉmakaṉaic
   cāṉṟō ṉeṉakkeṭṭa kāy = nāṉ
   taṉṉūṉ perukkakkut tāṉpiṟitūṉuṇpā
   ṉeṅṅaṉa māḷu pāruḷ. malar
   avicorin tāyiram vēṭṭaliṉoṉṟa
   ṉuyircekut tuṇṇāmai naṉṟu. = kācu.
   ellā viḷakkum viḷakkalla cāṉṟōrkkup
   poyyā viḷakkē viḷakku . = piṟappu.

                                1.4. taḷai.

   niṉṟacīriṉ īṟṟacaiyum, varuñcīriṉ mutalacaiyum vitippaṭi ottirup
   patākap pārppatu taḷaiyām.
   mā + nērnēroṉciriyattaḷai
   viḷam+ nirai niraiyoṉāciriyacṉai -- akavalukkuriyaṉ,
   mā + nirai iyan veṇṭaḷai, veṇpāviṟkuriyaṉa.
   kāy + nēr veṇcīr veṇṭaḷai.
   kāy + niraik kalittaḷai kalippāvukkuriyatu.
   kaṉi + nirai = oṉṟiyāṅcittaḷai
   kaṉi + nō oṉṟu tivañcittaḷai vañcippāviṟkuriyaṉa.

                                 1.5 ocai .

   cīrkaḷiṉpuṇarcciyāṅ tōṉṟit taḷaikaḷā vamainta ceyyuṭkaḷiṉ kuṇamāy
   icaippatu itu ceppalōcai, akavalōcai, tuḷḷalōcai, tūṅkalōcaiyeṉa
   nālvakaiyām.

   1.5.1. ceppalōcai.
   itu ēnticaicceppal, tūṅkicaic ceppal, oḻukicaic ceppaleṉa mūvakaiyām,
   ivai veṇpāviṟ kuriyaṉa.
   ēnticaicceppal
   veṇcīr veṇṭaḷaiyāl muṟṟu muṭiyum veṇpā
   (u-m.) yātāṉuṅṭāmā rāmā leṉṉoruvaṉ
   cāntuṇaiyum kallāta vāṟu.
   tuṅkicaicceppal
   iyaṟcīr veṇṭaḷaiyāl muṟṟu muṭiyum veṇpā
   (u-m.) irumai vakaiterin tiṇṭaṟam pūṇṭār
   perumai piṟaṅkiṟ ṟulaku.
   ōḻukicaic ceppal.
   iyaṟcīr veṇṭaḷaiyum, veṇcīr veṇṭaḷaiyum viravi muṭiyum veṇpā (u-m)
   kollāṉ pulālai maṟuntāṉaik kaikūppi
   yellā vuyirun toḻum.
   -----
   1.5.2 akavalōcai.
   itu ēnticaiyakaval, tūṅkicaiyakaval , oḻukicaiyakavaleṉa mūvakai yām.
   ivai akavalukkuriyaṉa.
   ēnticaiyakaval
   nēroṉṟāciriyattaḷaiyāl muṟṟu muṭinta akaval.
   (u - m.) pōtu cāntam poppā vēnti
   yāti nātaṟ cērvōr
   cōti vāṉan tuṉṉu vōrē.
   tāṅkicaiyakaval.
   niraiyoṉṟāciriyattaḷaiyāl muṟṟa muṭinta akaval.
   (u-m.) aṇiniḻa lacōkāk karuṇeṟi naṭāttiya
   maṇitika viroḷi varāṉaip
   paṇiyavar pavāṉi paricaṟupavarē
   ōkicaiyakaval
   nēroṉ ṟāciriyattaḷaiyum niraipoṉmuriyātavaiyum viravi muṭiyum akaval.
   (u-m.) tāṉē mutti karutavaṉ aṉaiva
   ṉaṭiyiṉ vān āraṇaik
   kaṭiviṉ maṉattāṟ kaṭṭaval kārkcē.
   1.5.3. tuḷḷalōcai.
   itu ēnticaittuḷḷal, pirinticait tuḷḷal, akavaṟṟuḷḷal eṉa mūvakaiyām.

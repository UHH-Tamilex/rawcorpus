tolkāppiyam -ciṟappuppāyiram

vaṭa vēṅkaṭam teṉ kumari
āyiṭit
tamiḻ kūṟum nal ulakattu
vaḻakkum ceyyuḷum āyiru mutaliṉ
eḻuttum collum poruḷum nāṭic 5
centamiḻ iyaṟki civaṇiya nilattoṭu
muntu nūl kaṇṭu muṟippaṭa eṇṇip
pulam tokuttōṉē pōkku aṟu paṉuval
nilam taru tiruviṉ pāṇṭiyaṉ aviyattu
aṟam kari nāviṉ nāṉmaṟi muṟṟiya 10
ataṅkōṭṭu ācāṟku aril tapat terintu
mayaṅkā marapiṉ eḻuttu muṟi kāṭṭi
malku nīr varippiṉ aintiram niṟinta
tolkāppiyaṉ eṉat taṉ peyar tōṟṟip
pal pukaḻ niṟutta paṭimiyōṉē. 15

mutal pākam - eḻuttatikāram

1.1. nūl marapu
eḻuttu eṉappaṭupa
akaram mutal
ṉakara iṟuvāy muppaḵtu eṉpa
cārntu varal marapiṉ mūṉṟu alaṅkaṭiyē. 1
avitām,
kuṟṟiyalikaram kuṟṟiyalukaram
āytam eṉṟa
muppāṟpuḷḷiyum eḻuttu ōraṉṉa. 2
avaṟṟuḷ,
a i u
e o eṉṉum ap pāl aintum
ōr aḷapu icikkum kuṟṟeḻuttu eṉpa. 3
ā ī ū ē ai
ō au eṉṉum ap pāl ēḻum
īr aḷapu icikkum neṭṭeḻuttu eṉpa. 4
mū aḷapu icittal ōr eḻuttu iṉṟē. 5
nīṭṭam vēṇṭiṉ av aḷapuṭiya
kūṭṭi eḻūutal eṉmaṉār pulavar. 6
kaṇ imi noṭi eṉa avvē māttiri
nuṇṇitiṉ uṇarntōr kaṇṭa āṟē. 7
aukāra iṟuvāyp
paṉṉīr eḻuttum uyir eṉa moḻipa. 8
ṉakāra iṟuvāyp
patiṉeṇ eḻuttum mey eṉa moḻipa. 9
meyyoṭu iyiyiṉum uyir iyal tiriyā. 10
meyyiṉ aḷapē ari eṉa moḻipa. 11
av iyal niliyum ēṉi mūṉṟē. 12
ari aḷapu kuṟukal makaram uṭittē
iciyiṭaṉ arukum teriyum kāli. 13
uṭ peṟu puḷḷi uru ākummē. 14
meyyiṉ iyaṟki puḷḷiyoṭu niliyal. 15
ekara okarattu iyaṟkiyum aṟṟē. 16
puḷḷi illā ellā meyyum
uru uru āki akaramoṭu uyirttalum
ēṉi uyiroṭu uruvu tirintu uyirttalum
āyīr iyala uyirttal āṟē. 17
meyyiṉ vaḻiyatu uyir tōṉṟu niliyē. 18
valleḻuttu eṉpa ka ca ṭa ta pa ṟa. 19
melleḻuttu eṉpa ṅa ña ṇa na ma ṉa. 20
iṭiyeḻuttu eṉpa ya ra la va ḻa ḷa. 21
am mū āṟum vaḻaṅku iyal maruṅkiṉ
meymmayakku uṭaṉili teriyum kāli. 22
ṭa ṟa la ḷa eṉṉum puḷḷi muṉṉar
ka ca pa eṉṉum mū eḻuttu uriya. 23
avaṟṟuḷ,
la ḷaḵkāṉ muṉṉar ya vavum tōṉṟum. 24
ṅa ña ṇa na ma ṉa eṉum puḷḷi muṉṉar
tamtam icikaḷ ottaṉa niliyē. 25
avaṟṟuḷ,
ṇa ṉaḵkāṉ muṉṉar
ka ca ña pa ma ya va ēḻum uriya. 26
ña na ma va eṉṉum puḷḷi muṉṉar
yaḵkāṉ niṟṟal mey peṟṟaṉṟē. 27
maḵkāṉ puḷḷi muṉ vavvum tōṉṟum. 28
ya ra ḻa eṉṉum puḷḷi muṉṉar
mutal āku eḻuttu ṅakaramoṭu tōṉṟum. 29
meyn nili cuṭṭiṉ ellā eḻuttum
tam muṉ tām varūum ra ḻa alaṅkaṭiyē. 30
a i u am mūṉṟum cuṭṭu. 31
ā ē ō am mūṉṟum viṉā. 32
aḷapu iṟantu uyirttalum oṟṟu ici nīṭalum
uḷa eṉa moḻipa iciyoṭu civaṇiya
narampiṉ maṟiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 33
----------------
1.2. moḻi marapu
kuṟṟiyalikaram niṟṟal vēṇṭum
yā eṉ ciṉimici uriyacik kiḷavikku
āvayiṉ varūum makaram ūrntē. 1
puṇariyal niliyiṭik kuṟukalum urittē
uṇarak kūṟiṉ muṉṉart tōṉṟum. 2
neṭṭeḻuttu imparum toṭarmoḻi īṟṟum
kuṟṟiyalukaram val āṟu ūrntē. 3
iṭippaṭiṉ kuṟukum iṭaṉumār uṇṭē
kaṭappāṭu aṟinta puṇariyalāṉa. 4
kuṟiyataṉ muṉṉar āytap puḷḷi
uyiroṭu puṇarnta val āṟaṉ micittē. 5
īṟu iyal maruṅkiṉum icimi tōṉṟum. 6
uruviṉum iciyiṉum arukit tōṉṟum
moḻik kuṟippu ellām eḻuttiṉ iyalā
āytam aḵkāk kāliyāṉa. 7
kuṉṟu ici moḻivayiṉ niṉṟu ici niṟikkum
neṭṭeḻuttu impar otta kuṟṟeḻuttē. 8
ai au eṉṉum āyīr eḻuttiṟku
ikara ukaram ici niṟivu ākum. 9
neṭṭeḻuttu ēḻē ōr eḻuttu orumoḻi. 10
kuṟṟeḻuttu aintum moḻi niṟipu ilavē. 11
ōr eḻuttu orumoḻi īr eḻuttu orumoḻi
iraṇṭu iṟantu icikkum toṭarmoḻi uḷappaṭa
mūṉṟē moḻi nili tōṉṟiya neṟiyē. 12
meyyiṉ iyakkam akaramoṭu civaṇum. 13
tam iyal kiḷappiṉ ellā eḻuttum
meyn nili mayakkam māṉam illi. 14
ya ra ḻa eṉṉum mūṉṟum muṉ oṟṟa
ka ca ta pa ṅa ña na ma īr oṟṟu ākum. 15
avaṟṟuḷ,
rakāra ḻakāram kuṟṟoṟṟu ākā. 16
kuṟumiyum neṭumiyum aḷaviṉ kōṭaliṉ
toṭarmoḻi ellām neṭṭeḻuttu iyala. 17
ceyyuḷ iṟutip pōlum moḻivayiṉ
ṉakāra makāram īr oṟṟu ākum. 18
ṉakāri muṉṉar makāram kuṟukum. 19
moḻippaṭuttu icippiṉum terintu vēṟu icippiṉum
eḻuttu iyal tiriyā eṉmaṉār pulavar. 20
akara ikaram aikāram ākum. 21
akara ukaram aukāram ākum. 22
akarattu impar yakarap puḷḷiyum
ai eṉ neṭuñ ciṉi mey peṟat tōṉṟum. 23
ōr aḷapu ākum iṭaṉumār uṇṭē
tērum kāli moḻivayiṉāṉa. 24
ikara yakaram iṟuti viravum. 25
paṉṉīr uyirum moḻi mutal ākum. 26
uyir mey allaṉa moḻi mutal ākā. 27
ka ta na pa ma eṉum āvintu eḻuttum
ellā uyiroṭum cellumār mutalē. 28
cakarak kiḷaviyum avaṟṟu ōraṟṟē
a ai au eṉum mūṉṟu alaṅkaṭiyē. 29
u ū o ō eṉṉum nāṉku uyir
va eṉ eḻuttoṭu varutal illi. 30
ā e o eṉum mū uyir ñakārattu uriya. 31
āvoṭu allatu yakaram mutalātu. 32
mutalā ēṉa tam peyar mutalum. 33
kuṟṟiyalukaram muṟippeyar maruṅkiṉ
oṟṟiya nakaramici nakaramoṭu mutalum. 34
muṟṟiyalukaramoṭu poruḷ vēṟupaṭāatu
ap peyar maruṅkiṉ niliyiyalāṉa. 35
uyir au eñciya iṟuti ākum. 36
ka vavoṭu iyiyiṉ auvum ākum. 37
e eṉa varum uyir mey īṟākātu. 38
ovvum aṟṟē na alaṅkaṭiyē. 39
ē o eṉum uyir ñakārattu illi. 40
u ūkāram na vavoṭu navilā. 41
uc cakāram iru moḻikku urittē. 42
up pakāram oṉṟu eṉa moḻipa
iru vayiṉ niliyum poruṭṭu ākummē. 43
eñciya ellām eñcutal ilavē. 44
ña ṇa na ma ṉa ya ra la va ḻa ḷa eṉṉum
ap patiṉoṉṟē puḷḷi iṟuti. 45
uc cakāramoṭu nakāram civaṇum. 46
up pakāramoṭu ñakāriyum aṟṟē
ap poruḷ iraṭṭātu ivaṇiyāṉa. 47
vakarak kiḷavi nāṉ moḻi īṟṟatu. 48
makarat toṭarmoḻi mayaṅkutal varinta
ṉakarat toṭarmoḻi oṉpaḵtu eṉpa
pukar aṟak kiḷanta aḵṟiṇi mēṉa. 49

1.3. piṟappiyal
unti mutalā muntu vaḷi tōṉṟi
taliyiṉum miṭaṟṟiṉum neñciṉum nilii
pallum itaḻum nāvum mūkkum
aṇṇamum uḷappaṭa eṇ muṟi niliyāṉ
uṟuppu uṟṟu amiya neṟippaṭa nāṭi
ellā eḻuttum collum kāli
piṟappiṉ ākkam vēṟu vēṟu iyala
tiṟappaṭat teriyum kāṭciyāṉa. 1
av vaḻi,
paṉṉīr uyirum tam nili tiriyā
miṭaṟṟup piṟanta vaḷiyiṉ icikkum. 2
avaṟṟuḷ,
a ā āyiraṇṭu aṅkāntu iyalum. 3
i ī e ē ai eṉa icikkum
ap pāl aintum avaṟṟu ōraṉṉa
avitām,
aṇpal mutal nā viḷimpu uṟal uṭiya. 4
u ū o ō au eṉa icikkum
ap pāl aintum itaḻ kuvintu iyalum. 5
tamtam tiripē ciṟiya eṉpa. 6
kakāra ṅakāram mutal nā aṇṇam. 7
cakāra ñakāram iṭi nā aṇṇam. 8
ṭakāra ṇakāram nuṉi nā aṇṇam. 9
av āṟu eḻuttum mū vakip piṟappiṉa. 10
aṇṇam naṇṇiya pal mutal maruṅkil
nā nuṉi parantu mey uṟa oṟṟa
tām iṉitu piṟakkum takāra nakāram. 11
aṇari nuṉi nā aṇṇam oṟṟa
ṟaḵkāṉ ṉaḵkāṉ āyiraṇṭum piṟakkum. 12
nuṉi nā aṇari aṇṇam varuṭa
rakāra ḻakāram āyiraṇṭum piṟakkum. 13
nā viḷimpu vīṅki aṇpal mutal uṟa
āvayiṉ aṇṇam oṟṟavum varuṭavum
lakāra ḷakāram āyiraṇṭum piṟakkum. 14
itaḻ iyintu piṟakkum pakāra makāram. 15
pal itaḻ iyiya vakāram piṟakkum. 16
aṇṇam cērnta miṭaṟṟu eḻu vaḷi ici
kaṇṇuṟṟu aṭiya yakāram piṟakkum. 17
melleḻuttu āṟum piṟappiṉ ākkam
colliya paḷḷi niliyiṉa āyiṉum
mūkkiṉ vaḷi ici yāppuṟat tōṉṟum. 18
cārntu variṉ allatu tamakku iyalpu ila eṉat
tērntu veḷippaṭutta ēṉi mūṉṟum
tamtam cārpiṉ piṟappoṭu civaṇi
otta kāṭciyiṉ tam iyalpu iyalum. 19
ellā eḻuttum veḷippaṭak kiḷantu
colliya paḷḷi eḻutaru vaḷiyiṉ
piṟappoṭu viṭuvaḻi uṟaḻcci vārattu
akattu eḻu vaḷi ici aril tapa nāṭi
aḷapiṉ kōṭal antaṇar maṟittē. 20
aḵtu ivaṇ nuvalātu eḻuntu puṟattu icikkum
mey teri vaḷi ici aḷapu nuvaṉṟiciṉē. 21
------------
1.4. puṇariyal
mūṉṟu tali iṭṭa muppatiṟṟu eḻuttiṉ
iraṇṭu tali iṭṭa mutal āku irupaḵtu
aṟu nāṉku īṟoṭu neṟi niṉṟu iyalum
ellā moḻikkum iṟutiyum mutalum
meyyē uyir eṉṟu āyīr iyala. 1
avaṟṟuḷ,
mey īṟu ellām puḷḷiyoṭu-niliyal. 2
kuṟṟiyalukaramum aṟṟu eṉa moḻipa. 3
uyirmey īṟum uyir īṟṟu iyaṟṟē. 4
uyir iṟu col muṉ uyir varu vaḻiyum
uyir iṟu col muṉ mey varu vaḻiyum
mey iṟu col muṉ uyir varu vaḻiyum
mey iṟu col muṉ mey varu vaḻiyum eṉṟu
iv eṉa aṟiyak kiḷakkum kāli
niṟutta collē kuṟittu varu kiḷavi eṉṟu
āyīr iyala puṇar nilic cuṭṭē. 5
avaṟṟuḷ,
niṟutta colliṉ īṟu āku eḻuttoṭu
kuṟittu varu kiḷavi mutal eḻuttu iyiya
peyaroṭu peyarip puṇarkkuṅ kālum
peyaroṭu toḻilip puṇarkkuṅ kālum
toḻiloṭu peyarip puṇarkkuṅ kālum
toḻiloṭu toḻilip puṇarkkuṅ kālum
mūṉṟē tiripu iṭaṉ oṉṟē iyalpu eṉa
āṅku an nāṉkē moḻi puṇar iyalpē. 6
avitām,
mey piṟitu ātal mikutal kuṉṟal eṉṟu
iv eṉa moḻipa tiriyum āṟē. 7
niṟutta collum kuṟittu varu kiḷaviyum
aṭiyoṭu tōṉṟiṉum puṇar nilikku uriya. 8
maruviṉ tokuti mayaṅkiyal moḻiyum
uriyavi uḷavē puṇar nilic cuṭṭē. 9
vēṟṟumi kuṟitta puṇarmoḻi niliyum
vēṟṟumi alvaḻip puṇarmoḻi niliyum
eḻuttē cāriyi āyiru paṇpiṉ
oḻukkal valiya puṇarum kāli. 10
ai oṭu ku iṉ atu kaṇ eṉṉum
av āṟu eṉpa vēṟṟumi urupē. 11
valleḻuttu mutaliya vēṟṟumi urupiṟku
olvaḻi oṟṟu iṭi mikutal vēṇṭum. 12
āṟaṉ urupiṉ akarak kiḷavi
īṟu āku akara muṉik keṭutal vēṇṭum. 13
vēṟṟumi vaḻiya peyar puṇar niliyē. 14
uyartiṇip peyarē aḵṟiṇip peyar eṉṟu
āyiraṇṭu eṉpa peyar nilic cuṭṭē. 15
avaṟṟu vaḻi maruṅkiṉ cāriyi varumē. 16
avitām,
iṉṉē vaṟṟē attē ammē
oṉṉē āṉē akkē ikkē
aṉ eṉ kiḷavi uḷappaṭa piṟavum
aṉṉa eṉpa cāriyi moḻiyē. 17
avaṟṟuḷ,
iṉṉiṉ ikaram āviṉ iṟuti
muṉṉark keṭutal urittum ākum. 18
aḷapu āku moḻi mutal niliiya uyirmici
ṉaḵkāṉ ṟaḵkāṉ ākiya nilittē. 19
vaḵkāṉ mey keṭa cuṭṭu mutal aim muṉ
aḵkāṉ niṟṟal ākiya paṇpē. 20
ṉaḵkāṉ ṟaḵkāṉ nāṉkaṉ urupiṟku. 21
āṉiṉ ṉakaramum ataṉ ōraṟṟē
nāḷ muṉ varūum val mutal toḻiṟkē. 22
attiṉ akaram akara muṉi illi. 23
ikkiṉ ikaram ikara muṉi aṟṟē. 24
aiyiṉ muṉṉarum av iyal niliyum. 25
ep peyar muṉṉarum valleḻuttu varu vaḻi
akkiṉ iṟuti meym miciyoṭum keṭumē
kuṟṟiyalukaram muṟṟat tōṉṟātu. 26
ammiṉ iṟuti ka ca tak kāli
taṉ mey tirintu ṅa ña na ākum. 27
meṉmiyum iṭimiyum varūum kāli
iṉmi vēṇṭum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 28
iṉ eṉa varūum vēṟṟumi urupiṟku
iṉ eṉ cāriyi iṉmi vēṇṭum. 29
peyarum toḻilum pirintu oruṅku icippa
vēṟṟumi urupu nilipeṟu vaḻiyum
tōṟṟam vēṇṭāt tokutikkaṇṇum
oṭṭutaṟku oḻukiya vaḻakkoṭu civaṇi
coṟ citar maruṅkiṉ vaḻi vantu viḷaṅkātu
iṭi niṉṟu iyalum cāriyi iyaṟki
uṭimiyum iṉmiyum oṭuvayiṉ okkum. 30
attē vaṟṟē āyiru moḻimēl
oṟṟu mey keṭutal teṟṟeṉṟaṟṟē
avaṟṟu muṉ varūum valleḻuttu mikumē. 31
kāramum karamum kāṉoṭu civaṇi
nērat tōṉṟum eḻuttiṉ cāriyi. 32
avaṟṟuḷ,
karamum kāṉum neṭṭeḻuttu ilavē. 33
varaṉmuṟi mūṉṟum kuṟṟeḻuttu uṭiya. 34
aikāra aukāram kāṉoṭum tōṉṟum. 35
puḷḷi īṟṟu muṉ uyir taṉittu iyalātu
meyyoṭum civaṇum av iyal keṭuttē. 36
mey uyir nīṅkiṉ taṉ uru ākum. 37
ellā moḻikkum uyir varu vaḻiyē
uṭampaṭumeyyiṉ urupu koḷal variyār. 38
eḻuttu ōraṉṉa poruḷ teri puṇarcci
iciyiṉ tirital niliiya paṇpē. 39
avitām,
muṉṉap poruḷa puṇarccivāyiṉ
iṉṉa eṉṉum eḻuttuk kaṭaṉ ilavē. 40

1.5. tokimarapu
ka ca ta pa mutaliya moḻimēl tōṉṟum
melleḻuttu iyaṟki colliya muṟiyāṉ
ṅa ña na ma eṉṉum oṟṟu ākummē
aṉṉa marapiṉ moḻivayiṉāṉa. 1
ña na ma ya va eṉum mutal āku moḻiyum
uyir mutal ākiya moḻiyum uḷappaṭa
aṉṟi aṉittum ellā vaḻiyum
niṉṟa col muṉ iyalpu ākummē. 2
avaṟṟuḷ,
melleḻuttu iyaṟki uṟaḻiṉum variyār
colliya toṭarmoḻi iṟutiyāṉa. 3
ṇa ṉa eṉ puḷḷi muṉ yāvum ñāvum
viṉi ōraṉiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 4
moḻi mutal ākum ellā eḻuttum
varu vaḻi niṉṟa āyiru puḷḷiyum
vēṟṟumi al vaḻit tiripu iṭaṉ ilavē. 5
vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum valleḻuttu al vaḻi
mēṟ kūṟu iyaṟki āvayiṉāṉa. 6
la ṉa eṉa varūum puḷḷi muṉṉar
ta na eṉa variṉ ṟa ṉa ākummē. 7
ṇa ḷa eṉ puḷḷi muṉ ṭa ṇa eṉat tōṉṟum. 8
uyir īṟu ākiya muṉṉilik kiḷaviyum
puḷḷi iṟuti muṉṉilik kiḷaviyum
iyalpu ākunavum uṟaḻpu ākunavum eṉṟu
āyīr iyala valleḻuttu variṉē. 9
au eṉa varūum uyir iṟu collum
ña na ma va eṉṉum puḷḷi iṟutiyum
kuṟṟiyalukarattu iṟutiyum uḷappaṭa
muṟṟat tōṉṟā muṉṉili moḻikkē. 10
uyir īṟu ākiya uyartiṇip peyarum
puḷḷi iṟuti uyartiṇip peyarum
ellā vaḻiyum iyalpu eṉa moḻipa. 11
avaṟṟuḷ,
ikara īṟṟup peyar tiripu iṭaṉ uṭittē. 12
aḵṟiṇi viravuppeyar iyalpumār uḷavē. 13
puḷḷi iṟutiyum uyir iṟu kiḷaviyum
valleḻuttu mikuti colliya muṟiyāṉ
tammiṉ ākiya toḻiṟcol muṉ variṉ
meymmi ākalum uṟaḻat tōṉṟalum
am muṟi iraṇṭum uriyavi uḷavē
vēṟṟumi maruṅkiṉ pōṟṟal vēṇṭum. 14
melleḻuttu miku vaḻi valippoṭu tōṉṟalum
valleḻuttu miku vaḻi melippoṭu tōṉṟalum
iyaṟki maruṅkiṉ mikaṟki tōṉṟalum
uyir mika varu vaḻi uyir keṭa varutalum
cāriyi uḷ vaḻic cāriyi keṭutalum
cāriyi uḷ vaḻit taṉ urupu niliyalum
cāriyi iyaṟki uṟaḻat tōṉṟalum
uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ oḻiyātu varutalum
aḵṟiṇi viravuppeyarkku av iyal niliyalum
mey piṟitu āku iṭattu iyaṟki ātalum
aṉṉa piṟavum taṉ iyal maruṅkiṉ
mey peṟak kiḷantu poruḷ varintu icikkum
aikāra vēṟṟumit tiripu eṉa moḻipa. 15
vēṟṟumi alvaḻi i ai eṉṉum
īṟṟup peyark kiḷavi mū vaki niliya
avitām,
iyalpu ākunavum valleḻuttu mikunavum
uṟaḻ ākunavum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 16
cuṭṭu mutal ākiya ikara iṟutiyum
ekara mutal viṉāviṉ ikara iṟutiyum
cuṭṭuc ciṉi nīṭiya ai eṉ iṟutiyum
yā eṉ viṉāviṉ ai eṉ iṟutiyum
valleḻuttu mikunavum uṟaḻ ākunavum
colliya maruṅkiṉ uḷa eṉa moḻipa. 17
neṭiyataṉ muṉṉar oṟṟu mey keṭutalum
kuṟiyataṉ muṉṉart taṉ urupu iraṭṭalum
aṟiyat tōṉṟiya neṟi iyal eṉpa. 18
āṟaṉ urupiṉum nāṉkaṉ urupiṉum
kūṟiya kuṟṟoṟṟu iraṭṭal illi
īṟu āku puḷḷi akaramoṭu niliyum
neṭu mutal kuṟukum moḻi muṉ āṉa. 19
num eṉ iṟutiyum an nili tiriyātu. 20
ukaramoṭu puṇarum puḷḷi iṟuti
yakaramum uyirum varu vaḻi iyaṟki. 21
uyirum puḷḷiyum iṟuti āki
aḷavum niṟiyum eṇṇum cuṭṭi
uḷa eṉappaṭṭa ellāc collum
tamtam kiḷavi tam akappaṭṭa
mutti varūum kālam tōṉṟiṉ
ottatu eṉpa ē eṉ cāriyi. 22
ari eṉa varūum pāl vari kiḷavikku
purivatu aṉṟāl cāriyi iyaṟki. 23
kuṟi eṉ kiḷavi muṉ varu kāli
niṟiyat tōṉṟum vēṟṟumi iyaṟki. 24
kuṟṟiyalukarakku iṉṉē cāriyi. 25
attu iṭi varūum kalam eṉ aḷavē. 26
paṉi eṉ aḷavum kā eṉ niṟiyum
niṉiyum kāli iṉṉoṭu civaṇum. 27
aḷaviṟkum niṟiyiṟkum moḻi mutal āki
uḷa eṉappaṭṭa oṉpatiṟṟu eḻuttē
avitām,
ka ca ta pa eṉṟā na ma va eṉṟā
akara ukaramoṭu avi eṉa moḻipa. 28
īṟu iyal maruṅkiṉ ivi ivaṟṟu iyalpu eṉak
kūṟiya kiḷavip pal āṟu ellām
meyt talippaṭṭa vaḻakkoṭu civaṇi
ottavi uriya puṇarmoḻi niliyē. 29
palar aṟi col muṉ yāvar eṉṉum
peyariṭi vakaram keṭutalum ēṉi
oṉṟu aṟi col muṉ yātu eṉ viṉā iṭi
oṉṟiya vakaram varutalum iraṇṭum
maruviṉ pāttiyiṉ tiriyumaṉ payiṉṟē. 30

1.6. urupiyal
a ā u ū ē au eṉṉum
ap pāl āṟaṉ nilimoḻi muṉṉar
vēṟṟumi urupiṟku iṉṉē cāriyi. 1
pallavi nutaliya akara iṟu peyar
vaṟṟoṭu civaṇal eccam iṉṟē. 2
yā eṉ viṉāvum āyiyal tiriyātu. 3
cuṭṭu mutal ukaram aṉṉoṭu civaṇi
oṭṭiya mey oḻittu ukaram keṭumē. 4
cuṭṭu mutal ākiya ai eṉ iṟuti
vaṟṟoṭu civaṇi niṟṟalum urittē. 5
yā eṉ viṉāviṉ ai eṉ iṟutiyum
āyiyal tiriyātu eṉmaṉār pulavar
āvayiṉ vakaram aiyoṭum keṭumē. 6
nī eṉ oru peyar neṭu mutal kuṟukum
āvayiṉ ṉakaram oṟṟu ākummē. 7
ōkāra iṟutikku oṉṉē cāriyi. 8
a ā eṉṉum marappeyark kiḷavikku
attoṭum civaṇum ēḻaṉ urupē. 9
ña na eṉ puḷḷikku iṉṉē cāriyi. 10
cuṭṭu mutal vakaram aiyum meyyum
keṭṭa iṟuti iyal tiripu iṉṟē. 11
ēṉi vakaram iṉṉoṭu civaṇum. 12
maḵkāṉ puḷḷi muṉ attē cāriyi. 13
iṉ iṭi varūum moḻiyumār uḷavē. 14
nūm eṉ iṟuti iyaṟki ākum. 15
tām nām eṉṉum makara iṟutiyum
yām eṉ iṟutiyum ataṉ ōraṉṉa
ā e ākum yām eṉ iṟuti
āvayiṉ yakara mey keṭutal vēṇṭum
ēṉi iraṇṭum neṭu mutal kuṟukum. 16
ellām eṉṉum iṟuti muṉṉar
vaṟṟu eṉ cāriyi muṟṟat tōṉṟum
ummi niliyum iṟutiyāṉa. 17
uyartiṇi āyiṉ nam iṭi varumē. 18
ellārum eṉṉum paṭarkki iṟutiyum
ellīrum eṉṉum muṉṉili iṟutiyum
oṟṟum ukaramum keṭum eṉa moḻipa
niṟṟal vēṇṭum rakarap puḷḷi
ummi niliyum iṟutiyāṉa
tam iṭi varūum paṭarkki mēṉa
num iṭi varūum muṉṉili moḻikkē. 19
tāṉ yāṉ eṉṉum āyīr iṟutiyum
mēl mup peyaroṭum vēṟupāṭu ilavē. 20
aḻaṉē puḻaṉē āyiru moḻikkum
attum iṉṉum uṟaḻat tōṉṟal
ottatu eṉpa uṇarumōrē. 21
aṉ eṉ cāriyi ēḻaṉ iṟuti
muṉṉart tōṉṟum iyaṟkittu eṉpa. 22
kuṟṟiyalukarattu iṟuti muṉṉar
muṟṟat tōṉṟum iṉ eṉ cāriyi. 23
neṭṭeḻuttu impar oṟṟu mikat tōṉṟum
ap pāl moḻikaḷ al vaḻiyāṉa. 24
avitām,
iyaṟkiya ākum ceyaṟkiya eṉpa. 25
eṇṇiṉ iṟuti aṉṉoṭu civaṇum. 26
oṉṟu mutal āka pattu ūrntu varūum
ellā eṇṇum collum kāli
āṉ iṭi variṉum māṉam illi
aḵtu eṉ kiḷavi āvayiṉ keṭumē
uytal vēṇṭum paḵkāṉ meyyē. 27
yātu eṉ iṟutiyum cuṭṭu mutal ākiya
āyta iṟutiyum aṉṉoṭu civaṇum
āytam keṭutal āvayiṉāṉa. 28
ēḻaṉ urupiṟkut ticip peyar muṉṉar
cāriyik kiḷavi iyaṟkiyum ākum
āvayiṉ iṟuti meyyoṭum keṭumē. 29
puḷḷi iṟutiyum uyir iṟu kiḷaviyum
colliya alla ēṉiya ellām
tērum kāli urupoṭu civaṇi
cāriyi niliyum kaṭappāṭu ilavē. 30

1.7. uyirmayaṅkiyal
akara iṟutip peyar nili muṉṉar
vēṟṟumi al vaḻi ka ca ta pa tōṉṟiṉ
tamtam otta oṟṟu iṭi mikumē. 1
viṉiyeñcukiḷaviyum uvamak kiḷaviyum
eṉa eṉ eccamum cuṭṭiṉ iṟutiyum
āṅka eṉṉum uriyacik kiḷaviyum
ñāṅkark kiḷanta valleḻuttu mikumē. 2
cuṭṭiṉ muṉṉar ña na ma tōṉṟiṉ
oṭṭiya oṟṟu iṭi mikutal vēṇṭum. 3
ya va muṉ variṉē vakaram oṟṟum. 4
uyir muṉ variṉum āyiyal tiriyātu. 5
nīṭa varutal ceyyuḷuḷ urittē. 6
cāva eṉṉum ceya eṉ eccattu
iṟuti vakaram keṭutalum urittē. 7
aṉṉa eṉṉum uvamak kiḷaviyum
aṇmi cuṭṭiya viḷinilik kiḷaviyum
ceymmaṉa eṉṉum toḻil iṟu collum
ēval kaṇṇiya viyaṅkōṭ kiḷaviyum
ceyta eṉṉum peyareñcukiḷaviyum
ceyyiya eṉṉum viṉiyeñcukiḷaviyum
amma eṉṉum uripporuṭ kiḷaviyum
palavaṟṟu iṟutip peyarkkoṭi uḷappaṭa
aṉṟi aṉittum iyalpu eṉa moḻipa. 8
vāḻiya eṉṉum ceya eṉ kiḷavi
iṟuti yakaram keṭutalum urittē. 9
uripporuṭ kiḷavi nīṭṭamum variyār. 10
palavaṟṟu iṟuti nīṭu moḻi uḷavē
ceyyuḷ kaṇṇiya toṭarmoḻiyāṉa. 11
toṭar al iṟuti tam muṉ tām variṉ
lakaram ṟakara oṟṟu ātalum urittē. 12
valleḻuttu iyaṟki uṟaḻat tōṉṟum. 13
vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 14
marappeyark kiḷavi melleḻuttu mikumē. 15
makappeyark kiḷavikku iṉṉē cāriyi. 16
attu avaṇ variṉum vari nili iṉṟē. 17
palavaṟṟu iṟuti urupu iyal niliyum. 18
ākāra iṟuti akara iyaṟṟē. 19
ceyyā eṉṉum viṉiyeñcukiḷaviyum
av iyal tiriyātu eṉmaṉār pulavar. 20
ummi eñciya iru peyart tokimoḻi
meymmiyāka akaram mikumē. 21
āvum māvum viḷippeyark kiḷaviyum
yā eṉ viṉāvum palavaṟṟu iṟutiyum
ēval kuṟitta uriyaci miyāvum
taṉ toḻil urikkum viṉāviṉ kiḷaviyoṭu
aṉṟi aṉittum iyalpu eṉa moḻipa. 22
vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 23
kuṟiyataṉ muṉṉarum ōreḻuttu moḻikkum
aṟiyat tōṉṟum akarak kiḷavi. 24
irā eṉ kiḷavikku akaram illi. 25
nilā eṉ kiḷavi attoṭu civaṇum. 26
yāmarak kiḷaviyum piṭāvum taḷāvum
ā mup peyarum melleḻuttu mikumē. 27
valleḻuttu mikiṉum māṉam illi. 28
māmarak kiḷaviyum āvum māvum
ā mup peyarum avaṟṟu ōraṉṉa
akaram valleḻuttu avi avaṇ niliyā
ṉakaram oṟṟum āvum māvum 29
āṉ oṟṟu akaramoṭu nili iṭaṉ uṭittē. 30
āṉ muṉ varūum īkāra pakaram
tāṉ mikat tōṉṟik kuṟukalum urittē. 31
kuṟiyataṉ iṟutic ciṉi keṭa ukaram
aṟiya varutal ceyyuḷuḷ urittē. 32
ikara iṟutip peyarnili muṉṉar
vēṟṟumi āyiṉ valleḻuttu mikumē. 33
iṉi aṇi eṉṉum kāliyum iṭaṉum
viṉiyeñcukiḷaviyum cuṭṭum aṉṉa. 34
iṉṟi eṉṉum viṉiyeñcu iṟuti
niṉṟa ikaram ukaram ātal
toṉṟu iyal maruṅkiṉ ceyyuḷuḷ urittē. 35
cuṭṭiṉ iyaṟki muṉ kiḷantaṟṟē. 36
patakku muṉ variṉē tūṇik kiḷavi
mutal kiḷantu eṭutta vēṟṟumi iyaṟṟē. 37
uri varu kāli nāḻik kiḷavi
iṟuti ikaram meyyoṭum keṭumē
ṭakāram oṟṟum āvayiṉāṉa. 38
paṉi eṉa varūum kāla vēṟṟumikku
attum iṉṉum cāriyi ākum. 39
vaḷi eṉa varūum pūtak kiḷaviyum
av iyal niliyal cevvitu eṉpa. 40
utimarak kiḷavi melleḻuttu mikumē. 41
puḷimarak kiḷavikku ammē cāriyi. 42
ēṉip puḷip peyar melleḻuttu mikumē. 43
valleḻuttu mikiṉum māṉam illi
olvaḻi aṟital vaḻakkattāṉa. 44
nāḷ muṉ tōṉṟum toḻilnilik kiḷavikku
āṉ iṭi varutal aiyam iṉṟē. 45
tiṅkaḷ muṉ variṉ ikkē cāriyi. 46
īkāra iṟuti ākāra iyaṟṟē. 47
nī eṉ peyarum iṭakkarp peyarum
mī eṉa marīiya iṭam vari kiḷaviyum
āvayiṉ valleḻuttu iyaṟki ākum. 48
iṭam vari kiḷavi muṉ valleḻuttu mikūum
uṭaṉ nili moḻiyum uḷa eṉa moḻipa. 49
vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 50
nī eṉ oru peyar urupu iyal niliyum
āvayiṉ valleḻuttu iyaṟki ākum. 51
ukara iṟuti akara iyaṟṟē. 52
cuṭṭiṉ muṉṉarum at toḻiṟṟu ākum. 53
ēṉavi variṉē mēl nili iyalpē. 54
cuṭṭu mutal iṟuti iyalpu ākummē. 55
aṉṟu varu kāli ā ākutalum
ai varu kāli mey varintu keṭutalum
ceyyuḷ maruṅkiṉ urittu eṉa moḻipa. 56
vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 57
eruvum ceruvum ammoṭu civaṇi
tiripu iṭaṉ uṭiya teriyum kāli
ammiṉ makaram ceruvayiṉ keṭumē
tam oṟṟu mikūum valleḻuttu iyaṟki. 58
ḻakara ukaram nīṭu iṭaṉ uṭittē
ukaram varutal āvayiṉāṉa. 59
oṭumarak kiḷavi uti mara iyaṟṟē. 60
cuṭṭu mutal iṟuti urupu iyal niliyum
oṟṟu iṭi mikā valleḻuttu iyaṟki. 61
ūkāra iṟuti ākāra iyaṟṟē. 62
viṉiyeñcukiḷavikkum muṉṉili moḻikkum
niṉiyum kāli av vaki variyār. 63
vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 64
kuṟṟeḻuttu imparum ōreḻuttu moḻikkum
niṟṟal vēṇṭum ukarak kiḷavi. 65
pū eṉ oru peyar āyiyalpu iṉṟē
āvayiṉ valleḻuttu mikutalum urittē. 66
ū eṉ oru peyar āvoṭu civaṇum. 67
akku eṉ cāriyi peṟutalum urittē
takka vaḻi aṟital vaḻakkattāṉa. 68
āṭūu makaṭūu āyiru peyarkkum
iṉ iṭi variṉum māṉam illi.- 69
ekara okaram peyarkku īṟu ākā
muṉṉili moḻiya eṉmaṉār pulavar
tēṟṟamum ciṟappum al vaḻiyāṉa. 70
tēṟṟa ekaramum ciṟappiṉ ovvum
mēṟ kūṟu iyaṟki valleḻuttu mikā. 71
ēkāra iṟuti ūkāra iyaṟṟē. 72
māṟu koḷ eccamum viṉāvum eṇṇum
kūṟiya valleḻuttu iyaṟki ākum. 73
vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 74
ē eṉ iṟutikku ekaram varumē. 75
cē eṉ marappeyar oṭumara iyaṟṟē. 76
peṟṟam āyiṉ muṟṟa iṉ vēṇṭum. 77
aikāra iṟutip peyarnili muṉṉar
vēṟṟumi āyiṉ valleḻuttu mikumē. 78
cuṭṭu mutal iṟuti urupu iyal niliyum. 79
vicimarak kiḷaviyum ñemiyum namiyum
ā mup peyarum cēmara iyala. 80
paṉiyum ariyum āvirik kiḷaviyum
niṉiyum kāli ammoṭu civaṇum
ai eṉ iṟuti ari varintu keṭumē
mey avaṇ oḻiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 81
paṉiyiṉ muṉṉar aṭṭu varu kāli
nili iṉṟu ākum ai eṉ uyirē
ākāram varutal āvayiṉāṉa. 82
koṭi muṉ variṉē ai avaṇ niṟpa
kaṭi nili iṉṟē valleḻuttu mikuti. 83
tiṅkaḷum nāḷum muntu kiḷantaṉṉa. 84
maḻi eṉ kiḷavi vaḷi iyal niliyum. 85
ceyyuḷ maruṅkiṉ vēṭki eṉṉum
ai eṉ iṟuti avā muṉ variṉē
meyyoṭum keṭutal eṉmaṉār pulavar
ṭakāram ṇakāram ātal vēṇṭum. 86
ōkāra iṟuti ēkāra iyaṟṟē. 87
māṟu koḷ eccamum viṉāvum aiyamum
kūṟiya valleḻuttu iyaṟki ākum. 88
oḻintataṉ niliyum moḻintavaṟṟu iyaṟṟē. 89
vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum ataṉ ōraṟṟē
okaram varutal āvayiṉāṉa. 90
illoṭu kiḷappiṉ iyaṟki ākum. 91
urupu iyal niliyum moḻiyumār uḷavē
āvayiṉ valleḻuttu iyaṟki ākum. 92
aukāra iṟutip peyarnili muṉṉar
alvaḻiyāṉum vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum
valleḻuttu mikutal vari nili iṉṟē
av iru īṟṟum ukaram varutal
cevvitu eṉpa ciṟanticiṉōrē. 93

1. 8. puḷḷimayaṅkiyal
ñakāri oṟṟiya toḻiṟpeyar muṉṉar
allatu kiḷappiṉum vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum
valleḻuttu iyiyiṉ av eḻuttu mikumē
ukaram varutal āvayiṉāṉa. 1
ña na ma va iyiyiṉum ukaram niliyum. 2
nakara iṟutiyum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 3
vēṟṟumikku uk keṭa akaram niliyum. 4
verin eṉ iṟuti muḻutum keṭuvaḻi
varum iṭaṉ uṭittē melleḻuttu iyaṟki. 5
āvayiṉ valleḻuttu mikutalum urittē. 6
ṇakāra iṟuti valleḻuttu iyiyiṉ
ṭakāram ākum vēṟṟumip poruṭkē. 7
āṇum peṇṇum aḵṟiṇi iyaṟki. 8
āṇmarak kiḷavi arimara iyaṟṟē. 9
viṇ eṉa varūum kāyap peyarvayiṉ
uṇmiyum urittē attu eṉ cāriyi
ceyyuḷ maruṅkiṉ toḻil varu kāli. 10
toḻiṟpeyar ellām toḻiṟpeyar iyala. 11
kiḷippeyar ellām koḷat tiripu ilavē. 12
vēṟṟumi alvaḻi eṇ eṉ uṇavup peyar
vēṟṟumi iyaṟki niliyalum urittē. 13
muraṇ eṉ toḻiṟpeyar mutal iyal niliyum. 14
makara iṟuti vēṟṟumi āyiṉ
tuvarak keṭṭu valleḻuttu mikumē. 15
akara ākāram varūum kāli
īṟṟumici akaram nīṭalum urittē. 16
melleḻuttu uṟaḻum moḻiyumār uḷavē
cel vaḻi aṟital vaḻakkattāṉa. 17
illam marappeyar vicimara iyaṟṟē. 18
alvaḻi ellām melleḻuttu ākum. 19
akam eṉ kiḷavikkuk ki muṉ variṉē
mutalnili oḻiya muṉṉavi keṭutalum
vari nili iṉṟē āciriyarkka
melleḻuttu mikutal āvayiṉāṉa. 20
ilam eṉ kiḷavikkup paṭu varu kāli
niliyalum urittē ceyyuḷāṉa. 21
attoṭu civaṇum āyirattu iṟuti
otta eṇṇu muṉ varu kāli. 22
aṭiyoṭu tōṉṟiṉum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 23
aḷavum niṟiyum vēṟṟumi iyala. 24
paṭarkkip peyarum muṉṉilip peyarum
toṭakkam kuṟukum peyarnilik kiḷaviyum
vēṟṟumi āyiṉ urupu iyal niliyum
melleḻuttu mikutal āvayiṉāṉa. 25
allatu kiḷappiṉ iyaṟki ākum. 26
allatu kiḷappiṉum vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum
ellām eṉum peyar urupu iyal niliyum
vēṟṟumi al vaḻic cāriyi niliyātu. 27
melleḻuttu mikiṉum māṉam illi. 28
uyartiṇi āyiṉ urupu iyal niliyum. 29
num eṉ oru peyar melleḻuttu mikumē. 30
allataṉ maruṅkiṉ collum kāli
uk keṭa niṉṟa meyvayiṉ ī vara
i iṭi nilii īṟu keṭa rakaram
niṟṟal vēṇṭum puḷḷiyoṭu puṇarntē
ap pāl moḻivayiṉ iyaṟki ākum. 31
toḻiṟpeyar ellām toḻiṟpeyar iyala. 32
īmum kammum urum eṉ kiḷaviyum
ā mup peyarum avaṟṟu ōraṉṉa. 33
vēṟṟumi āyiṉ ēṉi iraṇṭum
tōṟṟam vēṇṭum akku eṉ cāriyi- 34
vakāram miciyum makāram kuṟukum. 35
nāṭpeyark kiḷavi mēl kiḷantaṉṉa
attum āṉmici vari nili iṉṟē
oṟṟu mey keṭutal eṉmaṉār pulavar. 36
ṉakāra iṟuti valleḻuttu iyiyiṉ
ṟakāram ākum vēṟṟumip poruṭkē. 37
maṉṉum ciṉṉum āṉum īṉum
piṉṉum muṉṉum viṉiyeñcu kiḷaviyum
aṉṉa iyala eṉmaṉār pulavar. 38
cuṭṭu mutal vayiṉum ekaram mutal vayiṉum
ap paṇpu niliyum iyaṟkiya eṉpa. 39
kuyiṉ eṉ kiḷavi iyaṟki ākum. 40
ekiṉ maram āyiṉ āṇmara iyaṟṟē. 41
ēṉi ekiṉē akaram varumē
valleḻuttu iyaṟki mikutal vēṇṭum. 42
kiḷippeyar ellām kiḷippeyar iyala. 43
mīṉ eṉ kiḷavi valleḻuttu uṟaḻvē. 44
tēṉ eṉ kiḷavi valleḻuttu iyiyiṉ
mēl nili ottalum valleḻuttu mikutalum
ā muṟi iraṇṭum urimiyum uṭittē
valleḻuttu miku vaḻi iṟuti illi. 45
melleḻuttu mikiṉum māṉam illi. 46
melleḻuttu iyiyiṉ iṟutiyoṭu uṟaḻum. 47
iṟāal tōṟṟam iyaṟki ākum. 48
oṟṟu miku takaramoṭu niṟṟalum urittē. 49
miṉṉum piṉṉum paṉṉum kaṉṉum
an nāṟ collum toḻiṟpeyar iyala. 50
vēṟṟumi āyiṉ ēṉi ekiṉoṭu
tōṟṟam okkum kaṉ eṉ kiḷavi. 51
iyaṟpeyar muṉṉart tanti muṟi variṉ
mutaṟkaṇ mey keṭa akaram niliyum
mey oḻittu aṉ keṭum av iyaṟpeyarē. 52
ātaṉum pūtaṉum kūṟiya iyalpoṭu
peyar oṟṟu akaram tuvarak keṭumē. 53
ciṟappoṭu varu vaḻi iyaṟki ākum. 54
ap peyar mey oḻittu aṉ keṭu vaḻiyē
niṟṟalum urittē am eṉ cāriyi
makkaḷ muṟi tokūum maruṅkiṉāṉa. 55
tāṉum pēṉum kōṉum eṉṉum
ā muṟi iyaṟpeyar tiripu iṭaṉ ilavē. 56
tāṉ yāṉ eṉum peyar urupu iyal niliyum. 57
vēṟṟumi al vaḻik kuṟukalum tiritalum
tōṟṟam illi eṉmaṉār pulavar. 58
aḻaṉ eṉ iṟuti keṭa valleḻuttu mikumē. 59
muṉ eṉ kiḷavi muṉṉart tōṉṟum
il eṉ kiḷavimici ṟakaram oṟṟal
tol iyal maruṅkiṉ marīiya marapē. 60
poṉ eṉ kiḷavi īṟu keṭa muṟiyiṉ
muṉṉart tōṉṟum lakāra makāram
ceyyuḷ maruṅkiṉ toṭar iyalāṉa. 61
yakara iṟuti vēṟṟumip poruḷvayiṉ
valleḻuttu iyiyiṉ av eḻuttu mikumē. 62
tāy eṉ kiḷavi iyaṟki ākum. 63
makaṉ viṉi kiḷappiṉ mutal nili iyaṟṟē. 64
melleḻuttu uṟaḻum moḻiyumār uḷavē. 65
alvaḻi ellām iyalpu eṉa moḻipa. 66
rakāra iṟuti yakāra iyaṟṟē. 67
ārum vetirum cārum pīrum
melleḻuttu mikutal mey peṟat tōṉṟum. 68
cār eṉ kiḷavi kāḻvayiṉ valikkum. 69
pīr eṉ kiḷavi ammoṭum civaṇum. 70
lakāra iṟuti ṉakāra iyaṟṟē. 71
melleḻuttu iyiyiṉ ṉakāram ākum. 72
alvaḻi ellām uṟaḻ eṉa moḻipa. 73
takaram varu vaḻi āytam niliyalum
pukar iṉṟu eṉmaṉār pulamiyōrē. 74
neṭiyataṉ iṟuti iyalpumār uḷavē. 75
nellum cellum kollum collum
allatu kiḷappiṉum vēṟṟumi iyala. 76
il eṉ kiḷavi iṉmi ceppiṉ
valleḻuttu mikutalum ai iṭi varutalum
iyaṟki ātalum ākāram varutalum
koḷat taku marapiṉ āku iṭaṉ uṭittē. 77
val eṉ kiḷavi toḻiṟpeyar iyaṟṟē. 78
nāyum palakiyum varūum kāli
āvayiṉ ukaram keṭutalum urittē
ukaram keṭu vaḻi akaram niliyum.- 79
pūl vēl eṉṟā āl eṉ kiḷaviyoṭu
ā mup peyarkkum am iṭi varumē. 80
toḻiṟpeyar ellām toḻiṟpeyar iyala. 81
veyil eṉ kiḷavi maḻi iyal niliyum. 82
cuṭṭu mutal ākiya vakara iṟuti
muṟpaṭak kiḷanta urupu iyal niliyum. 83
vēṟṟumi alvaḻi āytam ākum. 84
melleḻuttu iyiyiṉ av eḻuttu ākum. 85
ēṉavi puṇariṉ iyalpu eṉa moḻipa. 86
ēṉi vakaram toḻiṟpeyar iyaṟṟē. 87
ḻakāra iṟuti rakāra iyaṟṟē. 88
tāḻ eṉ kiḷavi kōloṭu puṇariṉ
akku iṭi varutal urittum ākum. 89
tamiḻ eṉ kiḷaviyum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 90
kumiḻ eṉ kiḷavi marappeyar āyiṉ
pīr eṉ kiḷaviyoṭu ōr iyaṟṟu ākum. 91
pāḻ eṉ kiḷavi melleḻuttu uṟaḻvē. 92
ēḻ eṉ kiḷavi urupu iyal niliyum. 93
aḷavum niṟiyum eṇṇum varu vaḻi
neṭu mutal kuṟukalum ukaram varutalum
kaṭi nili iṉṟē āciriyarkka. 94
pattu eṉ kiḷavi oṟṟu iṭi keṭu vaḻi
niṟṟal vēṇṭum āytap puḷḷi. 95
āyiram varu vaḻi ukaram keṭumē.- 96
nūṟu ūrntu varūum āyirak kiḷavikkuk
kūṟiya neṭu mutal kuṟukkam iṉṟē. 97
ai am pal eṉa varūum iṟuti
al peyar eṇṇum āyiyal niliyum. 98
uyir muṉ variṉum āyiyal tiriyātu. 99
kīḻ eṉ kiḷavi uṟaḻat tōṉṟum. 100
ḷakāra iṟuti ṇakāra iyaṟṟē.-- 101
melleḻuttu iyiyiṉ ṇakāram ākum. 102
alvaḻi ellām uṟaḻ eṉa moḻipa.- 103
āytam niliyalum vari nili iṉṟē
takaram varūum kāliyāṉa. 104
neṭiyataṉ iṟuti iyalpu ākunavum
vēṟṟumi al vaḻi vēṟṟumi niliyalum
pōṟṟal vēṇṭum moḻiyumār uḷavē. 105
toḻiṟpeyar ellām toḻiṟpeyar iyala. 106
iruḷ eṉ kiḷavi veyil iyal niliyum. 107
puḷḷum vaḷḷum toḻiṟpeyar iyala.- 108
makkaḷ eṉṉum peyarccol iṟuti
takkavaḻi aṟintu valittalum urittē. 109
uṇarak kūṟiya puṇar iyal maruṅkiṉ
kaṇṭu ceyaṟku uriyavi kaṇṇiṉar koḷalē. 110

1.9. kuṟṟiyalukarappuṇariyal
īr eḻuttu orumoḻi uyirttoṭar iṭittoṭar
āytat toṭarmoḻi vaṉṟoṭar meṉṟoṭar
āyiru mūṉṟē ukaram kuṟuku iṭaṉ. 1
avaṟṟuḷ,
īr oṟṟut toṭarmoḻi iṭittoṭar ākā. 2
allatu kiḷappiṉum vēṟṟumikkaṇṇum
ellā iṟutiyum ukaram niṟiyum. 3
valloṟṟut toṭarmoḻi valleḻuttu varu vaḻi
tolli iyaṟki niliyalum urittē. 4
yakaram varu vaḻi ikaram kuṟukum
ukarak kiḷavi tuvarat tōṉṟātu. 5
īr eḻuttu moḻiyum uyirttoṭar moḻiyum
vēṟṟumi āyiṉ oṟṟu iṭi iṉam mika
tōṟṟam vēṇṭum valleḻuttu mikuti. 6
oṟṟu iṭi iṉam mikā moḻiyumār uḷavē
at tiṟattu illi valleḻuttu mikalē. 7
iṭiyoṟṟut toṭarum āytattoṭarum
naṭi āyiyala eṉmaṉār pulavar. 8
vaṉṟoṭar moḻiyum meṉṟoṭar moḻiyum
vanta valleḻuttu oṟṟu iṭi mikumē
melloṟṟut toṭarmoḻi melloṟṟu ellām
valloṟṟu iṟuti kiḷi oṟṟu ākum. 9
marappeyark kiḷavikku ammē cāriyi.-- 10
melloṟṟu valiyā marappeyarum uḷavē. 11
īr eḻuttu moḻiyum valloṟṟut toṭarum
am iṭi varaṟkum uriyavi uḷavē
am marapu oḻukum moḻivayiṉāṉa 12
oṟṟu nili tiriyātu akkoṭu varūum
ak kiḷimoḻiyum uḷa eṉa moḻipa. 13
eṇṇuppeyark kiḷavi urupu iyal niliyum. 14
vaṇṭum peṇṭum iṉṉoṭu civaṇum. 15
peṇṭu eṉ kiḷavikku aṉṉum variyār. 16
yātu eṉ iṟutiyum cuṭṭu mutal ākiya
āyta iṟutiyum urupu iyal niliyum. 17
muṉ uyir varum iṭattu āytap puḷḷi
maṉṉal vēṇṭum alvaḻiyāṉa. 18
ēṉi muṉ variṉē tāṉ nili iṉṟē. 19
allatu kiḷappiṉ ellā moḻiyum
colliya paṇpiṉ iyaṟki ākum. 20
valloṟṟut toṭarmoḻi valleḻuttu mikumē. 21
cuṭṭuc ciṉi nīṭiya meṉṟoṭar moḻiyum
yā viṉā mutaliya meṉṟoṭar moḻiyum
āyiyal tiriyā valleḻuttu iyaṟki. 22
yā viṉā moḻiyē iyalpum ākum. 23
an nāl moḻiyum tam nili tiriyā. 24
uṇṭu eṉ kiḷavi uṇmi ceppiṉ
munti iṟuti meyyoṭum keṭutalum
mēl nili oṟṟē ḷakāram ātalum
ā muṟi iraṇṭum urimiyum uṭittē
valleḻuttu varūum kāliyāṉa. 25
iru tici puṇariṉ ē iṭi varumē. 26
tiripu vēṟu kiḷappiṉ oṟṟum ukaramum
keṭutal vēṇṭum eṉmaṉār pulavar
oṟṟu mey tirintu ṉakāram ākum
teṟkoṭu puṇarum kāliyāṉa. 27
oṉṟu mutal āka eṭṭaṉ iṟuti
ellā eṇṇum pattaṉ muṉ variṉ
kuṟṟiyalukaram meyyoṭum keṭumē
muṟṟa iṉ varūum iraṇṭu alaṅkaṭiyē. 28
pattaṉ oṟṟuk keṭa ṉakāram iraṭṭal
ottatu eṉpa iraṇṭu varu kāli. 29
āyiram variṉum āyiyal tiriyātu. 30
niṟiyum aḷavum varūum kāliyum
kuṟiyātu ākum iṉ eṉ cāriyi. 31
oṉṟu mutal oṉpāṉ iṟuti muṉṉar
niṉṟa pattaṉ oṟṟuk keṭa āytam
vantu iṭi niliyum iyaṟkittu eṉpa
kūṟiya iyaṟki kuṟṟiyalukaram
āṟaṉ iṟuti al vaḻiyāṉa. 32
mutal īr eṇṇiṉ oṟṟu rakaram ākum
ukaram varutal āvayiṉāṉa. 33
iṭi nili rakaram iraṇṭu eṉ eṇṇiṟku
naṭi maruṅku iṉṟē poruḷvayiṉāṉa. 34
mūṉṟum āṟum neṭu mutal kuṟukum
mūṉṟaṉ oṟṟē pakāram ākum. 35
nāṉkaṉ oṟṟē ṟakāram ākum. 36
aintaṉ oṟṟē makāram ākum. 37
eṭṭaṉ oṟṟē ṇakāram ākum. 38
oṉpāṉ okaramicit takaram oṟṟum
munti oṟṟē ṇakāram iraṭṭum
paḵtu eṉ kiḷavi āyta pakaram keṭa
niṟṟal vēṇṭum ūkārak kiḷavi
oṟṟiya takaram ṟakaram ākum. 39
aḷantu aṟi kiḷaviyum niṟiyiṉ kiḷaviyum
kiḷanta iyala tōṉṟum kāli. 40
mūṉṟaṉ oṟṟē vantatu okkum. 41
aintaṉ oṟṟē melleḻuttu ākum. 42
ka ca ta pa mutal moḻi varūum kāli. 43
na ma va eṉṉum mūṉṟoṭu civaṇi
akaram variṉum eṭṭaṉ muṉ iyalpē. 44
aintum mūṉṟum na ma varu kāli
vantatu okkum oṟṟu iyal niliyē. 45
mūṉṟaṉ oṟṟē vakāram varu vaḻi
tōṉṟiya vakārattu uru ākummē. 46
nāṉkaṉ oṟṟē lakāram ākum. 47
aintaṉ oṟṟē muntiyatu keṭumē.- 48
mutal īr eṇṇiṉ muṉ uyir varu kāli
taval eṉa moḻipa ukarak kiḷavi
mutal nili nīṭal āvayiṉāṉa. 49
mūṉṟum nāṉkum aintu eṉ kiḷaviyum
tōṉṟiya vakarattu iyaṟki ākum. 50
mūṉṟaṉ mutal nili nīṭalum urittē
uḻakku eṉ kiḷavi vaḻakkattāṉa. 51
āṟu eṉ kiḷavi mutal nīṭummē. 52
oṉpāṉ iṟuti urupu nili tiriyātu
iṉ peṟal vēṇṭum cāriyi moḻiyē. 53
nūṟu muṉ variṉum kūṟiya iyalpē. 54
mūṉṟaṉ oṟṟē nakāram ākum. 55
nāṉkum aintum oṟṟu mey tiriyā. 56
oṉpāṉ mutal nili muntu kiḷantaṟṟē
munti oṟṟē ḷakāram iraṭṭum
nūṟu eṉ kiḷavi nakāram mey keṭa
ū ā ākum iyaṟkittu eṉpa
āyiṭi varutal ikāra rakāram
īṟu mey keṭuttu makāram oṟṟum. 57
āyirak kiḷavi varūum kāli
mutal īr eṇṇiṉ ukaram keṭumē. 58
mutal nili nīṭiṉum māṉam illi. 59
mūṉṟaṉ oṟṟē vakāram ākum. 60
nāṉkaṉ oṟṟē lakāram ākum. 61
aintaṉ oṟṟē yakāram ākum. 62
āṟaṉ maruṅkiṉ kuṟṟiyalukaram
īṟu mey oḻiyak keṭutal vēṇṭum. 63
oṉpāṉ iṟuti urupu nili tiriyātu
iṉ peṟal vēṇṭum cāriyi marapē. 64
nūṟāyiram muṉ varūum kāli
nūṟaṉ iyaṟki mutal nilik kiḷavi. 65
nūṟu eṉ kiḷavi oṉṟu mutal oṉpāṟku
īṟu ciṉi oḻiya iṉa oṟṟu mikumē. 66
avi ūr pattiṉum at toḻiṟṟu ākum. 67
aḷavum niṟiyum āyiyal tiriyā
kuṟṟiyalukaramum valleḻuttu iyaṟkiyum
muṉ kiḷantaṉṉa eṉmaṉār pulavar. 68
oṉṟu mutal ākiya pattu ūr kiḷavi
oṉṟu mutal oṉpāṟku oṟṟu iṭi mikumē
niṉṟa āytam keṭutal vēṇṭum. 69
āyiram variṉē iṉ ām cāriyi
āvayiṉ oṟṟu iṭi mikutal illi. 70
aḷavum niṟiyum āyiyal tiriyā. 71
mutal nili eṇṇiṉ muṉ valleḻuttu variṉum
ña na mat tōṉṟiṉum ya va vantu iyiyiṉum
mutal nili iyaṟki eṉmaṉār pulavar. 72
ataṉ nili uyirkkum yā varu kāli
mutal nili okaram ō ākummē
rakarattu ukaram tuvarak keṭumē. 73
iraṇṭu mutal oṉpāṉ iṟuti muṉṉar
vaḻaṅku iyal mā eṉ kiḷavi tōṉṟiṉ
makara aḷavoṭu nikaralum urittē. 74
la ṉa eṉa varūum puḷḷi iṟuti muṉ
ummum keḻuvum uḷappaṭap piṟavum
aṉṉa marapiṉ moḻiyiṭit tōṉṟi
ceyyuḷ toṭarvayiṉ mey peṟa niliyum
vēṟṟumi kuṟitta poruḷvayiṉāṉa. 75
uyirum puḷḷiyum iṟuti āki
kuṟippiṉum paṇpiṉum iciyiṉum tōṉṟi
neṟip paṭa vārāk kuṟiccoṟ kiḷaviyum
uyartiṇi aḵṟiṇi āyiru maruṅkiṉ
aim pāl aṟiyum paṇpu toku moḻiyum
ceyyum ceyta eṉṉum kiḷaviyiṉ
mey oruṅku iyalum toḻil toku moḻiyum
tam iyal kiḷappiṉ tam muṉ tām varūum
eṇṇiṉ tokuti uḷappaṭap piṟavum
aṉṉavi ellām maruviṉ pāttiya
puṇar iyal niliyiṭi uṇarat tōṉṟā. 76
kiḷanta alla ceyyuḷuḷ tirinavum
vaḻaṅku iyal maruṅkiṉ maruvoṭu tirinavum
viḷampiya iyaṟkiyiṉ vēṟupaṭat tōṉṟiṉ
vaḻaṅku iyal maruṅkiṉ uṇarntaṉar oḻukkal
naṉ mati nāṭṭattu eṉmaṉār pulavar. 77

2.1. kiḷaviyākkam
uyartiṇi eṉmaṉār makkaṭ cuṭṭē
aḵṟiṇi eṉmaṉār avar ala piṟavē
āyiru tiṇiyiṉ icikkumaṉa collē. 1
āṭūu aṟi col makaṭūu aṟi col
pallōr aṟiyum colloṭu civaṇi
am mup pāṟcol uyartiṇiyavvē. 2
oṉṟu aṟi collē pala aṟi col eṉṟu
āyiru pāṟcol aḵṟiṇiyavvē. 3
peṇmi cuṭṭiya uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ
āṇmi tirinta peyar nilik kiḷaviyum
teyvam cuṭṭiya peyar nilik kiḷaviyum
iv eṉa aṟiyum antam tamakku ilavē
uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ pāl pirintu icikkum. 4
ṉaḵkāṉ oṟṟē āṭūu aṟi col. 5
ḷaḵkāṉ oṟṟē makaṭū aṟi col. 6
raḵkāṉ oṟṟum pakara iṟutiyum
mārik kiḷavi uḷappaṭa mūṉṟum
nērat tōṉṟum palar aṟi collē. 7
oṉṟu aṟi kiḷavi ta ṟa ṭa ūrnta
kuṉṟiyalukarattu iṟuti ākum. 8
a ā va eṉa varūum iṟuti
ap pāl mūṉṟē pala aṟi collē. 9
iru tiṇi maruṅkiṉ aim pāl aṟiya
īṟṟiṉ niṉṟu icikkum patiṉōr eḻuttum
tōṟṟamtāmē viṉiyoṭu varumē. 10
viṉiyiṉ tōṉṟum pāl aṟi kiḷaviyum
peyariṉ tōṉṟum pāl aṟi kiḷaviyum
mayaṅkal kūṭā tam marapiṉavē. 11
āṇmi tirinta peyar nilik kiḷavi
āṇmi aṟi coṟku āku iṭaṉ iṉṟē. 12
ceppum viṉāvum vaḻāal ōmpal. 13
viṉāvum ceppē viṉā etir variṉē. 14
ceppē vaḻīiyiṉum vari nili iṉṟē
ap poruḷ puṇarnta kiḷaviyāṉa. 15
ceppiṉum viṉāviṉum ciṉi mutal kiḷavikku
ap poruḷ ākum uṟaḻ tuṇip poruḷē. 16
takutiyum vaḻakkum taḻīiyiṉa oḻukum
pakutik kiḷavi vari nili ilavē. 17
iṉac cuṭṭu illāp paṇpu koḷ peyarkkoṭi
vaḻakku āṟu alla ceyyuḷ āṟē. 18
iyaṟkip poruḷi iṟṟu eṉak kiḷattal. 19
ceyaṟkip poruḷi ākkamoṭu kūṟal. 20
ākkamtāṉē kāraṇam mutaṟṟē. 21
ākkak kiḷavi kāraṇam iṉṟiyum
pōkku iṉṟu eṉpa vaḻakkiṉuḷḷē. 22
pāl mayakku uṟṟa aiyak kiḷavi
tāṉ aṟi poruḷvayiṉ paṉmi kūṟal. 23
urupu eṉa moḻiyiṉum aḵṟiṇip pirippiṉum
iru vīṟṟum urittē cuṭṭum kāli. 24
taṉmi cuṭṭalum urittu eṉa moḻipa
aṉmik kiḷavi vēṟu iṭattāṉa. 25
aṭi ciṉi mutal eṉa muṟi mūṉṟum mayaṅkāmi
naṭi peṟṟu iyalum vaṇṇac ciṉic col. 26
oruvarik kūṟum paṉmik kiḷaviyum
oṉṟaṉik kūṟum paṉmik kiḷaviyum
vaḻakkiṉ ākiya uyar col kiḷavi
ilakkaṇa maruṅkiṉ col āṟu alla. 27
celaviṉum varaviṉum taraviṉum koṭiyiṉum
nili peṟat tōṉṟum an nāṟ collum
taṉmi muṉṉili paṭarkki eṉṉum
am mū iṭattum uriya eṉpa. 28
avaṟṟuḷ,
taru col varu col āyiru kiḷaviyum
taṉmi muṉṉili āyīr iṭatta. 29
ēṉi iraṇṭum ēṉi iṭatta. 30
yātu evaṉ eṉṉum āyiru kiḷaviyum
aṟiyāp poruḷvayiṉ ceṟiyat tōṉṟum. 31
avaṟṟuḷ,
yātu eṉa varūum viṉāviṉ kiḷavi
aṟinta poruḷvayiṉ aiyam tīrtaṟkut
terinta kiḷavi ātalum urittē. 32
iṉittu eṉa aṟinta ciṉi mutal kiḷavikku
viṉippaṭu tokutiyiṉ ummi vēṇṭum. 33
maṉṉāp poruḷum aṉṉa iyaṟṟē. 34
ep poruḷ āyiṉum allatu il eṉiṉ
ap poruḷ allāp piṟitu poruḷ kūṟal. 35
ap poruḷ kūṟiṉ cuṭṭik kūṟal. 36
poruḷoṭu puṇarāc cuṭṭuppeyar āyiṉum
poruḷ vēṟupaṭāatu oṉṟu ākummē. 37
iyaṟpeyark kiḷaviyum cuṭṭuppeyark kiḷaviyum
viṉikku oruṅku iyalum kālam tōṉṟiṉ
cuṭṭuppeyark kiḷavi muṟpaṭak kiḷavār
iyaṟpeyar vaḻiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 38
muṟpaṭak kiḷattal ceyyuḷuḷ urittē. 39
cuṭṭu mutal ākiya kāraṇak kiḷaviyum
cuṭṭuppeyar iyaṟkiyiṉ ceṟiyat tōṉṟum. 40
ciṟappiṉ ākiya peyarnilik kiḷavikkum
iyaṟpeyark kiḷavi muṟpaṭak kiḷavār. 41
oru poruḷ kuṟitta vēṟu peyark kiḷavi
toḻil vēṟu kiḷappiṉ oṉṟu iṭaṉ ilavē. 42
taṉmic collē aḵṟiṇik kiḷavi eṉṟu
eṇṇu vaḻi maruṅkiṉ viravutal variyār. 43
orumi eṇṇiṉ potup piri pāṟcol
orumikku allatu eṇṇu muṟi nillātu. 44
viyaṅkōḷ eṇṇuppeyar tiṇi viravu variyār. 45
vēṟu viṉip potuc col oru viṉi kiḷavār. 46
eṇṇuṅkālum atu ataṉ marapē. 47
iraṭṭikkiḷavi iraṭṭiṉ pirintu iciyā. 48
oru peyarp potuc col uḷ poruḷ oḻiyat
teripu vēṟu kiḷattal talimiyum paṉmiyum
uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉum aḵṟiṇi maruṅkiṉum. 49
peyariṉum toḻiliṉum piripavi ellām
mayaṅkal kūṭā vaḻakkuvaḻip paṭṭaṉa. 50
palavayiṉāṉum eṇṇut tiṇi viravuppeyar
aḵṟiṇi muṭipiṉa ceyyuḷuḷḷē. 51
viṉi vēṟupaṭūum pala poruḷ oru col
viṉi vēṟupaṭāap pala poruḷ oru col eṉṟu
āyiru vakiya pala poruḷ oru col. 52
avaṟṟuḷ,
viṉi vēṟupaṭūum pala poruḷ oru col
vēṟupaṭu viṉiyiṉum iṉattiṉum cārpiṉum
tēṟat tōṉṟum poruḷ teri niliyē. 53
oṉṟu viṉi maruṅkiṉ oṉṟit tōṉṟum. 54
viṉi vēṟupaṭāap pala poruḷ oru col
niṉiyum kāli kiḷantāṅku iyalum. 55
kuṟittōṉ kūṟṟam terittu moḻi kiḷavi. 56
kuṭimi āṇmi iḷami mūppē
aṭimi vaṉmi viruntē kuḻuvē
peṇmi aracē makavē kuḻavi
taṉmi tiri peyar uṟuppiṉ kiḷavi
kātal ciṟappē ceṟaṟcol viṟaṟcol eṉṟu
āvaṟu mūṉṟum uḷappaṭat tokii
aṉṉa piṟavum avaṟṟoṭu civaṇi
muṉṉattiṉ uṇarum kiḷavi ellām
uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ niliyiṉa āyiṉum
aḵṟiṇi maruṅkiṉ kiḷantāṅku iyalum. 57
kālam ulakam uyirē uṭampē
pāl vari teyvam viṉiyē pūtam
ñāyiṟu tiṅkaḷ col eṉa varūum
āyīr aintoṭu piṟavum aṉṉa
āvayiṉ varūum kiḷavi ellām
pāl pirintu iciyā uyartiṇi mēṉa. 58
niṉṟāṅku icittal ivaṇ iyalpu iṉṟē. 59
icittalum uriya vēṟiṭattāṉa. 60
eṭutta moḻi iṉam ceppalum urittē. 61
kaṇṇum tōḷum muliyum piṟavum
paṉmi cuṭṭiya ciṉi nilik kiḷavi
paṉmi kūṟum kaṭappāṭu ilavē
tam viṉikku iyalum eḻuttu alaṅkaṭiyē. 62

2.2. vēṟṟumiyiyal
vēṟṟumitāmē ēḻ eṉa moḻipa. 1
viḷi koḷvataṉkaṇ viḷiyoṭu eṭṭē. 2
avitām,
peyar ai oṭu ku
iṉ atu kaṇ viḷi eṉṉum īṟṟa. 3
avaṟṟuḷ,
eḻuvāy vēṟṟumi peyar tōṉṟu niliyē. 4
poruṇmi cuṭṭal viyaṅkoḷa varutal
viṉi nili urittal viṉāviṟku ēṟṟal
paṇpu koḷa varutal peyar koḷa varutal eṉṟu
aṉṟi aṉittum peyarp payaṉiliyē. 5
peyariṉ ākiya tokiyumār uḷavē
avvum uriya appālāṉa. 6
ev vayiṉ peyarum veḷippaṭat tōṉṟi
av iyal niliyal cevvitu eṉpa. 7
kūṟiya muṟiyiṉ urupu nili tiriyātu
īṟu peyarkku ākum iyaṟkiya eṉpa. 8
peyarnilik kiḷavi kālam tōṉṟā
toḻil nili oṭṭum oṉṟu alaṅkaṭiyē. 9
iraṇṭākuvatē,
ai eṉap peyariya vēṟṟumik kiḷavi
ev vaḻi variṉum viṉiyē viṉikkuṟippu
av iru mutaliṉ tōṉṟum atuvē. 10
kāppiṉ oppiṉ ūrtiyiṉ iḻiyiṉ
oppiṉ pukaḻiṉ paḻiyiṉ eṉṟā
peṟaliṉ iḻaviṉ kātaliṉ vekuḷiyiṉ
ceṟaliṉ uvattaliṉ kaṟpiṉ eṉṟā
aṟuttaliṉ kuṟittaliṉ tokuttaliṉ pirittaliṉ
niṟuttaliṉ aḷaviṉ eṇṇiṉ eṉṟā
ākkaliṉ cārtaliṉ celaviṉ kaṉṟaliṉ
nōkkaliṉ añcaliṉ citippiṉ eṉṟā
aṉṉa piṟavum am mutaṟ poruḷa
eṉṉa kiḷaviyum ataṉ pāla eṉmaṉār. 11
mūṉṟākuvatē,
oṭu eṉap peyariya vēṟṟumik kiḷavi
viṉimutal karuvi aṉi mutaṟṟu atuvē. 12
ataṉiṉ iyaṟal ataṉ taku kiḷavi
ataṉ viṉippaṭutal ataṉiṉ ātal
ataṉiṉ kōṭal ataṉoṭu mayaṅkal
ataṉoṭu iyinta oru viṉik kiḷavi
ataṉoṭu iyinta vēṟu viṉik kiḷavi
ataṉoṭu iyinta oppu al oppu uri
iṉ āṉ ētu īṅku eṉa varūum
aṉṉa piṟavum ataṉ pāla eṉmaṉār. 13
nāṉkākuvatē,
ku eṉap peyariya vēṟṟumik kiḷavi
ep poruḷ āyiṉum koḷḷum atuvē. 14
ataṟku viṉi uṭimiyiṉ ataṟku uṭampaṭutaliṉ
ataṟkup paṭu poruḷiṉ atu āku kiḷaviyiṉ
ataṟku yāppu uṭimiyiṉ ataṉ poruṭṭu ātaliṉ
naṭpiṉ pakiyiṉ kātaliṉ ciṟappiṉ eṉṟu
ap poruṭ kiḷaviyum ataṉ pāla eṉmaṉār. 15
aintākuvatē,
iṉ eṉap peyariya vēṟṟumik kiḷavi
itaṉiṉ iṟṟu itu eṉṉum atuvē. 16
vaṇṇam vaṭivē aḷavē cuviyē
taṇmi vemmi accam eṉṟā
naṉmi tīmi ciṟumi perumi
vaṉmi meṉmi kaṭumi eṉṟā
mutumi iḷami ciṟattal iḻittal
putumi paḻami ākkam eṉṟā
iṉmi uṭimi nāṟṟam tīrtal
paṉmi ciṉmi paṟṟu viṭutal eṉṟu
aṉṉa piṟavum ataṉ pāla eṉmaṉār. 17
āṟākuvatē,
atu eṉap periya vēṟṟumik kiḷavi
taṉṉiṉum piṟitiṉum itaṉatu itu eṉum
aṉṉa kiḷavik kiḻamittu atuvē. 18
iyaṟkiyiṉ uṭimiyiṉ muṟimiyiṉ kiḻamiyiṉ
ceyaṟkiyiṉ mutumiyiṉ viṉiyiṉ eṉṟā
karuviyiṉ tuṇiyiṉ kalattiṉ mutaliṉ
oruvaḻi uṟuppiṉ kuḻuviṉ eṉṟā
terintu moḻic ceytiyiṉ niliyiṉ vāḻcciyiṉ
tirintu vēṟupaṭūum piṟavum aṉṉa
kūṟiya maruṅkiṉ tōṉṟum kiḷavi
āṟaṉ pāla eṉmaṉār pulavar. 19
ēḻākuvatē,
kaṇ eṉap peyariya vēṟṟumi kiḷavi
viṉi cey iṭattiṉ nilattiṉ kālattiṉ
aṉi vakik kuṟippiṉ tōṉṟum atuvē. 20
kaṇ kāl puṟam akam uḷ uḻi kīḻ mēl
piṉ cār ayal puṭi tēvaki eṉāa
muṉ iṭi kaṭi tali valam iṭam eṉāa
aṉṉa piṟavum ataṉ pāla eṉmaṉār. 21
vēṟṟumip poruḷi virikkum kāli
īṟṟu niṉṟu iyalum tokivayiṉ pirintu
pal āṟākap poruḷ puṇarntu icikkum
ellāc collum uriya eṉpa. 22

2.3. vēṟṟumimayaṅkiyal
karumam allāc cārpu eṉ kiḷavikku
urimiyum uṭittē kaṇ eṉ vēṟṟumi. 1
ciṉi nilik kiḷavikku aiyum kaṇṇum
viṉi nili okkum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 2
kaṉṟalum celavum oṉṟumār viṉiyē. 3
mutaṟciṉik kiḷavikku atu eṉ vēṟṟumi
mutaṟkaṇ variṉē ciṉikku ai varumē. 4
mutal muṉ ai variṉ kaṇ eṉ vēṟṟumi
ciṉi muṉ varutal teḷḷitu eṉpa. 5
mutalum ciṉiyum poruḷ vēṟupaṭāa
nuvalum kāli coṟkuṟippiṉavē. 6
piṇṭap peyarum āyiyal tiriyā
paṇṭu iyal maruṅkiṉ marīiya marapē. 7
oru viṉi oṭuc col uyarpiṉ vaḻittē. 8
mūṉṟaṉum aintaṉum tōṉṟak kūṟiya
ākkamoṭu puṇarnta ētuk kiḷavi
nōkku ōraṉiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 9
iraṇṭaṉ maruṅkiṉ nōkku al nōkkam av
iraṇṭaṉ maruṅkiṉ ētuvum ākum. 10
atu eṉ vēṟṟumi uyartiṇit tokivayiṉ
atu eṉ urupu keṭa kukaram varumē. 11
taṭumāṟu toḻiṟpeyarkku iraṇṭum mūṉṟum
kaṭi nili ilavē poruḷvayiṉāṉa. 12
īṟṟup peyar muṉṉar mey aṟi paṉuvaliṉ
vēṟṟumi teripa uṇarumōrē. 13
ōmpaṭik kiḷavikku aiyum āṉum
tām pirivu ilavē toki varu kāli. 14
āṟaṉ maruṅkiṉ vāḻccik kiḻamikku
ēḻum ākum uṟi nilattāṉa. 15
kut toka varūum koṭi etir kiḷavi
ap poruḷ āṟaṟku urittum ākum, 16
accak kiḷavikku aintum iraṇṭum
eccam ilavē poruḷvayiṉāṉa. 17
aṉṉa piṟavum tol neṟi piḻiyātu
urupiṉum poruḷiṉum mey taṭumāṟi
iru vayiṉ niliyum vēṟṟumi ellām
tiripu iṭaṉ ilavē teriyumōrkkē. 18
urupu toṭarntu aṭukkiya vēṟṟumik kiḷavi
oru col naṭiya poruḷ cel maruṅkē. 19
iṟutiyum iṭiyum ellā urupum
neṟi paṭu poruḷvayiṉ nilavutal variyār. 20
piṟitu piṟitu ēṟṟalum urupu toka varutalum
neṟipaṭa vaḻaṅkiya vaḻi maruṅku eṉpa. 21
aiyum kaṇṇum allāp poruḷvayiṉ
mey urupu tokāa iṟutiyāṉa. 22
yātaṉ urupiṉ kūṟiṟṟu āyiṉum
poruḷ cel maruṅkiṉ vēṟṟumi cārum. 23
etir maṟuttu moḻiyiṉum tamtam marapiṉ
poruḷ nili tiriyā vēṟṟumic collē. 24
ku ai āṉ eṉa varūum iṟuti
avvoṭu civaṇum ceyyuḷuḷḷē. 25
a eṉap piṟattal aḵṟiṇi maruṅkiṉ
kuvvum aiyum il eṉa moḻipa. 26
itaṉatu itu iṟṟu eṉṉum kiḷaviyum
ataṉik koḷḷum poruḷvayiṉāṉum
ataṉāṉ ceyaṟpaṭaṟku otta kiḷaviyum
muṟi koṇṭu eḻunta peyarccol kiḷaviyum
pāl vari kiḷaviyum paṇpiṉ ākkamum
kālattiṉ aṟiyum vēṟṟumik kiḷaviyum
paṟṟu viṭu kiḷaviyum tīrntu moḻik kiḷaviyum
aṉṉa piṟavum nāṉkaṉ urupiṉ
tol neṟi marapiṉa tōṉṟal āṟē. 27
ēṉi urupum aṉṉa marapiṉa
māṉam ilavē col muṟiyāṉa. 28
viṉiyē ceyvatu ceyappaṭuporuḷē
nilaṉē kālam karuvi eṉṟā
iṉṉataṟku itu payaṉ āka eṉṉum
aṉṉa marapiṉ iraṇṭoṭum tokii
āyeṭṭu eṉpa toḻil mutaṉiliyē. 29
avitām,
vaḻaṅku iyal maruṅkiṉ kuṉṟuva kuṉṟum. 30
mutaliṉ kūṟum ciṉi aṟi kiḷaviyum
ciṉiyiṉ kūṟum mutal aṟi kiḷaviyum
piṟantavaḻik kūṟalum paṇpu koḷ peyarum
iyaṉṟatu moḻitalum irupeyaroṭṭum
viṉimutal urikkum kiḷaviyoṭu tokii
aṉi marapiṉavē ākupeyark kiḷavi. 31
avitām,
tamtam poruḷvayiṉ tammoṭu civaṇalum
oppu il vaḻiyāṉ piṟitu poruḷ cuṭṭalum
ap paṇpiṉavē nuvalum kāli. 32
vēṟṟumi maruṅkiṉ pōṟṟal vēṇṭum. 33
aḷavu niṟiyum avaṟṟoṭu koḷvaḻi
uḷa eṉa moḻipa uṇarnticiṉōrē. 34
kiḷanta alla vēṟu piṟa tōṉṟiṉum
kiḷantavaṟṟu iyalāṉ uṇarntaṉar koḷalē. 35

2.4. viḷimarapu
viḷi eṉappaṭupa koḷḷum peyaroṭu
teḷiyat tōṉṟum iyaṟkiya eṉpa. 1
avvē,
iv eṉa aṟitaṟku mey peṟak kiḷappa. 2
avitām,
i u ai ō eṉṉum iṟuti
ap pāl nāṉkē uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ
meyp poruḷ cuṭṭiya viḷi koḷ peyarē.3
avaṟṟuḷ,
i ī ākum ai āy ākum. 4
ōvum uvvum ēyoṭu civaṇum. 5
ukaramtāṉē kuṟṟiyalukaram. 6
ēṉi uyirē uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ
tām viḷi koḷḷā eṉmaṉār pulavar. 7
aḷapeṭi mikūum ikara iṟu peyar
iyaṟkiya ākum ceyaṟkiya eṉpa. 8
muṟippeyar maruṅkiṉ ai eṉ iṟuti
āvoṭu varutaṟku uriyavum uḷavē. 9
aṇmic collē iyaṟki ākum. 10
ṉa ra la ḷa eṉṉum an nāṉku eṉpa
puḷḷi iṟuti viḷi koḷ peyarē. 11
ēṉip puḷḷi īṟu viḷi koḷḷā. 12
aṉ eṉ iṟuti ā ākummē. 13
aṇmic colliṟku akaramum ākum. 14
āṉ eṉ iṟuti iyaṟki ākum. 15
toḻiliṉ kūṟum āṉ eṉ iṟuti
āy ākummē viḷivayiṉāṉa. 16
paṇpu koḷ peyarum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 17
aḷapeṭip peyarē aḷapeṭi iyala. 18
muṟippeyark kiḷavi ēyoṭu varumē. 19
tāṉ eṉ peyarum cuṭṭumutaṟ peyarum
yāṉ eṉ peyarum viṉāviṉ peyarum
aṉṟi aṉittum viḷi kōḷ ilavē. 20
ārum aruvum īroṭu civaṇum. 21
toḻiṟpeyar āyiṉ ēkāram varutalum
vaḻukku iṉṟu eṉmaṉār vayaṅkiyōrē. 22
paṇpu koḷ peyarum ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 23
aḷapeṭip peyarē aḷapeṭi iyala. 24
cuṭṭumutaṟ peyarē muṉ kiḷantaṉṉa. 25
nummiṉ tiripeyar viṉāviṉ peyar eṉṟu
am muṟi iraṇṭum avaṟṟu iyalpu iyalum. 26
eñciya iraṇṭiṉ iṟutip peyarē
niṉṟa īṟṟu ayal nīṭṭam vēṇṭum. 27
ayal neṭitu āyiṉ iyaṟki ākum. 28
viṉiyiṉum paṇpiṉum
niṉiyat tōṉṟum āḷ eṉ iṟuti
āy ākummē viḷivayiṉāṉa. 29
muṟippeyark kiḷavi muṟippeyar iyala. 30
cuṭṭumutaṟ peyarum viṉāviṉ peyarum
muṉ kiḷantaṉṉa eṉmaṉār pulavar. 31
aḷapeṭip peyarē aḷapeṭi iyala. 32
kiḷanta iṟuti aḵṟiṇi viravuppeyar
viḷampiya neṟiya viḷikkum kāli. 33
puḷḷiyum uyirum iṟuti ākiya
aḵṟiṇi maruṅkiṉ ellāp peyarum
viḷi nili peṟūum kālam tōṉṟiṉ
teḷi nili uṭiya ēkāram varalē. 34
uḷa eṉappaṭṭa ellāp peyarum
aḷapu iṟantaṉavē viḷikkum kāli
cēymiyiṉ icikkum vaḻakkattāṉa. 35
amma eṉṉum aciccol nīṭṭam
am muṟippeyaroṭu civaṇātu āyiṉum
viḷiyoṭu koḷpa teḷiyumōrē. 36
ta na nu e eṉa avi mutal ākit
taṉmi kuṟitta ṉa ra ḷa eṉ iṟutiyum
aṉṉa piṟavum peyar nili variṉē
iṉmi vēṇṭum viḷiyoṭu koḷalē. 37

2.5. peyariyal
ellāc collum poruḷ kuṟittaṉavē. 1
poruṇmi teritalum coṉmi teritalum
colliṉ ākum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 2
teripu vēṟu niliyalum kuṟippiṉ tōṉṟalum
iru pāṟṟu eṉpa poruṇmi niliyē. 3
col eṉappaṭupa peyarē viṉi eṉṟu
āyiraṇṭu eṉpa aṟinticiṉōrē. 4
iṭiccol kiḷaviyum uriccol kiḷaviyum
avaṟṟu vaḻi maruṅkiṉ tōṉṟum eṉpa. 5
avaṟṟuḷ,
peyar eṉappaṭupavi teriyum kāli
uyartiṇikku urimiyum aḵṟiṇikku urimiyum
āyiru tiṇikkum ōraṉṉa urimiyum
am mū urupiṉa tōṉṟal āṟē. 6
iru tiṇip pirinta aim pāl kiḷavikkum
uriyavi uriya peyarvayiṉāṉa. 7
avvaḻi,
avaṉ ivaṉ uvaṉ eṉa varūum peyarum
avaḷ ivaḷ uvaḷ eṉa varūum peyarum
avar ivar uvar eṉa varūum peyarum
yāṉ yām nām eṉa varūum peyarum
yāvaṉ yāvaḷ yāvar eṉṉum
āvayiṉ mūṉṟoṭu ap patiṉintum
pāl aṟi vanta uyartiṇip peyarē. 8
āṇmi aṭutta makaṉ eṉ kiḷaviyum
peṇmi aṭutta makaḷ eṉ kiḷaviyum
peṇmi aṭutta ikara iṟutiyum
nam ūrntu varūum ikara aikāramum
muṟimi cuṭṭā makaṉum makaḷum
māntar makkaḷ eṉṉum peyarum
āṭūu makaṭūu āyiru peyarum
cuṭṭu mutal ākiya aṉṉum āṉum
avi mutal ākiya peṇṭu eṉ kiḷaviyum
oppoṭu varūum kiḷaviyoṭu tokii
ap patiṉintum avaṟṟu ōraṉṉa. 9
ellārum eṉṉum peyarnilik kiḷaviyum
ellīrum eṉṉum peyarnilik kiḷaviyum
peṇmi aṭutta makaṉ eṉ kiḷaviyum
aṉṉa iyala eṉmaṉār pulavar. 10
nilap peyar kuṭip peyar kuḻuviṉ peyarē
viṉip peyar uṭip peyar paṇpu koḷ peyarē
pallōrk kuṟitta muṟi nilip peyarē
pallōrk kuṟitta ciṉi nilip peyarē
pallōrk kuṟitta tiṇi nilip peyarē
kūṭi varu vaḻakkiṉ āṭu iyaṟ peyarē
iṉṟivar eṉṉum eṇṇiyaṟ peyaroṭu
aṉṟi aṉittum avaṟṟu iyalpiṉavē. 11
aṉṉa piṟavum uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ
paṉmiyum orumiyum pāl aṟi vanta
eṉṉa peyarum at tiṇiyavvē. 12
atu itu utu eṉa varūum peyarum
avi mutal ākiya āytap peyarum
avi ivi uvi eṉa varūum peyarum
avi mutal ākiya vakarap peyarum
yātu yā yāvi eṉṉum peyarum
āvayiṉ mūṉṟoṭu ap patiṉintum
pāl aṟi vanta aḵṟiṇip peyarē. 13
palla pala cila eṉṉum peyarum
uḷḷa illa eṉṉum peyarum
viṉip peyark kiḷaviyum paṇpu koḷ peyarum
iṉittu eṉak kiḷakkum eṇṇukkuṟip peyarum
oppiṉ ākiya peyarnili uḷappaṭa
ap pāl oṉpatum avaṟṟu ōraṉṉa. 14
kaḷḷoṭu civaṇum av iyaṟpeyarē
koḷ vaḻi uṭiya pala aṟi coṟkē. 15
aṉṉa piṟavum aḵṟiṇi maruṅkiṉ
paṉmiyum orumiyum pāl aṟi vanta
eṉṉa peyarum at tiṇiyavvē. 16
terinili uṭiya aḵṟiṇi iyaṟpeyar
orumiyum paṉmiyum viṉiyoṭu variṉē. 17
iru tiṇic coṟkum ōraṉṉa urimiyiṉ
tiripu vēṟupaṭūum ellāp peyarum
niṉiyum kāli tamtam marapiṉ
viṉiyoṭu allatu pāl teripu ilavē. 18
nika ḻūu niṉṟa palar vari kiḷaviyiṉ
uyartiṇi orumi tōṉṟalum urittē
aṉṉa marapiṉ viṉivayiṉāṉa. 19
iyaṟpeyar ciṉippeyar ciṉimutaṟpeyarē
muṟippeyark kiḷavi tāmē tāṉē
ellām nīyir nī eṉak kiḷantu
colliya alla piṟavum āaṅku
aṉṉavi tōṉṟiṉ avaṟṟoṭum koḷalē. 20
avaṟṟuḷ,
nāṉkē iyaṟpeyar nāṉkē ciṉippeyar
nāṉku eṉa moḻimaṉār ciṉimutaṟpeyarē
muṟippeyark kiḷavi iraṇṭu ākummē
ēṉip peyarē tamtam marapiṉa. 21
avitām,
peṇmi iyaṟpeyar āṇmi iyaṟpeyar
paṉmi iyaṟpeyar orumi iyaṟpeyar eṉṟu
an nāṉku eṉpa iyaṟpeyar niliyē. 22
peṇmic ciṉippeyar āṇmic ciṉippeyar
paṉmic ciṉippeyar orumic ciṉippeyar eṉṟu
an nāṉku eṉpa ciṉippeyar niliyē. 23
peṇmi cuṭṭiya ciṉimutaṟpeyarē
āṇmi cuṭṭiya ciṉimutaṟpeyarē
paṉmi cuṭṭiya ciṉimutaṟpeyarē
orumi cuṭṭiya ciṉimutaṟpeyar eṉṟu
an nāṉku eṉpa ciṉimutaṟpeyarē. 24
peṇmi muṟippeyar āṇmi muṟippeyar eṉṟu
āyiraṇṭu eṉpa muṟippeyar niliyē. 25
peṇmi cuṭṭiya ellāp peyarum
oṉṟaṟkum oruttikkum oṉṟiya niliyē. 26
āṇmi cuṭṭiya ellāp peyarum
oṉṟaṟkum oruvaṟkum oṉṟiya niliyē. 27
paṉmi cuṭṭiya ellāp peyarum
oṉṟē palavē oruvar eṉṉum
eṉṟu ip pāṟkum ōraṉṉavvē. 28
orumi cuṭṭiya ellāp peyarum
oṉṟaṟkum oruvarkkum oṉṟiya niliyē. 29
tām eṉ kiḷavi paṉmikku urittē. 30
tāṉ eṉ kiḷavi orumikku urittē. 31
ellām eṉṉum peyarnilik kiḷavi
palvaḻi nutaliya nilittu ākummē 32
taṉ uḷḷuṟutta paṉmikku allatu
uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ ākkam illi. 33
nīyir nī eṉa varūum kiḷavi
pāl teripu ilavē uṭaṉ moḻip poruḷa. 34
avaṟṟuḷ,
nī eṉ kiḷavi orumikku urittē. 35
ēṉik kiḷavi paṉmikku urittē. 36
oruvar eṉṉum peyarnilik kiḷavi
iru pāṟkum urittē teriyum kāli. 37
taṉmi cuṭṭiṉ paṉmikku ēṟkum. 38
iṉṉa peyarē ivi eṉal vēṇṭiṉ
muṉṉam cērtti muṟiyiṉ uṇartal. 39
makaṭūu maruṅkiṉ pāl tiri kiḷavi
makaṭūu iyaṟki toḻilvayiṉāṉa. 40
ā ō ākum peyarumār uḷavē
āyiṭaṉ aṟital ceyyuḷuḷḷē. 41
iṟiccip poruḷvayiṉ ceyyuḷuḷ kiḷakkum
iyaṟpeyark kiḷavi uyartiṇi cuṭṭā
nilattuvaḻi maruṅkiṉ tōṉṟalāṉa. 42
tiṇiyoṭu paḻakiya peyar alaṅkaṭiyē. 43

2.6. viṉiyiyal
viṉi eṉappaṭuvatu vēṟṟumi koḷḷātu
niṉiyum kāli kālamoṭu tōṉṟum. 1
kālamtāmē mūṉṟu eṉa moḻipa. 2
iṟappiṉ nikaḻviṉ etirviṉ eṉṟā
am muk kālamum kuṟippoṭum koḷḷum
meyn nili uṭiya tōṉṟalāṟē. 3
kuṟippiṉum viṉiyiṉum neṟippaṭat tōṉṟik
kālamoṭu varūum viṉiccol ellām
uyartiṇikku urimiyum aḵṟiṇikku urimiyum
āyiru tiṇikkum ōraṉṉa urimiyum
am mū urupiṉa tōṉṟalāṟē. 4
avitām,
am ām em ēm eṉṉum kiḷaviyum
ummoṭu varūum ka ṭa ta ṟa eṉṉum
an nāṟ kiḷaviyoṭu āyeṇ kiḷaviyum
paṉmi urikkum taṉmic collē. 5
ka ṭa ta ṟa eṉṉum
an nāṉku ūrnta kuṉṟiyalukaramoṭu
ēṉ al eṉa varūum ēḻum
taṉ viṉi urikkum taṉmic collē. 6
avaṟṟuḷ,
ceyku eṉ kiḷavi viṉiyoṭu muṭiyiṉum
av iyal tiriyātu eṉmaṉār pulavar. 7
aṉ āṉ aḷ āḷ eṉṉum nāṉkum
oruvar maruṅkiṉ paṭarkkic collē. 8
ar ār pa eṉa varūum mūṉṟum
pallōr maruṅkiṉ paṭarkkic collē. 9
mārik kiḷaviyum pallōr paṭarkki
kālak kiḷaviyoṭu muṭiyum eṉpa. 10
paṉmiyum orumiyum pāl aṟi vanta
an nāl aintum mūṉṟu tali iṭṭa
muṉṉuṟak kiḷanta uyartiṇiyavvē. 11
avaṟṟuḷ,
paṉmi urikkum taṉmik kiḷavi
eṇ iyal maruṅkiṉ tiripavi uḷavē. 12
yāar eṉṉum viṉāviṉ kiḷavi
at tiṇi maruṅkiṉ mup pāṟkum urittē. 13
pāl aṟi marapiṉ am mū īṟṟum
ā ō ākum ceyyuḷuḷḷē. 14
āy eṉ kiḷaviyum avaṟṟoṭu koḷḷum. 15
atuc col vēṟṟumi uṭimiyāṉum
kaṇ eṉ vēṟṟumi nilattiṉāṉum
oppiṉāṉum paṇpiṉāṉum eṉṟu
ap pāl kālam kuṟippoṭu tōṉṟum. 16
aṉmiyiṉ iṉmiyiṉ uṇmiyiṉ vaṉmiyiṉ
aṉṉa piṟavum kuṟippoṭu koḷḷum
eṉṉa kiḷaviyum kuṟippē kālam. 17
paṉmiyum orumiyum pāl aṟi vanta
aṉṉa marapiṉ kuṟippoṭu varūum
kālak kiḷavi uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ
mēlik kiḷaviyoṭu vēṟupāṭu ilavē. 18
a ā va eṉa varūum iṟuti
ap pāl mūṉṟē palavaṟṟup paṭarkki. 19
oṉṟaṉ paṭarkki ta ṟa ṭa ūrnta
kuṉṟiyalukarattu iṟuti ākum. 20
paṉmiyum orumiyum pāl aṟi vanta
am mū iraṇṭum aḵṟiṇiyavvē. 21
at tiṇi maruṅkiṉ iru pāl kiḷavikkum
okkum eṉpa evaṉ eṉ viṉāvē. 22
iṉṟu ila uṭiya eṉṉum kiḷaviyum
aṉṟu uṭittu alla eṉṉum kiḷaviyum
paṇpu koḷ kiḷaviyum uḷa eṉ kiḷaviyum
paṇpiṉ ākiya ciṉimutaṟ kiḷaviyum
oppoṭu varūum kiḷaviyoṭu tokii
ap pāl pattum kuṟippoṭu koḷḷum. 23
paṉmiyum orumiyum pāl aṟi vanta
aṉṉa marapiṉ kuṟippoṭu varūum
kālak kiḷavi aḵṟiṇi maruṅkiṉ
mēlik kiḷaviyoṭu vēṟupāṭu ilavē. 24
muṉṉili viyaṅkōḷ viṉiyeñcukiḷavi
iṉmi ceppal vēṟu eṉ kiḷavi
ceymmaṉa ceyyum ceyta eṉṉum
am muṟi niṉṟa āyeṇ kiḷaviyum
tiripu vēṟupaṭūum ceytiya āki
iru tiṇic coṟkum ōraṉṉa urimiya. 25
avaṟṟuḷ,
muṉṉilik kiḷavi
i ai āy eṉa varūum mūṉṟum
oppat tōṉṟum oruvarkkum oṉṟaṟkum. 26
ir īr miṉ eṉa varūum mūṉṟum
pallōr maruṅkiṉum palavaṟṟu maruṅkiṉum
col ōraṉiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 27
eñciya kiḷavi iṭattoṭu civaṇi
aim pāṟkum uriya tōṉṟal āṟē. 28
avaṟṟuḷ,
muṉṉili taṉmi āyīr iṭattoṭu
maṉṉātu ākum viyaṅkōṭ kiḷavi. 29
pallōr paṭarkki muṉṉili taṉmi
av vayiṉ mūṉṟum nikaḻum kālattuc
ceyyum eṉṉum kiḷaviyoṭu koḷḷā. 30
ceytu ceyyū ceypu ceyteṉa
ceyyiyar ceyyiya ceyiṉ ceya ceyaṟku eṉa
av vaki oṉpatum viṉiyeñcukiḷavi. 31
piṉ muṉ kāl kaṭi vaḻi iṭattu eṉṉum
aṉṉa marapiṉ kālam kaṇṇiya
eṉṉa kiḷaviyum avaṟṟu iyalpiṉavē. 32
avaṟṟuḷ,
mutal nili mūṉṟum viṉimutal muṭipiṉa. 33
am muk kiḷaviyum ciṉi viṉi tōṉṟiṉ
ciṉiyoṭu muṭiyā mutaloṭu muṭiyiṉum
viṉi ōraṉiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 34
ēṉi eccam viṉimutalāṉum
āṉ vantu iyiyum viṉiniliyāṉum
tām iyal maruṅkiṉ muṭiyum eṉpa. 35
pal muṟiyāṉum viṉiyeñcukiḷavi
col muṟi muṭiyātu aṭukkuna variṉum
muṉṉatu muṭiya muṭiyumaṉ poruḷē. 36
nilaṉum poruḷum kālamum karuviyum
viṉimutaṟ kiḷaviyum viṉiyum uḷappaṭa
av aṟu poruṭkum ōraṉṉa urimiya
ceyyum ceyta eṉṉum collē. 37
avaṟṟoṭu varu vaḻi ceyyum eṉ kiḷavi
mutaṟkaṇ varinta mū īṟṟum urittē. 38
peyareñcukiḷaviyum viṉiyeñcukiḷaviyum
etir maṟuttu moḻiyiṉum poruḷ nili tiriyā. 39
tamtam eccamoṭu civaṇum kuṟippiṉ
ec col āyiṉum iṭinili variyār. 40
avaṟṟuḷ,
ceyyum eṉṉum peyareñcukiḷavikku
meyyoṭum keṭumē īṟṟumici ukaram
av iṭaṉ aṟital eṉmaṉār pulavar. 41
ceytu eṉ eccattu iṟanta kālam
eytu iṭaṉ uṭittē vārāk kālam. 42
mun nilik kālamum tōṉṟum iyaṟki
em muṟic collum nikaḻum kālattu
meyn nilip potuc col kiḷattal vēṇṭum. 43
vārāk kālattum nikaḻum kālattum
ōrāṅku varūum viṉiccoṟ kiḷavi
iṟanta kālattuk kuṟippoṭu kiḷattal
virinta poruḷa eṉmaṉār pulavar. 44
mikkataṉ maruṅkiṉ viṉiccol cuṭṭi
ap paṇpu kuṟitta viṉimutaṟ kiḷavi
ceyvatu il vaḻi nikaḻum kālattu
mey peṟat tōṉṟum poruṭṭu ākummē. 45
itu ceyal vēṇṭum eṉṉum kiḷavi
iru vayiṉ niliyum poruṭṭu ākummē
taṉ pālāṉum piṟaṉ pālāṉum. 46
vaṉpuṟa varūum viṉā uṭi viṉiccol
etir maṟuttu uṇarttutaṟku urimiyum uṭittē. 47
vārāk kālattu viṉiccol kiḷavi
iṟappiṉum nikaḻviṉum ciṟappat tōṉṟum
iyaṟkiyum teḷivum kiḷakkum kāli. 48
ceyappaṭuporuḷic ceytatu pōlat
toḻiṟpaṭak kiḷattalum vaḻakku iyal marapē. 49
iṟappē etirvē āyiru kālamum
ciṟappat tōṉṟum mayaṅkumoḻik kiḷavi. 50
ēṉik kālamum mayaṅkutal variyār. 51

2.7. iṭiyiyal
iṭi eṉappaṭupa peyaroṭum viṉiyoṭum
naṭipeṟṟu iyalum tamakku iyalpu ilavē. 1
avitām,

aḷapiṉ eṭutta iciya eṉpa. 13
maṟṟu eṉ kiḷavi viṉimāṟṟu acinili
ap pāl iraṇṭu eṉa moḻimaṉār pulavar. 14
eṟṟu eṉ kiḷavi iṟanta poruṭṭē. 15
maṟṟiyatu eṉṉum kiḷavitāṉē
cuṭṭu nili oḻiya iṉam kuṟittaṉṟē. 16

tañcak kiḷavi eṇmip poruṭṭē. 18

āvayiṉ āṟum muṉṉili aciccol. 26

aṉṉa piṟavum kuṟippoṭu koḷḷum. 34

tattamuḷ mayaṅkum uṭaṉili ilavē. 35

tirintu vēṟu variṉum terintaṉar koḷalē. 47

kiḷantavaṟṟu iyalāṉ uṇarntaṉar koḷalē. 48

2.8. uriyiyal
uriccol kiḷavi virikkum kāli
iciyiṉum kuṟippiṉum paṇpiṉum tōṉṟi
peyariṉum viṉiyiṉum mey taṭumāṟi
oru col pala poruṭku urimi tōṉṟiṉum
pala col oru poruṭku urimi tōṉṟiṉum
payilātavaṟṟip payiṉṟavi cārtti
tamtam marapiṉ ceṉṟu nili maruṅkiṉ
ec col āyiṉum poruḷ vēṟu kiḷattal. 1
veḷippaṭu collē kiḷattal vēṇṭā
veḷippaṭa vārā uriccol mēṉa. 2
avitām,
uṟu tava naṉi eṉa varūum mūṉṟum
mikuti ceyyum poruḷa eṉpa. 3
uru uṭku ākum puri uyarpu ākum. 4
kuruvum keḻuvum niṟaṉ ākummē. 5
callal iṉṉal iṉṉāmiyē. 6

ukappē uyartal uvappē uvaki. 8
payappē payaṉ ām. 9
pacappu niṟaṉ ākum. 10

icippu ici ākum. 12
alamaral terumaral āyiraṇṭum cuḻaṟci. 13
maḻavum kuḻavum iḷamip poruḷa. 14
cīrtti miku pukaḻ māli iyalpē. 15
kūrppum kaḻivum uḷḷatu ciṟakkum. 16
kataḻvum tuṉivum viriviṉ poruḷa. 17
atirvum vitirppum naṭukkam ceyyum. 18
vārtal pōkal oḻukal mūṉṟum
nērpum neṭumiyum ceyyum poruḷa. 19
tīrtalum tīrttalum viṭal poruṭṭu ākum. 20
keṭavaral paṇṇi āyiraṇṭum viḷiyāṭṭu. 21
taṭavum kayavum naḷiyum perumi. 22
avaṟṟuḷ,
taṭa eṉ kiḷavi kōṭṭamum ceyyum. 23
kaya eṉ kiḷavi meṉmiyum ceyyum. 24
naḷi eṉ kiḷavi ceṟivum ākum. 25
paḻutu payam iṉṟē. 26
cāyal meṉmi. 27
muḻutu eṉ kiḷavi eñcāp poruṭṭē. 28
vampu nili iṉmi. 29
mātar kātal. 30
nampum mēvum naci ākummē. 31
ōytal āytal niḻattal cāay
āvayiṉ nāṉkum uḷḷataṉ nuṇukkam. 32
pulampē taṉimi. 33
tuvaṉṟu niṟivu ākum. 34
murañcal mutirvē. 35
vemmi vēṇṭal. 36
poṟpē polivu. 37
vaṟitu ciṟitu ākum. 38
eṟṟam niṉivum tuṇivum ākum. 39
piṇiyum pēṇum peṭpiṉ poruḷa. 40
paṇiyē piḻittal peruppum ākum. 41
paṭarē uḷḷal celavum ākum. 42
piyuḷum ciṟumiyum nōyiṉ poruḷa. 43
eyyāmiyē aṟiyāmiyē. 44
naṉṟu peritu ākum. 45
tāvē valiyum varuttamum ākum 46
tevuk koḷal poruṭṭē. 47
tevvup paki ākum. 48
viṟappum uṟappum veṟuppum ceṟivē. 49
avaṟṟuḷ,
viṟappē verūup poruṭṭum ākum. 50
kampali cummi kaliyē aḻuṅkal
eṉṟu ivi nāṉkum aravap poruḷa. 51
avaṟṟuḷ,
aḻuṅkal irakkamum kēṭum ākum. 52
kaḻum eṉ kiḷavi mayakkam ceyyum. 53
ceḻumi vaḷaṉum koḻuppum ākum. 54
viḻumam cīrmiyum ciṟappum iṭumpiyum. 55
karuvi tokuti. 56
kama niṟintu iyalum. 57
ariyē aimmi. 58
kavavu akattiṭumē. 59
tuvittalum cilittalum iyampalum iraṅkalum
icip poruṭ kiḷavi eṉmaṉār pulavar. 60
avaṟṟuḷ,
iraṅkal kaḻinta poruṭṭum ākum. 61
ilampāṭu oṟkam āyiraṇṭum vaṟumi. 62
ñemirtalum pāytalum parattal poruḷa. 63
kavarvu viruppu ākum. 64
cērē tiraṭci. 65
viyal eṉ kiḷavi akalap poruṭṭē. 66
pēm nām urum eṉa varūum kiḷavi
ā muṟi mūṉṟum accap poruḷa. 67
vaya vali ākum. 68

tuya eṉ kiḷavi aṟiviṉ tiripē. 70
uyāvē uyaṅkal. 71
ucāvē cūḻcci. 72

kaṟuppum civappum vekuḷip poruḷa. 74
niṟattu uru uṇarttaṟkum uriya eṉpa. 75
nocivum nuḻivum nuṇaṅkum nuṇmi. 76
puṉiṟu eṉ kiḷavi īṉṟaṇimip poruṭṭē. 77
naṉavē kaḷaṉum akalamum ceyyum. 78
matavē maṭaṉum valiyum ākum. 79
mikutiyum vaṉappum ākalum urittē. 80
putitupaṭal poruṭṭē yāṇark kiḷavi. 81
amartal mēval. 82
yāṇuk kaviṉ ām. 83
paravum paḻiccum vaḻuttiṉ poruḷa. 84
kaṭi eṉ kiḷavi
varivē kūrmi kāppē putumi
virivē viḷakkam mikuti ciṟappē
accam muṉtēṟṟu āyīr aintum
meyppaṭat tōṉṟum poruṭṭu ākummē. 85
aiyamum karippum ākalum urittē. 86
ai viyappu ākum. 87
muṉivu muṉivu ākum. 88
viyē kūrmi. 89
eṟuḻ vali ākum. 90
mey peṟak kiḷanta uriccol ellām
muṉṉum piṉṉum varupavi nāṭi
otta moḻiyāṉ puṇarttaṉar uṇarttal
tamtam marapiṉ tōṉṟummaṉ poruḷē. 91
kūṟiya kiḷavip poruḷ nili alla
vēṟu piṟa tōṉṟiṉum avaṟṟoṭu koḷalē. 92
poruṭkup poruḷ teriyiṉ atu varampu iṉṟē 93
poruṭkut tiripu illi uṇartta valliṉ. 94
uṇarcci vāyil uṇarvōr valittē. 95
moḻip poruṭ kāraṇam viḻippat tōṉṟā. 96
eḻuttup pirintu icittal ivaṇ iyalpu iṉṟē 97
aṉṉa piṟavum kiḷanta alla
pal muṟiyāṉum parantaṉa varūum
uriccol ellām poruṭkuṟi kūṭṭa
iyaṉṟa maruṅkiṉ iṉittu eṉa aṟiyum
varampu tamakku iṉmiyiṉ vaḻi naṉi kaṭippiṭittu
ōmpaṭi āṇiyiṉ kiḷantavaṟṟu iyalāṉ
pāṅkuṟa uṇartal eṉmaṉār pulavar. 98

2.9. eccaviyal
iyaṟcol tiricol ticiccol vaṭacol eṉṟu
aṉittē ceyyuḷ īṭṭac collē. 1
avaṟṟuḷ,
iyaṟcoltāmē
centamiḻ nilattu vaḻakkoṭu civaṇi
tam poruḷ vaḻāmi icikkum collē. 2
oru poruḷ kuṟitta vēṟu col ākiyum
vēṟu poruḷ kuṟitta oru col ākiyum
iru pāṟṟu eṉpa tiricol kiḷavi. 3
centamiḻ cērnta paṉṉiru nilattum
tam kuṟippiṉavē ticiccol kiḷavi. 4
vaṭacol kiḷavi vaṭa eḻuttu orīi
eḻuttoṭu puṇarnta col ākummē. 5
citintaṉa variṉum iyintaṉa variyār. 6
an nāṟ collum toṭukkum kāli
valikkum vaḻi valittalum melikkum vaḻi melittalum
virikkum vaḻi virittalum tokukkum vaḻit tokuttalum
nīṭṭum vaḻi nīṭṭalum kuṟukkum vaḻik kuṟukkalum
nāṭṭal valiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 7
niralniṟi cuṇṇam aṭimaṟi moḻimāṟṟu
avi nāṉku eṉpa moḻi puṇar iyalpē. 8
avaṟṟuḷ,
niralniṟitāṉē
viṉiyiṉum peyariṉum niṉiyat tōṉṟi
col vēṟu nilii poruḷ vēṟu niliyal. 9
cuṇṇamtāṉē
paṭṭāṅku aminta īr aṭi eṇ cīr
oṭṭu vaḻi aṟintu tuṇittaṉar iyaṟṟal. 10
aṭimaṟic ceyti aṭi nili tirintu
cīr nili tiriyātu taṭumāṟummē. 11
poruḷ teri maruṅkiṉ
īṟṟu aṭi iṟu cīr eruttuvayiṉ tiriyum
tōṟṟamum variyār aṭimaṟiyāṉa. 12
moḻimāṟṟu iyaṟki
col nili māṟṟi poruḷ etir iyiya
muṉṉum piṉṉum koḷ vaḻik koḷāal. 13
ta na nu e eṉum avi mutal ākiya
kiḷi nutaṟ peyarum pirippap piriyā. 14
iciniṟi acinili poruḷoṭu puṇartal eṉṟu
avi mūṉṟu eṉpa oru col aṭukkē. 15
vēṟṟumittokiyē uvamattokiyē
viṉiyiṉtokiyē paṇpiṉtokiyē
ummittokiyē aṉmoḻittoki eṉṟu
av āṟu eṉpa tokimoḻi niliyē. 16
avaṟṟuḷ,
vēṟṟumittokiyē vēṟṟumi iyala. 17
uvamattokiyē uvama iyala. 18
viṉiyiṉtokuti kālattu iyalum. 19
vaṇṇattiṉ vaṭiviṉ aḷaviṉ cuviyiṉ eṉṟu
aṉṉa piṟavum ataṉ kuṇam nutali
iṉṉatu itu eṉa varūum iyaṟki
eṉṉa kiḷaviyum paṇpiṉtokiyē. 20
iru peyar pala peyar aḷaviṉpeyarē
eṇṇiyaṟpeyarē niṟippeyark kiḷavi
eṇṇiṉpeyaroṭu av aṟu kiḷaviyum
kaṇṇiya nilittē ummittokiyē. 21
paṇpu toka varūum kiḷaviyāṉum
ummi tokka peyarvayiṉāṉum
vēṟṟumi tokka peyarvayiṉāṉum
īṟṟu niṉṟu iyalum aṉmoḻittokiyē. 22
avitām,
muṉ moḻi niliyalum piṉ moḻi niliyalum
iru moḻi mēlum oruṅkuṭaṉ niliyalum
am moḻi niliyātu al moḻi niliyalum
an nāṉku eṉpa poruḷ nili marapē. 23
ellāt tokiyum oru col naṭiya. 24
uyartiṇi maruṅkiṉ ummittokiyē
palarcol naṭittu eṉa moḻimaṉār pulavar. 25
vārā marapiṉa varak kūṟutalum
eṉṉā marapiṉa eṉak kūṟutalum
aṉṉavi ellām avaṟṟu avaṟṟu iyalpāṉ
iṉṉa eṉṉum kuṟippuri ākum. 26
icip paṭu poruḷē nāṉku varampu ākum. 27
viri col aṭukkē mūṉṟu varampu ākum. 28
kaṇṭīr eṉṟā koṇṭīr eṉṟā
ceṉṟatu eṉṟā pōyiṟṟu eṉṟā
aṉṟi aṉittum viṉāvoṭu civaṇi
niṉṟa vaḻi acikkum kiḷavi eṉpa. 29
kēṭṭi eṉṟā niṉṟi eṉṟā
kātti eṉṟā kaṇṭi eṉṟā
aṉṟi aṉittum muṉṉili al vaḻi
muṉṉuṟak kiḷanta iyalpu ākummē. 30
iṟappiṉ nikaḻviṉ etirviṉ eṉṟa
ciṟappuṭi marapiṉ am muk kālamum
taṉmi muṉṉili paṭarkki eṉṉum
am mū iṭattāṉ viṉiyiṉum kuṟippiṉum
meymmiyāṉum iv iraṇṭu ākum
av āṟu eṉpa muṟṟu iyal moḻiyē. 31
ev vayiṉ viṉiyum av iyal niliyum. 32
avitām,
tamtam kiḷavi aṭukkuna variṉum
et tiṟattāṉum peyar muṭipiṉavē. 33
pirinili viṉiyē peyarē oḻiyici
etirmaṟi ummi eṉavē collē
kuṟippē iciyē āyīr aintum
neṟippaṭat tōṉṟum eñcu poruṭ kiḷavi. 34
avaṟṟuḷ,
pirinili eccam pirinili muṭipiṉa. 35
viṉiyeñcukiḷavikku viṉiyum kuṟippum
niṉiyat tōṉṟiya muṭipu ākummē
āvayiṉ kuṟippē ākkamoṭu varumē. 36
peyareñcukiḷavi peyaroṭu muṭimē. 37
oḻiyici eccam oḻiyici muṭipiṉa. 38
etirmaṟi eccam etirmaṟi muṭipiṉa. 39
ummi eccam iru īṟṟāṉum
taṉ viṉi oṉṟiya muṭipu ākummē. 40
taṉmēl ceñcol varūum kāli
nikaḻum kālamoṭu vārāk kālamum
iṟanta kālamoṭu vārāk kālamum
mayaṅkutal variyār muṟiniliyāṉa. 41
eṉa eṉ eccam viṉiyoṭu muṭimē. 42
eñciya mūṉṟum mēl vantu muṭikkum
eñcu poruṭ kiḷavi ila eṉa moḻipa. 43
avitām,
tamtam kuṟippiṉ eccam ceppum. 44
col eṉ eccam muṉṉum piṉṉum
col aḷavu allatu eñcutal iṉṟē. 45
aviyal kiḷavi maṟittaṉar kiḷattal. 46
maṟikkum kāli marīiyatu orāal. 47
ī tā koṭu eṉak kiḷakkum mūṉṟum
iraviṉ kiḷavi āku iṭaṉ uṭiya. 48
avaṟṟuḷ,
ī eṉ kiḷavi iḻintōṉ kūṟṟē. 49
tā eṉ kiḷavi oppōṉ kūṟṟē. 50
koṭu eṉ kiḷavi uyarntōṉ kūṟṟē. 51
koṭu eṉ kiḷavi paṭarkki āyiṉum
taṉṉip piṟaṉ pōl kūṟum kuṟippiṉ
taṉṉiṭattu iyalum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 52
peyarnilik kiḷaviyiṉ āakunavum
ticinili kiḷaviyiṉ āakunavum
tol neṟi moḻivayiṉ āakunavum
meyn nili mayakkiṉ āakunavum
mantirap poruḷvayiṉ āakunavum
aṉṟi aṉittum kaṭappāṭu ilavē. 53
ceyyāy eṉṉum muṉṉili viṉiccol

vēntu viṉi iyaṟki vēntaṉ orīiya
ēṉōr maruṅkiṉum eytu iṭaṉ uṭittē. 34

tōḻi tēettum kaṇṭō r pāṅkiṉum

ākiya kiḷaviyum av vaḻi uriya. 39

tāmē cellum tāyarum uḷarē. 40

cēy nilikku akaṉṟōr celaviṉum varaviṉum
kaṇṭō r moḻital kaṇṭatu eṉpa. 43
oṉṟāt tamariṉum paruvattum curattum

kaṭik koṇṭu peyartaliṉ kalaṅku añar eytik
kaṟpoṭu puṇarnta kuvi uḷappaṭa
ap pāl paṭṭa oru tiṟattāṉum
nāḷatu ciṉmiyum iḷamiyatu arumiyum
tāḷāṇ pakkamum takutiyatu amitiyum
iṉmiyatu iḷivum uṭimiyatu uyarcciyum

ūtiyam karutiya oru tiṟattāṉum
pukaḻum māṉamum eṭuttu vaṟpuṟuttalum
tūtu iṭiyiṭṭa vakiyiṉāṉum
ākit tōṉṟum pāṅkōr pāṅkiṉum

kāvaṟ pāṅkiṉ āṅkōr pakkamum
parattiyiṉ akaṟciyiṉ parintōṭ kuṟuki
irattalum teḷittalum eṉa iru vakiyoṭu
urit tiṟa nāṭṭam kiḻavōṉ mēṉa. 44
eñciyōrkkum eñcutal ilavē. 45
nikaḻntatu niṉittaṟku ētuvum ākum. 46

marapunili tiriyā māṭciya āki

3.2. puṟattiṇiyiyal
akattiṇi maruṅkiṉ aril tapa uṇarntōr
puṟattiṇi ilakkaṇam tiṟappaṭak kiḷappiṉ
veṭcitāṉē kuṟiñciyatu puṟaṉē
uṭku varat tōṉṟum īr ēḻ tuṟittē. 1
vēntu viṭu muṉiñar vēṟṟup pulak kaḷaviṉ
ā tantu ōmpal mēvaṟṟu ākum. 2
paṭi iyaṅku aravam pākkattu viricci
puṭi keṭap pōkiya celavē puṭi keṭa
oṟṟiṉ ākiya vēyē vēyppuṟam
muṟṟiṉ ākiya puṟattu iṟi muṟṟiya
ūr koli ā kōḷ pūcal māṟṟē
nōy iṉṟu uyttal nuvalvaḻit tōṟṟam
tantu niṟi pātīṭu uṇṭāṭṭu koṭi eṉa
vanta īr ēḻ vakiyiṟṟu ākum. 3
maṟam kaṭikkūṭṭiya kuṭinili ciṟanta
koṟṟavi niliyum at tiṇip puṟaṉē. 4
veṟi aṟi ciṟappiṉ vev vāy vēlaṉ
veṟiyāṭṭu ayarnta kāntaḷum uṟu paki
vēntiṭi terital vēṇṭi ēntu pukaḻ
pōnti vēmpē ār eṉa varūum
mā peruntāṉiyar malinta pūvum
vāṭā vaḷḷi vayavar ēttiya
ōṭāk kaḻal nili uḷappaṭa ōṭā
uṭal vēntu aṭukkiya uṉṉa niliyum
māyōṉ mēya maṉ peruñ ciṟappiṉ
tāvā viḻup pukaḻp pūvi niliyum
ār amar ōṭṭalum ā peyarttut tarutalum
cīr cāl vēntaṉ ciṟappu eṭuttu urittalum
talit tāḷ neṭumoḻi taṉṉoṭu puṇarttalum
aṉikku uri marapiṉatu karanti aṉṟiyum
varu tār tāṅkal vāḷ vāyttuk kaviḻtal eṉṟu
iru vakip paṭṭa piḷḷi niliyum
vāḷ malintu eḻuntōṉi makiḻntu paṟi tūṅka
nāṭu avaṟku aruḷiya piḷḷiyāṭṭum
kāṭci kālkōḷ nīrppaṭi naṭutal
cīrtta marapiṉ perumpaṭi vāḻttal eṉṟu
iru mūṉṟu marapiṉ kalloṭu puṇarac
collappaṭṭa eḻu mūṉṟu tuṟittē. 5
vañcitāṉē mulliyatu puṟaṉē
eñcā maṇ naci vēntaṉi vēntaṉ
añcu takat talic ceṉṟu aṭal kuṟittaṉṟē. 6
iyaṅku paṭi aravam eri parantu eṭuttal
vayaṅkal eytiya perumiyāṉum
koṭuttal eytiya koṭimiyāṉum
aṭuttu ūrntu aṭṭa koṟṟattāṉum
mārāyam peṟṟa neṭumoḻiyāṉum
poruḷiṉṟu uytta pērāṇ pakkamum
varu vicip puṉalik kaṟ ciṟi pōla
oruvaṉ tāṅkiya perumiyāṉum
piṇṭam mēya peruñcōṟṟu niliyum
veṉṟōr viḷakkamum tōṟṟōr tēyvum
kuṉṟāc ciṟappiṉ koṟṟa vaḷḷiyum
aḻi paṭi taṭṭō r taḻiñciyoṭu tokii
kaḻi peruñ ciṟappiṉ tuṟi patiṉmūṉṟē. 7
uḻiñitāṉē marutattup puṟaṉē
muḻu mutal araṇam muṟṟalum kōṭalum
aṉi neṟi marapiṟṟu ākum eṉpa. 8
atuvētāṉum iru nāl vakittē. 9
koḷḷār tēem kuṟitta koṟṟamum
uḷḷiyatu muṭikkum vēntaṉatu ciṟappum
tol eyiṟku ivartalum tōlatu perukkamum
akattōṉ celvamum aṉṟiyum muraṇiya
puṟattōṉ aṇaṅkiya pakkamum tiṟal paṭa
oru tāṉ maṇṭiya kuṟumiyum uṭaṉṟōr
varu paki pēṇār ār eyil uḷappaṭa
collappaṭṭa nāl iru vakittē. 10
kuṭiyum vāḷum nāḷkōḷ aṉṟi
maṭi ami ēṇimici mayakkamum kaṭiic
cuṟṟu amar oḻiya veṉṟu kikkoṇṭu
muṟṟiya mutirvum aṉṟi muṟṟiya
akattōṉ vīḻnta nocciyum maṟṟu ataṉ
puṟattōṉ vīḻnta putumiyāṉum
nīrc ceru vīḻnta pāciyum atāaṉṟu
ūrc ceru vīḻnta maṟṟataṉ maṟaṉum
matilmicikku ivarnta mēlōr pakkamum
ikal matil kuṭumi koṇṭa maṇṇumaṅkalamum
veṉṟa vāḷiṉ maṇṇoṭu oṉṟa
tokinili eṉṉum tuṟiyoṭu tokii
vaki nāl mūṉṟē tuṟi eṉa moḻipa. 11
tumpitāṉē neytalatu puṟaṉē
mintu poruḷāka vanta vēntaṉic
ceṉṟu tali aḻikkum ciṟappiṟṟu eṉpa. 12
kaṇiyum vēlum tuṇiyuṟa moyttaliṉ
ceṉṟa uyiriṉ niṉṟa yākki
iru nilam tīṇṭā aru nili vakiyoṭu
iru pāṟ paṭṭa oru ciṟappiṉṟē. 13
tāṉi yāṉi kutiri eṉṟa
nōṉār uṭkum mū vaki niliyum
vēl miku vēntaṉi moyttavaḻi oruvaṉ
tāṉ mīṇṭu eṟinta tār nili aṉṟiyum
iruvar talivar taputip pakkamum
oruvaṉ oruvaṉi uṭi paṭi pukku
kūḻi tāṅkiya erumiyum paṭi aṟuttu
pāḻi koḷḷum ēmattāṉum
kaḷiṟu eṟintu etirntōr pāṭum kaḷiṟṟoṭu
paṭṭa vēntaṉi aṭṭa vēntaṉ
vāḷōr āṭum amaliyum vāḷ vāyttu
iru peru vēntartāmum cuṟṟamum
oruvarum oḻiyāt tokinilikkaṇṇum
ceruvakattu iṟivaṉ vīḻnteṉa ciṉii
oruvaṉ maṇṭiya nal ici niliyum
pal paṭi oruvaṟku uṭitaliṉ maṟṟavaṉ
oḷ vāḷ vīciya nūḻilum uḷappaṭap
pullit tōṉṟum paṉṉiru tuṟittē. 14
vākitāṉē pāliyatu puṟaṉē
tā il koḷkit tamtam kūṟṟip
pākupaṭa mikutip paṭuttal eṉpa. 15
aṟu vakip paṭṭa pārppaṉap pakkamum
ai vaki marapiṉ aracar pakkamum
iru mūṉṟu marapiṉ ēṉōr pakkamum
maṟu il ceyti mū vakik kālamum
neṟiyiṉ āṟṟiya aṟivaṉ tēyamum
nāl iru vaḻakkiṉ tāpatap pakkamum
pāl aṟi marapiṉ porunarkaṇṇum
aṉi nili vakiyoṭu āṅku eḻu vakiyāṉ
toki nilipeṟṟatu eṉmaṉār pulavar. 16
kūtir vēṉil eṉṟu iru pācaṟik
kātaliṉ oṉṟik kaṇṇiya vakiyiṉum
ērōr kaḷavaḻi aṉṟi kaḷavaḻit
tērōr tōṟṟiya veṉṟiyum tērōr
veṉṟa kōmāṉ muṉtērk kuraviyum
oṉṟiya marapiṉ piṉtērk kuraviyum
perum paki tāṅkum vēliṉāṉum
arum paki tāṅkum āṟṟalāṉum
pullā vāḻkki vallāṇ pakkamum
ollār nāṇa periyavark kaṇṇic
colliya vakiyiṉ oṉṟoṭu puṇarntu
tol uyir vaḻaṅkiya avippaliyāṉum
ollār iṭavayiṉ pulliya pāṅkiṉum
pakaṭṭiṉāṉum āviṉāṉum
tukaḷ tapu ciṟappiṉ cāṉṟōr pakkamum
kaṭi maṉi nītta pāliṉkaṇṇum
eṭṭu vaki nutaliya aviyakattāṉum
kaṭṭu ami oḻukkattuk kaṇṇumiyāṉum
iṭi il vaṇ pukaḻk koṭimiyāṉum
piḻittōrt tāṅkum kāvalāṉum
poruḷoṭu puṇarnta pakkattāṉum
aruḷoṭu puṇarnta akaṟciyāṉum
kāmam nītta pāliṉāṉum eṉṟu
iru pāṟ paṭṭa oṉpatiṉ tuṟittē. 17
kāñcitāṉē peruntiṇip puṟaṉē
pāṅku aruñ ciṟappiṉ pal āṟṟāṉum
nillā ulakam pulliya neṟittē. 18
māṟṟa aruṅ kūṟṟam cāṟṟiya perumiyum
kaḻintōr oḻintōrkkuk kāṭṭiya mutumiyum
paṇpu uṟa varūum pakuti nōkkip
puṇ kiḻittu muṭiyum maṟattiṉāṉum
ēmac cuṟṟam iṉṟip puṇṇōṟ
pēey ōmpiya pēeyp pakkamum
iṉṉaṉ eṉṟu iraṅkiya maṉṉiyāṉum
iṉṉatu piḻippiṉ itu ākiyar eṉat
tuṉṉa aruñ ciṟappiṉ vañciṉattāṉum

3.3. kaḷaviyal
iṉpamum poruḷum aṟaṉum eṉṟāṅku
aṉpoṭu puṇarnta aintiṇi maruṅkiṉ
kāmak kūṭṭam kāṇum kāli
maṟiyōr tēettu maṉṟal eṭṭaṉuḷ
tuṟi ami nal yāḻt tuṇimiyōr iyalpē.1
oṉṟē vēṟē eṉṟu iru pālvayiṉ
oṉṟi uyarnta pālatu āṇiyiṉ
otta kiḻavaṉum kiḻattiyum kāṇpa
mikkōṉ āyiṉum kaṭi vari iṉṟē. 2
ciṟantuḻi aiyam ciṟantatu eṉpa
iḻintuḻi iḻipē cuṭṭalāṉa. 3
vaṇṭē iḻiyē vaḷḷi pūvē
kaṇṇē alamaral imippē accam eṉṟu
aṉṉavi piṟavum āṅkaṇ nikaḻa
niṉṟavi kaḷiyum karuvi eṉpa. 4
nāṭṭam iraṇṭum aṟivu uṭampaṭuttaṟkuk
kūṭṭi urikkum kuṟippuri ākum. 5
kuṟippē kuṟittatu koḷḷum āyiṉ
āṅku avi nikaḻum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 6
perumiyum uraṉum āṭūu mēṉa. 7
accamum nāṇum maṭaṉum muntuṟutal
niccamum peṇpāṟku uriya eṉpa. 8
vēṭki orutali uḷḷutal melital
ākkam ceppal nāṇu vari iṟattal
nōkkuva ellām aviyē pōṟal
maṟattal mayakkam cākkāṭu eṉṟu ic
ciṟappuṭi marapiṉavi kaḷavu eṉa moḻipa. 9
muṉṉili ākkal colvaḻippaṭuttal
nal nayam urittal naki naṉi uṟāa
an nili aṟital melivu viḷakkuṟuttal
taṉ nili urittal teḷivu akappaṭuttal eṉṟu
iṉṉavi nikaḻum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 10
mey toṭṭup payiṟal poy pārāṭṭal
iṭam peṟṟut taḻāal iṭiyūṟu kiḷattal
nīṭu niṉintu iraṅkal kūṭutal uṟutal
colliya nukarcci vallē peṟṟuḻit
tīrāt tēṟṟam uḷappaṭat tokii
pērāc ciṟappiṉ iru nāṉku kiḷaviyum
peṟṟavaḻi makiḻcciyum pirintavaḻik kalaṅkalum
niṟpavi niṉii nikaḻpavi urippiṉum
kuṟṟam kāṭṭiya vāyil peṭpiṉum
peṭṭa vāyil peṟṟu iravu valiyuṟuppiṉum
ūrum pērum keṭutiyum piṟavum
nīriṉ kuṟippiṉ nirampak kūṟit
tōḻiyik kuṟiyuṟum pakutiyum tōḻi
kuṟi avaṭ cārtti meyyuṟak kūṟalum
taṇṭātu irappiṉum maṟṟiya vaḻiyum
col avaṭ cārttaliṉ pulliya vakiyiṉum
aṟintōḷ ayarppiṉ av vaḻi maruṅkiṉ
kēṭum pīṭum kūṟalum tōḻi
nīkkaliṉ ākiya nilimiyum nōkki
maṭal mā kūṟum iṭaṉumār uṇṭē. 11
paṇpiṉ peyarppiṉum parivuṟṟu meliyiṉum
aṉpuṟṟu nakiṉum avaṭ peṟṟu maliyiṉum
āṟṟiṭi uṟutalum av viṉikku iyalpē. 12
pāṅkaṉ nimittam paṉṉiraṇṭu eṉpa. 13
muṉṉiya mūṉṟum kikkiḷik kuṟippē. 14
piṉṉar nāṉkum peruntiṇi peṟumē. 15
mutaloṭu puṇarnta yāḻōr mēṉa
taval aruñ ciṟappiṉ ain nilam peṟumē. 16
iru vakik kuṟi piḻippu ākiya iṭattum
kāṇā vakiyiṉ poḻutu naṉi ikappiṉum
tāṉ akam pukāaṉ peyartal iṉmiyiṉ
kāṭci āciyiṉ kaḷam pukkuk kalaṅki
vēṭkiyiṉ mayaṅkik kiyaṟu poḻutiṉum
pukāk kālip pukku etirppaṭṭuḻi
pakāa viruntiṉ pakutikkaṇṇum
vēḷāṇ etirum viruppiṉkaṇṇum
tāḷāṇ etirum piriviṉāṉum
nāṇu neñcu alippa viṭuttaṟkaṇṇum
varital vēṇṭit tōḻi ceppiya
puri tīr kiḷavi pulliya etirum
varivu uṭaṉpaṭutalum āṅku ataṉ puṟattup
puri paṭa vanta maṟuttaloṭu tokii
kiḻavōḷ mēṉa eṉmaṉār pulavar. 17
kāmat tiṇiyiṉ kaṇ niṉṟu varūum
nāṇum maṭaṉum peṇmiya ākaliṉ
kuṟippiṉum iṭattiṉum allatu vēṭki
neṟippaṭa vārā avaḷvayiṉāṉa. 18
kāmam collā nāṭṭam iṉmiyiṉ
ēmuṟa iraṇṭum uḷa eṉa moḻipa. 19
col etir moḻital arumittu ākaliṉ
alla kūṟṟumoḻi avaḷvayiṉāṉa. 20
maṟintu avaṟ kāṇṭal taṟ kāṭṭuṟutal
niṟinta kātaliṉ col etir maḻuṅkal
vaḻipāṭu maṟuttal maṟuttu etirkōṭal
paḻi tīr muṟuval ciṟitē tōṟṟal
kippaṭṭuk kalaṅkiṉum nāṇu mika variṉum
iṭṭup pirivu iraṅkiṉum arumi ceytu ayarppiṉum
vantavaḻi eḷḷiṉum viṭṭu uyirttu aḻuṅkiṉum
nontu teḷivu oḻippiṉum accam nīṭiṉum
pirintavaḻik kalaṅkiṉum peṟṟavaḻi maliyiṉum
varum toḻiṟku arumi vāyil kūṟiṉum
kūṟiya vā`yil koḷḷāk kāliyum
maṉip paṭṭuk kalaṅkic citintavaḻit tōḻikku
niṉittal cāṉṟa aru maṟi uyirttalum
uyirāk kālattu uyirttalum uyir cela
vēṟṟu varivu variṉ atu māṟṟutaṟkaṇṇum
neṟi paṭu nāṭṭattu nikaḻntavi maṟippiṉum
poṟiyiṉ yātta puṇarcci nōkki
orumik kēṇmiyiṉ uṟu kuṟi teḷintōḷ
arumi cāṉṟa nāl iraṇṭu vakiyiṉ
perumi cāṉṟa iyalpiṉkaṇṇum
poy tali aṭutta maṭaliṉkaṇṇum
kiyaṟu tōḻi kaṇṇīr tuṭippiṉum
veṟiyāṭṭu iṭattu veruviṉkaṇṇum
kuṟiyiṉ oppumi maruṭaṟkaṇṇum
varivu talivariṉum kaḷavu aṟivuṟiṉum
tamar taṟ kātta kāraṇa maruṅkiṉum
taṉ kuṟi taḷḷiya teruḷāk kāli
vantavaṉ peyarnta vaṟuṅ kaḷam nōkkit
taṉ piḻippākat taḻīit tēṟalum
vaḻu iṉṟu niliiya iyaṟpaṭu poruḷiṉum
poḻutum āṟum purivatu aṉmiyiṉ
aḻivu talivanta cintikkaṇṇum
kāmam ciṟappiṉum avaṉ aḷi ciṟappiṉum
ēmam cāṉṟa uvakikkaṇṇum
taṉvayiṉ urimiyum avaṉvayiṉ parattiyum
aṉṉavum uḷavē ōr iṭattāṉa. 21
varivu iṭi vitta kālattu varuntiṉum
variyā nāḷiṭi vantōṉ muṭṭiṉum
uri eṉat tōḻikku urittaṟkaṇṇum
tāṉē kūṟum kālamum uḷavē. 22
uyiriṉum ciṟantaṉṟu nāṇē nāṇiṉum
ceyir tīr kāṭcik kaṟpuc ciṟantaṉṟu eṉat
tollōr kiḷavi pulliya neñcamoṭu
kāmak kiḻavaṉ uḷvaḻip paṭiṉum
tā il nal moḻi kiḻavi kiḷappiṉum
ā vaki piṟavum tōṉṟumaṉ poruḷē. 23
nāṟṟamum tōṟṟamum oḻukkamum uṇṭiyum
cey viṉi maṟippiṉum celaviṉum payilviṉum
puṇarcci etirppāṭu uḷḷuṟuttu varūum
uṇarcci ēḻiṉum uṇarnta piṉṟi
meyyiṉum poyyiṉum vaḻinili piḻiyātu
pal vēṟu kavar poruḷ nāṭṭattāṉum
kuṟiyuṟaṟku etiriya kiḻavaṉi maṟiyuṟap
perumiyiṉ peyarppiṉum ulaku urittu oḻippiṉum
arumiyiṉ akaṟciyum avaḷ aṟivuṟuttup
piṉ vā eṉṟalum pētimi ūṭṭalum
muṉ uṟu puṇarcci muṟi niṟuttu urittalum
añci accuṟuttalum urittuḻik kūṭṭamoṭu
eñcātu kiḷanta iru nāṉku kiḷaviyum
vanta kiḻavaṉi māyam ceppip
poṟutta kāraṇam kuṟitta kāliyum
puṇarnta piṉ avaṉvayiṉ vaṇaṅkaṟkaṇṇum
kuṟintu avaṭ paṭariṉum maṟintavaḷ aruka
taṉṉoṭum avaḷoṭum mutal mūṉṟu aḷii
piṉṉili nikaḻum pal vēṟu maruṅkiṉum
nal nayam peṟṟuḻi nayam puri iṭattiṉum
eṇṇa arum pal naki kaṇṇiya vakiyiṉum
puṇarcci vēṇṭiṉum vēṇṭāp piriviṉum
vēḷāṇ peru neṟi vēṇṭiya iṭattiṉum
puṇarntuḻi uṇarnta aṟi maṭac ciṟappiṉum
ōmpaṭik kiḷavip pāṅkiṉkaṇṇum
ceṅ kaṭu moḻiyāṉ citivuṭittu āyiṉum
eṉpu nekap pirintōḷ vaḻic ceṉṟu kaṭii
aṉpu taliyaṭutta vaṉpuṟikkaṇṇum
āṟṟatu tīmi aṟivuṟu kalakkamum
kāppiṉ kaṭumi kiyaṟa variṉum
kaḷaṉum poḻutum vari nili vilakki
kātal mikuti uḷappaṭap piṟavum
nāṭum ūrum illum kuṭiyum
piṟappum ciṟappum iṟappa nōkki
avaṉvayiṉ tōṉṟiya kiḷaviyoṭu tokii
aṉi nili vakiyāṉ varital vēṇṭiṉum
aiyac ceyki tāykku etir maṟuttu
poy eṉa māṟṟi meyvaḻik koṭuppiṉum
avaṉ vilaṅkuṟiṉum kaḷam peṟak kāṭṭiṉum
piṟaṉ varivu āyiṉum avaṉ varivu maṟuppiṉum
muṉṉili aṟaṉ eṉappaṭutal eṉṟu iru vakip
puri tīr kiḷavi tāyiṭip pukuppiṉum
varivu uṭaṉpaṭṭō ṟ kaṭāval vēṇṭiṉum
āṅku ataṉ taṉmiyiṉ vaṉpuṟi uḷappaṭa
pāṅkuṟa vanta nāl eṭṭu vakiyum
tāṅka aruñ ciṟappiṉ tōḻi mēṉa. 24
kaḷavu alar āyiṉum kāmam meyppaṭuppiṉum
aḷavu mikat tōṉṟiṉum talippeytu kāṇiṉum
kaṭṭiṉum kaḻaṅkiṉum veṟi eṉa iruvarum
oṭṭiya tiṟattāṉ ceytikkaṇṇum
āṭiya ceṉṟuḻi aḻivu talivariṉum
kātal kimmikak kaṉaviṉ araṟṟalum
tōḻiyi viṉavalum teyvam vāḻttalum
pōkku uṭaṉ aṟinta piṉ tōḻiyoṭu keḻīik
kaṟpiṉ ākkattu niṟṟaṟkaṇṇum
piriviṉ eccattum makaḷ neñcu valippiṉum
iru pāl kuṭip poruḷ iyalpiṉkaṇṇum
iṉṉa vakiyiṉ patiṉmūṉṟu kiḷaviyoṭu
aṉṉavi piṟavum cevili mēṉa. 25
tāykkum variyār uṇarvu uṭampaṭiṉē. 26
kiḻavōṉ aṟiyā aṟiviṉaḷ ivaḷ eṉa
mi aṟu ciṟappiṉ uyarntōr pāṅkiṉ
aiyak kiḷaviyiṉ aṟitalum urittē. 27
taṉ uṟu vēṭki kiḻavaṉ muṉ kiḷattal
eṇṇum kāli kiḻattikku illi
piṟa nīr mākkaḷiṉ aṟiya āyiṭip
peyn nīr pōlum uṇarviṟṟu eṉpa. 28
kāmak kūṭṭam taṉimiyiṉ politaliṉ
tāmē tūtuvar ākalum urittē. 29
avaṉ varampu iṟattal aṟam taṉakku iṉmiyiṉ
kaḷam cuṭṭuk kiḷavi kiḻaviyatu ākum
tāṉ celaṟku uriya vaḻi ākalāṉa. 30
tōḻiyiṉ muṭiyum iṭaṉumār uṇṭē. 31
mun nāḷ allatu tuṇi iṉṟu kaḻiyātu
an nāḷ akattum atu varivu iṉṟē. 32
pal nūṟu vakiyiṉum taṉ vayiṉ varūum
nal naya maruṅkiṉ nāṭṭam vēṇṭaliṉ
tuṇic cuṭṭuk kiḷavi kiḻaviyatu ākum
tuṇiyōr karumam ākalāṉa. 33
āy peruñ ciṟappiṉ aru maṟi kiḷattaliṉ
tāy eṉappaṭuvōḷ cevili ākum. 34
tōḻitāṉē cevili makaḷē. 35
cūḻtalum ucāttuṇi nilimiyiṉ polimē. 36
kuṟiyuṟa uṇartal muṉ uṟa uṇartal
iruvarum uḷvaḻi avaṉ varavu uṇartal eṉa
matiyuṭampaṭuttal oru mū vakittē. 37
aṉṉa vakiyāṉ uṇarnta piṉ allatu
piṉṉili muyaṟci peṟāḷ eṉa moḻipa. 38
muyaṟcik kālattu ataṟpaṭa nāṭi
puṇarttal āṟṟalum avaḷvayiṉāṉa. 39
kuṟi eṉappaṭuvatu iraviṉum pakaliṉum
aṟiyak kiḷanta āṟṟatu eṉpa. 40
iravuk kuṟiyē illakattuḷḷum
maṉiyōr kiḷavi kēṭkum vaḻiyatuvē
maṉiyakam pukāak kāliyāṉa. 41
pakal puṇar kaḷaṉē puṟaṉ eṉa moḻipa
avaḷ aṟivu uṇara varu vaḻiyāṉa. 42
allakuṟippaṭutalum avaḷvayiṉ urittē
avaṉ kuṟi mayaṅkiya amivoṭu variṉē. 43
āṅku āṅku oḻukum oḻukkamum uṇṭē
ōṅkiya ciṟappiṉ oru ciṟiyāṉa. 44
maṟinta oḻukkattu ōriyum nāḷum
tuṟanta oḻukkam kiḻavōṟku illi. 45
āṟṟiṉatu arumiyum aḻivum accamum
ūṟum uḷappaṭa ataṉ ōraṉṉa. 46
tantiyum taṉṉiyum muṉṉattiṉ uṇarpa. 47
tāy aṟivuṟutal ceviliyoṭu okkum. 48
ampalum alarum kaḷavu veḷippaṭuttaliṉ
aṅku ataṉ mutalvaṉ kiḻavaṉ ākum. 49
veḷippaṭa varital paṭāmi varital eṉṟu
āyiraṇṭu eṉpa varital āṟē. 50
veḷippaṭitāṉē kaṟpiṉoṭu oppiṉum
ñāṅkark kiḷanta mūṉṟu poruḷāka
variyātu pirital kiḻavōṟku illi. 51

3.4. kaṟpiyal
kaṟpu eṉappaṭuvatu karaṇamoṭu puṇara
koḷaṟku uri marapiṉ kiḻavaṉ kiḻattiyi
koṭikku uri marapiṉōr koṭuppa koḷvatuvē. 1
koṭuppōr iṉṟiyum karaṇam uṇṭē
puṇarntu uṭaṉ pōkiya kāliyāṉa. 2
mēlōr mūvarkkum puṇartta karaṇam
kīḻōrkku ākiya kālamum uṇṭē. 3
poyyum vaḻuvum tōṉṟiya piṉṉar
aiyar yāttaṉar karaṇam eṉpa. 4
karaṇattiṉ amintu muṭinta kāli
neñcu taḷi aviḻnta puṇarccikkaṇṇum
eñcā makiḻcci iṟantu varu paruvattum
añca vanta urimikkaṇṇum
nal neṟip paṭarum tol nalap poruḷiṉum
peṟṟa tēettup perumiyiṉ nilii
kuṟṟam cāṉṟa poruḷ eṭuttu urippiṉum
nāmak kālattu uṇṭu eṉat tōḻi
ēmuṟu kaṭavuḷ ēttiya maruṅkiṉum
allal tīra ārvamoṭu aḷii
colluṟu poruḷiṉkaṇṇum col eṉa
ēṉatu cuvippiṉum nī ki toṭṭatu
vāṉōr amiḻtam puriyumāl emakku eṉa
aṭicilum pūvum toṭutaṟkaṇṇum
antaṇar tiṟattum cāṉṟōr tēettum
antam il ciṟappiṉ piṟar piṟar tiṟattiṉum
oḻukkam kāṭṭiya kuṟippiṉum oḻukkattuk
kaḷaviṉuḷ nikaḻnta arumiyip pulampi
alamaral uḷḷamoṭu aḷaviya iṭattum
antarattu eḻutiya eḻuttiṉ māṉa
vanta kuṟṟam vaḻi keṭa oḻukalum
aḻiyal añcal eṉṟu āyiru poruḷiṉum
tāṉ avaṭ piḻitta paruvattāṉum
nōṉmiyum perumiyum mey koḷa aruḷi
paṉṉal cāṉṟa vāyiloṭu porunti
taṉṉiṉ ākiya takutikkaṇṇum
putalvaṟ payanta puṉiṟu tīr poḻutiṉ
ney aṇi mayakkam purintōḷ nōkki
aiyar pāṅkiṉum amararc cuṭṭiyum
cey peruñ ciṟappoṭu cērtaṟkaṇṇum
payam keḻu tuṇi aṇi pulli pullātu
uyaṅkuvaṉaḷ kiṭanta kiḻattiyik kuṟuki
alkal muṉṉiya niṟi aḻi poḻutiṉ
melleṉ cīṟaṭi pulliya iraviṉum
uṟal aruṅkurimiyiṉ ūṭal mikuttōḷip
piṟa piṟa peṇṭiriṉ peyarttaṟkaṇṇum
piriviṉ eccattup pulampiya iruvarip
pariviṉ nīkkiya pakutikkaṇṇum
niṉṟu naṉi piriviṉ añciya piyuḷum
ceṉṟu kiyikantu peyarttu uḷḷiya vaḻiyum
kāmattiṉ valiyum kiviṭiṉ accamum
tāṉ avaṭ piḻitta niliyiṉkaṇṇum
uṭaṉ cēṟal ceykiyoṭu aṉṉavi piṟavum
maṭam paṭa vanta tōḻikkaṇṇum
vēṟṟu nāṭṭu akalvayiṉ viḻumattāṉum
mīṭṭu varavu āynta vakiyiṉkaṇṇum
av vaḻip perukiya ciṟappiṉkaṇṇum
pēr ici ūrtip pākar pāṅkiṉum
kāmak kiḻatti maṉiyōḷ eṉṟu ivar
ēmuṟu kiḷavi colliya etirum
ceṉṟa tēettu uḻappu naṉi viḷakki
iṉṟic ceṉṟa taṉṉili kiḷappiṉum
arun toḻil muṭitta cemmal kāli
viruntoṭu nallavi vēṇṭaṟkaṇṇum
māli ēntiya peṇṭirum makkaḷum
kēḷir oḻukkattup pukaṟcikkaṇṇum
ēṉiya vāyilōr etiroṭu tokii
paṇ ami pakuti muppatiṉorumūṉṟum
eṇṇa aruñ ciṟappiṉ kiḻavōṉ mēṉa. 5
avaṉ aṟivu āṟṟa aṟiyum ākaliṉ
ēṟṟaṟ kaṇṇum niṟuttaṟkaṇṇum
urimi koṭutta kiḻavōṉ pāṅkil
perumiyiṉ tiriyā aṉpiṉkaṇṇum
kiḻavaṉi makaṭūup pulampu peritu ākaliṉ
alamaral perukiya kāmattu mikutiyum
iṉpamum iṭumpiyum ākiya iṭattum
kayantali tōṉṟiya kāmar neyyaṇi
nayanta kiḻavaṉi neñcu puṇṇuṟīi
naḷiyiṉ nīkkiya iḷi varu niliyum
pukaṉṟa uḷḷamoṭu putuvōr cāyaṟku
akaṉṟa kiḻavaṉip pulampu naṉi kāṭṭi
iyaṉṟa neñcam talip peyarttu arukki
etir peytu maṟutta īrattu maruṅkiṉum
taṅkiya oḻukkattuk kiḻavaṉi vaṇaṅki
eṅkiyarkku uri eṉa irattaṟkaṇṇum
cellāk kāli celka eṉa viṭuttalum
kāmak kiḻatti taṉ makat taḻīi
ēmuṟu viḷiyāṭṭu iṟutikkaṇṇum
ciṟanta ceyki av vaḻit tōṉṟi
aṟam puri uḷḷamoṭu taṉ varavu aṟiyāmi
puṟam ceytu peyarttal vēṇṭu iṭattāṉum
tantiyar oppar makkaḷ eṉpataṉāl
antam il ciṟappiṉ makap paḻittu neruṅkalum
koṭiyōr koṭumi cuṭum eṉa oṭiyātu
nal ici nayantōr colloṭu tokii
pakutiyiṉ nīṅkiya takutikkaṇṇum
koṭumi oḻukkam kōṭal vēṇṭi
aṭimēl vīḻnta kiḻavaṉi neruṅki
kātal eṅkiyar kāṇiṉ naṉṟu eṉa
mātar cāṉṟa vakiyiṉkaṇṇum
tāyar kaṇṇiya nal aṇip putalvaṉi
māyap paratti uḷḷiya vaḻiyum
taṉvayiṉ ciṟippiṉum avaṉ vayiṉ pirippiṉum
iṉṉāt tol cūḷ eṭuttaṟkaṇṇum
kāmakkiḻattiyar nalam pārāṭṭiya
tīmiyiṉ muṭikkum poruḷiṉkaṇṇum
koṭumi oḻukkattut tōḻikku uriyavi
vaṭu aṟu ciṟappiṉ kaṟpiṉ tiriyāmi
kāytalum uvattalum pirittalum peṭṭalum
āvayiṉ varūum pal vēṟu niliyiṉum
vāyiliṉ varūum vakiyoṭu tokii
kiḻavōḷ ceppal kiḻavatu eṉpa. 6
puṇarntu uṭaṉ pōkiya kiḻavōḷ maṉi iruntu
iṭic curattu iṟicciyum viṉiyum cuṭṭi
aṉpuṟu takka kiḷattal tāṉē
kiḻavōṉ cey viṉikku accam ākum. 7
tōḻi uḷḷuṟutta vāyil pukuppiṉum
āvayiṉ nikaḻum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 8
peṟaṟku arum perum poruḷ muṭinta piṉ vanta
teṟaṟku aru marapiṉ ciṟappiṉkaṇṇum
aṟṟam aḻivu urippiṉum aṟṟam illāk
kiḻavōṭ cuṭṭiya teyvak kaṭattiṉum
cīruṭip perum poruḷ vittavaḻi maṟappiṉum
aṭaṅkā oḻukkattu avaṉvayiṉ aḻintōḷi
aṭaṅkak kāṭṭutaṟ poruḷiṉ kaṇṇum
piḻittu vantu irunta kiḻavaṉi neruṅki
iḻittu āṅku ākkik koṭuttaṟkaṇṇum
vaṇaṅku iyal moḻiyāṉ vaṇaṅkaṟkaṇṇum
puṟampaṭu viḷiyāṭṭup pulliya pukaṟciyum
ciṟanta putalvaṉit tērātu pulampiṉum
māṇ nalam tā eṉa vakuttaṟkaṇṇum
pēṇā oḻukkam nāṇiya poruḷiṉum
cūḷvayiṉ tiṟattāl cōrvu kaṇṭu aḻiyiṉum
periyōr oḻukkam peritu eṉak kiḷantu
peṟu taki illāp piḻippiṉum av vaḻi
uṟu taki illāp pulaviyuḷ mūḻkiya
kiḻavōḷpāl niṉṟu keṭuttaṟkaṇṇum
uṇarppuvayiṉ vārā ūṭal uṟṟōḷvayiṉ
uṇarttal vēṇṭiya kiḻavōṉpāl niṉṟu
tāṉ vekuṇṭu ākkiya takutikkaṇṇum
arumik kālattup perumi kāṭṭiya
eḷimik kālattu irakkattāṉum
pāṇar kūttar viṟaliyar eṉṟu ivar
pēṇic colliya kuṟiviṉi etirum
nītta kiḻavaṉi nikaḻumāṟu paṭīiyar
kātta taṉmiyiṉ kaṇ iṉṟu peyarppiṉum
piriyum kāli etir niṉṟu cāṟṟiya
marapu uṭi etirum uḷappaṭa piṟavum
vaki paṭa vanta kiḷavi ellām
tōḻikku uriya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 9
pullutal mayakkum pulavikkaṇṇum
illōr ceyviṉi ikaḻccikkaṇṇum
pal vēṟu putalvark kaṇṭu naṉi uvappiṉum
maṟiyiṉ vanta maṉiyōḷ ceyviṉi
poṟi iṉṟu perukiya paruvaraṟkaṇṇum
kātal cōrviṉ kaṭappāṭṭu āṇmiyiṉ
tāy pōl taḻīik kaḻaṟi am maṉiviyik
kāyvu iṉṟu avaṉvayiṉ poruttaṟkaṇṇum
iṉ nakip putalvaṉit taḻīi iḻi aṇintu
piṉṉar vanta vāyiṟkaṇṇum
maṉiyōḷ ottaliṉ taṉṉōr aṉṉōr
miki eṉak kuṟitta koḷkikkaṇṇum
eṇṇiya paṇṇi eṉṟu ivaṟṟoṭu piṟavum
kaṇṇiya kāmakkiḻattiyar mēṉa. 10
kaṟpum kāmamum naṟpāl oḻukkamum
mel iyal poṟiyum niṟiyum vallitiṉ
viruntu puṟantarutalum cuṟṟam ōmpalum
piṟavum aṉṉa kiḻavōḷ māṇpukaḷ
mukam pukal muṟimiyiṉ kiḻavōṟku urittal
akam pukal marapiṉ vāyilkaṭku uriya. 11
kaḻiviṉum nikaḻviṉum etirviṉum vaḻi koḷa
nallavi urittalum allavi kaṭitalum
cevilikku uriya ākum eṉpa. 12
colliya kiḷavi aṟivarkkum uriya. 13
iṭittu vari niṟuttalum avaratu ākum
kiḻavaṉum kiḻattiyum avar vari niṟṟaliṉ. 14
uṇarppu vari iṟappiṉum cey kuṟi piḻippiṉum
pulattalum ūṭalum kiḻavōṟku uriya. 15
pulattalum ūṭalum ākiya iṭattum
colat taku kiḷavi tōḻikku uriya. 16
parattimi maṟuttal vēṇṭiyum kiḻatti
maṭat taku kiḻami uṭimiyāṉum
aṉpili koṭiyi eṉṟalum uriyaḷ. 17
avaṉ kuṟippu aṟital vēṇṭiyum kiḻavi
akam mali ūṭal akaṟcikkaṇṇum
vēṟṟumik kiḷavi tōṟṟavum peṟumē. 18
kāmak kaṭappiṉuḷ paṇinta kiḷavi
kāṇum kāli kiḻavōṟku urittē
vaḻipaṭu kiḻami avaṭku iyalāṉa. 19
aruḷ muntuṟutta aṉpu poti kiḷavi
poruḷ paṭa moḻital kiḻavōṭkum urittē. 20
kaḷavum kaṟpum alar varivu iṉṟē. 21
alariṉ tōṉṟum kāmattu mikuti. 22
kiḻavōṉ viḷiyāṭṭu āṅkum aṟṟē. 23
maṉivi talittāḷ kiḻavōṉ koṭumi
tam uḷa ātal vāyilkaṭku illi. 24
maṉivi muṉṉark kiyaṟu kiḷavi
maṉivikku uṟuti uḷvaḻi uṇṭē. 25
muṉṉilip puṟamoḻi ellā vāyiṟkum
piṉṉilit tōṉṟum eṉmaṉār pulavar. 26
tollavi urittalum nukarcci ēttalum
pal āṟṟāṉum ūṭaliṉ takittalum
uṟuti kāṭṭalum aṟivu meyn niṟuttalum
ētuviṉ urittalum tuṇivu kāṭṭalum
aṇi nili urittalum kūttar mēṉa. 27
nilam peyarntu urittal avaḷ nili urittal
kūttarkkum pāṇarkkum yāttavi uriya. 28
āṟṟatu paṇpum karumattu viḷivum
ēval muṭivum viṉāvum ceppum
āṟṟiṭik kaṇṭa poruḷum iṟicciyum
tōṟṟam cāṉṟa aṉṉavi piṟavum
iḷiyōrkku uriya kiḷavi eṉpa. 29
uḻik kuṟun toḻilum kāppum uyarntōr
naṭakki ellām avarkaṇ paṭumē. 30
piṉ muṟi ākkiya perum poruḷ vatuvit
tol muṟi maṉivi etirppāṭu āyiṉum
iṉ iḻip putalvaṉi vāyil koṇṭu pukiṉum
iṟantatu niṉiik kiḻavōṉ āṅkaṇ
kalaṅkalum uriyaṉ eṉmaṉār pulavar. 31
tāy pōl kaḻaṟit taḻīik kōṭal
āy maṉik kiḻattikkum urittu eṉa moḻipa
kavavoṭu mayaṅkiya kāliyāṉa. 32
avaṉ cōrpu kāttal kaṭaṉ eṉappaṭutaliṉ
makaṉ tāy uyarpum taṉ uyarpu ākum
celvaṉ paṇi moḻi iyalpu ākalāṉa. 33
eṇ arum pācaṟi peṇṇoṭu puṇarār. 34
puṟattōr āṅkaṇ puṇarvatu ākum. 35
kāma nili urittalum tēr nili urittalum
kiḻavōṉ kuṟippiṉi eṭuttuk kūṟalum
āvoṭu paṭṭa nimittam kūṟalum
celavu uṟu kiḷaviyum celavu aḻuṅku kiḷaviyum
aṉṉavi piṟavum pārppārkku uriya. 36
ellā vāyilum iruvar tēettum
pulliya makiḻccip poruḷa eṉpa. 37
aṉpu talippirinta kiḷavi tōṉṟiṉ
ciṟippuṟam kuṟittaṉṟu eṉmaṉār pulavar. 38
taṟ pukaḻ kiḷavi kiḻavaṉ muṉ kiḷattal
et tiṟattāṉum kiḻattikku illi
muṟpaṭa vakutta iraṇṭu alaṅkaṭiyē. 39
kiḻavi muṉṉart taṟ pukaḻ kiḷavi
kiḻavōṉ viṉivayiṉ uriya eṉpa. 40
moḻi etir moḻital pāṅkaṟku urittē. 41
kuṟittu etir moḻital aḵkit tōṉṟum. 42
tuṉpuṟu poḻutiṉum ellām kiḻavaṉ
vaṉpuṟuttallatu cēṟal illi. 43
celaviṭi aḻuṅkal cellāmi aṉṟē
vaṉpuṟi kuṟitta tavircci ākum. 44
kiḻavi niliyē viṉiyiṭattu uriyār
veṉṟik kālattu viḷaṅkit tōṉṟum 45
pūppiṉ puṟappāṭu īr āṟu nāḷum
nīttu akaṉṟu uṟiyār eṉmaṉār pulavar
parattiyiṉ pirinta kāliyāṉa. 46
vēṇṭiya kalvi yāṇṭu mūṉṟu iṟavātu. 47
vēntu uṟu toḻilē yāṇṭiṉatu akamē. 48
ēṉip pirivum av iyal niliyum. 49
yāṟum kuḷaṉum kāvum āṭi
pati ikantu nukartalum uriya eṉpa. 50
kāmam cāṉṟa kaṭikkōṭ kāli
ēmam cāṉṟa makkaḷoṭu tuvaṉṟi
aṟam puri cuṟṟamoṭu kiḻavaṉum kiḻattiyum
ciṟantatu payiṟṟal iṟantataṉ payaṉē. 51
tōḻi tāyē pārppāṉ pāṅkaṉ
pāṇaṉ pāṭṭi iḷiyar viruntiṉar
kūttar viṟaliyar aṟivar kaṇṭō r
yātta ciṟappiṉ vāyilkaḷ eṉpa. 52
viṉi vayiṉ pirintōṉ mīṇṭu varu kāli
iṭiccura maruṅkiṉ tavirtal illi
uḷḷam pōla uṟṟuḻi utavum
puḷ iyal kali mā uṭimiyāṉa. 53

3.5. poruḷiyal
ici tirintu icippiṉum iyiyumaṉ poruḷē
aci tirintu iciyā eṉmaṉār pulavar. 1
nōyum iṉpamum iru vaki niliyiṉ
kāmam kaṇṇiya marapiṭi teriya
eṭṭaṉ pakutiyum viḷaṅka oṭṭiya
uṟuppuṭiyatu pōl uṇarvuṭiyatu pōl
maṟuttu urippatu pōl neñcoṭu puṇarttum
collā marapiṉ avaṟṟoṭu keḻīi
ceyyā marapiṉ toḻiṟpaṭuttu aṭakkiyum
avar avar uṟu piṇi tama pōl cērttiyum
aṟivum pulaṉum vēṟupaṭa niṟīi
iru peyar mūṉṟum uriya āka
uvamavāyil paṭuttalum uvamam
oṉṟu iṭattu iruvarkkum uriya pāṟ kiḷavi. 2
kaṉavum urittāl av iṭattāṉa. 3
tāykkum urittāl pōkku uṭaṉ kiḷappiṉ.4
pāl keḻu kiḷavi nālvarkkum urittē
naṭpiṉ naṭakki āṅku alaṅkaṭiyē. 5
uyirum nāṇum maṭaṉum eṉṟu ivi
ceyir tīr ciṟappiṉ nālvarkkum uriya. 6
vaṇṇam pacantu pulampuṟu kāli
uṇarnta pōla uṟuppiṉik kiḻavi
puṇarnta vakiyāṉ puṇarkkavum peṟumē. 7
uṭampum uyirum vāṭiyakkaṇṇum
eṉ uṟṟaṉakol ivi eṉiṉ allati
kiḻavōṟ cērtal kiḻattikku illi. 8
oru ciṟi neñcamoṭu ucāvum kāli
uriyatākalum uṇṭu eṉa moḻipa. 9
taṉvayiṉ karattalum avaṉvayiṉ vēṭṭalum
aṉṉa iṭaṅkaḷ al vaḻi ellām
maṭaṉoṭu niṟṟal kaṭaṉ eṉa moḻipa. 10
aṟattoṭu niṟkum kālattu aṉṟi
aṟattu iyal marapu ilaḷ tōḻi eṉpa. 11
eḷittal ēttal vēṭki urittal
kūṟutal ucāatal ētīṭu talippāṭu
uṇmi ceppum kiḷaviyoṭu tokii
av eḻu vakiya eṉmaṉār pulavar. 12
uṟṟuḻi allatu collal iṉmiyiṉ
ap poruḷ vēṭki kiḻaviyiṉ uṇarpa. 13
ceṟivum niṟivum cemmiyum ceppum
aṟivum arumiyum peṇpālāṉa. 14
poḻutum āṟum kāppum eṉṟu ivaṟṟiṉ
vaḻuviṉ ākiya kuṟṟam kāṭṭalum
taṉṉi aḻitalum avaṇ ūṟu añcalum
iraviṉum pakaliṉum nī vā eṉṟalum
kiḻavōṉ taṉṉi vāral eṉṟalum
naṉmiyum tīmiyum piṟitiṉik kūṟalum
puri paṭa vanta aṉṉavi piṟavum
varital vēṭkip poruḷa eṉpa. 15
vēṭki maṟuttuk kiḷantāṅku urittal
marīiya maruṅkiṉ urittu eṉa moḻipa. 16
tērum yāṉiyum kutiriyum piṟavum
ūrntaṉar iyaṅkalum uriyar eṉpa. 17
uṇṭaṟku uriya allāp poruḷi
uṇṭaṉa pōlak kūṟalum marapē. 18
poruḷ eṉa moḻitalum vari nili iṉṟē
kāppuk kimmikutal uṇmiyāṉa
aṉpē aṟaṉē iṉpam nāṇoṭu
tuṟanta oḻukkam paḻittu aṉṟu ākaliṉ
oṉṟum vēṇṭā kāppiṉuḷḷē. 19
curam eṉa moḻitalum vari nili iṉṟē. 20
uyarntōr kiḷavi vaḻakkoṭu puṇartaliṉ
vaḻakku vaḻippaṭutal ceyyuṭkuk kaṭaṉē. 21
aṟak kaḻivu uṭiyaṉa poruṭ payam paṭa variṉ
vaḻakku eṉa vaḻaṅkalum paḻittu aṉṟu eṉpa. 22
mikka poruḷiṉuḷ poruḷ vaki puṇarkka
nāṇut talippiriyā nalvaḻip paṭuttē. 23
muṟippeyar maruṅkiṉ keḻutakip potuc col
nilikku uri marapiṉ iru vīṟṟum urittē. 24
tāyattiṉ aṭiyā īyac cellā
viṉivayiṉ taṅkā vīṟṟuk koḷappaṭā
em eṉa varūum kiḻamit tōṟṟam
allāvāyiṉum pulluva uḷavē. 25
oru pāl kiḷavi eṉip pāṟkaṇṇum
varu vakitāṉē vaḻakku eṉa moḻipa. 26
ellā uyirkkum iṉpam eṉpatu
tāṉ amarntu varūum mēvaṟṟu ākum. 27
paratti vāyil nālvarkkum urittē
nilat tiripu iṉṟu aḵtu eṉmaṉār pulavar. 28
orutali urimi vēṇṭiṉum makaṭūup
pirital accam uṇmiyāṉum
ampalum alarum kaḷavu veḷippaṭukkum eṉṟu
añca vanta āṅku iru vakiyiṉum
nōkkoṭu vanta iṭiyūṟu poruḷiṉum
pōkkum varivum maṉivikaṇ tōṉṟum. 29
varutta mikuti cuṭṭum kāli
urittu eṉa moḻipa vāḻkkiyuḷ irakkam. 30
maṉivi uyarvum kiḻavōṉ paṇivum
niṉiyum kāli pulaviyuḷ uriya. 31
nikaḻ taki maruṅkiṉ vēṭki mikutiyiṉ
pukaḻ taki variyār kaṟpiṉuḷḷē. 32
iṟiccitāṉē urip puṟattatuvē. 33
iṟicciyiṉ piṟakkum poruḷumār uḷavē
tiṟattu iyal maruṅkiṉ teriyumōrkkē. 34
aṉpuṟu takuvaṉa iṟicciyuḷ cuṭṭalum
vaṉpuṟi ākum varuntiya poḻutē. 35
cey poruḷ accamum viṉivayiṉ pirivum
meypeṟa uṇarttum kiḻavi pārāṭṭē. 36
kaṟpuvaḻip paṭṭavaḷ parattimi ēttiṉum
uḷḷattu ūṭal uṇṭu eṉa moḻipa. 37
kiḻavōḷ piṟaḷ kuṇam ivi eṉak kūṟi
kiḻavōṉ kuṟippiṉi uṇartaṟkum uriyaḷ. 38
tam uṟu viḻumam parattiyar kūṟiṉum
meymmiyāka avarvayiṉ uṇarntum
talittāṭ kaḻaṟal tam etirppoḻutu iṉṟē
malitalum ūṭalum avi alaṅkaṭiyē. 39
poḻutu talivitta kiyaṟu kāli
iṟanta pōlak kiḷakkum kiḷavi
maṭaṉē varuttam maruṭki mikutiyoṭu
avi nāṟ poruṭkaṇ nikaḻum eṉpa. 40
irantu kuṟiyuṟṟa kiḻavaṉit tōḻi
nirampa nīkki niṟuttal aṉṟiyum
vāymi kūṟalum poy talippeytalum
nal vakiyuṭiya nayattiṉ kūṟiyum
pal vakiyāṉum paṭikkavum peṟumē. 41
uyar moḻik kiḷavi uṟaḻum kiḷavi
aiyak kiḷavi āṭūuviṟku urittē. 42
uṟukaṇ ōmpal taṉ iyalpu ākaliṉ
uriyatākum tōḻikaṇ uraṉē. 43
uyar moḻik kiḷaviyum uriyavāl avaṭkē. 44
vāyiṟ kiḷavi veḷippaṭak kiḷattal
tā iṉṟu uriya tamtam kūṟṟē. 45
uṭaṉuṟi uvamam cuṭṭu naki ciṟappu eṉak
keṭal aru marapiṉ uḷḷuṟi aintē. 46
antam il ciṟappiṉ ākiya iṉpam
taṉvayiṉ varutalum vakutta paṇpē. 47
maṅkala moḻiyum viiya moḻiyum
māṟu il āṇmiyiṉ colliya moḻiyum
kūṟiya maruṅkiṉ koḷḷum eṉpa. 48
ciṉaṉē pētimi nimpiri nalkuravu
aṉi nāl vakiyum ciṟappoṭu varumē. 49
aṉṉi eṉṉi eṉṟalum uḷavē
tol neṟi muṟimi colliṉum eḻuttiṉum
tōṉṟā marapiṉa eṉmaṉār pulavar. 50
oppum uruvum veṟuppum eṉṟā
kaṟpum ērum eḻilum eṉṟā
cāyalum nāṇum maṭaṉum eṉṟā
nōyum vēṭkiyum nukarvum eṉṟu āṅku
āvayiṉ varūum kiḷavi ellām
nāṭṭu iyal marapiṉ neñcu koḷiṉ allatu
kāṭṭalākāp poruḷa eṉpa. 51
imiyōr tēettum eṟi kaṭal varippiṉum
avi il kālam iṉmiyāṉa. 52

3.6. meyppāṭṭiyal
paṇṇit tōṉṟiya eṇ nāṉku poruḷum
kaṇṇiya puṟaṉē nāl nāṉku eṉpa. 1
nāl iraṇṭu ākum pālumār uṇṭē. 2
nakiyē aḻuki iḷivaral maruṭki
accam perumitam vekuḷi uvaki eṉṟu
ap pāl eṭṭē meyppāṭu eṉpa. 3
eḷḷal iḷami pētimi maṭaṉ eṉṟu
uḷḷappaṭṭa naki nāṉku eṉpa. 4
iḷivē iḻavē acivē vaṟumi eṉa
viḷivu il koḷki aḻuki nāṉkē. 5
mūppē piṇiyē varuttam meṉmiyoṭu
yāppuṟa vanta iḷivaral nāṉkē. 6
putumi perumi ciṟumi ākkamoṭu
matimi cālā maruṭki nāṉkē. 7
aṇaṅkē vilaṅkē kaḷvar tam iṟi eṉap
piṇaṅkal cālā accam nāṉkē. 8
kalvi taṟukaṇ pukaḻmi koṭi eṉac
collappaṭṭa perumitam nāṉkē. 9
uṟuppaṟi kuṭikōḷ ali koli eṉṟa
veṟuppiṉ vanta vekuḷi nāṉkē. 10
celvam pulaṉē puṇarvu viḷiyāṭṭu eṉṟu
allal nītta uvaki nāṉkē. 11
āṅkavi oru pāl āka oru pāl
uṭimi iṉpuṟal naṭuvunili aruḷal
taṉmi aṭakkam varital aṉpu eṉāa
kimmikal nalital cūḻcci vāḻttal
nāṇutal tuñcal araṟṟu kaṉavu eṉāa
muṉital niṉital verūutal maṭimi
karutal ārāycci virivu uyirppu eṉāa
kiyāṟu iṭukkaṇ poccāppu poṟāmi
viyarttal aiyam miki naṭukku eṉāa
iviyum uḷavē avi alaṅkaṭiyē. 12
puku mukam purital poṟi nutal viyarttal
naku nayam maṟittal citivu piṟarkku iṉmiyoṭu
taku muṟi nāṉkē oṉṟu eṉa moḻipa. 13
kūḻi virittal kātu oṉṟu kaḷital
ūḻ aṇi tivaral uṭi peyarttu uṭuttaloṭu
ūḻi nāṉkē iraṇṭu eṉa moḻipa. 14
alkul tivaral aṇintavi tiruttal
il valiyuṟuttal iru kiyum eṭuttaloṭu
colliya nāṉkē mūṉṟu eṉa moḻipa. 15
pārāṭṭu eṭuttal maṭam tapa urittal
īram il kūṟṟam ēṟṟu alar nāṇal
koṭuppavi kōṭal uḷappaṭat tokii
eṭutta nāṉkē nāṉku eṉa moḻipa. 16
terintu uṭampaṭutal tiḷippu viṉi maṟuttal
karantiṭattu oḻital kaṇṭavaḻi uvattaloṭu
poruntiya nāṉkē aintu eṉa moḻipa. 17
puṟam ceyac citital pulampit tōṉṟal
kalaṅki moḻital kiyaṟavu urittaloṭu
viḷampiya nāṉkē āṟu eṉa moḻipa. 18
aṉṉa piṟavum avaṟṟoṭu civaṇi
maṉṉiya viṉiya nimittam eṉpa. 19
viṉi uyir melivu iṭattu iṉmiyum urittē. 20
aviyum uḷavē avi alaṅkaṭiyē. 21
iṉpatti veṟuttal tuṉpattup pulampal
etir peytu parital ētam āytal
paci aṭa niṟṟal pacali pāytal
uṇṭiyiṉ kuṟital uṭampu naṉi curuṅkal
kaṇ tuyil maṟuttal kaṉavoṭu mayaṅkal
poyyāk kōṭal meyyē eṉṟal
aiyam ceytal avaṉ tamar uvattal
aṟaṉ aḷittu urittal āṅku neñcu aḻital
em mey āyiṉum oppumi kōṭal
oppuvaḻi uvattal uṟu peyar kēṭṭal
nalat taka nāṭiṉ kalakkamum atuvē. 22
muṭṭuvayiṉ kaḻaṟal muṉivu meyn niṟuttal
accattiṉ akaṟal avaṉ puṇarvu maṟuttal
tūtu muṉivu iṉmi tuñcic cērtal
kātal kimmikal kaṭṭuri iṉmi eṉṟu
āyiru nāṉkē aḻivu il kūṭṭam. 23
teyvam añcal puri aṟam teḷital
illatu kāytal uḷḷatu uvarttal
puṇarntuḻi uṇmi poḻutu maṟuppu ākkam
aruḷ mika uṭimi aṉpu toka niṟṟal
pirivu āṟṟāmi maṟintavi urittal
puṟañcol māṇāk kiḷaviyoṭu tokii
ciṟanta pattum ceppiya poruḷē. 24
piṟappē kuṭimi āṇmi āṇṭoṭu
uruvu niṟutta kāma vāyil
niṟiyē aruḷē uṇarvoṭu tiru eṉa
muṟiyuṟak kiḷanta oppiṉatu vakiyē. 25
nimpiri koṭumi viyappoṭu puṟamoḻi
vaṉcol poccāppu maṭimiyoṭu kuṭimi
iṉpuṟal ēḻimi maṟappoṭu oppumi
eṉṟu ivi iṉmi eṉmaṉār pulavar. 26
kaṇṇiṉum ceviyiṉum tiṇṇitiṉ uṇarum
uṇarvuṭi māntarkku allatu teriyiṉ
nal nayap poruḷkōḷ eṇṇa aruṅkurittē. 27

3.7. uvamayiyal
viṉi payaṉ mey uru eṉṟa nāṉkē
vaki peṟa vanta uvamat tōṟṟam. 1
viraviyum varūum marapiṉa eṉpa. 2
uyarntataṉ mēṟṟē uḷḷum kāli. 3
ciṟappē nalaṉē kātal valiyoṭu
an nāl paṇpum nilikkaḷam eṉpa. 4
kiḻakkiṭu poruḷoṭu aintum ākum. 5
mutalum ciṉiyum eṉṟu āyiru poruṭkum
nutaliya marapiṉ uriyavi uriya. 6
cuṭṭik kūṟā uvamam āyiṉ
poruḷ etir puṇarttup puṇarttaṉa koḷalē. 7
uvamamum poruḷum ottal vēṇṭum. 8
poruḷē uvamam ceytaṉar moḻiyiṉum
maruḷ aṟu ciṟappiṉ aḵtu uvamam ākum. 9
perumiyum ciṟumiyum ciṟappiṉ tīrāk
kuṟippiṉ varūum neṟippāṭu uṭiya. 10
avitām,
aṉṉa ēyppa uṟaḻa oppa
eṉṉa māṉa eṉṟavi eṉāa
oṉṟa oṭuṅka oṭṭa āṅka
eṉṟa viyappa eṉṟavi eṉāa
eḷḷa viḻiya viṟappa nikarppa
kaḷḷa kaṭuppa āṅkavi eṉāa
kāyppa matippa takiya maruḷa
māṟṟa maṟuppa āṅkavi eṉāa
pulla poruva poṟpa pōla
vella vīḻa āṅkavi eṉāa
nāṭa naḷiya naṭuṅka nanta
ōṭa puriya eṉṟavi eṉāa
āṟu āṟu aviyum aṉṉa piṟavum
kūṟum kālip pal kuṟippiṉavē. 11
aṉṉa āṅka māṉa viṟappa
eṉṉa uṟaḻa takiya nōkkoṭu
kaṇṇiya eṭṭum viṉippāl uvamam. 12
aṉṉa eṉ kiḷavi piṟavoṭum civaṇum. 13
eḷḷa viḻiya pulla poruva
kaḷḷa matippa vella vīḻa
eṉṟu āṅku eṭṭē payaṉili uvamam. 14
kaṭuppa ēyppa maruḷa puriya
oṭṭa oṭuṅka ōṭa nikarppa eṉṟu
ap pāl eṭṭē meyppāl uvamam. 15
pōla maṟuppa oppa kāytta
nēra viyappa naḷiya nanta eṉṟu
ottu varu kiḷavi uruviṉ uvamam. 16
tamtam marapiṉ tōṉṟumaṉ poruḷē. 17
nāl iraṇṭu ākum pālumār uṇṭē. 18
perumiyum ciṟumiyum meyppāṭu eṭṭaṉ
vaḻi maruṅku aṟiyat tōṉṟum eṉpa. 19
uvamap poruḷiṉ uṟṟatu uṇarum
teḷi maruṅku uḷavē tiṟattu iyalāṉa. 20
uvamap poruḷi uṇarum kāli
marīiya marapiṉ vaḻakkoṭu varumē. 21
iraṭṭikkiḷavi iraṭṭi vaḻittē. 22
piṟitoṭu paṭātu piṟappoṭu nōkki
muṉṉi marapiṉ kūṟum kāli
tuṇivoṭu varūum tuṇiviṉōr koḷiṉē. 23
uvamap pōli aintu eṉa moḻipa. 24
taval aruñ ciṟappiṉ at taṉmi nāṭiṉ
viṉiyiṉum payattiṉum uṟuppiṉum uruviṉum
piṟappiṉum varūum tiṟatta eṉpa. 25
kiḻavi colliṉ avaḷ aṟi kiḷavi. 26
tōḻikku āyiṉ nilam peyarntu uriyātu. 27
kiḻavōṟku āyiṉ uraṉoṭu kiḷakkum. 28
ēṉōrkku ellām iṭam varivu iṉṟē. 29
iṉitu uṟu kiḷaviyum tuṉi uṟu kiḷaviyum
uvama maruṅkiṉ tōṉṟum eṉpa. 30
kiḻavōṭku uvamam īr iṭattu urittē. 31
kiḻavōṟku āyiṉ iṭam varivu iṉṟē. 32
tōḻiyum ceviliyum poruntuvaḻi nōkkik
kūṟutaṟku uriyar koḷ vaḻiyāṉa. 33
vēṟupaṭa vanta uvamat tōṟṟam
kūṟiya maruṅkiṉ koḷ vaḻik koḷāal. 34
orīik kūṟalum marīiya paṇpē. 35
uvamat taṉmiyum urittu eṉa moḻipa
payaṉili purinta vaḻakkattāṉa. 36
taṭumāṟu uvamam kaṭi vari iṉṟē. 37
aṭukkiya tōṟṟam viṭuttal paṇpē
niral niṟuttu amittal niral niṟi cuṇṇam
varaṉ muṟi vanta mūṉṟu alaṅkaṭiyē. 38
------------
3.8. ceyyuḷiyal
māttiri eḻuttu iyal aci vaki eṉāa
yātta cīrē aṭi yāppu eṉāa
marapē tūkkē toṭi vaki eṉāa
nōkkē pāvē aḷavu iyal eṉāa
tiṇiyē kikōḷ kūṟṟu vaki eṉāa
kēṭpōr kaḷaṉē kāla vaki eṉāa
payaṉē meyppāṭu ecca vaki eṉāa
muṉṉam poruḷē tuṟi vaki eṉāa
māṭṭē vaṇṇamoṭu yāppu iyal vakiyiṉ
āṟu tali iṭṭa an nāl aintum
ammi aḻaku toṉmi tōlē
viruntē iyipē pulaṉē iḻipu eṉāap
poruntak kūṟiya eṭṭoṭum tokii
nal icip pulavar ceyyuḷ uṟuppu eṉa
vallitiṉ kūṟi vakuttu urittaṉarē. 1
avaṟṟuḷ,
māttiri vakiyum eḻuttu iyal vakiyum
mēl kiḷantaṉavē eṉmaṉār pulavar. 2
kuṟilē neṭilē kuṟil iṇi kuṟil neṭil
oṟṟoṭu varutaloṭu meyp paṭa nāṭi
nērum niriyum eṉṟiciṉ peyarē. 3
iru vaki ukaramoṭu iyintavi variṉē
nērpum niripum ākum eṉpa
kuṟil iṇi ukaram al vaḻiyāṉa. 4
iyalaci mutal iraṇṭu ēṉavi uriyaci. 5
taṉik kuṟil mutalaci moḻi citintu ākātu.6
oṟṟu eḻuttu iyaṟṟē kuṟṟiyalikaram. 7
muṟṟiyalukaramum moḻi citittuk koḷāa
niṟṟal iṉṟē īṟṟu aṭi maruṅkiṉum. 8
kuṟṟiyalukaramum muṟṟiyalukaramum
oṟṟoṭu tōṉṟi niṟkavum peṟumē. 9
aciyum cīrum iciyoṭu cērtti
vakuttaṉar uṇarttal vallōr āṟē. 10
īr aci koṇṭum mū aci puṇarttum
cīr iyintu iṟṟatu cīr eṉappaṭumē. 11
iyalaci mayakkam iyaṟcīr ēṉi
uriyaci mayakkam āciriya uriccīr. 12
muṉ niri uṟiṉum aṉṉa ākum. 13
nēr avaṇ niṟpiṉ iyaṟcīrp pāla. 14
iyalaci īṟṟu muṉ uriyaci variṉē
niriyaci iyala ākum eṉpa. 15
aḷapeṭi acinili ākalum urittē. 16
oṟṟu aḷapeṭuppiṉum aṟṟu eṉa moḻipa. 17
iyaṟcīr iṟuti muṉ nēr avaṇ niṟpiṉ
uriccīr veṇpā ākum eṉpa. 18
vañcic cīr eṉa vaki peṟṟaṉavē
veṇ cīr allā mū aci eṉpa. 19
taṉ pā al vaḻi tāṉ aṭivu iṉṟē. 20
vañci maruṅkiṉ eñciya uriya. 21
veṇpā uriccīr āciriya uriccīr
iṉ pā nēraṭikku oruṅku nili ilavē. 22
kalittaḷi maruṅkiṉ kaṭiyavum peṟāa. 23
kalittaḷi aṭivayiṉ nēr īṟṟu iyaṟcīr
nilikku urittu aṉṟē teriyumōrkkē. 24
vañci maruṅkiṉum iṟuti nillā. 25
icinili niṟiya niṟkuvatu āyiṉ
acinili variyār cīr nili peṟavē. 26
iyaṟcīrp pāṟpaṭuttu iyaṟṟiṉar koḷalē
taḷi vaki citiyāt taṉmiyāṉa. 27
veṇcīr īṟṟaci niriyaci iyaṟṟē. 28
iṉ cīr iyiya varukuvatu āyiṉ
veṇcīr variyār āciriya aṭikkē. 29
an nili maruṅkiṉ vañci uriccīr
oṉṟutal uṭiya ōr oru vaḻiyē. 30
nāṟ cīr koṇṭatu aṭi eṉappaṭumē. 31
aṭi uḷḷaṉavē taḷiyoṭu toṭiyē. 32
aṭi iṟantu varutal il eṉa moḻipa. 33
aṭiyiṉ ciṟappē pāṭṭu eṉappaṭumē. 34
nāl eḻuttu āti āka āṟu eḻuttu
ēṟiya nilattē kuṟaḷaṭi eṉpa. 35
ēḻ eḻuttu eṉpa cintaṭikku aḷavē
īr eḻuttu ēṟṟam av vaḻiyāṉa. 36
pattu eḻuttu eṉpa nēraṭikku aḷavē
otta nāl eḻuttu ēṟṟalaṅkaṭiyē. 37
mū aintu eḻuttē neṭilaṭikku aḷavē
īr eḻuttu mikutalum iyalpu eṉa moḻipa. 38
mū āṟu eḻuttē kaḻineṭiṟku aḷavē
īr eḻuttu mikutalum iyalpu eṉa moḻipa. 39
cīr nilitāṉē aintu eḻuttu iṟavātu
nēr nili vañcikku āṟum ākum. 40
eḻuttu aḷavu eñciṉum cīr nilitāṉē
kuṉṟalum mikutalusm il eṉa moḻipa. 41
uyir il eḻuttum eṇṇappaṭāa
uyirt tiṟam iyakkam iṉmiyāṉa. 42
vañci aṭiyē iru cīrttu ākum. 43
taṉ cīr eḻuttiṉ ciṉmi mūṉṟē. 44
muc cīrāṉum varum iṭaṉ uṭittē. 45
aci kūṉ ākum avvayiṉāṉa. 46
cīr kūṉ ātal nēraṭikku urittē. 47

potuvāy niṟṟal poruḷ vaki eṉpa. 200
av am makkaḷum vilaṅkum aṉṟip
piṟa avaṇ variṉum tiṟavatiṉ nāṭi
tamtam iyaliṉ marapoṭu muṭiyiṉ
at tiṟamtāṉē tuṟi eṉappaṭumē. 201
akaṉṟu poruḷ kiṭappiṉum aṇukiya niliyiṉum
iyaṉṟu poruḷ muṭiya tantaṉar uṇarttal
māṭṭu eṉa moḻipa pāṭṭiyal vaḻakkiṉ. 202
māṭṭum eccamum nāṭṭal iṉṟi
uṭaṉili moḻiyiṉum toṭarnili peṟumē. 203
vaṇṇamtāmē nāl aintu eṉpa. 204
avitām,
pāa vaṇṇam tāa vaṇṇam
vallici vaṇṇam mellici vaṇṇam
iyipu vaṇṇam aḷapeṭi vaṇṇam
neṭuñcīr vaṇṇam kuṟuñcīr vaṇṇam
cittira vaṇṇam nalipu vaṇṇam
akappāṭṭu vaṇṇam puṟappāṭṭu vaṇṇam
oḻuku vaṇṇam orūu vaṇṇam
eṇṇu vaṇṇam akippu vaṇṇam
tūṅkal vaṇṇam ēntal vaṇṇam
uruṭṭu vaṇṇam muṭuku vaṇṇam eṉṟu
āṅku eṉa moḻipa aṟinticiṉōrē. 205
avaṟṟuḷ,
pāa vaṇṇam
coṟcīrttu āki nūṟpāl payilum. 206
tāa vaṇṇam
iṭiyiṭṭu vanta etukittu ākum. 207
vallici vaṇṇam valleḻuttu mikumē. 208
mellici vaṇṇam melleḻuttu mikumē. 209
iyipu vaṇṇam iṭiyeḻuttu mikumē. 210
aḷapeṭi vaṇṇam aḷapeṭi payilum. 211
neṭuñcīr vaṇṇam neṭṭeḻuttup payilum. 212
kuṟuñcīr vaṇṇam kuṟṟeḻuttup payilum. 213
cittira vaṇṇam
neṭiyavum kuṟiyavum nērntu uṭaṉ varumē. 214
nalipu vaṇṇam āytam payilum. 215
akappāṭṭu vaṇṇam
muṭiyāt taṉmiyiṉ muṭintataṉ mēṟṟē. 216
puṟappāṭṭu vaṇṇam
muṭintatu pōṉṟu muṭiyātākum. 217
oḻuku vaṇṇam ōciyiṉ oḻukum. 218
orūu vaṇṇam orīit toṭukkum. 219
eṇṇu vaṇṇam eṇṇup payilum. 220
akippu vaṇṇam aṟuttu aṟuttu oḻukum. 221
tūṅkal vaṇṇam vañci payilum. 222
ēntal vaṇṇam
colliya colliṉ colliyatu ciṟakkum. 223
uruṭṭu vaṇṇam arākam toṭukkum. 224
muṭuku vaṇṇam
aṭi iṟantu ōṭi ataṉ ōraṟṟē. 225
vaṇṇamtāmē ivi eṉa moḻipa. 226
vaṉappu iyaltāṉē vakukkum kāli
cil meṉ moḻiyāṉ tāya paṉuvaliṉ
ammitāṉē aṭi nimirvu iṉṟē. 227
ceyyuḷ moḻiyāṉ cīr puṉintu yāppiṉ
av vakitāṉē aḻaku eṉappaṭumē. 228
toṉmitāṉē
uriyoṭu puṇarnta yāppiṉ mēṟṟē. 229
iḻumeṉ moḻiyāṉ viḻumiyatu nuvaliṉum
paranta moḻiyāṉ aṭi nimirntu oḻukiṉum
tōl eṉa moḻipa tol moḻip pulavar. 230
viruntētāṉum
putuvatu puṉinta yāppiṉ mēṟṟē. 231
ñakāri mutalā ḷakāri īṟṟup
puḷḷi iṟuti iyipu eṉappaṭumē. 232
cēri moḻiyāṉ cevvitiṉ kiḷantu
tērtal vēṇṭātu kuṟittatu tōṉṟiṉ
pulaṉ eṉa moḻipa pulaṉ uṇarntōrē. 233
oṟṟoṭu puṇarnta valleḻuttu aṭaṅkātu
kuṟaḷaṭi mutalā aintu aṭi oppittu
ōṅkiya moḻiyāṉ āṅku avaṇ moḻiyiṉ
iḻipiṉ ilakkaṇam iyintatākum. 234
ceyyuḷ maruṅkiṉ mey peṟa nāṭi
iḻitta ilakkaṇam piḻittaṉa pōla
varuva uḷa eṉiṉum vantavaṟṟu iyalāṉ
tiripu iṉṟi muṭittal teḷḷiyōr kaṭaṉē. 235

3.9. marapiyal
māṟṟa aruñ ciṟappiṉ marapu iyal kiḷappiṉ
pārppum paṟaḻum kuṭṭiyum kuruḷiyum
kaṉṟum piḷḷiyum makavum maṟiyum eṉṟu
oṉpatum kuḻaviyoṭu iḷamip peyarē. 1
ēṟum ēṟṟiyum oruttalum kaḷiṟum
cēvum cēvalum iraliyum kaliyum
mōttiyum takarum utaḷum apparum
pōttum kaṇṭiyum kaṭuvaṉum piṟavum
yātta āṇpāṟ peyar eṉa moḻipa. 2
pēṭiyum peṭiyum peṭṭiyum peṇṇum
mūṭum nākum kaṭamiyum aḷakum
mantiyum pāṭṭiyum piṇiyum piṇavum
antam cāṉṟa piṭiyoṭu peṇṇē. 3
avaṟṟuḷ,
pārppum piḷḷiyum paṟappavaṟṟu iḷami. 4
tavaḻpavitāmum avaṟṟu ōraṉṉa. 5
mūṅkā veruku eli mūvari aṇiloṭu
āṅku avi nāṉkum kuṭṭikku uriya. 6
paṟaḻ eṉappaṭiṉum uṟaḻ āṇṭu illi. 7
nāyē paṉṟi puli muyal nāṉkum
āyum kāli kuruḷi eṉpa. 8
nariyum aṟṟē nāṭiṉar koḷiṉē. 9
kuṭṭiyum paṟaḻum kūṟṟu avaṇ variyār. 10
piḷḷip peyarum piḻippu āṇṭu illi
koḷḷum kāli nāy alaṅkaṭiyē. 11
yāṭum kutiriyum navviyum uḻiyum
ōṭum pulvāy uḷappaṭa maṟiyē. 12
kōṭu vāḻ kuraṅkum kuṭṭi kūṟupa. 13
makavum piḷḷiyum paṟaḻum pārppum
aviyum aṉṉa ap pālāṉa. 14
yāṉiyum kutiriyum kaḻutiyum kaṭamiyum
māṉoṭu aintum kaṉṟu eṉaṟku uriya. 15
erumiyum mariyum variyār āṇṭē. 16
kavariyum karāmum nikar avaṟṟuḷḷē. 17
oṭṭakam avaṟṟoṭu oru vaḻi niliyum. 18
kuñcaram peṟumē kuḻavip peyarkkoṭi. 19
āvum erumiyum atu colappaṭumē. 20
kaṭamiyum mariyum mutal nili oṉṟum. 21
kuraṅkum mucuvum ūkamum mūṉṟum
nirampa nāṭiṉ ap peyarkku uriya. 22
kuḻaviyum makavum āyiraṇṭu allavi
kiḻava alla makkaṭkaṇṇē. 23
piḷḷi kuḻavi kaṉṟē pōttu eṉak
koḷḷavum amiyum ōr aṟivu uyirkkē. 24
nellum pullum nērār āṇṭē. 25
colliya marapiṉ iḷamitāṉē
collum kāli avi ala ilavē. 26
oṉṟu aṟivatuvē uṟṟu aṟivatuvē
iraṇṭu aṟivatuvē ataṉoṭu nāvē
mūṉṟu aṟivatuvē avaṟṟoṭu mūkkē
nāṉku aṟivatuvē avaṟṟoṭu kaṇṇē
aintu aṟivatuvē avaṟṟoṭu ceviyē
āṟu aṟivatuvē avaṟṟoṭu maṉaṉē
nēritiṉ uṇarntōr neṟippaṭuttiṉarē. 27
pullum maraṉum ōr aṟiviṉavē
piṟavum uḷavē ak kiḷip piṟappē. 28
nantum muraḷum īr aṟiviṉavē
piṟavum uḷavē akkiḷip piṟappē. 29
citalum eṟumpum mū aṟiviṉavē
piṟavum uḷavē ak kiḷip piṟappē. 30
naṇṭum tumpiyum nāṉku aṟiviṉavē
piṟavum uḷavē ak kiḷip piṟappē. 31
māvum mākkaḷum ai aṟiviṉavē
piṟavum uḷavē ak kiḷip piṟappē. 32
makkaḷtāmē āṟu aṟivu uyirē
piṟavum uḷavē ak kiḷip piṟappē. 33
oru cār vilaṅkum uḷa eṉa moḻipa. 34
vēḻakku urittē vitantu kaḷiṟu eṉṟal. 35
kēḻaṟkaṇṇum kaṭi vari iṉṟē. 36
pulvāy puli uḻi mariyē kavari
colliya karāmoṭu oruttal oṉṟum. 37
vār kōṭṭu yāṉiyum paṉṟiyum aṉṉa. 38
ēṟpuṭittu eṉpa erumikkaṇṇum. 39
paṉṟi pulvāy uḻiyē kavari
eṉṟu ivi nāṉkum ēṟu eṉaṟku uriya. 40
erumiyum mariyum peṟṟamum aṉṉa. 41
kaṭal vāḻ cuṟavum ēṟu eṉappaṭumē. 42
peṟṟam erumi puli mari pulvāy
maṟṟu ivi ellām pōttu eṉappaṭumē. 43
nīr vāḻ cātiyum atu peṟaṟku uriya. 44
mayilum eḻālum payilat tōṉṟum. 45
iraliyum kaliyum pulvāykku uriya. 46
kali eṉ kāṭci uḻikkum urittē
niliyiṟṟu ap peyar mucuviṉkaṇṇum. 47
mōttiyum takarum utaḷum apparum
yātta eṉpa yāṭṭiṉkaṇṇē. 48
cēval peyarkkoṭi ciṟakoṭu civaṇum
mā irun tūvi mayil alaṅkaṭiyē. 49
āṟṟaloṭu puṇarnta āṇpāṟku ellām
ēṟṟik kiḷavi urittu eṉa moḻipa. 50
āṇpāl ellām āṇ eṉaṟku uriya
peṇpāl ellām peṇ eṉaṟku uriya
kāṇpa avi avi appālāṉa. 51
piṭi eṉ peṇ peyar yāṉi mēṟṟē. 52
oṭṭakam kutiri kaḻuti mari ivi
peṭṭi eṉṉum peyarkkoṭikku uriya. 53
puḷḷum uriya ap peyarkku eṉpa. 54
pēṭiyum peṭiyum nāṭiṉ oṉṟum. 55
kōḻi kūki āyiraṇṭu allavi
cūḻum kāli aḷaku eṉal amiyā. 56
ap peyark kiḻami mayiṟkum urittē. 57
pulvāy navvi uḻiyē kavari
colvāy nāṭiṉ piṇi eṉappaṭumē. 58
paṉṟi pulvāy nāy eṉa mūṉṟum
oṉṟiya eṉpa piṇaviṉ peyarkkoṭi. 59
piṇaval eṉiṉum avaṟṟiṉ mēṟṟē. 60
peṟṟamum erumiyum mariyum āvē. 61
peṇṇum piṇāvum makkaṭku uriya. 62
erumiyum mariyum peṟṟamum nākē. 63
nīr vāḻ cātiyuḷ nantum nākē. 64
mūṭum kaṭamiyum yāṭu ala peṟāa. 65
pāṭṭi eṉpa paṉṟiyum nāyum. 66
nariyum aṟṟē nāṭiṉar koḷiṉē. 67
kuraṅkum mucuvum ūkamum manti. 68
kuraṅkiṉ ēṟṟiṉik kaṭuvaṉ eṉṟalum
maram payil kūkiyik kōṭṭāṉ eṉṟalum
cev vāyk kiḷiyit tatti eṉṟalum
vev vāy verukiṉip pūci eṉṟalum
kutiriyuḷ āṇiṉic cēval eṉṟalum
iruḷ niṟap paṉṟiyi ēṉam eṉṟalum
erumiyuḷ āṇiṉik kaṇṭi eṉṟalum
muṭiya vanta av vaḻakku uṇmiyiṉ
kaṭiyal ākā kaṭaṉ aṟintōrkkē. 69
peṇṇum āṇum piḷḷiyum aviyē. 70
nūlē karakam mukkōl maṇiyē
āyum kāli antaṇarkku uriya. 71
paṭiyum koṭiyum kuṭiyum muracum
naṭi navil puraviyum kaḷiṟum tērum
tārum muṭiyum nērvaṉa piṟavum
terivu koḷ ceṅkōl aracarkku uriya. 72
antaṇāḷarkku uriyavum aracarkku
oṉṟiya varūum poruḷumār uḷavē. 73
paricil pāṭāṇ tiṇit tuṟik kiḻamippeyar
neṭuntaki cemmal eṉṟu ivi piṟavum
poruntac collutal avarkku urittaṉṟē. 74
ūrum peyarum uṭittoḻiṟ karuviyum
yārum cārtti avi avi peṟumē. 75
talimik kuṇac colum tamtamakku uriya
nilimikku ēṟpa nikaḻttupa eṉpa. 76
iṭi iru vakiyōr allatu nāṭiṉ
paṭi vaki peṟāar eṉmaṉār pulavar. 77
vicikaṉ peṟumē vāṇika vāḻkki. 78
mey teri vakiyiṉ eṇ vaki uṇaviṉ
ceytiyum variyār ap pālāṉa. 79
kaṇṇiyum tārum eṇṇiṉar āṇṭē. 80
vēḷāṇ māntarkku uḻutūṇ allatu
il eṉa moḻipa piṟa vaki nikaḻcci. 81
vēntu viṭu toḻiliṉ paṭiyum kaṇṇiyum
vāyntaṉar eṉpa avar peṟum poruḷē. 82
antaṇāḷarkku aracu varivu iṉṟē. 83
villum vēlum kaḻalum kaṇṇiyum
tārum māliyum tērum māvum
maṉ peṟu marapiṉ ēṉōrkkum uriya. 84
aṉṉar āyiṉum iḻintōrkku illi. 85
puṟak kāḻaṉavē pul eṉa moḻipa. 86
akak kāḻaṉavē maram eṉa moḻipa. 87
tōṭē maṭalē ōli eṉṟā
ēṭē itaḻē pāḷi eṉṟā
īrkkē kuli eṉa nērntaṉa piṟavum
pulloṭu varum eṉac colliṉar pulavar. 88
iliyē taḷirē muṟiyē tōṭē
ciṉiyē kuḻiyē pūvē arumpē
naṉi uḷḷuṟutta aṉiyavi ellām
maraṉoṭu varūum kiḷavi eṉpa. 89
kāyē paḻamē tōlē cetiḷē
vīḻoṭu eṉṟu āṅku aviyum aṉṉa. 90
nilam tī nīr vaḷi vicumpoṭu aintum
kalanta mayakkam ulakam ātaliṉ
iru tiṇi aim pāl iyal neṟi vaḻāamit
tirivu il colloṭu taḻāal vēṇṭum. 91
marapunili tirital ceyyuṭku illi
marapu vaḻip paṭṭa colliṉāṉē. 92
marapunili tiriyiṉ piṟitu piṟitu ākum. 93
vaḻakku eṉappaṭuvatu uyarntōr mēṟṟē
nikaḻcci avar kaṭṭu ākalāṉa. 94
marapunili tiriyā māṭciya āki
uri paṭu nūltām iru vaki iyala
mutalum vaḻiyum eṉa nutaliya neṟiyiṉa. 95
viṉiyiṉ nīṅki viḷaṅkiya aṟiviṉ
muṉivaṉ kaṇṭatu mutal nūl ākum. 96
vaḻi eṉappaṭuvatu ataṉ vaḻittu ākum. 97
vaḻiyiṉ neṟiyē nāl vakittu ākum. 98
tokuttal virittal tokiviri moḻipeyarttu
atarppaṭa yāttaloṭu aṉi marapiṉavē. 99
otta cūttiram urippiṉ kāṇṭiki
meyppaṭak kiḷanta vakiyatu āki
īr aiṅ kuṟṟamum iṉṟi nēritiṉ
muppattiru vaki uttiyoṭu puṇariṉ
nūl eṉa moḻipa nuṇaṅku moḻip pulavar. 100
uri eṭuttu ataṉ muṉ yāppiṉum cūttiram
puri tapa uṭaṉpaṭak kāṇṭiki puṇarppiṉum
vitittalum vilakkalum eṉa iru vakiyoṭu
puri tapa nāṭip puṇarkkavum paṭumē. 101
mēṟ kiḷanteṭutta yāppiṉuḷ poruḷoṭu
cil vaki eḻuttiṉ ceyyuṭku āki
collum kāli uri akattu aṭakki
nuṇmiyoṭu puṇarnta oṇmittu āki
tuḷakkal ākāt tuṇimi eyti
aḷakkal ākā arum poruṭṭu āki
pala vakiyāṉum payaṉ teripu uṭiyatu
cūttirattu iyalpu eṉa yāttaṉar pulavar. 102
paḻippu il cūttiram paṭṭa paṇpiṉ
karappu iṉṟi muṭivatu kāṇṭiki ākum. 103
viṭṭu akalvu iṉṟi virivoṭu porunti
cuṭṭiya cūttiram muṭittaṟ poruṭṭā
ētu naṭiyiṉum eṭuttukkāṭṭiṉum
mēvāṅku aminta meyn neṟittu atuvē. 104
cūttirattuṭ poruḷ aṉṟiyum yāppuṟa
iṉṟiyamiyātu iyipavi ellām
oṉṟa urippatu uri eṉappaṭumē. 105
maṟutalik kaṭāa māṟṟamum uṭittāy
taṉ nūlāṉum muṭinta nūlāṉum
aiyamum maruṭkiyum cevvitiṉ nīkki
teṟṟeṉa oru poruḷ oṟṟumi koḷīi
tuṇivoṭu niṟṟal eṉmaṉār pulavar. 106
collappaṭṭaṉa ellā māṇpum
maṟutali āyiṉum maṟṟu atu citivē. 107
citivu il eṉpa mutalvaṉ kaṇṇē. 108
mutal vaḻi āyiṉum yāppiṉuḷ citiyum
vallōṉ puṇarā vāram pōṉṟē. 109
citivu eṉappaṭupavi vaci aṟa nāṭiṉ
kūṟiyatu kūṟal māṟu koḷak kūṟal
kuṉṟak kūṟal miki paṭak kūṟal
poruḷ ila kūṟal mayaṅkak kūṟal
kēṭpōrkku iṉṉā yāppiṟṟu ātal
paḻitta moḻiyāṉ iḻukkam kūṟal
taṉṉāṉ oru poruḷ karutik kūṟal
eṉṉa vakiyiṉum maṉam kōḷ iṉmi
aṉṉa piṟavum avaṟṟu viri ākum. 110
etir maṟuttu uṇariṉ at tiṟattavum aviyē. 111
otta kāṭci utti vaki virippiṉ
nutaliyatu aṟital atikāra muṟiyē
tokuttuk kūṟal vakuttu meyn niṟuttal
moḻinta poruḷoṭu oṉṟa vittal
moḻiyātataṉi muṭṭu iṉṟi muṭittal
vārātataṉāṉ vantatu muṭittal
vantatu koṇṭu vārātatu uṇarttal
muntu moḻintataṉ talitaṭumāṟṟē
oppak kūṟal orutali moḻital
taṉ kōḷ kūṟal muṟi piṟaḻāmi
piṟaṉ uṭaṉpaṭṭatu tāṉ uṭampaṭutal
iṟantatu kāttal etiratu pōṟṟal
moḻivām eṉṟal kūṟiṟṟu eṉṟal
tāṉ kuṟiyiṭutal orutali aṉmi
muṭintatu kāṭṭal āṇi kūṟal
pal poruṭku ēṟpiṉ nallatu kōṭal
tokutta moḻiyāṉ vakuttaṉar kōṭal
maṟutali citittut taṉ tuṇipu urittal
piṟaṉ kōḷ kūṟal aṟiyātu uṭampaṭal
poruḷ iṭiyiṭutal etir poruḷ uṇarttal
colliṉ eccam colliyāṅku uṇarttal
tantu puṇarntu urittal ñāpakam kūṟal
uyttukkoṇṭu uṇarttaloṭu meyppaṭa nāṭic
colliya alla piṟa avaṇ variṉum
colliya vakiyāṉ curuṅka nāṭi
maṉattiṉ eṇṇi mācu aṟat terintukoṇṭu
iṉattiṉ cērtti uṇarttal vēṇṭum
nuṉittaku pulavar kūṟiya nūlē. 112
poruḷatikāram muṟṟiṟṟu
tolkāppiyam muṟṟiṟṟu

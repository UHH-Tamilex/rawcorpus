mukavuri
(I-m patippu )
vēlaṇiyūr, yāḻppāṇattukku aṇmiyil viḷaṅkum ēḻutīvu nāṭukaḷuḷ “liṭaṉ" tīvic
cērntatākum. itu yāḻppāṇap paṭṭiṉattil iruntu ēṟakkuṟiya ēḻu milvari teṉticit
tolivil uḷḷatu. ivvūriṉ kīḻippālēyuḷḷa peruṅkuḷattiṉ kariyilētāṉ īvēla ṇip
peruṅkuḷam muttumāriyammaṉ" vīṟṟirukkiṉṟāḷ* ivvammaṉ mēlētāṉ ippatikam mutaliya
pāṭalkaḷ pāṭappeṟṟavi.
teyvatti makkaḷ vaḻipaṭukiṉṟaṉar. āṉāl avvat teyvattuk kēṟṟa naṉpāṭalkaḷic colli, naṟ
karut tukkaḷic colli vaṇaṅkuvōr – vaḻipaṭuvōr ellāru mallar. ammuṟi terintu
vaḻipāṭu ceyvār cilariṉum cilarē. innūl intavakiyil yātum, coṟpamēṉum
upakāramāyiruttal kūṭum eṉpatu ematu eṇṇam,
ivvammiyiṉ mēṟpāṭappaṭṭa ippatikap pāṭalkaḷil vittu ippāṭal āciriyaṉmār tamatu
yāppu - col- poruḷ eṉpavikaḷiṉ tiṟappāṭṭāl ilakkiya nayaṅkaḷik kiṇṭi
viṭṭirukkiṉṟārkaḷ! ataṉāl avi ammaṉ pakti pāvattōṭu ilakkiya iracikattiyum
koṭukkavallaṉavākiṉṟaṉa. ivarkaḷ ovvoruvar kaṇṭa karuntukkaḷum pāṭaliṉ pōkkukaḷum
ovvoru vaki yāka amintuḷḷaṉa, avaṟṟi avvavviṭaṅkaḷilē kaṇṭukoḷka. innūl ivvammaṉ
paktippāṭalkaḷik koṇṭa oru nūlāvatōṭu "teyvam paravutal" eṉṉum tamiḻttuṟiyil oru
ciṟu ilakkiya nūlākavum amivatu oṉṟākum,
tuppāṭalkaḷōṭu ivaṟṟiṉpiṉ vantucērntaḷḷa pāṭalkaḷiyum ivi aṉittukkum
arumpatavuriyiyum cērttu maṟu tippil veḷiyiṭa eṇṇiyuḷḷōm. ellā vaṟṟukkum namatu
jeṉaṉavūriṟ ḷēyil koṇṭu, namatum, nammuṉṉōr, piṉṉōratum vaḻipāṭṭukkiraṅki
aruḷpuri vēlaṇiyūrp peruṅkuḷattup periyanāyaki taṉi cey vāṉāka. aippāṭalkaḷ
kiṭittuḷḷa oḻuṅkiṉpaṭiyē cērkkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ippāṭalkaḷiyum ivaṟṟiṉ toṭarpāyuḷḷa
piṟapāṭalkaḷiyum pāṭittanta periyār pāl yām eṉṟum naṉṟiyuṭiyōm.
kokkuvil vāḻnarum civanēyac celvarumāṉa tiru. kumāracuvāmippiḷḷiyavarkaḷ ematu
pāṭalkaḷip pārviyiṭṭu iciyilum poruvilum tiruttaṅkaḷ ceytu tantuḷḷaṉar. avarkaḷ
naṉṟiyi yām naṉṟu pōṟṟutum. ipputtakatti ivvaḷavu uyarntamuṟiyil aḻakuṟa
acciṭṭuttanta vivēkāṉanta accukkūṭattārk kum eṉ maṉamārnta naṉṟiyic
celuttukiṉkiṉṟē

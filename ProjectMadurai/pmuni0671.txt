PREFACE

In our life's journey we come across a number of people and learn from
them a lot. Based on such wide experience and acquired knowledge, we
have collected a number of poems from Tamil classics, some popularly
known and some not so popular but equally important. We do hope that
this collection will enthuse students and others to delve more into the
source materials and benefit.
The collection includes poems which lay emphasis on moral values,
societal obligations, and the fruits of living a purposeful life.
We are living in a peculiar situation. A generation is growing up where
youngsters do not know to write or read Tamil, but only speak a little.
The translation in English is targeted mainly to address this problem.
Trust this will enthuse them to learn the Tamil language!
Our ancestral home in Mannargudi bears the door number 104. This is
etched in our memory. Hence, we selected 104 poems!
Thirukkural needs no introduction. But there are poems selected from
books not popular among the general public and students' community. In
order to bridge this gap, we have appended a bibiliography, giving broad
outlines of the source materials. In addition, we observed there are
some words in Tamil which are peculiar to that language, like, ali
(aram) which has multiple meanings, depending on the context. Such words
are also explained for the benefit of the readers.
Errors and ommissions if any, are incidental and we take the entire
responsibility for that.
We thank Mr.S. Edwin Raj and Mr.R.Sankar for helping us to publish this
booklet. We also thank the President, the Secretary and all the members
of "The Rotary Club of Madras Centenary Commemoration" for undertaking
to sponsor this booklet.
This compilation is a humble tribute to our parents.
        S. Manickavasagam, S.Sambandam

ammā (Mother)

patavurai : oru tāy, taṉ makaṉai naṟpaṇpu niṟaintavaṉ eṉappiṟar collak kēḷviyuṟṟapōtu
tāṉ, anta makaṉai peṟṟa kālattil aṭainta makiḻcciyai viṭapperitum makiḻcci aṭaivār.
A mother's joy will exceed the happiness she experi enced at the time of
son's birth, when she learns that he is recognized as a scholar by
learned persons.

patavurai : tuṉpaṅkaḷai poṟuttu, maṉatai aṭakki vāḻa māṭṭātavar nōṉpu nōṟṟal
tuṉpamākum; avvāṟē peṟṟa tāyai parāmarikkāmal viṭutal naṉmai payappatillai .
Observance of religious) rituals without taking care of one's mother
will not yield any benefit.

patavurai : taṉṉai peṟṟa tāyiṉ paciyaik kaṇṭu varunta nērntālum, cāṉṟōr paḻippataṟkuk
kāraṇamāṉa iḻivāṉa ceyalkaḷai oruvar ceyyakkūṭātu.
One should not do things which will be condemnable by Scholars, even
when his/her mother is starving.

patavurai : aracaṉ, āciriyar, tāy, tantai , taṉṉaiviṭa mūttavar, ivarkaḷai tēvarkaḷaip pōlak
karuti vaṇaṅka vēṇṭum eṉpatē aṉaivarukkum potuvāṉa nīti.
King, Teacher, Mother, Father and Elders are to be worshipped and this
is applicable to all.

patavurai : oruvaṉukku kaṇṇaip pōṉṟa ciṟanta uṟuppillai. kulamakaḷukkuk kaṇavaṉaippōl
neruṅkiya uṟaviṉar vēṟillai. peṟṟōrukku makkaḷaippōl oḷiyuḷḷa poruḷkaḷ vēṟillai.
kuḻantaikaḷukkut tāyaippōl kaṭavuḷ vēṟu ētumillai .
Eyes are very important organs of the body; nobody is a better relation
than her husband to a wife; children are the crown jewels of the
parents, and for children, mother is their God.

patavurai : ovvoruvarum taṅkaḷiṉ ammā, appāvai uṇavu, nalla āṭai koṭuttu taṉṉuṭaṉ nalla
illattil parāmarittu varavēṇṭum.
Everyone should take care of their mother and father by providing food,
clothes and shelter for all time to come.
7. aṉṉaiyum pitāvum muṉṉaṟi teyvam.
oḷavaiyār - koṉṟai vēntaṉ -1
patavurai : oruvaraippeṟṟa tāyum, tantaiyum teyvattiṟkuc camamāṉavarkaḷ.
Parents are like our gods.

2. appā (Father)
8. tantai makaṟku āṟṟum naṉṟi avaiyattu
munti yiruppac ceyal. tirukkuṟaḷ - 67
patavurai : taṉ makaṉukku oru tantai ceyyattakka nallutavi, kaṟṟavar avaiyil taṉ makaṉ
muntiyirukkum paṭiyāka,
avaṉai kalviyil mēmpaṭac ceytalākum.
A father's primary duty is to facilitate good education to his son, so
that he will be among eminent scholars.
9. makaṉ tantaikku āṟṟum utavi ivaṉtantai
eṉṉōṟṟāṉ koleṉuñ col. tirukkuṟaḷ - 70
patavurai :
oru makaṉ taṉ tantaikku ceyyattakka kaimmāṟu, ivaṉuṭaiya tantai ivaṉai makaṉākap peṟa eṉṉa
tavam ceytāṉō eṉṟu piṟar pukaḻntu colvatu.
A son's aim should be to excel in all walks of life, so that people
wonder what a noble father he is!
10. pantamillāta maṉaiyiṉ vaṉappiṉṉā
tantaiyillāta putalva ṉaḻakiṉṉā ....
kapilar- iṉṉānāṟpatu, veṇpā-1
patavurai : cuṟṟamillāta ilvāḻkkaikku aḻakillai. atupōla tantaiyillāta putalvaṉukku
aḻakillai.
A family life without relatives lacks beauty; fatherless son lacks
(guidance) beauty.
11. .... yeñcā viḻuccī rirumutu makkaḷaik
kaṇṭeḻutal kālai yiṉitu.
pūtañcēntaṉār- iṉiyavai nāṟpatu, veṇpā-18
patavurai : mikka ciṟappuṭaiya tāy tantaiyarai, kālaiyil avarkaḷ irukkumiṭam ceṉṟu,
pātaṅkaḷil vīḻntu eḻutal iṉimaiyāṉatu.
It is worthy to pay obeisance to parents everyday.

aṟam (Charity)
12. eṉpi lataṉai veyilpōlak kāyumē
aṉpi lataṉai aṟam. tirukkuṟaḷ - 77
patavurai : elumpu illāta uṭaluṭaṉ vāḻum puḻuvai, veyil kāyntu varuttuvatu pōl,
aṉpu illāta uyirai aṟam - tarmam varuttum.
Just like a worm squirms under hot sun, compassionless person will wilt
under dharma.
13. aṉṟaṟivām eṉṉātu aṟañceyka maṟṟatu
poṉṟuṅkāl poṉṟāt tuṇai. tirukkuṟaḷ - 36
patavurai : iḷaiñarāka uḷḷavar, piṟkālattil pārttuk koḷḷalām eṉṟu eṇṇāmal aṟam ceyya
vēṇṭum.
atuvē iṟakkum taruvāyil tuṇaiyākum.
Never postpone charitable deeds; that alone will give everlasting
company, till the end of life.
14. poruḷ uṭaiyāṉ kaṇṇatē, pōkam, aṟaṉum
aruḷ uṭaiyāṉ kaṇṇatē ākum, aruḷ uṭaiyāṉ
ceyyāṉ paḻi; pāvam cērāṉ; puṟamoḻiyum
uyyāṉ; piṟaṉ cevikku uyttu. ciṟupañcamūlam - veṇpā -1
patavurai : poruḷ uṭaiyavaṉ iṉpam aṭaivāṉ. aruḷ uṭaiyavaṉ aṟam ceyvāṉ. attakaiya
aruḷuḷḷavaṉ paḻiyaiyum, pāvattaiyum, piṟarai paḻipaṭakkūṟum puṟamoḻikaḷaik kūṟutalaiyum
ceyya māṭṭāṉ.
Richman will get pleasure out of his wealth. Blessed soul will do good
things. Such enlightened soul will not talk ill (of others), will not
commit sinful acts and will not blame others.
15. aṟañceypavarkkum aṟavaḻi nōkkit
tiṟanterintu ceytakkāl celvaḻi naṉṟām.
puṟañceyya, celvam perukum; aṟañceyya
allavai nīṅkiviṭum.
mūṉṟuṟai araiyaṉār - paḻamoḻi nāṉūṟu - veṇpā-364
patavurai : aṟam ceypavarum, takuti uṭaiyavarkaḷukkē ataṉaic ceyvataṉāl tāṉ, aṟattiṉ
payaṉai uṇmaiyākavē aṭaivārkaḷ. aṟam ceyya pāvam nīṅkiviṭum. “aṟam ceyya allavai
nīṅkiviṭum” eṉpatu paḻamoḻi.
One should do good deeds to others (who deserve such help), then only
they will get real benefit from such acts of charity. (Do good to
others, and bad things will go away from you).
16. ītaṟkucceyka poruḷai! aṟaneṟi
cērtaṟkuc ceyka, perunūlai - yātum
aruḷpurintu colluka collai! immūṉṟum
iruḷ ulakam cērāta āṟu. nallātaṉār- tirikaṭukam, veṇpā - 90
patavurai : tāṉ cērtta poruḷai piṟarukkuk koṭuttu utavutal, aṟaneṟiyil vāḻvataṟku
ciṟanta nūlkaḷaip paṭittal, ellōriṭattilum aṉpāṉa coṟkaḷaiyē payaṉ paṭuttutal -
immūṉṟu ceyalkaḷum, iṟantapiṉ narakattiṟku cellāmal taṭukkakkūṭiya vaḻikaḷ.
Wealth is for charity; for a dharmic life seek the company of good
books. Mind your language while talking to others. These three, if
followed scrupulously will ensure happy end of life.
17. pulnuṉimēl nīrpōl nilaiyāmai eṉṟeṇṇi
iṉṉiṉiyē ceyka aṟaviṉai, iṉṉiṉiyē
niṉṟāṉ, iruntāṉ, kiṭantāṉ taṉkēḷ alaṟac
ceṉṟāṉ eṉappaṭutalāl. nālaṭiyār-veṇpā -29
patavurai : inta ulakai vāḻkkai, ciṟiya pulnuṉi mēl uḷḷa nilaiyillāta nīrttuḷi
pōṉṟatu eṉṟu eṇṇi, aṟac ceyalkaḷai ovvoru nāḷum ceyka. nēṟṟuvarai nalamuṭaṉ
iruntāṉ, iṉṟu uṭal nalamillāmal kiṭantāṉ, ippōtu iṟantu viṭṭāṉ eṉṟu taṉatu
cuṟṟattār aḻuvar eṉṟa niṉaippil, aṟac ceyalkaḷai ovvoru nāḷum ceyka.
Do good deeds every day, as if life is like dew on the tip of a grass;
so, don't postpone. Life is transient; one who was alive yesterday, may
take ill and die today. Therefore, while alive do good deeds.
18. aḻukkāṟu avāvekuḷi iṉṉāccol nāṉkum
iḻukkā iyaṉṟatu aṟam. tirukkuṟaḷ - 35
patavurai :
poṟāmai, ācai, kōpam, kaṭuñcol ākiya inta nāṉku kuṟṟaṅkaḷukkum iṭaṅkoṭukkāmal,
avaṟṟai vilakki vāḻvatē aṟamākum.
It is best to be far away from the four evils, namely jealous, lust,
anger and harsh words.
19. maṟantum piṟaṉkēṭu cūḻaṟka cūḻiṉ
aṟañcūḻum cūḻntavaṉ kēṭu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 204
patavurai : piṟarkkut tīmaiyait tarum tīyaceyalkaḷai oruvar maṟantum niṉaikkakkūṭātu.
niṉaittāl, eṇṇiya -vaṉukkuk kēṭu viḷaiyumāṟu aṟam - tarmam eṇṇum.
Never ever think of harming others - if done, you will be harmed by
'dharma' in the same manner.
kalvi (Education)
20. kaṟka kacaṭaṟak kaṟpavai kaṟṟapiṉ
niṟka ataṟkut taka tirukkuṟaḷ - 391
patavurai : oruvar kaṟpataṟkut takunta nūlkaḷaik kuṟṟamaṟa kaṟka vēṇṭum, avvāṟu kaṟṟapiṉ,
kaṟṟa kalvi muṟaippaṭi vāḻtal vēṇṭum.
Learn appropriately all that is required to live unblemished life.
21. ūṇoṭu, kūṟai, eḻuttāṇi, puttakam
pēṇoṭum eṇṇum, eḻuttu, ivai māṇoṭu
kēṭṭu eḻuti, ōti, vāḻvārkku īyntār- immaiyāṉ
vēṭṭu eḻuta vāḻvār, virintu. ēlāti-veṇpā-63
patavurai :
ūkkattuṭaṉ kaṟkum māṇavarkaḷukku uṇaviṉaiyum, uṭaiyaiyum, eḻuttāṇiyum, eḻutu
poruḷkaḷ) nūlum koṭuttu utavukiṉṟavarkaḷ ippiṟappil celvarāy vāḻvar.
Those who supply food, clothes, books and stationery to students will
prosper.
22. pūvātu kāykkum maram uḷa; naṉṟu aṟivār
mūvātu mūttavar, nūl vallār; tāvā,
vitaiyāmai nāṟuva vittu uḷ; mētaikku
uraiyāmaic cellum uṇarvu ciṟupañcamūlam - veṇpā-20
patavurai : pūvātu kāykkum maram pōla vayatil iḷaiyavarum, aṟiviṉāl mūttavarāvār.
pātti kaṭṭi vitaikkāmalē muḷaikkiṉṟa vitaipōla, piṟar collit tarāmalēyē
aṟivuṭaiyavarkaḷukku aṟivu vaḷarum.
Some trees bear fruits without flowering; likewise, even young persons
will be taken as matured by their exemplary behaviour. Sometimes seeds
sprout informally; likewise, some gain knowledge without formal coaching
or guidance.
23. kēṭil viḻuccelvam kalvi oruvaṟku
māṭalla maṟṟai yavai. tirukkuṟaḷ - 400
patavurai : oruvarukku aḻivu illāta ciṟanta celvam kalviyē ākum. kalviyait tavira maṟṟa
poruḷkaḷ attakaiya ciṟappuṭaiya celvam alla.
Education is the greatest of all wealth and all other materials, pale in
comparison.
24. kaḷarnilattup piṟanta uppiṉaic cāṉṟōr
viḷainilattu nelliṉ viḻumitāk koḷvar
kaṭai nilattōrāyiṉum kaṟṟaṟintōrait
talainilattu vaikkappaṭum. nālaṭiyār-veṇpā-133
patavurai : uvar nilattil viḷaiyum uppiṉai cāṉṟōr, naṉcey nilattil viḷainta nel -
arici uṇavuṭaṉ kalantu virumpic cāppiṭuvārkaḷ. atupōl kīḻkuṭi piṟanta
kaṟṟavarkaḷaiyum, cāṉṟōr uyarkuṭi makkaḷākak karutuvārkaḷ.
Salt is obtained from saline water. Scholars will treat it as equivalent
to paddy grown in fertile farmland. Similarly, people from low social
status, if learned, will be equated with the higher societal people.
25. pattukkoṭuttum patiyiruntu vāḻviṉitē
vittukkuṟ ṟuṇṇā viḻuppa mika viṉitē
paṟpala nāḷum paḻutiṉṟip pāṅkuṭaiya
kaṟṟaliṟ kāḻiṉiya til.
pūtañcētaṉār - iṉiyavai nāṟpatu - veṇpā - 40
patavurai : pattu poruḷ koṭuttāyiṉum uḷḷūriliruntu vāḻtal iṉitu. vitaikkeṉa
vaittuḷḷa tāṉiyattai uṇṇāta ceyal mika iṉitu. naṉmaiyuṭaiya nūlkaḷai pala nāḷum
kuṟaivillāta teḷivuṭaṉ kaṟpatum iṉitu.
The following habits are ideal : 1) Live in your place of birth at any
cost. 2) Not consuming seeds meant for sowing. Studying good books to
lead better life.
maḻai (Rain)
26. keṭuppatūuṅ keṭṭārkkuc cārvāymaṟ ṟāṅkē
eṭuppatūum ellām maḻai. tirukkuṟaḷ - 15
patavurai : peyyāmal ulaka vāḻkkaiyaik keṭukka vallatu maḻai. maḻai illāmal, vaḷam keṭṭu
kaṣṭappaṭṭavarukku tuṇaiyāka kākka vallatum maḻaiyākum.
Rain is the elixir of life. It helps people suffering from drought, but
excessive rainfall is harmful resulting in deluge.
27. nellukkiṟaittanīr vāykkāl vaḻiyōṭip
pullukkum āṅkē pociyumām - tol ulakil
nallār oruvar uḷarēl avar poruṭṭu
ellārkkum peyyum maḻai. oḷavaiyār - mūturai - veṇpā-10
patavurai : vayalil uḷḷa neṟpayirukku pāyccappaṭum nīr, anta vayalil uḷḷa pullukkum
payaṉpaṭum. atupōla inta ulakil nallavar oruvar iruntālum, avarukkākap peyyum maḻai,
tīyavarkaḷukkum payaṉpaṭum. nallavar peṟum celvam, ellōrukkum payaṉpaṭum.
Water irrigation for paddy crop allows grass also to grow! Similarly,
rain does not discriminate good and bad people. Rain benefits all. In
the same way, the wealth earned by a good man helps the poor and the
needy.
28. vāṉam curuṅkiṉ tāṉam curuṅkum.

patavurai : ivvulakiṟku maḻainīr tēvaiyāṉa aḷavu kiṭaikkā viṭil, vaṟiyavarkaḷukku
kiṭaikkum tāṉam kuṟaintu viṭum.
Rain is the elixir of life. If there is not rain, there will be no
prosperity, and consequently charity will be a casualty.
29. nīriṉṟu amaiyātu ulakeṉiṉ yāryārkkum

Life without water is unimaginable. Drought conditions will result in
indisciplined and chaotic behaviour of people.
30. kaimmāṟu vēṇṭā kaṭappāṭu mārimāṭṭu

There are philonthrophists in the world who serve the society without
expecting anything in return, just like rain.
uḻavuttoḻil (Farming)
31. uḻutuṇṭu vāḻvārē vāḻvārmaṟ ṟellām
toḻutuṇṭu piṉcel pavar. tirukkuṟaḷ - 1033
patavurai : uḻavuttoḻil ceytu ataṉāl kiṭaittatai uṇṭu vāḻkiṉṟavarē urimaiyōṭu
vāḻkiṉṟavar. maṟṟavar ellōrum piṟarait toḻutu uṇṭu piṉ celkiṉṟavarē.
Farmers (because of the important position they hold in society by
producing edible products) will be like a fulcrum of the society and the
rest will revolve around them.
32. āṟṟaṅkaraiyiṉ maramum aracaṟiya
vīṟṟirunta vāḻvum viḻum aṉṟē- ēṟṟam
uḻutuṇṭu vāḻvataṟku oppillai kaṇṭīr

oḷavaiyār - nalvaḻi - veṇpā -12

āṟṟaṅkaraiyil āṟṟu nīrai uṭkoṇṭu vaḷamuṭaṉ irunta maramum, oru nāḷ viḻuntu viṭum.
aracaṉē eṇṇip pārkkumpaṭi perumitattōṭu vāḻnta vāḻvum oru nāḷ viḻuntu viṭum.
ākavē uḻavuttoḻilai ceytu, ataṉ viḷaiccalai uṇṭu vāḻtalē perumai. itaṟku oppāṉa

Even big trees on the banks of a river will fall one day, just like
persons well recognized by a king may eventually come to a naught; but a
farmer will always flourish and lead a dignified life.
33. kiḷaiñarkku utavātāṉ celvamum paiṅkūḻ
viḷaiviṉkaṇ pōṟṟāṉ uḻavum iḷaiyaṉāyk

The following three are sure to take one to the path of decline and
destruction.
1) Amassing wealth without helping relatives. 2) Failing to do farming
operations at the right time, and 3) Alcoholic addiction.
34. cīraittēṭiṉ ēraittēṭu.

Agriculture is a sure way to success and fame (in life).

Following acts will ensure heavenly ascendence : creating a tank (for
storing water), planting trees, paving roads, converting barren to
fertile land and digging wells for water.
illaṟam (Family Life)
36. aṟaṉeṉap paṭṭatē ilvāḻkkai aḵtum

A happy family life is the bed rock of every society; if such a family
is appreciated by all, it is all the more better.
37. nalviruntu ōmpaliṉ naṭṭāḷam; vaikalum
il puṟañceytaliṉ īṉṟa tāy - tolkuṭiyiṉ
makkaḷ peṟaliṉ, maṉaikkiḻatti, immūṉṟum

Being a good hostess, she is a good friend. As she looks after the
welfare of the family members, she is considered a kind-hearted woman.
Giving birth to children makes her a good mother. Such is the world of
chaste woman.

ellārum ceṉṟaṅketir koḷvar – illāṉai
illāḷum vēṇṭāḷ; maṟṟīṉṟeṭutta tāyvēṇṭāḷ
cellātu avaṉ vāyiṟ col.

avaṉai ellōrum etirkoṇṭu varavēṟpar. kaiyil poruḷ illāta ēḻaiyāka vāḻntāl, avaṉai
maṉaiviyum, peṟṟa tāyum kūṭa, matikka māṭṭārkaḷ. avaṉ vāyiliruntu varum coṟkaḷ

A wealthy person even if illiterate will be honoured; whereas, a poor
man will not be liked by his wife and even mother. His words will carry
no weight.
39. maṉaikku viḷakkam maṭavār maṭavār
taṉakkut takaicāl putalvar maṉakkiṉiya
kātaṟ putalvarkkuk kalviyē; kalvikkum,
ōtiṟ pukaḻcāl uṇarvu.

kalvikku viḷakku meyyuṇarvu.
Wife is the light of the family. Good children bring happiness to
mother. Education is a source of joy for children, and enlightenment is
the hall mark of education.
40. illāḷ akattirukka illātatu oṉṟillai

valikiṭanta māṟṟam uraikkumēl avvil

muraṇpāṭākavē pēcik koṇṭiruntāl anta nilaiyil avaṉ vāḻum vīṭu, puli

A good wife will fulfill all the requirements of a family life.
Conversely, if she is uncouth, the family life will be like living with
a tigress.

utavi (Help)
41. utavi varaittaṉṟu utavi, utavi

patavurai : kaimmāṟāka ceyyum utavi, muṉ ceyta utaviyiṉ aḷavai uṭaiyatu aṉṟu. anta

The value of the assistance rendered by a virtuous man, to a needy
person, is valued by the nobility and use fulness to the recipient.
42. uṟakkum tuṇaiyatōr ālamvit tīṇṭi
iṟappa niḻaṟpayantā aṅku - aṟappayaṉum
tāṉ ciṟitāyiṉum takkār kaippaṭṭakkāl

ālamarattu vitai ciṟiyatāka iruntālum, niḻal tarum mikapperiya maramāka vaḷarum. atupōl
nallavar īṭṭiya celvattai ciṟitu peṟṟālum, poruḷai peṟṟavar takutiyaip poṟuttu,
tāṉamākap peṟṟa celvam vāṉ aḷavukkup perukum.
The banyan seed though small grows to a mighty tree providing ample
shade. Likewise help from a virtuous per son, received by a worthy,
though small, will multiply mani fold. (Similar view is expressed in
chhandogya-Upanishad - Sama Veda book).
43. ceyyāmaṟ ceyta utavikku vaiyakamum
vāṉakamum āṟṟa laritu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 101

Unexpected help from unknown person is incompara ble, and no amount of
praise will compensate such a help.
aṭakkam (Humbleness)
44. ellārkkum naṉṟām paṇital avaruḷḷum
celvarkkē celvan takaittu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 125
patavurai : paṇivuṭaiyarāka iruttal potuvāka ellōrkkum nallatākum. avarkaḷuḷ,
ciṟappākac celvarkku aṭakkam, maṟṟoru celvam ākum.
Humility is an asset; particularly, it is an extra asset even to a
wealthy person.
45. yākāvā rāyiṉum nākākka kāvākkāl
cōkāppar colliḻukkup paṭṭu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 127
patavurai : kākka vēṇṭiyavaṟṟuḷ evaṟṟaik kākkā viṭṭālum, nāvaiyāvatu kākka vēṇṭum.
avvāṟu kākkat tavaṟiṉāl, col kuṟṟattil akappaṭṭut tuṉpappaṭuvar.
Appropriate speech is important, lest one is likely to give improper
speech and suffer adverse consequences thereof.
46. aṭakkam uṭaiyār aṟivilar eṉṟeṇṇik
kaṭakkak karutavum vēṇṭā -maṭait talaiyil
ōṭumīṉ ōṭa uṟumīṉ varumaḷavum
vāṭi irukkumām kokku.
oḷavaiyār-mūturai-veṇpā-16
patavurai :
taṉṉaṭakkattuṭaṉ iruppavaraip pārttu, ivar aṟivillātavar, eṉṟu eṇṇi avarai vella
niṉaikkak kūṭātu. nīr pāyum maṭai vāyilil ciṟiya mīṉkaḷ ōṭumpōtu vāṭṭattuṭaṉ
pārttuk koṇṭirukkum kokku, periya mīṉ vantatum kauvikkoḷvatu pōṉṟatu, valimai

Humble and simple behaviour does not mean a per son is useless. A stork
is known to wait patiently to pick up a large fish from the stream;
similar is the behaviour of a competent person who is otherwise calm and
composed.
47. orumaiyuḷ āmaipōl aintaṭakkal āṟṟiṉ
eḻumaiyum ēmāp puṭaittu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 126
patavurai: oru piṟappil, āmai pōl aimpoṟikaḷaiyum aṭakkiyāḷa vallavaṉāṉāl, atu
avaṉukku ēḻu piṟappilum pātukākkum ciṟappu uṭaiyatu.
If one exercises control over his five senses like a tortoise, he will
enjoy a good life in this birth and also in future births.
īkai (Charity)
48. vaṟiyārkkoṉṟu īvatē īkaimaṟ ṟellām
kuṟiyetirppai nīra tuṭaittu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 221
patavurai : vaṟiyavarkku oru poruḷaik koṭuppatē īkai eṉappaṭum. maṟṟavarkkuk
koṭuppatellām payaṉai etirpārttuk koṭukkum taṉmai uṭaiyatu.
Charity is that which reaches the poor and the needy. Charity to
well-to-do has the underlying meaning of expecting something in return
and hence not counted as charity.
49. ēṟṟakaim māṟṟāmai eṉṉāṉum tāmvaraiyār
āṟṟātārkku īvatām āṇkaṭaṉ - āṟṟiṉ
taṇcērppa māṟivārk kītal
polikaṭaṉ eṉṉum peyarttu.
nālaṭiyār - veṇpā -98
patavurai : vaṟumai kāraṇamāka, kai nīṭṭi oruvar utavi kēṭṭāl, avarukku maṟukkāmal utavi
ceyvatu ovvoru maṉitaṉiṉ kaṭamaiyākum. peṟṟa utaviyai oruvar tirumpac ceyya
iyalumāṉāl, atu avarukkuk kaṭaṉ koṭuttal pōṉṟatu.
Give to him who cannot recompense you. A gift to those who can return
the same in kind is like an investment. It is the duty of a man to help
the needy person who seeks help.
poruḷ - celvam (Wealth)
50. aṟṟār aḻipaci tīrttal aḵtoruvaṉ
peṟṟāṉ poruḷvaip puḻi. tirukkuṟaḷ - 226
patavurai : ēḻaikaḷiṉ vayiṟṟuppaciyai tīrkka vēṇṭum. atuvē poruḷ peṟṟa oruvar
apporuḷait taṉakku piṟkālattil utavumāṟu cērttu vaikkum iṭamākum.
Alleviating hungry of the poor is a sure way to invest your wealth.
51. aruḷillārkku avvulakam illai poruḷillārkku
ivvulakam illāki yāṅku. tirukkuṟaḷ - 247
patavurai : poruḷ illārkku ivvulaka vāḻkkai payaṉ illātatu pōla, piṟa uyirkaḷiṭattil

One can not enjoy life on earth without wealth, and one can not enjoy a
heavenly future if there is no benediction.
52. pāṭupaṭṭut tēṭip paṇattaip putaittuvaittuk
kēṭukeṭṭa māṉiṭarē kēḷuṅkaḷ - kūṭuviṭṭuṅku
āvitāṉ pōyiṉapiṉ yārē aṉupavippār
pāvikāḷ antappaṇam. oḷavaiyār-nalvaḻi-22

maṟaittu vaittirukkum kēṭukeṭṭa maṉitarkaḷē kēḷuṅkaḷ, nīṅkaḷ iṟanta piṟaku accelvattai
yārtāṉ aṉupavippārkaḷ?
You senseless people, who hide your wealth, have no idea as to who will
enjoy such hidden wealth after your death; so, don't do it.
53. illārai ellōrum eḷḷuvar celvarai
ellārum ceyvar ciṟappu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 752
patavurai :
poruḷ illātavarai vēṟu nalla kuṇaṅkaḷ iruntālum, ellōrum ikaḻvar. celvarai vēṟu nalla
kuṇaṅkaḷ illāviṭiṉum ellōrum ciṟappu ceyvār.
Poverty stricken man will be treated with disrespect even if he has some
good traits; but, wealthy will be looked upon with courtesy, even if
found to be a tainted man. (Generally, emphasis is given to wealth and
not to character)
54. iṉṉāmai vēṇṭiṉ iravu eḻuka! innilattu

iṟantapiṟaku taṉṉuṭaṉ tuṇaivara vēṇṭumāṉāl, tarumam ceyya vēṇṭum. piṟarai veṟṟi koḷḷa
vēṇṭumāṉāl kōpattai viṭa vēṇṭum.
Begging will bring insult to self. One must do something worthy to
establish himself in society. One must live dharmic life to ascend the
path to heaven and shed anger if he wants to win over others.
iṟappu (Death)
55. uṟaṅku vatupōlum cākkāṭu uṟaṅki
viḻippatu pōlum piṟappu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 339
patavurai : iṟappu eṉappaṭuvatu oruvarukku uṟakkam varutalaip pōṉṟatu, piṟappu

Death is like sleep, birth is like awakening from sleep.
56. iṟantār iṟantār aṉaiyar ciṉattait
tuṟantār tuṟantār tuṇai. tirukkuṟaḷ - 310
patavurai : ciṉattil aḷavu kaṭantu ceṉṟavar iṟantavaraip pōṉṟavar. kōpattai aṭiyōṭu

A sense of extreme anger is like death and those who are adept in
controlling anger are comparable to saints.
ūḻviṉai muṉviṉai ( Karma)
57. pariyiṉum ākāvām pālalla uyttuc

Fate will decide destiny. It can ensure good tidings and take away the
same at will.
58. uṟaṟpāla nīkkal uṟuvarkkum ākā

vaṟappiṉ taruvārum illai ataṉaic

Not even saints can drive away predestined ills and everything happens
according to some design (fate). In time of drought nobody can bring the

59. muṟpakal ceyyiṉ piṟpakal viḷaiyum.

You reap what you sow.
60. varunti aḻaittālum vārāta vārā
poruntuvaṉa pōmiṉ eṉṟāl pōkā- iruntēṅki
neñcam puṇṇāka neṭuntūram tām niṉaittu
tuñcuvatē māntar toḻil.
oḷavaiyār - nalvaḻi veṇpā - 5

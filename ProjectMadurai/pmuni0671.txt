PREFACE

In our life's journey we come across a number of people and learn from
them a lot. Based on such wide experience and acquired knowledge, we
have collected a number of poems from Tamil classics, some popularly
known and some not so popular but equally important. We do hope that
this collection will enthuse students and others to delve more into the
source materials and benefit.
The collection includes poems which lay emphasis on moral values,
societal obligations, and the fruits of living a purposeful life.
We are living in a peculiar situation. A generation is growing up where
youngsters do not know to write or read Tamil, but only speak a little.
The translation in English is targeted minly to address this problem.
Trust this will enthuse them to learn the Tamil language!
Our ancestral home in Mannargudi bears the door number 104. This is
etched in our memory. Hence, we selected 104 poems!
Thirukkural needs no introduction. But there are poems selected from
books not popular among the general public and students' community. In
order to bridge this gap, we have appended a bibiliography, giving broad
outlines of the source materials. In addition, we observed there are
some words in Tamil which are peculiar to that language, like, ali
(aram) which has multiple meanings, depending on the context. Such words
are also explined for the benefit of the readers.
Errors and ommissions if any, are incidental and we take the entire
responsibility for that.
We thank Mr.S. Edwin Raj and Mr.R.Sankar for helping us to publish this
booklet. We also thank the President, the Secretary and all the members
of "The Rotary Club of Madras Centenary Commemoration" for undertaking
to sponsor this booklet.
This compilation is a humble tribute to our parents.
        S. Manickavasagam, S.Sambandam

ammā (Mother)

patavuri : oru tāy, taṉ makaṉi naṟpaṇpu niṟintavaṉ eṉappiṟar collak kēḷviyuṟṟapōtu
tāṉ, anta makaṉi peṟṟa kālattil aṭinta makiḻcciyi viṭapperitum makiḻcci aṭivār.
A mother's joy will exceed the happiness she experi enced at the time of
son's birth, when she learns that he is recognized as a scholar by
learned persons.

patavuri : tuṉpaṅkaḷi poṟuttu, maṉati aṭakki vāḻa māṭṭātavar nōṉpu nōṟṟal
tuṉpamākum; avvāṟē peṟṟa tāyi parāmarikkāmal viṭutal naṉmi payappatilli .
Observance of religious) rituals without taking care of one's mother
will not yield any benefit.

patavuri : taṉṉi peṟṟa tāyiṉ paciyik kaṇṭu varunta nērntālum, cāṉṟōr paḻippataṟkuk
kāraṇamāṉa iḻivāṉa ceyalkaḷi oruvar ceyyakkūṭātu.
One should not do things which will be condemnable by Scholars, even
when his/her mother is starving.

patavuri : aracaṉ, āciriyar, tāy, tanti , taṉṉiviṭa mūttavar, ivarkaḷi tēvarkaḷip pōlak
karuti vaṇaṅka vēṇṭum eṉpatē aṉivarukkum potuvāṉa nīti.
King, Teacher, Mother, Father and Elders are to be worshipped and this
is applicable to all.

patavuri : oruvaṉukku kaṇṇip pōṉṟa ciṟanta uṟuppilli. kulamakaḷukkuk kaṇavaṉippōl
neruṅkiya uṟaviṉar vēṟilli. peṟṟōrukku makkaḷippōl oḷiyuḷḷa poruḷkaḷ vēṟilli.
kuḻantikaḷukkut tāyippōl kaṭavuḷ vēṟu ētumilli .
Eyes are very important organs of the body; nobody is a better relation
than her husband to a wife; children are the crown jewels of the
parents, and for children, mother is their God.

patavuri : ovvoruvarum taṅkaḷiṉ ammā, appāvi uṇavu, nalla āṭi koṭuttu taṉṉuṭaṉ nalla
illattil parāmarittu varavēṇṭum.
Everyone should take care of their mother and father by providing food,
clothes and shelter for all time to come.
7. aṉṉiyum pitāvum muṉṉaṟi teyvam.
oḷaviyār - koṉṟi vēntaṉ -1
patavuri : oruvarippeṟṟa tāyum, tantiyum teyvattiṟkuc camamāṉavarkaḷ.
Parents are like our gods.

2. appā (Father)
8. tanti makaṟku āṟṟum naṉṟi aviyattu
munti yiruppac ceyal. tirukkuṟaḷ - 67
patavuri : taṉ makaṉukku oru tanti ceyyattakka nallutavi, kaṟṟavar aviyil taṉ makaṉ
muntiyirukkum paṭiyāka,
avaṉi kalviyil mēmpaṭac ceytalākum.
A father's primary duty is to facilitate good education to his son, so
that he will be among eminent scholars.
9. makaṉ tantikku āṟṟum utavi ivaṉtanti
eṉṉōṟṟāṉ koleṉuñ col. tirukkuṟaḷ - 70
patavuri :
oru makaṉ taṉ tantikku ceyyattakka kimmāṟu, ivaṉuṭiya tanti ivaṉi makaṉākap peṟa eṉṉa
tavam ceytāṉō eṉṟu piṟar pukaḻntu colvatu.
A son's aim should be to excel in all walks of life, so that people
wonder what a noble father he is!
10. pantamillāta maṉiyiṉ vaṉappiṉṉā
tantiyillāta putalva ṉaḻakiṉṉā ....
kapilar- iṉṉānāṟpatu, veṇpā-1
patavuri : cuṟṟamillāta ilvāḻkkikku aḻakilli. atupōla tantiyillāta putalvaṉukku
aḻakilli.
A family life without relatives lacks beauty; fatherless son lacks
(guidance) beauty.
11. .... yeñcā viḻuccī rirumutu makkaḷik
kaṇṭeḻutal kāli yiṉitu.
pūtañcēntaṉār- iṉiyavi nāṟpatu, veṇpā-18
patavuri : mikka ciṟappuṭiya tāy tantiyari, kāliyil avarkaḷ irukkumiṭam ceṉṟu,
pātaṅkaḷil vīḻntu eḻutal iṉimiyāṉatu.
It is worthy to pay obeisance to parents everyday.

aṟam (Charity)
12. eṉpi lataṉi veyilpōlak kāyumē
aṉpi lataṉi aṟam. tirukkuṟaḷ - 77
patavuri : elumpu illāta uṭaluṭaṉ vāḻum puḻuvi, veyil kāyntu varuttuvatu pōl,
aṉpu illāta uyiri aṟam - tarmam varuttum.
Just like a worm squirms under hot sun, compassionless person will wilt
under dharma.
13. aṉṟaṟivām eṉṉātu aṟañceyka maṟṟatu
poṉṟuṅkāl poṉṟāt tuṇi. tirukkuṟaḷ - 36
patavuri : iḷiñarāka uḷḷavar, piṟkālattil pārttuk koḷḷalām eṉṟu eṇṇāmal aṟam ceyya
vēṇṭum.
atuvē iṟakkum taruvāyil tuṇiyākum.
Never postpone charitable deeds; that alone will give everlasting
company, till the end of life.
14. poruḷ uṭiyāṉ kaṇṇatē, pōkam, aṟaṉum
aruḷ uṭiyāṉ kaṇṇatē ākum, aruḷ uṭiyāṉ
ceyyāṉ paḻi; pāvam cērāṉ; puṟamoḻiyum
uyyāṉ; piṟaṉ cevikku uyttu. ciṟupañcamūlam - veṇpā -1
patavuri : poruḷ uṭiyavaṉ iṉpam aṭivāṉ. aruḷ uṭiyavaṉ aṟam ceyvāṉ. attakiya
aruḷuḷḷavaṉ paḻiyiyum, pāvattiyum, piṟari paḻipaṭakkūṟum puṟamoḻikaḷik kūṟutaliyum
ceyya māṭṭāṉ.
Richman will get pleasure out of his wealth. Blessed soul will do good
things. Such enlightened soul will not talk ill (of others), will not
commit sinful acts and will not blame others.
15. aṟañceypavarkkum aṟavaḻi nōkkit
tiṟanterintu ceytakkāl celvaḻi naṉṟām.
puṟañceyya, celvam perukum; aṟañceyya
allavi nīṅkiviṭum.
mūṉṟuṟi ariyaṉār - paḻamoḻi nāṉūṟu - veṇpā-364
patavuri : aṟam ceypavarum, takuti uṭiyavarkaḷukkē ataṉic ceyvataṉāl tāṉ, aṟattiṉ
payaṉi uṇmiyākavē aṭivārkaḷ. aṟam ceyya pāvam nīṅkiviṭum. “aṟam ceyya allavi
nīṅkiviṭum” eṉpatu paḻamoḻi.
One should do good deeds to others (who deserve such help), then only
they will get real benefit from such acts of charity. (Do good to
others, and bad things will go away from you).
16. ītaṟkucceyka poruḷi! aṟaneṟi
cērtaṟkuc ceyka, perunūli - yātum
aruḷpurintu colluka colli! immūṉṟum
iruḷ ulakam cērāta āṟu. nallātaṉār- tirikaṭukam, veṇpā - 90
patavuri : tāṉ cērtta poruḷi piṟarukkuk koṭuttu utavutal, aṟaneṟiyil vāḻvataṟku
ciṟanta nūlkaḷip paṭittal, ellōriṭattilum aṉpāṉa coṟkaḷiyē payaṉ paṭuttutal -
immūṉṟu ceyalkaḷum, iṟantapiṉ narakattiṟku cellāmal taṭukkakkūṭiya vaḻikaḷ.
Wealth is for charity; for a dharmic life seek the company of good
books. Mind your language while talking to others. These three, if
followed scrupulously will ensure happy end of life.
17. pulnuṉimēl nīrpōl niliyāmi eṉṟeṇṇi
iṉṉiṉiyē ceyka aṟaviṉi, iṉṉiṉiyē
niṉṟāṉ, iruntāṉ, kiṭantāṉ taṉkēḷ alaṟac
ceṉṟāṉ eṉappaṭutalāl. nālaṭiyār-veṇpā -29
patavuri : inta ulaki vāḻkki, ciṟiya pulnuṉi mēl uḷḷa niliyillāta nīrttuḷi
pōṉṟatu eṉṟu eṇṇi, aṟac ceyalkaḷi ovvoru nāḷum ceyka. nēṟṟuvari nalamuṭaṉ
iruntāṉ, iṉṟu uṭal nalamillāmal kiṭantāṉ, ippōtu iṟantu viṭṭāṉ eṉṟu taṉatu
cuṟṟattār aḻuvar eṉṟa niṉippil, aṟac ceyalkaḷi ovvoru nāḷum ceyka.
Do good deeds every day, as if life is like dew on the tip of a grass;
so, don't postpone. Life is transient; one who was alive yesterday, may
take ill and die today. Therefore, while alive do good deeds.
18. aḻukkāṟu avāvekuḷi iṉṉāccol nāṉkum
iḻukkā iyaṉṟatu aṟam. tirukkuṟaḷ - 35
patavuri :
poṟāmi, āci, kōpam, kaṭuñcol ākiya inta nāṉku kuṟṟaṅkaḷukkum iṭaṅkoṭukkāmal,
avaṟṟi vilakki vāḻvatē aṟamākum.
It is best to be far away from the four evils, namely jealous, lust,
anger and harsh words.
19. maṟantum piṟaṉkēṭu cūḻaṟka cūḻiṉ
aṟañcūḻum cūḻntavaṉ kēṭu. tirukkuṟaḷ - 204
patavuri : piṟarkkut tīmiyit tarum tīyaceyalkaḷi oruvar maṟantum niṉikkakkūṭātu.
niṉittāl, eṇṇiya -vaṉukkuk kēṭu viḷiyumāṟu aṟam - tarmam eṇṇum.
Never ever think of harming others - if done, you will be harmed by
'dharma' in the same manner.
kalvi (Education)
20. kaṟka kacaṭaṟak kaṟpavi kaṟṟapiṉ
niṟka ataṟkut taka tirukkuṟaḷ - 391
patavuri : oruvar kaṟpataṟkut takunta nūlkaḷik kuṟṟamaṟa kaṟka vēṇṭum, avvāṟu kaṟṟapiṉ,
kaṟṟa kalvi muṟippaṭi vāḻtal vēṇṭum.
Learn appropriately all that is required to live unblemished life.
21. ūṇoṭu, kūṟi, eḻuttāṇi, puttakam
pēṇoṭum eṇṇum, eḻuttu, ivi māṇoṭu
kēṭṭu eḻuti, ōti, vāḻvārkku īyntār- immiyāṉ
vēṭṭu eḻuta vāḻvār, virintu. ēlāti-veṇpā-63
patavuri :
ūkkattuṭaṉ kaṟkum māṇavarkaḷukku uṇaviṉiyum, uṭiyiyum, eḻuttāṇiyum, eḻutu
poruḷkaḷ) nūlum koṭuttu utavukiṉṟavarkaḷ ippiṟappil celvarāy vāḻvar.
Those who supply food, clothes, books and stationery to students will
prosper.
22. pūvātu kāykkum maram uḷa; naṉṟu aṟivār
mūvātu mūttavar, nūl vallār; tāvā,
vitiyāmi nāṟuva vittu uḷ; mētikku
uriyāmic cellum uṇarvu ciṟupañcamūlam - veṇpā-20
patavuri : pūvātu kāykkum maram pōla vayatil iḷiyavarum, aṟiviṉāl mūttavarāvār.
pātti kaṭṭi vitikkāmalē muḷikkiṉṟa vitipōla, piṟar collit tarāmalēyē
aṟivuṭiyavarkaḷukku aṟivu vaḷarum.
Some trees bear fruits without flowering; likewise, even young persons
will be taken as matured by their exemplary behaviour. Sometimes seeds
sprout informally; likewise, some gin knowledge without formal coaching
or guidance.
23. kēṭil viḻuccelvam kalvi oruvaṟku
māṭalla maṟṟi yavi. tirukkuṟaḷ - 400
patavuri : oruvarukku aḻivu illāta ciṟanta celvam kalviyē ākum. kalviyit tavira maṟṟa
poruḷkaḷ attakiya ciṟappuṭiya celvam alla.
Education is the greatest of all wealth and all other materials, pale in
comparison.
24. kaḷarnilattup piṟanta uppiṉic cāṉṟōr
viḷinilattu nelliṉ viḻumitāk koḷvar
kaṭi nilattōrāyiṉum kaṟṟaṟintōrit
talinilattu vikkappaṭum. nālaṭiyār-veṇpā-133
patavuri : uvar nilattil viḷiyum uppiṉi cāṉṟōr, naṉcey nilattil viḷinta nel -
arici uṇavuṭaṉ kalantu virumpic cāppiṭuvārkaḷ. atupōl kīḻkuṭi piṟanta
kaṟṟavarkaḷiyum, cāṉṟōr uyarkuṭi makkaḷākak karutuvārkaḷ.
Salt is obtined from saline water. Scholars will treat it as equivalent
to paddy grown in fertile farmland. Similarly, people from low social
status, if learned, will be equated with the higher societal people.
25. pattukkoṭuttum patiyiruntu vāḻviṉitē
vittukkuṟ ṟuṇṇā viḻuppa mika viṉitē
paṟpala nāḷum paḻutiṉṟip pāṅkuṭiya
kaṟṟaliṟ kāḻiṉiya til.
pūtañcētaṉār - iṉiyavi nāṟpatu - veṇpā - 40
patavuri : pattu poruḷ koṭuttāyiṉum uḷḷūriliruntu vāḻtal iṉitu. vitikkeṉa
vittuḷḷa tāṉiyatti uṇṇāta ceyal mika iṉitu. naṉmiyuṭiya nūlkaḷi pala nāḷum
kuṟivillāta teḷivuṭaṉ kaṟpatum iṉitu.
The following habits are ideal : 1) Live in your place of birth at any
cost. 2) Not consuming seeds meant for sowing. Studying good books to
lead better life.
maḻi (Rain)
26. keṭuppatūuṅ keṭṭārkkuc cārvāymaṟ ṟāṅkē
eṭuppatūum ellām maḻi. tirukkuṟaḷ - 15
patavuri : peyyāmal ulaka vāḻkkiyik keṭukka vallatu maḻi. maḻi illāmal, vaḷam keṭṭu
kaṣṭappaṭṭavarukku tuṇiyāka kākka vallatum maḻiyākum.
Rain is the elixir of life. It helps people suffering from drought, but
excessive rinfall is harmful resulting in deluge.
27. nellukkiṟittanīr vāykkāl vaḻiyōṭip
pullukkum āṅkē pociyumām - tol ulakil
nallār oruvar uḷarēl avar poruṭṭu
ellārkkum peyyum maḻi. oḷaviyār - mūturi - veṇpā-10
patavuri : vayalil uḷḷa neṟpayirukku pāyccappaṭum nīr, anta vayalil uḷḷa pullukkum
payaṉpaṭum. atupōla inta ulakil nallavar oruvar iruntālum, avarukkākap peyyum maḻi,
tīyavarkaḷukkum payaṉpaṭum. nallavar peṟum celvam, ellōrukkum payaṉpaṭum.
Water irrigation for paddy crop allows grass also to grow! Similarly,
rin does not discriminate good and bad people. Rain benefits all. In
the same way, the wealth earned by a good man helps the poor and the
needy.
28. vāṉam curuṅkiṉ tāṉam curuṅkum.
oḷaviyār - koṉṟi vēntaṉ - 82

Farmers (becuse of the important position they hold in society by

A happy family life is the bed rock of every society; if such a family

Wife is the light of the family. Good children bring happiness to

aṭakkam (Humbleness)

Humility is an asset; particularly, it is an extra asset even to a

If one exercises control over his five senses like a tortoise, he will

īki (Charity)

Charity is that which reaches the poor and the needy. Charity to
well-to-do has the underlying meaning of expecting something in return
and hence not counted as charity.

Give to him who cannot recompense you. A gift to those who can return
the same in kind is like an investment. It is the duty of a man to help
the needy person who seeks help.

Alleviating hungry of the poor is a sure way to invest your wealth.

You senseless people, who hide your wealth, have no idea as to who will

Begging will bring insult to self. One must do something worthy to

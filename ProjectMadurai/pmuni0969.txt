Source :
mēliccitamparamākiya srīpērūr pacci nāyakiyammi piḷḷittamiḻ.
iḵtu, kōyamuttūrukkaṭutta orāṭṭukkuppi ceṭṭipāḷiyattilirunta
vitvacikāmaṇiyākiya makā-srī. muttu niṉātti mutaliyāravarkaḷ iyaṟṟiyatu.
itaṉi ivaratu kōṭpēraṉākiya kōyamuttūr ṭisṭirikṭu muṉcīp kōrṭṭu vakkīl
makā -srī, aiyācāmi mutaliyāravarkaḷ vēṇṭukōḷiṉpaṭi
kōyamuttūr vittuvāṉ makā -srī, kantacāmi mutaliyāravarkaḷāṟ paricōtikkappaṭṭu,
kōyamuttūr kalāniti accukkūṭattil patippikkappaṭṭatu.
cittirapānu varu. puraṭṭāci mātam.
itaṉ vili aṇā - 2.

      kuḻittōṭiṇikka varukavoṇmūk kuttiporutta varukamulit

varumāteṉavan tutittamaṭa mayilēvaruka varukavē
      vaḷar taṉpēri marakatappeṇ vāḻvēvaruka varukavē. (3)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

vantacivaṉip puṇarumaṭa mayilēvaruka varukavē
      vaḷarteṉpēri marakatappeṇ vāḻvēvaruka varukavē. (4)

poṉṉulavuniṉatupaya caraṇavaṇiyāṭanī poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉaṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē. (1)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
mēvivaḷroḷitaḻiya maṇiyūcaṉmiciyammi viḻivuṭaṉiruntupalavāy
      mikumaṇikaṭicikaṭoṟu miṉṉiṭavuvantāṭu vēliyatupūviṉvaḷaruṅ
kāviviḻivaṭamoṉṟu kalivāṇivaṭamoṉṟu kāciṉimiṉvaṭamtoṉṟu
      kaṉakaṉāṭṭaracivaṭa moṉṟuceṅkikoṇṭu kaviṉoḻukupāṭalpāṭit
tāvukuḻiyūcaliṭa
      niṉṉūcalāṭṭavun tayavatucurantukaṉiviṟ
ṟaṇṇaḷimikuntapē rāṉantaveḷḷamoru taṭipaṭātuyirkaḷparukap
pūviyiḷamaḻalitikaḻ pavaḷavāyumiyammi poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē. (2)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
tuṅkamikupalapaṇiyi ṉoḷitaḻintiṭumūca ṟuṉṉiyeṉṉaṉṉinīyē
      tōṟṟupoḻutiṉilamara tuntumikaṟaṅkavuñ coṉmātupaḻiyapāṭaṟ
kaṅki mutaṉatimakaḷir maṅkalamiyampavuṅ kaṇavarkaṇkaḷikūravuṅ
      kakaṉamicimalarmāri kaṭavuḷarkaḷvīcamiku kantiruvarpāṭaṉmaliyat
tiṅkaḷorukōṭiyoḷi yāṭalkaṇṭaṭiyavarkaḷ cintikaḷikūravaḷarun
      tiruvūcaṉmakimiyuṭa ṉīyiruntaruḷumatu ceyamaṅkipukalaveḷitō
poṅkamikuvaḷamipuṉi pērinakarukkaraci poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉaṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē. (3)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
maṟikaṇṭutoḻavariya niṉatucaraṇulakilvaḷar maṉpatikaḷcaravacaramum
      vāṉamoṭupātāḷa varkkamettaṉikaḷum vantutoḻaveḻitiṉmaruvi
niṟikoṇṭamalarcaruvu mūcaliyaṭuttumalar naṅkikalivāṇipāṭa
      ṉaḷiṉcāraṇamakaḷiṉ naṭaṉamuṭaṉaruḷperuku nātavētāntamutalē
niṟikaṇṭavirimalari ṉaṟiperukimarukulavu neṭuvaḷamikuntapoḻilcūḻ
      nilimitarukamalavaya lūṟupērinakarmaruvu nityakalyāṇatiruvē
poṟituṉṉupuviyiluyar veḷḷiyaṅkiriyaraci poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉaṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē. (4)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
tōkimayiṉaṭaṉamiṭu cāliyiṟcōliyiṟ ṟuṅkamarukiṟkaḻaṉiyiṟ
      ṟuṟicaruvunaṉṉati maruṅkiṉiṟpaṇṇiyiṟ ṟuṉṉupaṇimaṉṉuvataṉiṟ
cākikaṭaḻittanila mettaṉiyatiṟkulavu carvacaramutalaveviyun
      taraṇimiciyiṟcaṉit toḷiyātavaḷamiyiṟ ṟakkapiṟavāneṟiyatāyt
tēkamuṉameppuviyi ṉuṟpavittavarkaḷun teṉpērinakarimaruvic
      cilapakaliruntuṭalam viṭumaḷavilamminiṉ ceyyamaṭiyiṟṟaliyuṟum
pōkamatutantuṉatu tāṉikoṭuvīcukoṭi poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉaṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉucalāṭiyaruḷē. (5)

kaviyiṉmucuvataṉamuṟu vaḷavaṉorukāvalaṉ kaṭalpuṭiyuṭuttavulakiṟ
      katikaṭarutavamiṟivar natikaṇmuṟimuṟiyiṉvaḻi
      kaṭukiviṉiyakalvatilatāy
navilumaṟicolviṉavi niṉatupatiyiṉimaruvi natiyiṉiṭimuḻukiyeḻalu
      karavuruviṉmataṉuruvi lariyuruvilvaḷamimiku navapuṉitavuruvamatuvā
yaviroḷiyiṉvataṉamuṟu mitaṉmakimimaṟipukalu matuvalatupukalavacamō
      vaḵtuṇartaṉiṉaturimi vaḻiyaṭiyālatucila raṟivaritukaṭavuṇmakimi
puvimakiminatimakimi puṉiyumvaḷanakararaci poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉaṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē. (6)

eḻilivayiṟatukiḻiya vuyarperumipuṉitaruvi ṉiyalparamacivaṉinikarā
      yitupuṉaliṉuṭaṉaḷikaṇ malarkuṭiyavarunaṟiyu miṉiyakaṉiyiḻiyiratamum
vaḻiyanilamatuniravi yaṇi muṭukineṭiyakavar maruvivayalkaḷiṉumulavi
      varukamukukaṉalkatali periyaceṉalivivaḷaṟa
      varupuṉalkaḷperukiyataṉmēṟ
ṟaḻuvinatiyuṭaṉmaruvi neṭitunilamatuturuvi cakalapuvikaḷumvaḷarvatāyt
      tāḷameṟitarutiriyi ṉakaṭukiḻitaravoḻuku taruṇanativaḷiyavaḷamār
poḻilkaḷpuṉivaḷamimiku maracavaṉanakararaci poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉaṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē. (7)

aṟavāḻiyaṉiyaniṉa taruḷvāriyevaruṟiṉu mavarātimutaṉmipeṟuvā
      ratuvātipuvikaṉaka maṉuvāticurarkaḷiṟu maṉiyārkaḷaramakaḷirē
yuṟavārumaṉamatuvu mamutākumalarmuḻutu mulavātanitipacuvelā
      muyartēṉutaruviṉamu matutēvarulakamitu vuriyātimaṟipukalumē
tuṟavātumuyalumavar tuṟavāramuyalumavar tukaṭīravuṟumuṟimiyāyt
      toliyātapavanilimi yaṟamāṟiyiruviṉika ṭoṭarpākumakaviṭarunīḷ
puṟavātikeṭavaruḷcey piṟavātaneṟiyaraci poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉaṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉūcalāṭiyaruḷē. (8)

niriyāṉapaḻaviṉiyi ṉuruvāṉacaṭalamitu neṭitūḻicuḻalvatakala
      neṭitāṉpataviyati ṉuyircōravarucaṉaṉa nilimāṟavalakiṉmuṉivar
tarimītumuyalariya tavayōkamatuperitu tavirātumuyalamuṭiyun
      taramāṉapataviyati ṉiṭimūḻkiyaruḷperumi taruyōkamutavumatuteṉ
piriyākiyiracitanal variyūṭupoḻikaruṇi peṟupāṉmiyuṟuvateṉavum
      picakāmalaripiramar toḻanāḷumakimikoṭu perunīriṉulavumamutap
poruḷākivalamaruvu piṟavātaneṟiyaraci poṉṉūcalāṭi yaruḷē
      puṉitaruṭaṉaṉavarata naṭaṉamarakatavaṉiti poṉṉūcalāṭi yaruḷē. (9)

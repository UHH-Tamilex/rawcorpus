mutalāvatu pākam - putu veḷḷam

mutalāvatu attiyāyam - āṭittirunāḷ

muppattu nāṉkām attiyāyam - latā maṇṭam

muppatteṭṭām attiyāyam - nantiṉiyiṉ ūṭal
muppattoṉpatām attiyāyam - ulakam cuḻaṉṟatu!
nāṟpatām attiyāyam - iruḷ māḷiki
nāṟpattoṉṟām attiyāyam - nilavaṟi
nāṟpattireṇṭām attiyāyam - naṭpukku aḻakā?
nāṟpattu mūṉṟām attiyāyam - paḻiyāṟi
nāṟpattu nāṉkām attiyāyam - "ellām avaḷ vēli!"
nāṟpattu aintām attiyāyam - kuṟṟam ceyta oṟṟaṉ
nāṟpattāṟām attiyāyam - makkaḷiṉ muṇumuṇuppu
nāṟpattēḻām attiyāyam - īcāṉa civapaṭṭar
nāṟpatteṭṭām attiyāyam - nīrc cuḻalum viḻic cuḻalum
nāṟpattoṉpatām attiyāyam - vintiyilum vinti!

aimpattiraṇṭām attiyāyam - kiḻavaṉ kalyāṇam
aimpattu mūṉṟām attiyāyam - maliyamāṉ āvēcam
aimpattu nāṉkām attiyāyam - "nañciṉum koṭiyāḷ"
aimpattintām attiyāyam - nantiṉiyiṉ kātalaṉ
aimpattāṟām attiyāyam - antappura campavam
aimpattēḻām attiyāyam - māyamōkiṉi

paṉṉiraṇṭām attiyāyam - kuruvum cīṭaṉum

patiṉintām attiyāyam - iravil oru tuyarak kural
patiṉāṟām attiyāyam - cuntara cōḻariṉ pirami
patiṉēḻām attiyāyam - māṇṭavar mīḷvatuṇṭō?
patiṉeṭṭām attiyāyam - turōkattil etu koṭiyatu?
pattoṉpatām attiyāyam - "oṟṟaṉ piṭippaṭṭāṉ!"
irupatām attiyāyam - iru peṇ pulikaḷ
irupattoṉṟām attiyāyam - pātāḷac ciṟi
irupattiraṇṭām attiyāyam - ciṟiyir cēntaṉ amutaṉ
irupattumūṉṟām attiyāyam - nantiṉiyiṉ nirupam
irupattu nāṉkām attiyāyam - aṉalil iṭṭa meḻuku
irupattintām attiyāyam - mātōṭṭa mānakaram
irupattāṟām attiyāyam - irattam kēṭṭa katti
irupattēḻām attiyāyam - kāṭṭup pāti
irupatteṭṭām attiyāyam - irājapāṭṭi
irupattoṉpatām attiyāyam - yāṉip pākaṉ
muppatām attiyāyam - tavanta yuttam
muppattoṉṟām attiyāyam - "ēlēla ciṅkaṉ" kūttu
muppattiraṇṭām attiyāyam - kiḷḷi vaḷavaṉ yāṉi
muppattu mūṉṟām attiyāyam - cili coṉṉa ceyti
muppattu nāṉkām attiyāyam - anurātapuram
muppattintām attiyāyam - ilaṅkic ciṅkātaṉam
muppattāṟām attiyāyam - takutikku matippu uṇṭā?
muppattu ēḻām attiyāyam - kāvēri ammaṉa
muppatteṭṭām attiyāyam - cittiraṅkaḷ pēciṉa!
muppatteṉpatām attiyāyam - "itō yuttam!"
nāṟpatām attiyāyam - mantirālōcaṉi
nāṟpattoṉṟām attiyāyam - "atō pāruṅkaḷ!"
nāṟpattiraṇṭām attiyāyam - pūṅkuḻaliyiṉ katti
nāṟpattu mūṉṟām attiyāyam - "nāṉ kuṟṟavāḷi!"
nāṟpattu nāṉkām attiyāyam - yāṉi miraṇṭatu!
nāṟpattintām attiyāyam - ciṟik kappal
nāṟpattāṟām attiyāyam - poṅkiya uḷḷam
nāṟpattēḻām attiyāyam - pēyc cirippu
nāṟpatteṭṭām attiyāyam - 'kalapati'yiṉ maraṇam
nāṟpattoṉpatām attiyāyam - kappal vēṭṭi
aimpatām attiyāyam - "āpattutavikaḷ"
aimpattoṉṟām attiyāyam - cuḻik kāṟṟu
aimpattiraṇṭām attiyāyam - uṭinta paṭaku
aimpattu mūṉṟām attiyāyam - apaya kītam

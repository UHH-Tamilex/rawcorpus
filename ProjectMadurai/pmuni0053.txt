mullippāṭṭu ārāycciyuri

1. pāṭṭiṉiyalpu

mullippāṭṭu eṉpatip paṟṟit teriya vēṇṭuvaṉa ellām ārāyummuṉ, pāṭṭu eṉpatu
ettakiyatu? eṉṟu ārāyntu aṟintu koḷḷal vēṇṭum. piṉṟik kālattut tamiḻp pulavar
pāṭṭeṉpatu iṉṉateṉṟē aṟiyārāyp putupputu muṟiyāṟ coṟkaḷik kōttup poruḷ
āḻamiṉṟic ceyyuḷ iyaṟṟukiṉṟār. paṇṭik kālattut taṇṭamiḻp pulavarō pāṭṭu eṉpataṉ
iyalpi naṉkaṟintu nalamuṭiya ceyyuṭkaḷ palappala iyaṟṟiṉār. iṅṅaṉam muṟkālattārāṟ
ceyyappaṭṭa pāṭṭiṉ iyalpoṭu māṟupaṭṭup piṟkālattār uṇmi piṟaḻntu pāṭiya
ceyyuṭkaḷik kaṇṭu māṇākkar pāṭṭiṉiyalpu aṟiyātu mayaṅkuvārākaliṟ, pāṭṭu eṉpatu
iṉṉateṉpataṉi oru ciṟitu viḷakkuvām.

ulaka iyaṟkiyiṟ kaṇ mutalāṉa pulaṉkaḷukku viḷaṅkit tōṉṟum aḻaki yellān taṉṉakattē
neruṅkap potintuvittup, piṉ avaṟṟi nam aṟiviṉiṭattē pulappaṭuvaṇṇan tōṟṟuvittup,
poruḷ nikaḻcciyaṭu māṟupaṭutal illā iṉiya ōciyuṭaṉ icintu naṭipeṟum iyalpiṉi
uṭiyatutāṉ pāṭṭeṉṟu aṟital vēṇṭum. iṉṉum eṅkeṅku nam aṟivit tamvayap
paṭuttukiṉṟa pēraḻakum pēroḷiyum peruntaṉmiyum viḷaṅkit tōṉṟukiṉṟaṉavō aṅkellām
pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉṟē aṟitalvēṇṭum. itaṉi viḷakkik kāṭṭumiṭattup, pēraḻakāṟ ciṟanta ōr
araci tāṉ mēṟpōrttirunta nīlappaṭṭu āṭiyiṉic ciṟitu ciṟitāka nīkkip, piṉ ataṉic
curuṭṭik kīḻē eṟintu viṭṭut tuyil oḻintu, oḷiviḷaṅku taṉ naḷimukaṅkāṭṭi
eḻuntati yappa, iruṭkūṭṭañ curuṇṭu maṭaṅki alikaṭaliṟ ceṉṟu aṭaṅkiviṭumāṟu iḷiya
ñāyiṟu urukkit tiraṭṭiya pacumpoṟ ṟiraḷipōlat taḷataḷaveṉak kīḻt ticiyil tōṉṟavum,
atticiyiṉ parappellām poṉ uritta kaṟpōṟ polintu tikaḻavum, pacumi poṉmi nīlam
civappu veṇmi mutalāṉa niṟa vēṟupāṭuḷḷa poṉ veḷḷikaḷ uruki ōṭukiṉṟa nilam pōla
vāṉ iṭamellām palavaṇṇamāy virintu viḷaṅkavum, kariyamukilka ḷellāñ cevvarakku vaḻitta
akaṉṟa tiric cīlikaḷ pōlavum āṅkāṅkuc collutaṟ kariya pēroḷiyaṭu tikaḻavum ulakamaṅki
nakittāṟ pōlap putumiyuṟṟut tōṉṟum viṭiyaṟkāla aḻakellām pāṭṭeṉṟē
aṟitalvēṇṭum. ā! iṅṅaṉan tōṉṟum av viṭiyaṟkāla aḻakiṉik kaṇṭuviyanta vaṇṇamāy
mīṉvaliyaṭu kaṭaṟkariyil niṟkuñ cempaṭavaṉik kāṭṭiṉuñ ciṟanta pulavaṉ yār?

av viṭiyaṟkāliyilē mulli nilattu mēypparkaḷ āṇkaṉṟukaḷit toḻuvattilē tāmpiṉāṟ
kaṭṭivittu āṉnirikaḷi aṭuttuḷḷa maliccāraliṟ koṇṭupōyp paciya pul mēyaviṭṭut tām
maranaḻaliṟ cāyntiruntu koṇṭu, tamakketirē paccilip pōrvi mēṟkoṇṭu kariya mukilkaḷ
neṟṟi taḻuvik kiṭappap peruntaṉmiyaṭu vāṉ aḷāvit tōṉṟum maliyiṉi aṇṇāntu
pārttavarāy avarkaḷ accamum makiḻcciyum aṭiyum pōtu aṅkum pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉṟē aṟital
vēṇṭum.

kātaliṉāṟ kaṭṭuṇṭa iḷiñarum makaḷirum nekiḻāta kātalaṉpiṉ mikutiyāl tōḷaṭu tōḷ
piṇiyat taḻuvikkoṇṭu, maliyaṭivārattil uḷḷa pūñcōlikaḷiṟ kaḷippāy ulavuntoṟun
tūṅkaṇaṅ kuruvikaḷ marakkiḷikaḷil viyappāṉa kūṭu kaṭṭutaliyum; marap pontukaḷi
liruntu maṇippuṟākkaḷ kūvutaliyum; āṇmayilkaḷ tam aḻakiya tōkiyiṉi virittup
peṭimayil kaṇṭukaḷippa orupuṟam āṭutaliyum; maliyiliruntoḻukum aruvinīr kūḻāṅ
kaṟpaṭiyiṉmēṟ ciluciluveṉṟu ōṭivantu accōliyiṉ oru pakkattuḷḷa āḻnta kuṭṭattil
nirampit tuḷumpa, ataṉkaṇ uḷḷa centāmari mukiḻkaḷ akaṉṟa ilikaḷiṉmēl itaḻkaḷi
virittu mikac civappāy alar taliyum virumpikkaṇṭu, naṟumaṇaṅ kamaḻum pūkkaḷi
maraṅkaḷiṉiṉṟun tāvip paṟittuk kariya kūntalil māṟimāṟi aṇintuñ, civakkap paḻutta
kovvikkaṉi pōṉṟa tam itaḻkaḷ aḻunta muttam vittukkoṇṭun, tēṉ oḻukiṉāleṉa iṉiya
nēyamoḻikaḷ pēcikkoṇṭum avarkaḷ cellumiṭattu aṅkum pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉṟē aṟital
vēṇṭum.

curuṅkac colluṅkāl eṅkellām namatuṇarvik kavarkiṉṟa pēraḻaku ulaka iyaṟkiyiṟ
kāṇappaṭumō aṅkellām pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉpatu teḷiyappaṭum. āyiṉum, oru nallicip
pulavaṉāl iyaṟṟappaṭukiṉṟa pāṭṭuppōla atu nūliṉiṭattē kāṇappaṭuvatilliyē yeṉiṉ;
naṉku viṉāyiṉāy, oru nūliṉkaṇ eḻutappaṭṭu, ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉ aḻaki namatuḷḷattiṟ
tōṉṟak kāṭṭi namakku uvappuṇarvu payakkuñ colliṉ tokutiyāṉa pāṭṭu nūliṉkaṇ
eḻutappaṭukiṉṟa vaṭivuṭiya parupporuḷākum; ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉ aḻakōṭu oruṅkottu
niṉṟu, kaṇ mutalāṉa pulaṉvaḻip pukuntu namakku uvap puṇarvu mikutiyiṉi varuvikkum
pāṭṭu vaṭivam illāta nuṇporuḷākum. iṅṅaṉa mākaliṉ ulaka iyaṟkiyilellām pāṭṭu
uṇṭeṉpatu tuṇipēyā meṉka.

allāmalum, uyir vāḻnāḷil ovvoru nāḷum nammuṭiya niṉivukaḷellām uṇavu
tēṭutalilum, poruḷ tokuppatilum, maṉivimakkaṭku vēṇṭuvaṉa tiraṭṭik koṭuttalilum,
piṟar iṭṭa ūḻiyañ ceytalilumākap palavāṟu citaṟi arumi perumiyiṉṟik koṉṉē kaḻintu
pōkum makkaḷuṭiya niṉivukaḷuñ coṟkaḷuñ ceyalkaḷum namakku iṉpan tarāvākaliṉ avaṟṟi
aṟiya vēṇṭumeṉa virumpuvārum ulakil yārum ilar. iṉi, ivvāṟu kaḻiyum nāṭkaḷil
orōvorukāl avar aṟivu vaṟiya niṉivukaḷiṉ vēṟākap pirintu, ulaka iyaṟkiyiṟ paṭintu
ataṉ vaṇṇamāyt tirintu teḷivuṟṟu viḷaṅkumpōtu avvaṟiviṟ curantu perukum ariya
periya karuttukkaḷiyē nām aṟitaṟku mika viḻikiṉṟōm. iṅṅaṉan tōṉṟum ariya periya
karuttukkaḷiṉ kōvi oḻuṅkiṉiyē pāṭṭeṉṟum aṟital vēṇṭum.

iṉṉum, makkaḷ vāḻnāḷ eṉkiṉṟa nīrōṭiyilē vaṟuniṉivukaḷāṉa kalaṅkaṟ perunīr perukic
cellumpōtu, ulaka iyaṟki yeṉṉum malik kukikaḷilē arittu eṭuttuvanta
aruṅkaruttukkaḷāṉa poṟṟukaḷ iṭiyiṭiyē āḻntu avvōṭiyiṉ aṭinilattiṟ citarntu
miṉṉikkiṭappa, nallicip pulavaṉ eṉṉum arippukkāraṉ mika viḻintu muyaṉṟu
appoṟcitarkaḷi yellām oṉṟākap poṟukki eṭuttut taṉ matinuṭpa neruppiliṭṭu
urukkip pacumpoṟ piṇṭamākat tiraṭṭit taruvatē pāṭṭu eṉṟum aṟital vēṇṭum.

iṉṉum, makkaḷ aṟivu eṉkiṉṟa tittippāṉa ariya amiḻtam palavakiyāṉa kuṟṟaṅkaḷaṭuṅ
kalappuṟṟut tūyataṉṟāyp pōka, nallicippulavaṉ taṉ pēraṟiviṉāl ataṉit teḷiya vaṭittu
ataṉ iṉcuviyiṉi mikutippaṭutti, nāmellām ataṉip parukip periyatōr āṟutalaṭiyak
koṭuppāṉ; aṅṅaṉaṅ koṭukkappaṭun tūya iṉiya aṟiviṉ viḷakkamum pāṭṭeṉṟē aṟital
vēṇṭum. ikkaruttup paṟṟiyē miliṭṭaṉ eṉṉum āṅkilamoḻi valla nallicip pulavarum,
"pāṭṭeṉpatu makkaḷ maṉa aṟiviṉiṉṟum vaṭittu iṟakkappaṭṭa tūya amiḻtam ām"¹ eṉṟu uri
kūṟiṉār. itu niṟka.

iṉi, iṅṅaṉam iyaṟṟappaṭukiṉṟa pāṭṭu ulaka iyaṟkiyaḻakuṭaṉ peritum porunti naṭattal
vēṇṭum. iṉṉum itaṉi nuṇuki nōkkumiṭattup pāṭṭup pāṭutalil vallavaṉāṉa nallicip
pulavaṉukkum ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉip palavaki vaṇṇaṅkaḷāṟ kuḻittu varintu kāṭṭukiṉṟa
ōviyakkāraṉukkum oṟṟumi mika uṇṭeṉpatu teḷḷitiṟ pulappaṭum. āyiṉum, ōviyakkāraṉ
varikiṉṟa ōviyaṅ kaṭpulaṉukku maṭṭumē tōṉṟuvatākum; nallicip pulavaṉ amikkiṉṟa
pāṭṭō kaṇ mutalāṉa pulaṉkaḷiṉ akattē viḷaṅkum uḷḷattilē ceṉṟu tōṉṟuvatākum.
ōviyakkāraṉ tāṉ eḻuta eṭuttukkoṇṭa poruṭṭōṟṟattip paṉmuṟiyum nuṇṇitāka aḷantaḷantu
pārttuppiṉ ataṉit tiṟampaṭa varintāl maṭṭum aṅṅaṉam varinta ōviyattik kaṇṭu
viyakkiṉṟōm; tāṉ virittu viḷakkamāy eḻutavēṇṭum pakutikaḷil avaṉ oru ciṟitu
vaḻuviviṭṭāṉāyiṉum avvōviyattiṉkaṇ namakku viyapput tōṉṟātoḻiyum.
nallicippulavaṉō aṅṅaṉam avaṉippōl ovvoṉṟaṉiyum virivāka viḷakkik kāṭṭa vēṇṭum
varuttam uṭiyāṉ allaṉ. ōviyakkāraṉ pulaṉ aṟivip paṟṟi niṟpavaṉ; pulavaṉō maṉa aṟivip
paṟṟi niṟpavaṉ.

pulaṉaṟivō parupporuḷkaḷi virittaṟiyum iyalpiluḷḷatu; maṉavaṟivō appulaṉaṟiviṉ akattē
niṉṟu nuṇṇitām poruḷiyun tāṉē oru noṭiyil virittaṟiyum āṟṟal vāyntatu!
ammamma! maṉavaṟiviṉ āṟṟali yām eṉṉaveṉṟu eṭutturippēm! aṇuvi oru noṭiyil
malipōṟ perukac ceyyum, maliyi maṟunoṭiyil ōr aṇuviṉuṅ kuṟukacceyyum. iṅṅaṉam
viyappāṉa iyalpuṭiya maṉavaṟiviṉi nallicip pulavaṉ eṉṉum mantirakāraṉ taṉ matinuṭpamākiya
māttirik kōlāl toṭṭa aḷavāṉē atu tiṭukkeṉṟeḻuntu avaṉ virumpiya vaṇṇamellāñ
cuḻaṉṟu cuḻaṉṟāṭum.

iṉṉum itaṉic ciṟitu viḷakkuvām. ōviyakkāraṉ accuṟuttum akaṉṟatoṟu kariya periya
kāṭṭiṉi eḻutal vēṇṭumāyiṟ palanāḷum palakālum ataṉ iyaṟkiyiṉi aṟintaṟintu
pārttup, pariya maraṅkaḷ aṭarntu ōṅki oṉṟōṭoṉṟu piṇintu veḷiccam pukutāmal taṭi
ceytu niṟṟaliyum, akkāṭṭiṉ veḷittōṟṟa amippiṉiyum, maraṅkaḷiṉ iṭiyiluḷḷa
iṭukku veḷikaḷil namatu pārvi nuḻiyuṅkāl avi tōṉṟun taṉmiyiṉiyum, uḷḷē iruḷ
tirintu paraviyiruttaliyum aṅkuḷḷavāṟē ciṟanta pala vaṇṇaṅkaḷik kuḻittu iraṭṭut
tuṇiyiṉ mēl mika varunti muyaṉṟu eḻutik kāṭṭal vēṇṭum. i·tu avaṉukkup perunāḷ
viṉiyāka muṭiyum. nallicippulavaṉō, 'pariya maraṅkaḷ aṭarntōṅkip piṇintu niṟkum
iruṇṭa kāṭu' eṉṟu cila coṟkaḷit tiṟampaṭac cērttuk kūṟutal oṉṟiṉālēyē oru
noṭippoḻutil avvōviyakkāraṉāluṅ kāṭṭa muṭiyāta oruperu viyappuṇirviṉi nam
maṉattakattē viḷivikkum āṟṟaluṭiyaṉāvaṉ. i·tu ivaṉukku mika eḷitilē muṭivatoṉṟām.
iṅṅaṉam maṉavuṇarviṉi eḻupputal mika eḷitilē ceyyakkūṭiya toṉṟāyiṉum, ammaṉaviyalpiṉ
nuṭpam uṇarntu avvāṟu ceyyavallarāyiṉa naṟperumpulavar ulakiṟ cilarēyāvar. pulavaṉuṭiya
tiṟamiyellāñ "cilvakiyeḻuttiṟ palvakip poruḷik" kāṭṭukiṉṟa arumperuñ
ceykiyiṉālē tāṉ aṟiyappaṭum. iṅṅaṉam pāṭṭu vaḻakkiṉ nuṭpamuṇarntu piṟa moḻikaḷiṟ
pukaḻpeṟṟu viḷaṅkiya nallicip pulavarkaḷ ōmar², tāntē³, cekappiriyar⁴, miliṭṭaṉār⁵,
kītē⁶, kāḷitācar mutaliyōrum, nañcentamiḻil tiruvaḷḷuvar, nakkīraṉār, iḷaṅkōvaṭikaḷ,
kūlavāṇikaṉ cāttaṉār, māṅkuṭi marutaṉār, kapilar, cēkkiḻār mutalāṉōrum
paṇṭikkālattu ēṉi nallicip pulavarumēyāvar. iṉṉum itaṉi virippiṟ perukumeṉṟañci
ittuṇiyiṉ niṟuttukiṉṟōm.

2. paḻantamiḻp pāṭṭiṉ ciṟappiyalpu

iṉip, paṇṭik kālattuc centamiḻp pulavarellārum ulaka iyaṟkittiṟam piṟaḻāmal, ataṉi
nuṇuki ārāyntu pāṭṭuppāṭum maṉavuṟuti mikutiyumuṭiyarāyiruntaṉar. ulaka
iyaṟkiyiṟ kāṇappaṭum oḷiviḷakkattiyum eḻiliyum mika viyantaṉar. tam maṉaṉuṇarviṟku
icinta vaṇṇamellām ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉit tirittuk kūṟāmal, avvulaka iyaṟkiyiṉ aḻakiṉ
vaḻiyē tamataṟiviṉip porunta vittut tam niṉiviṉi virivu ceytu viḷakki makiḻntaṉar.
immuṟimi naṟperum pulavarkku iṉṟiyamiyāmic ciṟappiṉatām eṉṉuṅ karuttup paṟṟiyē
iracikar¹ eṉṉum āṅkila moḻi urivalla āciriyar, kāṭṭu eṉṟa pulavarip paṟṟic colla
vantaviṭattu "avar tamatuṇarviṉ vaḻiyē ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉi niṟuttik koḷḷāmal, avvulaka
iyaṟkiyiṉ vaḻiyē tamatuṇarviṉi niṟutti niṉṟār" eṉṟu pukaḻnteṭuttuk kūṟiṉār.
ākavē, ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉ vaḻi niṉṟu pāṭṭup pāṭutalē arumiyā meṉpatum, atuvē
nallicip pulavarkku aṭiyāḷamām eṉpatum itaṉāl naṉku peṟappaṭum. paḻantamiḻp
pulavarkaḷellārum in nuṇukkam iṉitaṟintu viḷaṅkiṉārkaḷeṉpataṟkup paḻiya tamiḻp
pāṭṭukkaḷē cāṉṟākum. eṉiṉum, itaṉi oru paḻiya ceyyuḷ mukattāṉum oru ciṟitu
viḷakkik kāṭṭuvām.

  "muḷitayir picinta kāntaḷ melviral
  kaḻuvuṟu kaliṅkaṅ kaḻāa tuṭīik
  kuvaḷi yuṇkaṇ kuyppuki kamaḻat
  tāṉ tuḻan taṭṭa tīmpuḷip pākar
  iṉiteṉak kaṇavaṉ uṇṭaliṉ
  nuṇṇitiṉ makiḻntaṉṟu oṇṇutal mukaṉē."

eṉpatu kuṟuntoki² eṉṉum paḻiya tamiḻ nūlil uḷḷa oru pāṭṭu. taṉ makaḷ taṉ kātaṟkaṇavaṉ
vīṭṭil eppaṭiyirukkiṉṟāḷ eṉpatik kaṇṭaṟiyum poruṭṭuc ceṉṟa cevilittāy,
avviruvarum mikka nēyamuṭiyarāy vāḻvatu kaṇṭu, taṉṉuḷḷē makiḻntu colliyatāka itu
pāṭappaṭṭirukkiṉṟatu. " eṉmakaḷ vaṟṟakkāycciṉa kaṭṭit tayirip picinta kāntaḷ
malarmukiḻpōṟ civanta melliya viralkaḷāl, naṉku kaḻuvi veṇmiyāṉa uyarnta āṭi camiyal
ceyyum viriviṉāl iṭuppiṉiṉṟum aviḻntu kaḻala ataṉik kikaḻuvāmalē uṭuttuk
koṇṭu, kuvaḷip pūppōṉṟa mi tīṭṭiya taṉ kaṇkaḷilē tāḷippuc ceyyum pukipaṭṭu

nuṭpamāy makiḻcci aṭintatu" eṉpatu tāṉ ippāṭṭiṉ poruḷ. pāruṅkaḷ! icceyyuḷiṉ

picinta' eṉṟār; eṉṉi? tayirpāl mutaliyaṉa āṉirimikka mulli nilattiṟkē

pacittiruppāṉē eṉṉum niṉivāl virintu camiyal ceyyumpōtu iṭuppiṟkaṭṭiya uyarnta

picintu tiruttiya puḷippākiṉit tāḷikkum pōtu mēl eḻum puki taṉ kuvaḷippūvaṉṉa

cila collil mika viḷaṅkakkūṟiya nuṇmi peritum viyakkaṟpāla toṉṟām.

ōviyam eḻutik kāṭṭiṉāṟ pōl evvaḷavu uṇmiyākavum iṉitākavuñ collap
paṭṭirukkiṉṟatu! ip poruḷarumiyōṭu icceyyuḷil uḷḷa coṟkaḷ ellām nīrmaṭiyil
teḷinīr moḻu moḻuveṉṟu ōṭuvatu pōl ōciyiṉpam uṭiyavāy oḻukutalum, oru
collāyiṉum poruḷiṉṟi vēṇṭā kūṟalākāmal muṉṉum piṉṉumuḷḷa poruṭṭoṭarpukku ēṟpa
iṭiyē muḻumuḻuc coṟkaḷāy amintu niṟṟalum mikavum pārāṭṭaṟpālaṉavākum eṉpatu.

iṉṉum paṇṭik kālattut taṇṭamiḻp pulavar ulaka iyaṟkip poruḷkaḷi āṅkāṅkuhṭ tirintu
kaṇṭu peruṅkaḷippum perukiya maṉa veḻucciyum uṭiyarāy, varuttamiṉṟi iṉitākap
pāṭṭukaḷ pāṭiṉār eṉpatu avar tām virittuc collum poruḷkaḷukku eṭuttukkāṭṭum
uvamikaḷāl naṉku pulaṉām. ōriṭattil māṉ kompip paṟṟic collavantuḻi, 'irumpi
muṟukkiṉāṟ pōluṅ kariya periya kompu' eṉṉum poruḷ paṭa "irumpu tirittaṉṉa
māyiru maruppu" (akanāṉūṟu, 4) eṉṟum, ōriṭattil iruvar nēya oṟṟumiyiṉic colla
vanta pōtu 'kattiyuṟi ceyyuñ ciṟiya toḻilāḷaṉ arakkoṭu cērtta kallip pōlap
piriyōm' eṉṉum poruḷpaṭac "ciṟukārōṭaṉ³ payiṉoṭu cērttiya kaṟpōṟ piriyalam"
(akanāṉūṟu, 1) eṉṟum, ōriṭattiṟ puṟanturuttiya naṇṭiṉ kaṇkaḷukku vēppampū
mukiyiṉi uvamiyāka eṭuttu "vēppu naṉiyaṉṉa neṭuṅkaṭkaḷvaṉ" (aiṅkuṟunūṟu, 30)
eṉṟum, ōriṭattil 'vayal nel putitu īṉṟa paciya katirukkuc celvarkaḷ tamatu
kutiriyiṉ ucciyil tūkkik kaṭṭiya tiyal mūṭṭuḷḷa kavari mayiri' uvamiyāka eṭuttu
"muracuṭic celvar puravic cūṭṭum, mūṭṭuṟu kavari tūkkiyaṉaṉa, ceḻuñcey nelliṉ
cēyarip puṉiṟṟuk katir" (akanāṉūṟu, 156) eṉṟum, ōriṭattil 'maḻiyillāta vāṉam
pūttatu pōla ili neruṅkiya mucuṇṭic ceṭikaḷ veḷḷi malarkaḷip pūkka' eṉṉum poruḷ
pōtara "maḻiyil vāṉam mīṉ aṇintaṉṉa, kuḻiyamal mucuṇṭi vāliya malara" (akanāṉūṟu, 264)
eṉṟum, ōriṭattil 'pañciṉ ṟoṭarnuṉipōlun taliyiṉiyuṭiya putarkaḷiṉmēl ēṟippaṭarum
iṇṭik koṭikaḷiṉ nīril naṉinta taḷirkaḷ neyyil tōyttaṉa pōla viḷaṅki iraṇṭāka iruḷik
kūṟupaṭuttiṉāṟpōl ovvoru taḷirum iraṇṭu kūṟupaṭṭaṉavāyk kariya niṟattuṭaṉ aciya'
eṉṉum poruḷpaṭat "tuyttalippūviṉ putalivar īṅki, neytōyttaṉṉa nīrnaṉi antaḷir,
iruvakir iruḷiṉ īriya tuyalvara" (akanāṉūṟu, 294) eṉṟum, ōriṭattiṟ 'pacci mañcaḷiṉ
paciya mutukippōl cuṟṟilum poruttuṭampu uṭiyaṉavākak kaḻiyiṟ kiṭakkum iṟāmīṉ'
eṉpatu viḷaṅka "muṟṟā mañcaṭ pacumpuṟaṅ kaṭuppac, cuṟṟiya piṇara cūḻkaḻi yiṟavu"
(naṟṟiṇi, 101) eṉṟum, ōriṭattil 'mayiliṉ aṭipōl mūṉṟu piḷavāy irukkum
ilikaḷuṭiya periya katiruḷḷa nocci' eṉpatu tōṉṟa "mayilaṭi iliya mākkural nocci"
eṉṟum, ōriṭattiṟ 'katir arintuviṭṭa tiṉippayiriṉ tāḷ pōṉṟa ciṟiya
pacuṅkāliyuṭiyavāy ōṭum nīril āralmīṉip pārttuk koṇṭirukkum nāri' eṉpatu
pulappaṭat 'tiṉittāḷ aṉṉa ciṟupacuṅ kāla, oḻukunīr āral pārkkuṅ kuṟuku"
(kuṟuntoki, 25) eṉṟum, pōnta taṉmi naviṟciyaṇic coṟṟoṭarkaḷāṉē paḻiya tamiḻp
pulavariṉ viḻumiya ulakiyaṟporuḷ aṟiviṉi iṉitu aṟintu koḷḷalām. iṉṉum ivipōṉṟa
eṭuttukkāṭṭukaḷ nūṟu nūṟākap perukkalāmēṉum, iṅku ataṟku iṭampeṟutal kūṭāmiyiṉ
itaṉi ivvaḷavil niṟuttukiṉṟām.

ittuṇi nuṭpamāṉa ulakiyaṟporuḷ aṟivu paṇṭikkālattut taṇṭamiḻp pulavariṭaṅ kāṇappaṭutal
pōla, maṟṟimoḻikaḷil vallarāy viḷaṅkiya ēṉip paḻampulavariṭattuṅ kāṇappaṭutal aritu.
iṉṉum ivvāṟē paḻiya tamiḻppulavar ulaka iyaṟ poruṭkāṭcikaḷip puṉinturitta muṟiyum,
avvulaka iyaṟkikkum makkaḷiyaṟkikkum uḷḷa poruttampaṟṟi avar veḷiyiṭṭa ariya
karuttukkaḷiṉ viḻuppamum pattuppāṭṭu, eṭṭuttoki, patiṉeṇkīḻkkaṇakku nūlkaḷiṟ
parakkak kāṇalām. āṇṭuk kaṇṭu koḷka.

aminta viḷakkiṉcuṭar kuṟiyuntōṟun tiriyik koḷutti erikkiṉṟārkaḷ; kutiri
mutaliyaṉa uṟaṅkutaliṉ avaṟṟiṉ kaḻuttilē kaṭṭappaṭṭa maṇiyiṉ ōciyum aṭaṅkip pōṉa
naṭuyāmattil meykkāppāḷar tūkka mayakkattāl acintu kāvalākac cuṟṟit tirikiṉṟārkaḷ;
iṅṅaṉam naṭuyāmam ātalum, poḻutu aḷantaṟivōr talivaṉ etirē vantuniṉṟu vaṇaṅki
vaḻthṭik kaṭārattu nīrilēyiṭṭa nāḻikivaṭṭilāṟ poḻutu ivvaḷavāyiṟṟeṉṟu
aṟivikkiṉṟārkaḷ; ataṉik kēṭṭavuṭaṉ aracaṉ eḻuntu, yavaṉarkaḷālē puliccaṅkiliviṭṭu
aḻakitāka amikkappaṭṭa illiṉ uḷḷē viḷakkaṅkāṭṭappaṭac ceṉṟu, valiya kayiṟṟāl tiriyi
maṟittu vaḷiyakkaṭṭi muṉ oṉṟum piṉ oṉṟumāy iraṇṭāka vakukkappaṭṭa paḷḷi aṟiyiṟ pōyp
paṭukkiyil amarntirukkiṉṟāṉ; aṅṅaṉan talivaṉ paḷḷi koḷḷum uḷḷaṟiyiṉ muṉtirikkup
puṟattēyuḷḷa veḷiyaṟiyilē caṭṭiyiṭṭa milēccaril ūmikaḷ talivaṉ paḷḷiyaṟiyic cūḻntu
irukkiṉṟārkaḷ; aracaṉō nāḷikkuc ceyyavēṇṭum pōriṉi mika virumpi ataṉāṟ
paṭukkiyil uṟakkaṅkoḷḷāṉāy muṉ nāṭkaḷil naṭanta pōriṟ puṇpaṭṭa yāṉikaḷi niṉintum,
yāṉiyi veṭṭiyun tamakku veṟṟiyiṉi yuṇṭākkiyum iṟantu pōṉa pōrmaṟavari niṉintum,
ampu aḻuntiya varuttattāl tīṉikoḷḷāmal kātic cāyttuk koṇṭu kalaṅkuṅ kutirikaḷi
niṉintum, mikunta irakkam uṭiyōṉāy orukiyi amaḷi mēlvittu oru kiyiṉāl
muṭiyit tāṅki ivvāṟu nīḷa niṉintu irukkiṉṟāṉ.

(72-80) talivaṉatu veṟṟiyum, avaṉ pācaṟiyil iṉitu uṟaṅkutalum

iṉi, ivvāṟu muṉṉāḷiravu uṟakkamiṉṟik kavaliyōṭirunta talimakaṉ piṉṉāḷiṟ
pakivariyellām veṟṟi kaṇṭu, taṉ valiya viralālē nalla vāki māliyiṉic cūṭikkoṇṭu,
'nāḷi māliyil taliviyik kāṇpōm' eṉṉum makiḻcciyiṉāl oru kavaliyumiṉṟip
pakiyaracar kēṭṭu naṭuṅkutaṟkuk karuviyāṉa veṟṟi muracu muḻaṅkun taṉ pācaṟi vīṭṭil
iṉitu tuyil koṇṭirukkiṉṟāṉ.

(80-88) pāṭṭiṉ poruṭkāṭci tuyaramun tēṟutaluṅ kalanta niliyiṟ paṭuttuk kiṭakkun
talimakaḷiṉ mullikkāṭṭu māḷikikkut tirumpavum māṟukiṉṟatu.

iṉi, iṅṅaṉam pācaṟiyil iṉiya uṟakkattilē kiṭakkiṉṟa talimakaṉit taṉ pakkattilē kāṇāta
talimakaḷ avaṉiṭattē taṉ neñciṉip pōkki mika varuntum varuttattāl, mutupeṇṭir
naṟcoṟ kēṭṭu vantu āṟṟuvikkuñ coṟkaḷiyuṅ kēḷāmaṟ varuntukiṉṟavaḷ, "iṅṅaṉam āṟṟāmē
varuntiṉāl atu nam perumāṉ kaṟpitta collit tavaṟiyatāy muṭiyuṅkolō" eṉṟu neṭuka
niṉintu pārttut taṉṉit tēṟṟik koṇṭuñ, cuḻaṉṟu viḻukiṉṟa vaḷiyik kaḻalāmaṟ
ceṟittum, āṟṟāmi yuṇarvum ataṉit tēṟṟukiṉṟa uṇarvum oṉṟu cērtalāl aṟivu
mayaṅkiyum, avvaṟivu mayakkattāṟ perumūcceṟintu naṭuṅkiyum, annaṭukkattāl uṭampiṟ
ceṟitta aṇikalaṅkaḷ ciṟitu cuḻalap peṟṟum, ēḻaṭukkumāḷikiyiṟ pāvi viḷakku eriyak
kūṭalvāyilē maḻi coriyum ōci kātil viḻa immālik kālattiṟ paṭukkiyiṟ
kiṭakkiṉṟāḷ'

(89 - mutal kaṭicivariyil) talivaṉ mīṇṭu varutalu,m, nāṭṭiṉ maḻikkālac ciṟappum

iṉit, talimakaṉ taṉ māṟṟāriyellām veṉṟu pakip pulattik kavarntu koṇṭa perum
paṭiyaṭu veṟṟikkoṭiyi uyarattūkki ūtu kompuñ caṅkum muḻaṅkavum, kācāñceṭikaḷ
nīlamalarkaḷip pūkkavuṅ, koṉṟi maraṅkaḷ poṉpōl malaravuṅ, kāntaḷ aḻakiya kipōl
viriyavun, tōṉṟippūc civappāka alaravum, varakaṅkolliyil iḷamāṉkaḷ tāviyōṭavuṅ,
kārkālattu muṟṟuṅ kāyiṉiyuṭiya vaḷḷikkāṭu piṉpōkavum mulli nilattilē mīṇṭu
varumpōtu, avaṉatu tēriṟ kaṭṭiya kutiri kaṉikkum ōciyāṉatu āṟṟik koṇṭu aṅṅaṉaṅ
kiṭakkun talimakaḷ ceviyilē niṟintu āravārittatu eṉka.

9. pāṭṭiṉ poruḷ nalam viyattal

kaṭalil mukanta nīrip poḻintu koṇṭē eḻuntu uyarnta kariyamukiliṟku, māvali vārtta
nīr oḻukuṅ kiyuṭaṉē ōṅki vaḷarnta kariya tirumāli oppumi kūṟiyatu maṉaṉuṇarviṟku
icinta uvamiyākap porunti niṟkiṉṟatu. nilattil ūṉṟiya villilē ampuṟāt tuṇiyit
toṅkaviṭṭiruppataṟkup, pārppaṉat tuṟavi kāvikkallil tōytta uṭiyit taṉatu mukkōliṟ
ṟoṅkaviṭṭiruppati uvami kūṟiyatu mikavum poruttamāka virukkiṉṟatu. itaṉāl
ivvāciriyar tuṟavikaḷiṭattup paḻakkamuṭiyar eṉpatun, tuṟavoḻukkattil vēṭkiyuṭiya
reṉpatuṅ kuṟippāka aṟiyappaṭum.

meykāppāḷar pāṭivīṭṭil iṭiyāmattilē tūkkamayakkattōṭum acintu tirital, pūtta
puṉalik koṭi paṭarnta tūṟu vāṭikkāṟṟil acivatu pōl irukkiṉṟatu eṉpataṉāluṅ,
kāyāmalar kaṟuppākavuṅ, koṉṟi poṉṉiṟamākavun, tōṉṟi civappākavum irukkum
eṉpataṉālum, varakaṅ kolliyil māṉkaḷ tāvik kutikkiṉṟaṉa, kārkālattil vaḷḷikkiḻaṅku
muṟṟiviṭukiṉṟaṉa eṉpataṉālum, ivar iyaṟkip poruḷkaḷik kaṇṭaṟivatilum, avaṟṟit
tāṅkaṇṭavāṟē colvatilun tiṟami mikkuṭiyareṉpatu iṉitu viḷaṅkum.

iṉṉum, mutupeṇṭir naṟcoṟ kēṭkumporuṭṭu ūrppakkattē tirumāl kōyiliṟ pōy nāḻi
nellum mulliyun tūvi vaṇaṅkutaluṅ, kuḷir mikutiyāl tōḷiṟ kaṭṭiya kiyuṭaṉ
niṟkum ōr iṭippeṇ āṉkaṉṟukaṭkut tēṟtal collutaluṅ, kāṭṭilē pāṭivīṭu amittalum,
appāṭi vīṭṭiṉuḷ nāṟcanti kūṭum muṟṟattilē yāṉippākar yāṉiyik kuttik kavaḷam
ūṭṭutalum, villiṉāl vaḷivāka amikkappaṭṭa kūṭāraṅkaḷukku iṭiyilē aracaṉukkeṉṟu
vaṇṇattiriyiṉāl vēṟoru vīṭu vakukkappaṭṭiruttalum, avvīṭṭiṉ uḷḷē peṇkaḷ palar kiyil
viḷakku ēnti niṟṟaluṅ, kutiri mutaliyavaṟṟiṉ kaḻuttiṟ kaṭṭiya maṇiyōci iravil
aṭaṅkiviṭṭatum, meykāppāḷar aracaṉirukkiyic cuṟṟik kāvalākat tiritalum,
poḻutaṟivōr koppari nīril iṭṭa nāḻiki vaṭṭilip pārttu vantu aracaṉ etirilē
iṭiyāmam āyiṟṟu eṉṟalum, yavaṉarkaḷāṟ puliccaṅkili viṭṭu mika aḻakiyatāka vakukkappaṭṭa
paḷḷiyaṟiyuḷ avarkaḷ viḷakkuk kāṭṭac ceṉṟu aracaṉ paḷḷikoṇṭiruttalum, appōtu ūmi
milēccar paḷḷiyaṟiyic cuṟṟik kāvalāka iruttalum, paṭukkimēl uḷḷa aracaṉ maṟunāṭpōri
virumpum uḷḷattōṭu uṟakkam peṟāṉāy, muṉṉāṭ pōriṟ puṇpaṭṭa yāṉi kutirikaḷiyuñ
ceñcōṟṟuk kaṭaṉ kaḻittu iṟantoḻinta ariya pōrmaṟavariyum niṉintu varunti oru kiyi
mettiyiṉ mēlum maṟṟu oru kiyit taliyiṉ kīḻum vittup paṭuttiruttalum, talimakaḷ
ēḻaṭukku māḷikiyil taṉ kaṇavaṉ varukiyi niṉintu piriviṉ tuṉpatti āṟṟik koṇṭu
pāviyiṉ kiyiluḷḷa viḷakkāṉatu eriya māḷikiyiṉ kūṭalvāyililē vantuviḻum nīrttiraḷ
olippa mayilpōṟ paṭuttiruttalum, appōtu talivaṉ taṉṟēriṉi virivākac celuttik
koṇṭu kāṭṭilē varutalum nām nērē kāṇkiṉṟatu pōlavum, ōviyam eḻuti naṅkaṇṇetirē
kāṭṭukiṉṟatu pōlavum mikka aḻakuṭaṉ collappaṭutal kāṇka.

iṉi, iv vāciriyar tām puṉinturikkum poruḷkaḷiṉ uḷḷē nuḻintu avaṟṟi virivākap
puṉinturikkiṉṟā reṉpatum īṇṭu aṟiyaṟpāṟṟu; ivviyaṟki pattuppāṭṭukkaḷ iyaṟṟiya
pulavar ellāriṭattum potuvākak kāṇappaṭuvatoṉṟākum. āyiṉum, ivariyaḻinta ēṉippulava
rellarum nam uḷḷattiṉ kaṟpaṉiyuṇarvu taḷarvaṭiyā vaṇṇam virittup puṉintu collutaṟku
icinta naṉporuḷkaḷiyē viritturikkiṉṟaṉar ; maṟṟu ivarō puṉinturi virippatāṟ
cuvippaṭāta ōrovoṉṟiṉiyuñ ciṟitu akalavirittuk kūṟukiṉṟār ; pāṭivīṭu
amikkappaṭṭa taṉmiyiṉi ivar iṉṉuñ curukkik kūṟiyiruntāl ip pāṭṭu iṉṉum
poruṭcuvi mutirntu viḷaṅkum. tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭi mutalāṉa ēṉic cila
pāṭṭukkaḷukku im mullippāṭṭu ivvāṟṟāl oru ciṟitu tāḻntatupōlumeṉa avi tammi
oppu nōkkik kaṟpārkku orukāṟ ṟōṉṟiṉun tōṉṟum. eṉṟālum ip pāṭṭiṉkaṭ kaṇṭa
poruṭkōvi niṉikkuntōṟum iṉpam payakkum viḻuppam vāyntu miḷirkiṉṟami kāṇmiṉ!

10. pāvum pāṭṭiṉ naṭiyum

iṉi, ic ceyyuḷ nērici akavaṟpāvāṟ ceyyappaṭṭatoṉṟām. itil ovvōr aṭiyum nāṉku
cīrkaḷāṉ vakukkappaṭuvaṉa; ovvoru cīrum iraṇṭu acikkuk kuṟiyāmalum mūṉṟacikku
mēṟpaṭāmalum varum; pulavaṉ tāṉ karutiya arumporuḷkaḷiyellām varuttamiṉṟi eḷitāka
veḷiyiṭutaṟku iv vakavaṟpāviṉum icivāṉatu piṟitilli. etuki yiṉpamum
mōṉiyiṉpamun tōṉṟa iyintu niṟkuñ coṟkaḷ maṟṟi moḻikaḷiṟ pōlātu tamiḻil mikap
perukiyiruntālum, av vetukinayam mōṉinayaṅkaḷiyē peritum nōkkātu paḻiya tamiḻp
pulavarkaḷellārum poruḷ ceṉṟavaḻiyē coṟkaḷ toṭarkaḷ nirampac ceyyuṭkaḷ palavum
iyaṟṟuvārāyiṉar. poruḷaḻuṅku mutirat taṅ karuttukkaḷi iṇakki vittuc cellumpōtu
āṅkāṅku iṭarppāṭiṉṟi eḷiyavāyt tōṉṟum etuki mōṉikaḷiyē amippar; etuki
mōṉikaḷukku ēṟpap poruḷ poruttuvārallar.

piṟkālattil akavaṟpāp pāṭiṉa pulavar perumpālum ovvōraṭiyilum mutaṟcīrum mūṉṟāñ
cīrum etuki poruntat toṭuttārkaḷ; cilar ivviraṇṭu aṭikaḷ mutaṟcīr etuki
iṇiyakkoḷuviṉar. avar ceyta appāṭṭukkaḷ ellām mutaliliruntu iṟuti variyil orē
ōciyāy naṭantu kēṭpōrkku veṟuppuṇarviṉit tōṟṟuviyā niṟkiṉṟaṉa.

maṟṟup, paṇṭikkālattup pulavariṉ akavaṟ pāṭṭukkaḷō poruḷ iyipukku iṇaṅka ōci māṟi
māṟi naṭantu kēṭpārkkuk kaḻipēr uvappuṇarviṉip payantu niṟkiṉṟaṉa. iv vāciriyar
nappūtaṉār, cila aṭikaḷil mutaṟcīrum mūṉṟāñcīrum etuki poruntavittum,
vēṟucilavaṟṟil mutalum nāṉkum etuki poruntac ceytum, maṟṟuñ cilaaṭikaḷ
iraṇṭaṭiyāy mutaṟcīril avvāṟu etuki poruntak koḷuviyum, piṉṉuñ cilavaṟṟil
atutāṉumillāmal yāttum ippāṭṭiṉip palavakiyāl ōciyiṉpam māṟi māṟi varat
toṭuttār. iṉṉum āṅkāṅku amikkappaṭum poruḷkaḷukku iṇaṅka aṭikaḷ metuvākavum,
virivākavum, iṭiyiṭiyē teṟṟuppaṭṭuñ celkiṉṟatu; pāṭivīṭu iyaṟṟum iṭattilmōci
teṟṟuppaṭṭuc celkiṉṟatu; aracaṉ pācaṟiyiṉuḷ irukkum niliyic collumiṭattu ōci
metuvāka naṭakkiṉṟatu; avaṉ mīṇṭu virintu varumiṭattu virintu pōkiṉṟatu.
iviyellām aṟivu oruṅki ārāyntu uṇarntukoḷka.

iṉi, ip pāṭṭiṉuḷiṉ iṭiccoṟkaḷiyum vēṟṟumiyurupukaḷiyum nīkki eṇṇappaṭṭa coṟkaḷ
ciṟitēṟakkuṟiya ainnūṟu coṟkaḷākum; ivaṟṟuḷ muṉvanta collē piṉṉum varumāyiṉ
piṉvantatu eṇṇappaṭavilli. iv vinnūṟu coṟkaḷuḷ 'nēmi' 'kōvalar' 'paṭivam' 'kaṇṭam'
'paṭam' 'kaṇam' 'cintittu' 'vicayam' 'añcaṉam' eṉṉum oṉpatum vaṭacoṟkaḷ ; 'yavaṉar'
'milēccar' iraṇṭum ticiccoṟkaḷ. āka itaṉuṭ kāṇappaṭṭa piṟamoḻic coṟkaḷ
patiṉoṉṟētām. eṉavē ip pāṭṭiṉuḷ nūṟṟukku iraṇṭu viḻukkāṭu piṟacoṟkaḷ pukuntaṉa
eṉṟaṟika; ēṉiya vellān taṉic centamiḻc coṟkaḷākum.

iṉi, im mullippāṭṭi ēṉi oṉpatu pāṭṭukkaḷōṭum oppavittu nōkkuṅkāl, i·tu
ēṉiyavaṟṟippōl mika uyarnta tīñcuvi naṭiyiṉatākak kāṇappaṭavilli yeṉpatu tōṉṟukiṉṟatu.
porunarāṟṟuppaṭiyil vanta,

"turāay tuṟṟiya turuviyam puḻukkiṉ

parāri vēvi parukeṉat taṇṭik

kāḻiṟ cuṭṭa kōḻūṉ koḻuṅkuṟi"
eṉṉum aṭikaḷiṉ tēṉoḻukum ariyanaṭi pōlāvatu, maturikkāñciyiṟ pōnta,

"maḻitoḻi lutava mātiraṅ koḻukka

āḻnta kuḻumpil tirumaṇi kiḷara

eḻunta kaṭaṟṟiṉ naṉpoṉ koḻippa"

eṉṉum aṭikaḷiṉ tēṉoḻukum ariyanaṭi pōlāvatu iḻumeṉ ōciyuṭaṉ tittippāka
eḻutappaṭṭa ōraṭiyiyāvatu im mullippāṭṭiṟ kāṇṭal aritu; i·tu ēṉiyavaṟṟi nōkkap
perumpālum eṅkum valleṉṟa ōciyuṭiyatāyirukkiṉṟatu. ēṉip pāṭṭukkaḷiṟ pōlac
colliṉ koḻumi itaṉkaṇ mika mutirntu tōṉṟāmiyiṉ, itu taṉṉik kaṟpārkku ēṉiyapōl
mikka colliṉpam payavāteṉṟu karutukiṉṟōm. ippāṭṭiṉ naṭiyiṉāl itaṉi yiyaṟṟiya
āciriyar nappūtaṉār tuṟavoḻukkamum, valleṉṟa iyalpum, aṟivāḻamum, mikka maṉavamitiyum
uṭiyareṉpatu kuṟippāka aṟiyappaṭum; kāṭṭiṭattiyum, maḻi kālattiyun, talivi
taṉimiyiyum poruḷākak koṇṭu icceyyuḷ yattamiyāṉun, tuṟavōr karuvikaḷi uvami
eṭuttukkāṭṭutalāṉum aviyē ivar taṉmiyāmeṉpatu teḷiyappaṭum.

ippāṭṭiṉkaṭ kāṇappaṭṭa paṇṭikkālat tamiḻariṉ vaḻakkavoḻukka varalāṟṟuk kuṟippukaḷ

iṉi, ippāṭṭiṉāṟ paṇṭikkālat tamiḻariṉ vaḻakka oḻukkaṅkaḷ cila aṟiyappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. iṉi
nikaḻum nikaḻccikaḷi nimittaṅ kēṭṭu aṟiyalām eṉṟu nampiṉar. pakivar mēṟceṉṟa aracar
kāṭṭiṟ pāṭivīṭu amippatu vaḻakkam. yāṉippākar yāṉikaḷi vaṭanāṭṭuc coṟkaḷāṟ paḻakki
vantaṉar. aracaṉ pōrmēṟ cellum pōtu peṇkaḷum vāḷvarinta kaccuṭaṉē kūṭacceṉṟu
pāṭivīṭṭil avaṉi ōmpiṉar. peṇmakkaḷ iṅṅaṉam aracarōṭu uṭaṉ ceṉṟu avaṉukkup
paṇipurital muṟkālat tuṇṭeṉpatu vaṭamoḻiyiṟ kāḷitācar iyaṟṟiya cākuntala naṭakattāṉum
aṟiyappaṭum. kaṭārattu nīrilē iṭṭa nāḻikivaṭṭilāṟ poḻutu aṟintu vantaṉar. kirīcu
mutalāṉa ayal nāṭukaḷiluḷḷa yavaṉar eṉṉuṅ kammarkaḷi varavaḻittu arumimikka pala
kammavēlikaḷ ceytu vantaṉar. ivvāṟē cīvakacintāmaṇiyilun "tampulaṉkaḷāl yavaṉar tāṭpaṭutta
poṟiyē" eṉṟu ivarkaḷ kuṟippiṭappaṭṭami kāṇka. milēcca tēyattiluḷḷa ūmikaḷi

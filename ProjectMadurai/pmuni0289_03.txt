   877 iravivan tuṟṟuḻi eḻuntu cūrmakaṉ
   marapuḷi nāṭkaṭaṉ vaḻāmal āṟṟiyē
   ceruviṉil uṭaintiṭu ciṟumai cintiyāy
   poruvaru māyaiyaip pōṟṟal mēyiṉāṉ. 1
   878 pōṟṟiṉaṉ muṉṉuṟu poḻutiṉ māyavaḷ
   kōṟṟoḻil kaṉṟiya kumaraṉ muṉṉarē
   tōṟṟiṉaḷ niṟṟalun toḻuta kaiyiṉaṉ

   880 tuṉṉala rōṭupōr toṭaṅki īṟṟiṉil
   piṉṉiṭu vārpeṟum piḻaiyum peṟṟaṉaṉ
   eṉṉiṉi varumpaḻi itaṟku mēleṉṟāṉ

   kuṟaiyuṟu vittaṉai koṭumai pēṇiṉāy. 5

   884 nūṟṟivaṇ paṟpala nuvaliṉ āvateṉ

   cāṟṟuti vēṇṭuva taruvaṉ eṉṟalum
   āṟṟavum makiḻciṟan taṉaiyaṉ kūṟuvāṉ. 8
   885 niṉṟamar iyaṟṟiyē neṉṉal eṉṟaṉai
   veṉṟaṉaṉ ēkiya vīra vākuvai
   iṉṟaṉi kattoṭum īṟu ceytiṭa
   oṉṟoru paṭaiyiṉai utavu vāyeṉṟāṉ. 9
   886 aṭalvali piḻaittiṭum avuṇaṉ coṟṟaṉa
   keṭalarum māyavaḷ kēṭṭut taṉṉoru
   paṭaiyiṉai vitittavaṉ pāṇi nalkiyē
   kaṭitiṉil orumoḻi kaḻaṟal mēyiṉāḷ. 10
   887 maṟṟitu viṭuttiyāl maṟaiyil kantavēḷ
   oṟṟaṉaip piṟartamai uṇarvai vīṭṭiyē
   cuṟṟiṭum vāyuviṉ toḻiluñ ceyyumāl
   iṟṟaiyiṟ cayamuṉa tēku vāyeṉṟāḷ. 11
   888 uraittivai māyavaḷ umpar pōntuḻi
   varattiṉiṟ koṇṭiṭu māya māppaṭai
   parittavaṉ nerunaliṟ paḻiyai nīṅkiyē

   889 kūrppuṟu pallavaṅ koṇṭa tūṇiyaic

   pōrpperuṅ karuvikaḷ puṉaintu tōṉṟiṉāṉ. 13
   890 kāṟpaṭai aḻaṟpaṭai kālaṉ tolpaṭai

   cayantaṉat tēṟiṉaṉ takuvar yāvaruñ
   cayantaṉaip peṟukeṉa āci cāṟṟavē. 16
   893 oppaṟu ceṟunarmēl uruttup pōrceyat
   tuppuṟu cūrmakaṉ toṭarkiṉ ṟāṉeṉac
   ceppuṟum oṟṟarkaḷ terintu pōmeṉa
   eppuṟat tāṉaiyum eḻuntu pōntavē. 17
   894 paripati ṉāyira vau¢ḷam pāymata

   895 nāṟpaṭai ivvakai naṭantu kōmakaṉ

   māṟpaṭu puṇarinīr vaṟappac cūḻntatāl
   mēṟpaṭu mukiliṉam micaiya vanteṉa. 19
   896 tiṇṭiṟal aṉikamīc ceṉṟa pūḻikaḷ

   uṇṭaliṉ aṭaintaṉa uvari muṟṟumē. 20
   897 muracoṭu tuṭikuṭa muḻavañ callari

   898 uḻaiyuṭaik kaṟpiṉar uraiyiṟ ceṉṟiṭā

   puḻaiyuṭait taṉikkaram pōkkip poṅkucūl
   maḻaiyuṭait tiṭuvaṉa mataṅkoḷ yāṉaiyē. 22

   900 aruḷila rākiya avuṇar māṇṭuḻit

   maruḷoru tuṉpuṟum vaṇṇaṅ kāṭṭalpōl
   uruḷuva iraṅkuva ulappil tērkaḷē. 24
   901 karintiṭu mēṉiyuṅ kaṇippil tāṉavar

   903 nīḷa markku nerunalil pōntupiṉ

   909 maṉṉaṉ āṇaiyiṉ maṇṭamar āṟṟiyē

   muṉṉai vaikaliṟ pōreṉṟum uṉṉalāy. 33
   910 eṉṟa māṟṟam eṉaturai yākavē

   911 ēma kūṭa meṉappeya rākiya

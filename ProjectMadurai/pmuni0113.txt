vaṭa mali nikaṇṭu tokuppu : īsvara pārati

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The original work was compiled by Iswara Bharati, son of Chidambara
Bharati in the Kollam year 876, corresponding to 1700.A.D. It was called
"iriyUr vadamali nikaNtu". Although it contined three chapters as
mentioned in the dedication stanzas, only the second chapter is
avilable in manuscript and is secured in Dr. U.V.Swaminatha Iyer
Library.
It was due to the devoted efforts of late Dr. U.V.Swaminatha Iyer, who
is revered as tamiḻt tāttā, that this manuscript was collected and
preserved.
The source of this text is the edition published by Dr. U.V.Swaminatha
Iyer Library, edited by Tiru. R.Nagaswamy in 1983. This pmaduri file
contins only the original verses (moolam) and not the meaning and notes
included in the printed book.

kāppup pāyiram

kollameṇṇūṟ ṟeḻupattā ṟeṉakkaṇitap

pāvāṇar kuṟitta vāṇṭiṉ

palapeyark kūṭṭat torupeyark kāṇṭamum
coṟporuṭ kāṇṭat tokimūṉ ṟākki
īṭṭupukaḻ iriyūr vaṭamali nikaṇṭeṉa
nāṭṭiṉaṉ maṭaci naṉṉakar vāḻum
citampara pārati cēytiṉa mumiyāḷ
patampaṇi yīcura pārati tāṉē.
kāppup pāyiram muṟṟum.
kaṇṇaṉuṅ kaṇṇaṉik kātta nelvēlik
kaṇṇaṉuñ centamiḻk kāppā kuvarē.
tikkaṉ puṟupeyar nūlceyyavaṭi vāḷukanta
nakkaṉ pukaḻnelli nāyaka-mukkaṇṇiṟi
cāratitā ṉevavvulakan tantōṉcen nāviṉiṟcēr
pāratitāḷ vāḻttiniṉip pām.

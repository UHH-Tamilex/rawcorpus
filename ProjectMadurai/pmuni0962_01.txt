tirumākaṟal kārttikēya mutaliyār iyaṟṟiya kacci itaḻakal antāti

Source: nūl paṟṟiya vivaraṅkaḷ
kacci itaḻakal antāti
itu citāppēṭṭi, 'tiyalājikal kālēj' tamiḻp paṇṭitar
tirumākaṟal - pa -ti. kārttikēya mutaliyārāl iyaṟṟappaṭṭu
“irāmāyaṇasāram", "irājarājēsvari" mutaliyavaṟṟiṉ nūlāciriyar
ceyyūr - muttiya mutaliyār, pī.ē iyaṟṟiya uriyuṭaṉ
ceṉṉi: 'ṭiviṉ' accukkūṭattiṟ patippikkappaṭṭatu.
1907.
------------

mukavuri.

civamayam.
nīrāḻiyuṟṟa ikkilavulakil celvaṅkaḷ palavum ciṟantu viḷaṅkuvatu toṇṭimānāṭu.
annāṭṭukkut tilakameṉa oḷirvatu emmiṟivaṉ orumāviṟperumāṉ uṟiyum kacciyampati.
ippatiyē puṇṇiya talaṅkaḷ palavaṟṟuḷḷum ciṟantateṉa iṟivaṉālēyē iyampap peṟṟatu.
"puṇṇiyameyt tirukkāñci yevaṟṟiṉumēm paṭupuriyā mavvūr taṉṉi
yeṇṇiluṅkēṭ piṉumacoliṉum vaṇaṅkiṉumpē riṉpavī ṭevarkku nalkum"
eṉṟapaṭi intat talatti maṉattāl niṉikkiṉum nammuṭiya pāpam nacikkum eṉil itaṉ ciṟappi
vēṟu eṉṉeṉṟu collutal kūṭum?
ittuṇic ciṟappuvāynta ippatiyil pattarkkeḷiyarāy viḷaṅkum paramacivattiṉmītu
aḷavillāta patti yuṭiyarāvar innūliṉ āciriyar; ivar naṟkuṇattilum naṟceykiyilum
mikac ciṟantavar; tamiḻmītum, tamiḻapimāṉikaḷ mītum ārvam mikuntavar; eṉakkut tamiḻiṉ
arumi perumikaḷi viḷaṅkak kūṟi, atiluḷḷa vaṟṟāta cuviyi nāṉ eṉṟeṉṟum cuvittu
ānantikkumāṟu mutaṉmutalil cuvi kāṭṭiyavarum ivarē.
ivar civaperumāṉiṭattut tamakkuḷḷa aṉpiṉ mikutiyāl, ik "kacci itaḻakal antāti'yi
iyaṟṟuvārāyiṉar. antātiyāvatu oru ceyyuḷiṟutiyiḷḷa eḻuttu, aci, cīr allatu aṭi
aṭutta ceyyuḷiṉ ātiyāka varattoṭuppatu. “itaḻ akal'' eṉṟāl mēlitaḻum kīḻitaḻum
oṉṟōṭoṉṟu cērāmal akaṉṟē niṟpatu. atāvatu, icceyyuḷkaḷi uccarikkumpōtu utaṭukaḷ
oṉṟupaṭāmal akaṉṟē niṟkum. itaṉi vaṭanūlār "nirōṭṭakam" eṉpar.
innūlāciriyar kāñcīpurattil tammuṭiya āciriyar va. kaṇapati piḷḷiyavarkaḷiṉiṭam
tolkāppiya mutaliyaṉa pāṭaṅ kēṭṭakālattil iyaṟṟiya itaṉi ippōtu āṅkuḷḷa aṉpar palar
vēṇṭikkoṇṭapaṭi acciṭṭu veḷiyiṭuvārāyiṉar; āṉāl, ceyyuḷkaḷi maṭṭil
veḷiyiṭuvatu paṇṭitarkkēyaṉṟi ēṉiyōrkkum payaṉpaṭātākaliṉ, uriyuṭaṉ veḷiyiṭal
nalameṉṟu eṉṉiṭam kuṟippittamiyāl, yāṉ ippōtu itaṟku uriyeḻutalāṉēṉ. pōtiya
avakācamiṉmiyāl uri virivil eḻutappaṭavilli yāyiṉum, ippōtu eḻutappaṭṭirukkum
patavuri, kuṟippuri, karutturikaḷiṉiṉṟumē evarum poruḷ eḷitil aṟintukoḷḷak
kūṭumeṉṟu nampukiṉṟēṉ.
yāṉ mēṟkoṇṭa toḻilukkiṭikkiṭiyē kiṭitta avakācattilum avacarattilum itu
eḻutappaṭṭatātaliṉ, itiṟ piṟaḻvukaḷ uḷavēl, avaṟṟit tiruttiyamittuk koḷḷumāṟu
centamiḻc celvarkaḷik kēṭṭukkoḷḷukiṉṟēṉ.
ceṉṉi,
21-3-1907 }       ceyyūr - muttiya mutaliyār.

kacci itaḻakal antāti.

u - civamayam.
kāppu.
veṇpā.
kaṅkikaṉin tīṉṟa taṉik kātalaṉi yiṅkaraṉi
naṅki ciraniṟīi naṉṟēttic - caṅkiyaṟak
kacciyan tāti kaḻaṟinalaṅ kaṇṭakkā
licci yiṭarka ḷili.       (1)
tiṅka ḷaṇicaṭiyāṉ cēyaṭikaṭ kantāti
naṅkaḷaṅ kēka nayanatiyaṟṟac - ciṅkanikar
kantaṉi yiṟṟik kaṉintiṟiñci naṉṉeñcē
cintaṉi ceytē teḷi.       (2)
u -- civamayam.
nūl.
kaṭṭaḷikkalittuṟi
kaṅkā taraṉanta kāri caṭiyaṉ kayiliyaṉa
kaṅkā taraṉanta nantaṉa nātaṉ kaḻaṉiyiṉ ṟaṇ
kaṅkā taraṉantaṟ kīrṅkati tantaṉaṉ kāciṉiya
kaṅkā taraṉanta raṅkeṭak kacciyiṟ kaṇṭaṉaṉē.       (1)
patam - poruḷ. kaṅkātaraṉ - kaṅkiyit tarittavaṉum; antakāri - antakā cūraṉi
veṉṟavaṉum; caṭiyaṉ - caṭiyuṭiyōṉum; kayiliyaṉ - veḷḷimali yuṭiyōṉum; akam –
pāvaṅkaḷi; kātu - pōkkukiṉṟa; araṉ - araṉeṉṉum peyarōṉum; nantanantaṉaṉ –
tirumālukku; nātaṉ – iṟiyōṉum; kaḻaṉiyiṉ – kaḻaṉikaḷiṉ; taṇkaṅku - taṇṇiya
varampiṭattu; ātaraṉ – viruppamuḷḷōṉākiya; nantaṟku - tirunāḷip pōvārukku;
īrṅkati - kuḷirnta vīṭṭulakatti; tantaṉaṉ - koṭuttōṉumākiya civaperumāṉ; kāciṉiyakam
– ippūmiyiṭattu; kātaral - nammik kāttalic ceyka; nam taraṅ keṭa - namatu accam nīṅka;
kacciyiṟ kaṇṭaṉaṉ - aṉṉavaṉik kacciyiṟ kaṇṭēṉ.
karuttu: -kaṅkiyit tarittavaṉum, antakācūraṉi veṉṟavaṉum, jaṭātāriyum, veḷḷimali
yuṭiyavaṉum, araṉeṉṉum peyarōṉum, tirumālukku iṟiyōṉum, tirunāḷip pōvārukku
vīṭṭulakattit tantavaṉumākiya civaperumāṉi nāṉ kaccippatiyiṟ kaṇṭēṉ. aṉṉavaṉ nammik
kāttaruḷka. (1)
----------
kaṇṇalaṅ kāri yaḻikka naṭantē kaṉiceykari
kaṇṇalaṅ kāri yeṉaccāṟṟi neñcaṅ karintaṉaniṟ
kaṇṇalaṅ kāri yayaṟkari tāṅkati kāṇaleṟṟaṅ
kaṇṇalaṅ kāriyar caṅkañcēr kaccik kaṉanitiyē.       (2)
patam – poruḷ; kaṇṇalaṅkāri - kaṇṇalaṅkāramuḷḷa oru mātu; aḻikka - kūppiṭa; naṭantu –
pōy; kaṉicey - kaṉitalic ceykiṉṟa; kari - (uṉatu) moḻi; kaḷnalam – tēṉalam
payappatākum; kārinalam - kiḷinalam payappatākum; eṉaccāṟṟi - eṉṟu colli; neñcam
karintaṉam - maṉamurukiṉōm; niṟkaṇṇalam – niṉṉi yeṇṇilōm; kāri - intiraṉukkum;
ayaṟku – piramaṉukkum; aritām – kiṭittaṟkaritākiya; kati – mōṭcam; kāṇal ēṟṟu -
kāṇutaleppaṭi? aṅkaṇ – avviṭattu; alaṅku – viḷaṅkukiṉṟa; āriyar – periyāratu;
caṅkam cēr - kūṭṭam cērkiṉṟa; kaccik kaṉa nitiyē - kacciyiṉ kaṭperunitiyē.
kuṟippu:-- oru toṭaruḷ iṟuti niṉṟa peyarkku māttiram urupu virintu niṉṟu
maṟṟip peyarkaḷil urupu tokku varutalumuṇṭu.
karuttu:- intirāti tēvarviḷaṅkum kaccippatiyil vīṟṟirukkum emmiṟiyē!
alaṅkāramuḷḷa oru peṇṇip piṉ ceṉṟu "ā! uṉ moḻi tēṉmoḻiyō? kiḷimoḻiyō eṉṟu
maṉamurukiṉēṉēyoḻiya, uṉṉi oru kaṇamēṉum niṉittilēṉ. eṉ ceykēṉ? ippaṭippaṭṭa
eṉakku intiraṉ piramaṉ mutaliyavarkkum kiṭittaṟkariya mōṭcam eṅṅaṉam kiṭikkum? (2)
-----------
kaṉakaṅki yāralaṅ kacciyi ṉēcar kaṉiyiratak
kaṉakaṅki yāralaṅ kāra nakilak katirtikaḻ taṇ
kaṉakaṅki yāralaṅ kāṇā tiṟiñciyak kātalarcīr
kaṉakaṅki yāralaṅ kāṇari tērkiḷar kātaliyē.       (3)
patam – poruḷ: kaṉakam – poṉṉāl; ki – alaṅkarikkum; āral - matil cūḻnta; am
kacciyil - aḻakiya kāñciyil; nēcar – aṉpuṭiyārum; kaṉiyiratam - kaṉikaḷippeyyu
māvaṭiyil vāḻum; kaṉa kaṅkiyār - mēṉmiporuntiya kaṅkātararumākiya civaperumāṉ;
alaṅkāra nakilam - alaṅkāramuḷḷa koṅkiyākiya; katir tikaḻ taṇ kal nakam - oḷiviḷaṅkun
taṇṇiyamaliyiṉkaṇ; kiyār - veṟuppaṭiyār. (ākaliṉ); alam kāṇātu - tuṉpatti
nōkkāmal; iṟiñci – vaṇaṅki; akkātalar cīr kal - annāyakaratu cīrmiyikkaṟka; nakam -
nakutal ceyyām; ki – ciṟumitarum; āralam – paki; kāṇaritu - kāṇutal kūṭātu; ēr
kiḷar kātaliyē - aḻakāl viḷaṅkukiṉṟa kātaliyē.
karuttu:- pēraḻakuvāynta peṇṇē! aḻakiya kāñciyil aṉpuṭiyavarāy māmarattaṭiyil
viḷaṅkuñ civaperumāṉ uṉ mītu veṟuppaṭiyār. ākiyāl annāyakari vaṇaṅki avaruṭiya
cīrmiyik kaṟka. (3)
------------
kātala ṉantarañ cēriṇṭik kitiyaṅ kacciyikal
kātala ṉantara ṉēttanal lantaṇaṉ kaṇṭiṟiñcak
kātala ṉantara ṉiṉṟāci cāṟṟak kaḻinalaṉā
kātala ṉantara ṉākanaṉ ṉeñcaṅ kalantiṉitē.       (4)
patam – poruḷ: kātal – aṉpuṭaṉē; aṉam – aṉṉappaṟavikaḷ; taram – kūṭṭamāka; cēr –
cērkiṉṟa; iṇṭi – tāmarimalarkaḷiyuṭiya; kiti - vayalcūḻnta aḻakiya kacciyāṉatu;
ikalkātu - pakivarik kollum; alaṉ - kalappi yuṭiyōṉum; nantu - caṅkiṟku; araṉ -
iṟiyōṉumākiya tirumāl; ētta - tutikkavum, nal antaṇaṉ - nallapiramaṉ; kaṇṭu iṟiñca;
kātalaṉ - kaṟpakac cōliyum nalliṭamumuḷḷa; antaraṉ - cuvarkkēntiraṉ; niṉṟu ācicāṟṟa -
niṉṟu vāḻtteṭuppavum, kaḻinalaṉ - mika iṉpantaruvatākum; ākātu alal
-tuṉpamuṇṭākātu; nal neñcam - nalneñcattil ; iṉitu kalantu - iṉitēkalantu; nam taraṉ
āka - atu namakku vacappaṭṭatāka.
karuttu:- vaḷamimikka kaccippatiyāṉatu, tirumāl tutikkavum, piramaṉkaṇṭu vaṇaṅkavum,
cuvarkkēntiraṉ niṉṟu vāḻtteṭuppavum, mēlāṉa iṉpantaruvatākum. nāmum appatiyi
niṉintu naṟkatiyaṭivōmāka. (4)
------------
kalakaṅka ṇantat tikaḻkaccic caṅkaraṉ kaṉṉi
kalakaṅka ṇantac ciṟakalar kēkayaṅ kaṇṭeṉiṉi
kalakaṅka ṇanta karātila neñcē kaṉintiṅṅaṉē
kalakaṅka ṇantarit tantaṇar cērntatik kaṇṇinaṉṟē.       (5)
patam – poruḷ: kalakaṅkaḷ - tīmikaḷ; nanta - keṭa; tikaḻkacci – viḷaṅkukiṉṟa kacciyiṉ
kaṇ; caṅkaraṉ – civaperumāṉatu; kaṉṉi – aḻiviṉmiyākiya; nantikkallakam -
nantimaliyiṉiṭattu; kaṇnanta – kaṇkaḷ vaḷara; ciṟaku - tōkikaḷi; alar - virikkiṉṟa;
kēkayam – mayilkaḷi kaṇṭēṉ eṉil; ikal akam - pakikkiṉṟa pāvaṅkaḷ; kaṇam – kaṇappōtil;
takarātil - keṭuvaṉavām. (ākaliṉ) neñcē -maṉamē; kaṉintu – makiḻntu; iṅṅaṉē kala -
ivviṭattiṟ kalakka. antaṇar – muṉivarkaḷ; kaṅkaṇam tarittu - viratañceytukoṇṭu;
cērntati - cērntiruntati
periteṇṇi
karuttu:- kaccippatiyil civaperumāṉatu nantimaliyil uḷḷa mayiṟ kūṭṭaṅkaḷik
kaṇṭamāttirattilēyē nāmceyta pāvaṅkaḷellām aṟavē oḻintuviṭum. ākiyāl, maṉamē!
muṉivarkaḷ viratañ ceytukoṇṭirukkum paṭiyāṉa appatiyil nī eppōtum kalanti
ruppāyāka. (5)
---------------------
kaṇṭiki yāṉalañ ceykiṉṟa ceṅkik kaṉataṉattār
kaṇṭiki yāṉa karattiṟ ciṟantanaṅ kacciyiyē
kaṇṭiki yāṉañ ceṟinan taṉaṅkāṇ kariliṅṅaṉaṅ
kaṇṭiki yāṉa kalinitaṅ kaṟka kariyaneñcē.       (6)
patam – poruḷ: kaṇṭikiyāṉ – kaṭakattiṉāl; nalamceykiṉṟa – aḻakuceykiṉṟa; cemki –
civantakiyum; kaṉataṉattār - kaṉamākiya koṅkiyu muṭiya mātaratu; kaṇ tikiyāṉ -
kaṇṇiṟku mayaṅkātavaṉākiya civaperumāṉatu; nakarattiṟciṟanta - ellā nakaraṅkaḷilu mēmpaṭṭa;
nam kacciyiyē - namatu kāñciyiyē;
kaṇṭu iki – kaṇṭu koṭuppāyāka; yāṉamceṟi – tēṉ ceṟiyum; nantaṉam – pūṅkāvaṉam;
kāṇ - kāṇpāyāka; karil - kuṟṟaṅkaḷi; iṅṅaṉam kaṇṭi - iṅkē nīkkuvāyāka; kiyāṉa -
oḻukkamākiya; kali - nūlkaḷi; nitam kaṟka – tiṉamuṅ kaṟpāyāka; kariya neñcē - kariya
maṉamē.
karuttu:- kuṟṟaṅkaḷ niṟinta maṉamē! civanta kiyum, ciṟanta koṅkiyumuṭiya mātaratu
kaṇvalikku mayaṅkātavaṉākiya civaperumāṉi, puṇṇiya talaṅkaḷ palavaṟṟuḷḷum ciṟanta
kaccippatiyil nī kaṇṭu, oḻukkanūli ōti, uṉṉiṭattiluḷḷa kuṟṟaṅkaḷi oḻippāyāka. (6)
----------
kariyā ṉaṉantaḻi yāniṉṟa nāyakaṉ kantaṉayaṉ
kariyā ṉaṉanta cayaṉa ṉiṟiñceḻiṟ kacciyiṟcaṅ
kariyā ṉaṉanta nititan taṇaṅkiṉ kaṉataṉacci
kariyā ṉaṉanta raṭintēṟ kiṉiya katitaralē.       (7)
patam – poruḷ: kari āṉaṉam - yāṉi mukattiṉāl; taḻiyāniṉṟa – taḻintukoṇṭirukkiṉṟa;
nāyakaṉ – vināyakaṉum; kantaṉ - murukaṉum; ayaṉ – piramaṉum; kariyāṉ - intiraṉum;
aṉantacayaṉaṉ – tirumālum; iṟiñcu – vaṇaṅkukiṉṟa; eḻil kacciyil - aḻakiya
kāñciyiṉkaṇ; caṅkariyāṉ – maṅki pākaṉāṉa civaperumāṉē! anantam -
aḷaviṉmiyākiya;nititantu - poruḷ koṭuttu; aṇaṅkiṉ - peṇṇiṉatu; kaṉataṉam - kaṉatta
koṅkiyākiya; cikariyāṉ – maliyiṉāl; aṉantaraṭintēṟku – mayakkamaṭintēṉukku;
iṉiyakati – iṉiya vīṭṭulakatti; taral - taruka.
kuṟippu:- caṅkariyāṉ aṇmicuṭṭiya viḷinilikkiḷavi. iṉi, akati = narakam, taral=taraṟka
eṉalumām.
karuttu:- vināyakaṉ, murukaṉ, piramaṉ, intiraṉ, tirumāl mutaliyōr vaṇaṅkum
kaccippatiyiluḷḷa maṅkipākaṉāṉa civaperumāṉē! nāṉ oru peṇṇukkup perum poruḷ
koṭuttu, avaḷuṭiya koṅkiyākiya maliyāl mōtuṇṭu mayakka maṭintēṉ. eṉakku narakattit
tarāmal pēriṉpan tantaruḷal vēṇṭum. (7)
------------
taraṉan tiyākat taḻakiṉa ṉāraṇat tanticaci
taraṉan tiyākat tikaḻara ṉeṅkaḷ caracaṉaṟcī
taraṉan tiyāka nitañceyaṅ kacciyiṟ cārntaniran
taraṉan tiyāka naciyaṉañ cintiyiṟ ṟaṅkiṉaṉē.       (8)
patam – poruḷ: taraṉ – civanta; anti - antikkālampōla; ākattaḻakiṉaṉ -
uṭampilaḻakuṭiyāṉum, āraṇam – tanti - tantiyum; cacitaraṉ - piṟi cūṭiyum,
nantiyāka - nantīcaṉāka; tikaḻaraṉ - viḷaṅkukiṉṟa araṉum, eṅkaḷ caracaṉ - eṅkaḷukku
iṉiyaṉum, naṟcītaraṉ - nalla tirumāl; nantu – vaḷarkiṉṟa; yākam – yākaṅkaḷi; nitam –
nāḷtōṟum; cey – ceykiṉṟa; amkacciyil - aḻakiya kacciyiṉkaṇ; cārnta nirantaraṉ -
cārnta nirantaraṉum; nam - namakku; tiyākanaciyaṉ - koṭuttaṟku viruppamuḷḷavaṉu mākiya
civaperumāṉ; namcintiyil - nammaṉattil; taṅkiṉaṉ - taṅkiyirukkiṉṟaṉaṉ.
karuttu:- tirumāliṉāl yākaṅkaḷ ceyyappaṭum kaccippatiyiluḷḷa civaperumāṉ nam
maṉattil taṅkiyirukkiṉṟaṉaṉ. accivaperumāṉ mika vaṭivuṭiya cuntaraṉ; avaṉē
vētaṅkaḷukkut tanti; avaṉē piṟicūṭi; avaṉē nantīcaṉāka viḷaṅkum araṉ; avaṉē namakku
iṉiyaṉ; avaṉē evarkkum virumpiyatik koṭukkum viruppuṭiyavaṉ. (8)

taṅkat tikittaṉaṅ kiyila ṉīyeṉac cāṟṟilarcit
taṅkat tikittaṉa ṉallaṉeṉ kacciyiṟ cantaṉaṉē.       (9)
patam – poruḷ: taṅkam – taṅkampōlum; tiki - tēmaliyuṭiya; taṉattār -
koṅkiyiyuṭiya mātarkaḷ; nakikāṭṭi - muṟuvalceytu; tayaṅki – viḷaṅki; etirtaṅka -
etiril niṟka; tikittaṉam - mayaṅkiṉōm; kēkayam – mayilē; tēṉ – tēṉē; eṉa –
eṉṟurittu; nittam – tiṉamum; tāṅkiṉam - kāttōm; kiyilaṉ -
kiyiloṉṟu milēṉ (ātalāl) kattikit taṉam - pūmāli vāṅkittanta poruḷi;
ī eṉa - īyeṉṟu kēṭka; cāṟṟilar - oṉṟum pēcilar; cittam - avar maṉam; katti -
kattipōluṅ koṭitu; kittaṉaṉ allaṉ - (iṉṉum) veṟuttēṉ allaṉ. eṉ -
iḵtu eṉṉa kāraṇam? kacciyil – kāñciyiṉkaṇ; cantaṉaṉē - nalla poṉpōluñ civaperumāṉē.
karuttu:- taṅkam pōlum tēmal niṟinta mātorutti puṉṉaki purintu eṉṉetiril niṟka,
avaḷikkaṇṭu mayaṅkiṉēṉ; 'mayilē' eṉṟēṉ; 'tēṉē' eṉṟēṉ ; avaḷi aṉutiṉamuṅ kāttēṉ;
uḷḷaporuḷelām oḻiyavum, kiyi loṉṟumilli; pūmāli vāṅkittanta poruḷiyāvatu
koṭu' eṉṟu kēṭṭēṉ. avaḷō vāytiṟantilaḷ! ā! ā!! avaḷ maṉam mikak koṭitu.
iviyellām aṟintum, iṉṉum nāṉ avaḷi veṟuttilaṉē! kaccippatiyiluḷḷa
civaperumāṉē! itu eṉṉa kāraṇam? (9)

karuttu:- kacciyiṉkaṭ civaperumāṉē! niṉakkut tūtuviṭṭa aṉṉappuḷ niṉ tēr ceṉṟa

makiḻntu puṇara vāṭiyuyir taḷirkkum; celvamumuṇṭām; taliviyum viruppu
vittirukkiṉṟaṉaḷ. (10)

karuniṟamuṭiyōm; nalñāṉam – nalluṇarvi; tariṉ – tantāl; naṉṟu - naṉṟākum; nañcam –
uṇṭanañcamē; kari - (taḷḷāma lāḷutaṟku)c cāṉṟākum. antu -

kuṟippu :-- kāmāṭci: ka =kalimakaḷiyum, mā = tirumakaḷiyum,aṭci = kaṇṇil vittuk

nañciyuma taḷḷāmal uṭkoṇṭaṉiyō, aṅṅaṉamē eṉṉiyum taḷḷiviṭāmal ēṟṟaruḷal vēṇṭum.
(11)

ñāṉavitti niṟinta; caṅkaracaṅkara - caṅkaraṉē caṅkaraṉē; nītāy - nī eṉakkuttāy; eṉa - eṉṟu
tutikka; kēṭṭu eṉ - kēṭṭup payaṉeṉṉi? tāḻ neñcu -
tāḻnta maṉamē! iḷañcaṅkar - iḷamiyākiya caṅkavaḷiyalaṇinta makaḷiri nikarttāy;
acam – āṭukaḷi; karantu – vañcittu; yākañceykiṉṟaṉi – yākañceykiṉṟāy;
taṇ aṟiñar - taṇṇiya aṟiñaratu; caṅkaracam - caṅkattāl viḷiyum ñāṉaracattiṉāl; karam

tantuviḷaṅkuka.
karuttu:- pāñcacaṉṉiyamuṭiya tirumāl "caṅkaraṉē! nīyē eṉakkuttāy!" eṉṟu tutippati nī
kēṭpataṉāl yātu payaṉ? uṉṉiṭamuḷḷa vañcaṉiyi oḻittāyilli. ākiyāl, ē maṉamē! nī uṭaṉē
kaccippaticeṉṟu uṉkuṟṟatti oḻittu oḷiyuṭaṉ viḷaṅkuvāyāka. (12)

tiṉamuñceytu; iyaṅku – viḷaṅkukiṉṟa; āri – cōḻaṉi; kikkaṉṟiṉiṉ – iḷaṅkaṉṟiṉāl;
āyntaṉaṉ - ārāycciceytavaṉum, taṇṇaḷiyaṉ - taṇṇiya aruṭpiḻampāṉavaṉum, tanti -
yāṉiyāl vaḷarkkappaṭṭa; amkāriki -
aḻakiya peṇṇukku; kātalaṉ - nāyakaṉāṉa murukaṉukku; tanti - tantiyumākiya

(ākaliṉ) iki – koṭuppāyāka; kaccittalattil – kāñcittalattiṉkaṇ; calaṅkaṭintu -

eṉṟē - eṉṟu colliyē; karantu iṟiñca – vañcittuvaṇaṅka; karanti - (nīyum) vañcittu
niṉṟāy. alaṅkal ilaṉ – irakkamillātavaṉ; eṉṉal - eṉṟu collal; eṉ - eṉkāraṇam? kalik
kiyaraṉē - māṉpiṭitta kiyuṭiya civaperumāṉē.
kuṟippu :- tuttu tuppu mutaliyaṉa tuvveṉṉun taṉiviṉiyiṟ piṟanta toḻiṟpeyar.
karuttu:- ceñcaṭiyuṭiyāṉ mutaliya uṉ peyarkaḷi uṇmiyākac colli vaṇaṅkāmal vañcittuc
colli vaṇaṅkiṉamiyāl nīyum vañcittu niṉṟāy. nī irakkamillātavaṉ eṉṉal eṉṉi? (22)

kiyāṟ ṟiṉaṉanti nāyakaṉ cenneṟ kaḻaṉiyalik

kiyāṟ ṟiṉaṉanti yāḷaṉeṉ ṟēttik kariyāṟṟa
kiyāṟ ṟiṉaṉanti tāñcaṭi yāṉaṅṅaṉ kaṇṭilaṅkē.      (23)
patam – poruḷ: kiyāṟu – oḻukkamuṭiya; iṉ ananti - iṉiya pārvatikku; nāyakaṉ –
talivaṉ; cennel - cennel koḻikkum; kaḻaṉi – vayalkaḷ; alikkiyāl - aliyākiya

ēṟṟukiṉṟa; eḻiṟkacci – aḻakiyakacciyiṉkaṇ; kaṅkiyāṟṟiṉaṉ - kaṅkiyāṟṟiyuṭiyavaṉ; nam
tiyāḷaṉ - namatu tiruvaruṭ celvaṉ; eṉṟētti - eṉṟu toḻutu; kariya - maṉamuruka;
naltaki – naṟkuṇaṅkaḷi; āṟṟiṉaṉ – ceytāṉ; nantu – vaḷarkiṉṟa; ai tu ām –

– pārttu; ilaṅku - viḷaṅkuka.
kuṟippu:- naṟṟaki yāṟṟiṉaṉ = mikka taḷarcciyit taṇittāṉ eṉalumām. tayāḷaṉ -

avviṭattippārttu viḷaṅkuka. (23)

ilaṅki yaṉaṅkañ citittā ṉiṟiñcara ṉiṇṭiyalar

– tirukkuḷaṅkaḷil; ai aṉam - aḻakiya aṉṉappaṟavikaḷ; kañcaṉantikaḻ – kaṇṇāṭipōlviḷaṅkum;

(24)

anta nantu aṉa – anta caṅkippōlum; kantarattāy – kaḻuttuṭiya mātē.

pēraṟivāḷaṉ; avaṉē tirumāl vaṇaṅkum araṉ; avaṉē aḷavaṟṟa ciṟappuṭiyōṉ; avaṉē

tiriki yakaṅkic caṭittalik kacciyiṟ ciriyaṉē.       (30)
patam – poruḷ: tiriki akam - kuyavaṉ cakkarampōṉṟa maṉam; kiyaṭieṉ - aṭikkalameṉṟu;
cāṟṟi – colli; cilinakilam - malipōluṅ koṅkiyuṭiya; tiri – mātaratu; kiakam
-kivacappaṭṭu; kiyaṟalāyiṟṟē - oḻukkamillāmaṟ pōyiṟṟē; eḻil – eḻucciyuṭiya;
cētaṉakkatir – ñāṉacūriyaṉi; iki – aruḷka; akam – tīviṉiyāṉatu; kittu – veṟuttu;
irintu – murintu; aḻintiṭal – aḻintiṭuka; tēriṉ – ārāyiṉ; aḵtu –
attīviṉiyē; etiri – pakiyākum; kiya – alaṅkāramuḷḷa; kaṅki – kaṅkiyittaritta;
caṭittali – caṭiniṟinta taliyuṭiya; kacciyiṟ cīriyaṉē - kacciyiṉkaṭ cīriya
civaperumāṉē.
kuṟippu :- kiya kuṟippup peyareccam.
karuttu :-- cuṟṟic cuṟṟivarum kuyavaṉ cakkarattiyotta maṉatti aṭikkalameṉṟu colli,
malipōluṅ koṅkiyuṭiya maṅkiyar vacappaṭṭu oḻukkaṅ kuṟintavaṉāyiṉēṉ. eṉ ceyvēṉ?
kaṅkātāriyāy kacciyil viḷaṅkuñ civaperumāṉē! eṉṉuṭiya tīviṉiyi aḻittu eṉakku
ñāṉaccuṭar nalkuvāyāka. (30)

                      paḷḷikoṇṭāṉ piḷḷai
               iyaṟṟiya pirapantat tiraṭṭu - pākam 10
            tiruniṉṟavūr eṉṉaip peṟṟatāyār mālai

   srīmatē rāmānujāya nama:
   tiruniṉṟavūr eṉṇaip peṟṟatāyār mālai
   kāppu.
   naṉaiyīṉṟa nāṇmalarkkā nāppaṇuṟainiṉṟai
   yeṉaiyīṉṟa tāyi ṉiṇaiyil - kaṉaiyīṉṟa
   nūpurattāṇmītukavi nōṉṟoṭaicāttakkurukai
   māpurattāṉkāppaṉ makiḻntu.
   nūl.
   tiṅkaṭiṉakaraṉaṉṉa caṅkāḻitikaḻtirukkaip
   paṅkayanōkkempirāṉ pattarāvipparamaṉmakiḻ
   paṅkayaiyēyeṉpavappiṇipāṟapparintapaya
   aṅkaikaviniṉṟavūrvāḻeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (1)
   amutavuṇaviṉarēttavamutavaḷakkarvantāy
   amutamoḻitoṭuttaṉpiṟpaḻiccumaṭiyavarkaṭ
   kamutamutavuntiruvēyaṭiyaṉkaṇaṉpuvaittāḷ
   amutakiraṇamukattāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (2)
   poruṭkaṇṇum pūvaiyarkaṇṇumpuvikkaṇum poṅkavittai
   yiruṭkaṇṇirī iniṉṉaṭiyāriṇaiyaṭiyeṉṟumeṇṇun
   teruṭkaṇṇutaviyeṉceññāṉa naṉṉilai tiṇṇanaṇṇa
   aruṭkaṇpuriniṉṟavūrvāḻeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (3)
   piṟantaṅkumiṅkum peritalaippuṇṭupeyarttumuṭal
   iṟantaṅkumiṅkumiṭumpaiyuṟṟēṉiṉiyēṉuniṉṟaṉ
   niṟantaṅkutāṇiṉaintuyyavōr cūḻcciniṉaittaruḷvāy
   aṟantaṅkuniṉṟaippativāḻeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (4)
   tīkkālpuṉalviṇpuviceycekattaic ceḻuṅkaruṇai
   nōkkālaḷikkumaḷikku notumalarnōkkaruṭkum
   vākkāloruvakaivācivakukkiṉ vaṇvāritiyum
   ākkālaṟalunikarāmeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē         (5)
   aṭukkaluṟumaṇṭakōṭiyuḷārntavaṉaittuyirkkum
   iṭukkaṇceymāyaiyorī ikkāttaḷikku niṉṉiṉṉicaikkum
   eṭukkum piṟar tammicaikkumuvamaiyicaikkiṉ mukaṭ
   ṭaṭukkalaṇunikarākumeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (6)
   kalaikkum purāṇavakaikkuntalaimaik kaṉamaṟaiyiṉ
   ṟalaikkumeṭṭāniṉṟakavārkuṇakaṇaccālpiṉukkum
   malaikku maṟṟōr māṭcimaikkumuvamaivakuttiṭiṟkuṇ
   ṭalaikkuṅkaḻikkunikarāmeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (7)
   kāṇikkarumaṭkaṭampōṉṟa kāyaṅkaḻintiṭuṅkāṉ
   māṇikkacceppiṉ varuntip piyavuṭaṉ maṉṉimaṟai
   vāṇikkaṇaṅkoṭuvāḻttaṭiyār kuḻumaṉṉavaippāy
   āṇikkaṉakamuṭiyāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (8)
   kūrattiṉāluyarntōṉeṉṟeṉainiṉ koḻunaṉeṉṟuṅ
   kōrattiṉāluyaricciṟaiyiṭṭaṉaṉkūyppuruṭa
   kārattiṉāleṉaikkāttaruḷvāy naṟkaṉakamaṇi
   ārattiṉāyiṉṉamutēyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (9)
   taṉpuṭaiyaṉ pilaṉeṉṟu cutantaraṉāntalaivaṉ
   eṉpuṭaiyicciṟaiyiṭṭaṉaṉeṉṟumivarnteḻumpēr
   iṉpuṭaivīṭṭiṉaiyeytumvirakoṉṟiḻaittaruḷvāy
   aṉpuṭaiyārkkaraṇāvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (10)
   ōrāviyaṟkaiyorumāyaiyāṉiṉṉuṭaiyavaṉcey
   tīrāviyaṟkaic ciṟumiyar ciṟṟiṉpaccēṟṟaiyorīit
   tūrāviyaṟkaiccuṭarccaṭarakkuḻi tūrttuniṉpāl
   ārāviyaṟkaiyavā nalkeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (11)
   kavittaiyavēṭaṉuṅkālaṉṟamaruñceykavvaitammāṟ
   ṟavittaiyavōvammavōveṉṟaraṟṟuñ camayamatiṟ
   puvittaiyalōṭu niṉṉaṉpaṉu nīyum purintuvanteṉ
   avittaiyiṉāṟṟalaṟuttāḷeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (12)
   tīrttapperumaittiruniṉṟaivāḻtiruvēkoṭiya
   tūrttappaṭiṟar toṭarpāṟṟoṭarntatukaḷaiyellān
   tīrttappuṟaniṉṟiruppatañcērntuyat tiṇṇameṇṇum
   ārttappirapaṉṉaṉācceyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (13)
   malakkaṇmakiḻpuḻumāṉanirantaramammarceyyum
   nilakkaṇṇitamunaṉṉīlakkaṇṇēriḻaiyārnitampap
   pilakkaṇvirumpum perumpētaimaiyiṟpiṟantiṟakkum
   alakkaṇṭavirttalaṟamāmeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (14)
   pūttikaḻ poykai politiruniṉṟaippurittiruvē
   āttikanāttikaṉākiyaṟattaiviṭṭallavai cey
   tōttikaḻntēṉeṉṉuṟu piḻainītteṉṟumuḷḷattuṇmai
   yāttikaṉākkiyaruḷvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (15)
   mulaippālaruntāmutirñāṉamāmuṉimuṉṉiyaruḷ
   kalaippāṟporuḷeṉkaruttiṟpatittavaṉkāṭṭuneṟi
   nilaippālirukkaniṉaittaruḷvāyniṉṟainittilamē
   alaippāluttiyatēyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (16)
   paṭiyārtoṭarpaipparī ipparamāṉantappatti veḷḷap
   paṭiyārpaṭiyiṟpayilvitteṉainiṉṟaṉpaṅkayattāṭ
   taṭiyāraṭiyārkkaṭiyāraṭiyārtamakkaṭiyār
   aṭiyārkkaṭiyavaṉācceyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (17)
   āḻvārkaṭam pukaḻāriyar tañcīramaittamuta
   vāḻvāranalkiṭum vaṇṭamiḻmālaivakutturaitteṉ
   ṟāḻvārpiṟavitaṇappāṉaruḷ caṉmacākarattil
   āḻvārtamakkampiyāvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (18)
   maruṅkalanīrvayal cūḻniṉṟaimāmakaḷē viṉaiyāñ
   ciruṅkalantēykkumpaṭaipavaccintuviṟcērnteṭukkum
   peruṅkalaniṉpeyarpēcumeṉceṉṉipperumakuṭa
   aruṅkalaniṉṉaṭiyākumeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (19)
   kārkkōlamāyavaṉ kaṇmaṇiyāniṉṟaikkāraṇiniṉ
   cīrkkōlaṅkaṇṭu ciṟakkāta cintaiyaṉṟīya camaṉ
   pōrkkōlaṅkoṇṭuporukkeṉaveṉṉuḻaippōtaruṅkāl
   ārkkōlamiṭṭaḻaippēṉ colleṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (20)
   vituraṉcutaṉmāviḻaikūṉimuṉṉamvimalaṉukku
   maturavaṭicilumālaiyuñcāntumvaḻaṅkiyapōṟ
   caturarkaḷ vāḻniṉṟaittākkaṇaṅkēniṟkuttaṇpiṟavi
   yaturaṉaḷikkavalaṉōveṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (21)
   muttinilaiyai mukuntaṉainiṉṉai moḻimanuvaic
   citticeytēcikaṉaiccīrccamantamañcērkkavalla
   cattiyavaṉṉataḻaittiṭacceyti taḻaṅkiṭumpāl
   attipeṟunantaṉaiyēyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (22)
   valaippaṭumīṉiṉivvaiyattu māyaimayakkaveṉṟu
   nilaippaṭuneñcamilātuḻalvēṉukkunēyaniṉpāṟ
   ṟalaippaṭumvaṇṇantayai ceytukāttitaraiyuṭutta
   alaippaṭunittila maṉṉāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (23)
   tātēyumiccaṭantaḷḷiyiliṅkattaṉuvuṭaṉē
   mītēyamaiyarccirātivaḻiyiṉai mēviṭuṅkāṟ
   pātēyaniṉ peyar pāṭal parikkum paramporuṇī
   ātēya nāṉeṉai yāḷvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (24)
   kōṇaippaṭuniṉporuḷkoḷaṉīkuyikkoṇṭukoḷḷum
   ūṇaittaṭuttaluyarkurumantiramuḷḷuṟaitē
   ēṇaipparipavañceytalivaṟṟaiyiritteṉainiṉ
   āṇaippaṭiyoḻukacceyyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (25)
   cūriyaiyintattuvakkeṉaiviṭṭuttolai tuṇaiyuñ
   cīriyaiyāriyaṉ ceynnaṉṟiyeṉṟumeṉcintai taṉṉiṟ
   kūriyaiyaṉpiṟkuṟittuyya nīkuṟikkoṇṭaruḷvāy
   āriyaiyēniṉṟavūrvāḻeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (26)
   niṉakkumeṉakkumupakārakaṉ kurunērutavi
   taṉakkuttakutiyatākuṅkaimmāṟu camaikkaveṇiṉ
   eṉakkuppalaruntakuporuḷeṅkaṇumillaiyeṉpa
   aṉakkumaraṉṟoḻuntāḷāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (27)
   cīrpūttaniṉṟaṉkalyāṇakuṇattiṉ ciṟappiṉaiyum
   pārpūttaniṉṉaruṭpattarkaḷ pāvaṉappaṇpiṉaiyum
   nārpūttaveṉṉeñciṉāṭṭiyaruḷutinaṟpaḻaṉat
   tārpūttaniṉṟaippativāḻeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (28)
   teruṭikaḻvaṇṇañ ciruṭṭi toṭaṅkit tiṉañceyuniṉ
   kiruṭiparamparaikēḻpeṟaviṉṉēkiḷattiṭumaim
   poruṭiripuntiyeṉpuntiyiṉoḷḷitiṟpōntutikka
   aruṭi tiruniṉṟaiyūrvāleṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (29)
   aintuvitamāmarumpātakattaiyumāṟṟuvikkum
   tupulaṉumavikkaṟiyēṉaṟitaṟkariya
   aintuporuḷkaḷumaṉṉavaiyovvoṉṟiṉumaṭaṅkum
   aintupakuppumaṟiviyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (30)
   pāvapparappupperumpuṟavāḻipparappaṭakkuṅ
   kōvatturuppaṅkoṭu muṟkakkōraṅkuḷirttiviṭum
   ōvattamillaṉavaṉṉavai nīkkiyuṇarvutantāḷ
   āvattarumporuḷvaippēyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (31)
   kālamelāmviḻaicceytiṉumāyiraṅkaṇṇaṉṟaṉpēr
   cālamelāñceyuntaiyalarācai tavirtalillai
   ñālamelāmitai naccinacikkunaviṟṟumitaṟ
   kālamelā nallaṉavāmeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (32)
   akaṟṟavoṇṇāvaṉpamaintiṭumaṉṉaiyariciṉattāl
   akaṟṟiṉumaṉṉāḷaruṇiṉaintēyaḻumakkuḻavi
   ikattileṉum periyōrmoḻikēṭṭilaiyōveṉainī
   akaṟṟiviṭutalaṟamaṉṟeṉaip peṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (33)
   viratikaḷpōṟṟuntiruniṉṟai vāḻkiṉṟavittakiyē
   iratinikarillavaḷiṉpamātiyikaccukamuñ
   curatiruvāticcukamuntavveṉṟeṟkuttōṟṟum vaṇṇam
   aratipiṟappateṉṟō colleṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (34)
   viyarttu viṉaivaḻiyivviḷaiyāṭṭu vipūtiyileṟ
   peyarttumpeyarttum piṟappikkiṉallapirāppiyamā
   uyartturai pūmiyuṟuvateṉṟōvivvuṭaloḻikāl
   ayarttoḻiyātaruḷ ceyvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē         (35)
   cēṭpaṭṭa tīviṉaitteṭpāṟciṟumiyar ciṟṟiṉpattē
   kōṭpaṭṭacintaiyaṉāyavarēvalkuyiṟṟeṉainiṉ
   ṟāṭpaṭṭapattimaittāḷāṇmaiyōr tiruttāḷkaḷuk ē
   āṭpaṭṭaṭimaiceyacceyyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         ( 36)
   muttattuvamumukurapporuḷeṉa muṟṟumeṉṟaṉ
   cittattirīiccitticcintaiyaṉāyeṉaicceytiṭanī
   cittuvakkiṟṟirunāṭṭuppēriṉpaccelvameṉa
   tattattaṭaintatalavōveṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (37)
   pōkampalavum purātaṉa vīṭum pukuttuṅkaṉma
   yōkampayilavuṇarvātiyillēṉuriyaṉalēṉ
   cōkam palavuntarumippiṟavitolaivateṅṅaṉ
   yākampayiṉiṉṟavūrvāleṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (38)
   eṭṭaṅkaṅkūṭiyiyaṉñāṉayōkamicaintiyaṟṟal
   kaṭṭaṅkaṭaippiṭiyillāveṉakkuniṉkāmarutāḷ
   eṭṭaṅkamunilaṉēyaviṟaiñciṉaṉeṉviṉaiyai
   aṭṭaṅkamutamaruḷvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (39)
   ikaḻtal ceyātuṉaiyeṇṇumavaritayāmpuyattiṟ
   ṟikaḻtal ceyuniṉṟiruvaṭittāmaraiyait tiṉamum
   pukaḻtalceyvitteṉṟaṉ pollā viṉaiyiṉ pukarkkiḻaṅkai
   yakaḻtal ceytāḷalaṟamāmeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (40)
   tāyuntakappaṉuñ cantatiyuntamarumperumai
   vāyuṅkuravaṉunteyvamumaṟṟumvaḷaṉunaṉmai
   yēyumpataṅkaḷumīṟiṉal vīṭṭiṉpumeṉṟumeṉak
   kāyumpoḻutuṉṉaṭiyāmeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (41)
   maṭiyā niṉmāyai mayakkameṉumaṅkuṉ māṟṟuvatum
   vaṭiyāppavakkaṭal vaṟṟiṭacceyvatumvāravittai
   viṭiyāviravaiviṭintiṭacceyvatumeymmaiyeṟkuṉ
   ṉaṭiyākumātittiyaṉēyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (42)
   avattaikkoṭuttaṭaiyāvarumpēṟṟaiyaḷikkavalla
   tavattaikkeṭuttuccatānantavāḻvait taṭuttiruḷcēr
   pavattaikkoṭutteṉaip pāḻākkumāyai payiṟṟiniṟkum
   avattaikkorumuṭiviṉṟōveṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (43)
   poṟukkavoṇṇāpperumpollāppiḻaippoṟaippokkaṇattaṉ
   kuṟukkavoṇṇākkumārk vaññāṉattiṉ koṭṭakattaṉ
   iṟukkavoṇṇātavirucerukkēṉeṉṉiruviṉaiyum
   aṟukkavoṇṇākavaṟuttāḷeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (44)
   vaṟiñarkkuccelvampaciyarkkuṇval civaṟantiṭunāc
   ceṟiñarkkut tīmpuṉaṉōyōrkkatakanteṟumpaṭaiyāl
   eṟiñarkkicaintiṭumīriraṇṭōṭatiyētamilā
   aṟiñarkkunallamutāvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (45)
   eṉṉaiyuṭampaiyiṉattaikkuṭumpiyaiyeṇṇayaṟṟēt
   taṉṉaivayiṇavartammaiyeṉakkaruḷcaṟkuruvai
   niṉṉaiyemmāṉai niṉaittirukkumvaṇṇanērntaruḷvāy
   aṉṉaiyarukkaṉṉaiyāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (46)
   vētamirutimutaṉūlil vīṟiviḷaṅkulaka
   nātaṉuṟuporuḷākinammāriyar naṟṟaṉamāy
   ētamaṟucaraṇākatiyiṉ poruḷīraintaiyum
   ātamuṟaveṟkaruḷvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (47)
   cōtararputtirar collaṉṉaitantaiyar tollaiyayaṉ
   ōtaraṉātiyarōvuṟuvaṟpoḻutōmpal ceyyār
   kātaranīkkikkai cōrātaṭiyaraikkāttaḷikka
   ātaraṅkoḷpavaṇīyēyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (48)
   kuravarkulaniṉ kulaṅkūṭiniṉṟukuravarpukaḻ
   paraviyeṉ kuṟṟampakarntu kuravarparamparai man
   tiramaṇinaṉku cevittuyyacceyticeyyāmaṟaiyiṉ
   aravamaṟāniṉṟavūrvāḻeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (49)
   ātikkuravaruraiyumoḻukkamumāyntukoṇṭu
   vātikkumaṟṟaiyarvārttaiyum vākaiyum vāyntoruvi
   nītikkuravaṉ paṇiniṉpaṇi ceytuniṟkavaruḷ
   ātikkamalattaṇaṅkēyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (50)
   aruccittacittoṭuniṉṉilai naṉṉaraṟintavittai
   varuccittunāḷumvaḷarumoḷimayamāyvayaṅkuṉ
   ṉuruccittamvaitteṇvakai malarkoṇṭu vaṭṭātavaṉ piṉ
   aruccittu niṟkavaruḷvāyeṉaippeṟṟavammaṉaiyē.         (51)
   eṇṇikkurampaiyaiyēṇuṟacceyyaviḻiporuḷai

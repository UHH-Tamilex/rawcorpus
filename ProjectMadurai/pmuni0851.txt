malikkoḻuntu nāvalar iyaṟṟiya tiruttoṇṭar catakam

Source:
tiruttoṇṭar catakam.
tirumāṇikkamāmali
kāñcīpurattil talamūṟṟāreṉṉum kaccimayēcurakulatilakarākiya
tiruppāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi malikkoḻuntu nāvalaravarkaḷāl iyaṟṟappaṭṭu,
ceṉṉi: matarās rippaṉ acciyantiracāliyil patippikkappaṭṭatu.
1914, vili aṇā
---------------
kuruvētuṇi.
tirunelvēli pututteruvilirunta mā-srī, vitvāṉ
aḻakiya cokkanātapiḷḷiyavarkaḷiyaṟṟiya ciṟappuppāyiram.
koccakakkalippā.
poṉṉintic cōṇāṭṭiṟ kāvirippūm paṭṭiṉattuc
ceṉṉiyeṉu maṉṉikalāṟ cintinontu cārvaṇikar
muṉṉiyavā ṟātarittut tillimūvāyirar pōṟ
paṉṉiruva reṉavavarip pārcolaccērn tuṟiparamaṉ. (1)
tōṭpōkki vāṇaṉimāyt tavaṉ ṟuruvap pātalakkīḻt
kāṭpōkki niṉṟapirāṉ ṟaṉi nutaṟkaṭ ṭaḻaṟpaṭiyāl
vēṭpōkki malimakaḷi maṇantaruṇi veṟpaṉiya
vāṭpōkki civatiyeṉu māṇikka maliyamarn tōṉ. (2)
alikkoḻuntu muṭaṅkukaṅki yāṟṟiṉoṭuṅ kīṟṟumatik
kalikkoḻuntu paṭarumuṭit taḻumpar tiruk kaḻaliṇikkē
yilikkoḻuntu malariṉ vaki pularumeṉa veḻiṟkacci
malikkoḻuntu nāvalaṉpā māli toṭut taṇintaṉaṉāl. (3)
cāttiṭumam mālikip pēr tiruttoṇṭar catakamaḵ
tēttiṭunal laṭiyākat tiruḷakaṟṟi yeppaṟṟu
nīttiṭumeñ ñāṉa nalki nimalaṉaṭi niḻaliruttik
kāttiṭunic cayamataṉik karatalā malakameṉa. (4)
tērnturukka mikalāṉu makilameṅkuñ ciṟakkavuḷḷaṅ
kūrntu mēṟ kūṟiyapaṉ ṉiruvareṉuṅ kuḻuviṉaru
nērntu cila pattarkaḷum vēṇṭunimit tamatāṉu,
mōrntu peruṅ kalviyaṟi voḻukkātā ōroruvaṉ. (5)
aṟucīrkkaḻi neṭilaṭiyāciriya viruttam.
intiri yāṉṟāḷ pūcit tiṉṉaruḷ peṟṟa nīrāṟ
centiru vārū reṉṉat tikaḻtalattutitta celvaṉ
mantirāk karaṅka ḷañcum vikaluñ cinti ceyvōṉ
ṟantira matiyi ṉāṉ cen tamiḻvaḷaṅ koḻikkum vāyaṉ. (6)
civaṉaṭi yārka ṭammi yavaṉeṉap pēṇuñ cīlaṉ
ṟavamaruḷurutti rākka nīṟṟaṇi taḻikku meyyaṉ
kavarpuṟac camiya mēṟkoḷ kaṇṭakark kacaṉi pōlvā
ṉavaṉiyiṟ poṟiyu ḷāṉeṉ ṉaṇiyavark kuyirē yaṉṉāṉ (7)
apparcun tararcam panta raruṭṭirup pāṭa lellān
tuppavir caṭiyāṉ kātiṟ ṟumpuru veṉavē pāṭu
mopparuñ cokka liṅka vō tuvā mūrttiyeṉṉac
ceppapi tāṉam peṟṟa cekappira citta ṉāṉōṉ . (8)
௸ vēṟu.
karutupupal piratikaḷik koṇṭu naṉku
        pārviyiṭṭuk kamaḻun teṉṟaṉ
maruvumalik kuṟumuṉivaṉ kamaṇṭalō
        takapporuni vaḷappan taṉṉāṟ
paruvarali ṉellinakar muttamiḻā karavacciṟ
        patippit tumpa rirucuṭarka ḷuḷḷa maṭṭum
perukupukaḻp payiri vaḷart
        tiṭṭāṉ maṉṉō . (9)
kuruvē tuṇi.

         maturaiyāciriyar pārttuvāci nacciṉārkiṉiyaruraiyum"

   Source
       "pattuppāṭṭu mūlamum maturaiyāciriyar pārttuvāci
       nacciṉārkiṉiyaruraiyum:
       paṭṭiṉappālai"
       ivai makāmakōpāttiyāya tākṣiṇātya kalāniti
       uttamatāṉapuram vē. cāminātaiyarāl
       paricōtitu, palavakai ārāyccik kuṟippukaḷuṭaṉ
       ceṉṉai: kēcari accukkūṭattiṟ patikkap peṟṟaṉa
       mūṉṟām patippu, pirajōtpatti varuṭam, āvaṇi mātam
       1931, vilai rūpā 5. Copyrights reserved.

       mukavurai.
       ceṉṉai yūṉivarsiṭiyārāl 1906 -m varuṭa pi.ē.paṭṭapparīkṣaikkup
       pāṭamāka niyamikkappaṭṭa paṭṭiṉappālaiyaiyum, atai aṟintu
       koḷḷutaṟku iṉṟiyamaiyāta maturai āciriyar
       nacciṉārkkiṉiyaruraiyaiyum veḷippaṭuttat toṭaṅki, māṇākkarkaḷukku
       avai viḷaṅkum vaṇṇam nūtaṉamāka eḷiyanaṭaiyil eḻutiya kuṟippukkaḷai
       uriyaviṭaṅkaḷiṟ cērttup patippikkalāṉēṉ.
       paṭṭiṉappālaiyeṉpatu kaṭaiccaṅkappulavarkaḷāṉa nakkīraṉār
       mutaliyōriyaṟṟiya pattuppāṭṭiṉuḷ oṉpatāvatu; kaṭiyalūr
       uruttiraṅkaṇṇaṉārāl iyaṟṟap peṟṟatu. vēṟṟunāṭṭukkuccellattoṭaṅkiya
       talaivaṉ, taṉatu neñcainōkki, 'neñcē, kāvirippūmpaṭṭiṉattaip
       peṟṟālum yāṉ talaiviyaippirintu niṉṉuṭaṉvārēṉ' eṉṟu kūṟutal
       mukamākak kāvirippūmpaṭṭiṉattiṉ vaḷattaiyum cōḻaṉ
       karikāṟperuvaḷattāṉatu vīracceyalkaḷaiyum avaṉatu ceṅkōlaiyum
       pēraḻakupayappac ciṟappittup pārāṭṭik kūṟappeṟṟatu.
       itaṉmutalil uḷḷa 218. aṭikaḷāṟ kāvirippūmpaṭṭiṉattiṉ vaḷamum, 220
       -vatu mutaliya 79 -aṭikaḷāṟ cōḻaṉkarikāṟ peruvaḷattāṉuṭaiya
       perumaiyum, 300 -m aṭiyāl, cellakkarutiya kāṭukaḷ avaṉuṭaiya
       vēliṉum vemmaiyai -yuṭaiyaṉaveṉpatum, 301 -m aṭiyāl talaiviyiṉ
       tōḷkaḷ avaṉuṭaiya ceṅkōliṉum taṇmaiyuṭaiyaṉavātalāl, ivaḷ
       varutaṟkuriyaṉallaṉeṉpatum, 219, 220 -m aṭikaḷāl, neñcē, ivaḷ iṅkē
       taṉittiruppa yāṉ niṉṉoṭu varutalaic ceyyēṉeṉpatum kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
       āciriyar nacciṉārkkiṉiyar, pataṅkaḷai anvayittu ippāṭṭiṟkup
       poruḷeḻutiyatu, tolkāppiyam, poruḷatikāram, ceyyuḷiyal 211 -m
       cūttiramākiya māṭṭilakkaṇattai mēṟkōḷākakkoṇṭeṉṟu uṇarka. maṟṟa
       vicēṭaṅkaḷ, aṅkaṅkuḷḷa kuṟippuraiyāl viḷaṅkum.
       innūl, palaruraikaḷil mēṟkōḷāka eṭuttukkāṭṭappaṭṭuḷḷatu;
       cīvakacintāmaṇi mutaliya palanūlāciriyarkaḷ itiluḷḷavākkiyaṅkaḷait
       tāmiyaṟṟum nūlkaḷuḷ āṅkāṅku amaittu aḻakupaṭuttiyirukkiṉṟaṉar;
       "muṉṉōr moḻiporuḷē yaṉṟi yavarmoḻiyum, poṉṉēpōṟ pōṟṟuvam" eṉpatu
       vitiyaṉṟō; itaṉai āṇṭāṇṭuḷḷa kuṟippuraikaḷ oruvāṟu viḷakkum.
       itiṟ paṇṭaikkālacceytikaḷ pala kāṇappaṭum. itiluḷḷa viṣayam
       ovvoṉṟum yāvarum eppoḻutum aṉupavittukkoṇṭirukkum aṉupavamāka
       uḷḷatātaliṉ, innūl paṭippavarkaḷukku mikka iṉpattai
       viḷaivikkumeṉpatu kūṟāmalē viḷaṅkum; ceyaṟkaiyaḻakilum
       iyaṟkaiyaḻaku ciṟantateṉpatu ellārukkum terintaviṣayamē.
       viḷaṅka eḻutavēṇṭiyavaikaḷaip palakāraṇaṅkaḷāl nāṉ eḻutātiruttal
       kūṭum; kalviyaṟivu oḻukkaṅkaḷiṟciṟantu kalācālaikaḷil tamiḻp
       paṇṭitarkaḷāka vīṟṟirukkum vivēkikaḷ akkuṟṟattaip poṟukkum vaṇṇam
       nirampak kēṭṭukkoḷḷukiṟēṉ.
       iṅṅaṉam,
       vē.cāminātaṉ.

       nūl
       - - - - - - -
       nakkīraṉārmutaliya caṅkappulavarkaḷiyaṟṟiya pattuppāṭṭukkaḷ iṉṉavai
       -yeṉpataip piṉṉuḷḷa veṇpāvāluṇarka: -
       "muruku porunāṟu pāṇiraṇṭu mullai
       peruku vaḷamaturaik kāñci - -maruviṉiya
       kōlaneṭu nalvāṭai kōlkuṟiñci paṭṭiṉap
       pālai kaṭāttoṭum pattu."
       avaṟṟuḷ: -
       1. -tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai: - 317 -aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; murukak
       kaṭavuḷmītu maturaik kaṇakkāyaṉār makaṉār nakkīraṉār pāṭiyatu; itil
       murukak kaṭavuḷuṭaiya stalaṅkaḷuṭciṟanta tirupparaṅkuṉṟam,
       tiruccīralaivāy (tiruccentūr), tiruvāviṉaṉkuṭi(paḻaṉi),
       tiruvērakam, kuṉṟutōṟāṭal, paḻamutircōlai yeṉṉum āṟupaṭaivīṭukaḷum,
       avaṟṟil avar eḻuntaruḷiyirukkum vitamum muṟaiyē pārāṭṭik
       kūṟappaṭum.
       2. -porunarāṟṟuppaṭai: - 248 - aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; iḷañcēṭceṉṉi
       putalvaṉākiya cōḻaṉkarikāṟperuvaḷavaṉai muṭattāmak kaṇṇiyār
       pāṭiyatu; itil karikāṟperuvaḷaṉ koṭaiyum avaṉ vīramum avaṉāṇṭa
       cōḻanāṭṭiṉ vaḷamum kāvirinatiyiṉ ciṟappum naṉṟākak kūṟappaṭum.
       3. -ciṟupāṇāṟṟuppaṭai: - - 269 -aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; ēṟumānāṭṭu
       nalliyakkōṭa ṉeṉpavaṉai iṭaikkaḻināṭṭu nallūr nattattaṉār pāṭiyatu;
       itil nalliyakkōṭaṉatu vaṇmaiyum avaṉuṭaiya nakaraṅkaḷākiya
       eyiṟpaṭṭiṉam vēlūr āmūr mutaliyavaṟṟi ṉiyalpum, avaṟṟilirunta
       māntarkaḷatu naṟkuṇa naṟceykaikaḷum tamiḻnāṭṭu mūvēntarkaḷuṭaiya
       irājatāṉikaḷākiya maturai vañci uṟaiyū reṉṉu mūṉṟunakaraṅkaḷi
       ṉilaimaiyum, pēkaṉ pāri kāri āy atikaṉ naḷḷi ōriyeṉṉum
       vaḷḷalkaḷeḻuvarum iṉṉaiṉṉa koṭaiyāṟ peyar peṟṟārka ḷeṉpatum
       kūṟappaṭum.
       4. -perumpāṇāṟṟuppaṭai: -500 - aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; kāñci
       nakarattirunta toṇṭaimāṉ iḷantiraiyaṉaik kaṭiyalūr uruttiraṅ
       kaṇṇaṉār pāṭiyatu; itil avaṉatu vaṇmaiyum, toṇṭaināṭṭilēyuḷḷa
       kuṟiñcimutaliya aintiṇaivaḷaṅkaḷum, avaṟṟilvāḻum māntaruṭaiya toḻil
       ūṇ oppuravu mutaliyaṉavum, kāñcinakarattiṉ ciṟappum, tirumāliṉ
       nūṟṟeṭṭuttiruppatikaḷuḷ oṉṟākiya tiruveḵkāveṉpatum kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
       5. -mullaip pāṭṭu: - itu 103 - aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; pakaimēṟ ceṉṟa
       talaivaṉ varumaḷavum talaivi āṟṟiyiruntaviṭattu avaṉ
       vantataṉaikkaṇṭu tōḻi mutaliyōrākiya vāyilkaḷ tammuṭkūṟiyatākak
       kāvirippūmpaṭṭiṉattup poṉvāṇikaṉār makaṉār nappūtaṉār pāṭiyatu; itu
       talaivaṉaippirintu taṉittirukkum talaiviyiṉ iyalpaiyum kārkālattiṉ
       taṉmaiyaiyum paṭaivīṭṭiṟ pakaivar tuyarattōṭiruttalaiyum
       talaivaṉatu cauriyattaiyum viḷaṅkakkūṟum.
       6. -maturaikkāñci: -782 aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; talaiyālaṅkāṉattuc
       ceruveṉṟa pāṇṭiyaṉ neṭuñceḻiyaṉukku vīṭupēṟu nimittam
       nilaiyāmaiyaic ceviyaṟivuṟutti māṅkuṭimarutaṉār pāṭiyatu; itil
       pāṇṭi nāṭṭiṉ aintiṇaivaḷaṅkaḷum, maturaiyampatiyiṉaḻakum,
       appāṇṭiyaṉatu vīramutaliyaṉavum, cērarājaṉatu *nāḷōlakkac ciṟappum,
       akkālattirunta palaciṟṟaracarkaḷuṭaiya perumaiyum, aruccuṉaṉukkuk
       kaṇṇaṉ upatēcitta pakavatkītaiyiṉ mēmpāṭum iṉṉum palavum
       kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
       7. -neṭunalvāṭai: -188 - aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; pāṇṭiyaṉ neṭuñceḻiyaṉai
       nakkīraṉār pāṭiyatu; itil, aippaci kārttikai mātaṅkaḷākiya kūtir
       kālattiṉiyalpum taṉittirunta talaiviyatu varuttamikutiyum paṭai
       vīṭṭil talaivaṉirukkum vaṇṇamum viḷaṅkak kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
       8. -kuṟiñcippāṭṭu: - 261 aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; āriyavaracaṉ
       pirakattaṉait tamiḻaṟivuṟuttaṟkuk kapilar pāṭiyatu;itil
       malaivḷaṅkaḷum, illaṟamuṟaiyum, talaivaṉum talaiviyum tammuḷvaikkat
       tarumaṉpuṭaimaiyum, kaṟpiṉ iṉṟiyamaiyāmaiyum, pala
       malarvicēṭaṅkaḷum aḻakākak kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. itu peruṅkuṟiñciyeṉṟum
       peyarpeṟum.
       9. -paṭṭiṉappālai: -301 - aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; innūl, vañcippā
       eṉpatum perumpālum āciriyavaṭikaḷum ciṟupāṉmai veḷḷaṭiyum
       kaliyaṭiyum itilvantuḷḷaṉa eṉpatum orucār
       paḻaiyavuraiyāciriyarkaḷuṭaiya koḷkai; itaṉai,
       piṉvaruvaṉavaṟṟāluṇarka. 'paṭṭiṉappālaiyeṉṉum vañcineṭumpāṭṭiṉuḷ,
       (22) "nēriḻai makaḷi ruṇaṅkuṇākkavarum" eṉṟittoṭakkattaṉa
       āciriyavaṭi; (64 -65) "vayalāmaip puḻukkuṇṭum, vaṟaḷaṭampiṉ
       malarmalaintum" eṉpatu kaliyaṭi; itaṉai, "vayalāmaip puḻukkuṇṭum,
       vaṟaḷaṭampiṉ malarmalaintum, kayaṉāṭṭak kaṭaiciyartaṅ kātalartōḷ
       kalantaṉarē" eṉa uccarittuk kaliyaṭiyāmāṟu kaṇṭukoḷka. (23) "kōḻi
       yeṟinta koṭuṅkāṟ kaṉaṅkuḻai" eṉpatu veḷḷaṭi; itaṉai, "kōḻi yeṟinta
       koṭuṅkāṟ kaṉaṅkuḻai,yāḻi cūḻ vaiyak kaṇi" eṉa uccarittu,
       'veḷḷaṭiyāmāṟu kaṇṭukoḷka' eṉpatu, yāpparuṅkalam, aṭiyōttu,*9 -m
       cūttiraviruttiyurai; itaṉai, yāpparuṅkalakkārikai, oḻipiyal, 4 -m
       kalittuṟaiyiṉ vicēṭavuraiyālum uṇarka; itaṉ maṟṟavaralāṟṟai
       mukavuraiyiṟ kāṇka.
       10. -malaipaṭukaṭām: 583 - aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiyatu; palkuṉṟak kōṭṭattuc
       *ceṅkaṇmāttuvēḷ naṉṉaṉcēy naṉṉaṉai iraṇiyamuṭṭattup peruṅkuṉṟūrp
       peruṅkeḷacikaṉār pāṭiyatu; itil avaṉūrkkuc cellum vaḻiyiṉiyalpum,
       avvavviṭattukkiṭaikkum uṇaviṉ vakaikaḷum avaṉatu malai cōlai
       kāṭukaḷiṉ vaḷaṅkaḷum, avaṉatu vaḷḷaṉmaiyum, āṟṟalum, avaṉatu
       cuṟṟattiṉoḻukkaṅkaḷum, avaṉatu nāḷōlakkac ciṟappum, avaṉatu navira
       meṉṉum malaiyil kāriyuṇṭikkaṭavu ḷeṉṉum tirunāmattōṭu
       eḻuntaruḷiyirukkum civaperumāṉatu makimaiyum, avaṉuṭaiya
       muṉṉōrkaḷiṉ perumaiyum, avaṉūriṉiyalpum, avaṉatu cēyāṟeṉṉum
       āṟumutaliyavaṟṟiṉ vaḷaṅkaḷum viḷaṅkak kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. itu
       kūttarāṟṟuppaṭai yeṉavum peyarpeṟum.
       ____________
       nūlāciriyar.
       in nūlāciriyarākiya kaṭiyalūr uruttiraṅkaṇṇaṉāreṉpavar, kaṭaic
       caṅkappulavarkaḷuḷ oruvar. ivaruṭaiya ūrākiya kaṭiyalūreṉpatu iṉṉa
       nāṭṭiluḷḷateṉṟu teriyavillai.
       ivaruṭaiya tantaiyār: aiyamtīrtaṟporuṭṭut tantaiyiṉ peyaraip
       putalvaṉuṭaiyapeyarkku vicēṭaṇamākkivaḻaṅkutal marapāka
       iruntalālum, uruttiraṅkaṇṇaṉāreṉṉum peyarttokaiyai
       uruttiraṉāruṭaiya putalvarākiya kaṇṇaṉāreṉa virittaṟku
       iṭamuṇṭātalālum ‘uruttiraṉār’ eṉpavar, ivaruṭaiya tantaiyārākak
       karutappaṭukiṟār; uruttiraṉāreṉṉum peyaroṉṟu, kuṟuntokaiyil,
       “puṟavuppuṟattaṉṉa” eṉṉum 274 -m ceyyuḷiyaṟṟiyavar peyarākak
       kāṇappaṭukiṉṟatu.
       ivar cāti: tolkāppiyam, marapiyalil, “ūrum peyarum” eṉṉum 74 - m
       cūttirattiṉ vicēṭavuraiyil, ‘uṟaiyūr ēṇiccērimuṭamōci, peruṅkuṉṟūrp
       peruṅkaucikaṉ, kaṭiyalūruruttiraṅkaṇṇaṉ eṉpaṉa antaṇarkkuriyaṉa’
       eṉpatu varaiyappeṟṟiruttalāl, ivar antaṇarākak karutappaṭukiṟār.
       ivaratu camayam: perumpāṇāṟṟuppaṭaiyil, [aṭikaḷ, 372 -373]
       “kāntaṉañ cilampiṟ kaḷiṟupaṭin tāṅkup, pāmpaṇaip paḷḷi
       yamarntōṉāṅkaṇ” eṉak kāñcinakarattuḷḷa viṣṇustalaṅkaḷuḷ oṉṟākiya
       tiruveḵkāveṉṉun tiruppatiyaiyum atiṟ paḷḷikoṇṭaruḷiya tirumālaiyum
       pārāṭṭik kūṟiyiruttaliṉ, ivar vaiṣṇavacamayattil atikap
       paṟṟuṭaiyavareṉṟu colluvataṟku iṭamuṇṭu.
       ivariyaṟṟiyavai: pattuppāṭṭiṉuḷ, 4 -vatākiya perumpāṇāṟṟuppaṭaiyum,
       9 -vatākiya innūlum, eṭṭuttokaiyuṭ kuṟuntokaik kaṇṇatākiya,
       "neṭunīrāmpal" eṉṉum 352 -m ceyyuḷum, akanāṉūṟṟiṉ kaṇṇatākiya,
       "vayaṅkumaṇiporuta" eṉṉum 166 -m ceyyuḷumākiya ivaikaḷ ivarāl
       iyaṟṟappeṟṟavaiyeṉṟu ippoḻutu terikiṉṟatu.
       ivarai ātarittapirapukkaḷ: perumpāṇāṟṟuppaṭait talaivaṉākiya
       toṇṭaimāṉ iḷantiraiyaṉum, innūṟṟalaivaṉākiya cōḻaṉ karikāṟ
       peruvaḷattāṉum ivarai ātarittatalaivarkaḷ.
       ivar peṟṟa paricil: cōḻaṉ karikāṟperuvaḷattāṉ innūlaik
       kēṭṭumakiḻntu patiṉāṟunūṟāyirampoṉ ivarkkup paricaḷittāṉeṉṟu
       terikiṉṟatu. itaṉai, kaliṅkattupparaṇi, irācapārampariyam, 22:
       "taḻuvu centamiḻp paricil vāṇarpoṉ, patto ṭāṟunū ṟāyirampeṟap,
       paṇṭu paṭṭiṉap pālai koṇṭatum" eṉpataṉāluṇarka.
       ivaratu kālam: kaṭaiccaṅkappulavarkaḷuṭaiyakālam, iṟṟaikku āyirat
       teṇṇūṟu varuṣaṅkaḷukku muṟpaṭṭateṉṟu iṅkilīṣpāṣaiyilum
       tamiḻppāṣaiyilum payiṟciyuḷḷa palapulavarkaḷ nuṇuki ārāyntu takka
       ātāraṅkaḷkāṭṭiyeḻuti mēṉmēlum veḷippaṭuttivarutal ikkālattil
       yāvarukkum terintatātaliṉ, kaṭaiccaṅkappulavarkaḷuḷ oruvarākiya
       ivarkālamum atuvēyeṉpatu kūṟāmalēviḷaṅkum.
       _______________
       innūl uraiyāciriyarākiya nacciṉārkkiṉiyar varalāṟu.
       viruttam.

   evaṉāla vāyiṭaivan tamutavā yuṭaiyaṉeṉa viyampap peṟṟōṉ
       evaṉpaṇṭaip paṉuvalpala viṟavātu nilavavurai yeḻuti yīntōṉ
       evaṉparama vupakāri yevaṉacci ṉārkkiṉiya ṉeṉumpē rāḷaṉ

   kūṟuvar; itaṉai, "cuṭappaṭ ṭuyiruynta cōḻaṉ makaṉum, piṭarttalaip
   pērāṉaip peṟṟuk - kaṭaikkāṟ, ceyiraṟu ceṅkōl celīiyiṉā ṉillai,
   yuyiruṭaiyāreytā viṉai" (105) eṉṉum paḻamoḻi veṇpāvālum, 'iḷamaip
   paruvattup piṟarāṟcuṭappaṭṭu uyiruyntupōkiya karikālaṉum
   irumpiṭarttalaiyāreṉṉum peyaruṭaiya taṉmāmaṉait taṉakkut tuṇaiyākap
   pakaṟutālāl, piṉṉorukālattiṉkaṇ taṉṉurimaiyaracupeṟṟuk kuṟṟamaṟṟa
   ceṅkōlai naṭāttiṉāṉ; ātalāl, uyiruṭaiyār eytāttorunal viṉaip
   payaṉillai' eṉṟum ataṉuraiyālum uṇarka.

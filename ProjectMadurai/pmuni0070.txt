caṅka kāla nūlkaḷāṉa pattup pāṭṭukkaḷil
ēḻāvatāṉa "neṭunalvāṭi"

pāṭiyavar :: maturik kaṇakkāyaṉār makaṉār nakkīrar
tiṇi :: vāki
tuṟi :: kūtirppācaṟi
pāvaki :: āciriyappā
motta aṭikaḷ :: 188
-----------------------------
viyakam paṉippa, valaṉērpu vaḷiip
poyyā vāṉam putuppeyal poḻinteṉa
ārkali muṉiiya koṭuṅkōṟ kōvalar
ēṟuṭi iṉaniri vēṟupulam parappip
pulampeyar pulampoṭu kalaṅkik kōṭal
nīṭitaḻk kaṇṇi nīralik kalāva
meykkoḷ perumpaṉi naliyap palaruṭaṉ
kikkoḷ koḷḷiyar kavuḷpuṭiyūu naṭuṅka
māmēyal maṟappa manti kūrap
paṟavi paṭivaṉa vīḻak kaṟavi       10
kaṉṟukō ḷaḻiyak kaṭiya vīcik
kuṉṟukuḷirp paṉṉa kūtirp pāṉāḷ
puṉkoṭi mucuṇṭip poṟippuṟa vāṉpūp
poṉpōṟ pīramoṭu putaṟputal malarap
piṅkāṟ kokkiṉ meṉpaṟit toḻuti
iruṅkaḷi paranta īra veṇmaṇaṟ
cevvari nāriya ṭevvāyuṅ kavarak
kayalaṟal etirak kaṭumpuṉaṟ cāayp
peyalulan teḻunta poṅkal veṇmaḻi
akaliru vicumpil tuvali kaṟpa       20
aṅkaṇ akalvayal ārpeyaṟ kalitta
vaṇtōṭṭu nelliṉ varukatir vaṇaṅka
muḻumutaṟ kamukiṉ maṇiyuṟaḻ eruttiṟ
koḻumaṭal aviḻnta kurūukkoḷ peruṅkuli
nuṇṇīr teviḷa vīṅkip puṭitiraṇṭu
teṇṇīrp pacuṅkāy cēṟukoḷa muṟṟa
naḷikoḷ cimiya viravumalar viyaṉkāk
kuḷirkoḷ ciṉiya kurūuttuḷi tūṅka
māṭa mōṅkiya mallaṉ mūtūr
āṟukiṭan taṉṉa akaṉeṭun teruviṟ       30
paṭalik kaṇṇip parēreṟuḻt tiṇitōḷ
muṭali yākki muḻuvali mākkaḷ
vaṇṭumūcu tēṟal mānti makiḻciṟantu
tuvalit taṇtuḷi pēṇār pakaliṟantu
irukōṭṭa ṭaṟuviyar vēṇṭuvayiṉ tiritara
veḷḷi vaḷḷi vīṅkiṟip paṇittōḷ
metteṉ cāyal muttuṟaḻ muṟuval
pūṅkuḻik kamarnta ēnteḻil maḻikkaṇ
maṭavaral makaḷir piṭakip peyta
cevvi yarumpiṉ piṅkāṟ pittikattu       40
avvita ḻaviḻpataṅ kamaḻap poḻutaṟintu
irumpucey viḷakkiṉ īrntirik koḷīi
nellu malarun tūuyk kitoḻutu
mallal āvaṇam māli yayara
maṉiyuṟi puṟaviṉ ceṅkāṟ cēval
iṉpuṟu peṭiyaṭu maṉṟutērn tuṇṇātu
iravum pakalum mayaṅkik kiyaṟṟu
matalip paḷḷi māṟuvaṉa iruppak
kaṭiyuṭi viyaṉakarc ciṟukuṟun toḻuvar
koḷḷuṟaḻ naṟuṅkal palakūṭṭu maṟuka       50
vaṭavar tanta vāṉkēḻ vaṭṭam
teṉpula maruṅkiṟ cāntoṭu tuṟappak
kūntal makaḷir kōti puṉiyār
palliruṅ kūntal ciṉmalar peymmār
taṇṇaṟun takara muḷari neruppamittu
iruṅkāḻ akiloṭu veḷḷayir pukippak
kival kammiyaṉ kaviṉpeṟap puṉinta
ceṅkēḻ vaṭṭañ curukkik koṭuntaṟic
cilampi vāṉūl valantaṉa tūṅka
vāṉuṟa nivanta mēṉili maruṅkiṉ       60
vēṉiṟ paḷḷit teṉvaḷi tarūum
nērvāyk kaṭṭaḷi tiriyātu tiṇṇilip
pōrvāyk katavam tāḻoṭu tuṟappak
kalleṉ tuvali tūvaliṉ yāvarum
tokuvāyk kaṉṉal taṇṇīr uṇṇār
pakuvāyt taṭavil cennerup pāra
āṭal makaḷir pāṭalkoḷap puṇarmār
taṇmiyiṟ ṟirinta iṉkural tīntoṭi
kommi varumuli vemmiyiṟ ṟaṭiik
karuṅkōṭṭuc cīṟiyāḻ paṇṇumuṟi niṟuppak       70
kātalarp pirintōr pulampap peyalkaṉintu
kūtirniṉ ṟaṉṟāṟ pōtē mātiram
virikatir parappiya viyalvāy maṇṭilam
irukōṟ kuṟinili vaḻukkātu kuṭakkēr
porutiṟañ cārā arināḷ amayattu
nūlaṟi pulavar nuṇṇitiṟ kayiṟiṭṭut
tēeṅ koṇṭu teyvam nōkkip
perumpeyar maṉṉark koppa maṉivakuttu
oruṅkuṭaṉ vaḷii ōṅkunili varippiṟ
paruvirumpu piṇittuc celvarak kurīit       80
tuṇimāṇ katavam porutti iṇimāṇṭu
nāḷaṭu peyariya kōḷami viḻumarattup
pōtaviḻ kuvaḷip putuppiṭi kālamittut
tāḷaṭu kuyiṉṟa pōrami puṇarppiṟ
kival kammiyaṉ muṭukkaliṟ puritīrntu
aiyavi yappiya neyyaṇi neṭunili
veṉṟeḻu koṭiyōṭu vēḻañ ceṉṟupukak
kuṉṟukuyiṉ ṟaṉṉa ōṅkunili vāyil
tirunili peṟṟa tītutīr ciṟappiṉ
tarumaṇal ñemiriya tirunakar muṟṟattu       90
neṭumayi rekiṉat tūniṟa ēṟṟi
kuṟuṅkāl aṉṉamō ṭukaḷu muṉkaṭip
paṇinili muṉiiya palluḷip puravi
pulluṇāt teviṭṭum pulampuviṭu kuraloṭu
nilavuppayaṉ koḷḷum neṭuveṇ muṟṟattuk
kimpurip pakuvāy ampaṇa niṟiyak
kaluḻntuvīḻ aruvip pāṭiṟan tayala
olineṭum pīli olka melliyal
kalimayil akavum vayirmaruḷ iṉṉici
naḷimalic cilampiṟ cilampuṅ kōyil       100
yavaṉar iyaṟṟiya viṉimāṇ pāvi
kiyēn tiyakal niṟiyaney corintu
parūuttiri koḷīiya kurūuttali nimireri
aṟuaṟu kālitō ṟamivarap paṇṇip
palvēṟu paḷḷitoṟum pāyiruḷ nīṅkap
pīṭukeḻu ciṟappiṟ peruntaki yallatu
āṭavar kuṟukā aruṅkaṭi varippiṉ
varikaṇ ṭaṉṉa tōṉṟala varicērpu
vilkiṭan taṉṉa koṭiya palvayiṉ
veḷḷi yaṉṉa viḷaṅkuñ katiyurīi       110
maṇikaṇ ṭaṉṉa māttiraḷ tiṇkāḻc
cempiyaṉ ṟaṉṉa ceyvuṟu neṭuñcuvar
uruvap palpū orukoṭi vaḷiik
karuvoṭu peyariya kāṇpi ṉallil
tacanāṉ keytiya paṇimaruḷ nōṉṟāḷ
ikaṉmīk kūṟum ēnteḻil varinutal
porutoḻi nāka moḻiyeyi ṟarukeṟintu
cīruñ cemmiyum oppa vallōṉ
kūruḷik kuyiṉṟa īrili yiṭiyiṭupu
tūṅkiyal makaḷir vīṅkumuli kaṭuppap       120
puṭitiraṇ ṭirunta kuṭatta iṭitiraṇṭu
uḷḷi nōṉmutal porutti aṭiyamittup
pēraḷa veytiya perumpeyarp pāṇṭil
maṭimāṇ nuṇṇiḻi poliyat toṭimāṇṭu
muttuṭic cālēkam nāṟṟik kuttuṟuttup
pulippoṟik koṇṭa pūṅkēḻt taṭṭattut
takaṭukaṇ putiyak koḷīit tukaḷtīrntu
ūṭṭuṟu paṉmayir virii vayamāṉ
vēṭṭam poṟittu viyaṉkaṭ kāṉattu
mullip palpō tuṟaḻap pūnirittu       130
mellitiṉ virinta cēkki mēmpaṭat
tuṇipuṇar aṉṉat tūniṟat tūvi
iṇiyaṇi mēmpaṭap pāyaṇi yiṭṭuk
kāṭi koṇṭa kaḻuvuṟu kaliṅkattut
tōṭami tūmaṭi viritta cēkki
āran tāṅkiya alarmuli yākattup
piṉṉami neṭuvīḻ tāḻat tuṇituṟantu
naṉṉutal ulaṟiya ciṉmel lōti
neṭunīr vārkuḻi kaḷinteṉak kuṟuṅkaṇ
vāyuṟi yaḻuttiya vaṟituvīḻ kātiṟ       140
polantoṭi tiṉṟa mayirvār muṉki
valampuri vaḷiyaṭu kaṭikinūl yāttu
vāḷip pakuvāy kaṭuppa vaṇakkuṟuttuc
cevviraṟ koḷīiya ceṅkēḻ viḷakkattup
pūntukil marīiya ēntukōṭ ṭalkul
ammā cūrnta avirnūṟ kaliṅkamoṭu
puṉiyā ōviyaṅ kaṭuppap puṉivil
taḷirēr mēṉit tāya cuṇaṅkiṉ
ampaṇit taṭiiya meṉṟōḷ mukiḻmuli
vampuvicit tiyātta vāṅkucāy nucuppiṉ       150
melliyal makaḷir nallaṭi varuṭa
narivirā vuṟṟa naṟumeṉ kūntal
cemmukac ceviliyar kimmikak kuḻīik
kuṟiyavum neṭiyavum uripala payiṟṟi
iṉṉē varukuvar iṉtuṇi yōreṉa
ukattavi moḻiyavum ollāḷ mikakkaluḻntu
nuṇcēṟu vaḻitta nōṉilit tiraḷkāl
ūṟā vaṟumuli koḷīiya kāṟiruttip
putuva tiyaṉṟa meḻukucey paṭamicit
tiṇṇili maruppiṉ āṭutali yāka       160
viṇṇūrpu tiritarum vīṅkucelal maṇṭilattu
muraṇmiku ciṟappiṟ celvaṉoṭu niliiya
urōkiṇi niṉivaṉaḷ nōkki neṭituyirā
māyitaḻ ēntiya malintuvīḻ arippaṉi
cevviral kaṭikkaṇ cērttic cilateṟiyāp
pulampoṭu vatiyu nalaṅkiḷar arivikku
iṉṉā arumpaṭar tīra viṟaṟantu
iṉṉē muṭikatil amma miṉṉavir
ōṭiyaṭu polinta viṉinavil yāṉi
nīḷtiraḷ taṭakki nilamicip puraḷak       170
kaḷiṟukaḷam paṭutta peruñcey yāṭavar
oḷiṟuvāḷ viḻuppuṇ kāṇiya puṟampōntu
vaṭantit taṇvaḷi eṟitoṟum nuṭaṅkit
teṟkēr piṟiñciya taliya naṟpal
pāṇṭil viḷakkiṟ parūuccuṭa raḻala
vēmputali yātta nōṉkāḻ e·kamoṭu
muṉṉōṉ muṟimuṟi kāṭṭap piṉṉar
maṇipuṟat tiṭṭa māttāṭ piṭiyaṭu
parumaṅ kaḷiyāp pāyparik kalimā
iruñcēṟṟut teruviṉ eṟituḷi vitirppap . .180
puṭivīḻ antukil iṭavayiṉ taḻīi
vāḷtōḷ kōtta vaṉkaṭ kāḷi
cuvalmici yamitta kiyaṉ mukaṉamarntu
nūlkāl yātta māli veṇkuṭi
tavveṉ ṟaciit tātuḷi maṟippa
naḷḷeṉ yāmattum paḷḷi koḷḷāṉ
cilaroṭu tiritarum vēntaṉ
palaroṭu muraṇiya pācaṟit toḻilē. . . .188
neṭunalvāṭi muṟṟiṟṟu.

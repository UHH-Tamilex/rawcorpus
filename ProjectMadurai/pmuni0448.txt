       PREFACE.
       This poem has been based on the story of "Chameleon." Such
       alterations and additions as were considered suitable for the
       teatment of the subject in Tamil poetry have been freely made.
       A few notes have been added to enable the work to be understood by
       persons who have not got sufficient acquaintance with Tamil
       literature to understand works of the kind without help. The notes
       will also help purely Tamil Scholars in fully comprehending the
       English ideas embodied in the work. The substance of each stanza
       (not a verbatim paraphrase) has been given in the notes. Difficult
       words and phrases and allusions to English stories and ideas have
       also been fully explained. The notes are believed to be sufficient
       and not too many.
       If this work meets with the same appreciation and encouragement
       from the scholars and the Press as my poetrical Transalation of
       Paradise Lost, Book I, in Tamil, has done, I shall consider that my
       labour has not been thrown away.
       V.P.S.

       kōmpiviruttam : mukavurai.
       muṉkālattil, cīvakacintāmaṇi āciriyar, nariyai viṣayamākakkoṇṭu
       nariviruttameṉṟu oru nūl iyaṟṟiyatāka anēkar kēṭṭirukkalām.
       atupōla, itu, kōmpiyaip paṟṟi kūṟuvataṉāl, kōmpiviruttameṉṟu
       peyariṭap peṟṟatu. kōmpi iṉṉateṉṟu nūlāl viḷaṅkum.
       itu, iṅlīṣil uḷḷa *'Chameleon' oru pāṭaliṉ katācārattai maṭṭum
       kaikkoṇṭu ataṉaip pala varuṇaṉaikaḷ upakataikaḷ mutaliyavaṟṟāl mika
       virivākki "epporuḷ yārayār vāyk kēṭpiṉum apporuḷ - meypporuḷ
       kāṇpataṟivu" eṉṟa nītikku iṭamāka iyaṟṟappaṭṭirukkiṉṟatu.
       kaṟṟōrēyaṉṟi maṟṟōrum innūṟporuḷai eḷitāka aṟintukoḷḷum poruṭṭu,
       ilakuvāṉatōr urai eḻutappaṭṭirukkiṉṟatu. inta uraiyil, pāṭṭukkaḷiṉ
       mukkiyamāṉa karuttu viḷaṅkutaṟku aṉāvaciyakamāṉa pataṅkaḷiṉ
       poruḷkaḷ, curukkattaiyum teḷivaiyum uttēcittu, cērkkappaṭavillai;
       poruḷ naṉku pulappaṭuvataṟku iṉṟiyamaiyāta iṭaippiṟavaralkaḷ
       vēṇṭumiṭattu nirappap paṭṭirukkiṉṟaṉa. pāṭṭukkaḷiṉ muḻupporuḷaiyum
       terintu koḷḷa virumpuvōrkku utaviyāka arumpata viḷakkamum
       eḻutappaṭṭuḷḷatu.
       oru pāṭṭukkum aṭutta pāṭṭukkum uḷḷa poruṭ campantattāl oṉṟaiyoṉṟu
       iyalpākat toṭarntu varutal (innūlil eṭuttāḷappaṭṭuḷḷa) iṅkilīṣ
       karuttukaḷ kataikaḷ ākiya ivaikaḷ mutaliyavaṟṟait terivikkum
       vicēṣak kuṟippum cērkkappaṭṭirukkiṉṟatu. ivvicēṣak kuṟippil
       aṭaṅkātaṉavum uḷa. avai virivañci viṭukkappaṭṭaṉa.
       pāṭṭorupakkam uraiyorupakkamāka iruntāl, iraṇṭaiyum ottuppārttup
       poruḷ terintukoḷḷal ciṟitu pirayācamāka virukkumeṉṟu, avvap pāṭṭiṉ
       kīḻē ataṉataṉ uraiyum arumpataviḷakkamum vicēṣakkuṟippum cērkkap
       paṭṭirukkiṉṟaṉa.
       ve. pa. cu.

                              kōmpi viruttam.

   marutamā tiyanilam vāḻu māṉiṭar
       veruvaruṅ kuḷiriṉāṉ meyn naṭukkuṟīit
       terumaru paruvattuñ cērnta māntaraic
       curameṉa vetuppiṭuñ curamoṉ ṟuṇṭarō. (1)

   (itaṉ poruḷ.) kuḷirkālam vantapōtu, marutam kuṟiñci mullai neytal eṉṉum
   nālvakai nilattiluḷḷōrum kuḷiriṉāl uṭampu naṭuṅki varuntuvatu iyalpu.
   appaṭippaṭṭa kālattilum taṉṉai yaṭaintavarkaḷaic curanōypōla
   vetuppukiṉṟa pālainilam oṉṟu uṇṭu.
   ātiya - mutalāṉa; veruvarum - añcum; mey naṭukku; uṟīit terumarum -
   uṭampu naṭuṅkutal uṟṟu varuntum. curam eṉa vetuppiṭum curam, taṉṉaic
   curam (curanōy) eṉṟu collumpaṭi veppañceykiṉṟa pālaivaṉa meṉṟumām.
   taṇmaiyē vemmaiyāt taraṅkak kāṉalā
       voṇmaṇi paralkaḷā vuṟa nekiḻcciyē
       tiṇmaiyā mīṉiṉan tīya mākkaḷā
       vaṇmiya kaṭaleṉa vakṉṟa taccuram. (2)

   (i-ḷ.) taṉ (kaṭaliṉ) kuḷircciyē veppamākavum, irattiṉaṅkaḷ parukkaik
   kallukaḷākavum, alaikaḷ kāṉalākavum, nekiḻcciyāṉataṉmai (taraipōla
   iṟukik) kaṭiṉamākavum, mīṉ kūṭṭam cennāy mutaliya mirukaṅkaḷum
   vaḻippaṟiceyvōrum ākiya) koṭiya mākkaḷākavum māṟiya kaṭalpōla
   vicālamāka, antap pālaivaṉam uḷḷatu.
   taṇmai - kuḷircci; vemmai - veppam; taraṅkam - alai; voṇmaṇi -
   pirakācamuḷḷa irattiṉaṅkaḷ; paral - parukkaikkal; tiṇmai -
   iṟukiyataṉmai; mīṉiṉam - mīṉkūṭṭam; mākkaḷ, maṉitarukkum
   mirukaṅkaḷukkum potuppeyar; aṇmiya - aṭutta.
   [kaṭalum pālainilamum maṟutalaip poruḷkaḷāyiṉum parappākiya
   potuttaṉmaipaṟṟi oṉṟōṭoṉṟu uvamikkappaṭṭaṉa. avvuvamaikkiyaiyak
   kuḷircci mutaliyavai avaikaḷukku maṟutalaiyākiya veppam
   mutaliyavaikaḷāka māṟiyatupōlak kūṟiyatu kāṇka.]
   caṅkaraṉ kaṇṇeṉac cāṟṟum pāṉuvait
       tiṅkaḷaip pārttuḷēñ cevva ḻaṟkaṇai
       yeṅkaṇuṅ kaṇṭila meṉ ṟiyampuvārk
       kiṅkuḷa tituveṉa viṟutta taccuram. (3)

   (i-ḷ.) civaṉuṭaiya kaṇkaḷeṉṟu collappaṭṭa cūriya cantiraraip
   pārttirukkiṉṟōm, accivaṉuṭaiya civanta akkiṉik kaṇṇai maṭṭum eṅkum
   pārkkavillai eṉṟu colluvārkku antak kaṇ iṅkē irukkiṉṟateṉṟu collumpaṭi
   (avvaḷavu veppattōṭu kūṭiyatāka) antap pālaivaṉam irāniṉṟatu.
   cāṟṟum - collappaṭukiṉṟa; pāṉu - cūriyaṉ; tiṅkaḷ - cantiraṉ;
   cevvaḻaṟkaṇai - civanta akkiṉik kaṇṇai; eṅkaṇum - eṅkum; kaṇṭilam -
   pārkkavillai; uḷatu - uḷḷatu; iṟuttatu -taṅkiyatu.
   [civanta niṟattiṉālum veppattiṉālum pālaivaṉam, civaṉuṭaiya centaḻaṟ
   kaṇṇai ottteṉṟu kūṟappaṭṭatu. "cenneruppiṉait takaṭu ceytupār
   ceytatokkum ac centaraip parappu..." eṉṟu kaliṅkattup paraṇiyil
   varuvataip pārkka.]
   periyavar kōpattiṟ piṟanta cāpattī
       yarivaiyar kaṟputtī yaṉpar tuṉpuṟap
       puripiri veṉṉuntī poṟāmait tīyivai
       yoruvaḻit tokkeṉ vuḷatap pālaiyē. (4)

   (i-ḷ.) periyōrkaḷ kōpakālattil veḷippaṭṭa cāpattīyum mātarkaḷuṭaiya
   kaṟputtīyum aṉparkaḷāyiṉōr tuṉapam aṭaiyumpaṭi avaraip pirikkiṉṟa
   pirivākiya tīyum poṟāmai eṉṟatīyum ākiya ivaikaḷellām ōr iṭattiṟ kūṭit
   tiraṇṭatu pōlavum antap pālaivaṉam uḷḷatu.
   arivaiyar-stirīkaḷ; tuṉpu uṟa- tuṉpam aṭaiya; puri- ceykiṉṟa;
   oruvaḻittokkeṉa-ōr iṭattilē tiraṇṭāṟ pōla.
   nīrnaṭu vaṭavaiyuṇ ṭeṉa nikaḻttuvā
       rārumē kaṇṭila rataṉaip pōlaṉṟi
       pārnaṭut tīyeṉap pārtta pallavar
       cōrvilā turaipparac cuṭu curattaiyē. (5)

   (i-ḷ) camuttirattiṉ; mattiyilē vaṭavāmukākkiṉi (nīrnaṭuttīyāka)
   irukkiṉṟateṉṟu colvatu uṇṭu. āṉāl anta akkiṉiyaippārttavarkaḷ illai.
   atupōlallāmal mēṟkūṟiya pālaivaṉattaik kaṇṇālē kaṇṭōr anēkar ataṉai
   nilanaṭuttī yeṉṟu colluvārkaḷ.
   nīrnaṭu vaṭavai uṇṭu-kaṭal mattiyilē vaṭavāmukākkiṉi uṇṭu;
   pār-nilam-pūmi; pallavar- palar; cōrvilātu-coṟcōrvu paṭātu;
   uraippar-colluvār.
   mēyavav vaṭavaiyē vēlai nīrelām
       vāymaṭuk kuputaṉai vaḷaiyu nīriṉṟi
       yēyumveṇ ṭērppukai yiyaiveñ centarai
       yāyatō veṉavuṟu maccu rattiṉē. (6)

   (i-ḷ) aṉṟiyum anta vaṭavā mukākkiṉi tāṉē, taṉṉaic cūḻntirunta kaṭal
   muḻuvataiyum uṇṭu oḻittuviṭṭu, ataṉāl taṉṉaic cūḻavum nīrillātatāy,
   kāṉalākiya pukaiyuṭaṉ vemmaiyum cenniṟamum uḷḷa taraip parappāka
   ākiyatō eṉṟu collumpaṭiyākavum antap pālaivaṉam uḷḷatu. atilē
   mēya-poruntiya; vaṭavai-vaṭamukākkiṉi; vēlai- kaṭal;
   vāymaṭukkupu-uṇṭu-kuṭittu; ēyum-poruntiya; veṇṭēr-kāṉalākiya; pukai
   iyai-pukai poruntiya; veñcentarai-veppamum civanta niṟamum uḷḷa tarai;
   āyatō eṉa-ākiyatō eṉṟu collumpaṭi.
   [pālaivaṉam, taṉṉiṭattuḷḷa kāṉalāṉatu pukaiyai ottiruttaliṉālum,
   taṉakku iyalpākavuḷḷa veppam cenniṟam ivaikaḷiṉālum, taṉṉaiccūḻa nīr
   illātataṉālum kaṭalāl vaḷaiyappaṭāta vaṭavā mukākkiṉi pōṉṟatu eṉṟapaṭi]
   tīyatē nilamumāyt tirava nīrumāy
       vāyuvu māyativ vaiya katteṉa
       vēyumep poruḷkaḷum vemmai yeytiṭa
       mēyapaṅ kuṉiyiṉōr veyya nāḷiṉē. (7)

   (i-ḷ) intap pūmiyilē tīyoṉṟutāṉē kaṭiṉamāṉa maṇṇum āki,nekiḻcciyāṉa
   nīrum āki,āvit taṉmaiyuḷḷa vāyuvum ākiyatiṉṟu collumpaṭi
   (kaṭṭipporuḷkaḷ nekiḻccip poruḷkaḷ āvipporuḷkaḷāṉa) ellāpporuḷkaḷum
   cūṭu aṭaiyumpaṭiyākap paṅkuṉimātam vara, anta mātattilum maṟṟa nāḷ
   kaḷaikkāṭṭilum atika veppamāka irunta maru tiṉattilē.
   tiravam-nekiḻcci; āyatu-ākiyatu; vaiyakattu-pūmiyil;
   ēyum-poruntiya; eytiṭa-porunta; mēya-mēviya.
   [paṅkuṉimātam pōlavē ataṟkup pintiya cittirai vaikāci ma, mutaliya
   cilamātaṅkaḷum veppamāṉavaikaḷ. eṉṟālum, "cittirai pattil
   (pattāntētiyil) ciṟanta peruṅkāṟṟu aippaci pattil aṟaiyil aṭaipaṭum"
   eṉṟa paḻamoḻippaṭi anta veppam uḷḷa mātaṅkaḷil vaḻaṅkum kāṟṟup paṅkuṉi
   mātattil illātataṉāl,anta mātaṅkaḷilum inta mātam atika uruppam
   uṭaiyatu. āṉatu paṟṟiyē, muntiya kālattil yātāyiṉum oṉṟu ceyya
   uṭaṉpaṭṭōr, atu ceyyāviṭil "paṅkuṉi mātattil pakalvaḻippōvār tuṉpattai
   aṭaivēṉāka" eṉṟu piratikkiṉai ceyyum vaḻakkamiruntatu.]
   koṭiyatē ḷiṭattuveṅ koṭukkum pāmpiṉmāṭ
       ṭaṭuviṭap pallumpō laḻalpa kaṟkaṇē
       kaṭumaiyār veppati karitta naṇpaka
       liṭaivaḻic celkuvā riruvar pōntaṉar. (8)

   (i-ḷ) tēḷiṉiṭattilē koṭukkum pāmpiṉiṭattilē viṣappallum koṭiyavaikaḷāka
   iruppatupōla, pakaṟkālattil koṭiyatāṉa veppam mikunta mattiyāṉa
   camayattilē iraṇṭu vaḻippōkrkaḷ (mēṟkūṟiya curattūṭu) pōvārāyiṉār.
   veṅkoṭukku - koṭumaiyāṉa koṭukku; māṭṭu - iṭattil; aṭu - kollum; aḻal -
   uṭṭiṉam ceykiṉṟa; ār - niṟainta; naṇpakal - naṭuppakalilē;
   vaḻiccelkuvār - pirayāṇam ceypavarkaḷ; pōntaṉar - pōṉārkaḷ.
   [itukāṟum ceṉṟa eṭṭup pāṭalkaḷil, kuḷirkālattiluṅkūṭa veppañceyyum oru
   pālaivaṉattilē attiyanta uṣṇamuḷḷa paṅkuṉi mātattilē maṟṟat
   tiṉaṅkaḷilum atika veppamāka virunta orutiṉattilē iraṇṭu pirayāṇikaḷ
   ceṉṟu koṇṭiruntārkaḷ eṉpatu kūṟappaṭṭatu.]
   cantittār cilvaḻi tāñcela kālaiyiṉ
       muntittām paṭṭavel lāmoḻintaṉar
       pintittān taṅkiṭam peṟutu mōveṉac
       cintaittā pattiṉāṟ ṟiyaṅku cevviyil. (9)

   (i-ḷ.) avvāṟu vaḻicceṉṟavarkaḷ cantittuc ciṟitu tūram cellumpōtu tāṅkaḷ
   iruvarum antak koṭiya pālai vaṉattilē paṭṭa kaṣṭaṅkaḷellāvaṟṟaiyum
   paṟṟip pēciṉārkaḷ; (akkaṣṭaṅkaḷai niṉaintu atairiyam aṭaintu) iṉimēl
   taṅkutaṟkut takka iṭamum kiṭaikkumōveṉṟu tiyaṅkiṉārkaḷ.
   appaṭittiyaṅkiya camayattilē.)
   cilvaḻi - cilavaḻi - ciṟitutūram; celkālaiyil - ceṉṟa pōtu; moḻintaṉar
   - pēciṉārkaḷ; taṅkiṭam - taṅkum iṭam; peṟutumō - peṟuvōmō; cintait
   tāpam - maṉattuṉpam; cevvi - camayam.
   tarainaṭu neruppeṉac cāṟṟu maccurat
       terinaṭu nīreṉac cēytti ṉeytumōr
       maramiṭai vaṉattiṉai vaṟiyaṉ kaṇṭavō
       riruniti yeṉakkaṇṭaṅ keyta vēkiṉār. (10)

   (i-ḷ.) nilattiṉ mattiyilē neruppiruntāṟpōla iruntateṉṟu collappaṭṭa
   curattilē, neruppumattiyil nīr iruntāṟ pōṉṟa oru cōlaiyai, tarittiraṉ
   putaiyalaikkaṇṭatu pōlakkaṇṭu atiṟ cērumpaṭi pōkaluṟṟārkaḷ.
   cēyttiṉ-tūrattil; eytum-poruntiyirunta; maram- miṭaivaṉam-maraṅkaḷ
   neruṅkiya cōlai; vaṟiyaṉ-tarittiraṉ iruniti-perumputaiyal; eyta;
   aṭaiya.
   taraiyiṭaip paravalāṟ ṟaraṅka vēlaiyō
       viruvicum peytalā leḻili yēkolō
       curaneṭum pakaiteṟac cūḻn tiraṇṭumō
       ruruvuṟṟa vōveṉa vuḷatac cōlaiyē. (11)

   (i-ḷ) antaccōlai-,(kāṟṟiṉāl alaipōla alaiyum kiḷaikaḷum ilaikaḷum
   neruṅkit) taraimēlē (iruṇṭu) paraviyiruttaliṉāl,alaikaḷaiyuṭaiya
   camuttirantāṉō veṉṟum, ākāyattai aḷāvi iruttaliṉāl mēkantāṉōveṉṟum
   antac camuttiramum mēkamum avaikaḷukku neṭuṅkālamākap pakaiyāka uḷḷa
   pālaivaṉattai aḻikka (ttaṉittaṉiyē cāttiyappaṭāteṉṟu) yōcaṉaiceytu
   iraṇṭum cērntu ōr uruveṭuttaṉavō eṉṟu collumpaṭiyāka iruntatu.

       kaiyoṉṟu nelliyaṅ kaṉiyiṟ kaṇṭaṇam. (70)

   (i-ḷ.) poyyeṉṟu tōṉṟumpaṭiyākac collappaṭṭa ovvoru pirastāpattaiyum
   naṉṟāka vicārittāl, ataṉataṉ aṭippaṭaiyākac ciṟitāyiṉum uṇmai yiruppatu
   veḷippaṭum eṉṟu periyōr colvataṉ karuttu, iṉṟu uḷḷaṅkai cellikkaṉi pōla
   viḷaṅkiyatu.
   mey oṉṟum - mey ciṟitāyiṉum; ilātirātu – irukkavē ceyyum; ai oṉṟu -
   aḻakuporuntiya; kai oṉṟu – kaiyilē poruntiya; kaṉiyil kaṇṭaṉam -
   kaṉiyaip pōlap pārttōm.
   oruporuḷ palapakka muṭaiya taṉṟiyu
       maruvukā lammiṭa māti māṟalāṟ
       ṟirivuṟu meṉumuṇmai tērnti ṭārkaṭāñ
       cariyeṉak kaṇṭatē cātip pārō. (71)

   (i-ḷ.) oru poruḷukkup pala pakkaṅkaḷ uṇṭu; aṉṟiyum atu kālapētam
   tēcapētam mutaliya pētaṅkaḷiṉāl māṟutal aṭaiyum. inta uṇmaiyai
   aṟiyātavarkaḷē tāṅkaḷ kaṇṭatu māttiram cariyeṉṟu cātippārkaḷ.
   maruvu - poruntiya; āti - mutalāṉavaikaḷ; tirivu - māṟupāṭu.
   eṉvivā ṟiyalpiṟcan tittu mūvarmuṟ
       ṟaṉupava vaṟiviṉā laṟainta vāymaiyai
       yuṉupavar piṟarurai yevaṟṟi ṉuḷḷiṉu
       maṉumuṇmai yaṟintiṭa valla rāvarē. (72)

   (i-ḷ.) eṉṟu ivvāṟākat taṟceyalākac cantitta mūṉṟu pirayāṇikaḷum
   pūraṇamāṉa aṉupavattōṭu kūṭiya aṟiviṉ utaviyiṉāl colliya uṇmaiyaic
   cintippōrkaḷ, maṟṟavarkaḷ collum etaṉiṉ uḷḷēyum uḷḷa uṇmaiyai aṟiya
   vallavarkaḷāvārkaḷ.
   uṉupavā - uṉṉupavar - cintippavarkaḷ; maṉum - maṉṉum - nilaitta; vallar
   - vallavarkaḷ.
   kōmpi viruttam
   mūlamum uraiyum muṟṟuppeṟṟaṉa.

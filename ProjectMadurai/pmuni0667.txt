   INTRODUCTION
   The public are perhaps aware of the fact that the Government of Madras
   have launched upon a scheme of publication of rare manuscripts
   treasured in two premier institutions of this state, namely, the Madras
   Government Oriental Manuscripts Library, Madras and The Tanjore
   Maharaja Serfoji's Sarasvathi Mahal Library, Tanjore. Financial help
   has also been extended to Dr. U. V. Swaminatha Iyer Library, Adyar,
   Madras, for similar purpose. Though the publication of rare manuscripts
   was started by the Government of Madras as early as 1909, only 12 works
   were taken up for publication till 1942. Of these, the printing of 3
   works has not yet been completed.
   Only in the year 1949—50, a regular scheme for the publication of
   manuscripts was prepared and accepted by the Government of Madras under
   the MADRAS GOVERNMENT ORIENTAL SERIES. This scheme included the
   mauuscripts in all the South Indian Languages in which there are
   manuscripts in the Library. The selection of manuscripts was done by an
   Expert Committee appointed by the Government specially for this
   purpose. Till now 157 manuscripts in different languages have been
   printed. Of these, 35 manuscripts are from Tanjore Maharaja Serfoji's
   Sarasvathi Mahal Library, Tanjore and 2 from Dr. U.V. Swaminatha Iyer
   Library, Adyar. In addition, 3 manuscripts, two from this library and 1
   from the Dr. U. V. Swaminatha Iyer Library, Adyar, are in the press.
   While all the manuscripts under the Madras Government Oriental Series
   were edited by various scholars drawn from different learned
   institutions, the publication of manuscripts under the Madras
   GovernmentOriental Manuscripts Series was resumed in the year 1950–51.
   All the manuscripts published under this Series before 1942 were in
   Sanskrit. But from 1950–51, they included the manuscripts in other
   languages also. These are edited by the Curator with the assistance of
   the Pandits in respective languages. Till now 60 works have been
   printed including the total number of 51 since 1931.
   Since 1958-59 however no fresh manuscripts were taken up for
   publication in both the Series. There were several proposals for the
   printing of manuscripts. Of these, the Government of Madras have now
   sanctioned the publication of the following 7 Tamil Manuscripts in G.
   O. No. 81859/-E6/59-15, Education, dated 5-1-1960 communicated in
   Proceedings RC. No. 138-K2/60 dated 27-1-1960 of the Director of Public
   Instruction, Madras.

   Of the 7 manuscripts from this library, Pal̤amol̤i-t-tiraţţu has been
   dropped as it was found to contain many archaic proverbs. The entire
   publicationhas been recommended for aid from the Government of India
   under the scheme for the Development of Modern Indian Languages. It is
   also hoped that the Government of India aid will be forthcoming in due
   course. Since the amount has to be reimbursed from the Government of
   India, it was ordered that the expenditure on the scheme should be
   incurred before 31-3-1960. Though the time actually available for
   getting the manuscripts printed is less than 2 months, the publication
   has been started in right earnest and every possible efforts are being
   made to complete the same before the stipulated time. There has,
   however, been one change in the scheme of publication as submitted by
   the Curator on account of the limited time available. Originally the
   above manuscripts were intended to be edited by outside scholars under
   the Madras Government Oriental Series but now they have been edited by
   the Curator himself with the assistance of the Tamil staff of the
   Library under the Madras Government Oriental Manuscripts Series.
   On account of the great speed with which the press copies were
   prepared, edited and rushed through the press, a few mistakes might
   have crept in. It is hoped that the learned scholars who read the works
   will make necessary corrections, if any.
   In this connection, I will be failing in my duty if I do not express my
   thanks to the Controller of Stationery and Printing, Madras, for
   scrutinizing the quotations of the private presses, for supplying the
   required paper for printing, for passing the printing bills from the
   presses in a very short time and for taking up the printing of the
   biggest of the six manuscripts in the Government Press itself.
   In the ancient lore of Tamil Literature, it has been established: that
   there are ninety-six different divisions called “Prabandas," each
   unique in its respective technique. The ancient grammarian Tolkāppiyar
   has however not specifically indicated in his Tolkāppiyam about
   “Prabandam", but he has described a new set of literature as “Virundu”
   (viruntu). The later exponents of Tolkāppiyam have explained that what
   he meant by "viruntu" is the later acquisition of "Prabandas" in Tamil
   Literature It is also interesting to note that the usage of the word
   "viruntu" directly denotes pleasant thoughts. The ninety six Prabandas
   abound in this sense of pleasing the senses.
   Of these Prabandas, an important component is “Kalambakam". The later
   grammarians have amply described the nature of Kalambakam. It is always
   sung in praise of a God-head or an equally great being. It contains one
   hundred different stanzas approximately and is composed in all the
   different varieties of poetry, such as Venbā, Āśiriyam, Kalippā etc. It
   is invariably in the form of Antādi - the last word or letter of a
   piece following immediately thereafter in the subsequent piece.
   “Kalambakam" contains eighteen Aṅgas which are expounded in the Tamil
   Preface.
   In the series of Kalambakam, some of the most famous pieces are
   “Tiruvaraṅga-k-kalambakam, Madurai-k-Kalambakam and Tiruvāmāttūr
   Kalambakam. Many have praised the beauteous form of those Kalambakams.
   In the present book, three Kalambakams are published in one volume.
   They are “Pērai-k-kalambakam, Tirumayilāсala-k-kalambakam and
   Jñānavinodan Kalambakam."
   1. PĒRAI-K-KALAMBAKAM
   This series is sung in praise of the presiding deity at Tentiruppērai,
   a village in Tirunelveli district. It is one of the famous 108 Vişṇu
   Sthalams and has also been sungby Ā l̤vārs, the twelve pillars of
   Vaişṇava sect. The deityis known as Makara-Nedum-Ku l̤ai-k-kādar and
   therefore Pērai-k-kalambakam is also known as “ Ku l̤ai-k-kādar
   Kalambakam". Tentiruppērai happens to be one of the nine sacred places
   of pilgrimage near Tirukurukur, the birth place of the great Vaişṇava
   Saint Nammā l̤vār.
   The author of this Kalambakam is unfortunately unknown, but it is
   presumed that the author is one Tirumēni Tiruvēṅkaţak-kavirāyar. From
   the songs, it is found that he is a great Tamil scholar and a
   Vaişṇavaite. He seems, naturally, to have aboundant love and regard
   towards the great saint Nammā l̤vār as seen from the very first poem in
   his praise.
   The work was transcribed from Madurai Tamil Śangam manuscript in the
   year 1952-53 and is described in the Triennial Catalogue under number
   R. 3419. It is regrettable that a complete copy could not be had. Only
   fifty poems viz. 1 to 46 and 98 to 101 are available. However, the
   lyric is so full of pathos and devotion that it richly deserves to be
   widely read.
   2. TIRUMAYILĀCALA-K-KALAMBAKAM
   “Mayilam" very popularly known as a great Sri Subrahmanya Sthalam is
   situated in the South Arcot District. The great saint. Arunagirināthar,
   the author of "Tiruppuga l̤”, has sung in praise of Sri
   Subrahmanyasvāmi of Mayilam. It is a place of pilgrimage for a good
   number of devotees from all parts of the country. An Antādi
   "Tirumayilācala-t-tantādi" in praise of this Deity has been published
   by this library in Antādi-k-kottu, Part II. The present work describes
   the village of Mayilam, its presiding deity and his beauty.
   The author is one Dandapāṇi Svāmigal also known as Tirunelveli
   Tiruppuga l̤ Svāmigal. From the work, it is observed that this piece
   was sung under the instructions of Sri Pa l̤aiya Svāmigal, brother of
   the popular Śivaprakaśa Svāmigal.
   This piece contains one hundred songs, all of which are lucid, simple
   and full of interesting particulars regarding LordSubrahmaṇya. There is
   mention of one Poygaipākkam Vaidyanathan, who, it is presumed, was the
   scribe who wrote the piece. Nothing else of definite nature is heard of
   him.
   This is described in the Triennial Catalogue of Tamil manuscripts,
   under No. R. 2812, palm leaf. This has been restored on paper under R.
   No. 5969. This was presented by Sri K. Shanmugam of Kumarakuppam,
   Valavanur post, S. A. District in the year 1951-52.
   3. JNĀNA VINODAN KALAMBAKAM.
   This is a piece containing one hundred songs sung in praise of one
   "Svarūpānandar” by his disciple named “Tattuvarāyar". The author has
   sung several other pieces about the greatness and other qualities of
   his Guru. He proclaims that his ultimate aim in his life is to be
   blessed by his Guru. This contains, as already said, 100 songs besides
   the first prayer song. There are four copies of this manuscript
   described under No. D. 1531 to 1534, all of which are on palm-leaf.
   Srimans V. S. Krishnan, M. Pasupathi, and T. Krishnamurthi, Tamil
   Pandits of this library, were in charge of the preparation of the press
   copy of the three Kalamabakams and my thanks are due to them for
   helping me in editing the manuscript before giving it to the press. The
   Government of India have to be thanked for their kind grant to the
   Government of Madras for enabling the Government Oriental Manuscripts
   Library, Madras, to get printed these invaluable manuscripts.The
   Rathnam Press (Branch) also deserves my thanks for printing the
   manuscript neatly in a very short period of one month.

   eṉpavarāl in nūlnilaiyattukku naṉkoṭaiyāka aḷikkappeṟṟatākum.
   3. ñāṉa vinōtaṉ kalampakam
   itu, corūpāṉanta cuvāmikaḷ eṉṉum periyāraip paṟṟip pāṭappeṟṟuḷḷatoru
   kalampakamākum. corūpāṉantar kalittuṟai, antāti, corūpāṉanta upaniṭatam
   mutaliya pala nūlkaḷ ipperiyāraippaṟṟip pāṭappeṟṟuḷḷaṉa. piṟavi
   yeṭuttataṉ payaṉ corūpāṉantariṉ tāḷkaḷil paṇintu ñāṉam peṟutalē eṉavum,
   ivvulakiluḷḷa maṟṟac celvaṅkaḷ nilaiyaṟṟaṉ, corūpāṉantar tiruvaṭikaḷaip
   paṟṟutalē nilaiyāṉa iṉpantarum eṉṟum viḷakkamāka innūlil
   kūṟappeṟṟuḷatu.
   maṉam, corūpāṉantariṉ aṭiyārpālē cellum ; corūpāṉantariṉ ciṟanta
   pātaṅkaḷilṉṟit talai vaṇaṅkātu; eṉṉai āṭkoṇṭa corūpāṉantaṉaiyaṉṟi eṉ
   vāy vēṟoruvaraiyum paṟṟip pēcātu eṉṟu innūlāciriyar kūṟukiṟār. piṉvarum
   pāṭalil ikkarut tamaintiruppataik kāṇalām.
   vēṇṭā tēneñca maṭiyā ruṭaṉaṉṟi mēvat
   tīṇṭātu ceṉṉi yuṟaicē vaṭiyaṉṟi yeṉṉāt
   tūṇṭā vilaṅkucorupāṉantac cōti yeṉṉai
   yāṇṭā ṉaiyaṉṟi yuraiyāṭa vaṟinti rātē.
   (pak. 61 ; pāṭal 31)
   ilvulakil, paṭittavarkaḷum, ciṟanta karuttukkoṇṭa periyōrkaḷum,
   niṉaittaṉavellām peṟṟavarkaḷum, perumaiyuṭaiya nalla tavattaic
   ceytavarkaḷum, ñāṉikaḷum, ellā naṉmaikaḷaiyu maṭaintavarkaḷum ākiya
   ellōrum em corūpāṉantarkku aṭimait toḻil ceypavarē eṉṟu innūṟ pāṭaloṉṟu
   kūṟukiṉṟatu.
   kaṟṟavarka ḷāvāruṅ karuttuṭaiyā rāvāruṅ karutiṟ ṟellām
       peṟṟavarka ḷāvārum piṟantavarka ḷāvārum pīṭu cērnta
       naṟṟavarka ḷāvārum ñāṉikaṭāmāvārum naṉmai yellān
       tuṟṟavarka ḷāvāruñ corūpāṉan tarkkaṭimait toḻilcey vārē
       (ñāṉa, pā. 60)

   ivvulakil, "puṇṇiya nīril nīrāṭuvatum, ariya tavamiyaṟṟalum, aruccaṉai
   ceytalum ākiya ivaiyellām ceyvataṉāl ēṟpaṭum palaṉai ematu
   corūpāṉantariṉ aruḷ moḻikaḷaik kēṭṭaliṉālēyē peṟalām ; itaṉai maṉattil
   koḷḷuvāyāka"eṉṟu piṉvarum pāṭalil kūṟappeṟṟuḷatu.
   tīrtta māvatuñ ceytava māvatu
       mārtta māvatu maruccaṉai yāvatuñ
       cērtta māvatu meṉcorūpāṉantaṉ
       vārttai kēṭkai matiyē matikkoḷē.(ñāṉa. pak. 68)

   ivvāṟu ciṟanta nallupatēcaṅkaḷaik koṇṭa pāṭalkaḷ palavaṟṟai innūliṟ
   kāṇalām.
   innūṟ pāṭalkaḷaṉaittum eḷiya iṉiya naṭaiyuṭaiyaṉa. kaṭavuḷ vāḻttu
   nīṅkalāka nūṟu pāṭalkaḷuḷḷaṉa innūlil.
   in nūlāciriyar peyar tattuvarāyar eṉpatu. tattuva tēcika nāyaṉār eṉavum
   ivaraik kuṟippaṭuvatu uṇṭu. corūpāṉantariṉ cīṭar ivar eṉat terikiṉṟatu.
   corūpāṉantaraippaṟṟi ivvāciriyar pala nūlkaḷ iyaṟṟiyuḷḷār.
   ippoḻutu veḷiyiṭappeṟum mūṉṟu kalampakaṅkaḷilum, pāṭalkaḷiṉ talaippil
   avvavaṟṟukkuriya uṟuppukkaḷiṉ peyarkaḷ koṭukkappeṟavillai. ivaṟṟiṉ
   mūlap piratikaḷil cila pāṭalkaḷiṉ talaippil uṟuppukkaḷiṉ peyarkaḷ
   eḻutappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa ; perumpālāṉavaṟṟil illai. ātaliṉ, orutaṉmaiyaṉavāy
   iruttal vēṇṭi entap pāṭalukkum ataṉ talaippil uṟuppiṉ peyaraik
   kuṟikkāmal patippittuḷḷēṉ.
   ceṉṉai,
   2-3- 1960. ti. cantiracēkaraṉ.

   malarkkāntaṭ ceṅkaivari vaḷaika ḷāṭa
   vaṉamulaiyiṟ kiṭantumuttu vaṭaṅka ḷāṭa
   maḷaikaviḻak kuḻalviḻuntu maruṅku lāṭac
   cūḻivalam purikaḷiṟṟu mainta rāṭac
   curarmuṉiva ruyiraṉaittuñ cūṟai yāṭac
   cuṭarvayira vaṭampiṇaittuk kamuku mēṟṟit
   tūṅkumaṇip poṉṉuca tuvakki yāṭa
   mēḻivalam puripaḻaṉap pērai nāṭṭil
   mētakucīr vaḷampāṭi yāṭī rūñcal!
   viraittuḷapac ceḻumpuyalait toḻunūṟ ṟeṭṭu
   vētiyartam pukaḻpāṭi yāṭī rūñcal!
   āḻivalam puripāṭi yāṭī rūñcal!
   avaṉkaruṭak koṭipāṭi yāṭī rūñcal!
   āḻvārkaḷ tamiḻpāṭi yāṭī rūñcal!
   amutaṉaiyī raṇiyiḻaiyī rāṭī rūñcal! (19)
   vaṇṇa viruttam
   āṭilāḷkaḻa lāṭilāḷcuṉai yāṭilāḷ paṉinīr
   pōṭuvāḷkaḷai tēṭilāḷvaḷai pūṇilā ḷavaḷtāṉ
   pīṭulāviya vītikōliya pēraivāḻ mukilē
   nāṭuvāḷicai pāṭuvāṇama nāraṇā veṉavē. (20)
   canta vaṇṇa viruttam
   nārāya ṇāveṉa vōtāmal vīṇmoḻika
           ṇāvālu mētupaya ṉavarpukaḻē
   yārālu mōtilavai kēḷāta mūṭarcevi
           yāṉālu mētupaya ṉaṟivilikāḷ
   kārāṉa cōtiyaḻa kārāta kātaloṭu
           kāṇārka ṇātumoru payaṉuḷatō
   vārāḻi mītutuyil pērēcar kōyilvalam
           vārāta kālumoru payaṉilaiyē. (21)
   tāḻicai
   orupiḻaikaṇ ṭayaṟpāru mātaralla vōtā
           ṉuṟavuniṉain tiruppārkaḷ kōpamamai yātō
   viruvarumiṅ kitattōṭu kūṭiyaṇai yāṉā
           iḷamainalaṅ kiṭaittāliṅ kētupaya ṉāmō
   murukaviḻceṅ kaṉikkōvai vāymoḻiyi ṉālē
           muṉivarpeṟuṅ kuṭittāḻvu vārataṟi yīrō
   maruviyacan taṉakkāvil māmalarkoy vārē
           makaraneṭuṅ kuḻaikkātar pēraiyaṉai yārē. (22)
   kalittuṟai
   āraiya rāma liruppā ravaruk karuḷpuriyum
   pēraiya rātippar naṅkuḻaik kātar piṟaṅkalmiṉ[ṉē]
   tāraiya rāvik kaṭaintacev vēleṉac cāyntakuḻaṟ
   kāraiya rāviruppa ṉeñcai yarāvuṅ kaṭaikkaṇkaḷē. (23)
   kaḻineṭil
   kaṇṇakam putaippa veḷiyiṭañ cuvaṟak
   karuntaṭat tiruṭpaṭām virittuk
   kaṭalviṭak kuḻampai yaḷḷiyiṭ ṭulakaṅ
   karantukoṇ ṭeṉaveṉat taṇintu
   viṇṇakam putaitta parutiyu matiyum
   viḻuṅkiyuṇ ṭorupuṭai ceṟippa
   vērviḻun tūḻi muṭivilāk kaṅkul
   viṭivilāt takaimaiyē turaiyāy
   maṇṇakam putaitta tuṇaiyaṭi muṉivar
   maṉattakam putaittavēḻ puviyum
   vayiṟṟakam putaippa pērumveṉ ṟiṭaicci
   mārkkamum putaippanī veruvip
   paṇṇakam putaitta pavaḷavāy putaittup
   parumaṇik kuḻaipiṭit tāṭap
   paṭittavar curuti muṭittavar pēraip
   pativaḷam purakkumā mukilē. (24)
   veṇpā
   māvaḷartta vaṉṉaiyaru māraṉ kuyilvaḷarkka
   kāvaḷarttā reṉṟukuḻaik kātarē!—nāvaḷarttuc
   ceṟṟār nakaiveṭippat tīveṭippap pūntuḷaviṉ
   muṟṟār nakaiveṭikku mō. (25)
   kalittuṟai
   veṭittuc civanta vappālaik kappālai veḷiyilveppam
   piṭittuc civantaṉa vōyntaṇīr kaṇṇaṉ pēraiveṟpil
   vaṭittuc civanta cempañcōṭaṉicca malaruṟuttit
   taṭittuc civantatu kaṇṭīr maṭantaipoṟ ṟāḷiṇaiyē. (26)
   cantat tāḻicai
   tāḷeṭut tulaka ḷanta pēraimuki
   ṟaṉata ruṭkuṟuni lattuḷōr
   taiya laipputuma ṇaṅku ṟitteḻutu
   caruku koṇṭuvaru tūtaṉē
   vēḷe ṭuttavaṭi vēlpa ṭapporutu
   viṉaiye ṭuttavara caraiyellām
   veṭṭi viṭṭatiru mukama lātutiru
   mukamum vēṟaṟiva tillaiyē
   nāḷe ṭuttapaṭai pāṭe ṭuttatilai
   nāṇai yappiḻaiyi liṉṉamuṉ
   ṉarpa tittalaivar talaiye ṭuttatilai
   namaṉe tirttuvaru māyiṉum
   vāḷeṭuttuvari cilaiku kuttuvaḷai
   taṭipi ṭittumavar velvarāl
   maṉuva rampaḻiyu nāḷu meṅkaḷkula
   maṟava rampaḻiva tillaiyē. (27)
   kaḻineṭil
   illat taṭaṅkā maṭantaiyarkaṟ
   peṉavum pacikkoṉ ṟirantavarkkoṉ
   ṟīyā tavarkaip poruḷeṉavu
   miravik kiruḷpō lavumaṭiyār
   collat tolaiyā veḻupiṟapput
   tuṭaikkuṅ karuṇaik kuḻaikkātar
   tuṇaittā ḷaḷakkum puvimaruṅkul
   cuṟṟik kiṭakkuṅ karuṅkaṭalē
   kollat tuṇiyā taṉṉaiyaruṅ
   kotiyā matiyu mataṉpaṭaiyaik
   kūppiṭ ṭaḻaiyāk karuṅkuyiluṅ
   koṭumaip paṭuttāk kuḻalicaiyu
   mallaṟ paṭuttā vayalavaru
   maḷittup pirivī reṉavaṟaiyā
   tayarttuk koṭutta maṉamirukka
   yārai veṟukkak kaṭavōmē. (29)
   viruttam
   kaṭala ṭaṅkaluḻi toṟuci ṟaṅkaipuṉal kaṭukilumpukutu mūcivē
   liṭamvalañcuḻalum vaṭata ṭaṅkiriyu mematu campirata mītelā
   muṭaṉiruntumakiḻ kurupa raṉparavai yulaka ḷantamukil pēraimā
   laṭalpurintupakaliravu kaṇṭatuvu mariya campirata māṉatē. (29)
   veṇpā
   tēvakiyār peṟṟa tiruvaruttam pārāmaṟ
   kōviyartam maṉṟukuḻaik kātarē!—tāvi
   yaṭikkuṅkai māṟōnī yañciṉapoṟ kātu
   piṭikkuṅkai māṟō peritu. (30)
   viruttam
   peruviṭa varavaṇaip pērai mātavaṉ
   maruviṭa niṉaikkilāṉ maṅkai mātarē
   taruviṭa veṇṇilāt taḻaitta teṅkaṇu
   moruviṭa milainamak kuṟaivi ṭaṅkaḷē. (31)
   kalittuṟai
   uraikkoṭu māṭavar vāḷā lorukom piḻantumaṟṟak
   kuṟaikkoṭu koṇṭuḻal kuñcaram pōluṅ koṭiyiṭaiyē
   tuṟaikkoṭu vāyvaitta mālpērai yiṟṟeṉṉar cūḻntapaṇṭaic
   ciṟaikkoṭu mēkam piṟaikkoṭu tāṅkic ciṟakkiṉṟatē. (32)
   catta viruttam
   ciṟantār toḻattaku maruntāma ḻaippuyal
           ceḻumpērai yuttamarpō
   laṟantāṉaṟakkuru kiṉaṅkāṉ maṭappēṭai
           yaṉaṅkāḷuraittaruḷvī
   riṟantō meṉiṟpiḻai yiruntō meṉiṟpaḻu
           tiraṅkāma ṉattavarpōl
   maṟantāl maṟakkavu niṉaintāl niṉaikkavu
           maṉantāṉa makkillaiyē. (33)
   veṇpā
   emakku mukamvāṭa vintāl maraika
   ṭamakkumukam vāṭuñ calippē – ṉamaitturaiyum
   peṇmati yeṉṉātē mālpērai nāṭṭaṉṉaṅ
   koṇmati yuṇṭō kaḻal. (34)
   cantat tāḻicai
   taḻalpiḻintu cāṟukoṇṭu cantaṉatti liṭṭatār?
           taṇṇilavai yeripaṭac camaittuviṭṭa pāviyār?
   kuḻalpiḻinta vicaiyilē kuḷuntaciṅki vaittatār?
           koṭumaivanta kālamācai koṇṭirukka vallarō?
   niḻalpiḻintu parukaveṉṟu niṉaiyumāvu marutamu
           ṉeriyaveṉṟa kuricilpērai nīrkuḷikku nāraikāḷ
   aḻalpiḻinta vēlaremmai yāṇaiyiṭ ṭakaṉṟatā
           lantavāymai yuṭalpiḻinteṉāviyuṇṭu viṭṭatē.(35)
   piṉmuṭuku veṇpā
   āviyuṇ ṭumaiyuṇ ṭaṟivaiyuṇṭu niṟkumiru
   kāviyuṇṭu tāmaraikkē kaṇṭīrō—tēviyuṭaṉ
   mālvaḷarnta pēraiyiṉkaṇ vāvikaṇṭu pūvaiyuṇṭu
   kālkuṟainta nīlavaṇṭu kāṇ. (36)
   viruttam
   vaṇṭirukkuṅ kuḻalpurattu vāṭṭamaṭit tuppiṭittu
           vaṭinta vaḷḷait
   taṇṭirukkuṅ kuḻaimaṭavīr kuḻaikkātar pēraiyilmut
           tamiḻē pōla
   vuṇṭirukka vuvaṭṭāta vitaḻamu tumiḷañca
           mukaṅkaḷ pāliṟ
   koṇṭirukkap peruṅkāyam pacikkutavā tiruppatuvuṅ
           koṭumai tāṉē.(37)
   viruttam
   koṭittēriṉar kuḻaikkātiṉar kulakkār varaimēṟ
   ṟaṭittīrtaḻai toṭuttīrkari piṇaittē vēta
   ṉaṭittāraiyi ṉaṭappīravai yaṭaittā ḷuvarō
   koṭittōḻiyar puṉaiccārvoru toḻuttā ṉalavē.(38)
   kalittuṟai
   toḻumpāk kiyaṇṭar toḻakaṟ pakāṭavic cūḻalāṟṟē
   ṉeḻumpāk kiyameṉ ṟiruppatal lālintu viṭṭantiṭṭit
   taḻumpāk kiyapoḻil cūḻpērai mālcaraṇāravintac
   ceḻumpāk kiyameṉ ṟavaṉaṭi yārpaṇṭu ceytavamō. (39)
   viruttam
   ceyyiṟ karumpum vaḷarpērai tirunā ṭaṉaiyē yītāṉa
   ṟoyyiṟ karumpun toṭarntukolait toḻiluk karumpumviḻiyumataṉ
   kaiyiṟ karumpu malarkkaṇaiyuṅ kaikkoṇṭirukkakkaṇṭaṉaimē
   leyyiṟ karumpuṅ kaṇaiyumilaiyiṉṟaik kiṟavā tiruppēṉē. (40)
   kaḻineṭil
   irumpai nerittut tutikkaimaṭut
   tiḷaṅkō makaḷir viraṉerippa
   vetirttār cirattai nerittumalai
   yēṟi nerittuk kaṭappuṟattuc
   curumpai nerittu vaḷikaṟaṅkac
   cuṟṟum piḻaikkāṟ cevippaṭalat
   tuṅkak kaḷiṟṟiṉ micaippavaṉi
   toḻutāl virakan tolaiyātō
   karumpai nerittu viḻuppalaviṉ
   kaṉiyai nerittu maṭaimutukaṟ
   katalip paṭalaik kulainerittuk
   kaṉṉik kamukiṉ maṭaṉerittuk
   kurumpai nerittut tēṉoḻukak
   kuvaḷai nerittup puṭaittuvarāl
   kutikkum puṉaṟpē raiyilmakarak
   kuḻaiyē yevarkkuṅ kōmāṉē. (41)
   viruttam
   māṉeṉpār kalaiyeṉpār toṭukka vāṭu
           malart taḻaiyā leytatoru matatta vēḻan
   tāṉeṉpār patiyeṉpār vaḻiyē teṉpār
           tāmaraippūṅ kōvileṉṟē virantī reṉpār
   kōṉeṉpār kulattutikkuṅ karuṇai mēkaṅ
           kuḻaikkātar pēraiyiḷaṅ kompē vampē
   yāṉeṉpā rallaveṉpā rillai yeṉpā
           rivarkoṭṭik ketirutaippār yāvar tāmē. (42)
   viruttam
   tāmōtara matucūtaṉar takupēraiyil maṭavīr
   nāmōtara māmōkiṉi nalamōtara maṟiyī
   rāmōrtara malavōveḷi taṭiyēṉuṭaṉ muṉivāyp
   pōmōtara niṉaiyīrkamaḻ putuvāymala ramutē. (43)
   kalippā
   tēṉār poruṉait tirumāl miṭaipērai
   yāṉāta kalviyaṟivār payaṉaṉṟō
   kāṉār karuṅkuḻalār kāmattiṉ pālmaṟantu
   pōṉāraṟattiṉpāl pōṭṭupporuṭpālē. (44)
   iṉṉicaik kalippā
   pālvaṭivun tiraṇmulaiyum paccuṭampum pacunarampuñ
   cūlvaṭivun tōṉṟāmaṟ ṟūṇvayiṟṟiṟ piṟantanāḷ
   mēlvaṭivā miraṇiyaṉai viṉaitolaikkut tamiḻppērai
   mālvaṭivān tiruvaṭiva marakatattiṉ maṇivaṭivē. (45)
   kalittuṟai
   vaṭittūtuc caṅkoppa veṇṭōṭṭu mallikai vāyiṟkavvip
   piṭittūtum vaṇṭōṭum pēcuki lēṉpiri yātaaivar
   kuṭittūtu ceṉṟa kuḻaikkātark keṉmayal kūṟivarum
   paṭittūtu nīcollu vāymaḻai cāṭum paṉikkoṇṭalē. (46)
   (47 mutal 97 muṭiyavuḷḷa ceyyuṭkaḷ viṭupaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.)
   vaṇṇat tāḻicai
   caramaḻaikkum puyal koḻikkun tamiḻvaḷarpē raiyilē
   matikkumporuḷ mikappeṇṭu kiṟaḷaivoṟukkuṅ
   kuṭitaḻaikkum paṭivaḷarkkun tirumaṭamā mayilē
   vitikkumpaṭi paṭikkuñciṟi taṉiccaṅ kaḷum
   larappañciṉu veṟukkumpata muṉatā kaiyiṉā
   lutikkuṅkatir vaṟukkumparal kotikkuñcuṭar
   naṭakkuntoṟu muṟaikkumpoḻu tuyirvā ṭuvaiyē. (98)
   vāḻi yakaval
   vāṭaivan tiyaṅkap pīṭaikoṇṭuḷatē
   teṉṟalaṅ kaṉṟu meṉṟutiṉṟumiḻntu
   kōtupaṭa vāruyirk kuṟaiyaiyuñ cuḻittu
   kuḻaittuviral kuṟainta koḻittamaṇaṟ kaṭalu
   maḷḷi yiṭṭa puḷḷiyam pacalaiyun
   tuyiṟcuvai yaṟiyāp payirppuṟu taṭaṅkalu
   maṟavā vaṉpun tuṟavā vuḷḷamu
   maṉṉap pēṭum puṉṉaiyaṅ kāvun
   tuṇaipaṭa virintu tuyarvaruṅ kālattup
   paḻutilā vuyarnta vaḻutināṭ ṭavaiyum
   yārai yuntamiḻp pēraiyum purappa
   vaḷanakar vārkuḻaic cikarapū talattōṭ
   ṭeyva nāyaka ṉaivartērp pākaṉ
   malaya mālvarait talaivaraic cāraliṉ
   maṇaṅkoḷ pūṅkoṭi piṇaṅku taṇpō
   tumpariṟ ciṟanta kāṭciyiṟ piṟanta
   taṇṇaḷi poṟkai koṭṭut talaivarmey
   toṭṭup payiṉṟu paṇaimulaik kurumpai
   yiṇaivaraip puyatti ṉūṭuṟap porutu
   pāṭuṟak kiṭappa varivaḷait taḻumpu
   mārpiṭat taḻunta paripurac cillolip
   palakala ṉolippap pulliyuṅ kalliyum
   porunti naṟcīr nalamiku peruñcuvai
   namakkiṉi taḷikkuñ ciṟutuyiṟ kaṉavu
   tantaruḷu makaraneṭun takaivāḻi vāḻiyē. (99)
   veṇpā
   vāḻipukaḻp pērai makarak kuḻaikkātar
   vāḻitamiḻ nūṟṟeṭṭu māmaṟaiyōr—vāḻiyē
   tēkkum patakamalan tīrtteṉatu cintaiyuḷḷē
   pūkkum patakamalap pū. (100)
   viruttam
   patittanava ratṉamaṇi vāḻi [vāḻi]
           paṅkayappūn tirumukañcem pavaḷa vāynīḷ
   katittacīr vāṇutalveṇ ṭirumaṇ kāppuk
           kattūrit tilatamiku kaṇkaḷ vāḻi
   tutittanaṟum pacuntuḷavat tōḷkaḷ vāḻi
           karimukaccaṅ kāḻikatai vilvāḷ vāḻi
   matittapī tāmparañcē raraiṉūl vāḻi
           caraṇaṅkuḻaik kātarmāl vāḻi tāṉē. (101)
   pēraik kalampakam muṟṟum.

                       2. tirumayilācalak kalampakam

   pāyiram
   koccakak kalippā
   aṉṟucivap pirakāca ṉariyatuti taṉakkeytac
   ceṉṟupakarn tamaināṭic civañāṉa pālaiyaṉ
   kuṉṟuḷuyar mayilaimalaik kumaraṉukkuk kalampakamoṉ
   niṉṟu colat tiruppukaḻō ṉiṭattilitu icaittāṉē. (1)
   veṇpā
   āṭṭuppāl vāyvai yaṭakki yaṭuttuvantu
   cīṭṭukku ḷāṭaloṉṟu ceytupala—pāṭṭuṭaiya
   pālaiyaṉai yēviyeṉṉaip paṟṟivalin tāṇṭukoṇṭāṉ
   cōlai mikumayilat tōṉ. (2)
   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭi lāciriya viruttam
   cayilamu ḻukkavum veṭkacci rantaraṉār
           tamaturu vottaruvip poṟpicain tuḷatāl
   mayila malaikkuka ṉārukkuk kalampakanūl
           vaṉaikuvataṟ kuyircoṟ kaṭṭuvan taruḷvīr
   veyilava ṉuṟṟa vayiṟṟuñ ceḻuṅkaḷiṟē
           veḷiṟu malakkuḷi rukkaik kicaintavaṉē
   yayiṉmayil kukkiṭa muṟceppaṉiṉṟaṉavē
           yaṭiyar patakkama lattuṟṟa nuṇpoṭiyē. (3)
   kalampakattiṉuṟuppukkaḷ.
   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭi lāciriya viruttam
   mayaṅkicaikkoc cakakkalippāp puyavakuppu ammāṉai
           maṟaṅkaḷicit tūcaṟaḻai vāḻumūr maruṭpā
   vayaṅkeḻiṟcam piratamataṅ kiraṅkalkaṭ ṭumaṭakku
           vaṇkuṟampāṇ kaikkiḷai palavāṟāyt tūtu
   tayaṅkukaḻik karaippulampal kālamuta lākat
           tamiḻppulavark karuttilavar takkatuṟai yellā
   miyaṅkukalam pakattiṉukkā māṉālu moruvāṟī
           roṉpateṉac cilanū liyamputalu muḷavē. (4)
   nūl
   mayaṅkicaik koccakak kalippā
   tirumakaḷcēr neṭumāyaṉ ceṅkamalak kaṇkaṭaru
   mirumaṭavārk kiṭainīṭu meḻiṟpavaḷa malaipōlvāy
   paritimati picaintāṅku pakarmuṭiyu mukamoṉṟum
   virikaruṇai viḻimūṉṟum viḷaṅkuḻaic ceviyiraṇṭum
   vaṇpaṉuvaṟ paiṅkiḷḷai maṇanukaru maṇanācic
   caṇpakamum ciṟumuṟuvaṟ ṟaraḷanakaik kaṉivāyum
   poṟpaṇiyum paṉmalarum polintakaḻut toṭumārpum
   viṟpaṭumiṉ ṉeṉakkāṇum veṉṟivēl viyaṉcīrum
   maṭalvayiram tiricūla mapayavara tantulaṅkuṅ
   kaṭakaneṭuṅ karantāṅkuṅ kaviṉtōḷkaḷ orunāṉkuṅ
   kuñcimaṇic ceñcataṅkaik kōttaraiyuṅ kōcikamum
   viñciyapērāṉantam viḷaittaruḷum tuṇaittāḷuṅ
   koṇṭumayi lācalattiṟ kuvaṭṭiluṟuṅ kōyiliṭai
   maṇṭucaṭaic civañāṉi valattamara vāḻkiṉṟāy
   mayilēṟit takarēṟi maḻaveḷḷaik kaḷiṟēṟik
   kuyilpōlu mumaiyāḷkaṇ kuḷiravaruṅ kuruntōnī !
   ivaiyeṭṭun taravukaḷ.
   araṉvaḻi paṭamuṉa maruṇala maruḷiṉai ;
   murakari yamararmuṉ moḻipukaḻ maruviṉai;
   yayaṉmuta lamara varamoṭu taruviṉai ;
   puyalivar makapati puṉaimuṭi yutaviṉai.
   ivai nāṉkum arākaṅkaḷ.
   aṉpoṭumuṉ ṟutikūṟi yāṟeḻuttā laruccittut
   teṉpotiyai micaivāḻvāṉ ciṟappaṉaittumpeṟacceytāy;
   niṉṉāmat toṉṟiṉaiyē nitaṅkūṟac caliyāta
   miṉṉāḷaṉ ṟiḻantakaram viraintutavi mīṇṭuvantāy;
   naṟkīraṉ ciṟai nīkki navilariya tamiḻaṇivāṉ
   kaṟkīri yalakaiyaiyuṅ kaṭiyavaṭi vēlviṭuttāy;
   muṟṟucuvaik kavippoyyā moḻippulavaṉ kaḷikūṟap
   paṟṟumoru kōloṭumuṉ pālaiyumuṭṭaiyumāṉāy;
   teṉṉaruṇaik kōpurattiṟ ṟikaḻoruvaṉ taṉakkāka
   muṉṉamoru tūṇiṭattiṉ muḷaippataṟku micaivuṟṟāy;
   pōrinakar muṉivaṉmutaṟ pukalumavar pukaḻkiṉṟa
   cīriyavāḻ villār(kaḷ) cintitta pēraḷittāy.
   ivai yāṟun taravukaḷ.
   ēṉa maruppaṟa mōti vakuttaṉai ;
   yēḻicai yiṟkavi kōṭi toṭuttaṉai;
   māṉavir kaṭkuri yārai mayakkiṉai ;
   mācaṟu ciṭṭi viṉōta miyakkiṉai.
   ivai nāṉkum nāṟcīr ōraṭi yampōtaraṅkaṅkaḷ.
   māvaṭi yōṭaṟa veṉṟaṉai;
   mālvarai yāviṉu niṉṟaṉai;
   tāmata rōrvari tāyiṉai;
   cāraṇar vāḻvara mēyiṉai.
   ivai nāṉkum muccīr ōraṭi yampōtaraṅkaṅkaḷ.
   ātalil ;
   itu taṉiccol.
   cēval poṟuttaṉai pāva maṟuttaṉai
   cintaika ḷuṟṟaṉai vintaikaḷ peṟṟaṉai
   kaṭaliṟ poruṉaiyiṟ kaṉaviṟ ṟamiyēṉ
   ṟiṭamuṭaṉ viṭutta ceḻuntamiḻp paṉuva
   laṉaittiṉu mīṇṭoḷir taravaḷittu muṉṉiṉṟu
   tiṉaittuṇai tuyaruñ cērā torīit
   tirumalaik kuvaṭṭil ceytapōrāṭa
   lurumalaik kōyiluraitta māṟṟa
   mātiya piṟavu makilat tōrum
   pētiyā vaṇṇam pēcap purintu
   kaiccilaik kuṟavar kaviṉṟaru cāraṟ
   pacciḷañ cōlai paraṇmī tiruntāṉ
   matalaiyeṉ ṟiyāvarum vaḻutta vaittu
   pataniḻaṟ ṟaruvāy parameṉa vantēṉ
   vitimuṟai tavaṟi veḷḷilai meṉṟu
   katirmaṇi vaṭamulai karuttuṭ ṭōṉṟap
   paḻaiyapā voṉṟu pakarvāṉ ṟaṉaiyum
   viḻaitaru karuṇai mikaiyāṟ purantāy
   niṉṟiru vuḷḷameṉ ṉērat
   teṉṟicaip poḻikku meṉaviraṅ kiṉaṉē. (1)
   iḵtu āciriya viruttattiṉāl vanta curitakam.
   nēricai veṇpā
   iraṅkun tiruvuḷḷat tēṟṟamuṟṟa cevvēḷ
   varaṅkaḷel lānalku malaivēḷ - kuraṅkaṉaiya
   pāḻmaṉamē palkōṭi pattar paricupeṟṟu
   vāḻmayilat tuḷḷa malai. (2)
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalittuṟai
   malaitteyva mēpara meṉṟoppi ṭāccila māṉiṭaruñ
   cilaitteyvam vaittup palavāṟu nāmaṅkaḷ ceppaleṉṉō
   nilaitteyvam vāḻu mayilā calatti ṉiṟaipukaḻnēr
   kalaitteyva māṉa piṭiyēveṇ ṭāmaraik kaṉṉikaiyē. (3)
   kalippā
   kaṉṉik kuṟatti kaṇavaṉ mayilaveṟpai
   yuṉṉik karuttō ṭuṭalurukap pōṟṟicaikku
   meṉṉiṭṭa miṉṉateṉa yāraṟivā reṉṟaṉakku
   miṉṉiṟ ṟikaḻntakati vēlumayi luntuṇaiyē. (4)
   kalinilaittuṟai
   mayilāca lattuṟṟa kumarēca ṉukkāṉa vaḻipāṭatāṟ
   kayilāya moṭumēru varaimī tirukkuṅka ṉaṟkaṇṇutal
   payilāka mattaippa ḻikkiṉṟa tīyōrkaḷ pakaimiñcunā
   ṉoyilāru maṭavārta makkiccai koṭuvāṭa lovvātatē. (5)
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalippā
   vāta nūṟpiṇak kāḷarkaṇ kāṇaru
           malaiya mālvarai māmuṉi-taṉṉaruṭ
   pōta vāriyiṉ mūḻkiṭa nāṭuvēṉ
           punti nontu pulampiṭa lākumō
   vēta nāṉmuka kaḷvaṉaik kuṭṭiya
           vīṟu koṇṭoḷir meññāṉa tēcikā
   māta mōrtiru nāḷvaruñ cīrtikaḻ
           mayila mālvarai vāḻmuru kaiyaṉē. (6)
   kali viruttam
   ayyamiṉṟi muṉṉaṟainta vācakappaṭiyaruḷi
   meyyar tampuṭai yūṭuṟa viṭukkunā ḷuḷatō
   vaiyam vāṉoṭu tuticeyu mayilamā malaiyiṉ
   ceyya poṟkuva ṭāmeṉat tikaḻtaru cōtiyē. (7)
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   cēyamā malarkoṇ ṭuṉṉait
           tiṉantiṉam aruccit tēttun
   tūyavar kamalap pūntāṭ
           ṭukaḷaṇi talumvīṇ pōmō!
   māyavaṉ marukā viṇṇōr
           makaḷpuṇar kaṇavā vaḷḷi
   nāyakā mayila veṟpi
           ṉaṭañceyu muruka vēḷē. (8)
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   muruka ṟāmalark kaṭampaṇi puyattaṉai
           mūviru mukattāṉai
   yarukar pāḻmatat timaramiv vulakaiviṭ
           ṭakalavan taruḷvāṉaip
   peruku cīrttiru mayilamā malaimicai
           pēṇiya periyōrtaṅ
   karuṇai ñāṉamuñ cittiyu muṇarpavar
           kālaṉuk kañcārē. (9)
   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   añcalaḷit tāṇṭaperuṅ karuṇaic cīru
           maruṇakiri tāṉeṉanī yaṟainta collum
   vañcakameṉ ṟikaḻvataṟku mañcu kiṉṟēṉ
           varuttamiṉic cakippataṟkum vallē ṉallēṉ
   neñcakattaṉ ṟāṭiyatai maṟakka māṭṭēṉ
           neṭumayilak kuvaṭṭiṉmuṉ ṉiṉṟēṉ kaṇpār
   pañcavarṇac cikiyūrum pavaḷa mēṉip
           paṇṇavaṉē yeṇṇariya pāṉmai yāṉē. (10)
   iraṭṭaiyāciriya viruttam
   āṉamaṭ ṭiṟpaṇi purintuṉatu meyyaṉpa
   ravaiyiṉiṟ pukavirumpā
   taṉinakai maṭantaiyar mulaittuṇai niṉaitturuki
   yalamarun tamiyaṉēṉai
   yīṉaruk kemaṉaṉaiya vīrarmuta lōrkaḷu
   miṟaiñcumpa tattilvaittuṉ
   ṉeḻilvataṉa niraikulavu maṟuvakai neṟittalaiva
   ṉeṉṟoḷira vuñceyvaiyō
   kāṉavak kuṭiloru karuṅkala meṉavanta
   kaṉṉikki caintakaṇavā
   kaṉakanaka ratipaṉ vaḷarttapiṭi kaṇṭuḷaṅ
   kaḷikūra varukantaṉē
   vāṉaḷak kuṅkuvaṭu toṟumiḻiyu maruvipala
   mālikaiye ṉakkulāvu
   mayilā calattilvaru pulavōr tutikkumoru
   vaṭivēlpi ṭittakuruvē. (11)
   puyavakuppu
   kuruparar veṉattiṉamu murukināṭu meyppulavar
   koṇarvatāṉamuttamiḻai nēcittu yarntaṉa;
   kuravanī muṟpakaru maḷavilā malarttokuti
   kuravumā likaittiraḷkaḷ cūṭippo lintaṉa;
   kuṟaivuṟā voḷipparuti yaṉaiyatō ḷaṇippakuti
   koṭupalpū ṇeṭuttorumu nūluṟṟi laṅkiṉa;
   kulicamū vilaippaṭaivil kaṇaivaivēṉ mutaṟpalarcol
   kurutiyū ṉuṭaikkaruvi cērac cumantaṉa;
   kukukukū veṉattaṉatu ciṟakaimō taṭaṟparavai
   kuḷaṟiṭā moḻikkiḷiyō ṭēṟip piṟaṅkiṉa;
   kuḷirumvā viyiṟkeḷavuri mulaiyiṉōṭu kārttikaiyar
   kuyamumārva muṟṟamuta mīyap picaintaṉa;
   koṭiyarā viṉaippacalai yulakiṉmāri vaittamarar
   kuḻuvelā metirttamarcey tōṭak kaviṉṟaṉa;
   kuṟaḷaināratattavaci muyalumvēḷviyiṟṟakarōr
   kutiraiyā kiṭakkacaiyi ṉālviṭṭuvantaṉa;
   kuṭaiyatā meṉakkalapa niḻalkoṇmā mayiṟkaṭaval
   kuṇameṉā niṉaippavarkaḷūṭuṟ ṟavirntaṉa;
   kumiḻitōy matattayilam oḻukumvā raṇakkaḷiṟu
   kutaṟiṉā leyiṟṟalaiyiṉ mōtic civantaṉa;
   kuvaḷainēr viḻiccacimiṉ maruvuvāṉ vaḷarttutavu
   kumaripūṇ mulaikkaḷapa mārakka maḻntaṉa;
   koṭiyiṉuṭu vaittiṟuka muṭiyumvār taḻaikkalaikoḷ
   kuṟamiṉāḷaṇipparaṇmu ṉēkik kuvintaṉa;
   kolaiceyvāḷ viḻicciṟiya makaḷirpārvai meyttavarkaḷ
   kulaivuṟā maṉakkavaṉa mīṟap poruntiṉa;
   koṭumaiyē mikappuriyu mavarkaḷvēraṟakkaṟuvu
   kuṟiyuḷār tamakkiḻivu ṟāmaṟ purantaṉa;
   koṭaiyilā muḻukkayavar maṉaikaṭōṟu muṟṟaṭiyar
   kuṟukiṭā taruṭkaṭaliṉ mūḻkaccey paṇpiṉa;
   kucaiyuṉāṇu rakkatumai yuṭaiyamyā meṉapperitu
   kutikoḷvē ṉuḷattiṉuman nāḷcaṟ ṟoḷirntaṉa;
   varuvavāta miṭṭuḻalu mavarkaḷpōka maṟṟavarka
   ḷaṇukumāriraṭṭiyaṉ vītikku ḷoṉṟiṉa;
   vaṇikoḷvār cevikkuḻaika ḷulavucīrpo ruttaḻuvi
   vacalamē yeṉakkaviñar pēcak kiṭantaṉa;
   vaṭalaiyāku ṭaṟparamar piraṇavāka rapporuḷai
   yaṟiyunāḷ puṉaṟcaṭila mēṟik kuḷirntaṉa;
   vakilalōka muṟparuki yumiḻumāl vayappaṭaiyai
   yaṭaiyumāṟiyaṟṟiyanal vīṟuṟ ṟeḻuntaṉa;
   variya nāṉ maṟaikkiḻava ṉuṭalilvērvai viṭṭalaṟi
   yavacamā kiṭacciṟitu kōpikkai koṇṭaṉa;
   vamararkōṉmi kappayamō ṭiṇaiyilkāvirikkaraiyi
   lamaiyeṉāvu ṟuppeṟuta ṟīrap purantaṉa;
   vaṭalkoḷvī raruṭṭalaiva ṉoruvaṉī curakkaṇaiko
   ṭavuṇarkō ṉiṉaippaḻital kāṇac ciṟantaṉa;
   vaḷarumvāri cakkaṭavuḷ varamatā luvappaṭaiyu
   mavarkaṇmē letirttavaṉai yāḷap pukuntaṉa;
   vaḷaviṉ māyai veṟpuruva muṭaiyatāṉa vappayalai
   yaṇuvainēr poṭipaṭamu ṉāḷaṭṭu niṉṟaṉa;
   vaḻalumvāy viṭakkaṭitu porutatā rukakkoṭiya
   ṉaḻiyavēl viṭuttulaka mōtat tikaḻntaṉa;
   vaṟiviṉāṉ mikapperumai yuṭaiyakō ḷarittakuva
   ṉaṟaiyumvā cakappaṭiya ṉōṉait taṭintaṉa;
   valaimakōtatikkiṭaiyi ṉeṭiyamā maratturuvō
   ṭaruvivā ṉaiyappoḻuti lēveṭṭu moympiṉa;
   vamalayō kattavaciṉ mayacivōka muṟṟavarta
   maracaṉā makattiyaṉai nērmukat taraṉpiṉa;
   vayavuṟāma lekkaṇamu murukaṉē yeṉappakaru
   va(ḷa)[ḻa]kaivēṟu paṭṭataṉai yītā kuṟaṅkiṉa;
   valakaiyāṉ mikattaḷaru moruvaṉōṭu paṟpalarum
   virumpuṟātu kaṟkatavu kīḷap piḷantaṉa;
   vatikavāḻ vuṭaippaḻaiya maturaiyūriṉiṟpulava
   ralamaṟā tukaikkuṟiyiṉālvaitta naṇpiṉa;
   karutumāṟu muṟkulavi yutaviṭāta poṟkulavu
   kataṟumō tiṭattakuti yaravuṟṟa ceṇṭiṉa;
   kaṭalaiyē viṭattuṇiyum varuṇaṉār toḻattaṉatu
   katirkulā vucattiyiṉai vīcikkaṉaṉṟaṉa;
   kaṉakanā ṭuṭaittalaivaṉ valiyatū taraikkoṭucey
   kapaṭamāṉatiṟciṟitu nōvaṟṟiruntaṉa;
   kavicolē ṉeṉattiṉamu nuvalpoyā moḻikketirōr
   kariyakōl piṭitticaiyu māṭaṟpa yiṉṟaṉa;
   kaṉalatā malaikkarukiṉilavukō purattuyumōr
   kaviñaṉā tiyiṟpakarpal pāṭaṟṟu taintaṉa;
   kaḷiṟunā ṇuṟattoṉicey paṉuvalā ṉoruttaṉatu
   kaliyiṉvēraṟattiruṭi yīyattu ṇintaṉa;
   kalaivalār mikappukaḻu micaikaḷpōyiṟṟavaci
   kavalaitīr vataṟkoruka ḷācattiyaintaṉa;
   karaiyiṟpā laiyaṟkuvakai perukavōr kavikkuriya
   kaṉaviṉār malarkkoṭucel vāratta miḻntaṉa;
   kaluḻaṉār kaiyiṟṟalaiyai nirumalā natikkuriya
   karaiyilvī cumuṟkaṭiti lēkikka varntaṉa;
   kaḻaṟumēḻ vakaippuviyi ṉuyirelā maḻattaṉatu
   kaḷiyiṉāṉ mikakkutikoḷ ḷēṉattai veṉṟaṉa;
   kayilaiyī caṉaipporuvu muṉivaṉvāy moḻippaṭiyōr
   kaṭavuṇmā maṇiccuṭikai vīḻat turantaṉa;
   kaṉiyeṇmō takakkuviya laṉaiyavē mikapparuku
   kariyaṉīḷ vayiṟṟiravi mēvak kaṭintaṉa;
   kakaṉamā natippuṉalai yaṉaiyanī raḻaittumaikoḷ
   kaṉaviṭā yaṟacceyuvi ṉōtatto ḻumpiṉa;
   kamalavā viviṭṭeḻumōr mutiyatā taiyuṭkarutu
   kaḷavutīr tacakkaramtāṉ mēyap pukaṉṟaṉa;
   kavaḷayā ṉaiyottoḷirum vaḻutikaṭaṉ viṭaccuramōr
   kaṇanilā taṟattiruveṇ ṇīṟiṭ ṭukantaṉa;
   kaṭuvainē ṭuṭaṟcamaṇa muṉivarar muḻukkoṭiyar
   kaḻuvilē kiṭattamiḻai vāric corintaṉa;
   maruvaṟā malarkkaṇaika ḷaḷavilkō ṭitaitturuki
   makaḷiṟcē ṉaiyiṟkurimai mīṟippu ṇarntaṉa;
   maturacā karattamiḻai yutavucī ruṭaippotiyai
   varaiyiṉvāṇar meyttavaci pōlap paṭarntaṉa;
   vakuḷanāṇ malarttoṭaiyal puṉaiyuvēṇmu taṟcilaruṇ
   makiḻvelāmaṟapperuku kōlatta maintaṉa;
   varatamā ṉataipporuvi lapaiyamā ṉataikkoṭutaṉ
   valimainā ṭumuttamarvi cārattai viṇṭaṉa;
   maṟavaṉō ṭukaiccamarcey tiravilōr koṭikkuvakai
   maliyumā ṟeḻiṟkayalkaṇ mīḷakkoṇarntaṉa;
   maṟatiyā luṉakkaḷiyiṉ muṭukuvāṉ veḷippaṭavu
   maṉaiyiṉmē yatiṟciṟiti lāmaṟpa kirntaṉa;
   vayatiṉāl mikkuyaru mavaḷmuṉā valiṟkaṭiyiṉ
   maṇalilōr paḻattiṉaiya nāḷviṭ ṭeṟintaṉa;
   maṟuvilā varaikkavitai yoruvaṉō taliṟpuḷiya
   maramatūṭu koppoṭiva tākat terintaṉa;
   maṉaiyilātu maṟṟavarai yaṉaiyeṉāniṉaippavaṉmuṉ
   maṇiyiṉā vaṟappakarta lātikka marntaṉa;
   vaṉacalō caṉattorupeṇ muṭiyiṉmēl viḻittakukal
   vaḻukumōr paṭikkaṭiyi lēniṟkaik kaṇṭaṉa;
   vayiṉavāta rattoruva ṉicaiyuṉāḷiṉ muṟpuṉaiyum
   vaḷaiyamā maṭaṟṟaḷaika ḷōvatto ṭarntaṉa;
   vayiravāti kaṭkaḷaviṉiṇamumūṇu mikkutavum
   valiyapōri ṉukkuriya vīratta ḻuntiṉa;
   maṉatuvēṟu paṭṭiṭuta lilatunāṭi niṟpavarkaṇ
   maṟalitūtarukkayarvuṟāmaṟṟi rintaṉa;
   malaiyilēṟi muṟpuviyi luruḷumāṇa vacciṟuvaṉ
   maṭivuṟāte ṭuttulaki lēviṭṭa vampiṉa;
   variyirāviṉattaracar palarcoṉālu muṟṟutaliṉ
   makimaiyāṇmai yoppaṉavē lāmuṟ ṟaṭarntaṉa;
   vaḷamaicēr tiruppukaḻai moḻikuvār kaḷukkeḷiya
   mayilamā malaikkumaraṉ vākaippu yaṅkaḷē. (12)
   nēricai veṇpā
   puyalār mayilamalaip puṇṇiyamē pōlvāṉ
   kayalār kaṇvaḷḷi kaṇava—ṉiyalpārap
   pāṭumaṭi yēṉṟaṉakkup pattarmaṉap paṅkayantō
   ṟāṭumaṭi yēṉṟaramāṭṭāṉ. (13)
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalittuṟai
   māṭṭāta veṉṉai valiyavan tāṭkoṇṭu vaṇkamalat
   tāṭṭā maraittoṇṭu ceyyeṉṉa muṉpu taṭuttuniṉṟāṉ
   kēṭṭār makiḻa varaṅkaḷel lāntaruṅ kīrttikoṇṭu
   pāṭṭā luyarmayi lācaṉat tōṅkayiṟ paṇṇavaṉē. (14)

           mativi taṅkulavu maturana yattamiḻ

   iraṅkaṟ ṟāḻicai

   nīpa mutalā mālaikaḷi

   nēricai veṇpā
   vīravaṭi vēṟkai vittaṉai veḷḷimalaik

   kōṭṭumayi lācalattiṟ kumpiṭṭuk koṇṭapiṉṉu
   mīṭṭumayi lāriṭañcel lēṉ.(19)
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalittuṟai
   ēṉalaṅ kāṉiṟ paraṇmītirunta viḷaṅkuṟatti

   nāṉalaṅ kāṇa mayilā calattiṉai naṇṇiṉṉē. (20)
   kali viruttam
   naṇṇal kaṇṭu namakku mayilamvā
   ḻaṇṇa lōrvara māyiṉu nalkilāṉ

   vaṇṇa vāycceyal vaṉmaiyil vaṉmaiyē. (21)
   kalippā - kaikkiḷai
   vaṉṉamulait tuṇaiyai vāymoḻiyait tāḻkuḻalai
   naṉṉayañcēr kaṇṇaimika nampumeṉṉai nattukillāy
   kaṉṉalviṟkai māraṉ kaṭumpōr viḷaikkiṉṟāṉ
   eṉṉaṭināṉ ceyvēṉ eḻiṉmayilat tēntiḻaiyē. (22)
   tāḻicai : neñcuviṭu tūtu

   yēviṉālavar taṅkaḷuk
   kiṉpa meytiṭa nāṭi vāṭi
   yiṟaiñcu vāreḻiṉ vaṇṭiṉam
   pūntuṇarkaṭam pāra mītu
   polinta tēṉmika vuṇṭutaṉ
   puntiyiṉṟi mayaṅku mañcukam
   poṟpu yattuṟu naṟkiḷi
   vēntu ṭaṉkaḷi kūṟu nāraikaṇ
   melleṉappaka rātupō
   mēka mātiya yāvu meṉṟaṉ
   viruppa muṟṟurai yātavē
   nīntu taṟkari tāku meṉṟuyar
   neñca nīyaṟi kiṉṟayā
   ṉīṭu cīrmayi lāca lakkuka
   ṉēri ṉiṉṟu nikaḻttavē. (23)
   nilaimaṇṭila vāciriyappā
   nikaḻttarum perumai nilavuteṉ malayat
   takattiya muṉivark karuḷiya vīṟum

   toṇṭukoṇ ṭavirtaru tūya pukaḻuṅ

   maruṇaiyūr pulavark kaḷittacen tamiḻuṅ
   kaṭaṉaṭu viḻuntu karaimaṭat tavirtaru
   tiṭamati pālaya ṉātiya citta
   reṇṇilāṟ kutavu meḻiṟperuñ ciṟappu
   naṇṇiniṉ ṟaṉpā ṉāṉumvan taṭuttē
   ṉeṉperuṅ kuṟṟami yampari tāyiṉum
   niṉperuṅ karuṇai niṉaikkarum pāṟṟāṟ
   ṟavirkkumeṉ ṉuṉṉit tamiḻkoṭu ciṟitu
   kavittoṭai vaṉaiyak karutiṉa ṉātali
   ṉaiñiṟak kalāpat taṇikkuṭai paṟikku
   maññaivā kaṉattiṉ marakata vaṇṇak
   kiḷḷaiyōr pākaṅ kiḷartara vorupāl
   veḷḷaimāl kaḷiṟu mēṉāḷ vaḷartta

   luṉṉiya patiṉāṟuruviloṉ ṟeṭuttuc
   cēvalun taṇṭun tirukkai vēlum
   pāvalar paṉuval pakarvaṉa piṟavum
   tikaḻtarum vantaruṭ cēvai nalkip
   pukaḻoṭu tavamum poruntavait taruḷvāy
   teṉṉaiyu māvuñ ceruntiyuṅ kuruntaiyum
   puṉṉaiyum piṟavum politaru cāraṟ
   kuyiliṉam pāṭak kuraṅkiṉaṅ koṭṭa
   mayiliṉa māṭum vaḷantikaḻ mayila
   māmalaik kōyil vayiṉiṉi tamaruṅ
   kōmaḷak kumara kuruparak kukaṉē.(24)
   nēricai veṇpā
   kukaṉē mayilamalaik kōmāṉē yīcaṉ
   makaṉē yeṉattiṉamum vāḻtti—mukamāṟum
   paṉṉiraṇṭu tōḷum patamiraṇṭu nāṭuṇarmu

   kōḷvilakku mayilamalaik kōyi lāṉē

           variyacorupāṉantaṉār
   paṇikoḷpara māṉanta patamoḻiya vēṟiṅkōr
           pataniṉaivar nīḷcintainōy
   taṇiyaviru pātaṅka ṭalaimicaiyi lētanta
           takavainiṉai yāvantarē.(14)
   vēṟu
   vantavara lāṟoṉṟu maṟiyāy
   maṇṭupulaṉārkoṇṭu celavē
   pantaneṟi yēceṉṟu maṉaṉē
   paṅkamuṟa nīmaṅki yaḻiyā
   ventaiperu māṉiṉpa vaṭivāy
   niṉcoru pāṉanta ṉirutāḷ
   cintaimaya lālvanta pavanōy
   cintumvakai nīceṉṟu ceṟiyē. (15)
   patiṉāṟu cīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi yāciriyac canta viruttam
   ceṟivuṟu maṭiyār cintai ceṟintaṉa
   ciṉamuṭaṉ matamāṉaṅkaḷ kaḷaintaṉa
   ceyamaka ṭirunā maṅkai poruntiṉa
   ticaiyoru patucūḻ niṉṟu potintaṉa
   viravaṟu paramāṉantam viḷaintaṉa
   veṉatita yamelā muṇṭu tikaḻntaṉa
   vikapara meṉavē ṟiṉṟi yicaintaṉa
   vituveṉi ṉatutā ṉaṉṟi yoḻintaṉa
   pirivaṟu poruṭara niṉṟu niṟaintaṉa
   piramaṉō ṭaraṉmā leṉṟu pakuntaṉa
   perumaṟai palavā niṉṟu pukaḻntaṉa
   piṟaviyiṉ mutuvē riṉṟu piṭuṅkiṉa
   varivayar veṉalā yaṉṟi yiruntaṉa
   vakamita meṉumāl poṉṟa vaṇaintaṉa
   vaticaya mikumā ṟeṅka ṇuṟaintaṉa
   vaḻakiya corupāṉantar puyaṅkaḷē.(16)
   eṇcīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   puyam poruntanaṭu vēniṉṟu nīmakaḷ
           pulampa loṉṟumaṟi yāyaṉṟu kāṇavar
   tiyampa kaṉpiramaṉ māleṉṟu tēṭiya
           tiṟaṅkaṭan tacorupāṉantar kāṇavar
   vayampu kuntavara lāṟaṉpar pālaruḷ
           varuntukiṉṟatoḻi vāyiṉṟu nāḷaiyu
   miyampukiṉṟaveḻil vētaṅka ṭāṉuṇar
           viṟantu niṉṟavuṇar vēyeṉṟu mēṉiyē. (17)
   ituvumatu
   mēṉi vēṟumaṟi vēyeṉṟa tēyeṉa
           vēta moḻivuṟu mēniṉṟu tēṭunar
   vāṉiṉimaiyava rēkaṇṭu nāmika
           vāḻa nilaṉmicai yēvantu tāḻpavar
   ūṉil varupava nōyiṉṟi yēyiṭa
           vōrcoṉ moḻikuva rēpanta nīḷviṉai
   yāṉi ceyumava rēyiṉpa mēyaru
           ḷāki ṉavarcorupāṉanta nātarē.(18)
   koccakam
   taraiyataṉmēl vantē talaiyataṉmē luṉṟā
   ṭuraiyānī tantē cuṭarcorupāṉantā
   puraiyaṟavē niṉṟāy pulaṉaṟiyā voṉṟē
   yuraiyuṇarvā loṉṟā luṇarvaṟiyā yiṉarē. (19)
   kaliviruttam
   iṉṟu ḷēniṉṟi lēleṉat tōṟṟara
   niṉṟu ḷēṉaḻi yāvakai nēyamō
   ṭiṉṟu ḷēcoru pāṉantar vāymoḻi
   yoṉṟu ḷēpara māṉanta lōkamē. (20)
   eḻucīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   kamaiyāl vanta tuḷavāṉ muntu
           kalaiyā loṉṟu maṉaṉālō
   ramaiṉāl viñcu maṟivā lentai
           yaruṭā ṉiṉṟa viṭiṉākā
   temaiyāḷ kiṉṟa corupāṉanta
           ṉirupā taṅka ṭarilōrkā
   limaiyā toṉṟil viḻiyā teṉṟu
           miṟavā voṉṟai yuṟavāmē.(21)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalittuṟai
   uṟavē yuyirkaṭ kuyirē yorupaṟṟu maṟṟuniṉṟa
   tuṟavē cuṭarccoru pāṉanta ṉētuyar tīrakkaṇṭa
   tiṟavē yaḻivupaṭāccelva mēyeṉṟun tittikkiṉṟa
   naṟavē ciṟiyē ṉuraippatev vāṟuṉai ñālattilē. (22)
   iruvikaṟpa nēricai veṇpā
   ñālam pukuntaruḷi nāyēṉai yāṭkoṇṭa
   cīlam pukaḻumā ceykaṇṭāy—kōlaṅkoṇ
   ṭāṇṭāṉē māṟā varuṭcorupāṉantaṉē
   vēṇṭā vaṭiyēṟku vēṟu.(23)
   ammāṉai
   vēṟoṉ ṟeṉumanta vēṟeṅkē yammāṉai
           vēṟoṉṟumillai veṟumaṟivē yammāṉai
   pēṟeṉpa tippaṭiyē pēṟaṉṟō vammāṉai
           pēriṉpam pēcum vakaiyeṉṉō vammāṉai
   kūṟuñ curutikaḷiṉ kūṟeṅkē yammāṉai
           kūṟāmaṟ kūṟiyavar koṇṭāṭā yammāṉai
   yāṟun tiṟantāṉvan tēṟuṅkā ṇammāṉai
           yaṇṇalcoru pāṉanta ṉampoṟṟā ḷammāṉai. (24)
   eḻucīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   māṉā kuttuṇai viḻiyā kiṉṟaṉa
           mayilā miṅkiva riyaṟāṉun
   tēṉā ruṅkuḻaṉ mukilā ṉaṉṟicey
           tiruvā miṅkiva rurunērē
   pāṉā ṟuṅkuḻaṉ moḻiyā meṉṟiṭar
           paṭuvār tammoṭu taṭumāṟā
   yāṉā raṉpoṭu corupā ṉantartam
           mirupā tantoḻu tiṭuvēṉē. (25)
   kaliviruttam
   iṭuvār yāreṉ ṟēcaṟu kiṉṟā rippōtik
   keṭuvār mēluṅ kīṭa marikkuṅ koppattār
   naṭuvā niṉṟā ṉañcoru pāṉan taṉṟāḷiṟ
   paṭuvā rallā riṉṉamu mītō paṭuvārē.(26)
   kalinilaittuṟai
   vārāy varuvā yorumā ṉuṭaṉāki yeṉṉu
   nīrā yuruku neñciṉu miṅkiva ṇekkunekkuc
   cōrā veḻumeṉ corupā ṉantac cōtiyeṉṉum
   pērā yirattāṉai niṉaintivaḷ peṟṟa pēṟē. (27)
   eṇcīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   peṟuva tiṉiyoru pēṟeṉko lōvaru
           piṟavi yaṟumitil ñāṉantaṉ mēṉaṉi
   ceṟiva tiṉiyuṟa vētiṉpa vārkaṭal
           ceṟiya varuḷcoru pāṉantar tāḷala
   tuṟuva tiṉiyētu vēṟeṉpi ṉāṉaka
           muruku maṭiyava rōṭoṉṟi vāḻtali
   ṉaṟiva tiṉiyētu vōvoṉṟu vēṟaṟa
           vaṭaiya namaturu vāyniṉṟa tākilē. (28)
   eṇcīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   niṉṟunilai yātumpar vāḻvaṅku nērā
           neñcukulai yāniṉṟu vāḻimpar vāḻvō
   viṉṟunilai yākiṉṟa vāḻveṉ ṟirātē
           yeṉṟumuḷar pōliṅku vāḻkiṉ ṟuḷīrkā
   ḷoṉṟunilai yāteṉ ṟīreṅku māyvō
           roṉṟumila tāvoṉṟu māvaṉṟu māyē
   yeṉṟunilai yāviṉpa mākiṉṟa tāṉī
           rentaicoru pāṉantar pātanto ḻīrē. (29)
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalippā
   toḻuvār viṉaitīrttaruḷuñ corupāṉanta(ṉa)ṉṟō
   ṭaḻuvā veṉamuṉa tāyiṉu nīven taḻalāyē
   yeḻuvā yōtaṇ matiyē yeṉṉē yeṉṉēpōy
   viḻuvār mēlē taṭikoṭu ceṉṟāy vēṇṭātē. (30)
   ituvumatu
   vēṇṭā tēneñca maṭiyā ruṭaṉaṉṟi mēvat
   tīṇṭā tuceṉṉi yiṟaicē vaṭiyaṉṟi yeṉṉāt
   tūṇṭā vilaṅkucorupāṉantac cōti yeṉṉai
   yāṇṭāṉaiyaṉṟi yuraiyāṭa vaṟinti rātē.(31)
   puyavakuppu
   iravupaka luyirkaḻiyu mākaṇṭu vāṉavarka
   ḷeriyaṉaiya piṟaviyati lēniṉṟu māḷumema
   teḷivaravu keṭaviṉiya pātaṅka ṇīyaruḷi
   yituvateṉa vorucoṉmoḻi yāyeṉṟu tāḻuvatu;
   muriyapaḻa vaṭiyarveḷi yēkaṇṭu vāḻvatuvu
   mulakaroru varumaṟiyu māṟiṉṟi māḷuvatu
   mupaniṭata maṭaiyuneṟi yēniṉṟu pāṭuvatu
   muruvoṭaru valatōrporu ḷāmeṉṟu kūṟuvatu ;
   niraticaya paramacuka mēyeṉṟu nāṭuvatu
   niṉaiyavuri yavarkaḷpiṟa vāreṉṟu pēcuvatu
   nikaḻvaṭivu karuṇaiyeṉa vēniṉṟu vōtuvatu
   niṟaivukuṟai vaṟumamirta māyaṉpar mūḻkuvatu ;
   maraṉoṭari yayaṉamarar kōṉiṉṟu tēṭuvatu
   makilapuvaṉamumuṭaiya kōṉeṉṟu pēṇuvatu
   maṟucamaya vittipala vātaṅko ṭāṭuvatu
   maṟivileṉuḷē pukutucoru pāṉanta nātaṉaiyē. (32)
   aṟucīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   taṉaiyārun teriyā teṉṟu taṭumāṟum paṭitā ṉoṉṟu
   niṉaiyāviṅ kuruvāy vantu neṟitāṉoṉ ṟaṟiyā vaṉpi
   ṉeṉaiyāḷuñ corupāṉanta ṉirupātan talaimēl koṇṭu
   puṉaiyāvan takamē ṉiṉṟu pulaiyāṭum pulaṉā mañcē. (33)
   ūcal
   accamelā maṟuttāṇṭā ṉeṉṟāṭā mōvūca
           lāṇṭaruḷun tiṟampāṭi yāṭāmō vūcal
   mecciyavaṉ kaḻalparaviyāṭāmō vūcal
           veṟumaṟivē yāṉēmeṉ ṟāṭāmō vūcal
   liccakattē meyccorupā ṉantaṉeṉat tōṉṟi
           yiṉpaveḷḷat taḻuttiyiṭa veṉṉaiyumāṭ koṇṭa
   piccaṉeṉa vumpāṭi yāṭāmō vūcal
           peruṅkaruṇaip perumāṉeṉ ṟāṭāmō vūcal. (34)
   aṟucīrāciriya viruttam
   ūcalāṭumiv vuṭaluyi ritutaṉai yorupoḻu toḻiyātē
   pūca lāṭiṭu poṟipulaṉ maṉaṉoṭu puṇarvuṟu kuṇaṉmuṟṟun
   tēca lātaṉa citaivakai yuruvoṭu nikaḻcorupāṉantā
   pēcalāmaḷa valaveṉa tuḷamvaru perukiya varuṭāṉē. (35)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalittuṟai
   tāṉē vaṇaṅkumeṉ ceṉṉiyeṉ ṉāvuṉaic cāṟṟutoṟu
   mūṉē yuruku murōmañ cilirkku mukumeṉkaṇṇīr
   nāṉē tumoṉṟu maṟikiṉ ṟilēṉveṟu ñāṉakaṉat
   tēṉē yaruṭcorupāṉanta nītanta celviṉaiyē. (36)
   eḻucīrāciriya viruttam
   viṉaivaḷai kaḻaṉṟu puṉaikalai kulaintu
           viḻiyaruvi niṉṟu coriyavē
   niṉaivu piṟitiṉṟi nicipaka latiṉṟi
           niṉaiyanikaḻ kiṉṟa corupātōḷ
   viṉaivaḷai kaḻaṉṟu puṉaikalai kulaintu
           viḻiyaruvi niṉṟu coriyavē
   niṉaivu piṟitiṉṟi nicipaka latiṉṟi
           niṉaivumayi leṉṟu puṇarvatē. (37)
   eṇcīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   puṇṇiyamavaṉ koṇṭatō rākamē
           pōta mēcorupāṉanta rākamē
   yaṇṇi yaṉati tūratta ṉallaṉē
           yaṉparāṉavark kēyatta ṉallaṉē
   maṇṇi yaṅku kāliṉīr vāṉumāy
           vaitta ḷittu maṟaittiṭu vāṉumāyp
   paṇṇi yumaḻit tumviḷai yāṭumē
           paḻaiya nāṉmaṟai yēyurai yāṭumē. (38)
   nēricai yāciriyappā
   uraicey maṉaṉē uraicey maṉaṉē
   karaiyi lācaik kaṭaliṭaip paṭṭuk
   kalaṅkit tiraiyiṉ malaṅkiyalain talaintu
   paṉṉeṭu nāḷāp pāriṭai vicumpiṭai
   maṉṉupa yāviṉum vantuḻi vantuḻi
   yōrō ruruvi ṉuṭaleṭut taḻintatu
   pārē ḻaṉṟē paḻuveṉpu pakari
   ṉaṭṭa kiriyi ṉeṭṭaṭuk kuṭaittē
   kuḻaviyiṟ parukiya ceḻumulaip pāliv
   veḻukaṭa lataṉi ṉeḻumai yataṉṟē
   malartalai yulakiṟ palapala mākkaṭkut
   tāyeṉa vīṉṟun tantaiyeṉa vaḷittuñ
   cēyeṉap piṟantun tēvareṉap pakaittuṅ
   kaṇavareṉap puṇarntun tuṇaivareṉac ceṟintu
   mōrōr yōṉiyi ṉuṟṟatum pirintatum
   pārēḻ cūḻkaṭaṟ paṭutirai tokutta
   nuṇmaṇaṟ pakariṉu meṇvakai yaritē
   yivvakai piṟantiṟan tiyaipuḻi yavvayiṉ
   kūṭiya poruḷkaḷai nāṭuva taritē
   kalaviyiṟ kaḻaṟiya tellaiya tilaiyē
   maṟumai yaṉṟi maṟṟavai yaṉaittum
   piṟitu nīyē yaṟiti yaṉṟē
   yaṉṉa taṉmaiyai yaṟintu miṉṉu
   meṉṉati yāṉeṉ ṟicaintu maṉṉō
   vaṭivu meḻilum vaṉappuṟu kalviyuṅ
   kuṭiyun tiruvoṭu kulaṉu mikuvatōr
   nāmamuñ cumantu nāmpiṟa veṉṟu
   cāmaḷa vuñcaḻak kuraittup pōmitu
   taṉṉā ṉintap paṉṉeṭuṅ kāla
   meṉṉī peṟṟaṉai yiyampē yaṉṉō
   payaṉila ceytu kaḻiyā tayaṉara
   ṉintira ṉeṭumā liṉṉamu niṟpac
   cinturac cēvaṭi yantarat tiṉṟit
   tīṇṭavip puviyiṟ cīraṭiyārkaḷai
   yāṇṭukoṇ ṭaruḷvō rantaṇa ṉāki
   vantaruḷ corupā ṉantaṉaic
   cintaicey yiṭaruṟu pantavī ṭaṟumē.(39)
   eṇcīrāciriya viruttam
   aṟaiyāniṉ ṟiṭuñcuruti yaṟaiyu māṟu
           maṟivōmeṉ ṟaṟaiyavarka ḷaṟiyu māṟuñ
   ciṟaiyākum piṟaviciṟaic ceyyu māṟuñ
           cilaiyākuñ cintaicilai yāku māṟu
   maṟaiyāniṉ ṟiṭumāyai maṟaikku māṟu
           matiyālaṉṟākumeṉa vakukkap paṭṭa
   tuṟaiyāṟum paṭumāṟu maṟiya lākuñ
           corupāṉan taṉṟiruntāḷ cūṭu ñāṉṟē. (40)
   eḻucīrāciriya viruttam
   ñāṉṟavā ḷaruvi yiruviḻi coriya
           nāṉeṉai yēyati cayippa
   vāṉṟaṉmē lamarar viyappuṟa maṇṇōr
           mattaṉuṉ mattaṉeṉ ṟuraippat
   tōṉṟanī vantiṅ keṉṉaiyāṇ ṭavaṉē
           corupāṉan taccuṭarc cōti
   cāṉṟatāy niṉṟa vuruvamē tārāy
           tamiyaṉēṉ maruḷaṭut tāḷē. (41)
   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilā ciriya viruttam
   maruḷālvaṉ ṟaṟiyēnaṉ ṟaṟiyēṉā mataṉāl
           mataṉāraiṅ kaṇaiyānaṉ kaṇaiyāneñcuruḷac
   curuḷāraṅ kuḻalārcoṟ kuḻalārnañ cuḻalac
           cuḻalāṟiṟ cuḻalāṟiṟ cuḻalvēṉōr poruḷāp
   poruḷāmeṉ ṟaruṇāṭum paruṇāṭum pukalaip
           pukalvāreñ corupāṉan taṉtāḷā ṉeṉṟeṉ
   ṟaruḷāṉaru ḷāṉamey yaṉpālmey yaṉpālē
           (paṟṟār)paṟṟaṟṟār vētaṉaiyē tumpeṟṟārvētaṉaiyē. (42)
   koccakak kalippā
   vētanā yakaṉā reṉṉuṇ mēviya
   nātaṉār corupāṉantar nāṇmalarp
   pāta mētuti yāmoḻip palapiṟap
   pōta vēlai yolitaru mōcaiyē. (43)
   eṇcīr āciriyaviruttam
   ōcai tantamirta nāvanta tōvā
           tōti yoṉṟupala vāmeṉṟu vēṟu
   pēcu nuṅkaḷaḷa vēyeṅkaḷ vāṉōr
           pēṇu kiṉṟacorupāṉanta ṉārtāḷ
   vāca kaṅkaṇmaṉa ṉōṭuṅkaḷ vāṇāṇ
           māḷu meṅkaḷuṭa ṉēyiṉṟa ṭānīr
   pūca leṉṟuvaru pōteṅkaḷ kūrvāḷ
           pōtam vantavaḻi yēyiṉṟu pōmē.(44)
   eṇcīrāciriya viruttam
   iṉṟavā vituvāka yāṉēyā ṉākaviṉi
           yeṉceyalō vivaiyāka vimaippaḷavu moṉṟi
   niṉṟavā tillāta neñceṉakku neñcā
           niṉaivaritā yituveṉavōr neṟiyumataṟ kiṉṟi
   yaṉṟuvā ṉavarkaḷaru maṟaiyiṉuṭaṉ ṟēṭaṟ
           kariyavoru poruḷākum poruḷaiyā ṉuñceṉ
   ṟoṉṟuvā ṉeṟiyuṇṭō corupāṉan taccōti
           yuṭaiyatiru vuḷattiṉaru ḷuṇṭāki ṉuṇṭē. (45)
   aṟucīrāciriya viruttam
   uṇṭumil laiyuma tāki
           yuruvamiṅ karuva mākip
   paṇṭoṭiṉ ṟaṭaiya vākip
           palavoṭoṉ ṟākit tāṉē
   koṇṭakoṇ ṭaḷava tākik
           kuṟikkoṇāt tiṟatta tāki
   yaṇṭaṉeṉ corupāṉanta
           ṉaṭiyiṇai yalarnta vāṟē.(46)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalittuṟai
   alarāyiṉ ceyyu maimpulaṉ karaṇam maṟuvakaiyāṅ
   kalarkāḷ palkuṇaṅ kāṇamalaṅ kāḷkaraṇaṅkaḷeṉṉum
   palarkā ḷaruṭcoru pāṉanta ṉārpava ḷattiruvāy
   malarā taḷavumaṉ ṟōveṅka ḷōṭuṅkaḷ vāḻvukaḷē. (47)
   eṇcīrāciriya viruttam
   vāḻum patanī caṅkaraṉ mālpū
           vāḻkiṉ ṟavaṉī yumparkaḷ kōṉī
   cūḻun ticainī kālamu nīmā
           tūlaṅ kaḷunī nērmaiyu nīyē
   vīḻum pavanī vīṭatu nīyāṉ
           mēvuñ corupā ṉantave ṉeñcan
   tāḻu miṟainī yentuṇai nīyen
           tāyeṉ pavaṉī tantaiyu nīyē.(48)
   eṇcīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   tantaiyoṭu tāymaintar muṉpiṉuṭaṉ vantār
           tāramoṭu pērarmiku kūṟumuṟa vākum
   pantameṉa vēvanta tontamivai yoṉṟiṟ
           paṭṭatala kāṇumivai viṭṭuviṭu nērā
   vantamuta ṟāṉaṉṟi yeṉṟumuḷa tāṉē
           yaṇṭamuta lāyaṉṟi niṉṟaporu ṇāmā
   vintavula kēvantu tantaruḷu māṟā
           tentaicorupāṉanta ṉeṉṟumoḻi yīrē. (49)
   aṟucīrāciriya viruttam
   moḻikilīr corupāṉantar
           moykaḻal veyya vāḻkkai
   kaḻikilī riraṅki yāḷuṅ
           karuṇaiyai niṉaintu kaṇṇīr
   poḻikilīr pulavīr poyyē
           poruḷalā tavaraip pōṟṟa
   loḻikilī ravamē yuṅka
           ḷuraiyelā mukuttī raṉṟē.(50)
   patiṉāṉkucīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   kuttirama tāvulakar piccaiyiṭa veṉṟulakil
   kūṟiyaruḷ vēṟu paṭumak
   kuṟattiyal nāyakar tiṟattiṉvaḻu vātavaruḷ
   kūṟumavaḷ vēṟu niṉaivā
   mittaṉaivi ṭāyniṉaivai viṭṭuniṉai yāyaṟivi
   ṟantiṭa ṉirunta paṭipā
   reṅkumaṟi vēyiṉi yakampuṟa miṟantiṭa
   viruntuvi ṭirunta paṭiyē
   cuttamaṟai yōtavaṟi yātaporu ṭaṉṉaiveḷi
   coṉṉapaṭi yuṉṉi namaiyā
   ṭuyyacorupāṉantar ceyyakaḻa lōtoru
   tuvaṭciyuḷ colli viṭavō
   vettiyeṉai yāḷuṭaiya nāyakaṉu meṉpaṇi
   picaintu koḷumō veṉumatē
   yeppaṭiniṉaintaṉaikolappaṭiya tākumiti
   ṉētumoru pōtu milaiyē.(51)
   aṟucīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   ilatuḷa tikapara mitamaka miruḷveḷi
           yiḻipuyar peṉumellā
   malatala vaiyala vaṟivala vayarvala
           vamararu maṟivāṉōr
   valatila taruḷvaṭi voṭuvari ṉalateṉuḷ
           varucoru pāṉanta
   ṉalatiru vuruviṉa teṉumoḻi paṉuvalka
           ṇāṭutal kūṭātē. (52)
   kalippā
   kūṭātē yavaṉaṭiyār kuḻāttaiyavaṉ kuraikaḻaṟṟāḷ
   cūṭātē yeṉaiyāṇṭa corupāṉan taccuṭaraip
   pāṭātē yavantanta paramāṉan takkaṭalpuk
   kāṭātē yiṉṉeṭunā ḷeṉceytē ṉāṉēṉē. (53)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalippā
   āṉēṉatunā ṉeṉṟiṅ kōtu maḷavēyā
   yūṉē vaṭivā mavarō ṭuḻalā vakaineñcē
   tāṉē corupā ṉantaṉ kamalat tāḷvantuṉ
   pāṉēr paṭamā tavamēṉ ceytāy pakarāyē.(54)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalittuṟai
   pakarāniṉ ṟēṉevark kuñcorupāṉanta paṇṭaimaṟaic
   cikarālai yattumeṉ ceṉṉiyu maṉṉuñ ceḻuṅkamalac
   cukarāci yaiyeṉṟu mēttit toḻumiṉṉun tolpiṟavi
   makarālai yattai vaṟaḷākka nalla maruntituvē.(55)
   nēricai veṇpā
   maruntā mavaṉpeyarai vāḻttukilē miṉpan
   taruntā ḷiṇaicārēñ cāra—viruntu
   morupāka millē muṇarvilēm yāmāñ
   corupāṉan taṉkaḻaṟkut toṇṭu. (56)
   vañci viruttam
   toṇṭar vāḻcorupāṉanta ṉārpataṅ
   kaṇṭa nāṇmutal kaṇkaḷu nīrnilā
   paṇṭai yāḷala ḷeṉṟupal kālumāk
   koṇṭal kāḷmoḻi yīrkoṭi yēṉaiyē. (57)
   aṟucīrāciriya viruttam
   koṭiya viṉaiyāṉ maṅkaimār
           kuḻaṟu moḻiyē koṇṭunī
   muṭiya viṅṅaṉē niṉṟupoy
           muṟikai matikē ṭeṉṟīrā
   yaṭiyar pavanōy maṅkavē
           yaruḷu corupāṉantaṉār
   paṭiyiṉ micaiyē vantatāḷ
           paṇiya niṉaiyāy neñcamē. (58)
   ituvumatu
   neñca maṟiyat taṉvaṭivai
           nikaḻtti neṭunā ḷuṭaṉāyat
   tuñcal piṟatta laṟacceyta
           corupā ṉantar tuṇaiyaṭipōl
   vañca maṟukka vaṟṟāyō
           maṇamuṇ pāyō matiyātē
   kañca malarkoṇ ṭuvamikkak
           karutiṟ ṟeṉṉō kaṟṟavarē.(59)
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭiyāciriya viruttam
   kaṟṟavarkaḷāvāruṅ karuttuṭaiyā rāvāruṅ
           karutiṟ ṟellām
   peṟṟavarkaḷāvārum piṟantavarkaḷāvārum
           pīṭu cērnta
   naṟṟavarka ḷāvārum ñāṉikaṭā māvāru
           naṉmai yellān
   tuṟṟavarka ḷāvāruñ corupāṉan tarkkaṭimait
           toḻilcey vārē.(60)
   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   toḻilava raṭiyiṇai toḻalā veṉṟaṉ
           collavā tiṟaṉvaru tutiyā veṉṟalai
   viḻilavaraṭiyā raṭiyār paṅkaya
           viraimala raṭiyiṇai micaiyā vaṉpuko
   ṭaḻiṉavar piṟavika ḷaṟavē taṉtiru
           varuḷoru vaṭipukoḷ corupā ṉantarta
   meḻilpurai tiruvuru vituvē yaṉṟiyō
           rikapara mitamaka milatāy niṉṟatē.(61)
   eḻucīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   niṉṟataṭi yārtaṅka ṇeñciṉiṭai yēvañca
           neñcamuṭai yārkaḷ virakē
   veṉṟataṭi yāraṉpa raṉparaṭi yārtaṅkaḷ
           vempiṟavi yāṉa pakaiyē
   koṇṭataka lavarnaṉṟi niṉṟutaṭu māṟavaruḷ
           koṇṭuviḷai yāṭi yicaiyā
   veṉṟateṉai yēyiṅkē ṉentaicoru pāṉanta
           ṉiṉpaviru pātaṅkaḷē.(62)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalippā
   pātan talaiyiṟ patittēṉ pavaṅkaḷainta
   nātaṉ paraṉcoru pāṉantaṉ paraṅkaruṇaik
   kētaṅkaṭ ceviyāṟ kēṭkun toṟumeṉṉā
   vōtun toṟumuruki nīrāyu rukumūṉē.(63)
   eṇcīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   ūṉvaru perunōy tāṉviṭa viṟaipā
           lukutaru maṉpāy mikutava muṟavē
   vāṉvaru mimaiyōr kāṉavar nilaṉē
           vantaruḷ corupā ṉantartam malarcēr
   tēṉvaru mirutā ḷiṇaimala reṉṟaṉ
           ceṉṉiyiṉ micaiyē yiṉṉa morukā
   ṟāṉvarum vakaipōy nīpaṇi yāyē
           tārkuḻal vaṇṭē varikaḻal kaṇṭē.(64)
   kalinilaittuṟai
   kaṇṭē corupāṉantaṉ pātaṅ kāṇārpōl
   viṇṭē yuḻalvēṉ meyyaṭiyāravar pōlaṉpu
   koṇṭē ṉeṉavuṅ kūṟā niṉṟē ṉeṉṉaippō
   luṇṭō vuṭaiyā ṉoṭukai tavamiṅ kuṭaiyārē. (65)
   eṇcīrāciriya viruttam
   uṭaiyāṉe ṉavuvanta corupāṉanta
           ṉuruvāyvan tirukkavavaṉ ṟiruvā caṟkōr
   taṭaiyāṉa taṟṟirukka vaḻivoṉ ṟillāt
           tāṉākat taṉaiyaṭaivārk karuḷā niṉṟa
   koṭaiyāṉa tavaṉaṭiyāraṭiyē yākak
           kūṭuvatum pāṭuvatuṅ kuvalai yattōr
   kiṭaiyātu kiṭaittirukka vavamē tāmpōyk
           keṭukiṉṟāravamākkap paṭukiṉ ṟārē. (66)
   oruvikaṟpa veṇpā
   paṭakkaṭava tettaṉaiyum paṭṭēṉik kaṭṭa
   viṭakkoḻiyum vaṇṇam viḷampāy—kaṭaṟkaruṇai
   māmukilē yāḷa varucorupāṉantāveṉ
   ṟīmalaiyāñ cintainōy tīr. (67)
   vañci viruttam
   tīrtta māvatuñ ceytava māvatu
   mārtta māvatu maruccaṉai yāvatuñ
   cērtta māvatu meṉcorupāṉantaṉ
   vārttai kēṭkai matiyē matikkoḷē. (68)
   aṟucīrāciriya viruttam
   matikkai matikē ṭeṉṟīra
           malappo tiyainī reṉṟunī
   rutittu mariyā niṉṟatō
           yoḻikka niṉaiyā niṉṟilīrk
   katikku neṟiyē teṉpīrēl
           karuttiṉ mikunē yaṅkoṇṭē
   tutittap poḻutē cintainōy
           tuṭaippar corupā ṉantarē.(69)
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalippā
   pāṉantiya cuvaiyōcoru pāṉantaṉa tiṉpan
   tāṉoṉṟiya virucēvaṭi tarumiṉcuvai maturat
   tēṉoṉṟiya cuvaiyōtirai yamirtantaru cuvaiyō
   ñāṉantaru cuvaiyōvetu nāṭumperu nalaṉē.(70)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalittuṟai
   nalaṉā kuvatunaṉ ñāṉama tāvatu naṉmaimikuṅ
   kulaṉā kuvatuṅ kurampaikoṇ ṭārivar koṇṭukoṇṭa
   palaṉā kuvatu paṇitantu ceyta ṟaṇittaṉeñcē
   nalaṉā varucorupāṉanta nātaṉal laṉparukkē.(71)
   nilaimaṇṭila vāciriyappā
   parutiyu matiyu meṉumivai vittip
   paṭupū taṅka ḷuṟṟa vāruyir
   palapala vulakam viraviya piramaṉ
   mutalā vantu titta yōṉikaḷ
   pakarvuṟu ticaika ṇicipakaṉ mutala
   varukā laṅkaṇ muṟṟum vēṟoru
   paramaṟa vematu vaṭiveṉa varuḷi
   viḷaiyā ṭeṉṟu viṭṭa tēcuṟu
   curutiyiṉ muṭivu maṟaivuṟa variya
   corupāṉanta nitta mākiya
   tuṇaiyaṭi toḻutu tuticeytu mikavu
   maruciyā niṉṟa citta rēmaṇi
   tuḷava ṉōṭaya ṉaraṉa ruḷiya
   patamā kiṉṟa kuttira vāḻvukaḷ
   cukamala tiriya varumvaḻi paḻaiya
   kuruṭē naṉṟu ṉiṭṭa mēturai
   karutiya taritu maḻivaru poḻutu
   milatā kiṉṟa citti yēpala
   kalaikaḷi ṉalaiyi ṉalavatu viṉavi
   yeṉaiyāḷ kiṉṟa vatta ṉāraruṭ
   kaḻaliṇai toḻutu vaḻipaṭa vavarta
   maruḷāl vanta vittai kāṇvaru
   karuvaṟu kuḷikai yitumuṉa moruvā
   kiṭaiyā toṉṟu kiṭṭi ṉāymika
   varititu parama kuruvaru ḷuṭaiya
   paramā ṉanta citta ṉāyiru
   varupara ṉamaru maḻakiya karuṇai
   yitiṉmū laṅko ṭoppi lātava
   ṉatiraca muṭaiya vaṭiyavar tiraḷai
   mikavē kaṇṭuti ruṭṭaṇai yāṉava
   raruḷiya vamirta kirutaku ḷacakita
   mayilavē meṉṟu miṭṭa mākavē. (72)
   aṟucīrc canta viruttam
   āka muṭaiyī ruṅkaṇmē
           lāṭu pulaṉā vaintumā
   nākam naliyā tintiramā
           ñāla pavamē vantuṟā
   cōka meṉunō yiṅkuṟā
           tūya corupā ṉantartā
   māka varupā taṅkaṇmē
           lācai yorupō toṉṟilē.(73)
   koccakak kalippā
   pōtā taṉaṉmā lāṉē yeṉiṉum poyyillā
   vētā kamameyp poruṭā meykaṇ ṭavarēṉu
   nātā veṉaiyā ḷuṭaiyāy corupā ṉantāveṉ
   ṟōtā tavarmēṉ maiyelā moṉṟuk kuṭaṉaṉṟē.(74)
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   uṭalai yārttavaim pūtamaṭṭaṉṟeṉak
           kōraḷa vaṟumācaik
   kaṭalai yārttativ vaṭavaiyāṅ kāmakō
           pātiveṅ kaṉaṉmaṇṭuñ
   cuṭalai yārttavoṇ cilataruḷ vaṭivoṭu
           cuṭarccoru pāṉantar
   muṭalai yākkaiko ṭallaṉvāṉ muṭipunāṇ
           moḻitantaruḷ ceyyāyē.(75)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalittuṟai
   ceytavan tāṉillaic cīlamoṉ ṟāṉillai
           mālkoṇeñciṟ
   kaitava moṉṟillaik kāmaru pūṅkaḻaṟ
           kāmaḷavu
   naitalun tāṉillai ñāṉamoṉ ṟālillai
           yānaṭuvē
   yeytaveṉ ṟālvaru mōcoru pāṉanta
           ṉiṉṉaruḷē. (76)
   iruvikaṟpa nēricai veṇpā
   aruṇāṟu memperumā ṉāka maḻiyāp
   poruṇāṟu meṉṉuḷ varumpōtan—tiruvāyeṉ
   vāṇāḷē nāṟum varuñcorupāṉantaṉmalart
   tāṇāṟu meṉpuṉ ṟalai.(77)
   nēricai yāciriyappā
   eṉpuṉ ṟalaimicai yiṇaiyaṭik kamala
   maṉpuṟa vaḷikka veṉṟē muṉpu
   corupāṉantat tirumā muṉivaṉ
   ceṟintula kiṭaiyiṟ ceyta cēvaka
   maṟintaṉa ṉaṟintaṉa ṉamma! ceṟinta
   vāyiṟ kōṭi yaṉalkaṉaṉ ṟuyirkkum
   vāyiṉ veyya maṟantika ḻaimpulak
   kottoru moḻipaṭak kōḷarak kaṉciram
   pattoru kaṇaippaṭut tiṉaiyē cutta
   nañcamu muṇṭaṉai nampa! nāyēṉ
   vañca neñcam vāriyuṇ pataṟkē
   cilaipeṇ ṇākac cēvaṭi cērttiṉai
   nilaipeṟa veṉṉai nīyākku taṟkē
   varaittaṉu vāka vaḷaittaṉai
   karaippatu karutiyeṉ maṉapperuṅ kallē.(78)
   eṇcīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   kallaiyallā luvamaiyillā maṉaṉkaṉiyuṅ kālaṅ
           kātalelā mīcaṉukkē kūravaruṅ kālan
   tollaiyilā kiyapiṟavi tolaintuviṭuṅ kālañ
           corupāṉan taccuṭartāṉ ṟōṉṟavaru kālañ
   collavilā vorucukamā yeṉilvē ṟoṉṟun
           tōṉṟāta tiruvaṭiyeṉ ṟalaimē lēṟi
   yellaiyillā viṉpaveḷḷaṅ karaiyaḻiyuṅ kāla
           meṉṉaiyumāṇ ṭaruḷpurivā ṉeḻuntaruḷun kālam. (79)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalippā
   kālampala taraniṉkaḻal kāṇuntiṟa ṉēteṉṟāṉ
   mālampuyaṉ mālaṅkuṟa vāveṉṟeṉai yāḷuntiṟaṉ
   ñālampuku tāniṉṟaru ṇātaṉcoru pāṉantaṉōr
   kōlampaḻa vētampala kūṟumporu ḷākiṉṟatē. (80)
   eḻucīrāciriyac canta viruntam
   ākiṉ ṟavaiyaḻi yāniṉ ṟamaiyaṟi
           yāniṉ ṟaṟukiṉ ṟilaivāṇāḷ
   pōkiṉṟatupuku tāteṉ ṟaṟikilai
           poyyeṉ ṟaṟikilai meyyeṉṟē
   cākiṉ ṟaṉaipiṟa vāniṉ ṟaṉaitaṭu
           māṟum paṭikeṭu vāyneñcē
   yākiṉ ṟaḷaviṉu mākiṉ ṟilaicoru
           pāṉan taroṭuṟa vāmaṉpē. (81)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalippā
   aṉpē corupā ṉantac cuṭarē yakilattiṉ
   muṉpē piṉpē muṭivī ṟaṟiyā mutalōṉē
   yeṉpē ṟuniṉain timaiyō ralamvan tiṭaviṅkōr
   vaṉpē yaṉaiyē ṉeṉpā laṉpāy vantāyē. (82)
   iruvikaṟpa nēricai veṇpā
   vantupara māṉanta vāḻvinta maṇṇiṭaiyē
   tantacoru pāṉantar tāḷcūṭic—cintaimayal
   tīrppā ralātavarē tēvarkō vāccellap
   pārppā ramarar patikku.(83)
   eṇcīrāciriya viruttam
   patiyā kiṉṟa patanta niṉceyal
           paḻavē taṅka ḷiranta tāntaṉi
   tutiyā niṉṟu mīteṉ ṟaṟintilar
           corupāṉantar pukuntu cantata
   mutiyā niṉṟa viraṇṭu paṅkaya
           muḻutē ṉuṇṭa mataṅki yeṉṟaṉai
   matiyā viṉṟu vilaṅku keṉṟaṉai
           maṟaiyō ṉeṉṟu vilaṅko tuṅkiyē.(84)
   eḻucīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   tuṅkat tiruvaṭi taṉaittē ritaru
           corupāṉantaṉa taruḷālē
   caṅkait tiṟaṉatu tavirat taṉatiru
           tāḷait talaimicai yāḷattaru
   taṅkaik kaṉiyeṉa varuḷic ceytava
           rayarpuṟ ṟayalpaṭu mayaṉeñcē
   yeṅkut taṉaiyiṉi yeṅkut taṉaiyava
           ṉirupoṟ kaḻaliṇai malariṟkē.(85)
   nēricai yāciriyappā
   malarmicai maṭantai yorumaṇa vāḷa
   palarpukaḻ takka kalaimakaḷ kaṇava
   kevuri kēḷva pārmakaṭ kiṟaiva
   mavuṉa naṉmoḻi ñāṉa vinōta
   vaṟivaru maṟiva neṟiyaru ṇimala
   pōtaṅ koṭukkum pūta vallapa
   kētaṅ keṭukkum pāta paṅkaya
   veṉakkeṉa vanta taṉittuṇait talaiva
   voruvaru maṟiyāc corupā ṉanta
   peruma niṉpukaḻ periyōy kēṭṭaruḷ
   ōrurup puṉaiyiṉu mumpa rulakat
   tārurup pukaḻu maṟivuru vāmē
   yinnilat taṭiyiṭa viṟaiva vinnila
   mannilat tēṟa vaṭiyiṭ ṭaṟumē
   paṇṭai vaṭivoḻin tuṉpaṅ kayamalark
   kaṇkaṇ ṭavarkku uṭampukaṇ ṇākumē
   ceyya pātañ ciramicai yēṟiṉa
   veyya pācam viṭṭē ṟummē
   yaṉṉiya maṟumoḻi moḻiyiṉ
   muṉṉiya camaya moḻipiṇak kaṟumē.(86)
   eṇcīrāciriya viruttam
   piṇaṅkuvaṉō pētamā pētaṅka ḷeṉṟu
           pitaṟṟukiṉṟa pitaṟṟaruṭaṉ piṟavi yoṉṟuṟ
   ṟuṇaṅkuvaṉō kuṇaṅkaṭanta corupāṉanta
           ṉoḷimalarccē vaṭiyoḻiya vorutē vuṇṭā
   vaṇaṅkuvaṉō vavaṉaṭiyā raṭiyār tammai
           vaḻipaṭuvār kuḻuvoḻiya maṟṟai yārō
   ṭiṇaṅkuvaṉō veṉṉaiyeṉak keṉṉiṟ ṟanta
           viyalpainiṉain turukiyaḻin tiṭukiṉ ṟēṉē. (87)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalittuṟai
   iṭukiṉṟi lēṉoṉṟu mēṟkiṉṟi lēṉallaṉintiyattai
   yaṭukiṉṟi lēṉoṉṟu māykiṉṟi lēṉoṉṟumācaiyoṉṟum
   viṭukiṉṟi lēṉoṉṟiṉ mīṭkiṉṟi lēṉeñcuṉṟāṭkaṇvantāṭ
   paṭukiṉṟi lēṉeṉpa ṭakkaṭa vēṉcoru pāṉantaṉē. (88)
   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   corupāṉantat tirumā muṉivaṉ
           ṟuṇaiyār kamalat tolaiyā naṟavaip
   parukā vurukip perukā niṟaiyum
           paramāṉantap perumā kaḷiyē
   ṉorupā vaṉaiyaṟ ṟuraiyaṟ ṟuṇarpaṟ
           ṟurupaṟ ṟoḻipaṟ ṟulakaṟ ṟilakuṟ
   ṟirukā talumaṟ ṟavareṉ ṟulakat
           teṇpaṭ ṭavareṉ kaṇpaṭṭavarē. (89)
   iruvikaṟpa veṇpā
   paṭṭamata karimēṟ palakōṭi yāṇṭavaṉi
   vaṭṭamuḻu tāḷu maṉṉarpata—maṭṭō
   corupāṉan taṉkīrttit tollamutai vārip
   parukā vurukum pakaṟku.(90)
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   pakutta pētamum pūtamum pōtamum
           paṟṟuviṭ ṭatumuṟṟun
   tokutti yāvaiyu nāṉeṉat tāṉeṉuḷ
           cuṭarccorupāṉantaṉ
   mikutta tāmarai cekuttatā ḷallatu
           vēṟuvēṟulakattē
   vakutta mūvartam patattaiyoṉ ṟākayāṉ
           maṟaviyu matiyēṉē.(91)
   aṟucīrāciriya viruttam
   matiyilē ṉeṉaippōla valviṉaicey tāruṇṭō
           maṅku miṉṉār
   katiyilē cuḻaṉṟavalak kaṭaliṭaiyē naṭaṉāṭak
           kaṭavēṉākit
   tutiyilē yaruḷcurakkun tūyacoru pāṉantac
           cōti yeṉṉu
   natiyilē pukuntirukka vitiyilēṉ mukavātē
           niṉṟaṟ ṟēṉē. (92)
   kali viruttam
   aṟṟavarkaḷākiyaṟavēṭaṅkoṇ ṭācaṟātē
   kaṟṟavarkaḷākiyaritāya kalaikaḷellān
   tuṟṟava rilātic corupāṉantaṉ toṇṭarellā
   maṟṟavarkaḷ kōṭi palakōṭi maṭaṅkunaṉṟē.(93)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalippā
   naṉṟu maṟiyē eṉṟu maṟiyē ṉāyēṉuṭ
   ṭuṉṟu cuṭarē corupāṉantac cōtiyeṉ
   ṟeṉṟiṅ kuraiyā ṭaḷavēṉitaiyañ ceyumāṟiṅ
   koṉṟu maṟiyē ṉeṉiṉu muraiyā toḻiyēṉē. (94)
   kaṭṭaḷaikkalittuṟai
   oḻittilai ciṟṟiṉpa muṇṭilai nīyuṇṭu muṇmaitaṉiṉ
   viḻittilai meyccoru pāṉanta veṉṟu veṟumorukāṉ
   moḻittilai tīmai moḻintaṟ ṟaṉaipala mōkameṉṟuṅ
   kaḻittilai yāvi kaḻintaṉai yēyeṉ kaṭaineñcamē. (95)
   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   kaṭaiya tākip pipīlikai yātiyāk
           kamala yōṉik kaṇakkil carācarat
   toṭaiya tāyoṉṟu pōlula kaṅkaḷān
           tōṟṟa meṉcorupāṉantac cōtiyai
   yiṭaiyaṟāviṉpa vāriyai yiṅkuvan
           teṉṉai yāṇṭaveṅ kōṉai yiṟaiñciyē
   yaṭaiya yāṉviḻuṅkumpaṭi pāriṉi
           yakila lōkameṅ kēyaṟi vaṉṟiyē. (96)
   aṟucīrāciriyac canta viruttam
   aṉṟeṉṟu kūṟutaṉai yāmeṉṟu kūṟumaṟu
           camayaṅka ḷōtiyavaiyē
   naṉṟeṉṟu kūṟumavar naṭuveṉṟu ñāṉakaṉa
           voḷiyeṉṟu nālumaṟaiyā
   yoṉṟeṉṟu kūṟucorupāṉanta nātaṉuru
           vāyvantu mēvivaravā
   veṉṟeṉṟu kūṟiyiru pātaṅkaṇ mēvaviti
           yiṉṟiṅki lātavivarē.(97)
   kalinilaittuṟai
   tavira mītoṉṟu moṉṟupakāccamayattiṉoṉṟun
   tuvarē maṉaittuñ corupāṉantac cōtitollaip
   pavarōka māṟum paṭikaṇṭa pataṅkaḷeṉṉu
   mavirōta māṉa neṟiyallaṉa vaiyamuṇṭē. (98)
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilāciriya viruttam
   vaiyamiṉi yeṉaippukaḻnteṉ maṟṟeṉaiyiṅ kikaḻntāleṉ
           māṟā teṉṟuñ
   ceyyavaḷvan teṉṉuṭaṉē ceṟintirunteṉ pirintirunteṉ
           citaiyā teṉṟu
   maiyamaṟa virunteṉuṭam paḻintāleṉ ṉākkamaḻi
           vataṉā ṉuṇṭō
   tuyyacoru pāṉantaṉ ṟuṇaiyaṭiyeṉ ṟalaiyārac
           cūṭi ṉēṟkē. (99)
   veṇpā
   cūṭiṉēṉ pātañ corupāṉan taccuṭaraip
   pāṭiṉēṉ paṇṭai yaṭiyāriṟ—kūṭiṉēṉ
   kaṇṭēṉ kaḷikūrak kāyam paṭaittataṉā
   ṉuṇṭēṉ peṟātapaya ṉoṉṟu.(100)
   ñāṉavinōtaṉ kalampakam muṟṟum.

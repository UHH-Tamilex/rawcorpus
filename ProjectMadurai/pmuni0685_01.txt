mutal kāviyam
īrōṭṭut tāttā eṉṟa innūl 1948 ām āṇṭu ēppiral mātam poṉṉi veḷiyīṭāka vantatu.
mutalil eḻutiya ''koyyāk kātal' eṉṟa ciṟu kāviyamum atē ēppiral mātam
veḷivantatu.
iraṇṭu nūlkaḷukkum puraṭcik kaviñar pāratitācaṉ tam kuyil' itaḻil veṇpā ' vil
matippuri eḻutiṉār vēṟu pala tirāviṭa iyakka itaḻkaḷilum matippurikaḷ veḷivantaṉa.
puraṭṭukkaḷi veḷippaṭuttiya periyāriṉ puraṭcik karuttukkaḷ iḷamip paruvattil eṉṉi
īrttatil viyappilli. anta īṭupāṭṭiṉ veḷippāṭutāṉ "īrōṭṭut tāttā''
iruṭṭik kiḻikkum cōtiyāy vantavar īrōṭ ṭaṇṇal, periyār paṇic ciṟappi viḷakkip
peruṅ kāviyam oṉṟu paṭikkum paṇiyil īṭupaṭṭirukkiṟēṉ aṟiviyakkavātikaḷiṉ ātaravu
eṉ paṇiyi virivu paṭuttumeṉa nampukiṟēṉ
- nārā nācciyappaṉ

EROTU THAATHA
EXAMPLE OF FINE SIMPLICITY
The whole book is written in traditional Poetry of different kinds like
Kali Venba, Virutham, Agaval, Venba all confirming to the requirements
of prosody unlike Modern Verse libre to which most budding poets and
poetasters fall a prey. The Style is simple, racy, requiring no
dictionary or pandit for help.
The biography of E.V. Ramaswami Naicker, entitled "Erotu Thaatha" is an
example of such fine simplicity, recounting the struggle he carried on
aginst society and the Government to preach and achieve his ideals of
Social justice, Political equality and nationalism.

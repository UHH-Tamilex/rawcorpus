DAULAT VILLA,
Vincent Road, Matunga, Bombay 19.
8th February, 1950.
Sriman T. N. Pichumony Iyer is the senior Tamil Pandit of the South
Indian Education Society's High School and has been serving in the
school for the last 15 years. I am connected with the management of the
school since 1933. During all these years I have known Mr. Iyer as a
great Tamil Scholar. He has taken a great deal of interest in
inculcating a taste for Tamil among South Indians in Bombay and he has
been an active figure in the organising of the Tamil Sangham in Bombay.
He has had his share of calamities in life, but he has found time and
inclination to write Tamil Poems on various themes minly on religion
and philosophy, all in such tender sweet and understandable Tamil, He
has already completed over 5000 lines, but is publishing a small
part-about 800 lines. Tamil is read in every South Indian home
throughout the world and this publication is bound to find a ready
response.
I am happy that the publication of these poems has been undertaken by my
good old friend Mr. and Mrs Bharat K. S. Iyer who are themselves great
patrons o learning.
K. S. RAMACHANDRA IYER,
President, South Indian Association,
South Indian Welfare Society High School.

iṉamotta paṇpiṉar iṉiyavarāy nikaḻntavar,
      naṭpelām poykkum vēḷi ;
uḷiyēttu nāḷelām viḻaliliṭu nīratāy,
      payaṉaṟuttiṟuti vēḷi ;
nāyottuḻalvēṉi cuṭumaṇalil kāy cēyi,
      kaṇkuḷirak kaṇṭu tavuvāy;
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ,
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē!

3.2. iṉiyavipattu

1.oppariya niṉnāmam ceppaliṉ iṉṉamutam
      āytaliṉ piṟitumuṇṭō
eppiṟavi eṭuttālum niṉṉaṭik keṉmaṉam
      paṟṟaliṉ payaṉ vēṟili
tapparitu pavaviṉikaḷ toṭarpiṉiṉ eṉṉavē
      karumaṅkaḷ āyntu ceyvōm
tappilāṉ paramaṉmēl paḻikūṟi nontakkāl
      piṟavi payaṉuḷatākumō
oppilāttūyaveṇ pālatiṉ nīratip
      pirikkumā vaṉṉamēpōl
oppiyaṟi vētanūl cāttiram payiṉṟataṉ
      sārattiṉ uṇṇukarvār.
tappāmal ōrntutam paṅkayamām vāḻviṭi
paṟṟunīr paṟṟalākkār
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē.
2. veṟumpāṉi poṅkātu occuventaḻaliṭiyē
      pirāṇamaya aṉṉamiṉṟi
uṟumaṉamē akalāka taṉṉuṇarvu aṉalāka
      ātmaṉē araṇiyāka
veṟum yākki aṭutoḻiliṉ cāliyāy viḷaṅkavē
      intaṉam kali nalamumāy
uṟukarumam eṉumporuḷ camitali nōkkiyē
      naṭuniliyeṉu makappiyāl
tirikaraṇa cuttiyām piṭikoṇṭu ēntiyē
      uṟutiyuṭaṉ makiḻntu uṇpārk
kaṟumiṭarkaḷ, pavamoḻiyum, pētikkum neñcamum
      svāṉupava oḷiyiṉ teḷiyum,
ceṟiyupantaṅkaḷum paḷiṅkattil uṟukiṉṟa
      pratimipōl tōṉṟi yoḻiyum,
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē.
3. cakamēḻu maḻuntiyē vemkaṭalkaḷ poṅkiṭavē
      vaṭavāmukākkiṉitaṉiṉ
mikaveḻuñ cavāliyatiṉ nāppaṇṇiruppiṉum
      niṉpatattuṇiyiruppiṉ
katir eḻuṅkaṇappoḻutil mañcuvoṭu tuḷir
      maṟintiṭum pāṉmi pōlē paṉiyum
uṟattaḻuvu tuyariṉaṅkaḷ maṉameṉum pañcarattiṉ
      irukkiviṭṭōṭum mīḷā
aṉal meḻukurukkutaliṉ aṉiyavē uṉṉaruḷiṉ
      tāpamatu maṉaṉivāṭṭa
mikattoḻutu uṉivēṇṭi meṉmalariṉ patam nāṭi
eṉmaṉam viriyuṅkāli
aḻumatali yeṉiyāṇṭu uṉṉaṭikkāḷākkip
      piṟavi nōytīrttu vippāy
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē.
4. maṉamāṉa pēytaṉṉi āḷtiṟami koṇṭiṭat
      tiṭamāṉa nōkku vēṇṭum.
ciṉamāṉa koḷḷiyap picācam akamuṟṟu
      aṉalkoḷuttiṭum vēḷiyil
kaṉamāṉa niṉnāmam catamuṟikaḷ ōtiṭat
      taṇṇamutu eri avikka
iṉamāṉam poṟumiyuṟa akamvīya uṟumāka
      niṇak kotippaṭaṅku mammā
peṟumāṉa tavayōka eṇcittikaḷ yāvum
      oṭuṅku maṉamiṉṟiyilli
eṇmāṉam eṇṇilā eṇṇaṅkaḷ palvēṟu
      coṟceyki tavirttiṭṭumē
taṉmāṉam taṉkarumam taṉvīṭukāttumē
      taṉṉiliyaṟiya yaruḷvāy
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē.
5. vaṟumiyeṉum kaṭumpiṇiyiṉ paripavamum tāpamum,
      eṉpuruka uyir vīykkavum;
urimiyeṉum maṉamviṟṟu irattaloṭu niḻaleṉavē,
      piṉceṉṟu itam pēciyē;
tarumeṉiṉum valnitikaḷ avviḻiviṉ cārāta,
      peruntakimi peṟutal vēṇṭum;
karumiyarik karṇareṉak kolupavarittarmareṉat

aruneṟiyareṉa vēṟṟi ariya maṉaccāṉṟutaṉi,
      koṉṟu uyyāmi vēṇṭum;
ceṟumeṉiṉum tutināviṉ kōṭāmiyōṭu nal,
      iṉmoḻiyiṉ uṟuti vēṇṭum;
uṟuvaliyar eḷiyareṉum pētamil oppuraviṉ,
      taṉṉala maṟuttal vēṇṭum;
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ,
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē !
6. kalaniti pulaṅkaḷmika āviṉam mutalāṉa,
      eṇṇitikaḷ u uṟavu vēṇṭām;
pavaniti purakkumika pantaṅkaḷ cuṟṟamum,
      uyarniliyiṉ perumi vēṇṭām;
perunitiyeṉum ivuḷimītēṟi vaḻiyīṟi,
      aṉṉeṟi aḷāvimikka;
paḻinitikuvittiṭavē kaṇcevikaḷ aviyatayi,
      māypōlip pēṟu vēṇṭām;
iruniti kulapperumi kuṇanīttu uyyumoru,
      uyvaki upāyam vēṇṭām;
iḻivatiyum poykuṟaḷi puṟaṅkūṟal immūṉṟum
      vaḷantariṉum ompal vēṇṭām;
eṉvariṉum cāṉṟāṇmi kuṉṟāmiyōṭunal,
      naṭuniliya maṉamum tārāy;
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ,
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē !
7. parivoṭu iṭukkaṇum pāṅkuṟavē nīkkiṭum,
      pakuttaṟivu uṟutivēṇṭum;
ariaraṉ ayaṉāti tēvarkaḷ pālaṉpu,
      ekkālum toṭaravēṇṭum;
arintuyiri ūṉ uṇṭu ūṉperukkātu mika,
      iḻipulāl tavirkka vēṇṭum;
ceṟiyaṟaṅkaḷ palavaṟṟuḷ ceynaṉṟi kollāmi,
      vempakiyiṉ oṉṟalārum
karuttaḻiya kacaṭupaṭa uṟuitam purintumē,
      aṉpoṭu aḷāva vēṇṭuy;
poykkari pukaṉṟumē mācaṟṟa uḷḷamatiṉ,
      kaṭuñcāpam tavirttal vēṇṭum;
poruṇmoḻikaḷ nīṅkātu aṟavōravik kaḷam,
      akalātu vika vēṇṭum;
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ,
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē!
8. kuṇamilar kūṭṭuṟavu kaṉavilum viḻiyātu,
      mikucēymi vilakavēṇṭum;
taṇaleṉavē añciyē piṟamātar mukanōkki,
      iccittiṭāmi vēṇṭum;

      maṭamaṅkitaṉṉi maruvi;

      niṉṟuvaṟam kūṭṭavēṇṭum;
piṇamākat tōlvaṟṟic cāyiṉum parataṉatti,
      lōṣṭameṉak koḷḷal vēṇṭum;

kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ,
      aṉṉi kōmati aṉṉiyē!
9. vaṟumiyilum kōṭātu cemmaṉap pāṉmiyoṭu

kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ
      aṉṉi kōmati aṉṉiyē !
10. uṟṟavarkkelā meṉiyaṉākiyē peṟṟataṉam,

peṟṟavaṟam poruḷiṉpam vīṭupeṟavakutta vaḻi,

ikaparameṉṉīriṭamum iciyuṭiya vīṭṭiṭiyē,
      iṉṉaruḷiṉ vāḻavēṇṭum?

3.3. tōttirappattu

1. pakalavaṉiṉ katipōla vaṉutiṉamum eḻuvatum
      pacaṉakkalaṅkaḷōrntu
akaṭu niṟi poruḷaṉittum ākkuvatum uṇṇuvatum
      uṟaṅkuvatum māya voṉṟē
pakaṭeṉavē nuka panta maṉamēnti viḻi pituṅka
      pavamūṭṭi cumantu niṟkum
akatiyeṉi eṭuttāṇṭu niṉṉaruḷiṉ rasamūṭṭi
      kōtiṉeṟi yuyttiṭāyō
jakamuḻutum aṉiyeṉṟu toḻutēttum pāviyē
      imayavaṉ makiḻ celviyē
mikum piṇikaḷ mey vīḻtta maṉamociya uḷ kalaṅka
      ātaram aṟṟa innāḷ
cekuvalami taru muṉatu pataṅkaḷ tuṇi mikavuṟavē
      ceṅkamala nayaṉattāḷvāy
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē !
tiruppuṭimarutūr tala makimi
2. teṉkumariyelliyām tīmpuṉal yāṟu pala
      tōṉṟu muyar acalamākum;
tēṉmoḻiyām teṉ moḻiyil kālvirikkum viriyeṉavē,
      muttēyam makiḻac ceyta ;
taṉṉikaril kuṟumuṉivaṉ tavam purintu civacakti,
      maṇavaḻaku kaṇṭaveṟpām;
taṉivaṇṇap palporuḷiṉ vilipulamām teṉṟal uṟi,
      potiya maliyakaṭṭil tōṉṟi;
kaṉinta mutir ceḻum poḻilkaḷ paltalaṅkaḷ ūṭuruvi,
      arumaruntiṉ cenniṟattāy;
taṉippeyariṉ tāmparavarṇi yeṉumaṇaṅku makiḻntaṇikkum,
      toṇṭar vākkamutam pōṟṟum;
maṉṉutavac celvar palar hittipeṟu makimi yuṭi,
      palvaḷam cūḻtalattiṉ;
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ,
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē!
talavaralāṟuc curukkam
3. kaṇṇumuṉi muṉintiṭalum maṇṇiṉarelāmikaḻa
      veñcāpam tīravōṭi
viṇṇimiyar talineñcam vempiṭavē umipākaṉ
      patantaṉṉil caraṇ pukuntumē
kaṇṇutalum atukaṇṭu curapiyoṭu piṉpōntu
      parātap pūmi taṉṉiṉ
eṇṇu maruneṟi kaṭantu puṇyamākiṭak kiyuṟu
      talamellām pōṟṟi vārāy
paṇṇiya muṉ tavappayaṉāl teṉkōṭi puṉitanati
      aṇmi uyar marutattarukkīḻ
puṇṇiya karuttaminta toṇṭar tam toṇṭaṉāy
      svayampuvāy nāṉ iruppēṉ
eṇṇiya karuttiṉpaṭi uṉcāpam tīrumeṉ
      ṟōtu puṭārjuṉa vaḷḷaliṉ
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē.
tala purāṇac curukkam- toṭarcci
intiraṉ cāpam nīṅkiyatu.
4. karvitta mēruvic ceṟutta vacrāyutattōṉ,
      tarumūṉṟu marutavaṭikkīḻ;
nirmitta araṉuriyiṉ liṅka vaṭivāyttikaḻum,
      mūrttiyoru mūṉṟu kaṇṭāṉ;
uṟuvittakam peṟṟāṉ mallikāmatyamoṭu,
      puṭārjuṉattala meytiyē ;
malamaṟṟu tiruvuṟṟu kaṭaṉiyoṭu tāmiranīr,
      kalakkumiṭam nīrmūḻkiyē;
maṉamottu eṉiyāṇṭa nittaṉi nimalaṉi,
      yēttiyē uyarvu peṟṟāṉ;
ciliyottu ceyalaṟṟu niliyaṟṟu niṉṟēṉi,
      panta alinīnta aruḷāy;
variyaṟṟa tavaccelvi variyariyaṉaruñcelvi
      cacitaraṉiyaṇinta celvi;
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ,

paṇṭāram pakti mēmpāṭu
5. tolkulap paṇiyēnti curumpirintu naṟuktēṉuṇ
      aṉṟalar malar māliyi;
celkalā muṭceṟinta vampuceṟi koṭuṅkuṭiyar,
      naṭamāṭu neṟikaṭāvi;
koḷkalā miṉṉoḷiyum pēriṭiyum perumaḻiyum

7. mīṉmikka voḷiceytu pōkuṉari aḻittiṭum

meyttuṇi uṉpatam alāl
kāruṇī cīrmēvu puṭimarutūr kāttaruḷ
      aṉṉi kōmati ammiyē!
8. vallarakkaṉ camparaṉi mahiṣavuruk koṇṭavaṉi
      māyttulaku kātta tēvi
cellarikkum ceyal pōleṉ arakkartaṉi

palliṟakkam mēṭupala kāṭumali cuṟṟi naṟu

9. kaliyukat teyvameṉa aṇṭiṉōrk kātaravu koṭu

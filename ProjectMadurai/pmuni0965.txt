maturivīraṉ ammāṉi

caracuvati makāl nūlakam paṉmoḻic cuvaṭik karuvūlamākat tikaḻntuvarukiṉṟatu. innūlakatti
aṇiceykiṉṟa ariya cuvaṭikaḷi nūlkaḷākap patippittu veḷiyiṭukiṉṟa arumpaṇiyiyum
toṭarntu innūlakam āṟṟivarukiṉṟatu.
ilakkaṇam, ilakkiyam, cōtiṭam, marut- tuvam mutaliya tuṟikaḷic cārnta pala nūlkaḷ,
innūlaka veḷiyīṭukaḷāka malarntuḷḷaṉa, ilakkiya vakiyuḷ oṉṟāka oḷirum 'ammāṉi' ilakkiya
vaki nūlkaḷ palavaṟṟiyum in nūlakam ēṟkeṉavē veḷiyiṭṭuḷḷatu. avvariciyil taṟpoḻutu
‘maturivīraṉ ammāṉi' eṉum innūl cuvaṭiyiliruntu patippittu veḷiyiṭappaṭukiṉṟatu.
pukaḻvāynta camūkakkatippāṭal ilakkiyamāka im 'maturivīraṉ ammāṉi' nūl viḷaṅkukiṉṟatu.
ilakkiyanayam vāynta innūl, innū lakat tamiḻttuṟiyiṉ 1692 ām eṇṇiṭṭa ōliccuvaṭiyi
aṭippaṭiyākakkoṇṭu, in- nūlakattiṉ muṉṉāḷ nūlakar tiru ma. cīrāḷaṉ avarkaḷ
patippittu aḷittuḷḷār. patippāciriya rukku eṉatu pārāṭṭukaḷ.
innūl veḷivarat tēviyāṉa nitiyutavi- yi nalkiyuḷḷa miyaaracu paṇpāṭṭut tuṟikku
eṉatu naṉṟiyi urittākki makiḻkiṉṟēṉ. nūlakattiṟkup parāmarippu nitiyiṉi
vaḻaṅkivarum tamiḻaka aracukkum eṉatu maṉamārnta naṉṟiyip pulappaṭuttukiṉṟēṉ.
innūl naṉmuṟiyil veḷivara āvaṉa ceytuḷḷa innūlaka niruvāka aluvalar tiru nā.
tiyākarājaṉ em. ē., pi. el. avarkaḷukkum, nūlaka veḷiyīṭṭu mēlāḷar maṟṟum nūlakar
tiru a. pañcanātaṉ em. ē., em. el. ai. es. avarkaḷukkum, innūliṉi naṉku
acciṭṭaḷitta tañcāvūr makāliṅkam accakattārukkum eṉatu pārāṭṭukkaḷ.
innūl, tamiḻilakkiya aṉparkaḷiṉ, kuṟippāka nāṭṭuppuṟak katippāṭal ilakkiya aṉparkaḷ
maṟṟum āyvāḷarkaḷiṉ nalvaravēṟ pip peṟumeṉa nampukiṉṟēṉ.
tañcāvūr       muṉivar mū. irācārām, i ā pa.
30-3-99       māvaṭṭa āṭcit talivar maṟṟum iyakkunar,
caracuvati makāl nūlakam.

āyvuri
maturi vīraṉ ammāṉi' eṉṉum nāṭṭuppuṟak katippāṭal, tañci caracuvati makāl
nūlakattiṉ vāyilāka veḷivarukiṟatu. tamiḻakattil aiyaṉār, ammaṉ kōyilkaḷil ciṟu
teyvamāka iṭampeṟṟu vaṇaṅkappaṭum vīraṉ maturi vīraṉ'. maturi vīraṉ katiyit
tamiḻaka makkaḷ naṉku aṟivar.
tañci caracuvati makāl nūlakam ituvari 15 ammāṉi nūlkaḷip patippittu
veḷiyiṭ ṭuḷḷatu. aviyāvaṉa :-

7, 7. cittira puttirar ammāṉi

maturivīraṉ kācimaṉṉaṉ makaṉākap piṟantāṉ eṉiṉum māliyoṭu piṟantatāl kāṭṭil viṭap
paṭṭāṉ. cakkiliyar vakuppic cērnta oruvaṉ ivaṉi eṭuttu vaḷarttāṉ. ivvīraṉ cittikaḷ
peṟṟup pommaṇa nāyakkaṉ makaḷ pommiyik kāntarvattāl maṇantāṉ. piṉṉart tiruccirāp
paḷḷiyil aracu celuttum vicayaraṅka cokkaliṅka vēntariṭam mātamoṉṟukku āyirampoṉ
peṟṟuk koṇṭu naṟcēvaka pataviyil amarntāṉ. aṅkut taṉi vīraṉāyirukkiyil,
tirumalināyakkar maturiyil iruntu kaḷḷarpayam atikamāyiruttaliṉ, tāṅkaḷ vantu avarkaḷi
yaṭakka vēṇṭumeṉak kēṭṭukoṇṭār. itaṉi vicayaraṅka cokkaliṅkarukku oru kaṭitam eḻuti
aṉuppiṉār. avar ivvīriyaṉi maturikkuc ceṉṟu akkoṭiya kaḷḷarkaḷiyaṭakki varuvāyāka
eṉṟu āṇiyiṭṭār. maturivīraṉ avvaṇṇamē teṉmaturiyiṟ ceṉṟu akkaḷḷarkaḷi oḻittuk
kaḷḷarpayam nīkkiṉāṉ aṭuttut tirumalināyakkar araṇmaṉiyilē amarntiruntu vitivacattāl
an nāyakkarukku ālatti eṭukkum tātippeṇ veḷḷacci eṉpavaḷi maṇantāṉ. avaḷi aḻittuc
cellum pōtu kāvalarkaḷāl ki kāl vāṅkappeṟṟāṉ. iṟutiyil attirumalināyakkar
mīṉāṭciyammiyi vēṇṭikoḷḷamuṉpōla vēkikālkaḷippeṟṟuk kaṭiciyil pommiyammāḷ,
veḷḷiyammāḷ ivar kaḷuṭaṉ tīkkuḻiyil kutittut uyirip pōkkik koṇṭu teyvamāṉāṉ.
mīṉāṭciyammaṉ cannitiyilē kampattaṭiyil pūcikoṇṭirukkiṟār, mēlum ivari vīraṉāka
makkaḷ iṉṟum vaṇaṅkukiṉṟaṉar eṉṟa ceytiyi virivarka uṇarttukiṟatu innūl.
katipaṟṟi māṟupaṭṭa karuttukkaḷ
intavīraṉ katiyip paṟṟi māṟupaṭṭa karut tukkaḷ nilavukiṉṟaṉa. maturi vīraṉiṉ varalāṟṟi
aṟivataṟku ‘maturi vīraṉ cāmi kati' eṉṉum nūlum makkaḷ kūṟum katikaḷum cāṉṟukaḷāka
uḷḷaṉa. kāci maṉṉaṉukku makaṉākap piṟantu cakkiliyaṉ oruvaṉāl vaḷarkkappaṭṭavaṉ vīriyaṉ
eṉṟu colkiṟatu, maturivīraṉ cāmi kati. avaṉ piṟaviyilēyē cakkili eṉṉum karuttum,
pāṇṭiyar kaḷukkuk kappaṅkaṭṭik koṇṭirunta ciṟṟaracaṉ eṉṉum karuttum uḷḷaṉa.
pommi eṉṉum aracakumāriyit tūkkicceṉṟu maṇantavīraṉ. piṉṉar vēṟu ōr araṇmaṉi
yilirunta veḷḷiyammāḷ eṉṟa peṇṇi virum piya kāraṇattāl taṇṭikkappaṭṭāṉ. āṉāl
pommiyiṉ tanti vīraṉicciṟippiṭittut taṇṭittatākak kūṟuvārum uḷar. makkaḷ avaṉic
ciṟu teyvamāka vaṇaṅkukiṉṟaṉar eṉpatil karuttu māṟupāṭu illi.
maturi nāyakkarkaḷiṉ kālattil tāṉ maturi vīraṉ vaḻipāṭu tōṉṟiyatu. akkālattil
maturiyil koli, koḷḷi mutaliya tolli koṭuttu vanta kaḷvākaḷik karuvaṟuttu naṉmi
ceyta kāraṇattāl makkaḷ avaṉit teyvamākak koṇṭāṭukiṟārkaḷ. ivaṉ tirumali nāyakkar
kālattil iruntirukkalām eṉṟu karutappaṭukiṉṟatu. maturi vīraṉik karuppucāmi eṉṟu
aḻippatum uṇṭu. aiyaṉār, ammaṉ kōyilkaḷil ciṟuteyvamāka vaṇaṅ kappaṭum vīraṉukkut
taṉikkōyil illi. cila iṭaṅkaḷil maturivīraṉ patiṉeṭṭāmpaṭikkaruppaṉ eṉṟu
aḻikkappaṭuvatākavum terikiṟatu.
tañci māvaṭṭattil pala ūrkaḷil uḷḷa aiyaṉār kōyilkaḷil vīraṉukkut taṉikkōyilkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.
taliyil koṇṭi, kaṉatta mīci, kaṭṭāṉa uṭal koṇṭa vīraṉiṉ valakkiyil vāḷ, iṭak kiyil
kati, avaṉukku muṉpāka oru vēṭṭi nāy, etiril oṉṟu allatu pala maṇkutirikaḷ, inta
niliyil cilikaḷ kāṭci aḷikkiṉṟaṉa. cēlam māvaṭṭattiluḷḷa cila vīraṉ kōyilkaḷil
periya kutirikaḷiṉ mēlum kutirikaḷukkup pakkattilum vāḷum katiyum vittuḷḷa
periya vīraṉ cilikaḷik kāṇamuṭiyum.
piṟa cuvaṭikaḷ maṟṟum accunūlkaḷ

nūlkaḷi lum vēṟunūlakaṅkaḷil kāṇappaṭuvaṉavaṟṟiṉ paṭṭiyal oṉṟu tayār ceytēṉ.

       ------

                               uraip pāyiram

       āṟṟuppaṭai:
       oruvar ōriṭattil tām peṟṟa peruvaḷattaip peṟāta piṟarkkum
       aṟimukappaṭutti avviṭam ceṉṟu attakaiya vaḷattaip peṟṟuvarumāṟu
       vaḻippaṭutti yaṉupputal āṟṟup paṭaiyākum. (āṟṟuppaṭai =
       āṟṟuppaṭuttutal = vaḻip paṭuttutal) ippaṇpu makkaṭ paṇpukaḷuḷ
       uyariya ellaik koṭumuṭiyākum murukaṉiṉ aṉpar oruvar maṟṟōr aṉparai
       murukaṉiṭam āṟṟup paṭuttuvatāka amaintatē tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai eṉṉum
       nūl. itu 817 aṭikaḷ koṇṭa oru muḻunīḷap pāṭṭākum.
       āciriyar:
       caṅka kālap pattup pāṭṭuḷ mutal pāṭṭāka uḷḷa tiru
       murukāṟṟuppaṭaiyai iyaṟṟiyavar nakkīraṉār eṉṉum caṅkap pulavarāvār.
       piṟa āṟṟup paṭaikaḷ:
       pattup pāṭṭaic cērnta ciṟupāṇ āṟṟuppaṭai, perumpāṇ āṟṟuppaṭai,
       porunar āṟṟuppaṭai ākiya nūṟpeyarkaḷ, yāryārai āṟṟup
       paṭuttukiṟārkaḷō—avaravar peyarkaḷāl amaintuḷḷaṉa.
       aḵtāvatu,—pāṇaraiyum porunaraiyum aracariṭam āṟṟuppaṭuttum nūlkaḷ
       avarkaḷ peyarkaḷāl pāṇāṟṟuppaṭai eṉavum porunar āṟṟuppaṭai eṉavum
       vaḻaṅkap peṟukiṉṟaṉa. āṉāl tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭaiyiṉ peyaramaippu
       avaṟṟiṉum vēṟāṉatu. yāriṭam āṟṟuppaṭuttukiṟārkaḷō-avar peyarāl
       amaintatu tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai. aḵtāvatu,—murukaṉiṭam aṉpaṉai
       āṟṟuppaṭuttuvatāl ‘murukāṟṟuppaṭai’ eṉappaṭṭatu. eṉavē, āṟṟuppaṭai
       nūlkaḷuḷ taṉiyoru ciṟappiṭam peṟṟiruppatu tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai
       eṉpatu pulaṉākum. tolkāppiyattil:

   “kūttarum pāṇarum porunarum viṟaliyum
       āṟṟiṭaik kāṭci uṟaḻat tōṉṟip
       peṟṟa peruvaḷam peṟāarkku aṟivuṟīic
       ceṉṟu payaṉetirac coṉṉa pakkamum—”

   eṉṟu tolkāppiyattil āṟṟuppaṭaikku ōr ilakkaṇam kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷatu.
   tolkāppiyattil kaṭavuḷiṉ aṉpar kūṟappaṭavillaiyē eṉac cilar
   kaḻaṟukiṉṟaṉar. ‘tolkāppiyam ōraḷaviṟkuttāṉ ilakkaṇam kūṟa muṭiyum;
   ulakiyal aṉaittaiyum ilakkaṇa nūlil aṭakkik kūṟiviṭa muṭiyātu.’ –eṉpatu
   niṉaivirukkavēṇṭum. atē nērattil, paḻaiya ilakkaṇa nūlil
   kūṟiyuḷḷapaṭiyētāṉ ilakkiyam eḻuta vēṇṭum–putumātiri kūṭātu eṉṟu evarum
   kūṟakkūṭātu. paḻaiya ilakkaṇam putiya ilakkiyattiṉ vaḷarcciyai muṟṟilum
   kaṭṭuppaṭuttalākātu. īṇṭu, “The golden rule is that there is no golden
   rule” — eṉṉum aṟivumoḻi oppu nōkkaṟpālātu.
   nūṟ ciṟappu
   tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai murukaṉatu talaimaip perumaiyaik kūṟum mikac
   ciṟanta nūlākum. āciriyar nakkīraṉār tam kālattup paḻakka vaḻakkaṅkaḷai
   aṭippaṭaiyākak koṇṭu nūlaic ciṟappuṟa yāttuḷār. eṉavē, kālak kaṇ koṇṭu
   innūṟ karuttukkaḷai ārāya vēṇṭum. nakkīraṉāriṉ talaimaip pulamaikku
   innūl mikacciṟanta cāṉṟākum.
   innūṟkup paḻaiya uraikaḷ uḷḷaṉa. aṭiyēṉatu urai teḷivāṉa
   poḻippuraiyākum. itaṉaiyum ēṟṟaruḷa vēṇṭukiṟēṉ.
   cuntara caṇmukaṉ
   ------------

             tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai (nakkīrar aruḷiyatu)

   1 tirupparaṅkuṉṟam
   iṇaikkuṟaḷāciriyappā
   ulaka muvappa valaṉērpu tiritaru
   palarpukaḻ ñāyiṟu kaṭaṟkaṇ ṭāaṅ
   kōvaṟa vimaikkuñ cēṇviḷaṅ kaviroḷi
   uṟunart tāṅkiya mataṉuṭai nōṉṟāṭ
   ceṟunart tēytta celluṟaḻ taṭakkai         5
   maṟuvil kaṟpiṉ vāṇutal kaṇavaṉ
   kārkōṇ mukanta kamañcūṉ māmaḻai
   vāḷpōḻ vicumpiṉ vaḷḷuṟai citaṟit
   talaippeya ṟalaiiya taṇṇaṟuṅ kāṉat
   tiruḷpaṭap potuḷiya parārai marāat         10
   turuḷpūn taṇṭār puraḷu mārpiṉaṉ
   mālvarai nivanta cēṇuyar veṟpiṟ
   kiṇkiṇi kavaiiya voṇceñ cīṟaṭik
   kaṇakkāl vāṅkiya nucuppiṟ paṇaittōṭ
   kōpat taṉṉa tōyāp pūntukiṟ         15
   palkācu niraitta cilkā ḻalkuṟ
   kaipuṉain tiyaṟṟāk kaviṉpeṟu vaṉappi
   ṉāvaloṭu peyariya polampuṉai yaviriḻaic
   cēṇikantu viḷaṅkuñ ceyirtīr mēṉit
   tuṇaiyō rāynta viṇaiyī rōtic         20
   ceṅkāl veṭcic cīṟita ḻiṭaiyiṭupu
   paintāṭ kuvaḷait tūvitaḻ kiḷḷit
   teyva vuttiyoṭu valampuri vayiṉvaittut
   tilakan taiiya tēṅkamaḻ tirunutaṉ
   makarap pakuvāy tāḻamaṇ ṇuṟuttut         25
   tuvara muṭitta tukaḷaṟu muccip
   peruntaṇ caṇpakañ cerīik karuntakaṭ
   ṭuḷaippū maruti ṉoḷḷiṇa raṭṭik
   kiḷaikkaviṉ ṟeḻutaru kīḻnīrc cevvarum
   piṇaippuṟu piṇaiyal vaḷaiit tuṇaittaka         30
   vaṇkātu niṟainta piṇṭi yoṇṭaḷir
   nuṇpū ṇukan tiḷaippat tiṇkāḻ
   naṟuṅkuṟa ṭuriñciya pūṅkēḻt tēyvai
   tēṅkamaḻ marutiṉar kaṭuppak kōṅkiṉ
   kuvimuki ḻiḷamulaik koṭṭi virimalar         35
   vēṅkai nuṇṭā tappik kāṇvara
   veḷḷiṟ kuṟumuṟi kiḷḷupu teṟiyāk
   kōḻi yōṅkiya veṉṟaṭu viṟaṟkoṭi
   vāḻiya periteṉ ṟēttip palaruṭaṉ
   cīrtikaḻ cilampakañ cilampap pāṭic         40
   cūrara makaḷi rāṭuñ cōlai
   mantiyu maṟiyā māṉpayi laṭukkattuc
   curumpu mucāc cuṭarppūṅ kāntaṭ
   peruntaṇ kaṇṇi milainta ceṉṉiyaṉ
   pārmutir paṉikkaṭal kalaṅkavuḷ pukkuc         45
   cūrmuta ṟaṭinta cuṭarilai neṭuvē
   lulaṟiya katuppiṟ piṟaḻpaṟ pēḻvāyc
   cuḻalviḻip pacuṅkaṭ cūrtta nōkkiṟ
   kaḻalkaṭ kūkaiyoṭu kaṭumpāmpu tūṅkap
   perumulai yalaikkuṅ kātiṟ piṇarmōṭ         50
   ṭurukeḻu celavi ṉañcuvaru pēymakaḷ
   kuruti yāṭiya kūrukirk koṭuviraṟ
   kaṇṭoṭ ṭuṇṭa kaḻimuṭaik karuntalai
   oṇṭoṭit taṭakkaiyi ṉēnti veruvara
   veṉṟaṭu viṟaṟkaḷam pāṭittōḷ peyarā         55
   niṇantiṉ vāya ṭuṇaṅkai tūṅka
   irupē ruruvi ṉorupē riyākkai
   aṟuvēṟu vakaiyi ṉañcuvara maṇṭi
   avuṇar nalvala maṭaṅkak kaviḻiṇar
   māmuta ṟaṭinta maṟuvil koṟṟat         60
   teyyā nallicaic cevvēṟ cēey
   cēvaṭi paṭaruñ cemma luḷḷamoṭu
   nalampuri koḷkaip pulampurin tuṟaiyuñ
   celavunī nayantaṉai yāyiṟ palavuṭa
   ṉaṉṉar neñcat tiṉṉacai vāyppa         65
   iṉṉē peṟutinī muṉṉiya viṉaiyē
   ceruppukaṉ ṟeṭutta cēṇuyar neṭuṅkoṭi
   varippuṉai pantoṭu pāvai tūṅkap
   porunart tēytta pōraru vāyiṟ
   ṟiruvīṟ ṟirunta tītutir niyamattu         70
   māṭamali maṟukiṟ kūṭaṟ kuṭavayi
   ṉiruñcēṟ ṟakalvayal virintuvā yaviḻnta
   muṭṭāṭ ṭāmarait tuñci vaikaṟaik
   kaṭkamaḻ neyta lūti yeṟpaṭak
   kaṇpōṉ malarnta kāmar cuṉaimala         75
   rañciṟai vaṇṭi ṉarikkaṇa molikkuṅ
   kuṉṟamarn tuṟaitalu muriya ṉatāaṉṟu.
   ---------
   2 tiruccīralaivāy
   vainnuti poruta vaṭuvāḻ varinutal
   vāṭā mālai yōṭaiyoṭu tuyalvarap
   paṭumaṇi yiraṭṭu maruṅkiṟ kaṭunaṭaik         80
   kūṟṟat taṉṉa māṟṟaru moympiṟ
   kālkiḷarn taṉṉa vēḻa mēlkoṇ
   ṭaivē ṟuruviṟ ceyviṉai muṟṟiya
   muṭiyoṭu viḷaṅkiya muraṇmiku tirumaṇi
   miṉṉuṟa ḻimaippiṟ ceṉṉip poṟpa         85
   nakaitāḻpu tuyalvarūum vakaiyamai polaṅkuḻai
   cēṇviḷaṅ kiyaṟkai vāṇmati kavaii
   akalā mīṉi ṉavirvaṉa vimaippat
   tāvil koḷkait tantoḻiṉ muṭimār
   maṉaṉēr peḻutaru vāṇiṟa mukaṉē         90
   māyiruṇ ñāla maṟuviṉṟi viḷaṅkap
   palkatir virintaṉ ṟorumuka morumuka
   mārvala rētta vamarntiṉi toḻukik
   kātali ṉuvantu varaṅkoṭut taṉṟē yorumuka
   mantira vitiyiṉ marapuḷi vaḻāa         95
   antaṇar vēḷviyōrk kummē yorumuka
   meñciya poruḷkaḷai yēmuṟa nāṭit tiṅkaḷpōlat
   ticaiviḷak kummē yorumukañ ceṟunart tēyttuc
   celcama murukkik
   kaṟuvukoṇeñcamoṭu kaḷamvēṭ         100
   ṭaṉṟē yorumukaṅ
   kuṟavar maṭamakaḷ koṭipō ṉucuppiṉ
   maṭavaral vaḷḷiyoṭu nakaiyamarn taṉṟē yāṅkam
   mūviru mukaṉu muṟainaviṉ ṟeḻukali
   ṉāran tāḻnta vampakaṭṭu mārpiṟ
   cempoṟi vāṅkiya moympiṟ cuṭarviṭupu         105
   vaṇpukaḻ niṟaintu vacintuvāṅku nimirtōḷ
   viṇcelaṉ marapi ṉaiyark kēntiya
   torukai ukkañ cērttiya torukai
   nalam peṟu kaliṅkattuk kuṟaṅkiṉ micaiyacai iyatorukai
   aṅkucaṅ kaṭāva vorukai yirukai         110
   aiyiru vaṭṭamo ṭeḵkuvalan tirippa vorukai
   mārpoṭu viḷaṅka vorukai
   tāroṭu poliya vorukai
   kīḻvīḻ toṭiyoṭu mimīcaik koṭpa vorukai
   pāṭiṉ paṭumaṇi yiraṭṭa vorukai         115
   nīṉiṟa vicumpiṉ malituḷi poḻiya vorukai
   vāṉara makaḷirkku vatuvai cūṭṭa vāṅkap
   paṉṉiru kaiyum pāṟpaṭa viyaṟṟi
   antarap palliyaṅ kaṟaṅkat tiṇkāḻ
   vayireḻun ticaippa vālvaṉai ñarala         120
   urantalaik koṇṭa vurumiṭi muracamoṭu
   palpoṟi maññai velkoṭi yakava
   vicumpā ṟāka viraicelaṉ muṉṉi
   ulakam pukaḻnta vōṅkuyar viḻuccī
   ralaivāyc cēṟalu nilaiiya paṇpē         125
   yatāaṉṟu
   -------
   3. tiruvāviṉaṉkuṭi
   cīrai taiiya vuṭukkaiyar cīroṭu
   valampuri puraiyum vāṉarai muṭiyiṉar
   mācaṟa vimaikku muruviṉar māṉi
   ṉurivai taiiya vūṉkeṭu mārpi
   ṉeṉpeḻun tiyaṅku miyākkaiyar naṉpakaṟ         130
   palavuṭaṉ kaḻinta vuṇṭiya rikaloṭu
   ceṟṟa nīkkiya maṉattiṉa riyāvatuṅ
   kaṟṟō raṟiyā vaṟiviṉar kaṟṟōrkkut
   tāmvaram pākiya talaimaiyar kāmamoṭu
   kaṭuñciṉaṅ kaṭinta kāṭciya riṭumpai         135
   yāvatu maṟiyā viyalpiṉar mēvarat
   tuṉiyil kāṭci muṉivar muṟpukap
   pukaimukan taṉṉa māci ṟūvuṭai
   mukaivā yaviḻnta takaicū ḻākattuc
   cevinērpu vaitta ceyvuṟu tivavi         140
   ṉalliyāḻ naviṉṟa nayaṉuṭai neñciṉ
   meṉmoḻi mēvala riṉṉaram puḷara
   nōyiṉ ṟiyaṉṟa yākkaiyar māvi
   ṉavirtaḷir puraiyu mēṉiya ravirtoṟum
   poṉṉurai kaṭukkun titalaiya riṉṉakaip         145
   paruman tāṅkiya paṇintēn talkuṉ
   māciṉ makaḷiroṭu maṟuviṉṟi viḷaṅkak
   kaṭuvō ṭoṭuṅkiya tūmpuṭai vāleyiṟ
   ṟaḻaleṉa vuyirkku mañcuvaru kaṭuntiṟaṟ
   pāmpupaṭap puṭaikkum palvarik koṭuñciṟaip         150
   puḷḷaṇi nīḷkoṭic celvaṉum veḷḷēṟu
   valavayi ṉuyariya palarpukaḻ tiṇitō
   ḷumaiyamarntu viḷaṅku mimaiyā mukkaṇ
   muveyiṉ murukkiya muraṇmiku celvaṉu
   nūṟṟuppat taṭukkiya nāṭṭattu nūṟupal         155
   vēḷvi muṟṟiya veṉṟaṭu koṟṟat
   tīriraṇ ṭēntiya maruppi ṉeḻiṉaṭait
   tāḻperun taṭakkai yuyartta yāṉai
   erutta mēṟiya tirukkiḷar celvaṉu
   nāṟperun teyvattu naṉṉakar nilaiiya         160
   ulakaṅ kākku moṉṟupuri koḷkaip
   palarpukaḻ muvarun talaiva rāka
   ēmuṟu ñālan taṉṉiṟ ṟōṉṟit
   tāmarai payanta tāvi lūḻi
   nāṉmuka voruvaṟ cuṭṭik kāṇvarap         165
   pakaliṟ ṟōṉṟu mikalil kāṭci
   nālvē ṟiyaṟkaip patiṉoru mūvarō
   ṭoṉpatiṟ ṟiraṭṭi yuyarnilai peṟīiyar
   mīṉpūt taṉṉa tōṉṟalar mīṉcērpu
   vaḷikiḷarn taṉṉa celaviṉar vaḷiyiṭait         170
   tīyeḻun taṉṉa tiṟaliṉar tīppaṭa
   urumiṭit taṉṉa kuraliṉar viḻumiya
   uṟukuṟai maruṅkiṟṟam peṟumuṟai koṇmā
   rantarat koṭpiṉar vantuṭaṉ kāṇat
   tāvil koḷkai maṭantaiyoṭu ciṉṉā         175
   ḷāvi ṉaṉkuṭi yacaitalu muriya ṉatā aṉṟu.
   --------
   4. tiruvērakam
   irumūṉ ṟeytiya viyalpiṉiṉ vaḻāa
   tiruvarc cuṭṭiya palvēṟu tolkuṭi
   aṟunāṉ kiraṭṭi yiḷamai nalliyāṇ
   ṭāṟiṉiṟ kaḻippiya vaṟaṉavil koḷkai         180
   mūṉṟuvakaik kuṟitta muttīc celvat
   tirupiṟap pāḷar poḻutaṟintu nuvala
   oṉpatu koṇṭa muṉṟupuri nuṇñ
   pularāk kāḻakam pulara vuṭīi
   uccik kūppiya taṟpukaḻn         185
   tāṟeḻut taṭakkiya varumaṟaik kēḷvi
   nāviyaṉ maruṅki ṉavilap pāṭi
   viraiyuṟu naṟumala rēntip perituvan
   tērakat tuṟaitalu muriya ṉatāaṉṟu.
   ------------
   5. kuṉṟutōṟāṭal
   paiṅkoṭi naṟaikkā yiṭaiyiṭupu vēla         190
   ṉampotip puṭṭil viraiik kuḷaviyoṭu
   veṇkū tāḷan toṭutta kaṇṇiya
   ṉaṟuñcān taṇinta kēḻkiṉar mārpiṟ
   koṭuntoḻil valviṟ kolaiiya kāṉavar
   nīṭamai viḷainta tēkkaṭ ṭēṟaṟ         195
   kuṉṟakac ciṟukuṭik kiḷaiyuṭaṉ makiḻntu
   toṇṭakac ciṟupaṟaik kuravai yayara
   viraluṉarp paviḻnta vēṟupaṭu naṟuṅkāṟ
   kuṇṭucuṉai pūtta vaṇṭupaṭu kaṇṇi
   iṇaitta kōtai yaṇaitta kūntaṉ         200
   muṭitta kullai yilaiyuṭai naṟumpūc
   ceṅkāṉ marāatta vāliṇa riṭaiyiṭupu
   curumpuṇat toṭutta peruntaṇ māttaḻai
   tiruntukā ḻalku ṟiḷaippa vuṭīi
   mayilkaṇ ṭaṉṉa maṭanaṭai makaḷiroṭu         205
   ceyyaṉ civanta vāṭaiyaṉ cevvaraic
   ceyalait taṇṭaḷir tuyalvaruṅ kātiṉaṉ
   kacciṉaṉ kaḻaliṉaṉ ceccaik kaṇṇiyaṉ
   kuḻalaṉ kōṭṭaṉ kuṟumpal liyattaṉ
   takaraṉ maññaiyaṉ pukaril cēvalaṅ         210
   koṭiya ṉeṭiyaṉ ṟoṭiyaṇi tōḷa
   ṉarampārt taṉṉa viṉkuraṟ ṟokutiyoṭu
   kuṟumpoṟik koṇṭa naṟuntaṇ cāyaṉ
   maruṅkiṟ kaṭṭiya nilaṉērpu tukiliṉaṉ
   muḻavuṟaḻ taṭakkaiyi ṉiyala vēnti         215
   meṉṟōṭ palpiṇai taḻīit talaittantu
   kuṉṟutē ṟāṭalu niṉṟataṉ paṇpē yatāaṉṟu.

   6. paḻamutir cōlai
   ciṟutiṉai malaroṭu viraii maṟiyaṟuttu
   vāraṇak koṭiyoṭu vayiṟpaṭa niṟīi
   ūrūr koṇṭa cīrkeḻu viḻaliṉu         220
   mārvala rētta mēlaru nilaiyiṉum
   vēlaṉ ṟaiiya veṟiyayar kaḷaṉuṅ
   kāṭuṅ kāvuṅ kaviṉpeṟu turuttiyum
   yāṟuṅ kuḷaṉum vēṟupal vaippuñ
   catukkamuñ cantiyum putuppūṅ kaṭampu         225
   maṉṟamum potiyiluṅ kantuṭai nilaiyiṉu
   māṇṭalaik koṭiyoṭu maṇṇi yamaivara
   neyyō ṭaiyavi yappiyai turaittuk
   kuṭantam paṭṭuk koḻumalar citaṟi
   muraṇko ḷuruvi ṉiraṇṭuṭa ṉuṭīic         230
   cennūl yāttu veṇpori citaṟi
   matavali nilaiiya māttāṭ koḻuviṭaik
   kurutiyoṭu viraiit tūveḷ ḷarici
   cilpalic ceytu palpirap pirīic
   ciṟupacu mañcaṉoṭu naṟuvirai teḷittup         235
   peruntaṇ kaṇavīra naṟuntaṇ mālai
   tuṇaiyuṟa vaṟuttut tūṅka nāṟṟi
   naḷimalaic cilampi ṉaṉṉakar vāḻtti
   naṟumpukai yeṭuttuk kuṟiñci pāṭi
   imiḻicai yaruviyō ṭiṉṉiyaṅ kaṟaṅka         240
   uruvap palpūt tūuy veruvarak
   kurutic centiṉai parappik kuṟamakaṇ
   murukiyam niṟuttu muraṇiṉa ruṭka
   murukāṟṟup paṭutta vurukeḻu viyaṉaka
   rāṭukaḷañ cilampap pāṭip palavuṭaṉ         245
   kōṭuvāy vaittuk koṭumaṇi yiyakki
   ōṭāp pūṭkaip piṇimukam vāḻtti
   vēṇṭunar vēṇṭiyāṅ keytiṉar vaḻipaṭa
   āṇṭāṇ ṭuṟaitalu maṟinta vāṟē
   āṇṭāṇ ṭāyiṉu mākak kāṇṭaka         250
   muntunī kaṇṭuḻi mukaṉamarn tēttik
   kaitoḻūup paravik kāluṟa vaṇaṅki
   neṭumperuñ cimaiyattu nīlap paiñcuṉē
   aivaru ḷoruva ṉaṅkai yēṟpa
   aṟuvar payanta vāṟamar celva         255
   ālkeḻu kaṭavuṭ putalva mālvarai
   malaimkaḷ makaṉē māṟṟōr kūṟṟē
   veṟṟi velpōrk koṟṟavai ciṟuva
   iḻaiyaṇi ciṟappiṟ paḻaiyōḷ kuḻavi
   vāṉōr vaṇaṅkuviṟ ṟāṉait talaiva         260
   mālai mārpa nūlaṟi pulava
   ceruvi loruva poruviṟaṉ maḷḷa
   antaṇar veṟukkai yaṟintōr coṉmalai
   maṅkaiyar kaṇava mainta rēṟē
   vēlkeḻu taṭakkaic cālperuñ celva         265
   kuṉṟaṅ koṉṟa kuṉṟāk koṟṟattu
   viṇporu neṭuvaraik kuṟiñcik kiḻava
   palarpukaḻ naṉmoḻip pulava rēṟē
   arumpeṟaṉ marapiṟ perumpeyar muruka
   nacaiyunark kārttu micaipē rāḷa         270
   alantōrk kaḷikkum polampūṭ cēey
   maṇṭamar kaṭantaniṉ veṉṟā ṭakalattup
   paricilart tāṅku murukeḻu neṭuvēḷ
   periyō rēttum perumpeya riyavuḷ
   cūrmaruṅ kaṟutta moympiṉ matavali         275
   pōrmiku poruna kurici leṉappala
   yāṉaṟi yaḷavaiyi ṉētti yāṉātu
   niṉṉaḷan taṟitaṉ maṉṉuyirk karumaiyi
   ṉiṉṉaṭi yuḷḷi vantaṉa ṉiṉṉoṭu
   puraiyuna rillāp pulamai yōyeṉak         280
   kuṟittatu moḻiyā vaḷavaiyiṟ kuṟittuṭaṉ
   vēṟupal luruviṟ kuṟumpal kūḷiyar
   cāṟayar kaḷattu vīṟupeṟat tōṉṟi
   aḷiyaṉ ṟāṉē mutuvā yiravalaṉ
   vantōṉ perumaniṉ vaṇpukaḻ nayanteṉa         285
   iṉiyavu nallavu naṉipala vēttit
   teyvañ cāṉṟa tiṟalviḷaṅ kuruviṉ
   vāṉṟōy nivappiṟ ruṉvan teyti
   aṇaṅkucā luyarnilai taḻīip paṇṭaittaṉ
   maṇaṅkamaḻ teyvat tiḷanalaṅ kāṭṭi         290
   añca lōmpumati yaṟivaṉiṉ varaveṉa
   aṉpuṭai naṉmoḻi yaḷaii viḷiviṉ
   ṟiruṇiṟa munnīr vaḷaiiya vulakat
   torunī yākit tōṉṟa viḻumiya
   peṟalarum parici ṉalkumati palavuṭaṉ         295
   vēṟupaḵ ṟukili ṉuṭaṅki yakilcuman
   tāra muḻumuta luruṭṭi vēraṟ
   pūvuṭai yalaṅkuciṉai pulampavēr kīṇṭu
   viṇporu neṭuvaraip paritiyiṟ ṟoṭutta
   taṇkama ḻalariṟāl citaiya naṉpala         300
   āciṉi mutucuḷai kalāva mīmicai
   nāka naṟumala rutira yūkamoṭu
   māmuka mucukkalai paṉippap pūnuta
   virumpiṭi kuḷirppa vīcip peruṅkaḷiṟṟu
   muttuṭai vāṉkōṭu taḻīit tattuṟṟu         305
   naṉpoṉ maṇiniṟaṅ kiḷarap poṉkoḻiyā
   vāḻai muḻumuta ṟumiyat tāḻai
   iḷanīr viḻukkulai yutirat tākkik
   kaṟikkoṭik karuntuṇar cāyap poṟippuṟa
   maṭanaṭai maññai palavuṭaṉ verīik         310
   kōḻi vayappeṭai yiriyak kēḻalō
   ṭirumpaṉai veḷiṟṟiṉ puṉcā yaṉṉa
   kurūumayi riyākkaik kuṭāvaṭi yuḷiyam
   peruṅkal viṭaraḷaic ceṟiyak karuṅkōṭ
   ṭāmā nallēṟu cilaippac cēṇiṉ         315
   ṟiḻumeṉa viḻitaru maruvip
   paḻamutircōlai malaikiḻa vōṉē.

                  tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai - teḷivurai

   murukaṉ perumai
   (1-6: oṉṟu mutal āṟām aṭi varai)
   ulakattār makiḻumpaṭi valamāka eḻuntu ulakaic cuṟṟuvatupōl
   tōṉṟukiṟa—palarum pukaḻntu vaṇaṅkum ceññāyiṟṟaik (kālaiyil)
   nīlakkaṭalil kaṇṭāṟpōṉṟu (nīlamayiliṉ mēl tōṉṟi) viṭāmal vīcukiṟa
   neṭuntolaivu eṅkum ceṉṟu paravi viḷaṅkukiṟa cevviya oḷiviḷakkattaiyum
   (pirakācattaiyum), uṟṟa aṉparait tāṅkik kākkum aḻakuṭaiya valiya
   tiruvaṭikaḷaiyum, aḻikka vēṇṭiyavarkaḷait tēyttu aḻitta iṭi otta periya
   valiya kaikaḷaiyum uṭaiyavaṉum, kuṟṟam aṟṟa kaṟpiṉaiyum viḷakkamāṉa
   neṟṟiyiṉaiyum uṭaiya teyvayāṉaiyiṉ kaṇavaṉum,
   murukaṉatu mārpu mālai
   (7-11 )
   kaṭalilē nīrai moṇṭukoṇṭataṉāl niṟaivuṟṟuk karukkoṇṭa kariya mukil
   (mēkam) miṉṉal oḷi piḷantu vīcukiṉṟa viṇṇilē vaḷamāṉa nīrttuḷikaḷaic
   cinti āṇṭiṉ mutal maḻaiyai mikutiyākap peyta kuḷirnta naṟumaṇamuḷḷa
   kāṉakattil iruṭṭākumpaṭi aṭarntuḷḷa parutta aṭimarattaiyuṭaiya
   ceṅkaṭampiṉ uruḷai pōṉṟa pūkkaḷāl toṭutta kuḷirnta mālai puraḷukiṟa
   mārpai uṭaiyavaṉum,
   tēva makaḷiriṉ aṇikoḷ mēṉi
   (12-19)
   periya mūṅkil ōṅkiya neṭuntolaivu uyarntuḷḷa malaiyil, cataṅkai cuṟṟiya
   polivuḷḷa civanta ciṟiya aṭiyuṭaṉ (pātattuṭaṉ) kūṭiya tiraṇṭa
   kālkaḷaiyum, vaḷainta iṭuppiṉaiyum, mūṅkil pōṉṟa tōḷiṉaiyum,
   intirakōpap pūcci pōṉṟa-cāyam - tōykkāmalēyē iyaṟkaiyākavē civantuḷḷa
   pūvēlaippāṭuṭaiya uṭaiyiṉaiyum, pala maṇikaḷai niravik kōtta
   mēkalaiyaṇinta alkuliṉaiyum, kaiyāl ceyaṟkaiyāyp puṉaintu uṇṭākkāta -
   iyaṟkai yaḻaku vāytta poliviṉaiyum, nāval (cāmpūntam) eṉṉum peyaruṭaiya
   oruvakaip poṉṉāl puṉaintuceyta — viḷaṅkum aṇikalaṉkaḷaiyum,
   neṭuntolaivum tāṇṭi viḷaṅkukiṟa (pirakācikkiṟa) kuṟṟam aṟṟa
   tiruvuṭalaiyum (uṭaiyavarāy),
   tēvamakaḷiriṉ ceyalkaḷum āṭṭamum
   (20-41)
   tōḻimār āyntu muṭitta—iṇaiyāka iraṇṭāka vakirnta — neyyīrap pacaiyuḷḷa
   kūntalilē civanta kāmpaiyuṭaiya ciṟiya veṭcip pūkkaḷai iṭaiyiṭaiyē
   cerukiyum, pacumaiyāṉa taṇṭaiyuṭaiya kuvaḷai malariṉ tūya itaḻkaḷaik
   kiḷḷip patittum, teyva utti eṉṉum peyaruṭaiya talaikkōlattaiyum
   valampuriccaṅku vaṭivāyc ceyta talaikkōlattaiyum uriya iṭattilē
   aṇintuvaittum poṭṭu vaitta maṇam vīcukiṉṟa aḻakiya neṟṟiyil curuviṉatu
   piḷanta vāypōlac ceyta cuṭṭiyiṉait toṅkac ceytu aḻakuṟuttiyum, muḻutum
   muṭitta kuṟṟam aṟṟa koṇṭaiyilē periya kuḷirnta caṇpaka malaraic
   cerukiyum, kariya puṟa itaḻuṭaṉ tuyyiṉaiyum (pūmpañciṉaiyum) uṭaiya
   viḷakkamāṉa marutap pūkkottukkaḷai (akkoṇṭaiyiṉ mēlē) patittum,
   kiḷaiyiṉiṉṟum aḻakuṟat tōṉṟi eḻunta—nīriṉ kīḻuḷḷa cevviya arumpukaḷē
   iṇaittuttoṭutta mālaiyiṉaik koṇṭaiyaic cuṟṟi vaḷaiya vaittum, tuṇaiyāka
   iru pakkalum vaḷaviya kātukaḷil niṟaiyac cerukiya oḷḷiya acōkantaḷir
   (kīḻē toṅki) nuṇvēlai mikka aṇikalaṉ pūṇṭa mārpil acaintukoṇṭirukkavum,
   tiṇṇiya vayiramuṭaiya naṟumaṇamuḷḷa cantaṉattai araittu uṇṭākkiya
   polivum niṟamum mikka kuḻampiṉai maṇam kamaḻum marutap pūṅkottiṉaip
   patittāṟpōlak kōṅkiṉ kuvinta mokkuppōṉṟa iḷa mulaikaḷil appiyum, (mēlum
   antac cantaṉak kuḻampiṉ mēlē) virinta vēṅkaimalariṉ nuṇṇiya makarantap
   poṭiyai appippatittum, aḻakupeṟat tōṉṟumpaṭi viḷāviṉatu kuṟuntaḷiraik
   kiḷḷi (ammulaikaḷiṉ mēl) teṟittuk koṇṭum, kōḻiyaik koṇṭu uyarnta -
   etirniṉṟu vellum (murukaṉatu) veṟṟikkoṭi peritum vāḻka eṉṟu vāḻttiyum,
   palaruñ cērntu ciṟappu mikka malaippakutiyellām etirolikkumpaṭi
   (murukaṉ mēl) pāṭiyum, añcutaṟkuriya teyva makaḷir viḷaiyāṭukiṉṟa
   cōlaikaḷai uṭaiya,
   murukaṉiṉ muṭimālai
   (42-44)
   kuraṅkum aṟiya muṭiyāta maraṅkaḷ aṭarntu ceṟinta aṭukkiya
   malaippakutiyil malarntuḷḷa — vaṇṭukaḷum moykkāta — neruppuppōl cuṭar
   viṭukiṟa periya kuḷirnta kāntaḷ pū mālaik kaṇṇiyaic cūṭiya muṭiyai
   uṭaiyavaṉum,
   murukaṉatu vēl
   (45-46)
   pāriṉum muṟpaṭṭatāṉa kuḷirnta kaṭal kalaṅkumpaṭi uḷḷē pukuntu
   cūrarkaḷiṉ mutalvaṉāṉa cūrapaṉmaṉaik koṉṟa—oḷivīcukiṟa—ilaivaṭivāṉa
   nīṇṭa vēlai (uṭaiyavaṉum).
   arakkaroṭu poruta pōrkkaḷattil
   pēy makaḷ āṭiya tuṇaṅkaik kūttu
   (47-56)
   ularnta mayiriṉaiyum, varicai māṟiya paṟkaḷaiyum, piḷanta vāyiṉaiyum,
   cuḻalum karuviḻiyuḷḷa paciya kaṇkaḷaiyum, añcattakka pārvaiyiṉaiyum,
   pituṅkiya kaṇṇaiyuṭaiya kōṭṭāṉōṭu koṭiya pāmpum poruntit toṅkuvataṉālē
   periya mulaikaḷai alaikkaḻikkiṉṟa kātukaḷaiyum, cora corappāṉa
   vayiṟṟiṉaiyum, naṭuṅkac ceyyum naṭaiyiṉaiyum uṭaiya—kaṇṭār añcattakka
   pēyp peṇṇāṉavaḷ, kuruti tōynta kūriya nakattaiyuṭaiya koṭiya viralālē
   kaṇṉait tōṇṭit tiṉṟa mikka muṭai nāṟṟam vīcumkariya (arakkar) talaiyai
   oḷḷiya toṭi aṇinta kaiyilē ēntik koṇṭum, arakkar añcumpaṭi etir niṉṟu
   (murukaṉ) poruta veṟṟip pōrkkaḷattaip pukaḻntu pāṭik koṇṭum. tōḷait
   tūkkiyacaittu niṇattait tiṉṉum vāyai uṭaiyavaḷāyt ‘tuṇaṅkai’ eṉṉum
   kūttai āṭikkoṇṭirukka,
   arakkarai veṉṟa murukaṉ cēvaṭi paṭartal
   (57-66)
   makkaḷ—vilaṅku ākiya irupirivu vaṭivam amainta oru periya arakka uṭalai
   aṟuttu vēṟu vēṟu kūṟākkum vakaiyil añcumpaṭi etir ceṉṟu, arakkariṉ
   mikka valimai aṭaṅkumpaṭi, kaviḻnta pūṅkottukkaḷai uṭaiya māmaramāka
   niṉṟa arakkar mutalvaṉākiya cūrapaṉ maṉai veṭṭik koṉṟa—kaḷaṅkam aṟṟa
   veṟṟiyiṉaiyum muḻutum aṟiya muṭiyāta ciṟanta pukaḻiṉaiyum cemmaiyāṉa
   vēliṉaiyum uṭaiya cēyōṉumākiya murukap perumāṉuṭaiya tiruvaṭiyaic
   ceṉṟaṭaiyum ciṟanta uḷḷattuṭaṉ, nalam ceyyum taṉmaiyatākiya
   vīṭṭulakiṉai virumpivāḻum payaṇattiṉai nī virumpi mēṟkoṇṭaṉaiyeṉil,
   palavakaiyil nalla uḷḷattilē uḷḷa iṉiya viruppam niṟai vēṟumpaṭi
   ippoḻutē nī eṇṇiya nalviṉaip payaṉaip peṟuvāy! (aṉpaṉē! murukaṉ
   eḻuntaruḷiyuḷḷa iṭaṅkaḷai c cuṭṭik kūṟukiṟēṉ, kēḷ!)
   maturaiyaic cārnta tirupparaṅ kuṉṟam
   (67-77)
   pōrukku varuvār varuka eṉṟu kūṟi ēṟṟiya—viṇṇil neṭun tolaivu uyarntuḷḷa
   nīṇṭa pōrk koṭiyiṉaiyum, nūlāl varintu kaṭṭic ceyta pantum pāvaiyum
   (aṟukkum etirikaḷ iṉmaiyiṉ) vaṟitē toṅkik koṇṭu kiṭappa,
   pakaivaraiyellām aḻittu viṭṭa (kāraṇattāl) — pōr eṉpatē aṟṟup pōṉa
   peruvāyilaiyum, tirumakaḷ amarntuḷḷa — vañcakam illāta aṅkāṭik
   kaṭaitteruviṉaiyum, māṭa māḷikaikaḷ mikunta maṟṟa terukkaḷaiyum uṭaiya
   maturaiyiṉ mēṟkup pakkalil uḷḷa, mikka cēṟu poruntiya akaṉṟa vayalilē
   virintu malarnta—muḷḷōṭu kūṭiya taṇṭaiyuṭaiya tāmarai malarilē iravil
   tūṅkiyum mikka viṭiyaṟkālaiyil tēṉ kamaḻum neytal pūvai ūtikkōtiyum
   ñāyiṟu tōṉṟiya piṉṉar kaṇpōl malarntuḷḷa viruppamāṉa cuṉaip pūkkaḷilē
   aḻakiya ciṟakuṭaiya vaṇṭukaḷiṉ aḻakiya kūṭṭam āravārikkum vaḷamuṭaiya
   tirupparaṅ kuṉṟattilē virumpi eḻuntaruḷiyiruttalaiyum urimaiyākak
   koṇṭavaṉ murukaṉ attirupparaṅkuṉṟam allāmalum, —
   (tiruccīralaivāy)
   murukaṉiṉ yāṉai ūrti
   (78-82)
   kūriya tōṭṭi (aṅkucam) kuttiya vaṭu āḻnta puḷḷi poruntiya neṟṟiyil
   vāṭāta poṉṉari mālai paṭṭattōṭu toṅki acaiya, tāḻntu toṅkum maṇikaḷ
   iraṇṭum māṟi māṟi iraṭṭai oli ceykiṉṟa iru pakkaṅkaḷaiyum virainta
   naṭaiyiṉaiyum emaṉai otta—vellutaṟku ariya valimaiyiṉaiyum uṭaiya—kāṟṟu
   eḻuntāṟpōl viraintu celkiṟa kaḷiṟṟu yāṉaiyiṉmēl eḻuntaruḷi,
   murukaṉ tirumukam
   (83- 90) aivakai vaṭivuṭaṉ ceyvēlaippāṭu mutirnta muṭiyōṭu
   tikaḻkiṟa-muraṇpaṭṭa oḷi vīcum aḻakiya maṇikaḷ miṉṉalai otta oḷi
   viḷakkattuṭaṉ talaiyil polivutara, oḷivīci acaikiṟa-vēlaippāṭu amainta
   poṟkātaṇikaḷ, neṭuntolaivu viḷaṅkit tōṉṟum iyalpuṭaiya oḷiviṭum
   tiṅkaḷaic cūḻnta nīṅkāta viṇmīṉkaḷ pōṉṟu tikaḻvaṉavāy oḷivīca,
   kuṟṟamaṟṟa kuṟikkōḷuṭaṉ tam tavattoḻilai muṭikkum tavattōriṉ uḷḷattil
   etirppaṭṭu eḻuntu tōṉṟukiṟa nalloḷiyum cenniṟamum mikka āṟu mukaṅkaḷuḷ
   ,
   āṟu mukaṅkaḷiṉ aruñ ceyalkaḷ
   (91 - 102) orumukam, iruḷ cūḻnta periya ulakam kaḷaṅkam aṟṟut tikaḻap

   nīḷat toṅkum vāṭṭattil oṉṟum ataṉmēl kuṟukku vāṭṭattil oṉṟumāka orucēra
   uṭuttu, cenniṟa nūlaik kaiyilē kāppākak kaṭṭi, veṇmaiyāṉa poriyiṉaic
   citaṟit tūvi, mata valimai mikka periya kālaiyuṭaiya koḻutta kiṭāviṉ
   kurutiyōṭu (irattattōṭu) kalanta tūya veḷḷariciyiṉaic ciṟupaliyākap
   pōṭṭup pala pirappukaḷai (aricik kalaṅkaḷaip) paravalāka vaittu, ciṟiya
   pacu mañcaḷuṭaṉ naṟumaṇap poruḷkaḷaiyum tūvit teḷittu,
   (muṉṉatiṉ toṭarcci)
   (236-244)
   periya kuḷirnta cevvalari mālaiyiṉaiyum naṟumaṇa mikka kuḷircciyāṉa
   maṟṟa mālaikaḷaiyum orunikar aḷavāka aṟuttu acaintu koṇṭirukkumāṟu
   toṅka viṭṭu, ceṟivāṉa malaiccāralil uḷḷa nalla ūrkaḷ vaḷam peṟuka eṉa
   vāḻtti, naṟumaṇap poruḷkaḷaip pukaittu (tūpamiṭṭu), malai nilattiṟku
   uriya kuṟiñcip paṇṇaip pāṭi, muḻaṅkum icaiyaiyuṭaiya aruviyoliyuṭaṉ
   cērntu iṉiya icaik karuvikaḷum olittuk koṇṭirukka, palvēṟu
   niṟaṅkaḷaiyuṭaiya palavakai malarkaḷaiyum tūvi, accam tōṉṟak kurutiyōṭu
   (irattattōṭu) kalanta civanta tiṉai ariciyaip parappi, teyvam ēṟiya
   kuṟak kuṭimakaḷ murukaṉukku ukanta icaikkaruvikaḷē icaittu māṟupaṭuvōr
   añcumpaṭi iyaṅki, murukaṉ āṇṭu eḻuntaruḷumpaṭi vaḻippaṭuttik koṇarnta-
   oruvakait teyva accam (payapakti) niṟainta periya nakariṉkaṇṇē
   (toṭarcci)
   (245-249)
   veṟiyāṭum (cāmiyāṭum) kaḷaṅkaḷ etirolikkumāṟu pāṭi, palavakai ūtu
   kompukaḷai vāyil vaittu ūti, vaḷainta maṇikaḷai āṭṭi olikkac ceytu,
   aḻiyāta āṟṟaluṭaiya (murukaṉatu) yāṉaiyai vāḻtti, vēṇṭuvār
   vēṇṭuvaṉavaṟṟai vēṇṭiyapaṭi aṭaintavarāy vaḻipāṭu ceyya, anta anta
   iṭaṅkaḷilum murukaṉ amarntiruppatum aṟinta ceytiyēyām.
   murukaṉaip pōṟṟum muṟai
   (250-281)
   — (itukāṟum kūṟiya) avvav viṭaṅkaḷilē yāyiṉum piṟa iṭaṅkaḷilē yāyiṉum
   murukaṉ amarntirukka, avaṉ uṉṉaik kāṇumpaṭi nī muṟpaṭṭu avaṉaik
   kaṇṭakkāl, mukam virumpip pōṟṟutal ceytu kaiyāl toḻutu kumpiṭṭuk kālilē
   porunta viḻuntu vaṇaṅki (piṉ varumāṟu murukaṉai viḷittu,
   aḵtāvatu:-)“nīṇṭaperiya imayamalaiyiṉ ucciyilē nīlappul (taruppai)
   vaḷarnta pacumaiyāṉa cuṉaiyilē, viṇ, kāṟṟu neruppu, nīr, maṇ eṉṉum
   aintiṉ teyvaṅkaḷuḷ oruvaṉāṉa nerupput tēvaṉ (akkiṉi pakavāṉ) taṉ
   aḻakiya kaiyilē ēṟṟuvarak kārttikaip peṇṭir aṟuvar peṟṟeṭutta āṟu kūṟāy
   amarntuḷḷa celvaṉē! kallāla marattiṉ kīḻamarnta kaṭavuḷiṉ (civaṉiṉ )
   makaṉē! periya malaiyākiya imayamalaiyaracaṉ makaḷākiya umaiyiṉ makaṉē!
   pakaivarkku emaṉē! veṟṟivēl ēntiya pōrtteyvamākiya koṟṟavaiyiṉ
   kumaraṉē! aṇikalaṉ pūṇṭa ciṟappuṭaiya paḻamperun teyvamākiya
   kāṭukiḻāḷiṉ kuḻantaiyē! tēvarkaḷ vaṇaṅkum-villēntiya paṭaikaḷiṉ
   talaivaṉē! mālaiyaṇinta mārpaṉē! nūlkaḷait teḷinta pulavaṉē! pōrt
   toḻilil oppaṟṟavaṉē! porukiṉṟa veṟṟi vīraṉē! antaṇar celvamē!
   aṟiñarkaḷiṉ pukaḻccol malaiyē! teyva yāṉē, vaḷḷi ākiya maṅkaiyarkaḷiṉ
   kaṇavaṉē! maṟamaintar kaḷiṉ ariyēṟē (ciṅkamē)! vēlēntiya kaiyaiyuṭaiya
   perumai cāṉṟa celvaṉē! kurukumalaiyaip (kiravuñca kiriyaip)
   piḷanta-kuṟaiyāta veṟṟiyiṉaiyuṭaiya - viṇṇōṭu mōtum nīṇṭa malaikaḷ
   niṟainta kuṟiñci nilattiṟku urimai pūṇṭa talaivaṉē! palarum pukaḻum
   naṉmoḻi vaḻaṅkum pulavarkaḷiṉ talaimai ēṟē! peṟutaṟku ariya
   naṉmarapiṉaiyuṭaiya — periya peyar peṟṟa murukaṉē! oṉṟai virumpi
   vantōrkku ataṉē niṟaivikkum perum pukaḻāḷaṉē! tuṉpuṟṟōrkku aruḷ ceykiṟa
   — poṉṉaṇi pūṇṭa cēyōṉē! vantu maṇṭi mōtum pōrkaḷaik kaṭanta—veṟṟi
   pāṭalukku uriya mārpilē, paricil vēṇṭivarum aṉparkaḷait taḻuvit tāṅkik
   kākkiṉṟa — aṉpu accattiṟku (paya paktikku) uriya neṭiya cevvēḷē!
   periyōrkaḷ pōṟṟum-peṟalarum peyar peṟṟa kaṭavuḷ talaivaṉē! cūrapaṉmaṉiṉ
   kulattai vērōṭu aṟutta āṟṟalaiyum mata valimaiyaiyum uṭaiyavaṉē!
   pōrvalla maṟavaṉē! mēlāṉa koṭait talaivaṉē! eṉṟellām palavāṟāka yāṉ
   aṟinta aḷavu niṉṉaip pōṟṟiyum maṉaniṟaivu peṟātēṉāy, niṉ perumai
   muḻutum aḷaviṭṭu aṟintu pōṟṟutal ulakil uḷḷa uyirkaṭku aritātaliṉ
   iyaṉṟa aḷavu pōṟṟi, niṉ tiruvaṭiyaip peṟak karuti vantuḷḷēṉ ; niṉṉōṭu
   oppār illāta pulamaiyuṭaiyavaṉē! aṭiyēṟku aruḷ ceyka!” eṉṟu nī uḷḷattil
   kuṟitta eṇṇattaic collāta aḷavilēyē (colvataṟkuḷḷēyē),
   kūḷiyar aṟimukam
   (281-286)
   uṭaṉē uṉṉaik kuṟikkoṇṭu, palvēṟu vaṭiviṉaiyuṭaiya kuṟukiya pala kūḷiyar
   eṉṉum teyvap paṇiyāḷar (piṉ varumāṟu uṉṉai murukaṉukku
   aṟimukappaṭuttuvar; aḵtāvatu:—), viḻā naṭaipeṟum kaḷattilē vīṟuṭaṉ
   (polivuṭaṉ) tōṉṟi, ‘inta mutiya paṇpaṭṭa iravalaṉ iraṅkattakkavaṉ tāṉ;
   perumāṉē, niṉatu vaḷaviya pukaḻukkuriya aruḷai virumpi niṉpāl
   vantuḷḷāṉ’ eṉṟu aṟimukam ceytu, iṉiyavaiyum nallavaiyumāka mikap pala
   kūṟi ētti niṟka,
   murukaṉ paricaḷikkum muṟai
   (287-295)
   paḻamutir cōlai malaiyāṉ
   [malaiyaruviyiṉ ceyal]
   (295-306)
   (ittakaiya murukap perumāṉ iṉṉum eḻuntaruḷiyirukkum iṭam oṉṟu
   varumātu:-)
   pala vitamāka vēṟupaṭṭa pala tuṇikkoṭikaḷaip pōla acaintu acaintu
   (aruvikaḷ) ōṭivantu, akil marattaip peyarttuc cumantu koṇṭu, cantaṉa
   marattai muḻu aṭi marattōṭu uruṭṭit taḷḷi, mūṅkiliṉ pūvōṭu kūṭiya
   acaiyum kiḷaikaḷ taṉittup pōka vēraip piḷantu, vāṉattai mōtum nīṇṭa
   malaippakutiyilē ñāyiṟṟiṉ vaṭṭampōl tēṉīkkaḷ toṭuttuk
   kaṭṭiya—kuḷirnta—maṇam kamaḻkiṟa-virinta tēṉkūṭu citaintu aḻiyavum,
   nalla pala āciṉip palāviṉ mutirnta cuḷaikaḷ nīrōṭu kalakkavum, mikunta
   malai ucciyiluḷḷa curapuṉṉaiyiṉ naṟiya malarkaḷ utirntu koṭṭavum, yūkam
   eṉṉum karuṅkuraṅkuṭaṉ kariya mukamuṭaiya mucukkalai eṉṉum kuraṅkum
   naṭuṅkavum, puḷḷi poruntiya mattakam uṭaiya periya peṇyāṉai kuḷirāl
   utaṟavum nīrai vīciyeṟintu, periya āṇ yāṉaiyiṉ muttukkaḷai uṭaiya nīṇṭa
   kompukaḷai iḻuttuk koṇṭu vantu , nalla poṉṉum maṇiyum tam oḷiniṟam
   viḷaṅkac ceytu, poṉ tukaḷaik koḻittuk koṇṭu,
   (aruviyiṉ ceyal—toṭarcci)
   (307-317)
   vāḻaiyiṉ muḻu maramum oṭintu cāyavum teṉṉaiyiṉ periya iḷanīrk kulai
   utiravum nīrai vīciyeṟintu tākki, miḷakuk koṭiyiṉ kariya kottukkaḷ
   cāyavum, piṉpuṟattē pīli poruntiya iḷanaṭaiyuṭaiya mayilkaḷ pala cērntu
   añcavum, valiya peṭṭaik kōḻikaḷ irintōṭavum. āṇ paṉṟiyuṭaṉ, uḷḷē veḷiṟu
   uṭaiya (vayiram illāta) kariya paṉai marattiṉ pulliya ceṟumpu pōṉṟa
   karumayirōṭu kūṭiya uṭampiṉaiyum vaḷainta kālaṭiyiṉaiyum uṭaiya karaṭi
   periya kal piḷappuk kukaiyilē ceṉṟu aṭaintu kiṭakkavum, kariya
   kompukaḷaiyuṭaiya āmā iṉattiṉ nalla kāḷaikaḷ kataṟik kattavum palvēṟu
   ceyalkaḷ purintu uyarnta malaiyucciyiliruttu ‘iḻum’—‘iḻum’ eṉṉum
   oliyuṭaṉ kīḻnōkki iṟaṅki ōṭivarum aruviyiṉai uṭaiya—paḻam mutirntu
   kaṉinta cōlai cūḻnta — ‘paḻamutir cōlaimalai’ eṉṉum malaikkum urimai
   uṭaiyavaṉāvāṉ ammurukaṉ — avaṉatu aruḷ peṟuka!
   tirumurukāṟṟup paṭait teḷivurai muṟṟum.

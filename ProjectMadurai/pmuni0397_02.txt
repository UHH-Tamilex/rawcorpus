naṉṉeṟiyik kaṭippiṭittuk kariyēṟiṉa periyōrkaḷiṉ carittiram iṉṉateṉat terintu
koḷḷutal mikavum payaṉ tarakkūṭiyatu. intac carittirat talivarāṉa cuppiramaṇiya
piḷḷiyavarkaḷ carittirattāl "naṉmi kaṭipiṭi" "muyaṟci tiruviṉiyākkum" "kaṭavuḷi
nampiṉōr kiviṭap paṭār", eṉṉum āṉṟōr vākku meyppaṭutal naṉku pulappaṭum. ivar
iyaṟṟiya taṉippāṭalkaḷ ivaratu mutirnta paktiyiṉ niliyik kāṭṭum. ipperiyāriṉ
carittiratti eḻuta eṉṉit tūṇṭiṉavarum muruka paktaruṭ ciṟantavarumāṉa eṉatu naṇpar
ceṉṉi pōrāṭ ṭrasṭ āpīsil vēliyāyirukkum pirammasrī ī. vēṅkaṭarāv avarkaḷatu aṉpu
mikavum pārāṭṭaṟ pālatu. tamiḻ makkaḷ iccarittirattip paṭittu uyyum vaḻi iṉṉateṉa
kippiṭittut tamiḻkkaṭavuḷām srī cuppiramaṇiya mūrttiyiṉ tiruvaruṭku uriyarām
perumi yaṭital vēṇṭum eṉpatu aṭiyēṉ maṉak karuttu. āṟumukap perumāṉ eṉṟum aṅṅaṉē

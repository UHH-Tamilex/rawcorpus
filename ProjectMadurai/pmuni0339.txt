"mullittiṇi" : mu.varatarācaṉār

Source:
"mullittiṇi" : mu.varatarācaṉār
iraṇṭām patippu: akṭōpar 1955
urimi āciriyarkku
cātu accukkūṭam, irāyappēṭṭi, ceṉṉi-14
-----------------------

naṉṟiyuri

kātalpāṭṭukkaḷi neṟippaṭutti muṟiyuṟa vakutta perumi, paḻantamiḻ ilakkiyattiṉ
ciṟappiyalpākum. avvāṟu neṟiyum muṟiyum pōṟṟivaḷartta kāraṇattāltāṉ, paḻiya
kātalpāṭṭukkaḷil kaliccelvam miḷirvatuṭaṉ nuṭpamum tūymiyum kuṉṟāmal
viḷaṅkukiṉṟaṉa. maṟṟavarkaḷ uṭaliṉ paciyip puṉinturittuk kaḷittut tirinta
antakkālattil. tamiḻc cāṉṟōr uḷḷattiṉ uṇarvit terinteṭuttuk kalittoṇṭu āṟṟiya
ciṟappukkuk kāraṇam atuvē ākum.
mullittiṇiyāka vakukkappaṭṭa pāṭṭukkaḷiṉ peṟṟiyik kuṟittuc coṟpoḻivu nikaḻttum
vāyppi eṉakkuc ceṉṉip palkalik kaḻakam tantatu. palkalikkaḻakattār 'āṉarari rīṭar' eṉṉum
patavi aḷittu, ārāyccic coṟpoḻivukaḷ nikaḻttumāṟu kōriṉar. 1946 navampar 4,5
ākiya nāṭkaḷil avvāṟu nikaḻttiya coṟpoḻivē innūlāka amintatu. nūlvaṭivil iti
veḷiyiṭa urimi nalkiya palkalikkaḻakattārkku naṉṟiyuṭiyēṉ.
mu.va.

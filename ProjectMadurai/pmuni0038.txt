(patikam)
maṉṉiya perumpukaḻ maṟuil vāymoḻi
iṉici muraciṉ utiyañ cēraṟku
veḷiyaṉ vēṇmāḷ nalliṉi īṉṟamakaṉ

nayaṉil vaṉcol yavaṉarp piṇittu
neytalip peytu kipiṉ koḷīi
aruvili naṉkalam vayiramoṭu koṇṭu       10
peruviṟal mūtūrt tantupiṟark(ku) utavi
amiyārt tēytta aṇaṅ(ku)uṭi nōṉtāḷ
imiyavarampaṉ neṭuñcēralātaṉik
kumaṭṭūrk kaṇṇaṉār pāṭiṉār pattuppāṭṭu.
avitām: puṇṇumiḻ kuruti, maṟamvīṅku palpukaḻ, pūtta neytal,
cāṉṟōr meymmaṟi, niriya veḷḷam, tuyiliṉ pāyal,
valampaṭuviyaṉpaṇi, kūntal viṟaliyar, vaḷaṉaṟu pitiram, aṭṭumalarmārpaṉ
ivi pāṭṭiṉ patikam.
pāṭippeṟṟa paricil: umpaṟkāṭṭu ainnūṟūr piramatāyam* koṭuttu
muppatteṭṭuyāṇṭu teṉṉāṭṭuḷ varuvataṉiṟ pākam koṭuttāṉ.
imiya varampaṉ neṭuñcēralātaṉ aimpatteṭṭuyāṇṭu vīṟṟiruntāṉ.
[*piramatāyam = antaṇarkaḷukku viṭappaṭum iṟiyili nilam]

~~~~~~~~

~~~~~~~~

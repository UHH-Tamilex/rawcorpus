kavirāja paṇṭita, nā. kaṉakarājayyarāl iyaṟṟappeṟṟu
palavāṉkuṭi civanēcaccelvar uyartiruvāḷar

mukavuri.

mācil vīṇiyum māli matiyamum
vīcu teṉṟalum vīṅkiḷa vēṉilum
mūcu vaṇṭaṟi poykiyum pōṉṟatē
īcaṉ enti iṇiyaṭi nīḻalē.
viṟakiṟ ṟīyiṉaṉ pāliṟ paṭuneypōl
maṟiya niṉṟuḷaṉ māmaṇic cōtiyāṉ
uṟavu kōlnaṭ ṭuṇarvu kayiṟṟiṉāl
muṟuka vāṅkik kaṭiyamuṉ ṉiṟkumē.
      -tirunāvukkaracar tēvāram.
aṉpuru aṟivuru iṉpuru eṉṟu maṟikaḷ muḻaṅkum paramporuḷi 'āṇallaṉ peṇṇallaṉ allā
aliyallaṉ’ eṉṟu meyyaṉpar viḷakkuvatu tamiḻ makkaḷ aṟinta uṇmiyē, ''ammiyēyappā
voppilā maṇiyē aṉpiṉil viḷintavāramutē," eṉavum, “appaṉīyammiyu yaṇṇaṉunī," eṉavum,
‘tāyāṉa celvaṉ' eṉavum vākku maṉamiṟanta vaḷḷali vaḻuttuvatu tamiḻar marapākum. piṟapala
neṟipaṟṟiya makkaḷellōrum iṟivaṉi āṇpāl eṉṟa elliyil aṭakka virumpuvatu uṇmiyē.
avaṉ pāl, tiṇi, iṭam, kālam eṉṟa ellikaḷik kaṭantavaṉ eṉpatu tamiḻ makkaḷ naṉkaṟintuḷḷa
uṇmiyām. tamiḻakam eṅkum aruḷvaḷḷal amarvataṟkeṉap pala peruṅkōyilkaḷ amintuḷa.
civaparañcuṭari nāṭum aṉpar kuḻāṅkaḷukku uriya kōyilkaḷ āyiratteṭṭu eṉpar. mūvar
mutalikaḷ tēvāram aruḷic ceyta kālattē ivi viḷaṅkiṉa eṉpar. tirumāl eṉṟa
nāmarūpattāl iṟivaṉi vaḻipaṭum aṉpar kuḻāttukku nūṟṟeṭṭut tiruppatikaḷ uḷa eṉpar.
āḻvārkaḷ aruḷiya pirapantaṅkaḷip peṟṟa ivi iṟivaṉ aruḷ viḷakkam viḷakkuvaṉa ākum
eṉavum urippar. inta iru vakuppilum aṭaṅkāta civālayaṅkaḷ palavum tirumāl kōyilkaḷ
palavum, murukaṉ kōyilkaḷ palavum, piṟapala tēvālayaṅkaḷum nam nāṭeṅkum intāḷil uḷḷaṉa.
avaṟṟuṭ cila purāṇam peṟṟaṉavākavum uḷḷaṉa. vaṭamoḻiyiṟ purāṇam iruppatu talaṅkaḷiṉ
perumikku uṟutuṇiyākum eṉṟu karutiya aṉparkaḷ perumpāṉmiyāṉa tiruttalaṅkaḷukku
vaṭamoḻip purāṇaṅkaḷē kāṇpārāyiṉar. cila tiruppatikaḷukkut teṉmoḻip purāṇaṅkaḷum
tōṉṟiyuḷḷaṉa. iṅṅaṉam purāṇaṅkaḷip peṟātēyiruntum pallāyirak kaṇakkāṉa makkaḷ aṉpōṭu
pōṟṟi vaṇaṅki avaravar niṉitta yāvakip pēṟukaḷiyum eytavuriya nilikkaḷaṅkaḷāka aminta
pala talaṅkaḷ iṉṟum nam nāṭṭil uṇṭu. ivi cila putup pirapantaṅkaḷip peṟṟu
viḷakkamuṟuvatum uṇṭu.
tiruvaraṅkattiṟ pēraṉpu pūṇṭirunta piḷḷip perumāḷ aiyaṅkār eṉṟa tivviya kaviñarum,
tiruvaruṇiyiṟ pēraṉpu pūṇṭirunta ellappa nāvalar eṉṟa pulavar perumāṉum iyaṟṟiya pala
ciṟu pirapantaṅkaḷ uṇṭu. tiruppukaḻccelvar aruṇakiriyār amutamaya vākkiṉ arumiyit
tamiḻpēcum kuḻavikaḷum aṟiyum. paṭṭiṉattaṭikaḷ, tāyumāṉavar, irāmaliṅkacuvāmikaḷ
mutaliya periyōr aruḷiya ceyyuṭ celvaṅkaḷ paktip peruneṟikku iṭiyiṭiyiṭṭa tūṇṭā
viḷakkaṅkaḷāka utavukiṉṟaṉa. nallōr ceṉṟa teṟiyip piṉṉōrum piṉpaṟṟa muyalvatu iyalpē
eṉpataṉic ceṉṟa nāṉkintu nūṟṟāṇṭukaḷākat tamiḻakattil eḻunta pala antāti, kalampakam,
tūtu, ulā, kōvi mutaliya pirapanta vakikaḷ naṉku viḷakkukiṉṟaṉa.
innāḷiya aṟiñar palar 'pirapantaṅkaḷ iyaṟṟuvatum iṟivaṉi vaḻuttuvatum paḻiya muṟi;
uṇmip pulami iruppiṉ atu putupputu neṟikaḷil nuḻintu - putup putu nalaṉkaḷi
viḷakki moḻiyiṉ āṟṟali vaḷarkka vēṇṭum,' eṉpar. iḵtu uṇmiyē eṉpati naṉku uṇarntu,
putuk kavitiyulakil makuṭam pūṇṭa tiru. cuppiramaṇiya pāratiyār putumi vaḻikaḷiṟ
cantaṅkaḷip paṭittukkoṇṭu pala putup poruḷkaḷip paṟṟik kavitikaḷ puṉintaṉar.
putittuṟikku aṭikōlivitta appulavar kavitikaḷiṟ perumpālaṉa paḻiya neṟikku araṇāka
niṟpaṉa eṉṟu oruvar kūṟat tuṇintāl, navayukac celvarkaḷ nakiyātirār. avar nakippilum
uṇmi atuvē eṉṟu tuṇintu kūṟalām. vētāntap pāṭṭukkaḷ, kaṇṇaṉ pāṭṭukkaḷ, tēvi
kītaṅkaḷ mutaliyavi paḻiya neṟiyip putuvuruvattil viḷakkuvaṉa, pāñcāli capatamum
svacaritiyum paḻamiyi maṟitta putumi muṟiporuntiyamintaṉa. tiruppaḷḷiyeḻucci,
tiruttacāṅkam, navarattiṉa māli, tōttirap pāṭalkaḷ eṉpavi muḻuvatum paḻiya neṟiyiyē
piṉpaṟṟi amintaṉa. piṟa pāṭṭukkaḷ putumi viḷakka vantavi eṉṟu kūṟalām. iṅṅaṉam
kūṟuvatāl pāratiyār putumi maṇattukku oru kuṟiyum viḷintu viṭātu, paḻamiyi
muṟṟilum veṟuttu otukki nimirntu taṉittu niṟkum āṟṟal putumikku illiyeṉpatē
viḷaṅkum. paḻamiyum putumiyum valakkaramum iṭakkaramum pōla iṇintēyiruppatu ulaka
iyalpu eṉpati uyirvaki ovvoṉṟilum kāṇkiṟōm. aṅṅaṉamē uyirccaktiyuḷḷa
kaviticcelvamum irumiyum porunta amivatēyākum eṉpati aṟiñar ēṟṟukkoḷvar. inta
uṇmiyi uḷḷattiṟkoṇṭu ceyyuṭ celvattiṟṟiḷikka virumpuvōr tattamakku
evvettuṟiyil mikka cuvi tōṉṟumō avvattuṟiyilē muyaṟci purintu payaṉpeṟa
niṉippatē aṭivākum.
mōṉi etuki mutaliya muṭṭukkaṭṭikaḷ vaḷaruṅ kavitikkut taṭiviḷikkum eṉpatu
maliyāḷikaḷ karuttu ākum. pāratiyār putumikkaviti ovvoṉṟilum mōṉi etukikaḷ
muttumuttāka amintiruppati, avaṟṟi aṉupavittup pāṭuvōr aṟiyātirukka iyalātu.
āriya moḻikkum ataṉ iṉamākiya piṟamoḻikaḷukkum iṉṟiyamiyāta ceyyuḷaṅkam eṉṟu
karutappeṟāta mōṉi etukikaḷē tamiḻcceyyuḷiṉ taṉicciṟappi viḷakkuvaṉa. ivaṟṟiṉ
perumiyi matiyātu tamiḻukku ivi vēṇṭā eṉṟu urikkat tuṇivōr tamiḻmakaḷiṉ uyir
niliyi aṟintu tamatu karutti māṟṟikkoḷḷa vēṇṭum. caṅkacceyyuḷil inta niyatikaḷ
illiyē eṉpārkku, avaṟṟiṉ naṭiyum piṟkālacceyyuḷ naṭiyum vevvēṟāka iruppatum
ivaṟṟiṉ pōkkē māṟiyiruppatum, nōkkamē vevvēṟu vakiyāy amintiruppatumē ciṟanta
kāraṇaṅkaḷāka eṭuttukkāṭṭattakkaṉavām.
iṉi oru cilar "tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ vaḷarccikku in nāḷil vēṇṭuvatu urinaṭiyē; ceyyuḷ,
aḷavu kaṭantu vaḷarntu viṭṭatu; iṉi ataṉ vaḷarcciyi oṭukki uri naṭiyip perukka
vēṇṭum,'' eṉṟu kūṟuvatu uṇṭu. tamiḻmoḻi pūraṇa vaḷarcci peṟṟu ulakattiṉi āṇṭa pala
nūṟṟāṇṭukaḷiṉ piṟaku vaḷarcciyum iyakkamum peṟṟa moḻikaḷi eṭuttukkoḷvōm. avaṟṟuḷ
oṉṟēṉum iṉṟu ''ematu vaḷarccikku urinaṭiyoṉṟē pōtum; ceyyuḷ naṭi vēṇṭā,” eṉṟu
kūṟuvatu uṇṭā? kūṟa niṉippatum iliyē. aṅṅaṉam irukka, irutuṟikkum pōtiyavaḷavu
āṟṟal peṟṟirukkum nam amutat tamiḻmoḻi oru tuṟiyē vaḷarvatu cālum, maṟṟoru tuṟi
arukuka eṉṟu niṉippatu aṟivuṭimiyiṉpāṟpaṭumā? ayalnāṭukaḷil oru nāḷil
nūṟāyirakkaṇakkil puṟṟīcal pōl eḻunta urinaṭi nūlkaḷ ellām nīṇṭa vāḻvu paṭittu
uḷḷaṉavā? nam nāṭṭil inta nūṟṟāṇṭilē ettuṇi urinaṭi nūlkaḷ eḻuntaṉa? ivaṟṟuḷ
ettuṇiya iraṇṭām patippē peṟātu maṟintaṉa? intak kaṇakki evarēṉum caṟṟup poṟumiyāy
eṭuttup pārppatu uṇṭā? orumuṟi nūṟāyirak kaṇakkil viliyākiya puttakaṅkaḷ piṟaku
tēṭuvāraṟṟup potup puttaka cālikaḷiṉ mūlimuṭukkukaḷil uṟaṅkikkiṭappatu
uṇmiyaṉupavam aṉṟō? ātaliṉ oru nūl niliyāṉa pukaḻpeṟutaṟku ataṉ uḷḷuṟi
kāraṇamākumēyaṉṟi naṭi kāraṇam ākātu eṉpatu veḷippaṭi, ceyyuḷāyiṉum
urinaṭiyāyiṉum ataṉi ākkiyōṉ uṇmip pulami paṭittavaṉāy iruppiṉ, atu eṉṟum
aḻivuṟātu. cila pulavar pallāyirak kaṇakkilē ceyyuḷ ceytum pukaḻpeṟātu
maṟintatuṇṭu. oṉṟu iraṇṭu ceyyuḷ iyaṟṟiya pulavar cilar aḻiyāp pukaḻōṭu
viḷaṅkuvatumuṇṭu itaṟkuk kāraṇam kūṟavum vēṇṭumō? avaravar vāymoḻiyē kāraṇatti
viḷakkiviṭum. ceyyuṭ celvam oru nāṭṭiṉ uṇmip perumiyi viḷakkum karuviyātalāl,
ataṉ vaḷarcciyil ovvoru makaṉum ūkkam koḷḷal vēṇṭum.
putumi neṟiyilē pala ceyyuṭkaḷ vaḷartaṟkuriya innāḷilē paḻami neṟipaṟṟiya cila
ceyyuṭkaḷ āṅkāṅku tōṉṟutalum nalamē. malliki mulli cāti cāmanti mutaliya
malarkaḷiyē aṇintu paḻakiya tamiḻap peṇmaṇikaḷ innāḷil rōjāmalarilum kaṉakāmpara malarilum
maṉattic celuttit tam nāṭṭup paḻiya malarkaḷi maṟantu varukiṉṟaṉarāyiṉum katampam eṉṟa
peyarōṭēṉum cila camayaṅkaḷil avaṟṟiyum aṇiya virumpukiṉṟaṉar. aṅṅaṉamē putumik
kavitikaḷil uḷam pukuttiya tamiḻ makkaḷ paḻami muṟi paṟṟi varum pirapantaṅkaḷiyum
avaṟṟil uḷḷa cuviyi aṟiyum nōkkattōṭu payiṉṟu makiḻvar.
immuṟiyālē, taṭciṇa kōkarṇam eṉṟu kāntattāṟ pukaḻappeṟṟatum paḻami polinta purāṇam
uṭiyatum putukkōṭṭit tamiḻ maṉṉar kulattukku vaḻipaṭu teyvamākiya periyanāyakiyiyum
kōkaṉṉanātaṉiyum taṉṉakam koṇṭatumākiya tirukkōkarṇam eṉṟa tiruttalattip paṟṟi muṉpu
iyaṉṟa cila pirapantaṅkaḷ mutta miḻilum uḷavātalāl naṉṉāvalar ceṉṟa accenneṟi paṟṟiyē
aruḷveḷḷam poḻiyum tiruviḻiyum aṉpariyāḷum kaḻaṉmalaruṅ koṇṭa 'srī pirahatampiki'
eṉṟa periyanāyakiyiṉ tutiyuruvamāka aminta ippiḷḷit tamiḻ tamiḻulakiṟ pukukiṉṟatu.
piḷḷit tamiḻ ilakkaṇam naṉkuṇarnta periyōrkaḷ malinta tamiḻakattē taṉittamiḻaracil
icceyyuḷi iyaṟṟi aṉṉikku arppaṇam ceyyāta tuṇivu koṇṭatu, maṟi moḻikaḷiyē
malarkaḷākakkoṇṭu aruccikkum tiruvaṭiyil maṇṇil viḻunta malarkaḷum pukutaṟku iṭam
peṟum urimiyi nōkkiyēyākum. "kuṟṟamē terivār kuṟumā muṉicoṟṟa pāviṉum ōr
kuṟi colvarāl” eṉpatu uṇmiyē yāyiṉum, "pittar coṉṉavum pētiyar coṉṉavum pattā
coṉṉavum paṉṉap peṟupavō" eṉṟa kampar kavi muḻaṅkuvati ulakam aṟiyum.
āṉṟōr ceyyuṭkaḷālum kaṉ makkaḷ vāymoḻikaḷā lum kēṭṭuk kēṭṭup paḻakiyirukkum
tamiḻ makkaḷukku ipperiya nāyaki piḷḷit tamiḻiṉ tōṟṟam mikiyākat tōṉṟātu.
pirapantaṅkaḷ palavakiyāmāyiṉum avaṟṟuḷ antāti, piḷḷittamiḻ, kalampakam, ulā, kōvi
eṉṟa vakikaḷē mikutiyāka vaḻakkil uḷḷaṉa. pirapanta vakikaḷi yellām tam nāttiṉavu tīra
naṉku pāṭiya makāvittuvāṉ mīṉākṣicuntaram piḷḷiyavarkaḷ āṇpāṟ piḷḷittamiḻ, peṇpāṟ
piḷḷittamiḻ eṉṟa iruvakikkum pala amittuḷḷārkaḷ. avarkaḷ vitta vaḻipaṟṟi
innūṟṟāṇṭilum cilar ippirapantam iyaṟṟiyuḷar. ivaṟṟiyellām ātiyōṭantamākap payiṉṟu
iṉpuṟuvōr mikutiyāy illāta innāḷil, āḻattilum akalattilum nāṭṭam iṉṟic
collaṭukkāl aviyi veruṭṭum pulamiyil maruṇṭa palar mikka innāḷil tamiḻ makkaḷ
ātaravi nāṭi icceyyuḷ veḷi varukiṉṟatu.
icceyyuḷiṟ paṉṉūṟu kuṟikaḷ irukkalām. ilakkaṇa neṟip piṟaḻcci irukkalām. āyiṉum
teyvat tiruvaruḷ peṟum karuttu muṉṉāṭ pulavarkku iruntavaḷavil eṭṭuṇiyum
kuṟivilliyeṉpatē ākkiyōṉ vaṇakkattōṭu terivittuk koḷvatām. teyvatti enta
uruvattāl niṉintu, ekkālattil, ennāṭṭil, yār paraviṉālum aruḷvatu eṉpatē ataṉ
iyalpām. kaṇṇaṉum kītiyil ‘eṉpaktaṉ aḻivuṟuvāṉallaṉ' eṉṟu piratiññi ceytāṉ.
kalmaṣamikka cittamum pāvattiraviyattāl vāḻum vāḻkkiyiyum takkaṉa takātaṉa aṟiyāp
pētamiyum uḷḷa māntarkku ik kaliyukattil nāma caṅkīrttaṉamē katiyākum eṉpatu oru
vaṭamoḻic ceyyuḷiṉ karuttām. itupōṉṟa pala naṟ karuttukkaḷi āṉṟōr ceyyuṭkaḷālum
naṉmakkaḷ vāymoḻikaḷālum kēṭṭuk kēṭṭup paḻakiyirukkum tamiḻ makkaḷukku
ipperiyanāyaki piḷḷittamiḻiṉ tōṟṟam mikiyākat tōṉṟātu.
ceyyuḷ ceyyum tuṟiyil muppatu āṇṭukaḷākap payiṟci peṟṟamiyālum, pala nallicip
pulavarkaḷatu arumporuṭ pēḻikaḷilē pukuntu pukuntu collum poruḷum kavarum tiṟam
paṭittuṇmiyālum, iṟivaṉaruḷ oṉṟē tuṇiyeṉak koṇṭu toṇṭimāṉ nāṭṭil uttiyōka
patavi vakittirukkum urimiyālum tamiḻttiruppaṇi purivār iṉattuḷ evvaḻipālēṉum
kalantu koḷḷal vēṇṭum eṉa uḷḷattil eḻunta ārvattālum, kaṭaliṭiyil viḻuntāriyum
kāttaruḷum karuṇivalliyeṉa apperumāṭṭiyiṉ pukaḻ kēṭṭa ceyalālum vāḻvukkaṭalil
vīḻntu tavikkum ovvoru uyiriyum taṉatu kaḻaṟpuṇiyāṟ kariyēṟṟum nāyaki avaḷē eṉa
uṇarnta uṟutiyālum ippiḷḷittamiḻi avaḷ tiruvaṭi malariṟ camarppikkiṉṟēṉ. ic
ceyyuḷ iyaṉṟu iraṇṭāṇṭukaḷ ākiyum accuruvam koṭuttu ulakiṟkutavum amayam
ippoḻutē amivatāyiṟṟu.
tamiḻ makaḷiṉ tavappēṟākat tōṉṟi arumpaṇipurintu tamiḻ makkaḷit tamakkup palavaḻiyālum
kaṭappāṭuṭiyarākac ceytu irumatimuṉ civapatam pukunta mutuperum pulavar
makāmakōpāttiyāya u. vē. cāminātiyaravarkaḷ pārvikku icceyyuḷ aṉuppap
peṟṟiruntatu. avarkaḷ ampulip paruvattilē cila tiruttaṅkaḷ terivittārkaḷ. avarkaḷ
virumpiyavāṟu tiruttaṅkaḷ ellām muṟiyē amikkap peṟṟaṉa. avarkaḷ eṉatu vēṇṭu
kōṭkiyintu oru cāttukkavi ceytu aṉuppiyiruntārkaḷ. ataṉi ipputtakam accukku
varumpōtu upayōkittuk koḷḷalām eṉṟu eṇṇi eṉatu tamiḻp puttakacāliyil
vittiruntēṉ. iṟivaṉ tiruvaruḷ, tamiḻttāy pulampa, appulavar perumāṉi

      āṉiḷaṅkaṉ ṟumvittāy
aṇṭarkaḷu maṭivariya taṇṭamiḻi ṉamutatti
      aṭiyarukkaruḷ ceytuḷāy
taṅkamaya mērukiri vantatu nikarkkumoru
      cāmpunata maṇṭapattē
cattariṭi pattiṉiyar toṭṭaciyu mucaliṟ
      caṅkaraṉo ṭaṉpukulavip
poṅkivaḷar toṇṭiyarta marapaṭimi pūṇṭacivi
      poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
poṉṉacala villikō kaṉṉaṉiṭa muṟivalli
      poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē.       3

101. muttarpeṟu teyvikac celvamē yaṉpoḻuku
      mūvar tēvāra viciyi
muṟiyiṉāṟ kēṭṭaruḷi vātavū raṭikaḷtiru
      moḻikaḷum ceviyiṉēṟṟāy
pattarpalar kuḻuminiṉ ṟaṟivuruva mākukiṉ
      paṇpamiya maṟṟiyaṭiyār
pāṭiyuru kuntirup pāṭalu micittariya
      paḻamaṟic cāmattiyum
cuttavici mayamāṉa piṟaviṉiya pāṭalum
      colliniṉ ṟārkaḷaṉṉē
cuvaṇattiṉ mayamāki yuvamikka viyalāta
      cōtiniṟi yūcaliṉkaṇ
puttamuta maṉiyatamiḻ urimikoḷu maṉṉiyē
      poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
poṉṉacala villikō kaṉṉaṉiṭa muṟivalli
      poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē.       4

102. arikaḷpaṇ pāṭumalar kōṭāṉu kōṭiyāy
      alarumaki ḻaraṇiyattē
aṇikayili yituveṉṟu niṉakoḻuna ṉāṭivaru
      maruvari kuṭintakōyil
urimikoḷu maṉṉiyē yariyatava muyalkiṉṟa
      uttamarkaḷ neñcilniṉṟāy
uyarvaṟa vuyarntavira ṇiyakiriyi ṉikareṉṉa
      uḷḷatiru maṇṭapattē
patikaḷ maṉiviyarum maṟiyavar maṭantiyarum
      naṉṟavaṭa māṭṭiniṟka
caṉukumavar niṉiviṉvaru nalamelā niṟivēṟa
      nāvalam poṉṉūcalil
arumaṟikaḷ turuviniṉ ṟaṭivariya patavalli
      āṭiyaruḷ poṉṉūcalē

      āṭiyaruḷ poṉṉūcalē.       5

103. vaṉpuneṟi yiyalpuṭiya vēṭarkula nākaṉoṭu
      mayilaṉiya tattipuriyum
vaḷamikka meyttavap pēṟāka vulakiṉkaṇ
      vantatiṇṇak kuḻamakaṉ
naṉpuniṟi yuḷḷattiṉ māṇpataṉi vētamuṇar
      ñāṉa kōcariyumaṟiya

      nalviḻikaḷ cennīrtarap
puṉpulaṉi veṉṟatava muṉivariṉu mikkavap
      puṇṇiyaṉ kaṇṇiyappip
puṭavipukaḻ kaṇṇappa ṉāyakati pāṭiyē
      pulamaṅki yāṭṭukiṉṟāḷ
aṉpuṭiya malarmakaḷcey naṉpaṇiyu vantavumi

104. āṭaka cilōccayam villeṉṟu koṇṭulaka

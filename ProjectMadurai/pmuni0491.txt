Source:
kāñcippurāṇam
tirukkailāyaparamparait tiruvāvaṭutuṟaiyātīṉattumahācannitāṉam
srīlasrī ampalavāṇatēcika cuvāmikaḷ kaṭṭaḷaiyiṭṭaruḷiyapaṭi
cittānta carapam- aṣṭāvatāṉam
pūvai-kaliyāṇacuntaramutaliyāravarkaḷ māṇavarum

palapiratirūpaṅkaḷaikkoṇṭu paricōtittu
periyameṭṭu- vēṅkaṭācalañ ceṭṭiyāravarkaḷ kumārar ātimūlañceṭṭiyārāl
ceṉṉai: kalāratnākaravaccukkūṭattil patippikkappeṟṟatu.

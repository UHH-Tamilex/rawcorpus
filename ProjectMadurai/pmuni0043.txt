kāppu
vināyaka vaṇakkam
kārkoṇṭa kavuṇmatak kaṭiveḷḷa muṅkaṭ
        kaṭikkaṭik kaṉalu melli
   kaṭavātu taṭavuk kuḻiccevi mukanteṟi
        kaṭikkā ṟiṭṭa veṅkōṉ
pōrkoṇṭa veṇṭō ṭpolaṉkuvaṭu potiyumveṇ
        poṭituṭi yaṭittu vittup
   puḻutiyāṭ ṭayarāvo rayirāva ṇattulavu
        poṟkaḷiṟ ṟittu tippān
tārkoṇṭa matimuṭi yoruttaṉ ṟirukkaṇmalar
        cāttak kiḷarntu poṅkit
   tavaḻumiḷa veyilumaḻa nilavumaḷa vaḷavalāṟ
        ṟaṇṇeṉṟu vecceṉṟu poṉ
vārkoṇṭa ṭaṇintamuli malivalli karppūra
        valliyapi rāma valli
    māṇikka vallimara katavalli yapiṭēka
        valli coṟ ṟamiḻ taḻiyavē.

1-vatu kāppup paruvam

tirumāl
maṇikoṇṭa neṭunēmi valayañ cumantāṟṟu
        mākaṇac cūṭṭu mōṭṭu
    mālkaḷiṟu piṭarvitta vaḷaroḷi vimāṉattu
        vāluḷi maṭaṅka ṟāṅkum
aṇikoṇṭa pīṭikiyi ṉampoṉmuṭi muṭivittē
        miyaṉoṭu vīṟṟi rukku
    maṅkayaṟ kaṇṇamuti maṅkiyark karaciyiye
        mammaṉiyi yiṉitu kākka
kaṇikoṇṭa taṇṭuḻāyk kāṭalit tōṭutēṅ
        kaḻaluḻipāyn taḷaṟu ceyyak
    kaḻaṉipaṭu naṭaviyiṟ kamalat taṇaṅkaracor
        kiyaṇi mukantu cellap
paṇikoṇṭa tuttip paṭappāyc curuṭṭup
        paṇittō ḷerutta lippap
    paḻamaṟikaṇ muṟiyiṭap pintamiḻp piṉceṉṟa
        paccip pacuṅ koṇṭalē. (1)
paramacivaṉ
vēṟu
cikara vaṭavari kuṉiya nimirtaru
        ceruvi loruporu villeṉakkōṭṭiṉar
ceṭikoḷ paṟitali yamaṇa retiretir
        celavor matalicol viyiyiṟ kūṭṭiṉar
tiruvu mimiyavar taruvu maravoli
        ceyava lavarkoḷa nalkukit tīṭṭiṉar
ciṟiya veṉa tupuṉ moḻiyum vaṭitamiḻ
        teriyu mavarmutu colleṉac cūṭṭiṉar
pakaru micitici parava viruvarkaḷ
        payilu miyaṟeri veḷvaḷit tōṭṭiṉar
paciya vaṟukoṭu veḷiya nilaviri
        pavaḷa vaṉamaṭar palcaṭik kāṭṭiṉar
patuma mutalvaṉu meḻuta variyator
        paṉuva leḻutiya vitikap pāṭṭiṉar
paracu miracata capiyi ṉaṭamiṭu
        parata patayuka muḷḷamvit tēttun
takaramoḻukiya kuḻalu nilavumiḻ
        taraḷa nakiyume miyaṉip pārttetir
caruvi yamarporu viḻiyu maṟukiṭi
        taḷara vaḷarvator cevvimuṟ ṟākkaṉa
taṉamu maṉaṉuṟa veḻuti yeḻutaru
        tamatu vaṭiviyu meḷḷimaṭ ṭūṟṟiya
tavaḷa malarvaru miḷami ṉoṭucata
        taḷamiṉ vaḻipaṭu tiyalit tūttiri
makara meṟikaṭa lamuti yamutuku
        maḻali paḻakiya kiḷḷiyip pēṭṭaṉa
maṭava naṭipayil piṭiyi viriceṟi
        varicey puyamici viyam vittāṟṟiya
vaḻutiyuṭiya kaṇ maṇiyo ṭulavu peṇ
        maṇiyi yaṇitikaḻ celviyit tēkkamaḻ
matura moḻukiya tamiḻi ṉiyalpayiṉ
        maturi marakata valliyik kākkavē. (2)
citti viṉāyakar
vēṟu
kittala mōṭiru karaṭak karittiri
        kikkuka ṭāmuṭik kaṭaliṟ kuḷittemar
cittama tāmoru taṟiyiṟ ṟuvakkuṟu
        cittivi nāyaka ṉiciyip paḻiccutum
puttamu tōvaru ṭaḻiyat taḻittator
        poṟkōṭi yōveṉa maturit tuvaṭṭeḻu
muttamiḻ tērtaru maturit talattuṟi
        muttaṉa mēvupe ṇaracip purakkavē. (3)
murukavēḷ
vēṟu
pakara maṭuppak kaṭāmeṭut tūṟṟumor
       pakaṭu naṭattip pulōmacic cūṟpuyal
paruki yiṭakkaṟ pakāṭavip pāṟpoli
       paravi yiṭip paṟ pamāteṉat tōṟṟiya
kumari yirukkak kalāmayiṟ kūttayar
       kuḷirpuṉa moyttiṭ ṭacāraliṟ pōycciṟu
kuṟavar makaṭkuc calāmiṭaṟ kēkkaṟu
       kumaraṉi muttukkumāraṉip pōṟṟutum
imiḻtiri muṟṟat tumērumat tārttumuḷ
       ḷeyiṟu naccup paṇāṭavit tāppicit
tiṟuka viṟukkit tuḻāy muṭit tīrttaṉo
       ṭevaru matittup parāpavat tīccuṭa
vamutucey vittiṭ ṭapōṉakat tāṟcuṭa
       raṭaru miruṭṭuk kirīvamaṭ ṭākkiya
vaḻakiya cokkaṟ kumālceyat tōṭṭika
        lamarcey kayaṟkaṭ kumāriyik kākkavē. (4)
nāṉmukaṉ
vēṟu
mēkap pacuṅkuḻavi vāymaṭut tuṇṇavum
        viṭpulam virun tayaravum
veḷḷamutam vīcuṅ karuntirip pintukil
        virittuṭut tutti viriyum
nākattu mīccuṭiki naṭuvaṭ kiṭantamaṭa
        naṅkiyip peṟṟu maṟṟan
nākaṇit tuñcutaṉ ṟantikku vantutavu
        naḷiṉak kuḻanti kākka
pākattu marakatak kuṉṟeṉṟor tamaṉiyak
        kuṉṟoṭu kiḷittu niṉṟa
pavaḷat taṭaṅkuṉ ṟuḷakkaṇṇa teṉṟap
        parañcuṭar muṭikku muṭimūṉ
ṟākat tamittuppi ṉorumuṭit taṉmuṭivit
        taṇaṅkaracu vīṟṟi rukkum
apiṭēka valliyi yaḷikkula muḻakkukuḻa
        laṅkayaṟ kaṇṇa mutiyē. (5)
tēvēntiraṉ
vēṟu
cuḻiyuṅ karuṅkaṭ kuṇṭakaḻi
        cuvaṟṟuñ cuṭarvēṟ kiritiritta
tōṉṟaṟ kaḷittuc cuṟavuyartta
        cokkap perumāṉ cekkarmuṭi
poḻiyun taraṅkak kaṅkivirip
        puṉalkāl pāyccat taḻintuviri
puvaṉan taṉipūt taruḷpaḻutta
        poṉṉaṅ koṭiyip purakkavaḻin
tiḻiyun tuṇarkkaṟ pakattiṉaṟa
        vitaḻttēṉ kuṭittuk kumaṭṭiyeti
reṭukkum ciṟivaṇ ṭuvaṭṭuṟavuṇ
        ṭirikkak karikku matakkaluḻik
kuḻiyuñ ciṟuka ṇēṟṟurumuk
        kuralveṇ puyaluṅ karumpuyaluṅ
kuṉṟaṅ kuliya vukittēṟuṅ
        kulicat taṭakki puttaḷē. (6)
tirumakaḷ
vēṟu
veñcūṭṭu neṭṭuṭal virikkum paṭappāyaṉ
        mīmicit tuñcu nīla
mēkatti ṉākattu viṭucuṭarp paṭalimaṇi
        meṉpara luṟutta nontu
pañcūṭṭu cīṟaṭi patittumataṉ veṅkatirp
        paṭumiḷa veyiṟku ṭintum
pintuḻāyk kāṭuviri taṇṇiḻa lotunkumor
        pacuṅkoṭiyi yañca lippā
mañcūṭ ṭakaṭṭuneṭu vāṉmukaṭu turuvumoru
        maṟiyōti mañca likka
maṟitiric ciṟiviriyu māyira mukakkaṭavuṇ
        mantāki ṉippe yartta
ceñcūṭṭu veḷḷō timaṅkuṭi yirukkumvaḷar
        ceñcaṭik karumi ṭaṟṟut
tēvukku muṉṉiṉṟa teyvatti mummulit
        tiruvip purakka veṉṟē. (7)
kalimakaḷ
veḷḷit takaṭṭuneṭ ṭēṭaviḻt tiṉṉici
        virumpuñ curumpar pāṭa
viḷinaṟavu kakkum polaṉ pokuṭ ṭalarkamala
        vīṭṭuk koḻit teṭuttut
teḷḷit teḷikkum tamiḻkaṭali ṉaṉpiṉin
        tiṇiyeṉa veṭutta viṟinūṟ
ṟeḷḷamutu kūṭṭuṇumor veḷḷōti mattiṉiru
        cīṟaṭi muṭippam vaḷarpiṅ
kiḷḷikku maḻalip pacuṅkutali yoḻukutīṅ
        kiḷaviyuṅ kaḷi mayiṟkuk
kiḷariḷañ cāyalu navvikku nōkkumviri
        kiñcukac cūṭṭa racaṉap
piḷḷikku maṭanaṭiyu muṭaṉoṭu makaḷirkkor
        pētamiyu mutavi mutirāp
piḷḷimiyiṉ vaḷḷaṉmi koḷḷumoru pāṇṭip
        pirāṭṭiyik kākka veṉṟē. (8)
turkki
vaṭipaṭṭa mukkuṭumi vaṭivē ṟirittiṭṭu
        vaḷikaruṅ kōṭṭu mōṭṭu
makiṭaṅ kaviḻttuk kaṭāṅkaviḻk kuñciṟukaṇ
        mālyāṉi vīṅka vāṅkun
tuṭipaṭṭa koṭinuṇ ṇucuppiṟ kuṭinteṉac
        cuṭukaṭik kaṉali tūṇṭuñ
cuḻalkaṇ muṭaṅkuḷi maṭaṅkali yukittēṟu
        cūrarip piṇavu kākka
piṭipaṭṭa maṭanaṭik kēkkaṟṟa kūntaṟ
        piṭikkuḻāñ cuṟṟa voṟṟip
piṟimarup puṭiyator kaḷiṟṟiṉip peṟṟenti
        piṭṭuṇṭu kaṭṭuṇṭu niṉ
ṟaṭipaṭṭa tirumēṉi kuḻiyak kuḻittiṭṭa
        vaṇimaṇik kimpu rikkō
ṭākatta tākak kaṭampā ṭavikkuḷ viḷi
        yāṭumor maṭappiṭi yiyē. (9)
catta mātarkaḷ
vēṟu
kaṭakaḷi ṟutavuka pāymicip pōrttavaḷ
        kavikuvi tuṟukaliṉ vāriyit tūrttavaḷ
kaṭalvayi ṟeriyavoḷ vēliṉip pārttavaḷ
        kaṭikamaḻ tarumalar tārmuṭic cērttavaḷ
iṭiyuka vaṭalari yēṟukit tārttavaḷ
        eḻutaru muḻumaṟi nūliṉiṟ kūrttavaḷ
eyiṟuko ṭuḻuteḻu pāriṉip pērttavaḷ
        eṉumiva reḻuvarka ṭāṇmuṭic cūṭṭutum
kuṭamoṭu kuṭaviyar pāṇikik kōttiṭu
        kuraviyu malatorpa ṇāmuṭic cūṭṭaruḷ
kutikoḷa naṭamiṭu pāṭaluk kēṟpavor
        kuḻalici paḻakaḷi pāṭiṭak kēṭṭuṭi
maṭalaviḻ tuḷapana ṟāveṭut tūṟṟiṭa
        maḻakaḷi ṟeṉaveḻu kārmukac cūṟpuyal
varavaru miḷiyaku māriyik kōṭṭeyiṉ
        maturiyil vaḷarkavu māriyik kākkavē. (10)
muppattu mūvar
vēṟu
amaril venniṭuma vutiyar piṉṉiṭumo
        rapayar muṉṉiṭuva ṉattokka vōṭavum
aḷavu memmuṭiya tiṟiyi teṉṉamuṭi
        yaraca reṇṇilaror muṟṟattu vāṭavum
akila maṉṉaravar ticiyiṉ maṉṉariva
        ramara reṉṉumuri tikkeṭṭu mūṭavum
amuta veṇmatiyiṉ marapi yuṉṉiyuṉi
        yalari yaṇṇalmuḻu veppattu mūḻkavum
kumari poṉṉiviyi poruṇi naṉṉatikaḷ
        kutikoḷ viṇṇatiyiṉ mikkuk kulāvavuṅ
kuvaṭu teṉmaliyi ṉikara tiṉmicurar
        kuṭikoḷ poṉmalitu tittuppa rāvavuṅ
kumarar muṉṉiruva ramara raṉṉiyivaḷ
        kumari yiṉṉamume ṉaccittar pāṭavuṅ
kuravi vimmavara makaḷir maṇṇileḻil
        kulavu kaṉṉiyarkaḷ kikkokka vāṭavum
kamalaṉ muṉṉiyiṭu maraca vaṉṉameḻu
        kaṭali laṉṉamuṭa ṉaṭpukki kūṭavuṅ
kariya cemmaloṭu miḷiya cemmalviṭu
        karuṭaṉ mañciyoṭor kaṭcikku ḷūṭavuṅ
kaṭavi viṇṇaracu naṭavum vemmuṉiya
        kaḷiṟu kimmalicel koppattu vīḻavuṅ
kaṉaka maṉṉutaṭa naḷiṉi tuṉṉiyiru
        kamala miṉṉumoru paṟpattuṇ mēvavum
imaya meṉṉamaṉu muṟiko ṭeṉṉarume
        miṟiyi naṉmaruke ṉappeṟṟu vāḻavum
evarkol paṇṇavarka ḷevarkoṉ maṇṇavarka
        ḷetukol poṉṉulake ṉattaṭṭu māṟavum
eḻilcey teṉmaturi taḻiya mummuliyo
        ṭeḻume ṉammaṉi vaṉappukkor kāvalar
iruva reṇmarpati ṉoruvar paṉṉiruva
        reṉum viṇṇavarkaṇ muppattu mūvarē. (11)
kāppup paruvam muṟṟiṟṟu

2. ceṅkīrip paruvam

nīrāṭṭi yāṭṭupoṟ cuṇṇan timirntaḷḷi
neṟṟiyiṟ ṟoṭṭiṭṭa veṇ
ṇīṟṟiṉoṭu puṇṭarak kīṟṟukku mēṟṟiṭavor
nittilac cuṭṭi cāttit
tārāṭṭu cūḻiyak koṇṭiyu muṭittut
talippaṇi tirutti muttiṉ
ṟaṇṇoḷi tatumpuṅ kutampiyoṭu kātukkor
tamaṉiyak koppu miṭṭup
pārāṭṭu pāṇṭip peruntēvi tirumulip
pālamuta mūṭṭi yoruniṉ
pāṉāṟu kumutaṅ kaṉintūṟu tēṟaltaṉ
paṭṭāṭi maṭina ṉippac
cīrāṭṭi vittumut tāṭum pacuṅkiḷḷi
ceṅkīri yāṭi yaruḷē
teṉṉaṟku mampoṉmali maṉṉaṟku morucelvi
ceṅkīri yāṭi yaruḷē. (1)
uṇṇilā vuvakip peruṅkaḷi tuḷumpaniṉ
ṟuṉṟirut tāti niṉṉi
yorumuṟi karampotti varukeṉa vaḻittiṭumu
ṉōṭit tavaḻntu ceṉṟu
taṇṇulā maḻalip pacuṅkutali yamutiṉiya
tāyvayiṟu kuḷira vūṭṭit
taṭamārpa niṟikuṅ kumaccē ṟaḷintupoṟ
ṟāṭō y taṭakki paṟṟip
paṇṇulā vaṭitamiḻp pintāmam viriyum
paṇittō ḷeruttamēṟip
pācoḷiya marakatat tirumēṉi paccip
pacuṅkatir tatumpa maṇivāyt
teṇṇilā viriya niṉṟāṭum pacuntōki
ceṅkīri yāṭi yaruḷē
teṉṉaṟku mampoṉmali maṉṉaṟku morucelvi
ceṅkīri yāṭi yaruḷē. (2)
cuṟṟuneṭu nēmic cuvarkkiciya veṭṭuc
cuvarkkā ṉiṟutti mērut
tūṇoṉṟu naṭunaṭṭu veḷimukaṭu mūṭiyiru
cuṭar viḷakkiṭṭu muṟṟa
eṟṟupuṉa liṟkaḻuvu puvaṉap paḻaṅkala
meṭuttaṭuk kippu tukkū
ḻiṉṉamuta muñcamit taṉṉi nīpaṉmuṟi
yiḻittiṭa vaḻitta ḻittōr
muṟṟaveḷi yiṟṟiriyu mattap perumpittaṉ
muṉṉiṉṟu tontamiṭavu
muṉiyātu vikalu meṭuttaṭuk kipperiya
mūtaṇṭa kūṭamūṭuñ
ciṟṟilviḷi yāṭumoru pacciḷam peṇpiḷḷi
ceṅkīri yāṭi yaruḷē
teṉṉaṟku mampoṉmali maṉṉaṟku morucelvi
ceṅkīri yāṭi yaruḷē. (3)
maṅkulpaṭu kantarac cuntarak kaṭavuṭku
maḻakatirk kaṟṟi cuṟṟum
vāṇayaṉa mūṉṟuṅ kuḷirntamuta kalitali
maṭuppak kaṭikka ṇōkkum
poṅkumatar nōkkiṟ piṟantavā ṉantap
putuppuṇari nītta miyaṉ
puntit taṭattiṉi nirappavaḻi yaṭiyarpāṟ
pōkacā karama ṭuppa
aṅkaṇoṭu ñālattu vittiṉṟi vittiya
vaṉit tuyirka ḷuntaḷirppa
varuṇmaṭi tiṟantukaṭi veḷḷam perukkeṭut
taliyeṟin tukaḷa vukaḷuñ
ceṅkayal kiṭakkuṅ karuṅkaṭ pacuntōki
ceṅkīri yāṭi yaruḷē
teṉṉaṟku mampoṉmali maṉṉaṟku morucelvi
ceṅkīri yāṭi yaruḷē. (4)
paṇṇaṟā varimiṭaṟ ṟaṟukāṇ maṭuppap
pacuntēṟa lāṟa likkum
patumapī ṭikiyumutu paḻamaṟi virintoḷi
paḻuttacen nāvu mimiyāk
kaṇṇaṟā marakatak kaṟṟik kalāmaññi
kaṇmuki ṟatumpa vēṅkuṅ

8. ammāṉip paruvam

karikkuṅ kaṭāmiru kavuṭkuṭa muṭintūṟṟu
kaḷiṟuperu vayiṟutūrppak
kavaḷan tiraṭṭik koṭuppateṉa vuñcūḻntor
kalimatik kalacavamutuk
kirikkum peruntēvar puṉkaṇ ṭuṭittiṭa
veṭuttamuta kalacam vevvē
ṟīntiṭuva teṉavumuḻu muttiṭ ṭiḻittiṭṭa
veṟipantiṉ niriyeṉṉavum
virikkun taḷirkkik koḻuntā marittuñci
mīteḻun tārttapiḷḷi
veḷḷōti mattiraḷi teṉavun karumpāṟi
mīmicic cencāntuvit
tarikkuntirikkiveḷ ḷaruvivi yittuṟivi
yammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē. (1)
tiṅkaṭ koḻuntik koḻuntupaṭu paṭarcaṭic
cerukutiru maṇavāḷaṉmēṟ
ceḻumaṇap pantari leṭutteṟiyu mamutaveṇ
ṭiraḷiyiṟ puraḷumaṟukāṟ
piṅkaṭ curumpeṉa vicumpiṟ paṭarnteḻum
paṉimati micittāviṭum
paruvamaṭa māṉeṉave ṉammaṉini ṉammaṉip
paṭiviḻik kayalpāynteḻu
veṅkaṭ kaṭuṅkoliya vēḻakku ḻāmiteṉa
mēkak kuḻāttimuṭṭi
viḷiyāṭu maḻakaḷiṟu kaṭivāy kutaṭṭamuki
viṇṭavam pintukōtta
aṅkaṭ karumpēntu mapiṭēka vallitiru
vammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē. (2)
kaḷḷūṟu kañcak karattūṟu cēyoḷi
kalappac civappūṟiyum
karuṇip perukkūṟa vamutūṟu pārvik
kaṭikkaṭ kaṟuppūṟiyum
naḷḷūṟu maṟuvū ṟakaṟṟumuka matiyilveṇ
ṇakiyūṟu nilavūṟiyum
naṟṟāraḷa vammaṉiyor ciṟkuṇat tiṉimūṉṟu
naṟkuṇaṅ katuval kāṭṭa
uḷḷūṟu kaḷituḷum pakkurava riruvīru
muṟṟiṭu tuvāta cāntat
toruperu veḷikkē viḻittuṟaṅ kuntoṇṭa
ruḻuvalaṉ peṉpurukanek
kaḷḷūṟa vuḷḷē kacintūṟu pintēṟa
lammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē. (3)
kulipaṭṭa kāntaṭ ṭaḷirkkiyiṟ cemmaṇi
kuyiṉṟavam maṉinittilaṅ
kōttavam maṉimuṉ celappiṉ celuntaṉmi
kōkaṉaka maṉiyāṭṭipāṟ
kalipaṭṭa veṇcuṭark kaṭavuṭō yn tēkavatu
kaṇṭukoṇ ṭēpuḻuṅkuṅ
kāykatirk kaṭavuḷum piṉṟoṭarva tēyppak
kaṟaṅkaruvi tūṅkuvōṅku
malippaṭṭa vāramum vayiramum piṟavumā
māmaṇit tiraḷivāri
maṟitirik kiyā leṭutteṟiya nāṟkōṭṭu
matakaḷiṟu piḷiṟiyōṭum
alipaṭṭa viyit tuṟicciṟi yaṉappēṭi
yammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē. (4)
tamarāṉa niṉṟuṇic cēṭiyari lorucilar
taṭakkiyi neṭuttāṭuniṉ
ṟaraḷavam maṉipiṭit tetirvīci vīciyiṭa
cārivala cāritiriyā
nimirāmu ṉammaṉiyo rāyira meṭutteṟiya
niriniriya tāykakaṉamēl
niṟkiṉṟa tamminī peṟṟavaki lāṇṭamu
nirittuvit tatukaṭuppa
imirā variccurum pārtteḻap poḻilū
ṭeḻuntapin tātula kelām
iruḷceyac ceytuniṉ cēṉā parākameṉu
mēkkamaḷa kāpurikkum
amarā matikkuñcey maturā purittalivi
yammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē. (5)
uyirā yirukkiṉṟa cēṭiyariṉ malarmī
tutittava ḷetirttuniṉṉō
ṭoṭṭiyeṭ ṭippiṭit tiṭṭavam maṉitēṭi
yōṭiyā ṭittiriyanī
peyarā tiruntuviḷi yāṭuvatu kaṇṭenti
piṟimuṭi tuḷakka muṭimēṟ
perukucura kaṅkinuri poṅkalam māṉiyap
peṇkoṭiyu māṭaṉmāṉa
veyarā maṉampuḻuṅ kiṭumamarar taccaṉum
viyappac ceyuntavaḷamā
mēṭiyun taṇṭaraḷa māṭamun teṇṇilā
vīcat ticikkaḷiṟelām
ayirā vatattiṉi nikarkkumatu rittalivi
yammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉi yāṭiyaruḷē. (6)
vēṟu
muttama ḻuttiya vammaṉi kimmalar
muḷarima ṇaṅkamaḻa
moykuḻal vaṇṭuniṉ miviḻi vaṇṭiṉ
muyaṅki mayaṅkiyiṭak
kottu maṇittira ḷiṟceyu mammaṉi
kuyiliṉmi ḻaṟṟiyaniṉ
kuḻaliṉi cikkuru kippaṉi tūṅku
kuṟuntuḷi cintiyiṭa
vitturu mattili ḻittavu niṉki
viraṟpava ḷattaḷiriṉ
viḷitaru moḷḷoḷi tiruṭap pōvatu
mīḷvatu māyttiriya
attaṉ maṉatteḻu tiyavuyi rōviya
māṭuka vammāṉiyē
aḻaku taḻintakal yāṇacavuntari
yāṭuka vammāṉiyē. (7)
kiḷanila vumiḻparu muttiṉ kōvi
yeṭuttavar tirumārpuk
kiṭuva kaṭuppavu mappari cēpala
maṇiyi ṉiyaṟṟiyiṭum
vaḷaroḷi vimmiya vammaṉi celvatu
vāṉavi lottiṭavum
maṉaṉek kurukap paramā ṉanta
maṭutta tiruttoṇṭark
kaḷikaṉi yattiru varuḷkaṉi yuṅkaṉi
yāṭuka vammāṉiyē
aḻaku taḻintakal yāṇa cavuntari
yāṭuka vammāṉiyē. (8)
kimmala riṟpoli katirmut tammaṉi
nakimut toḷitōyak
kaṇṭavar niṟkap piṟarcilar ceṅkik
kamalac cuṭarkatuvac
cemmaṇi yiṟcey tiḻittaṉa veṉavuñ
ciṟcilar kaṭkaṭiyiṉ
cevviyi vavviya piṉkaru maṇiyiṟ
ceytaṉa kolleṉavun
tammaṉa moppa vurippaṉa maṟṟic
camayat tamivupeṟār
tattami ṉiṉṟu pitaṟṟuva poruvat
taṉimutal yāmeṉpārk
kammaṉi yāyavar tammaṉi yāṉava
ḷāṭuka vammāṉiyē
aḻaku taḻintakal yāṇa cavuntari
yāṭuka vammāṉiyē. (9)
oḷḷoḷi marakata mummuḻu nīlamu
moṇṭara ḷattiraḷum
oḻukoḷi poṅka viḷintiṭu mammaṉi
yorumūṉ ṟaṭivileṭāk
kaḷḷaviḻ kōti vicumpuṟa vīcuva
kaṇṇutal pāṟcelaniṉ
kiyil vaḷartta pacuṅkiḷi yumvaḷar
kāmar karuṅkuyilum
piḷḷive ḷōtima mummuṟi muṟiyāṟ
perukiya kātalimēṟ
pēca viṭuppa kaṭuppa vaṇittoru
peṭiyō ṭācavaṉam
aḷḷal vayiṟṟuyiṉ maturit turimaka
ḷāṭuka vammāṉiyē
aḻaku taḻintakal yāṇacavuntari
yāṭuka vammāṉiyē. (10)
ammāṉip paruvam muṟṟiṟṟu

9. nīrāṭaṟ paruvam

vaḷiyāṭu vaṇkip polaṉcaṅ koṭumpoṅku
maṟitiric caṅkoliṭa
matararik kaṭkayal varikkaya loṭumpuraḷa
makaranta muṇṭuvaṇṭiṉ
kiḷiyoṭu niṉṟiruk kēcapā cattiṉoṭu
kiḷarciva lakkotteḻak
kiṭiyāta putuvirun tetirkoṇṭu tattamiṟ
kēḷirka ṭaḻīikkoṇṭeṉat
taḷiyoṭu kariyaṭic ciṟukaṭ peruṅkit
taṭakkaḷi ṟeṭuttu maṟṟat
tavaḷak kaḷiṟṟiṉoṭu muṭṭaviṭ ṭeṭṭumata
tantiyum pantaṭittu
viḷiyāṭum viyit taṭantuṟi kuṭintuputu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭikkoṭi yavarkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (1)
niripoṅ kiṭuñceṅki veḷvaḷi kaḷippanaki
nilavuviri pavaḷamveḷiṟa
nīlak karuṅkuvaḷi ceṅkuvaḷi pūppavaṟa
ṉeṟikuḻaṟ kaṟṟi cariyat
tiripoṅku taṇṇan tuṟikuṭin tāṭuva
ceḻuntaraṅ kakkaṅkinuṇ
ciṟutivali yāppoṅku māṉanta mākkaṭa
ṟiḷittāṭu kiṉṟatēyppak
karipoṅku maṟitirik kiyāṟ ṟaṭampaṇik
kaḻaṉiyiṟ kaṉṉiyāmulik
kaḷapak kuḻampik karittuviṭ ṭaḷḷaṟ
karuñcēṟu ceñcēṟatāy
viripoṅ kiṭattuṅka vēkavati poṅkuputu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭikkoṭi yavarkkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (2)
paṇṇāṟu kiḷimoḻip pāviniṉ ṟirumēṉi
pācoḷi virippa vantaṇ
pavaḷak koṭikkāmar pacciḷaṅ koṭiyatāyp
parumutta marakatamāyt
taṇṇāṟu mallaṟ ṟuṟicciṟi yaṉaṅkaḷi
taḻikkuṅ kalāmaññipāyc
cakalamun niṉṟiruc corupameṉ ṟōliṭuñ
catumaṟip poruḷ veḷiyiṭak
kaṇṇāṟu kuḻaliyar kuṭakkoṅki poṅkuceṅ
kaḷapamuṅ kattūriyum
karppuramu mokkak karittōṭi vāṇiyuṅ
kāḷinti yuṅkaṅkiyām
viṇṇāṟu maḷavaḷāy viḷiyāṭu viyiputu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭikkoṭi yavarkkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (3)
tūṅkuciṟi yaṟukā luṟaṅkukuḻa ṉiṉṟuṇit
tōḻiyarkaṇ meṟkuṅkuman
tōyum paṉittuṟic civiṟavī cakkuṟun
tuḷiyem maruṅkumōṭi
vāṅkumali villimār viṇṇuṟu naṉintavar
vaṉintiṭu tikamparañ cev
vaṇṇamāc ceyvatac cevvāṉa vaṇṇaroṭu
mañcaḷviḷi yāṭalēyppat
tēṅkumali yaruvineṭu nīttatu mācuṇat
tiraḷpuṟañ cuṟṟiyīrppac
ciṉavēḻa moṉṟoru cuḻiccuḻalaṉ mantaran
tirikaṭaṉ matittaṉmāṉum
vīṅkupuṉal viyittaṭantuṟi kuṭintuputu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭikkoṭi yavarkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (4)
tuḷikkum paṉittivali citaṟak kuṭintāṭu
tuṟiyiṟ ṟuṟittamiḻoṭum
toṉmaṟi teḷikkuṅ kalikkoṭi yeṉuntuṇit
tōḻīmūḻ kippuṉaṉmaṭut
toḷikkum patattumaṟ ṟavaḷeṉa vaṉappēṭi
yōṭip piṭippatammi
yoṇpari purattoṉiyu maṭanaṭiyum vuviṉa
tuṇarntupiṉ ṟoṭarvatēyppa
neḷikkun taraṅkat taṭaṅkaṅki yuṭaṉoṭṭi
nittilap pantāṭavum
nirimaṇit tiraḷiṉ kaḻaṅkāṭa vuntaṉ
ṉeṭuttirik kiyeṭuttu
viḷikkum peruntaṇ ṭuṟikkaṭavuḷ viyineṭu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭikkoṭi yavarkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (5)
vēṟu
tuṅka mulippoṟ kuṭaṅkoṇṭu
tūnīrnīnti viḷiyāṭun
tuṇiccē ṭiyarkaṇ mēṟpacumpoṟ
cuṇṇa meṟiya varaccēnta
aṅkaṇ vicumpi ṉiṉkuḻaṟkāṭ
ṭaṟukāṟ karumpa reḻuntārppa
tiyaṉ ṟirumē ṉiyalammi
yaruṭkaṭ curumpārt teḻaṉmāṉac
ceṅka ṇiḷiñar kaḷirkāmat
tīmuṇ ṭiṭakkaṇ ṭiḷamakaḷir
ceḻumeṉ kuḻaṟkūṭ ṭakiṟpukiyāṟ
ṟiraḷkāyk katali paḻuttunaṟi
poṅku maturip perumāṭṭi
putunī rāṭi yaruḷukavē
porunit tuṟivaṉ poṟpāvāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷūkavē. (6)
iḻiyum puṉaṟṟaṇ ṭuṟimuṉṟi
lituvem perumāṉ maṇcumanta
iṭameṉ ṟalarveṇ kamalappeṇ
ṇicippak kacintuḷ ḷurukiyiru
viḻiyuñ civappavāṉanta
veḷḷampoḻintu niṉṟaṉiyāl
mīṇṭum peruka viṭuttavarkōr
vēli yiṭutaṉ mikiyaṉṟē
piḻiyu naṟikkaṟ pakamalarnta
piraca malarpūn tukaṇmūḻkum
piṟikkōṭ ṭayirā vataṅkūntaṟ
piṭiyō ṭāṭat tēṉaruvi
poḻiyum poḻiṟkū ṭaliṟpolivāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷukavē
porunit tuṟivaṉ poṟpāvāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷūkavē. (7)
maṟikkun tirittaṇ puṉalviyi
vaṇṭa liṭumaṇ kūṭikaṭṭi
vāric cumantōrk kammituṇi
maṇippoṟ kuṭattiṟ karittūṟṟum
veṟikkuṅ kumaccē ṟekkariṭum
virippūn tuṟimaṇ pōlorutti
veṇpiṭ ṭiṭavu maṭittoruvaṉ
vēli koḷavum vēṇṭumeṉak
kuṟikku miṭattiṟ ṟaṭantūnīr
kuṭiyap peṟiṉak kaṅkitiruk
kōṭī rattuk kuṭiyiruppuṅ
kūṭā pōlum polaṉkuvaṭṭup
poṟikkuñ cuṟavak koṭiyuyartatāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷukavē
porunit tuṟivaṉ poṟpāvāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷūkavē. (8)
vēṟu
coṟkoṭi yōṭu malarkkoṭi koytu
toṭutta virittoṭiyum
cuntari tīṭṭiya cintura mummiru
tuṅkak koṅkikaḷiṉ
viṟkoṭi kōṭṭiya kuṅkuma muṅkuṭi
veḷḷaṅ koḷḷikoḷa
veḷiyē kaṇṭuniṉ vaṭivaḻa kiyaṉ
viḻikku viruntu ceya
viṟkoṭi yōṭu kayaṟkoṭi vīra
ṉeṭutta karuppuvilum
intira taṉuvum vaṇaṅka vaṇaṅku
miṇippuru vakkoṭicēr
poṟkoṭi yimaya maṭakkoṭi viyip
putunī rāṭukavē
porunit tuṟiyoṭu kumarit tuṟiyavaḷ
putunī rāṭukavē. (9)
koḷḷive ḷaruvi paṭintiṭu mimayak
kūntaṉ maṭappiṭipōl
koṟkit tuṟiyiṟ ciṟiviri yappuṉal
kuṭiyu maṉappeṭipōl
teḷḷamu takkaṭa ṉaṭuviṟ ṟōṉṟu
ceḻuṅkama lakkuyilpōl
teyvak kaṅkit tiriyū ṭeḻumoru
cempava ḷakkoṭipōl
kaḷḷaviḻ kōtiyar kuḻaliṟ kuḻalici
kaṟṟup poṟṟaruviṟ
kaḷinaṟa vuṇṭa maṭappeṭiyōṭu
kalantu muyaṅkivarip
puḷḷuṟi pūmpoḻiṉ maturit turimakaḷ
putunī rāṭukavē
porunit tuṟiyoṭu kumarit tuṟiyavaḷ
putunī rāṭukavē. (10)
nīrāṭaṟ paruvam muṟṟiṟṟu

10. ūcaṟ paruvam

oḷḷoḷiya pavaḷak koḻuṅkāṉ micippoṅku
moḻukoḷiya vayiraviṭṭat
tūṟṟuñ ceḻuntaṇ ṇilākkāl viḻuntaṉiya
voṇṭaraḷa vaṭamvīkkiyē
aḷḷiṭa vaḻintuceṟ ṟoḷituḷum puṅkiraṇa
varuṇaran nappalakipuk
kāṭuniṉ ṟōṟṟamap paritimaṇ ṭalamvaḷa
rarumperuñ cuṭariyēyppat
teḷḷucuvi yamutaṅ kaṉintavā ṉantat
tirikaṭaṉ maṭittuḻakkuñ
celvac cerukkarkaṇ maṉakkamala nekkapūñ
cērkkiyiṟ paḻiya pāṭaṟ
puḷḷoli yeḻakkuṭi pukuntacun taravalli
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (1)
viṟpoliya nilavupoḻi veṇṇit tilampūṇṭu
viḻutupaṭa maḻakatirviṭum
veṇṭāḷa vūcaliṉ micippolital puṇṭarika
vīṭṭil polintumaturac
coṟpoli paḻampāṭal collukiṉ ṟavaḷuniṉ
corupameṉ patumiḷanilāt
tūṟṟumati maṇṭalat tamutamā yamminī
tōṉṟukiṉ ṟatumvirippa
eṟpoliya voḻukumuḻu māṇikka maṇimukap
pēṟimaḻi mukiṟavaḻvatav
veṟicuṭark kaṭavuṭiru maṭiyilavaṉ maṭamaka
ḷiruntuviḷi yāṭalēykkum
poṟpurici maturā purippoli tiruppāvi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (2)
urukiya pacumpoṉ ṉacumpaveyil vīcupoṉ
ṉūcali yutintāṭalum
oṇṭaḷi raṭiccuva ṭuṟappeṟu macōkunaṟa
voḻukumalar pūttutirvatuṉ
ṟirumuṉuru vaṅkaran tentiyār niṟpatu
terintiṭa namakkituveṉāc
ceñcilik kaḷvaṉoru vaṉṟoṭi maṭakkātu
terikaṇikaḷ corivatēyppa
erimaṇi kuyiṉṟapoṟ ceykuṉṟu maḻakati
rerippaveḻu ceñcōtiyū
ṭiḷamati yimippatuṉ ṟirumukac celvivēṭ
ṭeḻunāt talittavamavaṉ
purivatu kaṭukkumatu rāpuri maṭakkiḷḷi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (3)
kaṅkimuṭi makiḻnartiru vuḷamacin tāṭak
kalantāṭu poṉṉūcalak
kaṭavuṭiru nōkkattu nekkuruki yiṭaniṉ
kaṭikkaṇōk kattumaṟṟac
ceṅkaṇviṭi yavarmaṉamu mokkak karinturuku
ceykiyavar cittamē poṟ
ṟiruvūcalāvirun tāṭukiṉ ṟāyeṉuñ
ceytiyi yeṭutturippa
aṅkaṇeṭu nilamviṭar paṭakkiḻit tōṭuvē
raṭiyiṟ paḻutta palaviṉ
aḷipoṟ cuḷikkuṭak kaṉiyuṭin tūṟṟatē
ṉaruvipila mēḻumuṭṭip
poṅkivaḻi poḻiṉmatura maturinā yakitirup
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (4)
cērkkuñ cuvippāṭa lamitoḻuka voḻukupoṟ
ṟiruvūcal pāṭiyāṭac
civapirāṉ ṟirumuṭi yacippamuṭi mēṟpoṅku
ceṅkaṇā varacakilamvit
tārkkum paṇāṭavi yacippac carācaramu
macikiṉṟa tammaṉiyacin
tāṭalā laṇṭamu makaṇṭapaki raṇṭamu
macintāṭu kiṉṟa tēyppak
kārkon taḷakkōti maṭaviyar kuḻaṟkūṭṭu
kamaḻnaṟum pukiviṇmicik
kiparan teḻuvaturu māṟiravi maṇṭalaṅ
kikkoḷa viruppaṭalavāṉ
pōrkkiṉṟa tokkumatu rāpuri maṭakkiḷḷi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (5)
tērkkōla moṭuniṉ ṟirukkōla muṅkaṇṭu
cintaṉi puḻuṅku kōpat
tīyaviya mūṇṭeḻuṅ kāmā ṉalaṅkāṉṟa
cikiyeṉa veḻuntupoṅkun
tārkkōla vēṇiyarta muḷḷameṉa vēpoṟ
ṟaṭañciliyu murukiyōṭat
taṇmati muṭittatum veḷviṭik koṇmaṇi
tarittatum viruttamākak
kārkkōla nīlak karuṅkaḷat tōṭoruvar
ceṅkaḷat tēṟṟalamarak
kaṭkaṇi turakkuṅ karumpuruva villoṭoru
kiviṟ kuṉittuniṉṟa
pōrkkōla mētiru maṇakkōla māṉapeṇ
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (6)
kuḻiyum pacuṅkaṇ mucukkali verīicciṟu
kuṟumpalavi ṉeṭiyapārak
kompoṭi paṭattūṅku muṭpuṟak kaṉiyiṉ
kuṭaṅkoṇṭu nīntaṉ maṭivāy
vaḻiyuṅ koḻuntē ṉuvaṭṭeḻu taṭaṅkāviṉ
vaḷḷukirk karuviraṟkūṉ
mantika ḷirntēkum viciyiṉil vicinteḻu
marakkōṭu pāyavayiṟu
kiḻiyuṅ kalittiṅka ḷamutaruvi tūṅkuva
kiḷittuvaṇ ṭuḻupintuḻāyk
kēcavaṉ kālvīca vaṇṭakō ḷakimukaṭu
kīṇṭuveḷ ḷaruvipoṅkip
poḻiyun tiṟattiṉi nikarkkumatu rittalivi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (7)
olkuṅ koṭicciṟu maruṅkiṟ kiraṅkimel
lōtivaṇ ṭārnteḻappoṉ
ṉūcali yutintāṭu maḷaviṉmalar makaḷammi
yuḷḷaṭik kūṉpiṟitaḻīi
malkuñ cuvaṭṭiṉi valampurik kīṟṟitukol
vāṇiyeṉ ṟacatiyāṭi
maṇimuṟuval kōṭṭaniṉ vaṇaṅkā muṭikkoru
vaṇakkaneṭu nāṇvaḻaṅkap
pilkuṅ kuṟumpaṉik kūtirk kuṭinteṉap
piracanā ṟimpāṟkiḷam
pētiyarka ḷūṭṭum koḻumpuki maṭuttumeṉ
penaṭeyoṭu variccurumpar
pulkun taṭampaṇi yuṭuttamatu rittalivi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (8)
koṉceyta ceḻumaṇit tiruvūca laramakaḷir
        koṇṭāṭa vāṭuntoṟuṅ
        kuṟumuṟuva ṉeṭunila varuntuñ cakōramāyk
kūntalaṅ kaṟṟi cuṟṟun
teṉceyta maḻalic curumparāy maṅkiniṉ
        ceṅkip pacuṅkiḷḷiyāyt
tēvatē vaṉpoliva ṟevvuruvu māmavaṉ
        ṟiruvuruviṉ muṟiterippa
miṉceyta cāyalavar mēṟṟalat tāṭiya
        virippuṉali ṉaruvi kuṭiyum
veḷḷāṉi kuṅkumac ceñcēṟu nāṟamaṭa
        meṉpiṭiyi yañciniṟkum
poṉceyta māṭamali kūṭaṟ peruñcelvi
        poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
        poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (9)
irupatumu meṉkuraṟ kiṇkiṇiyu muṟiyiṭ
        ṭirintiṭu mariccilampum
iṟumiṟu maruṅkeṉ ṟiraṅkumē kaliyumpo
        ṉeḻutumcem paṭṭu vīkkun
tiruviṭiyu muṭitāra mummoṭ ṭiyāṇamuñ
        ceṅkip pacuṅkiḷḷiyun
tirumulit taraḷavut tariyamum maṅkalat
        tirunāṇu maḻakoḻukaniṉ
ṟaruḷpoḻiyu matimukamu mukamatiyi ṉeṭunila
        varumpukuṟu nakiyuñāṉa
āṉanta mākkaṭal kuṭintukuḻi makaratto
        ṭamarāṭu mōṭarikkaṭ
porukayalum vaṭivaḻaku pūttacun taravalli
        poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
        poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (10)

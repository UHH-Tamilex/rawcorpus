kāppu
vināyaka vaṇakkam
kārkoṇṭa kavuṇmatak kaṭaiveḷḷa muṅkaṭ
        kaṭaikkaṭaik kaṉalu mellai
   kaṭavātu taṭavuk kuḻaiccevi mukanteṟi
        kaṭaikkā ṟiṭṭa veṅkōṉ
pōrkoṇṭa veṇṭō ṭpolaṉkuvaṭu potiyumveṇ
        poṭituṭi yaṭittu vaittup
   puḻutiyāṭ ṭayarāvo rayirāva ṇattulavu
        poṟkaḷiṟ ṟaittu tippān
tārkoṇṭa matimuṭi yoruttaṉ ṟirukkaṇmalar
        cāttak kiḷarntu poṅkit
   tavaḻumiḷa veyilumaḻa nilavumaḷa vaḷavalāṟ
        ṟaṇṇeṉṟu vecceṉṟu poṉ
vārkoṇṭa ṭaṇintamulai malaivalli karppūra
        valliyapi rāma valli
    māṇikka vallimara katavalli yapiṭēka
        valli coṟ ṟamiḻ taḻaiyavē.

1-vatu kāppup paruvam

tirumāl
maṇikoṇṭa neṭunēmi valayañ cumantāṟṟu
        mākaṇac cūṭṭu mōṭṭu
    mālkaḷiṟu piṭarvaitta vaḷaroḷi vimāṉattu
        vāluḷai maṭaṅka ṟāṅkum
aṇikoṇṭa pīṭikaiyi ṉampoṉmuṭi muṭivaittē
        maiyaṉoṭu vīṟṟi rukku
    maṅkayaṟ kaṇṇamutai maṅkaiyark karaciyaiye
        mammaṉaiyai yiṉitu kākka
kaṇikoṇṭa taṇṭuḻāyk kāṭalait tōṭutēṅ
        kaḻaluḻipāyn taḷaṟu ceyyak
    kaḻaṉipaṭu naṭavaiyiṟ kamalat taṇaṅkaracor
        kaiyaṇai mukantu cellap
paṇikoṇṭa tuttip paṭappāyc curuṭṭup
        paṇaittō ḷerutta laippap
    paḻamaṟaikaṇ muṟaiyiṭap paintamiḻp piṉceṉṟa
        paccaip pacuṅ koṇṭalē. (1)
paramacivaṉ
vēṟu
cikara vaṭavarai kuṉiya nimirtaru
        ceruvi loruporu villeṉakkōṭṭiṉar
ceṭikoḷ paṟitalai yamaṇa retiretir
        celavor matalaicol vaiyaiyiṟ kūṭṭiṉar
tiruvu mimaiyavar taruvu maravoli
        ceyava lavarkoḷa nalkukait tīṭṭiṉar
ciṟiya veṉa tupuṉ moḻiyum vaṭitamiḻ
        teriyu mavarmutu colleṉac cūṭṭiṉar
pakaru micaiticai parava viruvarkaḷ
        payilu miyaṟeri veḷvaḷait tōṭṭiṉar
paciya vaṟukoṭu veḷiya nilaviri
        pavaḷa vaṉamaṭar palcaṭaik kāṭṭiṉar
patuma mutalvaṉu meḻuta variyator
        paṉuva leḻutiya vaitikap pāṭṭiṉar
paracu miracata capaiyi ṉaṭamiṭu
        parata patayuka muḷḷamvait tēttun
takaramoḻukiya kuḻalu nilavumiḻ
        taraḷa nakaiyume maiyaṉaip pārttetir
caruvi yamarporu viḻiyu maṟukiṭai
        taḷara vaḷarvator cevvimuṟ ṟākkaṉa
taṉamu maṉaṉuṟa veḻuti yeḻutaru
        tamatu vaṭivaiyu meḷḷimaṭ ṭūṟṟiya
tavaḷa malarvaru miḷami ṉoṭucata
        taḷamiṉ vaḻipaṭu taiyalait tūttirai
makara meṟikaṭa lamutai yamutuku
        maḻalai paḻakiya kiḷḷaiyaip pēṭṭaṉa
maṭava naṭaipayil piṭiyai viraiceṟi
        varaicey puyamicai vaiyam vaittāṟṟiya
vaḻutiyuṭaiya kaṇ maṇiyo ṭulavu peṇ
        maṇiyai yaṇitikaḻ celviyait tēkkamaḻ
matura moḻukiya tamiḻi ṉiyalpayiṉ
        maturai marakata valliyaik kākkavē. (2)
citti viṉāyakar
vēṟu
kaittala mōṭiru karaṭak karaittirai
        kaikkuka ṭāmuṭaik kaṭaliṟ kuḷittemar
cittama tāmoru taṟiyiṟ ṟuvakkuṟu
        cittivi nāyaka ṉicaiyaip paḻiccutum
puttamu tōvaru ṭaḻaiyat taḻaittator
        poṟkōṭi yōveṉa maturit tuvaṭṭeḻu
muttamiḻ tērtaru maturait talattuṟai
        muttaṉa mēvupe ṇaracaip purakkavē. (3)
murukavēḷ
vēṟu
pakara maṭuppak kaṭāmeṭut tūṟṟumor
       pakaṭu naṭattip pulōmacaic cūṟpuyal
paruki yiṭakkaṟ pakāṭavip pāṟpoli
       paravai yiṭaip paṟ pamāteṉat tōṟṟiya
kumari yirukkak kalāmayiṟ kūttayar
       kuḷirpuṉa moyttiṭ ṭacāraliṟ pōycciṟu
kuṟavar makaṭkuc calāmiṭaṟ kēkkaṟu
       kumaraṉai muttukkumāraṉaip pōṟṟutum
imiḻtirai muṟṟat tumērumat tārttumuḷ
       ḷeyiṟu naccup paṇāṭavit tāppicait
tiṟuka viṟukkit tuḻāy muṭit tīrttaṉo
       ṭevaru matittup parāpavat tīccuṭa
vamutucey vittiṭ ṭapōṉakat tāṟcuṭa
       raṭaru miruṭṭuk kirīvamaṭ ṭākkiya
vaḻakiya cokkaṟ kumālceyat tōṭṭika
        lamarcey kayaṟkaṭ kumāriyaik kākkavē. (4)
nāṉmukaṉ
vēṟu
mēkap pacuṅkuḻavi vāymaṭut tuṇṇavum
        viṭpulam virun tayaravum
veḷḷamutam vīcuṅ karuntiraip paintukil
        virittuṭut tutti viriyum
nākattu mīccuṭikai naṭuvaṭ kiṭantamaṭa
        naṅkaiyaip peṟṟu maṟṟan
nākaṇait tuñcutaṉ ṟantaikku vantutavu
        naḷiṉak kuḻantai kākka
pākattu marakatak kuṉṟeṉṟor tamaṉiyak
        kuṉṟoṭu kiḷaittu niṉṟa
pavaḷat taṭaṅkuṉ ṟuḷakkaṇṇa teṉṟap
        parañcuṭar muṭikku muṭimūṉ
ṟākat tamaittuppi ṉorumuṭit taṉmuṭivait
        taṇaṅkaracu vīṟṟi rukkum
apiṭēka valliyai yaḷikkula muḻakkukuḻa
        laṅkayaṟ kaṇṇa mutaiyē. (5)
tēvēntiraṉ
vēṟu
cuḻiyuṅ karuṅkaṭ kuṇṭakaḻi
        cuvaṟṟuñ cuṭarvēṟ kiritiritta
tōṉṟaṟ kaḷittuc cuṟavuyartta
        cokkap perumāṉ cekkarmuṭi
poḻiyun taraṅkak kaṅkaiviraip
        puṉalkāl pāyccat taḻaintuviri
puvaṉan taṉipūt taruḷpaḻutta
        poṉṉaṅ koṭiyaip purakkavaḻin
tiḻiyun tuṇarkkaṟ pakattiṉaṟa
        vitaḻttēṉ kuṭittuk kumaṭṭiyeti
reṭukkum ciṟaivaṇ ṭuvaṭṭuṟavuṇ
        ṭiraikkak karaikku matakkaluḻik
kuḻiyuñ ciṟuka ṇēṟṟurumuk
        kuralveṇ puyaluṅ karumpuyaluṅ
kuṉṟaṅ kulaiya vukaittēṟuṅ
        kulicat taṭakkai puttaḷē. (6)
tirumakaḷ
vēṟu
veñcūṭṭu neṭṭuṭal virikkum paṭappāyaṉ
        mīmicait tuñcu nīla
mēkatti ṉākattu viṭucuṭarp paṭalaimaṇi
        meṉpara luṟutta nontu
pañcūṭṭu cīṟaṭi pataittumataṉ veṅkatirp
        paṭumiḷa veyiṟku ṭaintum
paintuḻāyk kāṭuviri taṇṇiḻa lotunkumor
        pacuṅkoṭiyai yañca lippā
mañcūṭ ṭakaṭṭuneṭu vāṉmukaṭu turuvumoru
        maṟaiyōti mañca likka
maṟitiraic ciṟaiviriyu māyira mukakkaṭavuṇ
        mantāki ṉippe yartta
ceñcūṭṭu veḷḷō timaṅkuṭi yirukkumvaḷar
        ceñcaṭaik karumi ṭaṟṟut
tēvukku muṉṉiṉṟa teyvattai mummulait
        tiruvaip purakka veṉṟē. (7)
kalaimakaḷ
veḷḷit takaṭṭuneṭ ṭēṭaviḻt tiṉṉicai
        virumpuñ curumpar pāṭa
viḷainaṟavu kakkum polaṉ pokuṭ ṭalarkamala
        vīṭṭuk koḻit teṭuttut
teḷḷit teḷikkum tamiḻkaṭali ṉaṉpiṉain
        tiṇaiyeṉa veṭutta viṟainūṟ
ṟeḷḷamutu kūṭṭuṇumor veḷḷōti mattiṉiru
        cīṟaṭi muṭippam vaḷarpaiṅ
kiḷḷaikku maḻalaip pacuṅkutalai yoḻukutīṅ
        kiḷaviyuṅ kaḷi mayiṟkuk
kiḷariḷañ cāyalu navvikku nōkkumviri
        kiñcukac cūṭṭa racaṉap
piḷḷaikku maṭanaṭaiyu muṭaṉoṭu makaḷirkkor
        pētamaiyu mutavi mutirāp
piḷḷaimaiyiṉ vaḷḷaṉmai koḷḷumoru pāṇṭip
        pirāṭṭiyaik kākka veṉṟē. (8)
turkkai
vaṭipaṭṭa mukkuṭumi vaṭivē ṟirittiṭṭu
        vaḷaikaruṅ kōṭṭu mōṭṭu
makiṭaṅ kaviḻttuk kaṭāṅkaviḻk kuñciṟukaṇ
        mālyāṉai vīṅka vāṅkun
tuṭipaṭṭa koṭinuṇ ṇucuppiṟ kuṭainteṉac
        cuṭukaṭaik kaṉali tūṇṭuñ
cuḻalkaṇ muṭaṅkuḷai maṭaṅkalai yukaittēṟu
        cūrarip piṇavu kākka
piṭipaṭṭa maṭanaṭaik kēkkaṟṟa kūntaṟ
        piṭikkuḻāñ cuṟṟa voṟṟaip
piṟaimarup puṭaiyator kaḷiṟṟiṉaip peṟṟentai
        piṭṭuṇṭu kaṭṭuṇṭu niṉ
ṟaṭipaṭṭa tirumēṉi kuḻaiyak kuḻaittiṭṭa
        vaṇimaṇik kimpu rikkō
ṭākatta tākak kaṭampā ṭavikkuḷ viḷai
        yāṭumor maṭappiṭi yaiyē. (9)
catta mātarkaḷ
vēṟu
kaṭakaḷi ṟutavuka pāymicaip pōrttavaḷ
        kavikuvi tuṟukaliṉ vāriyait tūrttavaḷ
kaṭalvayi ṟeriyavoḷ vēliṉaip pārttavaḷ
        kaṭikamaḻ tarumalar tārmuṭic cērttavaḷ
iṭiyuka vaṭalari yēṟukait tārttavaḷ
        eḻutaru muḻumaṟai nūliṉiṟ kūrttavaḷ
eyiṟuko ṭuḻuteḻu pāriṉaip pērttavaḷ
        eṉumiva reḻuvarka ṭāṇmuṭic cūṭṭutum
kuṭamoṭu kuṭaviyar pāṇikaik kōttiṭu
        kuravaiyu malatorpa ṇāmuṭic cūṭṭaruḷ
kutikoḷa naṭamiṭu pāṭaluk kēṟpavor
        kuḻalicai paḻakaḷi pāṭiṭak kēṭṭuṭai
maṭalaviḻ tuḷapana ṟāveṭut tūṟṟiṭa
        maḻakaḷi ṟeṉaveḻu kārmukac cūṟpuyal
varavaru miḷaiyaku māriyaik kōṭṭeyiṉ
        maturaiyil vaḷarkavu māriyaik kākkavē. (10)
muppattu mūvar
vēṟu
amaril venniṭuma vutiyar piṉṉiṭumo
        rapayar muṉṉiṭuva ṉattokka vōṭavum
aḷavu memmuṭaiya tiṟaiyi teṉṉamuṭi
        yaraca reṇṇilaror muṟṟattu vāṭavum
akila maṉṉaravar ticaiyiṉ maṉṉariva
        ramara reṉṉumurai tikkeṭṭu mūṭavum
amuta veṇmatiyiṉ marapai yuṉṉiyuṉi
        yalari yaṇṇalmuḻu veppattu mūḻkavum
kumari poṉṉivaiyai poruṇai naṉṉatikaḷ
        kutikoḷ viṇṇatiyiṉ mikkuk kulāvavuṅ
kuvaṭu teṉmalaiyi ṉikara tiṉmaicurar
        kuṭikoḷ poṉmalaitu tittuppa rāvavuṅ
kumarar muṉṉiruva ramara raṉṉaiyivaḷ
        kumari yiṉṉamume ṉaccittar pāṭavuṅ
kuravai vimmavara makaḷir maṇṇileḻil
        kulavu kaṉṉiyarkaḷ kaikkokka vāṭavum
kamalaṉ muṉṉiyiṭu maraca vaṉṉameḻu
        kaṭali laṉṉamuṭa ṉaṭpukkai kūṭavuṅ
kariya cemmaloṭu miḷaiya cemmalviṭu
        karuṭaṉ mañcaiyoṭor kaṭcikku ḷūṭavuṅ
kaṭavi viṇṇaracu naṭavum vemmuṉaiya
        kaḷiṟu kaimmalaicel koppattu vīḻavuṅ
kaṉaka maṉṉutaṭa naḷiṉi tuṉṉiyiru
        kamala miṉṉumoru paṟpattuṇ mēvavum
imaya meṉṉamaṉu muṟaiko ṭeṉṉarume
        miṟaiyai naṉmaruke ṉappeṟṟu vāḻavum
evarkol paṇṇavarka ḷevarkoṉ maṇṇavarka
        ḷetukol poṉṉulake ṉattaṭṭu māṟavum
eḻilcey teṉmaturai taḻaiya mummulaiyo
        ṭeḻume ṉammaṉai vaṉappukkor kāvalar
iruva reṇmarpati ṉoruvar paṉṉiruva
        reṉum viṇṇavarkaṇ muppattu mūvarē. (11)
kāppup paruvam muṟṟiṟṟu

2. ceṅkīraip paruvam

nīrāṭṭi yāṭṭupoṟ cuṇṇan timirntaḷḷi
neṟṟiyiṟ ṟoṭṭiṭṭa veṇ
ṇīṟṟiṉoṭu puṇṭarak kīṟṟukku mēṟṟiṭavor
nittilac cuṭṭi cāttit
tārāṭṭu cūḻiyak koṇṭaiyu muṭittut
talaippaṇi tirutti muttiṉ
ṟaṇṇoḷi tatumpuṅ kutampaiyoṭu kātukkor
tamaṉiyak koppu miṭṭup
pārāṭṭu pāṇṭip peruntēvi tirumulaip
pālamuta mūṭṭi yoruniṉ
pāṉāṟu kumutaṅ kaṉintūṟu tēṟaltaṉ
paṭṭāṭai maṭina ṉaippac
cīrāṭṭi vaittumut tāṭum pacuṅkiḷḷai
ceṅkīrai yāṭi yaruḷē
teṉṉaṟku mampoṉmalai maṉṉaṟku morucelvi
ceṅkīrai yāṭi yaruḷē. (1)
uṇṇilā vuvakaip peruṅkaḷi tuḷumpaniṉ
ṟuṉṟirut tātai niṉṉai
yorumuṟai karampotti varukeṉa vaḻaittiṭumu
ṉōṭit tavaḻntu ceṉṟu
taṇṇulā maḻalaip pacuṅkutalai yamutiṉiya
tāyvayiṟu kuḷira vūṭṭit
taṭamārpa niṟaikuṅ kumaccē ṟaḷaintupoṟ
ṟāṭō y taṭakkai paṟṟip
paṇṇulā vaṭitamiḻp paintāmam viriyum
paṇaittō ḷeruttamēṟip
pācoḷiya marakatat tirumēṉi paccaip
pacuṅkatir tatumpa maṇivāyt
teṇṇilā viriya niṉṟāṭum pacuntōkai
ceṅkīrai yāṭi yaruḷē
teṉṉaṟku mampoṉmalai maṉṉaṟku morucelvi
ceṅkīrai yāṭi yaruḷē. (2)
cuṟṟuneṭu nēmic cuvarkkicaiya veṭṭuc
cuvarkkā ṉiṟutti mērut
tūṇoṉṟu naṭunaṭṭu veḷimukaṭu mūṭiyiru
cuṭar viḷakkiṭṭu muṟṟa
eṟṟupuṉa liṟkaḻuvu puvaṉap paḻaṅkala
meṭuttaṭuk kippu tukkū
ḻiṉṉamuta muñcamait taṉṉai nīpaṉmuṟai
yiḻaittiṭa vaḻitta ḻittōr
muṟṟaveḷi yiṟṟiriyu mattap perumpittaṉ
muṉṉiṉṟu tontamiṭavu
muṉiyātu vaikalu meṭuttaṭuk kipperiya
mūtaṇṭa kūṭamūṭuñ
ciṟṟilviḷai yāṭumoru pacciḷam peṇpiḷḷai
ceṅkīrai yāṭi yaruḷē
teṉṉaṟku mampoṉmalai maṉṉaṟku morucelvi
ceṅkīrai yāṭi yaruḷē. (3)
maṅkulpaṭu kantarac cuntarak kaṭavuṭku
maḻakatirk kaṟṟai cuṟṟum
vāṇayaṉa mūṉṟuṅ kuḷirntamuta kalaitalai
maṭuppak kaṭaikka ṇōkkum
poṅkumatar nōkkiṟ piṟantavā ṉantap
putuppuṇari nītta maiyaṉ
puntit taṭattiṉai nirappavaḻi yaṭiyarpāṟ
pōkacā karama ṭuppa
aṅkaṇoṭu ñālattu vittiṉṟi vittiya
vaṉait tuyirka ḷuntaḷirppa
varuṇmaṭai tiṟantukaṭai veḷḷam perukkeṭut
talaiyeṟin tukaḷa vukaḷuñ
ceṅkayal kiṭakkuṅ karuṅkaṭ pacuntōkai
ceṅkīrai yāṭi yaruḷē
teṉṉaṟku mampoṉmalai maṉṉaṟku morucelvi
ceṅkīrai yāṭi yaruḷē. (4)
paṇṇaṟā varimiṭaṟ ṟaṟukāṇ maṭuppap

8. ammāṉaip paruvam

karaikkuṅ kaṭāmiru kavuṭkuṭa muṭaintūṟṟu
kaḷiṟuperu vayiṟutūrppak
kavaḷan tiraṭṭik koṭuppateṉa vuñcūḻntor
kalaimatik kalacavamutuk
kiraikkum peruntēvar puṉkaṇ ṭuṭaittiṭa
veṭuttamuta kalacam vevvē
ṟīntiṭuva teṉavumuḻu muttiṭ ṭiḻaittiṭṭa
veṟipantiṉ niraiyeṉṉavum
viraikkun taḷirkkaik koḻuntā maraittuñci
mīteḻun tārttapiḷḷai
veḷḷōti mattiraḷi teṉavun karumpāṟai
mīmicaic cencāntuvait
taraikkuntiraikkaiveḷ ḷaruvivai yaittuṟaivi
yammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē. (1)
tiṅkaṭ koḻuntaik koḻuntupaṭu paṭarcaṭaic
cerukutiru maṇavāḷaṉmēṟ
ceḻumaṇap pantari leṭutteṟiyu mamutaveṇ
ṭiraḷaiyiṟ puraḷumaṟukāṟ
paiṅkaṭ curumpeṉa vicumpiṟ paṭarnteḻum
paṉimati micaittāviṭum
paruvamaṭa māṉeṉave ṉammaṉaini ṉammaṉaip
paṭaiviḻik kayalpāynteḻu
veṅkaṭ kaṭuṅkolaiya vēḻakku ḻāmiteṉa
mēkak kuḻāttaimuṭṭi
viḷaiyāṭu maḻakaḷiṟu kaṭaivāy kutaṭṭamukai
viṇṭavam paintukōtta
aṅkaṭ karumpēntu mapiṭēka vallitiru
vammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē. (2)
kaḷḷūṟu kañcak karattūṟu cēyoḷi
kalappac civappūṟiyum
karuṇaip perukkūṟa vamutūṟu pārvaik
kaṭaikkaṭ kaṟuppūṟiyum
naḷḷūṟu maṟuvū ṟakaṟṟumuka matiyilveṇ
ṇakaiyūṟu nilavūṟiyum
naṟṟāraḷa vammaṉaiyor ciṟkuṇat tiṉaimūṉṟu
naṟkuṇaṅ katuval kāṭṭa
uḷḷūṟu kaḷituḷum pakkurava riruvīru
muṟṟiṭu tuvāta cāntat
toruperu veḷikkē viḻittuṟaṅ kuntoṇṭa
ruḻuvalaṉ peṉpurukanek
kaḷḷūṟa vuḷḷē kacintūṟu paintēṟa
lammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē. (3)
kulaipaṭṭa kāntaṭ ṭaḷirkkaiyiṟ cemmaṇi
kuyiṉṟavam maṉainittilaṅ
kōttavam maṉaimuṉ celappiṉ celuntaṉmai
kōkaṉaka maṉaiyāṭṭipāṟ
kalaipaṭṭa veṇcuṭark kaṭavuṭō yn tēkavatu
kaṇṭukoṇ ṭēpuḻuṅkuṅ
kāykatirk kaṭavuḷum piṉṟoṭarva tēyppak
kaṟaṅkaruvi tūṅkuvōṅku
malaippaṭṭa vāramum vayiramum piṟavumā
māmaṇit tiraḷaivāri
maṟitiraik kaiyā leṭutteṟiya nāṟkōṭṭu
matakaḷiṟu piḷiṟiyōṭum
alaipaṭṭa vaiyait tuṟaicciṟai yaṉappēṭai
yammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē. (4)
tamarāṉa niṉṟuṇaic cēṭiyari lorucilar
taṭakkaiyi neṭuttāṭuniṉ
ṟaraḷavam maṉaipiṭit tetirvīci vīciyiṭa
cārivala cāritiriyā
nimirāmu ṉammaṉaiyo rāyira meṭutteṟiya
nirainiraiya tāykakaṉamēl
niṟkiṉṟa tammainī peṟṟavaki lāṇṭamu
niraittuvait tatukaṭuppa
imirā variccurum pārtteḻap poḻilū
ṭeḻuntapain tātula kelām
iruḷceyac ceytuniṉ cēṉā parākameṉu
mēkkamaḷa kāpurikkum
amarā matikkuñcey maturā purittalaivi
yammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē. (5)
uyirā yirukkiṉṟa cēṭiyariṉ malarmī
tutittava ḷetirttuniṉṉō
ṭoṭṭiyeṭ ṭippiṭit tiṭṭavam maṉaitēṭi
yōṭiyā ṭittiriyanī
peyarā tiruntuviḷai yāṭuvatu kaṇṭentai
piṟaimuṭi tuḷakka muṭimēṟ
perukucura kaṅkainurai poṅkalam māṉaiyap
peṇkoṭiyu māṭaṉmāṉa
veyarā maṉampuḻuṅ kiṭumamarar taccaṉum
viyappac ceyuntavaḷamā
mēṭaiyun taṇṭaraḷa māṭamun teṇṇilā
vīcat ticaikkaḷiṟelām
ayirā vatattiṉai nikarkkumatu raittalaivi
yammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē
ākaṅ kalantoruvar pākam pakirntapeṇ
ṇammāṉai yāṭiyaruḷē. (6)
vēṟu
muttama ḻuttiya vammaṉai kaimmalar
muḷarima ṇaṅkamaḻa
moykuḻal vaṇṭuniṉ maiviḻi vaṇṭiṉ
muyaṅki mayaṅkiyiṭak
kottu maṇittira ḷiṟceyu mammaṉai
kuyiliṉmi ḻaṟṟiyaniṉ
kuḻaliṉi caikkuru kippaṉi tūṅku
kuṟuntuḷi cintiyiṭa
vitturu mattili ḻaittavu niṉkai
viraṟpava ḷattaḷiriṉ
viḷaitaru moḷḷoḷi tiruṭap pōvatu
mīḷvatu māyttiriya
attaṉ maṉatteḻu tiyavuyi rōviya
māṭuka vammāṉaiyē
aḻaku taḻaintakal yāṇacavuntari
yāṭuka vammāṉaiyē. (7)
kiḷanila vumiḻparu muttiṉ kōvai
yeṭuttavar tirumārpuk
kiṭuva kaṭuppavu mappari cēpala
maṇiyi ṉiyaṟṟiyiṭum
vaḷaroḷi vimmiya vammaṉai celvatu
vāṉavi lottiṭavum
maṉaṉek kurukap paramā ṉanta
maṭutta tiruttoṇṭark
kaḷikaṉi yattiru varuḷkaṉi yuṅkaṉi
yāṭuka vammāṉaiyē
aḻaku taḻaintakal yāṇa cavuntari
yāṭuka vammāṉaiyē. (8)
kaimmala riṟpoli katirmut tammaṉai
nakaimut toḷitōyak
kaṇṭavar niṟkap piṟarcilar ceṅkaik
kamalac cuṭarkatuvac
cemmaṇi yiṟcey tiḻaittaṉa veṉavuñ
ciṟcilar kaṭkaṭaiyiṉ
cevviyai vavviya piṉkaru maṇiyiṟ
ceytaṉa kolleṉavun
tammaṉa moppa vuraippaṉa maṟṟaic
camayat tamaivupeṟār
tattami ṉiṉṟu pitaṟṟuva poruvat
taṉimutal yāmeṉpārk
kammaṉai yāyavar tammaṉai yāṉava
ḷāṭuka vammāṉaiyē
aḻaku taḻaintakal yāṇa cavuntari
yāṭuka vammāṉaiyē. (9)
oḷḷoḷi marakata mummuḻu nīlamu
moṇṭara ḷattiraḷum
oḻukoḷi poṅka viḷaintiṭu mammaṉai
yorumūṉ ṟaṭaivileṭāk
kaḷḷaviḻ kōtai vicumpuṟa vīcuva
kaṇṇutal pāṟcelaniṉ
kaiyil vaḷartta pacuṅkiḷi yumvaḷar
kāmar karuṅkuyilum
piḷḷaive ḷōtima mummuṟai muṟaiyāṟ
perukiya kātalaimēṟ
pēca viṭuppa kaṭuppa vaṇaittoru
peṭaiyō ṭācavaṉam
aḷḷal vayiṟṟuyiṉ maturait turaimaka
ḷāṭuka vammāṉaiyē
aḻaku taḻaintakal yāṇacavuntari
yāṭuka vammāṉaiyē. (10)

9. nīrāṭaṟ paruvam

vaḷaiyāṭu vaṇkaip polaṉcaṅ koṭumpoṅku
maṟitiraic caṅkoliṭa
matararik kaṭkayal varikkaya loṭumpuraḷa
makaranta muṇṭuvaṇṭiṉ
kiḷaiyoṭu niṉṟiruk kēcapā cattiṉoṭu
kiḷarcaiva lakkotteḻak
kiṭaiyāta putuvirun tetirkoṇṭu tattamiṟ
kēḷirka ṭaḻīikkoṇṭeṉat
taḷaiyoṭu karaiyaṭic ciṟukaṭ peruṅkait
taṭakkaḷi ṟeṭuttu maṟṟat
tavaḷak kaḷiṟṟiṉoṭu muṭṭaviṭ ṭeṭṭumata
tantiyum pantaṭittu
viḷaiyāṭum vaiyait taṭantuṟai kuṭaintuputu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭaikkoṭi yavarkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (1)
niraipoṅ kiṭuñceṅkai veḷvaḷai kaḷippanakai
nilavuviri pavaḷamveḷiṟa
nīlak karuṅkuvaḷai ceṅkuvaḷai pūppavaṟa
ṉeṟikuḻaṟ kaṟṟai cariyat
tiraipoṅku taṇṇan tuṟaikuṭain tāṭuva
ceḻuntaraṅ kakkaṅkainuṇ
ciṟutivalai yāppoṅku māṉanta mākkaṭa
ṟiḷaittāṭu kiṉṟatēyppak
karaipoṅku maṟitiraik kaiyāṟ ṟaṭampaṇaik
kaḻaṉiyiṟ kaṉṉiyāmulaik
kaḷapak kuḻampaik karaittuviṭ ṭaḷḷaṟ
karuñcēṟu ceñcēṟatāy
viraipoṅ kiṭattuṅka vēkavati poṅkuputu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭaikkoṭi yavarkkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (2)
paṇṇāṟu kiḷimoḻip pāvainiṉ ṟirumēṉi
pācoḷi virippa vantaṇ
pavaḷak koṭikkāmar pacciḷaṅ koṭiyatāyp
parumutta marakatamāyt
taṇṇāṟu mallaṟ ṟuṟaicciṟai yaṉaṅkaḷi
taḻaikkuṅ kalāmaññaipāyc
cakalamun niṉṟiruc corupameṉ ṟōliṭuñ
catumaṟaip poruḷ veḷiyiṭak
kaṇṇāṟu kuḻaliyar kuṭakkoṅkai poṅkuceṅ
kaḷapamuṅ kattūriyum
karppuramu mokkak karaittōṭi vāṇiyuṅ
kāḷinti yuṅkaṅkaiyām
viṇṇāṟu maḷavaḷāy viḷaiyāṭu vaiyaiputu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭaikkoṭi yavarkkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (3)
tūṅkuciṟai yaṟukā luṟaṅkukuḻa ṉiṉṟuṇait
tōḻiyarkaṇ meṟkuṅkuman
tōyum paṉittuṟaic civiṟavī cakkuṟun
tuḷiyem maruṅkumōṭi
vāṅkumalai villimār viṇṇuṟu naṉaintavar
vaṉaintiṭu tikamparañ cev
vaṇṇamāc ceyvatac cevvāṉa vaṇṇaroṭu
mañcaḷviḷai yāṭalēyppat
tēṅkumalai yaruvineṭu nīttatu mācuṇat
tiraḷpuṟañ cuṟṟiyīrppac
ciṉavēḻa moṉṟoru cuḻiccuḻalaṉ mantaran
tiraikaṭaṉ matittaṉmāṉum
vīṅkupuṉal vaiyaittaṭantuṟai kuṭaintuputu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭaikkoṭi yavarkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (4)
tuḷikkum paṉittivalai citaṟak kuṭaintāṭu
tuṟaiyiṟ ṟuṟaittamiḻoṭum
toṉmaṟai teḷikkuṅ kalaikkoṭi yeṉuntuṇait
tōḻīmūḻ kippuṉaṉmaṭut
toḷikkum patattumaṟ ṟavaḷeṉa vaṉappēṭai
yōṭip piṭippatammai
yoṇpari purattoṉiyu maṭanaṭaiyum vauviṉa
tuṇarntupiṉ ṟoṭarvatēyppa
neḷikkun taraṅkat taṭaṅkaṅkai yuṭaṉoṭṭi
nittilap pantāṭavum
niraimaṇit tiraḷiṉ kaḻaṅkāṭa vuntaṉ
ṉeṭuttiraik kaiyeṭuttu
viḷikkum peruntaṇ ṭuṟaikkaṭavuḷ vaiyaineṭu
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē
viṭaikkoṭi yavarkoru kayaṟkoṭi koṭuttakoṭi
veḷḷanī rāṭiyaruḷē. (5)
vēṟu
tuṅka mulaippoṟ kuṭaṅkoṇṭu
tūnīrnīnti viḷaiyāṭun
tuṇaiccē ṭiyarkaṇ mēṟpacumpoṟ
cuṇṇa meṟiya varaccēnta
aṅkaṇ vicumpi ṉiṉkuḻaṟkāṭ
ṭaṟukāṟ karumpa reḻuntārppa
taiyaṉ ṟirumē ṉiyalammai
yaruṭkaṭ curumpārt teḻaṉmāṉac
ceṅka ṇiḷaiñar kaḷirkāmat
tīmuṇ ṭiṭakkaṇ ṭiḷamakaḷir
ceḻumeṉ kuḻaṟkūṭ ṭakiṟpukaiyāṟ
ṟiraḷkāyk katali paḻuttunaṟai
poṅku maturaip perumāṭṭi
putunī rāṭi yaruḷukavē
porunait tuṟaivaṉ poṟpāvāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷūkavē. (6)
iḻiyum puṉaṟṟaṇ ṭuṟaimuṉṟi
lituvem perumāṉ maṇcumanta
iṭameṉ ṟalarveṇ kamalappeṇ
ṇicaippak kacintuḷ ḷurukiyiru
viḻiyuñ civappavāṉanta
veḷḷampoḻintu niṉṟaṉaiyāl
mīṇṭum peruka viṭuttavarkōr
vēlai yiṭutaṉ mikaiyaṉṟē
piḻiyu naṟaikkaṟ pakamalarnta
piraca malarpūn tukaṇmūḻkum
piṟaikkōṭ ṭayirā vataṅkūntaṟ
piṭiyō ṭāṭat tēṉaruvi
poḻiyum poḻiṟkū ṭaliṟpolivāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷukavē
porunait tuṟaivaṉ poṟpāvāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷūkavē. (7)
maṟikkun tiraittaṇ puṉalvaiyai
vaṇṭa liṭumaṇ kūṭaikaṭṭi
vāric cumantōrk kammaituṇai
maṇippoṟ kuṭattiṟ karaittūṟṟum
veṟikkuṅ kumaccē ṟekkariṭum
viraippūn tuṟaimaṇ pōlorutti
veṇpiṭ ṭiṭavu maṭittoruvaṉ
vēlai koḷavum vēṇṭumeṉak
kuṟikku miṭattiṟ ṟaṭantūnīr
kuṭaiyap peṟiṉak kaṅkaitiruk
kōṭī rattuk kuṭiyiruppuṅ
kūṭā pōlum polaṉkuvaṭṭup
poṟikkuñ cuṟavak koṭiyuyartatāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷukavē
porunait tuṟaivaṉ poṟpāvāy
putunī rāṭi yaruḷūkavē. (8)
vēṟu
coṟkoṭi yōṭu malarkkoṭi koytu
toṭutta viraittoṭaiyum
cuntari tīṭṭiya cintura mummiru
tuṅkak koṅkaikaḷiṉ
viṟkoṭi kōṭṭiya kuṅkuma muṅkuṭai
veḷḷaṅ koḷḷaikoḷa
veḷiyē kaṇṭuniṉ vaṭivaḻa kaiyaṉ
viḻikku viruntu ceya
viṟkoṭi yōṭu kayaṟkoṭi vīra
ṉeṭutta karuppuvilum
intira taṉuvum vaṇaṅka vaṇaṅku
miṇaippuru vakkoṭicēr
poṟkoṭi yimaya maṭakkoṭi vaiyaip
putunī rāṭukavē
porunait tuṟaiyoṭu kumarit tuṟaiyavaḷ
putunī rāṭukavē. (9)
koḷḷaive ḷaruvi paṭintiṭu mimayak
kūntaṉ maṭappiṭipōl
koṟkait tuṟaiyiṟ ciṟaiviri yappuṉal
kuṭaiyu maṉappeṭaipōl
teḷḷamu takkaṭa ṉaṭuviṟ ṟōṉṟu
ceḻuṅkama lakkuyilpōl
teyvak kaṅkait tiraiyū ṭeḻumoru
cempava ḷakkoṭipōl
kaḷḷaviḻ kōtaiyar kuḻaliṟ kuḻalicai
kaṟṟup poṟṟaruviṟ
kaḷinaṟa vuṇṭa maṭappeṭaiyōṭu
kalantu muyaṅkivarip
puḷḷuṟai pūmpoḻiṉ maturait turaimakaḷ
putunī rāṭukavē
porunait tuṟaiyoṭu kumarit tuṟaiyavaḷ
putunī rāṭukavē. (10)
nīrāṭaṟ paruvam muṟṟiṟṟu

10. ūcaṟ paruvam

oḷḷoḷiya pavaḷak koḻuṅkāṉ micaippoṅku
moḻukoḷiya vayiraviṭṭat
tūṟṟuñ ceḻuntaṇ ṇilākkāl viḻuntaṉaiya
voṇṭaraḷa vaṭamvīkkiyē
aḷḷiṭa vaḻintuceṟ ṟoḷituḷum puṅkiraṇa
varuṇaran nappalakaipuk
kāṭuniṉ ṟōṟṟamap paritimaṇ ṭalamvaḷa
rarumperuñ cuṭaraiyēyppat
teḷḷucuvai yamutaṅ kaṉintavā ṉantat
tiraikaṭaṉ maṭittuḻakkuñ
celvac cerukkarkaṇ maṉakkamala nekkapūñ
cērkkaiyiṟ paḻaiya pāṭaṟ
puḷḷoli yeḻakkuṭi pukuntacun taravalli
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (1)
viṟpoliya nilavupoḻi veṇṇit tilampūṇṭu
viḻutupaṭa maḻakatirviṭum
veṇṭāḷa vūcaliṉ micaippolital puṇṭarika
vīṭṭil polintumaturac
coṟpoli paḻampāṭal collukiṉ ṟavaḷuniṉ
corupameṉ patumiḷanilāt
tūṟṟumati maṇṭalat tamutamā yammainī
tōṉṟukiṉ ṟatumvirippa
eṟpoliya voḻukumuḻu māṇikka maṇimukap
pēṟimaḻai mukiṟavaḻvatav
veṟicuṭark kaṭavuṭiru maṭiyilavaṉ maṭamaka
ḷiruntuviḷai yāṭalēykkum
poṟpuricai maturā purippoli tiruppāvai
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (2)
urukiya pacumpoṉ ṉacumpaveyil vīcupoṉ
ṉūcalai yutaintāṭalum
oṇṭaḷi raṭiccuva ṭuṟappeṟu macōkunaṟa
voḻukumalar pūttutirvatuṉ
ṟirumuṉuru vaṅkaran tentaiyār niṟpatu
terintiṭa namakkituveṉāc
ceñcilaik kaḷvaṉoru vaṉṟoṭai maṭakkātu
terikaṇaikaḷ corivatēyppa
erimaṇi kuyiṉṟapoṟ ceykuṉṟu maḻakati
rerippaveḻu ceñcōtiyū
ṭiḷamati yimaippatuṉ ṟirumukac celvivēṭ
ṭeḻunāt talaittavamavaṉ
purivatu kaṭukkumatu rāpuri maṭakkiḷḷai
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (3)
kaṅkaimuṭi makiḻnartiru vuḷamacain tāṭak
kalantāṭu poṉṉūcalak
kaṭavuṭiru nōkkattu nekkuruki yiṭaniṉ
kaṭaikkaṇōk kattumaṟṟac
ceṅkaṇviṭai yavarmaṉamu mokkak karainturuku
ceykaiyavar cittamē poṟ
ṟiruvūcalāvirun tāṭukiṉ ṟāyeṉuñ
ceytiyai yeṭutturaippa
aṅkaṇeṭu nilamviṭar paṭakkiḻit tōṭuvē
raṭiyiṟ paḻutta palaviṉ
aḷipoṟ cuḷaikkuṭak kaṉiyuṭain tūṟṟatē
ṉaruvipila mēḻumuṭṭip
poṅkivaḻi poḻiṉmatura maturainā yakitirup
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (4)
cērkkuñ cuvaippāṭa lamitoḻuka voḻukupoṟ
ṟiruvūcal pāṭiyāṭac
civapirāṉ ṟirumuṭi yacaippamuṭi mēṟpoṅku
ceṅkaṇā varacakilamvait
tārkkum paṇāṭavi yacaippac carācaramu
macaikiṉṟa tammaṉaiyacain
tāṭalā laṇṭamu makaṇṭapaki raṇṭamu
macaintāṭu kiṉṟa tēyppak
kārkon taḷakkōtai maṭaviyar kuḻaṟkūṭṭu
kamaḻnaṟum pukaiviṇmicaik
kaiparan teḻuvaturu māṟiravi maṇṭalaṅ
kaikkoḷa viruppaṭalavāṉ
pōrkkiṉṟa tokkumatu rāpuri maṭakkiḷḷai
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (5)
tērkkōla moṭuniṉ ṟirukkōla muṅkaṇṭu
cintaṉai puḻuṅku kōpat
tīyaviya mūṇṭeḻuṅ kāmā ṉalaṅkāṉṟa
cikaiyeṉa veḻuntupoṅkun
tārkkōla vēṇiyarta muḷḷameṉa vēpoṟ
ṟaṭañcilaiyu murukiyōṭat
taṇmati muṭittatum veḷviṭaik koṇmaṇi
tarittatum viruttamākak
kārkkōla nīlak karuṅkaḷat tōṭoruvar
ceṅkaḷat tēṟṟalamarak
kaṭkaṇai turakkuṅ karumpuruva villoṭoru
kaiviṟ kuṉittuniṉṟa
pōrkkōla mētiru maṇakkōla māṉapeṇ
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (6)
kuḻiyum pacuṅkaṇ mucukkalai verīicciṟu
kuṟumpalavi ṉeṭiyapārak
kompoṭi paṭattūṅku muṭpuṟak kaṉiyiṉ
kuṭaṅkoṇṭu nīntaṉ maṭaivāy
vaḻiyuṅ koḻuntē ṉuvaṭṭeḻu taṭaṅkāviṉ
vaḷḷukirk karuviraṟkūṉ
mantika ḷirntēkum vicaiyiṉil vicainteḻu
marakkōṭu pāyavayiṟu
kiḻiyuṅ kalaittiṅka ḷamutaruvi tūṅkuva
kiḷaittuvaṇ ṭuḻupaintuḻāyk
kēcavaṉ kālvīca vaṇṭakō ḷakaimukaṭu
kīṇṭuveḷ ḷaruvipoṅkip
poḻiyun tiṟattiṉai nikarkkumatu raittalaivi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (7)
olkuṅ koṭicciṟu maruṅkiṟ kiraṅkimel
lōtivaṇ ṭārnteḻappoṉ
ṉūcalai yutaintāṭu maḷaviṉmalar makaḷammai
yuḷḷaṭik kūṉpiṟaitaḻīi
malkuñ cuvaṭṭiṉai valampurik kīṟṟitukol
vāṇiyeṉ ṟacatiyāṭi
maṇimuṟuval kōṭṭaniṉ vaṇaṅkā muṭikkoru
vaṇakkaneṭu nāṇvaḻaṅkap
pilkuṅ kuṟumpaṉik kūtirk kuṭainteṉap
piracanā ṟaimpāṟkiḷam
pētaiyarka ḷūṭṭum koḻumpukai maṭuttumeṉ
penaṭeyoṭu variccurumpar
pulkun taṭampaṇai yuṭuttamatu raittalaivi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (8)
koṉceyta ceḻumaṇit tiruvūca laramakaḷir
        koṇṭāṭa vāṭuntoṟuṅ
        kuṟumuṟuva ṉeṭunila varuntuñ cakōramāyk
kūntalaṅ kaṟṟai cuṟṟun
teṉceyta maḻalaic curumparāy maṅkainiṉ
        ceṅkaip pacuṅkiḷḷaiyāyt
tēvatē vaṉpoliva ṟevvuruvu māmavaṉ
        ṟiruvuruviṉ muṟaiterippa
miṉceyta cāyalavar mēṟṟalat tāṭiya
        viraippuṉali ṉaruvi kuṭaiyum
veḷḷāṉai kuṅkumac ceñcēṟu nāṟamaṭa
        meṉpiṭiyai yañciniṟkum
poṉceyta māṭamali kūṭaṟ peruñcelvi
        poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
        poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (9)
irupatumu meṉkuraṟ kiṇkiṇiyu muṟaiyiṭ
        ṭiraintiṭu mariccilampum
iṟumiṟu maruṅkeṉ ṟiraṅkumē kalaiyumpo
        ṉeḻutumcem paṭṭu vīkkun
tiruviṭaiyu muṭaitāra mummoṭ ṭiyāṇamuñ
        ceṅkaip pacuṅkiḷḷaiyun
tirumulait taraḷavut tariyamum maṅkalat
        tirunāṇu maḻakoḻukaniṉ
ṟaruḷpoḻiyu matimukamu mukamatiyi ṉeṭunila
        varumpukuṟu nakaiyuñāṉa
āṉanta mākkaṭal kuṭaintukuḻai makaratto
        ṭamarāṭu mōṭarikkaṭ
porukayalum vaṭivaḻaku pūttacun taravalli
        poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē
puḻukuneyc cokkartiru vaḻakiṉuk kottakoṭi
        poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē. (10)
ūcaṟp paruvam muṟṟiṟṟu

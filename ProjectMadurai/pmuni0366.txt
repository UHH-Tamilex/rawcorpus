civamayam

nūlāciriyar vī. turicāmi mutaliyār
putuvik kavarmeṇṭ kalviccālit tamiḻppaṇṭitar.
(tamiḻ makavup pattirāciriyar)
(putuvi aṉantā pirasiṟ patippikkappaṭṭatu)
--------
ipputtakattilaṭaṅkiyavi:
1. nūṉmukam
2. ciṟappukkavikaḷ
3. kaṇṇappar kalampakam.
4. aṉpuṅkaṇṇapparum
5. kaṇṇapparikkuṟitta vēṟu cilapāṭalkaḷ
-------------

1. nūṉmukam.

anātimalamutta citturuvāya civapirāṉatu tiruvaruḷiyaṉpiṉāṉē yaṭintuynta aṭiyārkaṭku
aṭimi ceyvatē, pavakkaṭal kaṭantu muttiyaṅkariyilēṟutaṟ kēṟṟa pātiyāmeṉṟu paṇpuṭip
periyōrkaḷ pakarntuḷḷārkaḷ. itu,
"taliyā ṉaṭantu muttinilañ cārta lokkum pacutaṉmaṅkaliyār karattāṉ civacariti kālā
ṉaṭattām paṟantaṭitaṟoliyē yokkuñ civapūci toṇṭar pūciyālaṭitaṉ malivāyu vēkattāṉ
maṉōvēkattāṉ ñāṉanili". eṉavum,
"poṭikoṇ mēṉiyaṉ pūciyi ṉummavaṉ-aṭiyarpūciyatika matikamē." eṉavum varuvaṉavaṟṟāl
mayalaṟa viḷaṅkumeṉka tampirāṉ ṟōḻarākiya cuntaramūrtti cuvāmikaḷaruḷiya tiruttoṇṭat
tokiyiṉ uṇmik karuttiyu muyttuṇarka.
ittakiya toṇṭar toṇṭuceytuyvāṉ karutiya ciṟu nāyēṉ cintiyiṉ kaṇṇē kaṇṇappanāyaṉār
pātamalariṟ kalantuyvā- meṉṟōreṇṇa miṉitutippa, ataṉāṟ kaṇṇappar kalampakam, kaṇṇappar
patikam eṉṉuntutikaḷi iyaṟṟiyuḷḷēṉ. attutikaḷikkēṭṭa aṉpar cilar kaṇṇappar kalampakatti
acciṭṭu veḷiyiṭavēṇṭumeṉṟu paṉmuṟi paṇikka, avvāṟu ārampittu mutaliṟ kalampakam
acciṭṭāṉapiṉ, atu kaṟpārkku iṉṟiyamiyātu vēṇṭappaṭum "aṉpuṅ kaṇṇapparum" eṉṉum
viṣayamum, kaṇṇapparik kuṟitta vēṟu cila pāṭalkaḷum ippatippil cērttuḷḷēṉ.
ikkalampakattiṟkuk kuṟippuriyoṉ ṟeḻuti yacciṭat toṭaṅkuṅkāl nūṉmuḻutum
virivuriyākavē ceytu acciṭuvatu aṉivarkkum payaṉpaṭumeṉṟu pakarntaṉar palar. avvāṟu
akalavuriyoṉ ṟiṉiyeḻuti yacciṭaṟ keṇṇiyē kuṟippuriyit taṟpōtu acciṭavilli.
iṅṅaṉamāya toṇṭar toṇṭil eṉatu pollāmaṉamum pukumāṟaruḷiya emmiṟivaṉākiya
tirukkāḷattiyapparatu tiruvaruḷip paṉmuṟiyāṉum pāṅkuṟap paravip paḻikeṭumāṟē
vaḻipaṭukiṉṟēṉ.
putuvi
uruttirōṟkāri āṇṭu*       iṅṅaṉam
cittiri mātam*       vī.turicāmi
* āṇṭu, mātam iviyiraṇṭum kuṟiyīṭukaḷāl acciṭappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

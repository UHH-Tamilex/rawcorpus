pu. a. kōvintarāja nāyakar avarkaḷāl pārviyiṭap peṟṟu,
ceṉṉip puraci, pu. a. vēṅkaṭēca nāyakar avarkaḷatu
“māṟaṉ” accukkūṭattiṟ patippikkappaṭṭatu,
rejistar ceytatu, 1927.
itaṉ vili aṇā 3
“māṟaṉ” tamiḻnūṟ patippuk kaḻakattiṉ veḷiyīṭu ne-6.

u
kaṭavuḷ tuṇi

mukavuri.

pala vittuvāṉkaḷ yāṭiya iccīṭṭukkavit tiraṭṭi muṉpu tiruttaṇiki caravaṇap
perumāḷiyaravarkaḷāl veku cuttamāka acciṭṭu veḷivantiruntum iṭiyil pala
piḻikaḷuṭaṉum oṉṟukkoṉ ṟovvāmalum iruntati oruvāṟu piḻikaḷit tiruttiyum,
pataṅkaḷip pirittum, ententa pirapukkaḷiṉ pēril pāṭiṉārkaḷō appirapukkaḷiṉ
nāmatēyaṅkaḷiyum appāṭṭukkaḷiṉ talippil amikkappaṭṭiruppatālum, itu tamiḻ payilum
māṇavarkaḷukku mikavum upayōkamā yirukkumeṉṟu nampukiṟēṉ.
ip puttakakti ivvaṇṇam tirutti acciṭṭu rejistar ceytiruppatāl ivvāṟu acciṭak
kūṭāteṉṟum itaṉāl terivikkiṟēṉ. appaṭi mīṟi acciṭuvarēl kaṉam poruntiya
kavarṉmeṇṭu caṭṭappaṭi cakala naṣṭattiṟku muḷḷāvārkaḷeṉṟu aṟivikkiṟēṉ.
ipputtakattiṟ kāṇum vaḻukkaḷi terintavarkaḷ eṉakkut terivittāl avaṟṟi maṟupatippil
tirutti acciṭuvatōṭu eṉ aṉpārnta vantaṉattipum avarkaḷukkuc celuttak kaṭamip
paṭṭuḷḷēṉ.
puracippākkam,       iṅṅaṉam:
      pu. a. vēṅkaṭēca nāyakar.

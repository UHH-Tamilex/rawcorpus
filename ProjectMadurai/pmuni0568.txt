colliṉ celvar rā. pi. cētuppiḷḷi avarkaḷiṉ
aṇinturi

iṉiya tamiḻp pāṭṭāl uḷḷatti aḷḷum kavittiṟam vāyntavar tiru. vaḷḷiyappā. piñcu
maṉattiṟku ukanta ceñcoṟ kaviñar avar.
'malarum uḷḷam’ eṉṉum innūl oru kavitip pūñcōli. aṅkē arumpu malarntu viriyum;
aruṇaṉ katiric coriyum. ciṉṉañ ciṟu kuruvi paṟakkum; peṉṉam periya yāṉi naṭakkum.
pattumātak kuḻanti tattit tavaḻum, pañcukkāl pūṉi piyac ceṉṟu pālik kuṭikkum;
puttar aṟamuṟippār; pāratiyār pāṭṭicippār. kaṇṇaṉum kāntiyum kāṭci taruvar.
accōliyil nalla malarkaḷ naṟumaṇaṅ kamaḻum.

eṉṟu kēṭkiṉṟār kaviñar. kaṉakāmparam cūṭi makiḻum kaṉṉi itaṟku eṉṉa māṟṟam
urippāḷō?
uyarnta uṇmikaḷip piḷḷiyuḷḷattiṟ kēṟpap paṭippaṭiyāka uṇarttukiṉṟār ikkaviñar.
tamiḻk kavitiyilē aṉpiṉ taṉmi parakkap pēcappaṭukiṉṟatu. tiruvaḷḷuvar mutalāya talimik
kaviñarkaḷ ‘ulaka vāḻviṟku aṉpu iṉṟiyamiyātatu eṉṉum uṇmiyip palavāṟākap pāṭi
puḷḷaṉar. ivvuṇmiyik kuḻantikaḷukku ēṟṟa muṟiyil kūṟukiṉṟār ikkaviñar. piḷḷikaḷ
kaṇṭu aṟintuḷḷa nakikaḷiyum, malarkaḷiyum oṉṟaṉpiṉ oṉṟāy niṉivūṭṭi iṟutiyāka,
aṉpiṉ nalaṅkaḷik kūṟukiṉṟār, kaviñar.

innāḷil ulaka makkaḷi alittuk kulittu allaṟ paṭuttum pōr mutaliya pīṭikaḷ oḻintu,
amiti nilava vēṇṭumāyiṉ aṉpu eṉṉum makkaṭ paṇpu immānilattil perukavēṇṭum eṉṟu
ācip paṭukiṉṟār kaviñar. ‘aṉpu perukumāyiṉ aruḷ piṟakkum; aruḷ piṟakkumāyiṉ
antamillāta iṉpanili vanteytum' eṉṟu āṉṟōr aruḷiya ariya uṇmiyip piḷḷikaḷ nilik
kēṟpap pēcukiṉṟār kaviñar.
ciṟanta nītikaḷik kati vaṭivāka uṇarttutalē ciṟuvarkkēṟṟa neṟiyākum. 'nallavaṉ eṉṟu
peyar eṭuppataṟku teṭunāḷ cellum; keṭṭavaṉ eṉṟa peyari caṭṭeṉṟu eṭuttuviṭalām eṉṟa
uṇmiyi māmpaḻak katiyil vittuc cuvipaṭa viḷakkukiṉṟār kaviñar. ivvāṟē 'ulaka
vāḻvil ēṟṟamaṭital aritu; iḻivaṭital eḷitu’ eṉṉum ciṟanta uṇmi maliyum vāḻvum
eṉṟa kavitiyil aḻakuṟak kāṭṭappaṭukiṉṟatu.
arumiyāṉa tamiḻc coṟkaḷiyum eḷimiyāka eṭutatāḷum tiṟam ikkaviñariṭam uṇṭu.

eṉṟa pāṭṭilē ‘kalaṅkari viḷakku' eṉṉum paḻaṅkālac collip paḻaku tamiḻākkiviṭṭār
kaviñar.
paḻamiyum putumiyum ivar kavitikaḷilē kalantu viḷiyāṭum.
vaṭṭa māṉa taṭṭu. taṭṭu niṟiya laṭṭu. eṉṟu pāṭukiṉṟār kaviñar. paḻiya taṭṭilē putiya
laṭṭik kāṇṭatu ōr āṉantamaṉṟō?
ittakiya napañcāṉṟa kavitiyi uḷḷam malarntu utaviya kaviñar vaḷḷiyappā avarkaḷukkut
tamiḻakattār naṉṟi eṉṟu uriyatākum.
ceṉṉi, 2-3-1954       - rā.pi. cētuppiḷḷi

āciriyar muṉṉuri

eṉṟa makākavi pāratiyāriṉ pāṭaliyum,

eṉṟa kavimaṇi tēcikavināyakam piḷḷiyavarkaḷiṉ pāṭaliyum nāṉ ciṉṉañciṟu kuḻantiyāka
iruntapōtu pāṭippāṭip paravacamaṭintēṉ; caṟṟup periyavaṉāṉatum maṟṟak kuḻantikaḷ
appāṭalkaḷi ārvamuṭaṉ pāṭuvatik kēṭṭuk kēṭṭu makiḻcciyaṭintēṉ.
ak kaviñarkaḷ eḻutiya pāṭalkaḷip paṭippatilum, kuḻantikaḷ avaṟṟip pāṭak kēṭpatilum
iṉpam peṟṟuvanta eṉakku, contamākac cila pāṭalkaḷ eḻutavēṇṭum eṉṟa āval eḻuntatu. anta
āvalil oru cila pāṭalkaḷi eḻutiṉēṉ. avaṟṟi eṉ naṇparkaḷiṭam kāṭṭiṉēṉ. avarkaḷ eṉ
muyaṟciyip pārāṭṭiṉar. avarkaḷiṉ pārāṭṭurikaḷ eṉakku ūkkamaḷittaṉa; uṟcāka mūṭṭiṉa.
anta ūkkattil, uṟcākattil nāṉ toṭarntu kuḻantip pāṭalkaḷ palavaṟṟi eḻutiṉēṉ. eḷiya
naṭiyil, iṉiya cantattil kavimaṇiyavarkaḷ iyaṟṟiya pala pāṭalkaḷiyum pārttup pārttup
paṭittup paṭittu avarkaḷ vaḻiyip piṉpaṟṟa vēṇṭum eṉṟa āciyuṭaṉē eḻutivantēṉ.
mutal mutalāka - atāvatu pattu āṇṭukaḷukku muṉpu malarum uḷḷam eṉṟa itē peyaril nāṉ
eḻutiya irupattu mūṉṟu pāṭalkaḷ koṇṭa oru puttam veḷivantatu. ataṟkup paḷḷi
āciriyarkaḷum, pattirikāciriyarkaḷum, peṟṟōrkaḷum aḷitta varavēṟpum ātaravum eṉ
muyaṟcikkut tuṇṭukōlāyiṉa.
mēṉmēlum toṭarntu eḻutiṉēṉ. kuḻantikaḷukkāka veḷivanta-veḷivarukiṉṟa pala
pattirikikaḷilum avi iṭam peṟṟaṉa. appāṭalkaḷi avvappōtu ciṟu ciṟu puttakaṅkaḷākap
putukkōṭṭit tamiḻ niliyattārum, irāyavaram pāppā malar kāriyālayattārum veḷiyiṭṭu
vantaṉar.
ciṟuciṟu puttakaṅkaḷāka iruppatu eṉ naṇparkaḷil cilarukkup piṭikkavilli.
ellāvaṟṟiyum cērttu oru puttakamāka veḷiyiṭṭāl naṉṟāyirukkumē eṉṟu yōcaṉi
kūṟiṉar. eṉakkum appaṭic ceyyavēṇṭum eṉṟa āci muṉpē iruntatu. naṇparkaḷiṉ
yōcaṉiyik kēṭṭapiṟaku anta āci nāḷukku nāḷ naṉku vaḷarntukoṇṭē vantatu. ataṉ
viḷivutāṉ ip puttakam!
eṉ pāṭalkaḷ puttaka uruvil varuvataṟkup pērutavi purintavarkaḷil mukkiyamāṉavar mūvar.
T. aiyaṉperumāḷ kōṉār avarkaḷ (Senior Lecturer in Tamil, St. Joseph's
College, Trichy), ku. maturi mutaliyār (Ex-Lecturer in Tamil, Loyola
College, Madras), vittuvāṉ taṇiki ulakanātaṉ avarkaḷ (Hindu Theological High
School, Madras) - im mūvarukkum naṉṟi.
kavimaṇiyavarkaḷiṉ vāḻttum, colliṉ celvariṉ aṇinturiyum ipputtakattiṟkuk
kiṭittatē nāṉ peṟṟa perum pēṟākum. avarkaḷiṉ aṉpukku vaṇakkam celuttukiṟēṉ.
ipputtakam nalla muṟiyil veḷivarap perumuyaṟci eṭuttukkoṇṭa eṉ iṉiya naṇpar ratṉam
avarkaḷukkum, neṟkuppi irāma cuppiramaṇiyaṉ avarkaḷukkum eṉ naṉṟi.
it tokutiyiluḷḷa pāṭalkaḷik kuḻantikaḷ paṭittu iṉpuṟṟāl atuvē nāṉ aṭiyum
pēriṉpamākum. cumār paṉṉiraṇṭu āṇṭukaḷāka nāṉ ceytuvarum muyaṟci oraḷavāvatu payaṉ
peṟṟateṉa makiḻcciyaṭivēṉ.
kuḻantikaḷ iṉpamē eṉatu iṉpam, avarkaḷukkut toṇṭu ceyvatē eṉ mukkiya kuṟikkōḷ.
ceṉṉi, 4-3-1954       -aḻa. vaḷḷiyappā

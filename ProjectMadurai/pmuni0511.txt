       maturait tamiḻc caṅkam
       THE MADURA TAMIL SANGAM

       srī:
       tirumāliruñcōlaimalai aḻakar piḷḷaittamiḻ
       mukavurai
       amiḻtiṉumiṉiya tamiḻmoḻiyakattē, aṉpum, iṉpum, aṟaṉum, maṟaṉum
       cāṉṟa akattiṇai puṟattiṇai taḻuviya tuṟaivakaikaḷil toṉṟutoṭṭa
       vaḻakkāyuḷḷa paṉuvalkaḷ ettuṇaiyō palavuḷḷaṉa; avaṟṟuḷ
       'piḷḷaittamiḻ' eṉṉum pirapantavakaiyumoṉṟu.*
       -------------
       *iṅkukkūṟiya kāmappakutiyāvatu, peṟalarum piḷḷaiyaip peṟṟatāy
       mutaliyōr pārāṭṭutaṟkuriya 'kuṟukuṟu naṭantu ciṟukainīṭaṭi,
       iṭṭuntoṭṭuṅ kauviyuntuḻantu neyyuṭaiyaṭiciṉmeypeṟa vitirttum,
       viḷaiyāṭutaṉ mutaliya ceyalkaḷai aṉupavikkumavariṭait tōṉṟum
       orutalaiyiṉpam. itaṉai 'makkaṇ mey tīṇṭaluṭaṟkiṉpam maṟṟu avar
       coṟkēṭṭaliṉpam cevikku, eṉṟaṟṟoṭakkat tāṉumaṟika. itaṉālavar
       viḷaiyāṭṭukkāṇṭalum kaṭkiṉpameṉpatu pōtarum.
       'piḷḷaittamiḻ'eṉpatu, peṟalaruñ ciṟappuvāynta makkaṭ kuḻaviyaip
       pārāṭṭippāṭum iṉiyapāṭalkaḷālākiya pirapantam eṉṟu poruḷpaṭum. iṅku
       'tamiḻ' eṉṉuñ col pirapantattai yuṇarttumeṉpatai, innūlāciriyar tam
       ñāṉāciriyar vaṇakkattuḷ, 'vētappāṭṭiṟṟarun tamiḻ' eṉṟu
       tivyappirapantaṅkaḷai vaḻaṅkutalālum, iyalicaināṭaka nūlkaḷai iyaṟ
       ṟamiḻ, icait tamiḻ, nāṭakattamiḻ eṉavum, muttamiḻ eṉavum
       vaḻaṅkutalālumaṟika.
       ippirapantam, puṟapporuḷ vakaiyākiya pāṭāṇ tiṇaiyil
       'kuḻavimaruṅkiṉuṅ kiḻavatākum' eṉṟa tolkāppiyac cūttirattāṟ
       koḷḷappaṭṭa *kāmap pakutiyiṉ pāṟpāṭṭu makkaṭ kuḻavikkurittāka
       vaḻaṅkappaṭumāyiṉum, orōvaḻi, teyvat tōṟṟamākiya makkaṭ kuḻaviyiṉ
       paruvattai ārōpittalāl, akkuḻaviyō ṭoṟṟumaiyuṭaiya teyvattukkum
       uriyatāka vaḻaṅkappaṭum. iccūttirattil, kiḷappatākum eṉṉātu
       'kiḻavatākum' eṉṟa kuṟippāl kuḻavipparuvaṅkaḻinta mutiyarai avaratu
       kuḻavipparuvampaṟṟip pārāṭṭippāṭiṉum, appāṭṭil,
       ammutiyarō-ṭoṟṟumaiyuṭaiya akkuḻavikku urimaiyuṭaimaiyāl atu
       vaḻuvākāteṉṟu koḷḷappaṭum.
       ippaṉuvalaip paṉṉirupāṭṭiyaluṭaiyār piḷḷaippāṭṭeṉa vaḻaṅki,
       ilakkaṇam pala tara miyampiyum, āṉṟōr kūṟiya cila ilakkaṇaṅkaḷai
       yeṭuttukkāṭṭiyum pōntaṉar; veṇpāmālaiyuṭaiyār (* 'iḷamaintar
       nalamvēṭṭa vaḷamaṅkaiyar vakaiyuraittal' eṉṉum kuḻavikkaṭṭōṉṟiya
       kāmappakutiyiṉ pāṟpaṭuppar.
       -----------
       *(iḷamaintar nalam vēṭṭa vaḷamaṅkaiyar vakaiyuraittalāvatu)
       kaṇṇapirāṉatu iḷamaipparuva viḷaiyāṭṭiṉpattai virumpiya
       yacōtaippirāṭṭiyum iṭaippeṇkaḷum pārāṭṭiyapaṭiyaip periyāḻvār
       aṉukarittal pōlvatu.
       'vaḻakkoṭu civaṇiyavakaimaiyāṉa' eṉṟa tolkāppiyac cūttirattuḷ,
       'cāṉṟōr ceyta pulaṉeṟivaḻakkōṭē porunti vanta pakutikkaṇṇēyāṉa
       poruḷkaḷuḷ' kuḻavimaruṅkiṉuṅ kiḻavatākiya piḷḷaippāṭṭup
       poruḷkaḷaiyum akappaṭuttuk kūṟiyiruttalāl ippirapantavakai
       tolkāpiyarkālattukku muṟpaṭṭa cāṉṟōrāṟ ceytupōnta
       paḻaiyavaḻakkuṭaiyateṉa viḷaṅkukiṉṟatu. itaṟkutāraṇamākap
       periyāḻvār tirumoḻiyuṭ palavēṟu poruḷ vakaikaḷālum piḷḷaippāṭṭup
       pāṭap peṟṟiruppatu kāṇattakum. ataṉkaṇ, tāl, cappāṇi, ceṅkīrai
       mutaliyavaṟṟuṭaṉ ikkālap piḷḷaittamiḻi lillāta piṟappiṉ uvakai,
       pātātikēcakkāṭci, taḷar naṭai, accōvaccō, puṟampulkal
       appūccikāṭṭal, nīrāṭṭal, pūccūṭal, kāppiṭal, ammamūṭṭal, mutaliya
       palavēṟu poruḷ paṟṟiya pārāṭṭalkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.
       iṅṅaṉam pala poruḷkaḷiruppavum, oruporuḷpaṟṟip pārāṭṭum pāṭṭup
       taṉittaṉi patikamākavum, pirapantamuḻutum catakamākavum
       ōraḷavuṭaiyatāka muṭikka vēṇṭik kāppumutaṟ ciṟutēriṟutiyāṉa
       pattupporuḷkaḷai ippirapantattuk-kuriyaṉavākap piṟkālattār
       terinteṭuttu niyamittup pōntaṉarpōlum.
       iṅṅaṉam paḻamaiyum arumaiyum vāynta pirapantavakaiyiṟ cērnta
       piḷḷaittamiḻkaḷiṟ ciṟantavaṟṟuḷ ittirumāliruñ cōlaimalai aḻakar
       piḷḷait tamiḻum oṉṟeṉṟeṇṇat takkatu.
       ivvaḻakar piḷḷaittamiḻ, kāppumutaṟ ciṟutēriṟutiyākap pattup paruvap
       pārāṭṭalaiyu muṭaiyatāyp palavēṟu cantappāṭalkaḷāṟ ciṟantatu.
       kāviyaṅkaṟpārkku, ilakkiya vilakkaṇa vaḻakku vakai
       palavumeḷituṉuṇarttic coṟporuḷuṇarcciyait tiṭpamuṟac ceyayun
       tiṟamaiyuṭaiyatu; ceyyuṭ ceyvārkku viṭayamillāviṭattum poruttamuḷḷa
       vicēṭaṇaṅkaḷai varuttamiṉṟit toṭuttup poruḷai vicēṭittup
       palapaṭiyākac ceyyuḷai yaḻakupeṟacceytu
       muṭikkumāṟṟalaiyaḷikkavallatu, aintiṇai mayakkam, nāṉilavaruṇaṉai,
       kaṟpaṉai, alaṅkāramutaliya palanayaṅkaḷamaintatu.
       tirumāliṉavatāramāka vanta kaṇṇapirāṉatu kuḻavipparuvattai
       oṟṟumaipaṟṟi aḻakar mēlvaittu, akkaṇṇapirāṉatu iḷamainalamvēṭṭa
       yacōtaip pirāṭṭiyumiṭaip peṇkaḷumākiya "vaḷamaṅkaiyar" paṭiyai
       attiyavacittu mikavum pārāṭṭip pāṭappeṟṟuḷḷatu.
       ippirapantattiṉ pāyiracceyyuḷil 'vaḷḷaittamiḻkūr vēmpattūr
       varumāṇpulavōrvakuttatu' eṉṟu collappaṭṭiruttalāl
       itaṉaiyiyaṟṟiyavar piṟantavūr vēmpattūr eṉa veḷiyākiṟatu.
       ivvūriṉkaṭ ṭoṉṟutoṭṭē ācu, matura, citra, vistāra kavikaḷum,
       pirapantaṅkaḷu miyaṟṟippōnta palapulavarkaḷiruntu
       vantirukkiṉṟaṉareṉpatai, tiruvālavāyuṭaiyār tiruviḷaiyāṭaṟ purāṇap
       patippiṉ mukavuraiyil mahāmahōpāttiyāya pirummasrī ve.
       cāminātaiyaravarkaḷeḻuti-yiruppatāl aṟiyalām. ippāyiracceyyuḷil
       "pulavōrvakuttatu" eṉṟa paṉmaikkiṇaṅka vēmpattūrc caṅkappulavar
       palarkūṭi ippirapantattai yiyaṟṟiṉareṉṟu cilar ōr aitikam colvatu
       muṇṭu.
       paḻicciṉarpparavaliṉ paṉṉiraṇṭām ceyyuḷilē "pēcupaya vētāntatēcikaṉ
       ṟāṭoḻuval pēraḻakaṉūṟaḻaiyalē" eṉṟu toḻuvaleṉṉum orumaiyāl
       aḻakaṉūlākiya ippirapantamuḻutum taḻaitaṟku
       maṅkaḷaṅkūṟiyiruttalālum maṟṟum cila pāṭalkaḷilum
       maṅkaḷaṅkūṟiyaviṭaṅkaḷilellām ivar ippirapantattai "eṉkavi" eṉṟu
       tāmē kūṟiyiruttalālum, ippirapantam orēpulavarāṟ pāṭap
       peṟṟirukkalāmeṉṟu tōṉṟukiṉṟatu.
       itaṟkaṉukuṇamākavē cētucamastāṉavitvāṉ srīmat rā.
       rākavaiyaṅkāravarkaḷ aritiṉ ārāycciceyteḻutic "centamiḻil"
       veḷiyiṭṭa cētunāṭum tamiḻum* eṉṟa viyācattil innūliyaṟṟiyavaratu
       peyar, cāmikavikāḷaruttirar eṉṟu eḻutiyuḷḷārkaḷ. kavikāḷarutrar
       eṉpatu piṟarāl vellappaṭāta āṟṟalpaṟṟi vaḻaṅkum "kavirākṣasaṉ"
       eṉpatupōla ivaratu kavittiṟamaipaṟṟip piṉpu vanta
       ciṟappuppeyarāyirukka vēṇṭumātalāl, appeyarkkumuṟpaṭṭu "māntarak
       koṅkēṉāki" eṉpaḻippōlap paṇputtokai nilaimoḻiyāyniṉṟa cāmi eṉṉum
       peyarē ivaratu iyaṟpeyarāyirukka vēṇṭumeṉṟu tōṉṟukiṉṟatu.
       paḻicciṉārpparavaliṉ patiṉāṉkāmpāṭṭil ivar tām aḻakar kōviṟ
       purōkita nirvākam peṟṟirunta tōḻappar eṉṉum srīvaiṣaṇavarāl
       "toṇṭarkuḻuvākiya attiyāpakakōṣṭiyiṟ cērkkappaṭṭavareṉṟu
       kūṟiyiruttalāl, ivar pañcacamaskārampeṟṟut tivyap pirapantaṅkaḷōtic
       cāttiṉavareṉpatum "vētappāṭṭiṟṟarun
       tamiḻumirunāleḻuttum"......"alaṅkārar paṭivum eṉṉeñcakattuḷnāṭṭi"
       eṉṟamaiyāl pakavatvipātikirantaeṅkaḷum, tirumantrārtta
       viyākyāṉamum, arttapañcakāti rahasyaṅkaḷum tōḻappariṭam
       atikarittavareṉpatum "naṟṟamiḻccīrpatippōṉ " eṉṟataṉāl ariya
       tamiḻilakkiya vilakkaṇaṅkaḷaiyum attōḻappariṭamēkēṭṭu neñciṟ
       patiyak koṇṭavareṉpatum viḷaṅkukiṉṟaṉa. itaṉāl ivaratukālam
       iṟṟaikkuc caṟṟēṟattāḻa nūṟṟaimpatuvaruṭaṅkaṟkumuṉpu tirumālai
       yāṇṭār cantatiyāruḷ oruvarkku mātularāyvantu aḻakar kōviṟpurōhita
       nirvāham peṟṟirunta tōḻapparkāla meṉṟaṟiyattakkatu.
       ivar vētāntatēcikaraiyum maṇavāḷamāmuṉikaḷaiyum vaḻipaṭum
       pācuraṅkaḷāl vaṭakalaiteṉkalaiyeṉṉu mupayavētāntaṅkaḷukkum muṟaiyē
       piravarttarkaḷāṉa avviruvaraiyum vaḻipāṭupuriyum teṉkalai
       vaiṣṇavareṉa aṟiyalām.
       -------------
       *centamiḻ tokuti 13 pakuti 2 pakkam 51.
       innūliluḷḷa palapāṭalkaḷaiyum pārkkumpōtu, caṅkanūlmutaliya paḻaiya
       tamiḻnūṟ payiṟciyiṟ ṟērcciyuṟṟa perumpulavareṉpatum, palarum
       collāta vaṇṇac cantaṅkaḷaik kaṟpittukkoṇṭu colliṉpamum poruḷiṉpamum
       cuvaiyum alaṅkāramu milaṅkak kauṭaviruttiyilum kavikaḷiyaṟṟu
       māṟṟaluṭaiyavareṉpatum viḷaṅkum.
       ivar vaiṣṇavattil mikka ūṟṟamuṭaiyarāyiruppiṉum kāppup
       paruvappārāṭṭiṟ paravutaṟkuriyareṉa vitikkappaṭṭa civaperumāṉ,
       vināyakar, murukavēḷ mutaliyōraiyum naṉku paravutalāl
       vitimuṟaitavaṟātoḻuku miyalpuṭaiyavareṉpatum viḷaṅkum.
       innūl cilapattāṇṭukaḷukku muṉṉarē oruvāṟacciṭap paṭṭiruppiṉum
       ippoḻutu accupputtakam eṅkum kiṭaikkāmaiyāl itaṉai acciṭṭāṟ kāviyaṅ
       kaṟpārpalarkkum mikappayaṉpaṭumeṉṟu karuti maturait
       tamiḻccaṅkattilirunta iraṇṭu kuṟaippiratikaḷaiyām ōraccup
       puttakattaiyum vaittup paricōtittuc "centamiḻ" vāyilāka
       veḷiyiṭalāyiṟṟu.
       innūṟ paricōtaṉaikkukkiṭaitta iraṇṭoru piratikaḷum
       piḻaimalintiruntamaiyāl avaṟṟuḷ terintavaṟṟait tiruttit
       teriyātavaṟṟai yiruntapaṭiyē vaittup patippikkalāyiṟṟu. ātalāṟ
       centamiḻppayiṟciyiṟ ṟērcciyaṭainta periyōritaṉaik kaṇṇuṟṟu,
       cuttappiratikoṇṭu, tiruttavēṇṭumavaṟṟait tirutti eṉatu tavaṟṟaip
       poṟuttaruḷa vēṇṭukiṟēṉ.
       itaṉai acciṭutaṟkut tamiḻccaṅkak kalācālai yupāttiyāyar
       nallacivaṉpiḷḷai pirati eṭuttukkoṭuttum, accuc cērkkaiyai oppu
       nōkkit tiruttiyum utavipurintatu pārāṭṭaṟpālatu.
       tiru. nārāyaṇaiyaṅkār.

                     nāṉkāvatu - cappāṇipparuvam
       orumuṟaiyuṉatu vayiṟṟumalarkku ḷuttavaṉappākaṉ
       umaimaṭamayilai yiṭattilirutti yirukkumvalappākaṉ
       uṭaliyalavuṇa ruṭaṟkaṟaikakka vaḻuttumukirccēṉam
       utatiyiṉaṭuvu tuyiṟcuvaimuṟṟiya vaṭṭavaṇaiccēṭaṉ
       iruḷpuṟamiriya niraikkatirviṭṭa valakkaṇveyiṟpāṉu
       itaḻpotikumuta malarttumiṭakka ṇoḻukkamutappāṉu
       eḻututalariya maṟaittamiḻmuṟṟa vaṭittavicaippāṇaṉ
       iḻuteḻumuṭaikamaḻ koccaiyiṭaicciyar peṟṟakulappūvai
       murukuṭaikamala malarttavicucci yirukkumaṉaittīpam
       mukiṉmutukiṭaṟu kaṭaṟṟalaivaṭṭa maḷikkumvetirkkōlaṉ
       mukainekiḻtoṭaiyaṉ muṭittutaṟittaru coṟputuvaikkō
       mutukiṭaviputar miṭaṟṟumuḻakku kuraṟkaṭavuṭkōṭu
       kurukulanirupa ramarkkaṇiruṭṭai yaḻaittapakaṭṭāḻi
       kuraikaḻalaṭiyai vaḻuttiyuḷattiṉ makiḻcciyuṟappāṭu
       kuṉitiraiyeṟiyaru vikkulaveṟpiṟai koṭṭukacappāṇi
       kuvalayamveḷiyaṟa vaittapatappuyal koṭṭukacappāṇi.         (1)
       viḻituyilpeṟamaṇi neṟṟikuyiṟṟiya toṭṭilalaittēmum
       viraikeḻupuḻukuney pottimukattalai pittaimuṭittēmum
       eḻukatirviḻupoḻu tuppilaicuṟṟi yaṉaṟṟalaiyiṭṭēmum
       iḷanilavumaḻmati cuṭṭumuṉakketir muṟṟamaḻaittēmum
       aḻukuraṟaṇiya maruṭṭiyaṇaittoci yokkalaivaittēmum
       aṭitoḻumimaiyavar varkkamumoppa vuvappamuṭattāḻai
       koḻumaṭaṉakavaḷi pukkuḻuveṟpiṟai koṭṭukacappāṇi
       kuvalayamveḷiyaṟa vaittapatappuyal koṭṭukacappāṇi.         (2)
       tiruvayiṟamaivara vaittaruḷvaitta vaḷappiluyirppāla
       tiraiviripuṉalveḷi pottuvaṭattilai muṟṟutuyiṟpāla
       varicilaikaṭaikuḻai yakkaṭalcuṭṭa viḻikkaṭaiyuṭkōpa
       maḻaimukileṟituḷi taṭṭumalaikkuṭai yiṭṭaniraikkōpa
       murukeḻumiḷanaṟai kakkupacuttapu ṉattuḷapattāma
       muṭaikamaḻiṭaimakaḷ kaṭṭavoṭukkiya ciṟṟutarattarma
       kurukukaṭalaiyaru vittuyilveṟpiṟai koṭṭukacappāṇi
       kuḷirmatitavaḻpoḻil kaṟṟiyaveṟpiṟai koṭṭukacappāṇi         (3)
       tirumakaṭaḻuviya paccaiyuṭaṟpuṟa muttaveyarppēṟat
       tiraitarumamuteṉa luṟṟitaḻiṟṟuḷi cuṟṟutarattārak
       karukiyacurikuḻa luccimuṭippiṇi viṭṭumukattāṭak
       katiriḷaveyileḻu muccimilaicciya cuṭṭinutaṟṟāḻap
       perukiḷanilavumiḻ kottuvaḷaikkula miṭṭapuyattālap

       kuraimatuvoḻuku toṭaittuḷapappuyal koṭṭukacappāṇi
       kuḷirmatitavaḻpoḻil cuṟṟiyaveṟpiṟai koṭṭukacappāṇi.         (4)

       tīṇṭi yaṟiyē ṉeṉṟupoyyē
       tēvar teḷiya muṉṉaiyuyir
       muḷaittut taḻaippak karikkaṭṭai
       mutuku mitittāy ciṟṟaṭiyēm
       muṉṟiṟ puṟatti letuteḷiya
       muḷarit tāḷāṉ mitippatutēṉ
       vaḷaittuc cuḻikkun tuḻāyppuyalyām
       vakutkuñ ciṟṟil citaiyēlē
       vāṉam piḷakkuñ cōlaimalai
       maṉṉaṉ ciṟṟil citaiyēlē.         (4)
       alampāy viḷaineṟ paḻaṉattil
       aḷḷaṉ maṭuviṟ ṟuḷḷukayal
       āṭun taraṅkak karuṅkaḻiyil
       aṟaiyuñ ciṟakarp pēṭṭekiṉak
       kulampāy kuvaḷaik kōṭṭakattiṟ
       kuṇṭu cuṉaiyiṟ ṟoṭṭaneṭuṅ
       kuḷattiṟ kuṟuntāṭ karumētik
       kuḻiyi luṉatu pēryāṇarc
       cilampāṟ ṟayali lāyiranīrt
       teyvat taṭatti ṉaṉṟimuṉṟiṟ
       ṟeḷḷip parappu maṇaṟkuvaiyiṟ
       cekkark kamala malarātu
       valampāy tikiri mukiṉmittu
       vaḷaikkuñ ciṟṟil citaiyēlē
       vāri makaṭku nilamakaṭkum
       maṉṉaṉ ciṟṟil citaiyalē         (5)

       kōvai maṇippan teṟintāṭum
       koṭipoṟ kaḻaṅku paṉalvāvi
       koṭkuṅ kiraṇac ceykuṉṟu
       koypūn taḷavap pantalvaṇṭaṟ
       pāvai yukaikkum poṉṉūcal
       parāraik kuravan tēṉkoḻukkum
       paṭappai kutikku māṉkaṉṟu
       pacumpoṟ kiḷḷaip piḷḷaiyiḷam
       pūvai yivaiyuṉ patattūḷi
       potiyap peruvī ṭuṟilaṭiyēm
       poyta loḻiyu malarvaṉacap
       pokuṭṭi lirukku nāṉkumukat
       tēvait tiravun tiyilaḷitta
       ciṟuvaṉ ciṟṟil citaiyēlē
       teḷḷun taraṅkap pālāḻic
       cērppaṉ ciṟṟil citaiyēlē.         (6)
       vēṟu
       koḷḷainīrk kuvaḷaik kōṭṭaka maṟantum
       kuḻantaiveṇ matikkōṭṭuk
       kuṉṟoṭuṅ kuyilpōṟ kūvutaṉ maṟantum
       kūruki riḷaṅkiḷḷaip
       piḷḷaiyai maṟantu mayiṉaṭa maṟantum
       pēṭṭiḷam puṟavōṭum
       piṇaiyiṉai maṟantum ūcalai maṟantum
       piḷḷaivaṇ ṭitaḻuntak
       kaḷḷaivā yoḻukkum veḷḷiveṇ ṇilavu
       kāṟṟiḷa mukaimullaik
       kaṉṟiṉai maṟantu miḻaittayām varuntak
       kaṉṉiyar maṉaivāyiṉ
       vaḷaḷaiyā laṉaṅkaṇ ṭuyiṉmalaik kiṟaivaṉ
       maṇaṟciṟṟil citaiyēlē
       vaṇṭamar tuḷavaṉ mālalaṅkāraṉ
       maṇaṟciṟṟil citaiyēlē         (7)
       cuḻitteṟi taraṅkat tiruccilam pāṟṟut
       tūṟṟiya tirai yīṭṭum
       corinilāk kiḷaikkum veṇmaṇaṟ kuppaic
       cuṭpolaṉ muṟattiṭṭuk
       koḻittuveṇ ṭukiliṟ ṟalaimaṭik koṭṭik
       kuvittatō ḻiyarōṭum
       kurumaṇi muṉṟiṟ parappimey tuvaḷak
       kōlvaḷaik kuralēṅkak
       kaḻittaṭaṅ kamalat tuṇaikkarañ cēppak
       kalaimati mukamvērppak
       kaṇṇimaip puṟāma liḻaittayām varuntak
       kaṭaivaḷai yalampāyum
       vaḻittalai malarttēṉ corimalaik kiṟaivaṉ
       maṇaṟciṟṟil citaiyēlē
       māliruñ cōlai malaiyalaṅ kāraṉ
       maṇaṟciṟṟil citaiyēlē         (8)
       vēṟu
       variyuṅ kaṭuṅkār mukaṅkaṇṭu
       vaḷarkkum piṇaikkaṉ ṟakalumatu
       maṭaṟpūn tuḷapat tirumēṉi
       maḻaikkār kāṭṭak kuyiṟpiḷḷai
       iriyum vaḷvāy neṭuntikiri
       eṟikkum veyilai mutuvēṉil
       eṉṟu kalāva muḷḷoṭukki
       iḷaiya mayiṟpē ṭemmaiviṭṭup
       piriyun tavaḷat taraḷamaṇi
       piṟaḻu nilavup peruveḷḷam
       peruku muṉṟiṟ ṟalaivantu
       peyyum pacumpo ṉoḷiparantu
       coriyuṅ kaḻaṟkāṟ ṟuṇaikkamalam
       tuvaḷac ciṟṟil citaiyēlē
       cōlai malaikku nāṉmaṟaikkum
       tōṉṟal ciṟṟil citaiyēlē.         9
       oṭuṅ kaluḻi viṭappāntaḷ
       uṭalaṅ kiḻiyat tūṇṭucaka
       ṭucci mitippap pērarakkaṉ
       uruva meṭutta teḻuntupukai
       āṭun taḻaṉmeyk karikkaṭṭai
       akala mitippak kurukulattil
       araca riḷaṅkō vāṉatatar
       aruṅkāṉ paṭarkaṟ ṟalaimitippat
       tōṭuñ curumpun tatuma*pukuḻaṟ
       ṟōkai yuruva meṭuttatoḷi
       coriyun tavaḷat tirumuṉṟiṟ
       ṟuvaikkiṉ muṉṉai vaṭiviḻakkum
       pāṭu maṟaināṉ kuṭuttapatam
       patittuc ciṟṟil citaiyēlē
       pārttaṉ ṟērkkum puḷḷiṟkum
       pākaṉ ciṟṟil citaiyēlē.         10
       muṭaṅkāp puraṇṭu nimirurakaṉ
       muṭiyiṟ kiṭakkum puviyēḻum
       munnī rēḻum varaiyēḻum
       muḻaṅku maruvip perukkeṭuppak
       kaṭaṅkāṟ ṟiyamā tirameṭṭum
       kaṉakak kuṭumip polaṉvaraiyum
       kakaṉa mēḻu muṭkiṭappak
       kamalat tiruttāḷ pottiyanāḷ
       aṭaṅkāp puviyō kuṟṟēval
       aṭiyō mārai yayarmuṉṟil
       aḷakka viṟṟaik kaṭivaippa
       talainī ruṭalan taḻalparappat
       taṭaṅkārc cilaiyiṟ pakaḻitoṭum
       talaivaṉ ciṟṟil citaiyēlē
       caṅkat taḻaka ṉiṭapakirit
       talaivaṉ ciṟṟil citaiyēlē.         11
       ciṟṟiṟparuvamuṟṟum.

                   oṉpatāvatu - ciṟupaṟaipparuvam.
       maṟutalaiyarakka ruyirayilkuṭippa
               vamararaiyaḷitta neṭuvēḷ
       mataṉaḻiyaveṟ*ṟu ciṟuvayiṟalaittu
               mayilvaraipaṟappa vaṉalpōy
       maṟitiraivayiṟṟiṉ muḻukimutuveppu
               mumiḻuṭalkuḷirppa varitā
       vaṭaticaipurakku makaḷpuṟamaḷippa
               maḻaituḷimaṟutta vaṉamī
       teṟuḻvaliyiruppu neṭunutimaruppu
               mutukalaiteṟippa vaṭalvāy
       eṟipaṭaipiṭitta karamviṭavuṭutta
               puliyataṇilattu viḻalāṉ
       iṭipaṭavaraṟṟu tamarukamveṭitta
               kuralaviyaneṭṭai yaravūr
       eriviḻutuviṭṭa caṭaipiṇinekiḻppa
               marakatamviritta vilaicūḻ
       aṟukitaḻikokki ṉiṟakutiravaṭṭa
               matiyiṉvakirpaṭṭa nutaṉmī
       talaiyumveyarneṟṟi viḻiyaḻalavippa
               vamaranatiyeṟṟi viḻunīr
       aṟaitiraikoḻippa vuṭalpotiveḷutta
               poṭikaḻuvavoṟṟai ratamūr
       aruṇaveyilkakku paḻaiyavoḷikaṭpa
               varaṉmutukaḷippa mutaṉāḷ
       teṟumuṉaimukatti lavuṇaṉeḻuvotta
               tiraḷpuyamatuṟṟa kaṟainīr
       cikaravaraiviṭṭa vuviyainikarppa
               veritikiriviṭṭa matitōy
       tirumalaipurakku maḻakakuṇileṟṟu
               ciṟupaṟaimuḻakkiyaruḷē
       tiruvaraivaṇaṅki yaḻakakuṇleṟṟu
               ciṟupaṟaimuḻakki ruḷē.         (1)
       vēṟu,
       oḷiperukittuḷumpu paramapatattiṟaivā
               ukaḷamutaiccuranta veḷiyakaṭaṟṟuṟaivā
       aḷiyuḻupaṟpavunti yeḻutaraṇippuravā
               ayaṉumaivaittapaṅkaṉ viputakulakkuravā
       viḷainakaiyuṭṭatumpu tikiriceluttuḻavā
               viḻunakaikakkupoṅka riṭapamalaikkiḻavā
       kuḷiramalarkkaikoṇṭu ciṟupaṟaikoṭṭukavē
               kuṇiṟalaiyeṟṟiniṉṟu ceṟupaṟaikoṭṭukavē         (2)
       vēṟu
       cekkari loḷikeḻu tuppiṉai yeṟikaṭal
               ceṟṟiya taḻaleṉavē
       tikkuḷa cuṟavakai neṟkulai caṭaipaṭu
               ceyttalai viḻavarucēl
       mokkiya kurukeḻa vuṭpoti ciṉaiciṟai
               muttiṭa vaḷaitavaḻā
       mukkeṟi kuraltaṉai viṭṭuyir nikarpeṭai
               muṟpayil veḷiyeṉavāl
       ekkari ṉilaviya vucciyi liḷamala
               riṟpuṟa maḷavirukāl
       eṭṭaṭi yiṭaviḻai peṭṭaiyi ṉoṭutirai
               yeṟṟiya mutupuṉalvāḻ
       kukkuṭa malamaru veṟpuṟai maḻaimukil
               koṭṭuka ciṟupaṟaiyē
       kottaḷi niraiyuḻu maittuḷa vaṇimukil
               koṭṭuka ciṟupaṟaiyē.         (3)
       vēṟu
       veṇṭiraip pāṟkaṭal viṭappām puṭaṟṟāmpu
               veṟpumat terimuḻakkum
       viṇkiḻik kuṅkuṭumi noccicūḻ mitilaivāy
               viṉmuṭak kiyamuḻakkum
       puṇṭiraik kurutinī rāṟupaṭa vāṭakaṉ
               poṉmārpi lukiraḻuttap
       puṭaikkum peruntūṇ piḷanteḻumuḻakkamum
               poṅkarmatu vāymaṭuttu
       vaṇṭirait titaḻkutaṭ ṭuntuṇart taruvūḻtta
               malarkoṇarn tamararaṟiya
       vāyvaitta veḷvaḷai muḻakkamum kēṭṭuḷam
               vāḻntayā miṉṟumakiḻat
       teṇṭiraip paripura natittalaiva kuṇileṟṟu
               ciṟupaṟai muḻakkiyaruḷē
       cekamalaiyu meṉavutara maṇaikayiṟu variyumukil
               ciṟupaṟai muḻakkiyaruḷē.         (4)
       vēṟu.
       puḷḷun tiraiyum porupuṉal kuṭaiyum
               pūntoṭi maṅkaiyartēm
       potivaṇ ṭalaiyuṅ kumutattiḷanakai
               poṅkoḷi corinilavum
       kaḷḷuṇ ciṟaivaṇ ṭiṉamum paravuṅ
               kaṟṟaik kuḻalcerukum
       kaṭikamaḻ pittikai viṭumalar tūṟṟuṅ
               kalainila vummiṭaiyait
       taḷḷuṅ koṅkaikat taraḷak kuvaicori
               taṇṇila vumparukic
       caṟṟum paṭupaci taṇiyā tiḷaiya
               cakōrak kulamulavum
       teḷḷum puṉaṉū puranati yiṟaivaṉ
               ciṟupaṟai koṭṭukavē
       cempoṟ cāraṟ kulamalai yiṟaivaṉ
               ciṟupaṟai koṭṭukavē.         (5)
       vēṟu.
       karaikaṭan tiṟaikku nīttanīrk kuḻiyiṟ
               kālviḻa maruppēṟṟum
       kaḻittalaik kumuta vāyitaḻ maṭuttuṅ
               kaṭcevi kiṭavāmal
       viraikamaḻ tuḷavap potumpari ṉīḻal
               veppaṟap puyamvaittum
       viṭumaṭaṟ kamala vuntiyiṟ pūttum
               viṇṇuṭa ṟoṭumeṭṭu.
       varaiyarā viruttu nūṟṟuvar kūṟṟiṉ
               vāypukac cumaitīrttum
       malarppatan tāvi yaḷantukaik koṇṭu
               maṟṟoru potunīṅkak
       kuraikaṭal vaḷāka memateṉak kuricil
               koṭṭuka ciṟu paṟaiyē
       kulamalai vāṇaṉ mālalaṅ kāraṉ
               koṭṭuka ciṟupaṟaiyē.         6
       kāṭṭiyumuṭalan tuṇṇeṉap purintu
               kaṇkaḷait ticaivaittum
       karattiṉāṉ mīḷap puṭaitteḻuṅ kuraṟkuk
               kāntaḷañ cevitāḻttum
       nīṭṭiyun tiraittu metuveṉa nakaittu
               neñcayarn talamantum
       niṉṟulāṅ kuricil puṟaviṉiṟ parappi
               niraiyiṉaik kaḻaiyūtik
       kūṭṭiyum yamuṉaik karaivaruṅ kaḷiṟu
               koṭṭuka ciṟupaṟaiyē
       kulamalai vāṇaṉ mālalaṅ kāraṉ
               koṭṭuka ciṟupaṟaiyē. .         7
       vēṟu
       viḻuttalai vaṭittaneṭ ṭūcinaṭ ṭuṅkuppai
               ventīp piḻampiliṭṭum
       veṇṇiṇat tīntaṭi yarintucem puṇṉiṉvāy
               veḷḷuppu nīriṟaittum
       cuḻittalai parantāṭu mutiranīt tattiṉiṟ
               ṟuyaruḻap pattaḷḷiyum
       cuṭarvā ḷilaikkaḷḷi mīteṟin tunnāci
               tūṇṭumūc cuḷḷaṭaṅkak
       kuḻittalai talaikkī ḻuṟapputait tumpulāṟ
               kurutiyuṭal pōṭṭunāyiṉ
       kōḷvāyp pukuntumā taṇṭamayar maṟalipati
               kuṟukāma laṭiyarukkāt
       teḻittalai yaṟāvaruvi malaivantu niṉṟamukil
               ciṟupaṟai muḻakkiyaruḷē
       cekamalaiyu meṉavutara maṇaikayiṟu variyumukil
               ciṟupaṟaimuḻakkiyaruḷē.         8
       pāntaḷiṟ paḵṟalaic cemmaṇik kuppaiyiṟ
               paṭarveyiṟ kuṭalvetumpip
       pāṇcurum paṟukāl kiḷaikkum pacuntuḻāyp
               paḷḷiyan tāmanīḻaṟ
       cāntaḷa ṟeṭuttuk koḻikkum paṇaippuyan
               tavicunaṭu vīṟṟirukkum
       taraimakaḷ virumpiyek kālamun nilaveḻun
               tavaḷavoṇ caṅkuṭukkum
       kāntaḷaṅ kaiyēntu pāvaiyip paṟaiyoli
               kaṟaṅkameli yacuṇamallaḷ
       kaṉivāyiṉ moḻikaṟṟu vaḷarkiḷip piḷḷaicūṟ
               karuvimukil kaṇpaṭuppat
       tēntaḻaip pāyalaru ṇaṟcōlai malaivāṇa
       ciṟupaṟai muḻakkiyaruḷē
       cekamalaiyu meṉavutara maṇaikayiṟu variyumukil
               ciṟupaṟai muḻakkiyaruḷē.         9
       tuḷikoṇṭa veḷḷamirta miṭaiyaṟā toḻukiviḷai
               tukirpaḻut taṉaiyaceyya
       tūyavā yūṟalu mukattalai kuḻaṟṟalai
               tuvaṟṟiviḻu puḻukuneyyum
       aḷikoṇṭa taṇṭuḻāyp paḷḷit tiruttoṅka
               lakavitaḻ varintōṭutēṉ
       alaiyum puṉaṟpaṭat taṭpamuṟ ṟeḻukura
               lavintatē ṉarukupāyum
       olikoṇṭa kaṭavuṭ kavuttuvac cekkar maṇi
               yucciyiṟ kaṟṟaiveyilvāy
       olipoṅka veṉṟataḷ vetuppuṅ karuṅkoṇṭa
               luṭupatik kuḻavitavaḻum
       teḷikoṇṭa talaiyaruvi tuñcukula malaivāṇa
               ciṟupaṟai muḻakkiyaruḷē
       cekamalaiyu meṉavutara maṇaikayiṟu variyumukil
               ciṟupaṟai muḻakkiiruḷē.         (10)
       ciṟupaṟaipparuva muṟṟum

                       pattāvatu - ciṟutērpparuvam
       irukaramuñcevappa valaṉōṭiḷaittuviḷaitēṉ
               eḻutaḷavampatitta koṭivērotukkimalarāl
       maruveḻupantariṭṭu veyilvāṉ maṟaittumalitūḷ
               maṭiyamukantukoṭṭi yiṟainīrmaḻaikkaṇmaṭavār
       niṟaiyaḷakaṅkulaippa viḻutātotukkiyaṭiyēm
               nilaviyamuṉṟilvaṭṭa naṭuvēniṟuttimaraivāḻ
       tiruvuṟaipoṅkarveṟpa ciṟutēruruṭṭiyaruḷē
               tikiriyiṇainataṇaitta ciṟutēruruṭṭiyaruḷē.         (1)
       poṟaiyuṭalampiḷappa vuḻucāṟuvaittum varucēl
               porukatavantiṟakku maṭaivāyaṭaittunuraipāy
       aṟaipuṉalpoṅkiyeṟṟu niṟaikāluṭaittumvayaṟōy
               aḷaṟupeṟuṅkuruttu muḷaināṟaḻittumaṇiyāl
       maṟaiyavarampumuṟṟu mutucūlukuttumvaḷaiyēr
               maḷavaruṭaṉpakaitta puṉaṉāṭuṭuttavaḷicūḻ
       ciṟaiyeṟipoṅkarveṟpa ciṟutēruruṭṭiyaruḷē
               tiruvaḷarumpuyatta ciṟutēruruṭṭiyaruḷē.         (2)
       vēṟu
       puḻutiyaḷaintu teruttalaiyiṉ
               puṟampō yaṟaiyiṉ maṇiyacaittum
       puṉiṟṟā vanta teṉavuraittum
               poṅka leṉaccē ṟaṭiyiḻukkum
       toḻuvi luralaip piṇippoḻittum
               tuḷḷuṅ kuḻakkaṉ ṟeruttaṇaittum
       toṭarpāyn taṟutta teṉavuraittu
               coriyum viḻinīr meypōr**
       aḻutu porumi niṉṟupoyyē
               aṭittā reṉṟu milaimaṉaiyil
       acōtai pataṟi yōṭivara
               aḻaittu nakaikku maḻalaimukil
       koḻutu maḷitōy cōlaimalaik
               kōmāṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭukavē
       kuḷiruñ cilampā ṟuṭaiyamaṟaik
               kōmāṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭukavē.         (3)
       mīṉkōṭ ṭalaikkum peruvāri
               veḷḷat teḻunta veṟpumvaṭa
       veṟpu maḻunta muṉṉāḷil
               veḷḷai varākap perumpōttuka
       kūṉkōṭ ṭiraṭṭai nutimaṭuttuk
               kuḻiyil vīḻu nilamakaḷaik
       koṇṭu nimira nāṟpuṟamum
               koḻikkun taraṅkap puṉalpōla
       vāṉkōṭ ṭiḷaiya matiyamutu
               vaḻikku mirata neṭuñcikara
       maruṅku toṭukku miṟālpiḷakka
               vaḻiyuṅ kaṭavā raṇaṅkuttit
       tēṉkōṭ ṭiḻiya varuñcōlaic
               cilampaṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭukavē
       teḷḷun taraṅkap pālāḻic
               cērppaṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭukavē.         (4)
       aṇṭa ruvalaik kuṭiṟpaṭalvāy
               aṉaittun tiṟantu tōṟṟavuṟi
       alaintu tōṟṟa vuraṉmititta
               aṭiyiṉ cuvaṭu tōṟṟamutir
       vaṇṭa laḷaipā ṉiṟaintataṭā
               vayiṟu kuṟaintu tōṟṟaviṭai

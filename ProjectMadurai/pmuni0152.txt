   aṟputa mūrttitaṉ alartaru taṉmaiyiṉ 5

                               1 potuppāyiram

   pāyirattiṉ peyarkaḷ

   mukavurai patikam aṇinturai nūṉmukam
   puṟavurai tanturai puṉainturai pāyiram
   1
   pāyira vakai

   pāyiram potucciṟap peṉairu pāṟṟē
   2
   potuppāyiram iṉṉateṉpatu

   nūlē nuvalvōṉ nuvalun tiṟaṉē
   koḷvōṉ kōṭaṟ kūṟṟām aintum
   ellā nūṟkum ivaipotup pāyiram
   3

   1. nūliṉatu varalāṟu

   nūl iṉṉateṉpatu

   nūliṉ iyalpē nuvaliṉ oriru
   pāyiran tōṟṟi mummaiyiṉ oṉṟāy
   nāṟporuṭ payattō ṭeḻumatan taḻuvi
   aiyiru kuṟṟamum akaṟṟiyam māṭciyōṭu
   eṇṇāṉ kuttiyiṉ ottup paṭalam
   eṉṉum uṟuppiṉiṟ cūttiraṅ kāṇṭikai
   virutti yākum vikaṟpanaṭai peṟumē
   4
   mūvakai nūl

   mutalvaḻi cārpeṉa nūlmūṉ ṟākum
   5
   mutaṉūl iṉṉateṉpatu

   avaṟṟuḷ
   viṉaiyiṉ nīṅki viḷaṅkiya aṟiviṉ
   muṉaivaṉ kaṇṭatu mutaṉū lākum
   6
   vaḻinūl iṉṉateṉpatu

   muṉṉōr nūliṉ muṭipuoruṅ kottup
   piṉṉōṉ vēṇṭum vikaṟpaṅ kūṟi
   aḻiyā marapiṉatu vaḻinū lākum
   7
   cārpunūl iṉṉateṉpatu

   iruvar nūṟkum oruciṟai toṭaṅkit
   tiripuvē ṟuṭaiyatu puṭainū lākum
   8
   vaḻinūlukkuñ cārpunūlukkum eytiyataṉmēṟ ciṟappuviti

   muṉṉōr moḻiporuḷē yaṉṟi yavarmoḻiyum
   poṉṉēpōṟ pōṟṟuvam eṉpataṟkum - muṉṉōriṉ
   vēṟunūl ceytumeṉum mēṟkōḷi leṉpataṟkuṅ
   kūṟupaḻañ cūttirattiṉ kōḷ
   9
   nāṟporuṭ payaṉ

   aṟamporu ḷiṉpamvī ṭaṭaitalnūṟ payaṉē
   10
   eḻumatam

   eḻuvakai matamē uṭaṉpaṭal maṟuttal
   piṟartam matamēṟ koṇṭu kaḷaivē
   tāaṉ nāṭṭit taṉātu niṟuppē
   iruvar māṟukōḷ orutalai tuṇivē
   piṟarnūṟ kuṟṟaṅ kāṭṭal ēṉaip
   piṟitoṭu paṭāaṉ taṉmataṅ koḷalē
   11
   pattuk kuṟṟam

   kuṉṟak kūṟal mikaipaṭak kūṟal
   kūṟiyatu kūṟal māṟukoḷak kūṟal
   vaḻūuccoṟ puṇarttal mayaṅka vaittal
   veṟṟeṉat toṭuttal maṟṟoṉṟu virittal
   ceṉṟutēyn tiṟutal niṉṟupaya ṉiṉmai
   eṉṟivai yīraiṅ kuṟṟam nūṟkē
   12
   pattaḻaku

   curuṅkac collal viḷaṅka vaittal
   naviṉṟōrk kiṉimai naṉmoḻi puṇarttal
   ōcai yuṭaimai āḻamuṭait tātal
   muṟaiyiṉ vaippē ulakammalai yāmai
   viḻumiyatu payattal viḷaṅkutā raṇattatu
   ākutal nūliṟ kaḻakeṉum pattē
   13
   muppattiraṇṭu utti

   nutaliṉ pukutal ōttumuṟai vaippē
   tokuttuc cuṭṭal vakuttuk kāṭṭal
   muṭittuk kāṭṭal muṭiviṭaṅ kūṟal
   tāṉeṭuttu moḻital piṟaṉkōṭ kūṟal
   coṟporuḷ virittal toṭarccoṟ puṇarttal
   iraṭṭuṟa moḻital ētuviṉ muṭittal
   oppiṉ muṭittal māṭṭeṟin toḻukal
   iṟantatu vilakkal etiratu pōṟṟal
   muṉmoḻintu kōṭal piṉṉatu niṟuttal
   vikaṟpattiṉ muṭittal muṭintatu muṭittal
   uraittum eṉṟal uraittām eṉṟal
   orutalai tuṇital eṭuttuk kāṭṭal
   eṭutta moḻiyi ṉeyta vaittal
   iṉṉa talla tituveṉa moḻital
   eñciya colli ṉeytak kūṟal
   piṟanūṉ muṭintatu tāṉuṭaṉ paṭutal
   taṉkuṟi vaḻakka mikaeṭut turaittal
   colliṉ muṭivi ṉapporuḷ muṭittal
   oṉṟiṉa muṭittal taṉṉiṉa muṭittal
   uyttuṇara vaippeṉa uttiyeṇ ṇāṉkē
   14
   utti iṉṉateṉpatu

   nūṟporuḷ vaḻakkoṭu vāyppak kāṭṭi
   ēṟpuḻi yaṟintitaṟ kivvakai yāmeṉat
   takumvakai celuttutal tantira utti
   15
   ōttu iṉṉateṉpatu

   nēriṉa maṇiyai niralpaṭa vaittāṅku
   ōriṉap poruḷai yoruvaḻi vaippatu
   ōtteṉa moḻipa uyarmoḻip pulavar
   16
   paṭalam iṉṉateṉpatu

   oruneṟi yiṉṟi viraviya poruḷāṟ
   potumoḻi toṭariṉ atupaṭala mākum
   17
   cūttiram iṉṉateṉpatu

   cilvakai yeḻuttiṟ palvakaip poruḷaic
   celvaṉ āṭiyiṟ ceṟittiṉitu viḷakkit
   tiṭpa nuṭpañ ciṟantaṉa cūttiram
   18
   cūttira nilai

   āṟṟoḻuk karimā nōkkan tavaḷaip
   pāyttup paruntiṉvīḻ vaṉṉacūt tiranilai
   19
   cūttiraṅkaḷukkuk kāraṇa
   vakaiyāl varum peyar vēṟupāṭu

   piṇṭan tokaivakai kuṟiyē ceykai
   koṇṭiyal puṟaṉaṭaik kūṟṟaṉa cūttiram
   20
   uraiyiṉatu potu ilakkaṇam

   pāṭaṅ karuttē colvakai coṟporuḷ
   tokutturai utāraṇam viṉāviṭai vicēṭam
   virivuati kāran tuṇivu payaṉōṭu
   āciriya vacaṉameṉ ṟīrē ḻuraiyē
   21
   kāṇṭikaiyurai iṉṉateṉpatu

   karuttup patapporuḷ kāṭṭu mūṉṟiṉum
   avaṟṟoṭu viṉāviṭai yākka lāṉum
   cūttirat tuṭporuḷ tōṟṟuva kāṇṭikai
   22
   viruttiyurai iṉṉateṉpatu

   cūttirat tuṭporuḷ aṉṟiyum āṇṭaikku
   iṉṟi yamaiyā yāvaiyum viḷaṅkat
   taṉṉurai yāṉum piṟanū lāṉum
   aiyam akalaaiṅ kāṇṭikai yuṟuppoṭu
   meyyiṉai eñcā ticaippatu virutti
   23
   nūleṉṉum peyark kāraṇam

   pañcitaṉ cellāp paṉuval iḻaiyākac
   ceñcoṟ pulavaṉē cēyiḻaiyā - eñcāta
   kaiyēvā yākak katirē matiyāka
   maiyilā nūlmuṭiyu māṟu
   24
   urattiṉ vaḷamperukki yuḷḷiya tīmaip
   purattiṉ vaḷamurukkip pollā - marattiṉ
   kaṉakkōṭṭan tīrkkunū laḵtēpōṉ māntar
   maṉakkōṭṭan tīrkkunūṉ māṇpu 25

   2 āciriyaṉatu varalāṟu

   nallāciriyar ilakkaṇam

   kulaṉaruḷ teyvaṅ koḷkai mēṉmai
   kalaipayil tauivu kaṭṭurai vaṉmai
   nilaimalai niṟaikōl malarnikar māṭciyum
   ulakiyal aṟivōṭu uyarkuṇam iṉaiyavum
   amaipavaṉ nūlurai yāciri yaṉṉē
   26
   terivarum perumaiyun tiṇmaiyum poṟaiyum
   paruvam muyaṟci yaḷaviṟ payattalum
   maruviya naṉṉila māṇpā kummē 27
   aḷakka lākā aḷavum poruḷum
   tuḷakka lākā nilaiyun tōṟṟamum
   vaṟappiṉum vaḷantarum vaṇmaiyu malaikkē 28
   aiyan tīrap poruḷaip yuṇarttalum
   meynnaṭu nilaiyu mikuniṟai kōṟkē 29
   maṅkala māki yiṉṟi yamaiyātu
   yāvaru makiḻntu mēṟkoḷa melkip
   poḻutiṉ mukamalar vuṭaiyatu pūvē 30
   āciriyarākātār ilakkaṇam

   moḻikuṇam iṉmaiyum iḻikuṇa iyalpum
   aḻukkā ṟavāvañcam accam āṭalum
   kaḻaṟkuṭam maṭaṟpaṉai paruttik kuṇṭikai
   muṭatteṅ koppeṉa muraṇkoḷ cintaiyum
   uṭaiyōr ilarā ciriyarā kutalē
   31
   peytamuṟai yaṉṟip piṟaḻa uṭaṉṟarum
   ceyti kaḻaṟpey kuṭattiṉ cīrē 32
   tāṉē tarakkoḷiṉ aṉṟit taṉpāl
   mēvik koḷakkoṭā viṭattatu maṭaṟpaṉai 33
   aritiṟ peyakkoṇ ṭapporuḷ tāṉpiṟarkku
   eḷitī villatu paruttik kuṇṭikai 34
   palvakai yutavi vaḻipaṭu paṇpiṉ
   allōrk kaḷikkum atumuṭat teṅkē 35

   3 pāṭañcolalliṉ varalāṟu

   ītal iyalpē iyampuṅ kālaik
   kālamum iṭaṉum vālitiṉ nōkkic
   ciṟantuḻi yiruntutaṉ teyvam vāḻtti
   uraikkap paṭumporuḷ uḷḷat tamaittu
   viraiyāṉ vekuḷāṉ virumpi mukamalarntu
   koḷvōṉ koḷvakai yaṟintuavaṉ uḷaṅkoḷak
   kōṭṭamil maṉattiṉnūl koṭuttal eṉpa 36

   4 māṇākkaṉatu varalāṟu

   māṇākkar ilakkaṇam

   taṉmakaṉ ācāṉ makaṉē maṉmakaṉ
   poruḷnaṉi koṭuppōṉ vaḻipaṭu vōṉē
   uraikō ḷāḷaṟku uraippatu nūlē
                                   37
   aṉṉam āvē maṇṇoṭu kiḷiyē
   illik kuṭamāṭu erumai neyyari
   aṉṉar talaiyiṭai kaṭaimā ṇākkar 38
   māṇākkarākātār ilakkaṇam

   kaḷimaṭi māṉi kāmi kaḷvaṉ
   piṇiyaṉ ēḻai piṇakkaṉ ciṉattaṉ
   tuyilvōṉ mantaṉ toṉṉūṟ kañcit
   taṭumā ṟuḷattaṉ taṟukaṇaṉ pāvi
   paṭiṟaṉiṉ ṉōrkkup pakarār nūlē
                                   39

   5 pāṭaṅkēṭṭaliṉ varalāṟu

   kōṭal marapē kūṟuṅ kālaip
   poḻutoṭu ceṉṟu vaḻipaṭal muṉiyāṉ
   kuṇattoṭu paḻaki yavaṉkuṟip piṟcārntu
   iruveṉa iruntu colleṉac collip
   parukuvaṉ aṉṉaār vatta ṉākic
   cittirap pāvaiyiṉ attakavu aṭaṅkic
   cevivā yāka veñcukaḷa ṉākak
   kēṭṭavai kēṭṭavai viṭātuḷat tamaittup
   pōveṉap pōtal eṉmaṉār pulavar 40
   nūlpayil iyalpē nuvaliṉvaḻak kaṟital
   pāṭam pōṟṟal kēṭṭavai niṉaittal
   ācāṟ cārntavai amaivarak kēṭṭal
   ammāṇ puṭaiyōr tammoṭu payiṟal
   viṉātal viṉāyavai viṭuttal eṉṟivai
   kaṭaṉāk koḷiṉē maṭamnaṉi yikakkum 41
   orukuṟi kēṭpōṉ irukāṟ kēṭpiṟ
   peruka nūliṟ piḻaipā ṭilaṉē 42
   mukkāṟ kēṭpiṉ muṟaiyaṟin turaikkum 43
   ācā ṉuraitta tamaivarak koḷiṉum
   kāṟkū ṟallatu paṟṟala ṉākum 44
   avviṉai yāḷaroṭu payilvakai yorukāṟ
   cevvitiṉ uraippa avviru kālum
   maiyaṟu pulamai māṇpuṭait tākum 45
   aḻaliṉ nīṅkāṉ aṇukāṉ añci
   niḻaliṉ nīṅkāṉ niṟainta neñcamōṭu
   ettiṟat tācāṉ uvakkum attiṟam
   aṟattiṟ ṟiriyāp paṭarcci vaḻipāṭē 46

   6. ciṟappup pāyirattilakkaṇam

   ciṟappup pāyirattukkup potuviti

   ākkiyōṉ peyarē vaḻiyē yellai
   nūṟpeyar yāppē nutaliya poruḷē
   kēṭpōr payaṉō ṭāyeṇ poruḷum
   vāyppak kāṭṭal pāyirat tiyalpē
   47
   kālaṅ kaḷaṉē kāraṇa meṉṟim
   mūvakai yēṟṟi moḻinaru muḷarē 48
   nūṟpeyarukkuc ciṟappuviti

   mutalnūl karuttaṉ aḷavu mikuti
   poruḷcey vittōṉ taṉmaimuta ṉimittiṉum
   iṭukuṟi yāṉumnūṟ keytum peyarē
   49
   vaḻiyiṉvakaiyākiya nūlyāppukkuc ciṟappuviti

   tokuttal virittal tokaiviri moḻipeyarppu
   eṉattakum nūlyāp pīriraṇ ṭeṉpa
   50
   ciṟappuppāyirañ ceytaṟkuriyār ivareṉpatu

   taṉṉā ciriyaṉ taṉṉoṭu kaṟṟēṉ
   taṉmā ṇākkaṉ takumurai kāraṉeṉṟu
   iṉṉōr pāyiram iyamputal kaṭaṉē
   51
   ciṟappuppāyiram piṟar ceytataṟkuk kāraṇam

   tōṉṟā tōṟṟit tuṟaipala muṭippiṉum
   tāṉtaṟ pukaḻtal takuti yaṉṟē
   52
   taṟpukaḻcci kuṟṟamākāta iṭaṅkaḷ

   maṉṉuṭai maṉṟat tōlait tūkkiṉum
   taṉṉuṭai yāṟṟal uṉarā riṭaiyiṉum
   maṉṉiya avaiyiṭai velluṟu poḻutiṉum
   taṉṉai maṟutalai paḻitta kālaiyum
   taṉṉaip pukaḻtalun takumpula vēṟkē
   53
   pāyiram nūlukku iṉṟiyamaiyāc ciṟappiṉa teṉpatu

   āyira mukattā ṉakaṉṟa tāyiṉum
   pāyira millatu paṉuva laṉṟē
   54
   māṭakkuc cittiramum mānakarkkuk kōpuramum
   āṭamaittōḷ nallārk kaṇiyumpōl - nāṭimuṉ
   aituraiyā niṉṟa aṇinturaiyai ennūṟkum
   peyturaiyā vaittār peritu 55

                             2. eḻuttatikāram

                                1 eḻuttiyal

   kaṭavuḷ vaṇakkamum atikāramum

   pūmali acōkiṉ puṉainiḻal amarnta
   nāṉmukaṟ ṟoḻutunaṉ kiyampuvaṉ eḻuttē
   56
   eḻuttilakkaṇattiṉ pakuti

   eṇpeyar muṟaipiṟappu uruvam māttirai
   mutalīṟu iṭainilai pōli yeṉṟā
   patampuṇarpu eṉappaṉ ṉirupāṟ ṟatuvē
   57

   1. eṇ

   eḻuttu iṉṉateṉpatum ataṉvakaiyum

   moḻimutaṟ kāraṇam āmaṇut tiraḷoli
   eḻuttuatu mutalcār peṉairu vakaittē
   58
   mutaleḻuttiṉ viri

   uyirum uṭampumām muppatu mutalē
   59
   cārpeḻuttiṉ vakai

   uyirmey āytam uyiraḷapu oṟṟaḷapu
   aḵkiya i u ai ōḷa maḵkāṉ
   taṉinilai pattuñ cārpeḻut tākum
   60
   cārpeḻuttiṉ viri

   uyirmey iraṭṭunūṟ ṟeṭṭuuya rāytam
   eṭṭuuyi raḷapueḻu mūṉṟuoṟ ṟaḷapeṭai
   āṟēḻ aḵkum immup pāṉēḻ
   ukaram āṟāṟu aikāṉ mūṉṟē
   ōḷakāṉ oṉṟē maḵkāṉ mūṉṟē
   āytam iraṇṭoṭu cārpeḻut tuṟuviri
   oṉṟōḻi munnūṟ ṟeḻupāṉ eṉpa
   61

   2. peyar

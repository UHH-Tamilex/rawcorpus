naṉṉūl
pavaṇanti muṉivar

tirucciṟṟampalam

ciṟappuppāyiram

malartali ulakiṉ malkiruḷ akala
ilakoḷi parappi yāviyum viḷakkum
paritiyi ṉorutā ṉāki mutalīṟu
oppaḷavu āci muṉivikan tuyarnta
aṟputa mūrttitaṉ alartaru taṉmiyiṉ

5

maṉiruḷ iriya māṇporuḷ muḻuvatum
muṉivaṟa aruḷiya mūaṟu moḻiyuḷum
kuṇakaṭal kumari kuṭakam vēṅkaṭam
eṉunāṉ kelliyiṉ iruntamiḻk kaṭaluḷ
arumporu ḷintiyum yāvarum uṇarat

10

tokivaki viriyiṟ ṟarukeṉat tuṉṉār
ikalaṟa nūṟi yirunila muḻuvatum
taṉateṉak kōlit taṉmata vāraṇam
ticitoṟum niṟuviya tiṟaluṟu tolcīrk
karuṅkaḻal veṇkuṭik kārnikar vaṇkit

15

tiruntiya ceṅkōṟ cīya kaṅkaṉ
aruṅkali vinōtaṉ amarā paraṇaṉ
moḻintaṉa ṉāka muṉṉōr nūliṉ
vaḻiyē naṉṉūṟ peyariṉ vakuttaṉaṉ
poṉmatiṟ caṉakic caṉmati muṉiyaruḷ

20

paṉṉaruñ ciṟappiṟ pavaṇanti
eṉṉu nāmat tiruntavat tōṉē

tirucciṟṟampalam

1 potuppāyiram

pāyirattiṉ peyarkaḷ

mukavuri patikam aṇinturi nūṉmukam
puṟavuri tanturi puṉinturi pāyiram

1

pāyira vaki

pāyiram potucciṟap peṉiru pāṟṟē

2

potuppāyiram iṉṉateṉpatu

nūlē nuvalvōṉ nuvalun tiṟaṉē
koḷvōṉ kōṭaṟ kūṟṟām aintum
ellā nūṟkum ivipotup pāyiram

3

1. nūliṉatu varalāṟu

nūl iṉṉateṉpatu

nūliṉ iyalpē nuvaliṉ oriru
pāyiran tōṟṟi mummiyiṉ oṉṟāy
nāṟporuṭ payattō ṭeḻumatan taḻuvi
aiyiru kuṟṟamum akaṟṟiyam māṭciyōṭu
eṇṇāṉ kuttiyiṉ ottup paṭalam
eṉṉum uṟuppiṉiṟ cūttiraṅ kāṇṭiki
virutti yākum vikaṟpanaṭi peṟumē

4

mūvaki nūl

mutalvaḻi cārpeṉa nūlmūṉ ṟākum

5

mutaṉūl iṉṉateṉpatu

avaṟṟuḷ
viṉiyiṉ nīṅki viḷaṅkiya aṟiviṉ
muṉivaṉ kaṇṭatu mutaṉū lākum

6

vaḻinūl iṉṉateṉpatu

muṉṉōr nūliṉ muṭipuoruṅ kottup
piṉṉōṉ vēṇṭum vikaṟpaṅ kūṟi
aḻiyā marapiṉatu vaḻinū lākum

7

cārpunūl iṉṉateṉpatu

iruvar nūṟkum oruciṟi toṭaṅkit
tiripuvē ṟuṭiyatu puṭinū lākum

8

vaḻinūlukkuñ cārpunūlukkum eytiyataṉmēṟ ciṟappuviti

muṉṉōr moḻiporuḷē yaṉṟi yavarmoḻiyum
poṉṉēpōṟ pōṟṟuvam eṉpataṟkum - muṉṉōriṉ
vēṟunūl ceytumeṉum mēṟkōḷi leṉpataṟkuṅ
kūṟupaḻañ cūttirattiṉ kōḷ

9

nāṟporuṭ payaṉ

aṟamporu ḷiṉpamvī ṭaṭitalnūṟ payaṉē

10

eḻumatam

eḻuvaki matamē uṭaṉpaṭal maṟuttal
piṟartam matamēṟ koṇṭu kaḷivē
tāaṉ nāṭṭit taṉātu niṟuppē
iruvar māṟukōḷ orutali tuṇivē
piṟarnūṟ kuṟṟaṅ kāṭṭal ēṉip
piṟitoṭu paṭāaṉ taṉmataṅ koḷalē

11

pattuk kuṟṟam

kuṉṟak kūṟal mikipaṭak kūṟal
kūṟiyatu kūṟal māṟukoḷak kūṟal
vaḻūuccoṟ puṇarttal mayaṅka vittal
veṟṟeṉat toṭuttal maṟṟoṉṟu virittal
ceṉṟutēyn tiṟutal niṉṟupaya ṉiṉmi
eṉṟivi yīriṅ kuṟṟam nūṟkē

12

pattaḻaku

curuṅkac collal viḷaṅka vittal
naviṉṟōrk kiṉimi naṉmoḻi puṇarttal
ōci yuṭimi āḻamuṭit tātal
muṟiyiṉ vippē ulakammali yāmi
viḻumiyatu payattal viḷaṅkutā raṇattatu
ākutal nūliṟ kaḻakeṉum pattē

13

muppattiraṇṭu utti

nutaliṉ pukutal ōttumuṟi vippē
tokuttuc cuṭṭal vakuttuk kāṭṭal
muṭittuk kāṭṭal muṭiviṭaṅ kūṟal
tāṉeṭuttu moḻital piṟaṉkōṭ kūṟal
coṟporuḷ virittal toṭarccoṟ puṇarttal
iraṭṭuṟa moḻital ētuviṉ muṭittal
oppiṉ muṭittal māṭṭeṟin toḻukal
iṟantatu vilakkal etiratu pōṟṟal
muṉmoḻintu kōṭal piṉṉatu niṟuttal
vikaṟpattiṉ muṭittal muṭintatu muṭittal
urittum eṉṟal urittām eṉṟal
orutali tuṇital eṭuttuk kāṭṭal
eṭutta moḻiyi ṉeyta vittal
iṉṉa talla tituveṉa moḻital
eñciya colli ṉeytak kūṟal
piṟanūṉ muṭintatu tāṉuṭaṉ paṭutal
taṉkuṟi vaḻakka mikeṭut turittal
colliṉ muṭivi ṉapporuḷ muṭittal
oṉṟiṉa muṭittal taṉṉiṉa muṭittal
uyttuṇara vippeṉa uttiyeṇ ṇāṉkē

14

utti iṉṉateṉpatu

nūṟporuḷ vaḻakkoṭu vāyppak kāṭṭi
ēṟpuḻi yaṟintitaṟ kivvaki yāmeṉat
takumvaki celuttutal tantira utti

15

ōttu iṉṉateṉpatu

nēriṉa maṇiyi niralpaṭa vittāṅku
ōriṉap poruḷi yoruvaḻi vippatu
ōtteṉa moḻipa uyarmoḻip pulavar

16

paṭalam iṉṉateṉpatu

oruneṟi yiṉṟi viraviya poruḷāṟ
potumoḻi toṭariṉ atupaṭala mākum

17

cūttiram iṉṉateṉpatu

cilvaki yeḻuttiṟ palvakip poruḷic
celvaṉ āṭiyiṟ ceṟittiṉitu viḷakkit
tiṭpa nuṭpañ ciṟantaṉa cūttiram

18

cūttira nili

āṟṟoḻuk karimā nōkkan tavaḷip
pāyttup paruntiṉvīḻ vaṉṉacūt tiranili

19

cūttiraṅkaḷukkuk kāraṇa
vakiyāl varum peyar vēṟupāṭu

piṇṭan tokivaki kuṟiyē ceyki
koṇṭiyal puṟaṉaṭik kūṟṟaṉa cūttiram

20

uriyiṉatu potu ilakkaṇam

pāṭaṅ karuttē colvaki coṟporuḷ
tokutturi utāraṇam viṉāviṭi vicēṭam
virivuati kāran tuṇivu payaṉōṭu
āciriya vacaṉameṉ ṟīrē ḻuriyē

21

kāṇṭikiyuri iṉṉateṉpatu

karuttup patapporuḷ kāṭṭu mūṉṟiṉum
avaṟṟoṭu viṉāviṭi yākka lāṉum
cūttirat tuṭporuḷ tōṟṟuva kāṇṭiki

22

viruttiyuri iṉṉateṉpatu

cūttirat tuṭporuḷ aṉṟiyum āṇṭikku
iṉṟi yamiyā yāviyum viḷaṅkat
taṉṉuri yāṉum piṟanū lāṉum
aiyam akalaiṅ kāṇṭiki yuṟuppoṭu
meyyiṉi eñcā ticippatu virutti

23

nūleṉṉum peyark kāraṇam

pañcitaṉ cellāp paṉuval iḻiyākac
ceñcoṟ pulavaṉē cēyiḻiyā - eñcāta
kiyēvā yākak katirē matiyāka
miyilā nūlmuṭiyu māṟu

24

urattiṉ vaḷamperukki yuḷḷiya tīmip
purattiṉ vaḷamurukkip pollā - marattiṉ
kaṉakkōṭṭan tīrkkunū laḵtēpōṉ māntar
maṉakkōṭṭan tīrkkunūṉ māṇpu

25

2 āciriyaṉatu varalāṟu

nallāciriyar ilakkaṇam

kulaṉaruḷ teyvaṅ koḷki mēṉmi
kalipayil tuivu kaṭṭuri vaṉmi
nilimali niṟikōl malarnikar māṭciyum
ulakiyal aṟivōṭu uyarkuṇam iṉiyavum
amipavaṉ nūluri yāciri yaṉṉē

26

terivarum perumiyun tiṇmiyum poṟiyum
paruvam muyaṟci yaḷaviṟ payattalum
maruviya naṉṉila māṇpā kummē

27

aḷakka lākā aḷavum poruḷum
tuḷakka lākā niliyun tōṟṟamum
vaṟappiṉum vaḷantarum vaṇmiyu malikkē

28

aiyan tīrap poruḷip yuṇarttalum
meynnaṭu niliyu mikuniṟi kōṟkē

29

maṅkala māki yiṉṟi yamiyātu
yāvaru makiḻntu mēṟkoḷa melkip
poḻutiṉ mukamalar vuṭiyatu pūvē

30

āciriyarākātār ilakkaṇam

moḻikuṇam iṉmiyum iḻikuṇa iyalpum
aḻukkā ṟavāvañcam accam āṭalum
kaḻaṟkuṭam maṭaṟpaṉi paruttik kuṇṭiki
muṭatteṅ koppeṉa muraṇkoḷ cintiyum
uṭiyōr ilarā ciriyarā kutalē

31

peytamuṟi yaṉṟip piṟaḻa uṭaṉṟarum
ceyti kaḻaṟpey kuṭattiṉ cīrē

32

tāṉē tarakkoḷiṉ aṉṟit taṉpāl
mēvik koḷakkoṭā viṭattatu maṭaṟpaṉi

33

aritiṟ peyakkoṇ ṭapporuḷ tāṉpiṟarkku
eḷitī villatu paruttik kuṇṭiki

34

palvaki yutavi vaḻipaṭu paṇpiṉ
allōrk kaḷikkum atumuṭat teṅkē

35

3 pāṭañcolalliṉ varalāṟu

ītal iyalpē iyampuṅ kālik
kālamum iṭaṉum vālitiṉ nōkkic
ciṟantuḻi yiruntutaṉ teyvam vāḻtti
urikkap paṭumporuḷ uḷḷat tamittu
viriyāṉ vekuḷāṉ virumpi mukamalarntu
koḷvōṉ koḷvaki yaṟintuavaṉ uḷaṅkoḷak
kōṭṭamil maṉattiṉnūl koṭuttal eṉpa

36

4 māṇākkaṉatu varalāṟu

māṇākkar ilakkaṇam

taṉmakaṉ ācāṉ makaṉē maṉmakaṉ
poruḷnaṉi koṭuppōṉ vaḻipaṭu vōṉē
urikō ḷāḷaṟku urippatu nūlē

37

aṉṉam āvē maṇṇoṭu kiḷiyē
illik kuṭamāṭu erumi neyyari
aṉṉar taliyiṭi kaṭimā ṇākkar

38

māṇākkarākātār ilakkaṇam

kaḷimaṭi māṉi kāmi kaḷvaṉ
piṇiyaṉ ēḻi piṇakkaṉ ciṉattaṉ
tuyilvōṉ mantaṉ toṉṉūṟ kañcit
taṭumā ṟuḷattaṉ taṟukaṇaṉ pāvi
paṭiṟaṉiṉ ṉōrkkup pakarār nūlē

39

5 pāṭaṅkēṭṭaliṉ varalāṟu

kōṭal marapē kūṟuṅ kālip
poḻutoṭu ceṉṟu vaḻipaṭal muṉiyāṉ
kuṇattoṭu paḻaki yavaṉkuṟip piṟcārntu
iruveṉa iruntu colleṉac collip
parukuvaṉ aṉṉār vatta ṉākic
cittirap pāviyiṉ attakavu aṭaṅkic
cevivā yāka veñcukaḷa ṉākak
kēṭṭavi kēṭṭavi viṭātuḷat tamittup
pōveṉap pōtal eṉmaṉār pulavar

40

nūlpayil iyalpē nuvaliṉvaḻak kaṟital
pāṭam pōṟṟal kēṭṭavi niṉittal
ācāṟ cārntavi amivarak kēṭṭal
ammāṇ puṭiyōr tammoṭu payiṟal
viṉātal viṉāyavi viṭuttal eṉṟivi
kaṭaṉāk koḷiṉē maṭamnaṉi yikakkum

41

orukuṟi kēṭpōṉ irukāṟ kēṭpiṟ
peruka nūliṟ piḻipā ṭilaṉē

42

mukkāṟ kēṭpiṉ muṟiyaṟin turikkum

43

ācā ṉuritta tamivarak koḷiṉum
kāṟkū ṟallatu paṟṟala ṉākum

44

avviṉi yāḷaroṭu payilvaki yorukāṟ
cevvitiṉ urippa avviru kālum
miyaṟu pulami māṇpuṭit tākum

45

aḻaliṉ nīṅkāṉ aṇukāṉ añci
niḻaliṉ nīṅkāṉ niṟinta neñcamōṭu
ettiṟat tācāṉ uvakkum attiṟam
aṟattiṟ ṟiriyāp paṭarcci vaḻipāṭē

46

6. ciṟappup pāyirattilakkaṇam

ciṟappup pāyirattukkup potuviti

ākkiyōṉ peyarē vaḻiyē yelli
nūṟpeyar yāppē nutaliya poruḷē
kēṭpōr payaṉō ṭāyeṇ poruḷum
vāyppak kāṭṭal pāyirat tiyalpē

47

kālaṅ kaḷaṉē kāraṇa meṉṟim
mūvaki yēṟṟi moḻinaru muḷarē

48

nūṟpeyarukkuc ciṟappuviti

mutalnūl karuttaṉ aḷavu mikuti
poruḷcey vittōṉ taṉmimuta ṉimittiṉum
iṭukuṟi yāṉumnūṟ keytum peyarē

49

vaḻiyiṉvakiyākiya nūlyāppukkuc ciṟappuviti

tokuttal virittal tokiviri moḻipeyarppu
eṉattakum nūlyāp pīriraṇ ṭeṉpa

50

ciṟappuppāyirañ ceytaṟkuriyār ivareṉpatu

taṉṉā ciriyaṉ taṉṉoṭu kaṟṟēṉ
taṉmā ṇākkaṉ takumuri kāraṉeṉṟu
iṉṉōr pāyiram iyamputal kaṭaṉē

51

ciṟappuppāyiram piṟar ceytataṟkuk kāraṇam

tōṉṟā tōṟṟit tuṟipala muṭippiṉum
tāṉtaṟ pukaḻtal takuti yaṉṟē

52

taṟpukaḻcci kuṟṟamākāta iṭaṅkaḷ

maṉṉuṭi maṉṟat tōlit tūkkiṉum
taṉṉuṭi yāṟṟal uṉarā riṭiyiṉum
maṉṉiya aviyiṭi velluṟu poḻutiṉum
taṉṉi maṟutali paḻitta kāliyum
taṉṉip pukaḻtalun takumpula vēṟkē

53

pāyiram nūlukku iṉṟiyamiyāc ciṟappiṉa teṉpatu

āyira mukattā ṉakaṉṟa tāyiṉum
pāyira millatu paṉuva laṉṟē

54

māṭakkuc cittiramum mānakarkkuk kōpuramum
āṭamittōḷ nallārk kaṇiyumpōl - nāṭimuṉ
aituriyā niṉṟa aṇinturiyi ennūṟkum
peyturiyā vittār peritu

2. eḻuttatikāram

1 eḻuttiyal

kaṭavuḷ vaṇakkamum atikāramum

pūmali acōkiṉ puṉiniḻal amarnta
nāṉmukaṟ ṟoḻutunaṉ kiyampuvaṉ eḻuttē

56

eḻuttilakkaṇattiṉ pakuti

eṇpeyar muṟipiṟappu uruvam māttiri
mutalīṟu iṭinili pōli yeṉṟā
patampuṇarpu eṉappaṉ ṉirupāṟ ṟatuvē

57

1. eṇ

eḻuttu iṉṉateṉpatum ataṉvakiyum

moḻimutaṟ kāraṇam āmaṇut tiraḷoli
eḻuttuatu mutalcār peṉiru vakittē

58

mutaleḻuttiṉ viri

uyirum uṭampumām muppatu mutalē

59

cārpeḻuttiṉ vaki

uyirmey āytam uyiraḷapu oṟṟaḷapu
aḵkiya i u ai ōḷa maḵkāṉ
taṉinili pattuñ cārpeḻut tākum

60

cārpeḻuttiṉ viri

uyirmey iraṭṭunūṟ ṟeṭṭuuya rāytam
eṭṭuuyi raḷapueḻu mūṉṟuoṟ ṟaḷapeṭi
āṟēḻ aḵkum immup pāṉēḻ
ukaram āṟāṟu aikāṉ mūṉṟē
ōḷakāṉ oṉṟē maḵkāṉ mūṉṟē
āytam iraṇṭoṭu cārpeḻut tuṟuviri
oṉṟōḻi munnūṟ ṟeḻupāṉ eṉpa

61

2. peyar

peyarkkellām potu ilakkaṇam

iṭukuṟi kāraṇap peyarpotuc ciṟappiṉa

62

eḻuttiṉ peyar

ammutal īṟāṟu āvi kammutal
meymmū āṟeṉa viḷampiṉar pulavar

63

avaṟṟuḷ
a i u e okkuṟil aintē

64

ā ī ū ē ai ō oḷa neṭil

65

a i ummutaṟ ṟaṉivariṟ cuṭṭē

66

e yā mutalum ā ō īṟṟum
ē yiru vaḻiyum viṉāā kummē

67

valliṉam ka ca ṭa ta pa ṟa veṉavāṟē

68

melliṉam ṅa ña ṇa na ma ṉa veṉavāṟē

69

iṭiyiṉam ya ra la va ḻa ḷa veṉavāṟē

70

iṉa eḻuttu

aioḷi uc ceṟiya mutaleḻuttu
ivviraṇ ṭōriṉa māyvaral muṟiyē

71

iṉam eṉpataṟkuk kāraṇam

tāṉam muyaṟci aḷavu poruḷvaṭivu
āṉoṉ ṟātiyōr puṭiyop piṉamē

72

3. muṟi

ciṟappiṉum iṉattiṉuñ ceṟintīṇ ṭammutal
naṭattal tāṉē muṟiyā kummē

73

4 piṟappu

piṟappiṉ potuviti

niaiṟuyuyir muyaṟciyiṉ uḷvaḷi turappa
eḻumaṇut tiraḷuraṅ kaṇṭam ucci
mūkkuṟ ṟitaḻnāp pallaṇat toḻiliṉ
vevvē ṟeḻuttoli yāyvaral piṟappē

74

mutaleḻuttukkaḷukku iṭappiṟappu

avvaḻi
āvi yiṭimi yiṭamiṭa ṟākum
mēvu meṉmimūk kurampeṟum vaṉmi

75

mutaleḻuttukkaḷukku muyaṟcip piṟappu

avaṟṟuḷ
muyaṟciyuḷ a ā aṅkāp puṭiya

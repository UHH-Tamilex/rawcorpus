                       2.0 kaṭavuḷ vāḻttu 1399

   1399 vāṉ niṉṟu iḻintu varampu ikanta mā pūtam attu iṉ vaippu eṅkum,
   ūṉum uyirum uṇarvum pōl uḷḷum puṟaṉum uḷaṉ eṉpa ;
   kūṉum ciṟiya kō tāy um koṭumai iḻaippak kōl tuṟantu,
   kāṉum kaṭalum kaṭantu, imaiyōr iṭukkaṇ tīrtta kaḻal vēntai. 2.0.1

                  2.1 . mantirap paṭalam (1400 – 1495)

   1400 tayarataṉ mantirālōcaṉai maṇṭapattai aṭaital
   maṇ uṟum muracu iṉam maḻaiyiṉ ārppu uṟa
   paṇ uṟu paṭar ciṉap paruma yāṉaiyāṉ ,
   kaṇ uṟu kavariyiṉ kaṟṟai cuṟṟu uṟa ,
   eṇ uṟu cūḻcciyiṉ irukkai eytiṉāṉ . 2.1.1
   1401 tayarataṉ taṉittiruttal
   pukka piṉ 'niruparum poru il cuṟṟamum
   pakkamum peyarka ' eṉap pariviṉ nīkkiṉāṉ ;
   okka niṉṟu , ulaku aḷittu , yōkiṉ eytiya
   cakkarattavaṉ eṉat tamiyaṉ āyiṉāṉ . 2.1.2
   1402 vaciṭṭaṉ mutaliya amaiccarkaḷai varavaḻaittal
   cantiraṟku uvamaicey taraḷa veṇ kuṭai
   antaram attu aḷavum niṉṟu aḷikkum āṇaiyāṉ ,
   intiraṟku imaiyavar kuruvai ēynta taṉ
   mantirak kiḻavarai 'varuka ' ! eṉṟu ēviṉāṉ . 2.1.3
   1403 vaciṭṭaṉ varukai
   pū varu polaṉ kaḻal poru il maṉṉavaṉ
   kāvaliṉ āṇai cey kaṭavuḷ ām eṉat
   tēvarum muṉivarum uṇarum tēvarkaḷ
   mūvariṉ nālvar ām muṉi vantu eytiṉāṉ . 2.1.4
   1404 amaiccarkaḷiṉ perumai (1404-1408)
   kulam mutal toṉmaiyum , kalaiyiṉ kuppaiyum ,
   pala mutal kēḷviyum , payaṉum , eytiṉār ,
   nalam mutal naliyiṉum naṭuvu nōkkuvār ,
   calam mutal aṟuttu , arum tarumam tāṅkiṉār . 2.1.5
   1405 uṟṟatu koṇṭu mēl vantu uṟu poruḷ uṇarum kōḷ ār ;
   maṟṟu atu viṉaiyiṉ vantatu āyiṉum , māṟṟal āṟṟum
   peṟṟiyar ; piṟappiṉ mēṉmaip periyavar ; ariya nūlum
   kaṟṟavar ; māṉam nōkkiṉ kavarimā aṉaiya nīrār . 2.1.6
   1406 kālamum iṭaṉum ēṟṟa karuviyum terintu kaṟṟa
   nūluṟa nōkkit teyvam nuṉittu , aṟam kuṟittu , mēlōr
   cīlamum pukaḻkku vēṇṭum ceykaiyum terintukoṇṭu ,
   pāl varum uṟuti yāvum talaivaṉ ku payakkum nīrār . 2.1.7
   1407 tam uyirkku uṟuti eṇṇār , talaimakaṉ vekuṇṭa pōtum
   vemmaiyait tāṅki nīti viṭātu niṉṟu uraikkum vīrar ;
   cemmai il tiṟampal cellāt tēṟṟattār , teriyum kālam
   mummaiyum uṇara vallār , orumaiyē moḻiyum nīrār . 2.1.8
   1408 nallavum tīyavum nāṭi , nāyakaṟku
   ellaiyil maruttuvaṉ iyalpiṉ eṇṇuvār ;
   ollai vantu uṟuvaṉa uṟṟa peṟṟiyil
   tollai nalviṉai eṉa utavum cūḻcciyār . 2.1.9
   1409 amaiccarkaḷiṉ varukai (1409-1410)
   aṟupatiṉ āyirar eṉiṉum , āṇ takaikku
   uṟutiyil , oṉṟu ivarkku uṇarvu eṉṟu uṉṉalām
   peṟal arum cūḻcciyar ; tiruviṉ peṭpiṉar ;
   maṟi tiraik kaṭal eṉa vantu cuṟṟiṉār . 2.1.10
   1410 muṟaimaiyiṉ eytiṉar , munti antam il
   aṟivaṉai vaṇaṅkit tam aracaik kai toḻutu ,
   iṟaiyiṭai varaṉmuṟai ēṟi , ēṟṟa col
   tuṟai aṟi perumaiyāṉ aruḷum cūṭiṉār . 2.1.11
   1411 tayarataṉ taṉ karuttaik kūṟutal (1411-1429)
   aṉṉavaṉ aruḷ amaintu irunta āṇṭaiyiṉ ,
   maṉṉavaṉ avar mukam marapiṉ nōkkiṉāṉ ;
   'uṉṉiyatu arum peṟal uṟuti oṉṟu uḷatu ;
   eṉ uṇarvu aṉaiya nīr iṉitu kēṭka ' eṉā . 2.1.12
   1412 'veyyavaṉ kulam mutal vēntar mēlavar
   ceykaiyiṉ oru muṟai tiṟampal iṉṟiyē
   vaiyam eṉ puyattiṭai , nuṅkaḷ māṭciyāl ,
   aiyiraṇṭu āyiram attu āṟu tāṅkiṉēṉ . ' 2.1.13
   1413 'kaṉṉiyarkku amaivarum kaṟpiṉ , mānilam
   taṉṉai i takai tara tarumam kaitara ,
   maṉ uyirkku uṟuvatē ceytu vaikiṉēṉ ,
   eṉ uyirkku uṟuvatum ceyya eṇṇiṉēṉ .' 2.1.14
   1414 'virumpiya mūppu eṉum vīṭu kaṇṭa yāṉ ,
   irum piyal aṉantaṉum , icainta yāṉaiyum ,
   perum peyark kirikaḷum , peyarat tāṅkiya
   arum poṟai iṉic ciṟitu āṟṟa āṟṟal ēṉ .' 2.1.15
   1415 'nam kulak kuravarkaḷ navaiyiṉ nīṅkiṉār ,
   tam kulap putalvarē taraṇi tāṅkap pōy ,
   vem kulap pulaṉ keṭa vīṭu naṇṇiṉār ,
   eṅku ulappu uṟuvar ? eṉṟu eṇṇi nōkkukēṉ .' 2.1.16
   1416 'veḷḷa nīr ulakiṉil , viṇṇil , nākaril ,
   taḷ arum pakai elām tavirttu niṉṟa yāṉ ,
   kaḷḷaril karantu uṟai kāmam ātiyām
   uḷ uṟai pakaiñarukku otuṅki vāḻveṉ ō ?' 2.1.17
   1417 'pañci meṉ taḷir aṭip pāvai kōl koḷa ,
   vem ciṉattu avuṇar tēr pattum veṉṟu uḷēṟku ,
   eñcal il maṉam eṉum iḻutai ēṟiya
   añcu tēr vellum ītu arumai āvatu ē ?' 2.1.18
   1418 'oṭṭiya pakaiñar vantu urutta pōr iṭai
   paṭṭavar allarēl , parama ñāṉam pōyt
   teṭṭavar allarēl , celvam īṇṭeṉa
   viṭṭavar allarēl , yāvar vīṭu uḷār ?' 2.1.19
   1419 'iṟappu eṉum meymaiyai immai yāvarkkum
   maṟappu eṉum ataṉiṉ mēl kēṭu maṟṟu uṇṭu ō ?
   tuṟappu eṉum teppamē tuṇai ceyāviṭiṉ
   piṟappu eṉum perum kaṭal piḻaikkal ākumō ?' 2.1.20
   1420 'arum ciṟappu amaivarum tuṟavum , av vaḻit
   teriñcu uṟavu eṉa mikum teḷivum , āy varum
   perum ciṟaku uḷa eṉil , piṟavi eṉṉum iv
   irum ciṟai kaṭattaliṉ iṉiyatu yāvatu ē ? ' 2.1.22
   1421 'iṉiyatu pōlum iv aracai eṇṇum ō
   tuṉi varum nalaṉ eṉat toṭarntu ? tōṟkalā
   naṉi varu perum pakai navaiyiṉ nīṅki , at
   taṉi aracāṭciyil tāḻum uḷḷam ē ? ' 2.1.22
   1422 'ummai yāṉ uṭaimaiyiṉ ulakam yāvaiyum
   cemmaiyiṉ ōmpi nal aṟamum ceytaṉeṉ ;
   immaiyiṉ utavi nal icai naṭāya nīr ,
   ammaiyum utavutaṟku amaiya vēṇṭumāl . ' 2.1.23
   1423 'iḻaitta tī viṉaiyai um kaṭakka eṇṇutal ,
   taḻaitta pēr aruḷ uṭai tavattiṉ ākumēl ,
   kuḻaittatu ōr amutu uṭaik kōram nīkki , vēṟu
   aḻaitta tī viṭattiṉai aruntal ākum ō ? ' 2.1.24
   1424 'kaccai am kaṭa karik kaḻuttiṉkaṇ uṟap
   piccamum kavikaiyum peyyum iṉ niḻal
   niccayam aṉṟu eṉiṉ , neṭitu nāḷ uṇṭa
   eccilai nukaruvatu iṉpam ākum ō ? ' 2.1.25

   1425 'maintarai iṉmaiyiṉ varampu il kālamum
   nontaṉeṉ , irāmaṉ eṉ nōvai nīkkuvāṉ
   vantaṉaṉ ; iṉi avaṉ varunta , yāṉ piḻaittu
   uyntaṉeṉ pōvatu ōr uṟuti eṇṇiṉēṉ . 2.1.26
   1426 'iṟantilaṉ ceruk kaḷattu irāmaṉ tātai tāṉ
   aṟam talai nirampa mūppu aṭainta piṉṉarum
   tuṟantilaṉ eṉpatu ōr col uṇṭāṉa piṉ
   piṟantilaṉ eṉpatil piṟitu uṇṭākum ō ? ' 2.1.27
   1427 'perum makaṉ eṉvayiṉ piṟakkac cītaiyām
   tiru makaḷ maṇaviṉai teriyak kaṇṭa yāṉ ,
   arum makaṉ , niṟai kuṇattu avaṉimātu eṉum
   oru makaḷ maṇamum kaṇṭu uvappa uṉṉiṉēṉ . ' 2.1.28
   1428 'nivappu uṟu nilaṉ eṉum nirampum naṅkaiyum ,
   civappu uṟu malar micai ciṟanta celviyum ,
   uvappu uṟu kaṇavaṉai uyiriṉ eytiya
   tavap payaṉ tāḻppatu tarumam aṉṟu arō . ' 2.1.29
   1429 'ātalāl irāmaṉukku aracai nalki , ip
   pētaimaittu āy varum piṟappai nīkkuvāṉ ,
   mā tavam toṭaṅkiya vaṉattai naṇṇuvēṉ ;
   yātu num karuttu ? ' eṉa iṉaiya kūṟiṉāṉ . 2.1.30
   1430 maṉṉaṉ māṟṟam kēṭṭa amaiccar nilai
   tiraṇṭa tōḷiṉaṉ ippaṭic ceppalum , cintai
   puraṇṭu mītu iṭap poṅkiya uvakaiyar , āṅkē
   veruṇṭu , maṉṉavaṉ pirivu eṉum vimmuṟum nilaiyāl ,
   iraṇṭu kaṉṟiṉukku iraṅkum ōr ā eṉa iruntār . 2.1.31
   1431 maṉṉaṉ karuttiṟku amaiccar icaital
   aṉṉar āyiṉum , aracaṉukku atu alatu uṟuti
   piṉṉar il eṉak karutiyum , perum nila varaippil
   maṉṉum maṉ uyirkku irāmaṉiṉ maṉṉavar illai
   eṉṉa uṉṉiyum , vitiyatu valiyiṉum , icaintār . 2.1.32
   1432 vaciṭṭaṉ moḻiyat toṭaṅkal
   irunta mantirak kiḻavar atu eṇṇamum , taṉpāl
   parinta cintai am maṉṉavaṉ karutiya payaṉum ,
   poruntum maṉ uyir ku uṟutiyum , potu uṟa nōkkit
   terintu , nāl maṟai ticaimukaṉ tiru makaṉ ceppum . 2.1.33
   1433 vaciṭṭaṉ vāymoḻi (1433-1439)
   'nirupa ! niṉ kula maṉṉavar nēmi paṇṭu uruṭṭip
   perumai eytiṉar yāvar ē irāmaṉaip peṟṟār ?
   karumamē itu ; kaṟṟu uṇarntōy ku iṉik kaṭava
   tarumamum itu ; takkatē niṉaittaṉai , takavōy ! ' 2.1.34
   1434 'puṇṇiyam toṭar vēḷvikaḷ yāvaiyum purinta
   aṇṇal ē ! iṉi aruntavam iyaṟṟavum aṭukkum '
   vaṇṇa mēkalai nilam makaḷ maṟṟu uṉaip pirintu
   kaṇ iḻantilaḷ eṉac ceyum , nī tanta kaḻalōṉ . ' 2.1.35
   1435 'puṟattu nām oru poruḷ iṉip pukalkiṉṟatu evaṉ ō ?
   aṟattiṉ mūrtti vantu avatarittāṉ eṉpatu allāl ,
   piṟattu yāvaiyum kāttu avai piṉ uṟat tuṭaikkum
   tiṟattu mūvarum tiruttiya tiruttum at tiṟalōṉ . ' 2.1.36
   1436 'poṉ uyirtta pū maṭantaiyum , puvi eṉum tiruvum ,
   iṉ uyirt tuṇai ivaṉ eṉa niṉaikkiṉṟa irāmaṉ ,
   eṉ uyirkku eṉkai pullitu ; iṅku ivaṟ payantu eṭutta
   uṉ uyirkku eṉa nallaṉ , maṉ uyirkku elām ; uravōy ! ' 2.1.37
   1437 `'vāram eṉ iṉip pakarvatu ? vaikalum aṉaiyāṉ
   pēriṉāl varum iṭaiyuṟu peyarkiṉṟa payattāl ,
   vīra ! niṉ kula maintaṉai vētiyar mutalōr
   yārum , 'yām ceyta nal aṟap payaṉ ' eṉa iruppār . '' 2.1.38
   1438 `'maṇṇiṉum nallaḷ ; malar makaḷ , kalaimakaḷ , kalaiyūr
   peṇṇiṉum nallaḷ ; perum pukaḻc caṉaki ; pēr ulakiṉ
   kaṇṇiṉum nallaṉ ; kaṟṟavar kaṟṟilātavarum
   uṇṇum nīriṉum , uyiriṉum , avaṉaiyē uvappār . '' 2.1.39
   1439 `'maṉitar vāṉavar maṟṟu uḷōr aṟṟam kāttu aḷippār ,
   iṉi im maṉ uyirkku irāmaṉil ciṟantavar illai ;
   aṉaiyatu ātaliṉ , araca ! niṟku uṟu poruḷ aṟiyil ,
   puṉita mātavam allatu oṉṟu il '' eṉap pukaṉṟāṉ . 2.1.40
   1440 vaciṭṭaṉ vāymoḻiyāl tayarataṉ makiḻtal
   maṟṟu avaṉ coṉṉa vācakam kēṭṭalum , makaṉaip
   peṟṟa aṉṟiṉum , piññakaṉ piṭitta ap perum vil
   iṟṟa aṉṟiṉum , eṟi maḻu vāḷavaṉ iḻukkam
   uṟṟa aṉṟiṉum periyatu ōr uvakaiyaṉ āṉāṉ . 2.1.41
   1441 maṉṉaṉ vaciṭṭaṉaip pārāṭṭutal (1441-1442)
   aṉaiyatu ākiya uvakaiyaṉ , kaṇkaḷ nīr arumpa ,
   muṉivaṉ mā malar pātaṅkaḷ muṟaimaiyiṉ iṟaiñci ,
   'iṉiya colliṉai ; emperumāṉ aruḷ viḻiyiṉ
   taṉiyaṉ nāl nilam tāṅkiyatu ; avaṟku itu takātu ō ? ' 2.1.42
   1442 'entai nī uvantu itam cola , em kulattu aracar ,
   antam il arum perum pukaḻ avaṉiyil niṟuvi ,
   muntu vēḷviyum muṭittut tam iru viṉai muṭittār ;
   vantatu av aruḷ eṉakkum ' eṉṟu uraiceytu makiḻntāṉ . 2.1.43
   1443 cumantiraṉ collat toṭaṅkutal
   paḻutu il mātavaṉ piṉ oṉṟum paṇittu ilaṉ iruntāṉ ;
   muḻutum eṇ uṟum mantiram kiḻavar , tam mukattāl
   eḻuti nīṭṭiya iṅkitam , iṟai makaṉ ku ēṟat
   toḻuta kaiyiṉaṉ , cumantiraṉ , muṉ niṉṟu collum . 2.1.44
   1444 cumantiraṉ kūṟṟu (1444-1445)
   `'uṟat takum aracu irāmaṟku eṉṟu uvakkiṉṟa maṉam attu ai
   tuṟatti nī eṉum col cuṭum ; niṉkulat tollōr
   maṟattal ceykilāt tarumattai maṟappatum vaḻakku aṉṟu ;
   aṟattiṉ ūṅku iṉik koṭitu eṉal āvatu oṉṟu yātē ? '' 2.1.45
   1445 `'puraicai mā kari niruparkkum , purattu uṟaivōrkkum ,
   uraicey mantirak kiḻavarkkum , muṉivarkkum , uḷḷam
   muraicam ārppa , niṉ mutal maṇip putalvaṉai muṟaiyāl
   araicaṉ ākkip piṉ a puṟam attu aṭuttatu purivāy . '' 2.1.46
   1446 tayarataṉ irāmaṉaik koṇṭuvarac cumantiraṉai aṉupputal
   eṉṟa vācakam cumantiraṉ iyampalum , iṟaivaṉ ,
   'naṉṟu colliṉai ; nampiyai naḷi muṭi cūṭṭi
   niṉṟu , niṉṟatu ceyvatu ; viraiviṉil nīyē
   ceṉṟu , koṇṭu aṇai tiru makaḷ koḻunaṉai ' eṉṟāṉ . 2.1.47
   1447 cumantiraṉ irāmaṉai aṭaital
   alaṅkal maṉṉaṉai aṭi toḻutu , avaṉ maṉam aṉaiyāṉ
   vilaṅkal māḷikai vītiyil viraivu oṭu ceṉṟāṉ ,
   talaṅkaḷ yāvaiyum peṟṟaṉaṉ tāṉ eṉat taḷirppāṉ ,
   polaṉ koḷ tēroṭum irākavaṉ tiru maṉai pukkāṉ . 2.1.48
   1448 cumantiraṉ irāmaṉaik kāṇṭal
   peṇṇiṉ iṉ amutu aṉṉavaḷ taṉṉoṭum , piriyā
   vaṇṇa vem cilaik kuricilum maruṅku iṉitu iruppa ,
   aṇṇal āṇṭu iruntāṉ aḻaku aru naṟavu eṉṉak
   kaṇṇum uḷḷamum vaṇṭu eṉak kaḷippu uṟak kaṇṭāṉ . 2.1.49
   1449 irāmaṉ tērmēṟkoḷḷal
   kaṇṭu kai toḻutu `'aiya ! ik
         kaṭal iṭaik kiḻavōṉ
   'uṇṭu or kāriyam , varuka ' eṉa
         uraittaṉaṉ '' eṉalum
   puṇṭarīkak kaṇ puravalaṉ
         porukkeṉa eḻuntu , ōr
   koṇṭal pōl avaṉ koṭi neṭum
         tēr micai koṇṭāṉ 2.1.50

   1450 irāmaṉ tēril cellal
   muṟaiyiṉ moym mukil eṉa muracu ārttiṭa , maṭavār
   iṟai kaḻaṉṟa caṅku ārttiṭa , imaiyavar 'eṅkaḷ
   kuṟai muṭintatu ' eṉṟu ārttiṭak , kuñciyaic cūḻnta

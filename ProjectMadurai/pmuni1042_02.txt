             paḻamalai nātar piccāṭaṇa navamaṇi mālai
karuppakkiḷar cu, a. irāmacāmip pulavar viḷakkak kuṟippuraiyuṭaṉ

   piccā aṭaṉam : piccā-piccai, aṭaṉam-ēṟṟal. navamaṇimālaiyāvatu oṉpatu
   vakai maṇikaḷaip pōṉṟa uyarnta ceyyuṭkaḷāl ceyyap peṟuvatu. iḵtu eṇṇāṟ
   peṟṟa peyar. oṉpatu vakaiyāṉa ceyyuṭkaḷāṟ pāṭuvatum uṇṭu. muṉṉāḷil
   civaperumāṉ tārukāvaṉa muṉivarkaḷiṉ tarukkaiyaṭakkum poruṭṭu āṭaiyaṟṟa
   tirukkōlattuṭaṉ avartam maṉaikkup piccai yēṟkac ceṉṟu avartam
   maṉaiviyar kaṟpaik kulaittu muṉivarkaḷiṉ tarukkaiyaṭakkiṉār eṉpatu
   purāṇak kūṟṟu, ataṉai aṭippaṭaiyāka vaittu innāḷil naṭaipeṟum
   piccāṇṭavar viḻāvaik kaṇṭa aṭikaḷār ip pāṭalkaḷaip pāṭiṉar. itaṉil
   iṭakkaraṭakkalkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.
   naṅkuṟṟan tīrkkum paḻamalai nātarkku naṟpalikoṇ
   ṭaṅkuṟṟu meṉṟukil pōkkiṉaḷ veṟṟarai yākiyantō
   iṅkuṟ ṟaṉaiyeṉa vemperu māṉiv virunilattiṟ
   ṟaṅkuṟṟam pārkku mavaruḷa rōveṉat tāḻntaṉaḷē. (1)
   1. naṟpali-nalla piccai. meṉtukil-melliya āṭai. veṟṟarai-āṭaiyillāta
   arai.
   tāḻntaṉaḷ-viṭaiyiṉmaiyiṟ kīḻppaṭṭāḷ. tōṟṟavar veṉṟavarai vaṇaṅkutal
   iyalpākaliṉ vaṇaṅkiṉāḷ. taṉakkum veṟṟavarai yeṉpatuṇarntu
   talaikuṉintāḷ. piccāṭaṉaraip piriya maṉamiṉṟit taṅkiṉāḷ.
   tāḻtal-kīḻppaṭutal. vaṇaṅkutal, kuṉital, taṅkutal.
   ---------
   kuṉṟā mutukuṉ ṟuṭaiyā ṉilātaveṇ kōvaṇattāṉ
   taṉṟā maraikkai viraṉmūṉṟu kāṭṭit taṉaṅkuṟittu
   naṉṟāka vittaṉai piccaiyuṇ ṭōcoṉ ṉaṟunutalāy
   eṉṟā ṉiraṇṭat taṉaiyuḷa kōṭiyeṉ ṟiṭṭaṉaḷē, (2)
   2. kuṉṟā mutukuṉṟuṭaiyāṉ-ekkālattiṉuṅ kuṟaivupaṭāta paḻamalainātar:
   kuṉṟākiya paḻamalainātar; aruḷ kuṉṟātavaṉumām. vaḷaṅkuṉṟāta
   mutukuṉṟumām. ilāta veṇ kōvaṇattāṉ eṉpatu āṭaiyaṟṟavaṉ eṉṉum poruḷait
   taruvatāy “kaḻinta paṟṟuṭai vaciṭṭaṉ” eṉṟāṟpōla niṉṟatu. iraṇṭattaṉai
   ciṟumaiyaik kuṟittaṟkuk kūṟum eṇṇaḷavai; iraṇṭākiya avvaḷavu. kōṭi-koḷ,
   putitu. eṉṟiṭṭāḷ-oru col.
   ---------
   ippāt tirantalai kīḻāp piṭittaṉai yeṉṉaimuṉan
   tappāp palikoṇ ṭaṟintilai yōveṉat tāḻkuḻalāḷ
   ceppār paṇaimulai yāynilat tēpali cintiyiṭṭāy
   eppāt tirattalu miṭṭaṟi yāykoṉmuṉ ṉeṉṟaṉarē. (3)
   3. eppāttiram-caṟpāttiram, acaṟpāttiram, piṭcapāttiram mutaliyaṉa.
   tappā-tavaṟāta. palikoṇṭu-piccaiyēṟṟu. ceppuār-ceppuk kiṇṇam pōṉṟa.
   paṇai-parutta.
   ---------
   vēṭṭaṉa mātukai tāḻttiṭa māmutu veṟparpali
   ōṭṭiṉai nītoṭa lāmō veṉavumai yuntoṭalām
   vīṭṭiṉil yāṉeṉa vēyavar tāmitu vēṇṭuvaikol
   yāṭṭiṉiṉ pāleṉa mūlamu māmeṉpa teṉṟaṉarē. (4)
   4. umai-ummai. umātēvi. vīṭu-illam. vīṭupēṟu, āṭṭiṉiṉ pālitu vēṇṭuvai
   kolleṉa eṉṟu māṟuka.
   ---------
   māṇṭa velumpaṇi kōlamo ṭēpali vāṅkiṭutaṟ
   kīṇṭu varumutu kuṉṟuṭai yīrumai yāṉmiṭaṟṟil
   nīṇṭa vukiruṟut tātaṇai vēṉeṉṟu nīrtalaiyait
   tīṇṭa vumatu palippāt tirameṉ cirikkiṉṟatē. (5)
   5. māṇṭa elumpu-māṇṭupōṉa nāṉmukar palaruṭaiya elumpu. miṭaṟu-kaṇṭam.
   ukir uṟuttātu-nakam tākkāmal. talaiyaik kiḷḷum taṉmaiyai uṭaiyavar
   eṉpatai uṇarāmal ivar collai nampiṉaḷē ivaḷuṭaiya aṟiyāmai
   iruntavāṟeṉṉēyeṉṟu nakutalai nakaittatu eṉka.
   ---------
   vāṉōr toḻuniṉ palippāt tirattai vaṉaintatunī
   tāṉō veṉaccak karantāṉ cuḻaṟṟat takuṅkuyatti
   yāṉōr kuyavaṉmey yeṉṟē mutukuṉ ṟiṟaiyiyampa
   nāṉō voruciṟ ṟiṭaicciyeṉ ṟāḷan naṟunutalē. (6)
   6. vaṉaital-ceytal. cakkaram, maṭcakkaram, kaṇṇākiya aram. cakku-kaṇ.
   kuyatti-kuyaccātip peṇ. koṅkaikaḷaiyuṭaiya peṇ, iḷamaiyuṭaiya peṇ,
   alkuṟṟēraiyuṭaiya peṇ, kuyavaṉ eṉpataṟkum ēṟṟa peṟṟi ciṟitu vikaṟpittu
   ipporuḷ koḷka. mutukuṉṟu iṟaiiyampa-paḻamalaiyār viṭaikūṟa,
   paḻamalainātarkūṟa. ciṟṟiṭaicci-ciṟiya iṭaiyiṉaiyuṭaiyavaḷ, ciṟiya
   iṭaiccātip peṇ. ōr-aṟi, oru
   ---------
   paṅkaya maṉṉa viḻiyār mutukuṉṟar pāttirattil
   aṅkaiyi laiya moṭuvaṇṭu vīḻanal kaiyaveṉa
   eṅkaiyil vanta tematāka lēvaḻak keṉṟuceṭṭi
   maṅkaiyar taṅkaṭ kiruṅkū ṭaliliṭa vaittaṉarē. (7)
   7. vaṇṭu-vaḷaiyal. paṅkayam aṉṉa-tāmarai malaraiyotta. ceṭṭi maṅkaiyar
   taṅkaṭku eṉpatu iṉi vaiciyappeṇkaḷāyp piṟakkum muṉimaṉaiviyarkaḷākiya
   taṅkaṭku eṉavum poruḷ tōṉṟa niṟkiṉṟatu. kūṭal-puṇartal-maturai.
   ---------
   nīrukkut takka caṭaiyār mutukuṉṟar nēṭiyuṅkaḷ
   ūrukkuṭ piccaiyeṉ ṟuṟṟōmuṇ ṭāyi ṉuraimiṉeṉa
   vārukkut takka mulaiyā rataṟkuḷar maṟṟoruvar
   yārukkuk kiṭṭu matucōṟu nīrkoḷu meṉṟaṉarē. (8)
   8. ūrukkuḷ piccai-nakarattiṉkaṇ pali; maṟṟoṉṟu iṭakkaraṭakkal. ataṟku
   ūru-toṭai. maṟṟoruvar-umā tēviyār; taṅkaṇavar; atu yārukkuk
   kiṭṭum-avvūrukkuṭ piccai evarkkuṅ kiṭṭātu. cōṟu nīrkoḷum-avvuṇmaiyaic
   collukiṟōm nīr ēṟṟukkoḷḷum; cōṟṟaiyum nīr ēṟṟukkoḷḷum. ataṟku uḷar
   maṟṟoruvar yārukkuk kiṭṭum atu cōṟum nīr koḷum eṉpataṉai maṟṟoruvar
   ataṟku uḷar yārukkuk kiṭṭum atu cōṟu nīrkoḷum eṉa māṟṟip piṟar evarkkuṅ
   kiṭaiyātu umakkē kiṭaikkum avvūrkkuṭ piccaiyaiyum cōṟṟaiyum nīr
   ēṟṟaruḷum eṉṟu maṟṟoru poruḷuṅ koḷka.
   ---------
   muḻaṅkun tuṭiyoṭu piccaiyeṉ ṟēmutu kuṉṟarvarac
   caḻaṅku mulaimuti yāḷeṉ paliyeṉat tammaṉaiyil
   vaḻaṅku maṉameṉ ṟaṉarpuṟam pōntu vaḷarmulaiyāḷ
   viḻuṅku maṉampakaṟ pōtukoḷ vāyeṉṟu vēṇṭiṉaḷē. (9)
   9. caḻaṅkal-carital. pakaṟ pōtu viḻuṅkum aṉṉaṅkoḷvāy eṉṟu vēṇṭiṉaḷ.
   eṉavē irāppōtu
   maṉaiyil maṉam vaḻaṅkuvaṉ eṉṟāḷ eṉpatu kuṟippeccam.
   -----------------

                      pikṣāṭaṉa navamaṇimālai
     tirumayilai vittuvāṉ caṇmukam piḷḷaiyavarkaḷ ceyta urai

   Source:
   tuṟaimaṅkalam civappirakāca cuvāmikaḷ tiruvāy malarntaruḷiya
   pikṣāṭaṉa navamaṇimālai
   itu tirumayilai vittuvāṉ caṇmukam piḷḷaiyavarkaḷ ceyta uraiyuṭaṉ
   vīracaivācāra viṟpaṉṉarākiya ce. nāki ceṭṭiyāravarkaḷāl
   pārvaiyiṭappaṭṭu,
   ceṉṉai kurupacavā aṇṭu kampeṉi limiṭeṭ avarkaḷāl patippikkappaṭṭatu.
   1931 vilai aṇā 21
   civācāra mālā saṅkyā -4

   viṅkuṟ ṟaṉaiyeṉa vemperu māṉiv virunilattiṟ
   ṟaṅkuṟṟam pārkku mavaruḷa rōveṉat tāḻntaṉaḷē. 1

   - periya, nilattil - nilavalakattiṉkaṇ, tam - tamatu, kuṟṟam -
   piḻaikaḷai, pārkkumavar uḷarō - uyttuṇarntu ārāyvā ruṇṭō ? eṉa -(

   vikārattāṉeṉka ; itumaṭṭō , vaḷai kaḻaṟal, kuḻal carital, kuṟu veyarvu
   poṭittal, viḷaiyāṭṭo ḻital, pāluṇa voṟuttaṉ mutaliya piṟavu maṉṉa;
   vantuḻi vantu ḻik kāṇka. antō veṉpatu irakkak kuṟippu.
   pārkkumavar - iṇaimoḻi. kuṟṟaṅkaḷeṉpatu kāmam, vekuḷi, mayakkameṉṉum
   mukkuṟṟaṅkaḷai ; āṇavāti malapantamākiya kuṟṟaṅkaḷeṉiṉu mamai yum.
   piccai pukkuṇpār piccai yēṟkumiṭattu vācaṟpaṭi kaṭa vāmai yēṟṟal
   vēṇṭumeṉpatu campira tāyamākali ṉaṉṟē "vācaṟpaṭi kaṭantu vārāta
   piccaikku" eṉṟār piṟarum. taṅ kuṟṟam pārkkumavaruḷarō veṉpataṉai
   "ētilār kuṟṟam pōl " eṉṉum tirukkuṟaḷāṉum kāṇka. mahāṉeṉṉum vaṭacol
   māṉeṉat tirintu niṉṟatu. itaṉait taṟpavameṉpa. uḷarō veṉṉum ōkāram -
   etirmaṟai. ēkāram - īṟṟacai. (1)
   ----------
   kuṉṟā mutu kuṉ ṟuṭaiyā ṉilātaveṇ kōvaṇattāṉ
   ṟaṉṟā maraikkai viraṉ mūṉṟu kāṭṭit taṉaṅkuṟittu
   naṉṟāka vittaṉai piccaiyuṇ ṭōcoṉ ṉaṟunutalā
   yeṉṟā ṉiraṇṭat taṉaiyuḷa kōṭiyeṉ ṟiṭṭaṉaḷē. 2
   (pa-rai) kuṉṟā -(vaḷattāṉeṉṟum) kuṟaivupaṭāta, mutu kuṉṟu uṭaiyāṉ -
   paḻamalaivāṇaṉum, ilāta - illāmaiyākiya, veṇ - veṇṇiṟamuḷḷa, kōvaṇattāṉ
   - kōvaṇakkīḷuṭaiya vaṉumākiya kaṭavuḷ, tāmarai - tāmaraiyaip
   paḻittālaṉṉa, taṉ - taṉatu, kaiviral - mūṉṟu - mūṉṟiṉai, taṉam -
   taṉattai, kuṟittu - kuṟippāka, kāṭṭi - kāṇpittu, naṉṟu āka - (uṉakku)
   naṉmai uṇṭākak kaṭavatu (eṉṟu vāḻtti), ittaṉai - ivvaḷavu, piccai.
   'pikṣāṉṉam, uṇṭō - (kiṭaippatu) uṇṭō , naṟu - (maṇam) vīcukiṉṟa,
   nutalāy - neṟṟiyiṉai yuṭaiyāḷē, col eṉṟāṉ - colleṉṟu kēṭṭalum (ataṟku
   avaḷ), iraṇṭu attaṉai - (aṅṅaṉaṅ kēṭṭa pikṣāṉṉam) iraṇṭattaṉai, uḷa -
   uṇṭu, kōṭi - (vēṇṭiṉaiyēl) peṟṟuk koḷḷakkaṭavāy, eṉṟu : eṉṟu colli,
   iṭṭaṉaḷ - (piccai) iṭṭāḷ, (e - ṟu).
   (vi-rai) iraṭṭuṟa moḻitaleṉṉun tantira vuttiyāṟ ṟaṉattaik kuṟittuk
   kāṭṭiya kuṟippu nōkkam paṟṟi, ittaṉai piccai yuṇṭō veṉṟamaiyāṉ
   eṇṇikkaikku iraṇṭattaṉai yuḷavākaliṉ vēṇṭiṉaiyēṟ peṟṟuk
   koḷḷakkaṭavāyeṉṟu piccaiyiṭṭaṉaḷeṉap poruḷ uraittuk koḷka. kuṉṟā
   veṉpatu īṟukeṭṭa etirmaṟaip peyareccak kiḷavi. kuṉṟāveṉavē nīrvaḷam,
   nilavaḷam, kuṭivaḷam mikun tuṭaiyateṉap peṟṟām. kōṭiyeṉaṟpālatu eval
   viṉaimuṟṟuk kiḷavi. eṉṟāṉiraṇṭat taṉaiyeṉpuḻi yeṉṟāṉeṉpataṉai yeṉaveṉak
   koṇṭatu, "viṉaimuṟṟē viṉaiyeccamākalum " eṉṉum naṉṉūl viṉaiyiyaṟ
   cūttirattāṉamaikka. ilāta veṇ kōvaṇattāṉeṉiṉun tikamparaṉeṉiṉu mokkum.
   "taṉṉaiyē kōvaṇamāc cāttiṉaṉkāṇ cāḻalō'' eṉṟa tūum ik karuttē paṟṟi
   yeṉka. uṇṭō veṉṉum ōkāram - aiyam. ēkāram - īṟṟacai. (2)
   ----------------

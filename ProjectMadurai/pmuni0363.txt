srīrāmajayam.

tirumāliruñcōlimali aḻakar kalampakam.

pāyiram.
kāppu.
kaṭṭaḷikkalittuṟi.
āṉikku muṉcel liṭapā calatti laḻakaṉiyem
māṉik karutik kalampakaṅ kūṟa varaṟkaraṟkut
tāṉic curarkkuc caṭakōpaṅ cittikkat tāṅkuceṅkōl
cēṉit talivar tiruttāḷ karuttoṭuñ cēvippaṉē.
attiyiṉ mattiyi lēviḷaṅ kālilam māmakiḻa
nittiri koḷḷunta mālat turuva ṉivaṉeṉpatē
cattiya meṉṉap puḷikkīḻ makiḻan tamiḻkkaracē
nittiya mālaḻa kaṉṟamiḻ kūṟamuṉ ṉiṉṟaruḷē.

kaliyaṉ ṟiruvaṭikaḷ kāppu.

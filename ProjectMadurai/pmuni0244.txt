kallāṭar avarkaḷiṉ kallāṭam

patiṉōrām nūṟṟāṇṭil tamiḻil tōṉṟiya oppaṟṟa akanūl kallāṭam. kallāṭar kaṭiccaṅkattup
pulavar nāṟpattoṉpatiṉmaril oruvar. civaperumāṉiṉ perumikaḷi, pāṇṭiya maṉṉaṉiṉ
ciṟappukaḷi iṭiiṭiyē nuṭpamākac cerittuk kūṟum ilakkiyam. varuṇaṇi, uvami,
uruvakaṅkaḷil vāḻviyal meymmikaḷik kallāṭam kūṟukiṟatu.
civamayam
kaṭavuḷ vāḻttu :
veṇpā
cevvitaḻ pokuṭṭuc centā marivataṉak
kovviyeḻiṟ cevvāy karumattēṉi-- navvi
viḻiyāḷi pippuṇaruma vēḷimata veḷaḷam
poḻiyāṉi yimaṉamē pōṟṟu.
viruttam
vāynta poruṭ koruporuḷāyk kalivāṇika
      karuḷakoḻikku maṉpāyp pāri
ṉāyrtamutu tamiḻvaṭi ttuk kallāṭa
      meṉavorunū laruḷi yiṭṭār
tēyntamatic caṭipparamar karuṇipeṟac
      caṅkamuṟu celvar vāḻttak
kāyntapula ṉaṭakkiyuyar peruñāṉam
      paḻuttaruḷakala lāṭaṉārē.
veṇpā
kallāṭār ceypaṉuvaṟ kallāṭa nūṟu nūl
vallārcaṅ kattil vatintaruḷic--collāyu
māmaturi yīcar maṉamuvantu kēṭṭumuṭi
tāmacikkār nūṟu taram.
viruttam
vaṭiveṭutta vētamaṉpiṟ pāṭukiṉu
      maṟintamiyā vaṇṇam vikum poṭiyaṇiyun tirumēṉip puṉitaṉmatu
      rikkiṟivaṉ pukaḻiṉ pāṭal
neṭiyakavik kallāṭar poruṭṭuṟiyi
      kaḻattutoṟu nilavu cūṭum
muṭiyacittu naṉṟu naṉṟī teṉamakiḻu
      meḷiṉikarāy moḻiva tiyātē.

kaṇapati tuti

tiṅkaḷ muṭipoṟutta poṉmali aruvi
karumaṇi koḻitta tōṟṟam pōla
irukavuḷ kaviḻtta matanati uvaṭṭiṉ
vaṇṭiṉam puraḷum vayaṅku pukarmukatta
ceṅkatirt tiraḷeḻu karuṅkaṭal pōla       5
mukkaṇmēl poṅkum veḷḷam eṟikaṭatta
perumalic ceṉṉiyil ciṟumati kiṭanteṉa
kaṇaruḷ niṟinta kaliṉperum eyiṟṟa
āṟiraṇṭu arukkar avirkatirk kaṉalum
veḷḷi matimuṭitta ceñcaṭi oruttaṉ       10
ulakuyir āṭa āṭuṟum aṉalamum
teṉkīḻt ticiyōṉ teṟutaru tīyum
ūḻittīp paṭarntu uṭaṟṟupu cikiyum
pācak karakam vitiyuṭi mukkōl
muṟikkalic curukkuk karampeṟu muṉivar       15
viḻipaṭum eriyum cāpavāy neruppum
niliviṭṭup paṭarātu kāṇiyil nilikkac
ciṟukāṟṟu uḻalum acikuḻic ceviya
āmpal mukaarakkaṉ kiḷiyoṭu maṟiyap
peruṅkāṟṟu viṭutta neṭumpuḻik karatta       20
karumiṭaṟṟuk kaṭavuḷi ceṅkaṉi vēṇṭi
iṭam koḷ ñālattu valamkoḷum patatta
kuṇṭunīr uṭutta neṭumpār eṇṇamum
eṇṇā ilakkamoṭu naṇṇiṭu tuyaramum
aḷantukoṭu muṭittal niṉkaṭaṉ ātaliṉ       25
varivuṭal cūḻak kuṭampinūl teṟṟiya
pōkkuvaḻi paṭiyātu uḷuyir viṭuttaliṉ
aṟivu puṟampōya ulaṇṭatu pōla
kaṭaltiri ciṟuka malakkutuyar kāṭṭum
uṭaleṉum vāyil ciṟinaṭuvu pukku       30
pōkātu aṇaṅkuṟum veḷḷaṟi vēmum
āraṇam pōṟṟumniṉ kāluṟa vaṇaṅkutum
kālmukam ēṟṟa tuḷikoḷ vāykkaṟaṅkum
vicitta naṭipōkum cakaṭak kālum
nīṭṭivali taḷḷiya neṭuṅkayiṟ ṟūcalum       35
alamaru kālum alakit tērum
kuṟitaru piṟaviyiṉ niṟitaru kalakkamum
eṉmaṉat teḻunta puṉmoḻit toṭiyum
aruḷpoḻi kaṭikkaṇ tākki
teruḷuṟa aiya! muṭippiiṉ ṟeṉavē.       40

vēlaṉ vaṇakkam

pāytiri uṭutta ñāla muṭiveṉṉa
muṭaṅkuḷi mukattup paltōḷ avuṇaṉoṭu
miṭiuṭu utira ceṅkaḷam porutu
ñāṭpiṉuḷ maṟintu naṭuvaṟu varattāl
vaṭavi neṭunākkiṉ kiḷikaḷ virinteṉṉa       5
centukir paṭarum tirikkaṭal pukku
kiṭanteri vaṭaviyiṉ taḷirmukam īṉṟu
tirieṟi malikaḷiṉ kavaṭupala pōkki
kalceṟi pāciyiṉ ciṉikkuḻi potuḷi
akaltirip parappiṉ caṭialintu aliyātu       10
kīḻiṇar niṉṟa mēṟpaki māviṉ
ōruṭal iraṇṭu kūṟupaṭa viṭutta
aḻiyāp pēraḷi umikaṇ niṉṟu
taṉpeyar puṇarttik kaṟpiṉoṭu koṭutta
amiyā veṉṟi arattaneṭu vēlōy!       15
kīḻmēl niṉṟaak koṭuntoḻiṟ kokkiṉ
kūṟiraṇ ṭāya orupaṅku eḻuntu
māyāp peruvarattu orumayil āki
puṭavivittu āṟṟiya paltalip pāntaḷ
maṇciṟuka viritta maṇippaṭam tūkki       20
viḻuṅkiya palkatir vāytoṟum umiḻnteṉa
maṇiniri cinti maṇpuka alippa
kār virintu ōṅkiya malittalik katireṉa
ō aṟap pōkiya ciṟiviri mutukil
puvaṉam kāṇap poruḷoṭu polintōy       25
pōḻpaṭak kiṭanta orupaṅku eḻuntu
miṉṉiṉ māṇṭa kaviralar pūtta
ceṉṉi vāraṇak koṭumpaki āki
tēvarmey paṉippuṟa vāṉmiṭi uṭuttiraḷ
poriyiṉ korippa purinta poruḷ nāṭit       30
tāmari paḻitta kimaruṅku amittōy
orumiyuḷ oruṅki iruki neyvārttu
nārataṉ ōmpiya centīk koṭutta
tirukupuri kōṭṭut takarvaru matiyōy!
mulieṉa iraṇṭu muraṇkuvaṭu marīik       35
kuḻaṟkāṭu cumanta yāṉimakaḷ puṇarntōy
ceṅkaṇ kuṟavar karuṅkāṭṭu vaḷartta
piṅkoṭi vaḷḷi paṭarntapuya maliyōy
imayam pūtta cuṉimāṇ toṭṭil
aṟiviṉ taṅki aṟutāy muliyuṇṭu       40
uḻalmatil cuṭṭa taḻalnakip perumāṉ
vaṇaṅkiniṉ ṟētta kurumoḻi vittōy!
ōm eṉum eḻuttiṉ piramam pēciya
nāṉmaṟi vitiyi naṭuṅkuciṟi vittu
paṭippumutal māya vāṉmutal kūṭit       45
tātiyum irappa taḷiatu viṭuttōy
kūṭam cumanta neṭumuṭi nēri
viṇtaṭi yātu maṇpukap putitta
kuṟumuṉi tēṟa neṭumaṟi virittōy
āṟutiru eḻuttum kūṟunili kaṇṭu       50
niṉtāḷ pukaḻunar kaṇṇuḷ polintōy
maṇikkāl aṟiñar peruṅkuṭit tōṉṟi
iṟiyōṉ poruṭkup paraṇar mutalkēṭpa
peruntamiḻ viritta aruntamiḻp pulavaṉum
pāypār aṟiya nīyē ātaliṉ       55
veṭcimalar cūḻnta niṉiru kaḻaṟkāl
kuḻanti aṉpiṉoṭu ceṉṉitali koḷḷutum
aṟivunili kūṭāc cilmoḻi koṇṭu
kaṭavuḷ kūṟā ulavā aruttiyum
caṉaṉap pīḻiyum taḷḷāk kāmamum       60
ataṉpaṭu tuyaramum aṭivukeṭ ṭiṟattalum
teṉpulak kōmakaṉ tītteṟu taṇṭamum
narakoṭu tuṟakkattu uḻalvaru pīḻiyum
nīḷātu impariṉ muṭittu
mīḷāk kāṭci tarutiiṉ ṟeṉavē.       65
------------

1. tamar niṉivu kūṟi varivu kaṭātal

amutamum tiruvum paṇivarap paṭitta
uṭalakkaṇṇaṉ ulaku kavarntu uṇṭa
kaḷavuṭi neṭuñcūr kiḷi kaḷamviṭṭu oḷitta
aruḷniṟintu aminta kalviyar uḷameṉat
tēkkiya tēṉuṭaṉ iṟālmati kiṭakkum       5
eḻumali poṭitta katirili neṭuvēl
vaḷḷi tuṇikkēḷvaṉ puḷḷuṭaṉ makiḻnta
kaṟaṅku kālaruvip paraṅkuṉṟu uṭutta
poṉṉakark kūṭal ceṉṉiyam piṟiyōṉ
potiyap poruppaṉ matiyak karuttiṉi       10
‍koṅkutēr vāḻkkic centamiḻ kūṟi,
poṟkuvi tarumikku aṟpuṭaṉ utavi
eṉuḷam kuṭikoṇṭu irumpayaṉ aḷikkum
kaḷaviḻ kuḻalcēr karuṇiem perumāṉ
malarppatam nīṅkā uḷapperuñ cilampa!       15
kallāk kayavarkku arunūl kiḷimaṟi
colliṉar tōmeṉa tuṇimuli yaruttaṉa
paluṭampu aḻikkum paḻiūṉ uṇaviṉar
tavameṉat tēyntatu tuṭieṉum nucuppē
kaṭavuḷ kūṟār uḷameṉak kuḻalum       20
koṉṟi puṟavuakaṟṟi niṉṟiruḷ kāṭṭiṉa
curumpu paṭintuṇṇum kaḻunīr pōla
kaṟuttuc civantaṉa kaṇiṇi malarē
īṅkivi niṟka cīṟūr peruntamar
illil ceṟikkum colluṭaṉ cilmoḻi
viḷḷum tamiyil kūṟiṉar
uḷḷam kaṟuttuk kaṇcivantu uruttē.      27
------------

2. tāy aṟivu kūṟi varivu kaṭātal

pūmaṇi yāṉi poṉeṉa eṭuttu
tiṅkaḷum puyalum paritiyum cumanta
malivarum kāṭcikku uriya ākaliṉ
niṟiyuṭik kalvi peṟumati māntar
īṉṟa ceṅkavi eṉattōṉṟi naṉiparantu       5
pāriṭi iṉpam, nīḷiṭip payakkum
perunīr viyi vaḷinīrk kūṭal
uṭaluyir eṉṉa uṟitaru nāyakaṉ
kaṭukkimalar māṟṟi vēppalar cūṭi
aivāyk kāppuviṭṭu aṇipūṇ aṇintu       10
viricaṭi maṟittu maṇimuṭi kavittu
viṭikkoṭi niṟuttik kayaṟkoṭi eṭuttu
vaḻuti āki muḻutulaku aḷikkum
pēraruḷ nāyakaṉ cīraruḷ pōla
maṇattuṭaṉ virinta kitiam kāṉal       15
nalattoṭar veṉṟip polampūṇ kuricil
ciṉṉam kiṭanta koṭiñci māṉtēr
noccip pūvutir naḷiruḷ naṭunāḷ
viṇṇam cumantu tōṟṟam ceyteṉa
taṉkaṇ pōlum eṇkaṇ nōkki       20
kaḷvarik kāṇum uḷḷam pōlac
cemmaṉam tiruki uḷḷam tuṭittu
puṟaṉvaḻaṅ kātu neñcoṭu kotittaṉaḷ
māṟāk kaṟpiṉ aṉṉi
kūṟuām matiyat tirunutaṟ koṭiyē!       25
---------------

3. pāṅki añci accuṟuttal

pakiyuṭaṉ kiṭanta nilipiri vaḻakkiṉip
poruttalum pirittalum porupaki kāṭṭalum
uṭpaki amittalum uṇarntucol poruttalum
orutoḻiṟku irupaki tīrātu vaḷarttalum
ceyyā ‍amiccuṭaṉ cērā aracaṉ       5
nāṭu karintaṉṉa kāṭukaṭantu iyaṅki
iṭumpi nirappiṉarkku ītaliṉ iṟantōrkku
itaḻniṟi matuvam tāmari tuḷitteṉa
viḻicori nīruṭaṉ paḻaṅkaṇ koṇṭāl
ulakiyal niṟuttum poruḷmarapu oṭuṅka       10
māṟaṉum pulavarum mayaṅkuṟu kāli
muntuṟum perumaṟi muḷittaruḷ vākkāl
'aṉpiṉ aintiṇi' eṉṟu aṟupatu cūttiram
kaṭalamutu eṭuttuk kariyil vittatupōl
parappiṉ tamiḻccuvi tiraṭṭi maṟṟavarkkut       15
teḷitarak koṭutta teṉtamiḻk kaṭavuḷ
taḻaṟkaṇ tarakkiṉ caruma āṭiyaṉ
kūṭalam perumpati kūṟār kiḷi eṉa
niṟinīrk kayattuḷ tarutāḷ niṉṟu
tāmari tavañceytu aḷiyuṭaṉ peṟṟa
tirumakaṭku aṭuttatueṉ eṉṟu
orumi kāṇkuvar tukirkiḷik koṭiyē!       22
------------

4. pirivu arumi kūṟi varivu kaṭātal

aṇṭamīṉṟu aḷitta kaṉṉi muṉivāka
tirunutal muḷitta kaṉalteṟu nōkkiṉil
āyira maṇikkarattu amittavāṉ paṭiyuṭaṉ
cayampeṟu vīraṉit tantuavaṉ taṉṉāl
uḷḷattu aruḷum teyvamum viṭutta       5
iruḷmaṉat takkaṉ perumakam uṇṇap
pukka tēvarkaḷ porukaṭaṟ paṭiyiṉi
āriya ūmaṉ kaṉaveṉa ākkiya
kūṭal perumāṉ potiyap poruppakattu
aruviam cāral iruviam puṉattiṉum       10
mayilum kiḷiyum kuruviyum naṉṟi
ceykunarp piḻittōrkku uyvuila eṉṉum
kuṉṟā vāymi niṉṟunili kāṭṭit
taṅkuvaṉa kaṇṭum valimaṉam kūṭi
ēkavum tuṇintaṉam emperum paṭiṟu       15
ciṟituniṉṟu iyampa uḻiyiṉam kēṇmiṉiṉṟu
ūṟṟeḻum irukavuḷ perumatat kolimalik
kumpam mūḻki uṭal kuḷittu ōṭa
piṟimati aṉṉa koṭumaram vāṅki,
tōkiyar kaṇeṉac cuṭucaram turakkum       20
emmuṭik kuṉṟavar tammaṉam pukutip
puṉakkuṭik kaṇiyartam malarkki ēṭaviḻttu
varippuṟa aṇilvāl karuntiṉi vaḷikural
koyyum kālamum nāḷpeṟak kuṟittu
niḻalum koṭuttu avarīṉṟa
maḻali makārkkum poṉaṇin taṉarē.       26
----------

5. iḷami kūṟi maṟuttal

iraṇṭuṭal oṉṟāyk karintu kaṇpaṭāmal
aḷaviyal maṇanili parappum kālam
taḷikari kaṭanta kāmak kaṭaluḷ
pulnuṉip paṉiyeṉa maṉṉutal iṉṟi
pīram malarnta vayāvunōy niliyātu       (5)
vaḷikāy viṭṭa puḷiarun tātu
cevvāy tirintu veḷvāy payavātu
maṉipuki yuṇṭa karumaṇ iṭantu
pavaḷa vāyil cuvikā ṇātu
poṟkuṭa mukaṭṭuk karumaṇi amitteṉak       (10)
kuṅkumak koṅkiyum talikkaṇ kaṟātu
malara aviḻnta tāmarik kayaleṉa
varikoṭu matartta kaṇkuḻi yātu
kuṟipaṭu tiṅkaḷ orupatum pukātu
poṉpeyar uṭiyōṉ taṉpeyar keṭuppa       (15)
tūṇam payanta māṇamar kuḻavikku
arakkar kūṭṭattu amarviḷi yāṭa
neruppumiḻ āḻi īntaruḷ nimalaṉ
kūṭal mānakar āṭa eṭutta
viritta tāmari kuvitta tāḷōṉ       (20)
pēraruḷ viḷiyāc cīrilar pōla
tulaṅkiya amutam kalaṅkiya teṉṉa
itaḻkuvittup paṇitta kutali teriyātu
muruntu niritta tiruntupal tōṉṟātu
teyvam koḷḷār tiṇimaṉam eṉṉa,       (25)
viritaru kūḻiyum tirumuṭi kūṭātu
tuṇimīṉ kāṭciyiṉ viḷikaru eṉṉa
pārviyiṉ toḻilkaḷ kūrvaḻi koḷḷātu
maṟupulattu iṭupaki vēntuaṭak kiyateṉa
vaṭutteḻu kolimuli poṭittil aṉṟē       (30)
cemmakaḷ māli immuṟi eṉṟāl
vaḻuttalum varutalum tavirti
moḻikkuṟi kūṭāc cevvē lōyē!       (33)

6. cuvaṭu kaṇṭu iraṅkal

niṇamuyir uṇṭa pulavu poṟātu
taliyuṭal acittu cāṇivāy tuṭittu
neykuḷittu akaṟṟum neṭuvēl viṭali
antaṇar ukumnīrkku aruṭkaru iruntu
kōṭā maṟimoḻi nīṭuṟak kāṇum       (5)
katiruṭal vaḻipōyk kalluḻi niṉṟōr
neruppu uruttaṉṉa ceruttiṟal varinta
vācakam kaṇṭu makiḻntatum ivaṇē;
tuṇiviḷakku eriyum niliviḻip pēḻvāyt
tōki maṇpuṭikkum kāypuli māykka       (10)
vāyceṟit tiṭṭa mākkaṭippu ituvē
ceṭittalik kāruṭal iṭikkura l kirātar
maṟintuṇṭu akkoli makiḻvuḻi innili
tavanati pōkum arumaṟit tāpatar
naṉṉarkoḷ āci nāṭṭiyatu ivvuḻi       (15)
kaṟiyaṇal puyaṅkaṉ eritaḻal viṭatti
malimaṟi atakam māṟṟiya atupōl
koṭumarak koliñar āṟṟiṭik kavara,
eṇṇātu kiṭitta puṇeḻu cerunilik
kivaḷar koḻuntu meypoṭi yākeṉa       (20)
ciṟṟiṭip perumulip poṟṟoṭi maṭantitaṉ
kaviiya kaṟpiṉik kāṭṭuḻi ituvē
kuravam cumanta kuḻalvirittu iruntu
pāṭalam puṉintakaṟ patukki iviṭaṉē
oṭṭuviṭṭu ulaṟiya parāri neṭṭākkōṭṭu       (25)
utirpaṟi eruvi uṇavuūṉ taṭṭi
vaḷivāyk karumparuntu iṭipaṟik tuṇṇak
kaṇṭuniṉṟu uvanta kāṭciyum ituvē
cemmaṇic cilampum marakatap poruppum
kuṭumiam taḻalum avaṇiruṭ kuviyum       (30)
muḷivarum pakaṉum ataṉiṭi mēkamum
cēyitaḻ muḷariyum kāritaḻk kuvaḷiyum
ōruḻik kaṇṭa uvakiyatu eṉṉa
evvuyir niṟinta cevvikoḷ mēṉiyiṉ
aṇṭap peruntiraṉ aṭivuīṉṟu aḷitta       (35)
kaṉṉi koṇṭirunta maṉṉaruṭ kaṭavuḷ
maliuruk koṇṭa uṭalvāḷ arakkar
veḷḷamum cūrum puḷḷiyal poruppum
‍neṭuṅkaṭaṟ kiṭaṅkum oruṅkuyir parukiya
maṇivēṟ kumaraṉ mutalnili vāḻum       (35)
kuṉṟuṭuttu ōṅkiya kūṭalam patiyōṉ
tāḷtali taritta kōḷiṉar pōla
neṭuñcuram nīṅkat tamkāl
aṭumtaḻal māṟṟiya kālkuṟi ivaṇē.       (39)

7. naṟṟāya varuntal

poṭittarum pātaciṉ mulikkoṭi maṭantiyaḷ
maṇimiḷir peruṅkaṭku imikāppu eṉṉa
viḻittuḻi viḻittum aṭaṅkuḻi aṭaṅkiyum
taṉṉiniṉṟu aḷitta eṉṉiyum oruvuka
palmaṇik kalaṉkaḷ uṭaṟkuaḻaku aḷitteṉa       (5)
cuṟṟuṭuttu ōṅkiya āyamum tuṟakkuka
piṇimuka maññi cerumukattu ēntiya
mūviru tirumukattu oruvēl avaṟku
vāṉuṟa nimirnta malittali muṉṟiliṉ
maṉavuaṇi maṭanti veṟiyāṭ ṭāḷaṉ       (10)
vēlmakaṉ kuṟatti māmati mutiyōḷ
toṇṭakam tuvippa murukiyam kaṟaṅka
oruṅkuvantu imiyā aruṅkaṭaṉ muṟṟiya
piṉṉarniṉṟu eṟṟakit tāyiyum piḻikkuka
karuntalic cāriki cevvāyp pacuṅkiḷi       (15)
tūviam tōki veḷōtimam toṭaruḻi
iviyuṭaṉ iṉpamum oruvaḻi iḻakkuka
cēyitaḻ ilavattu uṭikāyp pañci
pukimurintu eḻunteṉa viṇṇattu alamara
kuḻipoṭi kūviyiṉ ciṟiciṟi tīnta       (20)
paruntum āntiyum pārppuṭaṉ tavaḻa
uṭikavaṭṭu ōmi ularciṉi irukkum
vaḷikaṭ kūkiyum mayaṅki vāykuḻaṟa
āciyiṉ taṇiyā aḻalpaci taṇikkak
kāḷimuṉ kāval kāṭṭivittu ēkum       (25)
kuḻikaṭ karumpēy makavukaṇ mukiḻppa
vēmuṭal ciṉṉam veḷḷiṭi teṟippa
neṭuntāṭ kuṟṟili vākineṟṟu olippa
ticiniṉṟu eḻātu taḻalmukal teṟippa
cuṭaliyil cūṟi iṭiiṭi aṭikkum       (30)
pēraḻaṟ kāṉiṉum nāṭumeṉ uḷattiṉum
orupāl pacuṅkoṭi niṟipāṭṭu ayara
pāriṭam kuṉippa āṭiya perumāṉ
viyakattu uruviṉar malarā aṟiviṉip
pulaṉniri maṟitta puṇarppu atupōla       (35)
kuḷirkoṇṭu uṟiyum teḷinīr vāviyi
vaḷḷi ceṅkamalam kaḷḷaviḻ āmpal
pācaṭi maṟikkum kūṭal perumāṉ
centāḷ viṭuttuṟi antarkaḷ tammiṉum
mūvāt taṉinilikku iruvarum ōruyir       (40)
iraṇṭeṉak kavittanal laraṇtaru tōḻiyi
ceruviḻam icciyar tamatuṭal peṟṟa
iṉpukaḷ nōkkā iyalpatu pōla
maruṅkupiṉ ‍nōkkātu oruṅkuviṭṭu akala
poruntiyatu eppaṭi uḷḷam
aruntaḻaṟ curattiṉ oruvaṉ aṉpu eṭuttē?       (46)

8. celavu niṉinturittal

uyirpukum caṭṭakam uḻitoṟum uḻitoṟum
paḻaviṉi pukunta pāṭakam pōla
mutirpuyal kuḷiṟum eḻumali pukka
kaṭṭuṭic cūruṭal kāmam koṇṭu
paṟṟi uṭpukuntu pacuṅkaṭal kaṇṭu       (5)
māvoṭum koṉṟa maṇineṭun tiruvēl
cēvalam koṭiyōṉ kāvalkoṇ ṭirunta
kuṉṟam uṭutta kūṭalam patiiṟi
toṭarntu uyirvavviya viṭamkeḻu miṭaṟṟōṉ
puṇṇiyam taḻitta muṉōr nāḷil       (10)
iruviral nimirttup purivoṭu cērtti
kuḻivuṭal taliviri kittiri kaṟaṅka
oru viral teṟittum aiviral kuvittum
peruvāy orumukap paṭakam perukka
taṭāvuṭal umpart talipeṟum muḻavam       (15)
nāṉmukam taṭṭi naṭumukam urappa
oruvāy tiṟantu uḷkaṭippu uṭalvicitta
callari aṅkit taliviral tākka
kayantali aṭieṉa kayiṟami kittiri
iruviral uyarttic cerunili iraṭṭa       (20)
irutali kuvinta neṭṭuṭal taṇṇumi
orumukam tāḻtti irukaṭippu olippa
tirumalar eḻutiya variirupat tintu
aṅkuli iraṇṭiraṇṭu aṇittuviḷar niṟīi
mummukak kayaluṭaṉ mayirkkayiṟu vicitta       (25)
kalla vaṭattiraḷ viraltali kaṟaṅka
marakkāl aṉṉa oruvāyk kōti
mukattiṉum taṭṭa mūkkiṉum tākka
nāṭiru muṉivarkku āṭiya perumāṉ
tiruvaṭi viṉavāk karuvuṟi mākkaḷ       (30)
neñciṉam kiṭantu nīṇṭaval iravil
cellavum uriyam tōḻi nillātu
emetirvu iṉṟi iruntu etirppaṭṭu
maṟivaḻi oḻukā maṉṉavaṉ vāḻum
paḻināṭṭu ārnta pāvam pōlac       (35)
cēra maṟinta kūriruḷ naṭunāḷ
aritiṉ pōntaṉir eṉṟōr
peritiṉ vāymi veṟpaṉiṉ peṟiṉē!       (38)

9. tūtu kaṇṭu aḻuṅkal

vaḷintuniṉṟu uṭaṟṟum malikuḷirkku uṭintu
mukiltukil mūṭi maṇineruppu aṇittup
puṉameri kārakil pukipala koḷḷum
kuḷavaṉ vīṟṟirunta vaḷarpukaḻk kuṉṟamum
putavu toṭṭeṉat taṉpuyal mutirkarattiṉi       (5)
vāṉmuṟi ceyta kūṉmatik kōvum
teyvam amitta ceḻuntamiḻp pāṭalum
aintiṉil paṅkuceytu iṉpuvaḷar kuṭiyum
tavalarum ciṟappoṭu cālpuceytu aminta
mutunakark kūṭaluḷ mūvāt taṉimutal       (10)
ēḻici mutalil āyiram kiḷitta
kāṉam kāṭṭum puḷaṭit tuṇiyiṉar
paṭṭaṭi eṭuttu, pāliyil koḷuvi
kiḷiyil kāṭṭi aimmuṟi kiḷatti
kuralum pāṇiyum neytalil kumaṭṭi       (15)
viḷari eṭuttu mattimi vilakki
oṟṟit tāri oru narampu iraṭṭa
viḻuntum eḻuntum cevvaḻi cērtti
kuruviviṇ icikkum antarak kulitam
puṟappaṭu potuvuṭaṉ mulliyil kūṭṭi       (20)
virintavum kuvintavum viḷariyil vittu
tūṅkalum acittalum tuḷḷalum olittalum
āṅkavi nāṉkum aṇivuḻi ākki
pūrakam kumpakam puṭieḻu viḷari
tuttam tāram kikkiḷi ataṉukku       (25)
oṉṟiṉukku ēḻu niṉṟunaṉi virittu
taṉimukam malarntu tamici pāṭa
kūḷiyum tuḷḷa āṭiya nāyakaṉ
iṇiaṭi ēttum iṉpiṉarkku utavum
tiruvaṟam vanta oruvaṉ tūtukaḷ       (30)
iṉpamum iyaṟkiyum ikaḻāk kāmamum
aṉpum cūḷum aḷiyuṟat tantueṉ
neñcamum tuyilum niṉivum uḷḷamum
nāṇamum koṇṭa naṭuviṉar iṉṉum
koḷvatum uḷatō koṭuppatum uḷatō?       (35)
cēykuṟi iṉiya āyiṉ
kavviyiṉ kūṟuvir maṟikaḷ viṭṭemakkē.       (37)

10. aṟattoṭu niṟṟal

taṉṉuḻip palavuyir taṉittaṉi paṭittup
parappik kāṭṭaliṉ patumaṉ ākiyum
avvuyir evvuyir aṉittum kāttaliṉ
cevvikoḷ karumukil celvaṉ ākiyum
kaṭṭiya karivarampu uṭpuka aḻittu       (5)
nīrtali tarittaliṉ nimalaṉ ākiyum
taruvum maṇiyum caṅkamum kiṭittaliṉ
arimutir amararkku aracaṉ ākiyum
mūṉṟaḻal nāṉmaṟi muṉivar tōyntu
maṟinīr ukuttaliṉ maṟiyōṉ ākiyum       (10)
mīṉum koṭiyum viritiṇi aintum
tēṉuṟi tamiḻum tiruvuṟi kūṭalum
maṇattaliṉ matikkula maṉṉavaṉ ākiyum
navamaṇi eṭuttu naṉpulam kāṭṭaliṉ
vaḷarkuṟi mayaṅkā vaṇikaṉ ākiyum       (15)
viḻitarum uḻavum vittum nāṟum
taḻitaliṉ vēḷāṇ talivaṉ ākiyum
viritiri viyit tirunati cūḻnta
maturiyam patiniṟi mimmalark kaḷattiṉaṉ
iṇiaṭi vaḻuttār aṇitoḻil eṉṉa       (20)
kitiyam karicēr poytaṟ pāviyōṭu
irutiri eṭukkap porutiri eṭuttum
pūḻip pōṉim potuvuṭaṉ uṇṭum
cāytāḷ piḷḷi tantu koṭuttum
muṭavuṭaṟ kiti maṭalmuṟit tiṭṭum       (25)
kavittukirp pāvi kaṇṇi cūṭak
kuvalayat tirumalar koṇarntu koṭuttum
niṉṟāṉ uṇṭoru kāḷi
eṉṟāl, ittoḻil ceyvatu pukaḻē?       (24)

11. parattiyiṟ pirivu kaṇṭavar kūṟal

vaṭiviḻic ciṟṟiṭip perumuli maṭavīr
toḻumiṉ vaṇaṅkumiṉ cūḻmiṉ toṭarmiṉ
kaṭṭutir kōti kaṭimalar aṉpoṭu
muṇṭaka mukiyiṉ mulimukam tarumiṉ
uruḷiṉ pūmi uḷḷuṟa āṭumiṉ       (5)
etirmiṉ iṟiñcumiṉ ēttumiṉ iyaṅkumiṉ
karuppuram tutinta kalluyar maṇittōḷ
vācam paṭarum maruttiṉum uṟumiṉ
peruṅkaviṉ muṉnāḷ pēṇiya aruntavam
kaṇṇiṭi uḷattiṭi kāṇmiṉ karutumiṉ       (10)
pūvum cuṇṇamum pukaḻntetir eṟimiṉ
yāḻil paravumiṉ īṅkivi aṉṟi
kalattum eṉṟeḻumiṉ kaṇṇaḷi kāṇmiṉ
veṇcuṭar ceñcuṭar ākiya viṇṇoṭu
puvipuṉal aṉalkāl matipula vōṉeṉa       (15)
muḻutum niṟinta mukkaṭ perumāṉ
paṉikkatir kulavaṉ payantaruḷ pāviyit
tirupperu vatuvi poruntiya annāḷ
coṉṟip perumali tiṉṟunaṉi tolitta
kāruṭal ciṟunakik kuṟuntāṭ pāriṭam       (20)
āṟṟātu alinta nīrnaci aṭakka
maṟitirip perunati varavaḻittu aruḷiya
kūṭalam patiuṟi kuṇapperuṅ kaṭavuḷ
muṇṭakam alarttum mutirāc cēvaṭi
taritta uḷḷat tāmari ūraṉ       (25)
poṉtuṇart tāmam purintoḷir maṇittēr
vīti vantatu varalāṉnum
ētam tīra irumaruṅku eḻuntē.       (28)

12. kalvi nalam kūṟal

niliyiṉiṉ caliyā nilimi yāṉum
palulaku eṭutta orutiṟat tāṉum
niṟiyum poṟiyum peṟumnili yāṉum
tēvar mūvarum kāva lāṉum
tamaṉiyap parāric cayilam ākiyum       (5)
aḷakkeṉṟu amiyāp parappiṉa tāṉum
amutamum tiruvum utavuta lāṉum
palatuṟi mukattoṭu payiluta lāṉum
muḷḷuṭik kōṭṭu muṉieṟi cuṟavam
atirvaḷi taṭiyum aḷakkar ākiyum       (10)
niṟivuḷam karuti nikaḻpavi nikaḻpavi
tarutaliṉ vāṉat taruaintu ākiyum
maṟiveḷip paṭuttaliṉ kalimakaḷ iruttaliṉ
akamalar vāḻtaliṉ piramaṉ ākiyum
uyirparintu aḷittaliṉ pulamici pōkkaliṉ       (15)
paṭimuḻutu aḷanta neṭiyōṉ ākiyum
iṟutiyil caliyātu irutta lāṉum
maṟumitantu utavum irumi yāṉum
peṇiṭam kalanta puṇṇiyaṉ ākiyum
aruḷvaḻi kāṭṭaliṉ iruviḻi ākiyum       (20)
koḷḷunar koḷḷak kuṟiyātu ātaliṉ
niṟivuḷam nīṅkātu uṟiaruḷ ākiyum
ivimutal āki iruviṉi keṭukkum
puṇṇiyak kalvi uḷnikaḻ mākkaḷ
paripurak kampali irucevi uṇṇum       (25)
kuṭakkōc cēraṉ kiṭittuitu kāṇkeṉa
matimali puricit tirumukam kūṟi
aṉpuurut taritta iṉpuicip pāṇaṉ
peruniti koṭukkeṉa uṟaviṭut taruḷiya
mātavar vaḻuttum kūṭaṟku iṟivaṉ       (30)
irucaraṇ perukunar pōla
perumati nīṭuvar; ciṟumati nutalē!       (32)

13. muṉ nikaḻvu urittu ūṭal tīrttal

kuravam malarnta kuviiruḷ kuḻalī!
iruvēm orukāl eriatar iṟantu
viritali tōlmuli veḷvāy eyiṟṟiyarkku
arumputu virunteṉap poruntimaṟṟu avartarum
iṭiyum tuyttu curikkuṭam eṭuttu       (5)
nīḷnilik kūval teḷipuṉal uṇṭum
paḻampul kurampi yiṭampukku iruntum
muṭaṅkuataḷ uṟutta mukiḻnaki eytiyum
uṭaṉuṭaṉ payanta kaṭoli ēṟṟum
naṭimali eyiṟṟiṉ iṭittali vittum       (10)
uyarnta iṉpataṟku oṉṟuvamum uṇṭeṉiṉ
mulimūṉṟu aṇinta cilinutal tiruviṉi
arumaṟi vitikkat tirumaṇam puṇarntu
matikkulam vāytta maṉṉavaṉ āki
mētiṉi purakkum vitiyuṭi nalnāḷ       (15)
naṭuvūr nakarceytu aṭupavam tuṭikkum
aruṭkuṟi niṟuvi aruccaṉi ceyta
tēva nāyakaṉ kūṭalvāḻ iṟivaṉ
muṇṭakam malartti murukaviḻ irutāḷ
uṟikunar uṇṇum iṉpamē
aṟiyal aṉṟimaṟṟu oṉṟiṉum aṭātē!       (21)

14. nilavu veḷippaṭa varuntal

naṇṇiya pāti peṇṇiṉarkku amutam
aṭumaṭip paḷḷiyiṉ naṭuava tarittum
tiruvaṭivu eṭṭaṉuḷ oruvaṭivu ākiyum
mukkaṇil aruṭkaṇ muṟipeṟa muyaṅkiyum
paṭiitu eṉṉā aṭimuṭi kaṇṭum       (5)
puṇṇiya nīṟueṉap polikatir kāṟṟiyum
niṉṟaṉi perumati! niṉtoḻu tēṟkum
naṉṉariṉ ceykuṟum naṉṟioṉṟu uḷatāl
āyiram taḻaṟkarattu iruṭpaki maṇṭilattu
ororu paṉikkali oṭuṅkiniṉṟu aṭitaliṉ       (10)
kolinuti eyiṟueṉṟu irupiṟi muḷitta
pukarmukap puḻikki oruvici taṭintum
matuitaḻk kuvaḷieṉṟu aṭukaṇ malarnta
neṭuñcuṉi putiya pukunteṭuttu aḷittum
ceṟipiṟappu iṟappeṉa iruvaki tiriyum       (15)
neṭuṅkayiṟṟu ūcal parintukaluḻ kāli
muṉṉiyiṉ puṉintum mukamaṉ aḷittum
tantem kuricil taṉivantu ematu
kaṇeṉak kiṭittuem kaṇetir naṭunāḷ
camayak kaṇakkar mativaḻi kūṟātu       (20)
ulakiyal kūṟi poruḷitu eṉṟa
vaḷḷuvaṉ taṉakku vaḷarkavip pulavarmuṉ
mutaṟkavi pāṭiya mukkaṭ perumāṉ
mātuṭaṉ tōṉṟik kūṭaluḷ niṟintōṉ
taṉṉiniṉ ṟuṇarntu tāmum oṉṟiṉṟi       (25)
aṭaṅkiṉar pōla nīyum
oṭuṅkiniṉ ṟamiti innili aṟintē!       (27)

15. tēr varavu kūṟal

caliyāp parārit tamaṉiyap poruppeṉum
orukāl cumanta viṇpaṭar pantariṉ
mūṭiya nāltici mukiltukil virittu
poṟcili vaḷittu vāyil pōkki
curuppaṇi niritta kaṭukkiam polantār       (5)
niri niri nāṟṟi neṭuṅkāy mayir amittu
ūtiyil alakiṭṭu uṟipuyal teḷittu
pōṟṟuṟu tiruvam nālticip poliya
marakatat taṇṭiṉ tōṉṟi viḷakkeṭuppa
kuṭattiyar iḻukkiya aḷicita ṟiyapōl       (10)
kiṭantaṉa āmpi parantaṉa maṟippa
piṭavalar parappip pūvi pūiṭa
uyarvāṉ aṇṭar kiḷiviyap peyta
uṟavuiṇi naṭpu kiḷiviyap peyta
mukilmuḻavu atira ēḻicimukakkum       (15)
mulli yāḻoṭu curutivaṇṭu alampa
kaḷavalar cūṭi puṟavupāṭ ṭeṭuppa
pacuntaḻi parappik kaṇamayil āla
mulliyam tirumakaḷ kōpamvāy malarntu
nalmaṇam eṭuttu nāḷamittu aḻikka       (20)
varivaḷi muṉki varavara iṟappap
pōṉanam taṉinamar puḷiyal māṉtēr
kaṭuvici turanta kāṉyāṟ ṟoliyiṉ
eḷḷiṉar uṭka vaḷiṉam maṭakkimuṉ
tōṉṟiṉar ātaliṉ nīyē maṭamakaḷ!       (25)
muṉoru kālattu aṭukolikku aṇinta
mukilurup peṟumōr koṭumarak kirātaṉ
aṟumaṟit tāpataṉ amittiru cemmali
ceruppuṭit tāḷāl viruppuṭaṉ taḷḷi
vāyeṉum kuṭattil varampaṟa eṭutta       (30)
aḻutukaṭal taḷḷum maṇinīr āṭṭi
piṉṉalviṭ ṭamitta taṉtali mayiraṇi
tirumalar viṇpuka maṇimuṭi niṟittu
veḷvāy kutaṭṭiya viḻutuṭik karuntaṭi
vittami yāmuṉ makiḻntamutu uṇṭavaṉ       (35)
miccilukku iṉṉum iccicey perumāṉ
kūṭalniṉ ṟēttiṉar kulakkiḷi pōlat
tuṇarpeṟu kōtiyum āramum puṉika
putiyiruḷ turakkum veyil maṇit tiruvum
taṇṇam piṟiyum talipeṟa niṟuttuka       (35)
iṟiiruntu utavā niṟivaḷik kulaṉum
peruñcū ṭakamum oruṅkupeṟ ṟaṇika
naṭṭup pakiyiṉar uṭkuṭi pōla
uṟavuceytu oṉṟā nakitarum uḷattiyum
koliyiṉar neñcam kūṇṭaval iruḷeṉum       (40)
aimpāl kuḻaliyum aṇinili kūṭṭuka
viruntukoṇ ṭuṇṇum peruntavar pōla
nīṅkāt tiruvuṭi nalaṉum
pāṅkil kūṭṭuka iṉpattil polintē!       (44)

16. aḻuṅku tāykku urittal

kalluyar varitōḷ cemmaṉak kuricilum
kallā tavaruḷam pulliya kuḻalum
immaṉi niṟipukuntu eḻilmaṇam puṇara
kōḷoṭu kuṟittu varumvaḻi kūṟiya
maṟivāyp pārppāṉ makaṉum paḻutilaṉ       (5)
cōtiṭak kalimakaḷ tōṟṟam pōla
coriveḷ alakarum paḻutil vāymiyar
uṭaltoṭu kuṟiyiṉ varumvaḻi kuṟitta
mūtaṟi peṇṭirum tītilar eṉpa
peruntiraḷ kaṇṇuḷ pēccuniṉ ṟōrntu       (10)
vāyccol kēṭṭanal matiyarum periyar
āymalar terintuiṭṭu vāṉpali tūvi
teyvam parāya meyyarum tiruviṉar
karuṅkoṭi aṭampum kaṇṭalum cūḻnta
paṉikkuṭip paratavar kalattoṭum maṟiya       (15)
curimukac cevvāyc cūlvaḷi teṟippa
kaḻukkaṭi aṉṉa kūrvāyp peruṅkaṇ
paṉikiṭan taṉṉa uṭalmutal tuṇiya
āruyir kavarum kāruṭal ceṅkaṇ
kūṟṟam urutteḻunta koḷki pōla       (20)
neṭṭuṭal pēḻvāyp peruñcuṟavu taṭiyum
variniri kiṭanta tirivuvar pukuntu
neṭuñcaṭik kiṭanta kuṟumpiṟik koḻuntum
karumukil veḷutta tirumiṭaṟṟu iruḷum
nutalmati kiḻitta aḻalavīr nōkkamum       (25)
maṟittoru ciṟukuṭip paratavaṉ āki
pontalip puṇarvali koṭuṅkaram ākki
neṭuṅkaṭal kalakkum orumīṉ paṭutta
niṟiaruḷ nāyakaṉ uṟitaru kūṭal
vaṇaṅkār iṉameṉa māḻki,
kuṇamkuṭi pōyvitta āyvuḷam tavaṟē.       (31)

17. veṟi vilakkal

uḻiniṉ ṟīrum piḻiaṟin tīrum
paḻaṅkuṟi kaṇṭa neṭuṅkaṇ mātarum
oṉṟu kiḷakka niṉṟivi kēṇmiṉ
orupāl pacuṅkoṭi tirunutal poṭitta
kuṟuveyirppu oḻukku eṉappiṟi amuteṭukka       (5)
paṭiṟar coleṉak kaṭuvunañcu iṟippa
aṇṭap poṟcuvar koṇṭa aḻukki
iṟittuk kaḻuvuvatu eṉṉak kaṅkit
tuṟikoḷ āyiram mukamum cuḻala
apperuṅ kaṅki kakkiya tirieṉak       (10)
kokkiṉ tūval appuṟam āka
māṇik kattiṉ vaḷitta cuvareṉap
pāṇik kuḷpey centaḻal parappa
taṉṉāl paṭitta poṉaṇi aṇṭam
eṇtikku aḷantu koṇṭaṉa eṉṉap       (15)
purinta ceñcaṭi nimirntu cuḻala
mēruviṉ muṭicūḻ cūriyar eṉṉat
taṅkiya mūṉṟukaṇ eṅkaṇum āka
kūṭal mānakar āṭiya amuti
uṇṭu kaḷitta toṇṭarkaḷ eṉṉa       (20)
immatu uṇṇa ummiyiṉ uṭiyōr
muruka nāṟap parukutal ceyka
vēlaṉum veṟikkaḷaṉ ēṟutal āka
aṇaṅkāṭṭu mutiyōḷ muṟaṅkoḷ neleṭukka
piṇitara vicitta murukiyam tuvikka       (25)
aiyavi aḻaloṭu ceyyiṭam pukikka
iṉṉumpala toḻiṟku innili niṉṟu
māṟu pāṭu kūṟutal ilaṉē
īṅkivi niṟka yāṅkaḷav aruviyil
oḻuka pukkut taḻuvi eṭuttum       (30)
orumati muṟittuāṇṭu irukavuṭ cerukiya
ēntukōṭṭu umpal pūmpuṉam emuyir
aḻikkap pukunta kaṭikkoḷ nāḷil
neṭuṅki vēlāl aṭumtoḻil ceytu
peṟumuyir tantu maruvi aḷitta       (35)
poṉneṭuṅkuṉṟam maṉṉiya tōḷaṉ
cevvē tantami tuyari ruppa
kūṟu peyaroṭu vēṟu peyariṭṭu
maṟiuyir uṇṇak kuṟuki vantirunta
teyvam kaṟṟa aṟivi
uyyak kūṟilōr neñciṭam poṟātē.       (41)

18. ulakiyalpu urittal

paḻami nīṇṭa kuṉṟak kuṭiyiṉaṉ
varuntātu vaḷarttum kuṭaṅki tuyiṟṟiyum
māṉiṉ kuḻaviyoṭu keṭavaral varuttiyum
pantu payiṟṟiyum poṟkaḻaṅku untavum
pāvi cūṭṭavum pūvi kēṭkavum       (5)
uṭimi ceyta maṭamiyaḷ yāṉeṉa
emetir kūṟiya immoḻi taṉakkup
perumi nōkkiṉ ciṟumiyatu uṇṭē
ceṟitirip pāṟkaṭal vayiṟunontu īṉṟa
cemmakaḷ kariyōṟku aṟuti pōka       (10)
makaviṉ iṉpam kaṭal ceṉṟilavāl
aṉṟiyum viṭimīṉ muḷitta taraḷam
vavviṉa riṭattum avvaḻi āṉa
tirikkaṭal kuṭitta karattamā muṉikkum
tiṅkaḷ vāḻkulam taṅkum vēntaṟkum       (15)
amutūṟ ṟeḻuntu neñcam kaḷikkum
tamiḻeṉum kaṭalik kāṇi koṭutta
potiyap poruppum neṭumutuku varuntip
peṟṟu vaḷartta kalpuṭi āram
aṇiyum māmakiḻnar patiyuṟi pukuntāl       (20)
uṇṭō ceṉṟatu kaṇṭatu urikka
paḷḷik kaṇakkar uḷḷattup peṟṟa
puṟamār kalvi aṟamā makaḷik
koṇṭu vāḻunark kaṇṭu arukiṭattum
avarmaṉa aṉṉi kavarak kaṇṭilam       (25)
peruñcēṟṟuk kaḻaṉi karumpupeṟu kāli
koḷvōrk kaṉṟi avvayal cāyā
pūmpaṇi tirintu potiaviḻ muḷariyil
kāmpupoti naṟavam viḷariyō ṭarunti
kantit taṇṭali vantu vīṟṟiruntu       (30)
kaṭimalarp poḻilil ciṟitukaṇ paṭuttu
mayakkaniṟi kāmattu iyakkam koṇṭu
niṉṟa nāraṇaṉ paranta mārpil
kalavāk kuṅkumam nilaviya teṉṉak
kārvāṉ tanta pērkoḷ cekkaril       (35)
vītivāyt teṉṟal melleṉ ṟiyaṅkum
mūtūrk kūṭal vantaruḷ mukkaṇaṉ
kāmaṉi ayaṉi nāmak kālaṉi
kaṇṇāl ukirāl malarkoḷ kālāl
cuṭṭum koytum utittum tuṇitta       (40)
viṭṭoḷir māṇikka maliyiṉ orupāl
aṭaṅkap paṭarnta pacuṅkoṭi ataṉi
vaḷarnta cēṇmali uḷattuyar koṇṭu
toṭarntatum ilikīḻ naṭantacol kiṭakka
pālik kiḻatti tirumuṉ nāṭṭiya       (45)
cūlat taliyiṉ toṭarntuciki paṭarntu
viṭutaḻal uccam paṭukatir tākka
pāṭalcāl paccik kōṭakak kāṟṟi
miyil kāṭcik koyyuḷi niṟpa
vayiṟṟil iruntu vāymuḷit teṉṉa       (50)
irukāl mukaṉiṟku arukā turantu
paṭumaḻal nīkkak kuṭakaṭal kuḷikkum
nāvāya kuṟiyāt tīvāy pāliyil
tammil iṉpam cūḷuṭaṉ kūṭi
oṉṟi viḻintu ceṉṟāṭku uṭittu       (55)
poṉpati nīṅki uṇpatum aṭaṅki
muḻaṅkap peruṅkural kūuyp
paḻaṅkaṇ eytiyatu pētimi aṟivē.       (58)

19. makiḻntu urittal

kuṅkumak kōṭṭualar uṇaṅkal kaṭukkum
paṅkuṭic ceṅkāl pāṭṭaḷi aripiṭark
kuruvil tōynta arikeḻu marakatak
kaleṉak kiṭappac colliya mēṉit
tiruneṭu mā kku oruvici purintu       (5)
cōtivaḷar pākam īntaruḷ nittaṉ
muṉivar ēmuṟa veḷḷiam potuvil
maṉamum kaṇṇum kaṉiyak kuṉikkum
putiya nāyakaṉ paḻamaṟit taliyōṉ
kiññiṉṟavaṉ ceṅkāl kaṇṭavar pōla       (10)
viḷakkamum putumiyum aḷappil kāṭciyum
vēṟoppu eṭuttuk kūṟuvatu nīkkamum
aṟivōr kāṇum kuṟiyāya iruntaṉa
iruntiṇ pōrvip piṇivici muracam
muṉṉam eḷḷiṉar neñcukeṭat tuvippa       (15)
maṇamkoḷ pēraṇi peruṅkaviṉ maṟittatu eṉṟu
eḻumati kuṟitta muḻumatik karuṅkayal
vaṇṭu maruvi uṇṭu kaḷiyātu
maṟṟatu pūtta poṉtikaḻ tāmari
iraṇṭu mukiḻceytu neñcuṟap perukum       (20)
vaṟṟā mēṉi veḷḷattuḷ maṟiya
nuṉittali antaṇar kataḻeri vaḷarttuc
civanta vāytoṟum veṇpori citaṟic
cemmāntu maṇatta vaḷariya kūreri
mummuṟi cuḻaṉṟu tāyār uḷmakiḻa       (25)
illuṟi kalliṉ veṇmalar parappi
ilavalar vāṭṭiya ceṅkāl piṭittu
kaḷitūṅku uḷattoṭum melleṉac cērtti
iraṇṭupeyar kātta tōlāk kaṟpu
mukaṉuṟak kāṇum kariyōr pōla       (30)
iṭappāl niṟutti pakkam cūḻa
vaṭamīṉ kāṭṭi viḷakkaṇi eṭuttuk
kulavāḻttu vimma maṇaaṇip pakkam
kaṭpulam koṇṭa ippaṇi aḷavum
vāṭi niliniṉṟum ūṭi ēmāntum       (35)
eṉmukam aḷakkum kālak kuṟiyit
tāmarik kaṇṇāl uṭpuka aṟintum
ulakam mūṉṟum peṟutaṟku ariyateṉṟu
eṇṇā vāymi eṇṇik kūṟiyum
kalluyar neṭuntōḷ aṇṇal,
malluṟat tanta īrntaḻi tāṉē.       (41)

20. piṟi toḻukeṉṟal

neṭuvaḷi uyirttu maḻimatam oḻukki
eḻumali viḻumali puṭimaṇi āka
mīṉpukar niṟinta vāṉkuñ caramukam
vālpeṟa muḷitta kūṉkōṭu āṉum
pēca nīṇṭa palmīṉ niliiya       (5)
vāṉak kaṭalil tōṇi atuāṉum
koḻunar kūṭum kāma uta*tiyik
kariviṭa ukiyum nāvāy āṉum
kaḷḷamar kōtiyar veḷḷaṇi viḻavil
aiṅkaṇik kiḻavaṉ kāṭciyuḷ makiḻa       (10)
iḻittu vaḷitta karuppu villāṉum
neṭiyōṉ mutalām tēvar kūṭi
vāṅkik kaṭinta tēmpaṭu kaṭalil
aḻutuṭaṉ tōṉṟiya urimi yāṉum
nintiru nutali oḷivicumpu uṭalil       (15)
āṭiniḻal kāṭṭiya pīṭuatu vāṉum
kariaṟa aṇiyum māṉak kalaṉuḷ
talipeṟa irunta nilipuka ḻāṉum
maṇṇakam aṉittum niṟintapal uyirkaṭku
āyā amutam īkuta lāṉum       (20)
pāṟkaṭal uṟaṅkum māyavaṉ pōla
tavaḷa māṭattu akalmutuku paṟṟi
neṭuṅkār kiṭantu paṭumpuṉal piḻiyum
kūṭal vīṟṟirunta nāṭakak kaṭavuḷ
poṉcuṭar viritta kottalar koṉṟiyum       (25)
tāḷiyum aṟukum vāluḷi erukkamum
karantiyum vaṉṉiyum miṭintaceñ caṭiyil
iraṇṭuaiñ ñūṟu tiraṇṭamukam eṭuttu
maṇpilaṉ akaḻntu tikkunili mayakki
puriyāk katamōṭu orupāl aṭaṅkum       (30)
kaṅkiyil paṭinta poṅkutavat tāṉum
anneṭu vēṇiyiṉ kaṇṇieṉa irun*tu
tūṟṟum maṟuoḻinta ēṟṟat tāṉum
maṇivāṉ peṟṟip piṟiyip
paṇivāy purintu tāmari makaḷē!       (35)

21. āṟṟāmi kūṟal

poruppumali tōḷiṉum neruppumiḻ vēliṉum
cenlru makaḷi ceyamkoḷ maṅkiyi
vaṟṟāk kātaliṉ koṇṭamati aṉṟi
kaḷavu alartūṟṟa taḷavukoṭi naṭuṅka
vēyuḷam paṭṭup pūvi kaṟukka       (5)
taṇṭā mayalkoṭu vaṇṭuparantu araṟṟa
kālam karutit tōṉṟiki kulippa
tuṉpu pacappūrum kaṇniḻal taṉṉit
tirumalar eṭuttuk koṉṟi kāṭṭa
iṟivaḷi nillātu eṉpaṉa nilikka       (10)
kōṭal vaḷinta vaḷḷalar ukuppa
kaṇtuḷi tuḷikkum cāyāp piyuḷi
kūṟupaṭa nāṭi āciyoṭu mayaṅki
karuviḷi malarnīr arukuniṉ ṟukuppa
pēraḻal vāṭi āruyir taṭava       (15)
viḷikkum kālam muḷitta kāli
aṉpum cūḷum naṇpum naṭuniliyum
taṭiyā aṟivum uṭiyōy nīyē
eḻuntu kāṭṭip pāṭucey katirpōl
tōṉṟi nillā nilipporuḷ ceyya       (20)
maruṅkil pāti tarumtukil puṉintum
viḷivayal oṭuṅkum mutirnel uṇaviṉum
tammil vīḻunākku iṉpameṉ ṟaṟintum
taṇmati kaṭuñcuṭar vevvaḻal kaṇvittu
aḷavāp pātam maṇparap pāka       (25)
taṉineṭu vicumpu tiruvuṭal āka
irunticip pōkkup peruntōḷ āka
vaḻuvaṟu tirumaṟi ōcikaḷ aṉittum
moḻitara nikaḻum vārtti āka
uḷniṟin tuḻalum pāṭiraṇṭu uyirppum       (30)
pakaliravu oṭuṅkā viṭuvaḷi āka
aṭupaṭip pūḻiyaṉ kaṭumuraṇ paṟṟi
iṭṭaveṅ koṭuñciṟip paṭṭa kārkkulam
taḷiyoṭu niṟinīr viṭuvaṉa pōla
purici yoṭupācam aṟavuṭal nimirntu       (35)
kūṭamum kantum cēṟuniṉ ṟalippa
mūṉṟumata neṭumpuṉala* kāṉṟu mayaluvaṭṭi
ēḻuyar karittiraḷ katamoṭu piḷiṟum
perunakark kūṭal uṟitaru kaṭavuḷi
niṟiyap pēcāk kuṟiyuḷar pōlavum       (40)
kallā maṉaṉiṉum celluti peruma!
iḷamiyum iṉpamum vaḷaṉum kāṭciyum
piṉpuṟa nēṭiṉ muṉpavi aṉṟāla
nuṉitta mēṉit tiruviṉaṭku aṭitta
viṉitarum aṭiviṉ allatu
puṉiyak kāṇēṉ colā yiṉavē.       (46)

22. taṉṉuḷkiyāṟu eytiṭu kiḷavi

nīrnili niṉṟu kālkaṟut teḻuntu
tikkunili paṭarnta mukilpā caṭiyum
iṭiyiṭi ukaḷum mīṉām mīṉum
cemmukil paḻanuri veṇmukil putunuri
eṅkum citaṟip poṅkiyeḻu vaṉappum       (5)
palatali vittu muṭiyātu pāyum
eṅkum mukamvittak kaṅkik kālum
koṇṭu kuḷirparanta maṅkul vāvikkuḷ
muyaleṉum vaṇṭuṇa amutanaṟavu oḻukki
tēvar maṅkiyar malarmukam paḻittu       (10)
kuṟiyāp pāṇṭil veṇmiyiṉ malarnta
matittā mariyē! mayaṅkiya oruvēṉ
niṉpāl kēṭkum aḷimoḻi oṉṟuḷa
mīṉpāyntu maṟikkat tiriyiṭi mayaṅki
cūlvayiṟu uḷintu vaḷikiṭantu muralum       (15)
puṉṉiyam potumparil tammuṭi neñcamum
mīṉuṇavu uḷḷi iruntaveṇ kurukeṉac
cōṟu naṟikāṉṟa kitiya malarum
palatali arakkar pēraṇi pōla
maruṅku kūṇṭeḻuntu karuṅkāy neruṅki       (20)
viḷikaḷ cumanta taliviri peṇṇiyum
iṉṉum kāṇāk kāṭcikoṇ ṭirunta
aṉṉat tiraḷum peruṅkari yāka
collā iṉpamum uyiruṟat tantu
nāḷiḻik tirukkum ceyirkoḷ ‍aṟṟattu       (25)
meyyuṟat taṇanta poyyiṉar iṉṟu
neṭumali peṟṟa orumakaḷ kāṇa
nāṉmuka vitiyē tāḷam tākka
anta nāṉmukaṉi unti pūttōṉ
vicittu miṟipācattu iṭakki vicippa       (30)
mūṉṟupurattu oṉṟil aracuṭi vāṇaṉ
mēruk kiḷitta tōḷā yirattoṭum
eḻukaṭal kiḷarnta tiraḷkali aṭaṅka
mukamavēṟu icikkum kuṭamuḻuvu iraṭṭa
puṭkāl tumpuru maṇakkan tiruvar       (35)
nāṉmaṟip payaṉām ēḻici amittu
carukkarik kuṉṟil tēṉmaḻi nāṉṟeṉa
ēḻu muṉivarkaḷ tāḻum mātavar
aṉpiṉar uḷḷamoṭu eṉpukarin turuka
viralnāṉku amitta aṇikural vīṅkātu       (40)
nāṉmaṟi tuḷḷum vāypiḷa vātu
kāṭṭiyuḷ uṇarttum nōkkam āṭātu
pitirkaṇal maṇicūḻ muṭinaṭuk kātu
vayiṟu kuḻivāṅki aḻumukam kāṭṭātu
nāci kākuḷi veṭikural veḷḷi       (45)
pēcāk kīḻici orupuṟam oṭṭal
neṭṭuyirppu eṟital eṟintuniṉṟi raṭṭal
ōci iḻittal kaḻipōkku eṉṉap
pēcaṟu kuṟṟam ācoṭum māṟṟi
vaṇṭiṉ tāriyum kañca nātamum       (50)
ciralvāṉ niliyum kaḻiili vīḻvatum
aruvi ōciyum muḻaviṉ muḻakkamum
valampuric cattamum verukiṉ puṇarcciyum
iṉṉumeṉ ṟicippap paṉṉiya vitiyoṭu
mantaram mattimam tāram ivimūṉṟil       (55)
tuḷḷal tūṅkal teḷḷitiṉ melital
kūṭiya kāṉam aṉpoṭu parava
pūtam tuḷḷa pēyki maṟippa
eṅkuḷa uyirum iṉpam niṟintāṭa
nāṭaka vitiyoṭu āṭiya perumāṉ       (60)
maturi mānakarp pūḻiya ṉāki
katirmuṭi kavitta iṟivaṉ māmaṇik
kāltalik koḷḷāk kiyiṉar pōla
nīṅkiṉar pōkkum īṅkuḻi varuvatum
kaṇṭatu kūṟuti āyiṉ
eṇtakap pōṟṟiniṉ kālvaṇaṅ kutumē.       (66)

23. vēṟupaṭuttuk kūṟal

kaṇṭa kāṭci cēṇiṉ kuṟiyō
eṉṉuḻi niliyā uḷḷattiṉ matiyō
cūrppaki ulakil tōṉṟiṉarkku aḻaku
vitikkum aṭaṅkā eṉpaṉa vitiyō
eṉṉuṭik kaṇṇum uyirum āki       (5)
uḷnikaḻ iṉpam uḷḷāḷ orutti
malikkuñ carattiṉ kaṭakkuḻi yāki
neṭumali viḻitta kaṇṇē āki
ammalit tirunutaṟku aḻiyātu amitta
veḷḷikoḷ cintura nallaṇi āki       (10)
tūra naṭanta tāḷ eyppu āṟi
amutoṭu kiṭakkum niṟimatip pakkam
orupāl kiṭanta tuṇimati yāki
aruvi vīcap paṟavi kuṭipōki
vīṇṭunaṟavu oḻukkum pāṇṭil iṟālāy       (15)
iḷami nīṅkātu kāvalkoḷ amutam
variyara mātar kuḻuvuṭaṉ arunta
ākkiyiṭap patitta vaḷḷamum āki
iṭivaḷi pōkātu neruṅkumulik koṭicciyar
ciṟumukam kāṇum āṭi āki       (20)
ciṟantaṉa orucuṉi immali āṭa
aḷavāk kātal kimmikku aṇintaṉaḷ
avaḷē nīyāy eṉkaṇ kuṟitta
terumaral tanta aṟivunili kiṭakka
ciṟituniṉ kuṟuveyar peṟum aṇaṅku āṟi       (25)
orukaṇaṉ nilikka maruvuti āyiṉ
innili peyara uṉṉumak kaṇattil
tūṇṭā viḷakkiṉ īṇṭavaḷ utavum
avvuḻi uṟavu meypeṟak kalantiṉṟu
orukaṭal iraṇṭu tiruppayan tāṅku       (30)
vaḷitta neṭuṅkārp puṉattiru
maṇiniṟa ūcal aṇipeṟa ukittum
karuṅkāl kavaṇiṭic cemmaṇi vittu
peruntēṉ iṟāloṭu kuṟiviḻa eṟintum
veṇtukil nuṭaṅki poṉkoḻit tiḻiyum       (35)
aruvi ēṟṟum muḻimali *kūuyum
peruñcuṉi viḻitta nīlam koytum
koṭumaram paṟṟi neṭṭitaṇ polintu
tiṉikkural aṟiyum kiḷikkaṇam kaṭitir
veḷḷi irumpu poṉeṉap peṟṟa       (40)
mūṉṟupuram vēva tirunaki viḷiyāṭṭu
orunāḷ kaṇṭa perumāṉ iṟivaṉ
mātuṭaṉ oṉṟi eṉmaṉam pukuntu
pēṇā uḷḷam kāṇātu naṭantu
kolikaḷavu eṉṉum paḻumaram piṭuṅki       (45)
pavaccuvar iṭittup putukkak kaṭṭi
aṉpukoru mēynta neñca maṇṭapattu
pāṅkuṭaṉ kāṇat tōṉṟi uḷniṉṟu
poṉmalarc cōli vimmiya perumalar
imiyōr puratti niṟimaṇam kāṭṭum       (50)
kūṭalam patiyakam pīṭupeṟa iruntōṉ
irutāḷ peṟṟavar peruntirup pōla
maruviya paṇṇi iṉpamoṭu viḷinalam
colluṭaṉ amarātu īṅku
villuṭaṉ pakitta centiru nutalē!       (55)
-------------

24. kāmam mikka kaḻipaṭar kiḷavi

vāṉavarkku iṟivaṉ nilamkiṭi koṇṭu
tiruvuṭal niṟiviḻi āyirat tiraḷum
imiyātu viḻitta tōṟṟam pōla
kañcak koḷḷi iṭiyaṟa malarntu
maṇamcūḻ kiṭanta nīḷkaruṅ kaḻiyē!       (5)
karuṅkaḻi koṭukkum veḷiṟavu aruntak
kipārt tirukkum maṭappeṭi kurukē!
peṭikkuruku aṇaṅkiṉ viṭuttaveṇ ciṉiyoṭu
kāval aṭikkiṭakkum kitiam poḻilē!
vemmiyoṭu kūṭiyum taṇmiyoru poruntiyum       (10)
ulakiruḷ turakkum ceñcuṭar veṇcuṭar
kālam kōṭā muṟimuṟi tōṟṟa
maṇiniri kuyiṟṟiya maṇṭapam āki
poṟimāṇṭu uyirkkum tāyām maṇmakaḷ
kāḷiyātu uṭukkum pintukil āki       (15)
vēṉiṟ kiḻavaṉ pēraṇi makiḻa
muḻakkātu taḻaṅkum vārmuracu āki
neṭiyōṉ tuyilā aṟivoṭu tuyila
pāyaṟku aminta paḷḷiyaṟi yāki
calapati āyntu cēmanili vitta       (20)
muttumaṇi kiṭakkum cēṟṟiruḷ araṅkāy
pulavuuṭaṟ paratavar tamkuṭi ōmpa
nāḷum viḷikkum peruvayal āki
kalameṉum neṭuntēr toliyātu ōṭa
aḷappaṟap paranta vīti yāki       (25)
cuṟava vēntu neṭumpaṭi ceyya
muḻakkamoṭu vaḷitta amarkkaḷam āki
makarat teyvam nāḷ niṟintu uṟiya
maṇiviḷakku niṟinta ālayam āki
nīrney vārttuc cakarar amitta       (30)
tīvaḷar vaṭṭak kuṇṭam āki
eṇtikaḻ pakuvām iṉamaṇip pāntaḷ
taṇṭil niṉṟueriyum takaḷi yāki
pañcavaṉ niṟinta aṉpuṭaṉ vēṇṭa
māṟik kuṉitta nīṟaṇi perumāṟku       (35)
amuta pōṉakam katumeṉa utavum
aṭumtī māṟā maṭippaḷḷi āki
iṉṉum palamāy maṉṉum kaṭalē
nuṅkaḷ iṉpam peruntuṇi eṉṟāl
taṇṇam tuṟivaṟku iṉṟu ivaḷ orutti       (40)
neruppuṟu meḻukiṉ uḷḷam vāṭiyum
aruvi tūṅkak kaṇṇīr koṇṭum
araviṉvāy ariyiṉ palavum niṉintum
niliyāc cūḷiṉ niliyā neñcam
koṇṭaṉaḷ eṉeṉa eṉmukam nāṭi       (45)
uṟṟa vāymi caṟṟum tarukilīr
aṉṟeṉiṉ nummiṉ oṉṟupaṭ ṭorukāl
'ivaḷō tuyaram peṟuvateṉ?' eṉṟu
viṉavātu irukkum kēṇmi,
maṉaṉāl nāṭiṉ koliyiṉum koṭitē!       (50)
-------------

25. iṭam aṇittu eṉṟal

poruppu vaḷaṉvēṇṭi maḻikkaṇ tiṟappa
kurukupeyark kuṟittu uṭalpaka eṟinta
neṭuvēḷ kaṭavuḷ mayilkoṭi muṉṟil
peruṅkiḷi kūṇṭu ‍veṭcimalar parappi
iṟālnaṟavu aḷāya centiṉi veḷiṭi       (5)
tēkkiṉal virittu nāltici vittu
maṉavuaṇi mutiyōṉ vari aṇaṅku ayarntu
mūṉṟu kālamum tōṉṟak kūṟa
vēlaṉ cuḻaṉṟu kuṟumaṟi aṟuppa
karuvi nutikoḷ neṟiyiṉal īntiṉ       (10)
muṟṟiya perunaṟavu eṇṇuṭaṉ kuṭittu
neṭṭili arampik kuṟuṅkāy māṉum
uḷiyam taṇittakaṇi koḷvāyt tirikal
oppuṭit tāya vaṭṭavāyt toṇṭakam
kōltali paṉippa vāṉviṭu peruṅkural       (15)
vīyātu tuvikkum kaṭaṉmali nākir
varuntiyēṟ ṟeṭutta centiru maṭamakaḷ
oruvuka uḷattup perukiya naṭukkam
emmūrc cēṇum nummūrk kuṉṟamum
peruntavar kuḻuvum aruṅkati iruppum       (20)
potiyamum kaḷippa viritaru teṉṟalum
kaṉikaṭal kuṭitta muṉivaṉum tamiḻum
mēruvum mūvarkku ōtiya puramum
ulakamīṉ ṟaḷitta umiyum māaṟaṉum
tēvarkku aracaṉum kāval taruvum       (25)
vaḻuvā vitiyum eḻutā maṟiyum
ceṅkōl vēntum taṅkiya kuṭiyum
tavamcūḻ imayamum kamañcūl maḻiyum
elliyil īṅkivi colliya aṉṟi
kaṇṇaṉ karamum veṇ‍ṇeyum pōlap       (30)
pācaṭi putitta neṭṭāṟṟu ēriyuḷ
pūttalar viritta cēppaṭu tāmari
uḷvaḷi uṟaṅkum vaḷḷavāyk kūṭal
niṟintuṟi mukkaṇ peruntiṟal aṭikaḷ
aṭiyavarkku evvaḷavu atuām
koṭipuri nucuppiṉ perumuli yōḷē!       (36)

26. niṉkuṟi nīyē ceṉṟu uri eṉṟal

vēṟṟup piṭipuṇarnta tīrāp pulavi
cuṟṟamoṭu tīrkka uytta kātaliṉ
karuṅki veṇkōṭṭuk ciṟukaṇ peruṅkaḷiṟu
uḷattuniṉ ṟaḷikkum tiruttakum arunūl
paḷḷik kaṇakkar pālpaṭ ṭāṅku       (5)
kuṟiñcip peruntēṉ iṟāloṭu citittu
meṉṉaṭip piṭikkuk kipiṭit tutavi
aṭikkaṭi vaṇaṅkum cāral nāṭa!
antaṇar irukki akalvōr cūḻnteṉa
nalnayam kiṭanta poṉṉakar mūṭip       (10)
puliceytu uṭaṉṟu nilinili tēykkum
taḷḷā moympiṉ uḷuṭintu orukāl
vētiyaṉ mutalā amararum aracaṉum
pōtutūy irappa puṇarā mayakkam
nāraṇaṉ naṭitta peruvāyt tarukkattu       (15)
aṟivunili pōki aruccaṉi viṭutta
veḷḷamuraṇ arakkar kaḷḷamatil mūṉṟum
aṭukkunili cumanta valittaṭap poṉmali
kaṭumuraṇ kuṭikkum neṭuvil kūṭṭi
āyiram tīvāy aravunāṇ koḷīi       (20)
mātavaṉ aṅki vaḷikuti eḻunuṉi
ceñcaram pēriruḷ arukkaṉ matiāka
tērvari viyam ākat tirutti
ceṉṉimali īṉṟa kaṉṉiviṟ piṭippa
orukāl muṉvittu irukāl vaḷippa       (25)
vaḷittavil vaṭṭam kiṭittatu kaṇṭu
ciṟunaki koṇṭa oruperun tīyiṉ
ēḻuyarvāṉam pūḻipaṭak karukki
aruccaṉi viṭātaṅku oruppaṭum mūvaril
iruvarik kāval maruvutal īntu       (30)
maṟṟoru vaṟku vitta naṭam aṟintu
kuṭamuḻavu icippap perumaruḷ nalki
orunāḷ aruccaṉi purintiṭā avarkkum
arumpeṟal uḷatām perumpatam kāṭṭi
eriyiṭi māynta kaṉalviḻi arakkarkku       (35)
ulavāp poṉṉulaku aṭitara vitta
cuntarak kaṭavuḷ kantarak kaṟiyōṉ
māmi āṭap puṇari aḻitta
kāmar kūṭaṟku iṟivaṉ kaḻaliṇi
kaḷippuṭi aṭiyarkku veḷippaṭ ṭeṉṉa       (40)
orunī tāṉē maruvutal kiṭittu
kaḷḷamum veḷiyum uḷḷamuṟi aṉittum
virittuk kūṟi poruttamum kāṇṭi
īyā māntar poruḷtēyn teṉṉa
nuṇṇiṭi cumantu āṟṟātu
kaṇṇiya cuṇaṅkiṉ perumuli yōṭkē!       (46)
-------------

27. iravukkuṟi vēṇṭal

vaḷḷiyōr ītal variyātu pōla
eṇtici karuiruntu iṉamaḻi kāṉṟatu
veṇṇakik karuṅkuḻal centaḷirc ciṟaṭi
maṅkiyar uḷameṉa kaṅkulum parantatu
teyvam karutāp poyyiṉarkku uritta       (5)
nalvaḻi māṉa pulvaḻi puraṇṭatu
kālam muṭiya kaṇakkiṉ paṭiyē
maṟali viṭukka vanta tūtuvar
uyirtoṟum vaḷinteṉa uyircumantu uḻalum
pukarmali iyaṅkā vakivari cūḻntaṉa       (10)
veḷḷuṭaṟ pēḻvāyt taḻalviḻi maṭaṅkal
urivi mūṭi karittōl virittu
puḷḷi paranta vaḷḷukirt tarakkiṉ
ataḷpiyaṟ kiṭṭu kutiyāy navviyiṉ
carumam uṭuttu karumpāmpu kaṭṭi       (15)
muṉpuru vitikaḷ eṉpukural pūṇṭu
karumā eyiṟu tirumārpu tūkki
viyakat tuyariṉ vaḻakkaṟal karuti
toyyil āṭum kaṭaṉuṭik kaṉṉiyar
aṇṇānta vaṉamulic cuṇṇamum aḷaṟum       (20)
eḻilivāṉ cuḻalap piḷiṟukuraṟ pakaṭṭiṉam
tuṟinīr āṭap parantakār matamum
poykiyum kiṭaṅkum ceyyiṉum pukuntu
ciñci iṭaṅkari piñcilic cēli
uṭaṟpulavu māṟṟum paṭattiri viyi       (25)
niṟinīr vaḷikkum pukaḻnīrk kūṭal
veḷḷiyam potuvil kaḷḷaviḻ kuḻaloṭum
iṉpanaṭam puriyum teyva nāyakaṉ
aruviuṭaṟ kayiṟum cuṉimatak kuḻiyum
peruntēṉ ceviyum karuntēṉ toṭarcciyum       (30)
ōvā, perumalik kuñcaram maṇakka
vaḷamtarum uṅkaḷ tolkuṭic cīṟūrkku
aṇṇiya viruntiṉaṉ āki
naṇṇuvaṉ ciṟunutaṟ peruviḻi yōḷē!       (34)
-------------

28. nakar aṇimi kūṟal

puyaṟkārp pācaṭi eṇpaṭap paṭarnta
veḷḷap perunati koḷḷimukam vittu
nīṭa niṟipāyum vāṉa vāvikkuḷ
orucen tāmari naṭumalarn teṉṉa
mūvaṭi vaḻakkiṟku ōraṭi maṇkoṭu       (5)
orutāḷ viṇṇattu irumipeṟa nīṭṭiya
karuṅkaṭal vaṇṇaṉ ceṅkaruṅ karattu
oṉṟāl irumali aṉṟēn tiyateṉa
untioḻuk kēntiya vaṉamuli yāṭṭiyum
variporum marumattu orutiṟaṉ nīyum       (10)
muḻivāy arakkar pāṭukiṭan totta
niṟikiṭip poṟṟi varikaṭantu iṟantāl
eritaḻaṟ kuñci poṟiviḻi piṟaḻeyiṟṟu
iruḷuṭal antakaṉ maruḷkoḷa utitta
mūvāt tiruppatattu orutaṉip perumāṉ       (15)
eṇṇil peṟāta aṇṭap peruntiraḷ
aṭivuīṉ ṟaḷitta piṟinutaṟ kaṉṉiyoṭum
aḷavāk kaṟpam aḷivittu niliiya
pācaṭi neṭuṅkāṭu kāṇikoḷ nīrnāy
vāṉavil niṟatta neṭṭuṭal vāḷip       (20)
pēḻvāy oḷippa vēṭṭuvap peyar aḷi
iṭivuṟaḻ nutappiṉ kuravivāyk kaṭiciyar
kaḷikaṭun toḻilviṭuttu uḻavuceṟu maṇṭa
paṇkāl uḻavar pakaṭupiṭar pūṇṭa
muṭapputu nāñcil aḷḷal pukaniṟutti       (25)
cūṭunili uyarttum kaṭuṅkuli ēṟa
piṅkuvaḷi tuykkum ceṅkaṭ kavari
nākoṭu veruṇṭu kaḻikkarumpu uḻakka
amutavāy moḻicciyar naccuviḻi pōla
neṭuṅkuḻi kiḻippak kaṭuṅkayal pāyum       (30)
taṇṇam paḻaṉam cūḻnta
kaṇivar kūṭal peruvaḷam patiyē!       (32)
-------------

29. aṟiyāḷ pōṉṟu niṉivu kēṭṭal

paṟṟalart teṟutalum uvantōrp parittalum
veñcuṭar taṇmati eṉappukaḻ niṟīiya
neṭṭilik kuṟumpukak kuruti vēlava!
vētiyaṉ paṭikka mālavaṉ kākkap
peṟātatōr tiruvurut tāṉ peritu niṟutti       (5)
amutayil vāḻkkit tēvar‍kōṉ iḻicciya
matamali irunāṉku piṭarcumantu ōṅkic
cempoṉ maṇikuyiṟṟiya cikarak kōyiluḷ
amiyāt taṇṇaḷi umiyuṭaṉ niṟinta
ālavāy uṟitarum mūlak koḻuñcuṭar       (10)
karuvi vāṉam aṭikkaṭi poḻiyum
kūṭam cūḻnta ‍neṭumuṭip potiyattu
kaṇnuḻi yātu kāṭcikoṭu tōṟṟiya
veṟivīc cantiṉ niriiṭi eṟintu
maṟṟatu vēli koḷavaḷittu vaḷarēṉal       (15)
neṭuṅkāl kuṟṟuḻi itaṇuḻi kāttum
tēvar kōmāṉ ciṟi ari puṇṇiṉukku
āṟṟātu perumuḻi vāyviṭṭuk kaluḻnteṉa
kamañcūl koṇmū mutuku kuṭiyiruntu
vāṉuṭka muraṟṟum maliccuṉi kuṭintum       (20)
piracamum vaṇṭum iraliteṟu maṇiyum
vayiramum poṉṉum niriniri koḻittu
tukilnāṉṟu nuṭaṅkum aruvi ēṟṟum
maṟuvaṟu cemmaṇi kālkavaṇ niṟutti
niṟimati kiṭakkum iṟālviḻa eṟintum       (25)
etircol kēṭpak kālpukat tikitta
nerukkupoḻil pukku neṭumali kūyum
nukappiṉ pakikku nūpuram araṟṟap
piṅkāṭu nakitta veṇmalar koytum
maṉattoṭu kaṇṇum aṭikkaṭi koṭupōm       (30)
cempoṉ ceyta varippantu turantum
iṉiya palneṟip paṇṇi iyaṅkum
aḷavāk kaṉṉiyar avaruḷ
uḷamām vēṭkiyaḷ iṉṉaḷeṉ nuriyē       (34)
-------------

30. cuṭarōṭu irattal

īṉṟeṉ uḷamum tōṉṟum moḻipayiṉṟa
vaḷivāyk kiḷḷiyum varippuṉi pantum
pūviyum kōṅkiṉ poṉmalar cūṭṭiya
pāviyum māṉum teruḷpavar ūrum
neṭuntici naṭakkum poruḷniṟi kalattiṉip       (5)
peruvaḷi malakkac ceyalmaṟu maṟantāṅku
cēra maṟuka mutukkuṟi uṟutti
eriteṟum koṭuñcurattu iṟantaṉa ḷāka
natimatam taṟukaṇ pukarkoli maṟutta       (10)
kalipam ataṉik karumpukoḷa vitta
ālavāy amarnta nīlamniṟi kaṇṭaṉ
maṟitirip paravip puṭivayiṟu kuḻampat
tulakkumali orunāḷ kalakkuva pōla
uḻuvi ukiruḻakkum ēntu kōṭṭu umpal
urivi mūṭi oḷiyiṉi maṟittu       (15)
taripaṭu maṟukkam taṭintaṉa pōla
viṇṇuṟa viritta karumukiṟ paṭāmkoṭu
maṇṇakam urukak kaṉaṟṟumaḻal mēṉiyi
eṭuttu mūṭi eṟitirip paḻaṉattup
paṉicciṟumi koḷḷā muḷari muḷari       (20)
vaṇṭoṭu malarnta vaṇṇam pōla
kaṇṇum maṉamum kaḷivara malarttuti
malartali ulakattu iruḷeṟi viḷakkum
maṉṉuyir viḻikkak kaṇṇiya kaṇṇum
maṟiuku nīrkkuk karuvum kariyum       (25)
vaṭivam eṭṭaṉuḷ vanta oṉṟum
cēṇkuḷam malarnta centā mariyum
cōṟṟuk kaṭaṉkaḻikkap pōṟṟuyir aḻikkum
ācic cerunarkku aṭintucel vaḻiyum
aruḷum poruḷum ākit
tiruvulaku aḷikkum parutivā ṉavaṉē!       (31)
-------------

31. iṉṉal eytal

vaḷḷuṟi kaḻittut tuḷakkuvēl makaṉum
maṉavumayiṟ kaḻuttu māli yāṭṭiyum
nelpiṭittu urikkum kuṟiyi ṉōḷum
naṭuṅkañar uṟṟa paḻaṅkaṇ aṉṉiyarum
ayarum veṟiyil taṇṭā arunōy       (5)
īyātu uṇṇunar neṭumpaḻi pōlap
pōkāk kāli puṇarkkuvatu eṉṉō?
nāṉkeyiṟṟu oruttal piṭarppolivarippaki
aṟukāl kuḷikkum matuttoṭi ēnta
muḷtāḷ cemmalar nāṉmukattu oruvaṉ       (10)
eṇṇiney iṟittu maṇaaḻal ōmpa
puviaḷantu uṇṭa tiruneṭu mālōṉ
irukaram aṭukkip perunīr vārppa
oṟṟi āḻiyaṉ muyaluṭal taṇcuṭar
aṇṭam viḷarppap peruviḷakku eṭuppa       (15)
aḷavāp pulaṉkoḷa viñciyar eṇmarum
vaḷḷiyil karuviyil perumpukaḻ viḷippa
muṉivar ceṅkaram ceṉṉi āka
uruppaci muta‍lōr muṉvāḻttu eṭuppa
mummuli oruttiyi maṇantulaku āṇṭa       (20)
kūṭaṟku iṟivaṉ irutāḷ iruttum
kaviyā veṉṟi neñciṉar nōkka
piṟaviyum kūṟṟamum pirintaṉa pōlap
pīramum nōyum māṟil
vārit tuṟivaṟku eṉṉā tummē?       (25)
-------------

32. neñcoṭu nōtal

poruḷceyal aruttiyiṉ eṇvaḻi taṭintu
nāltici naṭakkum aṇaṅkiṉ avayavattu
alitaru taṭṭik karumpuṟam malimaṭal
kaṭaltiri ukaḷum kuṟuṅkayal māṉum
kaṭuṅkāṉ taḷḷi taṭitaru neñcam!       (5)
kayilit teṉpāl kāṉakam taṉitta
tēvarneñ cuṭikkum tāmari yōkiṉ
maṇakkōl turanta kuṇakkō mataṉi
tirukkuḷam muḷitta kaṇtā marikoṭu
teṉkīḻt ticiyōṉ ākkiya taṉimutal       (10)
tirumā maturi eṉumtirup poṟṟoṭi
eṉṉuyir aṭitta poṉmulic ceppiṉ
māḷā iṉpam karutiyō? aṉṟi
puṟaṉpayaṉ koṭukkum poruṭkō? vāḻi!
vaḷarmuli iṉpeṉiṉ maṟittu nōkkumati       (15)
perumporuḷ iṉpeṉiṉ peritutaṭi iṉṟē
yātiṉik karutiyatu? oṉṟi
ōtal vēṇṭum vāḻiya peritē!       (18)
-------------

33. allakuṟi aṟivittal

vaṟṟiya narampu neṭuṅkural pēḻvāy
kuḻiviḻi piṟaḻpal teṟṟaṟ karuṅkāl
tāḷip pōntiṉ karumayirp peruntali
viṇpuṭittu appuṟam viḷaṅkuṭaṟ kuṇaṅkiṉam
kāṉam pāṭic cuṟṟiniṉ ṟāṭa       (5)
cuḻalviḻi ciṟunaki kuṭavayiṟṟu irukuḻic
caṅkak kuṟuntāṭ pāriṭam kuṉippa
tēvar kaṇpaṉippa muṉivar vāykuḻaṟa
kalla vaṭattiraḷ maṭivāyt taṇṇumi
monti kallalaku tuttari ēṅka       (10)
kaṭcevi cuḻala tāḻcaṭi neṟippa
itaḻi tātutirppa piṟiamutu ukukka
veḷḷi ampalattuḷ tuḷḷiya perumāṉ
kūṭal mānakar aṉṉa poṟkoṭi!
iravikku aṇiya vikaṟi kāṟum       (15)
alamaral eṉṉikol? aṟintilam yāmē
veṇmuttu arumpi pacumpoṉ malarntu
kaṭinta cempavaḷat tottuṭaṉ kāṭṭum
irumpu kavittaṉṉa karuṅkōṭṭup puṉṉic
ciṉimukam ēntiya iṇarkoḷvāyk kuṭampiyiṉ       (20)
ekkarp puḷḷiṉum veṇmi iṭammaṟikkum
ciṟiviri tūvic ceṅkāl aṉṉam
kuṟumpārppu aṇikkum peṭiyoṭu verīi
cēvalum iṉamum cūḻum
kāvil māṟit tuyil aḻuṅku taṟkē.       (25)
-------------

34. vēḻam viṉātal

taṉṉuṭal aṉṟip piṟituṇ kaṉiyiruḷ
pakalvalikku otuṅkiya tōṟṟam pōla
perunilavu kāṉṟa nīṟukeḻu parappil
aṇṭa nāṭavarkku āruyir koṭutta
kaṇṭak kaṟiyōṉ kaṇtaru nutalōṉ       (5)
muṉṉoru nāḷil nālpaṭi uṭaṉṟu
ceḻiyaṉ aṭitta ceṉṉi pāṭa
eḷḷaruṅ karuṇiyiṉ naḷḷiruḷ naṭunāḷ
avaṉeṉat tōṉṟi aruñciṟi viṭutta
muṉṉavaṉ kūṭal mūtūr aṉṉa       (10)
veṇṇakic cevvāyk karuṅkuḻal makaḷir
cemmaṇi kiṭantanum pacumpuṉattu uḻiyāl
vāycori maḻimatat taḻicevip puḻikkik
kuḻikaṇ parūuttāḷ kūrṅ‍kōṭṭu oruttal
ciṉitaḻi viḷitta paḻumaram eṉṉa       (15)
aṟukāl kaṇamum paṟaviyum kaṇiyum
mēkamum piṭiyum toṭara
ēkiyatu uṇṭēl kūṟuvir purintē.       (18)
-------------

35. nalam puṉinturittal

aruḷ tarum kēḷvi amiyat tēkka
paṟpala ācāṉ pāṅkucel pavarpōl
mūṉṟuvaki aṭutta tēṉtaru koḻumalar
koḻutip pāṭum kuṇaccurum piṉaṅkāḷ
uḷattu vēṟaṭakki mukamaṉ kūṟātu       (5)
vēṭkiyiṉ nīyir vīḻnāḷ pūviṉattuḷ
kāruṭal piṟieyiṟṟu arakka‍ṉik koṉṟu
varaccittaṭak'ki' varippaki cumanta
paḻavuṭal kāṭṭum tīrāp perumpaḻi
paṉimali payanta mātuṭaṉ tīrttaruḷ       (10)
pemmāṉ vāḻum perunakark kūṭal
oppuṟu poṟṟoṭic ciṟṟiṭi maṭantitaṉ
koliyiṉar uḷḷamum kuṟikoḷa iruṇṭu
nāṉam nīvi nāḷmalar milintu
kūṭi uṇṇum kuṇattiṉar kiḷipōl       (15)
nīṭic ceṟintu neyttuṭal kuḷirnta
karuṅkuḻaṟ perumaṇam pōla
oruṅkum uṇṭō? pēcuvir emakkē!       (18)
-------------

36. ulakiṉmēl vittu urittal

iruḷoṭu tāraki iraṇṭiṉi mayakki
kuḻaleṉa malareṉa mayalvarac cumantu
villiṉik kuṉittu kaṇiyiṉi vāṅki
puruvam kaṇṇeṉa uyirviṭap payiṟṟi
maliyiṉit tāṅki amutiṉik kaṭintu       (5)
muliyeṉa colleṉa avāvara vittu
meyyiṉip parappi poyyiṉik kāṭṭi
alkul iṭiyeṉa neñcuḻalak koṭuttu
muṇṭakam malartti māntaḷir mūṭi
aṭiyeṉa uṭaleṉa alamaral uṟīi       (10)
mūri vīḻnta neṟiccaṭi muṉivar
carukkam kāṭṭum arumaṟi colli
uḷḷam kaṟuttu kaṇcivantu iṭṭa
mantirattu aḻalkuḻi toṭuvayiṟu varunti
muṉpiṉ īṉṟa pēḻvāyp puliyiṉi       (15)
kitimuḷ ceṟinta kūreyiṟṟu araviṉi
kāruṭal peṟṟa tīviḻik kuṟaḷiṉi
uriceytu uṭuttu ceṅkaram tarittu
cemmalar paḻitta tāṭkīḻk kiṭatti
tirunaṭam purinta teyva nāyakaṉ       (20)
orunāḷ mūṉṟu puramtīk koḷuva
poṉmali piṭuṅki kārmukam eṉṉa
vaḷitta ñāṉṟu neṭuviṇ taṭiyak
kāl koṭuttaṉṉa kantikaḷ nimirntu
nerukkupoḻil kūṭal aṉṉacem makaḷir       (25)
kaṇṇeṉum teyvak kāṭciyuḷ paṭṭōr
veṇpoṭi erukkam eṉpupaṉi kiḻiyiṉi
pūci aṇintu pūṇṭu parikaṭavi
karattatu ākki annō
arutti mīṭpar nilival lōrē.       (30)
-------------

37. nāṇ iḻantu varuntal

mikuḻit taṉṉa toḷḷiam ceṟuvil
kūrvāyp paṟitapu peruṅkiḻa nāri
vañcaṉi tūṅki āral uṇṇum
nīṅkāp paḻaṉap perunakark kūṭal
karammāṉ taritta perumāṉ iṟivaṉ       (5)
poṉpaḻit teṭutta iṉpuṟu tiruvaṭi
uḷamviḻuṅ kāta kaḷaviṉar pōleṉ
uyiroṭum vaḷarnta perunāṇ taṟiyiṉi
veṟpaṉ kātaṟ kāluli vēliyiṉ
valiyuṭik kaṟpiṉ neṭuveḷi cuḻaṟṟik       (10)
kaṭpulaṉ kāṇātu kāṭṭikeṭa untaliṉ
eṉpōl innili āṟuvarap paṭikkum
pēṟāṅku oḻika perunāṇ kaṟpiṉar
eṉ pēṟu uṭiyar āyiṉ
kaṟpil tōṉṟāk kaṭaṉā kukavē.       (15)
-------------

38. tōḻi iyaṟpaḻittu urittal

vaṭamīṉ kaṟpiṉem pīṭukeḻu maṭanti
peruṅkaṭal mukantu vayiṟuniṟi neṭuṅkār
viṇtirintu muḻaṅki vīḻā tākak
karuvoṭu vāṭum piṅkūḻ pōla
kaṟpunāṇ mūṭip paḻaṅkaṇ koḷḷa       (5)
uyarmaram muḷitta ūri pōla
ōruṭal ceytu maṟumaṉam kāṭṭum
māṇiḻi makaḷir vayiṉvi kutalāl
karumukiṟ kaṉiniṟat taḻaṟkaṇ piṟieyiṟṟu
aritaru kuṭṭi āyapaṉ ṉiraṇṭiṉi       (10)
ceṅkōl muḷiyiṭṭu aruḷnīr tēkki
kolikaḷavu eṉṉum paṭarkaḷi kaṭṭu
tīppaṭar āṇi vēli kōli
tarumap perumpayir ulakupeṟa viḷikkum
nālpaṭi vaṉṉiyar ākkiya perumāṉ       (15)
muḷ uṭip pēḻvāyc ceṅkaṇ varāliṉam
vaḷivāyt tūṇṭiṟ karuṅkayiṟu parintu
kuvaḷip pācaṭi muṇṭakam uḻakki
neṭuṅkāl pāyntu paṭutta oṇtoḻil
curuṅki vaḻiaṭikkum peruṅkaḻip paḻaṉak       (20)
kūṭaṟku iṟivaṉ irutāḷ viṭutta
poyyiṉar ceyyum pullam pōla
pērā vāymi ūraṉ
tāroṭu mayaṅki perumiyum ilaṉē.       (24)
-------------

39. poḻutu kaṇṭu iraṅkal

kōṭiya kōliṉaṉ cerumukam pōla
kaṉikatir tirukik kalcērntu muṟipuka
patiṉeṇ kiḷavi ūrtuñ ciyapōl
puṭkulam poyki vāytāḻk koḷḷa
vēḷ carattu uṭikunar kōlam nōkki       (5)
iruḷmakaḷ koṇṭa kuṟunaki pōla
mulliyum muvalum murukuyirttu aviḻa
taṇantōr uḷattil kāmat tīppuka
maṇantōr neñcattu amuta nīrviṭa
aṉṟilpuṟ cēkkipukku alakupeṭi aṇiya       (10)
antaṇar arumaṟi aruṅkiṭi aṭaṅka
mutukaṉi mūlam muṉikkaṇam maṟuppa
kalaviyum pūvum tōḷmuṭi kamaḻa
virivali nuḷiyar neytal ēnti
tuttam kikkiḷi aḷaviyiṉ viḷippa       (15)
nīrara makaḷir cevvāy kāṭṭip
pacuntāṭ cēkkōḷ āmpal malara
tōḷum iciyum kūṟiṭum kaliyum
aruḷtiru eḻuttum poruḷtiru maṟiyum
virumpiya kuṇamum aruntiru uruvum       (20)
mutaleṉ kiḷaviyum vitamuṭaṉ niriyē
eṭṭum ēḻum koṟṟaṉa āṟum
aintum nāṉkum aṇitaru mūṉṟum
tuñcalil iraṇṭum colarum oṉṟum
āruyir vāḻa aruḷvara niṟuttiya       (25)
pēraruṭ kūṭal perumpati niṟinta
mukkaṭ kaṭavuḷ mutalvaṉi vaṇaṅkār
tokkatīp peruviṉi cūḻntaṉa pōlavum
tuṟavāl aṟaṉāl peṟalil māntar
viḷḷā aṟivum uḷḷamum eṉṉavum       (30)
cekkart tīyoṭu pukkanal māli!
eṉṉuyir vaḷinta tōṟṟam pōla
nāṟpaṭi vēntaṉ pācaṟi
yōrkkum uḷiyō? maṉattiṟaṉ ōtukavē.       (34)
-------------

40. mā viratiyaroṭu kūṟal

nilavupakal kāṉṟa puṇṇiya aruṭpoṭi
iruviṉi turanta tiruvuṭal mūḻki
naṭuvuṭal varinta koṭikkāy pattar
cuttiamar nīṟuṭaṉ tōḷvalaṉ pūṇṭu
muṭaṅkuvīḻ aṉṉa vēṇi muṭikaṭṭi       (5)
irumūṉṟu kuṟṟam aṭiyaṟak kāyntiv
āṟu etirppaṭṭa aruntavat tiruviṉar
taṇiyāk koṭuñcuram tarumtaḻal tāvip
poṉṉuṭal tēvar okkaloṭu mayaṅki
koṇmūp paltirip puṉaluṭaṉ tāḻtti       (10)
potuḷiya taruviṉuḷ pukuntu imiyātu
maruntu pakuttuṇṭu valluyir tāṅkum
vaṭṭivan taṉieṉa vaḻaṅku moḻiniṟka
tāykāl tāḻntaṉaḷ āyam viṉaviṉaḷ
pāṅkiyip pulliṉaḷ ayalum coṟṟaṉaḷ       (15)
makkaṭ paṟavi parintuḷam māḻkiṉaḷ
pāṭalap pututtārk kāḷipiṉ oṉṟāl
taḷḷā vitiyiṉ celkunaḷ eṉṟu
taḻalviḻip pēḻvāyt tarakkiṉ tuḷimuli
piṅkaṇ pulvāy pāluṇak kaṇṭa       (20)
aruḷniṟi perumāṉ iruḷ niṟi miṭaṟṟōṉ
maṅkulniri pūtta maṇiuṭuk kaṇam eṉap
puṉṉiam petumparp pūniṟi kūṭalnum
poṉṉaṭi varuntiyum kūṭi
aṉṉiyark kutaval vēṇṭumik kuṟiyē.       (25)
-------------

41. āṭu iṭattu uyattal

muṉṉi āṭuka muṉṉi āṭuka
kumutam vaḷḷiyum nīlamum kumiḻum
tāmari oṉṟil taṭintuvaḷar ceyta
muḷariniṟi cemmakaḷ muṉṉi āṭuka
niṟpeṟu tavattiṉi muṟṟiya yāṉum       (5)
palakuṟi peṟṟiv ulakuyir aḷitta
pañciṉ mellaṭip pāvi kūṟāki
karuṅkuru vikkuk kaṇṇaruḷ koṭutta
veṇtiru nīṟṟuc cekkar mēṉiyaṉ
kiṭiyil tāpatar toṭimaṟi muḻakkum       (10)
poṅkark kiṭanta cūṟkārk kuḷiṟalum
valliyil pariyum pakaṭuviṭu kuralum
yāṇark koṭiñci neṭuntēr icippum
oṉṟi aḻuṅka niṉṟanili peruki
mātirak kaḷiṟṟiṉic ceviṭu paṭukkum       (15)
puṇṇiyak kūṭal uḷniṟi perumāṉ
tiruvaṭi cumanta aruḷiṉar pōla
karuntēṉ uṭittuc cemmaṇi citaṟi
pākaṟ kōṭṭil paṭarkaṟi vaṇakki
kalleṉṟu iḻintu kolliyil parakkum       (20)
kaṟaṅkici aruviyam cāral
puṟampu tōṉṟi niṉkaṇ ākuvaṉē.       (22)
-------------

42. iyal iṭaṅ kūṟal

vīti kuttiya kuṟuntāḷ pāriṭam
viṇtali uṭittup piṟivāy vippa
kuṇaṅkiṉam tuḷḷa kūḷiyum koṭpa
matti yantaṇaṉ varalcoli viṭuppat
tilli kaṇṭa pulikkāl muṉivaṉum       (5)
cūyi kiviṭap patañcali ākiya
āyiram paṇāṭavi aruntavattu oruvaṉum
kaṇṇāl vāṅki neñcaṟi niṟippa
tirunaṭam naviṉṟa ulakuyirp perumāṉ
kaṭalmāk koṉṟa tīppaṭar neṭuvēl       (10)
uruḷiṇark kaṭampiṉ neṭuntārk kaṇṇiyaṉ
arimakaḷ virumpip pākam ceytu
kaḷiyuṭaṉ niṟinta oruparaṅ kuṉṟamum
poṉam tōkiyum maṇiaric cilampum
nirittalic cuṭiki neruppumiḻ āramum       (15)
vaṇṭukiḷi muraṟṟiya pācilit tuḷavum
marakatam uṭaṟṟiya vaṭivoṭu mayaṅka
marakkāl āṭi arakkark koṉṟa
kavittali maṇivēl piṟittalik kaṉṉi
vaṭapāl parinta palimaṇak ‍kōṭṭamum       (20)
cūṭakam tōḷvaḷi kiṭantu vilvīca
yāvartam pakiyum yāviyiṉ pakiyum
vaḷaṉiṉ kāttu varuvaṉa aruḷum
ūḻiyum kaṇameṉa uyarmakaṉ paḷḷiyum
uvāmati kiṭakkum kuṇṭukaṭal kalakki       (25)
maruntu kikkoṇṭu vāṉavarkku ūṭṭiya
pākap pakka neṭiyōṉ uṟiyuḷum
tumpi uṇṇāt toṅkal tēvar
makkaḷoṭu neruṅkiya vītip puṟamum
matuniṟi piliṟṟiya pūvoṭu neruṅki       (30)
cūrarak kaṉṉiyar uṭalpaṉi ceyyum
kaṭikkāl maṭiyum poṅkarp pakkamum
ūṭi āṭunart tiriyoṭu piṇaṅkit
tōḻiyiṉ tīrkkum viyit tuḻaṉiyum       (35)
aḷavā ūḻi meyyoṭu cūḻntu
niṉṟu niṉṟōṅki niliaṟam perukkum
āṉāp perumpukaḻ aruḷnakark kūṭal
peṇṇuṭal peṟṟa ‍ceṉṉiam piṟiyōṉ
poṟṟakaṭu parappiya karumaṇi nirieṉa       (40)
vaṇṭum tēṉum maruḷkiḷi muraṟṟi
uṭintumiḻ naṟavuṇṭu uṟaṅkutārk koṉṟiyaṉ
tiruvaṭi pukaḻunar celvam pōlum
aṇṇāntu eṭutta aṇivuṟu vaṉamuli
avaṉkaḻal collunar aruviṉi māṉum       (45)
malimulip pakiaṭa māḻkuṟum nucuppu
maṟṟavaṉ acitta mācuṇam parappi
amittatu kaṭukkum aṇippāmpu alkul
āṅkavaṉ taritta kalimāṉ kaṭukkum
irukuḻi kiḻikkum arimatar malarkkaṇ       (50)
pukarmukap puḻikki tuyiltaru kaṉavil
muṭaṅkuḷi kaṇṭa peruntuyar pōla
uyiriṉum nuṉitta avvuruk koṇṭu
poṉmali paṉippiṉum paṉiyā
eṉṉuyir vāṭṭiya toṭiiḷaṅ koṭikkē.       (55)

43. aṉṉattōṭu aḻital

kavittukir vaṭaviyiṉ tiraḷciki parappi
aripeṟap piṇitta kalkuḷi mākkaḷ
uḷḷam tīkkum uvarkkaṭal uṭutta
nāvalam taṇpoḻil iṉpuṭaṉ tuyila
ulakaṟa viḻuṅkiya naḷḷeṉ kaṅkul       (5)
tuyilāk kēḷuṭaṉ uyiriri tērum
neṭṭuṭal pēḻvāyk kaḻutum uṟaṅka
piḷḷiyum peṭiyum paṟivāyt taḻīic
cuṟṟamum cūḻak kuruku kaṇpaṭuppa
kīḻarumpu aṇinta muḷari muḷari       (10)
itaḻkkatavu aṭittu malarkkaṇ tuyila
viriciṉi potuḷiya pācili oṭukki
pūvoṭum vaṇṭoṭum poṅkarum uṟaṅka
pālmukak kaḷaviṉ kuṟuṅkāyp pacciṇar
puṭkāl pāṭṭiṉarkku uṟiyuḷ koṭutta       (15)
mayirkuṟi karuvit tuṇikkuḻi alippa
varintin taṉaccumi matiaravu itaḻi
akaṉṟu kaṭṭaviḻnta cēkarat tirutti
vītiyum kavaliyum mikavaḷam pukaṉṟu
poḻutukaṇ maṟinta tīvāyc cekkar       (20)
taṇantōr uḷḷat tuḷuṟap pukuntapiṉ
kāruṭal kāṭṭi kaṇṭakaṇ putiya
aleṉum maṅki melleṉap pārkka
muraṉṟeḻu kāṉam muyaṉṟu vātiyinta
vaṭapula viñciyaṉ vikiṭattu akaṉkaṭi       (25)
teṉticip pāṇaṉ aṭimi yāṉeṉa
pōkā viṟakuṭaṉ talikkaṭi porunti
untit tōṟṟamum ōciniṉṟu oṭuṅka
pāliyil eḻuppi amarici payiṟṟi
tūṅkalum tuḷḷalum cuṇṭiniṉ ṟeḻutalum       (30)
tāriyil kāṭṭit tarumcā tāri
ulakuyir uḷḷamum oṉṟupaṭṭu oṭuṅka
iciviti pāṭi icippaki turanta
kūṭaṟku iṟiyōṉ tāḷviṭut tōreṉa
eṉkaṇ tuñcā nīrmi
muṉkaṇṭu ōtātu avarkkiḷaṅ kurukē.       (36)

44. talivaṟkup pāṅki tali varuttam kūṟal

īṉṟa ceñcūḻal kavarvaḻi piḻitta
veṟiviḻip piṇarmaruppu āmāṉ kaṉṟiṉi
meṉṉaṭik kuḻicevi peṟāveṟuṅ karumpiṭi
kaṇippaṇik kavaṭṭum maṇaṟcuṉip puṟattum
taḻikkuṟa maṅkiyar aivaṉam avikkum       (5)
uraṟkuḻi niritta kallaṟip parappum
māṉiṭa mākkaḷ arakkikip paṭṭeṉa
nāccuvi aṭukkum uṇavu uvavātu
vittuvit teṭukkum cāral nāṭaṉ
aṟivum poṟiyum poruḷaṟi kalviyum       (10)
oḻukkamum kulaṉum amukkaṟu tavamum
iṉimiyum paṇpum īṇṭavum naṉṟē
veṭivāl piṅkaṇ kuṟunari iṉattiṉi
ēḻiṭam tōṉṟi iṉaṉnūṟku iyintu
vīti pōkiya vālvuḷip paravi       (15)
ākkiya viñcip piṟimuṭi antaṇaṉ
koṇṭōṟku ēkum kuṟiyuṭi naṉṉāḷ
aṉṉiyar illattu aṇimaṭa maṅkiyar
kaṇṭaṉa kavarum kāṭci pōla
vēlaṉ pēci maṟicekuttu ōmpiya       (20)
kālam kōṭā varivaḷar paṇṭam
varuvaṉa vāri vaṇṭiṉam toṭara
kaṇkayal viḻittu pūttukil mūṭi
kuṟattiyar kuṭattiyar vaḻiviṭa naṭantu
karuṅkāl maḷḷar uḻavac cēṭiyar       (25)
niriniri vaṇaṅki mataketir koḷḷa
taṇṇaṭik kaṇavaṟ paṇpuṭaṉ puṇarum
viyi māmātu maṇattuṭaṉ cūḻnta
kūṭal perumāṉ poṉpiṟaḻ tiruvaṭi
neñcu iruttāta vañcakar pōla       (30)
caliyāc cārpu niliaṟa nīṅki
aranti yuṟṟu nīṭaniṉ ṟiraṅkum
murunteyiṟ ṟiḷampiṟik kōlam
tiruttiya tirunutal tukiriḷaṅ koṭiyē       (34)

45. collātu ēkal

ilateṉiṉ uḷateṉṟu uḷḷamoṭu vitittum
collā ni‍lipeṟum cūḷuṟiṉ mayaṅkic
ceykuṟik kuṇaṉum cintiyuḷ tirivum
uḻiniṉ ṟaṟintu paḻaṅkaṇ kavarntum
kaṇetir viki mukaṉkoḷiṉ kalaṅkiyum       (5)
vaḻaṅkuṟu kiḷaviyiṉ ticieṉa māḻkiyum
orutici nōkkiṉum irukkiṉum uṭintum
pōkkeṉa uḻiyar ayarppiṭik kiḷappiṉum
mulikuvaṭṭu oḻukkiya aruvitaṇ taraḷam
cemmaṇi karintu tīttara uyirttum       (10)
pōmeṉa vāyccol kēṭpiṉum pukintum
koḷḷār aṟutiyum koṇṭōr icittalum
īteṉak kāṭṭiya mayalmaṭa varaṟku
muṉṉoru vaṇikaṉ makappēṟu iṉmiyiṉ
marumāṉ taṉṉi makaveṉac caṭaṅkuceytu       (15)
uḷḷamum karaṇamum avaṉuḻi orukki
mukkavart tirunati tuṇiyuṭaṉ mūḻki
appulat tuyirkoṭuttu aruṭporuḷ koṇṭapiṉ
maṟṟavaṉ tāyam vavvuṟu mākkaḷ
kāṇi kikkoṇṭa maṟunili mintaṉi       (20)
nirittuk kiḷikoḷ neṭuvaḻakku uyttalum
mintaṉum kēḷirum matimuṭik kaṭavuḷniṉ
puntiyoṉ ṟiṟṟip pukalilam eṉṟayar
avvuḻi orucār avaṉmā tulaṉeṉa
aṟivoḷi niṟivē ōrurut tarintuvantu       (25)
aruḷvaḻak kēṟi avarvaḻak kuṭitta
kūṭal nāyakaṉ tāḷpaṇi yāreṉa
evvaḻik kiḷaviyiṉ kūṟic
cevvitiṉ cellum tiṟaṉiṉi yāṉē.       (29)

46. teḷital

niṉṟaṟi kalvi oṉṟiya māntar
puṉiperuṅ kaviyuḷ taruporuḷ eṉṉa
ōṅki puṭiparantu amutam uḷḷūṟi
kāṇkuṟi peruttu kaccavi kaṭintē
eḻuttumaṇi poṉpū maliyeṉa yāppuṟṟu       (5)
aṇiperu mulimēl kōtiyum oṭuṅkiṉa
ceṅkōl aracaṉ muṟittoḻil pōla
amutamum kaṭuvum vāḷum paṭitta
matarviḻit tāmari malarntimit tamarntaṉa
ceykuṟi muṭippavar ceṉmam pōlap       (10)
patamalar maṇmicip paṟṟip parantaṉa
amutam poṭitta muḻumati eṉṉa
mukamviyarppu uṟuttiṉa uḷḷamum cuḻaṉṟaṉa
itaḻiyum tumpiyum matiyamum karantu
vaḷivili mākkaḷ vaṭivu eḻuntaruḷi       (15)
muttamiḻ nāṉmaṟi muḷittaruḷ vākkāl
vīti kūṟi vitittamuṉ varattāl
karumukil viḷarppa aṟalnīr kuḷippa
kaṇpuki yāpput tiṇiiruḷ viṭiya
uṭaltoṟum piṇitta pāvamum pulara       (20)
kaṇṭanīḷ katuppiṉar kikuvi piṭittu
kurukuaṇi ceṟitta taṉimutal nāyakaṉ
kurukum aṉṉamum vālvaḷik kuppiyi
aṇṭamum pārppum āmeṉa aṇikkum
alinīrp paḻaṉa mutunakark kūṭal       (25)
oppaṭittu āyip poṟṟoṭi maṭanti
aṇaṅkiṉaḷ āmeṉa niṉiyal
piṇaṅki vīḻntu māḻkuṟum maṉaṉē.       (28)

47. melivu kaṇṭu cevili kūṟal

katirniri parappum maṇimuṭit tēvarkaḷ
kaṉavilum kāṇāp puṉivarun tiruvaṭi
mānilam tōyntōr vaṇikaṉ āki
eḻukatir virikkum tirumaṇi eṭuttu
variyāk kaṟpuṭaṉ nāṉkeṉap peyarpeṟṟu       (5)
āṅkāṅku āyira kōṭi cākikaḷ
miṭaloṭu virittu carukkam pāḻi
vīyā antam patamniri nātam
maṟippu puḷḷi mantiram oṭukkameṉṟu
iṉiyavi virittup palaporuḷ kūṟum       (10)
vētam muḷitta ētamil vākkāl
kuṭumic cēkarac camaṉoḷi cūḻnta
niṟimati nāṉkiṉ nikaḻntaṉa kuṟiyum
kuruvin tamceḷa kantikō vāṅku
cātaraṅ kameṉum cātikaḷ nāṉkum       (15)
tēkkiṉ neruppiṉ cērkkiṉ aṅkiyiṉ
tūkkiṉ takaṭṭiṉ cuṭarvāy veyiliṉ
kucciyiṉ mattakak kuṟiyiṉ ōrattiṉ
neyttup pārviyiṉ nērntu civantāṅku
otta naṟkuṇam uṭiyapaṉ ṉiraṇṭum       (20)
karukinoy tātal kāṟṟu vekuḷi
tirukal muraṇē cemmaṇ iṟukal
mattakak kuḻivu kācam iliccumi
eccam porivu pukital puṭāyam
canti neyppili eṉattaru patiṉāṟu       (25)
muntiya nūlil moḻintaṉa kuṟṟamum
cātakap puṭkaṇ tāmari kaḻunīr
kōpam miṉmiṉi koṭuṅkatir viḷakku
vaṉṉi mātuḷam pūviti eṉṉap
paṉṉucā taraṅka oḷikkuṇam pattum       (30)
cempañcu arattam tilakam ulōttiram
muyaliṉ cōri cinturam kuṉṟi
kavir‍alar eṉṉak kavarniṟam eṭṭum
kuruvin tattil kuṟittaṉa niṟamum
acōkap pallavam alari cempañcu       (35)
kōkilak kaṇnīṭu ilavalar cempeṉat
taruceḷa kanti taṉniṟam āṟum
ceṅkal kurāmalar mañcaḷ kōvi
kuṅkumam añcil kōvāṅku niṟamum
tiṭṭi ēṟu civanta vitāyam       (40)
okkal puṟṟām kuruti toḻuṉi
maṇikō kaṉakam kaṟpam pāṭi
māṅki cakanti vaḷarkāñcu uṇṭiyeṉṟu
āṅkoru patiṉmūṉṟu aṭintaṉa kuṟṟamum
iviyeṉak kūṟiya niṟiyaruṭ kaṭavuḷ       (45)
kūṭal kūṭā kuṇattiṉar pōla
muṉṉiyaḷ allaḷ muṉṉiyaḷ allaḷ
amutavāyk kaṭuviḻik kuṟuntoṭi neṭuṅkuḻal
peruntōḷ ciṟunaki muṉṉiyaḷ allaḷ
ulakiyal maṟanta katiyiṉar pōla       (50)
nammuḷ pārviyum vēṟuvēṟu āyiṉa
pakaḻicey kammiyar uḷḷam pōla
aimpulak kēḷum oruvāyp pukkaṉa
atiruvark kokkiṉ kaḷavuyir kuṭitta
pukarili neṭuvēl aṟumukak kuḷavaṉ       (55)
takaram kamaḻum neṭuvarik kāṭci
uṟṟaṉaḷ ātal vēṇṭum
ciṟṟiṭip peruntōḷ tēmoḻi tāṉē.       (58)

48. paruvaṅkaṇṭu perumakaḷ pulampal

pacimayal piṇitta piḷḷivaṇṭu araṟṟa

49. mukiloṭu kūṟal

karuṅkuḻaṟ cevvāyc ciṟṟiṭi maṭantikku

teyvak kulappaki viṇṇoṭum vimma       (5)

kōṭakaḻn teṭutta maṟinīrk kālum
veṅkaḷ peytu nāḷkuṟittu uḻunarum       (10)

50. taḻi viruppu urittal

51. viravik kūṟal

52. ūṭal taṇittal

avvuḻi avvuḻip peyuṇavu uṉṉi
mukaṉpeṟum iruceyal akaṉpeṟak koḷuvum
pullap pāṇmakaṉ cilliyum iṉṟi
iṉpak kiḷavi aṉpiṉarp pōkki
muṭittali maṉṉar cerukkunili oruvi       (5)
poṉṉuṟu ñāḻaṟ pūvuṭaṉ kaṭukkum
pēḻvāyp puliukir ciṟukural viḷaṅka
amutam tuḷikkum kumutavāy kutaṭṭip
paḻamkōḷ tatti vaḻaṅkucol pōlum
maḻalik kiḷaviyum irunilattu iṉpamum       (10)
oruvaḻi aḷikkum iruṅkatirc ciṟuvaṉi
taḻalviḻi maṭaṅkal koliarik kuruḷiyip

maṇikeḻu mārpattu aṇipeṟap pukutaliṉ
kaṟaṅkici aruvi aṟintunimir tivaliyum       (15)

neṭunili araṅkil paripeṟu taraḷamum
puṉampaṭa eṟinta kārakil tūmamum
antaṇar perukkiya centīp pukiyum       (20)
vēṅkiyiṉ tātuṭaṉ virumpiya curumpum
kantiviri paṭinta meṉciṟi vaṇṭum
cantaṉap poṅkart taḻicciṟi mayilum
muṉṟilam peṇṇik kuṭampikoḷ aṉṟilum
oṉṟi ṉoṭu oṉṟu ceṉṟutali mayaṅkum       (25)
kuḷavaṉ kuṉṟak kūṭalam patiniṟi
mañcaṭi kuḻalpeṟu ceñcaṭip perumāṉ
aruntamiḻk kīraṉ peruntamiḻp paṉuval
vāviyil kēṭṭa kāviam kaḷattiṉaṉ
tirukkaṇ kaṇṭa perukkiṉar pōl       (30)
muḷariam kōyil taḷiviṭa vantu
nallaṟam pūtta mulliam tiruviṉaḷ
niṉuḷattu iṉṉal maṉaṟak kaḷintu
poruttam kāṇṭi vaṇṭārum
aruttiam kōti maṉṉavaṉ pālē.       (35)

53. kulamuṟi kūṟi maṟuttal

perumaṟi nūlpeṟak kōṉmuṟi purakkum
peruntaki vēntaṉ aruṅkuṇam pōla
maṇantōrkku amutam taṇantōrkku eriyum
pukkuḻip pukkuḻip pulaṉpeṟak koṭukkum
maliyat tamiḻkkāl vāviyil pukuntu       (5)
pullitaḻt tāmarip putumuki aviḻppa
vaṇṭiṉam paṭintu matukkavarn tuṇṭu
cēyitaḻk kuvaḷiyiṉ niriniri uṟaṅkum
nilinīr nāṭaṉ nīyē ivaḷē
maliuṟi pakittu vāṉuṟikku aṇakkum       (10)
puṭkulam cūḻnta poruppuṭik kuṟavartam
peruntēṉ kavarum ciṟukuṭi makaḷē! nīyē
āyamoṭu ārppa arikiṇi muḻakki
māyā nallaṟam vaḷarnāṭ ṭiṉiyē! ivaḷē
toṇṭakam tuvippa toḻiṟpuṉam vaḷintu       (15)
pakaṭṭiṉam kollum paḻināṭ ṭavaḷē nīyē--
eḻunili māṭattu iḷamuli makaḷir
naṭamceyat taraḷa vaṭamteṟu nakarōy ivaḷē--
kaṭampeṟu karikkulam maṭaṅkalpukku akaḻat
teṟittiṭu muttam tiraṭṭuvip piṉaḷē       (20)
aṇikeḻu navamaṇi alareṉat toṭutta nīyē--
poṟkoṭit tērmicip polikuvi aṉṟē ivaḷē--
maṇivāyk kiḷḷi tuṇiyātu akaṟṟa
neṭṭitaṇ ēṟum ippuṉat tiṉaḷē
ātaliṉ, perumpukaḻ aṇikuti āyiṉ       (25)
nāraṇaṉ pāṟa tēvarkeṭ ṭōṭa
vaḷicuḻal vicumpiṉ kiḷarmukaṭu aṇavik
karumukil vaḷintu perukiyapōla
nilikeṭap paranta kaṭalkeḻu viṭatti
maṟittuavar uyirpeṟak kuṟittuṇ ṭaruḷi       (30)
tirukkaḷam kaṟutta aruṭpeṟu nāyakaṉ
kūṭal kūṭiṉar pōla,
nāṭal nī ivaḷ taḻittōḷ naciyē.       (33)
-------------

54. kāvaṟ pirivu aṟivittal

naṭittirip paravi nāṟkaṭal aṇittu
variyaṟuttu aminta vakināṉ kāka
vitivarat tiruttiya mētiṉip poṟiyi
kurumaṇi virittaliṉ tēṉoṭu kiṭantu
māyātu toṭutta maṇamalar cumattaliṉ       (5)
varieṉa niṟuttiya tiruvuṟi peruntōḷ
tarittum aṇittum tāṉeṉak kaṇṭum
ceytatum aṉṟi tirumaṇam paṇittuk
kākkavum kuricil karuttuṟum pōlum
viṭiyā vaṭanticey veḷḷiam cilampiṉum       (10)
teṉkāl viṭukkum cempiṉ poruppiṉum
koṇṭalvan tulavum nīlak kuvaṭṭiṉum
kōṭiceṉ nuṭaṟṟum kollik kiriyiṉum
piṟantavar piṟavāp perumpati yakattum
muṭintavar muṭiyā mūtūr iṭattum       (15)
kaṇṭavar kāṇāk kāṭcicey nakariṉum
vēkat taliyiṉum vitiā kamattiṉum
kalviyar uḷattum kalarneñ cakattum
teyvam viṭuttup poykoḷ cintiyiṉum
koliyiṉar kaṇṇum kuṉṟā tiyintu       (20)
veḷiyuṟat tōṉṟi iruḷuṟa maṟinta
viñcivan taruḷiya nañcaṇi miṭaṟṟōṉ
cantamum patamum carukkamum aṭakkamum
ciṉṉak kuṟaḷum ceḻuṅkār pōlap
perumaṟi muḻaṅkum tirunakark kūṭal       (25)
oppuṟ ṟaṭimalar cumanta
mippuṟak kūntal koṭivaṇaṅku iṭiyē!       (27)
-------------

55. uḷ makiḻntu urittal

nuṉikkaviṉ niṟinta tirupperu vaṭiviṉaḷ
uyirvittu uṭalam uḻaṉṟaṉa pōla
neṭumporuḷ īṭṭa niṟpirintu iṟantu
koṉṟuṇal añcāk kuṟiyiṉar pōkum
kaṭuñcuram tanta kallaḻal veppam       (5)
tēvar maruntum teṉtamiḻc cuviyum
eṉṉuyir yāviyum iṭṭaṭit tēnti
kuruviyum kuṉṟum kurumpiyum veṟuttaniṉ
perumuli mūḻkeṉ uḷattiṉil toṭāmuṉ
vīḻcuṟṟu oḻukkiya parārit tiruvaṭak       (10)
kuḷirniḻal iruntu kuṇacceyal mūṉṟum
uṭaloṭu paṭarum niliniḻal pōla
nīṅkāp pavattoki nikaḻmutal nāṉkum
uṭaṉiṟin toḻiyā uṭpaki aintum
matiyiṉiṉ paḻitta vaṭuiru mūṉṟum       (15)
aṇukātu akaṟṟi paṇimuṉi nālvarkku
aṟamutal nāṉkum peṟaaruḷ ceyta
kūṭaṟ perumāṉ nīṭaruḷ mūḻki
irupatam uḷvit tiruntavar viṉipōl
pōyiṉa tuṉiviṉi nōkki
ēkiṉa eṉakkē aṟputam tarumē!       (21)
-------------

56. puṉal āṭṭuvittami kūṟip pulattal

koṉṟiam tuṇaril cevvaḻi kuṟittu
vāluḷi erukkil vaḷaruḻi pāṭi
kūviḷaṅ kaṇṇiyil kulakkiḷi muraṟṟi
veṇkū taḷattil viḷariniṉ ṟicittu
vaṇṭum tēṉum ñimiṟum karumpum       (5)
umiḻnaṟavu arunti uṟaṅkuceñ caṭiyōṉ
matumalar paṟittuk tiruvaṭi niṟitta
nāṉmaṟip pālaṉi nalintuyir kavarum
kālaṟ kāynta kāliṉaṉ kūṭal
tirumaṟuku aṇintu varupuṉal viyi       (10)
varipuraṇ ṭeṉṉat tiriniri tuṟiyakattu
aṇinteṭut tēntiya arumpumukiḻ muliyōḷ
matinutal perumati malarmukattu oruttiyi
āṭṭiyum aṇittum kūṭṭiyum kulaviyum
ēntiyum eṭuttum oḻukkiyum īrttum       (15)
muḻakkiyum taputtiyum mulioḷi nōkkiyum
viḷimoḻi ēṟṟum vitaliyiṉ tiḷittum
pūciyum puṉintum pūṭṭiyum cūṭṭiyum
niṟuttiyum nirittum neṟittum ceṟittum
eḻutiyum tappiyum iyittum piṇittum       (20)
kaṭṭiyum kalattiyum kamaḻttiyum maṟittum
ceytaṉa ellām ceyyalar pōleṉ
neṭṭili polintapoṉ niṟitiru uṟiyuḷil
pācaṭik kuvaḷic cuḻalmaṇak kāṭṭiṉik
karuvaric ceṅkaṇ vacāliṉam kalakka       (25)
vērimalar muṇṭakattu aṭavitikku eṟiya
veḷḷuṭaṟ karuṅkaṇ kayalniri ukippa
marakatap paṉṉakattu āmpalam kuppiyic
‍cori eyiṟṟup pēḻvāy vāḷikaḷ tukippa
paṭintu cēṭeṟiyum ceṅkaṇ kavariyum       (30)
malicūḻ kiṭanta peruṅkulip parappum
maliyuṭaṉ a‍linta mutunīr veḷḷamum
miṭintu vayalirintu mutukucarin tuṭintu
ciṟiyōṉ cerueṉa muṟiyap pōki
uḻavak kaṇattik kulikkuṭil pukuttum       (35)
perunīr ūrar niṟinīr viṭuttuc
ceṟinta teṉeṉak kēṇmiṉ
maṟintuḻi viḻitta maṟinōk kiṉarē!       (38)
-------------

57. taṉṉi viyantu urittal

viṭamkotittu umiḻum paṭamkeḻu pakuvāyk
kaṇṭalmuṉ muḷitta kaṭieyiṟ ṟaravak
kuḻuviṉukku uṭintu kuḷirmati otuṅka
teyvap piṟiirunta tirunutaṟ pētiyik
kaṇṭukaṇṭu aravam mayileṉak kalaṅka       (5)
neṭuñcaṭik kāṭṭiṉi aṭumtīk koḻunteṉa
taliētu aliyā nakutali tayaṅka
aṇitali māliyi niṟimatit tiraḷeṉap
puṭipuṭi otuṅki aravuvāy piḷappa
oṉṟiṉukku oṉṟu kaṉṟiya naṭukkoṭu       (10)
kiṭantoḷi piṟaḻum neṭuñcaṭip perumāṉ
paṭināṉku uṭaṉṟu pañcavaṉ turantu
maturi vavviya karunaṭa vēntaṉ
arukarc cārntuniṉṟu aruṭpaṇi aṭippa
maṟṟavaṉ taṉṉi neṭuntuyil varutti       (15)
iṟiyavaṉ kulattu muṟiyar iṉmiyāl
karuti tōri kalloṭu piṟaṅka
meyyaṇi aḷaṟāk kimmuḻam tēytta
pēraṉpu uruvap pacukkā valaṉi
ulakiṉil tamatu mukkuṟi yāk       (20)
maṇimuṭi vēṇiyum uruttirak kalaṉum
nilavumiḻ puṇṇiyap pālniṟac cāntamum
aṇivit taruḷkoṭuttu aracaṉ ākki
aṭumāl akaṟṟi neṭunāḷ purakka
viyakam aḷitta maṇioḷik kaṭavuḷ       (25)
neṭumaṟik kūṭal viripuṉal viyiyuḷ
piṭikuḷi ceyyum kaḷiṟatu pōla
mayileṉum cāyal orumati nutaliyi
marumamum tōḷiṉum variyaṟap pulli
āṭṭuṟum ūraṉ aṉpukoḷ nalattiṉi       (30)
poṉṉulaku uṇṭavar maṇṇulaku iṉpam
talinaṭuk kuṟṟa taṉmi pōla
oṉṟaṟa akaṟṟi uṭaṉkalan tilaṉēl
aṉṉa ūraṉi emmil koṭuttu
tēriṉum kāliṉum aṭikkaṭi kaṇṭu       (35)
neṭṭuyirppu eṟintu neṭuṅkaṇ nīrukuttup
piṉṉum taḻuva uṉṉum avvorutti
avaḷē ākuvaḷ yāṉē tavalaruṅ
karunīrk kuṇṭu akaḻuṭutta
perunīr āḻit tollula kuḻikkē.       (40)
-------------

58. putalvaṉ mēlvittup pulavi tīrtal

aṭiyavar uḷattiruḷ akaṟṟaliṉ viḷakkum
eḻumali poṭittaliṉ aṉalteṟum acaṉiyum
karuṅkaṭal kuṭittaliṉ peruntaḻaṟ koḻuntum
maravuyir vuvaliṉ tīviḻik kūṟṟum
eṉṉuḷam iruttaliṉ iyintuṇar uyirum       (5)
nacciṉa koṭuttaliṉ naḷirtaru aintum
karuvaḻi nīkkaliṉ uyarnilik kuruvum
irunilam kāttaliṉ matiyuṭi vēntum
ākiya maṇivēl cēvalam koṭiyōṉ
vāṉaka maṅkiyum tēṉvari vaḷḷiyum       (10)
irupuṟam taḻitta tiruniḻal irukkum
oru paraṅ kuṉṟam maruviya kūṭal
perunatic caṭimicic ciṟumati cūṭiya
nāyakaṉ tiruvaṭi naṇṇalar pōla
poypala pukaṉṟu meyoḷittu iṉpam       (15)
viṟṟuṇum cēri viṭātuṟi ūraṉ
ūruṇi otta potuvāyt tampalam
nīyum kutaṭṭiṉi āyiṉ - cēyāy!
narampeṭuttu umiḻum perumulit tīmpāṟku
uḷḷamum toṭātu viḷḷamutu oḻukkum       (20)
kutalivāy tuṭippak kulakkaṭi uṇaṅkiyum
maṇṇuṟu maṇiyeṉap pūḻimey vāyttum
puṭimaṇi viritta uṭimaṇi iḻukkiyum
cuṭikiyum cikiyum cērntukaṇ paṉittum
paṟiyum tērum paṟipaṭṭu aṇaṅkiyum       (25)
maṟikkaṇ piṇāviṉar iḻikkum ciṟṟilil
ceṉṟaḻi yātu niṉṟayar kaṇṭum
uṟuvatum ippayaṉ aṉṟēl,
peṟuvatu eṉpāl iṉṟuniṉ pēṟē.       (29)
-------------

59. talivi tōḻiyoṭu pukaltal

naṭimali piṭitta corieyiṟṟu iṭaṅkari
āḻi valavaṉ aṭarttaṉa pōla
puṉtali mēti puṉaleḻa muṭṭiya
varivuṭaṟ ceṅkaṇ varāluṭaṉ mayaṅka
uḷkavit tūṇṭil urampukuntu uḻakkum       (5)
niṟinīr ūrar neñcakam pirikkum
piṇimoḻip pāṇaṉ uṭaṉuṟi nīkki
nūloṭu tuvaḷum tōltiri urattiṉ
mālkaḻittu aṭutta narimutir tāṭicey
veḷḷi kumiḻtta verūukkaṇ pārppāṉ       (10)
kōluṭaṉ paṭarum kuṟunaki oruvi
pūvilit toḻilmakaṉ kāval kiviṭṭu
tikkuviṇ paṭarnati tirumati kayili
nāmakaḷ peruṅkaṭal nāṟkōṭṭu oruttal
puṇṇiyam ivimutal veḷḷuṭal koṭukkum       (15)
pukaḻkkavip pāvalar puṇarcci iṉpakaṟṟi
ellāk kalviyum ikaḻccicey kalviyar
perunakik kūṭṭamum kaḻivuceytu ivviṭi
mayakkuṟu māli māmakaḷ etira
oruvaḻip paṭarntatu eṉṉat tirumukam       (20)
āyiram eṭuttu vāṉvaḻip paṭarntu
maṇṇēḻ uruvi maṟiyap pāyum
peruṅkatat tirunati oruṅkuḻi maṭaṅka
aimpaki aṭakkiya aruntava muṉivaṉ
irantaṉa varattāl orucaṭi iruttiya       (25)
kūṭal perumāṉ kurikaḻal kūṟum
cemmiyar pōla kōṭā
nammiyum nōkkiṉar ciṟitukaṇ purintē.       (28)
-------------

60. vaḻipāṭu kūṟal

niriitaḻ tiṟantu matukaṇṭu aruttum
viruntukoḷ malarum purintuṟi maṇamum
centamiḻp pāṭalum tēkkiya poruḷum
pālum cuviyum paḻamum iratamum
uṭalum uyirum oṉṟiyatu eṉṉa       (5)
kaṇṭum teḷintum kalantuḷ uṇarvāl
pālum amutamum tēṉum piliṟṟiya
iṉpamar colli naṇpum maṉakkuṟiyum
vāymiyum ciṟappum niḻaleṉak kaṭavār
viṇṇavar talivaṉum vīyā maruntum       (10)
aḷakikku iṟiyum arumporuḷ īṭṭamum
kaṇṇaṉum kāvalum muṉiyum pacuvum
oṉṟiṉum tavaṟā oḻuṅku iyintaṉapōl
nīṭiniṉ ṟutavum kaṟpuṭi niliyiṉar
tavamakaṟ ṟīṉṟa neṭuṅkaṟpu aṉṉi       (15)
muṉoru nāḷil mutaltoḻil iraṇṭiṉar
paṉṟiyum paṟaviyum niṉṟuru eṭuttu
kaviyā uḷattuk kāṇum kaḻalum
kalviyil aṟivil kāṇum muṭiyum
aḷavuceṉ ṟeṭṭā aḷaviṉar āki       (20)
maṇṇum umparum akaḻntum paṟantum
aḷavā nōṉmiyil neṭunāḷ varuntik
kaṇṇiṉil kāṇātu uḷattiṉil puṇarātu
niṉṟaṉa kaṇṭu neṭumpayaṉ paṭitta
tiruañ ceḻuttum kuṟiyātu iraṭṭa       (25)
irunilam uruviya orutaḻal tūṇattu
erimaḻu navvi tamarukam amitta
nāṟkaram nutalviḻi tīppuki kaṭukkaḷam
ulakupeṟ ṟeṭutta orutaṉic celvi
kaṭṭiya vēṇi maṭṭalar kaṭukki       (30)
āyiram tirumukattu aruḷnati ciṟumati
pakitavir pāmpum nakipeṟum erukkamum
oṉṟiya tiruvuru niṉṟunaṉi kāṭṭip
pēraruḷ koṭutta kūṭalam patiyōṉ
patamiraṇṭu amitta uḷḷak
katiyiraṇṭu āya ōraṉ piṉarē.       (36)
-------------

61. ātaram kūṟal

neṭuvarip poṅkarp puṉameri kārakil
karumpuki vāṉam kiyuṟap potintu
taruniḻal tēvar tamuṭal paṉippap
paṭarnteṟi kaṅki viṭumkuḷir akaṟṟum
poṉṉam poruppa! niṉuḷat tiyiyiṉ       (5)
kaṉaltalip paḻutta tiraḷparal murampu
vayalvaḷi kakkiya maṇinirip parappē
atarvirin teḻunta paṭarpuki nīḻal
potuḷiya kāñci marutaṇi niḻalē!
tīvāyp pulippaṟ ciṟukural eyiṟṟiyar       (10)
kaḻunīr miliyum vayalmā tiṉarē
ayaṟpulam aṟiyum eyiṉar māttuṭi
naṭunakark kiraṭṭum kaḷiari kiṇiyē!
iruḷkavar pulaṉeṉac cuḻaltarum cūṟi
matumalar aḷinta maliyak kālē       (15)
eḻuciṟi tīyum eruviyum paruntum
kuvaḷiyam kāṭṭuk kurukoṭu putāvē
valiyaḻi pakaṭu vāynīrc cennāy
taḻimaṭi mētiyum piṇariṭaṅ karumē
paṭṭular kaḷḷi neṟṟuṭi vāki       (20)
curuḷviri cāliyum kuliaram piyumē
vaṭatiru āla vāytiru naṭuvūr
veḷḷi yampalam naḷḷāṟu intiri
pañcava ṉīccaram añceḻuttu amitta
ceṉṉi māpuram cēraṉ tiruttaḷi       (25)
kaṉṉiceṅ kōṭṭam kariyōṉ tiruvuṟi
viṇṇuṭit tuṇṇum kaṇṇili oruttaṉ
maṟitirik kaṭaluḷ māveṉak kaviḻnta
kaḷavuṭaṟ piḷanta oḷikeḻu tiruvēl
paṇippaki ūrti aruṭkoṭi iraṇṭuṭaṉ       (30)
muṉṉum piṉṉum mutukkoḷa niṟinta
aruviam cāral oruparaṅ kuṉṟam
cūḻkoḷa irunta kūṭalam perumāṉ
muḻutum niṟinta irupatam pukaḻār
pōmvaḻi eṉṉum kaṭuñcuram marutam
māmi ūrum maṇiniṟattu ivaṭkē.       (36)
-------------

62. mukam kaṇṭu makiḻtal

niṟimati puriyā niṟimati puriyā
tērāṉ teḷiveṉum tirukkuṟaḷ pukuntu
kuṟimati maṉaṉē niṟimati puriyā
uvarkkaṭaṟ piṟantum kuṟivuṭal kōṭiyum
karuṅkavit tīnāp perumpoṟip pakuvāyt       (5)
taḻalviḻip pāntaḷ tāṉiri māntiyum
miccil umiḻntu meyyuḷ kaṟuttum
taṇantōrkku erintum maṇantōrkku aḷittum
kumutam malarttiyum kamalam kuvittum
kaṭalcūḻ ulakil matinaṭu ikantum       (10)
perumaṟi kūṟi aṟiviti tōṟum
muttaḻaṟku uṭiyōṉ mukkaṭ kaṭavuḷeṉṟu
uyttiṭum vaḻakkuk kiṭakkeṉ ṟorukāl
vāṉavar natikkari maruḷmakam eṭutta
tīkkuṇat takkaṉ cerukkaḷam taṉṉuḷ       (15)
kaṇtoṟum vicitta karupput taraḷamum
vaḷiumiḻ āramum curimukac caṅkum
valampurik kūṭṭamum calañcalap puñcamum
nantiṉ kuḻuvum vayalvayal nanti
uḻavak kaṇattar paṭivāḷ niṟuttum       (20)
kūṭaṟku iṟiyōṉ kurikaḻaṟ paṭiyāl
īreṇ kaliyum pūḻipaṭ ṭutira
nilaṉoṭu tēyppuṇṭu alamantu ulaṟiyum
citintu ninteḻu paḻittī matipuriyā
muṇṭakam malartti mutirātu alarntum       (25)
amutam niṉṟirittum aṟivu aṟivittum
tīkkatir uṭaluḷ cellā tiruntum
tiḷiyāt tārikaḷ cērā
muḷiyā veṉṟi ivaḷmuka matikkē.       (29)
-------------

63. āyattu uyttal

vaṭavaṉattu orunāḷ māṟupaṭṭu etirntu
vaḻinaṭam taṉatu marakkāl aṉṟi
mutaltoḻil patumaṉ muṉṉāy avvuḻi
māṉtalik karattiṉil kūṭi vayakki
tūkkal vaḷiyuṭaṉ toṭarpatam eṟintu       (5)
maṟṟataṉ tāḷam puttiri ākki
nimirtteṟi kālil kaṭikkaṇ kiṭatti
pāṇiyil cirampatittu orunaṭi patittu
koṭukoṭ ṭikkuk kuṟiaṭuttu eṭukkum
puṅkam vāram puṭinili poṟuttu       (10)
cacca puṭattil taṉieḻu māttiri
oṉṟiviṭ ṭorucīr iraṇṭuṟa uṟutti
eṭuttut tuḷḷiya iṉamut tirikku
maṅkalap pāṇi māttiri nāṉkuṭaṉ
ceṉṟeṟintu oṭuṅkum turumiṭi tirutti       (15)
ñeḷḷalil kuṉitta irumāt tirikkup
paṭṭaṭi eṭukkap pulutam parappi
puṟakkāl maṭittu kuṟitteṟi niliyam
patiṉāṉku amittu viṭumāt tirikku
vaṉmamum pitāvum pāṇiyil vakuttu       (20)
vaṭṭam koṭukkum intiri paṇikku
māttiri āṟuṭaṉ kumpam patittu
valavi iṭākiṉi maṇirun teṭutta
kāluṭaṉ cuḻala āṭiya kāḷi
nāṇiniṉ ṟoṭuṅkat tāṉumōr nāṭakam       (25)
pāṇṭaraṅ kattoru pāṭupeṟ ṟaminta
mōkap puyaṅka muṟittuṟi tūkki
ataṟkuc cāraṇi aruṭkaram oṉṟil
pāṇi iraṇṭum tāḷam ākki
orutāḷ mitittu viṇuṟa viṭṭa       (30)
maṟutāḷ malaril malarkkaram tuṭakki
pārppatip pāṇiyit tuṭimaṇi eṭuppa
curutiyit taṇṭi valikoṇṭu amippa
mutalēḻ ataṉi oṉṟiṉukku ēḻeṉa
vīṇi patittu tāṉam terikka       (35)
muṉtuṭi maṇiyil oṟṟiya pāṇiyi
nātam kūṭṭi māttiri aṟuttu
māṅkaṉi iraṇṭil āmkaṉi oṉṟāl
muṉoru nāḷil muḻukkati aṭinta
ammip peyarpeṟum aruṭpēy piṭippa       (40)
pūtamum kūḷiyum pēyum kuḷippa
amararkaṇ kaḷippa āṭiya perumāṉ
maturiyam patieṉum orukoṭi maṭanti
cīṟitaḻc cātip perumaṇam pōla
niṉṉuḷam niṟinta neṭuṅkaṟpu ataṉāl       (45)
viṉiyuṭal puṇara varumuyir paṟṟip
puṇṇiyam toṭarum puṇacci pōla
kālamuṟ ṟōṅkum nīḷmukil kūṭi
maṇitaru teruvil koṭineṭun tērum
nāṟkuṟip pulavar kūṭṭeḻu naṉipukaḻ       (50)
maruntayil vāḻkkiyar maṇinakar uruviṉa
uruḷeḻu pūmiyum avvuruḷ pūṇṭa
kaliṉamāṉ tukaḷum katirmaṟi niḻaliṉ
niṉṟumuṉ iṭṭa niṟiaṇi poṟuttu
peruṅkulik kayattuk karuntāḷ kaḻunīr       (55)
niṟiviṉuḷ pūtta tāmari oṉṟeṉa
niṉṉuyir āya nāppaṇ
maṉṉuka vēntaṉ varaviṉukku eḻuntē.       (58)
-------------

64. kaṟpup payappu urittal

eḻukaṭal vaḷinta peruṅkaṭal nāppaṇ
pattuṭi nūṟu poṟpamar parappum
āyirattu iraṭṭik kīḻmēl niliyum
yōcaṉi uṭutta mācaṟu kāṭcip
paṉikkup poruppil tiṭarkoḷ mūtūrk       (5)
kaḷavuṭi vāḻkki uḷamaṉak koṭiyōṉ
paṭarmali ēḻum kurukamar poruppum
māeṉak kaviḻnta maṟikaṭal oṉṟum
kaṭuṅkaṉal pūḻi paṭumpaṭi nōkkiya
tāri eṭṭuṟiyum kūrili neṭuvēl       (10)
kāṟpaṭik koṭiyiṉaṉ karuṇayoṭu amarnta
puṇṇiyak kuṉṟam puṭipoli kūṭal
piṟiccaṭi muṭiyiṉaṉ pēraruḷ aṭiyavarkku
orukāl tavaṟā uṭimit teṉṉa
piriyāk kaṟpeṉum niṟiyuṭaṉ vaḷarnta       (15)
neṭuṅkayal eṟiviḻik kuṟuntoṭit tiruviṉaḷ
teyvameṉ ṟorukāl teḷiyavum uḷattilaṉ
palavuyir taḻikka orukuṭi niḻaṟṟum
irukula vēntar maṟupulap perumpaki
nīrvaṭup poruva niṟuttiṭap paṭariṉum       (20)
ēḻuyar iraṭṭi matalinaṭ ṭamitta
taṉpaḻaṅ kūṭam taṉinili aṉṟi
uṭunili vāṉap perumukaṭu uyarac
ceyyumōr kūṭam puṇarttiṉ
neymmiti uṇṇātu avaṉkaṭak kaḷiṟē.       (25)
-------------

65. maruvutal urittal

peṇeṉap peyariya perumakaḷ kulaṉuḷ
uṇānilaṉ uṇṭu parāya ap peruntavam
kaṇṇuṟa uruppeṟum kāṭciyatu eṉṉak
karuvuyirt teṭutta kuṭimutal aṉṉi
niṉṉiyum kaṭantatu aṉṉavaḷ aruṅkaṟpu       (5)
arikaṭal mūḻkip peṟum aruḷ peṟṟa
nilamakaḷ kaṭantatu nalaṉavaḷ poṟiyē
iruviṉi nāṭi uyirtoṟum amitta
ūḻiyum kaṭantatu vāymiyiṉ mataṉē
kaṟpakam pōlum aṟputam paḻutta       (10)
niṉ ilam kaṭantatu aṉṉavaḷ illam
pērā vāyminiṉ ūraṉik kaṭantatu
maṟṟavaḷ ūraṉ koṟṟa veṇkuṭiyē
ēḻuḷip puraviyōṭu eḻukatir nōkkiya
ciṟṟili neriñcil poṟpū eṉṉa       (15)
niṉmukak kiḷiyiṉar tammiyum kaṭantaṉar
maṟṟavaḷ pārtta matikkiḷi yiṉarē
uṭalniḻal māṉa uṉataruḷ niṟkum
eṉṉiyum kaṭantaṉaḷ poṉṉavaṭku iṉiyōṉ
kolimatil mūṉṟum ikalaṟak kaṭantu       (20)
perunilavu eṟitta pukarmukat tuḷikki
poḻimatak kaṟiyaṭi aḻitarak kaṭantu
kaḷavil toḻilcey arimakaṉ uṭalam
tirunutal nōkkattu eripeṟak kaṭantu
māṟukoṇṭu aṟiyum matinūl kaṭalkiḷar       (25)
camayak kaṇakkar tamtiṟam kaṭantu
pulaṉoṭu tiyaṅkum poyuḷam kaṭanta
malaruṭaṉ niṟintu vāṉvaḻi kaṭanta
poḻilniṟi kūṭal putumatic caṭiyōṉ
maṉnili kaṭa vā maṉattavar pōla       (30)
oṉṉavar iṭumtiṟic celiṉum
taṉnili kaṭavātu avaṉparit tērē.       (32)
-------------

66. paḷḷiṭattu ūṭal

nīrara makaḷir nerukkupu pukuntu
kaṇmukam kāṭṭiya kāṭcittu eṉṉa
peruṅkuli maṇanta niṟinīrc ciṟippuṉal
maṇiniṟap paṭāmmutuku iṭiyaṟap pūttu
curumpoṭu kiṭanta coriitaḻt tāmari       (5)
kaṇṇiṉum koḷḷātu uṇṇavum peṟātu
niḻaltali maṇantu puṉalkiṭa vātu
viṇuṭit tuṇṇum viṉiccūr kavarnta
vāṉavar maṅkiyar mayakkam pōla
piṇarkkaru maruppiṉ pitirpaṭa uḻakki       (10)
veṇkārk kaḻaṉik kurukeḻap pukuntu
kaṭukkic ciṟukāy amittavāṟ karuppi
iṇieyiṟu eṉṉa iṭi iṭi muḷpayil
kuṟumputal muṇṭakam karumpeṉat tuyttu
ceṅkaṇ pakaṭu taṅkuvayal ūrarkku       (15)
arumaṟi vitiyum ulakiyal vaḻakkum
karuttuṟi poruḷum vitippaṭa niṉintu
vaṭacol mayakkamum varuvaṉa puṇartti
aintiṇi vaḻuvātu akapporuḷ amutiṉi
kuṟumuṉi tēṟavum peṟumutal pulavarkaḷ       (20)
ēḻeḻu peyarum kōtaṟap parukavum
pulaṉeṟi vaḻakkil puṇarulaka varkkum
muṉtavam perukkum mutaltā patarkkum
niṉṟaṟin tuṇara tamiḻppeyar niṟutti
eṭuttup parappiya imiyavar nāyakaṉ       (25)
meyttavak kūṭaviḷilporuḷ maṅkiyar
mukattiṉum kaṇṇiṉum muṇṭaka muliyiṉum
colliṉum tuvakkum pullam pōla
emmiṭattu ilatāl eṉṉi
tammuḷam tavaṟip pōntatu ivviṭaṉē.       (30)
-------------

67. vaḻippaṭuttu urittal

ceṅkōl tiruvuṭaṉ teḷintaṟam perukkiya
maṟupula vēntaṉ uṟupaṭi etirnta
koṭuṅkōl koṟṟavaṉ neṭumpaṭi aṉittum
cēra iṟanta *tiruktaku nāḷil
avaṉpaḻi nāṭṭu naṭuṅkunaṟ kuṭikaḷ       (5)
kaṇṇoṭu kaṇṇil kaḻaṟiya pōla
oruvariṉ oruvar uḷḷattu aṭakkit
tōṉṟā nakiyuṭaṉ tuṇṭamum cuṭṭi
ampal tūṟṟum ilvūr aṭakki
kaṭalkiṭan taṉṉa niri niri āya       (10)
veḷḷamum maṟṟavar kaḷḷamum kaṭantu
tāyavar mayaṅkum taṉittuyar niṟutti
paṟavi makkaḷip pariyunark koṭuttu
kiṭippal yāṉē nummi taḻitteḻu
tāḷiyum koṉṟiyum taḻittaliṉ mulliyum       (15)
pāntaḷum tarakkum payiltaliṉ kuṟiñciyum
muṭittali eripoṭi uṭimiyiṉ pāliyum
āmiyum calamum mēvaliṉ marutamum
kaṭuvum caṅkum oḷirtaliṉ neytalum
ākat taṉatu pēraruḷ mēṉiyil       (20)
tiṇiaintu amitta iṇiili nāyakaṉ
varumtoḻil aṉittum vaḷarperum pakalē
erivirin taṉṉa itaḻppal tāmari
aruḷmukat tiruvoṭu malarmukam kuviya
marakatap pācaṭi iṭiyiṭi nāppaṉ       (25)
nīlamum maṇiyum nirikiṭan teṉṉa
vaṇṭoṭu kumutam malarntu itaḻvirippa
kurukum cēvalum pārppuṭaṉ veruvip
pācaṭik kuṭampi yūṭukaṇ paṭuppa
tuṇiyuṭaṉ cakōram kaḷiyuṭaṉ peyarntu       (30)
viṭum amu tarunta viṇṇakattu aṇakka
curivaḷic cāttu niṟimati tavaḻum
eṟitirip paḻaṉak kūṭal
ceṟika iṉṟamma tiruvoṭum polintē       (35)
-------------

68. kaṭaliṭi vittut tuyar aṟivittal

irunilam tāṅkiya valikeḻu nōṉmip
poṉmuṭic cayilak kaṇavaṟ puṇarntu
tiruveṉum kuḻaviyum amuteṉum piḷḷiyum
matiyeṉum makavum amarulaku aṟiyak
kaṇṇoṭu muttam kaluḻntu uṭal kalaṅki       (5)
vāyviṭ ṭalaṟi vayiṟunon tīṉṟa
maṉaṉ eḻu varuttam atuuṭiyi ātaliṉ
perumayal eytā niṟiyiṉaḷ āka
eṉoru mayilum niṉmakaḷāk koṇṭu
tōṉṟiniṉ ṟaḻiyāt tukaḷaṟu peruntavam       (10)
nitieṉak kaṭṭiya kuṟumuṉikku aruḷuṭaṉ
taraḷa mum cantum erikeḻu maṇiyum
muṭaṅkuḷi akaḻnta koṭuṅkarik kōṭum
akilum kaṉakamum aruvikoṇ ṭiṟaṅkip
poruniam kaṉṉikku aṇiaṇi pūṭṭum       (15)
cempuṭal potinta teyvap potiyamum
uvaṭṭātu aṇiyātu uṇarveṉum paciyeṭuttu
uḷḷamum ceviyum urukiniṉṟu uṇṇum
peruntamiḻ amutum piriyātu koṭutta
tōṭaṇi kaṭukkik kūṭalem perumāṉ       (20)
evvuyir iruntum avvuyir ataṟkut
tōṉṟātu aṭaṅkiya toṉmit teṉṉa
ārtteḻu peruṅkural amintuniṉ ṟoṭuṅkiniṉ
peruntīk kuṇaṉum oḻintuḷam kuḷiruṟum
ipperu naṉṟi iṉṟeṟku utavuti       (25)
eṉiṉpatam paṇikuval aṉṟē naṉkamar
pavaḷa vāyum kiḷarpac cuṭampum
neṭuṅkayal viḻiyum niṟimali muliyum
mācaṟap paṭittu maṇivuṭal niṟitta
perumukil vayiṟaḷavu ūṭṭit
tiruvularu aḷikkum kaṭalmaṭa makaḷē!       (31)
-------------

69. pirivu iṉmi kūṟal

niliyuṭip peruntiru nērpaṭu kālik
kālāl taṭuttuk kaṉaṉṟu etirkaṟuttum
naṉiniṟi celva nāṭum naṉporuḷum
etirpeṟiṉ kaṇcivantu eṭuttavi kaḷintum
tāmari nitiyamum vālvaḷit taṉamum       (5)
illam pukutara iruṅkatavu aṭittum
ariayaṉ amarar malivaṭam pūṭṭip
peruṅkaṭal vayiṟu kiṭaṅkeḻak kaṭinta
amutam uṭkiyil utavuḻi ūṟṟiyum
meyyulaku iraṇṭiṉuḷ ceykunar uḷarēl       (10)
eḻukatir virikkum maṇikeḻu tiruntiḻi
niṉpirivu uḷḷum maṉaṉuḷaṉ ākuka
muḻutuṟa niṟinta poruḷmaṉam niṟuttimuṉ
vēṭam tuṟavā vitiyuṭic cākkiyaṉ
aruṭkari kāṇā aṉpeṉum peruṅkaṭal       (15)
palanāḷ peruki orunāḷ uṭintu
karinili iṉṟik kiyakaṉ ṟiṭalum
eṭuttuṭik kalmalar toṭuttavi cāttiya
pēroḷi iṇiyāk kūṭal māmaṇi
kulamalik kaṉṉiyeṉ ṟaruḷkuṭi yirukkum       (20)
vitineṟi tavaṟā orupaṅku uṭimiyum
paṟavi cellātu neṭumukuṭu uruviya
cēkarattu uṟaṅkum tirunatit tuṟiyum
neṭumpakal ūḻi niṉivuṭaṉ nīntiṉum
aruṅkari iṟanta ākamak kaṭalum       (25)
iḷaṅkō viṉarkaḷ iraṇṭaṟi peyarum
aṉṉamum paṉṟiyum olliyiṉ eṭuttup
paṟantum akaḻntum paṭiyitu eṉṉātu
aṟivakaṉṟu uyarnta kaḻalmaṇi muṭiyum
uṭimiyaṉ poṟkaḻal pēṇi
aṭiyalar pōla maruḷmaṉam tirintē.       (31)
-------------

70. ūṭal nīṭa vāṭi urittal

niṟivaḷi īṭṭamum taraḷak kuppiyum
aṉṉak kuḻuvum kurukaṇi iṉamum
karuṅkōṭṭup puṉṉi arumputir kiṭiyum
muṭaveṇ tāḻi ūḻttamuḷ malarum
alavaṉ kavikkāl aṉṉaveḷ alakum       (5)
vālukap parappiṉ valivalitu oṟṟiṉarkku
īteṉa aṟiyātu oṉṟiveḷ iṭiyām
mātuṭik kaḻikkaric cēriōr pāṅkar
puḷḷoṭu piṇaṅkum puḷkava rātu
veḷḷiṟa uṇaṅkal cēval āka       (10)
ulakuyir kavarum kolinilik kūṟṟam
makaḷeṉat taritta niliaṟi kuvaṉēl
viṇkuṟit teḻuntu mēlavarp puṭi ttu
nāṉmukaṉ tāṅkum tēṉuṭit tāmari
itaḻum koṭṭiyum citaṟak kutarntu       (15)
vāṉavarkku iṟivaṉ kaṭavukār piṭittup
pañceḻap piḻintu taṇpuṉal paruki
ainteṉap peyariya neṭumaram oṭittu
kaṇuḷattu aḷavā eḷḷuṇa vuṇṭu
poriyeṉat tārakik kaṇaṉuṭal kotti       (20)
aṭumtiṟal aṉiya koṭuntoḻil perukkiya
māyā varatta peruṅkuruku aṭittu
veṇciṟi muṭitta ceñcaṭip perumāṉ
kūṭaṟku iṟiyōṉ kuṟiuru kaṭanta
irupatam uḷattavar pōla
maruvutal oruvum matiyā kuvaṉē.       (26)
-------------

71. paṅkayattōṭu parivuṟṟu urittal

cilinutal kaṇiviḻit teriviyar uḷameṉa
āḻntakaṉ ṟiruṇṭa ciṟinīrk kayattuḷ
erivirin taṉṉa paltaṉat tāmari
neṭumayal pōrtta uṭaloru vēṟku
kurumaṇi koḻikkum puṉalmalik kōṭṭuḻi       (5)
niṉpati maṟinta neṭṭira vakattuḷ
kurukum puḷḷum arukaṇi cūḻa
tēṉoṭum vaṇṭoṭum tiruvoṭum keḻumi
peruntuyil iṉpam poruntupu naṭunāḷ
kāṇūmniṉ kaṉavuḷ nam kavarmaṉat tavarik       (10)
koyyuḷik kaṭumāṉ koḷuviya tēroṭu
pūvutir kāṉal puṟamkaṇ ṭaṉaṉeṉa
ciṟitoru vāymi utaviṉi aṉṟēl
cēkaram kiḻitta niṟimati uṭalam
kali kali cintiya kāṭciyatu eṉṉa       (15)
kaṭumāṉ kīḻnta kaṭamalip palmaruppu
eṭutteṭut tunti maṇikkulam citaṟi
kiḷiñarkaḷ naccāp poruḷiṉar pōla
cātakam veṟuppa carintakaḻn tārttu
tiraḷpaḷiṅ kuṭittuc citaṟuva teṉṉa       (20)
vaḻiyetir kiṭanta ulamuṭaṉ tākki
vēṅkiyum poṉṉum ōruḻit tiraṭṭi
variyara makaḷirkku aṇiyaṇi koṭuttu;
paṉikkik kaṭamā eruttuṟu pūḻi
vaṇṭeḻun tārppa maṇi eṭuttu alampi       (25)
mayilciṟi āṟṟa valimukam paṉippa
etircuṉik kuvaḷi malarppuṟam paṟittu
variyuṭaṉ niṟiya māliyiṭ ṭāṅku
neṭumuṭi aruvi akiloṭu koḻikkum
kayilivīṟ ṟirunta kaṇṇutal viṇṇavaḷ       (30)
nāṭakak kaṭavuḷ kūṭal nāyakaṉ
tāmari uṭitta kāmar cēvaṭi
niṟivuḷam tarittavar pōla
kuṟivuḷam nīṅki iṉpā kuvaṉē.       (34)

72. pati paricu urittal

eriteṟak karukiya poṭi poṟuttu iyaṅkiṉi
mukiltali cumantu ñimiṟeḻuntu icikkum
poṅkaruḷ paṭutta malarkāl poruntuka!
kaṭuṅkaṭattu eṟinta koṭumpulikku oṭuṅkiṉi
variuṭaṟ ceṅkaṇ varāliṉam etirppa       (5)
uḻavak kaṇattar uṭivatu nōkkuka
koliñar polinta koṭittērkku aṇaṅkiṉi
vētiyar nitimika vitimakam muṟṟi
avapira tattuṟi āṭutal keḻumi
poṉṉuruḷ viyam pōvatu kāṇka       (10)
āṟali eyiṉar amarkkalikku aḻuṅkiṉi
paṇitteḻu cāli nerukkupu pukuntu
kaḻunīr kaḷinar tam kampali kāṇka
taḻaltalip paṭutta paralmurampu aṭuttaṉi
curimukak kuḻuvaḷi nilaveḻac corinta       (15)
kuḷirveṇ taraḷak kuvālivi kāṇka
alakineṭ ṭiratam puṉaleṉak kāṭṭiṉi
vaṉmīṉ neṭuṅkayal potiviṉi yakattuk
kiṭaṅkeṉap peyariya karuṅkaṭal kāṇka
muṉakarkaḷ pūcal tuṭioli ēṟṟaṉi       (20)
kuṭumiam ceṉṉiyar karumukil viḷarppak
kiṭimuṟi eṭukkum maṟioli cēṇmati
amararkaḷ muṉikkaṇat tavarmuṉ tavaṟu
purintuṭaṉ umikaṇ putippa maṟṟumiyum
āṭakac cayilac cēkaram toṭarnta       (25)
oṟṟiyam pacuṅkaḻi olkiya pōla
ulakuyirk kuyireṉum tiruvuru aṇintu
vaḷikkaram koṭukaṇ putippa avvuḻiyē
ulakiruḷ turakkum ceñcuṭar veṇcuṭar
piramaṉ uṭpaṭṭa nilavuyir aṉittum       (30)
tamakkeṉak kāṭṭum oḷikkaṇ keṭalum
maṟṟavar mayakkam kaṇṭavar kaṇpeṟat
tirunutal kiḻitta taṉiviḻi nāyakaṉ
tāṅkiya kūṭal perunakar
īṅkitu kāṇka mutteḻil nakikkoṭiyē!       (35)
-------------

73. tēṟātu pulampal

puṭpeyark kuṉṟamum eḻuvakip poruppum
mēlkaṭal kaviḻmukap poriuṭal māvum
neṭuṅkaṭal parappum aṭumtoḻil arakkarum
eṉuḷattu iruḷum iṭipukuntu uṭitta
mantirat tiruvēl maṟamkeḻu mayilōṉ       (5)
kuñcarak koṭi yoṭum vaḷḷiyam koḻun toṭum
kūṟāk kaṟpam kuṟittunili ceyta
puṇṇiyam kumiḻtta kuṉṟuṭik kūṭal
niṟintuṟi kaṟimiṭaṟṟu aṟamkeḻu perumāṉ
pēraruḷ viḷitta mātavar pōla       (10)
muṉṉoru nāḷil uṭaluyir nīeṉa
uḷḷam karivittu uriceyta ūrar
tammoḻi tirintu tavaṟuniṉ ṟuḷavēl
avarkuṟi aṉṟāl oruvaṉ paṭitta
kālak kuṟikol aṉṟiyum muṉṉit       (15)
tiyaṅkuṭal īṭṭiya tarumkaṭu viṉiyāl
kālak kuṟiyi maṉamtaṭu māṟip
piṉmuṉ kuṟittanam perumati aḻakāl
naṉaviṭi naviṟṟik kaṉaviṭik kaṇṭa
uḷḷeḻu kalakkat tuṭaṉmayaṅ kiṉamāl       (20)
kuṟittiv iṭinili oṉṟē
maṟikkulattu uḻiyiṉ vaḻinōk kiṉaḷē!       (22)
-------------

74. mālip poḻutoṭu pulampal

āyiram paṇāṭavi aravuvāy aṇittuk
karumukil niṟatta kaṇṇaṉiṉ ciṟantu
niṟiyuṭal aṭaṅkat tiruviḻi niṟittut
tēvarniṉ ṟicikkum tēvaṉiṉ peruki
kuruvaḷar marakatap paṟittaḻi parappi       (5)
maṇitiri ukikkum kaṭaliṉiṉ kaviṉi
muḷeyiṟṟu aravam muṟittuyir parukip
puḷeḻu vāṉattu acaṉiyiṉ polintu
pūtam aintuṭiyum kālak kaṭiyiṉum
uṭaltaḻi nilitta maṟammiku mayilōṉ       (10)
purantaraṉ putalvi eyiṉartam pāvi
irupāl ilaṅka ulakupeṟa niṟinta
aruviam kuṉṟattu aṇiaṇi kūṭaṟku
iṟiyavaṉ piṟiyavaṉ kaṟikeḻu miṭaṟṟōṉ
malarkkaḻal vaḻuttumnam kātalar pācaṟi       (15)
muṉippatu nōkkieṉ muṉiaviḻ aṟṟattu
perumpaka liṭiyē potumparil pirinta
vaḷikaṭ kūrukirk kūkkural mōttiyi
karuṅkaṭ koṭiyiṉam kaṇṇaṟac cūḻntu
pukiuṭal puṭitta viṭaṉviṉi pōla       (20)
maṉamkaṭantu ēṟā matilvaḷittu eṅkum
karuneruppu eṭutta maṟaṉmaruḷ māli
niṉvaraṟku ēvar nalkiṉa niṉvaral
kaṇṭuṭal iṭintōrk kāṭṭutum kāṇmati
maṇṇuṭal pacantu kaṟuttatu viṇṇamum       (25)
āṟṟātu aḻaṉṟu kāṟṟiṉ mukammayaṅki
uṭueṉak koppuḷ uṭalniṟi poṭittaṉa
īṅkivaṟ ṟaṭaṅkiya irutiṇi uyirkaḷum
tammuṭaṉ mayaṅkiṉa oṭuṅkiṉa uṟaṅkiṉa
aṭaṅkiṉa avintaṉa ayarntaṉa kiṭantaṉa       (30)
eṉappeṟiṉ māli eṉṉuyir uḷippatum
avartiṟam niṟpatum orupuṭi kiṭakka
uḷḷatu moḻimō nīyē viṇṇuḻi
vantaṉi eṉṉil varumkuṟi kaṇṭilaṉ
maṇṇiṭi eṉiṉē avvaḻi yāṉa       (35)
kūṭiniṉ ṟaṉieṉiṉ kuṟitava ṟāvāl
tēmpaṭarn taṉieṉiṉ ticikuṟik kunarāl
ātaliṉ niṉvaravu eṉakkē
ōtal vēṇṭum pulaṉpeṟak kuṟittē.       (39)
-------------

75. vāymi kūṟi varuttam taṇittal

tirumalar irunta mutiyavaṉ pōla
nāṉmukam koṇṭuaṟi naṉṉar neñciruntu
vēṟṟaruḷ piṟavi tōṟṟuvit teṭuttu
nilamiraṇ ṭaḷanta neṭumukil māṉa
arakkartam kūṭṭam tolittu neyuṇṭu       (5)
kaḷiṟṟuri puṉinta kaṇṇutal kaṭuppa
aḻaleṭuttu oṉṉalar purameri ūṭṭi
iṉiyev vulakum toḻuteḻu tiruvēl
caravaṇattu utitta aṟumukap putalvaṉ
paraṅkuṉ ṟuṭutta payaṅkeḻu kūṭal       (10)
perunakar niṟinta ciṟupiṟic ceṉṉiyaṉ
mālayaṉ tēṭi maṟiaṟintu aṟiyāt
taṉuru oṉṟil aruḷuru iruttiya
āti nāyakaṉ akal malark kaḻaliṇi
naṇṇalar kiḷipōl taṉmaṉam tirintunam       (15)
tuṟivaṉ taṇakka aṟikilam yāmē
piṇarmuṭat tāḻi virimar kurukeṉa
neṭuṅkaḻik kuṟuṅkayal neytaluḷ maṟintum
puṉṉiam potumpark kuḻimukam kuḻimukam
karuntiri cumanteṟi veṇtara ḷattiṉi       (20)
arumpeṉac curumpiṉam alaraniṉ nicittum
kalamcuman tiṟakkum kariyiṉam poruppeṉa
parukiya mukiṟkulam paṭintukaṇ paṭuttum
pavaḷanaṉ kavikkoṭi vaṭaviyiṉ koḻunteṉa
curivaḷi kuḷikkunar kalaṉiṭic ceṟintum       (25)
veḷḷiṟa uṇṇa viḻintupuku kurukiṉam
karuṅkaḻi neytalik kāvalcey kaṇeṉa
aravueyiṟṟu aṇimuḷ kitiyuḷ aṭaṅkiyum
viṇtoṭa eḻuntu viḻutirik kuḻuviṉik
kaṭalvayiṟṟu aṭaṅkiya maliyiṉam varavueṉa       (30)
kuḻimaṇaṟ kēṇiyuḷ kompiṉar paṭintum
muyaṅkiya uḷḷam pōki
mayaṅkiya tuṟiyiṉam oruṅkuḻi vaḷarntē.       (33)
-------------

76. ayal aṟivu urittu aḻukkam eytal

āṭakac cayilattu oruṭal paṟṟi
kalitirip paraviyum kaṉaṉṟeḻu vaṭaviyum
aṭiyiṉum muṭiyiṉum aṇintaṉa pōla
pacuntaḻit tōkiyum ceñciṟic cēvalum
tāṅkiyum, malarkkaram taṅkiyum nilitta       (5)
pēroḷi mēṉiyaṉ pāruyirkku ōruyir
māvuṭik kūṟṟam malarayaṉ taṇṭam
kuṟumuṉi peṟummaṟi neṭumaṟi peṟāmutal
kuñcarat tōkiyum kuṟamakaṭ pētiyum
iruntaṉa irupuṟattu enti eṉamutam       (10)
piṟantaruḷ kuṉṟam oruṅkuṟap peṟṟa
mātavak kūṭal maticcaṭik kāraṇaṉ
irupatam tēṟā iruḷuḷam āmeṉa
ivaḷuḷam koṭpa ayaluḷam kaḷippa
arumporuṭ celvi eṉumtiru makaṭku       (15)
māṉiṭa makaḷir tāmumniṉ ṟetirntu
pulitaḻt tāmari ilaḷit teṉavum
ulakuviṇ paṉikkum orucaya makaṭku
tēvartam makaḷirum cerumukam nērntu
vīramaṅku īntupiṉ viḷivatu māṉavum       (20)
iruḷuṭal arakkiyar kalimakaṭ kaṇṭu
teṉtamiḻ vaṭakali cilakoṭut teṉavum
nīrara makaḷir pāntaḷ am kaṉṉiyarkku
āreri maṇittiraṉ aruḷiyatu eṉavum
cemmalark kuḻalivaḷ pōyaṟi vuṟuttak       (25)
kaṟṟatum kallātu uṟṟaṉa ūraṉi
avaḷtara ivaḷpeṟum arantiyam pēṟiṉukku
oṉṟiya uvamam iṉṟivaṇ uḷavāl
maṟṟavaḷ taraneṭuṅ kaṟpē
uṟṟivaḷ peṟṟāḷ eṉpatum takumē.       (30)
-------------

77. pirintami kūṟal

malaravaṉ paṉikkum kaviṉum kulamīṉ
arukiya kaṟpum karutiuḷ naṭuṅkit
tirumakaḷ malarpukum orutaṉi maṭantiyiṉṟu
irukaṭal ōruḻi maruviya teṉṉac
ceruppaṭi vēntar muṉimēl paṭarntanam       (5)
kātalar muṉippaṭi kaṉaṉṟuṭaṟ ṟeriyāl
muṭampaṭu nāñcil poṉmukam kiḻitta
neṭuñcāl pōkik kaṭuṅkayal turakkum
maṅkiyar kuḻipeṟu vaḷḷiyil taṭikoṇṭu
avarkaruṅ kaṇeṉak kuvaḷi pūtta       (10)
iruḷ akac cōliyuḷ iraveṉat taṅkiya
maṟṟataṉ cēkkiyuḷ vatipeṟum ceṅkāl
veḷḷuṭal ōtimam taṉṉuṭip peṭieṉap
paṟivarat taḻīippeṟ ṟuviiṉak kampalikku
āṟṟātu akaṉṟu tēkkuvaḻi kaṇṭa       (15)
kālvaḻi iṟantu pācaṭi pūtta
koḷḷam pukuntu vaḷḷuṟi vāṉattu
eḻilmati kāṭṭi niṟivaḷi cūluḷintu
iṭaṅkarum āmiyum eḻuveyil koḷuvum
malimutuku aṉṉa kulimukaṭu ēṟi       (20)
muḻumati uṭukkaṇam kātaliṉ viḻuṅki
umiḻvaṉa pōla curimukac cūlvaḷi
taraḷam coriyum paḻaṉak kūṭal
kuvaḷi niṉ ṟalarnta maṟieḻu kuralōṉ
imiyavar vēṇṭa orunaki mukiḻppa       (25)
ōr uḻik kūṭātu umparil pukuntu
vāṉuṭit tuṇṇum maṟakkoli arakkarmup
perumatil peṟṟaṉa aṉṟō
maruvalar aṭintamuṉ maṟamkeḻu matilē?       (29)
-------------

78. kalakkaṅ kaṇṭu urittal

peruntuyar akaṟṟi aṟamkuṭi nāṭṭi
uḷaccuruḷ virikkum nalattaku kalviyoṉṟu
uḷateṉa kuricil orumoḻi cāṟṟa
pēḻvāyk koyyuḷi aricuman teṭutta
palmaṇi ācaṉattu iruntucev vāṉiṉ       (5)
neṭuñcaṭik kuṟuñcuṭar nīkkiain taṭukkiya
āṟuaiñ nūṟoṭu vēṟuniri aṭutta
palmaṇi miḷirmuṭi palartoḻak kavittu
palatalip pāntaḷ cumitirut tōḷil
tarittulaku aḷikkum tiruttaku nāḷil       (10)
neṭunāḷ tiruvayiṟṟu aruḷuṭaṉ irunta
neṭuñcaṭi ukkiraṟ payantaruḷ nimalaṉ
maṟṟavaṉ taṉṉāl vaṭaviyiṉ koḻuntucuṭṭu
āṟṟātu uṭalamum imikkuṟum muttamum
viḷarttuniṉ ṟaṇaṅki vaḷikkulam muḻaṅkum       (15)
karuṅkaṭal poriya oruṅkuvēl viṭuttu
avaṟkaruḷ koṭutta mutaṟperu nāyakaṉ
vemmiyum taṇmiyum viṉiuṭaṟku āṟṟum
irucuṭar orucuṭar puṇarviḻi ākkimuṉ
vitiyavaṉ tārā uṭaloṭu nilitta       (20)
muttamiḻk kūṭal mutalvaṉ poṟṟāḷ
kaṉavilum kāṇāk kaṇṇiṉar tuyarum
pakuttuṇṭu īkunar nilittiru muṉṉar
illeṉum tīccol iṟuttaṉar tōmum
aṉittuyir ōmpum aṟattiṉar pāṅkar       (25)
kōṟaleṉ ṟayaliṉar kuṟittaṉa kuṟṟamum
naṉṟaṟi kalviyar nāṭṭuṟu moḻipukku
av araṇ iḻantōrkku aruviṭam āyatum
orukaṇam kūṭi oruṅkē
irucevi pukkatu ottaṉa ivaṭkē.       (30)
-------------

79. muṉpaṉikku nontu urittal

kaṭalmakaḷ uḷvittu vaṭavimey kāyavum
malimakaḷ taḻaltaru mēṉioṉṟu aṇikkavum
mācaṟu tirumakaḷ malarpukuntu āyiram
puṟitaḻp putavaṭittu ataṉvetuppu uṟikkavum
cayamakaḷ cīṟṟat taḻalmaṉam vittut       (5)
tiṇipukum veṉṟic ceruaḻal kūṭavum
aiyar payiṟṟiya vitiaḻal ōmpavum
av aṉaṟku amarar aṉittumvantu aṇiyavum
muṉiṭik kāṭaṉ piṉeḻa naṭantu
nōṉpuṟu viratiyar nukaruḷ irunteṉ       (10)
neñcakam niṉṟu niṉiviṉuḷ maṟintu
puri aṟum aṉpiṉar viḻipeṟat tōṟṟi
vāṉavar neṭumuṭi maṇittoki tiraṭṭip
patukkicey ampalat tirupperum patiyiṉum
piṟavāp pērūrp paḻanaka riṭattum       (15)
makiḻnaṭam pēypeṟum vaṭavaṉak kāṭṭiṉum
arumaṟi muṭiyiṉum aṭiyavar uḷattiṉum
kuṉittaruḷ nāyakaṉ kulamaṟi payantōṉ
iruntamiḻk kūṭal peruntavar kāṇa
veḷḷiyam palattuḷ tuḷḷiya ñāṉṟu       (20)
neruppoṭu cuḻalavum viruppeṭut tavvaḻal
kiyiṉil koḷḷavum karivuri mūṭavum
ākkiya paṉippakik kūṟṟivi niṟka
āṅkavar tuyarpeṟa īṉṟeṉ orutti
pukalviḻum aṉputaṟku iṉṟi
makaviṉip peṟalām varamvēṇ ṭiṉaḷē.       (26)
-------------

80. maṟavāmi kūṟal

maruvaḷar kuvaḷi malarntumut tarumpi
pacuntāḷ tōṉṟi malarnaṉi maṟittu
neṭṭeṟi ūti neruppoṭu kiṭantu
maṇipuṟam kāṉṟa purivaḷi vimmi
vitippavaṉ vitiyā ōvam niṉṟeṉeṉ       (5)
uḷḷamum kaṇṇum niliyuṟat taḻīiṉaḷ
uvaṇak koṭiyiṉaṉ untimalart tōṉṟip
pārmutal paṭittavaṉ naṭuttali aṟuttu
puṉitak kalaṉeṉa ulakutoḻak koṇṭu
vaṭṭam mukkōṇam caturam kārmukam       (10)
navattali tāmari vaḷivāyp parunteṉak
kaṇṭaṉa makamtoṟum kalipeṟac ceṉṟu
naṟavu irantaruḷiya periyavar perumāṉ
kūkkural koḷḷāk kolitaru navviyum
vitiroḷi kāṟṟak kaṉalkuḷir maḻuvum       (15)
irukaram taritta oruviḻi nutalōṉ
kūṭaloppu uṭiyāy kuluṭut taṭavum
taṭamatil vayiṟṟuḷ paṭumavar uyirkkaṇam
taṉittaṉi oḷittut taṇakkiṉum ariteṉap
pōkkaṟa vaḷintu puṇariruḷ nāḷum       (20)
kāval kāṭṭiya vaḻiyum
tēvark kāṭṭumnam pācaṟi yiṉumē.       (22)
-------------

81. ūṭi urittal

matiyam uṭalkuṟitta veḷḷāṅ kurukiṉam
piṅkāl taṭavic ceṅkayal turantuṇṭu
kaḻukkaṭi aṉṉatam kūrvāyp paḻippulavu
eḻilmati virittaveṇ taḷiitaḻt tāmari
malarmalar tuvaṭṭum vayalaṇi ūra       (5)
kōḷakik kuṭilil kuṉintiṭin tappuṟattu
iṭinili aṟṟapaṭar peruveḷi yakattu
uṭalmuṭakku eṭutta toḻiṟperu vāḻkkik
kavittalip piṟieyiṟ ṟiruḷeḻil arakkaṉ
amutam uṇṭimiyā avarum maṅkiyarum       (10)
kuṟavarum kuṟavat tuṇiyarum āki
nilampeṟ ṟimittu neṭuvari iṟumpiṭi
paṟaviuṇ ṭīṭṭiya iṟālnaṟavu arunti
annilat tavareṉa aṭikkaṭi vaṇaṅkum
veḷḷiam kuṉṟakam uḷḷuṟap pukuntoru       (15)
tēvaṉum ataṉmuṭi mēvavum uḷaṉām
eṉappuyam koṭṭi nakitteṭut tārkka
pilamtiṟan taṉṉa peruvāy orupatum
malinirit toḻuṅkiya karamiru pattum
viṇṇuṭit taraṟṟavum ticiyuṭki muriyavum       (20)
tāmari akavayiṉ cēyitaḻ vāṭṭiya
tiruvaṭip peruviral talinaka nutiyāl
ciṟitumali uṟitta matimuṭi antaṇaṉ
poṉaṇi māṭam polinakark kūṭal
āvaṇa vīti aṉiyavar aṟivuṟil       (25)
ūruṇi aṉṉaniṉ mārpakam tōynteṉ
iṇimuli naṉṉar iḻantaṉa atupōl
maṟṟavar kavimaṉam māḻki
ceṟṟamniṟ pukivarik kāltīṇṭaliyē.       (29)
-------------

82. tōḻi poṟi uvantu urittal

ularkavaṭṭu ōmip poriciṉik kūkiyum
vīcukōṭṭu āntiyum cēvaloṭu alamara
tiriviḻip paruntiṉam vaḷiukirp paṭiyāl
pārppiri kavarap payaṉuṟum ulakil
kaṭaṉuṟum yākkik kavarkaṭaṉ kaḻittut       (5)
taḻaluṇak koṭutta ataṉuṇa viṭiyē
kiviḷakku eṭuttuk kariiṉam kariya
piṇamvirit tuṇṇum kuṇaṅkiṉam koṭpa
cūṟpēy ēṟpa iṭākiṉa karappa
kaṇṭuḷam taḷirkkum karuṇiam celvi       (10)
piṟinutal nāṭṭi kaṭuvaḷar kaṇṭi
iṟālnaṟavu aruvi eḻuparaṅ kuṉṟattu
uṟicūrp pakiyiṉaṟ peṟutiru vayiṟṟiṉaḷ
orupāl polinta uyarnakark kūṭal
kaṭukkiam caṭiyiṉaṉ kaḻaluḷattu ilarpōl       (15)
poyvarum ūraṉ pukalarum ilpuka
eṉuḷam cikiviṭṭu eḻumaṉal pukka
matuppoḻi muḷariyiṉ māḻkiṉa eṉṟāl
tōḷil tuvaṇṭum toṅkaluḷ maṟintum
kivaral ēṟṟum kaṉaviṉuḷ taṭintum       (20)
tirikkaṭal teyvamuṉ teḷicūḷ vāṅkiyum
poruṭkāṉ taṭintum pācaṟip poruntiyum
pōkkaruṅ kaṭuñcuram pōkamuṉ iṟantum
kāvalil kavaṉṟum kalviyil karutiyum
vēntuviṭikku aṇaṅkiyum viḷiporuṭku urukiyum       (25)
niṉṟa ivaṭku iṉieṉām
kaṉṟiya uṭaluḷ paṭumnaṉi uyirē?       (27)
-------------

83. kalavi karutip pulattal

nilinīr mokkuḷiṉ viṉiyāyt tōṉṟi
vāṉtavaḻ uṭaṟkaṟi matiyeṉac curuṅki
pullarvāyc cūḷeṉap poruḷuṭaṉ aḻiyum
cīṟuṇavu iṉpat tiruntā vāḻkki
kāṉṟiṭu coṉṟiyiṉ kaṇṭu aruvaruttu       (5)
pulaṉaṟat tuṭitta nalaṉuṟu vēḷviyar
ārā iṉpap pēramutu arunti
tuṟaveṉum tiruvuṭaṉ uṟavucey vāḻkkiyar
vāyiṉum kaṇṇiṉum maṉattiṉum akalāp
pēroḷi nāyakaṉ kāroḷi miṭaṟṟōṉ       (10)
maṇtiru vēṭṭup pañcavaṟ poruta
kiḷḷiyum kiḷiyum kiḷarpaṭi nāṉkum
tiṇmiyum cerukkum tēṟṟamum poṉṟiṭa
erivāy urakar iruḷnāṭṭu uruvak
kolikkoṇ ṭāḻi kuṟiyuṭaṉ paṭittu       (15)
maṟiyap putitta maṟamkeḻu perumāṉ
nīrmāk koṉṟa cēyōṉ kuṉṟamum
kalviyum tiruvum kālamum koṭiyum
māṭamum ōṅkiya maṇinakark kūṭal
āla vāyiṉil aruḷuṭaṉ niṟinta       (20)
pavaḷac caṭiyōṉ patamtali cumanta
naliyal ūraniṉ pullamuḷ maṅkiyar
ōviya illamem uṟiyuḷ āka
kēḷāc ciṟucol kiḷakkum kalatiyar
ivvuḻi āyat tiṉarkaḷum āka       (25)
muval itaḻvirintu maṇamcūḻ pantarcey
muṉṟilum emmuṭi muṉṟil āka
malarccumic cēkki matumalar maṟuttit
tirumaṇam koḷḷāc cēkkiyatu āka
niṉvuḷam kaṇṭu nikaḻuṇavu uṉṉi       (30)
nāṇā navappoy pēṇiyuḷ puṇartti
yāḻoṭu mukamaṉ pāṇaṉum nīyum
tiruppeṟum ayalavar kāṇa
varappeṟu mātavam perituṭi yēmē.       (34)
-------------

84. varumpuṉam kaṇṭu varuntal

uḷḷirun teḻuntu puṟampuniṉ ṟeriyum
aḷavāt tirumaṇi aḷitta lāṉum
kolimutir kaṭamāṉ mutirmukam paṭarntu
koḻuñciṉi miṭintu kuḷiroṭu potuḷiya
neṭumarattu iḷaṅkā nilitta lāṉum       (5)
pācaṭi umpar neṭuñcuṉi virinta
pēritaḻt tāmari peruka lāṉum
neṭuvicumpu aṇavum perumati tāṅki
uṭiyā amutam uṟita lāṉum
iḷamiyum toṅkalum iṉpamum orukāl       (10)
vāṭāt tēvarkaḷ maṇatta lāṉum
nūṟuṭi makattil pēṟukemaṇ ṭirunta
purantaraṉ pōlum poṉeyil eṟinta
maṇivēṟ kumaraṉ tiruvaḷar kuṉṟam
pēraṇi uṭutta perunakark kūṭal       (15)
kōyilkoṇ ṭirunta kuṇapperuṅ kuṉṟam
aruntavak kaṇṇiṉōṭu iruntamā muṉipāl
pēriruḷ māyip peṇmakavu irakka
uvarmutal kiṭanta cuviyēḻ amittuk
koṭuttameyp piṇṭam kuṟiyuṭaṉ tōṉṟiya       (20)
eḻunīrc cakararkaḷ ēḻaṇi niṉṟu
maṇpuka mūḻkiya vāṉpari piṇikka
palmuka viḷakkiṉ paritiyil tōṭṭiya
vēlik kuṇṭakaḻ vayiṟalit teḻunta
peruṅkārk karuṅkaṭu arumpiya miṭaṟṟōṉ       (25)
eṟintuvīḻ aruviyum erimaṇi īṭṭamum
uḷḷutōṟu uḷḷutōṟu uḷnā amutuṟikkum
tirumut tamiḻum perukuteṉ maliyattu
ārap potumpari aṭikuḷir cāral
curumpuṭaṉ virinta tuṇimalark koṭiyē       (30)
viṇvirittu oṭukkum iraviveṇ kavikikku
iṭṭuṟi kāmpeṉa viṭṭeḻu kāmpē
marakatam ciṉitta ciṟimayiṟ kulamē!
nīlap pōtum pētiyum viḻitta
poṟiuṭal uḻiyē! eṟiparal maṇiyē!       (35)
pāciḻip paṭṭu nūlkaḻi parappiya
kiḷivāyk kiḷitta vaḷivāyk kiḷiyē!
mintarkaṇ ceṉṟu mātaruḷ taḻitta
poḻimatup putumalarp pōrkkuṭic curumpē!
veṟimutir cemmalar muṟimukam koṭukkum       (40)
cantaṉap potumpart taḻiciṉip poḻilē
koḷḷiam cukamum kuruviyum kaṭiya
irukāl kavaṇiṟku eṟimaṇi cumanta
neṭuṅkāl kuṟṟuḻi niḻalvippu itaṇē!
nerunal kaṇṭeṟku utavuḻi iṉpam       (45)
iṟṟiyiṉ karanta iruḷmaṉam eṉṉi?
ivaṇniṟka vitta ēlāk kaṭuṅkaṇ
koṭuttuṇ ṭavarpiṉ karantami kaṭukkum
īṅkivi kiṭakka eṉniḻal irumpuṉattu
iruntoḷir aruntēṉ ilatāl nīrum       (50)
niṉpuṉam alleṉṟu eṉpulam veḷippaṭa
aṟital vēṇṭum appuṉam nīrēl
muṉṉam kaṇṭavaṉ aṉṟeṉṟu
uṉṉā utavutal uyarntōr kaṭaṉē.       (54)
-------------

85. neñcoṭu varuntal

vaṭamoḻi matitta icinūl laḻakkuṭaṉ
aṭutta eṇnāṉku aṅkuli yakattiṉum
nāṟpatiṟ ṟiraṭṭi nālaṅ kuliyiṉum
kuṟumiyum neṭumiyum kōṭalpeṟ ṟitāy
āyiram tantiri niṟipotu vicittu       (5)
kōṭi mūṉṟil kuṟittu maṇikuyiṟṟi
irunilam kiṭatti maṉamkaram katuva
āyirat teṭṭil amittaṉa piṟappu
piṟavip pētat tuṟiyatu pōla
āriyap patamkoḷ nāratap pēriyāḻ       (10)
naṉṉarkoḷ aṉpāl naṉimikap pulampa
munnāṉku aṅkuli muḻuuṭal cuṟṟum
aimpatiṟ ṟiraṭṭi āṟuṭaṉ kaḻitta
aṅkuli neṭumiyum amittu uḷtūrntē
oṉpatu tantiri uṟunti nilinīkki       (15)
aṟuvāykku āyiraṇṭu aṇittu varikaṭṭi
tōḷkāl vatintu toḻippaṭat tōṉṟum
tumpuruk karuviyum tuḷḷiniṉ ṟicippa
eḻueṉa uṭampupeṟ ṟeṇpatu aṅkuliyiṉ
tantiri nūṟu taḻaṅkumati mukatta       (20)
kīcakap pēriyāḻ kiḷiyuṭaṉ murala
niṟimati vaṭṭattu muyaluri vicittu
nāppaṇ oṟṟi narampukaṭip pamittu
annarampu irupat tāṟuaṅ kulipeṟa
iṭakkaram tuvakki iṭakkīḻ amittu       (25)
puṟaviral mūṉṟiṉ nuṉiviral akattum
aṟupat tiraṇṭici aṉittuyir vaṇakkum
maruttuvap peyarpeṟum vāṉakkaruvi
tūṅkalum tuḷḷalum tuvakkaniṉ ṟicippa
nāṉmukaṉ mutalā mūvarum pōṟṟa       (30)
muṉivarañ caliyuṭaṉ mukamaṉ iyampa
tēvarkaḷ aṉivarum ticitici iṟiñca
iṉpap pacuṅkoṭi iṭappāl paṭara
veḷḷi am kuṉṟam viḷaṅka viṟṟirunta
muṉṉavaṉ kūṭal muṟivaṇaṅ kāreṉa       (35)
aravap pacuntali arumpaviḻ kaṇikkāl
neytaṟ pācaṭi neṭuṅkāṭṭu oḷikkum
kaṇeṉak kuṟitta karuṅkayal kaṇatti
veḷḷuṭal kūrvāy centāṭ kurukiṉam
aravueyi ṟaṇittamuḷ ilimuṭak kitikaḷ       (40)
kāṉṟalar kaṭimalar karantuṟin tuṇṇum
karuṅkaḻi kiṭanta kāṉalam karivāy
meypaṭu kaṭuñcūḷ miṉeṉat tuṟantavar
cuvaluḷik kavaṉap puḷiyal kalimāṉ
nōkkam miṟitta paritikoḷ neṭuntērp       (45)
piṉṉoṭum ceṉṟeṉ perumpiḻi neñcam
ceṉṟuḻic ceṉṟuḻic cēṟalum uḷavō
avviṉip payaṉuḻi aruntavam peṟumō?
iṭivaḻi nīṅkieṉ etiruṟuṅ kollō?
aṉṟiyum neṭunāḷ amintuṭaṉ varumō?       (50)
yāteṉa nilikkuvaṉ mātō
pēti koḷḷātu oḻimaṉam kaṭuttē.       (52)
-------------

86. kaṇ tuyilātu moḻital

kaṭuviṉi aṅkuram kāṭṭiuḷ aḻukkāṟu
eṇticic cākikoṇ ṭiruḷmaṉam potuḷi
koṭuṅkoli vaṭuttu kaṭumpaḻic caṭialintu
iraṇṭuaiñ ñūṟu tiraṇṭaak kāvatam
cuṟṟuṭal peṟṟu, tuṇippati ṉāyiram       (5)
maṟṟatiṉ nīṇṭu maṇivuṭal pōki
aimpatu nūṟuṭaṉ akaṉṟucuṟ ṟoḻukki
peruṅkaviḻ iṇartantu avikīḻk kulaviya
aṭalmāk koṉṟa neṭuvēṟ kuḷavaṉ
kuṉṟavar vaḷḷiam koṭiyoṭu tuvakkip       (10)
paṉṉiru kaṇvirittu eṉviṉi turakkum
aruṭparaṅ kuṉṟam uṭuttaṇi kūṭal
kuṟumpiṟi muṭitta neṭuñcaṭi oruttaṉit
teyvam koḷḷār cintiatu eṉṉak
kiṭantaval iravil kiḷarmaḻi kāṉṟa       (15)
avalum umparum aṭakkupuṉal oruvi
tēaruḷ kallār cintiyiṉ puraṇṭa
kavaliyum kāṟkuṟi kaṇṭupoḻil tuḷḷum
imiyāc cūrum palakaṇṭu oruṅkāt
tuṭiyiṉ kaṇṇum tuñcāk kaṇṇiṉar       (20)
kaṭiyum tuṉivil kiyakaṉṟu erimaṇit
tokiyiruḷ kollum muṉṟil pakkattu
iṇimukap paṟiaṟi kaṭippuṭit tōki
vayiṟṟuḷ aṭakki vaḷikiṭi kiṭakkum
muḻakkimey kavarum mukakkoli ñāḷi       (25)
atirkurippu aṭakki iṟpuṟattu aṇintanam
pūmpuṉal ūraṉi poruntā neṭuṅkaṇ
aṉṉiyiṉ pōkkiya arumperun tavaṟu
māliyum kaṇṇum mēṉiyum uḷḷamum
mayaṅkāt tēvar maruntuvāy maṭukka       (30)
mukamkaviḻ vēliyil aṟamkuṭi pōkiya
māyaval arakkar taṭṭik
kāypār ukutta vitiot taṉavē.       (33)
-------------

87. talivaṉ varavu urittal

nāṟkaṭal vaḷitta nāṉilattu uyiriṉi
aintaruk kaṭavuḷ avaṉpulat tiṉari
naṭantupukku uṇṭum paṟantupukku ayiṉṟum
muttoḻil tēvarum muruṅkuḷ uṟuttum
nōṉtalik koṭuñcūrk kaḷavuyir nukarnta       (5)
taḻalvēṟ kumaraṉ cālparaṅ kuṉṟam
maṇiyoṭum poṉṉoṭum mārpaṇi aṇitta
peruntiruk kūṭal aruntavar perumāṉ
irucaraṇ akalā orumiyar uḷameṉa
cuṭarviḷakku eṭumiṉ kōtikaḷ tūkkumiṉ       (10)
pūvum poriyum tūvumiṉ toḻumiṉ
cuṇṇamum tātum tuṉittukaḷ tūṟṟumiṉ
karumpeyal kuḷiṟiṉam kaḷimayil eṉṉak
kiṭantayar vāṭkumuṉ kiḷirviṉic ceṉṟōr
uṭaluyir taḻikkum aruḷvaravu uṇartta       (15)
mulliam paṭarkoṭi nīṅki, piṭavac
corialar taḷḷi tuṇarppolam kaṭukkik
kiṭitaravu oruvi kaḷavualar kiṭatti
pūliam puṭimalar pōkki arakkaṭuttuk
kaḻuviya tirumaṇi kālpeṟ ṟeṉṉa       (20)
naṟperun tūtu kāṭṭum
aṟputak kōpat tiruvaravu ataṟkē.       (22)
-------------

88. iraṅkal

paḻutaṟu teyvam kāṭṭip paṇṭiyiṉ
uḻuvaliṉ nalattāl ōruyir eṉṟum
kaṭuñcūḷ tantum kipuṉi puṉintum
pūḻiyam pōṉakam potuvuṭaṉ uṇṭum
kuḻamakaṟ kuṟittum cilamoḻi koṭuttum       (5)
kiyuṟi cumantum kaṭittaḻi tāṅkiyum
uyiriṉil taḷḷā iraṅkiyum uṇaṅkiyum
paṉiyum kiḻiyum paṭikkuvaṉ eṉṟum
iṟaṭiyam cēvaṟku eṟikavaṇ kūṭṭiyum
puṉamum emuyirum paṭarkari taṭintum       (10)
aḻuṅkuṟu puṉaleṭuttu akiṟpuki ūṭṭiyum
oḷimaṇi ūcal pariyaviṭṭu yāttum
iraviṉil taṅka eḷivaral irantum
iruviam puṉattiṭi eṟiuyirppu eṟintum
terittalar koytum poḻilkuṟi viṉaviyum       (15)
uṭaloṭum piṇintaki āytuyil oṟṟi
ceṟiiruḷ kuḻampakam ceṉṟupaḷiṅ keṭutta
iṟpoḻil kiṭikkum aḷavumniṉ ṟalintum
palnāḷ palneṟi aḻuṅkiṉar iṉṟu
mukaṉintu maṇatta muḻavam tuvikka       (20)
orukāl tūkki niliyam oḷirvittu
mūvuṭal aṇitta mummukattu ōrmukattu
eṇkaṭippu vicitta kallalaku eṟiya
iruṭkuṟaḷ ūṉṟiem aruṅkaḷi āṟṟi
uruḷvāyk kokkari umparnāṭ ṭolikka       (25)
karamkāl kāṭṭi taliyam iyakki
itaḻaviḻ tāmari eṉumtaku ṇiccam
tuvippa nīḷkarattuk kavikaḷ tōṟṟi
karikkāl aṉṉa montikalit tiraṅka
tuṭieṟintu icippa tukaḷam parappi       (30)
vaḷḷam piṇittaceṅ karaṭiki malakka
eriyakal ēntivem puyaṅkamici ākki
eritaḷirt taṉṉa vēṇiyil kuḻavip
pacumpiṟi amutoṭu nirampiya teṉṉa
matukkuḷir mattamum milittoru maṟupiṟi       (35)
mārpamum iruttiya teṉṉakkūṉ puṟattu
ēṉak kōṭuveṇ poṭippuṟat toḷira
polaṉmiḷir maṉṟap potuvakam nāṭit
taṉikkoṭi kāṇev iṭattuyir taḻippa
āṭiya perumāṉ amarntuniṟi kūṭal       (40)
kaṉavilum viṉavā tavariṉum nīṅki
cūḷum vāymiyum tōṟṟi
nīḷavum poyttaṟku avarmaṉam kariyē!       (43)
-------------

89. aiyam uṟṟu ōtal

pācaṭik karuṅkaḻi paṭarmaṇal ulakamum
eḻumali poṭittavarkku icittal vēṇṭi
variulaku aṉittum varuvatu pōla
tiriniri tirittuk karikarik kollum
viyinīr viḻavu pukuntaṉam eṉoru       (5)
poyyiṉaḷ aṉṟi meyyiṉi nīyum
polampūṇ peyarntuṟi pūṇi aruḷtarum
malarcci nīṅkik koṭuṅkōl vēnteṉac
cēkkōḷ kaṇṇi cemmoḻip peyartantu
oṉṟuṭaṉ nillā moḻiyi matutta       (10)
mutirā nāḷcey muṇṭakam malarntu
kaviḻnta mukattiem kaṇmaṉam tōṉṟa
arumpiya nakiyi aṉṟē niṉkeḻu
eṉkaṇ kaṇṭa iviṭi eṉṉuḷam
maṉṉiniṉ ṟaṭaṅkāk kuṭumiam peruntaḻal       (15)
pacuṅkaṭal vaḷintu parukak kotitta
tōṟṟamum kaṭantatu eṉṟāl āṟṟalcey
viṇṇakam puṭittu neṭuvari karakkum
koṭuñcūrk koṉṟa kūrili neṭuvēl
kuṉṟak kuṟavar kompiṉukku iṉiyōṉ       (20)
kurukoli ōvāp paṉimalar vāvi
vayiṟu vāytta kuḻaliyam kiḻavōṉ
vāḻparaṅ kuṉṟeṉum maṇiaṇi pūṇṭa
nāṉmaṟi pukaḻum kūṭal emperumāṉ
vāṉmutal īṉṟa malimakaḷ taṉṉoṭum       (25)
muḻutuṇar ñāṉam ellām uṭimi
muḻutaṉuk kirakam keḻuparam anāti
pācam ilāmi mācaṟu niṭkaḷam
avikā rakkuṟi ākiya taṉkuṇam
eṭṭum tarittu viṭṭaṟu kuṟṟamum       (30)
aruccaṉi vaṇakkam paruyirkku aṉpakam
pēraruḷ tirunūl peruntuṟavu eṅkum
niṟiporuḷ aḻuntal aruḷiṉark kūṭṭam
iruḷpavam naṭuṅkal eṉumkuṇam eṭṭum
tamakkum paṭitta vitippēṟ ṟaṭiyavar       (35)
niliyaruḷ kaṟpeṉa neṭuṅkaṟpu uṭiyōḷ
muṉṉuṟiṉ avaḷ maṉam aṅkē
naṉṉaril koṇṭu kuḷirum peṟumē?       (38)
-------------

90. talivaṉōṭu ūṭal

māyamum iṉpum maruṭciyum teruṭciyum
nakittoki kūṭṭik kavitteḻu collum
amutamum kaṭuvum viḻiyil vittu aḷikkum
irumaṉap poyuḷattu orumakaḷ taṉṉi
kariyōṉ kaṭuppat tukilkavarn toḷiralar       (5)
vitiyiṉum paṉmicey mukaṉpaṭittu aḷavāc
cōtiyiṉ paṭikkaṇ celuyttu arumpucey
muṇṭaka muliyil cāntaḻittu amittōḷ
eḻutiya kaḻikkarumpu eṟintunūl vaḷartta
kōti vakiparintu maṇikkalaṉ koṇṭu       (10)
kaḻittōḷ nekiḻat taḻivuṭal kuḻiyat
tiriyiṉit taḷḷi malarttukil kaṇputittu
oḷniṟa vēṅkiyiṉ tātum poṉṉum
cuṇṇam avikalantu timirntuṭal tūṟṟi
vaṇṭoṭu makiḻntu aviḻ tōṭṭalar cūṭṭi       (15)
iṟālpuṇar pututtēṉ īttuṭaṉ puṇarum
avvayiṉ maṟittum aṉṉavaḷ taṉṉuṭaṉ
keḻumiya viḻavuḷ pukumati nīyē
kavināk kaṭcevi aṇantiri tuytta
pācuṭaṟ pakuvāyp pīḻiam tavaḷiyum       (20)
pēḻvāyt taḻalviḻit tarakkaṭittu avinta
nilampaṭar tōkik kulamkoḷ cētāvum
avvuḻi māttiri arieḻu kāli
tirunutaṟ kaṇṇum maṭamakaḷ pakkamum
erimaḻu navviyum peṟumaruḷ tiruvuruvu       (25)
eṭuttuṭaṉ antak kaṭukkoli araviṉi
tīvāyp puliyiṉi tiruntalar nakippa
eṭuttaṇi pūṇa urittuṭi uṭuppa
muṉivarum tēvarum karamalar mukiḻppa
taruvaṉa aṉṟi malaravaṉ avaṉtoḻil       (30)
nāraṇaṉ āṅkavaṉ kūruṭik kāval
cērat tuṭikkum pēraruḷ nāḷiṉum
muttoḻil taṉatu mutaltoḻil ākki
orutāḷ tārikoḷ mukkavic cuṭarvēl
taliiruntu aruṅkati muḻutuniṉ ṟaḷikkum       (35)
tirunakark kācip patiyakattu eṉṟum
veḷiyuṟat tōṉṟiya iruḷmaṇi miṭaṟṟōṉ
nēmiyaṅ kuṉṟakaḻ neṭuvēṟ kāḷiyaṉ
taṉparaṅ kuṉṟam tamarpeṟu kūṭaṟku
iṟiyōṉ tiruvaṭi niṟiyuṭaṉ vaṇaṅkum       (40)
perumpuṉal ūra! emil
arumpuṉal viyiam putunīr aṉṟē.       (42)
-------------

91. uṭaṉpaṭa urittal

vēliam kuṟuñcūl viḷikāyp pañciṉam
peruveḷ ḷiṭiyil ciṟukāl paṭṭeṉa
niṟināṇ vēli nīṅkit tamiyē
ōruḻi nillātu alamaral koḷḷumeṉ
aruntuṇi neñciṟku uṟumpayaṉ kēṇmati       (5)
maṇṇuḷar vaṇaṅkum taṉṉuṭit takimiyum
iruḷaru pulaṉummeyp poruḷaṟi kalviyum
amararpeṟ ṟuṇṇum amuturuk koṇṭu
kuṟuñcol kutaṭṭiya maḻalimeṉ kiḷaviyil
vitaliuḷ viḷikkum taḷarnaṭic ciṟuvaṉum       (10)
niṉnalam pukaḻntuṇum nītiyum tōṟṟamum
tuvaruṟat tīrntanam kavarmaṉattu ūraṉ
pommalam katirmuli puṇarvuṟum koleṉac
ceṉṟuceṉṟu iraṅkali aṉṟiyum tavirmō
neṭṭukirk karuṅkāl tōlmulip perumpēy       (15)
amarpeṟṟu oṉṉalar aṟivuṟap paṭara,
pēḻvāy iṭākiṉi kāltoḻutu ēttik
kiyaṭi koṭutta veḷniṇa vāykkuḻavi
īmap peruviḷakku eṭuppa maṟṟataṉ
cuṭupoṭik kāppuṭal tuḷaṅkac curikural       (20)
āntiyum kūkiyum aṇiōl uṟutta
ōripāṭṭu eṭuppa uvaṇamum koṭiyum
ceñcevic cēval kavarvāyk kaḻukum
iṭṭacey pantar iṭiiṭi kāleṉa
paṭṭular kaḷḷiam pāltuyil koḷḷum       (25)
cuḷḷiam kāṉiṭi curartoḻutu ētta
marakatat tuḻāyum anniṟak kiḷiyum
tōkiyum cūlamum tōḷil muṉkiyil
maruṅkil karattil vāṭātu irutti
pōrvali avuṇar pukapporutu uṭaṟṟiya       (30)
mukkaṇ piṟieyiṟṟu eṇtōṭ celvi
kaṇṭuḷam kaḷippa kaṉikaḻal tāmari
vāṉaka vāvi yūṭuṟa malara
orutāḷ eḻupuvi uruvat tiṇtōḷ
pattut ticiyuḷ eṭṭavi uṭippa       (35)
orunaṭam kulaviya tiruvaṭi uravōṉ
kūṭalam patiyakam pōṟṟi
nīṭaniṉ ṟeṇṇār uḷameṉa nīyē.       (38)
-------------

92. parattiyiṟ pirivu urittal

perunilat tēvarkaḷ maṟinīr ukuppa
maṟṟavar makattuḷ vaḷaravi mānta
viṭiyōṉ aruccaṉikku urimiyaṉ muṉṉavaṉ
aṉṉavaṉ taṉṉuṭaṉ kaṭikiēḻ amara
aṉṟiyum imiyavar kaṇeṉak kāṭṭa       (5)
āyiram paṇāṭavi aravukaṭu vāṅka
tēvaruṇa maruntuṭal nīṭaniṉ ṟutava
uṭalmuṉi ceruviṉar uṭalvaḻi naṭappa
nāraṇaṉ mutalām tēvarpaṭi tōṟṟa
taṇmatik kalikaḷ tāṉaṟa oṭuṅka       (10)
eṟinteḻum arakkar ēṉiyar maṭiya
maṟiyavaṉ kuṇṭam muṟimuṟi vāyppa
avaṉtarum ulakattu aruntoḻil ōṅka
pācuṭal uḷimā ēḻaṇi peṟṟa
orukāl tērniṟintu iruḷuṭittu eḻunta       (15)
ceṅkatir virittacen tirumalart tāmarip
peruntēṉ aruntiep pērici aṉittiṉum
mutalicic cevvaḻi vitipeṟap pāṭiat
tātuṭal tutintameṉ taḻicciṟi vaṇṭiṉam
pacuntāḷ pulitaḻk karuntāḷ āmpal       (20)
ciṟituuvā matuvamum kuṟipeṟa arunti ap
pācaṭikku ulakavar payilāt tāriyi
maruḷoṭu kuṟikkum puṉalaṇi ūra!
tāṉavarkku uṭintu vāṉavar irappa
uḻaltēr pattiṉaṉ makavueṉa nāṟi       (25)
muṉitaḻaṟ celvam muṟṟip paḻaṅkal
peṇvarac caṉakaṉ mitiliyil koṭumaram
iṟuttu avaṉ makaṭpuṇarntu erimaḻu irāmaṉ
vilkavarntu aṉṉiviṉi uḷvittu ēva
tuṇiyum iḷavalum toṭarak kāṉpaṭarntu       (30)
mākukaṉ nativiṭa ūkki vaṉattuk
karāti mārīcaṉ kavantaṉuyir maṭittu
iruciṟik kaḻukiṉarkku ulantakaṭaṉ kaḻittu
eṟivaḷi makaṉinaṭ ṭēḻu marattiṉukku
arikku karuṅkaṭaṟku orōoru kaṇiviṭuttu       (35)
akkaṭal vayiṟuaṭittu arakkaṉuyir vuvi
ilaṅkiav arakkaṟku iḷiyōṉ peṟukeṉat
tamatūr pukuntu muṭicuman tōrkkum
nāṉmukat tavarkkum irupāl pakutta
orunutal kaṇṇavaṉ uṟitaru kūṭal       (40)
teḷivēṟ kaṇkuṟun toṭiyiṉar kāṇiṉ
niṉpāl aḷiyamum nīṅki
iṉpumiṉṟu oḻikkumem kāltoṭal ceṉmē.       (43)
-------------

93. pāṇaṉi vekuḷutal

uḷamnakit tuṭka ūkkumōr viruntiṉi
kuvaḷivaṭi pūttakaṇ tavaḷa vāḷnakik
kuṟuntoṭi maṭantinam tōḻiyum kēṇmō!
kaviralar pūttaceñ cemmivil kuṭumi
mañcaṭi kiḷitta varikkuṟu muḷtāḷ       (5)
kūrari vāḷiṉ tōkiam cēval
koṭiyōṉ kuṉṟam puṭivaḷar kūṭal
kaṇicciam kittalattu aruṭperuṅ kāraṇaṉ
ulakuyir makavuṭip pacuṅkoṭikku orupāl
pakuttuyirkku iṉpam tokuttameyt tuṟaviṉaṉ       (10)
muḷarinīrp pukuttiya patamalart tāḷtuṇi
maṇimuṭi cumantanam vayalaṇi ūrarpiṉ
vaḷarmaṟit takareṉat tiritarum pāṇmakaṉ
eṉakkuṟittu aṟikilam yāmē ematu
maṇioḷir muṉṟil orupuṭi niliniṉṟu       (15)
aṉṉa ūrar pullamum viḻukkuṭi kku
aṭāak kiḷaviyum paṭākkarum pukaḻum
eṅkiyar pulaviyil ikaḻcciyum tampāl
taṉatupuṉ pukaḻmoḻi nīḷat tantum
oṉṟupattu āyiram naṉṟupeṟap puṉintu       (20)
kaṭṭiya poypāp puṉintuniṟku uṟuttiṉ
pēreḻiṟ cakarar ēḻeṉap paṟitta
muritiri vaṭikkum pariti amtōḻam
kāṭṭiyuḷ imparum kāṇat
tōṭṭiniṉ ṟaḷakkum toṉmiyatu peṟumē.       (25)
-------------

94. pāṇaṉ pulantu urittal

ilavualar tūṟṟi aṉiccam kuḻittu
tāmari kuvitta kāmar cēvaṭit
tiruviṉaḷ orunaki aritiṉiṉ kēṇmō
ellām tōṟṟa iruntaṉa tōṟṟamum
taṉṉuḷ tōṉṟit tāṉatil tōṉṟāt       (5)
taṉṉiṭi niṟiyum orutaṉik kōlattu
iruvaṭivu ākiya paḻamaṟi vētiyaṉ
nāṉmaṟit tāpatar muttaḻaṟ kaṉalpukku
arakkar tuyttuṭaṟṟum atuvē māṉa
pācaṭi maṟitteḻu muḷariam kayattuḷ       (10)
kārāṉ iṉaṅkaḷ cēṭeṟintu uḻakkum
kūṭaṟku iṟiyavaṉ kālaṟ kāyntōṉ
tirunaṭam kuṟittanam porupuṉal ūraṉi
eṅkiyar kuḻumi emakkum taṅkiyarp
puṇarttiṉaṉ pāṇtoḻil pullaṉeṉ ṟivaṉi       (15)
kōliṉ karattiṉ tōliṉ puṭippa
kiḷimuḷ ceṟinta vēliam paṭappip
paṭarkāykku aṇintapuṉ kūḻiam kuṟunari
uṭiyōr timirppa varumuyirppu oṭukki
uyirpiri vuṟṟami kāṭṭiavar nīṅka       (20)
ōṭṭam koṇṭaṉa kaṭukkum
nāṭṭavar taṭiyamaṟ ṟutirntu naṭantatuvē.       (22)
-------------

95. tōḻikku urittal

vāyvalam koṇṭa vayiṟṟeḻu taḻaṟku
āṟṟātu alantu kāṟṟeṉak koṭpuṟṟu
uṭitiri aruvi oḷimaṇi kālum
cēyōṉ kuṉṟakat tiruppeṟu kūṭal
koṭuñcuṭar kiḷitta neṭuñcaṭip puyaṅkaṇ       (5)
pavaḷam taḻitta patamalar cumantanam
porupuṉal ūraṉi potueṉa amitta
akkaṭi kuṭimaṉi avarmaṉi pukutti
aṟuvāy niṟinta matippuṟat tōeṉa
curitali kiṭitta iciuḷar taṇṭeṭuttu       (10)
aḷikkārp pāṭum kuralnīr vaṟanta
malippuḷ pōla nilikkural aṇintāṅku
uṇavuḷam karuti oḷiici pāṭa
muḷtāḷ maṟutta muṇṭakam taliyamittu
orupāl aṇintiv virimatip pāṇaṟku       (15)
aṭuttaṉa utavuḻi vēṇṭum
kaṭuttikaḻ kaṇṇi akkalli ikkaṇamē.       (17)
-------------

96. pāṅki aṉṉattōṭu aḻuṅkal

veṟimaṟi maṭikkural tōlkāyt teṉṉa
irukkiṉum iṟakkiṉum utavāt tēvartam
poyvaḻik katiyakam meyeṉap pukāta
viḻiyuṭit toṇṭar kuḻumuṭi tēyppa
taḷirttuc civanta taṇṭiyam tuṇittāḷ       (5)
cēyōṉ paraṅkuṉṟu iḻieṉac ceṟittu
tamiḻkkali māli cūṭitāvāp
pukaḻkkali uṭuttup puṇṇiyak kaṇavar
palneṟi vaḷaṉiṉ pūṭciyiṉ pullum
tolnilik kūṭal tuṭikkarattu oruvaṉi       (10)
aṉpuḷattu aṭakki iṉpamuṇ ṇāreṉa
cēval maṇṭalittuc ciṉiaṭi kiṭakkum
kitiveṇ kurukueḻa moytiri ukaḷum
uḷikaṭaṟ cērppar aḷiviṭat taṇappa
nīlamum karuṅkoṭi aṭampum caṅkamum       (15)
kaṇṇiṟku iṭiyil kaḷattil kaḻitantu
alarntum ularntum uṭintum aṇuṅkaliṉ
vaṭkuṭi miyal akaṟṟiiṉpu orukāl
kūṟavum peṟumē āṟu atuniṟka
ivaḷnaṭi peṟṟum ivaṭpayiṉṟu iraṅkiyum       (20)
ōruḻi vaḷarnta nīriv aṉṉam
aṉṟeṉat taṭiyāk kēṇmi
kuṉṟum accūḷiṉar tammiṉum koṭitē.       (23)
-------------

97. iravu iṭi vilakkal

mutukuṟip peṇṭir varattiyal kuṟippa
vaḻimutal teyvatam varintumaṟ ṟataṟkup
parukkāṭu uruttip palimutal parāvak
kiḷḷiav ayaliṉar nāvuṭaṉṟu ēttap
pakkam cūḻunar kuraṅkam maṇpaṭap       (5)
peṟṟuyirt tayarum poṟṟoṭi maṭantitaṉ
kurumaṇi ōviyat tirunakarp puṟattum
kariyuṭaṉ uṇṇār paḻiuḷam otta
iruḷuṭip perumukil vaḻiterintu ēkaṉmiṉ
arimāṉ urutta nūṟṟuvar matitta       (10)
puṭimaṉac cakuṉi puḷḷiam kavaṟṟil
attoḻiṟku aminta aivarum puṟakiṭṭu
olivāy ōtimam erimalart taviciruntu
ūṭuukaḷ ciralip pacciṟa aruntum
paḻaṉak kurunāṭu aṇipati tōṟṟu       (15)
muṉṉuṟum uḻuvaliṉ paṉṉiru varuṭam
kaṇṭī ravattoṭu kaṟiyaṭi vaḷarum
kuḷirniḻal aṭavi iṟikoṇṭu akaṉṟapiṉ
aṉittuḷa vañcamum aḻittu niri mīṭci
muṭittut tamatu muṭiyāp patipuka       (20)
ūḻmuṟiyē emakku uḷamaṇ karutic
cēṟi eṉṟicippa celpaṇit tūtiṉarkku
orukāl aḷitta tirumā miṭaṟṟōṉ
pāṭal cāṉṟa teyvak
kūṭal kūṭār kuṇamkuṟit teṉavē.       (24)
-------------

98. paruvam kuṟittal

aḷikaḷ paṭṭeṭuppa, puṟavupāṭ ṭoṭuṅka
kāntaḷam kaṭukki kaṉaltaṉam malara
kōṭal īṉṟu koḻumuṉi kūmpa
piṭavamum kaḷavum oruciṟi pūppa
vāṉpuṟam pūtta mīṉpū maṟiya       (5)
kōpam ūrtara maṇiniri kiṭappa
teṉkāl tikippa vaṭakāl vaḷara
poṟiviḻip pāntaḷ puṟṟaḷi vatiya
variuṭal īyal vāytoṟum etirppa
iṭikkural āṉēṟ ṟiṉametir ceṟuppa       (10)
poṟikkuṟi maṭamāṉ cuḻittalik kaviḻa
muṭiyuṭal aṇṭar paṭaliṭam pukuta
kōviyar aḷiyuṭaṉ kulaṉoṭu kuḷirppa
kāyāk kaṇkoḷa mulli eyiṟuṟa
mucukkali piṉavuṭaṉ muḻiyuṟi aṭaṅkak       (15)
kaṇamayil naṭaṉeḻa kāḷi kūttoṭuṅka
cātakam muralkural vāymaṭi tiṟappa
mākkuyil māḻkik kūkkural aṭippa
paṉikkatir uṇṇac cakōram pacippa
uṭinaṟavu uṇṭu varuṭi veṟuppa       (20)
akilcuṭu perumpuṉam uḻupataṉ kāṭṭa
veṟiviḻic cavarar māaṭi oṟṟa
maṇantuṭaṉ pōkkiṉarkku uyaṅkuvaḻi maṟuppa
pulikkural eyiṟṟiyar pūviṉil parappa
kuḻaviam katirpeṟat tirumalar aṇaṅka       (25)
iṉattoṭu kayiravam etiretir malara
kumariyar kāmamum kūvalum vetuppuṟa
nilamakaḷ uṭalamum ticikaḷum kuḷira
olikaṭal ippi taraḷam cūlkoḷa
ivimutal maṇakka eḻuntakār kaṇṭi       (30)
vaṟunīrmalar eṉa māḻkali viṭumati
maṟiaṭi varuttiya maṟivaṉattu orunāḷ
maṇiccuṭar naṟuney kavarmatik karuppikku
iruvaki ēḻeṉum tiruulaku aṉittum
koṭuttavaṉ kūṭal vaḻuttiṉar pōla       (35)
irupuṟam pōṟṟa orutēr varattiṉarkku
oṉṉalar muṟṟi oruṅkupu paṭara
pācaṟi ceṉṟa nāḷnilam kuḻiya
eṇṇi viraltēynta ceṅkaram kūppuka
koytaḷir aṉṉa mēṉi
moyiḻi pūtta kaviṉmalark koṭiyē!       (41)
-------------

99. neṟi vilakkik kūṟal

vaṉappuṭi aṉiccam pukimūḻ kiyateṉa
ivvaṇaṅku avvatarp pēyttērkku iṭintaṉaḷ
teṉticik kōmakaṉ pakaṭupolin taṉṉa
kaṟiyaṭic ceṉṉiyiṉ nakanuti pōkki
kuruttayil pēḻvāyp palpaṭic cīyam       (5)
atartoṟum kuḻuvum avaṟṟiṉum maṟṟavaṉ
kaṭuṅkāl koṟṟattu aṭumtū tuvareṉat
taṉipārttu uḻalum kirātarum palarē
orukāl iratattu eḻupari pūṭṭi
iruvāṉ pōkiya ericuṭark kaṭavuḷ       (10)
mātavar āmeṉa mēlmali maṟintaṉaṉ
miṉpoli vēlōy! aṉpiṉarkku aruḷum
kūṭaṟ pativarum āṭaṟ pariyōṉ
eṭṭeṭṭu iyaṟṟiya kaṭṭamar caṭiyōṉ
irucaraṇ aṭinta maṟuvilar pōla       (15)
aruḷuṭaṉ tamiyi āṭiṉi aiya!
taṇṇīr vāyttarum cenniṟac citali
atavuutir arici aṉṉa centiṉi
nuṇpatam taṇtēṉ viḷaṅkaṉi muyaltaci
veṟikkaṇ kaviaṭik kaṭuṅkāl mēti       (20)
aṉpumakap piḻittuk kallaṟip poḻinta
vaṟaḷpāl iṉṉem muḻiuḷa ayiṉṟu
kāruṭal aṉuṅkiya piṅkaṉ kaṟiyaṭi
ceṉṉi tūkki niṉṟaṉa kāṭṭum
neṭumaṟi ataḷvēy ciliṭak kurampiyil       (25)
maṟṟataṉ tōlil uṟṟiru vīrum
kaṇpaṭuttu iravi kīṟumuṉ
eṇpaṭa numpati ēkutal kaṭaṉē !       (28)
-------------

100. āṟu pārttu uṟṟa accak kiḷavi

veṟikkuṟuṅ katuppiṉ veḷeyiṟṟu eyiṟṟiyar
cemmaṇi cuḻaṟṟit tēṉilakku eṟitara
perukkeṭuttu iḻitarum veḷḷap piracak
kāṉyāru untum kalvari nāṭa
coltavaṟu uvakkum pittiṉar cērpulaṉ       (5)
ciṟitiṭit teruḷvatum uṭaṉuṭaṉ maruḷvatum
āmeṉak kāṭṭum aṇiiruḷ miṉṉaliṉ
niṇampuṇar pukarvēl iṇaṅku tuṇiyāka
kāmam āṟuḷ kavartarum vekuḷanar
paṭiṟuḷam kamḻum ceṟitaru tīuṟaḻ       (10)
koḷḷivāyk kuṇaṅku uḷḷutōṟu ivaṟiya
miṉmiṉi umiḻum tuṉṉalar kaḷḷiyi
aṉṉieṉṟu aṇitarum ariiruḷ yāmattu
kaṭuñcuṭar iravi viṭumkatirt tēriṉi
mūla nicācarar mēlnilam puṭittu       (15)
tuṇikkaram piṭitteṉat tōṟṟiṭum poḻilcūḻ
kūṭal pativarum kuṇapperuṅ kuṉṟiṉaṉ
tāmari paḻitta irucaraṇ aṭiyāk
kōḷiṉar pōlak kuṟipala kuṟittē
aintamar katuppiṉaḷ amittōḷ nacii       (20)
taruviṉkiḻavaṉ tāṉeṉa niṟṟi
niṉṉuyirkku iṉṉal nērtarat tiruviṉ
taṉṉuyirkku iṉṉal tavaṟila ā!ā!
iraṇṭuyir taṇappeṉa eṉatukaṇ puṇarik
koṭuvaḻi ivvaravu eṉṟum
viṭuvatu neṭumpukaḻ aṭuvē lōyē !       (26)

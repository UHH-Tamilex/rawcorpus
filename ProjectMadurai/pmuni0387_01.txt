   PREFACE
   'Then felt I like some watcher of the sky
   When a new planet swims into his ken'
   John Keats thus described his joy on first looking into Chpman's Homer.
   I had a similar feeling when by chance I came across a few wornout
   scripts in Tamil language in my village house. They were brittle
   yellowise brown paper - manuscripts partly moth-eaten and partly
   destroyed by silver-fish (LAPISMA), a tiny insect that drills holes in
   papers. At once I rescued and resurrected them from oblivion and
   planned to give them when to the Tamil speaking world. The timely help
   of Thirumalai -Thiruppathi Devasthanam's financial help was in fact a
   blessing in guise. It encouraged and induced me to publish a good and
   devotional hymn book to sing in praise the glory of Sri Mahara
   Nedumkuzaikathar of the celebrated Vishnavite Pilgrim Center
   Thenthiruperai in Tamil Nadu. The outcome is this Onthology
   'Kuzhaikather Prabandha Thirattu'. May the Lord of the Seven Hills
   guide and guard me in this divine attempt.
   This present verse collection is a rare and rich compilation of
   devotional hymns in the form of minor Prabandhams. Some of them like
   'Kuzaikathar Kalampagam' ' Sobanam', 'Kirthanas', 'Santhada pathu' and
   'Thirupani Malai' are not yet published. Other works like the decade of
   'Nammalvar', 'Thenthiruperai Pasuram' is added as it forms Part and
   Parcel of the life of the inhabitants of Thenthiruperai. A number of
   lyrics are compiled lest they should be forgotten. Both 'Kuzhaikathar
   Pillai Tamil', and 'Kuzhaikathar Kalambagam' are directly copied from
   old manuscripts with stress and strain where the author's names were
   not found. Though 'Kuzhaikathar Pillai - Tamil' has been printed once
   it is not popular in the home town. But here it is added. Each and
   every prabandham is preceded by a foreword and succeeded by a glossary.
   The foreword in Tamil introduces the literary work. There is a general
   introduction in regional language about this anthology. A brief account
   of the tiny village of Thenthiruparai and the deity there will be a
   valuable guide to the readers.
   An introduction from Sri. Pattar-Piran Ramanuja Swami, the present Jeer
   of Vanamamalai Mutt adorns this collection at the outset. It is being
   followed by an introductory message from Thiru T.V. Makara Bhushanam,
   the retired assistant collector of customs; well-versed in
   Tamil,English and Sanskrit. His love for Vaishnavism is enffable.
   The aim in compiling this authology is to put into print the varied
   literary blossoms with religious aroma and to weave them into a garland
   of Poesy. This humble literary attempt has been dedicated to the memory
   of my beloved paternal uncle, Sri.P. Sri Acharya, the veteran Tamil
   Scholar. A learned man like him needs no introduction to the students
   of Tamil literature. His was a life devoted to the studies and research
   in Kamba Ramayanam and Vaishnavite Literature. His magnum opus Sri.
   RAMANUJA' won him the Sahitya Academy Award. He was the author of the
   celbrated serial Chithira Ramayana (awaiting publication). His
   'Chithira Thiruppavai' has already been published by the T.T.
   Devasthanam. He has hundred of books and essays written in Tamil to his
   credit. Hence it is my bounden duty to dedicate this anthology to him
   from whom I inherited the love and taste for my mother tongue.
   There are 96 kinds of minor literary poetic compositions according to
   Tamil tradition. More and more are added to them. The folk-song motif
   is the striking theme in them. One of the most popular forms of these
   prabandhams is 'Kalampagam'. It means a godevil of flowers of many
   hues. Learned people as the custodiens of literary traditions have no
   sympathy for folk-songs. But sometimes the folk-motif enters into the
   main literary stream. It also rejuvenates and enriches it into a minor
   literature or Prabandham. Poetry and folk songs are fused together and
   set in harmony in Kalambagam. Generally Kalampagam is a minor
   prabandham consisting of 100 Poems composed with the 18 parts of
   Kalampagam. Slowly this number of metrical devices increased from 18.
   But the style remains simple and appealing. Even today it retains its
   youthful charm and vigour. As per tradition Kalampagams are written to
   sing in praise of the deity of a temple such as Anzhahar Kalampagam or
   the place where the temple is situated such as 'Thiruvengada
   Kalampagam'. Sometimes they are sung in honour of a sing or a spiritual
   guru. But none of these rises to the height of 'Nandi-Kalampagam'.
   This tiny village of Thenthiruperai has the unique honour of having two
   Kalampagams to its credit. One is 'Thiruperai Kalampagam' already
   published by its author, Sri. Anantha Krisha Iyengar and the other is
   'Kuzhaikadhar Kalampagam' which is now being published for the first
   time. It appears to be an anonymous work. But its poetic merit speaks
   volumes about the unknown and unheard author. In brief it is a
   beautiful literary poetic price 'Kuzhaikadar Kalampagam' is a wonderful
   collection of 100 poems. It commences with 'Kappidal' or 'the song of
   Protection'. The Kappu Poem is followed by a Poem of worship to
   Nammalvar addressing his spiritual guru, Nammalvar as Parankusan the
   author makes an appeal to him. He wants his religion's leader to
   control other religions first as the elephant is controlled by the
   spear known as 'Ankusam'. The poet requests the reader to put up with
   him for what he lisped in verse may have flaw. Yet he has tested his
   poetic skill by making many to listen to his composition. The humility
   of the poet is obvious. The varied metrical devices and patterns reveal
   his poetic genius. His simple style flows fluently like the meanderings
   of the perennial river Thamirabarani. It is a pity that many a scholar
   of the past missed this noble piece of literature with its divine
   message to have faith in God and translate this faith however limited
   with constructive actions.
   'Kuzhaikadhar Pillai Tamil' occupies the second place in this edition.
   It has been taken down from an old worn out manuscript. This rich piece
   of literature is little known to villagers. It is said to have been
   published once from Dr. U.V.S.'s manuscript library. It has been added
   here to make it popular among the devotees of Kuzhaikadhar. 'Pillai -
   Tamil' is another popular type of literature. It is believed to be the
   first and foremost of the varied types of minor literature. Its seed is
   embedded in Tolkappiyam. But the real father of this kind of poetry is
   the great Vaishnava saint Perialvar. The poet describes Krishna's
   nativity making Thirukkottiyur in Tamil Nadu as the birth place of
   Krishna.
   Generally 'Pillai Tamil' is a graphic account of the childhood games
   and sports of the poetic hero in verse. It is in praise of the
   childhood in all its varying stages. The rapid growth and development
   of the child with its pranks and mischiefs are well brought-out as
   beautiful pen-pictures. In fact 'Pillai Tamil' is a lovely piece of
   poetic art making the reader become one with the child. The innocence,
   the gaiety and the sportive spirit of the infant enchants one and all.
   'Pillai Tamil' starts singing of the child from its third month to
   twenty-first month. The hero of the poem is either God or King.
   Imagining the hero as a child, the poet sings of the ten stages of its
   growth with ten songs for a stage sung on every alternate month from
   the third to the twenty-first month. 'Kuzhaikadhar Pillai - Tamil' is
   no exception to this. It sings in praise of Lord Kuzhaikadhar in His
   infancy.
   The first stage of Kuzhaikadhar 'Pillai Tamil' offers prayer for the
   welfare of the child in 14 poems seeking the blessings of Saraswathi,
   the Goddess of Learning, Sri. Ramanuja, the spiritual guru of
   Vaishnavites and all the twelve Alvars. This section is known as
   'Kappu' or 'the song of Protection'. The rest of the poem shows the
   beautiful and captivating gestures of the various games of the child.
   The to and from wavy movements of the child are quite picturesque.
   Lulllaby follows. The mother lulls the child to sleep by her melodious
   songs. The next few stages really enchant us. The Song of clapping
   hands and the song of kisses thrill us. In the welcome song the mother
   cheers up the tiny toddler to catch-hold of her to move forward. Next
   is the song of the Moon. Here we find the mother making a plea to the
   moon to come down to appease the crying child. All these songs are
   common to both the sexes. If it is a female child, the last stages are
   the songs of swing, bath and ammanai. As the child is a male one here,
   these are in addition the songs of destroying playfully the sand
   castles built by the female children, the song of beating the kettle
   drum and finally the song of pulling the toy- cart playfully. the
   spontaneous outpourings of the mother about her child's naughty games
   andmis- chievous tricks, capture our heart, mind and soul. The poet
   takes us from the world of the child to that of a divine one. The
   language describing these divine activities rises naturally to a grand
   and majestic tenor. In such a mood the poetry becomes genuine and
   sincere elevating us to a nobler plane. The words of Reed seem to
   endorse this view for he says:
   The experience of Poetry is in the art of creation; the experience is
   the poetry and is wholly a mental units. In the unconscious mind it may
   strike root: from that hidden source it may send up a shoot, strike
   with the conscious brain unasked'.
   Kuzhaikadhar Pillai Tamil is followed by 'Kuzhai kadhar Sobanam'. It is
   a piece of folk-lore taken down directly from the oral literature of
   the women-folk of Thenthiruperai. It is like a ballad poem with the two
   striking themes of the love and courage of Kuzhaikadhar. Then comes the
   collection of the simple but effective 13 poems entitled 'Santhana
   Pathu'. It is quite popular among the inhabitants of this village as an
   elixir for the childless couples. A sincere and pious recital of this
   piece has been rewarding to issueless couples as born in testimony to
   the people of Thenthiruperai. This oral piece in verse is being
   published for the first time. 'Santhana* Pathu' is followed by a
   variety of songs like 'Kirthanas'. These lyrics are short and sweet and
   can be set to music.
   A number of miscellaneous rural songs are also compiled here. The
   celebrated 11 pasurams of Nammalvar known as 'Thenthiruperai Pathigam'
   precede this collection of minor poems. Most of them are part and
   parcel of the daily rituals of worship in Kuzhaikadhar temple and also
   in the sweet homes of these villagers. A fairly good collection of
   various metrical pieces on the verge of being forgotten after their
   first publication are collected and compiled in this anthology. Some of
   them are prison-poems. Once many of the people from Thenthiruperai were
   arrested and imprisoned in Tirunelveli Town which part is even today
   remembered as Prison Street. (KAVAL PURAI THERU). The reason was
   non-payment of taxes due to the failure of monsoon. Their chief grief
   was not for leaving behind their kith and kin but because of their
   inability to worship their Lord Kuzhaikadhar every day and to proceed
   to the neighbouring village Alwar Thirunagari to offer their obeisance
   to Nammalvar Die-hards. As they were deprived of this divine
   opportunity, their sorrow and sadness burst out. Their outpourings in
   verse are reprinted here. The most prominent of these compositions is
   'Kuzhaikadhar Pamalai', a poetic garland of 103 Poems by Narayana
   Dishadhar. It was published byDr. U. V. Swaminatha Iyer as 'Poem of the
   Prison' (Chirai Thantha Chenkuyil) with a valuable introduction.
   Already it has been published by me separately as a hymn-book for daily
   worship. Some of the remarkable single pieces in metre added here were
   written by my grand - father Sri. Anantha Krishna Iyengar, a native of
   this village. An anonymous interrogative poem in 4 lines decorates the
   front wall of the first Mantapam of Kuzaikadhar temple. The poet
   wonders if there is anything equal to the eternal, never fading
   red-lotus face of Lord Kuzhaikadhar addressed as Vadatha Chenthamarai.
   The poet poses the problem to the Sun - god for He has visited all the
   planets of the universe. This enigmatic poem is also noted down in this
   edition. A mural poem presented in the form of the temple-car adorning
   the front part of the temple is also recorded here in its pictorial
   form 'Radha Banthanam'. The design or the verse is known as 'Chakra
   Banda (and anybody deciphred it used to get a reward in those days of
   yore).
   The contribution was made by my paternal grand-father, Kavi Pitchu
   Iyengar. The present Anthology is concluded with a song of blessing
   called Mangalam.
   In the days of my childhood my house used to rumble with the pasurams
   of Alars. The lively literary debates and discussions of my paternal
   circle with its pals left a deep impact on me. The musical recital of
   the gymns of 'Nalayira Divya Prabandam' in the Vaishnavite temples and
   in our sweet homes strengthened my mind, heart and soul. It often
   haunted me like a passion and kindled my religious fervour. Already I
   had been born and brought upin a literary atmosphere surcharged with
   the singing of these poetic compositions. No doubt God is present in
   several forms with different names. In the name of Swami Makara
   Nedumkuzhaikadhar I got the inspiration of undertaking this divine work
   and in the form of the Lord of Thiruvenkadam I received the financial
   assistance to complete it. In honour of Lord Kuzhaikadhar the minor
   works and sundry poems on Him are collected and compiled as a living
   monument to posterity that will not willingly let it die.
   May the Lord guide and guard us all.
   A. Padmaja Anantharaman.
   Printed by: Sri. Ratnam Press, Tin. - 6

   THENTHIRUPERAI SRI SWAMI MAKARA NEDUN KUZHAIKATHAR
   Thenthiruperai, situated on the trunk road connecting Tirunelveli and
   Tiruchendur in Tamil Nadu is a small village lying on the banks of
   river Tambirabarani (also known as porunai). The village has
   traditionally nurtured Tamil literature with religious fervour.
   Thenthiruperai is one of the 108 vaishnavite pilgrim centres and also
   forms part of the Nava Thirupathy of South Tamil Nadu. The name '
   Thenthiruperai' is derived from 'Per Aiel' which in Tamil connotes the
   lofty and majestic castles and forts.
   Nammalwar, the mystic saint has immortalised the village with his
   'MANGALA SASANAM' (10 Tamil verses in "Thiruvaimozhi", one of the four
   parts of "Nalayra Divya Prabantham"). These poems describe Nammalwar's
   passionate love for the Lord of Thenthiruperai in the Nayaki - Nayaka
   bhava, endeared as Parankusa Nayaki.
   Makara Nedunkuzhaikathar is the presiding deity. The Lord's name might
   have been a Tamil rendering of the original Sanskrit version
   "Makarayutha karna pasan" or simply Makara Bhushanam. The legend goes
   that on a full moon day in the month of Panguni under the star
   'UTHIRAM', Neela Devi (Mother Earth) who was doing penance on the banks
   of the river found a pair of glittering fish-like ear rings (Makara).
   Devi felt that they would well-adorn the long and big ears of her Lord,
   her lover, Mukil Vannan, the cloud-hued. The Lord appeared before her
   and made her happy by wearing them. Hence the name Makara
   Nedunkuzhaikathar. The place where the ear-studs were found is known as
   "Kudupunalthurai". It is believed that the long and beautiful ears of
   the Lord of Thenthiruperai relish the vedic recitation, festive sound,
   and the yell of the merry-making children. This is the reason why the
   "Garuda Sannathi" is not situated in front of the Lord but situated at
   some distance which is a peculiar feature not seen in any other
   Vaishnavite temple.
   The present resident community owe their origin to a holy ancestry
   which specialised a branch of Sama vedha, known as Jaimini Sama vedha.
   The swara recitation is unique to Thalavakara Jaimini Sama vedha.
   Thalavaikara was the chief disciple of Rishi Jaimini. Kenoupanishad is
   also known as Thalavakara upanishad. Presently there is only one
   patasala at Thogur in Tanjore District offering Jaimini saka of
   samaveda.
   Madurakaviyar the celebrated hagiographer of Nammalvar is believed to
   be the ancestor of the residents. The village had been made holy and
   divine by the visit to Sri Ramanuja. Thenthiruperai is also the birth
   place of many reputed poets, scholars, and freedom-fighters. It
   possesses a number of literary compositions like "Kuzhaikathar Pillai
   Tamil", "KUZHAIKATHAR KALAMBAKAM" and "SANTHANA PATHU". The pamalai,
   garland of 100 verses on Kuzhaikathar written in sweet and simple Tamil
   is being recited by all everyday.
   May Lord MAKARA NEDUN KUZHAIKATHAR Bless All.

   srī kuḻaikkātar kalampakam
   - nūlmukam
   tamiḻ moḻiyiluḷḷa toṇṇūṟṟāṟu vakaic ciṟṟilakkiyaṅkaḷil oṉṟu kalampakam
   eṉṉum pirapantam ākum. kalavaic cuvai nirampiya ippirapantam caṟṟup
   piṉṉar tōṉṟiya ilakkiyam eṉalām. kalampakam eṉṟa peyarē ippirapantattiṉ
   iyalpai viḷaṅkac ceyvatāyirukkiṟatu. iccoṟkuk kalavai eṉpatu poruḷ.
   pala vakaiyāṉa malarkaḷaic cērttuk kaṭṭiya malar mālaiyiṉaik katampam
   eṉṟu kūṟuvatuṇṭu. itu 'katampakam' eṉa vaṭa moḻiyil vaḻaṅkap peṟum.
   palvēṟu vakaiyāṉa niṟamum, uruvamum, maṇamum koṇṭa malarkaḷāl toṭutta
   malar mālai pōṉṟu ippirapantamum palvēṟu vakaip pākkaḷaiyum,
   poruḷkaḷaiyum, cuvaikaḷaiyum koṇṭu varum eṉṉum uvamai nayam poruntavē
   itu 'kalampakam' eṉṉum peyar peṟuvatāyiṟṟu.
   'kaḷi vaṇṭu miḻaṟṟiya kalampakam puṉainta
   alaṅkalat toṭaiyal ..... .... ... '
   eṉṟa toṇṭaraṭip poṭiyār pāt toṭarāl itu pulappaṭum.
   tamiḻil vaiṇavac cārpuṭaiya kalampakaṅkaḷ mikak kuṟaivu. teṉ tamiḻ
   nāṭṭil navatiruppatikaḷuḷ oṉṟāṉa teṉ tiruppērai nammāḻvārāl maṅkaḷā
   cācaṉam ceyyappaṭṭa vaiṇavat talamākum. iṅku eḻuntaruḷiyirukkum pirāṉiṉ
   tirunāmam srī makarāyuta karṇa pāca: tamiḻil makara neṭuṅkuḻaikkātar
   eṉṟu vaḻaṅkappaṭukiṟār. ippatiyiṉ taṉic ciṟappu eṉṉaveṉṟāl iṅku iraṇṭu
   kalampakaṅkaḷ uḷḷaṉa. oṉṟu apinava kāḷamēka ananta kiruṣṇaiyaṅkār eḻuti
   veḷiyiṭṭa 'tiruppēraik kalampaka' mākum. piṟitoṉṟu tāṉ 'kuḻaikkātar
   kalampakam'. itaṉai iyaṟṟiya āciriyar peyar paṟṟiya vivaraṅkaḷ oṉṟum
   kiṭaikkavillai. ituvarai acciṭappaṭṭatākavum teriyavillai. eṅkaḷ vīṭṭil
   kaṇṭeṭukkappaṭṭa kaippiratiyiṉ paṭivantāṉ iṅku koṭukkappaṭṭirukkiṟatu.
   'ēṟkaṉavē oru kalampakam iruppatu terintāl inta tiruppērai
   kalampakattai eḻutiyirukka māṭṭēṉ' eṉa eṉatu tāttā ananta
   kiruṣṇaiyaṅkār kūṟiyatuṇṭu. terintiruntāl 26-uṟuppukkaḷaik koṇṭa
   'tiruppērai kalampakam' namakkuk kiṭaittirukkumā?
   "palpū miṭainta paṭalaik kaṇṇi"
   (perumpāṇ: 174) eṉṟa varikku 'pala pūkkaḷ kalantu neruṅkiya
   kalampakamākiya mālai' eṉa nacciṉārkkiṉiyar urai vakuttuḷḷār.
   yāpperuṅkala viruttiyil aṭikaḷ eḻuttu ovvātu vevvēṟu vakaiyil amaintu
   varum kalippāviṟku 'kalampakak kavi' eṉṟa peyar tarappaṭukiṟatu.
   (yāp.vi:95) ivaiyum ippeyar kāraṇattai iṉitu viḷakkukiṉṟaṉa.
   'kalampakam' eṉṟa coṟṟoṭariṉai kalappu + akam eṉap pirittuc coṟporuḷ
   kūṟuvatuṇṭu. kalappu eṉpatu kalampu eṉa niṉṟatu. kalampakattiṟku
   uṟuppāka amainta pala tuṟaikaḷ kalattalait taṉṉakattē koṇṭa nūl
   eṉapporuḷ virippatuṇṭu. cilar paṉṉiraṇṭu marakkāl eṉṉum poruḷpaṭum
   kalam eṉṉum collum, kaṭavuḷiṉ āṟu kuṇaṅkaḷaik kuṟikkum 'pakam' eṉṉum
   collum ummait tokaiyākac cērntu 18 uṟuppukkaḷaiyuṭaiya nūl eṉa ippeyar
   kāraṇap peyarāyiṟṟu eṉpar. puya vakuppu, mataṅku, ammāṉai, kālam,
   campiratam, kār, tavam, kuṟam, maṟam, pāṇ, kaḷi, cintu, iraṅkal,
   kaikkiḷai, tūtu, vaṇṭu, taḻai, ūcal, eṉṉum patiṇeṇ uṟuppukkaḷaik koṇṭu
   palavakaip pākkaḷum, pāviṉaṅkaḷum viravivara, palvakai nayaṅkaḷum
   ceṟintu vara antātit toṭai amaiyap pāṭappaṭuvatu kalampakam ākum.
   nāḷaṭaivil patiṉeṭṭu eṉṟa varaiyaṟaiyaittāṇṭi irupattāṟu ilakkiya
   uṟuppukkaḷ varai kalampakattil kaiyāḷappaṭṭirukkiṉṟaṉa. teyvaṅkaḷaiyum,
   maṉṉarkaḷaiyum talaivarkaḷākak koṇṭu ippirapanta vakaikaḷ tōṉṟiṉa.
   teyvattaip poruḷākak koṇṭu eḻunta kalampaka nūl
   perumpāṉmaiyāṉavaiyākum. 'aḻakar kalampakam' ' tiruppēraik kalampakam'
   eṉṟār pōṉṟu teyvattiṉaip pāṭṭuṭait talaivaṉākak koṇṭatuṭaṉ, talattiṉ
   peyarāl 'tiruvaraṅkak kalampakam' 'tiruvēṅkaṭak kalampakam' pōṉṟaṉa
   eḻutappaṭṭaṉa. nantik kalampakam pōṉṟu pirapantat talaivarkaḷ peyaril
   eḻutappaṭṭa kalampakaṅkaḷum uṇṭu. ki.pi.9-ām nūṟṟāṇṭu tuvaṅki iṉṟu
   varai eṇṇaṟṟa kalampaka nūlkaḷ veḷi vantuḷḷaṉa. piṟkālattil kalampaka
   uṟuppukāḷāṉa ammāṉai, tūtu ūcal, kuṟam pōṉṟavai taṉit taṉi nūlāka
   virintaṉa.
   mutaṉ muṟaiyāka acciṭṭu veḷiyiṭappaṭṭuḷḷa 'kuḻaikkātar kalampakam'
   iṉiya eḷiya 101 ceyyuṭkaḷaik koṇṭatu. itu kāppuc ceyyuḷuṭaṉ
   tuvaṅkukiṟatu. kāppu eṉṟāl kāttal eṉṟu poruḷ. itu kavi tām eṭuttuk
   koṇṭa nūl iṉitu muṭiyum poruṭṭu āṉṟōr vaḻakkappaṭi vaḻipaṭu
   kaṭavuḷaiyāvatu, ēṟpuṭaik kaṭavuḷaiyāvatu vaṇaṅkuvatu ākum. itu
   kāttaruḷum kaṭavuḷ paṟṟiya vaṇakkattukkāṉa toḻilāku peyar ākum.
   aṭiyārkaḷaip paṟṟiya nūlkaḷukku kaṭavuḷ vaṇakkam kūṟuvatu pōla
   kaṭavuḷaip paṟṟik kūṟum nūlukku nammāḻvāraik kāppuc colkiṟār āciriyar.
   innūliṉ coṟkaḷ palar kātil pōṭṭup pārttu kaṉivu peṟṟirukkiṉṟaṉa
   eṉpataṉai ikkalampakattiṉ mutal variyē kāṭṭukiṟatu.
   cevitōṟuṅ kaṉinta centēṉ
   teḷinta col.........."
   (cevitōṟum kaṉinta col, centēṉ teḷinta col' eṉṟu kūṭṭuka.)
   puviyiluḷḷōr eṭuttuc collip pukaḻkiṉṟa pēraiyiṉ pukaḻiṉaip pāṭuvatu
   ikkuḻaikkakātar kalampakamākum. parāṅkucaṉ eṉappaṭum nammāḻvār piṟa
   camayaṅkaḷākiṟa yāṉaikaḷukku aṅkucam pōṉṟavaṉ. āḻvārai yāṉaiyeṉṟu
   uvamittaṟkēṟpa kavimatam poḻiyum ñāṉamākiṟa aṅkucattai uṭaiyatu eṉṟum
   kūṟiṉār. pākaṉaip paṟṟit toṭarkiṟatu. yāṉai vilaṅkaṟutta pākaṉallavā?
   pākaṉeṉṟa coṉṉataṟkēṟpa puḷḷiṉ pākaṉeṉṟu kūṟiṉār. ikkalampakamum pākaṉ
   pukaḻait tāṉē colkiṟatu. kaḷiṟu tāṉē kākkum eṉṟa taṉip poruḷum kāṇka.
   kāppuc ceyyuḷiṟkup piṟaku avaiyaṭakkam kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. nūlāciriyar
   kaṟṟōr kuḻumiya avaiyil tammiyalpai veḷiyiṭṭu aṭaṅkip pēcutal vaḻakkam.
   etaṟkāka innūlaic ceytār eṉṟa viṉā eḻumpalām. ulakam taḻaikkavum,
   maṉṉarkaḷ nīti neṟi tavaṟāmal āṭci puriyavum, vētaṅkaḷ nilai peṟavum
   innūliṉai eḻutiṉār.
   "kuḻaikkātar kalam pakameṉ ṟoru pēr nāṭṭik
   koḻitta tamiḻ cuḻit teṭuttuk kūṟum pāṭal"
   eṉṟu kūṟuvatiṉ mūlam āciriyar tamatu tamiḻp paṟṟē nūliṉaip
   paṭaittataṟkut talaiyāya kāraṇam eṉak kāṭṭukiṟār. innūlil avaratu tamiḻ
   paṟṟu koḻikkiṉṟatu. tamiḻppāṭalkaḷ cuḻitteḻukiṉṟaṉa! innūliṉ kaṇ
   coṟkuṟṟaṅkaḷ nirampi vaḻikiṉṟaṉa. ceyyuḷ uṟuppukaḷākiṟa cīr, taḷai,
   aṭi, toṭai pōṉṟavaṟṟil ettaṉaiyō piḻaikaḷ uḷḷaṉa. avai cuṭṭik kāṭṭāmalē
   pulappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. tamatu kavitai 'oruporuṭkum ovvātu; kācu peṟātu' eṉa
   mikat tāḻmaiyuṭaṉ nūlāciriyar kūṟic celvataik kāṇalām. āṉāl avar
   makarak kuḻaiyaṉiṉ tirunāmattai māṇpuṭaṉ allavā kūṟukiṟār.
   'oru poruṭṭu movvāta tēṉiṉumeṉ coṟ
   piḻaikkāka ikaḻvarō periyōr ñāṉap
   perumāṉṟaṉ tirunāmam peṟṟak kālē”
   oruvarum taṉatu col puṉ col eṉac colla māṭṭārkaḷ eṉa nampukiṟār
   āciriyar. ēṉeṉṟāl paṭippavar tam perumaiyum atil tāṉē
   aṭaṅkiyirukkiṟatu. kaṟṟaṟinta periyōr muṉ evvaḷavu taṉ aṭakkattuṭaṉ
   pēcukiṟār eṉpatu pulaṉākiṟatu.
   pāṭṭiyal vitiyiṉpaṭi pū eṉṉum maṅkalac colluṭaṉ “kuḻaikkātar
   kalampakam” maṅkaḷakaramākat tuvaṅki iṉiya eḷiya āṟṟoḻukku naṭaiyil
   kalampaka uṟuppukkaḷ viravivara 100 ceyyuṭkaḷaik koṇṭu viḷaṅkukiṟatu.
   nūliṉ iṟutiyil oru vāḻttup pāṭaluṭaṉ nūl muṭivaṭaikiṉṟatu. innūl
   muḻuvataiyum kaṟṟuṇarntāl pulavar perumāḷiṭam koṇṭa īṭupāṭum, tamiḻiṉ
   mītu koṇṭa paṟṟum, ittiruppatiyiṉ mītuḷḷa paktiyum naṉku pulaṉākiṉṟaṉa.
   “pērai māl kuḻaikkātar” 'makarak kuḻaiyaṉē' 'pērai vāḻ mukilē' eṉṟu
   palavāṟu emperumāṉai viḷittup pāṭukiṟār. irāmāvatārattilum,
   kiruṣṇāvatarāttilum nūlāciriyar koṇṭa mikunta īṭupāṭṭiṉai ik kalampakam
   eṭuttuk kāṭṭukiṟatu.
   'kāṉakam pukuntu virātaṉai vataittuk kavantaṉaik kavarntu cūrppaṉakai
   kariya mūkkarintu karaṉuyir kuṭittuk karuṅkaṭal vaḻipaṭak kaṭantu”
   eṉa irāmapirāṉiṉ cātaṉaikaḷaic curuṅkac colli viḷaṅka vaikkiṟār.
   cakaṭam uṭaittavaṉ, marutiṭaip pāyntavaṉ, naracimmāvatāram eṭuttavaṉ,
   kuṟaḷ vaṭivāṉavaṉ, irāvaṇaṉai veṉṟavaṉ...ēṉ ellāmē makarak kuḻaiyaṉē
   eṉa mīṇṭum mīṇṭum valiyuṟuttukiṟār. mēlum appirāṉaip 'pērēcar',
   'vaḻutit tirunāṭaṉ' eṉavum pōṟṟip paravukiṉṟār. appirāṉ
   eḻuntaruḷiyirukkum patiyāṉat teṉ tiruppēraiyiṉ ceḻippiṉai aṭikkaṭi
   niṉaivu kūrkiṟār:
   "mēḻi valam puripaḻaṉap pērai nāṭṭil" eṉṟum, (19)
   "vāḷaip pakaṭu pukuntaḻakkum vayal cūḻ
   vaḻutit tirunāṭaṉ" (17)
   eṉa āciriyar kuṟippiṭukiṟār. āciriyar tāmiraparaṇi natiyiṉaiyum
   pārāṭṭat tavaṟavillai.
   "vaṇikar pōṟ kaṭai nilam purakka
   varuperum porunait tuṟaivaṉē"
   anta natiyiṉ teṉ karaiyilirukkum ip pēraiyiṉait 'tamiḻppērai'
   'aṟipērai' 'vaṇṭamiḻp pērai' eṉap palavāṟu pōṟṟukiṉṟaṉar. mēlum
   ippatiyiṉ perumai vāynta talavakāra cāma vētikaḷaik kuṟippiṭat
   tavaṟavillai;
   'nūṟṟeṭṭu vētiyar tam pukaḻ pāṭi yāṭīrūcal' (19)
   mēlum iṅkirukkum āṭavaraip paṟṟik kuṟippiṭukaiyil tirukkuṟaḷ pāṇiyiṉaip
   piṉpaṟṟukiṟār.
   "kāṉār karuṅkuḻalār kāmattiṉ pāl maṟantu
   pērai aṟattiṉ poruṭṭu poruṭpālē" (44)
   aṅkirukkum āṭavar kāmattai maṟantu illaṟattai naṭattap poruḷ tēṭic
   ceṉṟaṉarām. tirukkuṟaḷil kāṇappaṭuvatu pōl kāmattup pāl ciṟitu; aṟattup
   pāl naṭuttaram; poruṭpāl atikamāka innūlilum iṭam peṟukiṉṟatu.
   innūl kaṟṟōrum, maṟṟōrum paṭittu makiḻattakka vitattil coṟ cuvai,
   poruṭcuvai, paktic cuvai coṭṭac coṭṭa amaintuḷḷatu. itaṉaip paṭikkum
   pōtellām anta ūr pēr teriyāta pulavariṉ pulamait tiṟamai,
   kavitaiyāṟṟal, kaṟpaṉai vaḷam nammai viyappilāḻttukiṉṟaṉa. aṇikaḷum,
   kavitai nayaṅkaḷum viravi niṟka āciriyar kūṟuvatu pōṉṟu, 'pākaiyum
   piḻintu tēṉaiyuṅ kavarntu pāluṭaṉ kalanta moḻi cēr' nta nūlē
   ikkuḻaikkātar kalampakamākum. nalamikum peruñcuvaiyiṉai namakku
   iṉitaḷikkum kuḻaikkātarai vāḻtti vaṇaṅkuvōmāka:
   "vāḻi pukaḻ pērai makarak kuḻai vāḻi
   vāḻi tamiḻ nūṟṟeṭṭu māmaṟaiyōr - vāḻiyavē"

   yaṭikkuṅkaim māṟōnī rañciṉarpōṟ kātu
   piṭikkumkaim māṟō peritu. 30

   kaliviruttam
   peruviṭa varavaṇaip pērai mātavaṉ
   maruviṭa niṉaikilāṉ maṅkai mātarē
   taruviṭa veṇṇilāt taḻaitta teṅkaṇu
   moruviṭa milainamak kuṟaivi ṭaṅkaḷē. 31
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalittuṟai
   uṟaikkōṭu māṭavar vāḷā lorukom piṟantu maṟṟaik
   kuṟaik kōṭu koṇṭuḻal kuñcaram pōlum koṭiyiṭaiyīr
   tuṟaik kōṭu vāyvaitta mālpē raiyiṟ ṟeṉṉar cūḻnta paṇṭaic
   ciṟaikkōṭu mēkam piṟaikkōṭu tāṅkic ciṟakkiṉṟatē. 32
   tūtu - eḻucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi yāciriya viruttam.
   ciṟantār toḻattaru maruntā maḻaippuyal
           ceḻumpē raiyut tamarpāl
   aṟantā ṉuṟakkuru kiṉaṅkāṇ maṭappeṭai
           yaṉaṅkā ḷurait taruḷvīr
   iṟantā meṉiṟpiḻai yiruntā meṉiṟpaḻu
           tiraṅkā maṉattavar pōṉ
   maṟantāṉ maṟakkavu niṉaintā ṉiṉaikkavu
           maṉantā ṉemak kilaiyē. 33
   veṇpā
   emakkumukam vāṭa viruntā maraikaḷ
   tamakkumukam vāṭuñ calippeṉ - amaitturaiyum
   peṇmatiyeṉ ṟōtāmaṟ pētaināṭ ṭaṉṉaṅkāḷ
   taṉmatiyi luṇṭō taḻal. 34
   patiṉāṉkucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi yāciriya viruttam
   taḻalpiḻintu cāṟukoṇṭu cantaṉatti liṭṭatār
          taṇṇilāvai yeriyeḻas camaittuviṭṭa pāviyār
   kuḻal piḻinta vicaiyilē kuḷirntaciṅki vaittatār
          koṭumaivanta kālamācai koṇṭirukka vallamō
   niḻalpiḻintu parukaveṉṟu niṉaiyumacura marutamum
          neṟiyaveṉṟa kuricilpērai nīrkuḷikku nāraikā
   ḷaḻalpiḻinta vēlaremmai yāṇaiyiṭ ṭakaṉṟatā
          lantavāymai yuṭalpiḻintē ṉāviyuṇṭu viṭṭatē. 35
   piṉmuṭuku veṇpā
   āviyuṇṭu maiyuṇ ṭaṟivaiyuṇṭu niṟkumiru
   kāviyuṇṭu tāmaraikkē kaṇṭīrō - tēviyuṭaṉ
   mālvaḷarnta pēraiyiṉkaṇ vāvikaṇṭu pūvaiyuṇṭu
   kālkiḷarnta nīlavaṇṭu kāḷ. 36
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭiyāciriya viruttam
   vaṇṭirukkuṅ kuḻaṟpuṟattu vāṭṭimaṭit tuppiṭittu vaṭinta vaḷḷait
   taṇṭirukkuṅ kuḻaimaṭavīr kuḻaikkātar pēraiyiṉ mut tamiḻē pōla
   vuṇṭirukka vuvaṭṭāta vitaḻamutu miḷa nīru muṅkaḷ pāliṟ
   koṇṭirukkap peruṅkāmap pacikkutavā tiruppatuvuṅ koṭumai tāṉē. 37
   vañci viruttam
   koṭittēriṉar kuḻaikkātiṉar kulakkārvarai mēṟ
   piṭittīrtaḻai keṭuttīrkari piṇaittēṭuva tēṉ
   aṭittāmarai naṭappīravai yaṭaittāḷuva tōr
   toṭittōḻiyar puṉaccārporu toḻuttālai vē 38
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalittuṟai
   toḻumpākki yaṇṭar toḻakkaṟpa kāṭavi cūḻalarttē
   ṉeḻumpāk kiyameṉ ṟiruppatel lāmintu viṭṭantaṭṭit
   taḻumpāk kiyapoḻil cūḻpērai mālcara ṇāravintac
   ceḻumpāk kiyameṉ ṟavaṉaṭi yārpaṇṭu ceytavamē 39
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭiyāciriya viruttam
   ceyyiṟ karumpu vaḷarpērait tirumā ṟirunā ṭaṉaiyīrum
   toyyiṟ karumpu malarkkaṇaiyun toḻiluk karumpu viḻiyumataṉ
   kaiyiṟ karumpu malarkkaṇaiyuṅ kaikkoṇ ṭatupōṟ kaṇṭaveṉmēl
   eyyiṟ karumpuṅ kaṇaiyumilai iṉṟaik kiṟavā tiruppēṉē. 40

   paṉṉirucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi yāciriya viruttam
   irumpai neṟittut tutikkaimaṭut
          tiḷaṅkō makaḷir vīraṉeṟippa
   vetirttār cirattai neṟittumalai
          yiṭaṟi neṟittuk kaṭarpuṟattuc
   curumpai neṟittu vaḻikaṟaṅkac
          cuṟṟum taḻaikkuñ cevippaṭalat
   tuṅkak kaḷiṟṟiṉ micaippavaṉi
          toḻutāḷ virakam tolaiyātō?
   karumpai neṟittu muṭappalaviṉ
          kaṉiyai neṟittu maṭaimutukiṟ
   katalip paṭalaik kulaineṟittuk
          kaṉṉik kamukiṉ maṭaṉeṟittuk
   kurumpai neṟittut tēṉoḻukuṅ
          kuvaḷai neṟittup puṭaittuvarāl
   kutikkum puṉaṟpē raiyiṉ makarak
          kuḻaiyē yevarkkuṅ kōmāṉē. 41
   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi yāciriya viruttam
   māṉeṉpār kalaiyeṉpār toṭukka lāku
          malarttaḻaiyā leytatoru matta vēḻan
   tāṉeṉpār patiyeṉpār vaḻiyē teṉpār
          tāmaraippūṅ kōyileṉṟu tavirnta teṉpār
   kōṉeṉpār kulattutikkuṅ karuṇai mēkaṅ
          kuḻaikkātar pēraiyiḷaṅ kompē vampē
   yāṉeṉpā rallaveṉpā rillai yeṉpā
          rivarkōṭṭik ketiruraippār yāvar tāmē. 42
   kaḻit tāḻicai
   tāmōtarar matucūtaṉar tarupēraiyiṉ maṭavīr
   nāmōtara māmōkiṉi nalamōtara maṟiyīr
   āmōtara malavōveḷi taṭiyēṟuṭaṉ muṉivāyp
   pōmōtara niṉaiyīrkamaḻ putuvāy mala ramutē. 43
   kaliviruttam
   tēṉār poruṉait tirumāl tamiḻppērai
   yāṉāta kalvi yaṟivār payaṉaṉṟō
   kāṉār karuṅkuḻalār kāmattiṉ pālmaṟantu
   pōṉāraṟattiṉ poruṭṭup poruṭpālē. 44
   kalittāḻicai
   pālvaṭiyun tiraṇmulaiyum paccuṭampum pacunarampuñ
   cūlavaṭivun tōṉṟāmaṟ ṟūṇvayiṟṟiṟ ṟōṉṟiyanāṇ
   mēlvaṭivā miraṇiyaṉai viṉaitolaikkun tamiḻppērai
   mālvaṭivān tiruvaṭiva marakatattiṉ maṇivaṭivē. 45
   kaṭṭaḷaik kalittuṟai
   vaṭittūtu caṅkoppa vaṇṭōṭṭu mallikai vāyiṟkauvip
   piṭittūtu vaṇṭōṭum pēcukilēṉ piriyāta vaivar
   kuṭittūtu ceṉṟa kuḻaikkātark keṉmayal kūṟivarum
   paṭittūtu nīcellu vāymaḻai cāṭum paṉik koṇṭalē 46
   kaḻittāḻicai
   koṇṭalaik kōti vakiriṭ ṭiruṇṭa kuḻalārē
           kuṅkumac cēṟu pūcit tiraṇṭa mulaiyārē
   teṇṭiraip pāyal mītiṟ ṟuyiṉṟa ruḷumāmāl
           teṉtirup pērai mītiṟ ciṟanta maṭavīrē
   aṇṭaṟpoṟ pūmi tāṉviṭ ṭeḻunta ruḷiṉīrō
           ampuyak kōyil vāḻap piṟanta varunāmō
   taṇṭamiḻp pāki ṉūṟik kaṉinta moḻitārīr
           cantaṉak kāvi ṉīḻaṟ poḻintu maruvīrē. 47
   maruttēṟa luṇṇuṅ kaḷivaṇṭu kāḷvammiṉ mālaṟintu
   karuttē makiḻavuṅ kaṇkaḷi kūravuṅ kāykatalik
   kuruttē vicumpaḷak kumpērai mālkuḻaikkātarcempoṟ
   ṟiruntē riluṅkaṭi tāyvaru mōvaṉpar tērvaravē 48
   eḻucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi vaṇṇa viruttam
   varampaṟuṅ kaṭaṟpāyal pirintanaṅ kuḻaikkātar
           makiḻntateṉ tiruppērai vaḷavayalcūḻ
   karumpaiyuṅ kacappāka viḷaintameṉ cuvaippāku
           kaṉintacen tamiḻppōlu moḻimaṭavīr
   irumpuneñ cavarkkāka nekiḻntaneñ cemakkāka
           viruntucañ carittāvi yavarpiṟakē
   vīrumpuneñ cemakkāka maṟantavaṉ pavarkkāka
           viriñcalum paṭaittāṉeṉ vitivacamē 49
   paṉṉirucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭiyāciriya viruttam
   vitikkun toḻilāṟ palakōlam
           vevvē ṟeṭuttu naṭittoruvaṉ
   viḷaiyā ṭuvapōṟ ṟolaiyāta
           viṉaiyiṟ cuḻaṉṟu taṭumāṟi
   etiṟkun ciṟitā meṟumpukaṭai
           yāṉai mutalān tolkulatti
   lellāp piṟappum piṟantaluttē
           ṉiṉiyuṉ tiruttā ḷeṉakkaruḷvāy
   kutikkuṅ kaluḻip peruñcuvaṭṭuk
           kuṟuṅkaṭ peruvāṉ maḻaimatittuk
   kolaivēṭ ṭeḻutev vuṭalpiḷakkak
           kuttum piṟaikkim puriyeyiṟṟu
   matikkum pukarmat takamukattu
           varivaṇ ṭiraikkum paṇaikkarattu
   matavā raṇattuk karuḷpuriyum
           makarak kuḻaiyem perumāṉē. 50

   eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilaṭiyāciriya viruttam
   makarakkuḻai māyaṉ poruṉaittuṟai nāṭaṉ
           maṇṭaṅkuṭi yāḷun toṇṭaṉpaṇi vātaṉ
   pakataṟkari tākum paratattuva pōtaṉ
           paimpoṟkiri vāḻuñ cempoṟkoṭi pōlvāy
   cikarattaṉa pāraṅ kuḻaiyakkuṟu vērvuñ
           cintuṅkaṉi vāyiṉ pantatturai māṟun
   takarakkuḻal cōruṅ kaḷavittoḻil pōkan
           taṅkuñcuṉai tāṉiṅ keṅkuṅkiṭai yātē. 51
   cantakkali viruttam
   eṅkuṅkiṭai yātapē riṉpantaru pēraimāl
   veṅkaṇkaḷi yāṉaipōm vēḷkaṇṭeḻu mātarār
   caṅkaṅkaḷai vārukē cantattuḻal varumā
   taṅkaṅkaṇi yākavē ḷampiṉṉuyir vāṭumē. 52
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi vaṇṇa viruttam
   vāṭu maṉaittuyir vāḻa vaḷippavar māmaka
           rakkuḻaimāl
   kūṭu puṉaṟṟuṟai yāṭi yiḷaittuṭal kūṟu
           naraikkurukīr
   pēṭaiyai viṭṭaka lātiru muttamiḻ pēraṟi
           viṟkuṇamē
   yāṭava rippaṭi pōṉa piḻaikkiṉi yārai
           veṟuppatuvē. 53
   patiṉāṅku cīrc canta viruttam
   ārveṟuppiṉu mayal veṟuppiṉu maṉṉaimārkaḷ veṟuppiṉu
          mamutacantira kalaiveṟuppiṉu mantimālai vetuppa vēḷ
   pōrveṟuppiṉu maṟalivantoru puṭaiveṟup piṉum vaḷaikaṭaṟ
          puṭaviyēḻum veṟuppiṉumoru poruḷatāka niṉaippaṉō
   vārveṟutteḻu koṅkaiyīmaka rakkuḻaittiru
           māyaṉār
   mārpiṭattum varaippuyattum maṇantaṇaintu
           muyaṅkupain
   tārveṟittuḷa vāyiṉuñ carukāyiṉum
           peṟa viṭṭatōr
   cāmakīta moḻiccurumpoṭu tāṉveṟuppila
           tākiyē. 54
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi vaṇṇa viruttam
   ilatāki yuḷatāki yuṭalāki yuyirāki yiruḷāki
           yoḷiyākanī
   palatārai vekumāyai viḷaiyāṭu kuṇanīti
           palatēva raṟivārkaḷō
   malaruṭu kayalcāṭa matakūru puṉalcāṭi varupērai
           nakarāḷaṉē
   cilapērka ḷaṟivārkaḷ catuvēta mutupōta
           teḷiñāṉa muṭaiyōrkaḷē. 55
   puya vakuppu
   muppattiraṇṭu cīrk kaḻineṭilaṭiyāciriyac canta viruttam
   uṭaiyak kalacat tayir koṭ ṭiyeṭut
           titaḻvaḻi yoḻukiya tivalai poḻintaṉa
   uralaik katavuk kaṭaiyiṭ ṭuyarat
           tuṟipala taṭaviya naṟuney kavarntaṉa
   uṭalkaṭ ṭiṟukat toḻil maṟ piṭiyiṭ
           ṭacurarai yemapura maḷavu turantaṉa
   uyiraip parukak kaḷaviṭ ṭalakaip
           paṇaivarai mulaimuka neruṭi yiruntaṉa.
   paṭiyiṟ ṟuṭaipaṭ ṭuḻalak kaṉakaṉai
           nakanuṉi yuḻutiṭu ceruvi yiṭantaṉa
   paṉaiyiṟ kaṉiyot tirupatu muṭiyat
           talaiyuruḷ paṭavaṭu pakaḻi corintaṉa
   paritik katiruṭputaiyat tamaṇap
           paṭaniḻal keḻumiya tikiri cumantaṉa
   parumac cikarak kayilaip paraṉuk
           kiṭupali keṭaniṟai paricil vaḻaṅkiṉa
   kaṭalaik kaṭaiyap parumat tuvalit
           timaiyavar paciyaṟa vamutam viḷampiṉa
   kaḷapat teḷiyiṟ ṟuḷapat toṭaiyil
           parimaḷa mrukapata muḻuki yaḷantaṉa
   kaṉavaṭ ṭamulait tiraḷpaṭ ṭuruvip
           potuviyar varivaḷai porutu civantaṉa
   kamalat tavaḷait taḻuvik kaḷaviyi
           liḷakiya puḷakama toḻuki malintaṉa
   maṭaiyiṟ kaḻiyiṟ poruṉait tiraiyiṉi
           lutaṟiya varimaṇa lalaku neṭumpuḻai
   matakiṟ katalip paṭalaik kulaiyiṉil
           vaḷamuka kaṭaviyil maruvi valampuri
   vayalek kariṭap putumut tamiḻcoṟ
           kurukaiya ratipati paravu neṭuntakai
   makarak kuḻaiyut tamaṉit tiyaṉuyar
           parakati mutlava ṉaṇipoṟ puyaṅkaḷē. 56
   kuṟam - patiṉāṉku cīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi vaṇṇa viruttam
   puyaṅka cēkara muyaṅku nāṭati
           pukaḻnta pēraiyai vaṇaṅkiyē
   purinta vāymaiyi ṉikaḻnta mākuṟi
           pukaṉṟu vāḻkuṟa maṭantai nāṉ
   iyaṅku mākevu ḷiyumpo lātala
           virunta mānila miṇaṅkavē
   yicaintu māniti tulaṅka vēyaruḷ
           porunti vāḻuvai yilaṅkiḻāy
   vayaṅku mātalai varaintu pēratil
           vaḷaṅkoḷ cēvaka riraṇṭupēr
   vaḷarnta nāvala riraṇṭu pērōru
           vaṉunta rātalam vaṇaṅkuvō
   ṉuyaṅki nāṉuṭal varunti ṉēṉeḻu
           kuḻantai vāypaci yaṭaṅkavē
   yuṭantai yāyoru ciṟaṅkai kūḻiṭu
           kiḻinta tūcuru ḷuṟaṅkavē. 57
   koṟṟiyār - eḻucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi yāciriya viruttam
   araṅkattu ḷētuyilu maruñcakra pāṇivaya
           lakañcuṟṟu pērai nakarvāy
   virumpuṟṟa tāmamulai yarumpit tāmaraiyil
           viḷaintukka māmaṇi kolō
   karuṅkaṟṟai vārkuḻalai muṭintiṭṭa nāmamoṭu
           kalantiṭṭa tāvaṭamu māy
   varuṅkoṟṟi yāraḻaki ṉaraṅkoṟṟi yāṭalatu
           maruṅkoṟṟi yāraṟi varē 58
   cantakkali viruttam
   varukārmukila tirupēraiyi lamarpū vaṇaicēr
   peruvāḻvoṭu morunāḷavai pirivō malavē
   tiruvēyamu turuvēporu cilaivēḷ kuruvā
   muruvēṟema tuṭal vēṟema tuyirō kuyicarē. 59
   nēricai veṇpā
   uyirmuṭikkuñ cevvanti yuṇṭeṉavē kōtai
   mayirmuṭikkuñ cevvanti vaiyāḷ - ayaṉār
   peruntuḷatik kēkamum pēraimāl cāttu
   maruttuḷatik kēmayalā vāḷ. 60

   eḻucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi canta viruttam
   mayalaṟ ṟavaikkaruḷcey makarak kuḻaik kaṭavuḷ
           vayirap poruppi ṉayalcūḻ
   muyalait tuṭaittumati yataṉaip patittateṉa
           mukavaṭṭa miṭṭu varuvīr
   puyaliṟ kaṟuttakuḻal varaiyiṟ paṇaittamulai
           puḷakikki viṟṟu vīṭumvē
   rayalaṟṟa vaṟpaviṭai tiruneṟṟi yiṟṟalatē
           maḻiyat tuṭaippa taḻakē. 61
   aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi vaṇṇa viruttam
   aḻaku taṅkiya makara vaṇkuḻai yamalar taṇkirivāy
   nuḻai nuḻainteṉa tuyirai yuṇṭatu nuvala vumpaṭumō
   maḻai cumantalai kaṭal cumantiṇai malai cumantaruḷē
   taḻaiya vantoru poḻili ṉiṉṟatu taṉiyiḷaṅ koṭiyē 62
   oṉpatu cīrk kaḻineṭilaṭic canta viruttam
   iḷaṅkoṭi yiṇaṅkumutu cūḻal
           neruṅkiya potumparveḷi nīḻal
           itaṇpuṭai yiruntu viḷaiyāṭi nīr
   viḷampiya kuḷirnta moḻi yūṭu
           karaintatu karuṅkaliṉai vīci
           veṟuṅkava ṇeṟintupaya ṉākumō
   vaḷantalai mayaṅkutamiḻ nāṭar
           cekantaṉil mukuntarvaru pērai
           maṭantaiyar vaṇaṅku mapiṣēkamē
   teḷintaco liṉiṅkaḷeṉa nūṟu
           pacuṅkiḷi viḻuntapuṉa mītu
           ceḻuntiṉai viḷaintukarai yēṟumē. 63
   kaḷi - patiṉāṉkucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭic cantat tāḻicai
   karaipaṭait tamaṭaimutu kuṭaippa voru
           kayal paṭait tulavu pērai māl
   karuṇaiyaip pukaḻa varu parappiramar
           kaḷiyarkā ṉaṟiyu naṟavumāy
   varicaiyiṭṭaṉa ḷilacci yākiṉi
           valaicciyait toḻu maṭattuḷē
   matukkuṭantaṉai yeṭuttuvait tataṉai
           vaḷaiya vaittu naṭa māṭuvōm
   viricaṭaik kaṭavuḷ puramerit tatuvum
           viṭamiṭaṟ ṟiṭai ceṟittatum
   vēluṭaik kaṭavuḷ cūraṉaic camaril
           veṉṟatum porutu koṉṟatum
   arimalarp piramaṉaṟutalaik kuṭumi
           yaṟutalaik kuṟai muḷaittatum
   aṉṟu pañcamitaṉ mantirap perumai
           yaṉṟi vēṟu varamallavē. 64
   paṉṉirucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi yāciriya viruttam
   allik kamala maṭipeyara varukiṟ kuvaḷaik
           kaḻuttoṭiya
   vāmpaṟ kuḻuviṉ maṭaluṭaiya valaiyiṟ
           ṟuḷaittu karaiyēṟi

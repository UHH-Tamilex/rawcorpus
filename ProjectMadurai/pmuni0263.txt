1. icittamiḻ

icittaṟ keṉavē iyaṉṟa pākkaḷ
icittamiḻ eṉap peyar iyampap peṟumē.
karuttu : paṇṇōṭum paṇṇuṭaṉ cērnta tāḷattōṭum icittaṟkākavē pāṭippāṭi uruvākkap
peṟṟa pāṭalkaḷ yāmum icittamiḻ eṉṟu collappaṭum.
viḷakkam : tamiḻmoḻi iyaṟṟamiḻ, icittamiḻ, nāṭakattamiḻ eṉa mūvakip pākupāṭukaḷik
koṇṭatu. avaṟṟuḷ:
iyaṟṟamiḻ eṉpatu niṉitta karutti uṇarttuvatiyē nōkkamākak koṇṭu naṭappatu, pēccum,
uriyum ceyyuḷum itil aṭaṅkum. iyaṟṟamiḻc ceyyuḷi iyaṟpā eṉpar. veṇpā,
āciriyappā, vañcippā, maruṭpā ākiyaṉa iyaṟpākkaḷ eṉappaṭum.
icittamiḻ eṉpatu iciyiṉpam aḷittaliyē mutaṉmi nōkkamākak koṇṭu naṭappatu; coṟporuḷ
iṉpaṅkaḷiyum koṭukkak kūṭiyatu. icittamiḻ nūlkaḷ; tēvāram, tiruvācakam, nālāyirap
paṉuvalil (nālāyira tivviya pirapantam) mutalāyiram, periya tirumoḻi, tiruvāymoḻi
ākiyaṉa; tiruppukaḻ mutaliyaṉa.

uruppaṭi mutaliyaṉa. iviyāvum icittaṟkeṉavē iyaṉṟa pākkaḷ. ākavē icittamiḻ eṉṟu
vaḻaṅkappaṭṭaṉa.

2. icippā

karuttu : paṇ eṉṟu collappaṭum irākattōṭu maṭṭum naṭakkum icippākkaḷ eṉṟum paṇṇuṭaṉ
tāḷattōṭu cērntu naṭakkum icippākkaḷ eṉṟum icippākkaḷ iraṇṭu vakippaṭum.
viḷakkam : tāḷamiṉṟip paṇṇuṭaṉ maṭṭum icikkum pāṭalkaḷ icippā eṉappaṭum.
tiruttāṇṭakaṅkaḷ icippā vakiyic cārntavi. avi mā, viḷam vāypāṭukaḷāl āṉavi.
tāṇṭakaṅkaḷ tāḷattuṭaṉ pāṭappaṭutal illi. tāḷamiṉṟi viruttam pāṭutali “cuttāṅkamākap
pāṭutal” eṉpar. avvāṟu pāṭappaṭum tiruttāṇṭakaṅkaḷic cilar ētēṉum tāḷattil aṭakkip
pāṭuvar. eṉiṉum iyalpāyirukkātu.
kāṭṭu :

paṇṇōṭum, pāṇi eṉappaṭum kāla aḷavōṭum (tāḷattōṭum) icikkum pāṭalkaḷ iciyaḷavu pā
eṉappaṭum. ippaṭip pāṭutali “ilayāṅkamākap pāṭutal” eṉpar.

4. cintuppā

karuttu : aḷavotta iraṇṭaṭikaḷāl naṭipeṟum pāṭalkaḷ cintuppā eṉṟu kūṟappaṭum vakiyic
cērumeṉṟu colvārkaḷ.
viḷakkam : icippā, iciyaḷavu pā eṉṟa iruvakiyuḷ, cintuppā iciyaḷavu pā eṉṟu
muṉpu kāṭṭappaṭṭatu (nū.2.uri). yāppilakkaṇaṅkaḷil cīrkaḷ eṉṟu koḷḷappaṭṭavi mā,
viḷam, kāy, kaṉi mutaliya vāypāṭukaḷiṉ aṭippaṭiyil amintavi. āṉāl iciyaḷavu
pākkaḷiṉ pāṟpaṭṭa cintuppākkaḷ tāḷa naṭiyi aṭippaṭiyākak koṇṭaviyākaliṉ appākkaḷ
ovvor aṭiyilum nāṉku cīrkaḷukkuk kuṟiyāmal koṇṭirukkum. yāppilakkaṇa muṟiyil
aminta mikac ciṟiya aṭiyum cintu ilakkaṇa muṟiyil nāṟcīrāka alakiṭappaṭum.
kāṭṭu : neñci luramumiṉṟi
nērmit tiṟamumiṉṟi (yāppilakkaṇa muṟi)

ivviru varikaḷum cērntu ōraṭiyākum. yāppilakkaṇa muṟiyil ivviraṇṭu varikaḷum 4 cīr
aṭiyākum. cintilakkaṇa muṟiyil 8 cīr koṇṭa aṭiyākum. ākavē iraṇṭaṭikaḷ aḷavottum,
ōretuki peṟṟum naṭakkum pāṭalkaḷ cintuppā eṉṟu kūṟappaṭum.

5. cintup peyarkkāraṇam

karuttu : nālaṭip pāṭal eṉṉum aḷaviṉiṉṟu kuṟintatākavum, oṉṟē mukkāl
aṭippāṭalākiya kuṟaṭpāviṉum aḷavil uyarntatākavum irukkum pāṭal iraṇṭaṭip pāṭa;
ataṉāl avviraṇṭaṭip pāṭalic cintuppāṭal eṉṟu peyariṭṭu vaḻaṅkiṉar eṉṟu colluvar
eṉṟaṟika.
viḷakkam : cintuppākkaḷ eḻuttaḷavāl peyar peṟṟiruttal iyalātu. ēṉeṉil eḻuttu
eṇṇikkiyālum, cīreṇṇikkiyālum, aṭikaḷukkup peyariṭuvārkaḷ. eḻuttaḷavu oṉṟiyē
koṇṭu pāvakikkup peyariṭuvatilli.
mūṉṟu cīrkaḷ koṇṭa aṭi cintaṭi eṉappaṭum. appaṭippaṭṭa cintaṭikaḷāl āṉa pāṭal
cintuppā eṉap peyar peṟṟatu* eṉpatum poruntavilli. ēṉeṉil kuṟaḷaṭi uṭiya pāṭali
kuṟaṭpā eṉṟu kūṟuvatilli; aḷavaṭi uṭiya pāṭali aḷavuppā eṉṟum kūṟuvatilli.
* ki.vā. jakannātaṉ, es irāmanātaṉ ākiyōr
mēlum mikak kuṟukiya aṭikaḷi uṭiya kiḷikkaṇṇi kūṭa yāppilakkaṇappaṭi nāṟcīraṭiyāl
amintatākavē uḷḷatu.

eṉṟa aṭiyil 4 cīrkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.

inta noṇṭic cintiṉ aṭiyil taṉiccollic cērkkāviṭṭālum, 6 cīrkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.
(ivviraṇṭu varikaḷiyum iraṇṭu aṭikaḷākak koṇṭu cilar mayaṅkukiṉṟaṉar. ivviraṇṭu
varikaḷum, ōraṭiyē. “añci añcic cāvār” eṉṟu toṭaṅkuvatutāṉ aṭutta aṭi)
tāḷa naṭiyi aṭipaṭiyākak koṇṭu cintuppākkaḷiṉ cīrkaḷ alakiṭappaṭuvatāl, anta alakīṭṭu
muṟippaṭi, cintup pākkaḷil cintaṭiyi kāṇal aritu. eṉavē cintaṭiyāl āṉa pā ātalāl

6. aci

karuttu : cintup pākkaḷil amintirukkiṉṟa cīrkaḷil uḷḷa uyirkkuṟil, uyirneṭil,
meyyōṭu kūṭiya kuṟil, neṭil (uyirmeyk kuṟil, uyirmey neṭil) ākiya
ōvvōruyirum ōraciyākavē koḷḷappaṭum.
viḷakkam : cintu oruvaki icippā. iyaṟpākkaḷ kavi, kaviti, ceyyuḷ eṉṟu kuṟippiṭap
peṟum.
iyaṟpākkaḷ, cantappākkaḷ, vaṇṇappākkaḷ, cintuppākkaḷ, ākiyavaṟṟil oṉṟiṉ ilakkaṇamuṟi
maṟṟavaṟṟiṟkup porunti varavilli.
iyaṟpākkaḷil: kuṟil, kuṟiloṟṟu, neṭil, neṭiloṟṟu (pa, pal, pā, pāl) nēraci
eṉṟum;
kuṟiliṇi, kuṟiliṇioṟṟu, kuṟilneṭil, kuṟilneṭiloṟṟu, (aṇi, aṇil, kaṭā,
kaṭām) niriyaci eṉṟum koḷḷappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.
cantappākkaḷil: kuṟil oru māttiri, kuṟiloṟṟu, neṭil, neṭiloṟṟukaḷ iraṇṭu
māttiri eṉṟa muṟiyil canta māttiri aḷavākak koḷḷappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.
(eḻuttilakkaṇattil kuṟil, kuṟiloṟṟu, neṭil, neṭiloṟṟu muṟiyē oṉṟu, oṉṟari,
iraṇṭu, iraṇṭari māttiri peṟum.)
vaṇṇappākkaḷ: cantak kuḻippukaḷiṉ vaḻi naṭakkiṉṟaṉa.
cintuppākkaḷ: tāḷa aḷaviṉ vaḻi naṭakkiṉṟaṉa.
cintup pāṭalkaḷ tāḷa aṭippaṭiyil amikkappaṭṭu uḷḷatāltāṉ maṟṟa pākkaḷāṉa iyaṟpā,
cantappā, vaṇṇappākkaḷukkāṉa aci, cīr, aṭi, toṭi, māttiri mutaliyaṉa itaṟku
ottuvarāmaṟ pōkiṉṟaṉa.
tāḷa aṭippaṭiyil aminta cintuppāṭalkaḷil uḷḷa acikaḷiyum, cīrkaḷiyum uṇara
vēṇṭumeṉil mutalil tāḷattiyum ataṉ uḷḷuṟuppukaḷiyum uṇara vēṇṭum. tāḷam ēḻu:
avi: turuvam, maṭṭiyam, rūpakam, campi, tirupuṭi, aṭa, ēka eṉpaṉavākum. ivaṟṟilṉ
uḷḷuṟuppukaḷ mūṉṟu; avi: kōl (ilaku) - cuḻi (tirutam) - ariccuḻi
(aṉuttirutam).
kōl 1 eṉṟum, cuḻi 0 eṉṟum, ariccuḻi arivaṭṭa vaṭivilum kuṟikkappeṟum.
cuḻi iraṇṭu eṇṇikkiyuṭiyatu. ariccuḻi or eṇṇṇikki uṭiyatu. iviyiraṇṭum
eṇṇikkiyil māṟātavi. kōl maṭṭum iṉattip poṟuttu eṇṇikki māṟak kūṭiyatu.
kōlum iṉaṅkaḷum (cātikaḷum):
mēṟkuṟitta ēḻu tāḷaṅkaḷukkum iṉaṅkaḷ uṇṭu. avi mummi (ticiram), nāṉmi
(catuciram), aimmi (kaṇṭa), eḻumi (miciram), oṉpāṉmi (caṅkīraṇam) eṉa aintākum.
ivaṟṟiṉ eṇṇikki muṟiyē mūṉṟu, nāṉku, aintu, eḻu, oṉpatu eṉpaṉavākum.
oru tāḷattil uḷḷa kōl inta aintu iṉattukkuriya eṇṇikkikaḷit taṉittaṉiyē peṟumpōtu
attāḷam aintu vakiyākiṟatu. ippaṭiyē ēḻu tāḷaṅkaḷum (7 tāḷam x 5 iṉam)= 35
vakiyākum. eṭuttukkāṭṭākat tiripuṭit tāḷam mummi iṉamāyiṉ ‘mummiyiṉat
tiripuṭi’ (ticira cātit tiripuṭi) eṉṟu peyarpeṟum. ataṉi ‘1300’ eṉṟu
kuṟippārkaḷ. itaṉ motta eṇṇikki (3+2+2) ēḻākum. itē tiripuṭit tāḷam nāṉmi
iṉamāyiṉ nāṉmit tiripuṭi (catucira cātit tiripuṭi) eṉṟu peyarpeṟum. ataṉ
kuṟiyīṭu ‘1400’ ākum. itaṉ motta eṇṇikki (4+2+2) eṭṭākum. ippaṭiyē
muppattintu tāḷattukkum kaṇakkiṭṭuk koḷḷavēṇṭum.
vaṭṭaṇi (āvarttam)
nāṉmi iṉat tiripuṭit tāḷattiṟku naṭimuṟiyil āti tāḷam eṉṟu peyar
vaḻaṅkappaṭukiṟatu. oru tāḷattiṉ muḻunīḷam oru vaṭṭaṇi eṉappaṭum.
tāḷam pōṭum muṟi:
kōl - oru taṭṭut taṭṭi ataṟkuriya eṇṇikki (taṭṭuṭaṉ cērttu) eṇṇa vēṇṭum. cuḻi -
oru taṭṭut taṭṭit tiruppa vēṇṭum. ariccuḻi - oru taṭṭut taṭṭa vēṇṭum.
cintup pāṭalkaḷil perumpālaṉa āti tāḷattil aṭaṅkumāṟu amintuḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟip
pāṭumpōtu aṭiyiṉ muṟpakuti oru kōlilum, 14 aṭuttapakuti iraṇṭu cuḻikaḷilum
‘00’ aṭaṅkukiṉṟaṉa. ippaṭit tāḷattiṉ oru vaṭṭaṇiyilō, pala vaṭṭaṇikaḷilō ōraṭi
aṭaṅkukiṟatu.

(aci nīṭṭaṅkaḷ puḷḷikaḷāl kuṟikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa)
mēṟkāṭṭiya kōliṟkuriya nāṉku eṇṇikkiyil mutal ariyaṭiyum, iru cuḻikaḷukkuriya
nāṉku eṇṇikkiyil aṭutta ariyaṭiyum āka ōr ātitāḷa vaṭṭaṇiyil, itaṉ ōraṭi
muḻuvatum aṭaṅkuvatik kāṇalām. eṭṭām eṇṇikkiyil eḻuttukaḷē illiyeṉṟālum eḻām
iṭattil iṟutiyāka varum aci eṭṭām cīrāka nīṇṭu icikkiṟatu. inta iraṇṭu varikaḷum
cērntu ōraṭi. eṉavē ivvaṭiyil 8 cīrkaḷ uḷḷaṉa. itaṉi eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi eṉalām.
intap pāṭalil ovvōraṭiyum ōr ātitāḷa vaṭṭaṇiyil aṭaṅkukiṟatu.
intap pāṭalil kōṭiṭṭuk kāṭṭiyapaṭi 8 cīrkaḷ uḷḷaṉa. oṟṟu nīkkiya uyirneṭil,
uyirmeyk kuṟil, uyirmey neṭil ākiya patiṉeṭṭu uyireḻuttukaḷ (cintuppāṭalil
uyirum uyirmeyyum uyirākavē koḷḷappaṭum) eṭṭu cīrkaḷāka icikkiṉṟaṉa.
cintuppākkaḷil acikaḷ, iyaṟppākkaḷukkuc collappaṭum nēraci, niriyaci ivaṟṟiṉiṉṟum
vēṟupaṭṭavi. cintuppāviṉ acikaḷ nīḷattil camamāṉavi eṉṟu kūṟalāmēyaṉṟi avaṟṟi
aḷaviṭṭu ittaṉi māttiri eṉṟu kūṟamuṭiyātu.
cintuppāṭalkaḷil uḷḷa cīrkaḷum iyaṟpākkaḷukku amintuḷḷa mā, viḷam, kāy, kaṉi, pū,
niḻal mutaliya cīrkaḷiṉiṉṟum vēṟupaṭṭavi eṉpati uṇara vēṇṭum. cintuppākkaḷit tāḷam
pōṭṭu pāṭumpōtu oru taṭṭil aṭaṅkuvatu oru cīr. antaccīr oru eḻuttālō, pala
eḻuttukaḷālō, oru collālō, pala coṟkaḷālō amintirukkalām. eḻuttuk kuṟivāka uḷḷa

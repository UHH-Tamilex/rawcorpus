1. icaittamiḻ

icaittaṟ keṉavē iyaṉṟa pākkaḷ
icaittamiḻ eṉap peyar iyampap peṟumē.
karuttu : paṇṇōṭum paṇṇuṭaṉ cērnta tāḷattōṭum icaittaṟkākavē pāṭippāṭi uruvākkap
peṟṟa pāṭalkaḷ yāmum icaittamiḻ eṉṟu collappaṭum.
viḷakkam : tamiḻmoḻi iyaṟṟamiḻ, icaittamiḻ, nāṭakattamiḻ eṉa mūvakaip pākupāṭukaḷaik
koṇṭatu. avaṟṟuḷ:
iyaṟṟamiḻ eṉpatu niṉaitta karuttai uṇarttuvataiyē nōkkamākak koṇṭu naṭappatu, pēccum,
uraiyum ceyyuḷum itil aṭaṅkum. iyaṟṟamiḻc ceyyuḷai iyaṟpā eṉpar. veṇpā,
āciriyappā, vañcippā, maruṭpā ākiyaṉa iyaṟpākkaḷ eṉappaṭum.
icaittamiḻ eṉpatu icaiyiṉpam aḷittalaiyē mutaṉmai nōkkamākak koṇṭu naṭappatu; coṟporuḷ
iṉpaṅkaḷaiyum koṭukkak kūṭiyatu. icaittamiḻ nūlkaḷ; tēvāram, tiruvācakam, nālāyirap
paṉuvalil (nālāyira tivviya pirapantam) mutalāyiram, periya tirumoḻi, tiruvāymoḻi
ākiyaṉa; tiruppukaḻ mutaliyaṉa.
icaittamiḻp pāṭalkaḷ: kalippā* paripāṭal* pāviṉaṅkaḷ* vaṇṇappā, cantappā, cintuppā,
uruppaṭi mutaliyaṉa. ivaiyāvum icaittaṟkeṉavē iyaṉṟa pākkaḷ. ākavē icaittamiḻ eṉṟu
vaḻaṅkappaṭṭaṉa.

2. icaippā

karuttu : paṇ eṉṟu collappaṭum irākattōṭu maṭṭum naṭakkum icaippākkaḷ eṉṟum paṇṇuṭaṉ
tāḷattōṭu cērntu naṭakkum icaippākkaḷ eṉṟum icaippākkaḷ iraṇṭu vakaippaṭum.
viḷakkam : tāḷamiṉṟip paṇṇuṭaṉ maṭṭum icaikkum pāṭalkaḷ icaippā eṉappaṭum.
tiruttāṇṭakaṅkaḷ icaippā vakaiyaic cārntavai. avai mā, viḷam vāypāṭukaḷāl āṉavai.
tāṇṭakaṅkaḷ tāḷattuṭaṉ pāṭappaṭutal illai. tāḷamiṉṟi viruttam pāṭutalai “cuttāṅkamākap
pāṭutal” eṉpar. avvāṟu pāṭappaṭum tiruttāṇṭakaṅkaḷaic cilar ētēṉum tāḷattil aṭakkip
pāṭuvar. eṉiṉum iyalpāyirukkātu.
kāṭṭu :

paṇṇōṭum, pāṇi eṉappaṭum kāla aḷavōṭum (tāḷattōṭum) icaikkum pāṭalkaḷ icaiyaḷavu pā
eṉappaṭum. ippaṭip pāṭutalai “ilayāṅkamākap pāṭutal” eṉpar.

4. cintuppā

karuttu : aḷavotta iraṇṭaṭikaḷāl naṭaipeṟum pāṭalkaḷ cintuppā eṉṟu kūṟappaṭum vakaiyaic
cērumeṉṟu colvārkaḷ.
viḷakkam : icaippā, icaiyaḷavu pā eṉṟa iruvakaiyuḷ, cintuppā icaiyaḷavu pā eṉṟu
muṉpu kāṭṭappaṭṭatu (nū.2.urai). yāppilakkaṇaṅkaḷil cīrkaḷ eṉṟu koḷḷappaṭṭavai mā,
viḷam, kāy, kaṉi mutaliya vāypāṭukaḷiṉ aṭippaṭaiyil amaintavai. āṉāl icaiyaḷavu
pākkaḷiṉ pāṟpaṭṭa cintuppākkaḷ tāḷa naṭaiyai aṭippaṭaiyākak koṇṭavaiyākaliṉ appākkaḷ
ovvor aṭiyilum nāṉku cīrkaḷukkuk kuṟaiyāmal koṇṭirukkum. yāppilakkaṇa muṟaiyil
amainta mikac ciṟiya aṭiyum cintu ilakkaṇa muṟaiyil nāṟcīrāka alakiṭappaṭum.
kāṭṭu : neñci luramumiṉṟi
nērmait tiṟamumiṉṟi (yāppilakkaṇa muṟai)

ivviru varikaḷum cērntu ōraṭiyākum. yāppilakkaṇa muṟaiyil ivviraṇṭu varikaḷum 4 cīr
aṭiyākum. cintilakkaṇa muṟaiyil 8 cīr koṇṭa aṭiyākum. ākavē iraṇṭaṭikaḷ aḷavottum,
ōretukai peṟṟum naṭakkum pāṭalkaḷ cintuppā eṉṟu kūṟappaṭum.

5. cintup peyarkkāraṇam

karuttu : nālaṭip pāṭal eṉṉum aḷaviṉiṉṟu kuṟaintatākavum, oṉṟē mukkāl
aṭippāṭalākiya kuṟaṭpāviṉum aḷavil uyarntatākavum irukkum pāṭal iraṇṭaṭip pāṭa;
ataṉāl avviraṇṭaṭip pāṭalaic cintuppāṭal eṉṟu peyariṭṭu vaḻaṅkiṉar eṉṟu colluvar
eṉṟaṟika.
viḷakkam : cintuppākkaḷ eḻuttaḷavāl peyar peṟṟiruttal iyalātu. ēṉeṉil eḻuttu
eṇṇikkaiyālum, cīreṇṇikkaiyālum, aṭikaḷukkup peyariṭuvārkaḷ. eḻuttaḷavu oṉṟaiyē
koṇṭu pāvakaikkup peyariṭuvatillai.
mūṉṟu cīrkaḷ koṇṭa aṭi cintaṭi eṉappaṭum. appaṭippaṭṭa cintaṭikaḷāl āṉa pāṭal
cintuppā eṉap peyar peṟṟatu* eṉpatum poruntavillai. ēṉeṉil kuṟaḷaṭi uṭaiya pāṭalai
kuṟaṭpā eṉṟu kūṟuvatillai; aḷavaṭi uṭaiya pāṭalai aḷavuppā eṉṟum kūṟuvatillai.
* ki.vā. jakannātaṉ, es irāmanātaṉ ākiyōr
mēlum mikak kuṟukiya aṭikaḷai uṭaiya kiḷikkaṇṇi kūṭa yāppilakkaṇappaṭi nāṟcīraṭiyāl
amaintatākavē uḷḷatu.

eṉṟa aṭiyil 4 cīrkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.

inta noṇṭic cintiṉ aṭiyil taṉiccollaic cērkkāviṭṭālum, 6 cīrkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.
(ivviraṇṭu varikaḷaiyum iraṇṭu aṭikaḷākak koṇṭu cilar mayaṅkukiṉṟaṉar. ivviraṇṭu
varikaḷum, ōraṭiyē. “añci añcic cāvār” eṉṟu toṭaṅkuvatutāṉ aṭutta aṭi)
tāḷa naṭaiyai aṭipaṭaiyākak koṇṭu cintuppākkaḷiṉ cīrkaḷ alakiṭappaṭuvatāl, anta alakīṭṭu

kāṭṭu : vīciṉa pamparam ōyvataṉ muṉnāṉ

6. acai

karuttu : cintup pākkaḷil amaintirukkiṉṟa cīrkaḷil uḷḷa uyirkkuṟil, uyirneṭil,
meyyōṭu kūṭiya kuṟil, neṭil (uyirmeyk kuṟil, uyirmey neṭil) ākiya
ōvvōruyirum ōracaiyākavē koḷḷappaṭum.
viḷakkam : cintu oruvakai icaippā. iyaṟpākkaḷ kavi, kavitai, ceyyuḷ eṉṟu kuṟippiṭap
peṟum.
iyaṟpākkaḷ, cantappākkaḷ, vaṇṇappākkaḷ, cintuppākkaḷ, ākiyavaṟṟil oṉṟiṉ ilakkaṇamuṟai
maṟṟavaṟṟiṟkup porunti varavillai.
iyaṟpākkaḷil: kuṟil, kuṟiloṟṟu, neṭil, neṭiloṟṟu (pa, pal, pā, pāl) nēracai
eṉṟum;
kuṟiliṇai, kuṟiliṇaioṟṟu, kuṟilneṭil, kuṟilneṭiloṟṟu, (aṇi, aṇil, kaṭā,
kaṭām) niraiyacai eṉṟum koḷḷappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.
cantappākkaḷil: kuṟil oru māttirai, kuṟiloṟṟu, neṭil, neṭiloṟṟukaḷ iraṇṭu
māttirai eṉṟa muṟaiyil canta māttirai aḷavākak koḷḷappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.
(eḻuttilakkaṇattil kuṟil, kuṟiloṟṟu, neṭil, neṭiloṟṟu muṟaiyē oṉṟu, oṉṟarai,
iraṇṭu, iraṇṭarai māttirai peṟum.)
vaṇṇappākkaḷ: cantak kuḻippukaḷiṉ vaḻi naṭakkiṉṟaṉa.
cintuppākkaḷ: tāḷa aḷaviṉ vaḻi naṭakkiṉṟaṉa.
cintup pāṭalkaḷ tāḷa aṭippaṭaiyil amaikkappaṭṭu uḷḷatāltāṉ maṟṟa pākkaḷāṉa iyaṟpā,
cantappā, vaṇṇappākkaḷukkāṉa acai, cīr, aṭi, toṭai, māttirai mutaliyaṉa itaṟku
ottuvarāmaṟ pōkiṉṟaṉa.
tāḷa aṭippaṭaiyil amainta cintuppāṭalkaḷil uḷḷa acaikaḷaiyum, cīrkaḷaiyum uṇara
vēṇṭumeṉil mutalil tāḷattaiyum ataṉ uḷḷuṟuppukaḷaiyum uṇara vēṇṭum. tāḷam ēḻu:
avai: turuvam, maṭṭiyam, rūpakam, campai, tirupuṭai, aṭa, ēka eṉpaṉavākum. ivaṟṟilṉ
uḷḷuṟuppukaḷ mūṉṟu; avai: kōl (ilaku) - cuḻi (tirutam) - araiccuḻi
(aṉuttirutam).
kōl 1 eṉṟum, cuḻi 0 eṉṟum, araiccuḻi araivaṭṭa vaṭivilum kuṟikkappeṟum.
cuḻi iraṇṭu eṇṇikkaiyuṭaiyatu. araiccuḻi or eṇṇṇikkai uṭaiyatu. ivaiyiraṇṭum
eṇṇikkaiyil māṟātavai. kōl maṭṭum iṉattaip poṟuttu eṇṇikkai māṟak kūṭiyatu.
kōlum iṉaṅkaḷum (cātikaḷum):
mēṟkuṟitta ēḻu tāḷaṅkaḷukkum iṉaṅkaḷ uṇṭu. avai mummai (ticiram), nāṉmai
(catuciram), aimmai (kaṇṭa), eḻumai (miciram), oṉpāṉmai (caṅkīraṇam) eṉa aintākum.
ivaṟṟiṉ eṇṇikkai muṟaiyē mūṉṟu, nāṉku, aintu, eḻu, oṉpatu eṉpaṉavākum.
oru tāḷattil uḷḷa kōl inta aintu iṉattukkuriya eṇṇikkaikaḷait taṉittaṉiyē peṟumpōtu
attāḷam aintu vakaiyākiṟatu. ippaṭiyē ēḻu tāḷaṅkaḷum (7 tāḷam x 5 iṉam)= 35
vakaiyākum. eṭuttukkāṭṭākat tiripuṭait tāḷam mummai iṉamāyiṉ ‘mummaiyiṉat
tiripuṭai’ (ticira cātit tiripuṭai) eṉṟu peyarpeṟum. ataṉai ‘1300’ eṉṟu
kuṟippārkaḷ. itaṉ motta eṇṇikkai (3+2+2) ēḻākum. itē tiripuṭait tāḷam nāṉmai
iṉamāyiṉ nāṉmait tiripuṭai (catucira cātit tiripuṭai) eṉṟu peyarpeṟum. ataṉ
kuṟiyīṭu ‘1400’ ākum. itaṉ motta eṇṇikkai (4+2+2) eṭṭākum. ippaṭiyē
muppattaintu tāḷattukkum kaṇakkiṭṭuk koḷḷavēṇṭum.
vaṭṭaṇai (āvarttam)
nāṉmai iṉat tiripuṭait tāḷattiṟku naṭaimuṟaiyil āti tāḷam eṉṟu peyar
vaḻaṅkappaṭukiṟatu. oru tāḷattiṉ muḻunīḷam oru vaṭṭaṇai eṉappaṭum.
tāḷam pōṭum muṟai:
kōl - oru taṭṭut taṭṭi ataṟkuriya eṇṇikkai (taṭṭuṭaṉ cērttu) eṇṇa vēṇṭum. cuḻi -
oru taṭṭut taṭṭit tiruppa vēṇṭum. araiccuḻi - oru taṭṭut taṭṭa vēṇṭum.
cintup pāṭalkaḷil perumpālaṉa āti tāḷattil aṭaṅkumāṟu amaintuḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟaip
pāṭumpōtu aṭiyiṉ muṟpakuti oru kōlilum, 14 aṭuttapakuti iraṇṭu cuḻikaḷilum
‘00’ aṭaṅkukiṉṟaṉa. ippaṭit tāḷattiṉ oru vaṭṭaṇaiyilō, pala vaṭṭaṇaikaḷilō ōraṭi
aṭaṅkukiṟatu.

(acai nīṭṭaṅkaḷ puḷḷikaḷāl kuṟikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa)
mēṟkāṭṭiya kōliṟkuriya nāṉku eṇṇikkaiyil mutal araiyaṭiyum, iru cuḻikaḷukkuriya
nāṉku eṇṇikkaiyil aṭutta araiyaṭiyum āka ōr ātitāḷa vaṭṭaṇaiyil, itaṉ ōraṭi
muḻuvatum aṭaṅkuvataik kāṇalām. eṭṭām eṇṇikkaiyil eḻuttukaḷē illaiyeṉṟālum eḻām
iṭattil iṟutiyāka varum acai eṭṭām cīrāka nīṇṭu icaikkiṟatu. inta iraṇṭu varikaḷum
cērntu ōraṭi. eṉavē ivvaṭiyil 8 cīrkaḷ uḷḷaṉa. itaṉai eṇcīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi eṉalām.
intap pāṭalil ovvōraṭiyum ōr ātitāḷa vaṭṭaṇaiyil aṭaṅkukiṟatu.
intap pāṭalil kōṭiṭṭuk kāṭṭiyapaṭi 8 cīrkaḷ uḷḷaṉa. oṟṟu nīkkiya uyirneṭil,
uyirmeyk kuṟil, uyirmey neṭil ākiya patiṉeṭṭu uyireḻuttukaḷ (cintuppāṭalil
uyirum uyirmeyyum uyirākavē koḷḷappaṭum) eṭṭu cīrkaḷāka icaikkiṉṟaṉa.
cintuppākkaḷil acaikaḷ, iyaṟppākkaḷukkuc collappaṭum nēracai, niraiyacai ivaṟṟiṉiṉṟum
vēṟupaṭṭavai. cintuppāviṉ acaikaḷ nīḷattil camamāṉavai eṉṟu kūṟalāmēyaṉṟi avaṟṟai
aḷaviṭṭu ittaṉai māttirai eṉṟu kūṟamuṭiyātu.
cintuppāṭalkaḷil uḷḷa cīrkaḷum iyaṟpākkaḷukku amaintuḷḷa mā, viḷam, kāy, kaṉi, pū,

karuttu : taṉiccollai mutalaṭiyiṉ iṟutiyil peṟṟu varum kummippāṭalkaḷum uḷḷaṉa.
taṉiccollē peṟātu amainta kummip pāṭalkaḷum uḷḷaṉa.
viḷakkam : mēl nūṟpāvil peruvaraviṉavāṉa araiyaṭi iṟutiyil taṉiccol peṟum kummikaḷ
kūṟappaṭṭaṉa. iṅku ciṟuvaraviṉavāṉa aṭi iṟutiyil taṉiccol peṟum kummikaḷaiyum,
taṉiccollē peṟāta kummikaḷaiyum kūṟukiṉṟaṉar.
kāṭṭu : taṉicollai mutalaṭiyiṉ iṟutiyil peṟṟuvarum kummi

itil mutalaṭiyiṉ iṟutiyil ‘eṅkaḷ’ eṉṟa taṉiccol vantatu.
kāṭṭu : taṉiccol illāmal vanta kummi

ikkummip pāṭalil taṉiccol illai.

40. oyiṟkummi

karuttu : viraivu mummaiyil naṭakkum; muṭukiyal aṭikaḷ tāṅkiyirukkum; etukait
toṭaiyum, iyaiput toṭaiyum iṭaiyiṭaiyē ceṟintirukkum; pāṭaliṉ oli
ciṟantirukkum; ivvāṟāṉa amaippil uḷḷatu oyiṟkummiyākum.
kāṭṭu : oyiṟkummi (viraivu mummai)

itil te-tē-ci-ti-ti eṉṟa mōṉaiyum, cikara-makara-tikaḻu eṉṟa etukaiyum iṭaiyiṭai
miṭaintu vantuḷḷataiyum, itaṉ piṟpakutiyil ‘takatattimi’ eṉṉum cantamuṭaiya nāṉku
muṭukiyaṟ cīrkaḷ vantuḷḷataiyum uṇaralām. itil iyaipu illai.
5 catucira iṉa ēka tāḷavaṭṭaṇaiyil aṭaṅkum 20 cīraṭi peṟṟu varum oyiṟkummiyum uṇṭu.
avai nāṉkacait taṉiccoṟkaḷaiyum iṭaiyē peṟṟu varum.
kāṭṭu : oyiṟkummi (catucira iṉa tāḷam)

itil paḻukkum, irukkum, vaḻukkum eṉṟa iyaipum, ‘makiḻntuvāy’ eṉṟa nālacait
taṉicollum, ‘malarnta’ eṉṟa mūvacait taṉiccollum vantuḷḷataik kāṇalām. eṉavē
oyiṟkummi; viraivu naṭaiyatu; muṭukiyal aṭikaḷaik koṇṭatu; etukaiyum, iyaipum
iṭaiyiṭaiyē ceṟintatu; oli ciṟantatu eṉpatu pōtarum.

piṅkalamuṉivar ceyta
piṅkalantiyeṉṉum "piṅkala nikaṇṭu"
iḵtu iṉspekṭiṅ iskūl mēṣṭar peṉcaṉar
vīrāṭcimaṅkalam tamiḻppulavar ti. civaṉpiḷḷi
pala piratirūpaṅkaḷikkoṇṭu paricōtittu iyaṟṟiya uriyōṭum,
kavarṉarmeṇṭu nārmal pāṭacālit tamiḻppulavarāyirunta
cōṭacāvatāṉam ti-ka. cupparāyaceṭṭiyār muṉṉiliyilum paricōtittu,
mēṟpaṭi civaṉpiḷḷiyāl kāñci-nākaliṅka mutaliyāratu
ceṉṉi: intu tiyalājikal yantiracāliyil patippikkappaṭṭatu.
vikurti varuṭam āvaṇi mātam

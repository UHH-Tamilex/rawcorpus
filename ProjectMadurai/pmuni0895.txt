kulacēkarappaṭṭiṉam teyvacikāmaṇik kavirāyar iyaṟṟiya aṟamvaḷartta nāyaki piḷḷit tamiḻ

Source:
kulacēkarappaṭṭiṉam teyvacikāmaṇik kavirāyar iyaṟṟiya
tūttukkuṭi māvaṭṭam, kulacēkarappaṭṭiṉam
aṟamvaḷartta nāyaki piḷḷit tamiḻ
patippittōṉ: kampapātacēkaraṉ, ātīṉa camayap parappuṉar, nelli .
kulacēkarappaṭṭiṉam, pututteru, pāmpē piḷḷi vīṭu,
teyvattiru. vī. āṟumukanayiṉār piḷḷi - teyvattiru. ā.ulakammāḷ
tampatiyar kuṭumpattāriṉ pērutaviyuṭaṉ
viḷinalam patippikkat tuṭaṅkiya 46 - ām āṇṭu malar
piḷḷit tamiḻ kaḷañciyam - 5
kampaṉ ilakkiyap paṇṇi
āṉantavalli ammaṉ kōyil teru, (mīṉāṭciyammaṉ kōyil arukil)
āralvāymoḻi - 629301. vaṭakkūr. kumari māvaṭṭam.

---------------
kulaci teyvacikāmaṇik kavirāyar varalāṟṟuk kuṟippu
ivar tūttukkuṭi māvaṭṭam kulacēkarap paṭṭiṉattil vēḷāḷar kulattil tōṉṟiṉār.
ippakuti kuṟunila maṉṉar centil kāttamūppaṉārukku tōḻarākavum, pulavarākavum
viḷaṅkiṉār.
orucamayam ayalnāṭṭu pulavaṉ vātiṭa vantāṉ. avaṉi pulavār aṟam vaḷartta nāyakiṉ
tirunīṟṟiṉi oru ciṟuvaṉukku aḷittu, avaṉiyē vātāṭac ceytu veṉṟār. tamakku varum
poruṭkaḷi ellām anta ciṟuvaṉukkum ataṉ piṉ avaṉvaḻi marapiṉarukkum peṟumpaṭi
tiṭṭaṅkaḷi vittār. atu iṉṟu vari naṭipeṟṟu varukiṟatu. ippulavar ivvūrukku teṟkē
uḷḷa citampara īcurar tirukkōyil kaṭṭappaṭṭu vaḻipāṭu viḻākkaḷukkāṉa vaḻikaḷum
ceyvittār. ivarip paṟṟiya muḻu varalāṟukaḷ kiṭikkavilli.
ōm

1. kāppup paruvam

tirumāl
nīrkoṇṭa taṇṭuḻāyt tirumāli mālayaṉ
      nilāmāli mālivaṇṇa
nittaṉmuta lettaṉi uyirttokuti yattaṉiyu
      nīpeṟṟatā yeṉṟucoṟ
cīrkoṇṭa nāṉmaṟi parāvumpañ cāṭcarac
      celviyit teṉkulaciyūrc
civakāma cuntariyi yaṟamvaḷartta vaḷimaka
      tēviyik kākkaparavip
pārkoṇṭa ciṟṟaṭiccu vaṭukava ṭillāta
      pattarcittat tiruttip
paṭippuyir aṉittumōr tiṉittuṇip poḻutiṉum
      paḻatilāmal parittut
tārkoṇṭa kuḻaliru varūṭā manīṭūḻi
      tāṉvīṟṟiruk kumamutat
taṇkalic cuṭarvā ṉiloṇ katirc cuṭarvīcu
      caṅkucakkara pāṇiyē!       1
-------------------------------
civapirāṉ
vēṟu
karuvimukil tavaḻporup pivalitiṟ peyarttunīḷ
      kaṭalakaṭu kiḻiyanaṭṭu muṟiyiṟ tiruppavē
kaṭavuḷarki viṭavutitta kaṭuvit taṭakkiyē
      kaṟimiṭa ṟatuṭiya karttareṉu maṟputattiṉār
tiripuramuma taṉu(m)muṟṟu moḻiyakkaṇat tilēciṉavu
      tirunakinu taṟkaṇṇiṉar pattarat taṉār
tikaḻkayili maliyiruppar porukik kaṇicciyār
      tiripataki yavaḷmaṇatta civaṉittu tippamyām
naramiruka vaṭiveṭut tiraṇiyaṉik kuṟittamāl
      naḷiṉamuta levarkaḷukku mutaṉmic ciṟappiṉāl
naliyumaṭiyavar niṉikku maḷavilcelac ceyvāṉ
      naṉavukaṉaviṉum pattaritayat tiṉuṭpaṭāḷ
kurumaṇikaṇ marimalarttu veyilot teṟikkavē
      kuṭavaḷiku vaḷimalart tunilavip parappavē
kulavupaka liravoḻitta vulakattu yarccicēr
      kulacinaka raṟamvaḷartta koṭiyip purakkavē!       2
--------------------------------
vināyakar
vēṟu
neṭiyamati ḷilpoṉmaṇ ṭapanilipeṟ ṟirukkumvāṉ
      eḻupiṟimarup poṭintuki nilipeṟṟa veṟpaṉāṉ
muṭivilaṭi yarkkunaṉka ruḷmuḷarippa tattiṉāṉ
      mutumaṟi vaḻuttukuñ caramukaṉit tutippanīḷ
kuṭilarcaṭi lattarnañ ciṭukuvaḷik kaḻuttiṉār
      kulacivaru kaccikoṇṭa varkuḻiyac ceymikkaṇāḷ
taṭavari kaḷcuṟṟumam puvitarumap perukkiṉāḷ
      tamiḻaṟam vaḷarttacun taritamiḻip purakkavē!       3
--------------------------------------
murukaṉ
vēṟu
aṭalacurar paṭamakiḻcci yaṇṭaruk kākkiṭa
      amarceya yilkiyile ṭuttuvanta cattārttaṉi
yamalaṉieṉ uḷamirukkum aṉpaṉit tōṭṭuṟu
      kaṭalviṭamatu veṉavutitta kañcaṉit tēyttiṭu
karuṇimukiṉ marukaṉip pirapantaṉik kūttuṟu
      kalapamayil taṉinaṭattu kantaṉip pōṟṟutum
vaṭavarivil liṉarmaṇatta maṉṟaluk kēṟṟavaḷ
      vaḻipeṟumey yaruḷviciṭ ṭariṉpuṟap pārttavaḷ
maṉatilaṭiya varniṉikku maṉpeṉap pūttavaḷ
      kuṭavaḷiveṇ matimaṇalcey kuṉṟiṉiṟ kōṭṭuṟu
kuḷirtaraḷa muṭunikarppa tuṇṭeṉak kāṭṭiya
      kulacinaka raṟamvaḷartta kompiṉik kākkavē!       4
--------------------------
ilakkumi
vēṟu
kaṭalik kaviyiṉaṭit taṭitta
kallaṇiyi laṭalpaṭi naṭattiyē
karapattiru tacamukattaṉ makkaḷoṭu
aḻiyavoḻit tamararuk kunīḷ
kavalittuyar kaḷakaṟṟi naṟkuṇa
vipīcaṇa ṉukkaraci yaṟṟimā
vaṭaveṟ peṉattaṉuveṭutta lakkumaṇaṉu(m)
mitilip poṉumvarat tamūr
makiḻattaya viṉarvarac ceya taṟputa
maṇimuṭi vittula kaḷitta māl
marumattuṟa virukku milakkumi
vaṉacamalarp patam vaḻuttuvām
naṭaṉakkaḷi mayiṉaṭattu cuppira
maṇiyaṉi okkali yiruttiyē
nakilatteḻu vamutuḷattu meypporuḷ
viḷiyavu ṇappaṇum viruppiṉāl
nakiyiṟ ṟiripura meritta mukkaṇaṉ
mutaliṉar muttoḻil muḷittavōr
koṭiyiṟ kuḻiyu(m) nucuppi ḷittiṭa
vaḷarumi ṇikkaṉa taṉattiṉāḷ
kuravittoṉi kuṭavaḷik kiḷittoṉi
maṇimuracat toṉima kiḻccicēr
kulacip patiyaṟam vaḷartta poṟkoṭi
yiṉimi yicittamiḻ taḻikkavē! 5
--------------------------------
vīramaṉōkari
vēṟu
muttali vēṟpaṭi nīlikalātari
      muritirik kaṭalvaḷi nilattoṭu
muccakamēṟ koṭitā mayiṭācuraṉ
      muṭiyiṉiṟ caraṇuṟa mitittavaḷ
mukkaṇi nāṟpuyaṉ māṉatapūraṇi
      muṉimuṟuk kuḷapiṟi yeyiṟṟiṉa
ḷuttiyiṉāṟ palavākama meyyaṟi
      vuṭiyavitta karuḷami ruppava
ḷottanilāc caṭiyārtami yāṭalce
      yurimimuṟ ṟiyaperumi peṟṟavaḷ
ḷuttaravāyc celvi vīramaṉōkari
      yupayapoṟpata malarvaḻut tutum
nittiyavāḻk kiyarkāra ṇakāriyar
      nimaliyaṟputa kamala lakkumi
niccayavākki ṉāḷnāṉmaṟi nūṉmuṟi
      niṟuvumeyppo ruḷaruḷi cikkuyil
ṉeṭṭuḷa mākkoṭi yūrtiyatāmeṉi
      ṉiliniṟut tayilaṟumu kaṟkaṉi
yuttamilāc coliṉōr ceyumāṇava
      maṟamveṟut tavaḷpuvaṉa raṭcaki
yaṟpartaṉ nāṭṭamaṭāta vaḷpēruṇmi
      aṭiyaruṭ ṭaṉikuṭi iruppavaḷ
ḷattiravāṭ kaṇivāṇa kivāṇuta
      laṟamvaḷartta vaḷtamiḻ taḻikkavē! 6
-----------------------------------
piravar
vēṟu
urumuk kuralpaṭu tuṭivit torukara
      moḷirakkaṉal viḻiyūkkiyē
yuṟumut taliyaṭu kaḻumuṭ paṭiyoru
      kiyiṉiṟṟiriya vulāttiyē
utayak katireṉa maṇineṭ ṭaravaṇi
      orukittalamici cērttiyē
mariyiṟ kuṭipuku vitikaṭ ṭalimali
      vaḷarkittala metirkāṭṭiyē
malicuṟ ṟulakiṉil ñamalip parimici
      varumukkirapuya vīṭṭiṉāṉ
vaṭiviṟ kariyava ṉaṭiyaṟ kuyireṉa
      vaṭukakkaṭavuḷi vāḻttuvā
maravap pakirati cuḻalac caṭiyuṭi
      yaviḻapparipura mārkkavē
yaṇukic civaciva veṉumuttar kaḷviṉi
      yakalappata metirtūkkiyē
yaṇipoṟ capimuḻa vatirac cakatala
      matirappavarita kūttaṉār
kurukiṟ polikara malartoṭ ṭaṇiciṟu
      kutalikkiḷiyeṉi āṭkoṭāy
kuṇaviṟ puriyiru puruvappe ṭimayil
      kuyilmuttamiḻici vēṭkiyāṉ
kulacip patiyumi yavaḷmup paṭitaru
      koṭiyippari voṭukākkavē! 7
-------------------------------------------
aiyaṉār
vēṟu
puḷḷaraciṉ mēṟkoṇṭa pūvaraci koṇkaṉum
      poṉṉaracu nāṭukākkum
purukūtaṉ mutalamara ramutuṇṇa viṭamuṇṭa
      puṇṇiyaṉu mutavucutaṉi
vaḷḷamuli yiruvaruk korukoḻu naṉiccāta
      vākaṉaṉi yayirāṇitaṉ
marapukātta vaṉittamiḻk kulaci nakarvāḻa
      vantavayya ṉittutippām
kaḷḷaviḻ malarppoḻi leḻiṟcimaya vimayamēṟ
      kippiṭittiṭu kaṇavarpoṟ
kālpiṭit tammimici vittarun tatiyaṉṟu
      kāṭṭavetir kaṇṭavavaḷit
taḷḷariya kaṟpunili peṟṟu malivaṟṟu
      muccakam elām pōṟṟamuṟṟun
tāpitta makatēvi yammiyumi yaṟamvaḷarttava
      ḷicittamiḻ taḻikkavē! 8
---------------------------------
nāmakaḷ
cittamum puttiyu(m) maṉamumāṅ kāramuñ
      ceyalumuri yumporuntit
tiripuvaṉa vuyirtoṟu miruntu viḷiyāṭiyun
      teriyāta ciṉmayattik
kottalark kontaḷac cinturat tirunutaṟ
      kuṟunakip peṇṇaraciyik
kulacēkarap paṭṭiṉat taṟamvaḷart tapoṟ
      koṭiyippu rakkaviciyuñ
cattamuñ cakalakali yattamuñ cāvittiri
      kāyattiri mantiramumuc
caṅkatta miḻppaṉuva lāṭṭiyeṉu nāmamuñ
      caṭcamaya muḻutu meḻutum
vittakam virittut tarittakara mumpaṭika
      meyyumey yumpaṭitta
mellarum palarvaṭṭa veḷḷimaṇ ṭapamotta
      veḷḷiveṇ kamalattaḷē! 9
----------------------------------------------
intiraṉ
veṇṭirit tamarakkaruṅ kaṭalcey tuvariṉ
      viḻutupaṭ ṭoḷipaḻuttu
miṉṉupiṉ ṉaṟkuṭila mōlippa rāṉanta
      veḷḷattiṉuḷ ḷattiṉmēṟ
koṇṭiruk kumporuḷi varapūta rattik
      kuṇakkaṭali vētamuṭivik
kulacēkarap paṭṭiṉat taṟamvaḷart tapoṟ
      koṭiyip purakkavēli
maṇṭalat tuṟucikara varikaḷ mēṟkoṇ ṭeḻāmal
      ciṟakaṟut tiruttu
vaccirat tāṟporutu virutukoṇ ṭetiriṭṭu
      vantavari veṉṟaṭakki
yaṇṭaruk kuminti rāṇikku nālukōṭ
      ṭayirāpatat tukkunī
ṭintutaru vukkumiṉ ṉamutukkum vēntāṉa
      vākaṇṭalak kaṭavuḷē! 10
----------------------------------------
muppattu mūvar - piramaṉ
ātittar paṉṉiruvar kāḷakaṇṭat tiṟiva
      rintāṟu pērvacukkaḷ
ḷāṉāta veṇma reḻumēṉā ḷilaṉ
      ṟavatarittavac cuṉiyiruvarum
pōtiṟpo runtiyira ṇiyakaṟpa muñceytu
      puvaṉappaṭip puyirkaṭam
puṇṇiya pāvattiṉ vaḻiyoḻuka veḻutiyavitip
      puttēḷiruṅ kākkapoṟ

      peṭuttukkuṉit tuccirittut

      cōticem pātiyuṭalu
mātikkac cakamaṉittu niṉṟoḷir pacci
      marakatac cuṭarparappi
vantukuṭi koṇṭa makatēvi cuntari
      yaṟamvaḷartta tēviyimuḻutumē! 11

2. ceṅkīrip paruvam

vimpac civantavā yammi mēṉātēvi
      veṟparaci kaṟparacipoṉ
mēkali yotukkimaṭi mītuṟavirut timuli
      vimmivaḻiyum pāṉmutaṟ
kampikkum vēlitta rippeytu peyta
      caṅkikkoṇṭu pālpukaṭṭik
kiccaṅki mummuṟi cirañcuṟṟi yorumuṟi
      kaviḻttiyuṭa laṅkulukkik
kumpakka ḷiṟṟerut tattiṟṟi ruttamoṭu
      koṇarunī rāṭṭimañcaṭ
kuḷirnilak kāppiṭṭu neypotti vaṭṭak
      kutampiyiru kuḻiyiliṭṭuc
cempoṟ pacuntoṭṭil vittuvit tāṭṭumayil
      ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē!
cīrivaḷa nāṭṭutamiḻ vīrivaḷa nāṭṭaraci
      ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē! 12

      pararkkēṟṟen nāḷumvāḻuñ
cīrivaḷa nāṭṭutamiḻ vīrivaḷa nāṭṭaraci
      ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē!

      ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē! 14
--------------------------------------

      loḷirumveḷi mukaṭumūṭi
yukantoṟu mukantiṭum piraḷayac calatikaḷum
      putukkum puvaṉamē
cuṟṟiluṅ kaleṉava ṭukkivit taṇṭac

      yattoṉmiyiṟ cūḻaniṟuvip

      peyarkuṟi paṭāmalūṭṭip
pittar tiriyampakak kattarkku miṉpap
      perukkamu taḷittaḷittuc
ciṟṟilviḷi yāṭumvaḷi yāṭukip peṇpiḷḷi
      ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē!

      ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē! 15

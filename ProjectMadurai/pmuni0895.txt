kulacēkarappaṭṭiṉam teyvacikāmaṇik kavirāyar iyaṟṟiya aṟamvaḷartta nāyaki piḷḷait tamiḻ

Source:

tūttukkuṭi māvaṭṭam, kulacēkarappaṭṭiṉam
aṟamvaḷartta nāyaki piḷḷait tamiḻ
patippittōṉ: kampapātacēkaraṉ, ātīṉa camayap parappuṉar, nellai .
kulacēkarappaṭṭiṉam, pututteru, pāmpē piḷḷai vīṭu,
teyvattiru. vī. āṟumukanayiṉār piḷḷai - teyvattiru. ā.ulakammāḷ

kulacai teyvacikāmaṇik kavirāyar varalāṟṟuk kuṟippu
ivar tūttukkuṭi māvaṭṭam kulacēkarap paṭṭiṉattil vēḷāḷar kulattil tōṉṟiṉār.
ippakuti kuṟunila maṉṉar centil kāttamūppaṉārukku tōḻarākavum, pulavarākavum
viḷaṅkiṉār.
orucamayam ayalnāṭṭu pulavaṉ vātiṭa vantāṉ. avaṉai pulavār aṟam vaḷartta nāyakiṉ
tirunīṟṟiṉai oru ciṟuvaṉukku aḷittu, avaṉaiyē vātāṭac ceytu veṉṟār. tamakku varum
poruṭkaḷai ellām anta ciṟuvaṉukkum ataṉ piṉ avaṉvaḻi marapiṉarukkum peṟumpaṭi
tiṭṭaṅkaḷai vaittār. atu iṉṟu varai naṭaipeṟṟu varukiṟatu. ippulavar ivvūrukku teṟkē
uḷḷa citampara īcurar tirukkōyil kaṭṭappaṭṭu vaḻipāṭu viḻākkaḷukkāṉa vaḻikaḷum
ceyvittār. ivaraip paṟṟiya muḻu varalāṟukaḷ kiṭaikkavillai.
ōm

1. kāppup paruvam

tirumāl
nīrkoṇṭa taṇṭuḻāyt tirumālai mālayaṉ
      nilāmālai mālaivaṇṇa
nittaṉmuta lettaṉai uyirttokuti yattaṉaiyu
      nīpeṟṟatā yeṉṟucoṟ
cīrkoṇṭa nāṉmaṟai parāvumpañ cāṭcarac
      celviyait teṉkulacaiyūrc
civakāma cuntariyai yaṟamvaḷartta vaḷaimaka
      tēviyaik kākkaparavaip
pārkoṇṭa ciṟṟaṭiccu vaṭukava ṭillāta
      pattarcittat tiruttip
paṭaippuyir aṉaittumōr tiṉaittuṇaip poḻutiṉum
      paḻatilāmal parittut
tārkoṇṭa kuḻaliru varūṭā manīṭūḻi
      tāṉvīṟṟiruk kumamutat
taṇkalaic cuṭarvā ṉiloṇ katirc cuṭarvīcu
      caṅkucakkara pāṇiyē!       1
-------------------------------
civapirāṉ
vēṟu
karuvimukil tavaḻporup paivalitiṟ peyarttunīḷ
      kaṭalakaṭu kiḻiyanaṭṭu muṟaiyiṟ tiruppavē
kaṭavuḷarkai viṭavutitta kaṭuvait taṭakkiyē
      kaṟaimiṭa ṟatuṭaiya karttareṉu maṟputattiṉār
tiripuramuma taṉu(m)muṟṟu moḻiyakkaṇat tilēciṉavu
      tirunakainu taṟkaṇṇiṉar pattarat taṉār
tikaḻkayilai malaiyiruppar porukaik kaṇicciyār
      tiripatakai yavaḷmaṇatta civaṉaittu tippamyām
naramiruka vaṭiveṭut tiraṇiyaṉaik kuṟaittamāl
      naḷiṉamuta levarkaḷukku mutaṉmaic ciṟappiṉāl
naliyumaṭiyavar niṉaikku maḷavilcelac ceyvāṉ
      naṉavukaṉaviṉum pattaritayat tiṉuṭpaṭāḷ
kurumaṇikaṇ maraimalarttu veyilot teṟikkavē
      kuṭavaḷaiku vaḷaimalart tunilavaip parappavē
kulavupaka liravoḻitta vulakattu yarccicēr
      kulacainaka raṟamvaḷartta koṭiyaip purakkavē!       2
--------------------------------
vināyakar
vēṟu
neṭiyamati ḷilpoṉmaṇ ṭapanilaipeṟ ṟirukkumvāṉ
      eḻupiṟaimarup poṭaintukai nilaipeṟṟa veṟpaṉāṉ
muṭivilaṭi yarkkunaṉka ruḷmuḷarippa tattiṉāṉ
      mutumaṟai vaḻuttukuñ caramukaṉait tutippanīḷ
kuṭilarcaṭi lattarnañ ciṭukuvaḷaik kaḻuttiṉār
      kulacaivaru kaccikoṇṭa varkuḻaiyac ceymaikkaṇāḷ
taṭavarai kaḷcuṟṟumam puvitarumap perukkiṉāḷ
      tamiḻaṟam vaḷarttacun taritamiḻaip purakkavē!       3
--------------------------------------
murukaṉ
vēṟu
aṭalacurar paṭamakiḻcci yaṇṭaruk kākkiṭa
      amarceya yilkaiyile ṭuttuvanta cattārttaṉai
yamalaṉaieṉ uḷamirukkum aṉpaṉait tōṭṭuṟu
      kaṭalviṭamatu veṉavutitta kañcaṉait tēyttiṭu
karuṇaimukiṉ marukaṉaip pirapantaṉaik kūttuṟu
      kalapamayil taṉainaṭattu kantaṉaip pōṟṟutum
vaṭavaraivil liṉarmaṇatta maṉṟaluk kēṟṟavaḷ
      vaḻipeṟumey yaruḷviciṭ ṭariṉpuṟap pārttavaḷ
maṉatilaṭiya varniṉaikku maṉpeṉap pūttavaḷ
      kuṭavaḷaiveṇ matimaṇalcey kuṉṟiṉiṟ kōṭṭuṟu
kuḷirtaraḷa muṭunikarppa tuṇṭeṉak kāṭṭiya
      kulacainaka raṟamvaḷartta kompiṉaik kākkavē!       4
--------------------------
ilakkumi
vēṟu
kaṭalaik kaviyiṉaṭait taṭaitta
kallaṇaiyi laṭalpaṭai naṭattiyē
karapattiru tacamukattaṉ makkaḷoṭu
aḻiyavoḻit tamararuk kunīḷ
kavalaittuyar kaḷakaṟṟi naṟkuṇa
vipīcaṇa ṉukkaraci yaṟṟimā
vaṭaveṟ peṉattaṉuveṭutta lakkumaṇaṉu(m)
mitilaip poṉumvarat tamūr
makiḻattaya viṉarvarac ceya taṟputa
maṇimuṭi vaittula kaḷitta māl
marumattuṟa virukku milakkumi
vaṉacamalarp patam vaḻuttuvām
naṭaṉakkaḷi mayiṉaṭattu cuppira
maṇiyaṉai okkalai yiruttiyē
nakilatteḻu vamutuḷattu meypporuḷ
viḷaiyavu ṇappaṇum viruppiṉāl
nakaiyiṟ ṟiripura meritta mukkaṇaṉ
mutaliṉar muttoḻil muḷaittavōr
koṭiyiṟ kuḻaiyu(m) nucuppi ḷaittiṭa
vaḷarumi ṇaikkaṉa taṉattiṉāḷ
kuravaittoṉi kuṭavaḷaik kiḷaittoṉi
maṇimuracat toṉima kiḻccicēr
kulacaip patiyaṟam vaḷartta poṟkoṭi
yiṉimai yicaittamiḻ taḻaikkavē! 5
--------------------------------
vīramaṉōkari
vēṟu
muttalai vēṟpaṭai nīlikalātari
      muritiraik kaṭalvaḷai nilattoṭu
muccakamēṟ koṭitā mayiṭācuraṉ
      muṭiyiṉiṟ caraṇuṟa mitittavaḷ
mukkaṇi nāṟpuyaṉ māṉatapūraṇi
      muṉaimuṟuk kuḷapiṟai yeyiṟṟiṉa
ḷuttiyiṉāṟ palavākama meyyaṟi
      vuṭaiyavitta karuḷami ruppava
ḷottanilāc caṭaiyārtamai yāṭalce
      yurimaimuṟ ṟiyaperumai peṟṟavaḷ
ḷuttaravāyc celvi vīramaṉōkari
      yupayapoṟpata malarvaḻut tutum
nittiyavāḻk kaiyarkāra ṇakāriyar
      nimalaiyaṟputa kamala lakkumi
niccayavākki ṉāḷnāṉmaṟai nūṉmuṟai
      niṟuvumeyppo ruḷaruḷi caikkuyil
ṉeṭṭuḷa mākkoṭi yūrtiyatāmeṉi
      ṉilainiṟut tayilaṟumu kaṟkaṉai
yuttamilāc coliṉōr ceyumāṇava
      maṟamveṟut tavaḷpuvaṉa raṭcaki
yaṟpartaṉ nāṭṭamaṭāta vaḷpēruṇmai
      aṭiyaruṭ ṭaṉikuṭi iruppavaḷ
ḷattiravāṭ kaṇivāṇa kaivāṇuta
      laṟamvaḷartta vaḷtamiḻ taḻaikkavē! 6
-----------------------------------
pairavar
vēṟu
urumuk kuralpaṭu tuṭivait torukara
      moḷirakkaṉal viḻiyūkkiyē
yuṟumut talaiyaṭu kaḻumuṭ paṭaiyoru
      kaiyiṉiṟṟiriya vulāttiyē
utayak katireṉa maṇineṭ ṭaravaṇi
      orukaittalamicai cērttiyē
maraiyiṟ kuṭipuku vitikaṭ ṭalaimalai
      vaḷarkaittala metirkāṭṭiyē
malaicuṟ ṟulakiṉil ñamalip parimicai
      varumukkirapuya vīṭṭiṉāṉ
vaṭiviṟ kariyava ṉaṭiyaṟ kuyireṉa
      vaṭukakkaṭavuḷai vāḻttuvā
maravap pakirati cuḻalac caṭaiyuṭai
      yaviḻapparipura mārkkavē
yaṇukic civaciva veṉumuttar kaḷviṉai
      yakalappata metirtūkkiyē
yaṇipoṟ capaimuḻa vatirac cakatala
      matirappavarita kūttaṉār
kurukiṟ polikara malartoṭ ṭaṇaiciṟu
      kutalaikkiḷiyeṉai āṭkoṭāy
kuṇaviṟ puraiyiru puruvappe ṭaimayil
      kuyilmuttamiḻicai vēṭkaiyāṉ
kulacaip patiyumai yavaḷmup paṭitaru
      koṭiyaippari voṭukākkavē! 7
-------------------------------------------
aiyaṉār
vēṟu
puḷḷaraciṉ mēṟkoṇṭa pūvaraci koṇkaṉum
      poṉṉaracu nāṭukākkum
purukūtaṉ mutalamara ramutuṇṇa viṭamuṇṭa
      puṇṇiyaṉu mutavucutaṉai
vaḷḷamulai yiruvaruk korukoḻu naṉaiccāta
      vākaṉaṉai yayirāṇitaṉ
marapukātta vaṉaittamiḻk kulacai nakarvāḻa
      vantavayya ṉaittutippām
kaḷḷaviḻ malarppoḻi leḻiṟcimaya vimayamēṟ
      kaippiṭittiṭu kaṇavarpoṟ
kālpiṭit tammimicai vaittarun tatiyaṉṟu
      kāṭṭavetir kaṇṭavavaḷait
taḷḷariya kaṟpunilai peṟṟu malaivaṟṟu
      muccakam elām pōṟṟamuṟṟun
tāpitta makatēvi yammaiyumai yaṟamvaḷarttava
      ḷicaittamiḻ taḻaikkavē! 8
---------------------------------
nāmakaḷ
cittamum puttiyu(m) maṉamumāṅ kāramuñ
      ceyalumurai yumporuntit
tiripuvaṉa vuyirtoṟu miruntu viḷaiyāṭiyun
      teriyāta ciṉmayattaik
kottalark kontaḷac cinturat tirunutaṟ
      kuṟunakaip peṇṇaraciyaik
kulacēkarap paṭṭiṉat taṟamvaḷart tapoṟ
      koṭiyaippu rakkavicaiyuñ
cattamuñ cakalakalai yattamuñ cāvittiri
      kāyattiri mantiramumuc
caṅkatta miḻppaṉuva lāṭṭiyeṉu nāmamuñ
      caṭcamaya muḻutu meḻutum
vittakam virittut tarittakara mumpaṭika
      meyyumey yumpaṭaitta
mellarum palarvaṭṭa veḷḷimaṇ ṭapamotta
      veḷḷaiveṇ kamalattaḷē! 9
----------------------------------------------
intiraṉ
veṇṭirait tamarakkaruṅ kaṭalcey tuvariṉ
      viḻutupaṭ ṭoḷipaḻuttu
miṉṉupiṉ ṉaṟkuṭila mōlippa rāṉanta
      veḷḷattiṉuḷ ḷattiṉmēṟ
koṇṭiruk kumporuḷai varapūta rattaik
      kuṇakkaṭalai vētamuṭivaik
kulacēkarap paṭṭiṉat taṟamvaḷart tapoṟ
      koṭiyaip purakkavēlai
maṇṭalat tuṟucikara varaikaḷ mēṟkoṇ ṭeḻāmal
      ciṟakaṟut tiruttu
vaccirat tāṟporutu virutukoṇ ṭetiriṭṭu
      vantavarai veṉṟaṭakki
yaṇṭaruk kuminti rāṇikku nālukōṭ
      ṭayirāpatat tukkunī
ṭaintutaru vukkumiṉ ṉamutukkum vēntāṉa
      vākaṇṭalak kaṭavuḷē! 10
----------------------------------------
muppattu mūvar - piramaṉ
ātittar paṉṉiruvar kāḷakaṇṭat tiṟaiva
      raintāṟu pērvacukkaḷ
ḷāṉāta veṇma reḻumēṉā ḷilaṉ
      ṟavatarittavac cuṉiyiruvarum
pōtiṟpo runtiyira ṇiyakaṟpa muñceytu
      puvaṉappaṭaip puyirkaṭam
puṇṇiya pāvattiṉ vaḻiyoḻuka veḻutiyavitip
      puttēḷiruṅ kākkapoṟ
cōtikko ḻuṅkatir virikkum vaṭaveṟ
      peṭuttukkuṉit tuccirittut
tuṭṭarpuram ventupoṭi paṭṭoḻiya vaṭumviṭaic
      cōticem pātiyuṭalu
mātikkac cakamaṉaittu niṉṟoḷir paccai
      marakatac cuṭarparappi
vantukuṭi koṇṭa makatēvi cuntari
      yaṟamvaḷartta tēviyaimuḻutumē! 11

2. ceṅkīraip paruvam

vimpac civantavā yammai mēṉātēvi
      veṟparaci kaṟparacipoṉ
mēkalai yotukkimaṭi mītuṟavirut timulai
      vimmivaḻiyum pāṉmutaṟ
kampikkum vēlaitta raippeytu peyta
      caṅkaikkoṇṭu pālpukaṭṭik
kaiccaṅkai mummuṟai cirañcuṟṟi yorumuṟai
      kaviḻttiyuṭa laṅkulukkik
kumpakka ḷiṟṟerut tattiṟṟi ruttamoṭu
      koṇarunī rāṭṭimañcaṭ
kuḷirnilak kāppiṭṭu neypotti vaṭṭak
      kutampaiyiru kuḻaiyiliṭṭuc
cempoṟ pacuntoṭṭil vaittuvait tāṭṭumayil
      ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!
cīraivaḷa nāṭṭutamiḻ vīraivaḷa nāṭṭaraci
      ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē! 12

      ṇīriṉiṟpaṭiya veṭipō

      ṉuṭkaṉiyu muṭkaṉiyuṭait
tūṭuruvi yāṭutirai vāṉatipu kuntuvari
      yuṇṭucūl koṇṭeḻuntu
pāraivaḷa mākkumaik koṇṭalkiḻi yacceṉṟu
      pāyntatiṟ ṟōyntatuḷiyiṉ
paṇṭaic cuṉaippuṉalil vantacca maṟṟup
      pararkkēṟṟen nāḷumvāḻuñ
cīraivaḷa nāṭṭutamiḻ vīraivaḷa nāṭṭaraci
      ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē!

      ceṅkīrai yāṭiyaruḷē! 14

yukantoṟu mukantiṭum piraḷayac calatikaḷum
      putukkum puvaṉamē

      perukkamu taḷittaḷittuc

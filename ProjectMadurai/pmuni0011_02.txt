   taṇṇīr tēcam (Thanneer Desam) kaviñar vairamuttu eḻutiya putukkavitai
       /nāval. 1996l tamiḻ vāra itaḻ āṉanta vikaṭaṉil 24 tokutikaḷāka
       veḷivantatu. kaṭal, taṇṇīr maṟṟum ulakam paṟṟiya pala aṟiviyal
       uṇmaikaḷ ipputtakattil eḷiya kavitai naṭaiyil vivarikkap
       paṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ik kataiyiṉ katānāyakaṉ kalaivaṇṇaṉ, nāyaki tamiḻrōjā.
       kalaivaṇṇaṉ oru puraṭcikaramāṉa pattirikai niruparākavum, tamiḻrōjā
       oru paṇakkāra kuṭumpattu peṇṇākavum, ivarkaḷiṉ kātalaiyum,
       ūṭalaiyum collumpōtu kaṭal, taṇṇīr paṟṟiya aṟiviyal vivaraṅkaḷum
       eṭutturaikkap paṭṭuḷḷaṉa. mīṉavarkaḷ vāḻviyal paṟṟiyum pala
       vivaraṅkaḷ tokukkap paṭṭuḷḷaṉa. -------------

   ---------------------- "taNNIr tEcam" of Vairamuthu
   "taNNIr tEcam" of Vairamuthu originally published in 1996 as a 24-part
   series in the tamil weekly magazine Anantha Vikatan. This aRiviyal
   kaaviyam is about the 'sea odyssey'. KalaivaNNan is the hero;
   Tamilrojaa is the heroine. A lot of scientific facts about the sea,
   water, and the universe are sown in this modern poetry (pudhukk
   kavidhai). The work depicts the adventure of fishermen's life at sea.

INTRODUCTION
-------
THOUGH my knowledge of the Tamil Language is so scanty that at first I
held myself absolutely disqualified from complying with Mr.
Suryanarayana Sastri's request that I would write an introduction to
this book, yet on his pressing it I could not deny myself the pleasure
of complying with his desire and showing my interest in the excellent
work which he has here done for his native tongue. It has been a
privilege to talk with him over his book and to see how he has
assimilated the methods of Western philologists and has applied them to
the study of the Dravidian languages. While however he has found his
models in the west, no one can fail to see that his inspiration comes
from his love of Tamil and that he has put himself into the book, with
the result that it is fresh and original.
That the book meets a need is certain. As a study of the growth of
language in general from Dravidian point of view it is a contribution to
Philology of notable value; while as a history of the Tamil Language in
particular it stands alone. And Tamil is a language which well deserves
such study. It belongs to a group which has a strongly marked family
type, and which, while it has features that seem to connect it, on this
side and on that, with widely differing groups, is also distinguished
from all other groups by features all its own. Its origin still remains
to be discovered -- a secret to reward some scholar patiently treading
in the path along which this book points the way. Its grammar is full of
interest and its literature is one which any nation might be proud. It
still has a vigorous life as spoken tongue, and when its emancipation
from the tyranny of the conventions of a dead past is complete it will
regain its literary productiveness. For a long time at any rate it will
be the medium in which the movements and the emotions of the Tamilians
will find their real and natural expression. It must continue the
language of the genuine literature of the people. In English their
thought and feeling will move as it were in shackles; in Tamil they will
have the spontaneity and ease and vigour of free movement.
I am not competent to pronounce upon all the details of Mr Suryanarayana
Sastriar's work, but I am convinved that the main lines on which it is
constructed are right. The patient work which I know that he has devoted
to the subject for years and his mastery of the language both as scholar
and as poet are a sufficient guarantee that his illustration of the
principles is wide and sufficently correct.
In the study of Tamil in our Collleges the book will mark an epoch. For
it puts that study upon a better basis, and will begin to do for it what
such writers as Trench and Morris and Skeat did for English a generation
or more ago. The author has by it gained for himself the distinction of
being the pioneer in a field into which many will doubtless follow him.
But none of his successors are likely to excel him in the real
usefulness of their work or in their love of the language whose past he
here illumines.
F. W. KELLETT.

iṭaikkālattilirunta naṉṉayapaṭṭa rātiya pirāmaṇa vaiyākaraṇarkaḷ ceyta tavaṟu. ittavaṟu
kāraṇamākat teluṅku tamiḻiṉ vaḻimoḻi yaṉṟeṉpatu acaṅkatamām.
ivvāṟē kaṉṉaṭamun teluṅkaiyoṭṭip peritum iyaṅkiṉamai yāṉ atupōlavē pallāṟṟāṉun
taṉṉaic cīrppaṭuttikkoṇṭatu. itaṉālaṉṟō 'paḻaṅkaṉṉaṭam' eṉṟum 'putukkaṉṉaṭam' eṉṟum
aḵtu iru vēṟu piriviṉatāki yiyaṅkukiṉṟatu. maḻaṅkaṉṉaṭattait tamiḻiṉiṉṟum
piṟantateṉak kūṟuṅ kaṉṉaṭappulavar palar iṉṟumuḷar.
iṉi malaiyāḷamō vekunāḷkāṟun tiruntātiruntatu. iṟutiyil ēṟkukaṟaiya
munnūṟṟiyāṇṭukaṭku muṉṉar 'eḻuttaccaṉ' eṉpā-ṉoruvaṉāl mikkat tiruttappāṭu
aṭainttu; uṭaṉē vaṭamoḻic coṟkaḷaiyuñ coṟṟoṭarkaḷaiyum cantikaḷaiyum muṭipukaḷaiyum
malaiyāḷam mēṟkoṇṭatu.
mēṟkūṟiya teluṅku kaṉṉaṭam malaiyāḷa meṉṉum mūṉṟu vaḻimoḻikaḷum vaṭanūl yāppaiyum
aṇiyaiyu muṭaṉ mēṟkoṇṭu iyaṅkap pukuntaṉa.
iṉit tamiḻmoḻiyum kalantiyaṅku māṟṟaip paṟṟic ciṟitu viritturaippām.
ātivaralāṟu muṟṟiṟṟu.

II. vaṭamoḻik kalappu.

vaṭamoḻi tamiḻnāṭṭil vekunāḷ kāṟum iyaṅkiyum ataṟkut tamiḻ moḻiyait taṉ vaḻiyilē
tiruppik koḷḷutaṟkuṟṟa āṟṟalillātu pōyiṟṟu. vaṭamoḻiyāḷar tamiḻarkaḷatu oḻukka
vaḻakkaṅkaḷai yuṇarntu avaṟṟiṟkēṟpa vaṭamoḻiyil nūlkaḷ vakuppāṉ pukuntaṉar. avarkaḷellām
āṉmanūṟ payiṟci mikkuṭaiyārāyum, kalaiyuṇarcci cāṉṟavarāyu miruntamaipaṟṟit tamiḻaratu
tivviya stalaṅkaḷukkup purāṇaṅkaḷ vakuttaṉar; tamiḻarkaḷiṭattillā-tirunta 'antaṇar,
aracar, vaṇikar, vēḷāḷar' eṉṟa nālvakaiccāti muṟaiyai mellamella nāṭṭiviṭṭaṉar.

eṉṟu āriyarai nōkki muḻaṅkum 'kapilarakava'laiyuṅ kāṇka. iṉṉuma avartam puntinalaṅ
kāṭṭit tamiḻaracarkaḷiṭam, amaiccarka ḷeṉavum mēlatikārap pirapukkaḷeṉavum amaintu
koṇṭaṉar; tamiḻariṭattirunta pala ariya viṣayaṅkaḷaiyum moḻi peyarttut tamaḻar
aṟiyumuṉṉarē avaṟṟait tāmaṟintaṉa pōlavum, vaṭamoḻiyiṉiṉṟumē tamiḻiṟku avai vantaṉa
pōlavum kāṭṭiṉar.
tamiḻaruṭ cāmāṉiya caṉaṅkaḷ avvāriyaratu viruppattiṟkēṟpa evvaḷaviṇaṅkiya pōtilum,
pulavarāyiṉār avarkaḷatu tiruttap pāṭṭiṟkup peritu miṇaṅkiṉarallar. oḻukkac cīrppāṭu
ēṟpaṭṭapōtiṉum pāṣait tiruttam ēṟpaṭavillai. tamiḻiṉ muppattōreḻuttukkaḷumavvaṟē
yiṉṟaḷavu mirukkiṉṟaṉa; ciṟitum vēṟupaṭavillai. tāṅkaḷ cellumiṭaṅkaḷukkut takkapaṭi
putiya putiya ilipikaḷ ēṟpaṭuttik koḷḷumiyalpuṭaiya āriyar tamiḻnāṭṭiṟkēṟṟapaṭi
tamiḻilipiyai yoṭṭik 'kirantam eṉṉum peyariṟ putuvatōr ilipivakuttaṉar; tamiḻarai
vacīkarikkumāṟu avvilipiyiṟ pala nūlkaḷ varaintaṉar. tamiḻp pulavarāvār etaṟkum
acaiyātu taṅkaḷ tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ pōkkaiyē taḻuvic celvārāyiṉār.
ivvāṟu tamiḻaruṭ paṇṭitarāyāṉār vaṭamoḻiḻait tamiḻiṉkaṇ viravavoṭṭātu vilakkiyum,
pāmararāyiṉār vaṭamoḻic coṟkaḷuṭ palavaṟṟai mēṟkoṇṭu vaḻaṅkap pukuntamaiyiṉ
nāḷāvaṭṭattil vaṭa coṟkaḷ pala tamiḻp pāṣaiyiṉkaṇṇē vērūṉṟiviṭṭaṉa. avvāṟāyiṉ
itupōlavē vaṭamoḻiyiṉ kaṇṇum tamiḻc coṟkaḷ pala ceṉṟu cērntiruttal vēṇṭumaṉṟō?
ataiyum ārāyvām.
vaṭamoḻi tamiḻ moḻiyoṭu kalakkap pukumuṉṉarē, muṉṉatu pēccuvaḻakkaṟṟu ēṭṭuvaḻakkāy
maṭṭilirukkum nilaimaikku vantuviṭṭatu. ēṭṭuvaḻak koṉṟumēyuḷḷa pāṣaiyōṭu iruvakai
vaḻakkamuḷḷa pāṣaiyoṉṟu kūṭi yiyaṅkap pukumāyiṉ muṉṉataṉ coṟkaḷē piṉṉataṉkaṭ ceṉṟu
cērumēyaṉṟip piṉṉataṉ coṟkaḷ muṉṉataṉ kaṭceṉṟu cērā. itu pāṣai nūliṉ
uṇmaikaḷḷoṉṟu. ituvē vaḻakkāṟṟu muṟai. immuṟai paṟṟiyē vaṭacoṟkaḷ pala tamiḻiṉkaṭ
pukuntaṉa. tamiḻc coṟkaḷiṟ cilatāmum vaṭamoḻiyiṉkaṇ ēṟāmaṟ pōyiṉa. eṉiṉum teṉṉāṭṭu
vaṭamoḻiyāḷar maṭṭiṟ cilar ūrppeyar, malaip peyar, yāṟṟuppeyar mutalāyiṉavaṟṟait taṅkaḷ
capta cāstiratiṟ kiyainta vaṇṇam, ōcai vēṟupāṭu ceytukoṇṭu tāṅkaḷ vakukkap pukunta
purāṇātikaḷil vaḻaṅkuvārāyiṉar. itutāṉuṇmai; itaṟkumē loṉṟuñ colla iyalātu.
iṉit tamiḻp pulavarkaḷāyiṉār, camskirutac coṟkaḷai evvaḷavō vilakkippārttum avaṟṟai
vilakkutal muṭiyātu pōyiṟṟu. pōkavē tamiḻppulavarkaḷun taṅkaḷ muyaṟcikaḷellām vīṇātal
kaṇṭu vēṇṭā veṟuppāyt tamakku vēṇṭiya ciṟcila coṟkaḷai maṭṭil taṅkaḷ eḻuttilakkaṇa
vitikaṭkut takkavāṟu tirittu mēṟkoḷvārāyiṉār; āriyac coṟkaḷ tamiḻil varuvataṟkēṟṟa
vitikaḷum vakuttaṉar. piṉṉark koñcaṅ koñcamāka vaṭa coṟkaḷ pala tamiḻ moḻiyiṉkaṇ iṭam
peṟuvaṉa vāyiṉa.
ataṉmēl mutaliṭai kaṭaiyeṉum muccaṅkattār kālattilum vaṭa coṟkaḷ tamiḻ nūlkaḷi lēṟiṉa.
āyiṉum avai ciṟitaḷavēyām. 'tolkāppiyam' eṉṉum ilakkaṇattiṉuḷḷum 'eṭṭuttokai,
pattuppāṭṭu, patiṉeṇkīḻkkaṇakku' eṉṉum nūṟṟokaikaḷuḷḷum āṅkāṅku iraṇṭoru vaṭacoṟ
kāṇappaṭalāmēyaṉṟi, ataṟkumēlillai.
piṉṉarp pauttarāyiṉār talaiyeṭuttut tam matattai yāṇṭum parappip pallāyirak kaṇakkākac
caṉaṅkaḷaic cērttukkoṇṭu akkālattirunta āriyarai yetirttaṉar. ippakaimai teṉṉāṭṭilum
paraviṟṟu. paravavē tamiḻaruṭ palar pauttamatam mēṟkoṇṭu āriyarai yetirppatil nōkka
muṟṟiruntaṉar. akkālattil maṟupaṭiyum tamiḻppulavarkaḷ taṅkaḷāṟ kūṭiya maṭṭil
vaṭacoṟkaḷait tamatu tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ kaṇṇē kalakka voṭṭātu taṭuttaṉar. muṉṉarē tamiḻiṟ
pōntu vērūṉṟiviṭṭa, vaṭacoṟkaḷait tolaippatu avarkaṭkup peruṅ kaṣṭamāyviṭṭatu. ātaliṉ
avarkaḷ eṉ ceyya vallar? muṉṉarē vantaṉapōka, iṉimēlātal appollāta vaṭacoṟkaḷ tamiḻiṉkaṇ
vārātavāṟu pātukāttal vēṇṭumeṉṟu ciṟitu kālam muyaṉṟaṉar. avvāṟē ivarkaḷatu
viṭāmuyaṟciyāṟ ciṟitu kālam vaṭacoṟkaḷ tamiḻil atikamāy vantu kalavāmalumiruntaṉa.
ikkālattilē tāṉ 'centamiḻ' 'koṭuntamiḻ' eṉat tamiḻ iru piriviṉatāki yiyaṅkap
pukuntatu. centamiḻāvatu pulavarāyiṉār payilun tamiḻ; koṭuntamiḻāvatu pulavarallāta
cāmāṉiya makkaḷ payiluntamiḻ. ivaṟṟaik kuṟittup piritōr amayattiṟ pēcuvām.
iṉi ettuṇai nāḷ mēṉmēleḻum veḷḷattaittaṭuttuk koṇṭiruttal iyalum? ātalāṟ
pauttaratu muyaṟciyā lēṟpaṭṭa karaikaḷ āriyapāṣaiyiṉ alaikaḷāl eṟṟuṇṭu aḻivaṉavāyiṉa.
pauttacamaya muntalai tāḻntatu.
innilaimaiyiṟ caiṉar eḻuntaṉar. aṅṅaṉameḻunta caiṉar āriyaratu ācāraṅkaḷuṭ palavaṟṟai
mēṟkoṇṭaṉar; aḵtaṉṟiyum vaṭamoḻiyiṉ kaṇ vicēṣa kaurava muṭaiyarāy ataṉaip peritum
payilvārāyiṉar. vaṭamoḻiyiṉ pākataṅkaḷaiyum avaṟṟi ṉilakkaṇaṅkaḷaiyum naṉkuṇarntu
koṇṭaṉar; ittakaiya caiṉap pulavarkaḷ tamiḻ moḻiyaiyum appiyacikkap pukuntu, tamiḻiṉ
kaṇṇē aḷaviṟanta vaṭacoṟkaḷai yēṟṟiṉar.
attuṇaiyōṭamaiyātu 'maṇippiravāḷam' eṉṟatōr putiya pāṣai vakuttuviṭṭaṉar. aḵtāvatu
teṉmoḻiyum vaṭamoḻiyum carikkuccari kalantapāṣaiyām. maṇiyum pavaḷamuṅ kalantu kōttatōr
mālai kāṭcik kiṉpam payattal pōlat tamiḻuñ camskirutamuṅ kalanta pāṣai kēḷvik kiṉpam
payakkumeṉṟa pōliyeṇṇamē ittakaiya āpāca pāṣaiyoṉṟu vakukkumāṟu tūṇṭiṟṟu.
'śrīpurāṇam' eṉṉum caiṉa nūl muḻuvatum maṇippiravāḷa meṉṉum ivvāpāca naṭaiyiṉ iyaṉṟa
tāmāṟu kāṇka.
ivvāṟu caiṉar orupuṟañ ceytukoṇṭu cellāniṟka, maṟṟoru puṟattil āriyappulavar cilar
tamiḻmoḻiyai yappiyacittukkoṇṭu nītimārkkattaiyum camaya cāstiraṅkaḷaiyum tamiḻarkkup
pōtippēmeṉap pukuntu, tamatu karuttukkaḷai yellām mēṟkūṟiya maṇippiravāḷa pāṣaiyil
veḷippaṭut turaippārāyiṉar. 'nālāyirappirapantam' eṉṟa tamiḻnūliṟku
viyākkiyāṉaṅkaḷum ittakaiya maṇippiravāḷa naṭaiyil vakukkappaṭṭiruttal kāṇka. ivvaṇṇam
palatiṟattālum vaṭacoṟkaḷ vantu tamiḻiṉkaṇ aḷaviṉṟi yēṟiṉa.
tamiḻiṉkaṇuḷḷa 'iraṭṭaikkiḷavi' yeṉpatu orupuṟamirukka, 'iraṭṭaippatam' eṉpatoru
vakaiyum maṟṟorupuṟatti lēṟpaṭā niṉṟatu. orē col nērē vaṭamoḻiyiṉiṉṟum
tamiḻiṟpōntu, vaḻaṅkuvataṉṟiyum, vaṭa moḻiyiṉ pākata vaḻiyākavum vantu tamiḻil
vaḻaṅkukiṉṟatu. utāraṇamāka, 'viññāpaṉam' eṉpatu nērē vaṭamoḻiyiṉiṉṟum pōntacol;
'viṇṇappam' eṉpatu vaṭamoḻiyiṉ pākata vaḻiyāka vanta col. ivviraṇṭiṟkum poruḷoṉṟē.
ivvāṟu vēṇṭāc coṟkaḷum tamiḻiṉ kaṇēṟiṉa, ēṟukiṉṟaṉa, ēṟum!
tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ nilaimai yivvāṟātalum, vaṭamoḻi yilakkaṇattaik kalantu tamiḻilakkaṇamum
vakukkap pukuntu viṭṭaṉar cilar. 'tolkāppiya'ttiṟku uraivakutta aivaruḷ mutaṉālvarum
ēṟakkuṟaiyat tamiḻp pōkkaiyē paṟṟi yuraivakuttuc ceṉṟaṉarākac cēṉāvaraiya roruvarmaṭṭil
vaṭamoḻippōkkaic ciṟitaḷavu kalantu collatikārattiṟku urai vakuttaṉar.
taṉittamiḻnūlākiya 'tirukkuṟa' ḷiṟkup parimēlaḻakarum, vaṭanūlār matam paṟṟiyē
yuraivakuttēkiṉar. 'naṉṉūl' ceyta pavaṇantiyārum, 'kiṉṉūl' ceyta kuṇavīrapaṇṭitarum
vaṭanūlilakkaṇap pōkkait taḻuvuvā rāyiṉar. 'vīracōḻiya'mum ataṉ uraiyumō colla
vēṇṭuvaṉavalla. ivai vicēṣamāy vaṭamoḻi yilakkaṇaṅkaḷait tamiḻiṉkaṭ pukuttuvaṉavāyiṉa. ataṉ
mēṟ 'pirayōkavivēkam' eṉṉum ilakkaṇa nūlvakutta cuppiramaṇiya tīkṣitar vaṭamoḻic
cattacāttirattait tamiḻiṉkaṭkūṟit tamiḻmoḻiyō ṭoppiṭṭuc cīrtūkki yārāyvārāyiṉar.
piṉṉar 'ilakkaṇakkotturai' ceyta īcāṉatēcikar vaṭamoḻi yilakkaṇattait tamiḻoṭu kalantaṉar;
kalantamaṭṭil nillātu tamatu nūṟpāyirattiṉkaṭ kūṟiya cila kūṟṟukkaḷ aṟivuṭaiyōr
otukkaṟpālaṉa vākiṉṟaṉa.

eṉṟa vivaiyaṉaittum 'pāṣai nūl' eṉṉum ariya cāstirattil payiṟciyum carittirakkaṇṇum
illāta kuṟaiviṉāleḻunta poruntāk kūṟṟukkaḷā meṉpatu tiṇṇam. ivai kēṭṭup
pēraṟivāḷarāyiṉār nakaittu viṭuppareṉpatu niccayam. ivar tēṟṟēkāra miraṭṭittuk
kūṟiyataṟku māṟāka, āciriyar civañāṉa cuvāmikaḷ tamatu 'tolkāppiyap
pāyiravirutti'yilē "tamiḻmoḻip puṇarccikkaṭpaṭuñ ceykaikaḷuṅ kuṟiyīṭukaḷum,
viṉaikkuṟippu viṉaittokai mutaliya cila collilakkaṇaṅkaḷum, uyartiṇai aḵṟiṇai mutaliya
coṟpākupāṭukaḷum, akampuṟameṉum poruṭpāku pāṭukaḷum, kuṟiñci veṭṭu mutaliya
tiṇaip pākupāṭukaḷum, avaṟṟiṉ pakutikaḷum, veṇpā mutaliya ceyyuḷilakkaṇamum,
iṉṉōraṉṉa piṟavum vaṭamoḻiyiṟ peṟappaṭā" eṉṟu kūṟuvarāyiṉar. itu yāvarum
naṉkuṇarntatu; tamiḻmoḻiyum vēṟē; vaṭamoḻiyum vēṟē; itaṉkaṇ aiyappāṭu eḷḷaḷavu
millai.
naṉṉūlār tamatu pataviyaliṉiṟṟilum meyyīṟṟup puṇariyaliṉīṟṟilum vaṭacollākkamum vaṭacoṟ
cantiyum muṟaiyē kūṟuvārāyiṉar.
iṉic camskiruta moḻiyiṉ kaṇṇē pālvakuppuc coṉṉōkkat tālēṟpaṭṭuḷatēyaṉṟip
poruṇōkkattā lēṟpaṭṭilatu. utāraṇamākak, kaiyeṉap poruḷpaṭum 'karam' eṉṟacol āṇpāl;
maṉaiviyeṉap poruḷpaṭuñ coṟkaḷilē, 'tāram' eṉpatu āṇpāl, 'kaḷattiram' eṉpatu
alippāl, aḵtāvatu oṉṟaṉpāl eṉa ōrāṟṟāṉamaikkalām. ivvāṟuḷatu vaṭamoḻip pāl
vakuppiṉ ciṟappu. maṟṟut tamiḻmoḻiyilō pāl vakuppellām poru ṇōkkattālēṟ
paṭṭuḷavē yaṉṟic coṉṉōkkattā leṟpaṭavēyillai. itu tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ ciṟappukkaḷuḷ oṉṟu.
vaṭanūṉmuṟai kuṟaipāṭuṭaiyatu. āriya pāṣaikaḷō ṭiyaipupaṭṭa pāṣaikaḷellām coṉṉōkkap pāl
vakuppuk kuṟaipāṭuṭaiyaṉavāmāṟu kāṇka. tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ vaḻimoḻika ḷellām poruṇōkkap
pālvakuppuc ciṟappuṭaiyaṉa. ivvuṇmai yoṉṟē tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ taṉi nilaiyai naṉku
viḷaṅkuvataṟkut takka cāṉṟu pakarum.

eṉṟa naṉṉūṟ cūttiravitiyiṟ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa aṇi nalaṉ āriyapāṣaic coṟkaḷukkup
perumpālum illātu pōtal kāṇka. aṇi nūṟ pulavar muṉṉar āriyapāṣaikku aḵtō
riḻukkāmumaṉṟē?
akapporuḷum ataṉ ṟuṟaikaḷum, puṟapporuḷum ataṉ ṟuṟaikaḷum, ivviruvakaip
poruḷkaḷiṉiyaipukaḷum vaṭamoḻiyiṉiṉṟum eṉṟeṉṟum kiṭaittaliyalāta ariya taṉit tamiḻ
viṣayaṅkaḷām. ivaṟṟiṉiṉṟum ātiyilirunta tamiḻarkaḷatu oḻukka nilai ittaṉmait teṉpatum,
avarkaḷatu nākarika nilai ittaṉmait teṉpatum naṉku viḷaṅkukiṉṟaṉa.
vaṭamoḻiyiṉ 'ilaku kuru kaṇayati viruttam' eṉṟa pākupāṭukaṭkut tamiḻiliṭamē-yillai.
tamiḻiṉ nālvakaip pākkaḷum mūvakaip pāliṉamum, avaṟṟiṉ pākupāṭukaḷum, etukai mōṉai
niyamaṅkaḷum, acai, cīr, taḷai ākiyavaṟṟiṉi-yalpukaḷum tamiḻmoḻikkē yuriyaṉa. ivai
vaṭamoḻikkaṭ kāṇappaṭuvaṉavalla. tamiḻiṟ 'pā' eṉṟata ṉilakkaṇamē mikavum iṉimai peṟa
vamaintuḷatu. 'pāveṉpatu, cēṭpulattirunta kālattum, oruvaṉ eḻuttuñ collun
teriyāmaṟ pāṭamōtuṅkāl, avaṉ collukiṉṟa ceyyuḷai vikaṟpittu iṉṉa ceyyuḷeṉṟu
uṇartaṟ kētuvākip parantu paṭṭuc colvatōr ōcai" eṉṟār āciriyar nacciṉārkkiṉiyar.
itu vaṭamoḻiyāḷarkkup putumaiyām.

mayaṅkik kāraṇamallātavaṟṟaik kāraṇameṉṟu kūṟutal tarukka nūṟ kuṟṟamaṉṟō?

eṉṟu ' kuṟiñcikkavi' yiṟ kūṟiṉatai yokkum ivar ceyal.
itu niṟka, ceṉṟa cillāṇṭukaḷāka, nāṭakattamiḻ aṟivuṭaiyōr cilaratu utavikoṇṭu
talaiyeṭuttu vaḷarā niṉṟatu. itaṟku vaṭamoḻi nāṭakappayiṟciyum āṅkilanāṭakap payiṟciyum
mikap payaṉpaṭuvaṉavāyiṉa. ivvāṟu putuvaḻiyiṟ puṉaiyappaṭṭu veḷippaṭum nāṭakaṅkaḷ iṉṉum
atikarittal vēṇṭum. ivaṟṟai yoḻuṅku paṭuttutal karuti, yām 'nāṭakaviyal' eṉṟōr
ilakkaṇa nūl ceytirukkiṉṟaṉam.
iṉiccilar, 'nāṭakam' eṉṟa col vaṭacollātal kaṇṭu, āriyaroṭu kalanta piṉṉarē
tamiḻarkaḷiṭattiṟ nāṭakattamiḻ ēṟpaṭṭiruttal vēṇṭumeṉkiṉṟaṉar. ikkūṟṟu vaṭamoḻiyil
'vāy' eṉpataṟkut takka collillāmaiyāl vaṭamoḻiyāḷarkku vāyillai yeṉpatu pōlum.
aḵtaṉṟiyum 'kūttu' eṉṟa collē ātiyil tamiḻarkaḷ vaḻaṅkiyatu; kūtteṉpatu potu;
nāṭakameṉpatu ciṟappu. kūttup palavaṟṟuḷ, nāṭakameṉpatu katai taḻuvivarum kūttu, eṉavē
nāṭakameṉṟa colvaḻakkiṉ muṉṉarē, nāṭakat toḻil vaḻakkum, nāṭaka nūl vaḻakkun
tamiḻarmāṭṭuṇṭeṉpatu peṟṟām.
mūvakaip pākupāṭu muṟṟiṟṟu.

IV. aivakai yilakkaṇam.

pāṣaiyāvatu, makkaḷatu iyaṟkai vēṭkaiyil tōṉṟi, avaratu nākarika virutti muṟaikkēṟp
vaḷarntu varuvatōr makkaḷākkap poruḷām. atu coṟkōvaippaṭṭup poruḷ aṟivuṟukkum
vākkiyaṅkaḷālāyatu; iv vākkiyaṅkaḷellām poruḷ aṟivuṟukkuñ coṟkaḷāyiṉa; iccoṟkaḷ
tāmum pakuti vikuti iṭainilai mutaliya pala vuṟuppukkaḷālāyiṉa.
ivvuṟuppukkaḷuḷḷum pakutikaḷellām, perumpāṉmaiyum ātiyilirunta tamiḻmakkaḷ
coṟcollat toṭaṅkiya kālattup piṟanta coṟkōvai citaintu maruviya coṟkaḷām; ciṟupāṉmai
olikkuṟippu viyappuk kuṟippu mutaliyakāraṇaṅkaḷ paṟṟi vanta coṟkaḷām. iṉi vikuti
mutalāyiṉa vellām oru kālattil muḻumutaṟ coṟkaḷāyp poruḷaṟivuṟutti niṉṟu,
piṉṉoru kālattu makkaḷ, kuṟippiṉmai, cōmpal, poccāppu, muyaṟcic curukkam ākiya
kāraṇaṅkaḷāṟ coṟ cōrvupaṭa moḻitalāṟ palavāṟu tirintu maruvi iṭaic coṟkaḷeṉṟu vaḻaṅkap
paṭuvaṉavāyiṉa. nākarikam mutiruntōṟum mutiruntōṟum pāṣai māṟupaṭṭuc coṟkaḷaic
citaittu vaḻaṅkum vaḻakkam makkaḷukkup pala acaukariyaṅkaḷ viḷaivittaṉa.
tamiḻccoṟkaḷai māntar mēlum mēluñ citaittu vaḻaṅkātavāṟu, āciriyar akattiyaṉār
tolkāppiyaṉār pōṉṟa naṉmakkaḷ tōṉṟi, āccoṟkaḷai mēlvaḻakku muṟaikāṭṭi varampaṟuttu
ilakkaṇa nūlkaḷ vakuttut tamiḻp pāṣaiyai yoḻuṅkupaṭutti niṟuttuvārāyiṉar. iṅṅaṉam
oḻuṅkupaṭuttappaṭāta pāṣaikaḷellāñ cila citaintu vaḻaṅki iṟutiyil urukkulaintu
vaḻakkamaṟṟu oḻintupōm.
mēṟkūṟiyavāṟu tamiḻmoḻikku mutaṉmutal ilakkaṇa nūl vakuttavar akattiyaṉār. avar
iyalicai nāṭakameṉṟa muttami ḻilakkaṇamum muṟaippaṭa vakut tōtiṉār. icaināṭakat
tamiḻilakkaṇaṅkaḷai yeṭuttuk kūṟātu iyaṟṟamiḻilakkaṇattaimaṭṭil vakut tōtiṉār
tolkāppiyaṉār. ippoḻutu 'akattiyat'tiṉ cūttiraṅkaḷ ciṟcilamaṭṭil āṅkāṅkut tollai
tūturaikaḷiṟ kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉavēyaṉṟi nūṉ muḻutuṅ kāṇappaṭavillai; ākavē 'tolkāppiya'
moṉṟumē muḻu mutalilakkaṇa nūlāyk kuṟaipāṭiṉṟi yitukāṟum ilaṅkāniṉṟatu; iṉiyum
ilaṅkā niṟkum. ivviyaṟṟami ḻilakkaṇanūl 'eḻuttu', 'col', 'poruḷ' eṉṟa
mūṉṟatikāraṅkaḷāka vakukkappaṭṭuḷatu.
eḻuttatikārattil eḻuttiṉiyalpukaḷum, eḻuttāṉākiya moḻikaḷum avaṟṟiṉ puṇarccikaḷum
mutalāyiṉa virittuk kūṟappaṭṭaṉa. collatikārattiṟ colliṉ pākupāṭukaḷum,
avaṟṟiṉiyalpukaḷum mutalāyiṉa viritturaikkappaṭṭuḷa. maṟṟup poruḷatikārattilō
kaḷaviyaluṅ kaṟpiyalumākiya akapporuḷum avaṟṟiṉ tuṟaikaḷum, puṟapporuḷum avaṟṟiṉ
tuṟaikaḷum, meyppāṭṭiyal uvamaviyal ceyyuḷiyal marapiyalakaḷum kūṟappaṭṭirukkiṉṟaṉa.
iṉi aivakai yilakkaṇaṅkaḷāvaṉa 'eḻuttu, col, poruḷ, yāppu, aṇi' yeṉpaṉa.
ivvaintaṉuḷḷē 'tolkāppiya'ttil mutal mūṉṟai maṭṭilē virittuk kūṟuvār, ceyyuḷiya
leṉṟataṉ kaṇṇē yāppai virittōtiyum, uvamaviyaleṉṟataṉ kaṇṇē aṇiyai yeṭuttōtiyum
ōrāṟṟāṉ aivakai yilakkaṇamuṅ kūṟiṉārāyiṉār. aṉṟiyun tolkāppiyaṉār kālattē aivakai
yilakkaṇameṉṟa pākupāṭiṉṟi yiruntiruttalu miyalpē; allatu yāppaṇikaḷ
poruḷatikārattiṉ pakutikaḷeṉavē karutap paṭṭiruntaṉa veṉṟalum amaiyum.
kaṭaiccaṅkattār kālatti lēṟpaṭṭa 'iṟaiyaṉārakap poruḷurai'yiṟ " peytapiṉ aracaṉ nāṭu
nāṭāyiṟ ṟākaliṉ nūlvallāraik koṇarkaveṉṟu ellāp pakkamum pōkka, eḻuttatikāramuñ
collatikāramum yāppatikāramum vallārait talaippaṭṭuk koṇarntu, poruḷatikāram vallārai
eṅkun talaippaṭṭilē meṉavantār. vara, aracaṉum puṭaipaṭak kavaṉṟu, 'eṉṉai? eḻuttuñ
collum yāppum ārāyvatu poruḷatikārattiṉ poruṭṭaṉṟē? poruḷatikāram peṟēmēyeṉiṉ,
ivai peṟṟum perṟilēm' eṉac collāniṟpa" eṉṟa kūṟṟiṉai uyttu nōkki yuṇarumpōtu,
akkālattil 'eḻuttu, col, poruḷ,yāppu' eṉa nālvakaiyē vaḻaṅkiṉa veṉpatu
tuṇiyappaṭum. aḵtāvatu tolkāppiyaṉār kālattiṟ poruḷatikārattiluḷḷaṭaṅkiya yāppu,
kaṭaic caṅkattār kālattil ataṉiṉṟum pirintu taṉittu ōratikāramāyiṟṟu eṉpatām.
ataṉpiṉ yāppatikāram mikka ciṟappaṭaintu pallōrālum pārāṭṭap paṭṭatu. akkālattirunta
pulavar palarum yāppu nūlkaḷ pala iyaṟṟiṉar. kākkaipāṭiṉiyar, avinayaṉār, mayēccuraṉār
mutalāyiṉār tattam peyarāl yāppilaṅkaṇaṅkaḷ vakuttaṉar. piṉṉar 'yāpparuṅkalam,'
'yāpparuṅkalak kārikai' eṉṟa nūlkaḷ vakukkappaṭṭu vaḻaṅkuvaṉa vāyiṉa.
iṭaiyiṟ poruḷatikārap payiṟci kuṉṟiyapōtu 'iṟaiyaṉārakap poruḷ' eṉṟa ciṟiya ilakkaṇa
nūlum, maṇivācakap perumāṉ vāy malarntaruḷiya 'tirukkōvaiyār' eṉṟa ilakkaṇa nūlum
ēṟpaṭṭaṉa. ataṉ mēṟ cila putiya karuttukkaḷai yuṭaṉkūṭṭi nāṟkalirāja nampi yeṉpavar
'akapporuḷ viḷakkam' eṉṟa ilakkaṇa nūliyaṟṟiṉar. piṉṉark 'kallāṭam', nammāḻvār
ceytaruḷiya 'tiruviruttam' eṉṟa akapporuḷilakkiya nūlkaḷ veḷippōntaṉa. aiyaṉāritaṉār
eṉpavar 'puṟapporuḷ veṇpā mālai' eṉṟa nūl vakuttaṉar.
ivvāṟu nālvakai yilakkaṇamē cilakālam vaḻaṅki vantaṉa. ataṉmēl vaṭanūlvāṇarōṭu ūṭāṭi
aintāvatu vakai yilakkaṇamākiya aṇiyatikārattaiyun tamiḻp pulavarkaḷ virutti ceytu
koḷḷap pukuntaṉar. 'aṇinūl', 'taṇṭiyalaṅkāram' mutaliya nūlkaḷ iyaṟṟappaṭṭaṉa. ivaṟṟuṭ
piṉṉatē tamiḻ makkaḷuṭ payiṟcimikkuṭaiyatāy viḷaṅkukiṉṟatu. innūlāciriyar potu
vaṇiyiyal, poruḷaṉiyiyal collaṇiyiyal eṉṟa mūṉṟu piriviṉil tamnūlai
muṭittirukkiṉṟaṉar. potuvaṇiyiyaliṟ potuvākap palavakai kalappāṭukaḷum puṉaivu
muṟaikaḷuṅ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa.
poruḷaṇiyiyal taṉmaiyaṇi mutalāka muppattaintaṇikaḷ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa. ipporuḷaṇikaḷiṟ pala
uvamameṉṟa ōraṇiyiṉaṭiyākavē tōṉṟiṉa veṉpatu pallōr tuṇipu. āciriyar
tolkāppiyaṉārum ittuṇipiṉar pōlum. itu vaṭamoḻi appaiya tīkṣitaravarkaḷukkum
uṭaṉpāṭātal,

eṉṟa avaratu 'cittiramīmāñcaik' kūṟṟiṉāṉ viḷaṅkum. iṉic collaṇiyiyalil maṭakkum
cittiramumākiya miṟaikkavikaḷiṉ pākupāṭukaḷum, avaṟṟiṉilakkaṇaṅkaḷum parakkak
kūṟappaṭṭuḷa.
maṟṟu, aintilakkaṇaṅkaḷaiyum muṟṟat taṉittaṉi kūṟum nūlkaḷ mikac cilavē. avaitām
'vīracōḻiyam', 'ilakkaṇa viḷakkam', 'toṉṉūl viḷakkam' eṉpaṉavām. vīracōḻiyam perumpālum
vaṭanūl muṟaiyaiyē taḻuvic celvatu. ilakkaṇa viḷakkamuṭaiyār yāppaṇikaḷaip piṉṉūlkaḷpōla
virittuk kūṟiṉum avaṟṟaip poruḷatikārattiṉ kaṇṇēyē yaṭakkik kūṟiṉar. toṉṉūl
viḷakkamuṭaiyōr poruḷatikārattaic curukik kūṟilum yāppaṇikaḷai viḷaṅkak kūṟiṉar.
taṇṭiyalaṅkārampōlat taṉiyē aṇiyilakkaṇaṅ kūṟuvatāy, āṉāl ataṉiṉum virintatāy,
'māṟaṉalaṅkāram' eṉṟatōr nūl ēṟpaṭuvatāyiṟṟu. itu mikac ciṟantatōr nūl. ataṉ mēṟ
'kuvalayānantam' eṉṟa vaṭamoḻi yalaṅkāracāstiram tamiḻiṉkaṇ moḻipeyarkkap paṭuvatāyiṟṟu.
itu putumai vaḻiyiliyaṉṟatō riṉiyanūl. itaṉkaṇṇē uvamaiyaṇi mutal etuvaṇiyīṟāka
nūṟaṇikaḷ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa.
ivvāṟu tamiḻmoḻi aivakai yilakkaṇamu muṭaiyatāya nilaimai yeyti viḷaṅkā niṉṟatu.
pavaṇanti muṉivarceyta 'naṉṉūl' eḻuttuñ collun teḷivākavum curukkamākavum
aḻakākavum kūṟukiṟatu. 'nēminātam' eṉṟatum appaṭiyē.
iṉip 'pāṭṭiyal' eṉṉumō rilakkaṇap pakutiyuḷatu. ataṉiyalpu caṟṟē viritturaikkaṟ
pālatu. atu tamiḻiṟ kūṟappaṭum toṇṇūṟṟaṟuvakaip pirapantaṅkaḷi ṉilakkaṇaṅkaḷum,
pāṭuvōṉukkum pāṭappaṭuvō ṉukku muḷḷa eḻuttup poruttam maṅkalapporuttam,
mutaliyaṉavum, īcceḻuttu amutaveḻutteṉṟa pākupāṭum, āṉantam mutaliya kuṟṟaṅ kaḷum
ivai pōlvaṉa piṟavum virittuk kūṟuvatākum. 'veṇpāppāṭṭiyal,' 'varaiyaṟuttapāṭṭiyal'
eṉṟa nūlkaḷellām pāṭṭiyal kūṟuvaṉavē. ilakkaṇa viḷakkatti ṉiṟutiyiṟ 'pāṭṭiyal'
eṉṟatōriyalum yāttuk kōkkappaṭṭuḷatu.
aṇikaḷuṭ collaṇikaḷē vicēṭamāy nallicaip pulavarkaḷāṉ matikkappaṭuvaṉavalla. muṟkālattuc
caṅkac ceyyuṭkaḷilellām collaṇikaḷ kāṇṭalaritu. iccollaṇikaḷilum cittirakavikaḷ
mikavu motukkap paṭuvaṉavāyiṉa. 'kāñcippurāṇa' mātiya cilanūlkaḷē yivaṟṟuṭ cila
koṇṭiyaṅkukiṉṟaṉa. maṭakkukaḷellām vicēṭamāy nūlkaḷiṟ payiṉṟu varāmal avaṟṟuḷ
āṅkāṅku varum. iṭaikkālattut tōṉṟiya cilar colliṉpam nāṭupavarāyc collaṇikaḷaip
peritum vaḻaṅkuvārāyiṉar. ivarkaḷ yamakam, tiripu mutaliya ceyyuṭkaḷ pala viyaṟṟiṉar.
piṉṉart tiripantātikaḷum yamaka vantātikaḷum cilēṭai veṇpākkaḷum aḷaviṟantaṉa
veḻumpiṉa. ivaiyaṉaittum perumpālum poruṭceṟivilavāy vīṇ captajālaṅkaḷāymaṭṭil
muṭintaṉa. ikkālattiṉum teṉṉāṭṭiṟ pulavar palar yamakantiripu pāṭutalaiyē
peritākaveṇṇivāṇāḷai vīṇāṉ kaḻippar.
coṉṉalattilum poruṇalamē ciṟantateṉap pēraṟivāḷar yāvaruṅ
kūṟuvar. yamakantiripuḷḷa pāṭalkaḷai ' uyirillāp pāṭṭukkaḷ' eṉṟum
karuttunalam vāynta kaṟpaṉaiyuḷḷa pāṭalkaḷai 'uyiruḷḷa pāṭṭukkaḷ eṉṟum aṟivuṭaiyōr palar
kūṟakkēṭṭirukkiṉaṟaṉam. iḵtuṇmaiyē yeṉpatu tiṇṇam. ivvāṟavarkaḷ kūṟutaṟkuṟṟa kāraṇaṅkaḷ
yāveṉṟu ārāyvōm. mutalāvatu: miṟaikkavikaḷ pāṭumiṭattu mikka kālañ cellum;
appaṭik kālañ ceṉṟum ciṟcila vēḷaikaḷil avaikaḷ pāṭuvōriṣṭappaṭi yamaiyāmaṟ
pōvatumuṇṭu. ivvāṟu muṟkimōti muṉaintaṭittu miṟaik kavikaḷaic collaḷavāl
oruvāṟamaittu muṭitta piṉṉarum avai ciṟantiruppatillai. ēṉeṉṟāl avaṟṟiṉkaṭ
poruṇalamillai. iraṇṭāvatu: kavikaḷiyaṟṟuvatu yāvarkkum payaṉpaṭal vēṇṭumeṉpatu karuti.
akkavikaḷiṉum cila, tiricoṟkaḷā lamaittu viṭṭāṟ paṭippavaruṭ palar tikaippar.
ivvāṟirukkac ceyalpaṭuṅ kavikaḷō miṟaikkavikaḷ; ammiṟait taṉmaiyōṭu ilakkaṇaccantikaḷuñ
ceṟintaṉa. etirpārātapaṭi
coṟkaḷ piḷantu pirittup poruḷ koḷḷak kiṭantaṉa. ippaṭiyeṉṟāl immiṟaik kavikaḷ
yārukkapayōkam? miṟaik kaviñaṉtāṉē makiḻntu
koḷḷataṟku avai oru vēḷai payaṉpaṭalām. 'vakaiyuḷi' eṉṟataṟku
vēṇṭiya utāraṇaṅkaḷ immiṟaik kavikaḷiṟ kāṇalām. mūṉṟāvatu: ittakaiya miṟaikkavikaḷ
paṭippōṉatu kālattaiyum vīṇākkip palaviṭaṅkaḷiṉum avaṉai mayaṅka vaikkiṉṟaṉa. ittaṉmaiyāṉa
iṭarppāṭukaḷ nirampiya miṟaikkavikaḷ nantamiḻp pulavarkaḷ maṉaṅkaḷaik kavarātoḻivaṉavāka.
ivvāṟē muṟkūṟiya pāṭṭiyaluṭ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa poruttaṅkaḷ mutaliyaṉavum, āṉantak kuṟṟam
mutaliyaṉavum nāḷāvaṭṭattiṟ payaṉilavāyk kaḻintupaṭumeṉpatu tōṉṟukiṟatu. anēkar
ikkālattil avaṟṟaik kavaṉippatillai. karunāṭakap pulavarkaḷu iraṇṭoruvar maṭṭil avaṟṟaip
piṭittukkoṇṭu avastaippaṭukiṉṟaṉar. avarkaḷum avaṟṟai viraiviṟ kaiviṭuvārkaḷeṉpatu
collamalē yamaiyum. iṉip 'pattuppāṭṭurai'yil āciriyar nacciṉārkkiṉiyar
"nūṟkuṟṟaṅkūṟukiṉṟa pattuvakaik kuṟṟattē 'taṉṉāṉoru poruḷ karutikkūṟal' eṉṉuṅ
kuṟṟattaip piṉṉuḷḷōr āṉantak kuṟṟameṉpatōr kuṟṟameṉṟu nūl ceytataṉṟi, akattiyaṉārum
tolkāppiyaṉārum ikkuṟṟaṅ kūṟāmaiyiṟ cāṉṟōr ceyyuṭku ikkuṟṟa muṇṭāyiṉuṅ koḷḷā
reṉamaṟukka" eṉṟuraitta vurai yāvaruṅ kavaṉikkattakkatu.
itukāṟum tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ aivakai yilkaṇaṅkaḷaiyum kuṟittuk
kūṟiṉōm. iṉit tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ tōṟṟamum toṉmaiyum paṟṟi viritturaippām.
aivakai ilakkaṇam muṟṟiṟṟu.

V. pāṣaiyiṉ tōṟṟamun toṉmaiyum.

ulakiṉkaṇṇē makkaḷ oruṅkukūṭi vāḻavēṇṭiyavarātaliṉ, avarkaḷ
aṭikkaṭi taṅkaḷ karuttukkaḷai yoruvaraikoruvar veḷiyiṭṭuk poḷvatu avaciyamāyiṟṟu.
ākavē avarkaḷ kaivāy mutaliya uṟuppukkaḷāṟ palavitac caikaikaḷ ceytu kāṭṭit taṅkaḷ

VII. pāṣai vēṟupaṭumāṟu

pēccu vaḻakkuḷḷa pāṣaikaḷellām eppoḻutum palavakaiyilum vēṟupaṭṭuk koṇṭē
yirukkiṉṟaṉa. vēṟupaṭutaliṉṟi yoru nilaippāṭu aṭaintuḷa pāṣaikaḷukku aḻivu
vekutūrattil illai. ākavē pēccu vaḻakkuḷḷa pāṣaikaḷ aḻiyātu nīṭittakālam iyaṅka
vēṇṭumāyiṉ, avaṟṟiṟku vēṟupāṭu iṉṟiyamaiyātatu. tamiḻum pēccu vaḻakkuḷḷatōr
ciṟappuṭaip pāṣaiyāmātaliṉ iḵtum vēṟupaṭṭiyaṅkuvatu niyāyamē. avvāṟē yāreṉṉa

cuḻikoṇṭu viḷaiyāṭic cellā niṉṟatu.

kuṟaivāṉ vaḻakkāṟaṟṟuppōka, maṟṟu ataṉ vaḷarccikkuttaṭai payavātaṉavum payaṉuḷḷuṉavumākiya
yāvaiyum payiṟci mikutiyāṉ vaḻakkāṟṟil nilaipeṟukiṉṟaṉa. itu pāṣai nūliṟ
koḷḷappaṭum 'iyaṟkaip pirinilai'.

coṟkaḷai makkaḷ, kālac curukkamum muyaṟcic curukkamum karutik kuṟukkiyum māṟṟiyum

toṉṟutoṭṭu varutaliṉṟi, avvak kālaṅkaḷil iṭaiyilē cilaveḻuttuk keṭṭum cila

'cuṟṟu' 'nēṟṟu' eṉpaṉa pōṉṟa ṟakara voṟṟiṭaiyiluḷḷa coṟkaḷ, takara voṟṟiṭaiyiluḷḷa

piṟavāyppāṭṭāṟ collum 'iṭakkaraṭakkal vaḻakku'm, maṅkalamillātatai yoḻittu

IX. pāṣaiyiṉ cīrtiruttam.

paravai vaḻakkuḷḷa pāṣaikaḷellām catā iyaṅkik koṇṭiruppataṉālē, taṅkaḷiyakkattiṟkut

pāṣaiyiṉkaṇ ēṟpaṭutal iṉṟiyamaiyātatē.

kaṇṇēyē ekkaruttukkun takkacol akappaṭum. mēṟkūṟiyavāṟu tirunti mutirāta pāṣai
*'pirāṇatāraṇap pirayattaṉa'ttil nilaittu nillātu kālakkiramattil aḻintupaṭum.

* Struggle for existence
tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ vaḷarcci nākarika vaḷarccikkut takkavāṟu amaiyavillai. nākarika vaḷarcciyiṉ
viraiviṉum tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ vaḷarcci viraivu kuṟaivupaṭutalālē iv viraṇṭiṟku muḷḷa
tūram mikuntu koṇṭē pōkiṉṟatu. ittūram iṉimēlum mikavoṭṭātu taṭuttal vēṇṭum.
itukāṟum ēṟpaṭṭuḷḷa tūrattaic ciṟitu ciṟitākak kuṟaittalum vēṇṭum. ivai
ceyyākkāl tamiḻmoḻi aruki aḵkuvatākum. tam tāymoḻiyākiya tamiḻ attaṉmaiyāṉa
nilaimaiyai yaṭaiyātu ataṉaip pōṟṟutal tamiḻ vāṇarāyiṉār yāvarkkum uṟṟa kaṭappāṭaṉṟō?
maṉpataiyiṉ ākkap poruḷākiya pāṣaiyiṉ kaṇṇē, taṉittaṉi makkaḷ karuttukkaḷiṉ pōkkuk
kiṭaittapaṭiyellām coṟkaḷ akappaṭumeṉṟetir pārttal cevvitaṉṟu. avaravar kaṣṭappaṭṭuc
coṟkaḷ paṭaittal vēṇṭum. avai maṟṟaiyōr cuvaikkum ēṟṟaṉavāyk kāṇappaṭiṉ uṭaṉē
mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭup pāṣaiyilē vaḻaṅkuvaṉa vākum allākkāl avaitāmē vīḻntupaṭum. orumuṟai
orukaruttu veḷippaṭṭu viṭṭatāyiṉ, appuṟam kaṣṭamillai. muṉṉarēyuḷḷa
coṟkaḷai,uruvattiṟ ciṟitaḷavu tirittātal ciṟitaḷavu kuṟaittātal putturuvam paṭaittātal
māṟṟit taṅkaruttukaḷai nākarikanilai mutirnta makkaḷ veḷippaṭuttiyē viṭuvārkaḷ.
tamiḻmoḻiyilō yār eṉṉa ceytapōtilum kēḷvimuṟai yillai. avaravar tattamakkut
tōṉṟiyavāṟum vāykkuvantaṉa vantavāṟum eḻutukiṉṟaṉar. ivvāṟu cellaviṭutaluṅ kēṭē.
tamiḻilakkaṇamuṭaiyār muṟpukuntu itaṉaic ciṟitu aṭakkiyāḷalum vēṇṭum. ikkālattiṟ
paṇṭitar tamiḻum pāmarar tamiḻum mikavum vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉa; iruvēṟu pāṣaikaḷeṉat
tōṉṟukiṉṟaṉa. ivviraṇṭiṟkum vēṟupāṭu mikuntukoṇṭēpōmāyiṉ, paṇṭitar tamiḻ
vaṭamoḻiyaip pōlap pēccu vaḻakkaṟṟu ēṭṭuvaḻakkāy maṭṭil niṉṟuviṭum; maṟṟup pāmarar
tamiḻō teluṅkumalaiyāḷaṅkaḷ pōla oru vaḻimoḻiyāy amaintuviṭum.
ātaliṉ tamiḻmoḻi yivvāṟu pirintu pirintu vaḻimoḻikaṭku iṭañ ceytukoṇṭu oru
nilaippāṭeytip payiṟcikuṉṟi āriyampōṟ paravai vaḻakku aḻintoḻintu pōtal virumpāta
tamiḻmakkaḷ tamatu arumaiyāṉa pāṣaiyai vēṇṭiyavaḷavu cīrtiruttikkoḷḷa iṉṉē pukutal
vēṇṭum. iṉṉuṅ kālantāḻkkalākātu.
appaṭiyāṉāl tamiḻmoḻi yuṭaṉē mēṟkoḷḷa vēṇṭiya cīrtiruttaṅkaḷ yāvai? paṇṭitarāyiṉār
tiricoṟkaḷ palavaḻaṅkiyum, arukiya colluruvaṅkaḷai yupayōkittum, ceyyuṭkaḷilum
arukivarum aḷa peṭaikaḷait toṭuttum, virikkavēṇṭiya vēṟṟumai yurupukaḷait;
tokuttum, cāmāṉiya jaṉaṅkaḷ maruṇṭu otuṅkic cellattakkavāṟu eḻutum vaḻakkattai
niṟuttiviṭal vēṇṭum.
oruvarum oṉṟai yeḻuti ataṉait tāmē paṭikka vēṇṭumeṉṟu vaittuk koḷḷamāṭṭār.
eḻutuvōrum paṭippōrum oruvarā yiruttalaritu. ātaliṉ oruvar oṉṟeḻutiṉāl ataṉaip
piṟarellām paṭittaṟintu koḷḷavēṇṭu meṉṟa nōkkattōṭē eḻutukiṉṟā rātal vēṇṭum.
annōkkamē avarkkup pukaḻum poruḷum taravallatu. ākavē piṟarellām paṭittuṇarntu
koḷḷattakka teḷivāṉa naṭaiyileḻutuvatē yāvarum mēṟkoḷḷat takkatu. attaṉmaiyāṉa teḷivu
naṭaikkut tiri coṟkaḷ vēṇṭām; iyaṟ coṟkaḷē pōtum. tappittavaṟi yiraṇṭoṉṟu
vaḻaṅkiviṭṭālum pātakamillai. itupōlavē pūrvāciriyarkaḷuṭaiya nūlkaḷiṟ kāṇappaṭṭu

ivaṟṟāṟ poruḷ mayakkam uṇṭākiṉṟatēyaṉṟi vēṟillai. piṟarai mayaṅka vaikka vēṇṭumeṉpatu

yēṟpaṭum? ēṟpaṭavē paṭātu. utāraṇamāka, 'avaṉ iṅkē taṅkiyiruntu maṟunāṭ pōṉāṉ' eṉṟa
vākkiyattai' aṉṉōṉ īṇṭai iṟut tirukkupu piṟṟai ñāṉṟai yēkiṉaṉ' eṉṟeḻutiṉāṟ
cāmāṉiya jaṉaṅkaḷ aṟintu koḷḷuvārkaḷā? orunāḷum aṟintu koḷḷa māṭṭārkaḷ. oruvēḷai
ceyyuḷeḻutuvatā yiruntāl yāppu nērmaikaruti iraṇṭōr arukiya colluruvaṅkaḷai
vaḻaṅkiṉālum aḵtu iḻukkillai.
ivai pōlavēyām vīṇāka aḷapeṭaikaḷai vaḻaṅkutalum. aḷapeṭaikaḷ yāppu nērmaikkākavē
vakukkappaṭṭaṉa. vacaṉa naṭaiyil yāppu nērmaiyētu? illai. āṉāl paṇṭitareḻutum vacaṉanaṭai
ceyyuṇaṭaiyai yoṭṭiyē naṭattalāl avar ōcai yiṉpaṅ karuti vaḻaṅkapavā-rāyiṉar. ivvaḷapeṭai
vaḻakkum nīṅka vēṇṭum, poruḷiṉ teḷivu karutiṉāl.
ivvāṟē, vēṟṟumai yurupukaḷum piṟavum vākkiyaṅkaḷil virintirā viṭṭāl,
avvākkiyaṅkaḷ neruṅkic ceṟintu karutiya poruḷai āḻtti viṭukiṉṟatu. ittaṉmaiyāṉa
nūlkaḷaip paṭittup poruḷ kāṇa vantavar, avaṟṟiṉ kaṇṇē nuḻaintu mūḻkip poruḷ
kāṇpareṉpateṉṉa niccayam? cātāraṇamākac caṉaṅkaḷ mēlēmutal nōkkattilēyē poruḷ teriyā
viṭṭāl ataṟkākak kaṣṭappaṭukiṟatillai.
ātalāl, yām mēṟkūṟiyavāṟu, kālattiṉarumai karutiyum, yāvarukkum poruḷ
viḷaṅkavēṇṭu meṉpatu karutiyum tamiḻp paṇṭitarkaḷum, pulavarkaḷum, vittuvāṉkaḷum,
nāvalarkaḷum mutaliya yāvarum, teḷivāṉa tamiḻ naṭaiyaik kaippaṟṟi, annaṭaiyilēyē nūlkaḷ
eḻutuvārkaḷāka. paṇṭitarkaḷ tamatu naṭait teḷivāṉāṟ peritum cāmāṉiya caṉaṅkaḷait
tirutti nalvaḻippaṭuttalām. cāmāṉiya caṉaṅkaḷum atika kaṣṭamillāmal eḷitil
tiruntuvar. iruvarkkum uḷḷa voṟṟumai yēṟpaṭum. iruvarum ottiyaṅkuvar. paṇṭitar
nūlkaḷ cāmāṉiya makkaḷāl ātarikkappaṭum. avvāṟu ātarikkappaṭumēṟ paṇṭitarkaṭku
kuṟaivēyillai.
ivviṣayattil āṅkilavāṇaraip pārttu nām poṟāmaip paṭutal vēṇṭum.

eṉṟapaṭi āṅkila nūlvāṇaraip pārttāvatu tamiḻ vāṇarum cāmāṉiya caṉaṅkaḷai yoṭṭic ceṉṟu
avarkaḷaik kaiviṭātu paṟṟikkoṇṭu tirutti, avarkaḷaiyum makiḻvittut tāmum
makiḻvārkaḷāka.

eṉṟa tiruvaḷḷuvar vākku orupoḻutum maṟakkaṟ pālataṉṟu.
iṉit tamiḻp pulavarkaḷ tām eḻutum nūlkaḷiṟ canti cērttē eḻutukiṉṟaṉar.
canticērtteḻutalē tamiḻilakkaṇa marapāyiṉum vacaṉa naṭaiyilum avvāṟu eḻututal
vēṇṭumeṉṟa niyamamillai. muṟkālattē ēṟpaṭṭa urai nūlkaḷilum, muṟṟum
canticērtteḻutiyirukkakkāṇōm ikkālattilum paṇṭitar palar cilaviṭaṅkaḷil
canticērttum cila viṭaṅkaḷil canti cērāmalum eḻuti varukiṉṟaṉar. ātaliṉ kaṭiṉa
cantikaḷai eppoḻutum piritteḻutuka. poruḷ mayakkamāvatu, poruḷ vēṟu pāṭāvatu
uṇṭākka valla cantikaḷaic cērāmal pirittē eḻutuka. canti cērāmaiyāṟ poruḷ
keṭuvatāyiruntāl avviṭtiṟ canticērt teḻutuka. eḷiya cantikaḷaic cērkkiṉum
cērāviṭiṉum oṉṟutāṉ. eḷiya cantikaḷaic cērppataṉāṟ cāmāṉiya caṉaṅkaḷukkup paṭippatiṟ
kaṣṭappaṭa vēṇṭiya-toṉṟumillai. naṉṟu, ivvitikaḷellām vacaṉa naṭaiyil anucarikkat
taruvaṉavākum; maṟṟun tamiḻc ceyyuṇaṭaiyilum ivai taḻuvat takkaṉavō? itu ciṟitu vātam
viḷaikkat takkatōr viṣayam. ceyyuḷiṉkaṇ ivai mēṟkoḷḷappaṭumēl, ceyyuḷai ōcaiyūṭṭi
icaiyaṟuttup
paṭikkumpōtu iṭarppāṭu kāṇappaṭum. ātalāl canticērttu icai nalaṅkeṭāmal oru
pāṭantanta piṉṉar cantipirittup piṟitoru pāṭam ataṉaṭiyil tarutal vēṇṭum.
ākavē cantipirit teḻutum viṣayattil evvaḷavu maṭṭil iṭarp pāṭiṉṟi mēṟ koḷḷalāmō
avvaḷavaiyum mēṟkoḷḷavē vēṇṭum. itaṉ kaṇṇē tamiḻvāṇar kālam pōkkaṟka.
iṉi āṅkilam mutaliya piṟa pāṣaikaḷilē mikavum pirayōcaṉa muḷḷatākak kāṇappaṭukiṉṟa
'kuṟiyīṭṭi lakkaṇam' tamiḻiṉ kaṇ muḻuvatun taḻuvikkoḷḷappaṭal vēṇṭum. ataṉāṟ
poruṭṭeḷivum viraivuṇarcci uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. ivai kāraṇamākap paṭittāṉukkup paṭitta
nūliṉ kaṇ ārvamuṇṭākiṉṟatu. ikkuṟiyīṭ ṭilakkaṇamellān tamiḻ vacaṉa naṭai kaivanta
vallāḷarākiya srīlasrī āṟumuka nāvalavarkaḷāṉē muṉṉē mēṟkoṇṭu vaḻaṅkappaṭṭuḷa. itaṉai vacaṉa
naṭaiyiṉkaṇ ākṣēpippā revarumillai. ceyyuṇaṭaiyilētāṉ uḷḷa vampellām. itaṉaic
ceyyuṇaṭaiyil oruvāṟu mēṟkoḷḷalā meṉṟu tuṇintu pukuntālum, kuṟṟiyalukarampōlvaṉa
varumiṭattu iṭarppāṭu kāṇa vēṇṭiyatākiṉṟatu. ataṉaiyum oruvakait talaikkuṟiyāṟ
kaṉamporuntiya ji.yū. pōppaiyar avarkaḷum, uṭukkuṟiyāṟ kālañceṉṟa tiruvaṉantaic
cuntaram piḷḷaiyavarkaḷum vilakkik koṇṭaṉar.
iṉi, uḷḷavāṟē uṟṟunōkkumiṭattuk kuṟiyīṭ ṭilakkaṇattaip paṟṟi ākṣēpittaṟkut takka
niyāyaṅkaḷumillai. ākṣēpikku miyalpuṭaiyārum ākṣēpikka villai. ātaliṉ atu
viraivil mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭu muṟṟilum payilappaṭum eṉpatiṟ cantēkamillai. ataṉāl
tamiḻiṟku vicēṣa naṉmaikaḷ viḷaiyumeṉpatilun taṭaiyillai.
tamiḻmakkaḷ āṅkilarōṭu nāṭoṟum ūṭāṭupavarāyiṉar. āṅkilar āḷvōruma tamiḻar
āḷappaṭuvōrumā yirukkiṉṟaṉar.

X. muṭivurai.

itukāṟum oṉpatu upanniyāsaṅkaḷilum yāṅ kūṟi vanta viṣayaṅkaḷait tavirttu av
vupanniyācat talaip peyarkaḷoṭu poruntātu, eñciya cila viṣayaṅkaḷaippaṟṟi iv
vupanniyācattiṟ kūṟit tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ varalāṟṟiṟkum ōrāṟṟāṉ muṭivurai kūṟuvām.
yāṅ kūṟum immuṭivarai 'tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ varalāṟu' eṉṟa vinnūliṟkē yaṉṟit
tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ varalāṟṟikaṉṟeṉavuṇarka. tamiḻ moḻi vaḷarntu mutirntukoṇṭē cellum
pāṣaiyām. ātalāl innūlum muṭinta nūlaṉṟeṉpatu tēṟṟam. itu tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ
vaḷarccikkēṟpa vaḷaravēṇṭuvatu.
ikkālattil āṅkila pāṣaikaṟṟa tamiḻmakkaḷiṟ palar tamatu tāymoḻiyākiya tamiḻaip
paṭikkavēṇṭuvatu āvaciyamaṉṟeṉṟum, tamakku vēṇṭiya viṣayaṅkaḷ yāvum āṅkilattilēyē
akappaṭukiṉṟaṉa veṉṟum palatiṟappaṭak kūṟuvar. avar āṅkila moḻic ciṟappumaṭṭiṟ
kūṟiyamaivatē naṉṟākumaṉṟit tamiḻkaṟkavēṇṭiya tavaciyamaṉṟu eṉpatu aṟiyāmaiyoṭu kūṭiya
tuṇinturaiyām. itu cutēcāpimāṉamum cupāṣāpimāṉamuma maṟṟavar kūṟṟām. ivviraṇṭum
mikka āṅkilar ivviraṇṭum aṟṟavarkaḷai evvāṟu matippārō? āṅkilam mutaliya
piṟapāṣaikkuriyarāy appāṣaikaḷ vallunarellām tamiḻmoḻiyiṉmī tārvamuṭaiyāraiyt tamiḻaik
kaṟṟum tamiḻnūlkaḷait taṅkaḷ pāṣaikaḷil moḻipeyarttuk koḷḷutalai uṟṟu nōkki yātal
tamiḻk kalvi avaciyamaṉṟu eṉpārkkup puttivaraṭṭum.
pāṣaiyaṟivu piṟitoru payaṉukkuk karuviyākumē yaṉṟi, atutāṉē payaṉākātu.

campāṣaṇaikaḷilāvatu cāturiyamākap pēcuvōr pāṣaiyaṟivuṭaiyōrē yaṉṟic cāsatirak
kalviyuṭaiyārallar. cāstirak kalviyuṭaiyār eṅkē pōṉālun taṅkaḷ kaṭaiyai virikkap
pukuvārēyaṉṟi vēṟaṉṟu. cāstirak kalviyuṭaiyārkkup pāṣaiyaṟivum iṉṟiyamaiyātatē.
taṅkaḷ karuttukkaḷāl ulakamaṉaittilum iṉpavuṇarcci yeḻuppa valla nallicaip pulavarkaḷ ceyta
pāmuṟaikaḷaip paṭittalāluṇṭākum payaṉ aḷaviṭaṟpālataṉṟu; makkaḷiṭattil aṭaṅkik kiṭakkum
manō vikāraṅkaḷai eḻuppic cuvai payakkum.
iṉi yitu niṟka. tamiḻnāṭṭil āṅkila varacāṭci yēṟpaṭṭa piṉṉart tāymoḻiyākiya tamiḻai
ataṉvaḻi moḻikaḷākiya teluṅku kaṉṉaṭa malaiyāḷatuḷuvaṅkaḷōṭu aṭakki 'uṇṇāṭṭumoḻikaḷ'

utaviceytu varukiṉṟateṉpatu yāvarumuṇarnta viṣayam; mēṟkūṟiyavāṟu pala caṅkaṅkaḷ
cērvataṟkuk kaikoṭuttum kaṭaik kaṇittum parīkṣaip pāṭa nūlkaḷuḷ tamiḻ nūlkaḷ palavaṟṟai
yēṟpaṭuttiyum āṅkilar tamiḻaip pōṟṟukiṉṟaṉar. acciyantiram mutaliyaṉa avar
kaṇṭupiṭittut tantutaviṉar; tamiḻaratu nākarīka viruttikkun taṉipperuṅ karuviyāy
irukkiṉṟaṉar. kaiyeḻuttu nūṟcālaiyum amaittirukkiṉṟaṉar. ivarkaḷatu aracu nīṭūḻi
nilaittut tamiḻmoḻiyai mēṉmēlum vaḷarppatāka.
pāṣai tirintu vēṟupaṭuvatellām taṉṉiyalpākavēyām. oruvaṉālun tāṉ virumpiyapaṭi,
tāṉpēcum pāṣaiyai māṟṟamuṭiyātu. makkaḷaṟiviṉṟiyē pāṣai mutirntukoṇṭucellum;
pallāṇṭukaḷ kaḻinta piṉṉarē mutirccik kuṟikaḷ tōṉṟum. pāṣaiyiṉiyalpu ivvāṟāka,
itaṉai yaṟiyap peṟāta vaittiyanāta tēcikar naṉṉūlāratu uṭkaruttaiyum uṇarātu,

eṉṟa naṉṉūṟ cūttira vitiyaip,

eṉṟu māṟṟit tamatu ilakkaṇaviḷakkam eṉṟa nūliṉkaṭ kūṟiṉar; eṉavē tamiḻp pāṣaiyiṉ
oḻukalāṟṟai māṟṟutal evarālum muṭiyātatām. ātalāl tamiḻp pāṣaiyiṉ oḻukalāṟu
iṉṉateṉṟu uyttuṇarntukoṇṭu, āṟṟu veḷḷattiṉ vēkattuṭaṉ celvāṉpōla, avvoḻuka
lāṟṟiṉai yoṭṭi ataṉōṭiyaintu tamiḻp pulavarun tam pāṣaiyaic cīr tiruttik
koḷḷavēṇṭum.
iṉic cilar 'putiyaṉa pukutal tamiḻiṟku okkumāyiṉum paḻaiyaṉa kaḻital ovvātu' eṉṟa
koḷkaiyuṭaiyārpōlat tām eḻutum nūlkaḷiṉkaṇ paḻaiyaṉa kaḻinta coṟkaḷilum
colluruvaṅkaḷilum aḷaviṟantavaṟṟai vaḻaṅkukiṉṟaṉar. ivar ceyal tammuṭaiya vallamai kāṭṭip
paṭippōrai maruṭṭit tammaip paṟṟi avarakaḷ nalleṇṇam koḷḷa vēṇṭumeṉṟu niṉaittuc
ceytatākak karutappaṭum. paṭalāl ivar tām eṇṇiyapaṭi naṉku matikkaṟpāṭu peṟuvatillai.
cāmāṉiya caṉaṅkaḷ iṉṉār nūlkaḷait toṭavum añcukiṉṟaṉar. mēlum iṅṅaṉa-meḻutuvār cuvai
pōliccuvai yeṉap palarum otukkuvar.
'tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ varalāṟu' eṉat talaippeyariṭṭu ematu ārāyccikkaṇ nērnta pala
viṣayaṅkaḷaiyum pattu upanniyācaṅkaḷiṟ kūṟi ōrāṟṟāṉ muṭittām. tamiḻmoḻiyum, tamiḻ
makkaḷum nīṭūḻi vāḻka.
vāḻttu.
kali viruttam.

tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ varalāṟu muṟṟiṟṟu.

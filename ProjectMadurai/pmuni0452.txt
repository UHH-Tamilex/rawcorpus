INTRODUCTION
-------
THOUGH my knowledge of the Tamil Language is so scanty that at first I
held myself absolutely disqualified from complying with Mr.
Suryanarayana Sastri's request that I would write an introduction to
this book, yet on his pressing it I could not deny myself the pleasure
of complying with his desire and showing my interest in the excellent
work which he has here done for his native tongue. It has been a
privilege to talk with him over his book and to see how he has
assimilated the methods of Western philologists and has applied them to
the study of the Dravidian languages. While however he has found his
models in the west, no one can fail to see that his inspiration comes
from his love of Tamil and that he has put himself into the book, with
the result that it is fresh and original.
That the book meets a need is certin. As a study of the growth of
language in general from Dravidian point of view it is a contribution to
Philology of notable value; while as a history of the Tamil Language in
particular it stands alone. And Tamil is a language which well deserves
such study. It belongs to a group which has a strongly marked family
type, and which, while it has features that seem to connect it, on this
side and on that, with widely differing groups, is also distinguished
from all other groups by features all its own. Its origin still remins
to be discovered -- a secret to reward some scholar patiently treading
in the path along which this book points the way. Its grammar is full of
interest and its literature is one which any nation might be proud. It
still has a vigorous life as spoken tongue, and when its emancipation
from the tyranny of the conventions of a dead past is complete it will
regin its literary productiveness. For a long time at any rate it will
be the medium in which the movements and the emotions of the Tamilians
will find their real and natural expression. It must continue the
language of the genuine literature of the people. In English their
thought and feeling will move as it were in shackles; in Tamil they will
have the spontaneity and ease and vigour of free movement.
I am not competent to pronounce upon all the detils of Mr Suryanarayana
Sastriar's work, but I am convinved that the min lines on which it is
constructed are right. The patient work which I know that he has devoted
to the subject for years and his mastery of the language both as scholar
and as poet are a sufficient guarantee that his illustration of the
principles is wide and sufficently correct.
In the study of Tamil in our Collleges the book will mark an epoch. For
it puts that study upon a better basis, and will begin to do for it what
such writers as Trench and Morris and Skeat did for English a generation
or more ago. The author has by it gined for himself the distinction of
being the pioneer in a field into which many will doubtless follow him.
But none of his successors are likely to excel him in the real
usefulness of their work or in their love of the language whose past he
here illumines.
F. W. KELLETT.

taṉṉic cīrppaṭuttikkoṇṭatu. itaṉālaṉṟō 'paḻaṅkaṉṉaṭam' eṉṟum 'putukkaṉṉaṭam' eṉṟum
aḵtu iru vēṟu piriviṉatāki yiyaṅkukiṉṟatu. maḻaṅkaṉṉaṭattit tamiḻiṉiṉṟum
piṟantateṉak kūṟuṅ kaṉṉaṭappulavar palar iṉṟumuḷar.
iṉi maliyāḷamō vekunāḷkāṟun tiruntātiruntatu. iṟutiyil ēṟkukaṟiya
munnūṟṟiyāṇṭukaṭku muṉṉar 'eḻuttaccaṉ' eṉpā-ṉoruvaṉāl mikkat tiruttappāṭu
aṭinttu; uṭaṉē vaṭamoḻic coṟkaḷiyuñ coṟṟoṭarkaḷiyum cantikaḷiyum muṭipukaḷiyum
maliyāḷam mēṟkoṇṭatu.
mēṟkūṟiya teluṅku kaṉṉaṭam maliyāḷa meṉṉum mūṉṟu vaḻimoḻikaḷum vaṭanūl yāppiyum
aṇiyiyu muṭaṉ mēṟkoṇṭu iyaṅkap pukuntaṉa.
iṉit tamiḻmoḻiyum kalantiyaṅku māṟṟip paṟṟic ciṟitu viritturippām.
ātivaralāṟu muṟṟiṟṟu.

II. vaṭamoḻik kalappu.

vaṭamoḻi tamiḻnāṭṭil vekunāḷ kāṟum iyaṅkiyum ataṟkut tamiḻ moḻiyit taṉ vaḻiyilē
tiruppik koḷḷutaṟkuṟṟa āṟṟalillātu pōyiṟṟu. vaṭamoḻiyāḷar tamiḻarkaḷatu oḻukka
vaḻakkaṅkaḷi yuṇarntu avaṟṟiṟkēṟpa vaṭamoḻiyil nūlkaḷ vakuppāṉ pukuntaṉar. avarkaḷellām
āṉmanūṟ payiṟci mikkuṭiyārāyum, kaliyuṇarcci cāṉṟavarāyu miruntamipaṟṟit tamiḻaratu
tivviya stalaṅkaḷukkup purāṇaṅkaḷ vakuttaṉar; tamiḻarkaḷiṭattillā-tirunta 'antaṇar,
aracar, vaṇikar, vēḷāḷar' eṉṟa nālvakiccāti muṟiyi mellamella nāṭṭiviṭṭaṉar.

eṉṟu āriyari nōkki muḻaṅkum 'kapilarakava'liyuṅ kāṇka. iṉṉuma avartam puntinalaṅ
kāṭṭit tamiḻaracarkaḷiṭam, amiccarka ḷeṉavum mēlatikārap pirapukkaḷeṉavum amintu
koṇṭaṉar; tamiḻariṭattirunta pala ariya viṣayaṅkaḷiyum moḻi peyarttut tamaḻar
aṟiyumuṉṉarē avaṟṟit tāmaṟintaṉa pōlavum, vaṭamoḻiyiṉiṉṟumē tamiḻiṟku avi vantaṉa
pōlavum kāṭṭiṉar.
tamiḻaruṭ cāmāṉiya caṉaṅkaḷ avvāriyaratu viruppattiṟkēṟpa evvaḷaviṇaṅkiya pōtilum,
pulavarāyiṉār avarkaḷatu tiruttap pāṭṭiṟkup peritu miṇaṅkiṉarallar. oḻukkac cīrppāṭu
ēṟpaṭṭapōtiṉum pāṣit tiruttam ēṟpaṭavilli. tamiḻiṉ muppattōreḻuttukkaḷumavvaṟē
yiṉṟaḷavu mirukkiṉṟaṉa; ciṟitum vēṟupaṭavilli. tāṅkaḷ cellumiṭaṅkaḷukkut takkapaṭi
putiya putiya ilipikaḷ ēṟpaṭuttik koḷḷumiyalpuṭiya āriyar tamiḻnāṭṭiṟkēṟṟapaṭi
tamiḻilipiyi yoṭṭik 'kirantam eṉṉum peyariṟ putuvatōr ilipivakuttaṉar; tamiḻari
vacīkarikkumāṟu avvilipiyiṟ pala nūlkaḷ varintaṉar. tamiḻp pulavarāvār etaṟkum
aciyātu taṅkaḷ tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ pōkkiyē taḻuvic celvārāyiṉār.
ivvāṟu tamiḻaruṭ paṇṭitarāyāṉār vaṭamoḻiḻit tamiḻiṉkaṇ viravavoṭṭātu vilakkiyum,
pāmararāyiṉār vaṭamoḻic coṟkaḷuṭ palavaṟṟi mēṟkoṇṭu vaḻaṅkap pukuntamiyiṉ
nāḷāvaṭṭattil vaṭa coṟkaḷ pala tamiḻp pāṣiyiṉkaṇṇē vērūṉṟiviṭṭaṉa. avvāṟāyiṉ
itupōlavē vaṭamoḻiyiṉ kaṇṇum tamiḻc coṟkaḷ pala ceṉṟu cērntiruttal vēṇṭumaṉṟō?
atiyum ārāyvām.
vaṭamoḻi tamiḻ moḻiyoṭu kalakkap pukumuṉṉarē, muṉṉatu pēccuvaḻakkaṟṟu ēṭṭuvaḻakkāy
maṭṭilirukkum nilimikku vantuviṭṭatu. ēṭṭuvaḻak koṉṟumēyuḷḷa pāṣiyōṭu iruvaki
vaḻakkamuḷḷa pāṣiyoṉṟu kūṭi yiyaṅkap pukumāyiṉ muṉṉataṉ coṟkaḷē piṉṉataṉkaṭ ceṉṟu
cērumēyaṉṟip piṉṉataṉ coṟkaḷ muṉṉataṉ kaṭceṉṟu cērā. itu pāṣi nūliṉ
uṇmikaḷḷoṉṟu. ituvē vaḻakkāṟṟu muṟi. immuṟi paṟṟiyē vaṭacoṟkaḷ pala tamiḻiṉkaṭ
pukuntaṉa. tamiḻc coṟkaḷiṟ cilatāmum vaṭamoḻiyiṉkaṇ ēṟāmaṟ pōyiṉa. eṉiṉum teṉṉāṭṭu
vaṭamoḻiyāḷar maṭṭiṟ cilar ūrppeyar, malip peyar, yāṟṟuppeyar mutalāyiṉavaṟṟit taṅkaḷ
capta cāstiratiṟ kiyinta vaṇṇam, ōci vēṟupāṭu ceytukoṇṭu tāṅkaḷ vakukkap pukunta
purāṇātikaḷil vaḻaṅkuvārāyiṉar. itutāṉuṇmi; itaṟkumē loṉṟuñ colla iyalātu.
iṉit tamiḻp pulavarkaḷāyiṉār, camskirutac coṟkaḷi evvaḷavō vilakkippārttum avaṟṟi
vilakkutal muṭiyātu pōyiṟṟu. pōkavē tamiḻppulavarkaḷun taṅkaḷ muyaṟcikaḷellām vīṇātal
kaṇṭu vēṇṭā veṟuppāyt tamakku vēṇṭiya ciṟcila coṟkaḷi maṭṭil taṅkaḷ eḻuttilakkaṇa
vitikaṭkut takkavāṟu tirittu mēṟkoḷvārāyiṉār; āriyac coṟkaḷ tamiḻil varuvataṟkēṟṟa
vitikaḷum vakuttaṉar. piṉṉark koñcaṅ koñcamāka vaṭa coṟkaḷ pala tamiḻ moḻiyiṉkaṇ iṭam
peṟuvaṉa vāyiṉa.
ataṉmēl mutaliṭi kaṭiyeṉum muccaṅkattār kālattilum vaṭa coṟkaḷ tamiḻ nūlkaḷi lēṟiṉa.
āyiṉum avi ciṟitaḷavēyām. 'tolkāppiyam' eṉṉum ilakkaṇattiṉuḷḷum 'eṭṭuttoki,
pattuppāṭṭu, patiṉeṇkīḻkkaṇakku' eṉṉum nūṟṟokikaḷuḷḷum āṅkāṅku iraṇṭoru vaṭacoṟ
kāṇappaṭalāmēyaṉṟi, ataṟkumēlilli.
piṉṉarp puttarāyiṉār taliyeṭuttut tam matatti yāṇṭum parappip pallāyirak kaṇakkākac
caṉaṅkaḷic cērttukkoṇṭu akkālattirunta āriyari yetirttaṉar. ippakimi teṉṉāṭṭilum
paraviṟṟu. paravavē tamiḻaruṭ palar puttamatam mēṟkoṇṭu āriyari yetirppatil nōkka
muṟṟiruntaṉar. akkālattil maṟupaṭiyum tamiḻppulavarkaḷ taṅkaḷāṟ kūṭiya maṭṭil
vaṭacoṟkaḷit tamatu tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ kaṇṇē kalakka voṭṭātu taṭuttaṉar. muṉṉarē tamiḻiṟ
pōntu vērūṉṟiviṭṭa, vaṭacoṟkaḷit tolippatu avarkaṭkup peruṅ kaṣṭamāyviṭṭatu. ātaliṉ
avarkaḷ eṉ ceyya vallar? muṉṉarē vantaṉapōka, iṉimēlātal appollāta vaṭacoṟkaḷ tamiḻiṉkaṇ
vārātavāṟu pātukāttal vēṇṭumeṉṟu ciṟitu kālam muyaṉṟaṉar. avvāṟē ivarkaḷatu
viṭāmuyaṟciyāṟ ciṟitu kālam vaṭacoṟkaḷ tamiḻil atikamāy vantu kalavāmalumiruntaṉa.
ikkālattilē tāṉ 'centamiḻ' 'koṭuntamiḻ' eṉat tamiḻ iru piriviṉatāki yiyaṅkap
pukuntatu. centamiḻāvatu pulavarāyiṉār payilun tamiḻ; koṭuntamiḻāvatu pulavarallāta
cāmāṉiya makkaḷ payiluntamiḻ. ivaṟṟik kuṟittup piritōr amayattiṟ pēcuvām.
iṉi ettuṇi nāḷ mēṉmēleḻum veḷḷattittaṭuttuk koṇṭiruttal iyalum? ātalāṟ
puttaratu muyaṟciyā lēṟpaṭṭa karikaḷ āriyapāṣiyiṉ alikaḷāl eṟṟuṇṭu aḻivaṉavāyiṉa.
puttacamaya muntali tāḻntatu.
innilimiyiṟ ciṉar eḻuntaṉar. aṅṅaṉameḻunta ciṉar āriyaratu ācāraṅkaḷuṭ palavaṟṟi
mēṟkoṇṭaṉar; aḵtaṉṟiyum vaṭamoḻiyiṉ kaṇ vicēṣa kurava muṭiyarāy ataṉip peritum
payilvārāyiṉar. vaṭamoḻiyiṉ pākataṅkaḷiyum avaṟṟi ṉilakkaṇaṅkaḷiyum naṉkuṇarntu
koṇṭaṉar; ittakiya ciṉap pulavarkaḷ tamiḻ moḻiyiyum appiyacikkap pukuntu, tamiḻiṉ
kaṇṇē aḷaviṟanta vaṭacoṟkaḷi yēṟṟiṉar.
attuṇiyōṭamiyātu 'maṇippiravāḷam' eṉṟatōr putiya pāṣi vakuttuviṭṭaṉar. aḵtāvatu
teṉmoḻiyum vaṭamoḻiyum carikkuccari kalantapāṣiyām. maṇiyum pavaḷamuṅ kalantu kōttatōr
māli kāṭcik kiṉpam payattal pōlat tamiḻuñ camskirutamuṅ kalanta pāṣi kēḷvik kiṉpam
payakkumeṉṟa pōliyeṇṇamē ittakiya āpāca pāṣiyoṉṟu vakukkumāṟu tūṇṭiṟṟu.
'śrīpurāṇam' eṉṉum ciṉa nūl muḻuvatum maṇippiravāḷa meṉṉum ivvāpāca naṭiyiṉ iyaṉṟa
tāmāṟu kāṇka.
ivvāṟu ciṉar orupuṟañ ceytukoṇṭu cellāniṟka, maṟṟoru puṟattil āriyappulavar cilar
tamiḻmoḻiyi yappiyacittukkoṇṭu nītimārkkattiyum camaya cāstiraṅkaḷiyum tamiḻarkkup
pōtippēmeṉap pukuntu, tamatu karuttukkaḷi yellām mēṟkūṟiya maṇippiravāḷa pāṣiyil
veḷippaṭut turippārāyiṉar. 'nālāyirappirapantam' eṉṟa tamiḻnūliṟku
viyākkiyāṉaṅkaḷum ittakiya maṇippiravāḷa naṭiyil vakukkappaṭṭiruttal kāṇka. ivvaṇṇam
palatiṟattālum vaṭacoṟkaḷ vantu tamiḻiṉkaṇ aḷaviṉṟi yēṟiṉa.
tamiḻiṉkaṇuḷḷa 'iraṭṭikkiḷavi' yeṉpatu orupuṟamirukka, 'iraṭṭippatam' eṉpatoru
vakiyum maṟṟorupuṟatti lēṟpaṭā niṉṟatu. orē col nērē vaṭamoḻiyiṉiṉṟum
tamiḻiṟpōntu, vaḻaṅkuvataṉṟiyum, vaṭa moḻiyiṉ pākata vaḻiyākavum vantu tamiḻil
vaḻaṅkukiṉṟatu. utāraṇamāka, 'viññāpaṉam' eṉpatu nērē vaṭamoḻiyiṉiṉṟum pōntacol;
'viṇṇappam' eṉpatu vaṭamoḻiyiṉ pākata vaḻiyāka vanta col. ivviraṇṭiṟkum poruḷoṉṟē.
ivvāṟu vēṇṭāc coṟkaḷum tamiḻiṉ kaṇēṟiṉa, ēṟukiṉṟaṉa, ēṟum!
tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ nilimi yivvāṟātalum, vaṭamoḻi yilakkaṇattik kalantu tamiḻilakkaṇamum
vakukkap pukuntu viṭṭaṉar cilar. 'tolkāppiya'ttiṟku urivakutta aivaruḷ mutaṉālvarum
ēṟakkuṟiyat tamiḻp pōkkiyē paṟṟi yurivakuttuc ceṉṟaṉarākac cēṉāvariya roruvarmaṭṭil
vaṭamoḻippōkkic ciṟitaḷavu kalantu collatikārattiṟku uri vakuttaṉar.
taṉittamiḻnūlākiya 'tirukkuṟa' ḷiṟkup parimēlaḻakarum, vaṭanūlār matam paṟṟiyē
yurivakuttēkiṉar. 'naṉṉūl' ceyta pavaṇantiyārum, 'kiṉṉūl' ceyta kuṇavīrapaṇṭitarum
vaṭanūlilakkaṇap pōkkit taḻuvuvā rāyiṉar. 'vīracōḻiya'mum ataṉ uriyumō colla
vēṇṭuvaṉavalla. ivi vicēṣamāy vaṭamoḻi yilakkaṇaṅkaḷit tamiḻiṉkaṭ pukuttuvaṉavāyiṉa. ataṉ
mēṟ 'pirayōkavivēkam' eṉṉum ilakkaṇa nūlvakutta cuppiramaṇiya tīkṣitar vaṭamoḻic
cattacāttirattit tamiḻiṉkaṭkūṟit tamiḻmoḻiyō ṭoppiṭṭuc cīrtūkki yārāyvārāyiṉar.
piṉṉar 'ilakkaṇakkotturi' ceyta īcāṉatēcikar vaṭamoḻi yilakkaṇattit tamiḻoṭu kalantaṉar;
kalantamaṭṭil nillātu tamatu nūṟpāyirattiṉkaṭ kūṟiya cila kūṟṟukkaḷ aṟivuṭiyōr
otukkaṟpālaṉa vākiṉṟaṉa.

eṉṟa viviyaṉittum 'pāṣi nūl' eṉṉum ariya cāstirattil payiṟciyum carittirakkaṇṇum
illāta kuṟiviṉāleḻunta poruntāk kūṟṟukkaḷā meṉpatu tiṇṇam. ivi kēṭṭup
pēraṟivāḷarāyiṉār nakittu viṭuppareṉpatu niccayam. ivar tēṟṟēkāra miraṭṭittuk
kūṟiyataṟku māṟāka, āciriyar civañāṉa cuvāmikaḷ tamatu 'tolkāppiyap
pāyiravirutti'yilē "tamiḻmoḻip puṇarccikkaṭpaṭuñ ceykikaḷuṅ kuṟiyīṭukaḷum,
viṉikkuṟippu viṉittoki mutaliya cila collilakkaṇaṅkaḷum, uyartiṇi aḵṟiṇi mutaliya
coṟpākupāṭukaḷum, akampuṟameṉum poruṭpāku pāṭukaḷum, kuṟiñci veṭṭu mutaliya
tiṇip pākupāṭukaḷum, avaṟṟiṉ pakutikaḷum, veṇpā mutaliya ceyyuḷilakkaṇamum,
iṉṉōraṉṉa piṟavum vaṭamoḻiyiṟ peṟappaṭā" eṉṟu kūṟuvarāyiṉar. itu yāvarum
naṉkuṇarntatu; tamiḻmoḻiyum vēṟē; vaṭamoḻiyum vēṟē; itaṉkaṇ aiyappāṭu eḷḷaḷavu
milli.
naṉṉūlār tamatu pataviyaliṉiṟṟilum meyyīṟṟup puṇariyaliṉīṟṟilum vaṭacollākkamum vaṭacoṟ
cantiyum muṟiyē kūṟuvārāyiṉar.
iṉic camskiruta moḻiyiṉ kaṇṇē pālvakuppuc coṉṉōkkat tālēṟpaṭṭuḷatēyaṉṟip
poruṇōkkattā lēṟpaṭṭilatu. utāraṇamākak, kiyeṉap poruḷpaṭum 'karam' eṉṟacol āṇpāl;
maṉiviyeṉap poruḷpaṭuñ coṟkaḷilē, 'tāram' eṉpatu āṇpāl, 'kaḷattiram' eṉpatu
alippāl, aḵtāvatu oṉṟaṉpāl eṉa ōrāṟṟāṉamikkalām. ivvāṟuḷatu vaṭamoḻip pāl
vakuppiṉ ciṟappu. maṟṟut tamiḻmoḻiyilō pāl vakuppellām poru ṇōkkattālēṟ
paṭṭuḷavē yaṉṟic coṉṉōkkattā leṟpaṭavēyilli. itu tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ ciṟappukkaḷuḷ oṉṟu.
vaṭanūṉmuṟi kuṟipāṭuṭiyatu. āriya pāṣikaḷō ṭiyipupaṭṭa pāṣikaḷellām coṉṉōkkap pāl
vakuppuk kuṟipāṭuṭiyaṉavāmāṟu kāṇka. tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ vaḻimoḻika ḷellām poruṇōkkap
pālvakuppuc ciṟappuṭiyaṉa. ivvuṇmi yoṉṟē tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ taṉi niliyi naṉku
viḷaṅkuvataṟkut takka cāṉṟu pakarum.

eṉṟa naṉṉūṟ cūttiravitiyiṟ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa aṇi nalaṉ āriyapāṣic coṟkaḷukkup
perumpālum illātu pōtal kāṇka. aṇi nūṟ pulavar muṉṉar āriyapāṣikku aḵtō
riḻukkāmumaṉṟē?
akapporuḷum ataṉ ṟuṟikaḷum, puṟapporuḷum ataṉ ṟuṟikaḷum, ivviruvakip
poruḷkaḷiṉiyipukaḷum vaṭamoḻiyiṉiṉṟum eṉṟeṉṟum kiṭittaliyalāta ariya taṉit tamiḻ
viṣayaṅkaḷām. ivaṟṟiṉiṉṟum ātiyilirunta tamiḻarkaḷatu oḻukka nili ittaṉmit teṉpatum,
avarkaḷatu nākarika nili ittaṉmit teṉpatum naṉku viḷaṅkukiṉṟaṉa.
vaṭamoḻiyiṉ 'ilaku kuru kaṇayati viruttam' eṉṟa pākupāṭukaṭkut tamiḻiliṭamē-yilli.
tamiḻiṉ nālvakip pākkaḷum mūvakip pāliṉamum, avaṟṟiṉ pākupāṭukaḷum, etuki mōṉi
niyamaṅkaḷum, aci, cīr, taḷi ākiyavaṟṟiṉi-yalpukaḷum tamiḻmoḻikkē yuriyaṉa. ivi
vaṭamoḻikkaṭ kāṇappaṭuvaṉavalla. tamiḻiṟ 'pā' eṉṟata ṉilakkaṇamē mikavum iṉimi peṟa
vamintuḷatu. 'pāveṉpatu, cēṭpulattirunta kālattum, oruvaṉ eḻuttuñ collun
teriyāmaṟ pāṭamōtuṅkāl, avaṉ collukiṉṟa ceyyuḷi vikaṟpittu iṉṉa ceyyuḷeṉṟu
uṇartaṟ kētuvākip parantu paṭṭuc colvatōr ōci" eṉṟār āciriyar nacciṉārkkiṉiyar.
itu vaṭamoḻiyāḷarkkup putumiyām.
tolkāppiyaṉār yāppaṇikaḷip poruḷatikārattilēyē aṭakkic cūttirañ ceytu ceṉṟaṉar.
ataṉkaṇ 'aṇiyiyal' eṉṟa peyarē kāṇappaṭātu, 'uvamaviyal' eṉṟa toṉṟu ataṟkup
piratiyākak kāṇappaṭukiṉṟatu. avvuvamaviyal tāṉum ikkālatti lulavukiṉṟa aṇinūlkaḷ
pōlātu mikac curuṅkiyatāyc ciṟcila potu tarumaṅkaḷi maṭṭil virittuk kūṟi,
uvamavurupukaḷum avi vaḻaṅkumāṟum iviyeṉac cuṭṭic cellā niṉṟatu. ivviṣayatti

iṉiccilar, 'nāṭakam' eṉṟa col vaṭacollātal kaṇṭu, āriyaroṭu kalanta piṉṉarē
tamiḻarkaḷiṭattiṟ nāṭakattamiḻ ēṟpaṭṭiruttal vēṇṭumeṉkiṉṟaṉar. ikkūṟṟu vaṭamoḻiyil
'vāy' eṉpataṟkut takka collillāmiyāl vaṭamoḻiyāḷarkku vāyilli yeṉpatu pōlum.
aḵtaṉṟiyum 'kūttu' eṉṟa collē ātiyil tamiḻarkaḷ vaḻaṅkiyatu; kūtteṉpatu potu;
nāṭakameṉpatu ciṟappu. kūttup palavaṟṟuḷ, nāṭakameṉpatu kati taḻuvivarum kūttu, eṉavē
nāṭakameṉṟa colvaḻakkiṉ muṉṉarē, nāṭakat toḻil vaḻakkum, nāṭaka nūl vaḻakkun
tamiḻarmāṭṭuṇṭeṉpatu peṟṟām.
mūvakip pākupāṭu muṟṟiṟṟu.

IV. aivaki yilakkaṇam.

pāṣiyāvatu, makkaḷatu iyaṟki vēṭkiyil tōṉṟi, avaratu nākarika virutti muṟikkēṟp
vaḷarntu varuvatōr makkaḷākkap poruḷām. atu coṟkōvippaṭṭup poruḷ aṟivuṟukkum
vākkiyaṅkaḷālāyatu; iv vākkiyaṅkaḷellām poruḷ aṟivuṟukkuñ coṟkaḷāyiṉa; iccoṟkaḷ
tāmum pakuti vikuti iṭinili mutaliya pala vuṟuppukkaḷālāyiṉa.
ivvuṟuppukkaḷuḷḷum pakutikaḷellām, perumpāṉmiyum ātiyilirunta tamiḻmakkaḷ
coṟcollat toṭaṅkiya kālattup piṟanta coṟkōvi citintu maruviya coṟkaḷām; ciṟupāṉmi
olikkuṟippu viyappuk kuṟippu mutaliyakāraṇaṅkaḷ paṟṟi vanta coṟkaḷām. iṉi vikuti
mutalāyiṉa vellām oru kālattil muḻumutaṟ coṟkaḷāyp poruḷaṟivuṟutti niṉṟu,
piṉṉoru kālattu makkaḷ, kuṟippiṉmi, cōmpal, poccāppu, muyaṟcic curukkam ākiya
kāraṇaṅkaḷāṟ coṟ cōrvupaṭa moḻitalāṟ palavāṟu tirintu maruvi iṭic coṟkaḷeṉṟu vaḻaṅkap
paṭuvaṉavāyiṉa. nākarikam mutiruntōṟum mutiruntōṟum pāṣi māṟupaṭṭuc coṟkaḷic
citittu vaḻaṅkum vaḻakkam makkaḷukkup pala acukariyaṅkaḷ viḷivittaṉa.
tamiḻccoṟkaḷi māntar mēlum mēluñ citittu vaḻaṅkātavāṟu, āciriyar akattiyaṉār
tolkāppiyaṉār pōṉṟa naṉmakkaḷ tōṉṟi, āccoṟkaḷi mēlvaḻakku muṟikāṭṭi varampaṟuttu
ilakkaṇa nūlkaḷ vakuttut tamiḻp pāṣiyi yoḻuṅkupaṭutti niṟuttuvārāyiṉar. iṅṅaṉam
oḻuṅkupaṭuttappaṭāta pāṣikaḷellāñ cila citintu vaḻaṅki iṟutiyil urukkulintu
vaḻakkamaṟṟu oḻintupōm.
mēṟkūṟiyavāṟu tamiḻmoḻikku mutaṉmutal ilakkaṇa nūl vakuttavar akattiyaṉār. avar
iyalici nāṭakameṉṟa muttami ḻilakkaṇamum muṟippaṭa vakut tōtiṉār. icināṭakat
tamiḻilakkaṇaṅkaḷi yeṭuttuk kūṟātu iyaṟṟamiḻilakkaṇattimaṭṭil vakut tōtiṉār
tolkāppiyaṉār. ippoḻutu 'akattiyat'tiṉ cūttiraṅkaḷ ciṟcilamaṭṭil āṅkāṅkut tolli
tūturikaḷiṟ kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉavēyaṉṟi nūṉ muḻutuṅ kāṇappaṭavilli; ākavē 'tolkāppiya'
moṉṟumē muḻu mutalilakkaṇa nūlāyk kuṟipāṭiṉṟi yitukāṟum ilaṅkāniṉṟatu; iṉiyum
ilaṅkā niṟkum. ivviyaṟṟami ḻilakkaṇanūl 'eḻuttu', 'col', 'poruḷ' eṉṟa
mūṉṟatikāraṅkaḷāka vakukkappaṭṭuḷatu.
eḻuttatikārattil eḻuttiṉiyalpukaḷum, eḻuttāṉākiya moḻikaḷum avaṟṟiṉ puṇarccikaḷum
mutalāyiṉa virittuk kūṟappaṭṭaṉa. collatikārattiṟ colliṉ pākupāṭukaḷum,
avaṟṟiṉiyalpukaḷum mutalāyiṉa viritturikkappaṭṭuḷa. maṟṟup poruḷatikārattilō
kaḷaviyaluṅ kaṟpiyalumākiya akapporuḷum avaṟṟiṉ tuṟikaḷum, puṟapporuḷum avaṟṟiṉ
tuṟikaḷum, meyppāṭṭiyal uvamaviyal ceyyuḷiyal marapiyalakaḷum kūṟappaṭṭirukkiṉṟaṉa.
iṉi aivaki yilakkaṇaṅkaḷāvaṉa 'eḻuttu, col, poruḷ, yāppu, aṇi' yeṉpaṉa.
ivvintaṉuḷḷē 'tolkāppiya'ttil mutal mūṉṟi maṭṭilē virittuk kūṟuvār, ceyyuḷiya
leṉṟataṉ kaṇṇē yāppi virittōtiyum, uvamaviyaleṉṟataṉ kaṇṇē aṇiyi yeṭuttōtiyum
ōrāṟṟāṉ aivaki yilakkaṇamuṅ kūṟiṉārāyiṉār. aṉṟiyun tolkāppiyaṉār kālattē aivaki
yilakkaṇameṉṟa pākupāṭiṉṟi yiruntiruttalu miyalpē; allatu yāppaṇikaḷ
poruḷatikārattiṉ pakutikaḷeṉavē karutap paṭṭiruntaṉa veṉṟalum amiyum.
kaṭiccaṅkattār kālatti lēṟpaṭṭa 'iṟiyaṉārakap poruḷuri'yiṟ " peytapiṉ aracaṉ nāṭu
nāṭāyiṟ ṟākaliṉ nūlvallārik koṇarkaveṉṟu ellāp pakkamum pōkka, eḻuttatikāramuñ
collatikāramum yāppatikāramum vallārit talippaṭṭuk koṇarntu, poruḷatikāram vallāri
eṅkun talippaṭṭilē meṉavantār. vara, aracaṉum puṭipaṭak kavaṉṟu, 'eṉṉi? eḻuttuñ
collum yāppum ārāyvatu poruḷatikārattiṉ poruṭṭaṉṟē? poruḷatikāram peṟēmēyeṉiṉ,
ivi peṟṟum perṟilēm' eṉac collāniṟpa" eṉṟa kūṟṟiṉi uyttu nōkki yuṇarumpōtu,
akkālattil 'eḻuttu, col, poruḷ,yāppu' eṉa nālvakiyē vaḻaṅkiṉa veṉpatu
tuṇiyappaṭum. aḵtāvatu tolkāppiyaṉār kālattiṟ poruḷatikārattiluḷḷaṭaṅkiya yāppu,
kaṭic caṅkattār kālattil ataṉiṉṟum pirintu taṉittu ōratikāramāyiṟṟu eṉpatām.
ataṉpiṉ yāppatikāram mikka ciṟappaṭintu pallōrālum pārāṭṭap paṭṭatu. akkālattirunta
pulavar palarum yāppu nūlkaḷ pala iyaṟṟiṉar. kākkipāṭiṉiyar, avinayaṉār, mayēccuraṉār
mutalāyiṉār tattam peyarāl yāppilaṅkaṇaṅkaḷ vakuttaṉar. piṉṉar 'yāpparuṅkalam,'
'yāpparuṅkalak kāriki' eṉṟa nūlkaḷ vakukkappaṭṭu vaḻaṅkuvaṉa vāyiṉa.
iṭiyiṟ poruḷatikārap payiṟci kuṉṟiyapōtu 'iṟiyaṉārakap poruḷ' eṉṟa ciṟiya ilakkaṇa
nūlum, maṇivācakap perumāṉ vāy malarntaruḷiya 'tirukkōviyār' eṉṟa ilakkaṇa nūlum
ēṟpaṭṭaṉa. ataṉ mēṟ cila putiya karuttukkaḷi yuṭaṉkūṭṭi nāṟkalirāja nampi yeṉpavar
'akapporuḷ viḷakkam' eṉṟa ilakkaṇa nūliyaṟṟiṉar. piṉṉark 'kallāṭam', nammāḻvār
ceytaruḷiya 'tiruviruttam' eṉṟa akapporuḷilakkiya nūlkaḷ veḷippōntaṉa. aiyaṉāritaṉār
eṉpavar 'puṟapporuḷ veṇpā māli' eṉṟa nūl vakuttaṉar.
ivvāṟu nālvaki yilakkaṇamē cilakālam vaḻaṅki vantaṉa. ataṉmēl vaṭanūlvāṇarōṭu ūṭāṭi
aintāvatu vaki yilakkaṇamākiya aṇiyatikārattiyun tamiḻp pulavarkaḷ virutti ceytu
koḷḷap pukuntaṉar. 'aṇinūl', 'taṇṭiyalaṅkāram' mutaliya nūlkaḷ iyaṟṟappaṭṭaṉa. ivaṟṟuṭ
piṉṉatē tamiḻ makkaḷuṭ payiṟcimikkuṭiyatāy viḷaṅkukiṉṟatu. innūlāciriyar potu
vaṇiyiyal, poruḷaṉiyiyal collaṇiyiyal eṉṟa mūṉṟu piriviṉil tamnūli
muṭittirukkiṉṟaṉar. potuvaṇiyiyaliṟ potuvākap palavaki kalappāṭukaḷum puṉivu
muṟikaḷuṅ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa.
poruḷaṇiyiyal taṉmiyaṇi mutalāka muppattintaṇikaḷ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa. ipporuḷaṇikaḷiṟ pala
uvamameṉṟa ōraṇiyiṉaṭiyākavē tōṉṟiṉa veṉpatu pallōr tuṇipu. āciriyar
tolkāppiyaṉārum ittuṇipiṉar pōlum. itu vaṭamoḻi appiya tīkṣitaravarkaḷukkum
uṭaṉpāṭātal,

eṉṟa avaratu 'cittiramīmāñcik' kūṟṟiṉāṉ viḷaṅkum. iṉic collaṇiyiyalil maṭakkum
cittiramumākiya miṟikkavikaḷiṉ pākupāṭukaḷum, avaṟṟiṉilakkaṇaṅkaḷum parakkak
kūṟappaṭṭuḷa.
maṟṟu, aintilakkaṇaṅkaḷiyum muṟṟat taṉittaṉi kūṟum nūlkaḷ mikac cilavē. avitām
'vīracōḻiyam', 'ilakkaṇa viḷakkam', 'toṉṉūl viḷakkam' eṉpaṉavām. vīracōḻiyam perumpālum
vaṭanūl muṟiyiyē taḻuvic celvatu. ilakkaṇa viḷakkamuṭiyār yāppaṇikaḷip piṉṉūlkaḷpōla
virittuk kūṟiṉum avaṟṟip poruḷatikārattiṉ kaṇṇēyē yaṭakkik kūṟiṉar. toṉṉūl
viḷakkamuṭiyōr poruḷatikārattic curukik kūṟilum yāppaṇikaḷi viḷaṅkak kūṟiṉar.
taṇṭiyalaṅkārampōlat taṉiyē aṇiyilakkaṇaṅ kūṟuvatāy, āṉāl ataṉiṉum virintatāy,
'māṟaṉalaṅkāram' eṉṟatōr nūl ēṟpaṭuvatāyiṟṟu. itu mikac ciṟantatōr nūl. ataṉ mēṟ
'kuvalayānantam' eṉṟa vaṭamoḻi yalaṅkāracāstiram tamiḻiṉkaṇ moḻipeyarkkap paṭuvatāyiṟṟu.
itu putumi vaḻiyiliyaṉṟatō riṉiyanūl. itaṉkaṇṇē uvamiyaṇi mutal etuvaṇiyīṟāka
nūṟaṇikaḷ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa.
ivvāṟu tamiḻmoḻi aivaki yilakkaṇamu muṭiyatāya nilimi yeyti viḷaṅkā niṉṟatu.
pavaṇanti muṉivarceyta 'naṉṉūl' eḻuttuñ collun teḷivākavum curukkamākavum
aḻakākavum kūṟukiṟatu. 'nēminātam' eṉṟatum appaṭiyē.
iṉip 'pāṭṭiyal' eṉṉumō rilakkaṇap pakutiyuḷatu. ataṉiyalpu caṟṟē viritturikkaṟ
pālatu. atu tamiḻiṟ kūṟappaṭum toṇṇūṟṟaṟuvakip pirapantaṅkaḷi ṉilakkaṇaṅkaḷum,
pāṭuvōṉukkum pāṭappaṭuvō ṉukku muḷḷa eḻuttup poruttam maṅkalapporuttam,
mutaliyaṉavum, īcceḻuttu amutaveḻutteṉṟa pākupāṭum, āṉantam mutaliya kuṟṟaṅ kaḷum
ivi pōlvaṉa piṟavum virittuk kūṟuvatākum. 'veṇpāppāṭṭiyal,' 'variyaṟuttapāṭṭiyal'
eṉṟa nūlkaḷellām pāṭṭiyal kūṟuvaṉavē. ilakkaṇa viḷakkatti ṉiṟutiyiṟ 'pāṭṭiyal'
eṉṟatōriyalum yāttuk kōkkappaṭṭuḷatu.
aṇikaḷuṭ collaṇikaḷē vicēṭamāy nallicip pulavarkaḷāṉ matikkappaṭuvaṉavalla. muṟkālattuc
caṅkac ceyyuṭkaḷilellām collaṇikaḷ kāṇṭalaritu. iccollaṇikaḷilum cittirakavikaḷ
mikavu motukkap paṭuvaṉavāyiṉa. 'kāñcippurāṇa' mātiya cilanūlkaḷē yivaṟṟuṭ cila
koṇṭiyaṅkukiṉṟaṉa. maṭakkukaḷellām vicēṭamāy nūlkaḷiṟ payiṉṟu varāmal avaṟṟuḷ
āṅkāṅku varum. iṭikkālattut tōṉṟiya cilar colliṉpam nāṭupavarāyc collaṇikaḷip
peritum vaḻaṅkuvārāyiṉar. ivarkaḷ yamakam, tiripu mutaliya ceyyuṭkaḷ pala viyaṟṟiṉar.
piṉṉart tiripantātikaḷum yamaka vantātikaḷum cilēṭi veṇpākkaḷum aḷaviṟantaṉa
veḻumpiṉa. iviyaṉittum perumpālum poruṭceṟivilavāy vīṇ captajālaṅkaḷāymaṭṭil
muṭintaṉa. ikkālattiṉum teṉṉāṭṭiṟ pulavar palar yamakantiripu pāṭutaliyē
peritākaveṇṇivāṇāḷi vīṇāṉ kaḻippar.
coṉṉalattilum poruṇalamē ciṟantateṉap pēraṟivāḷar yāvaruṅ
kūṟuvar. yamakantiripuḷḷa pāṭalkaḷi ' uyirillāp pāṭṭukkaḷ' eṉṟum
karuttunalam vāynta kaṟpaṉiyuḷḷa pāṭalkaḷi 'uyiruḷḷa pāṭṭukkaḷ eṉṟum aṟivuṭiyōr palar
kūṟakkēṭṭirukkiṉaṟaṉam. iḵtuṇmiyē yeṉpatu tiṇṇam. ivvāṟavarkaḷ kūṟutaṟkuṟṟa kāraṇaṅkaḷ
yāveṉṟu ārāyvōm. mutalāvatu: miṟikkavikaḷ pāṭumiṭattu mikka kālañ cellum;
appaṭik kālañ ceṉṟum ciṟcila vēḷikaḷil avikaḷ pāṭuvōriṣṭappaṭi yamiyāmaṟ
pōvatumuṇṭu. ivvāṟu muṟkimōti muṉintaṭittu miṟik kavikaḷic collaḷavāl
oruvāṟamittu muṭitta piṉṉarum avi ciṟantiruppatilli. ēṉeṉṟāl avaṟṟiṉkaṭ
poruṇalamilli. iraṇṭāvatu: kavikaḷiyaṟṟuvatu yāvarkkum payaṉpaṭal vēṇṭumeṉpatu karuti.
akkavikaḷiṉum cila, tiricoṟkaḷā lamittu viṭṭāṟ paṭippavaruṭ palar tikippar.
ivvāṟirukkac ceyalpaṭuṅ kavikaḷō miṟikkavikaḷ; ammiṟit taṉmiyōṭu ilakkaṇaccantikaḷuñ
ceṟintaṉa. etirpārātapaṭi
coṟkaḷ piḷantu pirittup poruḷ koḷḷak kiṭantaṉa. ippaṭiyeṉṟāl immiṟik kavikaḷ
yārukkapayōkam? miṟik kaviñaṉtāṉē makiḻntu
koḷḷataṟku avi oru vēḷi payaṉpaṭalām. 'vakiyuḷi' eṉṟataṟku
vēṇṭiya utāraṇaṅkaḷ immiṟik kavikaḷiṟ kāṇalām. mūṉṟāvatu: ittakiya miṟikkavikaḷ
paṭippōṉatu kālattiyum vīṇākkip palaviṭaṅkaḷiṉum avaṉi mayaṅka vikkiṉṟaṉa. ittaṉmiyāṉa
iṭarppāṭukaḷ nirampiya miṟikkavikaḷ nantamiḻp pulavarkaḷ maṉaṅkaḷik kavarātoḻivaṉavāka.
ivvāṟē muṟkūṟiya pāṭṭiyaluṭ kūṟappaṭṭuḷa poruttaṅkaḷ mutaliyaṉavum, āṉantak kuṟṟam
mutaliyaṉavum nāḷāvaṭṭattiṟ payaṉilavāyk kaḻintupaṭumeṉpatu tōṉṟukiṟatu. anēkar
ikkālattil avaṟṟik kavaṉippatilli. karunāṭakap pulavarkaḷu iraṇṭoruvar maṭṭil avaṟṟip
piṭittukkoṇṭu avastippaṭukiṉṟaṉar. avarkaḷum avaṟṟi viriviṟ kiviṭuvārkaḷeṉpatu
collamalē yamiyum. iṉip 'pattuppāṭṭuri'yil āciriyar nacciṉārkkiṉiyar
"nūṟkuṟṟaṅkūṟukiṉṟa pattuvakik kuṟṟattē 'taṉṉāṉoru poruḷ karutikkūṟal' eṉṉuṅ
kuṟṟattip piṉṉuḷḷōr āṉantak kuṟṟameṉpatōr kuṟṟameṉṟu nūl ceytataṉṟi, akattiyaṉārum
tolkāppiyaṉārum ikkuṟṟaṅ kūṟāmiyiṟ cāṉṟōr ceyyuṭku ikkuṟṟa muṇṭāyiṉuṅ koḷḷā
reṉamaṟukka" eṉṟuritta vuri yāvaruṅ kavaṉikkattakkatu.
itukāṟum tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ aivaki yilkaṇaṅkaḷiyum kuṟittuk
kūṟiṉōm. iṉit tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ tōṟṟamum toṉmiyum paṟṟi viritturippām.
aivaki ilakkaṇam muṟṟiṟṟu.

V. pāṣiyiṉ tōṟṟamun toṉmiyum.

ulakiṉkaṇṇē makkaḷ oruṅkukūṭi vāḻavēṇṭiyavarātaliṉ, avarkaḷ
aṭikkaṭi taṅkaḷ karuttukkaḷi yoruvarikoruvar veḷiyiṭṭuk poḷvatu avaciyamāyiṟṟu.
ākavē avarkaḷ kivāy mutaliya uṟuppukkaḷāṟ palavitac cikikaḷ ceytu kāṭṭit taṅkaḷ
karuttukkaḷi veḷiyiṭuvārāyiṉar. ittakiya karuttu veḷiyīṭṭuk karuviyākiya cikiyi
'iyaṟkip pāṣi' yeṉpar cilar.
ivviyaṟkip pāṣi mutaliṟ kāṭciyaḷavil niṉṟu, piṉṉark kēḷviyaḷaviṉum paraviṟṟu;
aḵtāvatu, cila olikaḷum olik kūṭṭaṅkaḷum karuttu veḷippāṭṭiṟkuk karuviyāka
accikikaḷuṭaṉ kūṭiṉa. avveḷum olikkūṭṭaṅkaḷum mūvitappaṭum. avi tām, pōṟal
vakiyāṉum, cuvi vakiyāṉum, aṟikuṟi vakiyāṉum eḻutappaṭum.
pēccu nilikku vārātu ciki niliyil niṉṟa kālattilellām tamiḻ makkaḷ puli
mutaliyavaṟṟik koṉṟu vēṭṭittoḻil ceytu vantaṉar. ivvēṭṭit toḻilic cevvipeṟa
naṭāttuvataṟku mirukaṅkaḷiṉ olikaḷi yuṟṟuk kēṭṭu naṉkuṇarntu avi pōlat tāmuṅ katti
avaṟṟi ēmāṟṟit tamvalip paṭuttiṉar. vēṭṭiyāṭutalip payaṉ karutiyatōr toḻilākavē
muṟṟum matittal nēritaṉṟu. iḵtu ātimakkaḷāl viḷiyāṭṭut toḻilākavum karutappaṭṭu
vantatu. ikkālattiṉum vēṭṭiyāṭutal palarkkup pirītiyāṉatōr viḷiyāṭṭām. ivvāṟu
ātit tamiḻ makkaḷ vēṭṭiyāṭiṉatāṟ palavakippaṭṭa mirukaṅkaḷiṉoli vēṟupāṭukaḷiyum
naṉkuṇarntu tamakkuḷḷē yavaṟṟi yaṟintu koḷḷumāṟu avvolikaḷiṉiṉṟuṅ kuṟippu moḻikaḷ
pala vakuttaṉar. oṉṟu piṟitupōṟkattiya oliyiṉiṉṟum uṟpattiyāṉa coṟkaḷip 'pōṟal vaki'
yāṉeḻuntaṉa veṉka.
makkaḷ cuviyuṭiyārātaliṉ, avaṟṟiṟkut takunta meyppāṭukaḷ viḷikkattakka poruḷkaḷ
etirppaṭṭaṉavāyiṉ, cila kuṟippuc coṟkaḷ tāmākak kūṟuvaraṉṟē? veṟuppuc cuviyiṟ
'cī' yeṉṟum, vekuḷic cuviyiṟ 'pō' veṉṟum, uvakic cuviyil 'vā' veṉṟum
kūṟappaṭuñ coṟkaḷik kāṇka. 'ai' yeṉpatu viyappiṉālum, 'ō' veṉpatu avalattiṉālum
'aiyō' veṉpatu accattiṉālum eḻuntaṉavāmā ṟuṇarka. ivikaḷellāñ 'cuvi vaki'
yāṉuṇṭākiya coṟkaḷām.
' aṟikuṟi vaki' yāṉeḻunta pakutikaḷ mikac ciṟappuṭiyaṉavām. ivikaḷ 'nāviṉ ciki'
eṉpataṉaṭiyāka mutaliṟ piṟantaṉa. iḵtu aṭikkaṭi, pal, utaṭu mutaliya vāyiṉ pakutikaḷic
cuṭṭutaṟkuriya 'kicciki'kaḷik kavaṉikkumāṟu kūvuṅ kūccalōṭu toṭaṅkum. vēṇṭu
meṉṟeḻātu anutāpattiṉā lutaviceyya veḻunta nāviṉ ciki iyaṟkiyākavē kiccikiyip
piṉpaṟṟivarum. ataṉmēṟ kicciki mikiyeṉṟu ciṟitu ciṟitākak kiviṭappaṭum.
utāraṇamāka, kavaṉikkumāṟu kūviya kūccal 'ā' veṉṟa uruvaṅkoṇṭatāka vittuk koḷvōm.
appaṭiyāṉāṟ 'pal'lik kuṟikkum nāviṉ ciki 'aṭā' eṉṟa uruvaṅkoḷḷum. ivvāṟē
taṉṉik kavaṉikkumāṟu piṟari viḷitta 'ē' eṉṟiruntatāyiṉ pallikkuṟikkum nāviṉ
ciki 'ēṭa' veṉṟirukkum. īṇṭuk kūṟiyataṟkiṇaṅkavē, tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ kaṇṇē, 'aṭā'
'ēṭa' eṉṟa viraṇṭu coṟkaḷum piṟari viḷittaṟkaṭ peritum payaṉpaṭukiṉṟaṉa. "ēṭa veṉpatu
tōḻaṉ muṉṉili" eṉṟu kūṟappaṭṭiruttaluṅ kāṇka.
vallāḷa ṉoruvaṉ eḷiyavaṉukku utavi ceyyappukuntu ciṉṉāḷ avaṉip pātukāttup piṉṉar
avaṉ mēṟkoṇṭa toḻili naṉṟāka naṭattāmi kaṇṭu avaṉit taḷḷiviṭṭut tāṉ attoḻili
mēṟkoṇṭu naṭāttap pukuntāṟpōla, nāviṉ cikiyum kic cikiyi ṉutavikkāka vantu
ataṉic ciṉṉāḷ pātukāttup pārttum atukaruttu veḷiyīṭṭut toḻili naṉku
naṭattāmiyāl ataṉit taḷḷiviṭṭut tāṉē attoḻili mēṟkoṇṭu naṭāttap pukuntatu.
pukuntu tāṉ naṭāttiṉatiṟ coṟkaḷ palavuṇṭāyiṉa.
cila taṉmi, muṉṉilip pakutikaḷ aṟikuṟivakiyā leḻuntirukkalā meṉṟeṇṇutaṟ
kiṭaṉuṇṭu. atutāṉum uccarikkac culapamākavum eḻuttukkaḷiṟ piratāṉamākavumuḷḷa 'ma',
'pa' eṉṟa eḻuttukkaḷiṉ campantamuṭiyatām. ivviraṇṭeḻuttukkaḷum kuḻantikaḷāṉ
mutaṉmutal uccarikkap paṭuvaṉavām. ivviraṇṭum kuḻantikaṭku arukiruntu utavi ceyyun
tāy tantiyarik kuṟippikkuñcoṟkaḷākiya 'ammā' 'appā' eṉṟaṉa piṟappataṟku mutaṟ
karuviyāy niṟpaṉa. mēlum makāram pala pāṣikaḷilun taṉmic coṟkaḷi orumiyiṟ kuṟittaṟaka
ṇupakārappaṭā niṟpa, tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ kaṇṇē avaṟṟip paṉamiyiṟ kuṟittaṟkaṇu pakārappaṭā
niṉṟatu. 'mām' (eṉṉi) eṉpatu vaṭamoḻit taṉmi yorumi. 'yām' 'emmi' yeṉpaṉa
tamiḻmoḻit taṉmip paṉmi. evvāṟāyiṉum makāran taṉmikkaṇ upakārap paṭukiṉṟateṉpatu
maliyilakkē. iṉi itaṉoṭu tāyik kuṟikkum 'ammā' eṉṟa colliṉ kaṇṇum makāram
upakārappaṭutali uṟṟu nōkkiṉ tāy kuḻantikkut taṉṉavaḷāyiṉaḷ. itik kavaṉikkumiṭattut
tanti ciṟitu taṉṉiṉiṉṟum vilakiṉavaṉāka matikkappaṭuvataṟku aṟikuṟiyākak pakāra
captattiṉāl 'appā'eṉṟaḻikkap paṭukiṉṟaṉaṉ.
tamiḻap pāṣiyiṉtōṟṟa viṣayamāka, yām mēṟkūṟiya pōntaṉa vaṉittum evvāṟu karutappaṭiṉum
paṭuka. karuttukkaḷ taṉ muyaṟci yāṉeḻum olikaḷiṉ campantamākavē veḷippaṭukiṉṟa
iyalpiṉiṉṟumē pāṣikaḷ uṇṭāta liyalumeṉpatu yāvarum maṟukka muṭiyāttōr pāṣi
nūluṇmi. ivvuṇmiyi yoṭṭiyē tamiḻp pāṣiyiṉ tōṟṟamum ēṟpaṭṭiruttal mikavum
kavaṉikkaṟpālatē. ītivvāṟaka, tamiḻ nūlāciriyar palarum, ikkālattiṉum āṅkilanūṟ
payiṟciyillāta naṇparuṭ cilarum tamiḻmoḻiyum vaṭamoḻiyun tēvapāṣikaḷeṉṟum,
ivviraṇṭum muṟiyē akattiyaṉārkkum pāṇiniyārkkum civaperumāṉāl
upatēcikkappaṭṭaṉaveṉṟuṅ kūṟāniṟpar. "ātiyiṟ ṟamiḻnū lakattiyaṟ kuṇarttiya,
mātorupākaṉi vaḻuttutum" eṉavum, "taḻaṟpuri niḻaṟkuvu ṭantamiḻ" eṉavum,
"vaṭamoḻiyip pāṇinikku vakuttaruḷiyataṟ kiṇiyāt, toṭarpuṭiya teṉmoḻiyit tokukaṭal
cūḻvarippataṉiṟ, kuṭamuṉikku vaṟpuṟuttār kollēṟṟup pākar" eṉavum, " irumoḻikkuṅ
kaṇṇutalār mutaṟkuravar" eṉavum muntiyōr kūṟiyiruttaliyuṅ kāṇka.
yām mēṟkūṟip pōntavāṟu makkaḷākkap poruḷāyviḷinta tamiḻp
pāṣiyāṉatu tamiḻmakkaḷ yāvarukkum oruṅkē viḷaṅkuvatāyiṟṟu, eṉṟatōr ācaṅki
iṭiyileḻutal iyalpē. itu veku culapamāka viṭukkappaṭum. yāvarukkum viḷaṅkattakka
cikikaḷ iṭiniṉṟu coṟkaḷi aiyamuṇṭāṉa kālaṅkaḷiṉ viḷakkiṉamiyiṉ tamiḻppāṣiyiṉ
coṟporu ḷuṇarcci tamiḻ makkaḷ yāvarukkum uṇṭākit tamakkuḷḷē yaṟintu koṇṭaṉar.
itukāṟum tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ tōṟṟattippaṟṟip pēciṉōm. iṉit
tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ toṉmiyip paṟṟik kūṟappukuvām.
ivvulakattiṉ kaṇṇē pēcappaṭuvaṉa ēṟakkuṟiya toḷḷāyiram moḻikaḷām. avaṟṟuḷḷē
nālintupāṣikaḷ tām 'toṉmoḻikaḷ'; eṉṉum peyarkkuriyaṉavāyk karutappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.
annālintuḷḷum, tamiḻ moḻiyum oṉṟukol? ituvē yām ippoḻutu ārāya vēṇṭuvatu.
paravi vaḻakkaṟṟu ēṭṭuvaḻakkāymaṭṭil niṟpaṉavē toṉmoḻikaḷ; maṟṟu ikkālattiṟ paravi
vaḻakkāy viḷaṅkuvaṉa vellān toṉ maḻikaḷākāveṉpatu cilar tuṇipu. ikattuṇipu
tarukkanūṟ kuṟṟamē. pāṣikaḷ paravi vaḻakkaṟṟup pōtaṟkuk kāraṇam ilipika ḷēṟpaṭāmiyum,
pāṣi pēcuvōr palvēṟiṭaṅkaṭkup pirintu cellalum, iṭaṅkaṭkut takkapaṭi putu ilipikaḷ
vakuttukkōṭalum piṟavumām. ēṭṭu vaḻakkup pāṣip pēccu vaḻakkupāṣikkiṇaṅki
vārāviṭil muṉṉatu paravi vaḻakkaṟṟut taṉṉili vēṟupaṭātu orunilippāṭaṭintu niṟkum.
piṉṉatu virintu koṇṭēpōy muṉṉatiṉiṉṟu mikavum vēṟupaṭṭu ataṉ vaḻimoḻiyākak
karutappaṭum. ākavēpēccu vaḻakkō ṭiṇaṅki varātaṉa vellān toṉmoḻikaḷō? alla.
tamiḻ moḻiyil, 'ṭ,ṇ,r, l,ḷ, ḻ,ṟ, ṉ,' eṉṟa eṭṭu meyyeḻuttukkaḷiyuṅ koṇṭu
coṟkaḷ toṭaṅkukiṉṟila: aḵtāvatu, ivveṭṭum moḻikku mutalil vārāta eḻuttukkaḷām.
ivikaḷēṉ moḻikku mutalil varavilli? ivveṭṭum nā mēlaṇṇattit toṭutalāṉē piṟakkum
nāveḻuttukkaḷām. aṅṅaṉam mēlaṇṇan toṭṭu uccarittaṟ poruṭṭu vēṇṭappaṭu muyaṟci
mikutiyā yiruttaliṉ avveṭṭu meykaḷum moḻikku mutalil vārāvāyiṉa. ivvuṇmi
koṇṭu, paṇṭikkālattē, vaḻaṅkiya makkaḷukku uṟuppukkaḷ uramēṟi aritu muyaṟci cellāta
iḷamparuvattē muṟpaṭṭut tōṟṟa muṟṟeḻunta moḻi tamiḻēyā meṉṟu kūṟi, ataṉ toṉmi
māṭci niṟutti viṭṭēmeṉa makiḻvārumuḷar.
"piṉpu poṉṉiṟintatāyum aḻakuṭittāyum muttumaṇi ivaṟṟāl aṇiyappaṭṭatāyum nakarattu
araṇōṭu iṇikkappaṭṭatāyumuḷḷa pāṇṭiyar vāyiṟkatavi vāṉararkāḷ! pōyk kāṇpīr" eṉa
vāṉmīki muṉivar kūṟukiṉṟamiyāṉum viyāta muṉivarum 'makāpāratat' tiṉ kaṇṇē
tamiḻnāṭṭiṉ ciṟappiyum pāṇṭiyar araciyumpaṟṟik kūṟukiṉṟamiyāṉum,pāṇṭiyar
āṇṭanāṭṭiṉ toṉmiyum avar pēciya tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ toṉmiyum ōrāṟṟāṟ ṟeḷiyappaṭumāṟu
kāṇka.
iṟṟikkuc caṟṟēṟak kuṟiyap patiṉāyiram varuṣaṅkaṭku muṟpaṭṭa paḻamiyāṉa eḻuttuc
cātaṉaṅkaḷ kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉavām. āyiṉum ki.mu. eṇṇāyira varuṣaṅkaḷukku muṉṉarē
makkaḷukkuḷ nākarikam toṭaṅkiyirukka vēṇṭum. ivvaḷavukālam muṉṉareṉpatu
variyaṟutturikka muṭiyātāyiṉum eḻuttu vaḻakku itaṟkum muṉṉarē yēṟpaṭṭiruttal
vēṇṭum. ivvāṟāyiṉ pāṣit tōṟṟattiṉ kālam ittuṇiyāyiram āṇṭukaṭku muṉṉareṉpatu
yāvarāṉ variyaṟuttuk kūṟaviyalumō? aṟiyēm. pāṣi mulam natimūla ruṣi mūlaṅkaḷ
pōlum.!
iṉittamiḻ nūlkaḷiṟ pala iṭiccaṅkamirunta kapāṭapuraṅ kaṭal koḷḷappaṭṭa kālattil
aḻintupaṭṭaṉaveṉak kūṟak kaṇṭuṉēm. aḵtaṉṟiyum tamiḻil mikappaḻaṅkāppiyameṉa

vaṭikaḷ

uriyāciriyar aṭiyārkku nallār "akkālattu avar nāṭṭut teṉpāli mukattiṟku
vaṭavelliyākiya paḵṟuḷiyeṉṉum āṟṟiṟkuṅ kumariyeṉṉu māṟṟiṟku miṭiyē eḻunūṟṟuk
kāvatanāṭum ivaṟṟiṉ nīrmali vāṉeṉa malinta ēḻteṅka nāṭum ēḻmaturi nāṭum,
ēḻmuṉpālināṭum, ēḻpiṉpālināṭum ēḻ kuṉṟa nāṭum ēḻkuṇa kāri nāṭum,
ēḻkuṟumpaṉināṭum eṉṉum inta nāṟpattoṉpatu nāṭum, kumari kollam mutaliya paṉmali

palyāka

pulavar cilaratu peyarkaḷum, 'peru nūl', 'iyaṉūl' eṉac cila nūṟpeyarum annūlāṉum
ataṉuriyāṉum veḷiyākiṉṟaṉa. annūl mutalūḻiyil taliccaṅkattār kalattiṟ
kumariyāṟṟiṟkum paḵṟuḷiyāṟṟiṟkum iṭiyēyuḷḷa peruvaḷa nāṭṭaracaṉākiya ceṅkōvi
mutalūḻit taṉiyūrc cēntaṉ pāṭiṉāṉeṉpatu,

tamiḻneṭukaḷ kaṭalkoḷḷappaṭṭaṉa veṉpatu pulaṉām. ikkālattu aḷaviṉpaṭi oru kāvatameṉpatu

nīḷamum patiṉāṟu lakṣam mil akalamuṭiya teṉpatu peṟappaṭum. peṟavē ippatiṉāṟu lakṣam

nīḷamuḷḷatām. mōrīcuttīviṟkum ataṟkut teṟkiluḷḷa 'kerkiyūlaṉ' eṉṉun tīviṟkum
iṭiyiluḷḷa nīḷamum avvaḷaviṉatēyām. ākavē nīḷattil ikkālattiluḷḷa
kumarimuṉi'yiliruntu kerkiyūlaṉ tīviṉ teṟku variyilum, akalattil maṭakācikartīvu'

akaṉṟu kiṭantatākak kūṟappaṭum 'ilemuriyā' eṉṟa nilapparappām. innilapparappu oru
kālatteḻunta peruveḷḷattil āḻntu pōyiṟṟeṉṟum avvāṟu āḻantupōṉa perunilam

innilattiliruntu piṉ nāṟṟiciyiṉum pirintu ceṉṟu vēṟupaṭṭaṉa reṉṟum, aṅṅaṉam
itilirunta tollōr vaḻāṅkiyatu tamiḻp pāṣiyā meṉṟum, palakāraṇaṅkaḷ kāṭṭi viḷakki
niṟuviṉār mēṟpul viññāṉikaḷḷoruvar. itu kēṭpataṟku iṉitākavum viñōtamākavu
mirukkiṉṟatu. ataṉāṉē yām mēleṭuttuk kāṭṭiya tamiḻnūṟ pakutikaḷiṟkaṇṭa
viṣayaṅkaḷellām valiyuṟumāṟu kāṇka.

VI. pāṣiyiṉ ciṟappiyalpu.

ivvulakiṉkaṇuḷḷa pāṣikaḷuḷ eviyēṉum iraṇṭu pāṣikaḷāyiṉum tammuḷ muṟṟum
ottiruppatilli. vaḻimoḻikaḷum nāy moḻiyiṉiṉṟu vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉ. collamippum
karuttiṉ parappum ilkaṇavamitiyum pāṣi tōṟum vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉa. pala pāṣikaḷiṟ payiṉṟu
avaṟṟiṉkaṇ orumippāṭu kāṇap pukuvārkkup pāṣikaḷiṉ ciṟpiyalpukaḷ perumpālun

kaṇviḻittup pārppārāyiṉar. anēkar āṅkila nūlkaḷi moḻipeyarkkap pukuntaṉar. ivvāṟu
moḻipeyarppu vakiyāl āṅkilak karuttukaḷ tamiḻiṉkaṇ evvaḷavu pukiṉumnalamē. im
moḻipeyarppuvaki oṉṟu pōtātu; iṉṉum vēṟuvakikaḷum vēṇṭum. tamiḻp
pakutikaḷiṉiṉṟu putuccoṟkaḷ paṭittuk karutti veḷip paṭuttalām. uḷḷa coṟkaḷit
tirittum cila putukkaruttiṟku iṭañceyyalām. cilar āñkilak karuttukaḷukku
ēṟṟacoṟkaḷ tamiḻ moḻiyiṟkāṇṭal aritāyiṉ vaṭacoṟkaḷiṉ mūlamāka avaṟṟit tamiḻil
veḷiyiṭukiṉṟaṉar, ivvaḷavu tūrañ cuṟṟuvāṉēṉ? tali cuṟṟittāṉ mūkkit toṭal
vēṇṭumō? nērē mūkkit toṭalkūṭātō? āṅkilac coṟkaḷiyum 'ticiccoṟ'kaḷāka
mēṟkoṇṭāleṉṉi? mēṟkum oru ticiyaṉṟō?

eṉṟa cūttira vitiyiṉkaṇ āṅkilanāṭu aṭaṅkavilliyē yeṉṟu vātittalum okkumō?
āṅkilamoḻi ticiccoṟkuriya nilaṅkaḷiṉum vaṭa coṟkuriya nilaṅkaḷiṉum
payilappaṭuvatēyaṉṟic centamiḻkkuriya nilattiṉum payilappaṭṭu varutalāl, āṅkilac
coṟkaḷit ticiccoṟkaḷeṉa mēṟkoḷḷuvatilē yātu taṭiyō? ivvāṟu ceytalē aṟivuṭiyōr
ceyalām. itaṉip pēraṟivāḷarāyiṉar piṟa paravi vaḻakkuḷḷa pāṣikaḷiṉ carittiraṅkaḷōṭu
oppiṭṭuc cīrtūkkic cevvaṉē yārāyntu uṇmi yeṉak kaṇṭu kālan tāḻttaliṉṟi yuṭaṉē
mēṟkoṇṭu tamiḻp pāṣi mutirntu nākarika niliyiluyarntu muṉṉukku varumāṟu
ceyvārāka.

eṉṟa pavaṇantimuṉivar kūṟṟu aṟivuṭiyōr aṉivaruñ; cintikkaṟ pālatu. ivvaḷavu
cuvātīṉamuḷḷa tamiḻp pāṣikku mēṟkūṟiyāṅku ceyvataṟkut taṭiyeṉṉa? orutaṭiyumilli.
paḻiyaṉa kaḻitalum putiyaṉa pukutalumākiya ivviraṇṭum pāṣi yiyakkattiṟkuriya iraṇṭu
kālkaḷeṉa matikkappaṭu-kiṉṟaṉa. ivvuṇmiyi yuyttuṇarntu tamiḻp pāṣiyiṉkaṭ
ceyyavēṇṭiya cīrtiruttaṅkaḷiyuṭaṉē mēṟkoḷḷutal tamiḻ makkaḷ kaṭaṉām.

X. muṭivuri.

itukāṟum oṉpatu upanniyāsaṅkaḷilum yāṅ kūṟi vanta viṣayaṅkaḷit tavirttu av
vupanniyācat talip peyarkaḷoṭu poruntātu, eñciya cila viṣayaṅkaḷippaṟṟi iv
vupanniyācattiṟ kūṟit tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ varalāṟṟiṟkum ōrāṟṟāṉ muṭivuri kūṟuvām.
yāṅ kūṟum immuṭivari 'tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ varalāṟu' eṉṟa vinnūliṟkē yaṉṟit
tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ varalāṟṟikaṉṟeṉavuṇarka. tamiḻ moḻi vaḷarntu mutirntukoṇṭē cellum
pāṣiyām. ātalāl innūlum muṭinta nūlaṉṟeṉpatu tēṟṟam. itu tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ
vaḷarccikkēṟpa vaḷaravēṇṭuvatu.
ikkālattil āṅkila pāṣikaṟṟa tamiḻmakkaḷiṟ palar tamatu tāymoḻiyākiya tamiḻip
paṭikkavēṇṭuvatu āvaciyamaṉṟeṉṟum, tamakku vēṇṭiya viṣayaṅkaḷ yāvum āṅkilattilēyē
akappaṭukiṉṟaṉa veṉṟum palatiṟappaṭak kūṟuvar. avar āṅkila moḻic ciṟappumaṭṭiṟ
kūṟiyamivatē naṉṟākumaṉṟit tamiḻkaṟkavēṇṭiya tavaciyamaṉṟu eṉpatu aṟiyāmiyoṭu kūṭiya
tuṇinturiyām. itu cutēcāpimāṉamum cupāṣāpimāṉamuma maṟṟavar kūṟṟām. ivviraṇṭum
mikka āṅkilar ivviraṇṭum aṟṟavarkaḷi evvāṟu matippārō? āṅkilam mutaliya
piṟapāṣikkuriyarāy appāṣikaḷ vallunarellām tamiḻmoḻiyiṉmī tārvamuṭiyāriyt tamiḻik
kaṟṟum tamiḻnūlkaḷit taṅkaḷ pāṣikaḷil moḻipeyarttuk koḷḷutali uṟṟu nōkki yātal
tamiḻk kalvi avaciyamaṉṟu eṉpārkkup puttivaraṭṭum.
pāṣiyaṟivu piṟitoru payaṉukkuk karuviyākumē yaṉṟi, atutāṉē payaṉākātu.
pāṣiyaṟivē payaṉeṉak karuti ataṉkaṇ iṉpaṅ kāṇpārumuḷar. capikaḷilāvatu
campāṣaṇikaḷilāvatu cāturiyamākap pēcuvōr pāṣiyaṟivuṭiyōrē yaṉṟic cāsatirak
kalviyuṭiyārallar. cāstirak kalviyuṭiyār eṅkē pōṉālun taṅkaḷ kaṭiyi virikkap
pukuvārēyaṉṟi vēṟaṉṟu. cāstirak kalviyuṭiyārkkup pāṣiyaṟivum iṉṟiyamiyātatē.
taṅkaḷ karuttukkaḷāl ulakamaṉittilum iṉpavuṇarcci yeḻuppa valla nallicip pulavarkaḷ ceyta
pāmuṟikaḷip paṭittalāluṇṭākum payaṉ aḷaviṭaṟpālataṉṟu; makkaḷiṭattil aṭaṅkik kiṭakkum
manō vikāraṅkaḷi eḻuppic cuvi payakkum.
iṉi yitu niṟka. tamiḻnāṭṭil āṅkila varacāṭci yēṟpaṭṭa piṉṉart tāymoḻiyākiya tamiḻi
ataṉvaḻi moḻikaḷākiya teluṅku kaṉṉaṭa maliyāḷatuḷuvaṅkaḷōṭu aṭakki 'uṇṇāṭṭumoḻikaḷ'
eṉa vakippaṭuttaṉar cilar. taṉimoḻi yoṉṟi ataṉ vaḻimoḻikaḷōṭu vakip paṭuttalāmō? atu
muṉṉataṉi iḻivu paṭutta tākātō? āriyamoḻikaḷuḷ taliniṉṟa vaṭamoḻiyi ataṉ pākataṅkaḷōṭu
oruṅkuvittu eṇṇat tuṇiyāmipōlat tamiḻmoḻiyiyum ataṉ vaḻimoḻikaḷōṭu
oruṅkuvitteṇṇat tuṇiyātiruttalē amivuṭittām. ivvāṟākavum namatu ceṉṉic
carvakalācāliyār mēṟkūṟiyāṅku, tamiḻi iḻivu paṭutti vakuttapōtē, tamiḻarāyiṉār
muṟpukuntu avvāṟu vakippaṭuttal cālāteṉa maṟuttirukkavēṇṭum. appōḻtellām
vāyvāḷāmi mēṟkoṇṭiruntu viṭṭaṉar tamiḻmoḻiyāḷar.
vaṭamoḻi, ilattīṉ, kirīkku mutaliyaṉa pōlat tamiḻmoḻiyum 'uyartaṉic cemmoḻi' yāmāṟu
ciṟitu kāṭṭuvām. tāṉ vaḻaṅkum nāṭṭiṉkaṇuḷḷa paṉmoḻikaṭkun talimiyum avaṟṟiṉum
mikkavē takavuṭimiyumuḷḷa moḻiyē 'uyarmoḻi'. ivvilakkaṇattāṉ ārāyumiṭattu tamiḻ,
teluṅku mutaliyavaṟṟiṟ kellān talimiyum avaṟṟiṉum mikka mētakavum uṭimiyāl tāṉum
uyarmoḻiyē yeṉka. tāṉ vaḻaṅkum nāṭṭiṟpayilum maṟṟiya moḻikaḷiṉ utaviyiṉṟit taṉittu
iyaṅkavalla āṟṟal cāṉṟatē 'taṉimoḻi' eṉappaṭum. tāṉ piṟamoḻikaṭkuc ceyyum utavi
mikuntum avi taṉakkuc ceyyum utavi kuṟintum iruttalē vaḻakkāṟu. tamiḻmoḻiyi
ṉutavi kaḷiyappaṭiṉ, teluṅku mutaliyaṉa iyaṅkuta lollā; maṟṟut tamiḻmoḻi avaṟṟiṉutavi
yillāmalē ciṟitu miṭaṟpaṭutaliṉṟit taṉittu iṉimiyiṉ iyaṅkavallatu. iḵtu intiya
nūṟpulavarkaḷ palarkkum ōppamuṭintatu. ātaliṉ tamiḻ taṉimoḻiyē yeṉka. iṉic
cemmoḻiyāvatu yātu?
tiruntiya paṇpuñ cīrtta nākarikamum, poruntiya tūymoḻi pukal 'cemmoḻi'yām eṉpatu
ilakkaṇam, immoḻi nūlilakkaṇam tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ kaṇṇum amintiruttal tēṟṟam. eṉṉi?
iṭarppaṭṭa coṉ muṭipukaḷum poruṇmuṭipukaḷumiṉṟic coṟṟāṉ karutiya poruḷik kēṭṭāṉ
teḷḷiti ṉuṇaravallatāyp paḻiyaṉa kaḻintu putiyaṉa pukuntu tiruttameyti niṟṟalē
paṇpeṉappaṭuvatu. itu tamiḻ moḻiyiṉkaṇ muṟṟum amintiruttal kāṇka. nāṭṭiṉ nākarika
mutirccik kēṟpac coṟkaḷum ēṟpaṭṭup pāṣikkum nākarika nalam viḷittal vēṇṭum.
avvāṟu coṟka ḷēṟpaṭumiṭattup piṟapāṣic coṟkaḷaṉṟit taṉcoṟkaḷē mikutal vēṇṭum.
iviyum uyartaṉit tamiḻmoḻikkup poruntuvaṉavām,. ākavē tamiḻ tūymoḻiyumām. eṉavē
tamiḻ cemmoḻi yeṉpatu tiṇṇam. itu paṟṟi yaṉṟē toṉṟu toṭṭut tamiḻmoḻi 'centamiḻ'
eṉa nallicip pulavarāl naviṉṟōtap peṟuvatāyiṟṟu. ākavē teṉṉāṭṭiṉkaṭ ciṟantoḷirā
niṉṟa amiḻtiṉumiṉiya tamiḻmoḻi evvāṟṟāṉ ārāynta vaḻiyum 'uyartaṉic
cemmoḻi'yēyām eṉpatu niccayam. ivvaḷa vuyarvuñ ciṟappum vāynta arumit tamiḻ
moḻiyi uṇṇāṭṭup puṉmoḻikaḷō ṭoruṅ keṇṇutal tavirntu, vaṭanāṭṭuyar taṉiccemmoḻi
camaskirutameṉak koṇṭāṟ pōlat, teṉṉāṭṭuyar taṉiccemmoḻi tamiḻeṉak koṇṭu pukutalē
ēṟpuṭittām.
pallāṇṭukaḷiṉ muṉṉarc ceṉṉimā nakarilē, āṅkila varacāṭciyiṉ payaṉāka,'paḷḷikkūṭappāṭa
nūṟcapi' eṉpa toṉ ṟēṟpaṭṭup pala naṟṟoḻilkaḷ ceytu vārāniṉṟatu. iccapiyār
paḷḷikkūṭattiṟ kalvi payilum ciṟuvarkaḷiṉ aṟivu vaḷarccikkēṟṟa pāṭanūlkaḷ amittaṉar;
avvaḷavil nillātu avarkaḷ camaskiruta nāṭakak katikaḷiyum āṅkila nāṭakak katikaḷiyum
teḷivāṉa tamiḻ vacaṉa naṭiyileḻuti veḷiyiṭṭaṉar; 'jana vinōtini' eṉṟatōr arumiyāṉa
tamiḻp pattirikiyi mātantōṟum piracurittu ēṟakkuṟiya irupattiraṇṭu varuṣakālam
naṭāttiṉar. yātu kāraṇattiṉālō atuniṉṟupaṭṭatu? appattiriki niṉṟu paṭṭami
tamiḻarkaḷatu turatirṣṭamēyeṉṉalām. mēṟkūṟiyavāṟu naṟṟoḻil purintu vanta accapiyāṉatu
ippōtu cillāṇṭukaḷāka irukkumiṭan teriyātavāṟu tūṅkik koṇṭirukkiṉṟatu. iṉṉum
uyiruṭaṉirukkiṉṟa teṉa veṇṇuvataṟku iṭaṉuṇṭu. ēṉeṉil, iccapiyār ciṟcila camayaṅkaḷil
tamiḻiṉkaṇ veḷippaṭum nalla nūlkaḷ cilavaṟṟiṟku nērnta patippuc celavukaruti avvan
nūlāciriyarkaṭkup poruḷutavi ceykiṉṟaṉar. iccapi mīṭṭun talikkiḷampi muṉpōla
ūkkamuṟṟup pala naṟṟoḻilkaḷ puriyilum puriyalām. itu tuyiloḻin teḻuka.
ivvāṟu paḷḷikkūṭap pāṭanūṟ capi tuyilvatu kaṇṭu naṉmakkaḷ cilar muṟpukuntu, tamiḻ
teluṅku maliyāḷa kaṉṉaṭa tuḷuvaṅkaḷākiya aṉittiyum pōṟṟuvēmeṉa eḻuntu 'tirāviṭa
pāṣā caṅkam' eṉṉum peyar puṉintu koṇṭu ciṉṉāḷ uḻittaṉar. ivarkaḷ cila āṅkila
cāstirac coṟkaḷi mēṟkūṟiya pāṣikaḷil moḻipeyarttu acciṭṭu veḷiyiṭṭaṉar. ataṉmēl
tuyiṟperun tēvi iccaṅkattiyum paṟṟik koṇṭaṉaḷ. ākavē ceṉṉi tirāviṭa pāṣā caṅkamum
tūṅkuvatāyiṟṟu. iḵtu eṉṟu eḻumō? aṟiyēm. ituvum viriviṉeḻuka.
iṉic carittira campantamākavum purāṇa campantamākavum tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ campantamākavum
mikappērpaṭit toḷirum maturimā nakariṉ kaṇṇē putitākat 'tamiḻc caṅkam' oṉṟu
tāpikkappaṭṭu naṭantu varukiṉṟatu. ataṟku aṅkamākap 'pāṇṭiyaṉ puttakacāli'yum
'cētupati centamiḻk kalācāli'yum ēṟpaṭuttap paṭṭuṉa. muṉṉataṉkaṭpala arumiyāṉa ēṭṭup
piratikaḷ cēmittu yāvaruñ ceṉṟupārkkumpaṭi vikkappaṭṭuṉa; accāṉa nūlkaḷuḷḷum anēka
mirukkiṉṟaṉa. piṉṉataṉ kaṭ kalvi payilum māṇākkar palar, nūlkaḷum āṭiyu muṇavum
caṅkattārutava, avaṟṟip peṟṟup paṭikkiṉṟaṉar.iccaṅkattiṟku avayavikaḷāka ēṟakkuṟiya
aimpatiṉmar pulavar irukkiṉṟaṉar. ivarkaḷellām varuṣam orumuṟi kūṭukiṉṟaṉar; kūṭit
tamiḻmoḻi muṉṉukku varutaṟkuriya yōcaṉikaḷ ceykiṉṟaṉar. caṅkattiṟkeṉat taṉiyē ōr
acciyantira cāli ēṟpaṭuttappaṭṭuḷatu. ataṉ mūlamākap pala ariya tamiḻ nūlkaḷic caṅkattār
acciṭappōkiṉṟaṉar. 'centamiḻ' eṉṉun talippeyariṭṭu oru mātāntat tamiḻp pattiriki
naṭātti varukiṉṟaṉar. ataṉ kaṇṇē pala arumiyāṉa viṣayaṅkaḷ veḷippaṭukiṉṟaṉa. caṅkattār
āṅkilak kalācālikaḷiṟ kaṟkum māṇākkarkkut tamiḻil ārvamuṇṭātal vēṇṭip
paṇapparicilum yōkkiyatā pattiramum vittup parīṭcikaḷēṟpaṭutti yirukkiṉṟaṉar. ivvāṟu
nal vaḻikaḷil uḻittu varum immaturit tamiḻccaṅkam nīṭūḻi niṉṟu ulavuvatāka.
āṅkilak kalācālikaḷiyoṭṭi āṅkāṅkut tamiḻc caṅkaṅkaḷ pala tāpikkappaṭṭu
naṭipeṟukiṉṟaṉa. utāraṇamākac ceṉṉik kiṟistava kalācāliyic cārntu 'tirāviṭa pāṣāpi
varttani caṅkam' eṉpatum ceṉṉi irācatāṉik kalācāliyiccārntu 'tamiḻccaṅkam' eṉpatum
naṭipeṟukiṉṟaṉa. eṉavē āṅkila varacāṭci tamiḻmoḻiyiṉ mutirccikkup pallāṟṟāṉum
utaviceytu varukiṉṟateṉpatu yāvarumuṇarnta viṣayam; mēṟkūṟiyavāṟu pala caṅkaṅkaḷ
cērvataṟkuk kikoṭuttum kaṭik kaṇittum parīkṣip pāṭa nūlkaḷuḷ tamiḻ nūlkaḷ palavaṟṟi
yēṟpaṭuttiyum āṅkilar tamiḻip pōṟṟukiṉṟaṉar. acciyantiram mutaliyaṉa avar
kaṇṭupiṭittut tantutaviṉar; tamiḻaratu nākarīka viruttikkun taṉipperuṅ karuviyāy
irukkiṉṟaṉar. kiyeḻuttu nūṟcāliyum amittirukkiṉṟaṉar. ivarkaḷatu aracu nīṭūḻi
nilittut tamiḻmoḻiyi mēṉmēlum vaḷarppatāka.

tāṉpēcum pāṣiyi māṟṟamuṭiyātu. makkaḷaṟiviṉṟiyē pāṣi mutirntukoṇṭucellum;

itaṉi yaṟiyap peṟāta vittiyanāta tēcikar naṉṉūlāratu uṭkaruttiyum uṇarātu,

eṉṟa naṉṉūṟ cūttira vitiyip,

oḻukalāṟṟi māṟṟutal evarālum muṭiyātatām. ātalāl tamiḻp pāṣiyiṉ oḻukalāṟu
iṉṉateṉṟu uyttuṇarntukoṇṭu, āṟṟu veḷḷattiṉ vēkattuṭaṉ celvāṉpōla, avvoḻuka
lāṟṟiṉi yoṭṭi ataṉōṭiyintu tamiḻp pulavarun tam pāṣiyic cīr tiruttik
koḷḷavēṇṭum.
iṉic cilar 'putiyaṉa pukutal tamiḻiṟku okkumāyiṉum paḻiyaṉa kaḻital ovvātu' eṉṟa
koḷkiyuṭiyārpōlat tām eḻutum nūlkaḷiṉkaṇ paḻiyaṉa kaḻinta coṟkaḷilum
colluruvaṅkaḷilum aḷaviṟantavaṟṟi vaḻaṅkukiṉṟaṉar. ivar ceyal tammuṭiya vallami kāṭṭip
paṭippōri maruṭṭit tammip paṟṟi avarakaḷ nalleṇṇam koḷḷa vēṇṭumeṉṟu niṉittuc
ceytatākak karutappaṭum. paṭalāl ivar tām eṇṇiyapaṭi naṉku matikkaṟpāṭu peṟuvatilli.
cāmāṉiya caṉaṅkaḷ iṉṉār nūlkaḷit toṭavum añcukiṉṟaṉar. mēlum iṅṅaṉa-meḻutuvār cuvi
pōliccuvi yeṉap palarum otukkuvar.

             tiruccukaṉūr alarmēṉ maṅkaittāyār mālai

   kāppu.
   vaṇmaittiruccukaṉūr vāḻalarmēṉ maṅkaittā
   yoṇmaittiruvaṭimē lōrcatamā - muṇmaittāñ
   coṉmālai cūṭṭaneñcē tolkurukaittaṇvakuḷa
   naṉmālaimāṟaṉṟāṇampu.
   nūl
   maṇimēviyavāritimakimāntarumvāṉavarum
   paṇimēviyapātalarumpaṇiniṉpatāmpuyamēṉ
   tiṇimēviyavuṭcilaipūttennāḷuntikaḻntiṭaccey
   aṇimēviyacukaṉūralarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (1)
   vañcakavalviṉaivittiṭṭumāyaivaṇṇīr maṭuttu
   viñcakaṅkāramviḷaittuppiṟaviviḻunalkum
   neñcakac ceyyiṉiṉpattippaiṅkūḻ nilai niṟkum vaṇṇam
   añcakappuṉkaḷai pōkkalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (2)
   kāṟṟiṟkaṟaṅkukaṟaṅkutaleṉṉavekkālamumvīṇ
   pēṟṟiṟṟiriyumeṉpētai maṉattai niṟpēṇumaṉpām
   ūṟṟiṟṟiḷaippittuyarniyā caniṭṭaiyutavimutti
   āṟṟiṟceluttiyaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (3)
   kōṟṟēṉoḻukumpoḻiṟcukaṉūrvāḻkulaviḷakkē
   kāṟṟēṉumōr nilainiṟkumatiṉuṅkaṭukineñcaṅ
   kūṟṟēyeṉaveṉaikkollumatuceykoṭumaitaṉai
   āṟṟēṉaṭakkiyaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (4)
   alaiyēṉumōrkālaṭaṅkiyumōrkālaṭaṅkalilā
   nilaiyēyumeṉṉuḷa niccalumōr nilainiṟṟaliṉṟip
   pulaiyēy vaḻiyē pukumiṉiyāṉappulaivaḻipōy
   alaiyēṉaḷitteṉaiyāḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (5)
   tuṉpakkaṭaliṟcuḻaṟṟiccuḻaṟṟittuṟuttiṭuñciṟ
   ṟiṉpakkaṭaṟkēyiyaineñcaiyeṉṟumiṉitteḻumpē
   riṉpakkaṭaṟkaṇiṉitāṭumvaṇṇamicaivi nalla
   aṉpakkayattar toḻumalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (6)
   alakaicceyaliṉalaiyumeṉṉeñcaniṉṉaṉpartamai
   ulakaittiruttavutittavareṉṟuṇarātavarpāṟ
   palakaittiṭuñceyal paṇṇumavar tampatapperumai
   alakaitterittuttiruttalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (7)
   navainavai neñcakanāṭoṟuñceytaliṉaṇṇukiṉṟa
   kuvaikuvaiyāmpavakkūṭṭamivvaṇṭamuṅkoḷḷakilā
   cuvaicuvaiyeṉṟeṉaittōṟpaiyiṭuti yeṉcolkuvalyāṉ
   avaiyavaittaṉṉataṭakkalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (8)
   kaṭippōtilvāḻayaṉ kaṟpampucittuṅkaḻivilpava
   noṭippōtiṟceyyavalatāmeṉṉeñcattiṉōṉmaiyeṉṉē
   piṭippōtakanaṭaippeṇṇaṇaṅkāmempirāṭṭiniṉ ṟaṉ
   aṭippōtaṭaintēṉaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (9)
   allavai ceyteṉakkākātatākiyalaiyumaṉam
   nallavai ceytu nalampeṟu nāḷeṉṟu naṇṇiṭumō
   pallavaiyōrcolcukācciramattuṟai pārkkaviyē
   allavaittaṉṉataṭakkalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (10)
   kuṭakkuṅkumamūlaikkōkaṉakāycukakkōppatiyāy
   kaṭakkuñcarātiyaṭakkuvaṉallakarumañceyyat
   toṭakkumpoḻutaṟkūṟu ceytē niṟkuntuṭṭaneñcai
   yaṭakkuntiṟameṉṉaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (11)
   viṭattai muṟikkumaruntuṇṭevarumviyakkavayat
   taṭattaippoṉṉākaccamaikkuṅkuḷikaikaṭāmumuṇṭu
   vaṭattaiyilaivayiṟṟemperumāṭṭiyeṉ vañcaneñciṉ
   aṭattaiyaṭakkuvateṉṉalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (12)
   pakattaiyuntaṉṟoḻilālveṉṟeḻiliḷampāvaiyartam
   pakattaiyuṅkaccuppaṟampaiyumeṇṇi yavarpaṇaittōṭ
   cukattai virumpittolaiyā narakattiṟṟu meṉṉe ñ
   cakattaittiruttiyaruḷalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (13)
   kaṉaikkuṅkaṭaṟpuvikaṇṇuñcukappatikkāraṇiyē
   niṉaikkuṅkaruviyeṉattantaneñcakaniṉṉaiyeṉṟum
   niṉaikkumpaṭi nirumitteṉṉaiyiṉṉumor nēriḻaiyām
   aṉaikkumpiyaṇṭātaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (14)
   cittupporuḷāmeṉakkaḷi neñceṉacceppucaṭa
   vattupporuḷeṉvaḻi paṭarātavakaiyeṉainiṉ
   cottupporuḷeṉaikkāttaṉiṉakkuccukirtamaṉṟō
   attuppatapaṅkayavalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (15)
   pantamumōkkamumpārkkiṉeṉṉeñcakappaṇpiṉiṟkum
   pantamuṟātataippakkuvañceytaṉiṉ pāramatāṅ
   kantamumpūvumeṉavempirāṉkaṭkalantirukkum
   antamumātiyumillalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (16)
   puṉaluṟupaṅkattaiyappuṉalkoṇṭaṟappōkkutalpōl
   uṉaluṟumuḷḷattiṉuṟṟa navaiyaiyavvuḷḷaṅkoṇṭē
   kaṉaluṟu tūceṉakkaṇṭaruḷaṉpākacaṭṭaiyaruḷ
   aṉaluṟuttittuṭaittāḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (17)
   taṭittiṉuñcañcalañcālavum vāyntu takāp pulaṉē
   piṭittataiyēyeṇṇippērātu niṟkiṉṟapētaineñcai
   vaṭitteṉvaya niṟkum vaṇṇañceyvāyeṉṟuṉ vāricanēr
   aṭittuṇaiyaṇṭaikoṇṭēṉalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (18)
   cāttuvikavakaṅkārattiṟṟōṉṟiyuñcāttuvikam
   nīttuvikkaṭavirācatatāmatanērnta vittaik
   kūttuvikaṟpaṅkoḷuneñcai niṉṉaṭi kūṭi niṟka
   yāttuvikacitañceyyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (19)
   pattuppoṟiyaiyumāḷuntalaimaipparākrama
   ramattiṉ cittupporuḷeṉṉaiyumaṅṅaṉāḷaccintikkumataṉ
   pittuppoṭittuppirapañcavāḻkkaiyaippēṇittuykkum
   attuppura voḻittāḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (20)
   upalamaṉamivvulakavuñaṟṟiluḻaittuḻaittu
   vipalamaṭaintatiṉiyēṉuniṉṉaṭi vīḻntiṟaiñcic
   capalamuṟumōvuṟātōvaṟiyēṉṟayai purivāy
   apalamillāccukaṉūralarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (21)
   viyarttaviṉaiyēvirumpivirumpiveṟuppilatāyp
   peyarttumpeyarttumatu taṉaiyēyeṉṟum pēṇiniṉṟu
   mayarttumayarttuvaruntumeṉpētaimaṉattukkeṉṟum
   ayarttalilaikolaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (22)
   aṟaṉkaṭaiyāṉateṉa nūlaṟaiyavaṟintiruntum
   piṟaṉkaṭai niṟpavaṭpēṇivaruntumeṉpētaineñcat.
   tiṟaṉkaṭaiyāṉatittīkkuṇantīrttuttirumiyeṉṟum
   aṟaṉkaṭaiyeṇṇātaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (23)
   niṉṉaippeṟiṉ maṉaṉēyeṉ kuṟaiyiṉa nērnteṉceyyēm
   eṉṉapparāṅkucamāmuṉicoṟṟaviyaliṉmaṉan
   taṉṉaippeṟileṟkuṉcāyucciyamuttitāṉkiṭaikkum
   aṉṉattuvacaṉṟoḻumalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (24)
   niṉpukaḻpāṭi niruttamiṭa niṉṉimalavuru
   iṉpuṟaveṇṇiyiṟaiñciṭalātiyiyaṟṟiṭavip
   puṉpuramvēṇṭaliṉaṉṉataimoyttiṭumpuṉkaṇelām
   aṉpuṭaṉīkkiyaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (25)
   cūraṇaṅkaittarumannōykaṭammaittuṭaikka voṇ
   kāraṇamuṇṭeṉilakakāraṇattaiyuṅkaṭṭuraiyāy
   nāraṇaṉākattiṉaṇṇunaṅkāy niṉai nampiniṉṟēṉ
   āraṇampōṟṟittoḻumalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (26)
   eḷiyēṉ muṟaikēṭṭiraṅkāyeṉiṉulakeṅkaṇuniṟ
   kiḷiyēyiyaiyumiyaintālunāṉiṉṟaṉeṇṇiṉvaṇam
   aḷiyēṉeṉileṉṉai ceyvalatuveṉṉakacceyalē
   aḷiyē tiruvuruvāmalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (27)
   olliyiyaṟṟiṉumollāteṉṟōtiṉumuḷḷanilai
   colliviṭuvaṉeṉṟollaiviṉaiyiṟṟoṭarntapava
   valliyaṟutteṉai vaikunta nāṭṭiṉkaṇvāḻavaippāy
   allimalarkkuḻalāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (28)
   kaṭuṅkālatūtuvarpōlaveruṭṭikkaṭukaṭuttu
   neṭuṅkālameṉṉainerukkiya nōykaḷunīrmaikoṇṭu
   piṭuṅkālam vantatuvēṇṭiṉaṉiṉṉai viḷampavaṟṟai
   aṭuṅkālamiḵtēyaruḷalar mēṉmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (29)
   caraṇavaraṇapperumaiyaṟintatalaiviniṉṉaic
   īraṇamaṭaintaveṉaikkaiviṭutārumamaṉṟuṉ
   caraṇakamalameṉṉeñcakkamalantayaṅkavamait
   karaṇameṉaniṉṟaḷiyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (30)
   cīrkkuṇayōkañ ciṟantatayāparaiyeṉṟu ceppum
   pērkkurittāṉavaḷāyeṉakkeṉṟum pirāppiyaiyāy
   kārkkurittāṉaniṉpālaṉṟiyeṉṟaṉalivaiyellām
   ārkkuraippēṉaruḷāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (31)
   nampiyaveṉṉaiyennāḷumpiṇiyiṉaṭunaṭuṅki
   vempiyaḻuṅkaviṭutalaṟameṉavēṇṭutiyēṟ
   pampiyapāvakkaṭaṉiṉṟumeṉṉaipparinteṭukkum
   ampiyaṉaiyavarāralarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (32)
   cēyiṉ ciṟumaiyaittīrttiṭuvāṉataiccērntoṟukkum
   nōyiṉṟakavukka nu kuṇamuṇṭatai nōkkalpeṟṟa
   kāyiṉ ceyaleṉiṟṟaṇṇaruḷāṟcakantantaḷikkum
   āyiṉ kaṭaṉeṉṉaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (33)
   aṇṭappiṇikuṭarvātamirumalukkākaramāṅ
   kaṇṭakkapāmayakkāraṇantaṉṉaikkaḻittaruḷvāy
   tuṇṭappiṟaikkuḷattāycukaṉūreñcuṭarkkoṭiyē
   aṇṭakkulātipaṉ pōṟṟalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (34)
   kapavarkkanōyneṭuṅkālamvaruttikkavaṟṟumataic
   cupavarkkamyāvuñceyavalla niṉṉaruḷāṟṟuṭaittāḷ
   nipavarkkañcoṟṟatai nīkkāviṭutaṉiyāyamaṉṟāl
   apavarkkamārkkamaruḷalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (35)
   koṭunōykkuvāṟkuṅkumaikkuṅkucumakkoṭuṅkaṇaiyāṉ
   viṭunōykkum vīṇē maṉattaiyeppōtum viṭātu paṟṟit
   toṭunōykkuñcūrkkaṭcuṭumukattoṉṉamaṉṟūtuvarvan
   taṭunōykkumañcātaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (36)
   kuṟṟañceyyāteṟṟiruttiyeṉṉāṟpaṇikoḷḷutiyēṟ
   kuṟṟaṅkaḷ kōṭiniṉakkuṟumō naṟkulamaṟaikaḷ
   kuṟṟaṅkaḷ kūṟiṭumōveṉaikkākkakkuṟittirukkum
   aṟṟamvaravilaiyōvalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (37)
   poṟukkavoṇṇāppurai ceytāraiyunī poṟuttaḷittāy
   poṟukkavoṇṇāppiḻai ceytavaṉeṉṟeṉaippuntitaṉil
   veṟukkaviṭukkavirakillai nantamviyaṉuṟavō
   aṟukkavoṇṇātu nammālalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (38)
   ippavanīṅkiṭiṉeppavam vāykku meṉavaṟiyēṉ
   ippavattē nīyiraṅkiyaruḷāyeṉiṉetirum
   appavattallalukkāṟṟēṉakamumaḻuṅkiyañcum
   appavappākoṭitāmalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (39)
   aṭiyēṉeṭuttavayaṉamanantamavaṟṟileṉṟun
   tuṭiyēṉuniṉṉait tutittilaṉākuntutittirukkiṟ
   kaṭiyēypiṟavikaḻiyātirātu kavalaiyuṟṟuṉ
   aṭiyēyaṭaintēṉaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (40)
   eṭuttapiṟavikaḷyāvumpatarāyiḻukkiṉavit
   toṭuttapiṟaviyiṉ kālamum vīṇētolaintatiṅṅaṉ
   viṭuttaṉaiyāyiṉeṉceykuval yāṉeṉvitiyaṟiyēṉ
   aṭuttaṉaṉiṉṉaiyaruḷalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (41)
   attiraviṉ pattaṭiyēṉaḻuntiyalaiyacceyniṉ
   mittiraṉeṉṉa niṉaikkumiṉṟōniṉaivēṇṭiniṟku m
   puttiravāṟcalliyameṉviṉaiyainiṉ poṅkaruḷām
   attirattālaṟuttāḷalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (42)
   ettaṉaikālameḷiyēṉiṟaiñciyiraṅkiṉumeṇ
   ṇattaṉaiyēṉumantōveṉṟiraṅkiyaruḷakillāc
   cittaṉainiṉ koṇkaṉaiyeṟkaruḷōr ciṟiyatiṉai
   attaṉai ceyyacceyvāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (43)
   akilāṇṭavāviyaḷakamatimukamāṭakanūṟ
   ṟukilāṇṭa ciṟṟiṭaittōkāy ciṟiyaṉaittoṇṭukoṇṭāḷ
   mukilāṇṭa cōlaittiruccukaṉūrccemmuḷarimiṉṉē
   akilāṇṭa nāyakiyēyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (44)
   mōkkattiliccaiyilaṉāyaṭiyaṉmuraṇavittait
   tūkkattirukkiṉṟaṉaṉantaviccaiyait tōṉṟuvitteṟ
   kākkattiruvuḷaṅkoḷvāycukappatikkaṟpakamē
   ākkattirumakaḷēyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (45)
   eṉṉēyeṉcai tanniyamōkkaviccaiyeḻāmalaṉṟō
   koṉṉē kaḻintaṉṟi tukāṟuṅkōkaṉakātaṉam vāḻ
   poṉṉēyatu viṉiyēṉumpayaṉpeṟappunticeyvāy
   aṉṉēyaruṭkaṭalēyalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (46)
   kuṭumpattaippūṇkaṭṭikkoḷḷumeṉakkataṉkōḷaṟuttu
   piṭumpattatiyillai nīyēnamaiyeṉṟum viṭṭoḻiyāk
   kaṭumpattanōkkiccukappativāḻkamalāyataṉai
   aṭumpattiravaruḷceyyalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (47)
   piṉṉaiyuniṉṉaṉpaṉaiyum vitiyiṉiṉērntaṭai yum
   muṉṉaimuttattuvañāṉamuṇṭākkimuṟaippaṭiyē
   eṉṉainiṉṟoṇṭariṉattoṭuñcērttiyellāvuyirkkum
   aṉṉaiyeṉum piṉṉaiyēyalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (48)
   tattuvañāṉamaṭaiyāttamōkuṇattaṉṉeṉiṉun
   tattuvañāṉikaḷcārpuṟuttittamiyēṉaiyavar
   cottuvamākkiyavarapimāṉattiṟṟoṇṭukoṇṭāḷ
   attuvamēṉippoṉṉēyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (49)
   cilaṅku paṟavaimiku caṭarēṉu namviṇṭucittar
   ilaṅkumaruḷiṉileytāta muttiyumeytumeṉpa
   ilaṅkuṅkaṭaiyaṉaiyaṉṉōrkaruṇaiyiṟkāttaruḷvāy
   alaṅkumaṇiyaṇiyāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (50)
   pātavamāyiṉuniṉṉaṭikkaṉpu ceypaṇpuṭaiya
   mātavarkaitoṭiṉkaṇpārkkiṉ māyaimayakkameṉa
   ōtavanīṅkiyuyarvīṭaṭaiyumeṉavuraippa
   ātavar nērkuḻaiyāyalar mēṉmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (51)
   acittuṟavāṉvarumaññāṉaniṉṉaiyaṇṭātavaṇam
   vicitteṉai nāṭoṟum vīḻkatiyiṟṟaḷḷi vīṇpayaṉē
   pucittiṭacceyyumeṉceykēṉatai niṉṟaṉ poṅkaruḷām
   acittumitteṉṟaṉaiyāḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (52)
   coṟṟavaññāṉameṉ ṟoṉmaikkuṇaṅkaḷait toṭṭaḻittu
   veṟṟamuṟṟeṉṟum vivitappiṟaviyil vīḻttiyaṅṅaṉ
   peṟṟavarukkatināṉeṉṟeṇṇacceyumpeṟṟiyatai
   aṟṟeṉṟuṉaiyaṭaivēṉalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (53)
   tēkattil vēṟeṉuñāṉameṉakkuc ceṉittatuṇṭēl
   ēkattaṉimutalpōkicutantaraṉeṉṟeṇṇuvēṉ
   pākattapputtiyaippāṟṟiṭuvāy paṇippātavattāṉ
   ākattuṟaiyamalāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (54)
   cēṭattuvamaticērntiṭumēlataittīyarkaḷpāl
   vīṭattuvamā viṉiyōkitteṉṟumvipalamuṟuṅ
   kūṭattuvattaikkulaitteṉainiṉṉaṉparkūṭṭaniṟīi
   āṭattuvaniṉatēyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (55)
   uyirapakārañ ceytūḻitoṟūḻiyuṇṭālumaṟāc
   ceyiraṉaittuñ ceytu tīppulattaṟparaṉāyttirivēṉ
   vayiraviṉaiyaṟutteṉṉainiṉ pātamvaṇaṅkacceyvāy
   ayiranaṟpākaṉaiyāyalar mēṉmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (56)
   naṉṟuniṉpātattai naṇṇiyuyvāṉalkiyavuṭalkoṇ
   oṉṟumilāmaṟkiṭantaveṉṟurttacaikkuṟṟiraṅki
   ṭeṉṟu niṉṉuḷḷakkukappāṅkarumameṉaittuñceyyā
   taṉṟiṉaṉāṉēṉaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (57)
   āṟukaṭakkavaruḷampipaṟṟiyataṉkaraiyai
   yēṟutaliṉṟiyiruṅkaṭaleytuvareṉṉa niṉak
   kūṟuceytēṉumuyaniṉṟiruvuḷattuṉṉimutti
   āṟunaṭattiyaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (58)
   piṟavipperuṅkaṭaṉīntakkoṭuttavippiṇṭamataip
   piṟavipperuṅkaṭaṟkēyuṭalākkumeṉpēravittai
   aṟavippiramantolaiyavaruḷ ceyyaṇiccukaṉūr
   aṟavippirartoḻuntāḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (59)
   anātiyeṉṟāraṇa nūlāticollumavittaiyiṉāṉ
   manāticeyyeṇṇaṟṟa puṇṇiya pāvamarapiṉvaṇam
   vanātipalapiṟappum piṟanteyttavivvañcakaṉuk
   kanātinalvīṭaḷittāḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē       (60)
   iṟantumuṉceṉṟaviṉiccelpavōrāviyaṟkaiyiṉāṟ
   piṟantapiṟavikaṭōṟumuttāpapperuṅkaṉaliṉ
   vaṟantaṉaṉaṅṅaṉvaṟantuñciṟitum varuttamilēṉ
   aṟanta virāccukaṉūralarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (61)
   karuppañcaṉaṉamiḷamainalyauvaṉaṅkāḻmutumai
   viruppaṟucākkāṭiruḷāmavattai viḷaivitteṉṟum
   uruppavuṟuvaṟparamparaiyuṇpittuṟai piṟavi
   aruppamoḻittaruḷvāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (62)
   aṉantavarantai kaṭakākaramāmayaṉakkaṭalil
   aṉan ta vitattumaḻuntāvaṇanīyaṉutiṉañcey
   aṉantamuyaṟciyaiyuntaṭutteṉṉaiyalaiyavittai
   aṉantaloḻittaruḷvāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (63)
   piṟantapoḻutuṉkaṭaiviḻinōkkiṉaippeṟṟirukkiṟ
   ciṟanta naṟcāttuvikaṉāyviññāṉacciṟappiṉaṉāyt
   tuṟantanilaiyiṉaṉāymutticcintaiyaittuṉṉi niṟpaṉ
   aṟantalai niṉṟavarpōṟṟalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (64)
   uṇmaimatiyutiyātāyiṉal vīṭuṟu talilai
   uṇmai naṉṉūliṉupatēcantaṉṉiṉavvuṇmai matit
   tiṇmaiyuṇṭākumeṉacceppupavacciṟantamati
   aṇmaiyuṇṭākavaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (65)
   cāttirañāṉam piṟattalarumaitavattuyarāñ
   cāttiyamāṉatu maṉṟupatēcattaṭattiṉvaru
   mēttiyañāṉamviḷaitteṉaikkāttimuṉmēyttu kanta
   āttiraḷāṉurattāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (66)
   eṉṉilai tēkam poṟiyuḷ pirāṇaṉilaṅkuputtit
   toṉṉilaikkeṉṟumvilakkaṇamākumeṉattuṇip
   innilaiyeṉṟumeṉakkuviḷaṅkātiyaṟṟiniṟkum
   aṉṉilainīttāḷutiyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (67)
   ñāṉantaṉaiyoḻintuntōṉṟaliṉeṉai ñāṉanaṉṉūl
   īṉantavirntacuyampirakācaṉeṉavuraikkum
   ēṉantavirnteṉṟuminnilainiṟkaveṉakkaruḷvāy
   āṉantavāritiyēyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (68)
   cukavuruvākaviruttaliṉeṉṉaiccuṭarmaṟaikaḷ
   takavuṟumāṉantattaṉmaiyaṉeṉṉumattaṉmaiyeṉṟum
   ukavuṟumāyaiyoḻitteṉakkannilaiyuṟṟu niṟka
   akamakiḻntiṉṉaruḷceyyalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (69)
   eppōḻtiṉumyāṉiruttaliṉittiyaṉivvuṭalam
   eppōḻtuñcērtal piṟattaloḻitaliṟattaleṉpa
   oppō tariyacukappatiyāyivvuṭalamviṭum
   appōteṉmuṉvantaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (70)
   irukkumitayakamalavirukkaiyirunteḻumpip
   porukkeṉappōtalvarutal puritaliṟ pōṟṟumeṉai
   arukkaṇuveṉpavataikkaṇṭutēṟavaruḷpurivāy
   arukkarpalaroḷiyāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (71)
   maṇiyātiyōruḻiniṉṟu moḷiyiṉ malartaleṉat
   tiṇiyāritayattiruntunteruṭciyiṟṟēkamuṟum
   piṇiyātiyāṉaṉkutērntiṭum peṟṟipiṟaṅkacceyvāy
   aṇiyārcukappatiyāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (72)
   viḻiyātikaṭkukkaṭātikaḷpōl viḷaṅkāmaiyiṉā l
   iḻiyāppoṟikaḷukkeṭṭāṉviyattaṉaṉ ṟeṉṟuraippa
   kaḻiyātanāṉintakkāṭciyaikkaṇṭukaḷittirukka
   aḻiyāppataviyaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (73)
   ēṇṇāracittōṭiyaiyuñcacātiyameṉṟuṇara
   oṇṇāmaiyiṟṟāṉacintiyaṉeṉṟeṉaiyō tuvarāṟ
   ṟiṇṇārntavinnilai tērvitteṉaiyuṇṭa tīnirayam
   aṇṇāttal ceyyavaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (74)
   collāracittupporuḷpōlavayavattiṉṟokuti
   illāmaiyiṉālavayavamillāṉeṉavuraippa
   villāruṇarvuruvēṉeṉṉumītu viḷakkiṭuvāy
   allārkuḻaṉmaṇattāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (75)
   ēyntavacittiṉvikarikkaiyiṉṟiyiruttaliṉāl
   vāyntavikāramilāṉeṉpavivvurai vāymaiyatē
   tōyntaveṉatu cataikavuruvantulaṅkacceyvāy
   āyntavarēttippu kaḻalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē. (76)
   ētippaṭaiyerinīr vāyuveyyilivvātiyiṉāṟ
   cētittalāticceyaleṉkaṭ cērātteḷivutantu
   nītittarumaniṟuttiniṉpūcaṉai niṭṭaitantāḷ
   ātikkamalavaṇaiyalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (77)
   cākkiyarāviyaiyavvaccarīrattaṉaiyateṉpa
   tūkkiṉatumuraṇākuñcurutikkattōmatari
   ṉīkkiyeṉaiyāḷutinikamāntanikaḻttaṉanta
   ākkiyaiyēcukaṉūralarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē. (78)
   ūṉāccirayavuṭaṉēṭiyeṉṟu muḻalumeṉai
   ñāṉāccirayaṉeṉa navilvāratainaṉkuraitteṉ
   nāṉācciramamunāmpinacikkacceynāṉmaṟaiyiṉ
   āṉāccirattuṟaivāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē. (79 )
   vittāṉañātāveṉaveṉaikkūṟaliṉ vēṭkaiyuṟuṅ
   kattāvun tuyttiṭum pōktāvumāyiṉaṉkaṇṇumintac
   cattāṉacāraviṭayattaiccāṟṟutitācaruḷḷa
   attāṇiyārntuṟaivāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (80)
   nīkkavariyavilakkaṇamākuniyāmiyattō
   ṭōkkamuṟutāriyañ cēṭameṉṉumuvaiyuḷattiṟ
   ṟēkkiṭumvaṇṇanteḷintiṭaccepputi tīṉacaṉark
   kākkamaḷittaruḷceyyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (81)
   naṉṟu ceycēṭattuvattoṭuñāturttuvamaṭiyaṟ
   koṉṟu milakkaṇameṉṟōtturaikkumuraittapeṟṟi
   niṉṟu tamiyaṉiṉaiyikkaḷēparaniṉṟeḻumpum
   aṉṟuniṉaikkavaruḷalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē. (82)
   nilakkaṇilaviniṉainiṉaippōrai nikaḻttumuttit
   talakkaṇuṟuvikkattaṇcukaṉūrvāḻtalaiviyeṉṟaṉ
   lakkaṇamuṟṟu milaṅkacceytiṉṉāviṭarppiṟavi
   alakkaṇaṟuttaruḷvāyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (83)
   īṭṭuṭaṉettaṉaikāṉmuṟaicolliṉumeṉṉaiyeṉṟu
   kēṭṭuraiyoṉṟukiḷakkiṉiṉakkuṟuṅkētameṉṉē
   kāṭṭutaṉāṭṭutal vīṭṭutaleṉṉuṅkarumaviḷai
   yāṭṭuṭaiyāycukaṉūralarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (84)
   pāvikaḷiṟṟalaiyāyaveṉakkaruḷpaṇṇaveṇṇiṟ
   ṟāvikaṉiṉṉoṭuceytu taṭukkaccamaipavarār
   nāvikamaḻkuḻalāy nākavētaṇṭaṉāyakiyē
   āvikaṭkāviyeṉumalarmēlmaṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (85)
   tirukkāṇilayañciṉañcērkaḷañciyañ ceṉmavittāñ
   cerukkāṇivēcameṉcintainiṉ cēvaṭi cēracceyteṉ
   karukkāṇinīkkiniṉkāṟkāṇivāḻakkaruṇai ceytāḷ

   oṇmaiyilaupātikattāyuñcēykkuṟṟamuṟṟuraiyāḷ
   kaṇmaikaruttuvaḷkāḻtta pallaṇṭaṅkaḷ kaṇṭaḷikkun
   tiṇmaipeṟunirupātikavaṉṉai ceyalituvō
   aṇmaiyiṉeṉṉaiyaḷiyalarmēṉ maṅkaiyammaṉaiyē.       (87)
   pētaippirākirutattāyuntaṉcutaṉ peṟṟikaḷait
   kātaikkuñcāṟṟalaṭaṉṉuḷaṭakakic camittiṭuvaḷ
   kōtaikkuḻalappirākirutattaṉṉai koḷkaiyitō

   tuṉpiḻaittāraiyun tūrttarkaḷeṉṟu turattiviṭā

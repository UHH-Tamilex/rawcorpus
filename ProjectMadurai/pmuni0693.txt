veḷiyiṭuvōr mukavuri

ñāṉak kuṟavañci eṉṉum innūlil kuṟavañci naṭiyil ñāṉattiṉi eḷimiyākak kūṟuvatu.
"kuṟavañci'' eṉpatu “kuṟattippāṭṭu" allatu kuṟam eṉum peyar peṟum. kuruva nāṭakam
eṉpatum ivvakiyiṉic cārntatē. itu 96 vakip pirapantaṅkaḷuḷ oṉṟu. perumpāṉmi nāṭaka
vaṭivil amiyap peṟṟatu. kaṭavuḷiyō, aracariyō, vaḷḷariyō, celvariyō pāṭṭuṭit
talivarākak koṇṭu pāṭappaṭuvatu.
innūliṉ patippāciriyar tiru vī. cokkaliṅkam eṉpavar. innūl paruva itaḻ vāyilāka
veḷivarappeṟṟu, taṟpoḻutu mutaṟ patippup puttakamāka veḷivantuḷḷatu.
innūl veḷivara āvaṉa ceytuḷḷa nūlaka niruvāka aluvalar tiru. cāmi. civañāṉam
avarkaḷukkum, nūlaka veḷiyīṭṭu mēlāḷar tiru. a. pañcanātaṉ avarkaḷukkum, innūli
naṉmuṟiyil acciṭṭuttanta nūlaka accakap paṇiyāḷarkaḷukkum eṉatu pārāṭṭukkaḷ.
        ci. kōcalarāmaṉ, i. ā. pa.,
        māvaṭṭa āṭcit talivar maṟṟum iyakkunar,
caracuvati makāl nūlakam.
tañcāvūr, 17-7-2002
-----
vēṇṭukōḷ
karuṇiyuḷḷam koṇṭa nam muṉṉōrkaḷ, ariya periya ilakkiyaṅkaḷiyum, piṟavaṟṟiyum,
paṉiyōlikaḷil eḻutic cuvaṭikaḷāka namakkut tantaṉar. avi, palvēṟu iṭaṅkaḷil muṭaṅki
uḷḷaṉa. cuvaṭikaḷ paḻutaṭivataṟku muṉ caracuvati makālukku aṉpaḷippākak koṭuttutaviṉāl,
avi makkaḷukkup payaṉpaṭum.
makālukkuk koṭuppataṉ mūlam, cuvaṭi tantavarkaḷum, cuvaṭi eḻutiyōrum aḻiyāp pukaḻi,
perumicāl caracuvati makāl uḷḷaḷavum peṟuvar. avi patippāki varumāyiṉ cuvaṭi tantār
peyar iṭam peṟuvatōṭu, appatippil aintu piratikaḷum peṟuvar.
eṉavē, "nām peṟṟa pēṟu peṟuka iv viyakam" eṉṟa eṇṇamuṭiya naṟpaṇpāḷarkaḷ tammiṭamuḷḷa
cuvaṭikaḷic caracuvati makālukkut tantutava vēṇṭukiṟēṉ.
tañcāvūr.         māvaṭṭa āṭciyar maṟṟum iyakkunar,
15-5-2002.         caracuvati makāl nūlakam.

tamiḻ vāḻka

muṉṉuri

kallāliṉ puṭiyamarntu nāṉmaṟiyā
        (ā)ṟaṅkamutaṟ kaṟṟa kēḷvi
vallārkaḷ nālvarukkum vākkiṟanta
       pūraṇamāy maṟikkap pālāy
ellāmāy allatumāy iruntataṉi
       iruntapaṭi iruntu kāṭṭic
collāmaṟ coṉṉavari niṉiyāmal
       niṉintupavat toṭakki velvām.
eṉpatu tiruviḷiyāṭaṟpurāṇam. ñāṉamē vaṭivāṉa kaṭavuḷ kallāla marattiṉ kīḻiruntu
caṉakāti nālvarukkum, nāṉku maṟi, āṟu aṅkaṅkaḷ mutalāṉa ellāvaṟṟiyum, tiruvāy
malarntaruḷutaliṉṟit taṉ tirukkara māttirac ciṉmuttiri eṉṉum aṭiyāḷaṅkāṭṭiyē
uṇartta, atu koṇṭu avarkaḷum aṉittum uṇarntaṉar.
avvāṟu peṟuvōr taliciṟanta māṇākkarāvar. tali, iṭi, kaṭi māṇākkar eṉpatu pōla
makkaḷum, koḷuttap paṟṟum kaṟpūra aṟivuṭiyār eṉṟum, caṟṟu muyaṉṟu paṟṟavikkap paṟṟum
kari aṟivuṭiyār eṉṟum, evvaḷavu muyaṉṟum paṟṟāta katali (vāḻittaṇṭu) aṟivuṭiyār
eṉṟum muttarattiṉarāvar. ettarattiṉarukkum payaṉ tarumāṟu eḻuntatē inta
'ñāṉakkuṟavañci'.
kuṟavañci naṭiyil ñāṉattiṉi eḷimiyākak kūṟuvatu 'ñāṉak kuṟavañci' yākum. itu ematu
makāl cuvaṭi eṇ. 821aik koṇṭatu. mūṉṟu pakutikaḷi uṭiyatu. āciriyar peyar
teḷivākak kiṭikkappeṟavilli eṉṟālum, mutaliraṇṭu pakutikaḷi iyaṟṟiyavar
'kumarakuruparaṉ' eṉṟu oruvāṟu terikiṉṟatu. mūṉṟām pakuti 'pīrumukammatu’ eṉpavarāṟ
ceyyappaṭṭatu. eḷiya naṭiyil uḷḷa itu oru perun tattuva nūl. nalla payaṉit taruvatu.
'kuṟavañci' eṉpatu 'kuṟattippāṭṭu' allatu 'kuṟam' eṉap peyar peṟum. 'kuḷuva
nāṭakam' eṉpatum ivvakiyiṉic cārntatē. itu toṇṇūṟṟāṟuvakip pirapantaṅkaḷuḷ oṉṟu.
perumpāṉmi nāṭaka vaṭivil amiyappeṟṟatu. kaṭavuḷiyō, aracariyō, vaḷḷaliyō,
celvariyō, pāṭṭuṭit talivarākak koṇṭu pāṭappaṭuvatu.
talivaṉ ulāvarutal, talivi kāmuṟutal, kāmanōykoṭu vāṭutal. tūtuviṭuttal, kuṟatti
varutal, kuṟi pakartal, kuṟavaṉ varutal, kuṟavaṉ kuṟatti vātiṭutal mutalāṉavikaḷ
amiyappāṭuvatākum.
inta 'ñāṉakkuṟavañci’yil mēṟkūṟiya talivaṉ ulāvaral mutalāṉavikaḷ iṉṟik kuṟavaṉ varuki,
kuṟavaṉ taṉ perumi kūṟutal, tirumalic ciṟappuk kūṟutal, kuṟatti varuki, kuṟavaṉ
kuṟatti vātiṭutal, kuṟavaṉ paṟavi varavu kūṟutal, amiti vēṇṭal, meññāṉappuḷ
pōkātiruttal, kaṇṇi koṇṭuvarutal, kaṇṇikaḷit tiruttivittal, kuṟavaṉ ñāṉappuḷḷip
piṭittal, vāḻttu ākiya talippukaḷuṭaṉ mutaṟpakuti muṭivuṟukiṉṟatu.
iraṇṭām pakutiyil kuṟavaṉ tōṟṟam, kuṟavaṉ taṉ contamali kūṟutal. civapaṭci varuvatik
kūṟutal, amiti vēṇṭal, civapaṭcikaḷic cuṟṟi vaḷittal, avikaḷ pōkāmaṟ kāttal, avikaḷ
irimēytal, kaṇṇi koṇṭuvarutal, civa paṭcikaḷip piṭittal, vāḻttu ākiya uṟuppukaḷ
kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
mūṉṟām pakutiyil ciṅkaṉ ciṅki tarukkam eṉṉum uriyāṭal pōṉṟa viṉāviṭi koṇṭu
vātiṭutal maṭṭumē kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷatu.
mutaṟpakutiyil ñāṉattiṉip paṟṟutal pōlavum, iraṇṭām pakutiyil civattiṉip paṟṟutal
pōlavum, mūṉṟām pakutiyil viṉāviṭi vāyilākat tattuvaṅkaḷip paṟṟutal pōlavum
kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
maruttuvattiṉi maṉatil patiyavittaṟporuṭṭu eḷiya naṭiyil vittiyakkummi, vittiyak
kuṟavañci mutalāṉa nūlkaḷ eḻuntaṉapōla, ñāṉam peṟa ñāṉakkummi. ñāṉakkuṟavañci.
meññāṉakkuṟavañci, ñāṉarettiṉakkuṟavañci, tattuvak kuṟavañci, vētāntak kuṟam mutalāṉa
pala nūlkaḷ eḻuntaṉa. eḷiya naṭiyēyāyiṉum tattuvak karuttukkaḷi uḷḷaṭakkik kūṟiyuḷḷa
viṭaṅkaḷil uruvakam tantu kūṟiyuḷḷār.
'tirumalic ciṟappuk kūṟum talippil 'kantaṉ eṅkōṉ tirumali makimi colvēṉ'
eṉkiṟār mutaṟṟokutiyil, 'tirumali' eṉpatu tiruppati (vēṅkaṭamali)yiṉik kuṟikkum
eṉpatu uṇaraṟpāṟṟu, taṟpōtu ' tirumali tiruppati' eṉa vaḻaṅkappaṭukiṉṟatu, vaṭavēṅkaṭam,
vaṭavēṅkaṭamuṭiyāṉik kantak kaṭavuḷ eṉpar oru cārār.
iṉikkuṟavañci variciyil pala kuṟavañcikaḷ uḷḷaṉa avaṟṟuḷ cila:

mēṟkaṇṭa kuṟavañcikaḷuḷ cilavaṟṟil ñāṉamum tattuvaṅkaḷum vētāntaṅkaḷum pēcappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.
innūl mikavum citintum muṟintum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟatu. 42 ēṭukaḷik koṇṭatu. itaṉi
āyntu veḷiyiṭap paramporuḷiṉ tiruvaruḷ tuṇi ceytamikku vāḻtti vaṇaṅkukiṉṟēṉ.
innūliṉi veḷiyiṭa aṉumatiyaḷitta ematu makāl keḷaravak kāriyatarici tirumiku
vittuvāṉ a. vaṭivēlaṉ avarkaṭku eṉatu naṉṟiyiṉit terivittukkoḷḷukiṉṟēṉ.
innūlil aṟiyāmiyiṉāl ēṟpaṭṭa piḻikaḷ iruppiṉ ataṉip poruttaruḷa vēṇṭuvatōṭu,
avikaḷ tiruttam peṟavum utava vēṇṭumeṉak kaṟṟuvalla pulavarkaḷi vaṇaṅkik
kēṭṭukkoḷḷukiṉṟēṉ.
ippaṇi iṉitu niṟiveytak kalimakaḷiṉ tiruppātaṅkaḷic ciramēṟ koḷḷu-kiṉṟēṉ.
vāḻka tamiḻ; vaḷarka meyññāṉam.

vēṇṭukōḷ

karuṇi paḷḷaṅ koṇṭa nam muṉṉōrkaḷ , ariya periya ilakkiyaṅkaḷiyum, piṟavaṟṟiyum,
paṉiyōlikaḷil eḻutic cuvaṭikaḷāka namakkut tantaṉar. avi, pal vēṟu iṭaṅkaḷil muṭaṅki
uḷḷaṉa. cuvaṭikaḷ paḻutaṭivataṟku muṉ caracuvati makālukku aṉpaḷippākak koṭuttutaviṉāl,
avi makkaḷukkup payaṉpaṭum.
makālukkuk koṭuppataṉ mūlam, cuvaṭi tantavarkaḷum, cuvaṭi eḻutiyōrum aḻiyāp pukaḻi,
perumicāl caracuvati makāl uḷḷaḷavum peṟuvar. avi patippāki varumāyiṉ cuvaṭi tantār
peyar iṭam peṟuvatōṭu, appatippil aintu piratikaḷum peṟuvar.
eṉavē, " nām peṟṟa pēṟu peṟuka iv viyakam" eṉṟa eṇṇamuṭiya naṟpaṇpāḷarkaḷ tammiṭamuḷḷa
cuvaṭikaḷic caracuvati makālukkut tantutava vēṇṭukiṟēṉ.
tañcāvūr māvaṭṭa āṭciyar maṟṟum iyakkunar.

                         veḷiyiṭuvōr mukavurai

       ñāṉak kuṟavañci eṉṉum innūlil kuṟavañci naṭaiyil ñāṉattiṉai
       eḷimaiyākak kūṟuvatu. "kuṟavañci'' eṉpatu “kuṟattippāṭṭu" allatu
       kuṟam eṉum peyar peṟum. kuruva nāṭakam eṉpatum ivvakaiyiṉaic
       cārntatē. itu 96 vakaip pirapantaṅkaḷuḷ oṉṟu. perumpāṉmai nāṭaka
       vaṭivil amaiyap peṟṟatu. kaṭavuḷaiyō, aracaraiyō, vaḷḷaraiyō,
       celvaraiyō pāṭṭuṭait talaivarākak koṇṭu pāṭappaṭuvatu.
       innūliṉ patippāciriyar tiru vī. cokkaliṅkam eṉpavar. innūl paruva
       itaḻ vāyilāka veḷivarappeṟṟu, taṟpoḻutu mutaṟ patippup puttakamāka
       veḷivantuḷḷatu.
       innūl veḷivara āvaṉa ceytuḷḷa nūlaka niruvāka aluvalar tiru. cāmi.
       civañāṉam avarkaḷukkum, nūlaka veḷiyīṭṭu mēlāḷar tiru. a.
       pañcanātaṉ avarkaḷukkum, innūlai naṉmuṟaiyil acciṭṭuttanta nūlaka
       accakap paṇiyāḷarkaḷukkum eṉatu pārāṭṭukkaḷ.
               ci. kōcalarāmaṉ, i. ā. pa.,
               māvaṭṭa āṭcit talaivar maṟṟum iyakkunar,
       caracuvati makāl nūlakam.
       tañcāvūr, 17-7-2002
       -----
       vēṇṭukōḷ
       karuṇaiyuḷḷam koṇṭa nam muṉṉōrkaḷ, ariya periya ilakkiyaṅkaḷaiyum,
       piṟavaṟṟaiyum, paṉaiyōlaikaḷil eḻutic cuvaṭikaḷāka namakkut
       tantaṉar. avai, palvēṟu iṭaṅkaḷil muṭaṅki uḷḷaṉa. cuvaṭikaḷ
       paḻutaṭaivataṟku muṉ caracuvati makālukku aṉpaḷippākak
       koṭuttutaviṉāl, avai makkaḷukkup payaṉpaṭum.
       makālukkuk koṭuppataṉ mūlam, cuvaṭi tantavarkaḷum, cuvaṭi
       eḻutiyōrum aḻiyāp pukaḻai, perumaicāl caracuvati makāl uḷḷaḷavum
       peṟuvar. avai patippāki varumāyiṉ cuvaṭi tantār peyar iṭam
       peṟuvatōṭu, appatippil aintu piratikaḷum peṟuvar.
       eṉavē, "nām peṟṟa pēṟu peṟuka iv vaiyakam" eṉṟa eṇṇamuṭaiya
       naṟpaṇpāḷarkaḷ tammiṭamuḷḷa cuvaṭikaḷaic caracuvati makālukkut
       tantutava vēṇṭukiṟēṉ.
       tañcāvūr.         māvaṭṭa āṭciyar maṟṟum iyakkunar,
       15-5-2002.         caracuvati makāl nūlakam.

       tamiḻ vāḻka

                                muṉṉurai

       kallāliṉ puṭaiyamarntu nāṉmaṟaiyā
               (ā)ṟaṅkamutaṟ kaṟṟa kēḷvi
       vallārkaḷ nālvarukkum vākkiṟanta
              pūraṇamāy maṟaikkap pālāy
       ellāmāy allatumāy iruntataṉai
              iruntapaṭi iruntu kāṭṭic
       collāmaṟ coṉṉavarai niṉaiyāmal
              niṉaintupavat toṭakkai velvām.
       eṉpatu tiruviḷaiyāṭaṟpurāṇam. ñāṉamē vaṭivāṉa kaṭavuḷ kallāla
       marattiṉ kīḻiruntu caṉakāti nālvarukkum, nāṉku maṟai, āṟu aṅkaṅkaḷ
       mutalāṉa ellāvaṟṟaiyum, tiruvāy malarntaruḷutaliṉṟit taṉ tirukkara
       māttirac ciṉmuttirai eṉṉum aṭaiyāḷaṅkāṭṭiyē uṇartta, atu koṇṭu
       avarkaḷum aṉaittum uṇarntaṉar.
       avvāṟu peṟuvōr talaiciṟanta māṇākkarāvar. talai, iṭai, kaṭai
       māṇākkar eṉpatu pōla makkaḷum, koḷuttap paṟṟum kaṟpūra aṟivuṭaiyār
       eṉṟum, caṟṟu muyaṉṟu paṟṟavaikkap paṟṟum kari aṟivuṭaiyār eṉṟum,
       evvaḷavu muyaṉṟum paṟṟāta katali (vāḻaittaṇṭu) aṟivuṭaiyār eṉṟum
       muttarattiṉarāvar. ettarattiṉarukkum payaṉ tarumāṟu eḻuntatē inta
       'ñāṉakkuṟavañci'.
       kuṟavañci naṭaiyil ñāṉattiṉai eḷimaiyākak kūṟuvatu 'ñāṉak
       kuṟavañci' yākum. itu ematu makāl cuvaṭi eṇ. 821aik koṇṭatu. mūṉṟu
       pakutikaḷai uṭaiyatu. āciriyar peyar teḷivākak kiṭaikkappeṟavillai
       eṉṟālum, mutaliraṇṭu pakutikaḷai iyaṟṟiyavar 'kumarakuruparaṉ' eṉṟu
       oruvāṟu terikiṉṟatu. mūṉṟām pakuti 'pīrumukammatu’ eṉpavarāṟ
       ceyyappaṭṭatu. eḷiya naṭaiyil uḷḷa itu oru perun tattuva nūl. nalla
       payaṉait taruvatu.
       'kuṟavañci' eṉpatu 'kuṟattippāṭṭu' allatu 'kuṟam' eṉap peyar peṟum.
       'kuḷuva nāṭakam' eṉpatum ivvakaiyiṉaic cārntatē. itu
       toṇṇūṟṟāṟuvakaip pirapantaṅkaḷuḷ oṉṟu. perumpāṉmai nāṭaka vaṭivil
       amaiyappeṟṟatu. kaṭavuḷaiyō, aracaraiyō, vaḷḷalaiyō, celvaraiyō,
       pāṭṭuṭait talaivarākak koṇṭu pāṭappaṭuvatu.
       talaivaṉ ulāvarutal, talaivi kāmuṟutal, kāmanōykoṭu vāṭutal.
       tūtuviṭuttal, kuṟatti varutal, kuṟi pakartal, kuṟavaṉ varutal,
       kuṟavaṉ kuṟatti vātiṭutal mutalāṉavaikaḷ amaiyappāṭuvatākum.
       inta 'ñāṉakkuṟavañci’yil mēṟkūṟiya talaivaṉ ulāvaral mutalāṉavaikaḷ
       iṉṟik kuṟavaṉ varukai, kuṟavaṉ taṉ perumai kūṟutal, tirumalaic
       ciṟappuk kūṟutal, kuṟatti varukai, kuṟavaṉ kuṟatti vātiṭutal,
       kuṟavaṉ paṟavai varavu kūṟutal, amaiti vēṇṭal, meññāṉappuḷ
       pōkātiruttal, kaṇṇi koṇṭuvarutal, kaṇṇikaḷait tiruttivaittal,
       kuṟavaṉ ñāṉappuḷḷaip piṭittal, vāḻttu ākiya talaippukaḷuṭaṉ
       mutaṟpakuti muṭivuṟukiṉṟatu.
       iraṇṭām pakutiyil kuṟavaṉ tōṟṟam, kuṟavaṉ taṉ contamalai kūṟutal.
       civapaṭci varuvataik kūṟutal, amaiti vēṇṭal, civapaṭcikaḷaic cuṟṟi
       vaḷaittal, avaikaḷ pōkāmaṟ kāttal, avaikaḷ iraimēytal, kaṇṇi
       koṇṭuvarutal, civa paṭcikaḷaip piṭittal, vāḻttu ākiya uṟuppukaḷ
       kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
       mūṉṟām pakutiyil ciṅkaṉ ciṅki tarukkam eṉṉum uraiyāṭal pōṉṟa
       viṉāviṭai koṇṭu vātiṭutal maṭṭumē kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷatu.
       mutaṟpakutiyil ñāṉattiṉaip paṟṟutal pōlavum, iraṇṭām pakutiyil
       civattiṉaip paṟṟutal pōlavum, mūṉṟām pakutiyil viṉāviṭai vāyilākat
       tattuvaṅkaḷaip paṟṟutal pōlavum kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
       maruttuvattiṉai maṉatil patiyavaittaṟporuṭṭu eḷiya naṭaiyil
       vaittiyakkummi, vaittiyak kuṟavañci mutalāṉa nūlkaḷ eḻuntaṉapōla,
       ñāṉam peṟa ñāṉakkummi. ñāṉakkuṟavañci. meññāṉakkuṟavañci,
       ñāṉarettiṉakkuṟavañci, tattuvak kuṟavañci, vētāntak kuṟam mutalāṉa
       pala nūlkaḷ eḻuntaṉa. eḷiya naṭaiyēyāyiṉum tattuvak karuttukkaḷai
       uḷḷaṭakkik kūṟiyuḷḷa viṭaṅkaḷil uruvakam tantu kūṟiyuḷḷār.
       'tirumalaic ciṟappuk kūṟum talaippil 'kantaṉ eṅkōṉ tirumalai
       makimai colvēṉ' eṉkiṟār mutaṟṟokutiyil, 'tirumalai' eṉpatu
       tiruppati (vēṅkaṭamalai)yiṉaik kuṟikkum eṉpatu uṇaraṟpāṟṟu, taṟpōtu
       ' tirumalai tiruppati' eṉa vaḻaṅkappaṭukiṉṟatu, vaṭavēṅkaṭam,
       vaṭavēṅkaṭamuṭaiyāṉaik kantak kaṭavuḷ eṉpar oru cārār.
       iṉikkuṟavañci varicaiyil pala kuṟavañcikaḷ uḷḷaṉa avaṟṟuḷ cila:

       mēṟkaṇṭa kuṟavañcikaḷuḷ cilavaṟṟil ñāṉamum tattuvaṅkaḷum
       vētāntaṅkaḷum pēcappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.
       innūl mikavum citaintum muṟintum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟatu. 42 ēṭukaḷaik
       koṇṭatu. itaṉai āyntu veḷiyiṭap paramporuḷiṉ tiruvaruḷ tuṇai
       ceytamaikku vāḻtti vaṇaṅkukiṉṟēṉ. innūliṉai veḷiyiṭa aṉumatiyaḷitta
       ematu makāl keḷaravak kāriyatarici tirumiku vittuvāṉ a. vaṭivēlaṉ
       avarkaṭku eṉatu naṉṟiyiṉait terivittukkoḷḷukiṉṟēṉ. innūlil
       aṟiyāmaiyiṉāl ēṟpaṭṭa piḻaikaḷ iruppiṉ ataṉaip poruttaruḷa
       vēṇṭuvatōṭu, avaikaḷ tiruttam peṟavum utava vēṇṭumeṉak kaṟṟuvalla
       pulavarkaḷai vaṇaṅkik kēṭṭukkoḷḷukiṉṟēṉ.
       ippaṇi iṉitu niṟaiveytak kalaimakaḷiṉ tiruppātaṅkaḷaic ciramēṟ
       koḷḷu-kiṉṟēṉ.

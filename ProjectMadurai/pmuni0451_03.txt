7. kāṉal vari

[yāḻiṉit toḻutu vāṅkiya mātavi paṇṇal mutaliya eṇvakiyālum iciyi eḻuppi, vārtal
mutaliya eṭṭuvaki icik karaṇattālum ārāyntu ceviyāl ōrttu, pāṇiyāteṉak
kūṟikkōvalaṉ kiyil yāḻiṉi nīṭṭa, avaṉ vāṅki, āṟṟuvariyum kāṉalvariyumākiya
icippāṭṭukkaḷ palavaṟṟi yāḻiliṭṭup pāṭiṉāṉ. avaṉ pāṭiya pāṭṭukkaḷ akapporuṭṭuṟi
yamintaṉavākaliṉ, avaṟṟik kēṭṭa mātavi, ‘ivaṟṟuḷ oru kuṟippu uṇṭu; ivaṉ taṉṉili
mayaṅkiṉāṉ’eṉak karuti, yāḻiṉi vāṅkit tāṉum oru kuṟippuṭiyāḷ pōla
varippāṭṭukkaḷ palavaṟṟip pāṭiṉāḷ. yāḻicimēl vittu ūḻviṉi vantu uruttatākaliṉ,
kōvalaṉ avaḷ pāṭiyavaṟṟik kēṭṭu, ‘yāṉ kāṉalvari pāṭa, ivaḷ mikka māyamuṭiyaḷākaliṉ
vēṟoṉṟiṉmēl maṉam vittup pāṭiṉāḷ’ eṉa uṭkoṇṭu, avaḷi aṇitta ki nekiḻntavaṉāy
eḻuntu ēvalāḷar cūḻtarap pōyiṉāṉ; pōka,mātaviyum kiyaṟṟa neñciṉaḷāy viyattiṉ uḷḷē
pukkuk kātalaṉuṭaṉṟiyē taṉ maṉiyi aṭintāḷ.(itiluḷḷa pāṭṭukkaḷ palavum kaṟpōri
iṉpattilēyē tiḷikka vikkum coṟporuḷ nayaṅkaḷ vāyntavi.)

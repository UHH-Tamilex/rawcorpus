maturi mInATciamman & cOmacuntarar aTimaTakku Aciriyaviruttam by T. Gopal Iyer

Source:
maturi mīṉāṭciyammaṉ, cōmacuntarakkaṭavuḷ pēril
aṭimaṭakku āciriyaviruttam.
tiruppōrūr ṭi. kōpāl nāyakar avarkaḷāṟṟamatu
matarās, eṉ.ci. 100 kōḷṭaṉ acciyantiracāliyiṟ patippikkappeṟṟatu.
1914
17-18 kāḷattiyappa mutalivīti matarās eṉ. ci.

1. maturi mīṉāṭciyammaṉ aṭimaṭakku āciriyaviruttam

kaṭavuḷ tuṇi.
katiyāḷik kuḻalaṇimā katiyāḷi yaṭiyaruḷaṅkalaṅkat tāṉcam,
matiyāḷik kayavarpaṇi matiyāḷi maṇṇum viṇṇum vaṇaṅkumācan,
nitiyāḷi yaḷakkariya nitiyāḷip poyyaṉpar niṉiviṟṟaṉpūḷ,
patiyāḷit tirumaturip patiyāḷit paṇivārtam pavamaṟṟōrē. (1)
vārāḷi vīṇaretir vārāḷit taṉṟāḷi vaṇaṅkārikkaṇ,
pārāḷit teṉpāṇṭip pārāḷip poyyaṉpar paṇikkumutti,
tārāḷi nimpamalart tārāḷi matayāṉit taṭakki vāḻi,
yūrāḷit tirumaturi yūrāṉiyuḷḷumeṉa tuḷḷamātō. (2)
parattāḷi yaṭiyavartā parattāḷi yaṅkucavempā camēntuṅ,
karattāḷi mantrapañcāka karattāḷi muttēvarkaṭkumēlān,
tarattāḷi yilaṅkucavuntarattāḷi viṇmuṭiyit taṭavum poṟkō
purattāḷic cīvaṉmutti purattāḷi niṉittiṭumeṉ puttimātō. (3)
cēyāḷic ceḻiyaṉaruḷ cēyāḷip palluyiruñ cekattuntāṉē,
āyāḷiya ṭiyarpiḻiyāyāḷip poyyaṉpa raṇitārittāḷ,
vēyāḷi tōṭpaciya vēyāḷic cempavaḷam viḷaṅkumāmpal,
vāyāḷit tiruvālavāyāḷit tūyāḷi vaṇaṅkivāḻvām. (4)
kaliyāḷi yaṇimaṇimē kaliyāḷik kaṉṉipuraṅ kāttamōlit,
taliyāḷik kiḷimoḻikku taliyāḷi marakatattāṟ camintacōtic,
ciliyāḷik karumpuruvac ciliyāḷi yeṉiyāḷac cittañcaṟṟum,
maliyāḷippaṅ kamarcem maliyāḷi paṇintupavamāṟṟuvāmē. (5)
pariyāḷip paṅkuṟinam pariyāḷi vaṭivāḷip paravārkkuḷḷaṅ,
kariyāḷi vikinatik kariyāṉi mukkaṉi kaṟkaṇṭucentēṉ,
uriyāḷiyāṉōvavuri yāḷiyaṉparpavat tuḻaṉṟuvāṭa,
variyāḷit taṉiyimaya variyāḷip paṇipavarē mācaṟṟārē. (6)
taviyāḷit toṇṭarukku taviyāḷik kayavarkuṭi tāṅkaccinti,
kaviyāḷi muvaricik kaviyāḷip piraḷayattuṅ kaṟpattuntāṉ,
avipāḷimakattūṭṭu maviyāḷi viḻikkaṭi ceṉṟaṭarntupāyuñ,
ceviyāḷi yemiyāḷuñ ceviyāḷik kaṇantōṟun tiyāṉippāmē. (7)
aṇiyāḷi muṭiyaṇiyām paṇiyāḷi maṇittōḷuk kaṇintanimpak,
kaṇiyāḷik kayavartoṇṭu kaṇiyāḷi muttēvarkaḷiyumēvum,
paṇiyāḷi viḷaṅkutirup paṇiyāḷit tirumaturip pāṇṭikkōṉkaṇ,
maṇiyāḷip puṉintu navamaṇiyāḷip paṇiyārnaṉmati yaṟṟārē. (8)
tuyyāḷi makavarintun tuyttāḷip peruṅ karuṇi curakkuṅ kaṭpār,
viyāḷit tāḷeṉmuṭi vittāḷi marakatamā malipōl vāytta,
mey yāḷi mūvartamiḻ meyttāḷic cuvikkaruppu villoṉṟēntuṅ,
kiyāḷi maṭaṅkaliyu kittāḷit tutittuviṉi kaṭattuvāmē. (9)
keliyāḷi yeṇṭiciyuṅ kelittāḷi yayaṉ ṟalikik kēntiyēṟṟa,
paliyāḷi yaṭivarkkē palit tāḷi maturivarap paṇittavēḻār
kaliyāḷi yevvu yiruṅ kalittāḷi yaṭiyēṉik kākkaccaṟṟuñ,
caliyā ḷik kaṇavaṉukkañ calittāḷit tañcameṉac caraṇ pukkēṉē. (10)
mīṉāṭciyammaṉ aṭimaṭakku āciriyaviruttam. muṟṟiṟṟu.

2. maturi cōmacuntarakkaṭavuḷ aṭimaṭakkāciriyaviruttam.

kaṭavuḷ tuṇi.
#
viṭiyāṉic caṉakarviṉā viṭiyāṉi yeṉip piṟavi vēlicārap,
paṭiyāṉi veṟṟimaḻuppaṭiyāṉi yaṭiyarit tāparikkaneñcañ,
caṭiyāṉic cempavaḷac caṭiyāṉi vērivēṅkic carumampūṇṭa,
uṭiyāṉit teṉkūṭaluṭiyāṉi maṟavāteṉ ṉuḷ ḷamātō. (1)
vāṭṭāṉit tacamukaṉi vāṭṭāṉit toṇṭarivaṉ ṉaracileṉṟum,
vīṭṭāṉip paramapata vīṭṭāṉi muṉivarukkā veḷḷimaṉṟuḷ,
āṭṭāṉi yeṉikkaliyi lāṭṭāṉi yantakaṉiyaṭiyār kaṇṇiṟ,
kāṭṭāṉi matukkaṭappaṅ kāṭṭāṉik karutiviṉi kaṭattuvēṉē. (2)
cukattāṉi vāmpariyañ cukattāṉi yerittāṉic cuṭarārveḷḷi,
nakattāṉi naṟuṅkōka nakattāṉit taṭintāṉi nālōṭoṉṟu,
mukattāṉiyaṭiyavar camukattāṉit taṉippāṭu mutaṉmit toṇṭaṟ,
akattāṉit teṉmaturi yakattāṉippaṇiyaṭiyārk kaṭiyēṉyāṉē. (3)
miyāṉi yurittavali miyāṉit tiricūla maḻumāṉēntuṅ,
kiyāṉic caraṇaruḷaṅ kiyāṉip paṇiyārkkuk karuṇināṭṭañ
ceyyāṉit tirumēṉic ceyyāṉit teṉkūṭal ceḻittapūti,
meyyāṉit taṉi mutalā meyyāṉip paṇintupaḻa viṉivelvēṉē. (4)
nalattāṉik karamēntaṉalattāṉic caṭimuṭimē ṉaṇṇuṅkaṅkā,
calattāṉic cilikkaṉakā calattāṉit tīraṉetir cāṟṟuñ ceñcol,
vaḷattāṉi malimaṭanti valattāṉi vaḷamikutu vāḻmāntat,
talattāṉit taḻaṉutaṟkaṭ ṭalattāṉik kaṇkaḷippat taricittēṉē. (5)
vaṇṇāṉi yēṟiyacev vaṇṇāṉi yiraviyaṅki matiyāmūṉṟu,
kaṇṇāṉi yaṉparpiḻi kaṇṇāṉi vañcaneñcak kayavarkāṇa,
voṇṇāṉi nāṉmaṟikku moṇṇāṉi yeṉiyiṉumō rutarañcērap,
paṇṇāṉi yiruvaricip paṇṇāṉi yeṉṉāḷum paṇiceyvēṉē. (6)
muṭiyāṉit tumpimalar muṭittāṉit tirumēṉi muḻutunīṟṟup,
poṭiyāṉik kāmaṉuṭal poṭittā ṉiyiṭattimayap poruppārpaccip,
piṭiyāṉi veṟṟimaḻup piṭittāṉit taṉṉilami pēcumālaṅ.
kuṭiyāṉik kaṭalviṭamuṉ kuṭittāṉit tutittirumi kuvittuvāḻvām (7)
viriyāṉi nāṉmaṟiyum virittāṉit taṉit tāṉē virumpumaṉpark,
kuriyāṉi matayāṉi yurittāṉi yevavuyirkku muyirtāṉākuṅ,
kariyāṉikkāmaṉuṭal karittāṉip poyyaṉpar kaṇmuṉvantā,
tari yāṉippiṟimuli tarittāṉik kūṭaliṭit taricittēṉē (8)
kallāṉi karumparuntak kaṟṟāṉip purampoṭiya kaṉakamēru,
villāṉi vaḷiyeṭuttu viṟṟāṉi veñcamaṇar viṭukkavanta,
kollāṉit taṉviṭiyāṟ koṉṟāṉi nāṉmāṭak kūṭalākkuñ
cellāṉip pāviyiṭañ ceṉṟāṉik kuṉṟāṉit tiyāṉippāmē. (9)
ēṟāṉip paliyulaki lēṟṟāṉi murukaḷittantiṭa vāñceṉṉi,
āṟāṉiccaṭiyilaṇi yāṟṟāṉipañce ḻutti yaṉpilārkkuk,
kūṟāṉiyumiyavaḷōr kūṟṟā ṉiyeṉiyaṭimi koḷḷuñ ceyki,
māṟāṉivañcar pavamāṟṟāṉi nīṟṟāṉi vāḻttuvēṉē. (10)
cōmacuntarakkaṭavuḷmītu aṭimaṭakkāciriyaviruttam. muṟṟiṟṟu.

Preface

The study of the lives of great men wło have left their foot-prints in
the sands of time enable the readers to emulate them and make themselves
worthy citizens by following their example closely. The object of this
volume is to enable the readers to appreciate in some degree the work of
one of the greatest Tamil scholars of the nineteenth century, who by his
profound learning, 'characteristic energy, great love for the Tamil
language and literature and unswerving devotion to duty has earned a
name scarcely paralleled in the history of Tamil Literature. It is
therefore but right that the life and work of such an eminent Tamil
Scholar 'should be recorded in Tamil for the benefit of the Tamil World.
The cold reception accorded in these days to Tamil publications coupled
with the very little encouragement Biographers meet with, made the
present author to hesitate for a while, but on the assurance of friends
who happen to be acquainted personally with the subject of this
biography the author boldly comes forward with this volume.
In writing out this book the author who has been associated with, the
late Rao Bahadur Thamotharam Pilli for a long time puts matters clearly
and concisely dividing the book into several chapters according to the
subjects dealt with. The narrative begins with a full description. of
the island of Ceylon and proceeds to detil the ancient history of the
peninsula of Jaffna, the birth place of Mr. Thamotharam Pilli. A brief
description of his family follows and then the birth, childhood, student
career and official life are dealt with in succession, taking care to
emphasise his work for the revival of Tamil Literature. His thoroughly
disinterested labours in that cuse holds him up to public esteem and
honour and in order to give that fact a wide recognition the author has
given a detiled history of the supposed origin of the Tamil language,
its gradual development, the disasters it underwent, its subsequent
triumph and its final decay, and has also mentioned the names of several
important Tamil works which at one time served as invaluable jewels that
added to the natural beauty of the Tamil nymph.. The author then traces
the gradual decay of Tamil down to the present time and has fully
described the labours of the subject of this life in redeeming ancient
Tamil works which were at the verge of extinction. Sufficient
information about each of the works so redeemed is also given and the
whole narrative concludes with a summary of his life as man.
The book is written in elegant and chaste Tamil to suit Tamil student of
the higher classes and contins enough matter for careful study. The
various immemoriums composed by competent Tamil scholars at the time of
the demise of Mr. Thamotharam Pilli have been also appended and the
book has been made very interesting in every way.
The late Rao Bahadur C. N. Thamotharam Pilli has been known all over
India and Ceylon for his scholarly attinments in Tamil. He combines in
himself deep learning with practical work which is both rare and
admirable. It is therefore hoped that this little volume will be
cheerfully received by the Tamil world.
MADRAS,
5-5-34.       THE AUTHOR.

2. piḷḷi avarkaḷiṉ jaṉaṉam

ittakiya ciṟappiṉiyuṭiya yāḻppāṇattilē puttūreṉṉu marumpatiyic cārnta
ciṟuppiṭṭiyeṉṉuñ ciṟukirāmattil māppāṇamutali eṉpāroruva riruntaṉar. ivar pakīrata
kōttirattōrum, vēḷāṇ marapiṉarum caṉmārkka neṟipūṇṭōrum, tiraṇṭa pūstiti
yuṭiyarum 'toḻutūṇ cuviyi ṉuḻutu ṇiṉitu'' eṉṉu mutumoḻiyik kaṭippiṭiyāk
koṇṭu payiriṭuntoḻiliyē yuṭiyarāyu miruntaṉar. ivartan tirukkumāraṉāyutittavar
vēlāyutaṉeṉpār. ivarkku neṭunāṭ puttiracantāṉa millātiruntu īṟṟil ampalavāṇaṉ

pirayāci yiyum viyantu taṅkaḷālāṉavāṟu poruḷutavi ceytupacarittaṉar. ākavē
piḷḷiyavarkaḷ tāmaṭinta naṣṭam oruvāṟu nivarttiyāyiṉami kaṇṭu pērukka maṭintaṉar

8. kalittoki.

ilvūkkattā luntap peṟṟamiyāl piḷḷi yavarkaḷ piṉṉuñ cila nūlkaḷip patippippatil
mikka muyaṟci yuṭiyarāyiṉar. tolkāppiyap paricōtaṉikkākap piratikaḷ tēṭiya pōtu srī
la srī, āṟumuka nāvalaravarkaḷ kalittokip piratiyoṉṟu avarkik kakappaṭṭatu. atu koṇṭu
kalittokiyiṉarumi yuṇarntu ati evvāṟṟāṉu macciṭal vēṇṭu meṉ ṉuṅ karuttuṭiyarāyp
paṟpala maṭātipatikaṭku viṇ ṇappañ ceytaṉar. īṇṭum tiruvāvaṭutuṟi ātīṉattuc caṟkurukāta
cuvāmikaḷē piḷḷiyavarkaḷukkup pērupakāriyāy niṉṟavar. piḷḷiyavarkaḷum annaṉ ṟikkup
piṉvarumāṟu kimmāṟu ceytirukkiṉṟaṉar:
"viṇṇāṭu kili vaḻittēcikar vevviṉikku neṟṟik
kaṇṇā ṉaṉa cupra maṇya cuvāmikaḷ kāṉ malari
naṇṇāt taliyiṉaci nīrattāṅka naṟkōkaḻivāy .
maṇṇāyp piṟantilaṉē aiyakō ! inta viyakattē.''
''ciramāliyākavuñ ciṉmuṭiyākavuñ ceyya kaṇṭa
caramāliyākavum yāṉaṭi yēṉiṉiyēṉ taruvāy
pāmār kayilip paramparik kōkaḻic cupramaṇyā
maramāy niṉpāta kuṟaṭāy varutaṟ koruvaramē."
kāruṇṇiyamuṅ kalāparipālaṉamumē tamatu tirumēṉi yākakkoṇṭu viḷaṅkun
tiruvāvaṭutuṟic caṟkuru nāta cuvāmikaḷ piḷḷiyavarkaḷ ceyta viṇṇappattic
ciramēṟṟāṅki tamatu kiyilirunta kalittokip pirati iraṇṭum teṉṟēcap pirati
iraṇṭumāka nāṉku piratikaḷ avarukkaḷittu avvēliyit toṭaṅkumāṟu ākyāpittaṉar.
tirukkuṭantiyil vacittamiyāl annakar vācarum toṇṭamāṉ putukkōṭṭi makārājāviṉ man
tiriyum piratikāvalarumākiya kurava a. cēṣiya cāstiriyāravarkaḷ, pūrvakkiranta
paripālaṉaṅ kāraṇamāka avar paṭum pirayāciyik kaṇṇuṟṟu avarmītu, mikka aṉpuṭiyarāyiṉar.
putukkōṭṭiyi uttiyōka stāṉamākavuṭiya cāstiriyāravarkaḷ tirukkuṭar . tikkuc
cellum vēḷikaḷiṟ piḷḷiyavarkaḷik kāṇpatum campāāṣippatum vaḻakkam.
orukāl iruvarum campāṣittuk koṇṭirukkumpōtu tamiḻp pāṣiyaṭinta niliyiyum,
pūrva kiranta paripālaṉaṅ kāraṇamākat tāmpaṭum pirayāciyiyum, ataṉāṟ ṟamakku nēriṭum
naṣṭattiyum piḷḷiyavarkaḷ vāyiliruntē kēṭṭa cāstirikaḷ, ''yāṉ yātu ceytal
vēṇṭum" eṉṟu kaṭikkaṇittaṉar. ''yātēṉu moru paḻiya nūlit taṅkaḷ celaviṟ patip
pittal nalam" eṉṟu piḷḷiyavarkaḷ kūṟāmuṉṉarē cāstirikaḷum "avvaṇṇamē yākuka'' eṉṟu
viṭi pakarntu, caṅkattār kālattup pērilakkiyamāyuḷḷa - toṉṟit terintu koḷḷumāṟu
āññāpittamiyāl "kaṟṟaṟintā rēttuṅ kaliyē'' avvilakkaṇa muṭiya teṉavum, ati
acciṭuvataṟkut tām muṉṉarē koṇṭa veṇṇam pūrttiyāvataṟkut takka kālam vantateṉavuṅ
koṇṭu akkalittokiyip paricōtikka vārampittaṉar.
innūl maturik kaṭiccaṅkattup pulavar nāṟpattoṉpatiṉmaruḷ oruvarāṉa nallantuvaṉār
iyaṟṟiyatu. accaṅkattār namakkaruḷiya eṭṭuttoki, pattuppāṭṭu patiṉeṇkīḻk kaṇakku eṉṉu
mūṉ ṟaṉuḷ iḵtu eṭṭuttokiyiṉpāṟ paṭumeṉpatu,
"naṟṟiṇi nalla kuṟuntoki yiṅkuṟunū .
ṟotta patiṟṟuppat tōṅku- paripāṭal
kaṟṟaṟintā rēttuṅ kaliyē yakampuṟameṉ
ṟittiṟatta veṭṭut toki.''
eṉṉuñ ceyyuḷāṟ ṟeḷiyak kiṭakkum. caṅkattār kālattu ilakkiyaṅkaḷuḷellām itu
pērilakkiya māka matikkappaṭṭateṉpatu "kaṟṟaṟintā rēttum" eṉṉum vicēṭaṇattāṟ
peṟappaṭum.
innūl vaṭamoḻic campanta millātiyalvatu , tamiḻ taṉippāṣi yeṉpataṟkōr pērātāramākavu
mirukkiṉṟatu. itu kaṭiccaṅkattār kālattu naṭuk kūṟṟiṉkaṭ tōṉṟiyamiyāṉum,
accaṅkamoḻintu 'taṟpōtu iraṇṭāyiram varuṭaṅkaḷukku mēṟpaṭṭa miyāṉum itaṉvayatu
mūvāyiram varuṭaṅkaḷukkuc caṟṟuṅ kuṟintatalla veṉpatu tāṉē pōtarum. itaṟku
uriyeḻutic centamiḻi uyvittavar maturiyāciriyar pārattuvāci nacciṉārkkiṉiyār
eṉṉum makā viyākkiyāṉiyē. ākavē itaṉ ciṟappu it tuṇitteṉṟu empōliyar eṭuttuk
kūṟutaliyalātu.

9. piḷḷiyavarkaḷ nītiyatiparāṉatu

kuṭantiyampatiyiliruntu innūlip paricō tittu varunāḷil piḷḷiyavarkaḷiṭattuḷḷa arum
peruṅ kuṇaṅkaḷiyum āṅkilēya kalvit tiṟaṉiyum, tamiḻp pāṇṭiyattiyum, niyāyacāstira
uṇarcciyiyum, peruntaṉmiyiyu maṟintuṇarnta cāstiri yāravarkaḷ avarip putukkōṭṭi
camastāṉa : makaramaṉṟattu niyāyātipatikaḷi loruvarāka niyamittaṉar. tamatu upakārac
campaḷattukku mēlatikamākat - tām campātikkum tiraviyattit tamiḻ nūṟpatippuk kē
celavu ceyya vēṇṭumeṉṟu muṉṉart tīrmāṉit tiruntamiyāl ippoḻutu kiṭitta patavi
attīrmāṉatti niṟivēṟṟuvataṟkuc ciṟanta vaḻi yākumeṉṟu karuti, avvuttiyōkatti
aṅkīkarittu 1887௵- tamatu puttirariyum marukariyum kal viyiṉ poruṭṭuc ceṉṉi
nakarukkaṉuppiviṭṭut , tām putukkōṭṭi ceṉṟu nītipatittuvatti vakittaṉar. avarukku
ivvuttiyōka mamintatu avar naṇpar cuṟṟattavarātiyōrkkup perumakiḻvit tantatu.
putukkōṭṭi cumastāṉattu makāmaṉṟattu niyāyātipatikaḷāyirunta mūvaril avar oruvarāyk
kaṭami pūṇṭu tamatu vēliyik kuṟivaṟa naṭātti vantamiyāl makārājā avarkaḷiṉ mantiriyum
pirati kāvalarumākiya mēṟkūṟiya cāstiriyāravarkaḷ iva riṭattu mikka
pirītiyuṭiyarāyiṉar. astamaṉa vēḷi yil nītiyatipar mūvarum, mantiriyārum
ratavākaṉārūṭarāyt tēkavappiyācattiṉ poruṭṭu, camastāṉa irāja vītiyiṟ cavāri celvatum,
pantaṭikkuñ cāliyiṟ ceṉṟu pantu viḷiyāṭuvatum, piṉṉar tammuḷ |ḷoruva
rakattucceṉṟu vinōta campāṣaṇi ceyvatum, aṭikkaṭi irāca camukañceṉṟu koṇṭāṭuvatum
akkālattuc ciṟuvarāyiruntu piḷḷiyavarkaḷ parāmarippiṉ kīḻvaḷarnta ematu kaṇkaḷiviṭṭiṉ
ṉumakaṉṟapāṭilli. makārājā avarkaḷ kalācāliyil māṇavarāyk kalvikaṟṟa emakkum nītiyatipar
vīṭṭup piḷḷikaḷeṉṉuṅ kaṇṇiyamum vicēṣa matippuṅ kiṭittaṉa.
piḷḷiyavarkaḷ putukkōṭṭiyil nāṉku varuṭam nītiyatiparāyiruntaṉar. nītistāṉattuk
kaṭutta vēliyil muṉṉātika payiṟciyillātavarāyiṉum niyāyācaṉattiṟ ṟamatu
kūṭṭāḷikaḷāyirunta maṟṟiyōrkku avar oru ciṟituṅ kuṟintavarākak kāṇappaṭṭilar.
kūrntaputtiyum tiṭacittamum āḻnta - niyāya cāstira vaṟivum uṇmiyitu poyyitu -
veṉṟum niyāyamitu aniyāyamitu eṉṟum pakut taṟiyuñ caktiyum avarpāṟ
kuṭikoṇṭiruntamiyā ṉē nītiyatipar kaṭamiyic celuttuvatu avarukku eḷitākavē
yiruntatu. ikkaṇṇiyamāṉa vēliyi vakittu naṭattiya kālattup piḷḷiyavarkaḷ naṭuvunili .
kuṉṟātu tamatu nāḷāntara vēliyik kiramamāka naṭat , tiyatu maṉṟic carva jaṉaṅkaḷālum
nītimāṉ tarma vāṉ eṉṟu : pukaḻntu koṇṭāṭappaṭṭum vantaṉar.
tamakkuk kiṭikkum cāvakāca muḻuvatiyum kalittokip paricōtaṉikkē celaviṭṭu vantami
yāl aḵtu ativirivil muṭiveytiyatu. ākavē ati srī toṇṭamāṉ putukkōṭṭi makārācā
viṉ mantiriyum pirati kāvalarumākiya kurava, a. cēṣiya cāstirikaḷiṉ
kāruṇyatiraviyōpakārat . tikkoṇṭu 1887௵ āṭimī acciṭṭu veḷiyiṭṭa ṉar. innūṟ
patippiṟ piḷḷiyavarkaḷ tam yuktik kiyintavāṟu ciṟcila vikaṟpaṅkaḷic ceytuḷareṉpatu
avar patippuriyiṟ kāṇakkiṭakkiṉṟatu. ēṭṭuppira tiyāyuḷḷa mutaṉūli acciṉiṟ
pukuttiya pirakaṭaṉā ciriyarākiya avar ataṉi accorūpamākavē patippiyātu, tamakkut
tōṉṟiyavāṟu vikaṟpittatu. perun tavaṟeṉṟum, avvāṟu vikaṟpittal vaḻinūl cārpu nūl
eṉṟiṉṉaṉa ceyvōrkkē takkateṉ ṟum cilar kūṟiṉar. cilar avar avvāṟu ceytatu tavaṟeṉṟu
vāstavamākavē piḷḷiyavarkaṭku nēriṟ kaṭitameḻutik kaṇṭittaṉar.
cila vākkiyaṅkaḷi iṭamāṟṟi vittatēyaṉṟi āciriyar moḻi naṭikaḷil ōreḻuttēṉum
māṟṟila reṉpatik kaṇṭārākavum, tām ceyta ciṟuvikaṟ paṅkaḷukkup pōtiya niyāyan
tantiruppavum avarkaḷ avvāṟu muraṇiyatu takkataṉṟu. aṭiyōṭaḻintu pōkum paḻiya
nūṟkaḷit tañ ciṟṟaṟaviṟ keṭṭiya maṭṭum paricōtittu nili niṟuttap pukunta
paricōtaṉāciriyarmēṟ kuṟikūṟuvatu mikavu makkiramameṉpatu collavum vēṇṭumā?
kalit tokip patippil piḷḷi yavarkaṭku nērnta pirayāci ciṟupāṉmiyataṉṟu. vīracōḻiyap
piratikaḷip pōlākātu kalittokip piratikaḷ āṅkāṅku akappaṭṭaṉa vāyiṉum muḻup
piratikaḷāka vakappaṭṭaṉa mikaccilavē. aviyum atikap pirayā ciyōṭum paṇaccelavōṭumē
campātikkappaṭṭaṉa. innūṟ patippil piḷḷiyavarkaḷ tām pattup pirati kaḷ koṇṭu
paricōtitta tākavum avaṟṟuṭ perum pāṉmiyāṉavi kuṟippiratikaḷeṉṟuṅ kūṟiyiruk
kiṉṟaṉar.

10. ilakkaṇa viḷakkam.

tamakkuc cāvakācam nērumpōtellām paḻiya tamiḻnūṟ piratikaḷ tēṭum vaṇṇam tamiḻ nāṭeṅka
ṇuñ cuṟṟuvatum, maṭātipatikaḷi yaṭikkaṭi taricippa tum piḷḷiyavarkaḷ vaḻakkameṉpati
muṉṉarē kūṟi yuḷḷēm. maṭātipatikaḷukkum tiruvāvaṭutuṟi yātīṉattu makā
caṉṉitāṉattiṭattu mikka paṟṟuṭiya rākaliṉ avvātīṉatti vicēṣamāyt taricittu van tār.
kalittokiyip paricōtittuk koṇṭiruk kuṅkālattu orukāl avvātīṉattukkuc ceṉṟaṉar.
akkālattu avvātīṉattu makā cannitāṉa māka veḻuntaruḷiya srīlasrī cuppiramaṇiya tēcika
mūrttikaḷum vaḻakkappirakāram tamatu maṭattu ōtuvā ri muṉṉarē ayaratavītistāṉattiṟ
kaṉuppip piḷ ḷiyavarkaḷi etirkoṇṭu ceṉṟu takka mariyātiyōṭu aḻittuvarumāṟu
paṇittaṉar. piḷḷiyavarka ḷum maṭattiṟ cērntu ciṉṉāṭṭaṅkiya piṉṉar oru nāḷ cuvāmikaḷ
paṇṭit tamiḻ nūlkaḷ viṣayamākap piḷḷiyavarkaḷōṭu campāṣittapōtu ilakkaṇa viḷakkattik
kuṟittum pēcavēṇṭiyatāyiṟṟu. ap poḻutu piḷḷiyavarkaḷ ilakkaṇa viḷakkattu maki miyip
perukakkūṟic camaṇa nūlākiya naṉṉūl pōlākātu aḵtu pañcalakṣaṇamum māṇākkarukkup
pōtiyaḷavu ceṟintuḷḷateṉṟum, aṅṅaṉañ ciṟap puṟṟa vōr ariyavilakkaṇa nūliṉ makimiyit
tiruvāvaṭutuṟi yātīṉattār kuṟikka niṉintu, tamatiṣ ṭattip pūrtti ceyvataṟku
naṉṉūli mēṉmip paṭuttuvatē takka vaḻiyeṉak koṇṭu tamatātīṉat tuc caṅkara namaccivāyap
pulavarāl oru viruttiyuri eḻutuvittatum, avvuri ciṟakkum vaṇṇam civañāṉa
muṉivarāṟ puttam putturi eḻutuvittatum, piṉṉar ammuṉivarāl ilakkaṇa viḷakkac
cūṟāvaḷi eṉṟu ōr aniyāyakaṇṭaṉa miyaṟṟuvittatumākiya iṉṉō raṉṉa ceykikaḷ
avvātīṉattārmāṭṭeñ ñāṉṟum māṟātu viḷaṅkumōr kaḷaṅkamēyā meṉṟuṅ kūṟiṉār.
cuvāmikaḷum tamatātīṉattār ceytatu tava ṟeṉṟu oppukkoṇṭatu maṉṟi ilakkaṇa viḷakkam
acciṭappaṭumāyiṉ paṇṭic ciṟappaṭivatu tiṇṇa meṉakkūṟi ataṉi evvāṟṟāṉum paricōtittu
ac ciṭal vēṇṭumeṉṟu piḷḷiyavarkaṭkuk kaṭṭaḷi yiṭṭaruḷiṉar. ittōṭuṅkūṭac
ciṟukāppiyaṅkaḷi loṉṟākiyatum iṟantupōkun taciyi appōtaṭintuḷḷatumāṉa
cūḷāmaṇiyiyum acciṭutal ulōkōpakāramāmeṉap paṇittaruḷiṉar. uṭaṉē piḷḷiyavarkaḷum
appaṇikaḷic ciṟamēṟṟāṅki,
''kuṟippiṉālariya kuraveṇṇamatu kūṟumuṉpuri kuṇat tiṉēṉ,
poṟuppaṉōvaṭikaḷ puviyileṉṉiyoru poru ḷeṉakkoṭu pukaṉṟa nūl,
ciṟappuṟappiḻi tiruttiyaccil veḷiceytorōr piratitēvarīr,
naṟipperuñcaraṇa naḷiṉa cannitiyi ṉal kiṭātiṉi yeṉṉāḷiṉē."
eṉṟu viṇṇappañ ceytaṉarām.
cuvāmikaḷum piḷḷiyavarkaḷuṭiya pēraṉpiyum kurupattiyiyum viyantu pārāṭṭit tamatu
maṭatti lirunta ilakkaṇa viḷakkap piratika ḷaṉittiyum cūḷāmaṇip piratiyoṉṟum
avariṭattiṟ koṭuttaruḷiyatu maṉṟi, vaḻakkap pirakāram maṭattiṟkāka vāṅkum piratika ḷiṉ
kirayattōṭu innūl ovvoṉṟiṟkāka nūṟu rūpā naṉkoṭiyaḷippatākavum vākkaḷittārkaḷ.
piḷḷiyavarkaḷ muyaṟcikaṭ kellāṅ kikoṭuttup pērupakāriyāy niṉṟa cuvāmikaḷ innūlkaḷ
accil veḷivaruvataṟku muṉṉarē yaṭintami avarukkup peruvicaṉamē.
ilakkaṇa viḷakkatti mūlamum uriyumākac ceytavar tiruvārūr apiṣēkattar marapiṟ ciṟappuṟ
ṟōṅkiya srī vaṉmīkanāta tēcikar kumārar vittiya nāta tēcikar. uriyum pāyiramum cila
cūttiraṅka ḷum vittiyanāta tēcikar kumārar catāciva tēcikarāl iyaṟṟappaṭṭaṉa veṉac cilar
kūṟāniṟpar. atu takka taṉ ṟeṉpatip piḷḷiyavarkaḷ uḷḷaṅkiyil nel liyaṅ kaṉipōṟ
ṟeḷḷitiṟ pulappaṭumāṟu kāṭṭi.. yirukkiṉṟaṉar.
innūlāciriyar kalvittiṟamiyi viyantu, avar kālattu vittuvāṉkaḷi loruvar
piṉvarumāṟu kūṟiyuḷḷār;
"aimpatiṉmar caṅkattar rākiviṭā rōnāṟpat
toṉpatiṉma reṉṟē yurippārō-imparpukaḻ
vaṉmīka nātaṉaruḷ vittiyanā taṉpuṭavi
taṉmītan nāṭcarittak kāl."
ākavē ivar kalvi cāmāṉiyamāṉa tallaveṉpatu teḷivākiṟatu. ''muṉṉū loḻiyap piṉṉūl pala
viṉu, naṉṉū lārtamak kennūlāru , miṇiyō veṉ ikamatiya ṉun tuṇivē maṉṉuka" eṉa
īcāṉatēcikarāṟ ciṟap pikkap paṭṭa naṉṉūliṟkuc ciṉamuṉiva reḻutiya vuri takka
taṉṟeṉakkoṇṭu, muṉṉar ataṟkuri yeḻutuvāṉ ṟoṭaṅkiya āciriyar, annūlārōṭu tam matam
māṟupaṭṭamikaṇṭu, pañcalakṣaṇamum ceṟintu viḷaṅkum piṟitōrilakkaṇañ ceyyakkaruti ilakkaṇa
viḷakkatti yiyaṟṟiṉar. innūlāciriyar paṭikkācup pulavar pōtakāciriyarākalāṉum,
appulavar cikāmaṇi 1685-3 mutal 1723- variyil aracupurinta irakunāta cētupatiyiṉ
camastāṉa vittuvāṉē yāka lāṉum, tiruvāvaṭutuṟi yātīṉattuc caṅkara namaccivāyap
pulavaratu iyaṟṟamiḻāciriyarākiya cāminātatēcikar ivar kālattuc ciṟuvayatiṉarākalāṉum,
ivi pōṉṟa piṟakāraṇaṅkaḷāṉum āciriyar kālam ēṟak kuṟiya 280- varuṭaṅkaḷukku

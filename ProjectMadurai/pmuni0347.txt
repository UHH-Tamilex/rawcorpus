tiruvāvaṭutuṟi ātīṉattu mahāvitvāṉ tiricipuram srī mīṉāṭcicuntaram piḷḷiyavarkaḷiṉ "pirapantattiraṭṭu" - pakuti 30 (3119-3220) srī cuppiramaṇiyatēcikar māli

kāppu.
3119
poṉmalar taṭittun taṉitamu muṟukārp puyalivar tantiyē poruva,
veṉmala reyiṟu muḻakkamum vāynta veṟuḻkkaru mūṭika mukittup,
paṉmalar muṭimuṉ ṉaṭivari malarntu payilumō rattiyit tutippān,
teṉmalar tuṟicic cuppira maṇiya tēcikaṉ mālicīr peṟavē.       0
nūl.
3120
pūmali niṉatā ḷuḷḷupu tutittup pōṟṟiṭum vāñciyē mataṉuk,
kāmali mūṉṟu moruvaḻip paṭuvā ṉāraṟuḷ puriyuṭa lāti,
tāmali tarakkē ḷātaḷit tāykkut takuvatō varuḷeṉak kēṭṭa,
ṟūmali yaṟavar cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       1
3121
eṇṇuta ṟutitta liyaṟṟaval lānī yeṟṟikku nammaṭit toḻumpē,
paṇṇuka veṉṟāy meyppaṇi yeṉṟu paṟṟiṉaṉitukoḷaṟ poruṭṭō,
naṇṇukūṟ ṟutittuṅ kalleṟi yēṟṟu nayantumuṉ paḻakiya tiya,
cuṇṇavoṇ māṭañ cūḻperuntuṟicic cuppira maṇiya tēcikaṉē.       2
3122
emmiyāḷ poruṭṭuk kayiliyu maḻumā ṉilakiya tirukkicār vaṭivuñ,
cemmicāl peyaru nīttuvē ṟaṭintāy tikaḻtaru muṉṉuḷa mūṉṟu,
tammi nōk kuṟiṉiṉ ṟuḷḷamūṉ ṟaṉukkun tāḻntaṉa veṉṟuḷan teḷintēṉ,
cumminīrt taṭaṅkaḷcūḻ perun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       3
3123
kēvalañ civāttu vitamviciṭ ṭattuk keḻumumat tuvitamōm pātu,
nāvalan tarucut taṅkoḷeṉ ṟaruḷa nāṭukē valattiṉiṉ ṟeṭuttu,
māvalam paṭaruñ cakalattu viṭuttāy varuviyāy cuttamī teṉṉē,
tūvala kaliñar cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppiramaṇiyatē cikaṉē.       4
3124
iṉṟunī yaṭiyār tamakkaru ṭiṟattā leḻumpukaḻp paṭalamikkeḻuntē,
yaṉṟunī puṉinta vamparaṅ kiḻitta tatukuṟit tuṇartari ṉiṉṟu,
naṉṟunī pittaṉ ṟāṉeṉal viḷakku nāṭuṟiṉ maṟṟatu maruḷē,
tuṉṟuvā ṉavarkaḷ cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppiramaṇiyatē cikaṉē.       5
3125
matikati riraṇṭu mukaviḻi yavaṟṟuṇ matiyamuḷ ḷuvappavōr malarip,
patitara muṭiyiṟ koṇṭaṉi maṟṟip paritiyu muvappōr malari,
nitinika raṭiyiṟ koḷalkaṭa ṉaḷittē ṉīkoḷeṉ ṉitayatā mariyit,
tutipeṟu muṉivar cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       6
3126
niṟitaki yaḻintum veṇmiyē vāyntu neṭiyakaṭ kuṟuntoṭi yavarpāṉ,
muṟitiṟam puṟṟuṅ kaḷaṅkamik kamintu muṉitakak kōṭiyu maṭinta,
piṟimati muṭiyiṟ koṇṭanī yatu pōṟ piṟaṅkumeṉ matiyaṭik koḷiṉeṉ,
ṟuṟitoṟu mekiṉam payilpoḻiṟ ṟuṟicic cuppiramaṇiyatē cikaṉē.       7
3127
maruvivē taṇṭat tiruntanī yaṭiyēṉ vaṉmaṉat tiruntiṭa laritō,
poruvilav varimē ṉicikara ṉoruvaṉ pōyaṟa vūṉṟiya niṉṟā,
ḷoruvivaṉ malampō yoḻiyamaṟ ṟitaṉka ṇūṉ ṟiṭa lariyatō collāy,
turuvimāṉ mutalōr cūḻperun tuṟi cic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       8
3128
vaḷi taru puṟattuk karukiruṭ paṭalam vayaṅkuniṉ pārviyā laṉṟi,
viḷitaru maṟṟoṉ ṟālvila kuvatō virāvakat tiruṭpaṭa lamuniṉ,
ṉaḷitaru karuṇip pārviyā laṉṟi yakalvati laṉamiṉā rōṭu,
tuḷitaru perunīrt tuṟikaḷcūḻ tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       9
3129
kōlaniṉ centā mariyaṭi viṭṭār kūṟṟuva ṉaṭiviṭā tavarē,
cīlani ṉaṅkic ceyyatā maritañ ceṉṉimēl vittiṭap peṟātār,
cālavu morutā marittavi camarvāṉ ṟaṉki vit tiṭutalpeṟ ṟavarē,
cūlaniṉṟoḷiru māḷikit tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       10
3130
attaniṉ ṟaṭantōṭ ceṅkaḻu nīri yavāviya varimatarmaḻikkaṭ,
puttamu taṉiyā ṭaḷavamu macōkum poṉṉiṟam politaḷar māvu,
nittila muyirkkuñ calacamum peṟṟā ṇīlamum peṟukuvaḷ pōlun,
tuttamoyt ticikku maḷippoḻiṟ ṟuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       11
3131
maṉṉiya niṉatu nāmavin teḻutti vayaṅkuniṉ pāluṟappeṟṟu,
miṉṉiya maṉattuc cumantiṭā rellām viriñcaṉār nalkupal leḻutti,
muṉṉiya tuyarat toṭukarun taliyiṉ muḻukkavuñ cumappava raṉṟō,
tuṉṉiya kaḻaṉi cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       12
3132
pittaveṉ ṟikaḻōm peṇkoṭi maṉivāyp pēyuṟaṅa kiruṭpoḻu tiṉṉē,
yattacel leṉṉōn tutipurin titaya māya vam pōrukat tirutti,
nittam muvappō maṭikaḷuk ketaṉka ṇikaḻvirup paruḷceyal vēṇṭuñ,
cuttanaṟ ṟavattār cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       13
3133
matikkumā rūriṉ maṟukuvaṉ pūviṉ malikaru kiruḷiṭi malarntu,
titikkuniṉ pātac ceyyatā marika ṭeṇṭiri yuṭutta vā rūriṟ,
katikkumem maṉamā maṟukuvaṉ pūviṟ kaviṉuṟa malartarumeṉṟē,
tutikkumōr kaṭami pūṇṭaṉan tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       14
3134
tavaruṭi yalivē ḷeyvatu kuṟiyār taḻiyuniṉ ceṅkaḻu nīrvēṭ,
ṭivaruṭi yiḻantā ḷeṉpatu kuṟiyā remmaṉi mārkaṇēṟ ṟirāvi,
yavaruṭi yaṟuttā riṉṉamum pacali yaḻa kiya mēṉiyiṟ ṟīrā,
ṭuvaruṭi yitaḻā ḷaruḷpuri tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       15
3135
aiyaniṉ potunā mattalāṟ ciṟappi ṉamintanā mattiṉumemakku,
meyyakaṉ kuṟalāṟ potumiyi ṉaṉṟi mēviya ciṟappiṉu maṭimi,
yuyyanaṉ kuṟalā meṉattuṇin tiruntō mutuniṟi vēṟumā ṟaruḷvāy,
tuyyanaṟ pulavar cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       16
3136
kolikuṟit teṭuttal ceytiṭā maḻuviṟ koṇṭa taṉ ṟuṉṟiru mēṉi,
yalipurin temmi yaṭarttiṭum panta maṭaṅ kalu maṭarttiṭaṟ kaṉṟō,
talimimaṟ ṟatukoṇ ṭatuceyā tirukkun taṉmicāṉ ṟavarkkaḻa kāmō,
tolivilpal vaḷamuñ cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       17
3137
karuviki yikappiṟ pōtamām pōtaṅ kiyikap piṟṟiru varuḷā,
maruviya varuḷki yikappiṉiṉ pāmim māṇpuniṉ ṉōkkiṉā laṉṟi,
yoruviya maṟṟi yoṉṟaṉā lāmō virippava ruriyelā māyun,
turuviya pukaḻō yaruṭperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       18
3138
iruṅkaṭaṟ puviyi liṭamaṟap paranta teḻuntumē liṭattiṉum paranta,
toruṅkuniṉ pukaḻmaṟ ṟiṉṉumaḵ tavāvi yuṟamuya ṟaṉmitērn taṉṟē,
yaruṅkali vāta vūrarmuṉ ṉiṉippē rācivā riyaṉeṉap pukaṉṟār,
curuṅkinīr muḻukka maṟāvayaṟ ṟuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       19
3139
matinati maḻumān mutaliya maṟittu māravēṇ mēṟpakimaṟiyāy,
potiyumat tiṟattā luṉṉiyā ṉakaṇṭa pūraṇacivaṉeṉat teḷintē,
ṉatirkaṭa lulakiṟ ṟeḷitarār tāmu maṟitarap pukaluvēṉ kaṇṭāy,
tutiyoṭu pulavar cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       20
3140
aiyayāṉ veḷippaṭ ṭiṭiṉaṭaṅ kuvinī yaṭaṅkiyalyā ṉīveḷip paṭuvi,
veyyayā ṉaṭaṅkum vakipurin torunī veḷippaṭā tiruppateṉ ṉuriyāy,
viyamē luyartam mutaṉmiyi maṟantu vāḻnaru muḷarkolō matittut,
tuyyamā tavarkaḷ cūḻ perun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       21
3141
eṉṟunām virumpuñ ceṅkaḻunīri yivaḷvirum piṉaḷeṉac ciṉantu,
kaṉṟumā matiyaṅ kaṉaṉṟeḻu muḻakkaṅ kāṉṟeḻu muyirttavā ritiyuṅ,
koṉṟutīr kuvaṉeṉ ṟuṭiyavēḷ ciliyiṟ kōṟoṭu maṭantiyeṉ ceyvā,
ṭuṉṟunīḷ karuṇi ceytaruṭuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       22
3142
yāṉmuta leṉṟē yeḻuvaṉep poḻutu meytaṟi yāminī yorunāṉ,
ṟāṉmuta leṉṟē yeḻutalaḵ taṉṟē cattiya meṉparāyn tamintā,
rūṉmuta liyaveṉ ṉoḻiviṉuk ketirpārt tuṟutaleṉ ṉurittiṭal vēṇṭuñ,
cūṉmutal paṇitta kaḻaṉicūḻ tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       23
3143
aṭiyava ṉīyeṉ ṟāvaṇa nīyē yamittavi pukūuvaḻakkāṭi,
neṭiyavā rūrar tammiyāṇ ṭāykku nikaḻumō rāvaṇa meḻutik,
koṭiyayāṅ koṭuppō maṟuttiṭō maṭimi koṇṭunaṉṟāṭarat taṭiyeṉ,
ṟuṭiyami yiṭiyārcūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       24
3144
pālinēr moḻiyāḷ vēṇṭuvār vēṇṭum paṭiyaruḷ paṭiyaṉī yeṉṟu,
māliyē vēṇṭa māliyē yaḷittāy māliyē niṉakkumī tiya,
nūliyō revaru mayakkaṟut tāḷu nōṉmiya ṉeṉpatu poyyō,
cōlimēṉ mukilvan tulāvarun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       25
3145
aṉṟuvaṇ kūṭa ṉakariṉmā pāvi yāyiṉā ṉoruvaṉi yāṇṭāy,
naṉṟumaṟ ṟataṉāṟ perumpuka ḻaṭintāy nāyiṉēṉ ṟaṉṉiyu māṇṭā,
liṉṟumaṟ ṟataṉāṟ perumpukaḻ paṭumē yilāpameṇ ṇārkaḷu muḷarō,
tuṉṟumeyt tavattār niṟiperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       26
3146
uyariya voḻukka meṉumperuṅ kariyu ḷuṇmiyeṉ ṟurittiṭu mavaliṟ,
peyarvilcē taṉameṉ ṟurittiṭuñ cēṟṟiṟ perukuṟu maṉpeṉum puṉaliṉ,
mayarvaṟat tōṉṟu niṉṉaṭik kamalam vakuttavi yilātaveṉ ṉuḷḷat,
tuyaraṟi yakattun tōṉṟumō tuṟicic cippira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       27
3147
nāṭuniṉ ṉaṭika ṭiyāṉiyā ruvantu nayattaka veṭutteṭut turiyār,
nīṭumaṇ ṭōyap paṇitarā rulaki ṉikaḻtaliṟ puḷvilaṅ kāti.
kūṭumaḵ ṟiṇiyāy nikaḻvatē naṉṟu kuṟitturi nintiyiṉ miyiṉār,
cūṭumeyt tavattar cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       28
3148
varikeḻu niṉatu puyattiyā ṉavāva maṟṟiyap puyamvaṭa variyip,
puritapa muṉṉaṅ kuḻittatā mantap porupaki niṉintataṟ ketirām,
virikeḻu variyi lutittamel vaḷiyum veppamik kuṟaveṉi varuttuñ,
curinuka raḷipaṇ payilpoḻiṟṟuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       29
3149
kalipala payiṉṟu teḷintavar varuntik kaṟṟataṟ kuṟupayaṉāka,
malivaru niṉiyē pāṭutal ciṟappu maṟṟuḷa pacukkaṇmēṟ pāṭa,
lalivaru tīmpā laṭṭeṭut tiḻināyk kāṅkalaṅ kaviḻppatu pōlān,
tolivilpal vaḷañcā laṟputat tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       30
3150
muṟṟiya karuṇi niṉṉiyā ṉavāva muṉṉamōr māṅkuyiṟaṉṉip,
paṟṟiya pakiyiṟ ṟaṭintaṉi yāmap pakiniṉin taṉṉi mārk kollāc,
ceṟṟiya ciṟiyak kuyiliṉa maḷiyēṉ cevivetup pēṟamīk kūvuñ,
coṟṟiyeṉ ṟaruḷvāy naṉṟucā ṟuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       31
3151
karumala viruṭṭuḷ yāṉeṉa teṉṉak katitteḻu morumati tōṉṟa,
voruvilpal koṭumip paṉivirā yuṭaṟṟa vuṟṟatō reṉṉiṭat tiṉumuṉ,
ṟiruvaṭik kamala meṅṅaṉa malarun tēcikōt tamapperu māṉē,
curutiyā yiramu muḻakkaṟāt tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       32
3152
terintuṉi viḻintēṉ mataṉmaṇak kōlañ ceyyavan tāṉitu muṟiyō,
purintapoṉ malarā lākiya pāyal poṅkaḻa ṟarumoru malarkku,
virintaven taḻaṉmuṉ ṟantaṉi yāmpiṉ virintavi māṟucey vaṉapār,
corintaveṇ ṭaraḷa nilāvarun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       33
3153
matiyiṉi varuttu niṉṉaṭik kamala māṇpiyam matiyiṉāl varuntum,
potiyaceṅ kamala meṇṇiyuḷ ḷuṭintu polivira kuḷacila polivāṉ,
vitiyiṉic cumantu muṟāmiyiṉ viḷarppa veḷḷiya tāmari yeṉpār,
tutipayil kaviñar kaḻakamār tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       34
3154
maṟiyavaṉ paṭittaṉ māyavaṉ purattal vāyttiṭāvorutiru vuruva,
maṟiperum pukaḻnī koṇṭakā raṇattā laṉṉa nāṉ mukaviti paṭitta,
ṉaṟikeḻu tuḷavat tāriṉāṉ purattaṉavilpala vuruvamyā moḻintēn,
tuṟikeḻu poṉṉi vaḷap perun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       35
3155
mēviya kiriyi ñāṉameṉ ṟētta viḷaṅkuniṉ ṟiruvaṭik kamalam,
pāviya cuvippē rāṉantat tēṟal parukupu tēkkumik keṟivāṉ,
vāviya vaṭiyēṉ maṉakkaruñ curumpar vantuvīḻ nāḷumuṇ ṭāmō,
tūviyan tōki payilpoḻiṟ ṟuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       36
3156
eṉakkuṉik koṭuttu niṉakkeṉik kōṭa liyalpiḵ taṉṟiyop pillā,
niṉakkeṉik koṭutteṉ ṟaṉakkuṉik kōṭaṉītiyaṉ ṟēmuta ṉīyē ,
maṉakkuvap puṟuttum palakatan tiraṉī maṟṟaḵ tillava ṉaṭiyēṉ,
coṉakkula matilkō purampoli tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       37
3157
maruḷaka laṭiyār cūṭṭiya kaḻunīr māliniṉ cēvaṭip polita,
liruḷpaṭu poḻuti leḻumoru koḻuna ṉeḻiṉmika maṇattalā laṅkup,
poruḷpeṟu tāṉu maṭuttuṟa maṇattup polintuvāḻn tiruppatu puriyuñ,
curuḷviri katalip poṅkarcūḻ tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       38
3158
kaṟiyaṭi varākaṅ kuraṇṭamīṉ matiyaṅ kariyili mutaṟpala vaṟṟi,
niṟivali tapanī ceytaṉi yavaṟṟi ṉittila maṉittumap pakiyāṉ,
muṟimiyi ṉuṉṟō ḷavāviya veṉṉi muṟukuvep puṟuttivāṭ ṭuvapār,
tuṟitoṟuñ caṅkam payilperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       39
3159
akattiru ḷaṭiyārk koḻippava ṉīyeṉ ṟaṟintiṭa luṇmiyē yaḻaku,
nakattikaḻ niṉatu tōḷaṇi kaḻunīr nayanta nā ṉippuṟat tiruḷum,
pukattiyaṅ kiṭuvē ṉoḻittiṭāt tiṟameṉ pukaluti puṇṇiyap poruḷē,
cukattiyaṉ muṉivar cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       40
3160

valliniṉ ṉaṭiyen ṟalimici yēṟṟiṉ malaravaṉ kivarin tiṭalav,
vellitīr taliṉāṉ malarkkimaṟ ṟaṉiyā ṉiruntali yoṉṟuveṉ ṟatumu,
ṉollimeṉ malarttā ḷaṉṉava ṉeṇki yoruṅkuveṉ ṟaḵtitaṉ pukaḻeṉ,
ṟollinaṟ ṟavattār niṟiperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       86
3206
puṉmiyēn talimē ṉiṉatukik kamalam politarak kuvintumē viṭumōr,
naṉmicār taliṉmaṟ ṟayaṉkara kamala nāṭoṟu malarntunīṅ kiṭumat,
taṉmitē rāta maṭavarap piramaṉ ṟaṉṟoḻil vaḷarppava raṉṟō
tonmimā tavattar niṟiperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       87
3207
parāriyā lamarntāṉ muṉṉamip poḻutu parāvara camaruvāṉ viḻiyiṟ,
ṟarātala muvavā teṉṉavuṅ kēḷā ṭalivaṉmaṟ ṟavaṉalā tiliyeṉ,
ṟarāvaka lalku laṉṉameṉ ṉaṭiyāḷayaruvāṇ mutukkuṟin taṉaḷkāṇ,
curātipar virumpic cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       88
3208
veyyavā pāca mārkkamat taṉiyum vīṭṭuniṉ ṟiruvaṭi yaṭintē,
ṉiyaveṅ kālaṉ vīcupā cattō ṭivakip pācamuṅ kaḻala,
vuyyavāḻ niṉatu mukattunam pācamoḷirtarap purintaṉi yuyntēṉ,
ṟuyyamā tavarkaḷ cūḻperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       89
3209
pulliya vōrū rēṉumil leṉṟu pukaṉṟumaṟ ṟataṟkumā ṟāka,
nalliyal veṇṇey nakupukaḻt tuṟiyūr nalamali kaṭantikoṟ ṟavaṉūr,
villiya ṟiruvā vaṭutuṟi purappāy veṟutta mūṉ ṟūrmakiḻ taravō,
colliya vavaṟṟoṉ ṟākiya tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       90
3210
uṉṉiṭik kaḷaṅka muṟutali ṉuṟāmeṉ ṟurittiyēṉ muṉṉiyam matiyan,
taṉṉiṭi yamarntāy kaḷaṅkamaṅ kiliyō cāṟṟeṉi ṉiyātucāṟ ṟiṭuvā,
yeṉṉiṭi yatunē reṉṟamarvāyē leytarum pukaḻniṉak kuṇṭān,
tuṉṉiṭi maṭavār cūḻ perun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       91
3211
karikuḻit teḻutip puṉitarap peṟṟa kaṇkaṇiṉ ṟiruvuruk kaṇṭu,
varivaḷik kulattik kiyiṉiṉ ṟoḻikka malarkkara naṉṟuko leṉṉap,
puriyumak kayaṟkaṇ muttukap poṟātu pom malvem mulimuttaṅ karukuñ,
curikuḻaṟ karuḷva teṉṟuvāṉ ṟuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       92
3212
pāyavep pakalā niṉṉuṭi yuḷattiṟ patitara vamartarē meṉṉiṉ,
mēyamuṟ pariti maṇṭalat tamarnta vittakaṉ yāreṉak kēṭpa,
ṉāyaviv viṉāviṟ kevviṭi koṭuppā yattaku tōlvi niṟ kāmō,
tōyameṉ kaḻaṉi vaḷapperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       93
3213
vēṇṭamuṉ kaṇṭat taṭakkiya viṭatti veḷiviṭutteṉaviru ṇimiru,
māṇṭaceñ caṭinīr kotippoṭu veḷiyē vanteṉap pāṉilā viriyum,
pūṇṭavem paṇiyi ṉuyirppeṉa viḷaṅkāl pōtaru meṅṅaṉam poṟuppā,
ṭūṇṭaniṉ ṟavirā viḷakkaṉā ṭuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       94
3214
āṉṟanī micinta parikalac cēṭa maṉpumik kēṟṟamātavaruk,
kēṉṟavem pacinōy maruntuko luṭali leytiya nōyteṟu maruntō,
cāṉṟamum malanōy tavirttiṭu maruntō tavāvataṉ perumiyā turikkēṉ,
ṟōṉṟameyñ ñāṉam payilperun tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       95
3215
kiyiṉun tavirttāy nutaliṉun tavirttāy kaṭuntaḻa laṉiyatu kuṟittu,
niyiṉu nika niṉiyetirp paṭṭa naṅkimār kāliyaṅ kuṟuvāṉ,
ceyyiṉu naṉṟu kāliyaṅ kuṟuvāṉ ceytaṉa raṉṉati naṉṟō,
tuyyiṉuṅ kūrtta matiyarcūḻ tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       96
3216
miṭicaṭi maṟittu nīyeḻun taruḷi mēviṉi yāyiṉu niṉakkup,
puṭiyuṟa malarkki koṭuppava riṉiyaṉ poṟcaṭi yuṭiyaṉmuṉ ṉeṉṟu,
taṭiyaṟa vevaru maṟitaru pākkut tāñ cuman tuṇarttiṭat teḷintēṉ,
ṟoṭimatup piliṟṟuṅ kāva ṇat tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       97
3217
vaḻivaḻi yaṭiyark kaṇuttuṇip pōtam vayaṅkumēṟ poṟāmiyu maṉiyār,
kaḻiyaviṉ paṭiyu mavāvumaṟ ṟaṉiyār karumala mēlaṭu muṉivu,
moḻinama pirama veṉumiṉā moḻiyu muṭiyiyā ṉīyumī taṟaṉē,
cuḻiyali yeṟiyum poṉṉicūḻ tuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       98
3218
kaṟṟava ruvakkuṅ kalviyil yāṉuṅ kaṭalakat taṅkaṇap puṉalcārn,
tuṟṟatēṟ ṟirutta māvatu kaṭuppa vuṉperum pukaḻvira vutalāṟ,
kuṟṟamiṉ ṟākic ceyyuḷā meṉṟē kuṇamilpuṉ coṟkoṭu tutikaḷ,
coṟṟaṉa ṉuvantu kōṭivāṉ ṟuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       99
3219
maṇṇiya karuṇip peruñcayi lāti marapupal lūḻiyum vāḻka,
viṉṉiya mukiliṉ muḻaṅkavek kālu milaṅkuniṉ canniti vāḻka,
paṉṉiya niṉatu nāmavin teḻuttum palliṭan toṟumpu kūu vāḻka,
tuṉṉiya niṉcīr vāḻkavāṉ ṟuṟicic cuppira maṇiyatē cikaṉē.       100

3.67 tiruppiramapuram - vaḻimoḻittiruvirākam

tiruppiramapuram - vaḻimoḻittiruvirākam
paṇ - cātāri
tirucciṟṟampalam


714
curarulaku nararkaḷpayil taraṇitalam muraṇaḻiya araṇamatilmup
purameriya viravuvaki caravicikoḷ karamuṭiya paramaṉiṭamām
varamaruḷa varaṉmuṟiyi ṉiriniṟikoḷ varucuruti ciravuriyiṉāṟ
piramaṉuyar araṉeḻilkoḷ caraṇaviṇi paravavaḷar piramapuramē. 3.67.1

715. tāṇumiku vāṇicikoḷ tāṇuviyar pēṇumatu kāṇumaḷaviṟ
kōṇunutal nīḷnayaṉi kōṇilpiṭi māṇimatu nāṇumvakiyē
ēṇukari pūṇaḻiya vāṇiyalkoḷ māṇipati cēṇamararkōṉ
vēṇuviṉi yēṇinakar kāṇiṟivi kāṇanaṭu vēṇupuramē. 3.67.2

716. pakaloḷicey nakamaṇiyi mukimalarinikaḻcaraṇa vakavumuṉivark
kakalamali cakalakali mikavuricey mukamuṭiya pakavaṉiṭamām
pakikaḷiyum vakiyilaṟu mukiṟiyi mikaaruḷa nikarilimiyōr
pukavulaku pukaḻeḻil tikaḻanika ḻalarperuku pukalinakarē. 3.67.3

717. aṅkaṇmati kaṅkinati veṅkaṇara vaṅkaḷeḻil taṅkumitaḻit
tuṅkamalar taṅkucaṭi yaṅkinikar eṅkaḷiṟi taṅkumiṭamām
veṅkatirvi ḷaṅkulakam eṅkumetir poṅkeripu laṉkaḷkaḷivōr
veṅkuruvi ḷaṅkiyumi paṅkarcara ṇaṅkaḷpaṇi veṅkuruvatē.3.67.4

718. āṇiyalpu kāṇavaṉa vāṇaviyal pēṇiyetir pāṇamaḻicēr
tūṇiyaṟa nāṇiyaṟa vēṇucili pēṇiyaṟa nāṇivicayaṉ
pāṇiyamar pūṇavaruḷ māṇupira māṇiyiṭa mēṇimuṟiyiṟ
pāṇiyula kāḷamika vāṇiṉmali tōṇinikar tōṇipuramē. 3.67.5

719. nirāmaya parāpara purātaṉa parāvuciva rākavaruḷeṉ
ṟirāvume tirāyatu parāniṉi purāṇaṉama rātipatiyām
arāmici yirāteḻil tarāyara parāyaṇa varākavuruvā
tarāyaṉi virāyeri parāymiku tarāymoḻi virāyapatiyē. 3.67.6

720. araṇiyuṟu muraṇarpalar maraṇamvara viraṇamati laramalipaṭik
karamviciṟu virakaṉamar karaṇaṉuyar paraṉeṟikoḷ karaṉatiṭamām
paravamutu viravaviṭal puraḷamuṟu maraviyari ciramariyavac
ciramaraṉa caraṇamavi paravaviru kirakamamar cirapuramatē. 3.67.7

721. aṟamaḻivu peṟavulaku teṟupuyavaṉ viṟalaḻiya niṟuviviraṉmā
maṟiyiṉoli muṟimuralcey piṟiyeyiṟa ṉuṟaaruḷum iṟivaṉiṭamāṅ
kuṟiviṉmika niṟitiyuḻi maṟiyamarar niṟiyaruḷa muṟiyoṭuvarum
puṟavaṉetir niṟinilavu poṟiyaṉuṭal peṟavaruḷu puṟavamatuvē. 3.67.8

722. viṇpayila maṇpakiri vaṇpiramaṉ eṇperiya paṇpaṭikoṇmāl
kaṇpariyu moṇpoḻiya nuṇporuḷkaḷ taṇpukaḻkoḷ kaṇṭaṉiṭamām
maṇpariyu moṇpoḻiya nuṇpucakar puṇpayila viṇpaṭaravac
caṇpimoḻi paṇpamuṉi kaṇpaḻicey paṇpukaḷi caṇpinakarē. 3.67.9

723. pāḻiyuṟi vēḻanikar pāḻamaṇar cūḻumuṭa lāḷaruṇarā
ēḻiṉici yāḻiṉmoḻi yēḻiyavaḷ vāḻumiṟi tāḻumiṭamāṅ
kīḻicikoḷ mēlulakil vāḻaracu cūḻaracu vāḻavaraṉuk
kāḻiyacil kāḻiceya vēḻulakil ūḻivaḷar kāḻinakarē. 3.67.10

724. naccaravu kacceṉava ciccumati yucciyiṉmi liccorukiyāṉ
meyccira maṇicculaki ṉiccamiṭu picciyamar piccaṉiṭamām
maccamata naccimata macciṟumi yicceytava vaccaviratak
koccimura vaccarpaṇi yaccurarkaḷ naccimiṭi koccinakarē. 3.67.11

725. oḻukalari taḻikaliyil uḻiyulaku paḻiperuku vaḻiyiniṉiyā
muḻutuṭalil eḻumayirkaḷ taḻuvumuṉi kuḻuviṉoṭu keḻuvucivaṉit
toḻutulakil iḻukumalam aḻiyumvaki kaḻuvumuri kaḻumalanakarp
paḻutiliṟi yeḻutumoḻi tamiḻvirakaṉ vaḻimoḻikaḷ moḻitakiyavē. 3.67.12

3.68 tirukkayilāyam - tiruvirākam


paṇ - cātāri
tirucciṟṟampalam


726
vāḷavari kōḷapuli kīḷaturi tāḷiṉmici nāḷumakiḻvar
āḷumavar vēḷanakar pōḷayila kōḷakaḷi ṟāḷivaravil
tōḷamarar tāḷamatar kūḷiyeḻa mīḷimiḷir tūḷivaḷarpoṉ
kāḷamukil mūḷumiruḷ kīḷaviri tāḷakayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.1

727. puṟṟaravu paṟṟiyaki neṟṟiyatu maṟṟorukaṇ oṟṟiviṭiyaṉ
ceṟṟateyil uṟṟatumi yaṟṟavarkaḷ naṟṟuṇivaṉ uṟṟanakartāṉ
cuṟṟumaṇi peṟṟatoḷi ceṟṟamoṭu kuṟṟamila teṟṟeṉaviṉāy
kaṟṟavarkaḷ coṟṟokiyiṉ muṟṟumoḷi peṟṟakayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.2

728. ciṅkavari maṅkiyarkaḷ taṅkaḷaṉa ceṅkiniṟi koṅkumalartūy
eṅkaḷviṉi caṅkiyavi iṅkakala vaṅkamoḻi yeṅkumuḷavāyt
tiṅkaḷiruḷ noṅkavoḷi viṅkimiḷir toṅkaloṭu taṅkavayalē
kaṅkiyoṭu poṅkucaṭi yeṅkaḷiṟi taṅkukayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.3

729. muṭiyacaṭi piṭiyatoru vaṭiyamaḻu vuṭiyarceṭi yuṭiyataliyil
veṭiyaviṉi koṭiyarkeṭa viṭucilpali noṭiyamakiḻ aṭikaḷiṭamāṅ
koṭiyakura luṭiyaviṭi kaṭiyatuṭi yaṭiyiṉoṭu miṭiyiṉatirak
kaṭiyakural neṭiyamukil maṭiyavata raṭikoḷkayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.4

730. kuṭaṅkiyi ṉuṭaṅkeri toṭarnteḻa viṭaṅkiḷar paṭaṅkoḷaravam
maṭaṅkoḷi paṭarntiṭa naṭantaru viṭaṅkaṉa tiṭantaṇmukilpōyt
taṭaṅkaṭal toṭarntuṭa ṉuṭaṅkuva viṭaṅkoḷa miṭintakuralāṟ
kaṭuṅkaliṉ muṭaṅkaḷi nuṭaṅkara voṭuṅkukayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.5

731. ētamila pūtamoṭu kōtituṇi yātimutal vētavikirtaṉ
kītamoṭu nītipala vōtimaṟa vātupayil nātaṉnakartāṉ
tātupoti pōtuviṭa vūtuciṟi mītutuḷi kŪtalnaliyak
kātaṉmiku cōtikiḷar mātupayil kōtukayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.6

732. ceṉṟupala veṉṟulavu puṉṟaliyar tuṉṟaloṭum oṉṟiyuṭaṉē
niṉṟamarar eṉṟumiṟi vaṉṟaṉaṭi ceṉṟupaṇi kiṉṟanakartāṉ
tuṉṟumalar poṉṟikaḻcey koṉṟiviri teṉṟaloṭu ceṉṟukamaḻak
kaṉṟupiṭi tuṉṟukaḷi ṟeṉṟivimuṉ niṉṟakayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.7

733. maruppiṭi neruppeḻu tarukkoṭu cerucceyta paruttakaḷiṟiṉ
poruppiṭi viruppuṟa virukkiyi yorukkuṭaṉ arakkaṉuṇarā
toruttiyi verukkuṟa veruṭṭalum nerukkeṉa niruttaviralāṟ
karuttila voruttaṉi yeruttiṟa nerittakayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.8

734. pariyatiri periyapuṉal variyapuli yuriyatuṭi pariciyuṭiyāṉ
variyavaḷi yariyakaṇi yuruviṉoṭu puriviṉavar pirivilnakartāṉ
periyeri yuruvamatu teriyavuru parivutarum arumiyataṉāṟ
kariyavaṉum ariyamaṟi puriyavaṉum maruvukayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.9

735. aṇṭartoḻu caṇṭipaṇi kaṇṭaṭimi koṇṭaviṟi tuṇṭamatiyō
ṭiṇṭipuṉi vuṇṭacaṭi muṇṭatara caṇṭaviruḷ kaṇṭariṭamāṅ
kuṇṭamaṇa vaṇṭaravar maṇṭikiyil uṇṭuḷaṟi miṇṭucamayaṅ
kaṇṭavarkaḷ koṇṭavarkaḷ paṇṭumaṟi yātakayi lāyamaliyē. 3.68.10

736. antaṇvari vantapuṉal tantatiri cantaṉamo ṭuntiyakiluṅ
kantamalar kontiṉoṭu mantipala cintukayi lāyamalimēl
entiyaṭi vantaṇuku cantamoṭu centamiḻ icintapukalip
pantaṉuri cinticeya vantaviṉi nintupara lōkameḷitē. 3.68.11

3.69 tirukkāḷatti - tiruvirākam


paṇ - cātāri
tirucciṟṟampalam


737
vāṉavarkaḷ tāṉavarkaḷ vātipaṭa vantatoru mākaṭalviṭan
tāṉamutu ceytaruḷ purintacivaṉ mēvumali taṉṉiviṉavil
ēṉamiṉa māṉiṉoṭu kiḷḷitiṉi koḷḷeḻi lārkkavaṇiṉāṟ
kāṉavartam māmakaḷir kaṉakamaṇi vilakukā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.1

738. mutuciṉavil avuṇarpuram mūṉṟumoru noṭivariyiṉ mūḷavericey
caturarmati poticaṭiyar caṅkarar virumpumali taṉṉiviṉavil
etiretira vetirpiṇiya eḻupoṟikaḷ citaṟeḻi lēṉamuḻuta
katirmaṇiyiṉ vaḷaroḷikaḷ iruḷakala nilavukā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.2

739. vallivaru kāḷiyiva kuttuvali yākimiku tārukaṉinī
kolleṉa viṭuttaruḷ purintacivaṉ mēvumali kūṟiviṉavil
palpala iruṅkaṉi *paruṅkimika vuṇṭavi neruṅkiyiṉamāyk
kallatira niṉṟukaru mantiviḷi yāṭukā ḷattimaliyē.
( * paruki eṉaccolvatu vikāravakiyāṟ paruṅkiyeṉa niṉṟatu.)3.69.3

740. vēyaṉiya tōḷumiyōr pākamatu vākaviṭi yēṟicaṭimēṟ
tūyamati cūṭicuṭu kāṭilnaṭa māṭimali taṉṉiviṉavil
vāykalaca mākavaḻi pāṭuceyum vēṭaṉmala rākunayaṉaṅ
kāykaṇiyi ṉāliṭan tīcaṉaṭi kūṭukā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.4

741. maliyiṉmici taṉilmukilpōl varuvatoru matakariyi maḻipōlalaṟak
koliceytumi yañcavuri pōrttacivaṉ mēvumali kūṟiviṉavil
alikoḷpuṉal aruvipala cuṉikaḷvaḻi yiḻiyavayal nilavumutuvēy
kalakaleṉa voḷikoḷkatir muttamavi cintukā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.5

742. pārakam viḷaṅkiya pakīrataṉ aruntavam muyaṉṟapaṇikaṇ
ṭāraruḷ purintalikoḷ kaṅkicaṭi yēṟṟaaraṉ maliyiviṉavil
vāratar iruṅkuṟavar cēvaliṉ maṭuttavar erittaviṟakiṟ
kārakil irumpuki vicumpukamaḻ kiṉṟakā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.6

743. ārumeti rātavali yākiyaca lantaraṉi āḻiyataṉāl
īrumvaki ceytaruḷ purintavaṉ iruntamali taṉṉiviṉavil
ūrumara vammoḷikoḷ māmaṇiyu miḻntaviyu lāvivaralāṟ
kāriruḷ kaṭintukaṉa kammeṉavi ḷaṅkukā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.7

744. eriyaṉiya curimayir irāvaṇaṉi yīṭaḻiya eḻilkoḷviralāṟ
periyavari yūṉṟiyaruḷ ceytacivaṉ mēvumali peṟṟiviṉavil
variyacili vēṭuvarkaḷ āṭavarkaḷ nīṭuvari yūṭuvaralāṟ
kariyiṉoṭu variyuḻuvi ariyiṉamum veruvukā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.8

745. iṉataḷavi livaṉataṭi yiṇiyumuṭi yaṟitumeṉa ikalumiruvar
taṉaturuvam aṟivariya cakalacivaṉ mēvumali taṉṉiviṉaviṟ
puṉavarpuṉa mayilaṉiya mātaroṭu mintaruma ṇampuṇarumnāḷ
kaṉakameṉa malarkaḷaṇi vēṅkikaḷ nilāvukā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.9

746. niṉṟukava ḷampalakoḷ kiyaroṭu meyyiliṭu pōrviyavarum
naṉṟiyaṟi yātavaki niṉṟacivaṉ mēvumali nāṭiviṉaviṟ
kuṉṟiṉmali tuṉṟupoḻil niṉṟakuḷir cantiṉmuṟi tiṉṟukulavik
kaṉṟiṉoṭu ceṉṟupiṭi niṉṟuviḷi yāṭukā ḷattimaliyē. 3.69.10

747. kāṭatiṭa mākanaṭa māṭucivaṉ mēvukā ḷattimaliyi
māṭamoṭu māḷikikaḷ nīṭuvaḷar koccivayam maṉṉutalivaṉ

3. tirumaṟikkāṭu

karuttila ṉēṉum niṉṟaṉ kaḻalaṭi paṇintu niṉṟēṉ
aruttiyi aṟiva taṉṟō emmiyāḷ ukakku māṟē. 3.5

ituvaṉṟō ulakiṉ nampi emmiyāḷ ukakku māṟē. 3.7

30. nīcaṉām arakkaṉ ṟiṇṭōḷ neritara viralāl ūṉṟun

āciyi yaṟukka uyntiṭ ṭavaṉaṭi parava meyyē
īcaṉārk kāḷa tāṉāṉ eṉpati aṟivit tāyē. 3.8

paramaṉuk kaṭiyar tāmē. 3.11

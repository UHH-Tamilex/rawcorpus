muṟukkuṭi naṟiccata taḷattiru malarttavicu corkkat talattōṭu cēṭacaya ṉattiyum
muṭittali yaṭittali patittetir tutittavarta makkuk koṭuttāṉi mātiramo reṭṭiyum
iṟippoḻu tiṉiṟpoṭi paṭuttaruḷ koṭukkavala cattik karattāṉi yūtiyame ṉattaṉi
iruppiṉu naṭappiṉu niṉippava rirukkavemar cittat tiruppāṉi yāṟirupu yattaṉi
veṟuppoṭu viruppiṉi yaṟuttava ruḷattumalar patmap patattāṉi vētapuri yiṟciṉa
viṭikkoṭi valattiṉu maṭakkoṭi yiṭattumuḷa mukkaṭ ṭiruttāti yārpaṇiku rukkaḷi
maṟikkiḻa vaṉittali puṭittula kaṉittiṉiyum okkap paṭittāṉi mūvirumu kattaṉi
maruttuvar vacukkati ruruttira reṉappoliyu muppat tumukkōṭi tēvarkaḷpu rakkavē. 1.10


2. ceṅkīripparuvam


āciriya viruttam

irukkō liṭumpari purakkōla munnutalil iṭṭapoṭ ṭuñcuṭṭiyum
erimaṇip paṭṭamuṅ kaṭṭupoṉ ṉariñāṇum iḷañāyi ṟutayañceyum
urukkōla muñcuḻi yakkoṇṭi yummucci ucciyum vāḷimuttum
oḷḷoḷi tatumpuṅ kutampiyuṅ kaṇṭukaṇ ṭōṭarik kaṇkaḷikkum
marukkōla nīlak kuḻaṟṟiya lāṭkaru maruntā yiruntateyva
makakkōla mēmutir kiḻakkōla māykkuṟa maṭantimu ṉaṭantumaṟṟat
tirukkōla muṭaṉoru maṇakkōla māṉavaṉ ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē
cettup piṟakkiṉṟa teyvaṅkaṇ maṇavāḷa ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē. 2.1


kumpāti kāriya meṉattami nimittamākak koṇṭavaki lāṇṭaṅkaḷiṉ
kuḻuvukku maṟṟutta miruvarkuṟi yuntalik kūṭiṉun tāṉṟaṉakkac
compāti yaṉmik kaṉittuntaṉ mayameṉum curutikari yāvittumac
curutikku mericuṭarp parutikku miravikula tōṉṟaṟku miḷavalukkum
campāti yoṭunaṟ caṭāyuvuk kumperun tavamuṉiva reḻuvarukkum
taṇṇaḷi curantiṭṭa tīrāta viṉitīrtta tampirāṉ ṟirumēṉiyiṟ
cempāti yuṅkoṇṭa tiyaṉā yakikumara ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē
cettup piṟakkiṉṟa teyvaṅkaṇ maṇavāḷa ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē. 2.2

kikkeṭṭu meṭṭuk kaḷiṟṟip piṭittak kaḷiṟṟoṭu muṭṭaviṭṭuk
kakaṉavaṭ ṭattiṉoṭu parutivaṭ ṭattivaḷi tikirivaṭ ṭattiliṭṭu
mukkaṭ ṭiruttāti kōtaṇṭa meṉavitta vētaṇṭa mātaṇṭamā
mūtaṇṭa kūṭantri kūṭat toṭuñcāṭi mūrik kaṭācalamavaṉ
meykkiṭṭa caṭṭikku nēriṭ ṭiṭappaṭṭa mēkapaṭa lattumoṇṭu
mēlkaṭa liṉipperuṅ kīḻkaṭal pukappeytu viḷiyāṭṭu vīrarkaḷoṭum
tikkeṭṭum viḷiyāṭu cēṉā patikkaṭavuḷ ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē
cettup piṟakkiṉṟa teyvaṅkaṇ maṇavāḷa ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē. 2.3


miviḻi ceṅkamala vallikku nēmiyāṉ maṇimārpu vāṇikkunāṉ
maṟimutali cennāt taṭantiya lāḷoṭum vayittiyak kaṭavuḷārkku
meyvirik kuntoṇṭa ruḷḷat taṭattiṉoṭu vētac ciraṅkaṭuppa
vētapuri kantapuri puḷḷu reṉappoliyum vēḷūra ticiticitoṟum
kivakut taramakaḷir kuraviyāṭ ṭayarperuṅ kayilit taṭañcāralum
kaṉakāca lattumvaḷa rimayā calattumuyar kantamā taṉaveṟpeṉat
teyvatap piṭiyoṭum viḷiyāṭu maḻakaḷiṟu ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē
cettup piṟakkiṉṟa teyvaṅkaṇ maṇāvāḷa ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē. 2.4

mīṉēṟu kuṇṭakaḻi tīvāy maṭuttataṉi villiyā riḷavaloṭum
vitimuṟi vaṇaṅkac caṭāyupuri yiṟkaruṇi veḷḷameṉa vīṟṟirukkum
āṉē ruyarttiṭṭa viyaṟku mammikkum arumarun tākiniṉṟa
ātip pirāṉeṉṟu mummutaṟ kaṭavuḷum aṭittoḻum pāṟṟamaṟṟak
kūṉēṟu matinutaṟ ṟeyvak kuṟappeṇ kuṟippaṟin tarukaṇintuṉ
kuṟṟēval ceyyak kaṭikkaṇ paṇikkeṉak kuṟiyiran tavaṭoṇṭivāyt
tēṉūṟu kiḷavikku vāyūṟi niṉṟavaṉ ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē
cettup piṟakkiṉṟa teyvaṅkaṇ maṇavāḷa ceṅkīri yāṭiyaruḷē. 2.5

vēṟu

cempoṉa ṭicciṟu kiṇkiṇi yōṭu cilampu kalantāṭat
tiruvari yariñā ṇarimaṇi yoṭumoḷi tikaḻari vaṭamāṭap
pimpo ṉanacumpiya tontiyo ṭuñciṟu paṇṭi carintāṭap
paṭṭa nutaṟpoli poṭṭoṭu vaṭṭac cuṭṭi patintāṭak
kampi vitampoti kuṇṭala muṅkuḻi kātu macintāṭak
kaṭṭiya cūḻiyu mucciyu muccik katirmut toṭumāṭa
ampava ḻattiru mēṉiyu māṭiṭa āṭuka ceṅkīri
āti vayittiya nāta purikkukaṉ āṭuka ceṅkīri 2.6

kuḻiyoṭu kuḻiyetir mōtik kātaṇi kuṇṭalam veyilvīcak
kumuta vitaḻkkaṉi vāyamu tūṟiya kuṟunaki nilavūra
muḻuvayi rappuya valayamu muṉki mutāriyu moḷikāla
mutta marumpi yeṉakkuṟu vērvu mukatti larumpiyiṭap
puḻuti yaḷinta pacuntiru mēṉip poṅkoḷi poṅkiyeḻap
puṇṭari kaṅkaṇ malarnta viḻikkaṭi poḻiyaruḷ karipuraḷa
aḻaku kaṉintu mutirnta viḷaṅkaṉi āṭuka ceṅkīri
āti vayittiya nāta purikkukaṉ āṭuka ceṅkīri. 2.7

viralcuvi yuṇṭu kaṉintamu tūṟiya mellitaḻ pularāmē
vimmip porumi viḻuntaḻu talaṟiyuṉ meṉkural kammāmē
karivuṟu mañcaṉa nuṇṭuḷi cintik kaṇmalar civavāmē
kaluḻkalu ḻippuṉa laruvi paṭintuṭal karuvaṭi vuṇṇāmē
uruva maṇicciṟu toṭṭi lutintuni ṉoṇpata nōvāmē
orutā ḷunti yeḻuntiru kiyum oruṅku patittunimirn
taruḷpoḻi tirumuka maciya vacintiṉi tāṭuka ceṅkīri
āti vayittiya nāta purikkukaṉ āṭuka ceṅkīri. 2.8

canta viruttam

kumpama takkaḷi yāṉiyi raṇṭē yoṉṟēmik
koṇṭalli ḻikkayaṉ mīṉumi raṇṭē koṇṭēkic
campara ṉipporu cēvakaṉ vantāṉ vantāṉmuṟ
caṇṭiko ḷaṟkeṉa nērvaru peṇpā laṉpāyē
ampava ḷakkoṭi yēvaḷar kōmpē yeṉṟēnī
aṉṟupu ṉattuyir cōrvatu kaṇṭē muṉpōtum
kampama tattarca kōtara ceṅkō ceṅkīri
kantapu rikkaru ṇāniti ceṅkō ceṅkīri. 2.9

tiṅkaṇu taṟṟiru mātoṭu niṉṟē maṉṟāṭum
ceṅkaṇvi ṭikkoṭi yōṉaruḷ kaṉṟē yoṉṟēyāy
eṅkaḷu ḷattamu tūṟuka rumpē yaṉpāḷark
kiṉpama ḷikkumeyñ ñāṉama runtē yentāyiṉ
koṅkalar mikkuḻal vāḻpoṟi vaṇṭē vaṇṭūtum
kontaḷa kakkuṟa māṉvaḷar kuṉṟē yeṉṟōtum
kaṅkima kaṭkoru kāṉmuḷi ceṅkō ceṅkīri
kantapu rikkaru ṇāniti ceṅkō ceṅkīri. 2.10


3. tālapparuvam


āciriya viruttam

pilkum pacuntē ṉacumpirunta piṉṟā ḻaḷakat taramakaḷir
pētik kuṟumpu viḷiyāṭum pērā yattuc ciṟumaruṅkuṟ
kolkuṅ koṭipōy nuṭaṅkiyiṭa ōṅkum paḷikku nilāmuṟṟat
tuyarcū ḷikiyiṉ marakatattiṉ oḷikāl vīcat teḷivicumpiṟ
palkuñ curapi taraṅkaneṭum pākī ratiyiṉ karikkiḷitta
pacumpul leṉaccen nāvaḷikkum pimpoṟ ṟalaṅkaḷ palakōṭi
malkuñ celvak kantapuri vāḻvē tālō tālēlō
maliyāḷ vayiṟu vāyttamuḻu maṇiyē tālō tālēlō. 3.1

toṭukkun toṭiveṇ ṭukiṟkoṭikaḷ toṭinīrp paravi mukampuḻuṅkat
tōṉṟum paruti maṇittiṇṭēr tūṇṭuṅ kaliṉa vāmpariyi
muṭukkuñ cuṭarppoṟ ṟalattiḻitta muḻunī latti ṉoḻukoḷiyiṉ
muḻukuṅ kaṭavuṇ mālyāṉi mukiliṟ ṟōṉṟa vakaliṭaniṉ
ṟaṭukkuṅ kaḷiṟeṉ ṟaramakaḷir aiyuṟ ṟiṭattaṉ veḷiṟumuṭam
paḷaṟu paṭinta teṉakkaṅki yāṟṟup pacumpo ṉacumpupuṉal
maṭukkuñ celvak kantapuri vāḻvē tālō tālēlō
maliyāḷ vayiṟu vāyttamuḻu maṇiyē tālō tālēlō. 3.2

tīṟṟuñ cutiveṇ ṇilaveṟippat tiraṇmā maṇikaḷ veyilvirikkum
cempoṟ ṟalattup pēramarkkaṭ ciṟiyār naṟiya vakiṟpukiyiṭ
ṭāṟṟuṅ kuḻaṟkāṭ ṭiṉippuyaleṉ ṟāṭa mayilkaṇ ṭampavaḷat
tarumpu nakiyic cakōrappuḷ arunta virinta muḻunakiviṇ
ṭūṟṟu nilavuk kintucili oḷirmā ḷikiyuñ cuḷikiyum
urukip perukuṅ kaluḻiveḷḷam uvarō ṭuvarik kaṭaṟpulavu
māṟṟuṅ kaṭavuṭ kantapuri vāḻvē tālō tālēlō
maliyāḷ vayiṟu vāyttamuḻu maṇiyē tālō tālēlō. 3.3

kiḷikkuñ cakarar toṭṭaperuṅ kiṭaṅkeṉ ṟiṭaṅkar māviyaṅkum
kīḻnī raḻuvak kuṇṭakaḻik keḻumu puṉaṟṟeṇ ṭirimēyntu
tiḷikkuṅ kamañcū ṉeṭumpuyalic ciṟukaṭ peruṅkip pakaṭeṉṟōr
ceṅkaṭ kaḷiṟu piḷiṟanimir tikiri kiriyeṉ ṟivarntuṭalam
iḷikkum paṭiviṇ ṭoṭanivanta eḻiṟpoṟ purici viṇṭupatat
tēṟu mēṇi poṟṟaruvuk kiṭuvē liyumā yēḻulakum
vaḷikkuṅ kaṭavuṭ kantapuri vāḻvē tālō tālēlō
maliyāḷ vayiṟu vāyttamuḻu maṇiyē tālō tālēlō. 3.4

cōlip puṟamup puṭikkaṉikaḷ tūkku muṭatteṅ kimiyavartam
tōṉṟa ṟiruvō lakkattut tōki mayili ṉaṭaṅkuyiṟṟum
ēlak karuṅkon taḷavaḷakat tiḷamā tarukku mutirvēṉil
iḷippāṟ ṟutaṟkut tāṟṟoṭuñcev viḷanīr koṭuppa vīṟṟuḷinta
kālap puyaliṉ mukantuṭikkum kamuku paḻukkāyp pavaḻamuṭaṉ
katarmut tiṭṭuc ceḻumpāḷik kaṟṟik kavari puṭiyiraṭṭum
mālip paḻaṉap parutipuri vāḻvē tālō tālēlō
maliyāḷ vayiṟu vāyttamuḻu maṇiyē tālō tālēlō. 3.5

vēṟu
māli mayattu maṭappiṭi peṟṟu vaḷartta viḷaṅkaḷiṟē
maḻaviṭi yētiru māmaṭi kaṭkeṉa vitta kavuttuvamē
mūla meṉakkula nāṉmaṟi yōliṭu muḻumuta lēmūvā
mukkaṭ kaṉikaṉi yuñcuvi yētaṉi muttik koruvittē
kāli yiḷaṅkati rukketir mutirum katircem pavaḷamuṭaṉ
katirmut tiṭṭuva ṇaṅkak kaṉṉalkol kamukuko leṉavaḷarum
cāli vayaṟṟamiḻ vēḷū raṭikēḷ tālō talēlō
caṅkat tamiḻiṉ ṟalimip pulavā tālō tālēlō. 3.6

karumuki lukkari tāmaṭi vāram kaṇṭiru paṟavikaṭam
kaṇkaḷi poṅkavor paṟavi muṭittali kāṇā tuṭkuvarap
porupuṉa laruvit taliyiṉ micittavaḻ putumati nilavoḻukap
puḷḷū riṟpoli veḷḷi malikkaṭ poṉmali yikkuvavu
kurumaṇi vayirap puyameṉu meṭṭuk kuvaṭuñ cuvaṭupaṭak
kuttip porumiru kōṭu paṭittator kūntaṟ piṭipeṟṟut
tarumoru tontit tantik kiḷiyāy tālō talēlō
caṅkat tamiḻiṉ ṟalimip pulavā tālō tālēlō. 3.7

uṇṇekiḻ toṇṭa ruḷattiruḷ cintiṭa oḷiviṭu muḻumaṇiyē
uyarmaṟi nūlkali muṭiviṉ muṭintiṭum oḻukoḷi marakatamē
viṇṇoṭu maṇṇi viḻuṅki yaruṭkatir viriyu miḷañcuṭarē
meyppulaṉ mēyntu camintator vīṭṭi viḷakkum viḷakkoḷiyē
puṇṇiya nāṟumor peṇkaṉi kaṉiyum puṉita naṟuṅkaṉiyē
puḷḷū reṉavema tuḷḷat taṭaniṟi puttamu takkaṭalē
taṇṇoḷi poṅkiya karuṇā nitiyē tālō tālēlō
caṅkat tamiḻiṉ ṟalimip pulavā tālō tālēlō. 3.8

canta viruttam

tōlāta muttamiḻ nāvā mūvā māvāmac
cūrvē raṟattoṭu vēlā nūlā nūlōtum
cīlāma likkoṭi pālā kīlā mēlākum
tēvāti paṟkoru tēvā vōvā tēkūvum
kālāyu takkoṭi vīṟā vēṟā vēṟēṟum
kāpāli peṟṟaku mārā vīrā pērāḷā
cēlārva yaṟkuru kūrā tālō tālēlō
cēṉāpa tipperu māḷē tālō tālēlō 3.9

ūṉāyu yirkkuyi rāṉāy tālō tālēlō
ōtātu ṇarttiṭu pōtā tālō tālēlō
āṉāva ruṭkaṉu pāṉā tālō tālēlō
āyāta coṟcolu pāyā tālō tālēlō
nāṉāye ṉakkari tāṉāy tālō tālēlō
nātāti kaṭkaṉu pūtā tālō tālēlō
tēṉārpo ḻiṟkuru kūrā tālō tālēlō
cēṉāpa tipperu māḷē tālō tālēlō 3.10




4. cappāṇip paruvam



muṭaṅkun tiripparavi vayiṟuḷin tīṉṟanaṟu muḷarip pirāṭṭivittu
muttāṭu paccip pacuṅkiḷḷi yeṉamaḻali mutirumeṉ kutalikaṟpat
toṭaṅkuṅ kuṟappāvi kaṟṟik kuḻaṟkakiṟ ṟūmamoṭu tāmamiṭṭuc
kaṭikinutal veyarvun tuṭittoḻuku kattūri tūriyaṅ koṇṭu tīṭṭik
kuṭaṅkik kaṭaṅkā neṭuṅkaṭ kaṭikkaḻaku kūravañ caṉameḻutimeṉ
koṅkit taṭattup pacuṅkaḷapa mappiyavaḷ kuṟṟēvaṉ muṟṟumāṟṟit
taṭaṅkuṅ kumappuyaṅ koṭṭinaṭa miṭṭavaṉ cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē
tiyaṉā yakimaruvu teyvanā yakaṉmatali cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē. 4.1

maḻikkon taḷakkalapa mayiliḷañ cāyaṉeṭu matararik keṇṭiyuṇkaṇ
māṉkaṉṟi yamarulaku vāḻap piṟantiṭu maṭappiṭiyi vāṉavillik
kuḻikkun taṭakkit tiruttāti nīroṭu koṭuppak kuṭaṅkiyēṟṟuk
koḻumalar maṇaṅkamaḻ maṇappantar niṟpavak kompumiṉ koṭiyiṉolki
iḻikkum pacumpoṟ ṟacumpeṉa vacumpupoṉ ṉiḷamuli mukaṅkōṭṭiniṉ
ṟeyyāmi nōkkum paṭikkaṭ kaṭikkaṇōk kiṉṉamuta mūṟṟaviṉpam
taḻikkum peruṅkātal veḷḷan tiḷittavaṉ cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē
tiyaṉā yakimaruvu teyvanā yakaṉmatali cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē. 4.2

uṇṇilā vuvakit taṭaṅkaṭal paṭintiṭṭa umparuñ ceṅkaḷampaṭ
ṭoṇparuti yuṭalkiḻit tōṭuṅ kaṭaṟṟāṉi oḷiṟuvā ḷavuṇarkuḻuvum
teṇṇilā matinutaṟ ṟeyvap piṇākkaḷvāyt tēṉamutu mamutavārit
teḷḷamutu muṭaṉirun tuṇṇap paṇittiṭṭa ceṅkivēl pimpuṉattup
paṇṇulā maḻalip pacuṅkiḷavi yeyiṉarpoṟ pāviviḻi vēloṭoppup
pārkkun toṟuntali kaviḻttuniṉ ṟavaṭirup pavaḷattu muttarumpum
taṇṇilā vukkoṇ cakōrameṉa niṉṟavaṉ cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē
tiyaṉā yakimaruvu teyvanā yakaṉmatali cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē. 4.3

maṭanaṭit teyvak kuṟappāvi tiruvuruviṉ mayiliḷañ cāyalunilā
maṇivaṭa maṟappuṭit tiṟumānta kaṉataṉa vaṉappuṅ kuṟittunōkki
iṭukiṭip pāvik kiṉippiḻip pillikol eṉattiru vuḷaṅkuḻintāṅ
kēntiḷaṅ koṅkiyi yiṇippuyat tēntiniṉ ṟellā vuṟuppuniṟkak
kuṭamulik kēyivaḷ kuṭippiṟap pukkiyi kuṇaṅkiṭit tatukoleṉṟak
kōtineṭu nāṇeyta vavayavaṅ kaṭkuḷa kuṇākuṇan taṉiterikkum
taṭamalark kittalañ cēntoḷi tuḷumpavoru cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē
tiyaṉā yakimaruvu teyvanā yakaṉmatali cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē. 4.4

viṇṇeṉ kaṭaṟpuvaṉa muṭavup paṭattēntu vēntupoli pāntaḷ vippum
virinīr varippumeḻil viñciya riruppumuki viṇṭunaṟi virimuṇṭakak
kaṇṇaṉ ṟiruppatamu nāṉmukak kaṭavuḷpūṅ kañcamuṅ kulicappirāṉ
kaṟpakak kāvuniṉ ṟoṇṭarka ṭoḻumpukkor kāṇiyā vittumaṟṟat
tiṇṇeṉ ṟaṭakkiveñ cilivēṭar kuṭikoṇṭa cīṟūru mūrūrtoṟum
centiṉip puṉamūṭu taṇcāral piṟavumām cītaṉak kāṇipeṟṟat
taṇṇeṉ kuṟiñcit talantali yaḷippavaṉ cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē
tiyaṉā yakimaruvu teyvanā yakaṉmatali cappāṇi koṭṭiyaruḷē. 4.5

kaṭalic cuvaṟa vaṭittu miṭittuk kaṉavari tukaḷkaṇṭum
kaṭitiṟ ṟiriya vakuppati yoppak kāravu ṇakkaṭaliṉ
uṭaliṟ perukiya kurutik kaṭalpiṇa vōṅkalo ṭōṅkavamit
toṭṭiya voṭṭala riṟpiṟa kiṭṭavar oḻiyap piṟariyelām
taṭavut tāḻiyiṉ matteṟi tayiriṟ ṟattuti rappuṉaliṟ
ṟacikuṭar niṇamoṭu mūḷi kuḻampac camarviḷi yāṭalceyum
kuṭumic cuṭarvaṭi vēlit toṭṭava koṭṭuka cappāṇi
koḻitamiḻ vēta purikkuma rēcaṉ koṭṭuka cappāṇi. 4.6

camariṟ paṭṭavar veṭṭiya pūtat talivarka ṇiṟpamutaṟ
ṟāmari nāyakaṉ vayiṟu kiḻittut tantoḻi ṟaliniṉṟāṅ
kamariṟ kuriya maṭakkoṭi yāri alittaṉar paṟṟiyeyiṟ
ṟavuṇa reṉattami yuṇarār kaṇavarkaḷ ārppil veṭittaperuṅ
kamariṟ kuruti pilatti nirappiṭu kaḷametir kaṇṭiṉiyak
kāravu ṇakkaṭal cūroṭu māḷak kaṭitiṟ ṟaṭitiyaṭaṟ
kumarak kaṭavu ḷeṉappaṇi vēlava koṭṭuka cappāṇi
koḻitamiḻ vēta purikkuma rēcaṉ koṭṭuka cappāṇi. 4.7

aṟṟa vuṭaṟkuṟi yiṟṟa muṭittali aṅkit talamvittiṭ
ṭāṭu paṟantali yōṭuti rappuṉal āṟu kaṭattukeṉum
ciṟṟala kikkoru pērala kippeṇ tēraḻi yakkaḻiyum
tikirip paricil viṭappaṭu cuḻiyiṟ ṟerumaral maṭpakiñaṉ
paṟṟiya tiriki tirittu viṭattiri pariceṉa vuñcuḻalum
pampara meṉavum varumpaṭi yavuṇar paṭakkaḷa vēḷviceyāk
koṟṟa makaṭpuṇa ruñcuṭar vēlava koṭṭuka cappāṇi
koḻitamiḻ vēta purikkuma rēcaṉ koṭṭuka cappāṇi. 4.8

canta viruttam

vaḷariḷa vaṉamuli malimaka ḷukkor tavappēṟē
maṟitiri poranimir karuṇi koḻitta perukkāṟē
aḷiyumi ṉamuteḻu veḷiyiṉil vitta cuvittēṉē
aṟamutu tavamoṭu vaḷara vaḷarttiṭu naṟṟāyē
kaḷimayil kaṭavivi ṇaṭiya muṭukkiya puttēḷē
kalimaṟi yeṉumural variyaḷi moytta malarkkāvē
teḷitamiḻ paḻakiya matavali koṭṭuka cappāṇi
tiṉakara purivaru taṉimutal koṭṭuka cappāṇi. 4.9

kaṉiyoṭu cuviyamu toḻukiya coṟpayi ṟattāyvēḷ
kaṇiyoṭu piṇiyeṉa vulavu kaṭikkaṇ maṭappāvāy
naṉimalar potuḷiya veḻili taḻitta kuḻaṟkōtāy
naḷirpuṉa micivaḷar kalapam viritta mayiṟpēṭē
eṉavoru kuṟamaka ḷaṭimuṭi vittaṉi muttēvām
iṟivaru muṟimuṟi paṇiyavirukku mutaṟṟēvē
ciṉaviṭi yavararuṇ maḻaviṭi koṭṭuka cappāṇi
tiṉakara purivaru taṉimutal koṭṭuka cappāṇi. 4.10


5. muttap paruvam




kuruku nāṟu centaḷirkkik koṭinuṇ ṇucuppiṟ kōṭṭimayak
kulappūṅ kompu naṟavūḻtta koḻuntā mariyō ṭaviḻntatuḻāyc
caruku nāṟu muṭittaliyiṉ tāma nāṟu tiraṭiṇṭōṭ
ṭātiyāruṅ kaṇṭukaṇṭu taṭaṅkaṇ kaḷippak kuravuvirin
taruku nāṟu tirumēṉi antīṅ kutali maḻalikaṉin
tamuta mūṟu pacuntēṟal acumpu nāṟat teyvamaṇa
muruku nāṟuñ ceṅkaṉivāy muttan taruka muttamē
mummit tamiḻtēr kantapuri murukā muttan tarukavē. 5.1

naṟavu virinta virittōṭṭu naḷiṉat toṭṭiṟ ṟaṭamulippāl
nalki vaḷartta kittāyar nakivāṇ mukattu mārpakattum
kuṟume ṉaṭiya ciṟupacuṅkāṟ kuruti tatumpa vutintucila
kuṟumpu ceyattā ṇōmeṉameṉ kōlkoṇ ṭōccap perumpuvaṉam
niṟuvu moruniṉ peruntaṉmi niṉintāy pōlak kaṉintamutu
nekkup pacuntē ṉacumpūṟa neṭuveṇ ṇilavu virintakuṟu
muṟuva larumpuñ ceṅkaṉivāy muttan taruka muttamē
mummit tamiḻtēr kantapuri murukā muttan tarukavē. 5.2

poḻiyuṅ karuṇip peruveḷḷap puṇari peruki yaliyeṟiyap
poṅki yeḻunta peruṅkātaṟ puḷakam pōrppap pōtuceyum
viḻiyu maṉamuṅ kuḷirtūṅka virinīrc caṭilat toruvaṉiru
vīṇik kutavun tiruccevikaḷ viruntāṭ ṭayara virikoḻittu
vaḻiyuṅ koḻuntēṉ piḻintiṭṭu matura vamutu kuḻittūṟṟum
maḻali tatumpap paḻamaṟiyi vaṭittut teḷitta vārttiyoṉṟu
moḻiyum pavaḷac ceṅkaṉivāy muttan taruka muttamē
mummit tamiḻtēr kantapuri murukā muttan tarukavē. 5.3

kalippā ṉiṟinta mutukkuṟiviṟ kalvic celvar kēḷvinalam
kaṉiyak kaṉiya vamutūṟum kaṭavuṇ maṟiyu mutaṟcaṅkat
talippā valartīñ cuvikkaṉiyum taṇṭē ṉaṟiyum vaṭitteṭutta
cāraṅ kaṉintūṟ ṟiruntapacun tamiḻu nāṟat taṭaṅkarikol
alippāy puṉaṟṟeṇ kaṭalvippum akilāṇ ṭamumpaṉ muṟiyīṉṟum
aḻaku mutira mutirāveṉ ammi yamutu cūṟkoṇṭa
mulippā ṉāṟuñ ceṅkaṉivāy muttan taruka muttamē
mummit tamiḻtēr kantapuri murukā muttan tarukavē. 5.4

puyaluṇ ṭiruṇṭa kontaḷamum poṉṉaṅ kuḻiyu miṉṉakiyum
puḷakam potinta viḷamuliyum puruvac ciliyum pōrttaṭaṅkaṭ
kayaluṅ kalapa mayiliyalum kaṉṉip puṉattōr peṇṇamutiṉ
kāmar nalaṉum paṉṉiraṇṭu kaṇṇāṉ mukantuṇ ṭiṉṉamutiṉ
iyaluñ cuvinal laviyoṭunīt tēkkaṟ ṟiruntat tākkaṇaṅkiṉ
iḻumeṉ kutali kaṉintūṟum itaḻttēṉ cuvikaṇ ṭēmāppāṉ
muyaluṅ kumutak kaṉivāyāl muttan taruka muttamē
mummit tamiḻtēr kantapuri murukā muttan tarukavē. 5.5

vēṟu

kōṭupaṭu koṅkik kuvaṭṭuk kiḷittiṭṭa koṭiyiṭik kaṭiciyarkuḻām
kuraviyiṭu tuḻaṉiyiṟ koṇṭaṟiri yattāvu kuḻavup pakaṭṭuvāḷi
cēṭupaṭu puttē ṇilattup puṉiṟṟiḷañ cētā vayuṟṟumuṭṭac
cēṅkaṉ ṟeṉattaṭavu maṭimaṭi tiṟantūṟṟu tīmpāl ciṉikkaṟpakat
tēṭupaṭu taṭamalart tēṉaruvi yoṭucorin tēriyoṭu kāṉirampa
iḻutupaṭu kaḻaṉiyun teyvamaṇa nāṟavēṉ ṟiṉcuvi mutirntuviḷiyum
kāṭupaṭu cennelpiṅ kaṉṉaṉikar puḷḷūra kaṉivāyiṉ mutta maruḷē
kaṅkikku neṭiyavaṉ ṟaṅkikku morumakaṉ kaṉivāyiṉ muttamaruḷē. 5.6

tuḷitūṅku maḻimukiṟ paṭalaṅ kiḻikkum tukiṟkoṭikaḷ cōliceyyat
tōraṇa mukappiṟ ṟavaḻntēṟu kalimatit tōṟṟatti yaṟukāṉmaṭut
taḷitūṅku tēṉiṟā litutammuṉ vammiṉeṉ ṟaḻinaṟā vārntuniṟkum
annalār kikūppa vāṭavar piḻintūṟṟu maḷavilapa rātamiteṉā
oḷitūṅku mukamatik koppeṉki lēṉviṭutir uyiroṉṟu meṉaviṭalumav
vuṭupatik kaṭavuṇaṟa vuṇṭamaṟ ṟavariṉum uyntō moḻintōmeṉāk
kaḷitūṅku māṭamali kantapuri varumuruka kaṉivāyiṉ mutta maruḷē
kaṅkikku neṭiyavaṉ ṟaṅkikku morumakaṉ kaṉivāyiṉ muttamaruḷē. 5.7

pūmaru vuyirkkuṅ karuṅkon taḷattuviri pūntukaṭ paṭalamumaṇam
poṅkiya naṟumpukip paṭalamuṅ kālamaḻi poḻimukiṟ paṭalañceyat
tāmari mukaccōti yeḻaveḻuñ ciṟumuṟuval taṇṇilavu ceyaveyilceyat
taḻaṉmaṇik kalaṉaka ṉitampamoṭu vemmuli taṭaṅkaṭalu maliyuñceyat
tēmaru kuḻaṟkōti mayilaṉīr kōcikac cemmaleṉa vēṟupuvaṉam
ceyavumval līreṉa maṇantiṭi taṇantavarcol ceñcoṟ pacuṅkuḷicolak
kāmaru maṇaṅkuḻiyu mātarpayil vēḷūra kaṉivāyiṉ mutta maruḷē
kaṅkikku neṭiyavaṉ ṟaṅkikku morumakaṉ kaṉivāyiṉ muttamaruḷē. 5.8

canta viruttam

matiyu natiyu maravum viravu mavuli yoruvaṉ mukkaṇum
vaṉaca mukamu makamu malara maḻali yoḻuku coṟcolum
putalva vimaya mutalvi yaruḷcey puṉita vamarar koṟṟavaṉ
putalvi taḻuvu koḻuna kuṟavar ciṟumi kuṭikoḷ poṟpuya
katiru matiyu moḷira voḷirum oḷiya vaḷiya kaṟpakak
kaṉiyi ṉiṉiya vuruva paruva maḻiyi ṉutavu kittala
mutiru maṟivi laṟiña ruṇaru mutalva taruka muttamē
muṉivar paravu paruti puriyiṉ muruka taruka muttamē. 5.9

vaṭivi ṉaḻaku meḻuta variya puyamu naṟiya cecciyum
marumam viravu kuravu mariyiṉ maṇiyu maṇikoḷ kacciyum
kaṭavu mayilu mayilu moḻuku karuṇi vataṉa paṟpamum
kamala viḻiyum viḻiyu maṉamum eḻuti yeḻuti nittalum
aṭika ḷeṉavu ṉaṭikaḷ paṇiyum aṭiya ralatu maṟṟumvē
ṟamarar kuḻuvu makila maṟiyum ariyu mayaṉu muṟṟuniṉ
muṭiyu maṭiyu muṇara variya mutalva taruka muttamē
muṉivar paravu paruti puriyiṉ muruka taruka muttamē. 5.10




6. varukip paruvam




cempoṟ karuṅkaḻa larikkuraṟ kiṇkiṇi cilampoṭu kaliṉkaliṉeṉat
tiruvariyi larimaṇi kiṇiṉkiṇi ṉeṉappolan tiṇṭōḷiṉ vaḷikalippa
ampoṟ pakaṭṭumār piṟcaṉṉa vīramum āramun tiruvilvīca
aṇimakara kuṇṭalam parutimaṇ ṭalameṉṉa alarkatirk kaṟṟicuṟṟap
pimpoṟ cuṭarccuṭṭi kaṭṭucū ḻiyamuṭaṉ paṭṭamoḷi viṭṭeṟippap
paṅkaya malarntatiru mukamaṇ ṭalantoṟum paṉimuṟuva ṉilavarumpak
kumpap paṭāmuli malipputalvi celvak kumāranā yakaṉvarukavē
kuravukamaḻ tarukanta puriyilaruḷ kuṭikoṇṭa kumarakuru paraṉ varukavē. 6.1

maḻavumutir kaṉivāyp pacuntēṟal veṇṭukil maṭittala naṉippavammi
maṇivayiṟu kuḷirat tavaḻntēṟi yempirāṉ mārpiṉiṟ kuraviyāṭi
muḻuvumutir tuṭiyiṉiṟ ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaṉal mōlinīr peytavittu
muḷimatiyi neḷiyaraviṉ vāymaṭut tiḷamāṉiṉ mutupacik kaṟukarutti
viḻavumutir cemmēṉi veṇṇīṟu tūḷeḻa mikappuḻuti yāṭṭayarntu
liricaṭik kāṭṭiṉiṉ ṟiruviḻikaḷ cēppamuḻu veḷḷanīrt tuḷiyamāṭik
kuḻavumutir celvip peruṅkaḷi varacciṟu kuṟumpucey tavaṉvarukavē
kuravukamaḻ tarukanta puriyilaruḷ kuṭikoṇṭa kumarakuru paraṉ varukavē. 6.2

iruḷaṟa vimikkuniṉ ṟiruvuruvi ṉalarcōti iḷañāyi ṟeṉamukiyaviḻn
tēṭuviri tāmarik kāṭumukiḻ nakinila veṟippavalar kumutavaṉamum
karipuraḷa valimōtu kaṭalik kalakkumaḻa kaḷiṟeṉ vuḻakkiyoruniṉ
kaṇmalarkaḷ cemmalarka ḷākamō kapperuṅ kalaviyaṅ kaṭaliṉmūḻkum
veruḷiṉmaṭa nōkkiṉī raramakaḷi ruṭaṉāṭum viḷiyāṭ ṭeṉattiriporum
veḷḷanīrc caravaṇap poykiyan tuṟiyiṉīḷ vīrarka ḷeṉuṅkōḷarik
kuruḷika ḷoṭumpuṉal kuṭintuviḷi yāṭiya kumāranā yakaṉvarukavē
kuravukamaḻ tarukanta puriyilaruḷ kuṭikoṇṭa kumarakuru paraṉ varukavē. 6.3

kaṭṭuṇṭa paṭarcaṭik kāṭṭempi rāṉvitta kalimatiyo ṭiyanīyak
kavuritiru muṭiyiṉit tilamiṭ ṭiḻittiṭṭa katiriḷam piṟiyiṇippat
teṭṭuṇṭa pōṉmuḻut tiṅkaḷeṉ ṟēkkaṟuñ ceḻumaṇic cūṭṭumōṭṭuc
cempāmpu pivirit tāṭutalu mōṭiniṉ ciṟunaṟuṅ kuñcikkiṭum
maṭṭuṇṭa piṅkulik kāntaḷeṉ ṟaṇiyavam mācuṇam veruṇṭōṭalum
maṇimuṭiyi ṉakutaliyi maṟṟemi nakittiyāl malaravaṉ ṟalinīmuṉam
kuṭṭuṇṭa taṟiyāyko leṉavita ḻatukkum kumāranā yakaṉvarukavē.
kuravukamaḻ tarukanta puriyilaruḷ kuṭikoṇṭa kumarakuru paraṉ varukavē. 6.4

aṅkit talattammi ceṅkaṇ putittaṟ kaṭaṅkāmi yālempirāṉ
alarviḻikaḷ pottalu miruṭpaṭala mūṭavuḷ ḷañciniṉ ṟēṅkavēṅkic
ceṅkit talaṅkoṇ ṭeṭuttaṇip pāṭkum tiruttāti yārkkumuttam
tirumukamo rāṟuṅ koṭuppak katuppiṟ ṟeṟittumut tantarukeṉum
kaṅkiku nalkā teḻuntalaṟi yōṭalum kaṇṇīr tuṭitteṭuttuk
kāṉmalaru nīvittaṉ mārpuṟa vaṇikkumak kavumāri yaruṇmāriyiṟ
koṅki kuṭaṅkoṭṭu pālaruvi yāṭum kumāranā yakaṉvarukavē.
kuravukamaḻ tarukanta puriyilaruḷ kuṭikoṇṭa kumarakuru paraṉ varukavē. 6.5

vēṟu

pintaṇ kamala vaṭṭavaṇip pāvi yaṉiyār pūviriyum
pacumeṉ kuḻaṟkūṭ ṭakiṟpukiyiṉ paṭala mūṭa muṭinaṟaviṉ
kantam potinta centuvarvāyk kaṭici makaḷir cennelippiṅ
kaṉṉa leṉavuṅ kaṉṉalippūṅ kamuka meṉavuṅ kaṭikkūṭāt
tantaṅ karuttuk kamintapaṭi cāṟṟic tāṟṟi muḻumāyac
calati mūḻkit taṭumāṟum camayat tavarpōṟ ṟalimayaṅkum
antaṇ paḻaṉak kantapurik karacē varuka varukavē
aruḷā ṉantak kaṭaṟpiṟanta amutē varuka varukavē. 6.6

vaḷḷik kuḻiyiṟ ṟāvaṭipōm maṭamā ṉōkkiṟ kaṭiciyarkaṇ
mālik kuḻalvaṇ ṭōlamiṭa maṭuvil veṭipōm varivāḷi
paḷḷat tiruṭūṅ kaḻuvanīrp parappeṉ ṟakalvāṉ micittāvap
pākī ratittīm puṉalkiṭitta paricu vīṭṭiṉ payaṉṟuykkum
uḷḷak karuttāṟ piṟitoṉṟi uṇmip poruḷeṉ ṟuḷḷavuntam
uṇarviṟ ṟeyvaṅ kaṭikkūṭṭa uṟuti kiṭitta paṭipōlum
aḷḷaṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūruk karacē varuka varukavē
aruḷā ṉantak kaṭaṟpiṟanta amutē varuka varukavē. 6.7

nañciṟ ṟōyttuk kolitīṟṟum nayaṉa vēluṅ karumpuruva
nāmac ciliyu makalalkul nakupoṟ ṟēru mikalkaṭantu
vañcik koṭinuṇ ṇiṭicāyttu matarttuk kaḷitta mālkaḷiṟum
maṟṟum paṭikaḷ paṟpalavum vakuttuk koṇṭu maṭalaviḻnta
kañcat taviciṟ ṟiruvaṉṉār kaṭalan tāṉik kinimirak
kāmaṉ paṭivī ṭeṉappoliyum kāṭci yāṉu mappeyariṭ
ṭañcoṟ ṟamiḻōr pukaḻvēḷūrk karacē varuka varukavē
aruḷā ṉantak kaṭaṟpiṟanta amutē varuka varukavē. 6.8

cantaviruttam

ulaku kuḷira vematu matiyi loḻuku mamuta kiraṇamē
uruku maṭiya ritaya nekiḻa vuṇarvi leḻuna lutayamē
kaliyu niṟiyu maṟivu mutira mutiru matura naṟavamē
kaḻuvu tukaḷar muḻuka neṭiya karuṇi peruku calatiyē
alakil puvaṉa muṭiyum veḷiyi laḷiyu moḷiyi ṉilayamē
aṟivu ḷaṟivi yaṟiyu mavaru maṟiya variya piramamē
maliyiṉ makaḷkaṇ maṇiyi yaṉiya matali varuka varukavē
vaḷami taḻuvu paruti puriyiṉ maruvu kumaraṉ varukavē. 6.9

iḻume ṉaruvi coriyu mimaya mutalvi putalvaṉ varukavē
iyalu naṭiyum vaṭivu maḻaku meḻuta variyaṉ varukavē
oḻuku karuṇi muḻuku kamala vataṉaṉ varuka varukavē
oruva ṉiruva roṭuki toḻuna lupaya caraṇaṉ varukavē
viḻutu viṭuve ṇilavu poḻiyu nakiyaṉ varuka varukavē
viḷari payilu maḷiyu ñimiṟum viravu kuravaṉ varuka varukavē
maḻali mutira mutiru matura vacaṉaṉ varuka varukavē
vaḷami taḻuvu paruti puriyiṉ maruvu kumaraṉ varukavē. 6.10



7. ampulip paruvam



āciriya viruttam

maṇṭalam pōṟṟuruva mamutamaya māymuḻu matikkaṭavu ḷeṉavarutalāl
vāṉāṟu talimaṭuk kappoṅku māṉanta mākkaṭa liṭittōṉṟalāl
taṇṭalil koṭiccivāyk kumutamviḷ ḷakkarat tāmari mukiḻttiṭutalāṟ
cakalapuva ṉattilu muyirppayir taḻippanaṟ ṟaṇṇiḷi curantiṭutalāl
tuṇṭamati natiyoṭu potintavē ṇipparañ cōtikaṭ poṟiyātalāl
tōṉṟaliva ṉiṉṉiyot tuḷaṉā ṉiṉakkumoru tuṇiyivaṉ pōlillikāṇ
aṇṭaraṇ ṭattoṭaki lāṇṭam paṭittavaṉo ṭampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.1

coṟṟaru perumpulavar kaliyamutu koḷavirun tōkimēl koṇṭaruḷiṉāy
tōṟṟimuṉ poṅkimali pōlavali mōtumac cōtivē liyumukantāy
kuṟṟamil kuṇattik kuṟittavira valarmukam kōṭā taḷittalceytāy
kōkaṉaka nāyakaṉ varakkū viṭuṅkuk kuṭaṅkoṭiya tākavittāy
uṟṟiṭu mitaḻkkumutam viṇṭutaṇ ṭēṉoḻuka oḷinilā nakimukiḻttāy
uṉceyki yempirāṉ ṟaṉceyki pōlumāl uṉiyumiva ṉovvātirāṉ
aṟpoti kaḷattava ṉaḷittakuma rēcaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.2

kaṅkimuṭi yaṭikaṭkor kaṇṇā yiruttiyak kaṇṇiṉuṇ maṇiyivaṉkāṇ
kalikaḷcila niṟitipiṉ kuṟitiyiva ṉeṉṟumoṇ kalimuḻutu niṟiyaniṉṟāṉ
eṅkumira vōṉeṉat tiritiyiva ṉaṭiyavar evarkkumira viṉiyoḻittāṉ
irunilat taṅkurik kumpayir vaḷarttiyivaṉ evvuyirum vāḻacceytāṉ
poṅkamuta mamutā caṉarkkutavi ṉāyivaṉ puttimut tiyumaḷittāṉ
puvaṉam paṭittaviva ṉiṉṉiṉmik kāṉeṉap pukalvatōr poruḷaṉṟukāṇ
aṅkaṇmaṟi yōliṭ ṭaraṟṟaniṉ ṟavaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.3

pāyiruṭ pōtat tiruṭṭaṉṟi yakaviruṭ paṭalaṅ kiḻippatuṇarāy
paṉivicum piṟpoliva toṉṟalāṟ puvaṉap parappelām polivatōrāy
cēyitaḻk kumutan tiṟappatal lātuḷat tirumalar tiṟakkavaṟiyāy
ciṟiviri cakōrappu ḷaṉṟiyev vuyirum tiḷittiṉpa māracceyāy
nīyivaṟ koppaṉmi ceppuvate ṉipparicil niṉperun tavameṉcolkēṉ
neṭiyavaṉ mutaṟṟēvar kuṟukiniṟ pavumuṉi niṉittaḻit taruḷiṉaṉkāṇ
āyira maṟikkumoru poruḷā yiruppavaṉō ṭampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.4

tarumaṉṉu poṉṉulaku maṇṇulaku mokkat talittali mayaṅkattokum
caṉṉiti yaṭintavarkaḷ piyuṇōy muṟṟum tavirntaka makiḻntutavirāk
karumaṉṉu mūḻip perumpiṇiyu māṟṟiṭutal kaṇṭaṉi yiruttiyāṉiṉ
kayarōka muṭaṉmuyaṟ kaṟiyun tuṭittiṭak karutiṭuti yēlempirāṉ
tirumuṉṉa raḷḷiyiṭu veṇcāntu maṟṟit tiruccāntu niṟkavaṟṟāc
cittāmir tattaṭat tīrttat tuṟikkuṟun tivaliyoṉ ṟēyamiyumāl
aruveṉṉa vuruveṉṉa vaṉṟeṉṉa niṉṟavaṉo ṭampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.5

oḻiyāta puvaṉat tuyirkkuyira tāyniṟpa toruteyva muṇṭeṉaveṭut
turiyāluṇarttuvati yoḻiyaveva revarkaṭkum ūṉkaṇ ṇuḷakkaṇṇatām
viḻiyāka muṉṉiṉṟu taṇṇaḷi curantavarkaḷ vēṇṭiya varaṅkoṭuppāṉ
meykaṇṭa teyvamit teyvamal lāṟpuviyil vēṟilli yeṉṟuṇartiyāṟ
poḻiyāta puyaṟaṅku puvaṉamun ticimukap puttēḷ perumpuvaṉamum
poṉṉulaku maṇṇulaku mevvulaku vēṇṭiṉum poruḷaṉ ṟivaṟkumaṟṟa
aḻiyāta vīṭun tarakkaṭava ṉivaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.6

neṭṭuṭaṟ piṅkaṭ karumpēykaḷ cemmayir nirittūṇam vīkkiyārttu
niṟkuṅ kuṟaṭpūta moṉṟiṉi viṭuttuṭaliṉ neṭiyapaḻu veṉpuneriyak
kaṭṭeṉap piṭiyeṉak koṭiṟuṭit taṭiyeṉak kaṇanātar kaṭukamuṭukik
kaṭalvāy tiṟanteṉap pilavāy tiṟantalaṟu kāṭcinī kāṇāyali
maṭṭuṭit tūṟun taṭaṅkamalaṉ mutaliyōr vāyputit tañciniṟpa
varukeṉ ṟaḻittiṭavum vārā tiruttiyāl maṟṟivaṉ muṉintāluṉak
kaṭṭatik kiṉilumoru tikkiliye miyaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.7

kuṉṟit tiṟantiṭṭa kuṭumivēl cūruyir kuṭittiṭa viṭuttuniṉṟāṉ
kuṇṭikik kaḷvaṉik kuṭumit talippacuṅ kurutipoṅ kappuṭittāṉ
iṉṟik kuḻantiyeṉ ṟeṇṇātu kulicaṉmutal eṇmarum piṟarumoruñāṉ
ṟetirniṉ ṟuṭaṟṟiyavar paṭṭapā ṭaṟiyā tiruttiyali yatukiṭakka
muṉṟakkaṉ vēḷvik kaḷaṅkolik kaḷameṉṉa muṭiyamarar mottuṇṭanāḷ
muḻumatik kaṭavuṇī yavamatip puṇṭatu moḻintiṭak kaṭavataṉṟāl
aṉṟik kaṇakkiṉṟum vantiruk kiṉṟatiṉi ampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.8

taḷḷum pavakkaṭa luḻakkumeṉi muttit taṭaṅkari viṭippavaṉuṉit
taliyaḷip pāṉvara vaḻippavum varāviṭiṟ ṟaṇṇaḷi curantukaruṇi
veḷḷaṅ koḻikkuṅ kaṭikkaṇ civappavivaṉ vekuḷātu viṭiṉumaḻutu
viḻicivap pakkāṇi ṉiravipaki cāyttaviḷa vīraṉ poṟuppāṉalaṉ
kaḷḷam paḻuttakaṭ kaṭiciyar ciṟārtirik kāvirit taṇṭuṟitoṟum
katirnit tilaṅkuvi maṇaṟkuṉṟa mēṟiyak kalimatik kalacavamuti
aḷḷun taṭampaṇic cōṇāṭa ṉivaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē.7.9

taṉṉotta teyvac ciṟārumiḷa vīrarum tāṇiḻaṟ kīḻniṟpavic
cakataṇṭa maṇṭala maṭukkaḻiyu nāḷamarar tamiyaḻak kāṇpavaṉivaṉ
niṉṉip poruṭpaṭut toruviraṟ ṟalicuṭṭi nīḷkaḻaṟ ṟāḷutintu
neṭumalark kaṇpicin taḻutaḻu taḻittaṉaṉ niṉakkitil viyappillikāṇ
piṉṉaṟ ṟiriccura natittaṇ ṭuṟittēvar pētik kuḻāṅkaḷeṉṉap
perukun taṭampuṉaṟ kāvirip pūviri peruntaṇ ṭuṟicciṟivirit
taṉṉak kuḻāntiḷit tāṭucō ṇāṭaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē
aḻakupoli kantapuri taḻiyavaru kantaṉuṭaṉ ampulī yāṭavāvē. 7.10

8. ciṟṟiṟ paruvam




kuṟumeṉ ṉaṭiyu neṭuveṇilāk kōṭṭu nakiyum vāṭṭaṭaṅkaṇ
kuḷira mukantuṇ ṭoḷircuṭṭik kuñci tirutti naṟuṅkutali
muṟuku naṟittēṉ kaṉipavaḷa muttuṇ ṭucci mōntukoṇṭuṉ
mukamun tuṭittu viḷiyāṭa muṉṟiṟ puṟattup poṉṟatumpi
iṟukum puḷakak kumpamuli empi rāṭṭi viṭuttatumaṟ
ṟiḷiyār maṟuka maṟukutoṟum iṭukkaṇ ceyaṟkō ventāyniṉ
ciṟukiṇ kiṇicceñ cīṟaṭiyāṟ ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.1

koḻunāṇ malarkkaṟ pakamunaṟik kuravu nāṟu naṟuṅkuñcik
kōmāṉ makaṉē naṅkaḷkulak koḻuntē yeṉṟu kuṟiyirantu
toḻuvā ṉavarta muṭicūṭṭum cōti muṭiyiṟ ṟukaḷeḻaniṉ
tuṇittāḷ vaṇṭaṟ ṟuṟippuḻutit tūḷi paṭiṉum paṭukacuṭark
kaḻuvā maṇiyu nilavuviri katirnit tilamu mumiyammi
kaṇṇi luṟutta vaṭikēṇiṉ kāli luṟuttal kaṭaṉaṉṟāṟ
ceḻunāṉ maṟiyiṉ peruñcelvac cerukkē ciṟṟil citiyēlē
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.2

vaḻikkup puṟampā yāmiḻitta vaṇṭaṇ maṉiyav vacurēcaṉ
vāṉkūṭ ṭuṇpā ṉaṭukkaṭalil vakutta nakaraṉ ṟikaḻāmē
koḻikkuñ ciṟumuṟ ṟililvārik koṭuvan taṭiyē maṉimuṉṟiṟ
kuviyā niṉṟa maṇikkuviyak kuruku peyarkkuṉ ṟamumaṉṟāṟ
kaḻikkuṇ ṭakaḻi vāymaṭuppac cuṭarvāl vaḷitteṇ ṭirikkarattāṟ
curapi ceripāṉ maṭiyaṭitta cōṟṟi ṉōṭuṅ kalantūṭṭic
ceḻikkun taṭaṅkā virināṭā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.3

mūrik kaḷiṟō maḻaviṭiyō muṭukiṟ ṟeṉappārt tuḻiyuṉatu
mukatti ṉaḻakem vaṭikkaṇṇāl moṇṭuṇ ṭaṉamyā meṉavumikap
pārit tōṅkip pūritta pimpoṟ puyattik kaṇṇēṟu
paṭṭē meṉavu maṭikaḷpaki pārāṭ ṭuvatōr paṇpaṉṟāl
vērik koḻuntāṟ ṟiḷampāḷi viripūṅ kamukum pālpāyum
vēḻakkarumpu miruṭpiḻampi viḻuṅkik kakkuñ cuṭarpparutit
tēruk keḻilcey cōṇāṭā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.4

poṉṉaṅ koṭipō ṉuṭaṅkumiṭip puttēṇ makaḷir viḷiyāṭap
puṉimā ḷikiyuñ cūḷikiyum putukkik koṭuttāy potuñāṉam
meynniṉ ṟavaruḷ viḻippāvi viḷiyāṭ ṭayara vaḻiyāta
vīṭuṅ koṭuttā yemmaṉiyum viṭuttuc ceṉṟāṉ mikiyuṇṭō
piṉṉun tirittīm puṉaṟkaṅkip pērāṟ ṟūṟṟu naṟikkōṭṭup
peruṅkaṟ pakattiṉ kaḻuttoṭiyap piṟaḻum vāḷip pakaṭutitta
teṉṉam paḻamvīḻ cōṇāṭā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.5

kāṉak kuṟappeṇ kuṭiyurunta kaṉṉip puṉattut tiṉimāvum
kamaḻtēṉ ṟeḷivu muṇṭucuvi kaṇṭā yeṉṟē matuvallāl
mīṉat taṭaṅka ṇavaṇ miccil micintiṭ ṭatuvu nacimikku
virittīṅ kumutat tamutaṭikaḷ viruntā ṭiyatum viṇṭōmō
kūṉaṟ piṟiyiṉ kōṭuriñcum koṭimā ṭattu veyilvirikkum
kurumā maṇiyāṟ curanatiyiṟ koḻuntā marikaṇ muṟukkuṭintu
tēṉak kalaruñ cōṇāṭā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē.
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.6

piḷḷi maticceñ caṭiccerukum perumā ṉārkku mulakēḻum
peṟṟa tāykku nīyarumip piḷḷi yeṉiṉem pērāya
veḷḷa mamitta ciṟucōṟu vēṇṭi ṉiṭukē malateḷiyēm
viḷiyā ṭiṭattuc ciṟukuṟumpu viḷittāṟ poṟukka vitiyuṇṭō
kaḷḷa viḻiccū raramakaḷir kāmaṉ koṭiyēṟ ṟeṉaviyappak
kaṟpa taruviṟ paṭarntēṟu kāmar koṭecceṅ kayalpāyum
teḷḷu puṉaṟkā virināṭā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē.
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.7

maṭalvā yaviḻnta kuḻaṟpēti orutti tiruttum pakiraṇṭa
maṇaṟciṟ ṟiliyōr kaṇattiṉkaṇ maṭṭit tāṭu mintaṉaruḷ
viṭalāy tamiyēñ ciṟṟiṉmuṟṟum viḷiyāṭ ṭāka vorunīyum
vīṭṭā niṟpat toṭaṅkiṉiyāl vittu muḷiyum vēṟaṉṟē
kaṭamā maruppuñ cuṭarmaṇiyum katirnit tilamu makaḷirmulik
kaḷapat toṭukuṅ kumaccēṟum karittu viṭuttak kaṭaṟkuṭṭam
tiṭarāc ceyuṅkā virināṭā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē.
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.8

vāmāṇ kalippē rakalalkul maṭamā ṇōkki ṉaramakaḷir
makiḻpūt tiruppap puttēṭku vanta viṭukkaṇ māṟṟiṉiyāl
kōmā ṉiṉakkap peruntēvar kulamē yaṉṟi yaṭiyēmum
kuṟṟē valukkā makampaṭimik kuṭiyāk koṇṭāṟ kuṟiyuṇṭō
kāymāṇ kuliccev viḷanīrik kaṭavuṭ cāti maṭanallār
katirppūṇ muliyeṉ ṟēkkaṟappiṅ kamuku nakivāṇ muttarumpum
tēmām poḻiṟṟīm puṉaṉāṭā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē.
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.9

karuvīṟ ṟirunta peruṅkaruṇi kaṭikkaṇ poḻiya vīṟṟirukkum
kaṭavu ṇīyē pakiraṇṭam kaṇṭā yeṉiṉvaṇ ṭaṭikiṭappa
maruvīṟ ṟirunta kuḻaṉmakaḷir vaṇṭaṟ ṟuṟikku maṇaṟciṟṟil
maṉikō luvatu maṟṟaṭikēḷ vakukkun toḻiṟku māṟaṉṟē
kuruvīṟ ṟirunta maṇimāṭak koṭimā nakaran toṟumalarnta
koḻuntā marippūṅ kōyiliṟpal kōṭi yuruvaṅ koṇṭuceḻun
tiruvīṟ ṟirunta cōṇāṭā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē.
cenneṟ paḻaṉap puḷḷūrā ciṟiyēñ ciṟṟil citiyēlē. 8.10



9. ciṟupaṟip paruvam




ūṟṟum pacuntē ṉuvaṭṭaneṭ ṭitaḻviriyum oṇkāntaṇ mucciyucci
oruvaṉī mummutaṟ kaṭavuḷu miḷippāṟa ulakelān taliyaḷittup
pōṟṟun tiṟattiṉap paḻamaṟik kiḻavaṉ purintapaki raṇṭaṅkaṭām
putukkuva kaṭuppaneṭu veḷimukaṭ ṭukkuviri putunilāk kaṟṟiyiṭṭut
tūṟṟum peyarkkaru mukiṟpaṭāt tiyumveṇ ṭukiṟpaṭā mākkivīkkit
toṭukaṭaṟ puvaṉap peruntaṭṭo ṭaṇṭac cuvarttalat tukkumveḷḷi
tīṟṟuñ cutittavaḷa māṭamali vēḷūra ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē
teṉkalik kumpaḻiya vaṭakalik kuntaliva ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē. 9.1

viḷikkum perumpuvaṉa mokkak karittakaṭi veḷḷañ curuṅkavīṅki
vētaṇṭa meṭṭiṉoṭu mūtaṇṭa kūṭattum viliyāṭi yulakamēḻum
vaḷikkuṅ karuṅkaṭal perumpuṟak kaṭalōṭum vāymaṭut tetireṭuppa
varupuṉaṟ kāvēri vaḷanāṭa nāṭoṟu matikkaṭavu ḷēṟiyēṟi
iḷikkum puḷikkaṟi muyaṟkaṟi yaṟakkālum iḷanilā veḷḷamūḻki
eṟitirip pākī ratippuṉal kuṭintiṭum iṭikkoṭi nakiṟkoṭiyeṉat
tiḷikkun tukiṟkoṭi mukiṟkoṭicey vēḷūra ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē
teṉkalik kumpaḻiya vaṭakalik kuntaliva ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē. 9.2

iruḷun taraṅkak karuṅkaṭaṉ mukaṭṭeḻum iḷampiṟi muyaṟkuḻantik
kēṟaviṭu mōṭameṉa vāṉmīṉ ṟaṭantiri eṭutteṟiyu neṭumīṉeṉat
taraḷam patintiṭṭa maṇimuṟuva lavarōṭu taruniḻaṟ celvaruykkum
tamaṉiya vimāṉamum veyiṟkatirp pōrviyāṉ taṉiyāḻi ticiyuruṭṭa
uruḷuṅ koṭittērum vīṟṟuvīṟ ṟeḻilpuṉin tōṭṭupoṟ ṟeppameṉṉa
ulakēḻu maliyeṭṭu moḻukukatir viḻutuviṭum oṇṇilāp puṇarikōppat
tiraḷum paḷikkumā ṭaṅkaḷpoli vēḷura ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē
teṉkalik kumpaḻiya vaṭakalik kuntaliva ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē. 9.3

moympiṟ perumpuvaṉa mokkac cumakkiṉṟa mōṭṭāmi mutukuḷukka
muṭavup paṭaṅkiḻin taravaraci ṉāyira muṭittaliyu mūḷipoṅkak
kampak kaṭāyāṉi yeṭṭum piṭarttali kaḻuttoṭu murintukaviḻak
katirmaṇic cūṭṭuneṭ ṭaraveṭṭum vaṭavik kaṭuṅkaṉaṟ kaṇpituṅka
ampoṟ ṟaṭampurici yeḻuperun taṭṭuruvi aṇṭakū ṭattaḷavalāl
avaravar vaḻaṅkutaṟ kiṭutalik kaṭiyeṉa aṭukkēḻu niliyēḻumām
cempoṟ ṟirukkō puraṅkaḷpoli vēḷūra ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē
teṉkalik kumpaḻiya vaṭakalik kuntaliva ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē. 9.4

tarikkuñ cuṭarpparut muḻumatik kaṭavuḷoṭu taṭamatil kaṭantakanakar
cāravari tāṟpuvaṉa kōṭikaṭ koḷiceyak catumukaṉ kaṟpantoṟum
virikkiṉṟa virucuṭaru moruvaḻit tokkeṉa viyaṉkatirp paṭalamūṭi
vīṅkiruḷ viḻuṅkucem maṇimāṭa niriyumoḷi viḷipacuṅ katirveṇpuri
purikkuñ ceḻuntaraḷa māṭamum veyiliṉoṭu poḻinilāp pōrppamuṟṟum
pōtuteri yāmiyiṟ kumutamoṭu catavitaḻp pōtumē yirupōtiyum
terikkun taṭampaṇi yuṭuttatamiḻ vēḷūra ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē
teṉkalik kumpaḻiya vaṭakalik kuntaliva ciṟupaṟi muḻakkiyaruḷē. 9.5

vēṟu

maḻimukil piḷiṟu muḻakkeṉa viriciṟi maṭamayi liṉamakava
mālkaṭa lōliṭu moliyeṉa viraka maṭantiyar maṉanekiḻap
paḻamaṟi yārppeṉa vākuti vēṭṭeḻu paṇṇava ruṇmakiḻap
paranā tattoli yeṉavaṉu pūti palittavar nekkuruka
aḻalavir cōtiye miya ṉaṭañceya āyira maṅkiyiṉōr
aṇṇa ṟuvittiṭu kuṭamuḻa voṭucuṭar āḻi yavaṉkoṭṭu
muḻaveṉa vamararu muṉivaru mārppa muḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē
muttamiḻ payilparu tippati murukaṉ muḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē. 9.6

peruveḷi mukaṭu tiṟantiṭ ṭaṇṭap pittiki veṭiyāmē
pēraṇ ṭattuḷa vētaṇ ṭaṅkaḷ pitirntuti rākāmē
kurumaṇi citaṟiya veṉṉa vuṭuttiraḷ koṭṭuṇ ṭutirāmē
kuvaṭu paṭuntici ceviṭu paṭaccilar kuṭarkaḷ kuḻampāmē
tiruvira loṭunaka kaṇkaḷi ṉuñceṅ kuruti tatumpāmē
cēyoḷi niṉṟu tuḷumpiṭa niṉciṟu ceṅki varuntāmē
murukalar tārava ṉorumuṟi mella muḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē
muttamiḻ payilparu tippati murukaṉ muḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē. 9.7

vammi ṉeṉappula vōri yaḻittiṭu vaṇkoṭi muracameṉa
vaṭakali teṉkali yoṭupayi luṅkavi vāṇarka ḷōṭivara
ammeṉ maṭappiṭi poṉṉula kīṉṟava ṇaṅki maṇampuṇarum
aṇikiḷar maṇamura ceṉṉave miyaṉo ṭammi maṉaṅkuḷirat
temmuṉi cāyac camarviḷi yāṭic ceṅkaḷa vēḷviceyum
tiṟaṉmura ceṉavimi yavarviḻa vayarac ceḻunakar vītitoṟu
mummura camumatir kāviri nāṭaṉ muḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē
muttamiḻ payilparu tippati murukaṉ muḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē. 9.8

canta viruttam

perukucuvitteḷi naṟavoḻukakkaṉi kaṉiyamutē
piṭinṭikaṟṟiṭa vaṭikaḷpeyarttiṭu maṭavaṉamē
karuvarinekkuṭa ṉurukamiḻaṟṟumor kiḷiyaracē
karuṇiceyattaku maḷiyaṉiṭatteṉu moḻipukalā
aruḷilpuṉattavar makaḷirupoṟpatam varuṭalceyā
avaṇmuṉeṭuttuniṉ muṭiyiṉmuṭittiṭu karamalarāṟ
paruvayirappaya cayilaṉmuḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē
parutipuricciṟu kumaraṉmuḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē. 9.9

iḻumeṉmoḻitteḷi tamiḻiṉvaṭittiṭu navaracamē
itayaviruṭṭaṟa vuṇarvilutittiṭu cuṭaroḷiyē
kaḻuvumaṇikkala ṉaṭuviliḻittiṭu kulamaṇiyē
kaṉitarumikkaṉi yoṭuvaṭikaṭṭiya cuviyamutē
oḻukunaṟicceḻu malarviriyakkamaḻ putumaṇamē
urukumuḷattaruḷ perukiyavaṭṭeḻu calanitiyē
paḻamaṟikaṭkoru mutalvaṉmuḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē
parutipuricciṟu kumaraṉmuḻakkuka ciṟupaṟiyē. 9.10



10. ciṟutērp paruvam




pōrōṭu paṭituṟan tuṭaṟiṟan toṭucem puṉalōṭa vōṭinimirum
puṇarip peruntāṉi yavuṇap paṭittalivar pūtap paṭittalivarmuṉ
tārō ṭaviḻntiṭṭa kuñcik kimittiṭṭa taḻalviḻik ketircelāta
tāḷukku vāḷcōr taṭakkikku nāmanum tālikku vēlikoleṉāp
pīrōṭu koṅkikka ṇīrōṭu veḷḷaruvi perukak kaṭikkaṇiṟkum
peyvaḷi yavarkkōta vavarviḻika ṭoṟumiḷam pētiyarkaḷ kaṇṭoṟumeṉat
tērōṭu moruperuñ ciliyōṭu niṉṟavaṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.1

kampak kaḷiṟṟuk kaṭaṟṟāṉi vīrarkaṭ kaṭiyiṟ kaṭikkaṉaleḻak
kaṇṭoṭ ṭuṇuñcila maruṭpēy karinteḻu kaṭuṅkuruti veḷḷamūḻkit
tumpit taliccem mayircciki yiṉiccuṭu kaṉaṟciki yeṉappatippac
cūṭṭiṟic cikkuc ciṇuṅkuṅ kuṟaṭpēykkor cūrppēy koḻuntacikaḷkōt
tampiṟ cuṭattāṉ kavantamoṭu tontamiṭ ṭāṭum paṟantalinilat
tāṉip piṇakkuṉṟu mavuṇap piṇakkāṭum aḷaṟupaṭ ṭoḻiyaniṉṟōr
cempoṟ ṟaṭantē ruruṭṭivaru cēvakaṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.2

vaṇṭēṟu cenniṟap paṅkit talikkamala vaṉamūṭu kurutiyāṟṟu
mālyāṉi kiyeṭut tārttunīn tappuṇari makaramī ṉeṉaniṉintu
koṇṭēku ciṟukuṭarp peruvali yeṭutteṟi kuṟaṭpēy neṭuñciṉamuṟak
kuṟunari piṭittīrppa valaṟuvatum vīrartoṭu kolinēmi yavuṇaruyiri
uṇṭēku vatumoruvaṉ viṭavō liṭuṅkarik kutavavaru tikiriyēykkum
olleṉ paṟantali maṟantalik koṇṭacūr uyttiṭṭa vintrañālat
tiṇṭēri yuruḷātu niṟkap paṇittavaṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.3

piṅkaṭ ciṟikkāl kaṭikkāl ceyakkirikaḷ viriciṟi paṭitteḻuvapōṟ
paṭaruntaṉ vēkatti ṉokkap paṟakkap paṟantalic centaliyaṟum
veṅkaṭ ṭayittiya ruṭaṟkuṟi talikkuṟi virintuyi riṉittoṭarntu
mīccellu māccel leṉaccelap pūmāri viṇṭūrppa teṉavuṭuvukap
poṅkaṟ kaṭaṟkuṭṭa maṭṭatik kuntamiṟ pōrceyap pārkaviḻavem
poṟiyuṭaṟ cēṭaṉ paṭantūkki yārkkum pukippaṭala vaṭavāmukac
ceṅkaṭ pacuntōki vāmpari yukittavaṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.4

neyvitta kūntaṟ piṭikkutava nāṟkōṭṭu nikaḷat taṭaṅkuṉṟuvāṉ
nimiruṅ katirkkulic cennelip pākupaṭu neṭṭilik kaṉṉalkoleṉāk
kivit tiṭappari mukañceytu veyyōṉ kaṭumpariyi naṭpukkoḷum
kaḻaṉiviri kāvirit tirunāṭa kaṟpakak kāṭṭiṟ piṟantupiriyā
meyvitta kātaṉmi yaramakaḷir pērāya veḷḷan tiḷittāṭiyōr
meṉṉaṭik kēkkaṟṟa piṭipiṉ piṭikkamuli vēḻaṅka ḷuṭaṉulāvum
teyvap piṭikkuk kiṭittatoru maḻakaḷiṟu ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.5

paṟṟuviṟ kāmaṉ koṭippaṭik kūrelām paṭivīṭu kayalkaṭāvum
paḻaṉaṅka ḷōvatra cālipūñ cōlip parappelāṅ kāṟṟēroṭum
koṟṟavaṅ kaṅkuṟ kaṭāyāṉi yuṅkaṭṭu kūṭamē yeṉiṉumarutak
kōmakaṉ kuṭikoṇṭa cōṇāṭa cēṇāṭu kuṅkumaṅ koṭṭutiṇṭōṭ
poṟṟaṭaṅ kuṉṟiṉiru koṅkip poruppumoru pūṅkuḻaṟ kāṭumveyilkāl
puṉimaṇik kaliyalkuṉ mākkaṭalu mēntiyor puṉattiṉkaṇ mikanuṭaṅkum
ciṟṟiṭik kolkumeṉ koṭipaṭara niṉṟavaṉ ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.6

kōlpāy pacumpuṇ ṇacumpukum pattaṭaṅ kuṉṟukavuṇ maṭitiṟantu
koṭṭuneṭ ṭaruviyoṭu tēṉaruvi yuntiri koḻittuṭaṉ kōppamētip
pālpāy perukkā ṟuvaṭṭeḻuta ṟaṉṟuṇip pāviya roṭuṅkuṟukumap
pākī ratikkunikar taṇṭuṟi toṟunticip pālarmēṟ paṭiyeṭuttu
vēlpāy neṭuṅkaṭ kaṭaṟṟāṉi yoṭumoruvaṉ mēṟcella nāṟṟiciyilum
veṟṟik kayaṟkoṭi yeṭutteṉa veṭutteṟiyum veṇṭiri kuḻittuveṭipōm
cēlpāy taṭampaṇi yuṭuttakā virināṭa ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.7

mīttanta mākattu mēkatti ṉōṭumuṭu mīṉiyariyal pōkavukaḷum
veṭivāḷi matiyaka ṭuṭittūṟṟu teḷḷamuta veḷḷaruvi yāṟpacuntaṇ
kāttanta caṇpakap pūvēri māricey kāvēri yāyiramukak
kaṅkiyā kacceytu mīṭṭunāṟ kōṭṭuveṅ kaḷiṟupiḷi ṟattāvivāṉ
pūttanta kaṟpakak kāṭṭiṉi yuḻakkiviri poṟṟā teḻuppimaṟṟap
pūntukaṭ paṭalatti ṉāṟṟeyva natiyiyum poṉṉinati yākacceyyum
tēttanta taṇpaṇiyu ṭuttatīm puṉaṉāṭa ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.8

kaḷḷaviḻ naṟuṅkoṭikaḷ kamukiṟ paṭarntupūṅ kaṟpakat tumpaṭartalāṟ
kāmaṉ peruñcelva maṉṉavarkaḷ kuḻumiyak kamukiṉ kaḻuttilyātta
oḷḷoḷiya cemmaṇip poṉṉūcal paṉmuṟi utintāṭa vāṭuntoṟum
oṇkamu koṭuntuṇarp piṅkaṟ pakakkāṭum okkavaci yattaliyacit
taḷḷili yalaṅkalvē lempirā ṉippāṭi āṭukiṉ ṟāreṉattām
alarmāri poḻivapō laṅkaṟ pakatteyvam ampoṉmalar māritūrkkum
teḷḷutamiḻ viripuṉaṟ kāvirit tirunāṭa ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.9

vārāṭṭu taṭamulip pāloḻuki vaḻivateṉa maṇimuṟuva ṉilavukālum
maḻalivāy muttuṇṭu muccimōn tuntiru maṭittalat tiṉiliruttip
pārāṭṭi yuntamatu kaṇmaṇip pāviniṉ paṭivamā kakkāṭṭiyip
pālaroṭum viḷiyā ṭeṉappaṇit tuntaṅkaḷ pārvikaḷi yāṭacceyum
tārāṭṭu matimuṭit tīrāta viṉitīrtta tampirā ṉuntampirāṉ
taḻaluruvi lorupāti kuḷiravoru puṟaniṉṟa tiyaṉā yakiyumvittuc
cīrāṭṭi viḷiyāṭu cēṉā patikkaṭavuḷ ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē
tiruvaḷara vaḷarkanta purivaḷa riḷaṅkumara ciṟutē ruruṭṭiyaruḷē. 10.10

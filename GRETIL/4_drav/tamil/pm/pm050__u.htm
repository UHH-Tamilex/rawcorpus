maturāpuri ampiki māli

kulacēkara pāṇṭiyaṉ



vināyakar kāppu

kaṭṭaḷik kalittuṟi



naṭakkum tiru viḷiyāṭṭu ōr aṟupattu nālum cokkar

aṭakkum teṉ kūṭalil ampiki mālikku aruvi matat,

taṭak kumpa, kampac, ciṟu kaṇ, pukar mukattu, āla vaṭṭam

muṭakkum taṭakki, oru kōṭṭu vāraṇam muṉ niṟkavē.



nūl



tiruvē! viḷinta centēṉē! vaṭi iṭṭa teḷ amutiṉ

uruvē! maṭap piḷḷi ōtimamē! oṟṟi āṭakap pūn

taruvē! niṉ tāmarit tāḷē caraṇam, caraṇam kaṇṭāy,

aruvē! aṇaṅku aracē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 1.



nāḷ koṇṭa koṅkit tuṇiyum, poṉ mēṉiyum, nañcu aḷitta

vāḷ koṇṭa nāṭṭamum, toṇṭic cev vāyum, maruṅku uṭuttut

tōḷ koṇṭa cem paṭṭum, muttu āramum koṇṭu tōṉṟi eṉi

āḷ koṇṭa nāyakiyē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 2.



karumpum, kaṇi aintum, pāca aṅkucamum, kik koṇṭu aṭiyēṉ

tirumpum tici toṟum tōṟṟu kaṇṭāy - ici tēkku maṇic

curumpu uṇṭa kāviyum, cōti nilāvum, tuḷirum caṟṟē

arumpum kaṉam kuḻalāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 3.



kuṉṟē eṉum muliyār tarum kātal koṭumi ellām

veṉṟēṉ, maṟaliyi viṭṭu viṭṭēṉ, virit tāmarit tāḷ

eṉṟē eṉ ceṉṉi vittāy, piṉṉi yāṉ ceyyum ēval ellām

aṉṟē uṉ ēval kaṇṭāy, maturāpuri ampikiyē! 4.



vaṭi vitta vēl viḻiyār anurāka mayakkil ceṉṟu

kuṭi vitta neñcu eṉṟu mīḷum kolō! aṉpu koṇṭa toṇṭar

muṭi vittavāṟu, eṉ pulit tali mēl vitta muttiṉ taṇṭi

aṭi vitta pēr iṉpamē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 5.



vaṭak kuṉṟa mēruvum, mūtu aṇṭam eṭṭi vaḷintu puṟam

kiṭakkum kaṭalum, puvaṉaṅkaḷ ēḻum, kiḷar marupput

taṭak kuñcaram eṭṭum, ellām tiru untit tāmariyil

aṭakkum parācaktiyē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 6.



oḷi koṇṭa veṇ piṟit tōṭum, poṉ ōliyum, ūṟiya tēṉ

tuḷi koṇṭa ceṅkaṉi vāyum, muttāramum, tōḷum eṉṟaṉ

kaḷi koṇṭa neñcam kuṭi koṇṭavā ici kakku maṇi

aḷi koṇṭa pūṅkuḻalāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 7.



maṇiyum, taraḷamum, veṇ nakiyō? vaḻi mūvar ceyat

tuṇiyum, toḻilum uṉ cey toḻilō? pattit tutti muṭip

paṇiyum, cuṭarum, kaṭal ēḻum niṉ kaḻal paṅkayattil

aṇiyum tiru uruvē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 8.



ceḻum tuṅkak koṅkiyum, muttu āramum, poṉ cilampum, tiṅkaḷ

koḻuntum, makarak kuḻiyum ellām, vaṇṭu koṇṭu cuṟṟi

uḻum tumpi cūṭum tiru mēṉiyum, uṉ uṭalum oṉṟāy

aḻuntum parācaktiyē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 9.


kaṇiyum, kumiḻum, iṇi neṭum cāpamum, kārum, vaḷḷit

tuṇiyum, pavaḷamum, cōti nilāvum, tuvaṇṭa paccip

paṇiyum, paṉit taṭam kāntaḷum, pāntaḷum pattum oṉṟāy

aṇiyum tiru uruvē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 10.



iṇaṅkēṉ oruvari; niṉ iru tāḷ aṉṟi eppoḻutum

vaṇaṅkēṉ oruvari vāḻttukilēṉ; vañca neñcar uṭaṉ

piṇaṅkēṉ - amutam perukum cem pātip piṟi muṭitta

aṇaṅkē! caraṇam kaṇṭāy, maturāpuri ampikiyē! 11.



mulikkē avacat tuyar viḷippār iṉpa mōkam eṉṉum

valikkē akappaṭṭu aḻunti viṭāmal, valiya vantu eṉ

talikkē patam vitta taṇ aḷiyāy! muttam tattu tiri

alikkē viḻi tuyilvāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 12.



koṭik koṇṭa ciṟu iṭiyār maṇi nūpuram koñcum aṭip

poṭik koṇṭa ceṉṉi uṉ kāl vikkumō? puṟṟu araveṭuttu

muṭik koṇṭa cokkar aḻiyā viratam muṭikka eṉṟē,

aṭik koṇṭa pūṇ muliyāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 13.



viḷiyum karuk kuḻi vīḻāmal, eṉ taṉ viṉip piṟavi

kaḷiyum paṭikkum karutu kaṇṭāy - kaḻunīri veṉṟu

vaḷiyum taraḷa maṇit tōṭu aḻuttiya vaḷḷiyic ceṉṟu

aḷiyum kayal viḻiyāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 14.



ūṭum poḻutu uṉ karuṇi viṭāmal uvappataṟkuk

kūṭum toḻili maṟappatu uṇṭō? naṟik koṉṟi am tār

cūṭum talivar tiru mārpil, vāri cori taraḷam

āṭum tuṇi muliyāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 15.



maḻik kontu aḷaka maṭavār tam kātal valit talippaṭṭu

uḻikkum tuyaram oḻippatu eṉṟō? iraṇṭu ūcal maṇik

kuḻikkum kalanta pacumi elām kuḻi ūṭu naṭantu

aḻikkum kayal viḻiyāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 16.



iṇik kumpa meṉ muliyār viḻi vēlukkum, eyyu mataṉ

kaṇikkum taṉi ilikkā viṭavō? maṇik kaccu akalāt

tuṇik kumpa meṉ muliyāy! cokkar mēṉi tuvaḷak kaṭṭi

aṇikkum kamalam uḷḷāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 17.



num kēḷvar pākattum, an nāṉ maṟi eṉum nūl iṭattum,

koṅku ēy pokuṭṭuk kamala ālayattum, kuṭi koṇṭa nī

eṅkē irukkiṉum nāy aṭiyēṉukku iṭar varum pōtu

aṅkē veḷippaṭuvāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 18.



poṉṉē! nava maṇiyē! amutē! puvi pūttu aṭaṅkā

miṉṉē! oḷi uṟṟa mey poruḷē! karu mētiyiṉ mēl

en nēram kālaṉ vantu āvi viṭṭālum, eṉakku añcēl eṉṟu

an nēram vantu aruḷvāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 19.



viruntu uṇṭu pōkikku nāyēṉ caṭalatti vem kaḻukum,

paruntum cuḻalu muṉṉē varuvāy - maṇap pantalilē

tiruntum karam paṟṟi niṟpār tiru mukac cevvi ellām

aruntum kayal viḻiyāy! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 20.



nāraṇaṉ koñcum pukaḻ nāṉmukaṟkum, poṉ nāṭṭavarkkum

kāraṇam koñcum niṉ ciṟu aṭikkē tici kaṭṭum eṭṭu

vāraṇam koñcum kaṭampu aṭaviyiṉ makiḻnaruṭaṉ

āraṇam koñcum ammē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 21.



neḷikkum puḻuvukku iṭam ām kurampiyiṉ niṉṟum, uyir

oḷikkum poḻutu veḷippaṭuvāy - oḻiyāp piṟavic

cuḷikkum kaṭalil cuḻalāmal vāḻvit tuṟantu, paṭittu,

aḷikkum parācaktiyē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 22.



oḻiyāp paṉit taṭam kaṇ nīr coriya vantu, uṉ aṭikkē

poḻiyāp putu malar iṭṭu niṟpārkku, uṉ poḻi karuṇi

viḻiyāl curappa, alarnta cem tāmari vīṭu oṉṟavē

aḻiyāp patam taruvāy, maturāpuri ampikiyē! 23.



ēlam aṭaṅkum kuḻalār kuṟu ēvalil eyttu, aṭiyēṉ

kālam aṭaṅkum muṉṉē varuvāy - viṇ kaṭantu niṉṟa

kōlam aṭaṅkum aṟiyāta paccik kuḻaviyip pōl

ālam aṭaṅkum ammē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 24.



pōrkkum kalā matiyum, koṉṟi māliyum, poṉ muṭi mēl

cērkkum talivar muṉ cellum ap pōtu, tiraṇṭa muttiṉ

vārk kuṅkumak koṅki yāṉikku muṉṉam maṇi muracam

ārkkum parācaktiyē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 25.



nōkkum karuṇi viḻiyāl potu aṟa nōkki, eṉṉik

kākkum paṭikkum karutu kaṇṭāy - oḷi kakku nilā

vīkkum caṭi aṭaviyār uṇṭa kāḷa viṭatti amutu

ākkum civa āṉantamē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 26.



uli kutikkum taḻal ūṟiya kāṉali uṉṉic ceṉṟu,

kali kutikkumpaṭi pōl iḻittēṉ - kaḻic cēl vekuṇṭu

vali kutikkum cem kaḻunīr uṭintu vaḻinta cem tēṉ

ali kutikkum taṭam cūḻ maturāpuri ampikiyē! 27.



poṭi paṭṭa piṭṭukkut tōḷ koṇṭu kūṭi maṇ pōṭṭup, poṅki
veṭi paṭṭa viyi aṭitta an nāḷ, cokkar mēṉi cuṟṟum

koṭi paṭṭa pōtu muḻu nīlak kōmaḷa mēṉiyilum

aṭi paṭṭatō? aṇaṅkē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 28.



pūṇṭa ki vārip, putu malar tūvi, niṉ poṉ aṭikkē

kūṇṭa ki, ceṉṉi kuvikkap peṟṟēṉ - piṟik kōṭu aṇintu,

nīṇṭa ki vēḻap piṭar ēṟi, vaṭṭa nilam purakkum

āṇ takiyē! aṇaṅkē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 29.



koñcum kutali miṉṉārkku aṉpu pūṇṭu kuliyum eṉ taṉ

neñcu, uṉ poṉ pātam niṉippatu uṇṭō? veṇ nilā matik kūṉ

piñcum, kaṇi villum, aṅkuca pācamum, piñcu malar

añcum tarittavaḷē! maturāpuri ampikiyē! 30.



nūṟpayaṉ



piṭittārik kaṭṭi aṇittu, amutu ūṭṭiya pēyiṉ muli

kuṭittu āṭum māyaṉ, kulacēkaraṉ, vaṭa kuṉṟic ceṇṭāl

aṭittāṉ vaṭitta col ampiki māli, ai āṟu kavi

paṭittārkaḷ kaṟpakak kāvum, poṉ nāṭum paṭippavarē.

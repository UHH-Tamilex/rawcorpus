2.9 tacik kaṭṭiyip peṇṇuruvamitta paṭalam (699 - 733)



699
marumalar cumantu tēṉvaḻin toḻuku
maṇippuya mukammatu napiyun
teritarun tīṉi ṉeṟimuṟi yavaruñ
cintiyiṟ kaḷippoṭuñ ciṟappa
variyameyp poruḷi muṟimuṟi vaṇaṅki
yaṟṟiyiṟ kaṭaṉkaḻit tamarar
tiruvaṭi paravat tammuyi raṉiya
celvarō ṭuṟintiṭuṅ kāli. 2.9.1
700
pūraṇak kaḷapa kaṉataṉa maṭavār
porutirik kavarikā lacippa
vāraṇi muraca matirtarac cīṟu
maṭaṅkaliṉ koṭimuṉaṅ kulava
vāraṇat talivar maruṅkiṉiṟ piriyā
taracarka ḷuṭaṉvarat toliyāk
kāraṇak kuriciṉ mukammati ṉiṭattil
vantaṉaṉ hapīpeṉu maracaṉ. 2.9.2
701
cemmalarp patattil veṇkatir kulavuñ
ceḻumaṇi muṭicirañ cērttit
tammiṉat tavarka ḷūṭaṉcalā murittut
takkatō riṭattuniṉ ṟavaṉi
vammeṉat tiruvā yuriyaruḷ koṭuttu
mukammatu maruṅkiṉi lirutti
vemmiyi ṉamutak kaṉiyeṉuṅ kalimā
viḷampuka veṉavirit turittār. 2.9.3
702
naṉṟeṉap pukaḻntu maṉaṅkaḷit teḻuntu
narapati timaṣkiṉuk karacaṉ
veṉṟiko ḷaracē yiṉamoru vacaṉam
viṉavutal vēṇṭumeṉ ṉiṭatti
leṉṟava ṉurippa mukammatu napiyu
miṉpuṟu muṟuvalkoṇ ṭiṉitāyt
tuṉṟumeṉ maṉattiṟ ṟerintuṉ makaṭaṉ
ṟolviṉi teḷippataṟ keṉṟār. 2.9.4
703
āṇṭaki yuritta putumoḻi naṟuntē
ṉakattiṉiṟ pukuntuṭal kaḷittu
vēṇṭunaṟ patavi paṭittaṉaṉ ciṟiyēṉ
viḷittiṭum pavakkaṭa ṟolittēṉ
kāṇṭakāp putumi yaṉittiyun terintēṉ
kaṭikama ḻaṇimalarp patattit
tīṇṭavum peṟṟē ṉiṉiyarum poruḷoṉ
ṟiliyeṉa vurittaṉaṉ ṟiṟalōṉ. 2.9.5
704
putiyavaṉ ṟūtar mukammatun timaṣkip
purantiṭu maruntavat tavaṉu
maturameṉ moḻiyā laḷavaḷā yuḷaṅkaṇ
makiḻntiṉi tirukkumak kāli
kakaṉiḻin tariya peruñciṟi yoṭukkik
kaṭitiṉiṟ kaṇṇimit tiṭumuṉ
cekatalat tuṟinta napiyiṭat tuvantār
terimaṟi koṭujipu rīlē. 2.9.6
705
maruṅkiṉi levarkkun tōṉṟiṭā tuṟintu
vallavaṉ calāmeṭut tiyampip
peruṅkulam viḷakku mukammati nōkkip
piṟaḻnturut tōṉṟilāt taciyi
neruṅkuveṇ koṭikkaḵ pāviṭat tēki
nirimayirp pōrviyāṉ mūṭi
yaruṅkatirkkala cat tāpusam satti
ṉariyanīr karaṅkoṭu teḷittē. 2.9.7
706
iṟivaṉi nōkkit tuāviran tiṉirē
lilaṅkurut tōṉṟumeṉ ṟicittuc
ciṟiniṟan tōṉṟā tamarula kataṉil
jipuṟayī lēkiya piṉṉar
kaṟiniṟaṅ kulavuñ ceḻuṅkatir vaṭivēṟ
karatala mukammatu nayiṉā
raṟimura catirat timaṣkiṟi yavaṉu
meḻuntaṉa rariyakaḵ pāvil. 2.9.8
707
vāṉava riṟiyō ṉaruṭpaṭi yamitta
makkamā nakariyi ṉāppaṇ
kāṉalar potuḷuṅ kakupattul lāviṟ
kaṭimatiṭ puṟattoru pālil
tīṉava ruṭaṉu maṇipeṟa viruntu
cevviyaṉ hapīpiṉi nōkki
yīṉamaṟ ṟuṉatu makaveṉun taciyi
yivaṇkoṭu varukaveṉ ṟicittār. 2.9.9
708
antanaṉ moḻikēṭ ṭaṭaṟpaṭi mālik
karuḷiya hapīpeṉu maracaṉ
cintiyiṟ kaḷittu maruṅkuniṉ ṟavarit
tiṇṇiya taciyiṉik koṇarkeṉ
ṟuntiṭa vurippa vōṭiṉar cilava
ruṟuporuṭ potinteṉap potintu
tantapeṭ ṭakatti ṉoṭumeṭut tuvantār
taṉuviṭu carattiṉuṅ kaṭitiṉ. 2.9.10
709
muṉṉuṟap paṇitta peṭṭakat tirunta
mutirtacik kaṭṭiyi yeṭuttu
maṉṉiya kuriciṉ mukammatu napimuṉ
vittaṉar curikuḻaṟ karuṅkaṇ
miṉṉeṉap piṟaḻu maṭantiya ruṭaṉum
viritirip pacuṅkaṭa laṉiya
vannakar mākka ḷaṉivarum virivi
ṉaṭuttati cayittiṭa vaṉṟē. 2.9.11
710
kaṭalkiṭan tuṭutta perumpuvi yiṭattiṟ
kaṇṭaṟi yāpperuṅ kāṭci
maṭalaviḻ kamala vāvicūḻ timaṣku
maṉṉavaṉ makaveṉap piṟantu
miṭaluṟuṅ katirvāṇ mukammatu poruṭṭāl
viḷikkuṅkā raṇattiṉuk kirunta
vuṭaleṉun tacita ṉuyiralā tiyintō
ruṟuppeṉum vaṭivupeṟ ṟilavē. 2.9.12
711
poruntuṟuṅ kuṟippoṉ ṟaṇuviṉu milatāyp
poruvaṟum putumiyi ṉetiri
ṉiruntavat taciyi mukammatu nōkki
yiṟiva ṉaruḷeṉak kuṟittut
tiruntuṟa mayiriṉ pōrviyiṟ pōrttuc
ceṅkarat tariyanī rēnti
maruntiṉu mamara ramutiṉuñ ciṟappa
makiḻvoṭun teḷittuniṉ ṟaṉarāl. 2.9.13
712
maṇiyeṉac ciṟantu malariṉmeṉ miyavā
maḻicceḻuṅ karaṅkaḷi yēnti
yaṇitarap pōṟṟik kaṉintaṟa nekiḻnta
vakattiṉi lariyanā yakaṉit
taṇivilā tuyarttip palapala pukaḻāṟ
cāṟṟinaṉ ṉeṟimuṟi tavaṟāp
paṇipaṇin tirantār tīṉili niṟuttum
patumameṉ patamukam matuvē. 2.9.14
713
pūtalat tevarkku maṟineṟi putukkip
poruvaruñ cuvaṉanā ṭaḷippat
tūteṉa vutitta mukammatiṉ tuāvit
tuyyava ṉuṟakkapū lākkap
pātakak kupirar maṉampati patippap
palaṉpaṭāp peruntacit tiraṭci
tītaṟat tōṉṟu mavayavañ ciṟappat
teriviyiṉ ṟiruvuru veṭutta. 2.9.15
714
variyiḻi mayirppō ruviyeṉuṅ kariya
valliru ḷiṭiyeḻu matipōl
virikaṭaṟ perunī ruṇṭucū luḷinta
vicumpiṭi yutittamiṉ kulampō
lariyiṉa naṟavuṇ ṭalampukuṅ kumattā
raṇipuya mukammatiṉ kalimāt
teritarap pavaḷa vitaḻtiṟan tōtic
ceṟitaru maviyiṭat teḻuntāḷ. 2.9.16
715
viṇṇakat tarampik kulattiṉum vaṭivāy
virikaṭaṉ makaḷiṉum viyappāy
maṇṇakat tuṟiyu meḻuvakip paruva
maṭantiya raṇintiṭu maṇiyāyk
kaṇṇiṉuk kaṭaṅkā taḻakiṉic cumanta
kaṉiyuru veṭuttakāṭ ciyatāyp
peṇṇalaṅ kaṉintu nalaṉeḻil piṟaṅkap
perunilat teḻuntuniṉ ṟaṉaḷē. 2.9.17
716
maṅkuliṟ peruki viṭattiṉuṅ karuki
variyaṟa liṉumiṉu miṉuttut
taṅkiya yitaḻit tiraḷiṉun tiraṇṭu
civalat toṭariṉun taḻittuk
koṅkuṟak kuḻaṉṟu neṟittuvārt toḻukik
kuvalayat tiḷiñarkaṇ vaḻukka
veṅkaṇaṉ ṉayiṉār muṉṉinā ḷaḻitta
viruḷiṉu miruṇṭamik kuḻalāḷ. 2.9.18
717
kīṟṟiḷam piṟiyuṅ kaṇicciyiṉ vaḷivuṅ
kiḷarntacev vakattiṉmeṉ malarun
tōṟṟiṭat tōṟṟi viḷaṅkunaṉ malarun tōṟṟiṭat
tōṟṟi viḷaṅkunaṉ ṉutalāḷ cuṭarumuḷ vāraṇat talaku
māṟṟarun taṉucuṅ karuṅkoṭi yetirvu
māṟṟimik kaṭkaṭaṟ kariyiṉ
mēṟṟikaḻ kariya pavaḷameṉ koḻuntāy
viḷaṅkiya ceḻumpuru vattāḷ. 2.9.19
718
maṭaṟkuḻi kiḻittut taṭakkuḻal kuḻittu
variyaḷi yiṉacciṟip paṭuttik
kaṭaṟkuḷan tēṟā talitarac ceytu
kaṇiyayil kaṭipaṭak kaṟuvi
viṭattiṉi yaravap paṭattiṭi paṭutti
mīṉiṉam payappaṭat tāḻttit
tiṭakkatir vaṭivā ḷeṉakkoli paḻakic
cevantari paṭarntami viḻiyāḷ. 2.9.20
719
vaḷḷiyi vāṭṭi yūcali yacittu
maṇṇeḻi lāṭava ruyirik
koḷḷikoṇ ṭuṭalaṅ kuḻippataṟ kaṉṟō
kuḻiyeṉum peyariṭuṅ kuḻiyā
ḷeḷḷiyuñ ciṟanta kumiḻiyum vācat
tiṉiyacaṇ pakamalar taṉiyum
viḷḷaruṅ kāṉat tiṭiyalar paṭutti
vilaṅkiṭa vilaṅkiya kumiḻāḷ. 2.9.21
720
veṇṉilāt taraḷa nakiniri potintu
virintacem pavaḷamō vilavō
vaṇṇavāyc ceḻuñcē tāmpaliṉ malarō
vaṭivuṟu toṇṭiyaṅ kaṉiyō
veṇṇinōk kiṉaruk kuvamiyi ṉaṭaṅkā
teḻilkuṭi yiruntamu toḻukip
paṇṇelāñ cuvaṟṟi yāṭava rirukaṭ
pārviyiṟ cevantamel litaḻāḷ. 2.9.22
721
mulliyu muruntu nirittaṉa pōṉṟu
mutteṉat tikaḻntaṟa neruṅki
melleṉac cevanta maṇiyiṉiṟ pirittu
viḷakkiyop pittuvit taṉapōl
villiṭak kaviṉkoṇ ṭirupuṟat toḻuṅkum
virintapūṅ kāvikaḷ paṭarntu
collaru maṉattā ṭavarmaya liruḷit
tuṇittiṭa nakikkumeṉ ṉakiyāḷ. 2.9.23
722
pāleṉa veḷiṟāk kaṉiyeṉa vaḻiyāp
pacumaṭaṟ ṟēṉeṉac citaṟā
vēlavār kuḻalār ceḻuṅkarat tēntu
miḷaṅkiḷi moḻiyeṉak kuḻaṟā
vēlivā ḻamutam piṟanteṉa vulakam
viḷaṅkiṭap poṉmaḻi poḻiyac
cālavu miṟanta taruviṉan taḻippat
taravaru miṉiyameṉ moḻiyāḷ. 2.9.24
723
veyyava ṉalartta vikacitam porunti
virinaṟik kamalameṉ malariṟ
ceyyava ḷiruppa teṉaveḻil ciṟantu
ceḻuṅkaḷik katirkaḷkāṉ ṟoḻuka
viyaka matippat timaṣkiṟi yuritta
vaḻimuṟi mukammataṅ kaḻitta
tuyyaveṇ matiya nikareṉa vulakiṟ
columpaṭic ciṟantamā mukattāḷ. 2.9.25
724
tiraḷiṉiṉ maṇiyāy muralviṉiṉ vaḷiyāyc
cevvineyp piṉiṟkamu keṉalāy
virikatir maṇippūṇ ṭāṅkumeṉ kaḻuttāḷ
vēyiṉik karumpimel laṇiyac
caruviṭap pacantu tiraṇṭumeṉ miyavāyt
taḻitteḻil piṟaṅkiya tōḷāḷ
varivaḷi cumanti yāḻiṉum viyantu
mayirnirin toḷirumuṉ kiyiṉāḷ. 2.9.26
725
kulikamārn taṉapō larakkiṉuñ civanta
koḻumaṭaṟ kāntaḷaṅ karattāḷ
maliciṉik keḷiṟṟiṉ vaṉappiṉum vaṉappāy
maṇiyaṇi cumantamel viralāḷ
polivuṟac civantīn tiliyeṉak kiḷarntu
puṉakkiḷi nāciyiṉ vaṭivāy
nalivilā tiḷitta vayiravoṇ katirāy
nalaṅkiṭan tilaṅkiya vukirāḷ. 2.9.27
726
taṭittaṭi parantiṭ ṭeḻuntapū rittut
taḷataḷat toṉṟoṭoṉ ṟamiyā
taṭarttimi yāta kaṟuttakaṇ ṇataṉā
laruntavat tavaruyir kuṭittu
vaṭattiṉu ḷaṭaṅkā tiṇittakac caṟuttu

paṭattiṉum piṟaṅkuñ cuṇaṅkaṇi paṭarnta
parumitat tuṇikkaṉa taṉattāḷ. 2.9.28
727

pacumatuk kalacamō vamirtam

pēriḷaṅ kurumpiyō katiriṉ
murukukop paḷikkum vaṉacameṉ mukiyō
muḻumaṇi patittameṉ muṭiyō
kariyilā vaḻakā ṟoḻukiya variyō
kavalutaṟ kariteṉun taṉattāḷ. 2.9.29
728
taṉameṉu mirukōṭ ṭattiyō rāliṟ

maṉanili kavaruṅ kaṭitaṭa varaviṉ

vivaḷvayi ṟaṇimayi roḻuṅkē. 2.9.30
729

kurumukam matunaṉ moḻivaḻi yaṭaṅkāk
kupirkkulan tēynteṉat tēyntu
terivari teṉalā yuvamiyiṟ poruvāc
cēyiḻi maṭanticiṟ ṟiṭiyē. 2.9.31
730

2.10 hapīpu rājā varici varaviṭutta paṭalam (734 - 774)





734
pūṅkoṭi yeṉamuṉa niṉṟa pūviyit

piṇakka ṟuttapit tālipu koṭuttaṉup piṉarāl. 2.11.25
800
putiya naṟporuḷ peṟatteri kavitiyiṉ pukaḻāṉ
matura vācaka meḻutiya tuṇarntuḷa makiḻntu
tutice yuṅkali māneṟip paṭumaṟit tūyōrk
kativi tappala varicicey tapaṣā ciruntār. 2.11.26
801
anta nāḷiyiṉ makkamā nakarava revarum
vantu naṟkali māvurit taṉareṉum vacaṉam
punti kūrtarak kēṭṭaṉar cilaratiṟ poruvāc
cinti yāyiṉar nakarkkeṉat tirumpiṉar cilarē. 2.11.27
802
paṭikki vēntutu māṉuṭaṉ maṉiviyum palarun
toṭikki ṇaṅkiya puyattavar cūḻvara neṟiyi
ṉaṭakka muṉmoḻi paḻuteṉa naviṟara naṭuṅki
yaṭikka lattiṉuṅ karaviṉum patiyivan taṭintār. 2.11.28
803
putitiṉ mūvoru patiṉmaru mūvarum puṟattiṟ
catiya ṟattiṉi yavaravar cārpiṉiṟ cārntā
ratila pūcalmā veṉpava raṟiviṉi luyarnta
matiyiṉ mikkapit tālipi yaṭuttuvan tiruntār. 2.11.29
804
ciṉattu vaṉkolik kāpirka ṭiraṇṭikal cekukku
maṉattiṉ mikkapit tālipu matimuka nōkki
yiṉatti ṉittavirt tapūcalmā veṉpavaṉ ṟaṉinum
maṉitta lattilvit tiruppatu paḻuteṉa vakuttār. 2.11.30
805
poytta māmaṟi mukammati maṉiyiṭip pukutti
vitti runtaṉi yapūcalmā taṉiyunum maṉikku
ḷeyttu vittirup patupaḻu teṉavika liṭarāy
moytta ṭarntaṉa rapūjaki loṭumuraṇ matattār. 2.11.31
806
eṭukkum vāḷayil paṭikkalam palakarat tēntit
toṭukkuṅ pūcaliṭ ṭaṭalapit tālipit turatti
viṭukku meṉpatu maṉiyuṭa ṉakariyum veṟuppak
keṭukku meṉpatu mapūlaku peṉumavaṉ kēṭṭāṉ. 2.11.32
807
eṉakku muṉṉavaṉ ṟaṉiyiṭar viḷittiṭa leṉatu
maṉakku ṟippaṭa rivitavirt tiṭīreṉiṉ matiyi
niṉikku muṉpaki rakumatu neṟinili niṟuvik
kaṉakka vittaliyā ṉalatili yeṉakkaḻa ṟiṉaṉāl. 2.11.33
808
mōtum vāymiyi ṉapūlaku peṉumavaṉ muraṇi
yōtum vācakaṅ kupirava ruḷaṅkaḷi yuruvip
pōtu kiṉṟateṉ ṟaṭarntuniṉ ṟavarpoṟi kalaṅkit
tīta kaṟṟiyaṅ kavaravar maṉivayiṟ cērntār. 2.11.34
809
kaṟita virntiṭā maṉakkuṟi ṣiyaṅkulak kāpi
raṟavu nontakat taṭaṅkiṉa reṉavaṟa mutirntu
niṟiyun tīṉilik kuriyavar makiḻntaneñ ciṉarāy
maṟipa ṭāmukam matiṉvaḻi vaḷarttirun taṉarāl. 2.11.35
810
ariya nāyakaṉ ṟūtuvā ṉavarkkiṟi yaṇukik
kiriyiṉ mītuniṉ ṟarumpeyar napiyeṉak kiḷattum
varuṭa māṟiṉiṉ māṟukoṇ ṭavarmaṉaṅ kaliyat
teruḷu mēṉmiyiṉ mukammatuñ ciṟantirun taṉarāl. 2.11.36
811
porunta māṉilat tulaviya pukaḻuci ṉayiṉā
rarunta vattiṉuṭ poruḷeṉa varumapul kācim
virinta meyneṟic cintiyi ṉaṭuvuṟa viḷaṅki
yirunta meṉmalarp patamukam matumiṉi tiruntār. 2.11.37

2.12 māṉukkup piṇi niṉṟa paṭalam (812 - 883 )




812
kuyiṉiḻal parappac cevvik koḻuntaṭi naṟavañ cintum
vayiravoṇ variyiṉ vimmi vaḷarntatiṇ puyattu vaḷḷal
ceyiraṟu maṟiyiṉ ṟīñcoṟ ceḻumaḻi poḻintu tīṉiṉ
payirvaḷan tēṟac ceytu parivuṭa ṉirukku nāḷil. 2.12.1
813
ariyiṉañ ceṟinta pōṉṟa aṟapikaḷ kuḻuvi ṉāppa
ṇorutaṉic cīya moppa vuṭiyavaṉ ṟūtar celvat
tirunakarp puṟattu nīṅkic ceḻumukiṉ muṭiyiṟ ṟāṅki
marumalar ceṟiyuñ cōli cūḻntatōr variyic cārntār. 2.12.2
814
koṉṟiyuṅ kuruntuṅ kārkkōṟ kuṟiñciyum vēyun taṟṟit
tuṉṟiya niḻalu naṉṉīr coritaru miṭamuñ centēṉ
maṉṟaloṇ malaru nīṅkā vaṉantikaḻ variyiṉ kaṇ ṇa
ceṉṟaṉa neṟikkuṅ kānti cevvimey mukamma taṉṟē. 2.12.3
815
vaṉantari vilaṅku māyttu vaṉṟaci vakirntu vārit
tiṉantoṟuṅ kōliṟ kōlit tīyiṭi yamiḻttik kāyttit
taṉantaṉi yiruntu niṉṟu taṉṟacik perukka laṉṟi
yaṉantaliṟ poḻutum vēṟō raṟiveṉpa taṟinti lāṉē. 2.12.4
816
kāliṉiṟ kaḻalu nīṇṭa kariyakā ḷattiṉ vīkkum
pāliṉil valiyuṅ kiyiṟ paruvarit taṉuvuṅ kūruṅ
kōlveṟi naṇiyun tōḷiṟ kūṉpiṟi vāḷu meṉmi
vāluṭip paṟavi cērttuṅ kaṇṇiyu maruṅkiṟ koṇṭōṉ. 2.12.5
817
kuṟuveyarp putitta meyyuṅ koḻuntaci maṇatta vāyum
paṟitali virippuṅ kūrnta paṭukoli viḻiyu māka
vaṟapiṉi laṟapi vēṭa ṉaṭaviyiṟ ṟoṭarntōr māṉik
kaṟuvoṭum valiyiṟ cērttik kaṭṭivit tiruppak kaṇṭār. 2.12.6
818
kuḻikuḻit teriyuñ centēṉ koḻumalark kāvi nōkkār
poḻimali yaruvi nōkkār puṟattunaṉ ṉiḻali nōkkār
ceḻumukiṟ kaviki vaḷḷal ceṟitaru mīntiṉ ceṅkāy
maṟiyeṉac coriva nōkkār māṉiyē nōkkic ceṉṟār. 2.12.7
819
aruḷaṭi kiṭanta kaṇṇu maḻakoḷir mukamuñ cōti
corinaṟi kamaḻnta meyyuñ cūṉmukiṟ kaviki yōṭum
varuvatu tūyōṉ ṟūtar mukammatu veṉṉat tēṟip
paruvara luḻakku muḷḷat toṭumpiṇi pakaru maṉṟē. 2.12.8
820
neṭiyavaṉ ṟūtar vantār vēṭaṉā ṉilatti ṉanta
muṭaluyirk kiṟuti yilli yuḻiyiṉat tōṭuñ cērntu
kaṭitiṉiṟ kaṉṟuṅ kāṇpō meṉamukam matuvik kaṇṇā
ṉoṭivari yimimū ṭāma ṉōkkiyē kiṭanta taṉṟē. 2.12.9
821
poruppiṭit tuṟukaṟ cārpiṟ poriyārit taruvi ṉīḻaṉ
maruppuṭip paṭalit tiṇṭōṉ maṉṉava ruṭaṉum pukku
neruppiṭit tacivā yārntu niṉṟavē ṭaṉiyuñ cemmāṉ
ṟirupputaṟ karuṅkaṭ ṭuṇṭu kiṭappatuñ ciṟappak kaṇṭār. 2.12.10
822
iṭinilat turukki viṭṭa viracitam paranta teṉṉa
maṭicuti yamutañ cinta vaṭikkaṇīr paṉippat tēṅku
muṭalakan turutti yoppa neṭṭuyirp puyirttuk kāliṟ
ṟuṭaroṭuṅ kiṭappat tūyōṉ ṟūtuva raṭuttu niṉṟār. 2.12.11
823
koṭiyaṭam piliyi māṉum kuḷampiṉmēṟ curukkum puḷḷip
poṭiyuṭaṟ patippum vīṅkip putiyuneṭ ṭuyirppu nōkki
neṭiyava ṉiṟacū lullā neñcunek kurukik kāṉiṉ
piṭipaṭu māṉiṉ ṟaṉpāṟ pēraruḷ curappa niṉṟār. 2.12.12
824
katirviri hapīpu niṟpak kāṉakat tarukka ḷiyāvum
putumala ralarttic centēṉ poḻivamāṉ varutta nōkki
vitirciṉik karaṅkaḷ cāyttu meṉṟaḻik kūntal cōra
matiyaḻin tiraṅkik kaṇṇīr vaṭippaṉa pōṉṟa taṉṟē. 2.12.13
825
kulattoṭum paṟavi tattaṅ kuṭampiyiṟ pukutaṉ māṉi
nilattiṭik kiṭattik kaṭṭi niṉṟavēṭ ṭuvaṉik kaṇṇā
ṉalattoṭuṅ kāṇpa tākā teṉanaṭu naṭuṅki yuḷḷa
mulittaṟap peṭiyi ṉōṭu moḷippaṉa pōṉṟa taṉṟē. 2.12.14
826
ēṭṭalar naṟava mānti yiruñcurum picikkun tōṟṟam
vāṭṭamiṉ mukamma tiṅṅaṉ vantaṉar varuntu māṉi
mīṭṭaṉar vēṭa ṉīmāṉ virumpiṉaṉ payaṅka ṭīrttār
kūṭṭuṟin toḷittaṉ māṟṟu meṉappala kūya pōlum. 2.12.15
827
niṟivaḷañ curanta kāṉi ṉiṉṟanan napiyi nōkkik
kuṟiyavā lacittu nīṇṭa koḻuṅkaḻut tuyartti nīṭṭi
maṟipaṭā matiyē vaṇmi mukammatē yeṉṉap pōṟṟit
taṟukiṭā tevarkkuṅ kēṭpac calāmeṭut turittuk kūṟum. 2.12.16
828
vallava ṉuṇmit tūtē maṉṉumā nilattiṉ mānta
rallali yakaṟṟi vētat taṟaneṟi payiṟṟic corkkat
tilliṭip pukuttap pūvi ṉiṭattiṉi lutitta kōvē
yolliyi ṉeṉatu coṟkēṭ ṭuvantaru ḷaḷikka vēṇṭum. 2.12.17
829
eṉṉuyi reṉanīṅ kāta viṉamumeṉ kaliyuṅ kaṉṟun
tuṉṉiṭat tiraṇṭu pimpuṟ ṟuṟitoṟu mēyntu nāḷu
muṉṉiya pacika ṭīrttōr mirukaṅkaṭ kuyirko ṭāmaṉ
maṉṉiya maliyiṉ cārpu maṉappaya makaṟṟi vāḻntēm. 2.12.18
830
irunilat tācik kāyō riḷaṅkaṉṟeṉ vayiṟṟu ṟātāṉ
maruviya kaliyu nāṉum varuttamuṟ ṟirukkuṅ kālam
perukutīṉ mukamma tēnum peyariṉip pōṟṟal ceytē
ṉuruvamin tiḷañcūṉ muṟṟi yutaramum vaḷarnta taṉṟē. 2.12.19
831
taṉiyaṉeṉ ṉuyiruṅ kākkuṅ kaliyuyir tāṉu moṉṟā
yiṉitiṉoṉ ṟāya teṉṉa viḷaṅkaṉṟoṉ ṟīṉṟē ṉiṉpa
naṉikaḷi kaṭali lāḻntu naṟumali yiṭattiṟ cērntu
tuṉipala vakaṟṟi ṉēṉmuṉ cūḻviṉi yaṟikē lēṉē. 2.12.20
832
uḷḷuyi raṉiya kaṉṟu moruttalu miyāṉu mōrnāḷ
veḷḷamot taṉiya māṉi ṉiṉamōr veṟpiṉ cārpi
ṉaḷḷili yaḷḷi vāykoṇ ṭarumpaci taṭintu nīruṇ
ṭeḷḷaḷa veṉiṉu macca miṉṟiniṉ ṟulavu nēram. 2.12.21
833
atticik ketiriṉ mēlpā laṭuttoru kuvaṭṭiṉ kaṇṇē
mattakak kariyu māykkum varippuli muḻakka nīṇṭa
kuttirat tacaṉit tākkiṉ kuvalaya matirak kēṭṭut
tattiyet ticiyun tikkun taṉittaṉic citaṟi ṉēmāl. 2.12.22
834
kūṭiya tūṟum pāriṟ kuḷittiṭak kutittu vallē
yōṭiya ticiyi ṉoṉṟi yoṉṟukāṇ kilatā yāṉum
vāṭiya maṉatti ṉōṭu maṟiyiyu nōkkā tākki
yāṭiyiṟ ṟurumpāy vēṟō raṭaviyi ṉaṭintiṭ ṭēṉāl. 2.12.23
835
aṭaviyi ṉaṭiyuṅ kāli yavvuḻik karantiv vēṭaṉ
ṟuṭariṭum valiyic cuṟṟic curukkiṭap pulivāyt tappi
miṭalari yuḻiyiṟ cikki miṭinteṉa miṭintu cevvi
yuṭaluyir patippat tēmpi yuṇarvaḻin toṭuṅkā niṉṟēṉ. 2.12.24
836
valiyiṭat tuṟinta teṉṉa makiḻnteḻun tōṭi vantu
nilipeṟa vaṭuttuc cāyttu niṉṟeṉi nōkki yākat
tulivuṟum pacikkiṉ ṟeṉpā luṟṟaṉi yeṉṉak kūṟic
cilakaṇi nilattiṟ cērttit terintoru pācan toṭṭāṉ. 2.12.25
837
tirukkaṟa nālu tāḷuñ cevvitiṟ kūṭṭi yaṅki
varikkayi ṟataṉāṟ cuṟṟi māṟukoṇ ṭīḻttuk kaṭṭik
carikkara meṉṉa nīṇṭa karattiṉāṟ ṟāṅki muṉṉarc
curukkiya valiyi nīkkit tōḷiṉi leṭuttuk koṇṭāṉ. 2.12.26
838
cuvimuṉik kōṭṭuc cevvik kaliyuṭa luyiru mīṉṟa
naviyuṭa luyiru mōrmā ṉuṭaṉkoṇṭu naṭappa tottuc
cavipuṟan tavaḻuṅ kōṭṭuc cārpiliv vaṉattiṉ kaṇṇē
cuviyaṟu moḻiyā ṉeṉṉic cumantiva ṇiṟakki vittāṉ. 2.12.27
839
kaṭṭuṭaṉ kiṭantu neñciṟ kavaliyuḷ ḷaḻintu māṟā
neṭṭuyirp peṟintu cōrntu nilattiṭik kiṭakku nēram
vaṭṭaveṇ kaviki vaḷḷal mukammatu napiyē yummit
tiṭṭiyiṟ ṟeriyak kaṇṭēṉ ṟiṭukkamun tīrnta taṉṟē. 2.12.28
840
eṉaviṉi yurittup piṉṉu meḻiṉapi mukatti nōkki
maṉanili vākki ṉōṭu mukammatē yeṉṉap pōṟṟip
puṉamuṟi vilaṅkiṉ cāti yāyiṉun tamiyēṉ puṉcoṟ
ṟaṉiyaruṭ paṭuttik kēṭpī reṉṟuri cāṟṟic cāṟṟum. 2.12.29
841
iccili vēṭaṉ kiyi ṉiṟattali yuḷatti leṇṇi
yaccamuṟ ṟurippa taṉṟiv vavaṉiyiṟ cīva ṉiyāvu
niccaya miṟatta lallā liruppavi nilatti luṇṭō
muccakam viḷaṅkun tīṉiṉ mutaṉmaṟi muṟimic collōy. 2.12.30
842
kaliyeṉap pirivi lātu kaṇṇimi kāppa teṉṉa
valivaṟak kāppac ciṉṉā ḷavaṉiyiṟ kalantu vāḻntēṉ
kulaviya maṟiyu mīṉṟēṉ kuṟittiṉi yiruppa teṉko
lilinuṉip paṉiyi ṉākki yiṟattalē nalattaṉ maṉṉō. 2.12.32
843
aṭaviyiṟ kiriyil vīṇi lavatiyuṟ ṟiṟanti ṭāmal
vaṭivuṭik kurici lēnum malarppatac cevvi nōkkip
paṭuparaṟ kāṉil vēṭaṉ pacippiṇi tīrppa tāka
vuṭaliṟat tiṭuta levvev viṟappiṉu muyarcci mēlōy. 2.12.33
844
varippuli muḻakkaṅ kēṭṭu māṉiṉañ citaṟit tattan
tarippiṭa maṟiyā toṉṟuk koṉṟuṭaṉ cārā teṅku
murippari teṉṉap pōnta tāleṉa torutta ṟēṭi
yirippaṟā neṭikkāṉ pōyppō yiruntatō viṟanta tēyō. 2.12.33
845
olliyi ṉōṭi nīṅkā toruttaliṉ ṟaḷavu mōntu
pulliṉik kaṟiyā nīrum pucittiṭā tiruntu tēṭi
yallaluṟ ṟaḻuṅkik kaṇṇi ṉaruvinīr coriya vāṭip
pallava meriyiṟ pukka teṉavuṭal patikku maṉṟē. 2.12.34
846
piṭipaṭu mitaṟku muṉṉē mūṉṟunāṭ piṟantu pulliṉ
koṭinuṉi mēyntu nīruṅ kuṭittaṟi yātu pāvi
maṭimuli yiṟaṅkip pālum vaḻintatu kuḻavi cōrntu
paṭimici kiṭanteṉ pāṭu paṭuvatō vaṟiki lēṉē. 2.12.35
847
kōṭṭuṭik kaliyi ṉōṭuṅ kūṭiṟṟō valatōr pāliṉ
mīṭṭatō viṉattic cērntu vimminiṉ ṟēṅkiṟ ṟōkāṉ
kāṭṭiṭip pulivāyp paṭṭuk kaḻintatō veṉṉit tēṭi
vāṭṭamuṟ ṟalaṟi yōṭi maṟukiṟṟō vaṟiki lēṉē. 2.12.36
848
tēṅkiya paciyāl vāṭit tirintatō viṟanta tōveṉ
ṟēṅkiya varutta mallā livviṭa rataṉi lāvi
nīṅkumeṉ ṟuḷḷat tuḷḷē ṉeṭṭuṭa luṭuppi ṉāvi
tāṅkiya taruma vēntē tavaṟaṉṟu cāta maṉṟē. 2.12.37
849
maṉṉiya kalimā veṉṉum vaḻinili mānta riyārum
poṉṉilam pukutac ceyyum puṇṇiyap pukaḻiṉ mikkōy
koṉṉilic cilikki vēṭaṉ koṭumpaci taṇippē ṉeṉṟāṭ
piṉṉiya piṇippu nīkkip piṇiyeṉa viṭuttal vēṇṭum. 2.12.38
850
viṭuttirēṟ kaliyic cērntu viḻivuṟuṅ kavali tīrap
paṭuttiyeṉ ṉiṉattuk kōtip paṟaḻiṉuk kiṉiya tīmpāl
koṭuttarum paciyi māṟṟik kulattoṭuñ cērttu vallē
yaṭuttoru kaṭikip pōtilaṭiva ṉeṉ ṟinta taṉṟē. 2.12.39
851
māṉuri vaḻaṅkak kēṭṭu maṉattiṉiṟ karuṇi poṅkik
kāṉavēṭ ṭuvaṉi nōkkik kaṉṟiṭi varuttan tīrttut
tāṉvaru maḷavu miyāṉē piṇiyeṉac cāṟri niṉṟār
tīṉeṉum payirik kāttuc ceḻumpukaḻ viḷakkuñ cemmal. 2.12.40
852
piriyamuṟ ṟiraṅkik kāṭṭiṉ piṇakkiyāṉ piṇiyeṉ ṟōtu
muriyiṉik kēṭṭu vēṭa ṉoṇpuyaṅ kuluṅka nakkit
teritaru maṟivi ṉōṭuñ ciṉattoṭuṅ kalantu tērntu
karumukiṟ kaviki vaḷḷal kaviṉmuka nōkkic colvāṉ. 2.12.41
853
muḷḷuṭik kāṉi lēki mukamaḻin tucci vērvi
yuḷḷaṅkā ṉaṉippa vōṭi yuṭalulin toṉṟuṅ kāṇā
viḷḷarum paciyāṉ mīḷum vēḷiyip piṇiyi nōkki
yoḷḷiḻi valiyiṟ ṟākkip piṭittiva ṇoruṅku cārntēṉ. 2.12.42
854

2.17 paruppata rājaṉik kaṇṇuṟṟa paṭalam (968-1002)

kāra ṇappala ṉaṟintumvañ caṉiyeṉuṅ kāpir
pāra ṇitteṟin tirukavuṇ matacalam parappum
vāra ṇattiṉu mummaṭaṅ keṉumpaṭi valittār. 2.17.1
969
mikitta vīṟari muḻipukun teṉaviṟa ṉayiṉā
rakattiṟ ṟuṉpiṉi laṭaṅkiṉa reṉavaṟi kilarāyp
pakitta puṉmaṉak koṭiyavar perumpaki toṭuttā
rikatti ṉummaṟu purattiṉu miviyili veṉavē. 2.17.2
970
kuṟiṣi yaṅkulak kāpirkaḷ viḷittiṭuṅ koṭumi
yaṟavu mēlvaḷarn taṉakuṟin tilaapit tāli
piṟivaṉ muṉviti yamittiṭum paṭiyiva ṇiṟantu
niṟiyun tiṅkaḷu mūṉṟeṉat tiṉanikaḻn taṉavē. 2.17.3
971
kāya muḷḷuri yuyireṉu miruvaruṅ kampuk
kāya tummiṉat tavarpaki yiyumaṉat taṭakkit
tūya nāyakaṉ tīṉili perukkiṭun tuṇivāṟ
ṟāyi peṉṉumat talattiṉuk keḻuntaru ḷiṉarē. 2.17.4
972
cavikoḷ veṇcuti māmatiḷ tāyipi lipiṉu
aputu yālileṉ ṟiṭumpeyark kuṟiṣiyeṉ pavaṉi
napika ṇāyakaṅ kaṇṭaṉa ravaṉetir naṭantu
kuviki koṇṭupiṉ ṉivaroṭu maṉikuṟu kiṉaṉē. 2.17.5
973
maṉiyi ṉiṟkoṭu pōymukam matutami yirutti
yiṉiya vācakat taṉpoṭum pukaḻnteṭut tētti
vaṉaca meṉmalar ceḻumpatat tiṇivarun tiṭavē
taṉiya ṉeṉvayiṉ cārntavi cāṟṟuka veṉṟāṉ. 2.17.6
974
kaṇṭa pōtiṉi luvakiyi ṉirukaraṅ kuvittuk
koṇṭu niṉṟunaṉ moḻipakarn taṉaṉeṉak kuṟittu
vaṇṭu vāḻmalarp puyamukam matunapi maṇivāy
viṇṭu tēṉcorin teṉaccila moḻiviḷam puvarāl. 2.17.7
975
āti taṉṉaruḷ vāṉavark karaceṉi yaṭuttu
nīti naṉṉapi yeṉumpeya raḷittunī ṇilattil
vēta mummeṉak karuḷitī ṉilivirit tiṭumeṉ
ṟōti viṇṇakat tuṟintaṉar ceḻuṅkati rulava. 2.17.8
976
anta nāṭṭoṭut tiṟṟināḷ variyunal laṟivar
punti kūrtarac ceḻuṅkali māttaṉip pukaṭṭi
vantu cūḻtaru pavakkaḷi tavirttumaṇ ṇilatti
luyntu naṟkati peṟuvataṟ kuṟuticey taṉaṉāl. 2.17.9
977
uṉṉu naṉmaṟi mutaṟkali māveṭut turittuṉ
ṟaṉṉi nalvaḻi yavaṉeṉun takimiyiṟ paṭutti
maṉṉun tīṉili virittaṟam vaḷarttiṭa vēṇṭiṟ
ṟeṉṉu ḷamati ṉaṭintaṉeṉ ṟurittaṉa riṟacūl. 2.17.10
978
katirti raṇṭuru veṭuttava rurittakaṭ ṭurikēṭ
ṭativi tattoṭu naṉkeṉac ciraṅkara macittup
putiya māmaṟik kiyami leṉappukaḻ paṭutti
matiyiṉ vēṟuvit ticintiṭuñ cilamoḻi vakuppāṉ. 2.17.11
979
eṭuttu rittavi yeṉṉiṉat tavarkkeṭut tiyampi
yaṭutti raṇṭoru tiṉattiṉum miṭattiṉi laṇuki
vaṭitta vāymiyi ṉoḻukuvaṉ maṟiteri matiyōy
paṭitta lampukaḻ nakariṉiṟ celumeṉap pakarntāṉ. 2.17.12
980
iṉiya vācaka murittava ṉiruppanam miṟacūl
viṉiya muṟṟativ viṭatteṉat tāyupi viṭuttu
niṉivu nēroṭu toḻuteḻun tiruntunaṉ ṉeṟikkē
naṉikoṇ meṉmalar kāṉakat taruttara naṭantār. 2.17.13
981
āla mumveḷi ṟiṭakkeṭuṅ koṭumaṉat taptu
yāli leṉpavaṉ ciṟiyavark kiṉiyavi yurittu
mēlum pētineñ cavaruṭa ṉivariyum viravik
kōlum vaṉkatam varaccila moḻikoḷut tiṉaṉāl. 2.17.14
982
veṟiyum pittumuṟ ṟavaṉivaṇ peruvaḻi viṭuttōr
neṟiyi ṭittaṉi ceṉṟaṉa ṉavaṉṟaṉi nēṭi
maṟiyuṅ kāṟali takarntiṭa valiyakal leṭuttiṭ
ṭeṟiyu mēkumeṉ ṟurittaṉa ṉarakiṭi yerivāṉ. 2.17.15
983
vañca kakkoṭi yavaṉurit tiṭumoḻi vaḻiyē
pañca pātakar naṭantarum pātiyik kuṟukik
kañca meṉpata mukammatik kaṭitiṉil vaḷintiṭ
ṭañca lātukaṟ kuṇileṭut terintuniṉ ṟaṭarntār. 2.17.16
984
kalli ṉālurañ ciraṅkaraṅ kāṉmukaṅ kāṇā
tella vaṉkatir poḻinteṉap pala toṭut teṟintu
palli ṉālita ḻatukkiyu muṟukkiyum paṭarntār
collo ṇāpperum pātakam viḷittiṭuñ cūmar 2.17.17
985
irumi yumpata miḻantavar celacciṉan teṟintu
karuṉu tālipu maṭṭiṉun toṭarntavar kalintār
taruki vaḷḷaṉan napimukam matiṉmuḻan tāḷi
lorukal lēṟupaṭ ṭūṟupaṭ ṭutirntaṉa vutiram. 2.17.18
986
ūṟu paṭṭatiṉ varuttamum paciyiṉuḷ ḷulivu
māṟu paṭṭavar toṭarntati ṉaṭantamey malivum
pēṟu paṭṭamun tantava ṉaruḷeṉap peritiṉ
ṟēṟu paṭṭava ṇiruntaṉar tirunapi yiṟacūl. 2.17.19
987

ṟirunta nōkkiṉar maṅkuliṉ vayiṉjipu rīlip
poruntak kaṇṭukaṇ kaḷittaṉar putiyavaṉ ṟūtar
virinta veḷḷiṭi kaṭantaṇit tuṟaviḷaṅ kiṉarāl. 2.17.20
988
vanta ṭuttiṟi yavaṉcilā murittumey varunta
linta mānilat tiṟṟinum miṉattava riṭarā
lanta nāyaka ṉamararil varikkara cavari
yunta mēvaluk kēviṉa ṉeṉaveṭut turittār. 2.17.21
989
iṟumpi ṉukkara cākiya malakkuma tiṭatti
ṉuṟumpa kittira ḷettaṉi yākilu moṭukkik
kuṟumpi ṉittavirt tiṭavaru kuvareṉak kūṟip
puṟampa rantaceṅ kaṭkaṭi yaruḷoṭum pōṉār. 2.17.22
990
uruppo tintanal vaṭivava rurittoru noṭikkuṭ
paruppa taṅkaḷuk kiṟiceḻun taṭañciṟi parappit
tiruppu nīralik kaṭalvari puvitiṭuk kiṭavē
viruppa muṟṟunan napiyiṭat taṭuttaṉar viriviṉ. 2.17.23
991
veṟpa ṭaṅkaluṅ karaṅkaḷiṟ picintuviṭ ṭeṟintu
puṟpu takkaṭa liṟittoru kaṭaliṉiṟ pukaṭṭip
paṟpa mākkumveṅ kaṉaliyum pukiyaṟap paṭutti
niṟpa vīramum valimiyum paṭittanaṉ ṉeṟiyār. 2.17.24
992
utaya mākirik korutaran taṭavuva roruki
putiya mēṟkiri taṭavuvar viṇṇimaṇ puraḷac
citaṟit tīvaka mēḻiyuṅ kaṭaliṭi citippār
vitiya vaṉvitit tiṭumavi yeviyeṉum viratar. 2.17.25
993
oruno ṭikkuḷan taramaṭaṅ kaluntirin tulavi
yoruno ṭikkuḷiv vāṉakaṅ kavintamaṭ ṭulavi
yiruma ṉattoṭum varavaram paṭittava reḻilā
riruce vikkoṭut tēvaliṉ paṭikkicin tiruppār. 2.17.26
994
citti rattaṭap puyavari mukammatu tirumu
ṉutta mattoṭu moṭuṅkiniṉ ṟorucalā murittu
mutta veṇkati ravariru meṉumoḻi kēṭṭup
patti yāyaru kiruntoru moḻipakarn tiṭuvār. 2.17.27
995
etta lattuyi riṉukkunal luṇavaḷit tiraṅku
matta ṉeṉṉinum mēvaluk karuḷiṉa ṉataṉā
litta lattilvan taṭintaṉa ṉiṉiyaruṭ kaṭikkaṇ
vitti rēṟpaṇi viṭitaṟu kilaṉmaṟi matiyōy. 2.17.28
996
aruntu māramu takkali māvurik kaṭaṅkā
tirunta vūrevi pakittava riyāvarnum mitayam
porunti ṭāttici yettici poruvarāk katirmey
varuntal ceytava revartiri taravaḻaṅ kiṭumē. 2.17.29
997
ceppi ṉīreṉiṟ ceṟunarka ṭiraḷumat ticiyu
muppu vāriyu ḷamiḻttuva ṉalatoru variyā
lippe rumpuvik kuḷḷarit tiṭukuva ṉeḷiyēṉ
ṟuppa ṟintiṭa vēṇṭumeṉ ṟiravoṭuñ coṉṉār. 2.17.30
998
viṇṭi ṉukkara civipakarn tiṭattuḷi vitirkkuṅ
koṇṭa laṅkavi kikkiṟi yakaṅkaḷi kūrntu
maṇṭa lattuma turivaḻi naṭattiṭiṉ maṟinēr
kaṇṭu tēṟuva revarpoṟi nilattiṉiṟ kaṭaṉē. 2.17.31
999
iṟiya vaṉṉaruṭ paṭikkiṭa raṭintateṉ ṉiṭattiṟ
kuṟiyi teṉṟumā ṉilattavar tamikkuṟi paṭuttaṉ
maṟiyi ṉērala vekuḷiyi maṉattiṉi laṭakki
niṟiyi ṉiṟpatu periyavark kuriyanaṉ niliyē. 2.17.32
1000
mikka vaṉkupirk kolittoḻiṉ maṉattiṉi viṭuttiṭ
ṭikka ṇattiṉal vaḻippaṭā reṉilivar payanta
mikka ḷāyiṉu nalvaḻik koḻukuvar maṟiyun
tikka ṭaṅkalum parantutī ṉeṟimuṟi ceyumē. 2.17.33
1001
vāṉa nāyaka ṉēvaluk kuriyava makiḻviṉ
yāṉi ṉittiṭum poḻutiṉil varukaviṟ ṟiyiṉun
tāṉa mītiṉiṟ celkaveṉ ṟicittaṉar taḷarā
tāṉa ceykiyī teṉaveḻun taṉarmalik karacar. 2.17.34
1002
uḷḷa mītilaṉ poṭunapik kuyarcalā murittu
veḷḷi ṭippaṭarn tavaṇaṭin taṉarmalar virintu
kaḷḷa lampiya poḻil ceṟi kaṟṉuta ālip
vaḷḷa lārirun taṉarpuvi yiṭiyeḻu matipōl. 2.17.35

2.18 attācu īmāṉ koṇṭa paṭalam (1003 -1014)





1003
maṟikali pukaḻnta cevvi mukammatu taṉittut tāka
niṟiyoṭum paciyun tuṉṉi nīṇeṟi yiruppa vavvū
riṟapīā putalvar tammi liruvarka ḷiṉitu nōkkik

1004
aṉṉavar toḻumpa ṉattā ceṉpava ṉavaṉik kūvik
kaṉṉalañ cuviyiṉ mikkān tirukiyiṉ kaṉiyi yēnti
yiṉṉaṇaṅ koṭupō yāṇṭi ṉiruppavar karatti ṉīntu
paṉṉarum paciyi māṟṟi vāveṉap pariviṟ coṉṉār. 2.18.2
1005
kācaṟun taṭṭat tiṭṭa paḻattiṉik karatti lēntit
tūciṉiṟ potintu tōḷiṟ cumantaru neṟiyi muṉṉip
pācaṭit tarukka ḷiyāvum palamalar coriya vāynta
vācamū ṭulavuñ cevvi mukammatu tirumuṉ vittāṉ. 2.18.3
1006
kaṉiyiṉik koṇarntu vittōṉ cemmukaṅ kaviṉa nōkki
yiṉitiṉi lirukki yīntiṭ ṭeḻiṟ ceḻuṅkamalak kiyāṟ
paṉimalart tukili nīttup paḻattiṉit tīṇṭi yiṉpa
maṉiyanal picumi lōti yamuteṉa nukartal ceytār. 2.18.4
1007
paṇṇiṉu miṉiya tēṉcār paḻattiṉiṟ paciyip pōkki
yuṇṇiṟi yuvaki kūrntev vūrava ṉiṉpē rēteṉ
ṟaṇṇalu murippac cevvi yakamakiḻn tattā ceṉpōṉ
puṇṇiyap poruḷē yeṉṉap pōṟṟi vāyputittuc colvāṉ. 2.18.5
1008
naḷḷeṉa vulaki ṉūḻiṉ varunacu ṟāṉi mārkkat
tuḷḷava ṉīṉa vāveṉ ṟōtiya vūri ṉuḷḷēṉ
ṟeḷḷiya ṉiṟapi ātaṉ ṟirumaṉik kiṇakaṉ cēnta
vaḷḷili vēlō yattā ceṉpava ṉaṭiyē ṉeṉṟāṉ. 2.18.6
1009
virintamuṉ maṟika ṭērntu meynneṟi muṟimi nāḷum
pirintiṭā tuṟiyū ṉūcu napiyeṉum peyariṉ vaḷḷa
liruntavū ravaṉō veṉṟā riṉitiṉmuṉ ṉavari yiṉṉōr
terintavā ṟevvā ṟeṉṉac cintiyuṭ cintit tāṉē. 2.18.7
1010
poṉṉula kamarar pōṟṟap pūviṭi yirunta yūṉu
ceṉṉunaṉ ṉapiyi nīvi revvaṇa maṟivī reṉṉa
naṉṉili yoṭumat tācu naviṉṟaṉaṉ vaṇaṅki lāta
maṉṉavark kurumē ṟeṉṉa varumukam matupiṉ colvār. 2.18.8
1011
paṭiyiṭat tiṉilyū ṉūcu napiyeṉum paṭṭam peṟṟōr
neṭiyavaṉ ṟūta riyāṉu napiyeṉu nilimi peṟṟēṉ
vaṭivuḷō yataṉā leṟku maṉṉuyirt tuṇiva rāku
maṭaṉapi māru meṉṟā rāraṇaṅ kiṭanta vāyār. 2.18.9
1012
napikaḷuk karacāy vanta nāyaka muṟitta māṟṟañ
ceviyiṉiṟ pukuta vuṇmit tirunapi yivarē yeṉṉat
tavirkilā tuḷḷat tuṉṉic caraṇiṇi yiṟiñci yēttik
kaviṉuṟuṅ kalimā vōtik kaṇṇiṇi kaḷippa niṉṟāṉ. 2.18.10
1013
makkanaṉ ṉakaril vāḻu mukammatuk kattā ceṉpō
ṉikkaṇat tīmāṉ koṇṭā ṉeṉumoḻi yiṟapī ātaṉ
makkaḷkēṭ ṭaṟavu nakki māyavañ cakattuṭ pukkic
cikkiṉaṉ ṟoḻumpa ṉiyāmeṉ ceykuvō meṉṉa nintār. 2.18.11
1014
matipakir napikkaṉ pāka mantirak kalimā vōti
yitayamot tiṉitī māṉkoṇ ṭirumaṉak kupiri nīttup
patamalar tutittut tēṭāp palaṉkati paṭittē ṉeṉṉap
putiyanal vaṭiva ṉākip poruvilat tācu pōṉāṉ. 2.18.12

2.19 jiṉkaḷ īmāṉ koṇṭa paṭalam (1015 - 1057)

kaṭantaṉiṟ kupireṉ ṟōtuṅ kaḷiṟaṭa rariyē ṟeṉṉa

1016

maṅkulaṅ kaviki vaḷḷaṉ mukammatu nakulāt taṉṉiṟ
ṟaṅkiṉar paṟavi tattaṅ kuṭampiyiṟ cāra vavavip
paṅkayaṅ kuviyac ceṅkē ḻalarimēṟ paravi cārnta. 2.19.2
1017

nilavukop piḷitta teṉṉa nīṇṭameyc cōti kāla
nalaṉuṟu nakulā veṉṉa nāṭṭiya talatti ṉōrpāṟ
calatarak kaviki yōṅkat taṉittava ṇiruntā rippāl. 2.19.3
1018
alikaṭaṟ ṟirikku nāppa ṇāḷiyā caṉattil viki
yulakelāṅ koṭuṅkō lōcci yorukuṭi niḻaliṟ ṟāṅkip
palakali maruvalārkkup paṭiṟeṉum paṭina ṭāttun

1019
koliyiṉuk kuriya tanti kōḷuyirt tuṇivaṉ māṟā
nilikeṭuṅ karavuk kaṉpa ṉintaṉik kuṟṟa tampi
yilipiḻi matuvuk kīṉṟa cēyiṉu miṉiya ṉīṇṭa
vulakiṉiṉ māyamellā mōruru veṭuttu niṉṟōṉ. 2.19.5
1020
eṇṇiṟan taṉiya kālamirun tiṟi yēvaṉ māṟi
viṇṇila kiḻantu meymi vitimaṟi taṉakku nāṇi
maṇṇilat tiruntu vāḻu māṉuṭa revarkkum veyya
taṇṇiya ṉipulī ceṉṉun taṉipperu nāmat tāṉē. 2.19.6
1021
makkaṭaṅ kuḻuviṉ viki mantiran talivar cūḻa
mikkajiṉ cilatikkūvi viṟaṉ mukam matuvi nīvi
rikkaṇat tiṟṟip pōti levviṭat tuṟintā reṉṟeṉ
pakkali lurippa nōkki vammeṉap pariviṟ coṉṉāṉ. 2.19.7
1022
aruntavan tavaṟi niṉṟa varacaṉī turippak kēṭṭup
peruntokik kuḻuvi ṉōṭum peri teḻun tāḻicūḻa
viruntavi yakatti kānta meṭṭiṉun tēṭic ceṉṟu
pirintati loṉpāṉ jiṉkaḷ pēraṟa paṭinta vaṉṟē. 2.19.8
1023
aṟṟiyiṟ poḻuti rāvil rakaciyat toḻuki yaṉpāy
muṟṟuṟa muṭittu vaḷḷaṉ mutalavaṉ ṟaṉṉi yēttik
kuṟṟamaṟ ṟirantu niṉṟa vacaṉattiṉ kuṟippuk kāti
ṉuṟṟaṭut toruṅku nōkki yōriṭat tuṟinta vaṉṟē. 2.19.9
1024
tarippoṭun tuvāvi yōtit taṉinaku lāvi ṉōrparl
viruppoṭu mirappak kēṭṭu mikamakiḻn titaya nōkki
yiruppatu napiyē vāykoṇ ṭicippatu puṟukkā ṉeṉṉat
tirupputaṟ karitāy niṉṟu jiṉkaṇ meycilirtta vaṉṟē. 2.19.10
1025
ittiṉat tiṉilaṉ pāka veḻiṉapi kamala pāta
muttipeṟ ṟīmāṉ koṇṭu mutaṟpavan tuṭippō meṉṉa
vottita mittut tammi loṉṟukkoṉ ṟuṟuti kūṟip

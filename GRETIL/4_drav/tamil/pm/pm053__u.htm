1. pāṭṭiṉiyalpu



mullippāṭṭu eṉpatip paṟṟit teriya vēṇṭuvaṉa ellām ārāyummuṉ, pāṭṭu eṉpatu

ettakiyatu? eṉṟu ārāyntu aṟintu koḷḷal vēṇṭum. piṉṟik kālattut tamiḻp pulavar
pāṭṭeṉpatu

iṉṉateṉṟē aṟiyārāyp putupputu muṟiyāṟ coṟkaḷik kōttup poruḷ āḻamiṉṟic ceyyuḷ

iyaṟṟukiṉṟār. paṇṭik kālattut taṇṭamiḻp pulavarō pāṭṭu eṉpataṉ iyalpi naṉkaṟintu
nalamuṭiya

ceyyuṭkaḷ palappala iyaṟṟiṉār. iṅṅaṉam muṟkālattārāṟ ceyyappaṭṭa pāṭṭiṉ iyalpoṭu
māṟupaṭṭup

piṟkālattār uṇmi piṟaḻntu pāṭiya ceyyuṭkaḷik kaṇṭu māṇākkar pāṭṭiṉiyalpu
aṟiyātu

mayaṅkuvārākaliṟ, pāṭṭu eṉpatu iṉṉateṉpataṉi oru ciṟitu viḷakkuvām.



ulaka iyaṟkiyiṟ kaṇ mutalāṉa pulaṉkaḷukku viḷaṅkit tōṉṟum aḻaki yellān taṉṉakattē

neruṅkap potintuvittup, piṉ avaṟṟi nam aṟiviṉiṭattē pulappaṭuvaṇṇan tōṟṟuvittup,
poruḷ

nikaḻcciyoṭu māṟupaṭutal illā iṉiya ōciyuṭaṉ icintu naṭipeṟum iyalpiṉi
uṭiyatutāṉ pāṭṭeṉṟu aṟital vēṇṭum. iṉṉum eṅkeṅku nam aṟivit tamvayap
paṭuttukiṉṟa pēraḻakum pēroḷiyum peruntaṉmiyum

viḷaṅkit tōṉṟukiṉṟaṉavō aṅkellām pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉṟē aṟitalvēṇṭum. itaṉi viḷakkik

kāṭṭumiṭattup, pēraḻakāṟ ciṟanta ōr araci tāṉ mēṟpōrttirunta nīlappaṭṭu āṭiyiṉic
ciṟitu

ciṟitāka nīkkip, piṉ ataṉic curuṭṭik kīḻē eṟintu viṭṭut tuyil oḻintu, oḷiviḷaṅku taṉ

naḷimukaṅkāṭṭi eḻuntati yoppa, iruṭkūṭṭañ curuṇṭu maṭaṅki alikaṭaliṟ ceṉṟu
aṭaṅkiviṭumāṟu iḷiya ñāyiṟu urukkit tiraṭṭiya pacumpoṟ ṟiraḷipōlat taḷataḷaveṉak
kīḻt ticiyil tōṉṟavum,

atticiyiṉ parappellām poṉ uritta kaṟpōṟ polintu tikaḻavum, pacumi poṉmi nīlam
civappu

veṇmi mutalāṉa niṟa vēṟupāṭuḷḷa poṉ veḷḷikaḷ uruki ōṭukiṉṟa nilam pōla vāṉ
iṭamellām

palavaṇṇamāy virintu viḷaṅkavum, kariyamukilka ḷellāñ cevvarakku vaḻitta akaṉṟa
tiric cīlikaḷ

pōlavum āṅkāṅkuc collutaṟ kariya pēroḷiyoṭu tikaḻavum ulakamaṅki nakittāṟ pōlap

putumiyuṟṟut tōṉṟum viṭiyaṟkāla aḻakellām pāṭṭeṉṟē aṟitalvēṇṭum. ā! iṅṅaṉan
tōṉṟum av viṭiyaṟkāla aḻakiṉik kaṇṭuviyanta vaṇṇamāy mīṉvaliyoṭu kaṭaṟkariyil
niṟkuñ cempaṭavaṉik kāṭṭiṉuñ ciṟanta pulavaṉ yār?

av viṭiyaṟkāliyilē mulli nilattu mēypparkaḷ āṇkaṉṟukaḷit toḻuvattilē tāmpiṉāṟ

kaṭṭivittu āṉnirikaḷi aṭuttuḷḷa maliccāraliṟ koṇṭupōyp paciya pul mēyaviṭṭut
tām maranaḻaliṟ

cāyntiruntu koṇṭu, tamakketirē paccilip pōrvi mēṟkoṇṭu kariya mukilkaḷ neṟṟi
taḻuvik

kiṭappap peruntaṉmiyoṭu vāṉ aḷāvit tōṉṟum maliyiṉi aṇṇāntu pārttavarāy avarkaḷ
accamum makiḻcciyum aṭiyum pōtu aṅkum pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉṟē aṟital vēṇṭum.

kātaliṉāṟ kaṭṭuṇṭa iḷiñarum makaḷirum nekiḻāta kātalaṉpiṉ mikutiyāl tōḷoṭu

tōḷ piṇiyat taḻuvikkoṇṭu, maliyaṭivārattil uḷḷa pūñcōlikaḷiṟ kaḷippāy ulavuntoṟun
tūṅkaṇaṅ kuruvikaḷ marakkiḷikaḷil viyappāṉa kūṭu kaṭṭutaliyum; marap
pontukaḷi liruntu maṇippuṟākkaḷ

kūvutaliyum; āṇmayilkaḷ tam aḻakiya tōkiyiṉi virittup peṭimayil kaṇṭukaḷippa
orupuṟam

āṭutaliyum; maliyiliruntoḻukum aruvinīr kūḻāṅ kaṟpaṭiyiṉmēṟ ciluciluveṉṟu
ōṭivantu

accōliyiṉ oru pakkattuḷḷa āḻnta kuṭṭattil nirampit tuḷumpa, ataṉkaṇ uḷḷa
centāmari mukiḻkaḷ

akaṉṟa ilikaḷiṉmēl itaḻkaḷi virittu mikac civappāy alar taliyum virumpikkaṇṭu,
naṟumaṇaṅ kamaḻum

pūkkaḷi maraṅkaḷiṉiṉṟun tāvip paṟittuk kariya kūntalil māṟimāṟi aṇintuñ, civakkap
paḻutta

kovvikkaṉi pōṉṟa tam itaḻkaḷ aḻunta muttam vittukkoṇṭun, tēṉ oḻukiṉāleṉa iṉiya

nēyamoḻikaḷ pēcikkoṇṭum avarkaḷ cellumiṭattu aṅkum pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉṟē aṟital
vēṇṭum.

curuṅkac colluṅkāl eṅkellām namatuṇarvik kavarkiṉṟa pēraḻaku ulaka iyaṟkiyiṟ

kāṇappaṭumō aṅkellām pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉpatu teḷiyappaṭum. āyiṉum, oru nallicip
pulavaṉāl

iyaṟṟappaṭukiṉṟa pāṭṭuppōla atu nūliṉiṭattē kāṇappaṭuvatilliyē yeṉiṉ; naṉku
viṉāyiṉāy, oru

nūliṉkaṇ eḻutappaṭṭu, ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉ aḻaki namatuḷḷattiṟ tōṉṟak kāṭṭi namakku
uvappuṇarvu payakkuñ colliṉ tokutiyāṉa pāṭṭu nūliṉkaṇ eḻutappaṭukiṉṟa
vaṭivuṭiya parupporuḷākum; ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉ aḻakōṭu oruṅkottu niṉṟu, kaṇ
mutalāṉa pulaṉvaḻip pukuntu namakku uvap puṇarvu
mikutiyiṉi varuvikkum pāṭṭu vaṭivam illāta nuṇporuḷākum. iṅṅaṉa mākaliṉ ulaka
iyaṟkiyilellām pāṭṭu uṇṭeṉpatu tuṇipēyā meṉka.

allāmalum, uyir vāḻnāḷil ovvoru nāḷum nammuṭiya niṉivukaḷellām uṇavu

tēṭutalilum, poruḷ tokuppatilum, maṉivimakkaṭku vēṇṭuvaṉa tiraṭṭik
koṭuttalilum, piṟar iṭṭa

ūḻiyañ ceytalilumākap palavāṟu citaṟi arumi perumiyiṉṟik koṉṉē kaḻintu pōkum
makkaḷuṭiya

niṉivukaḷuñ coṟkaḷuñ ceyalkaḷum namakku iṉpan tarāvākaliṉ avaṟṟi aṟiya
vēṇṭumeṉa virumpuvārum ulakil yārum ilar. iṉi, ivvāṟu kaḻiyum nāṭkaḷil orōvorukāl
avar aṟivu vaṟiya niṉivukaḷiṉ vēṟākap pirintu, ulaka iyaṟkiyiṟ paṭintu ataṉ
vaṇṇamāyt tirintu
teḷivuṟṟu viḷaṅkumpōtu avvaṟiviṟ curantu perukum ariya periya karuttukkaḷiyē
nām aṟitaṟku mika viḻikiṉṟōm. iṅṅaṉan tōṉṟum ariya periya karuttukkaḷiṉ kōvi
oḻuṅkiṉiyē pāṭṭeṉṟum aṟital vēṇṭum.

iṉṉum, makkaḷ vāḻnāḷ eṉkiṉṟa nīrōṭiyilē vaṟuniṉivukaḷāṉa kalaṅkaṟ perunīr

perukic cellumpōtu, ulaka iyaṟki yeṉṉum malik kukikaḷilē arittu eṭuttuvanta
aruṅkaruttukkaḷāṉa poṟṟukaḷ iṭiyiṭiyē āḻntu avvōṭiyiṉ aṭinilattiṟ citarntu
miṉṉikkiṭappa, nallicip pulavaṉ eṉṉum arippukkāraṉ mika viḻintu muyaṉṟu
appoṟcitarkaḷi yellām
oṉṟākap poṟukki eṭuttut taṉ matinuṭpa neruppiliṭṭu urukkip pacumpoṟ
piṇṭamākat tiraṭṭit taruvatē pāṭṭu eṉṟum aṟital vēṇṭum.

iṉṉum, makkaḷ aṟivu eṉkiṉṟa tittippāṉa ariya amiḻtam palavakiyāṉa kuṟṟaṅkaḷoṭuṅ

kalappuṟṟut tūyataṉṟāyp pōka, nallicippulavaṉ taṉ pēraṟiviṉāl ataṉit teḷiya vaṭittu
ataṉ

iṉcuviyiṉi mikutippaṭutti, nāmellām ataṉip parukip periyatōr āṟutalaṭiyak
koṭuppāṉ;

aṅṅaṉaṅ koṭukkappaṭun tūya iṉiya aṟiviṉ viḷakkamum pāṭṭeṉṟē aṟital vēṇṭum.

iṉi, iṅṅaṉam iyaṟṟappaṭukiṉṟa pāṭṭu ulaka iyaṟkiyaḻakuṭaṉ peritum porunti
naṭattal

vēṇṭum. iṉṉum itaṉi nuṇuki nōkkumiṭattup pāṭṭup pāṭutalil vallavaṉāṉa nallicip

pulavaṉukkum ulaka iyaṟkiyiṉip palavaki vaṇṇaṅkaḷāṟ kuḻittu varintu

āyiṉum, ōviyakkāraṉ varikiṉṟa ōviyaṅ kaṭpulaṉukku maṭṭumē tōṉṟuvatākum;
nallicip pulavaṉ amikkiṉṟa pāṭṭō kaṇ
mutalāṉa pulaṉkaḷiṉ akattē viḷaṅkum uḷḷattilē ceṉṟu tōṉṟuvatākum. ōviyakkāraṉ tāṉ
eḻuta eṭuttukkoṇṭa poruṭṭōṟṟattip paṉmuṟiyum nuṇṇitāka aḷantaḷantu pārttuppiṉ
ataṉit tiṟampaṭa varintāl maṭṭum aṅṅaṉam varinta ōviyattik kaṇṭu viyakkiṉṟōm;
tāṉ virittu
viḷakkamāy eḻutavēṇṭum pakutikaḷil avaṉ oru ciṟitu vaḻuviviṭṭāṉāyiṉum
avvōviyattiṉkaṇ namakku viyapput tōṉṟātoḻiyum. nallicippulavaṉō aṅṅaṉam avaṉippōl
ovvoṉṟaṉiyum virivāka viḷakkik kāṭṭa vēṇṭum varuttam uṭiyāṉ allaṉ. ōviyakkāraṉ
pulaṉ aṟivip paṟṟi niṟpavaṉ; pulavaṉō maṉa aṟivip paṟṟi niṟpavaṉ.

akattē niṉṟu nuṇṇitām poruḷiyun tāṉē oru noṭiyil virittaṟiyum āṟṟal vāyntatu!
ammamma!

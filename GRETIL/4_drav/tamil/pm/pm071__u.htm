maturikkanjci of mangkuTi marutanar
(work in pattuppaTTu anthologies) (in Tamil Script, TSCii format)
caṅka kāla nūlkaḷāṉa pattup pāṭṭukkaḷil āṟāvatāṉa
maturik kāñci
-----------------------

pāṭiyavar :: māṅkuṭi marutaṉār
pāṭappaṭṭavaṉ :: taliyālaṅkāṉattuc ceruveṉṟa pāṇṭiyaṉ
tiṇi :: kāñci
pāvaki :: āciriyappā
motta aṭikaḷ :: 782

-----------------------

ōṅku tiri viyaṉ parappiṉ
oli munnīr varam pākat
tēṉ tūṅkum uyar cimiya
mali nāṟiya viyaṉ ñālattu
vala mātirattāṉ vaḷi koṭpa
viya ṉāṇmī ṉeṟi yoḻukap
pakaṟ ceyyum ceñ ñāyiṟum
iravuc ceyyum veṇ tiṅkaḷum
mi tīrntu kiḷarntu viḷaṅka
maḻitoḻil utava mātiraṅ koḻukkat . . .10

toṭuppiṉ āyiram vittiyatu viḷiya
nilaṉu maraṉum payaṉetirpu nanta
nō yikantu nōkku viḷaṅka
mē taka mikap polinta
ōṅku nili vayak kaḷiṟu
kaṇṭu taṇṭāk kaṭkiṉ pattu
uṇṭu taṇṭā mikuvaḷat tāṉ
uyar pūrima viḻut teruviṟ
poy yaṟiyā vāymoḻi yāṟ
pukaḻ niṟinta naṉmānta roṭu . . .20

nal lūḻi aṭip paṭarap
pal veḷḷam mīk kūṟa
ulakam āṇṭa uyarntōr maruka
piṇak kōṭṭa kaḷiṟṟuk kuḻumpiṉ
niṇam vāyppeyta pēy makaḷir
iṇi yoliyimiḻ tuṇaṅkic cīrp
piṇi yūpam eḻun tāṭa
añcu vanta pōrkkaḷat tāṉ
āṇ ṭali aṇaṅ kaṭuppiṉ
vaya vēntar oṇ kuruti . . . .30

ciṉat tīyiṟ peyarpu poṅkat
teṟa laruṅ kaṭun tuppiṉ
viṟal viḷaṅkiya viḻuc cūrppiṉ
toṭit tōṭki tuṭup pāka
ā ṭuṟṟa ūṉ cōṟu
neṟi yaṟinta kaṭivā luvaṉ
aṭi yotuṅkip piṟ peyarāp
paṭi yōrkku muru kayara
amar kaṭakkum viyaṉ ṟāṉit
teṉṉavaṟ peyariya tuṉṉarun tuppiṉ . . .40

tolmutu kaṭavuṭ piṉṉar mēya
varittāḻ aruvip poruppiṟ poruna
viḻuc cūḻiya viḷaṅkō ṭiya
kaṭuñ ciṉatta kamaḻkaṭā attu
aḷaṟu paṭṭa naṟuñ ceṉṉiya
vari maruḷum uyar tōṉṟala
viṉi naviṉṟa pēr yāṉi
ciṉañ ciṟantu kaḷaṉu ḻakkavum
mā veṭutta malikurūut tukaḷ
akal vāṉattu veyil karappavum . . .50

vām pariya kaṭuntiṇ ṭēr
kāṟ ṟeṉṉak kaṭitu koṭpavum
vāḷ miku maṟa mintar
tōḷ muṟiyāṉ vīṟu muṟṟavum
iruperu vēntaroṭu vēḷir cāyap
poru tavaric ceru veṉṟum
ilaṅ karuviya vari nīntic
curam pōḻnta ika lāṟṟal
uyarn tōṅkiya viḻuc ciṟappiṉ
nilan tanta pē rutavip . . . .60

polantār mārpi ṉeṭiyōṉ umpal
maran tiṉṉūu vari yutirkkum
nari yurumiṉ ēṟa ṉiyi
aruṅ kuḻumiḷik kuṇṭuk kiṭaṅkiṉ
uyarn tōṅkiya nirip putaviṉ
neṭu matil niri ñāyil
am pumiḻ ayi laruppan
taṇ ṭātu talic ceṉṟu
koṇṭu nīṅkiya viḻuc ciṟappiṉ
teṉ kumari vaṭa peruṅkal . . . .70

kuṇa kuṭa kaṭalā vellit
toṉṟu moḻintu toḻil kēṭpa
veṟṟa moṭu veṟut toḻukiya
koṟṟa vartaṅ kōṉā kuvi
vāṉi yinta iru munnīrp
pēem niliiya irum peḷavattuk
koṭum puṇari vilaṅku pōḻak
kaṭuṅ kāloṭu kari cēra
neṭuṅ koṭimici iti yeṭuttu
iṉ ṉiciya muraca muḻaṅkap . . . .80

poṉ malinta viḻup paṇṭam
nā ṭāra naṉ kiḻitarum
āṭi yaṟ peru nāvāy
maḻi muṟṟiya mali puriyat
tuṟi muṟṟiya tuḷaṅ kirukkit
teṇ kaṭaṟ kuṇ ṭakaḻic
cīr cāṉṟa uyar nelliṉ
ūr koṇṭa uyar koṟṟava
nīrt tevvum nirit toḻuvar
pāṭu cilampu mici yēṟṟat . . . .90

tōṭu vaḻaṅkum aka lāmpiyiṟ
kaya ṉakiya vaya ṉiṟikku
meṉ ṟoṭi vaṉ kiḻāar
atari koḷpavar pakaṭupūṇ teṇmaṇi
irumpuḷ oppum iciyē eṉṟum
maṇippū muṇṭakattu maṇalmali kāṉaṟ
paratavar makaḷir kuraviyo ṭolippa
orucār, viḻavuniṉṟa viya lāṅkaṇ
muḻavut tōḷ muraṭ porunarkku
uru keḻu peruñ ciṟappiṉ . . . .100

iru peyarp pērā yamoṭu
ilaṅku maruppiṟ kaḷiṟu koṭuttum
polan tāmarip pūc cūṭṭiyum
nalañ cāṉṟa kalañ citaṟum
pal kuṭṭuvar vel kōvē!
kal kāyuṅ kaṭuvēṉi loṭu
iru vāṉam peyalo ḷippiṉum
varum vikal mīṉ piṟaḻiṉum
veḷḷamā ṟātu viḷiyuḷ peruka
nelli ṉōti arinar kampali . . .110

puḷḷimiḻn tolikkum iciyē eṉṟum
calam pukaṉṟu kaṟavuk kalitta
pulavu nīr viyaṉ peḷavattu
nilavuk kāṉal muḻavut tāḻik
kuḷirp potumpar naḷit tūval
niritimil vēṭṭuvar karicēr kampali
iruṅkaḻic ceṟuviṉ veḷḷuppup pakarnaroṭu
oli yōvāk kali yāṇar
mutu veḷḷili mīk kūṟum
viyaṉ mēval viḻuc celvattu . . . .120

iru vakiyāṉ ici cāṉṟa
ciṟu kuṭip perun toḻuvar
kuṭi keḻīiya nāṉilava roṭu
toṉṟu moḻintu toḻil kēṭpak
kā leṉṉak kaṭitu rāay
nāṭu keṭa eri parappi
ālaṅ kāṉat tañcuvara viṟuttu
aracu paṭa amaru ḻakki
muracu koṇṭu kaḷam vēṭṭa
aṭu tiṟaluyar pukaḻ vēntē . . . .130

naṭṭavar kuṭi yuyark kuvi
ceṟṟavar aracu peyark kuvi
pēru lakattu mēen tōṉṟic
cīru ṭiya viḻuc ciṟappiṉ
viḷintu mutirnta viḻu muttiṉ
ilaṅku vaḷi iruñ cērik
kaṭ koṇṭik kuṭip pākkattu
naṟ koṟki yōrnacip poruna
ceṟṟa tevvar kalaṅkat talicceṉṟu
añcuvarat taṭkum aṇaṅkuṭit tuppiṟ . . .140

kōḻū uṉkuṟik koḻu valcip
pulavu viṟ poli kūvi
oṉṟu moḻi oli yiruppil
teṉ paratavar pō rēṟē
ariya vellām eḷitiṉiṟ koṇṭu
uriya vellām ōmpātu vīci
naṉipukaṉ ṟuṟitu meṉṉā tēṟṟeḻuntu
paṉivār cimiyak kāṉam pōki
akanāṭu pukkavar viruppam veḷavi
yāṇṭupala kaḻiya vēṇṭupulat tiṟuttu . . .150

mēmpaṭa marīiya velpōrk kurucil
uṟu ceṟunar pulam pukkavar
kaṭi kāvi ṉili tolicci
iḻi paṟiyāp peruntaṇ paṇi
kurūuk koṭiya eri mēya
nā ṭeṉum pēr kāṭāka
ā cēnta vaḻi mācēppa
ūri runta vaḻi pāḻāka
ilaṅku vaḷi maṭa maṅkiyar
tuṇaṅki yañcīrt taḻūu maṟappa . . .160

avi yirunta perum potiyiṟ
kavi yaṭik kaṭu nōkkattup
pēy makaḷir peyar pāṭa
aṇaṅku vaḻaṅku makalāṅ kaṇ
nilat tāṟṟuṅ kuḻūup putaviṉ
arantip peṇṭir iṉintaṉar akavak
koḻum patiya kuṭi tēmpac
ceḻuṅ kēḷir niḻal cēra
neṭunakar vīḻnta karikutirp paḷḷik
kuṭumik kūki kurāloṭu muralak . . .170

kaḻunīr polinta kaṇṇakaṉ poykik
kaḷiṟumāy ceruntiyoṭu kaṇpamaṉ ṟūrtara
nallēr naṭanta nacicāl viḷivayal
paṉmayirp piṇavoṭu kēḻal ukaḷa
vāḻā miyiṉ vaḻitavak keṭṭup
pāḻā yiṉaniṉ pakivar tēem
eḻāat tōḷ imiḻmu ḻakkiṉ
māat tāḷ uyar maruppiṟ
kaṭuñ ciṉatta kaḷiṟu parappi
viri kaṭal viyaṉ ṟāṉiyoṭu . . .180

muru kuṟaḻap pakittalic ceṉṟu
akal vicumpiṉ ārp pimiḻap
peya luṟaḻak kaṇi citaṟip
pala puravi nī ṟukippa
vaḷi narala vayi rārppap
pī ṭaḻiyak kaṭan taṭṭavar
nā ṭaḻiyak eyil veḷavic
cuṟṟa moṭu tū vaṟuttaliṟ
ceṟṟa tevvar niṉvaḻi naṭappa
viyaṉkaṇ mutupoḻil maṇṭila muṟṟi . . .190

araciyal piḻiyā taṟaneṟi kāṭṭip
periyōr ceṉṟa aṭivaḻip piḻiyātu
kuṭamutal tōṉṟiya toṉṟutoḻu piṟiyiṉ
vaḻivaḻic ciṟakkaniṉ valampaṭu koṟṟam
kuṇamutal tōṉṟiya āriruḷ matiyiṉ
tēyvaṉa keṭukaniṉ tevvar ākkam
uyarnili ulakam amiḻtoṭu peṟiṉum
poycēṇ nīṅkiya vāynaṭ piṉiyē
muḻaṅkukaṭa lēṇi malartali yulakamoṭu
uyarnta tēettu viḻumiyōr variṉum . . .200

pakivark kañcip paṇintoḻu kaliyē
teṉpula maruṅkiṉ viṇṭu niṟiya
vāṇaṉ vitta viḻuniti peṟiṉum
paḻinamak keḻuka eṉṉāy viḻuniti
ītal uḷḷamoṭu icivēṭ kuviyē
aṉṉāy niṉṉoṭu muṉṉili evaṉō
koṉṉoṉṟu kiḷakkuval aṭupōr aṇṇal
kēṭṭiciṉ vāḻi keṭukaniṉ avalam
keṭātu niliiyarniṉ cēṇviḷaṅku nallici
tavāp perukkat taṟā yāṇar . . . .210

aḻit tāṉāk koḻun tiṟṟi
iḻit tāṉāp pala coṉṟi
uṇ ṭāṉāk kūr naṟavil
tiṉ ṟāṉā iṉa vika
ṉilaṉeṭuk kallā voṇpal veṟukkip
payaṉaṟa vaṟiyā vaḷaṅkeḻu tirunakar
narampiṉ muralu nayamvaru muraṟci
viṟaliyar vaṟuṅkik kuṟuntoṭi ceṟippap
pāṇar uvappa kaḷiṟupala tarīik
kalantō ruvappa veyiṟpala kaṭii . . .220

maṟaṅ kalaṅkat talic ceṉṟu
vāḷuḻan tataṉ tāḷ vāḻtti
nā ḷīṇṭiya nal lakavarkkut
tē rōṭu mā citaṟic
cū ṭuṟṟa cuṭarp pūviṉ
pāṭu pularnta naṟuñ cāntiṉ
viḻumiya periyōr cuṟṟa mākak
kaḷḷiṉ irumpik kalañcela vuṇṭu
paṇintōr tēen tamvaḻi naṭappap
paṇiyār tēem paṇittuttiṟi koṇmār . .230

paruntupaṟak kallāp pārvaṟ pācaṟip
paṭukaṇ muracaṅ kāli yiyampa
veṭipaṭak kaṭantu vēṇṭupulat tiṟutta
paṇikeḻu peruntiṟaṟ palvēl maṉṉar
kariporu tiraṅkum cuṉiyiru munnīrt
tiriyiṭu maṇaliṉum palarē uricela
malartali yulakam āṇṭukaḻin tōrē
ataṉāl, kuṇakaṭal koṇṭu kuṭakaṭalmuṟṟi
iravu melliyum viḷiviṭa ṉaṟiyātu
avalu miciyu nīrttiraḷ pīṇṭik . . .240

kavaliyaṅ kuḻumpiṉ aruvi olippak
kaḻivaḷar cāraṟ kaḷiṟṟiṉa naṭuṅka
varimuta liraṅkum ēṟoṭu vāṉñemirntu
citaraṟ perumpeyal ciṟattaliṟ ṟāṅkātu
kuṇakaṭaṟ kivartaruṅ kurūuppuṉa lunti
nivantuce ṉīttaṅ kuḷaṅkoḷac cāṟṟik
kaḷiṟu māykkuṅ katirk kaḻaṉi
oḷi ṟilañci aṭi nivanta
muṭ ṭāḷa cuṭart tāmari
kaṭ kamaḻu naṟu neytal . . . .250

vaḷ ḷitaḻ aviḻ nīlam
mel lili yari yāmpaloṭu
vaṇṭiṟi koṇṭa kamaḻpūm poykik
kampuṭ cēval iṉṟuyil iriya
vaḷḷi nīkki vayamīṉ mukantu
koḷḷi cāṟṟiya koṭumuṭi valiñar
vēḻap paḻaṉattu nūḻi lāṭṭuk
karumpi ṉentiraṅ kaṭpi ṉōti
aḷḷaṟ ṟaṅkiya pakaṭuṟu viḻumaṅ
kaḷḷār kaḷamar peyarkkum ārppē . . .260

olinta pakaṉṟi viḷinta kaḻaṉi
vaṉki viṉiñar aripaṟi yiṉkural
taḷimaḻi poḻiyun taṇparaṅ kuṉṟiṟ
kavikoḷ cummi yoliko ḷāyan
tatinta kōti tāroṭu poliyap
puṇarntuṭa ṉāṭum iciyē yaṉittum
akaliru vāṉat timiḻntiṉi ticippak
kuruku narala maṉi marattāṉ
mīṉ cīvum pāṇ cēriyoṭu
marutañ cāṉṟa taṇpaṇi cuṟṟiorucārc . .270

ciṟutiṉi koyyak kavvi kaṟuppak
karuṅkāl varakiṉ iruṅkural pulara
āḻnta kuḻumpil tirumaṇi kiḷara
eḻunta kaṭaṟṟi ṉaṉpoṉ koḻippap
peruṅkaviṉ peṟṟa ciṟutali neḷavi
maṭakkaṭ piṇiyoṭu maṟukuvaṉa ukaḷac
cuṭarppūṅ koṉṟi tāaya nīḻaṟ
pāa yaṉṉa pāṟi yaṇintu
nīlat taṉṉa pimpayir micitoṟum
veḷḷi yaṉṉa voḷvi yutirntu . . .280

curimukiḻ mucuṇṭiyoṭu mulli tāay
maṇimaru ṇeytal uṟaḻak kāmar
tuṇinīr mellavaṟ ṟoyyiloṭu malara
valloṉ tiiya veṟikkaḷaṅ kaṭuppa
mulli cāṉṟa puṟavaṇin torucār
naṟuṅkāḻ koṉṟu kōṭṭiṉ vittiya
kuṟuṅkatirt tōri neṭuṅkāl aiyavi
aivaṉa veṇṇelo ṭarilkoḷpu nīṭi
iñci mañcaṭ piṅkaṟi piṟavum
palvēṟu tāramoṭu kallakat tīṇṭit . . .290

tiṉiviḷi cāraṟ kiḷikaṭi pūcal
maṇippū avarik kurūuttaḷir mēyum
āmā kaṭiyuṅ kāṉavar pūcal
cēṇōṉ akaḻnta maṭivāyp payampiṉ
vīḻmukak kēḻal aṭṭa pūcal
karuṅkāl vēṅki iruñciṉip poṅkar
naṟumpūk koyyum pūcal iruṅkēḻ
ēṟaṭu vayappulip pūcalo ṭaṉittum
ilaṅkuveḷ ḷaruviyoṭu cilampakat tiraṭṭak
karuṅkāṟ kuṟiñci cāṉṟa veṟpaṇintu . . .300

aruṅkaṭi māmali taḻīi orucār
iruvetirp pintūṟu kūreri nippa
niḻatta yāṉi mēypulam paṭarak
kalitta iyavar iyantoṭ ṭaṉṉa
kaṇviṭu puṭiyūut taṭṭi kaviṉaḻintu
aruvi yāṉṟa aṇiyil māmali
vikaṇ ṭaṉṉa puṉmuḷi yaṅkāṭṭuk
kamaḻcūḻ kōṭi viṭaraka mukantu
kāluṟu kaṭaliṉ olikkuñ cummi
ilivēy kurampi uḻiyataṭ paḷḷi . . .310

uvalik kaṇṇi vaṉcol iḷiñar
ciliyuṭik kiyar kavali kāppa
niḻaluru viḻanta vēṉiṟkuṉ ṟattup
pāli cāṉṟa curañcērn torucār
muḻaṅkukaṭal tanta viḷaṅkukatir muttam
arampōḻn taṟutta kaṇṇēr ilaṅkuvaḷi
paratar tanta palvēṟu kūlam
iruṅkaḻic ceṟuviṟ ṟīmpuḷi veḷḷuppup
parantōṅku varippiṉ vaṉkit timilar
koḻumīṉ kuṟiiya tuṭikkaṇ tuṇiyal . . .320

viḻumiya nāvāy perunī rōccunar
naṉantali tēettu naṉkalaṉ uymmār
puṇarntuṭaṉ koṇarnta puraviyo ṭaṉittum
vikal tōṟum vaḻivaḻic ciṟappa
neytal cāṉṟa vaḷampala payiṉṟāṅku
aimpāl tiṇiyuṅ kaviṉi yamivara
muḻa vīmiḻum aka lāṅkaṇ

tērvaḻaṅku teruvi ṉīrtiraṇ ṭoḻuka
maḻiyamin tuṟṟa arinā ḷamayamum
acivilar eḻuntu nayamvantu vaḻaṅkaliṟ . . .650

kaṭavuḷ vaḻaṅkuṅ kiyāṟu kaṅkulum
acca maṟiyā tēma mākiya
maṟṟi yāmam pakaluṟak kaḻippip
pōtupiṇi viṭṭa kamaḻnaṟum poykit
tātuṇ tumpi pōtu muraṉṟāṅ
kōta lantaṇar vētam pāṭac
cīriṉitu koṇṭu narampiṉi tiyakki
yāḻōr marutam paṇṇak kāḻōr
kaṭuṅkaḷiṟu kavaḷaṅ kippa neṭuntērp
paṇinilip puravi pulluṇāt teviṭṭap . . .660

palvēṟu paṇṇiyak kaṭimeḻuk kuṟuppak
kaḷḷōr kaḷitoṭi nuvala illōr
nayanta kātalar kavavuppiṇit tuñcip
pularntuviri viṭiya leyta virumpik
kaṇporā veṟikku miṉṉukkoṭi puriya
oṇpo ṉaviriḻi teḻippa iyalit
tiṇcuvar nalliṟ katavaṅ kariya
uṇṭumakiḻ taṭṭa maḻali nāviṟ
paḻañceruk kāḷar taḻaṅkukural tōṉṟac
cūtar vāḻnta mākatar nuvala . . . .670

vētā ḷikaroṭu nāḻiki icippa
imiḻmura ciraṅka ēṟumāṟu cilippap
poṟimayir vāraṇam vikaṟi iyampa
yāṉiyaṅ kurukiṉ cēvaloṭu kāmar
aṉṉaṅ kariya aṇimayil akavap
piṭipuṇar peruṅkaḷiṟu muḻaṅka muḻuvalik
kūṭṭuṟi vayamāp puliyoṭu kuḻuma
vāṉa nīṅkiya nīṉiṟa vicumpiṉ
miṉṉunimirn taṉiya rāki naṟavumakiḻntu
māṇiḻi makaḷir pulantaṉar parinta . . .680

parūukkā ḻārañ corinta muttamoṭu
poṉcuṭu neruppi ṉilamuk keṉṉa
ammeṉ kurumpik kāypaṭupu piṟavun
tarumaṇaṉ muṟṟat tariñimi ṟārppa
meṉpūñ cemmaloṭu naṉkalañ cīppa
iravuttalip peyaru mēma vikaṟi
mipaṭu peruntōḷ maḻava rōṭṭi
iṭippulat toḻinta ēntukōṭ ṭiyāṉi
pakippulaṅ kavarnta pāyparip puravi
vēlkō lāka āḷcela nūṟik . . .690

kāyciṉa muṉpiṟ kaṭuṅkaṭ kūḷiyar
ūrcuṭu viḷakkiṟ ṟanta āyamum
nāṭuṭi nalleyil aṇaṅkuṭit tōṭṭi
nāṭoṟum viḷaṅkak kitoḻūup paḻicci
nāṭara vanta viḻukkala maṉittum
kaṅkiyam pēriyāṟu kaṭaṟpaṭarn tāṅku
aḷantukaṭi yaṟiyā vaḷaṅkeḻu tāramoṭu
puttē ḷulakam kaviṉik kāṇvara
mikkuppukaḻ eytiya perumpeyar maturic
ciṉitali maṇanta curumpupaṭu centī . . .700

oṇpūm piṇṭi aviḻnta kāviṟ
cuṭarpoḻin tēṟiya viḷaṅkukatir ñāyiṟṟu
ilaṅkukati riḷaveyiṟ ṟōṉṟi yaṉṉa
tamaṉiyam vaḷiiya tāvil viḷaṅkiḻi
nilamviḷak kuṟuppa mētakap polintu
mayilō raṉṉa cāyal māviṉ
taḷirē raṉṉa mēṉit taḷirppuṟattu
īrkki ṉarumpiya titaliyar kūreyiṟ
ṟoṇkuḻi puṇariya vaṇṭāḻ kātiṟ
kaṭavuṭ kayattamaṉṟa cuṭaritaḻt tāmarit . . .710

tātupaṭu perumpōtu puriyum vāṇmukat
tāytoṭi makaḷir naṟuntōḷ puṇarntu
kōtiyiṟ polinta cēkkit tuñcit
tiruntutuyil eṭuppa iṉiti ṉeḻuntu
tiṇkā ḻāra nīvik katirviṭu
moṇkāḻ āraṅ kaviiya mārpiṉ
varikkaṭip piraca mūcuvaṉa moyppa
eruttan tāḻnta viravuppūn teriyaṟ
polañceyap polinta nalampeṟu viḷakkam
valikeḻu taṭakkit toṭiyoṭu cuṭarvarac . .720

cōṟami vuṟṟa nīruṭik kaliṅkam
uṭiyaṇi poliyak kuṟiviṉṟu kavii
vallōṉ tiiya varippuṉi pāvi
murukiyaṉ ṟaṉṉa uruviṉi yāki
varupuṉaṟ kaṟciṟi kaṭuppa viṭiyaṟuttu
oṉṉā rōṭṭiya ceruppukal maṟavar
vāḷvaḷam puṇarntaniṉ tāḷvalam vāḻtta
villik kaviik kaṇitāṅku mārpiṉ
mātāṅ keṟuḻttōḷ maṟavart tammiṉ
kalliṭit tiyaṟṟiya iṭṭuvāyk kiṭaṅkiṉ . . .730

nalleyi luḻanta celvart tammiṉ
kollēṟṟup pintōl cīvātu pōrtta
mākkaṇ muracam ōvila kaṟaṅka
erinimirn taṉṉa tāṉi nāppaṇ
perunal yāṉi pōrkkaḷat toḻiya
viḻumiya vīḻnta kuricilart tammiṉ
puriyōrkkut toṭutta polampūn tumpi
nīryār eṉṉātu muṟikarutupu cūṭṭik
kāḻmaṇ ṭeḵkamoṭu kaṇiyalik kalaṅkip
piripiṇi yarinta niṟañciti kavayattu . . .740

vāṉat taṉṉa vaḷanakar poṟpa
nōṉkuṟaṭ ṭaṉṉa ūṉcāy mārpiṉ
uyarnta utavi ūkkalart tammiṉ
nivanta yāṉik kaṇaniri kavarnta
pularnta cāntiṉ viravuppūn teriyaṟ
peruṟcey āṭavart tammiṉ piṟarum
yāvarum varuka ēṉōrun tammeṉa
variyā vāyiṟ ceṟāa tiruntu
pāṇar varuka pāṭṭiyar varuka
yāṇarp pulavaroṭu vayiriyar varukeṉa . . .750

iruṅkiḷi purakkum iravalark kellām
koṭuñci neṭuntēr kaḷiṟṟoṭum vīcik
kaḷan tōṟum kaḷ ḷarippa
maran tōṟu mi vīḻppa
niṇa vūṉcuṭ ṭuruk kamiya
ney kaṉintu vaṟi yārppak
kurūuk kuyppuki maḻi maṅkuliṟ
parantu tōṉṟā viya ṉakarāṟ
pal cāli mutu kuṭumiyiṉ
nal vēḷvit tuṟi pōkiya . . . .760

tol lāṇi nal lāciriyar
puṇarkūṭ ṭuṇṭa pukaḻcāl ciṟappiṉ
nilantaru tiruvi ṉeṭiyōṉ pōla
viyappuñ cālpuñ cemmi cāṉṟōr
palarvāyp pukaraṟu ciṟappiṟ ṟōṉṟi
ariya tantu kuṭi yakaṟṟip
periya kaṟ ṟici viḷakki
munnīr nāppaṇ ñāyiṟu pōlavum
paṉmīṉ naṭuvaṇ tiṅkaḷ pōlavum
pūtta cuṟṟamoṭu polintiṉitu viḷaṅkip . . .770
poyyā nallici niṟutta puṉitārp
perumpeyar māṟaṉ taliva ṉākak
kaṭantaṭu vāyvāḷ iḷampal kōcar
iyaṉeṟi marapiṉiṉ vāymoḻi kēṭpap
polampūṇ aivar uṭpaṭap pukaḻnta
maṟamiku ciṟappiṟ kuṟunila maṉṉa
ravarum piṟakum tuvaṉṟip
poṟpuviḷaṅku pukaḻavi niṟpukaḻn tētta
ilaṅkiḻi makaḷir polaṅkalat tēntiya
maṇaṅkamaḻ tēṟal maṭuppa nāḷum . . .780

makiḻntiṉi tuṟimati perum
varintunī peṟṟa nallū ḻiyiyē.

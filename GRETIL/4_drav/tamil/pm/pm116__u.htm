1. vikkirama cōḻaṉulā


cīrtanta tāmariyāḷ kēḷvaṉ ṟiruvuruk
kārtanta vuntik kamalattup - pārtanta 1

ātik kaṭavuṭ ṭicimukaṉu māṅkavaṉṟaṉ
kātaṟ kulamintaṉ kācipaṉum - mētakka 2

mintaṉi yūrnta maṟavōṉum - pintaṭat 4

kūṭanī rūṭṭiya koṟṟavaṉum - nīṭiya 5

pōka puripurinta pūpatiyum -mākattuk 6

kūṟa variya maṉukkoṇarntu kūṟṟukkut
tēṟa vaḻakkuritta cempiyaṉum - māṟaḻin 7

tōṭi maṟali yoḷippa mutumakkaṭ
cāṭi vakutta tarāpatiyum - kūṭārtam 8

tūṅku meyileṟinta cōḻaṉu mēlkaṭalil
vīṅkunīr kīḻkaṭaṟku viṭṭōṉum - āṅkup 9

pilamataṉiṟ pukkuttaṉ pēroḷiyā ṉākar
kulamakaḷik kippiṭitta kōvum - ulakaṟiyak 10

kākkuñ ciṟapuṟavuk kākak kaḷikūrntu
tūkkun tulipukka tūyōṉum - mēkkuyarak 11

koḷḷuṅ kuṭakak kuvaṭū ṭaṟuttiḻiyak
taḷḷun tirippoṉṉi tantōṉum - teḷḷaruvic 12

ceṉṉip puliyē ṟiruttik kiritirittup
poṉṉik karikaṇṭa pūpatiyum -iṉṉaruḷiṉ 13

mētakka poyki kavikoṇṭu villavaṉip
pātat taḷiviṭṭa pārttivaṉum -mītelām 14

eṇkoṇṭa toṇṇūṟṟiṉ mēlu mirumūṉṟu
puṇkoṇṭa veṉṟip puravalaṉum - kaṇkoṇṭa 15

kōtilāt tēṟal kuṉikkun tirumaṉṟam
kātalāṟ poṉvēynta kāvalaṉum - tūtaṟkāp 16

paṇṭu pakaloṉṟi līroṉ patucuramum
koṇṭu malināṭu koṇṭōṉum - taṇṭēvik 17

kaṅkā natiyuṅ kaṭāramuṅ kikkoṇṭu
ciṅkā taṉattirunta cempiyaṉum - vaṅkatti 18

muṟṟu muraṇaṭakki mummaṭipōyk kalyāṇi
ceṟṟa taṉiyāṇmic cēvakaṉum - paṟṟalari 19

veppat taṭukaḷattu vēḻaṅka ḷāyiramum
koppat torukaḷiṟṟāṟ koṇṭōṉum - appaḻanūla 20

pāṭavarat teṉṉaraṅka mēyāṟkup paṉmaṇiyāl
āṭavarap pāya lamittōṉum - kūṭala 21

caṅkamattuk koḷḷun taṉipparaṇik keṇṇiṟanta
tuṅkamata yāṉi tuṇittōṉum - aṅkavaṉpiṉ 22

kāval purintavaṉi kāntōṉu meṉṟivarkaḷ
pūvalaya muṟṟum purantataṟpiṉ - mēvalartam 23

cēlit turantuciliyit taṭintirukāl
cālik kaḷamaṟutta taṇṭiṉāṉ - mēlik 24

kaṭalkoṇṭu koṅkaṇamuṅ kaṉṉaṭamuṉ kikkoṇ
ṭaṭalkoṇṭa mārāṭ rāṉi - uṭali 25

iṟakki vaṭavariyē yelliyāt tolli
maṟakkaliyuñ cuṅkamu māṟṟi - aṟattikiri 26

vārip puvaṉam valamāka vantaḷikkum
āriṟ politō ḷapayaṟkup - pārviḷaṅkat 27

tōṉṟiyakōṉ vikkirama cōḻaṉ ṟoṭittumpi
mūṉṟu muracu mukiṉmuḻaṅka - nōṉṟaliya 28

mummip puvaṉam purakka muṭikavittuc
cemmit taṉikkō ṟiciyaḷappat - tammi 29

viṭavuṭ paṭuttu viḻukkaviki yeṭṭuk
kaṭavuṭ kaḷiṟu kavippac - cuṭarcēr 30

iṇittār makuṭa miṟakki yaracar
tuṇittā ḷapiṭēkañ cūṭap - paṇittēṟu 31

nīrāḻi yēḻu nilavāḻi yēḻuntaṉ
pōrāḻi yoṉṟāṟ potunīkkic - cīrārum 32

mēy tikiri virimē kaliyalkuṟ
ṟūya nilamaṭanti tōḷkaḷiṉum - cāyaliṉ 33

ōtu mulakaṅka ḷēḻuṅ taṉittuṭiya
kōtil kulamaṅki koṅkiyiṉum - pōtil 34

niṟikiṉṟa celvi neṭuṅkaṇ kaḷiṉum
uṟikiṉṟa nāḷi lorunāḷ - aṟikaḻaṟkāṟ 35

ṟeṉṉar tiṟiyaḷanta muttiṟ cilapūṇṭu
teṉṉar maliyārac cēṟaṇintu - teṉṉar 36

varaviṭṭa teṉṟa laṭivaruṭa vāṭkaṇ
poraviṭṭa pērāyam pōṟṟa - viraviṭṭa 37

nittilap pantarkkīḻ nīṇilāp pāyaliṉmēl
tottalar mālit tuṇittōḷum - mintaṭaṅ 38

kaṇṇu muliyum periya kaḷiyaṉṉam
eṇṇu mulakaṅka ḷēḻuṭiya - peṇṇaṇaṅku 39

peyta malarōtip peṇcakra varttiyuṭaṉ
eytiya paḷḷi yiṉiteḻuntu - poyyāta 40

poṉṉit tirumañ caṉamāṭip pūcurarkik
kaṉṉit taḷiraṟukiṉ kāppaṇintu - muṉṉi 41

maṟikkoḻunti veḷḷi malikkoḻunti mōlip
piṟikkoḻunti vitta pirāṉik - kaṟikkaḷattuc 42

mukkaṭ kaṉiyi muṭivaṇaṅki - mikkuyarnta 43

alaṅkāraṅkaḷ ceytukoḷḷutal

tāṉat tuṟimuṭittuc cāttun takimiyaṉa
māṉak kalaṉkaḷ varavaruḷit - tēṉmoyttuc 44

tāḻu makarak kuḻitayaṅka - vāḻum 45

cuṭarmaṇik kēyūrañ cūḻap - paṭarum 46

maṇikkaṭakaṅ kiyil vayaṅkap - piṇippiṉ 47

muyaṅkun tiruvuṭaṉē munnīr koṭutta
vayaṅku maṇimārpiṉ malka - uyaṅkā 48

aruṅkoṟṟa mākku maṇaṅki ṉuṭaṉē
maruṅkiṟ ṟiruvuṭivāḷ vāyppap - poruntiya 49

aṇṇaṟ paṭivat tarumpē raṇiyaṇintu
vaṇṇat taḷavil vaṉappamintu - kaṇṇutalōṉ 50

kāmaṉ cilivaṇaṅka vāṅkiya kaṭṭaḻaku
tāma muṭivaṇaṅkat tantaṉiya - kāmarupūṅ 51

kālat tatiruṅ kaṭākkaḷiṟu - ñālattut 52

muṟṟap parittataṟpiṉ muṉputām - uṟṟa 59

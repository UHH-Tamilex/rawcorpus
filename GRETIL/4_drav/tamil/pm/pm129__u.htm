tiruvāṭutuṟi ātīṉattu mahāvitvāṉ
tiricirapuram śrī mīṉāṭcicuntaram piḷḷiyavarkaḷiṉ
pirapantattiraṭṭu - mutal pākam
mūlam:
tirukkilāyaparamparit tiruvāṭutuṟiyātīṉattu mahāvitvāṉ tiricirapuram śrī mīṉāṭcicuntaram
piḷḷiyavarkaḷ pirapantattiraṭṭu, itu mēṟpaṭi ātīṉattut talivarkaḷākiya śrīlaśrī vittiyaliṅka
tēcikaravarkaḷ utaviyikkoṇṭu, mēṟpaṭi piḷḷiyavarkaḷ māṇākkarum, citamparam
mīṉāṭci tamiḻkkālēj piriṉspālumākiya u.vē. cāminātiyarāl ceṉṉi kamarṣiyal
accukkūṭattiṟ patikkappeṟṟatu. iraṇṭām patippu, 1926.

uḷḷaṭakkam


1. tirutturuttik kacci viṉāyakar patikam (ceyyuḷ 1- 10)
2. tirutturuttic cuppiramaṇiyasvāmi patikam (ceyyuḷ 11 - 20)
3. tiruviṭimarutūr marutavāṇar tōttirappatikam (ceyyuḷ 21 - 30)
4. tirupperumaṇanallūrt tiruveṇṇīṟṟumi piḷḷittamiḻ (ceyyuḷ 31 - 133)



1. tirutturuttik kacci viṉāyakar patikam


(itu cōḻanāṭṭiṟ kāvirinatiyiṉteṉpāluḷḷa tiruppatikaḷuḷ oṉṟu. kuṟṟālameṉa ikkālattuvaḻaṅkum)

1.
māmēvu pavaḷā calakkuvaṭ ṭutayameḻu maḻavilaṅ katirnikarttu
maṇimeḷali poliyalav varimuṭiyi ṉirupālu maṇikoḻit tiḻiyaruliyiṟ
pamēvu karuñimi ṟirippak kapōlam paruttuviṭu tāṉamoḻukap
paṉimatip piriyoṉ ṟaṭuttamarn tāleṉap pakaroru maruppilaṅkat
tēmēvu malariṟit taṭiyarcaya cayaveṉṟu ticitoru muḻakkeṭuppac
ceṟuvalik karumūṭi kattivarn tiyāṅkaṇmakiḻ tēkkavan taruḷ purikuvāy
kāmēvu taṇṇaḷi yamiyintaṭiyu narppātu kāttaṟam vaḷarttiṭutalāṟ
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

2.
kulavutava muṉivararka ḷamarun tavaccāli kuvalayam paṭitarāmē
kūṟumaccāliyuṭ civā kamntīṭṭu kōcaṅkaḷ cititarāmē
nilavutaḻal paṭukuṇṭam vētimē kaliyoṭu nitamputi tiyaṟṟuṟāmē
nittiyamu ṉārayakaru mampalavu mattavar nikaḻttaliṟ kuṟivarāmē
pulavukama ḻaṅkucam pācaṉami yēliyatu pōtumeṉ ṟuvakimaliyap
poṅkuñ ciṉattulavu karumūṭi kattup porukkeṉa vivarntupalavuṅ
kalavupala vaṇṭat tulāvarun tiṟaluṭik karuṇiyā maṟiya valamō
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

3.
ētiyeṉa vevvaḷa viruppaṉa vavaṟṟā liruttalcey yātavaramumi
miyava ruṭampumuta lettuṇi yuṭampavi irintiṭaṟ kāyavaramu,
nītitaru maṭṭāṅka pañcāiṅka mutaliya nikaḻttuvan taṉivilakku
nikaḻumaṟ ṟuḷḷavu niṉintumic ciravuruva nilavamaṟ ṟoṉṟamintu,
cōtitaru kōṭoṉṟu koṇṭukaya mukaṉvali tolittuliṟa lūrtiyākkiyic
cōrvilava ṉamarar pāṟ peṟṟaviyu mēṟṟaruḷ curantupal luyirceyviṉiyik,
kātinalamēyutavu niṉatuperu māṇpevar karuttamit totavallār
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

4.
ēṟṟamiku morumiṉiva ṉōmpaḻaṟ kuḻināppa ṇiriyāta kiriyiñāṉa
meṉṉumiru cattika ḷoṭuntōṉṟi yammuṉiyo ṭēṟṟamar purintupalaruñ,
cāṟṟavoḷir puravumaṇi kikkoṇṭa māṟṟalaṉ ṟaṉvali yaṭaṅkāpperun
tāṉivali yoṭukavarn tammaṇiyum vāṅkiyat tāpataṟ kiṉitaḷikkat,
tēṟṟamata ṉālvara luṇarntaṉiya tavaṉuñ ciṟantamār piṟṟarittuc
cintā maṇikkuñca rappirā ṉeṉṟēttal ceyyalavaṉ viṉiyaṉittuṅ,
kāṟṟaveḻu maruḷpoḻin tiṉitamaru māṭalik kaṇṭuviṇ ṭiṭavallarār
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

5.
teḷḷamuta mayamāka vumiyammi nalkiya tiruntumō takanñcamāyt
tīyakoṭu nañcamaya mākavoru pētitaru ceḻunāva laṅkaṉikaṭā
meḷḷalaru mamutamāyt tāṉpurita ṟavaṟā tiyaṟṟiṭak kaṇṭōmiṉu
miyalutta māṅkamvē ṟākavum pārkkavu mirucevika ḷāṟkēṭkavu
muḷḷaporu ḷituveṉṟu vāypēca vumpiṟa vuñaṟṟavu miyaṅkalceyavu
muṇmiyiṟ kaṇṭuḷē meṉṉiṉevar niṉṉāṭa luṭkoṇ ṭaḷakka vallār
kaḷḷamakaṉ mātavark kāṉanta māypparavu kaṟpaṉi kaṭantavāḻvē
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

6.
entavā ṉavararuḷ peṟakkaruti ṉālumuṉi yēttivaḻi paṭuvareḻuvā
yēttivaḻi paṭalceyā reṉṉiṉava reṇṇiya tiḻantumiṭi yūṟuṟuvarāṉ
muntavār vamperuku māṟuni ṉaṭipppūci muṟṟum purintupōṟṟiṉ
muṉṉiya vaṉittumuṟ ṟātavala vēyinta muṟiminā ṉuṇartalālē
pantamā malakaṉma māyiyoru mūṉṟiyum pāṟṟiniṉ ṉaṟivutoḻilām
pātaniḻal pukkiṉpa muṟumāṟu niṉṉiyē paṟṟit tutittalceyvēṉ
kantamā malariṭuka reyppilvip pēpukaḻnar kaḷikaṇē yamalavāḻvē
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

7.
maruvupala puvaṉattu mentavā ṉalarvaṭivu maṇcili vaṭuttāramoṇ
mācika ḷivaṟṟuḷoṉ ṟāṟceytu vayiṟaḷaviṉ maruvāma liiḵkumeṉṉiṉ ,
veruvumika vāyntnta vaṭivaruc caṉiceyār viṭuvarav vāṟutērtal
vēṇṭātu pērutaram vāyntaniṉ ṟiruvuruva mētakap pūcipurita,
loruvutal ceyātavarkaḷ yāvarē yeṉiṉumava rumparmaka vāṉmalariṉmē
loruvaṉmutalōrkaḷiṉu matikareṉ ṟarumaṟi yurittalcila reṇṇāmiyeṉ
karuvutavir mātavar kacintēt teṭukkavoḷir karuṇiyā ṉantvāḻvvē
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

8.
poṅkuṅ karuṅkaṭal parantateṉ ṉeṉṟupala puvaṉamumo riyameytap
pukkakaṭa ṉiṉṟumali yiṉṟeḻun taḵteṉāp porutoḻil valāriyeṇṇit,
taṅkun tiṟaṟkoṭiya catakōṭi yollit taṭaṟṟuniṉ ṟuruvalceyyac
cakamuḻuva tumputi paṭacciṟi virikkumoru taṟukaṭkalāpamayiṉmē,
leṅkuṅ katirkkumoru pavaḷāca lampora livarntupala paṭiyoṭumeti
rēṟṟamāṟ ṟalaṉāka vappā riṭikkaviḻa veḻukāka mōṭukaḻukuṅ,
kaṅkum pakaḻntaṭi liṟaṟpaṭiki toṭṭaniṉ kāmarviṟal yāvar pukaḻvār
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

9.
tutikiyō ṭiruceviyi ṉiṉṟumoru mūvarit tōṟṟiṉā yaṇṭamaṇuvāt
tōṟṟiṭa vuyarntāyor caṉakaṉiṭu muṉavelān tuyttu miku paciterittu,
matikkiko ṭavaṉpaṇiya mupporuḷi ṇuṇmiyi vakuttavaṟ kuṟanaviṉṟāy
viyamutal yāvumumō raṟukaḷavu pōtāta vaṇṇan terittalceytāy,
vitikkipuri vāṉunika rātapuru cuṇṭimutaṉ mēlōrkaḷ palariṟiñca
viḻiyumā ṉantamaya mēyāy viḷaṅkiṉāy veyyēṉiṉ ṉuṇmitērntu,
itikkiyuṟu māṟupē raruḷpuriva teṉṟukalikāṇā vatītamutalē
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

10.
maliyumoru poruḷaṉ ṟeṉakkaṟik kuntiṟal vayaṅkuṉiṉ ṉūrtivāḻka
malarporuvu kittalap pācāṅku cappaṭi vayaṅkiyeṉ nāḷumvāḻka
nilimaruvu ñāṉamuṅ kiriyiyu meṉakkali nirampiṉa reṭutturikku
nēyamiku cittiput tikaḷeṉuñ cattika ṇirampucī rōṅkivāḻka
valikaṭa luṭuttapuvi muḻutuniṉa taruḷaṭin tātaram perukivāḻka
vaṟputam virāvuniṉ ṉaṇiyalpū ṇākiya varuntavat teviyumvāḻka
kalivaḻi niṉipparavu ceṅkuntar marapuṅ katittunī ṭūḻivāḻka
kaṟpat talattiṉum viruttit turuttiyamar kaccik kaḷiṟṟu mukaṉē.

tirutturuttik kaccivināyakarpatikam muṟṟiṟṟu



2. tirutturuttic cuppiramaṇiyasvāmipatikam


(itu cōḻanāṭṭiṟ kāvirinatiyiṉteṉpāluḷḷa tiruppatikaḷuḷ oṉṟu. kuṟṟālameṉa ikkālattuvaḻaṅkum)

11.
pūmēvu camayaṅka ḷōrā ṟiṉukkum pulaṅkoḷuṅ kāṭcinalkap
polintaṉa veṉccolun tirumuka moraṟum polaṉceymaṇi muṭiyorāṟum
pāmēvu ceṅkati rirāṟunika rāmeṉap pakarmaṇik kuḻiyirāṟum
paṉṉiru taṭampuyap peruvariyi ṉaruviyiṟ pacunara voraḻūkkutoṭiyu
māmēvu kāṉavar kulakkōti yoṭumēli vāṉavar kulakkōtiyum
vaḷaroḷiya vaṭivēlu mārpiṉu lumpoliya mayilvara kaṉattuvaruvā
temmēvu colipuṭi kuḻun turuttit tiruttaḷiyi lamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

12.
nīṭumaka mēruvit ticivari yoṭuṅkoṇṭu nilavupan tāṭalceyavunēmimāl
variyiyoru kikkaṭaka mākkoṇṭu nikaramaṟ ṟoṉṟāyavu,
mōṭukati ricciṟu matāṇiyeṉa mārpiṭi yuṟappuṉiya vuñcalatika
ḷōrēḻu moruviraṟ koṭuteṟit tiliyeṉa voḻikkavun ticiyyeṭṭiluṅ,
kūṭukaḷi ṟēṭṭut tutikkikaṇ muṭintoruki koṇṭiḻut tiṭavumvalla
kulavīira vākuvit tampiyeṉa makiḻuniṉ koṟṟamevar muṟṟa vuṇarvār,
cēṭumali kāvirit teṉpāṟ ṟurutti tiruttaḷiyu ḷamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

13.
poruvariya mātavat torunāra tappeyar puṅkamuṉi purimakattup
pōntupala vaṇṭamu miṭittut takarttunuṇ puḻuticey tulāvukiṉṟa
veruvariya ceṟimayirt tirukukōṭ ṭuttakariṉ viṟalkaṇṭu naṭunaṭuṅki
mēlivā ṉavaraṭin tōlameṉa varukupoli vīravā kuvinōkkiyap
paruvamiku takariṉi paṟṟivaru keṉṉap paṇippavava nutavavēṟip
pāriṭam pōtāmi teritarac ceṇṭāṭu paṇpeva reṭuttumoḻivār
tiruvamiku kalvicciṟappuyar turutti tiruttaḷiyu ḷamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

14.
veḷḷiyaṅ kayiliyiṉ mutaṟcikari vāytaliṉ vinōtap poruṭṭa marunāḷ
littakaṟ cēvicey tañcali ceyātēkum vitiyiṉaṟi yāmi yuḷḷat,
tuḷḷiyiva ṉippaṟṟi vāveṉa vurikkavāṅ korukuṟaṇmu ṉuyppanōkki
yoḻiiyā vakantiyuḷi yettoḻil valāyeṉa vuñaṟṟutalil vallēṉeṉa,
veḷḷiyo rirukkuri yeṉakkuṭili muṉcola viyampiyataṉuṭporuḷeva
ṉiyamputi yeṉatteri tarāmiyāṟ ceṉṉiyiliraṅkap puṭittaṭittāy,
teḷḷiyaru meṉṉuṇarvar niṉpukaḻ turuttit tiruttaḷiyu ḷamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

15.
perukukoṭu vañcanavil kurukuvari nilitapap peruvāṉ kākkumāṟu
pērama ruñaṟṟumaḷa villā viṟaṟṟakuvar piṇavari yurntutōṉṟak,
karukuniṟam vāyntukari koṉṟiraṅ kumperuṅ kaṭaṉmuḻutum vaṟṟa vaṉiyār
kāyattu niṉṟumveḷi vantaneyt tōreṉuṅ kaṭaṟiri yeṟintuperuka,
murukumali mālip puyattaṉiyar kuṭikoṇṭa mutunakara matupāḻpaṭa
muṉṉit tutikkumvā ṉavarkuṭiko ṇakarkaḷi mukantumēṉ mēṟṟaḻitarat,
tirukutavi rayiṟoṭuniṉ viṟaleṉ ṟuruttit tiruttaḷiyu ḷamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

16.
poruntiya tamiḻkkelli yākumvaṭa vēṅkaṭap pūtaramu mātaravi ṉāṟ-
pōtumūṉ ṟumpōtu muṉṟutavu veṟpum polintuyar paraṅkuṉṟamu,
maruntiya naṟumpaḻa mutirkkum poḻiṟkiriyu mavircanta ṉācalamumel
lalar poruñ ceṅkiyiṟ ṟaṇṭukoṭu maḻavaṭi vamintaruḷ koḻikkum variyu,
maruntiyavu ḷārmāntal pōṟpirā ṉorumoḻi vayapporuṇ maṉaṅkoḷḷuvāṉ
vāymalarn taruḷiyamar mālvariyu maṟṟunī maruvumaruḷ yāvaruṇarvār,
tiruntiya vaḷampala virāvun turuttit tiruttaḷiyu ḷamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

17.
kaṉimaruvu vēṭṭuvar kulakkoṭi paṭarntumakiḻ kalavat taḻintataruvē
kaṟpakap poḻilāṭu teyvatap piṭiyik kalantviḷi yāṭukaḷiṟē
yaḷimaruvu mātava ruḷakkamala malarkāppa ṇaḷḷūṟa vūṟutēṉē
yāyiraṅ katiroruṅ kutayameḻa leṉavaṭi yaṭintavark karaṅkāṭciyē
vaḷimaruvu puṭaviyi ṉayarntōti luṅkaruṇi maḻipoḻi ceḻuṅkoṇṭalē
vantit tuḷāriyev vāṉavaru meññāṉṟum vantikka vikkunitiyē
teḷimaruvu niṉṉaṭik kaṉparu ṭuruttit tiruttaḷiyu ḷamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

18.
mūvarmuta lāyapara civaṉmēṉi yiṟpāti muṉivariya tākkavarntu
mūtaṇṭa puvaṉap parappiṟ carācara mukiḻttiṭak karuṇiceytum,
yāvaretu karutiṉā ratiyap paṭikkavarta mitayat tamarntaḷittu
micciñā ṉaṅkiriyi pariyāti yivarmuta leṇṇilcat tikaḷākiyam ,
pūvarmuta lōrtoḻilaṉittumoru taṉṟoḻiṟ poliveṉac ceytumiṉṉum
pukalaḷakil cattiyuḷa tuṉatukic cattiyē pukaṉnaḷa viṭappaṭuvatō,
tēvarpala muṉivarka ṭutikkun turuttit tiruttaḷiyu ḷamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

19.
attaroru moḻiyuṇmi yiṟṟeṉat teḷiyumā ṟaruḷuniṉ kuravu pukaḻkō
vaḻakiya kuṟakkōti pāṟporuḷi ṉiyalpaṟiya vāṟṟipani ṉāṭalpukaḻkō,
muttarkaru naccū cerintamarar ciṟimīṭṭa muṉiviṉaṉ vākipukaḻkō
mūkiyeṉa vikiyaṟi vōṅkunā valarkalaka muḻutaṟut taruḷalpukaḻkō,
pattarpala reṇṇiya paṭikkavar muṉañceṉṟu parivaṉīyaruḷalpukaḻkō
payaṉuṟā nāyiṉēṉ yātiṉi yaṟintoru payaṉpaṭap pukaḻavallēṉ,
cittarpala muṉivarka ṭutikkun turuttit tiruttaḷiyu ḷamaraṇṇalē
cīṟumvem pakiyōvu māṟuvaṉ poṭukūvu cēvalaṅ koṭiyāḷaṉē.

20.

5. muttapparuvam

10. poṉṉūcaṟparuvam
124.
koṉceyta vayirak koḻuṅkā ṉiṟuttiyoḷi kūrpavaḷa viṭṭamiṭṭuk -
kuḷirmaruvu nittilat toṭarpūṭṭi navamaṇi kuyiṟṟupoṟ palaki māṭṭi
miṉceyta ciṟṟiṭip perumulik kōkaṉaka mītumoṇ puṇṭarīka -
mītumuṟi mātariru pāluniṉ ṟārvaṭam virumputaṅ kikaḷpaṟṟat ,
teṉceyta tamiḻmuṉiyi yeṇṇātu mīṭṭuñ cilampeḻuntatukoleṉṟu -
ceṅkatirumveṇkatiru niṉṟuṉṉi yuḷḷana tikippavāṉ kīṇṭeḻuntu ,
poṉceyta matilcūḻ tirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭiyaruḷē -
poruviṉman tiracorū pattaṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

125.
tēmaṇak kuṅkuḻaṟ ceyyavāy veṇṇakic cēlviḻip paṉuvalāṭṭi -
tirivīcu cuviyamu toḻukkiyatu pōṉmeṉat tiruvūca liṉitu pāṭap,
pāmaṇak kumpukaḻc ceñcaṭit tampirāṉ pārttuḷat tuvakipūppap -
pallā yiraṅkōṭi yaṇṭamu mavaṟṟuḷḷa palacarā caramu māṭa,
vāmaṇak kuṅkaru mukiṟpaṭāṅ kīṇṭumē lāyavula kampu kuntāṅ -
kamarvār ceḻuṅkaṟpa kattiṉiṉ ṟumpirit taṟitarā vaṇṇa nāḷum,
pūmaṇak kumpoḻil vaḷapperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē -
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

126.
koṅkumali tēmān taḷirppoḻi ṉaṭukkaṇamar kōkilappēṭu poruvak -
kōkaṉaka rākamaṇi yēcuṟṟu muṟṟuṅ kuyiṟṟupoṟ palakiyēṟic,
ceṅkumuta vāyara maṭantiyar virikkumalar ceṅkiniṟi yakkoṇṭu niṉ -
ṟirumuṉart tūvupu kuṭantamuṟ ṟakalātu cēvittu vantu niṟkac,
caṅku pala veṅkuṅ karumparam pikkulañ cāliyā yuṟalu ṇarntu -
tāvāta vētaṉi yoṭuntavaḻn tēṟiyan tāmariyiṉ muttu yirkkum,
poṅkupuṉal vāvittirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē -
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

127.
mēlāyacattuva meṉappakar kuṇattunaṭu vīṟṟiruntaruḷaleṉkō -
meyyaṭiyar muṉcela naṭāttuvā ṉōtima micipolin tamarva teṉkō,
nālāya maṟipukaḻum veḷḷiveṇ kayilivari nāppaṇamar tōkiyeṉkō -
nāṉeṉṟu kalimakaḷ viyantu pāṭaccurka ṇaṉkukaṇ ṭuvaki nīṭap,
pālāya voḷvayira muṟṟuṅ kuyiṟṟakaṉ palakiyi livarntiruntu -
pimpoṉcey kōpura meḻuntoḷi parappiyiruḷ pāṟṟivaṭa mēruveṟpip,
pōlāya vaḷamit tirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē -
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

128.
kayaṟaṅku niṉṟirukkaṭkaṭi yutittumici kāṇapparanteḻuntu
karutuvā riruviṉi maraṅkaḷkāl cāyavali kāṉṟaṉiyar piṟavivāri
mayaṟaṅkuvaṉkari yuṭintaṭi yaṟappoṅkum vāṉperuṅ karuṇi veḷḷam
vaṟṟātu niṉkik kaṭippiṟan teḻuveḷḷa maṉamañci yuṭki vaṭki
yayaṟaṅku morutivali yākapravākamuṟ ṟakilaniki lamumvirāva
vamarā vatikkaṟpa kattō ṭamarkkuma takaṟṟuvāṉ mēṟpaṭuttup
puyaṟaṅku cōlit tirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

129.
naṉṉayam virāviyuḷa nekkunekkurukunar nayakkuniṉ ṟāmarittā
ṇakupoḻiṟpiṇṭiyi yutintāṭu toṟumatunakattaḷirttiṉitupūttal
paṉṉayam virāvumac ciṟṟaṭip perumiyuṭ patitara vuṇarntu pūci
paṇṇuvā ṉiṟkunili kāṭṭavatu kaṇṭavi paṭuntoṟu marukkamalartū
miṉṉiyal pirāṉcaṭi yaḻukkāṟu mēvanaṉ mēkamu muṭukkūṭṭamum
virāviya veṉakkā raṭittira ḷarumpukaḷ viḷaṅkac cumantu cūḻnta
puṉṉiyaṅ kāṉaṟṟirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

130.
ātāra paṅkayan toṟumvaṉṉa rūpiyā yamarvatuṅ karuṇiyē mey
yāyaperu māṉ peṟaṟ kariyavā mattaliṉi tamarvatuṅ karuṇimikiyāṟ
pātāra vintam patittiruṅ kayilimuṟ paḵṟalat tamarvatumviṭār
paṟṟiṉāruḷḷakat tamarvatum poravoḷi paṭuntukirp palakiyiṉamrn
tōtāram villiṭak kuṟumūra ṉilaveḻa vuṟuṅkaruṇi poṅkacciṟi
yōtimañciṟiyilō timanikara viritērn turuṅkuṟip piṟkamalameṉ
pōtāra vamarun tirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

131.
valiyavara ṉālamarar vāṉakaṅ kūṭṭuṇṭu makiḻmuraṭ cūraṉiḷavaṉ
makkaḷoṭu vāḻkiṉṟa pērilaḻi yacciṟṟiṉ maṟukūṭu lāyaḻittum
kaliyavavaṉ vāytaṟ perumpaṟi muḻakkaṟak kāmarciṟu paṟi muḻakkaṅ
kaṇṭumava ṉūrumin tirañāla meṉṟuri katirppolan tēruruḷuṟā
toliyaciṟu tēriṉi turuṭṭiyum viḷiyāṭu moṇcataṅ kicciṟiyatā
ḷorukuḻali yittaṉi yuvanteṭut tuppulli yoṇmaṇit toṭṭilēṟṟip
poliyaviṉi tāṭṭun tirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

132.
ātaramperuka kacintunek kurukupaḻa vaṭiyarmaṉa māyatūya
vālayat tiṉitamarn tāṭutaliyoppamut tavirceḻum palakiyēṟik
kātaraṅkaṭiyēṅ kaḻintuyya vevviṉi kaḻīippoṅ kiṭuṅkaṭikaṭ
karuṇiveḷḷamperuka vempirāṉ caṭimuṭik kaṅkiveḷ ḷañcuruṅka
mītarampiyara mara roṭukī ḻiṟaṅkavaṉ mētiṉiyu ḷāravvāṟu
mēlēṟa vittaneṭṭēṇiyē yāmeṉa viḷaṅkukōpuram vaṭātu
pūtaram poruvun tirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

133.
aṟpumali yuḷḷam pukumpari purattāḷu maṟivili yilātacempaṭ
ṭāṭipuṉi mēkali maruṅkulu neruṅkupē raṇṭamuṟ ṟīṉṟavayiṟum
vaṟpumali parañāṉa mūṟumiru koṅkiyum maṅkalam polikaḻuttu
māṟātiraṇṭu tali yiṭṭamup paḵtaṟamum vaḷarpaṅ kayacceṅkiyum
veṟpumali pacuvēy cuvaṭṭumiru tōḷumiḷa veṇmūraluñ cevāyu
miḷarkuḻiyu malarmukamu maṇināci yuṅkaruṇi viḻiyuṅ karuṅkūntalum
poṟpumali yappoli tirupperu maṇattammi poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē
poruviṉman tiracorū pattiṉi vimāṉatti poṉṉūca lāṭi yaruḷē.

nērici veṇpā

ōrāṉi vante ṉuḷattu. 1


mayaṅkicik koccakkalippā

--- taravu ---

nīrkoṇṭa kaṭalāṭi nilamakaḷuk kaṇiyāṉa
kārkoṇṭa poḻiṟkācik kaṭinakaraṅ kuḷirtūṅka
iṭamaruṅkiṟ ciṟumaruṅkuṟ peruntaṭaṅka ṇiṉṉamirtum
caṭimaruṅki ṉeṭuntirikkip peṇṇamirtun taliciṟappak
kaṇkatuvu kaṭavuṇmaṇi terintamarar kammiyaṉcey
viṇkatuvu polaṅkuṭumi vimāṉattiṉ micippolintōy. .......(1)
niṟpaṉavun tavaḻvaṉavu naṭappaṉavu māynilattuk
kaṟpamaḷa vilakaṇṭu muṟukaḷikaṇ kāṇāmē
paḻaṅkaṇuṟu muyirkaṭuyark kaṭaṉīttup paraṅkaruṇi
vaḻaṅkupara māṉanta mākkaṭaliṟ ṟiḷittāṭa
uriyāta paḻamaṟiyiṉ mutaleḻutti ṉoṇporuḷi
variyātu koṭuttiṭuniṉ vaḷḷaṉmi vāḻttutumē. .......(2)

--- tāḻici ---

nīreḻuttuk kottavuṭa ṉīttārkku nīnavilva
tōreḻuttē muḻutumava revvaṇṇa muṇarvatuvē. .......(1)

eṉpaṇiva tuṭuppatutō lempirāṉ ṟamarkaḷavar
muṉpaṇiyum pēṟuṭiyār ticimukaṉu mukuntaṉumē. .......(2)

ceṭikoṇmuṭip puḻukkūṭē ciṟṟaṭiyō miṭutiṟimaṟ
ṟaṭikaḷaṭi yārkkaḷippa tāṉantap peruvāḻvē. .......(3)

paṟpakaṉōṟ ṟaruntavarum peṟaṟkariya parantāmam
eṟpuṭalviṟ ṟaḷiyēmuṅ koḷappeṟuva tiṟumpūtē. .......(4)

niṇampuṇarveṇ ṭalikkalaṉko ṉēriḻimut tittiruvi
maṇampuṇarvārk kiyaṉaruṇ maṇavāḷak kōlamē. .......(5)

muṭittaliyiṟ palikoḷvāṉ mūvulaku mavaravartaṅ
kaṭittaliyiṟ ṟirivatukol yāmpeṟuniṉ kāṇiyē. .......(6)

--- arākam ---

uḷateṉa vilateṉa voruvaro raḷaviyiṉ
aḷaviṉi laḷaviṭa lariyato ruruviṉi. .......(1)

ituveṉa larumiyi ṉeḻutaru moḻikaḷum
atuvala veṉumeṉi ṉevaruṉi yaṟipavar. .......(2)

avaṉava ḷatuveṉu mavikaḷi ṉuḷaṉalaṉ
evaṉava ṉivaṉeṉa vetirtaru takimiyi. .......(3)

aṟipava raṟiviṉu ḷaṟivuko ṭaṟivuṟu
neṟiyala toruvaru maṟivaru nilimiyi. .......(4)

--- nāṟcīrōraṭi ampōtaraṅkam ---

āṇoṭu peṇṇuru vamittu niṉṟaṉi.
pūṇmuli kalantumim pulaṉum veṉṟaṉi.
eṇvaki yuṟuppiṉō ruruve ṭuttaṉi.
toṉmaṟip paṉuvaliṉ ṟoṭito ṭuttaṉi.

-- muccīrōraṭi ampōtaraṅkam --

vaṭavari kuḻiya vaḷittaṉi.
malimakaṇ mulika ṭiḷittaṉi.
viṭamamir tamara viḷittaṉi.
vicayaṉo ṭamarcey tiḷittaṉi.
varicili vataṉi yerittaṉi.
matakari yurivi tarittaṉi.
arumaṟi teriya virittaṉi.
alakilpal kalika ṭerittaṉi.

-- irucīrōraṭi ampōtaraṅkam --



aḻalvi ḻittaṉipavamo ḻittaṉi.
āṟa ṇintaṉi māṟa ṇintaṉi..
maḻuva lattiṉi muḻuna lattiṉi.
māna ṭattiṉi māṉi ṭattiṉi..
alaki ṟantaṉi talici ṟantaṉi..
aruḷcu rantaṉi iruṭu rantaṉi..
ulaka ḷittaṉi tamiḻte ḷittaṉi.
oṉṟu māyiṉi palavu māyiṉi..


-- tāḻici --

alakilpala puvaṉaṅka ḷaṭaṅkalumuṇ ṭoḻippāykkuk
koliviṭamuṇ ṭaṉiyeṉṟu kūṟuvatōr vīṟāmē. .......(1)

payiṉmūṉṟu puvaṉamuṅkaṭ poṟikkiriyāp pālippāyk
keyiṉmūṉṟu merimaṭuttā yeṉpatumō riciyāmē. .......(2)

aṭiyavarē mukkuṟumpu maṟaveṟintā reṉiṉaṭikaḷ
viṭukaṇiviṟ kāmaṉinī veṉṟatumōr viyappāmē. .......(3)

ikkūṟṟiṉ ṟirunāmat torukūṟṟuk kilakkeṉṟāl
akkūṟṟaṅ kumittaṉiyeṉ ṟicippatumō raṟputamē. .......(1)

-- curitakam --

ulakucūṟ koṇṭa taliviyu nīyum
malipaka veṟinta maḻaviḷaṅ kuḻaviyi
amutamūṟ ṟirukkuṅ kumutavāyt tēṟal
vaṇṭuki ṉaṉippa maṭittalat tiruttik
kaṇkaḷiṟ parukiyak kāmaru kuḻavi
eḻutāk kiḷavi yiṉcuvi paḻutta
maḻalinā ṟamirtam vāymaṭut tuṇṇac
ceñcevi niṟittanum mañcevik kaṭikaḷeṉ
puṉmoḻik kaṭukkoḷap pukaṭṭiṉaṉ
iṉṉaruḷ viḻikuvā yiṟumpū tuṭittē. 2

puyavakuppu
canta viruttam

iṭamaṟa miṭitaru kaṭavuḷar maṭaviyar
eṟitaru kavarini ḻaṟkaṭ ṭuyiṉṟaṉa
iṉavaḷi koṭumata ṉiṭucaya viruteṉa
iṟiyava ḷeḻutucu vaṭṭuk kicintaṉa
iruvaru nikareṉa varicili vicayaṉo
ṭetirporu camarili ḷippuṟ ṟiruntaṉa
iṇiyaṭi paraviya malaṭimu ṉutaviya
iṭiyali ṉuṇavoru koṭṭip parintaṉa
paṭavara vumiḻtaru maṇiveyil viṭavaḷar
parutiyo ṭeḻumuta yattiṟ polintaṉa
parukumi ṉamirteṉa vurukiru kaviñrkaḷ
paṉuvaliṉ maturavi cikkuk kuḻintaṉa
paṭaroḷi viṭucuṭar valayama teṉavoru
paruvari neṭuvile ṭuttuc cumantaṉa
pararpura meriyoṭu pukiyeḻa malarmakaḷ
paṇimuli taḻuvuca rattit turantaṉa
maṭalaviḻ taṭamala ritaḻiyi ṉiḻitaru
matumaḻi yaruviku ḷittuk kiḷarntaṉa
vaḻitara vutiramu niṇamoṭu kuṭarkaḷum
varunara kariyiṉma tattit taṭintaṉa
matakari yuriyataḷ kulakiri mutukiṉiṉ
maḻimuki ṟavaḻvate ṉappoṟ pamintaṉa
malipukaḻ nilavoṭu maṭutiṟal veyileḻa
matikatir valamvaru veṟpot tuniṉṟaṉa
kuṭavaḷi tuṟitoṟu muṭuniri yeṉavari
kuḷirnila veḻavumiḻ muttit taṭaṅkari
kulaviya paṭarciṟi maṭavaṉa moṭucila
kurukukaḷ ciṉiyoṭa ṇittut tuyiṉṟiṭu
kuripuṉal varanati curartaru murukaviḻ
koḻumalar citaṟavi muttat tuviṇṭoṭu
kulakiri yutaviya vaḷariḷa vaṉamuli
koḻunarta maḻakiya koṟṟap puyaṅkaḷē. 5

kuRinjcippaTTu of kapilar

(work in pattuppaTTu anthologies)

caṅka kāla nūlkaḷāṉa pattup pāṭṭukkaḷil eṭṭāvatāṉa

kuṟiñcippāṭṭu

-----------------------

pāṭiyavar :: kapilar

tiṇi :: kuṟiñci

tuṟi :: aṟattoṭu niṟṟal

pāvaki :: āciriyappā

motta aṭikaḷ :: 261

-----------------------

aṉṉāy vāḻivēṇ ṭaṉṉi yoṇṇuta

lolimeṉ kūntaleṉ ṟōḻi mēṉi

viṟaliḻi nekiḻtta vīvaruṅ kaṭunō

yakalu ḷāṅka ṇaṟiyunar viṉāyum

paraviyun toḻutum viravumalar tūyum

vēṟupal luruviṟ kaṭavuṭ pēṇi

naṟiyum virayu mōcciyu malavuṟ

ṟeyyā miyali nīyum varuntuti

naṟkaviṉ toliyavu naṟuntō ṇekiḻavum

puṭpiṟa raṟiyavum pulampuvan talippavu .... 10



muṭkaran tuṟiyu muyyā varumpaṭar

ceppal vaṉmiyiṟ ceṟittiyāṉ kaṭavaliṉ

muttiṉu maṇiyiṉum poṉṉiṉu mattuṇi

nērvaruṅ kuriya kalaṅkeṭiṟ puṇaruñ

cālpum viyappu miyalpuṅ kuṉṟiṉ

mācaṟak kaḻīi vayaṅkupukaḻ niṟutta

lācaṟu kāṭci yiyarkku mannili

yeḷiya veṉṉār toṉmaruṅ kaṟiñar

mātaru maṭaṉu mōrāṅkut taṇappa

neṭuntē renti yaruṅkaṭi nīvi ...20



yiruvē māynta maṉṟa lituveṉa

nāmaṟi vuṟāliṟ paḻiyu muṇṭō

vāṟṟiṉ vārā rāyiṉu māṟṟa

vēṉiyula kattu miyivatā ṉamakkeṉa

māṉamar nōkkaṅ kalakkik kiyaṟ

ṟāṉāc ciṟumiya ḷivaḷun tēmpu

mikaṉmīk kaṭavu miruperu vēntar

viṉiyiṭi niṉṟa cāṉṟōr pōla

virupē raccamō ṭiyāṉu māṟṟalēṉ

koṭuppiṉaṉ kuṭimiyuṅ kuṭinira luṭimiyum .... 30



vaṇṇamun tuṇiyum porīi yeṇṇā

temiyēn tuṇinta vēmañcā laruviṉi

nikaḻnta vaṇṇa nīnaṉi yuṇarac

ceppa lāṉṟiciṟ ciṉavā tīmō

neṟko ṇeṭuvetirk kaṇanta yāṉi

muttār maruppi ṉiṟaṅkuki kaṭuppat

tuyttali vāṅkiya puṉiṟutīr peruṅkura

ṉaṟkōṭ ciṟutiṉip paṭupu ḷōppi

yeṟpaṭa varutiya reṉa nī viṭuttaliṟ

kalikeḻu maramicic cēṇō ṉiḻitta .... 40



puliyañ citaṇa mēṟi yavaṇa

cāraṟ cūraṟ ṟakipeṟa valatta

taḻalun taṭṭiyuṅ kuḷirum piṟavuṅ

kiḷikaṭi marapiṉa vūḻūḻ vāṅki

yuravukkatir teṟūu muruppavi ramayattu

vicumpāṭu paṟavi vīḻpatip paṭara

niṟiyirum puvaṅ kuṟipaṭa mukantukoṇ

ṭakaliru vāṉattu vīcuvaḷi kalāvaliṉ

muracatirn taṉṉa viṉkura lēṟṟoṭu

niricela ṉivappiṟ koṇmū mayaṅki .... 50



yiṉṉici muraciṟ cuṭarppūṭ cēe

yoṉṉārk kēntiya vilaṅkili yeḵkiṉ

miṉmayaṅku karuviya kaṉmicip poḻinteṉa

vaṇṇa ṉeṭuṅkōṭ ṭiḻitaru teṇṇī

ravirtukil puriyu mavveḷ ḷaruvit

tavirvil vēṭkiyēn taṇṭā tāṭip

paḷiṅkucori vaṉṉa pāycuṉi kuṭivuḻi

naḷipaṭu cilampiṟ pāyam pāṭip

poṉṉeṟi maṇiyiṟ ciṟupuṟan tāḻntavem

piṉṉiruṅ kūntal piḻivaṉan tuvari .... 60



yuḷḷakañ civanta kaṇṇēm vaḷḷita

ḻoṇceṅ kānta ḷāmpa laṉiccan

taṇkayak kuvaḷi kuṟiñci veṭci

ceṅkoṭu vēri tēmā maṇicciki

yuritunā ṟaviḻtot tuntūḻ kūviḷa

meripuri yeṟuḻañ cuḷḷi kūviram

vaṭavaṉam vāki vāṉpūṅ kuṭaca

meruvi ceruviḷi maṇippūṅ karuviḷi

payiṉi vāṉi palliṇark kuravam

pacumpiṭi vakuḷam palliṇark kāyā .... 70



virimala rāviri vēral cūral

kurīip pūḷi kuṟunaṟuṅ kaṇṇi

kuṟukili marutam viripūṅ kōṅkam

pōṅkan tilakan tēṅkamaḻ pātiri

cerunti yatiral peruntaṇ caṇpakaṅ

karanti kuḷavi kaṭikamaḻ kalimāt

tilli pāli kallivar mulli

kulli piṭavañ ciṟumā rōṭam

vāḻi vaḷḷi nīṇaṟu neyta

ṟāḻi taḷava muṭṭāṭ ṭāmari .... 80



ñāḻaṉ muva ṉaṟuntaṇ kokuṭi

cēṭal cemmal ciṟuceṅ kurali

kōṭal kiti koṅkumutir naṟuvaḻi

kāñci maṇikkulik kaṭkamaḻ neytal

pāṅkar marāam palpūn taṇakka

mīṅki yilavan tūṅkiṇark koṉṟi

yaṭumpama rātti neṭuṅkoṭi yavari

pakaṉṟi palācam palpūm piṇṭi

vañci pittikam cin tuvāram

tumpi tuḻāay cuṭarppūn tōṉṟi .... 90



nanti naṟava naṟumpuṉ ṉākam

pāram pīram piṅkuruk katti

yāraṅ kāḻvi kaṭiyirum puṉṉi

naranta nāka naḷḷiru ṇāṟi

māyiruṅ kuruntum vēṅkiyum piṟavu

marakkuvirit taṉṉa parēram puḻakuṭaṉ

mālaṅ kuṭiya malivaṉa maṟuki

vāṉkaṇ kaḻīiya vakalaṟik kuviip

puḷḷā riyatta vilaṅkumalic cilampiṉ

vaḷḷuyirt teḷviḷi yiṭiyiṭip payiṟṟik .... 100



kiḷḷi yōppiyuṅ kiḷiyitaḻ paṟiyāp

pivari yalkuṟ koytaḻi tiip

palvē ṟuruviṉ vaṉappami kōtiyem

melliru muccik kaviṉpeṟak kaṭṭi

yeriyavi ruruvi ṉaṅkuḻic ceyalit

tātupaṭu taṇṇiḻa liruntaṉa māka

veṇṇey nīviya curivaḷar naṟuṅkāḻt

taṇṇaṟun takaraṅ kamaḻa maṇṇi

yīram pulara viraluḷarp paviḻāk

kāḻaki lampuki koḷīi yāḻici .... 110



yaṇimiku varimiñi ṟārppat tēṅkalantu

maṇiniṟaṅ koṇṭa māyiruṅ kuñciyiṉ

maliyavu nilattavuñ ciṉiyavuñ cuṉiyavum

vaṇṇa vaṇṇatta malarāypu viriiya

taṇṇaṟut toṭiyal veṇpōḻk kaṇṇi

nalampeṟu ceṉṉi nāmuṟa miliccip

piṅkāṟ pittakat tāyita ḻalari

yantoṭi yorukāḻ vaḷiic centī

yoṇpūm piṇṭi yorukātu cerīi

yantaḷirk kuvavumoym palippac cāntarunti .... 120


mintiṟi koṇṭa malarntēn takalattut

toṉṟupaṭu naṟuttār pūṇoṭu poliyac

cempoṟik kēṟṟa vīṅkiṟit taṭakkiyiṉ

vaṇṇa varivil lēnti yamputerintu

nuṇviṉik kaccit tayakkaṟak kaṭṭi

yiyalaṇip polinta vīki vāṉkaḻa

ṟuyalvarun tōṟun tiruntaṭik kalāva

muṉipāḻ paṭukkun tuṉṉarun tuppiṟ

pakipuṟaṅ kaṇṭa palvē liḷiñari

ṉuravucciṉañ cerukkit tuṉṉutoṟum vekuḷu .... 130



muḷivā ḷeyiṟṟa vaḷḷukir ñamali

tiḷiyāk kaṇṇa vaḷikupu neritara

naṭuṅkuvaṉa meḻuntu nallaṭi taḷarntiyā

miṭumpikūr maṉattē maruṇṭupulam paṭara

māṟuporu tōṭṭiya pukalviṉ vēṟupulat

tākāṇ viṭiyi ṉaṇipeṟa vante

malamara lāyiṭi verūuta lañci

melliya viṉiya mēvarak kiḷante

mimpā lāykavi ṉētti yoṇṭoṭi

yacimeṉ cāya lavvāṅ kunti .... 140



maṭamatar maḻikka ṇiḷiyī riṟanta

keṭutiyu muṭiyē ṉeṉṟeṉa ṉataṉetir

collē mātali ṉallāntu kalaṅkik

keṭutiyum viṭīi rāyi ṉemmoṭu

collalum paḻiyō melliya līreṉa

nivaḷam paḻuniya pāli vallōṉ

kikavar narampi ṉimmeṉa vimiru

mātar vaṇṭoṭu curumpunayan tiṟutta

tātavi ḻalarit tāciṉi piḷantu

tāṟaṭu kaḷiṟṟiṉ vīṟupeṟa vōccik .... 150



kalleṉ cuṟṟak kaṭuṅkura lavitteñ

collaṟ pāṇi niṉṟaṉa ṉāka

viruvi vēynta kuṟuṅkāṟ kurampip

piṇiyēr nōkkiṉ maṉiyōṇ maṭuppat

tēmpiḻi tēṟaṉ mānti makiḻciṟantu

cēma maṭinta poḻutiṉ vāymaṭut

tirumpuṉa niḻattaliṟ ciṟumi nōṉā

taravuṟa ḻañcili koḷīi nōymik

kuravucciṉa muṉpā luṭaṟciṉañ cerukkik

kaṇiviṭu puṭiyūk kāṉaṅ kalleṉa .... 160



maṭiviṭu vīḷiyar veṭipaṭut tetirak

kārppeya lurumiṟ piḷiṟic cīrttaka

virumpiṇart taṭakki yirunilañ cērttic

ciṉantikaḻ kaṭāañ cerukki maraṅkolpu

miyal vēḻa maṭaṅkali ṉetirtara

vuyviṭa maṟiyē māki yoyyeṉat

tiruttukō lelvaḷi teḻippa nāṇumaṟantu

vituppuṟu maṉattēm virintavaṟ poruttic

cūruṟu maññiyi ṉaṭuṅka vārkō

luṭuvuṟum pakaḻi vāṅkik kaṭuvici .... 170



yaṇṇal yāṉi yaṇimukat taḻuttaliṟ

puṇṇumiḻ kuruti mukampāyn tiḻitarap

puḷḷi varinutal citiya nillā

tayarntupuṟaṅ koṭutta piṉṉar neṭuvē

ḷaṇaṅkuṟu makaḷi rāṭukaḷaṅ kaṭuppat

tiṇinilik kaṭampiṉ ṟiraḷari vaḷiiya

tuṇiyaṟi māliyiṟ kipiṇi viṭēe

nuriyuṭik kaluḻi pāytali ṉuravuttiri

yaṭuṅkari vāḻiyi ṉaṭuṅkap peruntaki

yañci lōti yaciyal yāvatu .... 180



mañca lōmpuniṉ ṉaṇinala nukarkeṉa

mācaṟu cuṭarnuta ṉīvi nīṭuniṉin

teṉmuka nōkki nakkaṉa ṉannili

nāṇu muṭku naṇṇuvaḻi yaṭitara
voyyeṉap piriyavum viṭāaṉ kavii

yāka maṭiya muyaṅkali ṉavvaḻip

paḻumiḷa kukka pāṟi neṭuñcuṉi

muḻumutaṟ kokkiṉ ṟīṅkaṉi yutirnteṉap

puḷḷeṟi piracamo ṭīṇṭip palavi

ṉekiḻntuku naṟumpaḻam viḷinta tēṟa .... 190



ṉīrcet tayiṉṟa tōki viyalūrc

cāṟuko ḷāṅkaṇ viḻavukkaḷa nanti

yarikkūṭ ṭiṉṉiyaṅ kaṟaṅka vāṭumakaḷ

kayiṟūr pāṇiyiṟ ṟaḷaruñ cāral

variyara makaḷiriṟ cāay viḻitaka

viṇporuñ ceṉṉik kiḷiiya kāntaṭ

ṭaṇkama ḻalari tāay naṉpala

vampuviri kaḷittiṟ kaviṉpeṟap polinta

kuṉṟukeḻu nāṭaṉem viḻitaru peruviṟa

luḷḷat taṉmi yuḷḷiṉaṉ koṇṭu .... 200



cāṟayarn taṉṉa miṭāac coṉṟi

varunarkku variyā vaḷanakar poṟpa

malarat tiṟanta vāyil palaruṇap
pinniṇa moḻukiya neymmali yaṭicil

vaciyil vāṉṟiṇip puriyōr kaṭumpoṭu

viruntuṇ ṭeñciya miccil peruntaki

niṉṉō ṭuṇṭalum puriva teṉṟāṅ

kaṟampuṇi yākat tēṟṟip piṟaṅkumali

mīmicik kaṭavuḷ vāḻttik kitoḻu

tēmuṟu vañciṉam vāymiyiṟ ṟēṟṟi .... 210



yantīn teṇṇīr kuṭittali ṉeñcamarn

taruviṭa raminta kaḷiṟutaru puṇarcci

vāṉuri yuṟiyuḷ vayaṅkiyō ravāvum

pūmali cōli yappakal kaḻippi

yelli cella vēḻūr piṟiñcip

palkatir maṇṭilaṅ kalcērpu maṟiya

māṉkaṇa maramutaṟ ṟeviṭṭa vāṉkaṇaṅ

kaṉṟupayir kurala maṉṟuniṟi pukutara

vēṅkuvayi riciya koṭuvā yaṉṟi

lōṅkirum peṇṇi yakamaṭa lakavap .... 220



pāmpumaṇi yumiḻap palvayiṟ kōvala

rāmpalan tīṅkuḻaṟ ṟeḷviḷi payiṟṟa

vāmpa lāyitaḻ kūmpuviṭa vaḷamaṉip

pūntoṭi makaḷir cuṭartalik koḷuvi

yanti yantaṇa rayarak kāṉavar

viṇṭōy paṇavi miciñekiḻi potta

vāṉamāmali vāycūḻpu kaṟuppak kāṉaṅ

kalleṉ ṟiraṭṭap puḷḷiṉa molippac

ciṉiiya vēntaṉ celcamaṅ kaṭuppat

tuṉiiya māli tuṉṉutal kāṇūu .... 230



nēriṟi muṉki paṟṟi numartara

nāṭaṟi naṉmaṇa mayarkañ ciṉṉāṭ

kalaṅka lōmpumi ṉilaṅkiḻi yīreṉa

vīra naṉmoḻi tīrak kūṟit

tuṇipuṇa rēṟṟi ṉemmoṭu vantu

tuñcā muḻaviṉ mūtūr vāyi

luṇṭuṟi niṟuttap peyarntaṉa ṉataṟkoṇ

ṭaṉṟi yaṉṉa viruppō ṭeṉṟu

miravaraṉ māliya ṉēvaru tōṟuṅ

kāvalar kaṭukiṉuṅ katanāy kurippiṉu .... 240



nītuyi leḻiṉu nilavuveḷip paṭiṉum

vēypuri meṉṟō ḷiṉṟuyi leṉṟum

peṟāaṉ peyariṉu muṉiya luṟāa

ṉiḷamiyi ṉikantaṉṟu milaṉē vaḷamiyiṟ

ṟaṉṉili tīrntaṉṟu milaṉē koṉṉūr

māya varavi ṉiyalpuniṉiit tēṟṟi

nīreṟi malariṟ cāayitaḻ cōrā

vīriya kaluḻumivaḷ perumatar maḻikka

ṇākat tarippaṉi yuṟippa nāḷum

valippaṭu maññiyi ṉalañcelac cāay .... 250



niṉittoṟuṅ kaluḻumā livaḷē kaṅku

laḷicceṟi yuḻuviyu māḷiyu muḷiyamum

puḻaṟkōṭ ṭāmāṉ pukalviyuṅ kaḷiṟum

valiyiṟ ṟappum vaṉkaṇ veñciṉat

turumuñ cūru miritē raravamu

moṭuṅkiruṅ kuṭṭat taruñcuḻi vaḻaṅkuṅ

koṭuntāṇ mutaliyu miṭaṅkaruṅ karāmu

nūḻilu miḻukku mūḻaṭi muṭṭamum

paḻuvum pāntaḷu muḷappaṭap piṟavum

vaḻuviṉ vaḻāa viḻumamavar



kuḻumali viṭaraka muṭiyavā leṉavē. .... 261

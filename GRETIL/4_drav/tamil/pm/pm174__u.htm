2.1 napip paṭṭam peṟṟa paṭalam (1- 55)




1
cōtiyā yevikku muḷḷuṟip poruḷāyt
tōṟṟamu māṟṟamun tōṉṟā
vātitaṉ parumāṉ koṇṭiṉi tōṅki
yamariḻin tamararuk karacaṉ
mētiṉi pukuntu mukammatu tamakku
viḷaṅkiya napiyeṉum paṭṭaṅ
kōtaṟak koṭuppat tīṉpayir viḷitta
kūṟelām viritteṭut turippām. 2.1.1
2
puḷḷivaṇ ṭaruṭṭa muṇṭici payilum
poḻiṟikaḻ makkamā purattiṟ
ṟeḷḷiya kuṟiṣik kulattiṉi lutitta
cemmaluk kuṟintapē rāṇṭu
viḷḷaruṅ kaṇakkā ṟāriraṇ ṭatiṉmēl
mēlavaṉ ṟiruvuḷap paṭiyāl
veḷḷiṭi yataṉiṟ ciṟitoḷi tiraṇṭu
viḻittiṭum viḻikketir tōṉṟum. 2.1.2
3
terintiṭu moḷiyik tammiru viḻiyāṟ
ṟeritara nōkkuvar kāṇā
tiruntiṭum piṉṉun tōṟṟiṭu mitaṉi
yevaroṭum viṭutteṭut turiyār
poruntuḷak kaḷippu maccamum piṟappap
puntiyiṟ ṟērkuvar poruvāk
karuntalik kavinā varavuṭa ṟaṭinta
kaviṉkara talamukam matuvē. 2.1.3
4
ticiyaṉit tiṉuman tarattiṉu mirunta
ticiyiṉu miraviṉum pakalu
miciyuṟac ciṟitu toṉikaḷē piṟakku
metirntunōk kiṭilurut teriyā
taciyiṉum piriyā taṭuttuṟin tavartañ
ceviyiṉu mattoṉi cārā
vaciyaṟuṅ kuṟiṣik kulattuṟuṅ kuriciṉ
maṉattiṉi laticayam peṟuvār. 2.1.4
5
ivvaṇañ ciṟitu pakaṉikaḻn tataṟpi
ṉeḻilpeṟum varicilik kuriciṉ
mivaṇa viḻiyā riṭattiṉi luṟiyā
maṟṟoru variyuṭaṉ kūṭṭāc
cevvaṇak karuttiṟ ṟaṉiyirup pataṟkē
cintikku mataṉpaṭi tēṟi
yevvari yiṭattuṅ kāliṉi lēki
yeḻilpeṟat taṉittaṉi yiruppār. 2.1.5
6
ulakiṉiṟ piṟantu varumeḻu vakikku
muyireṉuñ calatarak kaviki
nilipeṟu niḻalār mukammatu taṉittu
nirikatir tavaḻkiṟā maliyi
ṉalaṉuṟa vulavi maṉaṉuṟum paṭiyē
nālunā ḷiraṇṭunā ḷiruntu
ciliyeṉa vaḷinta ciṟunutaṟ katījā
tirumaṉi yiṭattiṉil varuvār. 2.1.6
7
varicikkuṅ katikku mutaṟṟirut talamāy
matittiṭa varuṅkiṟā maliyi
lurucikkuṅ kaṉivāy maṭamayiṟ katījā
vuṭaṉvara mukammatu meḻuntu
tericikkap poruntu mamarartam muruvun
terintiṭā tavaṇiṭi yiruntu
paricutta maṉiya kuyiloṭun tāmum
pakṟapoḻu tāṟṟiyē varuvār. 2.1.7
8
makkanaṉ ṉakarun teruttali matiḷum
vaḻiyiṭik kiṭantakal laṉittum
pukkunal liṭattiṟ ṟerintavi yeviyum
pukaḻoṭu mukammatu tamakkut
takkanaṟ poruḷā yuṟucalā murikkun
taṉittoṉi yirucevi taḻippa
mikkameyp putumi taṉiyuṇarn tuṇarntu
mikakkaḷīt taticayit tiruppār. 2.1.8
9
murukaviḻ puyavaḷ ḷalukkuṟum varuṭa
muppatiṟ ṟoṉpatiṉ mēlāy
teritarun tiṅka ḷāṟuñceṉ ṟataṟpiṉ
ṟiṉantoṟum putumiya tāka
marumalarc ceḻuntārk kaṉaṅkuḻaṟ katījā
veṉumayiṉ maṇamaṉi yiṭatti
liruviḻi tuyila maṇṇiṭit tōṉṟā
teḻilpeṟak kaṉavukaḷ kāṇpār. 2.1.9
10
veṇṭirik kaṭali lamutamum poruvā
viyaṉuṟu mellitaḻk katījā
koṇṭuṟu mayalu ḷuyiriṉu muyirāyk
kulaviya mukammatu nayiṉār
vaṇṭuṟi malarppañ caṇimici porunti
maṟuvilā toḷirmati mukattuk
kaṇṭuyil poḻutiṟ kaṉavuka ḷaṉittuṅ
kaṇṭatu kaṇṭatāyk palikkum. 2.1.10
11
tuyyava ṉaruḷā lātamā maṉuvāyt
tōṉṟiya vavaṉiyiṉ varuṭa
miyami lāṟā yirattiṉi lorunūṟ
ṟirupattu mūṉṟiṉi laḻakā
viyaka matikku mukammatiṉ vayatu
nāṟpatil ṟapīyulav valiṉi
leytiya veṭṭān tētiyiṟ caṉiyi
ṉiraviṉiṟ kiṟāmali yiṭattil. 2.1.11
12
nittila niritta viruciṟi yoḻuṅku
nīṇilāk katirkaḷviṭ ṭoḻuka
vitturu mattāḷ ciṟantaṇi tikaḻa
villumiḻ karaṅkiṭan tilaṅkap
pattiviṭ ṭeṟikkuñ cemmaṇi yirukaṭ
pārviyiṟ karuṇivīṟ ṟirukkac
cittira vaṭivic curukkimā ṉuṭarpōṟ
jipuṟayī lavviṭat taṭintār. 2.1.12
13
vēṟu
māṉuṭa vaṭivāy vanta vāṉavark karacaṉ cevvi
yāṉaṉak kurici leṉṉu makumatiṉ vataṉa nōkki
nāṉamuṅ kiyameyc cōti nāyakā variyiṉ kaṇṇē
tīṉuṟa virukkiṉ ṟīrō veṉṟucē ṇaṭintā raṉṟē. 2.1.13
14
maṟṟināṭ paruti rāvil kiṟāmali yiṭattil vāṉōr
koṟṟava ruṟintu vaḷḷaṟ kuvavuttōḷ vaṉappu nōkki
yuṟṟaveṉ ṉuyirē nīriṅ kuṟintiṉi rōveṉ ṟōti
veṟṟiveṇ vicumpu kīṟi mēlula kiṭattiṟ cārntār. 2.1.14
15
viṇṇakat taracar tōṉṟum vitimuṟi yaṟiyā vaḷḷaṉ
maṇṇakat tivari nērum vaṉappiṉil viciyi lantak
kaṇṇakaṉ vāṉa nāṭṭuṅ kāṇkuṟa varitē yeṉṉa
veṇṇamuṟ ṟitayat tārāyn tiruppiṭam peyarnti rārāl. 2.1.15
16
varicinēr ṟapīvu lavval mātamī rintu nāḷiṟ
ṟeritara viḷaṅkun tiṅka ḷiraviṉiṟ ciṟappu mikkō
ṉaruḷiṉil jipuṟa yīlvan taruvari yiṭattiṉ vikuṅ
kuriciṟaṉ kamalac ceṅkaṇ kuḷirtara vetirntu niṉṟār. 2.1.16
17
nāṉilam parappuñ cōti nāyakak kaṭavu ṭammi
vāṉakat tirunta cōti vantucan titta tēpōṟ
kāṉamar tuṇṭac ceṅkaṭ kalinilāt tavaḻu miyākki
yīṉamil jipuṟa yīlvan tiṟiyavaṉ calāmuñ coṉṉār. 2.1.17
18
marakatap patti kōli vaccirat tāri cātti
yurakamā maṇika ṇāppa ṉoḷipeṟak kuyiṟṟi veḷḷit
taraḷameṉ puruṭa rākam palamaṇi tayaṅkat tākkic
corikatir parappuñ cōtit tukiliṉik karattiṟ koṇṭār. 2.1.18
18
cekamatil viṇṇi lovvāc ceḻuntuki lataṉi yēnti
yakamakiḻn tiṉitu pōṟṟi yamararuk karacar vāytta
mukamati nōkki yāti muṟimaṟik kalimā vōti
mukammati viḷittuc cevvi valakkarat tiṭattil vittār. 2.1.19
20
marimalar vataṉac cōti mukammatiṉ karattil vāṉō
raracarmik kuvaki kūrntav vaṇituki liruttum pōtiṟ
ṟirimukil variyum viṇṇun tikāntamu naṭuvu mīkko
ḷirikaṭa lēḻum pāru milaṅkoḷi virinta taṉṟē. 2.1.20
21
maṇṭalam purakkuñ ceṅkōṉ mukammatiṉ vataṉa nōkki
viṇṭalam paravum vēta napiyeṉum paṭṭa numpāṟ
koṇṭalē kutāviṉ ṟīntā ṉeṉumoḻi kūṟip piṉṉu
maṇṭarvāḻt teṭuppac cevvi yāraṇam pukaṟi yeṉṟār. 2.1.21
22
ceppiya vacaṉaṅ kēṭṭu jipuṟayīl mukatti nōkki
muppaṭi viḷaṅkum vaṇṇa muḻumaṇik kurici lēyā
ṉipperum puviyiṟ ṟīṭṭu meḻuttiloṉ ṟaṟiyē ṉāti
yopparum vēta meṉpa tōtiṉē ṉalla ṉeṉṟār. 2.1.22
23
ciṟiniṟañ curukkit tōṉṟuñ jipuṟayīl mutalōṉ kūṟu
muṟivaḻi mukamma taṉpāṉ muṉṉirun tiruki yāra
viṟukuṟat taḻuvip piṉṉa riyampumeṉ ṟiyampat tōṉṟaṉ
maṟimutal vacaṉa nāviṉ vaḻakkiṉa ṉalla ṉeṉṟār. 2.1.23
24
etirirun taracar piṉṉu miṭaruṟat taḻuvi nōkki
matiyiṉu milaṅkuñ cōti mukammatē yōtu meṉṉap
putumiyi ṉariya pēṟē puviyiṭat tariya vētaṅ
katipeṟak kaṇṭuṅ kēṭṭuṅ kaṟṟaṟin tilaṉiyā ṉeṉṟār. 2.1.24
25
kēṭṭuvā ṉavarkō māṉuṅ kiḷaroḷi vaṉappu vāynta
tōṭṭuṇi neruṅka vuḷḷan tuṉivara viṭalañ cōrap
pūṭṭiya karaṅkaḷ cēppap pullinan napiyi nōkki
mīṭṭumeym maṟinūṉ māṟṟam viritteṭut tiyampu meṉṟār. 2.1.25
26
nerukkiviṭ ṭataṟpiṉ vēta neṟinapi yuḷḷata tuḷḷē
tirukkiḷar puviyil viṇṇōr teḷitaru maṟivu muṉṉū
lirukkiṉi laṟivun tōṉṟi yiṭaṉaṟap peruki nallō
rurukkoḷu maraci nōkki yōtaveṇ ṭuvatē teṉṟār. 2.1.26
27
mikkuyar maṟiyiṉ vaḷḷal viḷampaviṇ ṇavarkaḷ kōmā
ṉikṟavu veṉumcū ṟatti liruntunā lāyat tiṉpa
meykkuṟa mālam yaḵla meṉumaṭṭum viḷampu vīreṉ
ṟokkali luyiriṉ mikkā yuṟunapik kuṇartti ṉārāl. 2.1.27
28
eḻuttiṉiṟ ṟavaṟāc colli ṉiyaṉmuṟi citakā viṉpam
paḻuttapaṇ ṇoliyāṟ ṟīynta payiruyir peruva tākac
ceḻittamey jipuṟa yīltañ ceviyakaṅ kuḷirak kalvi
yaḻuttiya poruḷuṭ ṭōṉṟa akumatu mōti ṉārāl. 2.1.28
29
uṟintameym maṟiyiṉ ṟīñcoṉ mukammatāṇ ṭurippac cevvi
yaṟantaḻit taṉanaṉ mārkkat tarumpuvi taḻitta veṟṟit
tiṟantaḻit taṉaviṇ ṇōrkaḷ ceyaṟaḻit taṉati kāntap
puṟantaḻit taṉanan tīṉiṉ pukaḻtaḻit tōṅka vaṉṟē. 2.1.29
30
paṟavika ḷiṉaṅkaḷ pōṟṟa vilaṅkiṉam palavum pōṟṟa
vuṟitarun tarukkaḷ pōṟṟa vūrvaṉa veviyum pōṟṟa
niṟitaru malaki pōṟṟa niritirip paravi pōṟṟa
maṟivilā tamarar pōṟṟa mukammatu mōti ṉārāl. 2.1.30
31
uyiriṉuk kuyirāy vanta mukammatu murippak kēṭṭuc
ceyiraṟu jipuṟa yīltam meymayir cilirppa vōṅki
muyalakaṉ matiyam pōṉṟa mukammatip pōṟṟi vēka
veyilavaṉ katiriṟ ṟūṇṭi mēlula kiṭattiṟ pukkār. 2.1.31
32
ariyavaṉ ṟūta rāṉa akumatu kalakka muṟṟu
marumalark cōli cūḻnta mālvari yiṭatti nīṅki
yeripakaṟ katirkkā ṟōṉṟā viraviṉiṟ ṟaṉittu vallē
virikati rumiḻum pimpūṇ miṉṉakat tiṭattiṟ cārntār. 2.1.32
33
vēṟu
naṭukka muṟṟumeyc curattoṭuṅ kuḷirtara nalintu
miṭukka kaṉṟiṭap payattoṭu mariviyi viḷittut
tiṭukka muṟṟatu tukilkoṭu potimaṉan teḷiyā
tiṭukka ṇiyāteṉa vaṟikila ṉeṉṟaṉa riṟacūl. 2.1.33
34
talivar kūṟiya moḻicevi pukavuṭa ṟayaṅki
niliku linteḻun tayarvoṭu neṭṭuyirp peṟintu
palama larttoṭi ceṟintapañ caṇimicip paṭuttic
calavi koṇṭuṟap pōrttaru kiruntaṉar tiyal. 2.1.34
35
nontu meyyakam pataṟiṭak kaṇavari nōkki
yenta ṉāruyi rēyika laṭalari yēṟē
cinti cintimey tiṭukkoṭu matimukan tēmpa
vanta vāṟevi yurikkaveṉ ṟurittaṉar maṭamāṉ. 2.1.35
36
maliyi ṉucciyi ṉiruntatu moruvarvan taṭuttu
nilipe ṟuntukil karattaḷit turittatu neruṅka
vulivu ṟattaṉi yaṇittatu murittuḷap payattā
lalama larntaṉa ṉeṉṟaṉa raracaruk karacar. 2.1.36
37
tuṇivar kūṟiya māṟṟamu maṟikaḷcoṟ ṟatuvu
miṇipa ṭuttinērn tiṉpameṉ ṟuḷattiṉi liruttip
piṇiyi nērviḻi kaṉimoḻi ciṟupiṟi nutalā
raṇiyun tamvayiṉ ṟuṉipala vakaṟṟiṉa raṉṟē. 2.1.37
38
tāṅku meypporu ḷaṟivaruḷ kuṇantaya virakka
nīṅki ṭātaṟam perukiṭa vaḷarkkunaṉ ṉeṟiyī
rōṅku māṉilat tiṭattuṟi pavarkaḷā lumakkut
tīṅku ṟāteṉa vurittaṉar maṭantiyar tilatam. 2.1.38
39
cintu tēṉmoḻic ceḻuṅkuyil tūteṉun tiruppēr
vanta tāmeṉat teruḷuṟa mukammatu nayiṉā
runtu veṅkupir kaḷivatit tarameṉa vuyarvā
ṉintu nēriru ḷoṭukkiviṇ ṇeḻuntaṉa ṉiravi. 2.1.39
40
niṟakka ruṅkaḻu nīrkuvi taraniṟi vaṉacan
tiṟakka mellitaḻ veyyava ṉeḻuntapiṉ ṟiruvu
muṟakkat teṉṉumav vēntaṉik koṇarkeṉa voruva
ṉaṟakka ṭumvici koṇṭeḻun tēkiṉa ṉaṉṟē. 2.1.40
41
maṭaṅka lēṟeṉu mukammatum variparan tiruṇṭa
viṭaṅkoḷ vēliṉi nikarttumi poruviḻi mayilu
muṭaṉka lantiṉi taḻittaṉar varukaveṉ ṟuṇarvu
tuṭaṅku mummaṟi teḷintavaṟ kurittaṉaṉ ṟūtaṉ. 2.1.41
42
paḻuti lākkulap pāvitam pāliṉiṟ parivā
yeḻuka veṉṟaṉa reṉṟacoṟ ciramici yēṟṟi
vaḻuvi lāmaṟi vuṟakkatteṉ ṟōtiya vaḷḷa
ṟaḻuvu meykkatir mukammatu maṉivayiṟ cārntāṉ. 2.1.42
43
kaṟṟa vētiyaṉ varutaluṅ kiḷimoḻik katījā
poṟṟo ṭikkarañ cāyttiva ṇuṟikeṉap pukaṉṟu
maṟṟa ṭappuya mukammatu variyiṭip putumi
yuṟṟa ceytiyuṇ ṭeṉṟaṉa raṟikaveṉ ṟurittār. 2.1.43
44
matiyiṉ mikkanaṉ maṟiyavaṉ mukammati nōkkit
tuticey tummuḻi vantavā ṟaṉittiyun tokuttu
mutirta rumpuka ḻōyuri keṉṟalu muṟiyā
vitiya vaṉṟirut tūtarnaṉ ṟeṉaviḷam puvarāl. 2.1.44
45
variyi ṉiṟṟaṉi yiraviṉi lirukkiyiṉ matiyi
ṉuriyiṉ mikkava roruvarvan teṉpeya rurittup
parivi ṉāliva ṇuṟintiṉi rōveṉap pakarntu
virivi ṉiṉmaṟu nāḷumav vuriviḷam piṉarāl. 2.1.45
46
iruti ṉattiṉu murittavar maṟutiṉat teytit
terima ṇittuki leṉatuceṅ karattiṉiṟ cērtti
yariya nāyaka ṉapiyeṉum peyarumak kaḷittā
ṉurimi yīriṉi yōtumeṉ ṟurittaṉa ruvantē. 2.1.46
47
ōtu meṉṟacoṟ kēṭṭalu mōtiṉa ṉalaṉeṉ
ṟōta muṉṉirun tirukiyi ṉiṟukkimuṉ ṉuripō
lōtu meṉṟalum piṉṉaru mōtiṉa ṉalaleṉ
ṟōta maṟṟumeṉ ṟaṉimika viṟukkiṉa rurattiṉ. 2.1.47
48

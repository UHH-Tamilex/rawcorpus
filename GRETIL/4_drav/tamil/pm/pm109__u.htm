tirumaliyāṇṭavar kuṟavañci
(āciriyar yār eṉa teriyavilli)


This text has been entered from 1995 edition (second edition) of the book
by this name published by u.Ve. Swaminatha aiyar Library. This contins
only the verse (moolam) and not the meaning / commentary in the book.
it is not known who is the author of this work. The deity on whom this poem
has been sung is Muruga situated on the hill in the town PaNPuLi in
Tirunelveli district. Last few verses are missing.

pāyiram
kāppu

uccik kaṇapati
(aṟucīrk kaḻineṭilaṭi yāciriya viruttam)

cīrtaṅku paṇpi yūrvāḻ tirumali muruka ṉīpat
tārtaṅku puyavēṇ mītu tamiḻkkuṟa vañci pāṭa
ērtaṅku kamalam pūtta viṟiyavaṉ mutalōr pōṟṟum
kārtaṅku mēṉi yuccik kaṇapati kāppa tāmē.
....1

cuntarēcuvarar

cantira vataṉan taṉṉiṟ ṟarukayaṟ kaṇṇi vēṭṭa
cuntarē curaṉpoṟ pātat tuṇikkama latti nittam
cintiyā ṉiṉintu vāḻttit tirumalik kuvaṭṭiṉ mītu
vantaruḷ kanta vēṇmēṟ kuṟavañci vaḻutta luṟṟām. ....2

tirumāl mutaliyōr

nilavuvan tulavuñ cōli niṟitiru malimēl vantu
kulaviya kumaraṉ mītu kuṟavañcit tamiḻik kūṟa
malartaru tiruvi vēṭṭa mālunāṉ mukaṉu mēvum
kalivaḷar mātu meṉpār karuṇitan taruḷuvārē.
....3

nālvarum akattiya muṉivarum

naṟṟamiḻk kāḻi vēnta ṉāviṉuk karaca ṉārūrp
puṟṟiṭaṅ koṇṭār tōḻaṉ vātavūrp puṉita ṉalla
coṟmiḻ muṉivaṉṟaṅka ṭuṇippatap pōti vāḻttik
koṟṟavēṟ kumaraṉ mītu kuṟavañci kūṟa luṟṟām.
....4

aviyaṭakkam

aravaṇi parama ṉīṉṟō ṉaruṭṭiru malimē ṉiṉṟa
kuravaṇi kumaraṉ mītu kuṟavañcit tamiḻnāṉ pāṭi
viravucoṟ pulavōr tammuṉ viḷampuvē ṉeṉṟa ceyki
iraviyā yirattiṉ muṉṉa rilakumiṉ miṉipō lāmē. ....5

maṇitaṉiṉ mācu kaṇṭāṉ māciṉi māṟṟit taṅkap
paṇitaṉi laṇivarnallōr pāṉmiyeṉ ṟeṇṇi neñcat
tuṇiviṉāṟ ciṟiyōṉpāṭuñ coṟkuṟa vañci taṉṉik
kuṇameṉak koḷvār kaṟṟōr kukaṉpukaḻ kūṟa lālē.

pārtaṅku vīti vanta pavaṉiyec carikki pāṭak
kārtaṅku muḻakkaṅ kāṭṭuṅ kaṭṭiyak kāraṉ vantāṉ.

(cintu)

marumalarcceṅ kāvimali vālacupramaṇiyā
vaṭivēṟ karakkumarā varatāvecca rikki - ceya ceya

camaṇarkaḷik kaḻuvēṟṟuñ caturāvecca rikki - ceya ceya
amarar kiḷi vāḻavittē yacurarkiḷi tāḻavitta
alarkkuvaḷit tirumalivā ḻaṇṇalēyecca rikki - ceya ceya.

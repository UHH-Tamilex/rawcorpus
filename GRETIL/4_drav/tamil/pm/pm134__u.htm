Short notes on meikkIrtikaL
meykiirtikaL are prologue verses found in stone inscriptions and in copper plate grants which prises the king
in reign on date. These are useful for history as they indicateregnal year ofkings, detils on their dynasty etc.
In tamiz nATu thousands of inscriptions were recorded and archived as annals of history. We can find
detils on administration, tax system, random sampled record of various day to day activities of common
people on those era. Hence inscriptions are form part ofrecordedhistory and culture of tamiz naATu
Only the moolam texts (and not the commentaries and other research works of the author).

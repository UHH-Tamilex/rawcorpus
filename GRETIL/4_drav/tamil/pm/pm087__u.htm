1. tirumāl
aru maṟip poruḷ

āyiram viritta aṇaṅkuṭi arun tali
tī umiḻ tiṟaloṭu muṭimici aṇavara,
māyuṭi malar mārpiṉ, mi il vāl vaḷi mēṉic
cēy uyar paṇimici eḻil vēḻam ēntiya,
vāy vāṅkum vaḷi nāñcil, oru kuḻi oruvaṉi; 5

erimalar ciṉiiya kaṇṇi; pūvi
virimalar puriyum mēṉiyi; mēṉit
tiru ñemirntu amarnta mārpiṉi; mārpil
terimaṇi piṟaṅkum pūṇiṉi; māl vari
eri tirintaṉṉa poṉ puṉi uṭukkiyi- 10

cēval am koṭiyōy! niṉ vala vayiṉ niṟuttum
ēval uḻantami kūṟum,
nā val antaṇar aru maṟip poruḷē.

amar veṉṟa kaṇi

iṇipiri aṇi tuṇi paṇi eri puri
viṭar iṭu cuṭar paṭar polam puṉi viṉimalar 15

neri tiraṭeri puri taṉa miku taṉa muraṇ miku
kaṭaṟaru maṇiyoṭum muttu yātta nēraṇi
neṟi ceṟi veṟi uṟu muṟal viṟal vaṇaṅku aṇaṅku vil
tār aṇi tuṇi maṇi veyil uṟaḻ eḻil pukaḻ alar mārpiṉ
eri vayira nuti eṟi paṭi eruttu mali ivar naviyiṉiṟ 20

ṟuṇi paṭal iṉa maṇi veyil uṟaḻ eḻil nakku
imi iruḷ akala muṟu kiṟuku puri oru puri nāḷmalar
malar ilakiṉa vaḷar paritiyiṉ oḷi maṇi mārpu aṇi
maṇam mika nāṟu uruviṉa viri vaḷi miku kaṭu vici
uṭuvuṟu tali niri itaḻ aṇi vayiṟiriya amararip 25

pōreḻuntu uṭaṉṟu irittu uriiya tāṉavar
ciram umiḻ puṉal poḻipu iḻintu uram
utirpu atirpu alam toṭā amar veṉṟa kaṇi.

collil aṭaṅkāp perum pukaḻ
'poruvēm' eṉṟavar matam tapak kaṭantu,
ceru mēmpaṭṭa ceyir tīr aṇṇal! 30

iruvar tāti! ilaṅku pūṇ māal!
teruḷa niṉ varavu aṟital
maruḷ aṟu tērcci muṉivarkkum aritē:
aṉṉa marapiṉ aṉiyōy! niṉṉi
iṉṉaṉ eṉṟu urittal emakku evaṉ eḷitu? 35

arumi naṟku aṟiyiṉum, ārvam niṉvayiṉ
perumiyiṉ vallā yām ivaṇ moḻipavi
melliya eṉāa veṟāatu, alli am
tiru maṟu mārpa! nī aruḷal vēṇṭum.

tuti moḻikaḷ

viṟal miku viḻuc cīr antaṇar kākkum 40

aṟaṉum, ārvalarkku aḷiyum, nī;
tiṟaṉ ilōrt tiruttiya tītu tīr ciṟappiṉ
maṟaṉum, māṟṟalarkku aṇaṅkum, nī;
am kaṇ ēr vāṉattu aṇi nilāt tikaḻtarum
tiṅkaḷum, teṟu katirk kaṉaliyum, nī; 45

ain tali uyiriya aṇaṅkuṭi arun tiṟal
mintuṭi oruvaṉum, maṭaṅkalum, nī;
nalam muḻutu aḷiiya pukar aṟu kāṭcip
pulamum, pūvaṉum, nāṟṟamum, nī;
valaṉ uyar eḻiliyum, māka vicumpum, 50

nilaṉum, nīṭiya imayamum, nī.
ataṉāl,
'iṉṉōr aṉiyi; iṉiyiyāl' eṉa,
aṉṉōr yām ivaṇ kāṇāmiyiṉ,
poṉ aṇi nēmi valam koṇṭu ēntiya 55

maṉṉuyir mutalvaṉi ātaliṉ,
niṉṉōr aṉiyi, niṉ pukaḻōṭum polintē!
niṉ okkum pukaḻ niḻalavi;
poṉ okkum uṭiyavi;
puḷḷiṉ koṭiyavi; puri vaḷiyiṉavi; 60

eḷḷunark kaṭantu aṭṭa ikal nēmiyavi;
maṇṇuṟu maṇi pāy uruviṉavi;
eṇ iṟanta pukaḻavi; eḻil mārpiṉavi,
āṅku,
kāmaru cuṟṟamoṭu oruṅku niṉ aṭiyuṟi
yām iyintu oṉṟupu vikalum polika! eṉa,
ēmuṟu neñcattēm paravutum-
vāymoḻip pulava! niṉ tāḷ-niḻal toḻutē; 68

āḻi mutalva! niṟ pēṇutum, toḻutum

tirumāliṉ nilikaḷ

nīyē, 'vaḷiyoṭi puriyum vāliyōṟku avaṉ 20

iḷiyaṉ' eṉpōrkku iḷiyi ātām,
'puti iruḷ uṭukkip polam paṉikkoṭiyōṟku
mutiyi eṉpōrkku mutumi tōṉṟalum,
vaṭu il koḷkiyiṉ uyarntōr āynta
keṭu il kēḷviyuḷ naṭu ākutalum, 25

in nilit teri poruḷ tēriṉ, in nili
niṉ nilit tōṉṟum niṉ tol nilicciṟappē.

tirumāliṉ ciṟappu

ōṅku uyar vāṉiṉ vāṅku vil puriyum
pūṇ aṇi kaviiya ār aṇi nittila
nittila matāṇi attaku mati maṟuc 30

ceyyōḷ cērnta niṉ mācu il akalam-
vaḷar tiri maṇṇiya kiḷar poṟi nāppaṇ
vi vāl maruppiṉ kaḷiṟu maṇaṉ ayarpu
'puḷḷi nilaṉum puripaṭal aritu' eṉa
uḷḷunar urippōr uriyoṭu ciṟantaṉṟu. 35

paṭic ciṟappu

oṭiyā uḷḷamoṭu uruttu, oruṅku uṭaṉ iyintu,
iṭi etir kaḻaṟum-kāl uṟaḻpu eḻuntavar
koṭi aṟupu iṟupu, cevi ceviṭu paṭupu,
muṭikaḷ atira, paṭinili taḷara,
naṉi mural vaḷi muṭi aḻipu, iḻipu, 40

tali iṟupu tāroṭu puraḷa-
nili tolipu, vēr, tūr, maṭal,
kuruku, paṟiyā nīḷ irum paṉimicip
pala patiṉāyiram kuli tari utirvapōl-
nillātu, orumuṟi koypu kūṭi, 45

oruṅku uruṇṭu, piḷantu, nerintu, uruḷpu citaṟupu,
aḷaṟu coripu, nilam cōra,
cērār iṉ uyir cekukkum-
pōr aṭu kuricil! nī ēntiya paṭiyē:
oṉṉār uṭaṅku uṇṇum kūṟṟam uṭalē; 50

poṉ ērpu avir aḻal nuṭakku ataṉ niṟaṉē.

tirumāl tirumēṉiyiṉ oḷi mutaliya ciṟappukkaḷ

niṉṉatu tikaḻ oḷi ciṟappu iruḷ tirumaṇi;
kaṇṇē, pukaḻcāl tāmari alar iṇip piṇiyal;
vāymi, vayaṅkiya vikal; ciṟanta
nōṉmi nāṭiṉ, iru nilam; yāvarkkum, 55

cāyal niṉatu, vāṉ niṟi-eṉṉum
nā val antaṇar aru maṟip poruḷē:
avvum piṟavum ottaṉi; uvvum
ev vayiṉōyum nīyē.

uruvamum, uṇavum, veḷippāṭum
cevvāy uvaṇattu uyar koṭiyōyē! 60

kēḷviyuḷ kiḷanta ācāṉ uriyum,
paṭi nili vēḷviyuḷ paṟṟi āṭu koḷalum,
pukaḻ iyintu ici maṟi uṟu kaṉal muṟi mūṭṭit
tikaḻ oḷi oṇ cuṭar vaḷappāṭu koḷalum,
niṉ urupuṭaṉ uṇṭi; 65

piṟar uṭampaṭuvārā
niṉṉoṭu puriya
antaṇar kāṇum varavu.

pal pukaḻum paravalum

vāyaṭi amirtam niṉ maṉattakattu aṭittara,
mūvā marapum ōvā nōṉmiyum 70

cāvā marapiṉ amararkkāc ceṉṟa niṉ
... ... ... marapiṉōy niṉ aṭi
tali uṟa vaṇaṅkiṉēm, pal māṇ yāmum;
kali il neñciṉēm ēttiṉēm vāḻttiṉēm,
kaṭumpoṭum kaṭumpoṭum paravutum-
'koṭumpāṭu aṟiyaṟka, em aṟivu!' eṉavē. 76

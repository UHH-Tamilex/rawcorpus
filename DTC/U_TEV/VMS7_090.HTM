{7:90}__1+
{$} maṉamē
my mind!
maṭittāṭum aṭimaikkaṇ (vāḻtal) aṉṟiyē
not living by performing service to his foot which is bent in dancing, even in the days when you enjoy worldly pleasures, like eating and dressing well.
taṭuttu (m) āṭṭi
having prevented you from being doomed and directing your life according to his desire (These are the actions of god)
tarumaṉār tamar cekkiliṭum pōtu taṭuttum āṭkoḷvāṉ
when the servants of tarumaṉ (god of death) oppress and torment you, then also he saves you from that and bestows his grace on you.
[[tarumaṉār: ār is used ridicule him.]]
karatalattil tamarukamum eri akalum kaṭuttu āṭum kariya pāmpum piṭittu āṭi
one who dances holding in his hand the tuṭi, a hollow earthern lamp with fire and a snake which moves angrily spreading its hood.
puliyūrcciṟṟampalattu emperumāṉaip peṟṟām aṉṟē
we have definitely got the great Lord in ciṟṟampalam in puliyūr (what else is there to get ?)
[[Words within brackets are supplied to complete the sense; maṉamē should be added to the succeeding verses wherever necessary.]]
[[Variant reading: aṭimaikkaḷ, aṭimakkaḷ]]

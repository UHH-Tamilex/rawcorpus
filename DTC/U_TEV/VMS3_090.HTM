[NOTE ON TURUTTI (Verse 1): As this temple was an ait (?isle) in the middle of the Kāviri, this was known in the 7th century A.D. itself as Turutti]]

{3:90}__1+
{$} ōṅki mēl uḻitarum oli puṉaṟ kaṅkaiyai oru caṭaimēl tāṅkiṉār
Civaṉ bore Kaṅki which is in the form of sounding water and which rose and moved to and fro; on one of his matted hirs.
iṭupali talai kalaṉāk koṇṭa tammaṭikaḷ
the Lord who received the alms offered, using the skull as the bowl.
pāṅkiṉāl
by turns.
umaiyoṭum pakaliṭam pukaliṭam paim poḻil cūḻ vīṅku nīrtturuttiyār
stays in turutti which is surrounded by excessive water and by verdant gardens, during daytime as his place.
[[As this temple was an ait (?isle) in the middle of the Kāviri, this was known in the 7th century A.D. itself as Turutti]]
[[PP: poṉṉiyiṉ naṭuvu taṉṉuḷ pūm puṉal polintu tōṉṟum tuṉṉiya turuttiyāṉai
(appar, tirutturutti, 3)]]
[[The phrase vīṅku nīrtturutti is found in an inscription of this temple]]
iraviṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
He will stay during night in vēḷvikkuṭi.
[[The idea contined in the last line is repeated in the succeeding verses]]
{3:90}__2+
{$} tūṟu cēr cuṭalaiyil cuṭar eri āṭuvar
Civaṉ will dance with a burning fire in his hand in the cremation ground in which shrubs grow thick.
tuḷaṅku oḷi cēr nīṟu cāntam eṉa ukantu aṇivar
he will besmear himself with joy with the very bright holy ash he would smear sandal paste.
veṇpiṟai (1) pulku caṭaimuṭiyār
he has on his matted hirs made into a crown, a white crescent.
nāṟu cāntu iḷa mulai arivaiyōṭu amarntapirāṉ
the master who is united with a lady havings young breasts on which fragrant sandal paste has been smeared.
orupakal vīṟu cēr turuttiyār
stays during day-time in Turutti which has distinctive excellences;
iraviṭattu vēḷvik kuṭi uṟaivar
he stays in Vēḷvikkuṭi during the night
[[Variant reading: (1) malku]]
{3:90}__3+
{$} maḻai, vaḷar iḷamati, malaroṭu, talai pulku vār caṭai mēl
on the long matted hir where the skull, flowers, crescent and clouds are in combination.
kaḻaivaḷar puṉal pukakkaṇṭa em kaṇṇutal kapāliyār (tām)
the god, our Kapāli who has a frontal eye, who made the water which is helpful for the growth of sugar-cane, stay.
iḻaivaḷar tukil alkul arivaiyōṭu amarnta pirāṉ
the chief who was united with a lady with a waist on which is worn a saree and ornaments like mēkali.
oru pakal viḻai(vu) vaḷar turuttiyār
is in Turutti which kindles the desire to worship him agin and agin, during day time
iraviṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
he dwells in Vēḷvikkuṭi during night.
{3:90}__4+
{$} aṉa karumpu vari cilaip perutakaik kāmaṉaik kaviṉ aḻitta
Civaṉ destroyed the beauty of Kāmaṉ of noble mind who had such a great bow fastened with bands
[[As Kāmaṉ disturbed the penance of Civaṉ knowing fully well that his end would follow it, in order that the sufferings of the Tēvar might come to an end, he is sid to be noble-minded.]]
curum poṭu tēṉ malku tūmalark koṉṟai am cuṭarc caṭaiyār
he has a bright cati on which pure koṉṟi flowers are worn and on which honey and bees are profuse
arumpu aṉa vaṉamulai arivaiyoṭu amarnta pirāṉ
The chief who was united with a lady with beautiful breasts like lotus buds
[[arumpu: buds of common cung.]]
oru pakal virumpu iṭam turuttiyār
he is in Turutti, which place he desires, during daytime.
iravu iṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
he will dwell in Vēḷvikkuṭi during night.
{3:90}__5+
{$} vaḷam kiḷar matiyamum poṉ malark koṉṟaiyum vāḷ aravum kaḷam koḷac caṭaiyiṭai vaitta em kaṇṇutaṟ kapāliyār (tām)
our Kapāliyār who has a frontal eye and places on his caṭi a crescent with increasing excellence, golden flowers and a shining cobra having provided for them.
tuḷaṅku nūl mārpiṉar
has on his chest a sacred thread which moves to and fro.
arivaiyoṭu amarnta pirāṉ
the chief who was united with a lady.
oru pakal viḷaṅku nīrttu turuttiyār
is in Turutti which has clear water, during daytime
iraviṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
He will stay during night in Vēḷvikkuṭi.
{3:90}__6+
{$} aṭu puli poṟi ulām urivaiyar
Civaṉ who has a skin of a killing tiger, on which there are stripes.
vari arā pūṇṭu iyaṅkum neṟi ulām pali koḷum nīrmaiyar
has the habit of receiving alms wandering in the street having adorned himself with cobras having lines
cīrmaiyai niṉaippu ariyar
he has a greatness which it is difficult even to think.
maṟi ulām kaiyiṉar
has on his hand a young deer.
maṅkaiyoṭu amarnta pirāṉ
who is united with a lady
oru pakal veṟi ulām turuttiyār
stays in Turutti in which fragrance moves, during daytime
iraviṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
he will stay in Vēḷvikkuṭi during night.
{3:90}__7+
{$} puri taru caṭaiyiṉar
Civaṉ has a twisted caṭi
puli uri araiyiṉar
wears in the waist a tiger's skin.
poṭi aṇintu
smears himself with the holy ash.
tiritarum iyalpiṉar tiripuram mūṉṟaiyum tīvaḷaittār
he burnt with fire that surrounded the three cities of the acurar who were wandering everywhere
[[tiripuram simply means puram as it is followed by mūṉṟu and tiritarum]]
varitaru vaṉa mulai maṅkaiyoṭu amarntapirāṉ
the chief who is united with a young lady of beautiful breasts on which are found lines of sandal paste.
oru pakal viri taru turuttiyār
stays in extensive Turutti during daytime.
iraviṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
will stay in Vēḷvikkuṭi during night.
{3:90}__8+
{$} nīṇṭu ilaṅku avir oḷi neṭu muṭi arakkaṉ
Arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ) who had a tall crown which shone brightly to a great distance due to the precious stones set in it.
i nīḷ varaiyaik kīṇṭu iṭantiṭuvaṉ eṉṟu eḻuntavaṉ āḷ viṉai kīḻppaṭuttār
Civaṉ subdued his efforts when he rose saying I shall dislodge and uproot this high mountin.
nūl pūṇṭa mārpiṉar
wears on the chest a sacred thread.
arivaiyoṭu amarnta pirāṉ
the chief who is united with a lady
oru pakal vēṇṭu iṭam turuttiyār
stays in Turutti, desiring it as his place, during daytime.
iraviṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
will stay in Vēḷvikkuṭi during night.
{3:90}__9+
{$} karai kaṭal aravaṉaik kaṭavuḷum tāmarai nāṉ mukaṉum
the Māl who is sleeping on the bed of a serpent in the roaring sea and the Iramaṉ of four faces seated in the lotus.
kuraikaḻal aṭi toḻa
in order to make them worship the feet wearing jingling anklets, giving up their pride.
kūr eri eṉa niṟam koṇṭāṉ
Civaṉ who had the form of a big fire.
varai keḻu makaḷoṭum pakaliṭam pukaliṭam
the place of refuge during day along with the dughter of the mountin.
vaṇ poḻil cūḻ virai kamaḻ turuttiyār
is Turutti which spreads fragrance and is surrounded by gardens of good products.
iraviṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
he will stay in Vēḷvikkuṭi during night.
{3:90}__10+
{$} ayamuka veyil nilai amaṇarum kuṇṭarum cākkiyarum
the amaṇar who do penance standing in the hot sun which is as scorching as the red hot iron, house holders among them, and followers of buddha.
nayamuka uraiyiṉar
will speak words with pretended love.
nakuvaṉa caritaikaḷ ceytu uḻalvār
they will wander spreading fabricated stories that deserve only ridicule.
[[Not minding their words and stories as of any consequence]]
kayal aṉa vari neṭuṅ kaṇṇiyoṭu amarnta pirāṉ
the chief who is united with the lady who has long eyes with lines in them and are comparable to carp (a kind of fish)
oru pakal viyal nakart turuttiyār
stays in the extensive city of Turutti during daytime.
iraviṭattu vēḷvikkuṭi uṟaivar
He will stay in Vēḷvikkuṭi during night.
{3:90}__11+
{$} oṇ ulām oli kaḻal āṭuvār arivaiyoṭu uṟaipatiyai
on the places where Civaṉ who dances wearing bright and jingling anklets and resides with a lady.
viṇ ulām viri poḻil virai maṇal turutti vēḷvikkuṭiyum
which are Turutti of fragrant sand and a spreading garden which rises up to touch the sky, and Vēḷvikkuṭi
naṇ ulām pukaliyuḷ arumaṟai ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa paṇ ulām aruntamiḻ pāṭuvār paḻi ilar
those who sing the rare Tamiḻ verses composed with music, by Ñāṉacampantaṉ who knows the abstruse Vētam and is living in Pukali which gives refuge to all, and dance out of extasy, are without blemish.

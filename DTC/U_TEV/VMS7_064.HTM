{$} maṉaṉē
my mind!
ceṉṟu aṭai
approach going near Civaṉ
nīṟutāṅkiya tirunutalāṉai
Civaṉ who wears the sacred ash on the forehead in three horizontal lines.
neṟṟik kaṇṇaṉai
who has a frontal eye.
niraivaḷai maṭantai kūṟu tāṅkiya koḷkaiyiṉāṉai
who placed with an idea as a half a lady who wears rows of bangles.
kuṟṟam illiyai
who has not even the slightest fault
kaṟṟai am caṭaimēl
on the bundle of caṭai.
āṟutāṅkiya aḻakaṉai
the beautiful god who bore a river.
amararkku ariya cōtiyai
the light divine which is not easy to be approached by the immortals.
vari (1) varāl ukaḷum cēṟu tāṅkiya tiruttiṉai nakaruḷ civakkoḻuntiṉai
who is the uppermost limit of all good things and who dwells in tiruttiṉainakar which has mire in which the murrel fish with lines, frisks.

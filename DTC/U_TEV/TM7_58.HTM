*1
cātalum piṟattalum tavirttu, _eṉi vakuttu,
taṉ _aruḷ tanta _em talivaṉi; maliyiṉ-
mātiṉi matittu, _aṅku _orpāl koṇṭa maṇiyi;
varupuṉal caṭi_iṭi vitta _emmāṉi;
_ētileṉ maṉattukku _or _irumpu _uṇṭa nīri;
_eṇvaki_oruvaṉi; _eṅkaḷ pirāṉi;
kātil veṇkuḻiyaṉi;---kaṭal koḷa mitanta
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*2
maṟṟu _oru tuṇi _iṉi maṟumikkum kāṇēṉ;
varuntal_uṟṟēṉ; maṟavā varam peṟṟēṉ;
cuṟṟiya cuṟṟamum tuṇi _eṉṟu karutēṉ;
tuṇi _eṉṟu nāṉ toḻappaṭṭa _oṇcuṭari,
muttiyum ñāṉamum vāṉavar _aṟiyā
muṟimuṟi palapala neṟikaḷum kāṭṭik
kaṟpaṉi kaṟpitta kaṭavuḷi,---_aṭiyēṉ
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*3
tirut tiṉinakar _uṟi cēntaṉ_appaṉ(ṉ), _eṉ
ceyviṉi _aṟuttiṭum cempoṉi, _am poṉ
_oruttaṉi, _allatu _iṅku _āriyum _uṇarēṉ;
_uṇarvu peṟṟēṉ, _uyyum kāraṇamtaṉṉāl;
viruttaṉi, pālaṉi, kaṉavu_iṭi viravi
viḻittu _eṅkum kāṇamāṭṭātu viṭṭu _iruntēṉ;
karuttaṉi, niruttam cey kālaṉi,---vēlik
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*4
maḻikku _arumpum malarkkoṉṟiyiṉāṉi
vaḷikkal_uṟṟēṉ; maṟavā maṉam peṟṟēṉ;
piḻittu _oru kāl _iṉip pōyp piṟavāmip
perumi peṟṟēṉ; peṟṟatu _ār peṟukiṟpār?
kuḻik karuṅkaṇṭaṉik kaṇṭukoḷvāṉē
pāṭukiṉṟēṉ; ceṉṟu kūṭavum vallēṉ;---
kaḻikkarumpum katalip palacōli---
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*5
kuṇṭalam kuḻi tikaḻ kātaṉē! _eṉṟum,
koṭumaḻuvāḷpaṭik kuḻakaṉē! _eṉṟum,
vaṇṭu _alampum malarkkoṉṟiyaṉ! _eṉṟum,,
vāy veruvit toḻutēṉ, vitiyālē;
paṇṭi nam palamaṉamum kaḷintu _oṉṟu_āy,
pacupati pati viṉavi, palanāḷum,---
kaṇṭal _am kaḻik kari _ōtam vantu _ulavum---
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*6
varum, perum valviṉi _eṉṟu _iruntu _eṇṇi,
varuntal_uṟṟēṉ; maṟavā maṉam peṟṟēṉ;
virumpi, _eṉ maṉattu_iṭi mey kuḷirppu _eyti,
vēṇṭi niṉṟē toḻutēṉ,vitiyālē;
_arumpiṉi, _alariṉi, _amutiṉi, tēṉi,
_aiyaṉi, _aṟavaṉ, _eṉ piṟavivēr _aṟukkum
karumpiṉi,---peruñ cennel neruṅkiya kaḻaṉi---
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*7
_ayalavar paravavum, _aṭiyavar toḻavum,
_aṉparkaḷ cāyaluḷ _aṭiyal_uṟṟu _iruntēṉ---
muyalpavar piṉ ceṉṟu,muyal vali yāṉi
paṭum _eṉa moḻintavar vaḻi muḻutu _eṇṇi;
puyaliṉi, tiruviṉi, poṉṉiṉatu _oḷiyi,
miṉṉiṉatu _uruvi, _eṉ_iṭip poruḷi,---
kayal_iṉam cēloṭu vayal viḷiyāṭum---
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*8
niṉitaru pāvaṅkaḷ nācaṅkaḷ _āka,
niṉintu muṉ toḻutu _eḻappaṭṭa _oṇcuṭari;
maṉi taru malimakaḷ kaṇavaṉi; vāṉōr
māmaṇimāṇikkatti(m); maṟip poruḷi;
puṉitaru pukaḻiṉi; _eṅkaḷatu _oḷiyi;
_iruvarum, '_oruvaṉ _eṉṟu _uṇarvu _ariyavaṉi;---
kaṉitaru karuṅkaṭal _ōtam vantu _ulavum---
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*9
maṟi_iṭit tuṇintavar maṉi_iṭi _iruppa,
vaṅcaṉi ceytavar poykiyum māya,
tuṟi _uṟak kuḷittu _uḷatu_āka vittu _uytta
_uṇmi _eṉum takaviṉmiyi _ōrēṉ;
piṟi _uṭic caṭiyaṉi, _eṅkaḷ pirāṉi,
pēr_aruḷāḷaṉi, kār_iruḷ pōṉṟa
kaṟi _aṇi miṭaṟu _uṭi _aṭikaḷi,---_aṭiyēṉ
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭēṉē.

*10
ceḻu malarkkoṉṟiyum kūviḷamalarum
viraviya caṭimuṭi _aṭikaḷi niṉintiṭṭu
_aḻum malarkaṇ_iṇi _aṭiyavarkku _allāl,
_aṟivu _aritu, _avaṉ tiruvaṭiyiṇi_iraṇṭum;
kaḻumala vaḷa nakark kaṇṭukoṇṭu, _ūraṉ---
caṭiyaṉtaṉ kātalaṉ---pāṭiya pattum
toḻu malar _eṭutta ki _aṭiyavartammit
tuṉpamum _iṭumpiyum cūḻakilāvē.

{2:26}__1+
{$} (1) puṭaiyiṉ ār puḷḷi kāl poruntiya maṭaiyiṉ ār maṇinīr nelvāyilār
God who is in Nelvāyil which has water as blue as sapphire in the small sluices which is in a stream and has crabs by the side of the fields.
naṭaiyiṉ nālviraṟ kōvaṇam nayanta uṭaiyār
He wears a garment of loin-cloth four inches wide desiring it, as a mark of purity of his conduct
[[nayanta uṭaiyiṉār have become nayantuṭaiyiṉār]]
[[maṇi: sapphire;]]
[[PP: maṇṇuṟa āḻnta maṇi nīrk kiṭaṅkiṉ
(Maturaikkañci, 351);]]
[[maṇi may also mean marble {{or is it not crystal? (jlc)}}]]
[[PP: paḷiṅku vakuttaṉṉa tīm nīr
(Puṟam, 150-27);]]
[[PP_(nālviraṟ kōvaṇam):
kuṟaṅkāṭṭum nālviraliṟ kōvaṇattiṟkulōvippōy
(Campantar, Cāykkatu, 2-10)]]
[[There is ((also)) a reference to aiviraṟkōvaṇa āṭai (Campantar, Pāccilāccirāmam, 6) {1:44_6}]]

{$} aiyaṉ aiyāṟaṉār
the father who is in Aiyāṟu
kaṅkaiyaic caṭaiyuḷvaittār
placed kaṅki inside the caṭi
(1) katir poṟi aravum vaittār
placed a shinning cobra having spots
[[katir is a verb]]
[[PP: katirtta nakai maṉṉum ciṟṟavvai mārkaḷai (kōviyār, 396)]]
tiṅkaḷait tikaḻavaittār
placed the crescent to glow with brightness
ticai ticai toḻavum vaittār
He placed all living beings everywhere to worship him.
maṅkaiyaip pākam vaittār
placed a young lady on one half
māṉ maṟi maḻuvum vaittār
held in his hands a young deer and a battle-axe.
aṅkaiyuḷ aṉlum vaittār
placed in his palm fire
[[Variant reading: (1) katirppoṟi, katirpoti]]

poṭitaṉaip pūcavaittār
lid down rules in civa ākamam to besmear the holy ash. (+++)
poṅku veṇṇūlum vaittār
wore on his chest a sacred thread, of increasing whiteness.
kaṭiyatōr nākam vaittār
wore a fearful cobra
kālaṉaik kāla vaittār
he kicked kālaṉ to make his life to leave his body.
[[EXPL: kālavaittār: trampled and crushed under his feet is another meaning]]
vaṭivuṭai maṅkai taṉṉai mārpil ōrpākam vaittār
placed the lady of beautiful form in a portion of his chest.
aṭi iṇai toḻavum vaittār
in addition to all these he placed his two feet for worship
[[Variant reading: (1) kālil]]

[[PP: vaṇṭu uṇum naṟumalar iṇṭaikoṭu, paṇṭai namviṉaikeṭa eṉṟu (periya tirumoḻi 6-1-1)]]
emakku eṉṟumiṉpam vaittār

aṇṭavāṉavarkaḷ ēttum aiyaṉ aiyāṟaṉār

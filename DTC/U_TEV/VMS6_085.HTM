tirumuṇṭīccarattu ... cintaiyāṉē
see 1st verse.
aṟuttavaṉ (kāṇ) aṭiyavarkaḷ allal ellām
rooted out all the sufferings of his devotees.
arumporuḷāy niṉṟavaṉ (kāṇ)
remined as a thing rare to get.
aṉaṅkaḷ ākam maṟuttavaṉ (kāṇ)
destroyed the body of aṉankaṉ (aṉaṅkaṉ is simply used in the sense of maṉmataṉ, and not in its derivative sense)
malaitaṉṉai matiyātu ōṭi malaimakaḷ taṉ maṉam naṭuṅki vāṉōr añcak kaṟuttavaṉāyk kayilāyam eṭuttōṉ kaiyum katir muṭiyum kaṇṇum pituṅki ōṭac ceṟuttavaṉ (kāṇ)
repressed so that the hands and crowns emitting rays, and eyes to prolude and to snub the pride of the arakkaṉ who angrily lifted the Kayilāyam running towards it without the slightest regard for it, when the mind of the dughter of the mountin trembled, and the celestials were seized with fear.

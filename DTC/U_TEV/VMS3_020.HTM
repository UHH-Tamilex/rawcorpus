{3:20}__1+
{$} mātu amar mēṉiyaṉ āki
having a body in which a lady remains.
vaṇṭoṭu pōtu amar poḻil aṇi pūvaṇattu uṟai vētaṉai
the god who revealed the Vētam-s and who dwells in Pūvaṇam beautified by gardens where the bees settle on the buds about to blossom.
[[vaṇṭoṭu:
oṭu: expletive used to fill up a gap in the metre]]
[[PP: oṭuvum teyyavum icainiṟai moḻiyē (Naṉṉūl, 436)]]
[[PP_EXT: vitaik kuṟu vaṭṭi pōtoṭu potuḷa
(Kuṟuntokai, 155)]]
(1) viravalar araṇam mūṉṟu eyta nātaṉai aṭi toḻa naṉmai ākum (ē)
prosperity will increase to those who worship the feet of the master who discharged an arrow on the forts of the enemies.
[[Variant reading: (1) viravalār]]
{3:20}__2+
{$} vāṉ aṇi mati pulku ceṉṉi vaṇṭoṭu tēṉ aṇi poḻil tiruppūvaṇattu uṟai āṉa nal arumaṟai aṅkam ōtiya ñāṉaṉai aṭi toḻa
to worship the feet of Civaṉ who is himself spiritual knowledge, who chanted the aṅkams and the suitable good, abstruse Vētam-s, and who dwells in Tiruppūvaṇam which is adorned by gardens in which the two varieties of bees vaṇṭu and tēṉ live, the top of which touches the moon that adorns the sky.
naṉmai ākum (ē)
prosperity will increase.
{3:20}__3+
{$} ventuyar uṟupiṇi viṉaikaḷ tīrvatu ōr puntiyar toḻutu eḻu pūvaṇattu uṟai, anti veṇpiṟaiyiṉōṭu āṟu cūṭiya nantiyai aṭi toḻa
to worship waking up from sleep, the feet of Nanti who wears a river with a white crescent that rises in the evening and who dwells in pūvaṇam, with a mind to be cured of the acts, good and bad which are the cause of the diseases and are the cause for acute sufferings,
naṉmai ākum(ē)
prosperity will increase Nanti is one of the names of Civaṉ,
[[nantīvaram eṉṉum nāraṇaṉ (Kōvaiyār, 163);
nanti eṉpatu oru tiru nāmam (do. pērāciriyar)]]
{3:20}__4+
{$} aṭi toḻa
to worship the feet.
vācam nal malar mali mārpil veṇpoṭip pūcaṉai
of Civaṉ who smears on his chest full of fragrant flowers, white sacred ash.
poḻil tikaḻ pūvaṇattu uṟai īcaṉai
the Lord of the universe who dwells in pūvaṇam where gardens are prominent
malar puṉaintu ēttuvār viṉai nācaṉai
who destroys the acts of those who praise him adorning him with flowers.
naṉmai ākum (ē)
their prosperity will increase
{3:20}__5+
{$} kuruntoṭu mātavi kōṅku mallikai poruntiya poḻil tiruppūvaṇattu uṟai, aruntiṟal avuṇartam araṇam mūṉṟu eyta peruntakai aṭi toḻa
to worship the feet of the noble-minded god who destroyed the three forts of the avuṇar of rare strength by discharging an arrow and who dwells in tiruppūvaṇam which has gardens in which there are wild-lime trees, common delight of the woods and jasmine creepers.
pīṭai illai (ē)
there will be no afflictions.
{3:20}__6+
{$} veṟi kamaḻ puṉṉai poṉ ñāḻal vimmiya poṟi aravu aṇi poḻil pūvaṇattu uṟai kiṟipaṭum uṭaiyiṉaṉ
Civaṉ who wears a dress indicating playful mischief, who dwells in pūvaṇam made beautiful by mast-wood trees that spread their fragrance, beautiful feted cassia and curapuṉṉai;
[[aravu-nākam-curapuṉṉai;
oḷitikaḻ utti urukeḻu nākam-oḷi tikaḻā niṉṟa uttiyiṉaiyuṭaiya accattait tarukiṉṟa nākameṉappām piṟku uḷḷatu ataṉ peyarttāya marattiṉkaṇ ēṟṟappaṭṭatu- (paripāṭal, 12-4, parimēlaḻakar)]]
kēṭu il koḷkaiyaṉ
has doctrines which cannot be destroyed.
naṟumalaraṭi toḻa naṉmai ākum(ē)
prosperity will increase to worship his feet which are like fragrant flowers.
{3:20}__7+
{$} paṟai malku muḻavoṭu pāṭal āṭalaṉ
Civaṉ dance and sing to the measuring of time by drum and increasing number of muḻavu
poṟai malku poḻil aṇi pūvaṇattu uṟai, maṟai malku pāṭalaṉ
chants the flourishing vētam and dwells in pūvaṇam adorned by increasing weight of flowers.
mātu or kūṟiṉaṉ
has a lady as one half.
aṟai malku kaḻal toḻa allal illai (ē)
there will be no sufferings to worship his feet wearing Kaḻal whose sound increases.
{3:20}__8+
{$} varaitaṉai eṭutta val arakkaṉ nīḷmuṭiviral taṉil aṭarttavaṉ
Civaṉ pressed down the tall crowns of the strong arakkaṉ who lifted the mountain, by his toe.
veḷḷai nīṟṟiṉaṉ
smears white sacred ash.
porupuṉal puṭai aṇi pūvaṇamtaṉaip paraviya aṭiyavarkku illai pāvam (ē)
there will be no sins to those who praised pūvaṇam, made beautiful on all sides by water which dashes against the banks.
{3:20}__9+
{$} nīr malku malar uṟaivāṉum mālum āy
both Piramaṉ who dwells in a (lotus) flower which flourishes in water and Māl joining together.
cīr malku tiruntu aṭi cēra kiṟkilar
were not capable of reaching the perfect feet whose fame increases.
pōr malku maḻuviṉaṉ mēya pūvaṇam ēr malku malar puṉaintu ēttal iṉpam (ē)
it is happiness to praise by adorning with flowers of increasing beauty pūvaṇam where Civaṉ who has a battle-axe, well-trained in warfare, dwells with desire.
{3:20}__10+
{$} maṇṭai koṇṭu uḻitarum mati il tērarum
the buddhists without intelligence who wander holding an earthern begging bowl.
kuṇṭarum (1) kuṇam ala pēcum kōlattar
and low people camaṇar who have a form and talk useless words.
vaṇṭu amar vaḷar poḻil malku pūvaṇam kaṇṭavar aṭi toḻutu ēttal kaṉmam (ē)
it is a moral duty to praise and worship with joined hands the feet of those who gained sight of Pūvaṇam where growing gardens in which bees rest, are increasing.
[[Variant reading: (1) kuṇampala]]
{3:20}__11+
{$} puṇṇiyar toḻutu eḻu pūvaṇattu uṟai aṇṇalai aṭi toḻutu
worshipping with joined hands the feet of the god who dwells in Pūvaṇam which people of virtuous acts worship getting up from sleep.
amtaṇ kāḻiyaṉ
a native of beautiful and cool Kāḻi.
naṇṇiya arumaṟai ñāṉacampantaṉ paṇṇiya tamiḻ colap paṟaiyum pāvam (ē)
sins will gradually vanish to recite the Tamiḻ verses composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ who had knowledge of the abstruse Vētam-s by intuition.

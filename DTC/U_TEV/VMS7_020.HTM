{7:20}__1+
{$} kōḷili emperumāṉ
my Lord in Kōḷili!
vāḷ aṉakaṇ maṭavāḷ (avaḷ) vāṭivaruntāmē kuṇṭaiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ
I got a little quantity of paddy in Kuṇṭiyūr so that paravi, who has eyes like the sword may not be distressed pining away thinking she could not run the household.
nīḷa niṉaintu aṭiyēṉ umai nittalum kaitoḻuvēṉ
I worship you with joined hands dily, fixing all my thoughts on you since a long time.
emperumāṉ
my Lord!
āḷilai
there are no porters to bring them to ārūr.
avai aṭṭittarappaṇi (ē)
order somebody to carry and bring them to ārūr.
{7:20}__2+
{$} vaṇṭu amarum kuḻalāḷ umai naṅkai ōr paṅkuuṭaiyāy
the Lord who has on one half Umi, a lady of distinction, who has tresses of hir on which bees settle!
viṇṭavartam puram mūṉṟu ericeyta emvētiyaṉē
our brahmin who burnt the three cities of the enemies!
teṇ tirai nīḷvayal cūḻ tirukkōḷili emperumāṉ
our master in tirukkōḷili surrounded by long fields which have clear waves in the water!
aṇṭam atu āyavaṉē
who is the whole universe!
(kuṇṭaiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ
I got a little quantity of paddy in Kuṇṭiyur)
avai aṭṭittarappaṇi(ē)
see 1st verse.
{7:20}__3+
{$} pāti ōr peṇṇai vaittāy
Civaṉ! you placed a lady on one half of your person.
paṭarum caṭaik kaṅkaivaittāy
placed the river, Kaṅki on the spreeding caṭi.
mātar (1) nallār varuttam(m) atu nīyum aṟiti aṉṟē
you too know fully well the distress of the good-natured ladies of the house-hold life when there is want.
ātiyē, aṟputaṉē
Civaṉ who is older than all others! one who is capable of performing wonders!
kōtu ilpoḻil puṭaicūḻ kuṇṭaiyūrccila nellup peṟṟēṉ
I got a small quantity of paddy in Kuṇṭiyūr surrounded by defectless gardens!
avai aṭṭittarappaṇi (ē)
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: (1) nallāḷ]]
{7:20}__4+
{$} uṉai nāṉ colluvatu eṉ
what is there for me to tell you?
toṇṭaivāy umai naṅkaiyaip pulki iṭattil vaittāykku orupūcal ceytār (1) uḷarō
are there any persons who slandered you for wedding Umi, the lady of distinction who has lips red as the common creeper of the hedges and keeping her in your left side?
kollai vaḷam puṟavil kuṇṭaiyūrccila nellup peṟṟēṉ
I got a small quantity of paddy in Kuṇṭiyūr which has forest tract in which the crops of the dry land are grown.
allal kaḷaintu aṭiyēṟku avai aṭṭittarappaṇi (va)
order somebody to carry and bring them to ārūr for your slave, removing his sufferings.
[[Variant reading: (1) uḷarē]]
{7:20}__5+
{$} mullai muṟuval umai oru paṅku uṭai (ya) mukkaṇaṉē
Civaṉ with three eyes who has as a half Umi whose teeth are white as the arabian jasmine flowers!
pal ayarveṇ talaiyil palikoṇṭu uḻal pācupatā
the pācupataṉ who wanders receiving alms in the white skull which seems to lugh with its teeth!
kollai vaḷam puṟaviṉ tirukkōḷili emperumāṉ
our Lord in tirukkōḷili which has forest tract where crops of the dry land are grown.
[[kuṇṭaiyūrc cila nellup peṟṟēṉ see 1st verse]]
allal kaḷaintu (1) aṭiyēṟku avai (2) aṭṭittarappaṇi (ē)

{$} kōḷili emperumāṉ
see 1st verse
kuravu amarum kuḻalāḷ umainaṅkai ōrpaṅkuuṭaiyāy
you have on one half Umi the lady of distinction who adorns her tresses of hir with flowrs of bottle flower tree.
paravai pacivaruttam(m) (atu) nīyum aṟitiyaṉṟē
you too know fully well the suffering of paravi due to hunger.
kuravu amarum poḻilcūḻ kuṇṭaiyūrccila nellup peṟṟēṉ
I got a small quantity of paddy in Kuṇṭiyur surrounded by garden in which the bottle-flower trees are eminent.
aravam acaittavaṉē
Civaṉ who tied cobras!
avai aṭṭittarappaṇi (ē)
see 1st verse.
{7:20}__7+
{$} emperumāṉ uṉaiyē eppoḻutum niṉaintu ēttuvaṉ
I shall prise always you only who is my Lord, fixing my thoughts on you.
vampu amarum kuḻalāḷ orupākam amarntavaṉē
Civaṉ who desired on one half a lady who has fragrant tresses of hir!
cempoṉiṉ māḷikaicūḻ tirukkōḷili emperumāṉ
our Lord in tirukkōḷili surrounded by mansions where there is superior gold.
(kuṇṭaiyūrccila nelluppeṟṟēṉ)
see 1st verse.
aṉpu (atu) āy aṭiyēṟku avai aṭṭittarappaṇi (ē)
order somebody to carry and bring it to ārūr bestowing love on me.
{7:20}__8+
{$} arakkaṉ muṭikaraṅkaḷ aṭarttiṭṭa em ātippirāṉ
our supreme Lord who crushed the heads and hands of the arakkaṉ.
parakkum aravu alkulāḷ paravai (avaḷ) vāṭukiṉṟāḷ
paravi who has a broad waist like the hood of the cobra is pining away.
kurakkuiṉaṅkaḷ kutikoḷkuṇṭaiyūrccila nelluppeṟṟēṉ
I got a small amount of paddy in Kuṇṭiyūr where group of monkeys jump in the trees.
irakkam (atu) āy aṭiyēṟku avai aṭṭittarappaṇi (ē)
order somebody to carry and bring it to ārūr taking pity on me as I have no help of porters.

{$} paṇṭaiyamāl piramaṉ paṟantum iṭantum(m) ayarntum kaṇṭilarāy avarkaḷ kaḻal kāṇpu arituāya pirāṉ
the master whose feet and head could not be seen by the old Māl who dug the earth in search of the head and by Piramaṉ who flew into the sky in search of the feet, they becoming exhusted.

our Lord in tirukkōḷili surrounded by fields having water with clear waves!

{7:20}__10+
{$} kollaivaḷam puṟaviṉ kōḷili mēyavaṉai
on Civaṉ who dwells with desire in Kōḷili which has forest region and produce of dry lands.
nallavar tām paravum tiru nāvalavūraṉ (avaṉ)
the native of tirunāvalur which is prised by good people.
nel iṭaāṭkaḷ vēṇṭi
requesting him to order some porters to carry and deliver the paddy in his house at tiruvārūr.
niṉaintu ēntiyapattum vallār
those who are capable of reciting the ten verses with which he prised Civaṉ fixing his thoughts on him.
allal kaḷaintu (1) ulakil aṇṭar vāṉulaku āḷpavarē
will certinly rule over heaven, having weeded all sufferings in this world.
[[vāṉulaku simply means ulaku as it is preceded by aṇṭar]]

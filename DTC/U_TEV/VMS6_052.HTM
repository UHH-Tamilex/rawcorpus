viṇ iḻi taṇ vīḻimiḻalaiyāṉ
Civaṉ who is in Vīḻimiḻali that descended from heaven.
kaṇṇavaṉ
is the real eye.
kaṇ oḷi cēr kāṭciyāṉ kāṇ
is the power of sight which is possible by the light of the eye joining objects.
kantiruvam pāṭṭu icaiyil kāṭṭukiṉṟa paṇṇavaṉ kāṇ
is the major melody-type which is contined in the works on musicology
[[kantiruvam work on the theory of music; kantaruvam has become kantiruvam]]
paṇṇavaṟṟiṉ tiṟam āṉāṉ kāṇ
is the secondary melody-types of the primary melody-types
[[paṇ: the rākam which has all the seven notes]]
[[tiṟal - tiṟam: the secondary melody-types which has less than the seven notes]]
paḻam ākiccuvaiyākip payakkiṉṟāṉ
gives the benefit by being the fruit and its taste.
maṇ avaṉ kāṇ
is the earth.
nīr avaṉ kāṇ
is the water.
vantu alaikkum (0) mārutaṉ kāṇ
as the wind that blows and hits
maḻai mēkam cēr viṇṇavaṉ kāṇ
is the sky in which there are clouds that pour rin.
viṇṇavarkku mēlāṉāṉ kāṇ
is superior to all the celestials
[[All the kāṇ in this and the following verses are expletives]]
[[NOTE_VAR: (0) MS has marutaṉ; I correct as mārutaṉ according to PIFI and another edition seen by me {jlc}]]

[[kapāli one who holds a skull in his hand]]

kalpoli tōḷ calantaraṉaip piḷanta
benign grace by which he granted the discus which split the body of Calantaraṉ who had shoulders as strong as mountin into two, to the black Māl.
vil politōḷ vicayaṉ vali tēy vittāṉ kāṇ
cused the strength of Vicayaṉ whose shoulders were shining with the bow, to decrease.
vēṭuvaṉāyp pōr porutu kāṭṭiṉāṉ kāṇ
exhibited his valour by fighting with Vicayaṉ by assuming the form of a hunter.
1. taṟparamām taṟparamāy niṟkiṉṟāṉ kāṇ

vittakar tām virumpi ēttum viṇ iḻi taṇ vīḻimiḻalaiyāṉ
Civaṉ who is in cool vīḻmiḻali which descended from heaven, and who is worshipped with desire by skilful persons.
meyttavattil niṟpārkku ellām meyttavaṉ kāṇ
is the real thing to those who are steadfast in sincere penance.
viruppu ilā iruppu maṉaviṉaiyarkku eṉṟum poyttavaṉ kāṇ
is the non-existing thing who conceals himself to those who have a heart as hard as iron and commit bad acts and who have no love towards him
puttaṉ maṟavātu ōṭi eṟi calli putu malarkaḷ ākkiṉāṉ kāṇ
changed as fresh flowers the stones thrown by cakkiya nāyanār running without forgetting to worship Civaṉ in this manner
uḷkiṉārai uyar katikkē uyttavaṉ kāṇ
directed those who thought of him without ceasing, to reach higher stages of existence of the soul.
ulaku aṉaittum oḷittu aḷittiṭṭu uyyac ceyyum vittakaṉ kāṇ
is the skilful person who cuses all the worlds to be saved by absorbing them into himself and protecting them.

kaimmāviṉ uripōrttāṉ kāṇ

ōṅku malaikku araiyaṉ pāvaiyōṭum ōr uruvāy niṉṟāṉ kāṇ

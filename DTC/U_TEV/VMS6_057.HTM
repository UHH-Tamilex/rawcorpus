pāṭṭu āṉa nalla toṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
Civaṉ who wears never-fading garlands of poetry,
[[PP: perukiya ciṟappiṉ mikka aṟcaṉai pāṭṭē ākum
(Periyapurāṇam, taṭittaṭkoṇṭa pirāṇam, 70)]]
paricai aṟiyāmai niṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)
you remined without anybody knowing your nature.
cūṭṭu āṉa tiṅkaḷ muṭiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you wear a chaplet of a crescent on your head.
tūmālai mattam aṇintāy (pōṟṟi)
you adorned yourself with a pure garland of koṉṟi and datura flowers.
(1) āṭṭu āṉatu añcum amarntāy (pōṟṟi)
you desired being bathed in the five products of the cow.
aṭaṅkār puram eriya nakkāy (pōṟṟi)
you smiled to burn the cities of the enemies.
kāṭṭu āṉai meyt tōl urittāy (pōṟṟi)
you flayed the skin from the body of a wild elephant
[[kāṭṭu āṉai the underlined word is a generic epithet, as the elephant was created in the sacrifice performed by the sages of Tārukavaṉam]]
kayilai .... pōṟṟi
see 1st verse of 1st Tiruttaṇṭakam.
[[Variant reading: (1) āṭṭāṉeñcu]]

atirā viṉaikaḷ (1) aṟuppāy (pōṟṟi)
you cut at the root of the acts so that they may not make me tremble with fear.
āla niḻaṟ kīḻ amarntāy (pōṟṟi)
you sat under the shade of the banyan tree.
caturā
able god
caturak kuḻaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
Civaṉ who wears a men's ear-ring with decorum.
cāmpar mey pūcum talaivā (pōṟṟi)
the master who smears on his body sacred ash!
etirā ulakam amaippāy (pōṟṟi)
you grant me the world which has no equal
[[etirā ulakam here means the supreme bliss]]
eṉṟum mīḷā aruḷ ceyvāy (pōṟṟi)
you grant me your grace which is the cuse for not being born agin for ever
katir ār katirukku ōr kaṇṇē (pōṟṟi)
the god who is as precious as the eye to the sun which in has many rays.
kayilai .... pōṟṟi
see 1st verse.
[[variant readings : (1) atirppāy, tīrppāy (2) cāmpaṉmey]]

ceyyāy kariyāy veḷiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have the red colour, black colour and white colour;
[[They refer to the three qualities respectively of irācatam, tāmatam and cāttuvikam]]
cellāta celvam uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have the eight attributes which never leave you
[[The eight qualities are mentioned in the commentary of Parimēlaḻakar, Kuṟaḷ, 9]]
aiyāy periyāy ciṟiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have many wonder you are big and you are small.
ākāya (2) vaṇṇam uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have the nature of the sky
[[The sky's natures are having no form and contining everything in it]]
[[PP_X: vicumpu meyyāka
(Naṟṟiṇi invocating verse, 3)]]
veyyāy; taṇiyāy; aṇiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you are hot, you are cool, you are near at hand;
[[PP: veyyāy taṇiyāy iyamāṉiṉām vimalā
(tiruvācakam, civapuraṇam, 1.30)]]
vēḷāta vēḷvi uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have a sacrifice which you do not perform (this means the power of his grace)
kai ār taḻal ār viṭaṅkā (pōṟṟi)
the god of beautiful form who has in his hand a fire that is united with it.
kayilai ..... pōṟṟi
see 1st verse.
[[variant readings : 1. cevvāy 2. vaṇṇamuṭiyāy]]

āṭci ulakai uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you rule over the world.
aṭiyārkku amutu elām īvāy (pōṟṟi)
you will grant all kinds of happiness to your devotees.
cūṭci ciṟitum ilātāy (pōṟṟi)
you do not have the slightest deception;
[[cūṭci this word is a corruption of cūḻcci:
kāṭci mēya akkaṭi matiṟ katalikai kāṇūuc cūṭci nāṭiya paritiyum kīḻ uṟati toṭarntāṉ; tāṭci ceyyiṉum aṉaiyar pāla aṇukumō tavaṟu! (Kantapurāṇam, tirunakarappaṭalam, 22)]]
cūḻnta kaṭal nañcam uṇṭāy (pōṟṟi)
you consumed the poison that rose in the ocean surrounding the earth.
māṭci peritum uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have good attributes and good actions in an abundant measure.
maṉṉi eṉ cintai makiḻntāy (pōṟṟi)
you felt happy in staying in my mind permanently.
kāṭci peritum 1. ariyāy (pōṟṟi)
you are very difficult to be seen.
kayilai .... pōṟṟi
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: 1. aṟiyāy]]

muṉṉiyāy niṉṟa mutalvā (pōṟṟi)
you are the first cuse and remined as a sage.
[[muṉṉiyāy : the underlined letter is augmentation of rhyme;
cf. paṉiyāy veṅkatir pāypaṭar puṉcaṭai muṉiyāya (nāvukkaracar, maṉattokittirukkuṟuntōki, 3);
tāḻ caṭaip polinta aruntavattōṟkē (puṟam, 1-13)]]
eṉyāy entai pirāṉē (pōṟṟi)
my mother, our father, and master
eḻiṉ icaiyē ukappāy (pōṟṟi)
you rejoice only in hearing music having seven diatonic notes.
maṉṉiya maṅkai maṇāḷā (pōṟṟi)
the husband of Umi who has the age of maṅki permanently.
mantiramum tantiramum āṉāya (pōṟṟi)
you are the mantiram and the and the manual acts in performing worship.
2. kaṉṉi ār kaṅkait talaivā (pōṟṟi)
the husband of the Kumari in the south and Kaṅki full of water.
kayilai .... pōṟṟi
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: 1. muṉṉiyā niṉṟa for this reading the meaning is one who is capable of doing everything out of volition]]
[[variant readings : 1. muṉṉiyā niṉṟa 2. kaṉṉiyā kaṅkait]]

you have all the worlds as your possession.
uṇarvu eṉṉum ūrvatu uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)

you have a form in which you have a civite mark in three horizontal lines made on the forehead, with holy ashes, which is unique.

{$} eṇ mēlum eṇṇam uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have subtle thoughts above the thoughts of the living beings.
ēṟu ariya ēṟum kuṇattāy (pōṟṟi)
you have the nature of riding on a rare bull.
paṇmēlē pāvit tiruntāy (pōṟṟi)
you are partial to music which has all the seven notes.
paṇṇoṭu yāḻ vīṇai payiṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)
you were playing on yāḻ which can produce music and on viṇi
[[yāḻ, vīṇai are different;
vēyṅkuḻal viḷikoḷ nalyāḻ vīṇaiyeṉṟu iṉaiya nāṇa ēṅkiṉaḷ (Kamparāmāyaṇam tāri Pulampuram paṭalam, 3)]]
viṇ mēlum mēlum nimirntāy (pōṟṟi)
you shot up stright above the heaven and beyond that.
mēlārkaḷ mēlārkaḷ mēlāy (pōṟṟi)
you are superior to Ayaṉ and Māl who are considered to be superior to the ordinary celestials.
kaṇ mēlum kaṇ oṉṟu uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have one eye on the foreheads besides the two on the face.

you remined to prise your feet invoking you.
puṇṇiyaṉē (pōṟṟi)
the embodiment of all virtuous acts!
naṇṇal ariyāy (pōṟṟi)
you are difficult to be approached.
ēṟṟu icaikkum vāṉmēl iruntāy (pōṟṟi)
you were on the cloud which produces thunder
[[ēṟu has become ēṟṟu the augmentation is for rhyme]]
eṇ āyiram nūṟu peyarāy (pōṟṟi)
you have one thousand and one hundred names on which devotees meditate.
nāl ticaikkum viḷakku āya nātā (pōṟṟi)
the master who is the light to all the four cordinal directions.
nāṉ mukaṟkum māṟkum ariyāy (pōṟṟi)
you are a varity to Piramaṉ of four faces and Māl
kāṟṟu icaikkum ticaikkum ellām vittē (pōṟṟi)
you are the cuse for all the directions in which the air makes a sound.

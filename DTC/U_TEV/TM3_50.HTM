• patikam: [3:50]
  • talam: tirut taṇṭali nīṇeṟi
  • paṇ: kucikam
  • VMS: Vol.10, p.51

*1
virumpum tiṅkaḷum kaṅkiyum vimmavē,
curumpum tumpiyum cūḻ caṭiyārkku _iṭam---
karumpum cennelum kāy kamukiṉ vaḷam
neruṅkum taṇṭali nīṇeṟi; kāṇmiṉē!

*2
_ikaḻum kālaṉ _itayattum, _eṉ_uḷum,
tikaḻum cēvaṭiyāṉ tiruntum(m) _iṭam---
pukaḻum pūmakaḷum puṇar pūcurar
nikaḻum taṇṭali nīṇeṟi; kāṇmiṉē!

*3
paranta nīlap paṭar_erivalviṭam
karanta kaṇṭattiṉāṉ karutum(m) _iṭam---
curanta mēti tuṟi paṭintu _ōṭiyil
niranta taṇṭali nīṇeṟi; kāṇmiṉē!

*4
tavanta _eṉpum, tavaḷappoṭiyumē,
_uvanta mēṉiyiṉāṉ _uṟiyum(m) _iṭam---
civanta poṉṉum ceḻun taraḷaṅkaḷum
nivanta taṇṭali nīṇeṟi; kāṇmiṉē!

*5
_ilaṅki vēntaṉ _irupatutōḷ _iṟa,
vilaṅkalil _aṭarttāṉ virumpum(m) _iṭam---
calam koḷ _ippi taraḷamum caṅkamum
nilam koḷ taṇṭali nīṇeṟi; kāṇmiṉē!

*6
karu varu _untiyiṉ nāṉmukaṉ, kaṇṇaṉ, _eṉṟu
_iruvarum teriyā _oruvaṉ(ṉ) _iṭam---
ceru varuntiya cempiyaṉkōcceṅkaṇ-
nirupar taṇṭali nīṇeṟi; kāṇmiṉē!

*7
kalavu cīvarattār, kiyil _uṇpavar,
kulavamāṭṭāk kuḻakaṉ _uṟivu_iṭam---
culavu mā matilum, cuti māṭamum,
nilavu taṇṭali nīṇeṟi; kāṇmiṉē!

*8
nīṟṟar, taṇṭali nīṇeṟi nātaṉi,
tōṟṟum mēṉmiyar tōṇipurattu _iṟi---
cāṟṟu ñāṉacampantaṉ---tamiḻ valār
māṟṟu _il celvar; maṟappar, piṟappiyē.

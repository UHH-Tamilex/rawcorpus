{5:16}__1+
{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in Pēreyil
maṟaiyum ōtuvar
chants the vētams
māṉ maṟikkaiyiṉar
holds in his hand a young deer.
[[vētamāṉmaṟiēntutal maṟṟataṉ, nātaṉ nāṉ eṉa naviṟṟum āṟē
(11th book, tiruvottiyūr, orupā orupahtu, 6-10-11)]]
kaṟaikoḷ kaṇṭam uṭaiya kapāliyār
he holds in his hands a skull, thereby deriving the name, Kapāli, and has a neck in which there is poison.
tuṟaiyum pōkuvar
is well versed in all the branches of knowledge naturally
(He is omniscient)
tūyaveṇṇīṟṟiṉar
smears himself with spotless white sacred ash.
piṟaiyum cūṭuvar
will wear a crescent.
{5:16}__2+
{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in pēreyil
kaṇakku ilāraiyum kaṟṟuvallāraiyum vaṇakkilā neṟikaṇṭukoṇṭāraiyum taṇakkuvār
keeps aloof from people who have no learning, those who are learned and proud on account of that, and those who are obstinate in not bowing before god which act is unrighteous.
[[kaṇakku: books; cf. patiṉeṇkīḻkkaṇakku]]
[[taṇakkuvār: ku is an augment used in combination of nouns and verbs.]]
epporuḷaiyum taṇippār
will withdraw into himself all things
piṇakkuvār avar
will cuse them to be opposed to each other.
{5:16}__3+
{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in pēreyil
maḻaicorivippār
will cuse clouds to rin
cūḻkatirttiṅkaḷai virivippār
will cuse the moon with rays surrounding it to shed light
veyiṟpaṭṭaviḷaṅku oḷi erivippār
will cuse the sun which has sunlight to give light to the world in the day.
taṇippār epporuḷaiyum
will control within limits the strength of all things
pirivippār (avar)
will separate things which are united
{5:16}__4+
{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in Pēreyil.
ceṟuvippār, cilaiyāl matil
will cuse the forts to be destroyed by a bow.
tīrttaṅkaḷ uṟuvippār
will save people by creating many holy water resources.
palapattarkaḷ (1) ūḻviṉai aṟuvippār
will cut at the root of fate of many pious souls.
atu aṉṟiyum
in addition to that
nalviṉai peṟuvippār (avar)
will cuse to receive the benefits of good acts.
[[Variant reading: (1) pattarkaḷ uḷviṉai]]
{5:16}__5+
{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in pēreyil
maṟṟaiyār aṟiyār
could not be comprehended by others than true devotees.
maḻuvāḷiṉār
He has a battle-axe in his hand
paṟṟi āṭṭi ōr aintalaippāmpu araic cuṟṟiyār (avar)
he has would round his waist a cobra of five heads making it dance by catching hold of it in his hand.
tūneṟiyāl miku peṟṟiyār (avar)
has great nature by his blemishless path to salvation.
{5:16}__6+
{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in pēreyil
tirukkuvār kuḻal celvaṉ cēvaṭitaṉai irukkuvāy moḻiyāl ēttuvār tuyar curukkuvār
reducing the sufferings of those who prise the lotus red feet of Civaṉ who has Umi who has a twisted and brided long tresses of hir as one half, with the help of the truthful words of the vētams.
tōṟṟaṅkaḷ (1) āṟṟaṟap perukkuvār (avar)
having destroyed the strength of the appearance of those sufferings he will increase the virtuous acts and admit them into his grace.
[[Variant reading: (1)ālaṟap]]

if we speak about Civaṉ in pēreyil.
muṉṉaiyār
he is older than the oldest things.
mayil ūrti murukavēḷ taṉ aiyār
is the father of murukavēḷ who has the vehicle of a peacock.
tāṉ ōr talaimakaṉ
he is a chief without equal
eṉṉai āḷum iṟaiyavaṉ
is the Lord who has me as his protege.
empirāṉ
is my master.
piṉṉaiyār (avar)
is newer than all new thing

{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in pēreyil.
uḻaittum
working hard by doing manual labour and service to him.
tuḷḷiyum
dancing out of ..cstasy
uḷḷattuḷḷē uru iḻaittum
and drawing his form as a picture in the mind.
entaipirāṉ eṉṟu
thinking of him as our father and master.
irāppakal aḻaikkum aṉpiṉarāya aṭiyavar piḻaippai nīkkuvar
will remove the faults of the devotees who bestow their love on him and Utter a loud cry night and day.
{5:16}__9+
{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in pēreyil
nīr ulām nimir puṉcaṭaiyā eṉā ēr ulāvu (1) anaṅkaṉtiṟal vāṭṭiya (pēr)
who destroyed the valour of anaṅkaṉ (cupid) who is very beautiful and who did not prise him as god who has an erect, ruddy caṭi in which water is moving'
vārulā vaṉameṉmulaiyāḷoṭum pēr uḷār (avar)
has a name by being united with a lady who has beautiful, and soft, breasts in which a bodice in worn, which means, arttanaricvaravar' (half female half male) (if anaṅkaṉaivāṭṭiya pēr is the syntactical link this name will be the enemy of Kāmaṉ (cupid))
[[Variant reading: (1) arakkaṉ]]
{5:16}__10+
{$} pēreyilāḷar (ē)
Civaṉ in pēreyil
pāṇi ār paṭutam peyarntu āṭuvar
will perform a dance, called paṭutam' in varying styles appropriate to the measure of time.
tūṇiyār vicayaṟku aruḷ ceytavar
granted Vicayaṉ a weapon pācupatam assuming the form of a hunter carrying a quiver on his back.
māṇiyāy maṇ aḷantavaṉ nāṉmukaḷ pēṇiyār (avar)
was highly esteemed by Māl who measured the earth assuming the form of a bachelor, and Piramaṉ of four faces.

tirumāmalaikkīḻppukku

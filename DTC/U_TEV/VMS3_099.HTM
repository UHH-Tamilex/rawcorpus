{3:99}__1+
{$} muracu atirntu eḻutaru mutukuṉṟam mēviya paracuamar paṭaiyuṭaiyīrē
Civaṉ who has a weapon of paracu and who is in Mutukuṉṟam where the drum muracu increases in sound by being beaten.
paracu amar paṭai uṭaiyīrē umaipparavuvār aracarkaḷ ulakil āvārē
those who prise will become Kings in this world
[[as there are many countries, the plural aracarkaḷ is used]]
{3:99}__2+
{$} moy kuḻalāḷoṭu mutukuṉṟam mēviya pai aravam (m) acaittīrē
Civaṉ who tied a cobra with hold and who remins in Mutukuṉṟam with a lady of dense tresses of hir.
pai aravam acaittīrē (1) umaippāṭuvār naivuilar nāḷtoṟum nalamē
those who sing your prises will have no decrease in their prosperity but it will increase dily.
[[Variant reading: (1) umaipparavuvār]]
{3:99}__3+
{$} muḻavu amar poḻil aṇi mutukuṉṟam mēviya maḻaviṭai(atu) uṭaiyīrē
Civaṉ who has a young bull and who dwells in Mutukuṉṟam which is beautiful by gardens, and where muḻavu is played.
maḻaviṭaiatu uṭaiyīrē umai vāḻttuvār paḻiyoṭu pakai ilar (tām, ē)
those who sing songs of benediction about you will not have any reproach and enmity.
{3:99}__4+
{$} muruku amar poḻil aṇi mutukuṉṟam mēviya uru amar (1) caṭaimuṭiyīrē
Civaṉ who has a beautiful caṭi coiled into a crown and who is in Mutukuṉṟam beautified by gardens where fragrance stays.
uru amar (2) caṭaimuṭiyīrē umai ōtuvār tiruvoṭu tēciṉar (tām ē)
those who prise you will have wealth and fame;
[[tēcu: may also; means lustre of the body]]

VMS8. / {3:99}__PIFI5+
{$} muttitarum uyar mutukuṉṟam mēviya pattumuṭi aṭarttīrē
Civaṉ who pressed down the ten heads (of Irāvaṇaṉ) and who remins in superior Mutukuṉṟam which grants salvation.
pattumuṭi aṭarttīrē umaippāṭuvār citta nalla (v) aṭiyārē
those who sing your prise are devotees who have good minds.
VMS9. / {3:99}__PIFI6+
{$} muyaṉṟavar aruḷpeṟu mutukuṉṟam mēvi aṉṟu iyaṉṟavar aṟivu arayīrē
Civaṉ who was difficult to be seen by the two Māl and Ayaṉ who tried to see your head and feet and who is in Mutukuṉṟam where those who performed penance received your grace.
iyaṉṟavar aṟivu aṟiyīrē umai ēttuvār payaṉtalai niṟpavar (tām, ē)
those who prise you will obtin benefits.
VMS10. / {3:99}__PIFI7+
{$} moṭṭu avar poḻil aṇi mutukuṉṟam mēviyakaṭku amaṇ (1) tēraikkāyntīrē
Civaṉ who destroyed the buddhists and the amaṇar who had religious regulations.
kaṭṭu amaṇ (2) tēraikkāyntīrē umaikkarutuvār ciṭṭarkaḷ cīrpeṟuvārē
those who meditate on you will receive the renown of people of right conduct.
[[variant readings: (1)
tēraraik (2) tēraraik]]
VMS11. / {3:99}__PIFI8+
{$} mūṭiyacōlaicūḻ mutukuṉṟattu īcaṉai nāṭiya ñāṉacampantaṉ,
Ñāṉacampantaṉ who sought the grace of Civaṉ in Mutukuṉṟam surrounded by dense gardens covering it.
nāṭiya ñāṉacampantaṉa centamiḻ pāṭiya avar paḻiilarē
those who sang the refined Tamiḻ verses of Ñāṉacampantaṉ who sought the grace of Civaṉ, will have no reproach.

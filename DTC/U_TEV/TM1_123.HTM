• patikam: 1:123
  • talam: tiru valivalam
  • paṇ: viyāḻak kuṟiñci
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: VOL.10, P.102

*1
pū _iyal purikuḻal; varicili nikar nutal;
_ē _iyal kaṇi, piṇi, _etir viḻi; _umiyavaḷ
mēviya tiru_uru _uṭiyavaṉ---virimalar
mā _iyal poḻil valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*2
_iṭṭam_atu _amar poṭi _icitaliṉ, naci peṟu
paṭṭu _avir pavaḷanalmaṇi _eṉa _aṇi peṟu
viṭṭu_oḷir tiru_uru _uṭiyavaṉ---virimalar
maṭṭu _amar poḻil valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*3
_uru mali kaṭal kaṭivuḻi _ulaku _amar _uyir
veru_uṟu vaki _eḻu viṭam, veḷimali _aṇi
karumaṇi nikar kaḷam _uṭiyavaṉ---miṭitaru
maru mali poḻil valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*4
_aṉal nikar caṭi_aḻal _avi_uṟa _eṉa varu
puṉal nikaḻvatum, mati naṉi poṟi_aravamum
_eṉa niṉivoṭu varum_itum, mela muṭimici
maṉam _uṭiyavar---valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*5
piṭi_ataṉ_uru _umi koḷa, miku kari_atu
vaṭikoṭu, taṉatu _aṭi vaḻipaṭumavar _iṭar
kaṭi, kaṇapati vara _aruḷiṉaṉ---miku koṭi
vaṭiviṉar payil valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*6
tari mutal _ulakiṉil _uyir puṇar taki mika,
viri mali kuḻal _umiyoṭu viravu_atu ceytu,
naritiri keṭu taki_atu _aruḷiṉaṉ---_eḻil
varitikaḻ matil valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*7
nalitaru tari vara naṭi varum _iṭiyavar
politaru maṭavaraliyar maṉi_atu puku
pali koḷa varupavaṉ---_eḻil miku toḻil vaḷar
vali varu matil valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*8
_iravaṇaṉ _irupatukaram _eḻil malitaṉiṉ
_ira vaṇam niṉitara _avaṉ muṭi poṭiceytu,
_iravaṇam _amar peyar _aruḷiṉaṉ---nakaneti
_iravu _aṇa nikar valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*9
tēṉ _amartaru malar _aṇipavaṉ, vali mikum
_ēṉam_atu_āy nilam _akaḻ _ari, _aṭi muṭi
tāṉ _aṇiyā _uru _uṭiyavaṉ---miṭi koṭi
vāṉ _aṇi matil valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*10
_ili malitara miku tuvar_uṭiyavarkaḷum,
nilimiyil _uṇal _uṭiyavarkaḷum, niṉivatu
toli vali neṭumaṟi toṭar vaki _uruviṉaṉ---
mali mali matil valivalam _uṟi _iṟiyē.

*11
maṉṉiya valivalanakar _uṟi _iṟivaṉi,
_iṉ _iyal kaḻumalanakar _iṟi---_eḻil maṟi
taṉ _iyal kali vala tamiḻvirakaṉatu---_uri
_uṉṉiya _orupatum _uyarporuḷ tarumē.

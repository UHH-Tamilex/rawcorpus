{5:12}__1+
{$}
karaintu kai toḻuvāraiyum kātalaṉ
Civaṉ loves those who worship him with folded hands, their hearts melting
[[karaintu: prising him, with open mouths is another meaning]]
varaintu
denying his existence
vaitu ēḻuvāraiyum vāṭalaṉ
he does not cuse the heterodox religionists who wake up from sleep abusing him, to grieve.
niranta 1. pāriṭattōṭu avar nittalum viraintu pōvatu vīḻi miḻalaikkē
he goes hurriedly dily along with the pūtams which accompany him in rows, to viḻimiḻali to grant his grace to his devotees.
[[Variant reading: 1. pāriṭattōr ]]
{5:12}__2+
{$}
ēṟṟu velkoṭi īcaṉ
Civaṉ who has a flag of a bull which is victorious.
taṉ ātirai
during the festival of ārtiri
nīṟṟuc cantaṉa veḷḷai viravalār
will not smear himself with the white holy ash to which he is partial like the sandal paste.
vēṟṟuk kōlam koḷ vīḻi miḻalai
in vīḻimiḻali where he takes different forms.
nāṟṟam cūṭuvar
will adorn his head with fragrant flowers
nal naṟum tiṅkaḷār
will wear a very good crescent
{5:12}__3+
{$}
puṉai poṟcūlattaṉ
Civaṉ has an adorned trident made of gold.
pōr viṭai ūrtiyāṉ
rides on a bull which can give battle.
viṉai vel nākattaṉ
adorns himself with cobras and is naturally free from Karmam
veṇ maḻuvāḷiṉāṉ
has a battle-axe which is sharp.
viṉai ilār toḻum vīḻimiḻalai
vīḻimiḻali is the place worshipped by persons free from Karmam.
{5:12}__4+
{$}
māṭattu āṭum maṉattu uṭaṉ vaittavar
Māl who placed civa in the vimāṉam and his heart simultaneously.
kōṭattār
and Piramaṉ who chants aloud the vētam (kōṭam-kōṣam)
kurukkēttirattār palar
many people belonging to kurukkettiram.
pāṭattār
brahmins who preserve the text of the vētams.
paḻippār 1. paḻippu allatu ōr vēṭattār
who has a form without blemish by people who despise him.
toḻum vīḻimiḻalai
viḻimiḻali is the place worshipped by the above-mentioned persons.
[[Variant reading: 1. paḻippallāta]]
{5:12}__5+
{$}
eṭutta vel koṭi ēṟu uṭaiyāṉ tamar
the devotees of Civaṉ who has the flag of a victorious bull held acloft.
kōvaṇam uṭuppar
will have loin-cloth only as their dress
piccaiyē uṇpatu
it is only alms which is their food.
keṭuppatu āvatu 1. kīḻ niṉṟa valviṉai
it is the irresistible Karmams that were done in previous births that they destroy.
vīḻimiḻalaikkē viṭuttup pōvatu
they go to vīḻimiḻali having given up all wordly attachments.
[[Variant reading: 1. kīṇiṉṟa]]
{5:12}__6+
{$}
kuḻalai yāḻ moḻiyār icai vēṭkaiyāl
by the love placed on women who speak sweet words like the music of the flute and yāḻ.
uḻalai yākkaiyai ūṇum uṇarvilīr
men who have no sense and therefore make the body that strays into bad ways, strong.
cāṟṟiṉōm
we proclimed you many times to be known by many people
taḻalai nīr maṭik koḷḷaṉ miṉ
do not carry the fire in the fold of the cloth. (maṭi : fold in a cloth wrapped round the waist, answering for a poenet)
miḻalaiyāṉ aṭicāra viṇ āḷvarē
you can obtin the right to reign over heaven if you reach the feet of Civaṉ in miḻali.
[[Variant reading: 1. miḻalai]]
{5:12}__7+
{$}
tīraṉ
the valiant Civaṉ
tīttiraḷaṉ
holds a mass of fire in his hand (has a red body like the ball of fire)
caṭaittaṅkiya nīraṉ
has water that stays in his caṭi
āṭiya nīṟṟaṉ
smears all over his body with holy ash.
vaṇṭār koṉṟait 1. tāraṉ mālaiyaṉ
wears on the chest a garland of koṉṟi on which bees hum loudly; has a garland of the same flower
taṇ naṟuṅkaṇṇiyaṉ
wears a cool and fragrant chaplet of the same flower;
[[kaṇṇi kār naṟuṅkoṉṟai kāmar, vaṇṇamārpil tārum koṉṟai (puṟam, 1-1-2);
kār viri koṉṟaip poṉ ēr putumalart, tāraṉ mālaiyaṉ malainta kaṇṇiyaṉ (akam, invocatory verse, 11.1-2);
tāraṉ, mālaiyaṉ, malainta kaṇṇiyaṉ eṉṟatu, tār ōr vicēṭamāka iṭuvatu;mālai aḻakukku iṭuvatu; kaṇṇi pōrpū; iṉi tār cērppatākavum, mālai kaṭṭuva tākavum kaṇṇi taṉittut toṭuppatākavum ivvēṟupāṭukaḷ eṉa uraippārum uḷar (do. old gloss)]]
vīraṉ vīḻimiḻalai vikirtaṉ
the god different from the world who is in vīḻimiḻali is a hero.
[[Variant reading: 1. tār naṉ mālaiyaṉ]]
{5:12}__8+
{$}
iṟai ār eriyiṉār
Civaṉ holds fire in his hand.
iṭukāṭṭiṭai nariyiṉār
dwells in the cremation ground which has foxes.
pariyā makiḻkiṉṟatu ōr periyaṉār
is a noble person who is always joyful without being distressed.
piṟappōṭu cātalai viriyiṉār toḻum vīḻimiḻalaiyē
viḻimiḻali is the place worshipped by people who are afrid of birth and death.
{5:12}__9+
{$}
nīṇṭa cūḻ caṭaimēl ōr nilāmati
a bright crescent is on the long cati tied in coils round the head.
kāṇṭu cēvaṭi mēl ōr kaṉaikaḻal
on the red-lotus foot there is a Kaḻal that makes a sound within earshot
vēṇṭuvār avar vītipukuntilar
Civaṉ who is desired by us has not entered into our street.
vīḻi miḻalaikkē mīṇṭum pōvatu
is it right that Civaṉ returns to vīḻimiḻali itself? (is this his grace?)
[[These are the words of a lady who was robbed of her heart by the beauty of Civaṉ.]]
{5:12}__10+
{$}
pālai yāḻoṭu cevvaḻi paṇkoḷa mēlai vāṉavar vantu vaḻipaṭum (vīḻimiḻalai)
the celestials come in the evening with their yāḻ and sing songs in the melody types of pāli and cevvaḻi
ālai ār aḻal antaṇar ākuti vēlaiyār toḻum vīḻimiḻalai
in that evening the brahmins who perform the act of offering oblations in the consecrated fire in the hall and who worship vīḻimiḻali, is the abode of Civaṉ.
[[ālai a hall where sacrifice is performed;
ālai acar vēḷvi aḻittāṉ kaṇṭāy (appar, tirumaṟikkāṭu, taṇṭakam, 7)]]

maḻalai ēṟṟu maṇāḷaṉ tirumalai cuḻala ārttu eṭuttāṉ muṭi tōḷ iṟak kaḻal koḷ kāl viral ūṉṟalum
when Civaṉ pressed down the toe of the foot in which he wears a Kaḻal, to break the heads and shoulders of the arakkaṉ who lifted, roaring, the sacred mountin belonging to Civaṉ, the husband of Umi, and who has a tender bull, to cuse it to whirl
miḻalaiyāṉ aṭivāḻka eṉa viṭṭatē
when he cried, Let the feet of miḻaliyāṉ live long' he set him free without further punishment.

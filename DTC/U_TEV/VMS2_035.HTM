{2:35}__1+
{$} pāriṭam cūḻ iravil puṟaṅkāṭṭu eriyāṭi
Civaṉ who dances in the fire in the cremation-ground at night to be surrounded by pūtams.
aravac caṭai antaṇaṉ
one who has warm love on his devotees and on whose caṭi there are cobras.
mēya aḻaku ār kuravap poḻil cūḻ kuraṅku āṭu tuṟai parava valviṉai keṭum
if one prises Kuṟaṅkāṭutuṟi surrounded by gardens of beautiful bottle-flower trees, in which he dwells, the irresistible Karmams will be destroyed.
{2:35}__2+
{$} viṇṭār puram mūṉṟum erittavimalaṉ
the spotless god who burnt the three cities of the enemies.
iṇṭu ār puṟaṅkāṭṭiṭai niṉṟu eriyāṭi
who dances in the fire in the cremation-ground in which the tiger-stopper grows
[[PP_X: iṇṭu: iṇṭu ivar īṅkaiya curaṉē-
(Naṟṟiṇi 2-6.)]]
vaṇṭu ār karumeṉ kuḻal maṅkai ōr pākam koṇṭāṉ nakar (pōl) kuraṅkāṭu tuṟai
Kuraṇkāṭutuṟi is the city of Civaṉ who had as a half a young lady who has soft and black tresses of hir on which bees hum loudly.
[[PP: pōl : expletive; 5,7 and 8.]]
{2:35}__3+
{$} niṟaivu il puṟaṅkāṭṭiṭai
in the cremation which is never full of corpses, so that it may not say, `there is no place for the corpses yet to come'.
nēriḻaiyōṭum
with a lady who adorns herself with jewels fitting her beauty.
iṟaivu il eriyāṉ maḻu ēnti niṉṟu āṭi
Civaṉ who has fire that never extinguishes, and dances standing holding a red-hot iron.
maṟaiyiṉ oli vāṉavar tāṉavar ēttum kuṟaivu illavaṉ ūr kuraṅkāṭu tuṟaiyē
Kuraṇkāṭutuṟi is the place of Civaṉ who has nothing to want and who is prised by the celestials and acurar with the words of vētam.
{2:35}__4+
{$} viḻikkum nutalmēl oru veṇpiṟai cūṭi
having adorned himself with a white crescent on the forehead in which the eye opens on occasions;
[[PP_EXT_(nutal mēl piṟai cūṭi):
piṟai nutal vaṇṇam ākiṉṟu
(Puṟam, 1-9)]]
teḻikkum puṟaṅkāṭṭiṭaic cērntu eriyāṭi
reaching the cremation ground which is full of many noises and dancing in the fire.
paḻikkum paricē palitērntavaṉ ūr
the place of Civaṉ who begged alms to be ridiculed.
poṉ koḻikkum puṉal cūḻ kuraṅkāṭu tuṟai
is Kuraṅkāṭutuṟi surrounded by water which washes on the banks gold.
{2:35}__5+
{$} nīṟu ār taru mēṉiyaṉ
having smeared sumptuously with sacred ash on his body.
neṟṟi ōr kaṇṇaṉ
having an eye on the forehead.
ēṟu ār koṭi em iṟai
our Lord who has a flag on which is drawn the form of a bull.
īṇṭu eri āṭi
having danced in a big fire.
āṟu ārcaṭai antaṇaṉ
the brahmin who has on his caṭi the river (Kaṅki).
āyiḻaiyāḷ ōr kūṟāṉ nakar (pōl) kuraṅkāṭutuṟaiyē
Kuraṅkaṭutuṟi is the city of the god who has as his half a lady wearing choice ornaments
[[pōl : expletive.]]
{2:35}__6+
{$} naḷirum malark koṉṟaiyum nāṟu karantait tuḷirum culavi
having coiled the cool koṉṟi flowers and the tender leaves of the indian globe thistle.
cuṭukāṭṭu eri āṭi
and dancing in the fire in the cremation ground.
miḷirum aravu ārttavaṉ mēviya kōyil
the temple desired by Civaṉ who tied shining cobras.
kuḷirum, puṉalar cūḻ kuraṅkāṭutuṟai
is Kuraṅkāṭutuṟi surrounded by cool water.
{2:35}__7+
{$} paḻakum viṉai tīrppavaṉ
Civaṉ who removes the Karmams associated with the soul in previous births.
pārppatiyōṭum muḻavam kuḻal montai muḻaṅka
when the muzhavam, flute and monti are played on a high pitch.
eri āṭum aḻakaṉ
the beautiful god who dances in fire.
ayil mūvilai vēl valaṉ ēntum kuḻakaṉ nakar (pōl) kuraṅkāṭu tuṟai
the city of the youth who holds in his right hand a sharp trident of three blades is Kuraṅkāṭutuṟi
[[pōl is expletive.]]
{2:35}__8+
{$} varai ārttu eṭutta (v) arakkaṉ vali olka nirai ār viralāl nerittiṭṭavaṉ ūr (ām)
the place of Civaṉ who pressed down with the rows of toes, to diminish the strength of the arakkaṉ who lifted the mountin with a roaring noise.
kaṟai ārntu iḻi kāvirik kōlakkaraimēl kurai ār poḻil cūḻ kuraṅkāṭu tuṟaiyē
is Kuraṅkāṭutuṟi surrounded by gardens of noise, situated on the beautiful bank of the Kāviri which descends filling the banks.
{2:35}__9+
{$} neṭiyāṉoṭu nāṉ mukaṉum niṉaivu oṇṇāp paṭiyākiya paṇṭaṅkaṉ
Civaṉ who performed the dance of pāṇṭaraṅkam and who assumed the form of fire, could not even by thought by Māl and Piramaṉ of four faces.
niṉṟu eri āṭi
who dances standing in fire.
ceṭi ārtalai ēntiya ceṅkaṇ veḷḷēṟṟiṉ koṭiyāṉ nakar (pōl) kuraṅkāṭu tuṟaiyē
Kuraṅkāṭutuṟi is the city of Civaṉ who holds in his hand a skull of bad odour and has on his flag the form of a white bull of red eyes indicative of anger.
[[pōl expletive.]]
{2:35}__10+
{$} tuvar āṭaiyar
buddhists who wear robes soaked in myrtle-dye.
(1) vēṭamalāc camaṇkaiyar
and the low camaṇar who do not perform acts appropriate to their robes of ascetics.
kavarvāymoḻi kātal ceyyātavaṉ ūr
the place of Civaṉ who does not like their doubtful words that deviate from righteousness
navai ār maṇi poṉ akil cantaṇam unti
pushing the broken precious stones, gold, eagle-wood and sandalwood.
kuvai ār karaicēr kuraṅkāṭutuṟai ām
is Kuraṇkāṭutuṟi where (the Kāviri) collects and heaps them on the banks.
[[Variant reading: (1) vēṭamilāccamaṇ]]
{2:35}__11+
{$} kol ēṟu uṭaiyāṉ kuraṅkāṭutuṟai mēl
on Kuraṅkāṭutuṟi which is the temple of Civaṉ who has a bull capable of killing by going.
nallār payil kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kaḻi where good people are many in number.
col ār tamiḻ mālai pattum toḻutu ēttavallār (avar)
those who are capable of prising Civaṉ worshipping him with joined hands, with the garland of tamiḻ verses full of fame.
vāṉavarōṭu uṟaivār
will live with the celestials in heaven

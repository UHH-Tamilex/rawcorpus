*1
kaṇ kāṭṭum nutalāṉum, kaṉal kāṭṭum kiyāṉum,
peṇ kāṭṭum _uruvāṉum, piṟi kāṭṭum caṭiyāṉum,
paṇ kāṭṭum _iciyāṉum, payir kāṭṭum puyalāṉum,
veṇkāṭṭil _uṟivāṉum---viṭi kāṭṭum koṭiyāṉē.

*2
pēy _aṭiyā, pirivu _eytum; piḷḷiyiṉōṭu _uḷḷam niṉivu-
_āyiṉavē varam peṟuvar; _aiyuṟa vēṇṭā, _oṉṟum;
vēy _aṉa tōḷ _umi paṅkaṉ veṇkāṭṭu mukkuḷanīr
tōy viṉiyār _avartammit tōyā_ām, tīviṉiyē.

*3
maṇṇoṭu, nīr, _aṉal, kālōṭu, _ākāyam, mati, _iravi,
_eṇṇil varum _iyamāṉaṉ, _ikaparamum, _eṇticiyum,
peṇṇiṉoṭu, _āṇ, perumiyoṭu, ciṟumiyum, _ām pērāḷaṉ---
viṇṇavarkōṉ vaḻipaṭa---veṇkāṭu _iṭamā virumpiṉaṉē.

*4
viṭam _uṇṭa miṭaṟṟu _aṇṇal veṇkāṭṭiṉ taṇpuṟavil,
maṭal viṇṭa muṭattāḻimalar niḻalik kuruku _eṉṟu,
taṭam maṇṭu tuṟik keṇṭi, tāmariyiṉpū maṟiya,
kaṭal viṇṭa katir muttam naki kāṭṭum kāṭciyatē.

*5
vēli mali taṇkāṉal veṇkāṭṭāṉ tiruvaṭikkīḻ
māli mali vaṇ cāntāl vaḻipaṭu nal maṟiyavaṉtaṉ
mēl _aṭar veṅkālaṉ _uyir viṇṭa piṉi, namaṉtūtar,
_ālamiṭaṟṟāṉ _aṭiyār _eṉṟu, _aṭara _añcuvarē.

*6
taṇmatiyum veyya(_a)ravum tāṅkiṉāṉ, caṭiyiṉ_uṭaṉ;
_oṇmatiyanutal _umi _ōrkūṟu _ukantāṉ; _uṟi kōyil---
paṇ moḻiyāl _avaṉ nāmampala _ōta, pacuṅkiḷḷi
veṇ mukil cēr karumpeṇimēl vīṟṟirukkum veṇkāṭē.

*7
cakkaram māṟku _īntāṉum; calantaraṉip piḷantāṉum;
_akku _arimēl _acittāṉum; _aṭintu _ayirāvatam paṇiya,
mikku _ataṉukku _aruḷ curakkum veṇkāṭum, viṉi turakkum
mukkuḷam, naṉku _uṭiyāṉum---mukkaṇ _uṭi _iṟiyavaṉē.

*8
paṇ moytta _iṉmoḻiyāḷ payam _eyta mali _eṭutta
_uṉmattaṉ _uram nerittu, _aṉṟu _aruḷceytāṉ _uṟi kōyil---
kaṇ moytta karu maññi naṭam_āṭa, kaṭal muḻaṅka,
viṇ moytta poḻil varivaṇṭu _ici muralum veṇkāṭē.

*9
kaḷ _ār ceṅkamalattāṉ, kaṭal kiṭantāṉ, _eṉa _ivarkaḷ
_oḷ _āṇmi koḷaṟku _ōṭi, _uyarntu _āḻntum, _uṇarvu _ariyāṉ---
veḷ_āṉi tavam ceyyum mētaku veṇkāṭṭāṉ _eṉṟu
_uḷ _āṭi _urukātār _uṇarvu, _uṭimi, _uṇarōmē.

*10
pōtiyarkaḷ piṇṭiyarkaḷ miṇṭumoḻi poruḷ _eṉṉum
pētiyarkaḷ _avar; pirimiṉ! _aṟivu_uṭiyīr! _itu kēṇmiṉ:
vētiyarkaḷ virumpiya cīr viyaṉtiru veṇkāṭṭāṉ _eṉṟu
_ōtiyavar yātum _oru tītu _ilar _eṉṟu _uṇarumiṉē!

*11
taṇpoḻil cūḻ caṇpiyarkōṉ---tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ---
viṇ poli veṇpiṟic ceṉṉi vikirtaṉ _uṟi veṇkāṭṭip
paṇ poli centamiḻmāli pāṭiya pattu_ivi vallār,
maṇ poliya vāḻntavar, pōy vāṉ poliyap pukuvārē.

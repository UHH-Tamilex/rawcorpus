{5:17}__1+
{$}
muttiṉaip pavaḷattai muḷaitta em tottiṉai
Civaṉ who is the pearl, the coral and our bunch of flowers that sprout out.
cuṭarai
the bright light.
cuṭar pōl oḷip 1. pittaṉai
who has abundant love towards his devotees and whose brilliance is like the sun.
kolum nañciṉai
who is the poison that kills.
vāṉavar nittaṉai
the god without death among the tēvar.
neru naṟkaṇṭa veṇṇi(ē)
veṇṇi is in the place where I obtined a vision of him yesterday.
[[Variant reading: 1. pittaṉai]]
{5:17}__2+
{$}
veṇṇit tol nakar mēviya tiṅkaḷār
Civaṉ who wears crescent and who dwells in the ancient city of veṇṇi
kaṇṇit totta caṭaiyar
who has a caṭi on which there is a bundle of chaplets of koṉṟi.
kāpāliyār
who holds a skull in the hand.
eṇṇit tammai niṉaintiruntēṉukku eṉ nāvukku aṇṇittiṭṭu amutu ūṟum
he will flow as sweet as nectar in my tongue tasting sweet, as I was always thinking of him and meditating on him. (nāvukku-nāvil)
{5:17}__3+
{$}
kāṟṟiṉai
Civaṉ who is the air.
kaṉalai
the fire.
katir mā maṇi nīṟṟiṉai
who smeared his body with sacred ash like the big diamond which emits rays.
niṉaippār viṉai nīkkiṭum, kūṟṟiṉai utaittiṭṭa kuṇam uṭai(ya) vīṟṟiṉai
the diverse thing that kicked the god of death and who removes the acts of those who think of him.
nerunaṟkaṇṭa veṇṇi(ē)
veṇṇi which is the place where I obtined vision of Civaṉ yesterday.
{5:17}__4+
{$}
nallaṉai
Civaṉ who is the embodiment of all that is good.
tikaḻ nāṉmaṟai ōtiyai
who chants the eminent four vetam.
collaṉai
who is the form of words.
cuṭarai
who is the light.
cuṭar pōl 1. oḷir kallaṉai
who has the mountin, Kayili, which is shining like the sun.
kaṭimāmatil mūṉṟu eyta villaṉai nerunaṟ kaṇṭa veṇṇi(ē)
veṇṇi is the place where I obtined vision of Civaṉ who destroyed with his bow the three great and well-guarded forts, yesterday.
[[Variant reading: 1. oḷikkallaṉai]]
{5:17}__5+
{$}
cuṭaraip pōl 1 oḷir cuṇṇa veṇṇīṟṟaṉai
Civaṉ who has smeared himself with the fine powder of sacred ash which shines like the sun.
aṭarum ceṉṉiyil vaitta amutiṉai
who is like the nectar and who placed on his head petals of flowers and other things.
paṭarum ceñcaṭaip pāl mati cūṭiyai
who wears on his spreading red cati a crescent as white as milk.
iṭarai nīkkiyai yāṉ kaṇṭa veṇṇi(ē)
I obtined vision of Civaṉ who removes sufferings, in veṇṇi.
[[Variant reading: 1. oḷic cuṇṇa]]
{5:17}__6+
{$}
pūta nātaṉai
Civaṉ the master of the pūtams.
pūmpukalūraṉai
who is in beautiful pukalūr.
tātu eṉat tavaḻum mati cūṭiyai
who wears on his head a crescent which moves slowly and is like the pollen in colour.
nātaṉai
who is the master.
nalla nāṉmaṟai ōtiyai
who chants the four vetams contining good things.
vētaṉai neru naṟkaṇṭa veṇṇi(ē)
I obtined vision of Civaṉ who is the meaning of the vetams, yesterday in veṇṇi.

oruttiyai oru pākattu aṭakkiyum
having constrined one lady in one half.
puripuṉcaṭai porutti puṉitaṉai
the pure Civaṉ who fixed another lady (Kaṅki) on his twisted ruddy caṭi.
karuttaṉai
who dwells in my mind.
kaṟaik kaṇṭaṉai
who has a black neck.
kaṇṇutal niruttaṉai
who is the dancer and who has a frontal eye.
nerunaṟ kaṇṭa veṇṇi(ē)
I obtined vision of him yesterday in veṇṇi.
{5:17}__8+
{$}
caṭaiyaṉai
Civaṉ who has a caṭi
cari kōvaṇa āṭai koṇṭu uṭaiyaṉai
who has a dress of men's loin-cloth which slides down.
uṇarvār viṉai tīrttiṭum paṭaiyaṉai maḻuvāḷoṭu
who has a battle-axe in addition to other weapons and who removes the acts of those who realize him in themselves.
pāy tarum viṭaiyaṉai
who has a galloping bull
neru naṟ kaṇṭa veṇṇi(ē)
I had obtined vision of him yesterday in vaṇṇi.
{5:17}__9+
{$}
poruppaṉai
Civaṉ who has as his abode, the mountin Kayili.
puṉalāḷoṭu puṉcaṭai arumpu aṉai(ya) iḷantiṅkaḷ am kaṇṇiyāṉ
who wears as a chaplet a crescent which is like a bud along with Kaṅki who is in the form of water, on his ruddy caṭi
[[arumpu has become aruppu to suit rhyme]]
paruppatam paravit toḻum toṇṭarkaḷ viruppiṉai
who loves the devotees who prise and worship him with joined hands in paruppatam (Kayili or Sricilam)
nerunaṟkaṇṭa veṇṇi(ē)
I obtined vision of him yesterday in veṇṇi.

vañcaṉai 1. cūlavalla em cuntaraṉ
our beautiful god who can scoop out the treachery of those who practise it
kōlamā aruḷ ceytatu or koḷkaiyāṉ
has the principle of bestowing his grace showing his beautiful form.
kālaṉ añca utaittu
kicking the god of death to become frightened.
iruḷ kaṇṭam ām vēlai nañcaṉaik kaṇṭatu veṇṇi(ē)
I obtined vision of the god who has a black neck by consuming the poison that rose in the ocean, yesterday in veṇṇi.

ilaiyiṉ ār koṉṟai cūṭiya īcaṉār
the Lord of the universe, civan, who wears on his head koṉṟi flowers along with leaves.
malaiyiṉāl arakkaṉ tiṟal vāṭṭiṉār
destroyed valour of the arakkaṉ by a mountin.
cilaiyiṉāl matil eytavaṉ
who destroyed the forts by a bow.
veṇṇiyait talaiyiṉāl toḻuvār viṉai tāvum (ē)
the acts of those who bow their heads in worshipping veṇṇi, will disappear.

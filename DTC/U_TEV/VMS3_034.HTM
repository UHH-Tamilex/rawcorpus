{3:34}__1+
{$} vaṇṇamāmalarkoṭu vāṉavar vaḻipaṭa
the celestials to worship the Lord with big flowers of colours.
aṇṇalār āyiḻaiyāḷoṭum amarvu iṭam
the place where Civaṉ dwells with desire with a lady who wears choice ornaments.
viṇṇiṉ māmaḻai poḻintu iḻiya veḷḷaruvicēr tiṇṇil ār puṟavu aṇi tirumutukuṉṟamē
is Tiru Mutukuṉṟam which is made beautiful by the forest region in the hard earth, which has white streams descending as the clouds rin from the sky.
{3:34}__2+
{$} veṟi ulām koṉṟai amtāriṉāṉ
the Lord who wears a garland of koṉṟi whose fragrance spreads everywhere.
mētaku (1) poṟi ulām aravu acaittu āṭi ōr puṇṇiyaṉ
who dances tying a cobra which has eminent spots and who is the embodiment of all virtuous acts.
maṟi ulām kaiyiṉāṉ maṅkaiyōṭu amarvu iṭam
who has on his hand a young deer, the place where he dwells with desire with a lady.
ceṟiyuḷ ār puṟavu aṇi tirumutukuṉṟamē
is tirumutukuṉṟam which is made beautiful by dense forest tracts.
[[Variant reading: (1) poṟiyulā aravu]]
{3:34}__3+
{$} (1) ēṟiṉār viṭaimicai imaiyavar toḻa
the Lord rode on the bull to be worshipped by the celestials who do not wink.
umaikūṟaṉār
has on his half Umi.
kolpulittōliṉār
has a dress of a skin of a tiger that can kill.
mēṉimēl nīṟaṉār
smears on his body sacred ash.
niṟai puṉal caṭaiyaṉār nikaḻviṭam
the place where he who has on his caṭi flood of Kaṅki is present.
tēṟal ār poḻil aṇi tirumutukuṉṟamē
is tirumutukuṉṟam made beautiful by gardens where there is abundant honey.
[[Variant reading: (1) ēṟaṉār]]
{3:34}__4+
{$} umaiyoṭum uraiyiṉ ār uṟuporuḷ (1) āyiṉāṉ
Civaṉ is not only the words but also the meanings contined in those words, being united with Umi. (Umi is in the form of words; Civaṉ is in the form of their meanings)
[[cf. collāṉaip poruḷāṉai (nāvukkaracar, tirunāriyūr, 1)]]
viraiyiṉ ār koṉṟai cēr caṭaiyiṉāṉ mēvu iṭam
the place which the Lord who wears on his caṭi fragrant koṉṟi desires to stay.
uraiyiṉ ār oli eṉa ōṅku muttāṟu meyttiraiyiṉ ār eṟipuṉal tirumutukuṉṟamē
is tirumutukuṉṟam where the river, muttāṟu, which has a sound like the sound of words, throw from its water waves from the bed of the river.
[[Variant reading: (1) āyavaṉṉumaiyoṭum]]
{3:34}__5+
{$} kaṭiyavāyaṉa kural kaḷiṟṟiṉaip piḷiṟa ōr iṭiyaveṅkuraliṉōṭu āḷi (1) ceṉṟiṭum neṟi
the ways where the lions go with a cruel sound like the thunder to make the male elephants, which trumpet a fearful noise.
vaṭiyavāy maḻuviṉaṉ maṅkaiyōṭu amarvu iṭam
the place of the Lord who has a battle-axe of sharp edge, and dwells with a lady.
ceṭi(atu) ār puṟavu aṇi tirumutukuṉṟamē
is tirumutukuṉṟam made beautiful by the forest tract which has shrubs. (neṟiyaiyuṭaiya mutukuṉṟam : is the syntactical link)
[[Variant reading: (1) ceṉṟaṭuneṟi]]
{3:34}__6+
{$} kāṉam ār kariyiṉ īr urivaiyār
Civaṉ has a skin of an elephant that lives in the forest, wet with blood.
periyatu ōr vāṉam ār matiyiṉōṭu aravartām umaiyoṭum maruvu iṭam
the place where he who has a cobra along with a crescent which shines in the extensive sky, is united with Umi.
ūṉam āyiṉa piṇi avaikeṭuttu
destroying the diseases that cuse defects.
tēṉ am ārpoḻil aṇi tirumutukuṉṟamē
is tirumutukuṉṟam which is made beautiful by gardens where there is plenty of honey.
{3:34}__7+
{$} mañcar
the strong god; (mañcar-maintar; pōli)
[[mañcaṉē maṇiyum āṉāy (nāvukkaracar, tiruvāvaṭutuṟi (2) 1)]]
malarkoṭu vāṉavar vaṇaṅkiṭa
the residents of heaven to pay homage to him with flowers.
veñcolār vēṭarōṭu āṭavar virumpavē
to be desired by adult human males, and hunters who speak cruel words.
añcolāḷ umaiyoṭum amarvu iṭam
the place where Civaṉ remins with desire with Umi who speaks beautiful words.
aṇikalaic ceñcolār payiltarum tirumutukuṉṟamē
is tirumutukuṉṟam where the scholars who are well-versed in beautiful arts written a language in its direct primary significance are crowded.
{3:34}__8+
{$} tāriṉ ār amartarum kayilai nal malaiyiṉai ēriṉ ār muṭi irāvaṇaṉ eṭuttāṉ iṟa
Irāvaṇaṉ who has beautiful crowns and who lifted the good mountin, Kayili on which clouds rest to crackle and split.
vāriṉ ār mulaiyoṭum maṉṉiṉār maruvu iṭam
the place where Civaṉ dwells permanently with Umi who wears on her breasts a bodice.
cīriṉ ār (1) tikaḻtarum tirumutukuṉṟamē
is tirumutukuṉṟam which is eminent by its fame.
[[Variant reading: (1) tikaṭarum]]
{3:34}__9+
{$} āṭiṉār kāṉakattu
danced in the cremation ground.
arumaṟaiyiṉ poruḷ pāṭiṉār
explined the meaning of the abstruse vētams.
(1) palapukaḻpparamaṉār
the supreme god who has many kinds of fame.
iṇaiaṭi ēṭiṉ ār (2) malarmicai ayaṉum māl (um) iruvarum tēṭiṉār aṟivu oṇār tirumutukuṉṟamē
tirumutukuṉṟam is the place of the god who was not possible to be found out though both Ayaṉ who is seated on a flower with petals and Māl, the two feet and head.
[[variant readings: (1) palar (2) alarmicai]]
{3:34}__10+
{$} mācumey tūcukoṇṭu uḻal camaṇ cākkiyar pēcumey uḷa alla
the words of the camanar who have bodies full of dirt and cakkiyar who cover their bodies with Civaram do not contin any truth.
pēṇuvīr
devotees who esteem Civaṉ.
vācam ār tarupoḻil vaṇṭiṉam (m) ivai ceyattēcam ār pukaḻ mikum tirumutukuṉṟamē kāṇumiṉ
go and visit tirumutukuṉṟam whose fame increases in the country. where the swarm of bees hum like music in the garden full of fragrance.
{3:34}__11+
{$} tiṇṇiṉ ār puṟavu aṇitirumutukuṉṟarai
on Civaṉ in tirumutukuṉṟu which is made beautiful by strong forest tracts.
naṇṇiṉāṉ kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ col (1) eṇṇiṉāṉ īraintu mālaiyum iyalumāp paṇṇiṉāl pāṭuvārkkillai (ām) pāvamē
there will be no sins to those who sing with music according to their ability the ten garlands of verses done by meditating on Civaṉ by ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kaḻi who approached Civaṉ as refuge.
[[Variant reading: (1) eṇṇiṇārīraintu mālaiyum miyalumā paṇṇiṉāl]]

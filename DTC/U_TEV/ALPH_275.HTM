NOTE_VMS: (3_18, verse 1)

[[Vaikal: this is the name of the place;
māṭakkōyil is the name of the temple.

This is one of the seventy temples built by the Cōḻa King Ceṅkaṇaṉ;
[[PP: irukku ilaṅku tirumoḻi vāy eṇ tōḷ īcaṟku eḻil māṭam eḻupatu ceytu ulakam āṇṭa tirukkulattu vaḷac cōḻaṉ (Periya Tirumoḻi, 6-6-8);]]

Seventy-eight is the number mentioned by Appar;
[[PP: perukkāṟu caṭaik kaṇinta perumāṉ cērum peruṅkōyil eḻupatiṉōṭu eṭṭum (appar, aṭivut tiruttāṇṭakam, 5);]]

[[māṭakkoyil is a special type of construction into which elephants cannot enter]]

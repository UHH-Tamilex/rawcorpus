{4:4}__1+
{$} pāṭu iḷam pūtattiṉāṉum
Civaṉ who has young pūtams that sing.
pavaḷaccevvāy vaṇṇattāṉum
who has lips red like the coral and complexion like the same.
(1) kūṭiḷameṉmulaiyāḷaik kūṭiya kōlattiṉāṉum
who has the form in which a lady young with breasts which are close to each other, is united with him.
ōṭu iḷaveṇpiṟaiyāṉum
who has a white and young crescent that moves in the sky.
oḷitikaḻ cūlattiṉāṉum
who has a shining trident.
āṭu iḷam pāmpu acaittāṉum
and who tied a dancing young cobra in the waist.
ārūr amarnta ammāṉē
is the god, our father, who dwells with pleasure in ārūr.
[[Variant reading: (1) kōṭiḷa]]
{4:4}__2+
{$} nariyaik kutirai ceyvāṉum
Civaṉ who is capable of transforming jackals into horses.
[[This is the support for those who are of the opinion that Māṇikkavācakar was earlier than the Tēvāram trio]]
[[PP: ariyoṭu piramaṟku aḷavaṟioṇṇāṉ, nariyaikkutirai ākkiya naṉmaiyum;
narikaḷ ellām peruṅkutirai ākkiyavāṟu aṉṟē uṉ pēraruḷē;
nariyaik kutiraip pariākki ñālam ellām nikaḻvittu
(tiruvācakam, Kīrttit Tiruvakaval, 11, 35-36, tiruvācaṟavu, 1; āṉantamāli, 7)]]
narakarait tēvu ceyvāṉum
who transforms people in hell as tēvar.
viratam koṇṭāṭavallāṉum
who can celebrate religious vows and acts of austerity.
viccu iṉṟi nāṟu ceyvāṉum
who can produce seedlings fit for transplantation without seeds;
[PP: viccu atu iṉṟiyē viḷaivu ceykuvāy
(tiruvācakam, tiruccatakam 96)]]
muracu atirntu āṉaimuṉ ōṭa
when the elephant is running fast preceding the deity when muracu kept on it is beaten to announce royal bounty.
muṉpaṇintu aṉparkaḷ ētta
when devotees stand in the presence of god, prise him and pay obeisance to him.
aravu araic cāttiniṉṟāṉum
who wears in his waist a cobra.
ārūr amarnta ammāṉē
[see 1st verse]

{4:4}__5+
{$} ūḻi aḷakka vallāṉum
Civaṉ who can measure time by aeons.
ukappavar ucci uḷḷāṉum
who is on the heads of those who are glad.
tāḻ iḷañceñcaṭaiyāṉum
who has a red and hanging matted locks of hirs, not showing signs of ageing.
taṇṇam ār velkoṭiyāṉum
who has a victorious bonner on which the form of a battle-axe is drawn.
[[taṇṇam-maḻuppaṭai (tivākaram); kaṭṭaṅkakkoṭiyum (nāvukkaracar tiruvatiki vīraṭṭāṉam, paṇ-Kāntāram, 2)]]
tōḻiyar tūtu iṭai āṭa
the companions of ladies who have fallen in love with the Lord act as messengers.
toḻutu aṭiyārkaḷ vaṇaṅka
the devotees worship with joined hands and pay homage to him.
āḻivaḷaik kaiyiṉāṉum
who is also Māl who holds in his hands a discus and a conch. (Civaṉ is immanent in Māl, so he is described as such)
ārūr amar/nta ammāṉē
see 1st verse.

Civaṉ who is immanent in Māl who in sleeps in the ocean of milk which has slow-moving waves. ulaku iṟanta oṇporuḷāṉum
who is the valuable things which are beyond this world.
cīrtaru pāṭal uḷḷāṉum
who is in the famous musical cāna vētam.
ceṅkaṇ viṭaik koṭiyāṉum
who has a red-eyed bull on his flag.
vārtaru pūṅkuḻalāḷai maruvi uṭaṉ vaittavaṉum
who placed on one half the lady with long tresses of hir wearing flowers, being united with her.
ārtirai nāḷ ukantāṉum
who was glad to have ārtiri star as his star;
[[ārtirai has been corrupted into ātirai; ūrtirai vēlai ulāvum uyar mayilaik kūritaruvēl vallār koṟṟam koḷcēritaṉil, kārtarucōlaik kapālīccaram amarntāṉ; ārtirai nāḷ kāṇātē pōtiyō pūmpāvāy (campantar, mayilāppūr, 4); maṉṉiya nāṇmīṉmatikaṉali eṉṟivaṟṟai, muṉṉam paṭaitta mutalvaṉaip piṉṉarum-ārtiraiyāṉ ārtiraiyāṉ eṉṟu eṉṟu ayarumāl ūrtirainīrvēli ulaku (muttoḷḷayiram, invocatary verse).]]

{4:4}__7+
{$} toḻaṟku aṅkai tuṉṉi niṉṟārkkuttōṉṟi aruḷavallāṉum
Civaṉ who bestows his grace appearing before those who have their palms joined to pay obeisance to him.
kaḻaṟku aṅkai paṉmalarkoṇṭukātal kaṉaṟṟa niṉṟāṉum
who stood to cuse to shine brightly the love of his devotees who have in their palms many flowers to scatter at his feet.
kuḻal kaṅkaiyāḷai uḷvaittuk kōlac caṭaik karantāṉum
who concealed in his beautiful matted locks placing the lady, Kanki who had tresses of hir; keeping her inside.
aḻaṟku aṅkai ēttavallāṉum
who can hold fire in the palm.
ārūr amarnta ammāṉē
see 1st verse.

Civaṉ who has one thousand red feet like one thousand lotus flowers.
āyiram poṉvarai pōlum āyiram tōḷ uṭaiyāṉum

who has one thousand tall crowns like one thousand suns.
āyiram pēr ukantāṉum
and who rejoiced in having one thousand names;

ārūr amarnta ammāṉē
see 1st verse.
{4:4}__9+
{$} vīṭu araṅkā niṟpāṉum
Civaṉ who sets up as a stage where salvation dances, which is combined with spiritual wisdom and bliss.
vicumpiṉai vētitoṭara ōṭu araṅkāka vaittāṉum
who set up the sky as the stage for Piramaṉ to run after, transforming himself into a swan.
ōṅki ōr ūḻi uḷḷāṉum
who is at the end of the aeon without perishing.
kāṭu araṅkā makiḻntāṉum
who rejoiced the cremation ground as his stage for dancing.
kārikaiyārkaḷ maṉattuḷ āṭu araṅkattu iṭaiyāṉum
and who is in the minds of beautiful women as a stage for dancing.
ārūr amarnta ammāṉē
see 1st verse.

{$} pai āmcuṭar viṭu nākappaḷḷi koḷvāṉ uḷḷattāṉum
Civaṉ who is in the heart of Māl who sleeps on a bed of a serpent which emits rays from the jewels in its hoods.
[[PP: nīlattu ari vaṇaṅka niṉṟanāḷ pāṟkaṭaliṉ kōlattuḷi potuḷik koṇṭatō (tiruvārūr ulā, 61); tiruppāṟkaṭalil tirumāliṉ mārpil tiyākēcar mutalil eḻuntaruḷiyiruntamai īṇṭuaṟiyaṟpālatu (Do. glose)]]
kai añcu nāṉku uṭaiyāṉaik kālviralāl aṭarttāṉum
who pressed Irāvaṇaṉ who has twenty hands, with his toe.
poy añci vāymaikaḷ pēcippukaḻ purintārkku aruḷum ai añciṉ appuṟattāṉum
who is beyond the twenty five ultimate realities but who grants his grace to those who desire the fame of the Lord speaking truth, being afrid of uttering falsehood
[[ai añciṉ appuṟattāṉ: Civaṉ who is above the twenty different forms is another meaning.]]
ārūr amarnta ammāṉē
see 1st verse.

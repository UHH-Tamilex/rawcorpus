{4:8}__1+
{$} civaṉ eṉṉum ōcai allatu ulakil tiruniṉṟacemmai uḷatē! aṟaiyō
Is there any thing other than the sound Civaṉ, for wealth to the permanent and spotless? I challenge
[[uḷatē: ē is interrogative letter]]
avaṉum ōr aiyam uṇṇi
though he is great he eats getting alms.
ataḷ āṭai āvatu
his dress is a skin
ataṉ mēl ōr āṭu aravam
upon that there is a dancing cobra.
kavaṇ aḷavu uḷḷa (1) uṇṭu
he eats his food which is just like the size of the stone in a sling.
karikāṭu kōyil
his temple in the scorched cremation ground.
kalaṉ āvatu ōṭu karutil
his eating vessel is the skull; if we think about him.
avaṉatu peṟṟi kaṇṭum avaṉ nīrmai kaṇṭum
though they have understood his nature and his affability.
akam nērvar tēvar avarē
the celestials offer him their hearts as his seat.
[[variant readings: (1)vuḷku, vaḷku]]
{4:8}__2+
{$} eri aravu āram mārpar
Civaṉ who has on his chest as garlands cobras whose poison which is like fire.
virikatir ñāyiṟu alvar
is not the sun which spreads its rays.
mati allar
is not the moon.
vēta vitiyum allar
is not the acts to be done and prohibited in the vētams.
viṇṇum nilaṉum, tiritaruvāyu allar
is not the sky, the earth and the air that is always moving.
ceṟu tīyum allar
is not the fire that destroys everything.
teḷinīrum allar
is not the water that is clear.
teriyil
if we investigate his nature.
aritaru kaṇṇiyāḷai orupākamāka aruḷ kāraṇattil varuvār
he will appear for the purpose of granting his grace having a lady who has streaks in the eye, as one half.
ivar imaiyārum allar
this Civaṉ is not those who do not wink.
imaippārum allar
is not those who wink.
{4:8}__3+
{$} tēypoṭiveḷḷaipūci
smearing himself with a fine powder of white sacred ash.
ataṉ mēl or tiṅkaḷ tilakam patitta nutalar
over and above that, he has on his forehead a crescent which is fixed as a circular mark.
kāy katir vēlai nīla oḷi māmiṭaṟṟar
has a bright black neck like the ocean in which the scorching sun rises.
karikāṭar
dances in the scorched cremation ground
kāl or kaḻalar
has an ornament of Kaḻal worn on the right leg.
vēy uṭaṉ āṭu tōḷi avaḷ vimma
Umi who has shoulders like the bamboo between two joints to feel elated in spirit;
[[the part of the bamboo between two joints is compared to the shoulders; tōḷē .... āaykāṉattut, talaiyāṟṟu nilaiiya cēyuyar piṟaṅkal, vēy amaik kaṇ iṭaipuraiiccēya āyiṉum naṭuṅku tuyar tarumē (Akam, 152-14-24)]]
veyya maḻuvīci
swinging the red hot iron.
vēḻa uripōrttu ē ivar āṭumāṟum ivaḷ kāṇumāṟum
Civaṉ dances covering himself with the skin of an elephant and the lady witnesses it.
itutāṉ ivarkku oriyalpē
this is their nature?
{4:8}__4+
{$} vaḷarpoṟi āmai pulki, vaḷarkōtai vaiki
embracing the shell of a tortoise with increasing spots and a garland of increasing fragrance to stay.
vaṭitōlum nūlum vaḷara
and a spotless skin and sacred thread to lie.
kiḷar poṟi nākam oṉṟu miḷirkiṉṟa mārpaṉ
Civaṉ on whose chest a cobra with prominent spots is glittering
naḷir poṟi maññai aṉṉa taḷir pōṉṟu cāyalavaḷ tōṉṟu vāymai peruki
the truthfulness of having of the lady, Umi, who has a gentleness like the peacock which has dense spots and a glittering complexion like the newly putforth leaves.
kuḷir poṟi vaṇṭu pāṭu (1) kuḻalāḷ orutti yuḷaḷ(pōl) kulāvi uṭaṉē
delighting along with a lady who has tresses of hir on which bees which have dots and are cool hum like music.
kiḷar kāṭu nāṭu (2) kiḻavar
has the forests and the country sides as his places by right.
[[Variant reading: (1) kuḻalāy orutti (2) makiḻvar]]
{4:8}__5+
{$} uṟaivatu kāṭu pōlum
Civaṉ's dwelling place is the cremation ground.
uritōl uṭuppar
will dress himself in a skin flayed.
viṭai ūrvatu
rides on a bull.
ōṭukalaṉā
having the skull as the begging bowl.
iṟai ivar vāḻum vaṇṇam itu
this is the nature of this life of this god who is superior to all
ēlum īcaṉ orupāl
has on one an appropriate half of the Lord of the universe (male half)
orupāl icaintu piṟai nutal pētai mātar umai eṉṉum naṅkai
on the other half there is Umi, a lady of distinction who is young and has a forehead like the crescent.
piṟaḻ pāṭa niṉṟu piṉaivāṉ
will clasp his hands with hers when she sings songs of different tunes.
aṟai kaḻal vaṇṭu pāṭum aṭinīḻal āṇai kaṭavātu amarar ulakē
the world of the immortals will not transgress the authority of the shadow of his feet wearing sounding Kaḻal which makes a sound like the humming bee.
{4:8}__6+
{$} kaṇivaḷar vēṅkaiyōṭu kaṭitiṅkaḷ kaṇṇi
the chaplet worn by Civaṉ is the bright crescent along with the flowers of the east indian kino tree which is like an astrologer;
[[kaṇivaḷar vēṅkai:
pal nāḷum niṉṟa iṭattum kaṇivēṅkai; nal nāḷē nāṭi malartalāl (paḻamoḻi, 120);
īṭuiliḷa vēṅkai nāḷ uraippap poṉ ām, pōr vēlavar tām purintatu (Tiṇimāli Nūṟṟimpatu, 18).
As in the season in which the east indian kino tree blossoms the people living in the mountinous tract celebrate their marriage, it is called Kaṇi (astrologer);
paimputal vēṅkaiyum oḷḷiṇar virintaṉa, neṭu veṇ tiṅkaḷum ūr koṇṭaṉṟē (Akam, 2-15-17);
matiūrkoḷ veṟpil, moy ār vaḷar iḷavēṅkai poṉ mālaiyiṉ muṉṉiṉavē (Koviyar, 262)]]
kaḻal kāl cilampa
the ornament Kaḻal to make a sound in the leg.
aḻaku ār aṇikiḷar āraveḷḷai tavaḻ cuṇṇa (1) vaṇṇa iyalār oruvar
the only master who has the nature of the fine power of the sandal paste and beautiful white sacred ash which shines like an ornament.
iruvar
he is both male and female.
maṇikiḷar maññai āla maḻai āṭucōlai malaiyāṉ makaṭkum iṟaivar
is the Lord of the dughter of the mountin on which the clouds dance to make the peacocks which shine like the sapphire to dance.
aṇi kiḷar aṉṉavaṇṇam avaḷ vaṇṇa vaṇṇam
her beautiful colour is the colour of the exceedingly beautiful swan.
avar vaṇṇa vaṇṇam aḻalē
his beautiful colour is red like fire.
[[Variant reading: (1)vaṇṇamiyalār, vaṇṇamayalār]]
{4:8}__7+
{$} nakai vaḷar koṉṟai tuṉṟu naku veṇtalaiyar
Civaṉ has a garland of lughing skulls which is close to the koṉṟi which increases in brightness.
naḷir kaṅkai taṅku muṭiyar
the cool Kaṅki stays in his head.
mikai vaḷar vētakītam muṟaiyōṭum valla, kaṟai koḷ maṇicey miṭaṟar
has a neck which has poison that lands beauty to it and which is capable of chanting the vētam of increasing eminence with the proper accent and intonation.
mukai vaḷar kōtai mātar muṉi pāṭumāṟum eri āṭumāṟum
the lady who wears a garland of buds singing at the time of destruction of all things and Civaṉ dancing in the fire at that time.
ivar kaip pakaivaḷar nākam vīci
swinging the cobras which have increasing enmity and which are held in his lands.
mati aṅkumāṟum
the crescent sinking low in the caṭi
[[it would make better sense if the reading were matiyiṅkumāṟum]]
itupōlum īcar iyalpē
this is perhaps the nature of the Lord of the Universe.
{4:8}__8+
{$} oḷivaḷar kaṅkai taṅkum vaṇṇar
has the nature of bright Kaṅki staying in his head.
oḷimāl ayaṉtaṉ uṭal ventuvīya
the bodies of bright Māl and Ayaṉ are burnt and met with death.
cuṭar nīṟu(1) aṇi kiḷar āra veḷḷai tavaḻ cuṇṇa vaṇṇar
has the colour of the fine powder of the bright sacred ash which is like the beautiful pearl in colour.
tamiyar oruvar
he is single,
[[mūṉṟum oruvar irumpoḻil mūṉṟaṟkum; muṟṟum iṟṟāl piṉṉum oruvar (Kōviyār, 160)]]
iruvar
he has in one form male and female; and therefore he is two persons
kaḷi kiḷar aṉṉa vēṭam uṇṭu
assuming the form of virapattirar who has exultancy.
ōr kaṭamā uritta uṭai tōl
dressing himself in a skin of an elephant which he flayed.
toṭutta kalaṉār
has an ornament in which the skulls are strung.
(1)aṇikiḷar tollaiyavaḷ pākamāka
the ancient lady who has shining ornaments being on onehalf;
[[tollaiyavaḷ: iḻaiaṇi ciṟappiṟ paḻaiyōḷ kuḻavi (Tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭi, 259)]]
eḻil vētam ōtumavarē
Civaṉ who chants the beautiful vētams
[[Variant reading: (1) aḷi]]
{4:8}__9+
{$} malai maṭamaṅkaiyōṭum vaṭakaṅkai naṅkai maṇavāḷarāki makiḻvar
Civaṉ rejoices as the husband of the lady of distinction, Kaṅki in the north and the beautiful dughter of the mountin
[[maṭamātar iruvar ātarippār (Campantar, Tiruvalañcuḻi (2) 6)]]
talai kalaṉāka uṇṭu taṉiyē tirintu tavavāṇarāki muyalvar
he will try doing penance wandering alone, eating from a skull using it as an eating plate.
vilai ilicāntam eṉṟu veṟi nīṟupūci viḷaiyāṭum vēṭavikirtar
has forms different from this world and sports smearing himself with fragrant sacred ash considering it to sandal-paste of inestimable value.
alaikaṭal veḷḷam muṟṟum alaṟak kaṭainta aḻalnañcam uṇṭa avarē
it was he who consumed the burning poison which rose as a result of churning completely the water of the moving ocean to make roaring noise.
{4:8}__10+
{$} putuviri poṉ cey ōlai orukātu orkātu curicaṅkam niṉṟu puraḷa
on one ear the womens' ear-ring made of gold which spreads fresh brilliance and on the other a mens' earning made of spiralling conch to roll over.
viti viti vēta kītam orupāṭum ota
one half to chant the music of vētams which enjoins rules of conduct
oru pāṭum mella nakum (āl)
one half will smile gently.
matuviri koṉṟai tuṉṟu caṭai pākamātar
having a lady, Kaṅki as a half on the caṭi in which Koṅṟi flowers which pour honey are thickly tucked in.
kuḻal pākamāka varuvar
will appear with a half which has tresses of hirs, of Umi.
itu ivar vaṇṇavaṇṇam
this is his beautiful nature.
ivaḷ vaṇṇa vaṇṇam
this is her beautiful nature
eḻil vaṇṇa vaṇṇam iyalpē
this is the beautiful nature of their imposing appearance.

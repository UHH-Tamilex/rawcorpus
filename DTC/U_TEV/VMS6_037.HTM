nāṉ ārār tiripuraṅkaḷ nīṟā nōkkum aṉalāṭi āramutē eṉṟēṉ
I sid, `one who fixed his looks at the three cities of the enemies to be reduced to powder! rare nectar'!
[[aṉalāṭī : the reading with a long letter would be a better one]]
kūr ār maḻu vāṭpaṭai oṉṟu ēnti
holding a weapon of a battle-axe which has a sharp edge!
kuṟaṭ pūtap pal paṭaiyāy eṉṟēṉ nāṉ
I sid, one who has many armies of dwarf pūtams.
nāṉ pēr āyiram uṭaiyāy eṉṟēṉ
I sid, `you who have one thousand names!
[[Thousand does not mean exactly thousand, but used in the sense of innumerable.]]
nāṉ piṟai cūṭum piññakaṉē eṉṟēṉ
I sid, the destroyer who wears a crescent on his head!
ārā amutē aiyāṟaṉṉē eṉṟu eṉṟē nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉē
I faint by shounting with excitement many times continuously `insatiate nectar! Civaṉ in Aiyāṟu'
[[nāṉē : ē denotes determination in this decade;
aiyāṟaṉṉē: ṉ is an augmentation for the sake of metre, it will apply to other verses wherever it occurs.;
nāṉē: ē in all the verses excludes all others except Nāvukkaracar; this will apply to the whole of this decade]]

I sid, one who has a bow (piṉākam) to which the arrow is always fixed!
iṭumpaik kaṭal niṉṟum ēṟavāṅki āvā eṉṟu aruḷ puriyum aiyāṟaṉṉē eṉṟu eṉṟē nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉē
I faint shounting with excitement `Civaṉ in aiyāṟu who grants his grace with compassion having lifted up entirely from the ocean of sufferings, to his devotees'.

I sid, the four vetāms which are monopoly of poeple who can expound their meanings!

I sid, one who completely removed my suffering from me, who was not afrid of you out of respect, `thinking' that I am a worthless person.

civaṉ eṉṟu nāṉ uṉṉai ellām collac celvam taruvāṉē eṉṟēṉ nāṉ

• patikam: [4:47]
  • talam: tiruk kayilāyam
  • (()): tiru nērici
  • VMS: Vol.14, p.183

*1
kaṉakam mā vayiram _untum mā maṇik kayili kaṇṭu,
muṉakaṉāy _arakkaṉ _ōṭi _eṭuttalum, _umiyāḷ _añca,
_aṉakaṉāy niṉṟa _īcaṉ _ūṉṟalum, _alaṟi vīḻntāṉ;
maṉakaṉāy _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

*2
kaḻittavaṉ kaṇ civantu, kayili nal maliyi _ōṭi
_atirttu _avaṉ _eṭuttiṭa(l)lum, _arivitāṉ _añca, _īcaṉ
netittavaṉ _ūṉṟiyiṭṭa nili _aḻintu _alaṟi vīḻntāṉ;
matittu _iṟi _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

*3
kaṟuttavaṉ kaṇ civantu, kayili nal maliyik kiyāl
maṟittalum, maṅki _añca, vāṉavar_iṟivaṉ nakku,
neṟittu _oruviralāl _ūṉṟa, neṭuvari pōla vīḻntāṉ;
maṟittu _iṟi _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

*4
kaṭuttavaṉ kaṇ civantu, kayili nal maliyi _ōṭi
_eṭuttalum, maṅki _añca, _iṟiyavaṉ _iṟiyē nakku,
noṭippu_aḷa(vu) viralāl _ūṉṟa, nōvatum _alaṟiyiṭṭāṉ;
maṭittu _iṟi _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

*5
kaṉṟit taṉ kaṇ civantu, kayili nal maliyi _ōṭi
veṉṟit taṉ kittalattāl _eṭuttalum, veruva maṅki,
naṉṟu(t)tāṉ nakku nātaṉ _ūṉṟalum, nakaḻa vīḻntāṉ;
maṉṟit tāṉ _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

*6
kaḷittavaṉ kaṇ civantu, kayili nal maliyi _ōṭi,
neḷittu _avaṉ _eṭuttiṭa(l)lum, nēriḻi _añca, nōkki,
veḷittavaṉ _ūṉṟiyiṭṭa vēṟpiṉāl _alaṟi vīḻntāṉ;
maḷittu _iṟi _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

*7
karuttaṉāyk kaṇ civantu, kayili nal maliyik kiyāl
_eruttaṉāy _eṭutta_āṟē, _ēntiḻi _añca, _īcaṉ---
tiruttaṉāy niṉṟa tēvaṉ---tiruviral _ūṉṟa, vīḻntāṉ;
varuttuvāṉ _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

*8
kaṭiyavaṉ kaṇ civantu, kayili nal maliyi _ōṭi,
vaṭivu _uṭi maṅki _añca _eṭuttalum, maruva nōkkic
ceṭi paṭat tiruvira(l)lāl _ūṉṟalum, citintu vīḻntāṉ;
vaṭivu _uṟa _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

*9
kariyat tāṉ kaṇ civantu, kayili nal maliyip paṟṟi,
_iriyat tāṉ _eṭuttiṭa(l)lum, _ēntiḻi _añca, _īcaṉ
neriyat tāṉ _ūṉṟāmuṉṉam niṟkilātu, _alaṟi vīḻntāṉ;
maṟiyat tāṉ _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkkum _illi _aṉṟē!

*10
kaṟṟaṉaṉ, kayilitaṉṉik kāṇṭalum _arakkaṉ _ōṭik
ceṟṟavaṉ _eṭutta_āṟē, cēyiḻi _añca, _īcaṉ
_uṟṟu _iṟi _ūṉṟāmuṉṉam _uṇarvu _aḻi vakiyāl, vīḻntāṉ;
maṟṟu _iṟi _ūṉṟiṉāṉēl maṟittum nōkku _illi _aṉṟē!

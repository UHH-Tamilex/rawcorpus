{2:8}__1+
{$} vāṉ ulāvum mativantu (1) ulāvum matil māḷikai tēṉ ulāvum malarc cōlai malkum tikaḻ cikkaluḷ
in eminent Cikkal where the mansions with fortified walls on which the moon, which moves about in the sky comes and moves about, and the flower-gardens which have honey are in flourishing state.
vēṉal vēḷai viḻittiṭṭa veṇṇeyp perumāṉ aṭi ñāṉamāka niṉaivār viṉai āyiṉa naiyumē
the Karmams of those who meditate with the knowledge of the supreme being, upon the feet of the Veṇṇipperumāṉ who opened his frontal eye to destroy Vēḷ who has summer as his appropriate season,
[[vēṉal is another form of vēṉil; see notes on vēṉaṟ pūtta marāṅ kōtai (campantar, nākikārōṇam (1) 1.)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) ulavum]]
{2:8}__2+
{$} maṭam koḷ vāḷai kuti koḷḷum maṇam malarp poykai cūḻtiṭam koḷ māmaṟaiyōr avar malkiya cikkaluḷ
in cikkal where steady-minded brahmins are flourishing, and which is surrounded by natural tanks where fragrant flowers grow and the beautiful scabbard fish leap.
viṭam koḷ kaṇṭattu veṇṇeyp perumāṉ aṭi mēviyē
desiring the feet of veṇṇipperumāṉ who has a neck contining poison.
aṭaintu vāḻum (m) aṭiyār avar allal aṟuppar
the devotees who lead a life approaching the feet and will cut at the root of sufferings.
{2:8}__3+
{$} nīlam neytal nilavi(m) malarum cuṉai nīṭiya cēlum ālum kaḻaṉi (v) vaḷam malkiya cikkaluḷ
in cikkal which has fertile fields in which the fish, cēl, leaps and has tanks where blue nelumbo flowers and white indian water lilies are shining, having blossomed.
vēl oṇ kaṇṇiyiṉāḷai ōr pākaṉ
Civaṉ who has as his half a lady whose bright eyes are comparable to the weapon, vēl
veṇṇeyp pirāṉ pāl vaṇṇaṉ kaḻal ētta
to prise the feet of the Lord veṇṇippirāṉ who has the colour of milk.
nam pāvam paṟaiyum
our sins will vanish by degrees.
{2:8}__4+
{$} kantam untak kai tai pūttum kamaḻntu cērum poḻil
in the gardens where the fragrant screw-pine blossoms and spreads its fragrance taking precedence of all other fragrances.
centu vaṇṭu iṉṉicai pāṭal malkum tikaḻ cikkaluḷ
in eminent cikkal where the humming of the bees is like the primary melody-type centu and increasing in pitch
[[kuṟiñcikkup puṟam centu - (cilappatikāram, 14-160-167, atiyārkkunallar`s commentary).]]
venta veṇṇīṟṟaṇṇal veṇṇep pirāṉ virai ār kaḻal cintai ceyvār viṉai āyiṉa tēyvatu tiṇṇamē
It is quite certin that the Karmams of those who meditate upon the fragrant feet of veṇnippiraṉ who smears well-burnt white sacred ash, will decrease.
{2:8}__5+
{$} maṅkum taṅkum maṟaiyōrkaḷ māṭattu ayalē miku teṅku tuṅkap 1. poḻiṟ celvam malkum tikaḻ cikkaluḷ
in eminent cikkal where the wealth from the excellent cocoanut groves are in abundance by the side of the storeys of brahmins where clouds stay for rest.
veṅkaṇ veḷ ēṟu uṭai veṇṇeyp pirāṉ aṭimēvavē taṅkum 1. mēṉmai
the superior state of existence will stay with those who cherish with love the feet of veṇṇippirāṉ who has a white bull of angry looks.
caratam; tiru nāḷum takaiyumē
the goddess of wealth will be with them always; it is true.
[[Variant reading: 1. poḻil celvam 2. mēṟcaratam]]
{2:8}__6+
{$} vaṇṭu iraittu matuvimmiya māmalarppoykai cūḻ teṇ tirai koḷpuṉal vantu oḻukum vayal cikkaluḷ
in cikkal where the water with clear waves enters and flows into the fields; and which is surrounded by natural tanks which have big flowers with abundant honey for which the bees hum loudly.
maṉamē
my mind!
viṇṭu iraitta malarāl tikaḻ veṇ ṇeyppirāṉ aṭikaṇṭu iraittu matiyāy; kati ākavē
to get higher state of existence; you meditate on the feet of veṇṇippirāṉ which is shining with the blossomed flowers on which bees hum, and offered by devotees; being fascinated due to love, having obtined sight of them.
[[iraittu-mōkam koṇṭu; iraippu mōkam, centamiḻ (takkayākapparaṇi 129, old commentary)]]
{2:8}__7+
{$} maṉamē
my mind!
muṉṉum māṭam matil mūṉṟu eriyāy viḻattuṉṉuvār kaṇai oṉṟu celuttiya cōtiyāṉ
the supreme light who discharged an arrow to which was attached many parts, to be consumed by fire simultaneously the fortified walls of the mansions, in the distant past.
ceṅnel ārum vayal cikkal veṇṇeyp perumāṉ aṭi uṉṉi nīṭa niṉaiyāy
meditate for a long time on the feet of the veṇṇipperumāṉ in cikkal which has fields full of the superior variety of paddy of yellowish hue.
[[nīṭam maṉamē the underlined letter is augmentation for rhythm.]]
viṉai ōyavē
for the Karmams to come to an end.
{2:8}__8+
{$} maṉamē
my mind!
teṟṟal ākiya teṉ ilaṅkaikku iṟaivaṉ
the King of ilaṅki in the south, who had a clear intellect.
[[iṇai marutu iṟṟuvīḻa naṭai kaṟṟa teṟṟal viṉaipaṟṟu aṟukkum vitayē (periya tirumoḻi, 11-4-9);
teṟṟal may mean also one who has perverseness]]
malai paṟṟiṉāṉ muṭipattoṭu tōḷkaḷ neriyavē ceṟṟa tēvaṉ
the god who destroyed the strength to crush the ten heads and shoulders of (Irāvaṇaṉ) who had in his grip the mountin, Kayili.
viṉaiyāyiṉa ōya
our Karmams to come to an end.
nam cikkal veṇṇey perumāṉ aṭi uṟṟu niṉaiyāy
meditate on the feet of our veṇṇipperumān in cikkal, reaching them mentally.
{2:8}__9+
{$} māliṉōṭu arumāmaṟaivalla muṉivaṉum kōliṉār kuṟukaccivaṉ cēvaṭi
Māl and the sage (Piramaṉ) who was well-versed in the abstruse vētams chalked out a stratagem to reach the feet of Civaṉ.
kōliyum
though they did so
cīlam tām aṟiyār
they did not realise his nature of easy accessibility.
tikaḻ cikkal veṇṇeyp pirāṉ aṭipālum naṉ malar (um) tūva nam pāvam paṟaiyumē
our sins will decrease gradually if we bathe with milk and scatter flower at the feet of veṇṇippiraṉ in eminent cikkal.
{2:8}__10+
{$} paṭṭai nal tuvar āṭaiyiṉāroṭum pāṅku ilākkaṭṭu amaṇ kaḻukkaḷ colviṉaik karutātu
without paying heed to the words of the amaṇar who committed such heinous acts as to be impaled on the stake; and buddhist who dress in red ochre robes soaked in the bark of marutam (marutam) tree
nīr ciṭṭaṉ cikkal veṇṇeyp perumāṉ ceḻumāmaṟaip paṭṭaṉ cēvaṭiyē paṇimiṉ piṇipōka
(People of this world!) pay obeisance only to the lotus-red feet of the great scholar of the great vētam which are eternal, who is veṇṇipperumāṉ in cikkal and well-versed in vedic lore so that your diseases may leave you.
[[piṇi may also mean the disease of birth.]]
{2:8}__11+
{$} kantam ār poḻiṟ kāḻiyum ñāṉacampantamaṉ
ñāṉcampantaṉ of Kāḻi which has gardens full of fragrance.
centaṇ pūm (1) poḻil cikkal veṇṇeyp perumāṉ aṭi
at the feet of the veṇṇipperumāṉ in Cikkaḻ which has cool and flourishing flower-gardens.
cantamāccoṉṉa centamiḻvallavar
those who are able to recite the refined tamiḻ verses composed by him with rhythmic movement
vāṉiṭai, venta veṇṇīṟu aṇiyum perumāṉ aṭimēvar
will reach the feet of the Lord who smears well-burnt white sacred ash, in Civalōkam.

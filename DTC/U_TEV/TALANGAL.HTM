Abreviations for the names of countries

The Tēvāram contains (in the PIFI edition) 798 hymns. The largest part of them is associated with one site or stalam (and sometimes with several), except for a group of 48 potu patikam-s, that do not belong specifically to any site.

This list contains the names of the sites as they stand in the PIFI edition (PIFI 68.3, pp. 204-217 and 224-280). It also contains additional place names, such as those of the cities that appear on the maps [WORK IN PROGRESS]. It also contains alternate names, such as the modern names of the sites and special names (like the 12 names of Kāḻi). Regarding tiru, the following policy has been adopted. It is always placed after the radical, but:

  • If it is found in ordinary usage, it is placed between square brackets, as in vāṉmiyūr [tiru].
  • If it is found only in sacred contexts, it is placed between square plus ordinary brackets, as in ārūr ([tiru]).
  • However, if there is no ordinary usage, tiru is simply placed between square brackets, as in pukali [tiru].

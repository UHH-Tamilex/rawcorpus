{6:60}__1+
{$} nāṉ kaṇ ārakkaṇṭēṉ
I saw to the complete satisfaction of my eyes
vāṉavarkku mūttavaṉai
Civaṉ who is older than all other celestials
mūvāmēṉi mutalvaṉai
the origin of all things whose body does not show signs of old age
tiruvaraiyil mūkkap pāmpu oṉṟu ārttavaṉai
who tied a furious cobra in his holy waist
akku aravam āramāka aṇintavaṉai
who adorned himself as a garland bones and cobras
aṭiyār aṭainta aṉpōṭu paṇintu ēttavaṉai
to whom the devotees pay obeisance and whom they prise
iṟuvaraiyil tēṉai
who is the honey in the big mountin
ēṉōrkku iṉṉamutam aḷittavaṉai
who gave the sweet nectar to others, (himself drinking the poison)
iṭarai ellām kāttavaṉai
who protected me from being afflicted by sufferings
kaṟkuṭiyil viḻumiyāṉai
the superior god in Kaṟkuṭi
[[kaṟkuṭiyil viḻumiyāṉ- the underlined word is found in inscriptions in this temple to denote Civaṉ as the presiding deity here; viḻumiyār, āḷvār viḻumiya tēvar, viḻumiya nāyaṉār This shrine is now known by the name of uyyakkoṇtāṉ mali]]
kaṟpakattai
who is like the Kaṟpakam to me

• patikam: [5:93]
  • talam: POTU
  • (()): maṟakkiṟpaṉē _eṉṉum tiruk kuṟuntoki
  • VMS: Vol.15, p.119

*1
kācaṉi, kaṉali, katir mā maṇit-
tēcaṉi, pukaḻār---cilarteṇṇarkaḷ;
māciṉik kaḻittu _āṭkoḷa valla _em
_īcaṉi _iṉi nāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

*2
puntikku(v) viḷakku_āya purāṇaṉi,
cantikkaṇ naṭam_āṭum caturaṉi,
_antivaṇṇaṉi, _ār _aḻalmūrttiyi,
vantu _eṉ _uḷḷam koṇṭāṉi, maṟappaṉē?

*3
_īcaṉ, _īcaṉ _eṉṟu _eṉṟum _araṟṟuvaṉ;
_īcaṉtāṉ _eṉ maṉattil pirivu _ilaṉ;
_īcaṉtaṉṉiyum _eṉ maṉattuk koṇṭu(v),
_īcaṉtaṉṉiyum yāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

*4
_īcaṉ _eṉṉi _aṟintatu _aṟintaṉaṉ,---
_īcaṉ cēvaṭi _eṟṟappeṟutalāl,---
_īcaṉ cēvaṭi _ēttappeṟṟēṉ; _iṉi
_īcaṉtaṉṉiyum yāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

*5
tēṉi, pāliṉi, tiṅkaḷi, ñāyiṟṟi,
vāṉaveṇmati cūṭiya mintaṉi,
vēṉilāṉi melivu cey tī-_aḻal-
ñāṉamūrttiyi, nāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

*6
kaṉṉali, karumpu _ūṟiya tēṟali,
miṉṉaṉi, miṉ _aṉiya _uruvaṉi,
poṉṉaṉi, maṇikkuṉṟu piṟaṅkiya
_eṉṉaṉi, _iṉi yāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

*7
karumpiṉi, kaṭṭiyi, kantamāmalarc
curumpiṉi, cuṭarccōtiyuḷ cōtiyi,
_arumpiṉil perumpōtu koṇṭu, _āy malar
virumpum _īcaṉi, nāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

*8
tuñcumpōtum cuṭarviṭu cōtiyi,
neñcuḷ niṉṟu niṉippikkum nītiyi,
nañcu kaṇṭattu _aṭakkiya nampaṉi,
vañcaṉēṉ _iṉi yāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

*9
putiya pūviṉi, puṇṇiyanātaṉi,
nitiyi, nītiyi, nittilakkuṉṟiṉi,
katiyi, kaṇṭam kaṟutta kaṭavuḷi,
matiyi, mintaṉi, nāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

*10
karuku kārmukil pōlvatu _or kaṇṭaṉi,
_uruvam nōkkiyi, _ūḻimutalvaṉi,
paruku pālaṉi, pālmati cūṭiyi,
maruvum mintaṉi, nāṉ maṟakkiṟpaṉē?

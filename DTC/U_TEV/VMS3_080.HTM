{3:80}__1+
{$} cīr maruvu tēciṉoṭu tēcamali celvam maṟaiyōrkaḷ paṇiya
as the brahmins who possess eminent lustre and wealth of devotion to god which are prised throughout Tamiḻ Nāṭu, do obeisance.
tār maruvu koṉṟai aṇi tāḻ caṭaiyiṉāṉ amar cayaṅkoḷ pati (tāṉ)
the place which is victorious among the shrines, and where Civaṉ who has a hanging caṭi in which he wears koṉṟi flowers which blossom in garlands.
pār maruvu paṅkayam uyarnta vayal cūḻ paḻaṉam nīṭa arukē
when the agricultural tracts are excessively fertile which are surrounded by fields where lotus which has roots in the earth grows tall, nearby.
kār maruvu veṇ 1. kaṉakam māḷikai kaviṉ peruku vīḻi nakarē
is the city of vīḻi of increasing beauty where the white coloured mansions which abound in wealth and on which clouds settle.
[[Variant reading: 1. kaḷapam]]
{3:80}__2+
{$} paṭṭa muḻavu
with muḻavu which was played upon.
iṭṭa paṇilattiṉoṭu
and with conches which were blown.
pal maṟaikaḷ ōtu paṇi nal 1. ciṭṭarkaḷ
brahmins of good conduct who do the service of chanting vētams having many parts.
cayattuti ceyya aruḷ ceytaḻal koḷ mēṉiyavaṉ ūr
the place of Civaṉ who has a form of fire and grants his grace to them when they sing songs of prise, Let there be victory to you.
maṭṭu ulavu ceṅkamala vēlivayal cennel vaḷar maṉṉu poḻil vāyviṭṭu ulavu teṉṟal virai nāṟu pati vētiyārkaḷ vīḻi nakarē
the city of vīḻi where brahmins of vēdic lore live, is the place where the fragrant southern balmy breeze blows intermittently in the permanent gardens and where the superior variety of red paddy grows in the fields which are surrounded by red lotus flowers havign honey like fence.
[[Variant reading: 1. caṭṭarkaḷ]]
{3:80}__3+
{$} maṇ iḻi curarkku vaḷam mikka pati maṟṟum uḷa maṉṉuyirkaḷukku eṇ iḻivu il iṉpam nikaḻvu eyta
the celestials who descend to this earth and other human beings in fertile places, to derive happiness which is highly estimable and superior to others.
eḻil ār poḻil ilaṅku aṟupatam paṇ iḻivu ilātavakai pāṭa
the bees with six legs which are shining in the beautiful gardens hum melody-types without any mistake.
maṭa maññai naṭam āṭa
and the young peacocks dance.
aḻaku ār viṇ iḻi vimāṉam uṭai viṇṇavar pirāṉ maruvu vīḻi nakarē
is the city of vīḻi where the chief of the celestial being who have a vimāṉam of exquisite beauty that descended from heaven, dwells.
{3:80}__4+
{$} centamiḻar
persons who have knowledge of refined and standard tamiḻ.
teyvamaṟaināvar
persons whose tongues chant vētam endowed with divinity.
ceḻunaṟkalai terinta avarōṭu antam il kuṇattavarkaḷ arccaṉaikaḷ ceyya amarkiṉṟa araṉ ūr
the place where araṉ dwells with desire where people of countless good character perform arccaṉikaḷ along with people who have a discerning knowledge of good and flourishing arts.
kontu alar poḻil paḻaṉavēli, kuḷir taṇ puṉal vaḷam perukavē
as the fertility of the very cool water increases in the gardens which blossom in bunches, and the surrounding fields.
ventiṟal viḷaṅkivaḷar vētiyar virumpupati vīḻi nakarē
is the city of vīḻi which is desired by the brahmins who prosper with powers that are desirable.
{3:80}__5+
{$} pūtapati ākiya purāṇa muṉi
the ancient Civaṉ who is the chief of the pūtams and who assumed the form of an ascetic.
puṇṇiya naṉ mātaip pētam ilāta vakai maruvi
though he combined with the lady who is the personification of grace, without any outward difference.
pākam mika vaitta perumāṉatu iṭam ām
is the place of god who placed her on one half, at the same time the difference to be known to all.
mātavarkaḷ aṉṉa maṟaiyāḷarkaḷ
the brahmins who can be compared to great sages.
vaḷartta mali vēḷvi ataṉāl, ētam ilātavakai iṉpam amarkiṉṟa eḻilvīḻinakarē
is the beautiful city of vīḻi where happiness stays without anything being deficient to the many sacrifices performed (by them).
{3:80}__6+
{$} maṇṇil maṟaiyōr maruvu vaitikamum mātavamum maṟṟum ulakattu eṇ il poruḷ āyavai paṭaitta imaiyōrkaḷ perumāṉatu iṭam
the place of the god, Civaṉ who is the chief of the celestials who do not wink, and who created in this earth the conduct of the vēdic path pursued by the brahmins, great penance and other countless things.
naṇṇivaru nāvalarkaḷ nāḷ toṟum vaḷarkka nikaḻkiṉṟa pukaḻcēr, viṇ ulavu māḷikai neruṅki vaḷar nīḷ puricai vīḻi nakar ām
is the city of vīḻi which has a high wall of fortification inside which mansions on which clouds, crawl, and are crowded, and which has an increasing fame fostered dily by scholars who come to this place.
{3:80}__7+
{$} mantira naṉ māmaṟaiyiṉōṭu vaḷar vēḷvi micai mikka pukai pōy
the enormous smoke which rose in the sacrifices along with the beneficial mantirams of the vētams.
antaravicumpu aṇavi aṟputam eṉap paṭarum āḻi iruḷvāy
in the dense darkness which spreads, reaching the sky above, to make people wonder at the darkness even in broad daylight.
mantara naṉ māḷikai nilāvu maṇi nīṭu katir viṭṭa oḷi pōy ventaḻal viḷakku eṉa virumpiṉar taruntu pati vīḻirukarē
the city of vīḻi is the perfect place where people who desired the long rays emitted by the gems shining in the good mansions which are like the mountin, mantaram, like the light of lamps lit with fire.
{3:80}__8+
{$} āṉavaliyiṉ tacamukaṉ talai araṅka aṇi āḻi viralāl
with the toe which wears a beautiful ring to press down the heads of the ten-headed Irāvaṇaṉ, who was thinking only of his strength (and lifted Kayili)
ūṉ amar uyarnta kurutip puṉalil vīḻtara uṇarntaparaṉ ūr
the place of the supreme god who intentionally pressed him down to fall into the pool of superior blood which flowed from his body.
tēṉ amar tiruntu poḻil ceṅkaṉaka māḷikai tikaḻnta matiḷōṭu āṉa tiru uṟṟu vaḷar antaṇar niṟainta aṇi vīḻinakarē
is the beautiful city of vīḻi which is full of brahmins who have abundant wealth, and which has a wall of fortification protecting the eminent mansions having pure gold and has defectless gardens in which bees live.
{3:80}__9+
{$} ēṉa uru āki maṇ iṭanta imaiyōṉum
the god who digged into the earth taking the form of a pig;
eḻil aṉṉa uruvam āṉavaṉum
and Piramaṉ who took the form of a beautiful swan.
ātiyiṉoṭu antam aṟiyāta aḻal mēṉiyavaṉ ūr
the place of Civaṉ who took the form of fire whose head and feet could not be known (by those two)
vāṉ aṇavu māmatiḷ maruṅku
by the side of the high wall of fortification which touches the sky.
alar neruṅkiya vaḷam koḷ poḻil vāy 1. vēṉal amarvu eytiṭa
people like to stay during summer in the garden of fertility and dense flowers.
[[poḻil vati vēṉiṟ pēreḻil vāḻkkai eṉṟatu vēṉiṟ kālattu maṉaiyil vaikātu poḻil kaḷilē vatiyum periya celva aḻakaiyuṭaiya ilvāḻkkai (patiṟṟupattu, 48-75, old commentary)]]
viḷaṅku oḷiyiṉ mikka pukaḻ vīḻitakarē
is the city of vīḻi which has great fame on account of its eminent divinity.
[[Variant reading: 1. vēṉamalar, vēṉaṉmalar]]
{3:80}__10+
{$} kuṇṭu amaṇar āki oru kōlam miku pīliyoṭu kuṇṭikai piṭittu
holding an ascetic's pitcher and a bundle of beautiful peacock's feathers, being low as amaṇar.
eṇ ticaiyum illatu oru teyvam uḷatu eṉpar
they assert that there is a god which is not found in all the eight directions.
atu eṉṉa poruḷ ām
what is the use of their assertion?
paṇṭai ayaṉ aṉṉavarkaḷ pāvaṉai virumpu paraṉ mēvupati
the place where the supreme Civaṉ dwells and who desires the contemplation of the brahmins who are like the ancient Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ); Piramaṉ is compared to the brahmins in this place;
[[centāmaraimēl ayaṉōṭu civaṉum aṉaiya perumaiyōr nantā vaṇkai maṟaiyōr naṟaiyūr; ceṅkamalattu ayaṉ aṉaiyār teṉ aḻuntai (periya tirumoḻi, 6-7-8, 7-6-1)]]
cīr veṇtaraḷa vāḷ nakai nal mātarkaḷ viḷaṅkum eḻil vīḻi nakarē
is the beautiful city of vīḻi where the good-natured ladies whose teeth are as bright as the famous white pearls, shine brilliantly.
{3:80}__11+
{$} mattam mali koṉṟaivaḷar caṭaiyil vaitta paraṉ
on the supreme god who placed on his growing cati profuse by koṉṟi flowers datura flowers.
vīḻinakar vittakaṉai
and the skilful Civaṉ who is in the city of vīḻi.
veṅkuruvil vētiyaṉ virumpu tamiḻ mālaikaḷ valār
those who are able to recite the garland of tamiḻ verse which were adorned with desire by the brahmin (ñāṉacampantaṉ) of veṅkuru (Kāḻi)
cittiravimāṉam amar celvam malikiṉṟa civalōkam
reaching the Civalōkam which abounds in bliss and beautiful temples.
attaku kuṇattavarkḷa āki aṉupōkamoṭu 1. yōkam (m) avaratu
yōkam along with experience having the appropriate character, is theirs.
[[Variant reading: 1. yōkaravarē, yōkavaratē]]

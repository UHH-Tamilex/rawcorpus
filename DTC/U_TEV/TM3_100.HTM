• patikam: [3:100]
  • talam: tirut tōṇipuram (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: paḻam pañcuram
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.254

*1
karumpu _amar villiyik kāyntu, kātal kārikimāṭṭu _aruḷi,
_arumpu_amarkoṅki _ōrpāl makiḻnta _aṟputam ceppa(_a)ritāl;
perum pakalē vantu, _eṉ peṇmi koṇṭu, pērntavar cērnta _iṭam---
curumpu _amar cōlikaḷ cūḻnta cemmit tōṇipuramtāṉē.

*2
koṅku _iyal pūṅkuḻal kovviccevvāyk kōmaḷamātu_umiyāḷ
paṅku _iyalum tirumēṉi _eṅkum pālveḷḷinīṟu _aṇintu,
caṅku _iyal veḷvaḷi cōra vantu, _eṉ cāyal koṇṭārtamatu _ūr---
tuṅku _iyal māḷiki cūḻnta cemmit tōṇipuramtāṉē.

*3
mattakkaḷiṟṟu _uri pōrkkak kaṇṭu, mātu_umi pētu_uṟalum,
cittam teḷiya niṉṟu _āṭi, _ēṟu _ūr tīvaṇṇar, cilpalikku _eṉṟu,
_ottapaṭi vantu, _eṉ _uḷḷam koṇṭa _oruvarukku _iṭampōlum---
tuttam nal _iṉ_ici vaṇṭu pāṭum tōṇipuramtāṉē.

*4
vaḷḷal _irunta mali_ataṉi valamceytal vāymi _eṉa
_uḷḷam koḷḷātu, kotittu _eḻuntu, _aṉṟu, _eṭuttōṉ _uram neriya,
meḷḷa viral vittu, _eṉ _uḷḷam koṇṭār mēvum _iṭampōlum---
tuḷ _oli veḷḷattiṉmēl mitanta tōṇipuramtāṉē.

*5
vel paṟavik koṭi mālum, maṟṟi virimalarmēl _ayaṉum,
pal paṟavippaṭi_āy _uyarntum, paṉṟi_atu_āyp paṇintum,
celvu _aṟa nīṇṭu _em cinti koṇṭa celvar _iṭampōlum---
tol paṟavi cumantu _ōṅku cemmit tōṇipuramtāṉē.

*6
kuṇṭiki pīli taṭṭōṭu niṉṟu kōcaram koḷḷiyarum,
maṇṭi ki _ēnti maṉam koḷ kañci_ūṇarum, vāy maṭiya,
_iṇṭi puṉintu, _erutu _ēṟi vantu, _eṉ _eḻil kavarntār _iṭam_ām---
toṇṭu _ici pāṭal _aṟāta toṉmit tōṇipuramtāṉē.

*7
tū maru māḷiki māṭam nīṭu tōṇipurattu _iṟiyi,
māmaṟināṉkiṉoṭu _aṅkam_āṟum vallavaṉ---vāymiyiṉāl
nā maru kēḷvinalam tikaḻum ñāṉacampantaṉ---coṉṉa
pā maru pāṭalkaḷpattum vallār pār muḻutu _āḷpavarē.

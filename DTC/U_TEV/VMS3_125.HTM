[[A note on the importance of the shrine:
This shrine is now known as āccāpuram; it is 3 miles to the East of Koḷḷiṭam rilway station, in Tancāvūr Dt. Every year the festival of Campantar attining bliss is celebrated in the star of mūlam, in the month of vikāci. There is a separate temple for Ñāṉacampantar]]

{3:125}__1+
{$} perumaṇam ūr col cūṭalarē
one who adorns himself with the garlands of verses which have subtle meaning like fragrance!
toṇṭar nallūrp perumaṇammēya nampāṉē
Lord Civaṉ who is in Nallūrpperumaṇam where servants of god gather together!
[[nampāṉ this is the form in the nominative case;
nampāṉ mēya naṉṉakaram pōlum namaraṅkāḷ (Campantar, Kuṟṟālam, 1)]]
kal ūr perumaṇam vēṇṭā
I do not desire the marriage during which the bridegroom has to place the right foot of the bride on the grinding-stone
[[in Tamiḻ: ampi]]
and other ceremonies.
kaḻumalam pal ūrp pāṭṭu mey āyttu ila
Have not the songs which I sang of Kaḻumalam (Cīrkāḻi) and many other shrines, come true?
[[āyttu is a corruption of āyiṟṟu;
naṉṟāyttaṉṟōkarumam (Kamparāmāyaṇam, II-nakar niṅkupatalam 66)]]
{3:125}__2+
{$} ōtam taru maṇal cēr taṇ kaṭal nittilam
the pearls in the cool sea which reach the sand which is heaped by the waves.
pāvai nallārkaḷ
girls who are beautiful like the portrit
[[pāvai means kollippāvai which is supposed to have superb beauty]]
parumaṇalāk koṇṭu varumaṇam kūṭṭi maṇam ceyyum
Having them as big sand particles adding with them the flowers which have fragrance, play celebration of marriages.
nallūrpperumaṇattāṉ
god is in that Nallūrpperumaṇam.
peṇ ōr pākaṅkoṇṭāṉ
has a lady in one half.
{3:125}__3+
{$} aṉpu uṟu cintaiyarāki aṭiyavar naṉpuṟu nallūrp perumaṇam mēviniṉṟu
Residing in Nallūrpperumaṇam where the devotees having a mind full of love, get all good things.
iṉpuṟum entai iṇaiyaṭi ēttuvār tuṉpuṟuvār allar
those who prise the feet of my father who is the cuse for happiness will not undergo sufferings
toṇṭuceyvār
will do more and more service to god.
{3:125}__4+
{$} valliyam tōl uṭai ārppatu
it is the tiger's skin that is tied round the waist.
pōrppatu kol iyal vēḻattu uri
The blanket with which he covers is the skin of the elephant which has a nature of killing.
viri kōvaṇam
his loin cloth is wide.
nal iyalār toḻum nallūrp perumaṇam pulkiya vāḻkkai em puṇṇiyaṉārkku
the above mentioned belong to our god who is residing in Nallūrpperumaṇam where good-natured people worship him.
{3:125}__5+
{$} ēṟu ukantīr
you desired the bull as your vehicle.
iṭukāṭṭu eri āṭi veṇṇīṟu ukantīr
you desired the white ash, having danced in the fire in the cremation ground.
nirai ār viritēṉ koṉṟai nāṟu ukantīr
you were pleased to enjoy the fragrance of blossomed koṉṟi flowers which are in rows and have honey.
tirunallūrp perumaṇam vēṟu ukantīr
you delighted in Tirunallūrpperumaṇam as quite different from other shines.
umai kūṟu ukantīr
you were pleased to have Umi as your half.
[[This verse prised god in the 2nd person]]
{3:125}__6+
{$} ciṭṭappaṭṭārkku eḷiyaṉ
He is easily accessible to devotees who worship him without failing in their routine.
vēṭṭuvappaṭṭam kaṭṭum ceṉṉiyāṉ
He has a plate tied to his head when he assumed the form of a hunter.
patiyāvatu naṭṭakkoṭṭu āṭṭu aṟā nallūrp perumaṇattu iṭṭappaṭṭāl ottir
you seem to be more partial towards the city of Nallurpperumaṇam where dances and musical instruments played as their accompaniments never cease.
empirāṉ nir
you are our master
[[nīr has been shortened into nir.]]
[[In this verse God is prised both in the 3rd person and 2nd person]]
{3:125}__7+
{$} mēkatta kaṇṭaṉ
He has a neck as black as the sable cloud;
[[kāreṉṉa ārumkaṟaimiṭaṟṟam palavaṉ (Kōviyār, 56)]]
eṇ tōḷaṉ
Has eight shoulders.
veṇṇīṟṟu umaipākattaṉ
Has Veṇṇīṟṟu Umi as a half.
[[Veṇṇīṟṟu Umi is the name of the tēvi in this shrine]]
pāy pulit tōloṭu pantitta nākattaṉ
has a serpent tied as a girdle upon the skin of the tiger which can pounce;
[[cf. pāy pulittōlum (citamparam, tiruvirittamm 2-7, and notes)]]
nallūrp perumaṇattāṉ nalla pōkattaṉ
God in Nallūrpperumaṇam is in the enjoyment of all pleasures.
yōkattaiyē purintāṉ
Though he was like that, he was practising only yōkam.
{3:125}__8+
{$} takku iruntīr
you were deserving, and appropriate, to your greatness.
aṉṟu arakkaṉait tāḷāl ukku iruntu olka uyar varaikkīḻ iṭṭu
on that day having placed the arakkaṉ beneath the high mountin to pine and be disheartened.
nakku iruntīr
you were smiling (at his ignorance)
iṉṟu nallūrp perumaṇam pukku iruntīr
today you entered into Nallūrpperumaṇam and stayed there.
emaippōkku aruḷīr
please bestow grace upon us to reach your feet
[[This line is sid to hint that Campantar wanted deliverance from birth and death; according to periya purāṇam, campantar entered into the effulgent light at the entrance of the temple and merged with the Lord along with his wife.]]
{3:125}__9+
{$} ēlumtaṇ tāmaraiyāṉum
one who is seated in the cool lotus flower suitable to him.
iyalpu uṭai(ya) mālum taṉ māṇpu aṟikiṉṟilar
and the Māl of good nature are incapable of knowing his greatness.
māmaṟai nālum tam pāṭṭu eṉpar
great men say that all the four great maṟi (Vētam-s) are his songs.
puricaṭaiyārkku nallūrp perumaṇam tamkōyil
Nallūrpperumaṇam is the temple belonging to the Lord of twisted matted locks of hir.
[[pōlum expletive]]
{3:125}__10+
{$} ātar amaṇoṭu cākkiyar tāmcollum pētaimai kēṭṭup piṇakkuṟuvīr! vammiṉ
you who quarrel on hearing the ignorant words of ignorant amaṇar and cākkiyar! come
[[ātar: blind person who does not have the eye of knowledge.
cākkiyar: the followers of Buddha who is also called cākkiyamuṉi]]
nātaṉai
the chief.
nallūrp perumaṇam mēviya vētaṉatāḷ toḻa
to worship the feet of the Lord who is the meaning of the vētam
[[vētaṉa:a: plural suffix of the genitive case]]
[[tāḷ -- tāḷkaḷ, plural]]
vīṭu eḷitu
it is easy to attin eternal bliss.
{3:125}__11+
{$} peṟum patam nallūrp perumaṇattāṉai
about the god in Nallūrpperumaṇam who can give positions which can be received by devotees.
naṟum poḻil kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ
sung by Ñāṉacampantaṉ who was born in Kaḻi surrounded by fragrant gardens.
uṟum poruḷāl coṉṉa oṇ tamiḻ vallārkku
for those who are capable of reciting the Tamiḻ verses which can bestow good benefits, with the idea of reaching the feet of god.
paḻipāvam aṟum
reproch spoken by the people, and sins will become extinct.
avalam ilar
(they) will not undergo any suffering.
[[avalam may mean also the suffering of birth]]

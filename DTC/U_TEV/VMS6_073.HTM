kuru maṇipōl aḻaku amarum (kōmāṉ)
the King who has beauty like a lustrous precious stone.
koṭṭaiyūril kōṭīccarattu uṟaiyum kōmāṉ
the King who dwells in Kōṭīccaram in Koṭṭiyūr.
karumaṇipōl kaṇṭattu aḻakaṉ
Has a beautiful neck like the sapphire.
kallāl niḻaṟkīḻ iruntāṉ
sat under the shade of the Kallāl tree
[[Kallāl is sid to be a variety of the banyan tree which has no aerial roots]]
parumaṇi mā nākam pūṇṭāṉ
adorned himself with big cobras which have big gems on their hoods
pavaḷakkuṉṟu aṉṉa paramaṉ
is the superior god who is like a hill formed by coral.
varumaṇi nīrp poṉṉi valañcuḻiyāṉ
is in Valañcuḻi on the bank of the Poṉṉi which brings with its floods, gems
mātēvaṉ
has the name of Mātēvaṉ
varataṉ
grants boons to devotees
[[kaṇṭāy in this verse and the succeeding verses ((is)) expletive]]
[[Koṭṭiyūr is the name of the place and Kōṭīccaram is the name of the temple. Koṭṭi is the name of castor which is the tree under which god was worshipped here]]

the King who dwells in Kōṭīccaram in Koṭṭiyūr surrounded by grove of cocoanut trees having clusters of fruits.
kalaikkaṉṟu taṅku karattāṉ
holds in his hand a young deer

alaikkaṅkai ceñcaṭai mēl ēṟṟāṉ
received on the red caṭi Kaṅki with waves.

is beyond the wall of this universe (aṇṭakapālam - aṇṭac cuvariṉ ucci)
malaippaṇṭam koṇṭu varu nīrp poṉṉi valañcuḻiyiṉ mēviya maintaṉ
is the youth who dwells with pleasure in valañcuḻi on the bank of the poṉṉi which brings with its flood, products of the mountin.

koṭi āṭu neṭumāṭak koṭṭaiyūril kōṭīccarattu uṟaiyum kōmāṉ
the King who dwells in Kōṭīccaram which is in Koṭṭiyūr which has tall storeys where flags are waving in the air.
poṭi āṭu tirumēṉip puṉitaṉ
is the pure one who besmears his sacred body with holy ash.
puḷ pākaṟku āḻi koṭuttāṉ
gave a discus to Māl whose mount is a bird (Karuṭaṉ)
iṭi ār kaṭu muḻakku ēṟu ūrntāṉ
rides on a bull whose loud bellowing noise is like the thunder.
eṇ ticaikkum viḷakku āki niṉṟāṉ
He is the light to the eight quarters.
maṭal ār tirai puraḷum kāvirivāy valañcuḻiyil mēviyamaintaṉ
is the strong youth who dwells in valañcuḻi on the bank of the Kāviri in which waves with flowers roll up and down.

[[NOTE: At present this shrine is known by the name of Kuṟumāṇakkuti; Stanza 5 contins the reason for the name.]]

{1:101}__1+
{$} taṇ ār tiṅkaḷ poṅku aravam tāḻpuṉal cūṭi
Having adorned himself with the cool crescent, angry cobras and water whose nature is flowing downwards.
peṇ āṇ āya pēraruḷāḷaṉ
the god with infinite grace who has both male female halves.
piriyāta kaṇṇār kōyil kaitoḻuvōrkaṭku iṭar pāvam naṇṇā ākum
sufferings and sins which are the cuse for those will not approach who worship that shrine, which he never leaves with joined hands.
nalviṉai āya naṇukum
virtuous Karmam (puṇṇiyam) will come to them.
{1:101}__2+
{$} kantu amar cantum kār akilum taṇkatirmuttum vantu amar teṇṇīr maṇṇi vaḷam cēr vayal maṇṭi
Having fields which are made fertile by the maṇṇi river of clear water in which are deposited pearls of cool rays, black eagle-wood, and fragrant sandal-wood. (kantu-kantam: kaṭaikkuṟai; the last letter is elided)
kontu alar cōlaik kōkilam āṭa, kuḷir vaṇṭu centicai pāṭum cīrtikaḻ kaṇṇār kōyil
Kaṇṇārkōyil which shines with its eminence and where the cool bees the melody of cantu, when indian cuckoos dance in the gardens where bunches of flowers blossom, (is the lord's residence)
{1:101}__3+
{$} pal iyal pāṇip pāriṭam ētta
when the pūtams prise the Lord with songs of different nature.
paṭukāṉiṉ elli naṭam cey īcaṉ emmāṉ taṉ iṭam eṉpar
people say that the place of Civaṉ, our Lord, who dances at night in the cremation ground.
kollaiyiṉ mullai mallikai mauvaṟkoṭi piṉṉik, kaliyal iñci mañcu amar kaṇṇār kōyil
is Kaṇṇār koyil which has a wall of enclosure built of stone on which white clouds settle and where in the forest tract arabian jasmine, jasmine and wild jasmine grow intertwining.
{1:101}__4+
{$} maruvaḷar kōtai añcattaruvaḷar kāṉam taṅkiya tuṅkap peruvēḻam urittu
to cuse the lady who is like the fragrant garland to be frightened he flayed the huge and tall elephant wich lives in the forest where trees are growing (tall)
nālvarkku uruvaḷar ālanīḻal amarntu īṅkumaṟai urai ceytār
the god who tught the secrete of the vētam, to the four sages sitting under the banyan tree of increasing size.
karuvaḷar kaṇṇār kōyil aṭaintōr
Those who approach Kaṇṇār Koyil which is the embrayed all things.
kaṟṟōrē
people who are really learned.
{1:101}__5+
{$} maṟu māṇ uruvāy maṟṟu iṇai iṉṟi
Being the embodiment of great faults and without having any equal.
vāṉōraic ceṟu māvalipāl ceṉṟu ulaku elām aḷaviṭṭa kuṟumāṉ uruvaṉ
the dwarf, who took the form of a brahmachāri, and measured all the worlds going abegging to māvāli who gave trouble to the celestials.
taṟkuṟiyākak koṇṭāṭum kaṟumā kaṇṭaṉ mēyatu kaṇṇār kōyil
Kaṇṇār kōyil is the place desired by the Lord of black neck whom Māl appreciated as his symbol of Civaṉ.
{1:101}__6+
{$} viṇṇavarukku āy
in order to save the celestials.
vēlaiyuḷ nañcum viruppāka uṇṇavaṉai
the god who drank the poison of the ocean, relishing it.
tēvarkku amutu īntu evvulakiṟkum kaṇṇavaṉai
who is as precious as the eye to all the worlds, having given the nectar to the tēvar.
kaṇṇār kōyil kaṉitaṉṉai naṇṇavallōrkaṭku
to those who can approach the god in Kaṇṇār Koyil, who is as sweet as the fruit.
namaṉ pāl naṭalai illaiyē
there will be no suffering inflicted by the god of death.
{1:101}__7+
{$} muṉ orukālattu intiraṉ uṟṟa muṉicāpam piṉ oru nāḷ avviṇṇavar ēttap peyarvu eyti
the curse which intiraṉ had from muṉi (Kavutamaṉ) long ago, having left him, when those celestials worshipped god after some time;
taṉ aruḷāl kaṇ āyiram īntōṉ cārpu eṉpar
people say the people of the god who granted thousand eyes, out of his grace.
kaṉṉiyar nāḷum tuṉamar kaṇṇārkōyil
is Kaṇṇār Kōyil where virgins crowd dily in large numbers. (This stanza gives the reason for this shrine getting the name of Kaṇṇār Koyil)
{1:101}__8+
{$} perukku eṇṇāta pētai arakkaṉ varaikkīḻ (āl) nerukkuṇṇā
the foolish arakkaṉ who did not think of his prosperity, was pressed down under the mountin.
taṉ nīḷ kaḻal neñcil niṉaintu ētta
when he prised the lord's feet fixing them in his heart.
murukku uṇṇātu ōr moykatir vāḷtēr muṉ īnta tirukkaṇṇār eṉpar civalōkam cērvār
those who prise the beautiful Kaṇṇār Kōyil of the Lord who gave long ago a chariot, and a sword of rays crowding in it, which did not break into pieces, will reach the world of Civaṉ after death.
{1:101}__9+
{$} ceṅkamalap pōtil tikaḻ celvaṉ tirumālum
Piramaṉ who is in the red lotus flower, and tirumāl.
aṅku ammalarkkaṇ nōkkarum vaṇṇattu aḻal āṉāṉ
God who assumed the form of fire that could not be seen by those defective physical eyes.
taṅku amalakkaṇṇār tikaḻkōyil
the defectless Kaṇṇār Kōyil where the stays.
tamatu (1) uḷḷam aṅku amalattōṭu ēttiṭa aṇṭattu amarvārē
Those who prise with their minds free from impurities will be seated in heaven.
[[Variant reading: (1) uḷḷattu]]
{1:101}__10+
{$} tāṟu iṭu peṇṇait taṭṭu uṭaiyārum tām uṇṇum cōṟu uṭaiyār col tēṟaṉmiṉ
Do not believe as clear truth the words of the camaṇar who dress themselves in mats made of leaves of the palmyre which brings forth cluster of fruits, and buddhists who ae particular about their food only.
veṇṇūl cēr mārpaṉ
God who has a white sacred thread on his chest.
ēṟuṭaiyaṉ
has a bull.
paraṉ
the supreme being.
eṉpu aṇivāṉ
adorns himself with bones.
nīḷ caṭaimēl ōr āṟu uṭaiyaṇṇal cērvatu kaṇṇār kōyil
Kaṇṇār Kōyil is the place where the eminent one who has on his long matted hir a river, exists (People of this world! reach that place and worship God there)
{1:101}__11+
{$} kāmaru kaṇṇārkōyil uḷāṉai
about the god who is in beautiful Kaṇṇār Koyil
kaṭal cūḻnta, pūmarucōlaip poṉ iyal māṭappukalikkōṉ
the chief of pukali which has storeys which are beautiful, and gardens having flowers, and which is surrounded by the sea.
nāmaru toṉmait taṉmaiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ pāmarupāṭal pattum vallārmēl paḻippōm
the blemish that attaches to people who are able to recite the ten verses whose sweet sound travels to a great distance and sung by ñāṉacampantaṉ who thinks about the nature of the god whose names are often uttered by him since a long time, will vanish.

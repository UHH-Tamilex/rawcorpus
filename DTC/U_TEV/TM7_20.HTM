*1
nīḷa niṉintu _aṭiyēṉ _umi nittalum kitoḻuvēṉ;
vāḷ _aṉa kaṇ maṭavāḷ _avaḷ vāṭi varuntāmē,
kōḷili _emperumāṉ!kuṇṭiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ!
_āḷ _ili; _emperumāṉ, _avi _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*2
vaṇṭu_amarumkuḻalāḷ _uminaṅki _ōrpaṅku _uṭiyāy!
viṇṭaṭavartam purammūṉṟu _ericeyta _em vētiyaṉē!
teṇtiri nīr vayal cūḻ tiruk kōḷili _emperumāṉ!
_aṇṭam_atu_āyavaṉē, _avi _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*3
pāti _ōr peṇṇi vittāy; paṭarum caṭik kaṅki vittāy;
mātarnallār varuttam(m)_atu nīyum _aṟiti _aṉṟē!
kōtu _il poḻil puṭi cūḻ kuṇṭiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ;
_ātiyē, _aṟputaṉē, _avi _aṭṭitarap paṇiyē!

*4
colluvatu _eṉ, _uṉi nāṉ? toṇṭivāy _uminaṅkiyi nī
pulki _iṭattil vittāykku _oru pūcal ceytār _uḷarō?
kolli vaḷam puṟavil kuṇṭiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ;
_allal kaḷintu _aṭiyēṟku _avi _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*5
mullimuṟuval _umi _orupaṅku _uṭi mukkaṇaṉē!
pal _ayar veṇtaliyil pali koṇṭu _uḻal pācupatā!
kolli vaḷam puṟavil-tiruk kōḷili _emperumāṉ!
_allal kaḷintu, _aṭiyēṟku _avi _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*6
kuravu _amarum kuḻalāḷ _uminaṅki _orpaṅku _uṭiyāy!
paravi pacivaruttam(m)_atu nīyum _aṟiti _aṉṟē!
kuravu _amarum poḻil cūḻ kuṇṭiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ;
_aravam _acittavaṉē, _avi _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*7
_emperumāṉ! nuṉiyē niṉittu _ēttuvaṉ, _eppoḻutum;
vampu _amarum kuḻalāḷ _orupākam _amarntavaṉē!
cempoṉiṉ māḷiki cūḻ tiruk kōḷili _emperumāṉ!
_aṉpu_atu(v)_āy _aṭiyēṟku _avi _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*8
_arakkaṉ muṭikaraṅkaḷ(ḷ) _aṭarttiṭṭa _em _ātippirāṉ!
parakkum _aravu_alkulāḷ paravi_avaḷ vāṭukiṉṟāḷ;
kurakku_iṉaṅkaḷ kutikoḷ kuṇṭiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ;
_irakkam_atu_āy _aṭiyēṟku _avi _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*9
paṇṭiya māl, piramaṉ, paṟantum(m) _iṭantum(m) _ayarntum
kaṇṭilarāy, _avarkaḷ kaḻal kāṇpu _aritu_āya pirāṉ!
teṇtirinīr vayal cūḻ tiruk kōḷili _eṉperumāṉ!
_aṇṭam_atu_āyavaṉē, _avi _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*10
kolli vaḷam puṟavil-tiruk kōḷili mēyavaṉi

  • talam: tiruk kōḷili
  • paṇ: naṭṭarākam
  • VMS: Vol.10, p.133

*1
nīḷa niṉaintu _aṭiyēṉ _umai nittalum kaitoḻuvēṉ;
vāḷ _aṉa kaṇ maṭavāḷ _avaḷ vāṭi varuntāmē,
kōḷili _emperumāṉ!kuṇṭaiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ!
_āḷ _ilai; _emperumāṉ, _avai _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*2
vaṇṭu_amarumkuḻalāḷ _umainaṅkai _ōrpaṅku _uṭaiyāy!
viṇṭaṭavartam purammūṉṟu _ericeyta _em vētiyaṉē!
teṇtirai nīr vayal cūḻ tiruk kōḷili _emperumāṉ!
_aṇṭam_atu_āyavaṉē, _avai _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*3
pāti _ōr peṇṇai vaittāy; paṭarum caṭaik kaṅkai vaittāy;
mātarnallār varuttam(m)_atu nīyum _aṟiti _aṉṟē!
kōtu _il poḻil puṭai cūḻ kuṇṭaiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ;
_ātiyē, _aṟputaṉē, _avai _aṭṭitarap paṇiyē!

*4
colluvatu _eṉ, _uṉai nāṉ? toṇṭaivāy _umainaṅkaiyai nī
pulki _iṭattil vaittāykku _oru pūcal ceytār _uḷarō?
kollai vaḷam puṟavil kuṇṭaiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ;
_allal kaḷaintu _aṭiyēṟku _avai _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*5
mullaimuṟuval _umai _orupaṅku _uṭai mukkaṇaṉē!
pal _ayar veṇtalaiyil pali koṇṭu _uḻal pācupatā!
kollai vaḷam puṟavil-tiruk kōḷili _emperumāṉ!
_allal kaḷaintu, _aṭiyēṟku _avai _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*6
kuravu _amarum kuḻalāḷ _umainaṅkai _orpaṅku _uṭaiyāy!
paravai pacivaruttam(m)_atu nīyum _aṟiti _aṉṟē!
kuravu _amarum poḻil cūḻ kuṇṭaiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ;
_aravam _acaittavaṉē, _avai _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*7
_emperumāṉ! nuṉaiyē niṉaittu _ēttuvaṉ, _eppoḻutum;
vampu _amarum kuḻalāḷ _orupākam _amarntavaṉē!
cempoṉiṉ māḷikai cūḻ tiruk kōḷili _emperumāṉ!
_aṉpu_atu(v)_āy _aṭiyēṟku _avai _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*8
_arakkaṉ muṭikaraṅkaḷ(ḷ) _aṭarttiṭṭa _em _ātippirāṉ!
parakkum _aravu_alkulāḷ paravai_avaḷ vāṭukiṉṟāḷ;
kurakku_iṉaṅkaḷ kutikoḷ kuṇṭaiyūrc cilanellup peṟṟēṉ;
_irakkam_atu_āy _aṭiyēṟku _avai _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*9
paṇṭaiya māl, piramaṉ, paṟantum(m) _iṭantum(m) _ayarntum
kaṇṭilarāy, _avarkaḷ kaḻal kāṇpu _aritu_āya pirāṉ!
teṇtirainīr vayal cūḻ tiruk kōḷili _eṉperumāṉ!
_aṇṭam_atu_āyavaṉē, _avai _aṭṭittarap paṇiyē!

*10
kollai vaḷam puṟavil-tiruk kōḷili mēyavaṉai
nallavartām paravum tiru nāvala_ūraṉ_avaṉ
nel _iṭa _āṭkaḷ vēṇṭi(n) niṉaintu _ēntiya pattum vallār,
_allal kaḷaintu _ulakiṉ(ṉ), _aṇṭar vāṉ_ulaku _āḷpavarē.

{2:11}__1+
{$} nallāṉai
the god who is the embodiment of all that is god.
nāṉ maṟaiyōṭu (1) iyal āṟu aṅkam vallāṉai
one who is versed in the aṅkam-s which are associated with the four Vētam-s.
vallavar pāl malintu ōṅkiya collāṉai
one who is in the form of superior prises abundant with those who are well-versed in those ten books.
tol matil kāḻiyē kōyilām illāṉai ētta niṉṟārkku uḷatu iṉpamē
Happiness will be with people who prise the god who has as his dwelling place the temple in Kāḻi which has an old wall of enclosure.
[[Variant reading: (1) ikil]]
{2:11}__2+
{$} nam māṉam māṟṟi
Having removed all over attachments
[[māṉam - apimāṉam; the first two letters are dropped; mutaṟ kuṟai]]
namakku aruḷāy niṉṟa pemmāṉai
the great god who stood as grace to us.
pēyuṭaṉ āṭal purintāṉai
god who danced with goblins,
ammāṉai
that great god,
antaṇar cērum aṇikāḻi emmāṉai
our Lord who is in beautiful Kāḻi where brahmins gather together
ētta vallārkku iṭar illai
for those who are able to prise the above-mentioned god there will be no sufferings.
{2:11}__3+
{$} aṉpu ceytu ētta killār pāl aruntāṉai
the god who does not accept anything from people who do not possess the capacity to prise out of devotion,
poy aṭimait toḻil ceyvāruḷ poruntāṉai
God who does not consent to dwell in the minds of those who are insincere in their devotion.
viruntāṉai
the god whose novelty does not wear out.
vētiyar ōti miṭai kāḻi iruntāṉai numviṉai ēka ēttumiṉ
people of this world! prise the god who was seated in Kāḻi where brahmins throng chanting the vetam, so that the sins may take leave of you.
{2:11}__4+
{$} puṟṟāṉai
the god who in the form of anthills in several shrines
puṟṟu aravam araiyiṉ micaic cuṟṟāṉai
he has wound round his waist a cobra that lives in the anthill.
toṇṭu ceyvār avar tammoṭum aṟṟāṉai
he gave himself up completely to those who performed devoted service to him.
kāḻi amar kōyil paṟṟāṉaip paṟṟi niṉṟārkku illai pāvamē
There will be no sins for those who have the god who is attached to the temple in Kāḻi, as their firm support.
{2:11}__5+
{$} netiyāṉai
the god who is the hoarded treasure
neñcu iṭam koḷḷa niṉaivārtam vitiyāṉai
and one who is the mode of life who think about him for their minds to be filled with him.
viṇṇavar (tām) viyantu ēttiya katiyāṉai
and one who is the refuge for the celestials who prised him with amazement.
kār ulavum poḻil kāḻiyām, patiyāṉaip pāṭumiṉ num viṉai pāṟavē
people of this world! sing the prise of one who is in the city of Kāḻi where clouds move about, for your sins to be destroyed.
{2:11}__6+
{$} ceppu āṉa meṉmulaiyāḷaittikaḻ mēṉi, vaippāṉai
one who keeps on his shining person the lady whose breasts are like caskets in shape.
vārkaḻal ētti niṉaivārtam oppāṉai
one who is one with the devotees who prise, and meditate upon, the long feet
ōtam ulāvu kaṭaṟkāḻi meyppāṉai
and the one who is the truth in Kāḻi girt by sea where waves move about.
(1) mēviya māntar viyantār
those who worshipped him with desire are people and who are admired and prised by others
[[viyantār is used in the passive sense; viyakkap paṭṭār]]
[[Variant reading: (1) mēviyamarntār]]
{2:11}__7+
{$} tuṉpāṉai
the god who is the form of sufferings
tuṉpam aḻittu aruḷ ākkiya iṉpāṉai
he is the happiness, having destroyed all sufferings and conferring his grace.
ēḻ icaiyiṉ nilai pēṇuvār aṉpāṉai
one who loves those who encourage the musicians of the seven notes of music.
aṇi poḻiṟ kāḻi nakar mēya, nampāṉai naṇṇavallār viṇai nācamē
the sins of those who are able to approach with devotion the god who is desired by the living beings and who dwells in Kāḻi of beautiful parks, will be destroyed.

{$}
[[In this verse two stories, the story of Irāvavaṇaṉ and the story of Māl and Piramaṉ have been combined.]]
kuṉṟāṉai
the god who has hills as his abode.
kuṉṟu eṭuttāṉ puyam nāl aintum veṉṟāṉai
one who crushed the twenty shoulders of (the arakkaṉ) who lifted the hill (of Kailācam)
malarāṉoṭu māl tēṭa niṉṟāṉai
He who stood as a column of fire to be searched by the god in the (lotus) flower and Māl.
nēriḻaiyāḷoṭum kāḻiyuḷ, naṉṟāṉai nam perumāṉai naṇukumē
people of this world! approach our Lord who is the embodiement of all that is good and who dwells in Kāḻi with a lady wearing suitable ornaments

the jins who argue though their side is vanquished.
[[cāvakar house-holders among jins]]
cākkiyar
and the buddhists
mēvāta col avai kēṭṭu vekuḷēṉ miṉ
Do not get angry when you hear the unkind words of those two people.
pū āya koṉṟaiyiṉāṉai, puṉal kāḻik kō āya koḷkaiyiṉāṉ aṭi kūṟumē
prise the feet of the one who is fit to be the supreme god in Kāḻi which has plenty of water and who adorns himself with the brilliant flowers of koṉṟi (indian laburnum)

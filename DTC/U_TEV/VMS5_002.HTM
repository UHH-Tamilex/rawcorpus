{5:2}__1+
{$} paṉaikkai mummuta vēḻam urittavaṉ
one who flayed the skin of the elephant having three musts, and a trunk resembling the palmyra tree.
niṉaippavar maṉam kōyilākak koṇṭavaṉ
one who entered into the minds of those who meditate upon him, as a temple.
aṉaittum vēṭamām ampalakkūttaṉai
the dancing god in ampalam who has all things as his form.
tiṉaittaṉaip poḻutum maṟantu uyvaṉō
will I be saved if I forget him even for a single moment?
{5:2}__2+
{$} tīrttaṉai
the pure one.
civaṉai
one who has the name of Civaṉ.
civalōkaṉai
one whose abode is Civalōkam
mūrttiyai
one who has several forms
mutalāya oruvaṉai
the unequalled one who is the source for all things.
pārttaṉukku aruḷceyta ciṟṟampalak kūttaṉai
the dancing god of Ciṟṟampalam who granted his grace to Pārttaṉ
[[This is a story from Mahabharatam where Pārttaṉ is sid to have received a weapon from Civaṉ.]]
koṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
will I, who am cruel, be saved if I forget him?
{5:2}__3+
{$} kaṭṭum pāmpum kapālam kai māṉmaṟi iṭṭamāy
Being desirous of the serpent which is tied, skull and a young one of a deer held in his hand.
iṭukāṭṭu eri āṭuvāṉ
one who dances in the burial ground holding fire.
ciṭṭar vāḻtillaic ciṟṟampalak kūttiṉai
the dancing god of Ciṟṟampalam in tilli where people of good conduct live.
eḷtaṉaip poḻutum maṟantu uyvaṉō
will I be saved if I forget him even for a moment?
[[tiṉai in l; and eḷ in this stanza are similar used for smallness; tiṉaittuṇai naṉṟiceyiṉum, eṭpakavaṉṉa ciṟumaittē (Kuṟaḷ 104, 889)]]
{5:2}__4+
{$} māṇi pāl kaṟantu āṭṭi vaḻipaṭa
as the bachelor (caṇṭecaṉ) milked the cows and bathed you with milk and worshipped you
nīṇ ulaku elām āḷakkoṭuttava eṉ āṇiyai
my god who is the basis of all things, who granted him all the worlds which are at a great distance, to be ruled by him.
[[This refers to the story of caṇteca nāyaṉār of Cēyñallur; In tēvāram all the three refer to him in many places; āṇi can be interpreted as the pin of gold for testing the fineness of other gold. nīṇ is a pōli of nīḷ; nīṇutal ceytoḻiya (tēvāram P. 82-9, Kāci mutt edition 1968)]]
cempoṉ ampalattuḷ niṉṟa tāṇuvai tamiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
will I be saved if I, who am lonely, forget the pillar who was standing in the campalam which was covered with red gold? (There are five ampalams or sabhas in citamparam. The one alluded to here as cempoṉ ampalam perhaps refers to the hall in front of Natarājan who is dancing in ciṟṟambalam)
{5:2}__5+
{$} pittaṉai
one who has the name of pittaṉ; (or) one who has a mania for his devotees (in the first sense all his actions are entirely different from those of the world).
peruṅkāṭu araṅkā uṭai muttaṉai
one who is naturally free from all shakles and bordage, and dances in the burning ground using it as a stage.
muḷaiveṇmaticūṭiyai
one who adorned on his head the white crescent which is like a white sprout.
cittaṉai
one who possessess all the eight powers attined by performing penance.
cempoṉ ampalattuḷ niṉṟa attaṉai
the father who is standing in the ampalam covered with gold.
aṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
will I, his serf, be saved if I forget him?
{5:2}__6+
{$} nītiyai
the right conduct.
niṟaivai
the fullness
maṟaināṉku uṭaṉ ōtiyai
one who changed the four maṟi simultaneously
oruvarkkum aṟivu oṇāccōtiyai
the effulgent light which could not be known by anyone (by his own effort).
cuṭarc cempoṉiṉ ampalattu ātiyai maṟantu
having forgotton the primordial one who is in the brilliant golden ampalam.
aṭiyēṉ uyvaṉō
will I, who am his serf, be saved.
{5:2}__7+
{$} maikoḷ kaṇṭaṉ
one who has the black neck.
eṇ tōḷaṉ
has eight shoulders.
mukkaṇṇiṉaṉ
He has three eyes.
paikoḷpāmpu arai ārtta paramaṉār
the supreme one who has tied in his waist a serpant having a hood.
ceyya mātu uṟai ciṟṟam palattu eṅkaḷ aiyaṉai
our father who is in Ciṟṟampalam where the lady of red colour (goddess of wealth) resides.
aṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
will I be saved if I, who am his servant, forget him. (Notes aiyaṉ: venerable one)
{5:2}__8+
{$} vāṉ ulakattu uḷa tēvarkaḷ
the gods that are in heaven.
toḻutum pōṟṟiyum
having worshipped and prised
tūyacempoṉiṉ eḻuti mēynta ciṟṟampalak kūttiṉai
the dancer of the ciṟṟampalam whose roof was covered completely by being indented by pressure, with the fine red gold.
iḻutaiyēṉ maṟantu eṅṅaṉam uyvaṉō
How can I, who am an ignoramus, be saved?
{5:2}__9+
{$} kār ulām malark koṉṟai am tāraṉai
one who wears on his chest a garland of koṉṟi flowers which blossom and move about in winter.
vār ulām mulai maṅkai
the husband of young mid who has breasts on which the bodice moves about.
tēr ulāviya tillaiyuḷ kūttaṉai
the dancer in tilli where care are dragged.
ārkilā amutai maṟantu uyvaṉō
will I be saved by forgetting the insatiable nectar of god?
{5:2}__10+
{$} ōṅku mālvarai ēntal uṟṟāṉ ciram vīṅkuvimmuṟa ūṉṟiya tāḷiṉāṉ
one who fixed his feet to make the heads of that one who held up the high and big mountin to be swollen and be in distress.
tēṅkunīr vayalcūḻ tillaik kūttaṉaip pāṅkilāt toṇṭaṉēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
will I, his devotee, be saved if I forget the dancer in tilli which is surrounded by fields which are full of water.

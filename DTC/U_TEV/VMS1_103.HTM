{1:103}__1+
{$} tōṭu uṭaiyāṉ oru kātil
Civaṉ wears a woman's ear-ring in the left ear.
(orukātil) tūya kuḻaitāḻa ēṭu uṭaiyāṉ
wears flowers whjen the spotless mens' ear-ring hangs in the right ear.
talaikalaṉāka irantu uṇṇum nāṭu uṭaiyāṉ
has a country in which he can beg alms in a bowl of a skull, and eats.
naḷḷiruḷil ēma naṭam āṭum kāṭu uṭaiyāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which he who has a cremation ground in which he performs at midnight, dance affording protection is warmly attached.
{1:103}__2+
{$} kēṇavallāṉ kēḻal veṇkompu
is capable of breaking the white tusk of the hog and adorns it on his chest;
[[PP: muṟṟal āmai iḷanākamoṭu ēṉa mulaikkompu avai pūṇṭu
(Campantar, Piramapuram, (1) 2)]]
kuṟaḷ āmai pūṇavallāṉ
is capable of wearing the shell of a dwarf tortoise.
puricaṭaimēl ōrpuṉal koṉṟai pēṇavallāṉ
is desirous of having on the twisted caṭai water and koṉṟai.
peṇmakaḷ taṉṉai orupākam kāṇavallāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which Civaṉ, who has on one half a lady, is warmly attached
{1:103}__3+
{$} tēṉ akattu ār vaṇṭu (atu) uṇṭa tikaḻ koṉṟaitāṉ nakattār taṇmati cūṭit talaimēl
wearing on the head a cool crescent and a garland of shining blossomed koṉṟai from which bees drank the honey sitting inside the flower.
ōr vāṉakattōr vaiyakattōrkaḷ toḻutu ēttum kāṉakattāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which of Civaṉ who dwells in the cremation ground, who is praised with joined hands by the celestials who always think of him and the people of this world, is warmly attached.
{1:103}__4+
{$} tūya vaṇṭu yāḻ cuṭarkkoṉṟai tuṇaiyal ceytāṉ
Civaṉ wears a garland of shining koṉṟai on which spotless bees hum like the music of yāl, knitting them into two.
peṇṇil nallāḷai orupākam piṇaiyal ceytāṉ
was united on one half with a lady of good nature.
iṇaiyal ceyyā ilaṅku eyil mūṉṟum eri uṇṇak kaṇaiyal ceytāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which Civaṉ who discharged an arrow on all the three glittering forts which were not friendly, to be consumed by fire, is warmly attached.
{1:103}__5+
{$} pai uṭaiya pāmpoṭu nīṟu payilkiṉṟa mey uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has on his body sacred ash along with a cobra with hood, both united in him.
veṇpiṟai cūṭi virikoṉṟai maiuṭai māmiṭaṟṟu aṇṇal
the chief who has a black neck the blackness of which is increasing, and wears a white crescent and blossomed koṉṟai;
[[PP: maittaḻaiyāniṉṟa māmiṭaṟṟu ampalavaṉ
(Kōvaiyār, 102);]]
[[PP: mā-karumai: māmiṭaṟu eṉpatu paṇputtokaiyāy iṉṉatu itu eṉṉum tuṇaiyāy niṟṟalāṉum, maittaḻaiyā niṉṟa eṉpatu akkarumaiyatu mikutiyai uṇartti niṟṟalāṉum kūṟiyatu kūṟal ākāmai uṇarka
(Do. Pērāciriyar)]]
maṟi cērnta kai uṭaiyāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which Civaṉ who holds a deer in his hand, is warmly attached.
{1:103}__6+
{$} viricaṭai mēl ōr veḷḷam ellām virikoṉṟai koḷḷa vallāṉ kuraikaḻal ēttum ciṟuttoṇṭar uḷḷam ellām uḷki
thinking the hearts of humble devotees who praises the feet wearing sounding Kaḻal of Civaṉ who can receive on his spread out caṭai all the flood of Kaṅkai and blossomed koṉṟai.
niṉṟu aṅkē uṭaṉ āṭum kaḷḷam vallāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which Civaṉ the chief who moves remaining in the hearts is warmly attached.
[[Variant reading: (1) ciṟutoṇṭar]]

VMS8 / {1:103}__PIFI7+
{$} arakkar tam kōṉai aruvaraiyiṉ nōtal ceytāṉ (1) noṭi aḷavāṉkaṇ viral ūṉṟi
Civaṉ inflicted pain firing his toe firmly in an instant under the mountain which could not be uprooted.
pōtal ceytāṉ ātal ceytāṉ
he allowed him to go away from mountain and granted him boons.
peṇmakaḷ taṉṉoṭu orupākam kātal ceytāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which Civaṉ who bestowed his love by placing a lady on one half is warmly attached.
[[Variant reading: (1) noṭivaraiyiṉkaṇ]]
VMS9 / {1:103}__PIFI8+
{$} ēṉam atu āyum iṭanta pemmāṉ
the god whose feet mal could not see though assuming the form of a pig.
aṉam āyum toṭarnta pemmāṉ
who was the cause for Piramaṉ who followed to see the head assuming the form of a swam (Both could not see either feet or head).
tūmati cūṭi varaiyārtam maṭantai pemmāṉ
the god who is the husband of the daughter of the mountain and who wears a spotless crescent.
vārkaḻal ōcci kālaṉaik kaṭanta pemmāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which the god who gained a victory our Kālaṉ (god of death) by raising the long leg to strike and kick him, is warmly attached.
VMS10 / {1:103}__PIFI9+
{$} tiripuram tēya niṉṟāṉ
Civaṉ stood on the chariot to destroy the three cities!
kaṅkai caṭaimēl pāya niṉṟāṉ
stood when the Kaṅkai flowed swiftly on his caṭai.
palar pukaḻntu ētta ulaku ellām cāya niṉṟāṉ
stood when all the worlds were perishing many people to praise his fame, when all things are absorbed into him.
vaṉ camaṇ kuṇṭar (1) cākkīyar
the low and strong amanar and cākkiyar (cākkīyar-cākkīyar the lengthening of the letter is for metre)
kāya niṉṟāṉ
remains to be heated by them.
kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟē
Kaḻukkuṉṟu is the temple to which Civaṉ is warmly attached.
[[Variant reading: cākkiyarkaḷ]]
VMS11 / {1:103}__PIFI10+
{$} kaṇṇutalāṉ kātal cey kōyil kaḻukkuṉṟai
on Kaḻukkuṉṟu to which Civaṉ who has a frontal eye, is warmly attached.
naṇṇiyacīr ñāṉacampantaṉ tamiḻmālai paṉ iyalpāl pāṭiya pattum ivaivallār
those who are capable of reciting these ten tamiḻ verses which are like garlands composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ to whom fame is attached.
puṇṇiyarāy
having virtuous acts to their credit.
viṇṇavarōṭum pukuvārē
will enter into heaven along with the celestials.

*1
veḷḷanīrc caṭiyarpōlum; virumpuvārkku _eḷiyarpōlum;
_uḷḷuḷē _uruki niṉṟu _aṅku _ukappavarkku _aṉparpōlum;
kaḷḷamē viṉikaḷ_ellām karicu _aṟuttiṭuvarpōlum---
_aḷḷal _am paḻaṉi mēya _ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

*2
centaḻal_uruvarpōlum; ciṉa viṭi _uṭiyarpōlum;
ventaveṇnīṟukoṇṭu meykku _aṇintiṭuvarpōlum;
mantam _ām poḻil paḻa(ṉ)ṉi malkiya vaḷḷalpōlum;
_antam _il _aṭikaḷpōlum---_ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

*3
kaṇṇiṉāl kāmavēḷik kaṉal _eḻa viḻipparpōlum;
_eṇ_ilār puraṅkaḷmūṉṟum _eriyuṇac ciripparpōlum;
paṇṇiṉ _ār muḻavam _ōvāp pimpoḻil paḻaṉi mēya
_aṇṇalār---_emmi _āḷum _ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

*4
kāṟiṭu viṭatti _uṇṭa kaṇṭar; _eṇtōḷarpōlum;
tūṟu _iṭu cuṭalitaṉṉil cuṇṇaveṇnīṟṟarpōlum;
kūṟu _iṭum _uruvarpōlum; kuḷirpoḻil paḻaṉi mēya
_āṟu _iṭu caṭiyarpōlum---_ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

*5
pārttaṉōṭu _amar porūtu pattimi kāṇparpōlum;
kūrtta vāy _ampu kōttuk kuṇaṅkaḷi _aṟivarpōlum;
pērttum _ōr _āvanāḻi _ampoṭum koṭupparpōlum---
tīrttam _ām paḻaṉi mēya tiru _ālaṅkāṭaṉārē.

*6
vīṭṭiṉār cuṭuveṇnīṟu meykku _aṇintiṭuvarpōlum;
kāṭṭil niṉṟu _āṭal pēṇum karuttiṉi _uṭiyarpōlum;
pāṭṭiṉ _ār muḻavam _ōvāp pimpoḻil paḻaṉi mēyār;
_āṭṭiṉār, _aravamtaṉṉi;---_ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

*7
tāḷ _uṭic ceṅkama(l)lat taṭaṅ koḷ cēvaṭiyarpōlum;
nāḷ _uṭik kālaṉ vīḻa _uticeyta namparpōlum;
kōḷ _uṭip piṟavi tīrppār; kuḷirpoḻil paḻaṉi mēya
_āḷ_uṭi _aṇṇalpōlum---_ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

*8
kūṭiṉār, _umitaṉṉōṭē kuṟippu _uṭi vēṭam koṇṭu;
cūṭiṉār, kaṅkiyāḷic cuvaṟiṭu caṭiyarpōlum;
pāṭiṉār, cāmavētam; pimpoḻil paḻaṉi mēyār;
_āṭiṉār, kāḷi kāṇa;---_ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

*9
veṟṟu _aric camaṇarōṭu vili _uṭik kūṟi pōrkkum
_oṟṟaric coṟkaḷ koḷḷār kuṇaṅkaḷi _ukapparpōlum;
peṟṟamē _ukantu _aṅku _ēṟum perumiyi _uṭiyarpōlum;
_aṟṟaṅkaḷ _aṟivarpōlum---_ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

*10
mattaṉāy mali _eṭutta _arakkaṉik karattōṭu _olka
_ottiṉār, tiruvira(l)lāl _ūṉṟiyiṭṭu _aruḷvarpōlum;
pattartam pāvam tīrkkum pimpoḻil paḻaṉi mēya
_attaṉār; nammi _āḷvār---_ālaṅkāṭṭu _aṭikaḷārē.

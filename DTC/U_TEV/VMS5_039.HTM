{5:39}__1+
{$} koḷḷum kātaṉmai eytuṟum kōlvaḷai
this girl who wears a collection of bangles and who has the nature of having fallen in love with Civaṉ
vaḷḷal māmayilāṭutuṟai uṟai veḷḷam tāṅku caṭaiyaṉai vēṇṭiyē
desiring Civaṉ who supports the flood in his caṭi and the person of unbounded literality who dwells in the great Mayilāṭutuṟi.
uḷḷam uḷkittiruppeyar uraikkum
always utters his name thinking of him in her mind.
{5:39}__2+
{$} pāl mativaitta mā mayilāṭutuṟai araṉ kottiṉiṟpoli koṉṟai koṭukkil
if Araṉ who dwells in the great Mayilāṭutuṟi and who places on his head a crescent as white as milk, gives the koṉṟi shining in the bunches worn by him
eṉ paiṅkoṭi
my girl who is as tender as a green creeper
cittam tēṟum
will become clear in her mind
ceṟivaḷaicikkeṉum
will wear her bangles tightly which were once tight, as her body will swell in size out of joy.
paccai tīrum
has sallownes will also disappear
{5:39}__3+
{$} vaṇṭucēr mayilāṭutuṟai araṉ
Araṉ who dwells in Mayilāṭutuṟi where bees swarm
toṇṭar pātaṅkaḷ cūṭittutaiyilē
If I come into close contact with devotees, wearing their feet in my head.
aṇṭar vāḻvum amarar irukkaiyum kaṇṭuvīṟṟirukkum karuttu oṉṟu ilōm
I do not have the slightest idea of sitting majestically and seeing the pleasures of the position of the celestials and residing in heaven.
{5:39}__4+
{$} mañcaḷ
God of great strength
mayilāṭutuṟai añcalāḷ umaipaṅkaṉ aruḷilē
if Civaṉ who has Umi who has the name of Añcalāḷ and who dwells in Mayilāṭutuṟi grants his grace.
veñciṉak kaṭuṅkālaṉ viraikilāṉ
the dreaded Kālaṉ of cruel anger will not hasten to take our lives.
(1) añcu iṟappum piṟappum aṟukkalām
we can completely destroy death and birth which are dreadful.
[[the name of the goddess in this shrine is Apayāmpiki; añcalāḷ is a translation of that word]]
[[Variant reading: (1) aiñciṟappum; iñciṟappum]]
{5:39}__5+
{$} kaṟai nilāviya kaṇṭaṉ eṇ tōḷiṉaṉ
Civaṉ who has a neck in which the black colour is prominent and has eight shoulders.
maṟaivalāṉ mayilāṭutuṟai uṟai iṟaivaṉ nīḷkaḻal ētti irukkil
if we prise the feet which do not know destruction, of the Lord who dwells in Mayilāṭutuṟi and who is well-versed in the vētams
koṭumānuṭa vāḻkkaiyāl kuṟaivu ilōm
we will not have anything wanting in the cruel human life.
{5:39}__6+
{$} nilaimai collum neñcē!
my mind which is always taking about his benign nature!
kalaikaḷ āyavallāṉ
Civaṉ who is well-versed in what are called arts
[[āya-āyaṉa; āyvataṟku in the former meaning it is a personal noun in the form of a finite verb, in the latter it is verbal participle]]
kayilāya naṉ malaiyaṉ
who is in the mountin of Kayilāyam
mā mayilāṭu tuṟaiyaṉ
and who is in the great Mayilāṭutuṟi
nam talaiyiṉ mēlum maṉattuḷum taṅka tavam eṉ ceytāy
what is the penance you performed for that Lord to stay in our head and in the mind?
{5:39}__7+
{$} nīṟṟiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who smears himself with holy ash
nimirpuṉcaṭaiyāṉ
who has a ruddy caṭi standing upright
viṭai ēṟṟiṉāṉ
who has a bull
namai āḷ uṭaiyāṉ
who owns us as his slave
pulaṉ māṟṟiṉāṉ
who changed the course of the senses of the body
mayilāṭutuṟai eṉṟu pōṟṟuvārkkum uṇṭō puvi vāḻkkaiyē
will there be birth hereafter in this world even for those who prise Mayilāṭutuṟi of the god?
{5:39}__8+
{$} kōlum pullum orukaiyil kūrccamum tōlum pūṇṭu tuyaram uṟṟu eṉ payaṉ
what is the use of undergoing suffering by wearing deer's skin, and carrying the bundle of darbhā grass, (kus), tied into a knot, and the sacred gress, (kus) and the staff?
nīla mā mayilāṭutuṟaiyaṉē
god who is in the great Mayilāṭutuṟi which appears blue in colour!
nuṇ uṇarntōrkaṭku nūlum vēṇṭumō
Do people who have perceived the truth very keenly require books which are aids to realise god?
[[I have my own doubt whether this verse is the composition of Appar; generally his decades consist of ten verses occasionally it exceeds that limit; moreover it is a veiled attack on brahmins whose citadel was Mayilāṭutuṟi]]
{5:39}__9+
{$} paṇam koḷ āṭu aravu alkul pakīrati maṇam koḷac caṭai vaitta maṟaiyavaṉ
the brahmin who placed on his caṭi Pakīrati who has a waist like dancing cobra's hood, to receive the fragrance of the flowers on the caṭi.
vaṇaṅku mā mayilāṭutuṟai araṉ
Araṉ in Mayilāṭutuṟi which got that name as Umi worshipped Civaṉ in the form of a peacock
aṇaṅku ōr pāl koṇṭa kōlam aḻakitē
the external appearance of Araṉ who had a divine damsel on one half is exceedingly beautiful
{5:39}__10+
{$} nīḷ nilā aravac caṭai nēcaṉaip pēṇilātavar pētuṟavu ōṭṭiṉōm
we drove away the bewilderment of those who did not desire Civaṉ who is food of having in his caṭi a cobra, and a long crescent.
vāḷ nilā mayilāṭutuṟaitaṉaik kāṇil ārkkum kaṭuntuyar illaiyē
there will not be acute sufferings for anyone who has visited Mayilāṭutuṟi whose fame has spread everywhere.
{5:39}__11+
{$} paruttatōḷum muṭiyum poṭipaṭa iruttiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who pressed down the bulky shoulders and heads (of Irāvaṇaṉ) to be broken.
avaṉ iṉṉicai kēṭṭalum varattiṉāṉ mayilāṭutuṟai toḻum (1) karattiṉār viṉaikkaṭṭu aṟum; kāṇmiṉ
the bondage of karmams of those who worship with joined hands Mayilāṭutuṟi of Civaṉ who granted boons as soon as he heard his music; you know this.
[[Variant reading: (1) karuttiṉār]]

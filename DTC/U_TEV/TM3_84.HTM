• patikam: [3:84]
  • talam: tirup puṟavam (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.296

*1
peṇ _iyal _uruviṉar, perukiya puṉal viraviya piṟaik-
kaṇṇiyar, kaṭu naṭai viṭaiyiṉar, kaḻal toḻum _aṭiyavar
naṇṇiya piṇi keṭa _aruḷpuripavar, naṇuku _uyar pati---
puṇṇiya maṟaiyavar niṟai pukaḻ _oli mali puṟavamē.

*2
kokku_uṭai_iṟakoṭu piṟaiyoṭu kuḷircaṭaimuṭiyiṉar,
_akku_uṭaivaṭamum _or _aravamum _alar _araimicaiyiṉil
tikku_uṭai maruviya _uruviṉar, tikaḻ malaimakaḷoṭum
pukku _uṭaṉ_uṟaivatu---putumalar virai kamaḻ puṟavamē.

*3
koṅku _iyal curikuḻal, varivaḷai, _iḷamulai, _umai _oru-
paṅku _iyal tiru_uru _uṭaiyavar; paracuvoṭu _iralai mey
taṅkiya karatalam _uṭaiyavar; viṭaiyavar; _uṟaipati---
poṅkiya porukaṭal koḷa, _ataṉmicai _uyar puṟavamē.

*4
mātavam _uṭai maṟaiyavaṉ _uyir koḷa varu maṟaliyai,
mētaku tiruvaṭi _iṟai _uṟa, _uyir_atu vilakiṉār;
cātaka_uru _iyal curaṉ_iṭai, _umai veru_uṟa, varu
pōtaka _uri-_ataḷ maruviṉar; _uṟai pati---puṟavamē.

*5
kāmaṉai _aḻal koḷa viḻiceytu, karutalar kaṭimatil
tūmam_atu _uṟa viṟal cuṭar koḷuviya _iṟai toku pati---
_ōmamoṭu _uyarmaṟai, piṟa _iyavakaitaṉoṭu, _oḷi, keḻu
pūmakaḷ, _alaroṭu, puṉalkoṭu, vaḻipaṭu puṟavamē.

*6
col-nayam _uṭaiyavar, curutikaḷ karutiya toḻiliṉar,
piṉṉaiyar, naṭu _uṇar perumaiyar, tiruvaṭi pēṇiṭa,
muṉṉaiya mutalviṉai _aṟa _aruḷiṉar _uṟai mutu pati---
puṉṉaiyiṉ mukai neti poti _aviḻ poḻil _aṇi puṟavamē.

*7
vari taru puli_ataḷ _uṭaiyiṉar, maḻu _eṟi paṭaiyiṉar,
piritaru nakutalaivaṭam muṭimicai _aṇi perumaiyar,
_eritarum _uruviṉar, _imaiyavar toḻuvatu _or _iyalpiṉar,
puritaru kuḻal _umaiyoṭum _iṉitu _uṟai pati---puṟavamē.

*8
vaci tarum _uruvoṭu malartalai _ulakiṉai valiceyum
nicicaraṉ _uṭaloṭu neṭu muṭi_orupatum nerivu_uṟa
_ocitara _oruviral niṟuviṉar, _oḷi vaḷar veḷipoṭi
pocitaru tiru_uru _uṭaiyavar, _uṟai pati---puṟavamē.

*9
tēṉ _akam maruviya ceṟitaru muḷariceytaviciṉil
_ūṉ _akam maruviya pulaṉ nukarvu _uṇarvu _uṭai _oruvaṉum,
vāṉakam varai_akam maṟikaṭal nilaṉ _eṉum _eḻuvakaip
pōṉakam maruviṉaṉ, _aṟivu _ariyavar pati---puṟavamē.

*10
kōcaram nukarpavar, koḻukiya tuvar _aṉa tukiliṉar,---
pācura viṉai taru paḷakarkaḷ, paḻi taru moḻiyiṉar---
nīcarai viṭum, _iṉi! niṉaivu_uṟum---nimalartam _uṟaipati,
pūcurar maṟai payil niṟai pukaḻ _oli mali, puṟavamē!

*11
pōtu _iyal poḻil _aṇi puṟava naṉnakar _uṟai puṉitaṉai,
vētiyar_atipati---miku talai tamiḻ keḻu virakiṉaṉ---
_ōtiya _orupatum _uriyatu _or _icai koḷa _uraiceyum
nītiyar_avar, _iru nilaṉ_iṭai nikaḻtaru piṟaviyē.

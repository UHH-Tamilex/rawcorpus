• patikam: [3:84]
  • talam: tirup puṟavam (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.296

*1
peṇ _iyal _uruviṉar, perukiya puṉal viraviya piṟik-
kaṇṇiyar, kaṭu naṭi viṭiyiṉar, kaḻal toḻum _aṭiyavar
naṇṇiya piṇi keṭa _aruḷpuripavar, naṇuku _uyar pati---
puṇṇiya maṟiyavar niṟi pukaḻ _oli mali puṟavamē.

*2
kokku_uṭi_iṟakoṭu piṟiyoṭu kuḷircaṭimuṭiyiṉar,
_akku_uṭivaṭamum _or _aravamum _alar _arimiciyiṉil
tikku_uṭi maruviya _uruviṉar, tikaḻ malimakaḷoṭum
pukku _uṭaṉ_uṟivatu---putumalar viri kamaḻ puṟavamē.

*3
koṅku _iyal curikuḻal, varivaḷi, _iḷamuli, _umi _oru-
paṅku _iyal tiru_uru _uṭiyavar; paracuvoṭu _irali mey
taṅkiya karatalam _uṭiyavar; viṭiyavar; _uṟipati---
poṅkiya porukaṭal koḷa, _ataṉmici _uyar puṟavamē.

*4
mātavam _uṭi maṟiyavaṉ _uyir koḷa varu maṟaliyi,
mētaku tiruvaṭi _iṟi _uṟa, _uyir_atu vilakiṉār;
cātaka_uru _iyal curaṉ_iṭi, _umi veru_uṟa, varu
pōtaka _uri-_ataḷ maruviṉar; _uṟi pati---puṟavamē.

*5
kāmaṉi _aḻal koḷa viḻiceytu, karutalar kaṭimatil
tūmam_atu _uṟa viṟal cuṭar koḷuviya _iṟi toku pati---
_ōmamoṭu _uyarmaṟi, piṟa _iyavakitaṉoṭu, _oḷi, keḻu
pūmakaḷ, _alaroṭu, puṉalkoṭu, vaḻipaṭu puṟavamē.

*6
col-nayam _uṭiyavar, curutikaḷ karutiya toḻiliṉar,
piṉṉiyar, naṭu _uṇar perumiyar, tiruvaṭi pēṇiṭa,
muṉṉiya mutalviṉi _aṟa _aruḷiṉar _uṟi mutu pati---
puṉṉiyiṉ muki neti poti _aviḻ poḻil _aṇi puṟavamē.

*7
vari taru puli_ataḷ _uṭiyiṉar, maḻu _eṟi paṭiyiṉar,
piritaru nakutalivaṭam muṭimici _aṇi perumiyar,
_eritarum _uruviṉar, _imiyavar toḻuvatu _or _iyalpiṉar,
puritaru kuḻal _umiyoṭum _iṉitu _uṟi pati---puṟavamē.

*8
vaci tarum _uruvoṭu malartali _ulakiṉi valiceyum
nicicaraṉ _uṭaloṭu neṭu muṭi_orupatum nerivu_uṟa
_ocitara _oruviral niṟuviṉar, _oḷi vaḷar veḷipoṭi
pocitaru tiru_uru _uṭiyavar, _uṟi pati---puṟavamē.

*9
tēṉ _akam maruviya ceṟitaru muḷariceytaviciṉil
_ūṉ _akam maruviya pulaṉ nukarvu _uṇarvu _uṭi _oruvaṉum,
vāṉakam vari_akam maṟikaṭal nilaṉ _eṉum _eḻuvakip
pōṉakam maruviṉaṉ, _aṟivu _ariyavar pati---puṟavamē.

*10
kōcaram nukarpavar, koḻukiya tuvar _aṉa tukiliṉar,---
pācura viṉi taru paḷakarkaḷ, paḻi taru moḻiyiṉar---
nīcari viṭum, _iṉi! niṉivu_uṟum---nimalartam _uṟipati,
pūcurar maṟi payil niṟi pukaḻ _oli mali, puṟavamē!

*11
pōtu _iyal poḻil _aṇi puṟava naṉnakar _uṟi puṉitaṉi,
vētiyar_atipati---miku tali tamiḻ keḻu virakiṉaṉ---
_ōtiya _orupatum _uriyatu _or _ici koḷa _uriceyum
nītiyar_avar, _iru nilaṉ_iṭi nikaḻtaru piṟaviyē.

eṉ cintaiyāṉ
Civaṉ who dwells in my mind.
kai, māṉam, mata kaḷiṟṟiṉ urivaiyāṉ (kāṇ)
covers himself with the skin of a male elephant with a trunk, dignity and must.
kaṟaik kaṇṭaṉ (kāṇ)
has a black neck.
kaṇ ār neṟṟiyāṉ (kāṇ)
has an eye on the forehead.
ammāṉ (kāṇ)
is the great god.
[[ammāṉ = a + māṉ; māṉ = makāṉ]]
āṭu aravu oṉṟu āṭṭiṉāṉ (kāṇ)
made to dance an insignificant cobra which has the nature of moving its hood, spreading it.
aṉal āṭi (kāṇ)
dances in the fire.
ayil vāyc cūlattāṉ (kāṇ)
has a trident of sharp blades.
emmāṉ (kāṇ)
is our Lord.
ēḻ ulakum āyiṉāṉ kāṇ
pervades all the seven worlds.
eri cuṭarōṉ kāṇ
is like the burning lamp.
ilaṅku maḻuvāḷaṉ kāṇ
has a weapon of a shining battle axe.
cemmāṉattu oḷiyaṉṉa mēṉiyāṉ kāṇ
has a colour like that of the evening red sky
[[NOTE_LEX: cevvāṉam has become cemmāṉam;
in colloquial speech this is the form of the word]]
[[The nominative cintaiyāṉ takes urivaiyāṉ and other words as predicate.]]
tiruvārūrāṉ (kāṇ)
is in Tiruvārūr
[[All the kāṇ in this verse and the following ones are expletives.]]

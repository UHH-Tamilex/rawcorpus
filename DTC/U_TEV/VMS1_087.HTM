{1:87}__1+
{$} vaṭukūr puṉal cūḻnta vaṭukūr aṭikaḷē
the deity in Vaṭukūr surrounded by water in which fine black sand is found in plenty
[[vaṭu:
cēyāṟṟu vaṭuvāḻ ekkarmaṇaliṉum palarē (malipaṭukaṭam, 555-556)]]
cuṭukūr erimālai aṇivar
will adorn himself with a garland of fire which is very hot.
cuṭarvēlar
has a shining trident.
koṭukūr maḻuvāḷ oṉṟu uṭaiyār
has a weapon of a very cruel battle-axe.
viṭai urvar
rides on a bull.
kaṭukūr pacikāmam kavalai piṇi illār
has no acute hunger which is rigourous, lust, anxieties and diseases.
{1:87}__2+
{$} kōlam poḻiṟ cōlaik kūṭi maṭavaṉṉam ālum vaṭukūril
in Vaṭukūr where the young swans join together in the beautiful gardens and parks, and cry aloud.
aṭikaḷ
the deity!
pālum naṟuneyyum tayirum payiṉṟu āṭi
bathing often in milk! ghee of sweet smell and curd.
(1) ēlum cuṭunīṟum eṉpum oḷimalka
adorning himself with appropriate burnt ash and bones, their brightness to increase.
āṭum
dances.
[[Variant reading: (1) mēlum cuṭunīṟum]]
{1:87}__3+
{$} aṭikaḷ
the deity, Civaṉ.
ēṭumalar mōntu aṅku eḻil ār varivaṇṭu pāṭum vaṭukūril
in Vaṭukūr where the beautiful bees which have lines on their bodies smell the petals of flowers and hum.
iḷantiṅkaḷ cuṭar poṟ caṭai taṉmēl
(will adorn) on his shining golden caṭi a crescent
umai añca ōṭum kaḷiyāṉai uripōrttu
covering himself with the skin of an elephant of must fluid which ran aginst him, to make Umi frightened.
āṭum
dances.
{1:87}__4+
{$} kavarum aṇi kollaik kaṭiya mu(l)lai nallār pavarum vaṭukūṭum āṭum aṭikaḷ
the deity who dances in Vaṭukūr where many ladies crowd in the fragrant arabian jasmine creeper in the beautiful forest region which fascinates the eyes
[[mulai-mullai: the middle letter is elided]]
[[PP_EXT: mulaiyaṇinta muṟuvalāḷ
(Kalittoki, 1-13)]]
tuvarum puricaiyum tutainta maṇi māṭam kavara (1) eri ōṭṭi
spreading fire to the beautiful storeys which were close to one another and were pinted with red colour, to consume with avidity.
kaṭiyamatil eytār
shot an arrow on the well-guarded forts.
[[Variant reading: (1) eriyūṭṭik]]
{1:87}__5+
{$} tuṇi ār uṭai āṭai tuṉṉi arai taṉmēl
wearing on the waist as dress a loin-cloth torn from a big cloth.
taṇiyā aḻal nākam tariyāvakai vaittār
placed a cobra whose anger does not abate, not to stand without moving.
paṇi ār aṭiyārkaḷ palarum payiṉṟu ētta
to be prised without ceasing by the many devotees who are engaged in service to god.
aṇiār vaṭukūril aṭikaḷ āṭum
the deity will dance in beautiful Vaṭukūr.
{1:87}__6+
{$} taḷarum koṭi aṉṉāḷ taṉṉōṭu uṭaṉ āki
being united with a lady who is like a tender creeper.
kiḷarum (1) aravu ārttu
tying a cobra which is infuriated.
kiḷarum muṭimēl ōrvaḷarum piṟaicūṭi
wearing a waxing crescent on the shining head.
varivaṇṭu icaipāṭa
the bees which have lines on them to hum.
oḷirum vaṭukūril aṭikaḷ āṭum
the deity dances in shining Vaṭukūr.
[[Variant reading: (1) aravāṭṭik]]
{1:87}__7+
{$} varikūril āṭum aṭikaḷ
the deity who dances in Vaṭukūr.
neṭiyar
is tall.
ciṟitāya nirampāmaticūṭum muṭiyar
wears on his head a small crescent which has not grown to its full size.
viṭaiūrvar
rides on a bull.
(1) koṭiyar moḻi koḷḷār
will not regard the words of cruel people.
kaṭiya koḻil kālaṉ maṭiya utai ceyta aṭiyar
kicked with his foot to kill Kālaṉ (god of death) who has the cruel act of taking away the lives.
[[Variant reading: (1) koṭiyamoḻi]]
{1:87}__8+
{$} paṟaiyum atir kuḻalum pōlap palavaṇṭu (āṅku) aṟaiyum vaṭukūril āṭum aṭikaḷē
the deity who dances in Vaṭukūr where many bees hum loudly like the drum and the sounding flute.
piṟaiyum neṭu nīrum piriyā muṭiyiṉār
has on his head a crescent and extensive water.
maṟaiyum palapāṭi mayāṉattu uṟaivārum
and he dwells in the cremation ground and sings many vētams.
{1:87}__9+
{$} amtaṇ vaṭukūril āṭum(m) aṭikaḷ (ē)
the deity who dances in beautiful and cool Vaṭukūr.
cantam malar vēytta caṭaiyiṉṉiṭai vimmu kantam miku tiṅkaḷ katircintumālai
in the evening when the moon which has excessive fragrance as it stays in the caṭi which wears beautiful flowers, sheds its rays.
emmai nayantu vantu naṉṟum aruḷ ceyvār
will bestow his grace in an excessive measure, coming to us showing affection for us.
[[naṉṟum maruḷ ceyvār: if the words are split in this manner it will mean he will make my mind bewildered!]]
{1:87}__10+
{$} ticaivu illā arumāvaṭukūril āṭum aṭikaḷē
the god who dances in the rare and big Vaṭukūr, where there is no perplexity
[[ticaivu:
māyap pōrt, tērppākaṉārkku ivaḷ cintai tuḻāyt ticaikkiṉṟatē (tiruvāymoḻi, 4-6-1);
innilai niṉṟavaṉ taṉṉai eyta nōkki, neynnilai vēlavaṉ nīticaittatu uṇṭō;
ticaittatum illai; eṉakku vantu tīyōr icaittatum illai (Kamparāmāyaṇam, Kaikēyi cuḻviṉippatalam, 18-19).
tirumāl aṭivīḻa
Tirumāl to fall at his feet.
ticai nāṉmukaṉ āyaperumāṉ (1) uṇarkillāp perumāṉ
the god who could not be known by the god of four faces one for each quarter.
neṭumuṭicēr cerumāl viṭai ūrum cemmāṉ
the great red god, who rides on a bull which is Māl who can fight and wears a long crown.
[[Variant reading: (1) uṇarkillāṉ perumāṉ]]

{$} paṭinōṉpu avai āvar paḻiil pukaḻ āṉa kaṭināḷ nikaḻcōlai kamaḻum vaṭukūrai
about Vaṭukūr where the gardens are as fragrant as the marriage day and has fame without blemish of the god who is the ceremonial fastings in this world.
paṭiyāṉa cintai moḻi ār campantaṉ aṭi ñāṉam vallār
those who are able to recite the verse that contin knowledge of Civaṉ of Campantaṉ who has a disciplined mind and words.
aṭicērvārkaḷē

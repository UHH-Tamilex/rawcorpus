• patikam: [7:42]
  • talam: tiru veñcamākkūṭal
  • paṇ: kollikkauvāṇam
  • VMS: Vol.11, p.179

*1
_eṟikkum katir vēy _utir mutta(m)moṭu,
_ēlam(m), _ilavaṅkam, takkōlam, _iñci,
ceṟikkum puṉaluḷ peytukoṇṭu, maṇṭi,
tiḷaittu, _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkaraimēl---
muṟikkum taḻai mā muṭappuṉṉai, ñāḻal,
kurukkattikaḷmēl kuyil kūval _aṟā,
veṟikkum kalaimā---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*2
kuḷaṅkaḷpalavum kuḻiyum niṟaiya,
kuṭa mā maṇi cantaṉamum(m) _akilum
tuḷaṅkum puṉaluḷ peytukoṇṭu maṇṭi,
tiḷaittu, _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkaraimēl---
vaḷam koḷ matil māḷikai, kōpuramum,
maṇi maṇṭapamum(m), _ivai mañcutaṉṉuḷ
viḷaṅkum mati tōy---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*3
varai māṉ _aṉaiyār---mayilcāyal nallār,
vaṭivēlkaṇ nallār---palar vantu _iṟaiñca,
tirai _ār puṉaluḷ peytukoṇṭu maṇṭi,
tiḷaittu, _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkaraimēl---
nirai _ār kamukum, neṭun tāḷ-teṅkum,
kuṟun tāḷ palavum, viravik kuḷirum
virai _ār poḻil cūḻ---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*4
paṇ nēr moḻiyāḷai _ōrpaṅku _uṭaiyāy!
paṭu kāṭṭu_akattu _eṉṟum _ōr paṟṟu _oḻiyāy!
taṇ _ār _akilum, nala cāmaraiyum,
_alaittu _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkaraimēl---
maṇ _ār muḻavum kuḻalum _iyampa,
maṭavār naṭam_āṭum(m) maṇi _araṅkil
viṇ _ār mati tōy---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*5
tuḷai veṇkuḻaiyum curuḷ veṇtōṭum
tūṅkum kātil-tuḷaṅkum paṭiyāy!
ka(ḷ)ḷaiyē kamaḻum malarkkoṉṟaiyiṉāy!
kalantārkku _aruḷceytiṭum kaṟpakamē!
pi(ḷ)ḷaiveṇpiṟaiyāy! piṟaṅkum caṭaiyāy!
piṟavātavaṉē! peṟuvataṟtu _ariyāy!
ve(ḷ)ḷaimālviṭaiyāy! veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*6
toḻuvārkku _eḷiyāy! tuyar tīra niṉṟāy!
curumpu _ār malarkkoṉṟai tuṉṟum caṭaiyāy!
_uḻuvārkku _ariya viṭai _ēṟi! _oṉṉār
puram tī _eḻa _ōṭuvittāy! _aḻakā!
muḻavu _ār _olipāṭaloṭu _āṭal _aṟā
mutukāṭu _araṅkā naṭam_āṭa vallāy!
viḻavu _ār maṟukiṉ veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*7
kaṭam mā kaḷiyāṉai _urittavaṉē!
karikāṭu _iṭamā, _aṉal vīci niṉṟu
naṭam_āṭa vallāy! narai_ēṟu _ukantāy!
nallāy! naṟūṅkoṉṟai nayantavaṉē!
paṭam_āyiram _ām parut tuttip paiṅkaṇ
pakuvāy _eyiṟṟoṭu _aḻalē _umiḻum
viṭa vār_aravā! veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*8
kāṭum malaiyum nāṭum _iṭaṟi,
katir mā maṇi cantaṉamum(m) _akilum
cēṭaṉ(ṉ) _uṟaiyum(m) _iṭamtāṉ virumpi,
tiḷaittu, _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkaraimēl---
pāṭal muḻavum kuḻalum(m) _iyampa,
paṇaittōḷiyar pāṭaloṭu _āṭal _aṟā,
vēṭar virumpum---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*9
koṅku _ār malarkkoṉṟai_amtāravaṉē!
koṭukoṭṭi _or vīṇai _uṭaiyavaṉē!
poṅku _āṭu_aravum puṉalum caṭaimēl
potiyum puṉitā! puṉam cūḻntu _aḻaku _ār
tuṅku _ār puṉaluḷ peytukoṇṭu maṇṭi,
tiḷaittu _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkaraimēl---
veṅ kār vayal cūḻ---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*10
vañcinuṇ_iṭaiyār---mayilcāyal _aṉṉār,
vaṭivēlkaṇ nallār---palar vantu _iṟaiñcum
veñcamākkūṭal vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉaiyum
vēṇṭutiyē _eṉṟu tāṉ virumpi,
vañciyātu _aḷikkum vayal nāvalarkōṉ---
vaṉappakai_appaṉ, vaṉtoṇṭaṉ---coṉṉa
ceñcol-tamiḻmālaikaḷpattum vallār
civalōkattu _iruppatu tiṇṇam _aṉṟē!

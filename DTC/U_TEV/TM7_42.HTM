  • paṇ: kollikkuvāṇam

*1
_eṟikkum katir vēy _utir mutta(m)moṭu,
_ēlam(m), _ilavaṅkam, takkōlam, _iñci,
ceṟikkum puṉaluḷ peytukoṇṭu, maṇṭi,
tiḷittu, _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkarimēl---
muṟikkum taḻi mā muṭappuṉṉi, ñāḻal,
kurukkattikaḷmēl kuyil kūval _aṟā,
veṟikkum kalimā---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*2
kuḷaṅkaḷpalavum kuḻiyum niṟiya,
kuṭa mā maṇi cantaṉamum(m) _akilum
tuḷaṅkum puṉaluḷ peytukoṇṭu maṇṭi,
tiḷittu, _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkarimēl---
vaḷam koḷ matil māḷiki, kōpuramum,
maṇi maṇṭapamum(m), _ivi mañcutaṉṉuḷ
viḷaṅkum mati tōy---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*3
vari māṉ _aṉiyār---mayilcāyal nallār,
vaṭivēlkaṇ nallār---palar vantu _iṟiñca,
tiri _ār puṉaluḷ peytukoṇṭu maṇṭi,
tiḷittu, _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkarimēl---
niri _ār kamukum, neṭun tāḷ-teṅkum,
kuṟun tāḷ palavum, viravik kuḷirum
viri _ār poḻil cūḻ---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*4
paṇ nēr moḻiyāḷi _ōrpaṅku _uṭiyāy!
paṭu kāṭṭu_akattu _eṉṟum _ōr paṟṟu _oḻiyāy!
taṇ _ār _akilum, nala cāmariyum,
_alittu _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkarimēl---
maṇ _ār muḻavum kuḻalum _iyampa,
maṭavār naṭam_āṭum(m) maṇi _araṅkil
viṇ _ār mati tōy---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*5
tuḷi veṇkuḻiyum curuḷ veṇtōṭum
tūṅkum kātil-tuḷaṅkum paṭiyāy!
ka(ḷ)ḷiyē kamaḻum malarkkoṉṟiyiṉāy!
kalantārkku _aruḷceytiṭum kaṟpakamē!
pi(ḷ)ḷiveṇpiṟiyāy! piṟaṅkum caṭiyāy!
piṟavātavaṉē! peṟuvataṟtu _ariyāy!
ve(ḷ)ḷimālviṭiyāy! veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*6
toḻuvārkku _eḷiyāy! tuyar tīra niṉṟāy!
curumpu _ār malarkkoṉṟi tuṉṟum caṭiyāy!
_uḻuvārkku _ariya viṭi _ēṟi! _oṉṉār
puram tī _eḻa _ōṭuvittāy! _aḻakā!
muḻavu _ār _olipāṭaloṭu _āṭal _aṟā
mutukāṭu _araṅkā naṭam_āṭa vallāy!
viḻavu _ār maṟukiṉ veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*7
kaṭam mā kaḷiyāṉi _urittavaṉē!
karikāṭu _iṭamā, _aṉal vīci niṉṟu
naṭam_āṭa vallāy! nari_ēṟu _ukantāy!
nallāy! naṟūṅkoṉṟi nayantavaṉē!
paṭam_āyiram _ām parut tuttip piṅkaṇ
pakuvāy _eyiṟṟoṭu _aḻalē _umiḻum
viṭa vār_aravā! veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*8
kāṭum maliyum nāṭum _iṭaṟi,
katir mā maṇi cantaṉamum(m) _akilum
cēṭaṉ(ṉ) _uṟiyum(m) _iṭamtāṉ virumpi,
tiḷittu, _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkarimēl---
pāṭal muḻavum kuḻalum(m) _iyampa,
paṇittōḷiyar pāṭaloṭu _āṭal _aṟā,
vēṭar virumpum---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*9
koṅku _ār malarkkoṉṟi_amtāravaṉē!
koṭukoṭṭi _or vīṇi _uṭiyavaṉē!
poṅku _āṭu_aravum puṉalum caṭimēl
potiyum puṉitā! puṉam cūḻntu _aḻaku _ār
tuṅku _ār puṉaluḷ peytukoṇṭu maṇṭi,
tiḷittu _eṟṟu ciṟṟāṟu_ataṉ kīḻkkarimēl---
veṅ kār vayal cūḻ---veñcamākkūṭal
vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē.

*10
vañcinuṇ_iṭiyār---mayilcāyal _aṉṉār,
vaṭivēlkaṇ nallār---palar vantu _iṟiñcum
veñcamākkūṭal vikirtā! _aṭiyēṉiyum
vēṇṭutiyē _eṉṟu tāṉ virumpi,
vañciyātu _aḷikkum vayal nāvalarkōṉ---
vaṉappaki_appaṉ, vaṉtoṇṭaṉ---coṉṉa
ceñcol-tamiḻmālikaḷpattum vallār
civalōkattu _iruppatu tiṇṇam _aṉṟē!

• patikam: [7:98]
  • talam: tiru naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil
  • paṇ: pañcamam
  • VMS: Vol.8, p.75

*1
taṇ _iyal vemmaiyiṉāṉ; talaiyil kaṭaitōṟum pali,
paṇ _iyal meṉmoḻiyār, _iṭak koṇṭu _uḻal paṇṭaraṅkaṉ---
puṇṇiya nālmaṟaiyōr muṟaiyāl _aṭi pōṟṟu_icaippa
naṇṇiya---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*2
valam kiḷar mā tavam cey malaimaṅkai _orpaṅkiṉaṉāy,
calam kiḷar kaṅkai taṅkac caṭai _oṉṟu_iṭaiyē tarittāṉ---
palam kiḷar paimpoḻil-taṇpaṉiveṇmatiyait taṭava,
nalam kiḷar---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*3
kacciyaṉ; _iṉ karuppūr viruppaṉ; karutik kacivār
_ucciyaṉ; piccai_uṇṇi(y); _ulakaṅkaḷ_ellām _uṭaiyāṉ---
nocci _am paccilaiyāl, nurainīr-puṉalāl,-toḻuvār
nacciya---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*4
pāṭiya nālmaṟaiyāṉ; paṭu palpiṇakkāṭu _araṅkā
_āṭiya mānaṭattāṉ---_aṭi pōṟṟi! _eṉṟu _aṉpiṉarāyc
cūṭiya ceṅkaiyiṉār palatōttiram vāytta colli
nāṭiya---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*5
pilam taru vāyiṉoṭu peritum vali mikku _uṭaiya
calantaraṉ _ākam _irupiḷavu _ākkiya, cakkaram muṉ
nilam taru māmakaḷkōṉ neṭumāṟku _aruḷceyta pirāṉ---
nalam taru naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*6
veṇpoṭi mēṉiyiṉāṉ, karunīlamaṇimiṭaṟṟāṉ,
peṇ paṭi ceñcaṭaiyāṉ, piramaṉciram pīṭu _aḻittāṉ---
paṇpu _uṭai nālmaṟaiyōr payiṉṟu _ētti, palkāl vaṇaṅkum,
naṇpu _uṭai---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*7
toṭai mali koṉṟai tuṉṟum caṭaiyaṉ, cuṭar veṇmaḻuvāḷ
paṭai mali kaiyaṉ, meyyil pakaṭṭu_īr_urippōrvaiyiṉāṉ---
maṭai mali vaṇkamalammalarmēl maṭa_aṉṉam maṉṉi
naṭai mali---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*8
kuḷirtaru tiṅkaḷ, kaṅkai, kuravōṭu, _ara, kūviḷamum,
miḷirtaru puṉcaṭaimēl _uṭaiyāṉ; viṭaiyāṉ---virai cēr
taḷir taru kōṅku, vēṅkai, taṭa mātavi, caṇpakamum,
naḷirtaru---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*9
kamar payil veñcurattuk kaṭuṅ kēḻal piṉ kāṉavaṉāy,
_amar payilvu _eyti, _aruccuṉaṉukku _aruḷceyta pirāṉ---
tamar payil taṇ viḻavil-taku caivar, tavattiṉ mikka
namar, payil---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*10
karuvarai pōl _arakkaṉ kayilai(m)malaikkīḻk kataṟa,
_oruviralāl _aṭarttu, _iṉ_aruḷ ceyta _umāpatitāṉ---
tirai poru poṉṉi naṉnīrt tuṟaivaṉ, tikaḻ cempiyarkōṉ,
narapati,---naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉē.

*11
kōṭu _uyar veṅkaḷiṟṟut tikaḻ kōcceṅkaṇāṉ cey kōyil,
nāṭiya naṉṉilattup peruṅkōyil nayantavaṉaic
cēṭu _iyal ciṅkitantai---caṭaiyaṉ, tiru _ārūraṉ---
pāṭiya pattum vallār pukuvār, paralōkattuḷē.

*1
_ātikkaṇṇāṉ mukattil _oṉṟu ceṉṟu(v)
_allāta col _urikkat taṉ ki vāḷāl
cētitta tiruvaṭiyi, cella nalla
civalōkaneṟi vakuttuk kāṭṭuvāṉi,
mā matiyi, mātu _ōrkūṟu_āyiṉāṉi,
mā malarmēl _ayaṉōṭu mālum kāṇā
nātiyi, nampiyi, naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*2
paṭiyāṉi, pācupatavēṭattāṉi,
paṇṭu _aṉaṅkaṟ pārttāṉi, pāvam_ellām
_aṭiyāmik kāppāṉi, _aṭiyārtaṅkaḷ
_arumarunti, _āvā! _eṉṟu _aruḷceyvāṉi,
caṭiyāṉi, cantiraṉit tarittāṉtaṉṉi,
caṅkatta muttu _aṉiya veḷḷi_ēṟṟiṉ
naṭiyāṉi, nampiyi, naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*3
paṭa _aravam _oṉṟu koṇṭu _ariyil _ārtta
parāparaṉi, piññīli mēviṉāṉi,
_aṭal _aravam paṟṟik kaṭinta nañci
_amutu_āka _uṇṭāṉi, _ātiyāṉi,
maṭal _aravam maṉṉu pūṅkoṉṟiyāṉi,
māmaṇiyi, māṇikku_āyk kālaṉtaṉṉi
naṭal _aravam ceytāṉi, naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*4
kaṭṭaṅkam _oṉṟu tam kiyil _ēnti,
kaṅkaṇamum kātil viṭu tōṭum _iṭṭu,
cuṭṭa(_a)ṅkam koṇṭu tutiyap pūci,
cuntaraṉāyc cūlam ki _ēntiṉāṉi;
paṭṭa(_a)ṅkamāli niṟiyac cūṭi,
palkaṇamum tāmum paranta kāṭṭil
naṭṭaṅkam _āṭiyi, naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*5
_ulantārtam _aṅkam koṇṭu _ulakam_ellām
_oru noṭiyil _uḻalvāṉi, _ulappu _il celvam
cilantitaṉakku _aruḷceyta tēvatēvi,
tiruc cirāppaḷḷi _em civalōka(ṉ)ṉi,
kalantārtam maṉattu _eṉṟum kātalāṉi,
kacci _ēkampaṉi, kamaḻpūṅkoṉṟi
nalam tāṅkum nampiyi, naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*6
kulam koṭuttuk kōḷ nīkka vallāṉtaṉṉi,
kulavariyiṉ maṭappāvi _iṭappālaṉi,
malam keṭuttu mā tīrttam_āṭṭik koṇṭa
maṟayavaṉi, piṟi tavaḻ ceñcaṭiyiṉāṉi,
calam keṭuttut tayāmūlataṉmam _eṉṉum
tattuvattiṉvaḻi niṉṟu tāḻntōrkku _ellām
nalam koṭukkum nampiyi, naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*7
pū viriyum malarkkoṉṟic caṭiyiṉāṉi,
puṟampayattu _emperumāṉi, pukalūrāṉi,
mā _iriyak kaḷiṟu _uritta mintaṉtaṉṉi,
maṟikkāṭum valivalamum maṉṉiṉāṉi,
tē _iriyat tikaḻ takkaṉ vēḷvi_ellām
citittāṉi, _utittu _avaṉtaṉ ciram koṇṭāṉi,
nā viriya maṟi naviṉṟa naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ _aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*8
collāṉi, cuṭarp pavaḷaccōtiyāṉi,
tol _avuṇar purammūṉṟum _eriyac ceṟṟa
villāṉi, _ellārkkum mēl_āṉāṉi,
melliyalāḷ pākaṉi, vētamnāṉkum
kallāliṉ nīḻalkīḻ _aṟam kaṇṭāṉi,
kāḷattiyāṉi, kayili mēya
nallāṉi, nampiyi, naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*9
kuṉṟāta mā muṉivaṉ cāpam nīṅkak
kurikaḻalāl kūṟṟuvaṉik kumitta kōṉi,
_aṉṟāka _avuṇar purammūṉṟum vēva
_ār_aḻalvāy _ōṭṭi _aṭarvittāṉi,
ceṉṟu _ātu vēṇṭiṟṟu _oṉṟu _īvāṉtaṉṉi,
civaṉ _emperumāṉ _eṉṟu _iruppārkku _eṉṟum
naṉṟu_ākum nampiyi, naḷḷāṟṟāṉi,---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

*10
_iṟavātē varam peṟṟēṉ _eṉṟu mikka
_irāvaṇaṉi _irupatutōḷ neriya _ūṉṟi,
_uṟavu_āki, _iṉ_ici kēṭṭu, _iraṅki, mīṇṭē
_uṟṟa piṇi tavirttu, _aruḷa vallāṉtaṉṉi;
maṟavātār maṉattu _eṉṟum maṉṉiṉāṉi;
mā matiyam, malarkkoṉṟi, vaṉṉi, mattam,
naṟavu, _ār ceñcaṭiyāṉi; naḷḷāṟṟāṉi;---
nāṉ_aṭiyēṉ niṉikkappeṟṟu _uynta_āṟē!

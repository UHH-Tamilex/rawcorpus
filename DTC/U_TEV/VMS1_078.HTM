{1:78}__1+
{$} vari vaḷar avir oḷi aravu arai tāḻa
the cobra with shining brightness lines on its body to hang low.
vārcaṭai muṭimicai vaḷarmati cūṭi
wearing a waxing crescent on the long caṭai coiled into a crown.
karivaḷar tarukaḻal kālvalaṉ ēnti
raising the right leg which wears a Kaḻal on which the form of an elephant is embossed.
kaṉal eri āṭuvār kāṭu araṅkāka
will dance in the burning fire; having the cremation ground as the stage.
virivaḷar tarupoḻil iḷamayil āla
when the young peacocks dance in the gardens which are expanding and growing.
veḷ niṟattu aruvikaḷ tiṇṇeṉa vīḻum eri vaḷar iṉam maṇipuṉam aṇicāral iṭaiccuram mēviya ivarvaṇam eṉṉē
How wonderful is the colour of this god who is in iṭaiccuram which has slopes which are made beautiful by millet farms which abound in heaps of gems which are like fire; and from which streams fall with a vibrating sound.
{1:78}__2+
{$} āṟṟaiyum ēṟṟatu ōr avircaṭai uṭaiyavar
Civaṉ has a glittering caṭai on which be received even the river which descended with a roaring noise.
aḻakiṉai aruḷuvār
will grant beauty to his devotees.
kuḻaku alatu aṟiyār
does not know anything except youthfulness.
kūṟṟu uyir cekuppatu or koṭumaiyai uṭaiyavar
has the cruel nature of taking away the life of the god of death.
naṭu iruḷ āṭuvar
will dance at dead of night.
koṉṟai amtārar
wears a garland of koṉṟai.
cēṟṟu ayal miḷarvaṉa kayal iḷavāṉai ceruc ceya ōrppaṉa cemmukamanti
the red-faced female monkeys watch keenly the fight between carps which leap and young scarbard fish near the mire.
oṟṟaiyōṭu uḻitarum eḻil tikaḻ cāral
having slopes in which beauty is eminent, where they wander with their males.
iṭaiccuram mēviya ivarvaṇam eṉṉē
see 1st verse.

{$} kāṉamum cuṭalaiyum kaṟpaṭu nilaṉum kātalar
Civaṉ is attached towards forest, cremation ground and mountains.
tītuilar
has no defects.
kaṉal maḻuvāḷar
has a red hot iron which is burning.
vāṉamum nilamaiyum irumaiyum āṉār
he has the nature of both the things of heaven and this earth
[[see line 2 of verse 4]]
nāṉamum pukaioḷi viraiyoṭu kamaḻa
is worshipped with musk and incense which spread their fragrance with other fragrant things and light.
vaṇaṅkavum iṇaṅkavum vāḻttavum paṭuvar
He is paid obeisance, is befriended and is praised by songs of benediction.
naḷir poḻil iḷamaññai maṉṉiya pāṅkar
by the side of the cool gardens where young peacocks stay permanently.
ēṉamum piṇaiyalum eḻiltikaḻ cāral
in the slopes which are shining by the wild and its female.

{1:78}__5+
{$} kārkoṇṭa kaṭikamaḻ virimalark koṉṟaik kaṇṇiyar
Civaṉ wears a chaplet of blossomed koṉṟai flowers which spread their fragrance in the appropriate season of winter.
vaḷarmati katirviṭa
when the crescent emits rays.
kaṅkai nīr koṇṭa caṭaiyiṉar
has on his caṭai water of Kaṅkai which he received.
viṭai uyar koṭiyar
has a flag of a bull held aloft.
niḻal tikaḻ maḻuviṉar
has a battle axe of shining brightness.
aḻal tikaḻ niṟattar
has a colour shining like the fire.
cīr koṇṭa meṉciṟai vaṇṭu paṇ ceyyum ceḻum puṉal aṉaiyaṉa ceṅkulai vāḻai ēr koṇṭa palaviṉoṭu eḻil tikaḻ cāral
having slopes of shining beauty on which the plaintain trees which have red clusters of fruits like the fertile red-coloured water along with tall jack-fruit trees on which the bees of soft and graceful wings hum like melody-types.
iṭaiccuram ... eṉṉē
see 1st verse.

{$} tōṭu aṇi kuḻaiyiṉar
Civaṉ wears a womens ear-ring and a mens' ear-ring.
cuṇṇa veṇṇīṟṟavar
smears on his body the white powder of holy ash.
cuṭalaiyiṉ āṭuvar
dances in the cremation ground.
tōl uṭaiyākap pīṭu uyar ceytatu ōr perumaiyai uṭaiyar
has a greatness obtained by the might of having the skin as a dress.
pēyuṭaṉ āṭuvar
will dance along with pēy-s.
periyavar perumāṉ
is the Lord of great people.
oḻukuva kōṭalkaḷ muḻukuva tumpi
the superior Variety of bees, tumpi, dive into the long malabar glory lily flowers!
kuravamum maravamum maṉṉiya pāṅkar ēṭu aviḻ putumalar kaṭikamaḻ cāral
having slopes where fragrance of the fresh flowers which unfold their petals by the side of the places where bottle-flower trees and common cadamba trees are permanent.
iṭaiccuram ... eṉṉē
1st verse.
{1:78}__7+
{$} kaḻal malku kāliṉar, vēliṉar, nūlar kavartalai aravoṭu kaṇṭiyum pūṇpar
Civaṉ wears Kaḻal staying permanently in the leg; has a trident; wears a sacred thread; adorns himself with a necklace of uruttirākkan beads also with a cobra of many hoods.
aḻal malku eriyoṭum aṇimaḻu ēnti āṭuvar
will dance holding a red-hot iron with a fire of increasing heat.
pāṭuvar ār aṇaṅku uṭaiyar
will sing having a divine lady in his body.
poḻil malkum nīṭiya aravamum maravam(um) maṉṉiya kavaṭṭiṭaip puṇar kuyil ālum eḻil malku cōlaiyil vaṇṭu icaippāṭum
in the parks which increase in beauty and where the indian cuckoo united with its mate sings in the forked branch which is always there, where the maravam trees and nākam trees flourish in the gardens.
[[aravam the synonym of nākam; it means the tree nakam;]]
[[PP: oḷitikaḻ utti urukeḻu nākam (pāripāṭal, 12-4);]]
[[As it is placed along with maravam another tree, it must mean only a tree.]]
iṭaiccuram ... maṉṉi
see 1st verse.
{1:78}__8+
{$} tēm kamaḻ koṉṟai am tirumalar puṉaivār
Civaṉ adorns himself with beautiful flowers of koṉṟai in which the fragrance of honey spreads.
tikaḻ tiru caṭaimicait tiṅkaḷum cūṭi
wearing a crescent on the shining caṭai.
vīntavar cuṭalai veṇṇīṟu meypūci
smearing himself with the white ash of the cremation ground where people who have died are cremated.
vēṟum ōr caritaiyar vēṭamum uṭaiyar
see verse no.4.
cāntamum akiloṭu mukil potintu alampit tavaḻ kaṉamaṇiyoṭu miku paḷiṅku iṭaṟi
striking against crystal quartz bound in large quantity along with heavy gems which crawl, and making a sound, concealing the cloud, sandal-wood tree and eagle-wood trees.
ēntu veḷ aruvikaḷ eḻil tikaḻ cāral
having slopes of shining beauty, which have white streams which carry them.
iṭaiccuram ... eṉṉē
see 1st verse.
{1:78}__9+
{$} pala ilam iṭupali kaiyil oṉṟu ēṟpar
Civaṉ receives alms in the skull held in the hand, which is given in many houses.
pala pukaḻ allatu paḻi ilar tāmum
has fame of many kinds, has no blemish.
talai ilaṅku avir oḷi neṭumuṭi arakkaṉ taṭakkaikaḷ aṭarttu ōr taṉmaiyai uṭaiyar
has the nature of pressing down the big hands of the arakkaṉ who has tall crowns which glitter with rays, and shine on the heads.
malai ilaṅku aruvikaḷ maṇamuṭivu atira
the streams glittering in the hill make a sound like the muḻavu played on occasions of mariage.
maḻaitavaḻ iḷamaññai malkiya cāral
in the slopes where young peacocks are on the increases and clouds seem to crawl.
ilai ilavaṅkamum ēlamum kamaḻum iṭaiccuram
iṭaiccuram where clove-trees with leaves and cardamon spread their fragrance.
iṭaiccuram ... eṉṉē
see 1st verse.
{1:78}__10+
{$} perumaikaḷ tarukkiyōr pētu uṟukiṉṟa peruṅkaṭal vaṇṇaṉum piramaṉum ōrā arumaiyar
Civaṉ who was difficult to be investigated by Māl who has the colour of the ocean and Piramaṉ who were bewildered, and were proud, conscious of their greatness.
aṭiniḻal paraviniṉṟu ēttum aṉpuṭai aṭiyavarkku aṇiyarum āvar
is near at hand to the devotees who cherish him with love, praising in the third person and in the 2nd person the shelter of his feet.
karumai koḷ vaṭivoṭu cuṉaivaḷar kuvaḷaik kayal iṉam vayal iṟa vāḷaikaḷ iriya
the group of carp fish which are in the blue nelumbo flowers of black from growing in the mountain springs, and the young scabbard fish found in the fields to flee from their places.
erumaikaḷ paṭitara iḷaaṉam ālum iṭaiccuram
iṭaiccuram where the young swans make sound when the buffaloes sink in water.
iṭaiccuram ... eṉṉē
see 1st verse.
{1:78}__11+
{$} maṭaiccuram maṟivaṉa vāḷaiyum kayalum maruviya vayaltaṉil varupuṉal kāḻi
born in Kaḻi which has water arriving fresh in fields where the scabbard and carp fish go about often through sluices of fields and have become accustomed to the fields.
caṭaiccurantu uṟaivatu ōr piṟai uṭai aṇṇal caritaikaḷ paraviniṉṟu uruku campantaṉ
campantaṉ whose heart melts praising the many acts of the chief who has a crescent residing in the space of the caṭai.
puṭaiccarattu aruvaraip pūkkamaḻ cāral puṇar maṭanaṭaiyavar puṭai iṭai ārtta iṭaiccuram ēttiya icaiyoṭu pāṭal ivai cola vallavar piṇiilar (tām, ē)
those who are able to recite these verses with music, which were in praise of Iṭaiccuram which has ladies of gentle gait are on the sides and in the centre, and slopes where flowers of the mountain difficult to climb which has jungles by its sides spread their fragrance, will have no diseases.

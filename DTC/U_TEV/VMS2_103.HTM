{2:103}__1+
{$} pulku poṉ niṟam puricaṭai neṭumuṭip pōḻ iḷamaticūṭi
wearing a young crescent in the twisted caṭi coiled into a tall crown, which is golden in colour.
pilku tēṉ uṭai naṟu malark koṉṟaiyum piṇaiyal ceytavar mēya, malku taṇtuṟai ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam, allum naṉpakalum toḻum aṭiyavarkku
near those devotees who worship night and day mākāḷam which has freshes and situated on the north bank of the aricil where in the ghat people crowd, and where Civaṉ who wears a garland of fragrant koṉṟi flowers from which honey is dripping.
aruviṉai aṭaiyāvē
irresistible Karmams will not reach them.

{2:103}__2+
{$} aravam āṭṭuvar
Civaṉ cuses the cobra to dance spreadign its hood.
amtukil puli ataḷ
his beautiful cloth in tiger`s skin.
aṅkaiyil aṉal ēnti iravum āṭuvar
will dance at night holding a fire in his palm.
ivai ivar caritaikaḷ
these are his conducts.
icaivaṉa pala pūtam
many pūtams sing (when he dances)
maravam tōypoḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam paraviyum paṇintu ētta vallār avar
those who are able to pay obeisance to, and prise in the third and second persons, mākālan which has freshes and which is on the north bank of the aricil having gardens of common cadamba trees.
payaṉ talaippaṭuvār
will obtin benefits.
{2:103}__3+
{$} kuṇaṅkaḷ kūṟiyum
enumerating his good qualities.
kuṟṟaṅkaḷ paraviyum
prising the faults in the second person
kuraikaḻal aṭicērak kaṇaṅkaḷ pāṭavum
the groups of pūtams to sing his prises coming near his feet which wear sounding Kaḻal
kaṇṭavar paravavum
those who see him to prise him addressing him in the second person.
karuttu aṟintavarmēya
Civaṉ who knows their individual ideas, dwells.
maṇam koḷ pūm poḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam vaṇaṅkum uḷḷamōṭu aṇaiyavallārkaḷai valviṉai aṭaiyāvē
the irresistible Karmams will not approach those who come near with a mind to make obeisance to mākāḷam which has floods and is situated on the north bank of the aricil and has flower gardens of fragrance.
{2:103}__4+
{$} eṅkum ētum ōr piṇiilar
Civaṉ has no disease anywhere and even a little.
kēṭu ilar
knows no death
iḻai vaḷar naṟuṅkoṉṟai taṅku toṅkalum tāmamum kaṇṇiyum tām makiḻntavar
rejoiced in wearing as ornaments garland of koṉṟi which hang on the chest, chaplets worn on the shoulders and chaplets worn on the head.
mēya maṅkul tōy poḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakaraivarupuṉal mākāḷam, kaṅkulum, pakalum toḻum aṭiyāvar kātaṉmai uṭaiyār
the devotees who worship mākāḷam on the north bank of the aricil which brings freshes and which has gardens on which clouds settle and in which Civaṉ dwells, night and day, are really people who have love towards him.
{2:103}__5+
{$} matiyam tōy poḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam putiya pū voṭu cāntamum pukaiyum koṇṭu ēttutal purintārkkē
to those who prised Civaṉ with fresh flowers, sandal paste and incense in mākāḷam on the north bank of the aricil which bring freshes and has gardens touching the moon.
karutiya poruḷ kūṭil
as their wishes are realised.
netiyam eṉ uḷa
what is wealth other than this!
pōkam maṟṟu eṉ uḷa
what are pleasures other than this?
nila micai nalam āya katiyam eṉ uḷa
what else is the way to salvation, in this earth? (katiyam: am has no separate meaning)
vāṉavar eṉ uḷar
what use are the celestials?
{2:103}__6+
{$} kaṇ ulāviya katir oḷi muṭimicaik kaṉal viṭu cuṭar nākam teṇṇilā voṭu tilatamum nakutalai tikaḻa vaittavar mēya maṇ ulām poḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam
mākāḷam on the north bank of the aricil which brings freshes and has gardens famous throughout the world and where Civaṉ who placed prominently a small ciruclar mark on the forehead, a lughing skull and a clear crescent, and a bright cobra emitting poison on the head on which the ray of the frontal eye which moves about in the cati.
uḷ nilām niṉaippu uṭaiyavar yāvar ivvulakiṉil uyarvārē
those who always meditate in their minds upon god are really superior people in this world.
{2:103}__7+
{$} tūcu tāṉ arait tōl uṭaikkaṇṇi amcuṭar viṭu naṟuṅ koṉṟai pūcu veṇ poṭip pūcuvatu aṉṟiyum pukaḻ purintavar mēya, mācu ulām poḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam, pēcu nīrmaiyar yāvar
those who have the good nature of prising mākāḷam on the north bank of the aricil which brings floods and where on the gardens the clouds move about, where Civaṉ who desired fame in addition to smearing himslef with white holy ash, wears brilliant and fragrant chaplet of koṉṟi and wears as a cloth in his waist, a skin.
ivvulakiṉil perumaiyaip peṟuvārē
will attin greatness in this world.
{2:103}__8+
{$} pauvam ārkaṭal ilaṅkaiyar kōṉ taṉaip paruvaraik kīḻ ūṉṟi
pressing the King of the inhabitants of ilaṅki which is surrounded by ocean with eddies and foam.
evvam tīra aṉṟu imaiyavarkku aruḷceyta iṟaiyavaṉ uṟai kōyil
the temple where the Lord who granted his grace to the celestials in the past to remove their sufferings.
mavvam tōy poḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam kavvaiyāl toḻum aṭiyavarmēl viṇaikaṉaliṭaic cetiḷ aṉṟē
the Karmams of the devotees who worship mākālam on the north bank of the aricil which brings freshes and has beautiful garden, with the loud noise of prayer will be destroyed like saw-dust put into fire.
{2:103}__9+
{$} uyyum kāraṇam uṇṭu eṉṟu karutumiṉ
(People of this world!) think that there is a means to save yourselves
oḷikiḷar malarōṉum paikoḷ pāmpu aṇaippaḷḷi koḷ aṇṇalum parava niṉṟavar
Civaṉ who is prised by Piramaṉ who is seated in a brilliant (lotus) flower and the chief (Māl) who sleeps on the bad of a serpent which has many hoods.
mēya mākāḷam
mākāḷam where he dwells.
mai ulām poḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam kaiyiṉāl toḻutu avalamum piṇiyum tam kavalaiyum kaḷaivār
those who worship with their joined hands mākāḷam on the north bank of the aricil will weed ot their pin, diseases and cares.
{2:103}__10+
{$} piṇṭi pālarum pīli koṇṭu uḻalvārum, maṇṭaikoḷ tērarum kaṇṭanūlarum kaṭuntoḻilāḷarum kaḻaṟa niṉṟavar mēya mākāḷam
mākāḷam where Civaṉ who stays to be abused by camanar who are attached to the asoka tree and who carry a bundle of peacock feathers, and buddhists who holds a begging bowl resembling in shape the skull who created their religious works and have cruel acts.
vaṇṭu ulām poḻil ariciliṉ vaṭakarai varupuṉal mākāḷam paṇṭu nām ceyta pāvaṅkaḷ paṟṟu aṟap paravutal ceyvōmē
we shall prise mākāḷam on the north bank of the aricil which brings freshes and has gardens in which bees are wandering, so that the sins committed by us in previous births, and attachments might leave us.
{2:103}__11+
{$} varupuṉal mākāḷattu māṟu taṉṉoṭu maṇmicai illatu
in mākāḷam which has floods and has no other shrine comparable to it on the earth
īṟum ātiyum ākiya cōtiyai ēṟu amarperumāṉai
on the effulgent light that is the end and beginning of all things and the Lord who rides on a bull.
nāṟu pūm poḻil kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉa tamiḻ mālai kūṟu-vāraiyum kēṭkavallāraiyum kuṟṟaṅkaḷ kuṟukāvē
faults will not come near those who have the will to listen to, and recite, the garlands of tamiḻ verses composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kāḻi which has fragrant flower-gardens.

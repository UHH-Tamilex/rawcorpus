[[A note on the shrine. There are two shrines having name of Tirumullivāyil; one is near Cīrkāḻi eight miles to the East, so this is called Teṉ Tirumullivāyil; the other one is two and half miles to the Nort-East of Āvaṭi R.S. so it is called Vaṭa Tirumullivāyil]]

{2:88}__1+
{$} tirumullaivāyil itu
this tirumullivāyil
kaḷimaṇṭu cōlai kaḻaṉikkalanta kamalaṅkaḷ matuvil teḷi maṇṭi uṇṭu
where after over-drinking the essence of the honey which is found in lotuses growing in fields and gardens affording increasing delight.
ciṟaivaṇṭupāṭum
the winged bees hum.
tuḷimaṇṭi uṇṭu
having overdrunk the drops of poison.
niṟam vanta kaṇṭaṉ
the Lord who has a neck which turned black.
naṭam maṉṉu cuṭarōṉ
the brilliant light who is always dancing.
oḷi maṇṭi umpar ulakam kaṭanta umai paṅkaṉ
one who has Umi as his half; and who lustre being limitless went beyond the world of the celestials beings.
eṅkaḷ araṉ ūr
the place of our araṉ
[[araṉ: haraṉ]]
[[tirumullaivāyil itu predicate;
ūr subject;
this stands good for the following 9 verses.]]
{2:88}__2+
{$} paruvattil vantu payaṉuṟṟa paṇpaṉ
the Lord of eight qualities who bestows the benefit of his grace when the soul is ripe for receiving it.
ayaṉaip paṭaittaparamaṉ
the supreme one who created Ayaṉ (Brahma)
aravat toṭu aṅkam avaikaṭṭi eṅkum aravikka niṉṟa araṉ ūr
the place of araṉ who tied serpents and bones and cused them to make a sound.
[[aravikka: a word coined from aravam meaning sound;]]
[[PP: amararōṭu acurar kūṭi, aravittuk kaṭaiyat tōṉṟum āla nañcu (Appar - Naṉipaḷḷi 7)]]
uruvattil mikka oḷir caṅkoṭu ippiyavai ōtam mōta veruvi
the pearl oyster-shells along with bright conches big in size being afrid as they are dashed by the waves;
vantu ceḻu muttu alaikkoḷ tirumullaivāyil ituvē
this tirumullivayil where they leave the big pearls by coming to the streets.
{2:88}__3+
{$} vārāta nāṭaṉ
He has a place from where people who have gone there do not return to be born agin.
varuvārtam villiṉ urumelki
those who approach him, making their bodies which are transient like the rinbow lean by fast and penances.
(villiṉ urumelki the bodies bent like bow, due to old age)
nāḷum urukil
if their hearts melt dily
ārāta iṉpam
he grants them insatiable happiness.
akalāta aṉpaṉ
he is the loving friend who never forsakes them.
aruḷmēvi niṉṟa araṉ ūr
the place of araṉ who is always glad with grace.
pērāta cōti piriyāta mārpiṉ alar mēvu pētai piriyāḷ
the lady who is fond of the lotus flower and who is seated in the chest (of Māl) without leaving it which has brilliance, without leaving that place.
tīrāta kātal neti nēra nīṭu tirumullaivāyil ituvē
this tirumulivāyil where she stays permanently to grant wealth with love which never leaves her
[[pērāta cōti may mean also the jewel `kustupam' which adorns the chest of Tirumāl.]]
{2:88}__4+
{$} oṉṟu oṉṟoṭu oṉṟu oru nāṉkoṭu aintum iru ḻūṉṟoṭu ēḻum uṭaṉāy
pervading the twenty-five tattuvams
[[(1 + 1 + 1 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7) = 25;
there are 1) mūlappakuti 2) putti 3) akaṅkaram (ego) 4) mind 5) ear 6) body 7) eyes 8) mouth (tongue) 9) nose 11) feet 12) hands 13) anus 14) genital organs 15) sound 16) sense of touch 17) forms 18) tastes 19) smell 20) space 21) air 22) light 23) water 24) earth 25) the soul (purutaṉ) is]]
[[PP: cf. aiyañciṉ appuṟattāṉum ārur amarnta ammaṉē
(appar, tiruvarūr, paṇ kāntāram, verse 10).]]
[[It may mean also this: the Lord is beyond the twenty-five forms mentioned in āgamās]]
aṉṟu iṉ ṟoṭu eṉṟum aṟi vāṉavarkkum aṟiyāmai niṉṟa araṉ ūr
the place of araṉ who is unable to be comprehended by vāṉavar (tevar) who are capable of knowing other things, from early times to this day and for ever.
kuṉṟu oṉṟoṭu oṉṟu, kulai oṉṟoṭu oṉṟu koṭi oṉṟoṭu oṉṟu kuḻumic ceṉṟu
the mounds of sand, the ridges, and creepers combine together and join with one another.
oṉṟoṭu oṉṟu ceḷivāl niṟainta tirumullaivāyil ituvē
is the tirumullavāyil which is filled up by their denseness as one joins with the other.
{2:88}__5+
{$} kompu aṉṉa miṉṉiṉ iṭaiyāḷ ōr kūṟaṉ
god who has a lady comparable to a slender flower-branch and has a waist like lightning.
nāḷum viṭai ēṟu kuḻakaṉ
the youth who rides always on a bull.
nampaṉ em aṉpaṉ
one in whose we repose confidence and who is our loving friend.
maṟai nāvaṉ
one who is reciting the maṟi (vētam)
vāṉiṉ mati ēṟu ceṉṉi araṉ ūr
the place of araṉ on whose head the crescent which appears in the sky, has ascended.
ampu aṉṉa oṇ kaṇavar āṭu araṅkiṉ aṇi kōpuraṅkaḷ
the beautiful towers of stages where girls who have bright eyes like arrows, dance.
aḻaku ār cempoṉṉa cevvi tarumāṭam nīṭu tirumullaivāyil ituvē
is this tirumullivāyil where the storeys which spread beauty like the beauty of the red gold, rise high into the sky.
{2:88}__6+
{$} ūṉ ēṟu vēliṉ uru ēṟu kaṇṇi oḷiyēṟu koṇṭa oruvaṉ
the unequalled on who has on his form the bright complexion of the lady with eyes resembling the shaped of the vēl on which flesh is greatly sticking.
āṉēṟu (atu) ēṟi
one who rides on a bull.
aḻaku ēṟu nīṟaṉ
one who smears ash which increases beauty.
aravēṟu pūṇam araṉ ūr
the place of araṉ who wears as ornaments superior serpents.
māṉ ēṟu kollai, mayil ēṟi vantu kuyil ēṟu, cōlai maruvi
having becoming familiar with the garden in which Kuyil (indian cuckoo) is in abundance, and peacocks ascend the trees there, and sylvan tract where there are deer.
tēṉ ēṟu māviṉ vaḷam ēṟi āṭu tirumullaivāyil ituvē
is the tirumullivāyil where they dance ascending the dense and sweet mango-trees.
{2:88}__7+
{$} neñcu āra nīṭu niṉaivārai mūṭuviṉai tēya niṉṟa nimalaṉ
the spotless person who stays permanently to make the twin acts which envelope those who think about him for a long time with their minds, saturated with thoughts about him, decrease gradually.
añcu āṭu ceṉṉi aravu āṭu kaiyaṉ, aṉal āṭu mēṉi araṉ ūr
the place of the Lord whose form is like fire, who has in his hand a dancing serpent, a head on which the fine products got from the cow are poured for bathing.
mañcu ārum māṭam maṉai tōṟum aiyam uḷateṉṟu
thinking that alms can be surely obtined in every houses which has a storey on which the cloud is moving
vaikivariṉum ceñcāli nelliṉ vaḷar cōṟu aḷik koḷ tirumullaivāyil ituvē
is this tirumullivāyil where abundant rice of the red variety of paddy is given even if people come late.
{2:88}__8+
{$} varai vantu eṭutta valivāḷ arakkaṉ muṭi pattum iṟṟu neriya
he pressed down with his leg, the heads of arakkaṉ who had a strong sword, and came near and removed the mountin, to be broken and crushed.
urai vanta poṉṉiṉ uruvantamēṉi umai paṅkaṉ
one who has Umi as his half who has a brilliant form like gold of superior finess as tested on a touch stone.
eṅkaḷ araṉ ūr
the place of our araṉ.
varaivanta cantoṭu akil untivantu miḷirkiṉṟa poṉṉi vaṭapāl
- on the northern side of poṉṉi (kaviri) which is brilliant by bringing sandal-wood growing in the mountin and pushing forward akil (akil : a fragrant wood like sandal-wood; eagle-wood).
tirai vantu vantu ceṟi tēṟal āṭu tirumullaivāyil ituvē
is this tirmullivāyil where the waves come very often and mix the thick honey.
{2:88}__9+
{$} mēl oṭi nīṭu viḷaiyāṭal mēvuviri nūlaṉ
the Lord has a long sacred thread which is fond of playing, going up his shoulders.
vētamutalvaṉ
he is the source of all the vētams.
pāl āṭu mēṉi kariyāṉum (piramaṉum ) muṉṉiyavar tēṭaniṉṟa paraṉ ūr
the place of the supreme being who stood as a column of fire to be searched by Piramaṉ and the black-coloured one who is rocking in the ocean of milk, with the idea of knowing his head and feet.
[[By the use of um we have to supply piramaṉ (Brahma)]]
tāl āṭu nīlamalar tuṉṟi niṉṟa katir ēṟu cennel vayalil
in the fields of red paddy with abundant ears of corn where the blue nelumbo flowers move wafted by the wind.
cēlōṭu vāḷai kutikoḷḷa malku tirumullaivāyil ituvē
is this Tirumullivāyil where the scabbard fish jump along with the carnatic carp fish (cēl)
{2:88}__10+
{$} paṉai malku tiṇkai matamā uritta paramaṉ
the supreme Lord who flayed the skin of an elephant having a strong trunk resembling the trunk of palmyra
nam namapaṉ aṭiyē niṉaivu aṉṉa cintai aṭaiyātatu ōr amaṇ māya niṉṟa araṉ ūr
the place of araṉ who was the cuse of the ruin of amaṇ (jins) who did not have that mind which always think of the feet of our god only in whoms we can repose confidence.
vaṉam malku kaitai vakuḷaṅkaḷ eṅkum mukuḷaṅkaḷ eṅkum neriya
the buds are dense everywhere in all gardens where fragrant screw-pines and vakuḷam (pointed leave ape-flower)
ciṉaimalku puṉṉai tikaḻvācam nāṟu - tirumullaivāyil ituvē
is this tirumullivāyil where the superior fragrance of punni (puṉṉai = mast-wood tree) which has many branches, spreads everywhere.
{2:88}__11+
{$} aṇi koṇṭa kōtaiyavaḷ naṉṟum ētta aruḷceyta entai
our father who bestowed his grace when the beautiful kōti worshipped him devoutly. (kōtaiyam mai is the name of tēvi in this shrine)
maruvār tiṇi koṇṭa mūṉṟupuram eyta villi tirumullaivāyil itaṉmēl
composed on this tirumullivāyil where the god has a bow from which he spot an arrow on the strong three cities of enemies.
taṇi koṇṭa cintaiyavar kāḻiñāṉa miku pantaṉ oṇtamiḻkaḷiṉ aṇikoṇṭa pattum
all the beautiful ten verses of tamiḻ with great meaning, by ñaṉacampantaṉ of kaḻi where people with tranquil minds reside.
icai pāṭu pattar akal vāṉam āḷvar mikavē
the devotees who sing there with music will rule over the wide heaven for a long time.

• patikam: [3:75]
  • talam: tiruc caṇpinakar (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.304

*1
_emtamatu cinti piriyāta perumāṉ! _eṉa _iṟiñci, _imiyōr
vantu tuticeyya, vaḷar tūpamoṭu tīpam mali vāymi_ataṉāl,
_anti _amar canti pala_arccaṉikaḷ ceyya _amarkiṉṟa _aḻakaṉ,
cantam mali kuntaḷam nal mātiṉoṭu, mēvu pati---caṇpinakarē.

*2
_aṅkam viri tutti _aravu, _āmi, viravu _āram _amar mārpil _aḻakaṉ,
paṅkayamukattu _ariviyōṭu piriyātu, payilkiṉṟa patitāṉ---
poṅku paravit tiri koṇarntu pavaḷattiraḷ polinta _ayalē,
caṅku puri _ippi taraḷattiraḷ piṟaṅku _oḷi koḷ caṇpinakarē.

*3
pōḻum mati, tāḻum nati, poṅku _aravu, taṅku puripuṉcaṭiyiṉaṉ,
yāḻ_iṉmoḻi, māḻiviḻi, _ēḻi_iḷamātiṉoṭu _irunta patitāṉ---
vāḻi, vaḷar ñāḻal, makiḻ, maṉṉu puṉi, tuṉṉu poḻil māṭu, maṭal _ār
tāḻi mukiḻ vēḻam miku tantam _eṉa, _untu taku caṇpinakarē.

*4
koṭṭa muḻavu, _iṭṭa _aṭi vaṭṭaṇikaḷ kaṭṭa, naṭam_āṭi, kulavum
paṭṭam nutal, kaṭṭu malar maṭṭu mali, pāviyoṭu mēvu patitāṉ---
vaṭṭamati taṭṭu poḻiluḷ,-tamatu vāymi vaḻuvāta moḻiyār
caṭṭa kali_eṭṭumaruvu_eṭṭum vaḷar tatti payil caṇpinakarē.

*5
paṇ _aṅku _eḻuvu pāṭaliṉoṭu _āṭal piriyāta paramēṭṭi, pakavaṉ,
_aṇaṅku _eḻuvu pākam _uṭi _ākam _uṭi _aṉparperumāṉatu _iṭam_ām---
_iṇaṅku _eḻuvi _āṭu koṭi māṭam matil, nīṭu viri _ār puṟavu_elām,
taṇam keḻuvi _ēṭu _alar koḷ tāmariyil _aṉṉam vaḷar caṇpinakarē.

*6
pālaṉ _uyirmēl _aṇavu kālaṉ _uyir pāṟa _uticeyta paramaṉ,
_ālum mayil pōl _iyali _āyiḻitaṉōṭum, _amarvu _eytum _iṭam_ām---
_ēlam mali cōli _iṉavaṇṭu malar keṇṭi, naṟavu _uṇṭu _iciceya,
cāli vayal kōlam mali cēl _ukaḷa, nīlam vaḷar caṇpinakarē.

*7
viṇ poy_ataṉāl maḻi viḻātoḻiyiṉum, viḷivutāṉ mika _uṭi
maṇ poy_ataṉāl vaḷam _ilātoḻiyiṉum, tamatu vaṇmi vaḻuvār,
_uṇpa karavār, _ulakiṉ _ūḻipalatōṟum nili_āṉa patitāṉ---
caṇpinakar; _īcaṉ_aṭi tāḻum _aṭiyārtamatu taṉmi _atuvē.

*8
varikkulamakaṭku _oru maṟukkam varuvitta, mati _il, vali _uṭi
_arakkaṉatu _urak karacirattu _uṟa _aṭarttu, _aruḷpurinta _aḻakaṉ
_irukki_atu---_arukkaṉ mutalāṉa _imiyōr kuḻumi _ēḻ viḻaviṉil,
tarukkulam nerukkum mali taṇpoḻilkaḷ koṇṭal _aṉa caṇpinakarē.

*9
nīlavari pōla nikaḻ kēḻal_uru, nīḷ paṟavi nēr _uruvam, _ām
mālum malarāṉum, _aṟiyāmi vaḷar tī_uruvam _āṉa varataṉ,
cēlum _iṉa vēlum _aṉa kaṇṇiyoṭu naṇṇu pati---cūḻ puṟavu_elām
cāli mali, cōli kuyilpuḷḷiṉoṭu kiḷḷi payil, caṇpinakarē.

*10
pōtiyarkaḷ, piṇṭiyarkaḷ, pōtu vaḻuvāta vaki _uṇṭu, palapoy
_ōti, _avar koṇṭu ceyvatu _oṉṟum _ili; naṉṟu_atu _uṇarvīr! _urimiṉō---
_āti, _emi _āḷ_uṭiya _ariviyoṭu pirivu_ili, _amarnta patitāṉ,
cātimaṇi teṇtiri koṇarntu vayal puka _eṟikoḷ caṇpinakarē!

*11
vāriṉ mali koṅki _uminaṅkiyoṭu caṅkaraṉ makiḻntu _amarum _ūr,
cāriṉ mural teṇkaṭal vicumpu _uṟa muḻaṅku _oli koḷ caṇpinakarmēl,
pāriṉ malikiṉṟa pukaḻ niṉṟa tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ, _uricey
cīriṉ mali centamiḻkaḷ ceppumavar, cērvar, civalōka neṟiyē.

• patikam: [3:75]
  • talam: tiruc caṇpainakar (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.304

*1
_emtamatu cintai piriyāta perumāṉ! _eṉa _iṟaiñci, _imaiyōr
vantu tuticeyya, vaḷar tūpamoṭu tīpam mali vāymai_ataṉāl,
_anti _amar canti pala_arccaṉaikaḷ ceyya _amarkiṉṟa _aḻakaṉ,
cantam mali kuntaḷam nal mātiṉoṭu, mēvu pati---caṇpainakarē.

*2
_aṅkam viri tutti _aravu, _āmai, viravu _āram _amar mārpil _aḻakaṉ,
paṅkayamukattu _arivaiyōṭu piriyātu, payilkiṉṟa patitāṉ---
poṅku paravait tirai koṇarntu pavaḷattiraḷ polinta _ayalē,
caṅku puri _ippi taraḷattiraḷ piṟaṅku _oḷi koḷ caṇpainakarē.

*3
pōḻum mati, tāḻum nati, poṅku _aravu, taṅku puripuṉcaṭaiyiṉaṉ,
yāḻ_iṉmoḻi, māḻaiviḻi, _ēḻai_iḷamātiṉoṭu _irunta patitāṉ---
vāḻai, vaḷar ñāḻal, makiḻ, maṉṉu puṉai, tuṉṉu poḻil māṭu, maṭal _ār
tāḻai mukiḻ vēḻam miku tantam _eṉa, _untu taku caṇpainakarē.

*4
koṭṭa muḻavu, _iṭṭa _aṭi vaṭṭaṇaikaḷ kaṭṭa, naṭam_āṭi, kulavum
paṭṭam nutal, kaṭṭu malar maṭṭu mali, pāvaiyoṭu mēvu patitāṉ---
vaṭṭamati taṭṭu poḻiluḷ,-tamatu vāymai vaḻuvāta moḻiyār
caṭṭa kalai_eṭṭumaruvu_eṭṭum vaḷar tattai payil caṇpainakarē.

*5
paṇ _aṅku _eḻuvu pāṭaliṉoṭu _āṭal piriyāta paramēṭṭi, pakavaṉ,
_aṇaṅku _eḻuvu pākam _uṭai _ākam _uṭai _aṉparperumāṉatu _iṭam_ām---
_iṇaṅku _eḻuvi _āṭu koṭi māṭam matil, nīṭu virai _ār puṟavu_elām,
taṇam keḻuvi _ēṭu _alar koḷ tāmaraiyil _aṉṉam vaḷar caṇpainakarē.

*6
pālaṉ _uyirmēl _aṇavu kālaṉ _uyir pāṟa _utaiceyta paramaṉ,
_ālum mayil pōl _iyali _āyiḻaitaṉōṭum, _amarvu _eytum _iṭam_ām---
_ēlam mali cōlai _iṉavaṇṭu malar keṇṭi, naṟavu _uṇṭu _icaiceya,
cāli vayal kōlam mali cēl _ukaḷa, nīlam vaḷar caṇpainakarē.

*7
viṇ poy_ataṉāl maḻai viḻātoḻiyiṉum, viḷaivutāṉ mika _uṭai
maṇ poy_ataṉāl vaḷam _ilātoḻiyiṉum, tamatu vaṇmai vaḻuvār,
_uṇpa karavār, _ulakiṉ _ūḻipalatōṟum nilai_āṉa patitāṉ---
caṇpainakar; _īcaṉ_aṭi tāḻum _aṭiyārtamatu taṉmai _atuvē.

*8
varaikkulamakaṭku _oru maṟukkam varuvitta, mati _il, vali _uṭai
_arakkaṉatu _urak karacirattu _uṟa _aṭarttu, _aruḷpurinta _aḻakaṉ
_irukkai_atu---_arukkaṉ mutalāṉa _imaiyōr kuḻumi _ēḻ viḻaviṉil,
tarukkulam nerukkum mali taṇpoḻilkaḷ koṇṭal _aṉa caṇpainakarē.

*9
nīlavarai pōla nikaḻ kēḻal_uru, nīḷ paṟavai nēr _uruvam, _ām
mālum malarāṉum, _aṟiyāmai vaḷar tī_uruvam _āṉa varataṉ,
cēlum _iṉa vēlum _aṉa kaṇṇiyoṭu naṇṇu pati---cūḻ puṟavu_elām
cāli mali, cōlai kuyilpuḷḷiṉoṭu kiḷḷai payil, caṇpainakarē.

*10
pōtiyarkaḷ, piṇṭiyarkaḷ, pōtu vaḻuvāta vakai _uṇṭu, palapoy
_ōti, _avar koṇṭu ceyvatu _oṉṟum _ilai; naṉṟu_atu _uṇarvīr! _uraimiṉō---
_āti, _emai _āḷ_uṭaiya _arivaiyoṭu pirivu_ili, _amarnta patitāṉ,
cātimaṇi teṇtirai koṇarntu vayal puka _eṟikoḷ caṇpainakarē!

*11
vāriṉ mali koṅkai _umainaṅkaiyoṭu caṅkaraṉ makiḻntu _amarum _ūr,
cāriṉ mural teṇkaṭal vicumpu _uṟa muḻaṅku _oli koḷ caṇpainakarmēl,
pāriṉ malikiṉṟa pukaḻ niṉṟa tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ, _uraicey
cīriṉ mali centamiḻkaḷ ceppumavar, cērvar, civalōka neṟiyē.

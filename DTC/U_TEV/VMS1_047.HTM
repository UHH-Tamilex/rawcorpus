[[Cirapuram is one of the twelve names of Cīkāḻi; talaipaṇṭu āṇṭa mūtūr,
talaimuṉ āṇṭa aṇṇal nakar
parava mutu virava viṭal puraḷa muṟum aravai ariciram ariya acciram araṉa caraṇamavai parava iru kirakam amar cirapuramatē (campantar, tiruccakkaramāṟṟu, 4,5; vaḻimoḻittiruvirākam, 7)]]

{1:47}__1+
{$} col aṭainta toṉ maṟaiyōṭu aṅkam kalaikaḷ ellām cel aṭainta celvar vāḻum cirapuram mēyavaṉē
God in Cirapuram where brahmins who possess the wealth of knowledge, as their sharp intelligence is capable of quickly grasping the ancient vētam of meaningful words, six ankams, and sixty-four arts!
pōy, pal aṭainta veṇtalaiyil pali koḷvatu aṉṟiyum
besides receiving alms in the skull which has teeth intact, going to houses.
vil aṭainta puruvam nallāḷ mēṉiyil vaittal eṉṉē
what is the reason for placing on your body a good lady whose eye-brows are curved like bows.
{1:47}__2+
{$} colla nīṇṭa perumaiyāḷar, tolkalai kaṟṟu vallār, cella nīṇṭa celvam malku cirapuram mēyavaṉē
God who is in cirāpuram with gladness, where great people of fame to describe which words are poor vehicles, are well-versed in the ancient arts!
kollai mullai nakaiyiṉāḷ ōr kūṟu(atu) aṉṟiyum
Besides having as a half a lady who has teeth like the buds of arabian jasmine (mullai)
pōy allal vāḻkkaippalikoṇṭu uṇṇum ātaravu eṉṉai
what is the reason for your liking the miserable life of receiving alms and eating it, by going to houses.
{1:47}__3+
{$} kār aṭaintacōlai cūḻntu kāmaram vaṇṭu icaippac, cīr aṭainta celvam ōṅku cirapuram mēyavaṉē
God who resides gladly in cirāpuram where wealth earned by reputed means is increasing and where the bees surround the gardens on which clouds rest, and hum like the melody of cīkāmaram.
[[kāmaram=cīkāmaram the first is dropped; a poetic licence which consists in the shortening of a word by one or more letters at the beginning.]]
nīr aṭainta caṭaiyiṉmēl ōr nikaḻmatiyaṉṟiyum
Besides adorning the caṭai on which there is water, with a shining crescent.
ūr pōy, aṭainta ēṟu(atu) ēṟi, uṇpali koḷvatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for receiving alms for eating, riding on a bull that came to you of its own accord, and going to many places?
{1:47}__4+
{$} kai aṭainta kaḷaikaḷākac ceṅkaḻunīr malarkaḷ, ceyaṭainta vayalkaḷ cūḻnta cirapuram mēyavaṉē
god who is cirapuram which is surrounded by fields to which were done all agricultural operations and made fit for cultivation, in which the flowers of purple indian water lilies grow as weeds on the sides!
kai aṭainta māṇiṉōṭu kār aravu aṉṟiyum
Besides holding a deer and a black cobra that reached your hand.
pōy, mey aṭainta vēṭkaiyōṭu melliyal vaittal eṉṉē
what is the reason for having a tender-natured lady with a very true and strong desire?
{1:47}__5+
{$} maram uritta tōl uṭutta mātavar tēvarōṭum, ciram eṭutta kaikaḷ kūppum cirapuram mēyavaṉē
god who stays gladly in cirapuram where the great sages who wear the bark peeled off from trees, along with tēvar join their hands in worship raising them on their heads.
puram eritta peṟṟiyōṭum pōrmatayāṉai taṉṉaik karam eṭuttut tōl uritta kāraṇam āvatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for flaying the skin of the rutting and fighting elephant lifting it with your hands, besides the nature of burning the cities?
{1:47}__6+
{$} eṇṇum mūṉṟu kaṉalum ōmpi eḻumaiyum viḻumiyarāyt, tiṇṇam mūṉṟu vēḷviyāḷar cirapuram mēyavaṉē
God who resides gladly in cirapuram where the brahmins who perform the three sacrifices with certainty and who are excellent persons in the seven successive births, having maintained the three sacred fires which are considered important!
kaṇṇu mūṉṟum uṭaiyatu aṉṟi
Besides having three eyes.
kaiyiṉil veṇ maḻuvum, paṇṇu mūṉṟu vīṇaiyōṭu pāmpuṭaṉ vaittatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for having along with the cobra, a vīṇai which has three kinds of melodies and a white hot iron, in your hand.
[[Lord Civaṉ has in his hand a vīṇai.]]
[[PP: vētattu oli koṇṭu vīṇai kēṭpār (appar, tiruveṇkāṭu, tiruttāṇtakam, 2);
em iṟai nalvīṇai vācikkumē (Do tāṉittiruviruttam (1) 7);
viṭamuṇṭa kaṇṭaṉ mika nallavīṇai taṭavi (campantar, Kōlaṟu patikam, 1);]]
[[One form of Dakṣiṇāmūrtti is called vīṇātaratakṣiṇāmūrtti which means Dakṣiṇāmūrtti who holds in his hands a vīṇai]]
{1:47}__7+
{$} iṟaipaṭāta meṉ mulaiyār māḷikai mēliruntu, ciṟai paṭāta pāṭal ōṅku cirapuram mēyavaṉē
god who gladly resides in cirapuram where the faultless music of the ladies of soft breasts which do not incline even a little, increases in pitch, staying in the mansions!
kuṟaipaṭāta vēṭkaiyōṭu kōlvaḷaiyāḷ orupāl, poṟaipaṭāta iṉpamōṭu puṇartaru (1) meymmai eṉṉē
what is secret truth in joining on one half with a lady wearing a group of bangles, without a desire that does not diminish, and excessive pleasure that cannot be borne easily?
[[Variant reading: (1) mēṉmaiyeṉṉē]]
{1:47}__8+
{$} niṉaivār tulaieṭutta col payilvār mētaku vīti tōṟum, cilaieṭutta tōḷiṉāṉē cirapuram mēyavaṉē
God who resides gladly in cirapuram and who carries a bow on the shoulder where in every eminent street there are scholars who practise in words which are worthy of being compared to other words, meditating on you!
malai eṭutta vāḷ arakkaṉ añca oru viralāl, nilaieṭutta koḷkaiyāṉē niṉ malaṉē
the immaculate god! God who proved your state of godhood by a single toe to make the cruel arakkaṉ who lifted the mountain to be frightened!
{1:47}__9+
{$} nāluvētam ōtalārkaḷ namtuṇai eṉṟu iṟaiñcac cēlumēyum kaḻaṉicūḻnta cirapuram mēyavaṉē
god who resides gladly in cirāpuram surrounded by fields in which carnatic carp fish roam about, to be worshipped with bowing and prostrating, by brahmins who are well-versed in all the four vētams thinking, He is our guide!
māliṉōṭu malariṉāṉum vantavarkāṇātu cālum añcap paṇṇi nīṇṭa tattuvam mēyatu eṉṉē
what is the ultimate reality you wished, when your rose high to make Māl and the one seated in the (lotus) flower, though they came very neary, not to be found out by them but making them very much afraid.
{1:47}__10+
{$} mattayāṉai uriyum pōrttu maṅkaiyoṭum uṭaṉē cittar vantu paṇiyum celvac cirapuram mēyavaṉē
god who gladly resides in wealthy cirāpuram, with a lady having covered himself with the skin of an elephant having must, where persons having mystic powers, come and worship!
puttarōṭu camaṇar coṟkaḷ puṟaṉurai eṉṟirukkum pattar vantu paṇiyavaitta pāṉmai (atu) eṉṉai (kolām)
what is the reason for the property of making the devotees who think that the talks the buddhists and jains is more slender, to come to cirāpuram and worship you?
{1:47}__11+
{$} aṅkam nīṇṭa maṟaikaḷ valla aṇikoḷcampantaṉ, teṅku nīṇṭa cōlaicūḻnta cirapuram mēyavaṉai urai
the songs of beautiful Campantaṉ who is well-versed in the long Vētam-s by the six aṅkams, who praised the god residing gladly in Cirapuram which is surrounded by tall cocoanut groves.
paṅkam nīṅkap pāṭavalla pattarkaḷ pār itaṉ mēl caṅkamōṭu taṉmaiyiṉāl nīṭi vāḻvar
those devotees who are able to sing the songs without any defects, will live a long life in this world by their good quality, in the company of good devotees.

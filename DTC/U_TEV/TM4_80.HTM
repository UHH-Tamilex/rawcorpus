*1
pāḷi _uṭik kamuku _ōṅki, palamāṭam neruṅki, _eṅkum
vāḷi _uṭip puṉal vantu _eṟi, vāḻ vayal-tillitaṉṉuḷ,
_āḷa _uṭik kaḻal ciṟṟampalattu _araṉ _āṭal kaṇṭāl
pīḷi _uṭik kaṇkaḷāl piṉṉip pēyttoṇṭar kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*2
poru viṭi _oṉṟu _uṭip puṇṇiyamūrtti, puli_ataḷaṉ,
_uru _uṭi _am malimaṅki maṇāḷaṉ, _ulakukku _ellām
tiru _uṭi _antaṇar vāḻkiṉṟa tillic ciṟṟampalavaṉ,
tiruvaṭiyik kaṇṭa kaṇkoṇṭu maṟṟu _iṉik kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*3
toṭutta malaroṭu tūpamum cāntum koṇṭu _eppoḻutum
_aṭuttu vaṇaṅkum _ayaṉoṭu mālukkum kāṇpu _ariyāṉ,
poṭik koṇṭu _aṇintu poṉ _ākiya tillic ciṟṟampalavaṉ,
_uṭutta tukil kaṇṭa kaṇkoṇṭa maṟṟu _iṉik kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*4
vicca poruḷ namakku _ākum _eṉṟu _eṇṇi namaccivāya
_accam _oḻintēṉ; _aṇi tilli _ampalattu _āṭukiṉṟa
piccaṉ, piṟappu_ili, pēr nanti, _untiyiṉmēl _acitta
kacciṉ _aḻaku kaṇṭāl piṉṉik kaṇkoṇṭu kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*5
ceyñ ñiṉṟa nīlam malarkiṉṟa tillic ciṟṟampalavaṉ,
miñ ñiṉṟa _oṇkaṇ malimakaḷ kaṇṭu makiḻntu niṟka
neyñ ñiṉṟu _eriyum viḷakku _otta nīlamaṇimiṭaṟṟāṉ,
kiñ ñiṉṟa _āṭal kaṇṭāl piṉṉik kaṇkoṇṭu kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*6
_ūṉatti nīkki _ulaku _aṟiya(v) _eṉṉi _āṭkoṇṭavaṉ,
tēṉ _ottu _eṉakku _iṉiyāṉ, tillic ciṟṟampalavaṉ, _em kōṉ,
vāṉattavar _uyya vaṉnañci _uṇṭa kaṇṭattu _ilaṅkum
_ēṉattu_eyiṟu kaṇṭāl piṉṉik kaṇkoṇṭu kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*7
terinta kaṇiyāl-tiri purammūṉṟum centīyil mūḻka
_eritta _iṟivaṉ, _imiyavarkōmāṉ, _iṇi_aṭikaḷ
taritta maṉattavar vāḻkiṉṟa tillic ciṟṟampalavaṉ,
ciritta mukam kaṇṭa kaṇkoṇṭu maṟṟu _iṉik kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*8
cuṟṟum _amarar, curapati, niṉ tiruppātam_allāl
paṟṟu _oṟṟa _ilōm _eṉṟu _aḻippap paraviyuḷ nañci _uṇṭāṉ,
ceṟṟu _aṅku _anaṅkaṉit tīviḻittāṉ, tilli _ampalavaṉ,
neṟṟiyil kaṇ kaṇṭa kaṇkoṇṭu maṟṟu _iṉik kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*9
cittattu _eḻunta ceḻuṅ kamalattu _aṉṉa cēvaṭikaḷ
vitta maṉattavar vāḻkiṉṟa tillic ciṟṟampalavaṉ,
muttum vayiramum māṇikkamtaṉṉuḷ viḷaṅkiya tū
mattamalar kaṇṭa kaṇkoṇṭu maṟṟu _iṉik kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

*10
tarukku mikuttut taṉ tōḷvali _uṉṉit taṭavariyi
varikkikaḷāl _eṭuttu _ārppa, malimakaḷkōṉ cirittu,
_arakkaṉ maṇi muṭipattum---_aṇi tilli _ampalavaṉ---
nerukki mititta viral kaṇṭa kaṇkoṇṭu kāṇpatu _eṉṉē?

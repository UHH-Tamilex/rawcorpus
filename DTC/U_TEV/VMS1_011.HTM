{1:11}__1+
{$} caṭaiār puṉal uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has on his caṭi water
orucarikōvaṉauṭaiyāṉ
wears a loin-cloth which slides down.
paṭaiār maḻuuṭaiyāṉ
has a weapon of a battle-axe
palapūtappaṭaiuṭaiyāṉ
possesses an army of many pūtams
maṭam māṉ viḻi umai mātu iṭam uṭaiyāṉ
has on his left half a lady by name Umi who has eyes like the tender deer.
eṉai uṭaiyāṉ
has me as his slave.
viṭai ārkoṭi uṭaiyāṉ
has a flag on which the form of a bull is drawn.
iṭam vīḻi(m) miḻalaiyē
his place is vīḻimiḻali
[[m within brackets is augmentation for rhythm; it applies to the following ten verses]]
{1:11}__2+
{$} īṟāy
being the end of all movable and immorable things that are absorbed into him at the end of the world.
mutal oṉṟāy
and being the sole thing from which all things that have been absorbed come into existence at the time of creation.
irupeṇ āṇ
and being the two as male and female.
kuṇam mūṉṟāy
the being the three Kuṇam, namely namely cattuvam, rajas and tamas.
māṟāmaṟaināṉkāy
being the four vētams that are not contradictory to each other.
[[māṟā may also mean never undergoing any change being the same always]]
varupūtam aintāy
being the five elements that are born out of the sensations.
āṟuārcuvai
being the six kinds of flavours
[[tittippu, kacappu, uvappu, kāḻppu, puḷippu, tuvarppu ]].
ēḻ ōcaiyōṭu
being the seven notes of the diatonic scale.
[[They are according to tamil: kural, tuttam, kaikkiḷai, uḻai, iḷiviḷari, tāram, caṭkam, riṣapam, kāntāram, matyamam, pañcamam, niṣātam, taivatam (according to carnatic music)]]
eṭṭuticai tāṉ āy
and being himself the eight directions.
vēṟāy uṭaṉ aṟāṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who is separate from them and at the same time pervading them.
vīḻi(m) miḻalaiyē
is Vīḻimiḻali
{1:11}__3+
{$} aṭiyīr
devotees!
nāḷ malariṭṭut toḻutu uyya vammiṉ
please come to save yourselves by scattering flowers that blossomed at day-break, to worship him.
um aṉpiṉoṭu em aṉpuceytu īcaṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple where Civaṉ dwells to bestow on him your love as well as ours
[[ceytu must be changed into ceyya]]
mummeṉṟu icaimural vaṇṭukaḷ keṇṭit ticai eṅkum vimmum poḻilcūḻ taṇ vayalvīḻ(m) miḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻali which has cool fields and is surrounded by gardens in all places where the bees which hum like the sound mum and work into the flowers.
[[mummeṉṟu is onomatopoeia]]
{1:11}__4+
{$} paṇṇum
being the art of music
patam ēḻum
and the distonic seven scales
pala ōcaittamiḻ avaiyum
and the tamiḻ language of valliṉam melliṉam and iṭiyiṉam sounds
[[valliṉam: the class of hard consonants; melliṉam the group of soft or nasal consonants; iṭiyiṉam: the group of medial consonants]]
uḷniṉṟatu ōrcuvaiyum
the sweetness of the music which is enjoyed by the mind when it is properly sung.
uṟutāḷattu oli palavum
the sound of the measures of time accompanying music.
maṇṇum puṉal (um) uyirum varukāṟṟum cuṭar mūṉṟum viṇṇum muḻutu āṉāṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who is completely the earth, water, living beings; blowing wind, the three lights [[sun, moon and fire]] and the space.
vīḻi(m)miḻalaiyē
is Vīḻimiḻali
{1:11}__5+
{$} āyātaṉa camayam pala aṟiyātaṉa
Civaṉ who could not be known by many religious which did not research by means of correct knowledge and personal experience to know the truth as it is.
neṟiyiṉ tāyāṉavaṉ
the source of the several religious which try to realise god.
uyirkaṭku muṉtalai āṉavaṉ
who is the chief of the living beings from time immemorial
maṟaimuttī āṉavaṉ
is the three fires mentioned in the vētam [[ākarvanīyam, Kārukappattiyam, and takkiṇākkiṉi]]
civaṉ emiṟai
who has the name of Civaṉ; our master
celvattiruvārūr mēyāṉ
who dwells in tiruvārūr of great wealth
avaṉ uṟaiyum(m)iṭam vīḻi(m)miḻalaiyē
his abode is vīḻimiḻali.
{1:11}__6+
{$} kallāl niḻaṟkīḻāy! iṭarkāvāy' eṉa
praying civan who is under the shade of a Variety of banyan tree, called Kallāl! protect us from suffering!
vāṉōrellām oru tērāy
when all the celestials transformed themselves into a chariot.
ayaṉmaṟaipūṭṭi niṉṟu uyppa
when Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ) harnessed the vētams as horses and drove it, standing in his seat.
(1) vallāy eri, kāṟṟu īrkku, arikōl vācukināṇ, kalvillāl eyil eytāṉ iṭam
the abode of one who shot an arrow with the mountin converted into a bow which had vācuki as its bow-string, Māl as arrow feathers fixed in the arrow being the wind, and fire which is quickly ignited.
vīḻi(m)miḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻali
[[Variant reading:(1) valvāy ]]
{1:11}__7+
{$} cirattāl mali karattāṉ
Civaṉ holds in his palm the skull of Piramaṉ which occupied it fully.
kariurittuāyatuōrpaṭattāṉ
has a cloth with which he covered himself and which was got from flaying an elephant.
purattār poṭipaṭa
when all the other acurar in the three cities were burnt and reduced to ashes/
tam aṭipaṇimūvarkaṭku ōvāvarattāṉ mika aḷittāṉ iṭam
the abode of Civaṉ who granted very much his grace without ceasing, to those three who bowed to his feet
[[without being misled by the guileful teachings of Māl to create hatred towards Civaṉ]]
vaḷarpuṉṉai muttu arumpi
the thriving mast-wood tree having put forth buds like pearls.
viraittātu poṉ maṇi īṉṟu aṇi vīḻi(m)miḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻali which is made beautiful by producing fruits like emeralds and putting forth fragrant pollen resembling gold in colour.
{1:11}__8+
{$} muṉniṟpavar illāmuraṇ arakkaṉ
the arakkaṉ, irāvanaṉ who had nobody opposing him in battle standing in front of him;
vaṭakayilai taṉṉaip piṭittu eṭuttāṉmuṭi, taṭantōḷ iṟa ūṉṟi
pressing his toe to crush the big shoulders and heads of the arakkaṉ who cught hold of the mountin, Kayili in the north, and lifted it.
piṉṉaippaṇintu ēttap peruvāḷ pēroṭum koṭutta, miṉṉiṉpoli caṭaiyāṉ iṭam vīḻi(m)miḻalaiyē
vīḻimiḻali is the abode of Civaṉ who has glittering caṭi like the lightning, and gave the name of Irāvaṇaṉ and a big sword when afterwards he prised Civaṉ making obsisance to him.
[[avvaḻi avaṉumpāṭa araṉ makiḻntu aḻaittukkoṇṭu muvvakai ulakum āḷa mūṉṟaraik kōṭiyāṇṭum; evvakaip paṭaiyum vellum cantiravāḷum īntāṉ, poyvakai nirutaṉpeṟṟu viṭaikoṇṭu pōkaluṟṟāṉ porupparaiyaṉ maṭappāvai iṭappākaṉ poruppiṉ kīḻkkiṭantu pōkātu, irakkamuṟakkataṟutalāl irāvaṇaṉ tāṉ eṉappeyarum iyampivāveṉṟu arakkaṉukku mukkōṭi vāṇāḷum aḷittup pō eṉṟu, uraippa araṉ aṭi vaṇaṅki āṅku eḻil cēr puṭpakattiṉ uvantu pōṉāṉ (rāmāyaṇam, uttarakāṇṭam, Vari yeṭuttapatalam, 75-76)]]
{1:11}__9+
{$} paṇṭu ēḻulaku uṇṭāṉ (um) avai kaṇṭāṉum muṉ aṟiyā oṇtī uru āṉāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple where Civaṉ who assumed the form of a bright fire, which could not be found out by Māl who swallowed long ago the seven worlds, and Piramaṉ who created them.
niṟai poykai vaṉtāmarai malarmēl maṭavaṉṉam naṭai payila veṇtāmarai centātu utir vīḻi(m)miḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻali where from the white lotus red coloured pollen drops down, when the young swans practise walking on the fertile lotus flowers in the natural tanks full of water.
{1:11}__10+
{$} macaṅkaṟ camaṇ, maṇṭaikkaiyar, kuṇṭak kuṇamilikaḷ icuṅkum piṟappu aṟuttāṉ iṭam
the abode of Civaṉ who cut off the birth they may lead to the company of the characterless bow buddhists who hold an eating bowl in their hands and camaṇar of perplexed minds.
iruntēṉ kaḷittu iraittup pacum poṟkiḷi kaḷi maññaikaḷ oḷikoṇṭu eḻupakalōṉ vicumpaip polivikkum poḻil vīḻi(m)miḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻali which has gardens which has gardens which make the sky in which the sun shines brightly, appear grand by its mirthful peacocks and beautiful green parrots and where the swarms of bees having drunk honey hum noisily.
{1:11}__11+
{$} vīḻi(m)miḻalai(m) mēviya vikirtaṉ taṉai
on the god who is different from the world and who dwells in vīḻimiḻali
viraicēr kāḻi(n) nakarkkalai ñāṉacampantaṉ tamiḻpattum yāḻiṉ(ṉ) icai vallār colak kēṭṭār avar ellām
all those who can sing to the accompaniment of yāḻ the tamiḻ verses composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who has knowledge of arts and is a native of Kāḻi of fragrance.
ūḻiṉ(1)maliviṉaipōyiṭa uyarvāṉ aṭaivārē
will reach the superior heaven as the deeds done in previous births are removed.

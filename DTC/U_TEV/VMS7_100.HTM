{7:100}__1+
{$} noṭittāṉ malai uttamaṉ
The supreme God who is possessed of all moral attributes who dwells in Noṭittāṉmalai
tāṉ eṉai muṉpaṭaittāṉ
himself caused me to be form[ed] in this world, first.
atu aṟintu taṉ poṉ aṭikkē nāṉ eṉ (1) pāṭal antō
knowing that hint of creating me, did I compose verses to praise his golden feet, alas! (though I did not do so)
nāyiṉēṉaip poruṭ paṭuttu
having esteemed me who is as mean as a dog, (unworthy of receiving his grace) and including me in the company of devotees.
vāṉai eṉai vantu etirkoḷḷa
the celestials to receive me appearing before me.
mattayāṉai aruḷpurintu (1)
granting me a frenzied elephant as a vehicle.
(2) ūṉ uyir vēṟu ceytāṉ
he elevated my body and life within it. How great was his grace!
[[noṭittāṉ malai: the mountain, Kayilāyam belonging to Civaṉ, the destroyer;
noṭittāṉ: the destroyer]]
[[Variant reading: (1)pāṭavantō (2) purintūṉuyir]]
{7:100}__2+
{$} ūṉai uyir veruṭṭi oḷḷiyāṉai niṉaittiruntēṉ
I was always meditating upon Civaṉ who is embodiment of pure knowledge having kept under control my organs of senses and intellectual powers.
noṭittāṉ malai uttamaṉ
(even for that small thing) [[see 1st verse]]
vāṉai matitta amarar valañceytu eṉai ēṟa vaikka āṉai aruḷ purintāṉ
granted me an elephant for the immortals who consider heaven as great, to go from left to right, and to place me on that.
āṉai uritta pakai aṭiyēṉoṭu mīḷak kolō
whether that act was due to his grace bestowed on that animal on account of me to remove the long standing enmity caused by flaying an elephant in the past or whether it is due to his benign grace bestowed on me (I do not know)
{7:100}__3+
{$} neñcamē
my mind!
mantiram oṉṟu aṟiyēṉ
I do not know even a single mantiram of the Vētam-s.
maṉaivāḻkkai makiḻntu
rejoicing in the family life and being confounded.
aṭiyēṉ
I his slave.
cuntara vēṭaṅkaḷāl turicē ceyyum toṇṭaṉ
myself, a devotee who committed crimes by putting on beautiful forms, without desiring the services of devotees.
eṉai antaram māl vicumpil aḻaku āṉai aruḷpurintum taramō
is it a fitting action considering my lowness, for god to grant me a beautiful elephant that can travel in the sky of great space?
noṭittāṉ malaiuttamaṉ
see 1st verse.
{7:100}__4+
{$} vāḻvai ukanta neñcē
my mind which esteemed wordly life!
maṭavār taṅkaḷ valviṉaippaṭṭu āḻam mukanta eṉṉai atumāṟṟi
changing me from the state of being sunk deep being caught in the irresistible acts of women.
amarar ellām cūḻa aruḷpurintu
granting his grace to make all the immortals to surround me.
toṇṭaṉēṉ param allatu oru vēḻam aruḷpurintāṉ
granted me an elephant which I his devotee, am not fit to receive.
noṭittāṉ malai uttamaṉ
see 1st verse.

{7:100}__10+
{$} ūḻitoṟu ūḻimuṟṟum uyar poṉ noṭittāṉ malaiyai
upon the mountain which grows taller in every aeon and which is the seat of Civaṉ, the destroyer, and golden in colour.
ēḻ icaiyiṉ tamiḻāl icaintu ēttiya pattiṉaiyum
all the ten songs which praised being united in the Lord, in sweet tamiḻ which has seven musical notes, found in musical works.
cūḻ icaiyiṉ karumpiṉ cuvai nāval ūraṉ coṉṉa
composed by me, who is a native a nāvalur, which songs have fame that surrounds them, and are as sweet as the juice of the sugar-cane.
āḻikaṭal araiyā
the Lord of the deep ocean!
añcaiyapparkku aṟivippatē
Let you inform the father in Tiru Añcaikkaḷam.
[[aṟivippatu: verb in the optative mood]]

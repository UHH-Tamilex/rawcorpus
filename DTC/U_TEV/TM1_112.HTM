• patikam: 1:112
  • talam: tiruc civapuram
  • paṇ: viyāḻak kuṟiñci
  • VMS: VOL.8, P.44

*1
_iṉkural_ici keḻum yāḻ muralat
taṉ karam maruviya caturaṉ nakar---
poṉ kari poru paḻaṅkāviriyiṉ
teṉkari maruviya civapuramē.

*2
_aṉṟu _aṭal kālaṉip pālaṉukku_āyp
poṉṟiṭa _uticeyta puṉitaṉ nakar---
veṉṟi koḷ _eyiṟṟu veṇpaṉṟi muṉnāḷ
ceṉṟu _aṭi vīḻtaru civapuramē.

*3
malimakaḷ maṟukiṭa, matakariyik
koli malka _uriceyta kuḻakaṉ nakar---
_ali malkum _ariciliṉ_ataṉ _ayalē
cili malku matil _aṇi civapuramē.

*4
maṇ, puṉal, _aṉaloṭu, mārutamum,
viṇ, puṉi maruviya vikirtaṉ nakar---
paṇ puṉi kuralvaḻi vaṇṭu keṇṭic
ceṇpakam _alar poḻil civapuramē.

*5
vīṟu naṉku _uṭiyavaḷ mēṉi pākam
kūṟu naṉku _uṭiyavaṉ kuḷir nakartāṉ---
nāṟu naṉ kura viri vaṇṭu keṇṭit
tēṟal _uṇṭu _eḻutaru civapuramē.

*6
māṟu _etirvaru tiripuram _erittu,
nīṟu_atu _ākkiya nimalaṉ nakar---
nāṟu _uṭi naṭupavar _uḻavaroṭum
cēṟu _uṭi vayal _aṇi civapuramē.

*7
_āvil_aintu _amarntavaṉ _ariviyoṭu
mēvi naṉku _iruntatu _or viyalnakartāṉ---
pūvil vaṇṭu _amartaru poyki _aṉṉac-
cēval taṉ peṭi pulku civapuramē.

*8
_eḻil mali _eṭutta val_irāvaṇaṉtaṉ
muḻuvali _aṭakkiya mutalvaṉ nakar---
viḻaviṉil _eṭutta veṇkoṭi miṭintu,
ceḻu mukil _aṭukkum vaṇ civapuramē.

*9
caṅku _aḷaviya kiyaṉ, caturmukaṉum,
_aṅku _aḷavu _aṟivu _ariyavaṉ nakartāṉ---
kaṅkulum paṟavikaḷ kamukutoṟum
ceṅkaṉi nukartaru civapuramē.

*10
maṇṭiyiṉ, kuṇṭiki, mācu tarum,
miṇṭari vilakkiya vimalaṉ nakar---
paṇṭu _amartaru paḻaṅkāviriyiṉ
teṇtiri porutu _eḻu civapuramē.

*11
civaṉ _uṟitaru civapuranakarik
kavuṇiyarkulapati---kāḻiyarkōṉ---
tavam malku tamiḻ_ivi colla vallār
navamoṭu civakati naṇṇuvarē.

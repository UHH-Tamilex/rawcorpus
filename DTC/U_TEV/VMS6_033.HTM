porumkai matakari urivaip pōrvaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who covers himself with the skin of a frenzied elephant having a trunk with which it can fight;
[[PP: arumpaṇaittaṭakkai aparakāttiram vāyvāl eyiṟu aintiṉum kolva
(Cīvaka Cintāmaṇi, 2154);]]
[[kari is used not in its derivate sense but simply in the meaning of an elephant as it is preceded by the words kai]]
pūvaṇamum valañcuḻiyum poruntiṉāṉai
who dwells in Pūvaṇam and Valañcuḻi.
karumpu taru kaṭṭiyai
who is the jaggery that is got from sugar-cane
iṉṉamirtai
who is the sweet nectar.
tēṉai
who is the sweet honey;
[[PP: aṇiyum amiḻtum eṉ āviyum āyavaṉ
(Kōviyar, 5);]]
[[PP: aṇi eṉṟār aḻaku ceytaliṉ; amiḻtu eṉṟār, kaḻi peruñcuvaiyōṭu uṟutipayattal uṭaimaiyāṉ; āvi eṉṟār kātalikkappaṭum poruḷkaḷ ellāvaṟṟiṉum ciṟantamaiyāṉ īṟu il iṉpam payakkum iṟaivaṉōṭu cārtta aṇiyum amiḻtum āviyum iṟappa iḻintaṉavē āyiṉum ''poruḷatu puraivē puṇarppōṉ kuṟippiṉ maruḷ aṟa varū um marapiṟṟu ākum'' [source unknown] eṉpataṉāṉ īṇṭuc colluvāṉatu karuttu vakaiyāṉum, ulakattup poruḷkaḷuḷ avaṟṟiṉūṅkumikkaṉa iṉmaiyāṉum, uyarntaṉavāy uvamai āyiṉa
(Do. commentary of Pērāciriyar)]]
kāṇpu ariya ceḻuñcuṭarai
the effulgent divine light that cannot be seen with the physical eyes.
kaṉakakkuṉṟai
a hill of gold.
iruṅkaṉaka matil ārūr mūlaṭṭāṉattu eḻuntaruḷiyiruntāṉai
who condescended to dwell in ārūr mūlaṭṭāṉam which has a big fortified wall of gold.
imaiyōr ēttum aruntavaṉai
one who performed severe penance and who is prised by the celestials who do not wink;
[[PP_(aruntavaṉ):
tāḻcaṭaip polinta aruntavat tōṟkē
(Puṟanāṉūṟu, 1-13)]]
araneṟiyil (1) appaṉ taṉṉai
and the father in Araneṟi.
aṭiyēṉ aruviṉainōy aṟuttavāṟu (naṉṟu)
it is good that I, his humble servant, cut at the root of acts that cannot be removed and are like diseases.
[[Variant reading: (1) aiyaṉ taṉṉai]]

Civaṉ who is Kaṟpakam and the two lights, sun and moon.
kāḷatti kayilāya malai uḷāṉai
who is in Kāḷatti and Kayilāyam mountins.
vil payilum mataṉ aḻiya viḻittāṉ taṉṉai

our Lord who dwells in Ārūr Mūlaṭṭāṉam which has prospering gardens.
poruntār cintai aṟputaṉai
who is nothingness in the minds of those who have no love towards him.

pāti oru peṇ muṭimēṟ kaṅkaiyāṉai
Having a lady on one half of the body and another lady, Kanki, on the head.
1. pācūrum paraṅkuṉṟum mēyāṉ taṉṉai
who dwells with desire in pācūr and paraṅkuṉṟu.
vētiyaṉai
who is the brahmin among gods (who chants the vetams is another meaning)
taṉ aṭiyārkku eḷiyāṉai
who is easily accessible to his devotees.
meyñ ñāṉa viḷakkāṉai
who is the light of true knowledge
viraiyē nāṟum pōtu iyalum poḻil ārūr mūlaṭṭāṉam puṟṟu iṭaṅ koṇṭiruntāṉai
who has as his abode the anthill in mūlaṭṭāṉam in ārūr which has gardens in which buds when they blossom spread only good fragrance.
pōṟṟuvārkaḷ ātiyaṉai
the master of those who prise him.
aṟaneṟiyil appaṉ taṉṉai; aṭiyēṉ aruviṉai nōy aṟuttavāṟē
see 1st verse.

cuṭarppavaḷattirumēṉi veṇṇīṟṟāṉai
Civaṉ who smears white sacred ash on his sacred person which is like the brilliant red coral.
1. cōtiliṅkat tūṅkāṉai māṭattāṉai
who is in tūṅkāṉi māṭam in peṇṇākaṭam which has a self-brilliant liṅkam.
[[tūṅkāṉai māṭam
the wall of sanctum sanctoram is apidal in shape, like the backside of the elephant.]]
viṭakku iṭu kāṭu iṭamāka uṭaiyāṉ taṉṉai
who has as his place where the burial corpses are left without burning or burying and where there is flesh.
mikka araṇam eriyūṭṭa vallāṉ taṉṉai
who is capable of seting fire to the superior forts (mikkaraṇam = mikka araṇam)
maṭal kulavu poḻil ārūr mūlaṭṭāṉam maṉṉiya em perumāṉai
our god who dwelt permanently in mūlaṭṭāṉam in ārūr which has gardens in which the petals are shining.
matiyār vēḷvi aṭarttavaṉai
who destroyed the sacrifice performed by tākkaṉ with the help of tevar who were afrid of takkan's power
[[mitiyār = takkaṉ is spoken in the plural is denigrate him;
kāmarai veṉṟa kaṉṇōṉ (Kōviyār, 164);
kāmar eṉṉum rakaravīṟu iḻiviṉ kaṇ vantatu (Do. perāciriyar)]]

evvuyirkkum tāyavaṉai
Civaṉ who is like the mother to all living beings;

taku tillai naṭam payilum taṉ oppu illāt talaivaṉ taṉṉai

the strong youth who become superior when he consumed the poison of the ocean of rolling waves at the request of māyavaṉ, Piramaṉ who is seated in a (lotus) flower and the celestials

ellām muṉṉē āyavaṉai

[[Note: this shrine is at present known as Pirāṉmali]]

{1:14}__1+
{$} vāṉil polivu eytum maḻaimēkam kiḻittu ōṭikkūṉaṟpiṟai cērum kuḷir cāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭunkuṉṟam in whose cool slopes the curved crescent having torn the cloud which is about to rin and attins splendour in the sky, runs and joins.
(1) āṉil poli aintum amarntu āṭi
bathing with desire in the five renowned products of the cow.
ulaku ētta
to be prised by the people of this world.
tēṉiṉ poli poḻiyāḷoṭu mēyāṉ tirunakarē
is the wealthy city of Civaṉ who dwells with desire with a lady whose words are celebrated to be as sweet as the honey.
[[Variant reading: (1) āṉiṟ polivaintum]]
{1:14}__2+
{$} mayil pulku taṇpeṭaiyōṭu uṭaṉ āṭum vaḷarcāralkuyil iṉṉicai pāṭum kuḷircōlaik koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭuṅkuṉṟam which has cool gardens in which the indian cuckoo sings in the slopes where many trees grow and where the male peacock that embraces its cool female, plays together.
ayilvēlmali neṭuveñcuṭar aṉal ēntiniṉṟu āṭi
dancing in the standing posture holding a tall hot fire of brilliance and holding a sharp trident.
eyil muṉpaṭa eytāṉ (avaṉ) (1) mēya(v) eḻil nakarē
is the beautiful city of Civaṉ who long ago shot an arrow to destroy the forts, and who dwells there with desire.
[[Variant reading: (1) mēyaeḻil]]
{1:14}__3+
{$} miḷirum maṇi paimpoṉṉoṭu viraimāmalar untik kuḷirum puṉal pāyum cāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭuṅkuṉṟam in whose cool slopes cool water which flows pushing the gleaming gems, pure gold, and big fragrant flowers.
kiḷar kaṅkaiyoṭu iḷaveṇmati keḻuvum caṭaitaṉmēl vaḷarkoṉṟaiyum matamattamum vaittāṉ vaḷanakarē
is the wealthy city of Civaṉ who placed on his caṭi the young white crescent and rising Kaṅki to be united, thriving koṉṟi flowers and datura flowers.
{1:14}__4+
{$} parumā matakariyōṭu ari (1) iḻiyum viricāral kurumāmaṇi poṉṉōṭu iḻi aruvik koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭuṅkuṉṟam which has streams which descend with gold and bright gems in the extensive slopes where the lions descend along with the big elephants which have must.
porumā eyil varaivil taru kaṇaiyiṉ poṭiceyta perumāṉ(avaṉ) umaiyāḷoṭum mēvum perunakarē
is the big city where Civaṉ who reduced to dust with a single arrow discharged from the bow of a mountin the big forts which can give battle.
[[Variant reading: (1) iriyum]]
{1:14}__5+
{$} mēkattu iṭikural vantu eḻaveruvi(v) varai iḻiyum kūkaikkulam ōṭittiri cāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭuṅkuṉṟam in whose slopes the flocks of rock horned-owls run and wander about, when they descend down the mountin being frightened when the sound of the thunder was produced in the clouds.
nākattoṭum iḷaveṇpiṟai cūḻ(n) nalamaṅkai pākattavaṉ imaiyōr toḻa mēvum paḻanakarē
is the ancient city where Civaṉ who has a beautiful lady of distinction as a half, having worn on his head a young white crescent along with a cobra and dwells to be worshipped by the celestials who do not wink.
{1:14}__6+
{$} kaimmāmata kariyiṉ iṉam iṭiyiṉ kural atira
when the sound of the thunder reverberates the herd of frenzied and big elephants with long trunks.
koymmāmalarc cōlai puka maṇṭum koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭunkuṉṟam where they move swiftly to get into the gardens of big flowers which can be plucked.
ammāṉ eṉa uḷkit toḻuvārkaṭku aruḷ ceyyum pemmāṉ(avaṉ) imaiyōr toḻa mēvum perunakarē
is the big city where the god with greatness grants to those who meditate on him as their father, the god who dwells to be worshipped by the celestials who do not wink.
{1:14}__7+
{$} maravattoṭu maṇamātavi mauval(l) (atu) viṇṭa kuravattoṭu viravum poḻil cūḻtaṇ koṭuṅkuṉṟam
cool Koṭuṅkuṉṟam which is surrounded by gardens where common cadamba trees, fragrant common delight of the woods, jasmine and blossomed bottle-flowers are mingled.
aravattoṭum iḷaveṇpiṟai viravum malarkkoṉṟai niravac caṭai muṭimēl uṭaṉ vaittāṉ neṭunakarē
is the long city of Civaṉ who placed on his caṭi coiled into a crown in addition to a cobra, young white crescent and mingling koṉṟi flowers to fill up the space.
{1:14}__8+
{$} muṭṭā mutukariyiṉ iṉam mutuvēykaḷai muṉintu
the hord of old elephants which had no hindrance having disliked the old bamboos.
kuṭṭāccuṉai(avai) maṇṭi niṉṟu āṭum koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭuṅkuṉṟam where they bathe for a long time moving swiftly in the deep mountin springs.
[[kuṭṭaccuṉai has become kuṭṭāccuṉai to be in keeping with rhyme this is a poetic licence consisting in the lengthening of a short vowel into a long one]]
ōṭṭā (1) arakkaṉtaṉ muṭi orapaḵtu(avai) uṭaṉē piṭṭāṉ (avaṉ) umaiyāḷoṭum mēvum perunakarē
is the big city of Civaṉ who broke to pieces simultaneously the ten heads of the arakkaṉ who was not friendly with anybody, dwells with desire with Umayāḷ.
[[Variant reading: (1) arakkaṉṉai muṭi]]
{1:14}__9+
{$} aṟaiyum arikural ōcaiyai añci aṭumyāṉai kuṟaiyum maṉam(m) āki(m) muḻai vaikum koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭuṅkuṉṟam where in the caves the elephants which are capable of killing, having lost courage by being afrid of the roaring noise of the lions capable of killing their preys by striking them with the forelegs, stay.
maṟaiyum(m) (avai) uṭaiyāṉ eṉa neṭiyāṉ eṉa ivarkaḷ iṟaiyum(m) aṟivu oṇṇātavaṉ (1) mēya(v) eḻil nakarē
is the beautiful city where both Piramaṉ who is well-versed in the Vētam-s and the tall Māl were not capable of knowing even a little of Civaṉ who dwells there with desire.
[[Variant reading: (1) mēya eḻilnakarē]]
{1:14}__10+
{$} mattakkaḷiṟu āḷi(v) vara añci(m) (1) malaitaṉṉaik kuttip perumuḻaitaṉṉiṭai vaikum koṭuṅkuṉṟam
Koṭuṅkuṉṟam where in the caves the frenzied elephants being afrid of the lions stay, piercing the mountin with their tusks.
puttaroṭu pollā maṉaccamaṇar puṟaṅkūṟa
camanar with evil minds to spread slander along with the buddhists.
pattarkku aruḷ ceytāṉ(avaṉ) mēya paḻanakarē
is the ancient city of Civaṉ who granted his grace to his devoted votaries and dwells with desire.
[[Variant reading: (1) malaitaṉaik]]
{1:14}__11+
{$} kūṉalpiṟai caṭaimēl mika uṭaiyāṉ koṭuṅkuṉṟai
on Koṭuṅkuṉṟam of Civaṉ who has a curved crescent waxing on his caṭi.
kāṉal kaḻumalamānakart talaivaṉ(ṉ) nalakavuṇi ñāṉattu uyar campantaṉa nalam koḷ tamiḻ vallār
those who are capable of reciting the excellent Tamiḻ verses of Campantaṉ who is great by his spiritual knowledge, who was born in Kauṇtiṉiya Kōttiram and who is the chief of the big city of Kaḻumalam which has sea-shore gardens.
ūṉattoṭu tuyartīrntu ulaku ēttum(m) eḻilōrē
are people who are prised for their imposing appearance being freed from faults and sufferings.

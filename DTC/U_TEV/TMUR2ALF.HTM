The stalam-s ordered according to talamuṟi

This list follows the order given in Tiruppatikkōvi for the 274 traditional sites and the 10 nāṭu to which they are assigned. In the talamuṟi editions, the hymns are given site by site following this order for the 274 sites. After that come 48 potup patikam-s, that do not belong specifically to any site.

Inside this list, two additional sites have been included; these are given in the PIFI edition as piṟcērkki: they are KT128 (viṭivāy) and TO33 (kiḷiyaṉṉavūr).

  1. Cōḻa Nāṭu
    • KV: Kāvēri Vaṭa Kari (63 talam)
    • KT: Kāvēri Teṉ Kari ([127+1] talam)
  2. IN: Īḻa Nāṭu (2 talam)
  3. PA: Pāṇṭi Nāṭu (14 talam)
  4. MA: Mali Nāṭu (1 talam)
  5. KO: Koṅku Nāṭu (7 talam)
  6. NA: Naṭu Nāṭu (22 talam)
  7. TO: Toṇṭi Nāṭu ([32+1] talam)
  8. TU: Tuḷuva Nāṭu (1 talam)
  9. VA: Vaṭa Nāṭu (5 talam)

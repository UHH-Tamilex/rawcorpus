{2:62}__1+
{$} kāyac celvik kāmaṟ kāyntu kaṅkaiyaip pāyap paṭar puṉcaṭaiyiṟ patitta paramēṭṭi
Civaṉ in the most exalted place fixed in his ruddy and spreading caṭi Kanki which came rushing down, and destroyed Kāmaṉ who had a beautiful body.
māyaccūr aṉṟu aṟutta maintaṉ tātai taṉ mīyaccūrait toḻutu viṉaiyai vīṭṭumē
(People of this world!) destroy all your Karmams by worshipping Miyaccūr of the father of the one who cut the cūr of treacherous nature in the past.
[[Kāmaṉ is compared to people of beautiful form. Campantar chose this rhyme to fix the name Mīyaccur in the yetuki etukai]]
{2:62}__2+
{$} pū ār caṭaiyiṉ muṭimēl puṉalar aṉal koḷvar
Civaṉ bears water on his caṭi coiled into a crown, which is full of flowers, and holds fire.
nā ār maṟaiyar
chants vētams with his tongue.
piṟaiyar
wears a crescent.
(1) naṟa veṇtalai ēnti
holds a white skull filled with intoxicating liquor.
ē ār malaiyē cilaiyākkaḻi ampu eri vāṅki
converting the mountin into a bow for fixing arrows, drawing from it the excessive fire as an arrow;
[[PP: eri kāṟṟu īrkku ari kōl vācuki nāṇ kal villāl eyil eytāṉ
(Campantar, Tiruviḻimiḻali (1) 6)]]
mēvār puram mūṉṟu erittār mīyac cūrār
Civaṉ in Mīyaccūr burnt the three cities of the enemies.
[[Variant reading: (1) naku]]
{2:62}__3+
{$} poṉ ēr koṉṟai mālai puraḷum akalattāṉ
Civaṉ wears a garland of golden koṉṟi, rolling over his chest;
miṉnēr caṭaikaḷ uṭaiyāṉ mīyac cūrāṉai
the god in Mīyaccūr who has many caṭis which are shining like lightning.
taṉ nēr piṟar illāṉaittalaiyāl vaṇaṅkuvār
those who bow their heads to Civaṉ who has no equal.
annēr imaiyōr ulakam (1) eytal aritu aṉṟē
it is not difficult for them to reach that world of the celestials which is fit for them.
[[Variant reading: (1) eytaṟku]]
{2:62}__4+
{$} vēkam matam nalyāṉai veruva uri pōrttu
covering with a skin to frighten the elephant of must, speed and beauty.
pākam umaiyōṭu āka
being a half with Umi.
paṭitam pala pāṭa
to sing songs of prise.
nākam arai mēl acaittu naṭam āṭiya nampaṉ
Civaṉ who danced having fastened a cobra on his waist.
mēkam uriñcum poḻil cūḻ mīyac cūrāṉ
is the god in Mīyaccūr surrounded by gardens aginst which clouds rub when moving.
{2:62}__5+
{$} viṭai ār koṭiyār
Civaṉ has a flag on which the forms of a bull is drawn.
caṭaimēl viḷaṅkum piṟai vēṭam
has the appearance of a crescent shining on the caṭi.
paṭai ār pūtam cūḻap pāṭal āṭalār
sings and dances surrounded by the army of pūtam.
peṭai ār vari vaṇṭu aṇaiyum piṇaicēr koṉṟaiyār
wears a garland of koṉṟi which the male bee with lines reaches with its female.
viṭai ār naṭai oṉṟu uṭaiyār mīyac cūrārē
Civaṉ in Miyāccūr has the git like that of a bull;
[[ēṟu may also mean git like that of a lion.]]
{2:62}__6+
{$} kuḷirum caṭai koḷ muṭimēl kōlam ār koṉṟai oḷirum piṟai oṉṟu uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has a shining crescent and beautiful koṉṟi on his cool caṭi which is coiled into a crown.
oruvaṉ
the pearless one.
kai kōṭi naḷirum maṇicūṭi mālai naṭṭam navil nampaṉ
Civaṉ who practises dance bending his hand in the cool evening when there is light everywhere.
miḷirum aravam uṭaiyāṉ mīyac cūrāṉē
the Lord in Mīyaccūr has a shining cobra.

{$} miṭaṟu nīla (1) vaṭivar
Civaṉ has a blue neck.
neṭiyar
is tall.
nikar illār
has no one else to be compared to him.
kōla vaṭivu tamatu ām koḷkai aṟivu (2) oṇṇār
no one can understand the idea of his assuming many beautiful forms.
kālar kaḻalar
wears kaḻal on his leg.
kariyiṉ uriyar
has the skin of an elephant.
maḻuvāḷar
has a battle-axe.
mēlar matiyar
has a crescent on his head.
vitiyar
creates everything.
mīyc cūrār

{2:62}__8+
{$} puliyiṉ uritōl āṭai
Civaṉ wears as a dress the skin flayed from a tiger.
pūcum poṭi nīṟṟar
smears himself with a fine powder of holy ash.
oli koḷ puṉal (1) ōr caṭaimēl karantār
concealed on a single caṭi the water which makes a big sound.
umai añca
as Umi was frightened
valiya tiraḷ tōḷ vaṉkaṇ arakkar kōṉ taṉṉai meliya varaikkīḻ aṭarttār mīyac cūrār
the Lord in Mīyaccūr crushed under the mountin the king of the arakkar, who was cruel and has round shoulders, to languish.
[[Variant reading: (1) vārcaṭai]]
{2:62}__9+
{$} kātil miḷirum kuḻaiyar
Civaṉ wears a shining mens` ear-ring in his (right) ear.
kariya kaṇṭattār
has a black neck.
pōtil avaṉum mālum toḻap poṅku 1. eri āṉār
assumed the form of fire which shot up, to be worshipped by Piramaṉ in the (lotus) flower, and Māl
kōti vari vaṇṭu aṟai pūm poykaip puṉal mūḻkimēti paṭiyum vayal cūḻ mīyac cūrār
the Lord in Mīyaccūr surrounded by fields where buffaloes rest plunging into the water of the natural tank where in the flowers the bees with lines hum loudly pecking into them for honey.
[[Variant reading: (1) oḷi]]
{2:62}__10+
{$} kaṇṭār nāṇum paṭiyār
camanar who have bodies that make on lookers to feel shame.
kaliṅkal (1) uṭai paṭṭai koṇṭār
and buddhists who have a robe soaked in the bark of marutam, as their dress.
collaik kuṟukār uyarnta koḷkaiyār
people with superior doctrines will not approach the words of those two people.
peṇ tāṉ pākam uṭaiyār
has a lady as one half.
periyavarai villā viṇṭār puram mūṉṟu erittār mīyac cūrārē
the Lord in Mīyaccūr burnt the three cities of the enemies with the big mountin as the bow
[[Variant reading: (1) muṭaippaṭṭaik, uṭai maṭṭai]]
{2:62}__11+
{$} vēṭam uṭaiya perumāṉ uṟaiyum
where the Lord of many forms dwells.
mīyac cūr nāṭum pukaḻ ār pukali ñāṉacampantaṉ pāṭal āya tamiḻ īraintum moḻintu uḷki
meditating on Civaṉ and reciting all these ten tamiḻ verses composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who is native of pukali and who desires mīyaccūr which has great fame.
āṭum aṭiyār akal vāṉ ulakam aṭaivār
devotees who dance out of joy will reach the extensive heaven after life in this world.

[[This shrine is now known as Marutantanallūr]]

{3:21}__1+
{$} naṉavilum kaṉavilum nāḷum taṉ oḷi niṉaivilum eṉakku vantu eytum niṉ malaṉ
the immaculate Civaṉ who is the cause for his light of spiritual wisdom to appear in my thoughts daily when I am awake and asleep.
kaṉai kaṭal vaiyakam toḻukarukkuṭi aṉal eri āṭum em aṭikaḷ kāṇmiṉē
you know that he is the god who dances in the burning fire and who is in Karukkuṭi which is worshipped by the people of this world girt by the roaring ocean.
{3:21}__2+
{$} vētiyaṉ
Civaṉ chants the Vētam-s
[[He revealed the Vētam-s.]]
viṭai uṭai vimalaṉ
the spotless god who has a bull.
oṉṉalar mūtu eyil eri eḻamuṉinta mukkaṇaṉ
the three-eyed god who by his anger set fire to the old forts of the enemies.
kātu iyal kuḻaiyiṉaṉ karukkuṭi amar ātiyai aṭitoḻa allal illaiyē
there will be no sufferings if one worships the feet of Civaṉ being the first cause who dwells a Karukkuṭi and who has a men's ear-ring in his ear.
{3:21}__3+
{$} mañcu uṟu poḻil vaḷam mali karukkuṭi nañcu uṟu tirumiṭaṟu uṭaiya nātaṉār
the master who has a neck in which there is poison and who is in Karukkuti where fertility is abundant on account of gardens on which clouds stay.
am curumpu ār kuḻal arivai añcavē veñcuram taṉil viḷaiyāṭal eṉ (kol, ō)
what is the reason for playing in the hot forest to make the lady with tresses of hair on which bees have settled?
[[curam here means the worlds destroyed by Civaṉ and burnt reducing them to ashes, which are very hot.]]
{3:21}__4+
{$} ūṉ uṭaip piṟaviyai aṟukka uṉṉuvīr
People who think of completely rooting out births when you have to be born again.
kāṉiṭai āṭalāṉ payil karukkuṭik kōṉ uyar kōyilai vaṇaṅki
paying obeisance to the eminent temple of the King in Karukkuti and where Civaṉ who dances in the cremation ground, is staying always.
vaikalum vāṉavar toḻukaḻal vāḻtti vāḻmiṉ
Live long by praising the feet which is worshipped by the celestials, daily.
{3:21}__5+
{$} caṭaiyiṭaikkaṅkai cūṭuvar
Civaṉ wears on his catai Kaṅkai
naṅkaiyaik kūṭuvar
is united with a lady of distinction.
ulakiṭai aiyam koṇṭu oli pāṭuvar icai
will sing music with sweet sound receiving alms in the world.
paṟaikoṭṭa naṭṭiruḷ āṭuvar
will dance in the midnight when drums are beaten
[[naṭṭiruḷ: naṭu + iruḷ;
this is like naṭṭāṟu which means the middle of the river.]]
karukkuṭi aṇṇal vaṇṇamē
these are the nature of the chief in Karukkuṭi.
{3:21}__6+
{$} iṉpu uṭaiyār icai vīṇai
Civaṉ feels pleasure in playing upon viṇai to enjoy its music.
pūṇ arā eṉpu uṭaiyār
has as ornaments cobras and bones
eḻil mēṉimēl eri muṉpu uṭaiyār
has fire on his beautiful body since a long time.
mutal ēttum aṉparukku aṉpu uṭaiyār karukkuṭi em aṇṇal
our god, Civaṉ, in Karukkuṭi reciprocates love towards those who love him and praise him as the cause of all things.
{3:21}__7+
{$} kālamum ñāyiṟum tīyum āyavar
Civaṉ is time, sun and fire.
kōlamum muṭi aravu aṇinta koḷkaiyar
has the nature of adorning his head with a cobra to add beauty to it.
cīlamum uṭaiyavar
possesses also the great quality of easy accessibility.
(1) tirukkarukkuṭiccālavum iṉitu avaruṭaiya taṉmaiyē
the nature of Civaṉ who is in Karukkuṭi is very much pleasing
[[Variant reading: (1) karukkuṭicālavum]]
{3:21}__8+
{$} eṟi kaṭal puṭai taḻuvu ilaṅkai maṉṉaṉai muṟipaṭa varaiyiṭai aṭartta mūrttiyār
Civaṉ who crushed the King of ilaṅkai surrounded by ocean which dashes against the shore to make his body break under the mountain.
kaṟai paṭu poḻil matitavaḻ karukkuṭi aṟivoṭu toḻumavar āḷvar naṉmaiyē
those who worship Karukkuṭi in whose dark gardens the moon seems to crawl, with knowledge of him will possess all good things.
{3:21}__9+
{$} pūmaṉum ticai mukaṉ tāṉum, poṟpu amar vāmaṉaṉ (um) aṟikilā vaṇṇam ōṅku eri ām eṉa uyarntavaṉ
Civaṉ who grew high as a rising fire so that Piramaṉ who is seated in a flower and has four faces, and the dwarf who has beauty, had not the capability to know him.
[[poṟpu amar vāmaṉaṉ the underlined word is used ironically;]]
[[PP: payantavarkaḷum ikaḻ kuṟaḷaṉ
(Kamparāmāyaṇam, Vēḷvippaṭalam, 35)]]
aṇi karukkuṭi nām maṉaṉiṉil varaniṉaital naṉmaiyē
it is certainly good for us to remember the beautiful Karukkuṭi to stay in our minds.
{3:21}__10+
{$} cākkiyar camaṇ paṭukaiyar poym moḻi ākkiya urai koḷēl
Do not accept as truth the fabricated lies of the buddhists and low people of camaṇar.
aruntiru(n) namakku ākkiya araṉ uṟai aṇi karukkuṭip pūkkamaḻ kōyilē puṭaipaṭṭu uymmiṉ
(People of this world!) save yourselves by being near the temple where flowers spread their fragrance and where araṉ who created for us the wealth of caivism which is rare to obtain.
{3:21}__11+
{$} kāṉalil virai malar vimmu kāḻiyāṉ
the native of Kāḻi where in the sea-shore gardens fragrant flowers are in abundance.
vāṉavaṉ karukkuṭi maintaṉ taṉ oḷi āṉa meyññāṉa campantaṉ colliya ūṉam il moḻivalārkku
for those who are able to recite the words which are without defects and composed by Campantaṉ of true spiritual wisdom which is the light of the strong god in Karukkuṭi.
iṉpam uyarum
happiness will increase.

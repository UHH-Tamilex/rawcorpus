[[Karuvili is the name of the place; Koṭṭiṭṭi is the name of the temple.]]

{5:69}__1+
{$} (People of this world!)
maṭṭu iṭṭa kuḻalār cuḻalil valaippaṭṭu mayaṅkip pariyātu
without repenting being confused and cught in the net of the whirlpool of love of women who have tresses of hir into which is tucked fragrant flowers.
nīr
you
kaṭṭiṭṭa viṉai pōkak karuvilikkoṭṭiṭṭai uṟaivāṉ kaḻal (1) kūṭum
join the feet of Civaṉ who dwells in Karuvilikoṭṭiṭṭi so that the Karmams that bound you may leave you.
[[Variant reading: (1) kūṟumē]]
{5:69}__2+
{$}
ñālam malku maṉitarkāḷ
men who are prospering in this world!
nāḷtoṟum
dily.
ēlamāmalarōṭu ilai koṇṭu
bringing big flowers and leaves suitable for worship.
kālaṉār varutal muṉ
before the god of death comes to take away your lives.
nīr karuvilik kōlavār poḻil koṭṭiṭṭai cēr miṉ
you reach Karuvilikkoṭṭiṭṭi which has beautiful and tall gardens.
{5:69}__3+
{$}
paṅkam āyiṉa pēcap paṟaintu
becoming worn out by talking faults.
maṅkumā(ṟu) niṉaiyātē
without thinking of yourself being ruined.
nīr
you.
malarkoṇṭu
with flowers.
kaṅkai cēr caṭaiyāṉ taṉ karuvilik koṅku vār poḻil koṭṭiṭṭai cērmiṉ
reach Karuvilikkoṭṭiṭṭi which has tall gardens in whic there is abundant honey and which belongs to Civaṉ on whose caṭi there is Kanki
{5:69}__4+
{$}
maṉitarkāḷ
People of this world!
vāṭivaruntātē
without suffering, having become emaciated.
vēṭaṉāy vicayaṟku aruḷ ceyta veṇ kāṭaṉār uṟai kiṉṟa karuvilik kōṭu nīḷ poḻil koṭṭiṭṭai cērmiṉ
reach Karuvilikkoṭṭiṭṭi which has gardens which have long branches and where Civaṉ of Veṇkāṭu who granted a weapon to Vicayaṉ assuming the form of a hunter, dwells
{5:69}__5+
{$}
ulakattīr
People of this world!
uyyum āṟu itu
this is the way to save yourselves.
kēṇ miṉ
listen to me.
paikoḷ pāmpu araiyāṉ
Civaṉ wears a hooded cobra in the waist.
paṭai ār maḻukkaiyiṉāṉ uṟai kiṉṟa karuvilik koy koḷ pūmpoḻil koṭṭiṭṭai cērmiṉ
reach Karuvilikoṭṭiṭṭi which has flower-gardens where flowers can be plucked and where Civaṉ who holds in his hand a weapon of a battle-axe, dwells.
{5:69}__6+
{$}
āṟṟavum avalattu aḻuntātu
without being too much sunk in sufferings.
tōṟṟum tīyoṭu nīr nilam tūveḷi kāṟṟum āki niṉṟāṉ taṉ karuvilik kūṟṟam kāyntavaṉ koṭṭiṭṭai cērmiṉ
reach Karuvilikkoṭṭiṭṭi of Civaṉ who is rising fire, water, earth and pure space and who destroyed the god of death.
{5:69}__7+
{$}
nillā vāḻvu nilai peṟum eṉṟu eṇṇi
thinking that the transient life could be made permanent.
pollā āṟu puriyātu
without committing acts in bad ways
nīr
(people of this world!) you
kal ārum matil cūḻ taṇ karuvilik kol ēṟu ūrpavaṉ koṭṭiṭṭai cērmiṉ
reach cool Karuvilikoṭṭiṭṭi surrounded by fortified walls constructed with stones, belonging to Civaṉ who rides on a bull which can gore enemies to death.
{5:69}__8+
{$} (People of this world!)
piṇitta nōyp piṟavip pirivu eytu māṟu uṇarttal ām itu kēṇmiṉ
please listen to the advice which can relieve you from the birth of sufferings which bind you.
uruttira kaṇattiṉār toḻutu ēttum karuvilik kuṇattiṉāṉ uṟai koṭṭiṭṭai cērmiṉ
reach Karuvilikoṭṭiṭṭi which the groups of uruttirar worship with joined hands and prise and where Civaṉ of eight attributs dwells.
{5:69}__9+
{$}
nampuvīr
People who believe in my words!
itu kēṇmiṉkaḷ
listen to this.
nāḷ toṟum empirāṉ eṉṟu imaiyavar ēntum ēkampaṉār uṟai karuvilik kompaṉār payil koṭṭiṭṭai cērmiṉ
reach Karuvilikkoṭṭiṭṭi where ladies who are slender and supple like the twig of a plant are crowded, and where ēkampaṉar who is prised by the celestials who do not wink as `our chief' dwells.
[[(ēkampaṉār) - this word is split into ē and kampaṉār this is called in prosody vakaiyuḷi (vakiyuḷi), scansion in disregard of speech rhythm.]]
{5:69}__10+
{$}
pār uḷīr
People who dwell in this world!
itu kēṇmiṉ
listen to this.
paruvarai pērumāṟu eṭuttāṉai aṭarttavaṉ
Civaṉ who pressed (the arakkaṉ) who lifted the big mountin to remove it from its position.
kār koḷ nīr vayal cūḻ karuvilik kūr koḷ vēliṉaṉ koṭṭiṭṭai cērmiṉ
reach cool Karuvilikoṭṭiṭṭi which is surrounded by fields having water of black colour and where Civaṉ with a sharp trident, dwells.

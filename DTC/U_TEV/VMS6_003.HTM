{$} ēḻaiyēṉ
I who am of weak intellect.
veṟiviravu kūviḷam nal toṅkalāṉai
the god who wears a garland of fragrant bel leaves.
vīraṭṭattāṉai
who is in Vīraṭṭam (Atiki)
veḷḷēṟṟiṉāṉai
who has a white bull.
poṟi araviṉāṉai
who has a cobra with spots on its hood.
puḷ ūrtiyāṉai
who rides on a bird (Karutaṉ) (who is himself Māl also)
poṉ niṟattiṉāṉai
who is also Piramaṉ who is golden in colour.
pukaḻ takkāṉai
who is fit to be prised.
aṟitaṟku ariyacīr ammāṉ taṉṉai
who is the god whose greatness could not be known by all.
(2) ātiyaraiya maṅkai amarntāṉ taṉṉai
who desired Atiyarayamaṇki.
eṟikeṭilattāṉai
who has the river, Keṭilam, which throws waves.
(2) iṟaivaṉ taṉṉai
who pervades in all things.
paṇṭu ikaḻntavāṟē
what a pity I was negligent of him previously.

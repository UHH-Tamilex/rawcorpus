{$} eḻuvārkaḷ
devotees who wake up from sleep.
eṉṟu
saying.
nīlam ārtaru kaṇṭaṉē
Civan having a blue neck!
neṟṟi ōr kaṇṇaṉē
who has an eye on the forehead!
oṟṟai viṭaic cūlam ār tarukaiyaṉē
who holds a trident in the hand and has a single bull!
tuṉṟu paim poḻil cūḻntu aḻaku āya kōlamāmalar maṇam kamaḻ kōṭṭūr naṟkoḻuntē
the good and tender shoot in Kōttūr where the big and beautiful flowers spread their fragrance and which is made beautiful surrounded by verdant and dense gardenes!
(1) cāla nīḷtalaṉiṭaip pukaḻ mikat (2) taṅkuvar pāṅkāl (ē)
will live in this wide world for a long time as their fame increases by their good qualities.

{2:109}__2+
{$} eḻuvārkaḷ
devotees who wake up from sleep.
eṉṟu
saying.
paṅkayam malarc cīṟaṭip pañ cuṟu mel viral aravu alkul maṅkaimār palar
many young girls who have small feet like the lotus flowers and tender toes dyed with a paste prepared from red-cotton flower and a waist resembling the hood of the cobra.
mayil kuyil kiḷi eṉa miḻaṟṟiya moḻiyār
who speak softly like the peacock, indian cuckoo and parrot (the sound of peacock is not generally compared to the sweet words spoken by ladies).
meṉ koṅkaiyār kuḻām
and company of ladies with soft breasts.
kuṇalai cey kōṭṭūr naṟ koḻuntē
the good and tender shoot in Kōṭṭūr where they perform a dance attended with shouting.
(1) caṅkai oṉṟum ilarāki
without having the slightest doubt.
(2) caṅkaraṉ tiruvaruḷ peṟal eḷitu ām (ē)
it is easy for them to receive the benign grace of Civaṉ who has the name of caṅkaraṉ.
[[Variant reading: (1) caṅkai oṉṟu ilārāki naṟ caṅkaraṉ. (2) ilārākiṉṟa caṅkaraṉ]]
{2:109}__3+
{$} nalam malarkoṭu toḻutu eḻum aṭiyavar tamakku ellām nampaṉār
Civaṉ is the god in whom all the devotees who worship him with flawess flowers and rise, repose their confidence.
cempoṉ ār tarum eḻil tikaḻ mulaiyavar
ladies who have breasts which are eminently beautiful by the ornaments made of superior gold which they wear.
celvamalkiya (kōṭṭūr)
Kōltūr in which wealth is flourishing.
nalla kompaṉār toḻutu āṭiya kōṭṭūr naṟkoḻuntē eṉṟu eḻuvārkaḷ
those who wake up from sleep saying the young shoot that is in Koṭṭur where the beautiful ladies who are like the tender bough, worship and dance.
am poṉ ār tarum ulakiṉil amararōṭu amarntu iṉitu iruppār (ē)
will sit along with the immortals joining them, in the heaven which is made of gold.
{2:109}__4+
{$} palavum nīḷ poḻil tīṅkaṉi tēṉ palā māṅkaṉi payilvāya kalava maññaikaḷ nilavu coṟkiḷḷaikaḷ aṉṉam cērntu aḻakāya kulavu nīḷ vayal kayal ukaḷkōṭṭūr naṟkoḻuntē eṉṟu eḻuvārkaḷ
devotees who wake up from sleep saying the shoot that is in Kōṭṭūr where in the long and eminent fields the fish carp frisks, where the swans flock together to make them beautiful, where the peacocks with big til, eminent parrots which speak sweet words live in the long gardens which has many trees and creepers, has many sweet fruits, honey, jack-fruits, and mango fruits in abundance.
nilavu celvattar āki nīḷ nilattiṭai nīṭiya pukaḻār (ē)
will have permanent wealth and attin in this wideworld fame which will last for a long time.
{2:109}__5+
{$} urukuvār uḷḷattu oṇcuṭar
Civaṉ is the bright light in the hearts of those which become tender.
taṉakku eṉṟum aṉpar ām aṭiyārkaḷ parukum āramutu eṉa niṉṟu patti ceytu etticaiyum (eḻuvārkaḷ)
those who wake up from sleep in all directions, being devoted to Civaṉ thinking of him as the drinking rare nectar of the devotees who cherish him with love, being steady in their minds.
kuruku vāḻ vayal cūḻ taru kōṭṭūrk koḻuntē eṉṟu eḻuvārkaḷ
those who wake up from sleep saying the tender shoot in Kōṭṭūr surrounded by fields in which birds live.
aruku cēr taru viṉaikaḷum pōy akalum
their acts will go away from them which approach them.
avaṉ aruḷ peṟal ām (ē)
And they are the recipients of His grace.
{2:109}__6+
{$} tuṉṟuvārcaṭait tūmati, mattamum, tuṉ erukku, ār, vaṉṉi, poṉṟiṉār 1. talaikkalaṉoṭu parikalam, puli uri uṭai āṭaik (koḻuntu)
the tender shoot that adorns itself on the dense and long caṭi, a spotless crescent, datura flowers, dense yarkum flowers, flowers of holy mountin ebony, leaves of indian mesquit, and has ornaments of skulls of tevar who deceased, eating-plate of a skull and dress worn on the waist and covering on the body made of the tiger`s skin.
koṉṟai poṉ eṉa malar taru kōṭṭūr naṟ koḻuntē eṉṟu eḻuvārai eṉṟum ēttuvārkku iṭar ilai
there will be no suffering to those who prise those devotees who wake up from sleep uttering the name of good shoot in Kōṭṭūr where the koṉṟi blossoms like gold
kēṭu ilai
there will be no destruction for them.
ētam vantu aṭaiyā (ē)
faults will not reach them.

{$} māṭam māḷikai, kōpuram, kūṭaṅkaḷ, maṇi araṅku, aṇi cālai, pāṭu cūḻ matiṟpaim poṉ cey maṇṭapam paricoṭu payilvāya (kōṭṭūr)
Kōṭṭūr where the mansions with storeys, towers, halls, beautiful stages, beautiful feeding houses, and gilded pavilions put up during festivals which are surrounded by walls of fortification are close to each other with their nature.
kūṭu naṟpoḻil cūḻtaru kōṭṭūr naṟ koḻuntē eṉṟu eḻuvārkaḷ
those wake up from sleep uttering the name of good shoot in Kōṭṭūr surrounded by verdant gardens in which many trees are combined.
kēṭu (atu) oṉṟu ilarāki nal ulakiṉil keḻuvuvar pukaḻāl (ē)
will not know even a little of ruin and live in this good world full of fame.
{2:109}__8+
{$} oḷi koḷ vāḷ eyiṟṟu arakkaṉ av uyar varai eṭuttalum umai añca
as Umi was frightened when the arakkaṉ who had bright and sharp teeth like the sword, lifted that famous and mountin (Kayili).
cuḷiya ūṉṟalum cōrntiṭa
when he languished, as Civaṉ pressed the toe to feel pin.
vāḷoṭu nāḷ avaṟku aruḷ ceyta (koḻuntu)
Civaṉ who granted him a sword and long life.
kuḷir koḷ pūm poḻil cūḻtaru kōṭṭūr naṟ koḻuntiṉait toḻuvārkaḷ
those who worship with joined hands the good and tender shoot (Civaṉ) in Kōṭṭūr surrounded by beautiful and cool gardens.
taḷir koḷ tāmaraip pātaṅkaḷ aruḷ peṟum tavam uṭaiyavar (tām, ē)
are fortunate people who are granted the feet of the Lord resembling lotus which is ever fresh.
{2:109}__9+
{$} pāṭi āṭum meyp pattarkaṭku aruḷ ceyum muttiṉaip pavaḷattai
Civaṉ who is as precious as the coral and pearl and who grants his grace to sincere devotees who sing his prises and dance out of joy.
tēṭimāl ayaṉ kāṇa oṇṇāta attiruviṉai
-that wealth which Māl and Ayaṉ could not know even though they searched for him.
terivaimār āṭavar kūṭik kaitoḻu kōṭṭūr naṟ koḻuntē eṉṟu eḻuvārkaḷ
those who wake up from sleep uttering the name, the good and tender shoot in Kōṭṭūr where the local ladies and gents joining together worship with joined hands.
nīṭu celvattarāki
will enjoy wealth for a long times.
iv ulakiṉil nikaḻtaru pukaḻār (ē)
will have fame that exists in the world surviving them.

{$} kōṇal veṇ piṟaic caṭaiyaṉaik kōṭṭūr naṟ koḻuntiṉaic ceḻuntiraḷaip pūṇal ceytu aṭi pōṟṟu miṉ
(Devotees!) pay your respects to the feet of the Lord who wears on his caṭi a curved white crescent, by adorning the good tender shoot in Kōṭṭūr and flourshing round mass with flowers, clothes and ornaments.
[[kōṇal veṇpiṟai:
kōṇiya piṟai cūṭiyai (cuntarar, paṇṭikkoṭumuṭi, 10)]]
poy ilā meyyaṉ nal aruḷ eṉṟum kāṇal oṉṟum ilākkār amaṇtērar kuṇṭākkar coṟkarutātē
without minding the words of the low people, who are the buddhists and black amaṇar who do not know even a little of the good grace of the true Civaṉ who is not united with falsehood, for ever.
araṉaip pēṇal ceytu toḻum aṭiyavar perumaiyaip peṟuvār (ē)
the devotees who worship araṉ cherishing him with love, will attin greatness.

{$} pantu ulā viral, pavaḷavāyt tēṉ moḻip pāvaiyōṭu uru ārum (koḻuntiṉai
the tender shoot that has a form united with a beautiful lady who speaks words as sweet as honey and who has lips like coral and fingers that hold a ball.
kontu ulām malar viri poḻil kōṭṭūr naṟkoḻuntiṉai
on the good tender shoot that is in Kōṭṭūr which has garden in which flowers blossom in bunches.
ceḻumpavaḷam vantu ulāviya kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ vāyntu uṟai ceyta cantu ulām tamiḻ mālai vallavar
those who are capable of singing the garland of tamiḻ verses having verse rhythm, composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kaḻi where the high qualtiy coral comes to the shore and moves about, with the gift of Civaṉ grace.
tāṅkuvar pukaḻ (āl), (ē)
will possess fame (āl : expletive).

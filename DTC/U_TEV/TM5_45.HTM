*1

*2

*3

tuṇṭavāṉpiṟait tōṇipuravaraik
kaṇṭu kāmuṟukiṉṟaṉaḷ, kaṉṉiyē.

*4

cōlai _ārtaru tōṇipuravarkkuc
cāla nallaḷ _ākiṉṟaṉaḷ---taiyalē.

*5

cuṇṇam_āṭiya tōṇipurattu _uṟai
_aṇṇalārukkuc cāla _aḻakitē?

*6
mullaiveṇnakai moykuḻalāy! _uṉakku
_allaṉ _āvatu _aṟintilai, nī; kaṉit
tollai _ār poḻil-tōṇipuravarkkē
nallai _āyiṭukiṉṟaṉai---naṅkaiyē!

*7
_oṉṟutāṉ _aṟiyār, _ulakattavar;

*8
_uṟavu pēykkaṇam; _uṇpatu veṇtalai;
_uṟaivatu _īmam; _uṭalil _ōr peṇkoṭi;

_iṟaivaṉārkku _ivaḷ _eṉ kaṇṭu _aṉpu_āvatē?

*9

_āka, yāṉum---_avarkku---_iṉi _āḷ_atē.

*10
_iṭṭam_āyiṉa ceyvāḷ, _eṉ peṇkoṭi---

{1:116}__1+
{$} avviṉaikku ivviṉai ām eṉṟu (1) collum aḵtu aṟivīr
(Devotees) you know the saying that the present suffering is the result of acts done in previous births.
uyviṉai nāṭātiruppatum um tamakku ūṉam aṉṟē
is it not a drawback to you sitting idle without any effort to save yourselves from that?
kaiviṉaiceytu empirāṉ kaḻal pōṟṟatum
we shall prise the feet of our master by doing manual service.
nām aṭiyōm
we are his slave.
ceyviṉai vantu emaittīṇṭappeṟā; tirunīlakaṇṭam
our acts will not come in contact with us; tirunīlakantam is our refuge and support.
[[Variant reading: (1) collumatu vaṟiyīr]]
{1:116}__2+
{$} ēvaṉaiyāl eyil mūṉṟu erittīr eṉṟu
prising Civaṉ as you who burnt the three forts by your skill in archery.
kāviṉai iṭṭum
planting trees to grow gardens
kuḷam pala toṭṭum
and digging many tanks
kaṉi maṉattāl
with a tender heart.
irupoḻutum
both day and night.
pūviṉaik koytu malar aṭi pōṟṟutum nām aṭiyōm
we who are your devotees prise your feet which are soft as flower, plucking flower and scattering them at your feet.
tīviṉai vantu emaittīṇṭappeṟā
sins will not come into contact with us.
tirunīlakaṇṭam
[see 1st verse].
{1:116}__3+
{$} mulaittaṭam mūḻkiya pōkaṅkaḷum maṟṟu evaiyum ellām vilaittalai āvaṇam koṇṭu emai āṇṭa viricaṭaiyīr
Civaṉ who has a spread-out caṭi and who brought us as slaves by bond paying price so that all the pleasures enjoyed with women and all other pleasures may not catch hold of us!

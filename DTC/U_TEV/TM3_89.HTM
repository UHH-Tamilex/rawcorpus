*1
tiruntu mā kaḷiṟṟu _iḷa maruppoṭu tiraḷ maṇic cantam _unti,
kuruntu mā kuravamum kuṭacamum pīliyum cumantu koṇṭu,
nirantu mā vayal puku nīṭu kōṭṭāṟu cūḻ kocci mēvip
poruntiṉār tiruntu _aṭi pōṟṟi vāḻ, neñcamē! pukal _atu _āmē.

*2
_ēlam _ār _ilavamōṭu _iṉamalarttokuti_āy _eṅkum nunti,
kōla mā miḷakoṭu koḻuṅ kaṉi koṉṟiyum koṇṭu, kōṭṭāṟu
_āliyā, vayal pukum _aṇitaru kocciyē nacci mēvum
nīlam _ār kaṇṭaṉi niṉi, maṭa neñcamē! _añcal, nīyē!

*3
poṉṉum mā maṇi koḻittu, _eṟi puṉal, karikaḷvāy nurikaḷ _unti,
kaṉṉimār mulinalam kavara vantu _ēṟu kōṭṭāṟu cūḻa
maṉṉiṉār mātoṭum maruvu _iṭamkocciyē maruviṉ, nāḷum
muṉṉinōy toṭarum_āṟu _illikāṇ, neñcamē! _añcal, nīyē!

*4
kantam _ār kētakic cantaṉakkāṭu cūḻ katalimāṭē
vantu, mā vaḷḷiyiṉ pavar _aḷik kuvaḷiyic cāṭi _ōṭa,
kontu vārkuḻaliṉār kutikoḷ kōṭṭāṟu cūḻ kocci mēya
_entiyār _aṭi niṉintu, _uyyal _ām, neñcamē! _añcal, nīyē!

*5
maṟi koḷum tiṟaliṉār _ākutip pukikaḷ vāṉ _aṇṭa miṇṭi,
ciṟikoḷum puṉal _aṇi ceḻu mati tikaḻ matil koccitaṉpāl,
_uṟivu_iṭam _eṉa maṉam_atu koḷum, piramaṉārciram _aṟutta,
_iṟivaṉatu _aṭi_iṇi _iṟiñci vāḻ, neñcamē! _añcal, nīyē!

*6
cuṟṟamum makkaḷum tokka _at takkaṉiccāṭi, _aṉṟē,
_uṟṟa mālvari _uminaṅkiyip paṅkamā _uḷkiṉāṉ, _ōr
kuṟṟam _il _aṭiyavar kuḻumiya vīti cūḻ kocci mēvi
nal-tavam _aruḷpuri nampaṉi nampiṭāy, nāḷum, neñcē!

*7
koṇṭalār vantiṭa, kōla vār poḻilkaḷil kūṭi, manti
kaṇṭa vārkaḻi piṭittu _ēṟi, mā mukiltaṉik katuvu kocci,
_aṇṭa vāṉavarkaḷum _amararum muṉivarum paṇiya, _ālam
_uṇṭa mā kaṇṭaṉārtammiyē _uḷku, nī! _añcal, neñcē!

*8
_aṭal _eyiṟṟu _arakkaṉār nerukki, māmali _eṭuttu, _ārtta vāykaḷ
_uṭal keṭa, tiruviral _ūṉṟiṉār _uṟivu_iṭam---_oḷi koḷ veḷḷi
maṭal_iṭip pavaḷamum muttamum tottu vaṇ puṉṉimāṭē,
peṭiyoṭum kuruku_iṉam peruku---taṇ kocciyē pēṇu, neñcē!

*9
_araviṉil-tuyiltarum _ariyum, nal piramaṉum, _aṉṟu, _ayarntu,
kurikaḻal, tirumuṭi, _aḷavu _iṭa _ariyavar---kōṅku cempoṉ
viri poḻil_iṭi miku malimakaḷ makiḻtara vīṟṟirunta
kariya nal miṭaṟu _uṭik kaṭavuḷār---kocciyē karutu, neñcē!

*10
kaṭu mali _uṭal _uṭi _amaṇarum, kañci _uṇ cākkiyarum,
_iṭum _aṟa_uritaṉi _ikaḻpavar karutum nam _īcar; vāṉōr
naṭu _uṟi nampaṉi; nālmaṟiyavar paṇintu _ētta, ñālam
_uṭiyavaṉ; kocciyē _uḷki vāḻ, neñcamē! _añcal, nīyē!

*11
kāyntu tam kāliṉāl kālaṉic ceṟṟavar, kaṭi koḷ kocci
_āyntu koṇṭu _iṭam _eṉa _irunta nal _aṭikaḷi, _ātarittē
_ēytta tolpukaḻ mikum _eḻilmaṟi ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa

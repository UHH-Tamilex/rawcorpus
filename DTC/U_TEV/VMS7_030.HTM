[[Kōṭpuli Nāyaṉār invited Cuntarar to his house in Nāṭṭiyattāṉkuṭi and after performing pūja, requested Cuntarar to accept his two dughters, Ciṅkaṭi and her younger sister, Vanappaki as his wives. But he refused that request and adopted them as his dughters; so in every decade he sang after this, Cuntarar called himself as the father of Ciṅkati and Vaṉappaki or either of these, as the metre would permit]]

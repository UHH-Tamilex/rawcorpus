*1
naṉavilum kaṉavilum, nāḷum, taṉ _oḷi,
niṉivilum _eṉakku vantu _eytum niṉmalaṉ---
kaṉikaṭal viyakam toḻu karukkuṭi,
_aṉal-_eri_āṭum _em _aṭikaḷ; kāṇmiṉē!

*2
vētiyaṉ, viṭi _uṭi vimalaṉ, _oṉṉalar
mūteyil _eri _eḻa muṉinta mukkaṇaṉ,
kātu _iyal kuḻiyiṉaṉ, karukkuṭi _amar
_ātiyi, _aṭi toḻa _allal _illiyē.

*3
mañcu _uṟu poḻil vaḷam mali karukkuṭi---
nañcu _uṟu tirumiṭaṟu _uṭiya---nātaṉār,
_am curumpu _ār kuḻal _arivi _añcavē,
veñcuramtaṉil viḷiyāṭal _eṉkolō?

*4
_ūṉ _uṭip piṟaviyi _aṟukka _uṉṉuvīr!
kāṉ_iṭi _āṭalāṉ payil karukkuṭik
kōṉ _uyar kōyili vaṇaṅki, vikalum,
vāṉavar toḻu kaḻal vāḻtti, vāḻmiṉē!

*5
cūṭuvar, caṭi_iṭik kaṅkinaṅkiyi;
kūṭuvar, _ulaku_iṭi _aiyam koṇṭu; _oli
pāṭuvar, _ici; paṟi koṭṭa, naṭṭiruḷ
_āṭuvar; karukkuṭi _aṇṇal vaṇṇamē!

*6
_iṉpu_uṭiyār, _ici vīṇi; pūṇ _arā,
_eṉpu, _uṭiyār; _eḻil mēṉimēl _eri
muṉpu _uṭiyār; mutal _ēttum _aṉparukku
_aṉpu_uṭiyār---karukkuṭi _em _aṇṇalē!

*7
kālamum ñāyiṟum tīyum _āyavar;
kōlamum muṭi _aravu _aṇinta koḷkiyar;
cīlamum _uṭiyavar; tiruk karukkuṭi,
cālavum _iṉitu, _avar _uṭiya taṉmiyē!

*8
_eṟikaṭal puṭi taḻuvu _ilaṅki maṉṉaṉi
muṟipaṭa vari_iṭi _aṭartta mūrttiyār,
kaṟi paṭu poḻil mati tavaḻ, karukkuṭi
_aṟivoṭu toḻumavar _āḷvar, naṉmiyē.

*9
pū maṉum ticimukaṉtāṉum, poṟpu _amar
vāmaṉaṉ, _aṟikilā vaṇṇam _ōṅku _eri-
_ām _eṉa _uyarntavaṉ _aṇi karukkuṭi
nā maṉaṉiṉil vara niṉital naṉmiyē.

*10
cākkiyar, camaṇ paṭu kiyar, poymmoḻi
_ākkiya _uri koḷēl! _arun tiru(n) namakku
_ākkiya _araṉ _uṟi _aṇi karukkuṭip
pūk kamaḻ kōyilē puṭipaṭṭu _uymmiṉē!

*11
kāṉalil virimalar vimmu kāḻiyāṉ,
vāṉavaṉ karukkuṭi mintaṉtaṉ _oḷi
_āṉa, meyñ ñāṉacampantaṉ, colliya
_ūṉam _il moḻi valārkku _uyarum, _iṉpamē.

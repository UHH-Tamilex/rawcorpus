{3:15}__1+
{$} mantira maṟaiyavai vāṉavarōṭum intiraṉ vaḻipaṭa niṉṟa em iṟai
our Lord who was worshipped by Intiraṉ along with the inhabitants of heaven and many maṟi-s which contin mantiram
[[Variant reading: mantiramaṟaiyavar brahmins who are proficient in mantiram]]
venta veṇṇīṟṟar
the Lord who smears himself with well-burnt white ash.
veṇkāṭu mēviya, antamum mutal (um) uṭaiyā aṭikaḷ allarē
is he not the deity who is in Veṇkāṭu and who is the end and beginning of all things?
[[mēviya : the adjectival participle qualifies aṭikaḷ; in the following verses Nos 2,3,4,5,6,7, and 8 the same rule will apply]]
{3:15}__2+
{$} paṭai uṭai maḻuviṉar
the Lord has a battle-axe as his weapon.
pāy pulit tōliṉ uṭai viri kōvaṇam ukanta koḷkaiyar
he is pleased to wear a broad loin cloth upon the pouncing tiger's skin
[[pāy puli: The epithet pāy has been given to it when it was alive.]]
viṭaiyuṭaik koṭiyar
has a banner on which a bull is written
veṇkāṭu mēviya, caṭaiyiṭaip puṉal vaitta, caturar allarē
is he not an able person who is in Veṇkatu and placed the water (of the Kaṇki) in his matted locks of hir?
[[Water has the nature of flowing downwards; God controlled it and arrested it from flowing down; so he is called an able person.]]
[[mēviya caturar, vaitta caturar]]
{3:15}__3+
{$} pāloṭu ney tayir palavum āṭuvar
the god will bathe in milk, ghee, curd and many other things.
tōloṭu nūliḻai tutainta mārpiṉar
has on his chest a skin and sacred thread close to each other.
mēlavar paravu veṇkāṭu mēviya ālam (atu) amarnta em aṭikaḷ allarē
is he not the deity who is veṇtaku prised by great persons, who sits under the banyan tree (ālam amarnta aṭikaḷ one who drank the poison willingly is another meaning)
{3:15}__4+
{$} ñāḻalum ceruntiyum naṟu malarp puṉṉaiyum tāḻai veṇ kuruku ayal tayaṅku kāṉalil
in the sea-shore garden where naḻal (Feted cassia); pulinakak koṉṟai
koṉṟi flower whose stamens resemble tiger's claws) carunti, the fragrant flowers of puṉṉi (mast-wood tree) are shining by the side of the flowers of fragrant screw-pine which look like white cranes
[[Variant reading: nāḻalum]]
vēḻam (atu) uritta (iṟaivar)
the Lord who flayed the elephant.
veṇkāṭu mēviya (iṟaivar)
and who is in veṇkatu.
yāḻiṉatu icaiyuṭai iṟaivar
is he not the Lord who hears the music of yāḻ?
(kāṉalil (uḷḷa) veṇkāṭu, uritta, mēviya, uṭai(ya) iṟaivar/).
{3:15}__5+
{$} pūtaṅkaḷ pala uṭaip puṉitar
the holy person who has demons as his army.
puṇṇiyar
the embodiment of all virtuous acts.
ētaṅkaḷ pala iṭar tīrkkum em iṟai
our Lord who removes blemishes and many sufferings (of devotees)
vētaṅkaḷ mutalvar
is the eminent person spoken by the vētams. (the origin of the vētams).
veṇkāṭu mēviya (paramar)
the supreme being who is in veṇkātu.
pātaṅkaḷ toḻa niṉṟa paramar allarē
is he not the supreme being whose feet are worshipped (by devotees)?
{3:15}__6+
{$} maṇṇavar viṇṇavar vaṇaṅka
when the people of this earth and residents of heaven worship (him).
vaikalum eṇṇiya tēvarkaḷ iṟaiñcum em iṟai
our supreme god who is worshipped by other tēvars for their own benefit, dily.
viṇ amar poḻil koḷ veṇkāṭu mēviya aṇṇalai aṭi toḻa allal illai
there will be no distress if one worships the feet of the eminent god who is veṇkāṭu which has gardens so high as to touch the sky
{3:15}__7+
{$} nayantavarkkup pala aruḷ nalki
Having bestowed grace in many ways on loving devotees.
intiraṉ kayantiram vaḻipaṭa niṉṟa kaṇṇutal
the god who has an eye on the forehead and who was worshipped by intiraṉ and the eminent elephant (airātavam) (kaṇṇutal = kaṇ + nutal (kayantiram - kayēntiram kajēntiram))
viyantavar paravu veṇkāṭu mēviya (paramar)
the supreme god in veṇkātu which is prised by devotees who are admired by other people.
payam taru maḻuvuṭaip paramar allarē
is he not the supreme god who has a battle-axe that strikes fear?
{3:15}__8+
{$} malai uṭaṉ eṭutta val arakkaṉ nīḷ muṭi talaiyuṭaṉ nerittu aruḷ ceyta caṅkarar
the god who has the name of cankarar who bestowed his grace after crushing the ten heads of arakkaṉ (iravaṇaṉ) together with their crowns, who immediately lifted the mountin. (iravaṇaṉ lifted mount Kailācam as soon as his plane, pushpakam, could not fly over it.
vilai uṭai nīṟṟar
Has sacred ash which has a high price.
veṇkāṭu mēviya (aṭikaḷ)
the deity who is in veṇkātu.
alaiyuṭaip puṉal vaitta aṭikaḷ allarē
is he not the deity whobore on his head the water having waves?
{3:15}__9+
{$} ēṭu aviḻ naṟumalar ayaṉum mālu māy
Māl and Ayaṉ who is seated in the fragrant (lotus) flower of opened petals, joining together.
terintavar tēṭavum tērakiṟkilār
though both had the power of perception and searched for his head and feet, were not capable of finding them.
vēṭam (atu) uṭaiya veṇkāṭu mēviya (aṭikaḷ)
the deity who has several forms.
āṭalai amarnta em aṭikaḷ allarē
is he not our god who was glad to dance?
{3:15}__10+
{$} pōtiyar piṇṭiyar poruttam illikaḷ
the buddhists and jins who have no propriety.
nītikaḷ colliyum niṉaiyakiṟkilār
though they are told about the greatness of Caivam and other moral codes they are not able to ponder over them.
vētiyar paravu veṇkāṭu mēviya ātiyai aṭitoḻa allal illai
there will no suffering if one worships feet of the supreme being who is veṇkātu where the people well-versed in vētam, prise him.
{3:15}__11+
{$} celvaṉ em civaṉ uṟai tiruveṇkāṭṭiṉmēl
on tiruveṇkātu where our Lord Civaṉ resides.
nallavar pukaliyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ colliya arun tamiḻ pattum vallavar
those who are able to recite all the rare ten tamiḻ songs which were composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ born in pukali which has good people.
allalōṭu aruviṉai aṟutal āṇaiyē
it is our authority for the complete destruction of sufferings and acts which cannot be easily overcome.

NOTE_VMS: Nallam is now known as Kōnērirājapuram.

{1:85}__1+
{$} kallāl niḻalmēya kaṟaicēr kaṇṭā eṉṟu ellāmoḻiyālum imaiyōr toḻutu ētta
when the celestials who do not wink, worshipped with joined hands and prised Civaṉ as one who has a black neck and who is seated under the shade of the banyan tree, in all languages known to them.
villāl araṇ mūṉṟum ventuviḻa eyta nallāṉ namai āḷvāṉ nalla nakarāṉē
the benign god who shot an arrow, to be consumed by fire and fall down, on the three forts with a bow, and one who admits us into the grace, is in the city of Nallam.
{1:85}__2+
{$} takkaṉ peruvēḷvi taṉṉil amararaittukkam palaceytu
inflicting many sufferings on the immortals who had gathered in the big sacrifice performed by takkaṉ.
cuṭar poṟcaṭai tāḻa
as the shining beautiful caṭi is hanging low.
kokkiṉ iṟakōṭu kuḷir veṇpiṟai cūṭum nakkaṉ
the naked Civaṉ who wears cool and white crescent along with a white flower like the feather of the crane.
namai āḷvāṉ
and who admits us into his grace.
nallam nakarāṉē
is in the city of nallam.
{1:85}__3+
{$} antimatiyōṭum aravaccaṭaitāḻa munti aṉal ēnti mutukāṭṭu eriāṭi
one who dances in fire holding fire in the hand at the beginning of the aeon, the caṭi which has a cobra, and a crescent that rises in the evening, to hang low.
[[āṭi is a noun; not an adverbial participle.]]
(1) cintittu eḻavallār tīrāviṉai tīrkkum nanti
Nanti who can remove our Karmams otherwise irremovable, of those who fix their thoughts on his and wake up from sleep.
namai āḷvāṉ nallam nakarāṉē
and who admits us into his grace is in the city of Nallam.
[[Nanti is one of the many names of Civaṉ.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) cintittoḻa]]
{1:85}__4+
{$} kuḷirum maticūṭi
weaving a cool crescent on the head.
koṉṟaiccaṭai tāḻa
and the caṭi which wears koṉṟi flowers to hand low.
miḷirum aḻakōṭum veṇṇūl tikaḻ mārpil taḷirum tirumēṉittaiyal pākamāy
Civaṉ having a young lady whose body has the complexion of tender mango leaves, on the chest in which the sacred thread is prominent along with a gleaming cobra.
naḷirum vayal cūḻnta nalla nakarāṉē
is in the city of nallam surrounded by cool fields.
{1:85}__5+
{$} maṇi ār tikaḻ kaṇṭam uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has a shining neck which shines like the sapphire. cherishing with love the feet of our father and master who has a twisted long caṭi in which there is an abundance of flowers.
tuṇivār toṇṭar malarkoṇṭu ētta
devotees with a resolute mind to worship Civaṉ with flowers.
naṇiyāṉ
is near at hand.
namai āḷvāṉ
admits us as him protege.
nalla nakarāṉ
is in the city of nallam.
{1:85}__6+
{$} vācam malar malku malaiyāṉ makaḷōṭum pūcumcuṭunīṟu puḷaintāṉ
Civaṉ adorned himself with well burnt sacred ash which is intended for smearing, having on his half the dughter of the mountin (imavāṉ) who adorns herself profusely with fragrant flowers.
virikoṉṟai īcaṉ eṉa uḷki eḻuvārviṉaikaṭṭu nācaṉ
destroyes the Karmams of those who think of his as the Lord who wears blossomed koṉṟi flowers and then wake up from sleep.
namai āḷvāṉ nalla nakarāṉē
and who admits us as his protege is in the city of nallam.
{1:85}__7+
{$} amkōlvaḷai maṅkai kāṇa aṉal ēntik koṅkuār naṟuṅkoṉṟaicūṭi
Having adorned his head with fragrant koṉṟi which has honey, holding fire in his hand and to be witnessed by the young lady who wears a beautiful collection of bangles.
kuḻakuāka veṅkāṭu iṭamāka ventī viḷaiyāṭum namkōṉ
our master who dances in the hot fire as if it is his play having the hot cremation-ground as the stage, with youthful vigour. ((kāṇaviḷaiyāṭum: is the syntactical link)
namai āḷvāṉ nalla nakarāṉ
and who admits us into his grace is in the city of nallam.
{1:85}__8+
{$} peṇ ār tirumēṉip perumāṉ
Civaṉ who has on his holy body a lady.
piṟai (1) malku kaṇ ār nutaliṉāṉ
who has a forehead in which there is an eye, and the crescent is flourishing.
kayilai karuttiṉāl eṇṇātu eṭuttāṉai iṟaiyē viral ūṉṟi
who pressed down in a very small measure the one who intentionally lifted Kayili without thinking what is to happen to him.
naṇṇār puram eytāṉ
and who shot an arrow on the cities of enemies.
nalla nakarāṉē
is in the city of nallam.
[[Variant reading L (1) pulku]]
{1:85}__9+
{$} nākattu aṇaiyāṉum naḷirmā malarāṉum
Māl who sleeps on a serpet-bed and Piramaṉ seated in a cool and big (lotus) flower.
pōkattu iyalpiṉāl poliya
to be prominent by the enjoyment of pleasures (including sexual enjoyment)
aḻaku ākum ākattavaḷōṭum amarntu (aṅku) aḻaku ārum nākam(m) arai ārttāṉ nalla nakarāṉē
Civaṉ who tied a beautiful cobra in the waist, dwelling with desire with a lady in his body who is beauty itself, is in the city of nallam.
{1:85}__10+
{$} kuṟiil camaṇōṭu kuṇṭar vaṇtērar aṟivuil uraikēṭṭu aṅku avamē kaḻiyātē
to pass your life wasting it listening to the words which have no wisdom, of the low camaṇar who have no high aim and the strong tēvar (buddhists).
poṟi koḷ aravu ārttāṉ
Civaṉ who tied a cobra of stops.
pollāviṉai tīrkkum naṟaikoḷ poḻil cūḻnta nalla nakarāṉē
and who can remove the evil Karmams is in the city of nallam surrounded by gardens (tīrkkum qualifies nakarāṉ)
{1:85}__11+
{$} nalam ār maṟaiyōr vāḻ nalla nakar mēya kolaicēr maḻuvāṉai
on Civaṉ who has a battle-axe capable of killing and who is in the city of nallam where brahmins of great virtues live.
koccai amarntu ōṅku talam ār tamiḻ ñāṉa campantaṉ coṉṉa kalaikaḷ iaivavallār
those who are capable of reciting the arts which were composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ of vast knowledge of tamiḻ who is a resident of the great shrine Kocci.
kavalai kaḻivār
will be free from their anxieties.

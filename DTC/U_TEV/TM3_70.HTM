  • talam: tiru mayilāṭutuṟi
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.15

*1
_ēṉa_eyiṟu, _āṭu_aravoṭu, _eṉpu, vari_āmi, _ivi pūṇṭu, _iḷiñarāy,
kāṉa vari nīṭu _uḻuvi _ataḷ _uṭiya paṭarcaṭiyar kāṇi _eṉal _ām---
_āṉa pukaḻ vētiyarkaḷ _ākutiyiṉmītu puki pōki, _aḻaku _ār
vāṉam _uṟu cōlimici mācu paṭa mūcum mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*2
_am taṇmati ceñcaṭiyar, _am kaṇ _eḻil koṉṟiyoṭu _aṇintu, _aḻakar_ām
_emtam _aṭikaṭku _iṉiya tāṉam_atu, vēṇṭil, _eḻil _ār pati_atu_ām---
kantam mali cantiṉoṭu kār_akilum vāri varu kāviriyuḷāl
vanta tiri _unti, _etir manti malar cintum mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*3
tōḷiṉmici vari_aravam nañcu _aḻala vīkki, miku nōkku _ariyarāy,
mūḷi paṭu veṇtaliyil _uṇṭu, mutukāṭu _uṟiyum mutalvar _iṭam_ām---
pāḷi paṭu piṅkamuku ceṅkaṉi _utirttiṭa, nirantu, kamaḻ pū,
vāḷi kutikoḷḷa, maṭal viriya, maṇam nāṟum mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*4
_ētam _ilar, _ariya maṟi; maliyarmakaḷ _ākiya _ilaṅku nutal _oṇ
pēti taṭamārpu_atu _iṭam_āka _uṟikiṉṟa perumāṉatu _iṭam_ām---
kātal miku kavviyoṭu mavval_avi kūṭi varu kāviriyuḷāl,
mātar maṟitirikaḷ puka, veṟiya veṟi kamaḻum mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*5
pū viri katuppiṉ maṭamaṅkiyar _akamtoṟum naṭantu, pali tēr
pā viri _icikku _uriya pāṭal payilum paramar paḻami _eṉal _ām---
kāviri nurittu _irukarikkum maṇi cinta, varivaṇṭu kavara
mā viri matuk kiḻiya, manti kutikoḷḷum mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*6
kaṭam tikaḻ karuṅkaḷiṟu _urittu, _umiyum _añca, mika nōkku _ariyarāy,
viṭam tikaḻum mū_ilinalvēl _uṭiya vētiyar virumpum _iṭam_ām---
toṭarntu _oḷir kiṭantatu _oru cōti miku toṇṭi _eḻil koṇṭa tuvarvāy
maṭantiyar kuṭinta puṉal vācam mika nāṟum mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*7
_avva(t) ticiyārum _aṭiyārum _uḷar_āka _aruḷceytu, _avarkaḷmēl
_evvam _aṟa, vikalum _iraṅki, _eri_āṭum _ematu _īcaṉ _iṭam_ām---
kavviyoṭu kāviri kalantu varu teṉkari nirantu kamaḻpū
mavvaloṭu mātavi mayaṅki maṇam nāṟum mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*8
_ilaṅkinakar maṉṉaṉ muṭi_orupatiṉoṭu _irupatutōḷ neriya, viralāl
vilaṅkalil _aṭarttu, _aruḷpurintavar _irunta _iṭam viṉavutirkaḷēl---
kalaṅkal nuri _unti _etir vanta kayam mūḻki malar koṇṭu makiḻā,
malaṅki varu kāviri nirantu poḻikiṉṟa mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*9
_oṇtiṟaliṉ nāṉmukaṉum mālum mika nēṭi _uṇarāta vakiyāl,
_aṇṭam _uṟa _aṅki_uru _āki, mika nīṇṭa _araṉāratu _iṭam_ām---
keṇṭi_iri koṇṭu, keḷiṟu _ār _uṭaṉ_iruntu, kiḷarvāy _aṟutal cēr
vaṇṭalmaṇal keṇṭi, maṭanāri viḷiyāṭum mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*10
miṇṭu tiṟal _amaṇaroṭu cākkiyarum _alar tūṟṟa, mikka tiṟalōṉ
_iṇṭi kuṭikoṇṭa caṭi _eṅkaḷ perumāṉatu _iṭam _eṉpar---_eḻil _ār
teṇtiri parantu _oḻuku kāviriya teṉkari, nirantu kamaḻpū
vaṇṭu_avi kiḷikka, matu vantu _oḻuku cōli mayilāṭutuṟiyē.

*11
niṇam taru mayāṉam, nilam vāṉam matiyātatu _oru cūlamoṭu pēyk-
kaṇam toḻu kapāli kaḻal _ētti, mika vāyttatu _oru kātaṉmiyiṉāl,
maṇam taṇ mali kāḻi maṟi ñāṉacampantaṉ, mayilāṭutuṟiyip
puṇarnta tamiḻpattum _iciyāl _uriceyvār, peṟuvar, poṉṉulakamē.

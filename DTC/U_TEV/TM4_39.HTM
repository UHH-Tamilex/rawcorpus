• patikam: [4:39]
  • talam: tiru _aiyāṟu
  • (()): tiru nērici
  • VMS: Vol.4, p.165

*1
kuṇṭaṉāyc camaṇarōṭē kūṭi nāṉ koṇṭa mālit
tuṇṭaṉē! cuṭar koḷ cōtī! tū neṟi_āki niṉṟa
_aṇṭaṉē! _amarar_ēṟē! tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉē!
toṇṭaṉēṉ, toḻutu _uṉ pātam colli, nāṉ tirikiṉṟēṉē.

*2
pīli ki _iṭukki, nāḷum periyatu _ōr tavam _eṉṟu _eṇṇi,
vāliya taṟikaḷ pōla mati_ilār paṭṭatu _eṉṉē!
vāliyār vaṇaṅki _ēttum tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉōṭu
_āliyā _eḻunta neñcam _aḻakitā _eḻunta _āṟē!

*3
taṭṭu _iṭu camaṇarōṭē tarukki, nāṉ tavam _eṉṟu _eṇṇi,
_oṭṭiṭu maṉattiṉīrē! _ummi yāṉ ceyvatu _eṉṉē!
moṭṭu _iṭu kamalap poykit tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉōṭu
_oṭṭiṭum _uḷḷattīrē! _ummi nāṉ _ukantiṭṭēṉē.

*4
pācip palmācu meyyar palam _ilāc camaṇarōṭu
nēcattāl _irunta neñci nīkkum_āṟu _aṟiyamāṭṭēṉ;
tēcattār paravi _ēttum tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉi
vācattāl vaṇaṅka vallār valviṉi māyum_aṉṟē.

*5
kaṭup poṭi _aṭṭi meyyil, karuti _ōr tavam _eṉṟu _eṇṇi,
vaṭukkaḷōṭu _icinta neñcē! mati_ili paṭṭatu _eṉṉē!
maṭukkaḷil vāḷi pāyum tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉi
_aṭuttu niṉṟu _uṉṉu, neñcē! _aruntavam ceyta_āṟē!

*6
tuṟavi _eṉṟu _avam_atu _orēṉ; colliya colavu ceytu(v),
_uṟaviṉāl _amaṇarōṭum _uṇarvu_ilēṉ, _uṇarvu _oṉṟu _iṉṟi;
naṟavam _ār poḻilkaḷ cūḻnta tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉi
maṟavu _ilā neñcamē! nalmati _uṉakku _aṭinta_āṟē!

*7
pal_uric camaṇarōṭē palapala kālam_ellām
colliya colavu ceytēṉ; cōrvaṉ, nāṉ niṉintapōtu;
malliki malarum cōlit tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉi
_elliyum pakalum _ellām niṉintapōtu _iṉiya_āṟē!

*8
maṇ_uḷār viṇ_uḷārum vaṇaṅkuvār pāvam pōka,---
_eṇ _ilāc camaṇarōṭē _icintaṉi, _ēḻineñcē!
teṇnilā _eṟikkum ceṉṉit tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉik
kaṇṇiṉāl kāṇappeṟṟuk karutiṟṟē muṭinta_āṟē!

*9
kuruntam_atu _ocitta mālum, kulamalar mēviṉāṉum,
tiruntunal-tiruvaṭīyum tirumuṭi kāṇamāṭṭār;
_aruntava muṉivar _ēttum tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉip
porunti niṉṟu _uṉṉu, neñcē! poyviṉi māyum_aṉṟē.

*10
_aṟivu _ilā _arakkaṉ _ōṭi, _aruvari _eṭukkal_uṟṟu,
muṟukiṉāṉ; muṟukak kaṇṭu mūtaṟivāḷaṉ nōkki
niṟuviṉāṉ, ciṟuvira(l)lāl; nerintu pōy nilattil vīḻa,
_aṟiviṉāl _aruḷkaḷceytāṉ, tiru _aiyāṟu _amarnta tēṉē.

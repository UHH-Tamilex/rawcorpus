• patikam: [4:55]
  • talam: Tiru Valampuram
  • (()): tiru nērici
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.58

*1
teṇtiri tēṅki _ōtam ceṉṟu _aṭi vīḻuṅkāli,
toṇṭu _irittu _aṇṭarkōṉit toḻutu, _aṭi vaṇaṅki, _eṅkum
vaṇṭukaḷ matukkaḷ māntum valampurattu _aṭikaḷtammik
koṇṭu, nal kītam pāṭak kuḻakartām _irunta_āṟē!

*2
maṭukkaḷil vāḷi pāya vaṇṭu_iṉam _irinta poyki,
piṭik kaḷiṟu _eṉṉat tammil piṇi payiṉṟu _aṇi varālkaḷ---
toṭutta nal māli _ēntit toṇṭarkaḷ paravi _ētta,
vaṭittaṭaṅkaṇṇi pākar---valampurattu _irunta_āṟē!

*3
tēṉ _uṭi malarkaḷ koṇṭu tiruntu _aṭi poruntac cērtti,
_āṉ_iṭi_añcum koṇṭu _aṉpiṉāl _amara _āṭṭi,
vāṉ_iṭimatiyam cūṭum valampurattu _aṭikaḷtammi
nāṉ _aṭintu _ēttappeṟṟu, nalviṉip payaṉ _uṟṟēṉē.

*4
muḷi _eyiṟṟu _iḷa nal _ēṉam pūṇṭu, moycaṭikaḷ tāḻa,
vaḷi _eyiṟṟu _iḷiya nākam valittu _ari _iciya vīkki,
puḷikayap pōrvi pōrttu, puṉaloṭu matiyam cūṭi,
vaḷi payil _iḷiyar _ēttum valampurattu _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*5
curuḷ _uṟu variyiṉmēlāl-tuḷaṅku _iḷam paḷiṅku cinta,
_iruḷ _uṟu katir nuḻinta _iḷaṅ katirp pacalittiṅkaḷ---
_aruḷ _uṟum _aṭiyar_ellām _aṅkiyiṉ malarkaḷ _ēnta,
maruḷ _uṟu kītam kēṭṭār---valampurattu _aṭikaḷārē.

*6
niṉikkiṉṟēṉ, neñcutaṉṉāl,---nīṇṭa puṉcaṭiyiṉāṉē!---
_aṉittu_uṭaṉ koṇṭuvantu _aṅku _aṉpiṉāl _amiya _āṭṭi;
puṉi(k)kiṉṟēṉ, poymmitaṉṉi; meymmiyip puṇaramāṭṭēṉ;
_eṉakku nāṉ ceyvatu _eṉṉē, _iṉi?---valampuravaṉīrē!

*7
ceṅkayal cēlkaḷ pāyntu, tēm paḻam _iṉiya nāṭi,
tam kayam tuṟantu pōntu, taṭampoyki _aṭintu niṉṟu,
koṅkiyar kuṭiyuṅkālik koḻuṅ kaṉi _aḻuṅkiṉār _am
maṅkala maṉiyiṉ mikkār---valampurattu _aṭikaḷārē.

*8
_aruku_elām kuvaḷi, cennel, _akal _ili _āmpal, neytal;
teru_elām teṅku māviṉ paḻam viḻum, paṭappi_ellām
kuruku_iṉam kūṭi _āṅkē kummalittu _iṟaku _ulartti
maruval _ām _iṭaṅkaḷ kāṭṭum, valampurattu _aṭikaḷārē!

*9
karuvari _aṉiya mēṉik kaṭalvaṇṇaṉ_avaṉum kāṇāṉ;
tiru vari _aṉiya pūmēl-ticimukaṉ_avaṉum kāṇāṉ;
_oru vari _ucci _ēṟi _ōṅkiṉār, _ōṅki vantu(v)
_arumiyiṉ _eḷimi_āṉār_avar, valampuravaṉārē.

*10
vāḷ_eyiṟu _ilaṅka nakku vaḷar kayilāyamtaṉṉi
_āḷ vali karutic ceṉṟa _arakkaṉi varikkīḻ, _aṉṟu,
tōḷoṭu pattuvāyum tolintu _uṭaṉ_aḻunta _ūṉṟi,
_āṇmiyum valiyum tīrppār_avar---valampuravaṉārē.

[[vīraṭṭāṉam is a corruption of vīrastāṉam; it means the place where Civaṉ exhibited his valour.
They are eight in Tamiḻnāṭu: 1. Kaṇtiyur, 2. Kōvalūr, 3. Atiki, 4. Kuṟukki, 5. Kaṭavūr, 6. Paṟiyal, 7. Viṟkuti and 8. Vaḻuvūr.
The following old verse gives the names of the places and the valourous deeds that Civaṉ did in them;
pūmaṉ ciram kaṇṭi; antakaṉ kōval puram atikai; kāmaṉ kuṟukkai; namaṉ kaṭavūr; intakkāciṉiyil māmaṉ paṟiyal; calantaraṉ viṟkuṭi; māvaḻuvūr, tēmaṉṉu koṉṟaiyum tiṅkaḷum āṭataṉ cēvakamē]]

{3:8}__1+
{$} caṭai uṭaiyāṉum
one who has a caṭi
ney āṭalāṉum
who bathes in ghee
cari kōvaṇa uṭai (1) uṭaiyāṉum
who dresses in a loin-cloth that slips away a little,
mai ārnta oṇkaṇ umaikēḷvaṉum
who is the husband of Umi who has bright eyes on which collyrium is coated,
kaṭai uṭai nal neṭu māṭam ōṅkum kaṭavūr taṉṉuḷ viṭaiyuṭai aṇṇalum
and the god who has a bull and who is in Kaṭavūr where tall storeys rise high and which have beautiful entrances.
vīraṭṭāṉattu araṉ allaṉē
is he not Araṉ who is in Vīraṭṭāṉam?

{$} eri taruvār caṭaiyāṉum
one who has a long caṭi resembling fire;
[[ēṟṟu valaṉ uyariya eri maruḷ avircaṭai, māṟṟu aruṅkaṇicci maṇi miṭaṟṟōṉum- (puṟam, 56-1-2).]]
veḷḷai eḻutu ēṟiyum
one who rides on a white bull (ēṟi: is a noun).
puritaru māmalark koṉṟai mālai puṉaintu ētta
when mārkkaṇteyaṉ prised, adorning him with a garland of big koṉṟi flowers which he is fond cf.
kari taru kālaṉaic cāṭiṉāṉum
one who killed Kālaṉ who is black in colour. (This has special importance to this shrine as it was here that Civaṉ killed the god of death to save mārkaṇtēyaṉ)
kaṭavūrtaṉuḷ viritarutol pukaḻ vīraṭṭāṉattu araṉ allaṉē
is he not araṉ in vīraṭṭaṉam in Kaṭavūr of ancient fame that has spread everywhere?

{$} nātaṉum
one who is the master.
naḷḷiruḷ āṭiṉāṉum
who danced at midnight.
naḷir pōtiṉkaṇ pātaṉum
one who places his feet in the hearts of devotees which are like cool lotus.
pāypularit tōliṉāṉum

pacu vēṟiyum
who rides on a bull.
kātalar taṇkaṭavūriṉāṉum
one who dwells in cool Kaṭavūr where those who bestow their love on Civaṉ, live.
kalantu ēttavē
when all the devotees join together and prise him

is he not araṉ in vīraṭṭaṉam having learnt the vētams with their proper intonations? (ōti - ōtiṉavaṉ ; a noun.)
{3:8}__4+
{$} maḻu amar celvaṉum
the god who is fond of the battle-axe
mācu ilāta pal pūtam muṉ, muḻavu oli, yāḻ, kuḻal, montai koṭṭa mutukāṭṭiṭaik kaḻal vaḷar kāl kuñcittu āṭiṉāṉum
who danced by uprising and bending the left leg on which there is a Kaḻal, in the cremation ground in the presence of many spotless pūtams, when muḻavu, lute, flute and monti (a drum with one face) were played.
[[kuñcittu: kuñcittu acaintu acaintu- (Civakacintāmani, 34).]]

is he not araṉ in vīraṭṭāṉam where the bustle of festivals is increasing in Kaṭavūr?

{$} cuṭar maṇic cuṇṇaveṇṇīṟṟiṉāṉum
Civaṉ who wears bright beads of uruttirākkam and well-powered white holy ash.
cuḻalvu āyatu ōr paṭam maṇi nākam araikku acaitta paramēṭṭiyum
who is in the most exalted place and who tied a cobra with a hood and jewel, which form into a coil.
kaṭam aṇi māurit tōlaṉāṉum
and who covers himself with an elephant's skin flayed from it.
viṭam aṇikaṇṭaṉum
and the god who has a neck made beautiful by the poison.
kaṭavūr taṉṉuḷ vīraṭṭāṉattu araṉ allaṉē
is he not araṉ in Kaṭavūr vīraṭṭāṉam.

dancing, and singing the four vētams which are conspicious by their accent and intonation.
pala ūrkaḷ pōy

uṇpalikoṇṭu uḻalvāṉum
one who wanders receiving alms for food.
vāṉiṉ oḷi malkiya kaṇpoli neṟṟi veṇ tiṅkaḷāṉum
who has a white crescent whose brightness increases in the sky, located in the forehead where the eye is prominent.
veṇ poṭip pūciyum
and who has smeared the white holy ash.
kiṭavūr taṉṉuḷ vīraṭṭāṉattu araṉ allaṉē
is he not araṉ who dwells in vīraṭṭāṉam in Kaṭavūr?

{$} cevvaḻalāy nilaṉ āki niṉṟa civamūrttiyum
one who has a body full of auspiciousness and who is the red fire and the earth.
muvvaḻal, nāṉmaṟai, aintum āya muṉikēḷvaṉum
who is friendly towards the sages and who is the three fires, four vētams and five yākams.
kavvaḻalvāyk katanākam ārttāṉ
who tied the angry cobras which inject poison when it bites.
kaṭavūrtaṉuḷ vevvaḻal ēntukai vīraṭṭāṉattu araṉ allaṉē
is he not araṉ in kaṭavūr, vīraṭṭāṉam, who holds in his hand a hot fire?

{$} aṭi iraṇṭu, ōr uṭampu, aiññāṉku irupatu tōḷ, taca muṭiyuṭaivēntaṉai mūrkku aḻitta mutal mūrttiyum
the eminent Civaṉ who has a body and who destroyed the pride of the King (Irāvaṇaṉ) who has ten heads, twenty shoulders, single body and two feet.

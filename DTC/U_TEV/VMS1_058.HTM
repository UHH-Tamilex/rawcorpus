{1:58}__1+
{$} varikoḷ māmaṇi pōṟkaṇṭam kariyavaṉ tikaḻum karavīrattu em periyavaṉ kaḻal pēṇavē
to cherish with regard the feet of our great god who is in eminent Karavīram and who has a blue neck like the big sapphire which has streaks in it.
namviṉaiyuḷḷaṉa ācaṟa ariyum
that act will cut off entirely our Karmams that are still with us.
{1:58}__2+
{$} tāḻcaṭai mēlavaṉ
superior god who has hanging caṭi.
tiṅkaḷōṭu uṭaṉ cūṭiya kaṅkaiyāṉ tikaḻum karavīrattu emcaṅkaraṉ kaḻal cāravē
as we approach the feet of our god who does good and who is in eminent Karavīram, and who wears a crescent along with the river Kaṅki.
viṉaitaṅkumō
will our Karmam-s stay any longer?
{1:58}__3+
{$} pūtampalpaṭai ākkiya kātalāṉ
Civaṉ who created many armies of pūtam out of love towards them.
tikaḻum karavīrattu emnātaṉ pātam naṇukavē
to come near the feet of our master who is in eminent Karavīram.
ētamvantu aṭaiyā
sufferings will not come and reach us.
iṉinallaṉa
hereafter it will be all good things.
{1:58}__4+
{$} māmaṇi pōṟkaṇṭam kaṟaiyavaṉ tikaḻum karavīrattu em iṟaiyavaṉ kaḻal ētta
when we prise the feet of our master who is in eminent Karavīram and who has a black neck like the big sapphire.
namviṉai uḷḷaṉa pāḻpaṭap paṟaiyum
our acts that are still with us will vanish completely without any trace of them.
{1:58}__5+
{$} paṇṇiṉ ār maṟai pāṭalaṉ
Civaṉ sings the vētams which are full of music
[[iṉ: is an augmentation used in combination of nouns and case endings where it is not necessary; paṇārmaṟai is enough]]
āṭalaṉ
performs dance.
viṇṇiṉ ār matil eyta mukkaṇṇiṇāṉ
god who has three eyes and who shot an arrow on the forts which were flying in the sky.
uṟaiyum karavīrattai naṇṇuvārviṉai nācamē
the karmam-s of those who draw near Karavīram where he dwells, will be destroyed.
{1:58}__6+
{$} niḻaliṉ ārmati (1) cūṭiyanīḷcaṭai aḻaliṉār
Civaṉ has a long caṭi which is like fire on which he wears a shining crescent
[[PP: caṭi compared to fire,
ēṟṟuvalaṉ uyariya erimaruḷ avircaṭai
(Puṟam, 56-1)]]
ār (2) aḻal ēntiya (3) kaḻaliṉār uṟaiyum karavīrattait toḻavallārkku illai tukkamē
those who are able to worship with joined hands Karavīram where Civaṉ who wears Kaḻal and holds a fire of unbearable heat, will not experience any sorrow.
[[Variant readings: (1) cūṭiṉaṉ nīḷcaṭai (2) aṉal (3) kaḻaliṉāṉ]]
{1:58}__7+
{$} vaṇṭar mummatil māytara eytavaṉ
Civaṉ shot an arrow to destroy the three forts of wicked acurar.
aṇṭaṉ
tēvan
āraḻalpōl oḷirkaṇṭaṉār
warrior whose body glitters like the unbearable fire.
uṟaiyum karavīrattut toṇṭarmēl tuyar tūramē
the sufferings of the devotees who are in Karavīram where he dwells, will go far away from them.
{1:58}__8+
{$} puṉal ilaṅkaiyarkōṉ muṭipattu iṟac ciṉaval āṇmai cekuttavaṉ
Civaṉ destroyed the valour, which is the cuse of being angry with his enemies, of the King of the people of Ilaṅki to crush his ten heads.
[[ciṉaval the state of being angry;]]
[[PP_EXT: ciṉaval ōmpumati
(Kuṟuntoki, 362, 2)]]
[[PP_EXT: ciṉaval pōkiya puṉkaṇ mālai
(Akam, 365-3)]]
[[PP_EXT: ciṉaval ōmpumiṉ
(Puṟam, 292-4)]]
kaṉalava(ṉ)ṉ uṟaikiṉṟa karavīram eṉa vallārkku iṭar illaiyē
there will be definitely no sufferings to those who say Karavīṟam where Civaṉ who is like fire, dwells.
{1:58}__9+
{$} veḷḷat tāmaraiyāṉoṭu mālumāyt teḷḷat tīttiraḷ ākiya kaḷḷattāṉ
Civaṉ who practised deception by becoming a mass of fire to be examined by Piramaṉ who is seated in a lotus that grows in water and Māl.
uṟaiyum karavīrattai uḷḷat (tāṉ) viṉai ōyumē
the Karmams will come to an end if we meditate upon Karavīram where Civaṉ dwells.
[[tāṉ: expletive]]
{1:58}__10+
{$} ceṭi ama(ṇ)ṇoṭu cīvarattāravar koṭiya vevvurai (1) koḷḷēṉmiṉ
(Devotees!) Do not accept the very cruel words of the amaṇar of bad odour and buddhists who cover their bodies with a garment called cīVaram
[[ama(ṇ)ṇoṭu the letter within brackets is augmention.]]
kaṭiyavaṉ uṟaikiṉṟa karavīrattu aṭiyavarkku illai allal
there will be no distress for the humble servants of Civaṉ who protects all, and dwells in Karavīram.
[[Variant reading: (1)koḷḷaṉmiṉ)]]
{1:58}__11+
{$} vīṭu ilāṉviḷaṅkum karavīrattu emcēṭaṉmēl
on the great god who is naturally free from all bondage and dwells in Karavīram
[[vīṭu may also mean death]]
kacivāl tamiḻ(1) nāṭu ñāṉacampantaṉa col ivai pāṭuvārkku illai pāvamē
there will be no sins with those who sing these verses composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who made researches in Tamiḻ, with a tender heart.
[[Variant reading: (1) nāṭi]]

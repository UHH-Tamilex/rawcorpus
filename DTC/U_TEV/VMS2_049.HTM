{2:49}__1+
{$} paṇṇiṉ nērmoḻi maṅkaimār palar pāṭi āṭiya ōcai nāḷtoṟum
the sound of the dance when many young girls whose words are as sweet as the melody and sing, daily.
kaṇṇiṉ nērayalē poliyum kaṭaṟkāḻi
in Kāḻi girt by the sea where they abound before one's eyes.
peṇṇiṉ ēr oru paṅkuṭaip perumāṉai emperumāṉ eṉṉu eṉṟu uṉṉum (1) aṇṇalār aṭiyār
the holy slaves of the god, who think very often of the god who has a beautiful lady on his half as our God.
aruḷālum kuṟaivu ilar
are not wanting not only in his grace but also in other worldly wealth which is necessary for enjoying the pleasures of this world.
[[Variant reading (1) aṇṇalāṉ]]
{2:49}__2+
{$} moṇṭu
the clouds having drawn water;
alampiya vār tiraik kaṭal
the sea of long waves which make a big sound.
mōti mītu eṟi caṅka vaṅkamum
makes the conches and ships which are dashed and thrown on the shore.
kaṇṭal (am) puṭaicūḻ vayal cēr kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle, reach the fields which are surrounded on all sides by mangroves.
vaṇṭu alampiya koṉṟaiyāṉ aṭivāḻtti ēttiya māntar tamviṉai
the sins of people who praised and blessed the feet of the god wearing koṉṟai flowers on which bees hum.
viṇṭal aṅku eḷitām; atu nalvitiām
Leaving them is easy; that is the golden rule.
{2:49}__3+
{$} nāṭu elām oḷieyta nallavar naṉṟum ēttivaṇaṅkuvār poḻiṟ, kāṭu elām malar tēṉ tuḷikkum kaṭaṟkāḻi
in Kāḻi which is in proximity to the sea, where in all the dense gardens flowers give honey in drops and which has good people who worship and praise the god to make the whole of the country shine with fame.
tōṭu ulāviya kātu uḷāy! curicaṅka veṇkuḻaiyāy! eṉṟu eṉṟu uṉṉum vēṭaṅkoṇṭavarkaḷ
the ascetics who are dressed like Civaṉ think of god without ceasing as one who has on one ear a lady's ear-ring moving and a mens' ear-ring made of spiralling conch on the other.
viṉai nīṅkaluṟṟārē
their sins will take leave of them of their own accord. (The past tense is used for certainty).
{2:49}__4+
{$} maiyiṉ ārpoḻil cūḻa
as the parks on which clouds rest.
nīḻalil vācam ār matumalka
in the shade the fragrant honey increases in quantity. $$
nāḷ toṟum kaiyiṉ ār malar koṇṭu eḻuvār kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the devotees start full of flowers in their hands, to worship the god daily.
aiyaṉē araṉē' eṉṟu ātarittu
treating with regard saying, Master, Araṉ!
ōti nītiyuḷē niṉaippavar
and those who think with right conduct uttering those names.
uyyumāṟu ulakil uyarntāriṉ uḷḷārē
are among those exalted in virtue to save themselves from the cycle of birth and death.
{2:49}__5+
{$} malikaṭuntirai mēl (1) nimirntu etirvantuvantu oḷir nittilamviḻa
as the shining pearls fall coming very often crowded on the quickly flowing waves.
kalikaṭintakaiyār maruvum kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the donors who completely remove the proverty of supplicants.
valiyakālaṉai vīṭṭi māṇitaṉ iṉṉuyir aḷittāṉai vāḻttiṭa
if one praises the god who protected the previous life of the piramacāri, killing the strong kālaṉ (God of death)
tī viṉai nōy avaimeliyum
his sins and diseases will perish.
vīṭumēvuvār
and will attain eternal bliss.
[[Variant reading: (1) nirantu]]
{2:49}__6+
{$} maṟṟum ivvulakattuḷōrkaḷum vāṉuḷōrkaḷum vantu vaikalum kaṟṟa cintaiyarāyk karutum kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the people of this world and celestial beings come and meditate daily, with a mind, having learnt to worship god.
neṟṟimēl amar kaṇṇiṉāṉai niṉaintiruntu icai pāṭuvār viṉai ceṟṟa māntar eṉac cintaiyuḷ teḷimiṉkaḷ
people of this world! Be clear in your minds that those who sing the fame with music meditating on the god who has a frontal eye, are people who have destroyed their sins.
{2:49}__7+
{$} tāṉ nalampurai vētiyaroṭu takka mātavartām toḻappayil kāṉaliṉ viraicēr vimmu kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the fragrance of the sea-shore grove combines with others and increases and where the worthy great ascetics gather together along with brahmins of good qualities and eminence.
ūṉum āruyir vāḻkkaiyāy uṟavāki niṉṟa oruvaṉē eṉṟu
praising the god as the unequalled god who is the relation and the precious life that exists in the body.
āṉ nalam koṭuppār aruḷvēntar āvārē
those who give the five products from the cow will be kings in the matter of possessing the god's grace.
{2:49}__8+
{$} maittavaṇṭueḻu cōlai ālaikaḷ cālicēr vayal ār
to spread over the fields of red variety of paddy and sugar-canes, and gardens from which black bees rise after drinking honey.
kattuvārkaṭal ceṉṟu ulavum kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the long and roaring sea moves about.
attaṉē! araṉē! arakkaṉai aṉṟu aṭarttu ukantāy! uṉakaḻal pattarāypparavum payaṉ īṅku nalkāyē
Bestow on me the benefit of praising as a devotees your feet saying Father' araṉ! one who became great by crushing the arakkaṉ in the distant past!; in this birth itself.
{2:49}__9+
{$} parumarāmoṭu teṅku paiṅkatalip paruṅkaṉikaḷ mantikaḷ uṇṇa
when the female monkeys are eating the big fruits of the green plaintain, coconut palm and big common cadamba.
tiruviṉ nāyakaṉ āya māloṭu ceyya māmalarc celvaṉ ākiya iruvar kāṇpu ariyāṉ eṉa ēttutal iṉpamē
It gives pleasure to praise the god as one who could not be found out by both the Māl who is the husband of Tiru (Lakshmi) and the god who is seated in the big and red (lotus) flower.
{2:49}__10+
{$} piṇṭam uṇṭu uḻalvārkaḷum
the jains who roam about eating balls of cooked rice.
piriyātu vaṇtukil āṭai pōrttavar
and the buddhists who always cover their bodies with a yellow robe without removing it.
kaṇṭucērakilār
have not the capacity to reach Civaṉ realising his greatness.
aḻakuār kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle and much beauty.
toṇṭaivāy umaiyōṭum kūṭiya vēṭaṉē! cuṭalaippoṭi aṇi aṇṭavāṇaṉ! eṉpārkku allal (tāṉ) aṭaiyā
suffering will not reach those who praise god as, Hunter who is united with Umai whose lips are like the fruits of the common creeper of the hedge! Resident of the universe who smears the ash in the cremation ground!.
{2:49}__11+
{$} (1) peyar eṉum ivai paṉṉiraṇṭiṉum uṇṭu eṉappeyar peṟṟa ūr tikaḻ, kayal ulām vayal cūḻntu aḻakār kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle which is full of beauty as it is surrounded by fields in which carps move about; which is eminent by the fact of its having these twelve names among which Kāḻi is included.
nayaṉnaṭaṉkaḻal ētti
praising the feet of the god who is the right conduct and dancer.
vāḻttiya ñāṉacampantaṉ centamiḻ urai uyarumā(ṟu) moḻivār ulakattu uyarntārē
in this world those are eminent people who will recite the verses done in chaste Tamiḻ by ñāṉacampantaṉ, which are in the form of benediction.

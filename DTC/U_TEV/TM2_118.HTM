• patikam: [2:118]
  • talam: tirut tilataippati
  • paṇ: cevvaḻi
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.190

*1
poṭikaḷ pūcip palatoṇṭar kūṭi, pular kālaiyē,
_aṭikaḷ _ārat toḻutu, _ētta niṉṟa(v) _aḻakaṉ(ṉ) _iṭam---
koṭikaḷ _ōṅkik kulavum viḻavu _ār tilataippati,
vaṭi koḷ cōlai(m) malar maṇam kamaḻum matimuttamē.

*2
toṇṭar miṇṭi, pukai vimmu cāntum kamaḻ tuṇaiyalum
koṇṭu, kaṇṭār kuṟippu _uṇara niṉṟa kuḻakaṉ(ṉ) _iṭam---
teṇtiraip pūmpuṉal _aricil cūḻnta tilataippati,
vaṇṭu keṇṭu_uṟṟu _icai payilum cōlai(m) matimuttamē.

*3
_aṭal _uḷ _ēṟu _uyttu _ukantāṉ, _aṭiyār _amarar toḻak
kaṭaluḷ nañcam _amutu_āka _uṇṭa kaṭavuḷ(ḷ), _iṭam---
tiṭal _aṭaṅkac ceḻuṅ kaḻaṉi cūḻnta tilataippati,
maṭaluḷ vāḻaikkaṉi tēṉ piliṟṟum matimuttamē.

*4
kaṅkai, tiṅkaḷ, vaṉṉi, tuṉ _erukki(ṉ)ṉoṭu, kūviḷam,
veṅ kaṇ nākam, viricaṭaiyil vaitta vikirtaṉ(ṉ) _iṭam---
ceṅkayal pāy puṉal _aricil cūḻnta tilataippati,
maṅkul tōyum poḻil cūḻntu _aḻaku _ār matimuttamē.

*5
puravi_ēḻum maṇi pūṇṭu _iyaṅkum koṭittēriṉāṉ
paravi niṉṟu vaḻipāṭu ceyyum paramēṭṭi _ūr---
viravi ñāḻal, viri kōṅku, vēṅkai, curapuṉṉaikaḷ,
maravam,mavval, malarum tilatai(m) matimuttamē.

*6
viṇṇar, vētam virittu _ōta vallār, _orupākamum
peṇṇar, _eṇṇār _eyil ceṟṟu _ukanta perumāṉ, _iṭam---
teṇnilāviṉ(ṉ) _oḷi tīṇṭu cōlait tilataippati,
maṇ_uḷār vantu _aruḷ pēṇi niṉṟa(m) matimuttamē.

*7
_āṟucūṭi, _aṭaiyār puram ceṟṟavar, poṟṟoṭi
kūṟu cērum _uruvarkku _iṭam_āvatu kūṟuṅkāl---
tēṟal _ārum poḻil cūḻntu _aḻaku _ār tilataippati,
māṟu _ilā vaṇ puṉal _aricil cūḻnta(m) matimuttamē.

*8
kaṭuttu vanta kaṉamēṉiyiṉāṉ, karuvaraitaṉai
_eṭuttavaṉtaṉ muṭitōḷ _aṭarttārkku _iṭam_āvatu---
puṭaik koḷ pūkattu _iḷam pāḷai pulkum matup pāya, vāy-
maṭuttu manti _ukaḷum tilatai(m) matimuttamē.

*9
paṭam koḷ nākattu_aṇaiyāṉum, paintāmaraiyiṉmicai
_iṭam koḷ nālvētaṉum, _ētta niṉṟa _iṟaivaṉ _iṭam---
tiṭam koḷ nāviṉ(ṉ) _icai toṇṭar pāṭum tilataippati,
maṭaṅkal vantu vaḻipāṭu ceyyum matimuttamē.

*10
puttartērar, poṟi _il camaṇarkaḷum,---vīṟu _ilāp
pittar---coṉṉa(m) moḻi kēṭkilāta perumāṉ _iṭam---
pattar, cittar, paṇivu_uṟṟu _iṟaiñcum tilataippati,
mattayāṉai vaḻipāṭu ceyyum matimuttamē.

*11
mantam _ārum poḻil cūḻ tilatai(m) matimuttarmēl,
kantam _ārum kaṭal kāḻi _uḷḷāṉ---tamiḻ ñāṉacam-
pantaṉ---mālai, paḻi tīra niṉṟu _ētta vallārkaḷ, pōyc
cintaiceyvār, civaṉ cēvaṭi cērvatu tiṇṇamē.

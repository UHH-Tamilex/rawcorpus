Note: This shrine is known by the name of Harittuvāra Maṅkalam.

{3:30}__1+
{$} paitta pāmpu araik kōvaṇat tōṭu pāypuli
wearing in the waist a cobra with spreaded hood, men's loin-cloth and the skin of a pouncing tiger.
moytta pēykaḷ muḻakkam(m) mutukāṭṭiṭai nittamāka(n) naṭam āṭi
dancing dily in the old cremation ground while the pēy-s which crowd round, play on instruments in a high pitch.
[[(m, n are augmentations for rhythm.]]
veṇṇīṟu aṇi pittar kōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple of Civaṉ who adorns himself with white sacred ash.
[[pāḻi means cavern, as the Civaliṅkam, the presiding deity is known by the name of pataḷecurar, this temple should have got its name Aratipperumpāḻi]]
{3:30}__2+
{$} kayala cēla karuṅ kaṇṇiyar nāḷtoṟum payalai koḷḷap pali tērntu uḻal pāṉmaiyār
Civaṉ who has the nature of wandering to receive alms dily while the ladies with black eyes, which are like the carp and the carnatic carp, have their complexion turning sallow (R).
iyalai vāṉōr niṉaintōrkaḷukku eṇ arum peyarar kōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple of Civaṉ who has innumerable names to count even for the celestials who fix their thoughts on his nature
[[niṉaintōrkaḷ ākiya vāṉōrkkum]]
{3:30}__3+
{$} kōṭal cāla(v) uṭaiyār
Civaṉ has the habit of receiving alms in large quantities.
kolaiyāṉaiyiṉ mūṭal cāla(v) uṭaiyār
covers himself tightly with the skin of an elephant which is capable of killing.
muḷi kāṉiṭai āṭal cāla(v) uṭaiyār
dances very much in the scorched cremation ground.
aḻaku ākiya pīṭarkōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple if the great and beautiful god, Civaṉ.
[[The letter v in the first three lines are augmentation for `rhythm']]
{3:30}__4+
{$} maṇṇar, nīrār, aḻalār mali kāliṉār, viṇṇar
Civaṉ is the earth, water, fire, air which is found everywhere and the sky.
vētam meyp poruḷ virittu ōtuvar
will instruct his disciples about the true meaning of the Vētam-s explining them.
paṇṇar
is music itself.
pāṭal uṭaiyār
has the nature of singing.
oru pākamum peṇṇar kōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple of Civaṉ who has on one half completely a lady.
{3:30}__5+
{$} vāyiṉ moḻi maṟaiyar
the words that come out of Civaṉ's mouth is Vētam.
māṉoṭu, veṇmaḻu, kaṟai koḷ cūlam (m) uṭaikkaiyar
holds in his hands a deer, a sharp battle-axe and a trident which has stins.
[[veṇmaḻu a white hot iron is another meaning.]]
kār ār tarum naṟai koḷ koṉṟai (1) nayantār
desired the koṉṟi flowers which have honey (fragrance) and blossom profusely in riny season.
ceṉṉimēl tarum piṟaiyarkōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple of Civaṉ who placed on his head a crescent.
[[Variant reading: (1) nayantūrtarum]]
{3:30}__6+
{$} puṟṟu aravam, pulittōl araik kōvaṇam taṟṟu
wearing tightly on the waist a cobra that lives in the anthill, a tiger`s skin, and a men's loin-cloth.
iravil naṭam āṭuvar
will dance at night.
tāḻtaru cuṟṟu amar pāriṭam
the army of pūtam which bow before him will be surrounding him
[[amar is used in the place (R) of a finite verb like nēr in the Kuṟaḷ:
uṟuvatu cīrtūkkum naṭpum peṟuvatu, koḷvārum kaḷvarum nēr (813), which is used in the sense of nērvar]]
tolkoṭiyiṉ micaip peṟṟar kōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple of Civaṉ who has on his ancient flag the figure of a bull.
{3:30}__7+
{$} am curi caṅku tuṇai iṟuttu
cusing the beautiful spiraling two conches to stay in the ear.
amar veṇpoṭi (pūci)
smearing himself with the white sacred ash which he desires so.
iṇai il ēṟṟai ūrntu ēṟuvarum
Civaṉ who rides on a peerless bull with joy,
[[um is expletive.]]
eri kaṇaiyiṉāl muppuram ceṟṟavar
and who destroyed the three cites with an arrow spitting fire.
kaiyiṉil piṇaiyar kōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple of Civaṉ who holds in his hand a deer.
{3:30}__8+
{$} carivu ilā val arakkaṉ taṭantōḷ talai nerivil āra(v) aṭarttār
Civaṉ pressed completely by crushing the head and big shoulders of the strong arakkaṉ who never yielded in battles hiterto.
neṟi meṉ kuḻal arivai pākam amarntār
desired as a half a lady who has soft and curly tresses of hir.
aṭiyāroṭum pirivu ilāta kōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple in which Civaṉ is never separated from his devotees.
{3:30}__9+
{$} vari arā eṉpu aṇi mārpiṉar
Civaṉ wears on his chest a cobra with lines, and bones.
nīr malkum eri arāvum caṭaimēṟ piṟai ēṟṟavar
put on a crescent on his cati which is like fire and which is full of water.
kariya mālōṭu ayaṉ kāṇpu aritu ākiya periyar kōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
Aratipperumpāḻi is the temple of the great one whom Ayaṉ along with black Māl found it difficult to have a vision of.
{3:30}__10+
{$} nāṇ ilāta camaṇ cākkiyar nāḷtoṟum ēṇ ilāta(m) moḻiya(v) eḻilāyavar
Civaṉ who rose in esteem though camaṇar and cākkiyar who had no sense of shame spoke words which have no strength of arguments.
[[eḻil - eḻucci) rising in esteem;]]
[[PP: nuṇ māṇ nuḻai pulam illāṉ eḻil nalam (Kuṟaḷ 407)]]
cēṇ ulām mummatil tī eḻac ceṟṟavar
destroyed by making fire rise in the three forts which wandered in the sky.
pēṇu kōyil arataipperumpāḻiyē
aratirupperumpāḻi is the temple desired by him.
{3:30}__11+
{$} nīriṉ ār puṉcaṭai nimalaṉukku iṭam eṉappāriṉār paravu arataipperumpāḻiyai
about Aratipperumpāḻi which the people of this world prise as the place of the immaculate Civaṉ who has on his ruddy cati water.
cīriṉ ār kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ cey ēriṉ ār tamiḻvallārkku illai(ām) pāvamē
there will be no sins to those who can recite the beautiful tamiḻ verses composed by ñāṉcampantaṉ in famous Kāḻi
[[All the four iṉ are unnecessary augmentation used in combination, as of nouns and case endings, in which they are absolutely necessary (nīrār, pārār cīrār, ērār will do).]]

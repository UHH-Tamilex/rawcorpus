{1:51}__1+
{$} cōpuram mēyavaṉē
Civaṉ who dwells in Cōpuram!
kaṅkaiyoṭu tiṅkaḷ cūṭi
having worn the crescent along with the Kaṅki.
kaṭikamaḻum koṉṟait toṅkalāṉē
God who wears a garland of koṉṟi which spreads fragrance!
tūya nīṟṟāy
who smears pure sacred ash!
veṅkaṇ āṇai īruri pōrttu
covering yourself with the skin of an elephant of cruel eyes, wet with blood.
(1) viḷaṅku moḻi maṅkaipākam vaittu ukanta māṇpu (atu) eṉṉai (kol, ām)
what is the excellence of having a lady of clear words on one half and becoming great.
[[Variant reading: (1) viḷaṅkumeḻil]]

[[see 1st verse]]
kaṭai uyarnta mummatilum kāyntu aṉaluḷ(ḷ) aḻuntat (1) toṭai nekiḻnta veñcilaiyāy
Civaṉ who has a cruel bow from which the bow-string became loose to shoot an arrow to being angry with all the three forts which had high entrances to be engulfed by fire.
viṭai amarntu
seated on a bull.
veṇ maḻu oṉṟu ēnti
holding a sharp battle-axe in the hand.
virintu ilaṅku caṭai oṭuṅkattaṇpuṉalait tāṅkiyatu eṉṉai (kol, ām)
what is the reason for bearing the cool water of Kaṅki to be concealed in the shining caṭi which is loosened.
[[Variant reading: (1) toṭai nikaḻnta]]

pāyum veḷḷai ēṟṟai ēṟi
riding upon a white bull which gallops.
pāy pulittōl (1) uṭutta tūya veḷḷai nīṟṟiṉāṉē
god the smears pure white sacred ash and who dresses in a skin of a tiger which pounces on its prey.

tīyar āya val arakkar centaḻaluḷ aḻuntaccāya eytu
discharging an arrow to make the strong arakkar who were cruel, to sink in the red fire and perish.
vāṉavarait tāṅkiyatu eṉṉai (kol, ām)
what is the reason for supporting the celestials?
[[Variant reading: (1) uṭuttut]]

villai veṉṟanuṇpuruvavēl neṭuṅkaṇṇiyoṭum tollai ūḻi āki niṉṟāy
Civaṉ who existed from very ancient times many aeons along with a lady who has sharp eyebrows like the bow and long eyes resembling vēl.
palil ōṭukaiyil ēnti
holding a skull without teeth.
palkaṭaiyum pali tērntu
collecting alms in many entrances of houses.
allal vāḻkkai mēlatu āṉa ātaravu eṉṉai (kol, ām)
what is the reason for your love for the life of distress?

ūṟṟam mikka kālaṉ taṉṉai olka utaittaruḷi
kicking the god of death who is very enthusiastic in his duty, to grow weak.
tōṟṟam (um) īṟum āki niṉṟāy
you were permanent, being the beginning and end of all things.
nāṟṟam mikka koṉṟai tuṉṟu ceñcaṭaimēl matiyam ēṟṟam āka vaittu ukanta kāraṇam eṉṉai (kol, ām)
what is the reason for placing on the red caṭi in which koṉṟi flowers of strong fragrance are close, a crescent to grant it distinction, and feeling happy on account of that?

aṉṉam aṉṉa meṉṉaṭaiyāḷ pākam amarntu araicēr tuṉṉa vaṇṇa āṭaiyiṉāy
Civaṉ who has a man's loin-cloth which is bright in colour, and desiring on one half a lady who has gentle git like the swan?
koṉ naviṉṟa mū ilai vēlkūr maḻuvāṭpaṭaiyaṉ
holding a trident of three blades which has greatness.
[[koṉ may also mean fear; striking fear into the minds of enemies]]

what is the reason for appearing elegant by wearing a garland of koṉṟi which is superior to gold in colour?
[[Variant reading: (1) cūṭupoṟpu]]

vaṟṟal āmai vāḷ aravam pūṇṭu, ayaṉ veṇṭalaiyil tuṟṟal āṉa koḷkaiyāṉē
Civaṉ who has an idea of eating from the white skull of Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ), adorning yourself with a dried tortoise shell and a cruel cobra?
kuṟṟam iṉmai uṇmai nī eṉṟu uṉ aṭiyār paṇivār
your devotees will bow before you thinking that you are the good qualities and faults.
kaṟṟa kēḷvi ñāṉamāṉa kāraṇam eṉṉai (kol, ām)
what is the reason for yourself shining as the education, listening to words of wisdom spoken by learned men, and spiritual wisdom for those devotees?
[[Variant reading: (1) kaṟṟal kēḷvi]]

{$} cōlai mikka taṉvayal cūḻ cōpuram mēyavaṉai
on Civaṉ who is in Cōpuram surrounded by a large number of cool fields and gardens.
cīlam mikka tol pukaḻ ār cirapurakkōṉ nalattāṉ (1) ñālam mikka taṇ tamiḻāṉ ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa kōlam mikka mālaivallār
those who are able to recite the exceedingly beautiful garlands of Tamiḻ verses composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ, who has good knowledge of refined Tamiḻ which is superior to others in this world, who has good qualities, who is the chief of Cirapuram which has ancient fame born out of good conduct.
kūṭuvar vāṉ ulaku (ē)
will reach heaven.
[[Variant reading: (1) ñāṉam]]

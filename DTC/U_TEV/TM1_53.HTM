*3

*4

_ūṭu kāṇa, mūṭum veḷḷattu _uyarntatu---mutukuṉṟē.

*5

*6

*7

*8

*9
_uṟi koḷ kiyar, cīvarattar, _uṇṭu _uḻal miṇṭal colli
neṟikaḷ _eṉṉa niṉivu_uṟātē nittalum kitoḻumiṉ!

muṟi koḷ mēṉi maṅki paṅkaṉ, mēyatu---mutukuṉṟē.

*10

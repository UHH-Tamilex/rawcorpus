{1:133}__1+
{$} venta veṇpoṭiya pūcum mārpiṉ virinūl orupāl porunta
the sacred thread which is loose to come into contact with the chest on one side in which the well-burnt white sacred ash is smeared.
kantam malku kuḻaliyoṭum kaṭipoḻiṟ kaccitaṉṉuḷ
in Kacci which has fragrant gardens; along with the lady having tresses of hir of increasing fragrance.
antam il kuṇattār (avar) pōṟṟa aṇaṅkiṉoṭu āṭalpuri entai mēviya ēkampam toḻutu ētta
if one prises and worships with joined hands ēkampam where our father performing dance with the divine damsel, Umi, dwells with desire.
iṭarkeṭum
sufferings will perish. (Kacci is the name of the place; ēkampam is the name of the temple).
{1:133}__2+
{$} varam tikaḻum avuṇar mānakar mūṉṟu uṭaṉ māyntu aviya
all the three big cities of the avuṇar who were eminent having received boons, to perish by ceasing to exist.
karam turantu eri ceyta tāḻcaṭaic caṅkaraṉ mēya iṭam
the place where cankaraṉ who has a hanging caṭi, and who burnt them by discharging an arrow, dwells with desire.
kuruntam mallikai kōṅku mātavi nallakurā maravam tiruntu paimpoḻil kacci ēkampam cēra iṭar keṭumē
suffering will perish if one reaches Ekampam in Kacci which has verdant gardens in which the wild-lime tree, jasmine creeper, common cung, common delight of the woods, good bottle-flower trees and Katampa trees are in a flourishing condition.
{1:133}__3+
{$} vaṇṇa veṇpoṭip pūcum mārpiṉ vara aravam puṉaintu
adorning the chest which is smeared with white sacred ash, with cobras with lines on their bodies.
peṇ amarntu eriāṭal pēṇiya piññakaṉ
Civaṉ who wears a peacock's feather and desires a lady on one half and dances in the fire.
mēya iṭam
the place he desires.
viṇ amar (1) neṭumāṭam ōṅki viḷaṅkiya kacci taṉṉuḷ
in Kacci which is eminent by tall storeys rising high so as to touch the sky.
tiṇṇamām poḻil cūḻnta ēkampam
is ēkampam surrounded by strong mango-groves.
cēra iṭar keṭum
if we approach that shrine our sufferings will perish.
[[Variant reading (1) māṭam ōṅku viḷaṅkiya kacci]]
{1:133}__4+
{$} mālai veṇmati tōyum māmatiṟ kacci mānakaruḷ
in the great city of Kacci which has high fortified walls with which the white moon which rises in the evening seems to come into contact.
ēlam nāṟiya cōlaicūḻ ēkampam
ēkampam surrounded by parks where cardamon spreads its fragrance.
tōlum nūlum tutainta varaimārpil cuṭalai veṇṇīṟu aṇintu
having smeared the white sacred ash in the cremation ground on the chest which is as strong as the mountin and in which the skin and a sacred thread are close to each other.
kālaṉ māḷvuṟak kālāl kāynta kaṭavuḷ karutum iṭam
is the place which the god who became angry with the god of death and cused his death kicking him with his leg.
ētta iṭar keṭumē
to prise that temple suffering will perish.
{1:133}__5+
{$} tōṭu aṇi(m) malarkkoṉṟai cērca�ṭut tūmatiyam puṉaintu
adorning his caṭi to which koṉṟi flowers with beautiful petals are attached with a spotless crescent.
pāṭal nāṉmaṟai āka
his songs being the four vētams.
palkaṇappēykaḷ avai cūḻa
the pēys of many groups to surround him.
vāṭal veṇtalaiyōṭu aṉal ēnti
holding fire along with a dried white skull.
makiḻntu āṭalpuri cēṭar cēr kalik kacci ēkampam cēra iṭar keṭumē
sufferings will perish if one reaches ēkampam in Kacci of bustle where the youth who dances out of joy, dwells.
{1:133}__6+
{$} cākam poṉ varaiyākat tāṉavar mummatil cāya eytu
discharging an arrow to destroy the three forts of the tēṉaVar, using the golden mēru as the bow.
ākam peṇ orupākam āka aravoṭu nūl aṇintu
placing a lady on one half of the body, and wearing a sacred thread along with cobras.
mākam tōy maṇimāṭa māmatiṟ kacci mānakaruḷ
in the great city of Kacci which has big fortified walls and beautiful storeys which come into contact with the sky.
ēkampat tuṟai īcaṉ cēvaṭi ētta iṭar keṭumē
sufferings will perish by prising the red-lotus feet of the Lord of the universe who dwells in ēkampam.

{$} vāḷ nilā matipulku ceñcaṭai vāḷ aravam aṇintu
adorning with a bright cobra in the red caṭi to which is attached a crescent of bright light.
nāṇ iṭattiṉ il vāḻkkai pēṇi
desiring a lady who has the quality of bashfulness, in the left half, for the house-holder's life.
nakutalaiyil pali tērntu
collecting alms in a lughing skull.
ēṇ ilā arakkaṉ taṇ niḷmuṭi pattum iṟuttavaṉ ūr
the place of the Lord who smashed all the ten tall crowns of the arakkaṉ who had no firmness of mind.
cēṇ ulām poḻil kacci ēkampam cēra iṭar keṭumē
sufferings will perish if one approaches ēkampam in Kacci which has gardens which seem to reach the sky.

{$} piramaṉum tirumālum kaitoḻap pēr aḻal āṭiya pemmāṉ
the god who assumed the form of a big fire to be worshipped with joined hands by Piramaṉ and tirumāl.
aravam cērcaṭai antaṇaṉ aṇaṅkiyaṉoṭu amarum iṭam
the place where Civaṉ the brahmin who has on his caṭi cobras stays with desie with a divine damsel.
karavu il vaṇkaiyiṉārkaḷ vāḻkalik kacci mānakaruḷ
in the great city of Kacci of bustle where people of liberality who do not conceal what they can give.
maravam cūḻpoḻil ēkampam toḻa
if one worships with joined hands ēkampam surrounded by gardens which have Kaṭampa trees.
valviṉai māyntu aṟumē
the irresistible acts will vanish and perish completely.
VMS10 / {1:133}__PIFI9+
{$} kuṇṭu paṭṭu amaṇ āyavaroṭum kūṟai tammeypōrkkum miṇṭar kaṭṭiya ṉaṭṭurai avaikoṇṭu virumpēṉmiṉ
(people of this world!) Do not desire the falsehood fabricated by the strong buddhists who cover their bodies with a blanket along with the low amaṇar.
viṇṭavar puram mūṉṟum veṅkaṇai oṉṟiṉāl aviyak kaṇṭavaṉ kalikkacci ēkampam kāṇa iṭar keṭumē
suffering will perish if one visits ēkampam in Kacci of bustle, belonging to Civaṉ who cused the all the three cities of the enemies to cease to exist, by one cruel arrow.
VMS11 / {1:133}__PIFI10+
{$} ēriṉ ārpoḻil cūḻnta kacci ēkampam mēyaviṉai
on Civaṉ who is in ēkampam in Kacci surrounded by beautiful gardens.
kāriṉ ār maṇimāṭam ōṅku kaḻumala naṉṉakaruḷ pāriṉ ār tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ paraviya pattum vallār
those who are capable of reciting all the ten verses with which ñaṉacampantaṉ who had knowledge of tamiḻ which is famous in the world, and who is a native of the good city of Kaḻumalam where beautiful storeys on which clouds settle, rise high.
cīriṉ ārpukaḻ ōṅki viṇṇavarōṭum cērpavarē
are those who join the celestials, their eminent fame increasing day by day.

{2:65}__1+
{$} piramapuram amarntār
the Lord who dwells in Piramapuram.
kaṟai aṇi vēl ilar pōlum
perhaps has not a vēl which has a black colour due to the drying of blood.
[[kaṟaiyaṇi is a generic epithet; (God as the supreme being, the indivisible, the attributeless, has no actions, but when he performs the five acts of creation etc. in that aspect he has all these things, that is why campantar says in the last verse aṇṇal ceyyātaṉa ellām.]]
kapālam tarittilar pōlum
is not holding a skull.
maṟaiyum naviṉṟilar pōlum
has not chanted the maṟi without ceasing.
mācuṇam ārttilar pōlum
has not tied cobras on his body.
paṟaiyum karattilar pōlum
has no paṟi in his hand (paṟi here means tuṭi)
pācam piṭittilar pōlum
is not holding a noose (in his hand)
piṟaiyum caṭaikku ilar pōlum
has no crescent moon on his matted locks of hir.
(pōlum here expresses doubt; it is the same in the following stanzas also).

{$} piramapuram amarntār
same as 1st verse.
kūr ampu (atu) ilar pōlum
has no sharp arrows
kokkiṉ iṟaku ilar pōlum
has no feather of a crane adorning his head
[[Civaṉ killed an acuraṉ by name bakaṉ and wore his feathers on his head; a kind of flower of pure white in colour is called kokkiṟaku from its resemblance to the feather of the crane.]]
āramum pūṇṭilar pōlum
has not adorned himself with a garland of precious stones.
āmai aṇintilar pōlum
has not decked himself with the shell of the tortoise (when mēl took the incarnation of a tortoise he bceame very conceited and Civaṉ killed it and adorned himself with its shell (It is sid in civa purāṇams that Civaṉ punished mēl in the first five incarnations and granted him the boons he requested in the other five incarnations)
tārum caṭaikku ilar pōlum
has no garland (of konṟi) in his mattted locks of hir.
caṇṭikku aruḷilar pōlum
has not bestowed his grace on caṇti (The story of caṇtēca nāyaṉar is told in the 20th purāṇam of periya purāṇam)
[[Variant reading: taṇṭikkaruḷilar]]
pērum pala ilar pōlum
has not many names.

citta vaṭivu ilar pōlum
has no form appropriate to his possession of mystic powers.
tēcam tirintilar pōlum
has not wandered in the country.
kattivarum kāḷi kataṅkaḷ tavirttilar pōlum
has not removed the anger of many kinds of kāḷi who was coming roaring (In tiruvālaṅkātu, Civaṉ danced with kāḷi; when he knew that he could not defeat her, he rised his right leg perpendiculary when she became shy and accepted defeat)
meytta nayaṉam iṭantārkku āḻi aḷittilar pōlum
did not present a discus to the one who scooped out his eye in the body (see notes on cāykkāṭu, appar (1) 9th verse)
[[To split the word as eytta will be in keeping with the alliteration in iṭantārkku; then eytta will mean weary, tired.]]
pitta vaṭivu ilar pōlum
has not an appearance like a mad persons.

viṇṇavar kaṇṭilar pōlum

{$} piṉṟiyum pīṭum perukum piramapuram amarntār
the god who dwells in piramapuram whose greatness is increasing agin and agin (piṉṟi: agin; a rare word)
paṉṟiyiṉ kompu ilar pōlum
he does not wear the hog`s tusk.
pārttaṟku aruḷilar pōlum
has not granted boons to pārttaṉ (pārttaṉ: arujṉaṉ); the derivative meaning is the son of prutā (Kunti))
kaṉṟiya kālaṉai vīḻak kāl koṭu pāyntilar pōlum
-did not pounce upon the angry kālaṉ with his leg, to make him fall down.
tuṉṟu piṇam cuṭukāṭṭil āṭit tutaintilar pōlum
has not steeped (in white ash) by dancing in the ground where crowded corpses are cremated

the god who dwells in piramapuram where women who have a git like that of a female elephant are on the increase.
aṭi muṭi māl ayaṉ tēṭa aṉṟu (um) aḷappilar pōlum
could not be found by Māl and Ayaṉ who searched for his feet and head (Ayaṉ searched for his head; Māl tried to find out his head (The words should be arranged in this way; aṭi māl tēṭa; muṭi ayaṉ tēṭa)

did not open his eye (on the forehead) for the sparks of fire to fly and burn vēḷ (maṉmataṉ - cupid) who has five arrows of fragrant flowers (The five flowers are (1) lotus (2) flower of the mango tree (3) acokam flower (4) mulli (arabian-jasmine) (5) nīlam (blue nelumbo)
paṭimalarp pālakaṉukkākap pāṟ kaṭal īntilar pōlum
did not give the ocean of milk for the child who can be compared to the nature of a lotus. (upamaniya was the son of the sages who had the legs with tiger`s claws. He cried for milk as he was very hungry; so God ordered the ocean of milk to go to him to appease his hunger;

veṟṟarai (yārkku) cīvarattārkku veḷippaṭa niṉṟilar pōlum
the god has not become manifest (has not revealed himself) to the naked jins and followers of Buddha who don themselves with yellow coloured robes (These robes are known by the name of Cīvaram)
aṟṟavar nālvarkku āl niḻal aṟaṅkaḷ uraittilar pōlum
has not revealed spiritual wisdom to four sages who had left all attachments, under the shade of the banyan tree.
[[The name of the four sages are : canakar, canantanar, canātanar, and canatkumārar; they are known as the sons of Brahma, the creator)
uṟṟa alar oṉṟu ilar pōlum
has no calumny attached to him.
(uṟṟa alar have become uṟṟalar)
[[variant readings : uṟṟava reṉṟilar, uṟṟavar oṉṟilar]]

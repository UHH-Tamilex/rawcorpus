{2:34}__1+
{$} maittaḻai perum poḻaliṉ vācamatuvīcappattaroṭu cittar payilkiṉṟa paḻuvūr
Paḻuvūr where pious people and people who possess eight kinds of mystic powers gather in large numbers and where the fragrance of the big gardens in which clouds are thriving, spread in all directions.
muttaṉ
Civaṉ who is by nature free from bonds.
[[muttaṉ - iyalpākavē muttaṉ Kovaiyār-127, Pērāciriyar]]
miku mūvilainalvēlaṉ
who has a good and superior trident of three blades.
virinūlaṉ
who expounded works that became expanded
attaṉ
the master (father)
emai āḷ uṭaiya aṇṇal iṭam eṉpar
people say is the place of the chief who admitted us into his grace.
{2:34}__2+
{$} māṭam mali cūḷikaiyil ēṟi maṭavārkaḷ pāṭal oliceyya malikiṉṟa paḻuvūr
paḻuvūr where the ladies in large numbers sing songs loudly climbing up the open terrace of the mansions.
kōṭaloṭu
along with the white species of malabar glory lily.
kōṅku avai kulāvu muṭi taṉ mēl
on the head in which flowers of common caung shine.
āṭu aravamvaitta perumāṉatu iṭam eṉpar
people say it is the place of the Lord who placed a dancing cobra.
{2:34}__3+
{$} vēliyiṉ viraikkamalam aṉṉa mukam mātarpāl eṉa miḻaṟṟinaṭam āṭu paḻuvūr
Paḻuvūr where ladies whose faces are like the fragrant lotus in the fields, speak softly words as sweet as milk.
vāliya purattilavar vēva viḻiceyta pōliya voruttarpurinūlariṭa meṉpar
people say that it is the place of that unequalled one who opened his eyes to burn the acurar in the big cities.
[[Not only did Civaṉ destroy the three cities by his laughter but also by opening his eyes (This happened in one Kaṟpam);]]
[[PP: araṇam aṉalāka viḻittavaṉē- (campantar Kaḻippālai, 1);]]
[[PP: pārttatuvum araṇam paṭar eri mūḻkavē- (Do akattiyaṉ paḷḷi 5);]]
[[PP: kaṭai navil mum matilum eri ūṭṭiya kaṇṇutalāṉ- Do tiruveṇṭuṟai 3]]
{2:34}__4+
{$} maṇṇiṉ micai āṭi
dancing in the world
toḻutu ētti
worshipping and praising
paṇṇiṉ oli koṇṭu malaiyāḷar payilkiṉṟa paḻuvūr
Paḻuvūr where people [from] Malayāḷam crowd [to] the sound of music.
eṇṇum ōr eḻuttum icaiyiṉ kiḷavi tērvār kaṇṇum mutal āya kaṭavuṭku iṭam atu eṉpar
people say that it is the place of the god, the origin of all things who is always in the thoughts of people who do research in words connected with music, grammar and mathematics
[[Even before the time of Ñāṉacampantar worship in this temple was conducted by malayāḷi brahmins; see 11th verse:
antaṇarkaḷāṉa malaiyāḷaravar ēttum, pantam malikiṉṟa paḻuvūr]]
{2:34}__5+
{$} cātal purivār cuṭalai taṉṉil naṭam āṭum nātaṉ
the master who dances in the cremation ground which is desired only by people who die.
namai āḷuṭaiya nampaṉ iṭam eṉpar
people say that the place of Civaṉ who admits us as his protege.
vēta moḻi colli malaiyāḷar iṟaivaṉ taṉ pātam avai (1) ētta nikaḻkiṉṟa paḻuvūr
is paluvūr where the malayāḷi brahmins chant words from Vetam and worship the feet of Civaṉ.
[[Variant reading: (1) ōta]]
{2:34}__6+
{$} mēvu ayarum mummatilum ventaḻalviḷaittu
Having set hot fire to all the three forts which inflicted suffering on people according to their caprice
mā ayara aṉṟu uri cey maintaṉ iṭam eṉpar
people say that the place of strong Civaṉ who flayed the skin of elephant to make it faint.
pūvaiyai maṭantaiyarkaḷ koṇṭu pukaḻ collip pāvaiyarkaḷ kaṟpoṭu polinta paḻuvūrē
is paḻuvūr where the beautiful ladies who are eminent by their chastity and teach the fame of Civaṉ holding the bush-myna in their hands.
{2:34}__7+
{$} mantaṇam iruntu puri māmaṭikaḷ vēḷvi cinta viḷaiyāṭu civalōkaṉ iṭam eṉpar
people say the place of Civalōkaṉ who destroyed as if by play the sacrifice of the father-in-law who performed a sacrifice in secret.

[[PP: māykuṭikku nimittamāka makappeṟum tirumāmaṭip pēy;]]
[[PP: vaṇaṅkiyē naṉṟu niṟkum māmaṭippēyai- (takkayākapparaṇi 768, 793);]]
[[PP: māmaṭi sra� vallapatēvar - (J.A.S.1.257);]]
[[PP: maṟṟai māmaṭikaḷ iṭu cāpamum (Kumarakuruparar, 69)]]
antaṇarkaḷ ākutiyil iṭṭa akil maṭṭu ārpaintoṭi naṉmātar cuvaṭu oṟṟu paḻuvūr
is paluvūr where the ladies who wear bangles of pure gold and who have tresses of hair on which the fragrance of the eagle-wood logs placed into the sacrificial fire as offering by brahmins closely follow the footprints of their ancestors.
{2:34}__8+
{$} urakkaṭal viṭattiṉai miṭaṟṟil uṟavaittu
Having placed the poison that rose in the strong sea in the neck.
aṉṟu arakkaṉai aṭarttu aruḷum appaṉ iṭam eṉpar
People say that the place of the father who granted his grace after crushing down the arakkaṉ.
kurakkiṉam viraip poḻiliṉ mītu kaṉi uṇṭu parakkuṟu (1) puṉaṟ ceyviḷaiyāṭu paḻuvūr
is Paḻuvūr where the groups of monkeys having eaten the fruits in the fragrant garden play in the fields in which water spreads.
[[Variant reading: (1) puṇarc cey]]
{2:34}__9+
{$} niṉṟa neṭumālum oru nāṉ mukaṉum nēṭa aṉṟu taḻalāy nimirum āti iṭam eṉpar
people say that the place of Civaṉ, the origin of all things, who rose in the distant past as a column of fire to be searched by Māl who grew high taking a tall form and Piramaṉ of four faces.
oṉṟum iru mūṉṟum oru nālum uṇarvārkaḷ maṉṟiṉil iruntu uṭaṉ makiḻnta paḻuvūr
is paḻuvūr where brahmins who have learnt the six aṅkams and four vetams and thereby have realised the one object, Civaṉ, feel happy, seated together in the assembly of learned people.
{2:34}__10+
{$} moṭṭai amaṇ ātar, tukil mūṭu viritērar
the bald headed ignorant amaṇar and the buddhists who cover their bodies with the big robe
muṭṭaikaḷ moḻinta muṉivāṉtaṉ iṭam eṉpar
people say that the place of Civaṉ who dislikes what they who have no sharpness of intelligence say.
[[camaṇar are called bald-headed as they pull out by the root, the hairs on the head by their own hands which action is called ulōccu]]
maṭṭai mali tāḻai iḷa nīratu icai pūkam paṭṭaiyoṭu tāṟuvirikiṉṟa paḻuvūr
is paḻuvūr where the areca-palms which combines with the tender cocoa-nuts which have many leaf-stalks, unfold their spathes along with the outer rind that covers it.
{2:34}__11+
{$} antaṇarkaḷ āṉa malaiyāḷaravar ēttum pantam malikiṉṟa paḻuvūr araṉai
on araṉ who is in paḻuvūr to which the devotees are connected, worship and receive grace and where brahmins [who are] malayāḷis praise him.
āraccantam miku (1) ñāṉam uṇar pantaṉ urai pēṇi vantavaṇam ēttumavar vāṉam uṭaiyārē
those who are able to praise Civaṉ according to their ability cherishing with love the verses of ñāṉacampantaṉ which are full of rhythmic movement, shall definitely live in heaven.

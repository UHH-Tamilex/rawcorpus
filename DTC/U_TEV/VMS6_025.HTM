{$} ayirāvaṇam ēṟātu āṉēṟu ēṟi
riding on a bull without riding on an elephant ayirāvaṇam
[[ayirāvaṇam is an elephant which has one thousant tusks on each side of its trunk;
nirai nirai āyiram vakuttamāyiru maruppiṉ veṇṇiṟac ceṅkaṇ vēḻamum (Kōyil nāṉmaṇimāli, 11-13-15)]]
amarar nāṭu āḷātē ārūrkaṇṭa ayirāvaṇamē
ayirāvanam who ruled in Ārur without ruling over the heaven of the immortals!
[[ayirāvaṇamē may be split into two words as ayirā and vaṇamē; the meaning will be the object which is beyond all doubt]]
eṉ ammāṉē
my father!
uyirāvaṇam iruntu
controlling the breath.
uṟṟu nōkki
looking inwardly fixing the thoughts on you.
uḷḷak (1) kiḻiyiṉ urueḻuti
drawing your form on the canvas of my heart.
uyir āvaṇam ceytiṭṭu uṉ kaittantāl
if I hand over to you my life executing a bond of slavery.
uṇarap paṭuvārōṭu ottuvāḻti
you live along with those who are recognized by you, being united with them.
niṉ aruṭkaṇṇāl nōkkātār allātārē
those who are not seen with your looks full of grace are not fit to receive bliss granted by you.
[[Variant reading: (1) kiḻiyil]]

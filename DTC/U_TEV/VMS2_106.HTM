{2:106}__1+
{$} muḻumaṇittaraḷaṅkaḷ maṉṉu kāviricūḻ tiruvalañcuḻivāṇaṉai vāyārap paṉṉi ātarittu ēttiyum pāṭiyum vaḻipaṭumataṉāvē
by worshipping the Lord who dwells in Tiruvalañcuḻi surrounded by Kāviri where big precious stones of pearls are deposited, uttering many times his names to the complete satisfaction of your mouth, cherishing him with love, and singing his fame.
iruṅkaṭal vaiyattu
in the world girt by the great ocean.
muṉṉam nī puri nalviṉaip payaṉiṭai eṉṉa puṇṇiyam (1) ceytaṉai
what great act of virtue you performed among the good acts done in your previous births!
neñcamē
my mind!
[[whether Campantar performed acts of virtue or not is not the doubt. What was the special act of virtue to be performed to worship Civaṉ in Valañcuḻi is the doubt; that doubt is cleared by the words nalviṉaippayaṉ;
uttering many times the name of god and singing his fame is the act done by tongue, cherishing him with love is the act of the mind and worshipping is the act of the body]]
[[Variant reading: (1) ceytaṉa]]
{2:106}__2+
{$} virikaṭal varuneñcam uṇṭu iṟaiñcuvāṉavartamaittāṅkiya iṟaivaṉai
the Lord who protected the celestials who worshipped him by drinking the poison that rose in the expansive ocean.
vaṇṭu (1) vāḻkuḻal maṅkai ōrpaṅkaṉai
and the one who has on one half a lady on whose treasses of hir bees settle.
ulakattil valañcuḻi iṭamākak koṇṭa nātaṉ meyttoḻil puritoṇṭarōṭu iṉitu iruntamaiyālē
by associating ourselves with pleasure with the company of devotees who perform sincere service to, the chief who had as his place valañculi in this world.
nammuṭai valviṉaiviṇṭu oḻintaṉa
our irressistible Karmams have perished leaving
[[Variant reading: (1) vār]]
{2:106}__3+
{$} valañcuḻi iṭamāka irunta nāyakaṉ imaiyavar ēttiya iṇai aṭittalam tāṉē
the two feet of the master who dwelt in valañcuḻi as his place, which were prised by the celestials who do not wink.
tiruntalārpuram tīeḻac ceṟuvaṉa
are capable of destroying the cities of enemies by setting fire to them.
(1) iṟaliṉkaṇ aṭiyāraip parintu kāppaṉa
protect his devotees affectionately at the time of disaster. (iṟal disaster)
[[iṟal īṉum eṇṇātu veḵkiṉ (Kuṟaḷ, 180)]]
pattiyil varuvaṉa
they come to devotees in response to their piety.
mattamām piṇi nōykku maruntum āvaṉa
are also remedy to physical diseases and mental worries which are the products of confusion.
mantiram āvaṉa
are mantiram.
[[maruntum āvaṉa: mantiram āvaṉa maruntuvēṇṭillivai mantiraṅkaḷḷivai (campantar, tiruntutēvaṉkuti, 1); maruntavai mantiram (Do Tirunelvēli, 1)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) viṟaviṉ]]
{2:106}__4+
{$} kaṟaikoḷ kaṇṭattar
Civaṉ has a black neck.
kāykatir niṟattiṉar
has the colour of the scorching sun.
aṟattiṟam muṉivarkkaṉṟu iṟaivar āliṭai nīḻalil iruntu ukantu iṉitu aruḷ perumāṉār
the great god who sitting joyously under the shade of the banyan tree as the master, inititated the sages into the several aspects moral instruction, in a pleasing manner.
maṟaikaḷ ōtuvar
he will recite the vētams with the appropriate intonation.
varupuṉal valañcuḻi iṭam makiḻntu aruṅkāṉattu aṟaikaḻal cilampu ārkka niṉṟu āṭiya aṟputam aṟiyōmē
we are unable to comprehend the marvel of that god dancing standing in the cremation ground which is difficult to dwell, to make the Kaḻal and cilampu to make a big sound, having rejoiced to stay in valañcuḻi as his place which has floods coming perennially.
{2:106}__5+
{$} maṇṇar
Civaṉ has the earth as one of his forms.
nīrar
has water as one of his forms.
viṇkāṟṟiṉar
has the sky and air as his forms.
āṟṟal ām eri
has the form of fire which has the power of consuming everything.
uru oru pākam peṇṇar āṇ eṉatterivu aruvaṭiviṉar
has a form which cannot be easily distinguished whether it is male or female; has as on one half a lady.
peruṅkaṭaṟ pavaḷampōl vaṇṇar ākilum
he has the red colour like the coral which is produced in the ocean.
valañcuḻi pirikilār
and does not leave valañcuḻi.
paripavar maṉam pukka eṇṇar ākilum eṉaippala iyalpuvar iṇai aṭi toḻuvārē
those who worship his two feet will speak ever so many things about his greatness that he entered into the minds and thoughts of those who are affectionate to him. (ākilum is a corruption of āyiṉum, is used in the sense of a conjuction)
[[āyiṉum eṉpatu ōr eṇṇiṭaiccol (cilappatikāram, 1-14, atiyārkkunallūr)]]
{2:106}__6+
{$} oruvarāl uvamippatai ariyatu ōr mēṉiyar
Civaṉ has a form to which no one can compare anything as equal.
maṭamātar iruvar ātarippār
he loves two young ladies (Umi and Kañki)
pala pūtamum pēykaḷum aṭaiyāḷam
many pūtams and pēys are his marks of distinction.
aruvarātatu ōr veṇṭalai kaippiṭittu
holding a white skull which he does not loathe.
[[aruvarātatu is a noun derived from añcaṉam cēr kaṇṇār aruvarukkum aypatamāyk, kuñciveḷuttu uṭalam kōṇā muṉ (Kshettirattiruveṇpa, 15); aṉṉameṉṉaṭaiyiṉār kalaviyai aruvaruttu añciṉāyēl (periya tirumoḻi, 9-7-2)]]
akamtoṟum palikku eṉṟu varuvarēl avar valañcuḻi aṭikaḷō
if he comes for alms in every house he is definitely the god in valañcuḻi.
varivaḷaikavar/ntārē
he has stolen my bangles with lines.
{2:106}__7+
{$} kuṉṟiyūr kuṭamūkku iṭam valampuram kulaviya neyttāṉam eṉṟu ivvūrkaḷilōm eṉṟum iyampuvar
Civaṉ tells also that he dwells in Kuṉṟiyur, the place of Kutāmūkku, valampuram, and eminent neyttāṉam.
[[kuṉṟiyūr (Kuṉṟiyūr) is a shrine that has no separate tevaram for it, it is a vipputtalam; nīrūrvayal niṉṟiyūr kuṉṟiyūrum (campantar, potu, kshēttirakkōvi, 1). The other shrines mentioned in this verse have separate decades for each of them.]]
imaiyavar paṇi kēṭpar
the celestials who do not wink carry out his commands.
aṉṟi ūr tamakkuuḷḷaṉa aṟikilōm
we are not able to know the places in addition to these, which are Civaṉ's shrines.
valañcuḻiyaraṉār pāl ceṉṟu a(v)vūrtaṉil talaippaṭalāmeṉṟu cēyiḻai taḷarvāmē
the lady wearing beautiful ornaments, faints when she thinks that she can reach araṉ in valañcuḻi by going to that place.
{2:106}__8+
{$} kuyiliṉ nērmoḻik koṭiyiṭai veruvuṟa
Umi who speaks words as sweet as the voice of the Kuyil and has a tender waist like the creeper, to be frightened.
kula(1)varaip parappu āya kayilaiyaip piṭittu eṭuttavaṉ katirmuṭitōḷ irupatum ūṉṟi
fixing his toe firmly on the twenty shoulders and bright crowns of the one (iravaṇaṉ) who lifted, catching hold of the Kayili which is eminent, and extensive in area.
mayiliṉ nēr aṉa cāyalōṭu amarntavaṉ
Civaṉ who sat (as if nothing had happened) with a lady who is as graceful as the peacock.
valañcuḻi emmāṉ
and our Lord in valañcuḻi.
payilavallavar
those who can always meditate upon him.
parakatikāṇpavar
are those who will attin saluation.
allavarkāṇār
others will not attin that.
[[Variant reading: (1) varaip pārppāy, varai pāṟpāy, varai pāṟpāya]]
{2:106}__9+
{$} aḻal atu ōmpiya alarmicai aṇṇalum araviṇaittuyiṉṟāṉum
Piramaṉ who is seated on a (lotus flower) and who mintins the sacrificial fire, and Māl who slept on a serpent bed.
kaḻalum ceṉṉiyum kāṇpu aritāyavar
Civaṉ whose feet and head could not be seen (by them)
māṇpu amar taṭakkaiyil maḻalai vīṇaiyar
has a vīṇi of sweet music in his beautiful and big hand.
makiḻtiruvalañcuḻi valamkoṭu pātattāl cuḻalu māntarkaḷ tolviṉaiyataṉoṭu tuṉpaṅkaḷ kaḷaivārē
those who go often from left to right walking round the temple at valañcuḻi where he resides with joy, will root out accumulated Karmams, and sufferings.
{2:106}__10+
{$} aṟivu ilātavaṉ camaṇar cākkiyar
camaṇar and cākkiyar (buddhists) who have no intelligence.
tavam purintu avam ceyvār
do useless acts by pretending to perform penance.
neṟi ayalātaṉa kūṟuvar
they talk which is not right conduct
maṟṟu avai tēṟaṉmiṉ
do not accept them as true. (People of their world!)
māṟānīr maṟi ulām tiraik kāviri valañcuḻi maruviyaperumāṉaip pirivilātavar
those who worship without ceasing the Lord in valañcuḻi on the bank of the Kāviri of waves which roam about rolling upside down, and which has never failing freshes.
peṟukatipēciṭil
if we speak about their salvation.
aḷavu aṟuppu oṇṇātu
it is difficult to define it by words.
{2:106}__11+
{$} mātu ōr kūṟaṉai
on Civaṉ who has a lady as his half.
valañcuḻi maruviya maruntiṉai
and the nectar that is in valañcuḻi (maruntu-cāvāmaruntu)
vayaṟkāḻinātaṉ vētiyaṉ ñāṉacampantaṉ vāy naviṟṟiyatamiḻmālai
the garland of tamiḻ verses that were revealed though the mouth of ñāṉacampantaṉ, the brahmin, and the chief of Kāḻi which is famous for fields.
viṉai vātiyā
Karmams will not afflict.
ātarittu
wishing for these.
icaikaṟṟu vallōr colak kēṭṭu ukantavar tammai
those who learnt with music and those who were pleased to hear these.
maṟumaikkum immaikkum varuttam vantu aṭaiyā
sufferings will not come to them in the next birth and in this birth.

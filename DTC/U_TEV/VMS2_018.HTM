{2:18}__1+
{$} caṭaiyāyaeṉum(āl)
this lady will say, one who has a catai!'
caraṇ nī eṉum (āl)
she says you are my refuge'
viṭaiyāy eṉum (āl)
says, one who has a bull'.
veruvāviḻum (āl)
she falls on the ground frightened
maṭaiārkuvaḷai malarum marukal uṭaiyāy
Civaṉ who has as his abode marukal where blue nelumbo flowers that grow in the sluices to the fields blossom.
ivaḷ uḷmelivu takumō
is it proper for this lady to suffer mental fatique?
{2:18}__2+
{$} cintāy eṉum (āl)
this lady says, Civaṉ is my mind!'
civaṉē eṉum (āl)
says, Civaṉ'
muntāyeṉum (āl)
says, one who is primordial'
mutalvāeṉum (āl)
says, the cause of all things.
kontu ārkuvaḷaikulavum marukalentāy
our father who is in marukal where bunches of blue nelumbo flowers are shining'.
ivaḷ ēcaṟavu takumō
is it proper for this lady to be troubled in mind.
{2:18}__3+
{$} aṟai ārkaḻalum aḻalvāy aravum piṟai ārcaṭaiyum uṭaiyāy
Civaṉ who wears a Kaḻal making sound, has a cobra that has poison in its mouth, and a catai which bears the crescent!
periya maṟaiyār marukal makiḻvāy
you rejoice in staying in marukal where great brahmins who have learnt the vētams live.
ivaḷai iṟai ār vaḷaikoṇṭu eḻilvalviṉaiyē
you took away the bangles that are worn in the wrist and snatched her beauty.
{2:18}__4+
{$} olinīr caṭaiyil karantāy
you concealed in your caṭai water which makes a sound.
ulakam pali nī tirivāy
you wander throughout the world for alms.
paḻiilpukaḻāy
you have fame that has blame attached to it.
malinīrmarukal makiḻvāy
you rejoice in staying in marukal which has plenty of water.
ivaḷai meli nīrmaiyaḷ ākkavum vēṇṭiṉaiyē
you desired this lady to become enfeebled also.
{2:18}__5+
{$} tuṇinīlavaṇṇam mukil (1) tōṉṟiaṉṉa maṇinīlakaṇṭam
you have a beautiful blue neck which resembles the cloud which has clear blue colour and appears in the sky.
marukal kaṇinīla vaṇṭu ār kuḻalāḷ ivaḷ taṉaṇinīla oṇkaṇ ayarvu ākkiṉaiyē
you made the beautiful bright eyes resembling blue nelumbo flowers of this lady on whose tresses of hair black bees him in the chaplets and who is to be treated with esteem, in Marukal to forget herself.
[[Variant reading: (1) tōṉṟiyāṉ;]]
kaṇi -- kaṇṇi:
chaplet worn on the head]]
{2:18}__6+
{$} palarum paravappaṭuvāy
you are praised by many people.
caṭaimēl malarumpiṟai oṉṟu uṭaiyāy
you have a flourishing crescent on your caṭai.
marukal
in marukal
pularum taṉaiyum tuyilāḷ puṭaipōntu
turning in the bed without sleeping till day-break.
alarum paṭumō aṭiyāḷ ivaḷē
is it proper that this lady who is your slave to be subject of slanderous talk?
{2:18}__7+
{$} vaḻuvāḷ perumāṉ kaḻala vāḻkaeṉā (1) eḻuvāḷ
this lady will walk up from sleep saying, Let the feet of our Lord live long, without failing.
niṉaivāḷ iravum pakalum
she will meditate on them night and day.
maḻuvāḷ uṭaiyāy
one who has a weapon of a battle-axe!
marukaṟ perumāṉ
the Lord in marukal!
toḻuvāḷ ivaḷaittuyar ākkiṉaiyē
you made this lady who worship you, to suffer.
[[Variant reading: (1) eḻuvāṉ]]
{2:18}__8+
{$} ilaṅkaikku iṟaivaṉ vilaṅkal eṭuppa
when the King of ilaṅkai lifted the mountain.
(1) tulaṅka(v)viral ūṉṟalum
when Civaṉ fixed firmly his toe to make him confused.
tōṉṟalaṉāy
becoming dazed wihout knowing what to do
valamkoḷ maticūḻ marukaṟperumāṉ
the Lord in marukal surrounded by a fortified wall, from left to right, and received boons from you!
alaṅkal ivaḷai (2) alar ākkiṉaiyē
you made this lady who wears a garland the subject of slanderous talk.
[[Variant reading: (1) tulaṅkal (2) ayarvu]]
{2:18}__9+
{$} eriārcaṭaiyum aṭiyum(m) iruvar, teriyātatu ōrtīttiraḷ āyavaṉē
Civaṉ who assumed the form of a ball of fire whose caṭai which is like fire, and feet could not be found out by the two (Māl and Piramaṉ).
mariyār piriyā marukaṟ perumāṉ
the Lord in marukal whom your flowers could not leave;(mariyār-marīiyār)
[[maruvippiriyamāṭṭēṉ nāṉ (cuntarar, tiruvaiyāṟu, 3); kariyavaṉ tirumuṭikaviḻtta cēvaṭip, periyavaṉ tirumoḻipiṟaḻtal iṉṟiyē mariyavar uṟaitaliṉ (cīvaka cintāmaṇi, 1211)]]
ariyāḷ ivaḷai ayarvu ākkiṉaiyē
you made this pre-eminent lady to faint.
{2:18}__10+
{$} aṟivu ilcamaṇum alar cākkiyarum neṟi allaṉa ceytaṉar uḻalvār
the camaṇar who have no sense and cakkiyar who have spread themselves everywhere wander doing what is not the proper path.
maṟiēntukaiyāy
you, holding a young deer in the hand!
marukaṟperumāṉ
the Lord in marukal!
neṟiārkuḻali niṟai nīkkiṉaiyē
you removed the firm adherence to a life of chastity of this lady who has tresses of curly hair.
{2:18}__11+
{$} vayañāṉamvallār marukaṟ perumāṉ uyarñāṉam uṇarntu aṭiuḷkutalāl
meditating on the feet of Civaṉ realising the superior spiritual wisdom granted by the Lord in marukal where people who are capable of the powerful spiritual wisdom, live.
iyal ñāṉacampantaṉpāṭal vallār pukaḻviyal ñālam ellām viḷaṅkum
the fame of those who can recite the songs composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who has knowledge of iyal tamiḻ, will shine in this wide world.

{1:80}__1+
{$} kaṟṟāṅku eri ōmpi
Having maintained the fires according to the lore of the Vēda-s which they learnt.
kaliyai vārāmē ceṟṟār vāḻtillai
in Tillai where brahmins live who have destroyed the evil effects of Kaliyukam (poverty).
ciṟṟampalam mēya muṟṟā veṇ tiṅkaḷ mutalvaṉ pātamē
the feet of the primordial one who adorns his head with the crescent of the third phase and who resides with desire in Ciṟṟampalam, to the exclusion of other things.
paṟṟā niṉṟāraip paṟṟā pāvamē
sins will not adhere to people who have caught hold of (those feet).
{1:80}__2+
{$} paṟappaip paṭuttup pacuvēṭṭu
Having constructed a dais in the form of Karutaṉ (or kite) for sacrificial fire and offered an animal in sacrifice.
eri ōmpum ciṟapparvāḻtillai
in Tillai where eminent brahmins live who maintain the fires.
ciṟṟampalam mēya piṟappu il perumāṉai
the great one who is not born and who resides with desire in Ciṟṟampalam.
piṉ tāḻcaṭaiyāṉai
one who has matted locks flowing on the nape.
maṟappu ilār kaṇṭīr maiyal tīrvārē
only those who do not forget him will be free from mental delusion:
{1:80}__3+
{$} mai ār oṇ kaṇṇār māṭa neṭuvītik kaiyāl pantu ōccum kaḻicūḻ tillaiyuḷ
inside Tillai which is surrounded by backwaters and where in the long streets of storeys the girls of bright and colliriyum-coated eyes play balls with their hands.
poyyā maṟaipāṭal purintāṉ
one who sang the Maṟai which never becomes untrue.
ulaku ētta
to be worshipped by the people of this world.
ceyyāṉ uṟaikōyil ciṟṟampalamtāṉē
Ciṟṟampalam is the eminent temple where the red-coloured one resides.
{1:80}__4+
{$} niṟaiveṇkoṭimāṭam neṟṟi nērtīṇṭa
the group of white flags touch the facade of the storeys straight.
piṟaivantu iṟaitākkum pērampalam
the sacred hall where the crescent moon comes and strikes against the caves.
tillaiciṟaivaṇṭu aṟai ōvācciṟṟampalam mēya iṟaivaṉ
the Lord who resides in the Ciṟṟampalam of Tillai where the winged bees hum without ceasing.
kaḻal ēttum iṉpam iṉpamē
the pleasure derived by praising his feet is the real pleasure.
{1:80}__5+
{$} celvam neṭumāṭam ceṉṟu cēṇ ōṅki
the tall storeys having wealth are high and rise into the sky.
celvar vāḻtillaicciṟṟampalam mēya celvaṉ
the Lord who resides in the Ciṟṟampalam of Tillai where people who possess the wealth of devotion reside.
kaḻal ēttum celvam celvamē
the wealth one gets by praising the feet of the Lord is the real wealth.
{1:80}__6+
{$} varumāntaḷir mēṉimātu ōr pākamām
Having on one part a lady who has the complexion of the mango-leaves that are just sprouting.
tirumāntillaiyuḷ ciṟṟampalam mēya
who is residing in Ciṟṟampalam in Tillai which is great by its wealth.
karumāṉ uri āṭai, kaṟaicēr kaṇṭattu emperumāṉ
our great one who has a blue neck and clothing of skin of spotted deer (elephant)
kaḻal allāl pēṇātu uḷḷamē
my mind will not desire worshipping the feet of others except the feet of the Lord at Ciṟṟampalam.
{1:80}__7+
{$} alai ārpuṉal cūṭi
Having born on the head the water (of the Kaṅkai) which is full of waves.
ulaku ētta ākattu orupākam malaiyāṉ makaḷōṭum makiḻntāṉ
he rejoiced having on one part of his form the daughter of the King of mountains, to be praised by the world.
cilaiyāl eyil eytāṉ
He discharged arrows at the fortress with his bow.
ciṟṟampalam taṉṉaittalaiyāl vaṇaṅkuvār talaiyāṉārkaḷē
those who bow with their heads to his Ciṟṟampalam would be considered foremost.
{1:80}__8+
{$} kūrvāḷ arakkaṉ valiyaikkuṟaivittu
Having caused to decrease the strength of Arakkaṉ (Rāvaṇaṉ) who had a sharp sword.
cīrālē malku ciṟṟampalam mēyanīr ārcaṭaiyāṉai
one who has on his matted locks water and is residing in Ciṟṟampalam which has ever-increasing fame.
nittal ētuvār
those who praise that daily.
tīrānōy ellām tīrtal tiṇṇamē
It is definite that all their incurable diseases would be cured.
{1:80}__9+
{$} kōḷ nākaṇaiyāṉum kuḷirtāmaraiyāṉum kaḻal ēttakkāṇār
The one who is sleeping on the bed of a killing serpent and one who is seated on a cool lotus could not see the feet to praise them.
kaṉalāy ōṅkiṉāṉ
who rose like a column of fire.
cēṇār vāḻtillaic ciṟṟampalam ētta
when we praise the Ciṟṟampalam in Tillai where the residents of the upper world live.
māṇānōy ellām vāḷāmāyum
all the inglorious diseases will vanish without any effort.
{1:80}__10+
{$} paṭṭaittuvar āṭaippaṭimam koṇṭāṭum muṭṭaikkaṭṭuraimoḻiva kēḷātu
without listening to the fabricated falsehood which is as blunt as the egg, spoken by those who pride on their ascetic austerities and who wear a cloth soaked in ochra of the bark of maruta maram (myrobalam)
[[muṭṭai: if this is taken as an expansion of muṭai, then the meaning will be offensive odour.]]
ciṭṭar vāḻ tillaic ciṟṟampalam mēya naṭṭap perumāṉai nāḷum toḻuvōm
we will worship with our hands daily the dancing Lord who is residing in Ciṟṟampalam desiring it, in Tillai where learned men well-versed in vedic lore live.
{1:80}__11+
{$} cīlattār keḷkaic ciṟṟampalammēya cūlappaṭaiyāṉai
about the one who has the trident weapon and who resides in Ciṟṟampalam which is meditated upon by people of good conduct.
ñālattu uyar kāḻi ñāṉacampantaṉ
Ñāṉacampantaṉ who was born in Kāḻi which is famous in this world.
coṉṉatamiḻ mālai
the garland of Tamiḻ verses sung by him.
kōlattāl pāṭavallār nallārē
those who are able to sing in the proper manner beautifully are definitely good people

This [last] poem is called ``tirukaṭaikkāppu'' according to caivaite tradition. This stanza contains the author's name, his birth place and the gotra in which he is born, and the benefits accruing to one who sings the stanzas with intense devotion.

[[These three things distinguish Ñāṉacampantar's Tēvāram:
(1) The anecdote about Rāvaṇaṉ
(2) Brahma and Vishnu trying to find out the feet and head of Civaṉ who appeared between them as a pillar of fire.
(3) Reference to jains and buddhists.
In a very few patikams this arrangement is not found.]]

  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭi

*1
_uṇṇāmuli_umiyāḷoṭum _uṭaṉ_ākiya _oruvaṉ,
peṇ_ākiya perumāṉ, mali---tiru mā maṇi tikaḻa,
maṇ _ārntaṉa _aruvittiraḷ maḻali(m) muḻavu_atirum---
_aṇṇāmali toḻuvār viṉi vaḻuvā vaṇam _aṟumē.

*2
tēmāṅkaṉi kaṭuvaṉ koḷa viṭu kompoṭu tīṇṭi,
tū mā maḻi tuṟukalmici ciṟunuṇtuḷi citaṟa,
_āmāmpiṇi _aṇiyum poḻil _aṇṇāmali _aṇṇal
pū māṅ kaḻal puṉi cēvaṭi niṉivār viṉi _ilarē.

*3
pīli(m)mayil peṭiyōṭu _uṟi poḻil cūḻ kaḻi muttam
cūli(m) maṇi tarimēl niṟi coriyum viri cāral,
_āli(m) maḻi tavaḻum poḻil _aṇṇāmali _aṇṇal
kālaṉ vali toli cēvaṭi toḻuvāraṉa pukaḻē.

*4
_utirum mayir _iṭu veṇtali kalaṉā, _ulaku_ellām
_etirum pali _uṇavu_ākavum, _erutu _ēṟuvatu_allāl,
mutirum caṭi _iḷaveṇpiṟi muṭimēl koḷa, _aṭimēl
_atirum kaḻal _aṭikaṭku _iṭam---_aṇṇāmali_atuvē.

*5
maravam, cili, taraḷam, miku maṇi, _untu veḷ_aruvi
_aravam ceya, muravam paṭum _aṇṇāmali _aṇṇal
_uravam caṭi _ulavum puṉal _uṭaṉ_āvatum _ōrār,
kuravam kamaḻ naṟumeṉkuḻal _umi pulkutal kuṇamē?

*6
perukum puṉal _aṇṇāmali, piṟicēr, kaṭalnañcip
parukumtaṉi tuṇivār, poṭi _aṇivār, _atu parukik
karukum miṭaṟu _uṭiyār, kamaḻ caṭiyār, kaḻal paravi
_urukum maṉam _uṭiyārtamakku _uṟu nōy _aṭiyāvē.

*7
kari kālaṉa, kuṭar koḷvaṉa, kaḻutu _āṭiya kāṭṭil
nari _āṭiya nakuveṇtali _utiyuṇṭavi _uruḷa,
_eri_āṭiya _iṟivarkku _iṭam---_iḷavaṇṭu _ici murala,
_ari _āṭiya kaṇṇāḷoṭum _aṇṇāmali_atuvē.

*8
_oḷiṟū puli_ataḷ_āṭiyaṉ, _umi _añcutalporuṭṭāl,
piḷiṟū kural matavāraṇam vataṉam piṭittu_urittu,
veḷiṟūpaṭa viḷiyāṭiya vikirtaṉ; _irāvaṇaṉi
_aḷaṟūpaṭa _aṭarttāṉ; _iṭam---_aṇṇāmali_atuvē.

*9
viḷavu _ār kaṉi paṭa nūṟiya kaṭalvaṇṇaṉum, vētak
kiḷar tāmarimalarmēl _uṟi kēṭu _il pukaḻōṉum,
_aḷavā vaṇam _aḻal _ākiya _aṇṇāmali _aṇṇal---
taḷarāmuli, muṟuval(l), _umi talivaṉ(ṉ)---_aṭi caraṇē!

*10
vēr vantu_uṟa, mācu _ūrtara, veyil niṉṟu _uḻalvārum,
mārvam puti mali cīvaram maṟiyā varuvārum,
_ārampartam _uri koḷḷaṉmiṉ! _aṇṇāmali _aṇṇal,
kūrveṇmaḻuppaṭiyāṉ, nala kaḻal cērvatu kuṇamē!

*11
vempu _untiya katicōṉ _oḷi vilakum viricāral,
_ampu _unti mū_eyil _eytavaṉ _aṇṇāmali_ataṉi,
kompu _untuva, kuyil _āluva, kuḷir kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉa-
campantaṉ tamiḻ vallavar _aṭi pēṇutal tavamē.

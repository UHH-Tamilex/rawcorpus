• patikam: 1:10
  • talam: tiru _aṇṇāmalai
  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭai
  • VMS: VOL.12, P.255-259

*1
_uṇṇāmulai_umaiyāḷoṭum _uṭaṉ_ākiya _oruvaṉ,
peṇ_ākiya perumāṉ, malai---tiru mā maṇi tikaḻa,
maṇ _ārntaṉa _aruvittiraḷ maḻalai(m) muḻavu_atirum---
_aṇṇāmalai toḻuvār viṉai vaḻuvā vaṇam _aṟumē.

*2
tēmāṅkaṉi kaṭuvaṉ koḷa viṭu kompoṭu tīṇṭi,
tū mā maḻai tuṟukalmicai ciṟunuṇtuḷi citaṟa,
_āmāmpiṇai _aṇaiyum poḻil _aṇṇāmalai _aṇṇal
pū māṅ kaḻal puṉai cēvaṭi niṉaivār viṉai _ilarē.

*3
pīli(m)mayil peṭaiyōṭu _uṟai poḻil cūḻ kaḻai muttam
cūli(m) maṇi taraimēl niṟai coriyum viri cāral,
_āli(m) maḻai tavaḻum poḻil _aṇṇāmalai _aṇṇal
kālaṉ vali tolai cēvaṭi toḻuvāraṉa pukaḻē.

*4
_utirum mayir _iṭu veṇtalai kalaṉā, _ulaku_ellām
_etirum pali _uṇavu_ākavum, _erutu _ēṟuvatu_allāl,
mutirum caṭai _iḷaveṇpiṟai muṭimēl koḷa, _aṭimēl
_atirum kaḻal _aṭikaṭku _iṭam---_aṇṇāmalai_atuvē.

*5
maravam, cilai, taraḷam, miku maṇi, _untu veḷ_aruvi
_aravam ceya, muravam paṭum _aṇṇāmalai _aṇṇal
_uravam caṭai _ulavum puṉal _uṭaṉ_āvatum _ōrār,
kuravam kamaḻ naṟumeṉkuḻal _umai pulkutal kuṇamē?

*6
perukum puṉal _aṇṇāmalai, piṟaicēr, kaṭalnañcaip
parukumtaṉai tuṇivār, poṭi _aṇivār, _atu parukik
karukum miṭaṟu _uṭaiyār, kamaḻ caṭaiyār, kaḻal paravi
_urukum maṉam _uṭaiyārtamakku _uṟu nōy _aṭaiyāvē.

*7
kari kālaṉa, kuṭar koḷvaṉa, kaḻutu _āṭiya kāṭṭil
nari _āṭiya nakuveṇtalai _utaiyuṇṭavai _uruḷa,
_eri_āṭiya _iṟaivarkku _iṭam---_iḷavaṇṭu _icai murala,
_ari _āṭiya kaṇṇāḷoṭum _aṇṇāmalai_atuvē.

*8
_oḷiṟū puli_ataḷ_āṭaiyaṉ, _umai _añcutalporuṭṭāl,
piḷiṟū kural matavāraṇam vataṉam piṭittu_urittu,
veḷiṟūpaṭa viḷaiyāṭiya vikirtaṉ; _irāvaṇaṉai
_aḷaṟūpaṭa _aṭarttāṉ; _iṭam---_aṇṇāmalai_atuvē.

*9
viḷavu _ār kaṉi paṭa nūṟiya kaṭalvaṇṇaṉum, vētak
kiḷar tāmaraimalarmēl _uṟai kēṭu _il pukaḻōṉum,
_aḷavā vaṇam _aḻal _ākiya _aṇṇāmalai _aṇṇal---
taḷarāmulai, muṟuval(l), _umai talaivaṉ(ṉ)---_aṭi caraṇē!

*10
vēr vantu_uṟa, mācu _ūrtara, veyil niṉṟu _uḻalvārum,
mārvam putai mali cīvaram maṟaiyā varuvārum,
_ārampartam _urai koḷḷaṉmiṉ! _aṇṇāmalai _aṇṇal,
kūrveṇmaḻuppaṭaiyāṉ, nala kaḻal cērvatu kuṇamē!

*11
vempu _untiya katicōṉ _oḷi vilakum viricāral,
_ampu _unti mū_eyil _eytavaṉ _aṇṇāmalai_ataṉai,
kompu _untuva, kuyil _āluva, kuḷir kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉa-
campantaṉ tamiḻ vallavar _aṭi pēṇutal tavamē.

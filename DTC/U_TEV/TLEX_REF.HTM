Tamil Lexicon References

The references to Tēvāram inside Tamil Lexicon follow the 1911 edition published by the civa vittiyānupālaṉa yantiracāli. But the references are to page number, which makes it almost impossible to locate the hymn if one does not have that edition.

It is our aim to provide a link to the text through that numbering also.

For that purpose, we are now accumulating evidence

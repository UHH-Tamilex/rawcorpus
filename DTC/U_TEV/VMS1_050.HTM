{$} valivalam mēyavaṉē
Civaṉ who desires Valivalam!
ollai āṟi
haste having subsided.
uḷḷam oṉṟi
concentrating the mind.
kaḷḷam oḻintu
leaving deceit.
veyya collai āṟi
having stopped speaking cruel words.
tūymai ceytu
purifying the heart.
kāmaviṉai akaṟṟi
banishing the acts in connection with love (Kāmam)
nallavāṟē uṉtaṉ nāmam vallavāṟē nāviṉ (1) naviṉṟu ētta
to prise you uttering often according to my capability in a good manner your names in my tongue.
(2) vantu nalkāy
you appear before me and grant me your grace.
[[Variant readings : (1) naviṉṟu ēttukiṉṟēṉ (2) nalku kaṇṭāy]]

having separated myself from the ladies, children and relations who are ignorant and as large as the ocean.
paṇṭē vāḻamāṭṭēṉ
I am unable to live since a long time.
vētaṉai nōy naliyak kaṇṭu kaṇṭē
knowing very often the pinful diseases to afflict me.

my mind desires your names.
vaṇṭu kiṇṭippāṭum cōlai valivalam mēyavaṉē
Civaṉ who dwells in valivalam where in the gardens bees penetrate into the flowers and hum like music.

(1) meyyar ākip poyyai nīkki
Being always people who have knowledge of the ultimate reality, and completely leaving uttering lies.
(2) vētaṉaiyait tuṟantu
renouncing pin which one has to suffer on account of desires.
ceyyar āṉār cintaiyāṉē
god who dwells in the minds of just and impartial people!
tēvar kulakkoḻuntē
who is the tender leaf in the community of the tēvar.
naivaṉ nāyēṉ
I who am as mean as a dog will languish.

I am uttering your names dily without ceasing.
vaiyam muṉṉē vantu nalkāy
you appear before me in the presence of the people of this world and grant me your grace.

see 1st verse.
tuñcum pōtum tuṟṟum pōtum colluvatu uṉ tiṟamē
when I go to sleep and eat I speak about your qualities only.
tañcam illāt tēvar vantu uṉ tāḷ iṇaik kīḻppaṇiya
as the celestials who had no other refuge came and fell at your feet.
nañcam uṇṭāykku eṉ ceykēṉō
what return can I do to you who consumed the poison (to save their lives)?
nāḷum niṉaintu aṭiyēṉ vañcam uṇṭu eṉṟu añcukiṉṟēṉ
I fear that there is deceit, pondering dily, on my part who is your slave.
[[The cuse of the fear is due to the fact whether god would accept me or reject me becuse of my deceitful nature.]]

{4:62}__1+
{$} vētiyā
the supreme god who revealed the Vētam-s!
vētakītā
who chants the vetam!
viṇṇavaraṇṇā eṉṟu eṉṟu
uttering many times as the tēvaṉ superior to the celestials!
ōti
and repeating your names.
malarkaḷ tūvi
scattering flowers.
oruṅkiniṉkaḻalkaḷ kāṇa
to have a vision of your feet by having singleness of him.
pāti ōr peṇṇai vaittāy
god who placed a lady on one half!
paṭarcaṭaimatiyam cūṭum ātiyē
the first cuse of all things who wears a crescent in the spreading caṭi!
ālavāyil appaṉē
the father in ālavāy!
aruḷceyāy (ē)
grant me your grace.
[[syntactical link: ōti, tūvi, kaḻalkaḷkāṇa aruḷ ceyāyē]]
{4:62}__2+
{$} nampaṉē
God who is desired by all living beings!
nāṉ mukattāy
god who has four faces;
[[nālukolām avartam mukam āvaṉa (nāvukkaracar, viṭam tīrttapatikam 4)]]
nātaṉē
my Lord.
ñāṉamūrtti
god with a form which is the embodiment of spiritual wisdom!
eṉpoṉē
Gold which is my property!
īcā
the Lord of the universe!
aṉpaṉē ālavāyil appaṉē
my friend! the father in ālavāy
eṉṟu eṉṟu ētti nāṉ ēcaṟṟu
myself longing for you by prising without ceasing.
eṉṟum piṉpiṉē tirintu nāyēṉ
myself who is as low as a dog always wandering after you.
pērttu iṉip piṟavā vaṇṇam aruḷceyāy (ē)
please grant me your grace so that I may not be born agin.
{4:62}__3+
{$} orumaruntu āki uḷḷāy
you are the medicine without comparison.
umparōṭu ulakukku ellām perumaruntu āki niṉṟāy
you remined as the big medicine superior to others both for the heaven and this world.
pēramutiṉ cuvaiyāyk karumaruntuāki uḷḷāy
you are the remedy for removing birth being the sweetness in the great nectar.
āḷum valviṉaikaḷ tīrkkum arumaruntu
the rare medicine that removes the irresistible acts that bind souls!
ālavāyil appaṉē
the father in ālavāy!
aruḷceyāy (ē)
grant me your grace.
{4:62}__4+
{$} tēvartēvē
the supreme god to all celestials!
maiaṇi kaṇṭattāṉē
Civaṉ who has a neck adorned by the poison!
māṉmaṟi maḻuoṉṟu ēntum caivaṉē
the first civaṉ who holds a young deer and a battle-axe in the hands!
ceyya(1)niṉ kamalapātam cērumā cāla ñāṉam kaṟṟu aṟivilāta nāyēṉ
myself like the low dog and do not possess knowledge by studying works or possessing spiritual knowledge in a great measure to meditate upon your feet without ceasing;
cērtal
thinking without ceasing;
[[taṉakku uvamai illātāṉ tāḷ cērntārkku allāl (Kuṟaḷ, 7)]]
aiyaṉē
my Lord!
ālavāyil appaṉē aruḷceyāy(ē)
the father in ālavāy! grant me your grace.
[[Variant reading: (1) nuṉ]]

mikavum ūrpali koṇṭu eṉṟum uṇṭatum illai

collil, uṇṭatu nañcutaṉṉai

{$} nañciṉai miṭaṟṟil vaitta naṟporuḷ patamē
the word, Civaṉ, which has good meaning and which placed the poison in the neck!
ālavāyil appaṉē
the father in ālavāy!
eñcalil pukaḻ itu eṉṟu eṉṟu
thinking always that this is my refuge which never knows extinction.
ētti nāṉ ēcaṟṟu eṉṟum vañcakam oṉṟum iṉṟi malaraṭi kāṇum vaṇṇam
with the purpose of having a vison of your lotus feet without the slightest deceit and always prising them longing for them.
nāyēṟku añcal eṉṟu aruḷceyāy (ē)
grant your grace to me who is low as a dog, assuring me Do not fear.

{$} teṉtirai nañcam uṇṭa kuḻakaṉē
the youth who consumed the poison that rose in the ocean of clear waves!
kōlavilli
god who holds a beautiful bow of mēru!
kūttaṉē
the dancer!
māttāy uḷḷa aḻakaṉē
the beautiful god who is the embodiment of greatness!
ālavāyil appaṉē
the father in ālavāy!
vaḻu ilātu uṉṉai vāḻtti vaḻipaṭum toṇṭaṉēṉ
myself who is your servant who worships and prises you without any blemish.
uṉ ceḻumalarp pātam kāṇa aruḷceyāy (ē)

{$} maṟikaṭal vaṇṇaṉpākā
Civaṉ who has on one half Māl who has the colour of the ocean where the waves roll up and down!
māmaṟai aṅkamāṟum aṟivaṉē
Civaṉ who knows the great vētams and six aṅkams!
ālavāyil appaṉē
the father in ālavāy!
naṟumalar nīrum koṇṭu nāḷtoṟum ētti vāḻtti

ceṟivaṉa cittam vaittu

tiruvaṭicērum vaṇṇam
to be always thinking of your holy feet.
aruḷ ceyāy (ē)
grant me your grace.

thinking that you granted your grace to be born in the coḻa family and rule over tamiḻakam, to the spider which built a cobweb like a beautiful panṭal with the fibre from its mouth so that dried leaves might not fall on the civaliṅkam;
[[ñāṉamuṭaiya oru cilanti nampar cempoṟ ṟirumuṭimēl, kāṉal viḷaiyum caruku utirā vaṇṇam kalanta vāynūlāl, mēlnal tirumēṟkaṭṭi eṉa virintu ceṟiyap purintuḷatu naṉṟum iḻaitta cilampivalaip parappai nātaṉ aṭivaṇaṅkac, ceṉṟayāṉai anucitam' eṉṟu ataṉaic citaikkac cilampitāṉ, iṉṟu kaḷiṟṟiṉ karam culaviṟṟu' eṉṟu mīḷa iḻaittu ataṉai, aṉṟu kaḻitta piṟṟai nāḷ aṭal veḷḷāṉai aḻittatāl, empirāṉtaṉ mēṉiyiṉmēl carukuviḻāmai yāṉ varunti, umpar iḻaitta nūlvalayam aḻippatē eṉṟu uruttu eḻuntu, vempic cilampi tutikkaiyiṉil pukkuk kaṭippa vēkattāl, kumpayāṉai kai nilattil mōtikkulaintu vīḻntatāl (periya purāṇam, Kōcceṅkaṭcōḻa nāyaṉār purāṇam, 3,4,5).]]
tiṇṇam kalantu uṭaṉ vantu niṉtāḷ karuti nāṉ kāṇpatāka alantaṉaṉ

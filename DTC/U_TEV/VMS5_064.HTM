{5:64}__1+
{$} pala ātarkaḷ
many ignorant people.
vēḻampattu aivar vēṇṭiṟṟu vēṇṭip pōy
having gone in the way the five jeering sensory organs desire, desiring them.
[[vēḻampam jeering lughter;]]
[[PP: nakai vēḻampa roṭu;
(cilappatikāram 5-53);]]
[[PP: cirikkum pariyāca karuṭaṉumeṉpa
(Do. aṭiyārkkunāllār's commentary)]]
āḻam paṟṟi vīḻvar
will fall into deep pit of worldly pleasures.
kōḻampattu urai kūttaṉ kurai kaḻal tāḻum pattarkaḷ cālac caturarē
those devotees who bow down to the feet which wear jingling armour, of the dancer who dwells in Kōlampam, are indeed very clever people.
[[kōḻampam is placed in the rhyme (cf.3).]]
{5:64}__2+
{$} kayilai naṉ malai āḷum
Civaṉ who is in the Kayili mountin
(1) kapāliyai
who has a skull in his hand.
mayil iyal malaimātiṉ maṇāḷaṉai
who is the husband of the dughter of the mountin, who is as beautiful as the peacock.
kuyil payil poḻiṟ kōḻampam mēviya eṉ uyiriṉai niṉaintu
thinking of my life who is in Kōḻampam which has gardens where indian cuckoos live.
uḷḷam urukum
my heart melts.
[[Variant reading: (1) kavāliyai)]]
{5:64}__3+
{$} vāḻum pāṉmaiyar ākiya vāṉ celvam
the great wealth that people who make their lives worth living, possess
tāḻum pāṉmaiyar āki
having the nature of making obeisance.
tam vāyiṉāl tāḻampū maṇam nāṟiya tāḻ poḻil kōḻampā eṉak kūṭiya celvamē
is the wealth that comes to them by saying with their tongues the Lord in Kōḻampam which has low gardens which spread the fragrance of the flowers of screw-pines.
{5:64}__4+
{$} peṇ ivaḷ
this girl.
paṇṇoṭu pāṭal ākkiṭum
will sing songs with tunes.
kuṉṟiṉ maṇaṟ koṭu kūṭal ākkiṭum
will draw with the sand which is large as a hill, circles.
[[kūṭal is a pastime of love-lorn ladies; they close their eyes and draw circles on sand: if they meet, their wish will be fulfilled, if not, it will not be fulfilled; there is another explanation: if circles are drawn on sand and counted, and if the number is an even number it hints the arrival of the lover, if it is an odd number it does not hint his coming.]]
kōṭal pūttu alar kōḻam pattuḷ makiḻntu āṭum kūttaṉukku aṉṟē aṉpu paṭṭāḷ
she fell in love with Civaṉ who dances with joy in Kōḻampam where the white and red malabar glory-lily blossom and unfolds its petals, on the very day she saw him.
{5:64}__5+
{$} taḷir koḷ mēṉiyaṉ tāṉ mika añca
the lady who has the complexion of tender leaves to be very much frightened.
piḷiṟu vāraṇattu īr uri pōrttavaṉ
one who covered himself with skin wet with blood of the roaring elephant.
kuḷir koḷ nīḷ vayal kōḻampam mēviṉāṉ naḷir koḷ nīr caṭaimēlum nayantu
It creates wonder that the god in Kōḻampam which has long and cool fields, desired the cool water of the Kaṅki on the caṭi also.
{5:64}__6+
{$} vēta nāvar
Civaṉ who chants the vetams by his tongue
viṭaik koṭiyār
who has a flag on which is drawn the form of a bull.
veṟpil kōtai mātoṭum kōḻampam kōyil koṇṭa āti pātam aṭaiya vallārkaḷ tām
only those who can approach the feet of the first cuse of all things who dwells in the temple with the lady wearing garlands, who is the dughter of the mountin.
nātar āvar, namakkum piṟarkkum
they will be masters for us and others.
{5:64}__7+
{$} aṉṉam ār vayal kōḻampattuḷ amar piṉṉal vār caṭaiyāṉaip pitaṟṟiyē
only by prattling the names of Civaṉ who has a long twining caṭi and who dwells in Kōḻampam having fields in which swans live
muṉṉai nāṉ ceyta pāvam mutal aṟap piṉṉai nāṉ peritum aruḷ (1) peṟṟatu
I received the grace of god in abundance after my sins which were committed in previous births, were cut at the root completely in this birth.
[[Variant reading: (1) peṟṟatum]]
{5:64}__8+
{$} ēḻaimāriṭam niṉṟu iru kaik koṭu uṇ (1) kōḻaimāroṭum kūṭiya kuṟṟam ām
It is due to the mistake of having joined with the timid people, jins who eat with both hands, standing before women.
(2) kūḻai pāy vayal kōḻampattāṉ aṭi ēḻaiyēṉ muṉ maṟantu aṅku iruntatē
that I who was not endowed with intelligence, lived in their company before, forgetting the feet of the Lord in Kōḻampam which has fields where mire spreads throughout the whole surface.
[[Variant readings: (1) kūḻaimāroṭum (2) kōḻai]]
{5:64}__9+
{$} aravaṇaip payil (1) māl ayaṉ vantu aṭi paravaṉai
Civaṉ whose feet Māl who always sleeps on the serpent bed and Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ) prise leaving their worlds (respectively Vaikuṇṭam and Catyalōkam) and coming to Civalōkam.
(2) param ām parañcōtiyai
the pre-eminent light divine
kuravaṉai
the preceptor (dakṣiṇāmūrti)
kuravu ār poḻil kōḻampattu uravaṉai oruvarkku uṇarvu oṇṇumē
is it possible for any one to know Civaṉ who has the form of knowledge and who is in Kōḻampam which has gardens of bottle flower-trees?
[[Variant reading: (1) mālavaṉ (2) paramā]]
{5:64}__10+
{$} camara (1) cūrapaṉmāvait taṭinta vēl kumaraṉ tātai
Civaṉ the father of Kumaraṉ who cut with his vēl Cūrapaṉmā who opposed him in battle.
naṟ kōḻampam mēviya amarar kōviṉukku aṉpuṭait toṇṭarkaḷ
those devotees who bestow their love on the chief of immortals, Civaṉ who dwells in the good place of Kōḻampam.
amara lōkam atu (2) āḷ uṭaiyārkaḷē
will rule over the world belonging to immortals.
[[PP_(cūrapaṉmāvait taṭinta vēl):
cūr mutal taṭinta cuṭar ilai neṭu vēl
(murukāṟṟuppaṭi, 46.)]]
[[The purāṇic story is that Cūraṉ assumed the form of a mango tree with roots above, contrary to nature and Murukaṉ cut that tree with his vēl;]]
[[PP: kaviḻ iṇar māmutal taṭinta...... cevvēṟ cēey
(murukāṟṟuppaṭi, 59-61.)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) cūravaṉmārpai, cūravaṉmāvai; (2) āḷvu]]
{5:64}__11+
{$} koṭṭam nāṟiya kōḻampattu īcaṉ eṉṟu iṭṭa kītam icaitta arakkaṉ
the arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ) who sang the songs which Civaṉ loved, saying, `Civaṉ in Kōḻampam which has the fragrance of costus root'.
tuṭṭaṉāki
being mischievous by nature.
malai (1) eṭuttu aḵtiṉ kīḻppaṭṭu
lifting the mountin, Kayili and being cught under it
vīḻntu
and falling.
paṭarntu uyyap pōyiṉāṉ
got the grace of Civaṉ with his help.
[[Variant readings: (1) eṭuttāṉuṅ kīḻ, eṭuttātiṉ kīḻ.]]

{2:55}__1+
{$} nalam caṅka veṇ kuḻaiyum tōṭum peytu
wearing a white and beautiful men's ear-ring made of conch and a women's ear-ring
ōr nāl vētam colac caṅkai ilātīr
you are above numbers when the Vētam-s describe you
cuṭukāṭu allāl karutātīr
you do not think of any other place (for dancing) except the cremation ground)
kulaic ceṅkāyp paiṅkamukiṉ kuḷirkoḷ cōlaik kuyil ālum, talaiccaṅkaik kōyilē (1) kōyilākat tāḻntīrē
you stayed in the temple at taliccanki where indian cuckoos sing in the cool gardens of tender areaca-palms which have put forth clusters of red fruit, as the temple fitting your greatness.
[[Variant reading: (1) kōyilākac cārntīrē]]
{2:55}__2+
{$} tuṇi malku kōvaṇamum tōlum kāṭṭit toṇṭu āṇṭīr
you admitted me as your protege people, showing the skin and the loin-cloth which is torn from a big cloth.
maṇimalku kaṇṭattīr
you have a neck which is full of blue colour like the sapphire.
aṇṭarkku ellām māṇpu āṉīr
you have greatness which is above the greatness of the celestials.
piṇi malku nūl mārpar periyōr vāḻum talaic caṅkai aṇi malku kōyilē kōyilāka amarntīr
you desired to stay in the very beautiful temple in taliccaṅki where great men who wear on their chest twisted sacred thread, as the temple fitting your greatness.
{2:55}__3+
{$} cīr koṇṭa pāṭalīr
you sing to the measure of time
[[you chant camavetam which can be set to music.]]
ceṅkaṇ veḷḷēṟu ūrtiyīr
you ride on a bull having red eyes.
nīr koṇṭum pūkkoṇṭum nīṅkāt toṇṭar niṉṟu ētta
the devotees who carry water to bathe you and flowers to adorn you and who never leave you, prise you standing.
tār koṇṭa nūl mārpar takkōr vāḻum talaic caṅkai, ērkoṇṭa kōyilē kōyilāka iruntīr
you occupied the beautiful temple in taliccanki where worthy brahmins who wear the sacred thread and a garland (of lotus) live, as the temple fitting your greatness
[[garland of lotus is the distinctive garland of brahmins;]]
[[PP: vaṇkamalat tār mikutta varai mārpaṉ campantaṉ- (campantar, tirutōṇipuram 11);]]
[[PP: alli am tāmaraittār ārūraṉ (cuntarar, paḻamaṇṇippaṭikkari, 10)]]
{2:55}__4+
{$} vēṇṭi vēṭam cūḻ koḷkaiyīr
you have the nature of thinking of assuming many forms, desiring them.
nīṇṭa veṇ tiṅkaḷ ōṭam cūḻ kaṅkaiyum uccivaittīr
you placed on the top of your head Kaṅki too where the white and long crescent goes round and round like a boat;
[[the crescent is compared to the boat;]]
[[PP: viyaṉ kaṅkai poṅki, varunīr maṭuvuḷ malaic ciṟutōṇi vaṭiviṉ veḷḷaik, kurunīr matipotiyum caṭai;
mantākiṉi nuntum pantap perumai, taḻicciṟai nīriṟ piṟaikkalam cērtaru tāravaṉē- (tiruvācakam, nīttal viṇṇappam, 26, 47);]]
[[PP: nāvāy piṟaic ceṉṉi (campantar talicceṅkāṭu, 4.)]]
talaic caṅkaik kūṭam cūḻ maṇṭapamum kulāyavācal koṭit tōṉṟum māṭam cūḻ kōyilē kōyilāka makiḻntīrē
you were happy to dwell in the temple at tāliccaṅki which has a pavilion surrounded by tower and storeys where flags are to be seen at the shining entrances, as the temple fitting your greatness.
{2:55}__5+
{$} cūlam cēr kaiyiṉīr
you hold a trident in your hand
cuṇṭai veṇṇīṟu āṭalīr
you bathe in the holy ash of fine powder just like bathing in water.
nīlam cēr kaṇṭattīr
you have a blue colour in your neck.
nīṇṭa caṭaimēl nīr ēṟṟīr
you received water on your long caṭi.
ālam cēr taṇ kāṉal aṉṉam maṉṉum talaic caṅkaik kōlam cēr kōyilē kōyilākak koṇṭīr
you occupied the beautiful temple in taliccanki where swans stay in the cool littoral gardens where there is plenty of water, as the temple fitting your greatness
{2:55}__6+
{$} nilam nīroṭu ākācam aṉal kāl āki niṉṟu
Being the earth, the water, the sky, the fire and the air.
aintu pulam nīrmai puṟaṅkaṇṭār
those who controlled the nature of the five sense perceptions.
pokkam ceyyār pōṟṟu ōvār
who do not commit any false acts, and never cease from prising god.
cala nītar allātār takkār vāḻum talaic caṅkai
in taliccanki where worthy people who are not low by their deceptive nature, live.
[[nītar-nīcar (low people)
ca and ta are interchangeable;]]
[[PP: nītar allār toḻu mā marukal (campantar; 1:006_9{s1_006}__ tirumarukalum tirucceṅkāttaṅkuṭiyum, 10);]]
[[PP: nītaṉēṉ - (Appar: tiruppuṉkūrum tirunīṭūrum (1) 10; tiruvāvatutuṟi, 4)]]
nala nīra kōyilē kōyilāka nayantīr
you desired the beautiful temple as the temple fitting your greatness.
{2:55}__7+
{$} aṭi pulku paiṅkaḻalkaḷ ārppap pērntu ōr aṉal ēnti
holding fire and dancing to make the golden armours of the feet that embrace them to make a sound
[[kaḻalkaḷ: the plural is used to denote the ornament Kaḻal worn for valour and victory;
kālaṉa puṉaikaḻal eṉpatu vīrattukkum veṉṟikkum kaṭṭiṉa; pōrtōṟum veṉṟu kaṭṭiṉa eṉavumām - (puṟam, 100-1, old commentary).]]
koṭi pulku meṉ cāyal umai ōr pākam kūṭiṉīr
you were united with Umi who has tenderness like the creeper, on one half.
poṭi pulku nūl mārpar, puri nūlāḷar talaic caṅkaik, kaṭi pulku kōyilē kōyilākak kalantīr
you joined the well-guarded temple in taliccaṅki where brahmins who desired the study of the vētams wear a sacred thread on their chest smeared with holy ash, as the temple fitting your greatness.
{2:55}__8+
{$} tirai ārnta mākaṭal cūḻ teṉ ilaṅkaik kōmāṉai varai ārnta tōḷ aṭara viralāl ūṉṟum māṇpiṉīr
you have the honour of pressing down your toe to crush the shoulders which were comparable to the mountin, of the king of ilaṅki in the south, girt by a great sea with waves.
arai ārnta mēkalaiyīr
you wear a girdle in the waist as an ornament
[[This refers to Umi who is one half.]]
[[Mēkali: a jewelled girdle of 7 or 8 strands worn by women on their waists.]]
antaṇāḷar talaic caṅkai nirai ārnta kōyilē kōyilāka niṉaintīr
you thought of dwelling in the temple with columns in taliccanki where brahmins are prosperous, as the temple fitting your greatness.
{2:55}__9+
{$} pāy ōṅku pāmpu aṇai mēlāṉum paintāmaraiyāṉum
Māl who lies on a superior serpent bed which is like a mat, and Piramaṉ who is seated on a cool lotus.
pōy ōṅkik kāṇkilār
were not able to find you though the former went digging into the earth and the latter went flying in the sky.
puṟam niṉṟu ōrār pōṟṟu ōvār
those who do not search for you outside the pale of civism will not cease from prising you
tī ōṅku maṟaiyāḷar tikaḻum celvat talaic caṅkaic cēyōṅku kōyilē kōyilākac cērntīr
you joined the tall temple which is visible from a great distance and where the wealthy brahmins who mintin the three fires are prosperous, as the temple fitting your greatness.
{2:55}__10+
{$} alai ārum puṉal tuṟanta amaṇar, kuṇṭar (1) cākkiyar
amaṇar who do not bathe in water which has waves, and cākkiyar, the low people
tolaiyātu aṅku alar tūṟṟa
spread slander without being exhusted
tōṟṟam kāṭṭi āṭ koṇṭīr
you admitted me into your grace showing me your divine form.
talaiyāṉa nāl vētam tarittār vāḻum talaic caṅkai, nilai ārnta kōyilē kōyilāka niṉṟīr
you stayed for ever in the temple with many floors in taliccanki where brahmins who vetam in mind all the four important vetams liue, as the temple fitting your greatness.
{2:55}__11+
{$} kuḷirum talaic caṅkai ōṅku kōyil mēyāṉai
on Civaṉ who dwells in the tall temple in cool taliccanki
kuḷirum piṟaiyāṉai
and who wears a cool crescent
naḷirum puṉal kāḻi nalla ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ of good qualities who is a native of Kāḻi of cool water
uraitta pāṭal ivai vallār
those who are capable of reciting these verses composed (by him)
miḷirum tirai cūḻnta vaiyattārkku mēlār
will become tevar to the people of this world which is girt by the sea having waves rolling up and down.

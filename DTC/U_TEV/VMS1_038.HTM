{1:38}__1+
{$} karavuiṉṟi nal māmalar (1) koṇṭu
with big and defectless flowers, and without deceit.
iravum pakalum toḻuvārkaḷ ciram oṉṟiya ceñcaṭaiyāṉ vāḻ
the place where the god of red caṭai, who is on the heads of those who worship him night and day, dwells.
varam māmayilāṭu tuṟaiyē
is the superior and great mayilāṭutuṟai
[[Mayilāṭutuṟai is now known as māyūram]]
[[Variant reading: (1) koṇṭē ]]
{1:38}__2+
{$} kuravam curapuṉṉaiyum vaṉṉimaruvum mayilāṭutuṟai
mayilāṭutuṟai where bottle-flower trees, curapuṉṉai trees (Long-leaved two-sepalled gamboge) and indian mesquit trees, grow.
uraveṅkariyiṉ uripōrtta paramaṉ uṟaiyum pati eṉpar
people say that it is the place where the supreme Civaṉ who covered himself with the skin of a strong and cruel elephant, dwells
[[curapuṉṉai tree is now commonly known as nākalinkam tree.]]
{1:38}__3+
{$} ūṉattu iruḷ nīṅkiṭavēṇṭil
(People of this world!) if you want to be free from aṇavam, the impurity which causes blemish.
tēṉ ottu iṉiyāṉ amarum cērvāṉa(m) mayilāṭutuṟaiyē ñāṉapporuḷkoṇṭu aṭipēṇum
cherish with love the feet with spiritual wisdom mayilāṭutuṟai which is the place of Civaṉ who is as sweet as honey (to devotees)
[[cērvāṉam mayilāṭutuṟai: m is augmentation for rhythm]]
{1:38}__4+
{$} añcu oṇpulaṉum avai ceṟṟa mañcaṉ mayilāṭutuṟaiyē neñcu oṉṟi niṉaintu eḻuvārmēl piṇiyāyiṉa
the diseases which are with those who wake up from sleep and meditate upon on mayilāṭutuṟai which is the place of the strong youth who controlled the five clever sense perceptions.
tuñcum
will die.
{1:38}__5+
{$} piṇi āyiṉatīrttu aruḷ ceyyum maṇiyāṉ taṇi ārmati ceñcaṭaiyāṉtaṉ mayilāṭutuṟai aṇi ārntavarukku aruḷ eṉṟum (uḷatām)
there will always be the grace to those who have approached mayilāṭutuṟai which is the place of Civaṉ of red caṭai on which there is a cool crescent, and who is like a ruby, which grants its grace curing diseases
[[taṉi coolness; taṇipuṉal, taṇimaḻai (paripātal 6-29, 9-11)]].
[[aṇi nearness
PP: iṉi aṇi eṉṉum kālaiyum iṭaṉum viṉai eñcukiḷaviyum cuṭṭum aṉṉa
(tolkāppiyam, 1-7-34)]].
{1:38}__6+
{$} paṇṭum palavētiyar ōta
when many brahmins chant the vetams from old times.
vaṇṭu ār mayilāṭutuṟaiyē
mayilāṭutuṟai where bees hum as if opposed to it.
toṇṭar icaipāṭiyum kūṭikkaṇṭu tuticeypavaṉ ūrām
is the place of Civaṉ who is praised by devotees who see him by singing music and gathering there
[[the humming of the bees is compared to the sound produced when brahmins chant the vētams;
PP: pōtu piṇiviṭṭa kamaḻ naṟumpoykait, tātu uṇ tumpipōtu muraṉṟāṅki, ōtal antaṇam vētam pāṭa
(maturaikkāñci, 654-656)]].
{1:38}__7+
{$} nuṇaṅkum purinūlarkaḷ kūṭivaṇaṅkum mayilāṭutuṟaiyē
mayilāṭutuṟai where the brahmins wearing moving sacred thread of three strands, join together in worship.
aṇaṅkōṭu orupākam amarntu iṇaṅki aruḷ ceytavaṉ ūrām
is the place of Civaṉ who bestows his grace by being united with the divine lady on one half.
{1:38}__8+
{$} ciramkaiyiṉil ēnti
holding a skull in the hand.
iranta paramkoḷ paramēṭṭi
the god who is in an exalted place which is superior to all others, and who begged alms.
arakkaṉ araṅka varaiyāl valiceṟṟa, varamkoḷ mayilāṭutuṟaiyē
mayilāṭutuṟai is the eminent place of Civaṉ (peramētti) who destroyed the strength of the arakkaṉ to suffer, by the mountain.
[[araṅkav varakkaṉ: v augmentation for rhythm]]
{1:38}__9+
{$} ñālattai nukarntavaṉ(tāṉ) um kōlattu ayaṉum aṟiyāta cīlattavaṉ ūr
the place of Civaṉ whose nature could not be known by the beautiful Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ) and Māl who swallowed the earth.
cilar kūṭi mālaittīr mayilāṭutuṟaiyē
is mayiḷātutuṟai where some devotees join together and remove their confusion.
{1:38}__10+
{$} niṉṟu uṇ camaṇum neṭuntērar oṉṟum aṟiyāmai uyarnta veṉṟi (1) aruḷāṉ avaṉ ūrām
the place of Civaṉ who cause victory by his grace and who is great so that the camaṇar who eat standing and the tall tēvar (buddhists) know nothing about him.
maṉṟal mayilāṭutuṟaiyē
is mayilāṭutuṟai where there is always sweet fragrance.
[[Variant reading: (1) aruḷām]]
{1:38}__11+
{$} nayar kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ who was born in Kaḻi where people of good qualities reside.
mayaltīr mayilāṭutuṟaimēl ceyalāl uraiceytaṉapattum ivai uṟṟu uṇarvārkku uyarvu ām
those who understand clearly the ten verses composed with the grace of god on mayilāṭutuṟai which removes delusion of mind will rise to eminence.

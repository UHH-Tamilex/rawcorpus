• patikam: [3:3]
  • talam: tirup pukali (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: kāntārap pañcamam
  • (()): nālaṭimēl vippu
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.221

*1
_iyal _ici _eṉum poruḷiṉ tiṟam_ām
puyal _aṉa miṭaṟu _uṭip puṇṇiyaṉē!
kayal _aṉa varineṭuṅkaṇṇiyoṭum
_ayal _ulaku _aṭi toḻa _amarntavaṉē!---
kalaṉ _āvatu veṇtali; kaṭipoḻil pukalitaṉṉuḷ,
nilaṉ nāḷtoṟum _iṉpu_uṟa, niṟi mati _aruḷiṉaṉē.

*2
nili_uṟum _iṭar niliyāta vaṇṇam
_ili _uṟu malarkaḷ koṇṭu _ēttutum, yām;
maliyiṉil _ariviyi veruva, vaṉ tōl
_alivaru matakari _urittavaṉē!
_imiyōrkaḷ niṉ tāḷ toḻa, _eḻil tikaḻ poḻil pukali
_umiyāḷoṭu maṉṉiṉi---_uyar tiruvaṭi_iṇiyē.

*3
pāṭiṉi, _arumaṟi varalmuṟiyāl;
_āṭiṉi, kāṇa muṉ _aruvaṉattil;
cāṭiṉi, kālaṉi; tayaṅku _oḷi cēr
nīṭu veṇpiṟi muṭi niṉmalaṉē!
niṉiyē _aṭiyār toḻa, neṭumatil pukali(n)nakar-
taṉiyē _iṭam mēviṉi; tavaneṟi _aruḷ, _emakkē!

*4
niḻal tikaḻ maḻuviṉi! yāṉiyiṉtōl
_aḻal tikaḻ mēṉiyil _aṇintavaṉē!
kaḻal tikaḻ cilampu _oli _alampa, nalla
muḻavoṭum _arunaṭam muyaṟṟiṉaṉē!
muṭimēl mati cūṭiṉi! muruku _amar poḻil pukali
_aṭiyār_avar _ēttu_uṟa, _aḻakoṭum _iruntavaṉē!

*5
karumiyiṉ _oḷir kaṭalnañcam _uṇṭa
_urimiyiṉ, _ulaku_uyir _aḷitta niṉtaṉ
perumiyi nilattavar pēciṉ_allāl,
_arumiyil _aḷappu _aritu_āyavaṉē!
_aravu _ēr _iṭiyāḷoṭum, _alikaṭal mali pukali,
poruḷ cērtara nāḷtoṟum puvimicip polintavaṉē!

*6
_aṭi _arimāvoṭu, vēṅkiyiṉtōl,
puṭi paṭa _arimicip puṉintavaṉē!
paṭi _uṭi neṭumatil paricu _aḻitta,
viṭi _uṭik koṭi malku, vētiyaṉē!
vikirtā! paramā!---niṉṉi viṇṇavar toḻa, pukalit
takuvāy, maṭamātoṭum, tāḷ paṇintavartamakkē.

*7
_aṭiyavar toḻutu _eḻa, _amarar _ētta,
ceṭiya valviṉipala tīrppavaṉē!
tuṭi_iṭi _akal_alkul-tūmoḻiyip
poṭi _aṇi mārpu _uṟap pulkiṉaṉē!
puṇṇiyā! puṉitā! pukar _ēṟṟiṉi! pukalinnakar
naṇṇiṉāy!---kaḻal _ēttiṭa, naṇṇakilā, viṉiyē.

*8
_iravoṭu, pakal_atu, _ām _emmāṉ! _uṉṉip
paravutal _oḻikilēṉ, vaḻi_aṭiyēṉ;
kura, viri naṟuṅkoṉṟi, koṇṭu _aṇinta
_ara viricaṭimuṭi _āṇṭakiyē!
_aṉameṉnaṭiyāḷoṭum, _atirkaṭal _ilaṅki maṉṉi
_iṉam _ārtaru tōḷ _aṭarttu, _iruntaṉi, pukaliyuḷē.

*9
_urukiṭa _uvaki tantu _uṭaliṉuḷḷāl,
parukiṭum _amutu _aṉa paṇpiṉaṉē!
porukaṭalvaṇṇaṉum pū_uḷāṉum
perukiṭum maruḷ _eṉap piṟaṅku _eri_āy
_uyarntāy! _iṉi, nī _eṉi _oṇmalar_aṭi_iṇikkīḻ
vayantu _āṅku _uṟa nalkiṭu, vaḷarmatil pukali maṉē!

*10
kiyiṉil _uṇpavar, kaṇikanōṉpar,---
ceyvaṉa tavam _alāc cetumatiyār,
poyyavar---_urikaḷip poruḷ _eṉāta
meyyavar _aṭi toḻa virumpiṉaṉē!
viyantāy, veḷ_ēṟṟiṉi viṇṇavar toḻu pukali
_uyarntu _ār peruṅkōyiluḷ _oruṅku_uṭaṉ _iruntavaṉē!

*11
puṇṇiyar toḻutu _eḻu pukali(n)nakar,
viṇṇavar _aṭi toḻa viḷaṅkiṉāṉi,
naṇṇiya ñāṉacampantaṉ vāymi
paṇṇiya _aruntamiḻpattum vallār,
naṭali_avi _iṉṟip pōy naṇṇuvar, civaṉ_ulakam;
_iṭar_āyiṉa _iṉṟit tām _eytuvar, tavaneṟiyē.

*1
vāṉavarkaḷ tāṉavarkaḷ vātipaṭa vantatu _oru mākaṭalviṭam
tāṉ _amutuceytu, _aruḷpurinta civaṉ mēvum malitaṉṉi viṉavil---
_ēṉam _iṭamāṉiṉoṭu kiḷḷi tiṉi koḷḷa, _eḻil _ār kavaṇiṉāl,
kāṉavartam mā makaḷir kaṉakam maṇi vilaku kāḷattimaliyē.

*2
mutu ciṉa vil _avuṇar purammūṉṟum _oru noṭivariyiṉ mūḷa _ericey
caturar, mati poti caṭiyar, caṅkarar, virumpum malitaṉṉi viṉavil---
_etir _etira vetir piṇiya, _eḻu poṟikaḷ citaṟa, _eḻil _ēṉam _uḻuta
katir maṇiyiṉ vaḷar _oḷikaḷ, _iruḷ _akala nilavu kāḷattimaliyē.

*3
valli varu kāḷiyi vakuttu, vali _āki miku tārukaṉi nī
kol! _eṉa viṭuttu, _aruḷpurinta civaṉ mēvum mali kūṟi viṉavil---
palpala _iruṅ kaṉi paruṅki mika _uṇṭavi neruṅki _iṉam_āy,
kal _atira niṉṟu, karu manti viḷiyāṭu kāḷattimaliyē.

*4
vēy _aṉiya tōḷ _umi _orpākam_atu_āka viṭi _ēṟi, caṭimēl
tūya mati cūṭi, cuṭukāṭil naṭam_āṭi, malitaṉṉi viṉavil---
vāy kalacam _āka vaḻipāṭu ceyum vēṭaṉ malar _ākum nayaṉam
kāykaṇiyiṉāl _iṭantu, _īcaṉ_aṭi kūṭu kāḷattimaliyē.

*5
maliyiṉmicitaṉil mukil pōl varuvatu _oru matakariyi maḻi pōl _alaṟak
koliceytu, _umi _añca, _uri pōrtta civaṉ mēvum mali kūṟi viṉavil---
_ali koḷ puṉal _aruvi palacuṉikaḷ vaḻi _iḻiya, _ayal nilavu mutu vēy
kalakaleṉa _oḷi koḷ katir muttam_avi cintu kāḷattimaliyē.

*6
pār_akam viḷaṅkiya pakīrataṉ _aruntavam muyaṉṟa paṇi kaṇṭu,
_ār _aruḷ purintu, _ali koḷ kaṅki caṭi _ēṟṟa _araṉ maliyi viṉavil---
vār _atar _iruṅ kuṟavar cēvalil maṭuttu, _avar _eritta viṟakil
kār_akil _irum puki vicumpu kamaḻkiṉṟa kāḷattimaliyē.

*7
_ārum _etirāta vali_ākiya calantaraṉi _āḻi_ataṉāl
_īrum vaki ceytu, _aruḷpurintavaṉ _irunta malitaṉṉi viṉavil---
_ūrum _aravam(m) _oḷi koḷ mā maṇi _umiḻntavi _ulāvi varalāl,
kār_iruḷ kaṭintu, kaṉakam(m) _eṉa viḷaṅku kāḷattimaliyē.

*8
_eri _aṉiya curimayir _irāvaṇaṉi _īṭu _aḻiya, _eḻil koḷ viralāl,
periya vari _ūṉṟi _aruḷceyta civaṉ mēvum mali peṟṟi viṉavil---
variya cili vēṭuvarkaḷ_āṭavarkaḷ nīṭu vari_ūṭu varalāl,
kariyiṉoṭu vari_uḻuvi _ari_iṉamum veruvu kāḷattimaliyē.

*9
_iṉatu _aḷavil, _ivaṉatu _aṭi_iṇiyum, muṭi, _aṟituma _eṉa _ikalum _iruvar
taṉatu _uruvam _aṟivu _ariya cakala civaṉ mēvum malitaṉṉi viṉavil---
puṉavar puṉamayil _aṉiya mātaroṭu mintarum maṇam puṇarum nāḷ,
kaṉakam _eṉa malarkaḷ _aṇi vēṅkikaḷ nilāvu kāḷattimaliyē.

*10
niṉṟu kavaḷampala koḷ kiyaroṭu, meyyil _iṭu pōrviyavarum,
naṉṟi _aṟiyāta vaki niṉṟa civaṉ mēvum mali nāṭi viṉavil---
kuṉṟil mali tuṉṟu poḻil niṉṟa kuḷir cantiṉ muṟi tiṉṟu kulavi,
kaṉṟiṉoṭu ceṉṟu piṭi niṉṟu viḷiyāṭu kāḷattimaliyē.

*11
kāṭu_atu _iṭam_āka naṭam_āṭu civaṉ mēvu kāḷattimaliyi,
māṭamoṭu māḷikikaḷ nīṭu vaḷar koccivayam maṉṉu talivaṉ---
nāṭupala nīṭu pukaḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ---_uri nalla tamiḻiṉ
pāṭaloṭu pāṭum _ici vallavarkaḷ nallar; paralōkam _eḷitē.

veḷḷattaic caṭaiyil vaitta vētakītaṉ taṉ pātam meḷḷattāṉ aṭaiya vēṇṭiṉ
(People of this world!) if you want to reach the feet of the Lord who sang the Vētam-s and who placed the flood of the Kaṅki on his head, patiently listen to the way by which you can attin them.
mey taru ñāṉat tīyāl kaḷḷattaik kaḻiya niṉṟār (1) kāyattuk kalantu niṉṟu
being united in the body of those who have burnt with the help of the fire of spiritual wisdom which grants the ultimate reality, all evil thoughts.
uḷḷattuḷ eḷiyum ākum oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē
the king of Oṟṟiyūr is the light in the hearts of such people
[[PP: uṭampu eṉum maṉaiyakattu uḷḷamē takaḷiyāka maṭampaṭu uṇarney aṭṭi uyireṉum tirimayakki iṭampaṭu ñāṉattīyāl erikoḷa iruntu nōkkil kaṭampu amar kāḷai tātai kaḻalaṭi kāṇalāmē (nāvukkaracar potu, taṉitirunērici, 4)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) kāyattaikkalantu - kālattum kalantu - kayattuk kalantu]]

vacippu ēṉum vāḻkkai vēṇṭā
you do not want the life of bondage in a body in this world subject to time and space.

tāṉattaic ceytu vāḻvāṉ calattuḷē aḻuntu kiṉṟīr
(people of this world!) you sink low in the falsehood with the idea of living long by being liberal in your gifts.
vāṉattai vaṇaṅka vēṇṭil vallīrākil vammiṉkaḷ
if you are capable of bowing before the space of grace (god) please come.
ñāṉattai viḷakkai ēṟṟi nāṭi
investigating, having lit the lamp of spiritual wisdom.
oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē
the king who has oṟṟiyūr.
uḷ virava vallār ūṉattai oḻippār (pōlum)
will completely remove the defects of those who can mingle with Civaṉ in the heart.

kāmattuḷ aḻunti niṉṟu
sinking deeply in love.
1. kaṇṭarāl oṟuppuṇṇāmē
without being punished by the god of death and his servants.
cāmattu vētam āki
being the cāma vetam (attu is an augmentation used where it is not absolutely necessary)
niṉṟatu ōr cayamputaṉṉai emattum iṭai irāvum ēkāntam 2. iyampuvārkku
to those who utter the names of the self-existent Civaṉ who is eternal in all the four periods of three hours in the day time and at midnight with the decision that Civaṉ is the supreme god.
oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē ōmattuḷ oḷi (atu) ākum
the Lord who has oṟṟiyūr is the light in the sacrificial fire.
[[Variant reading: 1. kaṇṭārāl 2. vārukku ōmattu.]]

camaiya mēl āṟum āki
being all the six religious systems.
tāṉ oru cayampu āki
himself being a self existent god.
imaiyavar paravi ētta iṉitiṉ aṅku irunta īcaṉ
the Lord of the universe who remined sweetly to be prised by the celestials, who do not wink, in the second and the third persons.
kamaiyiṉai uṭaiyarāki
having patience.
kaḻal aṭi 1. paravuvārkku
to those who prise in the second person his feet wearing kaḻal
oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē
the Lord who has oṟṟiyūr as his place.
umai oru pākar (pōlum)
will grant vision as the Lord has Umi on one half.
[[Variant reading: 1. paravuvāru/kku umaiyorupākar]]

orutti taṉ talaic ceṉṟāṉaik karantiṭṭāṉ ulakam ētta
Civaṉ concealed one lady kaṅki who descended down, on his head to be prised by the people of this world.
oruttikku nallaṉ āki
being like a good person to that kaṅki.
maṟuppaṭuttu
creating a blot.
oḷittu
concealing her from being seen by Umi.
mīṇṭē oruttiyaip pākam vaittāṉ
placed agin one lady on one half.
uṇarviṉāl aiyaṉ uṇṇi
eats the alms of intelligence.
oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē oruttikkum nallaṉ allaṉ
the Lord who has oṟṟiyūr as his place is not good to either of those two.

piṇam muṭai uṭalukkākap pittarāyt tirintu nīṅkaḷ puṇarvu eṉum pōkam vēṇṭā
(People of this world!) Do not have the desire of having sexual intercourse and deriving pleasure from it, wandering having excessive attachment towards the body which will ultimately become a corpse and from which stench of flesh will be issuing, to support it on several ways.
pōkkalām poyyai nīṅka
to be separated from the acts which are the cuse of births.
niṇam uṭai neñciṉ uḷḷāl niṉaiyumā 1. niṉaikkiṉṟārkku
to those who meditate in their hearts which have fat, in the proper manner.
oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē uṇarviṉōṭu iruppar (pōlum)
the Lord who has oṟṟiyūr as his place will be united with their knowledge of Civaṉ and remin as such.
[[Variant reading: 1. niṉaikkiṉṟāru/kku ]]

piṉṉuvār caṭaiyāṉ taṉṉaip pitaṟṟilāp pētaimārkaḷ
the ignorant people who do not babble the names of Civaṉ who has a long and interwoven caṭi.
narakam taṉṉuḷ tuṉṉuvār
will sink into the hell.
tolviṉai tīra vēṇṭiṉ
if you want to be rid of the old accumulated acts of previous births.
maṉṉu vāṉ maṟaikaḷ ōti maṉattiṉuḷ viḷakku oṉṟu ēṟṟi
chanting the great and eternal vētam and lighting the lamp of spiritual knowledge.
uṉṉuvār uḷḷattu uḷḷār oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē
the Lord who has oṟṟiyūr as his place dwells in the minds of those who think of him always.

veṟuttu ukap pulaṉkaḷ aintum vēṇṭiṟṟu vēṇṭum neñcē
my mind! desires the five sense organs will desire only those which they desired agin and agin, so that they will be destroyed detesting them.
ārvac ceṟṟak kurōtaṅkaḷ āṉa māya maṟuttu uka
rejoice refusing to satisfy desires, enmity and anger to perish.
poṟuttu ukap puṭpakattēr uṭaiyāṉai aṭara ūṉṟi oṟuttu ukantu aruḷkaḷ ceytār oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē
the Lord who has Oṟṟiyūr as his place, punished by fixing his toe firmly to press down the arakkaṉ who has a chariot by name Putpakam; putting up with his mischief and to make him perish and later granted his graces by giving him a sword, long life, and the title Irāvaṇaṉ

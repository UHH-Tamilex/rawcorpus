[[When Cuntārar came to Tiruvālampoḻil after worshipping shrines on the way, Civaṉ appeared in his dream and asked him, Did you forget Maḻapāṭi? After waking up from sleep, he reached Maḻapāṭi and sang this decade of verses]]

{7:24}__1+
{$} poṉ ār mēṉiyaṉē
Civan who has a divine body like gold?
pulittōlai araikku acaittu
having fastened a tiger's skin in the waist.
miṉ ār ceñcaṭaimēl miḷirkoṉṟai aṇintavaṉē
one who adorned his red caṭai which is like the lightning, with shinning koṉṟai flowers!
[[miṉeṟiceñcaṭaikkūttappirāṉ (Kōvaiyār, 49)]]
maṉṉē
the chief!
māmaṇiyē
you who are as highly valuable as a big ruby!
maḻapāṭiyuḷ māṇikkamē
the ruby in maḻapāṭi!
aṉṉē
one who is as affectionate as a mother!
uṉṉai allāl iṉi ārai niṉaikkēṉē
whom else shall I think now except you?
[[I won't think of anyone else is the implied meaning. It is the same for the following nine verses.]]
{7:24}__2+
{$} kīḷ ār kōvaṇamum tirunīṟum meypūci
you who besmeared yourself with the holy ash and having worn a loin-cloth with a piece if cloth used as waist-band!
[[pūci is not an adverbial participle: it is a noun.]]
uṉtaṉtāḷē vantu aṭaintēṉ
I approached your feet themselves as my refuge.
talaivā
my master!
nī eṉai ēṉṟukoḷ
you please receive me as your protege.
vāḷ ār kaṇṇipaṅkā
one who has as his half a lady whose eyes are like sword.
maḻapāṭiyuḷ māṇikkamē
the ruby in maḻapāṭi.
niṉṉaiyallāl (1) kēḷā iṉiyārai niṉaikkēṉē
whom else shall I think now except you as my relation?
[[Variant reading: (1) āḷāy, kēḷāy]]
{7:24}__3+
{$} maimmāmpūmpoḻilcūḻ maḻapāṭiyuḷ māṇikkamē
Civaṉ who is as precious as a ruby in maḻapāṭi surrounded by thriving groves of mango trees on which the cloud seems to crawl!
emmāṉ emmaṉai eṉṟu eḷtuṇaiccārvu ākār
These relations like my father and mother will not be of even a small amount of support to me.
[[eḷtuṇai: size of a single grain of sesame: a simile used in literature in denote smallness of anything just like indian mustard; kaṭukaḷavu]]
[[PP: eḷtuṇaiyāṉum iravātutāṉ ītal (iṉiyavai nāṟpatu, 16).]]
immāyappiṟavi piṟantē iṟantu eyttu oḻintēṉ
thinking of them as my companions, I was born in this birth which is not permanent and died; I became completely tired.
(1) ammāṉ
our master.
niṉṉai allāl iṉi ārai niṉaikkēṉ
whom else shall I think now except you?
[[Variant reading: (1) emmāṉ]]
{7:24}__4+
{$} vaṇṭu ār pūmpoḻilcūḻ maḻapāṭiyuḷ māṇikkamē
Civaṉ who is as valuble as a ruby in maḻapāṭi surrounded by flower-gardens where bees hum noisily.
paṇṭē niṉ aṭiyēṉ
I am your serf since time immemorial when you accepted me as your protege.
aṭiyār aṭiyārkaṭku ellām koṇṭē pūṇṭoḻintēṉ
I definitely performed service to all the devotees of your devotees.
toṭarāmaitturicu aṟuttēṉ
I effaced, without even a trace of it, the fault which was the cause for not having any correction with you or with your devotees.
aṇṭā
Civaṉ dwelling in heaven!
niṉṉai allāl iṉi ārai niṉaikkēṉ
whom else shall! I think now except you?
{7:24}__5+
{$} kaṇṇāy
being the knowledge.
ēḻ ulakum karuttāya aruttamumāy
being the objects which are desired by the living beings in the seven worlds.
paṇ āriṉtamiḻāy
Being the sweet tamiḻ which is full of music.
param āya parañcuṭarē
the supreme light which transcends all things!
maṇ ār pūmpoḻilcūḻ maḻapāṭiyuḷ māṇikkamē
the god who is as valuable as a ruby in maḻapāṭi surrounded by flower-gardens which beautify that place.
aṇṇā
civan who is older than all and therefore is in the relation of elder brothers to all!
niṉṉai allāl iṉi ārai niṉaikkēṉ
whom else shall I think now except you?
{7:24}__6+
{$} nāḷār vantu aṇuki naliyāmuṉam
before the last day approaches and afflicts me.
niṉ taṉakkē āḷāvantu aṭaintēṉ
I came to you only as your protege.
nī aṭiyēṉaiyum ēṉṟukoḷ
you receive your humble servant too.
māḷā nāḷ aruḷum maḻapāṭiyuḷ māṇikkamē
the ruby in maḻapāṭi who grants everlasting life!
āḷāy niṉṉai allāl iṉi ārai niṉaikkēṉ
having become your humble servant, whom else shall I think now except you?
{7:24}__7+
{$} cantu ārum kuḻaiyāy
one who wears an ear-ring which has joints!
caṭaimēṟpiṟaitāṅki
Civaṉ who bears a crescent on the caṭai.
[[tāṅki a noun; if it is taken as an adverbial participle it should be linked with poṭiyāy]]
nallaventuārveṇpoṭiyāy
one who smears white, well burnt and good holy ash!
viṭaiēṟiyavittakaṉē
able person who rides on a bull!
maintu ārcōlaikaḷcūḻ maḻapāṭiyuḷ māṇikkamē
the ruby in maḻapāṭi surrounded by very beautiful gardens.
entāy
my father!
niṉṉai allāl iṉi yārai niṉaikkēṉ
whom else shall I think now except you!
{7:24}__8+
{$} veyya viricuṭarōṉ mikutēvar kaṇaṅkaḷ ellām ceyyamalarkaḷ iṭamikucemmaiyuḷniṉṟavaṉē
Civaṉ who stood steadfast in impartiality and granted your grace when the hot sun of radiating rays and other superior groups of tēvar scattered flawless flowers and worshipped you!
mai ārpūmpoḻilcūḻ maḻapāṭiyuḷ māṇikkamē
the ruby in maḻapāṭi surrounded by flower gardens on which clouds settle
[[mai ār may mean think darkness due to the denseness of trees.]]
aiyā
master!
niṉṉai allāl iṉi ārai niṉaikkēṉ
whom else shall I think now except you!
{7:24}__9+
{$} neṟiyē
Civaṉ who is the right path to living beings!
niṉmalaṉē
one who is not contaminated by the impurities of the soul!
neṭumāl ayaṉ pōṟṟiceyyum kuṟiyē
the object of meditation and prayer for tall Māl and Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ)
nīrmaiyaṉē
one who has benign qualities!
koṭi ēr iṭaiyāḷ talaivā
the husband of Umai who has a beautiful waist like a creeper!
maṟicēr aṅkaiyaṉē
one who holds in his palm a young deer!
maḻapāṭiyuḷmāṇikkamē
the ruby in maḻapāṭi!
aṟivē
the embodiment of all knowledge!
niṉṉai allāl iṉi ārai niṉaikkēṉē
whom else shall I think now except you?
{7:24}__10+
{$} ēr ār muppuramum eriyaccilai toṭṭavaṉai
on Civaṉ who made use of the bow to burn the beautiful three cities.
vārārkoṅkaiyuṭaṉ maḻappāṭiyuḷmēyavaṉai
and the one who dwells in maḻapāṭi along with a lady wearing a bodice on her breast.
cīrār nāvalarkōṉ ārūraṉ uraitta tamiḻ-pārōr ēttavallār
the people of this world who can praise Civaṉ with the tamiḻ verses composed by ārūraṉ who is the important person in the far-famed nāvalūr.
paralōkattu iruppār
will live happily in the superior civalokam.

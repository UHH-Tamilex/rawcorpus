*1
vaṭivu _uṭi maḻu _ēnti, matakari _uri pōrttu,
poṭi _aṇi tirumēṉip purikuḻal_umiyōṭum,
koṭi _aṇi neṭumāṭak kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_aṭikaḷ _iv vaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*2
viyakammuḻutu _uṇṭa māloṭu, nāṉmukaṉum,
pi_aravu _iḷa _alkul pāviyoṭum(m), _uṭaṉē,
koy _aṇi malarc cōlik kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_aiyaṉ _ivvaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*3
_ūrtoṟum veṇtali koṇṭu, _uṇ pali _iṭum! _eṉṟu,
vār taru meṉmuliyāḷ maṅkiyoṭum(m)_uṭaṉē,
kūrnuṉi maḻu _ēnti, kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_ārvaṉ _iv vaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*4
cantu _aṇavum puṉalum tāṅkiya tāḻcaṭiyaṉ
pantu _aṇavum viralāḷ pāviyoṭum(m)_uṭaṉē,
kontu _aṇavum poḻil cūḻ kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_antaṇaṉ vaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*5
vētiyar viṇṇavarum maṇṇavarum toḻa, nal
cōti_atu_uru _āki, curikuḻal_umiyōṭum,
kōtiya vaṇṭu _aṟiyum kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_āti _iv vaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*6
vittaka vīṇiyoṭum, veṇpurinūl pūṇṭu,
muttu _aṉa veṇmuṟuval maṅkiyoṭum(m)_uṭaṉē,
kottu _alarum poḻil cūḻ kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_attaṉ _iv vaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*7
maḻi nuḻi matiyamoṭu vāḷ_aravam caṭimēl
_iḻi nuḻi tukil _alkul _ēntiḻiyāḷōṭum,
kuḻi _aṇi tikaḻ cōlik kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_aḻakaṉ _iv vaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*8
maṟi mutal vāṉavarum, māl, _ayaṉ, _intiraṉum,
piṟinutal maṅkiyoṭum, pēykkaṇamum, cūḻa,
kuṟaḷpaṭi _ataṉōṭum, kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_aṟavaṉ _iv vaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*9
vēliyiṉ nañcu _uṇṭu, viṭi_atu tāṉ _ēṟi,
pāl _aṉa meṉmoḻiyāḷ pāviyoṭum(m)_uṭaṉē,
kōlam_atu _uru_āki, kūṭaliyāṟṟūril
_ālaṉ _iv vaḻi pōnta _aticayam _aṟiyēṉē!

*10

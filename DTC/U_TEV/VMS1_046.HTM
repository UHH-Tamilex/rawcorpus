{1:46}__1+
{$} kuṇṭaik kuṟaḷ pūtam kuḻuma
the pūtams which are dwarf, short and stout to collect in large numbers.
aṉal ēnti
holding a fire.
keṇṭaip piṟaḻ teṇṇīrk keṭila vaṭa pakkam
on the northern side of the Ketilam which has clear water on which Keṇti, a fresh water fish, leaps.
vaṇṭu maruḷpāṭa
the bees hum, resembling marūḷ
[[a secondary melody-type]]
vaḷarpoṉ virikoṉṟaiviṇṭa toṅkalāṉ āṭum vīraṭṭāṉattu (ē)
Civaṉ who wears a garland of blossomed koṉṟi which unfolds like gold, dances in Vīraṭṭāṇam
[[when Campantar visited this shrine Civaṉ granted him a vision of his dance; vide Periya Purāṇam, Tiru Ñāṉacampantar Purāṇam, verses 964-965]]
{1:46}__2+
{$} arumpum kurumpaiyum alaitta meṉkoṅkaik karumpiṉ moḻiyāḷōṭu uṭaṉ kai aṉal vīci
flinging the fire held in the hand, United with a lady whose words are as sweet as the juice of the sugar-cane and who has soft breasts that harassed the lotus-bud and the immature cocoanut.
curumpu uṇvirikoṉṟaic cuṭar poṟcaṭai tāḻa
the glittering golden caṭi which wears blossomed koṉṟi flowers from which the bees drink honey, to hang low.
virumpum atikaiyuḷ vīraṭṭāṉattē āṭum
Civaṉ dances in Atiki Vīraṭṭāṉam.

āṭappāṭal maṟai vallāṉ
Civaṉ is capable of singing Vētams when he dances.
paṭutampalipeyarvāṉ
wanders from place to place for alms performing a kind of dance by name paṭutam
[[pāṭal icaikoḷ karuvi paṭutam palavum payilvār (cuntarar, tirukkaṭavūr)]]
māṭamukaṭṭiṉmēl matitōy atikaiyuḷ vēṭam pala vallāṉ āṭum viraṭṭāṉattē
Civaṉ who is capable of manifesting himself in many forms, dances in Atiki Vīraṭṭāṉam where the moon seems to come into contact with the top of the storeys.
{1:46}__4+
{$} eṇṇāreyil eytāṉ iṟaivaṉ
the supreme god, Civaṉ who shot an arrow on the forts of enemies.
aṉal ēnti
holding the fire.
maṉār muḻavu atira
when the muḻavu on whose head the paste is smeared, sounds.
mutirāmaticūṭi
wearing a crescent
paṇ ār maṟai pāṭapparamaṉ atikaiyuḷ viṇṇōr parava niṉṟu āṭum vīraṭṭāṉattē
the supreme god dances in Atiki Vīraṭṭāṉam to be prised in the second person by the celestials and the Vētams which have melody-types, to sing his prises.
{1:46}__5+
{$} karipuṉpuṟam āya kaḻintār iṭukāṭṭil
in the burial-ground of deceased persons which is black and low and is situated outside the village.
tiruniṉṟu orukaiyāl
as the goddess of wealth stays permanent by leaving other places
[[orukai-oruvakai]]
tiruām atikaiyuḷ
in Atiki where wealth increases.
eri ēntiya perumāṉ eripuṉcaṭaitāḻa viriyum puṉalcūṭi āṭum vīraṭṭāṉattē
the god who held the fire in his hand dances in Vīraṭṭāṉam, wearing spreading water when the glittering ruddy caṭi hangs low.
{1:46}__6+
{$} tuḷaṅkum cuṭar aṅkaittutaiya viḷaiyāṭi
playing when the shaking fire was very close to the palm.
iḷaṅkompu aṉacāyal umaiyōṭu icaipāṭi
singing music with Umi who is as gentle as a tender bough.
vaḷamkoḷ puṉalcūḻnta vayal ār atikaiyuḷ viḷaṅkum piṟaicūṭi āṭum vīraṭṭāṉattē
Civaṉ dances wearing a shining crescent in Atiki Vīraṭṭāṉam which has fields surrounded by fertile water.
{1:46}__7+
{$} paramaṉ
the supreme god.
paramēṭṭi
who is in the most exalted place.
pūtam puṭaicūḻa
the pūtams to surround him on all sides.
pulittōl uṭai āka
dressing himself in a tiger's skin.
kītam umai pāṭa
Umi to sing musical compositions.
keṭila vaṭapakkam
on the northern side of the river Keṭilam.
vētamutalvaṉ
the origin of all vetams.
pātam palar ētta āṭum vīraṭṭāṉattē
dances in Vīraṭṭāṉam when many prise his feet.

{$} kal ārvarai
in the mountin which is full of boulders.
arakkaṉ taṭantōḷ kaviṉvāṭa
the beauty of the big shoulders of the arakkaṉ to perish.
ollai aṭarttu
pressing down quickly
avaṉukku aruḷceytu
granting grace later.
palār pakuvāya nakuveṇtalaicūṭi

villāl eyil eytāṉ atikaiyuḷ vīraṭṭāṉattē

nimirntāṉai

poṭiyāṭum mārpaṉai
who smears on his chest sacred ash.
purinūl uṭaiyāṉai
who wears a sacred thread of strands
kāṇkilār

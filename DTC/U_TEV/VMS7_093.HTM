vārum aruvi maṇipoṉ koḻittuc cērum naṟaiyūrc cittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr where the scooping streams reach sifting precious stones and gold, like a winnow.
nīrum malarum nilavum caṭaimēl, ūrum aravam uṭaiyāṉ iṭam ām
is the place of Civaṉ who has a crawling cobra on his caṭi, where water and flowers are shining.
{7:93}__2+
{$}
vaḷaikkai maṭavār maṭuvil taṭanīrt tiḷaikkum naṟaiyūrc cittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr where ladies who wear bangles on their hands bathe for a long time, in the deep pools which have extensive water.
aḷaippai aravu ēr iṭaiyāṉ añcattuḷaikkaik karittōl urittāṉ iṭam ām
is the place of Civaṉ who flayed the skin of an elephant having a trunk with holes, to make the lady with a waist resembling the cobra with a hood which lives in the anthill, fear,
[[Cobra is compared to the waist of ladies]]
[[PP: miṉṉum paṇiyum puraimaruṅkuṟ peruntōḷi
(Kōviyār, 5)]]
{7:93}__3+
{$}
(1) mukiḻmeṉ mulaiyār mukamē kamalam tikaḻum naṟaiyūrccittīccaramē
citticcaram in naṟiyūr where the lotus flowers are as brilliant as the faces of the young ladies whose breasts are resembling buds;
[[PP: tirumukam aviḻnta teyvattāmarai
(Ciṟupāṇāṟṟupaṭi, 73)]]
ikaḻum takaiyōr eyil mūṉṟu eritta pakaḻiyoṭu viluṭaiyāṉ patiām
is the place of Civaṉ who possesses the bow, along the arrow with which he burnt the three forts of the acurar who despised him.
[[Variant reading: (1) mukiṇmeṉ]]
{7:93}__4+
{$}
naṟakkoḷ kamalam naṉipaḷḷieḻat tiṟakkum naṟaiyūrc cittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr where the bees open the petals of the lotus which contins honey
[[The lotus having closed petals is described to be sleeping.]]
maṟakkoḷ arakkaṉ varaitōḷ varaiyāl iṟakkoḷ viralkōṉ irukkum iṭam ām
is the place where Civaṉ, the chief who pressed by his toe the mountin to make the shoulders which were as strong as mountin, of the arakkaṉ who was valourous before.
{7:93}__5+
{$}
kaḻunīrkamaḻak kayal cēl ukaḷum ceḻunīr naṟaiyūrccittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr which has fertile water in which the different kinds of fish, kayaḻ and cēl, frisk to make the purple indian water-lily to spread its fragrance.
muḻunīṟu aṇimēṉiyaṉ
Civaṉ who smears holy ash all over his body.
moy kuḻalār eḻunīrmaikoḷvāṉ amarum iṭam ām
is the place of Civaṉ who steals the noble qualities of the ladies with dense tresses of hir
[[The ladies referred to here are the wives of the sages in Tārukāvaṉam]]
{7:93}__6+
{$}
vāṉ ār matiyam pativaṇpoḻilvāyt, tēṉ ārnaṟaiyūrc cittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr where the flourishing gardens in which the moon shining in the sky seems to be fixed, have abundant honey.
ūṉ ār uṭaiveṇtalai uṇpalikoṇṭu āṉ ārāṭal ēṟu amarvāṉiṭam ām
is the place of Civaṉ who is seated on a strong bull which belongs to the bovine species, receiving alms for eating in the broken white skull which has flesh sticking to it.
{7:93}__7+
{$}
vār ūrmulaiyār maruvum maṟukil tērūr naṟaiyūrc cittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr where in the streets cars run and young girls who wear bodies on their breasts are moving frequently.
kār ūr kaṭalilviṭam uṇṭu aruḷcey, nīr ūrcaṭaiyaṉ nilavum iṭam ām
is the place of Civaṉ on whose caṭi water of the Kaṅki spreads, who drank the poison that rose in the black sea.
{7:93}__8+
{$}
puriyum maṟaiyōr niṟai coṟ poruḷkaḷ teriyum naṟaiyūrc cittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr where brahmins who perform their duties with love make research into the words and their meanings which are profuse.
kariyiṉ uriyum kalai māṉ maṟiyum, eriyum maḻuvum uṭaiyāṉ iṭam ām
is the place of Civaṉ who has the skin of an elephant, a young male deer and a burning red-hot iron.
{7:93}__9+
{$}
pāṇ ārkuḻalum muḻavum viḻavil cēṇ ār naṟaiyūrccittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr where in the festival the music of the flute playing many melody-types and the sound of the muḻavu can be heard at a great distance.
pēṇā (1) muṉivaṉ peruvēḷvi elām māṇāmai ceytāṉ maruvum iṭam ām
is the place of Civaṉ who destroyed completely the great sacrifice performed by (Takkaṉ), the sage who detested Civaṉ without liking him, to lose all its eminence.
[[Variant reading: (1) muṉivāṉ]]
{7:93}__10+
{$}
neṟiyil vaḻuvā niyamattavarkaḷ ceṟiyum naṟaiyūrc cittīccaramē
Cittīccaram in Naṟiyūr where great men of dily routines, do not swerve from the right, are crowded.
kuṟiyil vaḻuvākkoṭuṅkūṟṟu utaitta eṟiyum maḻuvāṭ paṭaiyāṉ iṭam ām
is the place of Civaṉ who has a battle-axe to be thrown on enemies and who kicked the cruel god of death who never missed his purpose.
{7:93}__11+
{$}
pōrār puram eypuṉitaṉ amarum cīrār naṟaiyūrccittīccarattai
on Cittīccaram of great fame where the pure Civaṉ who shot an arrow on the cities of acurar who were always engaged in war.
ārūraṉ col ivai vallavarkaḷ ērār imaiyōr ulakueytuvar
those who are capable of reciting these verses composed by Ārūraṉ will reach the beautiful world of the celestials who do not wink.

{7:88}__1+
{$} antaṇar nāṉmaṟaikku iṭam āya vēḷviyuḷ nampiṉārkku aruḷ ceyyum
Civaṉ who grants his grace to those who worship you with piety in the sacrifice performed by brahmins and which is mentioned in the four Vētam-s!
cempoṉnērmaṭavār aṇipeṟṟa tirumiḻalai
in tirumiḻali which is adorned by ladies who are as beautiful as the red tirumakal.
umparārtoḻutu ētta māmalaiyāḷoṭum(m) uṭaṉē uṟaiviṭam ampoṉ vīḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied the beautiful shade of vīḻi as your abode along with the dughter of the big mountin, to be worshipped with joined hands and prised by the tevars in heaven.
aṭiyēṟkum aruḷutir
you grant your grace to me also, your slave.
{7:88}__2+
{$} viṭamkoḷ māmiṭaṟṟīr
one who has a black neck which swallowed the poison?
veḷḷaiccuruḷ oṉṟu iṭṭuviṭṭakātiṉīr eṉṟu
prising, you as one who has on one ear mens' ear-ring made of conch, hanging.
tiṭamkoḷ cintaiyiṉār kalikākkum tirumiḻalai
in tirumiḻi where brahmins who have a firm mind protect the world from poverty,
maṭaṅkalpūṇṭavimāṉam maṇmicaivantu iḻicciya vāṉanāṭṭaiyum aṭaṅkal vīḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied the shade of the vīḻi plant which contined within it the heaven which cused to decend on this early a vīmāṉam which was supported by lions.
[[aṭaṅkal - aṭakkal]]
aṭiyēṟkum aruḷutir
you grant your grace to me also, your slave.
{7:88}__3+
{$} ūṉaiuṟṟauyir āyiṉīr
one who is the life in the body!
oḷimūṉṟumāy
being the three lights (sun, moon and the fire)
teḷinīrōṭu āṉ añciṉtēṉai āṭṭu ukantīr
you desired being bathed in clear water, five products of the cow and honey between them
ceḻumāṭattirumiḻalai
in tirumiḻali which has wealthy mansions.
māṉaimēviya kaiyiṉīr
one who has a deer in the hand!
maḻu ēntiṉīr
one who holds a battle-axe!
maṅkaipākattīr
one who has on one half a lady
viṇṇil āṉa viḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied the shade of vīḻi which grew tall and rose into the sky.
{7:88}__4+
{$} pantam vīṭu ivai paṇṇiṉīr
you created bondage and liberation for the souls.
paṭiṟīr
though you created them you conceal yourself from them; therefore you are deceitful.
matippitirkkaṇṇiyīr
you wear a chaplet of a crescent
eṉṟu
prising you in this manner.
cintai ceytirukkum ceṅkaiyāḷar tirumiḻalai
in tirumiḻali where people with good conduct meditate on you always.
nāḷtoṟum vantu nāṭakam vāṉa nāṭiyar āṭa
the divine damsels descend down and perform dances, dily.
māl ayaṉ ētta
Māl and Ayaṉ to prise you.
amtaṇ vīḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied the cool shade of the beautiful vīḻi.
aṭiyēṟkum aruḷutir
see 1st verse.
{7:88}__5+
{$} puricai mūṉṟaiyum poṉṟai
to destroy completely the three forts.
kuṉṟavil ēnti
holding the bow of a mountin.
vētappuravittērmicaittiricey
you changed them into ash, standing on a chariot drawn by horses which were vētams.
nāṉmaṟaiyōr ciṟantu ēttum tirumiḻalai
in tirumiḻali where brahmins who have learnt the four vetams prise you in an extra-ordinary manner.
pariciṉāl aṭipōṟṟum pattarkaḷ pāṭi āṭapparintu nalkiṉīr
you granted with love all the wishes of those pious people who prised your feet with love and sang and danced out of joy.
ariya vīḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied the shade of the rare vīḻi.
aṭiyēṟkum aruḷutir
see 1st verse.
{7:88}__6+
{$} eṟinta caṇṭi iṭanta kaṇṇappaṉ (1) ēttupattarkaṭku ēṟṟam nalkiṉīr
you granted pre-eminence to pious persons like caṇṭi who cut off the leg of his father and Kaṇṇappaṉ who scooped his eye.
ceṟinta pūmpoḻil tēṉtuḷivīcum tirumiḻalai
in tirumiḻali where the dense flower gardens spray drops of honey.
niṟainta antaṇar nittam nāḷtoṟum nēcattāl umaippūcikkum iṭam aṟintu
knowing the place where the brahmins full of knowledge dily worship you permanently, with love.
vīḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied the shade of vīḻi.
aṭiyēṟkum aruḷutir
grant you grace to me also who is your slave.
[[Variant reading: (1) ēṟṟa]]
{7:88}__7+
{$} paṇinta pārttaṉ pakīrataṉ pala pattar cittarkkup paṇṭu nalkiṉīr
you granted your grace to many pious people, persons with miraculous powers, beginning with pārttaṉ and pakīrataṉ, in the distant past.
tiṇinta māṭam toṟum celvammalku tirumiḻalai
in tirumiḻali where wealth increases in every mansion which is being close to each other.
taṇinta antaṇar nāṭoṟum canti antivāṉ iṭum pūcciṟappu avai aṇintu
having adorned the superior decoration with flowers during morning, midday and evening dily by brahmins who have tranquility of mind.
vīḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied the shade of vīḻi.
aṭiyēṟkum aruḷutir
you grant your grace to me also, your slave.
{7:88}__8+
{$} ūriṭaippalipaṟṟi
receiving alms in the villages
paranta pāriṭam pāttu uṇum cuṟṟam āyiṉīr
you have the relation of pūtam with which you share it.
terinta nāṉmaṟaiyōrkku iṭam āya tirumiḻalai iruntu
residing in tirumiḻi, which is the place for brahmins who have made researches into the four vētam-s.
nīrtamiḻōṭu icai kēṭkum iccaiyāl kācu nittal nalkiṉīr
you dily gave money in the form of coins wishing to hear tamiḻ verses and music.
[[This refers to the story of Civaṉ giving money to campantar and nāvukkaracar during a famine in this shrine.]]
aruntaṇvīḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied the shade of cool and rare vīḻi.
{7:88}__9+
{$} tūyanīr amutu āyavāṟu atucolluka eṉṟu (1) umai kēṭka
when Umi questioned you please tell me how pure water became like nectar to you.
colliṉīr
you replied to her.
tī (2) arukkulaiyāḷar ceḻumāṭattirumiḻalai
in tirumiḻali which has flourishing storeys belonging to brahmins of big crowds who mintin the sacrificial fires without extinguishing.
mēya nīrpali (3) ēṟṟatu eṉ eṉṟu viṇṇappam ceypavarkku meypporuḷ āyavīḻikoṇṭīr
you occupied vīḻi which is the true object for those who respectfully represented to you why did you who is loved by all receive alms.
aṭiyēṟkum aruḷutir
you grant your grace to me also who is your slave.
[[Variant reading: (1) umaikkēṭka (2) arākkolaiyāḷar (3) ēṟṟaṟku]]
{7:88}__10+
{$} vētavētiyar vētanītiyatu ōtuvārvirinīr miḻalaiyuḷ āti vīḻikoṇṭīr aṭiyēṟkum aruḷuka eṉṟu
petitioning to Civaṉ in miḻali of expansive water where brahmins who have studied the vētams and observe the conduct lid down in them, you occupied the shade of the old vīḻi grant your grace to me also who is your slave.
nātakītam vaṇṭu ōtuvārpoḻil nāvalūraṉ vaṉtoṇṭaṉ naṟṟamiḻ pātam ōtavallār paraṉōṭu kūṭuvarē
those who can sing the faultless tamiḻ verses at the feet of Civaṉ, which were composed by vaṉṟoṇṭaṉ, and a native of nāvalūr which has tall gardens in which bees hum sweet songs, will be united with the supreme Civaṉ.

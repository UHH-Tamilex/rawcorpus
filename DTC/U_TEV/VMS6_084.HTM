(1) ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭiyataṉuḷ
In Ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭi
nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
I obtined a vision of
peruntakaiyai
the noble-minded god
peṟaṟku ariya māṇikkattai
the ruby which is rare to get.
pēṇi niṉaintu eḻuvār tam maṉattē maṉṉi irunta maṇi viḷakkataṉai
the lamp of precious stone which took his abode permanently in the minds of those who wake up from sleep remembering him with love.
niṉṟa pū mēl eḻuntaruḷi iruntāṉai
who was seated majestically in the flower of the heart to be seen outside.
eṇ tōḷ vīci
swinging the eight shoulders
aruntiṟal mā naṭam āṭum ammāṉ taṉṉai
the god who performs the great dance of rare victory;
[[eṇ ṭōḷ vīci niṉṟu āṭum pirāṉtaṉai
(Nāvukkaracar, Tiruvaṅkamāli, 2: [4:009])]]
am kaṉakac cuṭark kuṉṟai
who resembles the hill of brilliant and beautiful gold.
aṉṟu āliṉ kīḻt tiruntu maṟaipporuḷ nālvarkku aruḷ ceytāṉai
who gave the meaning of the perfect Vētam in the distant past to four sages, sitting under the shade of the banyan tree.
[[Variant reading: (1) ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭiyataṉil; this reading is ((also)) applicable for the rest of the verses in this decade.]]

ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭiyataṉuḷ nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
I obtined a vision of in Cenkāṭṭaṅkuṭi.
tuṅka nakattāl aṉṟit tolaiyā veṉṟit tokutiṟal al iraṇiyaṉai ākam kīṇṭa am kaṉakattirumālum ayaṉum tēṭum āraḻalai
the column of fire of unbearable heat which (could not be found by) Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ) and (by) Tirumāl who had the colour of gold and who rent away the chest of that famous Iraṇiyaṉ who had victories by his accumulated strength and who could not killed by any other weapon except by the exalted nils.
aṉaṅkaḷ uṭal poṭiyāy vīḻntu maṅka nakattāṉ valla maruntu taṉṉai
the nectar which can open its frontal eye to destroy Aṉaṅkaṉ to make his body to fall down as ashes.
vaṇ kayilai māmalaimēl maṉṉi niṉṟa ceṅkaṉakattiraḷ tōḷ em celvaṉ taṉṉai
our god who has round shoulders like pure gold and who stayed permanently in the fruitful and big mountin of Kayili.

ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭiyataṉuḷ nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
in Ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭi I obtined a vision of
uruku maṉattu aṭiyavarkaṭku ūṟum tēṉai
the honey that springs in the minds of the devotees whose hearts melt.
umpar maṇi muṭikku aṇiyai
the ornament for the crowns of Tēvar in which precious stones are set.
uṇmai niṉṟa peruku nilaikkuṟiyāḷar aṟivu taṉṉai
the intellect of those who have the aim and who thrive by being steady in the realisation of the real thing.
pēṇiya antaṇarkku maṟaipporuḷai
the meaning of the Vētam revealed to brahmins who cherish him with love.
piṉṉum muruku viri naṟumalar mēl ayaṟkum māṟkum muḻumutalai
in addition to that, the sole cuse for Māl, and Ayaṉ who is seated on a good flower whose fragrance spread everywhere;
[[naṟumai poṉṉiṟku iṉṟu eṉiṉum teriyaṟku aṭaiyāy niṉṟatu; naṉmaiyum ām (Puṟam, 29-3, old commentary)]]
meyttavattōr tuṇaiyai
the companion of those who perform sincere penance.
vāytta tiruku kuḻal umai naṅkai paṅkaṉ taṉṉai
and Civaṉ who has as a half a lady of distinction, Umi, who has brided tresses of hir and who is quite appropriate to him.
[[The names of the goddess in this shrine is now known as Tirukkuḻal Mātu; I think it is a corruption of Tirukukuḻal.]]

ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭiyataṉuḷ nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
in Ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭi, I obtined a vision of
kantamalark koṉṟai aṇi caṭaiyāṉ taṉṉai
Civaṉ who adorns his caṭi with fragrant flowers of koṉṟi.
katir viṭu mā maṇi piṟaṅku kaṉakac cōtic cantamalart terivai oru pākattāṉai
who has as his half a lady who wears colourful flowers and who has the lustre of the gold with the brilliant lustre of the big emerald which emits rays,
carācara nal tāyāṉai
the mother who gave birth to movable and inmovable things.
nāyēṉ muṉṉaip pantam aṟuttu āḷ ākkip paṇikoṇṭu
accepting my devoted services having made me his slave after cutting at the roots of my Karmams done in previous births of mine who is as vile on a dog
āṅkē paṉṉiya nūl tamiḻ mālai pāṭuvittu
making me to compose garlands of verse in strict accordance with grammer, then itself, which are useful for research.
eṉ cintaimayakku aṟutta tiruvaruḷiṉāṉai
and who by his benign grace removed completely the confusion in my mind.

ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭi ataṉuḷ nāṉkaṇṭēṉ
in ceṅkaṭṭaṅkuti I obtined a vision of
nañcu aṭainta kaṇṭattu nātaṉ taṉṉai
the master whose neck the poison reached as its refuge.
naḷir malarp pūṅkaṇai vēḷai nācamāka veñciṉattī viḻittatu oru nayaṉttāṉai
who has a frontal eye which he opened to issue fire of cruel anger to destroy maṉmataṉ who has as his arrows cool and beautiful flowers.
viyaṉ keṭila vīraṭṭam mēviṉāṉai
who dwells with desire in the expansive tiruvatiki vīraṭṭam on the bank of the river, Ketilam.
mañcu aṭutta nīḷ cōlai māṭavīti matil ārūr iṭam koṇṭa maintaṉ taṉṉai
the youth who took as his abode ārūr having fortified walls and streets having storeys, and long gardens on which clouds stay.
ceñciṉatta tiricūlappaṭaiyāṉ taṉṉai
and the god who has a trident of three blades which he wieldss with righteous indignation.

ceṅkāṭṭaṅ kuṭiyataṉuḷ nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
in Ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuti, I obtined a vision of
kaṉṉiyai aṅku oru caṭaiyil karantāṉ taṉṉai
Civaṉ who concealed, Kaṅki, the lady in one portion of his caṭi.
kaṭavūril vīraṭṭam karutiṉāṉai
who considered Kaṭavūr Vīraṭṭam as his abode.
poṉṉi cūḻ aiyāṟṟu em (1) puṉitaṉ taṉṉai
the holy god who dwells in Aiyāṟu surrounded by the river, Poṉṉi.
pūnturutti neyttāṉam poruntiṉāṉai
who dwells in Pūnturutti and Neyttāṉam
paṉṉiya nāṉ maṟai virikkum paṇpaṉ taṉṉai
who has the nature of expounding the four Vētam-s fit for research.
parintu imaiyōr toḻutu ētti
the celestials who do not wink worshipping with joined hands and prising with love.
paraṉē eṉṟu ceṉṉi mikaik koṇṭu aṇi (2) cēvaṭiyiṉāṉai
and the god whose red-lotus feet they adorn on their heads saying, 'supreme god'.
[[Variant reading: (1) purāṇaṉ (2) cēvaṭiyāṉ taṉṉai]]

ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭiyataṉuḷ nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
in Ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭi, I obtined a vision of
ettikkum āy niṉṟa iṟaivaṉ taṉṉai
the supreme god who pervaded everywhere.
ēkampam mēyāṉai
who dwells in Ēkampam with desire
illāta teyvam potti
fabricating a god which has no divinity in it.
tam mayir paṟikkum camaṇar poyyil pukku aḻunti vīḻāmē pōta vāṅki
rescuing me well without sinking low in the false doctrines which are like deep pits, of the camaṇar who pull out the hirs on their heads
[[Pulling out the hirs on the head with one's hands is called ulōccu by camaṇar]]
pattikkē vaḻikāṭṭi
and showing the path to devotion to me.
pāvam tīrttu
removing my sins.
paṇṭai viṉaip payam āṉa ellām pōkki
ridding me of all the fruits of Karmams committed in previous births,
eṉ maṉattuḷḷē tittittu ūṟum tēṉai
and the honey which springs sweetly in my mind.

ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭiyataṉuḷ nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
in ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭi I obtined a vision of
kallātār maṉattu aṉukākkaṭavuḷ taṉṉai
the god who does not approach the minds of people who do not study works which expound philosophy.
kaṟṟārkaḷ uṟṟu ōrum kātalāṉai
the object of love which is considered attentively well by those who have studied philosophical works.
pollāta neṟi ukantār puraṅkaḷ mūṉṟum poṉṟi viḻa aṉṟu porucaram toṭṭāṉai
who shot an arrow which could give fight, in the distant past to make all the three places of the acurar who desired with joy the evil ways, to fall down completely destroyed.
nillāta niṇakkukarampaip piṇakkam nīṅka niṟai tavattai aṭiyēṟku niṟai vimattu
cused the immense penance to filled up for me his devotees in order to remove the contradications in the body of flesh which is transcient.
eṉṟum cellāta cenneṟikkē celvippāṉai
and who cuses me to tread the right path in which I did not tread before.

ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuṭiyataṉuḷ nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
in ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuti I obtined a vision of
ariya perum poruḷ āki niṉṟāṉ taṉṉai
Civaṉ who is a great wealth difficult to get.
alai kaṭalil ālālam amutu ceyta kariyatu oru kaṇṭattuc ceṅkaṇ ēṟṟuk katir viṭu māmaṇi piṟaṅku kāṭciyāṉai
who has in appearance of a glittering precious stone emitting rays, who has a bull of red eyes, and a black neck which drank the poison which rose in the ocean of wandering waves.
uriya pala toḻil ceyyum aṭiyār taṅkaṭku ulakam ellām muḻutu aḷikkum ulappilāṉai
who does not know death, who grants tillthe end of a Kaṟpam all the worlds to his servants who perform services according to their status.
terivai oru pākattuc cērttiṉāṉai
and who was united with a lady on one half.

ceṅkāṭaṭṭāṅkuṭiyataṉil nāṉ kaṇṭēṉ
in ceṅkāṭṭaṅkuti I obtined a vision of.
pōr aravam mālviṭai oṉṟu ūrtiyāṉai
Civaṉ rides on a big bull which bellows when fighting its fores.
[[oṉṟu ūrti-ōr ūrti: oṉṟu tēriṉāl oruvuṉu/ kūṟṟamē eṉṟu kūṟiṉum oruvaṉ eṉ ceyum (cīvakacintāmaṇi, 417)]]
puṟam payamum pukalūrum maṉṉiṉāṉai
who stayed permanently in puṟampayam and pukalūr.
nīr aravac ceñcaṭaimēl nilāveṇ tiṅkaḷ nīṅkāmai vaittāṉai, nimalaṉ taṉṉai
who placed on his cati which has water and a cobra, a white crescent which gives moonlight, who is the immaculate god.
pēr aravap puṭpakattēr uṭaiya veṉṟip piṟaṅku oḷi vāḷ arakkaṉ muṭi iṭiyac ceṟṟa cīr aravak kaḻalāṉai
who has feet in which he wears beautiful jingling Kaḻal, with which he destroyed to crumble into powder the heads of arakkaṉ who had a glittering sword, and victorious in possessing the vehicle of puṭpakam which makes a loud sound
[[NOTE_MYTHO: puṭpakam originally belonged to Kupēraṉ, Irāvaṇaṉ took it from him as a spoil of war]]
[[PP_X: vaṇṭu cēr poḻaḻiṟ kupēra maṉṉaṉ vāḻumā nakark, kaṇṭa kaṇṭamāṭu elām kavarntu koṇṭu paṇṭai nāḷ, puṇṭarīkavākaṉaṉ koṭutta puṭapakattaiyum, koṇṭu veṉṟi koṇṭu arakkar kōṉ uvantu pōyiṉāṉ
(rāmāyaṇam, uttarakāṇṭam, variyeṭutta patalam, 62)]]
celvaṉ taṉṉai
and who is our Lord.

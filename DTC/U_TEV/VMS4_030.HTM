{4:30}__1+
{$} kaḻippālaic cērppaṉār
the chief of the maritime trace of Kaḻippāli.
naṅkaiyaip pākam vaittār
He kept an eminent lady as his half.
ñāṉattai navila vaittār
He has spiritual wisdom which is to be repeated very often by the tongue.
aṅkaiyil aṉalum vaittār
He placed on his palm fire (aṅkai=am kai beautiful palm, for the first meaning it should be split as akam+kai)
āṉaiyiṉ urivai vaittār
He has an elephants skin (āṉai-yāṉai the consonant sound has been dropped)
tamakkaiyiṉ yāḻum vaittār/
He has on his hand a yāḻ.
tāmarai malarum vaittār
He has a lotus flower also in his hand.
kaṅkaiyaic caṭaiyuḷ vaittār
He kept Kaṅki inside his matted locks of hir.

viṇṇiṉai virumpa vaittār
He made some people to desire heaven (viṇ liberation from the cycle of birth and death; eternal bliss)
vēḷviyai vēṭka vaittār
He has prescribed sacrifice to be performed.
paṇṇiṉaip pāṭavaittār

He has devotees who are trined to worship his fest.

He bestowed grace on māḻ who grew tall to measure the world (grace-the strength to measure the three worlds)
kaṇṇiṉai neṟṟi vaittār
He placed one eye on his forehead.

kūriruḷ kiḻiya niṉṟa koṭu maḻukkaiyil vaittār
He kept in his hand a cruel maḻu which by its brightness rends the dense darkness.
pēriruḷ kiḻiya malku piṟai puṉaṟ caṭaiyil vaittār

He is the author of the six different religions (aṟuvakaiccamayam: Appar, potu.1-3; tirumaṟikkātu, 6; tiruvoṟṟiyur 5; tiruviṉṉampar, 4,7; taṉitirukkuṟuntoki, 6; tiruvīḻimiḻali, 7; tirukkacci ākampam, 7; tirumutuKuṉṟam, 5)

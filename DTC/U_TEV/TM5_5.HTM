*1
paṭṭi_ēṟu _ukantu _ēṟi, pala_ilam
_iṭṭam_āka _irantu _uṇṭu,_uḻitarum
_aṭṭamūrtti _aṇṇāmali kitoḻak
keṭṭup pōm, viṉi; kēṭu _illi; kāṇmiṉē!

*2
peṟṟam _ēṟuvar, pey palikku _ēṉṟu _avar;
cuṟṟamā miku tolpukaḻāḷoṭum
_aṟṟam tīrkkum _aṇṇāmali kitoḻa
nal-tavattoṭu ñāṉattu _irupparē.

*3
pal_il_ōṭu ki _ēntip pala_ilam
_olli ceṉṟu _uṇaṅkal kavarvār_avar,
_allal tīrkkum, _aṇṇāmali kitoḻa
nalla_āyiṉa nammi _aṭiyumē.

*4
pāṭic ceṉṟu palikku _eṉṟu niṉṟavar
_ōṭip pōyiṉar; ceyvatu_oṉṟu _eṉkolō?
_āṭip pāṭi _aṇṇāmali kitoḻa
_ōṭip pōkum, nam mēliviṉikaḷē.

*5
tēṭic ceṉṟu tiruntu _aṭi _ēttumiṉ!
nāṭi vantu _avar nammiyum _āṭkoḷvar;
_āṭip pāṭi _aṇṇāmali kitoḻa
_ōṭip pōm, namatu _uḷḷa viṉikaḷē.

*6
kaṭṭi _okkum, karumpiṉ_iṭi; tuṇi
veṭṭi vīṇikaḷ pāṭum vikirtaṉār,
_aṭṭamūrtti, _aṇṇāmali mēviya
naṭṭam_āṭiyi, naṇṇa naṉku _ākumē.

*7
kōṇikkoṇṭiyar vēṭam muṉ koṇṭavar,
pāṇi naṭṭaṅkaḷ_āṭum paramaṉār,
_āṇippoṉṉiṉ, _aṇṇāmali kitoḻap
pēṇi niṉṟa peruviṉi pōkumē.

*8
kaṇṭamtāṉ kaṟuttāṉ, kālaṉ _ār_uyir
paṇṭu kālkoṭu pāynta paramaṉār,
_aṇṭattu _ōṅkum _aṇṇāmali kitoḻa
viṇṭu pōkum, nam mēliviṉikaḷē.

*9
muntic ceṉṟu muppōtum vaṇaṅkumiṉ,
_antivāy _oḷiyāṉtaṉ _aṇṇāmali!
cintiyā _eḻuvār viṉi tīrttiṭum,
kantamāmalar cūṭum karuttaṉē.

*10
maṟiyiṉāṉoṭu mālavaṉ kāṇkilā
niṟiyam nīrmiyuḷ niṉṟu _aruḷceytavaṉ
_uṟiyum māṇpiṉ _aṇṇāmali kitoḻap
paṟiyum, nām ceyta pāvaṅkaḷ_āṉavē.

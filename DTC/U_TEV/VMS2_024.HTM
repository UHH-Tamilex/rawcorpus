{2:24}__1+
{$} poṉ ēr tarum mēṉiyaṉē
Civaṉ who has a brilliant body like the gold!
puriyum miṉ ēr caṭaiyāy
who has a twisted caṭi like the lightning!
(1) virai kāviriyiṉ naṉṉīr vayal nākēccara nakariṉ maṉṉē
and who is the Lord in the city of nakēccaram which has fields fed by the fertile water of the swift-flowing Kāviri.
eṉa
to utter the above-mentioned names.
valviṉai māyntu aṟum
the irresistible Karmams will vanish and completely leave us.
[[Variant reading: (1) viri]]
{2:24}__2+
{$} ciṟavār puram mūṉṟu eriyac cilaiyil uṟa vārkaṇai uyttavaṉē
Civaṉ who discharged a long arrow having fixed it in the bow to burn the three cities of the enemies who had no merit.
uyarum naṟavu ār poḻil nākēccaranakaruḷ aṟavā eṉa
to prise as the god in the city of nākēccaram which has tall gardens in which there is plenty of honey.
valviṉai ācaṟum
the irresistible karmams will quickly leave us.
{2:24}__3+
{$} kallāl niḻal mēyavaṉē
Civaṉ who is seated under the shade of the banyan tree known as Kallāl!
[[Kallāl - the banyan tree without aerial roots.]]
karumpiṉ villāṉ eḻil vēva viḻittavaṉē
one who opened the frontal eye to burn the beauty of Maṉmataṉ (Cupid) who has sugar-cane as his bow!
nallār toḻu nākēccaranakaril celvā eṉa
to prise him as the god in the city of nākēccaram which is worshipped by good people!
valviṉai tēyntu aṟum
irresistible Karmams will gradually decrease and disappear.
{2:24}__4+
{$} naku (1) vāḷ matiyōṭu aravum puṉalum takuvār caṭaiyiṉ muṭiyāy
Civaṉ who has in his fitting long caṭi coiled into a crown, water, a cobra, and a shining bright crescent!
taḷavam nakuvār poḻil nākēccara nakaruḷ pakavā eṉa
to prise as the one who has the six attributes and dwells in the city of nākēccaram which has long gardens in which red arabian jasmine blossoms.
valviṉai paṟṟaṟumē
the grip of the irresistible Karmams will be completely destroyed.
[[Variant reading: (1) vāṉmati]]
{2:24}__5+
{$} kalaimāṉ maṟiyum kaṉalum maḻuvum nilaiyākiya kaiyiṉaṉē
Civaṉ in whose hands the young stag, fire and battle are held permanently!
nikaḻum nalam ākiya nākēccara nakaruḷ talaivā eṉa
to prise him as the master in the city of nākēccaram where auspicious actions are taking place.
valviṉai (tāṉ) aṟum
the irresistible Karmams will be cut at their root.
{2:24}__6+
{$} kurai ār kaḻal āṭa
the warriors anklet which can make a sound when moving.
varaiyāṉ makaḷ kāṇa naṭam kulavi makiḻntavaṉē
one who rejoiced in bending his leg in dancing to be witnessed by the dughter of the mountin
narai ār viṭai ēṟum nākēccarattu em araicē eṉa
to prise him as the King in nākēccaram riding on a spotless white bull.
aruntuyar nīṅkum
great sufferings which will not leave us otherwise will leave us.
{2:24}__7+
{$} muṭai ārtaru veṇtalai koṇṭu ulakil kaṭai ār pali koṇṭu uḻal kāraṇaṉē
the origin of all things who wanders holding a white skull which gives out an offensive odour, and receiving alms in the entrances of houses in the world.
naṭai ārtaru nākēccara nakaruḷ caṭaiyā eṉa
to prise him as the god having a caṭi in the city of nākēccaram where people conduct themselves in the right path.
valviṉai (tāṉ) aṟumē
irresistible karmam-s will leave us.
{2:24}__8+
{$} ōyāta arakkaṉ oṭintu alaṟa
the arakkaṉ who did not decrease in his cruelty, to roar, his body having been broken.
nī āraruḷ ceytu nikaḻntavaṉē
you become prominent by bestowing upon him abundant grace
vāy āra vaḻuttuvar nākēccarattu āyē eṉa valviṉai (tāṉ) aṟum
the irresistible Karmams of people who prise him to the complete satisfaction of their tongue as the mother in nākeccaram, will leave them.
{2:24}__9+
{$} neṭiyāṉoṭu nāṉ mukaṉ nēṭal uṟac cuṭumāl eriyāy nimir cōtiyaṉē
the effulgent light which rose as the great burning fire when Piramaṉ searched for you along with tall Māl!
naṭu māvayal nākēccara nakarē iṭamā uṟaivāy eṉa iṉpu uṟum
the pleasure of this world and the next world will reach us of their own accord if we prise you who dwells in the city of nākēccaram which has big fields in which seedlings are transplanted.
{2:24}__10+
{$} malam pāviya kaiyoṭu maṇṭaiyatu uṇ kalam pāviyar kaṭṭurai viṭṭu
leaving the fabrications of sinful persons who eat from an earthern vessel using it as a plate, with their hands which is full of dirt.
ulakil nalam pāviya nākēccura nakaruḷ cilampā eṉat tīviṉai tēyntu aṟumē
evil Karmams will diminish and disappear completely if we prise, one who is seated in the mouth Kayili and who is in the city of nākēccaram where many good things spread out.
{2:24}__11+
{$} kalam ār kaṭal cūḻtaru kāḻiyar kōṉ
the chief among the resisdents of Kāḻi which is surrounded by the sea in which ships sil.
talam ārtaru centamiḻiṉ virakaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ clever in chaste tamiḻ which spreads throughout tamiḻ nāṭu.
nalam ārtaru nākēccarattāṉaic (1) colal mālaikaḷ colla ni(l)lā viṉaiyē
Karmams will not remin with us if we recite the garlands of words which were composed (by him) in prise of the god in Nākēccaram where all good things are avilable in plenty.
[[Variant reading: (1) cola]]

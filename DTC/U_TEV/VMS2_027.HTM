{2:27}__1+
{$} kulavu pāriṭam pōṟṟa vīṟṟiruntu
sitting majestically to be praised by the rejoicing pūtam-s.
ilaku (1) māmaḻu ēntum aṅkaiyaṉ
holding in his palms a big shining battle-axe.
nilavum intira nīlapparppatattu ulaviṉāṉ aṭi uḷka nalkumē
to meditate on the feet of Civaṉ who moved about in intiranilapparuppatam which is eternal, that meditation will bestow all good things.
[[Variant reading: (1) māṉ maḻu ]]
{2:27}__2+
{$}
(People of this world!)
kuṟaivu il ār mati cūṭi
wearing a crescent that neither waxes wanes.
āṭal vaṇṭu aṟaiyum māmalark koṉṟai ceṉṉi cēr iṟaivaṉ
the Lord who pervades everywhere and who has on his head big koṉṟai flowers on which the bees which hover them hum loudly.
intira nīlap parppatattu uṟaiviṉāṉtaṉai ōti uymmiṉē
save yourselves by praising Civaṉ who dwells in intirapīlapparppatam.
{2:27}__3+
{$} eṉpoṉ eṉmaṇi eṉṉa ēttuvār (1) nampaṉ
Civaṉ is the object of desire for those who praise him, my gold! my gem.
nāṉmaṟai pāṭum nāviṉāṉ
sings the four vetams with his tongue.
iṉpaṉ intira nīlapparppatattu (2) aṉpaṉ pātamē aṭaintu vāḻmiṉē
(People of this world!) live happily approaching the feet themselves of the friend in intiranīlapparppatam, who is the cause of all happiness.
[[Variant reading: (1) naṇpaṉ nāṉ maṟai (2) aṉpaṉ pātam]]
{2:27}__4+
{$} tēcam ār pukaḻ āya cemmai em īcaṉ intira nīlapparppatam kūcivāḻttutum kuṇam (atu) ākavē
we shall praise intiranīlapparppatam of our Civaṉ who is impartial and whose fame has filled the country with shyness to be of good character.
viṉai nācam ām, naṉmai tāṉ urum
our acts will perish, good things will come to us of their accord.
{2:27}__5+
{$} maruvu māṉ (1) maṭamātu ōr pākamāyp paravuvār viṉai tīrtta (2) paṇpiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who has the nature of removing the acts of those who praise him, having as one half a young lady who is united with him.
iravaṉ
one who begs alms.
intira nīlapparppatattu aruvi cūṭiṭum aṭikaḷ vaṇṇamē
that is the nature of the god who wears the mountain streams in intiranīlapparppatam
[[variant readings : (1) mātor paṅkaṉār: mātor paṅkamāyp (2) paṇpiṉār]]
{2:27}__6+
{$} veṇṇilā maticūṭum vēṇiyaṉ
Civaṉ wears on his catai a white crescent that gives light.
eṇṇilār matil eyta villiṉāṉ
discharged an arrow from the bow on the forts of the enemies.
aṇṇal intira nīlapparppatattu uḷ nilāvuṟum oruvaṉ allaṉē
is he not the god who has no equal and who remain permanently in the eminent intiranīlapparppatam?
{2:27}__7+
{$} (1) koṭi koḷ ēṟṟiṉar
Civaṉ has a flag on which the form of a bull is drawn.
kūṟṟu utaittavar
Kicked the god of death.
poṭi koḷ mēṉiyil pūṇṭa pāmpiṉar
adorns his body on which the sacred ash is smeared, with cobra
aṭikaḷ intira nīlapparppatam (2) uṭaiya vāṇaṉ ukanta koḷkaiyē
these are the great notions the god, who always stays in intiranīlaparppatam.
[[Variant reading: (1) koṭiya vēṟṟiṉar (2) uṭaiya vāṇar]]
{2:27}__8+
{$} eṭutta val arakkaṉ karam puyam aṭarttatu ōr viralāṉ avaṉai āṭpaṭuttaṉ
Civaṉ pressed down with a single toe the hands and shoulders of the strong arakkaṉ who lifted the mountain, Kayilai and then accepted him on his protege.
intira nīlapparppatam muṭittalam(m) uṟa muyalum iṉpamē
(People of this world!) try to worship his intiranīlapparppatam by bowing your heads; you will obtain happiness.
{2:27}__9+
{$} pūviṉaiṉoṭu mālum pōṟṟuṟum tēvaṉ intira nīlapaparppatam 1. pāviyātu eḻuvārait tamviṉai 2. kōviyā varum collum kūṟṟamē
the god of death will kill those who rise without meditating on intiranīlapparppatam of the god who is praised by Piramaṉ in the lotus flower and Māl; their acts will reach them getting angry.
[[Variant reading: 1. pāviyā eḻuvārait 2. kōviya toḻuṅ kolluy kūṟṟamē]]
{2:27}__10+
{$} kaṭṭar kuṇṭu amaṇ tēvar cīrilar viṭṭar intiranīlapparppatam eḷtaṉai niṉaiyātatu eṉ kolō ciṭṭatāy uṟai āti cīrkaḷē
what is the reason for the unfortunate low amanar, tēvar who have no eminence and who have been given up as lost for not thinking about the fame of the first cause who dwells in intiranīlapparppatam being the embodiment of good knowledge?
{2:27}__11+
{$} kantam ār poḻil cūḻnta kāḻiyāṉ ñāṉacampantaṉ intiraṉ toḻum nīlapparppatattu anta milliyai ēttu pāṭal koṇṭu ōtivāḻmiṉē
live happily chanting the verses which praise Civaṉ and who dwells in nīlapparppatam worshipped by intiraṉ and who dwells in nīlapparppatam worshipped by intiraṉ and who has no end composed by ñaṉacampantaṉ, who is a native of Kāḻi surrounded by gardens of fragrance.

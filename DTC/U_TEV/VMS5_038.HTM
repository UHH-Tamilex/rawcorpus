kaṭavūriṉ mayāṉattār perumāṉaṭikaḷ
the divine being who dwells in Kaṭavūr Mayāṉam.
kuḻaikoḷ kātiṉar
wears a men's ear-ring in the right ear.
kōvaṇa āṭaiyar
dresses in a loin-cloth
uḻaiyar
holds a deer in his hand (is near at hand to devotees)
paḻaiya tam aṭiyār ceyta pāvamum piḻaiyum tīrppar
will remove the sins and wrongs committed by his hereditary devotees.
{5:38}__2+
{$}
kaṭavūriṉ mayāṉattār
see 1st verse.
uṉṉi vāṉavar ōtiya cintaiyil kaṉṉal tēṉ
when the celestials utter his names, he is as sweet as the juice of the sugar cane and honey, in their minds.
taṉṉai nōkkittoḻutu eḻuvārkku elām piṉṉai eṉṉār
he will not say `we shall grant our grace sometime later' but grants it at once to those who think of him and worship him with joined hands.

kaṭavūr mayāṉattār perumāṉaṭikaḷ
see 1st verse
cūlam ēntuvar
holds a trident
tōl uṭai āṭaiyar
has a dress and upper garment of skin.
ālam uṇṭu amutē mikat tēkkuvar
will consume the poison himself and give sumptuously nectar to others (gives out the smell of nectar when belching)
kālakālar
is himself the god of death to Kālaṉ (god of death)
mālai mārpar
wears a garland of koṉṟi on his chest.

kaṭavūriṉ mayāṉattār perumāṉaṭikaḷ
see 1st verse.
imaiyōr toḻu paiṅkaḻal iṟaivaṉār
is the Lord whose feet the celestials worship.
maṟavaṉār
assumed the form of a hunter to grant a weapon to aruccuṉaṉ
aṭiyār aṟavaṉār
is the embodiment of all virtues to his devotees.
aṭiyār taṅkaḷ piṟavi tīrppar
will remove rebirth to his devotees

kaṭavūriṉ mayāṉattār
see 1st verse.
kattu kāḷi katam taṇittavar
had the anger of roaring Kāḷi reduced (by his dance)
mattar (tām)
wears datura flowers
ottu ovvātaṉa ceytu uḻalvār oru pittar (kāṇum)
is like a mad man who does acts conforming to morals and non-conforming to them.
{5:38}__6+
{$}
kaṭavūriṉ mayāṉattār perumāṉaṭikaḷ
see 1st verse.
eri koḷ mēṉi iḷam piṟai vaittavar
Civaṉ has a body of fire and placed a crescent (on his head)
kariyar (tām)
has an elephant by name ayirāvaṇam.
aṇṭattuḷōr ayaṉ mālukkum ariyar
is difficult to be seen by the tēvar, Ayaṉ and even Māl.
periyar
has greatness.
kāṇum
(People!) you inderstand this.
{5:38}__7+
{$} aṇaṅku pākattar
Civaṉ has a lady as his half
āraṇa nāṉ maṟai kaṇaṅkaḷ cēr kaṭavūr mayaṉattār perumāṉaṭikaḷ
the divine being who is in Kaṭavur mayāṉam, is worshipped by the four vētams which have āraṇam as their part, and pūta kaṇams (āraṇam nāṉ maṟai āraṇam can be put in apposition with nāṉ maṟai ; that is another meaning.)
vaṇaṅku vār iṭar tīrppar
will remove the sufferings of those who bow to him.
mayakku uṟum piṇam koḷ kāṭar
has as his place the cremation ground where there are corpses which increase one's confusion.

maravu cēr kaṭavūriṉ mayāṉattār perumāṉaṭikaḷ
the divine being in Kaṭavūr mayāṉam where common Kaṭampa trees are growing densely (maravu-marā)
aravukaiyiṉar
holds a cobra in his hand
ātipurāṇaṉār
the old one who came self-existing before the world was created.
paravuvār iṭar tīrppar
will remove the sufferings of those who prise him.
piramaṉ māṟkum paṇikoḷvar
will accept the services of Piramaṉ and Māl.

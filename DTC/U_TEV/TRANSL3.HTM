English translations for 3rd Tirumuṟi

Standard editions contined 125 patikam-s; an additional patikam [3:126] was found on a temple wall; a last one [3:127] was published in a journal, as found in a palm leaf manuscript; there is also an apocryphal patikam, known as veḷḷip patikam [3:AAA] which has been collected in some editions (along with the standard 125). All these 128 have been translated by VMS.

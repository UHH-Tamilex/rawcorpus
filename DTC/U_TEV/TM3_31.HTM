• patikam: [3:31]
  • talam: tiru mayēntirappaḷḷi
  • paṇ: kolli
  • VMS: Vol.1, p.88

*1
tiri taru pavaḷamum, cīr tikaḻ vayiramum,
kari tarum _akiloṭu kaṉa vaḷi pukutarum,
varivilāl _eyil _eyta, mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ,
_aravu _ari, _aḻakaṉi _aṭi_iṇi paṇimiṉē!

*2
koṇṭal cēr kōpuram, kōlam _ār māḷiki,
kaṇṭalum kitiyum kamalam _ār vāviyum,
vaṇṭu _ulām poḻil, _aṇi mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ,
ceṇṭu cēr viṭiyiṉāṉ tiruntu _aṭi paṇimiṉē!

*3
kōṅku _iḷa vēṅkiyum, koḻu malarppuṉṉiyum,
tāṅku tēṉ koṉṟiyum, taku malarkkuravamum,
māṅ karumpum, vayal mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
_āṅku _iruntavaṉ kaḻal_aṭi_iṇi paṇimiṉē!

*4
vaṅkam _ār cēṇ _uyar varu kuṟiyāl miku
caṅkam _ār _oli, _akil taru puki kamaḻtarum
maṅki _ōrpaṅkiṉaṉ, mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
_eṅkaḷnāyakaṉtaṉatu _iṇi_aṭi paṇimiṉē!

*5
nittilattokipala niri taru malar _eṉac
cittirap puṇari cērttiṭa, tikaḻntu _iruntavaṉ,
mit tikaḻ kaṇṭaṉ, nal mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
kittalam maḻuvaṉik kaṇṭu, _aṭi paṇimiṉē!

*6
cantiraṉ, katiravaṉ, taku pukaḻ _ayaṉoṭum,
_intiraṉ, vaḻipaṭa _irunta _em _iṟiyavaṉ---
mantiramaṟi vaḷar mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
_antam _il _aḻakaṉi---_aṭi paṇintu _uymmiṉē!

*7
caṭimuṭi muṉivarkaḷ camivoṭum vaḻipaṭa
naṭam navil puriviṉaṉ, naṟavu _aṇi malaroṭu
paṭarcaṭi matiyiṉaṉ, mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
_aṭal viṭi _uṭiyavaṉ _aṭi paṇintu _uymmiṉē!

*8
ciram_orupatum _uṭic ceru vali _arakkaṉik
karam_irupatum _iṟak kaṉavari _aṭarttavaṉ,
maravu _amar pūmpoḻil mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
_aravu _amar caṭiyaṉi _aṭi paṇintu _uymmiṉē!

*9
nāka(_a)ṇit tuyilpavaṉ, nalam miku malaravaṉ,
_āka(_a)ṇintu _avar kaḻal _aṇiyavum peṟukilar;
māku _aṇintu _alarpoḻil mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
yōku _aṇintavaṉ kaḻal _uṇarntu _iruntu _uymmiṉē!

*10
_uṭi tuṟantavarkaḷum, _uṭi tuvar_uṭiyarum,---
paṭu paḻi _uṭiyavar---pakarvaṉa viṭumiṉ, nīr!
maṭi vaḷar vayal _aṇi mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
_iṭam _uṭi _īcaṉi _iṇi_aṭi paṇimiṉē!

*11
vampu _ulām poḻil _aṇi mayēntirappaḷḷiyuḷ
nampaṉār kaḻal_aṭi ñāṉacampantaṉ col,
nam param _itu _eṉa, nāviṉāl navilpavar,
_umparār _etirkoḷa, _uyar pati _aṇivarē.

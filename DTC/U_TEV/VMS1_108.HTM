{1:108}__1+
{$} (1) miṉ iyal ceñcaṭaimēl
on the red caṭi which glitters like the lightning.
viḷaṅkum mati mattamoṭu nalla poṉ iyal koṉṟaiyiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who wears along with the shining crescent datura flowers and flawless koṉṟi which is like gold in colour.
puṉalcūṭi
wearing the water (of Kaṅki)
(2) poṟpu amarum aṉṉam aṉa naṭaiyāḷ oru pākattu amarntaruḷi
Having placed with desire a lady who has a git like the graceful swan, on one half.
nāḷum paṉṉiya pāṭaliṉāṉ uṟaikōyil pātāḷē
pātaḷ is the temple where Civaṉ who dily sings songs dwells.
[[Note : This shrine is now known by the names of pāmpaṇi or pāmaṇi]]
[[Variant readings : (1) maṉṉiya (2) pōtu]]
{1:108}__2+
{$} nīṭu alarkoṉṟaiyoṭu nirampāmaticūṭi
wearing with the long flowers of koṉṟi a crescent.
veḷḷaittōṭu amar kātil nalla kuḻaiyāṉ
wears a men's ear-ring in the ear in which there is women's ear-ring made of pearls;
[[muttiṉ kaṉaṅkuḻaiyaiyum kumiḻaiyum katuvi (verse quoted in the commentary of tākkayākappraṇi, 13)]]
cuṭu nīṟṟāṉ
smears himself with well burnt sacred ash.
āṭu aravamperuka aṉalēntik kaivīci
swinging the arms and holding fire and the noise of dance to increase.
vētam pāṭaliṉāl iṉiyāṉ uṟaikōyil pātāḷē
pātaḷ is the temple where Civaṉ sings sweetly the vētams, dwells.
{1:108}__3+
{$} nākamum vāṉmatiyum nalam malku ceñcaṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has a red caṭi on which a cobra and a crescent that shines in the sky, are increasing in beauty (if vāṉmati is split into vālmati the meanings is white crescent)
(1) cāmam pōka
when the midnight elapsed.
nalvil varaiyāl puram mūṉṟu erittu ukantāṉ
became great by burning the three cities with a good mountin converted into a bow.
tōkai naṉ māmayil pōl vaḷar cāyal tūmoḻiyaik kūṭappākamum vaittu ukantāṉ uṟaikōyil pātāḷē
Pātāḷ is the temple where Civaṉ who felt pleasure in having on one half a lady who speaks blemishless words and who has an increasing gentleness like the good and big peacock which has a big til.
[[Variant reading: (1) cāpam]]
{1:108}__4+
{$} aṅkamum nāṉmaṟaiyum aruḷ ceytu
being the author of six aṅkams and the four vētams.
aḻaku ārnta am col maṅkai ōr kūṟu uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has a lady of exquisite beauty speaking beautiful words, on one half.
maṟaiyāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple where he who is the brahmin among gods, dwells.
ceṅkayal niṉṟu ukaḷum ceṟuvil tikaḻkiṉṟa cōtip paṅkayam niṉṟu alarum vayal cūḻnta pātāḷē
is pātaḷ which is surrounded by fields where the lotus blossoms always, which are shining brilliantly in the tanks where the red carp is always frisking.
{1:108}__5+
{$} pēy palavum nilavu ap peruṅkāṭu araṅkāka uṉṉi niṉṟu
thinking of that big cremation-ground in which many pēy are moving, and standing there.
tīyoṭu māṉmaṟiyum maḻuvum tikaḻvittu
cusing the fire, young one of a deer, and a battle-axe to be eminent.
tēypiṟaiyum(m) aravum poli koṉṟaic (1) caṭai taṉmēl cēra
to join on the caṭi wearing koṉṟi flowers, on which the waning crescent and cobra are conspicuous.
pāy puṉalum(m) uṭaiyāṉ uṟaikōyil pātāḷē
pātāḷ is the temple of Civaṉ who has swift-flowing water also.
[[Variant reading: (1) caṭaiyataṉmēl]]
{1:108}__6+
{$} kaṇ amar neṟṟiyiṉāṉ
Civaṉ has a forehead in which there is an additional eye.
kamaḻ koṉṟaic caṭaimēl (1) naṉṟu viṇiyal māmatiyum uṭaṉ vaittavaṉ
and placed on the caṭi which wears fragrant koṉṟi, the big crescent which moves in the sky brightly along with it.
virumpum peṇ amar mēṉiyiṉāṉ
has on his body a lady who loves him.
peruṅkāṭu araṅkāka āṭum paṇ iyal pāṭaliṉāṉ uṟaikōyil pātāḷē
pātāḷ is the temple where Civaṉ who sings songs set to melody-types and dances, having the big cremation ground as his stage, dwells.
[[Variant reading: (1) caṭaiyataṉmēl]]
{1:108}__7+
{$} viṇṭu alar mattamoṭu miḷirum iḷanākam vaṉṉitikaḻ vaṇṭu alarkoṉṟai nakumatipulku vār caṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has a long caṭi in which are crowded the datura flowers which blossom unfolding their petals, a shining young cobra, leaves of indian mesquit, glittering koṉṟi which blossoms to the humming of bees and a shining crescent.
viṇṭavartam puram mūṉṟu ericeytu
having burnt the three cities of the enemies.
urai vētam nāṉkum avai paṇṭu icai pāṭaliṉāṉ uṟai kōyil pātāḷē
pāṭāl is the temple where Civaṉ who revealed the four vētams and sang them setting them to music long ago.
{1:108}__8+
{$} malkiya nuṇiṭaiyāḷ umainaṅkai maṟuka
Umi, the lady of distinction who has a waist of increasing minuteness to whirl in her mind.
aṉṟukaiyāl tollai malai eṭutta arakkaṉ talai tōḷ nerittāṉ
Civaṉ who crushed the heads and shoulders of the arakkaṉ who lifted the ancient mountin with his hands, in the distant past.
kollaiviṭai ukantāṉ
who rejoiced riding on a bull belonging to the forest tract.
kuḷirtiṅkaḷ caṭaikku (1) aṇintōṉ
who adorned his caṭi with a cool crescent.
palicai pāṭaliṉāṉ uṟai kōyil pātāḷē
pātāḷ is the temple where he sings many melody types dwells.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṇintoli]]
{1:108}__9+
{$} tāmaraimēl ayaṉum ariyum
Piramaṉ who is sitting on a lotus and ari - (tirumāl)
tamatu āḷ viṉaiyāl tēṭik kāmaṉai vīṭuvittāṉ kaḻal kāṇpilarāy akaṉṟār
gave up their search without being able to see the feet and head of Civaṉ who destroyed Kāmaṉ (cupid), by their own efforts.
pūmaruvum kuḻalāḷ umai naṅkai poruntiyiṭṭa nalla pāmaruvum kuṇattāṉ uṟaikōyil pātāḷē
pāṭāḷ is the temple where Civaṉ whose attributes are sung in songs and who was united with Umi, a lady of distinction and who has tresses of hir into which flowers are tucked.
{1:108}__10+
{$} kālaiyil uṇpavarum camaṇkaiyarum kaṭṭuraiviṭṭu
discarding as of no substance the fabricated words of the buddhists who eat in the early morning and the low camaṇar.
aṉṟu ālaviṭam nukarntāṉ (avaṉ) taṉ aṭiyē paravi
prising only the feet of Civaṉ who consumed the poison, by name ālam in the distant past.
mālaiyil vaṇṭu iṉaṅkaḷ matuuṇṭu icaimurala vāytta pālai yāḻp pāṭṭu ukantāṉ uṟaikōyil pātāḷē
pāṭāl is the temple where Civaṉ who rejoiced in hearing the songs of pāṭāḷ yāḻ which is appropriate to the region when the swarms of bees drink the honey in flowers in the evening and hum like music dwells.
{1:108}__11+
{$} palmalar vaikupoḻil cūḻnta pātāḷaic cēra
to suit pāṭāḷ which is surrounded by gardens where many kinds of flowers are to be found.
poṉ iyal māṭam malku pukali(n)nakar maṉṉaṉ
the chief of the city of pukali where beautiful storeys are flourishing.
taṉ oḷimikku uyarnta tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉa coṉṉa iṉicai pattum vallār
those who are able to recite the ten verses of sweet music composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ having good knowledge of tamiḻ and great by his increasing divine lustre of the soul.
eḻil vāṉattu iruppār (ē)
will dwell after life in this world comes to an end, in heaven.

{2:10}__1+
{$} cīriṉ ār maṇiyum akil cantum ceṟivarai vāri nīr varu poṉṉi vaṭa maṅkalakkuṭi
in Maṅkalakkuṭi which is on the northern bank of the poṉṉi (Kaviri) which brings with its floods from the mountin which has in an abundant measure famous precious stones, eagle-wood trees, and sandal-wood trees
nīriṉ māmuṉivaṉ neṭuṅkai koṭu nīr taṉaip pūrittu āṭṭi arccikka irunta purāṇaṉē
Civaṉ is the primordial god who was there to be worshipped with flowers uttering his names; and bathing him, with water filled up in a vessel from the Kaviri, by stretching his long hands as far as the water in the Kaviri, by a great sage.
{2:10}__2+
{$} paṇam koḷ āṭu aravu alkul nallār payiṉṟu ētta maṇam koḷ, māmayil ālum, poḻil maṅkalakkuṭi
in maṅkalakkuṭi which has fragrant gardens in which great peacocks dance, when the ladies who have sides like the dancing cobra which has a hood, always sing his prises
iṇaṅku ilā (1) maṟaiyōr imaiyōr toḻutu ēttiṭa
and to be prised and worshipped with joined hands by brahmins who have no comparison and tēvar who do not wink
[[iṇaṅku comparison iṇaṅku ākum uṉakku avaḷē (Kōviyār, 68)]]
aṇaṅkiṉōṭu iruntāṉ aṭiyē caraṇ ākum
the feet of the god who was with a divine damsel, is our refuge
[[Variant reading: (1) maṟaiyōṉ]]
{2:10}__3+
{$} karuṅkaiyāṉai īr uri pōrttiṭu kaḷvaṉār
the thief who covered himself with the wet skin of a black elephant of large trunk
maruṅku elām maṇam ār poḻil cūḻ maṅkalakkuṭi
in maṅkalakkuṭi surrounded by gardens full of a fragrance which spreads on all sides
arumpu cēr malark koṉṟaiyiṉāṉ aṭi aṉpoṭu virumpi ēttavallār viṉai āyiṉa vīṭumē
the evil actions of those who are able to prise with desire and love the feet of the god who wears koṉṟi flowers along with their buds, with perish.
{2:10}__4+
{$} paṟaiyiṉōṭu oli pāṭalum āṭalum pāriṭam maṟaiyiṉōṭu iyal malkiṭuvār maṅkalakkuṭi
in maṅkalakkuṭi where brahmins who are eminent by their knowledge of vētam and conduct prescribed in the vētam, and where groups of pūtam indulge in singing and dancing to the accompaniment of the sound of drums
[[paṟaiyiṉōṭu oli should be rearranged as paṟaiyiṉ oliyōṭu]]
kuṟaivu ilā niṟaivē
`you who are full without deficiency'
kuṇam il kuṇamē
`you who possess the eight great qualities'
eṉṟu
prising like this
muṟaiyiṉāl vaṇaṅku(m) mavar muṉṉeṟi kāṇpar
those who bow to him according to rules will perceive the foremost path which leads to eternal bliss.
{2:10}__5+
{$} āṉil amkiḷar aintum avir muṭi āṭi ōr māṉ ilaṅku kaiyiṉāṉ maṇam ār maṅkalakkuṭi
in maṅkalakkuṭi which is full of fragrance, and belonging to Civaṉ on whose hand a deer is prominent and who has a shining head on which the important five products from the cow are poured for bathing
[[ilaṅkukaiyiṉāṉ these words have become ilaṅkaiyiṉāṉ; kulaiyalaṅkāntaḷ -(Kalittoki, 40-12);
[[kulai alaṅku kāntaḷ have become kulaiyalaṅkāntaḷ; the words may also be split into māṉ nil aṅkaiyiṉāṉ one on whose beautiful hand the deer stands]]
ūṉ il veṇ talaik kaiyuṭaiyāṉ uyar pātamē ñāṉamāka niṉṟu ēttavallārviṉai nācamē
all the actions of those who can prise the eminent feet of Civaṉ who has on his hand a white skull which has no flesh, which is itself Civañāṉam, will meet with destruction
{2:10}__6+
{$} tēṉum āy amutu āki niṉṟāṉ, teḷi cintaiyuḷ
in the clear minds of devotees Civaṉ was always like honey and nectar
vāṉum āy mati cūṭavallāṉ maṅkalakkuṭik kōṉai
the Lord in maṅkalakkuṭi who wears a crescent, being himself the spiritual atmosphere.
nāḷ toṟum ēttik kuṇam koṭu kūṟuvār ūṉam āṉavai pōy aṟum, uyyum vakai (atu)
the defects of those who prise and appreciate his qualities dily, will completely vanish so that they can be saved
{2:10}__7+
{$} vēḷ paṭuttiṭu kaṇṇiṉaṉ, mēruvillākavē vāḷ arakkar puram erittāṉ, maṅkalakkuṭi āḷum ātip pirāṉ
Civaṉ who made cupid to die from the fire issued from the frontal eye, who burnt the cities of the cruel arakkar using the mountin, mēru, as a bow and who reigns in maṅkalakkuṭi and is the origin of all things
aṭikaḷ aṭaintu ētta
If people prise his feet having sought them as refuge
kōḷum nāḷum pōy aṟum
the evil effects of planets and stars will completely disappear from them
kuṟṟam illārkaḷ
they are without blemish
{2:10}__8+
{$} poliyum mālvarai pukku eṭuttāṉ pukaḻntu ēttiṭa
when (Irāvaṇaṉ) who went under the big and celebrated mountin of Kailācam, and lifted it, sang the Lord`s prises
valiyum vāḷoṭu nāḷ koṭuttāṉ maṅkalakkuṭip puliyaṉ āṭaiyiṉāṉ aṭi ēttiṭum puṇṇiyar
the virtuous people who prise the feet of the Lord in maṅkalakkuṭi who wears a tiger`s skin, and granted strength, a sword and a long span of life, to him
maliyum vāṉ ulakam pukavallavar; kāṇmiṉ
are able to enter into heaven which abounds in very many good things; you know this.
{2:10}__9+
{$} ñālam muṉ paṭaittāṉ naḷir māmalarmēl ayaṉ mālum kāṇa oṇā eriyāṉ maṅkalakkuṭi
in maṅkalakkuṭi where Civaṉ who had a form of fire which could not be known by Māl and Ayaṉ who is seated on a cool and big (lotus) flower, the creator of the world in the beginning
ēlavār kuḻalāḷ oru pākam iṭaṅkoṭu kōlam āki niṉṟāṉ kuṇam kūṟum; kuṇam atē
(people of this world!) sing the qualities of the god who was beautiful by having on the left half a lady with long tresses of hir on which an unguent is rubbed for adding perfume to it; that is the proper thing to do; others are not.
{2:10}__10+
{$} meyyil māciṉar, 1. mēṉivirituvar āṭaiyar poyyai viṭṭiṭum puṇṇiyar cēr maṅkalakkuṭi
in maṅkalakkuṭi where virtuous people who have completely turned a deaf ear to the lies of jins who have dirt on their bodies, and buddhist who don unfolded saffron robes on their bodies.
ceyyamēṇi, ceḻumpuṉaṟ kaṅkai ceṟicaṭai aiyaṉ cēvaṭi ēttavallārkku aḻaku ākum
Beauty will come to those who are able to prise the red feet of Civaṉ who has on his thick cati Kaṅki of abundant water, and who has a red body
[[Variant reading: 1. tukil]]
{2:10}__11+
{$} mantam ām poḻil cūḻ maṅkalakkuṭi maṉṉiya entaiyai
about my father who stays permanently in maṅkalakkuṭi surrounded by gardens from which gentle southern breeze starts
eḻil ār poḻiṟ kāḻiyar kāvalaṉ ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ, the eminent person among the natives Kāḻi which has beautiful gardens
cintai ceytu aṭi cērttiṭu col
the verses that were composed by fixing his mind on god, and are capable of helping to reach the feet of the god
munti ēttavallār imaiyōr mutal āvar
those who are able to prise god with these early in their lives will become the king of tēvar who do not wink

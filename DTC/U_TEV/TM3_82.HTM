*1

*2

_āmaiyoṭu pūṇum _aṭikaḷ(ḷ); _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*3

_āṟu _uṭaiya vārcaṭaiyiṉāṉ _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*4

_aṇiyum _aḻaku_āka _uṭaiyāṉ _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*5
kuḻaliṉ varivaṇṭu mural melliyaṉapoṉmalarkaḷ koṇṭu,

_aḻalum maḻu _ēntu kaiyiṉāṉ; _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*6

nañcu miṭaṟu _uṇṭu, karitu_āya veḷitu_āki _oru nampaṉ;

_añca, matavēḻa _uriyāṉ; _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*7

_āṭu_aravam _ārtta perumāṉ _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*8
_oruvaraiyum mēl vali koṭēṉ _eṉa _eḻunta viṟalōṉ, _ip

_aru varaiyil _ūṉṟi _aṭarttāṉ _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*9

_aṟivu _ariyaṉ_āya perumāṉ _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*10

_aḻi _aruvi tōynta perumāṉ _uṟaivatu---_avaḷivaṇalūrē.

*11

• patikam: [4:82]
  • talam: tiruk kaḻumalam (_allatu kāḻi)
  • (()): tiru viruttam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.461

*1
pār koṇṭu mūṭik kaṭal koṇṭañāṉṟu niṉ pātam _ellām
nāl-_añcu puḷ_iṉam _ēntiṉa _eṉpar; naḷirmatiyam
kāl koṇṭa vaṇkic caṭi virittu _āṭum kaḻumalavarkku
_āḷ_aṉṟi maṟṟum _uṇṭō, _am taṇ _āḻi _akaliṭamē?

*2
kaṭi _ār koṭi neṭumāṭaṅkaḷ _eṅkum kalantu _ilaṅka
_uṭiyāṉ, _uṭitalimāliyum cūṭi _ukantu_aruḷi,
viṭitāṉ _uṭiya _av vētiyaṉ vāḻum kaḻumalattuḷ
_aṭivār---viṉikaḷ_avi _eṉka!---nāḷtoṟum _āṭuvarē!

*3
tirivāyp peruṅkaṭal muttam kuvippa, mukantukoṇṭu
nurivāy nuḷicciyar _ōṭik kaḻumalattuḷ(ḷ) _aḻuntum
viri vāy naṟumalar cūṭiya viṇṇavaṉtaṉ _aṭikkē
variyāp paricu_ivi nāḷtoṟum namtami _āḷvaṉavē.

*4
virikkum, _arum patam; vētaṅkaḷ _ōtum; viḻumiya nūl
_urikkil _arumporuḷ _uḷḷuvar; kēṭkil _ulakam muṟṟum
_irikkum paṟiyoṭu pūtaṅkaḷ pāṭa, kaḻumalavaṉ
niruttam paḻampaṭi _āṭum kaḻal nammi _āḷvaṉavē.

*5
cintittu _eḻu,---maṉamē!---niṉiyāmuṉ kaḻumalatti!
pantitta valviṉi tīrkka vallāṉi, pacupatiyi,
cantitta kālam _aṟuttum _eṉṟu _eṇṇi _iruntavarkku
muntit toḻu kaḻal nāḷtoṟum namtammi _āḷvaṉavē.

*6
niliyum perumiyum nītiyum cāla _aḻaku _uṭittu_āy,
_aliyum peruveḷḷattu _aṉṟu mitanta _it tōṇipuram,
ciliyil-tiri purammūṉṟu _erittār, tam kaḻumalavar,
_alarum kaḻal_aṭi nāḷtoṟum namtami _āḷvaṉavē.

*7
muṟṟik kiṭantu munnīriṉ mitantu, _uṭaṉmoyttu _amarar
cuṟṟik kiṭantu, toḻappaṭukiṉṟatu---cūḻ _aravam
teṟṟik kiṭantu veṅ koṉṟiyum tuṉṟi veṇtiṅkaḷ cūṭum
kaṟṟic caṭimuṭiyārkku _iṭam _āya kaḻumalamē.

*8
_uṭalum _uyirum _oruvaḻic cellum _ulakattuḷḷē
_aṭiyum _uṉi vantu _aṭintār, _amarar _aṭi_iṇikkīḻ;
naṭiyum viḻavoṭu nāḷtoṟum malkum kaḻumalattuḷ
viṭiyaṉ taṉippatam nāḷtoṟum namtami _āḷvaṉavē.

*9
paravik-kaṭalnañcam _uṇṭatum _illi; _ip pārmuḻutum
niravik kiṭantu toḻappaṭukiṉṟatu;---nīṇṭu _iruvar
ciramappaṭa vantu cārntār, kaḻal_aṭi kāṇpataṟkē---
_aravak kaḻal_aṭi nāḷtoṟum namtami _āḷvaṉavē.

*10
kali _ār kaṭal cūḻ _ilaṅkiyarkōṉtaṉ muṭi citaṟat
toliyā malar_aṭi _ūṉṟalum, _uḷḷam vitirvitirttut
tali_āyk kiṭantu, _uyarntāṉtaṉ kaḻumalam kāṇpataṟkē
_aliyāp paricu_ivi nāḷtoṟum namtami _āḷvaṉavē.

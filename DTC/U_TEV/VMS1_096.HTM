{1:96}__1+
{$} ceṉṉi ārpiṟai maṉṉi ūr iṟai
the Lord on whose head the crescent is staying permanently and seems to crawl.
aṉṉiyūr amar maṉṉu cōtiyē
is the permanent light that dwells in Aṉṉiyūr.
{1:96}__2+
{$} paḻakum toṇṭarvam
devotees who practise worshipping god! Please come (vam-vammiṉ)
aḻakaṉ aṉṉiyūrk kuḻakaṉ cēvaṭi toḻutu vāḻmiṉ
Live happily by worshipping the red feet of the young and beautiful god in aṉṉiyūr.
{1:96}__3+
{$} nītipēṇuvīr
you who are desirous of right conduct!
āti aṉṉiyūrc cōtināmamē ōti
uttering only the names of the light in aṉṉiyūr who is the origin of all things.
uymmiṉ
save yourselves.
{1:96}__4+
{$} pattar āyiṉīr
Devotees!
attar aṉṉiyūrccittartāḷ toḻa
if you worship the feet of the cittar in aṉṉiyūr who is also our father.
muttar āvarē
you will be free from the cycle of birth and death (If the reading were āvirē it would be appropriate)
{1:96}__5+
{$} niṟaivu vēṇṭuvīr
People who desire fulness!
aṟavam aṉṉiyūr maṟai uḷāṉ kaḻaṟku uṟavu ceymmiṉē
Develop intimacy with the feet of Civaṉ in aṉṉiyūr who is the hidden meaning of the vētam and the embodiment of all virtues.
{1:96}__6+
{$} iṉpam vēṇṭuvīr
People who are in quest of happiness!
umpar āka
to become tēvar.
aṉpaṉ aṉṉiyūr naṉpoṉ eṉṉumiṉ
always say, our friend! the gold without impurities in aṉṉiyūr.
{1:96}__7+
{$} antaṇāḷartam tantai, aṉṉiyūr entaiyē eṉa
People of this world! if you say, Father of the brahmins! our father in aṉṉiyūr!
pantam nīṅkumē
all your bondages will leave you of their own accord.
{1:96}__8+
{$} tūrttaṉaic ceṉṟa tīrttaṉ aṉṉiyūr āttamā aṭaintu
reaching with friendly feelings aṉṉiyūr belonging to the pure Civaṉ who destroyed the debuchee (Irāvaṇaṉ)
ētti (1) vāḻmiṉ
Live happily by prising him.
[[Variant reading: (1) uymmiṉ]]
{1:96}__9+
{$} iruvar nāṭiya aravaṉ aṉṉiyūr paravuvār
Those who prise aṉṉiyūr belonging to Civaṉ who was searched by both Māl and Piramaṉ and who has a cobra.
viṇṇukku oruvar āvar
will become the sole ruler of heaven.
{1:96}__10+
{$} kuṇṭar tērarukku aṇṭaṉ aṉṉiyūrt toṇṭu uḷār viṉai
all the Karmams of those devotees in aṉṉiyūr belonging to Civaṉ who could not be approached by the low persons (jins) and tērar (buddhists) (aṇṭaṉ-aṇṭāṉ)
viṇṭupōkum
will go away from them, incapable of yielding their fruits.
{1:96}__11+
{$} pūntarāyp pantaṉ āynta pāṭalāl
with the help of the verse composed by Pantaṉ of Pūntarāy, with chosen words.
vēntaṉ aṉṉiyūr cērntu vāḻmiṉ
Live happily thinking always Aṉṉiyūr belonging to the chief (Civaṉ)

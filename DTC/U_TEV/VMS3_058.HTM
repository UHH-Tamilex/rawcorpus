[[This place is now known as Ceyyāttamaṅki; cāttamanki is the name of the place and ayavanti is the name of the temple.]]

{3:58}__1+
{$} tirumalarkkoṉṟai mālai tiḷaikkum mati ceṉṉi vaittīr
you placed on your head the crescent which is always enjoying the fragrance of the garland made with the distinctive koṉṟi flowers.
irumalarkkaṇṇi taṉṉōṭu uṭaṉāvatum ēṟpatu oṉṟē
Is being united with a lady having large eyes like big lotus a thing that is consented to be good by all?
perumalarc cōlai mēkam uriñcum peruñcātta maṅkai arumāl ātimūrtti ayavanti amarntavaṉē
one who dwells in the temple ayavanti! - the first cuse who has a form and is seated in the hearts of devotees in the great cāttamaṅki where the clouds, rub aginst the gardens which have big flowers.
{3:58}__2+
{$} poṭitaṉaip pūcum mārpil purinūl oru pāl porunta
when the sacred thread of three strands stays in one part of the chest on which sacred ash is smeared.
koṭi aṉa cāyalāḷōṭu uṭaṉ āvatum kūṭuvatē
is it a fit pact to be united with a tender lady who is like a slender creeper?
kaṭi maṇam malki nāḷum kamaḻum poḻiṟ cāttamaṅkai aṭikaḷ nakkaṉ parava
to be prised by the sage nakkaṉ in cāttamaṅki which has gardens from which new fragrance spreads dily. (This cāttamaṅki was the birth-place nīlanakkanāyaṉār; see also 11th verse of this decade; his story is told in detil in periya purāṇam; tirunīlanakka nayaṉār purāṇam)
{3:58}__3+
{$} nūl nalam taṅku mārpiṉ nukarnīṟu aṇintu
having adorned his chest where a good sacred thread is staying, smearing sacred ash.
ēṟu atu ēṟi
riding upon a bull.
māṉ aṉa nōkkiyōṭu uṭaṉ āvatum māṇpatuvē
is it dignified to be united with a lady who has looks like the deer?
tāṉ nal am koṇṭu mēkam tavaḻum poḻil cāttamaṅkai
in cāttamaṅki which has gardens on which the clouds with good water crawl.
āṉ nalam tōynta emmāṉ ayavanti amarntavaṉē
our Lord who bathed in the good five products of the cow and who dwells in ayavanti desiring it!
{3:58}__4+
{$} maṟṟavil mālvaraiyā matil eytu
having shot an arrow on the forts improvising the big mēru mountin as ginst the forts of his enemies.
veṇṇīṟu pūci
having smeared yourself with white sacred ash.
puṟṟu aravu alkulāḷōṭu uṭaṉ āvatum poṟpatuvē
is it graceful to be united with a lady whose waist resembles the hood of the cobra that lives in an anthill?
kaṟṟavar cāttamaṅkai nakarkai toḻac ceyta pāvam aṟṟavar nāḷum ētta
in the city of cāttamaṅki where learned scholars live, to be prised dily by people whose sins committed in previous birth become extinct as they worship Civaṉ with their hands.
ayavanti amarntavaṉē
Civaṉ who dwells in ayavanti with desire!
{3:58}__5+
{$} venta veṇṇīṟu pūci viṭai ēṟiya vētakītaṉ
one who sings the vētams, rides on a bull, and smears himself with well-burnt white sacred ash!
pantu aṇavum viralāḷ uṭaṉāvatum poṟpatuvē
is it elegance to be united with a lady who holds a ball in her fingers?
cantam āṟu aṅkam vētam tarittār toḻum cāttamaṅkai
in cāttamaṅki where brahmins who retin in their memory the beautiful six aṅkams and the four vetams, worship.
antamāy āti āki
being the end and the cuse of all things.
ayavanti amarntavaṉē
Civaṉ who dwells with desire in ayavanti!
{3:58}__6+
{$} vētam āy vēḷvi āki
himself being the vētam and the sacrifices.
viḷaṅkum poruḷ vīṭu (atu) āki
being salvation the fourth objective worthy of human pursuit which is well known to all.
cōti āy
and being the effulgent light.
maṅkai pākam nilaitāṉ collal āvatu aṉṟē
can the state of having a lady as a half be spoken prisingly?
cātiyāl mikkacīrāl takuvārtoḻum cāttamaṅkai
in cāttamaṅki where worthy people of great fame and superior by their caste conduct worship.
ātiyāy niṟai 1. pemmāṉ
the great one who is the first cuse of all things!
ayavanti amarntavaṉē
one who dwells in ayavanti with desire!
[[Variant reading: 1. emmāṉ]]
{3:58}__7+
{$} imayam ellām iriya
all the mountins beginning from imayam to quake in their position.
matil eytu
shooting an arrow on the forts
veṇṇīṟu pūci
smearing white sacred ash.
umaiyai ōr pākam vaitta nilaitāṉ uṉṉal āvatu oṉṟē
is the state of having on one half Umi to be thought of with appreciation?
camayam āṟu aṅkam vētam tarittār toḻum cāttamaṅkai
in cāttamaṅki where brahmins retin vetam in their minds, religious works, six ankams and four vētams, worship you.
amaiya vēṟu ōṅku cīrāṉ
Civaṉ who has a fame different from others and is appropriate!
ayavanti amarntavaṉē
one who dwells in ayavanti with desire!
{3:58}__8+
{$} paṇ ulām pāṭal vīṇai payilvāṉ ōr paramayōki
the supreme yōki who practises on vīṇi songs which have hepatatonic primary melody-types!
viṇ ulām māl varaiyāṉ makaḷ pākamum vēṇṭiṉaiyē
is it right on your part to desire on your half the dughter of the big mountin which seems to touch the sky!
taṇ nilā veṇ matiyam tavaḻum poḻil cāttamaṅkai
in cāttamaṅki which has gardens in which the white moon which gives cool moonlight seems to crawl.
aṇṇal āy niṉṟa emmāṉ
our Lord who remined as an eminent god!
ayavanti amarntavaṉē
Civaṉ who dwells in ayavanti with desire.
{3:58}__9+
{$} pēr eḻil tōḷ arakkaṉ vali ceṟṟatum peṇ ōr pākam īr eḻil kōlam āki uṭaṉ āvatum ēṟpatu oṉṟē
is it fitting to be two different form having a lady on one half and destroying the strength of the arakkaṉ who had very beautiful shoulders!
kār eḻil vaṇṇaṉōṭu kaṉakam(m) aṉaiyāṉum kāṇā ār aḻal vaṇṇam maṅkai ayavanti amarntavaṉē
Civaṉ who dwelt in the temple, ayavanti, in maṅki, having the form of fire of unbearable heat which could not be seen by Māl who has the beauty of the sable cloud, and Piramaṉ who is golden in colour.
{3:58}__10+
{$} kaṅkai ōr vār caṭaimēl aṭaiya
when the river, Kaṅki reached the single long caṭi.
puṭaiyē
by the side of your body.
kamaḻum maṅkaiyōṭu oṉṟi niṉṟa(m) mati tāṉ collal āvatu ōṉṟē
Is the fact of being united with a lady whom you married to be spoken prisingly?
caṅkai illā maṟaiyōr avartām toḻum cāttamaṅkai
in cāttamaṅki, which brahmins well-versed in the vētams and whose num,ber is countless, worship.
aṅkaiyil ceṉṉi vaittāy
you placed in your palm the skull (of Piramaṉ)
ayavanti amarntavaṉē
Civaṉ who dwelt in the temple, ayavanti, with desire!
{3:58}__11+
{$} maṟaiyiṉār malku kāḻit tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ possessing knowledge of tamiḻ and a native of Kaḻi where brahmins are flourishing.
maṉṉum niṟaiyiṉār, nīla nakkaṉ neṭumānakar eṉṟu toṇṭar aṟaiyum ūr
the place which is spoken prisingly by the devotees as the big and long city of nialanakkaṉ of moral firmness which never leaves him.
cāttamaṅkai ayavantimēl
on the temple, ayavanti, which is in cāttamaṅki.
āynta pattum
with the ten verses composed with beauty
muṟaimaiyāl ēttavallār
those who are capable of prising Civaṉ in the proper manner.
imaiyōrilum muntuvarē
will get precedence over the celestials who do not wink. (nīlanakkar, one of the nāyaṉmār, was a contemporary of Ñāṉacampantar is prised in the 2nd and 10th verse of this decade; It is note-worthy that camaṇar and buddhists are not mentioned in this decade as it is his wont)

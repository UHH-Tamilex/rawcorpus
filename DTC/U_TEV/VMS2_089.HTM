{$} aṟaiyum pūm puṉalōṭum āṭu aravac caṭai taṉmēl pi�yuñ cūṭuvar
the god wears on the caṭi which has a dancing cobra, a crescent in addition to the sounding beautiful water.
mārpil peṇṇoru pākam amarntār
He desired to have a lady on his form.
[[(mārpu - uṭal ; the part stands for ther whole;
melliyilai mallal, taṉ niṟam oṉṟil irutti niṉṟōṉ (Kōviyār, 58);
melliyalai aḻakaiyuṭaiya taṉ tirumēṉi oṉṟiṉkaṇ irutti niṉṟavaṉ (Do pērāciriyar`s commentary)]]
maṟaiyiṉ olloli ōvā, mantira vēḷvi aṟāta, kuṟaivu il antaṇar vāḻum koccaivayam amarntār
was residing happily in Koccivayam where brahmins who are without wants, and where the great sound produced by the chanting of the vētam and sacrifices performed with mantiram, are never ceasing. (kuṟaivu mistakes committed in chanting the vētam according to the dictates of the vētam and performing sacrifices)
{2:89}__2+
{$} cuṇṇattar
god who smears his person with sacred ash
tōloṭu nūl cēr mārpiṉar
has on his chest a skin and the sacred thread.
tuṉṉiya pūtak kaṇṇattar
has the crowd of putams which are close to him. (kaṇṇattār - kaṇattar, kaṇam crowd)
veṅkaṉal ēntik kaṅkum niṉṟu āṭuvar
he will dance standing at night holding a hot fire.
kēṭu il eṇṇattar
people who have no evil thoughts.
kēḷvi nal vēḷvi aṟātavar
who perform ceaselessly sacrifices ordined in the vetam. (kēḷvi vētam cf. Sanskrit cruti)
māl eri ōmpum vaṇṇatta antaṇar vāḻum koccai vayam amarntār
dwells happily in Kōccivayam where good natured brahmins mintin the great fires.
{2:89}__3+
{$} pālai aṉṉa veṇṇīṟu pūcuvar
God besmears white sacred ash as white as milk.
pal caṭai tāḻa mālai āṭuvar
He will dance at night to make the many caṭi to hang low.
māmaṟai pāṭutal makiḻvar
He delights in singing the great vētam.
vēlai māl kaṭal ōtam veṇtirai karai micai viḷaṅkum kōlam mā maṇi cintum koccai vayam amarntār
dwells happily in Kōccivayam where the white-created waves that rise in the big ocean protected by the shore, scatters on the shore big and beautiful pearls.
{2:89}__4+
{$} kaṭi koḷ kūviḷam mattam kamaḻ caṭai neṭu muṭikku aṇivar
the god adorns on his fragrant caṭi coiled into a crown, fragrant bel leaves and dhatura flowers.
poṭikaḷ pūciya mārpil puṉaivar
he will adorn his chest on which ash is besmeared (shell of the tortoise, bones, and hog's tusk)
nal maṅkai ōr paṅkar
Has on one half a beautiful lady
koṭikaḷ ōṅkiya māṭak koccai vayam amarntār
he dwells happily in Koccivayam which has storeys where flags are flying high
kaṭi koḷ nīṭu oli caṅkiṉ oliyōṭu kalai oli tutaintu
where the bustle at the time of marriages, the sound of the conch, the sound of the people who learn vetam and other arts combine together.
{2:89}__5+
{$} āṭal māmati uṭaiyār
the god has a big crescent which is quivering.
pāriṭam āyiṉa cūḻa
surrounded by pūtams
vāṭal veṇ talai ēnti
holding a dried white skull
vaiyakam iṭupalikku uḻavār
he wanders to receive alms given by the people of this world.
kūṭu taṇ poḻil cūḻnta koccai vayam amarntār
dwells happily in Koccivayam surrounded by cool parks
āṭal mā maṭa maññai aṇi tikaḻ pēṭaiyoṭu āṭi
where the great young male peacock having danced with its beautiful female one, mates.
{2:89}__6+
{$} maṇṭu kaṅkaiyum aravum malkiya vaḷar caṭai taṉ mēl, tuṇṭa veṇ piṟai aṇivar
God adorns on his growing caṭi on which the Kaṇki is moving swiftly, and cobras are flourishing a white crescent which is a segment of the moon.
tol varaivil atu āka
using as a bow the ancient (mēru mountin
viṇṭa tāṉavar araṇam vev vaḻal erikoḷa
the fortress of the inimical acurar to be consumed by the fire of unbearable heat.
viṭai mēl koṇṭa kōlam atu uṭaiyār koccai vayam amarntār
god who rode beautifully, is dwelling in Koccivayam

{$} aṉṟu a āl niḻal amarntu
sitting under that banyan tree in the distant past (a : demonstrative letter denotes its fame)
aṟavurai nālvarkku aruḷi
and bestowing religious instruction to four sages.
poṉṟiṉār talai ōṭṭil uṇpatu
ate in the skull of (Piramaṉ) who died.
porukaṭal ilaṅkai veṉṟi vēntaṉai olka ūṉṟiya viraliṉar
God has a toe with which he pressed down the King of ilaṇki surrounded by the sea dashing aginst the shore, who was fermerly victorious, to be reduced in strength.
vāṉ tōy kuṉṟam aṉṉa poṉ māṭak koccai vayam amarntār
dwells in Koccivayam which has beautiful storeys which resemble the hill on which the cloud rests.

ēr koḷ vev vaḻal āki eṅkum uṟa nimirntārum

resides in Koccivayam with a lady speaking words as sweet as milk, holding a sharp trident on the right hand.

{$} kuṇṭar

vaṇtuvar āṭai pōrttatu ōr koḷkaiyiṉārkaḷ
Buddhists who observe the principle of covering their bodies with fast coloured yellow robes.
miṇṭar pēciya pēccu mey ala
the words which those ignoramus talk is not truth.
mai aṇi kaṇṭaṉ
one who has a neck made beautiful by the poison
paṇṭai nam viṉai tīrkkum paṇpiṉar
and who destroys our past actions (good and bad)

{$} kontu aṇi poḻil cūḻnta kockai vaya nakar mēya antaṇaṉ aṭi ēttum arumaṟai ñāṉacampantaṉ
composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who was well-versed in the abstruse vetam and who prised the feet of the Lord who dwells happily in the city of Koccivayam surrounded by parks which have bunches of flowers.
cantam ārntu aḻaku āya taṇ tamiḻ mālai vallār
those who are able to recite the cool, tamiḻ garland of beautiful verses which are full of rhytmic movement.

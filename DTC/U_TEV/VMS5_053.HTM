{5:53}__1+
{$} kōṇal māmati cūṭi
wearing a curved and big crescent.
ōr kōvaṇam nāṇ il vāḻkkai nayantum
though desiring a life without shame wearing a men's loin cloth.
payaṉnilai
Civaṉ is the stand for all benefits.
(1) pāṇil vīṇai payiṉṟavaṉ
among music of several instruments he practises on vīṇai;
[[vēyuṟu tōḷi paṅkaṉ viṭam uṇṭa kaṇṭaṉ mika nalla vīṇai taṭavi (campaṇtar Korḷaṟu patikam, 1)]]
[[em iṟai nal vīṇai vācikkumē (nāvukkaracar, taṉittiruviruttam (1) 7)]]
vīraṭṭam kāṇil allatu
unless I visit and see his vīraṭṭam.
eṉkaṇtuyil koḷḷumē
will my eyes get sleep
[[cūṭi, payiṉṟavaṉ vīraṭṭam is the syntactical link]]
[[Variant reading: (1) pāṇaivīṇai]]
{5:53}__2+
{$} paṇṇiṉai
Civan who is in the form of music.
pavaḷattiraḷ māmaṇi aṇṇalai
the god who is like the heap of coral which is one of the nine gems.
amarar toḻum ātiyai
the first cause whom the immortals worship with joined hand.
cuṇṇa veṇ poṭiyāṉ vīraṭṭam naṇṇil allatu eṉ kaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
unless I reach vīraṭṭam of Civaṉ who smears sacred ash as scented power, will my eyes get sleep?
{5:53}__3+
{$} uṟṟavar tam nōy kaḷaipavar
Civaṉ who weeds out the many afflictions of those who reach him as their support.
peṟṟam ēṟumpiṟaṅku caṭaiyiṉar
who rides on a bull and has a shining caṭai.
cuṟṟum pāypuṉal cūḻtaru vīraṭṭam kaṟkil allatu eṉkaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
unless I learn about his vīraṭṭam surrounded by water which flows on all sides, will my eyes get sleep?
{5:53}__4+
{$} muṟṟā veṇmati cūṭum mutalvaṉār
the first cause who wears a white crescent.
ceṟṟār vāḻum tiripuram tī eḻa viltāṉ koṇṭu eyil (1) eytavar vīraṭṭam kaṟṟāl allatu
unless I learn about vīraṭṭam of the Lord who shot an arrow on the forte by using a bow to set a blaze the three cities in which the enemies resided.
eṉkaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
will my eyes get sleep
[[though the destruction of the three cities is a valourous act of Civaṉ it gains added significane as atikai is the place where that valourous act was performed; pūmaṉ ciram kaṇṭi; antakaṉ kōval, puram atikai māmaṉ paṟiyal calantaraṉ viṟkuṭi; māvaḻuvūr; kāmaṉ kuṟukkai; namaṉ kaṭavūr; intak kāciṉiyil, tēmaṉṉu koṉṟaic caṭaiyāṉ porutiṭṭa cēvakamē (old verse) Kantiyūr is the place where one of the five heads of Piramaṉ was severed; Kōvālūr is the place where the acuraṉ, antakaṉ was killed; the destruction of the three cities took place in atikai; takkaṉ's sacrifice was devastated in paṟiyalūr; the acuraṉ calantaraṉ was killed in vīṟkuṭi; the elephant was flayed in vaḻuvūr; Kamaṉ (cupid) was burnt at Kuṟukkai; the god of death was killed in Kaṭavūr; these are the eight valourous acts of Civaṉ and the places where they took place.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) eytavaṉ]]
[[The destruction of the three cities is mentioned in verses 4,5,6 and 10 in this decade]]
{5:53}__5+
{$} pallārum pala tēvar (um) paṇipavar
Civaṉ to whom many people of this world and immortals pay homage
nallārum nayantu ēttap(1) paṭupavaṉ
who is praised with devotion by good people also.
villāl mūeyil eytavaṉ vīraṭṭam kallēṉ ākil eṉ kaṇtuyil koḷḷumē
unless I learn about vīraṭṭam of Civaṉ who shot an arrow from the bow on the three forts, will my eyes get sleep?
[[Variant reading: (1) paṭupavar]]
{5:53}__6+
{$} vaṇṭu ārkoṉṟaiyum mattam (um) kōlamatiyōṭu aravamum vaḷarcaṭaikkoṇṭāṉ
Civaṉ held in his growing caṭai koṉṟai flowers on which bees hum, datura flowers, a beautiful crescent and a cobra.
viṇṭār mummatil eytavaṉ vīraṭṭam kaṇṭāl allatu eṉ kaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
will my eyes get sleep unless I weep on behalf of the eminent Vīrattam where the god who smears fragrant sacred ash and who is on the bank of the Keṭilam whose water flows having waves during its course.
{5:53}__7+
{$} arai ār kōvaṇa āṭaiyaṉ
Civaṉ wears in his waist a men's loin cloth.
āṟu elām tirai ār oṇ puṉal pāy keṭilakkarai virai ār niṟṟaṉ viḷaṅku vīraṭṭaṉ pāl karaiyēṉākil eṉ kaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
will my eyes get sleep unless I weep on behalf of the eminent vīrattam where the god who smears fragrant sacred ash and who is on the bank of the Keṭilam whose water flows having waves during its course.
{5:53}__8+
{$} nīṟu uṭait taṭantōḷ uṭai niṉmalaṉ
the spotless god who smears on his big shoulders sacred ash.
āṟuṭaip puṉal pāy keṭilak karai ēṟu uṭaikkoṭiyāṉ tiruvīraṭṭam kūṟil allatu eṉ kaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
will my eyes get sleep unless I speak about Tiruvīrattam where on the bank of the river Keṭilam whose water always flows, the Lord who has a flag of a bull dwells?
{5:53}__9+
{$} ceṅkaṇ māl viṭai ēṟiya celvaṉār
the god who rode on a big bull with red eyes.
paiṅkaṇ āṇaiyiṉ īruri (1) pōrttavar
who covered himself with the skin wet with blood of an elephant of tender eyes.
amkaṇ ñālam (atu) ākiya vīraṭṭam (2) kaṅkul āka eṉ kaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
will my eyes get sleep when night comes unless I see Vīraṭṭam where Civaṉ who pervades the world of beautiful places, dwells?
[[variant readings: (1) pōrttavaṉ (2) kaṅkil āka]]
{5:53}__10+
{$} pūṇ nāṇ āram porunta uṭaiyavar
the Lord who has ornaments, waist-cord and garlands suited to him.
nāṇ nāka(v) varai villiṭai ampiṉāl pēṇār mummatil eytavaṉ vīraṭṭam kāṇēṉācil eṉ kaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
will my eyes get sleep unless I see Vīraṭṭam of Civaṉ who discharged an arrow on the three forts of the enemies using the Meru mountain as a bow and Vācuki as the bow-string?
{5:53}__11+
{$} varai ārnta vayirattiraḷ māṇikkam; tirai ārnta puṉalpāy keṭilakkarai viraiyār (1) nīṟṟaṉ viḷaṅkiya vīraṭṭam, uraiyēṉākil eṉ kaṇ tuyil koḷḷumē
will my eyes get sleep unless I speak about the shining viraṭṭam of Civaṉ who smears fragrant sacred ash which is on the bank of Keṭilam whose water flows with waves which has heaps of diamonds and rubis which are in plenty in the mountain.
[[Variant reading: (1) nīṟṟiṉaṉ]]
{5:53}__12+
{$} ulantār veṇtalai uṇ kalaṉ ākavē
using the white skull of people who died, as eating vessel.
valam tāṉ (1) mikka avvāḷ arakkaṉtaṉaic cilampu ār cēvaṭi ūṉṟiṉāṉ vīraṭṭam pulampēṉākil
unless I utter repeatedly vīraṭṭam where Civaṉ who fixed firmly his foot in which the anklet sounds, on that cruel arakkaṉ who had excessive strenght.
[[Variant reading: (1) mikkavaṉ vāḷarakkaṉtaṉai]]

{1:26}__1+
{$} veṅkaḷ vimmu veṟiār poḻilcōlai tiṅkaḷōṭu tiḷaikkum tirupputtūrk kaṅkai taṅkum muṭiyār(avar) (pōlum)
Civan on whose head Kaṅki stays and who dwells in Tirup Puttūr where the large gardens which have good fragrance and have increasing honey desired by all, always plays with the moon.
eṅkaḷ ucci uṟaiyum iṟaiyār (ē)
is the supreme god who stays on our heads.
{1:26}__2+
{$} vēṉal vimmu veṟiār poḻilcōlait tēṉum vaṇṭum tiḷaikkum tirupputtūr ūṉamiṉṟi uṟaivār (avar) (pōlum)
the god, Civaṉ who dwells without any defect in tirupputtūr where tēṉ and vaṇṭu are alwaysin the large gardens which have good fragrance which increases in summer.
ēṉamuḷḷum eyiṟum puṉaivār (ē)
adorns himself with sharp bones of the hog and its tusk.
{1:26}__3+
{$} pāṅku nalla varivaṇṭu icaipāṭat tēmkoḷ koṉṟai tiḷaikkum tirupputtūr ōṅku kōyil uṟaivār (avar) (pōlum)
Civaṉ who dwells in the eminent temple in tirupputtūr where the koṉṟi trees which contin honey so that the good bees with lines on their body hum like music in the sides, are flourishing.
tāṅku tiṅkaḷ tavaḻpuṉcaṭaiyār(ē)
has a ruddy caṭi on which the crescent he bears crawls.
{1:26}__4+
{$} nāṟa viṇṭa naṟumāmalar kavvi, tēṟal vaṇṭu tiḷaikkum tirupputtūr, ūṟu ala vāḻkkai uṭaiyār (avar) (pōlum)
Civaṉ who leads a life having amassed property in tirupputtūr where the bees are always drinking the honey seizing it with eargerness in the big and good flowers which unfold their petals to spread fragrance.
ēṟukoṇṭa koṭi em iṟaiyār (ē)
is our supreme god who has on his flag the form of a bull.
{1:26}__5+
{$} icaiviḷaṅkum eḻil cūḻntu aḻakākat ticai viḷaṅkum poḻil cūḻ tirupputtūrp pacai viḷaṅkap paṭintār(avar)(pōlum)
Civaṉ who moves with his votaries, their devotion to become prominentin tirupputtūr which is surrounded by gardens which are famous in all directions and which is beautiful as fame which is eminent surrounds it with loftiness.
vacai viḷaṅkum (1) vaṭicēr nutalār (ē)
has a forehead which is perfect according to rules of beauty on which there is an eminent lady.
[[vacai is a rare word]]
[[Variant reading: (1) maṭi]]
{1:26}__6+
{$} viraiyōṭu veṇṇiṟatta avar unti
pushing fragrant white coloured flowers.
teṇṇiṟatta puṉalpāy tirupputtūr oḷ niṟatta oḷiyār (avar)(pōlum)
Civaṉ who is a very bright light in tirupputtūr where water of clear colour flows.
veḷniṟatta viṭaicēr koṭiyār (ē)
has a flag on which there is the form of a bull of white colour drawn.
{1:26}__7+
{$} neytal āmpal kaḻunīr malarntu eṅkum ceykaḷ malkum civaṉār tirupputtūrt taiyalpākam makiḻntār(avar)(pōlum)
god who felt pleasure in having on one half a young lady in tirupputtūr, belonging to Civaṉ where blue nelunbo flowers, white indian water-lily and purple indian water lily flowers have blossomed everywhere in the fields and are flourishing
mai uṇ nañcam maruvum miṭaṟṟār (ē)
is one who has a neck where the black poison is staying.
{1:26}__8+
{$} karukkam ellām kamaḻum poḻilcōlait tirukkoḷcemmai viḻavuār tirupputtūr irukkavalla iṟaivar(avar) (pōlum)
the supreme god who is capable of remining in tirupputtūr where eminent festivals with wealth are conducted and which has big gardens which lend fragrance when rin-clouds come into contact with them.
arakkaṉ olka viralāl aṭarttār (ē)
pressed down by his toe the arakkaṉ to be reduced in strength.
{1:26}__9+
{$} maruvi eṅkum vaḷarum maṭamañcai teruvutōṟum tiḷaikkum tirupputtūr perukivāḻum perumāṉ (avaṉ)(pōlum)
the god who dwells with increasing fame in tirupputtūr where the young peacocks which rejoice everywhere having become accustomed to living in that place, are permanently staying in every street.
paramaṉ(um) mālum aṟiyāp periyōṉ(ē)
is the great god whom Piramaṉ and Māl could not know.
{1:26}__10+
{$} kūṟaipōrkkum toḻilār amaṇkūṟal tēṟal vēṇṭā
(People of this world!) Do not accept as truth what the buddhists who cover their bodies and amanar, talk.
teḷimiṉ
be clear about this.
tirupputtūr āṟum nāṉkum amarnatār (avar) (pōlum)ēṟukoṇṭa koṭiem iṟaiyār(ē)
Civaṉ who is in the six aṅkams and the four vetams and is in tirupputtūr is the supreme god who has a flag on which the form of a bull is drawn.
{1:26}__11+
{$} nalla kēḷvi ñāṉacampantaṉ celvar cēṭar uṟaiyum tirupputtūrc collap pāṭal vallār tamakku eṉṉum allal tīrum
sufferings will come to an end to those who are able to recite the verses in prise of Tirupputtūr where the god who is the repositary of all wealth and who survives everything, dwells, done by Ñāṉacampantaṉ who had gined knowledge by listening to good words of wisdom
[[kēḷvi can be taken as Vētam as this word is a translation of curuti]]
avalam aṭaiyā(ē)
poverty of any kind will not reach them.

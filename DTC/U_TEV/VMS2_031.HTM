{2:31}__1+
{$} karuppaṟiyalūr the shrine in Karuppaṟiyalūr.
cuṟṟamoṭu paṟṟu avai tuyakku aṟa aṟuttu
Having cut completely the ties towards relations and attachments towards others.
kuṟṟam il kuṇaṅkaḷoṭu kūṭum aṭiyārkaḷ (maṟṟu) avarai
the devotees who are united with good qualities and have no faults.
vāṉavartam vāṉulakam (1) ēṟṟakkaṟṟavaṉ iruppatu
is the place of the god who has learnt naturally to lift them to occupy the celestial world of the Tēvar.
[[Variant reading: (1) ēṟa]]
{2:31}__2+
{$} vaṇṭu aṇaicey koṉṟaiyatu vārcaṭaikaḷ mēlē koṇṭu
having on the long caṭi koṉṟi flowers which bees approach (for honey)
kōṉ araviṉōṭum aṇaicey kōlam atu
God has a beauty which is due to the cobras capable of killing.
viṇṭu aṇaicey mummatilum vīḻtara ōr ampāl kaṇṭavaṉ iruppatu
the residence of Civaṉ who made all the three forts which were opposition to fall down (burnt).
karuppaṟiyalūr
is Karuppaṟiyalūr.
{2:31}__3+
{$} vētamoṭu vētiyarkaḷ vēḷvimutalāka
the vētiyar (brahmin) chanting Vētam and performing good acts beginning with sacrifices.
pōtiṉoṭu pōtu malar koṇṭu puṉaikiṉṟa nātaṉeṉa
to prise him as the master who is adorned with blossomed flowers and buds about to blossom, at the proper times.
naḷḷiruḷ muṉ āṭu kuḻaitāḻum kātavaṉ iruppatu
the place of the god who has in his ear an ear-ring which hangs low as he dances at midnight.
karuppaṟiyalūr
is Karuppaṟiyalūr.
{2:31}__4+
{$} maṭampaṭu malaikku iṟaivaṉ maṅkai orupaṅkaṉ
Civaṉ who has as a half, the beautiful dughter of the King of mountins.
uṭampiṉai viṭakkaruti niṉṟa maṟaiyōṉait toṭarntu aṇavu kālaṉ uyir kālāl kaṭantavaṉ iruppatu
the place where Civaṉ who kicked with his leg to destroy the life of Kālaṉ (god of death) who approached the brahmin (Mārkantēyaṉ), following him who was thinking of leaving this body.
karuppaṟiyalūr
is Karuppaṟiyalūr.
{2:31}__5+
{$} orutti umaiyōṭum orupākam (atu) āya niruttaṉ avaṉ
He is the dancer who has as half Umi who is unequalled (in beauty)
nīti avaṉ
he is the right moral conduct
nīttaṉ
the permanent one.
neṟiyāya viruttaṉ avaṉ
he is the elder one who is the right path.
vētam eṉa aṅkam avai ōtum karuttavaṉ iruppatu
the place where the god who chants the Vētam-s and six Aṅkam-s and who is in the minds of all.
karuppaṟiyalūr
is Karuppaṟiyalūr.
{2:31}__6+
{$} viṇṇavarkaḷ aracu
Civaṉ, the king of the celestials.
erikālum kaṇṇavaṉ iruppatu karuppaṟiyalūr
Karuppaṟiyalūr is the place of Civaṉ from whose eyes fire flowed.
veṟpu aracu peṟṟa makaḷ, meyttēṉ, paṇ amarum meṉ moḻiyiṉāḷai aṇaivippāṉ eṇṇivarukāmaṉ uṭal vēva
to burn the body of Kāmaṉ (Cupid) who came with the idea of making the dughter of the King of the mountin, the lady who spoke soft and sweet words like melodies and pure honey unite (with Civaṉ)
{2:31}__7+
{$} āti aṭiyaippaṇiya appoṭumalarccēr cōtioḷi naṟpukai vaḷarkkuvaṭupukku
when Mārkaṇteyaṉ entered into the tall artificial hillock to worship the feet of Civaṉ, the origin of all things, with water, flowers, bright lamp, and fragrant incense.
tītu ceya vantu aṇaiyum antakaṉ araṅkakkātiṉaṉ iruppatu
the residence of god who killed Antakaṉ who approached (Mārkanṭēyaṉ) to take away his life, to be destroyed.
karuppaṟiyalūr
is Karuppaṟiyalūr.
{2:31}__8+
{$} pukaḻvāyntaviṇṇavarum
Tēvar who possess fitting fame.
maṇṇavarum añca
and the people of this world to fear.
pāyntu amar ceyum toḻil ilaṅkai nakar vēntaṟku ēynta puyam attaṉaiyum iṟṟuviḻa
to make all the shoulders of the King of the city of Ilanki, who had the act of fighting by pouncing upon enemies, to fall down broken,
mēṉāḷ kāyntavaṉ iruppatu karuppaṟiyalūr
the place of the god who was angry with Irāvaṇaṉ is Karuppaṟuyalūr.
{2:31}__9+
{$} parantatu (1) nirantu varupāy tiraiya kaṅkai karantu ōr caṭaimēlmicai ukantu avaḷai vaittu
Civaṉ placed with joy the goddess of Kaṅki which descended with leaping waves and spreading and coming in a row, by concealing it on the top of a single caṭi.
nirantaram nirantu iruvar nēṭi aṟiyāmal karantavaṉ iruppatu karuppaṟiyalūrē
Karuppaṟiyalūr is the place where Civaṉ who kept himself out of sight always, when though two persons Māl and Piramaṉ searched for him and was not found out by them.
[[Variant reading: (1) nimirntu]]
{2:31}__10+
{$} coṉṉa colaiviṭṭu
leaving the words spoken by
aṟam maṟaiyā amaṇar ātam ili puttar coṟṟam aṟiyātavar
the amaṇar who do not cover their private parts and buddhists who do not have any regard and do not know to speak proper words.
[[ātam regard;
ātam eytiniṉṟu añcalittu ēttiyē (Kantapurāṇam, tirukkalyāṇappaṭalam, 11)]]
kuṟṟam aṟiyāta perumāṉ
the Lord who is blind to the faults of his devotees
[[kuṟṟam poṟuttanātar is the name of Civaṉ in this shrine]]
kokuṭik kōyil kaṟṟeṉa iruppatu karuppaṟiyalūr
Karuppaṟiyalūr which has a temple called Kokuṭikkōyil' is the place where he stays permanently as learnt by him
[[Karuppaṟiyalūr is the name of the place and Kokuṭikkōyil is the name of the temple. This name of the temple is mentioned by Cuntarar in every verse of his decade on this shrine, which follows this ((in talamuṟi editions))]]
{2:31}__11+
{$} kalantavar karuppaṟiyal mēya kaṭavu(ḷ)ḷai nalam tarupuṉal pukali ñāṉacampantaṉ palam taru tamiḻkkiḷavi (1) pattum ivai kaṟṟu
Learning all these ten Tamiḻ songs which will be productive of good and which were composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ in Pukali which has abundant water, and confers good on people, on the god who resides in Karuppaṟiyalūr where devotees gather together.
valam tarum avarkkuviṉai vāṭal eḷitām
it is easy for those people who circumambulate the temple, to put an end to their birth.
[[Variant reading: (1) paṟṟum]]

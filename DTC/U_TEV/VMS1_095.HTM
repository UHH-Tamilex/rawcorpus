{1:95}__1+
{$} kāṭu pēṇiniṉṟu āṭum marutaṉ
Civaṉ, who is under the tree marutu (arjuna tree) and dances in the cremation ground desiring it.
tōṭu ōr kātiṉaṉ
wears a woman's ear-ring in one ear.
pāṭum maṟaiyiṉaṉ
chants vetam with music.
{1:95}__2+
{$} karutārpuram eyvar
Civaṉ will shoot an arrow on the cities of enemies.
erutē iṉitu ūrvar
will ride on a bull with joy.
marutē iṭam ākum
arjuna tree is his place.
virutu āmviṉai tīrppu
destruction of Karmams of devotees is his badge of victory.
{1:95}__3+
{$} eṇṇum aṭiyārkaḷ
devotees who meditate upon.
aṇṇal marutaraip paṇṇiṉ moḻicolla
and if they prise with words of music eminent marutar.
viṇṇum tamatu ām
even the heaven will become theirs.
{1:95}__4+
{$} viriār caṭaimēṉi eriār marutaraittariyātu ēttuvār
those who prise Civaṉ under the arjuna tree, who has a spreading caṭi and a body like fire, without stopping even for a minute.
ulakilē periyār
are great men in this world.
{1:95}__5+
{$} panta viṭai ēṟum entai marutaraic cintai ceypavar punti nallarē
those who think of our father, marutar who rides upon a bull made ready for riding, have good minds.
{1:95}__6+
{$} kaḻalum cilampu(um) ārkkum eḻilār marutarait toḻalē pēṇuvārkku aḻalumviṉai pōm
the afflicting Karmams will leave those who desire worshipping the highly beautiful marutar who has on one leg a warrior's armour and on the other leg an anklet both of which make a big sound.
{1:95}__7+
{$} piṟai ār caṭai aṇṇal
the eminent one who has a crescent on his caṭi.
maṟaiār marutarai
Civaṉ under the marutu tree who revealed the vētams.
niṟaiyāl niṉaipavar
those who think of him by concentration.
iṉpam kuṟaiyār
will not be deficient in happiness.
{1:95}__8+
{$} eṭuttāṉ puyam taṉṉai aṭuttār marutarai toṭuttu ār malar cūṭṭa
to adorn Civaṉ under the marutu tree who pressed down the shoulders of the (arakkaṉ) who lifted (the mountin Kayili), with garlands knitted with flowers.
viṭuttār vēṭkaiyē
gave up all kinds of attachments.
{1:95}__9+
{$} iruvarkku eriāya uruva marutaraip paravi ēttuvār
those who prise in the 2nd person and the 3rd person marutar who had the form of fire not to be found by the two persons (Māl and Piramaṉ)
maruvivāḻvārē
will live getting all good things.
{1:95}__10+
{$} niṉṟu uṇcamaṇ tērar eṉṟum marutaraiaṉṟi uraicolla naṉṟumoḻiyārē
the camaṇar who eat their food standing and tēvar (buddhists) speak always inimical words about marutar; do not speak good words.
{1:95}__11+
{$} marutar aṭipāṭik karutu campantaṉ tamiḻperitum collap porutaviṉaipōmē
the Karmams which were inimical to us will leave us if we recite often the Tamil verses of Campantaṉ who fixes his thoughts on Marutar singing the glory of his feet.

• patikam: [3:24]
  • talam: tiruk kaḻumalam (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: kolli
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.391

*1
maṇṇiṉ nalla vaṇṇam vāḻal _ām, vikalum;
_eṇṇiṉ nalla katikku yātum _ōr kuṟivu _ili---
kaṇṇiṉ nalla(ḵ)tu _uṟum kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
peṇṇiṉnallāḷoṭum peruntaki _iruntatē!

*2
pōti _ār poṉ kiṇṇattu _aṭicil pollātu _eṉat
tātiyār muṉivu_uṟa, tāṉ _eṉi _āṇṭavaṉ;
kāti _ār kuḻiyiṉaṉ; kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
pētiyāḷ_avaḷoṭum peruntaki _iruntatē!

*3
toṇṭu _aṇicey toḻil-tuyar _aṟuttu _uyyal _ām---
vaṇṭu _aṇi koṉṟiyāṉ, matumalarc caṭimuṭi;
kaṇ tuṇi neṟṟiyāṉ; kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
peṇ tuṇi_āka _ōr peruntaki _iruntatē!

*4
_ayarvu _uḷōm! _eṉṟu nī _acivu _oḻi, neñcamē!---
niyar vaḷi muṉkiyāḷ nēriḻi_avaḷoṭum,
kayal vayal kutikoḷum kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
peyarpala tuticeya, peruntaki _iruntatē!

*5
_aṭivu _ilōm _eṉṟu nī _ayarvu _oḻi, neñcamē!---
viṭi _amar koṭiyiṉāṉ, viṇṇavar toḻutu _eḻum,
kaṭi _uyar māṭam _ār kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
peṭinaṭi_avaḷoṭum peruntaki _iruntatē!

*6
maṟṟu _oru paṟṟu _ili, neñcamē!---maṟipala
kaṟṟa nal vētiyar kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
ciṟṟiṭip pēr_alkul tiruntiḻi_avaḷoṭum
peṟṟu _eṉi _āḷ_uṭip peruntaki _iruntatē!

*7
kuṟi vaḷivatu moḻi kuṟivu _oḻi, neñcamē!---
niṟi vaḷi muṉkiyāḷ nēriḻi_avaḷoṭum,
kaṟi vaḷar poḻil _aṇi kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
piṟi vaḷar caṭimuṭip peruntaki _iruntatē!

*8
_arakkaṉār---_aru vari _eṭuttavaṉ---_alaṟiṭa,
nerukkiṉār, viraliṉāl; nīṭu yāḻ pāṭavē,
karukku vāḷ _aruḷceytāṉ; kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
perukkum nīravaḷoṭum peruntaki _iruntatē!

*9
neṭiyavaṉ, piramaṉum, niṉippu _aritu_āy, _avar
_aṭiyoṭu muṭi _aṟiyā _aḻal_uruviṉaṉ;
kaṭi kamaḻ poḻil _aṇi kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
piṭinaṭi_avaḷoṭum peruntaki _iruntatē!

*10
tār _uṟu taṭṭu _uṭic camaṇar cākkiyarkaḷtam
_ār _uṟu col kaḷintu, _aṭi_iṇi _aṭintu _uymmiṉ!---
kār _uṟu poḻil vaḷar kaḻumala vaḷa nakar,
pēr_aṟattāḷoṭum peruntaki _iruntatē!

*11
karun taṭan tēṉ malku kaḻumala vaḷa nakarp
peruntaṭaṅkoṅkiyoṭu _irunta _empirāṉtaṉi
_aruntamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉa centamiḻ
virumpuvār_avarkaḷ, pōy, viṇṇulaku _āḷvarē.

{6:49}__1+
{$} mākaṭalcūḻ kōkaraṇam maṉṉiṉaṉē
Civaṉ who remains permanently in Kōkaraṇam surrounded by the big sea.
[[Kōkaraṇam: this shrine is mentioned by Nāvukkaracar in his tiru aṅkamālai as kōlak kōpurak kōkaraṇam. As the linkam in the sanctum is like the ear of a cow it got the name of Kōkarnam; this has become in Tamiḻ as Kōkaraṇam]]
cantiraṉum taṇpuṉalum cantittāṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ made the crescent and the cool water to be united together.
tāḻ caṭaiyāṉ (kāṇ)
has a hanging caṭai.
cārntārkaṭku amutu āṉāṉ (kāṇ)
is the nectar for those who approached him.
antarattil acurar puram mūṉṟu aṭṭāṉ kāṇ
destroyed the three cities of the acurar which were wandering in the sky.
avvuruvil avvuruvam āyiṉāṉ (kāṇ)
he dwells in those forms suited to those form.
pantarattu nāṉ maṟaikaḷ pāṭiṉāṉ (kāṇ)
gave out the four vētams which are store-houses of knowledge
palapalavum pāṇi payilkiṉṟāṉ (kāṇ)
is practising many kinds of dances.
mantirattu(1) maṟaiporuḷum āyiṉāṉ (kāṇ)
is also the hidden meaning of the mantiram
[[All the kāṇ in this as well as the following verses are expletives]]

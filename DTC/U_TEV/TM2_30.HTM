• patikam: [2:30]
  • talam: tirup puṟampayam
  • paṇ: _intaḷam
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.4, p.81

*1
maṟam payam malintavar matil paricu _aṟuttaṉi;
niṟam pacumi cemmiyoṭu _icintu, _uṉatu nīrmi
tiṟam payaṉ _uṟum poruḷ terintu _uṇarum nālvarkku
_aṟampayaṉ _urittaṉi---puṟampayam _amarntōy!

*2
virittaṉi, tiruccaṭi; _arittu _oḻuku veḷḷam
tarittaṉi; _atu _aṉṟiyum, mikap periya kālaṉ
_eruttu _iṟa _utittaṉi; _ilaṅkiḻi _orpākam
poruttutal karuttiṉi---puṟampayam _amarntōy!

*3
virintaṉi; kuvintaṉi; viḻuṅku _uyir _umiḻntaṉi;
tirintaṉi; kuruntu _oci peruntakiyum nīyum
pirintaṉi; puṇarntaṉi; piṇam puku mayāṉam
purintaṉi; makiḻntaṉi---puṟampayam _amarntōy!

*4
vaḷam keḻu kaṭumpuṉaloṭum caṭi _oṭuṅka,
tuḷaṅku _amar _iḷampiṟi cumantatu viḷaṅka,
_uḷam koḷa _aḷintavar cuṭum cuṭali nīṟu
puḷam koḷa viḷaṅkiṉi---puṟampayam _amarntōy!

*5
perum piṇi piṟappiṉoṭu _iṟappu _ili; _orpākam,
karumpoṭupaṭumcoliṉmaṭantiyi makiḻntōy;
curumpu _uṇa _arumpu _aviḻ tirunti _eḻu koṉṟi
virumpiṉi---puṟampayam _amarnta _iṟiyōṉē!

*6
_aṉal paṭu taṭakkiyavar, _et toḻilarēṉum,
niṉippu _uṭi maṉattavar viṉippakiyum nīyē;
taṉal paṭu cuṭarac caṭi taṉip piṟiyoṭu _oṉṟap
puṉal paṭu kiṭakkiyi---puṟampayam _amarntōy!

*7
maṟattuṟi maṟuttavar, tavattu _aṭiyar, _uḷḷam
_aṟattuṟi _oṟuttu _uṉatu _aruḷkiḻami peṟṟōr,
tiṟattu _uḷa tiṟattiṉi matittu _akala niṉṟum,
puṟattu _uḷa tiṟattiṉi---puṟampayam _amarntōy!

*8
_ilaṅkiyar _iṟiñcu _iṟi, vilaṅkalil muḻaṅka
_ulam keḻu taṭakkikaḷ _aṭarttiṭalum, _añci,
valamkoḷa _eḻuntavaṉ nalam kaviṉa, _añcu-
pulaṅkaḷi vilaṅkiṉi---puṟampayam _amarntōy!

*9
vaṭam keṭa nuṭaṅkuṇa _iṭanta _iṭi _allik
kiṭantavaṉ, _iruntavaṉ, _aḷantu _uṇaral_ākār
toṭarntavar, _uṭampoṭu nimirntu, _uṭaṉvaṇaṅka,
puḷ taṅku _aruḷceytu _oṉṟiṉi---puṟampayam _amarntōy!

*10
viṭaṅku _oruvar naṉṟu _eṉa, viṭakku _oruvar tītu _eṉa,
_uṭaṟku _uṭi kaḷintavar, _uṭampiṉi maṟikkum
paṭakkarkaḷ, piṭakku_uri paṭuttu, _umi _orpākam
_aṭakkiṉi---puṟampayam _amarnta _uravōṉē!

*11
karuṅkaḻi porum tiri karik kulavu muttam
tarum kaḻumalattu _iṟi---tamiḻk kiḻami ñāṉaṉ---
curumpu _aviḻ puṟampayam _amarnta tamiḻ vallār,
perum piṇi maruṅku _aṟa, _oruṅkuvar, piṟappē.

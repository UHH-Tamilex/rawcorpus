{1:125}__1+
{$} kalaimali akal alkul arivai taṉ uruviṉaṉ
Civaṉ has on his body a lady wearing on her broad waist a spreading garment.
mulai malitaru tiruuruvam atu uṭaiyavaṉ
and has a holy body in which the breast of Umi is prominent.
cilaimali matilpoti civapura nakartoḻa, ilai naliviṉai
if one worships the city of civapuram which is concealed by a fortified wall built with granite stones there will be no Karmams that afflict him.
irumaiyum iṭar keṭum
the sufferings in this birth and future births will be destroyed.
{1:125}__2+
{$} paṭar oḷi caṭaiyiṉaṉ; viṭaiyiṉaṉ
Civaṉ has a caṭi whose brightness spreads; has a bull.
matil (avai) cuṭar eri koḷuviya civaṉ (avaṉ) uṟaipati
the abode of Civaṉ who set burning fire to the forts.
tiṭal iṭu puṉal vayal civapuram aṭaiya
if we reach civapuram which has fields full of water which creates small islands.
namiṭarkeṭum
our sufferings will be destroyed.
uyarkatipeṟuvatu tiṭaṉē
it is definite that we will obtin higher state of existence.
{1:125}__3+
{$} varai tiritara, aravu akaṭu aḻal eḻa
the mountin which was used as churning staff revolved and the body of the serpent which was wound round it as a churning cord became heated.
varunuraitaru kaṭalviṭam nukarpavaṉ eḻiltikaḻ, tiraiporu puṉal aricil (atu) aṭai civapuram, uraitarum aṭiyavar
the devotees who speak about civapuram which is reached by the river, aricil of plenty of water whose beautiful waves erode the banks; which is the abode of Civaṉ who consumed the poison of the ocean which has rising foams.
uyarkatiyiṉarē
will definitely attin higher states of existence.
{1:125}__4+
{$} (1) taṇivu uṭaiyavarpayil civapuram maruviya maṇimiṭaṟaṉatu aṭiiṇai toḻumavar
those who worship the feet of Civaṉ who has a blue neck and who dwells in civapuram where devotees who have controlled passions are in large numbers.
tuṇivu uṭaiyavar
have strength of mind.
cuṭupoṭiyiṉar
smear their bodies with well-burnt holy ash.
uṭal aṭupiṇi aṭaivu ilar
do not get diseases which afflict the bodies.
piṟaviyum aṟa viciṟuvar
will cast away even future births.
[[Variant reading: (1) tiṇivuṭaiyavar]]
{1:125}__5+
{$} emai uṭaiyavaṉ
Civaṉ who has us as his protege.
maṟaiyavaṉ
revealed the vētams (is the brahmin among gods)
matiyavaṉ
is the pure intellect.
malaiyavaṉ
lives in the mountin, Kayili.
nilaiyavaṉ
does not know destruction.
niṟaiyavaṉ
has nothing to wish for.
umaiyavaḷ makiḻnaṭam navilpavaṉ
practises dance which is enjoyed by Umayavaḷ.
iṟaiyavaṉ
is the chief.
imaiyavar paṇikoṭu
accepting the services of the immortals who do not wink.
civapuram uṟaivu eṉa uṭaiyavaṉ
has civapuram as his abode.
{1:125}__6+
{$} mutircaṭai iḷamatinatipuṉal pativuceytu
having firmly fixed in the caṭi of mature growth, a crescent and a river of plenty of water.
atirkaḻal oliceya arunaṭam (1) navilpavaṉ
practise rare dance ceaselessly to cuse the resounding Kaḻal to make a sound.
etirpavar puram eyta iṇaiili aṇaipati
the city of the unequalled Civaṉ who discharged an arrow, on the cities of enemies.
catirpeṟum uṭaiyavar civapuramē
is civapuram where people of noble minds live.
[[Variant reading: (1) navilvōṉ]]
{1:125}__7+
{$} vaṭivuṭaimalaimakaḷ calamakaḷ uṭaṉ amar, poṭipaṭum (1) uḻai ataḷ politiru uruviṉaṉ
Civaṉ has a holy body in which the deer's skin on which sacred ash falls, and where the dughter of the mountin, of beautiful form, and the lady in the form of water (Kaṅki) dwell.
ceṭipaṭupalitiri civaṉ uṟai civapuram aṭaitarum aṭiyavar aruviṉai ilar
the devotees who reach civapuram where Civaṉ who wanders to receive alms of offensive odour will have no irresistible karmam-s.
[[Variant reading: (1) iḻai]]
{1:125}__8+
{$} karam irupatum muṭi orupatum uṭaiyavaṉ uram neritara varai aṭarvu ceytavaṉ uṟaiparaṉ eṉa
thinking that the supreme god who pervades everywhere and who pressed under the mountin to crush the chest of Irāvaṇaṉ who had twenty hands and ten heads.
aṭiyavar paṇi tiru civapura nakar (atu) pukutal nam uyarkati (atuvē)
entering into the city of civapuram to which the devotees pay obeisance is itself a higher stage of existence.
{1:125}__9+
{$} aṉṟu iyal uruvukoḷ ariayaṉ eṉum avar ceṉṟu aḷaviṭal ariyavaṉ uṟai cirapuram eṉṟu
thinking that civapuram is the place where Civaṉ who could not be searched by flying in the sky and digging into the earth by ari and Ayaṉ who assumed the form of a pig and a swan, out of anger.
irupoḻutum muṉvaḻipaṭum avar tuyar oṉṟu ilar
those who worship day and night standing in the presence of Civaṉ will not have even the slightest suffering.
pukaḻoṭum ivvulaku uṭaiyar
will attin fame along with this world.
{1:125}__10+
{$} puttaroṭu amaṇarkaḷ aṟavurai puṟavurai
the moral teachings of buddhists and amaṇar are outside the range of morality.
vittakam oḻikila
and they do not leave off cunning.
viṭaiuṭai aṭikaḷtam ittavam muyalvuṟil iṟaivaṉa civapuram meyttaka vaḻipaṭal viḻumiyakuṇamē
if one wants to perform this penance fixing thoughts of Civaṉ who has a bull, worshipping his civapuram sincerely, will do him excellent good.
{1:125}__11+
{$} puntiyar maṟai navil pukalimaṉ ñāṉacampantaṉa tamiḻkoṭu
with the aid of Tamiḻ verses composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ, chief of Pukali where people of noble minds recite the Vētam-s often.
civapura nakar uṟai entaiyai uraiceyta icaimoḻipavar viṉaicintimuṉ uṟa uyarkati peṟuvarkaḷ
those who recite with music, the verses prising our father who dwells in the city of Civapuram, will first of all destroy all Karmams and attin higher stage of existence.
[[peṟuvarkaḷ: kaḷ is superfluous]]

{4:50}__1+
{$} neṭiya māl piramaṉōṭu nīr eṉum pilayam koḷḷa aṭiyoṭu muṭiyum kāṇār
The tall Māl and Piramaṉ could not see the feet and head as water gushed out as at the time of the destruction of the world, when he went digging into the nether world
[[Māl could not see the feet; Piramaṉ could not see the head]]
tuṭiyuṭai vēṭarāki, tūya mantiraṅkaḷ colli aruccuṉaṟku ampumvillum koṭineṭuntēr koṭuttār kuṟukkai vīraṭṭaṉār
the god in Kuṟukkai Vīraṭṭam disguising himself as a hunter having a small drum tapering in the middle, taught many sacred mantirams and gave Aruccuṉaṉ arrows, bow and a tall chariot adorned with flags.
{4:50}__2+
{$} āttam ām ayaṉum mālum aṉṟi
except Ayaṉ and Māl who had intimacy with him.
oḻintatēvar
the remaining Tēvar
emperumāṉ! cōttam eṉṟu toḻutu tōttiraṅkaḷ colla
worshipped with joined hands and were singing his praises, saying our Lord! accept the salutations from us who are inferior to you!
tīrttamām aṭṭamīmuṉ cīruṭai ēḻu nāḷum
on all the famous seven days before the eighth day when the idol bathed in a river or tank.
[[aṭṭami has become aṭṭamī to suit metre]]
kuṟukkai vīraṭṭaṉār kūttarāy vīti pōntār
the Lord in Kuṟukkai Vīraṭṭam came in procession in the streets in the form of a dancer.

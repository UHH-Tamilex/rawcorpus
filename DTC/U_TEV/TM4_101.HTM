• patikam: [4:101]
  • talam: tiru _ārūr
  • (()): tiru viruttam
  • VMS: Vol.9, p.58

*1
kulam palam pāvaru kuṇṭarmuṉṉē namakku _uṇṭukolō---
_alampu _alampā varu taṇpuṉal _ārūr _avircaṭaiyāṉ,
cilampu _alampā varu cēvaṭiyāṉ, tiru mūlaṭṭāṉam
pulampu _alampā varu toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*2
maṟṟu _iṭam _iṉṟi maṉai tuṟantu _al _uṇā val _amaṇar
col-tiṭam _eṉṟu turicupaṭṭēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
vil-tiṭam vāṅki, vicayaṉoṭu _aṉṟu _oru vēṭuvaṉāy,
puṟṟu _iṭamkoṇṭāṉtaṉ toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*3
_oru vaṭivu _iṉṟi niṉṟu _uṇ kuṇṭarmuṉ namakku _uṇṭukolō---
ceru vaṭi veñcilaiyāl puram _aṭṭavaṉ, ceṉṟu _aṭaiyāt
tiru _uṭaiyāṉ, tiru _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, ceṅkaṇ
poru viṭaiyāṉ, _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*4
māciṉai _ēṟiya mēṉiyar, vaṉkaṇṇar, moṇṇarai viṭṭu
_īcaṉaiyē niṉaintu _ēcaṟuvēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
tēcaṉai, _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉai, cintaiceytu
pūcaṉaip pūcurartoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*5
_aruntumpoḻutu _uraiyāṭā _amaṇar tiṟam _akaṉṟu,
varunti niṉaintu, _araṉē! _eṉṟu vāḻttuvēṟku _uṇṭukolō---
tiruntiya mā matil _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉukkup
poruntum tavam _uṭait toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar_ām puṇṇiyamē?

*6
vīṅkiya tōḷkaḷum tāḷkaḷum_āy niṉṟu, veṟṟu _araiyē
mūṅkaikaḷ pōl _uṇṇum mūṭarmuṉṉē namakku _uṇṭukolō---
tēm kamaḻ cōlait teṉ_ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, ceyya-
pūṅkaḻalāṉ, _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*7
paṇṇiya cāttirap pēykaḷ paṟi talaik kuṇṭarai viṭṭu
_eṇ _il pukaḻ _īcaṉtaṉ _aruḷ peṟṟēṟkum _uṇṭukolō---
tiṇṇiya mā matil _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, _eṅkaḷ
puṇṇiyaṉtaṉ _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*8
karapparkaḷ, meyyai; talai paṟikkac cukam _eṉṉum kuṇṭar
_uraippaṉa kēḷātu, _iṅku _uyyap pōntēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
tirup poli _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, tiruk kayilaip-
poruppaṉ, viruppu _amar toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*9
kaiyil _iṭu cōṟu niṉṟu _uṇṇum kātal _amaṇarai viṭṭu,
_uyyum neṟi kaṇṭu, _iṅku _uyyap pōntēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
_aiyaṉ, _aṇi vayal _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉukkup
poy _aṉpu _ilā _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*10
kuṟṟam _uṭaiya _amaṇar tiṟam_atu kai _akaṉṟiṭṭu,
_uṟṟa karumam ceytu, _uyyap pōntēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
mal poli tōḷāṉ, _irāvaṇaṉtaṉ vali vāṭṭuvitta
poṉkaḻalāṉ, _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

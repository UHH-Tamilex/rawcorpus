• patikam: [4:101]
  • talam: tiru _ārūr
  • (()): tiru viruttam
  • VMS: Vol.9, p.58

*1
kulam palam pāvaru kuṇṭarmuṉṉē namakku _uṇṭukolō---
_alampu _alampā varu taṇpuṉal _ārūr _avircaṭiyāṉ,
cilampu _alampā varu cēvaṭiyāṉ, tiru mūlaṭṭāṉam
pulampu _alampā varu toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*2
maṟṟu _iṭam _iṉṟi maṉi tuṟantu _al _uṇā val _amaṇar
col-tiṭam _eṉṟu turicupaṭṭēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
vil-tiṭam vāṅki, vicayaṉoṭu _aṉṟu _oru vēṭuvaṉāy,
puṟṟu _iṭamkoṇṭāṉtaṉ toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*3
_oru vaṭivu _iṉṟi niṉṟu _uṇ kuṇṭarmuṉ namakku _uṇṭukolō---
ceru vaṭi veñciliyāl puram _aṭṭavaṉ, ceṉṟu _aṭiyāt
tiru _uṭiyāṉ, tiru _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, ceṅkaṇ
poru viṭiyāṉ, _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*4
māciṉi _ēṟiya mēṉiyar, vaṉkaṇṇar, moṇṇari viṭṭu
_īcaṉiyē niṉintu _ēcaṟuvēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
tēcaṉi, _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉi, cinticeytu
pūcaṉip pūcurartoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*5
_aruntumpoḻutu _uriyāṭā _amaṇar tiṟam _akaṉṟu,
varunti niṉintu, _araṉē! _eṉṟu vāḻttuvēṟku _uṇṭukolō---
tiruntiya mā matil _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉukkup
poruntum tavam _uṭit toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar_ām puṇṇiyamē?

*6
vīṅkiya tōḷkaḷum tāḷkaḷum_āy niṉṟu, veṟṟu _ariyē
mūṅkikaḷ pōl _uṇṇum mūṭarmuṉṉē namakku _uṇṭukolō---
tēm kamaḻ cōlit teṉ_ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, ceyya-
pūṅkaḻalāṉ, _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*7
paṇṇiya cāttirap pēykaḷ paṟi talik kuṇṭari viṭṭu
_eṇ _il pukaḻ _īcaṉtaṉ _aruḷ peṟṟēṟkum _uṇṭukolō---
tiṇṇiya mā matil _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, _eṅkaḷ
puṇṇiyaṉtaṉ _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*8
karapparkaḷ, meyyi; tali paṟikkac cukam _eṉṉum kuṇṭar
_urippaṉa kēḷātu, _iṅku _uyyap pōntēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
tirup poli _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, tiruk kayilip-
poruppaṉ, viruppu _amar toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*9
kiyil _iṭu cōṟu niṉṟu _uṇṇum kātal _amaṇari viṭṭu,
_uyyum neṟi kaṇṭu, _iṅku _uyyap pōntēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
_aiyaṉ, _aṇi vayal _ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉukkup
poy _aṉpu _ilā _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

*10
kuṟṟam _uṭiya _amaṇar tiṟam_atu ki _akaṉṟiṭṭu,
_uṟṟa karumam ceytu, _uyyap pōntēṉukkum _uṇṭukolō---
mal poli tōḷāṉ, _irāvaṇaṉtaṉ vali vāṭṭuvitta
poṉkaḻalāṉ, _aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar _ām puṇṇiyamē?

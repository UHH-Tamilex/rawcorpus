{1:7}__1+
{$} pāṭakam mellaṭip pāvaiyōṭum paṭupiṇakkāṭu iṭam paṟṟiniṉṟu nāṭakam āṭum naḷḷāṟu uṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord who is in Naḷḷāṟu and dances in the cremation ground full of corpses, along with a lady wearing in her feet pāṭakam!
[[Pātakam ((is)) a kind of anklet worn by women]]
cūṭakamuṉkai maṭantaimārkaḷ tuṇaivaroṭum toḻutu ētti vāḻtta āṭakamāṭam neruṅku kūṭal ālavāyiṉkaṇ amarntavāṟu itu eṉ (kol); collāy
what is the reason for dwelling, desiring it, in Ālavāy in Kūṭal where golden mansions are close to each other, and where the ladies who wear bracelets to utter benedictions, by prising you and worshipping you with joined hands, along with their husbands? please tell me.

{1:7}__2+
{$} tiṅkaḷ ampōtum ceḻumpuṉalum ceñcaṭaimāṭṭu ayal vaittu ukantu
feeling happy in having placed side by side on the red caṭi fertile water and the crescent which is like a bud in an advanced stage.
namkaṇ makiḻum naḷḷāṟu uṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord in naḷḷāṟu who rejoices in our company!
poṅkuiḷameṉ mulaiyārkaḷōṭum puṉamayil āṭa nilā muḷaikkum am(1)kaḷakaccutai māṭak kūṭal ālavāyiṉkaṇ amarntavāṟu itu eṉ (kol); collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in ālavāy in kūṭal which has beautifully plastered storeys from which white light like moonlight rise, when the peacocks which are found in forest tracts dance with ladies who have soft, young and swelling breasts?

{1:7}__3+
{$} (1) taṇ naṟu mattamum kūviḷamum veṇtalaimālaiyum tāṅki ārkkum, naṇṇal ariya naḷḷāṟu uṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord in naḷḷāṟu who cannot be easily approached by anybody, bearing on the head white skulls strung together, bel, and cool, fragrant datura flowers!
[[naṇṇal ariya qualifies namperumāṉ]]
puṇṇiyavāṇarum mātavarum pukuntu uṭaṉ ēttap, puṉaiyiḻaiyār, aṇṇal iṉpāṭal eṭukkum kūṭal ālavāyiṉkaṇ amarntavāṟu itu eṉ (kol) collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in ālavāy in Kūṭal where people who have virtuous acts to their credit, and ascetics who have done severe penance enter into the temple and are prising together, when the ladies who adorn themselves with ornaments begin to sing songs of prise about you? please tell me.
[[Variant reading: (1) taṇuṟu]]
{1:7}__4+
{$} pūviṉil vācam, puṉalil poṟpu, putuviraiccāntiṉil nāṟṟattōṭu nāviṉil pāṭal naḷḷāṟu uṭaiyanamperumāṉ
our Lord in naḷḷāṟu who is the songs in the mouth, sweet scent in the new and fragrant sandal and fragrance in flowers!
[[PP_EXT: tīyiṉuḷ teṟal nī pūviṉuḷ nāṟṟam nī
(Paripāṭal, 3-63)]]
tēvarkaḷ, tāṉavar, cittar, viccātarar kaṇattōṭum ciṟantupoṅki, āviṉil aintu ukantu āṭṭuṅkūṭal ālavāyiṉ kaṇ amarntavāṟu itu eṉ (kol) collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in Ālavāy in Kūṭal where the tēvar, tāṉavar, ciṭṭar and viccātarar become highly elated and bathe you with the five products of the cow? please tell me.
{1:7}__5+
{$} cempoṉ cey mālaiyum vācikaiyum tiruntu pukaiyum aviyum pāṭṭum nampum perumai naḷḷāṟu uṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord who dwells in Naḷḷāṟu and who has the greatness of desiring songs, offerings made in sacrificial fire and fragrant incense, a kind of garland worn on the head and a garland of fine gold worn on the chest!
umparum nākar ulakam tāṉum olikaṭal cūḻnta ulakattōrum amputanālkaḷāl nīṭum kūṭal ālavāyiṉkaṇ amarntavāṟu itueṉ(kol) collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in ālavāy in Kūtal which become famous by four clouds joining together, and which is worshipped by the residents of the upper world, the serpent world and people of this world surrounded by the roaring sea? please tell me.
[[nālkaḷ: kaḷ plural suffix: this is a rare usage.]]
{1:7}__6+
{$} pākamum tēviyai vaittukkoṇṭu
placing the tēvi (Umi) on one half.
paivirituttippariya pēḻvāy nākamum pūṇṭa naḷḷāṟu uṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord who is in Naḷḷāṟu and who adorn yourself with a big cobra of large mouth and spots spreading in its hood!
pōkamum niṉṉai maṉattu vaittup puṇṇiyar naṇṇum puṇarvu pūṇṭa ākamuṭaiyavar cērum kūṭal ālavāyiṉ kaṇ amarntavāṟu itu eṉ(kol) collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in Ālavāy in Kūṭal where people who have the mind to be in contact with you, and virtuous people who enjoy all pleasures by meditating on you? please tell me.
{1:7}__7+
{$} kōvaṇa āṭaiyum nīṟup pūccum koṭu maḻu ēntalum ceñcaṭaiyum nāvaṇappāṭṭum naḷḷāṟuṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord in naḷḷāṟu who has songs of several rhythmic movements, red caṭi holding a cruel battle-axe and wearing a loin-cloth.
[[nīṟṟup pūccu has become nīṟuppūccu for the rythm.]]
pūvaṇam mēṉi iḷaiyamātar poṉṉum maṇiyum koḻittu eṭuttu āvaṇavītiyil āṭum kūṭal ālavāyaṉkaṇ amarntavāṟu itueṉ (kol) collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in Ālavāy in Kūṭal where young ladies who have bodies as tender as flowers take by sifting in a winnowing fan gold and precious stones, and play in the bazaar street? please tell me.
{1:7}__8+
{$} ilaṅkai irāvaṇaṉ veṟpu eṭukka
when Irāvaṇaṉ of ilaṅki lifted the mountin (Kayili)
eḻilviral ūṉṟi
pressing him with the beautiful toe.
icaivirumpi
hearing his music with desire.
nalamkoḷac cērnta naḷḷāṟu uṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord who is in naḷḷāṟu who granted him many good things!
pulaṉkaḷaic ceṟṟup poṟiyainīkki
destroying sensation of the five senses and removing the mischief of the five senses.
puntiyilum niṉaiccintai ceyyum alaṅkal nallārkaḷ amarum kūṭal ālavāyiṉkaṇ amarntavāṟu itu eṉ (kol) collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in Ālavāy where devotees wearing garlands of victory, who meditate also in their minds on you, dwell? please tell me.
{1:7}__9+
{$} paṇiyuṭaimālum malariṉōṉum paṉṟiyum veṉṟip paṟavaiyāyum naṇukal ariya naḷḷāṟu uṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord in Naḷḷāṟu, who is difficult to be approached by Māl who has a serpent-bed, and Piramaṉ seated in the lotus who climed a false victory saying I have seen the head by assuming the form of a swan!
maṇi oli caṅku oliyōṭum maṟṟai māmuraciṉ oli eṉṟum ōvātu, aṇikiḷar vēntar pukutum kūṭal ālavāyiṉkaṇ amarntavāṟu itu eṉ (kol)? collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in Ālavāy in Kūṭal into which Kings adorned with ornaments enter with the sound of bells, the sound of conches and the sound of big drums (muracu) which never cease? Please tell me.
{1:7}__10+
{$} taṭukku uṭaikkaiyarum cākkiyarumcātiyiṉ nīṅki avattavattar, naṭukkuṟa niṉṟa naḷḷāṟu uṭaiya namperumāṉ
our Lord who dwells in Naḷḷāṟu, who is the cuse for the camaṇar who carry mats between their arms for sitting, and cākkiyar (buddhists) who do fruitless penance and who have given up their castes!
eṭukkum viḻavum naṉṉāḷ viḻavum irumpali (1) iṉpiṉōṭu etticaiyum aṭukkum perumaicēr māṭakkūṭal ālavāyiṉkaṇ amarntavāṟu itu eṉ (kol); collāy
what is the reason for dwelling in Ālavāy in Kūṭal having storeys, where festivals conducted on special occasions and dily festivals and worship and offering of food in all directions add greatness to the temple! please tell me.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṉpiṉōṭu]]
{1:7}__11+
{$} kūṭal ālavāymēviyatu eṉkol eṉṟu aṉpu�ṭuyāṉai araṉai, naṉpoṉai, nātaṉai, naḷḷāṟṟāṉai nayampeṟap pōṟṟi
prising with joy the god in naḷḷāṟu, who is the master, fine gold, araṉ, who has parental love towards all living beings, and questioning him what is the reason for dwelling in ālavāy in Kūṭal
nalam kulāvum, poṉpuṭai cūḻtarum māṭakkāḻip pūcuraṉ ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa iṉpuṭaip pāṭalkaḷ pattum vallār imaiyavar ētta iruppar (tām, ē)
those who are able to recite all the ten songs which give pleasure and were composed by the brahmin in Kaḻi, which has storeys which are surrounded by wealth and conspicious by many beauties, who has the name of Ñāṉacampantaṉ, will be in heaven to be prised by the celestials who do not wink.

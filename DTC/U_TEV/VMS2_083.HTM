[[Koccivayam is one of the twelve names of Cīkāḻi]]

[[This decade is composed in what is called canta viruttam (canta viruttam) which means stanza in viruttam metre with a uniform rhythmic movement]]

{2:83}__1+
{$} nīla naṉ mā miṭaṟṟaṉ
one who has a beautiful black neck like the sapphire.
iṟaivaṉ
the god who abides in everything.
ciṉatta neṭumā uritta nikar il (tēvaṉ)
the peerless god who flayed the skin of an angry and tall elephant
cēl aṉa kaṇṇi vaṇṇam oru kūṟu urukkoḷ tikaḻ tēvaṉ
the god who is brilliant by having as his beautiful half a lady whose eyes are like the carnatic carp.
mēvu pati (tāṉ)
the city where he resides
vēl aṉa kaṇṇi mārkaḷ viḷaiyāṭum ōcai
the noise produced when young girls whose eyes are like the lance, play.
viḻavu ōcai
the bustle of the festivals
vēta oliyiṉ cāla nal vēlai ōcai taru māṭa vīti koṭi āṭu koccai vayamē
is Koccivayam which has streets having storeys where flags flutter and where the sweet sound of the abundant waves of the sea, is like the sound of the vētam when it is recited
[[The sound produced when Vētam is recited is compared to the sound produced in the sea by the waves. This indicates the large number of brahmins who chant the Vētam.]]
{2:83}__2+
{$} viṭai uṭai (ya) appaṉ
The father who has a bull
oppu il naṭam āṭavalla vikirtattu urukkoḷ vimalaṉ
the spotless god who has a form quite different from the world and who is able to dance which has no comparison
caṭai yiṭai veḷ ḷerukka malar kaṅkai tiṅkaḷ taka vaitta cōti pati
the city of the effulgent light who placed properly on his caṭi the flowers of white yārcum, kaṇki and the crescent.
maṭaiyiṭai eṅkum aṉṉam niṟaip parantu kamalattu vaikum vayal cūḻ (koccai vayam)
surrounded by fields where the swans being full in all the sluices of the fields, spread themselves and stay in the lotus flowers.
koṭaiyuṭai vaṇkaiyāḷar maṟaiyōrkaḷ eṉṟum vaḷarkiṉṟa koccai vayam
is Koccivayam where brahmins and munificent persons are on the increase.
[[vaṇkaiyāḷar can be put in apposition with maṟaiyōrkaḷ and can be interpreted as brahmins of great munificence.]]
{2:83}__3+
{$} paṭa aravu āṭum muṉkai uṭaiyāṉ
the god has on his forearm a hooded cobra which dances.
iṭumpai kaḷaivikkum eṅkaḷ paramaṉ
our supreme god who, by his grace, weeds out the sufferings (of the devotees)
iṭam uṭai(ya) veṇ talaikkai pali koḷḷum iṉpam
god who is the embodiment of happiness and receives alms in a spacious white skull which he holds in his hand.
iṭam āya ēr koḷ pati (tāṉ)
the beautiful city which is the place of (that god)
maññai naṭam iṭa
as the peacocks are dancing spreading their tils.
vaṇṭu matu uṇṭu pāṭum naḷir cōlai (koccai vayam)
surrounded by cool gardens where the beetles hum having drunk honey.
kōlu kaṉakak kuṭam iṭu kūṭam ēṟivaḷar pūvai nalla maṟai ōtu koccai vayam
is Koccivayam where the bush mynas chant the good vetam climbing on the top of the building on which gold pots are constructed and fixed.
{2:83}__4+
{$} eṇ ticai pālar eṅkum (1) ikalip pukuntu
The guardian deities of the eight directions enter everywhere vying with others
(2) muyal vuṟṟa cintai muṭuki
and hastening in their minds which are endeavouring in their efforts.
paṇṭu oḷi tīpamālai iṭutūpamōṭu paṇivuṟṟa pātar pati (tāṉ)
the place of the god whose feet are worshipped with offerings of fruits, lights, rows of lamps and burning incense
[[paṇṭu - paḻam;]]
[[PP: paṇṭu otta moḻiyāḷai (appar, tiru aṉṉiyur tirukkuṟuntoki, 75);]]
[[PP: olku tīm paṇṭam peytu oḻukum paṇṭiyum (Cīvakacintāmaṇi 62)]]
maṇṭiya vaṇṭal miṇṭi varunīra poṉṉi vayal pāya
as the water of the river poṉṉi (Kaviri) which brings floods with silt, moving swiftly and pushing forward and flowing into the fields
vāḷai kuḻumikkuṇṭu akaḻ pāyum ōcai paṭai nīṭu (atu) eṉṉa vaḷarkiṉṟa koccai vayam
is Koccivayam where the sound of the scabbard fish joining in a group and jumping into the deep moat resembles the sound of the long army when it marches, and is increasing.

{2:83}__5+
{$} paṉi vaḷar māmalaikku marukaṉ
the god is the son-in-law of the big mountin where snow is lying.
kupēraṉoṭu tōḻamaikkoḷ pakavaṉ
and the god of six attributes who has friendship with Kupēraṉ
[[the guardian deity of the North, who is the possessor of wealth.]]
iṉiyaṉa allavaṟṟai iṉitāka nalkum iṟaivaṉ nalkum iṟaivaṉ iṭam koḷ pati (tāṉ)
the city which is the abode of the Lord who grants pleasureless things converting them into pleasureable things.
muṉivarkaḷ tokku mikka maṟaiyōrkaḷ ōmam vaḷar tūmam ōṭi aṇavik kuṉi mati mūṭi
the smoke issuing from the consecrated fire mintined by many brahmins when the sages gather together, rises high and reaches the sky and conceals the curved moon.
nīṭum uyar vāṉ maṟaittu niṟaikiṉṟa koccaivayam
is Kocccivayam where the smoke hides the high and extensive sky and fills everywhere.
{2:83}__6+
{$} puli ataḷ kōvaṇaṅkaḷ uṭai āṭaiyāka uṭaiyāṉ
the god has the skin of the tiger and loin-cloths as his dress
[[uṭai āṭai: uṭuttalaiyuṭaiya āṭai (the dress he wears)]]
niṉaikkum aḷavil nalitaru mup puraṅkaḷ eri ceyta nātaṉ nalamā irunta nakar (tāṉ)
the city where the master who burnt the three cities which afflicted the tēvar, the moment he thought of destroying them, resides happily.
kali keṭa antaṇāḷar kalaimēvu cintaiyuṭaiyār niṟaintuvaḷara
the brahmins whose minds desire the arts, prosper being full, to destroy the evils of the Kali Yukam
poli taru maṇṭapaṅkaḷ uyar māṭam nīṭuvaraimēvu koccai vayam
is Koccivayam whose brilliant halls and tall storeys are like the high mountin.

{$} kaiyiṉil uṇṭu mēṉi utir mācar kuṇṭar
the despicable people from whose bodies dirt drops off and who use their hands as plates for eating.
iṭucīvarattiṉ uṭaiyār
and the Buddhists who cover themselves with yellow robes called cīvaram
mey uraiyāta vaṇṇam viḷaiyāṭa valla vikirtattu urukkoḷ vimalaṉ
(the city of) the spotless one who has a form quite different from the world and who can sport so that both cannot speak about his real nature.
pai uṭai(ya) nākavāyil eyiṟu āra mikka kuravam payiṉṟu malara
when the large number of common bottle-flower blossoms abundantly like the teeth in the mouth of the cobra which has a hood.
ceyyiṉil nīla moṭṭu viriyakkamaḻntu maṇam nāṟu koccai vayam
is Koccivayam where in the fields the buds of blue nelumbo flower blossoms extensively and spreads its fragrance.

{$} iṟaivaṉai
on god who abides in every thing and everywhere,
oppu ilāta oḷi mēṉiyāṉai
who has a brilliant form which has no comparison.
ulaṅkaḷ ēḻum uṭaṉē maṟai taru veḷḷa mēvi vaḷar kōyil maṉṉi iṉitā irunta maṇiyai
and who is like the precious stones who was seated without any trouble being permanent in the temple which rose above the floods into which all the seven worlds were submerged simultaneously.
kuṟaivila ñāṉamēvu kuḷir pantaṉ vaitta tamiḻ mālai pāṭumavar pōy
those who sing the garland of verses kept on the feet of the Lord by pantaṉ of warm love who had spiritual wisdom without deficiency.
aṟai kaḻal īcaṉ āḷum nakarmēvi eṉṟum aḻakāyiruppatu aṟivē
it is an act of wisdom to reside beautifully for ever, having reached the city ruled by Civaṉ who wears sounding anklets.

*1
maṭṭu _oḷi viritaru malar niṟai curikuḻal maṭavaral---
paṭṭu _oḷi maṇi _alkul _umai---_amai _uru _orupākamā,
kaṭṭu _oḷirpuṉaloṭu kaṭi_aravu _uṭaṉ_uṟai muṭimicai
viṭṭu _oḷi _utir pitir matiyavar pati---viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*2
_eṇ niṟa vari vaḷai, neṟikuḻal, _eḻil moḻi, _iḷamulaip-
peṇ _uṟum _uṭaliṉar; perukiya kaṭalviṭam miṭaṟiṉar;
kaṇ _uṟu nutaliṉar; kaṭiyatu _or viṭaiyiṉar; kaṉaliṉar;
viṇ _uṟu piṟai _aṇi caṭaiyiṉar; pati---viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*3
mait taku matar viḻi malaimakaḷ_uru _orupākamā
vaittavar, matakari _urivaiceytavar, tamai maruviṉār
tetteṉa _icai mural caritaiyar, tikaḻtarum _araviṉar,
vittaka nakutalai _uṭaiyavar, _iṭam---viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*4
cev_aḻal _eṉa naṉi perukiya _uruviṉar, ceṟitaru
kavvu _aḻal_araviṉar; katir mutir maḻuviṉar; toḻu _ilā
muv_aḻalnicicarar viṟal_avai _aḻitara, mutu matil
vev_aḻal koḷa, naṉi muṉipavar; pati---viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*5
paiṅkaṇatu _oru peru maḻalai veḷ_ēṟṟiṉar; pali _eṉā
_eṅkaṇum _uḻitarvar; _imaiyavar toḻutu _eḻum _iyalpiṉar;
_aṅkaṇar; _amararkaḷ _aṭi_iṇai toḻutu _eḻa, _āramā
veṅ kaṇa _araviṉar; _uṟaitaru pati---viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*6
poṉ _aṉa puritaru caṭaiyiṉar, poṭi _aṇi vaṭiviṉar,
_uṉṉiṉar viṉai_avai kaḷaitalai maruviya _oruvaṉār,
teṉṉeṉa _icai mural caritaiyar, tikaḻtarum mārpiṉil
miṉ _eṉa miḷirvatu _or _araviṉar, pati---viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*7
_akkiṉoṭu, _aravu,---_arai---_aṇi tikaḻ _oḷiyatu _or _āmai, pūṇṭu,
_ikku _uka, mali talai kalaṉ _eṉa _iṭu pali _ēkuvar;
kokkarai, kuḻal, muḻa, viḻavoṭum _icaivatu _or caritaiyar;
mikkavar; _uṟaivatu---virai kamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*8
pātam _orviral _uṟa, malai _aṭar palatalai neritara,
pūtamoṭu _aṭiyavar puṉai kaḻal toḻutu _eḻu pukaḻiṉar;
_ōtamoṭu _olitirai paṭu kaṭal viṭam _uṭai miṭaṟiṉar;
vētamoṭu _uṟu toḻil matiyavar; pati---viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*9
nīr _aṇi malarmicai _uṟaipavaṉ, niṟai kaṭal _uṟu tuyil
nāraṇaṉ, _eṉa _ivar_iruvarum---naṟumalar_aṭi muṭi
_ōr _uṇarviṉar---celal_uṟal _arum _uruviṉoṭu _oḷi tikaḻ
vīra(_a)ṇar _uṟaivatu---veṟi kamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*10
_iccaiyar, _iṉitu _eṉa _iṭu pali; paṭutalai makiḻvatu _or
piccaiyar; perumaiyai _iṟaipoḻutu _aṟivu _eṉa _uṇarvu _ilar
moccaiya _amaṇarum, muṭai paṭu tukilarum, _aḻivatu _or
viccaiyar; _uṟaivatu---virai kamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻalaiyē.

*11
_uṉṉiya _arumaṟai _oliyiṉai muṟai miku pāṭalcey
_iṉ_icaiyavar _uṟai _eḻil tikaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻalaiyai,
maṉṉiya pukaliyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉa vaṇtamiḻ
coṉṉavar tuyar _ilar; viyaṉ_ulaku _uṟu kati peṟuvarē.

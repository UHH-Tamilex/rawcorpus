*1
maṭṭu _oḷi viritaru malar niṟi curikuḻal maṭavaral---
paṭṭu _oḷi maṇi _alkul _umi---_ami _uru _orupākamā,
kaṭṭu _oḷirpuṉaloṭu kaṭi_aravu _uṭaṉ_uṟi muṭimici
viṭṭu _oḷi _utir pitir matiyavar pati---viḻimiḻaliyē.

*2
_eṇ niṟa vari vaḷi, neṟikuḻal, _eḻil moḻi, _iḷamulip-
peṇ _uṟum _uṭaliṉar; perukiya kaṭalviṭam miṭaṟiṉar;
kaṇ _uṟu nutaliṉar; kaṭiyatu _or viṭiyiṉar; kaṉaliṉar;
viṇ _uṟu piṟi _aṇi caṭiyiṉar; pati---viḻimiḻaliyē.

*3
mit taku matar viḻi malimakaḷ_uru _orupākamā
vittavar, matakari _uriviceytavar, tami maruviṉār
tetteṉa _ici mural caritiyar, tikaḻtarum _araviṉar,
vittaka nakutali _uṭiyavar, _iṭam---viḻimiḻaliyē.

*4
cev_aḻal _eṉa naṉi perukiya _uruviṉar, ceṟitaru
kavvu _aḻal_araviṉar; katir mutir maḻuviṉar; toḻu _ilā
muv_aḻalnicicarar viṟal_avi _aḻitara, mutu matil
vev_aḻal koḷa, naṉi muṉipavar; pati---viḻimiḻaliyē.

*5
piṅkaṇatu _oru peru maḻali veḷ_ēṟṟiṉar; pali _eṉā
_eṅkaṇum _uḻitarvar; _imiyavar toḻutu _eḻum _iyalpiṉar;
_aṅkaṇar; _amararkaḷ _aṭi_iṇi toḻutu _eḻa, _āramā
veṅ kaṇa _araviṉar; _uṟitaru pati---viḻimiḻaliyē.

*6
poṉ _aṉa puritaru caṭiyiṉar, poṭi _aṇi vaṭiviṉar,
_uṉṉiṉar viṉi_avi kaḷitali maruviya _oruvaṉār,
teṉṉeṉa _ici mural caritiyar, tikaḻtarum mārpiṉil
miṉ _eṉa miḷirvatu _or _araviṉar, pati---viḻimiḻaliyē.

*7
_akkiṉoṭu, _aravu,---_ari---_aṇi tikaḻ _oḷiyatu _or _āmi, pūṇṭu,
_ikku _uka, mali tali kalaṉ _eṉa _iṭu pali _ēkuvar;
kokkari, kuḻal, muḻa, viḻavoṭum _icivatu _or caritiyar;
mikkavar; _uṟivatu---viri kamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻaliyē.

*8
pātam _orviral _uṟa, mali _aṭar palatali neritara,
pūtamoṭu _aṭiyavar puṉi kaḻal toḻutu _eḻu pukaḻiṉar;
_ōtamoṭu _olitiri paṭu kaṭal viṭam _uṭi miṭaṟiṉar;
vētamoṭu _uṟu toḻil matiyavar; pati---viḻimiḻaliyē.

*9
nīr _aṇi malarmici _uṟipavaṉ, niṟi kaṭal _uṟu tuyil
nāraṇaṉ, _eṉa _ivar_iruvarum---naṟumalar_aṭi muṭi
_ōr _uṇarviṉar---celal_uṟal _arum _uruviṉoṭu _oḷi tikaḻ
vīra(_a)ṇar _uṟivatu---veṟi kamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻaliyē.

*10
_icciyar, _iṉitu _eṉa _iṭu pali; paṭutali makiḻvatu _or
picciyar; perumiyi _iṟipoḻutu _aṟivu _eṉa _uṇarvu _ilar

vicciyar; _uṟivatu---viri kamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻaliyē.

*11
_uṉṉiya _arumaṟi _oliyiṉi muṟi miku pāṭalcey
_iṉ_iciyavar _uṟi _eḻil tikaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻaliyi,

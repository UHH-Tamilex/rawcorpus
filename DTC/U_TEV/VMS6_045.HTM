vaṇṭu ōṅku ceṅkamalam kaḻunīr malkum (1) mata mattam cēr caṭaimēl matiyam cūṭi
wearing on the caṭi which contins red lotus from which bees fly high, purple indian water-lily, fertile datura flowers, a crescent.
tiṇ tōḷkaḷ āyiramum vīci niṉṟu ticai cēra naṭam āṭi
dancing to reach the directions swinging all the strong thousand shoulders.
[[PP: āyiram poṉvarai pōlum āyiram tōḷ uṭaiyāṉum
(Nāvukkaracar, Tiruvārūr (1) 8)]]
civalōkaṉār
God who is in Civalōkam
oṟṟiyūr mēya oḷi vaṇṇaṉār
the god who has a bright colour and who is attached to Oṟṟiyūr.
ulakukku ōr uṟuti vēṇṭi nañcu uṇṭār
consumed the poison with the intention of doing good to the world
kaṇṭēṉ nāṉ kaṉavakattil
I saw him in my dream.
kaṇṭēṟku
from me who saw him thus.
eṉ taṉ kaṭum piṇiyum cuṭum toḻilum kai viṭṭavē
my severe disease of love towards him and the consequent burning act of that disease, left me.
[[Variant reading: (1) mata mattañ ceñcaṭai]]

ākattu ōr pāmpu acaittu
tying on the chest a cobra as garland.
veḷḷēṟu ēṟi
riding on a white bull.
aṇi kaṅkai ceñcaṭaimēl 1. ārkkac cūṭi
wearing on the red caṭi beautifying Kaṅki to make a big sound.
pākattu ōr poṉ uṭaiyār
Civaṉ has on a half a lady.
āṇum āvar
is also male.
pacu ēṟi uḻitarum em paramayōki
our supreme yōki who wanders riding on a bull.
kāmattu āl aiṅkaṇaiyāṉ taṉṉai vīḻak kaṉalā eri viḻitta kaṇ mūṉṟiṉār
has the third eye which opened to issue fire being angry so that the cupid who has five arrows which rejoice in kindling love
[[kaṇ mūṉṟiṉār should be construed as mūṉṟām kaṇṇiṉar]]
ōmattāl nāṉ maṟaikaḷ ōtal ōvā oḷitikaḻum oṟṟiyūr uṟaikiṉṟārē
the god who dwells in oṟṟiyūr where the chanting of the four vētams do not cease along with mintining the sacrifical fires.
[[Variant reading: 1. araṅkaccūṭi]]

1. veḷḷattaic caṭaimēlē virumpi vaittīr
you placed the flood of the Kaṅki on the caṭi, desiring it.
veṇ matiyam pāmpum uṭaṉē vaittīr
you placed the white crescent and the cobra together.
kaḷḷattai maṉattakattē karantu vaittīr
you placed in the mind the emotion of love which is secret.
kaṇṭārkkup pollātu kaṇṭīr
it is an evil thing for people who know that.
ellē
in the daytime.
koḷḷattāṉ icaipāṭip puliyum koḷḷīr
you do not receive even the alms singing music to receive it.
kōḷ aravum kuḷir matiyum koṭiyum kāṭṭi
showing me the killing cobra, cool crescent and a flag.
uḷḷattai nīr koṇṭīr
you took away my mind.
ōtal ōvā oḷitikaḻum oṟṟiyūr uṭaiya kōvē
the King who has oṟṟiyūr which is shining with brilliance where the chanting of the vētams does not cease.
[[Variant reading: 1. veḷḷattait taṭañcaṭai veḷḷattaṭañcaṭai]]

narai ārnta viṭai ēṟi
riding on a spotless white bull.
nīṟu pūci
smearing himself with sacred ash.
nākam araikkuk kaccu ārttu
fastening as a belt in the waist a cobra.
talai kai ēnti
holding a skull in the hand.
uraiyā vantu ilpukuntu palitāṉ vēṇṭa
when he wanted alms entering into the house coming there without talking (silently)
em aṭikaḷ! um ūrtāṉ ētō? eṉṉa
when I asked him my Lord! which is your place?
vēṟkaṇ nallāy! viraiyātu kēṭṭiyēl
good lady who has eyes like the vēḷ! if you listen to me without hurry!
viṭum kalaṅkaḷ neṭuṅkaṭaluḷ niṉṟu tōṉṟum tiraimōtak karai ēṟic caṅkam ūrum tiruvoṟṟiyūr
he replied, `it is tiruvoṟṟiyūr where the conches crawl on the shore when the waves dash aginst them, and the ships that are put out to the extensive sea appear tall!'
tīya āṟē
it was the result of my evil act.
[[The lady misunderstood oṟṟiyūr as the place mortgaged to somebody with the right of possession where could I meet him definitely?]]

mattamā kaḷiyāṉai urivai pōrttu
covering himself with the skin of a rutting and frenzied big elephant.
vāṉ akattār tāṉ akattār āki niṉṟu
Civaṉ who should be in civalokam, himself coming into my house.
pittar tām pōl aṅku ōr perumai pēci
talking about his superiority like a mad man.
pētaiyarai accuṟutti
frightening ladies.
peyarak kaṇṭu
seeing him return.
pattarkaḷ tām palar uṭaṉē kūṭip pāṭip payiṉṟirukkum ūr ētō paṇiyīr eṉṉa
when I questioned him which is the place where the many devotees gather together and live for a long time? you tell me
ottu amaintu uttaranāḷ tīrttam āka oḷi tikaḻum oṟṟiyūr eṉkiṉṟārē
he replies `It is oṟṟiyūr of shining brilliance where the idol is immersed in a river or tank at the close of a festival on the stār, uttaram, which is appropriate to that shrine, what is this' (Is there no place which he can clim for himself as of right?)

kaṭiya viṭai ēṟi
riding on bull of quick git.
kāḷa kaṇṭar
Civaṉ who has a black neck.
kalaiyōṭu maḻuvāḷ ōr kaiyil ēnti
holding in each hand a stag and a battle-axe.
iṭiya pali koḷḷār pōvār allar
will not receive alms made of rice flour which is the min ingredient, will not go from there.
ellām tāṉ ivvaṭikaḷ yār eṉpārē
all people doubted, who is this god?
vaṭivuṭaiya maṅkaiyun tāmum ellām varuvārai etīrkaṇṭōm
we met Civaṉ who was coming himself along with the lady of excellent beauty (vaṭivuṭai maṅkai is the name of the goddess in this shrine).
mayilāp puḷḷē ceṭipaṭu veṇtalai oṉṟu ēnti vantu
coming into mayilappu holding a white skull which has stench of flesh.
tiruvoṟṟiyūr pukkār
entered into tiruvoṟṟiyūr.
tīyavāṟē
It was the result of my evil act.

vāṉavarkaḷ ellām vallārāyk kūṭi
all the celestials who are capable joining together.
vaṇaṅkuvār vāḻttuvār vantu niṟpār
will pay homage by prostrating; will sing songs of benediction and stand coming from their abode of heaven.
ellē
even in the daytime.
em perumāṉaik kāṇōm eṉṉa
will say `we could not see our Lord'
evvāṟṟāl evvakaiyāṟ kāṇa māṭṭār (ātaliṉ)
are not capable of seeing him by any means and in any form (therefore)
nallārkaḷ nāṉ maṟaiyōr kūṭi nēṭi
the good brahmin who are well-versed in the four vetam joining together and searching for him.
aṭikēḷ nām irukkum ūr paṇiyīr eṉṉa
when they requested him `God! declare the place where you stay'
[[cf. nām is used in the sense of second person pronoun, you nām araiyāmattu eṉṉō vaiki nayantatuvē (Koviyar, 164);
nām eṉṉum muṉṉilai uḷappāṭṭut taṉmai uyarvu tōṉṟa muṉṉilaikkaṇ vantatu (Do. perāciriyar);
naṅkai ivar eṉ neṟānaṭal naṭantavarō nām eṉṉa? (Kamparāmāyaṇam, cūrppaṇakippaṭalam 119)]]
ollai tāṉ tirai ēṟi ōtam mīḷum oḷi tikaḻum oṟṟiyūr eṉkiṉṟār
he replies our place in oṟṟiyūr where the waves of the sea surge on the shore and return quickly'.

ēṭī!
my friend!
nilaippāṭē nāṉ kaṇṭatu kēḷāy
you hear the place where I saw Civaṉ dwelling permanently.
nerunal naṟpakal iṅku ōr aṭikaḷ vantu
one god came here yesterday in broad daylight.
[[PP: nerunalai; nerunalaiyāy iṉṟu āki nāḷai āki
(nāvukkaracar, {6-94_(1)}, niṉṟa tiruttāṇṭakam, 1)]]
kalaippāṭum kaṇmalarum kalakka nōkki
looked at me to make the greatness of my dress and eyes which are beautiful like flowers, to lose self-control.
kalantu paliyiṭuvēṉ eṅkum kāṇēṉ
I went to place alms mixing many things, I could not see him any where.
calappāṭē
this is definitely a decepact.
iṉi oru nāḷ kāṇpēṉ ākil
If I am likely to see him one day hereafter.
taṉ ākattu eṉ ākam oṭuṅkum vaṇṇam valaippāṭē paṭat taḻuvip pōkal oṭṭēṉ
I would not allow him to go away from me, by embracing him to produce the nature of the iron in the smithy, so that my breasts would be concealed in his chest.
oṟṟiyūr uṟaintu iṅku tirivāṉaiyē
the one who is wandering here being the resident of oṟṟiyūṟ.

nallārkku uḷ nallai

young lady who had tresses of hir into which fragrant flowers were tucked, to fear.
malai tuḷaṅka
the mountin to quake.
ticai naṭuṅka
the people of the four quarters to tremble with fear.
ceṟuttu nōkki
looking at the mountin with anger.
ceru uṟṟa vāḷ arakkaṉ valitāṉ māḷa
the strength of the cruel arakkaṉ who came to fight, to perish
tiruvaṭiyiṉ viral oṉṟāl alaṟa ūṉṟi
fixing one toe of the holy foot to make him roar.
uru oṟṟi
looking attentively at the form.
aṅku iruvar ōṭik kāṇa
to see it both Māl and Piramaṉ running, at the place.
ōṅkiṉa av oḷ aḻalār iṅkē vantu
the god who rose as a bright column of fire came here.
tiruvoṟṟiyūr nam ūr eṉṟu pōṉār
and went away saying `tiruvoṟṟiyūr is our place'.
ceṟi vaḷaikaḷ oṉṟu oṉṟāc ceṉṟa vāṟē (koṭitu)
it is a very cruel act that the tight fitting bangles slipped off one after another ( am I became lean)

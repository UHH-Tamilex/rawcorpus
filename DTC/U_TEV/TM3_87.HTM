• patikam: [3:87]
  • talam: tiru naḷḷāṟu
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.143

*1
taḷir _iḷa vaḷar _oḷi taṉatu _eḻil taru tikaḻ malimakaḷ
kuḷir _iḷa vaḷar _oḷi vaṉamuli_iṇi_avi kulavaliṉ,
naḷir _iḷa vaḷar _oḷi maruvum naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
miḷir _iḷa vaḷar_eri _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*2
pōtu _amartaru purikuḻal _eḻil malimakaḷ pūṇ _aṇi
cītam_atu _aṇitaru mukiḻ_iḷavaṉamuli ceṟitaliṉ,
nātam_atu _eḻil _uru _aṉiya naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
mī tamatu _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*3
_iṭṭu _uṟum maṇi _aṇi _iṇar puṇar vaḷar _oḷi _eḻil vaṭam
kaṭṭu_uṟu katir _iḷavaṉamuli_iṇiyoṭu kalavaliṉ,
naṭṭu _uṟu ceṟi vayal maruvu naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
_iṭṭu _uṟum _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*4
miccu _aṇi vari _arinayaṉi tolmalimakaḷ payaṉ _uṟu
kaccu _aṇi katir _iḷavaṉamuli_aviyoṭu kalavaliṉ,
naccu _aṇi miṭaṟu _uṭi _aṭikaḷnaḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
meccu _aṇi _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*5
paṇ _iyal malimakaḷ katirviṭu paru maṇi _aṇi niṟak
kaṇ _iyal kalacam_atu _aṉa muli_iṇiyoṭu kalavaliṉ,
naṇṇiya kuḷirpuṉal pukutum naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
viṇ _iyal _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*6
pōtu _uṟu purikuḻal malimakaḷ _iḷa vaḷar poṉ _aṇi
cūtu _uṟu taḷir niṟa vaṉamuli_aviyoṭu tutitaliṉ,
tātu _uṟu niṟam _uṭi _aṭikaḷnaḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
mītu _uṟum _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*7
kār mali neṟipuricurikuḻal malimakaḷ kaviṉ _uṟu
cīr malitarum maṇi _aṇi muli tikaḻvoṭu ceṟitaliṉ,
tār mali nakutali _uṭiya naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
_ēr mali _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi pamutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*8
maṉṉiya vaḷar _oḷi malimakaḷ taḷirniṟam matam miku
poṉ _iyal maṇi _aṇi kalacam_atu _aṉa muli puṇartaliṉ,
taṉ _iyal tacamukaṉ neṟiya, naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
miṉ _iyal _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*9
kāṉ muka mayil_iyal malimakaḷ katirviṭu kaṉam miku
pāl mukam _ayal paṇi_iṇimulituṇiyoṭu payiṟaliṉ,
nāṉmukaṉ _ari _aṟivu _ariya naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
mēl muka _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*10
_attiranayaṉi tolmalimakaḷ payaṉ _uṟum _aticayac
cittira maṇi _aṇi tikaḻ muli_iṇiyoṭu ceṟitaliṉ,
puttaroṭu _amaṇar poy peyarum naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē,
meyt tiraḷ _eriyiṉil _iṭil, _ivi paḻutu _ili; meymmiyē!

*11
ciṟṟiṭi _arivitaṉ vaṉamuli_iṇiyoṭu ceṟitarum
nal-tiṟam _uṟu, kaḻumalanakar ñāṉacampantaṉa
koṟṟavaṉ _etir_iṭi _eriyiṉil _iṭa, _ivi kūṟiya
col-teri _orupatum _aṟipavar tuyar _ilar; tūyarē.

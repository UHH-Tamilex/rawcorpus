{3:60}__1+
{$} kaṟaiyaṇi māmiṭaṟṟāṉ
Civaṉ has a black neck beautiful by the poison
[[kaṟaimiṭaṟu aṇiyalum aṇintaṉṟu (puṟam, 1-5)]]
karikāṭu araṅkā uṭaiyāṉ
has the scorched cremation ground as his stage.
piṟai aṇi koṉṟaiyiṉāṉ
has adorned himself with koṉṟai along with the crescent.
orupākamum peṇ amarntāṉ
desired to have on one half a lady.
maṟaiyavaṉ taṉ talaiyil pali koḷpavaṉ
receives alms with the skull of Piramaṉ who is well-versed in the vētams.
vakkaraiyil uṟaipavaṉ
dwells in vakkarai
eṅkaḷ pirāṉ
is our chief.
oliār kaḻal uḷkutumē
we shall meditate on his feet wearing sounding Kaḻal.
{3:60}__2+
{$} kālaṉai muṉ pāyntavaṉ
Civaṉ pounced upon Kālaṉ long ago.
paṉaittōḷi ōr pākam (atu) ā (ka) ēyntavaṉ
was united with a lady who has shoulders like bamboo on one half.
(1) eṇ iṟantu av imaiyōrkaḷ toḻutu iṟaiñca vāyntavaṉ
who is fit to be paid homage with joined by those celestials who do not wink and who are countless.
muppuraṅkaḷ eri ceytavaṉ
who burnt the three cities.
vakkaraiyil tēynta iḷa veṇpiṟai cēr caṭaiyāṉ aṭi cepputumē
we shall praise the feet of Civaṉ in vakkarai in whom the young white crescent took refuge having diminished in size by takkaṉ's curse.
[[Variant reading: (1) eṇṇiṟaintava]]
{3:60}__3+
{$} cantiracēkaraṉē! aruḷāy eṉṟu taṇvicumpil intiraṉum mutalā imaiyōrkaḷ toḻutu iṟaiñca
the celestials beginning from intiraṉ who dwell in the cool heaven to worship with joined hands and fail prostrate at his feet, by saying Lord who wears a crescent on your head! bestow your grace on us (cantiracēkaraṉ is the name of Civaṉ in this shrine).
antara mū eyilum aṉalāy viḻa ōr ampiṉāl mantara mēru villā vaḷaittāṉ iṭam vakkaraiyē
vakkarai is the place of Civaṉ who bent the mountain, mantaram which is also called meru as bows at different ages and discharged an arrow, the fire to engulf and the three cities which were wandering in the sky
[[mantaravilliṉ antaṇaṉviṭṭa tīvāy ampu tiritaru nakariṉ (peruṅkatao, 1-43-120-121); mantarammēru this is caiṉa tradition; mantaram naṟporu cilaiyā vaḷaittuk koṇṭār (taṉittiruttaṇṭakam); mantaramā neṭumalaiyiṉ (cūlamani tuṟavuccarukkam, 209)]]
{3:60}__4+
{$} ney aṇi cūlamoṭu niṟai veṇ maḻuvum (m) aravum kai aṇi koḷkaiyiṉāṉ
Civaṉ has the intention of having as ornaments in the had a strong and white battle-axe, a cobra along with a trident on which fat is smeared.
kaṉal mēviya āṭaliṉāṉ
dances in the fire desiring it.
mey mēl aṇi veṇpoṭiyāṉ
smears on his body white sacred ash.
virikōvaṇa āṭaiyiṉmēl
in addition to the broad mens' loin cloth.
mai aṇi māmiṭaṟṟāṉ
has a black neck in which blackness adds beauty
[[maittaḻaiyā niṉṟa mā ukanta ampalavaṉ (Kōvaiyar, 102)]]
uṟaiyum iṭam vakkaraiyē
vakkarai is his dwelling place.
{3:60}__5+
{$} ēṉa veṇkompiṉoṭum (m) iḷa āmaiyum pūṇṭu ukantu
being pleased with adorning himself with the white hog's tusk and the shell of a young tortoise.
kūṉ iḷa veṇpiṟaiyum kuḷir mattamum cūṭi
wearing a young, curved and white crescent and cool datura flowers.
nalla māṉ āṉa meṉ viḻiyāḷoṭum vakkaraimēviyavaṉ
Civaṉ who dwells in vakkarai along with a good natured lady who has gentle looks like the deer.
tāṉavar muppuraṅkaḷ ericeyta talaimakaṉē
is the Lord who burnt the three cities of the tāṉavār (acurar)
{3:60}__6+
{$} (1) nīr maliyum caṭaimēl
on the catai where there is abundant water
tār mali koṉṟaiyoṭum katir mattamum vāḷ aravum nirampā mati (um) cūṭi
wearing a crescent and bright datura flowers, a shining cobra along with koṉṟai which blossoms profusely in winter.
nalla vārmali meṉ mulaiyāḷoṭum vakkarai mēviyavaṉ
Civaṉ who dwells in vakkarai with a good natured lady who wears on her soft breasts a bodice.
pārmali veṇtalaiyil palikoṇṭu uḻal pāṉmaiyaṉē
has the nature of wandering to receive alms in a pure white skull, in this world.
[[Variant reading: (1) nīrmalipuṉcaṭai]]
{3:60}__7+
{$} kāṉ aṇavum māṉmaṟi orukaiyatu
Civaṉ has in one hand a young deer which lives in the forest (kāṉ aṇavum
is a generic epithet)
ōrkai maḻuvāḷ
in the other hand he holds a battle-axe.
tēṉ aṇavum kuḻalāḷ umaicēr tirumēṉiyiṉāṉ
has a holy body in which Umai who has tresses of hair on which tēṉ (a variety of bee) stays.
vāṉ aṇavum poḻil cūḻ tiruvakkarai mēviyavaṉ
Civaṉ dwells with desire in tiruvakkarai surrounded by gardens which touch the sky.
ūṉ aṇavum talaiyil palikoṇṭu uḻal uttamaṉē
is the supreme god possessed of all moral attributes who wanders receiving alms in a skull in which flesh is sticking.
{3:60}__8+
{$} ilaṅkaiyar maṉṉaṉ āki
being the King of the inhabitants of ilaṅkai.
eḻil peṟṟa irāvaṇaṉaik kalaṅka ōrkāl viralāl katirpoṉ muṭipattu alaṟa
all the ten crowns which emit ray of the beautiful Irāvaṇaṉ to become confused in mind to make a great noise by fixing a single toe.
nalam keḻu cintaiyaṉāy
becoming afterwards with good intention.
aruḷ peṟṟalum
when he deserved the grace
naṉku aḷitta valamkeḻu mūvilaivēl uṭaiyāṉ iṭam vakkaraiyē
vakkarai is the place of Civaṉ who has a victorious trident of three blades and who bestowed on him boons.
{3:60}__9+
{$} kāmaṉai īṭu aḻittiṭṭu
when Civaṉ destroyed the power of Kāmaṉ (cupid)
avaṉ kātali ceṉṟu irappa
when his dear wife went and entreated Civaṉ.
cēmamē uṉtaṉakku eṉṟu aruḷ ceytavaṉ
granted his grace saying he will safe so far as you are concerned.
tēvar pirāṉ
the master of the celestials.
cāma veṇṭāmaraimēl ayaṉum taraṇi aṉṟu aḷanta vāmaṉaṉum
Ayaṉ who is seated on a white lotus which has green outer-petals and the dwarf who measured the world in the distant past.
aṟiyā vakaiyāṉ iṭam vakkaraiyē
vakkarai is the place of Civaṉ whose nature both could not know.
{3:60}__10+
{$} mūṭiya cīvarattar
buddhists who cover with a robe called Civaram.
mutir piṇṭiyar
amaṇar who consider the piṇṭi (aśoka) tree which is fully grown as sacred.
eṉṟu ivarkaḷ
these two people
tēṭiya tēvar tammāl iṟaiñcappaṭum tēvar pirāṉ
the chief of the celestials who is paid homage by the gods who are very much sought after by those two people.
pāṭiya nāṉmaṟaiyaṉ
Civaṉ who chanted the four vetams;
[[cf.naṉṟu āynta nīḷ nimircaṭai mutu mutalvaṉ vāy pōkātu oṉṟu purinta īriraṇṭiṉ āṟu uyarnta oru mutunūl (puṟam, 166-)]]
palikku eṉṟu palvītitoṟum vāṭiya veṇtalai koṇṭu uḻalvāṉ iṭam vakkaraiyē
vakkarai is the place of Civaṉ who wanders holding a dried-up white skull in every one of the many streets to receive alms.
{3:60}__11+
{$} taṇpuṉalum aravum caṭaimēl uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has on his caṭai cool water and a cobra.
piṟai tōy vaṇpoḻil cūḻntu
surrounded by luxuriant gardens with which the moon seem to come into contact.
aḻaku ār iṟaivaṉ uṟai vakkaraiyai
about vakkarai which is full of beauty and where Civaṉ dwells.
caṇpaiyār tamtalaivaṉ tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa paṇpuṉai pāṭal vallār avartam viṉai paṟṟu aṟumē
the acts of those who are capable to reciting the songs which are set to music and composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ of having knowledge of tamiḻ and the chief of the residents of campai, will have complete severance of connection with them.

{2:17}__1+
{$} eḻilār ulavum vayalukku oḷi ār muttam vilakum kaṭal ār vēṇupuram
Vēṇupuram which floated above the sea during deluge where the bright pearls leave the sea and reach the fields where the women-folk of the farmers wander.
nilavum puṉalum niṟaivāḷ aravum ilakum caṭaiyārkku iṭam ām
is the place for the god who has matted locks of hir on which the crescent moon, water and the extremely cruel cobra glitter
[[nilavu: the light of the moon; here it stands for the moon]]
{2:17}__2+
{$} karavāta koṭaikku alantāravarkku viravāka vallār vēṇupuram
vēṇupuram where there are people who become sympathetic towards supplicants who suffered much in their minds for donation which comes without concealing.
aravu ār karavaṉ amai ār tiraḷ tōḷ kuravu ār kuḻalāḷ oru kūṟaṉ iṭam
is the place of the Lord who has as his half a lady with tresses of hir adorned with flowers of kuravu (bottle-flower), globular shoulders like the middle portion of the bamboo, and who has a cobra in his hand
[[The middle portion between two joints in a bamboo is compared to a lady's shoulders;]]
[[PP_EXT: tōḷē...... vēy amaikkaṇ iṭai puraii
(Akam, 152, 14-23)]]
{2:17}__3+
{$} pōkam tarucīr vayal cūḻ poḻilkaḷ mēkam tavaḻum vēṇupuram
Vēṇupuram where there are gardens on which clouds traverse and which is surrounded by fertile [fields] which give abundant yield in every season
[[pōkam produce from the field; cf. orupōkam, irupōkam, muppōkam]]
ākam aḻakāyavaḷ tāṉ veruva nākam uri pōrttavaṉ naṇṇum iṭam
is the place where the Lord, who covered himself with the skin of an elephant to frighten the lady with a beautiful form, resides.
{2:17}__4+
{$} iṉ naṟava vācak kamalattu aṉam vaṉ tiraikaḷ vīcattuyilum vēṇupuram
Vēṇupuram where the swans sitting on the fragrant lotus of sweet honey, sleep when the strong waves gently blow like fan.
kācu akkaṭalil viṭam uṇṭa kaṇṭattu īcarkaku iṭam ām
is the place of the Lord of the universe who has a neck where he kept the poison that he drank and which rose in that ocean of milk which has pearls and other precious stones.
[[GRAM: a is what is known as ulaku aṟi cuṭṭu: a demonstrative letter meaning which is famous in the world.]]
[[kācu kaṭal is a generic epithet; the jewel Koustūpam rose from it when it was churned; so that epithet can be justified;]]
[[PP: ceṟi itaḻt tāmarait tavicil tikaḻntōṅkum ilaik kuṭaik kīḻc ceyyār cennel, veṟikatirc cāmarai iraṭṭa iḷa aṉṉam vīṟṟirukkum miḻalaiyāmē- (campantar, tiruviḻimiḻali (7th patikam) 2]]
[[PP: aṉṉam māmalar aravintattu amaḷiyil peṭaiyoṭum iṉitu amarac cennelār kavarikku kulai vīcu taṇtiruvayintirapuramē (Tirumaṅki Āḻvar, 3-1-7);]]
[[PP: tāy taṉ kaiyiṉ mellat taṇṇeṉ kuṟaṅkiṉ eṟiya, āypoṉ amaḷit tuñcum aṇi ār kuḻavi pōlat tōyum tiraikaḷ alaippat tōṭār kamalappaḷḷi, mēya vakaiyiṉ tuñcum veḷḷai aṉṉam kāṇmiṉ (Cīvakacintāmaṇi, 930)]]
{2:17}__5+
{$} nirai ār kamukiṉ nikaḻ pāḷaiyuṭai virai ār poḻil cūḻ vēṇupuram
vēṇupuram which is surrounded by fragrant gardens with shining spathes of areca-palms situated in rows.
arai ār kalai cēr a(ṉ)ṉam mel naṭaiyai uraiyā ukantāṉ uṟaiyum iṭam ām
is the place of the Lord who prised and rejoiced in having a lady with the gentle git of a swan and wearing on the waist a girdle (mēkali), resides
[[ALTERNATE INTERPRETATION:kalai saree; [otherwise], if it means mēkalai, it is mutaṟkuṟai (the first letter dropped).
[[uṭai virai: as the spathe unfolds itself, the fragrance spreads in the garden; in the meaning, uṭai should be taken as a verb.]]
{2:17}__6+
{$} naḷirum puṉalil nala ceṅkayalkaḷ miḷirum vayalcūḻ vēṇupuram
vēṇupuram surrounded by fields where the red and beautiful carp fish roll upside down in the cool water.
oḷirum piṟaiyum uṟu kūviḷa iṉ taḷirum caṭaimēl uṭaiyāṉ iṭamām
is the place of the Lord who has on his matted locks of hir shining crescent moon and abundant leaves of vilam.

VMS8 / {2:17}__PIFI7+
{$} tāvum maṟimāṉoṭu taṇ matiyam mēvum poḻil cūḻ vēṇupuram
vēṇupuram surrounded by gardens in which the cool moon and the jumping young ones of deer are found.
[[The moon is above the garden and young ones of deer are inside it.]]
ēvum paṭai vēntaṉ irāvaṇaṉai ā eṉṟu alaṟa aṭarttāṉ iṭam ām
is the place of the Lord who pressed down the king, Irāvaṇaṉ, who had an army under his command, to cry aloud `ā'
[[ēvum paṭai may also mean weapons that can be thrown on enemies.]]
VMS9 / {2:17}__PIFI8+
{$} vaṇṇac cutai māḷikai mēṟ koṭikaḷ viṇṇil tikaḻum vēṇupuram
vēṇupuram where flags hoisted on mansions of different colours of plaster, glitter in the air.
kaṇṇaṉ kaṭimā malaril tikaḻum aṇṇal iruvar aṟiyā iṟai ūr
is the place of the Lord who could not be known by both kaṇṇaṉ (tirumāl) and the god who is eminent in the big and fragrant (lotus) flower.
[[As Kaṇṇaṉ is the incarnation of Tirumal, he is spoken of as Kaṇṇaṉ.]]
VMS10 / {2:17}__PIFI9+
{$} mūkam aṟivār kalai muttamiḻ nūl mīkam aṟivār vēṇupuram
vēṇupuram where there are scholars who know the abode of Civaṉ which is above that of the tēvar, by their knowledge of arts and works on the three divisions of Tamiḻ, and who know the value of silence
[[Speechlessness is the zenith of god-realization]]
pōkam aṟiyār
jins who do not know worldly pleasures
tuvar pōrttu uḻalvār
Buddhists who wander about covering their bodies with yellow robes.
ākam aṟiyā aṭiyār iṟai ūr
is the place of the Lord where devotees live who do not even look at those who indulge in abusing Civaṉ.
VMS11 / {2:17}__PIFI10+
{$} nalam ārtaru ñāṉacampantaṉ
By Ñāṉacampantaṉ who is full of good qualities.
kalam ār kaṭal pōl vaḷam ārtaru naṟpulam ār taru vēṇupurattiṟaiyaic coṉṉa
composed on the Lord of Vēṇupuram where there are good paddy-fields which are full of produces like the sea on which ships sil from place to place.
kulam ār tamiḻ kūṟuvar kūrmaiyar
those who recite the eminent Tamiḻ verses, will receive grace in a very large measure.

• patikam: 1:14
  • talam: tiruk koṭuṅkuṉṟam
  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭai
  • VMS: VOL.11 ,P.70

*1
vāṉil polivu _eytum maḻaimēkam kiḻittu _ōṭi,
kūṉalpiṟai cērum kuḷircāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_āṉilpoli_aintum _amarntu _āṭi, _ulaku _ētta,
tēṉiṉ poli moḻiyāḷoṭum mēyāṉ tiru nakarē.

*2
mayil pulku taṇ peṭaiyōṭu _uṭaṉ_āṭum vaḷar cāral,
kuyil _iṉ(ṉ)_icai pāṭum kuḷircōlai, koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_ayilvēl mali neṭu veñcuṭar_aṉal _ēnti niṉṟu _āṭi,
_eyil muṉpaṭa _eytāṉ_avaṉ mēya(v) _eḻil nakarē.

*3
miḷirum maṇi paimpoṉṉoṭu viraimāmalar _unti,
kuḷirumpuṉal pāyum kuḷircāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
kiḷarkaṅkaiyoṭu _iḷaveṇmati keḻuvum caṭaitaṉmēl
vaḷar koṉṟaiyum matamattamum vaittāṉ vaḷa nakarē.

*4
paru mā matakariyōṭu _ari _iḻiyum viricāral,
kuru mā maṇi poṉṉōṭu _iḻi _aruvik koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
poru mā _eyil varaiyil-taru kaṇaiyiṉ poṭiceyta
perumāṉ_avaṉ _umaiyāḷoṭum mēvum peru nakarē.

*5
mēkattu _iṭi kural vantu _eḻa, veruvi(v) varai _iḻiyum
kūkaikkulam _ōṭit tiri cāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
nākattoṭum _iḷaveṇpiṟai cūṭi(n) nala maṅkai
pākattavaṉ _imaiyōr toḻa mēvum paḻa nakarē.

*6
kaimmāmatakariyiṉ(ṉ)_iṉam _iṭiyiṉ kural _atira,
koym mā malarccōlai puka maṇṭum koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_ammāṉ! _eṉa _uḷkit toḻuvārkaṭku _aruḷceyyum
pemmāṉ _avaṉ, _imaiyōr toḻa, mēvum peru nakarē.

*7
maravattoṭu maṇamātavi mauval_atu viṇṭa
kuravattoṭu viravum poḻil cūḻ taṇ koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_aravattoṭum _iḷaveṇpiṟai viravum malarkkoṉṟai
niravac caṭaimuṭimēl _uṭaṉvaittāṉ, neṭu nakarē.

*8
muṭṭā mutu kariyiṉ(ṉ)_iṉam mutu vēykaḷai muṉintu,
kuṭṭāc cuṉai_avai maṇṭi niṉṟu _āṭum koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_oṭṭā _arakkaṉtaṉ muṭi_orupaḵtu_avai _uṭaṉē
piṭṭāṉ_avaṉ _umaiyāḷoṭum mēvum peru nakarē.

*9
_aṟaiyum(m) _ari kural_ōcaiyai _añci, _aṭum _āṉai
kuṟaiyum maṉam _āki(m), muḻai vaikum koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
maṟaiyum(m)_avai _uṭaiyāṉ _eṉa, neṭiyāṉ _eṉa, _ivarkaḷ
_iṟaiyum(m) _aṟivu _oṇṇātavaṉ mēya(v) _eḻil nakarē.

*10
mattakkaḷiṟu _āḷi(v) vara _añci(m), malaitaṉṉaik
kuttip peru muḻaitaṉ_iṭai vaikum koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
puttaroṭu pollā maṉac camaṇar puṟamkūṟa,
pattarkku _aruḷceytāṉ_avaṉ mēya paḻa nakarē.

*11
kūṉalpiṟai caṭaimēl mika _uṭaiyāṉ koṭuṅkuṉṟaik
kāṉal kaḻumalamānakart talaivaṉ(ṉ)---nala kavuṇi,
ñāṉattu _uyar campantaṉa---nalam koḷ tamiḻ vallār,
_ūṉattoṭu tuyar tīrntu, _ulaku _ēttum _eḻilōrē.

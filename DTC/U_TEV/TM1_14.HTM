• patikam: 1:14
  • talam: tiruk koṭuṅkuṉṟam
  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭi
  • VMS: VOL.11 ,P.70

*1
vāṉil polivu _eytum maḻimēkam kiḻittu _ōṭi,
kūṉalpiṟi cērum kuḷircāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_āṉilpoli_aintum _amarntu _āṭi, _ulaku _ētta,
tēṉiṉ poli moḻiyāḷoṭum mēyāṉ tiru nakarē.

*2
mayil pulku taṇ peṭiyōṭu _uṭaṉ_āṭum vaḷar cāral,
kuyil _iṉ(ṉ)_ici pāṭum kuḷircōli, koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_ayilvēl mali neṭu veñcuṭar_aṉal _ēnti niṉṟu _āṭi,
_eyil muṉpaṭa _eytāṉ_avaṉ mēya(v) _eḻil nakarē.

*3
miḷirum maṇi pimpoṉṉoṭu virimāmalar _unti,
kuḷirumpuṉal pāyum kuḷircāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
kiḷarkaṅkiyoṭu _iḷaveṇmati keḻuvum caṭitaṉmēl
vaḷar koṉṟiyum matamattamum vittāṉ vaḷa nakarē.

*4
paru mā matakariyōṭu _ari _iḻiyum viricāral,
kuru mā maṇi poṉṉōṭu _iḻi _aruvik koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
poru mā _eyil variyil-taru kaṇiyiṉ poṭiceyta
perumāṉ_avaṉ _umiyāḷoṭum mēvum peru nakarē.

*5
mēkattu _iṭi kural vantu _eḻa, veruvi(v) vari _iḻiyum
kūkikkulam _ōṭit tiri cāral koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
nākattoṭum _iḷaveṇpiṟi cūṭi(n) nala maṅki
pākattavaṉ _imiyōr toḻa mēvum paḻa nakarē.

*6
kimmāmatakariyiṉ(ṉ)_iṉam _iṭiyiṉ kural _atira,
koym mā malarccōli puka maṇṭum koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_ammāṉ! _eṉa _uḷkit toḻuvārkaṭku _aruḷceyyum
pemmāṉ _avaṉ, _imiyōr toḻa, mēvum peru nakarē.

*7
maravattoṭu maṇamātavi muval_atu viṇṭa
kuravattoṭu viravum poḻil cūḻ taṇ koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_aravattoṭum _iḷaveṇpiṟi viravum malarkkoṉṟi
niravac caṭimuṭimēl _uṭaṉvittāṉ, neṭu nakarē.

*8
muṭṭā mutu kariyiṉ(ṉ)_iṉam mutu vēykaḷi muṉintu,
kuṭṭāc cuṉi_avi maṇṭi niṉṟu _āṭum koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
_oṭṭā _arakkaṉtaṉ muṭi_orupaḵtu_avi _uṭaṉē
piṭṭāṉ_avaṉ _umiyāḷoṭum mēvum peru nakarē.

*9
_aṟiyum(m) _ari kural_ōciyi _añci, _aṭum _āṉi
kuṟiyum maṉam _āki(m), muḻi vikum koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
maṟiyum(m)_avi _uṭiyāṉ _eṉa, neṭiyāṉ _eṉa, _ivarkaḷ
_iṟiyum(m) _aṟivu _oṇṇātavaṉ mēya(v) _eḻil nakarē.

*10
mattakkaḷiṟu _āḷi(v) vara _añci(m), malitaṉṉik
kuttip peru muḻitaṉ_iṭi vikum koṭuṅkuṉṟam---
puttaroṭu pollā maṉac camaṇar puṟamkūṟa,
pattarkku _aruḷceytāṉ_avaṉ mēya paḻa nakarē.

*11
kūṉalpiṟi caṭimēl mika _uṭiyāṉ koṭuṅkuṉṟik
kāṉal kaḻumalamānakart talivaṉ(ṉ)---nala kavuṇi,
ñāṉattu _uyar campantaṉa---nalam koḷ tamiḻ vallār,
_ūṉattoṭu tuyar tīrntu, _ulaku _ēttum _eḻilōrē.

  • talam: tiru viḷamar
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.9, p.204

*1
mattakam _aṇi peṟa malarvatu _or mati puri nutal, karam
_ottu, _akam naka, maṇi miḷirvatu _or _araviṉar; _oḷi kiḷar
_at takavu _aṭi toḻa, _aruḷ peṟu kaṇṉoṭum _umiyavaḷ-
vittakar; _uṟivatu---viri poḻil vaḷa nakar---viḷamarē.

*2
paṭṭu _ilakiya muli _ariviyar _ulakiṉil _iṭu pali
_oṭṭu _ilaku _iṇimaravaṭiyiṉar, _umi _uṟu vaṭiviṉar,
ciṭṭu _ilaku _aḻakiya poṭiyiṉar, viṭimici cērvatu _or
viṭṭu _ilaku _aḻaku _oḷi peyaravar, _uṟivatu---viḷamarē.

*3
_am katir _oḷiyiṉar; _ari_iṭi miḷirvatu _or _aravoṭu,
ceṅkatir _aṉa niṟam, _aṉiyatu _or ceḻu maṇi mārpiṉar;
caṅku, _atir paṟi, kuḻal, muḻaviṉoṭu, _icitaru caritiyar;
veṅkatir _uṟum maḻu _uṭiyavar; _iṭam _eṉil---viḷamarē.

*4
māṭam_atu _eṉa vaḷar matil_avi _ericeyvar, viravu cīrp
pīṭu _eṉa _arumaṟi _uriceyvar, periya palcaritikaḷ
pāṭalar, _āṭiya cuṭaliyil _iṭam _uṟa naṭam navil
vēṭam_atu _uṭiyavar, viyalnakar_atu colil---viḷamarē.

*5
paṇtali maḻalicey yāḻ_eṉamoḻi_umi pākamāk
koṇṭu, _ali kurikaḻal _aṭi toḻumavar viṉi kuṟukilar---
viṇtali _amararkaḷ tuticeya---_aruḷpuri viṟaliṉar;
veṇtali pali koḷum vimalartam vaḷa nakar---viḷamarē.

*6
maṉikaḷtoṟu _iṭu pali_atu koḷvar, mati poti caṭiyiṉar,
kaṉikaṭal _aṭu viṭam _amutucey kaṟi _aṇi miṭaṟiṉar,
muṉi keṭa varu matil _ericeyta_avar, kaḻal paravuvār
viṉi keṭa _aruḷpuri toḻiliṉar, ceḻu nakar---viḷamarē.

*7
neṟi kamaḻtarum _uri _uṇarviṉar, puṇarvu_uṟu maṭavaral
ceṟi kamaḻtaru _uru _uṭiyavar, paṭipala payilpavar,
poṟi kamaḻtaru paṭa_araviṉar, viraviya caṭimici
veṟi kamaḻtaru malar _aṭipavar, _iṭam _eṉil---viḷamarē.

*8
teṇkaṭal puṭi _aṇi neṭumatil _ilaṅkiyartalivaṉip
paṇ paṭa varitaṉil _aṭarceyta piṅkaḻal vaṭiviṉar,
tiṇ kaṭal _aṭi puṉal tikaḻcaṭi pukuvatu _or cērviṉār,
viṇ kaṭalviṭam mali _aṭikaḷtam vaḷa nakar---viḷamarē.

*9
toṇṭu _acivu_uṟa varu tuyar _uṟu kālaṉi māḷvu_uṟa
_aṇṭalceytu, _iruvari veru_uṟa _ār_aḻal _āyiṉār;
koṇṭal ceytaru tirumiṭaṟiṉar; _iṭam _eṉil---_aḷi_iṉam
viṇṭu _ici_uṟu malar naṟu matu viri poḻil viḷamarē.

*10
_oḷḷiyar toḻutu _eḻa, _ulakiṉil _uriceyum moḻipala;
koḷḷiya kaḷaviṉar kuṇṭikiyavar---tavam _aṟikilār---
paḷḷiyi mey _eṉak karutaṉmiṉ! parivoṭu pēṇuvīr,
veḷḷiya piṟi _aṇi caṭiyiṉar vaḷa nakarviḷamarē!

*11
ventaveṇpoṭi _aṇi _aṭikaḷi, viḷamaruḷ vikirtari,
cintiyuḷ _iṭipeṟa _uriceyta tamiḻ_ivi---ceḻuviya
_antaṇar pukaliyuḷ _aḻaku _amar _arumaṟi ñāṉacam-
pantaṉa moḻi_ivi---_uriceyumavar viṉi paṟiyumē.

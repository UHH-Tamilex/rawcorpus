*1
mattakam _aṇi peṟa malarvatu _or mati purai nutal, karam
_ottu, _akam naka, maṇi miḷirvatu _or _araviṉar; _oḷi kiḷar
_at takavu _aṭi toḻa, _aruḷ peṟu kaṇṉoṭum _umaiyavaḷ-
vittakar; _uṟaivatu---viri poḻil vaḷa nakar---viḷamarē.

*2
paṭṭu _ilakiya mulai _arivaiyar _ulakiṉil _iṭu pali
_oṭṭu _ilaku _iṇaimaravaṭiyiṉar, _umai _uṟu vaṭiviṉar,
ciṭṭu _ilaku _aḻakiya poṭiyiṉar, viṭaimicai cērvatu _or
viṭṭu _ilaku _aḻaku _oḷi peyaravar, _uṟaivatu---viḷamarē.

*3
_am katir _oḷiyiṉar; _arai_iṭai miḷirvatu _or _aravoṭu,
ceṅkatir _aṉa niṟam, _aṉaiyatu _or ceḻu maṇi mārpiṉar;
caṅku, _atir paṟai, kuḻal, muḻaviṉoṭu, _icaitaru caritaiyar;
veṅkatir _uṟum maḻu _uṭaiyavar; _iṭam _eṉil---viḷamarē.

*4
māṭam_atu _eṉa vaḷar matil_avai _ericeyvar, viravu cīrp
pīṭu _eṉa _arumaṟai _uraiceyvar, periya palcaritaikaḷ
pāṭalar, _āṭiya cuṭalaiyil _iṭam _uṟa naṭam navil
vēṭam_atu _uṭaiyavar, viyalnakar_atu colil---viḷamarē.

*5
paṇtalai maḻalaicey yāḻ_eṉamoḻi_umai pākamāk
koṇṭu, _alai kuraikaḻal _aṭi toḻumavar viṉai kuṟukilar---
viṇtalai _amararkaḷ tuticeya---_aruḷpuri viṟaliṉar;
veṇtalai pali koḷum vimalartam vaḷa nakar---viḷamarē.

*6
maṉaikaḷtoṟu _iṭu pali_atu koḷvar, mati poti caṭaiyiṉar,
kaṉaikaṭal _aṭu viṭam _amutucey kaṟai _aṇi miṭaṟiṉar,
muṉai keṭa varu matil _ericeyta_avar, kaḻal paravuvār
viṉai keṭa _aruḷpuri toḻiliṉar, ceḻu nakar---viḷamarē.

*7
neṟi kamaḻtarum _urai _uṇarviṉar, puṇarvu_uṟu maṭavaral
ceṟi kamaḻtaru _uru _uṭaiyavar, paṭaipala payilpavar,
poṟi kamaḻtaru paṭa_araviṉar, viraviya caṭaimicai
veṟi kamaḻtaru malar _aṭaipavar, _iṭam _eṉil---viḷamarē.

*8
teṇkaṭal puṭai _aṇi neṭumatil _ilaṅkaiyartalaivaṉaip
paṇ paṭa varaitaṉil _aṭarceyta paiṅkaḻal vaṭiviṉar,
tiṇ kaṭal _aṭai puṉal tikaḻcaṭai pukuvatu _or cērviṉār,
viṇ kaṭalviṭam mali _aṭikaḷtam vaḷa nakar---viḷamarē.

*9
toṇṭu _acaivu_uṟa varu tuyar _uṟu kālaṉai māḷvu_uṟa
_aṇṭalceytu, _iruvarai veru_uṟa _ār_aḻal _āyiṉār;
koṇṭal ceytaru tirumiṭaṟiṉar; _iṭam _eṉil---_aḷi_iṉam
viṇṭu _icai_uṟu malar naṟu matu viri poḻil viḷamarē.

*10
_oḷḷiyar toḻutu _eḻa, _ulakiṉil _uraiceyum moḻipala;
koḷḷiya kaḷaviṉar kuṇṭikaiyavar---tavam _aṟikilār---
paḷḷiyai mey _eṉak karutaṉmiṉ! parivoṭu pēṇuvīr,
veḷḷiya piṟai _aṇi caṭaiyiṉar vaḷa nakarviḷamarē!

*11
ventaveṇpoṭi _aṇi _aṭikaḷai, viḷamaruḷ vikirtarai,
cintaiyuḷ _iṭaipeṟa _uraiceyta tamiḻ_ivai---ceḻuviya
_antaṇar pukaliyuḷ _aḻaku _amar _arumaṟai ñāṉacam-
pantaṉa moḻi_ivai---_uraiceyumavar viṉai paṟaiyumē.

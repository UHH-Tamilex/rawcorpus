{1:88}__1+
{$} muṟṟum caṭaimuṭimēl mutirā iḷampiṟaiyaṉ
Civaṉ has on his caṭi which is fully grown a young crescent not grown to its full size.
oṟṟaippaṭa aravam (atu) koṇṭu araikku acaittāṉ
tied a cobra which has a single hood, in the waist.
ceṟṟam il cīrāṉai
has a fame without rancour which is in the mind for a long time.
tiruvāppaṉūrāṉaip paṟṟum maṉam uṭaiyār viṉai paṟṟu aṟuppārē
those who have a mind to seek the Lord in tiruvāppaṉūr as their refuge will cut at the roots of acts and attachments.
{1:88}__2+
{$} kuravam kamaḻ kuḻalāḷ kuṭikoṇṭu niṉṟu
the lady whose tresses of hir are fragrant with the flowers of the common bottle flower tree, occupying his body taking possession of it.
viṇṇōr viravum tirumēṉi viḷaṅkum vaḷaieyiṟṟiṉ aravam aṇintāṉai
Civaṉ who adorned his holy body which is worshipped by the celestials joining together, with shining cobras which have curved fangs.
aṇiāppaṉūrāṉaip paravum maṉam uṭaiyār
those who have a mind to prise the Lord in beautiful appaṉūr.
viṉaipaṟṟu aṟuppārē
see 1st verse.
{1:88}__3+
{$} muruku virakuḻalār maṉamkoḷ (1) anaṅkaṉai muṉ peritum muṉintu ukantāṉ
Civaṉ became great long ago by being severely angry with Anaṅkaṉ (Cupid) who captivates the minds of ladies from whose tresses of hir fragrance spreads
[[Anaṅkaṉ is not used in its derivative sense.]]
perumāṉ
Civaṉ.
peruṅkāṭṭiṉ aravam aṇintāṉai
who adorned as ornaments cobras that live in big forests.
aṇi āppaṉūrāṉaip paravum ... aṟuppārē
see 2nd verse.
[[Variant reading: (1) anaṅkaṉaip peritum]]
{1:88}__4+
{$} piṇiyum piṟappu (um) aṟuppāṉ
Civaṉ will completely remove diseases and births
[[The conjunction um in piṇiyum should be added to piṟappu]]
perumāṉ
Civaṉ.
peruṅkāṭṭil tuṇiyiṉ uṭaitāḻac cuṭar ēnti āṭuvāṉ
dances holding fire in the hand in the big cremation ground, the loin cloth torn from a big piece of cloth hanging low.
āṇiyum puṉalāṉai
who adorns his head with water.
aṇi āppaṉūrāṉaip paṇiyum maṉam uṭaiyār
those who have the mind to pay homage to him who is in beautiful āppaṉūr.
viṉaipaṟṟu aṟuppārē
see 1st verse.
{1:88}__5+
{$} takaram aṇi aruvit taṭamāl varai cilaiyā
using the big mountin having elevated places and streams beautiful by the wax-flower dog-bane trees (a fragrant tree), as bow
nakaram orumūṉṟum nalam kuṉṟa veṉṟu ukantāṉ
become great by gining a victory over the three cities, their beauty to decrease.
akaramutalāṉai
who is the first cuse of all things as is the first letter of the tamiḻ alphabet;

aṇi āppaṉūrāṉaip pakarum maṉam uṭaiyār
those who have the mind to sing the prises of Civaṉ in beautiful āppaṉūr.
viṉaipaṟṟu aṟuppārē
see 1st verse.
{1:88}__6+
{$} ōṭum tiripuraṅkaḷ uṭaṉē ulantu aviya
the flying three cities to cease to exist being cught in distress simultaneously.
kāṭu (atu) iṭam ākik kaṉalkoṇṭu naṭaṉṟu iravil āṭum toḻilāṉai
who performs dance at night standing and holding fire in the hand, having the cremation-ground as the stage.
aṇi āppaṉūrāṉaip pāṭum maṉam uṭaiyār
those who have a mind to sing the prises of Civaṉ in beautiful āppaṉūr.
viṉaipaṟṟu aṟuppārē
see 1st verse.
{1:88}__7+
{$} iyalum viṭai ēṟi
riding on a bull which moves forward according to the intention of the rider.
erikoḷ maḻuvīci
flourishing a burning-red-hot iron.
kayaliṉ iṇaikkaṇṇāṉ orupāl kalantu āṭa
Umi who has eyes comparable to the fish carp to dance being united with him on one half.
iyalum icaiyāṉai
who has a fame that abides.
eḻil āppaṉūrāṉaip payilum maṉam uṭaiyār
those who have minds that always practise to think of him.
viṉaipaṟṟu aṟuppārē
see 1st verse.
{1:88}__8+
{$} karukkum maṇimiṭaṟaṉ
Civaṉ has a beautiful neck darkened by the poison.
katanākak kaccaiyiṉāṉ
wears a belt of an angry cobra.
aṭiyavarai urukkum
he will melt the hearts of his devotees.
oḷiveṇpiṟai cūṭi
wears a bright white crescent.
arakkaṉtiṟal aḻittāṉ
destroyed the strength of the arakkaṉ.
aṇi āppaṉūrāṉaip parukkum maṉam uṭaiyār
those who devour with avidity Civaṉ in beautiful āppaṉūr.
viṉaipaṟṟu aṟuppārē
see 1st verse (parukkum-parukum; the consonant is an augmentation for rhyme)
{1:88}__9+
{$} kaṇṇaṉ kaṭikkamalamalarmēl iṉitu uṟaiyum aṇṇaṟku aḷappu aṟitāy niṉṟu
Being immeasurable by both Kaṇṇaṉ(tirumāl) and the chief seated in a fragrant lotus flower.
(aṅku) aṭiyārmēl eṇil viṉai kaṉaivāṉ
will weed out the countless acts good and bad which are with the devotees.
eḻil āppaṉūrāṉaip paṇṇiṉ icai pakarvār
those who sing, set to music which has melody-types, prises of Civaṉ in beautiful āppaṉūr.
viṉaipaṟṟu aṟuppārē
see 1st verse.
{1:88}__10+
{$} ceyyakaliṅkattār, ciṟutaṭṭu uṭaiyārkaḷ
buddhists who wear robes soaked in red ochre and amaṇar who dress in small mats.
poyyar
are liars.
puṟaṅkūṟa
as they spread slander.
purinta aṭiyārai aiyam akaṟṟuvāṉ
Civaṉ will remove the doubts of the devotees who cherish him with love.
aṇiāppaṉūrāṉaip paiya niṉaintu eḻuvār
those who wake up from sleep thinking slowly of the Lord in beautiful āppaṉūr.
viṉaipaṟṟu aṟuppārē
see 1st verse.
{1:88}__11+
{$} amtaṇ puṉal vaikai aṇi āppaṉūr mēya, cantamalark koṉṟai caṭaimēl uṭaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who wears on his caṭi beautiful and colourful koṉṟi flowers, who dwells with desire in beautiful āppaṉūr on the banks of the river, viki which has cool and beautiful floods.
nantiaṭi paravum nalañāṉacampantaṉ cantam ivai vallār
those who are capable of reciting these verses of rhythmic movement composed by good-natured ñāṉacampantaṉ who prises the feet of Nanti

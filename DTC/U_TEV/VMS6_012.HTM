{6:12}__1+
{$} ūṉ uṭutti
having covered with the flesh.
oṉpatu vācal vaittu
Having made nine gateways.
oḷ elumpu tūṇā urōmam mēyntu
Having covered it with hir, the bones bling used as supporting pillars.
tām eṭutta kūraip pōvār
God will go to people voluntarily who have assumed the body, in order to remove it.
tayakkam pala paṭaittār
he created many forms.
tām maraiyiṉār
Has a jumping deer.
[[Has a lotus in his hand; cf. tirunērici, 1 of appar]]
kāṉ eṭuttu māmayilkaḷ ālum cōlaik kaḻippālai mēya kapālappaṉār
father who has a peculiarity in his head and who resides in Kaḻippāli where in the gardens big peacocks spreading their tils dance.
vāṉ iṭattai ūṭu aṟuttuvallaic cellumvaḻi (vaittār)
He has created my paths to go quickly to heaven, by cutting through the sky.

{$} muṟai ārntamum matilum poṭiyāc ceytu
Having destroyed the three forths which were contructed according to rules lid down for them, to become dust.
muṉṉumāyp piṉṉumāy
existing before all things that come into existence and also after they perished.
mukkaṇ entai
my father who has three eyes.
piṟai ārnta caṭaimuṭimēl pāmpu kaṅkai piṇakkam tīrttu uṭaṉ vaittār
He has on his matted locks of hir on which there is a crescent, serpent and Kaṅki, having removed their disagreement.
periya nañcuk kaṟai ārnta miṭaṟṟu aṭaṅkak kaṇṭa entai
my father who retined the black colour of the big poison to settle in the beautiful neck.
kaḻippālai mēya kapālappaṉār
father who has a peculiarity in his head and who is in Kaḻippāli.
maṟai ārnta vāymoḻiyāl yākkaimāya vaḻi (vaittār)
He has shown ways to leave this body by his truthful words which are found abundantly in maṟi (Vētam)
vaittārkku avvaḻiyē nām pōtum

{$} nimilaṉ taṉṉai neḷivuṇṭāk karutātē
without thinking about the spotless supreme person with slackness.
(nimilaṉ taṉṉai) nittalumē niṉaimiṉkaḷ
think about him dily.
nēr iḻaiyāḷ āya oḷi vaṇṭu ār karuṅkuḻali umaiyāḷ taṉṉai orupākattu amarntu
Having on one half Umayāḷ who wears suitable jewels and has black tresses of hir on which the bright bees hum.
kaḷi vaṇṭu ār karumpoḻilcūḻ kaṇṭal vēlik kaḻippālai mēya kapālappaṉār
Lord with a peculiarity in his head who is in Kaḻippāli which has fragrant screw-pines as fence and is surrounded by dark gardens where the bees which have drunk the honey make a buzzing noise.
vaḷi uṇṭu ār māyakkurampainīṅka vaḻi (vaittār)
He has indicated ways to leave the transient body which exists only due to inhaling and exhaling of oxygen
[[kurampai means the body which is a like a hut in which the soul dwells]]

{4:94}__1+
{$} eṉakku īṉṟāḷumāy entaiyāy uṭaṉ tōṉṟiṉarāy mūṉṟāy ulakam paṭaittu ukantāṉ
Civaṉ who felt joy in creating the three worlds, being my mother, my father and children of the same parents.
maṉattuḷ(ḷ) irukka ēṉṟāṉ
he condescended to be seated in my mind.
imaiyavarkku aṉpaṉ
has affection towards the celestial who do not wink.
tiruppātirippuliyūrt tōṉṟāt tuṇaiyāy iruntaṉaṉ taṉ aṭiyōṅkaḷukkē
to us who are his devotees he was an unseen companion in Tiruppātirippuliyūr.
{4:94}__2+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
intappārai muṟṟum cuṟṟāy alaikaṭal mūṭiṉumpukal namakkuk kaṇṭēṉ
I found out a refuge to us if the moving ocean which girts this world on all sides, covers the earth at the time of destruction.
namakku uṟṟāṉ
Civaṉ is our relation.
umaiyavaṭku aṉpaṉ
is the husband of Umayavaḷ
tiruppātirippuliyūr muṟṟāmuḷaimatik kaṇṇiyiṉāṉ taṉ moykkaḻalē
it is the feet wearing tight fitting kaḻal of the god who wears a chaplet of a crescent in Tiruppātirippuliyūr.
paṟṟāy (1) niṉaintiṭappōtu
proceed to meditate on them as our support.
[[Variant reading: (1) niṉaintiṭeppōtum]]
{4:94}__3+
{$} viṭaiyāṉ virumpi eṉ uḷḷattu iruntāṉ
Civaṉ who has a bull was seated in my heart desiring it.
iṉi namakku iṅku avalam aṭaiyā
sufferings will not reach us hereafter.
aruviṉai cārā
irresistible acts will not come near us.
namaṉai añcōm
we will not be afrid of the god of death (we will not be afrid of death)
puṭai ār kamalattu ayaṉ pōlpavar pātirippuliyūr uṭaiyāṉ aṭiyār aṭiyōṅkaṭku ariyatu uṇṭē
is there anything which is difficult to attin to us who are the slaves of the feet of the devotees of Civaṉ in tiruppātirippuliyūr where brahmins who are as famous as Ayaṉ, who came into existence from the navel of Māl, which is a part of the body, live prosperously.
{4:94}__4+
{$} māyam ellām muṟṟaviṭṭu (1) iruḷnīṅka
as the spiritual impurity known as māyi leaves completely and the āṇavan leaves us.
malai makaṭkē nēyam nilāva (2) iruntāṉ avaṉtaṉ tiruvaṭikkē tēyam ellām iṟaiñcum tiruppātirippuliyūr mēya nallāṉ malarppātam
the feet which are like the lotus flower, of the god who dwells with desire in tiruppātirippuliyūr where all the people of this country bow their heads to the holy feet of Civaṉ who remins for his love towards the dughter of the mountin being fixed.
eṉ cintaiyuḷ niṉṟaṉa (ē)
stayed in my mind permanently
[[cf. kaṇṇār nutalāy kaḻal nam karuttil uṭaiyaṉavē (7) of this decade.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) viṭṭiruḷ nīṅki (2) iruntāṉ aṉṉavaṉ taṉ tiruntaṭikkē]]
{4:94}__5+
{$} aṟivu ilāp pētai neñcē
my ignorant mind which has no intelligence!
namakku vaitta poruḷ ām eṉṟu colli maṉattu aṭaittu, cittam orukkiccivāya nama eṉṟu (iru)
Be always uttering civāyanama controlling the determinate faculty, placing securely in the mind telling it that it is the wealth that has been set apart for us.
irukkiṉ allāl
if you are not like that.
moytta katirmati (1) pōlvar avar pātirippuliyūr attaṉ aruḷ peṟalāmō
is it possible to receive the grace of the master who is in tiruppātirippuliyūr where brahmins who are as lustrous as the sun and moon and live crowded.
[[Variant reading: (1) pōlvār tiruppātirippuliyūr]]
{4:94}__6+
{$} pātirippuliyūr araṉē
araṉ in pātirippuliyūr!
karuvāyk kiṭantu uṉ kaḻalē niṉaiyum karuttu uṭaiyēṉ
I had the intention of meditating upon your feet only lying in the embryo of my mother;
[[cf karuvuṟṟanāḷ mutalāka uṉpātamē kāṇpataṟku urukiṟṟu eṉ uḷḷamum (nāvukkaracar Kacci ēkampam (3) 6)]]
uruvāyt terintu
discerning after I was born as a baby.
uṉṟaṉ nāmam payiṉṟēṉ
I repeated without ceasing your names.
uṉatu aruḷāl
with the help of your grace.
(1) tiruvāyp poliyac civāya nama eṉṟu nīṟu aṇintēṉ
I smeared beautifully with the sacred ash uttering the mantiram, `civāya nama' to make the sacred ash auspicious, as my wealth.
nī civakati taruvāy
you will bestow on me salvation
[[Variant reading: (1) tiruvāypoliyac]]
{4:94}__7+
{$} eṇṇātu amarar (1) irakkap paravaiyuḷ (2) nañcai uṇṭāy
you consumed the poison which rose in the ocean when the immortals besought you to consume the poison, without any thought about the consequences (eṇṇātu may be linked with irakka as well as uṇṭāy; eṇṇātu irakka: will mean without the slightest compunction that we are asking him to consume the poison)
(3) tiṇṇār acurartiripuram tī eḻac ceṟṟavaṉē
Civaṉ who destroyed the three cities of the strong acurar for the fire to rise in them.
paṇ ārntu amainta poruḷkaḷ payil pātirippuliyūr kaṇ ār nutalāy
god who has a frontal eye and who is in pātirippuliyūr where vētams which are full of music are chanted without ceasing!
kaḻal nam karuttil uṭaiyaṉavē
your feet are in pur mind having us as your slaves and you as the master.
[[variant readings : (1) irukka (2) nañcamuṇṭāy (3) tiṇṇā]]
{4:94}__8+
{$} puṇṇiyā
the holy being, Civaṉ.
ivvaiyakattē toḻuvārkku iraṅki aruḷcey tīvaṇṇaṉē
who has the colour of the fire, who bore on his red caṭi Kaṅki having fertile water and who is in pātirippuliyūr where he takes compassion on those who worship him with joined hands in this world and grants his grace!
puḻuvāyppiṟakkiṉum uṉ aṭi eṉ maṉattē vaḻuvātu irukka varam taravēṇṭum
even if I am destined to be born as a worm in the next birth you must grant me a boon that your feet will not slip away from my mind.
[[cf. taṉakkuṉṟam mā vaiyam caṅkaraṉ taṉ aruḷ aṉṟip peṟṟāl, maṉakku eṉṟum nañciṉ kaṭaiyā niṉaivaṉ, matuviriyum, puṉakkoṉṟaiyāṉ aruḷāl puḻuvākip piṟantiṭiṉum, eṉakku eṉṟum vāṉavar poṉṉulakōṭu okka eṇṇuvaṉē; poyyā narakam pukiṉum tuṟakkam pukiṉum pukku iṅku, uyyā uṭampiṉōṭu ūrva naṭappa paṟappa eṉṟu naiyāviḷiyiṉum nāṉilam āḷiṉum nāṉmaṟaicēr maiyār miṭaṟṟāṉ aṭi maṟavāvaram vēṇṭuvaṉē (poṉvaṇṇattantāti, 43,98)]]
{4:94}__9+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
maṇpātalampukku mālkaṭal mūṭiṉum
Even if the big ocean covers the earth when it entered into the nether worlds.
maṟṟu ēḻ ulakum viṇpāl ticai keṭṭu irucuṭar vīḻiṉum
Even if the seven worlds fall from their positions and the directions are lost in the sky and the sun and the moon fall down.
añcal
you need not fear.
tiṇpāl namakku oṉṟu kaṇṭōm
we have found a place which will not be destroyed (if anybody asks what it is).
tiruppātirip puliyūrk kaṇ pāvu neṟṟik kaṭavuṭcuṭarāṉ kaḻal iṇaiyē
that is the twin feet of Civaṉ who is the light divine; who has an eye spreading on the forehead and who is tiruppalirippuliyūr.
{4:94}__10+
{$} tiruppātirippuliyūr iruntāy
Civaṉ who dwelt in tiruppātirippuliyūr!
varai eṭutta poruntā arakkaṉ uṭal nerittāy
you crushed the body of the inimical arakkaṉ who lifted the mountin; Kayili.
tiruntā amaṇartam tī neṟippaṭṭu
being cught in the evil path of the amaṇar who have not reformed themselves.
tikaittu
being bewildered (and then attining clarity of mind).
mutti tarum tāḷ iṇaikkē caraṇam pukuntēṉ
I took refuge in the twin feet which can grant me salvation.
aṭiyēṉ iṉip piṟavāmal vantu ēṉṟu koḷ (ē)
take charge of me who is your devotee from being born agin.

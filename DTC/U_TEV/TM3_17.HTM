• patikam: [3:17]
  • talam: tiru vicayamaṅki
  • paṇ: kāntārap pañcamam
  • VMS: Vol.4, p.95

*1
maru _amar kuḻal _umi paṅkar, vārcaṭi
_aravu _amar koḷki _em _aṭikaḷ, kōyil_ām---
kuravu, _amar curapuṉi, kōṅku, vēṅkikaḷ,
viraviya poḻil _aṇi vicayamaṅkiyē.

*2
kītam muṉ _icitarak kiḷarum vīṇiyar,
pūtam muṉ _iyalpu _uṭip puṉitar, poṉ nakar---
kōtaṉam vaḻipaṭa, kulavu nālmaṟi
vētiyar toḻutu _eḻu vicayamaṅkiyē,

*3
_akku _aravu _ariyiṉar, _arivi pākamāt
tokka nal viṭi _uṭic cōti, tol-nakar---
takka nal vāṉavar, talivar, nāḷtoṟum
mikkavar, toḻutu _ēḻu vicayamaṅkiyē.

*4
toṭi mali _itaḻiyum tuṉ _erukkoṭu
puṭi mali caṭimuṭi _aṭikaḷ poṉ nakar---
paṭi mali maḻuviṉar, piṅkaṇ mūri veḷ-
viṭi mali koṭi _aṇal, vicayamaṅkiyē.

*5
tōṭu _amar kātiṉaṉ, tutinta nīṟṟiṉaṉ,
_ēṭu _amar kōtiyōṭu _iṉitu _amarvu_iṭam---
kāṭu _amar mā kari kataṟap pōrttatu _ōr
vēṭam_atu _uṭi _aṇal---vicayamaṅkiyē.

*6
mip puri kaṇ _umi paṅkaṉ, vaṇ taḻal
_oppu _uri mēṉi _em _uṭiyavaṉ, nakar---
_appoṭu malarkoṭu _aṅku _iṟiñci, vāṉavar
meyppaṭa _aruḷpuri vicayamaṅkiyē.

*7
_irum poṉiṉmaliviliṉ, _ericarattiṉāl,
varum puraṅkaḷip poṭiceyta mintaṉ _ūr---
curumpu _amar koṉṟiyum, tūya mattamum,
virumpiya caṭi _aṇal vicayamaṅkiyē.

*8
_uḷaṅkiyil, _irupatōṭu _oṉpatum koṭu, _āṅku
_aḷantu _arum vari _eṭuttiṭum _arakkaṉi,
taḷarntu _uṭal neritara, _aṭartta taṉmiyaṉ
viḷaṅkiḻiyoṭum pukum, vicayamaṅkiyē.

*9
maṇṇiṉi _uṇṭavaṉ malariṉmēl _uṟi
_aṇṇalkaḷtamakku _aḷappu _ariya _attaṉ _ūr---
taṇnaṟuñcāntamum pūvum nīrkoṭu,
viṇṇavar toḻutu _eḻu vicayamaṅkiyē.

*10
kañciyum kavaḷam _uṇ kavaṇar kaṭṭuri
nañciṉum koṭiyaṉa; namarkaḷ tērkilār;
ceñcaṭimuṭi _uṭit tēvaṉ naṉnakar---
viñciyar toḻutu _eḻu vicayamaṅkiyē.

*11
viṇṇavar toḻutu _eḻu vicayamaṅkiyi,
naṇṇiya pukaliyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ,
paṇṇiya centamiḻpattum vallavar,
puṇṇiyar; civakati pukutal tiṇṇamē.

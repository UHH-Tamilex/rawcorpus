• patikam: [4:11]
  • talam: POTU
  • paṇ: kāntārapañcamam
  • (()): namaccivāyat tiruppatikam
  • VMS: Vol.15, p.39

*1
col-tuṇi vētiyaṉ, cōti vāṉavaṉ,
poṉ-tuṇit tiruntu _aṭi poruntak kitoḻa,
kal-tuṇip pūṭṭi _ōr kaṭalil pāycciṉum,
nal-tuṇi _āvatu namaccivāyavē!

*2
pūviṉukku _aruṅ kalam poṅku tāmari;
_āviṉukku _aruṅ kalam _araṉ _añcu_āṭutal;
kōviṉukku _aruṅ kalam kōṭṭam_illatu;
nāviṉukku _aruṅ kalam namaccivāyavē!

*3
viṇ _uṟa _aṭukkiya viṟakiṉ vev_aḻal
_uṇṇiya pukil, _avi _oṉṟum _illi_ām;
paṇṇiya _ulakiṉil payiṉṟa pāvatti
naṇṇi niṉṟu _aṟuppatu namaccivāyavē!

*4
_iṭukkaṇpaṭṭu _irukkiṉum, _irantu yāriyum,
viṭukkiṟpirāl! _eṉṟu viṉavuvōm_allōm;
_aṭukkalkīḻk kiṭakkiṉum, _aruḷiṉ, nām _uṟṟa
naṭukkattik keṭuppatu namaccivāyavē!

*5
ventanīṟu _aruṅ kalam, viratikaṭku _elām;
_antaṇarkku _aruṅ kalam _arumaṟi, _āṟu_aṅkam;
tiṅkaḷukku _aruṅ kalam tikaḻum nīḷ muṭi;
naṅkaḷukku _aruṅ kalam namaccivāyavē!

*6
calam _ilaṉ; caṅkaraṉ; cāntavarkku _alāl
nalam _ilaṉ; nāḷtoṟam nalkuvāṉ, nalaṉ;
kulam_ilar_ākilum, kulattiṟku _ēṟpatu _ōr
nalam mikak koṭuppatu namaccivāyavē!

*7
vīṭiṉār, _ulakiṉil viḻumiya toṇṭarkaḷ;
kūṭiṉār, _an neṟi; kūṭic ceṉṟalum,
_ōṭiṉēṉ; _ōṭic ceṉṟu _uruvam kāṇṭalum,
nāṭiṉēṉ; nāṭiṟṟu, namaccivāyavē!

*8
_il_aka viḷakku_atu _iruḷ keṭuppatu;
col_aka viḷakku_atu cōti _uḷḷatu;
pal_aka viḷakka_atu palarum kāṇpatu;
nal _aka viḷakku_aka namaccivāyavē!

*9
muṉneṟi _ākiya mutalvaṉ mukkaṇaṉ-
taṉ neṟiyē caraṇ _ātal tiṇṇamē;
_an neṟiyē ceṉṟu _aṅku _aṭintavarkku _elām
naṉ neṟi _āvatu namaccivāyavē!

*10
māppiṇi taḻuviya mātu_ōrpākattaṉ
pūp piṇi tiruntu _aṭi poruntak kitoḻa,
nāp piṇi taḻuviya namaccivāyappattu
_ētta vallārtamakku _iṭukkaṇ _illiyē.

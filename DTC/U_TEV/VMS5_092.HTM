[[This is the advice given by the god of death to his messengers not to approach devotees of Civaṉ]]

{5:92}__1+
{$}
kaṇṭukoḷ ariyāṉaik (1) kaṉivittu
having caused Civaṉ who is difficult to be seen, to become soft by love.
(2) paṇṭu nāṉ ceyta pāḻimai kēṭṭirēl
if you listen to the strength of my devotion which I had to Civaṉ
[[PP: pāḻimai-matucūtaṉ pāḻimaiyil paṭṭu avaṉkaṇ pācattāl naivāyē (tiruvāymoḻi, 2-1-5)]]
pāṇikoṇṭa koṭukoṭṭi tāḷam kaikkoṇṭa toṇṭarait tuṉṉilum cūḻalē
if you are very near the devotees who have the instrument koṭukoṭṭi which is played to the measure of time and cymbals in their hands do not think of snatching their lives.
[[Variant readings: (1) kaṉavittu (2) paṇṭunāñceyta]]
{5:92}__2+
{$}
naṭukkattuḷḷum nakaiyuḷḷum namparkkuk kaṭukkak kallavaṭam iṭuvārkaṭkuk koṭukkak koḷka eṉa uraippārkaḷai (1) iṭukkaṇ ceyyappeṟīr
Do not inflict sufferings on those who say give anything to the devotees and those who actually give to them who play on the instrument Kallavaṭam with speed, in times of adversity when their minds tremble and when people laugh derisively.
[[kallavaṭam a kind of drum;
kallavaṭam iṭṭut ticaitoḻutu āṭiyum pāṭuvār cintaiyuḷ cērvarē (campantar, tiruviḻimiḻalai (8) 6);
aṭiyār kallavaṭap paricum kāṇuvatu eṉṟu kolō (cuntarar, tirukkāṉapēr, 5)]]
iṅkum nīṅkumē
leave from this place also.
[[Variant reading: (1) iṭukkaṇ cārappeṟīr]]
{5:92}__3+
{$}
kārkoḷ koṉṟaik kaṭimalark kaṇṇiyāṉ cīrkoḷ nāmam civaṉ eṉṟu araṟṟuvār ārkaḷ ākilum āka
whoever they may be, those people who shout with excitement uttering the famous name `Civaṉ' who wears a chaplet of fragrant flowers of koṉṟai which blossoms in the winter.
avarkaḷai nīrkaḷ cārappeṟīr
you do not go even near them;
[[nīrkaḷ: this form is also found in literature;
nīrkaḷ uraikkiṉṟatu eṉa itaṟku (tiruvāymoḻi, 7-3-6)]]
iṅkum nīṅkumē
(see 2nd verse)
{5:92}__4+
{$}
cāṟṟiṉēṉ
I tell you publicly.
caṭainīḷ muṭiccaṅkaraṉ
caṅkaraṉ who has a tall caṭai coiled into a crown.
cīṟṟam kāmaṉkaṇ vaittavaṉcēvaṭi āṟṟavum kaḷippaṭṭa maṉittarāyp pōṟṟi eṉṟu uraippār puṭai pōkalē
Do not go near the devotees who say Let the red feet of Civaṉ who burnt Kāmaṉ protect us, with their minds, filled with excessive joy.
{5:92}__5+
{$}
namaṉtūtuvīr
the messengers of the god of death!
iṟai eṉ col maṟavēl
do not forget my words even a little.
piṟaiyum pāmpum uṭaip perumāṉtamar, naṟavam nāṟiya nal naṟuñcāntilum niṟaiya nīṟu aṇivār etir cellalē
do not go in front of those devotees who are relations of Civaṉ who wears a crescent and cobras, and who smear themselves profusely with sacred ash which has the smell of flowers esteeming it to be superior to good and fragrant sandal-paste.
{5:92}__6+
{$}
kāmam oṉṟu ilarāy
(messenger of the god of death!) without having anyother desire in the least except the attachment to Civaṉ's feet.
vāmatēvaṉ vaḷanakar kaiviḷakkoṭu tāmam tūpamum taṇ naṟuñcāntamum ēmamum puṉaivār etir cellalē
do not go infront of devotees of Civaṉ who adorn the temples in which Civaṉ dwells with small protable lamps, garlands, incenses, cool, fragrant sandal-paste and sacred ashes even during nights
[[vāmatēvaṉ one of the names of Civaṉ); (????ōmam-iraviṭaiyāmam) (tol-poruḷ- nac)
[[Variant reading: (1) tāmatūpamun]]
{5:92}__7+
{$}
paṭaiyum pācamum paṟṟiya kaiyiṉīr
messengers who hold in your hands weapons and a noose!
namatu īcaṉ aṭiyarai aṭaiyaṉmiṉ
do not approach the devotees of our Civaṉ.
viṭaikoḷ ūrtiyiṉāṉ aṭiyārkuḻām puṭaipukātu nīr pōṟṟiyē pōmiṉē
you go praising without going by the side of the group of devotees of Civaṉ who has a vehicle of a bull.
{5:92}__8+
{$}
viccai āvatum vēṭkaimai āvatum nīccal nīṟu aṇivārai niṉaippatē
to think of people who smear themselves with sacred ash daily is education and desire.
accam eyti aruku aṇaiyātu nīr piccai pukkavaṉ aṉparaippēṇumē
cherish with love the devotees of Civaṉ who begged alms, being afraid of them and not going near them.
{5:92}__9+
{$}
iṉṉam kēṇmiṉ
Listen again still.
iḷampiṟai cūṭiya maṉṉaṉ pātam maṉattuṭaṉ ēttuvār maṉṉum añcu eḻuttu ākiya mantiram taṉṉil oṉṟu vallāraiyum cāralē
Do not go near those people who are capable of one letter of the mantiram consisting of five letters which are permanent and who praise with intention the feet of the King who wears a young crescent.
{5:92}__10+
{$}
maṟṟum kēṇmiṉ
Listen to what I say further.
maṉapparippu oṉṟu iṉṟi
without any other thought in your minds.
cuṟṟum pūciya nīṟṟoṭu kōvaṇam oṟṟai ēṟu uṭaiyāṉ aṭiyē allāl paṟṟu oṉṟu illikaḷ mēl paṭai pōkalē
do not go with your weapons near the devotees who have no other attachment except the feet themselves of Civaṉ who has an unequalled bull, a loin-cloth and sacred ash smeared all over the body.
{5:92}__11+
{$}
arakkaṉ (1) īraintalaiyum ōrtāḷiṉāl nerukki ūṉṟiyiṭṭāṉ tamar niṟkilum
wherever the devotees of Civaṉ who fixed his single toe to crush all the ten heads of the arakkaṉ, stand.
curukku eṉātu aṅkup pērmiṉkaḷ
go away from that place without saying tighten the noose.
nīr curukku eṉiṉ cuṭarāṉ kaḻal cūṭumē
if you play any mischief with them, the feet of Civaṉ who is the light divine will burn you
[[cūṭum: cuṭum the lengthening of the vowel is for metre]]
[[Variant reading: (1) īraintutalai]]

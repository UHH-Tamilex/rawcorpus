*1
cilaitaṉai naṭu_iṭai niṟuvi, _or ciṉam mali _aravu_atukoṭu, tivi-
nalam mali curar _acurarkaḷ, _oli calacala kaṭal kaṭaivuḻi, miku
kolai mali viṭam _eḻa, _avar _uṭal kulaitara, _atu nukarpavaṉ---_eḻil
malai mali matil puṭai taḻuviya maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*2
karam mutaliya _avayavam_avai kaṭuviṭa_aravu_atukoṭu varu
varalmuṟai _aṇitarumavaṉ, _aṭal vali miku puli_ataḷ_uṭaiyiṉaṉ---
_iravalar tuyar keṭu vakai niṉai, _imaiyavarpuram _eḻil peṟa vaḷar
maram nikar koṭai, maṉitarkaḷ payil maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*3
_iḻai vaḷartarum mulai malaimakaḷ _iṉitu _uṟaitarum _eḻil _uruviṉaṉ;
muḻaiyiṉil miku tuyil_uṟum _ari mucivoṭum _eḻa, muḷariyoṭu _eḻu
kaḻai nukartaru kari _iritaru kayilaiyil malipavaṉ---_iruḷ _uṟum
maḻai tavaḻtaru poḻil nilaviya maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*4
nalam miku tiru _itaḻi_iṉmalar, nakutalaiyoṭu, kaṉakiyiṉ mukai-
pala, curanati, paṭa_aravoṭu, mati poti caṭaimuṭiyiṉaṉ---miku
talam nilaviya maṉitarkaḷoṭu tavam muyaltarum muṉivarkaḷtama
malar _aṟu vakai maṉam niṉaitaru maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*5
kati mali kaḷiṟu_atu piḷiṟiṭa _uriceyta _atikuṇaṉ; _uyar pacu-
pati_ataṉmicai varu pacupati---palakalai_avai muṟaimuṟai _uṇar
viti _aṟitarum neṟi _amar muṉikaṇaṉoṭu miku tavam muyaltarum
_atinipuṇarkaḷ vaḻipaṭa, vaḷar maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*6
kaṟai mali tiricikai paṭai, _aṭal kaṉalmaḻu, _eḻutā veṟi maṟi,
muṟaimuṟai _oli tamarukam, muṭaitalai, mukiḻ mali kaṇi, vaṭamukam,
_uṟaitaru karaṉ---_ulakiṉil _uyar _oḷi peṟu vakai niṉaivoṭu malar
maṟaiyavaṉ maṟaivaḻi vaḻipaṭum maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*7
_iru nilaṉ_atu puṉal_iṭai maṭitara, _eri puka, _eri_atu miku
peru vaḷiyiṉil _avitara, vaḷi keṭa, viyaṉ_iṭai muḻuvatu keṭa,
_iruvarkaḷ _uṭalpoṟaiyoṭu tiri _eḻil _uru _uṭaiyavaṉ---_iṉamalar
maruviya _aṟupatam _icai mural maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*8
caṉam veru_uṟa varu tacamukaṉ _orupatumuṭiyoṭum _irupatu
kaṉam maruviya puyam neri vakai, kaḻal _aṭiyil _orviral niṟuviṉaṉ---
_iṉam mali kaṇa nicicaraṉ makiḻvu_uṟa _aruḷceyta karuṇaiyaṉ _eṉa
maṉa makiḻvoṭu maṟaimuṟai _uṇar maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*9
_aṇi malarmakaḷtalaimakaṉ, _ayaṉ, _aṟivu _ariyatu _or pariciṉil _eri
tiṇitaru tiraḷ _uru vaḷartara, _avar veru_uṟaloṭu tuticeytu
paṇivu_uṟa, veḷi _uruviya paraṉ_avaṉ---nurai mali kaṭal tiraḷ _eḻum
maṇi vaḷar _oḷi veyil mikutarum maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*10
_iyalpu _aḻitara, vitu celavu_uṟa, _iṉamayil _iṟaku _uṟu taḻaiyoṭu
ceyal maruviya ciṟukaṭam muṭi _aṭai kaiyar, talai paṟiceytu tavam
muyalpavar, tuvarpaṭam _uṭal potipavar, _aṟivu_aru paraṉ_avaṉ---_aṇi
vayaliṉil vaḷai vaḷam maruviya maṟaivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*11
vacai _aṟu malarmakaḷ nilaviya maṟaivaṉam _amar paramaṉai niṉai
pacaiyoṭu, miku kalaipala payil pulavarkaḷ pukaḻ---vaḻi vaḷartaru
_icai _amar---kaḻumalanakar _iṟai, tamiḻvirakaṉatu _urai _iyal vala
_icai mali tamiḻ_orupatum vala_avar _ulakiṉil _eḻil peṟuvarē.

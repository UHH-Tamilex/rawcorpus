*1
cilitaṉi naṭu_iṭi niṟuvi, _or ciṉam mali _aravu_atukoṭu, tivi-
nalam mali curar _acurarkaḷ, _oli calacala kaṭal kaṭivuḻi, miku
koli mali viṭam _eḻa, _avar _uṭal kulitara, _atu nukarpavaṉ---_eḻil
mali mali matil puṭi taḻuviya maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*2
karam mutaliya _avayavam_avi kaṭuviṭa_aravu_atukoṭu varu
varalmuṟi _aṇitarumavaṉ, _aṭal vali miku puli_ataḷ_uṭiyiṉaṉ---
_iravalar tuyar keṭu vaki niṉi, _imiyavarpuram _eḻil peṟa vaḷar
maram nikar koṭi, maṉitarkaḷ payil maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*3
_iḻi vaḷartarum muli malimakaḷ _iṉitu _uṟitarum _eḻil _uruviṉaṉ;
muḻiyiṉil miku tuyil_uṟum _ari mucivoṭum _eḻa, muḷariyoṭu _eḻu
kaḻi nukartaru kari _iritaru kayiliyil malipavaṉ---_iruḷ _uṟum
maḻi tavaḻtaru poḻil nilaviya maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*4
nalam miku tiru _itaḻi_iṉmalar, nakutaliyoṭu, kaṉakiyiṉ muki-
pala, curanati, paṭa_aravoṭu, mati poti caṭimuṭiyiṉaṉ---miku
talam nilaviya maṉitarkaḷoṭu tavam muyaltarum muṉivarkaḷtama
malar _aṟu vaki maṉam niṉitaru maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*5
kati mali kaḷiṟu_atu piḷiṟiṭa _uriceyta _atikuṇaṉ; _uyar pacu-
pati_ataṉmici varu pacupati---palakali_avi muṟimuṟi _uṇar
viti _aṟitarum neṟi _amar muṉikaṇaṉoṭu miku tavam muyaltarum
_atinipuṇarkaḷ vaḻipaṭa, vaḷar maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*6
kaṟi mali tiriciki paṭi, _aṭal kaṉalmaḻu, _eḻutā veṟi maṟi,
muṟimuṟi _oli tamarukam, muṭitali, mukiḻ mali kaṇi, vaṭamukam,
_uṟitaru karaṉ---_ulakiṉil _uyar _oḷi peṟu vaki niṉivoṭu malar
maṟiyavaṉ maṟivaḻi vaḻipaṭum maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*7
_iru nilaṉ_atu puṉal_iṭi maṭitara, _eri puka, _eri_atu miku
peru vaḷiyiṉil _avitara, vaḷi keṭa, viyaṉ_iṭi muḻuvatu keṭa,
_iruvarkaḷ _uṭalpoṟiyoṭu tiri _eḻil _uru _uṭiyavaṉ---_iṉamalar
maruviya _aṟupatam _ici mural maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*8
caṉam veru_uṟa varu tacamukaṉ _orupatumuṭiyoṭum _irupatu
kaṉam maruviya puyam neri vaki, kaḻal _aṭiyil _orviral niṟuviṉaṉ---
_iṉam mali kaṇa nicicaraṉ makiḻvu_uṟa _aruḷceyta karuṇiyaṉ _eṉa
maṉa makiḻvoṭu maṟimuṟi _uṇar maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*9
_aṇi malarmakaḷtalimakaṉ, _ayaṉ, _aṟivu _ariyatu _or pariciṉil _eri
tiṇitaru tiraḷ _uru vaḷartara, _avar veru_uṟaloṭu tuticeytu
paṇivu_uṟa, veḷi _uruviya paraṉ_avaṉ---nuri mali kaṭal tiraḷ _eḻum
maṇi vaḷar _oḷi veyil mikutarum maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*10
_iyalpu _aḻitara, vitu celavu_uṟa, _iṉamayil _iṟaku _uṟu taḻiyoṭu
ceyal maruviya ciṟukaṭam muṭi _aṭi kiyar, tali paṟiceytu tavam
muyalpavar, tuvarpaṭam _uṭal potipavar, _aṟivu_aru paraṉ_avaṉ---_aṇi
vayaliṉil vaḷi vaḷam maruviya maṟivaṉam _amartaru paramaṉē.

*11
vaci _aṟu malarmakaḷ nilaviya maṟivaṉam _amar paramaṉi niṉi
paciyoṭu, miku kalipala payil pulavarkaḷ pukaḻ---vaḻi vaḷartaru
_ici _amar---kaḻumalanakar _iṟi, tamiḻvirakaṉatu _uri _iyal vala
_ici mali tamiḻ_orupatum vala_avar _ulakiṉil _eḻil peṟuvarē.

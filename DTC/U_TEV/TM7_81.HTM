• patikam: [7:81]
  • talam: tiruk kaḻukkuṉṟam
  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭi
  • VMS: Vol.14, p.109

*1
koṉṟu ceyta koṭumiyāl pala, collavē
niṉṟa pāvaviṉikaḷtām pala, nīṅkavē
ceṉṟuceṉṟu toḻumiṉ---tēvarpirāṉ _iṭam---
kaṉṟiṉōṭu piṭi cūḻ taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē!

*2
_iṟaṅkic ceṉṟu toḻumiṉ, _iṉ_ici pāṭiyē!
piṟaṅku koṉṟic caṭiyaṉ, _eṅkaḷ pirāṉ, _iṭam---
niṟaṅkaḷ ceyta maṇikaḷ, nittilam, koṇṭu _iḻi
kaṟaṅku veḷḷi_aruvit taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē.

*3
nīḷa niṉṟu toḻumiṉ, nittalum nītiyāl
_āḷum namma viṉikaḷ _alki _aḻuntiṭa---
tōḷum_eṭṭum _uṭiya mā maṇiccōtiyāṉ,
kāḷakaṇṭaṉ, _uṟiyum taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē!

*4
veḷiṟu tīrat toḻumiṉ, veṇpoṭi_āṭiyi!
muḷiṟu _ilaṅku maḻuvāḷaṉ munti _uṟivu_iṭam---
piḷiṟu tīrap peruṅkip peym matammūṉṟu _uṭik
kaḷiṟiṉōṭu piṭi cūḻ taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē!

*5
pulikaḷ tīrat toḻumiṉ---puṉcaṭip puṇṇiyaṉ,
_ili koḷ cūlappaṭiyaṉ, _entipirāṉ, _iṭam---
mulikaḷ _uṇṭu taḻuvik kuṭṭiyoṭu mucuk-
kalikaḷ pāyum puṟaviṉ-taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē!

*6
maṭam _uṭiya _aṭiyārtam maṉattē _uṟa
viṭam _uṭiya miṭaṟaṉ, viṇṇavarmēlavaṉ,
paṭam _uṭiya _aravaṉtāṉ, payilum(m) _iṭam---
kaṭam _uṭiya puṟaviṉ-taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē.

*7
_ūṉam _illā _aṭiyārtam maṉattē _uṟa
ñāṉamūrtti, naṭṭam_āṭi, navilum(m) _iṭam---
tēṉum vaṇṭum matu _uṇṭu _iṉ_ici pāṭiyē,
kāṉamaññi _uṟiyum taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē.

*8
_antam _illā _aṭiyārtam maṉattē _uṟa
vantu, nāḷum vaṇaṅki, māloṭu nāṉmukaṉ
cinticeyta malarkaḷ nittalum cēravē
kantam nāṟum puṟaviṉ-taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē.

*9
piḻikaḷ tīrat toḻumiṉ---piṉcaṭip piññakaṉ,
kuḻi koḷ kātaṉ, kuḻakaṉ, tāṉ _uṟiyum(m) _iṭam---
maḻikaḷ cālak kalittu nīṭu _uyar vēy_avi
kaḻi koḷ muttam coriyum taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟamē!

*10
pal_ilveḷḷittaliyaṉtāṉ payilum(m) _iṭam,
kallil veḷḷi_aruvit taṇ kaḻukkuṉṟiṉi,
malliṉ malku tiraḷtōḷ _ūraṉ vaṉappiṉāl
collal collit toḻuvārit toḻumiṉkaḷē!

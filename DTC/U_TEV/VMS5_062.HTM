oruttaṉai
Civaṉ who has no equal.
mūvulakoṭu tēvarkkum aruttaṉai
who is the wealth to the inhabitants of the three worlds and the celestials.
aṭiyēṉ maṉattuḷ amar karuttaṉai
who is the author of the ideas that rise in my mind, who am his slave.
kaṭuvāykkaraip puṉal āṭiya tiruttaṉaip 1. puttūrc ceṉṟu kaṇṭu uynteṉ(ē)
I saved myself by going to puttūr and obtining a vision of the perfect god who bathed in the water of the river, Kaṭuvāy.
[[Kaṭuvay is a rivulet branching from the Kāviri]]
[[uyntēṉ has become uynteṉ to suit metre.]]
uynteṉē: ē
is expletive.
[[Variant reading: 1. puttūr ceṉṟu]]

yāvarum aṟitaṟku ariyāṉ taṉṉai
Civaṉ who could not be comprehended by all.
mūvaril mutalākiya mūrttiyai
the god who has a form and who is the chief of the three deities, Ayaṉ, ari and uruttiraṉ.
nāvil nallurai ākiya nātaṉai
the master who is the good words that rise in the tongue.

aṉpaṉai
Civaṉ who is the embodiment of love.
aṭiyār iṭar nīkkiyai
who removes the sufferings of his devotees.
cempoṉai
who is the superior gold.
tikaḻum tirukkacci ēkampaṉai

mātaṉat tai
Civaṉ who is the greatest wealth.
mātēvaṉai
who has the name of makātēvaṉ.
māṟu ilāk kōtaṉattil aintu 1. āṭiyai
who bathes in the five products of the cow which is considered as wealth and whiuch has no comparison.
veṇ kuḻaik kātaṉai
who wears in his (right) ear a mens` ear-ring made of conch.

kuṇṭu paṭṭa kuṟṟam tavirttu eṉṉai āṭ koṇṭu
admitting me into his grace, separating me from the fault of having been in the company of low amaṇar.
nal tiṟam kāṭṭiya kūttaṉai
who is the dancer who showed me the good path.
kaṇṭaṉai
who is conspicuous by his neck.
kaṭuvāykkarait teṉ puttūr aṇṭaṉaik kaṇṭu aruviṉai aṟṟeṉ(ē)
I removed myself from the Karmams from which I cannot release myself by my own efforts, by obtining vision of the god in beautiful puttūr on the bank of the river, Kaṭuvāy.

panta pācam aṟuttu eṉai āṭ koṇṭa maintaṉai
the strong youth who admitted me into his grace by cutting the desires which bind me.
maṇavāḷaṉai
who has the form of a bridegroom.
māmalark kanta nīrk kaṭuvāykkarait teṉ puttūr entai īcaṉaik kaṇṭu iṉitu āyiṟṟu
my life became happy on obtining vision of the Lord of the universe, my father, who is in beautiful puttūr on the bank of Kaṭuvāy which has water fragrant with the big flowers which it brings.

umparāṉai
Civaṉ who is above the world of the celestials.
uruttira mūrttiyai
who has the form of uruṭṭiraṉ
amparāṉai
who dwells in the shrine in ampar.
amalaṉai
who is naturally free from impurities of the soul.
ātiyai

kampu nīrkkaṭuvāykkarait teṉ puttūr em pirāṉaik kaṇṭu iṉpam (atu) āyiṟṟu(ē)
I felt joy on obtining vision of my master who is in beautiful puṭṭūr on the bank of the river, Kaṭuvāy in whose water conches are found.

mācu ār pāca mayakku 1. aṟuvittu
having cut off the mental delusion born of attachments which are tinted, through his grace.
e(ṉ)ṉuḷ nēcam ākiya 2. nitta maṇāḷaṉai
Civaṉ who is ever in the form of a bridegroom and who is in the form of love in me.
3. pūca nīrkkaṭuvāykkarait teṉ puttūr īcaṉē eṉa iṉpam(atu) āyiṟṟu(ē)
I felt happy when I sid `the Lord of the universe in beautiful puttūr on the bank of the river, Kaṭuvāy which is holy for bathing on the day which has the star pūcam.

iṭuvār iṭṭa kavaḷam kavarntu
eating with avidity the mouthfuls of food placed in my hands by people who give alms
iru kaṭuvāy iṭṭavar kaṭṭurai kēḷātē
without listening to the fabrication of the amaṇar who place in their big mouths gall-nut (iruvāy kaṭu iṭṭavar/).
kaṭuvāyt teṉ karaip puttūr aṭikaṭku āṭpaṭavē peṟṟu nāṉ pākkiyam ceyteṉ(ē)
I had the good fortune of becoming the protege of the god in puttūr on the southern bank of river, Kaṭuvāy.

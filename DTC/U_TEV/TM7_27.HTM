• patikam: [7:27]
  • talam: tiruk kaṟkuṭi
  • paṇ: naṭṭarākam
  • VMS: Vol.5, p.159

*1
viṭai _ārum koṭiyāy! veṟi _ār malarkkoṉṟaiyiṉāy!
paṭai _ār veṇmaḻuvā! param_āya paramparaṉē!
kaṭi _ār pūmpoḻil cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_aṭikēḷ! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉaiyum, _añcil! _eṉṉē!

*2
maṟaiyōr vāṉavarum toḻutu _ētti vaṇaṅka niṉṟa
_iṟaivā! _emperumāṉ! _eṉakku _iṉ_amutu _āyavaṉē!
kaṟai _ār cōlaikaḷ cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa

*3
cilaiyāl muppuraṅkaḷ poṭi_ākac citaittavaṉē!
malaimēl mā maruntē! maṭamātu _iṭam koṇṭavaṉē!
kalai cēr kaiyiṉaṉē! tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_alai cēr ceñcaṭaiyāy!-_aṭiyēṉaiyum, _añcal! _eṉṉē!

*4
ceyyār mēṉiyaṉē! tiru nīlamiṭaṟṟiṉaṉē!
mai_ārkaṇṇi paṅkā! matayāṉai _urittavaṉē!
kai _ār cūlattiṉāy! tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_aiyā! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉaiyum, _añcal! _eṉṉē!

*5
cantu _ār veṇkuḻaiyāy! cari kōvaṇa_āṭaiyaṉē!
pantu _ārum viralāḷ _orupākam _amarntavaṉē!
kantu _ār cōlaikaḷ cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_entāy! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉaiyum _ēṉṟukoḷḷē!

*6
_arai _ār kīḷoṭu kōvaṇamum(m) _aravum(m) _acaittu
virai _ār koṉṟai_uṭaṉ viḷaṅkum piṟai mēl _uṭaiyāy!
karai _ārum vayal cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_araiyā! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉaiyum, _añcal! _eṉṉē!

*7
pārār viṇṇavarum paravip paṇintu _ētta niṉṟa
cīr _ār mēṉiyaṉē! tikaḻ nīlamiṭaṟṟiṉaṉē!
kār _ār pūmpoḻil cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_ārā _iṉṉamutē!-_aṭiyēṉaiyum, _āñcal! _eṉṉē!

*8
nilaṉē, nīr, vaḷi, tī, neṭuvāṉakam, _āki niṉṟa
pulaṉē! puṇṭarikattu _ayaṉ, mālavaṉ, pōṟṟiceyyum
kaṉalē! kaṟpakamē! tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_aṉal cēr kaiyiṉaṉē!-_aṭiyēṉaiyum, _añcal! _eṉṉē!

*9
varum kālaṉ(ṉ) _uyirai maṭiyat tiru melviralāl
perum payalaṉtaṉakku_āyp pirivitta peruntakaiyē!
karumpu _ārum vayal cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
virumpā! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉaiyum vēṇṭutiyē!

*10
_alai _ār taṇpuṉal cūḻntu, _aḻaku_āki, viḻavu _amarum
kalai _ār mā tavar cēr tiruk kaṟkuṭik kaṟpakattaic
cilai _ār vāḷnutalāḷ nalla ciṅkaṭi _appaṉ _urai
vilai _ār mālai vallār viyalmū_ulaku _āḷpavarē.

  • talam: tiruk kaṟkuṭi
  • paṇ: naṭṭarākam
  • VMS: Vol.5, p.159

*1
viṭi _ārum koṭiyāy! veṟi _ār malarkkoṉṟiyiṉāy!
paṭi _ār veṇmaḻuvā! param_āya paramparaṉē!
kaṭi _ār pūmpoḻil cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_aṭikēḷ! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉiyum, _añcil! _eṉṉē!

*2
maṟiyōr vāṉavarum toḻutu _ētti vaṇaṅka niṉṟa
_iṟivā! _emperumāṉ! _eṉakku _iṉ_amutu _āyavaṉē!
kaṟi _ār cōlikaḷ cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa

*3
ciliyāl muppuraṅkaḷ poṭi_ākac citittavaṉē!
malimēl mā maruntē! maṭamātu _iṭam koṇṭavaṉē!
kali cēr kiyiṉaṉē! tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_ali cēr ceñcaṭiyāy!-_aṭiyēṉiyum, _añcal! _eṉṉē!

*4
ceyyār mēṉiyaṉē! tiru nīlamiṭaṟṟiṉaṉē!
mi_ārkaṇṇi paṅkā! matayāṉi _urittavaṉē!
ki _ār cūlattiṉāy! tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_aiyā! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉiyum, _añcal! _eṉṉē!

*5
cantu _ār veṇkuḻiyāy! cari kōvaṇa_āṭiyaṉē!
pantu _ārum viralāḷ _orupākam _amarntavaṉē!
kantu _ār cōlikaḷ cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_entāy! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉiyum _ēṉṟukoḷḷē!

*6
_ari _ār kīḷoṭu kōvaṇamum(m) _aravum(m) _acittu
viri _ār koṉṟi_uṭaṉ viḷaṅkum piṟi mēl _uṭiyāy!
kari _ārum vayal cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_ariyā! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉiyum, _añcal! _eṉṉē!

*7
pārār viṇṇavarum paravip paṇintu _ētta niṉṟa
cīr _ār mēṉiyaṉē! tikaḻ nīlamiṭaṟṟiṉaṉē!
kār _ār pūmpoḻil cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_ārā _iṉṉamutē!-_aṭiyēṉiyum, _āñcal! _eṉṉē!

*8
nilaṉē, nīr, vaḷi, tī, neṭuvāṉakam, _āki niṉṟa
pulaṉē! puṇṭarikattu _ayaṉ, mālavaṉ, pōṟṟiceyyum
kaṉalē! kaṟpakamē! tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
_aṉal cēr kiyiṉaṉē!-_aṭiyēṉiyum, _añcal! _eṉṉē!

*9
varum kālaṉ(ṉ) _uyiri maṭiyat tiru melviralāl
perum payalaṉtaṉakku_āyp pirivitta peruntakiyē!
karumpu _ārum vayal cūḻ tiruk kaṟkuṭi maṉṉi niṉṟa
virumpā! _emperumāṉ!-_aṭiyēṉiyum vēṇṭutiyē!

*10
_ali _ār taṇpuṉal cūḻntu, _aḻaku_āki, viḻavu _amarum
kali _ār mā tavar cēr tiruk kaṟkuṭik kaṟpakattic
cili _ār vāḷnutalāḷ nalla ciṅkaṭi _appaṉ _uri
vili _ār māli vallār viyalmū_ulaku _āḷpavarē.

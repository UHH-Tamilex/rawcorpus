{4:78}__1+
{$} īcaṉēyō Lord of the universe
[[ō denotes grieving;
pulampiṉ ōvum ākum (Naṉṉūl, 312, Mayilainātar)]]
pulaṉkaḷ aintum veṉṟilēṉ
I have not subjugated the five organs of sense.
veṉṟavar (1) vaḷākam taṉṉuḷ ceṉṟilēṉ
I have not gone into the assembly of those who have subjugated them.
ātalāl (ē)
therefore
cenneṟiyataṟkum cēyēṉ
I am at to great a distance from the path leading to salvation.
niṉṟu uḷē tuḷumpukiṉṟēṉ
I am agitated in my mind always.
nīcaṉēṉ
I am a low person.
iṉṟu uḷēṉ nāḷai illēṉ
I am alive today; tomorrow I will be no more;
[[nerunal uḷaṉ oruvaṉ iṉṟu illai eṉṉum perumai uṭaittu ivvulaku (Kuṟal, 336);
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
to what purpose was I born?
[[Variant reading: (1) viḷākan]]
{4:78}__2+
{$} iṟaivaṉē
Civaṉ who pervades everywhere and everything!
kalaikaḷ kaṟṟilēṉ
I have not learnt the arts
ñāṉam kaṟṟavar taṅkaḷōṭum uṟṟilēṉ
I did not even come into contact with those who studied works bearing on spiritual wisdom.
ātalāl (ē) uṇarvukkum cēyaṉ āṉēṉ
therefore I was at a great distance from clear discernment.
peṟṟilēṉ
I have not derived any benefit.
peruntaṭaṅkaṇ pētaimār tamakkum pollēṉ
I am an ugly sight before the ladies who have very big eyes.
eṟṟu uḷēṉ
I have nothing in this world or the upper world to attain salvation.
nāṉ eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
[[see 1st verse]]
{4:78}__3+
{$} maṉattai māṭṭiṉēṉ
I directed my mind in the pleasure of this world.
muṉṉai maṟumaiyai uṇara māṭṭēṉ
I was incapable of understanding the next birth, before that.
nāṉmūṭṭi
being attached to the pleasures of the next world.
muṉṉai nāḷē
early in my life.
mutalvaṉai vaṇaṅkamāṭṭēṉ
I was not capable of paying homage to the god who is the cause of all things.
pāṭṭiṉāy pōla niṉṟu
being like the Lord Civaṉ who made me sing his praises.
paṟṟatām pāvam taṉṉai īṭṭiṉēṉ
I accumulated sins which cling to me.
kaḷaiyamāṭṭēṉ
I will not weed them out.
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
[[see 1st verse]]
{4:78}__4+
{$} karaikkaṭantu ōtam ēṟum kaṭalviṭam uṇṭa kaṇṭaṉ
Civaṉ who has a neck in which he stationed the poison which he consumed and which rose in the sea in which the waves surge up and cross the shore!
uraikkaṭantu ōtum nīrmai uṇarntilēṉ ātalālē
I had no clear discernment of the nature of Civaṉ who is described as having transcended the words. Therefore
(1) araikkiṭantu acaiyum nākam acaippaṉē
God who has tied a cobra which moves staying in the waist.
iṉpa (2) vāḻkkaikku iraikku aṭaintu urukukiṉṟēṉ
my heart melts for the worldly life which gives momentary pleasures and food, approaching people whom I should not do so
[[irai is used to denigrate food for human beings]]
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
[[see 1st verse]]
[[variant readings: (1) araikkaṭaintu (2) vāḻkkaikkiraikkiṭantu, vāḻkkait tiraikkaṭaintu]]
{4:78}__5+
{$} cemmai veṇṇīṟu pūcum civaṉ avaṉ, tēvatēvaṉ vemmai nōḷ viṉaikaḷ tīrkkum vikirtaṉukku ārvam eyti aquiring love towards the god who is different from this world, who removes cruel diseases and sins, the god above other gods, Civaṉ who smears on his red body white sacred ash.
[[PP: tēvatēvaṉ:
tēvatēvaṉ tiruppeyarākavum,
tēvarkō aṟiyāta tēvatēvaṉ;
tēvatēvaṉ
(tiruvācakam, 2-122, tiruccatakam 34, ceṉṉippattu, 1)]]
ammai niṉṟu aṭimai ceyyā vaṭivu ilā muṭivu ilvāḻkkaikku immai niṉṟu urukukiṉṟēṉ
my heart melts in this birth on account of the life which is endless and has no form as it did not perform service steadily in the previous births.
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)

{$} pēccoṭu pēccukku ellām piṟartamaip puṟamē pēcak kūccilēṉ
I did not feel ashamed to backbiting others for each and every word they speak.
ātalālē
therefore.
koṭumaiyai viṭumāṟu ōrēṉ

nāḷum mūrtti naṉmaiyai nāccoli uṇaramāṭṭēṉ
daily I am unable to realise the good that you do to me and other souls by speaking about them by my mouth.
ēccu uḷēniṉṟu
remaining in the body which is the target of ridicule by others.
meyyē
truely

{$} tēcaṉai
Civaṉ who is the light divine
tēcam ākum tirumāl ōr paṅkaṉ taṉṉai
who has Tirumāl who has lustre, on one half.
pūcaṉaip puṉitaṉ taṉṉai
who is pure and worthy of worship.
puṇarum puṇṭarikattāṉai
who is seated in the heart which is like a lotus.
nēcaṉai
who has love towards his devotees.
neruppaṉ taṉṉai
who is shining like the fire.
(1) nivañcakattu akaṉṟai cemmai īcaṉai aṟiyamāṭṭēṉ
I am unable to realise the Lord of the universe who is impartial and who is above the five defects of ignorance, ego, hankering after pleasures, likes and dislikes.
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
[[see 1st verse]]
[[Variant reading: (1) nilañcakattu]]

{$} veṇ mayirviravi
grey hairs being mixed with black ones
[[the idea is, having become aged]]
viḷaikkiṉṟaviṉaiyai nōkki
thinking of the old acts that give their fruits, and new acts.
mēlum muḷaikkiṉṟa viṉaiyaip pōka muyalkilēṉ
I have not the capacity to remove the acts that come into existence hereafter.
iyalaveḷḷam tiḷaikkiṉṟa muṭiyiṉāṉ taṉ tiruvaṭi paravamāṭṭātu iḷaikkiṉṟēṉ, irumi, ūṉṟi
I am exhausted, coughing and fixing the stick to prevent falling down, without praising the holy feet of Civaṉ on whose head the flood which is always present there, without leaving it to move about
[[iyalaveḷḷam may be a scribal error for ilayaveḷḷam is my humble opinion]]
[[ilayaveḷḷam: flood that is restrained or absorbed in the caṭai]]

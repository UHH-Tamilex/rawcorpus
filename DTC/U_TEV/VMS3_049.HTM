{3:49}__1+
{$} kātal āki
with love
kacintu
growing tender hearted.
kaṇṇīr malki
with profuse tears rolling down the cheeks.
ōtuvārtamai naṉṉeṟikku uyppatu
will direct those who recite the mantiram in the path to salvation.
vētam nāṉkiṉum meypporuḷ āvatu
is the essential truth dealt with in the four Vētam-s.
nātaṉ nāmam namaccivāyavē
namacCivaya which is the name of the master, Civaṉ.
{3:49}__2+
{$} (1) nampuvār avar
those who cherish with love.
nāviṉ naviṟṟicel
if they recite without ceasing in their tongues.
vampu nāḷ malar vārmatu oppatu
is sweet like the honey that pours from the fragrant flowers that unfolds its petals at day break.
ulakukku elām cempoṉ tilakam
is like the small circular mark on the forehead of the whole word.
nampaṉ nāmam namaccivāyavē
namacCiva, is the name of Civaṉ
[[namaccivāya oppatu tilakam]]
[[Variant reading: (1)nampuvār namar]]
{3:49}__3+
{$} nekku uḷ ārvam mikapperukin niṉaintu
the heart having melted the love in the mind increasing and thinking of the mantiram.
akkumālai koṭu aṅkaiyil eṇṇuvār
those who repeat reciting having in their beautiful hands a garland of uruttirukkam.
takka vāṉavarāttakuvippatu
makes them qualified to become fitting celestials though they are not qualified to become so otherwise.
nakkaṉ nāmam namaccivāyavē
namaccivāya is the name of Civaṉ who is naked.
{3:49}__4+
{$} niyamam tāṉ niṉaivārkku iṉiyāṉ
god who dispenses good things to those who meditate the mantiram as a daily routine
neṟṟinayaṉaṉ nāmam namaccivāyavē
namaccivāya, the name of Civaṉ who has a frontal eye.
iyamaṉ tūtarum añcuvar, iṉcolāl nayam vantu ōtavallārtamai naṇṇiṉāl
if people who do not recite the mantiram befriend with sweet words those great people who recite that, even the messengers of the god of death will be afraid to go near them.
[[nāmam namaccivāyavē: tirunāmam añceḻuttum ceppārākil (nāvukkaracar, taṉittiruttaṇtakam (1) 6)]]
[[ālaippaṭu karumpiṉ cāṟupōla aṇṇikkum añcu eḻuttiṉ nāmattāṉ kāṉ (Do tiruvīḻimiḻalai (7) 2)]]
{3:49}__5+
{$} kolvārēṉum
though people kill living beings.
kuṇam pala naṉmaikaḷ illārēṉum
and though they have no good character and many good conduct.
nallāṉ nāmam namaccivāyavē
the name Namaccivāya of the god, who dispenses ausipicious things.
iyampuvarāyiṭiṉ
if they recite the mantiram.
ellāttīṅkaiyum nīṅkuvar eṉpar āl
will be free from all the evils.
[[(1) nallār is the reading in all printed editions and manuscripts]]
{3:49}__6+
{$} mantaram(m) aṉa pāvaṅkaḷ mēviya pantaṉaiyavar tāmum
if those people also who are bound by bondage which are the result of sins and which are great as the mountain mantaram.
nanti nāmam namaccivāyavē pakarvarēl
if they recite the namaccivāya, the name of Nanti Civaṉ
valviṉaicintum
that mantiram will destroy the irresistible acts.
celvamum malkum (āl)
wealth will also increase.
{3:49}__7+
{$} narakam ēḻpukanāṭiṉarāyiṉum
though people have committed sins to be cast into all the seven hells.
nātaṉ nāmam namaccivāyavē
namaccivāya the name of the lorn destowar, Civaṉ
urai ceyvāyiṉar āyiṉaṉ
if they recite with their mouth.
uruttirar (1) viraviyē pukuvittiṭum eṉpar (āl)
people of spiritual wisdom say that the mantiram will cause them to mix with the host of Uruttirar.
[[Variant reading: (1) niraviyē]]
{3:49}__8+
{$} nalam koḷ nāmam namaccivāyavē
namaccivāya the name which has many good things.
ilaṅkai maṉṉaṉ eṭutta aṭukkal mēl talam koḷ kālviral caṅkaraṉ ūṉṟalum
when the dispenser of auspicious things who had mountain Kayilai which was lifted by the king of Ilankai, as his shrine, fixed his toe on it.
malaṅki vāymoḻi ceytavaṉ uyvakai (aruḷiyatu)
granted the manner of saving him from death, who sang the Vētam, the eternal truth, being confounded
[[cf. paṇṭai irāvaṇaṉ pāṭi uyntaṉ (campantar, pañcākkarattiruppatikam, 8)]]
{3:49}__9+
{$} pōtaṉ pōtu aṉa kaṇṇaṉum aṇṇal taṉ pātam (tāṉ) muṭinēṭiya paṇparāy
Piramaṉ who is in a (lotus) flower and Māl who has eyes like the lotus, making efforts to search and find out the feet and head of the chief.
yātum kāṇpu aritāki alantu avar ōtum nāmam namaccivāyavē
namaccivāya is the name of Civaṉ which they chanted being in distress as they could not see anything.
{3:49}__10+
{$} kañci maṇṭaiyar
buddhists who drink gruel from a earthern vessel resembling in shape the skull.
kaiyil uṇkaiyarkaḷ
and camaṇar who eat their food keeping it in their hands.
veñcol miṇṭar viñcai aṇṭarkaḷ vēṇṭa amutu cey(1) nañcu uḷ uṇṭaṉ namaccivāyavē viravu ilar eṉpar (āl)
wise people say that those strong people who speak cruel words are not friendly towards namaccivāya of Civaṉ who has within his neck poison, which he consumed at the request of tēvar endowed with intelligence.
[[Variant reading: (1) nañcuuṇkaṇṭaṉ]]
{3:49}__11+
{$} nanti nāmam namaccivāya eṉum cantaiyāl tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ col
the words of Ñāṉacampantaṉ having knowledge of Tamiḻ having the refrain of namaccivāya the name of Nanti.
cintaiyāl makiḻntu ētta vallār elām panta pācam aṟukka vallārkaḷē
all those who are able to praise Civaṉ with joy in their minds will cut at the root of attachments which bind them.

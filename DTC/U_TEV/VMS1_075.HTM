[[veṅkuru is one of the twelve names of Cīkāḻi]]

{1:75}__1+
{$} kālai naṉ māmalar koṇṭu aṭi paravik kai toḻu māṇiyaik kaṟutta veṅkālaṉ ōlam (atu) iṭamuṉ uyiroṭu māḷa utaittavaṉ
the Lord who kicked the cruel god of death who came with anger upon the brahmachāri (Mārkkaṇṭēyaṉ) who worshipped with his hands and praised the feet, daily with defectless big flower, to cry aloud and his life to ebb out.
[[uyiroṭu: ōṭu: is expletive]]
umaiyavaḷ viruppaṉ emperumāṉ
Has love towards Umayavaḷ; our great one.
mēvi vīṟṟiruntār
sat majestically with gladness.
mālai vantu aṇuka
when the evening approaches.
ōtam vantu ulavi maṟitirai caṅkoṭu pavaḷammuṉ unti vēlai vantu aṇaiyum cōlaikaḷ cūḻnta veṅkuruvuḷ
in Veṅkuru which is surrounded by gardens approached by the sea by moving about on account of the tide when the rolling waves push forward coral and conch.
{1:75}__2+
{$} peṇṇiṉaip pākam amarntu
Having a lady on one half.
caṭaimēl piṟaiyoṭum araviṉai aṇintu
and having adorned on the caṭai a cobra along with a crescent.
aḻakākap paṇṇiṉaip pāṭi āṭimuṉ palikoḷ paramar em aṭikaḷār
our deity and superior one who received alms in the past, dancing and singing melodies beautifully.
paricukaḷpēṇi
exhibiting grace which is his nature.
maṇṇiṉaimūṭi
having covered the earth.
vāṉmukaṭu ēṟi
having climbed to the top of the sky.
maṟitiraikaṭal mukantu eṭuppa uyarntu
having risen high when the sea was lifted up by the rolling waves.
viṇ aḷavu ōṅki
and growing as high as the sky.
vantu iḻi kōyil veṅkuruvuḷ mēvi vīṟṟiruntār
sat majectically with gladness in the temple in Venkuru, which descended coming down,
[[In a deluge the temple rose above it and after it was over it descended to its former place]]
{1:75}__3+
{$} ōr iyalpu illā uruvam (atu) āki
Assuming a form which has no properties.
oṇtiṟal vēṭaṉatu uru (atu) koṇṭu
Having the form of a very skillful hunter.
kārikai kāṇat taṉañcayaṉ taṉṉaik kaṟuttu
and fighting with taṉancayaṉ (arjunaṉ) to be witnessed by the lady.
(1) avaṟku aḷittu uṭaṉ kātal ceyperumāṉ
the great Civaṉ who granted him (a weapon) and loved him.
nēricaiyāka aṟupatam muraṉṟu niraimalart tātukaḷ mūca
the six footed beetle like the melody, nēricai, and swarm upon the pollen of the row of flowers.
viṇṭu utirntu vērikaḷ eṅkum vimmiya cōlai veṅkuruvuḷ mēvi vīṟṟiruntār
sat majestically and gladly in venkuru which has gardens which are full of different kinds of honey everywhere, when the pollen drops as the flowers unfold their petals.
[[utirntu should be changed into utira]]
[[Variant reading: (1) atiṟkaḷittu]]
{1:75}__4+
{$} vaṇṭu aṇai koṉṟai (yum) vaṉṉiyum mattam maruviya kūviḷam erukkoṭu mikka koṇṭu aṇicaṭaiyar
the Lord having a caṭai on which he adorns yarcum, vilvam leaves to which he has become accustomed, dhātura flowers, leaves of vaṉṉi (Indian mesquit), and koṉṟai which are approached by bees.
viṭaiyiṉar
he has a bull
pūtam koṭukoṭṭi kuṭamuḻāk (1) kūṭiyum muḻava
when the pūtams play upon koṭukotti and ghatam for their sound to combine together without difference.
paṇtikaḻvākap pāṭi ōrvētam payilvar
will always chant one vētam in a melody which is clear.
[[Koṭukoṭṭi: a kind of drum now spelt as kiṭukiṭṭi]]
caṭaimēl pāypuṉaṟ kaṅkaiyai veṇpiṟai(yai) cūṭi
having borne on the caṭai the Kaṅkai of spreading water and white crescent.
umaiyavaḷōṭum veṅkuruvuḷ mēvi vīṟṟiruntār
sat majestically with gladness in Veṅkuru along with Umayavaḷ.
[[Variant reading: (1) kūṭiya muḻavam]]
{1:75}__5+
{$} caṭaiyiṉar
The god has a caṭai.
mēṉinīṟu (atu) pūci
having smeared on the body sacred ash
takkai koḷ pokkaṇam iṭṭu
having hung on the shoulder a cloth bag in which the musical instrument takkai is placed.
[[takkaikoḷ pokkaṇam this may means also a wallet containing sacred ash to which a cork is fixed]]
uṭaṉākak kaṭaitoṟum vantu vali (atu) koṇṭu kaṇṭavar maṉam avai kavarntu
coming to the entrance of house with that bag and receiving alms and captivating the minds of those who look at him.
aḻakākap paṭai (atu) ēnti
holding a weapon beautifully.
(1) paiṅkayaṟkaṇṇi umaiyavaḷ pākamum amarntu aruḷ ceytu
having fixed with love on one half Umayavaḷ who has elegant eyes like carp fish
viṭaiyoṭu pūtam cūḻtarac ceṉṟu
and having gone to be surrounded by pūtam-s and a bull.
veṅkuruvuḷ mēvi vīṟṟiruntār
is seated majestically and gladly in veṅkuru.
[[Variant reading: (1) paṅkayakkaṇṇi]]
{1:75}__6+
{$} karai porukaṭaliṉ tirai (atu) mōta
As the waves in the sea which seem to fight with the shore, push against with force.
kaṅkul vantu ēṟiya caṅkamum ippi uraiyuṭai muttamum maṇaliṭai vaiki
the conches and famous pearls which come out of the oysters which come and climb on the shore, stay in the sand.
ōṅkuvāṉ iruḷ aṟatturappa
dispel completely the darkness in the high sky.
puraimali vētam pōṟṟu pūcurarkaḷ purintu avar nalam koḷ ākutiyiṉil niṟainta viraimalitūpam vicumpiṉai maṟaikkum veṅkuruvuḷ
is Venkuru where the smoke of excessive fragrance which is full in the oblation offered in the consecrated beneficial fire, which is maintained by the brahmins who protect the Vētam-s of increasing greatness hides the sky.
mēvivīṟṟiruntār
is seated majestically and gladly in Veṅkuru.
{1:75}__7+
{$} valli nuṇṇiṭaiyāḷ umaiyavaḷ taṉṉai maṟukiṭavaru matakaḷiṟṟiṇai mayaṅka ollaiyiṟkiṭittu aṅkuurittu avaḷ veruvalkeṭuttavar
when a rutting elephant came to make Umayavaḷ who has a thin waist like a creeper, whirl in mind, the Lord who caught hold of it quickly and flayed its skin, removed her fright.
poḻil miku tiru ālil nal aṟam uraittu ñāṉamōṭu iruppa
when he was with spiritual wisdom after having explained good aṟam under the sacred banyan tree which is like a big garden (though single)
nalintiṭaluṟṟu vanta akkaruppu villiyaip poṭipaṭa viḻittavar
the Lord who opened his third eye to reduce to ash that well-known Cupid who has a bow of sugar-cane and who came to give trouble to him (the Lord)
[[nalintiṭaluṟṟu should be changed into nalintiṭaluṟa]]
virumpi veṅkuruvuḷ mēvi vīṟṟiruntār
sat majestically and gladly in Veṅkuru, desiring that place.
{1:75}__8+
{$} pāṅkilā arakkaṉ kayilai aṉṟu eṭuppa
when on that day the arakkaṉ who had no good qualities, lifted up Kayilai.
palatalai muṭiyoṭu tōḷ avai neriya ōṅkiya viralāl ūṉṟi
having pressed with the great toes to break the many heads with their crowns and shoulders, into pieces.
aṉṟu avaṟkē oḷitikaḻvāḷ (atu) koṭuttu
and presenting the same arakkaṉ with a shining sword on that day itself.
aḻakāya kōṅkoṭu cerunti kūviḷam mattam koṉṟaiyum kulāviya ceñcaṭaic celvar
the Lord with a red caṭai on which the beautiful flowers of common caung, cerunti, vilvam, dhatura flowers and koṉṟai flowers, are shining.
(1) vēṅkai poṉmalar ār virai taru kōyil veṅkuruvuḷ mēvivīṟṟiruntār
sat majestically and gladly in veṅkuru which has a temple where the golden coloured flowers of the east Kino tree are abundant and spread their fragrance.
[[Variant reading: (1) vēṅkai poṉmalarā viraitaru]]
{1:75}__9+
{$} āṟuṭaiccaṭai em aṭikaḷaik kāṇa
to see our Lord who has a river on his caṭai.
ariyoṭu piramaṉum aḷappataṟkāki
Ari and Piramaṉ trying to fathom his nature.
cēṟiṭait tikaḻ vāṉiṭaippukkum celavu aṟattavirntaṉar
the Lord was the cause for completely giving up their journey by digging the earth and flying in the bright sky.
eḻiluṭaittikaḻ veṇṇīṟuṭaik kōla mēṉiyar
has a beautiful form on which the white sacred and beautiful ash is shining.
neṟṟik kaṇṇiṉar
has an eye on the forehead.
viṇṇavar kaitoḻutu ētta
when the residents of heaven were praising him by worshipping with joined hands.
vēṟu emai āḷavirumpiya vikirtar
the Lord who is different from the world desired to admit me into his grace in a quite different manner.
veṅkuruvuḷ mēvi vīṟṟiruntār
sat majestically and gladly in Veṅkuru.
{1:75}__10+
{$} pāṭuṭaikkuṇṭar cākkiyar camaṇar payiltarum maṟavurai viṭṭu
Being far beyond the unrighteouss precepts which are repeated often by camaṇar and the low cākkiyar who know only suffering
aḻakāka ēṭuuṭai malarāḷ poruṭṭu vaṉtakkaṉ ellai il vēḷviyait takarttu aruḷ ceytu
Bestowing his grace after destroying the sacrifice which had no specified time and performed by Takkaṉ for the sake of the lady who is as tender as a flower having petals arranged beautifully.
kāṭi�ṭuk kaṭi nāykalantu uṭaṉcūḻakkaṇṭavar veruvuṟa viḷittu
making a sound to make people who saw him to be frightened, and to be surrounded on the sides by the frightening dogs joining together in the forest.
veytāya vēṭuṭaik kōlam virumpiya vikirtar
the god who is different from the world and who desired the form of a cruel hunter.
veṅkuruvuḷ mēvi vīṟṟiruntār
sat majestically and gladly in Veṅkuru.
{1:75}__11+
{$} viṇiyal vimāṉam virumpiya perumāṉ
the god who desired to stay in the vimāṉam touching the sky.
veṅkuruvuḷ mēvi vīṟṟiruntārai
and who sat majestically with gladness in veṅkuru.
naṇṇiya nūlaṉ
one who had the knowledge of the Vētam-s which came to him of their own accord.
ñāṉacampantaṉ naviṉṟa
composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ.
ivvāymoḻinalam miku pattum
all these ten verses which have excellent beauty and truthful words.
paṇ iyalpākap pattimaiyālē pāṭiyum āṭiyum payilavallōrkaḷ
those who are capable of repeating and singing them dancing out of extasy with devotion, according to the paṇ natural to them.
viṇṇavar vimāṉam koṭuvara ēṟi
ascending the fabulous self-moving serial van brought by the celestials.
viyaṉ ulaku āṇṭu vīṟṟiruppavar
will rule over that extensive world of heaven and be care-free.

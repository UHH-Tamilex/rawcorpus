{5:14}__1+
{$} palapattarkaḷ
many devotees.
pācam oṉṟu ilarāy
not having even the stightest attachments to worldly things.
vācam nāḷmalar koṇṭu aṭivaikalum vaḻipaṭum
worship dily his feet with fragrant flowers that blossom in the early morning.
īcaṉ emperumāṉ iṭaimarutiṉil pūcampuṉalāṭa nām pukutum
we shall go to bathe in the star pūcum, in iṭimarutu of Civaṉ who is our master (tippūcam is the big annual festival in iṭimarutu)
{5:14}__2+
{$} vāṉavar, muṉikaḷ maṟaiyiṉ muṟaiyiṉāl nāḷ malar koṇṭu aṭi vaḻipāṭucey iṟaivaṉ
the Lord whose feet the celestials and sages worship, according to the rules lid down in Vētam-s, with flowers that blossom in the morning.
emperumāṉ
our chief.
iṭaimarutiṉil uṟaiyum īcaṉai uḷkum eṉ uḷḷamē
my mind will think of Civaṉ who dwells in Iṭimarutu.
{5:14}__3+
{$} koṉṟai mālaiyum kūviḷam mattamum ceṉṟu cērat tikaḻcaṭaivaittalaṉ
one who placed on his shining caṭi garlands of koṉṟi, leaves of beal and datura flowers; all of them to reach the caṭi and be close to each other.
eṉṟum entaipirāṉ iṭaimarutiṉai naṉṟukai toḻuvār viṉainācamē
the karmams of those who worship with joined hands always iṭimarutu of our father and chief, to get good things, will be destroyed.
{5:14}__4+
{$} emmai āḷum iṭaimarutaṉ kaḻal
the feet of Civaṉ in iṭimarutu who admits us as his protege.
immaivāṉavar celvam viḷaittiṭum
will give us in this birth itself the riches of the celestials.
(1) ammaiyēl piṟavittuyar nīttiṭum
will remove the sufferings of being born in the next world.
cemmaiyē toḻuvār viṉai cintumē
will destroy the Karmams of those who worship them in the proper manner.
[[Variant reading: (1) ammaiyē piṟa]]
{5:14}__5+
{$} vaṇṭu aṇaintaṉa vaṉṉiyum koṉṟaiyumkoṇṭu (1)aṇinta caṭaimuṭik kūttaṉār
the dancer who has adorned his caṭi coiled into a crown with koṉṟi flowers and indian mesquit leaves on which bees settle!
eṇticaikkum iṭai marutā eṉa
if we say, Lord in iṭimarutu who is the master for the eight directions!
mēlaiviṉaikaḷ viṇṭupōyaṟum
our accumulated Karmams done in previous births will lose their grip on us and will be destroyed completely.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṇainta]]
[[ This verse is found in the next tirukkuṟuntoki with slight changes]]
{5:14}__6+
{$} ēṟuatuēṟum iṭaimarutu īcaṉār
Civaṉ in iṭimarutu who rides on a bull.
kūṟuvār viṉaitīrkkum kuḻakaṉār
the youth who removes the karmams of those who prise him
āṟuceñcaṭaivaitta aḻakaṉārkku
on behalf of the beautiful god who placed on his red caṭi the river (Kaṅki)
ūṟiūṟi
steeped in thoughts about him
eṉ uḷḷam urukum
my heart will melt.
{5:14}__7+
{$} viṇṇuḷārum (1) virumpappaṭupavar
Civaṉ is liked by the celestials who are in heaven.
maṇṇuḷārum (2) matikkappaṭupavar
and is held in high esteem by the people of this world.
eṇṇiṉār
is in the thoughts of all. (eṇṇiṉ ār can also be added to iṭaimarutu then the meaning will be iṭimarutu which is uppermost in the thoughts of all).
poḻilcūḻ iṭaimarutiṉai naṇṇiṉārai naṇṇā, viṉai nācamē
the evils that are the result of Karmams will not approach those who reach iṭimarutu surrounded by gardens; they will be destroyed.
[[variant readings: (1)virumpappaṭumavar (2) matikkappaṭumavar]]
{5:14}__8+
{$} ventaveṇpoṭippūcum vikirtaṉār
Civaṉ who is different from the world and smears himself with the well-burnt white holy ash.
kantamālaikaḷ cūṭum karuttaṉār
the chief who adorns himself with fragrant garlands (karuttaṉār may also be interpreted as one who is in the thoughts of people; in the first meaning it is karttaṉār
in the secone it is karuttu aṉ ār)
entai eṉ iṭai marutiṉil īcaṉaiccintaiyāl niṉaivārviṉaitēyum
the Karmams of those who think of Civaṉ in my iṭimarutu and our father in their minds, will become weak gradually.
{5:14}__9+
{$} vētam ōtum viricaṭai aṇṇalār
the chief with a spreading caṭi, who chants the vetams.
pūtam pāṭaniṉṟu āṭum puṉitaṉār
the pure Civaṉ who dances standing when the pūtams sing.
ētam tīrkkum iṭai marutāeṉṟu pātam ēttap paṟaiyum nam pāvamē
our sins will become weak if we prise the feet saying, the Lord in iṭimarutu who removes all sufferings.'
{5:14}__10+
{$} kaḷiyiṉum kaṭṭi paṭṭa karumpiṉum, paṉimalarkkuḻal pāvai nallāriṉum taṉimuṭikavittu āḷum araciṉum, taṉ aṭaintārkku iṭaimarutaṉ iṉiyaṉ
the Lord in iṭimarutu is sweeter than fruits, than the jaggery that is got from sugar-cane, than the beautiful ladies who adorn their tresses of hir with cool flowers and than the rule over the whole world being the sole morarch, wearing a crown; to those who have approached him.
{5:14}__11+
{$} muṟṟilā maticūṭum mutalvaṉār
the first cuse, Civaṉ, who wear on his head a crescent.
oṟṟiṉārmalaiyāl arakkaṉ muṭi
gently pressed down the heads of arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ) with the mountin.
eṟṟiṉār koṭiyār iṭaimarutiṉaip paṟṟiṉāraip paṟṟā viṉaipāvaṉē
Karmams and sins will not catch hold of those who have as their support iṭimarutu belonging to Civaṉ who has on his flag the figure of a bull. (ēṟṟiṉār koṭiyār has become shortened as eṟṟiṉār to suit rhyme).

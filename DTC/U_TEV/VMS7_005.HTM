{$} ōṇakāntaṉ taḷiuḷīrē
Lord who is ōṇakāntaṉ taḷi.
neyyum pālum tayirum koṇṭu (1) nittal pūcaṉai ceyyaluṟṟār kaiyil oṉṟum kāṇam illai; kaḻal aṭitoḻutu uyyiṉ allāl
unless they receive something by worshipping with hands joined your feet wearing Kaḻal, there is not even a single coin in the hands of those who worship you with ghee, milk and curd.
kāṇam an ancient gold coin.)
[[oṉpatu kāp poṉṉum nūṟāyiram kāṇamum koṭuttu (patiṟṟuppattu, 60, Patikam); kāṇam ilieṉak kaiyutirk kōṭalum, kāṇam palavum kainniṟai koṭuppa, kāṇam peṟṟēṉ kaṭuntuyar (maṇimēkali, 16-10, 23-48, 54); kāṇam-paḻaṅkācu (civakacintāmani, 1117, nacciṉārkkiṉiyar)]]
aivar koṇṭu iṅku āṭṭaāṭi āḻkuḻippaṭṭu (2) aḻuntuvēṟku uyyumāṟu oṉṟu aruḷic ceyyīr
you do not show me a way to save myself who is sinking in the deep pit of sufferings dancing to be when being harassed by the five organs of sense and wandering about
[[aivar-aintu is spoken as personal class of beings, out of anger.]]
[[VAR: Variant reading: (1) nittam (2) aḻuntuvēṉukku]]

aṭikēḷ
God!
(1) peṟṟa pōḻtum peṟātapōḻtum pēṇiumkaḻal ēttuvārkaḷ maṟṟu ōrpaṟṟu ilar eṉṟu iraṅki
taking pity on those who prise your feet with love whether they receive anything from you or not and thinking that they have no their support.
matiyuṭaiyavar ceykai (2) ceyyīr
you do not do anything which people endowed with intellect do.
aṟṟapōḻtum alantapōḻtum āvaṟkālattu
in times of misfortune when one has no money and suffering on account of that (āpati has become āvaṟ in caṇti).
oṟṟivaittu iṅku uṇṇalāmō
can we lead our lives by mortgaging you?

vallatu ellām colli varmai vāḻttiṉālum vāytiṟantu oṉṟu illai eṉṉīr uṇṭum eṉṉīr
neither do you not say that I have money to give though we prise you saying to the ulmost of our ability.
emmai āḷvāṉ (1) nīriruppatu eṉa
how is it that you can demand service from us?
pal (ai) ukka paṭutalaiyil pakal ellām pōy palatirintu (2) iṅku
in this world, wandering for alms during the whole of the day time holding a deed skull from which the teeth have fallen.
ollaivāḻkkai oḻiyamāṭṭīr
you do not give up the house-holders life, quickly.

{$} ōṇakāntaṉtaḷiyuḷīrē
see 1st verse.
kūṭikkūṭittoṇṭar taṅkaḷ koṇṭapāṇi kuṟaipaṭāmē āṭippāṭi
dancing and singing without any fault when your devotees gather many times.
aḻutunekku aṅku aṉpuṭaiyavarkku iṉpam ōnīr
you do not think of doing good to those who love you weeping and their hearts melting becoming tender.

even if I become weary wandering in many places searching for you often.
cittam eṉpāl vaikkamāṭṭīr
you do not bestow on me your audience.
ōṭippōkīr
you do not leave the temple and go else where.
paṟṟum tārīr
you do not give any support to me who comes to your temple and sing your prises.

valaiyam vaitta kūṟṟam mīvāṉ vantuniṉṟa vārttai kēṭṭu
Having heard the news of the god of death holding a noose standing above in the sky.
(1) cilai amaitta cintaiyālē tiruvaṭi toḻutu (2) uyyiṉ allāl
except thinking of saving myself by worshipping with joined hands with a mind that you gave me as strong as the stone.
(3) kalai amaitta kāmacceṟṟak kurōta lōpa matapavaruṭaiya(1) ulai amaittu iṅku oṉṟa māṭṭēṉ
I would not be united with you having destroyed the six internal enemies of men, the six emotions namely Kāmam Ceṟṟam, Kurōtam, lopam, matam, varuta (desire, rancour, wrath, miserliness, arrogance and malice, which are mentioned in philosophical works)

the Lord who is in ōṇakāntāṉ tāḷi and who rides on a bull that has the nature of being discarded.
āvaṇam ceytu āḷum koṇṭu
fabricating a bond of slavery and admitting ārūraṉ as his protege.
araitukiloṭu paṭṭu vīkki
tying a cotton cloth and silk cloth as dress in the waist at that time.
kōvaṇam mēṟkoṇṭa vēṭam kōvaiyāka ārūraṉ coṉṉa pāva(ṇ)ṇat tamiḻ pattum vallārkku
to those who can sing the ten tamiḻ verses which are in the form of pā, describing and arranging in his songs in an order the form in which he stood with only a loin-cloth when he did the service of prising him with songs, approaching him according to his command.

the sins committed by them will gradually vanish.

{1:49}__1+
{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu
poṉ akalam
in the beautiful chest
pōkam ārnta pūṇ mulaiyāḷoṭum pākam ārtta paiṅkaṇveḷḷēṟṟu aṇṇal, paramēṭṭi
the god in the most exalted place, the chief who has a white bull of greenish eyes and who has as a half a lady with breasts wearing ornaments, and giving immense pleasure.
ākam ārtta tōl uṭaiyaṉ
has a dress of a skin tied in the chest.
kōvaṇa āṭaiyiṉ mēl nākam ārtta nam perumāṉ mēyatu
is the place desired by our Lord who has tied a cobra on the dress of a loin-cloth.

{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu
tōṭu uṭaiya kātu uṭaiyaṉ
Civaṉ wears a women's ear-ring in one ear
tōl uṭaiyaṉ
has a dress of a skin
tolaiyāp pīṭu uṭaiya pōr viṭaiyaṉ
has a fighting bull which has greatness that does not become extinct.
peṇṇum ōrpāl uṭaiyaṉ
has a lady also on one half.
ēṭu uṭaiyamēl ulakōṭu ēḻkaṭalum cūḻnta nāṭu uṭaiya namperumāṉ mēyatu
is the place desired by our Lord who has this world surrounded by the seven seas, in addition to the upper worlds which are piled one above the other.
{1:49}__3+
{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu
āṉmuṟaiyāl veṇṇīṟu āṟṟa āṭi
having bathed profusely in the holy white ash which is prepared from the cow according to rules.
āyiḻai ōrpāl muṟaiyāl vaittapātam pattarpaṇintu ētta
the pious people pay obeisance to, and praise, the feet of Civaṉ who placed a lady of choice ornaments on one side as she is in the relation of wife to him.
māṉ maṟiyum veṇ maḻuvum cūlamum paṟṟiyakai nāṉmaṟaiyāṉ namperumāṉ mēyatu
is the place desired by our Lord who revealed the four vētams and who holds in his hand a young deer, battle-axe whose edge is which because of sharpness, and a trident.
{1:49}__4+
{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu.
pulkavallavārcaṭaimēl pūmpuṉalpeytu ayalē malkavallakoṉṟaimālai matiyōṭu uṭaṉ cūṭi
pouring the shining water on the long caṭai which can hold everything, and wearing by its side a garland of koṉṟai of increasing fragrance in addition to the crescent.
palkavallatoṇṭartam poṟpāta niḻaṟcēra nalkavalla namperumāṉmēyatu
is the place desired by our Lord who can grant the refuge of his golden feet for those devotees who are capable of increasing in numbers.

{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu.
ēṟutāṅkiūrtipēṇi
desiring the bull as a vehicle and a flag.
bearing a dancing cobra on the head which supports a river and a beautiful crescent.
is the place desired by our Lord who bears the fragrance of the rows of koṉṟai garlands worn on the chest where sacred thread is resting and holy ash is smeared.

{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu.
tiṅkaḷ uccimēl viḷaṅkum tēvaṉ
the god on whose crown of head the crescent is shining.
imaiyōrkaḷ eṅkaḷ ucci em iṟaivaṉ eṉṟu aṭiyē iṟaiñca
the celestials who do not wink pay obeisance saying our Lord who stays on the crowns of our heads!
taṅkaḷ ucciyāl vaṇaṅkum taṉ aṭiyārkaṭku ellām naṅkaḷ ucci namperumāṉ mēyatu
is the place desired by our Lord to whom his devotees bow their heads and say he is our Lord who stays on our heads.

{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu.
veñcuṭarttī aṅkai ēnti
holding a fire which is hot and brilliant.
viṇkoḷ muḻavu atira
the muḻavu to resound as the thunder in the cloud.
pōy añcu iṭattu ōr āṭalpāṭal pēṇuvatu aṉṟiyum
in addition to desiring dancing and singing in the place which all fear, having gone there.
ceñcaṭaikku ōr tiṅkaḷ cūṭi
having adorned the red caṭai with a crescent.
tikaḻtaru kaṇṭattuḷḷē nañcu aṭaitta nam perumāṉ mēyatu
is the place desired by our Lord who placed in his shining neck the poison.
{1:49}__8+
{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu.
ciṭṭam ārtta mummatilum cilaivaraittī ampiṉāl cuṭṭu māṭṭi
destroying having burnt the brilliant three forts with an arrow having in its tip fire, fixed in the bow of a mountain.
paṭṭam ārnta ceṉṉiyiṉmēl pālmatiyam cūṭi
wearing on the head which has a plate of gold a crescent as white as milk.
naṭṭam āṭum namperumāṉ mēyatu
is the place desired by our Lord who dances.
{1:49}__9+
{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu.
uṇṇal ākā nañcu uṇṭu uṭaṉē kaṇṭattu oṭukki
having consumed the poison which could not be consumed by any other person and immediately subdued it by stationing it in the neck.
aṇṇal ākā aṇṇal nīḻal āraḻal pōl uruvam eṇṇal ākā uḷviṉai eḷka valittu iruvar naṇṇal ākā namperumāṉmēyatu
is the place of our Lord who cannot be approached by the two (Māl and Piramaṉ) to be bashful though they were determined to find the head and feet, thinking the form of hot fire of which was unbearable eminent and brilliant, as the result of the Karmam for which no reason could be attributed.

{$} naḷḷāṟu
naḷḷāṟu.
mācumeyyar, maṇṭaittērar kuṇṭar kuṇam ilikaḷ pēcum pēccaimey eṉṟu anneṟi cellaṉmiṉ
(people of this world!) do not follow that path of the low people without character, camaṇar, whose bodies are dirty, and tēvar who hold a begging bowl, believing their teaching as truth.
mūcuvaṇṭu ār koṉṟaicūṭi
wearing koṉṟai flowers on which swarming bees hum noisily.
mummatiḷum uṭaṉē nācam ceyta namperumāṉ mēyatu
is the place desired by our Lord who destroyed the three forts simultaneously.
{1:49}__11+
{$} naṇpu nallār malku kāḻi ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kāḻi where good people of friendly nature are on the increase.
taṇpuṉalum veṇpiṟaiyum tāṅkiya tāḻcaṭaiyaṉ nalla paṇpu naḷḷāṟu ēttu pāṭal pattum ivai vallār
those who are able to recite all the ten verses in praise of naḷḷāṟu with good nature of Civaṉ having caṭai hanging down who has on it cool water and white crescent.
uṇpunīṅki vāṉavarōṭu ulakil uṟaivār
will dwell in heaven along with the celestials as the Karmam destined to be enjoyed in this birth are removed.

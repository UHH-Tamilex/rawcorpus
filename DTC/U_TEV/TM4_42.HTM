• patikam: [4:42]
  • talam: tirut turutti
  • (()): tiru nērici
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.5

*1
poruttiya kurampitaṉṉip poruḷ _eṉak karutavēṇṭā;
_irutti _eppōtum neñcuḷ, _iṟivaṉi, _ēttumiṉkaḷ!
_oruttiyip pākam vittu _aṅku _oruttiyic caṭiyil vitta
turutti _ama cuṭariṉāṉit toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*2
cavitaṉic ceytu vāḻvāṉ calattuḷē _aḻuntukiṉṟa
_ivi _oru poruḷum _alla; _iṟivaṉi _ēttumi(ṉ)ṉō!
_avipurammūṉṟum _eytum _aṭiyavarkku _aruḷicceyta
cuviyiṉi, turuttiyāṉi, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*3
_uṉṉi _eppōtum neñcuḷ _oruvaṉi _ēttumi(ṉ)ṉō!
kaṉṉiyi _orupāl vittu, kaṅkiyic caṭiyuḷ vittu,
poṉṉiyiṉ naṭuvutaṉṉuḷ pūm puṉal polintu tōṉṟum
tuṉṉiya turuttiyāṉit toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*4
_ūṉtali valiyaṉ_āki, _ulakattuḷ _uyirkaṭku _ellām
tāṉ talippaṭṭu niṉṟu, cār kaṉal_akattu vīḻa,
vāṉtalit tēvar kūṭi, vāṉavarkku _iṟivā! _eṉṉum
tōṉṟali, turuttiyāṉi, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*5
_uṭaltaṉik kaḻikkal_uṟṟa _ulakattuḷ _uyirkaṭku _ellām
_iṭartaṉik kaḻikka vēṇṭil _iṟivaṉi _ēttumi(ṉ)ṉō!
kaṭaltaṉila nañcam _uṇṭu kāṇpu _aritu_āki niṉṟa
cuṭartaṉi, turuttiyāṉi, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*6
_aḷḷalik kaṭakka vēṇṭil _araṉiyē niṉimiṉ, nīrkaḷ!
poḷḷalaḻ _ikkāyamtaṉṉuḷ puṇṭarīkattu _irunta
vaḷḷali, vāṉavarkkum kāṇpu _aritu_āki niṉṟa
tuḷ _alit turuttiyāṉi, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*7
pātiyil _umiyāḷtaṉṉip pākamā vitta paṇpaṉ;
vētiyaṉ _eṉṟu colli viṇṇavar virumpi _ēttac
cāti _ām catumuka(ṉ)ṉum cakkarattāṉum kāṇāc
cōtiyi, turuttiyāṉi, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*8
cām maṉivāḻkki_āṉa calattuḷē _aḻuntavēṇṭā;
tūmam nal _akilum kāṭṭit toḻutu _aṭi vaṇaṅkumi(ṉ)ṉō!
cōmaṉic caṭiyuḷ vittut tol-neṟipalavum kāṭṭum
tū maṇal-turuttiyāṉit toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*9
kuṇṭarē, camaṇarputtar; kuṟi _aṟiyātu niṉṟu
kaṇṭatē karutuvārkaḷ karuttu _eṇṇātu _oḻimiṉ, nīrkaḷ!
viṇṭavar puraṅkaḷ _eytu viṇṇavarkku _aruḷkaḷceyta
toṇṭarkaḷtuṇiyiṉāṉit turutti nāṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*10
piṇṭattik kaḻikka vēṇṭil pirāṉiyē pitaṟṟumiṉkaḷ!
_aṇṭattik kaḻiya nīṇṭa _aṭal _arakkaṉtaṉ _āṇmi
kaṇṭu _ottuk kālvira(l)lāl _ūṉṟi, mīṇṭu _aruḷicceyta
tuṇṭattut turuttiyāṉit toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

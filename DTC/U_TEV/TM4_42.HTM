• patikam: [4:42]
  • talam: tirut turutti
  • (()): tiru nēricai
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.5

*1
poruttiya kurampaitaṉṉaip poruḷ _eṉak karutavēṇṭā;
_irutti _eppōtum neñcuḷ, _iṟaivaṉai, _ēttumiṉkaḷ!
_oruttiyaip pākam vaittu _aṅku _oruttiyaic caṭaiyil vaitta
turutti _ama cuṭariṉāṉait toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*2
cavaitaṉaic ceytu vāḻvāṉ calattuḷē _aḻuntukiṉṟa
_ivai _oru poruḷum _alla; _iṟaivaṉai _ēttumi(ṉ)ṉō!
_avaipurammūṉṟum _eytum _aṭiyavarkku _aruḷicceyta
cuvaiyiṉai, turuttiyāṉai, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*3
_uṉṉi _eppōtum neñcuḷ _oruvaṉai _ēttumi(ṉ)ṉō!
kaṉṉiyai _orupāl vaittu, kaṅkaiyaic caṭaiyuḷ vaittu,
poṉṉiyiṉ naṭuvutaṉṉuḷ pūm puṉal polintu tōṉṟum
tuṉṉiya turuttiyāṉait toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*4
_ūṉtalai valiyaṉ_āki, _ulakattuḷ _uyirkaṭku _ellām
tāṉ talaippaṭṭu niṉṟu, cār kaṉal_akattu vīḻa,
vāṉtalait tēvar kūṭi, vāṉavarkku _iṟaivā! _eṉṉum
tōṉṟalai, turuttiyāṉai, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*5
_uṭaltaṉaik kaḻikkal_uṟṟa _ulakattuḷ _uyirkaṭku _ellām
_iṭartaṉaik kaḻikka vēṇṭil _iṟaivaṉai _ēttumi(ṉ)ṉō!
kaṭaltaṉila nañcam _uṇṭu kāṇpu _aritu_āki niṉṟa
cuṭartaṉai, turuttiyāṉai, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*6
_aḷḷalaik kaṭakka vēṇṭil _araṉaiyē niṉaimiṉ, nīrkaḷ!
poḷḷalaḻ _ikkāyamtaṉṉuḷ puṇṭarīkattu _irunta
vaḷḷalai, vāṉavarkkum kāṇpu _aritu_āki niṉṟa
tuḷ _alait turuttiyāṉai, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*7
pātiyil _umaiyāḷtaṉṉaip pākamā vaitta paṇpaṉ;
vētiyaṉ _eṉṟu colli viṇṇavar virumpi _ēttac
cāti _ām catumuka(ṉ)ṉum cakkarattāṉum kāṇāc
cōtiyai, turuttiyāṉai, toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*8
cām maṉaivāḻkkai_āṉa calattuḷē _aḻuntavēṇṭā;
tūmam nal _akilum kāṭṭit toḻutu _aṭi vaṇaṅkumi(ṉ)ṉō!
cōmaṉaic caṭaiyuḷ vaittut tol-neṟipalavum kāṭṭum
tū maṇal-turuttiyāṉait toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*9
kuṇṭarē, camaṇarputtar; kuṟi _aṟiyātu niṉṟu
kaṇṭatē karutuvārkaḷ karuttu _eṇṇātu _oḻimiṉ, nīrkaḷ!
viṇṭavar puraṅkaḷ _eytu viṇṇavarkku _aruḷkaḷceyta
toṇṭarkaḷtuṇaiyiṉāṉait turutti nāṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

*10
piṇṭattaik kaḻikka vēṇṭil pirāṉaiyē pitaṟṟumiṉkaḷ!
_aṇṭattaik kaḻiya nīṇṭa _aṭal _arakkaṉtaṉ _āṇmai
kaṇṭu _ottuk kālvira(l)lāl _ūṉṟi, mīṇṭu _aruḷicceyta
tuṇṭattut turuttiyāṉait toṇṭaṉēṉ kaṇṭa_āṟē!

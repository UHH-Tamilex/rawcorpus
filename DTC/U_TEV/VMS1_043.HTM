{1:43}__1+
{$} vaṭam tikaḻ meṉmulaiyāḷaip pākamāka matittu
placing with esteem as a half a lady with soft breasts wearing eminent clins of necklace.
taṭantirai cēr puṉal mātaittāḻcaṭaivaitta caturar
the skillful person who placed on his hanging caṭi a lady who is in the form of water which has big waves.
iṭam tikaḻ muppurinūlar
wears a sacred thread of three strands shining on his left shoulders.
tuṉpamoṭu iṉpam(atu) ellām kaṭantavar
who is above all pins and pleasures.
kātalil vāḻum kaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārē
that god is one who dwells with love in the great hill of Kaṟkuṭi.
{1:43}__2+
{$} aṅkam ōr (1) āṟoṭu vēḷvi āṉa
contining six aṅkams of the vētam-s and five kinds of sacrifices;
[[PP: aivēḷvi āṟaṅkam āṉār pōlum (appar, tiruvīḻimiḻi, (4) 4).]]
arumaṟai nāṉkum paṅkam il pāṭalōṭu āṭal pāṇi payiṉṟa paṭiṟar
the terrific person who practised dancing measuring time with hands, together with singing faultlessly the four abstruse vētams.
caṅkam atu ār kuṟamātar tam kaiyiṉ maintarkaḷ tāvik kaṅkuliṉ māmati paṟṟum kaṟkuṭi malaiyārē
is in the great hillock Kaṟkuti where the childrem jump from the hands of the crowd of Kuṟavar women and catch hold of the moon.
[[Variant reading: (1) āṟuṭai]]
{1:43}__3+
{$} nīrakalam taru ceṉṉi nīṭiya mattamum vaittu
placing long datura flowers on the head which has the expanse of the water of the Kaṅki.
tārakaiyiṉ oḷi cūḻnta taṇmati cūṭiya caivar
the civar who wore the cool crescent surrounded by the light of the stars.
pōr akalam taru vēṭar puṉattiṭai iṭṭa viṟakil kār akiliṉ pukai vimmum kaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārē
is in the great hillock of Kaṟkuti where the smoke of the black eagle-wood tree which was placed along with the firewood in the upland dry fields by the hunters who have chests fit for fighting;
[[So is the nature of the ignorant hunters to set fire to such valuable trees such as sandal wood to prepare the land to sow millet (tiṉai)]]
[[PP: naṟuntaṇtakaram vakuḷam ivaṟṟai veṟumputal vēṇṭātu, vēṇṭi eṟintu uḻutu, centiṉai vittuvār,
mēkam tōy cāntam vicai, timicu kāḻ akil, nākam tōy nākam eṉa ivaṟṟaip pōka, eṟintu uḻuvār
(tiṇimāli nūṟṟimpatu, 24, 28)]]
{1:43}__4+
{$} oruṅku aḷi, nī iṟaivā eṉṟu umparkaḷ ōlamiṭak kaṇṭu
seeing the tevar to cry for protection saying, Lord! you save us all at the same time
iruṅkaḷam ālaviṭattai iṉṉamutu (1) uṇṇiya īcar
Civaṉ who consumed the poison to fill the big neck as he would eat the nectar
[[uṇṇiya-uṇṭa: this is a rare usage; the reading uṉṉiya seems to be a better one; Civaṉ transformed the poison into nectar when he thought of it to reach his hand.]]
[[PP: muṉṉum kaṭuviṭam tēvar ceṉṟu irappa nañcai niṉaittalum kaimmalark kaṇ vantiruntatākaliṉ karutappaṭum vevviṭam eṉiṉum amaiyum
(Kōviyār, 236, Perāciriyar)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) uṉṉiya]]
maruṅku aḷi ārpiṭivāyil vāḻvetiriṉ muḷaivāri, karuṅkaḷiyāṉai koṭukkum kaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārē
is in the great hillock of Kaṟkuṭi where the black and male elephant which has must, puts into the mouth of the loving female elephant standing near its side, gathering abundantly the shoots of the prospering bamboo.
{1:43}__5+
{$} pōr malitiṇcilaikoṇṭu
carrying a strong bow which is prominent in battle.
pūta kaṇam puṭaicūḻa
and being surrounded by the crowds of pūtam.
pārmali vēṭu uru āki
assuming the form of a hunter who lives in this world.
paṇṭu oruvarkku aruḷceytār
he who granted the weapon pācupetam to one (aruccuṉaṉ) long ago.
ērmali kēḻal kiḷaitta iṉṉoḷi māmaṇi eṅkum, kārmali vēṭar kuvikkum kaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārē
is in the great hillock of Kaṟkuṭi where the very dark coloured hunters heap the big precious stones of pleasing brightness which were dug up by exceedingly beautiful pige.
{1:43}__6+
{$} ulantavar eṉpu aṇintu
having worn the bones who died.
ūr iṭupiccaiyar āki
and collecting the alms given by the people of the village.
vilaṅkal vil veṅkaṉalālē mūeyil vēva muṉintār
Civaṉ grew angry and burnt the three forts by the hot fire issuing from the bow of a mountin.
nalam taru cintaiyar āki
having minds always thinking of good things.
nāmali mālaiyiṉālē kalantavar kātalil vāḻuṅkaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārē
is in the great hillock of Kaṟkuṭi where people who concentrated their thoughts on god with the help of the garlands of verses which are always on the months, live with desire.
{1:43}__7+
{$} māṉiṭam ārtarukaiyar
Civaṉ has a deer on his left hand.
māmaḻu ārum valattar
has a big battle-axe on his right hand.
ūṉ iṭai ārtalai ōṭṭil uṇkalaṉāka ukantār
He felt glad to receive the alms using the skull as a eating vessel, which has flesh in some places.
tēṉiṭai ārtaru cantiṉ tiṇ ciṟaiyāl tiṉai vitti
having sown in the farms which have fences of strong sandal-wood trees in which there are honey-combs.
kāṉiṭai vēṭar viḷaikkum kaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārē
is in the great hilloc of Kaṟkuṭi where the hunters grow millet in the forests.
{1:43}__8+
{$} vāḷ amar vīram niṉainta irāvaṇaṉ māmalaiyiṉkīḻttōḷ amarvaṉtalai kuṉṟattolviral ūṉṟu tuṇaivar
the companion who pressed down the big toe to discrease the strength of the shoulders and strong heads which plan battles, under the big mountin, of Irāvaṇaṉ who thought only of his valour in the sword-fight.
tāḷ amarvēytalai paṟṟittāḻkariviṭṭa vicaipōykkāḷam (atu) ār mukilkīṟum kaṟkuṭimāmalaiyār
is in the great hillock of Kaṟkuṭi where the tops of the bamboos which are pulled down and released and which are fixed in their feet rend the sable clouds by their force.
{1:43}__9+
{$} taṇṭu amar tāmaraiyāṉum, tāvi immaṇṇai aḷantukoṇṭavaṉum aṟivu oṇṇākkoḷkaiyar
Civaṉ who had the nature of not being found out by one who measured by world by pacing out distance and one who is seated on a lotus having a long stalk.
veḷviṭai ūrvar
and who rides on a white bull.
vaṇṭu icai āyiṉapāṭa nīṭiyavārpoḻil nīḻalkaṇṭu amar māmayil āṭum kaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārē
is in the great hillock of Kaṟkuṭi where seeing the shade of the long gardens of tall trees the peacocks which are sitting there dance when the bees are humming;
[[cf. ālimāmukil atirtara aruvarai akkurauṟa mukaṭu ēṟippīli māmayil naṭam ceyyum piruti (periya tirumoḻi, tiruppiruti, 1)]]
{1:43}__10+
{$} mūttuvar āṭaiyiṉārum mūcukaṭuppoṭiyārum nāttuvar poym moḻiyārkaḷ nayam ilarā mativaittār
Civaṉ took it into his mind that the buddhist who wear saffron robes well soaked in myrtle dye and amaṇar who thickly coat their bodies with gall-nut powder, to make them utter falsehood which is distasteful to the tongue, and to be without love.
ēttu uyar pattarkaḷ cittar iṟaiñca avar iṭar ellām kāttavar
when pious people and mystics who are great by prising god, bow before him, Civaṉ who affords protection from all sufferings,
kāmarucōlaikkaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārē
and is in the great hillock of Kaṟkuṭi which has gardens of attractive beauty.
{1:43}__11+
{$} kāmaru vārpoḻil cūḻum kaṟkuṭi māmalaiyārai
about Civaṉ who is in the great hillock of Kaṟkuti surrounded by tall and attractive gardens.
nāmaruvaṇpukaḻkkāḻi nalam tikaḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ
composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ, eminent by the knowledge of Civaṉ and who is a native of Kaḻi of fame obtined by liverality which is in the mouth of all.
pāmaru centamiḻ mālaipattu ivai pāṭavallārkaḷ
who are able to sing the ten chaste Tamiḻ verses which have the distinguishing sound and which are like flower garlands.
pūmalivāṉavarōṭum poṉ ulakiṟ polivārē
will be shining in the heaven along with the original celestials who have excessive brilliance.

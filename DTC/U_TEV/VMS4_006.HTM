[[This decade is composed as the speech of the foster-mother about her dughter who had fallen in love with god.]]

{4:6}__1+
{$} vaṉa pavaḷavāytiṟantu
opening her lips which are like beautiful coral.
vāṉavarkkum tāṉavaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
(my dughter) says he bestows grace upon celestial beings also.
cīṉa pavaḷattiṇtōḷmēl cērntu ilaṅku veṇṇīṟṟaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he has smeared shining white ash which is suited to the strong shoulders resembling in colour red coral.
aṉapavaḷamēkalaiyōṭu
joining with a lady wearing on the waist a jewelled girdle of strands made of coral, and who is like a swan by her git.
appālaikku appālāṉ
He is farther than the farthest things (we conceive of)
kaṉapavaḷam cintum kaḻippālaic cērvāḷaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the god who is Kaḻipāli where the sea scatters the heavy coral?
{4:6}__2+
{$} vaṇṭu ulavu koṉṟai vaḷar puṉcaṭaiyāṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says he has golden matted locks on which the koṉṟi circling around which bees hum, is thriving
[[puṉcaṭai is a corruption of poṉcaṭai]]
viṇṭu alarntu nāṟuvatu ōr veḷḷerukkam nāḷ malar uṇṭu eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that there is a flower of white yarcum blossomed in the early morning, which spread its fragrance by releasing its petals, and blossoming
[[PP: cf.veḷḷerukkaravam viravum caṭai
(appar, puḷḷirukkuvēḷur, tirukkuruntoki, 1)]]
[[This is different from the ordinary yarkum found everywhere.]]
ayalē uṇṭu tōṉṟuvatu ōr uttariyappaṭṭu uṭaiyaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he has an upper garment of silk by the side which really exists and appears (to the eye)
kaṇṭu ayalē tōṉṟum kaḻippālaic cērvāṉaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the god who is Kaḻippāli where the plant called Kaḻimuḷḷi, grows on all sides?
[[kaṇṭalayalē: have become kaṇṭayal]]
[[As Kaḻippāli is very near the sea coast, it is described as a maritime tract;
kaṇṭal is a thorny plant of the maritime tract.]]
{4:6}__3+
{$} piṟantu iḷaiya tiṅkaḷ empemmāṉ muṭimēlatu eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that a crescent just born and young is on the head of our great one.
avaṉ niṟam, niṟam kiḷarum kuṅkumattiṉ mēṉi eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says his colour is like that of the shining saffron.
maṟamkiḷar vēṟkaṇṇāḷ
the dughter who has eyes comparable to the vēl which is infuriated with valour.
maṇicēr miṭaṟṟavaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says he has a neck resembling sapphire.
kaṟaṅku ōtammalku kaḻippālaic cērvāṉaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the god in Kaḻippāli where the roaring waves are always increasing?
{4:6}__4+
{$} irumpu ārnta kulattaṉ ēntiya ōrveṇ maḻuvaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says he has a trident made of iron and a white maḻu held in his hand (maḻu;
here refers to fire which is white in colour becuse of its intense heat.)
karumpu ārmalark koṉṟai, cuṇṇa veṇṇīṟṟavaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he has koṉṟi flowers on which bees hum, and a fine power of sacred white ash.
perumpālaṉāki
in addition to being an aged youth,
piññaka vēṭattaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he has a disguised form on which peacock's feathers are tucked in.
karumpāṉalpūkkum kaḻippālaic cērvāṉaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the god in Kaḻippāli where blue nalumbo (karuṅkuvaḷai)
flowers blossom.
{4:6}__5+
{$} paḻiilāṉ
He is without reproach
pukaḻ uṭaiyaṉ
He has fame
pālnīṟṟaṉ
He smears ash as white as milk.
eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says so
viḻi ulām peruntaṭaṅkaṇ iraṇṭu alla; mūṉṟu uḷavē eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that the big and spacious eyes in which the eye-balls move are not two; but there are three.
cuḻi ulām varu kaṅkaitōynta caṭaiyavaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he has matted locks on which Kaṅki which came moving with eddies, has settled
ulām kaḻicūḻnta kaḻippālaiccērvāṉaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the god in Kaḻippāli which is surrounded by backwaters which are always moving?
{4:6}__6+
{$} paṇ ārnta vīṇaipayiṉṟa viralavaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he is playing with his fingers on the vīṇi which can produce paṇ (paṇ is the melody which has all the seven notes)
eṇṇār puram eritta entai perumāṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he is the great one that begat my father (grandfather) and who burnt the cities of the enemies.
paṇ ārmuḻavu atirappāṭaloṭu āṭalaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he dances singing when the muḻavu which is made ready to be played to produce a high pitch sound.
kaṇ ār pūñcōlaikkaḻippālaic cērvāṉaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the god in Kaḻippali which has flower-gardens feasting to the eyes?
{4:6}__7+
{$} mutirum caṭaimuṭimēl mūḻkum iḷanākam eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that a young serpent is hidden in the matted locks which are tied as a crown and which is very old.
atu kaṇṭu ataṉ arukē tōṉṟum iḷamatiyam eṉkiṉṟāḷ
after seeing that, she says that a young crescent appears near that.
caturveṇpaḷiṅkuk kuḻaikātil miṉṉiṭumē eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that superior and white ear-ring made of marble is shining in the ear
[[kuḻai: is worn on the right ear.]]
katirmuttam cintum kaḻippālaic cērvāṉaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the god in Kaḻippāli in which the sea scatters pearls which emit rays.
{4:6}__8+
{$} ōrōtam ōti
singing songs that are obstacles to get alms
[[ōrōtam may be taken as a corrupted form of uvarōtam; uvakai becomes ōkai]]
ulakam palitirivāṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he wanders in the world for alms.
nīr ōtam ēṟa nimirpuṉcaṭaiyāṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that when the water of the Kaṅki rises, he has a golden malted lock which is stright.
{4:6}__9+
{$} vāṉ ulām tiṅkaḷ vaḷar puṉ caṭaiyāṉē eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that one who has matted locks of hir golden in colour on which the crescent which moves in the sky, is thriving
ūṉ ulām veṇtalai koṇṭu
holding a white skull which has flesh.
ūr ūr palitirivāṉē eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says one who is wandering from village to village begging alms.
tēṉ ulām koṉṟai tiḷaikkum tirumārpaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says one has a beautiful chest in which koṉṟi from which honey flows, is staying permanently.
kāṉ ulām cūḻnta kaḻippālaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the Lord in Kaḻippāli where fragrance is always spreading, and around which devotees go?
{4:6}__10+
{$} aṭarppu ariya irāvaṇaṉai aruvaraikkīḻ aṭarttavaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that the Lord pressed down under the mountin which is difficult to remove, Irāvaṇaṉ who could not be defeated by anybody else.
cuṭarp periya tirumēṉic cuṇṇa veṇṇīṟṟavaṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he has a fine powder of ash on his big, beautiful and shining form.
maṭal periya āliṉkīḻ aṟam nālvarkku aṉṟu uraittāṉ eṉkiṉṟāḷ
she says that he explined aṟam to four sages under the banyan tree of big leaves.
[[According to grammar maṭal should be used to denote leaves of trees which have a hard exterior]]
kaṭal karuvi cūḻntakaḻippālaic cērvāṉaik kaṇṭāḷ kol
Did she see the Lord in Kaḻippali which is surrounded by the sea and which is the support of the world?

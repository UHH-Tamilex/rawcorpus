{6:14}__1+
{$} nallūr emperumāṉār
our Lord in Nallūr.
niṉaintu urukum aṭiyārai naiyavaittār
made the hearts of devotees to melt further which are already melting thinking of him.
nillāmē tīviṉaikaḷ nīṅkavaittār
he caused the sins to leave me without staying with me.
ciṉam tiruku kaḷiṟṟu urivaip pōrvai vaittār
he covered himself in the skin of an elephant of severe anger.
ceḻumatiyiṉ taḷir vaittār
placed on his caṭai a thriving crescent as a tender leaf.
ciṟantu vāṉōr iṉam turuvi maṇi makuṭattu ēṟattuṟṟa iṉamalarkaḷ pōtu aviḻntu
the several kinds of flowers being in an advanced stage and having infolded their petals, which are close to each other and which are adorned in the crowns set with precious stones by the celestials in large numbers, searching for them and obtaining them.
matuvāyppilki
as they cause to flow honey being honey itself.
naṉainta aṉaiya tiruaṭi eṉ talaimēl vaittār
placed his such great feet which are wet on my humble head which is not fit for that act.
nalla āṟē
this is a good act of grace
[[Civaṉ fulfilled Nāvukkaracar's request to place god's feet on his head, in Nallur (periya purānam, tirunāvukkaracar purāṇam, 194-196)]]

our Lord in nallur.
vil aruḷi varupuruvattu oruttipākam poruttāki
being united with the half of a lady who has eye-brows on which she placed her grace, to be compared to her eye-brows.
viricaṭaimēl aruvi vaittār
kept on his losse caṭai a stream (of Kañkai)
kal aruḷi varicilaiyā vaittār
had as a bow with bands bestowing his grace on the mountain (mēru).
ūrāk kayilāyamalai vaittār
had the mountain Kayilāyam as his place.
kaṭavūr vaittār
had as his place Kaṭavūr (Kaṭavūr is in Tanjore Dt. where Civaṉ destroyed the god of death, yamaṉ to protect mārkaṇṭēyaṉ)
col aruḷi aṟam nālvarkku aṟiyavaittār
expounded moral instruction to four sages to make them understand, revealing the vetams (col here means vētam; ākupeyar;
[[virittāṉai nālvarkku vevvēṟu vētaṅkaḷ (appar, tirukkacci ēkampam, (1)8)]]
cuṭu cuṭalaip poṭi vaittār; tuṟavi vaittār
has sacred ash of the cremation ground where corpses are burnt, to smear; placed the path of renunciation to soul who deserve it.
[[tuṟavi-tuṟavu; vayanta mālaiyum mātavi tuṟavikku ayarntu meyvāṭiya aḻiviṉaḷ (maṇimēkalai, 2-10-11); tuṟavu tuṟaviyeṉa niṉṟāṟ pōla aḷavu aḷaviyeṉa niṉṟatu (Kōvaiyār 10, pērāciriyar's commentary)]]

our Lord in nallūr.
viṇ iriyum tiripuraṅkaḷ eriya vaittār
set ablaze the three cities from which the celestials had to flee to save their lives.
toḻuvārkku viṉai aṟavaittār
made the Karmams to completely leave those who worshipped him.
tuṟavi vaittār

kaṇ eriyāl kāmaṉaiyum poṭiyā vaittār
reduced even Kāmaṉ to become ash by the fire issuing from his frontal eye.

made the heart which is in the form of a fragrant lotus flower as his seat.
kayilai vaittār
has Kayilai as his great residence.
tiṇ eriyum taṇpuṉalum uṭaṉē vaittār
placed the strong fire not easily extinguished, and cool water in one and the same holy body.

placed on my head which is unfit for that act, his holy feet which cannot be easily approached by the celestial living in heaven and praised by them, worshipping him towards the direction he dwells.

our Lord in nallūr.
piṇi uṟṟu ulavu ulakattu eḻumai vaittār
made the seven kinds of births in this world where diseases are spreading greatly.
uyir vaittār
made the souls to be born in those births.
uyir cellum katikaḷ vaittār
created heaven and hell into which the souls enter. (maṟṟu)
amarar kaṇam vaittār
created the group of immortals (maṟṟu: expletive).
amarar kāṇā maṟai vaittār
revealed thevētams which even the immortals could not know.
kuṟai matiyam vaḷaravaittār
made the decreased moon to increase in size (this story is told Kanatapuraṇam, canties cāpappaṭalam)
ceṟṟam mali ārvamoṭu kāmalōpam iṟavāta neṟi vaittār
placed the path in which anger, intense fascination, lust, miserlinens are not prominent (other facults not included in this must be taken along with these; kāmam, kurōtam, lōpam, mōkam, matam, māṟcariyam these are called internal enemies; matam arrogance; māṟcariyam envy).
tuṟavivaittār
placed renunciation.
naṟṟavar cēr tiruvaṭi eṉ talaimēl vaittār
place his holy feet which people who perform penance to be rid of the cycle of births and deaths, on my head which is not fit for that act.
nalla āṟē

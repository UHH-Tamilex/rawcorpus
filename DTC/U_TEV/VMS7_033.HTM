{7:33}__1+
{$} (Devotees!)
namakku aṭikaḷ ākiya aṭikaḷ (ē)
the god who is God to us.
pāṟu tāṅkiya kāṭarō
Does he live in the cremation ground which supports kites?
paṭutalaiyarō
Does he hold in his hand a dead skull?
malaippāvai ōrkūṟu tāṅkiya kuḻakarō
is he the youth who carries as one half the beautiful dughter of the mountin?
kuḻaik kātarō
does he wear on one ear a men's ear-ring?
kuṟaṅkōṭṭu iḷa ēṟu tāṅkiya koṭiyarō
has he a flag on which is drawn a young bull with short horns?
cuṭupoṭiyarō
does he smear the sacred ash well burnt?
viḷaṅkumpiṟai, āṟu tāṅkiya caṭaiyarō
does he bear on his head a shining crescent and a river?
{7:33}__2+
{$} numakku icaiyumā niṉaittu ēttuvīr
devotees who prise Civaṉ according to your ability, thinking of him!
iṭṭitāka vantu uraimiṉ (ō)
come near me and tell me.
namakku aṭikaḷ ākiya aṭikaḷ (ē):
see 1st verse.
kaṭṭi vāḻvatu nākamō
is it a cobra that your master ties on the neck, hands and waist?
caṭaimēlum nāṉu karantaiyō
is it indian globe thistle that he has on his caṭi also?
paṭṭi ēṟu ukantu ēṟarō
does he ride with joy on a bull that is straying
[[paṭṭi ēṟu may also mean a bull that is tethered in the cattle-shed.]]
paṭu veṇtalaiyil pali koṇṭu vantu aṭṭi āḷavum kiṟparō
has he the strength to serve the alms that he receives in the dead white skull and to admit us into his grace?

mikac ceyyarō

veḷḷai nīṟṟarō

[[PP: tiripuram tīmaṭuttu eriyakkaṇṭu iraṅkātu kaikoṭṭi niṉṟu āṭutaliṉ, koṭumai uṭaittātal nōkkik koṭuṅkoṭṭi' eṉṟu peyar kūṟiṉār (Do atiyārkkunallār);]]

devotees!
paṇintu ēttiṉēṉ
I prised you, submitting my homage.
paṭicey nīrmaiyil
imitating the actions of sincere devotees.

I having no perfection.
tirunāvalūraṉ
a native of tirunāvalūr.
vaṉappakai appaṉ
the father of vaṉappaki.
vaṉ toṇṭaṉ
with the title of vaṉtoṇtaṉ.
ceṭiyaṉ ākilum
although I may be a sinner.
tīyaṉ ākilum
and a wicked person.

see 1st verse.
tammaiyē maṉam cintikkum aṭiyaṉ ūraṉai āḷvarō
will he admit ārūraṉ who is his slave and always meditates on him only?
aruḷ paṇiyīr
please tell me about the nature of his grace.

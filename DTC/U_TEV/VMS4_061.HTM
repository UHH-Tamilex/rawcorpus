{4:61}__1+
{$} pācamum kaḻikkakillā arakkaraip paṭuttu
having destroyed the arakkar who were not able to remove even the attachments.
takka vācammikka alarkaḷkoṇṭu matiyiṉāl māl ceykōyil
the temple established by Māl (whose incarnation is Irāmaṉ) by his spiritual knowledge and worshipped by him with suitable flowers of excellent fragrance.
nēcam mikka aṉpiṉāl nīr (1) niṉṟu nāḷum tēcam mikkāṉ irunta tiruvirāmēccura(m)mē niṉaimiṉ
(people of this world!) you dily meditate upon Tiruvirāmēccuram where Civaṉ who is the effulgent light stays, for a long time, with love born out of excessive affections.
[[Variant reading: 1.ceṉṟu]]
{4:61}__2+
{$} (1) muṟṟiṉa nāḷkaḷ eṉṟē muṭippatē kāraṇamāy uṟṟavaṉ pōrkaḷālē uṇarvu ilā arakkartammaic ceṟṟamāl ceyta kōyil tiruvirāmēccurattaip paṟṟi
having as a refuge Tiruvirāmēccuram which was established by Māl who destroyed the arakkar who had no intelligence, by severe wars which were the reason for destroying them, as their days came to an end.
neñcē
my mind!
paṭarcaṭai īcaṉpālē nī paravu
you pray to the Lord of the universe who has a spreading caṭi.
[[Variant reading:1. muṟṟiṉaṉāḷkaḷeṉṟu]]
{4:61}__3+
{$} kaṭaliṭai malaikaḷ tammāl aṭaittu
blocking the sea with mountins.
māl karumam muṟṟi
Māl after having finished the destruction of arakkar.
tiṭaliṭaic ceyta kōyil tiruvirāmēccurattait toṭaliṭaivaittu nāvil nāṉ cuḻalkiṉṟēṉ
I am distressed in my mind, keeping in my speech having contact with Tiruvirāmēccuram established in the rised ground.
tūymai iṉṟi
without purity.
uṭaliṭai niṉṟum pērā aivar āṭṭuṇṭu
being tossed by the five senses which are in my body and never leave it.
{4:61}__4+
{$} kuṉṟupōl tōḷuṭaiya kuṇam ilā arakkartammaikkoṉṟu
having killed the arakkar who had shoulders as strong as hills and had no good qualities.
pōrāḻi ammāl vēṭkaiyāl ceytakōyil tiruvirāmēccuramē ceṉṟu nī toḻutu uy (kaṇṭāy)
save yourself by going to Tiruvirāmēccuram which was established by his desire to remove the sin of killing the arakkar by the beautiful Māl who had a disc used in war, and by worshipping it with joined hands.
naṉṟu (1) pōl neñcamē
my mind which appears to be good!
nī naṉmaiyai (2) aṟitiyāyil
if you know what is good to you
[[naṉmaiyai aṟitiyāyil kōyil irāmēccuram toḻutu uy is the syntactical link.]]
[[variant readings:1.pēr 2. yuṟutiyāyil]]
{4:61}__5+
{$} vīram mikku
being excessively valourous.
eyiṟu kāṭṭi
showing his deformed and curved teeth.
viṇ uṟa nīṇṭa arakkaṉ kūram mikkavaṉaic ceṉṟu koṉṟu
killing the arakkaṉ who was as tall as to touch the sky and who was exceedingly cruel, having gone to his place.
uṭaṉ kaṭaṟpaṭuttu
and having sunk his body in the sea.
tīram mikkāṉ irunta tiruvirāmēccurattaik kōram tavattāl mikkār (1) kūṭuvār kuṟippu uḷār(ē)
those who have performed severe penance, and reach Tiruvirāmēccuram where Irāmaṉ of excessive valour stayed, will get their intentions fulfilled
[[kōram may also the mantiram akōram]]
[[Variant reading: 1.kūṟuvār]]
{4:61}__6+
{$} maṭaneñcamē
my ignorant mind!
(1) nammiṉ valam mikkār ār eṉṟa av arakkar kūṭi
the arakkar who thought, ``who are those that are superior to us in strength'', joining together.
pōrvalam ceytu
as they fought a severe battle.
[[ceytu -- ceyya]]
mikkupporutavartammai vīṭṭi
killing those who attacked him in multitude.
tērvalam ceṟṟa māl cey tiruvirāmēccurattai
in Tiruvirāmēccuram which was established by Māl who destroyed the strength of the charioteers.
nī ceñcaṭai entaipālē cēr
you reach the side of our father who has a red caṭi.
[[Variant reading: 1. ārvalaṉammiṉ mikkār, ārvalar nammiṉ mikkār]]
{4:61}__7+
{$} vākkiṉāl iṉpu uraittu
speaking pleasant and good words in their speech.
vāḻkilārtammai ellām pōkkiṉāl puṭaittu
attacking all the arakkar who were not able to lead a good life, with weapons that took out their lives.
avarkaḷ uyirtaṉai uṇṭu
destroying their lives
māltāṉ tēkkunīr ceyta kōyil tiruvirāmēccurattai nōkkiṉāl (1) vaṇaṅkuvārkaḷ nōyviṉai nuṇukum (aṉṟē)
the diseases and acts of those who look at by the physical and mental eyes Tiruvirāmēccuram the temple established by Māl in the dam across the sea, will be shattered to pieces.
[[Variant reading:1. vaṇaṅkuvārkku]]
{4:61}__8+
{$} palavum nāḷ tīmai ceytu
having committed sinful deeds for many days.
pārtaṉmēl kuḻumi vantu
coming to fight joining in large numbers in this earth.
kolaivilār koṭiyarāya arakkaraikkoṉṟu vīḻtta cilaiyiṉāṉ ceyta kōyil tiruvirāmēccurattait talaiyiṉāṉ vaṇaṅkuvārkaḷ
those who bow their heads before Tiruvirāmēccuram which was established by Irāmaṉ who with his bow killed and made the arakkar fall on the ground who were cruel and had cruel bows.
tāḻvar ām
will have humility
tavam atu ām (ē)
that is equal to penance.
{4:61}__9+
{$} neñcamē
my mind!
kōṭimātavaṅkaḷ ceytu, kuṉṟiṉārtammai ellām vīṭavē cakkarattāl eṟintu
throwing the disc and killing the arakkar who were deficient in good conduct though they performed penance amounting to crores of years to lose their lives.
piṉ aṉpu koṇṭu tēṭi māl ceyta kōyil tiruvirāmēccurattai nāṭi nī vāḻ
you live desiring earnestly Tiruvirāmēccuram, the temple established by Māl having sought for it with love, after killing the arakkar.
naṉṉeṟi ākum(aṉṟē)
that is the right path for you.
{4:61}__10+
{$} vaṉkaṇṇar vāḷ arakkar vāḻviṉai oṉṟu aṟiyār puṉkaṇṇar āki niṉṟu pōrkaḷ ceytārai māṭṭi
cusing the cruel arakkar who were hard-hearted and did not know even a little of the acts for the longevity of their lives having only meanness, to die, fighting many battles standing firm.
ceṅkaṇmāl ceyta kōyil tiruvirāmēccurattait taṅkaṇāl eyta vallār tāḻvarām talaivaṉpāl (ē)
those who are able to approach the temple Tiruvirāmēccuram established by Māl of red eyes will stay with the master.
{4:61}__11+
{$} varaikaḷ ottē uyarnta maṇimuṭi arakkarkōṉai viraiya muṟṟu(um) aṟa oṭukki
having subdued quickly and completely the victory of the King of the arakkar who had crowns set with gems and which were similar to mountins in their height.
mīṇṭu māl ceyta kōyil
the temple which was established by Māl on his return.
tiraikaḷ muttāl vaṇaṅkum tiruirāmēccurattai uraikaḷ pattāl uraippār aṉpiṉāl uḷkuvār (ē)
those who are able to recite the ten verses on Tiruvirāmēccuram which the waves worship by throwing pearls, will cherish Civaṉ with love.

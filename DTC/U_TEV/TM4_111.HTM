*1
viṭaiyum viṭaip perum pākā! _eṉ viṇṇappam: vemmaḻuvāḷ-
paṭaiyum, paṭai_āy niraitta palpūtamum, pāy pulittōl-
_uṭaiyum, muṭaittalaimālaiyum, mālaip piṟai _otuṅkum
caṭaiyum, _irukkum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*2
viṅcat taṭavarai veṟpā! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
caṅkak kalaṉum, cari kōvaṇamum, tamarukamum,
_antip piṟaiyum, _aṉal vāy _aravum, viravi _ellām
cantittu _irukkum carakkō_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*3
vīntārtalaikalaṉ _ēntī! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
cāntu _āya ventatavaḷa-veṇnīṟum, takuṇiccamum,
pūntāmaraimēṉi, puḷḷi_uḻai-māṉ_ataḷ, pulittōl,
tāmtām _irukkum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*4
veñcamar vēḻattu _uriyāy! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
vañcamā vanta varupuṉalkaṅkaiyum, vāṉmatiyum,
nañcam mā nākam, nakuciramālai, nakuveṇtalai,
tañcamā vāḻum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*5
vēlaik-kaṭalnañcam _uṇṭāy! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
kālaṟ kaṭantāṉ _iṭam kayilāyamum, kāmar koṉṟai,
mālaip piṟaiyum, maṇi vāy _aravum, viravi _ellām
cālak kiṭakkum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*6
vīḻiṭṭa koṉṟai_amtārār! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
cūḻ_iṭṭu _irukkum nal cūḷāmaṇiyum, cuṭalai nīṟum,
_ēḻ _iṭṭu _irukkum nal _akkum, _aravum, _eṉpu, _āmai_ōṭum,
tāḻ_iṭṭu _irukkum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*7
viṇṭār purammūṉṟum _eytāy! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
toṇṭu_āṭiya toṇṭu _aṭippoṭi-nīṟum, toḻutu pātam
kaṇṭārkaḷ kaṇṭirukkum kayilāyamum, kāmar koṉṟait-
taṇtār _irukkum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*8
viṭu paṭṭi _ēṟu _ukantu _ēṟī! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
koṭukoṭṭi, kokkarai, takkai, kuḻal, tāḷam, vīṇai, montai,
vaṭu viṭṭa koṉṟaiyum, vaṉṉiyum, mattamum, vāḷ_aravum,
taṭukuṭṭam _āṭum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*9
veṇtiraik kaṅkai vikirtā! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
kaṇṭikai pūṇṭu, kaṭicūttirammēl kapālavaṭam,
kuṇṭikai, kokkarai, koṉṟai, piṟai, kuṟaḷpūtappaṭai,
taṇṭi vaittiṭṭa carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*10
vētitta vemmaḻu_āḷī! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
cōtit tirukkum, nal cūḷāmaṇiyum, cuṭalai nīṟum,
pātippiṟaiyum, paṭutalaittuṇṭamum, pāy pulittōl,
cātittu _irukkum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*11
vivantu _āṭiya kaḻal _entāy! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
tavantāṉ _eṭukkat talaipattu _iṟuttaṉai; tāḻ pulittōl
civantu _āṭiya poṭi-nīṟum, ciramālai cūṭi niṉṟu
tavamtāṉ _irukkum carakku_aṟaiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

• patikam: [4:111]
  • talam: POTU
  • (()): carakkaṟi tiru viruttam
  • VMS: Vol.15, p.90

*1
viṭiyum viṭip perum pākā! _eṉ viṇṇappam: vemmaḻuvāḷ-
paṭiyum, paṭi_āy niritta palpūtamum, pāy pulittōl-
_uṭiyum, muṭittalimāliyum, mālip piṟi _otuṅkum
caṭiyum, _irukkum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*2
viṅcat taṭavari veṟpā! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
caṅkak kalaṉum, cari kōvaṇamum, tamarukamum,
_antip piṟiyum, _aṉal vāy _aravum, viravi _ellām
cantittu _irukkum carakkō_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*3
vīntārtalikalaṉ _ēntī! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
cāntu _āya ventatavaḷa-veṇnīṟum, takuṇiccamum,
pūntāmarimēṉi, puḷḷi_uḻi-māṉ_ataḷ, pulittōl,
tāmtām _irukkum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*4
veñcamar vēḻattu _uriyāy! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
vañcamā vanta varupuṉalkaṅkiyum, vāṉmatiyum,
nañcam mā nākam, nakuciramāli, nakuveṇtali,
tañcamā vāḻum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*5
vēlik-kaṭalnañcam _uṇṭāy! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
kālaṟ kaṭantāṉ _iṭam kayilāyamum, kāmar koṉṟi,
mālip piṟiyum, maṇi vāy _aravum, viravi _ellām
cālak kiṭakkum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*6
vīḻiṭṭa koṉṟi_amtārār! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
cūḻ_iṭṭu _irukkum nal cūḷāmaṇiyum, cuṭali nīṟum,
_ēḻ _iṭṭu _irukkum nal _akkum, _aravum, _eṉpu, _āmi_ōṭum,
tāḻ_iṭṭu _irukkum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*7
viṇṭār purammūṉṟum _eytāy! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
toṇṭu_āṭiya toṇṭu _aṭippoṭi-nīṟum, toḻutu pātam
kaṇṭārkaḷ kaṇṭirukkum kayilāyamum, kāmar koṉṟit-
taṇtār _irukkum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*8
viṭu paṭṭi _ēṟu _ukantu _ēṟī! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
koṭukoṭṭi, kokkari, takki, kuḻal, tāḷam, vīṇi, monti,
vaṭu viṭṭa koṉṟiyum, vaṉṉiyum, mattamum, vāḷ_aravum,
taṭukuṭṭam _āṭum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*9
veṇtirik kaṅki vikirtā! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
kaṇṭiki pūṇṭu, kaṭicūttirammēl kapālavaṭam,
kuṇṭiki, kokkari, koṉṟi, piṟi, kuṟaḷpūtappaṭi,
taṇṭi vittiṭṭa carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*10
vētitta vemmaḻu_āḷī! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
cōtit tirukkum, nal cūḷāmaṇiyum, cuṭali nīṟum,
pātippiṟiyum, paṭutalittuṇṭamum, pāy pulittōl,
cātittu _irukkum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

*11
vivantu _āṭiya kaḻal _entāy! _eṉ viṇṇappam: mēl _ilaṅku
tavantāṉ _eṭukkat talipattu _iṟuttaṉi; tāḻ pulittōl
civantu _āṭiya poṭi-nīṟum, ciramāli cūṭi niṉṟu
tavamtāṉ _irukkum carakku_aṟiyō, _eṉ taṉi neñcamē!

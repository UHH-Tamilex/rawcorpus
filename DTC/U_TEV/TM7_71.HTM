*1
yāḻippaḻittaṉṉamoḻimaṅki _orupaṅkaḷ,
pēḻic caṭimuṭimēl piṟi vittāṉ, _iṭam pēṇil---
tāḻippoḻil_ūṭē ceṉṟu pūḻittali nuḻintu
vāḻikkaṉi kūḻikkuraṅku _uṇṇum maṟikkāṭē.

*2
cikarattu_iṭi _iḷaveṇpiṟi vittāṉ _iṭam, teriyil---
mukarattu_iṭi muttiṉ(ṉ) _oḷi pavaḷattiraḷ, _ōtam,
takarattu_iṭi tāḻittiraḷ ñāḻal-tiraḷ, nīḻal,
makarattoṭu cuṟavam, koṇarntu _eṟṟum maṟikkāṭē.

*3
_aṅkaṅkaḷum maṟināṉku_uṭaṉ virittāṉ _iṭam _aṟintōm---
teṅkaṅkaḷum neṭumpeṇṇiyum paḻam vīḻ maṇal paṭappi,
caṅkaṅkaḷum _ilaṅku _ippiyum valampurikaḷum _iṭaṟi,
vaṅkaṅkaḷum _uyarkūmpoṭu vaṇaṅkum maṟikkāṭē.

*4
nari viraviya mayirtaṉṉoṭu pañca(v)vaṭi mārpaṉ,
_uri viraviya _uttamaṉ, _iṭam---_uṇaral(l)_uṟu, maṉamē!---
kuri viraviya kuli cēkarak koṇṭal-tali viṇṭa
vari purivaṉa tiri porutu _iḻintu _eṟṟum maṟikkāṭē.

*5
caṅkip paṭa niṉiyātu _eḻu, neñcē, toḻutu _ētta!
kaṅkic caṭimuṭi _uṭiyavarkku _iṭam _āvatu---paravi
_aṅkak kaṭal _aru mā maṇi _untik karikku _ēṟṟa,
vaṅkattoṭu cuṟavam koṇarntu _eṟṟum maṟikkāṭē.

*6
_aṭal viṭiyiṉaṉ, maḻuvāḷiṉaṉ, _alarāl _aṇi koṉṟip
paṭarum caṭimuṭi _uṭiyavarkku _iṭam_āvatu---paravik-
kaṭal_iṭi_iṭi kaḻi _arukiṉil kaṭi nāṟu taṇ kiti
maṭal_iṭi_iṭi veṇkuruku _eḻu maṇinīr maṟikkāṭē.

*7
muḷivaḷar_iḷamati _uṭiyavaṉ, muṉ ceyta valviṉikaḷ-
kaḷi kaḷintu _eṉi _āḷal(l)_uṟu kaṇṭaṉ, _iṭam---cennel
vaḷi viḷivayal kayal pāytaru kuṇa, vār maṇal, kaṭalvāy
vaḷi vaḷiyoṭu calañcalam koṇarntu _eṟṟum maṟikkāṭē.

*8
nalam periyaṉa, curumpu _ārntaṉa, namkōṉ _iṭam _aṟintōm;
kalam periyaṉa cārum kaṭal kari porutu _iḻi---kaṅkic-
calam puri caṭimuṭi _uṭiyavarkku _iṭam_āvatu---paravi
valampuriyoṭu calañcalam koṇarntu _eṟṟum maṟikkāṭē.

*9
kuṇṭāṭiyum camaṇ_āṭiyum kuṟṟu_uṭukkiyartāmum
kaṇṭār kaṇṭa kāraṇam(m)_avi karutātu kitoḻumiṉ---
_eṇtōḷiṉaṉ, mukkaṇṇiṉaṉ, _ēḻ_iciyiṉaṉ, _aṟukāl
vaṇṭu _āṭu taṇpoḻil cūḻntu _eḻu maṇinīr maṟikkāṭē!

*10
pār _ūrpala puṭi cūḻ vaḷavayal nāvalarvēntaṉ
vār _ūr vaṉamuliyāḷ_umi paṅkaṉ maṟikkāṭṭi
_ārūraṉa tamiḻmālikaḷ pāṭum(m) _aṭittoṇṭar
nīr _ūrtaru nilaṉōṭu _uyar pukaḻ _ākuvar, tāmē.

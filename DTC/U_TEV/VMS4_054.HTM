tiruppukalūraṉīrē
Lord in Tiruppukalūr?
pakaittiṭṭār puraṅkaḷ mūṉṟum pāṟi nīṟāki vīḻap pukaittiṭṭa tēvar kōvē
the king of the tevar, who burnt the three forts of the enemies to fall down as powder and be scattered!
poṟi ilēṉ uṭalam taṉṉuḷ
in the body of mine who has no wisdom.
akaittiṭṭu aṅku ataṉai nāḷum aivar koṇṭu āṭṭa āṭi
acting to the commands of the five senses which have mastery over me to make me do their bidding, dily oppressing my body (aivar - aintu is called as aivar in anger).
tikaittiṭṭēṉ
I became perplexed
ceyvatu eṉṉē
what can I do?

kai eri cūlam ēntum kaṭavuḷai niṉaiya māṭṭēṉ

ceppaṉē tikaḻum mēṉit tiruppukalūraṉīrē
Civaṉ in tiruppukalūr who has a shinning red body!
muppatum muppattāṟum muppatum iṭu kurampai appar pōl
just like the masters of the body in which thirty-six tattuvams and sixty-tattuvikaṇkal dwell.
aivar vantu atu taruka itu viṭu eṉṟu oppavē naliyaluṟṟāl
if the five sense enter into it and afflict me with equal strength commanding give this; leave-this
uyyumāṟu aṟiyamāṭṭēṉ
I am utterly incapable of knowing the way to save myself.

uṉ aṭi paravum ñāṉam celavu ilēṉ

pārttaṉukku aruḷkaḷ ceyta paramaṉē
supreme god who granted many boons to pārttaṉ.
paravuvārkaḷ tīrttamē tikaḻum poykait tiruppukalūraṉīrē
the Lord in tiruppukalūr which has devotees who prise god, and has natural tanks eminent by their holy water.
kāttilēṉ iraṇṭum mūṉṟum
I have not kept under control the five senses.
kalviyēl eṉpāl illai
if it is education I do not possess it.
aṭimai taṉṉuḷ vāyttilēṉ
I did not have the good fortune to become one of your slaves.
vāymaiyāl tūyēṉ allēṉ
I am not pure in my speach.

nīrumāy
Civaṉ being water.
tīyum āki nilaṉum āy vicumpum āki ēr uṭaik katirkaḷ āki
being fire, and also the earth and the space and the beautiful sun and moon.
imaiyavar iṟaiñca 1. niṉṟu
remining to be worshipped by the celestials who do not wink.
(2) āyvataṟku ariyar āki
being very difficult to investigate
aṅkaṅkē āṭukiṉṟa tēvarkkum tēvar āvar tiruppukalūraṉār
Civaṉ in tiruppukalūr is above the deities that do his bidding in their spheres of activities.
[[Variant reading: 1. niṉṟār 2. ārvataṟku]]

meyyuḷē viḷakkai ēṟṟi
having lit the lamp of spiritual wisdom.
vēṇṭu aḷavu uyarattūṇṭi
and trimming it to the required height.
uyvatu ōr upāyampaṟṟi ukakkiṉṟēṉ
I try to rise high catching hold of a means of saving myself.
ukavā vaṇṇam aivarai akattē vaittīr
but you placed within me the five senses, so that I could not rise high.
avarkaḷē cālavaliyar
they themselves are very powerful.
ceyvatu oṉṟu aṟiyamāṭṭēṉ
I am incapable of doing anything that is to be done.

aruvarai tāṅkiṉāṉum
Māl who held the mountin as an umbrella, which is very difficult to be uprooted.
arumaṟai ātiyāṉum
and the creator who knew the abstruse vētams.
iruvarum aṟiyamāṭṭār īcuṉār
īcaṉ who could not be known by both of them.
ilaṅkai vēntaṉ karuvarai eṭutta ñāṉṟu kaṇvaḻi kuruti cōrat tiruviral ciṟitu vaittār tiruppukalūraṉār
Civaṉ in tīruppukalūr gently fixed his toe on that day when the king of ilaṅki lifted the great mountin, kayili, the blood to trickle down through the eyes.

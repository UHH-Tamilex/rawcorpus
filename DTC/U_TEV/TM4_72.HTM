• patikam: [4:72]
  • talam: tiru iṉṉampar
  • (()): tiru nērici
  • VMS: Vol.4, p.65

*1
viṇṇavar makuṭakōṭi miṭinta cēvaṭiyarpōlum;
peṇ _orupākampōlum; pēṭu_ali_āṇarpōlum;
vaṇṇa māl _ayaṉum kāṇā mālvari _eriyarpōlum;
_eṇ _uru _anēkarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*2
paṉṉiya maṟiyarpōlum; pāmpu _ari _uṭiyarpōlum;
tuṉṉiya caṭiyarpōlum; tū mati mattarpōlum;
maṉṉiya maḻuvarpōlum; mātu _iṭam makiḻvarpōlum;
_eṉṉiyum _uṭiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*3
maṟi _oru kiyarpōlum; mātu_umi _uṭiyarpōlum;
paṟi talip piṟavi nīkkip paṇi koḷa vallarpōlum;
ceṟivu _uṭi _aṅkamāli cēr tiru_uruvarpōlum;
_eṟipuṉal caṭiyarpōlum--_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*4
viṭam mali kaṇṭarpōlum; vēḷviyi _aḻipparpōlum;
kaṭavu nal viṭiyarpōlum; kālaṉik kāyvarpōlum;
paṭam mali _aravarpōlum; pāy pulittōlarpōlum;
_iṭar kaḷintu _aruḷvarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*5
_aḷi malarkkoṉṟi tuṉṟum _avircaṭi _uṭiyarpōlum;
kaḷi mayilcāyalōṭum kāmaṉi viḻipparpōlum;
veḷi vaḷar _uruvarpōlum; veṇpoṭi _aṇivarpōlum;
_eḷiyavar, _aṭiyarkku _eṉṟum;---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*6
kaṇi _amar ciliyarpōlum; kari_uri _uṭiyarpōlum;
tuṇi _amar peṇṇarpōlum; tū maṇik kuṉṟarpōlum;
_aṇi _uṭi _aṭiyar kūṭi _aṉpoṭu malarkaḷ tūvum
_iṇi_aṭi _uṭiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*7
poruppu _amar puyattarpōlum; puṉal _aṇi caṭiyarpōlum;
maruppu _iḷa _āmi tāṅku mārpil veṇnūlarpōlum;
_uruttiramūrttipōlum; _uṇarvu_ilār puraṅkaḷmūṉṟum
_erittiṭu ciliyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*8
kāṭu_iṭam _uṭiyarpōlum; kaṭikural viḷiyarpōlum;
vēṭu_uru _uṭiyarpōlum; veṇmatikkoḻuntarpōlum;
kōṭu _alar vaṉṉi, tumpi, kokku_iṟaku, _alarnta koṉṟi-
_ēṭu, _amar caṭiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*9
kāṟiṭu viṭatti _uṇṭa kaṇṭar; _eṇtōḷarpōlum;
nīṟu _uṭi _uruvarpōlum; niṉippiṉi _ariyarpōlum;
pāṟu _uṭit tali ki _ēntip pali tirintu _uṇparpōlum;
_ēṟu _uṭik koṭiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*10
_ārttu _eḻum _ilaṅkik kōṉi _aruvari _aṭarpparpōlum;
pārttaṉōṭu _amar porūtu paṭi koṭuttu _aruḷvarpōlum;
tīrttam _ām kaṅkitaṉṉit tiruccaṭi vipparpōlum;
_ētta _ēḻ_ulakum vittār---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

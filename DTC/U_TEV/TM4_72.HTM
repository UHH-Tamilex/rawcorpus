• patikam: [4:72]
  • talam: tiru iṉṉampar
  • (()): tiru nēricai
  • VMS: Vol.4, p.65

*1
viṇṇavar makuṭakōṭi miṭainta cēvaṭiyarpōlum;
peṇ _orupākampōlum; pēṭu_ali_āṇarpōlum;
vaṇṇa māl _ayaṉum kāṇā mālvarai _eriyarpōlum;
_eṇ _uru _anēkarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*2
paṉṉiya maṟaiyarpōlum; pāmpu _arai _uṭaiyarpōlum;
tuṉṉiya caṭaiyarpōlum; tū mati mattarpōlum;
maṉṉiya maḻuvarpōlum; mātu _iṭam makiḻvarpōlum;
_eṉṉaiyum _uṭaiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*3
maṟi _oru kaiyarpōlum; mātu_umai _uṭaiyarpōlum;
paṟi talaip piṟavi nīkkip paṇi koḷa vallarpōlum;
ceṟivu _uṭai _aṅkamālai cēr tiru_uruvarpōlum;
_eṟipuṉal caṭaiyarpōlum--_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*4
viṭam mali kaṇṭarpōlum; vēḷviyai _aḻipparpōlum;
kaṭavu nal viṭaiyarpōlum; kālaṉaik kāyvarpōlum;
paṭam mali _aravarpōlum; pāy pulittōlarpōlum;
_iṭar kaḷaintu _aruḷvarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*5
_aḷi malarkkoṉṟai tuṉṟum _avircaṭai _uṭaiyarpōlum;
kaḷi mayilcāyalōṭum kāmaṉai viḻipparpōlum;
veḷi vaḷar _uruvarpōlum; veṇpoṭi _aṇivarpōlum;
_eḷiyavar, _aṭiyarkku _eṉṟum;---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*6
kaṇai _amar cilaiyarpōlum; kari_uri _uṭaiyarpōlum;
tuṇai _amar peṇṇarpōlum; tū maṇik kuṉṟarpōlum;
_aṇai _uṭai _aṭiyar kūṭi _aṉpoṭu malarkaḷ tūvum
_iṇai_aṭi _uṭaiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*7
poruppu _amar puyattarpōlum; puṉal _aṇi caṭaiyarpōlum;
maruppu _iḷa _āmai tāṅku mārpil veṇnūlarpōlum;
_uruttiramūrttipōlum; _uṇarvu_ilār puraṅkaḷmūṉṟum
_erittiṭu cilaiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*8
kāṭu_iṭam _uṭaiyarpōlum; kaṭikural viḷiyarpōlum;
vēṭu_uru _uṭaiyarpōlum; veṇmatikkoḻuntarpōlum;
kōṭu _alar vaṉṉi, tumpai, kokku_iṟaku, _alarnta koṉṟai-
_ēṭu, _amar caṭaiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*9
kāṟiṭu viṭattai _uṇṭa kaṇṭar; _eṇtōḷarpōlum;
nīṟu _uṭai _uruvarpōlum; niṉaippiṉai _ariyarpōlum;
pāṟu _uṭait talai kai _ēntip pali tirintu _uṇparpōlum;
_ēṟu _uṭaik koṭiyarpōlum---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

*10
_ārttu _eḻum _ilaṅkaik kōṉai _aruvarai _aṭarpparpōlum;
pārttaṉōṭu _amar porūtu paṭai koṭuttu _aruḷvarpōlum;
tīrttam _ām kaṅkaitaṉṉait tiruccaṭai vaipparpōlum;
_ētta _ēḻ_ulakum vaittār---_iṉṉampar _īcaṉārē.

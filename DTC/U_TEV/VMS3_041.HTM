{3:41}__1+
{$} karu ār kaccit tiruvēkampattu oruvā eṉṉa
if one prises Civaṉ as the unequalled Lord in Tiruvēkampam, in Kacci which is the embryo for all things.
viṉaiyē maruvā
the acts will not come near him.
{3:41}__2+
{$} mati ār kacci nati ēkampam vitiyāl ēttap pati āvārē
if people prise Ēkampam on the bank of the river, Kampi in Kacci of high esteem according to rules they will become masters
[[mati - matittal]]
{3:41}__3+
{$} kali ār kacci mali ēkampam 1. paliyāl pōṟṟa naliyā viṉaiyē
the acts will not inflict sufferings if one worships with the things mentioned for worship, ēkampam which is full of all things in Kacci of great bustle.
[[Variant reading: 1. paliyār]]
{3:41}__4+
{$} varam ār kaccip puram ēkampam, paravā ētta viravā viṉaiyē
acts will not mingle with us if we prise in the second persons and third person ēkampam in the city of eminent Kacci.
{3:41}__5+
{$} paṭam ār kacci iṭam ēkampattu, uṭaiyāy eṉṉa aṭaiyā viṉaiyē
acts good and bad will not reach us if we prise the Lord who has as his shrine ēkampam in Kacci which has flags on the storeys.
{3:41}__6+
{$} nalam ār kacci nilavu ēkampam, kulavā ēttak kalavā viṉaiyē
acts, good and bad, will not mix with us if we prise rejoicingly ēkampam which is permanent in Kacci which is full of good things.
{3:41}__7+
{$} kariyiṉ(ṉ) uriyaṉ
Civaṉ who has the skin of an elephant.
tiruvēkampaṉ
who is in tiruvēkampam.
periya puram mūṉṟu ericeytāṉē
burnt the three big cities.
{3:41}__8+
{$} ilaṅkai aracait tulaṅka ūṉṟum nalam koḷ kampaṉ ilaṅku caraṇē
the shining feet of Kampaṉ which have beauty, pressed firmly the King of ilaṅki to be agitated
[[Kampaṉ is the corruption of ēkampaṉ, the first letter being dropped.]]
{3:41}__9+
{$} maṟaiyōṉ ariyum aṟiyā aṉalaṉ neṟi ēkampam kuṟiyāl toḻumē
(People of this world!) worship with intention ēkampam which shows the right path, of the god who rose as a cloumn of fire which could not be known by Piramaṉ who had knowledge of the vētams and ari (Māl)
{3:41}__10+
{$} paṟiyāt tērar neṟiil kaccic ceṟikoḷ kampam kuṟukuvōmē
we shall go near Kampam packed with all good things, in Kacci which does not follow the path of the tērar (buddhists) who do not pluck their hirs on their heads (and amanar)
{3:41}__11+
{$} koccai vēntaṉ, kaccik kampam meccum collai naccum pukaḻē
fame will desire of its accords people who recite the words of the chief of Kacci and who prise Kampam in Kacci.
[[Variant reading: 1. paṟiyar tērar]]

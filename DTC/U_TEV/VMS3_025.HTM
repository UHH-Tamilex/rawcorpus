{3:25}__1+
{$} tiruntu tēvaṉ kuṭittēvar tēvu eytiya, aruntavattōr toḻum aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷē maruntu vēṇṭil ivai, mantiraṅkaḷ
the forms of the god who is worshipped by sages who performed severs penance, and which are taken by him who is superior to other gods in Tiruntutēvaṉkuti, are medicine if one desires it; these are mantiraṅkal
[[Forms are holy ash, uruttirākkam, and caṭi coiled into a crown.]]
purintu kēṭkappaṭum puṇṇiyaṅkaḷ ivai
these are the virtuous acts which should be heard with eagerness.
{3:25}__2+
{$} tītuil tēvaṉ kuṭittēvar tēvu eytiya, ātiantam ilā aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷē
the forms of the god superior to other deities who dwells, in tēvaṉkuti which is without any blemish, assumed by him who has no beginning and end.
vītipōkku āvaṉa
they serve as ornaments which can be worn when one circumbulates the streets.
vīṇaiyaivīṭṭu (v) vaṉ
will destroy karmams.
ōti ōrkkappaṭāpporuḷai ōrvippaṉa
help to understand clearly abstruse subjects which one cannot understand by learning with his own skill (when one reads a work and does not understand its real meaning, but if one thinks of these forms of Civaṉ he can easily understand it)
[[vīṭṭu(v)vaṉa: v is an augmentation.]]
{3:25}__3+
{$} 3. tēṉum vaṇṭum micaipāṭum tēvaṉkuṭi, āṉ añcu āṭum muṭi aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷē
the forms of the god whose head is bathed in the five products of the cow and who is in tēvaṉkuti where teṉ and vaṇtu hum like melodies.
māṉam ākku (v) vaṉ
will secure dignity to people.
mācu nīkkuvaṉa
will remove all blemishes from the mind.
vāṉai uḷkac cellum vaḻikaḷ kāṭṭu(v) vaṉa
will show the way to think about the cosmis space. (ākku (v)vaṉa; kāṭṭu (v) vaṉa
the letters within brackets are augmentation for the sake of rhythm)
{3:25}__4+
{$} puvikaḷ poṅkap puṉalpāyum tēvaṉkuṭi, avikaḷ uykkappaṭum aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷ
the beautiful forms of the god for whom offerings are made into the sacrificial fire and who is in tēvaṉkuti where water spreads so that the fields may yield a rich harvest.
cevikaḷ ārvippaṉa
help to fill the ears with good knowledge.
cintaiyuḷ cērvaṉa
enter into the mind.
kavikaḷ pāṭu(v)vaṉa, kaṇkuḷirvippaṉa
make people to compose sings; cuse pleasure to the sense of sight.
{3:25}__5+
{$} teṇṇilā veṇmati tīṇṭutēvaṉkuṭi, aṇṇal āṉēṟu uṭai aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷ
the beautiful forms of the god who has an eminent bull and who is in tēvaṉkuti where the temple seems to touch the white moon which spreads clear moonlight.
viṇulāvum neṟi
are the path to enter into heaven
vīṭukāṭṭum neṟi
are the path that shows the way to eternal bliss
maṇ ulāvum neṟi
are the path to wander happily in this world.
mayakkam tīrkkum neṟi
are the path that removes confusion of mind.
{3:25}__6+
{$} tiṅkaḷ tōyum poḻil tīṇṭutēvaṉ kuṭi, aṅkam āṟum coṉṉa aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷ
the beautiful forms of the god who revealed the six aṅkams, and who is in tēvaṉkuti which has gardens which seem to touch the moon.
paṅkam eṉṉap, paṭar paḻikaḷ eṉṉap paṭā
they cannot be called defects and cannot be thought as objects of ridicule that spread easily.
puṅkam eṉṉap paṭar pukaḻkaḷ eṉṉap paṭum
instead they will be considered as articles of excellence and as different kinds of fame that spread everywhere. (Those who wear holy ash, uruttirākkam in this birth are not people who committed in acts of reproach, and sinful acts.) (They bring such great fame to wearer to describe which words are inadequate vehicles)
{3:25}__7+
{$} tiraikaḷ poṅkap puṉal pāyum tēvaṉkuṭi araiyil veṇkōvaṇattu aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷ
the beautiful forms of Civaṉ who wears a loin-cloth in his waist and who is in tēvaṉkuti where water flows with surging waves.
karaital oṉṟum illai
there is nothing to be sid disparagingly about them.
karutavallār tamakku uraiyil ūṉam illai
there is no defect for those who can fix their thoughts on them if they dilate upon their greatness.
ulakiṉil maṉṉuvar
they will live long in this world.
{3:25}__8+
{$} tilakam ārum poḻil cūḻnta tēvaṉkuṭi, alartayaṅkum muṭiaṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷ
the beautiful forms of the god on whose head flowers are shining and who is in tēvaṉkuti surrounded by gardens which are pre-eminent
ulakam uṭkum tiṟal uṭai (ya) arakkaṉ vali vilakupūtakkaṇam veruṭṭum vēṭattiṉa
the groups of putam which are capable of making the might of arakkaṉ who had such great strength that the world feared him, can frighten things that one could be afrid of, so that they will not approach devotees.
[[Variant reading: (1) pūtaṅkaṇam]]
{3:25}__9+
{$} veṟivaṇṭu ārum poḻil tiḷaikkum tēvaṉkuṭit ticaimukaṉōṭu māl aḷakka oṇṇā vaṇṇattu aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷ
the beautiful forms of the god whose nature could not be measured by Māl, and Piramaṉ who has four faces for the four quarters, and who is tēvaṉkuti where in the gardens bees fond of fragrance are in big swarms and stay permanently there.
tuḷakkam illātaṉa
have no agitation of mind on body.
tūya tōṟṟattaṉa
they are capable of purifying the minds of those who see them.
viḷakkam ākku(v)vaṉa
make things clear. (ākku(v)vaṉa-v is augmentation)
{3:25}__10+
{$} tirumaruvum poykaicūḻnta tēvaṉkuṭi aṭikaḷ vēṭaṅkaḷ arumaruntāvaṉa
the beautiful forms of the god in Tēvaṉkuṭi surrounded by natural tanks which are frequented by the goddess of wealth, are really rare nectar.
cerumarutantuvartēr amaṇ ātarkaḷ urumaruvappaṭāt toḻumpātam uraikoḷēl
People! do not take into your minds the words of the base persons with whose bodies one cannot come into contact, people who are the ignorant amaṇar and tēvar who wear robes soaked in thick myrtle-dye.
[[I am of the opinion that the reading marutaṇṭuvar is a scribal error! it should be marutantuvar]]
{3:25}__11+
{$} māṭam ōṅkum, poḻil malku, taṇ kāḻiyāṉ, nāṭavalla tamiḻ (1) ñāṉacampantaṉ
Ñāṉacampantaṉ who has knowledge of Tamiḻ and is capable of research, and who is a native of cool Kāḻi where storeys are high and gardens are thriving.
cēṭartēvaṉ kuṭittēvar tēvaṉtaṉaip pāṭal pattum vallārkku illai ām pāvam
there will be no sins to those who are able to recite the ten verses composed by him on the exalted god superior to minor deities, in Tēvaṉkuti which is worshipped by great persons.
[[Variant reading: ñācampantaṉaṟ]]

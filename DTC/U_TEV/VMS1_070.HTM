{1:70}__1+
{$} vāṉattu uyar taṇ matitōy caṭaimēl mattamalarcūṭi
wearing datura flowers on the caṭi on which the cool crescent which is eminent in the sky stays.
tēṉ ottaṉa meṉmoti māṉviḻiyāḷ tēvipākamāka
the tēvi who speaks soft words which are sweet as honey and has looks like the deer, being on one half.
kāṉattu
in the cremation ground.
iravil erikoṇṭu āṭum kaṭavuḷ
the god who dances holding fire in the hand at night.
ulaku ētta
to be prised by the people of the world.
ēṉattiraḷ vantu iḻiyum cāral īṅkōy malaiyār
is in īṅkōymali in whose slopes herds of wild hog descend down.
{1:70}__2+
{$} cūlappaṭai oṉṟu ēnti
holding a trident in his hand.
iravil cuṭukāṭu iṭamākak kōlac caṭaikaḷ tāḻak, kuḻal yāḻ montai koṭṭavē, pāl ottaṉaiya moḻiyāḷ kāṇa āṭum paramaṉār
the supreme god, Civaṉ, who dances at night to be witnessed by the lady whose words are as sweet as milk, when monti (a musical instrument) is beaten to measure time and lute is played and flute is blown, as the catis hang low, having the cremation ground as his stage.
ēlattoṭu nal ilavam kamaḻum īṅkōy malaiyār
is in Īṅkōymali where cardamoms and cloves spread their sweet smell.
[[ilavam is the shortened form of ilavaṅkam]]
{1:70}__3+
{$} kaṇkoḷ nutalār
Civaṉ has an eye on his forehead.
kaṟaikoḷmiṭaṟṟār
has poison in his neck.
kariyiṉ uritōlār
covers himself with a skin flayed from an elephant.
viṇkoḷ maticēr caṭaiyār
has on his caṭi a crescent that shines in the sky.
viṭaiār koṭiyār
has a banner on which the form of a bull is drawn.
peṇ kol tirumārpil veṇṇīṟupūcum pemmāṉ
the god who besmears white sacred ash in the chest where there is a lady.
emai āḷvār
admits us into his grace.
eṇkum ariyum tiriyum cāral īṅkōy malaiyār
is in Īṅkōymali in whose slopes bears and lions are roaming.
{1:70}__4+
{$} maṟaiyiṉ icaiyār
sings the musical vētam (camavētam)
neṟimeṉ kūntal malaiyāṉ makaḷōṭum
in company with the dughter of the mountin, who has soft and curly tresses of hir.
kuṟaiveṇpiṟaiyum puṉalum nilavum kuḷirpuṉcaṭaitāḻa
when the cool caṭi of golden colour on which a white crescent decreased in size due to the curse of takkaṉ, and water shine, are hanging low.
paṟaiyum kuḻalum kaḻalum ārppappaṭukāṭṭu eriāṭum iṟaivar
the Lord who dances in the fire, when the armour of the leg, flute and drum make a loud sound.
ciṟaivaṇṭu aṟaipūñcāral īṅkōy malaiyār
is in Īṅkōy mali in whose beautiful slopes bees with wings hum loudly.
[[makaḷōṭum, tāḻa, āṭum iṟaivar is the syntactical link.]]
{1:70}__5+
{$} nontacuṭalaip poṭi nīṟu aṇivār
Civaṉ wears over-burnt, powdered holy ash of the cremation ground.
nutalcēr kaṇṇiṉār
has an eye on the forehead.
kantamalarkaḷ palavum nilavum kamaḻ puṉcaṭai tāḻa
when the fragrant golden caṭi hangs low, on which many fragrant flowers are shining.
pantu aṇ viralāḷ pākamāka
having the lady on whose finger she holds a ball as a half.
paṭukāṭṭu eri āṭum emtam aṭikaḷ
our god who dances in fire in the cremation ground.
kaṭikoḷcāral īṅkōy malaiyār
is in Īṅkōymali which has slopes having divine fragrance.
{1:70}__6+
{$} nīṟuār akalam uṭaiyār
Civaṉ has a chest on which sacred ash is thickly smeared.
nirai ārkoṉṟai aravōṭum āṟu ār caṭaiyār
has a caṭi on which are a river, rows of koṉṟi flowers and a cobra.
ayil veṅkaṇaiyāl avuṇar puram mūṉṟum cīṟā ericey tēvar perumāṉ
the god superior to all tēvars who burnt the three cities of the acurar out of anger, with a sharp and cruel arrow.
ceṅkaṇ aṭal veḷḷai ēṟu ār koṭiyār
has on his banner the form of a strong white bull of red eyes.
umaiyāḷōṭum īṅkōy malaiyār
is in īṇkōymali with Umayāl.
{1:70}__7+
{$} viṉaiāyiṉa tīrttu aruḷē puriyum vikirtaṉ
Civaṉ who is different from the world bestows his grace only after removing the Karmams.
virikoṉṟai naṉaiārmuṭimēl matiyamcūṭum nampāṉ
Civaṉ who bears the crescent on his head which has buds of koṉṟi which are in the blossoming stage.
taṉaiār kamalam malarmēl uṟaivāṉ talaiyōṭu aṉal ēntum eṉai āḷ uṭaiyāṉ
who has as his servant me and who holds in his hands fire and the skull of Piramaṉ who stays on the lotus flower which can be compared to itself.
umaiyāḷōṭum īṅkōy malaiyār
is in īṇkōymali with Umayaḷ.
{1:70}__8+
{$} parakkum perumai ilaṅkai eṉṉum patiyil polivu āya arakkarkkuiṟaivaṉ muṭiyum tōḷum aṇiārviral taṉṉāl nerukki aṭarttu
when Civaṉ squeezed and pressed down by the small toe the shoulders and heads of the King of arakkar who is eminent by his grandness of appearance in the city of ilanki whose greatness had spread everywhere.
nimalā! pōṟṟi' eṉṟu niṉṟu ētta
when he prised, spotless god protect me for a long time.
irakkam purintār
who took pity on him and granted his grace.
umaiyāḷōṭum īṅkōy malaiyār
is in īṇkōymali with Umayāḷ.
{1:70}__9+
{$} vari ār puliyiṉ uritōl uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ dresses himself in the skin flayed from a tiger having stripes.
āṭal pēṇum pemmāṉ
the god who desires dancing.
malaiyāṉ makaḷōṭum piriyātu
without separation from the dughter of the mountin and along with her.
tirumēṉi ariyōṭu ayaṉum aṟiyāvaṇṇam aḷavuil peru�muyōṭu eriyāy nimirnta eṅkaḷ perumāṉ
Our god who shot up as a column of fire with limitless greatness not to be known by Ari who has Tiru on his form, and by Ayaṉ.
īṅkōy malaiyārē
is in Īṅkōymali.
{1:70}__10+
{$} piṇṭi ēṉṟu peyarāniṟkum piṇaṅkucamaṇarum
quarrel some camaṇar who have adopted the acōka as their sacred tree and stand steadfast worshipping it.
maṇṭai kalaṉāk koṇṭutiriyum matiil tērarum
and the buddhists without senses who wander holding an earthern pot as begging bowl.
uṇṭivayiṟār
eat sumptuously.
uraikaḷ koḷḷātu
without heeding their words.
umaiyōṭu uṭaṉ āki
in company with Umi.
iṇṭaic caṭaiyāṉ, imaiyōr perumāṉ
Civaṉ who wears a circlet of flowers on his head, and who is the chief of the celestials who do not wink.
īṅkōy malaiyār
is in īṅkōymali.
{1:70}__11+
{$} viḻavu ār oliyum muḻavum ōvā vēṇupuram taṉṉuḷ
in vēṇupuram where the bustle of festivals and the music played on muḻavu never cease.
aḻal ār vaṇṇattu aṭikaḷ aruḷcēr aṇikoḷ campantaṉ
beautiful campantaṉ who is in union with the grace of god who has the colour of the fire.
eḻil ār cuṉaiyum poḻilum puṭaicūḻ īṅkōymalai īcaṉ kaḻalār pāṭal pattum vallār
those who are able to sing all the ten verses which are dedicated to the feet of Civaṉ who is in īṅkōymali surrounded on all sides by gardens and beautiful mountin springs.
kavalai (1) kaḷaivārē
will weed out all cares and anxieties.
[[Variant reading: (1) kaḻalvārē]]

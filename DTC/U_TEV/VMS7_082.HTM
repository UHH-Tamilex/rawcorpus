ūṉ āy
being the different bodies.
pukal uyir āy
and the lives that have entered into them.
akal iṭam āy
being the wide world.
mukilpoḻiyum (1) vāṉāy
being the sky from where the clouds pour rain.
(1) vitivaru matiāy
being the mind which is the gate for fate to enter to bear their fruits.
varuvāṉ iṭam
the place of god who stays in that manner.
poḻiliṉ tēr ātarittu vaṇṭiṉam icai miḻaṟṟum tiruccuḻiyal nāṉāvitam viṉaivārtamai naliyār namaṉtamar (ē)
the servants of the god of death will not inflict pain on those who think in many ways tiruccuḻiyal where in the gardens the swarms of bees desiring honeyhum like music.
[[Variant reading: (1) vāṉāyataṉmatiyāy]]
{7:82}__2+
{$}
taṇṭu ēr maḻuppaṭaiyāṉ maḻaviṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ who has a club and a beautiful battle-axe and a young bull.
imaiyavarkkāk kaṭal eḻu nañcu uṇṭu(ē) puram eriyac cilai vaḷaittāṉ tiṇtērmicai niṉṟāṉ (avaṉ) uṟaiyum tiruccuḻiyal
in tiruccuḻiyal where he stood on the strong chariot bending the bow to set fire to the cities and consumed the poison which rose in the sea in order to protect the celestials who do not wink.
toṇṭē ceyavallār (avar) nallār; tuyar ilar(ē)
those who are able to do service to him are good natured people and they will not have any suffering.
{7:82}__3+
{$}
kavvaikkaṭal kataṟik koṇar muttam (1) karaikku ēṟṟa
when the ocean roars and brings and pearls to pile upon the noisy shore.
kovvait tuvarvāyār kuṭaintu āṭum tiruccuḻiyal teyvattiṉai vaḻipāṭuceytu eḻuvār aṭitoḻuvār
those who worship the feet of the devotees who in their turn worship god and then wake up from sleep in tiruccuḻiyal where the ladies who have red lips like the fruit of the common creeper of the hedge, dive and bathe.
avvatticaikku aracu ākuvar
will rule over the regions in which they live.
alarāṉ piriyāṉ (ē)
the goddess of wealth will never leave them.
[[Variant readings: (1) karaiyēṟṟak (2) karaiyēṟak]]

{$}
malaiyāṉmakaḷ maṭamātu iṭam ākattavaṉ
when the daughter of the mountain who is a young lady is on the left half of his body.
kolaiyāṉaiyiṉ uripōrtta emperumāṉ tiruccuḻiyal alaiār caṭaiuṭaiyāṉ aṭitoḻuvār
those who worship the feet of Civaṉ who has on hiscaṭai water with waves and who dwells in tiruccuḻiyal and who covers his body with the skin of an elephant capable of killing.
paḻutu uḷḷam niṉaiyār
will not have any evil thoughts in their minds.
tikaḻ (1) pukaḻāl neṭu vāṉattu uyarvār (ē)
will ascend into the extensive heaven after establishing their eminent fame in this world.
[[Variant reading: (1) pukaḻvāṉeṭu]]

namakku uṟṟār
Civaṉ is our relation.
uyarum maticcaṭaiyāṉ
wears on his caṭai an eminent crescent.
pulaṉ aintum ceṟṟu ār tirumēṉip perumāṉ ūr tiruccuḻiyal
tiruccuḻiyal is the place of Civaṉ who has a holy body in which all the perceptions of the senses have been subdued.
[[tirumēṉipperumāṉ is the name of the presiding deity in this shrine.]]
iṉitu uṟaiyap peṟṟāṉ tirunāmam tiṟampāmaik kaṟṟār (avar) katiyuḷ celvar(ē)
those who have learnt to utter the names of Civaṉ who dwells with pleasure there, without deviating from the right path will enter into higher stages of existences.
ēttu(m) matu kaṭaṉ (ē)
(People of this world) it is your religious duty to praise his names.
{7:82}__6+
{$} malam tāṅkiya pācappiṟappu aṟuppīr
People of this world who wish to exterminate birth with is connected with the spiritual impurities, like āṇavam!
tuṟaikkaṅkaic calam tāṅkiya muṭiyāṉ
Civaṉ has a head on which he bears the water of the Kaṅkai which has many ghats.
amarnta iṭam ām tiruccuḻiyal, nilam tāṅkiya palarāl koḻumpukaiyāl niṉaintu ēttum
praise with intention worshipping him with fragrant incense and flowers that are borne by the earth, tirucculiyal which is his desired place.
(1) talam tāṅkiya pukaḻ ām
you will obtain fame, which is supported by this earth.
mikutavam ām
it is also increasing penance.
catur ām
that is skill.
[[Variant reading: (1) nalam tāṅkiya pukaḻāṉ mikutavamām]]
{7:82}__7+
{$} caivatta cevvuruvaṉ
Civaṉ has a red form described in the caiva ākamams.
tirunīṟṟaṉ
smears sacred ash.
urum ēṟṟaṉ
has a bull that bellows like the thunder
kaivaitta oru cilaiyāl araṇmūṉṟum eri ceytāṉ
set fire to all the three forts with a bow held in the hand.
teyvattavar toḻutu ēttiya kuḻakaṉ tiruccuḻiyal mey vaittu
thinking in the mind about tiruccuḻiyal of the youth who is praised and worshipped with joined hands, by divine sages.
aṭiniṉaivār viṉai tīrtal(l) eḷitu (aṉṟu, ē)
perishing of the acts of those who think of his feet is easy.

cē ēntiya koṭiyāṉ (avaṉ) uṟaiyum tiruccuḻiyal mā ēntiya karattāṉ ema cirattāṉtaṉatu aṭiyē
the feet of Civaṉ only who holds a flag of a bull, who holds a deer in his hand and who dwells on our heads; in his tiruccuḻiyal.
pū ēntiya pīṭattavaṉ(tāṉ) um, aṭal ariyum
Piramaṉ who is in an elevated seat of a (lotus) flower and the strong ari (Māl)
kōēntiya viṉayattoṭu kuṟukappukal aṟiyār
do not know to reach them with supreme humility.

{$} koṇṭāṭutal puriyā varu takkaṉ peruvēḷḷi ceṇṭāṭutal purintāṉ tiruccuḻiyal perumāṉai
about the Lord in tiruccuḻiyal who ruined the big sacrifice of takkaṉ who indulged in self-praise.
kuṇṭuāṭiya camaṇātarkaḷ kuṭaiccākkiyar aṟiyā miṇṭu āṭiya atuceytu atu āṉāl (1) varuviti (ē)
it is their fate if the low and ignorant camanar, and cākkiyar who have an umbrella, enter into a crimonious religious discussion and if they stick to their own ways.
[[Variant reading: (1) varumatiyē]]

nīr ūrtaru nimalaṉ tirumalaiyārkku ayal arukē tēr(2) ūrtarum arakkaṉ ciram nerittāṉ tiruccuḻiyal
having tiruccuḻiyal of Civaṉ who crushed the heads of the arakkaṉ who was driving in a chariot near the holy mountain and by its side, of the spotless god, where streams flow down.
pēr ūr eṉa uṟaivāṉ (2) aṭippeyar nāvalar kōmāṉ ārūraṉa tamiḻmālai pattu aṟivār
those who know to recite the ten garlands of tamiḻ verses done by ārūraṉ, the chief of the residents of nāvalūr and who has the name of the feet of the Lord who dwells in tiruccuḻiyal esteeming it as a great city.
tuyar ilar (ē)
will have no sufferings.

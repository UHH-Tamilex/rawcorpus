• patikam: [3:80]
  • talam: tiru vīḻimiḻali
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.232

*1
cīr maruvu tēciṉoṭu tēcam mali celva maṟiyōrkaḷ paṇiya,
tār maruvu koṉṟi _aṇi tāḻcaṭiyiṉāṉ _amar cayam koḷ patitāṉ---
pār maruvu paṅkayam _uyarnta vayal cūḻ paḻaṉam nīṭa, _arukē
kār maruvu veṇkaḷapa māḷiki kaviṉ peruku vīḻinakarē.

*2
paṭṭa muḻavu, _iṭṭa paṇilattiṉoṭu, palmaṟikaḷ _ōtu paṇi nal
ciṭṭarkaḷ cayattutikaḷ ceyya, _aruḷcey taḻal koṉ mēṉiyavaṉ _ūr----
maṭṭu _ulavu ceṅkamala vēli vayal cennel vaḷar, maṉṉu poḻilvāy
viṭṭu _ulavu teṉṟal viri nāṟu, pati vētiyarkaḷ vīḻinakarē.

*3
maṇ _iḻi curarkku vaḷam mikka pati---maṟṟum _uḷa maṉ_uyirkaḷukku
_eṇ _iḻivu _il _iṉpam nikaḻvu _eyta, _eḻil _ār poḻil _ilaṅku _aṟupatam
paṇ _iḻivu _ilāta vaki pāṭa, maṭamaññi naṭam_āṭa, _aḻaku _ār
viṇ_iḻivimāṉam _uṭi viṇṇavarpirāṉ maruvu vīḻinakarē.

*4
centamiḻar, teyvamaṟi nāvar, ceḻu naṉkali terinta_avarōṭu
_antam _il kuṇattavarkaḷ _arccaṉikaḷ ceyya _amarkiṉṟa _araṉ _ūr---
kontu _alar poḻil paḻaṉa vēli kuḷir-taṇpuṉal vaḷam perukavē,
ven tiṟal viḷaṅki vaḷar vētiyar virumpu pati vīḻinakarē.

*5
pūtapati _ākiya purāṇamuṉi puṇṇiyanalmāti maruvi,
pētam_atu _ilāta vaki pākam mika vitta perumāṉatu _iṭam_ām---
mātavarkaḷ _aṉṉa maṟiyāḷarkaḷ vaḷartta mali vēḷvi_ataṉāl,
_ētam_atu _ilāta vaki _iṉpam _amarkiṉṟa _eḻil vīḻinakarē.

*6
maṇṇil maṟiyōr maruvu vitikamum, mā tavamum, maṟṟum _ulakattu
_eṇ _il poruḷ_āyavi paṭitta _imiyōrkaḷperumāṉatu _iṭam_ām---
naṇṇi varu nāvalarkaḷ nāḷtoṟum vaḷarkka nikaḻkiṉṟa pukaḻ cēr
viṇ _ulavu māḷiki neruṅki vaḷar nīḷpurici vīḻinakarē.

*7
mantira nalmāmaṟiyiṉōṭu vaḷar vēḷvimici mikka puki pōy,
_antara-vicumpu _aṇavi, _aṟputam _eṉap paṭarum _āḻi _iruḷvāy,
mantara nal māḷiki nilāvu maṇi nīṭu katirviṭṭa _oḷi pōy,
ventaḻal viḷakku _eṉa virumpiṉar tiruntu pati---vīḻinakarē.

*8
_āṉa valiyiṉ tacamukaṉ tali _araṅka, _aṇi _āḻiviralāl,
_ūṉ _amar _uyarnta kurutippuṉalil vīḻtara _uṇarnta paraṉ _ūr---
tēṉ _amar tiruntu poḻil, ceṅkaṉaka māḷiki, tikaḻnta matilōṭu,
_āṉa tiru _uṟṟu vaḷar, _antaṇar niṟinta _aṇi vīḻinakarē.

*9
_ēṉa_uru _āki maṇ _iṭanta _imiyōṉum, _eḻil _aṉṉa_uruvam
_āṉavaṉum, _ātiyiṉoṭu _antam _aṟiyāta _aḻalmēṉiyavaṉ _ūr---
vāṉ _aṇavum mā matil maruṅku _alar neruṅkiya vaḷam koḷ poḻilvāy
vēṉal _amarvu _eytiṭa, viḷaṅku _oḷiyiṉ mikka pukaḻ vīḻinakarē.

*10
kuṇṭu_amaṇar_āki, _oru kōlam miku pīliyoṭu kuṇṭiki piṭittu,
_eṇticiyum _illatu _oru teyvam _uḷatu _eṉpar; _atu _eṉṉa poruḷ _ām?
paṇṭi _ayaṉ _aṉṉavarkaḷ pāvaṉi virumpu paraṉ mēvu pati---cīr
veṇtaraḷavāḷnaki nal mātarkaḷ viḷaṅkum _eḻil vīḻinakarē.

*11
mattam mali koṉṟi vaḷarvārcaṭiyil vitta paraṉ, vīḻinakar cēr
vittakaṉi, veṅkuruvil vētiyaṉ virumpu tamiḻmālikaḷ valār,
cittira vimāṉam _amar celvam malikiṉṟa civalōkam maruvi,
_attaku kuṇattavarkaḷ _āki, _aṉupōkamoṭu yōku _avaratē.

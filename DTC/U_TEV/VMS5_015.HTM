{5:15}__1+
{$} paṟaiyiṉ ōcaiyum
the sound produced by percussion instruments made to leather.
pāṭaliṉ ōcaiyum
the sound of vocal music.
maṟaiyiṉ ōcaiyum
and the sound produced when the Vētam is chanted.
vaikum (1) ayal ellām
stay on all the sides where they are permanent
iṟaivaṉ
the chief.
eṅkaḷ pirāṉ iṭai marutiṉil uṟaiyum īcaṉai uḷkum eṉ uḷḷamē
my mind will think of Civaṉ, our master who dwells in Iṭaimarutu
[[the last two lines are found in the 2nd verse of the previous decade with this slight variation: emperumāṉ]]
[[Variant reading: (1) vayal]]
{5:15}__2+
{$} maṉattuḷ māyaṉai
the deceitful person who stays in the mind.
mācu aṟu cōtiyai
the spotless light
puṉiṟṟup piḷḷai veḷḷai (m) maticūṭiyai
Civaṉ who adorns his head with the just-risen white crescent.
[[piḷḷaim mati the underlined letter is an augmentation for rhythm]]
eṉakkut tāyai
my mother
emmāṉ iṭaimarutaṉai niṉaittiṭṭu ūṟi (1) niṟaintatu eṉ uḷḷamē
my mind became full thinking of our master who is in Iṭaimarutu when the thoughts spreaded about him.
[[Variant reading: (1) niṉaintatu]]
{5:15}__3+
{$}
[[This verse is found as the fifth verse in the previous decade with these changes:
mattamum, kūttaṉai]]
vaṇṭu aṇaintaṉa vaṉṉiyum mattamum koṇṭu aṇinta caṭaimuṭik kūttaṉai eṇticaikkum iṭaimarutā eṉa
when we say the Lord in Iṭaimarutu who is the refuge for people in the eight directions and who is the dancer who wears on his caṭai coiled into a crown leaves of indian mesquit and dhatura flowers which are approached by bees.
viṇṭu pōy aṟum mēlai viṉaikaḷē
all the accumulated karmam-s done in previous births will go away from us and get destroyed.
{5:15}__4+
{$} tuṇai ilāmaiyiṉ
As there are no other companions
tūṅku iruḷ pēykaḷōṭu aṇaiyal āvatu emakku aritē eṉā
thinking that it would be difficult to while away the time, being in the company of the pēy-s in the dense darkness at the end of the world.
iṇaiilā iṭai māmarutil eḻupaṇaiil ākamam collum taṉ pāṅkikkē
Civaṉ will be explaining the meanings of the ākamam under the marutam tree which has grown in the great iṭaimarutu which has no equal, to his consort who is on one half.
{5:15}__5+
{$} maṇṇai uṇṭa mālkāṇāṉ malaraṭi
Māl who swallowed the earth could not see the lotus feet.
viṇṇai viṇṭu ayaṉ kāṇāṉ viyalmuṭi
Ayaṉ could not see the broad head by flying, piercing through the sky.
moṇṇai māmarutā veṉṟu eṉmoykuḻal paṇṇaiāya mumtāṉum payilum
my daughter who has dense tresses of hair says always along with her friends who play with her, Bold and great marutā.
[[moṇṇai marutā:
kaṇṇeṉṟum namtamakku ōr kāppu eṉṟum kaṟṟirukkum, eṇṇeṉṟum mūla eḻutteṉṟum moṇṇai marutaappā eṉṟum uṉai vāḻttārēl maṟṟum, karuta appāl uṇṭō kati (tiruviṭaimarutūr munmaṇikkōvai, 5).]]

{5:15}__6+
{$} maṇa mālaiyai maṅkaikāṇak (1) koṭār
Civaṉ will not give away the fragrant garland worn on the chest when Umai is watching it keenly.
muṭikkaṇṇiyaikaṅkai kāṇam (2) koṭār
will not give the chaplet worn on the head when Kaṅkai is keenly seeing it.
naṅkaimīr
young girls of distinction!
iṭai marutar innaṅkaikkē itaḻi eṅkuvāṅkik koṭuttār?
from where did the Lord in Iṭaimarutu get the koṉṟai flowers and give it only to this girl of distinction?
[[The girl who fell in love with the Lord got paleness of complexion due to love-sickness. That colour is spoken as itaḻi, as itaḻi (koṉṟai) is pale yellow in colour.
(How did the Lord give the koṉṟai to this girl, without being noticed by Umai and Koṅkai?)]]

• patikam: [4:95]
  • talam: tiru vīḻimiḻali
  • (()): tiru viruttam
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.262

*1
vāṉ coṭṭaccoṭṭa niṉṟu _aṭṭum vaḷarmatiyōṭu _ayalē
tēṉ coṭṭaccoṭṭa niṉṟu _aṭṭum tiruk koṉṟi ceṉṉi vittīr!
māṉpeṭṭi nōkki maṇāḷīr! maṇinīr miḻali _uḷḷīr!---
nāṉ caṭṭa _ummi maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*2
_antamum _ātiyum _āki niṉṟīr! _aṇṭam _eṇticiyum
pantamum vīṭum parappukiṉṟīr! pacu _ēṟṟu _ukantīr!
ventaḻal _ōmpum miḻali _uḷḷīr!---_eṉṉit teṉticikkē
_untiṭumpōtu maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*3
_alikkiṉṟa nīr, nilam, kāṟṟu, _aṉal, _amparam, _āki niṉṟīr!
kalikkaṉṟu cērum karattīr! kalipporuḷ_āki niṉṟīr!
vilakku _iṉṟi nalkum miḻali _uḷḷīr---meyyil kiyoṭu kāl
kulikkiṉṟu nummi maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*4
tīt toḻilāṉ tali tīyil _iṭṭu, ceyta vēḷvi ceṟṟīr!
pēyttoḻilāṭṭiyip peṟṟu_uṭiyīr! piṭittut tiriyum
vēyt toḻilāḷar miḻali _uḷḷīr! vikki _añcu_eḻuttum
_ōttu _oḻintu _ummi maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*5
tōḷ paṭṭa nākamum, cūlamum, cuttiyum, pattimiyāl
mēṟpaṭṭa _antaṇar vīḻiyum, _eṉṉiyum vēṟu _uṭiyīr!
nāḷ paṭṭu vantu piṟantēṉ, _iṟakka, namaṉtamartam
kōḷpaṭṭu nummi maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*6
kaṇṭiyil paṭṭa kaḻuttu _uṭiyīr! karikāṭṭil _iṭṭa
paṇṭiyil paṭṭa parikalattīr! pativīḻi koṇṭīr---
_uṇṭiyil, paṭṭiṉi, nōyil, _uṟakkattil,---_ummi,---_aivar
koṇṭiyil paṭṭu maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*7
tōṟṟam kaṇṭāṉ ciram _oṉṟu koṇṭīr! tūya veḷ_erutu _oṉṟu
_ēṟṟam koṇṭīr! _eḻil vīḻimiḻali _irukki koṇṭīr!---
cīṟṟam koṇṭu _eṉmēl civantatu _ōr pācattāl vīciya veṅ
kūṟṟam kaṇṭu _ummi maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*8
cuḻippaṭṭa kaṅkiyum tiṅkaḷum cūṭic cokkam payiṉṟīr!
paḻippaṭṭa pāmpu _arip paṟṟu _uṭiyīr! paṭar tīp paruka
viḻippaṭṭa kāmaṉi viṭṭīr! miḻali _uḷḷīr!---piṟavic-
cuḻippaṭṭu nummi maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*9
piḷḷiyiṉ paṭṭa piṟimuṭiyīr! maṟi _ōta vallīr!
veḷḷiyil paṭṭatu _ōr nīṟṟīr! virinīr miḻali _uḷḷīr!---
naḷḷiyil paṭṭu _aivar nakku _arippikka namaṉtamartam
koḷḷiyil paṭṭu maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

*10
kaṟukkoṇṭu _arakkaṉ kayiliyip paṟṟiya kiyum meyyum
neṟukkeṉṟu _iṟac ceṟṟa cēvaṭiyāl kūṟṟi nīṟuceytīr!
veṟik koṉṟimāli muṭiyīr! virinīr miḻali _uḷḷīr!---
_iṟakkiṉṟu nummi maṟakkiṉum, _eṉṉik kuṟikkoṇmiṉē!

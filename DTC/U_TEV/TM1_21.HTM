*1
puvam, vaḷi, kaṉal, puṉal, puvi, kali, _uri maṟi, tirikuṇam, _amar neṟi,

*2

_alikaṭal naṭuvu _aṟituyil _amar _ari _uruvu _iyal paraṉ _uṟi pati---
cili mali matil---civapuram niṉipavar tirumakaḷoṭu tikaḻvarē.

*3

kuḻuviya curar, piṟar, maṉitarkaḷ, kulam malitarum _uyir_avi_avi

ceḻu maṇi _aṇi---civapuranakar toḻumavar pukaḻ mikum, _ulakilē.

*4

niṟi puṉalkoṭu, taṉi niṉivoṭu niyatamum vaḻipaṭum _aṭiyavar

*5

maṉaṉ _uṇarvoṭu malarmici _eḻutaru poruḷ niyatamum _uṇarpavar
taṉatu _eḻil _uru_atukoṭu _aṭi taku paraṉ _uṟivatu nakar---matil
kaṉam maruviya---civapuram niṉipavar kalimakaḷ tara nikaḻvarē.

*6

_uru _iyal _ulaku_avi pukaḻtara, vaḻi _oḻukum mey _uṟu poṟi _oḻi

*7

*8
_acivu_uṟu tava muyalviṉil, _ayaṉ _aruḷiṉil, varu valikoṭu civaṉ

*9
_aṭal mali paṭi _ari_ayaṉoṭum _aṟivu _ariyatu _or _aḻal mali taru

*10

*11

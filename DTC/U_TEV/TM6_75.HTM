*1
col malinta maṟināṉku _āṟu_aṅkam_ākic
colporuḷum kaṭanta cuṭarccōtipōlum;
kal malinta kayilimalivāṇarpōlum;
kaṭalnañcam _uṇṭu _iruṇṭa kaṇṭarpōlum;
mal malinta maṇi varittiṇtōḷarpōlum;
mali_ariyaṉmaṭappāvi maṇāḷarpōlum;
koṉ malinta mū_ilivēl kuḻakarpōlum---
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*2
kāṉ nal _iḷaṅ kali maṟavaṉ _āki, pārttaṉ
karuttu _aḷavu cerut tokuti kaṇṭārpōlum;
_āṉ nal _iḷaṅ kaṭu viṭi _oṉṟu _ēṟi, _aṇṭattu
_appālum pali tiriyum _aḻakarpōlum---
tēṉ nal _iḷan tuvali mali teṉṟal muṉṟil
ceḻum poḻil pūmpāḷi viri tēṟal nāṟum,
kūṉal_iḷampiṟi taṭavu koṭi koḷ, māṭak
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*3
nīṟu _alitta tiru_uruvum, neṟṟikkaṇṇum,
nilā _alitta pāmpiṉoṭu, niṟi nīrkkaṅki-
_āṟu _alitta caṭimuṭiyum, _am poṉ-tāḷum,
_aṭiyavarkkuk kāṭṭi _aruḷpurivārpōlum;
_ēṟu _alitta nimirkoṭi _oṉṟu _uṭiyarpōlum;
_ēḻ_ulakum toḻu kaḻal _em _īcarpōlum;
kūṟu _alitta malimaṭantikoḻunarpōlum---
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*4
takkaṉatu peru vēḷvi takarttārpōlum;
cantiraṉik kali kavarntu tarittārpōlum;
cekkar_oḷi, pavaḷa_oḷi, miṉṉiṉ cōti,
ceḻuñ cuṭarttī, ñāyiṟu, _eṉac ceyyarpōlum---
mikka tiṟal maṟiyavarāl viḷaṅku vēḷvi
miku puki pōy viṇ poḻiya, kaḻaṉi_ellām
kokku _iṉiya kaṉi citaṟit tēṟal pāyum
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*5
kālaṉ vali tolitta kaḻalkālarpōlum;
kāmaṉ _eḻil _aḻal viḻuṅkak kaṇṭārpōlum;
_āl_ataṉil _aṟam nālvarkku _aḷittārpōlum;
_āṇoṭu peṇ _ali _allar, _āṉār, pōlum;
nīla_uru, vayira niri, pacci, cempoṉ,
neṭum paḷiṅku, _eṉṟu _aṟivu _ariya niṟattārpōlum---
kōla maṇi koḻittu _iḻiyum poṉṉi naṉnīrk
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*6
muṭi koṇṭa vaḷarmatiyum, mūṉṟu_āyt tōṉṟum---
muḷiñāyiṟu _aṉṉa---malarkkaṇkaḷmūṉṟum,
_aṭi koṇṭa cilampu _oliyum, _aruḷ _ār cōti
_aṇi muṟuval cevvāyum, _aḻaku_āyt tōṉṟa;
tuṭi koṇṭa _iṭi maṭavāḷ pākam koṇṭu;
cuṭarccōtikkaṭal cempoṉmali pōl, _in nāḷ
kuṭikoṇṭu _eṉ maṉattu_akattē pukuntārpōlum---
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*7
kār _ilaṅku tiru_uruvattavaṟkum, maṟṟik
kamalattil kāraṇaṟkum, kāṭci _oṇṇāc
cīr _ilaṅku taḻalpiḻampiṉ civantārpōlum;
cili vaḷivittu _avuṇar puram citittārpōlum;

paruti, mati, curutiyum_āy, parantārpōlum;
kūr _ilaṅku vēl kumaraṉ tātipōlum---
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*8
pūc cūḻnta poḻil taḻuvu pukalūr _uḷḷār;
puṟampayattār; _aṟam puri pūnturutti pukku,
māc cūḻnta paḻaṉattār; neyttāṉattār;
mā tavattu vaḷar cōṟṟuttuṟiyār; nalla
tīc cūḻnta tikiri tirumālukku _īntu,
tiru _āṉikkāvil _ōr cilantikku _an nāḷ
kōc cōḻarkulattu _aracu koṭuttārpōlum---
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*9
poṅku _aravar; pulittōlar; purāṇar; mārpil
poṟi kiḷar veṇpūṇanūl puṉitarpōlum;
caṅku _aravak kaṭal mukaṭu taṭṭaviṭṭu,
catura naṭam_āṭṭu _ukanta civarpōlum;
_aṅku _aravat tiruvaṭikku _āṭpiḻippa, tanti-
_antaṇaṉi _aṟa _eṟintārkku, _aruḷ _appōtē
koṅku _aravac caṭik koṉṟi koṭuttārpōlum---
kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*10
_ēvi, _iṭarkkaṭal_iṭip paṭṭu _iḷikkiṉṟēṉi
_ip piṟavi _aṟuttu _ēṟa vāṅki, _āṅkē
kūvi, _amarulaku_aṉittum _uruvip pōka,
kuṟiyil _aṟukuṇattu _āṇṭukoṇṭārpōlum---

caracuvati, poṟṟāmarippuṭkaraṇi, teṇnīrk

kuṭantik kīḻkkōṭṭattu _em kūttaṉārē.

*11
ceṟikoṇṭa cintitaṉuḷ teḷintu tēṟit

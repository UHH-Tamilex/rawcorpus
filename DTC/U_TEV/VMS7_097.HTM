{7:97}__1+
{$} ātiyaṉ
Civaṉ is the source of all things.
ātiraiyaṉ
he has the star Ātirai as his favourite star.
ayaṉmāl aṟitaṟku ariya cōtiyaṉ
is the light that Ayaṉ and Māl could not know.
coṟporuḷāy
being himself the words and their meanings.
curuṅkā maṟaināṉkiṉaiyum ōtiyaṉ
he recited with the proper intonation the four elaborate vētams.
umpar tam kōṉ
is the chief of the celestials in heaven.
ulakattiṉuḷ evvuyirkkum nātiyaṉ namperumāṉ naṇṇum ūr naṉipaḷḷi (atē)
naṉipaḷḷi is the place that our Lord who is the chief of all living being in this world, dwells.
{7:97}__2+
{$} uṟavu ili
Civaṉ has no relations attached to him
ūṉam ili
has no defects
uṇarārpuram mūṉṟu eriyac ceṟuvi(l)li
is an archer who destroyed the three cities of those who did not esteem him by burning them
taṉ niṉaivārviṉai āyiṉa tēyntu aḻiya aṟailakum aruḷāṉ
has pre-eminent grace to make the Karmams of people who meditate upon him to decrease and perish.
maruḷ ār(1) poḻil vaṇṭu aṟaiyum naṟavirikoṉṟaiyiṉāṉ naṇṇum ūr naṉipaḷḷi (atē)
naṉipalli is the place of Civaṉ who wears koṉṟai flowers which blossom with honey, where in the wonderful gardens bees hum in a high pitch.
[[vaṇṭu aṟaiyumūr, naṇṇum ūr is the syntactical link.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) moḻi]]
{7:97}__3+
{$} vāṉ uṭaiyāṉ periyāṉ; maṉattālum niṉaippu ariyāṉ
Civaṉ has the heaven as his abode, is greater than all other gods; could not be comprehended even by the mind.
āṇiṭai aintu amarntāṉ
desired the five products obtained from the cow.
aṇu āki
being the minute atom
ōr tī urukkoṇṭu
assuming the form of fire
ivvuṭalam oṭuṅkip pukuntāṉ, parantāṉ
entered into this body shinking himself, pervaded everywhere
nāṉuṭai(ya) māṭu
is my wealth
empirāṉ
is our master
naṇṇum ūrnaṉipaḷḷi (atu)
the place where he dwells is naṉipaḷḷi.
{7:97}__4+
{$} kalaṉā ōṭu uṭaiyaṉ
Civaṉ has a skull as eating vessel
(kalaṉ: uṇkalaṉ)
uṭaikōvaṇavaṉ
has as dress a loin-cloth
umai ōrpāṭu uṭaiyaṉ
has Umai on one side
pali tērntu uṇṇum paṇpu uṭaiyaṉ
has the nature of eating food by begging alms.
payilakkāṭu uṭaiyaṉ
has the cremation ground to dwell
iṭamā (ka) malai ēḻum karuṅkaṭalcūḻ nāṭu uṭai nam perumāṉ naṇṇum ūr naṉipaḷḷi(atu)
naṉipaḷḷi is the place which our Lord who has the seven countries girt by the black oceans, and the seven important mountains, as his places
[[nāṭu here means islands; seven islands are formed in hindu mythology. ēḻum should be added to nāṭu also.]]
{7:97}__5+
{$} paṇṇaṟku ariyatu oru āḻippaṭaitaṉaip paṭaittu
producing the weapon of a discus which is difficult to make.
kaṇṇaṟku aruḷ purintāṉ
granted it to Kaṇṇaṉ
[[Kaṇṇaṉ is used in the sense of Māl. As the discus granted to Māl is not like the one made in this world, it is qualified as paṇṇaṟkariyatu oru āḻippaṭai);]]
[[PP: taruppam miku calantaraṉ taṉ uṭal taṭitta cakkarattai vēṇṭi īṇṭu, viruppoṭu māl vaḻipāṭu ceyya iḻi vimāṉam cēr miḻalaiyāmē taṉ tavam periya calantaraṉ uṭalam taṭinta cakkaram eṉakku aruḷ eṉṟu, aṉṟu arivaḻipaṭṭu iḻicciya vimāṉattu iṟaivaṉ (campantar, tiruvīḻimiḻalai, paṇ-mēkarākakkuṟiñci, 8; paṇ-puṟanīrmai, 7);]]
[[PP: nīṟṟiṉai niṟaiyap pūci nittal āyiram pūkkoṇṭu, ēṟṟuḻi orunāḷ oṉṟu kuṟaiyak kaṇ niṟaiya iṭṭa, āṟṟalukku āḻi nalki;
kaṇ tuñcum karuneṭumāl āḻi vēṇṭikkaṇ iṭantu cūṭṭakkaṇṭu aruḷuvāṉ;
kaṟpolitōṭcalantaraṉaip piḷanta āḻi karumālukku aruḷceyta karuṇaiyāṉ kāṇ
(nāvukkaracar, tirunēricai, tiruviḻimiḻalai, 8;
do. tāṇṭakam (3) 6,7);
Kōvaiyār, 163,180]]
karutātavar vēḷvi avi uṇṇaṟku imaiyavarai uruṇṭu ōṭa utaittu ukantu
be...... great by kicking to make the celestials who had gathered to eat the offering made in the fire of the sacrifice that takkaṉ who did not esteem Civaṉ, performed, roll on the floor, and then granted his grace to them.
[[karutātavar: the plural is used to denigrate Takkaṉ.]]
naṇṇaṟku ariyapirāṉ naṇṇum ūr naṉipaḷḷi (atu, ē)
is Naṉipaḷḷi where the master who is difficult to be approached by anybody, dwells.
{7:97}__6+
{$} malkiya ceñcaṭai mēl matiyum aravum uṭaṉē pulkiya āraṇaṉ
the origin of all the vētam who has a body in which the crescent and the cobra are very close to each other, on the growing red caṭai.
em puṉitaṉ
our spotless god.
purinūl vikirtaṉ
one who is different from the world and wears a sacred thread of three stands.
melkiya viṟṟoḻilāṉ viruppaṉ perum pārttaṉukku nalkiya namperumāṉ naṇṇum ūr naṉipaḷḷi (atu, ē)
naṉipaḷḷi is the place where our Lord who granted a weapon, pācupatam, to the great pārttaṉ who had love towards Civaṉ, fighting with him with a slight show of archery.
{7:97}__7+
{$} aṅkam ōr āṟu (avaiyum) aru māmaṟai vēḷvikaḷum eṅkum iruntu antaṇar eri mūṉṟu (avai) ōmpum iṭam
the place where the brahmins who have mastered the six aṅkams and the great and abstruse vētams and sacrifices, .... them in adition to maintaining the three sacrificial fires everywhere.
paṅkaya māmukattāḷ umaipaṅkaṉ uṟai kōyil ceṅkayal pāyum vayal (1) tiruūr naṉipaḷḷi (atu, ē)
is naṉipaḷḷi of great wealth where red carp fish leaps in the fields and has a temple in which the Lord who has Umai whose face is like the lotus flower, as a half.
[[Variant reading: (1)tirunaṉi]]
{7:97}__8+
{$} tiṅkaṭ kuṟunteriyal tikaḻkaṇṇiyaṉ
Civaṉ has a crescent which is like a chaplet
[[NOTE_GRAM: kaṇṇi is put in apposition with teriyal]]
[[PP_X: māl varai puraiyum māṭak kūṭal ālavāyiṟpālpurai pacuṅkatirkkuḻavittiṅkaḷaik kuṟuṅkaṇṇiyāka uṭaiya aḻal avir cōti arumaṟaikkaṭavuḷ
(iṟaiyaṉār akapporuḷ urai,
p.3; Kaḻakam first edition; 1953)]]
nuṇṇiyaṉāy
being very minute not to be seen
naṅkaṇ piṇikaḷaivāṉ
in order to remove the diseases of Karmams which are with us.
[[kaḷaivāṉ the word suggests that the Karmams are like weeds hindering the march of the soul towards god.]]
ēḻpiṟappum arumāmaruntu
he is the great and rare remedy in the seven births.
val arakkaṉaiyum maṅkattiruviralāl aṭarttāṉ
pressed down by his toe even the strong arakkaṉ to diminish in strength.
naṅkaṭku aruḷum pirāṉ naṇṇum ūrnaṉi paḷḷi (atu, ē)
naṉipaḷḷi is the place of the master who bestows his grace on us.
{7:97}__9+
{$} ēṉam maruppiṉoṭum eḻil āmaiyum pūṇṭu ukantu
adorning himself with joy a beautiful shell of the tortoise and a hog's tusk.
vāṉam matil araṇam malaiyē cilaiyā vaḷaittāṉ
destroyed the forts that wandered in the sky by bending the mountain into a bow.
ūṉam il kāḻi taṉṉuḷ uyar ñāṉacampantarkku aṉṟu ñāṉam aruḷ purintāṉ naṇṇum ūr naṉipaḷḷi (atu, ē)
naṉipaḷḷi is the place of Civaṉ who granted knowledge of Civaṉ to the superior Ñāṉacampantar in the past in Kāḻi which knew not destruction even at the time when all things were destroyed.
{7:97}__10+
{$} kālamum (1) nāḻikaiyum
When time will pass off every twenty four minutes
[[nāḻikai: twenty four minutes duration of time.]]
kuḷir nāvala ūraṉ naṉipaḷḷi maṉattiṉ uḷkik kōlam atu āyavaṉaic coṉṉa mālai matittu uraippār
those who recite the garland of verse composed on the Lord who is the embodiment of grace, by Ūraṉ of cool Nāvalūr, thinking in his mind about Naṉipaḷḷi, with reverence.
maṇmaṟantu vāṉōr ulakil naliṉpam eytit tavalōkattu iruppār
will dwell in civalokam, after enjoying very much, pleasures in the heaven; forgetting being born in this world.
[[Variant reading: (1) nāḷkaḻiyum]]

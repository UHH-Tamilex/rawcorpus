• patikam: [5:57]
  • talam: tiruk kōḷili
  • (()): tiruk kuṟuntoki
  • VMS: Vol.10, p.129

*1
muṉṉamē niṉiyātoḻintēṉ, _uṉi;
_iṉṉam nāṉ _uṉa cēvaṭi _ēttilēṉ,
cennel _ār vayal cūḻ tiruk kōḷili
maṉṉaṉē, _aṭiyēṉi maṟavalē!

*2
viṇ_uḷār toḻutu _ēttum viḷakkiṉi,
maṇ_uḷār viṉi tīrkkum maruntiṉi,---
paṇ_uḷār payilum tiruk kōḷili
_aṇṇalār---_aṭiyē toḻutu _uymmiṉē!

*3
nāḷum nammuṭi nāḷkaḷ _aṟikilōm;
_āḷum nōykaḷ _ōr_aimpatōṭu_āṟu-_eṭṭum;
_ēḻimippaṭṭu _iruntu, nīr, niyātē,
kōḷili(y) _araṉ pātamē kūṟumē!

*4
viḻaviṉ _ōci _oli _aṟāt taṇpoḻil,
paḻakiṉār viṉi tīrkkum, paḻam pati---
_aḻal kiyāṉ _amarum---tirukkōḷilik
kuḻakaṉār tiruppātamē kūṟumē!

*5
mūlam _ākiya mūvarkkum mūrttiyi,
kālaṉ _ākiya kālaṟkum kālaṉi,---
kōlam _ām poḻil cūḻ tiruk kōḷilic
cūlapāṇitaṉ---pātam toḻumiṉē!

*6
kāṟṟaṉi, kaṭalnañcu _amutu_uṇṭa veṇ-
nīṟṟaṉi, nimirpuṉcaṭi _aṇṇali,
_āṟṟaṉi,---_amarum tiruk kōḷili
_ēṟṟaṉār---_aṭiyē toḻutu _ēttumē!

*7
vētam_āya viṇṇōrkaḷtalivaṉi,
_ōti maṉ_uyir _ēttum _oruvaṉi,---
kōti vaṇṭu _aṟiyum tiruk kōḷili
vētānāyakaṉ---pātam virumpumē!

*8
nītiyāl-toḻuvārkaḷ talivaṉi,
vāti_āṉa viṭukkum maṇiyiṉi,---
kēti vaṇṭu _aṟiyum tiruk kōḷili
vētanāyakaṉ, nittal niṉimiṉē!

*9
mālum nāṉmukaṉālum _aṟivu _oṇāp
pāliṉ meṉmoḻiyāḷ _orupaṅkaṉi,
kōlam _ām poḻil cūḻ tiruk kōḷili
nīlakaṇṭaṉi, nittal niṉimiṉē!

*10
_arakkaṉ_āya _ilaṅkiyarmaṉṉaṉi
nerukki _am muṭipattu _iṟuttāṉ, _avaṟku
_irakkam _ākiyavaṉ, tiruk kōḷili
_arutti_āy _aṭiyē toḻutu _uymmiṉē!

• patikam: 1:1
  • talam: tirup piramapuram (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭi
  • vi.mu.cu.: tokuti 2, pakkam 164

*1
tōṭu _uṭiya ceviyaṉ, viṭi _ēṟi, _ōr tū veṇmati cūṭi,
kāṭu _uṭiya cuṭalip poṭi pūci, _eṉ _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
_ēṭu _uṭiya malarāṉ muṉināḷ paṇintu _ētta, _aruḷceyta,
pīṭu _uṭiya piramāpuram mēviya pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*2
muṟṟal_āmi _iḷa nākamoṭu _ēṉamuḷikkompu _avi pūṇṭu,
vaṟṟal_ōṭu kalaṉāp pali tērntu, _eṉatu _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
kaṟṟal kēṭṭal _uṭiyār periyār kaḻal kiyāl---toḻutu _ētta,
peṟṟam _ūrnta, piramāpuram mēviya, pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*3
nīr paranta nimirpuṉcaṭimēl _or nilāveṇmati cūṭi,
_ūr paranta _iṉaveḷvaḷi cōra, _eṉ _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
_ūṉ paranta _ulakiṉ mutal_ākiya _ōr _ūr _itu _eṉṉap
pēr paranta piramāpuram mēviya pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*4
viṇ makiḻnta matil _eytatum _aṉṟi, viḷaṅku tali_ōṭṭil
_uḷ makiḻntu, pali tēriya vantu, _eṉatu _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
maṇ makiḻnta _aravam, malarkkoṉṟi, malinta varimārpil
peṇ makiḻnta, piramāpuram mēviya, pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*5
_orumi peṇmi _uṭiyaṉ! caṭiyaṉ! viṭi _ūrum(m)_ivaṉ! _eṉṉa
_arumi_āka _uriceyya _amarntu, _eṉatu _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
karumi peṟṟa kaṭal koḷḷa, mitantatu _or kālam(m) _itu _eṉṉap
perumi peṟṟa piramāpuram mēviya pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*6
maṟi kalanta _olipāṭaloṭu _āṭalar_āki, maḻu _ēnti,
_iṟi kalanta _iṉaveḷvaḷi cōra, _eṉ _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
kaṟi kalanta kaṭi _ār poḻil, nīṭu_uyarcōli, katir cintap
piṟi kalanta, piramāpuram mēviya, pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*7
caṭi muyaṅku puṉalaṉ(ṉ), _aṉalaṉ(ṉ), _eri vīcic catirvu _eyta,
_uṭi muyaṅkum _aravōṭu _uḻitantu, _eṉatu _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
kaṭal muyaṅku kaḻi cūḻ kuḷirkāṉal _am poṉ _am ciṟaku _aṉṉam
peṭi muyaṅku piramāpuram mēviya pemmāṉ _ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*8
viyar _ilaṅku vari _untiya tōḷkaḷi vīram viḷivitta
_ur _ilaṅki _ariyaṉ vali ceṟṟu, _eṉatu _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
tuyar _ilaṅkum(m) _ulakil pala_ūḻikaḷ tōṉṟumpoḻutu_ellām
peyar _ilaṅku piramāpuram mēviya pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*9
tāḷ nutal ceytu, _iṟi kāṇiya, māloṭu taṇtāmariyāṉum,
nīṇutal ceytu _oḻiya(n) nimirntāṉ, _eṉatu _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
vāḷnutal cey makaḷīr mutal_ākiya viyattavar _ētta,
pēṇutalcey piramāpuram mēviya pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*10
puttaroṭu poṟi _il camaṇum puṟamkūṟa, neṟi nillā
_otta colla, _ulakam pali tērntu, _eṉatu _uḷḷam kavar kaḷvaṉ---
mattayāṉi maṟuka(v), _uri pōrttatu _ormāyam(m)_itu! _eṉṉa,
pittarpōlum, piramāpuram mēviya pemmāṉ---_ivaṉ _aṉṟē!

*11
_aruneṟiya maṟi valla muṉi _akaṉ poyki _alar mēya,
peru neṟiya, piramāpuram mēviya pemmāṉ_ivaṉtaṉṉi,
_oru neṟiya maṉamvittu _uṇar ñāṉacampantaṉ(ṉ) _uriceyta
tiru neṟiya tamiḻ vallavar tolviṉi tīrttal _eḷitu_āmē.

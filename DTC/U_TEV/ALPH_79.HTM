[[A short note on the shrine (VMS):
This shrine was on the north bank of Koḷḷitam river, at a place called Karimēṭu; prior to the Mēṭṭūr dam, the floods in Koḷḷitam completely damaged the temple; one Paḻaṉiappa Mudaliar who belonged to the paṭuki mutaliyār family built a temple to the south of nelvāyil and established the lord and tēvi and other deities.

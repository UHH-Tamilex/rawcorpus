*1

_irumpiṉ _ūṟal _aṟātatu _ōr veṇtalai
_eṟumpiyūrmalaiyāṉ, _eṅkaḷ _īcaṉē.

*2
piṟaṅku ceñcaṭaip piññakaṉ; pēṇu cīrk
kaṟaṅku pūtakaṇam _uṭaik kaṇṇutal---
naṟuṅkuḻalmaṭavāḷoṭu nāḷtoṟum
_eṟumpiyūrmalaiyāṉ, _eṅkaḷ _īcaṉē.

*3
maruntu, vāṉavar tāṉavarkku _iṉcuvai;

poruntu pūṇ mulai maṅkainallāḷoṭum
_eṟumpiyūrmalaiyāṉ, _eṅkaḷ _īcaṉē.

*4

_aṟam purintu _aruḷceyta _em _amkaṇaṉ---
_eṟumpiyūrmalaiyāṉ, _eṅkaḷ _īcaṉē.

*5

_uṟum poṉmālvaraip pētaiyōṭu _ūrtoṟum
_eṟumpiyūrmalaiyāṉ, _eṅkaḷ _īcaṉē.

*6

_eṟumpiyūr _araṉ ceyta _iyaṟkaiyē!

*7

_eṟumpiyūr _araṉ ceyta _iyaṟkaiyē!

*8

māṭṭuppaḷḷi makiḻntu uṟaivīrkku elām
for all those who live happily in the wealthy places, without any idea of god
itu kēṭṭuppaḷḷi kaṇṭīr
you know that this is the place for ruin.
keṭuvīr
Those who will ruin yourselves, if you believe this life, which is transient as permanent
ōṭṭuppaḷḷiviṭṭu ōṭal uṟā muṉam, kāṭṭuppaḷḷi uḷāṉ kaḻal cērmiṉē
approach the feet of Civaṉ in Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi before life goes out of its abode which is not permanent
{5:84}__2+
{$}
(People of this world!)
nīr māṭṭait tēṭi nummuḷē makiḻntu
you feel happy within yourself by earning wealth.
[[māṭu: wealth]]
[[PP: māṭu alla maṟṟaiyavai (Kuṟal, 400)]]
nāṭṭup poy elām pēciṭum nāṇilīr
you who are shameless and speak all sorts of lies found in the country!
kūṭṭai viṭṭu uyir pōvataṉ muṉṉamē
before life leaves the body
kāṭṭuppaḷḷiyuḷāṉ kaḻal cērmiṉē
approach the feet of Civaṉ in Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi
{5:84}__3+
{$}
tēṉai veṉṟa collāḷoṭu celvamum
losing the wealth and the wife who speaks words sweeter than honey
ūṉai viṭṭu uyir pōvataṉ muṉṉamē
before life goes out of the body
kāṉa vēṭar karutu kāṭṭuppaḷḷi, ñāṉa nāyakaṉaic ceṉṟu naṇṇumē
go and approach the chief of spiritual wisdom in Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi which is worshipped by the hunters living in the forest regarding it to be a great shrine
{5:84}__4+
{$}
aruttamum maṉaiyāḷoṭu makkaḷum poruttam illai
wealth, wife and children will not be always agreeing with us
pollātatu 1. pōkkiṭum karuttaṉ
the cuse of all things who will remove all evils (karuttaṉ=karttaṉ)
kaṇṇutal aṇṇal kāṭṭuppaḷḷit tiruttaṉ cēvaṭiyaic ceṉṟu cērmiṉē
(People of this world!) go and reach the red feet of the pure god in Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi who has an eye on the forehead
[[Variant reading: (1) pōkaṭum]]
{5:84}__5+
{$}
(People of this world)
cuṟṟamum tuṇaiyum maṉai vāḻkkaiyum aṟṟapōtu aṇaiyār avar eṉṟu eṉṟē
always thinking that the relations, wife and house-holder's life will not follow us when life goes out of the body
kaṟṟavarkaḷ karutum kāṭṭuppaḷḷip peṟṟam ēṟum pirāṉ aṭi cērmiṉē
approach the feet of the Lord who rides on a bull in Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi on which learned people fix their thoughts
[[eṉṟu eṉṟē cērmiṉ is the syntactical link.]]

{$}
aṭumpum koṉṟaiyum vaṉṉiyum mattamum tuṭumpal cey caṭait tū maṇic cōtiyāṉ
the brilliant light that is emitted from flawless diamond who has on his caṭi flowers of hare-leaf, koṉṟi flowers, leaves of indian mesquit and datura flowers arranged closely
[[aṭumpu is a variant form of aṭampu;
aṭumpu ottaṉaiya aḻal maḻuvā (appar, pacupatittiruviruttam, 2)]]
kaṭampaṉ tātai
the father of Kaṭampaṉ (Murukaṉ)
[[As Murukaṉ is partial to the Kaṭampa tree and its flowers he is called Katampaṉ;
kaṭampu amar kāḷai tātai (appar, taṉittirunērici, 4)]]
karutum kāṭṭuppaḷḷi
Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi which is desired by him
uṭampiṉārkku ōr uṟutuṇai ākumē
is a boon companion to all those who have bodies

meyyiṉ mācu uṭaiyār uṭal mūṭuvār poyyai mey eṉṟu pukku uṭaṉ vīḻaṉ miṉ
(People of this world!) All of you do not fall into the mire of the lies spoken by buddhists who cover their bodies and amaṇar who have dirt on their bodies, mistaking them to be the truth
kaiyil māṉ uṭaiyāṉ kāṭṭuppaḷḷi em aiyaṉ taṉ aṭiyē aṭaintu uymmiṉē
save yourselves by approaching the feet only of our father in Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi who has a deer in his hands
{5:84}__8+
{$}
pētai kāḷ
ignorant people!
vēlai veṉṟa kaṇṇārai virumpi
desiring the women who have eyes more cruel than the vēl
nīr cīlam keṭṭut tikaiyaṉ miṉ
do not get confounded having lost your good conduct.
kāṭṭuppaḷḷi(y) uṟai nīlakaṇṭaṉai nittal niṉaimiṉ; kālaiyē toḻum
dily worship the god with the blue neck who dwells in Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi fixing him in your thought, early in your life.
[[(paḷḷi(y) uṟai
the letter within brackets is an augmentation for metre]]

iṉṟu uḷār nāḷai illai eṉum poruḷ oṉṟum ōrātu uḻitarum ūmarkāḷ
Dumb persons who wander without Knowing little the fact that people who are alive today are no more the next day;
[[cf. neru nal uḷaṉ oruvaṉ iṉṟu illai eṉṉum, perumai uṭaittu iv ulaku (Kuṟal, 336)]]
aṉṟu vāṉavarkku āka viṭam uṇṭa kaṇṭaṉār kāṭṭuppaḷḷi kaṇṭu uymmiṉē
redeem yourselves by visiting Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi of Civaṉ who drank the poison in the distant past, in order to save the celestial beings (The melliṉa etukai in this verse is noteworthy)

eṇṇilā arakkaṉ malai ēntiṭa
when the thoughtless arakkaṉ lifted the mountin (kayili) and held it in his hand
eṇṇi
thinking he could be corrected only by punishment
nīḷ muṭi pattum iṟuttavaṉ kāṭṭuppaḷḷiyai
Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi of Civaṉ who crushed all the ten tall crowns.
kaṇṇuḷār karutum kāṭṭuppaḷḷiyai naṇṇuvār avar tam viṉai nācamē
the Karmams of those who reach Kāṭṭuppaḷḷi on which people endowed with the eye of wisdom fix their thoughts, will be destroyed.

{2:90}__1+
{$} kantam māmalar unti
pushing big fragrant flowers
kaṭumpuṉal nivāmalku karaimēl
on the bank of the river nivā in which swift water is inceasing.
amtaṇcōlai nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷtamaruḷ (ē)
the grace of the god in the temple arattuṟai in nelvayil which has beautiful and cool gardens.
entai īcaṉ emperumāṉ, ēṟuamar kaṭavuḷ' eṉṟu ēnti
praising Civaṉ as, our father, Lord of the universe, our Lord and who is seated on a bull'
cintai ceypavarkku allāl
except for those who meditated on him in the above-mentioned manner.
ceṉṟu kai kūṭuvatu aṉṟu (āl)
approaching him proper is difficult.
[[(nelvāyil is the name of the place; arattuṟai is the name of the shrine) (nivā-the river now known as veḷḷāṟu flowing about 4 miles north of citamparam) cuntarar, nelvayil arattuṟai, 1,2,4,5,6,7; tirumoḻi 3-2-7]]
{2:90}__2+
{$} vāri māmalar untivarupuṉal nivāmalku karaimēl
on the bank of the river, nivā in which the arriving floods take in a sweep big flowers and pushing them.
ārum cōlai nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷ tamaruḷ (ē)
the grace of the god in arattuṟai in nelvāyil which is full of gardens.
īravārcaṭai taṉmēl iḷampiṟai aṇinta emperumāṉ cīrum celvamum (1) ēttāc(2) citaṭarkaḷ toḻalcelvatu aṉṟu (āl)
is not such a thing that it will reach the intellectually blind people who do not sincerely praise the wealth and fame of our Lord who adorns his cool and long caṭai with a young crescent, but simply worship with joined hands mechanically.
[[(variant readings: (1) eytāc (2) cittarkaḷ]]
{2:90}__3+
{$} maṇikalantu poṉ untivarupuṉal nivāmalku karaimēl
on the bank of the river, nivā bringing floods pushing gold and mingling with precious stones.
aṇikalanta nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷtam aruḷ (ē)
the grace of god in arattuṟai in nelvāyil united with beauty.
piṇi kalanta puṉcaṭaimēl piṟai aṇicivaṉ eṉappēṇi
cherishing with love the Lord as Civaṉ who adorns his ruddy caṭai intertwined with a crescent.
paṇi kalantu ceyyāta pāvikaḷ toḻac celvatu aṉṟu (āl)
will not reach the cruel people who do not perform service to him by the mind, the speech, and the body being in agreement, but simply worship with joined hands mechanically.
{2:90}__4+
{$} poṉṉum māmaṇi(um) untip porupuṉal nivāmalku karaimēl
on the bank of the river, nivā in which the floods pushing gold and precious stones and dash against the bank.
aṉṉam ārum nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷtam aruḷ(ē)
the grace of the god who dwells in the temple arattuṟai in nelvāyil where swans live in large numbers.
tuṉṉa āṭai oṉṟu uṭuttu
wearing as dress a loin cloth which is stitched
[[eṉ appaṉ, em pirāṉ, ellārkkum tāṉ īcaṉ, tuṉṉam pey kōvaṇamāk koḷḷumāṟu eṉṉēṭi, maṉṉukalai tuṉṉu poruḷ maṟai nāṉkē vāṉcaraṭāt, taṉṉaiyē kōvaṇamāc cāttiṉaṉ kāṇ cāḻalō (tiruvācakam, tiruccāḻal, 2)]]
tūyaveṇṇīṟṟiṉarāki
smearing themselves with pure sacred ash.
uṉṉi naipavarkku allāl oṉṟum kai kūṭuvatu aṉṟu (āl)
is not such an easy thing to reach even a little to other people except those who think of him and languish.
{2:90}__5+
{$} muruku uriñcu pūñcōlai moym malar cumantu iḻi nivāvantu aruku uriñcu nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷtam aruḷ (ē)
the grace of the god who dwells in arattuṟai in nelvāyil where the river, sivā, comes and rubs the banks carrying the crowded flowers from the flower gardens from which the fragrance rubs all things and flows.
veruku uriñcu veṅkāṭṭil āṭiyavimalaṉ eṉṟu uḷki
meditating as the immaculate god who danced in the hot cremation ground where the wild cats rub their bodies.
urukinaipavarkku allāl
except for those whose hearts melt and languish.
oṉṟum kai kūṭuvatu aṉṟu (āl)
for others is impossible to reach them at even a little.
{2:90}__6+
{$} (1) kuravam māmalar untik kuḷirpuṉal nivāmalku karaimēl
on the bank of the river, niva which has cool increasing water which is pushing big flowers of bottle-flower trees.
aravam ārum nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷ tam aruḷ(ē)
the grace of the god in the temple arattuṟai in nelvāyil where bustle fills everywhere.
uravu nīrc caṭaikkaranta oruvaṉ eṉṟu uḷkuḷirntu ētti
praising to the complete satisfaction of the heart as the unequalled one who concealed the spreading water in the caṭai.
paravinaipavarkku allāl
except for those who praise him in the second person and languish
parintukai kūṭuvatu aṉṟu (āl)
for others is difficult to reach that with affection.
[[Variant reading: (1) kuravam nīṭuyarcōlaik]]
{2:90}__7+
{$} kōlam māmalar untik kuḷirpuṉal nivāmalku karaimēl
on the bank of the river, nivā of increasing cool water which is pushing big and beautiful flow-ers.
(mayil) ālum cōlai nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷ tam aruḷ(ē)
the grace of the god in the temple arattuṟai in nelvāyil which has gardens in which peacocks dance.
nīla māmaṇi miṭaṟṟu nīṟu aṇi civaṉ eṉappēṇum cīlam māntarkaṭku allāl ceṉṟu kai kūṭuvatu aṉṟu (āl)
it is difficult for others except for those who cherish Civaṉ with love and have good conduct as Civaṉ who smears sacred ash and has a blue neck like the sapphire
{2:90}__8+
{$} ceḻuntaṇ mālvarai eṭuttaceruvali irāvaṇaṉ alaṟa
Irāvaṇaṉ who has strength enough to fight and who lifted the fertile, cool and big mountain, Kayilai, to roar.
aḻunta ūṉṟiyaviralāṉ! pōṟṟi eṉpārkku allatu
except for those who say god who has a toe which he fixed to press close! protect me.
koḻuṅkaṉi cumantu untik kuḷir puṉal nivā malku karaimēl aḻuntum cōlai nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷ tam aruḷ (ē) aruḷāṉ
the god in the temple arattuṟai, in nelvāyil which has gardens close to one another, on the bank of the river, nivā, which has swelling cool water which carries and pushes luxuriant fruits, will not grant his grace.
{2:90}__9+
{$} maṇam kamaḻntu poṉ unti varupuṉal nivā malku karaimēl
on the bank of the river, nivā which has swelling floods and which spreads fragrance and pushes gold.
aṇaṅkum cōlai nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷ tam aruḷ (ē)
the grace of the god in the temple arattuṟai in nelvāyil which has gardens united with that place.
nuṇaṅku nūl ayaṉ (um) mālum iruvarum nōkka ariyāṉai vaṇaṅki naipavarkku allāl
except for those who bow before Civaṉ who could not be seen by Ayaṉ who had studied works of subtle truths, and Māl, and languish.
vantu kai cūṭuvatu aṉṟu (āl)
for others is difficult to reach it.
{2:90}__10+
{$} pūkkamaḻntu poṉ untip porupuṉal nivāmalku karaimēl
on the bank of the river, nivā of swelling floods, which dashes against the banks and carries fragrance of flowers and pushes gold.
ārkkum cōlai nelvāyil arattuṟai aṭikaḷ tam aruḷ (ē)
the grace of the god who dwells in the temple, arattuṟai in nelvāyil having gardens which make a big sound when wind passes through them.
cākkiyap paṭuvārum
those who have fallen into the buddhists religion.
camaṇpaṭuvārkaḷum maṟṟum pākkiyappaṭal illāp pāvikaḷ toḻac celvatu aṉṟu (āl)
and the sinners like those who have fallen into amaṇ religion and others who do not have the good fortune to embrance caivism, will feel it difficult to reach it if they worship with joined hands mechanically.
{2:90}__11+
{$} kaṟaiyiṉ ārpoḻil cūḻnta kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ
campantaṉ who is a native of kāḻi surrounded by dark gardens due to the densensess of trees.
aṟaiyum pūmpuṉal paranta arattuṟai aṭikaḷtam aruḷai
about the grace of the god in arattuṟai where the beautiful water which dashes against the banks, spreads.
muṟaimaiyāl coṉṉa pāṭal moḻiyum māntartam viṉaipōyp paṟaiyum
the acts, good and bad of those who can recite the verses which were sung by him in the proper manner will decrease gradually leaving them.
pāṭṭu ivai pattum vallārkkē
only for those who are well versed in the meaning of these ten verses.
aiyuṟavu illai
there is no doubt about it.

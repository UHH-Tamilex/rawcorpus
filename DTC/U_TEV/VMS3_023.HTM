[[This shrine is popularly know as Kūvam]]
{3:23}__1+
{$} uruviṉ ār umaiyoṭum oṉṟi niṉṟatu ōr tiruviṉāṉ
Civaṉ has the brilliance of being united with Umi who is full of beauty.
vaḷar caṭait tiṅkaḷ kaṅkaiyāṉ
has on his growing caṭi a crescent and the river, Kañki.
veruvi vāṉavar toḻa vekuṇṭu nōkkiya ceruviṉāṉ (1) uṟaiviṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
Tiruviṟkōlam is the dwelling place of Civaṉ who gazed in the battle with anger when the celestials worshipped him being afrid (of the acurar in the three cities)
[[The words within brackets are supplied to complete the sense, in keeping with following verses.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) uṟai tarum tiruviṟkōlamē.]]
[[The reading will apply to all the following verses in this decade]]
{3:23}__2+
{$} ciṟṟiṭai umai oru paṅkaṉ
Civaṉ has Umi of small waist on one half.
aṅkaiyil uṟṟatu ōr eriyiṉaṉ
has on the palm a fire.
oru carattiṉāl veṟṟikoḷ avuṇarkaḷ puraṅkaḷ ventu aṟac ceṟṟavaṉ uṟaiviṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
tiruviṟkōlam is the dwelling-place of Civaṉ who destroyed completely by burning the cities of the avuṇar who had been formerly victorious, by a single arrow.

{3:23}__3+
{$} aiyaṉ
Civaṉ is the chief.
nal aticayaṉ
has great wonders.
ayaṉ viṇṇōr toḻum mai aṇi kaṇṭaṉār 1. vaṇṇam vaṇṇavāṉ
the colour of the god who is beautified in the neck by the poison and who is worshipped by the celestials led by Ayaṉ; is the colour of the evening sky.
pai aravu alkulāḷ 2. pākam ākavum ceyyavaṉ uṟaiviṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
tiruviṟkōlam is the dwelling place of Civaṉ who is red in colour though Umi who has a waist like the hood of the cobra, is on one half. (The blue colour of Umi has not diminished the red colour of Civaṉ)
[[Variant reading: 1. vaṇṇam vaṇṇavāṉ vaṇṇam vaṇṇavāṉ 2. ākamum]]
{3:23}__4+
{$} muṉikku muṉ aṟam vitaittavaṉ
Civaṉ impressed in the minds of the sages, long ago, moral teachings.
muṉ kālaṉai utaittu avaṉ uyir iḻantu uruṇṭu vīḻtarap putaittavaṉ
who diminished the strength of Kīlaṉ by kicking him long ago to make him fall rolling on the ground losing his life.
puraṅkaḷ muṉṟaiyum citaittavaṉ uṟaiviṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
tiriṟkōkam is the dwelling place of the Lord who destroyed all the long cities which were known by the names of puram.
{3:23}__5+
{$} muntiṉāṉ
Civaṉ is the primordial god.
mūvaruḷ mutalvaṉ āyiṉāṉ
is the chief among Ayaṉ, ari and Uruttiraṉ, the trinity.
1. kontu ulām malarppoḻil kūkam mēviṉāṉ
desires to dwell in kūkam which has gardens in which the breeze moves in the bunches of flowers. ((kūkam Kūkam; this is now known as Kūvam)
antivāṉ piṟaiyiṉāṉ
wears a crescent that rises in the evening.
aṭiyār mēl viṉai cintuvāṉ uṟaiviṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
-tiruviṟkōlam is the dwelling place of Civaṉ who destroys all the acts which are attached to devotees.
[[Variant reading: 1. kontalar]]
{3:23}__6+
{$} tokuttavaṉ arumaṟai aṅkam
Civaṉ codified the abstruse vētams which were spread out as many and the six aṅkams.
ākamam vakuttavaṉ
classified the ākamams.
vaḷar poḻil kūkam mēviṉāṉ
desired Kūkam which has growing gardens.
mikuttavaṉ
has no equals and superiors.
mikuttavar puraṅkaḷ ventu aṟac cekuttavaṉ
destroyed completely the cities of the proud acurar, to be burnt by fire.
uṟaiviṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
his dwelling place is tiruviṟkōlam.
{3:23}__7+
{$} arumaṟai virittavaṉ
Civaṉ gave out in detil the meanings of the vētams.
viricaṭai veḷḷam tarittavaṉ
bore the flood on the loosened caṭi.
tariyalar puraṅkaḷ ācu aṟa erittavaṉ
burnt the cities of the enemies entirely without any trace of them remining.
ilaṅkaiyar kōṉ iṭarpaṭac cirittavaṉ uṟaiviṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
tiruviṟkōlam is the dwelling place of Civaṉ who lughed when the King of ilanki underwent suffering. (To make him roar was sport for Civaṉ)

VMS9 / {3:23}__PIFI8+
{$} tiritarupuram ericeyta cēvakaṉ
Civaṉ, the warrior, who burnt the cities wandering in the sky.
viri aravoṭu mati caṭaiyil vaittavaṉ
placed the crescent along with a cobra with lines.
ariyoṭu piramaṉatu āṟṟalāl urutteriyalaṉ uṟai viṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
tiruviṟkōlam is the dwelling place of Civaṉ whose form Piramaṉ along with Māl, could not know, by their own efforts.
VMS10 / {3:23}__PIFI9+
{$} cīrmai il camaṇoṭu cīvarakkaiyar nīrmaiyil uraikaḷ koḷḷāta nēcarkku
to those loving devotees who do not accept as truth the words which have no inherent goodness, of the camaṇar who have no excellence and the buddhists who are despicable people wearing a robe called cīvaram!
pārmali peruñcelvam parintu nalkiṭum cīrmaiyiṉāṉ iṭam tiruviṟkōlamē
tiruviṟkōlam is the place of the famous Civaṉ who grants with affection great wealth which is abundant in this world.
VMS11 / {3:23}__PIFI10+
{$} kōṭal veṇpiṟaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who wears a curved white crescent
[[cf. kōṇiya piṟai cūṭiyai cuntarar, tiruppaṇṭikoṭumuṭi, 10]]
kūkam mēviya cēṭaṉa ceḻumatil tiruviṟkōlattai
about tiruviṟkōlam which has strong walls of fortifications of the god who desired Kūkam.
1. nāṭavalla tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ pāṭavallārkaḷukku illaiyām pāvamē
there will be no sins attaching to those who can sing the verses of ñaṉacampantaṉ whose knowledge of tamiḻ is fit for research.
[[Note. In every verse of this decade the destruction of the three cities is mentioned this is the shrine where Civaṉ remins holding bow which destroyed the cities in his hand so this shrine got the name of tiruviṟkōlam.]]

*1
vaḷ vāya mati miḷirum vaḷarcaṭiyiṉāṉi,
maṟiyavaṉi, vāymoḻiyi, vāṉavartam kōṉi,
puḷ vāyik kīṇṭu _ulakam viḻuṅki _umiḻntāṉi,
poṉniṟattiṉ muppurinūl nāṉmukattiṉāṉi,---
muḷ vāya maṭal-taḻuvi, muṭattāḻi _īṉṟu
moṭṭu _alarntu, viri nāṟum muruku viri poḻil cūḻ,
kaḷ vāya karuṅkuvaḷi kaṇvaḷarum kaḻaṉi---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*2
_oru mēka-mukil_āki, _ottu _ulakamtāṉ_āy,
_ūrvaṉavum niṟpaṉavum _ūḻikaḷum tāṉ_āy,

karumēti centāmari mēyum kaḻaṉi---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*3
_irumpu _uyarnta mū_iliyacūlattiṉāṉi,
_iṟiyavaṉi, maṟiyavaṉi, _eṇkuṇattiṉāṉi,

_arumpu _uyarnta _aravintattu _aṇi malarkaḷ _ēṟi,
_aṉṉaṅkaḷ viḷiyāṭum _akaṉ kuṟiyiṉ _arukē
karumpu _uyarntu, peruñ cennel neruṅki viḷi kaḻaṉi---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*4
pūḷi puṉi koṉṟiyoṭu puricaṭiyiṉāṉi,
puṉal_āki, _aṉal_āki, pūtaṅkaḷ_aintu_āy,
nāḷi _iṉṟu nerunal_āy, _ākāyam_āki,
nāyiṟu_āy, matiyam_āy, niṉṟa _emparaṉi,---
pāḷi paṭu piṅkamukiṉ cūḻal, _iḷan teṅkiṉ

kāḷivaṇṭu pāṭa, mayil _ālum vaḷar cōli---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*5
cerukku vāyp piṅkaṇ veḷ_aravu _ariyiṉāṉi,
tēvarkaḷ cūḷāmaṇiyi, ceṅkaṇ viṭiyāṉi,
murukkuvāy malar _okkum tirumēṉiyāṉi,
muṉṉili_āy muḻutu_ulakam_āya perumāṉi,---
_irukku vāy _antaṇarkaḷ _eḻupiṟappuḷ _eṅkum
vēḷvi _iruntu _iru nitiyam vaḻaṅkum nakar, _eṅkum
karukku vāyp peṇṇiyoṭu teṅku mali cōli---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*6
viṭi _aravak koṭi _ēntum viṇṇavartam kōṉi,
veḷḷattu māl_avaṉum vētamutalāṉum
_aṭi_iṇiyum tirumuṭiyum kāṇa _aritu_āya
caṅkaraṉi, tattuvaṉi,---tiyalmaṭavārkaḷ
_uṭi _aviḻa, kuḻal _aviḻa, kōti kuṭintu_āṭa,
kuṅkumaṅkaḷ _unti varu koḷḷiṭattiṉ karimēl,
kaṭikaḷ viṭu vār kuvaḷikaḷi vārum kaḻaṉi---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*7
_arumaṇiyi, muttiṉi, _āṉ_añcum_āṭum
_amararkaḷtam perumāṉi, _arumaṟiyiṉ poruḷi,
tirumaṇiyi, tīmkarumpiṉ _ūṟal _irun tēṉi,

karumaṇikaḷ pōl nīlam malarkiṉṟa kaḻaṉi---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*8
_iḻi taḻuvu veṇnūlum mēvu tirumārpiṉ
_īcaṉ, taṉ _eṇkōḷkaḷ vīci _eri_āṭa,
kuḻi taḻuvu tirukkātil kōḷ _aravam _acittu,
kōvaṇam koḷ kuḻakaṉi, kuḷircaṭiyiṉāṉi,---
taḻi taḻuvu taṇ niṟatta cennel_ataṉ _ayalē
taṭan taraḷa meṉ karumpiṉ tāḻ kiṭaṅkiṉ _arukē,
kaḻi taḻuvit tēṉ toṭukkum kaḻaṉi cūḻ paḻaṉa---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*9
kuṉivu _iṉiya katir matiyam cūṭu caṭiyāṉi,
kuṇṭalam cēr kātavaṉi, vaṇṭu_iṉaṅkaḷ pāṭap
paṉi _utirum caṭiyāṉi, pālveṇnīṟṟāṉi,
pala_uruvum taṉ _uruvē _āya perumāṉi,---
tuṉivu _iṉi tūya moḻit toṇṭivāy nallār,

kaṉivu _iṉiya katalivaṉam taḻuvu poḻil-cōli---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

*10
tēvi, _ampoṉmalikkōmāṉtaṉ pāvi, _ākat
taṉatu _uruvam _orupākam cērttuvitta perumāṉ;

tūvi vāy nāriyoṭu kuruku pāyntu _ārppa,

kāvivāy vaṇṭupala paṇ ceyyum kaḻaṉi---
kāṉāṭṭumuḷḷūril kaṇṭu toḻutēṉē.

{1:86}__1+
{$} koṭṭum paṟai cīrāl kuḻuma aṉal ēnti naṭṭam payiṉṟu āṭum nallūrp perumāṉai
the god in Nallūr, who dances always holding fire, to the time-measure of the drum that is beaten in a high pitch.
muṭṭu iṉṟu irupōtum muṉiyātu eḻuntu aṉpupaṭṭa maṉattārkaḷ aṟiyār pāvamē
those who have love in their minds waking up from sleep without dislike, night and day without ceasing, will know no sins.
{1:86}__2+
{$} ēṟil erutu ēṟum
if at all he rides, he rides on a bull.
eḻil āyiḻaiyōṭum vēṟum uṭaṉum ām vikirtar avar eṉṉa
to be prised as the god who is different from the world, and who is united with a beautiful lady of choice ornaments and at the same [time] separate from her;
[[PP: vēṟāy uṭaṉ āṉāṉ iṭam vīḻimmiḻalaiyē
(Campantar, Tiruvīḻimiḻali (1) 2)]]
nāṟum malarp poykai nallūrp perumāṉaik kūṟum aṭiyārkku aṭaiyā kuṟṟamē
faults will not go near the devotees who prise as mentioned above, the god in Nallūr which has a natural tank of fragrant flowers.
{1:86}__3+
{$} cūṭum iḷantiṅkaṭcuṭar poṟcaṭai tāḻa
when the glittering golden caṭi on which a crescent is worn, hangs low.
ōṭu uṇ kalaṉāka ūr ūr iṭupiccai nāṭum neṟiyāṉai
one who has the nature of begging alms given in many places, having the skull as the eating plate.
nallūrpperumāṉaippāṭum aṭiyārkaṭku aṭaiyā pāvamē
sins will not reach the devotees who sing the prises of the god in Nallūr.
{1:86}__4+
{$} nītta neṟiyāṉai
Civaṉ who has renounced all rules.
nātta neṟiyāṉai
who showed us the path framed by him. (ñātta has been changed into nātta)
nallūrp perumāṉai
the god who is in nallūr.
kāttaneṟiyāṉai
who has well-protected modes of devotion.
kai kūppit toḻutu ēttum aṭiyārkaṭku illai iṭar(tāṉ, ē)
there will be no sufferings to the devotees who prise and worship joining their hands.
{1:86}__5+
{$} ākattu umaikēḷvaṉ
Civaṉ is the husband of Umi who is in his body.
nākam acaittāṉai
who tied a cobra.
nallūrp perumāṉai
the god in nallūr.
tākam pukuntu aṇmit tāḷkaḷ toḻum toṇṭar
the servants of god who worship the feet of Civaṉ reaching them with eagerness.
pōka maṉattarāy pukaḻat tirivārē
will wander with their minds filled with enjoyment of pleasure, to be prised by the people of this world.
{1:86}__6+
{$} umai añcakkollum kaḷiyāṉai uri pōrttu
having covered himself with the skin of a rutting and killing elephant, to make Umi fear.
nalla neṟiyāṉai
who is the good path for people of tread.
nallūrp perumāṉai
the god in nallūr.
collu neṟiyāṉaic cērntār
who meditated on the bliss that all should attin at the end.
iṭar tīrac collum aṭiyārkaḷ tukkamē aṟiyār
the devotees who prise him to get their sufferings removed, will not know sorrow.
{1:86}__7+
{$} eṅkaḷ perumāṉai
the god who is exclusively ours.
imaiyōr toḻutu ēttum naṅkaḷ perumāṉai
and our god who is prised and worshipped with joined hands by the celestials who do not wink.
nallūr pirivu illāt tam kai talaikku ēṟṟi āḷ eṉṟu aṭinīḻal taṅkum maṉattārkaḷ
the devotees whose mind stay under the shade of the feet saying I am your slave without redemption, placing their hands on their heads, without leaving nallur.
taṭumāṟṟu aṟuppārē
will completely cut at the root of unsteadiness.
{1:86}__8+
{$} kāmaṉ eḻil vāṭṭi
having destroyed the beauty of Kāmaṉ (by burning him)
kaṭal cūḻ ilaṅkaikkōṉ (1) nāmam iṟuttāṉai
who destroyed the fame of the King of ilaṅki surrounded by the sea.
nallūrp perumāṉai
the god in nallūr.
(1) ēmamaṉattārāy ikaḻātu eḻum toṇṭar
the slaves of god who get up from sleep without negligent with a well guarded mind.
tīpamaṉattārkaḷ aṟiyār tīyavē
having a steady mind like the lamp will not know evil things.
[[Variant readings : (1) ākam (2) ēka, ēva]]
{1:86}__9+
{$} naṇṇa ariyāṉai
Civaṉ who could not be approached.
vaṇṇa malarāṉum
by Piramaṉ who is seated in the red lotus.
vaiyam aḷantāṉum
and by Māl who measured the earth.
nallūrpperumāṉai
who is in nallūr.
(1) taṇ ammalartūvit tāḷkaḷ toḻutu ēttaeṇṇum aṭiyārkaṭku illai iṭukkaṇē
there will be no woes to the devotees who think of prising and worshipping the feet scattering cool and beautiful flowers at them
[[The very thought will be enough there will be no woes.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) taṇṇaṉ malar]]
{1:86}__10+
{$} piccakkuṭai nīḻal camaṇar cākkiyar niccam alartūṟṟa niṉṟaperumāṉai
the god who remins to be slandered dily by the cākkiyar (buddhists) and camaṇar who wander under the shade of an unbrella made of peacock's feathers.
eccum aṭiyārkaṭku illai iṭar (tāṉ, ē)
there will be no sufferings to the devotees who speak highly of Civaṉ (eccum is a rare word)
{1:86}__11+
{$} naṇṇum puṉalvēli nallūrp perumāṉait taṇṇam puṉalkāḻi ñāṉacampantaṉ vaṇṇam puṉaimālai vaikal ēttuvār
those devotees who prise dily with the aid of the garland of beautiful verses composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ, a native of Kāḻi of cool water, on the god in Nallūr bounded on all sides by water.
viṇṇum nilaṉumāy viḷaṅkum pukaḻārē
will attin fame which is renowned in heaven and the earth.

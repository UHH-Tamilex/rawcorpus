māmāttu ākiya māl ayaṉ, mālkoṭu tāmāttēṭiyum tāḷmuṭi kāṇkilar
Māl and Ayaṉ who were great arrogant gods could not find out the feet and head by their own efforts without the grace of Civaṉ.
āmāttūr araṉē!(1) aruḷāy eṉṟu eṉṟu ēmāppu eytik kaṇṭār iṟaiyāṉaiyē
they saw Civaṉ saying many times: Araṉ in Āmāttūr! grant us your grace filled with excessive joy.
[[Variant readings: (1) aruḷāyeṉṟē, aruḷāyeṉṟu]]
{5:44}__2+
{$}
cantiyāṉai
Civaṉ who is worshipped in the mornings and evenings.
camāti ceyvār taṅkaḷ puntiyāṉai
who dwells in the minds of those who follow the path of yōkam.
puttēḷir toḻappaṭum antiyāṉai
who has the colour of the evening sky and is worshipped by the celestials.
āmāttūr aḻakaṉai
and who is the beautiful god in āmāttūr.
cintiyātavar tīviṉaiyāḷarē
those who do not think of him are definitely evil persons.
(aḻakaṉ is the name of Civaṉ in this shrine)
{5:44}__3+
{$}
kāmāttam eṉṉum kārvalaippaṭṭu
being cught in the evil net of love and wealth.
nāṉ pōm āttai aṟiyātu pulampuvēṉ
I would wait not knowing the path which I should follow.
āmāttūr araṉē eṉṟu aḻaittalum
when I invoke him by calling by name araṉ in āmāttūr.
tēmāttīṅkaṉipōlat tittikkumē
he is as sweet as the sweet fruit of the extremely sweet mango tree.
{5:44}__4+
{$}
pañcapūtavalaiyil paṭuvataṟku añci
being afrid of being cught in the net of the five elements.
nāṉum
myself who is unworthy.
āmāttūr aḻakaṉai neñciṉāl niṉaintēṉ
I thought of the beautiful god in āmāttūr in my heart.
niṉaivu eytalum
when I thought of him.
vañca āṟukaḷ vaṟṟiṉa
the rivers which have the floods of deceit dried up.
kāṇmiṉ (ē)
(people of this world!) who know this.
{5:44}__5+
{$}
kurāma(ṉ)ṉum kuḻalāḷ orukūṟaṉai
Civaṉ who has the lady whose tresses of hir are adorned with flowers of the bottle flower tree.
arāmaṉṉum caṭaiyāṉ
who has on his caṭi cobras staying permanently.
tiruāmāttūr irāmaṉum vaḻipāṭu cey īcaṉai
Civaṉ in āmāttūr whom Irāmaṉ himself worshipped.
nirāmayaṉtaṉai nāḷum (1) niṉaimiṉē
(People of this world!) Fix your thoughts on the god who is without diseases.
[[Variant reading: (1) niṉaivaṉē]]
{5:44}__6+
{$}
pittaṉai
Civaṉ who has the name pittaṉ.
peruntēvar toḻappaṭum attaṉai
who is the father worshipped by eminent celestials.
aṇi āmāttūr mēviya muttiṉai
who is the pearl dwelling in beautiful āmāttūr.
aṭiyēṉ uḷ muyaṟalum
when I, his slave, tried to meditate on him.
pattiveḷḷam parantatu
the flood of devotion spread everywhere.
kaṇmiṉ (ē)
(People of this world) you know this.
{5:44}__7+
{$}
nīṟṟiṉ ār tirumēṉiyaṉ
Civaṉ has a holy body smeared thickly with sacred ash.
nēriḻai kūṟṟiṉāṉ
has one half a lady adorned with jewels suited to her beauty.
kuḻaṟ kōlac caṭaiyil ōr āṟṟiṉāṉ
has on his beautiful curly caṭi a river.
aṇi āmāttūr mēviya ēṟṟiṉāṉ
who has a bull and who dwells with desire in beautiful Āmāttūr.
(1) emai āḷ uṭai(ya) īcaṉē
is the Lord of the universe who has us as his slave.
[[Variant reading: (1) namai]]
{5:44}__8+
{$}
paṇṇiṟpāṭalkaḷ patticey vittakarkku aṇṇittu ākum amutiṉai
Civaṉ who is the nectar that tastes sweet to those skilled persons who have devotion by singing songs with melody-types.
āmāttūr caṇṇippāṉai
who dwells in āmāttūr.
tamarkku aṇittu āyatuōr kaṇṇiṟ pāvai aṉṉāṉa avaṉ kāṇmiṉ (ē)
(People of this world!) you know that he is as precious as the pupil of the eye and is near at hand to his devotees;

karumaṇiyiṟ pāvāy nīpōtāy (Kuṟal, 1123);
kaṇṇiṟ karumaṇiyēmaṇi āṭupāvāy (nāvukkaracar, tiruvāvaṭutuṟi (5)1)]]

kuṇṭar pīlikaḷ koḷḷum kuṇam ilā piṇṭarōṭu eṉai vēṟupaṭuttu uyyakkoṇṭa nātaṉ
the master who accepted me to be saved separating me from the company of the strong persons who have no good characters and are low persons who hold in their hands bundle of peacock feathers.
kuḷirpuṉal vīraṭṭalattu aṇṭaṉār iṭam
the place of the god who is the first cuse of the world and who is in atiki vīraṭṭam of cool water (Though there are eight viraṭṭams in the one uppermost in the mind of tirunavukkaracar is atiki vīraṭṭam where he was cured of his disease)
āmāttūr kāṇmiṉē
(People of this world!) is āmāttūr (you know this)
{5:44}__10+
{$}
vāṉam cāṭum mati aravattoṭu tāṉ añcātu uṭaṉ vaitta caṭaiyiṭait tēṉ añcu āṭiya teṅku iḷanīroṭum āṉ añcu āṭiya āmāttūr aiyaṉē
the master in āmāttūr is the god who bathed in the five products of the cow along with the tender cocoanut milk on his caṭi in which he placed the crescent which dispels darkness shining in the sky, without fear of the cobra, and which bathed in the mixture of five deliciouss substances; the foremost among them being honey.

vilaṅkal (l) eṭuttāṉ muṭi aṭara ōr viral ūṉṟiya āmāttūr iṭam (atu) ā(ka)k koṇṭa īcaṉukku eṉ uḷam iṭam (atu) ākak koṇṭu iṉpuṟṟiruppēṉē
I am enjoying happiness having set apart my heart as the place to Civaṉ who has Āmāttūr as his shrine and who fixed firmly single toe to press down the heads of the arakkaṉ who lifted the mountin.
viṭalaiyāy

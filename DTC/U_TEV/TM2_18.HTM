*2

_entāy!---takumō, _ivaḷ _ēcaṟavē?

*3
_aṟi _ār kaḻalum, _aḻal vāy _aravum,
piṟi _ār caṭiyum(m), _uṭiyāy! periya
maṟiyār marukal makiḻvāy! _ivaḷi
_iṟi _ār vaḷi koṇṭu, _eḻil vavviṉiyē?

*4
_olinīr caṭiyil karantāy! _ulakam
pali nī tirivāy! paḻi _il pukaḻāy!
mali nīr marukal makiḻvāy! _ivaḷi
meli nīrmiyaḷ _ākkavum vēṇṭiṉiyē?

*5
tuṇi nīlavaṇṇam mukil tōṉṟiyaṉṉa
maṇinīlakaṇṭam(m) _uṭiyāy, marukal!
kaṇi nīlavaṇṭu _ār kuḻalāḷ_ivaḷtam
_aṇi nīla_oṇkaṇ _ayarvu _ākkiṉiyē?

*6
palarum paravappaṭuvāy! caṭimēl
malarum piṟi _oṉṟu _uṭiyāy, marukal!
pularumtaṉiyum tuyilāḷ, puṭi pōntu
_alarum paṭumō, _aṭiyāḷ_ivaḷē?

*7

_eḻuvāḷ; niṉivāḷ, _iravum pakalum;---

*8
_ilaṅkikku _iṟivaṉ vilaṅkal(l) _eṭuppa,

_alaṅkal(l)_ivaḷi _alar _ākkiṉiyē?

*9
_eri _ār caṭiyum(m), _aṭiyum(m), _iruvar

_ariyāḷ_ivaḷi _ayarvu _ākkiṉiyē?

*10

*11

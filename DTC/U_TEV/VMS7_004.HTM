{7:4}__1+
{$} malaikku(n) nikar oppaṉa vaṉ tiraikaḷ valittu eṟṟi muḻaṅki valampuri koṇṭu alaikkum kaṭal am karaimēl makōtai aṇiār poḻil añcaikkaḷattu appaṉē
the father at añcikkaḷam which has gardens full of beauty situated in makōti which is on the shore on which the strong waves that are similar to one another and are as high as the mountin, seize the conches whose spirals turn to the right, dash, roar and drive them hither and thither!
[[makōtai is the name of the city; añcikkalam is the name of the temple there, cūta vakuḷa caraḷa nirai tutaiyum cōlai vaḷanakartāṉ, kōtai aracar makōtai eṉakkulavu peyarum uṭaittu ulakil (periya pūrānam, Kaḻaṟiṟṟaṟivār purāṇam, 4).]]
talaikkut talaimālai aṇintatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for wearing like a jewel a garland of skulls on the head?
caṭaimēl kaṅkaiveḷḷam tarittatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for carrying the flood of Kaṅki on the caṭi?
alaikkum pulittōl koṇṭu acaittatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for tying the skin of a tiger which can kill?
ataṉmēl kata (1) nākam nañcu ārttatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for tying as a belt an angry cobra on that skin?
[[Variant reading: (1) nākakkaccu]]

the maturing oysters which are dashed aginst by the strong waves running fast and curling, open their shells and pour out their pearls.

aṭittu ār kaṭal amkaraimēl makōtai aṇi ārpoḻil añcaikkaḷattu appaṉē

what is the reason for adorning as an ornament a cobra catching hold of it and cusing it to dance?

what is the reason for wearing a crescent on the glittering caṭi?

{$} cantit taṭamāl varaipōl tiraikaḷ taṇiyātu iṭaṉum kaṭal amkaraimēl aṇi ārpoḻil añcaikkaḷattu appaṉē
the father in añcikkalam having gardens full of beauty on the shore of the sea where the waves similar to very big mountins in which bamboos grow, strike aginst the shore without being reduced in its force!
cintittu eḻuvārkku nellik kaṉiyē
you who are as sweet as the ripe fruit of emblic myrobalam to those who think of you and wake up from sleep!
ciṟiyār periyār maṉat tēṟaluṟṟāl
if small people are clear in their minds, about your greatness they will also become great (maṉan tēṟaluṟṟāl has become maṉattēṟaluṟṟāl; the soft consonant being changed into a hard one).
muntittoḻuvār iṟavār piṟavār
those who worship you desiring priority, will not die and will not be born agin.
muṉikaḷ muṉiyē
you who are superior to all sages!
amararkku amarā
the immortal god superior to all so-called immortals!
antittalaic cekkarvāṉē otti (āl)
you have a beautiful form like the red sky in the evening to captivate the minds of devotees!

{$} maḻaikku(n) nikar oppaṉ vaṉtiraikaḷ valintu eṟṟi muḻaṅki valampuri koṇṭu aḻaikkum kaṭal amkaraimēl makōtai aṇi ārpoḻil añcaikkaḷattu appaṉē
the fathe at añcikaḷam which has gardens full of beauty in makōti on the sea-shore, where the strong waves which are like the clouds seize, dash aginst and made a loud noise taking conches whose spirals turn to the right!
iḻaikkum eḻuttukku uyirē otti(āl)
you are like the vowel to the letters that are written, as you actuate and influence the movements of all living beings.
(1) ilaiyē otti (āl); uḷaiyē otti (āl)
you appear to be non-existing; at the same time you are like an existing things.
kuḻaikkum payirukku ōr puyalē otti(āl); aṭiyār tamakku ōr kuṭiyē otti(āl)
you are like the cloud that cuse the crops to sprout; you are like a noble lineage equal to the devotees as you are very near them.
[[variant readings: (1) iṇaiyē; uṉaiyē]]

{$} netiyam pala ceyta kalaccelaviṉ āṭum kaṭalamkaraimēl makōtai aṇiār poḻil añcaikkaḷattu appaṉē
the father at añcikkaḷam which has gardens full of beauty in the city of makōti on the sea-shore which is fit for bathing and which has many ships which have amassed wealth voyaging in the sea!
vīṭiṉ payaṉeṉ
what is the benefit of salvation?
piṟappiṉ payaṉ eṉ
what is the benefit of birth?
(without creating either of the two, salvation and birth what is the benefit of creating both which are quite opposite to each other?)

[[PP: maltērum parimāvum matakaḷiṟum ivai oḻiyap peṟṟu ēṟum perumāṉār

[[PP: kaṭakariyum parimāvum tērum ukantu ēṟātē, iṭapam ukantu ēṟiya vāṟu

the vehicle of an elephant of Civaṉ is called ayirāvaṇam; it has one thousand tusk on each side;

(nāvukkaracar, tiruvarūr, 13-1)]]
[[PP: nirainirai āyiram vakuttamāyiru, maruppiṉ veṇṇiṟac ceṅkaṇ vēḻamum

kūṭum malaimaṅkai oruttiyuṭaṉ caṭaimēl kaṅkaiyāḷai nī cūṭiṟṟu eṉṉē
what is the reason for wearing on your caṭi a lady Kaṅkliyaḷ, in adition to the lady who is the dughter of the mountin who is united with you?
pāṭum pulavarkku aruḷum poruḷ eṉ
what is the gift you give to the poets who sing your prises?

ārkkum kaṭal amkaraimēl .... appaṉē

ākkum aḻivum (m) aiya nī eṉpaṉ nāṉ

colluvār coṟporuḷ (avai) nī eṉpaṉ nāṉ

{$} viḷaṅkum kuḻaikkātu uṭai (ya) vētiyaṉē
the brahmin who wears a shining men's ear-ring in the ear!
kaṭalam karaimēl makōtai aṇiār poḻil añcaikkaḷattu appaṉē
the father in añcikkaḷam having gardens full of beauty in the city of makōti on the sea-shore!
ilaṅkaikku iṟai āyavaṉait talai pattoṭu tōḷ pala iṟṟu viḻak kaṉuttāy
you grew angry so that the ten heads and twenty shoulders of the King of ilanki were crushed and fell
nañcu amutuuṇṭu kaṇṭam kaṉuttāy
your neck became black by consuming the poison that rose in the ocean as if you consumed nectar.
kaṭukap piramaṉtalai aintilum oṉṟu aṟuttāy
you cut off one of the five heads of Piramaṉ, quickly.
maṉaivāḻkkaiyai veṟuttēṉ
I detested the house-holder's life in my mind.
viṭṭu oḻintēṉ
I completely renounced it physically also.

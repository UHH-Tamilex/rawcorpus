  • talam: tirup pukali (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: _intaḷam
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.209

*1
muṉṉiya kalaipporuḷum, mū_ulakil vāḻvum,
paṉṉiya _oruttar paḻa _ūr viṉaviṉ---ñālam
tuṉṉi _imaiyōrkaḷ tuticeytu muṉ vaṇaṅkum
ceṉṉiyar viruppu_uṟu tirup pukali_āmē.

*2
vaṇṭu _irai matic caṭai milaitta puṉal cūṭip
paṇṭu _eri kai _āṭu paramaṉ pati_atu _eṉpar---
puṇṭarika vācam_atu vīca, malarccōlait
teṇtirai kaṭal poli tirup pukali_āmē.

*3
pā _aṇavu cintaiyavar pattaroṭu kūṭi,
nā _aṇavum _antaṇaṉ viruppu_iṭam_atu _eṉpar---
pū _aṇavu cōlai, _iruḷ mālai _etir kūra,
tē vaṇa viḻā vaḷar tirup pukali_āmē.

*4
mai tavaḻum mā miṭaṟaṉ, mānaṭam_atu _āṭi,
kai vaḷaiyiṉāḷoṭu kalanta pati _eṉpar---
cey paṇi peruttu _eḻum _uruttirarkaḷ kūṭi,
teyvam_atu _iṇaṅku _uṟu tirup pukali_āmē.

*5
muṉṉam _iru-mūṉṟucamayaṅkaḷ_avai _āki,
piṉṉai _aruḷceyta piṟaiyāḷaṉ _uṟai kōyil---
puṉṉaiya malarppoḻilkaḷ _akkiṉ _oḷi kāṭṭa,
cennel vayal _ārtaru tirup pukali_āmē.

*6
vaṅkam maliyum kaṭal viṭattiṉai nukarnta
_aṅkaṇaṉ _arutti ceytu _irukkum _iṭam _eṉpar---
koṅku _aṇa viyaṉpoḻiliṉ mācu paṉi mūca,
teṅku _aṇavu tēṉ mali tirup pukali_āmē.

*7
nalkuravum _iṉpamum nalaṅkaḷ_avai _āki,
valviṉaikaḷ tīrttu_aruḷum maintaṉ _iṭam _eṉpar---
palkum _aṭiyārkaḷ paṭi _āra _icai pāṭi,
celva maṟaiyōr _uṟai tirup pukali_āmē.

*8
parappu_uṟu pukaḻp perumaiyāḷaṉ, varaitaṉṉāl
_arakkaṉai _aṭarttu _aruḷum _aṇṇal, _iṭam _eṉpar---
nerukku_uṟu kaṭal-tiraikaḷ muttammaṇi cinta,
cerukku_uṟu poḻil poli tirup pukali_āmē.

*9
kōṭaloṭu kūṉmati kulāya caṭaitaṉmēl
_āṭu_aravam vaittu_aruḷum _appaṉ, _iruvarkkum
nēṭa _eri _āki, _irupālum _aṭi pēṇit
tēṭa, _uṟaiyum nakar---tirup pukali_āmē.

*10
kaṟṟa(_a)maṇar, _uṟṟu _ulavu tērar, _uraiceyta
kuṟṟam moḻi koḷkai_atu _ilāta perumāṉ _ūr---
poṉ-toṭi maṭantaiyarum, maintar, pulaṉ_aintum
ceṟṟavar, viruppu_uṟu tirup pukali_āmē.

*11
centamiḻ parappu_uṟu tirup pukalitaṉmēl,
_antam mutal_āki naṭuvu_āya perumāṉaip
pantaṉ _urai centamiḻkaḷpattum _icai kūra
vanta vaṇam _ēttumavar vāṉam _uṭaiyārē.

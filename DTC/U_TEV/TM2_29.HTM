• patikam: [2:29]
  • talam: tirup pukali (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: _intaḷam
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.209

*1
muṉṉiya kalipporuḷum, mū_ulakil vāḻvum,
paṉṉiya _oruttar paḻa _ūr viṉaviṉ---ñālam
tuṉṉi _imiyōrkaḷ tuticeytu muṉ vaṇaṅkum
ceṉṉiyar viruppu_uṟu tirup pukali_āmē.

*2
vaṇṭu _iri matic caṭi militta puṉal cūṭip
paṇṭu _eri ki _āṭu paramaṉ pati_atu _eṉpar---
puṇṭarika vācam_atu vīca, malarccōlit
teṇtiri kaṭal poli tirup pukali_āmē.

*3
pā _aṇavu cintiyavar pattaroṭu kūṭi,
nā _aṇavum _antaṇaṉ viruppu_iṭam_atu _eṉpar---
pū _aṇavu cōli, _iruḷ māli _etir kūra,
tē vaṇa viḻā vaḷar tirup pukali_āmē.

*4
mi tavaḻum mā miṭaṟaṉ, mānaṭam_atu _āṭi,
ki vaḷiyiṉāḷoṭu kalanta pati _eṉpar---
cey paṇi peruttu _eḻum _uruttirarkaḷ kūṭi,
teyvam_atu _iṇaṅku _uṟu tirup pukali_āmē.

*5
muṉṉam _iru-mūṉṟucamayaṅkaḷ_avi _āki,
piṉṉi _aruḷceyta piṟiyāḷaṉ _uṟi kōyil---
puṉṉiya malarppoḻilkaḷ _akkiṉ _oḷi kāṭṭa,
cennel vayal _ārtaru tirup pukali_āmē.

*6
vaṅkam maliyum kaṭal viṭattiṉi nukarnta
_aṅkaṇaṉ _arutti ceytu _irukkum _iṭam _eṉpar---
koṅku _aṇa viyaṉpoḻiliṉ mācu paṉi mūca,
teṅku _aṇavu tēṉ mali tirup pukali_āmē.

*7
nalkuravum _iṉpamum nalaṅkaḷ_avi _āki,
valviṉikaḷ tīrttu_aruḷum mintaṉ _iṭam _eṉpar---
palkum _aṭiyārkaḷ paṭi _āra _ici pāṭi,
celva maṟiyōr _uṟi tirup pukali_āmē.

*8
parappu_uṟu pukaḻp perumiyāḷaṉ, varitaṉṉāl
_arakkaṉi _aṭarttu _aruḷum _aṇṇal, _iṭam _eṉpar---
nerukku_uṟu kaṭal-tirikaḷ muttammaṇi cinta,
cerukku_uṟu poḻil poli tirup pukali_āmē.

*9
kōṭaloṭu kūṉmati kulāya caṭitaṉmēl
_āṭu_aravam vittu_aruḷum _appaṉ, _iruvarkkum
nēṭa _eri _āki, _irupālum _aṭi pēṇit
tēṭa, _uṟiyum nakar---tirup pukali_āmē.

*10
kaṟṟa(_a)maṇar, _uṟṟu _ulavu tērar, _uriceyta
kuṟṟam moḻi koḷki_atu _ilāta perumāṉ _ūr---
poṉ-toṭi maṭantiyarum, mintar, pulaṉ_aintum
ceṟṟavar, viruppu_uṟu tirup pukali_āmē.

*11
centamiḻ parappu_uṟu tirup pukalitaṉmēl,
_antam mutal_āki naṭuvu_āya perumāṉip
pantaṉ _uri centamiḻkaḷpattum _ici kūra
vanta vaṇam _ēttumavar vāṉam _uṭiyārē.

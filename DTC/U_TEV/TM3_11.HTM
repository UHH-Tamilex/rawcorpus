• patikam: [3:11]
  • talam: tirup puṉavāyil
  • paṇ: kāntārap pañcamam
  • VMS: Vol.11, p.84

*1
miṉ _iyal ceñcaṭai veṇpiṟaiyaṉ, viri nūliṉaṉ,
paṉṉiya nālmaṟai pāṭi _āṭi, pala_ūrkaḷ pōy,
_aṉṉam _aṉṉa(n) naṭaiyāḷoṭum(m) _amarum(m) _iṭam---
puṉṉai nalmāmalar poṉ _utirkkum puṉavāyilē.

*2
viṇṭavartam purammūṉṟu _erittu, viṭai _ēṟip pōy,
vaṇṭu _amarum kuḻal maṅkaiyoṭum makiḻntāṉ _iṭam---
kaṇṭalum ñāḻalum niṉṟu, peruṅkaṭal kāṉalvāyp
puṇṭarīkammalarp poykai cūḻnta puṉavāyilē.

*3
viṭai _uṭai vel koṭi _ēntiṉāṉum, viṟal pāriṭam
puṭai paṭa _āṭiya vēṭattāṉum, puṉavāyilil
toṭai navil koṉṟai_amtāriṉāṉum, cuṭar veṇmaḻup-
paṭai valaṉ _ēntiya, pālney_āṭum, paramaṉ _aṉṟē!

*4
caṅkaveṇtōṭu _aṇi kātiṉāṉum, caṭai tāḻavē
_aṅkai _ilaṅku _aḻal _ēntiṉāṉum(m), _aḻaku_ākavē
poṅku _aravam(m) _aṇi mārpiṉāṉum---puṉavāyilil,
paiṅkaṇ veḷ_ēṟṟu _aṇṇal_āki niṉṟa paramēṭṭiyē.

*5
kali paṭu taṇkaṭal nañcam _uṇṭa kaṟaikkaṇṭaṉum,
puli_ataḷ pāmpu _araic cuṟṟiṉāṉum---puṉavāyilil,
_olitaru taṇpuṉalōṭu, _erukkum, matamattamum,
melitaru veṇpiṟai, cūṭi niṉṟa viṭai_ūrtiyē.

*6
vār _uṟu meṉmulai maṅkai pāṭa naṭam_āṭip pōy,
kār _uṟu koṉṟai veṇtiṅkaḷāṉum, kaṉal vāyatu _ōr
pōr _uṟu veṇmaḻu _ēntiṉāṉum---puṉavāyilil,
cīr _uṟu celvam malka(v) _irunta civalōkaṉē.

*7
peruṅkaṭalnañcu _amutu_uṇṭu, _ukantu peruṅkāṭṭu_iṭait
tiruntu _iḷameṉmulait tēvi pāṭa(n) naṭam_āṭip pōy,
poruntalartam purammūṉṟum _eytu, puṉavāyilil
_iruntavaṉtaṉ kaḻal _ēttuvārkaṭku _iṭar _illaiyē.

*8
maṉam miku vēlaṉ _av vāḷ _arakkaṉ vali _olkiṭa,
vaṉam miku mālvaraiyāl _aṭarttāṉ _iṭam---maṉṉiya
_iṉam miku tolpukaḻ pāṭal _āṭal _eḻil malkiya,
puṉam miku koṉṟai _am teṉṟal _ārnta, puṉavāyilē.

*9
tiru vaḷar tāmarai mēviṉāṉum, tikaḻ pāṟkaṭal
karuniṟavaṇṇaṉum, kāṇpu _ariya kaṭavuḷ(ḷ) _iṭam---
naral curicaṅkoṭum _ippi _unti(n), nalam malkiya
porukaṭal veṇtirai vantu _eṟiyum puṉavāyilē.

*10
pōti _eṉap peyar_āyiṉārum, poṟi _il camaṇ-
cāti, _uraippaṉa koṇṭu, _ayarntu, taḷarvu _eytaṉmiṉ!
pōtu _aviḻ taṇpoḻil malkum _am taṇ puṉavāyilil
vētaṉai nāḷtoṟum _ēttuvārmēl viṉai vīṭumē.

*11
poṉ-toṭiyāḷ_umai paṅkaṉ mēvum puṉavāyilai,
kaṟṟavartām toḻutu _ētta niṉṟa kaṭal kāḻiyāṉ---
nal-tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ---coṉṉa tamiḻ, naṉmaiyāl
_aṟṟam _il pāṭalpattu, _ētta vallār _aruḷ cērvarē.

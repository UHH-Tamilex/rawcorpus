• patikam: [3:11]
  • talam: tirup puṉavāyil
  • paṇ: kāntārap pañcamam
  • VMS: Vol.11, p.84

*1
miṉ _iyal ceñcaṭi veṇpiṟiyaṉ, viri nūliṉaṉ,
paṉṉiya nālmaṟi pāṭi _āṭi, pala_ūrkaḷ pōy,
_aṉṉam _aṉṉa(n) naṭiyāḷoṭum(m) _amarum(m) _iṭam---
puṉṉi nalmāmalar poṉ _utirkkum puṉavāyilē.

*2
viṇṭavartam purammūṉṟu _erittu, viṭi _ēṟip pōy,
vaṇṭu _amarum kuḻal maṅkiyoṭum makiḻntāṉ _iṭam---
kaṇṭalum ñāḻalum niṉṟu, peruṅkaṭal kāṉalvāyp
puṇṭarīkammalarp poyki cūḻnta puṉavāyilē.

*3
viṭi _uṭi vel koṭi _ēntiṉāṉum, viṟal pāriṭam
puṭi paṭa _āṭiya vēṭattāṉum, puṉavāyilil
toṭi navil koṉṟi_amtāriṉāṉum, cuṭar veṇmaḻup-
paṭi valaṉ _ēntiya, pālney_āṭum, paramaṉ _aṉṟē!

*4
caṅkaveṇtōṭu _aṇi kātiṉāṉum, caṭi tāḻavē
_aṅki _ilaṅku _aḻal _ēntiṉāṉum(m), _aḻaku_ākavē
poṅku _aravam(m) _aṇi mārpiṉāṉum---puṉavāyilil,
piṅkaṇ veḷ_ēṟṟu _aṇṇal_āki niṉṟa paramēṭṭiyē.

*5
kali paṭu taṇkaṭal nañcam _uṇṭa kaṟikkaṇṭaṉum,
puli_ataḷ pāmpu _aric cuṟṟiṉāṉum---puṉavāyilil,
_olitaru taṇpuṉalōṭu, _erukkum, matamattamum,
melitaru veṇpiṟi, cūṭi niṉṟa viṭi_ūrtiyē.

*6
vār _uṟu meṉmuli maṅki pāṭa naṭam_āṭip pōy,
kār _uṟu koṉṟi veṇtiṅkaḷāṉum, kaṉal vāyatu _ōr
pōr _uṟu veṇmaḻu _ēntiṉāṉum---puṉavāyilil,
cīr _uṟu celvam malka(v) _irunta civalōkaṉē.

*7
peruṅkaṭalnañcu _amutu_uṇṭu, _ukantu peruṅkāṭṭu_iṭit
tiruntu _iḷameṉmulit tēvi pāṭa(n) naṭam_āṭip pōy,
poruntalartam purammūṉṟum _eytu, puṉavāyilil
_iruntavaṉtaṉ kaḻal _ēttuvārkaṭku _iṭar _illiyē.

*8
maṉam miku vēlaṉ _av vāḷ _arakkaṉ vali _olkiṭa,
vaṉam miku mālvariyāl _aṭarttāṉ _iṭam---maṉṉiya
_iṉam miku tolpukaḻ pāṭal _āṭal _eḻil malkiya,
puṉam miku koṉṟi _am teṉṟal _ārnta, puṉavāyilē.

*9
tiru vaḷar tāmari mēviṉāṉum, tikaḻ pāṟkaṭal
karuniṟavaṇṇaṉum, kāṇpu _ariya kaṭavuḷ(ḷ) _iṭam---
naral curicaṅkoṭum _ippi _unti(n), nalam malkiya
porukaṭal veṇtiri vantu _eṟiyum puṉavāyilē.

*10
pōti _eṉap peyar_āyiṉārum, poṟi _il camaṇ-
cāti, _urippaṉa koṇṭu, _ayarntu, taḷarvu _eytaṉmiṉ!
pōtu _aviḻ taṇpoḻil malkum _am taṇ puṉavāyilil
vētaṉi nāḷtoṟum _ēttuvārmēl viṉi vīṭumē.

*11
poṉ-toṭiyāḷ_umi paṅkaṉ mēvum puṉavāyili,
kaṟṟavartām toḻutu _ētta niṉṟa kaṭal kāḻiyāṉ---
nal-tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ---coṉṉa tamiḻ, naṉmiyāl
_aṟṟam _il pāṭalpattu, _ētta vallār _aruḷ cērvarē.

The following verse is not found in palm-leaf manuscripts: this has been quoted by Saint Tāyumāṉavar in a verse under the title of ``aṟinnaruri''

{5:aaa}__1+
{$} என்னுள் ஈசன் இருந்த இயற்கை (ஏ)
the nature of Civaṉ who dwelt within me,
பன்னில் ஊறும் பழத்தினும் இன்சுவை
if I describe it, is sweeter than the sweetness of the fruit.
என்னில் ஊறி எனக்கே கனிதரும்
It gives joy springing within me.
உன்னில் உன்னும்
he will think of me if I think of him.
உன்னாவிடில் விட்டிடும்
he will forsake me if I do not think of him.

The following verse is not found in palm-leaf manuscripts: this has been quoted by Saint Tāyumāṉavar in a verse under the title of ``aṟinnarurai''

{5:aaa}__1+
{$} eṉṉuḷ īcaṉ irunta iyaṟkai (ē)
the nature of Civaṉ who dwelt within me,
paṉṉil ūṟum paḻattiṉum iṉcuvai
if I describe it, is sweeter than the sweetness of the fruit.
eṉṉil ūṟi eṉakkē kaṉitarum
It gives joy springing within me.
uṉṉil uṉṉum
he will think of me if I think of him.
uṉṉāviṭil viṭṭiṭum
he will forsake me if I do not think of him.

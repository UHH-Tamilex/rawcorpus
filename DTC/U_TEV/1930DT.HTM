Situation of Taluks (and districts) in Tamil area in 1930

  • Alphabetical list of abbreviations
  • Alphabetical list of names
  • Names of districts for T. Nadu area in 1930

The following lists are compiled from the
Alphabetical list of villages in the taluks and districts
of the Madras Presidency (corrected upto september 1930)
[Asian Educational Services Reprint, 1992]
and it concerns itself only with the taluks that are contained in present day Tamil Nadu.
Note, however, that the data concerning present day Pudukkottai and Kanniyakumari districts is missing from this book, as those did not belong to the Madras Presidency.

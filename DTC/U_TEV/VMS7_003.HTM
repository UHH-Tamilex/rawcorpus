{$} kal vāy akilum katir māmaṇiyum kalantu untivarum nilaviṉ karaimēl
on the bank of the river Nilavu, which flows pushing, mixing the eagle-wood that is found in the mountin and big gems which shed rays.
nel vāyil arattuṟai niṉ malaṉē
the spotless Civaṉ who dwells in the temple Arattuṟi in Nelvayil!
nalvāy il ceytār
people of this world married wives who are good life-partners.
naṭantār
led a house-holder's life
uṭuttār
dressed foppishly
naraittār
became old.
iṟantār
died.
eṉṟu nāṉilattil collāyk kaḻikkiṉṟatu aṟintu
knowing well that these people pass out of this world being spoken like this and knowing the transience of human life.
toṭarntēṉ
I approached you
aṭiyēṉ uyyap pōvatu ōr cūḻal col (ē)
please tell me a means to save myself from that talk by people.

{$} nīr ūrum neṭuvayal cūḻpuṟaviṉ nelvāyil arattuṟai niṉmalaṉai
about the spotless god in the temple, Arattuṟi in Nelvāyil situated in the forest tract surrounded by long fields into which water flows slowly.
tēr ūr neṭuvīti naṉ māṭam mali teṉ nāvalarkōṉ aṭittoṇṭaṉ aṇi ārūraṉ uraittaṉa nal tamiḻiṉ mikumālai ōr pattu ivaikaṟṟu vallār
those who are able to recite having learnt these ten verses which form a garland of much devotion in flawless tamiḻ composed by beautiful ārūraṉ, who does service to the feet of Civaṉ, the chief of the inhabitants of nāvalūr in the south which has many good mansions of storeys and long streets where cars are moving.
kār ūr kaḷivaṇṭu aṟaiyāṉai maṉṉar (avar) āki
becoming rulers of his world who ride on an elephants on whose temples the intoxicated black bees hum loudly to drink the fluid of must.
ōr viṇ muḻutu āḷpavarē
will definitely rule over the entire heaven as their King, intiraṉ.
[[yāṉaimaṉṉavar elephants are an important division of the four divisions of the army; yāṉai uṭaippaṭaikāṇṭal mikaiṉitē (iṉiyavi nāṟpatu, 4) riding on elephants by Kings was considered a great honour in olden days; malaimicait tōṉṟum matiyampōla yāṉait talaimicaik koṇṭa kuṭaiyar (nālaṭiyār, 21)]]

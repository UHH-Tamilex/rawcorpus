[[Note: these ten verses were published in the 11th issue of the journal Cittāntam vol. 5 as found in a palm leaf manuscript]]

[[It is my candid opinion that this decade falls very short of the standard of the verses of Ñāṉacampantar. The veḷḷi patikam on Maṟikkāṭu which is an interpolation is thousand times better than these ten verses.]]

{3:127}__1+
{$} perumoḻi uyvakai ēr ciṟakkum kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉ (ē)
Civaṉ who is in Kiḷiyaṉṉavūr which is eminent by its beauty, for people to save themselves by the great word of his mantiram of five letters.
tār ciṟakkum caṭaikku aṇivaḷḷaliṉ cīr ciṟakkum tuṇaippatam uṉṉuvōr pēr ciṟakkum
the names of those who meditate on the two feet which are eminent by their reputation, of Civaṉ of unbounded liberality who wears on his head a garland, will become conspicuous in this world.
{3:127}__2+
{$} vaṟumai vantālum(ē)
even if one becomes poor.
taṉmai illavar cārpu iruntālum (ē)
even if there is the support of those who are ill-natured.
puṉmaikkaṉṉiyar pūcal uṟṟālum (ē)
even if the low-minded ladies pick up a quarrel.
naṉmai uṟṟa kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉ (ē)
Civaṉ in kiḷiyaṉṉavūr who has abundant goodness.
vaṉmai ceyyum
shall grant us strength to get over them.
{3:127}__3+
{$} maṉṉi nākamukattavar ōtalum, muṉṉil niṉṟa kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉ (ē)
Civaṉ in Kiḷiyaṉṉavūr who stood appearing before the one who had the face of the cobra, standing there permanently chanting his name.
paṉṉi niṉṟa paṉuval akattiyaṉ uṉṉi niṉṟu uṟuttum cukattavaṉ
when Akattiyaṉ who investigated many works, meditated on him, gave him immense pleasure.
{3:127}__4+
{$} iṉpam tēkkum kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉ (ē)
Civaṉ in Kiḷiyaṉṉavūr where happiness is stored to the ulmost capacity.
aṉpar vēṇṭum avai aḷi cōtiyāṉ
is the divine light that grants all those which his devotees desire.
vaṉpar neñcil maruval illā mutal
is the first cuse that does not approach the heart of hard-hearted people.
tuṉpam tīrttuc cukam koṭu kaṇṇutal
is the three-eyed god who grants happiness, removing sufferings.
{3:127}__5+
{$} ceyyum vaṇṇam cirittu
lughing to do an act.
puram micai peyyum vaṇṇam peruntakaiyāṉ
Civaṉ is a noble-minded god who cused fire to rage in the cities.
atu ōr
ponder over that.
uyyum vaṇṇam iṅku uṉ aruḷ nōkkiṭa meyyum vaṇṇak kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉ (ē)
the Lord in Kiḷiyaṉṉavūr will make it become true if one regards his grace to save oneself in this world.
{3:127}__6+
{$} puṇ poṟāta kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉē
Civaṉ in Kiḷiyaṉṉavūr who could not bear wounds!
eṇpeṟāviṉaikku īṟucey niṉ aruḷ naṇpu uṟāppavam iyaṟṟiṭiṉ
even if one commits sins which have not love for your grace which puts an end to countless sinful acts.
anneṟi paṇpoṟā muḻuc celvamum malkum (āl)
will increase all kinds of wealth the weight of which the earth cannot bear, by that way.
[[viṉaikku īṟuceya is my emendation]]
{3:127}__7+
{$} tāvu uṟātu uṉatu aintu eḻuttu uṉṉiṭat tēvar ākum kiḷiyaṉṉapūraṉē
the Lord in Kiḷiyaṉṉavūr which transforms men into celestials if they meditate upon your mantiram consisting of five letters, without fault!
mukkaṇṇa
the Lord of three eyes!
mūvarāyiṉum niṉ aruḷ mēvuṟātu vilakkiṭal pālarō
even if the three deities, Ayaṉ, Māl and uruttiraṉ try to prevent, will they have the strength to prevent your grace reaching those devotees?
{3:127}__8+
{$} tiram mikutta caṭaimuṭiyāṉ varai uram mikutta irāvaṇaṉ kīṇṭalum
when Irāvaṇaṉ of excessive strength rent the mountin, Kayili of Civaṉ who has a very strength caṭi coiled into a crown.
niram mikuttu nerittu avaṉ ōtalāl
when he uttered the names of Civaṉ, after his chest was crushed.
varam mikutta kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉ (ē)
is the Lord in Kiḷiyaṉṉavūr who granted boons to him in a large measure.
{3:127}__9+
{$} nīti uṟṟiru nāṉmukaṉ nāraṇaṉ
Piramaṉ of four faces who has right conduct, and Nārayaṇaṉ.
pētam uṟṟu
with differences between themselves.
pirintu
having gone separately.
aḻalāy nimirntanātaṉ
the master who rose stright as a column of fire.
uṟṟaṉa naṉmalar, pāy irukkītam ēṟṟa kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉ (ē)
is the Lord in Kiḷiyaṉṉavūr who accepted the spreading songs of the vētams, flawless flowers reached him.
{3:127}__10+
{$} maṅkaiyarkkaracōṭu kulacciṟai poṅku aḻal curam pōkku eṉap pūḻiyaṉ caṅkai māṟṟi
the Lord who removed the doubt of the Pāṇṭiyaṉ when Maṅkiyarkkaraci along with the minister Kulacciṟi requested to remove the fever of increasing heat.
camaṇattait tāḻttavum iṅku uraitta kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉ (ē)
is the Lord in Kiliyaṉṉavūr who ordered Ñāṉacampantaṉ to lower the camaṇar in estimation.
{3:127}__11+
{$} niṟaiya vāḻ kiḷiyaṉṉavūraṉai
on the Lord in Kiliyaṉṉavūr where he dwells with splendour.
uṟaiyum ñāṉacampantaṉ col cīriṉai aṟaiya niṉṟa pattum vallārkkumē kuṟai ilātu koṭumai tavirvar (ē)
those who are able to recite the ten verses describing the fame of the Lord prised by Ñāṉacampantaṉ who dwells in that place, will leave cruelty, without wanting in anything.

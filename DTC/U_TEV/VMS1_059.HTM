[[As the wall enclosing the sanctum sanctorum is apsidal in shape, it is called tūṅkāṉaimāṭam;
it is called in sanskrit as the back of the elephant']]

{1:59}__1+
{$} oṭuṅkum piṇi piṟavi kēṭu eṉṟu ivai uṭaittu āyvāḻkkai oḻiya
to be rid of the human life which has these things such as diseases which are concealed and gain upperland when the resistance power is less, birth and other losses.
tavam aṭaṅkum iṭam karuti niṉṟīr ellām
all of you who think of performing penance in a place where all desires and the five senses can be subdued.
aṭikaḷ aṭiniḻaṟkīḻāḷ ām vaṇṇam
to become slaves under the shade of the feet of the god.
kiṭaṅkum matilum culāvi
surrounded by a moat and a wall of fortification.
eṅkum keḻu maṉaikaḷ tōṟum maṟaiyiṉ oli toṭaṅkum kaṭantait taṭaṅkōyil cēr tūṅkāṉai māṭam toḻumiṉkaḷ (ē)
worship with joined hands the tuṅkāṉai māṭam in the big temple in Kaṭantai where in every place in the houses which are close to one another the sound of chanting the Vētam-s begins.
{1:59}__2+
{$} piṇinīra cātal piṟattal ivai piriya
when the death which has the nature of disease and birth leave you.
piriyāta pēriṉpattōṭu aṇinīramēl ulakam eytal uṟiṉ aṟimiṉ
know this thing if you want to reach the upper world which has beauty as its nature along with the supreme bliss which will never leave you.
kuṟaivu illai
there will be no deficiency in that.
āṉēṟu uṭai(ya) maṇi nīlakaṇṭam uṭaiya pirāṉ
the master who has a blue neck like the sapphire, and a bull
malaimakaḷum tāṉum makiḻntu vāḻum tuṇinīrkkaṭantait taṭaṅkōyil cēr tūṅkāṉaimāṭam toḻumiṉkaḷ (ē)
(People of this world! worship tirukāṉai mātam which is in the big temple at Kaṭantai which has clear water and where Civaṉ and the daughter of the mountain dwell with joy
[[tuṇinīr clear water; tuṇinīr mel aval toyyiloṭu malara
(manturaikkāñci, 283)]]
{1:59}__3+
{$} cānāḷum vāḻ nāḷum tōṟṟam ivai calippāy vāḻkkai oḻiya
to get rid of this life which has the day of death, the days we live and birth with which we are disgusted.
tavam āmāṟu aṟiyātu
without knowing the way to perform penance.
(1) alamantu nīr ayarntum kuṟaivu illai
there is no deficiency in your languishing and whirling in your minds.
āṉēṟu uṭaip pūmāṇ alaṅkal ilaṅku koṉṟai puṉalpotinta puṉ caṭaiyiṉāṉ uṟaiyum tūmāṉ kaṭantait taṭaṅkōyil cērtūṅkāṉai māṭam toḻumiṉkaḷ (ē)
(People of this world) worship tuṅkāṉai māṭam which is in the big temple at Kaṭantai, great by purity where Civaṉ who has a ruddy caṭai in which he contains famous garlands of shining koṉṟai flowers and water, and has a bull.
[[(Variant reading: (1)alam vantu]]
{1:59}__4+
{$} (People of this world!)
piṇi ūṉṟum (1) piṟavi kēṭu eṉṟu ivai uṭaittāy vāḻkkai oḻiya
to be rid of the life which has such things as this birth in which diseases have gained a firm footing, and death.
māṉṟu
being confused.
tavam maṉam karuti niṉṟīr ellām
all of your who have decided to perform penance.
maṉam tirintu
changing your mind.
maṇṇil mayaṅkātu
without being confused in this world.
nīr
you
mūṉṟumatil eyta mūvāccilai mutalvarkku iṭam (pōlum) mukiltōy koṭi tōṉṟum kaṭantait taṭaṅkōyilcēr tūṅkāṉaimāṭam toḻumiṉkaḷ (ē)
worship tuṅkāṉai māṭam in the big temple at Kaṭantai which has flags which touch the clouds and can be seen from a distance, and which is the place of the first cause who has a bow which does not age and with which he discharged an arrow on the three forts.
[[(Variant reading: (1) piṟavikkēṭu]]

mayal tīrmai illāta tōṟṟam(m) ivai maraṇattoṭu ottu oḻiyum āṟu ātalāl
as this is the way by which the birth which has no cessation of confusion will end with death.
viyaltīra mēl ulakam eytal uṟiṉ
if you think of reaching the upper world being rid of diverse difficulties;
[[viyal diversity;]]
[[PP: viyaṉkala irukkaiyum-vēṟṟumaippaṭṭa aṇikalam peyta carakkaṟaiyum

mikku oṉṟum vēṇṭā
there is no necessity for a higher act.
uyartīra ōṅkiya nāmaṅkaḷāl ōvātu nāḷum aṭiparaval cey
praise the feet of Civaṉ daily without ceasing, uttering his names which are great and above which there is no great thing.
vimalaṉ iṭam tuyartīra kaṭantait taṭaṅkōyilcēr tūṅkāṉai māṭam toḻumiṉkaḷ (ē)
worship tuṅkāṉai māṭam in the big temple in Kaṭantai which removes all sufferings and which is the place of the spotless god.

{$} palnīrmai kuṉṟi
the strength of the organs of sense which have the nature of enjoying their senses having become weak.
cevi(1)kēṭpilā
the ears having become hard of hearing.
paṭarnōkkiṉkaṇ pavaḷa(n) niṟam naṉṉīrmai kuṉṟi
the red streaks in the eyes resembling the good nature of the colour of coral become decreased due to cataract which spreads in the eye.
tiraitōloṭu
with wrinkles in the skin.
narai tōṉṟum kālam namakku ātalmuṉ
before old age overtakes us when hairs turn grey.
poṉ nīrmai tuṉṟap puṟam tōṉṟum naṉpuṉal potinta puṉcaṭaiyiṉāṉ uṟaiyum tol nīrkkaṭantait taṭaṅkōyil cēr tūṅkāṉaimāṭam toḻumiṉkaḷ (ē)
worship tuṅkāṉai māṭam in the big temple in Kaṭantai which has water older than other elements where Civaṉ who has a ruddy caṭai which is like gold in colour and contains water to be seen outside it, dwells.
[[(Variant readings : (1) kēṭpilāp, kēṭpila]]

tavam niṟai ūṇneṟi karutiniṉṟīr ellām

nīḷkaḻalē nāḷum niṉaimiṉ

ceṉṉippiṟai cūḻ alaṅkal ilaṅku koṉṟai piṇaiyum perumāṉ

tavam il cūḻ iṭam karuti niṉṟīr ellām

iṟaiyē piriyātu eḻuntu pōtum
rise and come without parting even for a moment.
kalcūḻ arakkaṉ kataṟac ceytāṉ kātaliyum tāṉum karutivāḻum tolcīrk kaṭantait taṭaṅkōyil cēr tūṅkāṉai māṭam toḻumiṉkaḷ (ē)
worship tuṅkāṉai māṭam in the big temple in Kaṭantai of ancient fame, where Civaṉ who caused the arakkaṉ who thought of uprooting Kayilai, to roar, dwells with his consort thinking it to be the most suitable temple.

to be rid of the life in which we undergo sufferings which cannot be over-come due to physical diseases and mental worries.
tavamvāyum maṉam karuti niṉṟīr ellām
all of you who thought of in your mind penance suitable to you.
malarmicaiya nāṉ mukaṉum maṇṇum viṇṇum tāya aṭi aḷantāṉ kāṇa māṭṭāt talaivarkku iṭam(pōlum)
the place of the master who could not be seen by Māl who leapt over the earth and heaven and measured them with his feet and Piramaṉ of four faces who is seated on a (lotus) flower;

nalla peṇṇākaṭattup peruṅkōyil cēr piṟai uriñcum tūṅkāṉai māṭam mēyāṉ kaṇār kaḻal paravupāṭal pattum
all the ten verses which praise the feet which can have a full view to one's complete satisfaction, of Civaṉ who dwells in tūṅkāṉai māṭam on which the moon rubs and which is the big temple at fine peṇṇākaṭam.

*1
vari vaḷar _avir_oḷi _aravu _ari tāḻa,
vārcaṭimuṭimici vaḷarmati cūṭi,
kari vaḷartaru kaḻalkāl valaṉ _ēnti,

veṇniṟattu _aruvikaḷ tiṇṇeṉa vīḻum,
_eri vaḷar _iṉamaṇi puṉam _aṇi cāral
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*2
_āṟṟiyum _ēṟṟatu _ōr _avircaṭi _uṭiyar;
_aḻakiṉi _aruḷuvar; kuḻaku_alatu _aṟiyār;

_ēṟṟiyoṭu _uḻitarum _eḻil tikaḻ cāral
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*3
kāṉamum, cuṭaliyum, kal paṭu nilaṉum,
kātalar; tītu _ilar; kaṉal maḻuvāḷar;
vāṉamum nilamiyum _irumiyum _āṉār;
vaṇaṅkavum _iṇaṅkavum vāḻttavumpaṭuvārā;
nāṉamum puki _oḷi viriyoṭu kamaḻa,
naḷirpoḻil _iḷa maññi maṉṉiya pāṅkar,
_ēṉamum piṇiyalum _eḻil tikaḻ cāral
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*4
kaṭa maṇi mārpiṉar; kaṭaltaṉil _uṟivār
kātalar; tītu _ilar; kaṉal maḻuvāḷar;
viṭam _aṇi miṭaṟiṉar; miḷirvatu _ōr _aravar;
vēṟum _ōr caritiyar; vēṭamum _uṭiyar;
vaṭam _uli _ayalaṉa karuṅkuruttu _ēṟi,
vāḻiyiṉ tīmkaṉi vārttu tēṉ _aṭṭum
_iṭam muli _ariviyar _eḻil tikaḻ cāral
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*5
kār koṇṭa kaṭi kamaḻ virimalarkkoṉṟik-
kaṇṇiyar; vaḷarmati katirviṭa, kaṅki-
nīr koṇṭa caṭiyiṉar; viṭi_uyar koṭiyar;
niḻal tikaḻ maḻuviṉar; _aḻaltikaḻ niṟattar;
cīr koṇṭa meṉciṟivaṇṭu paṇceyyum
ceḻum puṉal _aṉiyaṉa ceṅkuli vāḻi
_ēr koṇṭa palaviṉoṭu _eḻil tikaḻ cāral
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*6
tōṭu _aṇi kuḻiyiṉar; cuṇṇaveṇnīṟṟar;
cuṭaliyiṉ _āṭuvar; tōl _uṭi_ākap
pīṭu _uyarceytatu _ōr perumiyi _uṭiyavar;
pēy_uṭaṉ _āṭuvar; periyavarperumāṉ;
kōṭalkaḷ _oḻukuva, muḻukuva tumpi,
kuravamum maravamum maṉṉiya pāṅkar,
_ēṭu _aviḻ putumalar kaṭi kamaḻ cāral
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*7
kaḻal malku kāliṉar; vēliṉar; nūlar;
kavartali_aravoṭu kaṇṭiyum pūṇpar;
_aḻal malkum _eriyoṭum _aṇi maḻu _enti
_āṭuvar; pāṭuvar; _ār_aṇaṅku _uṭiyar;

_eḻil malku cōliyil vaṇṭu _ici pāṭum
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*8
tēm kamaḻkoṉṟi_amtirumalar puṉivār;
tikaḻtaru caṭimicit tiṅkaḷum cūṭi,
vīntavar cuṭali veṇnīṟu mey pūci,
vēṟum _ōr caritiyar; vēṭamum _uṭiyar;
cāntamum _akiloṭu mukil potintu _alampi,
tavaḻ kaṉa maṇiyoṭu miku paḷiṅku _iṭaṟi,
_ēntu veḷ_aruvikaḷ _eḻil tikaḻ cāral
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*9
pala_ilam _iṭu pali kiyil _oṉṟu _ēṟpar;
palapukaḻ_allatu paḻi _ilar, tāmum;
tali _ilaṅku _avir_oḷi neṭu muṭi _arakkaṉ

_ili _ilavaṅkamum _ēlamum kamaḻum
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*10
perumikaḷ tarukki _ōr pētu_uṟukiṉṟa
peruṅkaṭalvaṇṇaṉum piramaṉum _ōrā
_arumiyar; _aṭiniḻal paravi niṉṟu _ēttum
_aṉpu _uṭi _aṭiyavarkku _aṇiyarum _āvar;

_erumikaḷ paṭitara, _iḷa _aṉam _ālum
_iṭiccuram mēviya _ivar vaṇam _eṉṉē?

*11
maṭiccuram maṟivaṉa vāḷiyum kayalum
maruviya vayaltaṉil varupuṉal kāḻic
caṭiccurattu _uṟivatu _ōr piṟi _uṭi _aṇṇal

_iṭiccuram _ēttiya _iciyoṭu pāṭal,
_ivi cola vallavar piṇi _ilartāmē.

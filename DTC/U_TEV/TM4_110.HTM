• patikam: [4:110]
  • talam: POTU
  • (()): pacupati tiru viruttam
  • VMS: Vol.15, p.87

*1
cāmpalip pūcit tariyil puraṇṭu, niṉ tāḷ paravi,
_ēmpalippārkaṭku _iraṅkukaṇṭāy---_iruṅ kaṅki _eṉṉum
kāmpu _alikkum paṇittōḷi katirp pūṇ vaṉamulimēl
pāmpu _alikkum caṭiyāy! _emmi _āḷum pacupatiyē!

*2
_uṭampit tolivittu _uṉ pātam tali vitta _uttamarkaḷ
_iṭumpip paṭāmal _iraṅkukaṇṭāy---_iruḷ _ōṭac centī
_aṭumpu _ottu _aṉiya _aḻalmaḻuvā! _aḻalē _umiḻum
paṭam pottu _aravu _ariyāy! _emmi _āḷum pacupatiyē!

*3
tārittiram tavirā _aṭiyār taṭumāṟṟam _eṉṉum
mūrit tirippuvam nīkkukaṇṭāy---muṉṉināḷ _oru kāl
vērit taṇ pūñ cuṭar _aiṅkaṇi vēḷ ventu vīḻac centīp
pāritta kaṇ _uṭiyāy! _emmi _āḷum pacupatiyē!

*4
_oruvarit tañcam _eṉṟu _eṇṇātu _uṉ pātam _iṟiñcukiṉṟār
_aruviṉic cuṟṟam _akalvikaṇṭāy---_aṇṭamē _aḷavum
peruvarikkuṉṟam piḷiṟap piḷantu, vēyttōḷi _añcap
paruvarit tōl _urittāy! _emmi _āḷum pacupatiyē!

*5
_iṭukku _oṉṟum _iṉṟi, _eñcāmi _uṉ pātam _iṟiñcukiṉṟārkku
_aṭarkkiṉṟa nōyi vilakkukaṇṭāy---_aṇṭam _eṇticiyum
cuṭart tiṅkaḷ cūṭi, cuḻal kaṅkiyōṭum curumpu tuṉṟi,
paṭarkkoṇṭa ceñcaṭiyāy! _emmi _āḷum pacupatiyē!

*6
_aṭalikkaṭal kaḻivāṉ niṉ _aṭi_iṇiyē _aṭintār
naṭalip paṭāmi vilakkukaṇṭāy---naṟuṅkoṉṟi, tiṅkaḷ,
cuṭalip poṭic-cuṇṇam, mācuṇam, cūḷāmaṇi, kiṭantu
paṭarac cuṭar makuṭā! _emmi _āḷum pacupatiyē!

*7
tuṟavittoḻilē purintu _uṉ curumpu _aṭiyē toḻuvār
maṟavittoḻil_atu māṟṟukkaṇṭāy---matilmūṉṟu _uṭiya
_aṟavittoḻil purintu _antarattē cellum mantirattērp-
paṟavippuram _erittāy! _emmi _āḷum pacupatiyē!

*8
cittattu _uruki, civaṉ, _empirāṉ _eṉṟu cintiyuḷḷē
pittup perukap pitaṟṟukiṉṟār piṇi tīrttu _aruḷāy---
mattattu _arakkaṉ _irupatutōḷum muṭiyum _ellām
pattu _uṟṟu _uṟa nerittāy! _emmi _āḷum pacupatiyē!

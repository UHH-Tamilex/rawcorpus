{5:82}__1+
{$}
(People of this world!)
viṇṭa māmalar koṇṭu
taking big blossomed flowers.
viraintu nīr (1) aṇṭa nāyakar taṉ aṭi cūḻmiṉkaḷ
go from left to right, to the feet of the master of the worlds hastening towards him.
vaṇṭu cēr poḻil vāṉmiyūr īcaṉ(ē)
the Lord of the universe in Vāṉmiyūr which has gardens where bees swarm.
paṇṭu nīr ceyta pāvam paṟaintiṭum
the sins committed by you in previous births will vanish gradually.
[[Note - if the readings were paṟaittiṭum it would make better sense.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) aṇṭar nāyakaṉ]]
{5:82}__2+
{$}
(People of this world!)
nīr poruḷum cuṟṟamum poymmaiyum viṭṭu
by giving up wealth, relatives and falsehood as having any substance.
maruḷum māntarai māṟṟi mayakku aṟuttu aruḷumā valla ātiyāy eṉṟalum
as soon as you say the first cuse of all things who is able to grant me your grace completely destroying their illusion changing the delusion of those people.
vāṉmiyūr īcaṉ maruḷ aṟuttiṭum
the Lord in Vāṉmiyūr will completely remove your ignorance.
{5:82}__3+
{$}
(People of this world!)
mantam ākiya cintai mayakku aṟuttu
cutting at the root of confusion in the mind which is slow in understanding.
antam il kuṇattāṉai aṭaintu niṉṟu
approaching Civaṉ who has countless attributes and standing in his presence.
entai! īcaṉ! eṉṟu ēttiṭa vallirēl
if you are able to prise him as `my father! Civaṉ'
vāṉmiyūr īcaṉ(ē) vantu niṉṟiṭum
the Lord in vāṉmiyūr will will come and stand before you to grant his vision.
{5:82}__4+
{$}
veḷḷamum aravum viravum caṭai vaḷḷal ākiya vāṉmiyūr īcaṉē
Civaṉ in vāṉmiyūr who has unbounded liberality and who has on his caṭi flood of Kaṅki and a cobra joining together.
uḷḷam uḷ kalantu ētta vallārkku alāl
except for those who can prise him sincerely.
kaḷḷam uḷḷavaḻik kacivāṉ alaṉ
he will not bestow compassion on people who have deceitful thoughts.
{5:82}__5+
{$}
paṭam koḷ pāmparai
Civaṉ who wears cobras with hoods.
pāl mati cūṭiyai
who wears a crescent as white as milk.
vaṭam koḷ meṉmulai mātu oru kūṟaṉai
who has as one half a lady of soft breasts wearing strands of chins.
toṭarntu niṉṟu toḻutu eḻuvār viṉai maṭaṅka niṉṟiṭum vāṉmiyūr īcaṉē
Civaṉ in vāṉmiyur will diminish the acts of those who wake up worshipping with joined hands, thinking of him without break.
(People of this world)
{5:82}__6+
{$}
aivar
the five sense organs
neñcil niṉaikka niṉaikkuṟār
will not allow you to think of the god when you try to think of him.
pañciṉ mel aṭiyāḷ umai paṅka eṉṟu añci
fearing out of respect Civaṉ saying, the Lord who has Umi whose feet are as soft as cotton, on one half!
nāḷmalar tūvi aḻutirēl
if you weep out of joy scattering flowers blossomed at day-break.
vāṉmiyūr īcaṉ vañcam tīrttiṭum
the Lord in vāṉmiyūr will remove the deceitful nature of the five sense-organs.
{5:82}__7+
{$}
1. nuṇaṅku nūl ayaṉ mālum aṟikilāk kuṇaṅkaḷ tām paravik kuṟaintu ukkavar
those who pine after Civaṉ having controlled their egoism and prise the qualities which Ayaṉ who has knowledge of the subtle vētams and Māl could not know.
cuṇaṅku pūṇ mulait tūmoḻiyār avar 2. vaṇaṅka niṉṟiṭum vāṉmiyūr īcaṉē
Civaṉ remins permanently in vāṉmiyūr, for the ladies who speak the fame of civan and who have breasts wearing ornaments and having yellow spreading spots considered as beautiful.
[[Variant reading: 1. iṇaṅku 2. vaṇaṅki niṉṟiṭum.]]
{5:82}__8+
{$}
ātiyum
the first supreme being.
araṉāy ayaṉ mālumāyp pāti peṇ uru āya paramaṉ eṉṟu ōti
uttering the names of Civaṉ, as the supreme being superior to all, who is half female and who is himself araṉ, Ayaṉ and Māl.
uḷ kuḻaintu ētta vallār avar
those who can prise their hearts becoming tender and soft.
vātai tīrttiṭum vāṉmiyūr īcaṉē
Civaṉ in vāṉmiyūr will remove the suffering of birth.
{5:82}__9+
{$}
ōṭṭai māṭattil oṉpatu vācalum kāṭṭil vēvataṉ muṉṉam kaḻal aṭi nāṭṭi
fixing the feet wearing Kaḻal of Civaṉ in the heart before the nine apertunes in the body full of holes, are burnt in the cremation ground,
[[ōṭum nīriṉai ōṭṭaik kuṭattu aṭṭi, mūṭivaitta mūrkkaṉoṭu okkumē (nāvukkaracar, pāvanācattirukkuṟuntoki, 9)]]
nāḷ malar tūvi valañceyil vāṉmiyūr īcaṉ vāṭṭam tīrttiṭum
Civaṉ in vāṉmiyūr will remove our troubles, if we surround him spreading fresh flowers on him.
{5:82}__10+
{$}
pāramāka malai eṭuttāṉ taṉaic cīramākat tiruviral ūṉṟiṉāṉ
Civaṉ fired his holy toe heavily to crush Irāvaṇaṉ who lifted the mountin as a heavy weight.
ārvamāka aḻaittu āvaṉ ēttalum vāṉmiyūr īcaṉ vāram āyiṉaṉ
called him with desire Civaṉ in Vāṉmiyūr became partial towards him when he uttered a loud cry and then prised Civaṉ.

{1:84}__1+
{$} puṉaiyum virikoṉṟaik kaṭavuḷ
the god who adorns himself with the blossomed koṉṟi.
puṉal pāya naṉaiyum caṭaimēl ōr naku veṇtalaicūṭi
wearing on the caṭi which becomes wet as the water (of the Kaṅki) flows, a white smiling skull.
viṉaiil aṭiyārkaḷ vitiyāl vaḻipaṭṭu
the devotees who are free from Karmams to perform worship according to rules.
kaṉaiyum kaṭal nākaik kārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōnam on the shore of the sea which is always roaring.
{1:84}__2+
{$} peṇ āṇ eṉa niṉṟa pemmāṉ
The great god who is both female and male.
piṟaicceṉṉi aṇṇāmalai nāṭaṉ
who dwells in the country named after aṇṇāmali, and who wears on his head a crescent.
ārūr uṟai ammāṉ
and the god as father who dwells in ārūr.
maṇ ār muḻavu ōvā māṭa(n) neṭuvītikkaṇ ārkaṭal nākaik kārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōṇam on the shore of the sea pleasing to the eye, which has long streets with storeys where the muḻavu on the head of which paste is smeared for toning it, never ceases from being played (kaṇ ārkaṭal may also be translated as expansive sea).
{1:84}__3+
{$} pārōr toḻa, viṇṇōr paṇiya(m) matil mūṉṟum ārār aḻal ūṭṭi
setting fire to all the three forts of the enemies, when he was worshipped by the people of this world and the tēvar in heaven pid obeisance.
[[ārār enemies;
ārār tiripuraṅkaḷ nīṟānōkkum aṉalāṭi (nāvukkaracar, tiruviyaṟu tāṇṭakam (1) 1)]]
aṭiyārkku aruḷ ceytāṉ
bestowed his grace on his devotees.
tēr ār viḻavu ōvāc (1) celvaṉ
the god for whom the car festival never ceases.
tirai cūḻnta kār ār kaṭal cūḻnta nākaikkārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōṇam surrounded by the black ocean where the waves roam about.
[[Variant readings : (1) celvat]]
{1:84}__4+
{$} moḻicūḻ maṟai pāṭi
chanting the vētam which is full of words of mantiram.
aḻicūḻpuṉal ēṟṟa aṇṇal
the ominent god who received the water which descended with the intention of destroying the world.
aṇi āya, paḻi cūḻvu ilar āya pattar paṇintu ētta
the pious people who are an ornament to this world and who do not think of reproachful acts, to pay obeisance and prise.
kaḻicūḻ kaṭal nākaik kārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōṇam on the shore of the ocean which is surrounded by back-waters.
{1:84}__5+
{$} āṇum peṇṇum āy
being male and female.
aṭiyārkku aruḷ nalki
granting his grace to devotees.
cēṇ niṉṟavarkku iṉṉam cintai ceyavallāṉ
Civaṉ who is the object of meditation still to those who are far removed from him. (cēṇniṉṟavar may also mean tēvar who are in heaven)
pēṇa vaḻipāṭu piriyātu eḻum toṇṭar kāṇum kaṭal nākaikkārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōṇam on the shore of the ocean, whom the devotees see in their mental eyes and worship him with love without leaving him.
{1:84}__6+
{$} ēṉattu eyiṟōṭum aravam meypūṇṭu
having adorned himself with the hog's tusk and cobra on the body.
vāṉattu iḷantiṅkaḷ vaḷarum caṭai aṇṇal
the god on whose caṭi the crescent that shines in the sky is flourishing.
ñāṉattu urai vallār nāḷum paṇintu ētta
people who possess words of spiritual wisdom pay obeisance to, and prise Civaṉ
kāṉal kaṭal nākaik kārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōṇam on the shore of the sea which has sea-shore garden.
{1:84}__7+
{$} arai ār aḻal nākam akkōṭu acaittiṭṭu
having tied a cobra as cruel as the fire and chank-beads.
virai ārvarai mārpiṉveṇṇīṟu aṇiaṇṇal
the god who smears sacred ash on his chest which is as strong as the mountin, and which is full of fragrance.
varai ārvaṉapōla vaṅkaṅkaḷ
the ships that make the sea appear to be full of mountins.
vaḷarum karai ārkaṭal nākaik kārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōṇam where on the shore near the see the ships seems to be sleeping.
{1:84}__8+
{$} valamkoḷ pukaḻ pēṇi
desiring the fame that comes from going from left to right.
varaiyāl uyartiṇtōḷ ilaṅkaikku iṟaivāṭa aṭarttu aṅku aruḷ ceytāṉ
Civaṉ granted his grace after pressing by the mountin to decrease the strength of the King of ilaṅki who has tall and strong shoulder.
palam koḷ pukaḻ maṇṇil pattar paṇintu ētta
the pious people pay obeisance and prise the fame of the god that brings good to them.
kalam koḷ kaṭal nākaik kārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōṇam on the shore which has ships.
{1:84}__9+
{$} tirumāl aṭivīḻa
tirumāl to fall at his feet.
ticai nāṉmukaṉ ētta
Piramaṉ who had four faces one for each of the quarters, to prise him.
perumāṉ eṉaniṉṟa pemmāṉ
the god who stood as one possessing greatness.
piṟaic ceṉṉic cerumāl viṭai ūrum celvaṉ
the god who rides on a bull capable of fighting and who has on his head a crescent.
tiraicūḻnta karumāl kaṭal nākaik kārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōṇam on the shore of the great black sea in which waves spread everywhere.
{1:84}__10+
{$} nallār aṟam colla
good people to talk words of moral instruction.
pollār puṟam kūṟa
wicked people to indulge in back-biting.
allār alartūṟṟa
others to spread slandour.
aṭiyārkku aruḷ ceyvāṉ
will grant his grace to his devotees.
pal ār talai mālai aṇivāṉ
puts on a garland a skulls with their teeth intact.
paṇintu ētta
to pay obeisance and prise.
kal ārkaṭal nākaik kārōṇattāṉ
is in nākikkārōnam on the shore of the sea which is always noisy.
{1:84}__11+
{$} karai ār kaṭal nākaik kārōṇam mēya naraiār viṭaiyāṉai navilum campantaṉ urai ār tamiḻmālai pāṭum(m) avar ellām
all those who sing the famous garlands of Tamiḻ verses composed by Campantaṉ on Civaṉ who has a white bull and who dwells with desire in Nākikkārōṇam near the sea is roaring.
karaiyā uruvākik kalivāṉ aṭaivār
will reach heaven which is flourishing, without their bodies being destroyed.

{4:5}__1+
{$} mey elām veṇṇīṟucaṇṇittamēṉiyāṉ tāḷ toḻātē
without worshipping the fest of Civaṉ who has a body completely smeared with white sacred ash.
[[caṇṇittal-
muḻunīṟu caṇṇitta mūrttī pōṟṟi
mey elām veṇṇīṟu caṇṇittārum
āmāttūr caṇṇippāṉai
(nāvukkaracar, tirukkayilāyam, 8; tiruvaṇṇiyūr 3; āmattūr, 8)]]
uyyalām eṉṟu eṇṇi
thinking that I can save myself.
uṟitūkki
carrying in the hand a network of ropes in which is placed the ascetics pitcher.
(1) uḻitantēṉ
I wandered.
uḷḷam viṭṭu
being robbed of my mind.
koy ulām malarccōlaik kuyil kūva mayil ālum ārūraraik kaiyiṉāl toḻātu oḻintu
completely giving up worshipping with joined hands the Lord in Ārūr where in the gardens flowers are plucked and where the indian cuckoos sing and the peacocks dance.
kaṉi irukkak kāy kavarnta kaḷvaṉēṉē
I am a robber who stole unripe fruits when there were ripe fruits to eat;
[[iṉiya uḷavāka iṉṉāta kūṟal,
kaṉi iruppak kāykavarntaṟṟu (Kuṟal, 100)]]
[[Nāvukkaracar calls himself a robber as one cannot steal even unripe fruits with the consent of the owner. Having been a jin is compared to eating the unripe fruits]]
[[Variant reading: (1) uḻintu eṉ]]
{4:5}__2+
{$} eṉpu irutti
having fixed bones.
narampu tōl pukap peytiṭṭu
and putting into it nerves and skin.
eṉai ōr uruvam ākki
and endowing me with a body.
iṉpu irutti
and placing me in happiness.
muṉpu iruntaviṉai tīrttiṭṭu
having removed all my Karmams that were accumulated.
eṉ uḷḷam kōyil ākki
and converting my heart into a temple.
aṉpu irutti
having placed me steady in love.
aṭiyēṉaik kūḻāṭkoṇṭu aruḷceyta ārūrartam muṉpu irukkum viti iṉṟi
without having the good fortune to be in the presence of the Lord in Ārūr who bestowed his grace and admitted me as his slave simple for food.
[[kūḻāṭpaṭutal:
kūḻāṭpaṭṭu niṉṟīrkaḷai eṅkaḷ kuḻuviṉil pukutal oṭṭōm (periyāḻvār, tiruppallāṇṭu, 3);
muyal viṭṭuk kākkaippiṉ pōṉavāṟē
is like giving up hunting for hares and following crows.
[[aṟṟāka nōkki aṟattiṟku aruḷ uṭaimai, muṟṟa aṟintār mutal aṟintār teṟṟa, mutalviṭṭu aḵtu oḻintār ompā oḻukkam, muyalviṭṭu kākkai tiṉal (paḻamoḻi, 141);
iṉaiyataṉmaiyaṉ eṉṟu aṟivariyavaṉ taṉaimuṉviṭṭut tāmmaṟku niṉaippōr, māmuyalviṭṭuk kākkaipiṉpōm kalavar pōlavum (11th book, Kōpappiracātam, 75-78);
kār ārpuravi ēḻpūṇṭa taṉiyāḻit tēr ār niṟaikatirōṉ maṇṭalattaik kīṇṭupukku ārāamutam aṅku eyti atiṉiṉṟum, vārātu oḻivatu oṉṟu uṇṭō? atu niṟka, ēr ār muyalviṭṭuk kākkaippiṉ pōvatē (tirumaṅki āḻvār, ciṟiya tirumaṭal, 6-8)]]
{4:5}__3+
{$} eṉ pāvattaip perukuvittu
having multiplied my sins
paṇṭu elām kuṇṭarkaḷtam collē kēṭṭu
listening only to the words of the low people, camaṇar before I re-embraced civism.
urukuvittu
cusing me to melt.
eṉ uḷḷattiṉ uḷḷirunta kaḷḷattaittaḷḷip pōkki
having removed and driving away and deceitful nature which was in my mind.
arukuvittu
having become scarce.
(1) piṇikāṭṭi
cusing the disease in the stomach.
āṭ koṇṭu
admitting me as his slave.
piṇitīrtta ārūrartam aruku irukkum vitiiṉṟi
without having the good fortune to stay by the side of the Lord in Ārūr who removed the three impurities of the soul [[i.e. āṇavam, mayi and Kaṉmam]]
aṟam irukka
when there was virtue to be had by even paying the price for it.
maṟam vilaikkuk koṇṭavāṟē
my nature is like a ignorant person who brought evils by getting them free of cost which act is to be detested.
[[Variant reading: (1) paṇi]]
{4:5}__4+
{$} kuṇṭaṉāy
Being a strong stout person.
talaipaṟittu
plucking the hirs on the head.
kuvimulaiyār nakai nāṇātu uḻitarvēṉaip paṇṭamāp paṭuttu
converting me as a person of substance who was wandering without being ashamed of the derisive lughter of ladies having conical breasts.
eṉṉaip pāl talaiyil teḷittu
sprinkling on my head milk to purify me.
pātam kāṭṭi
showing his feet.
toṇṭu elām icaipāṭa
when all the devotees sing his prises.
tūmuṟuval aruḷceyyum ārūraip paṇṭu elām aṟiyātē
without esteeming when I was a camanar, the Lord in Ārūr who grants his holy smile on the devotees.
paṉinīrāl (1) paravai ceyap pāvittēṉ
I conceived of creating an ocean with cool water.
[[Variant reading: (1) pāvai]]
{4:5}__5+
{$} tuṉ nākattēṉ āki
Being like a cruel cobra
turccaṉavar coṟkēṭṭu
listening to the words of wicked person.
tuvarvāyk koṇṭu
filling the mouth with the powder of gall-nut of astringent taste.
eṉ ākat tiritantu īṅku irukai ēṟṟu iṭa (1) uṇṭa ēḻaiyēṉnāṉ
I, who am ignorant, who ate food which was given by reaching it in both the hands, and wandering without any use, unmindful of my own duties and without paying attention to anything else.
poṉ ākattu aṭiyēṉaip pukap peytu
Having made me take a golden body
[[in Jainism the bodies of tēvar are called by the name of poṉṉuṭampu'
oḻukkiyal aruntavattu uṭampu nīṅkiṉār aḻipparum poṉ uṭampu aṭaintatu oppavē (civakacintamaṇi, 325)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) uṇṭēṉ]]
poruṭpaṭutta ārūrarai eṉ ākattē iruttātē
without fixing majestically in my body the Lord in Ārūr who made me an important person.
ētaṉ pōrkku ātaṉāy akappaṭṭēṉē
I was cught as an ignorant person in the fight fought by an evil minded person.
{4:5}__6+
{$} pappu ōti
studying works which increases the cycle of birth and death
[[pappu is derived from pampu]]
pavaṇaṉāy
having got the name of Pavaṇanti.
paṟittu oru talaiyōṭē
with the head from which the hirs have been plucked.
(1) oppu ōṭa ōtuvittu
having tught me spiritual wisdom that has no equal.
eṉ uḷḷattiṉ uḷḷiruntu aṅku uṟutikāṭṭi
showing me the appropriate good thing to do, being seated in the innermost recess of my heart.
appōtaikku appōtum aṭiyavarkku ār amutuām ārūrarai eppōtum niṉaiyātē
without always thinking of the Lord in Ārūr who is like the rare nectar for his devotees when they think of him from time to time.
iruṭṭu aṟaiyil malaru kaṟantu eytta vāṟē
is like being exhusted by milking a sterile cow in a dark room.
[[Variant reading: (1) oppōṭē]]
{4:5}__7+
{$} kati oṉṟum aṟiyātē
without knowing even a little about the higher states of existence.
kaṇ aḻalat talai paṟittu
plucking the hirs on the head for the eyes to burn.
kaiyil uṇṭu
eating the food in the hands.
pati oṉṟum neṭuvītip palarkāṇa nakai nāṇātu uḻitarvēṟku
to me who was wandering in the long streets in the cities, and without being ashamed of the derisive lughter, and to be seen by many people.
matitanta ārūril vārtēṉai vāymaṭuttup paruki
drinking the honey which flows in Ārūr which bestowed on me good sense, pouring it into my mouth.
uyyum vitiiṉṟi
without having the good fortune to save myself;
matiyiliyēṉ
I who an devoid of intelligence.
viḷakkirukka miṉmiṉit tīkkāyntavāṟē
is just like warning oneself to remove cold with the heat of the glow-worm when there is a lamp;
[[viḷakku aṅku iruppa miṉmiṉi kavarum aḷappu aruñciṟappuil ātar pōlavum (11th book, Kōpappiracātam, 79-80)]]
{4:5}__8+
{$} pūvaiyāyttalaipaṟittu
plucking the hirs on the head like a woman.
poṟi aṟṟa camaṇ nīcar collē kēṭṭu
accepting only the words of the low camaṇar who have no wisdom.
kāviri cēr kaṇ maṭavārkkaṇṭu ōṭi
running on seeing the ladies whose eyes resemble the blue nelumbo-flowers.
katavu aṭaikkum kaḷvaṉēṉ taṉ āviyaip pōkāmē tavirttu
preventing the life without leaving the body of mine who is a robber and who closes the door, ashamed of seeing those ladies.
eṉṉai āṭkoṇṭa ārūraraippāviyēṉ aṟiyātē
without esteeming the Lord in Ārūr who admitted as a protege me who am a sinner.
pāḻūril payikkampukku eyttavāṟē
is like feeling exhusted after entering into a deserted village to beg alms.

{$} oṭṭāta vāḷ avuṇar puram mūṉṟum ōr ampiṉ vāyiṉ vīḻak kaṭṭāṉai
Civaṉ who weeded out all the three cities of the inimical and cruel acurar by the pointed and of an arrow to fall down as dust.
kāmaṉaiyum kālaṉaiyum kaṇṇiṉoṭukāliṉ vīḻa aṭṭāṉai
who killed Kāmaṉ by his frontal eye and Kālaṉ (the god of death) by his leg.
ārūril ammāṉai
god, our father in Ārūr.
ārvac ceṟṟak kurōtam taṭṭāṉai
who is unaffected by desire, anger and rancour.

I was puffed with pride by doing wasteful acts when there was penance to be undertaken

Civan who crushed the ten heads and shoulders and feet of the strong arakkaṉ who refused to listen to the advice of Nanti
[[uttara-rāmāyaṇam, variyaṭutta patala,, 63-68]]

who cut one of the heads of Piramaṉ who is sitting on the seat of a beautiful lotus.
ārūril ammāṉai
our father in Ārūr.
ālālam uṇṭu kaṇṭam kaṟuttāṉaik karutātē

karumpu irukka irumpukaṭittu eyttavāṟē

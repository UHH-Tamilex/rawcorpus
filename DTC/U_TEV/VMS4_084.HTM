eṭṭām ticaikkum iruticaikkum iṟaivā
the chief of the eight directions, upper and lower directions
[[The eight directions are the four cardinal points and the intermediate directions between cardinal points]]
muṟai eṉṟu iṭṭār amarar vempūcal eṉakkēṭṭu
on hearing the immortals who cried loudly with a complint.
eriviḻiyā
opening the eye of fire.
oṭṭakkayavar tiripuram mūṉṟaiyum ōr ampiṉāl aṭṭāṉ aṭi niḻaṟ kīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyir (ē)
is not my precious life under the shelter of Civaṉ who destroyed with a single arrow all the three cities of the cruel enemies, which were wandering.
[[mūvā uruvattu mukkaṇ mutalva mikkūr iṭumpai, kāvāyeṉak kaṭaitūṅkum maṇiyaik kaiyāl amarar nāvāy acaitta oli olimāṟiyatillai appāl, tīpāy erintu poṭiyāyk kaḻinta tiripuramē (Nāvukkaracar, general 17-3)]]
{4:84}__2+
{$}
pēḻvāy araviṉ araikku amarntu
tying a cobra of large mouth in the waist.
ēṟippiṟaṅku ilaṅku tēyvāy iḷampiṟai ceñcaṭaimēl vaitta tēvarpirāṉ
the master of the celestials who placed on his red caṭi a young waning crescent which ascended on the head, shone and became conspicuous.
mūvāṉ
will not become aged.
iḷakāṉ
will not become tired.
muḻuulakōṭum maṇviṇṇum maṟṟum (1) āvāṉ aṭiniḻaṟkīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyirē
is not my precious life under the shelter of the feet of Civaṉ who is himself complete with this earth, heaven and other worlds?
[[Variant reading: (1) āḷvāṉaṭi]]
{4:84}__3+
{$}
tariyā vekuḷiyaṉāy
with uncontrollable anger.
takkaṉ vēḷvi takarttu ukanta eri ār ilaṅkiya cūlattiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who has a trident shining with fire and who became great by laying waste the sacrifice performed by Takkaṉ.
imaiyāta mukkaṇ periyāṉ
the great god who has three eyes that do not shine with splendour
[[imaittal= piraccuvalittal (takkayākapparam, 334, old commentary)]]
periyār piṟappu aṟuppāṉ
will cut at the root of birth of those who are great by scholarship;
[[periyār:
kaṟṟal kēṭṭal uṭaiyār periyār (campantar, piramāpuram (1) 2]]
eṉṟum taṉ piṟappai ariyāṉ aṭiniḻaṟ kīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyirē
is not my precious life under the shelter of the feet of Civaṉ who does not know his birth at any time?
{4:84}__4+
{$}
vaṭivuṭai vāḷ neṭuṅkaṇ umaiyāḷai ōrpāl makiḻntu
feeling elated in having on one half Umiyāḷ who has exquisite beauty and long eyes which are like the sword.
veṭikoḷa aravoṭu vēṅkai ataḷkoṇṭu mēl maruvi
adorning himself with cobras that live in clefts and covering the upper part of his body with the skin of a tiger
[[mikattāṉ periyator vēṅkai ataḷ koṇṭu mēl maruvi (of this decade)]]
poṭikoḷ akalattu
in the chest in which sacred ash is smeared.
poṉ pitirntaṉṉa paiṅkoṉṟai amtār aṭikaḷ aṭiniḻaṟkīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyirē
is not my precious life under the shelter of the feet of the god who wears a garland of fresh koṉṟi which resembles gold separated into small particles!
{4:84}__5+
{$}
poṟuttāṉ
forgiving the celestials for requesting him to consume the poison.
amararkku amutu aruḷi
giving the immortals the nectar (amarar is used in the sense of tēvar and not in its derivative sense).
nañcam uṇṭu kaṇṭam kaṟuttāṉ
his neck became black by consuming the poison.
kaṟuppu aḻakā uṭaiyāṉ
has that blackness as a beauty;
[[cf.kaṟaimiṭaṟu aṇiyalum aṇintaṉṟu (puṟam, 1-5)]]
kaṅkai ceñcaṭaimēl ceṟuttāṉ
restrined Kaṅki on his red caṭi.
taṉañcayaṉ cēṇ ār akalam kaṇai oṉṟiṉāl aṟuttāṉ aṭiniḻaṟkīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyirē
is not my precious life under the shelter of the feet of Civaṉ who cleft asunder in two with a single arrow the long armour worn on the chest of tāṉañcayaṉ (taṉañcayaṉ is one of the ten names of aruccuṉaṉ; one who has victory as his wealth)

{$}
ceṟaṟku ariyāṉ eṉṟu kālaṉaik kāl oṉṟiṉāl kāyntāṉ, pāyntāṉ
Civaṉ pounced upon Kālaṉ with one leg, being angry with him, as he was difficult to be killed
[[Civaṉ killed Kālaṉ with his left leg;
cf. patimūṉṟum citaittāṉ vāmaccēvaṭi taṉṉāl ciṟitu unti, utaittāṉ, kūṟṟaṉ viṇmukilpōl maṇuṟa vīḻntāṉ (Kanta purāṇam, mārkkaṇṭeyappaṭalam, 253)]]
paṇaimatil mūṉṟum kaṇai eṉṉum oḷḷaḻalāl mēyntāṉ
covered all the three big forts with the bright fire of an arrow; (the arrow was itself fine-god)
[[kuṉṟavārcilai, nāṇ arā, arivāḷi, kūreri kāṟṟiṉ mummatil, veṉṟa āṟu eṅṅaṉē (campantar, tiruvāmāttūr (2) 1)]]
viyaṉ ulaku ēḻum viḷaṅka āyntāṉ viḻumiyanūl
Civaṉ investigated pre-eminent works vētams and ankams so that the wide world may prosper.
aṭiniḻaṟkīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyirē
is not my precious life under the shelter of his feet.

(1) ceṟutaṟku ariyāṉ talaiyai uḷaintāṉ ukir oṉṟiṉāl kaḷaintāṉ
removed with one nil, suffering in mind, the head of Piramaṉ who was difficult to be killed (ceṟaṟku ariyāṉ: previous verse)
ataṉai niṟaiya neṭumāl kaṇ ār kuruti vaḷaintāṉ
filled it to the brim with the blood from the body of tall Māl;
[[cf. tāmaraiyōṉ ciram arintu kaiyil koṇṭār talaiyataṉil pali koṇṭār, niṟaivām taṉmai vāmaṉaṉār mākāyattu utiram koṇṭār (nāvukkaracar, taṉittiruttāṇtkam general (35)]]
oru virali(ṉ)ṉoṭu vīḻvittu
cusing it to fall by a single finger.
cāmpar veṇṇīṟu aṇintāṉ aṭiniḻaṟkīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyirē
is not my previous life under the shelter of the feet of Civaṉ who wallowed in the ash which is white sacred ash.
[[Variant reading: (1) ceṟuttaṟkariyāṉ]]

{$}
muntu i vaṭṭattiṭaippaṭṭatu ellām
all the persecutions that I had to undergo in former days in this world, for forsaking canaṇam and re-embracing civam.
muṭivēntar taṅkaḷ pantivaṭṭattiṭaippaṭṭu alaippuṇpataṟku añcikkollō
perhaps being afrid of being cught in the stable of horses and elephants and kings wearing crown, being put to trouble.
nantivaṭṭam naṟumalarkkoṉṟaiyum nakkaceṉṉi antivaṭṭattu oḷiyāṉ aṭiccērntatu eṉ āruyirē
my precious life reached the feet of Civaṉ who has the brilliant colour of the evening sky and on whose head the flowers nantivaṭṭam and fragrant koṉṟi flowers bloomed.
[[nantivaṭṭam: a white flower;
ciṟitu alarnta, nantivaṭṭattoṭu koṉṟaivaḷāviya nampaṉaiyē (nāvukkaracar, tiruviyāṟu (6) 1);
It is commonly known as nantiyāvaṭṭam;
antaṇaṉ nāṟum āṉpāl aviyiṉai alarntakālai; nantiyāvaṭṭam nāṟum nakaimuṭi aracaṉāyiṉ (Cīvakacintāmaṇi 1287)]]
[[nantivaṭṭam (peruṅkaṭi 2-3-32, 2-15-103)]]

mikattāṉ periyatu or vēṅkai ataḷkoṇṭu meymmaruvi
covering the body with a very big tiger's skin.
akattāṉ veruva nallāḷai naṭukkuṟuppāṉ
Civaṉ will frighten the lady to be afrid of in her mind.
poṉvarum mukattāl kuḷirntiruntu uḷḷattiṉāl ukappāṉ icainta akattāṉ aṭiniḻaṟkīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyirē
is not my precious life under the shelter of the feet of Civaṉ who agrees to feel joy by his mind, satisfying completely his devotees by his brilliant face!

paimmāṉ aravu alkul paṅkayaccīṟaṭiyāḷ veruva, kaimmā (1) varicilaikkāmaṉai aṭṭa kaṭavuḷ
the god who burnt Kāmaṉ (cupid) who had a big bow of many bands held in his hand to make the lady who has small feet like the lotus flower and a private part like the eminent hood of a cobra, to be seized with fear.
mukkaṇ emmāṉ ivaṉ eṉṟu iruvarum ētta
Both Māl and Piramaṉ to prise, this god who has three eyes is our master'
erinimirnta ammāṉ aṭiniḻaṟkīḻatu aṉṟō eṉtaṉ āruyirē
is not my precious life under the shelter of the feet of the father who shot up erect as a column of fire!
[[Variant reading: (1) māvaṭucilaik]]

paḻaku aor ūrti araṉ
Civaṉ who has a vehicle of that bull trined to carry him.
paiṅkaṇ pāriṭam pāṇi ceyya
the pūtams of tender eyes to keep measure of time.
kuḻalum muḻavoṭu mānaṭam āṭi
performing a great dance to the accompaniment of flute and muḻavu.
uyar ilaṅkaik kiḻavaṉ irupatu tōḷum oru viralāl iṟutta aḻakaṉ aṭi niḻaṟ kīḻatu aṉṟō eṉṟaṉ āruyirē
is not my precious life under the shelter of the feet of beautiful Civaṉ who crushed with a single toe all the twenty shoulders of the Lord of eminent ilanki!

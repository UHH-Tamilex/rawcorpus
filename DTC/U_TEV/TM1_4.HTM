• patikam: 1:4
  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭai
  • talam: tirup pukaliyum (_allatu kāḻi), tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyum
  • (()): viṉā_urai
  • VMS: VOL.2 ,P.231

*1
maim maru pūṅkuḻalkaṟṟai tuṟṟa, vāḷnutal māṉviḻi maṅkaiyōṭum,
poym moḻiyā maṟaiyōrkaḷ _ētta, pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_em _iṟaiyē! _imaiyāta mukkaṇ _īca! _eṉ nēca! _itu _eṉ kol collāy---
meymmoḻi nālmaṟaiyōr miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*2
kaḻal malku pantoṭu _ammāṉai muṟṟil kaṟṟavar, ciṟṟiṭaik kaṉṉimārkaḷ,
poḻil malku kiḷḷaiyaic col payiṟṟum pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_eḻil malarōṉciram _ēnti _uṇṭu _or _iṉpu_uṟu celvam_itu _eṉkol collāy---
miḻalaiyuḷ vētiyar _ētti vāḻtta, viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*3
kaṉṉiyar _āṭal kalantu, mikka kantuka vāṭai kalantu, tuṅkap
poṉ _iyal māṭam neruṅku celvap pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_iṉ_icai yāḻmoḻiyāḷ _orpākattu _em _iṟaiyē! _itu _eṉkol colvāy---
miṉ _iyal nuṇ_iṭaiyār miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*4
nākapaṇamtikaḻ _alkul malkum naṉnutal māṉviḻi maṅkaiyōṭum
pūka vaḷampoḻil cūḻnta _am taṇ pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_ēka peruntakai_āya pemmāṉ! _em _iṟaiyē! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
mēkam _uriñcu _eyil cūḻ miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*5
cantu_aḷaṟu _ēṟu taṭam koḷ koṅkait taiyaloṭum, taḷarāta vāymaip
puntiyiṉ nālmaṟaiyōrkaḷ _ēttum, pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_emtamai _āḷ_uṭai _īca! _emmāṉ! _em _iṟaiyē! _itu _eṉkol colvāy---
ventaveṇnīṟū _aṇivār miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*6
caṅku, _oli _ippi, cuṟā, makaram, tāṅki nirantu, taraṅkam mēlmēl
poṅku _oli nīr cumantu _ōṅku cemmaip pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_eṅkaḷ pirāṉ! _imaiyōrkaḷpemmāṉ! _em _iṟaiyē! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
veṅkatil tōy poḻil cūḻ miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*7
kāmaṉ _erippiḻampu _āka nōkki, kāmpu _aṉa tōḷiyōṭum kalantu,
pū maru nāṉmukaṉ pōlvar _ētta, pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_īmavaṉattu _eri_āṭṭu _ukanta _emperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
vī maru taṇpoḻil cūḻ miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*8
_ilaṅkaiyarvēntu _eḻil vāytta tiṇtōḷ _iṟṟu _alaṟa(v) viral _oṟṟi, _aintu-
pulam kaḷaikaṭṭavar pōṟṟa, _am taṇ pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_ilaṅku _eri _ēnti niṉṟu _elli _āṭum _em _iṟaiyē! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
vilaṅkal_oṇmāḷikai cūḻ miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*9
ceṟi muḷarittavicu _ēṟi _āṟum ceṟṟu _atil vīṟṟiruntāṉum, maṟṟaip
poṟi_aravattu_aṇaiyāṉum, kāṇāp pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_eṟimaḻuvōṭu _iḷamāṉ kai _iṉṟi _irunta pirāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
veṟi kamaḻ pūmpoḻil cūḻ miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*10
pattarkaṇam paṇintu _ētta vāytta pāṉmai_atu _aṉṟiyum, palcamaṇum
puttarum niṉṟu _alar tūṟṟa, _am taṇ pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_ettavattōrkkum _ilakku_āy niṉṟa _emperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
vittakar vāḻ poḻil cūḻ miḻalai viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*11
viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpi mēvum vittakam _eṉkol _itu! _eṉṟu colli,
puṇṇiyaṉai, pukali(n) nilāvu pūṅkoṭiyōṭu _iruntāṉaip pōṟṟi,
naṇṇiya kīrtti nalam koḷ kēḷvi nālmaṟai ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa
paṇ _iyal pāṭal vallārkaḷ _intap pāroṭu viṇ paripālakarē.

• patikam: 1:4
  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭi
  • talam: tirup pukaliyum (_allatu kāḻi), tiru vīḻimiḻaliyum
  • (()): viṉā_uri
  • VMS: VOL.2 ,P.231

*1
mim maru pūṅkuḻalkaṟṟi tuṟṟa, vāḷnutal māṉviḻi maṅkiyōṭum,
poym moḻiyā maṟiyōrkaḷ _ētta, pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_em _iṟiyē! _imiyāta mukkaṇ _īca! _eṉ nēca! _itu _eṉ kol collāy---
meymmoḻi nālmaṟiyōr miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*2
kaḻal malku pantoṭu _ammāṉi muṟṟil kaṟṟavar, ciṟṟiṭik kaṉṉimārkaḷ,
poḻil malku kiḷḷiyic col payiṟṟum pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_eḻil malarōṉciram _ēnti _uṇṭu _or _iṉpu_uṟu celvam_itu _eṉkol collāy---
miḻaliyuḷ vētiyar _ētti vāḻtta, viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*3
kaṉṉiyar _āṭal kalantu, mikka kantuka vāṭi kalantu, tuṅkap
poṉ _iyal māṭam neruṅku celvap pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_iṉ_ici yāḻmoḻiyāḷ _orpākattu _em _iṟiyē! _itu _eṉkol colvāy---
miṉ _iyal nuṇ_iṭiyār miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*4
nākapaṇamtikaḻ _alkul malkum naṉnutal māṉviḻi maṅkiyōṭum
pūka vaḷampoḻil cūḻnta _am taṇ pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_ēka peruntaki_āya pemmāṉ! _em _iṟiyē! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
mēkam _uriñcu _eyil cūḻ miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*5
cantu_aḷaṟu _ēṟu taṭam koḷ koṅkit tiyaloṭum, taḷarāta vāymip
puntiyiṉ nālmaṟiyōrkaḷ _ēttum, pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_emtami _āḷ_uṭi _īca! _emmāṉ! _em _iṟiyē! _itu _eṉkol colvāy---
ventaveṇnīṟū _aṇivār miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*6
caṅku, _oli _ippi, cuṟā, makaram, tāṅki nirantu, taraṅkam mēlmēl
poṅku _oli nīr cumantu _ōṅku cemmip pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_eṅkaḷ pirāṉ! _imiyōrkaḷpemmāṉ! _em _iṟiyē! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
veṅkatil tōy poḻil cūḻ miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*7
kāmaṉ _erippiḻampu _āka nōkki, kāmpu _aṉa tōḷiyōṭum kalantu,
pū maru nāṉmukaṉ pōlvar _ētta, pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_īmavaṉattu _eri_āṭṭu _ukanta _emperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
vī maru taṇpoḻil cūḻ miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*8
_ilaṅkiyarvēntu _eḻil vāytta tiṇtōḷ _iṟṟu _alaṟa(v) viral _oṟṟi, _aintu-
pulam kaḷikaṭṭavar pōṟṟa, _am taṇ pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_ilaṅku _eri _ēnti niṉṟu _elli _āṭum _em _iṟiyē! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
vilaṅkal_oṇmāḷiki cūḻ miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*9
ceṟi muḷarittavicu _ēṟi _āṟum ceṟṟu _atil vīṟṟiruntāṉum, maṟṟip
poṟi_aravattu_aṇiyāṉum, kāṇāp pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_eṟimaḻuvōṭu _iḷamāṉ ki _iṉṟi _irunta pirāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
veṟi kamaḻ pūmpoḻil cūḻ miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*10
pattarkaṇam paṇintu _ētta vāytta pāṉmi_atu _aṉṟiyum, palcamaṇum
puttarum niṉṟu _alar tūṟṟa, _am taṇ pukali nilāviya puṇṇiyaṉē!
_ettavattōrkkum _ilakku_āy niṉṟa _emperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
vittakar vāḻ poḻil cūḻ miḻali viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpiyatē?

*11
viṇ _iḻi kōyil virumpi mēvum vittakam _eṉkol _itu! _eṉṟu colli,
puṇṇiyaṉi, pukali(n) nilāvu pūṅkoṭiyōṭu _iruntāṉip pōṟṟi,
naṇṇiya kīrtti nalam koḷ kēḷvi nālmaṟi ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa
paṇ _iyal pāṭal vallārkaḷ _intap pāroṭu viṇ paripālakarē.

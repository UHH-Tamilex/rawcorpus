{3:53}__1+
{$} vāṉaik(1) kāvil veṇmatipulku vārcaṭait, tēṉaikkāvil iṉmoḻittēvipākam āyiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who has one half tēvi (umi) whose words carry the sweetness of honey, and who has a long caṭi on which the crescent which shines in the garden of the sky
[[Sky is compared to a green garden]]
[[vāṉai-ai is expletive.]]
apayamākavāḻpavar
those who lead their lives taking refuge for protection from fear.
āṉaikkāvil aṇṇalai
in the god in āṉikkā.
ēṉaikkāval (2) vēṇṭuvar
will they seek any other protection? (A slight modulation of the voice will change this affirmative sentence into an interrogative sentence.)
ētum ētam illai
they will not have even the slightest blekish.
[[Variant reading: (1) kāval (2) vēṇṭuvārkku]]
{3:53}__2+
{$} cēṟupaṭṭa taṇvayaṟ ceṉṟu ceṉṟu cēṇ ulāvu āṟupaṭṭa taṇtuṟai āṉaikkāvil aṇṇalār
god in āṉikkā which has cool ghats in the river which, coming from a long distance, enters often into the cool fields which are full of mire.
nīṟupaṭṭamēṉiyār
has a holy body on which holy ash is smeared
nikar ilpātam ēttuvār
those who prise his feet which have no comparison.
vēṟupaṭṭacintaiyār
will have a mind different from the common multitude.
viṇṇil eṇṇavallar
they can be considered as having attined eternal bliss.
{3:53}__3+
{$} tāram āya mātarāḷ tāṉ ōrpākam āyiṉāṉ
Civaṉ has on one half a lady as his wife.
īram āya puṉcaṭai ēṟṟa tiṅkaḷ cūṭiṉāṉ
adorned his golden and wet caṭi with a suitable crescent.
āram āya mārpuṭai āṉaikkāvil aṇṇalai vāramāy vaṇaṅkuvār valviṉaikaḷ māyuram
the irresistible Karmams of those who bow with love before the god in āṉikka who adorned his chest with a garl and of pearls.
[[The anecdote referred to in the 3rd line is found in the seventh verse of this decade; tāram ākiya poṉṉit taṇṭuṟai āṭiviḻuttum; nīril niṉṟu aṭipōṟṟi niṉmalā koḷ eṉ āṅkēi āram koṇṭa em āṉaikkāvuṭai ātiyai nāḷum, īram uḷḷavar nāḷum, emmaiyum āḷuṭaiyārē (cuntarar, āṉikka, 7); (maṇiāramārpiṉāṉai) (appar, āṉikkā tāṇṭakam, 4)]]
{3:53}__4+
{$} viṇṇil naṇṇu pulkiya vīram āya mālviṭaic cuṇṇa veṇṇīṟu āṭiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who bathed in the powder of white holy ash and who has a big bull which is valourous and can travel in the sky.
cūlam ēntukaiyiṉāṉ
holds in his hand a trident.
aṇṇal kaṇōr mūṉṟiṉāṉ āṉaikkāvukaitoḻa eṇṇum vaṇṇam vallavarkku
to those who can meditate upon, and can worship with joined hands, āṉikkarū belonging to the god who has three eyes.
ētam oṉṟumillai
there will not be the slightest blemish.
{3:53}__5+
{$} veyya pāvamkaiviṭa vēṇṭuvīrkaḷ
People who wish that cruel sins should leave you!
āṇṭacīrmaikoḷkaṇṭaṉ
Civaṉ who has a black neck and a fame for accepting people as his protege.
veyyatī āṭu kātal mālaiyāṉ
who has the nature of dancing with desire in the hot fire. (mālai-iyalpu; tolkāppiyam (2) Uriyal, 15)
koyyaviṇṭa nāḷmalark koṉṟai tuṉṟu ceṉṉi em aiyaṉ mēya poykai cūḻ āṉaikkāvucērmiṉ
reach āṉikkāvu that blossomed in the early morning to be plucked, are closely placed.
{3:53}__6+
{$} nāṇum ōrvum
wisdom which is the cuse for feeling bashful.
cārvum
wealth that is the support of everyone.
muṉnakaiyum
the fame that produces joy which is the foremost wish for everybody.
uṭkum
the valour that is aweinspiring.
naṉmaiyum
all good things.
pēṇuṟātacelvamum
wealth which is the cuse for not minding anyone.
pēcaniṉṟa peṟṟiyāṉ
Civaṉ who has the nature of being prised for these six attributes.
āṇum peṇṇum ākiya āṉaikkāvil aṇṇalār
who is the god in āṉikka who is both male and female.
kāṇum kaṇṇum mūṉṟuṭaik kaṟaikoḷmiṭaṟaṉ allaṉē
is he not the one who has a black neck and three eyes which posses the power of seeing things?
{3:53}__7+
{$} kūrum mālai naṇpakal kūṭivalla toṇṭarkaḷ
the able devotees gathering together in the evening donse darkness, noon (and morning) (morning is also included as the other two parts of the day are mentioned).
pērum ūrum celvamumpēca niṉṟapeṟṟiyāṉ
Civaṉ who has the nature of being spoken about his names, places, and wealth of grace.
pārum viṇṇum kaitoḻap pāyum kaṅkai nīroṭu ceñcaṭai āram ēntiṉāṉ āṉaikkāvu cērmiṉ
(People of this world!) Reach āṉikkāvu of Civaṉ who bore one his red caṭi the water of Kaṅki which came rushing, to be worshipped by the people of this world and the celestials along with a garland of precious stones.
[[The form āṉaikkāvu (āṉikkāvu) is found in the 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th and 10th verses]]
{3:53}__8+
{$} poṉṉam malku tāmaraippōtu tātuvaṇṭiṉam aṉṉam malku taṇtuṟai āṉaikkāvil aṇṇalaip paṉṉavalla nāṉmaṟai pāṭavalla taṉmaiyōr
those who are capable of singing the four vētams which are capable of prising god in āṉikkā which has ghats in which swans live in large flock and has swarms of bees which feed upon the pollen in the lotus flower in which the goddess of wealth is seated. (poṉ can be linked with tātu and the meaning will be as follows:- the lotus flower in which pollen golden in colour is found in plenty)
moykaḻalmuṉṉavallar
and those who can meditate upon the strong feet.
viṇṇaittuṉṉavallar
are capable of attining eternal bliss (viṇ here denote liberation from bondage of every kind).
{3:53}__9+
{$} ūṉ (ōṭu) uṇṭal naṉṟu eṉa ūṉ (ōṭu)uṇṭaltītu eṉa āṉatoṇṭar aṉpiṉāl pēcaniṉṟa taṉmaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has the nature of being spoken by his devotees eating meat is god and eating meat is bad, with love (The first refers to the worship by Kaṇṇappar in Kāḷatti and the second Civakōcariyār in the same shrine; Kaṇṇappar being a hunter offered well-roasted meat; ōṭu an expletive to fill a gap in the metre)
[[oṭuvum teyyavum icai niṉṟa moḻiyē (naṉṉūl, 436)]]
vāṉoṭu oṉṟu cūṭiṉāṉ
bore on his head a crescent of a single phase that is in the sky.
vāymaiyāka maṉṉi niṉṟu āṉoṭu añcum āṭiṉāḷ āṉaikkāvu cērmiṉ
People of the world! reach āṉikkāvu of Civaṉ who, having stood permanently as the truth, bathed in all the five products of the cow.
{3:53}__10+
{$} kaiyil uṇṇum kaiyarum kaṭukkaḷtiṇ kaḻukkaḷum
the camaṇar who are like the stakes for impaling criminals, who eat the powder of gall-nut; and who are low persons eating food in their hands.
meyyaip pōrkkum poyyarum vētaneṟiyai aṟikilār
and the buddhists, lians, who cover their bodies are not capable of knowing the religion of the vētams.
taiyal pākam āyiṉāṉ taḻal atu uruvattāṉ
Civaṉ who has a young lady as a half and has a body like fire.
eṅkaḷ aiyaṉmē poykai cūḻ āṉaikkāvu cērmiṉ
(People of this world!) reach āṉikkāvu surrounded by a natural tank where our master resides with desire.
{3:53}__11+
{$} ūḻi ūḻi vaiyakattu uyirkaḷ tōṟṟuvāṉoṭum; āḻiyāṉum kāṇkilā āṉaikkāvil aṇṇalai
about the god in āṉikkā whom Piramaṉ who does the functions of creation of living beings in every aeon and Māl who has a discus, were not capable of seeing.
kāḻiñāṉacampantaṉ karutic coṉṉapattivai vāḻiyākak kaṟpavar valviṉaikaḷ māyum
the irresistible karmams of those who learn these ten verses as giving a new life which were composed with a purpose by ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kāḻi, will vanish.
[[There is no reference to Irāvaṇaṉ in the 8th verses as usual and the anecdoto about Māl and Piramaṉ which is usually found in the 9th verse is mentioned in the last verse which is called according to civite tradition, tirukkaṭikkāppu!]]

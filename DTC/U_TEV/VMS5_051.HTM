{5:51}__1+

nīla māmaṇi kaṇṭattar
Civaṉ has a neck like the sapphire
nīḷ caṭaik kōla māmati kaṅkaiyum kūṭṭiṉār
united on his long caṭi the beautiful crescent and Kaṅki
cūlam māṉ maḻu ēnti
holding a trident, deer and a battle-axe
cuṭar muṭip pāl ney āṭuvar pālaittuṟaiyar
the god in Pālittuṟi will bathe in milk and ghee on his shining head.
{5:51}__2+
{$}
kavaḷam māl kaḷiṟṟiṉ uri pōrttavar
Civaṉ covered himself with the skin of big elephant which eats balls of rice or other food
tavaḷa veṇṇakai maṅkai ōr paṅkiṉar
has on one half a lady whose teeth are extremely white
tivaḷa vāṉavar pōṟṟit ticai toḻum pavaḷa mēṉiyar pālaittuṟaiyarē
Civaṉ in pālittuṟi has a holy form like coral, who is worshipped by joined hands towards the direction where he dwells by the celestials so that they may shine prominently;
[[PP: vāḻttu vatum vāṉavarkaḷ tām vāḻ vāṉ; maṉam niṉ pāl tāḻttuvatum tām uyarntu tammai ellām toḻa vēṇṭi
(tiruvācakam, tiruccatakam, 16).]]
{5:51}__3+
{$}
miṉṉiṉ nuṇṇiṭaik kaṉṉiyar mikku eṅkum poṉṉi nīr mūḻki
virigins who have a minute waist like the lightning gather in large numbers and bathe in the river poṉṉi.
aṭi toḻa maṉṉi
staying permanently to worship his feet.
nāṉ maṟaiyōṭu pal kītamum paṉṉiṉār avar pālaittuṟaiyar
god in pālittuṟi sang many songs together with the four vētams.
{5:51}__4+
{$}
nīṭu kāṭu iṭamāy niṉṟa pēykkaṇam kūṭu pūtam kuḻumi niṉṟu ārkkavē āṭiṉār
Civaṉ danced when the groups of pēy which have the cremation ground as their dwelling place and the putams joining them, roared together
aḻaku ākiya nāṉ maṟai pāṭiṉār avar pālaittuṟaiyarē
Civaṉ in pālittuṟi sang the beautiful four vētams.
{5:51}__5+
{$}
cittar, kaṉṉiyar, tēvarkaḷ, tāṉavar, pittar, nāṉmaṟaivētiyar pēṇiya attaṉē
`our father who is desired by people who possess eight mystic powers, virgins, tēvar, acurar, mad people, and brahmins who have learnt the four vētams!
namai āḷ uṭaiyāy eṉum pattar kaṭku aṉpar pālaittuṟaiyarē
Civaṉ in pālittuṟi has love towards the devotees who say, `one who admits us as your protege!'
{5:51}__6+
{$}
viṇṇiṉār paṇintu ētta viyappu uṟum maṇṇiṉār maṟavātu civāya eṉṟu eṇṇiṉārkku iṭamā eḻil vāṉakam paṇṇiṉār avar pālaittuṟaiyarē
Civaṉ in pālittuṟi created the beautiful heaven as the place for the inhabitants of this world who always meditated upon the mantiram `civāya' without forgetting it, with wonder when the celestials who are superior bow to, and prise, Civaṉ.
{5:51}__7+
{$}
kuravaṉār
Civaṉ is the preceptor
koṭu koṭṭiyum kokkarai paṇ keḻumiya vīṇaiyum viraviṉār
he harmonized the instruments Kotukoṭṭi, Kokkari and vīṇi on which melodies can be played.
maruvu nāḷ malar mallikai ceṇpakam paravu nīrp poṉṉip pālaittuṟaiyarē
Civaṉ in pālittuṟi on the banks of the river poṉṉi which has on its water jasmine flowers blossoming in the early morning, and champak which are spread on the surface.
[[pālaittuṟaiyar koṭu koṭṭiyum kokkaraiyum vīṇaiyum viraviṉār is the syntactical link]]
{5:51}__8+
{$}
toṭarum toṇṭaraittukkam toṭarntu vantu aṭarum pōtu araṉāy aruḷ ceypavar
Civaṉ who grants his grace as araṉ at the time when sufferings which come in succession afflict devotees who had taken refuge in him since a long time. (araṉ = haraṉ; one who destroys)
kaṭaliṉ nañcu aṇi kaṇṭar
has a neck which adorns itself with the poison that rose in the sea.
kaṭipuṉal paṭarum ceñcaṭaip pālaittuṟaiyarē
is he who is in pālittuṟi and on whose red cati the rushing water spreads.
{5:51}__9+
{$}
pālaittuṟaiyarē
Civaṉ in pālittuṟi
mēkam tōy piṟai cūṭuvar
wears a crescent that touches the clouds.
mēkalai nākam tōynta araiyiṉar
wears on his waist a cobra as a jewelled girdle
nalliyal pōkam tōynta puṇar mulai maṅkai ōr pākam tōyntavar
has on his half a lady of distinction who has breasts close to each other which has good qualities for embracing.
{5:51}__10+
{$}
pālaittuṟaiyar
Civaṉ in pālittuṟi
veṅkaṇ vāḷ aravu āṭṭi veruṭṭuvar
will frighten people by making the bright and cruel cobra to dance spreading its hood.
am kaṇār
has beautiful eyes (The beauty for the eyes is compassionate looks)
aṭiyārkku aruḷ nalkuvar
will grant his grace to his devotees.
ceṅkaṇ māl ayaṉ tēṭaṟku ariyavar
is difficult to be searched by Māl of red eyes and Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ)
paiṅkaṇ ēṟṟiṉar
has a bull which has tender eyes.
{5:51}__11+
{$}
urattiṉāl arakkaṉ uyar māmalai nerukkiṉāṉai nerittu
crushing the arakkaṉ who frightened the dughter of the mountin uprooting the high and great mountin by his strength
avaṉ pāṭalum irakkamā aruḷ ceyta pālaittuṟai karattiṉāl toḻuvār viṉai ōyumē
the Karmams of those who worship with joined hands pālittuṟi which granted his grace taking pity on him, as soon as he sang the prises of Civaṉ, will come to an end.

{4:41}__1+
{$} ceyvarāl ukaḷum cemmait tiruc cōṟṟut tuṟaiyaṉārē
Civaṉ in Tiruccoṟṟuttuṟi where in the fertile fields murrel fish frisk!
poy virām mēṉi taṉṉaip poruḷ eṉak kālam pōkki
having wasted my life thinking the body which is unreal as a real thing.
mey virām maṉattaṉ allēṉ
I am not having a mind which is united with truth.
vētiyā
one who revealed the vētams!
vētanāvā
Civaṉ who chants the vētams!
aivarāl alaikkappaṭṭa ākkai koṇṭu ayarttup pōṉēṉ
I became weary with a body that is tossed by the five organs of sense.
[[aivar aintu is spoken as noun denoting personal class of beings out of anger]]
{4:41}__2+
{$} (People of this world!)
kaṭṭarāy niṉṟu
remining with bondages
nīṅkaḷ kālattaik kaḻikka vēṇṭā
you need not waste your precious life.
eṭṭaām kaikaḷ vīci elli niṉṟu āṭuvāṉai 1. aṭṭamā malar koṇṭē āṉ añcum āṭṭa āṭi
having bathed in the five products of the cow when devotees bathe him along with eight flowers, Civaṉ who dances standing swinging the eight hands.
[[PP: aṭṭamāmalarcūṭi
(Nāvukkaracar, Tirumaṟikkāṭu, Tirukkuṟuntoki, 1)]]
ciṭṭarāy
being himself well-versed in vedic lore
tiruc cōṟṟuttuṟaiyaṉār aruḷkaḷ ceyvār
Civaṉ in Tiruccōṟṟuttuṟi will bestow many kinds of his grace (on them).

{4:41}__3+
{$} ēttum
(people of this world!) prise
kātalālē
with piety.
elliyum pakalum uḷḷē ēkāntamāka kalliṉālpuram mūṉṟu eyta kaṭavuḷai
in solitude in your minds throughout night and day the god who shot an arrow on the three cities with a mountin improvised into a bow.
pal il veṇtalai kai ēnti
holding in his hand a white skull devoid of teeth.
pal ilam tiriyum celvar
the Lord who wanders in many houses.
tiruc cōṟṟuttuṟaiyaṉār collum naṉ poruḷum āvār
Civan in Tiruccōṟṟuttuṟi is in the form of words and their good meanings;

[[PP: collāṉaip poruḷāṉai

{4:41}__4+
{$} kaṇṭam kaṟaiyarāy
Having a neck of black colour cused by the poison.
neṟṟik kaṇṇarāy
Having a frontal eye.
peṇ ōr pākam iṟaiyarāy
having a lady who stays in one half.
iṉiyar āki
being sweet to meditate upon.
taṉiyarāy
being a person without equal.
paṉi veṇtiṅkaḷ piṟaiyarāy
wearing a crescent of the white and cool moon.
ceyta ellām pīṭarāy
having all he does as great acts.
kēṭu il cōṟṟuttuṟaiyarāy
and being a resident of ōṟṟuttuṟi which is without destruction.
eṉ uḷḷac cōrvu kaṇṭu aruḷiṉār
Civaṉ granted his grace realising the weakness of my mind.
{4:41}__5+
{$} pontaiyaip poruḷā eṇṇi
thinking the body with several holes as a thing of reality.
poruk keṉakkālam pōṉēṉ
I wasted my time quickly
entaiyē
my father!
ēkamūrtti
single god having a form.
eṉṟu niṉṟu ēttamāṭṭēṉ
I am unable to prise continuously by uttering the above mentioned names
pantam āy vīṭum ākip paramparam āki niṉṟu
being bondage and liberation and supreme above superior gods;

pantamum āy vīṭum āyiṉār
(tiruvācakam, tiruvaṇṭāppakuti, 50; tiruppoṟcuṇṇam, 20)]]

tiruccōṟṟuttuṟaiyaṉār
Civaṉ in Tiruccōṟṟuttuṟi.
cintaiyuḷ tēṟalpōlum
is like the honey in the mind.
{4:41}__6+
{$} iṉip pērttup piṟavā vaṇṇam pitaṟṟumiṉ pētai paṅkaṉ pārttaṉukku aruḷ ceyta pācupataṉ tiṟamē
prate the qualities of Pācupatan who bestowed his grace on Pārttaṉ, and who has a young lady on one half, so that you may not be born agin hereafter.
ārttu vantu iḻivatu otta alai puṉaṟ kaṅkai ēṟṟut tīrttamāyppōtaviṭṭār tiruc cōṟṟut tuṟaiyaṉār
the Lord in Tiruccōṟṟuttuṟi let down as holy water after receiving the Kaṅki of moving waves which appeared like descending with a roaring noise.
{4:41}__7+
{$} antō! entai empirāṉāy niṉṟa iṟaivaṉai ēttātu
Alas! without prising the Lord who was my father and my master.
kontu ār pūṅkuḻaliṉāraik kūṟiyē kālam pōṉa
all my time was wasted in prising women who had adorned their soft tresses of hir with bunch of flowers.
muntu arā alkulāḷai uṭaṉ vaitta ātimūr/tti
the primordial god who has a form and who placed by his side an eminent lady whose waist is like the hood of the cobra.
centātu puṭaikaḷ cūḻnta tiruccōṟṟuttuṟaiyaṉārē
is the one who is in Tiruccōṟṟuttuṟi surrounded on all sides by red pollen (falling from flowers)
{4:41}__8+
{$} amkatirōṉ avaṉai (1) aṇṇalāk karutavēṇṭā
Do not regard the beautiful sun as the chief god;
[[arukkaṉpātam vaṇaṅkuvar antiyil, aṭukkaṉ āvāṉ araṉ uru allaṉō (appar, ātipurāṇakkuṟuntoki,8)]]
veṅkatirōṉ vaḻiyē pōvataṟku amaintu koḷmiṉ
prepare yourseelf to go to heaven by piercing through the sun of hot rays.
amkatirōṉ avaṉai uṭaṉ vaitta ātimūrtti
the primordial god having a form who placed in him the sun-god.
ceṅkatirōṉ vaṇaṅkum tiruccōṟṟut tuṟaiyaṉār
is the Lord in Tiruccōṟṟuttuṟi where sun of red rays worshipped Civaṉ.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṇṇamāk]]
{4:41}__9+
{$} ulakattīr
people of this world.
ōtiyē kaḻikkiṉṟīrkaḷ
you spend your life by reading.
oruvaṉ taṉṉai nītiyāl niṉaikka māṭṭīr
you do not think of the unequalled god by the moral conduct.
niṉ malaṉ eṉṟu collīr
you do not say, `God without blemish'
cātiyā nāṉmukaṉum cakkarattāṉum kāṇāc cōtiyāyc cuṭar (atu) āṉār tiruccōṟṟuttuṟaiyaṉār
the god in Tiruccoṟṟuttuṟi became a ball of fire which could not be found by Piramaṉ of four faces and Māl who has a discus, and who did not try to find him within themselves.
{4:41}__10+
{$} maṟṟu nīr maṉam vaiyātē maṟumaiyaik kaḻikkavēṇṭil
if you do not want to be born agin, without thinking of anything else.
peṟṟatu ōr upāyam taṉṉāl pirāṉaiyē pitaṟṟumiṉkaḷ
prate the names of the master, with the means of initiation by a preceptor.
kaṟṟu (um)
though learned.
vantu arakkaṉ ōṭik kayilāya malai eṭukka
when the arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ) came and lifted the mount, Kayili, running towards it.
tiruccōṟṟut tuṟaiyaṉār ceṟṟu ukantu aruḷic ceytār
the god in Tiruccōṟṟuttuṟi destroyed his strength and later granted him long life, a sword and a title īcuvaraṉ, when he chanted Vētam, being pleased with it.

{1:18}__1+
{$} paṇṭaikkālaṉ valikāliṉṉoṭu pōkki
Having destroyed the strength of the old Kālaṉ by his leg.
kaṭikamaḻum nīlam malarp poykai niṉṟiyūril nilaiyōrkku
to the god who stays permanently in Niṉṟiyūr which has natural tanks in which fragrant blue nelumbo flowers grow.
cūlam paṭai
trident is the weapon.
cūṭunīṟu cuṇṇappoṭi cāntam
well burnt holy ash is the toilet powder and sandal-wood paste.
pāl ammati caṭai muṭimēlatu
the crescent which is as white as milk is on the matted hir coiled into a crown.
[[paṇṭaikkālaṉ: This suggests that Kālaṉ is now without self-conceit, and does not go near devotees of Civaṉ]]
{1:18}__2+
{$} aṭiyār
devotees.
niṉṟiyūril (1) naccam miṭaṟuṭaiyār naṟuṅkoṉṟai (2) nayantu āḷum paccam uṭai aṭikaḷ tiruppātam paṇivār
who bow to the holy feet of the god who has affection to admit people who offer fragrant koṉṟi flowers, himself having desired it, who is in niṉṟiyūr and has stationed in his neck poison.
accam ilar
have nothing to fear.
pāvam ilar
have no sins.
kēṭum ilar
have no destruction which is the result of sins
niccam uṟu nōyum ilar
do not have acute sufferings also, dily.
[[Variant reading: (1) naccam (2) nayantāṉām, nayantāṉāy.]]
{1:18}__3+
{$} paṟaiyiṉ oli
the sound of drums.
caṅkiṉ oli
the sound produced in conches.
pāṅku āravum
is full on all sides.
āra aṟaiyum oli uṅkum avai aṟivār
those devotees who know other sounds produced everywhere, from instruments beaten with a stick.
niṟaiyum puṉal caṭaimēl uṭai aṭikaḷ niṉṟiyūril uṟaiyum iṟai avar (1) taṉmai allatu eṉatu uḷḷam uṇarātu
my mind will not know any other thing except the nature of the god who dwells in niṉṟiyūr and who has on his matted hir water (of Kaṅki) which is full.
[[Variant reading: (1)tammai]]
{1:18}__4+
{$} pūṇṭa(v) varaimārpiṟ purinūlaṉ
He has a beautiful sacred thread of three strands on his chest which can be compared to a mountin.
virikoṉṟai ataṉōṭum īṇṭa (v)
blossomed koṉṟi flowers are dense along with the sacred thread.
pāl ammatitaṉṉait tīṇṭum poḻilcūḻnta tiruniṉṟiyūril āṇṭa kaḻal (1) toḻil allatu
unless the devotees worship the feet of the god which can accept them into their grace, in niṉṟiyūr surrounded by gardens which seem to touch the milk-white moon.
avar aṟivu aṟiyār
those devotees cannot know god with the help of their own knowledge, which is defective.
[[unless god himself grants spiritual vision or inward illumination one cannot see god]]
[[Variant reading: (1)toḻal]]
{1:18}__5+
{$} kuḻaliṉ icai vaṇṭiṉ icai kaṇṭu
Hearing the music of the flute and the humming of the bees.
kuyil kūvum niḻaliṉ eḻiltāḻnta poḻil cūḻnta niṉṟiyūril
in niṉṟiyūr surrounded by gardens where the beautiful shadows interspersed with spots of light are found and where the indian cuckoo sings.
aḻaliṉ valaṉ aṅkaiyatu ēnti aṉal āṭum kaḻaliṉ oli āṭum puri kuṭavuḷ kaḷaikaṇ
the god who is loved and dances to make the anklets sound, holding a red-hot iron which has flames burning clock-wise and dances in the fire at the end of the world, is our support.
{1:18}__6+
{$} mural muṟuval veṇkai uṭaiyāḷ orupākam
Having as a half a lady of smiling white teeth.
cāral mati ataṉōṭu uṭaṉ calavam caṭai vaittavīraṉ mali
the hero who placed water(of Kaṅki) on his matted hir along with the crescent on one side, reside cheerfully.
aḻaku ārpoḻil miṭaiyum tiruniṉṟiyūraṉ kaḻal allātu eṉatu uḷḷam uṇarātu
my mind will not know anything except the feet of the god in niṉṟiyūr where beautiful gardens are growing densely.
{1:18}__7+
{$} orutalaikaiyiṉil paṟṟi ēntip pali tērum peṟṟiyatu āki
assuming a form for begging alms and holding tightly a skull in his hand.
tiritēvarperumāṉār
the great Lord of the tēvar who wanders for that.
cuṟṟi oru vēṅkai ataḷōṭum piṟaicūṭum neṟṟi oru kaṇṇār niṉṟiyūril nilaiyār
the god with a frontal eye, who adorns himself with a crescent, besides tying round his waist the skin of a tiger, is staying permanently in Niṉṟiyūr.

VMS9 / {1:18}__PIFI8+
{$} nalla(m) malar mēlāṉoṭu ñālam (m) atu uṇṭāṉ, allar eṉa āvar eṉa niṉṟum aṟivu ariya
one who could not be known by piraman seated in a fertile (lotus)flower and Māl who swallowed all the worlds, who were so proud as to fight to decide who was the chief and who was not.
nelliṉ poḻil cūḻnta niṉṟiyūril nilaiyār em celvar
the Lord who is permanent in niṉṟiyūr surrounded by vast stretch of paddy fields, and who is our wealth.
aṭi allātu eṉa cintai uṇarātu
my mind will not know any other thing except his feet as worthy of worship
VMS10 / {1:18}__PIFI9+
{$} neṟiyil varu pērāvakai niṉaiyā niṉaivu oṉṟai aṟivu il camaṇ ātar uraikēṭṭum mayarātē
without getting confused on hearing the words of the ignorant, camaṇar who could not understand the one thing which is meditated upon, losing one's identity without forgetting and not changing from it, which has come down to us as the preceptor showed spiritual path from time immemorial.
neṟiyillavar kuṟikaḷ niṉaiyātē
unmindful of the symbols of those people who have no right paths of their own.
niṉṟiyūril maṟi ēntiyakaiyāṉaṭi vāḻttum atu vāḻttē
that prise is really worth the name, which extolls the feet of the Lord who holds in his hand a young deer, in niṉṟiyūr.
VMS11 / {1:18}__PIFI10+
{$} kuṉṟam(m) atu eṭuttāṉ uṭal tōḷum nerivāka
the body and the shoulders (of that arakkaṉ) who lifted the mountin to be crushed.
niṉṟu aṅku oru viralāl uṟavaittāṉ niṉṟiyūrai
prising niṉṟiyūr of the god who fixed firmly one toe, standing.
naṉṟu ārtaru pukalit tamiḻ ñāṉam mikupantaṉ kuṉṟāttamiḻ colla
if one says the tamiḻ verses whose fame does not diminish, composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ wall-versed in Tamiḻ, a native of pukali which abounds in good things.
kuṟaiviṉṟi niṟai pukaḻē
his fame will be filling the earth without decreasing at any time

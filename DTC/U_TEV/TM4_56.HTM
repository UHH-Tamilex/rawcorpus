*1
mā-_iru ñālam_ellām malar_aṭi vaṇaṅkumpōlum;
pāy _iruṅ kaṅkaiyāḷaip paṭarcaṭai vaipparpōlum;
kāy _irum poḻilkaḷ cūḻnta kaḻumala_ūrarkku _am poṉ-
_āyiram koṭupparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*2
maṭantai pākattarpōlum; māṉmaṟik kaiyarpōlum;
kuṭantaiyil kuḻakarpōlum; kol pulit tōlarpōlum;
kaṭainta nañcu _uṇparpōlum; kālaṉaik kāyvarpōlum;
_aṭaintavarkku _aṉparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*3
_uṟṟa nōy tīrpparpōlum; _uṟu tuṇai _āvarpōlum;
ceṟṟavar puraṅkaḷmūṉṟum tī _eḻac ceṟuvarpōlum;
kaṟṟavar paravi _ēttak kalantu _ulantu _alantu pāṭum
_aṟṟavarkku _aṉparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*4
maḻu _amar kaiyarpōlum; mātu_avaḷ pākarpōlum;
_eḻu nuṉai vēlarpōlum; _eṉpu koṇṭu _aṇivarpōlum;
toḻutu _eḻuntu _āṭip pāṭit tōttirampalavum colli
_aḻumavarkku _aṉparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*5
poṭi _aṇi meyyarpōlum; poṅku veṇnūlarpōlum;
kaṭiyatu _ōr viṭaiyarpōlum; kāmaṉaik kāyvarpōlum;
veṭupaṭutalaiyarpōlum; vēṭkaiyāl paravum toṇṭar
_aṭimaiyai _aḷapparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*6
vakkaraṉ _uyirai vavvak kaṇmalarkoṇṭu pōṟṟac
cakkaram koṭupparpōlum; tāṉavartalaivarpōlum;
tukka mā mūṭartammait tuyarilē vīḻpparpōlum---
_akku _arai _ārpparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*7
viṭai taru koṭiyarpōlum; veṇpurinūlarpōlum;
paṭai taru maḻuvarpōlum; pāy pulittōlarpōlum;
_uṭai taru kīḷarpōlum; _ulakamum _āvarpōlum---
_aṭaipavar _iṭarkaḷ tīrkkum _āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*8
munti vāṉōrkaḷ vantu muṟaimaiyāl vaṇaṅki _ētta;
nanti, mākāḷar_eṉpār, naṭu_uṭaiyārkaḷ niṟpa;
cintiyātē _oḻintār tiripuram _eripparpōlum---
_anti vāṉmatiyam cūṭum _āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*9
pāṉ _amar _ēṉam _ākip pār _iṭantiṭṭa mālum,
tēṉ _amarntu _ēṟum _allit ticaimukam _uṭaiya kōvum,
tīṉarait tiyaṅku _aṟutta tiru_uru _uṭaiyarpōlum;
_āṉnarai_ēṟṟarpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

*10
pārttaṉukku _aruḷvarpōlum; paṭarcaṭaimuṭiyarpōlum;
_ēttuvār _iṭarkaḷ tīra _iṉpaṅkaḷ koṭupparpōlum;
kūttarāyp pāṭi, _āṭi, koṭu vali _arakkaṉtaṉṉai
_ārtta vāy _alaṟuvippār---_āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉārē.

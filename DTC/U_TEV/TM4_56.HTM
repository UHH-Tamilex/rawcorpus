*1
mā-_iru ñālam_ellām malar_aṭi vaṇaṅkumpōlum;
pāy _iruṅ kaṅkiyāḷip paṭarcaṭi vipparpōlum;
kāy _irum poḻilkaḷ cūḻnta kaḻumala_ūrarkku _am poṉ-
_āyiram koṭupparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*2
maṭanti pākattarpōlum; māṉmaṟik kiyarpōlum;
kuṭantiyil kuḻakarpōlum; kol pulit tōlarpōlum;
kaṭinta nañcu _uṇparpōlum; kālaṉik kāyvarpōlum;
_aṭintavarkku _aṉparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*3
_uṟṟa nōy tīrpparpōlum; _uṟu tuṇi _āvarpōlum;
ceṟṟavar puraṅkaḷmūṉṟum tī _eḻac ceṟuvarpōlum;
kaṟṟavar paravi _ēttak kalantu _ulantu _alantu pāṭum
_aṟṟavarkku _aṉparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*4
maḻu _amar kiyarpōlum; mātu_avaḷ pākarpōlum;
_eḻu nuṉi vēlarpōlum; _eṉpu koṇṭu _aṇivarpōlum;
toḻutu _eḻuntu _āṭip pāṭit tōttirampalavum colli
_aḻumavarkku _aṉparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*5
poṭi _aṇi meyyarpōlum; poṅku veṇnūlarpōlum;
kaṭiyatu _ōr viṭiyarpōlum; kāmaṉik kāyvarpōlum;
veṭupaṭutaliyarpōlum; vēṭkiyāl paravum toṇṭar
_aṭimiyi _aḷapparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*6
vakkaraṉ _uyiri vavvak kaṇmalarkoṇṭu pōṟṟac
cakkaram koṭupparpōlum; tāṉavartalivarpōlum;
tukka mā mūṭartammit tuyarilē vīḻpparpōlum---
_akku _ari _ārpparpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*7
viṭi taru koṭiyarpōlum; veṇpurinūlarpōlum;
paṭi taru maḻuvarpōlum; pāy pulittōlarpōlum;
_uṭi taru kīḷarpōlum; _ulakamum _āvarpōlum---
_aṭipavar _iṭarkaḷ tīrkkum _āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*8
munti vāṉōrkaḷ vantu muṟimiyāl vaṇaṅki _ētta;
nanti, mākāḷar_eṉpār, naṭu_uṭiyārkaḷ niṟpa;
cintiyātē _oḻintār tiripuram _eripparpōlum---
_anti vāṉmatiyam cūṭum _āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*9
pāṉ _amar _ēṉam _ākip pār _iṭantiṭṭa mālum,
tēṉ _amarntu _ēṟum _allit ticimukam _uṭiya kōvum,
tīṉarit tiyaṅku _aṟutta tiru_uru _uṭiyarpōlum;
_āṉnari_ēṟṟarpōlum---_āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

*10
pārttaṉukku _aruḷvarpōlum; paṭarcaṭimuṭiyarpōlum;
_ēttuvār _iṭarkaḷ tīra _iṉpaṅkaḷ koṭupparpōlum;
kūttarāyp pāṭi, _āṭi, koṭu vali _arakkaṉtaṉṉi
_ārtta vāy _alaṟuvippār---_āvaṭutuṟiyaṉārē.

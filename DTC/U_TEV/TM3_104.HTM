*1

*2

*3

*4

_am curumpu _ār kuḻal cōra, _uḷḷam kavarntārkku _iṭampōlum---

*5

_ēr pulku cāyal _eḻil kavarnta _iṟivarkku _iṭampōlum---

*6

*7

_iṟi vaḷi cōra _eḻil kavarnta _iṟivarkku _iṭampōlum---

*8

*9

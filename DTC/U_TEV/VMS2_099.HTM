(People of this world!)
iṉṟu naṉṟu nāḷai naṉṟu eṉṟu niṉṟa iccaiyāl
with the standing desire, thinking Today is good; tomorrow is good'
poṉṟukiṉṟa vāḻkkaiyaip pōkaviṭṭup pōtumiṉ
go giving up this life which will not be permanent.
miṉtayaṅku cōtiyāṉ
Civaṉ has a lustre that shines like lightning
veṇmati, viripuṉal, koṉṟai tuṉṟu ceṉṉiyāṉ kōṭi kāvucērmiṉ
Reach Kōṭikāvu which is the abode of Civaṉ on whose head a white crescent spreading water (of the Kaṅki) and flowers of koṉṟi are close to one another
{2:99}__2+
{$} nīr allal mikka vāḻkkaiyai ātarittirātu
(People of this world!) without wishing for this life in which sufferings are great.
nallatu ōr neṟiyiṉai nāṭutum; naṭa(m)miṉō
Let us seek a good path; you come along with me.
villai aṉṉa vāḷnutal veḷviṉai ōr pākam ām kollai veḷḷai ēṟṟiṉāṉ kōṭi kāvu cērmiṉ
you reach Kōṭikāvu of the god who has a white bull of the forest tract and who has as a half a lady who wears white bangles and has a forehead like the bow.
[[villai: ai expletive; The bow that is bent into the form of a semi-circle is compared to the forehead]]

(People of this world!)
tukkamikka vāḻkkaiyiṉ cōrviṉaittuṟantu
renouncing the languishing nature of the worldly life which is for the most part full of sufferings
takkatu ōr neṟiyiṉaic cārtal ceyyap pōtumiṉ
you go to be near to the path which is proper
araimicai akku aṇintu
Having worn on the waist chank beads
āṟu aṇinta ceṉṉimēl kokku iṟaku aṇintavaṉ kōṭikāvu cērmiṉ
you reach Kōṭikāvu of Civaṉ who wore on his head on which there is a river (Kaṅki) and a flower resembling the crane's father
{2:99}__4+
{$}
(people of this world!)
paṇṭu ceyta valviṉai paṟṟu aṟakkeṭum vakai uṇṭu
there is a way to destroy the irresistible actions done in previous births without even the trace of their existence
umakku uraippaṉ nāṉ
I shall tell you
ollai nīr eḻumiṉō
you start quickly
maṇṭu kaṅkai ceñcaṭai vaittu mātu ōr pākamākak koṇṭu ukanta mārpiṉāṉ kōṭikāvu cērmiṉ
you reach Kōṭikāvu belonging to Civaṉ who has on his chest with gladness a lady, having placed on his red cati Kaṅki which is rushing
{2:99}__5+
{$}
(People of this world)
muṉṉai nīr ceyta pāvattāl mūrtti pātam cintiyātu
without bestowing your thoughts on the feet of Civaṉ of a beautiful form, due to the sins committed in your previous births
iṉṉam nīr iṭum paiyil (1) mūḻkiṟīr; eḻu(m)miṉō
you are still sinking in the ocean of sufferings; you rise
poṉṉai veṉṟa koṉṟaiyāṉ
Civaṉ who wears flowers of koṉṟi which are superior to gold in colour.
pūtam pāṭa āṭalāṉ
who dances when the pūtams sing
(2)kōṉ navilum vēliṉāṉ kōṭi kāvu cērmiṉ
you reach Kōtikāvu of Civaṉ who holds a trident which strikes fear
[[Variant reading: (1) vīḻkiṟīr (2) koṉṉavillum]]

(people of this world!)
ēvam mikka cintaiyōṭu iṉpam eytalām eṉappāvam ettaṉaiyum nīr ceytu orupayaṉ ilai
There is no use in committing all the sins enumerated in works by exhusting them; with a mind full of evil thoughts, thinking, we can enjoy happiness'
kāval mikka mānakar kāyntu
Having destroyed by his anger the great cities of very great protection.
veṅkaṉalpaṭak kōvam mikka neṟṟiyāṉ kōṭikāvucērmiṉ
Reach Kōṭikāvu of Civaṉ who has an eye in the forehead from which hot fire started out of anger
{2:99}__7+
{$} ēṇ aḻinta vāḻkkaiyai iṉpam eṉṟu iruntu
thinking that this life which is without any greatness, is happy
nīr māṇ aḻinta mūppiṉāl varuntal muṉṉam vammiṉō
(people of this world!) you come along with me before you suffer due to old age which has lost all its charms.
pūṇal veḷḷelumpiṉāṉ
Civaṉ whose ornaments are white bones
poṉ tikaḻ caṭai muṭikkōṇal veṇpiṟaiyiṉāṉ kōṭikāvucērmiṉ
reach Kōtīkāvu belonging to him who wears curved crescent on his caṭi coiled into a crown which shines like gold
{2:99}__8+

(People of this world!)
maṟṟu i(v) vāḻkkai mey eṉum maṉattiṉaittavirntu
giving up the idea that this earthly life is real and permanent
nīr cēvaṭi paṟṟivāḻmiṉ
you live catching hold of the red feet of the Lord.
paṇintu vantu eḻumiṉō
rise bowing (before his feet)
veṟṟi koḷ tacamukaṉ viṟal keṭa iruntatu ōr kuṟṟam ilvaraiyiṉāṉ kōṭi kāvu cērmiṉ
you reach Kōṭikāvu of Civaṉ who has a faultless mountin on which he sat to destroy the strength of Irāvaṇaṉ of ten faces who was victorious formerly
{2:99}__9+
{$} maṅkum nōy uṟumpiṇimāyum vaṇṇam colluvaṉ
(People of this world!) I shall tell you the way by which the bondage of birth which is the cuse for the diseases to afflict the body, to disappear.
ceṅkaṇ māl ticai mukaṉ ceṉṟu aḷantum kāṇkilā veṅkaṇmāl viṭai yuṭaivētiyaṉ virumpum ūr
the place that is desired by Civaṉ, the Vētiyaṉ who has a big bull of cruel looks, and who was not found out by the red-eyed Māl and Piramaṉ though they went digging the earth and flying high in the sky respectively.
koṅku ulām vaḷam poḻil kōṭi kāvu cērmiṉ
reach Kōṭikāvu which has fertile gardens from which fragrance is moving about.
{2:99}__10+
{$} taṭṭoṭu taḻaimayiṟ pīlikoḷ camaṇarum, paṭṭuṭaiviri tukiliṉārkaḷ col payaṉilai
(You) cannot profit by the words of camaṇar who hold in their hands a bundle of luxuriant peacock feathers and mats to sit on, and the buddhists who cover their bodies with unfolded robes of silk; (People of this world)
viṭṭa puṉ caṭaiyiṉāṉ mētakum muḻavoṭum koṭṭu amainta āṭalāṉ kōṭikāvucērmiṉ
reach Kōṭikavu of the performer of dance which is appropriate to the beating of the eminent muḻavu, and has hanging caṭi
{2:99}__11+
{$} kontu aṇi kuḷir poḻiṟ kōṭikāvu mēviya centaḻal uruvaṉai
about Civaṉ who has a body like the red fire, and who dwells willingly in Kōtikāvu which has cool gardens made beautiful by bunches of flowers
cīrmiku tiṟaluṭai antaṇar pukaliyuḷ āya kēḷviñāṉacampantaṉatamiḻ vallār pāvamāṉapāṟum
the sins of those who are able to recite the tamiḻ verses of Ñāṉacampantaṉ who has acquired knowledge of listening to words of wisdom, in Pukali of brahmins whose strength is preeminent, will be destroyed.

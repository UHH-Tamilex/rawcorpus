  • paṇ: kāntāram
  • VMS: Vol.12, p.48

*1
cuṇṇaveṇcantaṉaccāntum, cuṭart tiṅkaḷcūḷāmaṇiyum,
vaṇṇa _urivai_uṭaiyum, vaḷarum pavaḷaniṟamum,
_aṇṇal _araṇ muraṇ _ēṟum, _akalam vaḷāya _aravum,
tiṇṇeṉ keṭilavappuṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*2
pūṇṭatu _or kēḻal_eyiṟum, poṉtikaḻ _āmai puraḷa,
nīṇṭa tiṇtōḷ valam cūḻntu nilākkatir pōla veṇnūlum,
kāṇtaku puḷḷiṉ ciṟakum, kalanta kaṭṭaṅkak koṭiyum,
_īṇṭu keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*3
_otta vaṭattu _iḷa nākam _uruttirapaṭṭam_iraṇṭum,
muttuvaṭak kaṇṭikaiyum, muḷaittu, _eḻu mū_ilaivēlum,
cittavaṭamum, _atikaic cēṇ _uyar vīraṭṭam cūḻntu
tattum keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*4
maṭamāṉmaṟi, poṉkalaiyum, maḻu, pāmpu, _oru kaiyil vīṇai,
kuṭamāḷvaraiya tiṇtōḷum, kuṉicilaikkūttiṉ payilvum,
_iṭam māl taḻuviya pākam, _iru nilaṉ _ēṟṟa cuvaṭum,
taṭam _ār keṭilap puṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*5
palapala kāmattar _ākip pataittu _eḻuvār maṉattuḷḷē
kalamalakkiṭṭut tiriyum kaṇapati _eṉṉum kaḷiṟum,
valam _ēntu _iraṇṭucuṭarum, vāṉ kayilāyamalaiyum,
nalam _ār keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*6
karantaṉa koḷḷiviḷakkum, kaṟaṅku tuṭiyiṉ muḻakkum,
paranta patiṉeṇkaṇamum, payiṉṟu_aṟiyātaṉa pāṭṭum,
_araṅku_iṭai nūl _aṟivāḷar _aṟiyappaṭātatu _or kūttum,
niranta keṭalappuṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*7
kolai varivēṅkai _ataḷum, kulavōṭu _ilaṅku poṉ-tōṭum,
vilai peṟu caṅkakkuḻaiyum, vilai _il kapālakkalaṉum,
malaimakaḷ kaikkoṇṭa mārpum, maṇi _ārntu _ilaṅku miṭaṟum,
_ulavu keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*8
_āṭal purinta nilaiyum, _araiyil _acaitta _aravum,
pāṭal payiṉṟa palpūtam, pal_āyiram koḷ karuvi
nāṭaṟku _ariyatu _or kūttum, naṉku _uyar vīraṭṭam cūḻntu
_ōṭum keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*9
cūḻum _aravattukilum, tukil kiḻi kōvaṇakkīḷum,
yāḻiṉ moḻiyavaḷ _añca _añcātu _aru varai pōṉṟa
vēḻam _uritta nilaiyum, viri poḻil vīraṭṭam cūḻntu
tāḻum keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭaiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*10
narampu _eḻu kaikaḷ piṭittu, naṅkai naṭuṅka, malaiyai
_uraṅkaḷ_ellām koṇṭu _eṭuttāṉ _oṉpatum_oṉṟum _alaṟa,
varaṅkaḷ koṭuttu _aruḷceyvāṉ, vaḷar poḻil vīraṭṭam cūḻntu
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illai; _añca varuvatum _illai.

*1
cuṇṇaveṇcantaṉaccāntum, cuṭart tiṅkaḷcūḷāmaṇiyum,
vaṇṇa _urivi_uṭiyum, vaḷarum pavaḷaniṟamum,
_aṇṇal _araṇ muraṇ _ēṟum, _akalam vaḷāya _aravum,
tiṇṇeṉ keṭilavappuṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*2
pūṇṭatu _or kēḻal_eyiṟum, poṉtikaḻ _āmi puraḷa,
nīṇṭa tiṇtōḷ valam cūḻntu nilākkatir pōla veṇnūlum,
kāṇtaku puḷḷiṉ ciṟakum, kalanta kaṭṭaṅkak koṭiyum,
_īṇṭu keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*3
_otta vaṭattu _iḷa nākam _uruttirapaṭṭam_iraṇṭum,
muttuvaṭak kaṇṭikiyum, muḷittu, _eḻu mū_ilivēlum,
cittavaṭamum, _atikic cēṇ _uyar vīraṭṭam cūḻntu
tattum keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*4
maṭamāṉmaṟi, poṉkaliyum, maḻu, pāmpu, _oru kiyil vīṇi,
kuṭamāḷvariya tiṇtōḷum, kuṉicilikkūttiṉ payilvum,
_iṭam māl taḻuviya pākam, _iru nilaṉ _ēṟṟa cuvaṭum,
taṭam _ār keṭilap puṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*5
palapala kāmattar _ākip patittu _eḻuvār maṉattuḷḷē
kalamalakkiṭṭut tiriyum kaṇapati _eṉṉum kaḷiṟum,
valam _ēntu _iraṇṭucuṭarum, vāṉ kayilāyamaliyum,
nalam _ār keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*6
karantaṉa koḷḷiviḷakkum, kaṟaṅku tuṭiyiṉ muḻakkum,
paranta patiṉeṇkaṇamum, payiṉṟu_aṟiyātaṉa pāṭṭum,
_araṅku_iṭi nūl _aṟivāḷar _aṟiyappaṭātatu _or kūttum,
niranta keṭalappuṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*7
koli varivēṅki _ataḷum, kulavōṭu _ilaṅku poṉ-tōṭum,
vili peṟu caṅkakkuḻiyum, vili _il kapālakkalaṉum,
malimakaḷ kikkoṇṭa mārpum, maṇi _ārntu _ilaṅku miṭaṟum,
_ulavu keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*8
_āṭal purinta niliyum, _ariyil _acitta _aravum,
pāṭal payiṉṟa palpūtam, pal_āyiram koḷ karuvi
nāṭaṟku _ariyatu _or kūttum, naṉku _uyar vīraṭṭam cūḻntu
_ōṭum keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*9
cūḻum _aravattukilum, tukil kiḻi kōvaṇakkīḷum,
yāḻiṉ moḻiyavaḷ _añca _añcātu _aru vari pōṉṟa
vēḻam _uritta niliyum, viri poḻil vīraṭṭam cūḻntu
tāḻum keṭilappuṉalum, _uṭiyār_oruvartamar, nām!---
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

*10
narampu _eḻu kikaḷ piṭittu, naṅki naṭuṅka, maliyi
_uraṅkaḷ_ellām koṇṭu _eṭuttāṉ _oṉpatum_oṉṟum _alaṟa,
varaṅkaḷ koṭuttu _aruḷceyvāṉ, vaḷar poḻil vīraṭṭam cūḻntu
_añcuvatu yātoṉṟum _illi; _añca varuvatum _illi.

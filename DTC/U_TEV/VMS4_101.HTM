alampu alampāvaru taṇpuṉal ārūr avircaṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ who has a glittering caṭi and who is in Ārūr which has cool water that makes a sound and washes away sins.
cilampu alampāvaru cēvaṭiyāṉ
who has red feet on which the anklet is making a jingling sound.
tirumūlaṭṭāṉam pulam pal ampāvarum toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming slaves to the devotees of Civaṉ in tirumūlaṭṭaṉam to which they come bathing in the flood of tears as they weep.
kulam palam pāvarum kuṇṭar muṉṉē namakku (um) uṇṭukol
is it avilable to us also in the presence of low people, amaṇar, who live in groups and possess excessive strength
[[Jains lived in groups; the illustration quoted by Mayilinātar, himself a jin, amaṇkuṭi, for kuḻūuppeyar (Naṉṉūl, 210) will explin this.]]
{4:101}__2+
{$}
viltiṭam vāṅki vicayaṉoṭu aṉṟu vēṭuvaṉāy
assuming the form of a hunter long ago, Civaṉ who bent the bow with his strength.
puṟṟu iṭam koṇṭāṉ toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming the slave of devotees of Civaṉ who had as his abode the anthill.
maṟṟu iṭam iṉṟi
Having no other place.
maṉaituṟantu
leaving the house.
aluṇāval amaṇar coltiṭam eṉṟu turicu paṭṭēṉukkum uṇṭu kol
is it avilable to me who was at fault taking the words of the strong amanar who do not have their food at night, to be truth.
[[vāṅki is a noun;
maṉaituṟantu al uṇāval amaṇ kuṇṭar mayakkam nīkki, eṉai niṉaintu āṭkoṇṭāykku (nāvukkarcar, tirunākikkārōṇam, tiruviruttam, 6).
valci - pakaṟcōṟu (civakacintāmaṇi, 591, nacciṉarkkiṉiyar.)]]

ceruvaṭi veñcilaiyālpuram aṭṭavaṉ
Civaṉ who destroyed the cities by the cruel bow which was in use in warfare (veñcilai transfered epithet)
ceṉṟu aṭaiyāta tiruuṭaiyāṉ
who has wealth which does not reach him by going to him. (He is naturally rich).

tiruvārūr tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ
who is in mūlaṭṭāṉam in tiruvārūr.
ceṅkaṇ poruviṭaiyāṉ aṭit toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming the slaves of the devotees to the feet of god who has a fighting bull with red eyes.
oruvaṭivu iṉṟi
without the slightest beauty (ugly looking is the sense)
niṉṟu uṇ kuṇṭarmuṉ namakku (um) uṇkol
is it avilable to us also in the presence of the low amaṇar who eat their food standing!

tēcaṉai ārūrttirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉaic cintai ceytu
fixing thier thoughts on Civaṉ in tirumūlaṭṭāṉam in ārūr who has a dazzling complexion.
pūcaṉaip pūcurar toṇṭarkkut toṇṭarām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming the slaves of the devotees of the brahmins who worship by performing pūcaṉi to him.
māciṉai ēṟiya mēṉiyar vaṉ kaṇṇar moṇṇaraiviṭṭu
leaving the company of the bald-headed amaṇar, who are heartless people and who have bodies in which dirt has accumulated. (amaṇar are called moṇṇar as they pluck the hirs on their heads and are bald-headed).
īcaṉaiyē niṉaintu ēcaṟuvēṉukkum uṇṭukol
is it avilable to me also who is longing for Civaṉ thinking of him to the exclusion of other gods?
{4:101}__5+
{$}
tiruntiya māmatil ārurt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉukkup poruntum tavam uṭait toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming slaves to the devotees who have the good fortune to be so, to tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ is ārūr which has a big and perfect wall of fortification.
aruntum poḻutu uraiyāṭā amaṇar tiṟam akaṉṟu
leaving the religious doctrines of amaṇar who do not talk when they have their food.
varunti niṉaintu
repenting and remembering god
araṉē eṉṟu vāḻttu vēṟku (um) uṇṭu kol
is it avilable to me say words of benedication, araṉ
{4:101}__6+
{$}
tēṉ kamaḻ cōlait teṉ ārūrt tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ
Civaṉ in tirumūlaṭṭaṉam in ārūr in the south which has gardens where honey spreads.
ceyya pūṅkaḻalāṉ aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming the slaves of the devotees to the feet of Civaṉ who wears a beautiful red kaḻal.
vīṅkiya tōḷkaḷum tāḷ māyniṉṟu veṟṟaraiyē mūṅkaikaḷ pōl uṇṇum mūṭar muṉṉē namakku uṇṭu kol
is it avilable to us in the presence of amaṇar who have their food without talking like dumb persons being naked, and standing with shoulders and legs of big size.
{4:101}__7+
{$}
tiṇṇiya māmatil ārūttirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ, eṅkaḷ puṇṇiyaṉ taṉ aṭit toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming slaves to the devotees to the feet of Civaṉ who is the embodiment of all virtues and who dwells in tirumūlaṭṭāṉam in ārūr which has big and strong fortified walls.
paṇṇiya cāttirap pēykaḷ
camanar, who are like pēy and who fabricate religious books.
paṟitalaikkuṇṭarai viṭṭu
leaving the association of the low people who pluck the hirs on their heads.
eṇ ilpukaḻ īcaṉtaṉ aruḷ peṟṟēṟkum uṇṭu kol
is it avilable to me who received the grace of Civaṉ who has innumerable famous acts to his credit?

tiruppoli ārūrttirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉ tirukkayilaip poruppaṉ viruppu amar toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming slaves to the devotees who bestow their love on Civaṉ who dwells in the mountin, kayili and who is near at hand in mūlaṭṭaṉam in ārūr where wealth flourishes.
[[poruppaṉ viruppu - poruppaṉ mēlvaitta viruppu(kōviyār, 140, pēraciriyar);
poruppar viruppu - porupparmēl yāṉ vaitta viruppam (Do.208, do. )
meyyaikkarapparkaḷ
amaṇar will cover their bodies with mats.
talaipaṟikkac cukam eṉṉum kuṇṭar uraippaṉa kēḷātu
without heeding the words of the low amaṇar who say it is a pleasure to pluck the hirs on the head.
iṅku uyyap pōntēṉukkum uṇṭukol
is it avilable to me also who left them and embraced civam to save myself in this world?

aiyaṉ aṇivayal ārūrttirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉukkup poy aṉpu ilā aṭittoṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming slaves to the devotees to the feet of god and who possess sincere love to the Lord in tirumūlaṭṭaṉam in ārūr which has beautiful fields and who is our father.
kaiyil iṭu cōṟu niṉṟu uṇṇum kātal amaṇarai viṭṭu uyyum neṟikaṇṭu iṅku uyyap pōntēṉukkum uṇṭukol
is it avilable to me also who came back to save myself to civam knowing the way to redeem myself after leaving amaṇar who are partial towards eating in the standing posture the food that is served in the hand.

malpoli tōḷāṉ irāvaṇaṉ taṉ valivāṭṭu vittapoṟ kaḻalāṉ aṭit toṇṭarkkut toṇṭar ām puṇṇiyam
the religious merit of becoming slaves to the devotees to the feet of Civaṉ who wears golden kaḻal and who had by his toe, destroyed the strength of īrāvaṇaṉ whose shoulders were eminent by wrestling.
kuṟṟam uṭai amaṇar tiṟam (atu) kaiyakaṉṟiṭṭu
having separated myself from the faulty doctrines of amaṇar.
uṟṟa karumam ceytu uyyap pōntēṉukkum uṇṭu kol
is it avilable to me also who came leaving them to save myself by performing useful actions?

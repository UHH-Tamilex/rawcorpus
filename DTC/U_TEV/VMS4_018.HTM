{4:18}__1+
{$} avar cintai uyar varai oṉṟu (kol, ām)
the high mountin that rises high like his mind is Kayili
uyarum mati oṉṟu (kol, ām) cūṭuvar
he wears a crescent that is increasing in greatness;
[[PP: karumuṭivaittaru kālakaṟpam elām kaṭakkavum niṉ, tirumuṭiyil piṟaiciṟitum tēyātu, vaḷarātē
(aruṇikkalampakam, 1)]]
oṉṟu (kol, ām) iṭu veṇtalai kaiyatu
has in his hand a white skull in which alms is placed.
avar ūrvatu (tāṉ, ē) oṉṟu (kol, ām)
the vehicle on which he rides is a bull.
{4:18}__2+
{$} imaiyōr toḻu pātam iraṇṭu (kol, ām)
the feet which the celestials who do not wink, worship with joined hands, are two.
iraṇṭu (kol, ām) ilaṅkum kuḻai
the two ornaments worn in the ears are a men's ear-ring and a womans' ear-ring.
peṇ āṇ iraṇṭu (kol, ām) uruvam
male and female are his two forms.
ciṟu māṉ maḻu iraṇṭu (kol, ām) avar (1) eytiṉa, (tām, ē)
a young deer and a battle-axe are the two things that he obtined (it would be better reading if it were ēntiṉatāmē)
[[Variant reading: (1) eytiṉartāmē]]
{4:18}__3+
{$} mūṉṟu (kol, ām) avarkaṇṇutal āvaṉa
his eyes including the third eye on the forehead are three (mūṉṟu is to be taken as an ordinal and not as a cardinal number).
mūṉṟu (kol ām) avar cūlattiṉ moyilai
the strong blades of his trident are three in number.
mūṉṟu (kol, ām) kaiyatu kaṇai, vil nāṇ
the three things arrow, bow and bowstring are in his hands. (kaiyatu should be separately added to kaṇai, vil, nāṇ the subjects are three)
mūṉṟu (kol, ām) puram eytiṉa (tām, ē)
the cities on which he discharged an arrow are three.
{4:18}__4+
{$} nālu (kol, ām) avartam mukam āvaṉa
his faces are four in number
[[āti nāṉmukattu aṇṭavāṇa (civaperumaṉ tirumumaṇikkōvi, 28: 11th book)]]
nālu (kol, ām) caṉaṉam mutal tōṟṟamum
the coming into existence of beings that are born are four in number they are (1) viviperous animals (2) Lives that sprout or germinate from seeds, roots, twigs etc., plant life (3) nou viviparous animals, like lines (4) sweat-born lives, like lice.
nālu (kol, ām) avar ūrtiyiṉ pātaṅkaḷ
the feet of his vehicle, bull, are four in number.
nālu(kol, ām) maṟai pāṭiṉa (tām, ē)
the vetams, that he chanted are four.
{4:18}__5+
{$} añcu (kol, ām) avar āṭu araviṉ paṭam
the hoods of the dancing cobra which he wears are five.
añcu (kol, ām) avar velpulaṉ āvaṉa
the senses of the body which he has conquered are five.
añcu (kol, ām) avar kāyappaṭṭāṉkaṇai
the arrows of the cupid who was burnt by the fire issuing from the frontal eye are five.
añcu (kol, ām) avar āṭiṉa (tām, ē)
the five products of the cow are the five in which he bathed;
[[āviṉukku aruṅkalam araṉ añcu āṭutar (nāvukkaracar, namaccivāyattiruppatikam, 2)]]
{4:18}__6+
{$} āṟu(kol, ām) avar aṅkam paṭaittaṉa
he is the author of the six aṅkams of the vētams,
āṟu (kol, ām) avar tārmicai vaṇṭiṉ kāl
the legs of the bees that stay on his garland are six.
āṟu (kol, ām) cuvai ākkiṉa (tām, ē)
created six flavours.
{4:18}__7+
{$} ēḻu (kol, ām) avar ūḻi paṭaittaṉa
he created the seven aeons;
[[PP: ūḻi ēḻāṉa oruvā pōṟṟi
(Nāvukkaracar śrīkayilāyam, pōṟṟittiruttāṇṭakam (1) 8]]
ēḻu (kol, ām) avar kaṇṭa iruṅkaṭal
the big oceans he created are seven oceans; (they are: saltish water honey, sugar-cane juice, curd, ghee, milk and pure water);
[[upputtēṉ ikku veṇ tayir neypāl, appukkaṭal ēḻ ākum eṉpa (pinkalanti, 585)]]
ēḻu (kol, ām) avar āḷum ulakaṅkaḷ
seven are the worlds over which he rules; the seven upper world are pōo (2) puvam,(3) cuvarkkam, (4) māha, (5) tavam, (6) piramam (7) civalōkam the seven under worlds are (1) atalam (2) vītalam (3) cutalam (4) nitalam (5) tarātalam (6) racātalam, (7) pātālam.
ēḻu (kol, ām) icai ākkiṉa (tām, ē)
[[the seven notes of the diatonic scale were created by him; they are
  • (1) Kural
  • (2) tuttam,
  • (3) Kaikkiḷi,
  • (4) uḻi,
  • (5) iḷi
  • (6) viḷari
  • and (7) tāram
according to tamiḻ.]]
{4:18}__8+
{$} eṭṭuk (kol, ām) avar īṟuil peruṅkuṇam
his great attributes which have no end are eight in number.
[[kōḷ il poṟiyiṉ kuṇam ilavē eṇ kuṇattāṉ, tāḷai vaṇaṅkāttalai (Kuṟaḷ, 9) parimēlaḻakar gives the eight attributes : (1) taṉvayattaṉātal (2) tūya uṭampiṉaṉātal (3) iyaṟkai uṇarviṉaṉātal (4) muṟṟum uṇartal (5) iyalpākavē pācaṅkaḷiṉ nīṅkutal (6) pēraruḷ uṭaimai (7) muṭivil āṟṟaluṭaimai (8) varampil iṉpamuṭaimai; ivvāṟu caivākamattuk kūṟappaṭṭatu). eṭṭuk (kol, ām) avar cūṭum iṉamalar; the group of flowers which Civaṉ wears are eight; they are (1) puṉṉi (2) white yarcum (3) champak (4) nantiyavarttam (5) pātiri (6) kuvaḷi (7) alari (8) red lotus.]]
eṭṭuk (kol, ām) avar tōḷ iṇai āvaṉa
his shoulders are eight in number;
[[eṇ tōḷ vīci niṉṟu āṭum pirāṉtaṉai (nāvukkaracar, tiruvankamāli, 2)]]
eṭṭuk (kol, ām) ticai ākkiṉa (tāmē)
the eight directions were created by them.
{4:18}__9+
{$} oṉpatu(pōl) avar vācal vakuttaṉa
nine apertures were created by him;
[[oṇṇuḷē oṉpatu vācal vaittāl (nāvukkaracar, pukalūr, (5) 1)]]
oṉpatu (pōl) avar mārpiṉiṉ nūl iḻai
the twisted threads in the sacred thread worn on the chest are nine.
oṉpatu(pōl) avar kōlak kuḻaṟcaṭai
his caṭi which are curely are nine in number.
oṉpatu(pōl) avar pār iṭam (tāṉ, ē)
the portion of the worlds are nine in number; they are called navakaṇtan, their names are: (1) Kīḻpāḷ vitēkam (2) mēlpālvitēkam, (3) veṭapāl vitēkam, (4) tenpāl vitēkam, (5) vāṭapal īrepatam (6) teṉpāl īrepatam (7) vaṭapal paratam (8) teṉpāl paratam and (9) mattima Kāṇṭam.
{4:18}__10+
{$} pattuk (kol, ām) avar pāmpiṉkaṇ, pāmpiṉ pal
the cobra that he wears has ten eyes and ten fangs: the five fangs in the upper row and five fangs in the lower row of the mouth.
pattuk (kol, ām) eyiṟum nerittu ukkaṉa
the teeth of Irāvaṇaṉ which were broken and fell down were ten in number.
pattuk (kol, ām) avar kāyappaṭṭāṉ talai.
The heads of Irāvaṇaṉ punished by him were ten.
pattuk (kol, ām) aṭiyār ceykai (tāṉ, ē)
[[The acts of devotees are ten in number:
  • (1) ciṉṉam tarittal (wearing emblems of Caiva religion, as uruttirākkam beads and sacred ashes)
  • (2) kuruvai vaḻipaṭal (worship of the preceptor as god himself)
  • (3) tōttiram ceytal (prising god)
  • (4) nāmauccāraṇam (reciting the names of god)
  • (5) tarumācaraṇai (right conduct)
  • (6) purāṇam kēṭṭal (listening to the exposition of purāṇam-s
  • (7) ālaya taricaṉam visiting temples
  • (8) aṭiyār vīṭṭil allāta piṟa iṭattu uṇṇāmai (Not eating in any other house except in devotees houses)
  • (9) aṭiyārkku ītal (giving to devotees)
  • (10) pūcittal: performing of pūci]]

  • (3) tattuvacutti

  • (5) āṉmataricanam
  • (6) āṉmacutti
  • (7) civarupam
  • (8) civataricaṉam

  • (10) civapōkam;]]

{3:62}__1+
{$} kaṇ poli neṟṟiyiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who has a forehead in which the eye is shining.
tikaḻ kaiyil ōr veṇ maḻuvāṉ
has in his shining hand a white-hot iron.
peṇ puṇar kūṟu uṭaiyāṉ
has a half in which a lady is united with him
mikupīṭu uṭai māl viṭaiyāṉ
has a big bull which has great eminence
viṇ poli māmaticērtaru ceñcaṭaivētiyaṉūr
the place of the vētiyaṉ of red caṭi on which there is a crescent which shines in the sky.
[[ceñcaṭaivētiyaṉ: the name of Civaṉ in this temple is aruṇacaṭēcar]]
taṇpoḻil cūḻ paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccaramē
is hold tāṭaki īccaram in paṉantāl surrounded by cool gardens.
[[tāṭakai īccaram this temple got this name becuse one lady by name tāṭaki (not the tāṭaki mentioned in the Rāmāyaṇam) worshipped Civaṉ here]]
{3:62}__2+
{$} nāṉ maṟaiyai virittavaṉ
Civaṉ expounded the meaning of the four vētams
mikka viṇṇavar vantu iṟaiñca
as the celestials worshipped in large numbers.
muppuraṅkaḷ erittavaṉ
burnt the three cities
iyal ēḻ ulakil uyirum pirittavaṉ
difentiated the living being in the seven worlds that are in existence, having come down as a preceptor
ceñcaṭai mēl niṟai pēr oli veḷḷam taṉṉait tarittavaṉ ūr paṉantāḷ tāṭakai īccaramē
the holy tāṭaki iccaram in paṉantāḷ is the place of the god who bore the floods coming with great noise which filled everywhere, on his red-coloured cati.
{3:62}__3+
{$} māṉ uritōl uṭuttavaṉ
Civaṉ dressed himself in the skin of a deer flayed
[[The deer's skin also serves as dress to Civaṉ]]
[[PP: uḻai uritta māṉ uritōl āṭaiyāṉē (appar, tiruvāvaṭutuṟi 7)
puḷḷi uḻai māṉ tōl oṉṟu uṭuttu (Kārikkālammiyār, patikam (2)5;]]
kaḻal uḷka vallār viṉaikaḷ keṭuttu aruḷ ceyya vallāṉ
can destroy the Karmams of those who can meditation his feet, and grant his grace
kiḷar kītam ōr nāṉ maṟaiyāṉ
has four vētams in addition to musical compositions which are sung in a high pitch
nañcu amutā maṭuttavaṉ
swallowed the poison as nectar
mikka mātavar vēḷviyai muṉ taṭuttavaṉ ūr paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccaramē
the holy tāṭaki iccaram in Paṉantāḷ is the place of that God who, in the distant past, averted the evils effects of the sacrifice performed by the great sages in tārukāvaṉam
{3:62}__4+
{$} cūḻtaru valviṉaiyum uṭal tōṉṟiya palpiṇiyum pāḻpaṭa vēṇṭutirēl
(People of this world!) If you wish that the irresistible Karmams surrounding you and diseases that appeared simultaneously with your bodies, to be ruined.
pāy puṉalum pōḻila veṇmatiyum aṉal poṅku aravum puṉainta tāḻcaṭaiyāṉ paṉantāḷ tāṭakai īccaramē mikaēttumiṉ
prise often tāṭaki īccaram in paṉantāḷ of Civaṉ who adorns on his hanging caṭi, spreading water (of Kaṅki), a young crescent and a cobra of increasing poison
{3:62}__5+
{$} viṭam paṭu kaṇṭattiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who has poison in his neck.
iruḷ veḷvaḷai maṅkaiyoṭum naṭampurikoḷkaiyiṉāṉ
likes the idea of dancing at the end of the world when everything is enveloped in darkness, to be witnessed by a lady wearing white bangles.
avaṉ em iṟaicērum iṭam
the place where such a great chief as our god dwells
paṭam puri nākamoṭu tirai paṉ maṇiyum koṇarum taṭam puṉal cūḻ paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccaramē
is the holy tāṭaki iccaram in paṉantāḷ surrounded by the famous water of the Kāviri whose waves bring with them the jewels in the hoods of cobras and many other precious stones.
{3:62}__6+
{$} viṭai uyar vel koṭiyāṉ
Civaṉ who has an eminent and victorious flag on which the form of a bull is drawn
viṇṇoṭu maṇṇum ellām aṭipuṭaipaṭa āṭa vallāṉ
is able to dance to make the heaven, this earth and other worlds be beaten by his feet.
mikupūtam ārpal paṭaiyāṉ
has many armies of pūtam of enormous strength.
toṭai navil koṉṟaiyoṭu vaṉṉi tuṉ eṭukkum aṇinta caṭaiyavaṉ ūr paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccaramē
holy tāṭaki īccaram in paṉantāḷ is the place of Civaṉ who adorns on his caṭi closely flowers of yārcum, vaṉṉi leaves, and koṉṟi flowers made into garlands.
{3:62}__7+
{$} malaiyavaṉ muṉpayanta maṭamātai ōr kūṟuṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has as a half a simple lady who is the dughter of the mountin
cilaimali veṅkaṇaiyāl puram mūṉṟu avai ceṟṟu ukantāṉ
was pleased to destroy the three cities with a cruel arrow fired in bow
alaimali taṇ puṉalum, mati, āṭu aravum aṇinta talaiyavaṉ ūr paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccaramē
holy tāṭaki iccaram in paṉantāḷ is the place of that chief who adorned his head with water full of waves, a crescent, and a dancing cobra.
{3:62}__8+
{$} tiru melviralāl aṭarttu arakkaṉ valiyaic ceṟṟu
pressing down with the holy soft toe and destroying the strength of arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ)
muṟṟum veṇṇīṟu aṇinta tirumēṉiyaṉ
has besmeared the whole of his body with white holy ash
mummaiyiṉāṉ
has a form, has no form, and has a form and at the same formless
[[These are called uru, aru, aruvuru]]
puṟṟu aravum puliyiṉ uri tōloṭu kōvaṇamum taṟṟavaṉ ūr paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccaramē
holy tāṭaki iccaram in paṉantāḷ is the place for him who wore tightly a cobra living in the anthill, a skin flayed from the tiger, a loin-cloth and a cloth.
[[Cloth is covered by the um in kōvaṇamum]]
{3:62}__9+
{$} vil malai nāṇ aravam miku veṅkaṉal ampu ataṉāl puṉmai cey tāṉavartam puram mūṉṟum poṉṟuvittāṉ
the pure Civaṉ had the three cities of the acurar who committed mean acts, destroyed by the arrow which is the god of fire of great heat, and which was fixed in the bow-string which was a serpent, and converting the mountin into a bow.
naṉ malarmēl ayaṉum naṇṇum nāraṇaṉum aṟiyāt taṉmaiyaṉ ūr paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccaramē
the holy tāṭaki iccaram in Paṉantal is the place of the god whose nature could not be known by Nāraṇaṉ who is by his side, and Ayaṉ who is seated on a fertile lotus flower.
{3:62}__10+
{$} ātar camaṇaroṭum aṭai aintukil pōrttuḻalum nītaruraikkum moḻiyavai koḷḷaṉmiṉ
(People of this world) Do not accept as truth the words that are spoken by the low buddhists who cover their bodies with the beautiful robes soaked in myrtle dye, and the words of camaṇar who are ignorant. (nītar nīcar) niṉmalar ūr
the place of the sportless god.
pōtu aviḻum poykaitaṉuḷ tikaḻ puḷ iriyap poḻil vāyttātu aviḻum paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccaramē
tāṭaki iccaram in paṉantaḷ where in the gardens the pollen in the flowers shed as the petals loosen to make the beautiful birds in the natural tanks in which buds blossom into flowers, to fly in a disorderly manner (visit that place and save yourselves)
{3:62}__11+
{$} taṇvayalcūḻ paṉantāḷ tiruttāṭakai īccarattuk kaṇ ayalē piṟaiyāṉ avaṉ taṉṉai
on Civaṉ who has the crescent by the side of the frontal eye and who is in the holy tāṭaki īccaram in paṉanṭāl surrounded on all sides by cool fields.
muṉkāḻiyar kōṉ naṇṇiyacentamiḻāṉ mikuñāṉacampantaṉ nalla paṇiyal pāṭalvallār avartam viṉaipaṟṟu aṟum
the Karmams of those who are able to recite the verses that are set to music, and can confer benefit on them, and which were composed by the foremost eminent person of Kāḻi, ñāṉacampantaṉ of great knowledge of chaste Tamiḻ, will leave their hold on them.

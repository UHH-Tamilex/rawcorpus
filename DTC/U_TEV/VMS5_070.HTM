{5:70}__1+
{$}
kāḷaiyar taṅkaḷ pāl maṇṭi
being united with youths
[[PP_: kāḷai
patiṉāṟu pirāyattāṉ;
takkayākapparaṇi, 5, old commentary.]]
kaṇṭapēcciṉil ēccuṇum mātaraic cērātē
without joining the women who indulge in abuse according to their fancy and pocketing their insults.
caṇṭiyīccuvararkku aruḷ ceyta akkoṇṭiyīccuvaraṉ kaḻal kūṟumē
(People of this world!) speak about the greatness of the feet of that famous Koṇṭiyīccuravaṉ who bestowed his grace on Caṇṭiyīccuravaṉ.
{5:70}__2+
{$}
cuṟṟamum tuṇai nal maṭavāḷoṭu peṟṟa makkaḷum pēṇal oḻintaṉar
the relations, the good-natured lady who is the life-partner and children that were born, have given up loving me.
kuṟṟam il pukaḻk koṇṭīc curavaṉār paṟṟalāl maṟṟu oru paṟṟu illaiyē
there is no other support except the support of Koṇṭiiccuravaṉar, of spotless fame.
{5:70}__3+
{$}
māṭu(tāṉ) atu il eṉiṉ mānuṭar pāṭu tāṉ celvārillai
if one has no wealth people will not go near him.
pal mālaiyāl kūṭa nīr ceṉṟu koṇṭīccuravaṉaip pāṭumiṉ paralōkattu iruttum
(People of this world!) you sing about the fame of Koṇṭīccuravaṉ, approaching him with many garlands; he will fix you permanently in the world of liberated souls.
{5:70}__4+
{$}
tantai tāyoṭu tāram eṉum taḷaip pantam āṅku aṟuttu
cutting the bondages of father, mother and wife which bind you.
payilvu eytiya
in order to attin eternal life.
kontu aviḻ poḻil koṇṭīccuravaṉaic cintai 1. ceymiṉkaḷ cēvaṭicēra
to reach the lotus-red feet of the Lord in Koṇṭiccuram which has gardens where bunches of flowers unfold their petals, meditate on him. (People of this world!)
[[Variant reading: 1. ceymiṉavaṉaṭiccēravē, ceymmiṉavaṉaṭicēravē]]
{5:70}__5+
{$}
kēḷumiṉ
(People of this world!) listen to me.
iḷamai(yya) atu kēṭu vantu
having lost the period of youth.
īḷaiyōṭu irumal(l) atu eytaṉ muṉ
before you get cough and phlegm (the letters within brackets are augmentation for metre)
kōḷ 1. aravu aṇi koṇṭīccuravaṉai nāḷum ēttit toḻumiṉ
worship with folded hands prising Koṇṭiccuravaṉ who adorns himself with cobra which has the nature of killing.
naṉku ākumē
it will do you immense good.
[[Variant reading: 1. arā]]
{5:70}__6+
{$}
vempu nōyum iṭarum veṟumaiyum tuṉpamum tuyarum(m) eṉum cūḻviṉai
the fruits of the Karmams that encircle us, ie; the diseases which make the body hot, mental sufferings, poverty, physical sufferings and the sufferings that one has to undergo by being born.
kompaṉār payil koṇṭīccuravaṉai empirāṉ eṉa vallavarkku illaiyē
will not be with those who are able to say our Lord in Koṇtīccuram where ladies who are like the tender supple twigs of a plant are in large numbers.
{5:70}__7+
{$}
allalōṭu aru nōyil 1. aḻunti nīr cellumā niṉaiyātē
without thinking about the right path, having sunken in sufferings and incurable diseases. (People of this world!)
kaṉai kural kollai ēṟuṭaik koṇṭīccuravaṉai vallavāṟu toḻa viṉaimāyumē
all your Karmams will vanish if you worship Koṇṭiccuravaṉ who has a bellowing bull which lives in the forest tract, according to your ability.
[[Variant reading: 1. aḻuntu nīr]]
{5:70}__8+
{$}
nāṟu cāntu aṇi nal mulai meṉ moḻi māṟu ilā malai maṅkai ōr pākamāk kūṟaṉār uṟai koṇṭīccaram niṉaintu
thinking of Koṇṭiccuram where Civaṉ who has as a half the dughter of the mountin to whom none can be compared, who has beautiful breasts on which is smeared fragrant sandal-paste.
ūṟuvār tamakku ūṉam oṉṟu illaiyē
for those who increase in their piety there will not be the slightest defect.
{5:70}__9+
{$}
ayil ār ampu eri, mēruvil ākavē
using the fire as the sharp-pointed arrow and the mountin, mēru as the bow.
eyilārum poṭiyāy viḻa eytavaṉ
Civaṉ shot an arrow to reduce to ashes all those who lived in the three forts.
kuyil ārum poḻil koṇṭīccuravaṉaip payil vārum perumai peṟum pālarē
those who worship Civaṉ in Koṇṭiccuram which has gardens which has cuckoos in abundance, are by nature great people.
{5:70}__10+
{$}
nilaiyiṉ ār varai niṉṟu eṭuttāṉ taṉai malaiyiṉāl aṭarttu viṟal vāṭṭiṉāṉ, kulaiyiṉ ār poḻil koṇṭīccuravaṉait talaiyiṉāl vaṇaṅkat tavam ākumē
paying obeisance by lowering the head to the Lord in Koṇṭīccuram which has gardens having clusters of fruits, and who destroyed the erst - while victory of the arakkaṉ who lifted the mountin, firmly fixed, with that mountin itself, is equal to penance

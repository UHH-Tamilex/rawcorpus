(regarding the designation paḻiciyuḷ māṭa maḻapāṭi uṟi paṭṭiccuram)

[[maḻapāṭi is quite different from the shrine of that name in Tiruccirāppaḷḷi district on the left bank of the river Koḷḷiṭam]]

[[paḻaicai is a corrupt form of the word paḻaiyāṟai (paḻiyāṟi) sanctioned by usage]]

[[Cēkkiḻār describes this town as follows:
cīriṉ nīṭiya cempiyar poṉṉi nal nāṭṭuk, kāriṉ mēviya kaḷi aḷi malarp poḻil cūḻntu, tēriṉ mēviya ceḻumaṇi vītikaḷ ciṟantu, pāril nīṭiya perumaicēr pati paḻaiyāṟai
(amarnīti nāyanār puranam, 1);]]

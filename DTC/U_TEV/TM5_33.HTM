*1
kolli _ēṟṟiṉar, kōḷ _aravattiṉar,
tillic ciṟṟampalattu _uṟi celvaṉār,
tolli_ūḻiyar, cōṟṟuttuṟiyarkkē
valli_āyp paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*2
mutti _āka _oru tavam ceytili;
_attiyāl _aṭiyārkku _oṉṟu _aḷittili;
tottu niṉṟu _alar cōṟṟuttuṟiyarkkē
patti_āyp paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*3
_oṭṭi niṉṟa _uṭal _uṟu nōyviṉi
kaṭṭi niṉṟa kaḻintu _avi pōy _aṟa,
toṭṭu niṉṟum _ac cōṟṟuttuṟiyarkkē
paṭṭi_āyp paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*4
_ātiyāṉ, _aṇṭavāṇarkku _aruḷ nalkum
nītiyāṉ _eṉṟum, niṉmalaṉē _eṉṟum,
cōtiyāṉ _eṉṟum, cōṟṟuttuṟiyarkkē
vāti_āyp paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*5
_āṭṭiṉāy, _aṭiyēṉ viṉi_āyiṉa
_ōṭṭiṉāy; _oru kātil _ilaṅku veṇ-
tōṭṭiṉāy _eṉṟu cōṟṟuttuṟiyarkkē
nīṭṭi nī paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*6
poṅki niṉṟu _eḻunt(k) kaṭalnañciṉip
paṅki _uṇṭatu _ōr teyvam _uṇṭō? colāy!
toṅki nī _eṉṟum cōṟṟuttuṟiyarkkut
taṅki nī paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*7
_āṇi pōla nī _āṟṟa valiyikāṇ;
_ēṇi pōl _iḻintu _ēṟiyum, _ēṅkiyum,
tōṇi _ākiya cōṟṟuttuṟiyarkkē
pūṇi_āyp paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*8
peṟṟam _ēṟil _eṉ? pēy paṭi _ākil _eṉ?
puṟṟil _āṭu _aravē_atu pūṇil _eṉ?
cuṟṟi nī, _eṉṟum cōṟṟuttuṟiyarkkē
paṟṟi, nī paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*9
_alliyāṉ, _aravu_aintalināka(_a)ṇip-

*10

_uṇṭu, nī paṇi cey, maṭa neñcamē!

*11

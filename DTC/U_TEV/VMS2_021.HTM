[[In this decade also there is no reference to Rāvaṇaṉ in the 8th verse]]

{2:21}__1+
{$} puṉal āṭiya puṉcaṭaiyāy
you have golden matted locks in which water is moving (puṉcaṭai - poṉcaṭai)
araṇam aṉalāka viḻittavaṉē
you opened your eye to make the forts to be consumed by fire.
[[This is a new thing that the three forts were destroyed by the fire of the third eye.]]
aḻaku ārkaṉal āṭaliṉāy
you dance in the beautiful fire (at the end of the world)
kaḻippālai uḷāy
you are in kaḻippali
uṉ vārkaḻal kaitoḻutu uḷkutum
we will meditate on your long feet by worshipping them with out hands
[[uṉa: a is the possessive case suffix denoting plural as it is followed by Kaḻal; Kaḻal is plural.]]
{2:21}__2+
{$} tuṇaiyāka ōr tūvaḷam mātiṉaiyum iṇaiyāka ukantavaṉē
you rejoiced in having a pure and wealthy lady as your consort so that it is difficult to say you are two
[[iṇaiyāka : to be equal to you]]
iṟaivā
one who pervades in all things
iṟai-taṅkutal kaṇaiyāl eyil eyta kaḻippālai uḷāy
god who are in Kaḻippāli and who discharged an arrow on the forts.
iṇaiyār kaḻal ētta iṭar keṭum
all the sufferings will vanish if we worship the two feet.
{2:21}__3+
{$} neṭiyāy
one who is tall!
kuṟiyāy
one who is short.
nimir puṉcaṭaiyiṉ muṭiyāy
one who has tied as a knot the golden matted locks which are erect.
cuṭuveṇpoṭi muṟṟa aṇivāy
you smear the well-burnt white ash on the whole of your form.
kaṭi ār poḻil cūḻ kaḻippālai uḷāy
you are in Kaḻippāli surrounded by fragrant gardens.
aṭiyārkku avalammavai aṭaiyā
sufferings will not approach your devotees.
{2:21}__4+
{$} eḷiyāy
you are easy to approach for your devotees.
ariyāy
(at the same time) you are difficult to be approached by others.
nilam, nīroṭu, tī, vaḷi, kāyam eṉa veḷimaṉṉiya tū oḷiyāy
you who are the earth, water, fire, air and sky; and the spotless light which is permanent, and well-known to everybody.
uṉaiyē toḻutu uṉṉumavarkku aḷiyāy
you have compassion on those who worship, and meditate on, you only.
kaḻippālai amarntavaṉē
you who are residing in Kaḻippāli out of your will.
{2:21}__5+
{$} naṭa naṇṇi ōr nākam acaittavaṉē
you tied a serpent, performing dance.
viṭam naṇṇiya tūmiṭaṟā
you have a neck in which there is poison and which is the support of tēvar.
vikirtā
God who is different from the world.
kaṭal naṇṇu kaḻippati kāvalaṉē
the ruler of Kaḻippāli which is very near the sea.
uṭal naṇṇi uṉ aṭiyē vaṇaṅkuvaṉ
I will adore your feet only, with my body (falling on the earth)
{2:21}__6+
{$} piṟai ārcaṭaiyāy
you have matted locks on which there is a crescent.
periyāy
Oh great one!
periya maṟai ārtaru vāymaiyiṉāy
you are the true meaning mentioned in the great vētams.
periyam maṟai;
[[m is doubled for metre.]]
ulakil kaṟaiyār poḻilcūḻ kaḻippālai uḷāy
you are in Kaḻippali in the world which is surrounded by dark gardens
[[As the trees are dense the sunlight cannot pierce; so the gardens are dark even at daytime.]]
iṟai ārkaḻal ētta iṭam keṭum
all distresses will perish of their own accord, to worship the feet which are pervading (all things and places)
{2:21}__7+
{$} mutirum caṭaiyiṉ muṭimēl viḷaṅkum katir veṇ piṟaiyāy
you have a shining crescent of spreading rays, on the knot of the matted locks which are old.
kaḻippālai uḷāy
you are in Kalippāli!
etirkoḷ moḻiyāl irantu ēttumavarkku
for those who prise you in the second person begging for boons.
atirum viṉaiyāyiṉa ācaṟumē
All the actions which produce mental shock will perish quickly (ācu- quicky)
{2:21}__8+
{$} eriya ārkaṉaiyāl eyil eytavaṉē
you shot arrow tipped with fire, on the forts.
viri ārtaruvīḻcaṭaiyāy
you have matted locks falling and spreading, on the nape.
iravil karikāṭaliṉāy
you dance in the night in the cremationground.
kaḻippālai uḷāy
you are in Kaḻippāli
uritu āki uṉ aṭiyē vaṇaṅkuvaṉ
I shall adore your feet only, myself having become your rightful property.
{2:21}__9+
{$} nalam nāraṇaṉ nāṉmukaṉ naṇṇal uṟak kaṉalāṉavaṉē
you assumed the form of a pillar of fire when beautiful nāraṇaṉ (nārāyaṇaṉ) and the four-faced one tried to search your head and feet.
kaḻippālai uḷāy
you are in Kaḻippāli
eyil eytavaṉē
one who destroyed the forts with an arrow.
uṉavār kaḻalē toḻutu uṉṉumavarkku viṉai ilatām
sins will disappear for those who adore, and meditate on, your long feet only.
[[uṉa: see notes on Kalippāli, Ñāṉacampantar 1-1)
{2:21}__10+
{$} tavar koṇṭa toḻiṟ camaṇ vēṭaroṭum
along with those caman who have an exterior appearance like people who performed sincere penance, and took upon themselves the action of people doing penance.
tuvar koṇṭaṉa nuṇtukil āṭaiyarum (ākiya) avar
those who done themselves with a fine cloth soaked in yellow ochre.
koṇṭaṉaviṭṭu
leaving aside their doctrines.
aṭikaḷ uṟaiyum uvar koṇṭa kaḻippati uḷkutum
we shall think of the supreme Lord residing in Kaḻippāli having saltish water nearby.
{2:21}__11+
{$} pukalip paḻiyā maṟai ñāṉacampantaṉ col vaḻipāṭu ivai koṇṭu
with the words of Ñāṉacampantaṉ who has a knowledge of maṟi without reproach and who is a native of pukali (sīrkāḻi), which can be used for worship
[[pukali: one of the 12 names for Cīrkāḻi.]]
imaiyōroṭu keḻiyār
will join with the tēvar who do not wink.
kēṭu ilar
they will not know ruin.

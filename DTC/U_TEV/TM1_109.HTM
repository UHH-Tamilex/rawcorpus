• patikam: 1:109
  • talam: tiruc cirapuram (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: viyāḻak kuṟiñci
  • VMS: VOL.2, P.279

*1
vār _uṟu vaṉamuli maṅki paṅkaṉ,
nīr _uṟu caṭimuṭi nimalaṉ, _iṭam---
kār _uṟu kaṭipoḻil cūḻntu _aḻaku _ār
cīr _uṟu vaḷavayal cirapuramē.

*2
_aṅkamoṭu _arumaṟi _aruḷpurintāṉ,
tiṅkaḷoṭu _aravu _aṇi tikaḻ muṭiyaṉ,
maṅkiyoṭu _iṉitu _uṟi vaḷa nakaram---
ceṅkayal miḷir vayal, cirapuramē.

*3
parintavaṉ, pal muṭi_amararkku _ākit
tirintavar puram_avi tīyiṉ vēva
varinta veñcili piṭittu, _aṭucarattit
terintavaṉ, vaḷa nakar---cirapuramē.

*4
nīṟu _aṇi mēṉiyaṉ, nīḷ matiyoṭu
_āṟu _aṇi caṭiyiṉaṉ, _aṇiyiḻi _ōr-
kūṟu _aṇintu _iṉitu _uṟi kuḷir nakaram---
cēṟu _aṇi vaḷavayal, cirapuramē.

*5
_aruntiṟal _avuṇarkaḷ _araṇ _aḻiyac
caram turantu _ericeyta caṅkaraṉ _ūr---
kuruntoṭu koṭiviṭu mātavikaḷ
tiruntiya puṟavu _aṇi cirapuramē.

*6
kali _avaṉ, maṟi _avaṉ, kāṟṟoṭu tī
mali _avaṉ, viṇṇoṭu maṇṇum _avaṉ,
koliya vaṉ koṭimatil kūṭṭu_aḻitta
ciliyavaṉ, vaḷa nakar---cirapuramē.

*7
vāṉ _amar matiyoṭu mattam cūṭit
tāṉavar puram _eyta civaṉ _iṭam---
kāṉ _amar maṭamayilpeṭi payilum
tēṉ _amar poḻil _aṇi cirapuramē.

*8
maṟuttavar tiripuram māyntu _aḻiyak
kaṟuttavaṉ, kār_arakkaṉ muṭitōḷ
_iṟuttavaṉ, _iruñ ciṉak kālaṉi muṉ
ceṟuttavaṉ, vaḷa nakar---cirapuramē.

*9
vaṇṇa nalmalar _uṟi maṟiyavaṉum
kaṇṇaṉum kaḻal toḻa, kaṉal_uru_āy
viṇ _uṟa _ōṅkiya vimalaṉ _iṭam---
tiṇṇa nalmatil _aṇi cirapuramē.

*10
veṟṟu _ari _uḻalpavar, viri tukilār,
kaṟṟilar _aṟa_uri puṟaṉ_urikka,
paṟṟalar tiri purammūṉṟum vēvac
ceṟṟavaṉ vaḷa nakar---cirapuramē.

*11
_arumaṟi ñāṉacampantaṉ, _am taṇ
cirapuranakar _uṟi civaṉ _aṭiyip
paraviya centamiḻpattum vallār
tiruvoṭu pukaḻ malku tēciṉarē.

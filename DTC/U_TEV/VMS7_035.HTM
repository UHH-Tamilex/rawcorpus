neñcamē
my mind!
aṅkam ōtiyōr āṟai mēṟṟaḷi niṉṟum pōntu vantu iṉṉampart taṅkiṉōmaiyum iṉṉatu eṉṟilar īcaṉār
Civaṉ did not command us what is to be done by us who stayed for many days in Iṉṉampar coming there, having left Āṟi Mēṟṟaḷi, where brahmins who have studied the six aṅkam-s live.
kaṅkul ēmaṅkaḷ koṇṭu (1) tēvarkaḷ ētti vāṉavar tām toḻum, poṅkumālviṭai ēṟi celvap puṟampayam toḻap pōtum
we shall go to worship Puṟampayam which is the shrine of Civaṉ who rides on an angry, big bull, where people living in heaven in order to obtin divinity worship god at day-break waiting all through the night, so that they may rise still higher in their status.
[[Variant reading: (1) tēvarkaḷ ōti]]

maṭaneñcē
my ignorant mind!
kuṟṟu oruvvaraik kūṟaikoṇṭu kolaikaḷ cūḻnta kaḷavu elām
the sins which are the result of theft and planning murder, after snatching their clothes and stabbing them with a sharp weapon.
ceṟṟu oruvvaraic ceyta tīmaikaḷ imaimaiyē varum, tiṇṇamē
being inimical towards one who follows the right path and sins that come to us by committing cruel acts aginst them, in the previous births will bear their fruit in this birth; it is a certinty.
maṟṟu oruvaraip paṟṟilēṉ
I do not have any one else as my hold.
maṟavātu eḻu
get up without forgetting.
puṟṟu aravvuṭaip peṟṟa mēṟipuṟam payam toḻap pōtum
we shall go to worship puṟampayam which is the place of Civaṉ who rides on a bull and wears cobras living in anthills.
[[All the v in the word kuṟṟoruvvarai, ceṟṟoruvvarai, maṟṟoruvvarai, puṟṟaravvuṭai are augmentation for the sake of rhythm. There is also a reading without that letter.]]

rise with clarity in mind so that sins and cruel acts committed by you by deceptive means, may vanish.

we shall go to worship puṟampayam where the crabs with dots sleep on the lotus flowers growing in the fields where the white and young scabbard fish leap and jump.

neñcamē
my mind!
immaiyē malam elām aṟum
all the impurities of the soul will disappear in this birth itself.
maṟumaikkum valviṉai cārkilā
the irresistible actions will not reach us even in the next birth, as its cuse.
calam elām oḻi
give up all the evil deeds.
pulam elām maṇṭip poṉviḷaikkum puṟampayam, eṅkaḷ caṅkaraṉ vantu taṅkum ūr toḻappōtum
we shall go to worship puṟampayam in which our god who does suspicious things stays coming from Kayili, cuse to grow paddy as valuable as gold by flowing swiftly into the fields and irrigating them?
kalam elām kaṭal maṇṭu kāviri āṭiya kaṅkai nīr
by the water which is as holy as the water of the Kaṅki in which Kāviri bathed and in which all the boats rush towards the sea.

to make the habit of doing evil actions that could not be resisted and done in previous births and the sins that were the result of those actions, to vanish by degrees.
kaṇṭa arīyaṉakēṭṭiyēl
if you pay heed to the difficult means which I found out in which you would conduct yourself.

we shall go to worship puṟampayam where there are natural tanks in which lotus flowers blossom and where the celestials of heaven sing and leap and dance out of ecstasy and worship god.

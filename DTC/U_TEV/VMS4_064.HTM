vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ vikirtaṉār
Civaṉ in Vīḻimiḻi who is different from the world.
pūtattiṉ paṭaiyar
has an army of pūtams
pāmpiṉ pūṇiṉar
wears cobras as ornaments.
pūṇanūlar
wears a sacred thread.
cītattiṉ polinta tiṅkaṭ koḻuntar
wears a crescent which is flourishing by its coolness (tiṅkaṭ koḻutu crescent).
nañcu aḻuntu kaṇṭar
has poison in his neck which has become fixed firmly in it (the poison neither comes out, not goes into the stomach; so it is sid to be firm in the neck)
kītattiṉpolinta ōcaik kēḷviyarvēḷviyāḷar vētattiṉ poruḷar
he is the meaning of the vētams of the brahmins who have learnt vētams by listening to chanting them which has sound which is flourishing by its music, and who performs sacrifices.

see 1st verse.
kālaiyil katircey mēṉi
has a body like the morning sun with brilliant rays.
kaṅkuliṉ kaṟutta kaṇṭar
has a neck as black as the night.
mālaiyil matiyam cērnta makuṭattar
has a crown on which the crescent that rises in the evening, is attached
matuvum pālum ālaiyil pākum pōla aṇṇittiṭṭu
tasting sweet like honey, milk and the sugar-syrup got from the sugar-cane.
[[nal iṭṭamāyt tirupputtūraṉaic cintai ceyac ceyak karuppuc cāṟṟilum aṇṇikkum kāṇmiṉē (appar, ariciṟ karipputtūr, 4).]]
aṭiyārkku eṉṟum vēlaiyiṉ amutar
is always like the nectar in the ocean of milk, to his devotees.

see 1st verse.
(1) maṟaiyiṭaip poruḷar
is the meaning hidden in the vētams.
moṭṭiṉ malarvaḻi vācat tēṉar
is the fragrant honey that flows when the bud blossoms.
kaṟaviṭaip pālilneyyar
is the ghee in the milk of the milk-cow. (kaṟavu is a rare usage)
karumpiṉil kaṭṭiyāḷar
is the jaggery obtined from the juice of the sugar-cane.
(2) piṟaiyiṭaip pāmpu koṉṟaippiṇaiyal cēr caṭaiyuḷ nīrar
holds water in his caṭi where the cobra is near the crescents, and garland of koṉṟi, are combined.
viṟakiṭait tīyar
is the fire that is hidden in the fire-wood;

eṇ akattu illaiyallar
one cannot say that he does not dwell in the mind which thinks about him.
ūrallar
neither does he dwell in that mind.
imavāṉ peṟṟa pey akattu araiyar
has the lady who is the dughter of ināvaṉ, as a half in him.
kāṟṟil peruvali iruvar āki
being the strong two qualities in the air.
maṇ akattu aivar
-he is the five in the earth.
nīril nālvar
he is the four in water.
tīyataṉil mūvar
he is the three in the fire.
viṇṇakattu oruvar
he is the one in the sky;
[[In these lines the five subtle rudiments of the five elements are mentioned;
in the earth all the five are found;
in the water four are found;
in the fire three in the air two, and in the sky one;

(appar, kaṟkuti, tāṇṭakam, 3);]]
[[PP: pāriṭai aintāyp parantāy pōṟṟi, nīriṭai nāṉkāy nikaḻntāy pōṟṟi, tīyiṭai mūṉṟāyt tikaḻntāy pōṟṟi, vaḷiyiṭai iraṇṭāy makiḻntāy pōṟṟi veḷiyiṭai oṉṟāy viḷaintāy pōṟṟi

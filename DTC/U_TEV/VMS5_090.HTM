{5:90}__1+
{$} īcaṉ entai iṇaiyaṭi nīḻal (ē)
the shelter afforded by the twin feet of our father, the Lord of the universe.
pōṉṟatu (ē)
is like
mācuil vīṇaiyum
the music produced in a flawless vīṇi.
[[mācuil can be added to vīṇi or the music produced in it]]
mālaimatiyamum
the moon that rises in the evening
vīcuteṉṟalum
the balmy southern breeze that blows gently.
vīṅku iḷavēṉilum(1)
the increasing milder part of the hot season
(the months of cittiri and vikāci)
mūcuvaṇṭu (1) aṟai poykaiyum
and the coolness of the natural tank in which the swarming bees hum with a great noise on flowers.
[[Variant readings: (1) vēṉalum (2) vaṇṭiṟaip poykaiyum]]
{5:90}__2+
{$} nāṉ aṟi ñāṉamum kalviyum namaccivāyavē
the spiritual wisdom and education useful for the mundane world are namaccivaya only.
nāṉ aṟiviccaiyum namaccivāyamē
the mantiram that I know is namaccivaya only.
nā namaccivāyavē naviṉṟu ēttumē
my tongue will prise Civaṉ by chanting Namaccivāya only without ceasing.
namaccivāyavē naṉṉeṟikāṭṭumē
namaccivaya only will show the path to salvation.
{5:90}__3+
{$} (1) āḷ āṉārkku āḷ āṉārai aṭaintu uyyār
some people will not save themselves by approaching persons who have been admitted as protege who in their turn became slaves to god.
nīḷā āḷceytu meymmaiyuḷ niṟkilār
they have not the strength of mind to stand steadfast in the nature of the god who is the reality.
toḻumpar cevi tōḷātacuraiyō
are their ears cavities which are not bored through by good words.
toḻumpar
base persons, who serve other people;
[[valañcuḻi īcaṉaip payilkilār cilar pāvit toḻumparē
(nāvukkaracar, Valañcuḻi, (1) 2)]]
vāḷā māyntu maṇ ākik kaḻivarē
they will disappear from this world and become one with dust, dying without any benefit.
[[Variant reading: (1) āḷākār āḷ āṉārai]]
{5:90}__4+
{$} nāṇilīr
People who have no sense of shame!
naṭalai vāḻvukoṇṭu eṉceytir
what good act did you do to yourself, with this life full of sufferings!
cuṭalai cērvatu coṟpiramāṇamē
reaching the cremation-ground is not only verbal authority but also personal experience.
kaṭaliṉ nañcu amutu uṇṭavar kaiviṭṭāl
if Civaṉ who consumed the poison that rose in the ocean as nectar, forsakes you.
uṭaliṉār kiṭantu ūrmuṉi paṇṭamē
the body will by lying detested by all the people of the place.
[[uṭaliṉār is added to denigrate the body]]
[[iṉ: augmentation used in combination as if nouns and case endings]]
{5:90}__5+
{$} pūkkaikkoṇṭu araṉ poṉaṭi pōṟṟilār
those who do not worship the golden feet of Araṉ holding flowers in the hands.
nākkaikkoṇṭu araṉ nāmam navilkilār
and those who do not recite the names of Araṉ without ceasing, with the help of their tongues.
ākkaikkē iraitēṭi alamantu
being confused in mind by searching for food for the body only.
[[irai is generally used in connection with the food of birds and beasts; here it is used to denigrate human beings who do not think of god;
[[iḻivu aṟintu uṇpāṉkaṇ iṉpam pōl niṟkum, kaḻipēr iraiyāṉ kaṇ nōy (Kuṟaḷ, 946)]]
kākkaikkē iraiyākik kaḻivarē
will pass away from the world leaving their bodies to become prey for crows after death.
{5:90}__6+
{$} poṟiyilīr
People who do not possess wisdom!
kuṟikaḷum aṭaiyāḷamum kōyilum neṟikaḷum avar niṉṟatu ōr nērmaiyum aṟiya
for all people to know the forms of Civaṉ, the emblems like the flag, bull etc., temples, the systems of religions showing the right path; God's impartiality in showing these as saving features of human beings.
āyiram āraṇam ōtilum
even if they are instructed in the teachings of the many vētams
[[āyiram is used in the sense of many]]
maṉam pukutātu eṉ (kol)
what is the reason for those teachings, not entering into your minds?
{5:90}__7+
{$} vāḻtta vāyum, niṉaikka maṭaneñcum, tāḻttac ceṉṉiyum tanta talaivaṉaic cūḻtta māmalar tūvittutiyātē
without prising, scattering selected flower at the feet of the master who endowed us with mouth to prise, the ignorant mind to meditate on him and the head to bow down before him.
viṉaiyēṉ neṭuṅkālam vīḻttavā
what a pity I who am a sinner, wasted a long time?
{5:90}__8+
{$} eḻutu pāvai nallārtiṟam viṭṭu
leaving being associated with ladies who are as beautiful as portrits drawn on a canvas
nāṉ toḻutu (1) pōṟṟi niṉṟēṉaiyum cūḻntukoṇṭu
surrounding me who worshipped and prised Civaṉ.
(1) uḻutacālvaḻiyē uḻuvāṉ poruṭṭu
in order to plough in the same furrow agin and agin (to be interested in efforts to earn money agin and agin)
[[Ploughing once agin in the furrow is not a fruitful effort]]
iḻutai neñcam itu eṉpaṭukiṉṟatu
what a suffering does this ignorant mind have to undergo?
[[Variant reading: (1) pōkaniṉṟēṉaiyum (2) uḻutakālvaḻiyē]]
{5:90}__9+
{$} nekku nekku niṉaippavar neñcuḷē, pukku niṟkum poṉ ārcaṭaippuṇṇiyaṉ
Civaṉ who is the embodiment of all virtuous acts and whose caṭi is golden in colour, and who enters into the minds of those who meditate on him, their hearts melting without ceasing, and remins there.
pokkam mikkavar pūvum nīrum kaṇṭu avar tamaināṇiyē nakku niṟpar
will be lughing internally feeling ashamed for their acts, seeing the flowers and water used in worship with deceit in a large measure.
{5:90}__10+
{$} viṟakil tīyiṉ, naṉpālil paṭuneypōl, māmaṇic cōtiyāṉ maṟaiya niṉṟuḷaṉ
Civaṉ is hiding in our mind like the fire in the firewood, like the ghee which is got from good milk and the brilliance in the big gem.
uṟavukōl naṭṭu uṇarvukayiṟṟiṉāl muṟukavāṅkik kaṭaiya muṉ niṟkumē
he will appear before us to grant his vision if we churn with increasing force with the churning rope of love fixing the stick of clear discernment
[[churning it with the rope wound round the churning rod will apply to the former two.]]
[[In olden days fire was produced by rubbing one stick on another for producing fire]]
[[maṇikaḻal is to give sides in cutting or polishing gems]]
[[this will apply to gem;
it is commonly called paṭṭaitīrtal]]
[[Among the two sticks one will be placed horizontally and one vertically; and this will be churned to produce fire by friction]]

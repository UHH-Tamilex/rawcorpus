[[Tāṇṭakam is what is called in later literature as eṇ-cīr viruttam. There are also two tāṇṭakam in Vaishnava hymnal literature by Tirumaṅki Āḻvār: tirukkuṟuntāṇṭakam, tiruneṭuntāṇṭakam. As Tirunāvukkaracar specialized in tāṇṭakam, the sixth book of Tēvāram arranged according to paṇ contins tāṇṭakam. He is also called tāṇṭakaccaturar, one who is capable in composing tāṇṭakam]]

{6:1}__1+
{$} ariyāṉai
the pre-eminent one or the one who is difficult to attin.
antaṇartam cintaiyāṉai
one who is in the minds of antaṇar
[[antaṇar refers to the proverbial tilli mūvayiravar]]
arumaṟaiyiṉ akattāṉai
one who is the inner meaning of the maṟi difficult to be understood.
aṇuvai
one who is as small as an atom.
yārkkum teriyāta tattuvaṉai
the ultimate reality that could not be understood by anybody, however great he may be
tēṉai, pālai, tikaḻ oḷiyai
one who is honey, milk, the self luminous light.
tēvarkaḷtam kōṉai
The King of the tēvars
maṟṟaikkariyāṉai, nāṉmukaṉai, kaṉalai, kāṟṟai, kaṉaikaṭalai, kalavaraiyaik kalantu niṉṟa periyāṉai
the great one who is pervading the black coloured one, four-faced one, fire, air, the roaring sea, the chief mountin ranges.
perumpaṟṟap puliyūrāṉaip pēcāta nāḷ ellām
all those days when we did not prise such a great one in perumpaṟṟapuliyūr, who has the foregoing eminences.
piṟavā nāḷē
are definitely days when we were not born.

{$} kaṟṟāṉai
one who learnt everything without being tught by anybody.
kaṅkaivārcaṭaiyāṉ taṉṉai
one from whose matted locks Kaṅki is flowing, (one on whose long matted locks he concealed Kaṅki.)
kāviricūḻ valañcuḻiyum karutiṉāṉai
one who thought of Valañcuḻi also, surrounded by Kāviri, as his abode
[[Valañcuḻi is the 25th shrine on the Southern bank of Kāviri.]]
aṟṟārkkum alantārkkum aruḷceyvāṉai
one who bestows his grace on destitute persons and persons in distress
[[aṟṟapōtum alanta pōtum āpaṟkālattu (cuntarar, ōṇakāntan tali, 3)]]
ārūrum pukuvāṉai aṟintōm aṉṟē
we knew him who enters into ārūr also.
maṟṟu ārumtaṉ oppār illātāṉai
one who has none comparable to him
vāṉavarkaḷ eppoḻutum vaṇaṅki ēttap peṟṟāṉai
one who is always bowed to and prised by the inhabitants of the celestial world.

kaṉaikaḻal kalantu olippa

vaḷarmatiyam caṭaikku aṇintu

arumāṉa vāḷmukattāṉ amarntu kāṇa
the dear lady with shining face which can be compared to others' faces, to witness with desire.
amararkaṇam muṭivaṇaṅka āṭukiṉṟa perumāṉai
the great one who dances for the group of immortals to bow their heads.

{6:1}__4+
{$} aruntavarkaḷ toḻutu ēttum appaṉ taṉṉai
the father who is worshipped with both hands and prised by people who have done difficult penance.
amararkaḷ tam perumāṉai
the Lord of the immortals.
araṉai
one who has got the name of araṉ.
mūvāmaruntu amararkku aruḷpurinta maintaṉ taṉṉai
the strong person who gave out of grace the nectar which prevents old age, to the immortals.
maṟikaṭalum, kulavaraiyum, maṇṇum, viṇṇum, tiruntu oḷiya tārakaiyum, ticaikaḷ eṭṭum, tiricuṭarkaḷ ōr iraṇṭum piṟavum āyaperuntakaiyai
the noble-minded person who is the sea where waves roll up and down, chief mountin ranges, the earth, the sky, the stars of defectless light, the eight directions, the two heavenly lights which are always moving, and other things.

{6:1}__5+
{$} aruntuṇaiyai
the rare companion
aṭiyārtam allal tīrkkum arumaruntai
the rare remedy which removes the sufferings which can be compared to diseases, of his devotees (This is partial metaphor)
paṟṟum akal ñālattu akattuḷ tōṉṟivarum tuṇaiyum cuṟṟamum viṭṭu
having renounced the attachment, relations and the friends who have been born in this wide world.
vāṉ pulaṉkaḷ akattu aṭakki
having controlled internally the strong five senses.
maṭavārōṭum poruntu aṇaimēl varum payaṉaip pōka māṟṟi
having completely given up the sexual pleasure derived from union with women, lying on the bed.
potu nīkkittaṉai niṉaiya vallārkku eṉṟum peruntuṇaiyai
one who is always the great guide for those who think about him, distinguishing him from other gods (one should not think of him clubbing him with minor deities).

the same as for the first verse.
{6:1}__6+
{$} karumpu amarum moḻi maṭavāḷ paṅkaṉ taṉṉai
one who has on his half a young lady speaking words as sweet as the juice of sugar-cane.
kaṉavayirak kuṉṟuaṉaiya kāṭciyāṉai
one who has the majestic appearance of a hill of heavy diamond.
arumpu amarum pūṅkoṉṟait tārāṉ taṉṉai
one who wears a garland of beautiful koṉṟi (koṉṟai: indian laburnam) in which there are buds.
arumaṟaiyōṭu āṟu aṅkam āyiṉāṉai
one who is the six aṇkams and the maṟi which is difficult to understand.
curumpu amarum kaṭipoḻilkaḷ cūḻteṉ ārūrc cuṭark koḻuntai
the flame of light in ārūr in the south which is surrounded by fragrant gardens which are desired by bees.
tuḷakku illā viḷakkai
the light which is without shaking.
mikkaperum poruḷai
the one who is of great value and superior to other things.
perumpaṟṟa .... nāḷē

{$} varumpayaṉai
the pleasure derived from music
eḻunarampiṉ ōcaiyāṉai
the music produced by the seven strings (in yāḻ)
varai cilaiyā
using the (mēru) mountin as a bow.
vāṉavarkaḷ muyaṉṟavāḷi, arumpayamcey avuṇarpuram eriyak kōtta ammāṉai

alaikaṭal nañcu ayiṉṟāṉ taṉṉai
one who drank the poison of the ocean which is always on the motion.

Having a steady mind which is not shaken by the looks of the outer corner of the eyes of young ladies who have tresses of hir which are desired by bees.
tuṟantōr uḷḷap perumpayaṉai
the great and final fruit to be obtined by the mind of people who have renounced (all attachments)

one who is well versed in all kinds of dances (there) (Kuṟṟālam is one of the five ampalams where the Lord dances; it is called chitra sabha)

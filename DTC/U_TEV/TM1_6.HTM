*2

*3

kāl pulku paiṅkaḻal _ārkka _āṭum kaṇapatiyīccuram kāmuṟavē?

*4

mā maruvum maṇik kōyil mēya marukal nilāviya mainta! collāy---

kāmaru cīr makiḻntu _elli _āṭum kaṇapatiyīccuram kāmuṟavē?

*5

kāṭu_akamē _iṭam_āka _āṭum kaṇapatiyīccuram kāmuṟavē?

*6

kaṉai vaḷar kūr _ēnti _āṭum kaṇapatiyīccuram kāmuṟavē?

*7

{7:48}__1+
{$} kaṟṟavar toḻutu ēttum cīrkkaṟaiyūril pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi naṟṟavā
God who is the form of severe penance and who dwells at Pāṇṭikkotumuṭi in famous Kaṟiyūr which is worshipped and prised by learned people!
paṟṟu maṟṟu eṉakku iṉṟi
as there is no other support for me.
niṉ tiruppātamē maṉam pāvittēṉ
I meditated upon your holy feet only.
peṟṟalum
as I did so.
piṟantēṉ
I enjoyed the good fortune of being born as a human being.
iṉivantu piṟavāta taṉmai eytiṉēṉ
I got the state of not-being born agin in this world.
uṉai nāṉ (1) maṟakkiṉum
even if I may forget you.
nā namaccivāyavē collum
my tongue will be uttering your name, ``namaccivāya'' without ceasing.
[[Kaṟiyūr is the name of the place; Pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi is the name of the temple]]
[[Variant reading: (1) maṟakkilum; this reading will apply to the following verses upto the ninth]]
{7:48}__2+
{$} kiḷar puṉal kāviri vaṭṭa vācikai koṇṭu aṭi toḻutu ēttu pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi naṭṭavā
my friend in Pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi which the river Kāviri of rising water worship and prises bringing with it a garland circular in shape!
(1) iṭṭaṉ
I, who was desired by you.
(1) umaṭi ēttuvār ikaḻntiṭṭa nāḷ
those days when I was despised by devotees who prise your feet, as a fickleminded person.
maṟantiṭṭa nāḷ
and those days when I forgot you which were the cuse for their despising me.
keṭṭanāḷ eṉṟu alāl karutēṉ
I would consider those days as wasted days only, I would not think otherwise,
uṉai nāṉ maṟakkiṉum namaccivāyavē nā collum
[[see 1st verse]]
[[iṭṭaṉuṉṉaṭi can be split into iṭṭaṉ uṉaṭi or iṭṭaṉnuṉ aṭi]]
[[Variant reading: (1) iṭṭaṉummaṭi]]
{7:48}__3+
{$} parañcōti
supreme being as the light divine!
puṉalkāviri pāvu taṇpuṉal vantu iḻi pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi nāvalā
the great orator who is in Pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi where the cool floods of the Kāviri with water spreads coming to that place!
ōvum nāḷ
the days when I did not think of you.
uṇarvu aḻiyum nāḷ, uyirpōkum nāḷ, uyar pāṭaimēl kāvum nāḷ ivai eṉṟu alāl karutēṉ
I would not consider otherwise the days when I lost consciousness, when life leaves my body and I am carried on a funeral bier which is carried high, except as wasted.
uṉai nāṉ maṟakkiṉum nānamaccivāyavē collum
see 1st verse.
[[As Civaṉ revealed the vetam-s and their meanings he is called nāvalaṉ]]
{7:48}__4+
{$} ellai ilpukaḻ empirāṉ
our master whose fame is limitless!
entai tampirāṉ
the master of my father!
eṉpoṉ, eṉ māmaṇi
who is like my gold! who is like the big gem in my possession.
kallai unti
pushing out stones
[[kal may also mean precious stones]]
vaḷampoḻintu
pouring abundantly wealth everywhere.
iḻikāviriataṉ karaivāy
on the bank of the flowing Kāviri.
nallavar toḻutu ēttum cīrk kaṟaiyūril pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi vallavā
God of ability who dwells at pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi in Kāṟiyūr which good people worship with joined hands and prise!
nāṉ maṟakkiṉum nā ... collum
see 1st verse.
{7:48}__5+
{$} pañciṉ melaṭip pāvaimār kuṭaintu āṭu pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi nañcu aṇikaṇṭa
Civaṉ who has a neck made beautiful by poison and who is in pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi where the ladies who are as beautiful as a portrit and whose feet are pinted with red-cotton dye plunge and bathe!
añciṉārkku araṇāti eṉṟu
with the thought that you afford protection to those who are afrid of the cycle of birth and death, and seek refuge in you.
aṭiyēṉ nāṉum mika añciṉēṉ
I too who is your slave was very much afrid of that, and sought refuge in you
añcal eṉṟu aṭittoṇṭaṉēṟku aruḷ nalkiṉāykku aḻikiṉṟatu eṉ
you granted your grace to me who is your slave saying Do not fear. What do you lose by that?
nāṉ maṟakkiṉum nā ... collum
see 1st verse.
{7:48}__6+
{$} kolpulit tōliṉmēl āṭu pāmpu (atu) araikku acaitta aḻakaṉē
beautiful god who tied on the waist a dancing cobra on the skin of the tiger which is capable of killing!
parañcōti
the supreme being as the light divine!
amtaṇ kāvirippāṭu taṇpuṉal vantu iḻi pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭiccēṭaṉē
the great god who is in pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi where the cool water comes and flows into the beautiful and cool Kāviri!
[[cēṭaṉ: when all things are destroyed Civaṉ only remins, surviving all of them is another meaning]]
ēṭuvāṉ iḷantiṅkaḷ cūṭiṉai
you wore a crescent that shines in the sky which resembles the petal of a flower.
piṉeṉ
what is there after that to prise about your affording protection to those who surrender to you
[[The moon sought the protection of Civaṉ when he lost all phases except one on account of Takkaṉ's curse; Civaṉ wore it on his head and granted it the life of waxing and waning]]
{7:48}__7+
{$} kurumpai meṉmulaik kōtaimār kuṭaintu āṭu, neruṅki vaṇpoḻil cūḻntu eḻilpeṟa niṉṟa kāvirik kōṭṭiṭaip pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi virumpaṉē
God who is liked by all and who dwells in pāṇṭikkotumuṭi on the bank of the Kāviri where the fertile gardens are close to one another and beautifully situated, and where the young girls who are like soft garlands and have breasts like immature cocoanuts dive and bathe!
virumpiniṉ malarppātamē niṉaintēṉ
I meditated upon your feet only cherishing them with love.
viṉaikaḷum viṇṭaṉa
all my acts got separated from me.
uṉaināṉ maṟakkiṉum ... collam
see 1st verse.
{7:48}__8+
{$} cempoṉ nēr caṭaiyāy
Civaṉ who has a caṭi like superior gold!
tiripuram tī eḻaccilai kōliṉāy
who bent the bow for the fire to rise in the three cities!
vampu ulām kuḻalāṉaip pākam amarntu
having placed with desire a lady from whose tresses of hir fragrance spreads.
kāvirik kōṭṭiṭai, kompiṉ mēl kuyil (1) kūva māmayil āṭu pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi nampaṉē
Civaṉ fit to be desired who is in pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi on the bank of the Kāviri, where on the branches of trees the great peacocks dance when the indian cuckoo cries!
uṉai nāṉ maṟakkiṉum ... collum
see 1st verse.

Civaṉ, the cuse of all things, who is at the temple pāṇṭikkoṭumuṭi in Kaṟiyur, and who is worshipped by nāraṇaṉ and Piramaṉ!
eṇāyirakōṭi tēvar

māmaṇi

eṉṟu
prising like this

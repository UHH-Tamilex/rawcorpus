{2:29}__1+
{$} muṉṉiya kalaipporuḷum mūvulakil vāḻvum paṉṉiya oruttar paḻaūr viṉaviṉ
If any one asks about the old place of the unequalled god who gave out after great consideration the life in the three worlds and the subject matter of the arts that are to be pondered.
ñālam tuṉṉi
having descended on the earth and joining as a crowd
imaiyōrkaḷ tuticeytu muṉ vaṇaṅkum ceṉṉiyar viruppuṟu tiruppukali ām
is Tiruppukali which is desired by the god who is on the heads of the tēvar who sing his praises and worship him first.
[[Arts are traditionally said to be sixty-four in number;]]
[[mūvulaku: heaven, earth, and nether-worlds.]]
{2:29}__2+
{$} miti milaittacaṭai vaṇtirai puṉalcūṭi
having worn the water which has surging waves on the caṭai which has put on the crescent.
paṇṭu eri kai āṭu paramaṉ pati (atu) eṉpar
People say that the city belongs to the sublime Lord who danced with a fire in his hand, in the distant past.
puṇṭarikavācam (atu) vīca malarc cōlai teṇṭirai kaṭaṟpoli tiruppukali ām
the garden with flowers from which issues the fragrance of lotus; flourishes like the sea of clear waves in the beautiful pukali the garden is compared to the sea:
[[PP_EXT: kaṭalpōl tōṉṟala kāṭu iṟantōrē
(Akam, 1, 19)]]
[[Variant reading: vācamatuvīcumalar']]
[[ Some have taken kaṭaṟpoli to mean, pukali which floated on the sea during deluge]]
[[cōlaiyaiyuṭaiya tiruppukali will be the syntactical link for that meaning]]
{2:29}__3+
{$} pā aṇavu cintaiyavar pattaroṭu kūṭi
people whose minds are closed to poetry (are saturated with poetry) joining with devotees.
nā aṇavum antaṇaṉ viruppu iṭam (atu) eṉpar
say that the place desired by the antaṇaṉ who is always on the lips of devotees when they praise him.
[[PP_EXT: yāḻkeḻu maṇimiṭaṟṟu antaṇaṉ
(Akam, invocatory verse, 15)]]
pū aṇavu cōlai iruḷ mālai etir kūrt tēva(ṇ)ṇa viḻāvaḷar tiruppukali ām
that place is Tiruppukali where festivals full of divinity are celebrated on an increasing scale, and where the gardens which have abundant flowers become increasingly dark in contrast to the evening
[[Variant reading: tēvaṇar viḻā]]
{2:29}__4+
{$} maitavaḻum mā miṭaṟaṉ
the Lord has a black neck on which the black colour of the poison spreads
[[maittaḻaiyā niṉṟa māmiṭaṟṟam palavaṉ mā-karumai; māmiṭaṟu eṉpatu paṇputtokaiyāy iṉṉatu itu eṉṉum tuṇaiyāy niṟṟalātum, maittaḻaiyā niṉṟa eṉpatu akkarumaiyatu mikutiyai uṇartti niṟṟalāṉum kūṟiyatu kūṟalākāmai aṟika (Kōvaiyār 102; pēraciriyar)]]
maitavaḻummāmiṭaṟaṉ
one who has a black neck on which black colour spreads like the sable cloud;
[[koṇṭalum nīlamum puraitirumiṭaṟar (Ñāṉacampantar tirukkaḻumalam (2)2) puṟalaṉa miṭaṟuṭaippuṇṇiyaṉē (tiruppukali (7) 1)]]
mā naṭam atu āṭi
one who performed the big dance.
kai vaḷaiyiṉāḷoṭukalanta pati eṉpar
people say the place where the Lord is residing jointly with the lady who wears bangles on her hands.
ceypaṇi peruttueḻum uruttirarkaḷ kūṭitteyvamatu iṇakkuṟu tiruppukaliām
that place is tiruppukali where the uruttirarkaḷ (uruttirarkaḷ)
jain together and do great services and thereby add divinity to that place.
{2:29}__5+
{$} muṉṉam irumūṉṟu camayaṅkaḷ avai āki
Having become in the beginning six religions
[[PP: aṟucamayam, pauttam, piratilōkāyitam eṉṉum peṟṟiya
(tivākaram, 12)]]
[[PP: pāṅkuṟum ulōkāyatamēpauttam, cāṅkiyam, naiyāyikam, vaicēṭikam, mīmāñcakam
(maṇimēkalai, 27-78-80);]]
[[PP: ariya uṭcamayam āṟē āvaṇa caivam tūymai, peruku pācupatattōṭu piṟaṅku māviratam maṟṟum, karutiya vīṭupēṟu kāṭṭu kāḷāmukantāṉ, maruviyavāmam, mikka vairavam eṉṉalāmē, akappuṟaccamayantāmē aṟai ulōkāyakampiṉ, uṟaitaku pauttamē ārukatamīmāñcamōṭu, kuṟipeṟumāyāvātam kūṟiya pāñcarātram, neṟipeṟum ivaiyē eṉṉa nikaḻttuvar nūlvallōrē
(cuṭāmaṇi nikaṇṭu, 12 58-59);]]
[[Civāñāṉamuṉivar, who has written an elaborate commentary on Civañāṉapōtam has classified religions into four ie., akaccamayam, akappuṟaccamayam, puṟaccamayam, puṟappuṟaccamayam.
Under puṟaccamayam he includes vaicēṭikam, naiyāyikam mīmāñcai, māyāvātam, pāṟkariyavātam, kirīṭāppiramavātam, cattappiramavātam, cāṅkiyamatam, yōkamatam, pāñcarāttiramatam, akappuṟaccamayam: pācupatam, māviratam, kāpālam, vāmam, vairavam, aikkiyavātacaivam, puṟappuṟaccamayam: ulakāyatam, pauttam, māttiyamikar, yōkācārar, cauttirāntikar, vaipāṭikar, ārukatam; akaccamayamāṟu:- pāṭāṇavātacaivam, pētavātacaivam, civacamavātacaivam, civacaṅkirāntavātacaivam, īcuvaravavikāra vātacaivam, civāttuvitacaivam]]
piṉṉai aruḷ ceyta piṟaiyāḷaṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple in which the Lord who has a crescent moon and who gave his grave even after that.
puṉṉaiya malarppoḻilkaḷ akkiṉ oḷikāṭṭac cennel vayal ārtaru tiruppukali ām
is Tiruppukali where there are abundant crope of red variety of paddy in the fields; the buds in gardens of mast-wood trees shine like chank beads.
[[The buds of the puṉṉai (mast-wood) tree are compared to akku (chankbead);]]
[[PP: puṉṉaik kaṉṉikaḷ akku arumpu puṟampayam
(cuntarar, puṟampayam, 7);]]
[[PP: akku arumpuperumpuṉṉai
(appar, tirupputtūr, 8);]]
[[PP: naṟumpuṉṉai, akkuvarum kaḻikkāṉal aiyāṟarai
(Civaperumāṉ tiruviraṭṭaimaṇimālai, 30)]]
{2:29}__6+
{$} koṅku aṇaviyaṉ poḻiliṉ mācupaṇimūcat teṅkuaṇavu tēṉmali tiruppukali
Tiruppukali where abundant honey is available in the cocoanut trees on which the dew envelopes them like dust in the extensive gardens where honey is gathered.
vaṅkam maliyum kaṭalviṭattiṉai nukarnta aṅkaṇaṉ arutticeytu irukkum iṭam ām eṉpar
people say that it is the shrine where the Lord of gracious eyes, who enjoyed the poison that rose in the ocean where there are many ships.
[[vaṅkam maliyum:
this epithet is a generic epithet, as there are no ships sailing in the ocean of milk]]
{2:29}__7+
{$} palkul aṭiyārkaḷ paṭi āraicaipāṭic celvam maṟaiyōruṟai tiruppukali
tiruppukali where the brahmins possessing the wealth of devotion sing music (in praise of the Lord) so that the ever-increasing devotees may enjoy sumptuously all happiness in this world.
nalkuravum iṉpamum nalaṅkaḷ (um) avai āki
having become poverty, objects of pleasure and welfare.
valviṉaikaḷ tīrttaruḷum maintaṉiṭam ām eṉpar
People say that it is the place of the valiant hero who completely removes the effects of irresistible sins
[[Variant reading: malkum]]
{2:29}__8+
{$} parappaṟupukaḻp perumaiyāḷaṉ
the Lord who has great fame spreading everywhere.
varai taṉṉāl arakkaṉai aṭarttaruḷum aṇṇal iṭam eṉpar
people say that the place of the eminent one who pressed down arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ) by the mountain
nerukkuṟu kaṭal tiraikaḷ muttamaṇi cinta
the crowded waves of the scatter precious stones of pearls.
cerukkuṟu poḻiṟ tiruppukali ām
is tiruppukali which is shining by the fardens which give joy. (muttamaṇi
as pearls is one of the nine gems it is called so)
{2:29}__9+
{$} kōṭaloṭu kūṉmatikulāya caṭaitaṉmēl āṭu aravam vaittaruḷum appaṉ
the father who placed the dancing cobra on the caṭai on which the white specious of malabar glory lily and curved crescent moon are conspeciuous.
tēṭauṟaiyum nakar tiruppukali ām
tiruppukali is the city where the Lord dwells, to be searched above and below, desiring to find out the feet and head of the Lord who rose as a column of fire for Māl and Piramaṉ to search.
{2:29}__10+
{$} kaṟṟa amaṇar uṟṟu ulavutērar urai ceytakuṟṟamoḻi koḷkaiyatu ilāta perumāṉ ūr
the place of the great one who does not accept the faulty words of the buddhists who wander in large numbers, and amaṇar who have studied heratic works challenging the authority of the vētams.
poṉ toṭi maṭantaiyarum maintar (um) pulaṉ aintum ceṟṟavar (um) viruppuṟutiruppukaliām
is tiruppukali which is desired by women wearing gold bangles, and people who have annihilsted the sense of the body, which is of five kinds.
[[Variant reading: ceṟṟavarvaṉappuṟu]]
{2:29}__11+
{$} centamiḻ parappuṟu tiruppukalitaṉmēl antam mutal āki naṭuāya perumāṉaip pantaṉ uraiceytamiḻkaḷ pattum
all the ten verses which were sung upon the great one who is the beginning, middly and end of all things and who is desirous of tiruppukali which spreads chaste Tamiḻ, by pantāṉ.
[[Variant reading:centamiḻkaḷ]]
icaikūra vantavaṇam ēttumavar vāṉam uṭaiyārē
those who are able to recite and praise with rich music as it comes to their mind, will reach heaven definitely.

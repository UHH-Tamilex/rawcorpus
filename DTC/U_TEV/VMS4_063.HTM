{4:63}__1+
{$} umaiyavaḷ paṅkā
Civaṉ who has Umayavāḷ on one half.
mikka cōtiyē
very bright diving light!
(1) tuḷaṅkum eṇ tōḷ cuṭar maḻuppaṭaiyiṉāṉē
who has eight shoulders and a battle-axe of luminous brightness!
ātiyē
the cuse of all things!
amarar kōvē
the Lord of the immortals!
aṇi aṇāmalai uḷāṉē
who is in aṇṇamali!
ōti māmalarkaḷ tūvi nītiyāl niṉṉai allāl niṉaiyumā(ṟu) niṉaivilēṉē
I have no other thought except thinking of you by right counduct uttering your names and scattering big flowers
[[niṉaital is act of the mind;
ōtal is the act of speech;
malartūvutal is the act of the body.]]
[[Note: there is one separate shrine by name aṇi aṇṇāmalai when one goes round the big hill aṇṇāmali; perhaps this decade is about that shrine.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) tulaṅkum]]
{4:63}__2+
{$} paṇ taṉai veṉṟa iṉcol pāvai ōr pāṅkā
Civaṉ who has a beautiful lady whose words are sweeter than melody-types!
nīlakaṇṭaṉē
who has a blue neck!
kārkoḷ koṉṟaik kaṭavuḷē
god who wears koṉṟi flowers which blossom in riny season.
kamalapātā
who has lotus-red feet!
aṇṭaṉē
Lord of the universe!
amarar kōvē
the King of the immortals!
aṇi aṇāmalaiyuḷāṉē
see 1st verse.
toṇṭaṉēṉ
I who am your devotee.
uṉṉai allāl collumā col ilēṉ (ē)
I find no other word except speaking about your greatness.
{4:63}__3+
{$} uruvamum uyirumāki
being the bodies and the life within them.
ōtiya ulakukku ellām
for all the beings mentioned in works about this world.
(1) peruviṉai-piṟappu vīṭu āy niṉṟa emperumāṉ
our Lord who remined as the original acts, birth and salvation.
mikka aruvi poṉ coriyum aṇṇāmalai uḷāy
who is in aṇṇāmali where the many streams pour out gold!
aṇṭarkōvē
the ruler of the celestials!
niṉpātam maruvi allāl maṟṟu oru māṭuilēṉē
I have no other wealth except approaching your feet.
[[Variant reading: (1)peruviṉaip piṟappu.]]
{4:63}__4+
{$} paimpoṉē
fine gold!
pavaḷakkuṉṟē
a hill created by coral!
paramaṉē
the supreme being!
pālveṇṇīṟā
one who smears with sacred ash which is as white as milk.
cempoṉē
superior gold.
malarceypātā
one who has lotus-red feet!
cīrtamaṇiyē
heavy gem!
mikka ampoṉē koḻittu vīḻum aṇi aṇāmalaiuḷāṉē
one who is aṇṇāmali where the beautiful gold falls being sifted.
eṉ poṉē
my gold!
uṉṉai allāl ētum niṉaivu ilēṉē
I have no other thing in my thoughts except you.
{4:63}__5+
{$} piṟai aṇi muṭiyiṉāṉē
Civaṉ who wears a crescent on the head!
piññakā
who adorns his head on it a peacocks feather!(Destroyer of all things!)
peṇ ōr pākā
who has a lady on one half.
maṟaivalāy
who revealed the vētams! (who is well versed in the vētams!)
iṟaivā who pervades everything and every where!
vaṇṭu ār koṉṟaiyāy
who wears koṉṟi flowers on which the bees hum loudly.
vāmatēvā
who has the name of vāmatēvaṉ!
aṟaikaḻal amarar ēttum aṇi aṇāmalaiyuḷāṉē
the god in aṇṇāmali who is prised by the importals wearing sounding Kaḻal.
iṟaivaṉē
the master!
uṉṉai allāl yātum nāṉ niṉaivilēṉ (ē)
I have no other thing in my thoughts except you.
{4:63}__6+
{$} puricaṭaimuṭiyiṉ mēl ōr porupuṉal kaṅkaivaittu
placing the river Kaṅki which is in the form of water dashing on the twisted caṭi coiled into a crown!
kariuri pōrvaiyākak karutiya kālakālā
the Lord who dealt death to the god of death and who wished as the covering for the body the skin of an elephant!
arikulam malinta aṇṇāmalai uḷāy
who is in aṇṇamali where there is a large number of herds of lions!
varimiku vaṇṭu paṇ cey alariṉ mikkapātam nāṉ maṟappilēṉē
I shall not forget the feet which are superior to the lotus flower on which the bees with many lines hum like music.
{4:63}__7+
{$} iraviyum matiyum viṇṇum
the sun, moon and the sky in which they shine.
irunilampuṉalum kāṟṟum
the wide world, the water and the air.
urakam ār pavaṉam (1) eṭṭum
the eight worlds that are supported by āticētaṉ.
ticai oḷi uruvam āṉāy
you are the form of the eight direction and the fire.
aravu umiḻ maṇi koḷ cōti aṇiaṇāmalai uḷāṉē
who is in aṇi aṇāmali which is lit by the light of the jewel that is spat by cobras.
parama
the supreme god!
paravum niṉpātam allāl nāṉ paṟṟu ilēṉē
I have no other support except your feet prised by all.
{4:63}__8+
{$} pārttaṉukku aṉṟu nalki
showing deep love to pārttaṉ (aruccuṉaṉ)
pācupatattai īntāy
you gave him pācupatam, a weapon.
nīrttumpu ulāvu kaṅkai neṭumuṭi nilāva vaittāy
you placed Kaṅki which over flows and is moving to and fro to stay on your tall head.
ārttu vantu īṇṭu koṇṭal aṇi aṇāmalai uḷāṉē
God who is in aṇi aṇāmali which has clouds which collect together coming with thunders.
tīrttaṉē
holy god!
niṉtaṉ pātattiṟam alāl tiṟam ilēṉē
I do not know any other ways except the way chalked out by you.

{$} pālum ney(um) mutalāmikka pacuvil aintu āṭuvāṉē
God who bathes in the five products of the cow abundantly beginning with milk and ghee!
mālum nāṉmukaṉum kūṭikkāṇkilā vakaiyuḷ niṉṟāy
you remined in a manner which both Māl and Piramaṉ of four faces were not able to see.
ālum nīr, koṇṭal, pūkam aṇi aṇāmalaiuḷāṉē
God who is in aṇi aṇāmali which has sounding water, clouds and areca-palms.
vāluṭaiviṭaiyāy
who has a white bull;
vāl
white colour
[[ūrtivāl veḷḷēṟē (puṟam, 1-3)]]
uṉ taṉ malaraṭi maṟappu ilēṉē
I never forget your lotus-red feet.
{4:63}__10+
{$} irakkam yātu oṉṟum illāk(1) kālaṉaik kaṭinta emmāṉ
our Lord who destroyed the god of death who did not have even a little of pity.
urattiṉāl varaiyai ūkka oruviral nutiyiṉālē arakkaṉai neritta aṇṇāmalai uḷāy
God who is in aṇṇāmali and who crushed the arakkaṉ by the tip of a single toe when he lifted the mountin with his strength by making an effort
amarar ēṟē
the eminent one among the celestials!
cirattiṉāl vaṇaṅki ēttit tiruvaṭi maṟappu ilēṉē
I shall never forget your holy feet by bowing down my head and prising them.

*1
toḻum_āṟu vallār, tuyar tīra niṉintu
_eḻum_āṟu vallār, _ici pāṭa vimmi
_aḻum_āṟu vallār, _aḻunti maṟiyōr
vaḻipāṭucey mā maṭam maṉṉiṉiyē.

*2
kaṭal _ēṟiya nañcu _amutu_uṇṭavaṉē!
_uṭalē! _uyirē! _uṇarvē! _eḻilē!
_aṭal _ēṟu _uṭiyāy! _aḻunti maṟiyōr-
viṭalē! toḻa, mā maṭam mēviṉiyē.

*3
kaḻikāṭalaṉē! kaṉal_āṭaliṉāy!
paḻipāṭu _ilaṉē! _aviyē payilum
_aḻipāṭu _ilarāy, _aḻunti maṟiyōr
vaḻipāṭucey mā maṭam maṉṉiṉiyē.

*4
vāṉē! maliyē! _eṉa maṉ_uyirē!
tāṉē toḻuvār toḻu tāḷ maṇiyē!
_āṉē! civaṉē! _aḻuntiyavar, _em-
māṉē! _eṉa, mā maṭam maṉṉiṉiyē.

*5
_ali _ār puṉal cūḻ _aḻuntip perumāṉ!
nili _ār maṟiyum, niṟi veṇmaḻuvum,
_ili_ārpaṭiyum(m), _ivi _ēntu celva!
niliyā _atu koḷka _eṉa, nī niṉiyē!

*6
naṟavu _ār taliyiṉ nayavā! _ulakil
piṟavātavaṉē! piṇi_illavaṉē!
_aṟi _ār kaḻalāy! _aḻunti maṟiyōr
maṟavātu _eḻa, mā maṭam maṉṉiṉiyē.

*7
taṭumāṟu vallāy! talivā! matiyam
cuṭum_āṟu vallāy! cuṭar _ār caṭiyil
_aṭum _āṟu vallāy! _aḻunti maṟiyōr
neṭu mā nakar kitoḻa, niṉṟaṉiyē.

*8
periyāy! ciṟiyāy! piṟiyāy! miṭaṟum
kariyāy! karikāṭu _uyarvīṭu _uṭiyāy!
_ariyāy! _eḷiyāy! _aḻunti maṟiyōr
veriyār toḻa, mā maṭam mēviṉiyē.

*9
maṇi nīḷ muṭiyāl maliyi _arakkaṉ
taṇiyātu _eṭuttāṉ _uṭalam neritta
_aṇi _ār viralāy! _aḻunti maṟiyōr
maṇi mā maṭam maṉṉi _iruntaṉiyē.

*10
muṭi _ār caṭiyāy! muṉamnāḷ, _iruvar---
neṭiyāṉ, malarāṉ---nikaḻvāl _ivarkaḷ
_aṭi mēl _aṟiyār; _aḻunti maṟiyōr
paṭiyāl-toḻa, mā maṭam paṟṟiṉiyē.

*11
_aru ñāṉam vallār _aḻunti maṟiyōr
peru ñāṉam _uṭip perumāṉ_avaṉit
tiruñāṉacampantaṉa centamiḻkaḷ,
_uruñāṉam _uṇṭu_ām, _uṇarntārtamakkē.

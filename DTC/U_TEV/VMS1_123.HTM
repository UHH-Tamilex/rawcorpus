{1:123}__1+
{$} viraimalar māiyal poḻil valivalam uṟai iṟai(ē)
the supreme god who dwells in Valivalam which has groves of mango trees and fragrant flowers.
[[mā may also bees]]
pū iyal purikuḻal, varicilai nikarnutal, ēvu iyalkaṇai piṇai etirviḻi, umaiyavaḷ mēviya tiruvuru uṭaiyavaṉ
has a holy body in which umayavaḷ who has curly tresses of hir adorned with flowers a forehead like the bow which has many bands and eyes comparable to the deer and arrow which is to be discharged, stays with desire.
{1:123}__2+
{$} viraimalar maṭṭu amar poḻil valivalam, uṟai iṟai (ē)
the supreme god who dwells in Valivalam which has gardens of fragrant flowers having honey.
iṭṭam (atu) amarpoṭi icaitaliṉ
as the body is agreably smeared with the sacred ash to which Civaṉ is partial.
nacai peṟupaṭṭu avir pavaḷa naṉ maṇiyeṉa aṇipeṟu viṭṭu oḷir tiruvuṭaiyavaṉ
has a holy form which is beautiful like the gem of coral which shines in the silk which everyone desires, and glitters.
{1:123}__3+
{$} miṭaitaru marumali poḻil valivalam uṟai iṟai(ē)
the supreme god who dwells in Valivalam which has gardens with abundant fragrance and dense by the trees in it.
urumali kaṭal kaṭaivuḻi ulaku amar uyir veruvuṟuvakai eḻuviṭam
the poison that rose when the fearful ocean was churnned, to frighten the living beings in this world.
veḷimalai aṇi karumaṇi nikar kaḷam uṭaiyavaṉ
has a neck which is like the sapphire which adorns a mountin of silver.
{1:123}__4+
{$} valivalam uṟai iṟai (ē)
the supreme god who dwells in Valivalam.
aṉal nikar caṭai aḻal (1) aviyuṟa eṉa varupuṉal nikaḻvatu matinaṉai poṟi aravamum (2) eṉa niṉaivoṭu varumitu me(l)la muṭimicai maṉam uṭaiyavar
is always thinking in his mind about the water of the Kaṅki which flows down to put out the fire of the caṭi which resembles fire and about the cobra which is wet in that water has lines on its body and is moving slowly to swallow the crescent.
[[Variant readings: (1) avivuṟa (2) eṉaviṉaiyoṭu]]
{1:123}__5+
{$} miku koṭai vaṭiviṉar payil valivalam uṟai iṟai (ē)
the supreme god in Valivalam where many people who are the incarnation of unbounded liberality, crowd.
piṭi ataṉ uru umaikoḷa
when Umi assumed the form of a female elephant.
miku kariyatu vaṭikoṭu
Civaṉ assuming the form of a strong male elephant.
taṉatu aṭi vaḻipaṭumavar iṭar kaṭi kaṇapati vara aruḷiṉaṉ
was gracious enough to beget kaṇapati who destroys obstacles to devotees who worship his feet.
{1:123}__6+
{$} eḻilvarai tikaḻ matil valivalam uṟai iṟai (ē)
the supreme god who dwells in valivalam surrounded by a beautiful wall of fortification which is eminent like a mountin.
taraimutal ulakiṉil puṇar takai
the living being in the worlds beginning with this earth to have union with their females.
mika virai mali kuḻal umaiyoṭu viravatu ceytu
being united with Umi who has exceedingly fragrant tresses of hir.
naraitirai (1) keṭu takai atu aruḷiṉaṉ
bestowed his grace to destroy grey hirs and wrinkling of the skin, due to old age.
[[Variant reading: (1) keṭutakavatu aruḷiṉaṉ]]
{1:123}__7+
{$} eḻilmiku toḻil vaḷar valivarum matil valivalam uṟai iṟaivaṉ (ē)
the supreme god in valivalam which has a strong fortified wall of workmanship of exquisite beauty.
nalitaru (1) tarai vara naṭaivarum iṭaiyavar; politaru maṭavaraliyar maṉaiatu pukupalikoḷa varupavaṉ
is the god who come to receive the alms, and enters into the houses of beautiful ladies who have slendar waists and whose soft feet fear to tread upon the earth.
[[Variant reading: (1) karaivara]]
{1:123}__8+
{$} akam neti (1) iravu a(ṇ)ṇa nikar valivalam uṟai iṟai (ē)
the supreme god who dwells in the brilliant valivalam and who grants his grace when devotees seek his monetary assistance, from the treasures which is with him.
irāvaṇaṉ irupatu karam eḻil malaitaṉiṉ iravaṇam niṉaitara
having thought that the twenty hands of Irāvaṇaṉ should be separate from the body under the beautiful mountin, Kayili. (irāvaṇaṉ and irāvaṇam have become respectively iravaṇam the long vowel has been shortened.)
avaṉ muṭi poṭi ceytu
breaking his head to pieces.
iravaṇam amar peyar aruḷiṉaṉ
gave him the name of one who wept (rāvaṇaṉ means who wept)
[[Variant reading: (1) iravaṇaṉikar]]
{1:123}__9+
{$} miṭaikoṭi vāṉ aṇaimatil valivalam uṟai iṟai (ē)
the supreme god who dwells in valivalam which has fortified walls in which the crowded flags seem to touch the sky.
tēṉ amartarumalar (1) aṇaipavaṉ
Piramaṉ who is seated in a (lotus) flower in which there is honey.
valimikum ēṉam (atu) āy nilam akaḻ ari
and ari (Māl) who dug the earth transforming himself into a strong pig.
aṭimuṭi(tāṉ) aṇaiyā uru uṭaiyavaṉ
has a form whose feet and head they could not reach.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṇaiyavaṉ]]
{1:123}__10+
{$} malaimali matil valivalam uṟai iṟai (ē)
the supreme god who dwells in valivalam which has a fortified wall like the mountin.
ilaimalitara mikutuvar uṭaiyavarkaḷum
the buddhists who don a robe soaked in myrtle dye of the marutu tree which is full of leaves.
[[ilai marutē aḻakāka nāḷum iṭu tuvarkkāyoṭu cukku tiṉṉum nilai amaṇ (campantar, tirumarukalum) tiruccenkāttankutiyum, 10)]]
nilaimaiyil uṇal uṭaiyavarkaḷum niṉaivatu tolai valineṭu maṟai toṭar vakai uruviṉaṉ
has a person which is sought by the old and strong vētam, and destroyed the ideas of the amaṇar who have the habit of eating their food standing.
{1:123}__11+
{$} maṉṉiya valivala nakar uṟai iṟaivaṉai
on the Lord who dwells Valivalam which knows no destruction.
iṉiyal kaḻumala nakar (1) iṟai eḻil maṟai taṉ iyalkalai vala tamiḻ virakaṉatu urai uṉṉiya orupatum
all the ten verses which were composed with mature thought by the expert in Tamil who learnt the beautiful vētam and arts by his skill with the grace of god and who is the chief of the city of Kaḻumalam which has a pleasant natural beauty.
uyar poruḷ tarum (ē)
will bestow on the reciters by supreme objective of eternal bliss.
[[Variant reading: (1) uṟai]]

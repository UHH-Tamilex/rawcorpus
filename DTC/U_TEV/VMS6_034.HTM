tiruvārūr kōyilāk koṇṭa nāḷē
the day when you had Tiruvārūr as your temple.
oruvaṉāy ulaku ētta niṉṟa nāḷō
is it the day when you stood as the unequalled chief to be prised by the people of this world!
ōr uruvē mū uruvam āṉanāḷō
is it the day when the single form divided itself into three forms as Ayaṉ, Ari and Araṉ?
karuvaṉāyk kālaṉai muṉ kāynta nāḷō
is it the day when you got angry with the god of death and killed him?
[[kaṟuvaṉ has become karuvaṉ to suit rhyme]]
kāmaṉaiyum kaṇ aḻalāl viḻitta nāḷō
is it the day when you opened your frontal eye and destroyed Kāmaṉ too with the fire issuing from it?
maruvaṉāy maṇṇum viṇṇum teritta nāḷō
is it the day when you were united with the earth and the sky and created them?
māṉ maṟikkai ēnti ōr mātu ōr pākam tiruviṉāḷ cērvataṟku muṉṉō piṉṉō
is it before or after the beautiful lady, Umi, was united with you as a half and your holding a young deer in the hand?

tiruvārūr kōyilāk koṇṭa nāḷ
the day when you had your grace in the temple in tiruvārūr.
pukai eṭṭum
the eight kinds of birth the soul takes.
[[The eight are: tēvar, human beings, crawling beings, acquatic beings, birds, beasts, immovable things and those who are in hell.]]
pōkku eṭṭum
the eight defects.
[[They are ignorance, delusion, ego, attachment towards one's body and other things, likes, dislikes, virtuous acts and sins]]
pulaṉ eṭṭum
the aids like the sensation,
[[There are the rudimentary or subtle elements from which the five sensations of taste, sight touch, sound and smell are produced, 2) the five elements, 3) the five sensory organs, 4) the five organs of motor actions (5) quality 6) mūlappakuti, the ultimate reality of purutaṉ 7) internal intellect 8) the faculties of the mind]]
pūtalaṅkaḷ avai eṭṭum
the earth combined with the seven upper worlds or the nether regions.
[[These are the worlds where the souls transmigrate.]]
poḻilkaḷ eṭṭum
the eight islands?
[[They are, nāval, cākam, kucai, cirauñcam, cāṉmali, kōmētakam, puṭkaram and the abode of the celestials which is gold]]
kalai eṭṭum
the seven oceans
[[The salty sea, ocean of milk, ocean of curd, ocean of ghee, ocean of sugar-cane juice, ocean of honey, ocean of fresh water and the big ocean surrounding the cakkaravālakiri;
They are calledkalai as they form as the dress for the respective islands]]
kāppu eṭṭum
the mountins that stand as fortifications to those oceans
[[They are the seven, mountins surrounding the seven oceans and the cakkaravālakiri which girts the heaven of gold]]
kāṭci eṭṭum
the eight different sights that can be seen in the seven worlds
[[These are mentioned in detils in civākamams, Kantapurānam, civañaṉa pātiyam]]
kaḻaṟ cēvaṭi aṭaintār 1. kaḷai kaṇ eṭṭum
the eight worlds which one can reach if he worships Civaṉ expecting rewards for his actions
[[They are 1) puvalōkam, 2) civalōkam, 3) makalōkam 4) caṉalokam, 5) tēvalōkam, 6) piramalokam, 7) the world of Māl, and uruttiralōkam]]
nakai eṭṭum
eight kinds of brilliant lights;
[[They are the sun, moon, mars, uranus, napture and venus and the stars nakai stands for those things that give light; nāku and kōtu are not included as they are shadows of the moon and the earth.]]
nāḷ eṭṭum
the eight division of time
[[They are day, week, fortnight, month, season, the two ayanams, year and acon]]
naṉmai eṭṭum
the four positive objective of human pursuit
aṟam, poruḷ, iṉpam, vīṭu
and the oppositive of these as maṟam, iṉmai, tuṉpam, piṟappu)
nalam ciṟantār maṉattakattu malarkaḷ eṭṭum
the eight flowers or qualities
[[Such as (1) non-killing, 2) control of the senses, 3) forbearance, 4) pity, 5) knowledge 6) truth 7) penance and 8) love) which are in the minds of eminent people who possess spiritual knowledge.]]
tikai eṭṭum terippataṟku muṉṉō piṉṉō
is it the day before or after you demarked the four cardinal points and the intermediate directions between the cardinal points

[[There is an anecdote about this patikam. Ñāṉacampantar was staying in Tiruvīḻimiḻali after worshipping in some shrines nearby. The brahmins from Cīkāḻi went to Tiruvīḻimiḻi to request him to return to Cīkāḻi, becuse it was a long time since he left his native place. He replied, we shall start for Kaḻi getting the permission of the Lord in Vīḻimiḻali. In that night Lord of Kāḻi appeared in his dream and sid we shall show you the form in which we are in Kāḻi, here itself. After daybreak, Campantar worshipped the Lord in Vīḻimiḻali and to his wonder he found that the Lord gave him audience as it was in Kāḻi. These ten verses were sung in the form of questioning the Lord for his presence in Miḻi.]]

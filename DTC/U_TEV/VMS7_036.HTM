[[This decade was composed as the words of those ladies who fell in love with the fascinating beauty of Civaṉ when he went begging and were afraid of coming into contact with his form; this idea is mentioned by Cuntarar himself in the last verse]]

{7:36}__1+
{$} kār ulāviya nañcai uṇṭu iruḷkaṇṭar
Civaṉ who has a black neck due to drinking the poison which was black in colour!
veṇtalai ōṭukoṇṭu ūr elām tirintu eṉceyvīr
what would you get wandering in all the villages holding a white skull as a begging bowl?
nīr pali ōriṭattilē koḷḷum
you receive alms in one place only.
pār elām paṇintu ummaiyē paravippaṇiyum paiññīliyīr
Civaṉ who is Paiññīli, to whom only all the people of this world do obeisence and praise and do service!
āraṇīyaviṭaṅkarē
Civaṉ who has the name of Āraṇiya vitaṅkar!
[[āraṇiyam has been made long as āraṇīyam]]
āramāvatu nākamō? collum
is your garland cobra? please tell us.
{7:36}__2+
{$} malaittacantoṭu vēṅkai kōṅkamum maṉṉukārakil caṇpakam alaikkum paimpuṉal cūḻ paiññīliyil āraṇīyaviṭaṅkarē
Āraṇiya vitankar in Paiññīli surrounded by cool water which pushes champak, black eagle-wood tress in plenty, common caung, east indian kino tree and sandal-wood which grow in the mountain!
veḷḷēṟu cilaittunōkkum
your bull looks angrily at us bellowing.
centaḻal vāyapāmpatu mūceṉum
the cobra which has in its sac the poison like the red fire makes a hissing noise.
pirāṉirē
our master!
palikku nīrvarumpōtu numkaiyil pāmpu vēṇṭā
Do not have the cobra in your hand when you come for alms
[[pirāṉirē-pirāṉīrē is shortened for metre; it is the nominative of address of pirāṉār]]
{7:36}__3+
{$} pāyum nīrkkiṭaṅku ārkamalamum paintaṇ mātavi puṉṉaiyum āya paimpoḻilcūḻ paiññīliyil āraṇīya viṭaṅkarē
Civaṉ who is known by the name of Āraṇiya viṭaṅkar in Paiññīli surrounded by a verdant garden which has mast wood trees, cool and green delight of the woods, and lotus flowers grawing in the moats into which water flows.
empirāṉirē
our master!
yavar kaṇṇum vāyum mēṉiyum
you are pure in your eyes, mouth and body
[[yavar the third person is used in the sense of 2nd person]]
tuṉṉa āṭai
you wear a sewn loin-cloth
cuṭalaiyil pēyoṭu āṭalaittavirum
give up dancing along with pēy in the cremation-ground
nīr orupittarō
are you a mad person?
{7:36}__4+
{$} paintaṇ māmalar untucōlaikaḷ kantam nāṟu paiññīliyil
Civaṉ who is in Paiññīli where the gardens spread fragrance by the big, cool and fertile flowers shedding from the trees!
āraṇīyaviṭaṅkarē
Āraṇiya viṭaṅkar!
centamiḻttiṟam vallīrō
are you well versed ((in akam and puṟam, or iyal, icai and nātakam)), the divisions of refined Tamiḻ!
ceṅkaṇ aravam muṉkaiyil āṭavē vantu (1) niṟkum itu eṉkol
what is the reason for your standing before us with a cobra with red eyes dancing in your forearm?
palimāṟṟamāṭṭōm; iṭakilōm
we will not take back the alms intended to give you nor we are able to give you
ummēṉiyum antivāṉamō
is your form the evening sky?
colum
please tell us
[[Variant reading: (1) niṟkumiteṉ eṉalē]]
{7:36}__5+
{$} āraṇīyaviṭaṅkarē
Civaṉ who has the name of Āraṇiya viṭaṅkar!
nīṟunumtirumēṉi nittilam
the sacred ash on your form shine likes pearl.
nīlneṭuṅkaṇṇiṉāḷoṭum kūṟarāy vantu niṟṟir
you come and stand before us having on your half a lady of dark and long eyes.
[[āl:acai- expletive]]
pali koṇarntu iṭakilōm
we are unable to bring alms and place it in your bowl
naṭamiṉō
you walk out from this place
pāṟu veṇṭalai kaiyil ēntip paiññīliyē eṉṟir aṭikaḷ
you who are our master said, I am an inhabitant of Paiññīli, holding a ruined white skull in the hand
nīr āṟutāṅkiya caṭaiyarō
Have you on your caṭai a river?
colum
please tell us.
{7:36}__6+
{$} paravi nāṭoṟum pāṭuvār viṉai paṟṟaṟukkum paiññīliyīr
you who are in Paiññīli and can grant absolute severance from Karmams to those who daily praise and sing about you.
āraṇiyaviṭaṅkarē
Āraṇiya viṭaṅkar!
kuravam nāṟiya kuḻaliṉār vaḷaikoḷvatē toḻilāki nīr iravum immaṉai aṟitirē
Do you know this house in the night also, having as your business snatching the bangles of the ladies whose tresses of hair have the fragrance of bottle-flower?
iṅkē vantu pōkavum vallirē
are you capable of going over here and returning?
aravam āṭṭavum vallirō
are you capable of causing the cobra to dance?
{7:36}__7+
{$} āraṇīyaviṭaṅkarē
Civaṉ who has the name of Āraṇiya viṭaṅkar!
ēṭu ulām malarkkoṉṟaicūṭutir
you adorn yourself with koṉṟai flowers which has spread out petals
eṉpu elām aṇintu eṉ
what would you get by adorning yourself with all the bones?
kāṭumnumpati
your place of residence is the cremation-ground
ōṭukaiyatu
the skull is in your hand
kātal ceypavar peṟuvatu eṉ
what is the benefit that people who lavish their love on you, get?
pāṭalvaṇṭu icai ālumcōlaip paiññīliyēṉ eṉṟu niṟṟir
you stand before us saying, I am an inhabitant of Paiññīli where the bees which are capable of singing hum like music'
āṭal pāṭal vallirō
Are you capable of dancing and singing?
colum
please tell us.

{$} āraṇīyaviṭaṅkarē
Civaṉ who has name of Āraṇiya viṭaṅkar!
mattam māmalark koṉṟai vaṉṉiyum kaṅkaiyāḷoṭu tiṅkaḷum moytta veṇtalai (1) kokkiṟaḵkoṭu veḷḷerukkam um caṭaiyatām
dātura flowers, big koṉṟai flowers, leaves of indian mesquit, Kaṅkai who is in the form of a lady, crescent, crowded white skulls, flowers resembling feathers of crane, white yarcum flowers are in your caṭai
pattar cittarkaḷ pāṭi āṭum paiññīliyēṉ eṉṟu niṟṟir
you stand before us saying I am a resident of Paiññīli where pious people and mystic persons sing and dance out of joy
atti īruripōrttirō
Do you cover yourself with the skin of an elephant wet with blood?
colum
please tell us.

{$} āraṇiyaviṭaṅkarē
Civaṉ who bears the name of Āraṇiya viṭaṅkar!
takkai, taṇṇumai, tāḷam, vīṇai, takuṇiccam, kiṇai, callari, kokkarai, kuṭamuḻaviṉōṭu icai kūṭippāṭi niṉṟu āṭuvīr
you dance for a long time singing in time with the music produced in kuṭamuḻa, kokkarai, callari, kiṇai, takuṇiccam, viṇai, cymbals, taṇṇumai and takkai
[[From takkai to kuṭamuḻā these are musical instruments played as accompaniment to vocal music]]
pakkamē kuyil pāṭum cōlaip paiññīliyēṉ eṉṟu niṟṟir
You stand before us saying, I am a resident of Paiññīli which has gardens in which indian cuckoos sing in different sides
akkum āmaiyum pūṇṭirō
Have you adorned yourself with bones and the shell of a tortoise?
colum
please tell us.

{$} āraṇīyaviṭaṅkarē
Civaṉ who has the name of Āraṇiya viṭaṅkar!
kai ōr pāmpu
having cobra in the hand.
arai ārttu ōr pāmpu
tying a cobra in the waist
kaḻuttu ōr pāmpu
and having a cobra in the neck
avai piṉpu tāḻ mey elām poṭik koṇṭu pūcutir
you besmear the whole of the body in which they hang low in the back, with holy ash.
vētam ōtutir
you chant the vetams
kītamum paiyavē viṭaṅkāka niṉṟu paiññīliyēṉ eṉṟir
you said, I am a resident of Paiññīli' standing before us singing the music composition also slowly and beautifully.
aiyam ēṟkumitu eṉ kol
what is the reason for begging alms?
colum
please tell us.

{$} aṉṉam cēr vayalcūḻ paiññīliyil āraṇīya viṭaṅkarai
addressing Āraṇiya vitaṅkar in Paiññīli surrounded by fields in which swans flock
miṉṉum nuṇṇiṭai maṅkaimārpalar vēṇṭik kātal moḻintacol
the words expressing love wishing it by many young ladies whose waists are as slender as the lightning that flashes across the sky.
maṉṉu tolpukaḻ nāvalūraṉ vaṉtoṇṭaṉ vāymoḻi pāṭalpattu uṉṉi
remembering the ten verses that came from the mouth of Vaṉṟoṇtaṉ a native of Nāvalur which has undying ancient fame.
iṉicai pāṭuvār umaikēḷvaṉ cēvaṭicērvarē
those who will sing with sweet music will reach ultimately the red feet of Civaṉ, the husband of Umai.

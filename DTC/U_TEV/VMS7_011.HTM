tiruuṭaiyār
Civaṉ has the wealth of eternal bliss.
tirumāl ayaṉālum uru uṭaiyār
has in his form Tirumāl and Ayaṉ
[[ENCYCL: This manifestation of Civaṉ is known as Trimūrti Ēkapāṭar]]
umaiyāḷai ōrpākam parivu uṭaiyār
desires on one half Umiyāḷ.
(1) aṭaivār viṉai tīrkkum purivu uṭaiyār uṟai pūvaṇam ītō
is this shrine Pūvaṇam where he who removes with desire all the acts of those who seek refuge in him?
[[This is in the form of questioning the devotees, out of joy. This will apply to the following verses also.]]
[[LOCAL: aṭaivār viṉai tīrppār' is the name of Civaṉ in this shrine.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṭaiyā]]

eṇṇiyiruntum kiṭantum naṭantum aṇṇal eṉā ni*ṣa*vārviṉai tīr/ppār
Civaṉ will remove the acts of those who esteem him as the foremost among gods having decided, whether standing, lying or walking.
[[cf. niṉṟum iruntum kiṭantum niṉaimiṉkaḷ, eṉṟum civaṉtāḷ iṇai (varuttam aṟa uyyum vaḻi);
niṉṟum iruntum kiṭantum naṭantum yām, eṉṟum niṉaintālum eṉkolō ceṉṟutaṉ tāṉvāṉavar iṟaiñcum taṇcāral kāḷatti, āḷvāṉ aruḷātavāṟu (Kayilipāti kāḷatti pāti antāti, 42);
niṉṟālum iruntālum kiṭantālum naṭantālum, meṉṟālum tuyiṉṟālum viḻittālumi imaittālum, maṉṟāṭum malarppātam orukālum maṟavāmai, kuṉṟāta uṇarvuṭaiyār toṇṭarām kuṇam mikkār (periya puraṇam, pattarippaṇivār puraṇam, 7)]]
paṇ icai ār moḻiyār palar pāṭap puṇṇiyaṉār uṟai pūvaṇam ītō
is this shrine pūvāṇam where the god who is the embodiment of all virtues dwells, and where many ladies whose words are like melody-types, sing his prises?

teḷḷiya pēypala pūtam avaṟṟoṭu naḷḷiruḷ naṭṭam (atu) āṭal naviṉṟōr
Civaṉ who was practising dance at midnight along with the pēy and many pūtams which have a clear idea about Civaṉ's supremacy.
puḷḷuvar ākumavarkku avartāmum puḷḷuvaṉār uṟai pūvaṇam ītō
is this shrine pūvanam where Civaṉ himself practises deceit towards deceitful persons?

nilaṉ uṭai(ya)māṉ maṟi kaiyatu
a young dear which lives on the earth is in Civaṉ's hand.
teyvakkaṉal uṭai(ya) māmaḻu ēnti
holding a red-hot big iron which has a fire of divine nature.
ōr kaiyil aṉal uṭaiyār
has in another hand fire.
aḻaku ārceṉṉip puṉal uṭaiyār uṟai pūvaṇam ītō
is this shrine pūvaṇam where the god who has water on his beautiful head?

naṭai uṭai(ya) nalerutu ēṟuvar
Civaṉ rides on a good bull which has a quick git.
nallār kaṭaikaṭaitōṟu iṭumiṉpali eṉpār
will say, going to every entrance of the houses of the ladies, gime alms
tuṭiiṭai nalmaṭavāḷoṭu mārpil poṭi aṇivār uṟai pūvaṇam ītō
is this shrine pūvaṇam where the god who smears on his chest sacred ash to appear beautiful, being united with a lady of good nature, who has a waist like the middle portion of a drum tapering in the middle?

(1) miṉ aṉaiyāḷ tirumēṉi viḷaṅka ōr taṉ amar pākamatu ākiya caṅkaraṉ
Civaṉ who does good, has placed on one half of his holy body a lady who is as bright as the lightning to be eminent (miṉṉammi is the name of the goddess in this shrine).
(2) muṉniṉaiyār puram mūṉṟu eri ūṭṭiya poṉ aṉaiyāṉ uṟai pūvaṇam ītō
is this shrine puvaṇam where Civaṉ who is as bright as gold and who set fire to the three cities of the acurar who did not think of him?
[[Variant reading: (1) muṉṉaṉaiyār (2) muṉṉiṉaivār]]

Irāvaṇaṉ who was on the end of his pride thinking of his superiorty only wanted to throw the mountin, Kayili by lifting it.
nakku

cīriṉ mikappoliyum tiruppūvaṇam
in tiruppūvaṇam which is very eminent by its fame.
viruppu āra iṭamāka uṟaivāṉtaṉai
on Civaṉ who dwells as his place to the complete satisfaction of his desire.
ūraṉ uraitta colmālaikaḷ pattu (ivai) pāril uraippavar pāvam aṟuppar(ē)
those who recite these ten verses which are garlands of words composed by ūraṉ, in this world, will root out sins.

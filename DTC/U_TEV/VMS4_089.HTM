nīr aṭainta karai niṉṟa neyttāṉattu iruntavaṉ
Civaṉ who was in Neyttāṉam which is on the bank close to water
pār iṭam cāṭiya pal uyir, vāṉ amararkku aruḷi
Having compassion on the immortals in heaven and the group of acurar who destroyed the living beings in the world.
kār aṭainta kaṭalvāy umiḻnañcu amutāka uṇṭāṉ
consumed the poison that was spat by the snake, vācuki, in the black-coloured ocean as he would consume nectar.

tēyntu ilaṅkum ciṟuveṇmatiyāy
Civaṉ who wears a small white crescent which is dwindling in size and yet shining!
niṉ tiruccaṭaimēl pāynta kaṅkaip puṉal palmukamākipparantu oḷippa
the water of the kaṅki which poured on your holy caṭi to make a sound and spread itself by forming many branches.
1. āyntu ilaṅkum maḻuvēl uṭaiyāy
you who have a weapon of a battle-axe shining being sharp!
ēntu iḷa maṅkaiyum nīyum neyttāṉattu iruntatu aṭiyēṟku nī urai
you please tell the reason for you and the very young lady of distinction to reside in neyttāṉam, to me who am your devotee

koṉṟu aṭaintu āṭikkumaittiṭum kūṟṟam oṉṉār matilmēl ceṉṟu aṭaintu āṭip poruttum tēcam ellām aṟiyum
The whole world knows well how the god of death went on the forts of the enemies and fought displaying its skill which afflicts living beings by taking out their lives, and gins victory.
kuṉṟu aṭaintu āṭum kuḷir poḻil kāviriyiṉ karaimēl neyttāṉattu iruntavaṉ ceṉṟu aṭaintār viṉai tīrkkum
Civaṉ who is in neyttāṉam on the bank of the kaviri, which has cool gardens, and which plays with its waves with mounds of sand on its way, will remove the sins of those who go there and approach him.

when the mulavu that is beaten makes a sound
palakōlam aṇintu
adorning himself with many costumes
nākam araikku acaittu naṭṭam pala payiṉṟu āṭuvār
will dance, without stopping, many kinds of dances, fastening a cobra in his waist.

iṭṭam umaiyoṭum neyttāṉattu iruntavaṉ
is he who is residing in neyttāṉam with affection towards Umi.

koym malark koṉṟai tuḻāy vaṉṉi mattamum kūviḷamum meymmalar vēynta viricaṭaik kaṟṟai viṇṇōr perumāṉ
ivaṉ who is the chief of the celestials, who has a loosened collection of caṭi on which koṉṟi flowers recently plucked, leaves of sacred basil, leaves of Indian mesquit, datura flowers, leaves of ball and truth (one of the eight flowers) are put on!
maimmalar nīla niṟak karuṅkaṇṇi ōrpāl makiḻntāṉ
rejoiced in having on one haf a lady with black eyes which resemble the blue nelumbo flowers.
niṉ malaṉ
the spotless god
āṭal nilayam neyttāṉattu iruntavaṉ
dwelt in neyttāṉam which is the stage for performing dance.

Civaṉ apppeared very beautiful by adorning on his caṭi receiving the garland of koṉṟi which blossoms like garlands, (from devotees)

paṟṟiṉa pāmpaṉ
Civaṉ is holding in his hand a cobra (pāmpaip paṟṟiyavaṉ -: the termination is detached from one word and joined to another word; this is known as vikuti pirittukkūṭṭal in grammer)
paṭutta puliyurit tōluṭaiyaṉ
has a dress of tiger's skin which was killed and flayed.
muṟṟiṉa mūṉṟu matilkaḷai mūṭṭi erittu aṟuttāṉ
destroyed by setting fire by kindling it to the three forts which were at the height of cruelty.
cuṟṟiya pūtap paṭaiyiṉaṉ
has an army of pūtam surrounding him
cūlam maḻu oru māṉ neyttāṉattiruntavaṉ
god who is in neyttāṉam and who has a trident, a battle-axe and a deer
ceṟṟu nam tīvīṉai tīrntiṭum
will remove our sins by destroying them.

viritta caṭaiyiṉaṉ
ivaṉ has a loosened caṭi
viṇṇavarkōṉ
the Chief of the celestials
viṭamuṇṭakaṇṭaṉ
has a neck that consumed poison
uritta kari uri mūṭi
covering himself with the skin of an elephant he flayed.
oṉṉār matil mūṉṟu uṭaṉē eritta cilaiyiṉaṉ
burnt with a bow simultaneously the three forts of enemies
īṭu aḻiyātu eṉai āṇṭu koṇṭa, taritta umaiyavaḷōṭum neyattāṉattu iruntavaṉ
Civaṉ who was in the company of Umayavaḷ who supported all worlds, and who admitted me into his grace, without suffering any loss of dignity (koṇṭa iruntavaṉ: is the syntactical link; taritta qualities Umayavaḷ)

tūṅkāṉ
Civaṉ is not slow in granting his grace
tuḷaṅkāṉ
is not perturbed
tuḻāykoṉṟai tuṉṉiya ceñcaṭaimēl vāṅkā matiyamum vāḷ aravum kaṅkai (tāṉ) puṉaintāṉ
adorned on his caṭi in which basil leaves, flower of koṉṟi which are close together, a crescent that does not increase in size, cruel cobra and Kaṅki
tēṅkār tiripuram tī eḻa eytu tiyakku aṟuttu
Having completely removed the bewilderment of the celestials by shooting an arrow on the three cities of the acurar who were not in trepidation; to blaze with fire.
neyttāṉattu umaiyavaḷōṭum iruntavaṉ nīṅkāṉ
Civaṉ who is in Neyttāṉam with Umiyavaḷ will not leave that shrine.

poruvāṉil amum matilum ampiṉāl tī ūṭṭi niṉṟāṉ māṭṭi niṉṟāṉ
Civaṉ destroyed by setting fire to the three forts by an arrow in the sky where the battle took place.
aṉṟiṉār ventu vīḻavum vāṉavarkkuk kāṭṭi niṉṟāṉ
showed to the celestials, the enemies burning and falling down.
kata mākkaṅkai pāya ōr caṭaiyai nīṭṭi niṉṟāṉ tiruniṉṟa neyttāṉattu iruntavaṉ
Civaṉ in neyttāṉam where weath is permanent stretched out a single caṭi to contin kaṅki which gushed with great anger.

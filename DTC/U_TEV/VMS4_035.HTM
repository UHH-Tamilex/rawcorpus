{4:35}__1+
{$}
kāṭu uṭaic cuṭalai nīṟṟar
Civaṉ smears himself with the ash in the cremation ground which has the nature of the forest.
kaiyil veṇ talaiyar
has in his hand a white skull
taiyal pāṭu uṭaip pūtam cūḻp paramaṉār
the supreme god who is surrounded by pūtam-s and who has on one half a young lady.
marutavaippil
in the agricultural tract.
tōṭu uṭaikkai taiyōṭu cūḻ kiṭaṅku ataṉai cūḻnta ēṭu uṭaikkamala vēli iṭai marutu iṭam koṇṭār
occupied as his residence Iṭimarutu surrounded on all sides as a fence, lotus flowers of petals which surrounds it girting the moat with the fragrant screw-pine of big petals.
[[PP: ēruṭaik kamalam ōṅkum iṭaimarutu (3)]]
{4:35}__2+
{$}
muntaiyār
Civaṉ is primordial.
munti uḷḷār
existed before every thing came into existence
mūvarkku mutalvar ār
is the master of the trinity of Piramaṉ, Māl and Uruttiraṉ.
cantiyār (1) canti uḷḷār
he is in the meditation of those who perform worship of cantiyā [?= san-dhyā (jlc)] and is the cantiyā itself.
tavaneṟi tarittu niṉṟār
takes upon himself the path of doing penance.
civa neṟi aṉaittum āṉār
is all the paths that bestow on one auspiciousness.
entaiyār
is our father
empirāṉār
is our master
iṭaimarutu iṭam koṇṭār
occupied iṭimarutu as his residence.
[[Variant reading: (1) anti]]
{4:35}__3+
{$}
kār uṭaik koṉṟai mālai katir maṇi araviṉōṭu nīr uṭaic (1) caṭaiyuḷ vaitta nītiyār
Civaṉ has the nature of placing inside the caṭi which has water (of the kaṅki) a garland of koṉṟi flowers which blossom in the winter together with a cobra which has a jewel emitting rays.
nīti āyapōr uṭai viṭai oṉṟu ēṟavallavar
is able to ride on a bull which can give a fair battle
poṉṉit teṉpāl ēr uṭaik kamalam ōṅkum iṭaimarutu iṭam koṇṭār
occupied as his residence iṭimarutu which has beautiful lotus flowers in abundance, on the southern bank of Poṉṉi.
[[Variant reading: (1) caṭaiyil]]
{4:35}__4+
{$}
viṇṇiṉār
Civaṉ has the sky as his place
viṇṇiṉ mikkār
has a place superior to, and above, the sky.
vētaṅkaḷ nāṉkum aṅkam paṇṇiṉār
is the author of the four vētam-s and six aṅkam-s.
paṇṇiṉ mikka pāṭalār
has songs which are rich in music
[[he chanted cāmavētam which has music.]]
pāvam tīrkkum kaṇṇiṉār
has the idea of removing the sins of devotees
kaṇṇiṉ mikka (1) nutaliṉār
has a forehead which is superior, by its eye.
kāmaṟ kāynta eṇṇiṉār
is the yōki who burnt Kāmaṉ
eṇṇiṉ mikka iṭaimarutu iṭam koṇṭār
occupied Iṭimarutu which is esteemed greatly, as his residence
[[Variant reading: (1) nutaliṉāṟ.]]
{4:35}__5+
{$}
vētaṅkaḷ nāṉkum koṇṭu viṇṇavar paravi ētta
the celestials worshipped and prised Civaṉ with the help of the vētam-s.
pūtaṅkaḷ pāṭi āṭal uṭaiyavaṉ
has the songs and dances of pūtam-s.
punitaṉ entai
is the spotless god, our father.
pātaṅkaḷ paravi niṉṟa pattarkaḷ taṅkaḷ mēlai ētaṅkaḷ tīra niṉṟār iṭaimarutu iṭam koṇṭār
occupied iṭimarutu as his residence; stood permanently to remove the blemishes of the previous births of the pious people who prise his feet.
{4:35}__6+
{$}
poṟi aravu araiyil ārttu
having tied in the waist a cobra of spots.
pūtaṅkaḷpalavum cūḻa
to surrounded by many pūtams.
muṟitaru vaṉṉi koṉṟai mutircaṭai mūḻka vaittu
having placed hidden in the caṭi which has grown to its full size, koṉṟi, indian mesquit which has leaves.
maṟitaru kaṅkai taṅkavait tavar
placed the kaṅki rolling upside down, to stay in the caṭi
etticaiyum eṟitaru puṉal koḷvēli iṭaimarutu iṭam koṇṭār
occupied iṭimarutu which has on all sides water that throws waves, as his residence.
{4:35}__7+
{$}
oḷipaṭar caṭaiyiṉuḷḷāl
inside the caṭi which is spreading its brightness.
pāypuṉal araviṉōṭu cuṭar oḷi matiyam vaittu
placing the crescent of shining light along with the cobra and flowing water.
tū oḷi tōṉṟum entai
ou father who appears as the pure knowledge.
aṭar oḷi viṭai oṉṟu ēṟavallār
is able to ride upon a bull of thick brightness.
aṉpartaṅkaḷ iṭar avai keṭavum niṉṟār iṭaimarutu iṭamkoṇṭār
occupied iṭimarutu as his residence, and stood to remove the afflictions and to increase the happiness of his devotees.
[[By the um in keṭavum the increase of happiness is also included.]]
{4:35}__8+
{$}
kamaḻtaru caṭaiyiṉuḷḷāl kaṭumpuṉal araviṉōṭu tavaḻtaru matiyam vaittu
placing inside the fragrant caṭi swift-flowing water, cobra and a crawling crescent.
taṉ aṭi palarum ētta
many people to worship his feet.
maḻu (atu) valaṅkai ēnti
holding a battle-axe in the right hand.
mātu orupākam āki
having a lady on one half
eḻil taru poḻilkaḷ cūḻnta iṭaimarutu iṭamkoṇṭārē
occupied iṭimarutu surrounded by gardens which add beauty as his residence.
{4:35}__9+
{$}
poṉtikaḻ koṉṟai mālai putuppuṉal vaṉṉi mattam miṉ tikaḻ caṭaiyil vaittu
keeping in the caṭi shining like the lightning a garland of koṉṟi which is golden in colour, fresh water, indian mesquit and dhatura flowers.
mētakat tōṉṟukiṉṟa
and appearing excellent
[[tōṉṟukiṉṟa nīṇṭār is the syntactical link]]
aṉṟu avar aḷakkal ākā aṉal eri āki nīṇṭār
Civaṉ shot up high as a buring fire which could not be measured by those two persons in the distant past.
[[avar: neñcu aṟi cuṭṭu]]
iṉṟu uṭaṉ ulakam ētta iṭaimarutu iṭam koṇṭār
occupied as his residence iṭimarutu to be prised by the people of this world along with those two, at the present time.

matiilā arakkaṉ
when the arakkaṉ without sense,
malaiyuṭaṉ viravi niṉṟu
having approached the mountin (kayili)
nūkka
shook it.
talaiyuṭaṉ aṭarttu
pressing down the heads at the same time.
mīṇṭē talaivaṉā aruḷkaḷ nalki
bestowing his grace agin, to make him chief.
[[aruḷkaḷ: the plural is intended to denote that Civaṉ gave him a sword, long life and a title]]
cilaiyuṭai malaiyai vāṅkit tiripuram mūṉṟum eytār
shot an arrow bending the mountin which he used as a bow, on the wandering three cities.
[[tiripuram is to be taken as viṉaittokai as it is followed by mūṉṟum]]
ilaiyuṭaik kamala vēli iṭaimarutu iṭam koṇṭār
Civaṉ occupied Iṭimarutu which is surrounded on all sides like a fence by lotus which has a big leaf.

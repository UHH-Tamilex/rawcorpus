*2

_ēṟu _aṇi _aṭikaḷtam _iṭam---valampuramē.

*4

_eṅkaḷatu _aṭikaḷ nal _iṭam---valampuramē.

*5

paṭumaṇi muttamum pavaḷamum mikac cumantu
_iṭu maṇal _aṭikari _iṭam---valampuramē.

*6

maruṅkoṭu, valampuri calañcalam maṇam puṇarntu
_iruṅkaṭal _aṇikari _iṭam---valampuramē.

*7

_eri _eri_āṭitaṉ _iṭam---valampuramē.

*8

_ēṟu _uṭi _aṭikaḷtam _iṭam---valampuramē.

*9

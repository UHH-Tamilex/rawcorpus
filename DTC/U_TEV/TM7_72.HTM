*1
_eṉakku _iṉit tiṉaittaṉaip pukal_iṭam _aṟintēṉ;
paṉaik kaṉipaḻam paṭum paravaiyiṉ karaimēl---
_eṉakku _iṉiyavaṉ, tamarkku _iṉiyavaṉ, _eḻumaiyum
maṉakku _iṉiyavaṉtaṉatu _iṭam---valampuramē.

*2
puram_avai _eritara vaḷainta villiṉaṉ, _avaṉ;
mara_uri puli_ataḷ _araimicai maruviṉaṉ;
_ara_uri _irantavaṉ, _irantu _uṇa virumpi niṉṟu;
_iravu _eri_āṭitaṉ _iṭam---valampuramē.

*3
nīṟu _aṇi mēṉiyaṉ, neruppu _umiḻ _araviṉaṉ,
kūṟu _aṇi komumaḻu _ēnti(ya) _or kaiyiṉaṉ,
_āṟu _aṇi _avircaṭai _aḻal vaḷar maḻalai veḷ-
_ēṟu _aṇi _aṭikaḷtam _iṭam---valampuramē.

*4
koṅku _aṇai curumpu _uṇa, neruṅkiya kuḷir _iḷan
teṅkoṭu paṉai paḻam paṭum _iṭam; tēvarkaḷ
taṅkiṭum _iṭam; taṭaṅkaṭal-tirai puṭaitara
_eṅkaḷatu _aṭikaḷ nal _iṭam---valampuramē.

*5
koṭumaḻu virakiṉaṉ, kolai mali cilaiyiṉaṉ,
neṭumatil ciṟumaiyiṉ nirava vallavaṉ, _iṭam;
paṭumaṇi muttamum pavaḷamum mikac cumantu
_iṭu maṇal _aṭaikarai _iṭam---valampuramē.

*6
karuṅkaṭakkaḷiṟṟu _urik kaṭavuḷatu _iṭam; kayal
neruṅkiya neṭumpe(ṇ)ṇai_aṭumpoṭu viraviya
maruṅkoṭu, valampuri calañcalam maṇam puṇarntu
_iruṅkaṭal _aṇaikarai _iṭam---valampuramē.

*7
nari puri kāṭu _araṅkā naṭam_āṭuvar,
vari puri pāṭa niṉṟu _āṭum _emmāṉ, _iṭam;
puricurivarikuḻal _arivai _orpāl makiḻntu
_eri _eri_āṭitaṉ _iṭam---valampuramē.

*8
pāṟu _aṇi muṭaitalai kalaṉ _eṉa maruviya,
nīṟu _aṇi, nimircaṭaimuṭiyiṉaṉ; nilaviya
māṟu _aṇi varu tirai vayal _aṇi poḻilatu,
_ēṟu _uṭai _aṭikaḷtam _iṭam---valampuramē.

*9
caṭacaṭa viṭu pe(ṇ)ṇai paḻam paṭum _iṭa vakai;
paṭa vaṭakattoṭu pala kalantu _ulaviya
kaṭaikaṭai pali tiri kapālitaṉ _iṭam_atu;
_iṭi karai maṇal _aṭai _iṭam---valampuramē.

*10
kuṇṭikaip paṭappiṉil viṭakkiṉai _oḻittavar
kaṇṭavar, kaṇṭu _aṭi vīḻntavar, kaṉaikaḻal
taṇṭu _uṭait taṇṭitaṉ _iṉam _uṭai _ara_uṭaṉ
_eṇticaikku _oru cuṭar _iṭam---valampuramē.

*11
varum kalamum pala pēṇutal, karuṅkaṭal,
_iruṅ kulap piṟappartam _iṭam valampurattiṉai,
_aruṅ kulattu _aruntamiḻ _ūraṉ---vaṉtoṇṭaṉ---col

{6:40}__1+
{$} alaiaṭutta peruṅkaṭal nañcu amutā uṇṭu
Having consumed the poison that rose in the ocean in which there are waves, as nectar.
amararkaḷ tamtalaikātta aiyar
the god of respectability who saved the lives of the immortals.
(1)cempoṉcilai eṭuttu mānākam neruppuk kōttut tiripuraṅkaḷ tī iṭṭa celvar (pōlum)
the god who set fire to the three cities by taking the bow of Mēru (golden mountin) firing the great cobra as string, and fire as the tip of the arrow.
[[pōlum expletive]]
nilai aṭutta pacumpoṉṉāl muttāl nīṇṭa nirai vayirappalakaiyāl (2) kuvai ārttu uṟṟa malai aṭutta maḻapāṭi vayirattūṇē eṉṟu eṉṟu nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉ
I forget myself shouting with excitement often Pillar of diamond in Maḻapāṭi which is like a mountin, which is shining, being made by the heaps of slabs of diamond of many long rows, pearls and pure gold of fine quality.
[[Variant reading: (1) celvic (2) kuvaiyāttuṟṟa]]
[[Vaccirattampaṇātar is the name for Civaṉ in this shrine]]
{6:40}__2+
{$} aṟai kalanta kuḻal montai vīṇaiyāḻum antarattil kantaruvar amarar, maṟaikalanta mantiramum nīrumkoṇṭu vaḻipaṭṭār ētta vāṉ āḷak koṭutti (aṉṟē-
you granted the rule over the heaven for the immortals and Kantaruvar living in heaven who worshipped with mantiram contined in the Vētam, and water, playing upon the flute of sweet sound, and monti, vīni, and lute and prised you
[[monti: a musical instrument]]
kaṟaikalanta poḻiṟkaccikkampam mēya kaṉavayirattiraḷ tūṇē kalicūḻ māṭam maṟaikalanta maḻapāṭi vayirattūṇē eṉṟu eṉṟu nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉ
I forget myself by shouting with excitement often, The heavy and globular pillar of diamond that is in Kampam in Kacci which is joined by sable clouds! the pillar of diamond in Maḻapāṭi where Vētam-s are chanted in the storeys having great bustle.
{6:40}__3+
{$} uram koṭukkum iruḷmeyyar mūrkkar pollā ūttai vāyc camaṇartamai uṟavākkoṇṭa param keṭuttu
Destroying the load of sins having been associated in a friendly manner with camaṇar of filthy mouths, wicked and obstinate persons, who have dark bodies which are made strong by nutritious foods.
iṅku aṭiyēṉai āṇṭukoṇṭa pavaḷattiṉtiraḷtūṇē
the globular pillar made of coral who admitted me, your serf, into your grace in this world!
pacumpoṉ muttē
pearls which are set in gold plates!
puram keṭuttu
destroying the cities
pollātakāmaṉ ākam poṭiyāka viḻittaruḷi
Having opened your frontal eye to reduce to ashes the body of the mischievous Kāmaṉ.
puviyōrkku eṉṟum varamkoṭukkum maḻapāṭivayirattūṇē eṉṟu eṉṟu nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉ
I forget myself by shouting with excitement often the pillar made of diamond in Maḻapāṭi which grants boons to the people of this world!
{6:40}__4+
{$} ūṉ ikanta ūṇ uṟikkaiyar kuṇṭar pollā ūttaivāyccamaṇar uṟavākakkoṇṭu
Being intimately associated with the camaṇar having filth accumulated in their teeth, who are wicked and low, who carry a network of ropes to keep water-pots suspended from the hand and who have eschewed meat from their food.
[[ikanta: the a in the participle has been dropped.]]
ñāṉa akam cērntu uḷḷa vayirattai naṇṇā nāyēṉaip poruḷāka āṇṭukoṇṭa, mīṉ akam cēr veḷḷa nīr vitiyāṟcūṭum vēntaṉē
the King who bears on the head the floods (of Kaṅki) which has fish, who admitted into your grace your devotee, who is as low as a dog thinking to be of any importance and who did not approach you who are like a diamond which dwells in the minds of people having spiritual illumination'
[[ñāṉa akamhave become ñāṉakam]]
viṇṇavartam perumāṉē
the chief of the celestials!
mēkavāṉakam cēr maḻapāṭi vayirattūṇē eṉṟu eṉṟu nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉ
I forget myself by shouting with excitement often, a pillar made of diamond in Maḻapāṭi where the mansions touch the sky where clouds are staying!
{6:40}__5+
{$} ciram ēṟṟa nāṉmukaṉtaṉ talaiyum maṟṟait tirumāltaṉ ceḻuntalaiyum poṉṟac cinti
Having destroyed to perish the head of Nāṉmuḵaṉ (Piramaṉ) who had many heads and the graceful head of Tirumāl.
uram ēṟṟa iravipal takarttu
knocking out the teeth of the sun who had great strength
cōmaṉ (1) oḷirkalaikaḷ paṭa uḻakki
trampling down under the feet the shinning phases of the moon, to perish.
uyirainalki
granting their lives.
naraiēṟṟaviṭai ēṟi
riding on a white bull.
nākam pūṇṭa nampiyaiyē maṟaināṉkum ōlam iṭṭu varam ēṟkum maḻapāṭi vayirattūṇē eṉṟu eṉṟu nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉ
I forgot myself by shouting with excitement often about the elite among gods who wears as ornaments cobras, and the pillar made of diamond in Maḻapāṭi from whom all the four vētams receive boons by appealing for protection.
[[Variant reading: (1) oḷikalaikaḷ]]
{6:40}__6+
{$} ciṉamtiruttum ciṟupperiyārkuṇṭartaṅkaḷ cetumatiyār tīviṉaikkē viḻuntēṉ
Having sunk low in the sins of evil-minded low persons, who are great people possessing meanness and who have given up anger
[[ciṉam tiruttum: the underlined word is used ironically, ciṟupperiyār usually this word is used in the sense of great persons even at a young age; here it is used as big people having low qualities.]]
tēṭip puṉam tiruttum pollāta piṇṭipēṇum poṟiyiliyēṉtaṉaip poruḷā āṇṭukoṇṭu
having admitted as his protege as a worthy thing, who had no wisdom and cherished with love the evil asoka tree which grows in the forest region converting it to be suitable for growing that tree.
taṉam tiruttum avar tiṟattai oḻiyappāṟṟi
and having removed me away from the group of women and adorn their breasts.
tayāmūlataṉmavaḻi eṉakku nalki
granting me with grace the path of the righteousness which has compassion on all being as its base.
maṉam tiruttum maḻapāṭi vayirattūṇē eṉṟu eṉṟu nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉ
I forget myself shouting with excitement often pillar made of diamond which is in Maḻapāṭi and which corrected and reformed my mind!
{6:40}__7+
{$} cuḻittuṇaiyām piṟavi vaḻittukkam nīkkum tūya teṇṇīrc (1) curuḷ caṭai em perumāṉē
our god who has a curly caṭi which holds pure and clear water and who removes the sorrow which comes in the wake of births which are comparable to whirlpools!
iḻippu ariya pacu pācappiṟappai nīkkum eṉ tuṇaiyē
my companion who removes the birth which is the result of being a living being with attachments, and which load cannot be known off by my own skill.
eṉṉuṭaiyapemmāṉ
my great Lord!
tammāṉ
the chief of all living beings;
[[tammāṉaiyaṟiyāta cātiyār uḷarō (Cuntarar, Tiruvatiki Vīraṭṭam, 1)]]
paḻippu ariya tirumālum ayaṉum kāṇāp parutiyē
the effulgent light like the sun which could not be found out by Tirumāl and Ayaṉ who are without reproach!
curutimuṭikku aṇiyāy vāytta vaḻittuṇaiyām maḻapāṭi vayirattūṇē eṉṟu eṉṟu nāṉ araṟṟi naikiṉṟēṉ
I forget myself by shouting with excitement often as the pillar made of diamond in Maḻapāṭi which is the escort and guide, and which is the ornament suited to the upanishads

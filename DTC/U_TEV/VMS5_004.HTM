{5:4}__1+
{$} vaṭṭaṉai(m) maticūṭiyai
Civaṉ who has a dress called vaṭṭu and wears a crescent.
[[vaṭṭuṭai:
muḻantāḷ aḷavāka vīrar uṭukkum uṭai vicēṭam (cīvakacintāmaṇi 468, nacciṉārkkiṉiyar)
ikanta vaṭṭuṭai (maṇimēkali, 3-122);
vaṭṭuṭaip polinta vaṇṇak kalāpamoṭu, paṭṭuc cumantu acainta paravai alkul .... vācavatattaiyai (perunkati, 2-4-122-129); cloth tied round the waist and reaching down the knee]]
vāṉavar ciṭṭaṉai
the superior one to the celestials.
tiruvaṇṇāmalaiyaṉai
the god in Tiruvaṇṇāmali.
iṭṭaṉai
who is the endeared god
ikaḻntatār puram mūṉṟaiyum aṭṭaṉai aṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
shall I be saved if I forget the god who destroys all the three cities of the enemies?
[[Note: that it is enough to attin salvation if one thinks of Aṇṇāmali is the belief of civites]]
[[The destruction of the three cities is mentioned in every verse of this decade]]
{5:4}__2+
{$} vāṉaṉai
Civaṉ who dwells in the great cosmic space.
maticūṭiya maintaṉai
the strong god who wore a crescent
tēṉaṉai
who is as sweet as the honey.
tiruvaṇṇāmalaiyaṉai
who is in Tiruvaṇṇāmali.
ēṉaṉai
who wears a hog's tusk.
ikaḻntār puram mūṉṟu eyta āṉaṉai aṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
shall I be saved if I, his slave, forget him who rides on a bull and shot an arrow on the three cities of the enemies?
{5:4}__3+
{$} mattaṉai
Civaṉ who wears datura flowers.
matayāṉai uritta em cittaṉai
who dwells in our minds and who flayed an elephant of must.
tiruvaṇṇāmalaiyaṉai
who is in Tiruvaṇṇāmali.
muttaṉai
who is naturally free from all kinds of bondage.
muṉintār puram mūṉṟu eyta attaṉai aṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
shall I who is his slave be saved if I forget the father who destroyed by discharging an arrow on the three cities of the enemies?
{5:4}__4+
{$} kāṟṟaṉai
Civaṉ who is the air.
kalakkum viṉaipōy aṟattēṟṟaṉai
who bestows clarity to be free from the acts that confuse ones mind.
tiruvaṇṇāmalaiyaṉai
who is in Tiruvaṇṇāmali.
kūṟṟaṉai
who classifies the world into two portions as good and bad.
koṭiyār puram mūṉṟu eyta āṟṟaṉai aṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
shall I, his slave, be saved if I forget him who is the religious path and who destroyed the cities of the cruel persons, by discharging an arrow?
{5:4}__5+
{$} miṉṉaṉai
Civaṉ who has a luminous body like the lightning.
viṉaitīrttu eṉai āṭkoṇṭa teṉṉaṉai
who is the beautiful god who admitted me as his slave removing all my sins.
[[teṉ-aḻaku: beauty]]
tiruvaṇṇāmalaiyaṉai
who is in Tiruvaṇṇāmali
eṉṉaṉai
who is mine.
ikaḻntār puram mūṉṟu eyta aṉṉaṉai aṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
shall I, his slave, be saved if I forget the god who cannot be described in so many words and who destroyed the three cities of the enemies?
{5:4}__6+
{$} maṉṟaṉai
Civaṉ who dances in the five halls.
matiyātavaṉ vēḷvimēl ceṉṟaṉai
who went angrily to the sacrifice of Takkaṉ who did not esteem him, and destroyed it.
tiruvaṇṇāmalaiyaṉai
who is in Tiruvaṇṇāmali.
veṉṟaṉai
who controlled the five senses and gined victory over them.
vekuṇṭār puram mūṉṟaiyum koṉṟaṉaik koṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
shall I, who is cruel by nature, be saved if I forget him who destroyed all the three cities of those enemies who were angry with him?
{5:4}__7+
{$} vīraṉai
Civaṉ who performed valourous acts.
viṭam uṇṭaṉai
who consumed the poison.
viṇṇavar tīraṉai
who removes the fears of the celestials
[[tīraṉai may also mean the valiant god among the celestials; in the former meaning the root is tīr: tīraṉai is derived from that]]
tiruvaṇṇāmalaiyaṉai
who is in Tiruvaṇṇāmali.
ūraṉai
who dwells in the agricultural tract.
uṇarārpuram mūṉṟu eyta āraṉai aṭiyēṉ maṟantu uyvaṉō
shall I, who is his slave, be saved if I forget him who wears holy mountin ebony and destroyed the three cities of the enemies who did not know his greatness?

[[pēr aravam pūṇattiruttiya poṅku oḷiyōṉ (Kōviyur, 215)]]

karuttaṉai

{1:82}__1+
{$} irumpoṉ malaivillā eri ampā nāṇil tirinta puram mūṉṟum ceṟṟāṉ uṟai kōyil
the temple where Civaṉ who destroyed the three wandering cities, having as a bow the precious golden mountain, Meru, and fixing in the bow-string fire as an arrow.
terinta aṭiyārkaḷ ceṉṟa ticaitōṟum virumpi etir koḷvār vīḻimiḻalaiyē
is Vīḻimiḻalai where discerning devotees welcome and receive with desire, devotees who may reach by whatever way that shrine.
{1:82}__2+
{$} vātaippaṭukiṉṟa vāṉōr tuyartīra, ōtakkaṭal nañcai uṇṭāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple where Civaṉ who consumed the poison to remove the sufferings of the celestials who had mental torment.
kītattu icaiyōṭum kēḷvikkiṭaiyōṭum vētattu oli ōvāvīḻimiḻalaiyē
is viḻimiḻalai where the sound of reciting vētam by disciple does not cease, along with the music of songs,
[[kiṭai Band of disciples chanting the vētam-s;]]
[[PP: ōtukiṭaiyuḷ uṭai pōvār (periyapurāṇam, Caṇtēca nāyanār purāṇam, 17);]]
[[kēḷvi is the exact translation of cruti in sanskrit;]]
[[As vētams were learnt by rote and were not committed to writing they are called kēḷvi in Tamil.]]
{1:82}__3+
{$} payilum maṟaiyāḷaṉ talaiyil palikoṇṭu
receiving alms in the skull of Piramaṉ who is known as one who is practising chanting vētams.
tuyilum poḻutu āṭum cōti uṟaikōyil
the light, Civaṉ who dances at midnight when everything is fast asleep.
mayilum maṭamāṉum matiyum iḷavēyum veyilum (1) poli mātar vīḻimiḻalaiyē
is viḻimiḻalai where ladies are shining like the peacock, tender deer, the moon, the young bamboo, and the sunlight
[[Peacock denotes their gentleness, young deer their looks, moon their face, bamboo their shoulders, sunlight their chastity.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) pōl]]
{1:82}__4+
{$} eccattu
in the sacrifice (performed by Takkaṉ)
iravaṉ pakalōṉum imaiyōrai niraviṭṭu aruḷceyta nimilaṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple where the spotless Civaṉ dwells who punished the moon, the sun and other tēvars and then granted his grace.
kuravam, curapuṉṉai, kuḷirkōṅku, iḷavēṅkai viravum poḻil amtaṇ vīḻimiḻalaiyē
is Vīḻimiḻalai which has gardens in which young east indian kino trees, cool common caung, curapuṉṉai (nākaliṅkam) and bottle-flower trees are mixed.
{1:82}__5+
{$} kaṇṇil kaṉalālē kāmaṉ poṭiyāka
having burnt Kāmaṉ with the fire issuing from the frontal eye, to become ash.
peṇṇukku aruḷceyta perumāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple where the Lord who granted his grace to his wife, rati; this is mentioned in the following verses in tēvāram itself.
[[karumpu amar villiyaik kāyntu kātaṟ kārikai māṭṭu aruḷi; makarattu āṭukoṭiyōṉ uṭalam poṭiceytu avaṉuṭaiya, nikar ōppilāttēvikku aruḷcey nīlakaṇṭaṉār; mataṉaṉaip poṭiyā, viḻittu avaṉ tēvi vēṇṭa muṉkoṭutta vimalaṉār; kāmaṉai īṭaḻittiṭṭu avaṉ kātali ceṉṟu irappac cēmamē uṉtaṉakku eṉṟu aruḷ ceytavaṉ (campantar, tēṇipuram (3) 1) caṇpainakār (1) 3; tirukkōṇamalai, 4; tiruvakkarai, 9);
Some people take peṇ to mean pārvati; but this is a better meaning.]]
maṇṇil peruvēḷvivaḷar tīppukai nāḷum, viṇṇil puyal kāṭṭum vīḻimiḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻalai where the smoke that rises from the big sacrifices performed in this world appear like clouds in the sky.
{1:82}__6+
{$} māl āyiram koṇṭu malarkkaṇ iṭa āḻi, ēlāvalayattōṭu īntāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple where Civaṉ who granteda discus with a ring that could not be born when Māl worshipped daily with one thousand lotus flowers and on the final day when one flower was wanting, be scooped his lotus eye and laid it at the feet of Civaṉ (some words have been supplied to complete the sense; this is a lengend about this shrine).
cēl ākiya poykaic ceḻunīrk kamalaṅkaḷ mēlāl uri kāṭṭum vīḻimiḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻalai where in the natural tanks full of carp fish the fertile lotus flowers appear like fire above water.
{1:82}__7+
{$} matiyāl vaḻipaṭṭāṉ vāṇāḷ koṭupōvāṉ, kotiyāvaru kūṟṟaik (1) kumaittāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple of Civaṉ who trod upon the god of death who came enraged to snatch the life of (markkaṇtēyaṉ) who worshipped Civaṉ with discernment.
netiyāl mikucelvar nitta niyamaṅkaḷ vitiyāl niṟkiṉṟār vīḻimiḻalai
is vīḻimiḻalai where very rich people who possess every kind of wealth stand firmly in their daily routines according to prescribed rules.
[[Variant reading: (1) kuṟaittāṉ]]
{1:82}__8+
{$} eṭuttāṉ tarukkiṉai viral ūṉṟi iḻittāṉ
Civaṉ let down the pride of Irāvaṇaṉ who lifted the mountain, by pressing the toe.
koṭuttāṉ vāḷ; āḷākkoṇṭāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple of the god who gave a sword and admitted him as his protege.
maṟaipaṭittār, vēḷvipayiṉṟār; pāvattai viṭuttār mikavāḻum vīḻimiḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻalai where brahmins who learnt the vētams, practised performance to sacrifices and gave up sins live in large members.
{1:82}__9+
{$} kiṭantār (um) iruntāṉum kīḻmēl kāṇātu toṭarntu āṅku avar ēttac cuṭar āyavaṉ kōyil
the temple of Civaṉ who took the form of fire to be praised by Māl who is lying and Piramaṉ who is seated and both could not see the head and feet in the earth and the sky, following it.
paṭam tāṅku aravu alkul pavaḷattuvar vāymēl viṭam tāṅkiya kaṇṇār vīḻimiḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻalai which has ladies who hard poison in their eyes which are above the red lips like coral, and a waist like the cobra with a hood.
{1:82}__10+
{$} cikku ārtuvar āṭaic ciṟutaṭṭu uṭaiyārum nakku āṅku alar tūṟṟum nampāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple of Civaṉ who is spokenslanderously by buddhists who wear a dress soaked in red ochre issuing a rancid smell of oil and camaṇar who dress with mats with derisive laugh.
takkār maṟai vēḷvit talaiyāy ulakukku mikkār avarvāḻum vīḻimiḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻalai where superior people in the world and eminent by performing sacrifices prescribed in the vētams and worthy persons live.
{1:82}__11+
{$} mēl niṉṟu iḻikōyil vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ, ēṉattu eyiṟṟāṉai
on Civaṉ who wears a hog's tusk and dwells in the temple which descended from heaven, in vīḻimiḻalai.
[[taṉtavam periya calantaraṉ uṭalam taṭinta cakkaram eṉakku aruḷ eṉṟu aṉṟu arivaḻipaṭṭu iḻicciya vimāṉattu iṟaiyavaṉ (campantar, viḻimiḻalai, 14-7);
mikutiruvīḻimiḻalai viṇ iḻiceḻuṅkōyil yōkanāyakaṉai (tiruvicaippā, tiruvīḻimiḻalai, 1);
viṇ iḻitaṉ vīḻi miḻalaiyāṉē (nāvukkaracar, tiruvīḻimiḻalai, (7) 1-10);
tirumiḻalai, maṭaṅkal pūṇṭa vimāṉam maṇmicai vantu iḻicciya vāṉa nāṭṭaiyum aṭaṅkal vīḻikoṇṭīr (cuntarar, tiruvīḻimiḻalai, 2)]]
eḻil ārpoḻil kāḻi, ñāṉattu uyarkiṉṟa nalamkoḷ campantaṉ (1) vāymaittu icai colla vallōr nallōrē
those who are able to recite these songs which contain truths and composed by campantaṉ who is eminent by his knowledge of Civaṉ and a native of Kāḻi which has beautiful gardens, are good people.
[[Variant reading: (1) vāymeyttuivai, vāymettuivai]]

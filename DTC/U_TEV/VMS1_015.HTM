{1:15}__1+
{$} maiyāṭiya kaṇṭaṉ
Civaṉ who has a neck in which there is poison.
[[mai black colour; here it means poison black in colour]]
malaimakaḷ pākam (atu) uṭaiyāṉ
has on one half the dughter of the mountin.
kai āṭiya kēṭu ilkari urimūṭiya oruvaṉ
the unequalled one who covered himself with the skin of an elephant of undying fame and whose trunk is swinging.
[[kēṭu il kari the elephant supplied a cover to Civaṉ and therefore it got an undying fame]]
cey āṭiya kuvaḷaim malar nayaṉattavaḷōṭum ney āṭiya perumāṉ iṭam neyttāṉam eṉīrē
people of the world! you simply utter the name, Neyttāṉam which is the abode of the god who bathed in ghee and who is in company with a lady having eyes resembling blue nelumbo flowers that dance in the fields.
{1:15}__2+
{$} aṟaiyum puṉal varu kāviri alaicēr vaṭakaraimēl niṟaiyum puṉai maṭavār payil neyttāṉam, piṟaiyum puṉal (um) aravum paṭu caṭai emperumāṉ ūr eṉīrē
(people of the world) Utter simply Neyttāṉam which is the place of our god who places on his caṭi a crescent, water and a cobra, where ladies who adorn themselves by the firm adherence to a life of chastity, and which is on the northern bank of the Kaviri of waves and water that dashes on the banks.
{1:15}__3+
{$} pēy āyiṉapāṭap peru naṭam āṭiya perumāṉ
the god who performed the big dance when numbers of pēy were singing.
vēy āyiṉa tōḷikku orupākam mika uṭaiyāṉ
who gave one half to a lady whose shoulders are like bamboos.
tāyākiya ulakaṅkaḷai nilaipēṟu ceytalaivaṉ
the chief who placed the worlds on a firm basis in order to become their mother (tōḷikku-tōḷiyai)
nēy āṭiya perumāṉ iṭam neyttāṉam eṉīrē
(People of the world!) utter simply the world neyttāṉam which is the place of the god who bathed in ghee.
[[ney has become nēy to suit rhyme.]]
{1:15}__4+
{$} cuṭunīṟuaṇi aṇṇal
the chief who besmears himself with well-burnt holy ash.
cuṭarcūlam aṉal ēnti
holding a trident that burns brightly, and fire.
naṭu naḷḷiruḷ naṭam āṭiya nampaṉ (1) uṟaivu iṭam ām
the place of the god who danced in the dense darkness of midnight.
kaṭu (2) vāḷ iḷa aravu āṭu umiḻ kaṭal nañcam (atu) uṇṭāṉ
Civaṉ who drank the poison which was spat out by the cruel and shining young dancing cobra (vācuki) in the ocean.
neṭuvāḷaikaḷ kutikoḷ uyarneyttāṉam eṉīrē
(People of this world!) simply utter the name of Neyttāṉam where the long scabbard fish leap.
[[Variant reading: (1) uṟaiyiṭapām (2) vāṉ]]
{1:15}__5+
{$} nukar āramoṭu ēlam maṇi cempoṉ nurai unti
pushing foam, pure gold, precious stones, cardamon and sandal-wood which is one of the things for enjoyment.
pakarā varupuṉal kāviri paravip paṇintu ēttum nikarāṉ
Civaṉ with splendour who is prised and bowed by the Kaviri of floods which is supplies to fields.

maṇal iṭu taṇkarai nikaḻvāya neyttāṉa nakarāṉ aṭi ētta(n) namainaṭalai aṭaiyā
sufferings will not reach us if we prise the feet of Civaṉ in the city of Neyttāṉam which is on the cool bank where sand is deposited
[[ēttannamai-n is augmentation for rhythm.]]
{1:15}__6+
{$} viṭaiyār koṭiyuṭaiya(v) aṇal
the god who has a flag on which the form of a bull is drawn
[[aṇṇal has become aṇal; v is augmentation for rhythm]]
vīntārveḷai elumpum uṭaiyār
who has the white skeletons of those who died.
naṟumālai caṭaiyuṭaiyār (avar) mēya, puṭaiyēpuṉal pāyum vayal poḻil cūḻnta neyttāṉam aṭaiyātavar
those who have not reached neyttāṉam surrounded by gardens and fields into which water flows everywhere and which is desired by the god who has on his caṭi fragment garlands.
amarulakam aṭaiyār
will not reach the heaven of the calestials
[[amarar ulakam have become amarulakam]]
{1:15}__7+
{$} niḻal ārvayal kamaḻ cōlaikaḷ niṟaikiṉṟa neyttāṉattu aḻalāṉ
Civaṉ who has the form of fire in neyttāṉam which is full of fragrant gardens and cool fields.
aṉal aṅkaiyil ēnti
and who holds in the palm fire.
aḻaku āya kaḻalāṉ aṭi nāḷum kaḻalātēviṭaliṉṟit toḻalār avar
those who worship unintermittently without running away from the feet of the god who wears beautiful anklets.
nāḷum tuyar iṉṟit toḻuvār
will worship him always without suffering.
{1:15}__8+
{$} aṟai ār kaṭal ilaṅkaikku iṟai
the King of ilaṅki surrounded by the roaring sea.
aṇicēr kayilāyam iṟai āramuṉ eṭuttāṉ irupatutōḷ iṟa ūṉṟi
pressing down to crush the twenty shoulders of Irāvaṇaṉ who lifted the beautiful Kayilāyam with his hands.
niṟai ārpuṉal neyttāṉaṉ nalnikaḻcēvaṭiparavak
when he prised the good and red feet of the god in neyttāṉam which has abundant water.
kaṟai ār katirvāḷ īntavarkaḻal ēttutal katiyē
prising the feet of the God who gave a sword having the name of moon is the way to deliverence from future births.
[[That sword is called cantirakācam.]]
[[kaṟaiār katir moon which has a black spot in it.]]
{1:15}__9+
{$} kōlam muṭi neṭu māloṭu koytāmaraiyāṉum
the tall Māl who wears a beautiful crown and Piramaṉ who is seated in a lotus flower which is plucked.
[[koytāmarai: the underlined word is a generic epithet.]]
[[kōlammuṭi
may also mean Māl who decided to transform himself into a pig;]]
[[kōlam pig.]]
cīlam(m) aṟivaritāy oḷitikaḻvāy neyttāṉam kālam peṟa malar nīr avai tūvittoḻutu ēttum ñālam pukaḻ aṭiyār uṭal uṟunōy naliyā
diseases which reach the bodies of devotees who are prissed by the people of this world and, who, early in their lives, scatter flowers and sprinkle water, who worship with joined hands prise neyttāṉam where the light of easy accessibility was difficult to be known by those two persons, will not inflict pin.
{1:15}__10+
{$} mattam mali cittattu iṟai matiyillavarcamaṇarputtar (avar) coṉṉa(m) moḻi poruḷā niṉaiyēṉmiṉ
Do not think the words spoken by camaṇar and puttar (buddhists) who do not have any idea of god in their minds of excessive bewilderment, as having any sense.
nittampayil nimalaṉ(ṉ) uṟai neyttāṉam (atu) ēttum, cittam(m) uṭai aṭiyār uṭal ceṟunōy aṭaiyā
Diseases which torment will not get near the bodies of devotees who have the mind to prise neyttāṉam where the spotless one who always dances, dwells
[[nimilaṉṉ uṟai, cittamm uṭai are augmentations for metre;]]
[[nittam-niruttam dance.]]
{1:15}__11+
{$} nilam malkiya pukaḻāl miku neyttāṉaṉai
on Civaṉ in neyttāṉam which is eminent by the fame which has spread throughout the world.
talam malkiya puṉaṟkāḻiyuḷ tamiḻñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ having knowledge of Tamiḻ in Kaḻi having abundant water, and famous among the shrines.
nikaril palam malkiya pāṭal(l) ivai pattum mika vallār
those who are very capable of reciting all these ten verses, which have matchless and increasing benefit.
cila malkiya celvaṉ(ṉ) aṭicērvar civakatiyē
will reach the twin feet of Civaṉ of increasing wealth as an auspicious refuge.

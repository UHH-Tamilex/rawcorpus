Patikams on Mayelāṭuturi
I - 38

Totally 3

In every Patikam The goddess Umi finds place in one stanza, with special reference.

aṇaṅkōṭu orupākam amarntu ai - 38 - 7 iṇaṅki aruḷceytavaṉ Being united with Umi Civan showers grace on the devotees. malaiyarmakaḷ ... taṭamārpuatu iṭamāka uṟaikiṉṟa perumāṉ - aiaiai - 70 - 4 Civan is residing in the chest (mind) of Umi. veñciṉak kaṭuṅkālaṉ viraikilāṉ añcu iṟappum piṟappum aṟukkal ām mañcaṉ, mā mayilāṭu tuṟaiuṟai añcalāḷ umaipaṅkaṉ aruḷilē By the grace of Civan who has Umi giving shelter to devotees, as his half, we can remove our fear about death and birth. The god of death will not come fast to take away our lives. añcolāḷ is a common attribute to Umi. But in this holy place she has the speciality of removing the fear of devotees. So Dr. U.V. Swaminatha Iyer has written in his notes that añcalāḷ will give better meaning. We have taken that reading. The goddess in Mayelāṭuturi is being called as añcalnāyaki even today.
T.V.Gopal Iyer

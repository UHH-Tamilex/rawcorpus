{3:35}__1+
{$} muṉṉai nāṉ maṟai avai muṟai muṟai kuṟaiyoṭum taṉṉatāḷ toḻutu eḻa (1) niṉṟavaṉ taṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who was there for the old Vētams to worship his feet in an order, with their requests.
maṉṉumā kāviri vantu aṭi varuṭa nal cennel ārvaḷa vayal teṉkuṭittiṭṭaiyē
is Teṉkuṭittiṭṭi which has fertile fields where the superior roots of paddy crops are rubbed by the waves of the great Kāviri which has perennial water.
[[Variant reading: (1) niṉṟavaṉṉiṭam]]
{3:35}__2+
{$} makaram āṭum koṭi maṉmata vēḷtaṉai nikaral ākā neruppu eḻa viḻittāṉ iṭam
the place of one who opened his frontal eye to issue fire which has no equal, to burn Maṉmatavēl who has a flag in which the shark is waving.
[[vēḷ is common to Murukaṉ and Maṉmataṉ; to differentiate Maṉmataṉ the epithet maṉmata is added to it]]
pakaravāḷ nittilam pal makarat toṭum cikaramāḷikai toṭum teṉ kuṭittiṭṭaiyē
is Teṉkuṭittiṭṭi where in the pinnacles of storeys beautiful and brilliant pearls combine with many ornamental hangings representing makara fish (shark)
{3:35}__3+
{$} karuviṉāl aṉṟiyē karu elām āyavaṉ
Civaṉ became all living beings without the need of an embryo.
uruviṉāl aṉṟiyē uruvuceytāṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who created all forms without the aid of any form as model.
paruva nāḷ viḻavoṭum pāṭalōṭu āṭalum tiruviṉāl miku pukuḻt teṉkuṭittiṭṭaiyē
is teṉkuṭittiṭṭi where in the festivals in the five ceremonial days in a month and in the big annual festivals, songs and dances are conducted with pomp and thereby attins fame.
[[The five ceremonial days are - caṅkiramaṇam, eighth and fourteenth titi of the dark half or the 11th titi, the day of the deity, the new moon and the full moon days.]]
{3:35}__4+
{$} uḷ nilāvu āviyāy ōṅku taṉ taṉmaiyai viṇṇilār aṟikilā vēta vētāntaṉ ūr
the place of Civaṉ who is spoken in the vētantam of the vētams whose big nature of being the inner life that moves in living beings, could not be known even by the celestials.
eṇṇilār eḻil maṇik kaṉaka māḷikai iḷanteṇṇilā viritarum teṉ kuṭittiṭṭaiyē
is teṉkuṭittiṭṭi where the evenings bright moonlight is refleded by golden storeys in which rare set numberless beautiful precious stones.
{3:35}__5+
{$} varunti vāṉōrkaḷ vantu aṭaiya mānañcu tāṉ arunti āramutu avarkku aruḷceytāṉ amarum ūr
the place where Civaṉ who gave the rare nectar to the celestials, himself drinking the great poison, when the celestials came suffering and sought protection by him.
cerunti pū mātavip pantar, vaṇ ceṇpakam tiruntu nīḷ vaḷar poḻil teṉ kuṭit tiṭṭaiyē
is teṉkuṭittiṭṭi where in the thriving, perfect and long gardens are found cerunti, bowers of the brilliant common delight of the woods and perfect champak flowers.
{3:35}__6+
{$} ūṟiṉār
Civaṉ is in the sense of touch.
ōcaiyuḷ oṉṟiṉār
is one with the sound.
oṉṟi māl kūṟiṉār
has as a portion Māl who is combined with him.
amar tarum kumara vēḷ tātai ūr
the place of the father of Kumaraṉ who is loved by him.
poy akattu āṟiṉār
subdued the desires that wish for unreal things.
ai uṇarvu eyti mey tēṟiṉār
having obtined the knowledge of the five senses, focussed on god, the real thing.
mey tēṟiṉār vaḻipaṭum teṉkuṭittiṭṭaiyē
is teṉkuṭittiṭṭi where people who have clearly understood that Civaṉ is the truth, worship him.
{3:35}__7+
{$} kāṉ alaikkum avaṉ kaṇ iṭantu appa
when the hunter (Kaṇṇappaṉ) who drove hither and thither the beasts in the forest, stuck his eye, having scooped it.
nīḷ vāṉ alaikkum tavattēvuvaittāṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who elevated that hunter to the level of celestials by his penance to make them feel jealous about him.
tāṉ alait teḷ am ūr tāmarait taṇtuṟai, tēṉ alaikkum vayal teṉkuṭittiṭṭaiyē
is teṉkuṭittiṭṭi which has fields and the cool ghats which have lotus flowers on which the clear waves of honey flow.
{3:35}__8+
{$} māloṭum porutiṟal vāḷ arakkaṉ nerintu, ōliṭum paṭi viral oṉṟavaittāṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who fixed firmly his toes to make the brilliant arakkaṉ who fought with Māl during his campign of conquering enemies in the four quarters, to be crushed and to make a roaring noise.
kāloṭum (1) kaṉaka mūkku uṭaṉ varak kayal varāl cēloṭum pāy vayal teṉ kuṭittiṭṭaiyē
is teṉkuṭittiṭṭi of fields where carp, carnatic carp and murrel coming along with the fish with a golden nose in the channels, leap.
[[Variant reading: (1) kaṉakamukku]]
{3:35}__9+
{$} nāraṇaṉ taṉṉoṭu nāṉmukaṉ tāṉumāyk kāraṇaṉ aṭimuṭi kāṇa oṇṇāṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who is the cuse of all things and whose head and feet could not be found by Nāraṇaṉ and Nāṉmukaṉ (Piramaṉ) by their united efforts.
āraṇam koṇṭu pūcurarkaḷ vantu aṭi toḻaccīr aṇaṅkum pukaḻt teṉkuṭittiṭṭaiyē
is Teṉkuṭittiṭṭi which has fame and divinity by its greatness as the brahmins worship his feet coming to the temple chanting vētams.
{3:35}__10+
{$} kuṇṭikaik kaiyuṭaik kuṇṭarum puttarum
the low amaṇar who carry in their hands an ascetic`s pitcher, and buddhists.
paṇṭu uraittē iṭum paṟṟu viṭṭīr
Having left attachment to the words of those two, since a long time.
vaṇṭu iraikkum poḻil taṇṭalaik koṇṭal ār teṇtiraittaṇpuṉal teṉkuṭittiṭṭaiyē toḻum
People of this world! worship teṉkuṭittiṭṭi where in the parks and gardens where bees hum noisily, the clouds rest and has cool water in the clear waves.
{3:35}__11+
{$} tēṉ al ār cōlai cūḻ teṉkuṭittiṭṭaiyai
about Teṉkuṭittiṭṭi surrounded by gardens which have abundant honey, and are full of darkness
kāṉal ār kaṭi poḻil cūḻtarum kāḻiyuḷ
is Kāḻi surrounded by fragrant gardens by the sea-shore.
(1) ñāṉam ār ñāṉacampantaṉa centamiḻ pāl nal ār moḻi valārkku illai ām pāvamē
there will be sins to those who are able to recite the verses of chaste Tamiḻ which can do good like milk, composed by Ñāṉacampataṉ who had knowledge of Civaṉ.
[[Variant reading: (1) ñāṉavār]]

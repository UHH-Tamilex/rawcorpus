{6:76}__1+
{$} amarar purintu toḻutu ēttum pukaḻ (1) takkōṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ is worthy of prise, and is prised and worshipped with joined hands by the immortals, with love.
pōrviṭaiyiṉ pākaṉ(kāṇ)
is the master of the bull which can give fight.
puvaṉam ēḻum virintu pala uyirāki viḷaṅkiṉāṉ (kāṇ)
became renowned, himself being many living beings spreading himself as the seven worlds.
viraikkoṉṟaik kaṇṇiyāṉ (kāṇ)
wears a chaplet of fragrant koṉṟi flowers.
vētam nāṉkum terintu mutaṟpaṭaittōṉaic ciram koṇṭōṉ (kāṇ)
severed one head of Piramaṉ who created everything at the beginning of creation having learnt all the four vetams.
tīrttaṉ (kāṇ)
is very pure.
tirumāl ōr (2) pākattāṉ (kāṇ)
has tirumāl on one half.
tiruntu vayal puṭaitaḻuvu tirupputtūril tiruttaḷiyāṉ(kāṇ)
is at tiruttali in tirupputtūr which is surrounded on all sides by fields prepared and made suitable for cultivation (tiruttali is the name of the temple)
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
that god dwells in my mind. (all the kāṇ in this verse and the following verses are expletives).
[[variant readings: 1.takkāṉ 2. paṅkattāṉ]]
{6:76}__2+
{$} vār ārum mulaimaṅkai pākattāṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ has as a half a lady of distinction on whose breast a bodice is worn.
māmaṟaikaḷ āyavaṉ (kāṇ)
is himself the great vētams.
maṇṇum viṇṇum kūr ār eḻuntaḻalavaṉum kāṟṟum nīrum kulavaraiyum āyavaṉ (kāṇ)
is himself the earth, the sky, the fire that is hidden and grows and appears when wanted, the air, the water and the chief mountin ranges.
koṭu nañcu uṇṭa kār ārum kaṇṭaṉ (kāṇ)
has a neck resembling the sable cloud as he consumed the cruel poison;
[[kāreṉṉa ārum kaṟaimiṭaṟṟu ampalavaṉ Kōviyār, 56]]
eṇtōḷaṉ (kāṇ)
has eight shoulders.
kayilaimalaip poruppaṉ (kāṇ)
dwells in the mountin, Kayili.
viruppōṭu eṉṟum, tēr ārum neṭuvītit tirupputtūril tiruttaṉiyāṉ (kāṇ)
is at tiruttaḷi with desire, in tirupputtūr which has long streets in which temple cars are stationed.
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.
{6:76}__3+
{$} miṉkāṭṭum koṭimaruṅkul umaiyāṭku eṉṟum viruppavaṉ(kāṇ)
Civaṉ is ever the husband of Umayāḷ whose waist is like the lightning and the creeper
poruppu (1) valiccilaik kaiyōṉ (kāṇ)
holds in his hand a strong bow of a mountin.
naṉpāṭṭup pulavaṉāyc caṅkam ēṟi
ascending the academy of poets assuming the form of a poet who can compose good verses.
naṟkaṉakakkiḻi tarumikku aruḷiṉōṉ (kāṇ)
granted the handsome gold tied in a piece of cloth to tarumi (this is one of the sixty four sports of Civaṉ in maturi).
kaṭikkoṉṟai poṉ kāṭṭa
the fragrant koṉṟi resembles the gold.
maruṅkē niṉṟa puṉak kāntaḷ kaikāṭṭa
when the flowers of malabar glory lily in the sylvan tract which grows nearby, resembles the hand of the supplicants.
kaṇṭu
seeing this.
vaṇṭu teṉkāṭṭum ceḻumpuṟaviṉ tirupputtūril tiruttaṇiyāṉ(kāṇ)
is at tiruttaḷi in tirupputtūr situated in the forest tract where the bees hum like music.
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: 1. valicilaik]]
{6:76}__4+
{$} ēṟumalark kamalattu ayaṉum mālum intiraṉum paṇintu ētta irukkiṉṟāṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ is sitting majestically to be prised and made obeisance to by Ayaṉ who is seated in a lotus flower which has very many petals and by Māl.
tōṭu ēṟu malark kaṭukkai vaṉṉi mattam tuṉṉiya ceñcaṭaiyāṉ (kāṇ)
has a red caṭi in which koṉṟi of many petals, leaves of indian masquit and datura flowers, are crowded together.
tukaḷtīr caṅkam māṭu ēṟi muttu īṉum kāṉal vēli maṟaikkāṭṭu māmaṇi (kāṇ)
is the big gem in maṟikāṭu surrounded by the sea-shore gardens where the flawless oyster climbs up on the side and brings forth the pearl.
vaḷamkoḷ mēti (1) cēṭu ēṟi maṭuppaṭiyum tirupputtūril tiruttaḷiyāṉ (kāṇ)
is at tiruttaḷi in tirupputtūr where the great buffaloes climb up on an elevated bank and plunge into the pool.
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: (1)cēṭeṟintu]]
{6:76}__5+
{$} karumaruvu valviṉai nōy (1) akaṟṟiṉāṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ expelled the irresistible act which leads to birth and is like disease.
kāmaru pūṅkacci ēkampattāṉ (kāṇ)
is at ēkampam is beautiful and lovable Kacci.
perumaruvu pēr ulakil piṇikaḷ tīrkkum perumpaṟṟattaṇpuliyūr maṉṟāṭi (kāṇ)
is the dancer in the golden hall of cool perumpaṟṟappuliyūr which itself removes the diseases in the big world which has greatness.
tarumaruvu koṭait taṭakkai (2) aḷakaik kōṉtaṉ (3) caṅkātti
is the friend of the Lord of aḷaki who has a big hand as liberal as the tree Kaṟpakam in heaven
(3) ārūril taṉiyāṉai (kāṇ)
is the unequalled elephant in ārūr
[[ārūr āṇṭa ayirāvaṇamē (nāvukkaracar, tiruvārūr, (13) 1)]]
tirumaruvu poḻil puṭaicūḻ tirupputtūril tiruttaḷiyāṉ (kāṇ)
is at tiruttaḷi in tirupputtūr surrounded on all sides by beautiful gardens.
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.
[[variant readings: (1) māṟṟiṉāṉ, kāṟṟiṉāṉ (2) aḷakaikkōṉkāṇ (3) caṅkātiyārūril, caṅkāṟṟiyārūril]]

{$} kāmpu āṭu tōḷ umaiyāḷ kāṇa naṭṭam kalantu āṭal purintavaṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ performed dance joining with the pūtams to be witnessed by Umayāl who has shoulders resembling bamboo.
kaiyil veyya pāmpu āṭap paṭutalaiyil pali (1) koḷvōṉ (kāṇ)
receives alms in the dead skull when the cruel cobra held in the hand dances spreading its hood.
pavaḷattiṉ paruvaraipōl paṭivattāṉ (kāṇ)
has a body red like a big mountin of coral.
tāmpu āṭu ciṉaviṭaiyē pakaṭākkoṇṭa caṅkaraṉ (kāṇ)
is cankaraṉ (dispenser of good things) who has as his elephant the angry bull that is tethered in a rope.
poṅku aravak kaccaiyōṉ (kāṇ)
wears as a belt a cobra that bursts in anger.
cēmpu āṭu vayal puṭai cūḻ tirupputtūril tiruttaṉiyāṉ(kāṇ)
is at tiruttaḷi in tirupputtūr surrounded on all sides by fields where arrow head aquatic plant dances in the breeze.
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: (1) koḷvāṉ]]

{$} veṟi viravu malarkkoṉṟai viḷaṅku tiṅkaḷ vaṉṉiyoṭu viricaṭaimēl milaicciṉāṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ wore on his loose caṭi koṉṟi flowers united with fragrance, shining crescent and leaves of indian mesquit.
poṟiviravu katanākam akkiṉōṭu pūṇṭavaṉ(kāṇ)
adorned himself with an angry cobra united with spots and bones.
porupulittōl āṭaiyāṉ (kāṇ)
has a dress of the skin of a tiger capable of fighting.
aṟivu ariya nuṇporuḷkaḷ āyiṉāṉ (kāṇ)
is himself minute things which could not be easily known
āyiram pēr uṭaiyavaṉ (kāṇ)
has one thousand names.
am taṇ kāṉal ceṟipoḻil cūḻ maṇimāṭat tirupputtūril tiruttaṉiyāṉ (kāṇ)
is at tiruttaḷi in tirupputtūr which has beautiful storeys and which is surrounded by beautiful and cool sea-shore garden and the dense gardens.
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.

{$} pukku aṭainta (1) vētiyaṟkāk kālaṟ kāynta puṇṇiyaṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ the embodiment of all virtuous acts, who killed the kālaṉ (the god of death) in order to protect the brahmin who approached him and sought refuge in him.
veḷnakai veḷvaṉaiyāḷ añca, mikku etirnta kari uruva (2) uritta kōṉ (kāṇ)
the Lord who flayed an elephant which came opposing him with pride, to make the lady with white teeth wearing white bangles, frightened.
(3) veṇmatiyaik kalai cērtta tiṇmaiyōṉ (kāṇ)
the strong god who added the phases to the white crescent
akku arumpu perumpuṉṉai neruṅkucōlai ārūrukku (4) atipati (kāṇ)
the Lord of ārūr which has gardens where the mast-wood trees which put forth buds like the chank beads, are crowded; (chank-beads are compared to the buds of mast-wood trees)
[[puṉṉaiya malarp poḻilkaḷ akkiṉoḷikāṭṭa (campantar, pukali (4)5); puṉṉaik kaṉṉikaḷ akku arumpu puṟampayam (cuntarar, puṟampayam, 7), puṉṉai muṉṉam akku varum kāṉal aiyāṟarai (11th tirumuṟi civaperumāṉ tiruviraṭṭimaṇimāli, 30).]]
amtaṇ (5) teṉṟal tikku aṇaintu varumaruṅkil tirupputtūril tiruttaḷiyāṉ (kāṇ)
is at tiruttaḷi in tirupputtūr which has many places where the beautiful and cool balmy breeze from the south blows coming into contact with the directions.
[[teṉṟal maṇam kamaḻum teṉtiruvārūr (cuntārar, tiruvārur, (6) 2).]]
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse

{$} vāṉōrukkum ēṉōrkkum paṟṟu avaṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ is the support for the celestials and others
parāparaṉkāṇ
is both the sublime and ridiculous things.
takkaṉtaṉ vēḷvi ceṟṟa koṟṟavaṉ (kāṇ)
is the victor who destroyed the sacrifice of takkaṉ.
koṭuñciṉattai aṭaṅkac ceṟṟu ñāṉattai mēl mikuttal kōḷāk koṇṭa peṟṟiyaṉ (kāṇ)
has the nature of increasing the spiritual wisdom having overcome and subdued the cruel auger, to his devotees.
piṟaṅku aruvik kaḻukkuṉṟattu empiññakaṉ (kāṇ)
is the destroyer who dwells in Kaḻukkuṉṟam which has glittering mountin streams.
pēr eḻil ār kāmavēḷaic ceṟṟavaṉ(kāṇ)
destroyed Kāmavēḷ noted for his great beauty.
cīrmaruvu tirupputtūril tiruttaṉiyāṉ (kāṇ)
is at tiruttaḷi in tirupputtūr which has fame that is always with it.
avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.

{$} uram matitta calantaraṉtaṉ ākamnīṇṭa ōrāḻi paṭaittavaṉ (kāṇ)
Civaṉ created a discus which tore the body of calantaraṉ who estimated his own strength as superior to others.
ulaku cūḻum varam matitta katiravaṉaip pal (1) koṇṭāṉ(kāṇ)
seized the teeth of the sun who is esteemed by all and goes round the world.
vāṉavarkōṉ puyam neritta vallāḷaṉ (kāṇ)
is the mighty person who crushed the shoulders of the king of the celestials.
ara matittuc cempoṉṉiṉ āram pūṇā aṇintavaṉ (kāṇ)
put on the cobras esteeming them as golden garlands and other ornaments (arā has become ara)
[[aravaru nuṇṇiṭai (Kōviyar, 81, Koḷu)]]
alaikaṭal cūḻ ilaṅkaivēntaṉ ciram neritta cēvaṭi (kāṇ)
has the lotus red-feet which crushed the heads of the King of ilaṅki surrounded by the ever moving ocean.
tirupputtūril tiruttaṉiyāṉ (kāṇ) avaṉ eṉ cintaiyāṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.

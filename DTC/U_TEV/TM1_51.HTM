*1
veṅ kaṇ _āṉai _īr_urivai pōrttu, viḷaṅkum moḻi
maṅkai pākam vaittu _ukanta māṇpu_atu _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
kaṅkaiyōṭu tiṅkaḷ cūṭi, kaṭi kamaḻum koṉṟait-
toṅkalāṉē! tūya nīṟṟāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*2
viṭai _amarntu, veṇmaḻu _oṉṟu _ēnti, virintu _ilaṅku
caṭai _oṭuṅka, taṇpuṉalait tāṅkiyatu _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
kaṭai _uyarnta mummatilum kāyntu _aṉaluḷ _aḻunta,
toṭai nekiḻnta veñcilaiyāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*3
tīyar_āya val_arakkar centaḻaluḷ(ḷ) _aḻuntac
cāya _eytu, vāṉavarait tāṅkiyatu _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
pāyum veḷḷai_ēṟṟai _ēṟi, pāy pulittōl _uṭutta
tūya veḷḷainīṟṟiṉāṉē! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*4
pal_il_ōṭu kaiyil _ēnti, palkaṭaiyum pali tērntu,
_allal vāḻkkaimēlatu_āṉa _ātaravu _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
villai veṉṟa nuṇ puruva vēlneṭuṅkaṇṇiyoṭum
tollai_ūḻi_āki niṉṟāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*5
nāṟṟam mikka koṉṟai tuṉṟu ceñcaṭaimēl matiyam
_ēṟṟam_āka vaittu _ukanta kāraṇam _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
_ūṟṟam mikka kālaṉtaṉṉai _olka _utaittu_aruḷi,
tōṟṟam _īṟum_āki niṉṟāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*6
kol-naviṉṟa mū_ilaivēl, kūrmaḻuvāḷpaṭaiyaṉ,
poṉṉai veṉṟa koṉṟaimālai cūṭum poṟpu _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
_aṉṉam _aṉṉa meṉnaṭaiyāḷ pākam _amarntu, _arai cēr
tuṉṉa vaṇṇa _āṭaiyiṉāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*7
kuṟṟam_iṉmai, _uṇmai, nī _eṉṟu _uṉ _aṭiyār paṇivār,
kaṟṟal kēḷvi ñāṉam _āṉa kāraṇam _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
vaṟṟal_āmai vāḷ_aravam pūṇṭu, _ayaṉ veṇtalaiyil-
tuṟṟal_āṉa koḷkaiyāṉē! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*8
vilaṅkal _oṉṟu veñcilaiyāk koṇṭu, viṟal _arakkar-
kulaṅkaḷ vāḻum _ūr _eritta koḷkai_itu _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
_ilaṅkai maṉṉu vāḷ _avuṇarkōṉai _eḻil viralāl-
tulaṅka _ūṉṟivaittu _ukantāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*9
viṭam koḷ nākam mālvaraiyaic cuṟṟi, viritirainīr
kaṭainta nañcai _uṇṭu _ukanta kāraṇam _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
_iṭantu maṇṇai _uṇṭa mālum, _iṉ malarmēl _ayaṉum,
toṭarntu muṉṉam kāṇamāṭṭāc cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*10
puttarōṭu puṉcamaṇar poy_uraiyē _uraittu,
pittar_ākak kaṇṭu _ukanta peṟṟimai _eṉṉaikol_ām?---
mattayāṉai _īr_urivai pōrttu, vaḷarcaṭaimēl-
tuttinākam cūṭiṉāṉē! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*11
cōlai mikka taṇvayal cūḻ cōpuram mēyavaṉai,
cīlam mikka tolpukaḻ _ār cirapurak kōṉ---nalattāṉ,

*1
veṅ kaṇ _āṉi _īr_urivi pōrttu, viḷaṅkum moḻi
maṅki pākam vittu _ukanta māṇpu_atu _eṉṉikol_ām?---
kaṅkiyōṭu tiṅkaḷ cūṭi, kaṭi kamaḻum koṉṟit-
toṅkalāṉē! tūya nīṟṟāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*2
viṭi _amarntu, veṇmaḻu _oṉṟu _ēnti, virintu _ilaṅku
caṭi _oṭuṅka, taṇpuṉalit tāṅkiyatu _eṉṉikol_ām?---
kaṭi _uyarnta mummatilum kāyntu _aṉaluḷ _aḻunta,
toṭi nekiḻnta veñciliyāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*3
tīyar_āya val_arakkar centaḻaluḷ(ḷ) _aḻuntac
cāya _eytu, vāṉavarit tāṅkiyatu _eṉṉikol_ām?---
pāyum veḷḷi_ēṟṟi _ēṟi, pāy pulittōl _uṭutta
tūya veḷḷinīṟṟiṉāṉē! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*4
pal_il_ōṭu kiyil _ēnti, palkaṭiyum pali tērntu,
_allal vāḻkkimēlatu_āṉa _ātaravu _eṉṉikol_ām?---
villi veṉṟa nuṇ puruva vēlneṭuṅkaṇṇiyoṭum
tolli_ūḻi_āki niṉṟāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*5
nāṟṟam mikka koṉṟi tuṉṟu ceñcaṭimēl matiyam
_ēṟṟam_āka vittu _ukanta kāraṇam _eṉṉikol_ām?---
_ūṟṟam mikka kālaṉtaṉṉi _olka _utittu_aruḷi,
tōṟṟam _īṟum_āki niṉṟāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*6
kol-naviṉṟa mū_ilivēl, kūrmaḻuvāḷpaṭiyaṉ,
poṉṉi veṉṟa koṉṟimāli cūṭum poṟpu _eṉṉikol_ām?---
_aṉṉam _aṉṉa meṉnaṭiyāḷ pākam _amarntu, _ari cēr
tuṉṉa vaṇṇa _āṭiyiṉāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*7
kuṟṟam_iṉmi, _uṇmi, nī _eṉṟu _uṉ _aṭiyār paṇivār,
kaṟṟal kēḷvi ñāṉam _āṉa kāraṇam _eṉṉikol_ām?---
vaṟṟal_āmi vāḷ_aravam pūṇṭu, _ayaṉ veṇtaliyil-
tuṟṟal_āṉa koḷkiyāṉē! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*8
vilaṅkal _oṉṟu veñciliyāk koṇṭu, viṟal _arakkar-
kulaṅkaḷ vāḻum _ūr _eritta koḷki_itu _eṉṉikol_ām?---
_ilaṅki maṉṉu vāḷ _avuṇarkōṉi _eḻil viralāl-
tulaṅka _ūṉṟivittu _ukantāy! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*9
viṭam koḷ nākam mālvariyic cuṟṟi, viritirinīr
kaṭinta nañci _uṇṭu _ukanta kāraṇam _eṉṉikol_ām?---
_iṭantu maṇṇi _uṇṭa mālum, _iṉ malarmēl _ayaṉum,
toṭarntu muṉṉam kāṇamāṭṭāc cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*10
puttarōṭu puṉcamaṇar poy_uriyē _urittu,
pittar_ākak kaṇṭu _ukanta peṟṟimi _eṉṉikol_ām?---
mattayāṉi _īr_urivi pōrttu, vaḷarcaṭimēl-
tuttinākam cūṭiṉāṉē! cōpuram mēyavaṉē!

*11
cōli mikka taṇvayal cūḻ cōpuram mēyavaṉi,
cīlam mikka tolpukaḻ _ār cirapurak kōṉ---nalattāṉ,
ñālam mikka taṇtamiḻāṉ, ñāṉacampantaṉ---coṉṉa
kōlam mikka māli vallār kūṭuvar, vāṉ_ulakē.

(1) ūṉ aṅkattu uyirppāy
Civaṉ being the breath in the body made of flesh.
(2) ulaku ellām ōṅkārattu uruāki niṉṟāṉai
who is the form of ōmkāram for all the worlds.
(3) vāṉam kaittavarkkum aḷappu ariya vaḷḷalai
the god of Unbounded liberality who is difficult to be measured by the logical modes of proof even for people who have disliked heaven
[[aṉattal may also mean to define.]]
aṭiyārkaḷ tam uḷḷat tēṉ (4) amkaittu amutu āki uḷḷūṟum tēcaṉai
who is the divine light who springs like the nectar, in the mind making even the sweet honey to taste bitter, in the hearts of devotees
[[tēṉam - am: expletive]]
tiḷaittaṟku iṉiyāṉai
who is sweet to enjoy unceasingly.
māṉ amkaittalattu ēntavallāṉai
who is capable of holding a deer in the hand.
valivalantaṉil vantu kaṇṭēṉ (ē)
I saw after coming to Valivalam.
[[Variant readings: (1) ūṉaṅkaittu (2) uyirkku (3) vāṉaṅkattavarkkum (4) tēṉaṅkaittamutu]]

pal aṭiyār (1) paṇikkup parivāṉai
Civaṉ who is affectionate towards the services of many devotees.
pāṭi āṭum pattarkku aṉpuṭaiyāṉai
who loves the pious people who sing and dance out of ecstasy.
cel aṭiyē nerukkit tiṟampātu cērntavarkkē citti mutti ceyvāṉai
who grants salvation and eight kinds of miraculous power only to those who have approached him without going astray from the path prescribed to reach him.
nal aṭiyār maṉattu eyppiṉil vaippai
who is like the wealth that is lid to be of use in times of adversity in the minds of good devotees.
nāṉ uṟu kuṟai aṟintu aruḷ purivāṉai
who fulfils my wants knowing them well.
val aṭiyār maṉattu iccai uḷāṉai
who desires to stay in the minds of learned devotees.
valivalamtaṉil vantu kaṇṭēṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: (1) parikkum]]

akaṉṟē

uyarntāṉai

āti antam (1) paṇivārkku aṇiyāṉai
who is near at hand to those who bow to him from the time of birth till death.
(2) kūḻaiyar āki
realising one's duties.
poyyē kuṭi ōmpi
running the family as if they are attached to it, without really being attached.
kuḻaintu mey aṭiyār kuḻuppeyyum vāḻiyavarkkē
only to those who lead a life mixing with the group of sincere devotees who are tender at heart.
vaḻuvāneṟikāṭṭi
showing them the path to perform service without fault.
maṟupiṟappu mācu eṉṉai aṟuttāṉai
who completely destroyed the blemish which is the cuse for re-birth.
māḻai oṇkaṇ umai makiḻntāṉai
who rejoiced to be united with Umi who has bright eyes like the tender mango cut into two (māḻaioṇkaṇ umai is the name of the goddess here)

nallicai ñāṉa campantaṉum (1) nāviṉukku araiyaṉum pāṭiya naltamiḻ mālai colliyavē colli ēttu ukappāṉai
Civaṉ who desires being prised by tamiḻ verses which contin true knowledge, by repeating them which were composed by nāṉacampantaṉ having knowledge of music and by nāvukkaracaṉ.
toṇṭaṉēṉ aṟiyāmai aṟintu kaliyal maṉattaik kacivittu
cusing my mind which is as hard as stone to melt, knowing well the ignorance of myself, his slave.
kaḻal aṭikāṭṭi
showing his feet wearing Kaḻal.
eṉ kaḷaikaḷai aṟukkum, val iyalvāṉavar vaṇaṅka niṉṟāṉ
who is bowed by the celestials and who has the strength to weed out all my obstacles.

pāṭumā pāṭip paṇiyum āṟu aṟiyēṉ
I do not know how to bow to Civaṉ in the manner in which I would sing his prises with the help of old songs.
paṉuvumā (1) paṉuvip paravumāṟu aṟiyēṉ
I do not know to prise him in the manner in which I should compose verses.
tēṭumātēṭit tiruntumāṟu aṟiyēṉ
I do not know to rectify the faults in the manner I should search them within me.
cellumā cellac celuttumāṟu aṟiyēṉ
I do not know how to direct my mind in the proper manner.
kūṭumāṟu eṅṅaṉamō eṉṟu kūṟa
when good people take pity on me and say How could this person get a good status?
kuṟittuk kāṭṭik koṇarntu eṉai āṇṭu
admitting me as his protege, showing me to all and saying he is my slave, and bringing me before the public.
vāṭi nī varuntal eṉpāṉai
who comforts me saying you do not feel sorrow by becoming weak.
valivalamtaṉil vantukaṇṭēṉēṉa (ē)
see 1st verse.

(1) pantitta valviṉaippaṟṟu aṟappiṟavip paṭu kaṭaṟparapput tavirppāṉai
Civaṉ who removed the births which are like extensive ocean as the grip of the irresistible acts which bound me are out asunder.
cantitta (2) tiṟalāl paṇi pūṭṭi
performing services to him by strength of worshipping always.
tavattai īṭṭiya taṉ aṭiyārkkut tiruppātam cintittaṟku eḷitāy
making his holy feet easy to meditate upon, for his devotees who have amassed penance.
civalōkam tiṟantu ēṟṟavallāṉai
who is capable of opening the civalōkam and lifting them up into it.
vantippār tam maṉattiṉuḷḷāṉai
who dwells in the minds of those who pay homage to him.
valivalamtaṉil vantu kaṇṭēṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.

vavvi eṉ āvi maṉam kalantāṉai

tiriyum muppuram ceṟṟatum kuṟṟattiṟal arakkaṉaic ceṟuttatum maṟṟaip periya nañcu amutu uṇṭatum (1) muṟṟum piṉṉaiyāy
Civaṉ who survived finishing the following acts and was the cuse for destroying the wandering three cities, punishing the arakkaṉ for his reproachful strength and consuming the great poison as nectar.
muṉṉamē muḷaittāṉai
who was in existence even before anything came into existence.
ariya nāṉmaṟai antaṇar ōvātu aṭipaṇintu aṟitaṟku ariyāṉai
who could not be known even by the brahmins who have learnt the four abstruse vētams, even though they prostrated at his feet ceaselessly

varaiyiṉ pāvai maṇāḷaṉ emmāṉai

ēṉṟa antaṇaṉ talaiyiṉai aṟuttu
cutting off the head of the brahmin (Piramaṉ) who opposed Civaṉ.
niṟaikka māl utirattiṉai ēṟṟu
receiving the blood of Māl to fill it.
tōṉṟu tōḷmicaikkaḷēparam taṉṉaic cumanta māviratta kaṅkāḷaṉ
the god who observes māviratam, and who bore the skeleton on the shoulder which is to be seen conspicuously.
cāṉṟukāṭṭutaṟku ariyavaṉ
about whom it is difficult to show evidence about his existence.
taṉṉil ām maṉattārkku eḷiyavaṉ taṉṉai
who is easy of approach to those who always think of him.
māṉṟu ceṉṟu aṇaiyātavaṉ taṉṉai
who could not be reached by treading the path of ignorance.

kalivalamkeṭa ār aḻal ōmpum, kaṟṟa nāṉmaṟai muṟṟu aṉal (1) ōṭi valivalam taṉil vantu kaṇṭu
obtining vision of Civaṉ by coming of valivalam where oblations are offered in the consecraated fire by brahmins who have learnt all the four vētams and who mintin the fire to destroy the strength of Kaliyukam;
[[kaṟṟāṅku eri ōmpik kaliyaivārāmē, ceṟṟār vāḻtillai (campantar; Kōyil (1) 1)]]
aṭiyēṉ, maṉṉu nāval ārūraṉ, vaṉtoṇṭaṉ olikoḷ iṉṉicaic centamiḻ pattum
all the ten verses, composed in refined tamiḻ which has music and sweet sound.
uḷḷattāl ukantu ēttavallār
those who are able to prise feeling happy in mind.
pōy melivuil vāṉ ulakattavar virumpi ētta viṇ ulaku eytuvar (tām, ē)
to be prised with love by the celestials enter into heaven which has no sufferings, after life in this world comes to an end.

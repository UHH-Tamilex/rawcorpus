{$} nāṉ pūnturuttik kaṇṭēṉ
I saw in Pūnturutti
nillāta nīrcaṭaimēl niṟpittāṉai
Civaṉ who cused the water which is not stationary to remin without movement in his caṭi.
niṉaiyā eṉ neñcai niṉaivittāṉai
who cused my mind did not think of him, to think to him
[[cuḻiyāvaruperunīr ceṉṉivaittu eṉṉaittaṉtoḻumpil, kaḻiyā aruḷ vaitta ciṟṟampalavaṉ (Kōviyār, 261);
parantuvarum perumpuṉalai vēkam taṇittut taṉceṉṉiyiṉ kaṇ vaittāṟpōla nillātu parakkum neñcaiyuṭaiyēṉait taṉ aruṭkaṇ aṭakkiṉāṉ eṉpatu karuttu (Do pērāciriyar's commentary)]]
kallātaṉa ellām kaṟpittāṉai
who tught me what all I was not capable of learning.
kāṇātaṉa ellām kāṭṭiṉāṉai
who showed me what all I did no t see before.
collātaṉa ellām eṉṉaic colli
who revealed to me what all I did not tell before

having accepted me as his servant in this world and bestowing his grace without ceasing.
pollā eṉ nōy tīrtta puṉitaṉ taṉṉaip puṇṇiyaṉai
who is the abode of virtue and the pure god who cured me of my disease which gave me excruciating pin.

see 5th verse.
vāṉōr ulakam ellām vaittāṉai
Civaṉ who created the heaven and other worlds as places for living being.
vāṉōr ulakam ellām vantu iṟaiñci malarkoṇṭu niṉṟu pōṟṟum vittāṉai
who is like the seed and is worshipped with flowers by all the celestials in heaven coming to this world and paying homage and prising him:
vēṇṭiṟṟu oṉṟu īvāṉai
who grants anything that one desires.
viṇṇavar tam perumāṉai
who is the master of the celestials.
viṉaikaḷ pōka uyttāṉai
who drove out the Karmams to leave his devotees.
olikaṅkai caṭaimēl tāṅki oḷittāṉai
who concealled the roaring Kaṅki, carrying it on his caṭi.
oru pākattu umaiyōṭu āṅkē poyttāṉai
who uttered falsehood to Umi who is on one half, I never knew any other lady except you, at that time itself.

{$} puṇṇiyaṉaip puṉitaṉtaṉṉaip poyyiliyai nāṉ pūnturuttik kaṇṭēṉ
see 5th verse.
vāṉōr ulakam ellām āṇṭāṉai
Civaṉ who ruled over the world belonging to celestials.
annāḷ aṟiyāta takkaṉ vēḷvi viṇṇavarkaḷōṭum kūṭimīṇṭāṉai
who returned from the sacrifice of ignorant takkaṉ along with the celestials who were brought back to life, in the distant past (The return was done after sacking the sacrifice of takkaṉ and bringing back to life the tēvar who attended the sacrifice.)
viraimalarmēl nāṉmukaṉum mālum (1) tēṭa nīṇṭāṉai neruppu uruvamāṉāṉ taṉṉai
who assumed the form of a tall column of fire to make Māl and Piramaṉ who is on the fragrant lotus flower, to search for him.
nilai ilār mummatilum vēva villaippūṇṭāṉai
who were the bow as an ornament to burn the three forts of the acurar whose minds were not firm in their devotion to Civaṉ.

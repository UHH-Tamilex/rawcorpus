{5:10}__1+
{$} paṇṇiṉ nēr moḻiyāḷ umai paṅkarō
O Lord who has Umai whose words are as sweet as music, on one half!
maṇṇiṉār valamcey maṟaikkāṭarō
O Lord in maraikkāṭu around which people of this world go from left to right!
umaik kaṇṇiṉāl kāṇak kataviṉait tiṇṇamākat tiṟantaruḷ ceymmiṉ (ē)
Kindly open the doors with certainty to have a vision of you with my eyes.
[[The Vētam-s worshipped Civaṉ in Maṟaikkāṭu and closed the doors in front of the temple turned towards him. People made a side entrance and went into the temple and worshipped God. When Campantar and Nāvukkaracar were there the devotees requested then to open the door closed by the Vētam-s. Nāvukkaracar sang verses to open the doors and Campantar sang to close them. After that people entered into the temple by the direct entrance and worshipped the Lord.]]
{5:10}__2+
{$} ākattuḷ īṇṭu ceñcaṭai īcarō
O Civaṉ who has a red caṭai which is close together in the body!
mūṇṭa kārmukiliṉa, muṟikkaṇṭarō
O Lord who has a neck whose colour is like that of the sable cloud just rising and has the brightness of the fresh tender leaf!
āṇṭu koṇṭa nīrē aruḷ ceytiṭum
you yourself who admitted me as your protege bestow your grace on me.
nīṇṭa māk kataviṉ vali nīkkum(ē)
remove the strength of thses long and big doors.
{5:10}__3+
{$} aṭṭamūrtti (atu) ākiya apparē
O Father who has the eight forms!
tuṭṭar vāṉpuram cuṭṭa cuvaṇṭarō
O God who had the fitness to burn the famous cities of the acurar who were mischievous
paṭṭam kaṭṭiya ceṉṉip paramarō
O superior Lord who tied a plate on the head!
caṭṭa ikkatavam tiṟappimmiṉ (ē)
order the doors to be opened speedily (properly)
[[PP: caṭṭa nērpaṭa vantilāta caḻakkaṉēṉ
(Tiruvācakam, tirukkaḻukkuṉṟappatikam, 2)]]
{5:10}__4+
{$} ariya (4) nāṉmaṟai ōtiya nāvarō
O Lord who chanted the abstruse four Vētam-s!
periya vāṉpuram cuṭṭa cuvaṇṭarō
O Lord who burnt with propriety the big cities which were flying in the sky!
virikoḷ kōvaṇa āṭai viruttarō
O old Lord who wears a broad men's loincloth!
periya vāṉ (2)katavam pirivikka (ē)
order to separate the big and sacred doors.
[[variant readings: (1) māmaṟai (2) katavaip]]
{5:10}__5+
{$} malaiyiṉ nīṭu irukkum maṟaikkāṭarō
O Lord in Maṟaikkāṭu which exists without destruction like the mountain, Kayilai! (malaiyiṉ-malaiyaippōla Like the mountain, Kayilai)
kalaikaḷ vantu iṟaiñcum kaḻal ēttarō
O Lord whose feet are praised by those who are well-versed in arts and by their authors who come and pay homage!
vilaiyil (2) māmaṇi vaṇṇa uruvarō
O Lord who has the form which has the colour of the invaluable big gem!
tolaivu ilāk katavam tuṇai nīkkum (ē)
open the doors which are united and not opened till now.
[[Variant reading: (1) malaiyiṉ mēlirukkum (2) māmaṇi aṉṉa]]
{5:10}__6+
{$} pūkkum tāḻai puṟaṇi arukuelām ākkum taṇpoḻil cūḻ maṟaikkāṭarō
O Lord in Maṟaikkāṭu surrounded by gardens which create coolness and where in the neighbourhood of the forest tract the fragrant screw-pines blossom.
ārkkum kāṇpu ariyīr
O Lord who is difficult to be seen by anyone however great the may be!
aṭikēṉ
God!
umainōkki kāṇak katavai tiṟavum (ē)
open the doors to have a vision of you directly looking at you.
[[Note: This verse is the same except for a few words like the 2nd verse in the previous decade.]]
[[tiṟavum: this is a modern usage of the verb in the optative mood.]]
{5:10}__7+
{$} venta veṇpoṭip pūcum vikirtarō
O Lord who is different from the world and who smears himself with well burnt white sacred ash!
antam illi
O Lord who has no death!
aṇi maṟaikkāṭarō
O Lord in beautiful Maṟaikkāṭu!
entai
our father!
aṭiyār vantu iṟaiñciṭa, nī inta māk katavam (1) piṇi nīkkum (ē)
you remove the fastening of these great doors so that devotees can enter directly and pay homage to you.
[[nī nīkkum: is a peculiar usage. I am of the opinion that the reading should be nīraṭiyār in the plural;]]
[[PP: inta is a later usage; it occurs in Cilappatikāram:
aṟattuṟai mākkaṭku allatu intap puṟacciṟai irukkai poruntātākaliṉ
(15-107-108).]]
[[Variant reading: (1) piṇai]]
{5:10}__8+
{$} āṟu cūṭum aṇi maṟaikkāṭarō
O Lord in beautiful maṟaikkāṭu, who wears on the head the river, Kaṅkai!
umai mātukkuk kūṟu īnta kuḻakarō
O youth who gave one half of your body to the lady, Umai!
ēṟu(atu) ēṟiya emperumāṉ
our Lord who rides on a bull!
inta māṟuilāk katavam vali nīkkum (ē)
remove the strength of these doors which have not changed from their being closed centuries ago
[[māṟuilāk katavam can also mean doors which have no comparison.]]
{5:10}__9+
{$} cuṇṇa veṇpoṭiya pūcum cuvaṇṭarō
O Lord who has the propriety of smearing white sacred ash of fineness!
[[cuṇṇa veṇpoṭi : fragrant powder and white sacred ash is another meaning.]]
paṇṇi ēṟu ukantu ēṟum paramarō
the supreme god who rides on a bull fitting it out for riding!
aṇṇal
the chief!
āti arumaṟaikkāṭarō
O Lord in maṟaikkāṭu worshipped by vetams and who is the first cause of all things!
tiṇṇam mākkatavam tiṟappimmiṉ (ē)
have the doors opened which are closed tightly.
{5:10}__10+
{$} viṇuḷār virumpi etirkoḷḷavē, maṇuḷār vaṇaṅkum maṟaikkāṭarō
O Lord in Maṟaikkāṭu to whom people of this world pay homage when the celestials in heaven advance towards them to welcome and receive them!
kaṇṇiṉāl umaik kāṇak kataviṉait tiṇṇamākat tiṟantu aruḷ ceymmiṉ (ē)
condescend to open the doors certainly to have a vision of you with my eyes.
[[Note: this verse has been wrongly included in the previous decade in the Tēvāram edition of the Kasi mutt; in a previous edition by Kayappākkam cataciva ceṭṭiyār, this is found in this decade which is its rightful place. This fact is corroborated by two references in periya purāṇam by cekkiḻar when he narrates this incident. Those verses are:
pāṭiyaap patikap pāṭṭāṉa pattum pāṭal nirampiya piṉṉum paimpoṉvāyiṟi cēṭuuyar poṉ tirukkāppu nīṅkācceykai
(tiruñāṉacampantar purāṇam 582)
uḷ nīrmaiyiṉāl piḷḷaiyār uraiceytaruḷa ataṉālē, paṇṇiṉ nērum moḻiyāḷ' eṉṟu eṭuttup pāṭap payaṉ tuyppāṉ, teṇṇīr aṇintār tirukkāppu nīkkat tāḻkkat tirukkaṭaikkāppu, eṇṇīr irakkam oṉṟu illīr' eṉṟu pāṭi iṟaiñcutalum'
(tirunāvukkaracu cuvāmikaḷ purāṇam, 268);
In the tarumapuram edition, it is included correctly in this decade.]]

arakkaṉai viralāl aṭarttiṭṭa nīr irakkam oṉṟu ilīr
you who pressed down the arakkaṉ by your toe do not have enen the slightest pity on me.
curakkum puṉṉaikaḷ cūḻ maṟaikkāṭarō

curakka ikkatavam tiṟappimmiṉ (ē)
order there doors to open quickly.

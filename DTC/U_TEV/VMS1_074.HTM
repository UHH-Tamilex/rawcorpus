{1:74}__1+
{$} naṟavam niṟai vaṇṭu aṟaitārkkoṉṟai nayantu
Having desired the garland of koṉṟi which is full of honey and on which bees hum loudly.
cuṟavam ceṟi vaṇ koṭiyōṉ uṭalam poṭiyā nayaṉattāl viḻi ceytāṉ
God opened his eyes to reduce to ash the body of the cupid who has a flag on which the beautiful shark is drawn.
puṟavam uṟai vaṇ patiyā
having fertile puṟavam, as his residence.
{1:74}__2+
{$} uravaṉ puliyiṉ uri tōl āṭai uṭai mēl
on the dress of the skin of the very strong tiger which was flayed.
paṭa nākam viravi viripūṅkaccā acaitta vikirtaṉ
the god who is different from the world fastened as a beautiful and broad belt, a cobra with a hood to be united with him
ukir taṉṉāl poru veṅkaḷiṟu piḷiṟa urittu
having flayed the skin of the fighting and wrathful elephant to roar, with his nil;
puṟavam patiyāka
having puṟavam as his residence.
iravum pakalum imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntāṉ
was seated with Umi to be prised night and day by the immortals who do not wink.
[[uravaṉ can be taken as a separate word; then the meaning is strong person.]]
{1:74}__3+
{$} panta muṭaiya pūtam pāṭap pātam cilampu ārkka
when the pūtams which are attached to the god sing and the anklets worn on the leg make a jingling sound
kantam malku kuḻali kāṇ
the lady who has tresses of hir with abundant fragrance to witness it.
kari kāṭṭu eri āṭi
Having danced in the fire of the charred cremation ground.
am taṇ kaṭal cūḻntu aḻaku ār puṟavam patiyā amarvu eyti
being pleased with puṟavam which is full of beauty being surrounded by the beautiful and cool sea, as his abode.
em tam perumāṉ imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntāṉ
our Lord was seated with Umi to be prised by the immortals who do not wink.
{1:74}__4+
{$} paṉi ār malar tūy nittalum, niṉaivār niṉaiya iṉiyāṉ
the god who is sweet to meditate upon dily by scattering very cool flowers.
kaṉai ār viṭai oṉṟu uṭaiyāṉ
Has a bellowing bull.
kaṅkai tiṅkaḷ pukaḻ koṉṟaipuṉaivār caṭaiyiṉ muṭiyāṉ
Has a long caṭi which is dressed in the form of a crown on which he adorns Kaṅki, crescent and fragrant koṉṟi flowers.
kaṭalcūḻ puṟavam patiyāka
puṟavam surrounded by the sea as his residence.
eṉai āḷ uṭaiyāṉ imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntōṉ
the god who admitted me as his protege was seated with Umi to be worshipped by the immortals who do not wink.
{1:74}__5+
{$} ceṅkaṇ aravum naku veṇtalaiyum mukiḻveṇ tiṅkaḷum taṅkucaṭaiyaṉ
the god on whose caṭi, cobra having a red eye, lughing white skull and a white crescent stay.
viṭaiyaṉ
has a bull.
carikōvaṇa āṭaiuṭaiyaṉ
has a dress of a loin-cloth which slides down;
poṅkutirai vaṇkaṭal cūḻntu aḻaku ār puṟavam patiyāka
having as his puṟavam of grand beauty which is surrounded by the sea productive of many things and having surging waves.
eṅkum paravi imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntāṉ
was seated with Umi to be prised by the immortals who do not wink having spread themselves everywhere.
{1:74}__6+
{$} piṉṉucaṭaikaḷ tāḻa
the interwoven caṭi to fall low on (the nape)
kēḻal eyiṟu piṟaḻappōy
and going with the hog's tusk to shine
aṉṉa naṭaiyār maṉaikaḷ tōṟum aḻakāl pali tērntu
begging alms beautifuly going to every house of the ladies whose git resembles that of the swan.
puṉṉai maṭaliṉ poḻil cūḻntu
surrounded by the parks of mast-wood tree which has abundant flowers.
[[maṭal is used for the flowers of a tree which has a rough exterior.]]
aḻaku ār puṟavam patiyāka
having the beautiful puṟavam as his abode.
eṉṉai uṭaiyāṉ
my master.
imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntāṉ
was seated with Umi to be prised by the immortals who do not wink.
{1:74}__7+
{$} uṇṇutaṟku ariya nañcai uṇṭu oru tōḻam tēvar, viṇṇil poliya amutam aḷitta viṭaicēr koṭi aṇṇal
the god who has a flag on which the form of a bull is drawn, drank the poison which is difficult to drink and gave ambrosia so that the tēvar of the big number, toḻam could prosper in heaven.
[[tōḻam a big number.]]
[[PP: ukā valiyiṉ oru tōḻamkālam, aṟāatu aṇintārum tām (paripāṭal, tiraṭṭu, 1-70-71);
ōta ulavā oru tōḻam toṇṭaruḷaṉ (tiruvācakam, tiruvempāvi, 10)]]
paṇṇiṉ ciṟai vaṇṭu aṟai pūñcōlaip puṟavam patiyāka
Having as his abode puṟavam which has flower-gardens where the bees with wings hum like heptatonic.
eṇṇil ciṟanta imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntāṉ
was seated with Umi to be prised by immortals who do not wink and who are foremost in meditating upon god.
{1:74}__8+
{$} viṇtāṉ atira viyaṉ ār kayilai vērōṭu eṭuttāṉ taṉ, tiṇtōḷ uṭalum muṭiyum neriyac ciṟitē ūṉṟiya, puṇtāṉ oḻiya aruḷ cey perumāṉ
the god who bestowed on him his grace to remove the wound cused by gently pressing down to crush the strong shoulders, body and heads belonging to that one who uprooted the spacious Kayili to make residents of the heaven tremble.
puṟavam patiyāka, eṇ tōḷ uṭaiyāṉ imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntāṉ
the god with eight shoulders was seated with Umi, having Puṟavam as his abode, to be prised by the immortals who do not wink.
{1:74}__9+
{$} neṭiyāṉ nīḷ tāmaraimēl ayaṉum nēṭik kāṇkillāppaṭi ār mēṉi uṭaiyāṉ
God who has a body that is compared to others and which was not found out though the tall one (Māl) and Ayaṉ who is seated on a long lotus, went in search of his head and feet
[[paṭi-oppu; comparison.]]
[[PP: paṭi oruvar illāp paṭiyār pōlum (appar tiruccāykkāṭu, tāṇtakam, 7)]]
pavaḷa varaipōl tirumārpil, poṭi ār kōlam uṭaiyāṉ
he has the white colour of the sacred ash on the beautiful chest which is like a mountin of coral.
kaṭal cūḻ puṟavam patiyāka, iṭi ār muḻavu ār imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntāṉ
he was seated with Umi to be prised by the immortals who do not wink and who play upon the muḻavu to make a sound like thunder, having as his abode puṟavam surrounded by sea.
{1:74}__10+
{$} ālum mayiliṉ pīli amaṇar aṟivu il ciṟu tērar, kōlum moḻikaḷ oḻiya
to make the words fabricated by the ignorant, low buddhists and amaṇar who hold in their hands a bundle of feathers of the dancing peacock, meaningless and empty.
kuḻuvum taḻalum eḻil vāṉum pōlum vaṭivum uṭaiyāṉ
the god has a body resembling the beautiful red sky at sunset and several fires combined together.
kaṭal cūḻ puṟavam patiyāka
having puṟavam surrounded by the sea as his abode.
ēlum vakaiyāl imaiyōr ētta umaiyōṭu iruntāṉ
was seated with Umi to be worshipped by the immortals who do not wink, in a manner suitable to them.
{1:74}__11+
{$} poṉ ār māṭam nīṭum celvap puṟavam patiyāka, miṉ ār iṭaiyāḷ umaiyāḷōṭum irunta vimalaṉai
on the spotless god who was seated with Umiyāḷ who has a waist like the lightning, having as his abode Puṟavam of prospering wealth and which has beautiful storeys.
taṉ ārvam cey tamiḻiṉ virakaṉ uraitta tamiḻ mālai, paṉṉāḷpāṭi āṭa
to sing and dance with the garland to Tamiḻ which was composed by the expert in Tamiḻ placing his love on god.
paralōkam piriyār
will always reside in the upper world.

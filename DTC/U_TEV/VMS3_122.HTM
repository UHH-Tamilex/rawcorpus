{3:122}__1+
{$} pūṅkoṭi maṭavāḷ umai orupākam puritaru caṭaimuṭi aṭikaḷ
the deity who has matted hair coiled into the form of a crown and desired to have as a part Umai, a lady as slender as a beautiful creeper.
vīṅku iruḷ naṭṭam āṭum em vikirtar viruppoṭum uṟaiviṭam viṉavil
if one asks about the place where the god who is different from the world, and dances in the dense darkness.
[[naḷḷiruḷil naṭṭam payiṉṟu āṭum nātaṉē (Tiruvācakam, Civapurāṇam)]]
tēm kamaḻ poḻilil ceḻumalar kōti
picking the fertile flowers in the garden where honey spreads its fragrance
ceṟitaruvaṇṭu icai pāṭum
crowded beetles hum like music
ōṅkiya pukaḻ ār ōmam ām puliyūr uṭaiyavar vaṭataḷi atuvē
it is the northern temple of the god who has Ōmampuliyūr which has increasing fame.
[[kōtippāṭum ōmamām puliyūr, pukaḻ ār ōmamām puliyūr is the syntactical link]]
{3:122}__2+
{$} camparaṟku aruḷi
Bestowing his grace on Camparaṉ
[[This story is not clear]]
calantaraṉ vīyattaḻal umiḻ cakkaram paṭaitta emperumāṉār
our god who created a discus which spat fire to kill Calantaraṉ
imaiyavar ētta iṉitiṉ aṅku uṟaiviṭam viṉavil
if any one asks the place where he happily dwells to be praised by the Tevar who do not wink.
amparam āki
Being the sky
aḻal (1) umiḻ pukaiyiṉ ākutiyāl maḻaipoḻiyum umparkaḷ ēttum ōmamām puliyūr uṭaiyavar
the god who has Ōmāmpuliyūr praised by the inhabitants of heaven, and where the rain pours because of the smoke of oblations offered in the consecrated fire.
[[poḻiyum, ēttum adjectival participles qualify ōmamām puliyūr]]
vaṭataḷi atuvē
is the northern temple.

{3:122}__3+
{$} pāṅkuṭait tavattuppakīrataṟku aruḷip paṭar caṭaikkaranta nīrk kaṅkai tāṅkutal tavirttut tarātalattu iḻitta tattuvaṉ uṟaiviṭam viṉavil
if any one asks about the residence of the ultimate reality who let down Kaṅkai without making it stay on his head, which he concealed in his spreading matted hair, in order to satisfy the wish of Pakīrataṉ who did severe penance.
āṅku erimūṉṟum amarntu uṭaṉ irunta aṅkaiyāl ākuti vēṭkum ōṅkiya maṟaiyōr ōmamām puliyūr uṭaiyavar vaṭataḷiyatuvē
is the northern temple of the god who is in Ōmāmpuliyūr where famous brahmins maintain the three fires which remain in their palms wishing it, by offering oblations.
{3:122}__4+
{$} puṟṟu aravu aṇintu
adorning himself with the cobras living in anthills.
nīṟumeypūci
besmearing his body with the holy ash.
pūtaṅkaḷ cūḻtara
being surrounded by pūtam.
peṟṟam oṉṟu ēṟi
and riding on a bull
ūr ūr pey pali koḷḷum pirāṉavaṉ uṟaiviṭam viṉavil
if any one asks where the master who receives alms given, in many places, dwells.
kaṟṟa nāl vētam aṅkam ōr āṟum karutiṉār aruttiyāl teriyum uṟṟa pal pukaḻār ōmamāmpuliyūr uṭaiyavar vaṭataḷiyatuvē
it is the northern temple of the god who has Ōmāmpuliyūr where brahmins who have great fame by retaining in their memory the six aṅkams and the four vētams which they have learnt by heart.
{3:122}__5+
{$} nilattavar vāṉam āḷpavar kīḻōr tuyarkeṭa
in order to remove the sufferings of the people of this earth, the rulers of heaven and the inhabitants of the nether regions
neṭiya māṟku aruḷāl, alaittaval acurar ācaṟa āḻi aḷittavaṉ uṟaiviṭam viṉavil
if one asks about the abode of the god who, out of his grace, granted a discus to the tall Māl, to completely destroy the strong acurar who harassed good people.
calattiṉāl poruḷkaḷ vēṇṭutal ceyyāt taṉmaiyār
people who do not desire wealth got by unfair and dishonest means.
naṉmaiyāl mikka ulappu il pal pukaḻār ōmamām puliyūr uṭaiyavar vaṭataḷiyatuvē
it is the northern temple of the god in Ōmāmpuliyūr where those who have earned several kinds of fame which will never die, and which are eminent by performing good acts.
{3:122}__6+
{$} maṇam tikaḻ ticaikaḷ eṭṭum ēḻ icaiyum
the eight directions in which fragrance is eminent and seven notes of music
maliyum āṟu aṅkam aivēḷvi iṇainta nālvētam mūṉṟu eri iraṇṭu piṟappu eṉa orumaiyāl uṇarum kuṇaṅkaḷum avaṟṟiṉ koḷ poruḷ kuṟṟam maṟṟavai uṟṟatum ellām uṇarpavar vāḻum ōmamām puliyūr uṭaiyavar vaṭataḷiyatuvē
is the northern temple of the god in Ōmāmpuliyūr where brahmins who are able to understand the flourshing six aṅkams, five sacrifices, four vetams that can be compared to themselves, maintain the three fires, have two births and know how to differentiate good qualities, and faults and other things pertaining to them, by their concentration of mind

malaieṉa ōṅkum māḷikai nilavum (ōmām puliyūr)

is the northern shrine of the Lord who has Ōmāmpuliyūr which has permanent high walls of enclosure which cannot be conquered by enemies, and where scholars whose different kinds of fame have spread far and wide.

{6:8}__1+
{$} kāḷatti kāṇappaṭṭa kaṇanātaṉ (kāṇ) avaṉ eṉ kaṇ uḷāṉē
the Lord whom I had a vision in Kāḷatti is the master of Civakaṇam; he remins in my eyes.
viṟṟu ūṇ oṉṟu illāta nalkūrntāṉ (kāṇ)
is such a poor person that he has no sundry articles which can be sold for dily food.
viyaṉ kaccik kampaṉ (kāṇ)
is Kambaṉ in extensive Kacci.
paccaiyallāl maṟṟu ūṇ oṉṟu illāta mācaturaṉ (kāṇ)
is such a great skilled person that he has no other food except alms.
mayāṉattu maintaṉkāṇ
is the strong youth in the cremation ground.
mācu oṉṟu illāp poṉ tūṇ(kāṇ)
is the pillar of gold which has no defected.
māmaṇi naṟkuṉṟu oppāṉ (kāṇ)
is like the beautiful hill made of big gems.
poyyātu poḻil ēḻum tāṅki niṉṟa kaltūṇ (kāṇ)
is the pillar of strong granite which supports all the seven worlds without failure and did not break.

Third Tirumuṟai (127 patikam-s in 9 paṇ-s)

This is the text as it stands in the PIFI edition. Standard editions only contain 125 patikam-s. The PIFI edition also includes: (1) a patikam which was found in an inscription engraved at a temple [3:126]; (2) another one supposed to have been found in an old palm leaf manuscript [3:127]; (3) an aprocryphal patikam, known as veḷḷip patikam [3:AAA] (see PIFI, vol.68.2, appendix, p.507).

List of paṇ-s
  1. kāntārapañcamam: 1 to 23
  2. kolli: 24 to 41
  3. kollikkauvāṇam: 42
  4. kaucikam: 43 to 55, 117, and 127
  5. pañcamam: 56 to 66
  6. cātāri: 67 to 99
  7. paḻampañcuram: 100 to 116
  8. puṟanīrmai: 118 to 123
  9. antāḷikkuṟiñci: 124 to 125

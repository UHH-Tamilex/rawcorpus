{$}
nontā oṇcuṭarē
Civaṉ who is like the lamp which is burning brightly without trimming! (perpetual lamp)
entaipirāṉ
the master of my father.
tirumēṟṟaḷi uṟaiyum entāy
my father who dwells in Tiru Mēṟṟaḷi.
nuṉaiyē niṉaintiruntēṉ maṉamē pukuntu niṉṟa cintāy
the object of meditation which entered into my mind which was always thinking of you only.
vantāy pōy aṟiyāy
having entered into my mind you do not know to leave it.
uṉṉai allāl iṉi ētta māṭṭēṉ
definitely I will not prise any one else here after.

{$}
cēṭṭu ār māḷikai cūḻ tirumēṟṟaḷi uṟaiyum māṭṭē
my wealth that dwells in tirumēṟṟaḷi surrounded by great mansions!
[[māṭṭē the underlined letter is augmentation for rhyme.]]
āḷtāṉ paṭṭamaiyāl
having become your slave.
aṭiyārkkut toṇṭu paṭṭu
doing service to your devotees.
kēṭṭēṉ kēṭpatu ellām
I received all that I request you to grant me.
(1) piṟavāvakai kēṭṭu oḻintēṉ
I stopped with asking from you the means for not being born agin.
uṉṉai allāl makiḻntu ētta māṭṭēṉē
definitely I will not prise with joy any one else except you.
[[Variant reading: (1) piṟavāmai]]
{7:21}__3+
{$}
cēṟu ār taṇ kaḻaṉit tirumēṟṟaḷi uṟaiyum ēṟē
Civaṉ who is like the lion and dwells in Tirumeṟṟaḷi surrounded by cool fields full of mire!
mōṟāntu ōr orukāl niṉaiyātiruntālum
though I may not think of you at some times out of laziness.
[[mōṟāntu may also mean being like a savage.]]
vēṟāvantu eṉ uḷḷam pukavalla meypporuḷē
the real thing that is capable of entering into my mind by some other way
uṉṉai allāl iṉi ēttamāṭṭēṉē
.... definitely I will not hire anyone hereafter.
{7:21}__4+
{$}
tirumēṟṟaḷiyuṟaiyum paṟṟē
the support which dwells in tirumēṟṟaḷi!
uṟṟār cuṟṟam eṉṉum(m) atuviṭṭu nuṉ aṭaintēṉ
I approached you renouncing near relatives and distant relatives
[[uṟṟār denotes wife and children who are near relatives.]]
eṟṟāl eṉkuṟai
is there any want for my by anything?
eṉ iṭarait tuṟantu oḻintēṉ
I definitely renounced my sufferings.
mummatilum ceṟṟāy
you destroyed all the three forts.
(1) nuṉṉai allāl paṇintu ēttamāṭṭēṉē
definitely I will not prise any one else except you, bowing before them.
[[Variant reading: (1) uṉṉaiallāl]]
{7:21}__5+
{$}
meymmāl āyiṉa tīrttu aruḷceyyum meypporuḷē
the real thing that grants its grace removing the confusion born out of this body.
kaṉam mēṟṟaḷi uṟaiyum pemmāṉ
the god who dwells in the eminent mēṟṟaḷi!
kaimmā īr uriyāy
you who wears a skin wet with blood flayed from an elephant!
(1) emmāṉ emmāṉai eṉṟavar iṭṭu iṟantu oḻintār
those who are my father and my mother are dead and gone, leaving me to shift for myself.
uṉṉai allāl peritu ētta māṭṭēṉē
definitely I will not prise any other god except you thinking him to be great.
[[Variant readings: (1) emmāṉ emmaṉaiyē veṉṟava: emmāṉ emmaṉaiyēl eṉpava]]

eṉ oṇcuṭarē
my bright lamp!
tēṉē
who is as sweet as honey!
iṉṉamutē
the sweet nectar!
tirumēṟṟaḷiyuṟaiyum kōṉē
the King who dwells in tirumēṟṟaḷi!
nāṉēl
speaking about myself.
uṉ aṭiyē niṉaintēṉ
I meditated on your feet only.
niṉaitalumē
as soon as I did so.
ūṇ ēr ivvuṭalam pukuntāy
you entered into this body of mine which is made of flesh.
uṉṉai allāl kuḷirntu ētta māṭṭēṉē
definitely I will not prise any other god except you to the gratification of my mind.
[[Variant reading: (1) ūṉār ivvuṭalam]]

ceyyār paiṅkamalat tirumēṟṟaḷi uṟaiyum aiyā
the master who dwells in tirumēṟṟali which has fertile lotuses growing in the fields!
emperumāṉ
my god, Civaṉ!
kai ār veñcilai nāṇ ataṉmēl caram kōttēmum matilum eriyuṇṇac caramkōttāy
you fixed arrow on the bow-string of the cruel bow held in the hand and discharged it for the fire to consume all the three forts.
uṉṉai allāl aṟintu ētta māṭṭēṉē
definitely I will not prise any other god except you, knowing your greatness and their inferiority;
[[ōṅku malaip peruvil pāmpu nāṇ koḷīi, orukaṇaikoṇṭu mūeyil uṭaṟṟip, peruviṟal amararkku veṉṟi tanta kaṟaimiṭaṟṟu aṇṇal (puṟam, 55-1-4)]]

{$}
viraiār koṉṟaiyiṉāy
god who wears a garland fragrant koṉṟi flowers!
vimalā
immaculate Civaṉ!
tiraiyār taṇ kaḻaṉit tirumēṟṟaḷiyuṟaiyum araiyā
the King in tirumēṟṟaḷi which has cool fields which have water with waves.
iṉi uṉṉai allāl uṭalil uyir uḷḷaḷavum naravataṉāl uraiyēṉ
I will not speak about others as gods, as long as life exists in my body, now with my tongue.
uṉṉai allāl aṟintu ēttamāṭṭēṉ
definitely I will not prise any other god except you esteeming him superior to you.

talaivā
the chief!
cilai ār māmatil cūḻ tirumēṟṟaḷiyuṟaiyum malaiyē
Civaṉ who is the mountin who dwells in tirumēṟṟali surrounded by big walls of fortifications built with granite stones.
nilaiyā niṉ aṭiyē niṉaintēṉ
I meditated on your feet only steadfastly.
niṉaittalumē
as I did so.
niṉniṉaiyap paṇittāy
you ordered me to think of you only without wavering.
calam oḻintēṉ
I had my sufferings removed.
uṉṉai allāl makiḻntu ētta māṭṭēṉē
definitely I will not prise any other god except you; with joy.

pār ūr pallavaṉ ūr matilkāñci mānakarvāy
in the big city of Kāñci which is the capital of the pallavaṉ whose away extends over the whole world.
cīrūrum puṟaviṉ tirumēṟṟaḷic civaṉai
on Civaṉ who dwells in tirumēṟṟali which has forest tracts whose renown has spread everywhere.
ārūraṉ(ṉ) aṭiyāṉ aṭittoṇṭaṉ ārūraṉ coṉṉa cīr ūr pāṭal vallār civalōkam cērvārē
those who are able to sing the songs which have measure of time composed by ārūraṉ who venders service in the sanctum of Civaṉ and who is the slave of the god in ārūr.

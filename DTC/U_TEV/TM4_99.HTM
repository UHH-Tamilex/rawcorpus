  • talam: tiru kacci ēkampam
  • (()): tiru viruttam
  • VMS: Vol.13, p.26

*1
_ōtuvittāy, muṉ _aṟa_uri; kāṭṭi _amaṇaroṭē
kātuvittāy; kaṭṭam, nōy, piṇi, tīrttāy; kalantu_aruḷip
pōtuvittāy; niṉ paṇi piḻikkiṉ puḷiyamvaḷārāl
mōtuvippāy; _ukappāy; muṉivāy---kacci _ēkampaṉē!

*2
_ettikkoṭu _ettaki _ēḻi _amaṇoṭu _icivittu,---_eṉi,---
kottikku mūṅkar vaḻi kāṭṭuvitteṉṉak kōku ceytāy?---
muttiṉtiraḷum paḷiṅkiṉiṉ cōtiyum moy pavaḷat-
tottiṉi _ēykkum paṭiyāy! poḻil kacci _ēkampaṉē!

*3
mey _ampu kōtta vicayaṉoṭu _aṉṟu _oru vēṭuvaṉāyp
poy _ampu _eytu, _āvam _aruḷicceytāy; purammūṉṟu _eriyak
ki _ampu _eytāy; nuṉ kaḻal_aṭi pōṟṟāk kayavar neñcil
kuyyam peytāl---koṭi mā matil cūḻ kacci _ēkampaṉē!

*4
kuṟikkoṇṭu _iruntu centāmari_āyiram vikalvikal
neṟippaṭa _iṇṭi puṉikiṉṟa māli niṟi _aḻippāṉ,
kaṟikkaṇṭa! nī _oru pūk kuṟivittuk kaṇ cūlvippatē?---
piṟittuṇṭa vārcaṭiyāy! peruṅ kāñci _em piññakaṉē!

*5
_urikkum kaḻintu _iṅku _uṇarvu _ariyāṉ; _uḷkuvār viṉiyik
karikkum _eṉak kitoḻuvatu_allāl, katirōrkaḷ_ellām,
virik koḷ malaravaṉ, māl, _eṇvacukkaḷ, _ēkātacarkaḷ,
_irikkum _amirtarkku, _aṟiya _oṇṇāṉ---_eṅkaḷ _ēkampaṉē.

*6
karu _uṟṟa nāḷ mutal_āka _uṉ pātamē kāṇpataṟku(v)
_urukiṟṟu, _eṉ _uḷḷamum; nāṉum kiṭantu _alantu _eyttoḻintēṉ;
tiru _oṟṟiyūrā! tiru _ālavāyā! tiru _ārūrā!
_oru paṟṟu _ilāmiyum kaṇṭu _iraṅkāy---kacci _ēkampaṉē!

*7
_ari, _ayaṉ, _intiraṉ, cantirātittar, _amarar_ellām,
_uriya niṉ koṟṟak kaṭittaliyār _uṇaṅkāk kiṭantār;
puritaru puṉcaṭip pōka muṉivar pulampukiṉṟār;---
_eritaru ceñcaṭi _ēkampa!---_eṉṉō, tirukkuṟippē?

*8
pāmpu _aric cērttip paṭarum caṭimuṭip pālvaṇṇaṉē!
kūmpalic ceyta karatalattu _aṉparkaḷ kūṭip pal-nāḷ
cāmpalip pūci, tariyil puraṇṭu, niṉ tāḷ caraṇ _eṉṟu
_ēmpalippārkaṭku _iraṅkukaṇṭāy---kacci _ēkampaṉē!

*9
_ēṉṟukoṇṭāy, _eṉṉi; _emperumāṉ! _iṉi, _allam _eṉṉil,
cāṉṟukaṇṭāy _iv _ulakam_ellām; taṉiyēṉ _eṉṟu _eṉṉi
_ūṉṟi niṉṟār _aivarkku _oṟṟi vittāy; piṉṉi _oṟṟi_ellām
cōṉṟukoṇṭāy---kacci _ēkampam mēya cuṭarvaṇṇaṉē!

*10
_unti niṉṟār, _uṉtaṉ _ōlakkac cūḷikaḷ; vāytal paṟṟit
tuṉṟi niṉṟār, tollivāṉavar_īṭṭam; paṇi _aṟivāṉ
vantu niṉṟār, _ayaṉum tirumālum;---matil kacciyāy!---
_inta niṉṟōm _iṉi _eṅṅaṉamō, vantu _iṟiñcuvatē?

{1:120}__1+
{$} paṇintavar aruviṉai paṟṟu aruḷ ceyattuṇintavaṉ
Civaṉ who is determined to cut the roots of attachments and Karnams that cannot be removed except by his grace, of those who bow before him.
tōloṭu nūltutai mārpiṉil aravoṭupēr eḻil āmai koṇṭu piṇintavaṉ
who tied the shell of a big and beautiful tortoise and a cobra in his chest in which an elephant's skin and sacred thread are close to each other. (piṇintavaṉ=piṇittavaṉ)
vaḷanakar amtaṇ aiyāṟu
his fertile city is beautiful and cool aiyāṟu.
{1:120}__2+
{$} kīrttimikkavalnakar kiḷar oḷiyuṭaṉ aṭap pārttavaṉ
Civaṉ fixed his gaze for the raging fire to destroy the strong and very famous cities.
paṉimati paṭar caṭaivaittu
having placed on the spreading caṭai the cool crescent.
kariuri pōrttavaṉ
covered himself with the skin of an elephant.
puli ataḷ aravu arai ārttavaṉ vaḷanakar amtaṇ aiyāṟu
the beautiful and cool aiyāṟu is the fertile city of one who fastened in his waist a tiger's skin and a cobra. (There are some Variation about the storys of destruction of three cities) They are Civaṉ destroyed them by discharging an arrow, by his looks, and by his laughter. All these find a place in tēvāram).
{1:120}__3+
{$} varinta veñcilai piṭittu avuṇartam vaḷanakar erintu aṟa eytavaṉ
Civaṉ who shot an arrow to destroy by fire the flourishing cities of the acurar, holding a cruel bow fastened by several bands.
[[varinta veñcilaiyoṉṟuṭaiyāṉ (campantar, maḻapāṭi, 6);
varinta veñcilaiyāl antaratteyilai vāṭṭiya (cuntarar, tiruppaccilācirāmam, 5)]]
eḻil tikaḻ malarmēl iruntavaṉ ciram atu imaiyavarkuṟai koḷa arintavaṉ vaḷanakar amtaṇ aiyāṟu
the cool and beautiful aiyāṟu is the fertile city of Civaṉ who cut off the head of Piramaṉ who was seated in the eminent and beautiful flowers of lotus, when the celestials who do not wink, wanted to get their grievances redressed.
[[ātikkaṇāṉ mukattil oṉṟu ceṉṟu allāta colluraikkat taṉ kai vāḷāl, cētittatiruvaṭiyai (appar, tirunaḷḷāṟu, 1)]]
{1:120}__4+
{$} am taṇ aiyāṟu
beautiful and cool aiyāṟu.
vāyntaval avuṇartam vaḷanakar ericaṭai māyntu aṟa eytavaṉ
Civaṉ who destroyed the properous cities of the strong acurar who had the benefit of boons, to be consumed by fire, by discharging an arrow.
vaḷarpiṟai viripuṉal tōyntu eḻu caṭaiyiṉaṉ
has a rising caṭai in which the growing crescent andspreading water have a dip.
tol maṟai āṟu aṅkam āyntavaṉ vaḷanakar
is the fertile city of Civaṉ who is incestigated by the old vetams and six aṅkams.
{1:120}__5+
{$} amtaṇ aiyāṟu
the beautiful and cool aiyāṟu.
vāṉ amar matipulku caṭaiyiṭai aravoṭu tēṉ amar koṉṟaiyaṉ
Civaṉ who wears koṉṟai flowers having honey, along with the cobra, in the caṭai to which the crescent which is in the sky, is attached.
tikaḻtaru (1) mārpiṉaṉ
who has a chest in which many adornments shine.
māṉ aṉa meṉviḻi maṅkai ōrpākamum āṉavaṉ vaḷanakar
is the fertile city of one who has a lady of gentle looks like a deer, as his half,
[[Variant reading: (1) mārpiṉiṉ this seems to be a better reading according to the 7th verse]]
{1:120}__6+
{$} amtaṇ aiyāṟu
the beautiful and cool Aiyāṟu.
muṉpaṉai
the strong Civaṉ.
muṉivarōṭu (1) amararkaḷ aṭitoḻum iṉpaṉai
who is the fountain of happiness, and whose feet the immortals along with sages worship.
iṇaiila iṟaivaṉai
the supreme god who has nothing comparable to him.
eḻiltikaḻ eṉpoṉai
my possession of beautiful gold.
ētam il vētiyartāmtoḻum (2) aṉpaṉa vaḷanakar
is the fertile city of one who is love and who is worshipped by the blemishless brahmins.
[[Variant reading: (1) amarartoḻuteḻum (2) aṉpaṉaṉ]]
{1:120}__7+
{$} amtaṇ aiyāṟu
the beautiful and cool aiyāṟu.
(1) vaṉtiṟal avuṇartam vaḷanakar eriyiṭai ventaṟa eytavaṉ
Civaṉ who shot an arrow to be burnt well by fire, at the three flourishing cities of acurar of immense strength.
viḷaṅkiya mārpiṉil pantu amar melviral pākam atuāki taṉ antam il vaḷanakar
is the fertile city which knows no destruction and which belongs to Civaṉ who has a lady of soft fingers which hold a ball, as his half on his chest.
[[Variant reading: (1) vantiṟal]]

{$} viṭaitta val arakkaṉ nalveṟpiṉai eṭuttalum
as soon as the strong arakkaṉ burst into a rage and uprooted and lifted the good mountain.
[[viṭaitta to burst into a rage;
viṭaittuvarum ilaṅkaikkōṉ (campantar puḷḷirukkuvēḷur, 10);
veṅkaṇ poṟi citaṟak kaṭitu urumēṟu eṉa viṭaiyā(Kamparāmayanam, paracurāmappaṭalam, 16); it means also abounding in;
puṉṉai malarnāṟṟam viṭaittēvarum teṉṟal miku vēṇupuramatuvē (campantar vēṇupuram, 2)]]
aṭittalattāl iṟai ūṉṟi maṟṟavaṉatu muṭittalaitōḷ avai neritara muṟaimuṟai aṭarttavaṉ vaḷanakar amtaṇaiyāṟu
the cool and beautiful aiyāṟu is the fertile city of Civaṉ who pressed down to crush in an order the shoulders and heads wearing crowns, by gently fixing his feet.
{1:120}__9+
{$} viṇṇavar tammoṭu veṅkatirōṉ nal eṇili tēvarkaḷ intiraṉ vaḻipaṭa
to be worshipped by intiraṉ, countless tēvar and the sun-god in company with residents of heaven.
kaṇṇaṉum piramaṉum kāṇpu aritu ākiya aṇṇaltaṉavaḷanakar amtaṇaaiyāṟu
the beautiful and cool Aiyāṟu is the fertile city of the eminent god whom it was difficult to find out for Kaṇṇaṉ (Māl) and Piramaṉ.
{1:120}__10+
{$} maruḷ uṭai maṉattu vaṇcamaṇarkaḷ
the strong amaṇar who have confused minds.
mācu aṟā iruḷ uṭai iṇaittuvarp pōrvaiyiṉārkaḷum
and ignorant buddhists who have two coverings for their bodies soaked in myrtle dye, and are not free flaws.
teruḷuṭai maṉattavar tēṟumiṉ
without coming into contact with the above mentioned people, you who are endowed with clear knowledge, be definite.
tiṇṇamā aruḷuṭai aṭikaḷ tam amtaṇ aiyāṟu
Be clear in your minds dispelling doubts that the beautiful and cool aiyāṟu is the shrine of the god who has grace.

{$} alaimalipuṉal malkum amtaṇ aiyāṟṟiṉai
about aiyāṟu in which water with waves is increasing.
nalammali iṉtamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉatu kalaimali tamiḻ ivai kaṟṟu vallār
those who have learnt these Tamiḻ verses which are full of artistic beauty composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ proficient in sweet Tamiḻ of many beauties, and become well-versed in them.
mika nalam malipukaḻmiku naṉmaiyarē
are good people with increasing fame and get many good things accruing from it.

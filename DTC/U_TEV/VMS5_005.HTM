{5:5}__1+
{$}
paṭṭi ēṟu ukantu ēṟi
riding with joy on a straying bull.
pala i(l)lam iṭṭamāka irantu uṇṭu uḻitarum aṭṭamūrtti aṇṇāmalai kaitoḻa
to worship with joined hands Aṇṇāmali of Civaṉ who has eight forms, and wanders collecting alms with desire, in many houses and eats it.
keṭṭuppōmviṉai
the acts, good and bad, will definitely be destroyed.
kēṭu illai; kāṇmiṉ(ē)
there will be no indigence (People of the world!) you know this in your experience.
{5:5}__2+
{$}
peṟṟam ēṟuvar peypalikku eṉṟu avar; cuṟṟam ām miku (1) tol pukaḻāḷoṭum
Civaṉ will come riding on a bull along with Umi who has increasing ancient fame, and who is related to him as his wife to receive alms offered to him.
aṟṟam tīrkkum aṇṇāmalai kaitoḻa
to worship with joined hands aṇṇāmali which removes the sufferings of devotees.
naṟṟavattōṭu ñāṉattu iruppar (ē)
those devotees will be performing penance to be rid of the cycle of birth and death and be firm in their spiritual knowledge.
[[Variant reading: (1) tol kuḻalāḷoṭum]]
{5:5}__3+
{$}
pal il ōṭu kai ēntip pala i(l)lam ollai ceṉṟu uṇaṅkal kavarvār avar allal tīrkkum aṇṇāmalai kaitoḻa
to worship with joined hands aṇṇāmali which removes the sufferings and which is the shrine of Civaṉ who eagerly begged cooked food going quickly to many houses holding in the hand a skull without teeth.
nalla āyiṉa nammai aṭaiyum (ē)
all good things will reach us of their own accord.
{5:5}__4+
{$}
pāṭic ceṉṟu
going singing songs.
palikku eṉṟu niṉṟavar
Civaṉ who stood before houses to receive alms.
ōṭippōyiṉar
ran away stealing our heart.
ceyvatu oṉṟu eṉ(kol, ō)
what is to be done.
āṭippāṭi aṇṇāmalaikaitoḻa (1) ōṭippōkum nam mēlaiviṉaikaḷē
our accumulated acts done in previous births will flee from us if we worship with joined hands aṇṇāmali dancing and singing.
[[Variant readings: (1) ōṭippōnnammallal viṉaikaḷē, ōṭippōṉa nam mallal viṉaikaḷē]]
{5:5}__5+
{$}
tēṭic ceṉṟu tiruntu aṭi ēttumiṉ
prise the perfect feet of Civaṉ enquiring about Aṇṇāmali, his dwelling place going there (People of this world!)
nāṭivantu avar nammaiyum āṭkoḷvar
he will admit us who are not worthy of receiving his grace, coming to us and seeking us.
āṭippāṭi aṇṇāmalai kaitoḻa; ōṭippōm namatu uḷḷa viṉaikaḷ (ē)
the acts that are still with us will flee away from us if we worship with joined hands aṇṇāmali, dancing and singing.
{5:5}__6+
{$}
karumpiṉiṭaik kaṭṭi okkum
Civaṉ is as sweet as the lump of jaggery.
tuṇi veṭṭi vīṇaikaḷpāṭum vikirtaṉār
who is different from the world and plays on the vīṇi ascertining the sound and severing its strings.
aṭṭamūrtti aṇṇāmalai mēviya naṭṭam āṭiyai naṇṇa naṉku ākumē
It will be good if one approaches the dancer who dwells with desire in aṇṇāmali and who has eight forms.
{5:5}__7+
{$}
kōṇik koṇṭaiyar
Civaṉ has a curved caṭi dressed in a large coil on the head.
vēṭam muṉ koṇṭavar
he took many forms long ago.
pāṇi naṭṭaṅkaḷai āṭum paramaṉār
the supreme god who performs dances to measure of time.
āṇippoṉṉaṉ aṇṇāmalai kaitoḻap pēṇi niṉṟa peruviṉai pōkumē
the big acts which are with us with intense attachment will take leave of us, if we worship with joined hands aṇṇāmali of the god who is like the gold of the finest quality;
[[ārāyntu aṭittoṇṭar āṇippoṉ (nāvukkaracar, tiruvārūr (9) 2);
āṇip poṉṉāṟceyta āypoṉ uṭaimaṇi (periyāḻvār tirumoḻi, 1-7 = 1)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) kōṇik koṇṭayarvēṭam, kōṇikkoṇṭavarvēṭam]]
{5:5}__8+
{$}
kaṇṭam tāṉ kaṟuttāṉ
Civaṉ has a black neck.
kālaṉ āruyir paṇṭu kāl koṭu pāynta paraṉār
the supreme being who pounced upon the previous life of Kālaṉ (god of death) and took it away kicking with his leg, long ago.
aṇṭattu ōṅkum aṇṇāmalai kaitoḻa viṇṭupōm (1) nammēlaiviṉaikaḷē
our act done in previous births will go away separated from us; if we worship with joined hands aṇṇāmali which rises high to touch the heaven.
[[Variant reading: (1) nammēlaviṉaikaḷē]]
{5:5}__9+
{$}
muntic ceṉṟu
going early in life.
anti vāy oḷiyāṉ taṉ aṇṇāmalai muppōtum vaṇaṅkumiṉ
pay homage thrice in a day to Aṇṇāmali of the god who has the colour of the evening red sky. (People of this world!)
kanta māmalar cūṭum karuttaṉ cintiyā eḻuvārviṉai tīrttiṭum
the chief who wears big fragrant flowers will destroy the acts of those who get up from sleep thinking of the hill.
{5:5}__10+
{$}
maṟaiyiṉāṉoṭu mālavaṉkāṇkilā niṟaiyum nīrmaiyuḷ niṉṟu aruḷ ceytavaṉ uṟaiyum māṇpiṉ aṇṇāmalai kaitoḻa
if we worship with joined hands aṇṇāmali which has the greatness as the dwelling place of Civaṉ who granted his grace by his nature of pervading everywhere and who could not be seen by Piramaṉ well-versed in vētams, and by Māl.
nām ceyta pāvaṅkaḷ āṉavē paṟaiyum
sinful acts only committed by us will disappear.
[[Note : there is no mention of irāvaṉaṉ in this 10th verse]]

• patikam: [3:7]
  • talam: tirup pukali (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: kāntārap pañcamam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.227

*1
kaṇnutalāṉum, veṇnīṟṟiṉāṉum, kaḻal _ārkkavē
paṇ _icai pāṭa niṉṟu _āṭiṉāṉum, parañcōtiyum---
puṇṇiya nālmaṟaiyōrkaḷ _ēttum pukali(n)nakar,
peṇṇiṉnallāḷoṭum vīṟṟirunta perumāṉ _aṉṟē!

*2
cāmpalōṭum taḻal_āṭiṉāṉum, caṭaiyiṉmicaip
pāmpiṉōṭum mati cūṭiṉāṉum, pacu_ēṟiyum---
pūm paṭukal(l) _iḷa vāḷai pāyum pukali(n)nakar,
kāmpu _aṉa tōḷiyōṭum(m) _irunta kaṭavuḷ _aṉṟē!

*3
karuppu nal vār cilaik kāmaṉ vēvak kaṭaikkaṇṭāṉum,
maruppu nal _āṉaiyiṉ _īr_uri pōrtta maṇāḷaṉum---
poruppu _aṉa mā maṇi māṭam _ōṅkum pukali(n)nakar,
viruppiṉ nallāḷoṭum vīṟṟirunta vimalaṉ _aṉṟē!

*4
_aṅkai _ilaṅku _aḻal _ēntiṉāṉum, _aḻaku_ākavē
kaṅkaiyaic ceñcaṭai cūṭiṉāṉum, kaṭaliṉ(ṉ)_iṭaip
poṅkiya nañcu _amutu_uṇṭavaṉum---pukali(n)nakar,
maṅkainallāḷoṭum vīṟṟirunta maṇavāḷaṉē.

*5
cāmanalvētaṉum, takkaṉtaṉ vēḷvi takarttāṉum,
nāmam nūṟu-_āyiram colli vāṉōr toḻum nātaṉum---
pū malku taṇpoḻil maṉṉum _am taṇ pukali(n)nakar,
kōmaḷamātoṭum vīṟṟirunta kuḻakaṉ _aṉṟē!

*6
_iravu_iṭai _oḷ_eri_āṭiṉāṉum, _imaiyōr toḻac
ceru_iṭai muppuram tī _eritta civalōkaṉum,
poru viṭai _oṉṟu _ukantu _ēṟiṉāṉum---pukali(n)nakar,
_aravu_iṭai mātoṭum vīṟṟirunta _aḻakaṉ _aṉṟē!

*7
cērppatu tiṇ cilai mēviṉāṉum, tikaḻ pālaṉmēl
vērppatuceyta veṅkūṟṟu _utaittāṉum---vēḷvippukai
pōrppatuceytu _aṇi māṭam _ōṅkum pukali(n)nakar,
pārppatiyōṭu _uṭaṉvīṟṟirunta paramaṉ _aṉṟē!

*8
kal-neṭumālvaraikkīḻ _arakkaṉ(ṉ) _iṭar kaṇṭāṉum,
vil-neṭum pōr viṟal vēṭaṉ_āki vicayaṟku _oru
poṉ neṭuṅkōl koṭuttāṉum---taṇ pukali(n)nakar,
_aṉṉam _aṉṉa(n) naṭai maṅkaiyoṭum _amarntāṉ _aṉṟē!

*9
poṉniṟa nāṉmukaṉ, paccaiyāṉ, _eṉṟu _ivar pukkuḻit
taṉṉai _iṉṉāṉ _eṉak kāṇpu _ariya taḻalcōtiyum---
puṉṉai poṉtātu _utir malkum _am taṇ pukali(n)nakar,
miṉ_iṭai mātoṭum vīṟṟirunta vimalaṉ _aṉṟē!

*10
piṇṭiyum pōtiyum pēṇuvār pēṇaip pēṇātatu _ōr,
toṇṭarum kātalcey, cōti_āya cuṭarccōtiyāṉ---
puṇṭarīkammalarp poykai cūḻnta pukali(n)nakar,
vaṇṭu _amar kōtaiyoṭum(m) _irunta maṇavāḷaṉē.

*11
pūṅ kamaḻ kōtaiyoṭum(m) _iruntāṉ, pukali(n)nakarp
pāṅkaṉai, ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa tamiḻ pattu_ivai,
_āṅku _amarvu _eytiya _āti_āka, _icai vallavar,
_ōṅku _amarāvatiyōr toḻac celvatum _uṇmaiyē.

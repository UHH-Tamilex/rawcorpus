{6:55}__1+
{$} vēṟṟu āki viṇ āki niṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)
Civan who remins as the sky and the other four elements! Let you protect me!
mīḷāmē āḷ eṉṉaik koṇṭāy (pōṟṟi)
you admitted me as your slave without redemption.
ūṟṟu āki
being a spring of joy.
uḷḷē oḷittāy (pōṟṟi)
you hid yourself without being seen by the physical eye, in the intelligence of the soul.
ōvāta cattattu oliyē (pōṟṟi)
the letters born out of the increasing sound.
āṟṟu āki aṅkē amarntāy (pōṟṟi)
you remined in the five elements, mind and the light.
āṟu aṅkam nālvētam āṉāy (pōṟṟi)
you are the six ankams and the four vetams.
kāṟṟu āki eṅkum kalantāy (pōṟṟi)
you pervaded everywhere like the air.
kayilai malaiyāṉē (pōṟṟi pōṟṟi)
Civaṉ in Kayilimali!
[[Variant reading: (1) iruntāy]]

{$} piccāṭal pēyoṭu ukantāy (pōṟṟi)
you desired to perform many kinds of dances in the company of pēys, according to your wish.
piṟavi aṟukkum pirāṉē (pōṟṟi)
the master who completely removes births!
vaiccu āṭal (1) naṉṟu makiḻntāy (pōṟṟi)
placing the living being in many kinds of birth, greatly rejoiced in seeing them functioning.
maruvi eṉ cintai pukuntāy (pōṟṟi)
you entered into my mind coming near me.
poyccār puram mūṉṟum eytāy (pōṟṟi)

you entered into my mind never leaving it.
kaccu āka nākam acaittāy (pōṟṟi)
you tied a cobra as a girdle in your waist.
kayilai malaiyāṉē pōṟṟi pōṟṟi
see 1st verse.

tēvar aṟiyāta tēvē (pōṟṟi)
the god whom the ordinary celestials could not know.
pul uyirkkum pūṭci puṇarntāy (pōṟṟi)

kal uyirāy niṉṟa kaṉalē (pōṟṟi)

{$} paṇṇiṉ icaiyāki niṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)
you remined as the music produced by the aggregation of the seven notes of the diatonic scale.
pāvippār pāvam aṟuppār (pōṟṟi)
you cut at the root of the sins of those who meditate on you.
eṇṇum eḻuttum col āṉāy (pōṟṟi)

eṉ cintai nīṅkā iṟaivā (pōṟṟi)
you have never taken leave of my mind.
viṇṇum nilaṉum tī (um) āṉāy (pōṟṟi)

mēlavarkkum mēl āki niṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)
you remined superior to those who are considered superior celestials.
kaṇṇiṉ maṇi āki niṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)

{6:55}__8+
{$} imaiyātu uyirātu iruntāy (pōṟṟi)
you were without winking and breathing unlike other being endowed with bodies.
eṉ cintai nīṅkā iṟaivā (pōṟṟi)
the all-pervading god who does not leave my mind.
umai pākam ākattu aṇaittāy (pōṟṟi)
you joined the part of Umi in your body.
ūḻi ēḻ āṉa oruvā (pōṟṟi)
the unequalled god who is himself the seven aeons
[[cf. eḻukolām avar ūḻ paṭaittaṉa (nāvukkaracar, viṭam tīrtha patikam, 7); seven does not mean exactly seven but stands for many]]
arumaiyā arunañcam ārntāy (pōṟṟi)
you consumed the rare poison which is not suited to the body.
ātipurāṇaṉāy niṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)
you remined as the supreme being, being the primordial one.
kamai āki niṉṟa kaṉalē (pōṟṟi)
the fire that has patience (perhaps this refers to the submarine fire in the shape of a mare's head believed to consume the world at the end of an aeon, but inactive till that time)
kayilai malaiyāṉē pōṟṟi pōṟṟi)
see 1st verse.

you are not born, do not become aged and die.
muṉṉamē tōṉṟi muḷaittāy (pōṟṟi)
you came into existence before everything was created.
tē āki (1) tēvartoḻum tēvē (pōṟṟi)
god whom the celestials Ayaṉ, ari and uruttiran worships!

āvā! aṭiyēṉukku ellām (pōṟṟi)
you are all things to me (āvā an exclamation expressive of joy)
allal naliya alantēṉ (pōṟṟi)
I suffered when mis-fortune inflicted on me pin.
kaṉakat tiraḷē kāvāy (pōṟṟi)
the help of gold! protect me.

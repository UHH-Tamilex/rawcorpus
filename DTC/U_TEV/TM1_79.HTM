• patikam: 1:79
  • talam: tiruk kaḻumalam (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: kuṟiñci
  • VMS: VOL.2 ,P.373

*1
_ayil _uṟu paṭiyiṉar; viṭiyiṉar; muṭimēl
_aravamum matiyamum viraviya _aḻakar;
mayil _uṟu cāyala vaṉamuli _orupāl
makiḻpavar; vāṉ_iṭi mukil pulkum miṭaṟar;
payilvu_uṟu caritiyar; _erutu _ukantu _ēṟip
pāṭiyum _āṭiyum pali koḷvar; vali cēr
kayiliyum potiyilum _iṭam _eṉa _uṭiyār---
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*2
koṇṭalum nīlamum puri tirumiṭaṟar;
koṭumuṭi _uṟipavar; paṭutalik kiyar;
paṇṭu _alar_ayaṉ ciram _arintavar; poruntum
paṭarcaṭi _aṭikaḷār---pati_ataṉ _ayalē
vaṇṭalum vaṅkamum caṅkamum cuṟavum
maṟikaṭal-tiri koṇarntu _eṟṟiya karimēl
kaṇṭalum kitiyum neytalum kulavum
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*3
_eṇ_iṭi _oṉṟiṉar; _iraṇṭiṉar _uruvam;
_eri_iṭi mūṉṟiṉar; nālmaṟiyāḷar;
maṇ_iṭi _aintiṉar; _āṟiṉar _aṅkam;
vakuttaṉar _ēḻ_ici; _eṭṭu _iruṅkali cēr
paṇ_iṭi _oṉpatum _uṇarntavar; pattar
pāṭi niṉṟu _aṭi toḻa, mataṉaṉi vekuṇṭa
kaṇ_iṭik kaṉaliṉar---karutiya kōyil
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*4
_eri _oru karattiṉar; _imiyavarkku _iṟivar;
_ēṟu _ukantu _ēṟuvar; nīṟu mey pūcit
tiritarum _iyalpiṉar; _ayalavar puraṅkaḷ
tī _eḻa viḻittaṉar; vēy puri tōḷi,
vari taru kaṇ_iṇi maṭavaral, _añca,
mañcu _uṟa nimirntatu _ōr vaṭivoṭum vanta
kari _uri maruviya _aṭikaḷukku _iṭam_ām
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*5
_ūr _etirntu _iṭu pali, tali kalaṉ_āka
_uṇpavar; viṇ polintu _ilaṅkiya _uruvar;
pār _etirntu _aṭi toḻa, viri tarum mārpil
paṭa_aravu _āmi _akku _aṇintavarkku _iṭam_ām---
nīr _etirntu _iḻi maṇi, nittala-muttam
niri cori caṅkamoṭu, _oṇmaṇi varaṉṟi,
kār _etirntu _ōtam vaṉ tiri karikku _ēṟṟum---
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*6
muṉ _uyirt tōṟṟamum _iṟutiyum _āki,
muṭi _uṭi _amararkaḷ _aṭi paṇintu _ētta,
piṉṉiya caṭimicip piṟi niṟivitta
pēr_aruḷāḷaṉār pēṇiya kōyil---
poṉ _iyal naṟumalar puṉaloṭu tūpam
cāntamum _ēntiya kiyiṉar _āki,
kaṉṉiyar nāḷtoṟum vēṭamē paravum---
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*7
kolikku _aṇittā varu kūṟṟu _uticeytār,
kurikaḻal paṇintavarkku _aruḷiya poruḷiṉ
nilikku _aṇittā vara niṉiya vallārtam
neṭun tuyar tavirtta _em nimalarukku _iṭam_ām---
malikku _aṇittā vara vaṉ tiri murala,
matu viri puṉṉikaḷ muttu _eṉa _arumpa,
kalikkaṇam kāṉaliṉ nīḻalil vāḻum---
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*8
puyampala _uṭiya teṉ_ilaṅkiyarvēntaṉ,
poruvari _eṭuttavaṉ, poṉmuṭi tiṇtōḷ
payampala paṭa _aṭarttu, _aruḷiya perumāṉ
parivoṭum _iṉitu _uṟi kōyil_atu _ākum---
viyaṉpala viṇṇiṉum maṇṇiṉum _eṅkum
vēṟuvēṟu _ukaṅkaḷil peyar _uḷatu _eṉṉa,
_iyampala paṭak kaṭal-tiri karikku _eṟṟum---
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*9
vilaṅkal _oṉṟu _ēnti vaṉmaḻi taṭuttōṉum,
veṟi kamaḻ tāmariyōṉum, _eṉṟu _ivar tam
palaṅkaḷāl nēṭiyum _aṟivu _aritu_āya
pariciṉaṉ maruvi niṉṟu _iṉitu _uṟikōyil---
malaṅki vaṉ tiri vari _eṉap parantu, _eṅkum
maṟikaṭal _ōṅki, veḷ_ippiyum cumantu,
kalaṅkaḷ tam carakkoṭu nirakka vantu _ēṟum---
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*10
_ām palatavam muyaṉṟu _aṟa_uri collum
_aṟivu _ilāc camaṇarum, tērarum, kaṇi cēr
nōm palatavam _aṟiyātavar noṭinta
mūturi koḷkilā mutalvar---tam mēṉic
cāmpalum pūci, veṇtali kalaṉ_ākat
tiyalār _iṭu pali viyakattu _ēṟṟu,---
kāmpu _aṉa tōḷiyoṭu _iṉitu _uṟi kōyil,
kaḻumalam niṉiya, nam viṉikaricu _aṟumē.

*11
kali keḻu pār_iṭi _ūr _eṉa _uḷatu_ām
kaḻumalam virumpiya kōyil koṇṭavarmēl,
vali keḻu maṉam mika vittavaṉ, maṟi cēr-
varum kali ñāṉacampantaṉa tamiḻiṉ
_olikeḻumāli _eṉṟu _uriceyta pattum
_uṇmiyiṉāl niṉintu _ētta vallārmēl
meli keḻu tuyar _aṭiyā; viṉi cintum;
viṇṇavar _āṟṟaliṉ mikap peṟuvārē.

{2:28}__1+
{$} toṇṭu elām malar tūvi nañcu (1) uṇṭalār
Civaṉ consumed the poison to be prised by all the devotees by scattering flowers.
(2) uyir āya taṉmaiyar
is the life to all beings
kaṇṭaṉār
is the warrior
[[kaṇṭu aṉār: is as sweet as the rock-candy is another meaning.]]
karuvūruḷ āṉilai aṇṭaṉār aruḷ (3) īyum aṉpar (ē)
the god at the temple āṉili in Karuvūr has love to grant his grace
[[Karuvūr is the name of the place; āṉili is the name of the temple.]]

{$} nītiyār niṉaintu āya nāṉ maṟai ōtiyāroṭum kūṭalār
Civaṉ will join those who chant the four Vētam-s with intention to investigate them.

wears a men's ear-ring in the ear
karuvūr āṉilai ātiyār
the first cuse of all things at āṉili in Karuvūr.

{2:28}__5+
{$} paṅkayam malarp pātar
Civaṉ has feet as lotus-red feet
pāti or maṅkaiyar
has as a half a lady
maṇi nīla kaṇṭar
has a beautiful blue neck
vāṉ kaṅkaiyar

karuvūruḷ āṉilai (1) amkai āṭu aravattu em aṇṇal (ē)
is our god who wears on his beautiful hands a dancing cobra and who is at āṉili in Karuvūr.

{$} tēvar

tiṅkaḷum pāmpum ceṉṉiyil mēvar

mummatil eyta (1) villiyar

the master who admits us as his protege is attached to āṉili in Karuvūr.
{2:28}__8+
{$} kaṭutta vāḷ arakkaṉ kayilaiyai eṭuttavaṉ talai tōḷum tāḷiṉāl aṭarttavaṉ
Civaṉ pressed down firmly with his leg the heads and shoulders of the angry and cruel arakkaṉ who lifted the mountin, Kayili.
karuvūruḷ āṉilai koṭuttavaṉ aruḷ kūttaṉ allaṉē
he who is at āṉili in Karuvūr granted his grace later; is he not a dancer?
{2:28}__9+
{$} ēṉam āki mānilattu uḻutu māl, mā malarōṉum toḻutu kāṇkilār
Māl transforming himself into a pig and digging the big earth and Piramaṉ who is in a big flower had not the ability to see Civaṉ though they worshipped him with joined hands.
kaḻutiṉāṉ
has pēy as his army.
karuvūruḷ āṉilai muḻutum ākiya mūrtti pātam (ē)
the feet of the god who has a visible form and who is all things and who is at āṉili in Karuvūr.
[[mūrtti pātam kāṇkilār is a syntactical link.]]
{2:28}__10+
{$}
(People of this world !)
puttar puṉ camaṇ ātar poyyuraip pittar pēciya pēccai viṭṭu
leaving as having no substance the speeches of buddhists and low and ignorant camaṇar who speak only lies and are mad people.
meyppattar cērum karuvūruḷ āṉilai attar pātam aṭaintu vāḻmiṉ (ē)
live happily approaching the feet of the father at āṉili in Karuvūr where sincere devotees gather together.
{2:28}__11+
{$} kantam ār poḻil kāḻi ñāṉacampantaṉ cēr karuvūruḷ āṉilai entaiyaic coṉṉa pattum valalvar
those who are capable of reciting the ten verses on our father at āṉili in Karuvūr which Ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kāḻi which has gardens full of fragrance, always meditates upon,
cintaiyil tuyar tīravār (ē)
will be free from all mental worries.

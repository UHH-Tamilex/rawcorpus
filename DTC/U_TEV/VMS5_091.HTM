{5:91}__1+
{$} eṉ āviyē
my soul.
ēilāṉai
Civaṉ who has no superior master to him.
eṉ iccai (1) akampaṭik kōyilāṉai
who dwells in the temple of my heart which is filled with love.
kuṇapperuṅkuṉṟiṉai
who is like a mountin having all good qualities (god having noble character)
vāyilāṉai
who has as his house the mouths of those who are prising him.
maṉōṉmaṉiyaippeṟṟa tāyilāṉaittaḻuvum
will mix with Civaṉ who has no mother and be got maṉōṉmaṇi (Umi)
[[PP: imavāṉ makaṭkut taṉṉuṭaik kēḷvaṉ makaṉ takappaṉ tamaiyaṉ
(tiruvācakam, tiruppoṟcuṇṇam, 13)]]
[[araṉukkut tāyum makaḷum naltāramum āvaḷē
(tirumantiram)]]
[[PP: tavaḷatta nīṟu aṇiyum taṭantōḷ aṇṇal taṉ oru pāl avaḷ attaṉām makaṉām tillaiyāṉ (Koviyār, 112)]]
[[PP: kaṉakam ār kaviṉ ceymaṉṟil, aṉaka nāṭakaṟku em aṉṉai, maṉaivi, tāy, tantai, makaḷ
(Kumarakuruparar, citamparacceyyuṭkōvi, 33).]]
[[Variant reading: (1) akampaṭikōyilāṉai]]
{5:91}__2+
{$} muṉṉai ñāṉa mutal taṉivittiṉai
the unequalled seed who is the cuse for the wisdom to come into existence, before all other things.
piṉṉai ñāṉappiṟaṅku caṭaiyaṉai
who has a brilliant caṭi who is the embodiment of wisdom, after.
eṉṉai ñāṉattu iruḷ aṟuttu āṇṭavaṉ taṉṉai
he admitted me as his protege by completely dispelling the darkness of ignorance which hides the wisdom.
ñāṉattaḷaiyiṭṭu vaippaṉē
I shall place him in my heart by binding him with the rope of wisdom.
{5:91}__3+
{$} cila ñāṉikaḷ ñāṉattāl toḻuvār
some people endowed with divine knowledge will worship Civaṉ following the path of spiritual wisdom.
nāṉ ñāṉattāl uṉaittoḻuvēṉ alēṉ
I have not the capability to worship you following the path of spiritual knowledge.
ñāṉattāy
Civaṉ who is the embodiment of divine knowledge!
ñāṉattāl toḻuvār toḻakkaṇṭu
seeing your being worshipped by people who follow the path of divine knowledge.
uṉaināṉum toḻuvaṉē
I too worship you.
[[Variant reading: (1) ñāṉattāluṉai]]
{5:91}__4+
{$} puḻuvukkum kuṇam nāṉku
the worm has four qualities.
[[uṇṭiyum puṇarppum āki (tattuvārāyar aṭaṅki muṟināṉmanimāli, 33-29-30)]]
(1) eṉakkum atē
I also have those four qualities
puḻuvukkum (2) iṅku eṉakku uḷḷa pollāṅku illai
the worm has not the evils that I have.
puḻuviṉum kaṭaiyēṉ
I am lower than even the worm
puṉitaṉtamar kuḻuvukkuc ceṉṟu kūṭa evviṭattēṉ
where am I to mix with approaching the group of devotees of Civaṉ who is pure?
[[variant readings: (1) eṉakkumaḵtē (2) puḻuvukkīṅku]]
{5:91}__5+
{$} maṉitarkāḷ
men!
malaiyē vantu viḻiṉum (1) nilaiyil niṉṟu kalaṅkap peṟutirēl
Do not get perplexed even if the mountin comes down rolling and falls on you, from your steadfastness of devotion;
[[veṟutir can be used in the affirmative sense only. I think the reading should be peṟātir; but it can give a negative meaning implied by the modulation of voice]]
talaivaṉ ākiya īcaṉ tamarkaḷaik kolaiceyyāṉaitāṉ koṉṟiṭukiṟkumē
is the elephant which is capable of killing, kill the devotees of Civaṉ who is the master
[[This verse is born out of his personal experience when he was about to be trampled under the feet of an elephant]]
[[Variant reading: (1) nilaiyilniṉṟīr]]
{5:91}__6+
{$} kaṟṟuk koḷvaṉa vāy uḷa; nā uḷa
we have mouths and tongues which are of help to learn the names of Civaṉ.
iṭṭukoḷvaṉa pū uḷa nīr uḷa
to bathe Civaṉ there is plenty of water; to adorn him there are flowers.
kaṟṟaic ceñcaṭai yāṉ uḷaṉ nām uḷōm
Civaṉ who has a collection of red caṭi is there to grant his grace; we are born here to receive his grace.
namaṉāl muṉivuṇpatu eṟṟukku (ō)
why should we incur the anger of Namaṉ (god of death)?

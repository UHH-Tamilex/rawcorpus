{1:29}__1+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
ūrulāvu palikoṇṭu
receiving alms wandering in the villages,
ulakuētta
to be praised by the people of this world,
nīrulāvu nimirpuṉcaṭai aṇṇal
the chief who has a caṭai of golden colour and which is erect and on which the water (of the Kaṅkai) is wandering.
cīr ulāvu maṟaiyōr naṟaiyūril cērum cīttīccaram ceṉṟuaṭai
reach the temple, citticcaram which is in naṟaiyur which has a population of brahmins of spreading fame, going to that place
[[Naṟaiyūr is the name of the place; Citticcaram is the name of the temple.]]
{1:29}__2+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
kāṭum nāṭum kalakkap pali naṇṇi
approaching for alms to make the forest and the country to be combined in them.
ōṭukaṅkai oḷir puṉcaṭaitāḻa
the golden caṭai on which the flowing Kaṅkai shines to be hanging.
(1) ākam vīṭum maṟaiyōr naṟaiyūril
in naṟaiyūr where brahmins who leave the body which is the result of past Karmams and attain bliss.
nīṭum cittīccaram niṉai
think of citticcaram which has been in existence since time immemorial.
[[Variant reading: (1) vīṭamāka]]
{1:29}__3+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
kaṉakam aḻalmēṉi pulku kaṅkai puri puṉcaṭaiyāṉ ūr
the place of Civaṉ who has a golden, caṭai to which is attached the river, Kaṅkai, and who has a body which is like gold and fire.
kalviyāḷar malkutiṅkaṭ poḻilcūḻ naṟaiyūril
in naṟaiyūr surrounded by gardens on which the moon rests and where educated people are prosperous.
celvar cittīccaram ceṉṟu aṭai
reach citticcaram of the Lord by going there.
{1:29}__4+
{$} neñcē
my mind.
pāṭal vaṇṭupayilum naṟaiyūril cēṭar cittīccaramē
citticcaram is the place of the great god in naṟaiyūr where humming bees are found in big swarms.
nīṭavalla nimirpuṉcaṭaitāḻa āṭavalla aṭikaḷ iṭam ākum
and is the place of the god who is able to dance when the golden caṭai which is and grows longer, hangs low.
teḷi
be clear about it.
{1:29}__5+
{$} neñcē
my mind.
umparālum ulakiṉṉavarālum tam perumai aḷattaṟku ariyāṉ ūr
the place of Civaṉ whose greatness could not be measured by the celestials and the people of this world.
[[The celestials are immersed in enjoyment of pleasures and makes them forget god and think only of their greatness, as spiritual impurities hide their mental eye. The people of this world are bound by spiritual impurities and are full of ego and therefore cannot know god.]]
naṇpu ulāvu maṟaiyōr naṟaiyūril cempoṉ cittīccaramē teḷi
be clear about citticcaram of the pure gold (god) in naṟaiyūr where brahmins who are friendly towards all, dwell.
{1:29}__6+
{$} naṟaiyūril cērum cittīccaramē
citticcaram which is situated in naṟaiyūr.
kūr ulāvu paṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has the weapon of a trident which is sharp.
pōr ulāvu maḻuvāṉ
and who has a battle axe which moves about in battles.
aṉal āṭi
and who dances in the fire.
pēr ulāvuperumāṉ iṭam ām
is the place of the god whose fame spreads everywhere.
{1:29}__7+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
aṉṟiniṉṟa avuṇar puram eyta veṉṟivilli
the victorious archer who shot an arrow on the cities of the avuṇar who were inimical towards him.
vimalaṉ virumpum ūr
the place which the blemishless Civaṉ desires.
(1) maṉṟil vācam maṇam ār naṟaiyūril cittīccaramē
is citticcaram in naṟaiyūr when the village halls are full of fragrance.
ceṉṟu teḷi
go there and be clear about it.
[[Variant reading: (1) maṉṟalvāca]]
{1:29}__8+
{$} neñcē
my mind.
arakkaṉ āṇmai aḻiya varaitaṉṉāl nerukka ūṉṟum viralāl virumpum ūr
the place desired by Civaṉ who by his toe pressed to crush by the mountain, the conceit of the arakaṉ to be destroyed.
parakkum kīrtti uṭaiyār naṟaiyūril tirukkoḷ cittīccaramē
is citticcaram in naṟaiyūr which has wealth and where brahmins of spreading fame dwell.
{1:29}__9+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
āḻiyāṉum alariṉ uṟaivāṉum
Māl who has a discus and Piramaṉ who dwells in a (lotus) flower.
ūḻināṭi uṇarārtirintu mēl cūḻam nēṭa
to search for civan for a very long time and could not known him, wandering in the sky and surrounding places and seeking for him.
eriām oruvaṉ cīrnīḻal cittīccaramē niṉai
think of citticcaram of Civaṉ who assumed the form of fire and which is under the shade of his greatness.
{1:29}__10+
{$} meyil mācar virinuṇ (1) tukil ilār kaiyil uṇṭu kaḻaṟum urai koḷḷēl
do not accept the abusing words of amaṇar of dirty bodies, who do not wear fine and long cloth and who eat their food from their hands.
uyyavēṇṭil iṟaivaṉ naṟaiyūril cittīccaramē ceyyum tavam (iṭam) ām
Citticcaram in Naṟaiyūr of Civaṉ is the place for doing penance, if one wants to save himself.
[[Variant reading: (1) tukiliṉār]]
{1:29}__11+
{$} meyttu ulāvu maṟaiyōr naṟaiyūril cittaṉ cittīccarattai
about citticcaram in naṟaiyūr where brahmins lead a life without telling lies, and which is the place of Civaṉ having supernatural powers and capable of performing miracles.
uyarkāḻi attaṉ pātam aṇi ñāṉacampantaṉ
composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who adorns on his head the feet of the father (Civaṉ) in superior kāḻi.
pattum pāṭappaṟaiyum pāvamē
sins will vanish by degrees if one sings the ten songs.

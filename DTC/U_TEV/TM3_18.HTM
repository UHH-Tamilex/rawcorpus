• patikam: [3:18]
  • talam: tiru vikalmāṭakkōyil
  • paṇ: kāntārap pañcamam
  • VMS: Vol.6, p.163

*1
tuḷa mati _uṭi maṟi tōṉṟu kiyiṉar,
_iḷamati _aṇi caṭi _entiyār, _iṭam---
_uḷam mati _uṭiyavar vikal _ōṅkiya,
vaḷa mati taṭaviya, māṭakkōyilē.

*2
mey_akam miḷirum veṇnūlar, vētiyar,
miya kaṇ malimakaḷōṭum viku _iṭam---
viyakam makiḻtara, vikal mēl-tici
ceyyakaṇvaḷavaṉ muṉ ceyta kōyilē.

*3
kaṇi _aṇi malarkoṭu, kāli māliyum
paṇi _aṇipavarkku _aruḷceyta pāṉmiyar,
taṇi _aṇi _umiyoṭu tāmum taṅku _iṭam---
maṇi _aṇi kiḷar vikal māṭakkōyilē.

*4
kompu_iyalkōti muṉ _añca, kuñcarat-
tumpi_atu _uriceyta tuṅkar taṅku _iṭam---
vampu _iyal cōli cūḻ vikal mēl-tici,
cempiyaṉ kōcceṅkaṇāṉ cey kōyilē.

*5
viṭam _aṭi miṭaṟṟiṉar, vēta nāviṉar,
maṭamoḻi malimakaḷōṭum viku _iṭam---
maṭa_aṉam naṭi payil vikal mā nakark
kuṭatici nilaviya māṭakkōyilē.

*6
niṟipuṉal piṟiyoṭu nilavu nīḷcaṭi
_iṟiyavar _uṟivu_iṭam---_ilaṅku mū_eri
maṟiyoṭu vaḷarvuceyvāṇar vikalil,
tiṟi _uṭi niṟi celvaṉ ceyta kōyilē.

*7
_ericaram varicili vaḷiya _ēvi, muṉ
tiripuram _ericeyta celvar cērvu_iṭam---
varivaḷiyavar payil vikal mēl-tici,
varu mukil _aṇaviya māṭakkōyilē.

*8
mali _aṉa _irupatutōḷiṉāṉ vali
tolivu ceytu _aruḷceyta cōtiyār _iṭam---
malar mali poḻil _aṇi vikal vāḻvarkaḷ
valamvaru mali _aṉa māṭakkōyilē.

*9
mālavaṉ, malaravaṉ, nēṭi māl koḷa,
māl_eri _ākiya varatar viku _iṭam---
mālikoṭu _aṇi maṟivāṇar vikalil,
māl _aṉa maṇi _aṇi māṭakkōyilē.

*10
kaṭu _uṭi vāyiṉar, kañci vāyiṉar,
piṭaku_uri pēṇilār pēṇu kōyil_ām---
maṭam _uṭiyavar payil vikal mā nakar,
vaṭamali _aṉiya nal māṭakkōyilē.

*11
mintaṉatu _iṭam vikalmāṭakkōyili,
cantu _amar poḻil _aṇi caṇpi ñāṉacam-
pantaṉa tamiḻ keḻu pāṭal pattu_ivi
cinticeypavar, civalōkam cērvarē.

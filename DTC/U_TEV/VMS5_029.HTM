This decade is composed as the words of the mother of the girl who has fallen in love with Civaṉ.

{5:29}__1+
{$}
piṟaik (1) kavālap porum puṉal āvaṭu, tuṟaik kavāliyōṭu āṭiya cuṇṇamē niṟaikkavāliyaḷ allaḷ innēriḻai
this girl who wears ornaments fitting her beauty is not pure enough to diffuse all over the body the holy ash she had in her body by embracing Kavāli in Āvaṭutuṟi who has the crescent, the skull and the big water on his head.
immātarāḷ
this girl.
maṟaikkavāliyaḷ allaḷ
has not the strength to hide it.
[[vāliyaḷ=valiyaḷ this is a poetic license which consists in lengthening a short vowel into a long one]]
[[This is a stanza whose initial letters except the first are identical in each line but the meaning is different, this is called (tiripu aṇi)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) kavālip]]
{5:29}__2+
{$}
tavaḷa māmatic cāyal ōr cantiraṉ piḷavu cūṭiya piññakaṉ em iṟai aḷavu kaṇṭilaḷ
though this girl did not know the nature of love of our master, the destroyer who wore a crescent which is a part of the white esteemed and graceful moon.
āvaṭu taṇ tuṟaik kaḷavu kaṇṭaṉaḷ ōttaṉaḷ kaṉṉiyē
this virgin girl is like the one who became united with the Lord in āvaṭutuṟi in clandestine love (The rhyme is this verse is worthy of note)
{5:29}__3+
{$}
1. pātip peṇ aru pākattaṉ
Civaṉ has on one half a lady!
pal maṟai ōti eṉ uḷam koṇṭavaṉ
occupied my mind chanting many vetams!
oṇ poruḷ āti
who is the cuse of all great things in this world!
āvaṭu taṇtuṟai mēviya cōtiyē cuṭarē eṉṟu collumē
(this girl) always says the divine light which is partial to āvaṭutuṟi and the brilliant light.
[[Variant reading: 1. pātipeṇ]]
[[pākattaṉ, koṇṭavaṉ, āti these are nominatives of address]]
{5:29}__4+
{$}
kārk koḷ māmukil pōlvatu ōr kaṇṭattaṉ
Civaṉ who has a neck like the sable cloud that rises in the winter season.
vārk koḷ meṉ mulai cērntu iṟumāntu
Civaṉ who feels exulted being united Umi who has soft breasts on which a bodice is worn.
ivaḷ ārk koḷ koṉṟaiyaṉ
and who wears koṉṟi flowers which have captivated the mind of this girl.
āvaṭu taṇ tuṟaittārkku niṉṟu ivaḷ tāḻumāḷaṟuṉa kāṇmiṉ
See how this girl is engrossed in the thought to receive the garland of koṉṟi worn on the chest by the Lord in the cool āvaṭutuṟi.
{5:29}__5+
{$}
karuku kaṇṭattaṉ
Civaṉ who has a black neck.
kāykatirc cōtiyaṉ
who is as brilliant as the sun.
paruku pāl amutē eṉum paṇpiṉaṉ
who has the quality of milk food which is fit for drinking.
aruku 1. ceṉṟilaṉ
she did not go even near that god; but says
āvaṭu taṇtuṟai oruvaṉ
the unequalled god in cool āvaṭutuṟi!
eṉṉai uṭaiya kō eṉṉum
he is the master who has me as his wife
[[Variant reading: 1. ceṉṟivaḷ]]
{5:29}__6+
{$}
kuḻalum koṉṟaiyum kūviḷam (um) mattamum taḻalum taiyal ōr pākamāt tāṅkiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who bore on his caṭi coiled into a crown, koṉṟi flowers, bel leaves, datura flowers, fire and a lady on his half.
aḻakaṉ
the beautiful one
āvaṭu taṇtuṟaiyā eṉa
when she says, the god in cool āvatutuṟi!
kārikaiyāḷukkuk kaivaḷai kaḻalum
the bangles worn on the hands of this beautiful girl, slip off.
{5:29}__7+
{$}
ivaḷ
this girl.
pañciṉ mel aṭip pāvai ōr paṅkaṉait tañcam eṉṟu iṟumāntu
feeling proud, thinking as her refuge the god who has as his half a beautiful lady whose feet are as soft as cotton.
āraiyum añcuvāḷ allaḷ
does not fear anybody
āvaṭu taṇtuṟai mañcaṉōṭu ivaḷ āṭiya maiyal
Due to the infatuation she had by having intercourse with the youth in cool āvaṭutuṟi.
[[mañcaṉ-maintaṉ;
mañcaṉē maṇiyumāṉāy (appar, tirunērici (2) 1 of āvaṭutuṟi)]]
{5:29}__8+
{$}
piṟaiyum cūṭi
wearing the eminent crescent
peṇṇoṭu 1. āṇ āki eṉ niṟaiyum neñcamum nīrmaiyum koṇṭavaṉ
Civaṉ who being himself a lady and a male took away my complete self-control, my mind, and other feminine qualities.
aṟaiyum pūm poḻil āvaṭu taṇtuṟai iṟaivaṉ eṉṉai uṭaiyavaṉ eṉṉum
she says `I am the possession of the Lord in cool āvaṭutuṟi which has flower gardens in which bees hum with great noise'
[[Variant reading: 1. āṇ ākiya]]
{5:29}__9+
{$}
vaiyam tāṉ aḷantāṉum ayaṉum āy meyyaik kāṇaluṟṟārkku aḻal āyiṉāṉ
god who assumed the form of a column of fire when Māl who measured the earth and Ayaṉ, tried to see the ultimate truth? (āyiṉāṉ nominative of address)
aiyaṉ! āvaṭu taṇ tuṟaiyā eṉa
when this girl cries `my father! Civaṉ who is in cool āvaṭutuṟi'
kaiyil veḷvaḷaiyum kaḻal kiṉṟatē
even the white bangles in the hands slip off.
[[vaḷaiyum the um hints that all other beautiful bangles had fallen from her hands before]]
{5:29}__10+
{$}
innāṇ ili
this girl who lost her bashfulness
pakkam pūtaṅkaḷ pāṭap pali koḷvāṉ ḷaeṉṉumṉa
says that Civaṉ receives alms when the pūtams sing by his side.
mikka vāḷ arakkaṉ vali vīṭṭiṉāṉ ḷaeṉṉumṉa
and says he destroyed the excessive strength of the cruel and proud arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ)
akku aṇintavaṉ; āvaṭu taṇtuṟainakkaṉ ḷaeṉṉumṉa
and says he adorns himself with chank-beads (akku rudrāksha bead, bone) and the naked one in cool āvaṭutuṟi
kāṇmiṉ
Ladies, please look at her.

Chronology of text entering [Series details]

The Tēvāram translation by VMS was entered successively by two typists:
- Ms. Vaidehi (from August 1997 upto May 1998)
- Ms. Ramya (from June 1998 upto January 1999)

In order to have a representative selection from the beginning (representative from several point of views), it was decided not to enter the translations volume-wise (First Vol.1, then Vol.2, etc.), since the volumes are organized on the tala muṟai order, but to enter it by series, the composition of which was based on several criteria (for example, we wanted to have the most well-known patikam-s entered first ...)

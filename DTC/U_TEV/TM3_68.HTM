*1
vāḷa vari kōḷa puli kīḷ_atu _uri tāḷiṉmici nāḷum makiḻvar,
_āḷumavar vēḷ _anakar, pōḷ _ayila kōḷa kaḷiṟu _āḷi, vara _il---
tōḷ _amarar tāḷam, matar kūḷi, _eḻa mīḷi, miḷir tūḷi, vaḷar poṉ
kāḷamukil mūḷum _iruḷ kīḷa, viri tāḷa kayilāyamaliyē.

*2
puṟṟu _aravu paṟṟiya ki, neṟṟiyatu maṟṟu _oru kaṇ, _oṟṟi viṭiyaṉ,
ceṟṟatu _eyil, _uṟṟatu _umi, _aṟṟavarkaḷ nal-tuṇivaṉ, _uṟṟa nakartāṉ---
cuṟṟum maṇi peṟṟatu _oḷi; ceṟṟamoṭu kuṟṟam _ilatu; _eṟṟu? _eṉa viṉāy,
kaṟṟavarkaḷ col-tokiyiṉ muṟṟum _oḷi peṟṟa kayilāyamaliyē.

*3
ciṅka_ari maṅkiyarkaḷ taṅkaḷaṉa ceṅki niṟi koṅkumalar tūy,
_eṅkaḷ viṉi, caṅki_avi, _iṅku _akala! _aṅkam moḻi _eṅkum _uḷa_āy,
tiṅkaḷ _iruḷ noṅka, _oḷi viṅki, miḷir toṅkaloṭu taṅka, _ayalē
kaṅkiyoṭu poṅku caṭi _eṅkaḷ _iṟi taṅku kayilāyamaliyē.

*4
muṭiya caṭi, piṭiyatu _oru vaṭiya maḻu _uṭiyar, ceṭi _uṭiya taliyil
veṭiya viṉi koṭiyar keṭa, _iṭu cilpali noṭiya makiḻ _aṭikaḷ _iṭam_ām---
koṭiya kural _uṭiya viṭi kaṭiya tuṭiyaṭiyiṉoṭum _iṭiyiṉ _atira,
kaṭiya kural neṭiya mukil maṭiya, _atar _aṭi koḷ kayilāyamaliyē.

*5
kuṭaṅkiyiṉ nuṭaṅku _eri toṭarntu _eḻa, viṭam kiḷar paṭam koḷ _aravam
maṭaṅku _oḷi paṭarntiṭa, naṭam taru viṭaṅkaṉatu _iṭam---taṇmukil pōyt
taṭaṅkaṭal toṭarntu, _uṭaṉ nuṭaṅkuva _iṭam koḷa miṭinta kuralāl,
kaṭuṅ kaliṉ muṭaṅku _aḷi nuṭaṅku _aravu _oṭuṅku kayilāyamaliyē.

*6
_ētam _ila pūtamoṭu, kōtituṇi _ātimutal, vēta vikirtaṉ,
kītamoṭu nītipala _ōti maṟavātu payil nātaṉ, nakartāṉ---
tātu poti pōtu viṭa, _ūtu ciṟi mītu tuḷi kūtal naliya,
kātal miku cōti kiḷar mātu mayil kōtu kayilāyamaliyē.

*7
ceṉṟu pala veṉṟu _ulavu puṉtaliyar tuṉṟaloṭum _oṉṟi, _uṭaṉē-
niṉṟu, _amarar _eṉṟum _iṟivaṉtaṉ _aṭi ceṉṟu paṇikiṉṟa nakartāṉ---
tuṉṟu malar poṉtikaḻcey koṉṟi viri teṉṟaloṭu ceṉṟu kamaḻa,
kaṉṟu, piṭi, tuṉṟu kaḷiṟu, _eṉṟu _ivi muṉ niṉṟa kayilāyamaliyē.

*8
maruppu_iṭi neruppu _eḻu tarukkoṭu ceruc ceyta parutta kaḷiṟiṉ
poruppu_iṭi viruppu_uṟa _irukkiyi _orukku_uṭaṉ _arakkaṉ _uṇarātu,
_oruttiyi verukku_uṟa veruṭṭalum, nerukku_eṉa nirutta viralāl,
karuttu _ila _oruttaṉi _eruttu _iṟa neritta kayilāyamaliyē.

*9
pariya tiri periya puṉal, variya puli_uri_atu _uṭi, parici _uṭiyaṉ,
variya vaḷi _ariya kaṇi _uruviṉoṭu puriviṉavar, pirivu _il nakartāṉ---
periya _eri_uruvam_atu teriya, _uru parivu tarum _arumi_ataṉāl,
kariyavaṉum, _ariya maṟi puriyavaṉum, maruvu kayilāyamaliyē.

*10
_aṇṭar toḻu caṇṭi paṇi kaṇṭu _aṭimi koṇṭa _iṟi, tuṇṭamatiyōṭu
_iṇṭi puṉivuṇṭa caṭi muṇṭatara caṇṭa _iruḷkaṇṭar _iṭam_ām---
kuṇṭu_amaṇa vaṇṭar_avar, maṇṭi kiyil _uṇṭu _uḷaṟi miṇṭu camayam
kaṇṭavarkaḷ koṇṭavarkaḷ, paṇṭum _aṟiyāta kayilāyamaliyē.

*11
_am taṇ vari vanta puṉal tanta tiri cantaṉamoṭu _unti, _akilum
kantamalar kontiṉoṭu mantipala cintu kayilāyamalimēl,
_enti _aṭi vantu _aṇuku cantamoṭu centamiḻ _icinta pukalip
pantaṉ _uri cinticeya, vanta viṉi nintu, paralōkam _eḷitē.

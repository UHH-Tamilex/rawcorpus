paṭaiyum pūtamum pāmpum pulvāy ataḷ uṭaiyum tāṅkiya uttamaṉārkku iṭam
the place of the supreme being, Civaṉ, who bore weapon, pūtams, cobra, a dress made of deer skin.
puṭai nilāviya pūm poḻil vāñciyam aṭaiyavallavarkku allal oṉṟu illai
there will not be the slightest suffering for those who can reach Vāñciyam which has flower-gardens which are thriving on all sides.

{$}
paṟappaiyum pacuvum paṭuttup pala tiṟattavum(m) uṭaiyōr tikaḻum pati
the city where brahmins who have construction a dia's for sacrificial fire in the form of a bird and offered a sacrifical animal and many other things.
[[paṟap�ppa paṭuttup pacu vēṭṭu eri ōmpum ciṟappar (campantar, Koyil (1) 2)]]
kaṟaippiṟaic caṭaik kaṇṇutal cērtaru ciṟappuṭait tiruvāñciyam cērmiṉ(ē)
reach tiruvāñciyam which has the eminence of being the abode of Civaṉ who has a frontal eye and who has on his cati a crescent with a dark spot, (People of this world!)
{5:67}__3+
{$}
cuṟṟum māṭaṅkaḷ cūḻ tiruvāñciyam puṟṟil āṭu 1. aravōṭu puṉal mati teṟṟu ceñcaṭait tēvar pirāṉ pati
the city of the master of the tēvar who has on his red cati a dancing cobra that lives in the anthill, water and crescent, becoming interwined, which is tiruvāñciyam surrounded by mansions all round.
paṟṟip pāṭuvārkkup pāvam illaiyē
there will no sins to those who sing about its greatness reaching it.
[[Variant reading: 1. aravōṭap]]

aṅkam āṟum arumaṟai nāṉ kuṭaṉ taṅku vēḷviyar tām payilum nakar
the city where brahmins who perform sacrifices and retin in their mind the six ankams and all the four abstruse vētams.
ceṅkaṇ māl iṭam ār tiru vāñciyam taṅkuvār nam amararkku amararē
our immortal Civaṉ who is the chief of the immortals will stay in tiruvāñciyam with red-eyed Māl being on his left side.
{5:67}__5+

nīṟu pūci nimir caṭaimēl piṟai āṟu cūṭum aṭikaḷ uṟaipati
the place of the god who wears on his erect cati, having smeared sacred ash, a crescent and a river.
māṟu tāṉ oruṅkum vayal vāñciyam tēṟi vāḻpavarkkuc celvam ākumē
wealth will increase to those who live with a clear idea that vāñciyam which has fields where the weeds that are enemies of crops become perished, is our refuge.

aṟṟup paṟṟu iṉṟi
having given up all desires and without two kinds of attachments.
yāraiyum illavarkku
to those who have none to support them.
uṟṟa nal tuṇai āvāṉ
Civaṉ who is the trutworthy and good companion.
uṟaipati
place where he dwells,
teṟṟu māṭaṅkaḷ cūḻ tiruvāñciyam
vāñciyam surrounded by crowded mansions.
kaṟṟuc cērpavarkkuk karuttu āvatē
is the intention for those learned people to reach that place.

arukkaṉ, aṅki, yamaṉoṭu tēvarkaḷ tiruttum cēvaṭiyāṉ tikaḻum nakar
the city where Civaṉ who has red lotus feet which are adorned by the sun-god, god of fire, the god of death and other immortals.
oruttipākam ukantavaṉ vāñciyam
which is desired by Civaṉ as his half a lady.
aruttiyāl aṭaivārkku allal illai
there will be no afflictions to those who reach that place with love.

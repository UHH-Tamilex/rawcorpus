{3:3}__1+
{$} iyal icai eṉum poruḷiṉ tiṟamām (puṇṇiyaṉē)
God who is the meaning of the division of iyal, ici (and nāṭakam) the three divisions of Tamiḻ
puyal aṉa miṭaṟuṭaip puṇṇiyaṉē
God who has a neck as black as the cloud
[[PP: koṇṭalum nīlamum puraitirumiṭaṟar (Ñāṉacampantar, tirukkaḻumalam (2) 2);]]
[[cf. koṇṭaṟkaṇṭaṉ,]]
[[PP: karumāl viṭaiyuṭaiyōṉ kaṇṭam pōṟ koṇṭal- (Kōviyār, 325, 326)]]
kayal aṉa ari neṭuṅkaṇṇiyoṭum ayal ulaku aṭitoḻa amarntavaṉē
you resided gladly, for the people of other world to worship your feet, along with the lady with long eyes, resembling carp, having lines on the white portion.
kalaṉ āvatu veṇtalai
your ornament is white skull
[[kalaṉ: eating plate;
(your eating plate is skull)]]
kaṭi poḻiṟpukalitaṉṉuḷ
is pukali which has fragrant parks.
nilaṉ nāḷtoṟum iṉpuṟ niṟai mati aruḷiṉaṉē
you bestowed on me wisdom in full measure so that people of this world may derive happiness dily.
[[Variant reading: (1) poruṇiṉṟiṟamām]]
{3:3}__2+
{$} malaiyiṉil arivai veruva vaṉṟōl alaivaru matakari urittavaṉē
you who flayed the strong skin of the elephant having must which came to kill you, to frighten the dughter who was born in the mountin
imaiyōrkaḷ niṉ tāḷ toḻa eḻil tikaḻ poḻiṟpukali umaiyāḷoṭum maṉṉiṉai
you resided permanently with umayal in pukali which has parks of shining beauty, for the tēvar to worship your feet.
yām uyar tiruvaṭi iṇai nilaiyuṟum iṭar nilaiyāta vaṇṇam ilai uṟu malarkaḷ koṇṭu ēttum
we will worship your eminent by our own efforts which appear to be permanent! may be only transitory
[[veruva is the shortened form of veruvuvikka]]
[[Variant reading: veruvavaṉ tolaivarumatakari]]
{3:3}__3+
{$} varal muṟaiyāl arumaṟai pāṭiṉai
you sang the difficult vētam acccording to tradition.
muṉ aruvaṉattil karaṇam āṭiṉai
in the part you danced in the difficult forest to be witnessed (by the wives of the sages in tārukāvanam; you danced in the cremation ground to be witnessed by Umi is another meaning)
kālaṉaik cāṭiṉai
you killed the god of death
tayaṅku oḷi cēr nīṭuveṇpiṟaimuṭi niṉ malaṉē
Immaculate god who has on his head a white crescent that increases in size and which has brilliant light.
niṉaiyē aṭiyār toḻa neṭu matil pukalin nakar taṉaiyē iṭam mēviṉai
you desired as your place the city of pukali itself which has a high wall of enclosure, for your devotees to worship you only.
emakkut tava neṟi aruḷ
grant us the way to do penance.
{3:3}__4+
{$} niḻal tikaḻ maḻuviṉai
you have a shining battle axe
yāṉaiyiṉ tōl aḻal tikaḻ mēṉiyil aṇintavaṉē
you adornedas an upper cloth, the skin of an elephant, on your form which is as as bright as fire.
kaḻal (oli) tikaḻ cilampu oli alampa nalla muḻavoṭum arunaṭam muyaṟṟiṉaṉē
you performed a rare dance to the accompaniment of flawless muḻavu when the sound of Kaḻal and the sound of cilampu made a big sound.
muyaṟṟiṉaṉ : muyaṟṟu
is the root;
[[muyaṟṟiyāl iṭarkaḷ vantāl (appar, oṟṟiyūr (1) 9);
muyaṟṟiṉmai iṉmai pukuttiviṭum- (kural 616);
muyaṟṟi cumanteḻuntu muntuṟṟa neñcē (periya tiruvantāti, 1)]]
muṭimēl maticūṭiṉai
you wore the crescent moon on your head
muruku amar poḻiṟ pukali aṭiyār avar ēttuṟa aḻakoṭum iruntavaṉē
you resided with beauty in pukali having fragrant parks to be worshipped by the devotees.
{3:3}__5+
{$} karumaiyiṉ oḷirkaṭal nañcam uṇṭa urimaiyiṉ ulaku uyir aḷitta niṉṟaṉ perumaiyai nilattavar pēciṉ allāl
Except that the people of this world can speak only about your greatness in having saved the world and its inhabitants by the right of your having swallowed the poison shining with black colour, which rose in the ocean.
arumaiyil aḷappu aritu āyavaṉē
you cannot be measured by any amout of difficult research.
aravu ēr iṭaiyāḷoṭum alaikaṭal malip pukalip poruḷ cērtara nāḷtoṟum puvimicaip polintavaṉē
you were shining with brilliance dily in this world in pukali which abounds in the wealth got from the sea, along with a lady whose waist is as slender as a serpent for the four obsectives of human pursuit to gather there (serpent is also compared to the waist of ladies;
[[araviṭai mātoṭum vīṟṟirunta aḻakaṉaṉṟē (campantar, tiruppukali (8) 6);
miṉṉum paṇiyum purai maruṅkul (Koviyār 5);
four obsectives; aṟam, poruḷ, iṉpam, vīṭu; poruḷ: may mean also also ordinary wealth.]]
{3:3}__6+
{$} aṭai ari māvoṭu vēṅkaiyiṉ tōl puṭai paṭa araimicaip puṉaintavaṉē
you who adorned your waist on its sides with a tiger's skin in addition to the skin of the lion which approached you to kill.
paṭaiyuṭai neṭumatil paricu aḻitta viṭaiyuṭaik koṭi malku vētiyaṉē
oh vētiyaṉ on whose banner the bull is thriving and who destroyed the nature of the three high walls of enclosure which has weapons wax.
[[paṭaiyuṭai neṭumatil : may mean this also; several layers in the wall of enclosure;
paṭaiyār puricaip paṭṭiṉam (campantar, pallavaṉīccaram, 1);
paṭai amai iṭṭikai- (peruṅkati, īlāvāṇkāṇṭam, 5 (41)]]
vikirtā paramā
you who are wholly different from the world! supreme Lord!
niṉṉai viṇṇavar toḻap pukalit takuvāy, maṭamātoṭum tāḷ paṇintavar tamakkē
you are fit to give audience only to people who bow to your feet and reside along with the young lady in pukali where the tēvar in heaven worship you.
{3:3}__7+
{$} aṭiyavar toḻutu eḻa amarar ēttac ceṭiyavalviḷai tīrppavaṉē
you remove the irresistible many sins which are as crowded as a thick bush, when the devotees worship you and the immortals prise you.
tuṭi iṭai akal alkul tūmoḻiyaip poṭi aṇi mārpu uṟap pulliṉaṉē
you embraced tightly in your chest smeared with ash, a lady speaking good words, having a waist like tuti and broad sides.
puṇṇiyā! puṉitā!
the embodiment of all virtuous acts! the pure one!
pukar ēṟṟiṉai
you have a bright coloured bull.
pukalin nakar naṇṇiṉāy
you are attached to the city of pukali
kaḻal ēttiṭa viṉai naṇṇakilā
sins are not capable of adhering to devotees if they worship your feet.
[[Variant reading: 1. pukali naṉṉakar]]
{3:3}__8+
{$} iravoṭu pakal atu ām emmāṉ
My Lord who is day and night!
vaḻiyaṭiyēṉ uṉṉaip paravutal oḻikilēṉ
I, who am a hereditary worshipper will never cease from prising you
kuraviri naṟuṅkoṉṟai koṇṭu aṇinta araviri caṭaimuṭi āṇ takaiyē
Lord of great eminence who has a spreading caṭi on which there are cobras, and which is adorned with blossomed fragrant koṉṟi flowers and Kurāl (bottle-flower)
[[kurā, and arā have become respectively kura and ara; kuṟiyataṉ iṟutic ciṉaikeṭa ukaram; aṟiya varutal ceyyuḷuḷ urittē (tolkāppiyam, uyirmyaṅkiyal, 32) is the rule for this change.]]
atirkaṭal ilaṅkai maṉṉai iṉamārtaru tōḷ aṭarttup pukaliyuḷē a(ṉ)ṉam meṉṉaṭaiyāḷoṭum iruntaṉai
you resided in pukali with a lady of graceful git like the swan, having pressed down the group of shoulders of the king of ilanki surrounded by the roaring sea.
[[Variant reading: 1. niṉṉai]]
{3:3}__9+
{$} urukiṭa uvakai tantu uṭaliṉ uḷ (āl) parukiṭum amutu aṉa paṇpiṉaṉē
you have the property of the nectar which can be enjoyed within the body bestowing joy to melt the heart (parukiṭum amutu nectar which in the liquid form).
[[aṉaittu elumpu uḷ neka āṉantattēṉ coriyum kuṉippuṭaiyāṉ (tiruvācakam, Kōttumpi, 3)]]
porukaṭal vaṇṇaṉum, pū uḷāṉum perukiṭum maruḷ eṉap piṟaṅku eriyāy uyarntāy
you rose like a cloumn of dazzling fire as high as the pride of the two, Māl who had a blue complexion like the sea which is always at war with the shore, and (Piramaṉ) who is seated in the (lotus) flower, and was at its utmost limit.
matil pukaliyaṉē
God residing in pukali surrounded by a wall of enclosure
1. iṉi nī eṉai oṇ malaraṭikkīḻ vayam tāṅkuṟa nalkiṭu
Bestow your grace on me to grant my desire to remin under your bright lotus feet, hereafter.
[[Variant reading: 1. nī eṉai oṇ; eṉai oṇ]]
{3:3}__10+
{$} kaiyiṉil uṇpavar 2. kaṇikai nōṉpar
Those who eat their food using their hands as plates and perform hypocritical austerities
[[Variant reading: 1. kaṇikai nōṉpavar]]
ceyvaṉa tavam alāc cetu matiyār
People of evil mind whose penance is not sincere penance
peyyavar uraikaḷaip poruḷ eṉāta meyyavar aṭi toḻa virumpiṉaṉē
you desired your feet to be worshipped by the truthful devotees who do not heed as truth the false utterence of those people.
viyantāy veḷḷēṟṟiṉai
you commanded as a vehicule a white bull.
viṇṇavar toḻupukali uyarntār peruṅkōyiluḷ oruṅkuṭaṉ iruntavaṉē
you who resided (with a lady) in the big temple worshipped by eminent people in pukali which is also worshipped by the celestials.
{3:3}__11+
{$} puṇṇiyar toḻutu eḻu pukalin nakar viṇṇavar aṭitoḻa viḷaṅkiṉāṉai
on thelord who is renowned in the city of pukali after worshipping whose feet people of virtuous acts rise from sleep and which is also worshipped by the residents of heaven.
naṇṇiya ñāṉacampantaṉ vāymai paṇṇiya aruntamiḻ pattum vallār
all the rare ten tamiḻ verses contining truth and composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who was close to god.
naṭalaiyavai iṉṟi
without the sufferings of being born agin and agin.
pōyc civaṉulakam naṇṇuvar
after death they will go and reach the abode of Civaṉ.
iṭarāyiṉa iṉṟit tām tavaneṟi eytuvar
they will tread the path leading to penance without troubles (in this world).

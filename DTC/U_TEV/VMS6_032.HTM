kaṟṟavarkaḷ uṇṇum kaṉiyē pōṟṟi
the fruit that is eaten by learned people, (let you protect me)
[[pōṟṟi = pōṟṟu i ; verb in the optative mood;]]
[[PP: akalam nāḷaik kaṉavil aruḷi vantu kāṭṭi yām kāṇaveṉpār
(Cīvaka Cintāmaṇi, 466);]]
[[PP: kāṭṭi ikaravīṟṟu viyaṅkōḷ
(Do. nacciṉārkkiṉiyar);]]
[[All the pōṟṟi in this as well as the following verses and in other verses of Tēvāram, are verbs in the optative mood; so the meaning will not be given for every pōṟṟi]]
[[PP: kaṟṟavar viḻuṅkum kaṟpakak kaṉiyai
(tiruvicippā, cēṇtaṉar, tiruvīḻimiḻali, 2)]]
kaḻal aṭaintār cellum katiyē (pōṟṟi)
the higher state of existence which those who reach your feet attin!
aṟṟavarkaṭku āramutam āṉāy (pōṟṟi)
you are the rare nectar to those who have left all attachments.
allal aṟuttu aṭiyēṉai āṇṭāy (pōṟṟi)
you admitted me your slave, into your grace after completely destroying my suffering
maṟṟu oruvar oppu illā maintā (pōṟṟi)
the strong god who has no one comparable to you!
vāṉavarkaḷ pōṟṟum maruntē (pōṟṟi)
the nectar that is prised by the celestials who have knowledge of reality!
ceṟṟavar tam puram eritta civaṉē (pōṟṟi)
O Civaṉ who burnt the cities of the enemies!
tirumūlaṭṭāṉaṉē (pōṟṟi, pōṟṟi)
The Lord in tirumulaṭṭaṉam!

hill of refined gold which has no equal.
civaṉē (pōṟṟi)

piramak taṉ ciram arinta periyōy (pōṟṟi)
the great god who cut off the head of Piramaṉ!
peṇ uruvōṭu āṇ uruvāy niṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)
you stood with a female form and male form in yourself and the world!
karam nāṉkum 1. mukkaṇṇum uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have four arms and three eyes.
kātalippārkku āṟṟa eḷiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you are very easily accessible to those who cherish you with love.
arumanta tēvarkku aracē (pōṟṟi)
the King of the tēvar who had tasted nectar

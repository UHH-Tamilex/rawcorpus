tōṟṟum kōyilum tōṉṟiya kōyilum
temples which will come into existence in future and temples that have been in existence till now
[[tōṟṟum is used in the sense of tōṉṟum.
tōṟṟum may mean also temples that may be built by many in future]]
vēṟṟuk kōyil pala uli
other temples which are not Civaṉ's temples are many in number.
mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil
the pālālayanam in mīyaccūr.
ēṟṟam
is the superior temple.
kūṟṟam pāynta kuḷir puṉcaṭaiyāṟku
to araṉ who has a cool and ruddy caṭi and who kicked the god of death being angry with him.
[[iḷaṅkōyil is the translation of the word bālālayam in sanskrit]]
{5:11}__2+
{$}
vantaṉai aṭaikkum aṭittoṇṭarkaḷ
the devotees to the feet of god who have assigned worship to god.
pantaṉai ceytu pāvikka niṉṟavaṉ
Civaṉ who is meditated upon having controlled their minds.
tiru mīyaccūr em tamai uṭaiyār iḷaṅkōyil cintaṉai tiruttum
the iḷankōyil in mīyaccur of Civaṉ who owns us as his slaves, corrects the thoughts of the devotees.
{5:11}__3+
{$}
neñcam! vāḻi!
my mind! let you live long!
pañcamantiram ōtum paramaṉār
the superior god who is prised by a special mantiram by name `pañcamantiram'! (it prises the five faces of Civaṉ)
añca āṉai urittavar
flayed the elephant to make it fear
em tamai uṭaiyār mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil niṉaintiru
be always thinking about the iḷaṅkōyil in mīyaccūr belonging to Civaṉ who owns us as his slaves (The etuki (etukai) in this verse is noteworthy)

tiru mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil ēṟu koṇṭu ukantār
Civaṉ who is in tirumīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil and feels joy in riding on a bull.
nāṟu mallikai, kūviḷam, ceṇpakam, vēṟu vēṟuviritta caṭaiyiṭai āṟu koṇṭu ukantāṉ
felt joy in having a river in his caṭi in spread in different ways which has champak flowers, leaves of bel, and fragrant jasmine (which were adorned by his devotees)
{5:11}__5+
{$}
�vvava vaṇṇattu nākam veruvavē, kavvavaṇṇam kaṉal virittu
Civaṉ will dance spreading the fire which destroys everything to make the cruel-natured cobra to be afrid of.
tikaḻ tiru mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil cevva vaṇṇam tikaḻ pirāṉ evva 1. vaṇṇam
what are the natures of the master who has a shining red colour and who is in tirumīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil?
[[Variant reading: 1. vaṇṇap.]]

{$}
miṉṉum mēkalaiyāḷoṭu mīyaccūr iṉṉa nāḷ akalār iḷaṅkōyil
Civaṉ who does not leave even in this day with a lady who wears a shining girdle, in mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil.
poṉ am koṉṟaiyum pū aṇi mālaiyum
koṉṟi which are golden in colour and other garlands knitted beautifully with other flowers.
piṉṉum ceñcaṭaimēl piṟai cūṭiṟṟu
he wore along with the crescent on the interwining red caṭi.
{5:11}__7+
{$}
paṭai koḷ pūtattaṉ
Civaṉ has an army of pūtam
paiṅkoṉṟaittāriṉaṉ
wears a garland of ever fresh koṉṟi.
caṭai koḷ veḷḷattaṉ
bears the flood (of the Kaṅki)
cānta veṇṇīṟṟiṉaṉ
smears sacred ash as sandal paste
tiru mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil
in the iḷaṅkoyil in tirumīyaccur.
viṭai koḷ ūrtatiyiṉāṉ
Civaṉ who has a bull as his vehicle.
iṭai koṇṭu ētta niṉṟār
dwelt to be worshipped knowing the proper time.
{5:11}__8+
{$}
āṟu koṇṭa caṭaiyiṉar
Civaṉ received a river on his caṭi
vēṟu koṇṭatu ōr vēṭattar ākilum
though he has assumed many different forms.
kūṟu koṇṭu ukantāḷoṭu
accompanied by the lady who is rejoicing in having his half.
mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil ēṟu koṇṭu ukantār
he dwells in mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil, riding on a bull, to grant his grace to devotees.
{5:11}__9+
{$}
puṇṇiyaṉ taṉṉaiyē
Civaṉ who is the embodiment of all virtuous acts.
vētattāṉ eṉpar
learned men say that he is in the vētams
vēḷviyuḷāṉ eṉpar
is in the sacrificial fire.
pūtattāṉ eṉpar
is in the five elements.
kītattāṉ kiḷarum tiru mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil ētam tīrkka niṉṟār
stood in the mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil where music in pre-eminent, to remove the defects of his devotees.
{5:11}__10+
{$}
karukkaṇṭaṉ
Civaṉ who has poison in his neck.
kayilāyamalai eṭukkaluṟṟa irāvaṇaṉ īṭaṟa
to destroy the strength of Irāvaṇaṉ who lifted the mountin, Kayilāyam.
viṭukkaṇ iṉṟi vekuṇṭavaṉ
grew angry so that there was no way of escape.
mīyaccūr iḷaṅkōyil iṭukkaṇtīrkka niṉṟār
was in miyāccūr ilaṅkōyil to remove the afflictions of his devotees.

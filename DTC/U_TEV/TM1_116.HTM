*1

*2

tīviṉi vantu _emit tīṇṭappeṟā;---tirunīlakaṇṭam!

*3

vilittali _āvaṇam koṇṭu _emi _āṇṭa viricaṭiyīr!
_ilittaliccūlamum taṇṭum maḻuvum _ivi _uṭiyīr!
cilittu _emit tīviṉi tīṇṭappeṟā;---tirunīlakaṇṭam!

*4
viṇṇulaku _āḷkiṉṟa viccātararkaḷum vētiyarum,
puṇṇiyar _eṉṟu _irupōtum toḻappaṭum puṇṇiyarē!
kaṇ_imiyātaṉamūṉṟu _uṭiyīr! _um kaḻal _aṭintōm;
tiṇṇiya tīviṉi tīṇṭappeṟā;---tirunīlakaṇṭam!

*5
maṟṟu _iṇi _illā mali tiraṇṭaṉṉa tiṇtōḷ _uṭiyīr!
kiṟṟu _emi _āṭkoṇṭu kēḷātu _oḻivatum taṉmikollō?

ceṟṟu _emit tīviṉi tīṇṭappeṟā;---tirunīlakaṇṭam!

*6

ciṟappu_ilit tīviṉi tīṇṭappeṟā;---tirunīlakaṇṭam!

*7

_uruki, malar koṭuvantu, _umi _ēttutum, nām_aṭiyōm;

tiru_ilit tīviṉi tīṇṭappeṟā;---tirunīlakaṇṭam!

*8

cīṟṟam_atu_ām viṉi tīṇṭappeṟā;---tirunīlakaṇṭam!

*9

tīkkuḻit tīviṉi tīṇṭappeṟā;---tirunīlakaṇṭam!

*10

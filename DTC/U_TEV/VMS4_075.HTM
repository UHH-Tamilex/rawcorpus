tūya kāviriyiṉ naṉṉīr koṇṭu irukku ōti āṭṭi, kuṅkumakkuḻampu cātti
adorning Civaṉ with a liquid of thick consistency contining saffron, after bathing him with good water from the pure Kāviri, chanting mantiram-s of the Vētam-s.
iṇṭai koṇṭu (1) ēṟa nōkki
looking forward at him with a circlet of flowers.
īcaṉai, empirāṉai, kaṇṭaṉaik kaṇṭirātē kālattaik kaḻittavāṟē
what a pity I wasted away time without having a vision of the Lord of the universe, our master and warrior.
toṇṭaṉēṉ paṭṭatu eṉṉē
what is the gin that I, his servant, obtined? (nothing).
[[Variant reading: (1) ēṟanūkki]]

{$}
nāṉ piṉ ilēṉ
I have no end.
muṉ ilēṉ
I have no beginning.
piṟappu aṟuttu aruḷ ceyvāṉē
Lord who grants his grace destroying births!
nāyiṉēṉ nāṉ eṉ ilēṉ
I am as low as a dog; In there anything that I do not have?
iḷaṅkatirp payalait tiṅkaḷ cilnilā eṟikkum ceṉṉic civapurattu amarar ēṟē
the superior god of the immortals, who dwells in civapuram, and who has on his head, a crescent which has mild rays and sheds a little light.
nīṟu cēr akalattāṉē
one who has smeared his chest with sacred ash!
niṉ alālkaṉaikaḷ ārē
who is my support except you?

kaḷḷaṉēṉ
I am a deceiptful person.
kaḷḷat toṇṭāy
my service also being deceptive in nature.
kālattaik kaḻittup pōkki
wasting my time and life.
teḷḷiyēṉ āki
after attining clear knowledge.
niṉṟu tēṭiṉēṉ
I searched for you for a long time.
nāṭik kaṇṭēṉ
after search I found you.
uḷkuvār uḷkiṟṟu ellām uṭaṉ iruntu aṟiti eṉṟu veḷkiṉēṉ
I was ashamed when I came to know that you know all the thought that people think, being associated with them without being seen.
veḷki
being ashamed.
nāṉum vilā iṟa cirittiṭṭēṉē
I too lughed to make the ribs to split;

uṭampu eṉum maṉaiyakattu
in the house which is the body.
uḷḷamē takaḷi āka
having the heart as the hollow of the lamp into which oil is poured
[[takaḷi: bowl of a lamp]]
maṭampaṭum uṇarney aṭṭi
pouring the ghee of the limited knowledge of the soul acquired by the senses which is combined with ignorance.
uyir eṉum tirimayakki
fixing the wick which is life
[[PP: uṭal eṉum iṭiñcil taṉṉil ney amar tiriyum āki neñcattuḷ viḷakkum āki (Nāvukkaracar, Tiruccempoṉpaḷḷi (1) 2)]]
iṭampaṭu ñāṉattīyāl eri koḷa iruntu nōkkil
if one meditates burning the lamp with the fire of the extensive spiritual wisdom, without any other activity of the mind.
kaṭampu amar kāḷaitātai kaḻal aṭikāṇalāmē
one can obtin a vision of the feet wearing Kaḻal of Civaṉ who is the father of the youth, Murukaṉ, who is partial to the Kaṭampa tree.

vañcappeṇ araṅku kōyil vāḷ eyiṟṟu aravam tuñcā
the cobras, with shining fangs do not sleep having as their temple the stage, cati, as the place of the deceitful lady, Kaṅki.
vañcappeṇ iruntacūḻal vāṉtavam matiyam tōyum
the crescent that crawls in the sky lives in that locality of the deceiptful Kaṅki.
vañcappeṇ vāḻkkaiyāḷaṉ vāḻviṉai vāḻaluṟṟu
trying to lead the life of Civaṉ who lives with a deceiptful lady.
vañcappeṇ uṟakkam āṉēṉ
I slept pretending to sleep like a lady always thinking of her paramour (The meaning of this verse is not as clear as it should be)
vañcaṉēṉ eṉ ceykēṉē
what shall I who am a deceiptful person, do?

uḷkuvār uḷḷattāṉai
Civaṉ who occupies the heart of those who meditate on him.
uṇarvu eṉum perumaiyāṉai
who has the greatness of being the spiritual knowledge.
(1) uḷkiṉēṉ nāṉum kāṇpēṉ
to have a vision I too meditated on him.
ūṟi ūṟi urukiṉēṉ
my heart melted, as love was flowing incessantly.
entai, pemmāṉ
our father! god!
eḷkiṉēṉ
I was bashful.
(2) irutalai miṉṉukiṉṟa koḷḷimēl eṟumpu eṉ uḷḷam eṅṅaṉam kūṭumāṟē
How is it possible for my mind which is like the ant that is cught between two ends of firebrand that are burning to reach Civaṉ?

Civaṉ who bears on his caṭi flood, came to me as if to question me to get my consent.
eṉ uḷḷamē pukuntu niṉṟārkku uṟaṅku nāṉ puṭaikaḷ pōntu
when I changed my poisition in sleeping sid to Civaṉ who had entered into my heart and stayed there.

kalantu tāṉ nōkki nakku

veḷḷar viḷaṅku iḷam piṟaiyaṉār ōm eṉṟu niṉṟār
Civaṉ who has on his head flood and a shining young crescent replied yes

opening his mouth to show his teeth which were like the crescent when Civaṉ pressed down for a moment his big toe.
aruvarai aṉaiyatōḷāṉ arakkaṉ aṉṟu alaṟi vīḻntāṉ

Civaṉ, the god of bounded liberality who had in on one body which contins two people who dwells in that mountin.

maṭa neñcamē
my ignorant mind!
ūṟu virumpiviṭēl
do not waste your life desiring ruin.
kalantārkku avaṉ karumpiṉ ūṟal (kaṇṭāy)
he is as sweet as the juice of sugar-cane to those who are united with him
irumpiṉ ūṟal aṟātatu ōr veṇṭalai eṟumpiyūr malaiyāṉ eṅkaḷ īcaṉē
our Lord who is in the hillock in eṟumpiyūr and has a white skull which is as strong as iron and from which fat is oozing.
[[The etukai in this verse is noteworthy;
The name of the shrine is placed in etukai in this verse and verses nos 2,3,4,5,6,7 and 10;]]
[[eṟumpi-eṟumpu ant;]]
[[PP: eṟumpiyaḷaiyiṟ kuṟumpal aṉaiya
(Kuṟuntoki, 12-1)
Var? cf. kuṟum pal cuṉiya]]
{5:74}__2+
{$}
piṟaṅku ceñcaṭaip piññakaṉ
the destroyer who has a shining red caṭi
pēṇu cīrk kaṟaṅku pūtakaṇam uṭaik kaṇṇutal
the god with a frontal eye who has a group of pūtams which dance revolving round him and which have a fame desired by all
naṟuṅkuḻal maṭavāḷoṭu nāḷ toṟum eṟumpiyūr malaiyāṉ eṅkaḷ īcaṉē
our Lord dwells always in the hillock of eṟumpiyūr along with a young lady of fragrant tresses of hir. (naṟunkuḻal nāyaki is the name of the goddess in this shrine)
{5:74}__3+
{$}
vāṉavar maruntu
Civaṉ is the ambrosia to the celestials
tāṉavarkku iṉcuvai
is the sweet taste to the acurar
purinta ceñcaṭaip puṇṇiyaṉ
the embodiment of all virtuous acts who has a fastened red cati
kaṇṇutal
has a frontal eye
pūṇ poruntu mulai maṅkai nallāḷoṭum eṅkaḷ īcaṉ eṟumpiyūr malaiyāṉ
our God, civan, is in the hillock of eṟumpiyūr with a good lady of distinction who wears on her breats ornaments.
{5:74}__4+
{$}
niṟam koḷ kaṇṭattu niṉ malaṉ
the spotless god with a neck of blue colour
em iṟai
our master
maṟaṅkoḷ vēl kaṇṇivāḷ nutal pākamāy
Having a lady of bright forehead and eyes resembling in shape the wrathful vēl
aṟam purintu aruḷ ceyta em am kaṇaṉ
our Civaṉ who has beautiful eyes and who bestowed his grace on devotees, desiring righteousness
eṟumpiyūr malaiyāṉ eṅkaḷ īcaṉ
our Lord is in the hillock in eṟumpiyūr

naṟum poṉ nāḷ malark koṉṟaiyum nākamum tuṟumpu ceñcaṭait tūmati 1. vaittu
Having placed the spotless crescent on the red caṭi in which cobras and koṉṟi flowers that blossomed in the early morning and resemble fine gold, are close to each other
[[nākam may mean also long-leaved two sepelled gamboge: it is commonly called curapuṉṉai]]
[[tuṟumpu is a rare word;]]
[[PP: aṭumpum koṉṟaiyum vaṉṉiyum mattamum, tuṭumpal ceycaṭai (appar, mēlittirukkāṭṭuppaḷḷi, 6)]]
[[ṟa and ṭa are interchangeable]]
vāṉ uṟum poṉ māl varaip pētaiyoṭu ūrtoṟum eṟumpiyūr malaiyāṉ eṅkaḷ īcaṉē
our Lord, Civaṉ, who is in every village along with a lady, the dughter of the big and golden mountin of Kayili on which clouds settle, is in the hillock of eṟumpiyūr
[[Variant reading: 1. vaitta]]

kaṟumpi ūrvaṉa aintu kāyattil uḷa
there are five senses in the body, which eat one little by little and give an itching sensation.
tiṟampi ūrvaṉa maṟṟum pala uḷa
and there are many other things which move slowly and swerve from the right path
kuṟumpi ūrvatu ōr kūṭṭakattu iṭṭu eṉai eṟumpiyūr araṉ ceyta iyaṟkaiyē
this is the nature of Civaṉ in eṟumpiyūr by casting me into a body in which all impurities are moving.
{5:74}__7+
{$}
maṭa neñcamē
my ignorant mind!
(maṟṟu) itu pēr iṭar
this body is the cuse for sufferings
maṟantu nāḷ toṟum tiṟampi nī niṉaiyēl
forgetting god, do not think of anything dily, deviating from the right path
puṟam cey kōlak kurampaiyil iṭṭu eṉai eṟumpiyūr araṉ ceyta iyaṟkaiyē
That is the nature of araṉ in eṟumpiyūr in casting me into a body which is beautiful to outward appearance

iṉpamum piṟappum iṟappi(ṉ)ṉoṭu tuṉpamum uṭaṉē vaitta cōtiyāṉ
the effulgent light which created pleasure, birth, death and suffering simultaneously
eṟumpiyūr īcaṉ aṉpaṉē, araṉē eṉṟu araṟṟuvārkku iṉpaṉ ākum
the Lord in eṟumpiyūr will be granting happiness to those who shout with excitement one who is worthy of love!, araṉ
{5:74}__9+
{$}
kaṇ niṟainta kaṉa pavaḷattiṟaḷ
the heap of weighty coral which is pleasing to the eye.
viṇ niṟainta viricuṭarc cōtiyāṉ
the light that sheds brightness and pervaded the sky
uḷ niṟaintu uruvāy uyir āyavaṉ
and Civaṉ who is the life and the body pervading the mind.
eṇ niṟainta eṟumpiyūr īcaṉē
is the Lord in eṟumpiyūr who completely occupies my mind.

niṟam koḷ mālvarai ūṉṟi eṭuttalum
as soon as (arakkaṉ) lifted the white-coloured big mountin of Kayili by fixing his hands firmly
naṟuṅkuḻal maṭavāḷ naṭukku eytiṭa
when the lady with fragrant tresses of hir shook with fear
eṟumpiyūr malai em iṟai maṟam koḷ vāḷ arakkaṉ vali vāṭṭiṉāṉ
our master in the hillock of Eṟumpiyūr destroyed the strength of the arakkaṉ who had a valourous sword.

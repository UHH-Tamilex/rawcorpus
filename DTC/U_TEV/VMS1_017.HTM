{1:17}__1+
{$} maṉam ār taru maṭavāroṭu makiḻ maintarkaḷ malar tūyt taṉam ārtaru
the youths who are happy in the company of young girls who are full of love in their hearts, scatter flowers and a result of that act get wealth.
caṅkakkaṭal vaṅkattiraḷ unti
the sea with conches pushes the fleet of ships.
ciṉam ārtaru tiṟal vāḷ eyiṟṟu arakkaṉ miku kuṉṟil
in the superior Kuṉṟaḷūr which belongs to the arakkaṉ, itumpaṉ, who has teeth like the sword, valour, and a mind full of anger.
iṉam mātavar iṟaivarkku iṭam iṭumpāvaṉam itu (ē)
this is Itumpāvaṉam which is the place of Civaṉ who is the Lord of the assembly of sages.
[[taṉam ārtaru kuṉṟu, untimiku kuṉṟil: is the syntactical link;
kuṉṟu is kuṉṟaḷūr
see verse 11 of this decade, arakkaṉ mentioned here is Itūmpaṉ who worshipped Civaṉ in this shrine and therefore derives this name]]
{1:17}__2+
{$} malai ārtaru maṭavāḷ orupākam makiḻvu eyti nilai ārtaru nimalaṉ
the supreme spotless Civaṉ who is permanent, feeling happy in having on one half the dughter of the mountin.
vali nilavum pukaḻ oḷicēr(kuṉṟil)
(in the hill) which has strength, undying fame and brilliance.
kalai ārtaru pulavōr(avar) kāval miku kuṉṟil
in the hill which is guarded heavily by the poets who have studied the arts.
ilai ārtaru poḻil cūḻtarum iṭumpāvaṉam itu (ē)
this is Iṭumpāvaṉam which is surrounded by gardens full of foliage.
{1:17}__3+
{$} cīlammiku (1) cittattavar (2) cintittu eḻum entai
our father whom the devotees who have good conduct think of, and wake up from sleep.
ñālam miku kaṭal cūḻtarum ulakattavar nayam ārkōlam mikum malarmeṉmulai maṭavārmiku kuṉṟil
in the hill where ladies who have soft breasts like the lotus flower, and beauty which is increasing and males of this world, which is surrounded by the ocean whose area is more than that of the earth, crowd to attin salvation.
[[Variant reading: (1) cittaravar (2) cintittoḻumentai]]
ēlam kamaḻpoḻil cūḻtarum iṭumpāvaṉam itu (ē)
this is Iṭumpāvaṉam surrounded by plantations from which the fragrance of cardamom spreads.
{1:17}__4+
{$} poḻil ārtarukulai vāḻaikaḷ eḻil ārtaru (1) pōḻtil
at that time when the plantin gardens appear beautiful with clusters of fruits.
toḻilāl miku toṇṭaravar toḻutu āṭiya mūṉṟil kuḻal ār tarum alar meṉmulai maṭavār miku kuṉṟil
n the hill which has a frontyard where the ladies who have flowers tucked in their tresses of hirs and soft breasts, and the devotees who excel in their service to god worship him and dance out of ecstasy.
eḻil ārtaru iṟaivarkku iṭam iṭumpāvaṉam ituvē(ē)
Iṭumpāvaṉam which is the place for the Lord who is exceedingly beautiful.
[[mūṉṟilaiyuṭaiya kuṉṟil is the syntactical link]]
[[Variant reading: (1) pōtil]]
{1:17}__5+
{$} pantu ār viral umaiyāḷ oru paṅkā kaṅkai muṭimēl entāy eṉa
to be prised by devotees as Civaṉ who has Umayāl who holds a ball in her hand, as one half! our father who has Kaṅki on his head!'
centāmarai malar malkiya ceḻunīr vayaṟkaraimēl
on the ridges of the fields which has fertile water in which the red lotus flower grows in abundance.
kontuār (1) malarppuṉṉai, makiḻ, kuravam, kamaḻ kuṉṟil
in the hill where the mast-wood tree which has bunches of flowers, pointed leaved ape-flowertrees and bottle-flower trees spread their fragrance.
iruntāṉ iṭam iṭumpāvaṉam itu (ē)
this is Iṭumpāvaṉam where Civaṉ resides.
[[Variant reading: (1) malarpuṉṉai]]
{1:17}__6+
{$} neṟi nīrmaiyar, nīḷ vāṉavar, niṉaiyum niṉaivāki
being the object of meditation to the tall celestials and sages who follow the rightous path.
aṟinīrmaiyil eytum(m) avarkku aṟiyum aṟivu aruḷi
granting the spiritual wisdom to those who realize Civaṉ and approach him in the proper way to know him.
kuṟinīrmaiyar (1) kuṇam ārtarumaṇam ārtaru kuṉṟil
in the hill which is full of fragrance which cuses the devotees whose aim is to obtin vision of god, to experience all his qualities.
eṟi nīr vayalpuṭai cūḻtarum iṭumpāvaṉam itu (ē)
this is Itumpāvaṉam which is surrounded on all sides by fields where water dashes aginst the ridges by its waves.
[[Variant reading: (1) kuḷamārtaru]]
{1:17}__7+
{$} nīṟu eṟiya tirumēṉiyar
Civaṉ who smears sumptuously with sacred ash all over his holy body.
nilavum ulaku ellām
in all the existing worlds.
pāṟu ēṟiya paṭuveṇtalai kaiyiṟ pali (1) vāṅkā
receiving alms in the dead white skull on which kites settle holding it in his hand.
kūṟu ēṟiya maṭavāḷoru pākam makiḻvu eyti
feeling happy by keeping on one half a young lady as a part.
ēṟu ēṟiya iṟaivaṟku iṭam iṭumpāvaṉam itu (ē)
this is Itumpāvaṉam, the place of the Lord who rides on a bull.
[[Variant reading: (1) pāṅkāyk]]
{1:17}__8+
{$} tēr ārtaru tikaḻ vāḷ eyiṟṟu arakkaṉ civaṉ malaiyai ōrātu eṭuttu ārttāṉ muṭi oru paḵtu (avai) nerittu
crushing the ten heads of the arakkaṉ who was riding on a chariat and had shining teeth like swords as he lifted without any thought the mountin and roared out of joy.
kūr ārtaru kolai vāḷoṭu kuṇa nāmamum koṭutta ēr ārtarum iṟaivarkku iṭṭi iṭumpāvaṉam itu(ē)
this is Iṭumpāvaṉam for the beautiful Lord who gave the name and a sword which is sharp and capable of killing enemies as he wept.
{1:17}__9+
{$} poruḷ ārtaru maṟaiyōr pukaḻ viruttar poli mali cīrtteruḷ ārtaru cintaiyoṭu cantam (m) malar pala tūy
the brahmins who studied the vētams full of abstruse meanings and those who have excessive fame, scattering beautiful flowers, with a clear mind, by which they shine and have great renown (worship Civaṉ).
maruḷ ārtaru māyaṉ(ṉ) ayaṉ kāṇār mayarvu eyta
Māyaṉ (Tirumāl) and Ayaṉ who have great ignorance to be bewildered as they could not obtin vision of Civaṉ
iruḷ ārtaru kaṇṭarkku iṭam iṭumpāvaṉam itu (ē)
this is Iṭumpāvaṉam which is the place for Civaṉ who has a very dark neck.
{1:17}__10+
{$} taṭukkai uṭaṉ iṭukkit talaipaṟittuc (1) camaṇ naṭappār
those who follow camaṇ plucking the hirs on the head and taking a mat under their armpit.
uṭukkai pala tuvark kūṟaikaḷ uṭampu iṭṭu uḻalvārum
and the buddhists who put on their bodies many dresses as robes soaked in red ochre (to indulge in slander).
maṭukkaḷ malar vayal cērcennelmali nīrmalark karaimēl (iṭumpāvaṉam)
iṭumpāvaṉam which is on the banks which have plenty of water and deep tanks and extensive fields in which red Variety of paddy is grown.
iṭukkaṇ pala kaḷaivāṉiṭam iṭumpāvaṉam itu (ē)
this is iṭumpāvaṉam the place of Civaṉ who weeds out many sufferings to his devotees.
[[Variant reading: (1) camaṇtappār]]
{1:17}__11+
{$} koṭiār neṭumāṭak kuṉṟaḷūriṟkaraikkōla iṭiār kaṭal aṭi vīḻtarum iṭumpāvaṉattu iṟaiyai
about the Lord in iṭumpāvaṉam where the sea falls at the feet of the Lord and strikes aginst the beautiful shore in Kuṉṟaḷūr which has tall storeys in which flags are flying.
aṭiāyum antaṇarkāḻiyuḷ aṇi ñāṉacampantaṉ (1) paṭiyāl coṉṉa pāṭal cola
to receite the songs which were composed according to the aptitute of the people by beautiful ñāṉacampantaṉ who resides in Kāḻi where brahmins always think of the pleasure derived from meditating on the feet of Civaṉ.
paṟaiyum viṉai (tāṉ, ē)
the acts of those people will disappear.
[[Variant reading: (1) paṭiyār]]

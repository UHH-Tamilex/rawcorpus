{3:112}__1+
{$} paracupāṇiyar
the Lord has a battle-axe in his hand.
pāṭal vīṇaiyar
has a vīṇi to play upon.
paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu aracupēṇi niṉṟār ivar taṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know the real nature of this Lord who wishes to rule in Pallavaṉīccaram situated within Paṭṭiṉam?
{3:112}__2+
{$} paṭṭa neṟṟiyar
the Lord has a plate on his forehead
naṭṭam āṭuvar
He will dance.
paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu iṭṭamāy iruppār ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know the real nature of this Lord who stays with desire in Pallavaṉiccaram situated within Paṭṭiṉam?
{3:112}__3+
{$} pavaḷamēṉiyar
the Lord has a body red like coral.
tikaḻum nīṟṟiṉar
has smeared shining ashes.
paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu aḻakarāy iruppār ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know the real nature of this Lord who is a beautiful person in Pallavaṉiccaram situated within Paṭṭiṉam?
[[The rhyme (etukai) is to be noted]]
{3:112}__4+
{$} paṇṇil yāḻiṉar
the Lord has a yāḻ on which paṇ can be played
[[paṇ the melody that has all the seven notes]]
payilum montaiyar
has a drum with one face on which he practises
paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu aṇṇalāyiruppār ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know the real nature of this great good who is in Pallavaṉīccaram situated within Paṭṭiṉam?
{3:112}__5+
{$} pal il ōṭṭiṉar
the Lord has a skull without teeth.
palikoṇṭu uṇpavar
He eats from that, receiving alms.
paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu elli āṭṭu ukantār ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know the real nature of this Lord who gladly dances at night and who is in Pallavaṉīccaram situated within Paṭṭiṉam?
{3:112}__6+
{$} paccai mēṉiyar
the lord's form is green in colour.
[[cf. paccai niṟamuṭaiyar pālar cālap paḻaiyar (appar, tiruviṭimarutūr tāṇṭakam (2) 7).
[[Civaṉ is sid to have five colour;
niṟaṅkaḷ ōr aintuṭaiyāy (tiruvācakam, Civapurāṇam)]]
paccai koḷpavar
He receives alms.
paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu iccaiyāy iruppār ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know the real nature of this Lord who receives with desire in Pallavaṉiccaram situated within Paṭṭiṉam?
{3:112}__7+
{$} paiṅkaṇ ēṟṟiṉar
the Lord has a bull of tender eyes.
tiṅkaḷ cūṭuvar
adorns on his head the crescent moon.
eṅkumāyp paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu iruppār
He is everywhere; he is in Pallavaṉīccaram situated in Paṭṭiṉam, to receive devotees as his protege.
ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know his real nature?
{3:112}__8+
{$} pātam kai toḻa vētam ōtuvar
the Lord will chant the Vētam-s, when his devotees worship his feet.
paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu ātiyāy iruppār
he is the cuse for all things, in Pallavaṉīccaram situated in Paṭṭiṉam.
ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know his real nature?
{3:112}__9+
{$} paṭikoḷ mēṉiyar
He assumes different kinds of form.
kaṭikoḷ koṉṟaiyar
He has flowers of fragrant koṉṟi.
paṭṭiṉattuṟai pallavaṉīccarattu aṭikaḷāy iruppār
he is the deity in Pallavaṉiccaram situated within Paṭṭiṉam.
ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know his real nature?
{3:112}__10+
{$} paṟai koḷ pāṇiyar
the Lord has a drum in his hand.
piṟaikoḷ ceṉṉiyar
has a crescent on his head.
paṭṭiṉattu uṟai pallavaṉīccarattu iṟaivarāy iruppār
he will be the eminent god in Pallavaṉīccaram situated within Paṭṭiṉam.
ivartaṉmai ār aṟivār
who can know his real nature?
{3:112}__11+
{$} ñāṉacampantaṉ naṟṟamiḻ mātar pallavaṉīccarattāṉaic collavallavar nallavarē
Those who are able to prise the god in beautiful Pallavaṉīccaram with the good verses composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ are definitely good people.
vāṉam āḷvataṟku ūṉam oṉṟu(m) ilai
there will not be the slightest obstacle to their ruling heaven.
[[In these ten verses there are no references to Rāvaṇaṉ, Piramaṉ and Māl, and to jins and buddhists which Ñāṉcampantar never fails of mention in the 8th, 9th and 10th verses]]

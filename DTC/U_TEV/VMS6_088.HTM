cērātē
Keeping myself away from
ār ārum mūvilai vēl aṅkaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who holds in his palm a trident of three sharp blades.
[[ār-kūrmai ; sharpness]]
[[PP: ār eyiṟu aḻuntiṉar
(Cilappatikāram 5-124)]]
[[vēl is used in the sense of weapon.]]
alaikaṭal nañcu ayiṉṟāṉai
who drank the poison that rose in the roaming sea
amarar ēttum ēr ārum mati potiyum caṭaiyiṉāṉai
who hides in his caṭi a beautiful crescent which is prised by the immortals
[[PP: piṟainutal vaṇṇam ākiṉṟu, appiṟai, patiṉeṇ kaṇaṉum ēttavum paṭumē
(Puṟam, 1, 9-10)]]
eḻu piṟappum eṉai 11. āḷā uṭaiyāṉ taṉṉai
who has the right to clim me as his slave in all the seven births.
ūr ārum paṭa nākam āṭṭuvāṉai
who cuses the cobra with a hood which moves crawling, to dance
uyar pukaḻ cēr tarum ōmām puliyūr maṉṉum cīr ārum vaṭataḷi em celvaṉ taṉṉai
our Lord who is in the northern temple of eminence which is permanent in Ōmāmpuliyūr which has great fame.
tikaittu nāḷ celuttiṉēṉ
I passed many days fruitlessly being confused in mind.

cērātē
By keeping myself away from
ātiyāṉ ari ayaṉ eṉṟu 1. aṟiya oṇṇā
Civaṉ who could not be known was urruttiraṉ, ari and Ayaṉ
amarar toḻum kaḻalāṉai
whose feet are worshipped by the immortals.
amalaṉ taṉṉai
who is free from impurities
cōti mati kalai 2. tolaiya
who trampled under his feet the bright moon to lose its phases
takkaṉ eccaṉ cuṭar iravi ayil eyiṟu tolaivittāṉai
who cused to destroy the head of Takkaṉ, the god of sacrifice and the sharp teeth of the sun with rays, by Vīrappattirar.
mika ōti antaṇarkaḷ eri mūṉṟu ōmpum uyar pukaḻ ār tarum ōmām puliyūr maṉṉum tītu il tiruvaṭataṉi em celvaṉ taṉṉai
our god who dwells in beautiful northern shrine which is without any blemish and is permanent in Ōmāmpuliyūr which is full of increasing fame as the brahmins there are very learned and mintin the three fires
tikaittu nāḷ celuttiṉēṉ
I spent many days fruitlessly, being confused in mind.

pāṅku uṭaiya eḻil aṅki aruccaṉai muṉ virumpap parintu avaṉukku aruḷ ceyta paramaṉ taṉṉai
the supreme god who bestowed his grace on the god of fire of good qualities and beauty, who performed worship here, being affectionate towards him.

our Lord in the holy nothern temple which always wards off evil to the people of this world.

cērātē
By keeping myself away from
vār keḻuvumulai umaiyāḷ veruva
Umayāl who has breasts on which bodice is fixed, to get frightened,
aṉṟu malai eṭutta vāḷ arakkaṉ tōḷum tāḷum ēr keḻuvu ciram pattum iṟuttu
Having crushed the beautiful ten heads, feet and shoulders of the cruel arakkaṉ who lifted the mountin in the hoary past.
mīṇṭu iṉṉicai kēṭṭiruntāṉai
who was pleased to hear the sweet music after that
imaiyōr kōṉai
the chief of the immortals who do not wink
pār keḻuvu pukaḻ maṟaiyōr payilum māṭap paim poḻil cēr tarum ōmām puliyūr 1. maṉṉum cīr keḻuvu vaṭataḷi em celvaṉ taṉṉai
our Lord in the eminent northern temple which is permanent in Ōmāmapuliyūr where there are verdant gardens, and mansions where brahmins with fame spread throughout the length and breath of the world, live together.
tikaittu nāḷ celuttiṉēṉ
I spent many days fruitlessly, being confused in my mind.

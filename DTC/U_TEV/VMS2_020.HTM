[[NOTE: aḻuntai is a word which is a contracted form of aḻuntūr sanctioned by usage]]

[[there are two sivite shrines in this place, one is called Māmatēcuvarar; the other one is known as Vētapurīcuvar; Campantar's decade is on Māmatēcuvarar.]]

{2:20}__1+
{$} aḻuntai maṟaiyōr
the brahmins in aḻunti
toḻumāṟu vallār
who are capable of worshipping with joined hands.
tuyartīra niṉaintu eḻumāṟu vallār
who are able to wake up thinking of god as their suffering of birth is removed.
icaipāṭa vimmi aḻumāṟu vallār
and who are able to weep and sob in the midst of singing.
vaḻipāṭu ceymāmaṭam maṉṉiṉaiyē
you stayed in the great maṭam where they perform worship with rituals
{2:20}__2+
{$} kaṭal ēṟiya nañcu amutu uṇṭavaṉē
one who consumed as nectar the poison which came on the shore of the ocean!
uṭalē
the body!
uyirē
the life within it!
uṇarvē
the knowledge!
eḻilē
the beauty!
aṭal ēṟu uṭaiyāy
one who has a strong bull
viṭalē
the chief!
aḻuntai maṟaiyōr toḻa māmaṭam mēviṉaiyē
you desired the big maṭam to be worshipped by the brahmins of Aḻunti.
{2:20}__3+
{$} kaḻikāṭalaṉē
you dance in the cremation ground at night;
[[cf. karikāṭaliṉāy (campantar, Kaḻippāli (1) 8)]]
kaṉal āṭaliṉāy
you dance in the fire
paḻipāṭu ilaṉē
you have nothing to be despised
avaiyē payilum aḻipāṭu ilarāy
without knowing defeat and practising the learning in assemblies
aḻuntai maṟaiyōr vaḻipāṭu ceymāmaṭam maṉṉiṉaiyē
you stayed permanently in the big maṭam where brahmins of aḻunti perform worship with rituals.
{2:20}__4+
{$} vāṉē
the cosmic space!
malaiyē
the mountin!
eṉ maṉṉuyirē
my eternal life
(tāṉē) toḻuvār toḻu tāḷ maṇiyē
the ruby whose feet are worshipped by worshippers.
āṉē
the cow!
civaṉē
Civaṉ!
aḻuntaiyavar emmāṉē eṉa māmaṭam maṉṉiṉaiyē
you stayed permanently in the great maṭam to be prised by the people of aḻunti as our master.
{2:20}__5+
{$} alai ār puṉalcūḻ aḻuntaipperumāṉ
the great one in aḻunti surrounded by water full of rippling waves!
nilai ār maṟiyum niṟai veṇ maḻuvum ilai ār paṭaiyum ivai ēntu (1) celva
the god who holds in his hands a standing young deer. a pure white battle-axe a trident of three blades.
nilaiyāvatu
To meditate upon these is permanent wealth
koḷ eṉa nī niṉaiyē
you make me think you keep them in your mind'
[[Variant reading: (1) celvā, celvaṉ]]
{2:20}__6+
{$} naṟavu ār talaiyiṉ nayavā
Civaṉ who feels happy in holding a skull filled with today!
[[see notes on campantar, tirukkaḻumalam, tiruvirākane, 7]]
ulakil piṟavātavaṉē
you are not born in the earth as a human being;
[[cf. piṟavāyākkaip periyōṉ (cilappatikāram, 5-169)]]
piṇi illavaṉē
you are free from diseases!
aṟai ār kaḻalāy
you wears Kaḻal which makes a sound!
aḻuntai maṟaiyōr
the brahmins in aḻunti
maṟavātu eḻa
to wake up from sleep remembering you
māmaṭam maṉṉiṉaiyē
you stayed permanently in the great maṭam.
{2:20}__7+
{$} taṭumāṟuvallāy
you who are capable cusing bewilderment to souls to understand you properly!
talaivā
the chief!
matiyam cuṭumāṟu vallāy
you who are capable of inflicting pin to those ladies who fall in love with you, by the crescent!
cuṭar ārcaṭaiyil aṭum āṟu vallāy
you who are capable of carrying a river cusing damage in your brilliant cati!
aḻuntai maṟaiyōr neṭumānakar kaitoḻaniṉṟaṉaiyē
you stayed permanently in the big and long temple to be worshipped by brahmins of aḻunti.
{2:20}__8+
{$} periyāy
great one!
ciṟiyāy
and at the same time a small one!
piṟaiyāy
one who has a crescent!
miṭaṟukariyāy
one who has a black neck!
karikāṭu uyar vīṭu uṭaiyāy
you who have the scorched cremation-ground as your temple;
[[cf. kōyil cuṭukāṭu kol pulitōl nallāṭai (tiruvācakam, tiruccāḻal, 3)]]
ariyāy eḷiyāy
you who are difficult to attin to non-devotees; but at the same time easy of accessebility to devotees!
aḻuntai maṟaiyōr veriyār toḻa māmaṭam maṉṉiṉaiyē
you stayed permanently in the big maṭam to be worshipped by the brahmins in aḻunti who are afrid of birth.

{2:20}__9+
{$} maṇinīḷ muṭiyāṉ arakkaṉ
the arakkaṉ who were a long crown set with precious stones.
malaiyait taṇiyātu uṭuttāṉ uṭalam neritta aṇi ār viralāy
you have a beautiful toe with which you crushed the body (of that arakkaṉ) when he lifted the mountin, his anger not being softened.
aḻuntai maṟaiyōr (1) paṇi māmaṭam maṉṉiyiruntaṉaiyē
you were permanently staying in the big matam to which the brahmins pay obeisance
[[Variant reading: (1) maṇi]]
{2:20}__10+
{$} muṭi ārcaṭaiyāy
you have a caṭi coiled into a crown!
muṉam nāḷ iruvar neṭiyāṉ malarāṉ ivarkaḷ nikaḻvāl aṭi mēl aṟiyār
these two persons the tall Māl and Piramaṉ in the lotus flower could not find the head and feet in the distant past, by their efforts to find them.
{2:20}__11+
{$} aruñāṉam vallār aḻuntai maṟaiyōr
god who is worshipped by the brahmins in aḻunti who possess the previous spiritual wisdom rare to obtin.
peru ñāṉam uṭaip perumāṉ avaṉai
and who has abundant spiritual wisdom.
tiruñāṉacampantaṉa centamiḻkaḷ
the verses composed on him by tiruñāṉacampantaṉ, in refined standard tamiḻ
uruñāṉam uṇṭām uṇarntār tamakkē
the knowledge of realising the true form of Civaṉ will come to them to its own accord.

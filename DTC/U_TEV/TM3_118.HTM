• patikam: [3:118]
  • talam: tiruk kaḻumalam (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: puṟanīrmi
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.395

*1
maṭal mali koṉṟi, tuṉṟu vāḷ_erukkum, vaṉṉiyum, mattamum, caṭimēl
paṭal _oli tirikaḷ mōtiya kaṅkit talivaṉārtam _iṭam pakaril,---
viṭal _oli paranta veṇtiri muttam _ippikaḷ koṇarntu, veḷ_aruvik
kaṭal _oli _ōtam mōta, vantu _alikkum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*2
miṉṉiya _aravum, veṟimalarpalavum, virumpiya tiṅkaḷum, taṅku
ceṉṉi_atu _uṭiyāṉ, tēvartam perumāṉ, cēyiḻiyoṭum _uṟivu_iṭam_ām---
poṉ _iyal maṇiyum, muri karimaruppum, cantamum, _untu vaṉ tirikaḷ
kaṉṉiyar _āṭa, kaṭal _oli maliyum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*3
cīr _uṟu toṇṭar, koṇṭu _aṭi pōṟṟa, ceḻu malar puṉaloṭu tūpam;
tār _uṟu koṉṟi tam muṭi vitta civaṉār taṅku _iṭam---_eṅkum
_ūr _uṟu patikaḷ _ulaku_uṭaṉ poṅki _olipuṉal koḷa, _uṭaṉmitanta,
kār _uṟu cemmi naṉmiyāl mikka---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*4
maṇṇiṉār _ētta, vāṉ_uḷār paraca, _antarattu _amararkaḷ pōṟṟa,
paṇṇiṉār _ellām; palapala vēṭam _uṭiyavar; payilvu _iṭam---_eṅkum
_eṇṇiṉāl mikkār, _iyalpiṉāl niṟintār, _ēntiḻiyavaroṭu mintar
kaṇṇiṉāl _iṉpam kaṇṭu, _oḷi parakkum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*5
curutiyāṉ taliyum, nāmakaḷ mūkkum, cuṭaravaṉ karamum, muṉ _iyaṅku
parutiyāṉ pallum, _iṟuttu, _avarkku _araḷum paramaṉār payiṉṟu _iṉitu _irukki---
virutiṉ nālmaṟiyum, _aṅkam_ōr_āṟum, vēḷviyum vēṭṭavar, ñāṉam
karutiṉār, _ulakil karuttu _uṭiyār, cēr---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*6
puṟṟil vāḷ_aravum _āmiyum pūṇṭa puṉitaṉār, paṉimalarkkoṉṟi
paṟṟi vāṉmatiyam caṭi_iṭi vitta paṭiṟaṉār, payiṉṟu _iṉitu _irukki---
ceṟṟu vaṉ tirikaḷ _oṉṟoṭu _oṉṟu _ōṭic ceyirttu, vaṇ caṅkoṭu vaṅkam
kal-tuṟi vari koḷ karikku vantu _urikkum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*7
_ali puṉal kaṅki taṅkiya caṭiyār, _aṭal neṭumatil_orumūṉṟum
koli_iṭic centī ventu _aṟak kaṇṭa kuḻakaṉār, kōyilatu _eṉpar---
maliyiṉ mikku _uyarnta marakkalam carakku maṟṟumaṟṟu _iṭi_iṭi _eṅkum
kali kaḷittu _ēṟik kāṉalil vāḻum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*8
_orukka muṉ niṉiyāt takkaṉtaṉ vēḷvi _uṭitara _uḻaṟiya paṭiyār,
_arakkaṉi variyāl _āṟṟal _aṉṟu _aḻitta _aḻakaṉār, _amarntu _uṟi kōyil---
parakkum vaṇpukaḻār paḻi_avi pārttup palapala _aṟaṅkaḷē payiṟṟi,
karakkum_āṟu _aṟiyā vaṇmiyāl vāḻum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*9
_aru vari poṟutta _āṟṟaliṉāṉum, _aṇi kiḷar tāmariyāṉum,
_iruvarum _ētta, _eri_uru _āṉa _iṟivaṉār _uṟivu_iṭam viṉavil,---
_oruvar _iv _ulakil vāḻkilā vaṇṇam _olipuṉalveḷḷam muṉ parappa,
karuvari cūḻnta kaṭal_iṭi mitakkum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*10
_urintu _uyar _uruvil _uṭi tavirntārum, _at tukil pōrttu _uḻalvārum,
terintu puṉ moḻikaḷ ceppiṉa kēḷāc cemmiyār naṉmiyāl _uṟivu_ām---
kuruntu, _uyar kōṅku, koṭiviṭu mulli, malliki, caṇpakam, vēṅki,
karuntaṭaṅkaṇṇiṉ maṅkimār koyyum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*11
kāṉal _am kaḻaṉi _ōtam vantu _ulavum kaḻumalanakar _uṟivārmēl
ñāṉacampantaṉ nal-tamiḻmāli naṉmiyāl _uriceytu navilvār
_ūṉa campantattu _uṟu piṇi nīṅki, _uḷḷamum _oruvaḻik koṇṭu,
vāṉ_iṭi vāḻvar; maṇimicip piṟavār; maṟṟu _itaṟku _āṇiyum namatē.

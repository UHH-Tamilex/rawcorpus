• patikam: [3:118]
  • talam: tiruk kaḻumalam (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: puṟanīrmai
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.395

*1
maṭal mali koṉṟai, tuṉṟu vāḷ_erukkum, vaṉṉiyum, mattamum, caṭaimēl
paṭal _oli tiraikaḷ mōtiya kaṅkait talaivaṉārtam _iṭam pakaril,---
viṭal _oli paranta veṇtirai muttam _ippikaḷ koṇarntu, veḷ_aruvik
kaṭal _oli _ōtam mōta, vantu _alaikkum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*2
miṉṉiya _aravum, veṟimalarpalavum, virumpiya tiṅkaḷum, taṅku
ceṉṉi_atu _uṭaiyāṉ, tēvartam perumāṉ, cēyiḻaiyoṭum _uṟaivu_iṭam_ām---
poṉ _iyal maṇiyum, muri karimaruppum, cantamum, _untu vaṉ tiraikaḷ
kaṉṉiyar _āṭa, kaṭal _oli maliyum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*3
cīr _uṟu toṇṭar, koṇṭu _aṭi pōṟṟa, ceḻu malar puṉaloṭu tūpam;
tār _uṟu koṉṟai tam muṭi vaitta caivaṉār taṅku _iṭam---_eṅkum
_ūr _uṟu patikaḷ _ulaku_uṭaṉ poṅki _olipuṉal koḷa, _uṭaṉmitanta,
kār _uṟu cemmai naṉmaiyāl mikka---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*4
maṇṇiṉār _ētta, vāṉ_uḷār paraca, _antarattu _amararkaḷ pōṟṟa,
paṇṇiṉār _ellām; palapala vēṭam _uṭaiyavar; payilvu _iṭam---_eṅkum
_eṇṇiṉāl mikkār, _iyalpiṉāl niṟaintār, _ēntiḻaiyavaroṭu maintar
kaṇṇiṉāl _iṉpam kaṇṭu, _oḷi parakkum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*5
curutiyāṉ talaiyum, nāmakaḷ mūkkum, cuṭaravaṉ karamum, muṉ _iyaṅku
parutiyāṉ pallum, _iṟuttu, _avarkku _araḷum paramaṉār payiṉṟu _iṉitu _irukkai---
virutiṉ nālmaṟaiyum, _aṅkam_ōr_āṟum, vēḷviyum vēṭṭavar, ñāṉam
karutiṉār, _ulakil karuttu _uṭaiyār, cēr---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*6
puṟṟil vāḷ_aravum _āmaiyum pūṇṭa puṉitaṉār, paṉimalarkkoṉṟai
paṟṟi vāṉmatiyam caṭai_iṭai vaitta paṭiṟaṉār, payiṉṟu _iṉitu _irukkai---
ceṟṟu vaṉ tiraikaḷ _oṉṟoṭu _oṉṟu _ōṭic ceyirttu, vaṇ caṅkoṭu vaṅkam
kal-tuṟai varai koḷ karaikku vantu _uraikkum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*7
_alai puṉal kaṅkai taṅkiya caṭaiyār, _aṭal neṭumatil_orumūṉṟum
kolai_iṭaic centī ventu _aṟak kaṇṭa kuḻakaṉār, kōyilatu _eṉpar---
malaiyiṉ mikku _uyarnta marakkalam carakku maṟṟumaṟṟu _iṭai_iṭai _eṅkum
kalai kaḷittu _ēṟik kāṉalil vāḻum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*8
_orukka muṉ niṉaiyāt takkaṉtaṉ vēḷvi _uṭaitara _uḻaṟiya paṭaiyār,
_arakkaṉai varaiyāl _āṟṟal _aṉṟu _aḻitta _aḻakaṉār, _amarntu _uṟai kōyil---
parakkum vaṇpukaḻār paḻi_avai pārttup palapala _aṟaṅkaḷē payiṟṟi,
karakkum_āṟu _aṟiyā vaṇmaiyāl vāḻum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*9
_aru varai poṟutta _āṟṟaliṉāṉum, _aṇi kiḷar tāmaraiyāṉum,
_iruvarum _ētta, _eri_uru _āṉa _iṟaivaṉār _uṟaivu_iṭam viṉavil,---
_oruvar _iv _ulakil vāḻkilā vaṇṇam _olipuṉalveḷḷam muṉ parappa,
karuvarai cūḻnta kaṭal_iṭai mitakkum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*10
_urintu _uyar _uruvil _uṭai tavirntārum, _at tukil pōrttu _uḻalvārum,
terintu puṉ moḻikaḷ ceppiṉa kēḷāc cemmaiyār naṉmaiyāl _uṟaivu_ām---
kuruntu, _uyar kōṅku, koṭiviṭu mullai, mallikai, caṇpakam, vēṅkai,
karuntaṭaṅkaṇṇiṉ maṅkaimār koyyum---kaḻumalanakar _eṉal _āmē.

*11
kāṉal _am kaḻaṉi _ōtam vantu _ulavum kaḻumalanakar _uṟaivārmēl
ñāṉacampantaṉ nal-tamiḻmālai naṉmaiyāl _uraiceytu navilvār
_ūṉa campantattu _uṟu piṇi nīṅki, _uḷḷamum _oruvaḻik koṇṭu,
vāṉ_iṭai vāḻvar; maṇimicaip piṟavār; maṟṟu _itaṟku _āṇaiyum namatē.

{$} tīviṉaiyēṉ aṟiyātē
I, who had committed sins, without knowing.
karumaṇiyai
Civaṉ as precious as the apple of the eye.
kaṉakattiṉkuṉṟu oṉṟu oppāṉai
who is like the hill of gold.
karutuvārkku āṟṟa eḷiyāṉ taṉṉai
who is very easy of approach to those who meditate on him.
kurumaṇiyai
who is the brilliant gem?
kōḷ (1) āravu oṉṟu āṭṭuvāṉai
who causes a cobra capable of killing to dance.
(2) kolvēṅkai ataḷāṉai
who wears a skin of a tiger which can kill.
kōvaṇavaṉtaṉai
who wears a loin-cloth.
arumaṇiyai
who is a rare gem.
aṭaintavarkaṭku amutu oppāṉai
who is as sweet as the nectar and removes death like the nectar, to those who approached him.
(3) āṉ añcum āṭiyai
who bathes in all the five products of the cow.
nāṉ apayampukka tirumaṇiyai
who is the sapphire in whom I sought refuge (tirumaṇi-nīlamaṇi)
[[maṇṇuṟutirumaṇi puraiyumēṉi (puṟam, 56-5); kaḻuvappaṭṭa aḻakiya nīlamaṇi pōlum tirumēṉiyaiyum (do old commentary); vāṉ katirttiru maṇiviḷaṅkum ceṉṉi (puṟam, 150-6); vāliya oḷiyaiyuṭaiya aḻakiya nīlamaṇi viḷaṅkum ucci (do. old commentary)]]
tirumutukuṉṟu uṭaiyāṉ taṉṉai
who has Tirumutukuṉṟu as his shrine.
tikaittavāṟē
How much pitiable it is that I was confused!

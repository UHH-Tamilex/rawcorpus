{4:56}__1+
{$} mā iruñālam ellām malaraṭi vaṇaṅkum (pōlum)
all the people of the big and wide world will bow before the lotus feet.
pāy iruṅkaṅkaiyāḷaip paṭarcaṭai vaippar (pōlum)
will place the big and rushing Kaṅki in his spreading caṭi (kaṅkaiyāḷ as Kanka is feminine gender in sanskrit she is spoken thus)
kāy irumpoḻilkaḷ cūḻnta kaḻumala ūrarkku ampoṉ āyiram koṭuppar (pōlum) āvaṭu tuṟaiyaṉār
the Lord in āvaṭutūṟi will give one thousand gold coins to Ñāṉacampantar, whose birth-place is Kaḻumalam surrounded by big gardens bearing fruits (all the pōlum is this decade are expletives)

the Lord in āvaṭutuṟi.

holds a young deer in his hand
kuṭantaiyil kulakar (pōlum)

[[kuḻakaṉ kuṭamūkkuiṭamā ... iruntāṉ (campantār, tirukkuṭamūkku, 2)]]
(1) kolpulittōlar (pōlum)
wears the skin of tiger capable of killing (kol puli: the epithet refers to its nature when it was alive).
kaṭainta nañcu uṇpar(pōlum)
will consums the poison which rose when the ocean was churned. (kaṭainta-kaṭaintataṉāl eḻunta which rose on account of churning).
kālaṉaik kāyvar (pōlum)
will kill the god of death.
aṭaintavarkku aṉpar (pōlum)
loves those who have approached him as their refuge.
[[Variant reading: (1) kōṭpuli]]

{$} āvaṭutuṟaiyaṉār
the in āvaṭutuṟi.
uṟṟa nōy tīrppar(pōlum)
will cure disease that afflict people (this is an auto-biographical incident).
uṟutuṇai āvar (pōlum)
is the great companion.
ceṟṟavar puraṅkaḷ mūṉṟum tī eḻac ceṟuvar
will destroy the three cities of the enemies and set them ablaze.
kaṟṟavar paravi ēttikkalantu ulantu alantu pāṭum aṟṟavarkku aṉpar (pōlum)
loves those who surrendered themselves to him only, who are learned and who prise him in the 2nd person and 3rd person, their minds absorbed in him, who are become diminished and suffer.
[[aṟṟavarkku aṉpar: aṟṟavarkku aṟṟa civaṉ (campantar, ālavāy, paṇ-puṟanīrmi, 2)]]

the Lord in āvaṭutuṟi.
maḻuamarkaiyar (pōlum)
has a battle-axe in his hand.
mātu (avaḷ) pākar (pōlum)
has a lady as his half.
eḻunuṉai vēlar (pōlum)
has a trident whose ends are sharp.
eṉpu koṇṭu aṇivar (pōlum)

toḻutu eḻuntu āṭippāṭittōttiram palavum colli aḻumavarkku aṉpar (pōlum)

tukkam ām mūṭartammaittuyarilē vīḻppar (pōlum)

akku arai ārppar (pōlum)
will fasten conchbeads in his waist.

paṭaitaru maḻuvar(pōlum)
holds the weapon of a battle-axe.

the Lord in āvaṭatuṟi, who removes the sufferings of those who seek him as refuge.

{$} munti vāṉōrkaḷ vantu muṟaimaiyāl vaṇaṅki ētta nantimā kāḷar eṉpār naṭu uṭaiyārkaḷ niṟpa
when those who are called Nanti and Mākāḷar stood firm in the right path without any harm and when the celestials came first and bowed and prised in the proper manner.
cintiyātē oḻittār tiripuram erippar (pōlum) antivāṉmatiyam cūṭum āvaṭu tuṟaiyaṉār
the Lord in Āvaṭutuṟi who has on his head the crescent that rises in the sky at sunset will burn the three cities of those who never thought of Civaṉ.
[[When the three cities were distroyed by Civaṉ, three acurar escaped destruction; these three were those who did not become inimical, succumbing to the wily teachings of Māl; of these three, two were appointed as guards of Kayili; their names are Nanti and Mākāḷar.]]

pāṉ amar ēṉam āki
assuming the form of a pig among the ten incarnations (pāṉ a poetic license which consists in the shortening of a word by one or more letters at the beginning as mari for tamari; pāṉ-orupāṉ meaning ten.)
pār iṭantiṭṭa mālum
Māl who went digging the earth.
tēṉ amarntu ēṟum allitticaimukam uṭaiya kōvum
and the chief who has four faces and is seated in the lotus which has abundant honey. (alli-tāmarai)
[[allimātarpulka niṉṟa āyiram tōḷaṉ iṭam (periya tirumoḻi, 1-7-9).]]
tīṉarait tiyakkuaṟutta tiruuru uṭaiyar (pōlum)
has a beautiful form which removes the bewilderment of those two poor persons who did not possess the wealth of knowledge.
āṉ narai ēṟṟar (pōlum)
has a white bull which belongs to the species of cow.

{$} āvaṭu tuṟaiyaṉār
see 1st verse.
pārttaṉukku aruḷvar (pōlum)
will grant his weapon pācupatam to pārttaṉ (aruccuraṉ)
paṭarcaṭaimuṭiyar (pōlum)
has a spreading caṭi coiled into a crown.
ēttuvār iṭarkaḷ tīra iṉpaṅkaḷ koṭuppar (pōlum)
will grant many kinds of happiness to remove the sufferings of those who prise him.
kūttarāyppāṭi āṭi
being well-versed in dance, singing and dancing.
koṭuvali arakkaṉ taṉṉai ārttavāy alaṟuvippār
will cuse the mouth of the arakkaṉ of cruel might roaring already, to cry from sorrow.

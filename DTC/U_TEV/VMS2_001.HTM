This decade of verses got its title of `viṉāvuri' as Campantar puts questions to god and eagerly awaits explanations in the form of replies.

{2:1}__1+
{$} cennel amkaḻaṉippaḻaṉattu ayalē ceḻum, puṉṉai veṇkiḻiyil pavaḷam purai pūntarāy-nal (1) imaiyōr muṭi tuṉṉit tōykaḻalīr
oh Lord with whose feet the crowns of the good tevar closely come into contact, in Pūntarāy, where by the sides of the tanks adjacent to the red paddy fields, the flowers of Puṉṉi (mast-wood tree) resemble coral lying on a white cloth!
(2) piṉṉu ceñcaṭaiyil piṟai pāmpuṭaṉ vaittatu colīr
please tell me the reason for having placed the crescent with the cobra in the red and entwining caṭi.
[[The 6th verse in tiru voṟṟiyūr orupā oru paḵtu in the 11th book gives the inner meaning for the actions of Civaṉ in lines 1-14]]
[[The decade on Kaṇtiyūr Vīrattam and Tiruvalañcuḻi are in the form of questions put to the devotees and the god respectively]]
[[Variant reading: (1) imaiyōr toḻu poṟkaḻalīr, (2) miṉṉu ceñcaṭai]]
{2:1}__2+
{$} eṟṟu teṇ tirai ēṟiya caṅkiṉoṭu ippikaḷ, poṉ tikaḻ kamalappaḻaṉam puku pūntarāyc, cuṟṟi nal imaiyōr toḻu poṟ kaḻalīr
oh God who has on your feet anklets made of gold which the tēvar worship surrounding you in pūntarāi where the conches and oysters which rose in the clear waves dashing aginst the shore, and enter into the tanks where lotus flowers shine like gold.
perumāṉirē
oh great one!
peṟṟam ēṟutal peṟṟimaiyō? colīr
is it greatness to ride on a bull? please tell me.
[[perumāṉirē is the nominative of address of perumāṉār; perumāṉīrē has been shortened into perumāṉirē.]]
{2:1}__3+
{$} caṅku cempavaḷattiraḷ muttavai tām koṭu vantu, poṅku teṇ tirai alaikkum puṉal pūntarāyt, tuṅkamāl kaḷiṟṟiṉ uripōrttu ukantīr
oh god who covered yourself with the skin of a great and rutting elephant and became great in pūntarāi where the clear rising waves toss hither and thither, bringing with them conches, cluster of red coral and pearls!
maṅkai paṅkamum aṅkat toṭu oṉṟiya māṇpu atu colīr
please tell me the dignity of having united with your body as a member the half of a young lady.
{2:1}__4+
{$} cēmavaṉmatil poṉ aṇi māḷikai cēṇ uyar, pūmaṇam kamaḻum poḻil cūḻtaru pūntarāyc, cōmaṉum aravum toṭar ceñcaṭaiyīr
oh god who has red caṭi on which the crescent and cobra are joined, and who resides in pūntarāi surrounded by parks spreading the fragrance of flowers, where the mansions adorned with gold and have strong walls of enclosure as protection rise into the sky.
kāmaṉ veṇpoṭiyākak kaṭaikkaṇ civantatu colīr
please tell me the reason for the outer corner of the eye to turn red to reduce Kāmaṉ to white ash.
{2:1}__5+
{$} paḷḷam mīṉ irai tērntu uḻalum pakuvāyaṉa, puḷḷu nāḷ toṟum cēr poḻil cūḻtaru pūntarāyt, tuḷḷumāṉ maṟi ēntiya ceṅkaiyiṉīr
oh God who holds in your red hand a leaping young deer and who resides in puntarāi surrounded by parks where the birds with split beaks wander in search of their food, fish, in the hollow pits, stay dily!
veḷḷanīr oru ceñcaṭai vaitta viyappu (atē) colīr
please tell me the wonder of placing on a single red caṭi the water of the flood (of Kaṅki) (water has the property of spreading everywhere and descending downwards; Lord Civaṉ controlled it and kept it on his caṭi, that is a wonder).
{2:1}__6+
{$} mātu ilaṅkiya maṅkaiyar āṭa maruṅku elām, pōtil am kamalam matuvār puṉal pūntarāyc, cōti am cuṭar mēṉi veṇṇīṟu aṇivīr
oh Lord who adorned your effulgent and lustruous body with white sacred ash who reside in pūtarāi which has abundant water into which the lotus about to blossom, pours its honey on all sides where the young girls of brilliant beauty dance!
kātil am kuḻai caṅka veṇ tōṭuṭaṉ vaittatu colīr
please tell me why you wore in your ears mens` ear-ring along with the women`s white ear-ring made of conch.

VMS8 / {2:1}__PIFI7+
{$} varukkam ārtaru vāṉ kaṭuvaṉṉoṭu mantikaḷ, tarukkoḷ cōlaitarum kaṉi māntiya pūntarāyt, turakkum mālviṭaimēl varuvīr aṭikēḷ
oh God who comes riding on a big bull which you drive, and who resides in Pūntarāy where female monkeys along with the groups of big male monkeys eat the fruits that are found in the trees in the gardens full of fruit-trees.
arakkaṉ āṟṟal aḻittu ākkiya ākkam colīr
please tell me the reason for destroying the prowess of the arakkaṉ and confering on him the gin of your grace.
VMS9 / {2:1}__PIFI8+
{$} varikoḷ ceṅkayal pāy puṉal cūḻnta maruṅku elām
on all sides which are surrounded by water in which the red carp fish with lines leap.
puricai nīṭu uyar māṭam nilāviya pūntarāyc, curuti pāṭiya pāṇ iyal tūmoḻiyīr
oh Lord who has pure and musical words and who sang the curuti in Pūntarāy where the tall storeys with high walls of enclosure are shining!
kari māl ayaṉ nēṭi (um) umaik kaṇṭilāmaiyē colīr
please tell me the reason for black Māl and Ayaṉ for not knowing you even though they searched for you.
VMS10 / {2:1}__PIFI9+
{$} vaṇṭal am kaḻaṉi(m) maṭai vāḷaikaḷ pāypuṉal, puṇṭarīkam malarntu matuttaru pūntarāyat, toṇṭar vantu pōṟṟi cey tol kaḻalīr
oh Lord who has very old feet which the devotees come and prise in Pūntarāy where the lotus buds which grow in water in which the scabbard fish leap in the small sluices of the fields which have slit!
kuṇṭar cākkiyar kūṟiya (tām) kuṟi iṉmaiyē colīr
please tell me why that the base people of camaṇar and buddhists talked had no substance in them (had no religious doctrines) (what they talked missed the mark)
VMS11 / {2:1}__PIFI10+
{$} makara vār kaṭal vantu aṇavum maṇaṟ (1) kāṉal vāyp, pukali ñāṉacampantaṉ eḻil miku pūntarāyp, pakavaṉāraip paravu col mālai pattum vallār
those who are capable of reciting the garland of ten verses composed on the Lord, adoring him in the second person, who resides in Pūntari, which has excellent beauty, by Ñāṉacampantaṉ of Pukali situated in the sandy sea-shore garden to which the long sea in which sharks live, comes near

nalviṉaiyōṭum uṭaṉ āvar

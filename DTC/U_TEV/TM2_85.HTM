*1

_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---

caṉi, pāmpu_iraṇṭum, _uṭaṉē
_ācu _aṟum; nallanalla; _avi nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*2
_eṉpoṭu kompoṭu _āmi _ivi mārpu _ilaṅka,
_erutu _ēṟi, _ēḻi_uṭaṉē,

_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
_oṉpatoṭu, _oṉṟoṭu, _ēḻu, patiṉeṭṭoṭu, _āṟum,
_uṭaṉ_āya nāḷkaḷ_avitām,
_aṉpoṭu nallanalla; _avi nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*3
_uru vaḷar pavaḷamēṉi _oḷi nīṟu _aṇintu,
_umiyōṭum, veḷḷiviṭimēl,
muruku _alar koṉṟi tiṅkaḷ muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---

_aru neti nallanalla; _avi nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*4

maṟi _ōtum _eṅkaḷ paramaṉ,
natiyoṭu koṉṟimāli muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---

_atikuṇam nallanalla; _avi nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*5
nañcu _aṇi kaṇṭaṉ, _enti, maṭavāḷtaṉōṭum
viṭi _ēṟum naṅkaḷ paramaṉ,
tuñcu _iruḷ---vaṉṉi, koṉṟi, muṭimēl _aṇintu---_eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---

miki_āṉa pūtam_aviyum,
_añciṭum; nalla nalla; _avi nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*6
vāḷvari_ataḷ_atu _āṭi vari kōvaṇattar
maṭavāḷtaṉōṭum _uṭaṉ_āy,

_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---

koṭu nākamōṭu, karaṭi,
_āḷ _ari, nallanalla; _avi nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*7

_oppu _iḷamatiyum _appum muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ

viṉi_āṉa, vantu naliyā;
_appaṭi nallanalla; _avi nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*8

_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---

_iṭar_āṉa vantu naliyā;

*9

_alikaṭal, mēru, nalla; _avi nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*10

_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---

_attaku nallanalla; _avi nallanalla,

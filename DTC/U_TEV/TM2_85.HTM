*1
vēy _uṟu tōḷi paṅkaṉ, viṭam _uṇṭa kaṇṭaṉ,
mika nalla vīṇai taṭavi,
mācu _aṟu tiṅkaḷ kaṅkai muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
ñāyiṟu, tiṅkaḷ, cevvāy, putaṉ, viyāḻam, veḷḷi,
caṉi, pāmpu_iraṇṭum, _uṭaṉē
_ācu _aṟum; nallanalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*2
_eṉpoṭu kompoṭu _āmai _ivai mārpu _ilaṅka,
_erutu _ēṟi, _ēḻai_uṭaṉē,
poṉ poti mattamālai puṉal cūṭi vantu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
_oṉpatoṭu, _oṉṟoṭu, _ēḻu, patiṉeṭṭoṭu, _āṟum,
_uṭaṉ_āya nāḷkaḷ_avaitām,
_aṉpoṭu nallanalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*3
_uru vaḷar pavaḷamēṉi _oḷi nīṟu _aṇintu,
_umaiyōṭum, veḷḷaiviṭaimēl,
muruku _alar koṉṟai tiṅkaḷ muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
tirumakaḷ, kalai_atu_ūrti, ceyamātu, pūmi,
ticaiteyvam_āṉapalavum,
_aru neti nallanalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*4
matinutal maṅkaiyōṭu, vaṭapāl _iruntu
maṟai _ōtum _eṅkaḷ paramaṉ,
natiyoṭu koṉṟaimālai muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
koti_uṟu kālaṉ, _aṅki, namaṉōṭu tūtar,
koṭunōykaḷ_āṉapalavum,
_atikuṇam nallanalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*5
nañcu _aṇi kaṇṭaṉ, _entai, maṭavāḷtaṉōṭum
viṭai _ēṟum naṅkaḷ paramaṉ,
tuñcu _iruḷ---vaṉṉi, koṉṟai, muṭimēl _aṇintu---_eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
veñciṉa _avuṇarōṭum, _urum-_iṭiyum, miṉṉum,
mikai_āṉa pūtam_avaiyum,
_añciṭum; nalla nalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*6
vāḷvari_ataḷ_atu _āṭai vari kōvaṇattar
maṭavāḷtaṉōṭum _uṭaṉ_āy,
nāḷmalar vaṉṉi koṉṟai nati cūṭi vantu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
kōḷ _ari, _uḻuvaiyōṭu, kolai yāṉai, kēḻal,
koṭu nākamōṭu, karaṭi,
_āḷ _ari, nallanalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*7
ceppu_iḷamulai nal maṅkai _orupākam_āka
viṭai _ēṟu celvaṉ, _aṭaivu _ār
_oppu _iḷamatiyum _appum muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
veppoṭu, kuḷirum, vātam, mikai_āṉa pittum,
viṉai_āṉa, vantu naliyā;
_appaṭi nallanalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*8
vēḷ paṭa viḻiceytu, _aṉṟu, viṭaimēl _iruntu,
maṭavāḷtaṉōṭum _uṭaṉ_āy,
vāḷmati vaṉṉi koṉṟaimalar cūṭi vantu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
_ēḻkaṭal cūḻ _ilaṅkai _araiyaṉtaṉōṭum
_iṭar_āṉa vantu naliyā;
_āḻ kaṭal nallanalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*9
palapalavēṭam _ākum paraṉ, nāripākaṉ,
pacu _ēṟum _eṅkaḷ paramaṉ,
calamakaḷōṭu _erukku muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
malarmicaiyōṉum mālum maṟaiyōṭu tēvar
varu kālam_āṉapalavum,
_alaikaṭal, mēru, nalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*10
kottu _alar kuḻaliyōṭu vicayaṟku nalku
kuṇam_āya vēṭa vikirtaṉ,
mattamum matiyum nākam muṭimēl _aṇintu, _eṉ
_uḷamē pukunta_ataṉāl---
puttaroṭu _amaṇai vātil _aḻivikkum _aṇṇal
tirunīṟu cemmai tiṭamē;
_attaku nallanalla; _avai nallanalla,
_aṭiyār_avarkku mikavē.

*11

_āṉa colmālai _ōtum _aṭiyārkaḷ, vāṉil

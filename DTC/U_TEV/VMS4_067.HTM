{4:67}__1+
{$} pulaṉkaḷ aintum varaikilēṉ
I have not restrined the desires of the five senses.
varaikilāp piṟavi māyap puraiyilē aṭaṅki niṉṟu
being cught in the hollow of deceptive births which are limitless.
vaḻiyum kāṇēṉ
I do not see any way of escape from that.
araiyilē miḷirum nākattu aṇṇalē
the god, Civaṉ who has tied a shining cobra in the waist!
tiraiulām paḻaṉavēlittirukkoṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
god who is tirukkoṇtīccaram which has a boundary of tanks in which waves are moving hither and thither
añcal' eṉṉāy
comfort me by saying, Do not be afrid!
{4:67}__2+
{$} aṇṭaṉē
the god!
aṇṭavāṇā
who dwells inside the worlds and outside them!
aṟivaṉē
who has the form of pure knowledge!
[[one who has the capacity to know all things is another meaning]]
teṇtiraip paḻaṉam cūḻnta tirukkoṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
god who dwells in Tiruk Koṇṭīccuram surrounded by tanks having clear waves!
toṇṭaṉēṉ
I, your slave,
piṟantu
having born in this world
vāḷā
without any good to me
tolviṉaik kuḻiyil vīḻntu
and having fallen into the pit of karmams that follow me from time immemorial.
piṇṭamē cumantu
and having carried the body which is a mass of flesh.
naintu
having become worn out
pōvatu ōr vaḻiyum kāṇēṉ
I do not know any way of escape from this.
añcal eṉṉāy
[[see 1st verse]]
{4:67}__3+
{$} cēluṭaippaḻaṉam cūḻnta tirukkoṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
Civaṉ who is in Tiruk Koṇṭīccaram surrounded by tanks having cēl fish!
kāl koṭuttu
supported by two legs.
elumpu mūṭṭi
fixing the bones.
katir narampu ākkai ārttu
binding the body with the ropes of shining nerves.
tōl uṭuttu
covering it with a skin.
utiram aṭṭi
having poured blood into it.
toku mayir mēynta kūrai ōleṭuttu uḻaiñarkūṭi oḷippataṟku añcukiṉṟēṉ
I am afrid of people around me, crying and burying the hut which has been covered with plenty of hir as a thatched roof when I am dead.
[[therefore grant me that way in which there will no recurrence of this sad state]]
{4:67}__4+
{$} āṭu aravu acaitta kōvē
the King who tied a dancing cobra in the waist!
kāṭṭiṭai araṅkamāka āṭiya kaṭavuḷēyō
oh god who danced in the cremation-ground using it as a stage!
[[ō is used in lamentation]]
cēṭu irum paḻaṉa vēlittirukkoṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
Civaṉ in Tiruk Koṇṭīccaram which is surrounded on all sides by big tanks of excellence!
(1)kūṭṭamāy aivarvantu koṭun toḻiṟ kuṇattarāki āṭṭuvārkku āṟṟakillēṉ
I have no strength to stand aginst the five senses which have the nature of cruel acts and come in a group to me and toss me in many ways.
[[Variant reading: (1) kūṭṭamām]]
{4:67}__5+
{$} cekkarētikaḻum mēṉittirukkeṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
Civaṉ in Tirukkoṇṭīccaram who has a body shining like the evening sky!
pokkamāy niṉṟa pollāp puḻumiṭai muṭaikoḷ ākkai tokku niṉṟu
and staying combing in the body with bad odour which is full of worms and which is unreal.
aivar toṇṇūṟṟu aṟuvarum tuyakkam eyta mikkuniṉṟu ivarkaḷ ceyyum vētaṉaikku alantu pōṉēṉ
I suffered much due to the pin inflicted on me in an increasing measure by these, the five senses and the tattuvam thirty-six in number and those connected with those tattuvams sixty in number.
[[aivar can be linked with eyta; in the first meaning eyta is the act of Nāvukkaracar; in the second meaning it is the act of the ninety-six; aivar toṇṇūṟṟu aṟuvarum tuyakkam eytac ceyyum vētaṉai is the syntactical link]]
{4:67}__6+
{$} tēṉ ulām poḻilkaḷ cūḻnta tirukkoṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
Civaṉ in Tirukkoṇṭīccaram surrounded by gardens in which there is honey!
ūrulām muṭaikoḷ ākkai uṭaikalam āvatu eṉṉum
thinking of the body with stench, major portion of which is flesh, as a ship that has the risk of being wrecked in the middle of the ocean.
māṉulām maḻaikkaṇṇārtam vāḻkkaiyai mey eṉṟu (um) eṇṇi
and thinking as real the life led with ladies with cool eyes and looks like the deer.
nāṉ iṇaiya kālam ellām naṇṇilēṉ
I did not approach you all these years.
eṇṇam illēṉ
I had no idea about the purpose of my life.
{4:67}__7+
{$} cēṇ uyar māṭam nīṭu tirukkoṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
Civaṉ in Tirukkoṇtīccaram where tall storeys of the temple rise high into the sky!
cāṇ iru (1) maṭaṅku nīṇṭa caḻakkuṭaippatikku nātar
the master of the body, the abode of wickedness, which is eight spans in length.
vāṇikar aivar toṇṇūṟṟu aṟuvarum mayakkam ceytu
the five senses and the tattuvam thirty-six and tāttuvikan sixty in number which are like merchants
pēṇiya (2) patiyiṉiṉṟu peyarumpōtu aṟiyamāṭṭēṉ
I am incapable of knowing the exact time when they will leave their abode in which they dwelt with desire.
[[at that time I could not remember you. (As the merchants amass money, the ninety six also pile up evil acts, pretending to do good)]]
[[Variant reading:(1) maruṅkum (2) patiyiṉiṉṟum]]
{4:67}__8+
{$} poymmaṟittu iyaṟṟivaittup pulāl kamaḻpaṇṭampeytu
placing things that give an offensive odour of flesh and having committed all falsehood and detined them without going out.
paimmaṟittu iyaṟṟiyaṉṉa pāṅku ilāk kurampai niṉṟu
leaving the body which has no good and looks ugly if inside is turned outside like a bag. (if the seamy is made visible)
kaim maṟittaṉaiya āvi kaḻiyum pōtu aṟiyamāṭṭēṉ
I could not know you when the life which is like a thing which is disapproved with waving of the open hand.
(1) cenneṟic celavu kāṇēṉ
I do not see treading the right path.
tirukkoṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
Civaṉ in Tirukkoṇṭīccaram
[[Variant reading: (1) ceṉṉeṟiccelavu]]
{4:67}__9+
{$} pālaṉāykkaḻinta nāḷum
in the days that have elapsed as a youth.
paṉimalarkkōtaimārtam mēlaṉāykkaḻinta nāḷum
in the days that elapsed living with ladies who adorn themselves with cool flowers
melivoṭu mūppu vantu kōlaṉāyk kaḻinta nāḷum
and in the days when old age sets in with weakness, to walk with a stick.
kuṟikkōḷ ilātu keṭṭēṉ
I ruined myself without any aim to reach you.
cēl ulām paḻaṉavēlit tirukkoṇṭīccarattu uḷāṉē
Civaṉ in Tirukkoṇṭīccaram which is surrounded by tanks in which cēl roam about.
{4:67}__10+
{$} viraitarukarumeṉ kūntal viḷaṅkiḻaivēl oṇkaṇṇāḷ veruvara
the lady with bright eyes like the vēl, who wears shining jewels and has fragrant, black and soft tresses of hir, to be frightened.
ilaṅkaik kōmāṉ vilaṅkalai eṭutta ñāṉṟu
on that day when the King of Ilaṅki lifted the mountin.
paruvarai aṉaiya tōḷum muṭikaḷum pāṟi vīḻat tiruviral ūṉṟiṉāṉē
god who fixed firmly the holy toe to make the shoulders which were like huge mountins and heads, to fall down scattered!
tirukkoṇṭīccattu uḷāṉē
Civan in Tirukkoṇṭīccaram.

*3

_emtamperumāṉ, _imaiyōr _ētta, _umaiyōṭu _iruntāṉē.

_eṉai _āḷ_uṭaiyāṉ, _imaiyōr _ētta, _umaiyōṭu _iruntāṉē.

*5

*6

_eṉṉai _uṭaiyāṉ, _imaiyōr _ētta, _umaiyōṭu _iruntāṉē.

*7

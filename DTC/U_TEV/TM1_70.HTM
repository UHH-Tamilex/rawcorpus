*1
vāṉattu _uyar taṇmati tōy caṭimēl mattamalar cūṭi,
tēṉ _ottaṉa meṉmoḻi māṉviḻiyāḷ tēvi pākamā,
kāṉattu _iravil _eri koṇṭu _āṭum kaṭavuḷ---_ulaku _ētta,
_ēṉattiraḷ vantu _iḻiyum cāral _īṅkōymaliyārē.

*2
cūlappaṭi _oṉṟu _ēnti, _iravil cuṭukāṭu _iṭam_āka,
kōlac caṭikaḷ tāḻa, kuḻal, yāḻ, monti koṭṭavē,
pāl _ottaṉiya moḻiyāḷ kāṇa, _āṭum paramaṉār---
_ēlattoṭu nal _ilavam kamaḻum _īṅkōymaliyārē.

*3
kaṇ koḷ nutalār, kaṟi koḷ miṭaṟṟār, kariyiṉ_uri-tōlār,
viṇ koḷ mati cēr caṭiyār, viṭi_ār koṭiyār, veṇnīṟu
peṇ koḷ tirumārpu_ataṉil pūcum pemmāṉ, _emi _āḷvār---
_eṇkum _ariyum tiriyum cāral _īṅkōymaliyārē.

*4
maṟiyiṉ(ṉ) _iciyār, neṟimeṉkūntal maliyāṉmakaḷōṭum,
kuṟiveṇpiṟiyum puṉalum nilavum kuḷirpuṉcaṭi tāḻa,
paṟiyum kuḻalum kaḻalum _ārppa, paṭukāṭṭu _eri_āṭum
_iṟivar---ciṟivaṇṭu _aṟi pūñcāral _īṅkōymaliyārē.

*5
nonta cuṭalip poṭi-nīṟu _aṇivār, nutal cēr kaṇṇiṉār,
kantamalarkaḷpalavum nilavu kamaḻ puṉcaṭi tāḻa,
pantu _aṇ viralāḷ pākam_āka, paṭukāṭṭu _eri_āṭum
_emtam(m) _aṭikaḷ---kaṭi koḷ cāral _īṅkōymaliyārē.

*6
nīṟu _ār _akalam _uṭiyār, niri _ār koṉṟi _aravōṭum
_āṟu _ār caṭiyār, _ayilveṅkaṇiyāl _avuṇar purammūṉṟum
cīṟā _ericey tēvarperumāṉ, ceṅkaṇ _aṭal veḷḷi-
_ēṟu _ār koṭiyār-_umiyāḷōṭum _īṅkōymaliyārē.

*7
viṉi_āyiṉa tīrttu _aruḷē puriyum vikirtaṉ, virikoṉṟi
naṉi _ār muṭimēl matiyam cūṭum nampāṉ, nalam malku

_eṉi _āḷ_uṭiyāṉ---_umiyāḷōṭum _īṅkōymaliyārē.

*8
parakkum perumi _ilaṅki _eṉṉum patiyil polivu_āya
_arakkarkku _iṟivaṉ muṭiyum tōḷum _aṇi _ār viraltaṉṉāl

_irakkam purintār---_umiyāḷōṭum _īṅkōymaliyārē.

*9
vari _ār puliyiṉ_uri-tōl _uṭiyāṉ, maliyāṉmakaḷōṭum
piriyātu _uṭaṉ_āy _āṭal pēṇum pemmāṉ, tirumēṉi
_ariyōṭu _ayaṉum _aṟiyā vaṇṇam _aḷavu _il perumiyōṭu,
_eri_āy nimirnta _eṅkaḷ perumāṉ---_īṅkōymaliyārē.

*10
piṇṭi _ēṟṟu peyarāniṟkum piṇaṅku camaṇarum,

_uṇṭi vayiṟār _urikaḷ koḷḷātu, _umiyōṭu _uṭaṉ_āki,
_iṇṭic caṭiyāṉ, _imiyōrperumāṉ---_īṅkōymaliyārē.

*11
viḻavu _ār _oliyum muḻavum _ōvā vēṇupuramtaṉṉuḷ,
_aḻal _ār vaṇṇattu _aṭikaḷ _aruḷ cēr _aṇi koḷ campantaṉ,
_eḻil _ār cuṉiyum poḻilum puṭi cūḻ _īṅkōymali _īcaṉ

māliṉai māl uṟa niṉṟāṉ
Civaṉ stood with a form for begging alms to make Māl to be confounded
malaimakaḷ taṉṉuṭaiya pālaṉai
who has on his left half theghter of the mountin
pāl mati cūṭiyai
who has worn the crescent as white as milk
paṇpu uṇarār matil mēl pōlaṉai
who waged war on the forts of the acurar who could not understand the eminence of Civaṉ (pōlaṉ-pōraṉ-la is interchanged for to suit rhyme)
pōrviṭai ēṟiyai
who rides on a fighting bull
pūnturutti(m) makiḻum ālaṉai ātipurāṇaṉai nām aṭi pōṟṟuvatē
we prise the feet of the ancient primordial Civaṉ who is seated under the banyan tree and dwells happily in pūnturutti (m is augmentation)

maṟi uṭaiyāṉ maḻuvāḷiṉaṉ
Civaṉ has a young deer and a battle-axe
māmalai maṅkai ōrpāl kuṟi uṭaiyāṉ
has the mark of the dughter of the mountin on one half
kuṇam oṉṟu aṟintār illai
there is none who has understood even a little of his properties.
kūṟil
if we define him
poṟi uṭaivāḷ aravattavaṉ
has a cobra which is capable of killing and has spots in the hood.
pūnturutti(y) uṟaiyum aṟivuuṭai ātipurāṇaṉai nām aṭi pōṟṟuvatē
we prise the feet of the ancient primordial Civaṉ who is pure knowledge and who dwells in pūnturutti ((y) augmentation)

maṟuttavar mummatil māya ōr veñcilai kōttu ōr ampāl aṟuttaṉai
Civaṉ who cut down fixing an arrow in the cruel bow to destroy the three forts of the enemies
ālataṉ kīḻaṉai
who is seated under the banyan tree
ālviṭam uṇṭu ataṉaip poṟuttaṉai
who cosumed the special poison of ālakālam and kept it in his neck;
[[āl alāl amutam illai aiyaṉ aiyāṟaṉārkkē (appar, tiruvayyāṟu, tirunērici (3) 2)]]
pūtappaṭaiyaṉai
who has an army of pūtams
pūnturutti (y) uṟaiyum niṟattaṉai
who has the idea of dwelling in pūtuṟutti (niṟam thought, idea)
nīla miṭaṟṟaṇai yāṉ aṭi pōṟṟuvatē
I prise the feet of Civaṉ who has a blue-neck.

uruviṉai
Civaṉ who is the form of pure knowledge
ūḻi mutal vaṉai
who is the cuse of all aeons
ūḻi niṟaintu niṉṟa tiruviṉai
who is the wealth that stands permanently pervading everywhere, chanting the vētams (ōti niṟaintu niṉṟatiru may also mean the fulness of wisdom; ōti - is a noun in this sense)
tēcam paṭaittaṉāṉai
who created all the countries
[[tēcam-tēcu+am; then the meaning will be one who created lustre in himself;
tēcam uṭaiyāy (tiruppāvi,9)]]
ceṉṟu aṭaintēṉuṭaiya poruviṉai ellām turantaṉai
who drove away all the karmams which were engaged in battle with me who appporached him, going to him
pūnturutti(y) uṟaiyum karuviṉaikkaṇ mūṉṟuṭaiyaṉai yāṉ aṭi pōṟṟuvatē
I worship the feet of him who has three eyes, the embryo of all things and who dwells in pūntuṟutti

takkaṉ taṉ vēḷvi takarttavaṉ
Civaṉ destroyed the sacrifice performed by takkaṉ
cāram atu aṉṟu
that is not of importance
1. kōḷ mikkaṉa mummatil vīya ōr veñcilai kōttu ōr ampāl pukkaṉaṉ
who went aginst the three forts which were excessively strong, in order to destroy them, by an arrow which was fixed in a cruel bow.

aruku aṭai mālaiyum tāṉ uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has Māl also who approached near him.
aḻakāl amainta uruuṭai maṅkaiyum taṉ oru pāl āyum ulaku āyum niṉṟāṉ
he is the world and has on one half a lady also who has a beautiful body

villiṉaṉ

I prise the feet of Civaṉ who bears on his head a beautiful and bright crescent and who dwells in pūnturutti.

niṉṟa matilarai maṉṟiyum
punishing the acurar in the forts who stood without fear
māya vakai keṭukkakkaṉṟiyum
and being angry with them to destroy the different kinds of their deceitful acts;
[[maṉṟi-taṇṭittu; punishing;
aṭikeṭamaṉṟiviṭal (paḻamoḻi, 103)]]
kaṭuñcilai vāṅkik kaṉalam piṉāl poṉṟiyum pōkappuraṭṭiṉaṉ
made them roll down, to be completely destroyed by an arrow spitting fire, by bending a cruel bow
pūnturutti(y) uṟaiyum aṉṟiyum ceyta pirāṉtaṉai yāṉ aṭi pōṟṟuvatē
I prise the feet of the master who is all things good and bad, and who dwells in pūnturutti

miṉ niṟam mikka iṭai umai naṅkai ōrpāl makiḻntāṉ
Civaṉ rejoiced in having on one half Umi, a lady of distinction, who has a waist as flexible as the bright lightning
eṉniṟam eṉṟu amarar periyār iṉṉamtām aṟiyār
the immortals and the great men among human beings still do not know what kind of colour, Civaṉ has.
[[PP: appaṭiyum anniṟamum avvaṇṇamum avaṉ aruḷē kaṇṇākak kāṇiṉ allāl, ippaṭiyaṉ inniṟattaṉ ivvaṇṇattaṉ ivaṉ iṟaivaṉ eṉṟu eḻutikāṭ ṭoṇātē
(appar, viṉāviṭit tiruttāṇtakam, 10)]]
poṉniṟam mikka caṭaiyavaṉ
has caṭi whose colour is superior to gold.
pūnturutti(y) uṟaiyum elniṟa entai pirāṉtaṉai yāṉ aṭi pōṟṟuvatē
I prise the feet of our father and master who has the colour of the sun and who dwells in pūntūrutti

antiyai
Civaṉ who is the evening
nalla matiyiṉai
who is the moon that shines in the evening
yārkkum aṟivu ariya centiyai vāṭṭum cempoṉṉiṉai
the superior gold that cuse the red fire to fade and who is unable to be known by anyone however great he may be
ceṉṟu aṭaintēṉuṭaiya puntiyaip pukka aṟiviṉai
the wisdom that entered into my mind when I went and reached him.
punturutti(y) uṟaiyum nantiyai naṅkaḷ pirāṉ taṉai nāṉ aṭi pōṟṟuvatē
I prise the feet of our master who is known by the name of Nanti and who dwells in Pūnturutti

maṟai (atu) pāṭip piccaikku eṉṟu akamtirintu vāḻvār
Civaṉ leads a life of wandering from house to house to receive alms, singing Vētam-s.
piṟai (atu) caṭaimuṭimēl peyvaḷaiyāḷ taṉōṭum kaṟai (atu) kaṇṭam koṇṭār
had poison in the neck, and on the caṭi coiled into a crown wearing a crescent, a lady Kaṅki who wears bangles on her hands.
kāñcimā (1) nakartaṉṉuḷ (āl)
within the big city of Kāñci.
iṟaiyavar
the Lord who pervades everywhere.
pāṭal āṭal ilaṅku mēṟṟaḷiyaṉār (ē)
is in Mēṟṟaḷi where music and dance are prominent.
[[Variant reading: (1) nakartaṉṉuḷḷār;
this reading should be following throughout this decade]]

viṇṇiṭai viṇṇavarkaḷ virumpivantu iṟaiñci vāḻtta
when the celestials who are in heaven descend down to this earth with desire and sing songs of benedication bowing their heads.
paṇṇiṭaic cuvaiyiṉmikka kiṉṉaram pāṭal 1.kēṭpār
will listen to the songs of a kind of lute, kiṉṉaram, which is superior to the sweetness of music to other instruments.
kiṉṉaram
is a king of lute;
[[kaṉṉara mituṉaṅkaḷum tam tam kiṉṉaram toṭukilēm eṉṟaṉarē (periyāḻvar tirumoḻi, 3-6-5)]]
kaṇṇiṭai maṇiyiṉ nappār
is like the apple of the eye.
[[kaṇṇiṭai maṇi nappāy (cuntarar, Kurukāvur, veḷḷati, 6)]]

māyaṉāy, mālaṉāki, malaravaṉāki, malaravaṉāki
Civaṉ, being the black Māl, Intiraṉ and Piramaṉ who is in a (lotus) flower
maṇṇāyt, tēyam āyt, ticai eṭṭuākit, tīrttam āy
being the eaarth, the country the eight directions and holy waters.

cēntaṉai makaṉāk koṇṭār

elliyai viḷaṅka niṉṟār
remined inside the sun to make it shine.

iṇai vēṟu iṉṟi eṉṟum viḷaṅku oḷi maruṅkiṉāḷaik (1) kūṟu iyal pākam vaittār
civan placed a lady Umi, who had a waist of brightness always and who has no other thing to be compared to her, to be associated with him.
kōḷ arā matiyam vaittār
placed on his head a cobra capable of killing, and a crescent.
aṇi poḻil kacci taṉṉuḷ ilaṅku mēṟṟiḷiyaṉār (ē) ēṟiṉai ēṟum entai
our father who rides on a bull and who is in shining mēṟṟali within kacci which is made beautiful by gardens.
āṟiṉaic caṭaiyuḷ vaittār
placed the river, kaṅki, inside his caṭi.

{7:75}__1+
{$} maṟaikaḷ āyiṉa nāṉkum
Civaṉ who is the four Vētam-s,
maṟṟuḷa poruḷkaḷum ellām
who is all other objects,
tuṟaiyum tōttirattu iṟaiyum
who is the god which is prised in religions,
toṉmaiyum naṉmaiyum āya (ātiyai)
the āti who is the oldest thing beyond beginning and end, and the supreme goodness and bliss,
aṟaiyum pūmpuṉal āṉaikkā uṭai ātiyai
the supreme being who has āṉikka of beautiful and dashing water.
nāḷum iṟaivaṉ eṉṟu aṭicērvār emmaiyum āḷ uṭaiyār
those who approach the feet of the god dily thinking as my Lord not only derive benefit but also clim us as their servants.
{7:75}__2+
{$} vaṅkam mēviya vēlai nañcu eḻa vañcakarkaḷ kūṭi
when the poison rose in the ocean (of milk) in which ships are moving, the cunning tevar joined together.
taṅkaḷmēl aṭarāmai uṇ eṉa uṇṭu iruḷ kaṇṭaṉ
one who has a black neck having consumed the poison, requesting him Drink it so that it might not attack them.
aṅkam ōtiya āṉaikkā uṭaiya ātiyai nāḷum, eṅkaḷ īcaṉ eṉpārkaḷ emmaiyum āḷ uṭaiyār
those who clim the supreme being as their god who is the author of the six aṅkams and has āṉikkā, dily, not only derive benefit but also clim us as their servants.
{7:75}__3+
{$} nīlavaṇṭu aṟaikoṉṟai nēriḻaimaṅkai, ōrtiṅkaḷ, cālavāḷaravaṅkaḷ taṅkiya ceñcaṭai entai
our father on whose caṭi koṉṟi flowers on which blue bees hum, a lady (Kanki) who wears suitable ornaments, a crescent, and many cruel cobras stay.
ālanīḻaluḷ
and who is under the shade of the banyan tree.
āṉaikkā uṭai ātiyai nāḷum ēlumāṟu vallavārkaḷ
those who are able to perform such acts of welcoming the supreme being who has āṉikkā as his place, dily
[[ēlumāṟu act of meditation.]]
emmaiyum āḷuṭaiyār
not only derive benefit but also can clim us as their servants.
{7:75}__4+
{$} ulakukkuttantaitāy
the father and mother of all the worlds
ōrtattuvaṉ
the unequalled ultimate reality.
meyttavattōrkkup pantam āyiṉa perumāṉ
the god who is the relation to those who have performed sincere penance (sincere penance is not thinking of Civaṉ as one among the gods but the one superior to all)
paricuṭaiyavar tiruvaṭikaḷ
holy deity of the pious people
[[paricu here means piety.]]
amtaṇ pūmpuṉal āṉaikkā uṭai ātiyai nāḷum entai eṉṟu aṭicērvār emmaiyum āḷ uṭaiyār
those who approach the feet of Civaṉ climing him as our father, dily, the supreme being in Āṉikkā which has cool, beautiful water with flowers, not only derive benefit but also can clim us as their servants.
{7:75}__5+
{$} ēyulakuuyya
in order that the fit living being in the worlds might be saved.
kaṇaicentī aravam nāṇ kalvaḷaiyum cilaiyākattuṇai ceyam matil mūṉṟum cuṭṭavaṉ
one who burnt all the three forts which were helpful to one another, having the stone (mountin) as the bending bow, the serpent as the bowstring and red fire as the arrow.
aṇaiyum pūmpuṉal āṉaikkā uṭai ātiyai nāḷum iṇaikoḷ cēvaṭicērvār emmaiyum āḷuṭaiyār
those who approach the twin red feet of the first supreme being which has āṉikkā which is reached by water having flower, dily, not only derive benefit but also can clim us as their servants.
{7:75}__6+
{$} viṇṇiṉ māmaticūṭi
Having worn the eminent crescent shining in the sky.
vilai ilikalaṉ aṇivimalaṉ
the spotless Civaṉ who adorns himself with ornaments that will not fetch a price
paṇṇiṉ nērmoḻi maṅkai paṅkiṉaṉ
who has a half on which a lady of distinction whose words are like sweer melodies.
pacuukantu ēṟi
riding on a bull wishing it
aṇṇal ākiya āṉaikkā uṭai ākiyai nāḷum eṇṇumāṟu vallārkaḷ
those who are capable of meditating upon dily, on the first supreme being who has āṉikkā which is eminent among shrines.
emmaiyum āḷuṭaiyār
not only derive benefit by that act but also can clim us as their servants.
{7:75}__7+
{$} tāram ākiya poṉṉit taṇṭuṟai āṭiviḻuntum nīril niṉṟu aṭipōṟṟi niṉmalā! koḷ eṉa āṅkē āram koṇṭaem āṉaikkāuṭai ātiyai
the first supreme being in āṉikkā who a received the garland of pearls which a Cōḻan who cused to fall it when bathing in the cool ghat of the Poṉṉi which is considered to be the consort of Cōḻan, when he prised god's feet and requested him spotless God! please accept it at that place itself
[[The story is as follows; a certin cōḻaṉ, while bathing in the poṉṉi lost his garland of pearls in the river. Becuse of the loss he stood in the water and prised Civaṉ to receive it. when the temple priest came to that spot to fetch water for bathing it went into it and when the deity was bathed it was there to the joy of the cōḻaṉ who had lost it.]]
[[tāramākiya poṉṉi:
tāram: consort; it is a literary convention to speak of rivers flowing through their countries as the consorts of those kings]]
[[PP: tiṅkaḷ mālai veṇkuṭaiyāṉ ceṉṉi ceṅkōl atu ōccik, kaṉṉi taṉṉip puṇarntālum pulavāyvāḻi kāvēri;
maṉṉu mālai veṇkuṭaiyāṉ vaḷaiyāc ceṅkōlatu ōccikkaṉṉi taṉṉaip puṇarntālum pulavāyvāḻi kāvēri (cilappattikāram Kāṉalvari, 2-3);
vaiyai eṉṟa poyyākkulakkoṭi (Do. 13-170);
vaiyai eṉṟu peyar kūṟappaṭuvāḷ; paruvam poyyāmaiyāṉum vēṟṟumaivēntar aṇukāmaiyāṉum teṉṉar kulakkoṭiyāyuḷḷavaṉ (Do. aṭiyārkkunallār);
kōṭāc ceṅkōtcōḻartam kulakkoṭi (maṇimekali, patikam, 23)]]
{7:75}__8+
{$} uravam uḷḷatu ōr uḻaiyiṉ uri, puli ataḷ uṭaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who dresses himself with the skin of a strong spotted deer and the star of a tiger
viraikoḷkoṉṟaiyiṉāṉai
who has fragrant koṉṟi flowers
viricaṭaimēṟpiṟaiyāṉai
who has a crescent on his spreaded cati.
aravam vīkkiya āṇaikkāvuṭai ātiyai nāḷum (1) iravoṭu elliyum ēttuvār emmaiyum āḷ uṭaiyār
those who prise dily both in the night and day the first supreme being in āṉikkā who has tied a cobra, not only derive benefit but also can clim us as their servants.
[[uravam: the va is an augmentation.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) iravoṭelliyum pakalum]]
{7:75}__9+
{$} valaṅkoḷvār avar taṅkaḷ valviṉai tīrkkum maruntu
the cure for the irresistible karmams of those who go from left to right round him
kalaṅkak kālaṉaik kālāl kāmaṉaikkaṇ civappāṉai
who with anger kicked Kālaṉ with his foot to make him confused in mind, and burnt Kāmaṉ.
alaṅkaḷ nīrporum āṉaikkāvuṭai ātiyai nāḷum ilaṅku cēvaṭicērvār emmaiyum āḷuṭaiyār
those who dily bow to the red and eminent feet of the first supreme being in āṉikkā where the moving water dashes aginst the banks, not only derive benefit but can also clim us as their servants.
{7:75}__10+
{$} āḻiyāṟku aruḷceyyum āṉaikkāvuṭai āti poṉ aṭiyiṉ nīḻalē caraṇ āka niṉṟu aruḷ kūra niṉaintu vāḻa vallavaṉ
one who is capable of leading a life meditating to receive god's grace in an ample measure standing in the golden feet of the first supreme being who granted his grace to Māl who possesses a discus, as his refuge.
vaṉtoṇṭaṉ
who has the appellation of vaṉṟoṅtān
vaṇtamiḻ mālaivallār pōy ēḻumāpiṟappu aṟṟu emmaiyum āḷ uṭaiyār
those who are able to recite the truthful garland of tamiḻ verses will be free from the seven kinds of births which are innumerable, will ascend into heaven, and not only derive benefit but can also clim us as their servants.

{4:96}__1+
{$} muḻaṅkum taḻaṟkait tēvā
the god holding in his hand a fire that is making a loud noise!
tiruccatti muṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
civakkoḻuntu dwelling in tiruccatti muṟṟam!
aṭutiṟal kūṟṟam kōvāy muṭukik kumaippataṉmuṉ
before the god of death which has the strength to kill and having eminence, hastens to take away my life.
pū ār aṭiccuvaṭu eṉmēl poṟittu vai
stamp your footprints which are like the lotus flowers.
pōkāviṭil
if you send me without doing so.
mūvā muḻuppaḻimūṭum (kaṇṭāy)
reproach which will never die, will envelope you.
[[Cattimuṟṟam - the temple of catti]]
[[muṟṟam is a word used to devote vishnu temples; aṇi araṅkaṉ tirumuṟṟattu aṭiyār taṅkaḷ iṉpammiku peruṅkuḻuvu (perumal tirumoḻi, 1-10);
As civites consider Vishnu as one of the consorts of Civaṉ, this shrine might have got the name catti muṟṟam]]
[[Civakkoḻuntu is the name of Civaṉ in this shrine;
kāvātu oḻiyiṟkalakkum uṉ mēṟpaḻi (appar, tiruttūṅkāṉi mātam, 2).]]
[[The request of Appar was fulfilled in Nallūr about which he has composed a tāṇṭakam referring to this act of Civaṉ]]
{4:96}__2+
{$} tiruccattimuṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
[[see 1st verse]]
aṉaṅkaṉ uṭalam poṭipaṭak kāyntāy
you fixed your gaze to reduce to ash the body of aṉaṅkaṉ (aṉaṅkaṉ is not used in its derivate meaning, it simply means maṉmataṉ; only after he was burnt by the frontal eye of Civaṉ he got the name, aṉankaṉ.)
uyircekak kālaṉaimuṉ pāyntāy
you pounced upon Kālaṉ to kill him, in the distant past.
pātam paṇivār palpiṟavi āyntu āyntu aṟuttāy
you destroyed the many births of those who bow before your feet after much consideration.
uṉ aṉparcintai cērntāy
one who dwelt in the minds of your devotees and never left them!
aṭiyēṟku aruḷāy
grant your grace to your slave.
{4:96}__3+
{$} tiruccatti muṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
see 1st verse.
pottu ār kurampai pukuntu aivar pukal aḻippa
the five senses entering into the body which can be compared to a hut, and which has nine apartuna, destroy the hold of your feet which is my refuge. (aintu is spoken of as aivar out of angers).
mattuār tayirpōl maṟukum, eṉ cintai
my mind is unsteady like the curd which is churned by the churning staff.
[[This simile is much used in Tamiḻ literature;]]
[[PP: mattu uṟu tayirē pōl maṟukum eṉ uḷḷam tāṉum
(appar, tiruvārūr, tiranērici, 9)]]
[[PP: mattu uṟu taṇtayiriṉ pulaṉ cīkkatuvakkalaṅki
(tiruvācakam, nīttal viṇṇappam, 30);]]
[[PP: valmattu iṭauṭaintu tāḻiyaip pāvu tayirpōl taḷarntēṉ
(Do atikkalappattu, 6);]]
[[PP_X: āyarmattu eṟi tayiriṉ āyiṉār
(Cīvakacintāmaṇi, 422);]]
[[PP_X: tayir uṭaikkum mattu eṉṉa ulakai nalicamparaṉ
(Kamparāmāyaṇam, āraṇiya Kāṇtam, cātāyu Kāṇ paṭalam, 23).]]
maṟukku oḻili
cuse the unsteadiness to leave me.
attā
our father.
aṭiyēṉ aṭaikkalam kaṇṭāy
I, your slave, seek refuge in you, please protect me.
[[kaṇṭāy- expletive.]]
amararkaḷ tamcittā
one who stays in the minds of the immortals.

{$} tiruccatti muṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
see 1st verse.
vil ēr puruvattu umaiyāḷ kaṇavā
the husband of Umiyāl whose eye-brows are curved like the bow.
celvā
one who possesses riches that are permanent.
nallāk kurampai nilaiyāk karuti in nīḷnilattu oṉṟu allāk kuḻi vīḻntu ayarvuṟuvēṉai vantu āṇṭu (1) koṇṭāy
you came voluntarily and admitted as your protege me who forgot you by falling into the pit of other religion which cannot be considered as anything having any merit in this wide world, thinking the transcient body to be permanent.
viṭiṉ keṭuvēṉ
I will be ruined if you foresake me
[[Variant reading: (1) koṇṭa]]
{4:96}__5+
{$} tiruviṉ poli catti muṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
civakkoḻuntu who dwells in cattimuṟṟam which is eminent by its wealth!
karuppuviyil karuvuṟṟu iruntu uṉ kaḻalē niṉaintēṉ
when I was in the embryo in the world of embryo I was thinking of your feet only.
[[karuvāykkiṭantu uṉ kaḻalēniṉaiyum karuttuṭaiyēṉ (appar, tiruppātirippuliyūr, 6)]]
teruvil pukuntēṉ tikaittu
when I went into the street after being born in the world, in my childhood I was perplexed (1)
aṭiyēṉait tikaippu oḻi
have my perplexity removed by your grace.
uruviṉ tikaḻum umaiyāḷ kaṉavā
husband of Umayāḷ who is eminent by her beautiful body!
viṭiṉ keṭuvēṉ
I will be ruined if your forsake me.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṭiyēṉtaṉ]]
{4:96}__6+
{$} cemmai taru cattimuṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
civakkoḻuntu who dwells in cattimuṟṟam which grants rectitude!
vemmai namaṉtamar mikku viraviviḻuppataṉmuṉ
Before the servants of the cruel god of death come in large numbers towards me and make the body fall dead.
immai uṉtāḷ eṉtaṉ neñcattu eḻutivai
you inscribe your feet in my humble mind in this birth itself.
īṅku ikaḻil
if you are negligent in this world.
ammai aṭiyēṟku aruḷuti eṉpatu iṅku ār aṟivār
who will know in this world that you will grant your grace to your protege after death, in the next world?
{4:96}__7+
{$} tiruccatti muṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
see 1st verse.
viṭṭār puraṅkaḷ oru noṭivēva ōrnoṭivēva ōr veṅkaṇaiyāl cuṭṭāy
you burnt with a hot arrow the cities of the enemies in one instant which is the time taken for the time-measure of the snap of the finger. (viṭṭār those who have left friendship; or it can be taken as viṇṭār becoming viṭṭār to suit rhyme).
eṉpācat toṭarpu aṟuttu āṇṭu koḷ
admit me as your protege removing the connection of attachments.
tumpi (1) pampum maṭṭu ār kuḻalimalaimakaḷ pūcai makiḻntu koḷḷum ciṭṭar
one who is the embodiment of pure knowledge and who accepted with gladness the worship performed by the dughter of the mountin who has fragrant tresses of hirs on which tumpi (a superior variety of bee) swarms (From this we learn that Umi worshipped Civaṉ in this shrine).
[[Variant reading: (1) vavvum]]
{4:96}__8+
{$} tikaḻntu tiruccatti muṟṟattu uṟaiyum civak koḻuntē
civakkoḻuntu who dwells in the eminent tiruccattīmuṟṟam!
ikaḻntavaṉ vēḷvi aḻittiṭṭu
having destroyed the sacrifice performed by takkaṉ who slighted Civaṉ.
imaiyōr poṟai irappa nikaḻntiṭa aṉṟē vicayamum koṇṭatu
when the celestials who do not wink begged to forgive them, on the some day you were victorious, to become renowned.
nīlakaṇṭā
one who has a blue neck!
pukaḻnta aṭiyēṉ taṉ puṉmaikaḷ tīrap purintu nalkāy
you grant your grace to remove completely the several kinds of meanness of your protege who prised you.

{$} tiruccattimuṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
see 1st verse
takka ārvam eyti
Having the suitable eagerness to re-embrance civism.
camaṇ tavirntu
leaving camaṇam.
uṉ caraṇ pukuntēṉ
I took refuge in you.
ekkātal eppayaṉ uṉ tiṟam allāl eṉakku uḷatē
is there anything else except your greatness to be considered as attachment and gin?
mikkār tilaiyuḷ viruppā
one who is very much attached to tilli where great people (three thousand brahmins) live. (tilai-tillai).
vaṭamēru eṉum tikkā
one who remins in the superior mēru mountin which is in the north!

{$} tiruccattimuṟṟattu uṟaiyum civakkoḻuntē
see 1st verse.
poṟittēr arakkaṉ poruppu eṭuppuṟṟavaṉ poṉmuṭi tōḷ iṟattāḷ oruviral ūṉṟiṭṭu
fixing firmly a toe of the leg to crush the shoulders, and crowns made of gold, of the arrakaṉ who had a car fitted with machanical equipments and who lifted the mountin (Kayili).
alaṟa iraṅki oḷvāḷ kuṟittē koṭuttāy
you gave a bright sword fixing a name for it, taking pity on him when he roared (kuṟittu
may also mean with a purpose in mind).
koṭiyēṉ cey kuṟṟakkoṭuviṉainōy ceṟuttāy
you were angry at the diseases and which is the result of the acts of cruel faults committed by me who has a cruel nature.

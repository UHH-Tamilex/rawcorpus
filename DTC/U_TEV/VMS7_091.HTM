{7:91}__1+
{$} pāṭṭum pāṭip paravit tirivār īṭṭum viṉaikaḷ tīrppār kōyil
The temple of Civaṉ who destroys the accumulated acts of those who wander prising the fame of Civaṉ in verse and prose of ordinary people.
[[pāṭṭum: includes the prose also]]
[[PP: uraiyum pāṭṭum uṭaiyōr cilarē
(Puṟam, 27-5)]]
kāṭṭum kalamum timilum karaikkē ōṭṭum tiraivāy oṟṟiyūr
is Oṟṟiyūr which has waves which drive the big ships that are seen on the ocean and small boats ((used for fishing)) to the shore.
{7:91}__2+
{$} pantum kiḷiyum payilum pāvai cintai kavarvār centīvaṇṇar
Civaṉ who captivates the minds of the beautiful Umi who always plays with balls and parrots has a colour like the fire
em tam aṭikaḷ iṟaivarkku iṭam(pōl) untum tiraivāy oṟṟiyūrē
is Oṟṟiyūr on the waves which pushes many precious things and which is the place of our god and supreme being.
{7:91}__3+
{$} pavaḷak kaṉivāyp pāvai paṅkaṉ
Civaṉ has on one half a beautiful lady whose lips are like coral and the fruit of the common creeper of the hedge.
kavaḷak kaḷiṟṟiṉ urivai pōrttāṉ
covered himself with the skin of an elephant which eats mouthful of food to its satisfaction.
tavaḻum maticēr caṭaiyāṟku iṭam(pōl) ukaḷum tiraivāy oṟṟiyūrē
Oṟṟiyūr which has rolling waves is the place of the god who has on his caṭi a crescent which crawls.
{7:91}__4+
{$} eṉṉatu eḻilum niṟaiyum kavarvāṉ
Civan with the intention of capturing my strength of mind and beauty.
ḷapuṉṉai malarum puṟavil tikaḻumṉa taṉṉai muṉṉam niṉaikkat taruvāṉ
he offers himself to meditate upon.
uṉṉap paṭuvāṉ puṉṉai malarum puṟavil tikaḻum oṟṟiyūrē
is Oṟṟiyūr of the god who is meditated upon by devotees, in that way, which shines in the sea-shore garden where the mustwood tree blossoms.
{7:91}__5+
{$} paṇam koḷ aravam paṟṟip paramaṉ
the supreme god; catches hold of a cobra which has a hood.
kaṇaṅkaḷ cūḻak kapālam ēnti
holding a skull surrounded by the hosts of pūtams.
vaṇaṅkum iṭaimeṉ maṭavār iṭṭa uṇaṅkal kavarvāṉ oṟṟiyūrē
Oṟṟiyūr is the place of Civaṉ who receives the food given by the gentle ladies who have a bending waist.
{7:91}__6+
{$} paṭai ār maḻuvaṉ
Civaṉ has the weapon of a battle-axe.
pāl veṇṇīṟṟaṉ
smears himself with sacred ash as white a milk.
viṭaiyār koṭiyaṉ
has a flag of a bull.
vēta nāvaṉ
always chants the Vētam-s.
aṭaivār viṉaikaḷ aṟuppāṉ
completely destroys the acts of those who approach him.
eṉṉai uṭaiyāṉ uṟaiyum oṟṟiyūr
Oṟṟiyūr is the place where Civaṉ who has me as his protege dwells without leaving that place.
{7:91}__7+
{$} (1) ceṉṟu puraṅkaḷ tīyil vēva veṉṟa vikirtaṉ
Civan who is different from the world and gined a victory over the cities to make them burn in fire, going to their places;
[[it is a more valourous act to gin a victory in the enemies territory]]
[[tantai tammūr āṅkaṇ teṇkaṇai kaṟaṅkac ceṉṟu āṇṭu aṭṭaṉṉē (puṟam, 78-11-12);
ceṉṟu aṅkut teṉ ilaṅkai ceṟṟāy tiṟal pōṟṟi
ceṟṟār tiṟal aḻiyac ceṉṟu cerucceyyum kuṟṟam oṉṟu illāta kōvalar (tiruppāvi, 11,24)]]
viṉaiyai vīṭṭa naṉṟum nalla nātaṉ
the master who is good enough to destroy in a large measure, the acts of his devotees.
narai ēṟu oṉṟaiyuṭaiyāṉ oṟṟiyūrē
and who has a white bull dwells in Oṟṟiyūr without leaving it.
{7:91}__8+
{$} kalava mayilpōl vaḷaikkai nallār palarum paravum pavaḷap paṭiyāṉ
Civaṉ has a red body like coral, which is prised by many ladies who are as gentle as the peacock with a til and who wear bangles.
ulakiṉ uḷḷār viṉaikaḷ tīrppāṉ ulavum tiraivāy oṟṟiyūrē
is in Oṟṟiyūr on the shore of the ocean in which waves are moving, and removes the acts of the people of this world.
{7:91}__9+
{$} paṟṟi varaiyai eṭutta arakkaṉ iṟṟu muriya viralāl aṭarttār
Civaṉ pressed down with a single toe the arakkaṉ who cught hold of the mountin and lifted it, to be bent and be broken.
ōtam oṟṟum tiraivāy oṟṟiyūr
Though Civaṉ dwells in Oṟṟiyūr on the shore where the waves come into contact.
eṟṟum viṉaikaḷ tīrppār
will remove the acts which attack the devotees wherever they are.
{7:91}__10+
{$} oṟṟi ūrum aravum piṟaiyum paṟṟi ūrum pavaḷac caṭaiyār
Civaṉ who has a caṭi as red as coral in which the cobra and crescent crawl to rub aginst each other and having the caṭi as their support.
oṟṟiyūrmēl ūraṉ uraitta kaṟṟuppāṭak kaḻiyum viṉaiyē
the acts of those who learn and sing the verses which were spoken in prise of Oṟṟiyūr by Ārūraṉ, will come to an end.

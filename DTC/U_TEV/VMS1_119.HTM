[[This shrine is now known as Tirukaḷḷam]]

{1:119}__1+
{$} muḷḷil mēl mutukūkai muralum cōlai veḷḷilmēl viṭu kūṟaikkoṭi viḷainta kaḷḷil mēya aṇṇal kaḻalkaḷ nāḷum uḷḷumēl
if anyone meditates dily on the feet of the god in Kaḷḷil where on the wood apple tree flags made of cloth are found, occuring in the gardens where on the trees which have thorns; the old rock-horned owl cries.
uyarvu eytal orutalaiyē
it is a certinty that he will attin eminence
{1:119}__2+
{$} āṭalāṉ pāṭalāṉ aravaṅkaḷ pūṇṭāṉ
Civaṉ dances, sings and adorns himself with cobras.
ōṭu a(l)lāl kalaṉ illāṉ uṟaipati (āl)
the dwelling place of the god who has no other eating plate except the skull.
kāṭu alāl karutāta kaḷḷil mēyāṉ (1) pāṭu elām periyōrkaḷ paracuvārē
great people will prise all the greatness of the god in Kaḷḷil who does not consider any other place except the cremation ground for his dance.
[[Variant reading: 1.pāṭalām]]
{1:119}__3+
{$} eṇṇār mummatil eyta imaiyā mukkaṇ paṇār nāṉ maṟai pāṭum parama yōki
the supreme yōki who sings the four Vētam-s which are full of music, who has three eyes whose eyelids do not close and who shot an arrow on the three forts of his enemies.
kaṇ ār nīṟu aṇi mārpaṉ
who smears on his chest sacred ash beautiful to look at.
kaḷḷil mēyāṉ
who is in Kaḷḷil
peṇ āṇ ām perumāṉ em piññakaṉē
our Lord who adorned himself with a peacock's feather has both male and female forms in one body.
{1:119}__4+
{$} piṟai peṟṟa caṭai aṇṇal
the chief who has a crescent on his caṭi.
peṭai vaṇṭu ālum naṟaipeṟṟa viri koṉṟaittār nayanta kaṟai peṟṟa miṭaṟṟu aṇṇal
the chief who has a black neck, who desired a garland of blossomed koṉṟi having honey on which the female bee hums.
kaḷḷil mēyāṉ
who is in Kaḷḷil.
niṟaipeṟṟa (1) aṭiyārkaḷ neñcu uḷāṉē
is in the minds of devotees who have restrined them from going astray.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṭiyār tam neñcuḷāṉē]]
{1:119}__5+
{$} viraiyālum malarālum viḻumai kuṉṟā uraiyālum ūrār etir koḷḷa
to be received by the residents of that place with fragrant things; flowers, and words which are not deficient in magnificence.
karai ār poṉ puṉal vēlik kaḷḷil mēyāṉ
Civaṉ who is in Kaḷḷil which is surrounded by water on the bank of which gold in deposited.
arai ār veṇ (1) kōvaṇatta aṇṇal (tāṉ ē)
is the chief who wears in his waist a white loin-cloth.
[[Variant reading: 1. kōvaṇattaṇṇal tāṉē]]
{1:119}__6+
{$} nalaṉ āya pali koḷkai nampāṉ
Civaṉ who receives the alms which does good to those who give it.
nalla valaṉ āya maḻuvāḷum vēlum vallāṉ
who is capable of wielding a battle-axe which gives a fair victory, and a trident.
kalaṉ āya talai ōṭṭāṉ
has a skull which is his begging bowl.
kaḷḷil mēyāṉ
who is in Kaḷḷil.
mātavat tōrkkē malaṉ āya tīrttum eytum
removing the spiritual impurities of the people who have performed great penance, only he will join them.
{1:119}__7+
{$} poṭi ār mey pūciṉum
if the devotees smear their bodies with sacred ash sumptuously.
puṟaviṉ naṟavam kuṭiyā ūr tiriyiṉum
if they wander in the places having drunk the honey god from jungles.
kūppiṭiṉum
and if they utter loud sound
kaṭi ār pūñcōlaik kaḷḷil mēyāṉ aṭiyārkaḷ paṇpu ikaḻvārkaḷ ātarkaḷē
ignorant persons will ridicule the nature of the devotees of the Lord in Kaḷḷil which has fragrant flower gardens.
{1:119}__8+
{$} karu nīla malar vimmu kaḷḷil
Kaḷḷil where the blue nelumbo flowers unfold their petals
tiru nīla malar oṇkaṇ tēvipākam purinūlum tirunīṟum pulku mārpiṉ perunīla miṭaṟṟu aṇṇal eṉṟum pēṇuvatē
is the place which the chief with a big blue neck, in whose chest the part of the tēvi who has bright eyes, like the beautiful blue nelumbo flower, a sacred thread of three strands and sacred ash are combined, always desires.
{1:119}__9+
{$} vari āya malarāṉum vaiyam taṉṉai uritu āya aḷantāṉum uḷḷutaṟku (aṅku) ariyāṉum
Civaṉ who is difficult even to be thought of by Piramaṉ who is in a flower of lines and Māl who measured the worlds which were his property as if right.
(1) aritu āya kaḷḷil mēyāṉ
the Lord in Kaḷḷil which is a rare shrine
periyāṉ eṉṟu aṟivārkaḷ pēcuvārē
those who know him will speak about him as a great god.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṟiyāta]]
{1:119}__10+
{$} ācciyap pēykaḷōṭu amaṇar kuṇṭar pēccu ivai neṟi alla
the talks of the buddhists who behave like pēy and who are fit to be ridiculed and of the low amaṇar are not proper paths.
mācceyta vaḷavayal malku kaḷḷil tīcceyta caṭai aṇṇal tiruntu aṭiyē pēṇumiṉkaḷ
(people of this world) worship the perfect feet of the chief who has a caṭi resembling fire in colour, and who is in Kaḷḷil which has great and fertile fields of increasing yield.
{1:119}__11+
{$} ticai nāṉkum pukaḻ kāḻic celvam malku pakal pōlum pēr oḷiyāṉ pantaṉ
Pantaṉ who has brilliance like the sun; who has the increasing wealth of spiritual knowledge and who is a native of Kāḻi which is prised by the people of the four quarters.
nallamukaimēvum mutircaṭaiyāṉ kaḷḷil otta
To those who prise Kaḷḷil of the Lord who has a caṭi of mature growth to which fragrant buds are attached.
pukaḻōṭum pēriṉpam pukutum (aṉṟē)
supreme happiness and fame will reach them.

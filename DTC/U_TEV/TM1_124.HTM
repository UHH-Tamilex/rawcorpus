  • talam: tiru vīḻimiḻali
  • paṇ: viyāḻak kuṟiñci
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: VOL.7, P.218

*1
_alarmakaḷ malitara, _avaṉiyil nikaḻpavar---
malar mali kuḻal _umitaṉi _iṭam makiḻpavar,
nalam mali _uru _uṭiyavar, nakar miku pukaḻ
nilam mali miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*2
_irunilam_itaṉmici _eḻil peṟum _uruviṉar---
karu malitaru miku puvi mutal _ulakiṉil
_iruḷ _aṟu matiyiṉar, _imiyavar toḻutu _eḻu
nirupamaṉ, miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*3
kalimakaḷtalimakaṉ, _ivaṉ _eṉa varupavar---
_ali malitaru puṉal, _aravoṭu, nakutali,
_ili mali _itaḻiyum, _icitaru caṭiyiṉar
nili mali miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*4
māṭu _amar caṉam makiḻtaru maṉam _uṭiyavar---
kāṭu _amar kaḻutukaḷ_avi muḻavoṭum _ici
pāṭaliṉ navilpavar mikutarum _ulakiṉil
nīṭu _amar miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*5
pukaḻmakaḷ tuṇiyiṉar---purikuḻal _umitaṉi
_ikaḻvu ceytavaṉ _uṭi _eḻil maṟivaḻi vaḷar
mukam_atu cititara muṉivu ceytavaṉ miku
nikaḻtaru miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*6
_aṉṟiṉar _ari _eṉa varupavar---_aritiṉil
_oṉṟiya tiripuram _orunoṭiyiṉil _eri
ceṉṟu koḷ vaki ciṟumuṟuvalkoṭu _oḷi peṟa
niṉṟavaṉ miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*7
karam payil koṭiyiṉar---kaṭimalar _ayaṉatu _or
ciram payilvu _aṟa _eṟi civaṉ _uṟi ceḻu nakar,
varam payil kalipala maṟi muṟi _aṟaneṟi
nirampiṉar, miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*8
_orukkiya _uṇarviṉoṭu _oḷineṟi celumavar---
_arakkaṉ nalmaṇimuṭi_orupatum _irupatum-
karakkaṉam neritara, malar_aṭiviralkoṭu
nerukkiṉaṉ miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*9
_aṭiyavar kuḻumiṭa _avaṉiyil nikaḻpavar---
kaṭimalar _ayaṉ _ari karuta(_a)ru vaki taḻal-
vaṭivu _uru_iyalpiṉoṭu _ulakukaḷ niṟitaru
neṭiyavaṉ miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*10
maṉmataṉ _eṉa _oḷi peṟumavar---marutu _amar
vaṉ malar tuvar_uṭiyavarkaḷum, mati_ilar
tuṉmati _amaṇarkaḷ, toṭarvu _aru miku pukaḻ
niṉmalaṉ miḻaliyi niṉiya vallavarē.

*11
nittilaṉ miḻaliyi, nikar_ili pukaliyuḷ
vittakamaṟi mali tamiḻvirakaṉa moḻi
pattiyil varuvaṉa pattu_ivi payilvoṭu
kaṟṟu vallavar _ulakiṉil _aṭiyavarē.

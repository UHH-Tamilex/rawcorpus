• patikam: 1:124
  • talam: tiru vīḻimiḻalai
  • paṇ: viyāḻak kuṟiñci
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: VOL.7, P.218

*1
_alarmakaḷ malitara, _avaṉiyil nikaḻpavar---
malar mali kuḻal _umaitaṉai _iṭam makiḻpavar,
nalam mali _uru _uṭaiyavar, nakar miku pukaḻ
nilam mali miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*2
_irunilam_itaṉmicai _eḻil peṟum _uruviṉar---
karu malitaru miku puvi mutal _ulakiṉil
_iruḷ _aṟu matiyiṉar, _imaiyavar toḻutu _eḻu
nirupamaṉ, miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*3
kalaimakaḷtalaimakaṉ, _ivaṉ _eṉa varupavar---
_alai malitaru puṉal, _aravoṭu, nakutalai,
_ilai mali _itaḻiyum, _icaitaru caṭaiyiṉar
nilai mali miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*4
māṭu _amar caṉam makiḻtaru maṉam _uṭaiyavar---
kāṭu _amar kaḻutukaḷ_avai muḻavoṭum _icai
pāṭaliṉ navilpavar mikutarum _ulakiṉil
nīṭu _amar miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*5
pukaḻmakaḷ tuṇaiyiṉar---purikuḻal _umaitaṉai
_ikaḻvu ceytavaṉ _uṭai _eḻil maṟaivaḻi vaḷar
mukam_atu citaitara muṉivu ceytavaṉ miku
nikaḻtaru miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*6
_aṉṟiṉar _ari _eṉa varupavar---_aritiṉil
_oṉṟiya tiripuram _orunoṭiyiṉil _eri
ceṉṟu koḷ vakai ciṟumuṟuvalkoṭu _oḷi peṟa
niṉṟavaṉ miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*7
karam payil koṭaiyiṉar---kaṭimalar _ayaṉatu _or
ciram payilvu _aṟa _eṟi civaṉ _uṟai ceḻu nakar,
varam payil kalaipala maṟai muṟai _aṟaneṟi
nirampiṉar, miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*8
_orukkiya _uṇarviṉoṭu _oḷineṟi celumavar---
_arakkaṉ nalmaṇimuṭi_orupatum _irupatum-
karakkaṉam neritara, malar_aṭiviralkoṭu
nerukkiṉaṉ miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*9
_aṭiyavar kuḻumiṭa _avaṉiyil nikaḻpavar---
kaṭimalar _ayaṉ _ari karuta(_a)ru vakai taḻal-
vaṭivu _uru_iyalpiṉoṭu _ulakukaḷ niṟaitaru
neṭiyavaṉ miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*10
maṉmataṉ _eṉa _oḷi peṟumavar---marutu _amar
vaṉ malar tuvar_uṭaiyavarkaḷum, mati_ilar
tuṉmati _amaṇarkaḷ, toṭarvu _aru miku pukaḻ
niṉmalaṉ miḻalaiyai niṉaiya vallavarē.

*11
nittilaṉ miḻalaiyai, nikar_ili pukaliyuḷ
vittakamaṟai mali tamiḻvirakaṉa moḻi
pattiyil varuvaṉa pattu_ivai payilvoṭu
kaṟṟu vallavar _ulakiṉil _aṭiyavarē.

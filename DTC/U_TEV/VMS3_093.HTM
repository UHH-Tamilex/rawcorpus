{3:93}__1+
{$} paṭiyuḷ ār viṭaiyiṉar
Civaṉ has a bull that lives in this world.
pāy pulit tōliṉar
has the skin of a tiger that springs upon its prey
[[pāy: an epithet denoting [the tiger's] quality when it was alive]]
pāvanācar
destroys all sins.
[[or dwells in a shrine called pāvanācam]]
poṭi koḷ mā mēṉiyar
has a big body on which holy ash is smeared.
pūtam ār paṭaiyiṉar
has an army of pūtams.
pūṇanūlar
wears a sacred thread on his chest
kaṭi koḷ māmalar iṭum aṭiyiṉar
has feet on which the devotees place big and fragrant flowers.
piṭi naṭai maṅkaiyōṭum aṭikaḷār aruḷ purintu iruppiṭam amparmākāḷam tāṉē
Amparmākāḷam is the place where he dwells granting grace, along with a young lady whose git is like that of the female elephant.
{3:93}__2+
{$} kaiyil (1) mā maḻuviṉar
Civaṉ holds a big battle-axe in his hand.
kaṭuviṭam uṇṭa em kāḷakaṇṭar
our Lord with a neck which become black due to consuming the cruel poison.
ceyya māmēṉiyar
has a big red body.
(2) ūṉ amar uṭai talaip pali tirivār
will wander to receive alms holding a broken skull to which flesh is sticking.
vaiyam ār potuviṉil maṟaiyavar toḻutu eḻa naṭam atu āṭum aiyaṉ
the father who dances in the hall which is common to the whole world, to be worshipped by the brahmins.
mātēviyōṭu iruppiṭam amparmākāḷam tāṉē
is amparmākāḷam is the place where he dwells with goddess parvati.
[[Variant reading: (1) māṉ (2) ūṉam ār]]
{3:93}__3+
{$} paraviṉa aṭiyavar paṭutuyar keṭuppavar
Civaṉ destroys the sufferings of the devotees who prise him, have to undergo
parivu ilār pāl karaviṉar
he conceals himself from those who have no love towards him.
kaṉal aṉa uruviṉar
has a form as red as fire.
paṭu talaip palikoṭu ēkum iraviṉar
has the nature of going for begging alms in a dead skull
pakal eri kāṉiṭai āṭiya vēṭar
has a form with which he danced in the cremation ground which scorches like the midday.
pūṇam araviṉar
wears cobras as ornaments
arivaiyōṭu iruppiṭam amparmākāḷam tāṉē
is amparmākāḷam where he dwells with a lady.
{3:93}__4+
{$} nīṟṟiṉar
Civaṉ has smeared himself with holy ash
nīṇṭa vār caṭaiyiṉar
has a long and hanging cati
paṭaiyiṉar
has many weapons.
nimalar
is the immaculate god
veḷḷai ēṟṟiṉar
has a white bull.
eri puri karattiṉar
holds in his hand fire, desiring it
purattu uḷār uyirai vavvum kūṟṟiṉar
is the god of death to the life of the acurar in the three cities.
koṭiyiṭai muṉivuṟa naṇi varum kulavu kaṅkai āṟṟiṉar
has on his head a shining river, Kanki which came with great fore to make Umi who has a waist as slender as a creeper, to feel sulky.
arivaiyōṭu iruppiṭam amparmākāḷam tāṉē
amparmākāḷam is the place where he dwells with a lady.
{3:93}__5+
{$} puṟattiṉar akattiṉar
Civaṉ is present both outside and inside of everything.
pōṟṟi niṉṟu aḻutu eḻum aṉpar cintait tiṟattiṉar
appear according to the idea of those pions people who prise him and weep out of joy.
cetu matit takkaṉ taṉ vēḷvi ceṟṟa maṟattiṉar
has the valour to destroy the sacrifice of Takkaṉ who had a mind heading towards destruction
[[PP_(cetu mati):
cetu mati miku camaṇar (Campantar, Tiruccivapuram 10)
kuṇṭar taṅkaḷ cetu matiyār tīviṉaikkē viḻuntu (Nāvukkaracar, Maḻapāṭi (2) 6.)]]
mātavar nāl varukku āliṉ kīḻ aruḷpurinta aṟattiṉar
gave religions instruction to four sages under the banyan tree.
arivaiyōṭu iruppiṭam amparmākāḷam tāṉē
the place he dwells with a lady is amparmākāḷam
{3:93}__6+
{$} paḻaka māmalar paṟittu iṇṭai koṇṭu iṟaiñcuvār pāl ceṟinta kuḻakaṉār
the youth who is intimate with those who bow before him preparing a circlet by gathering big flowers without even a day's break.
kuṇam pukaḻntu ēttuvār palar kūṭa niṉṟa kaḻakaṉār
who is in the assemblage of many people who prise his good qualities.
kari urittu āṭukaṅkāḷar
who puts on the complete skeleton and dances out of joy having flayed an elephant.
nam kāḷi ēttum aḻakaṉār
our beautiful god who is prised by Kāḷi.
arivaiyōṭu iruppiṭam amparmākāḷam tāṉē
is amparmākāḷam where he dwells with a lady, Umi.
{3:93}__7+
{$} caṅkavār kuḻaiyiṉar
Civaṉ wears a long men's earring made of conch.
taḻal aṉa uruviṉar
has a form, as red as fire.
tamatu aruḷē eṅkumāy iruntavar
is omnipresent, his grace having spread everywhere.
arunta muṉivarukku aḷittu ukantār
became eminent by granting spiritual wisdom to sages who did severe penance.
poṅkumā puṇal paranta ariciliṉ vaṭakarai tiruttam pēṇi
desirous of the holy water on the north bank of the river aricil whose surging floods spreads everywhere.
aṅkam āṟu ōtuvār iruppiṭam amparmākāḷam tāṉē
amparmākāḷam is the place where he who chants the skin ankams, dwells.
{3:93}__8+
{$} porucilai mataṉaṉaip poṭipaṭa viḻittavar
Civaṉ who opened his frontal eye to reduce to ash mataṉaṉ who has a bow with which he afflicts lovers.
poḻil ilaṅkaik kuricilaik kulavaraik kīḻ uṟa aṭarttavar
crushed under the superior mountin, Kayili, the King of ilaṅki, which had the famous `acōka vaṉam'.
kōyil kūṟil
if we mention about his temple.
porucilai nalamaṇi pīliyōṭu ēlamum perukanuntum ariciliṉ vaṭakarai
situated on the north bank of the river aricil which pushes in large quantities cardamon, peacock's feather, the flawless precious stones from the big mountin.
aḻaku amar amparmākāḷam tāṉē
is the beautiful amparmākāḷam.
{3:93}__9+
{$} vari arā ataṉ micaittuyiṉṟavaṉ tāṉum māmalaruḷāṉum
Māl who slept on a serpent which has lines on its body, and Piramaṉ who is on a big (lotus) flower.
eriyarā
having becoming a fire.
aṇikaḻal ētta oṇṇā vakai uyarntu
and growing tall so that both could not prise the beautiful feet of Civaṉ.
piṉṉum piriyarām aṭiyavarkku aṇiyarāy
within easy reach of devotees who cherish him with love, at the same time.
paṇivilātavarukku eṉṟum ariyarāy
difficult of approach to those who have no humility.
arivaiyōṭu iruppiṭam amparmākāḷam tāṉē
is amparmākāḷam where he dwells with a lady.
{3:93}__10+
{$} cākkiyakkayavar vaṉtalai paṟikkaiyarum poyyiṉāl nūl ākkiya moḻiyavai piḻai (avai) ātalil, vaḻipaṭuvīr!
People who worship Civaṉ! base buddhists and low people of camaṇar who pluck the hir on their heads, have fabricated works contining falsehood and they are faulty.
vīkkiya aravuṭaikkaccaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has fastened as a girdle a cobra.
iccaiyāṉavarkaṭku ellām ākkiya araṉ uṟai amparmākāḷamē nīr aṭaimiṉ
you reach Amparmākāḷam where Araṉ who grants all things to those who love him, dwells.
{3:93}__11+
{$} cempoṉ māmaṇi koḻittu eḻutirai varu puṉal aricil cūḻnta amparmākāḷamē kōyilā
having as the temple amparmākāḷam surrounded by the aricil which brings freshes with rising waves which carry away natural gold and precious stones.
aṇaṅkiṉōṭu irunta kōṉai
on the master who dwelt with a divine damsel, Umi.
kampiṉ ār neṭu matil kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa
the verses that were sung by Ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kāḻi which has a high wall of enclosure plastered with lime of the conch-shell.
nampi nāḷmoḻipavarkku illai (ām) viṉai; nalam peṟuvar (tām, ē)
to those who recite dily faith, there will be no Karmam; they will receive all good things.

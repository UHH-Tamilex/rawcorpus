*1
mantira maṟiyavar, vāṉavaroṭum,
_intiraṉ, vaḻipaṭa niṉṟa _em _iṟi;
ventaveṇnīṟṟar---veṇkāṭu mēviya,
_antamum mutal _uṭi, _aṭikaḷ _allarē!

*2
paṭi _uṭi maḻuviṉar, pāy pulittōliṉ
_uṭi viri kōvaṇam _ukanta koḷkiyar,
viṭi _uṭik koṭiyar---veṇkāṭu mēviya,
caṭi_iṭip puṉal vitta, caturar _allarē!

*3
pāloṭu, ney, tayir, palavum _āṭuvar;
tōloṭu nūl-_iḻi tutinta mārpiṉar---
mēlavar paravu veṇkāṭu mēviya,
_ālam_atu _amarnta, _em _aṭikaḷ _allarē!

*4
ñāḻalum ceruntiyum naṟumalarppuṉṉiyum
tāḻi veṇkuruku _ayal tayaṅku kāṉalil,
vēḻam_aḻu _uritta, veṇkāṭu mēviya,
yāḻiṉatu _ici _uṭi, _iṟivar _allarē!

*5
pūtaṅkaḷpala _uṭip puṉitar, puṇṇiyar,
_ētaṅkaḷpala _iṭar tīrkkum _em _iṟi,
vētaṅkaḷ mutalvar---veṇkāṭu mēviya,
pātaṅkaḷ toḻa niṉṟa, paramar _allarē!

*6
maṇṇavar viṇṇavar vaṇaṅka, vikalum
_eṇṇiya tēvarkaḷ _iṟiñcum _em _iṟi---
viṇ _amar poḻil koḷ veṇkāṭu mēviya
_aṇṇali---_aṭi toḻa, _allal _illiyē.

*7
nayantavarkku _aruḷpala nalki, _intiraṉ
kayantiram vaḻipaṭa niṉṟa kaṇṇutal---
viyantavar paravu veṇkāṭu mēviya,
payam taru maḻu _uṭi, paramar _allarē!

*8
mali _uṭaṉ_eṭutta val_arakkaṉ nīḷ muṭi
tali_uṭaṉ nerittu, _aruḷceyta caṅkarar;
vili _uṭi nīṟṟar---veṇkāṭu mēviya,
_ali _uṭip puṉal vitta, _aṭikaḷ _allarē!

*9
_ēṭu _aviḻ naṟumalar _ayaṉum mālum_āyt

vētam_atu _uṭiya veṇkāṭu mēviya,
_āṭali _amarnta, _em _aṭikaḷ _allarē!

*10

_ātiyi _aṭi toḻa, _allal _illiyē.

*11

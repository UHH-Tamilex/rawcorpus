*1

nāṭakam_āṭum, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
cūṭaka muṉki maṭantimārkaḷ tuṇivaroṭum toḻutu _ētti vāḻtta,
_āṭakamāṭam neruṅku kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*2

nam kaṇ makiḻum, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
poṅku _iḷameṉmuliyārkaḷōṭum puṉamayil _āṭa, nilā muḷikkum
_am kaḷakac-cuti māṭak kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*3

naṇṇal _ariya, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
puṇṇiyavāṇarum mā tavarum pukuntu _uṭaṉ_ētta, puṉiyiḻiyār
_aṇṇaliṉ pāṭal _eṭukkum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*4
pūviṉil vācam, puṉalil poṟpu, putu viriccāntiṉil nāṟṟattōṭu,
nāviṉil pāṭal, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
tēvarkaḷ, tāṉavar, cittar, viccātarar, kaṇattōṭum ciṟantu poṅki,
_āviṉil_aintu _ukantu _āṭṭum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*5
cempoṉ cey māliyum, vācikiyum, tiruntu pukiyum, _aviyum, pāṭṭum,
nampum perumi, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
_umparum, nākar_ulakamtāṉum, _olikaṭal cūḻnta _ulakattōrum,
_amputamnālkaḷāl nīṭum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*6
pākamum tēviyi vittukkoṇṭu, pi viri tuttip pariya pēḻvāy
nākamum pūṇṭa, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
pōkamum niṉṉi maṉattuvittup puṇṇiyar naṇṇum puṇarvu pūṇṭa
_ākam _uṭiyavar cērum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*7
kōvaṇa_āṭiyum, nīṟṟuppūccum, koṭutaḻu _ēntalum, ceñcaṭiyum,
nāvaṇap pāṭṭum, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
pūvaṇa mēṉi _iḷiya mātar, poṉṉum maṇiyum koḻittu _eṭuttu,
_āvaṇavītiyil _āṭum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*8
_ilaṅki _irāvaṇaṉ veṟpu _eṭukka, _eḻil viral _ūṉṟi, _ici virumpi,
nalam koḷac cērnta, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
pulaṉkaḷic ceṟṟu, poṟiyi nīkki, puntiyilum niṉic cinticeyyum
_alaṅkal nallārkaḷ _amarum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*9

naṇukal _ari, naḷḷāṟu _uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---

_aṇi kiḷar vēntar pukutum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*10

naṭukku_uṟa niṉṟa, naḷḷāṟu_uṭiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
_eṭukkum viḻavum naṉnāḷ viḻavum _irum pali _iṉpiṉōṭu _etticiyum
_aṭukkum perumi cēr māṭak kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*1

nāṭakam_āṭum, naḷḷāṟu _uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
cūṭaka muṉkai maṭantaimārkaḷ tuṇaivaroṭum toḻutu _ētti vāḻtta,
_āṭakamāṭam neruṅku kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*2
tiṅkaḷ_ampōtum ceḻumpuṉalum ceñcaṭaimāṭṭu _ayal vaittu _ukantu,

poṅku _iḷameṉmulaiyārkaḷōṭum puṉamayil _āṭa, nilā muḷaikkum
_am kaḷakac-cutai māṭak kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*3

naṇṇal _ariya, naḷḷāṟu _uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
puṇṇiyavāṇarum mā tavarum pukuntu _uṭaṉ_ētta, puṉaiyiḻaiyār
_aṇṇaliṉ pāṭal _eṭukkum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*4
pūviṉil vācam, puṉalil poṟpu, putu viraiccāntiṉil nāṟṟattōṭu,
nāviṉil pāṭal, naḷḷāṟu _uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
tēvarkaḷ, tāṉavar, cittar, viccātarar, kaṇattōṭum ciṟantu poṅki,
_āviṉil_aintu _ukantu _āṭṭum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*5
cempoṉ cey mālaiyum, vācikaiyum, tiruntu pukaiyum, _aviyum, pāṭṭum,
nampum perumai, naḷḷāṟu _uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
_umparum, nākar_ulakamtāṉum, _olikaṭal cūḻnta _ulakattōrum,
_amputamnālkaḷāl nīṭum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*6
pākamum tēviyai vaittukkoṇṭu, pai viri tuttip pariya pēḻvāy
nākamum pūṇṭa, naḷḷāṟu _uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
pōkamum niṉṉai maṉattuvaittup puṇṇiyar naṇṇum puṇarvu pūṇṭa
_ākam _uṭaiyavar cērum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*7
kōvaṇa_āṭaiyum, nīṟṟuppūccum, koṭutaḻu _ēntalum, ceñcaṭaiyum,
nāvaṇap pāṭṭum, naḷḷāṟu _uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
pūvaṇa mēṉi _iḷaiya mātar, poṉṉum maṇiyum koḻittu _eṭuttu,
_āvaṇavītiyil _āṭum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*8
_ilaṅkai _irāvaṇaṉ veṟpu _eṭukka, _eḻil viral _ūṉṟi, _icai virumpi,
nalam koḷac cērnta, naḷḷāṟu _uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
pulaṉkaḷaic ceṟṟu, poṟiyai nīkki, puntiyilum niṉaic cintaiceyyum
_alaṅkal nallārkaḷ _amarum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*9
paṇi _uṭai mālum malariṉōṉum, paṉṟiyum veṉṟip paṟavai_āyum,
naṇukal _ari, naḷḷāṟu _uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
maṇi_oli caṅku_oliyōṭu maṟṟai mā muraciṉ(ṉ)_oli _eṉṟum _ōvātu
_aṇi kiḷar vēntar pukutum kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

*10

naṭukku_uṟa niṉṟa, naḷḷāṟu_uṭaiya, namperumāṉ! _itu _eṉkol collāy---
_eṭukkum viḻavum naṉnāḷ viḻavum _irum pali _iṉpiṉōṭu _etticaiyum
_aṭukkum perumai cēr māṭak kūṭal _ālavāyiṉkaṇ _amarnta_āṟē?

These ten verses are the speech of the foster-mother who tries to console her dughter who suffers having fallen in love with the lord. This is akapporuḷ.

1. vaṇṇamum vaṭivum ceṉṟu kaṇṭilaḷ;
(This girl) has not gone and seen the (lord's) colour and form.
_eṇṇi nāmaṅkaḷ _ētti niṟintilaḷ;
she has not attined peace of mind by meditating upon him and chanting his names (she is not content with the twin acts of meditation and chanting of his names)
kaṇ _ulām poḻil cūḻ kaḻippāli _em_aṇṇalē [[A1]]
_ivaḷ taṉmi [*ē*]! [[A3]]
_aṟivāṉ, [[A2]]
only the exalted one in Kaḻippāli which is surrounded by spacious gardens can know his dughter's nature
2.maruntu (* uṇṭu *) vāṉavar _uyya
in order that celestial beings may survive by partaking of nectar.
nañcu [[B1]]
_ukantu [[B3]]
_uṇṭu [[B2]]
_iruntavaṉ [[B4]],
one who was without any bad effects after having drunk the poison with pleasure,
kaḻippāliyuḷ _empirāṉ, tiruntu cēvaṭi cintiyuḷ vittu,
thinking in her mind about the perfect feet, of our lord in Kaḻippali,
parintu _urikkilum, [[C2]]
_ivaḷ, [[C1]]
_eṉ col paḻikkum [*ē*]. [[C3]]
If I tell her the means of removing her distress speaking affectionately this girl ridicules my words.
3.maḻalitāṉ varac
speaking only lisping words that come out of her mouth.
col-terikiṉṟilaḷ;
she does not know other words to express her minds.
kuḻaliṉ nēr moḻi kūṟiya kēṇmiṉ [*ō*];
(Ladies!) Please hear what the girl who speaks sweet words like the music of the flute, sid.
_aḻakaṉē! kaḻippāli _em _aṇṇalē!
! - oh beautiful one! exalted god in Kaḻippāli!
(* eṉi *) _ikaḻvatō,
is it right on your part to neglect me?
_eṉi? _ēṉṟukoḷ! _eṉṉum [*ē*].
she says `Please accept me (as your devotee!)
[The rhyme (etuki) in this verse is noteworthy]
4.ceyyamēṉi veṇnīṟu _aṇivāṉtaṉi miyal _āki,
having infatuation of love with the god who smears white ash on his red form. [...]
_āriyum [[D2]]
matikkilaḷ, [[D1]]
she does not mind anyone.
ki koḷ veṇmaḻuvaṉ,
one who has a white fire in his hand.
kaḻippāli _em _aiyaṉē
only the lord in Kaḻippāli.
_ivaḷ taṉmi [*ē*] [[E2]]
_aṟivāṉ [[E1]]
can know this girl's nature.
5.karuttaṉi,
one who is in the minds of people.
kaḻippāliyuḷ mēviya _oruttaṉi,
the unequalled one in Kaḻippali.
_umiyāḷ _orupaṅkaṉi,
one who has Umayāḷ on one half
_aruttiyāl ceṉṟu kaṇṭiṭa vēṇṭum _eṉṟu
thinking with desire; `I must go and see him personally.'
_orutti [*y*]ār _uḷam _ūcal (_atu) {V-}_āṭumē{-V}.
the mind of one girl which is full of love, is going forwards and backwards like a swing.
[The desire to see god drags her forward; but the shyness due to the derision of others pushes her back].
6.kaṅkiyic caṭi vittu
Having placed Kaṅki in the matted locks of hir.
malimakaḷ naṅkiyi _uṭaṉē vitta {V-} maṇāḷaṉār {-V},
he is the bride-groom who kept by his side a lady who is the dughter of the mountin.
tiṅkaḷcūṭi, tiruk kaḻippāliyāṉ,
the lord in Tirukkaḻippali and is wearing the crescent.
_iṅku vantiṭum _eṉṟu _iṟumākkumē.
she feels proud that he would definitely come to her (searching for her).
7._aiyaṉē!
Lord!
_aḻakē!
the very embodiment of beauty!
_aṉal _ēntiya kiyaṉē!
one who holds in his hand fire!
mi _ulām poḻil cūḻ kaḻippāli {V-} uḷ {-V} _aiyaṉē,
the lord in Kaḻippāli surrounded by gardens on which clouds traverse.
vitiyē,
one who ordins everything!
_aruḷ! _eṉṉum [*ē*].
she says, Bestow grace on me!
8.pattarkaṭku _amutu_āya parattiṉi,
The supreme one who is as sweet as nectar to his devotees.
muttaṉi,
one who is free from all bondage naturally.
muṭivu _oṉṟu _ilā mūrttiyi,
the one who assumed a form for which there is no end.
_attaṉi,
the father
_aṇi _ār kaḻippāli _em cittaṉi
our lord who is capable of performing miracles and who is in Kaḻippāli which has great beauty
[cittaṉ: cittu + aṉ: the knowledge itself]
[cittam: mind; cittaṉ: one who is in my mind]
ceṉṟu cērumā(*ṟu*) ceppumē!
she talks about going and joining him.

10.poṉ cey mā muṭi vāḷ _arakkaṉ tali- _añcum nāṉkum _oṉṟu(m) _iṟuttāṉ_avaṉ
she who knocked out the ten heads of arakkaṉ who has a sword and big crowns of gold.
kaḻippāliyuḷ [[F2]]
_empirāṉ [[F3]]
_eṉ ceyāṉ? [[F1]]
what is there that our lord in Kaḻippāli cannot do?
tuñcumpōtum tuṇi _eṉal _ākum [*ē*].
he will be our guide even at the time of death.
[[tuñcum: uṟaṅkum; tuñcum pōtum even when I am asleep]]

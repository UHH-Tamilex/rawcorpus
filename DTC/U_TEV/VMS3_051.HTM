{3:51}__1+
{$} ceyyaṉē
impartial god!
[[God who has a red colour]]
tiruvālavāy mēviya attaṉē
father who dwells with desire in Tiruvālavāy!
eṉai añcal eṉṟu aruḷcey
grant me your grace by assuring me Do not fear?
poyyar ām amaṇar koḷuvum cuṭar paiyavē ceṉṟu pāṇṭiyaṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire that was set to the building in which I am staying, by the amanar who are liars go and gradually seize the pāṇṭiyaṉ.
{3:51}__2+
{$} cittaṉē
Civaṉ who took the form of one who possessed eight miraculous powers!
tiruvālavāy mēviya attaṉē
the father who dwells with desire in Tiruvālavāy.
eṉai añcal eṉṟu aruḷ cey
[[see 1st verse]]
ettar ām amaṇar koḷuvum cuṭar pattimaṉ teṉṉaṉ pāṇṭiyaṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire that was lit by the amaṇar who are deceits, seize the pāṇṭiyaṉ who rules over the southern part of Tamiḻ Nāṭu, and who is a very devout camaṇaṉ.
{3:51}__3+
{$} takkaṉ vēḷvi takarttu aruḷ ālavāyc cokkaṉē
the beautiful god, Civaṉ who destroyed the sacrifice performed by Takkaṉ and then granted him your grace!
eṉai añcal eṉṟu aruḷcey
see 1st verse.
ekkar ām amaṇar koḷuvumcuṭar pakkamē ceṉṟu pāṇṭiyaṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire that was set to my buinding by the hughty amaṇar go to the side of the amaṇar themselves and then seize pāṇṭiyaṉ.
{3:51}__4+
{$} ciṭṭaṉē
estimable god!
tiruvālavāy mēviya aṭṭamūrttiyaṉē
one who has eight forms and dwells with desire in tiruvālavāy!
añcal eṉṟu aruḷ
see 1st verse.
tuṭṭar ām amaṇar koḷuvum cuṭar paṭṭimaṉ teṉṉaṉ pāṇṭiyaṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire set to my building by the mischievous amaṇar seize the Pāṇṭiyaṉ who rules the Southern portion of Tamiḻ Nāṭu and who is a well-educated scholar.
{3:51}__5+
{$} naṇṇalārpuram mūṉṟu eri ālavāy aṇṇalē
the chief in ālavāy who burnt the three cities of the enemies!
eṉai añcal eṉṟu aruḷcey
see 1st verse.
eṇ ilā amaṇar koḷuvum cuṭar paṇiyal tamiḻp pāṇṭiyaṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire set to my building by the amaṇar who did not think it improper, seize pāṇṭiyaṉ who is well versed in īyal and īcittamiḻ.
{3:51}__6+
{$} tañcam eṉṟu uṉ caraṇ pukuntēṉaiyum añcal eṉṟu aruḷ; ālavāy aṇṇalē
the chief in ālavāy! grant me your grace, who took refuge in you, thinking you are my protector.
vañcam ceytu amaṇar koḷuvum cuṭar pañcavaṉ teṉṉaṉ pāṇṭiṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire set to my building by the amanar practising deceit, seize pāṇṭiyaṉ who rules the southern portion of tamil nāṭu, which has all the five regions; (pañcavaṉ the King whose Kingdom has all the five regions; Kuṟiñci; mulli, marutam, neytal and pāli; aimpāl tiṇaiyum kaviṉi (maturikkāñci, 326).
{3:51}__7+
{$} ceṅkaṇ veḷviṭaiyāy
Civaṉ who rides on a white bull which has bright glowing eyes!
tiruvālavāy aṅkaṇā
Civaṉ who is in tiruvālavāy and has gracious looks!
eṉai añcal eṉṟu aruḷcey
see 1st verse.
kaṅkulār amaṇkaiyar iṭum (1) kaṉal paṅkamil teṉṉaṉ pāṇṭiyaṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire set to my building by the low people of amaṇar who are as black as the night, seize pāṇṭiyaṉ who rules over the southern portion and who has no defect.
[[Variant reading: (1) aṉal]]
{3:51}__8+
{$} tūrttaṉ vīram tolaittu aruḷ ālavāy (1) āttaṉē
trustworthy Civaṉ in ālavāy who destroyed the valour of the wicked person, Irāvaṇaṉ who is a dissolute person!
eṉai añcal eṉṟu aruḷcey
see 1st verse.
ēttu ilā amaṇar koḷuvum cuṭar pārttivaṉ teṉṉaṉ pāṇṭiyaṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire lit by the amaṇar who did not prise you seize the King who rules the southern portion and who belongs to the pāṇṭiyaṉ dynasty.
[[Variant reading: (1) ārttaṉē]]
{3:51}__9+
{$} tāviṉāṉ ayaṉtāṉ aṟiyā vakai mēviṉāy
Civaṉ who assumed a form which could not be known by Māl who measured the world and Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ)
tiruvālavāyāy
god in tiruvālavāy!
aruḷ
grant me your grace.
tū ilā amaṇar koḷuvum cuṭar pāviṉāṉ teṉṉaṉ pāṇṭiyaṟku āka(ē)
Let the fire lit by the impure amaṇar seize pāṇṭiyaṉ who rules the southern portion and who is eulogized in poetry.
{3:51}__10+
{$} eṇticaikku(um) eḻil ālavāy mēviya aṇṭaṉē
the god who dwells with desire in ālavāy which adds beauty to all the eight directions of the world!
eṉai añcal eṉṟu aruḷcey
see 1st verse.
kuṇṭar ām amaṇar koḷuvum cuṭar paṇṭimaṉ teṉṉaṉ pāṇṭiyaṟku āka (ē)
Let the fire set to my building by the amaṇar who are low in character, seize pāṇṭiyaṉ who rules the southern region and who is a great scholar (paṇṭimaṉ: paṭṭimaṉ has been changed into paṇṭimaṉ to soften a hard consonant into the corresponding soft or nasal consonant)

veppam teṉṉavaṉmēl uṟa

mētiṉikku oppa

  • talam: tiru _iṉṉampar
  • (()): tiru viruttam

*1
maṉṉum malaimakaḷ kaiyāl varuṭiṉa; māmaṟaikaḷ
coṉṉa tuṟaitoṟum tūp poruḷ _āyiṉa; tūk kamalattu
_aṉṉa vaṭiviṉa; _aṉpu _uṭait toṇṭarkku _amutu _arutti
_iṉṉal kaḷaivaṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*2
paitalpiṇakkuḻaik kāḷi veṅkōpam paṅkappaṭuppāṉ
ceytaṟku _ariya tirunaṭam ceytaṉa; cīr maṟaiyōṉ
_uytalporuṭṭu veṅ kūṟṟai _utaittaṉa; _umparkku _ellām
_eytaṟku _ariyaṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉai _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*3
cuṇaṅku niṉṟu _ār koṅkaiyāḷ _umai cūṭiṉa; tū malarāl
vaṇaṅki niṉṟu _umparkaḷ vāḻttiṉa; maṉṉum maṟaikaḷ tammil
piṇaṅki niṉṟu _iṉṉa(_a)ḷavu _eṉṟu _aṟiyātaṉa; pēykkaṇattōṭu
_iṇaṅki niṉṟu _āṭiṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*4
_āṟu _oṉṟiya camayaṅkaḷiṉ _av_avarkku _ap poruḷkaḷ
vēṟu _oṉṟu _ilātaṉa; viṇṇōr matippaṉa; mikku _uvamaṉ
māṟu _oṉṟu _ilātaṉa; maṇṇoṭu viṇṇakam māyntiṭiṉum
_īṟu _oṉṟu _ilātaṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*5
_arakkartam muppuram _ampu _oṉṟiṉāl _aṭal _aṅkiyiṉvāyk
karakka muṉ vaitikattērmicai niṉṟaṉa; kaṭṭu_uruvam
parakka veṅkāṉ_iṭai vēṭu_uru _āyiṉa; palpatitōṟu
_irakka naṭantaṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*6
kīṇṭum kiḷarntum poṉ kēḻal muṉ tēṭiṉa; kēṭu paṭā
_āṇṭum palapala_ūḻiyum _āyiṉa; _āraṇattiṉ
vēṇṭum poruḷkaḷ viḷaṅka niṉṟu _āṭiṉa; mēvu cilampu
_īṇṭum kaḻaliṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*7
pōṟṟum takaiyaṉa; pollā muyalakaṉ kōpap puṉmai
_āṟṟum takaiyaṉa; _āṟucamayattavar_avarait
tēṟṟum takaiyaṉa; tēṟiya toṇṭaraic cenneṟikkē
_ēṟṟum takaiyaṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*8
payam, puṉmai, cērtaru pāvam, tavirppaṉa; pārppatitaṉ
kuyam poṉmai mā malar_ākak kulāviṉa; kūṭa _oṇṇāc
cayampu _eṉṟē, taku tāṇu _eṉṟē, caturvētaṅkaḷ niṉṟu
_iyampum kaḻaliṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*9
_ayaṉ, neṭumāl, _intiraṉ, cantirātittar, _amarar_ellām
cayacaya _eṉṟu muppōtum paṇivaṉa; taṇkaṭal cūḻ
viyalnilam muṟṟukkum viṇṇukkum nākar viyalnakarkkum
_iyaparam _āvaṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

*10
tarukkiya takkaṉtaṉ vēḷvi takarttaṉa; tāmaraippōtu,
_urukkiya cempoṉ, _uvamaṉ _ilātaṉa; _oṇ kayilai
nerukkiya vāḷ _arakkaṉ talaipattum nerittu, _avaṉtaṉ
_irukku _iyalpu_āyiṉa---_iṉṉamparāṉtaṉ _iṇai_aṭiyē.

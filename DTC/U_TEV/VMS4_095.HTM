vāṉ coṭṭac coṭṭa niṉṉu aṭṭum vaḷarmatiyōṭu
in addition to the crescent which sheds its rays, pouring in the sky its moon-light.
ayalē tēṉcoṭṭac coṭṭaniṉṟu aṭṭum tiruk koṉṟai ceṉṉi vaittīr
you placed on your head the beautiful koṉṟi from which honeys flows, dripping by drops, by its side.
māṉ peṭṭai nōkkimaṇāḷīr
the husband of Umi whose looks are like those of the female deer! (maṇāḷīr the nominative of address of maṇāḷar)
maṇinīr miḻalaiyuḷḷīr
you who are in miḻali which was water like sapphire.
ummai nāṉ caṭṭa maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you entirely
eṉṉai kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.

antamum ātiyum āki niṉṟīr
you are the end and beginning of all things.
aṇṭam 1. eṇticaiyum pantamum vīṭum parappukiṉṟīr
you spread in the eight directions of all the worlds, bondage, and liberation from the cycle of birth and death.
pacu ēṟṟu ukantīr
you rejoiced in riding on a bull.
ventaḻal ōmpum miḻalai uḷḷīr
you dwell in miḻali where brahmins mintin and guard the three fires.
eṉṉait teṉticaikkē untiṭum pōtu maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you when the god of death pushes me to the southern direction.
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully
[[Variant reading: 1. eṇticaikkum ]]

alaikkiṉṟanīr, nilam, kāṟṟu, aṉal amparam āki niṉṟīr
you are pervading the moving water, the earth, the wind, the fire, and the sky.
kalaikkaṉṟucērum karattīr
you hold in your hand a young male deer.
kalaipporuḷ āki niṉṟīr
you are the real meaning of all the arts.
vilakku iṉṟi nalkum miḻalaiyuḷīr
Civaṉ in miḻali who gives everything to everybody without exception.
meyyilkaiyoṭu kāl kulaikkiṉṟu nummai maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you when my arms and legs do not move.
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.
[[kulaikkiṉṟu is the used in the sense of kulaittāl;
nāraṇaṉ taṉ vāṭāta malaraṭik kīḻvaikkavē vakukkiṉṟu.... urait tīrāy eṉatu uṭalē,āmakiḻntu kantavai mēykkiṉṟu uṉṉōṭu acurarkaḷ talaip peyil evaṉ kol -(tiruvāimoḻi, 1-4-9,10-3-7);
pōkiṉṟa māmukilē poṟkayilai veṟpaḷavum ēkiṉṟu.... eṉṉuḷḷuṟu nōy viṇṇappam cey (kayili pāti kāḷatti pāti antāti, 75); this is an adverbial participle ending in kiṉṟu this is found in works later than caṅkam literature;
toḷḷāyira nāḻi celaviṭuvatum celavu muṭṭukiṉṟē aiññāḻi ari taṇṭappaṭuvatu (Travancore Archeological series, kollam Civaṉ temple, latter half of the 11th century A.D.)]]

tīttoḻilāṉ talai tīyil iṭṭu
throwing into the fire the head of (takkaṉ) who performed a scarifice and mintined the fire.
ceytavēḷvi ceṟṟīr
you destroyed the sacrifice that he performed.
pēyttoḻilāṭṭiyaip peṟṟuṭaiyīr
you begot a lady who reigned over the activities of a pēy;
[[PP: pēcattakāteṉap pēy erutum keruccāḷiyum eṉṟu; ēcattakumpaṭi ēṟuvatē imaiyāta mukkaṭ kūcattakum toḻil nuṅkaiyum nuntaiyum, nīyumintat, tēcattavar toḻu nāraippatiyuḷ civakkaḷiṟē
(tirunāriyūrppiḷḷiyār īraṭtimaṇimāli, 4);]]
[[PP: ēṟiyacīr vīraṇakkuṭi ēntiḻaikkum iruntēṉ, nāṟiya pūntārkkumaraṟkum muṉṉiṉai

[[PP: vētakaccintai virumpiyavaṉtillai ampalattu, mētakak kōyil koṇṭōy cēyavaṉ vīraṇakkuṭivāyp, pōtakap pōrvaip poṟitanta talaimakaṉē

[[PP: perhaps she is vīri who is mentioned in vēlārkai vīriyai muṉpaṭaittār pōlum

vēyt toḻilāḷar miḻalai uḷḷīr
Civaṉ who is in the miḻali where the brahmins who carry a trident staff of bamboo!
[[nūlēkarakam mukkōl maṇaiyē, āyum kālai antaṇarkku uriya (tolkāppiyam, marapiyal,71)]]
vikki añcu eḻuttum ōttu oḻintu ummai marakkiṉum
even if I forget you when I hiccup and cease chanting the mantiram consisting of five letters.
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.
[[Hiccup will occur at the time of death; it means that death is near;
nācceṟṟu vikkuḷmēl vārāmuṉ nalviṉai, mēṟ ceṉṟu ceyyappaṭum (kuṟaḷ, 336)]]

tōḷpaṭṭa nākamum cūlamum cuṟṟiyum
having adorned himself with the cobra that lies on the shoulder, and the trident.
pattimaiyāl mēṟpaṭṭa antaṇar vīḻiyum eṉṉaiyum vēṟuuṭaiyīr
you have me and the shrine vīḻi in which brahmins who are superior by their piety, in a different way (He dwells in the high place of vīḻimiḻali as well as in the low place of mind without any difference is the idea)
nāṭpaṭṭu vantu piṟantēṉ, iṟakka
I was reborn very late in life only to die (embracing civism is mentioned as rebirth)
namaṉ tamartam kōṭpaṭṭu nummai maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you being cught in the noose of the messengers of the god of death.
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.

kaṇṭiyil paṭṭa kaḻuttu uṭaiyīr
you have a string of uruttirākkam beads in your neck.
karikāṭṭil iṭṭa paṇṭiyil paṭṭa pari kalattīr
you have an eating bowl of the skull of the body which was abandoned in the charred cremation ground, as an eating plate (paṇṭi the body; the whole stands for the part.)
pativīḻi koṇṭīr
you had vīḻi as your place.
uṇṭiyil, paṭṭiṉi(yil), nōyil, uṟakkattil, aivar koṇṭiyil paṭṭu ummai maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you being cught in eating, fasting, disease, sleep and the plunder of my intellect by the five sense organs.
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.

tōṟṟam kaṇṭāṉ ciram oṉṟu koṇṭīr
you removed one head of the creator (Piramaṉ) and had it in your hand.
tūya veḷḷerutu oṉṟu ēṟṟam koṇṭīr
you rode on a spotless white bull.
vīḻi miḻalai irukkai koṇṭīr
you desired as your abode viḻimiḻali
eṉmēl cīṟṟam koṇṭu
being angry, with me.
civantu ōr pācattāl vīciyaveṅkūṟṟam kaṇṭu
seeing the cruel god of death who threw on me the angry noose.
ummai maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.

cuḻippaṭṭa kaṅkaiyum tiṅkaḷum cūṭic cokkam payiṉṟīr
you practised cokkam (a dance of 108 poses) wearing on your head kaṅki which has whirlpools, and a crescent
paḻippaṭṭa pāmpu araip paṟṟu uṭaiyīr
you wear in your waist a cobra which has reproach attached to it.
paṭartīpparuka viḻippaṭṭa kāmaṉaiviṭṭīr
you let the spreading fire to consume kamaṉ who appeared before you.
miḻalaiuḷḷīr
Civaṉ who is in miḻali!
piṟavic cuḻippaṭṭu nummai maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you being cught in the whirlpool of birth.
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.

piḷḷaiyil paṭṭa piṟai muṭiyīr
Civaṉ who wears on his head a young crescent!
maṟai ōtavallīr
who is capable of chanting the vētams.
veḷḷaiyilpaṭṭatu ōr nīṟṟīr
you have smeared yourself with the white holy ash.
viri nīr miḻalai uḷḷīr
you are in miḻali which has expanse of water.
aivar naḷḷaiyil paṭṭu nakku araippikka
cught in the middle of the five sense organs which lugh at me and cuse my life to be ground by sufferings.
namaṉtamartam koḷḷaiyil paṭṭu maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you cught in the plunder of the messengers of the god of death,
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.

kaṟṟukkoṇṭu arakkaṉ kayilaiyaippaṟṟiya kaiyum meyyum neṟukkeṉṟu iṟacceṟṟa cēvaṭiyāl
with the red feet which destroyed the hands which cught hold of the mountin, kayili, of the arakkaṉ with implacable hatred, and his body to break with a snapping sound. (neṟukkeṉṟu momatopoiea)
kūṟṟai nīṟuceytīr
you killed the god of a death.
veṟikkoṉṟai mālai muṭiyīr
you who wear on your head a garland of fragrant koṉṟi flowers!
iṟakkiṉṟu nummai maṟakkiṉum
even if I forget you when I am dying.
eṉṉaik kuṟik koḷmiṉ
guard me carefully.

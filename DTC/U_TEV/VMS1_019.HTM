[[see: 3:84]]

{1:19}__1+
{$} piṟai aṇi caṭai muṭiyiṭai perukiya puṉal uṭaiyavaṉ
the god has water (of Kaṅki) swelling in the matted hir coiled into a crown on which he wears a crescent.
iṟai
he who pervades all things.
iṟai aṇivaḷai iṇaimulaiyavaḷ iṇaivaṉatu eḻil uṭai iṭavakai
the place of great beauty of the god who is intimately united with a lady whose breasts are comparable only to each other and who wears bangles on the forearm.
kaṟai aṇi poḻil, niṟaivayal aṇi kaḻumalam amarkaṉal uruviṉaṉ
he who has a form of fire, who is in Kaḻumalam which is made beautiful by dark parks and fields full of crops.
naṟai aṇi malar naṟuvirai pulku nalam mali kaḻal (1) toḻal maruvum
(People of this world!) meditate upon worshipping the feet of the Lord which abounds in granting bliss and have in them the good fragrance of flowers with honey.
[[Variant reading: (1) toḻa]]
{1:19}__2+
{$} (people of this world!)
piṇipaṭu kaṭal piṟavikaḷ aṟal eḷitu
it is easy to cut the births which are like the ocean and bind the soul with illness.
atu perukiya tirai uḷatu
it has increasing waves of sufferings.
aṇipaṭu kaḻumalam iṉitu amar aṉal uruviṉaṉ
God who has the form of fire and who happily stays in beautiful Kaḻumalam.
avircaṭai micai taṇipaṭu katir iḷamati puṉaivaṉai
and adorns on his glittering matted hir a crescent of cool rays.
[[taṇi coolness; puṉalāṭun takai miku pōrk kaṇ (paripāṭal, 6-2-29)]]
umai talaivaṉai
and the husband of Umi.
niṟa maṇipaṭu kaṟai miṭaṟaṉai nalimali kaḻal iṇai toḻal maruvum
meditate upon worshipping the feet which grant abundant bliss, of the god who has a blue neck resembling the precious stone, sapphire.
{1:19}__3+
{$} vari uṟu puli ataḷ uṭaiyiṉaṉ
the god wears as a dhoti of the tiger's skin having a large number of stripes.
vaḷar piṟai oḷikiḷarkatirpotiviriyuṟucaṭai (iṟaivaṉ)
the god of spreading matted hir which encloses in it the bright rays of the waxing crescent.
virai purai poḻil, viḻavu oli mali kaḻumalam amar eri uṟu niṟa iṟaivaṉatu aṭi
the feet of the Lord who pervades everything, who has a form like fire and who is in Kaḻumalam which has superior fragrant parks and has bustle during festivals.
iravoṭu pakal paravuvar tamatu eri uṟuviṉai ceṟikatirmuṉai iruḷkeṭa naṉi niṉaiveytumatē
meditating upon (those feet) always is the thing to be done, by those and who prise them day and night, wishing their burning sins which inflict suffering to vanish like darkness before the sun.
{1:19}__4+
{$} kulamatipuṉai, koṭiyiṭai poruḷtarupaṭukaḷiṟṟiṉatu uriputai uṭaliṉaṉ
the god has a person which he covers with the skin of an elephant he killed, and which ragged with it many things among the creepers of the forest, and who adorns themself with the superior crescent.
maṉaikuṭa vayiṟu uṭaiyaṉa cilavarukuṟaḷ paṭaiyuṭaiyavaṉ
has an army of a few pūtams which have bellies like pots used in the houses.
mali kaṉaikaṭal aṭai kaḻumalam amar katir matiyiṉaṉ atir kaḻalkaḷē, viṉaikeṭa, maṉa niṉaivatu muṭika eṉiṉ naṉitoḻutu eḻu
(my mind!)arise worshipping always the feet wearing tinkling anklets of the god who has the sun and moon as his eyes and who is in Kaḻumalam which is reached by the roaring sea where waves are in plenty, if you want to destroy the sins and to bring to fruition your thoughts
[[keṭa is used in the optative mood; cf; nivantōṅku veṇkuṭai maṇṇakam niḻaṟceya (manimēkali, 22-14).]]
{1:19}__5+
{$} nalakalai maruviya puṟavu aṇitaru kaḻumalam iṉitu amar talaivaṉ
the Lord who resides happily in Kaḻumalam which is made beautiful by small jungles where beautiful stags are living.
talaimati, puṉal, viṭa aravu ivai talaimaiyatu oru caṭaiyiṭai uṭaṉ nilai maruva ōr iṭam aruḷiṉaṉ
he granted a place in the middle of the superior matted hirs for the crescent having the first phase, water, and poisonous cobra to live together.
niḻal maḻuviṉoṭu aḻal kaṇaiyiṉaṉ
he has a burning arrow along with a glittering battle-axe.
malai maruviya cilaitaṉiṉ (1) matil eriyuṇa maṉamaruviṉaṉ
he simply thought the forts to be consumed by five, with the help of a mountin converted into a bow.
[[Variant reading: (1) matiḷ]]
{1:19}__6+
{$} varaiporutu iḻi aruvikaḷpala paruku oru kaṭalvarimaṇaliṭai, karai poru tirai oli keḻumiya kaḻumalam amarkaṉal uruviṉaṉ
the Lord having the form of fire who resides in Kaḻumalam where the sound of the waves that dash upon the shore in the sand having lines near the sea which absorbs into it many streams that descend having dashed aginst hills.
arai poru puli ataḷ uṭaiyiṉaṉ aṭi iṇai toḻa
to worship the feet of the Lord who has a dress of a tiger's skin which is wound round the waist.
aruviṉai eṉum urai(yum) poṭipaṭa uṟutuyar keṭa uyar ulaku eytal orutalaimaiyē
it is an excellence to reach the superior world of heaven, when even the word irressistible actions, will crumble like powder and great sufferings will be destroyed.
{1:19}__7+
{$} naṟa niṟaitalaitaṉil
on his head which is full of honey.
mutiruṟu katir vaḷar iḷamati caṭaiyaṉai
who has matted hirs on which there is the waxing crescent of rays which are getting brighter everyday.
utir uṟu mayir piṇai tavirtacaiyuṭaipuli ataḷiṭai, iruḷkaṭi cuṭar oḷikeḻumiya (aṭikaḷ)
the god who has plenty of brightness which dispels darkness, and who wears on his waist a tiger's skin which has flesh but has the hirs which should drop off but are united with it without doing so.
kaḻumalam amar, maḻumali paṭai, atiruṟukaḻal, aṭikaḷatu aṭitoḻum aṟivualatu aṟivu aṟiyamē
we do not know any other knowledge except that of worshipping the feet of the god who is Kaḻumalam, who has a flourshing battle-axe, and anklets which resound like thunder.
{1:19}__8+
{$} kaṭal eṉa niṟam neṭumuṭiyavaṉ aṭutiṟal teṟa aṭicaraṇ eṉa aṭal niṟai paṭai aruḷiya pukaḻ aravu araiyiṉaṉ
God who ties a cobra on the waist and has the fame of granting a sword full of strength, when the killing strength of the arakkaṉ of tall crown who had a dark complexion like the sea, was destroyed, after he prayed, your feet are my refuge (It would be more appropriate if the reading were kaṭalaṉa)
aṇikiḷar piṟai, viṭa niṟai miṭaṟu uṭaiyavaṉ, viṭai uṭaiyavaṉ umaiyuṭaṉ uṟaipati
the city where the god who has a beautiful crescent, a neck full of poison and a bull, resides with Umi.
kaṭal maṟukuṭai uyar kaḻumalaviyaṉ nakaratē
is the expansive city of great Kaḻumalam which has streets as broad as the sea.
[[kaṭal maṟukuṭai uyar kaḻumalam: Kaḻumalam which rose above at the time of deluge when the sea was whirling.]]
{1:19}__9+
{$} koḻumalar uṟaipati uṭaiyavaṉ neṭiyavaṉ eṉa ivarkaḷum avaṉ viḻumaiyai yaḷavu aṟikilar iṟai (yum)
both Piramaṉ who has the fertile lotus flower as his abode and the tall Māl, could not know even a little of the measure of his greatness
[[viḻumai is a rare word]]
virai puṇar poḻil aṇi, viḻavu amar kaḻumalam amarkaṉal uruviṉaṉ aṭiiṇai toḻumavar
those who worship the feet of the Lord who has the form of fire, who is in Kaḻumalam where festivals are conducted and which is made beautiful by fragrant parks.
aruviṉai nilavakaitaṉil eḻumaiyum ila
the actions which could not be got rid of otherwise will not be with them during the seven births in the different worlds.
imaiyavar viyaṉ ulakam eḷitu
the extensive world of the celestials who do not wink is very easy to obtin.
{1:19}__10+
{$} (1) amaivaṉa tuvar iḻukiya tukil aṇi uṭaiyiṉar amaṇ uruvarkaḷ oruporuḷ eṉum camayamum, aṟavuraikaḷum calaneṟiyaṉa
the religious instructions and the religions are not at all religion, of both the buddhists who dress in robes smeared in the myrtle dye which is fitting them, and the naked jins, are deceptive paths.
imaiyavar toḻutu eḻu kaḻumalam amar iṟaivaṉatu aṭipuravuvartamai viṉai namaiyala
actions good and bad, will not trouble those who prise the feet of the Lord in Kaḻumalam which the celestials who do not wink worship and then rise.
nalaṉ aṭaitaliṉ uyarneṟi naṇukuvarkaḷ
will approach the superior path by getting all good things.
[[Variant reading: (1) amaiyaṉa]]
{1:19}__11+
{$} perukiya tamiḻ virakiṉaṉ mali peyaravaṉ (kaḻumalam)
composed on Kaḻumalam in which (ñāṉacampantaṉ) who had great proficency in Tamiḻ and increasing fame.
uṟai piṇar tiraiyoṭu karukiya niṟavirikaṭal aṭai kaḻumalam uṟaiviṭam eṉa naṉi perukiya civaṉ aṭi paraviya piṇaimoḻiyaṉa orupatum
the ten verses of devotion which prise the feet of Civaṉ who grew very great, who chose Kaḻumalam as his abode which is reached by the expansive sea of black colour and has rolling rough waves with water particles.
uṭaṉ maruviya maṉam uṭaiyavar
those who memorize all the verse.
mati uṭaiyavar
and have good sense.
viti uṭaiyavarkaḷ
are fortunate in having good luck.

Tēvāram school for ōtuvar-s at Dharmapuram (tarumapuram) ātīṉam

In february 2000, this school is having 1 teacher (Mr caṅkaranārāyaṇaṉ) and 12 students who follow a 5 years syllabus (see below).

From the left to the right: 1. piracaṉṉā (5th year, age 21), 2. parvatinātaṉ (4th year, age 19), 3. centil (2nd year, age 17), 4. caṅkaraṉ (2nd year, age 17), 5. vikkiṉēṣ (1st year, age 12).

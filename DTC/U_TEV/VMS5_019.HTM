{$}
taḷarum kōḷ aravattoṭu taṇ mati vaḷarum kōla vaḷar caṭaiyārkku iṭam
the shrine of Civaṉ who has a growing and beautiful caṭi on which the cool crescent and a cobra which bends its body and swallows it prey
kiḷarum pēricaik kiṉṉaram pāṭṭu aṟākkaḷarum kārk kaṭampūrk karakkōyil
is the Karakkōyil in the village which has Kaṭampu trees which blossoms in winter and which is black in colour and where the musical instrument, Kiṉṉoram, producing music at a high pitch, never ceases.
[[Kaṭampūr is the name of the place; Karakkōyil is a special type of temple;
This verse is found as the 7th verse in the next decade, with slight-changes in words. It should be checked with palm-leaf manuscripts]]
{5:19}__2+
{$}
pulaṉ aintoṭu velavalāṉ
Civaṉ is able to conquer the knowledge of the five senses and other enemies
vētamum colavalāṉ
he is able to chant also the vētam
cuḻalum taṭumāṟṟamum alavalāṉ
He is naturally free from confustion due to ignorance and unsteadiness
maṉai ārnta meṉ tōḷiyaik kalavalāṉ kaṭam pūrkkarak kōyilē
Karakkōyil in Kaṭampūr is the shrine of Civaṉ who is united with a lady of soft shoulders who stays in his house.
{5:19}__3+
{$}
poy toḻātu
without becoming slaves to things unreal (getting rid of attachments to things of the world)
puli uriyōṉ paṇi ceytu eḻuvār paṇi ceytu eḻā
rising from sleep doing services to devotees who rise from sleep doing service to the god who wears a tiger's skin
(1) vaitu eḻātu, eḻuvār avar eḷka
to make those who rise from sleep and indulge in abuse about Civaṉ, feel ashamed
nīr karakkōyil kai teḻā eḻumiṉ
you rise in esteem by worshipping with your hands the Karakkōyil
[[Variant reading: (1) vai toḻāteḻu]]
{5:19}__4+
{$}
neñcamē
my mind!
paṇṇiṉāl muṉam pāṭal atu ceytē
singing songs with melodies in his presence
eṇ ilār eyil mūṉṟum eritta mukkaṇṇiṉāṉ kaṭam pūrk karakkōyil tuṇ eṉā maṉattāl toḻu
you worship the Karakkōyil in Kaṭampūr belonging to the god of three eyes, who burnt all the three forts of the acurar who had no good thoughts
{5:19}__5+
{$}
cuṉaiyuḷ nīlamalar aṉa kaṇṭattaṉ
Civaṉ whose neck is like the blue nelumbo flowers blossoming in the tank
puṉaiyum poṉ niṟak koṉṟai puricaṭaikkaṉaiyum paiṅkaḻalāṉ karak kōyilai niṉaiyum uḷḷattavar viṉai nīṅkumē
the Karmams of those who think about the Karakkōyil in Kaṭampūr of the god who has a jingling anklet and a cati which desires koṉṟi flowers of golden colour which adorn it, will leave them.
{5:19}__6+
{$}
kuṇaṅkaḷ colliyum
dilating upon the good qualities of the true devotees and explining them to others
kuṟṟaṅkaḷ pēciyum
and talking about their own faults
aṉpuṭaiyār elām vaṇaṅki vāḻttuvar
all those who have love towards god will bow before him and sing his prises
vaṇaṅki
bowing before him
vāl malar koṇṭu
with defectless flowers
aṭi vaikalum kaṇaṅkaḷ pōṟṟu icaikkum, karakkōyil
in Karakkōyil the pūtakaṇaṅkaḷ will sing the prise of the feet, dily
[[kaṇaṅkaḷ this may also mean the eighteen kinds of celestial beings; icaikkum : finite verb]]

paṇṇiṉ ār maṟai pal pala pūcaṉai maṇṇiṉār ceyvataṉṟiyum
in addition to different kinds of pūcaṉi such as is done according to vedic rules, civite injuctions and devotion, chanting vētam with melody done by the people of this world
vaikalum
dily
kaṭampūrkkarak kōyil
in Karakkōyil in Kaṭampūr
viṇṇiṉārkaḷ viyakkappaṭuvaṉa kaṇṇiṉār
the inhabitants of heaven think of performing acts which are the wonder of the people of this world, and do them

aṅkai ār aḻal ēnti niṉṟu āṭalaṉ maṅkai pāṭa makiḻntu uṭaṉ
when the young lady is by his side, and sings, Civaṉ dances holding a fire of unbearable heat in his palm
vārcaṭaik kaṅkaiyāṉ uṟaiyum karakkōyilait, tamkaiyāl toḻuvār viṉai cāyum
the Karmams of those who worship with their hands Karakkoyil where Civaṉ who has on his long caṭi Kaṅki, resides, will get decreased gardually, losing their vehemence.
{5:19}__9+
{$}
nam kaṭampaṉaip peṟṟavaḷ paṅkiṉaṉ
Civaṉ who has as a half Umi who gave birth to our Kaṭampaṉ (kaṭampaṉ Murukaṉ got this name as he is partial towards the flowers of common Kaṭampa)
teṉ kaṭampait tirukkarak kōyilāṉ
who dwells in the holy temple of Karakkōyil in beautiful Katampi (Kaṭampūr)
taṉ kaṭaṉ aṭiyēṉaiyum tāṅkutal
It is his duty to protect even me who is low and who is his devotee
eṉ kaṭaṉ paṇi ceytu kiṭappatē
my duty is to follow the path shown by his grace without ever thinking with egotism even in a small measure in a small measure It is my action

pāṟkaṭaṟ paṇam koḷ pāmpu aṇaiyāṉoṭum maṇam kamaḻ malart tāmarai yāṉavaṉ piṇaṅkum pēraḻal em perumāṟku iṭam
the shrine of our Lord who assumed the form of a big fire which was the cuse for disagreement between Māl who slept on a bed of a serpent having a hood in the ocean of milk, and Piramaṉ who was seated in fragrant lotus flower, as to their respective superiority
kaṇaṅkaḷ pōṟṟu icaikkum karakkōyil
is Karakkōyil where the pūtakaṇam sing the prise of Civaṉ

nāl añcu tōḷ uṭaiyāṉ talaivaraikkaṇ araikka ūṉṟi aruḷ ceyta īcaṉār
Civaṉ who pressed down to grind under the mountin the heads of Irāvaṇaṉ of twenty shoulders, and bestowed his grace on him afterwards.
tiraikkum taṇpuṉal cūḻ karakkōyilai uraikkum uḷḷattavar viṉai ōyumē
The Karmams of those who have the mind to prise the Karakkōyil surrounded by cool water in which waves are rolling, will be destroyed

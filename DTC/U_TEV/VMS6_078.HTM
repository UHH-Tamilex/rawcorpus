{6:78}__1+
{$} oṉṟā ulaku aṉaittum āṉār (tāmē)
Civaṉ has all the worlds in his pervasion and is omnipresent in them, without any one being his equal
[[oṉṟāka
without equal]]
[[PP_EXT: oṉṟā ulakattu uyarnta pukaḻ
(Kuṟaḷ 233)]]
[[NOTE_TEXT: If the reading were oṉṟāy ulaku aṉaittum āṉār, it would make better sense; the meaning will be Being themself the sole thing existing before creation, Civaṉ manifested himself as all the things in the world;]]
[[PP: oṉṟāy muḷaittu eḻuntu ettaṉaiyō kavaṭuviṭṭu
(tiruvācakam, tirukkōttumpi 8)]]
[[PP: oṉṟāy arumpip palavāy virintu ivvulaku eṅkumāy niṉṟāḷ aṉaittaiyum nīṅkiniṟpāḷ
(Apirāmi Antāti, 56)]]
ūḻitōṟu ūḻi(tōṟu) uyarntār (tāmē)
became great in every aeon by granting salvation in those aeons.
[[tōṟu should be added to the second ūḻi also.]]
niṉṟu eṅkum āki nimirntār(tāmē)
Being in a state without change, he spread himself everywhere.
nīr vaḷitī ākācam āṉār (tāmē)
became the water, the air, the fire and the sky.
koṉṟu āṭum kūṟṟai utaittār (tāmē)
kicked the god of death who wanders killing beings.
kōlappaḻaṉai uṭaiyār (tāmē)
has as his abode beautiful paḻaṉi (paḻiyaṉūr)
ceṉṟu āṭum tīrttaṅkaḷ āṉār (tāmē)
is himself the holy water to which people go and have a dip.
tiruvālaṅkāṭu uṟaiyum celvar (tāmē)
is Civaṉ who has the wealth of grace and dwells in tiruvālaṅkāṭu
[[All the ..... in this and the following verses are expletives.]]

{6:78}__4+
{$} nāṟupūṅkoṉṟai muṭiyār (tāmē)
Civaṉ has on his head brilliant and fragrant koṉṟi flowers.
nāṉ maṟaiyōṭu āṟu aṅkam ceṉṟār (tāmē)
revealed the six aṅkams along with the four vētams.
māṟu ilā mēṉi uṭaiyār (tāmē)
has a holy body which does not show signs of change as age advances such as wrinkles grey hirs etc. (māṟu may also mean oppu (comparison) there is no other form to be compared to his form)
(1) māmatiyam ceñcaṭaimēl vaittār (tāmē)
placed the great crescent on the red caṭi.
pāṟiṉār veṇtalaiyil uṇṭār (tāmē)
ate from the white skull of those who died.
paḻaṉai patiyā uṭaiyār (tāmē)
see 2nd verse.
tēṟiṉār cittattu iruntār (tāmē)
dwelt permanently in the minds of those who are clear in their minds about his being the sole cuse.
tiruvālaṅ .... tāmē
see 1st verse.

{4:23}__1+
{$} paramaṉē
the supreme one!
parama yōkī
the foremost among yokīs!
muttaṉē
one who is naturally free from all bonds!
mutalvā
the first cuse!
tillai ampalattu āṭukiṉṟa attā
My father who dances in the ampalam in Tilli!
ettiṉāl patticeykēṉ
In what way can I do devotion?
eṉṉai nī ikaḻa vēṇṭā
Please do not be negligent towards me.
uṉ āṭal kāṇpāṉ aṭiyaṉēṉ vanta āṟu
the reason for coming here is to witness your dance.
{4:23}__2+
{$} kāmpu āṉa tōḷi paṅkā
one who has on his half a lady with shoulders like bamboos.
oruttarāl aṟiya oṇṇāttiruvuru uṭaiya cōti
the effulgent light which has a divinely beautiful form which cannot be known by anybody.
[[oruttarālum: in this word um has been dropped]]
karuttaṉāyp pāṭa māṭṭēṉ
I won't sing your prise intentionally
tiruttamāntillai taṉṉuḷ tikaḻnta ciṟṟampalattē niruttam kāṇa vēṇṭi
wishing to see the dance you perform in shining Ciṟṟampalam in Tilli having sacred waters.
nāṉ nērpaṭa vanta āṟē
I came just in front of you.
{4:23}__3+
{$} kēṭṭilēṉ
I have not inquired about you
kiḷaipiriyēṉ
I have not parted from my relations who are you devotee
kēṭkumā kēṭṭiyākil
if you listen to my words in the proper way they should be listened to attentively.
neñciṉuḷḷē naṭuppaṭa niṉṟaṉpātam nāṭṭiṉēṉ
I established your feet in the centre of my mind.
māṭṭiya nīr vāṉai pāyum malkuciṟṟampalattē
in the Ciṟṟampalam which is increasing in fame, and where the veḷi (scabbard fish) leaps in places where there is water.
kūṭṭam ām kuvimulaiyāḷ kūṭa nī āṭum āṟē
I came here (to see) your dance combining with the lady of conical breasts and who is joined with you
{4:23}__4+
{$} ceṟivuṭaiya aṭimai ceyya
to do to you with restrint a servant's duties
cintaiyait tikaippiyātē
without confusing my mind
entai! nī aruḷicceyyāy
My father! you command me out of your grace.
yātu nāṉ ceyvatu
what is there which I can do of my own accord?
eṉṉē
what a wonder it is!
centiyār vēḷvi ōvāt tillaicciṟṟampalattē
in the ciṟṟampalam in Tilli where sacrifices with red fires are ceaselessly going on.
[[centi: centī; tī has been shortened into ti to be in conformity with rhyme (etukai)]]
antiyum pakalum āṭa aṭi iṇaialacum kollō
will not both your feet feel exhusted when you dance night and day?
{4:23}__5+
{$} nāḷum kiṇṭavā tirintu
wandering according to my experience dily.
karuttiṉāl niṉṟaṉpātam koṇṭiruntu
Having in my idea your feet.
pāṭi āṭikkuṟippiṉālē kūṭuvaṉ
I will join with your suggestion by singing and dancing.
vaṇṭu paṇpāṭum cōlai malkuciṟṟampalattē
in the thriving ciṟṟampalam surrounded by groves where bees sing melodies.
iṟaivā! eṇticaiyōrum ētta nī āṭumāṟē
Lord! your dance to be prised by people from all the eight directions!
{4:23}__6+
{$} pārttiruntu aṭiyaṉēṉ nāṉ pāṭi āṭipparavuvaṉ
I who am your servant shall worship you singing your prise and dancing, fixing my gaze upon you.
mūrttiyē eṉpaṉ
I shall call you as one who is manifested in a form.
uṉṉai mūvariṉ mutalvaṉ eṉpaṉ
I shall say that you are the chief among the trinity.
ēttuvār iṭarkaḷ tīrppāy
you will remove the troubles of those who prise you.
kūttā! uṉ kūttukkāṇpāṉ kūṭa nāṉ vantavāṟē
Dancer! I came with other people to see your dance.
{4:23}__7+
{$} poyyiṉait taviraviṭṭu
Having completely given up falsehood.
puṟam alā aṭimai ceyya
to perform internal duties pertining to a servant.
aiya! nī aruḷicceyyāy
Of great one! order me.
ātiyē ātimūrtti
primordial one! the one first to assume a form
[[ceyyāy: used in the affirmative sense]]
vaiyakan taṉṉiṉ mikka malku ciṟṟampalattē
in the flourishing Ciṟṟampalam which is superior to other shrines in this world.
parama! paiyaniṉāṭal kāṇpāṉ nāṉ vantavāṟē
I came slowly to see your dance! Supreme one!
[[Variant reading: nuṉṉāṭal]]
{4:23}__8+
{$} maṉattiṉārtikaittu
my mind having become confused
māṇ palā neṟikaḷ mēlē kaṉaippar
will proceed in eager haste on worthless ways.
eṉ ceykēṉ
what shall I do?
kaṟai aṇi kaṇṭattāṉē
one who has a neck which is adorned with poison
[[In line maṉattiṉār is spoken as a rational being to ridicule it]]
tiṉaittaṉai vētam kuṉṉāttillaic ciṟṟampalattē
in ciṟṟampalam in Tilli where Vētam does not decrease even by a small measure.
niṉ ilayam aṉaittum kāṇpāṉ aṭiyaṉēṉ vantavāṟē
your servant, came to see all your dances set to measure of time.
{4:23}__9+
{$} neñciṉait tūymai ceytu niṉaikkumā niṉaippiyātē
without making me to think of you in the proper way having purified my mind
nī vañcamē ceyti
you deceive me
vāṉavar talaivaṉē
the chief of those who are released from the cycle of birth and death.
mañcu aṭai cōlaittillai malku ciṟṟampalattē
in the ciṟṟampalam flourishing in Tilli surrounded by groves where clouds collect together.
am colāḷ kāṇa
to be witnessed by the lady who speaks beautiful words.
aḻaka
the beautiful god!
niṉṟu nī āṭumāṟu
you stand and dance (I came to see that)
{4:23}__10+
{$} maṇṇuṇṭa mālavaṉum
Tirumāl who swallowed the worlds
malarmicaimaṉṉiṉāṉum
one who stayed permanently in the (lotus) flower.
viṇṇuṇṭa tiruvuruvam virumpiṉār kāṇamāṭṭār
were unable to see your divine form which enveloped the sky, though they desired it.
tiṇṇuṇṭa tiruvē
permanent wealth!
tillaic ciṟṟampalattē
in the ciṟṟampalam in Tilli
parama! paṇṇuṇṭa pāṭalōṭum nī āṭumāṟu
I came to see your dance, singing songs set to melody!
[[In this tenth stanza there is no reference to Rēvaṇaṉ]]

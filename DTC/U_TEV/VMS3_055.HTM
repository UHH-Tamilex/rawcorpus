{3:55}__1+
{$} virai ār koṉṟaiyiṉāy
Civaṉ who wears koṉṟi flowers abundant in fragrance!
viṭam uṇṭa miṭaṟṟiṉāṉē
who has neck which consumed the poison!
urai ār palpukaḻāy
who has many forms of fame which are spoken by devotees!
umai naṅkai or paṅku uṭaiyāy
who has Umi; a lady of distinction, as a half!
tirai ār teṇ kaṭal cūḻ tiruvāṉmiyūr uṟaiyum araiyā
the King who dwells in tiruvāṉmiyūr, surrounded by the clear sea full of waves. (araiyā-araiyā= aracā)
uṉṉai allāl eṉatu ātaravu aṭaiyātu
my love will not reach other gods except you.
{3:55}__2+
{$} iṭi ār ēṟu uṭaiyāy
Civaṉ who has a bull that bellows like the thunder.
imaiyōrtam maṇimuṭiyāy
who is seated on the beautiful heads of the celestials who do not wink!
koṭi ār māmatiyoṭu aravam malark koṉṟaiyiṉāy
who wears a great crescent of orderliness, a cobra and koṉṟi flowers.
[[koṭi-oḻuṅku-;
āykarumpiṉ koṭikkūrai'' - mīṉ cuṭu pukaiyiṉ pulavu nāṟu neṭuṅ koṭi- (puṟam, 22-15, 52-9).]]
ceṭi ār mātavi cūḻ tiruvāṉmiyūr uṟaiyum aṭikēḷ
the deity who dwells in tiruvāṉmiyūr surrounded by the shrubbery of the common delight of the woods!
uṉṉai ..... ātaravē
see 1st verse.
{3:55}__3+
{$} kai ār veṇ maḻuvā
Civaṉ who holds in his hand a white battle-axe!
kaṉal pōl tirumēṉiyaṉē
who has a holy body as red as the fire.
mai ār oṇkaṇ nallāḷ umaiyāḷ vaḷar mārpiṉaṉē
who has a chest on which Umayal, a good natured lady of bright eyes coated with collyrium is flourishing
cey ār ceṅkayal pāy tiruvāṉmiyūr uṟaiyum aiyā
the master who dwells in tiruvāṉmiyūr where in the fields red carp fish leap.
uṉṉai .... ātaravē
see 1st verse.
{3:55}__4+
{$} poṉ pōlum caṭaimēl puṉal tāṅkiya puṇṇiyaṉē
Civaṉ, the embodiment of all virtuous acts, who carried on his cati resembling gold, water!
miṉ pōlum purinūl viṭai ēṟiya vētiyaṉē
the brahmin who rode on a bull and wears a sacred thread of three strands which are bright like lightning!
teṉpāl vaiyam ellām tikaḻum tiruvāṉmitaṉṉil aṉpā
the companion in tiruvāṉmi which is eminent throughout the southern part of the tamiḻ nāṭu!
uṉṉai .... ātaravē
see 1st verse.
{3:55}__5+
{$} kaṇ ārum nutalāy
Civaṉ who has on his forehead an eye.
katir cūḻ oḷi mēṉiyiṉ mēl eṇ ār veṇpoṭi nīṟu aṇivāy
who adorns with the white powder of sacred ash which is highly esteemed, on his brilliant body which has rays surrounding it.
1. eḻil vār poḻil cūḻ tiṇ ār vaṇpuricait tiruvāṉmiyūr uṟaiyum aṇṇā
who is in the relation of an elder brother and who dwells in tiruvāṉmiyūr which has very strong wall of surrounded by beautiful and tall gardens.
uṉṉai .... ātaravē
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: 1. eḻilār]]
{3:55}__6+
{$} nītī
Civaṉ who is the right conduct!
niṉṉai allāl neṟiyātum niṉaintu aṟiyēṉ
I do not know even thinking of other paths except thinking of you only.
nāṉ maṟaikaḷ ōtī
who chanted the four vētams.
maṟaiyōṉ talai oṉṟiṉaiyum cētī
who cut off with the nil only one head of Piramaṉ who had knowledge of the vētams.
cētam illāt tiruvāṉmiyūr uṟaiyum ātī
the first cuse who dwells in tiruvāṉmiyūr which does not know damage.
uṉṉai .... ātaravē
see 1st verse.
{3:55}__7+
{$} vāṉ ār māmaticēr caṭaiyāy
Civaṉ who has on his cati a big crescent which shines in the sky!
varai pōla varum kāṉ ār āṉaiyiṉ tōl urittāy
you flayed the skin of an elephant which lives in the forest and came towards you as a mountin.
kaṟai māmiṭaṟṟāy
who has a big neck in which there is poison.
tēṉ ār cōlaikaḷ cūḻ tiruvāṉmiyūr uṟaiyum āṉāy
my elephant which dwells in tiruvāṉmiyūr surrounded by gardens full of honey
[[āṉāy : is the vocative case of āṉai;
ēṉ āṉāy eṉ āṉāy eṉṉiṉ allāl (tiruneṭuntaṅtakam, 10) and its ancient commentary;
eṉ āṉāy eṉ āṉāy eṉṉiṉ allāl ēḻaiyēṉ ēṉ colli ēttu kēṉē (nāvukkaracar, potu, taṉittiruttāṅṭakam (1) 7);
kātaṟ col pāvai yāṉaiyeṉa oppumai karutātu kātal paṟṟi varuvaṉ (tolkappiyam, Kiḷaviyākkam, 56, cāṉāvariyar);
eṉ pōr yāṉai vantīka īṅku (Kalittoki, 86-9-10);
āṉai : a term of endearment, see Kaṭavūr vīraṭṭam, nāvukkaracar, tirukkuṟuntoki]]
uṉṉai .... ātaravē
see 1st verse.
{3:55}__8+
{$} poṟivāy nākaṇaiyāṉoṭu pūmicai mēyavaṉum
Māl who sleeps on a bed of a serpent which has spots and Piramaṉ who is seated on a (lotus) flower.
neṟi ār nīḷkaḻal mēlmuṭi kāṇpu aritāyavaṉē
Civaṉ whose feet wearing long Kaḻal with curves and the head which could not be seen beyond the heaven by both those!
ceṟivu ār māmaticūḻ tiruvāṉmiyūr uṟaiyum aṟivē
the embodiment of knowledge which dwells in tiruvāṉmiyūr surrounded by great walls of fortification which are thick.
uṉṉai .... ātaravē
see 1st verse.
{3:55}__9+
{$} kuṇṭu āṭum camaṇar koṭuñcākkiyar eṉṟu ivarkaḷ kaṇṭār kāraṇaṅkaḷ karutātavarkaḷ pēca niṉṟāy
you stood unaffected when these people, the camaṇar who practise low things and the cruel cākkiyar though they know somethings do not know the cuses for those, spoke slander about you.
tiṇ tēr vīti (atu) ār tiruvāṉmiyūr uṟaiyum aṇṭā
the god who dwells in tiruvāṉmiyūr in whose streets the strong temple-cars are drawn during festivals!
uṉṉai .... ātaravē
see 1st verse
{3:55}__10+
{$} ceṉṟār tam iṭartīr tiruvāṉmiyūr ataṉmēl
on Tiruvāṉmiyūr which removes the sufferings of people who go there.
kaṉṟu ārum kamukiṉ vayal cūḻtaru kāḻitaṉil naṉṟāṉa pukaḻāṉ ñāṉacampantaṉ urai kuṉṟātu ēttavallār
those who are able to prise, without any deficiency, with the help of the verses composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ whose fame is sweet to hear, and who is a native of Kāḻi surrounded by fields and gardens where tender areca-palms are growing.
koṭu valviṉaipōy aṟumē
their cruel and irresistible acts will leave them and completely perish.

  • talam: tiru veṇkāṭu
  • (()): tiruk kuṟuntokai
  • VMS: Vol.1, p.138

*1
paṇ kāṭṭip paṭi_āya taṉ pattarkkuk
kaṇ kāṭṭi, kaṇṇil niṉṟa maṇi _okkum,
peṇ kāṭṭip piṟaic ceṉṉi vaittāṉ tiru
veṇkāṭṭai _aṭaintu _uy(m), maṭa neñcamē!

*2
koḷḷi ventaḻal vīci niṉṟu _āṭuvār,
_oḷḷiya(k) kaṇam cūḻ _umaipaṅkaṉār,
veḷḷiyaṉ, kariyaṉ, pacu _ēṟiya
teḷḷiyaṉ, tiru veṇkāṭu _aṭai, neñcē!

*3
_ūṉ nōkkum(m) _iṉpam vēṇṭi _uḻalātē,
vāṉ nōkkum vaḻi_āvatu niṉmiṉō!
tāṉ nōkkum taṉ _aṭiyavar nāviṉil-
tēṉ nōkkum tiru veṇkāṭu _aṭai, neñcē!

*4
paru veṇkōṭṭup paiṅkaṇ matavēḻattiṉ
_uruvam kāṭṭi niṉṟāṉ, _umai _añcavē;
peruveṇkāṭṭu _iṟaivaṉ(ṉ) _uṟaiyum(m) _iṭam
tiru veṇkāṭu _aṭaintu _uy(m), maṭa neñcamē!

*5
paṟṟu _avaṉ, kaṅkai pāmpu mati_uṭaṉ
_uṟṟa vaṉ caṭaiyāṉ, _uyar ñāṉaṅkaḷ
kaṟṟavaṉ, kayavar puram _ōr _ampāl
ceṟṟavaṉ, tiru veṇkāṭu _aṭai, neñcē!

*6
kūṭiṉāṉ, _umaiyāḷ _orupākam_āy;
vēṭaṉāy vicayaṟku _aruḷceytavaṉ;
cēṭaṉār; civaṉār; cintai mēya veṇ-
kāṭaṉār; _aṭiyē _aṭai, neñcamē!

*7
tarittavaṉ, kaṅkai, pāmpu, mati_uṭaṉ;
puritta puṉcaṭaiyāṉ; kayavar puram
_erittavaṉ; maṟaināṉkiṉōṭu _āṟu_aṅkam
virittavaṉ(ṉ), _uṟai veṇkāṭu _aṭai, neñcē!

*8
paṭṭam _iṇṭai _avaikoṭu pattarkaḷ
ciṭṭaṉ, _āti _eṉṟu(c)cintaiceyyavē,
naṭṭamūrtti---ñāṉaccuṭar_āy niṉṟa
_aṭṭamūrttitaṉ---veṇkāṭu _aṭai, neñcē!

*9
_ēṉavēṭattiṉāṉum piramaṉum
tāṉ _a(v)vēṭam muṉ tāḻntu _aṟikiṉṟilā
ñāṉavēṭaṉ, vicayaṟku _aruḷceyyum
kāṉa vēṭaṉtaṉ, veṇkāṭu _aṭai, neñcē!

*10
pālai _āṭuvar, palmaṟai _ōtuvar,
cēlai _āṭiya kaṇ _umai paṅkaṉār,
vēlai _ār viṭam _uṇṭa veṇkāṭarkku
mālai_āvatu māṇṭavar _aṅkamē.

*11
_irā vaṇam ceya, mā mati paṟṟu _av, _ai-
yirā vaṇam(m) _uṭaiyāṉtaṉai _uḷkumiṉ!
_irāvaṇaṉtaṉai _ūṉṟi _aruḷceyta
_irāvaṇaṉ tiru veṇkāṭu _aṭaimiṉē!

• patikam: 1:19
  • talam: tiruk kaḻumalam (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: naṭṭapāṭi
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: VOL.2 ,P.368

*1
piṟi _aṇi paṭarcaṭimuṭi_iṭip perukiya puṉal _uṭiyavaṉ; niṟi
_iṟi _aṇi vaḷi, _iṇimuliyavaḷ, _iṇivaṉatu _eḻil _uṭi _iṭa vaki---
kaṟi _aṇi poḻil niṟi vayal _aṇi---kaḻumalam _amar kaṉal_uruviṉaṉ;
naṟi _aṇi malar naṟuviri pulku nalam mali kaḻal toḻal maruvumē.

*2
piṇi paṭu kaṭalpiṟavikaḷ _aṟal _eḷitu; _uḷatu _atu perukiya tiri;
_aṇi paṭu kaḻumalam _iṉitu _amar _aṉal_uruviṉaṉ, _avircaṭimici
taṇipaṭu katir vaḷar _iḷamati puṉivaṉi, _umitalivaṉi, niṟa
maṇi paṭu kaṟimiṭaṟaṉi, nalam mali kaḻal_iṇi toḻal maruvumē!

*3
vari _uṟu puli_ataḷ_uṭiyiṉaṉ, vaḷarpiṟi _oḷi kiḷar katir poti
viri_uṟu caṭi,---viri puḻi poḻil viḻavu _oli mali---kaḻumalam _amar
_eri _uṟu niṟa _iṟivaṉatu _aṭi _iravoṭu pakal paravuvartamatu
_eri_uṟu viṉi, ceṟikatir muṉi _iruḷ keṭa, naṉi niṉivu _eytumatē.

*4
viṉi keṭa maṉaniṉivu_atu muṭika _eṉiṉ, naṉi toḻutu _eḻu---kulamati
puṉi koṭi_iṭi poruḷ taru paṭu kaḷiṟiṉatu _uri puti _uṭaliṉaṉ,
maṉi kuṭavayiṟu _uṭiyaṉacila varu kuṟaḷpaṭi _uṭiyavaṉ, mali
kaṉikaṭal _aṭi kaḻumalam _amar katir matiyiṉaṉ, _atirkaḻalkaḷē!

*5
talimati, puṉal, viṭa_aravu, _ivi talimiyatu _oru caṭi_iṭi _uṭaṉ-
nili maruva _or _iṭam _aruḷiṉaṉ; niḻal maḻuviṉoṭu _aḻalkaṇiyiṉaṉ;
mali maruviya cilitaṉil matil _eriyuṇa maṉam maruviṉaṉ---nala
kali maruviya puṟavu _aṇitaru kaḻumalam _iṉitu _amar talivaṉē.

*6
vari porutu _iḻi _aruvikaḷpala paruku _orukaṭal vari maṇal_iṭi,
kari poru tiri _oli keḻumiya kaḻumalam _amar kaṉal_uruviṉaṉ;
_ari poru puli_ataḷ_uṭiyiṉaṉ; _aṭi_iṇi toḻa, _aruviṉi _eṉum
_uri poṭipaṭa _uṟu tuyar keṭa, _uyar_ulaku _eytal _orutalimiyē.

*7
mutir_uṟu katir vaḷar _iḷamati caṭiyaṉi, naṟa niṟi talitaṉil;
_utir_uṟu mayir piṇi tavir taci _uṭi puli_ataḷ _iṭi; _iruḷ kaṭi
katir _uṟu cuṭar_oḷi keḻumiya kaḻumalam _amar---maḻu mali paṭi,
_atir_uṟu kaḻal,---_aṭikaḷatu _aṭi toḻum _aṟivu_alatu _aṟivu _aṟiyamē.

*8
kaṭal _eṉa niṟa neṭumuṭiyavaṉ _aṭutiṟal teṟa, _aṭi caraṇ! _eṉa,
_aṭal niṟi paṭi _aruḷiya pukaḻ _aravu _ariyiṉaṉ, _aṇi kiḷar piṟi,
viṭam niṟi miṭaṟu _uṭiyavaṉ, viricaṭiyavaṉ, viṭi _uṭiyavaṉ, _umi-
_uṭaṉ _uṟi pati---kaṭal maṟuku _uṭi _uyar kaḻumala viyalnakar_atē.

*9
koḻu malar _uṟi pati _uṭiyavaṉ, neṭiyavaṉ, _eṉa _ivarkaḷum, _avaṉ
viḻumiyi _aḷavu _aṟikilar, _iṟi; viri puṇar poḻil _aṇi viḻavu _amar
kaḻumalam _amar kaṉal_uruviṉaṉ _aṭi_iṇi toḻumavar _aruviṉi
_eḻumiyum _ila, nila vakitaṉil; _eḷitu, _imiyavar viyaṉ_ulakamē.

*10
_amivaṉa tuvar _iḻukiya tukil _aṇi _uṭiyiṉar, _amaṇ_uruvarkaḷ,
camiyamum, _oru poruḷ _eṉum_avi,---calaneṟiyaṉa,---_aṟa_urikaḷum;
_imiyavar toḻu kaḻumalam _amar _iṟivaṉatu _aṭi paravuvartami
namiyala viṉi; nalaṉ _aṭitalil _uyarneṟi naṉi naṇukuvarkaḷē!

*11
perukiya tamiḻvirakiṉaṉ, mali peyaravaṉ,---_uṟi piṇar tiriyoṭu
karukiya niṟa virikaṭal _aṭi---kaḻumalam _uṟivu_iṭam _eṉa naṉi
perukiya civaṉ_aṭi paraviya, piṇi moḻiyaṉa, _orupatum _uṭaṉ-
maruviya maṉam _uṭiyavar mati_uṭiyavar; viti_uṭiyavarkaḷē.

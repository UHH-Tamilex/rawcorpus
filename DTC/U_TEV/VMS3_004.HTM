{3:4}__1+
{$} iṭariṉum
when I am undergoing sufferings.
taḷiriṉum
when I am depressed in spirits.
eṉatu uṟunōy toṭariṉum
when my big karmam-s follow me
uṉakaḻaltoḻutu eḻuvēṉ
I shall wake up from sleep worshipping your feet.
kaṭaltaṉil amutoṭu kalanta nañcai miṭaṟiṉil aṭakkiya vētiyaṉē
Civaṉ who gave out the Vētam-s and who controlled the poison which was mixed with the nectar in the ocean (of milk) and made it stay in the neck!
āvaṭutuṟai araṉē
Civaṉ in Āvaṭutuṟai!
emakku īvatu oṉṟu illaiyēl
if there is nothing to give to us.
ituvē emai āḷumāṟu
is this way you admit us as your protege?
atuvō uṉatu iṉṉaruḷ
is your sweet grace like that?
{3:4}__2+
{$} tāḻ iḷam taṭam puṉal tayaṅku ceṉṉip pōḻiḷamati vaitta puṇṇiyaṉē
one who is the embodiment of all virtuous acts and who placed on your head on which expansive and suining soft water and stays crescent!
vāḻiṉum cāviṉum varuntiṉum pōy vīḻiṉum
when I am alive, when I am dead, when I suffer and when I fall down walking.
uṉakaḻal viṭuvēṉ allēṉ
I would not leave the support of your feet.
ituvō ... araṉē
[[see 1st verse]]
{3:4}__3+
{$} puṉal virinaṟuṅkoṉṟaip pōtu aṇinta kaṉaleri aṉalpulku kaiyavaṉē
Civaṉ who holds in his hand a burning and scorching fire, and wears flowers of fragrant and blossmed koṉṟai, and water!
ammāṉ
my father!
nampā
one in whom anyone can repose confidence!
naṉaviṉum kaṉaviṉum maṉaviṉum vaḻipaṭal maṟavēṉ
I would not forget worship in my mind whether I am in the waking state or sleeping state.
ituvō ... araṉē
see 1st verse
{3:4}__4+
{$} kaimmalku varicilaikkaṇai oṉṟiṉāl mummatiḷ erieḻa muṉintavaṉē
Civaṉ who out of anger, discharged single arrow from the bow with bands which flourishes to cause fire to rise in the three cities.
tummaloṭu aruntuyar tōṉṟiṭiṉum ammalar aṭialāl (1) araṟṟātu eṉ nā
even if I smeeze, and sufferings arise, my tongue will not cry about anything else except the beautiful feet which are like (lotus) flowers.

ituvō ... araṉē:

{3:4}__5+
{$} koy aṇi naṟumalar kulāya ceṉṉi mai aṇi miṭaṟu uṭai(ya) maṟaiyavaṉē
Civaṉ who revealed the vetams, who wears conspicuously on the head the fragrant and beautiful flowers placked just then and who has a neck made beautiful by the poison!
kaiyatu vīḻiṉum kaḻivuṟiṉum
if the thing kept in the hand slips down and leaves me completely.
ceykaḻal aṭialāl cintai ceyvēṉ
I won't think of anything else except the feet wearing armours of exquisite workmanship
ituvō ... araṉē
same as 1st verse.
{3:4}__6+
{$} aintalai aravukoṇṭu araikku acaitta, cantaveṇpoṭi aṇi caṅkaraṉē
Civaṉ who does good, who smears beautiful white holy ash and who has tied in the waist a cobra of five hoods!
entāy
my father!
ventuyar tōṉṟi ōr veru uṟiṉum uṉaṭialāl eṉ nā ēttātu
my tongue will not praise any other thing except your feet, even when I am frightened by a cruel suffering that happens all on a sudden.
ituvō ... araṉē
same as for 1st verse.

{$} oppuṭai oruvaṉai uru aḻiyaappaṭi aḻal eḻa viḻittavaṉē
Civaṉ who opened his frontal eye for the fire to consume and destroy the form of the one who is compared to the beauty of others in that manner! (appaṭi it can be only imagined but cannot be described in so many words).
[[ampalattuḷ āṅku eṉṉai āṇṭu koṇṭu (Kōvaiyar, 19); āṅku eṉṟār āṇṭavāṟu coltaṟku arumaiyāṉ (do.Pērāciriyar's commentary).]]

{3:4}__8+
{$} ēr uṭai maṇimuṭi irāvaṇaṉai āriṭar paṭavarai aṭarttavaṉē
Civaṉ who pressed down Irāvaṇaṉ who wore a crown set with beautiful gems , under the mountain, to undergo unbearable pain.
pēriṭar peruki ōr piṇivariṉum
even though I suffer from a disease, when a great affliction is increasing.

{3:4}__9+
{$} kaṇṇaṉum kaṭikamaḻtāmaraimēl aṇṇalum aḷappu aritāyavaṉē
Civaṉ whose limits could not be fathomed by both Kaṇṇaṉ and Piramaṉ who is seated on a fragrant lotus flower! Kampar makes a pun on the word Kaṇṇaṉ;
[[cf. vīti vāyc celkiṉṟāṉ pōl viḻittu imaiyātu niṉṟa, mātarāḷ kaṇkaḷūṭē, vāvu māṉtēril celvāṉ, yātiṉum uyarntōr taṉṉai yāvarkkum kaṇṇaṉeṉṟē ōtiyapeyarkkut tāṉē uṟuporuḷ uṇarttiviṭṭāṉ (pālakāṇtam, ulāviyaṟpaṭalam, 6) as Kaṇṇaṉ is an incarnation of Māl, that word is used to denote Māl himself).]]
uṇṇiṉum, pacippiṉum, uṟaṅkiṉum
when I am eating, when I am hungry and when I am asleep.
eṉ nā oṇmalar aṭialāl uraiyātu
my tongue will not speak about anything else except your shining feet which can be compared to lotus flowers.
ituvō ... araṉē
see 1st verse.

{$} alaipuṉal āvaṭutuṟai amarnta, ilainuṉaivēṟpaṭai em iṟaiyai
about our master who has a trident of sharp blades and who is staying with pleasure in āvaṭutūṟai where water is always moving.
nalammiku ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa vilaiyuṭai aruntamiḻ mālai vallār
those who are able to recite the highly valuable garland of tamiḻ verse composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ of increasing good qualities.
viṉaiyāyiṉa nīṅkippōy
having gone freed from the bondage of Karmams.
viṇṇavar viyaṉ (1) ulakam nilaiyākamuṉ ēṟuvar
will ascend into the world of the celestials as permanent residents.
nilamicai nilaiilar

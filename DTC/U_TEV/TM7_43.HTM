• patikam: [7:43]
  • talam: Mutukuṉṟam
  • paṇ: kollikkuvāṇam
  • VMS: Vol.12, p.163

*1
nañci, _iṭi _iṉṟu nāḷi _eṉṟu _ummi naccuvār
tuñciyiṭṭāl piṉṉic ceyvatu _eṉ? _aṭikēḷ, colīr!
pañci _iṭap puṭṭil kīṟumō? paṇiyīr, _aruḷ!---
muñci_iṭic caṅkam _ārkkum cīr mutukuṉṟarē!

*2
_ērik kaṉakakkamalammalar _aṉṉa cēvaṭi
_ūr_ittaṉiyum tirintakkāl _avi nōmkolō?---
vārikkaṇ ceṉṟu vaḷikkappaṭṭu, varuntip pōy,
mūrik kaḷiṟu muḻakku _aṟā mutukuṉṟarē!

*3
toṇṭarkaḷ pāṭa, viṇṇōrkaḷ _ētta _uḻitarvīr!
paṇṭu _akamtōṟum palikkuc celvatu pāṉmiyē?---
kaṇṭakar vāḷikaḷ villikaḷ puṟaṅkākkum cīr
moṇṭa ki vēḷvi muḻakku _aṟā mutukuṉṟarē!

*4
_iḷippu _aṟiyīr; _immi _ēttuvārkku _ammi ceyvatu _eṉ?
viḷippu _aṟiyāta veṅ kālaṉi _uyir vīṭṭiṉīr;---
_aḷip piriyā _aravu _alkulāḷoṭu kaṅki cēr
muḷippiṟic ceṉṉic caṭimuṭi mutukuṉṟarē!

*5
_āṭi _acintu _aṭiyārum nīrum _akamtoṟum
pāṭip paṭitta poruḷ_elām _umiyāḷukkō?---
māṭam, matil, _aṇi kōpuram, maṇi maṇṭapam,
mūṭi mukil tavaḻ cōli cūḻ mutukuṉṟarē!

*6
_iḻi vaḷar nuṇ_iṭi maṅkiyoṭu _iṭukāṭṭu_iṭik
kuḻi vaḷar kātukaḷ mōta niṉṟu kuṉippatē?---
maḻi vaḷarum neṭuṅkōṭṭu_iṭi matayāṉikaḷ,
muḻi vaḷar _āḷi, muḻakku _aṟā mutukuṉṟarē!

*7
ceṉṟu _il_iṭic ceṭi nāy kurikka, ceṭiccikaḷ
maṉṟil_iṭip pali tērap pōvatu vāḻkkiyē?---
kuṉṟil_iṭik kaḷiṟu _āḷi koḷḷa, kuṟattikaḷ
muṉṟil_iṭip piṭi kaṉṟu _iṭum mutukuṉṟarē!

*8
_anti tirintu _aṭiyārum nīrum _akamtoṟum
cantikaḷtōṟum palikkuc celvatu takkatē?---
manti kaṭuvaṉukku _uṇ paḻam nāṭi malippuṟam
munti _aṭi toḻa niṉṟa cīr mutukuṉṟarē!

*9
ceṭṭu niṉ kātali _ūrkaḷtōṟum _aṟam ceya,
_aṭṭumiṉ, cilpalikku! _eṉṟu _akamkaṭi niṟpatē?
paṭṭi veḷ_ēṟu _ukantu _ēṟuvīr! paricu _eṉkolō?---
muṭṭi _aṭi toḻa niṉṟa cīr mutukuṉṟarē!

*10
_etticiyum tirintu _ēṟṟakkāl piṟar _eṉ colār?
pattiyiṉāl _iṭuvār_iṭip pali koṇmiṉō!---
_etticiyum tiri _ēṟa mōtik karikaḷmēl
mutti muttāṟu valamceyum mutukuṉṟarē!

*11
mutti muttāṟu valamceyum mutukuṉṟarip
pittaṉ _oppāṉ _aṭittoṇṭaṉ---_ūraṉ---pitaṟṟu _ivi
tattūvañāṉikaḷ _āyiṉār taṭumāṟṟu_ilār,
_ettavattōrkaḷum, _ēttuvārkku _iṭar _illiyē.

  • talam: tiru _irumpūḷi
  • paṇ: _intaḷam
  • (()): viṉā_uri

*1
cīr _ār kaḻalē toḻuvīr! _itu ceppīr---
vār _ār muli maṅkiyoṭum(m) _uṭaṉ_āki,
_ēr _ār _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
kār _ār kaṭalnañcu _amutu_uṇṭa karuttē?

*2
toḻal _ār kaḻalē toḻu toṇṭarkaḷ collīr---
kuḻal _ār moḻik kōlvaḷiyōṭu _uṭaṉ_āki,
_eḻil _ār _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
kaḻaltāṉ karikāṉ_iṭi _āṭu karuttē?

*3
_aṉpāl _aṭi kitoḻuvīr! _aṟivīrē---
miṉ pōl maruṅkul maṭavāḷoṭu mēvi,
_iṉpu_āy _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
poṉ pōl caṭiyil puṉal vitta poruḷē?

*4
naccit toḻuvīrkaḷ! namakku _itu collīr---
kaccip poli kāmakkoṭi_uṭaṉ kūṭi,
_iccittu, _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
_uccittaliyil pali koṇṭu _uḻal _ūṇē?

*5
cuṟṟu _ārntu _aṭiyē toḻuvīr! _itu collīr---
nal-tāḻkuḻal naṅkiyoṭum(m) _uṭaṉ_āki,
_eṟṟē _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
puṟṟu _āṭu_aravōṭu pūṇṭa poruḷē?

*6
tōṭu _ār malar tūyt toḻu toṇṭarkaḷ! collīr---
cēṭu _ār kuḻal cēyiḻiyōṭu _uṭaṉ_āki,
_īṭu_āy _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
kāṭu _ār kaṭu vēṭuvaṉ _āṉa karuttē?

*7
_orukkum maṉattu _aṉpar_uḷḷīr! _itu collīr---
paruk ki matavēḻam _urittu, _umiyōṭum
_irukki _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
_arakkaṉ(ṉ) _uram tīrttu _aruḷ _ākkiya_āṟē?

*8
tuyar_āyiṉa nīṅkit toḻum toṇṭar! collīr---
kayal _ār karuṅkaṇṇiyoṭum(m) _uṭaṉ_āki,
_iyalpu_āy _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
muyalvār _iruvarkku _eri _ākiya moympē?

*9
tuṇi nalmalar tūyt toḻum toṇṭarkaḷ! collīr---
paṇimeṉmulip pārppatiyōṭu _uṭaṉ_āki,
_iṇi _il(l) _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ
_aṇivu _il camaṇ cākkiyam _ākkiya_āṟē?

*10
_enti _irumpūḷi _iṭamkoṇṭa _īcaṉ,
cantam payil caṇpiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ
centaṇtamiḻ ceppiya pattu_ivi vallār,
pantam(m) _aṟuttu _ōṅkuvar, pāṉmiyiṉālē.

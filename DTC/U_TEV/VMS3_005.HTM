[[This decade got its name of īraṭi mēl vaippu as there are two more lines in addition to the first two lines which have a different rhyme; except in the 11th verse]]

{3:5}__1+
{$} takkaṉ vēḷvi takarttavaṉ
the Lord who destroyed the sacrifice performed by Takkaṉ
pūntarāy, mikka cemmai vimalaṉ kaḻal
the sacred feet of the Lord who is naturally free from bondage, which can grant the highest bliss of freedom from birth and death.
ceṉṟu cintaiyil vaikka, meykkati, naṉṟu atu ākiya nampaṉ tāṉē
the good god in whom one can have confidence is the true and final beatitude when people go to pūntarāi and meditate on his feet.
{3:5}__2+
{$} puḷ iṉam pukaḻ pōṟṟiya pūntarāy
in pūntarāi where the flocks of birds sing the praise of that place
veḷḷam tāṅkiya vikirtaṉ aṭi toḻa
to worship the feet of the Lord who is different from the world and who bore on his head the floods.
ñālattil uyarvār uḷkum naṉṉeṟi
the good way that is always in the thought of people who want to raise themselves in e the world.
mūlam ākiya mutalvaṉtāṉē
is the cheif who is the origin of all things.
{3:5}__3+
{$} vēntarāy ulaku āḷa viruppuṟiṉ
People of this world ! if you desire to rule the world as kings.
pūntarāy nakar mēyavaṉ poṟ kaḻal
the beautiful feet of the Lord who is in the city of pūntarāi (will grant your wish.)
nītiyāl niṉaintu ētti uḷkiṭa viṉai āṉa cātiyā
the acts both good and bad will not do their mischief if you think of, and meditate on, those feet with discipline.
{3:5}__4+
{$} pūcurar toḻutu ēttiya pūntarāy īcaṉ cēvaṭi ētti iṟaiñciṭa
when one worships the lotus red feet of Civaṉ in pūntarāi which is worshipped and praised by brahmins (pūcurar : brahmins are called by this name as they were considered to be tēvar in this world)
cintai nōy (avai) tīra, intu vārcaṭai emiṟai nalkiṭum
our Lord who wears the crescent on his long catai will bestow his grace to remove the mental afflictions.
{3:5}__5+
{$} polinta eṉpu aṇi mēṉiyaṉ pūntarāy malinta puntiyarāki vaṇaṅkiṭa
if people adore pūntarāi, with their minds full of devotion, belonging to the Lord who adorns his body with shining bones.
num tam mēl viṉai oṭa vīṭucey, entai āya em īcaṉ tāṉē
our Civaṉ will be your trustworthy guide to liberate you from the acts done in previous births and those done in this birth.
{3:5}__6+
{$} pūtam cūḻap polintavaṉ pūntarāy, nātaṉ cēvaṭi nāḷum naviṉṟiṭa
to praise the lotus red feet of the chief of pūntarāi who looks grand when surrounded by putams. (polintavaṉ should be put in apposition with nātaṉ)
naḷir puṉal pulku vārcaṭaip piññakaṉ, iṉpam nāḷtoṟum nalkiṭum
the god who is the destroyer and who has a long caṭai from which cool water is dripping, will grant his grace daily.
{3:5}__7+
{$} puṟṟiṉ nākam aṇintavaṉ pūntarāy paṟṟi vāḻum paramaṉaip pāṭiṭa
if people sing the praises of the supreme god who resides with atttachment to pūntarāi and who adorns himself with cobras living in ant-hills (puṟṟiṉ nākam the underlined word is generic epithet)
cē (atu) ēṟiya celvaṉ tāṉē, pāvam āyiṉa tīrap paṇittiṭum
the god who rides on a bull will order acts which are sinful, to take leave of them.
{3:5}__8+
{$} pōtakattu uri pōrttavaṉ pūntarāy, kātilittāṉ kaḻal viral oṉṟiṉāl, arakkaṉ āṟṟal aḻittu avaṉukku aruḷ 1. perukki niṉṟa em piññakaṉē
our god who is the destroyer granted his grace abundantly to arakkaṉ after destroying his might by a single toe of the feet, and covered himself with the skin of an elephant, is fond of pūntarāi.
[[Variant reading: 1. perukka]]
{3:5}__9+
{$} mattam āṉa iruvar maruva oṇā attaṉ āṉavaṉ mēviya pūntarāy āḷ (atu) āka aṭaintu uymmiṉ
people of this world! save yourselves by reaching pūntarāi desired by the god, as protege of the father, who could not be approached by the two persons (Māl and Piramaṉ) intoxicated with the pride of their power (maruva oṇā have become maruvoṇā)
tāṉē num viṉai māḷumāṟu aruḷ ceyyum
he himself will grant you his grace for your acts to be destroyed, without even yourselves asking for it.
{3:5}__10+
{$} poruttam il camaṇ 1. cākkiyap poy kaṭintu, iruttal ceyta pirāṉ
the Lord who stays permanently after discarding the falsehood of the camaṇar and cākkiyar (buddhists)
imaiyōr toḻap, pūntarāy nakarkōyil koṇṭu, kai ēntu māṉ maṟi em iṟaiyē
is our god pervading everywhere, who holds on his hand a young deer, residing in the temple in pūntarāi to be worshipped by the celestials who do not wink.
[[Variant reading: 1. cākkiyar poy]]
{3:5}__11+
{$} puntiyāl mika nallavar pūntarāy antam il em aṭikaḷai
about our deity who has no end, residing in pūntarāi where very good wise people live.
ñāṉacam, pantaṉ mālai koṇṭu ētti vāḻum
spend your life by praising god with the garland of verses composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ.
[[the full name ñāṉacampantaṉ (ñāṉacampantaṉ) has been split into ñāṉacam, pantaṉ to suit the rhyme;
(ētukai) This is what is called in prosody (vakaiyuḷi); scansion in complete disregard of speech rhythm;
This last verse is also addressed to the people of this world]]

*1
nīṟu _alittatu _ōr mēṉi, nimircaṭi
_āṟu _alikka niṉṟu _āṭum, _amutiṉi;

_ūṟali; kaṇṭukoṇṭatu---_eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*2
pontiyip pukku nīkkap pukuntiṭum
tantiyi, taḻal pōlvatu _ōr mēṉiyi,
cintiyi, teḷivi, teḷi vāyttatu _ōr
_entiyi, kaṇṭukoṇṭatu---_eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*3
veḷḷattār viñciyārkaḷ virumpavē
veḷḷattic caṭi vitta vikirtaṉār,
kaḷḷattik kaḻiya(m) maṉam _oṉṟi niṉṟu
_uḷḷattil, _oḷiyik kaṇṭatu---_eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*4
_ammāṉi,---_amutiṉ _amutē! _eṉṟu---
tammāṉi, tattuvattu _aṭiyār toḻum
cem māṉa(n)niṟam pōlvatu _ōr cintiyuḷ
_emmāṉi, kaṇṭukoṇṭatu, _eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*5
kūṟu _ēṟum(m) _umi pākam _ōrpālarāy,
_āṟu _ēṟum caṭimēl piṟi cūṭuvar,
pāṟu _ēṟum tali _ēntip pala_ilam
_ēṟu _ēṟum _entiyik kaṇṭatu---_eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*6

vaṉneñcam(m)_atu nīṅkutal vallirē?
_eṉ neñcil _īcaṉik kaṇṭatu---_eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*7
veṉṟāṉi, pulaṉ _aintum; _eṉ tīviṉi

_oṉṟāṉi; kaṇṭukoṇṭatu---_eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*8

tiruviṉi, cintiyuḷ civaṉāy niṉṟa
_uruvaṉi, kaṇṭukoṇṭatu---_eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*9

_īcaṉi, kaṇṭukoṇṭatu---_eṉ _uḷḷamē.

*10

*1
_iṭariṉum, taḷariṉum, _eṉatu _uṟu nōy
toṭariṉum, _uṉa kaḻal toḻutu _eḻuvēṉ;---
kaṭaltaṉil _amutoṭu kalanta nañci
miṭaṟiṉil _aṭakkiya vētiyaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*2
vāḻiṉum, cāviṉum, varuntiṉum, pōy
vīḻiṉum, _uṉa kaḻal viṭuvēṉ_allēṉ;---
tāḻ _iḷan taṭampuṉal tayaṅku ceṉṉip
pōḻ_iḷamati vitta puṇṇiyaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*3
naṉaviṉum, kaṉaviṉum, nampā! _uṉṉi,
maṉaviṉum, vaḻipaṭal maṟavēṉ;---_ammāṉ!
puṉal virinaṟuṅkoṉṟippōtu _aṇintu,
kaṉal _eri-_aṉal pulku kiyavaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*4
tummaloṭu _aruntuyar tōṉṟiṭiṉum,
_am malar_aṭi_alāl _araṟṟātu, _eṉ nā;---
kim malku varicilik kaṇi _oṉṟiṉāl
mummatil _eri _eḻa muṉintavaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*5
kiyatu vīḻiṉum, kaḻivu_uṟiṉum,
cey kaḻal _aṭi_alāl cinticeyyēṉ;---
koy _aṇi naṟumalar kulāya ceṉṉi
mi _aṇi miṭaṟu _uṭi maṟiyavaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*6
ventuyar tōṉṟi _ōr veru_uṟiṉum,
_entāy! _uṉ _aṭi_alāl _ēttātu, _eṉ nā;---
_aintali_aravukoṇṭu _arikku _acitta
canta veṇpoṭi _aṇi caṅkaraṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*7
veppoṭu viravi _ōr viṉi variṉum,
_appā! _uṉ _aṭi_alāl _araṟṟātu, _eṉ nā;---
_oppu _uṭi _oruvaṉi _uru _aḻiya
_appaṭi _aḻal _eḻa viḻittavaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*8
pēr _iṭar peruki, _ōr piṇi variṉum,
cīr _uṭik kaḻal_alāl cinticeyyēṉ;---
_ēr _uṭi maṇi muṭi _irāvaṇaṉi
_ār _iṭar paṭa vari _aṭarttavaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*9
_uṇṇiṉum, pacippiṉum, _uṟaṅkiṉum, niṉ
_oṇmalar_aṭi_alāl _uriyātu, _eṉ nā;---
kaṇṇaṉum, kaṭi kamaḻ tāmarimēl
_aṇṇalum, _aḷappu _aritu_āyavaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*10
pittoṭu mayaṅki _ōr piṇi variṉum,
_attā! _uṉ _aṭi_alāl _araṟṟātu, _eṉ nā;---
puttarum camaṇarum puṟaṉ_urikka,
pattarkaṭku _aruḷceytu payiṉṟavaṉē!---
_ituvō _emi _āḷum_āṟu? _īvatu _oṉṟu _emakku _illiyēl,
_atuvō _uṉatu _iṉ_aruḷ?---_āvaṭutuṟi _araṉē!

*11
_ali puṉal _āvaṭutuṟi _amarnta
_ili nuṉi vēlpaṭi _em _iṟiyi,
nalam miku ñāṉacampantaṉ coṉṉa
vili _uṭi _aruntamiḻmāli vallār,
viṉi_āyiṉa nīṅkip pōy, viṇṇavar viyaṉ_ulakam
nili_āka muṉ _ēṟuvar; nilammici nili _ilarē.

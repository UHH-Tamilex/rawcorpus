(0) alai ār puṉal kaṅkai naṅkai kāṇa ampalattil aru naṭṭam āṭi
Performing the rare dance in the hall for the lady of distinction Kaṅki to witness it, who is in the form of water with waves, and who was concealed in his caṭi.
vēṭam tolaiyāta (1) veṉṟiyār
he has the victory of that form which does not become extinct.
niṉṟiyūrum neṭuṅkaḷamum mēvi
dwelling in Niṉṟiyūr and Neṭuṅkaḷam.
viṭaiyai mēṟkoṇṭu
riding upon a bull.
ilai ār paṭaikaiyil ēnti
holding in the hand a weapon of (three) blades (trident).
eṅkum imaiyavarum umaiyavaḷum iṟaiñci ētta
to be prised by falling prostrate by the celestials and Umiyavaḷ, in all places.
malai ār tiraḷ aruvip poṉṉi cūḻnta valañcuḻiyē pukku iṭamā (2) maruviṉārē
Civaṉ stayed as his place entering into Valañcuḻi surrounded by Poṉṉi which is the collection of streams which take their source in the mountin.
[[Variant reading: (1) veṉṟiyarāy (2) maṉṉiṉārē]]

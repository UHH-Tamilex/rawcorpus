{7:37}__1+
{$} kuruku
O cranes!
pāya
as you move by flying
koḻuṅkarumpukaḷ nerinta cāṟu
the juice of the fertile sugar-canes which are crushed by you.
vayal aruku pāyum antaṇ ārūraraip paruku māṟum
enjoying the beautiful of the god in cool and beautiful ārūr where it flows by the side of fields.
paṇintu ēttumāṟum
praising and paying obeisance.
niṉaintu urukumāṟum
and the melting of my heart thinking of him.
ivai uṇarttavallīrkaḷē
are you capable of informing him of these things, on my behalf.
{7:37}__2+
{$} paṟakkum em kiḷḷaikāḷ
our parrots which fly merrily in the sky!
[[em denotes that they were kept as pets]]
pāṭum em pūvaikāḷ
our bush-mynā birds which sing sweet
aṟakkaṇ eṉṉattakum aṭikaḷ ārūrarai maṟakkakillāmaiyum
my state which is incapable of forgetting the deity who is in ārūr and who is fit to be considered as important as the eye for righteous conduct.
vaḷaikaḷ nillāmaiyum
the nature of bangles not staying in their places and slipping off.
uṟakkam illāmaiyum
and sleeplessness.
uṇarttavallīrkaḷō
are you capable of informing him?
{7:37}__3+
{$} cūḻum ōṭic cuḻalum veṇ nāraikāḷ
the white cranes which hover about, running in all surrounding places.
āḷum am poṟkaḻal aṭikaḷ ārūraṟku
to the deity in ārūr whose feet admit devotees as his protege.
vāḻumāṟum
my life existing in the body without leaving it.
vaḷaikaḻalumāṟum
the sleeping off of the bangles.
ūḻum (1) eṉakku māṟum
the nature of fate which never changes, having changed in my case.
uṇarttavallīrkaḷē
are you capable of informing him about these things?
[[Variant reading: (1) māṟum mikai]]
{7:37}__4+
{$} (1) cakravākattu iḷam pēṭaikāḷ
the young females of the cakravākam bird!
cēvalkāḷ
and their male birds!
vakram illāmaiyum
that I do not have crookedness.
vaḷaikaḷ nillāmaiyum
that my bangles are not staying in their position but slip off.
ukram illāmaiyum
and that I have no wreth towards him.
akramaṅkaḷ ceyyum aṭikaḷ ārūrkku uṇartta vallīrkaḷē
are you capable of informing these to the deity in ārūr who commits irregular acts?
[[Variant reading: (1)cakravāḷat]]
{7:37}__5+
{$} ilaikoḷ cōlaittalai irukkum veṇ nāraikāḷ
cranes which are perched upon the gardens which has rich foliage!
kalaikaḷ cōrkiṉṟatum, kaṉavaḷaikaḻaṉṟatum, mulaikaḷpīr koṇṭatum
the loosening of the girdles worn on the waist, the slipping off of heavy bangles and the colour of the breasts growing sallow
(1) alai koḷ cūlappaṭai aṭikaḷ ārūrkku moḻiyavallīrkaḷē
are you capable of informing the deity in Ārūr who holds a trident which is capable of destruction?
[[Variant reading: (1) alaikaḷ]]
{7:37}__6+
{$} vaṇṭukāḷ
Bees!
koṇṭalkāḷ
clouds!
vārmaṇalkurukukāḷ
cranes sitting on the vast stretch of sand!
aṇṭavāṇartoḻum aṭikaḷ ārūraik kaṇṭavāṟum
obtaining vision of the deity in ārūr who is worshipped by the tēvar in heaven
ivai
these things which are.
kāmattīkkaṉaṉṟu erintu mey uṇṭavāṟum
the fire of lust smouldering then burning outwardly and consuming my body.
uṇarttavallīrkaḷē
are you capable of informing him on my behalf.
{7:37}__7+
{$} tēṉ nalam koṇṭatēṉ vaṇṭukāḷ
different varieties of bees such as tēṉ vaṇṭu which have enjoyed the sweetness of honey!
koṇṭalkāḷ
clouds!
pāl nalam koṇṭa empaṇaimulai payantu
my big breast which contain good milk having become sallow.
poṉ ūṉnalam koṇṭatum
the sallow colour having destroyed the fair complexion of my body.
āṉnalam koṇṭa em aṭikaḷ ārūrarkku uṇartta vallīrkaḷē
are you capable of informing the deity in ārūr who bathed with joy in the five products of the cow?
{7:37}__8+
{$} cuṟṟum muṟṟum cuḻaṉṟu uḻalum veṇ nāraikāḷ
the white cranes which whirl on all sides and wander about!
paṟṟumaṟṟu illāmaiyum pāṭu (maṟṟu) iṉmaiyum (1) uṟṟu maṟṟu iṉmaiyum aṟṟam muṟṟappakarntu aṭikaḷ ārūrkku uṇartta vallīrkaḷē
are you capable of informing the deity in ārūr talling him my suffering from beginning to end and also my having no other support, people indulging in slandar about me, and my having no other relations, on my behalf?
[[Variant reading: (1) muṟṟumaṟṟiṉmaiyum moḻiyavallīrkaḷē]]
{7:37}__9+
{$} kuravam nāṟa
when the bottle-flower spreads its fragrance
kuyil vaṇṭu iṉam pāṭa
when the indian cuckoo and swarms of bees are singing and humming.
niṉṟu aravam āṭum poḻil antaṇ ārūraip paravi nāṭu(m) atum, pāṭi nāṭu(m) atum, uruki nāṭu(m) atum uṇartta vallīrkaḷē
(white cranes!) are you capable of informing on my behalf, searching the god in cool and beautiful ārūr where cobras stand erect and dance, searching him by singing his praises and searching him, when the heart melts losing it hardness?
{7:37}__10+
{$} kūṭum aṉṉap peṭaikāḷ kuyil(kāḷ) vaṇṭukāḷ
the female swans which are in the company of their males, indian cuckoos, and bees!
āṭum ampoṟkaḻal aṭikaḷ ārūraip pāṭumāṟum
singing the fame of the deity in ārūr who wears a beautiful golden Kaḻal, and dances.
paṇintu ēttumāṟum
bowing to, and praising, him.
kūṭiūṭumāṟum ivai uṇarttavallīrkaḷē
are you capable of informing these which are having union with him and being sulky with him?
{7:37}__11+
{$} pattarē
pious devotees!
uḷḷam nittamāka(n) niṉaintu
thinking about him daily in your minds.
ēttit toḻum attaṉ
the father who is worshipped by praising.
ampoṉkaḻal aṭikaḷ ārūraicittam vaitta pukaḻcciṅkaṭi appaṉ
the father of ciṅkati who has the fame of thinking always in his mind the deity in ārūr who wears a beautiful golden Kaḻal.
meyppattaṉ ūraṉcoṉṉa pāṭumiṉ
sing the verses composed by Nampi Ārūraṉ who is a real devotee of god.
[[Variant reading: (1)toḻu attaṉ]]

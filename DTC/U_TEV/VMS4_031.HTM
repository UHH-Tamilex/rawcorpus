{$} poruntiya kurampai taṉṉuḷ
in the body in which ninety-six ultimate realities are combined.
(1) poy naṭai celuttukiṉṟīr
you are following a false conduct.
oruttaṉai uṇara māṭṭīr
you have no ability to realise Civaṉ who has no equal.
uḷḷattil koṭumai nīkkīr
you do not remove from your hearts cruel thoughts.
(2) varuttiṉa kaḷiṟutaṉṉai varuttumā varuttavallār karuttiṉil iruppar (pōlum) kaṭavūr vīraṭṭaṉār
Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr vīraṭṭam will dwell in the thoughts of those who can inflict pain to the five senses which gave trouble to them, in the proper way to cause sufferings to them.

Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr virattam
muntu uru iruvarōṭu mūvarum āyiṉārum
assumed the forms of Ayaṉ and Ari, which are prominent and was himself Uruttiraṉ, Makērcaṉ and CatāCivaṉ.
intiraṉōṭu tēvar iruṭikaḷ iṉpam ceyya
when the celestials along with Intiraṉ and sages to make Civaṉ feel happy by worshipping him.
vantu irupatukaḷ tōḷāl eṭuttavaṉ valiyai vāṭṭi
having destroyed the strength of Irāvaṇaṉ who lifted Kayilai having approached it, by his twenty shoulders,
[[irupatukaḷ the plural suffix should be added to tōḷ as tōḷkaḷ]]
kantiruvaṅkaḷ kēṭṭār
and listened to the songs sung by him (and granted his grace)
[[kantaruvar: one of the eighteen groups of celestials who are well-versed in music;
kantaruvam is the proper word; but kantiruvam is also in usage]]

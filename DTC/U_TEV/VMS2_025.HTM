{2:25}__1+
{$} ukali āḻkaṭal ōṅkupār uḷīr
people of this eminent world surrounded by deep sea in which waves rise and roll!
[[ukali - ukaḷi]]
viṉai akaliyā
our sins will disappear without increasing.
allal pōy aṟum
sufferings will go away from us.
ikaliyār puram eytavaṉ uṟai pukali ām nakar pōṟṟi vāḻmiṉ
enjoy a happy life by worshipping the city of pukali where the god who shot an arrow on the enemies' cities, resides.
[[pukali is placed in etukai]]
{2:25}__2+
{$} paṇṇi āḷvatu ōr ēṟṟar
the Lord has a bull made ready to ride, with all adornments.
pāl matik kaṇṇiyār
has as a chaplet the crescent moon as white as milk.
kamaḻ koṉṟai cēr muṭip puṇṇiyaṉ uṟaiyum pukaliyai nalamāṉa vēṇṭil naṇṇumiṉ
People of this world! If you are desirous of good things in this life, go and worship pukali where the Lord who has on his head fragrant koṉṟi flowers.
[[The reading found in printed books is uṟaiyum pukaliyai: I think that should be uṟai pūm pukaliyai; uṟaiyum pukaliyai may be a scribal error; in verse 3, 4, and 9, pūmpukali is found; this is in keeping with the alliteration (mōṉai)]]
{2:25}__3+
{$} vīcumiṉ purai kātal
throw off all low attachments
mētaru pāca valviṉai tīrtta paṇpiṉaṉ
the Lord of such a property as to completely destroy the very strong action of pācam which is eminent by its evil effects.
pūcum nīṟṟiṉaṉ pūm pukaliyaip peritu iṉpamākap pēcumiṉ
People of this world! always talk about the beautiful pukali of the Lord who has smeared sacred ash, to define great pleasure.
[[Variant reading: iṉpamākumē]]
{2:25}__4+
{$} kaṭi koḷ kūviḷam mattam vaittavaṉ
the Lord placed on his head the fragrant leaves of vilvam as well as dhatura flowers which are not fragrant.
paṭi koḷ pāriṭam pēcum pāṉmaiyaṉ
has the property of being spoken highly by the groups of pūtam of grotesque forms.
poṭi koḷ mēṉiyaṉ
has smeared his form with the fine powder of sacred ash.
pūmpukaliyuḷ aṭikaḷai aṭaintu aṉpu ceyyumē
people of this world! Love the god in beautiful pukali with devotion, approaching him.
[[ceyyum has been used as a verb in the imperative mood to denote plural]]
[[Variant reading: aṉpu ceymmiṉē]]
{2:25}__5+
{$} pātattu ār oli palcilampiṉaṉ
the Lord has worn many anklets of tinkling sound that are tied to the feet.
ōtattu ār viṭam uṇṭavaṉ
He drank the poison which was produced in the ocean.
paṭaip pūtattāṉ
has an army of pūtam.
pukalinnakar toḻa ētattārkku iṭam illai eṉpar
Learned people say that if one worships the city of pukali there is no room for suffering
[[ētam is spoken of as a rational being to ridicule it.]]
{2:25}__6+
{$} maṟaiyiṉār
the Lord is the author of the vētam.
olimalku vīṇaiyaṉ
has a vīṇi on which he plays sweet music.
niṟaiyiṉār nimir puṉcaṭaiyaṉ
Has a golden and erect caṭi which is full of water.
[[niṟai the part of malir niṟai meaning water.]]
em poṟaiyiṉāṉ uṟai pūm pukaliyai niṟaiyiṉāl toḻa nēcam ākum
If anyone worships the beautiful pukali with a firm and resolute mind, belonging to the Lord who carries our burden, it will increase piety.
{2:25}__7+
{$} karaviṭai maṉattāraik kāṇkilāṉ
The Lord cannot even condescend to look at people who deceitful ideas in their minds.
iraviṭaip pali koḷḷum em iṟai
our Lord who receives alms at night.
poru viṭai uyarttāṉ
He has given prominence to the form of a fighting a bull by exhibiting it in his flag.
pukaliyaip paraviṭap payil pāvam pāṟum
the crowded sins will get scattered and destroyed if one prises his pukali.
{2:25}__8+
{$} aruppiṉ ār mulai maṅkai paṅkiṉaṉ
the Lord has the young lady of the mountin who has breasts comparable to buds
[[GRAM: arumpu has become aruppu (valittalvikāram)]]
[[The bud may denote lotus or common cung (kōṅku), as they are compared to breasts.]]
viruppiṉāṉ
he has love on his devotees.
arakkaṉ uram cekum poruppiṉāṉ
He destroyed the might of arakkaṉ with the help of the mountin.
poḻil ār pukali ūr iruppiṉāṉ aṭi ētti vāḻttum
people of this world! invoke his blessings by worshipping the feet of the Lord who resides in the city of Pukali which has many parks.
{2:25}__9+
{$} mālum nāṉ mukaṉ tāṉum vār kaḻal cīlamum muṭi tēṭa nīṇṭa eripōlum mēṉiyaṉ
the Lord has a form like fire which grew in length when Māl, and Piramaṉ with four faces searched the nature of the feet and head
pūmpukaliyuḷ pālatu āṭiya paṇpaṉ nallaṉ
the Lord who has good properties and who bathed in milk in the beautiful pukali, is good
[[ALTERNATE: paṇpaṉ allaṉē (is he not the Lord with good qualities?)]]
{2:25}__10+
{$} niṉṟu tuyppavar nīcar col
the words of low people who eat their food standing (jins).
tērar col
the words of the buddhists.
oṉṟatāka vaiyā uṇarviṉuḷ niṉṟavaṉ nikaḻum pukaliyaic ceṉṟu kaitoḻac celvam ākumē
wealth will increase if one goes to, and worship with both hands, pukali where the Lord is permanent in the minds of the people who do not attach any great importance (to their words)
[[Variant reading: vaiyār uṇarviṉuḷ]]
{2:25}__11+
{$} pullam ēṟitaṉ pūmpukaliyai nalla ñāṉacampantaṉ nāviṉāl collu mālai īraintum vallavarkku viṉai iru nilattuḷ illaiyām
There will be no sins in their wide world for those who are capable of reciting the ten verses which are like garlands and which came from the mouth of good ñāṉacampantaṉ, which did not prise any other deity, on the beautiful Pukali of the Lord who rides on a bull
[[pullam bull]]
[[Campantar did not prise any other god except Civaṉ.]]

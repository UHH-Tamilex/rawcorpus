[[Note on this shrine: This is now known by the names of Kaṟpakanātar Kōyil, Kaṟpakaṉār Kōyil and Kaṟpaṉakulam; The name of Civaṉ is known as Kaṟpakēcurar]]

{2:104}__1+
{$} poṭi koḷ mēṉi veṇnūliṉar tōliṉar
Civaṉ wears on his body which is smeared with sacred ash, a white sacred thread.
puli uri ataḷ āṭai koṭi koḷ ēṟṟiṉar
has a flag on which the figure of a bull is drawn and a dress made of the skin flayed from a tiger.
maṇi kiṇiṉ eṉa varu kuraikaḻal cilampu ārkka
the sound producing Kaḻal, the tiny bell in it to make a sound resembling kiṇiṉ and the anklet to produce a sound.
kaṭi koḷ pūm poḻil cūḻ taru kaṭik kuḷattu uṟaiyum kaṟpakat taittam muṭikaḷ cāyntu
bowing their heads before the Kaṟpakam who resides in Kaṭikkuḷam surrounded by beautiful and fragrant gardens.
aṭi vīḻtarum aṭiyārai muṉviṉai mūṭā (ē)
the accumulated acts done in previous births will not encompass the devotees who fall at his feet.
{2:104}__2+
{$} viṇkaḷār toḻum viḷakkiṉai
Civaṉ, the light worshipped by the celestials (viṇkaḷār should be taken as viṇṇārkaḷ).
tuḷakku ilā vikirtaṉai
who is different from this world and who is unshakable.
maṇkaḷār tutittu aṉparāy iṉpuṟum vaḷḷalai
the perseon of unbounded liberality in whom the people of this world enjoy pleasure being devotees by praising him.
maruvittam kaṇkaḷ ār tarak kaṇṭu
having a vision of him to the complete satisfaction of the eyes by being united together in affection.
nam kaṭikkuḷattu uṟai kaṟpakattaip paṇkaḷ ārap pāṭuvār
those who sing about our Kaṟpakam who dwells in Kaṭikkuḷam setting his praises in melody-types.
kēṭu ilar
will not know destruction
paḻi ilar
will have no blemish.
pukaḻ ām (ē)
they will attain fame.
{2:104}__3+
{$} poṅku naṟkari uri (atu) pōrppatu
Civaṉ has a covering of the skin of a well-grown and angry elephant.
puli ataḷ 1. aḻal nākam taṅka maṅkaiyaip pākam (atu) uṭaiyavar taḻal purai tirumēṉi
has as one half a lady, the skin of a tiger, and a poisonous cobra to stay in his body which is like fire.
kaṅkai cērtaru caṭaiyiṉar
has a catai in which Kaṅkai has joined.
kaṭikkuḷattu uṟai kaṟpakattai eṅkum ētti niṉṟu iṉpuṟum aṭiyārai iṭumpai vantu aṭaiyā (ē)
suffering will not come near and reach the devotees who feel immense pleasure in praising everywhere the Kaṟpakam who dwells in Kaṭikkuḷam
[[Variant reading: 1. uṭainākam]]
{2:104}__4+
{$} nīr koḷ nīḷ caṭai muṭiyaṉai
Civaṉ who has a long catai coiled into a crown which contains water of (the Kaṅkai)
nittilat tottiṉai
who is cluster of pearls.
nikar illāp pār koḷ pāriṭattavar toḻum pavaḷattai
the unequalled coral which is worshipped by the people of this world which has rocks.
pacum poṉai
the superior gold
vicumpu ārum kār koḷ pūm poḻil cūḻtaru kaṭik kuḷattu uṟaiyum kaṟpakam taṉṉai
and the Kaṟpakam which dwells in Kaṭikkuḷam surrounded by flower gardens on which clouds settle and reach the sky by their height.
cīr koḷ 1. celvaṅkaḷ ēttavallār viṉai tēyvatu ti(ṇ)ṇam ām (ē)
It is certain that the acts of those who are able to praise all the renowned wealth of Civaṉ will decrease gradually.
[[Variant reading: 1. celvam koṇṭu]]
{2:104}__5+
{$} curumpu cēr caṭai muṭiyiṉaṉ matiyoṭu tuṉṉiya taḻal nākam
Civaṉ has on his caṭai coiled into a crown which the bees reach, a crescent and a poisonous cobra which is by its side.
arumpu tātu aviḻntu alarntaṉa malar pala koṇṭu aṭiyavar pōṟṟa
when the devotees praise him with many flowers which have blosomed and unfolded the pollen leaving the stage of bud.
karumpu kār mali koṭi miṭai kaṭik kuḷattu uṟai taru kaṟpakattai 1. virumpu vēṭkaiyōṭu uḷam makiḻntu uraip pavar viti uṭaiyavar (tām, ē)
those whoo speak with their hearts full of joy and desire which cherishes him with love, the Kaṟpakam who dwells in Kaṭikkuḷam where the sugar-cane and the flags which touch the clouds are crowded, are really fortunate.
[[Variant reading: 1. virumpum vēṭkaiyōṭu uḷ makiḻntu]]
{2:104}__6+
{$} mātu ilaṅkiya pākattaṉ
Civaṉ has on one half of his body a lady who is eminent.
matiyamoṭu, alaipuṉal, aḻal nākam, pōtu ilaṅkiya koṉṟaiyum, mattamum, puricaṭaikku aḻakāka
the crescent, moving water, poisonous cobra, shining buds of koṉṟai, datura flowers being beautiful in the twisted caṭai
kātu ilaṅkiya kuḻaiyiṉaṉ
wearing the men`s ear-ring in the right ear, which is shining.
kaṭikkuḷattu uṟaitaru kaṟpakattiṉ pātam kai toḻutu ētta vallār viṉai
the acts of those who are able to praise with joined hands the feet of the Kaṟpakam who dwells in Kaṭikkuḷam.
paṟṟu aṟak keṭum (aṉṟu, ē)
will perish losing their grip.
{2:104}__7+
{$} 1. kulavu kōlatta koṭi neṭu māṭaṅkaḷ kuḻāmpala kuḷir poykai ulavu puḷ iṉam aṉṉaṅkaḷ āliṭum (kaṭikkuḷattu)
in Kaṭikkuḷam which has many cool natural tanks, in which swans and flocks of other birds are wandering, societies of devotees, tall storeys in which flags are hoisted and which have beautiful forms.
pūvai cērum kūntal kalavai cērtaru kaṇṇiyaṉ
wearing a chaplet made with different kinds of flowers, and a lady with tresses of hair on which she wears flowers of iron-wood tree;
[[pūvai cērum kuḻal: (pūvai-kāyā malar; popularly known as kācāmalar);
kācai cēr kuḻalāḷ;
kācai cey kuḻal umai (campantar, pūntarai, (2) 8; (4) 8)]]
kaṭikkuḷattu uṟaiyum kaṟpakattaic cīr nalavi niṉṟu niṉṟu ēttavār mēl viṉai niṟka killā (tāṉ, ē)
the acts have not the strength to stay with those who praise always being steadfast and fixing in their minds the fame of Kaṟppakam who dwells in Kaṭikkuḷam.
[[Variant reading: 1. kulavu kōlattar]]
{2:104}__8+
{$} maṭutta vāḷ arakkaṉ (avaṉ)
that arakkaṉ who has a sword that pierced into the bodies of enemies.
malai taṉmēl matiyilāmaiyil ōṭi
running towards the mountain, Kayilai, as he had no good sense.
eṭuttalum
as soon as he lifted it.
muṭi, tōḷ, karam, nerintu 1. iṟa iṟaiyavaṉ viral ūṉṟa
as the Lord Civaṉ fixed his toe to make the heads, shoulders and hands to be crushed and crackled and be split.
kaṭuttu vāyoṭu kaiyeṭuttu alaṟiṭa
raising his hands to roar by his mouth feeling pain.
2. kaṭikkuḷam taṉil mēvi
staying in Kaṭikkuḷam.
pēraruḷ koṭutta kūttaṉai ēttuvār kuṇam uṭaiyavar (tām, ē)
those who praise the dancer who bestowed his benign grace on him, are people endowed with good qualities.
[[Variant reading: 1. iṭa 2. kaṭikkuḷamatu mēvi]]
{2:104}__9+
{$} nīriṉār kaṭal tuyiṉṟavaṉ ayaṉoṭu nikaḻ aṭi muṭi kāṇār pāriṉār vicumpu uṟap parantu eḻuntatu or pavaḷattiṉ paṭi āki
assuming the form of a fire which is like coral in colour and raising high in the sky and spreading everywhere, when Māl who slept in the ocean of water and Ayaṉ, being unable to find the shining feet and head, even though they digged the earth and flew into the sky (respectively).
kāriṉ ār poḻil cūḻtaru kaṭikkuḷattu uṟaiyum kaṟpakattiṉ taṉ cīriṉ ār kaḻal ētta vallār kaḷait tīvīṉai aṭaiyā (ē)
the sins will not go near those who are able to praise the famous feet of the Lord, Kaṟpakam, who dwells in Kaṭikkuḷam surrounded by gardens which are like sable clouds;

{2:104}__10+
{$} kuṇṭar tammoṭu
the low people (tam, oṭu are expletives).
cākkiyar camaṇarum kuṟiyiṉil neṟinillā miṇṭar miṇṭu urai kēṭṭu avai mey eṉak keḷḷaṉ miṉ
(Devotees!) Do not accept as truth the presumptuous talks of the ignorant cākkiyar (Buddhists) and camaṇar who do not follow the proper way with any aim;
viṭam uṇṭa kaṇṭar
Civaṉ who has a neck which retains the poison that he consumed.
muṇṭa naṉmēṉiyar
has a body on which he wears the sacred ash in three horizontal lines;
[[muṇṭam is the corruption tiripuṇtaram;
ceñcavē āṇṭukoṇṭāṉ tirumuṇṭam tīṭṭa māṭṭātu, añcuvār avaraik kaṇṭāl- (tiruvacakam accappattu, 9)]]
kaṭikkuḷattu uṟaitarum em īcar
our Lord of the universe who dwells in Kaṭikkuḷam.
toṇṭar toṇṭarait toḻutu aṭi paṇimiṉkaḷ
(Devotees!) worshipping the devotees of the devotees with joined hands, fall at their feet.
tū neṟi eḷitu (ām, ē)
the pure path will be easy of attainment.
{2:104}__11+
{$} kaṉam mali kaṭal ōtam vantu ulaviya kaṭik kuḷattu amarvāṉai
on Civaṉ who resides with pleasure in Kaṭikkuḷam where the waves of the sea with many clouds, move.
taṉam mali pukaḻ tayaṅku pūnta rāyavar maṉṉaṉ
the chief of the residents of pūntarai which is eminent by its wealth and great fame.
nalcampantaṉa
composed by the good natured campantaṉ
maṉamali pukaḻ vaṇṭamiḻ mālkaḷ 1. mālatāy mikaḻvōṭum iṉam malintu icai pāṭa vallārkaḷ pōy iṟaivaṉōṭu iruppār (ē)
those who sings with music in groups of devotees with pleasure and desire the rich tamiḻ verses containing the fame of Civaṉ who occupied his mind, will be with the Lord entering into Civalōkam after leaving this world.

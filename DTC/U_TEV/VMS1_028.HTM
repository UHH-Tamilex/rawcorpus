{1:28}__1+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
(1) neṟikoḷ ciṟṟiṉpam tuppaṉ eṉṉātu aruḷē tuṇaiyākac ceppa oppar oppar
Civaṉ will give his assent definitely when we enjoy momentary pleasures according to rules prescribed for house-holders without the slightest egoism, when we say, I enjoy them with the aid of his grace
perumāṉ
Civaṉ.
oḷi veṇṇīṟṟu appar cōṟṟuttuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall reach coṟṟuttuṟi of the father who smears bright and white holy ash, by going there.
[[Variant reading: (1) neṟikaḷ]]
{1:28}__2+
{$} pālum neyyum tayirum payiṉṟu āṭi
bathing many times in milk, ghee and curd.
tōlum nūlum tutainta varai mārpar
Civaṉ who has a chest as strong and broad as the mountin on which the skin of a deer and sacred thread are close together.
mālum cōlai puṭaicūḻ maṭamaññai, ālum cōṟṟut tuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall reach by journey coṟṟuttuṟi where young peacocks which are in the gardens, and surrounded on all sides by gardens which confound people.
{1:28}__3+
{$} ceyyar
Civaṉ who is red in colour.
ceyya caṭaiyar
has a ruddy caṭi.
viṭai ūrvar
rides on a bull.
kaikoḷ vēlar
holds a trident in his hand.
kaḻalar
wears an armour in the leg.
karikāṭar
dwells in the charred cremation-ground.
taiyalāḷ ōrpākamāya em aiyar cōṟṟuttuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall reach cōṟṟuttuṟi belonging to our father who has a young lady as one half, by making a journey.
{1:28}__4+
{$} piṇikoḷ ākkai oḻiyap piṟappu uḷīr
wise people who want to derive benefit from this birth in order to be free from this body which is heir to many diseases!
tuṇikoḷ pōrār tuḷaṅkumaḻuvāḷar
Civaṉ who handles the battle-axe which is shaking and which is useful in battle for cutting the bodies of enemies.
maṇikoḷ kaṇṭar mēya vār poḻil aṇikoḷ cōṟṟuttuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we will go and reach cōṟṟuttuṟi which is made beautiful by long gardens and where Civaṉ with a neck like sapphire dwells.
{1:28}__5+
{$} piṟaiyum aravum puṉalum caṭaivaittu
placing the crescent, cobra and water in the caṭi.
maṟaiyum ōti
Chanting also the vētams.
mayāṉam iṭamāka uṟaiyum celvam uṭaiyār kāviri aṟaiyum cōṟṟuttuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall go and reach cōṟṟuttuṟi where the Kaviri dashes on its banks and which belongs to Civaṉ who has the cremation ground as his dwelling place.
{1:28}__6+
{$} tuṭikaḷōṭu muḻavam vimmavē
when many drums which taper towards the middle, and muḻavu are played on a high pitch.
poṭikaḷ pūci
smearing himself with many kinds of sacred ash.
puṟaṅkāṭu araṅkākappaṭikoḷ pāṇipāṭal payiṉṟāṭum aṭikaḷ cōṟṟuttuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall reach Cōṟṟuttuṟi of the god who dances to the songs and measure of time which can be compared to others, having the cremation ground as the stage.
{1:28}__7+
{$} kālaṉ māḷaccāṭi
having killed Kālaṉ (god of death) to die.
talaimālaicūṭi mikkuc cuvaṇṭāy varuvār (tām)
Civaṉ who appears in the very appropriate form wearing a garland of skulls.
[[cuvaṇṭu fitness, appropriateness]]

puram cuṭṭa cuvaṇṭarō,
cuṇṇa veṇpoṭip pūcum cuvaṇṭarō (appar, tirumaṟikkāṭu, tirukkuṟuntoki (2) 3, 9;
tūnīṟu aṇiyum cuvaṇṭar tāmē (Do. maṟikkāṭu, tāṅtakam, 6);

pāṭi āṭip paravuvār uḷḷattu āṭi cōṟṟuttuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall go and reach Cōṟṟuttuṟi of Civaṉ who dances cut of joy in the hearts of those who prise him singing and dancing.
{1:28}__8+
{$} peṇ ōrpākam uṭaiyār
Civaṉ has a lady on one half.
piṟaic ceṉṉikkaṇ ōr pākam kalanta nutaliṉār
has a head on which there is the crescent and a forehead with an eye in one place.
eṇṇātu arakkaṉ eṭukka ūṉṟiya aṇṇal cōṟṟut tuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall go and reach Coṟṟuttuṟi of the god who pressed down the arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ) who lifted (the mountin, Kayili) thoughtlessly.
{1:28}__9+
{$} toḻuvār iruvar tuyaram nīṅkavē
to remove the sorrow of the two, Māl and Piramaṉ who worshipped him casting away their pride.
aḻalāy ōṅki aruḷkaḷ ceytavaṉ
Civaṉ bestowed his many kinds of grace by rising high as a column of fire.
viḻavu ār maṟukil vitiyāl (1) mikka emeḻil ār cōṟṟuttuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall go and reach our Cōṟṟuttuṟi which has streets in which festivals are celebrated and looks very beautiful lid according to the rules in the works on architecture
[[Variant reading: (1) mikka eḻilār]]
{1:28}__10+
{$} kōtu cāṟṟit tirivār amaṇ kuṇṭar ōtum ōttai uṇarātu oḻi; neñcē
my mind! leave without studying the scriptures which the amaṇar who go about dilating upon defects to be known to all, and the low buddhists.
eḻu
start.
nīti niṉṟu niṉaivār vēṭam ām āti cōṟṟuttuṟai ceṉṟu aṭaivōm
we shall go and reach Cōṟṟuttuṟi of primordial god who assumes the form on which people who are firm in their righteousness, meditate.
{1:28}__11+
{$} aṭiyār āyiṉīr
You who are devotees?
antaṇ cōṟṟuttuṟai em ātiyaic cintai ceymiṉ
meditate upon the primordial Civaṉ in beautiful and cool Cōṟṟuttuṟi.
cantam paravu ñāṉacampantaṉ vantavāṟē puṉaital vaḻipāṭē
the mods of worship for that is to adorn the god with songs composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ who prises the nature of Civaṉ, which came out of his mouth by god's grace.

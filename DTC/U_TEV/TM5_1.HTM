*2

*3

ciriccu_uṟṟup pala pēcappaṭāmuṉam,
tiruc ciṟṟampalam ceṉṟu _aṭaintu _uymmiṉē!

*4

_ellai_illatu _ōr _aṭimai pūṇṭēṉukkē.

*5

*6

*8

*9

*10

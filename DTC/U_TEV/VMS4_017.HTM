A note on the temple: this temple is situated in the courtyard of the second wall of enclosure within the big temple of Tirumūlaṭṭāṉam, and is facing west. In this temple Naninanti Nāyaṉār lit lamp pouring water instead of oil.]]

araneṟiyār attī niṟattār
Civaṉ in Araneṟi has a beautiful colour like the fire;
[[EXPL: a: aḻaku (beauty);]]
[[PP: eyyā iḷañcūl ceyyōḷ avvayiṟṟu (porunar āṟṟuppaṭi, 6)]]
muttī aṉaiyatu ōr mūvilai vēl piṭittu
holding a trident having three blades like the three sacrificial fires.
tettē eṉa muraṉṟu emmuḷ uḻitarvar
he will be moving within me making a sound resembling tettē
[[tettē: onomatopoeia;]]
[[PP: nāṉ maṟaiyait tettē eṉṟu pāṭum teṉ tillai
(tiruvicippā kaṅtarātittaṉ, 3)]]
(1) ettī pukiṉum emakku oru tītu illai
no harm will come to us even if we enter into any kind of fire.
[[Variant reading: (1) ettīppukiṉum, ettīppukilum, etticaipukiṉum]]

araneṟiyār
Civaṉ in araneṟi
vicayaṉoṭu āyatu ōr vīramum pūṇpar
will wear as an ornament the valour with which he fought with Vicayaṉ.
tāramum pūṇpar
as he went with Umi to grant Vicayaṉ a weapon he has a consort.
tamakku aṉpu paṭṭavar pāramum pūṇpar
he will bear the burden of those who cherish him with love.
naṟpaiṅkaṇ miḷir aravu āramum pūṇpar
adorns himself with the garland of cobras also which glitter and have greenish eyes and are devoid of their cruelty.
[[PP: ārattaḻai arāppūṇṭu;
pēraravam pūṇat tiruttiya poṅku oḷiyōṉ
(kōviyār, 92, 215)]]

araneṟiyār
Civaṉ in araneṟi
tañcavaṇṇattar
supports those who take refuge in him.
caṭaiyiṉar
has a caṭi
vañcavaṇṇattar
has a deceptive nature towards others.
vaṇṭu ār kuḻalāḷoṭum tuñcavaṇṇattar
has the nature of sleeping with a lady on whose tressess of hir bees hum.
tuñcāta (1) kaṇṇār toḻum añcavaṇṇattar
has five colours; he is worshipped by the celestials who do not sleep.
[[añcavaṇṇattar may be interpreted as amcamantira japayōkam puriyum vaṇṇattar the dance performed by tiyākarājar in tiruvārūr is called ajapānaṭaṉam]]
[[Variant reading: (1) kaṇṇar]]

ārūr araneṟiyār
Civaṉ in araneṟi in ārūr
kāmaṉai vīḻtara viḻittaṉar
opened his frontal eye and burnt Kāmaṉ to fall down as dust.
viṇṇiṉṟu kaṅkaiyai iḻittaṉar
made Kaṅki to descend from heaven.
ēttiṉar pāvam kaḻittaṉar
removed the sins of those who prised him.
kalcūḻ kaṭi araṇ mūṉṟum aḻittaṉar
destroyed all the three well-guarded forts built on all sides with stones.

veṇtalaiyil tuṟṟavar
eats his food receiving alms in the white skull.
curuḷ kōvaṇam taṟṟavar
wore tightly a rolling loin-cloth.
tam viṉai elām aṟa aṟṟavar araneṟi kai toḻa uṟṟavar
worshipped by those, who are completely free from their acts and who appproach araneṟi to worship with joined hands.
oḷi (1) peṟṟaṉar tāmē
and who are people who have got internal illumination.
[[Variant reading: (1) peṟṟavar]]

araneṟiyār
Civaṉ in Araneṟi
kūṭu aravattar
wears an anklet that makes sound.
kural kiṇkiṇi aṭi nīṭu aravattar
makes a big sound produced by the ornament kiṇkiṇ worn on the leg.
[[kiṇkiṇi is an onomatopoetic word.]]
muṇ mālai iṭai iruḷ pāṭu aravattar
has the big sound produced by singing in the evening and midnight.
paṇam añcu pai virittu āṭu aravattar
wears a cobra that dances; spreading the five hoods in its heads.

kuṟippil umaiyāḷoṭum kūṭavallār
if one meditates upon him Civaṉ will appear along with Umayāḷ and bestow bliss
[[kuṟippil: according to his wish;
umaiyāḷoṭum kūṭavallār Civaṉ is united with Umayāḷ]]
pāṭavallār
is capable of singing.
payiṉṟu antiyum cantiyum āṭuvār
will dance very often in the evening and when night and day, day and night meet.
[[antiyum cantiyum āṭuvār is one of the names of Civaṉ in tiruvārūr; see preface to tiruvārūrulā, p.VI, by Dr.Swaminatha Ayyar, 2nd edition, 1925.]]
tiruvārūr araneṟi nāṭavallār viṉaivīṭavallārē
those who desire earnestly his Araneṟi in Tiruvārūr have the strength to destroy their acts of sin and virtue.

ārūr (1) araneṟiyārkku
to Civaṉ in Ārūr Araneṟi.
muṭivaṇṇam vāṉa miṉ vaṇṇam
the colour of his caṭi is like the colour of the lightning that is produced by the clouds.
tam (2) mārpiṉ poṭi vaṇṇam tampukaḻ ūrtiyiṉ vaṇṇam
the colour of the sacred ash that is smeared on the chest is as white as the colour of his famous vehicle, the bull.
paṭivaṇṇam pāṟkaṭal vaṇṇam
the colour of his body is the colour of the ocean of milk.
ceññāyiṟu aṭivaṇṇam
the red sun is the colour of his feet;
[[PP: arumpitta ceññāyiṟu ēykkum aṭi
(nāvukkaracar, tiruvatiki vīraṭṭaṉam, tiruvaṭittiruttāṇtakam, 4)]]
[[Variant reading: (1) araneṟiyārē (2) mārpil]]

poṉ navil puṉ caṭaiyāṉ aṭiyiṉ niḻal iṉṉaruḷ cūṭi eḷkātum irāppakal
without feeling shy, having been blessed with the shade of his feet, which is the sweet-grace of Civaṉ who has red cati resembling gold; night and day.
maṉṉavar kiṉṉarar vāṉavar tām toḻum aṉṉavar
has such a great person who is worshipped by kings of this world, kiṉṉarar and the celestials.

poruḷ maṉṉaṉaip paṟṟip puṭpakam koṇṭa maruḷ maṉṉaṉai eṟṟi
crushing the king who had confusion and who seized the vehicle, putpakam, capturing the Lord of wealth, kupēraṉ.
vāḷuṭaṉ īntu
granting him name long life and name along with a sword.
karuḷ maṉṉu kaṇṭam kaṟukka nañcu uṇṭa aruḷ maṉṉar ārūr araneṟiyār
Civaṉ in ārūr araneṟi who consumed the poison to make the neck which is already black to become still blacker, is the king of benign grace.

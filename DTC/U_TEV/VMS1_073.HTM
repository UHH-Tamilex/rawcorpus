{1:73}__1+
{$} vāṉ ār cōti maṉṉu ceṉṉi
in the head on which the sun and moon that move in the sky are staying.
vaṉṉipuṉam koṉṟaittēṉ ārpōtutāṉ ārkaṅkai tiṅkaḷoṭucūṭi
having worn vaṉṉi leaves (leaves of Indian mesquit), buds in an advanced stage of the koṉṟi growing in the forest region which have honey, and roaring kaṅki along with the crescent.
māṉ ēr nōkkikaṇṭu aṅku uvappa mālai āṭuvār
Civaṉ who dances in the evening to be witnessed and enjoyed by Umi who has beautiful looks like those of the deer.
kāṉūr mēya neṟṟikkaṇṇār
and who has a frontal eye and dwells in kāṉur.
āṉ ūr celvar
is Civaṉ who rides on a bull.
{1:73}__2+
{$} nīntal ākā veḷḷam mūḻku nīḷcaṭaitaṉmēl
on the long caṭi in which is concealed the floods (of Kaṅki) which is difficult to swim.
ōr ēynta kōṉaṟpiṟaiyōṭu aravu koṉṟai
a suitable curved crescent, cobra and koṉṟi flowers, to remin with beauty.
eḻil ārappērntameṉcol iṉpam payantamaintar avar (pōlām)
the strong person who gave pleasure by the soft words which came out of his mouth beautifully.
kāntaḷ vimmu kāṉūr mēyacānta nīṟṟār
is Civaṉ who besmeared himself with holy ash as one would do with sandal-paste and dwells with pleasure in Kāṉur where malabar glory lilies open their petals.
{1:73}__3+
{$} ciṟaiār vaṇṭum tēṉum vimmu ceyya malarkkoṉṟai maṟai ār pāṭal āṭalōṭu māl viṭaimēl varuvār
one who comes riding on a bull singing vētams and dancing, wearing flawless koṉṟi flowers on which bees such as vaṇṭu with wings and teṉ swarm to hide them.
[[vaṇṭu, tēṉ: different Varieties of bees.]]
iṟaiyārvantu eṉ ilpukuntu eṉ eḻil nalamum koṇṭār kaṟai ār cōlaikkāṉūr mēya piṟai ārcaṭaiyār
is Civaṉ who has a crescent on his caṭi and dwells in Kāṉūr which has dark gardens, who entered into my house and snatched away my beauty.
{1:73}__4+
{$} viṇ ār tiṅkaḷ kaṇṇi veḷḷai mālai atu cūṭi
Having worn a white garland and a chaplet of a crescent which moves in the sky.
taṇ ār akkōṭu āmai pūṇṭu
and having adorned himself with cool bones and shell of a tortoise.
taḻai puṉcaṭai tāḻa
when the thriving golden caṭi was hanging low.
eṇṇāvantu eṉil pukuntu aṅku evvanōy ceytāṉ kaṇār cōlaikkāṉūrmēya viṇṇōr perumāṉ
is the chief of the celestials who dwells in Kāṉūr which has gardens feasting the eyes, who entered into my house voluntarily when I least thought of his coming, due to my ignorance, had union with me and gave me pangs by his separation.
{1:73}__5+
{$} tārkoḷ koṉṟaikkaṇṇiyōṭum taṇmatiyam cūṭi
Having worn a cool crescent along with a garland of koṉṟi on the chest and a chaplet on the head.
cīrkoḷ pāṭal āṭalōṭum cēṭarāy vantu
and coming as a youth whose mission is being a messenger, with songs measuring time and with dances.
ūrkaḷ tōṟum aiyam ēṟṟu eṉ uḷ vennōy ceytār
who created the cruel disease of love in my mind receiving alms in every village.
kārkoḷcōlaik kāṉūrmēya kaṟaikkaṇṭattārē
is the god with a black neck who dwells in Kāṉūr having gardens on which sable clouds stay.
{1:73}__6+
{$} muḷiveḷḷelumpum nīṟum nūlum mūḻkum mārparāy
Having a chest on which dried white bones, holy ash and sacred thread remin.
eḷivantārpōl aiyam eṉṟu eṉillē pukuntu
entering into my house saying, give me alms like poor persons.
uḷḷatteḷivum nāṇumkoṇṭa kaḷvar
the thief who stole clarity of my mind and bashfulness.
tēṟal ār pūvil kaḷivaṇṭu yāḻ ceykāṉūr mēya oḷi veṇpiṟaiyār
is Civaṉ who wears a bright and white crescent, and who dwells in Kāṉūr where intoxicated bees hum like the music of yāḻ in the flowers having honey.
{1:73}__7+
{$} mūvāvaṇṇar
Civaṉ who has a beauty not spoiled by age.
muḷaiveṇpiṟaiyar
wearing a rising white crescent.
pūvār koṉṟai puṉaintu vantār
came adorning himself with brilliant koṉṟi flowers.
muṟuval ceytu iṅkē pokkam palapēci
speaking here many lines, smilling all the while.
pōvār pōla mālceytu uḷḷampukka purinūlar
wears a sacred thread of many strands who entered into my heart, kindling desire in me, pretending to go away from me.
tē ār cōlaikkāṉūrmēya tēvatēvar
is the supreme god above all gods, who dwells in Kāṉūr which has gardens full of divinity.
{1:73}__8+
{$} tamiḻiṉ nīrmai pēci
speaking words that have sweetness like tamiḻ.
tāḷam vīṇaipaṇṇi
measuring time by cymbals and playing upon the viṇi.
nalla montai malkupāṭalceykai iṭam ōvār
and singing many songs to the accompaniment of defeotless monti (a musical instrument) and not moving from that place.
kumiḻiṉ mēṉitantu kōla nīrmai atu koṇṭār
gave me the colour of the flower of the smallcashmers trees, taking away my beauty.
kamaḻum cōlaik kāṉūr mēya pavaḷavaṇṇar
Civaṉ who is as red as coral and who dwells in Kāṉūr which has gardens that spread their fragrance.
{1:73}__9+
{$} antamāti ayaṉum mālum ārkkum aṟivu ariyāṉ
one whose end and beginning could not be known even by Ayaṉ and Māl and all others.
cintaiyuḷḷum nāviṉ mēlum ceṉṉiyum maṉṉiṉāṉ
who stayed permanently in my mind, speech and head.
vantu eṉ uḷḷam pukuntu kālaimālai āṭuvāṉ
will dance in the morning and evening, heaving entered into my heart.
kantam malku kāṉūr mēya entai pemmāṉ
is God, my father, who dwells in Kāṉūr where sweet fragrance is increasing.
{1:73}__10+
{$} āmai aravōṭu ēṉaveṇ kompu akkumālai pūṇtām ōr kaḷvar veḷḷarpōla uḷ vennōy ceytār
brought about the unbearable suffering of love in my mind, coming to me like an honest person, who the thief who adorns himself with a garland of bones, hog's white tusk, cobra and shell of a tortoise.
ōmavēta nāṉmukaṉum kōḷ nākaṇaiyāṉum cēmam āya celvar kāṉūr mēya cēṭar
God who dwells in Kāṉūr and the Lord of riches to whom nāṉmukaṉ (Piramaṉ) who chants the vetam which ordins sacrificial offerings and Māl who has a bed of a serpent that is capable killing, afford protection as body-guards.

[[Note: In this decade there is no reference to Irāvaṇaṉ, camaṇar and buddhists; The last verse which leaves the name of Ñāṉacampantaṉ and the benefit one would get by chanting the verses is also not found; but the mention of Māl and Piramaṉ is in the 9th verse.]]

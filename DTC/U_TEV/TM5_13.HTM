• patikam: [5:13]
  • talam: tiru vīḻimiḻali
  • (()): tiruk kuṟuntoki
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.271

*1
_eṉ poṉē! _imiyōr toḻu piṅkaḻal
naṉpoṉē! nalam tīṅku _aṟivu _oṉṟu _ilēṉ;
cempoṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
_aṉpaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

*2
kaṇṇiṉāl kaḷi kūrak kiyāl-toḻutu
_eṇṇum_āṟu _aṟiyātu _iḷippēṉtaṉi,---
viṇ_uḷār toḻum vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
_aṇṇalē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

*3
ñālamē! vicumpē! nalamtīmiyē!
kālamē! karuttē! karuttāl-toḻum
cīlamē! tiru vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
kōlamē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

*4
muttaṉē! mutalvā! mukiḻum muḷi
_ottaṉē! _oruvā! _uru_ākiya
cittaṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
_attaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkōḷē!

*5
karuvaṉē! karu_āyt teḷivārkku _elām
_oruvaṉē! _uyirppu_āy _uṇarvu_āy niṉṟa
tiruvaṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
kuruvaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

*6
kāttaṉē, poḻil _ēḻiyum! kātalāl
_āttaṉē, _amararkku! _ayaṉtaṉ tali
cērntaṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
kūttaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

*7
nīti vāṉavar nittal niyamam ceytu
_ōti vāṉavarum(m) _uṇarātatu _ōr
vētiyā! vikirtā! tira vīḻiyuḷ
_ātiyē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

*8
paḻaki niṉ _aṭi cūṭiya pālaṉik
kaḻakiṉmēl vitta kālaṉic cāṭiya
_aḻakaṉē! _aṇi vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
kuḻakaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

*9
_aṇṭa vāṉavar kūṭik kaṭinta nañcu
_uṇṭa vāṉavaṉē! _uṇarvu _oṉṟu _ilēṉ;
viṇṭa vāṉ poḻil vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
koṇṭaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

*10
_oruttaṉ _ōṅkalit tāṅkal_uṟṟāṉ _uram
varuttiṉāy! vañcaṉēṉ maṉam maṉṉiya
tiruttaṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻaliyuḷ
_aruttaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉik kuṟikkoḷē!

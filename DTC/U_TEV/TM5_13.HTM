• patikam: [5:13]
  • talam: tiru vīḻimiḻalai
  • (()): tiruk kuṟuntokai
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.271

*1
_eṉ poṉē! _imaiyōr toḻu paiṅkaḻal
naṉpoṉē! nalam tīṅku _aṟivu _oṉṟu _ilēṉ;
cempoṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
_aṉpaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

*2
kaṇṇiṉāl kaḷi kūrak kaiyāl-toḻutu
_eṇṇum_āṟu _aṟiyātu _iḷaippēṉtaṉai,---
viṇ_uḷār toḻum vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
_aṇṇalē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

*3
ñālamē! vicumpē! nalamtīmaiyē!
kālamē! karuttē! karuttāl-toḻum
cīlamē! tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
kōlamē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

*4
muttaṉē! mutalvā! mukiḻum muḷai
_ottaṉē! _oruvā! _uru_ākiya
cittaṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
_attaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkōḷē!

*5
karuvaṉē! karu_āyt teḷivārkku _elām
_oruvaṉē! _uyirppu_āy _uṇarvu_āy niṉṟa
tiruvaṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
kuruvaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

*6
kāttaṉē, poḻil _ēḻaiyum! kātalāl
_āttaṉē, _amararkku! _ayaṉtaṉ talai
cērntaṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
kūttaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

*7
nīti vāṉavar nittal niyamam ceytu
_ōti vāṉavarum(m) _uṇarātatu _ōr
vētiyā! vikirtā! tira vīḻiyuḷ
_ātiyē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

*8
paḻaki niṉ _aṭi cūṭiya pālaṉaik
kaḻakiṉmēl vaitta kālaṉaic cāṭiya
_aḻakaṉē! _aṇi vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
kuḻakaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

*9
_aṇṭa vāṉavar kūṭik kaṭainta nañcu
_uṇṭa vāṉavaṉē! _uṇarvu _oṉṟu _ilēṉ;
viṇṭa vāṉ poḻil vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
koṇṭaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

*10
_oruttaṉ _ōṅkalait tāṅkal_uṟṟāṉ _uram
varuttiṉāy! vañcaṉēṉ maṉam maṉṉiya
tiruttaṉē! tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ
_aruttaṉē!---_aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷē!

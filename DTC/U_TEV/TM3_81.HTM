• patikam: [3:81]
  • talam: tirut tōṇipuram (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.249

*1
caṅku _amarum muṉkai maṭamātai _orupāl _uṭaṉvirumpi,
_aṅkam _uṭalmēl _uṟa _aṇintu, piṇi tīra _aruḷceyyum
_eṅkaḷ perumāṉ _iṭam _eṉat takum---muṉaik kaṭaliṉ muttam,
tuṅka maṇi, _ippikaḷ, karaikku varu tōṇipuram_āmē.

*2
callari(yi), yāḻ, muḻavam, montai, kuḻal, tāḷam_atu, _iyampa,
kal _ariya māmalaiyarpāvai _orupākam nilaiceytu,
_al _eri kai _ēnti, naṭam_āṭu caṭai _aṇṇal _iṭam _eṉpar---
colla(_a)riya toṇṭar tuticeyya, vaḷar tōṇipuram_āmē.

*3
vaṇṭu _aravu koṉṟai vaḷarpuṉcaṭaiyiṉmēl matiyam vaittu,
paṇṭu _aravu taṉ _araiyil _ārtta paramēṭṭi; paḻi tīrak
kaṇṭu _arava _oṇ kaṭaliṉ nañcam _amutu_uṇṭa kaṭavuḷ; _ūr---
toṇṭar_avar miṇṭi, vaḻipāṭu malku tōṇipuram_āmē.

*4
kollai viṭai_ēṟu _uṭaiya kōvaṇavaṉ, nā _aṇavum mālai
_ollai _uṭaiyāṉ, _aṭaiyalār _araṇam _oḷ_aḻal viḷaitta
villai _uṭaiyāṉ, mika virumpu pati---mēvi vaḷar toṇṭar
collai _aṭaivu_āka _iṭar tīrttu, _aruḷcey tōṇipuram_āmē.

*5
tēyum matiyam caṭai _ilaṅkiṭa, vilaṅkal mali kāṉil
kāyum _aṭu tiṇ kariyiṉ _īr_urivai pōrttavaṉ; niṉaippār
tāy _eṉa niṟaintatu _oru taṉmaiyiṉar; naṉmaiyoṭu vāḻvu---
tūya maṟaiyāḷar muṟai _ōti niṟai tōṇipuram_āmē.

*6
paṟṟalartam muppuram _erittu, _aṭi paṇintavarkaḷ mēlaik
kuṟṟam_atu _oḻittu, _aruḷu koḷkaiyiṉaṉ; veḷḷil mutukāṉil
paṟṟavaṉ; _icaikkiḷavi pāriṭam_atu _ētta naṭam_āṭum
tuṟṟa caṭai _attaṉ; _uṟaikiṉṟa pati---tōṇipuram_āmē.

*7
paṇ _amarum nālmaṟaiyar, nūl muṟai payiṉṟa tirumārpil
peṇ _amarum mēṉiyiṉar, tam perumai pēcum _aṭiyār meyt
tiṇ _amarum valviṉaikaḷ tīra _aruḷceytal _uṭaiyāṉ, _ūr---
tuṇṇeṉa virumpu cariyaittoḻilar tōṇipuram_āmē.

*8
teṉticai _ilaṅkai _araiyaṉ ticaikaḷ vīram viḷaivittu
veṉṟi cey puyaṅkaḷai _aṭarttu _aruḷum vittakaṉ _iṭam---cīr
_oṉṟu _icai_iyalkiḷavi pāṭa, mayil _āṭa, vaḷar cōlai
tuṉṟuceya vaṇṭu, mali tumpi mural tōṇipuram_āmē.

*9
nāṟṟam miku mā malariṉmēl _ayaṉum, nāraṇaṉum, nāṭi,
_āṟṟal_ataṉāl mika _aḷappu _ariya vaṇṇam, _eri _āki,
_ūṟṟam miku kīḻ_ulakum mēl_ulakum _ōṅki _eḻu taṉmait
tōṟṟam mika, nāḷum _ariyāṉ _uṟaivu---tōṇipuram_āmē.

*10
mūṭu tuvar_āṭaiyiṉar, vēṭam nilai kāṭṭum _amaṇ_ātar,
kēṭupala colliṭuvar; _am moḻi keṭuttu, _aṭaiviṉāṉ, _ak
kāṭu pati_āka naṭam_āṭi, maṭamātoṭu _iru kātil-
tōṭu kuḻai peytavartamakku _uṟaivu---tōṇipuram_āmē.

*11
tuñcu _iruḷil niṉṟu naṭam_āṭi miku tōṇipuram mēya
mañcaṉai vaṇaṅku tiru ñāṉacampantaṉa colmālai,
tañcam _eṉa niṉṟu _icai moḻinta _aṭiyārkaḷ, taṭumāṟṟam
vañcam _ilar; neñcu _iruḷum nīṅki, _aruḷ peṟṟu vaḷarvārē.

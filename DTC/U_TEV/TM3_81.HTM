  • talam: tirut tōṇipuram (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • (()): tiruvirākam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.249

*1
caṅku _amarum muṉki maṭamāti _orupāl _uṭaṉvirumpi,
_aṅkam _uṭalmēl _uṟa _aṇintu, piṇi tīra _aruḷceyyum
_eṅkaḷ perumāṉ _iṭam _eṉat takum---muṉik kaṭaliṉ muttam,
tuṅka maṇi, _ippikaḷ, karikku varu tōṇipuram_āmē.

*2
callari(yi), yāḻ, muḻavam, monti, kuḻal, tāḷam_atu, _iyampa,
kal _ariya māmaliyarpāvi _orupākam niliceytu,
_al _eri ki _ēnti, naṭam_āṭu caṭi _aṇṇal _iṭam _eṉpar---
colla(_a)riya toṇṭar tuticeyya, vaḷar tōṇipuram_āmē.

*3
vaṇṭu _aravu koṉṟi vaḷarpuṉcaṭiyiṉmēl matiyam vittu,
paṇṭu _aravu taṉ _ariyil _ārtta paramēṭṭi; paḻi tīrak
kaṇṭu _arava _oṇ kaṭaliṉ nañcam _amutu_uṇṭa kaṭavuḷ; _ūr---
toṇṭar_avar miṇṭi, vaḻipāṭu malku tōṇipuram_āmē.

*4
kolli viṭi_ēṟu _uṭiya kōvaṇavaṉ, nā _aṇavum māli
_olli _uṭiyāṉ, _aṭiyalār _araṇam _oḷ_aḻal viḷitta
villi _uṭiyāṉ, mika virumpu pati---mēvi vaḷar toṇṭar
colli _aṭivu_āka _iṭar tīrttu, _aruḷcey tōṇipuram_āmē.

*5
tēyum matiyam caṭi _ilaṅkiṭa, vilaṅkal mali kāṉil
kāyum _aṭu tiṇ kariyiṉ _īr_urivi pōrttavaṉ; niṉippār
tāy _eṉa niṟintatu _oru taṉmiyiṉar; naṉmiyoṭu vāḻvu---
tūya maṟiyāḷar muṟi _ōti niṟi tōṇipuram_āmē.

*6
paṟṟalartam muppuram _erittu, _aṭi paṇintavarkaḷ mēlik
kuṟṟam_atu _oḻittu, _aruḷu koḷkiyiṉaṉ; veḷḷil mutukāṉil
paṟṟavaṉ; _icikkiḷavi pāriṭam_atu _ētta naṭam_āṭum
tuṟṟa caṭi _attaṉ; _uṟikiṉṟa pati---tōṇipuram_āmē.

*7
paṇ _amarum nālmaṟiyar, nūl muṟi payiṉṟa tirumārpil
peṇ _amarum mēṉiyiṉar, tam perumi pēcum _aṭiyār meyt
tiṇ _amarum valviṉikaḷ tīra _aruḷceytal _uṭiyāṉ, _ūr---
tuṇṇeṉa virumpu cariyittoḻilar tōṇipuram_āmē.

*8
teṉtici _ilaṅki _ariyaṉ ticikaḷ vīram viḷivittu
veṉṟi cey puyaṅkaḷi _aṭarttu _aruḷum vittakaṉ _iṭam---cīr
_oṉṟu _ici_iyalkiḷavi pāṭa, mayil _āṭa, vaḷar cōli
tuṉṟuceya vaṇṭu, mali tumpi mural tōṇipuram_āmē.

*9
nāṟṟam miku mā malariṉmēl _ayaṉum, nāraṇaṉum, nāṭi,
_āṟṟal_ataṉāl mika _aḷappu _ariya vaṇṇam, _eri _āki,
_ūṟṟam miku kīḻ_ulakum mēl_ulakum _ōṅki _eḻu taṉmit
tōṟṟam mika, nāḷum _ariyāṉ _uṟivu---tōṇipuram_āmē.

*10
mūṭu tuvar_āṭiyiṉar, vēṭam nili kāṭṭum _amaṇ_ātar,
kēṭupala colliṭuvar; _am moḻi keṭuttu, _aṭiviṉāṉ, _ak
kāṭu pati_āka naṭam_āṭi, maṭamātoṭu _iru kātil-
tōṭu kuḻi peytavartamakku _uṟivu---tōṇipuram_āmē.

*11
tuñcu _iruḷil niṉṟu naṭam_āṭi miku tōṇipuram mēya
mañcaṉi vaṇaṅku tiru ñāṉacampantaṉa colmāli,
tañcam _eṉa niṉṟu _ici moḻinta _aṭiyārkaḷ, taṭumāṟṟam
vañcam _ilar; neñcu _iruḷum nīṅki, _aruḷ peṟṟu vaḷarvārē.

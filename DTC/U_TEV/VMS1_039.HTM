This is the 2nd shrine on the North bank of the river Kāviri; now it is within the campus of the Aṇṇāmali University.

{1:39}__1+
{$} antamum ātiyum ākiya aṇṇal
the god who is the end and beginning all things.
vēṭkaḷa naṉṉakarār
and who is in the good city of Vētkaḷam.
[[aṇṇal- should be put in apposition with nakarār]]
āraḻal aṅkai amarntu ilaṅka
the fire of unbearable heat to shine, being held on the palm.
mantam muḻavam iyampa
the muḻavu to be played on a low tone
malaimakaḷ kāṇa niṉṟu āṭi
having danced standing to be witnessed by the dughter of the mountin.
cantam ilaṅku nakutalai, kaṅkai, taṇmatiyam ayalē tatumpa
the lughing skull having prominent holes, Kaṅki and the cool crescent to swing to and fro by each others side
[[cantam: holes, joints]]
venta veṇṇīṟumeypūcum
smears his form with well burnt white ashes.
[[nakarār ākiya aṇṇal ilaṅka, iyampa, āṭi, tatumpap, pūcum]]

{$} tāḻtalum caṭaitaṉai ēṟamuṭittu
having tied up high the matted locks above the nape when they were hanging low.
caṅka veṇtōṭu carittu ilaṅka
the white ear-rings made of conch were shining having slided down.
puṭaitaṉil pāriṭam cūḻa
to be surrounded at the sides by pāritam

vēṭkaḷa naṉṉakarār veḷḷai viṭaitaṉai ūrti nayantār

{$} pūtamum pal kaṇamum puṭaicūḻa
pūtam and many other groups to surround him on all sides.
pūmiyum viṇṇum uṭaṉ porunta
having pervaded the earth and the sky simultaneously.
cītamum vemmaiyum āki
Being the cold and heat
cīroṭu niṉṟa em celvar
our Lord who was famous
ōtamum kāṉalum cūrtaruvēlai vēṭkaḷa naṉṉakarār
he is in Vēṭkaḷam which is in proximity to the sea which is surrounded by waves and sea-shore groves.
uḷḷam kalantu icaiyāl eḻunta vētamum vēḷviyum ōvā vēṭkaḷam
Vētkaḷam where sacrifices and Vētam which rose with music, combining with the wish in the mind.

{$} arai pulkum aintalai āṭal aravam amaiya veṇ kōvaṇattōṭu acaittu
Having tied securely on the white loin-cloth the five headed dancing serpent which is round the waist, to become quiet.
varaipulku mārpil ōr āmaivāṅki aṇintavar
one who adorned his chest which is as strong as the mountin with the shell of the tortoise, having removed it from its body
[[Variant reading: aṇintavaratar]]
tirai pulku teṇkaṭal taṇkaḻi ōtam (cūḻnta)
surrounded by the tide of the cool back-water and the clear sea which is embraced by the waves
tēṉ nalam kāṉalil vaṇṭu paṇ ceyya
the bees to him melodies in the sea-shore grove which has good yield of honey.
[[tēṉkāṉalil: al, am can be taken as clitics;
vide. kūmpalam ṉakattalattu (Koviyar, II);
kamaḻalantuṟaivaṉ, nīṭalantuṟai (Koviyar, 88, 186, Koḷu and their commentaries]]
viraipulku paimpoḻil cūḻnta vēṭkaḷa naṉṉakarār-m
in the good city of Vēṭkaḷam which is surrounded by fragrant and green gardens.

{$} paṇ uṟu vaṇṭu aṟai koṉṟai alaṅkal, pālpurai nīṟu, veṇṇūl kiṭanta peṇṇuṟu mārpiṉar
has a chest on which there are a lady, a garland of koṉṟi on which bees hum melodies, ash as white as milk and a white thread.
vēṇār mummatil eytaperumāṉ
the Lord who discharged an arrow on the three forts of the enemies.
kaṇ uṟuneṟṟikalanta veṇ tiṅkaḷ kaṇṇiyar
has a white crescent which is like a chaplet and which is on the forehead where there is the third eye.

veṇṇiṟamāl viṭai aṇṇal

{$} kaṟivaḷar kuṉṟam eṭuttavaṉ kātal kaṇkavar aiṅkaṇaiyōṉ uṭalam, poṟivaḷar ār aḻal uṇṇa
the big fire with growing sparks to consume the body of the Cupid who has five arrows and has a form captivating the eyes and who is the dear son of that one who lifted the hill on which pepper-creeper was growing.
poṅkiya pūtapurāṇar
the ancient and pure one who become angry.
maṟivaḷar aṅkaiyar
has in his palm a young deer.
maṅkai ōr paṅkā
has a lady as his half.
maiññiṟamāṉ uritōl uṭai āṭai
has a garment made of the skin of the black elephant worn on the waist.
veṟivaḷar koṉṟai amtārār
has a garland of koṉṟi of increasing smell.
vēṭkaḷa naṉṉakarār
such one is in the good city of Vētkaḷam.
{1:39}__7+
{$} maṇ poṭikkoṇṭu erittu ōrcuṭalai
in the cremation ground when the world was destroyed to be reduced to dust.
māmalai vēntaṉ makaḷ makiḻa
the dughter of the king of the big mountin, the rejoice.
nuṇ poṭiccēra niṉṟu āṭi
one who danced for the minute dust to settle on his form.
noyyaṉa ceyyal ukantār
he felt joy in performing the subtle five functions
[[creation, protection, destruction, veiling and bestowing grace]]
kaṇpoṭiveṇtalai ōṭukai ēnti
holding in his hand a white skull from which the eye-balls have gone.
kālaṉaik kālāl kaṭintu ukantār
punished Kālaṉ with his leg and then bestowed his grace on him.
veṇpoṭiccēr tirumārpar
one who has a chest on which white ash is smeared.
vēṭkaḷa naṉkairār
such a one is in the good city of vētkaḷam
{1:39}__8+
{$} amutam amararkku aruḷi
having given the nectar to the immortals
[[amarar is used in the sense of tēvar, as they became immortals only after taking nectar]]
āḻtarumālkaṭal nañciṉaiyuṇṭār
He drank the poison of the big and deep ocean.
cūḻtarupāmpu arai ārttu
having tied a serpent round the waist.
cūlamōṭu oṇ maḻuēnti
holding the bright meḻu (a kind of axe) and the trident.
taṇmatiyam ayalē tatumpa
the cool crescent to become full by the side
tārtaru puṉcaṭai oṉṟiṉai vāṅki vīḻtarukaṅkai karantār
he concealed the Koṅki descending from heaven by taking one among the golden matted locks which fall on the nape.
vēṭkaḷa naṉṉakarār
such a one is in the good city of Vētkaḷam.
{1:39}__9+
{$} tiru oḷi kāṇiya pētuṟukiṉṟa ticaimukaṉum ticaimēl aḷanta karuvarai ēntiya mālum
the one who has four faces for the four directions and Māl who held up in his hand a black mountin which measured all the directions, got bewildered to see the holy light.
kaitoḻa niṉṟatum allāl
in addition to his standing before them to be worshipped with folded hands.
aruvarai olka eṭutta arakkaṉ āṭu eḻil tōḷkaḷ āḻattu amunta
the beautiful and victorious shoulders of arakkaṉ (Irāvaṇaṉ) who removed the great mountin to shake, the sink deeply.
veruvuṟa ūṉṟiya pemmāṉ
the great Lord who pressed his leg to be seized with fear.

{$} attam maṇtōy tuvarār amaṇkuṇṭar
the low amaṇar who have a saffron robe soaked in red ochre.
yātum allā uraiyē uraittu
speaking only empty meaningless words
poyttavam pēcuvatu allāl
only talking about penance hypocritically.
puṟaṉuṟai yātu oṉṟum koḷḷēṉ
Do not take as true anything in their empty utterances.
muttu aṉa veṇmuṟuvalumai añca
Umi who has white teeth like pearls to become frightened.
mūrival āṇaiyiṉ īruri pōrtta vittakar
an able person who covered his form with the skin newly flayed and wet with blood, of the very strong elephant.
vētamutalvar
He is the source of all the Vētam
vēṭkaḷanaṉṉakarār
such one is in the good city of Vēṭkaḷam

{$} viṇ iyal māṭam viḷaṅku oḷi vīti veṇkoṭi eṅkum virintu ilaṅka
in the bright streets where storeys seem to touch the sky, the white flags fly spreading themselves and shine everywhere.
naṇṇiya cīrvaḷar kāḻi naṟṟamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ
Ñāṉacampantaṉ of chaste Tamiḻ, a native of Kāḻi, with fame which reached him and is ever increasing.
peṇṇiṉallāḷ orupākam amarntu pēṇiya vēṭkaḷam mēl
on Vēṭkaḷam which is desired by the Lord who wished on one half a good lady.
moḻinta
composed by him (Ñāṉacampantaṉ)
paṇiyal pāṭal vallārkaḷ
those who are well versed in the verses which can be set to music.
paḻiyoṭupāvam ilarē
will have no reproach and sin, attached to them.

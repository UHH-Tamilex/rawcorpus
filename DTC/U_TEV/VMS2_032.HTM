{2:32}__1+
{$} tiruttikaḻ malaicciṟumiyōṭumikutēcar
Civaṉ is beautiful in the company of the young girl of the mountain, whose beauty is excellent.
uruttikaḻ eḻil kayilai veṟpil uṟaitaṟkē viruppuṭaiya aṟputar irukkum iṭam
the place where the marvellous and wonderful god who is very partial only to dwell in the mountain Kayilai of shining white colour
ērār maruttikaḻ poḻilkulavuvaṇ tiruvaiyāṟē
is fertile Tiruvaiyāṟu which is conspicuous by gardens of great beauty and eminent fragrance.
{2:32}__2+
{$} kantu amara untu pukai untal ila ēr viḷakku
with incense which sends fragrance in all directions, and a beautiful lamp which needs no trimming
[[kantu=kantam: fragrance]]
intiraṉ uṇarntupaṇi entaiiṭam
the place of our father whom Intiraṉ worships, knowing the fame of this shrine.
entai cantam maliyum tarumiṭainta poḻilcāra (1) vantavaḷi nantu aṇavuvaṇtiruvaiyāṟē
fertile Tiruvaiyaṟu where the air that comes near the gardens in which beautiful trees are dense everywhere, touches the garden in heaven.
[[nantu perhaps this is a part of Nantanavaṉam, which is Intiraṉ's flower garden; the meaning of this word is not clear]]
[[Variant reading: (1) vantavaṉiṉaintaṇavu]]
{2:32}__3+
{$} kaṭṭu vaṭam eṭṭum uṟu vaṭṭamuḻuvattil koṭṭukaram iṭṭaoli taṭṭum vakai
to play on the round muḻavu which is fastened by all the light leather straps to produce pleasing sounds by the hands which beat it.
nantikku iṭṭam mika naṭṭam avai iṭṭavariṭam
the place of Civaṉ who performed several dances to please very much Nanti
[[Nanti is one who plays on the muḻavu when Civaṉ dances.]]
cīrvaṭṭamatiluḷ tikaḻumvaṇtiruvaiyāṟē
is the fertile Tiruvaiyāṟu which is eminent inside the famous and circular wall of enclosure.
{2:32}__4+
{$} ōrvaṭṭattiṉ niḻal naṇṇi nālvar muṉivarkku aṉṟu eṇṇili maṟaip poruḷ virittavar iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who expounded in detail the countless truths contained in the vetam, long ago, to the four sages, sitting under the shade of the banyan tree.
cīrttaṇṇiṉ malicantu akiloṭu untivarupoṉṉimaṇṇiṉ micaivantu aṇavuvaṇtiruvaiyāṟē
is the fertile tiruvaiyāṟu where the river, poṉṉi, which pushes from the mountain sandal-wood trees and eagle-wood trees famous for their coolness, joins the plains.
{2:32}__5+
{$} veṉṟimiku tārukaṉatu āruyir maṭaṅka
even after putting an end to the precious life of carukaṉ who was previous by victorious in many battles.
kaṉṟivarukōpam miku kāḷikatam ōva
to cause the enraged and increasing anger and fury of Kāḻi to abate.
niṉṟunaṭam āṭi iṭam
the place where Civaṉ stood and danced with her for a long time.
nīṭumalarmēlāl maṉṟal maliyum poḻil koḷvaṇtiruvaiyāṟē
is the fertile Tiruvaiyāṟu which has gardens which are full of fragrance because of the long flowers.

(tiruvācakam, tiruccāḻal, 14)]]

{$} pūtamoṭupēykaḷpala pāṭa naṭamāṭi
dancing when pūtams and peys sing many songs.
pātamutal paiaravukoṇṭu aṇipeṟutti
having worn a hooded cobra in the leg as an ornament.
[[mutal is the seventh case suffix; aravuvār kaḻaliṇaikaḷ (tiruvācakam, tiruccatakam, 21) pātam mutal can be interpreted in this way also from head to foot every where he is wearing cobras; tirumuṭiyiṟ kaṇṇiyum mālaiyum pāmpu, tirumārpil āramum pāmpu-perumāṉ, tiruvaraiyiṟ kaṭṭiya kackaiyum pāmpu, porupuyattiṟ kaṅkaṇamum pāmpu (Kumarakuruparar, citamparacceyyuṭkōvai, 4)]]
kōtaiyar iṭumpalikoḷumparaṉ iṭam
the place of the supreme god who receives the alms offered by the ladies (of tārukāvaṉam)
pūmātavi maṇamkamaḻum vaṇtiruvaiyāṟē
is the fertile tiruvaiyāṟu where the flowers of the common delight of the woods spread their fragrance.
{2:32}__7+
{$} tuṉṉukuḻalmaṅkai umainaṅkai cuḷiveyta
Umai, a lady of distinction who is a girl of 12 to 13 years and who has luxuriant tresses of hair, to get angry.
piṉ orutavam ceytu uḻalpiññakaṉ
Civaṉ who wears a peacock's feather and who was the cause for wandering to perform penance afterwards.
aṅkē (1) cati! eṉṉa eṉṟu uraicey aṅkaṇaṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ whose eyes are full of campassion questioned on the slopes of the himālayan caṭi, what is the reason for your penance?
cīrmaṉṉum koṭaiyāḷarpayila vaṇtiruvaiyāṟē
is the fertile tiruvaiyāṟu where there are many eminent munificent people.
[[Variant reading: (1) caki]]

{$} irakkam ilkuṇattoṭu ulaku eṅkum nalivempōr arakkaṉ muṭipattu alai puyattoṭum aṭaṅkat (1) turakka
to drive away completely the strength of the harassing shoulders and ten heads of arakkaṉ (īrāvaṇaṉ) who waged cruel wars everywhere in the world, without pity

viralilciṟitu vaittavariṭam
the place of Civaṉ who gently pressed him with the small toe.
cīrvarakkaruṇaiyāḷar payilvaṇ tiruvaiyāṟē
is the fertile tiruvaiyāṟu where people of eminent compassion and fame live, crowded.
[[Variant reading: (1) turakkam]]

{$} paruttu uru atu ākiviṇaṭaintavaṉ, ōrpaṉṟiperutta uru atu āy ulaku iṭantavaṉum karuttu uru oṇāvakai nimirntavaṉiṭam
the place of Civan who grew tall into a column of fire, so that Piramaṉ who took the form of a common kite and reached the sky, and Māl who assumed the form of a pig and went digging the earth, could not comprehend the form of the Lord in their minds.

eḻil pukali maṉṉavaṉ meyññāṉappūcuraṉ uraittatamiḻpattum ivai vallār

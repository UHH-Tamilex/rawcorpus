{2:78}__1+
{$} oḷir iḷam piṟai ceṉṉimēl uṭaiyar
Civaṉ has on his head a shining crescent.
kōvaṇa āṭaiyar
wears a loin cloth
kuḷir iḷam maḻai tavaḻ poḻil kōlam nīr malku kāviri naḷir iḷam puṉal vār tuṟai naṅkai kaṅkaiyai naṇṇiṉār
is united with the lady, Kaṅki which has a long ghat of fresh and cool floods and the Kāviri which has beautiful and increasing water, and gardens in which the cool clouds laden with seem to crawl.
[[tuṟai is a generic epithet]]
miḷir iḷam poṟi araviṉār mēyatu viḷa nakaratē
the place the god who has a shining and young cobra with spots desired to stay is Viḷanakar.
{2:78}__2+
{$} akku aravu aṇi kalaṉ eṉa ataṉoṭu ārttatu ōr āmai pūṇṭu
having adorned himself with the shell of a tortoise in addition to the bones and cobras which Civaṉ wears as ornaments.
ukkavar cuṭunīṟu aṇintu
having smeared himself with the holy ash of those who were cremated by fire;
oḷi malku puṉaṟ kāvirip pukkavar
those who bathed in the Kāviri of shining water.
tuyar keṭuka eṉap pūcu veṇ poṭi mēviya mikkavar vaḻipāṭu ceyviḷanakar avar mēyatē
viḷanakar, is the place which Civaṉ desired where the superior persons who desired the white holy ash to be smeared on their bodies, saying Let our sufferings be destroyed
{2:78}__3+
{$} vāḷi cēr aṭaṅkār matil tolaiya nūṟiya ampiṉ
Civaṉ has an arrow with which he destroyed by demolishing the forts of the enemies who had arrows.
vēyt tōḷi pākam amarntavar
felt joy in having as a half a lady having shoulders as soft as bamboo. (The portion between two joints of a bamboo is compared to the shoulder;
[[PP_EXT: tōḷē......vēy amaikkaṇ iṭai puraii
(Akam, 152, 14-23)]]
uyarnta tol kaṭal 1. nañcuṇṭa kāḷam malkiya kaṇṭattar
has a neck of increasing black colour as a result of his drinking poison that rose in the ancient and great ocean.
katir viricuṭar muṭiyiṉar
has a caṭi coiled into a crown which spreads rays like the sun.
mīḷi ēṟu ukantu ēṟiṉār mēyatu viḷanakaratu
viḷanakar is the place that Civaṉ who rode with joy on an eminent bull, desires to dwell.
[[Variant reading: 1. nañcuṭaṉ]]
{2:78}__4+
{$} kāl viḷaṅku eri kaḻaliṉār
Civaṉ wears Kaḻal on his leg which is prominent and shining.
kai 1. ilaṅkiya vēliṉār
holds a shining trident in his hand
nūl viḷaṅkiya mārpiṉār
wears on his chest a prominent sacred thread.
nōy ilār piṟappum ilār
has no diseases and has no birth.
māl ilaṅku oḷi malkiya mācu ilā maṇi miṭaṟiṉār
has Māl who is prominent on one half; has a neck like the flawless sapphire whose brilliance is increasing;
[[māl viḷaṅkiya: mai arikkaṇṇiṉāḷum mālum ōr pākamāki (appar, Kōyil, tirunērici (1) 4);

māl oru pākam āki makiḻnta .nayttāṉaṉārē (do. neyttāṉam, tirunērici, 7);
ari alāl tēvi illai aiyaṉ aiyāṟaṉārkkē- (do. tiruviyāṟu, tirunērici, 5);
maṟikaṭal vaṇṇaṉ pākar (do. tiruvālavāi, tirunērici, 8);
pākam māl uṭaiyār pōlum (do. tirunākēccaram, 8);
tirumāl ōr paṅkaṉ taṉṉai (do. Kuṟinta tirunērici 7);
cīr ēṟu tirumāl ōr pākattāṉ kāṇ (do. tiruvārūr, taṇṭakam, (1) 5).]]
mēl viḷaṅku veṇ piṟaiyiṉār mēyatu viḷanakarē
viḷanakar is the place desired by Civaṉ on whose head the white crescent is shining
[[Variant reading: 1. viḷaṅkiya]]
{2:78}__5+
{$} maṟai paṉṉiṉār pāṭiṉār
Civaṉ revealed the vetams and sang them
(maṟai should be linked both with paṉṉiṉār and pāṭiṉār)
pāya cīrppaḻaṅkāvirittuṉṉu taṇ tuṟai muṉṉiṉār
those who approached the cool ghat of the old Kāviri whose fame has spread far and wide and where people crowd (The name of Civaṉ in this shrine is tuṟai kāṭṭum vaḷḷal it is hinted)
tūneṟi peṟuvār eṉa
to indicate that they will attin eternal bliss.
ceṉṉi tiṅkaḷaip poṅku arākkaṅkaiyōṭu uṭaṉ cērttiṉār
joined together along with Kaṅki an angry cobra, and a crescent, on his head.
miṉṉu poṉ puri nūliṉār mēyatu viḷanakaratu
viḷanakar is the place desired by Civaṉ who wears a shining sacred thread of three strands made of gold.
{2:78}__6+
{$} tēvarum amararkaḷum ticaikaḷ mēl uḷa teyvamum
the tevar who become so by doing sacrifices in this world, the immortals who were created as such, and the tēvar who are the guardian deities of the eight directions.
yāvarum aṟiyātatu ōr amaitiyāl taḻal uruviṉār
assumed the form of fire as not to be known by anyone of those above mentioned
mūvarum 1. ivar eṉṉavum mutal varum 2. ivar eṉṉavum mēvarum poruḷ āyiṉārmēyatu viḷanakaratu
viḷanakar is the place desired by the one who is loved by all as the cuse of all things and the trinity who perform creation, protection and destruction
[[Variant reading: 1. avar 2. avar]]
{2:78}__7+
{$} coltarum(m) maṟai pāṭiṉār
Civaṉ revealed the vētams which are to be memorized the words (m is an augmentation)
cuṭar viṭum caṭai muṭiyiṉār
has a caṭi coiled into a crown which sheds lustre.
kal taru (v) vaṭam kaiyiṉār
has a rosary of crytals in his hand (taruv vaṭam: v is an augmentation for rhythm)
kāvirit tuṟai kāṭṭiṉār
showed to the devotees the ghat in Kāviri (The name of Civaṉ in this temple is known as tuṟaikāṭṭum vaḷḷal)
mal tarum tiraḷ tōḷiṉār
has round and strong shoulders
mācu il veṇ poṭip pūciṉār
smeared himself with pure white holy ash.
vil taru maṇi miṭaṟiṉār mēyatu viḷanakaratu
viḷanakar is the place where Civaṉ who has a beautiful neck that spreads light, dwells with desire.
{2:78}__8+
{$} paṭar tarum caṭai muṭiyiṉār
-Civaṉ has a caṭi coiled into a crown which can spread at times.
paiṅkaḻal aṭi paravuvār aṭartarum piṇi keṭuka eṉa aruḷuvār
will bestow his grace on the devotees who prise his feet wearing golden Kaḻal, Let the diseases that afflict them be destroyed.
aravu araiyiṉār
has tied a cobra in the waist
viṭar tarum maṇi miṭaṟiṉār
has a neck that resembles the sapphire that is got from the clefts in the mountin.
miṉṉu poṉ puri nūliṉār
wears a glittering sacred thread of three strands made of gold (cf. 4th line, verse 5 of this decade)
miṭal tarum paṭai maḻuviṉār mēyatu vaḷanakaratu
viḷanakar is the place which Civaṉ who holds a strong weapon of a battle-axe, desires to dwell.
{2:78}__9+
{$} kai ilaṅkiya vēliṉār
Civaṉ holds in his hand a shining trident
tōliṉār
wears an elephant`s skin and a tiger`s skin
kari kāliṉār
the only survivor after everything else is destroyed and burnt. (kāl - kālam: time of destruction)
pai ilaṅku aravu alkulāḷ pākam ākiya paramaṉār
the supreme god who has as his half a lady whose waist is like the prominent hood of a cobra.
mai ilaṅku oḷi malkiya mācu ilā maṇi miṭaṟiṉār
has a neck which can be compared to a flawless sapphire and which has increasing brightness like the cloud (mai - mēkam: clouds; it means also blackness)
[[maittaḻaiyā niṉṟa māmiṭaṟṟu ampalavaṉ (Kōviyār, 102).]]
mey ilaṅku veṇṇīṟṟiṉār mēyatu vaḷanakaratu
viḷanakar is the place desired by Civaṉ who has smeared on his body shining white sacred ash.
{2:78}__10+
{$} uḷḷataṉ taṉaik kīḻ kāṇpaṉ eṉṟa māmaṇi vaṇṇaṉum
Māl who has the colour of the sapphire and who was determined to see below, the truth which is everywhere.
uḷḷataṉtaṉai mēl kāṇpaṉ eṉṟa māmalar aṇṇalum
the Piramaṉ who is seated in a big (lotus) flower and who was determined to sea above, the truth which is everywhere
uḷḷataṉtaṉaik kaṇṭilār
could not find out the truth in both places.
oḷi ār tarum caṭai muṭiyiṉ mēl uḷḷataṉ taṉaik kaṇṭilā oḷiyār mēyatē viḷanakar
viḷankar is the place desired by the light which could not be found in either place who has a caṭi coiled into a crown which is full of lustre
(uḷḷatu the thing that is eternal)
{2:78}__11+
{$} meṉciṟai vaṇṭu yāḻ mural viḷa nakarttuṟai mēviya naṉ piṟai nutal aṇṇalai
about Civaṉ who has the beautiful crescent on his forehead and who dwells in the ghat of viḷanakar where the soft-winged bees buzz like the music produced on yāḻ
caṇpai ñāṉacampantaṉ cīr iṉpuṟu tamiḻāṟ coṉṉa ēttuvār
those wh prise with the tamiḻ verses of fame which give pleasure, and were composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ of caṇpi.
viṉai nīṅkip pōy tuṉpu uṟu tuyaram ilar; tūneṟipeṟuvārkaḷē
will atti eternal bliss; will not suffer from afflictions and mental worries as a result of those, as they are free from Karmams.

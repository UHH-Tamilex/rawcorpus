{2:114}__1+
{$} vaṇṭu pāṭa mayilāla māṉkaṉṟu tuḷḷa varikkeṇṭaipāyac cuṉainīlam moṭṭu alarum kētāramē
Kētāram where the buds of blue nelumbo flowers that grow in the mountain springs blossom as the fish, Keṇtai leaps, the young one of deer frisks, peacocks dance and bees hum.
toṇṭar añcu kaḷiṟum aṭakkic curumpu ār malar iṇṭai kaṭṭi vaḻipāṭu ceyyum iṭam eṉpar(āl)
(People say it is the place where the devotees having controlled the five sense organs which are like elephants make circlets of flowers on which the superior variety of bees hum, place them on the head of Civaṉ and worship him.
{2:114}__2+
{$} pātam viṇṇōr palarum paravip paṇintu ēttavē vētam nāṉkum patiṉeṭṭoṭu āṟum virittārkku iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who expounded the four vetams, eighteen purāṇam-s and six aṅkam-s to be praised in the second and third persons when all the celestials pay homage to his feet.
tātu viṇṭa matuuṇṭu miṇṭivarum vaṇṭiṉam kītam pāṭa maṭamanti kēṭṭu ukaḷum kētāramē
is Kētāram where the young female monkeys leap over, listening to the songs sung by the swarms of bees which come thronging and exultantly drinking the honey from the flowers in which poller is unfolded.
{2:114}__3+
{$} mantipāyac carēlac corintum murintu ukkapūṅkentam nāṟak kiḷarum caṭai entai kētāram eṉpar (āl)
People say that Kētāram of our father who has an eminent caṭai which spreads fragrance of flowers which shed bending, and pouring, when the female monkeys suddently leap.
muntivantu muṉikaḷ palar purōtāyam mūḻki entai pemmāṉ eṉa niṉṟu iṟaiñcum iṭam
is the place where many sages come early and bathe before sunrise and bow to Civaṉ uttering the names our father, Lord'
[[purōtāyam seems to be corruption of purōtayam which means the time before sunrise nīrāṇṭa purōtāyam āṭappeṟṟōm (nāvukkaracar, maṟumaṟṟattiruttaṇṭakam, 3)]]
{2:114}__4+
{$} piḷḷai tuḷḷik kiḷaipayilva kēṭṭup piriyātu pōykkiḷḷai ēṉal koṇarntu vāyppeyyum kētāramē
Kētaram where the parrots on hearing the young ones of parrots leap and throng in the bamboos; go and bring ears of millet and put them into their beaks to feed them.
uḷḷam mikkār kutiraim mukattār orukkālarkaḷ
heavenly hosts who are very zealous, who have the face of horses and have one leg;
[[kompaip piṭittu orukālarkaḷ (cuntarar, Kētāram, 3)]]
eḷkal illā imaiyōrkaḷ cērum iṭam eṉpar (āl)
is the place where such celestials who have nothing to fear gather together.
{2:114}__5+
{$} mēḻi tāṅki uḻuvārkaḷ pōla (v) iraitēriya kēḻal pūḻti kiḷaikka maṇicintum kētāramē
kētāram where the wild pigs root up the dust with the purpose of searching for food like farmers who plough the fields holding the plough, gems are scattered (pūḻti-puḻuti this is like pōḻtu which is a corruption of poḻutu)
ūḻiūḻi uṇarvārkaḷ vētattiṉ oṇporuḷkaḷāl vāḻa entai eṉa vantu iṟaiñcum iṭam eṉpar (āl)
People say that it is the place where people who learn the vētam-s for many aeons come and bow before him saying, our father, may you live long!) with the help of their excellent meanings.
{2:114}__6+
{$} ēṟi māviṉ kaṉiyum palaviṉ iruñ cuḷaikaḷum kīṟi nāḷum mucuk kiḷaiyoṭu uṇṭu ukaḷum kētāramē
Kētāram where the langur climbs up on the mango tree and jack tree, rend the fruits and big pulps, and eat them with their relations, and jump over.
nīṟu pūci nilattu uṇṭu nīr mūḻki
bathing, smearing themselves with sacred ash and eating having the earth as a plate.
nīḷ varaitaṉmēl tēṟu cintai uṭaiyārkaḷ cērum iṭam eṉpar (āl)
People say is the place where people who have clear minds in the high mountains, gather together.
{2:114}__7+
{$} uṭainta kāṟṟukku uyarvēṅkai pūttu utirak kallaṟaikaḷ mēl kiṭanta vēṅkai ciṉamāmukam ceyyum kētāramē eṉpar
People say that Kētāram where the flowers of tall east indian kino trees which blossom when the wind blows suddenly on the rocks, resemble the lie tigers of angry faces;
[[PP_EXT:
karuṅkāl vēṅkai vī uku tuṟukal
irum pulik kuruḷaiyiṉ tōṉṟum kāṭṭiṭai
(Kuṟuntokai, 47, 1-2)
and its parallel quotations in Dr.V.Swaminatha Ayyar's edition]]
maṭantai pākattu aṭakki
containing on one half a lady.
maṟaiōti
chanting the vetams.
vāṉōr toḻa
to be worshipped with joined hands by the celestials.
toṭarnta nammēl viṉaitīrkka niṉṟārkku iṭam eṉpar (āl)
is the place of Civaṉ who remained to remove the accumulated acts which have been following us.
{2:114}__8+
{$} aravam munnīr aṇi ilaṅkaikkōṉai aruvarai taṉālveruva ūṉṟi viralāl aṭarttārkku iṭam eṉpar (āl)
People say that the place which belongs to Civaṉ who pressed down with his toe fixing it firmly, in the pre-eminent mountain, the King of ilaṅkai made beautiful by the ocean of roaring sound.
kuravam kōṅkam kuḷirpiṇṭi, ñāḻal, curapuṉṉaimēl kiramamāka vaṇṭu paṇceyyum kētāramē
is Kētāram where the bees hum like music in the proper manner in the bottleflower trees, common caung, cool asoka tree, fetid cassia and long leaved two-sepalled gamboge [kiramam may also mean a particular method of reciting vedic texts in the forms ab, bc, cd, etc.]
{2:114}__9+
{$} vīḻntu ceṟṟu niḻaṟku iṟaṅkum vēḻattiṉ veṇmaruppiṉaik kīḻntu ciṅkam kuruku uṇṇa muttu utirum kētāramē
Kētāram where the pearls drop off when the lion cleaves the white tusk of elephants which descend for shade, and falls on it kills it and eats the pith in the tusk

āḻantu kāṇār uyarntu eytakillār alamantār tāḻntu tammuṭi cāya niṉṟārkku iṭam eṉpar (āl)
people say it in the place of Civaṉ who remained for Māl, when he digged into the earth and Piramaṉ who flew into the sky, were not capable of seeing Civaṉ and being confounded in mind fell at his feet and bowed their heads.
{2:114}__10+
{$} kaṭukkaḷ tiṉṟu
eating gall-nuts.
kaḻimīṉ kavarvārkaḷ
amaṇar who catch fish in the backwater
[[kaḻikkaraippaṭu mīṉkavarvār amaṇaḻipparai (campantar, tiruvālāvāy (4), 10)]]
mācu uṭampiṉar
who have dirty bodies.
iṭukkaṇ uyppār (avar) eyta oṇṇāiṭam eṉpar (āl)
(people say) the place which could not be reached by those people who undergo distress.
aṭukka niṉṟu avaṟavuraikaḷ kēṭṭāṅku avar viṉaikaḷaik keṭukka niṉṟa perumāṉ uṟaikiṉṟa kētāramē
is Kētāram where Civaṉ who remains to remove the acts of those who listen to words of moral instruction, being fit to receive them.
{2:114}__11+
{$} vāynta cennel viḷaikaḻaṉi malkum vayaṟ kāḻiyāṉ
the native of kāḻi which has fields and an increasing number of fields in which red variety of paddy suited to the soil is produced.
ēyanta nīrkkōṭṭu imaiyōr uṟaikiṉṟa kētārattu
on Kētāram where in the peaks which have fitting water the celestials who do not wink, dwell.
āyntu coṉṉa aruntamiḻkaḷ pattum icai vallavar vēntar āki ulaku āṇṭa vīṭukati peṟuvarē
who are able to recite with music the rare tamiḻ verses done with deep thought will rule over this world becoming kings and will reach the higher state of existence which is eternal bliss.

{$} iṉṉampar īcaṉār
Civaṉ in Iṉṉampar
viṇṇavar makuṭakōṭi miṭainta cēvaṭiyar
has the rows of the crowns of the celestials croweded on his red feet (when they bow before him)
peṇ orupākar
has a lady on one half
pēṭu ali āṇar
is hermaphrodite with female characters predominating;
[[is a hermaphrodite being which is neither man nor woman wholly, is male]]
vaṇṇam māl ayaṉum kāṇāmāl varai eriyar
assumed the form of a fire as big as a mountin the nature of which could not be known by Māl and Ayaṉ
eṇuru anēkar
is many in the eight forms, (has no end to his form if we count them;
[[eṇ uru can be taken as a compound of eṭṭu uru]]
[[pōlum in this verse and the succeeding verses are expletive]]

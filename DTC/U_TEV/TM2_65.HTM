• patikam: [2:65]
  • talam: tirup piramapuram (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: kāntāram
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.173

*1
kaṟai _aṇi vēl _ilarpōlum; kapālam tarittilarpōlum;
maṟaiyum naviṉṟilarpōlum; mācuṇam _ārttilarpōlum;
paṟaiyum karattu _ilarpōlum; pācam piṭittilarpōlum;
piṟaiyum caṭaikku _ilarpōlum---piramapuram _amarntārē.

*2
kūr_ampu_atu _ilarpōlum; kokkiṉ_iṟaku _ilarpōlum;
_āramum pūṇṭilarpōlum; _āmai _aṇintilarpōlum;
tārum caṭaikku _ilarpōlum; caṇṭikku _aruḷilarpōlum;
pērum pala _ilarpōlum---piramapuram _amarntārē.

*3
citta vaṭivu _ilarpōlum; tēcam tirintilarpōlum;
katti varum kaṭuṅkāḷi kataṅkaḷ tavirttilarpōlum;
meytta nayaṉam _iṭantārkku _āḻi _aḷittilarpōlum;
pittavaṭivu _ilarpōlum---piramapuram _amarntārē.

*4
naccu_aravu _āṭṭilarpōlum; nañcam miṭaṟṟu _ilarpōlum;
kaccut tarittilarpōlum; kaṅkai tarittilarpōlum;
moycca vaṉpēy _ilarpōlum; muppuram _eytilarpōlum;
piccai _irantilarpōlum---piramapuram _amarntārē.

*5
tōṭu cevikku _ilarpōlum; cūlam piṭittilarpōlum;
_āṭu taṭakkai valiya _āṉai _urittilarpōlum;
_ōṭu karattu _ilarpōlum; _oḷ_aḻal kai _ilarpōlum---
pīṭu mikuttu _eḻu celvap piramapuram _amarntārē.

*6
viṇṇavar kaṇṭilarpōlum; vēḷvi _aḻittilarpōlum;
_aṇṇal _ayaṉtalai vīḻa, _aṉṟum _aṟuttilarpōlum;
vaṇṇa _elumpiṉoṭu _akkuvaṭaṅkaḷ tarittilarpōlum---
peṇ_iṉam moyttu _eḻu celvap piramapuram _amarntārē.

*7
paṉṟiyiṉkompu _ilarpōlum; pārttaṟku _aruḷilarpōlum;
kaṉṟiya kālaṉai vīḻak kālkoṭu pāyntilarpōlum;
tuṉṟu piṇam cuṭukāṭṭil _āṭit tutaintilar pōlum---
piṉṟiyum pīṭum perukum piramapuram _amarntārē.

*8
paracu tarittilarpōlum; paṭutalai pūṇṭilarpōlum;
_aracaṉ_ilaṅkaiyarkōṉai _aṉṟum _aṭarttilarpōlum;
purai cey puṉattu _iḷamāṉum, puliyiṉ_ataḷ, _ilarpōlum---
piracamalarppoḻil cūḻnta piramapuram _amarntārē.

*9
_aṭi muṭi māl _ayaṉ tēṭa, _aṉṟum _aḷappilarpōlum;
kaṭimalar_aiṅkaṇai vēḷaik kaṉala viḻittilarpōlum;
paṭi malarppālaṉukku_ākap pāṟkaṭal _īntilarpōlum---
piṭinaṭai mātar perukum piramapuram _amarntārē.

*10
veṟṟu_araic cīvarattārkku veḷippaṭa niṉṟilarpōlum;
_aṟṟavar, _ālniḻal, nālvarkku _aṟaṅkaḷ _uraittilarpōlum;
_uṟṟavar _oṉṟu _ilarpōlum; _ōṭu muṭikku _ilarpōlum;
peṟṟamum _ūrntilarpōlum---piramapuram _amarntārē.

*11
peṇ_uru _āṇ_uru _allāp piramapuranakar mēya
_aṇṇal ceyyātaṉa_ellām _aṟintu, vakaivakaiyālē,
naṇṇiya ñāṉacampantaṉ naviṉṟaṉapattum vallārkaḷ,
viṇṇavaroṭu _iṉitu_āka vīṟṟiruppār, _avartāmē.

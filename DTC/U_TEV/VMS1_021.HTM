{1:21}__1+
{$} puvam vaḷi, kaṉal, puṉal, puvi, kalai uraimaṟai tirikuṇam amarneṟi, tivammalicurar mutaliyar, tikaḻtarum uyir avai avai tamapavamali toḻil(atu) niṉaivoṭu patumanaṉmalar (atu) maruviyacivaṉatu civapuram niṉaipavar
those who meditate upon civapuram of Civaṉ who is seated on a good lotus flower with the idea of endowing functions pertining to their births, to the living beings which are eminent, the teVar, etc. who flourish in the heaven, the desirable paths, the three qualities of Cattuvam, rājācam and tāmoṭam, the vētams that deal with the flame of god, arts, the space, the air fire, water, earth.
ceḻunilaṉiṉil nilaiveṟuvarē
will obtin a permanent life in the prosperous world.
{1:21}__2+
{$} malaipalavaḷartaru puviyiṭai
in the earth where many mountins grow.
maṟaitaruvaḻimalimaṉitarkaḷ
man who follow faithfully the path lid down in the vētams.
nilaimalicurarmutal ulakukaḷ nilaipeṟuvakai niṉaivoṭu
with the intention of being permanent till the end of their span of life, in the heaven of the tēvar whose lives are longer, and other worlds.
mikum alaimali kaṭal naṭu aṟituyil amarariuru iyal paraṉuṟai pati, cilaimalimatiḷ civapuram niṉaipavar
those who meditate upon civapuram which has a fortified wall constructed with stones and which is the dwelling place of the supreme Civaṉ who assumes the form, ari(Māl) who sleeps in the centre of the ocean which has raging and increasing waves, being conscious of the outer world
[[aṟituyil yogic sleep]]
tirumakaḷoṭu tikaḻvarē
will shine with wealth.
{1:21}__3+
{$} paḻutu ilakaṭal puṭai taḻuviya paṭi mutaliya ulakukaḷ
the worlds beginning from this earth which is surrounded by the ocean and which is without any defect.
malikuḻuviya curar piṟar maṉitarkaḷ kulam malitarum uyir avai avai
the tēvar of increasing groups, other human beings and other groups of living beings.
muḻuvatum aḻivakai niṉaivoṭumutal uruiyal paraṉ uṟaipati ceḻumaṇi aṇi civapuram nakar toḻumavar pukaḻmikum ulakilē
the fame of those people who worship with joined hands the city of civapuram made beautiful by big precious stones and which is the dwelling place of the supreme god who assumes the form of uruttiraṉ who is the cuse for destroying them completely with that intention.
[[The first three verses describes Civaṉ as creator, sustiner and destroyer. The three functions are done out of his volition]].
{1:21}__4+
{$} naṟaimalitarum aḷaṟoṭu
with the much fragrant sandalpaste.
mukai naku malar, pukai, mikuvaḷaroḷi, niṟaipuṉal koṭutaṉainiṉaivoṭu niyatamum vaḻipaṭum aṭiyavar
the devotees who dily worship him intentionally, with plenty of water, very bright lamps; incense, and blossomed flowers which have left the stage of buds.
kuṟaivu ilapatam aṇaitara aruḷkuṇam uṭai iṟai uṟaivaṉapati
the beautiful city where the god dwells, who has the nature of granting higher states of existence which are without any wants.
ciṟai puṉal amar civapuram (atu) niṉaipavar ceyamakaḷ talaivar
and those who meditate upon civapuram which has water with dams, will be the Lord of the goddess of victory.
{1:21}__5+
{$} ciṉammali aṟupakai mikupoṟi citai taruvakaivaḷi niṟuviya
having retined and controlled the vital breath so as to destroy the evil acts of the five senses and six enemies, (the six emotions) beginning with excessive anger.
maṉaṉ uṇarvoṭum
with the help of meditation
malarmicai eḻutaruporuḷ niyatamum uṇarpavar
those whose realise always the object that exists in the lotus flower in the fontanells which is sid to have a thousand petals.
taṉatu eḻil uru atukoṭu aṭaitakuparaṉ uṟaivatu nakar matiḷkaṉam maruviya civapuram niṉaipavar
and who meditate on civapuram on whose fortified walls clouds stay and which is the city where the supreme and great god who reaches them with his beautiful form.
kalaimakaḷ taranikaḻvar/
will live as the goddess of learning grants knowledge.
{1:21}__6+
{$} curutikaḷ pala nalamutalkalai tukaḷ aṟuvakai payilvoṭu miku uruiyal ulaku avai (1) pukaḻtara vaḻi oḻukum mey uṟupoṟi oḻi (2) arutavam muyalpavar
those who undertake severe penance to destroy the senses that exist in the body and who conduct themselves in the proper way to be prised by the people of the worlds which have visible forms, and who have practice of chanting of vētams and many other good arts without any faults.
taṉatu aṭi aṭaivakai niṉaiparaṉ uṟaipati tiruvaruḷ civapuram niṉaipavar
and those who meditate upon civapuram which grants the highest wealth of eternal bliss and which is the abode of the supreme Civaṉ who thinks of those people reaching his feet.
[[Variant reading: (1) pukaṭara (2) tarutavam]]
{1:21}__7+
{$} katammiku karuuruvoṭu (1) ukiriṭai vaṭavarai kaṇakaṇaeṉa
the mountin, meru which is in the north to make a sound resembling gaṇa gaṇa' in its nil assuming a great form with excessive anger.
[[kaṇakaṇa: onomatopoeia.]]
matammikuneṭumukaṉ amarvaḷaimati tikaḻeyiṟataṉ nutimicai itam amarpuvi (atu) niṟuviya eḻil ari
beautiful ari (Māl) who fixed on a firm basis the earth in which many confortable things exist, on the tip of its tusk which is on the tall and very strong face, and which resembles in shape the crescent
[[karumai-perumai not blackness]]
vaḻipaṭa aruḷceyta patam uṭaiyavaṉ amar civapuram niṉaipavar
those who meditate upon civapuram where Civaṉ granted the version of his feet when Māl worshipped him at this shrine.
nilavuvar paṭiyilē
will shine in this world with fame.

{1:21}__8+
{$} acaivu uṟutavam muyalviṉil ayaṉ aruḷiṉil varupalikoṭu
with the strength got from the grace granted by Ayaṉ, as a result of having performed penance with great exhustion
[[That Irāvaṇaṉ performed severe penance and obtined great strength from Ayaṉ is mentioned in uttara Kāṇṭam. Irāvaṇaṉ piṟappuppaṭalam, verses, 26-28.]]
civaṉ icai kayilaiyai eḻutaruvakai irupatukaram (avai) niṟuviya nicicaraṉ muṭiuṭaitaraoruviral paṇikoḷum avaṉ uṟaipati
the abode of Civaṉ who put to use a single toe to break the crowns of Irāvaṇaṉ who set up his twenty hands to uproot the mountin, Kayili where Civaṉ was seated.
ticaimali civapuram niṉaipavar ceḻu nilaṉil nikaḻvu uṭaiyar
those who meditate upon civapuram which is well-known in all the quarters, will live always in this fertile world.
[[uṟaipatiyākiya civapuram is the syntactical link]]
{1:21}__9+
{$} aṭalmali paṭaiari ayaṉoṭum aṟivu ariyatu ōr aḻal malitarucuṭar uruvoṭu nikaḻtara
when Civaṉ appeared in a brilliant form of five which could not be known by Ayaṉ, and ari (Māl) who has the strong weapon of a discus.
avar veruvoṭu tuticeya etir viṭam malikaḷam nutal amarkaṇ aru uṭai uru veḷipaṭum avaṉ nakar tiṭam malipoḻil eḻilcivapuram niṉaipavar vaḻipuvi tikaḻumē
the family of those who meditate upon beautiful civapuram which has strong gardens and which is the city of Civaṉ who taking pity on them became manifest with a form having a frontal eye and a neck where poison is full, when they both prised him with fear, will be eminent in this world.
{1:21}__10+
{$} kuṇam aṟivukaḷ nilaiyila; poruḷ urai maruviya poruḷkaḷum ila ti(ṇ)ṇam eṉum avaroṭu cetumatimiku camaṇarum malitamatukai uṇal uṭaiyavarum uṇarvaruparaṉ uṟaipati; ulakiṉil nala kaṇam maruviya civapuram niṉaipavar
those who meditate on civapuram where good groups of devotees combine together in this world and which is the abode of the supreme god who could not be understood by the camaṇar who eat food in their hands who have minds of increasing evil ideas, and buddhists who assert that characters and knowledge are not permanent and the things in this world are also such.
eḻil uru uṭaiyavarkaḷ
will have beautiful bodies.
{1:21}__11+
{$} tikaḻcivapura nakar maruviya avaṉ aṭi iṇaipaṇicirapuranakar iṟai tamiḻvirakaṉatu urai nalam mali orupatum navilpavar
those who recite the ten beneficial verses which are the words of (campantaṉ) one proficient in tamiḻ, who is a native of the city of cirapuram, and who worships the feet of Civaṉ who dwells in the eminent city of civapuram.
nikaḻkulam, nilam, niṟaitiru, uru, nikaril koṭai, mikucayamakaḷ, pukaḻ, puvivaḷarvaḻi, (1) aṭimaiyiṉ mikai puṇartaranalam mikuvar
will excel in all virtues when their family continues without any break, they have landed property, wealth without any wants, beautiful body, liberality without any equal, superior goddess of victory, the fortune of being hereditary worshippers of god, all these joining them.
[[Variant reading:(1) aṭimaiyiṉ micai, aṭimaiyiṉ mika ]]

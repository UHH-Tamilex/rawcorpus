{2:59}__1+
{$} nalam koḷmuttum maṇiyum aṇiyum tiraḷ ōtak kalaṅkaḷ taṉṉiṉ koṇṭu karaicēr kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the ships that sil on the accumulated waves, reach the shore with beautiful pearls, other precious stones and ornaments.
valam koḷ maḻu oṉṟu uṭaiyāy
one who possesses a victorious battle axe!
viṭaiyāy eṉa ēnti
prising one who has a bull'
arunōy
the diseases of birth which cannot be cured by other means.
aṭaiyā, alaṅkal cūṭṭa vallārkku
will not reach those who can adorn the God with garlands.
[[syntactical link: ēttic cūṭṭavallārkku arunōy aṭaiyā]]
{2:59}__2+
{$} ūrār uvaric caṅkam vaṅkam koṇṭu
Having been brought by the ships the conches of the sea which move slowly.
kār ār ōtam
the waves of the sea from which clouds draw their water.
karaimēl uyarttum kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where they are heaped to a great height on the shore.
nīrār caṭaiyāy neṟṟikkaṇṇā eṉṟu eṉṟu pēr āyiramum pitaṟṟat tīrum piṇi (tāṉē)
all disease will be removed if one bables all the thousand names without ceasing, prising the god as, ``one who has on his caṭi water! one who has a frontal eye!''
{2:59}__3+
{$} vaṭikoḷ poḻiliṉ maḻalai varivaṇṭu icai ceyya
when the tender bees with lines on their bodies hum like music in the gardens which are mintined in perfect condition.
kaṭi koḷ pōtil teṉṟal aṇaiyum kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the balmy southern breeze comes in contact with the buds of fragrance just before they unfold their petals.
muṭikoḷ caṭaiyāy! mutalvā eṉṟu muyaṉṟu ētti
Having prised with the aid of their virtue done in previous births the god as one who has dressed the caṭi as a crown! the first cuse! (the origin of all things!)
aṭikai toḻuvārkku allal avalam illai
there will be no anxiety and suffering for those who worship his feet with their hands.
{2:59}__4+
{$} maṉaikkē ēṟa
to enter into the houses.
vaḷañcey pavaḷam vaḷar muttam kaṉaikkum kaṭaluḷ ōtam ēṟum kalikkāḻi
in Kāli of great bustle where the waves of the roaring sea rise to push the mature coral and mature pearls.
paṉaikkaip pakaṭṭu īr uriyāy periyāy eṉappēṇi
Having cherished with love the god as one who has a skin of wet with the blood of an elephant whose trunk is like the palmyra! great person!'
niṉaikkavalla aṭiyār neñciṉ nallār
the devotees who are able think him as such, are people having good minds.
{2:59}__5+
{$} paruti iyaṅkum pāril
in this world where the sun moves.
cīrār paṇiyālē, karutiviṇṇōr maṇṇōr virumpum kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the celestials and inhabitants of this world desire to gather, having in their minds the eminent service to god.
curuti maṟai nāṉku āṉa cemmai taruvāṉaikkaruti
thinking of him who gave the eminent works of maṟi (Vētam) which is memorized by hearing (and not written).
eḻumiṉ
wake up from your sleep! People of this world!
vaḻuvāvaṇmai tuyarpōm
all the sufferings will go away without fail.
{2:59}__6+
{$} mantam maruvum poḻilil eḻilār matu uṇṭu
Having drunk the sweet honey in the parks where the slow-moving southern breeze is staying.
kantam maruva varivaṇṭu icai cey kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the bees having lines on them hum like music as they have come into contact with fragrance.
panta nīṅka aruḷum paraṉē' eṉa ēttic cintai ceyvār
those devotees who meditate upon god prising him, Most exalted one who bestows his grace, to remove all bondage'.
cemmai nīṅkātu iruppārē
are those who stand firmly in the path of righteousness.
{2:59}__7+
{$} puyal ārpūmi nāmam ōti
Having repeated the names of God in the world fed by rin.
pukaḻ malkak kayal ār kaṇṇār paṇ ār oli cey kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle where the ladies whose eyes are comparable to the carp fish produce the sound of music to spread the fame of god.
payilvāṉ taṉṉaippatti ārattoḻutu ētta muyalvārtammēl
on those who try to worship and prise the god who is permanent so that their devotion may become satisfactory.
vemmaikkūṟṟam muṭukātu
the cruel god of death will not hasten (to snatch their lives)
{2:59}__8+
{$} arakkaṉ muṭitōḷ neriya aṭarttāṉ
God who pressed down to crush the heads and shoulders of Arakkaṉ (Iravaṇaṉ)
aṭiyārkkuk karakkakillātu aruḷ ceyperumāṉ
and who grants his grace to his devotees without being able to conceal it.
kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle.
parakkum pukaḻāṉ taṉṉai ēttip paṇivārmēl perukkum iṉpaṉ
Happiness will increase for people who prise and worship him whose fame spreads everywhere.
tuṉpam āṉa piṇipōm
diseases which are the cuses for sufferings will vanish.
{2:59}__9+
{$} māṇāy ulakam koṇṭa mālum
Māl who received the world as a gift in the form of a bachelor (from Māvali)
malarōṉum
and the Piramaṉ seated in the (lotus) flower.
kāṇāvaṇṇam eriyāy nimirntāṉ
He who shot up as a column of fire so as not to be seen (by them)
pūṇ ārmulaiyāḷ paṅkattāṉaip pukaḻntu ētti
prising and worshipping the god who has on one half a lady who wears ornaments on her breasts.
kōṇā neñcam uṭaiyārkkuk kuṟṟam illai
there will be no pin for those whose minds do not deviate from the path of virtue.
{2:59}__10+
{$} ariyāṉ
god who is difficult to be recognized by
añci allal moḻintu tirivār amaṇ ātar
the ignorant camaṇar who speak distressing words behind the back, being afrid of saying them to the face and wander.
kañci kālai uṇpārkku
and by the buddhists who have their morning gruel
[[kañci: liquid food]]
kalikkāḻi
in Kāḻi of great bustle
tañcamāy talaivaṉtaṉṉai niṉaivarkaḷ
Those who think of god as their refuge.
tuñcal illā nalla ulakam peṟuvār
will attin the good world of bliss which does not know destruction.
{2:59}__11+
{$} ūḻi āya pāril ōṅkum uyarcelvak, kāḻi īcaṉ kaḻalē pēṇum campantaṉ
Campantaṉ who cherishes with regard only the feet of Civaṉ in Kāḻi having indestructible wealth, which floated above the floods of the deluge in this world which is destroyed at the end of the aeon.
tāḻum maṉattāl urainta tamiḻkaḷ ivai vallār
those who are able to say the Tamiḻ verses composed with a mind engrossed in God.
(vāḻi) nīṅkā vāṉōr ulakiṉ vāḻvār
will dwell in heaven which they will not leave.

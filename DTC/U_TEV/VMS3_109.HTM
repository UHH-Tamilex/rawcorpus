[[This decade is noteworthy; in every verses four shrines are combined; therefore it is called kūṭaṟ catukkam, which means four are combined together. In every verses the anecdote about Māl and Ayaṉ is mentioned]]

{3:109}__1+
{$}
maṇ atu (1) uṇṭa ari malarōṉ kāṇā veṇṇāval (2) virumpum mayēntirarum
Civaṉ in Mayēntiram who desires the white jaumoon plum tree to dwell, who cannot be found out by Ari who swallowed the world, and Piramaṉ seated on a (lotus) flower.
kaṇ atu ōṅkiya kayilaiyārum
who resides in Kayilai which has extensive area
[[kaṇ space]]
aṇṇal ārūr āti āṉaikkāvē
and the one who is in the eminent Ārūr is the god who is the origin of all things; who is in Āṉaikkā
[[Variant reading: (1) uṇṭa māl (2) uṟaiyum mayēntiramum]]
{3:109}__2+
{$}
vantu māl ayaṉ avar kāṇpu ariyār
Civaṉ who was difficult to be known by Māl and Ayaṉ coming near him.
venta veṇṇīṟu aṇi mayēntirarum
who is in Mayēntiram and who besmears himself with well-burnt holy white ash
kanta vār caṭaiyuṭaik kayilaiyārum
who is in Kayilai and has a fragrant long caṭai.
am taṇ ārūr āti āṉaikkāvē
Āṉaikka is the shrine of the origin of all things who is in beautiful and cool Ārūr.
{3:109}__3+
{$}
māl ayaṉ tēṭiya mayēntirarum
Civaṉ in Mayēntiram whom Māl and Ayaṉ searched
kālaṉai uyir koṇṭa kayilaiyārum
who is in Kayilai, and took away the life of the god of death.
vēlaiyatu ōṅkum veṇṇāvalārum
and who is under the white jaumoon plum tree under which water springs like the sea
[[Āṉaikkā is the shrine important for water, one of the five elements.]]
ālai ārūr āti āṉaikkā
is Āṉaikkā of the origin of all things who is in Ārūr noted for sugar-canes.
{3:109}__4+
{$}
karuṭaṉai ēṟu ari (1) ayaṉār kāṇār
Civaṉ who could not be found out by Ari who rides on Karuṭaṉ, and [by] Ayaṉ
[[ār: a termination used in the sense of ridiculing Ayaṉ.]]
veruḷ viṭai ēṟiya mayēntirarum
who rides on a bull which is feared by enemies and is in Mayēṉtiram
karuḷ taru kaṇṭattu em kayilaiyārum
who stays in Kayilai and has a black neck.
aruḷaṉ ārūr āti āṉaikkāvē
the origin of all things who is the embodiment of grace and who is in Ārūr is in Āṉaikkā
[[Variant reading: (1) ayaṉōr]]
{3:109}__5+
{$}
matucūtaṉaṉ nāṉ mukaṉ vaṇaṅka ariyār
Civaṉ who is difficult to be worshipped by Matucūtaṉaṉ (Māl) and Piramaṉ of four faces.
[[Matucūtaṉaṉ: Māl killed one acuraṉ named Matu: so he is called Matucūtaṉaṉ]]
mati atu colliya mayēntirarum
who revealed the Ākamam-s in Mayēṉtira mountain.
katir mulai pulkiya kayilaiyārum
who is in Kayilai and is embraced by Umai who has brilliant breasts
ārūr atiyaṉ
the supreme god above all other deities in Ārūr
[[atikaṉ has become atiyaṉ]]
āṉaikkā āti
is the origin of all things in Āṉaikkā
{3:109}__6+
{$}
cakkaram vēṇṭum māl piramaṉ kāṇā mikkavar
superior person who could not be seen by Māl who got a discus on request, and by Piramaṉ
kayilai mayēntirarum
who is in Kayilai and Mayēṉtiram
takkaṉaittalai ari taḻal uruvar
who has a body like fire and who cut off the head of Takkaṉ
akku aṇiyavar ārūr āṉaikkā
who has as his ornaments uruttirākkam is in Ārūr and Āṉaikkā
[[It is only in this verse and the 10th that the word āti is not found;
āti has a special significance with Civaṉ in this shrine;
in Cuntarar's decade on this shrine the word āti occurs in every verse]]
{3:109}__7+
{$}
kaṇṇaṉum nāṉmukaṉ (um) kāṇpu ariyār
Civaṉ who could not be seen by Kaṇṇaṉ (Māl) and Piramaṉ of four faces.
veṇṇāval virumpum mayēntirarum
who dwells in Mayēṉtiram and desires to remain under the white jaumoon plum tree
[[veṇṇāval is the tree in this shrine under which the linkam is established]]
kaṇṇapparkku aruḷ ceyta kayilai eṅkaḷ ārūr aṇṇal āṉaikkā āti
who is in Kayilai, who granted his grace to Kaṇṇappar and our chief in Ārūr is the origin of all things who is in Āṉaikka
[[Kannaṉ is a corruption of a prakrit word meaning one who is black.]]
{3:109}__8+
{$}
kaṭal vaṇṇaṉ nāṉmukaṉ kāṇpu ariyār
Civaṉ is difficult to be seen by Māl who has the colour of the sea, and Piramaṉ of four faces.
taṭavarai arakkaṉait talai nerittār
he crushed the heads of arakkaṉ under the big mountain.
viṭam (atu) uṇṭa emmayēntirarum
our Lord in Mayēntiram who drank the poison.
aṭal viṭai ārūr
and one who has a strong bull and dwells in Ārūr.
āṉaikkā āti
is the first supreme being who is in Āṉaikkā.
{3:109}__9+
{$}
ātimāl ayaṉ kāṇpu ariyār
the first supreme being whom Māl and Ayaṉ could not know
vētaṅkaḷ tuti ceyum mayēntirarum
and the one in Mayēṉtiram who is praised by the Vētam-s.
kātil ōr kuḻaiyuṭaik kayilaiyārum
and one who has on one ear a men's earring.
ārūr entai
and our father in Ārūr
āti āṉaikkā
is the first supreme being dwelling in Āṉaikkā

aṟivu il amaṇ puttar aṟivu koḷḷēl
(People of this world!) Do not pay heed to the knowledge of the amaṇ and buddhists who have no good knowledge.
veṟiya māṉ karattu ārūr mayēntirarum
Civaṉ who holds in his hands a deer with frightened looks is in Ārūr and Mayēṉtiram
maṟikaṭalōṉ ayaṉ tēṭat tāṉum aṟivu aru kayilaiyōṉ āṉaikkā
and who is in Kayilai who could not be known by Māl who is sleeping in the ocean which has rolling waves, and Ayaṉ when both searched (his head and feet) is in Āṉaikkā
[[There is no āti in this verse.]]
{3:109}__11+
{$}
ēṉam māl ayaṉ avar kāṇpu ariyār
Civaṉ whom Māl who took the form of a pig and Ayaṉ (who took the form of a swan) could not find out.
kāṉam ār kayilai naṉ mayēntirarum
and who is Kayilai which has many gardens, and in Mayēṉtiram
āṉa ārūr āti āṉaikkāvai
is in Āṉaikkā belonging to the supreme being who is in Ārūr.
ñāṉacampantaṉ tamiḻ collumē
(People of the world!) recite the tamiḻ verses composed by Ñāṉacampantaṉ.

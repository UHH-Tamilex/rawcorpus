{5:60}__1+
{$} ētum oṉṟum aṟivilar āyiṉum
if people who do not know even a little about anything.
ōti añcu eḻuttum(m) uṇarvārkaṭku
to those who understand the meaning of the mantiram of five letters, chanting them.
pētam iṉṟi
without making any distinction.
avaravar uḷḷattē
in the hearts of such people individually.
mātum tāmum makiḻvar māṟpēṟarē
the Lord in Māṟpēṟu will dwell with joy, along with the lady, Umai.
{5:60}__2+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
araṉ nāmaṅkaḷ niccalum niṉaiyāy viṉaipōy aṟa
you meditate on the name of Araṉ (Civaṉ) daily, for your acts to leave you and meet with destruction.
accam illai
there is no cause for fear.
kaccamāviṭam uṇṭa kaṇṭā eṉa
if you say the Lord who has a neck which consumed the poison which was bitter in taste (kacca-kaitta; pōli)
vaitta māniti āvar māṟpēṟarē
the Lord in māṟpēṟu will be useful like the great hoarded wealth
[[vaicca-vaitta; pōli]]
{5:60}__3+
{$} cāttiram palapēcum caḻakkarkāḷ
wicked people who talk about many religious treatises and branches of knowledge!
kōttiramum kulamum koṇṭu eṉ ceyvīr
what could you do with high lineage and noble birth?
pāttiram civam eṉṟu paṇitirēl
if you pay homage to civam who is worthy of it.
(1) māttiraikkuḷ aruḷum māṟpēṟarē
the Lord in māṟpēṟu will grant his grace within an instant.
[[Variant reading: (1) māttiraikkaṇaruḷum]]
{5:60}__4+
{$} ēḻaikaḷ
People who are not endowed with intelligence!
iruntu colluvaṉ
I tell you after deep pondering.
kēṇmiṉkaḷ
listen to what I say.
aruntavam tarum añcu eḻuttu ōtiṉāl
if you utter the mantiram of five letters it will give you the fruit of austere penance.
poruntu nōy piṇipōkat turappatu ōr maruntum ākuvar maṉṉum māṟpēṟarē
Civaṉ who dwells permanently in māṟpēṟu will also be the remedy to drive away the diseases of the body and mental disorders completely.
{5:60}__5+
{$} taraṇiyīr
People of this world!
cāṟṟic colluvaṉ
I shall announce publicly to be known to all of you.
kēṇmiṉ
listen.
ēṟṟiṉ mēl varuvāṉ kaḻal ēttiṉāl
if you praise the feet of Civaṉ who comes riding on a bull.
kūṟṟai nīkki
preventing the god of death from taking away your lives.
kuṟaivu aṟuttu
completely removing your deficiencies
āḷvatu ōr māṟṟu ilāc cem poṉ āvar māṟpēṟarē
the Lord in māṟpēṟu will be the gold of limitless degree of fineness, and will admit you into his grace.
{5:60}__6+
{$} īṭṭum māniti cāla kiḻakkiṉum
if you lose very much the great wealth which you earned by strenous efforts.
vīṭṭum kālaṉ viraiya aḻaikkiṉum
and if the Kālaṉ (god of death) whose duty is to kill beings quickly, call you
kāṭṭil mānaṭam āṭuvāy kā' eṉil
if you say the Lord who performs a great dance in the cremation-ground! protect me'
vāṭṭam tīrkkavum vallar māṟpēṟarē
the Lord in māṟpēṟu is also capable of removing both the distresses.
{5:60}__7+
{$} aiyaṉē! araṉē! eṉṟu araṟṟiṉāl uyyalām
(People of this world!) you can save yourselves if you cry aloud my master araṉ!
ulakattavar pēṇuvar
the people of this world will esteem you with regard.
ceyya pātam iraṇṭum niṉaiyavē
if you meditate on both the red feet.
vaiyam āḷavum vaippar māṟpēṟarē
the Lord in māṟpēṟu will bestow on you the greatest grace of ruling over the world, too.

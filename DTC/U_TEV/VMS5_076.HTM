tiruviṉ nātaṉum cemmalar mēl uṟai uruvaṉāy
Being himself Māl, the husband of Tiru, and the beautiful Piramaṉ who is seated on a red lotus flower.
ulakattiṉ uyirkku elām karuvaṉāki muḷaittavaṉ
Civaṉ came into existence by himself as the embryo for all living beings in this world.
kāṉūril paramaṉ āya parañcuṭar; kāṇmiṉ
is the divine light which is the supreme god in Kāṉūr; (people of this world!) You realise this fact.
{5:76}__2+
{$}
peṇṭir makkaḷ peruntuṇai naṉṉiti (1) uṇṭu iṟē eṉṟu ukavaṉ miṉ; ēḻaikāḷ!
Ignorant people! Do not feel elated thinking Do we not have wives, children, great companions, and good wealth!
nīr puṇṭarīkap potumparil otuṅkiyē kāṉūr muḷaiyiṉaik kaṇṭu koṇmiṉ
you have a vision of the sprout in Kāṉūr detaching yourselves from the attachments of wordly things searching him in the recess of your heart which is like a lotus flower.
[[Note: all editions have the reading uṇṭiṉṟē. It should be uṇṭaṉṟē I think it should be uṇṭiṟē;]]
[[iṟē a particle frequently used in vishṇava religious writings and mostly occuring at the end of sentences to indicate that the subject mentioned is either (1) common knowledge or (2) one which must be known by all or (3) one that is emphatically so. A slight variation of this word as eṟē is found in the old gloss on Akanāṉuṟu, 41-12.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) uṇṭiṉṟē, uṇṭitē]]
{5:76}__3+
{$}
tāyattār tamar nal niti eṉṉum im māyattē kiṭantiṭṭu mayaṅkiṭēl
(my mind!) Do not get confused falling into the illusion of agnates, relations and wealth by which one can do all good things.
kayāttē uḷaṉ kāṉūr muḷaiyiṉai viṉai māya vāy attāl vaṇaṅkīr
worship the shoot in Kāṉūṟ which is hidden in your body and prise him with your sincere love, for the Karmams to perish. (The first two lines are addressed to his mind and the next two lines to the people of this world.)
{5:76}__4+
{$}
kuṟiyil niṉṟu uṇṭu
Eating the food standing with the intention of begging.
kūṟai ilāccamaṇ neṟiyai viṭṭu niṟaikaḻal paṟṟiṉēṉ
I cught hold of the feet which pervades everywhere, abandoning the religion of the camaṇar who are without clothes (naked)
aṟiyaluṟṟirēl
if you desire to know.
kāṉūr muḷaiyavaṉ
Civaṉ who became self-existent in Kāṉūṟ.
eṉ cintaiyē ceṟivu ceytiṭṭiruppatu
is staying in my mind itself being united with it.
{5:76}__5+
{$}
pottal maṇ cuvarp pollāk kurampaiyai meyttaṉ eṉṟu viyantiṭal; ēḻaikāḷ!
ignorant people! Do not wonder at the body which can be compared to a hut, which is ugly and is like a wall constructed with mud and having nine holes, as real.
[[meyttāṉ has become meyttaṉ for rhyme, the underlined letters are also augmentations for the same.]]
cittar pattarkaḷ cēr tirukkāṉūril attaṉ pātam aṭaital karumamē
Reaching the feet of the father in Kāṉūṟ where persons of eight mystic powers and pious people throng, is that which most be done by us.
{5:76}__6+
{$}
kalvi, ñāṉakkalaip poruḷ āyavaṉ
Civaṉ who is education, spiritual wisdom, and the one who can be realised by a study of the arts.
celvam malku tirukkāṉūr īcaṉai elliyum pakalum icaivu āṉavā(ṟu) colliṭīr, num tuyaraṅkaḷ tīravē
sing night and day the prises of Civaṉ in Kāṉūr where wealth is increasing, according to your ability, so that your sufferings may leave you.
{5:76}__7+
{$}
nīrum pārum neruppum arukkaṉum kārum (0) mārutam kāṉūr muḷaittavaṉ
Civaṉ who came into self-existence is himself the water, the earth the fire, the sun, the clouds and the air.
cērvum oṉṟu aṟiyātu
knowing little the place where he stays.
(1) ticai ticai ōrvum oṉṟu ilar oṭittirivar
people of this world will wander in all directions with little reflections.
[[Variant reading: (1) tikai tikai]]
[[NOTE_VAR: (0) MS had mātarum; I have corrected as mārutam according to PIFI and to another edition {jlc}]]

ōmattōṭu ayaṉ māl aṟiyāvaṇṇam, vīmap pēroḷiyāya viḻupporuḷ
the highest object that became the great dreadful fire that could not be known by Ayaṉ who performs sacrifices and Māl.
kāmaṟ kāyntavaṉ
Civaṉ who burnt Kāmaṉ
kāṉūr muḷaittavaṉ
came into self-existence in Kāṉūṟ
cēmattāl iruppu āvatu eṉ cintaiyē
my mind is the place which he thinks as affording protection.

kaṉṉi māmatil kāṉūrk karuttaṉ
Civaṉ as creator in Kāṉūr which has a big wall of enclosure which enemies are afrid of approaching and therefore does not know destruction
vaṉṉi koṉṟai erukku aṇintāṉ malai uṉṉiyē ceṉṟu eṭuttavaṉ oṇ tiṟal taṉṉai vīḻattaṉiviral vaittavaṉ
is the one who placed a single toe to destroy the excessive strength of arakkaṉ who went and intentionally lifted the mountin of Civaṉ who adorned himself with leaves of indian mesquit, koṉṟi and yārcum.

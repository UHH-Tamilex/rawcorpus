taṇ pātittiruvuruvil peṇ koṇṭāṉai
Civaṉ who has on his holy body a lady on a half.
paṇṭu oru kāl tacamukaṉai (1) aḻuvittāṉai
who made Irāvaṇaṉ (of ten faces) to weep once, in the distant past.
vātittu
arguing with him.
taṭa malarāṉ ciram koṇṭāṉai
who removed one of the heads of Piramaṉ who is seated in a big lotus flower.
(2) vaṉkaruppuc cilaik kāmaṉ uṭal (3) aṭṭāṉai
who burnt the body of Kāmaṉ who has a strong bow of sugar-cane.
cōtic cantiraṉ mēṉi maṟuc ceytāṉai
created a blot in the body of the moon which gives light
[[This was done during the sacrifice of Takkaṉ; Vīrappettirar trampled upon the moon and created a blot;]]
[[PP: nāmakaḷ nāci ciram piramaṉ paṭac, cōmaṉ mukaṉ nerittu untīpaṟa, tollaiviṉai keṭa untī paṟa;
cantiraṉait tēyttaruḷit takkaṉ taṉ vēḷviyiṉil
(tiruvācakam, tiruvuntiyār, 13, tiruvanmāṉai, 15)]]
cuṭar (4) aṅkitēvaṉai ōr kai(k) koṇṭāṉai
removed one hand of the fire-god who is brilliant
[[kaik koṇṭāṉai the underlined letter is augmentation;]]
[[PP: veyyavaṉ aṅki viḻuṅkattiraṭṭiya, kaiyait taṟittāṉ eṉṟu untīpaṟa, kalaṅkiṟṟu vēḷvi eṉṟu untīpaṟa
(tiruvācakam, tiruvuntiyār, 7)]]
ātittaṉ pal koṇṭa ammāṉ taṉṉai
and the god, our father who knocked out the teeth of the sun-god;
[[PP: etirnta pakal kuṉṟappal ukuttōṉ;
pal ilaṉākap pakalai veṉṟōṉ
(Kōvaiyār, 4, 60);]]
[[The name of this sun-god is pūsha;]]
[[PP: cūriyaṉār toṇṭai vāyiṉil paṟkaḷai vāri nerittavāṟu untīpaṟa, mayaṅkiṟṟu vēḷvi eṉṟu untī paṟa
(tiruvācakam, tiruvuntiyār, 15)]]
ārūril kaṇṭu aṭiyēṉ ayarttavāṟu (naṉṟu)
it is good that I forgot all other things, having obtained vision of Civaṉ in Ārūr.
[[Variant reading: (1) aḻivittāṉai (2) vaṇ karuppuccilai (3) koṇṭāṉai (4) aṅkittēvaṉai).]]

veṟpu uṟutta tiruvaṭiyāl kūṟṟu aṭṭāṉai
Civaṉ who killed Irāvaṇaṉ by his holy feet which pressed down the mountain, Kayilai, by crushing him.
viḷakkiṉ oḷi, miṉṉiṉ oḷi, muttiṉ cōti, oppuṟutta tiruvuruvattu oruvaṉ taṉṉai
who is the unequalled god who has a holy body which is like the light of the lamp, the brilliance of the lightning and the lustre of the pearl.
ōtātē vētam uṇarntāṉ taṉṉai
who knew the vetams without learning them from a teacher.
appu uṟutta kaṭal nañcam uṇṭāṉ taṉṉai
who consumed the poison that rose in the ocean that absorbs all the water into itself
[[amutu kaṭaintatu lavaṇa camuttirat teṉa uṇarka (takkayākapparani, 284, commentary)]]
amutu uṇṭār ulantālum ulavātāṉai
who does not die even though the so-called inimortals who partook of the nectar die;
[[viṇṇōr amutu uṇṭum cāva oruvarum, uṇṇāta nañcu uṇṭu iruntu aruḷ ceykuvāy (cilappatikaram, 12-22)]]
appu uṟutta nīrakattē aḻal āṉāṉai
who is the fire in the ocean which is filled with water
[[This fire is known by the name of vaṭavāmukakkiṉi; submarine fire in the shape of a mare's head believed to consume the world at the end of a yukam.]]
ārūril kaṇṭu aṭiyēṉ ayarttavāṟu (naṉṟu)
see 1st verse

oru kālattu oru tēvar kaṇ koṇṭāṉai
Civaṉ who took away the eyes of one sun god at the time of takkaṉ's sacrifice.
[[The name of that sun is pakaṉ]]
[[PP: uṇṇappukunta pakaṉ oḷittu oṭāmē kaṇṇaip paṟittavāṟu untīpaṟa, karukkeṭa nām ellām untipaṟa
(tiruvācakam tiruvuntiyār, 12)]]
[[PP: pakaṉ tāmaraik kaṇkeṭak kaṭantēṉ;
cīr arukkaṉ kuruṭṭiṟpukac ceṟṟa kōṉ
(Kōvaiyār, 184, 270)]]
ūḻi tōṟu ūḻi uyarntāṉ taṉṉai
who became great in every acon.
varukālam celkālam āyiṉāṉai
who is the future and the past.
1. vaṉ karuppuc cilaik kāmaṉ uṭal aṭṭāṉai
who burnt the body of Kāmaṉ who has a strong bow of a sugar-cane.
poru vēḻak kaḷiṟṟu urivaip pōrvaiyāṉai
who covers himself with the blanket of the skin of a male elephant which can fight.
puḷ araiyaṉ uṭal taṉṉaip poṭi ceytāṉai
who reduced to dust the body of Karuṭaṉ, the King of birds.
[[PP: puḷḷaracaik koṉṟu uyir piṉ koṭuttār pōlum
(Nāvukkaracar, tiruvīḻimiḻalaittāṇṭakam (4) 8);]]
[[PP: kārmali uruvak karuṭaṉaik kāyntum
(11th book, Kōpappiracātam, 59);
[[PP: takkaṉ veṅkatirōṉ calantaraṉ piramaṉ cantiraṉ intiraṉ eccaṉ mikka neñcu arakkaṉ puram kari karuṭaṉ maṟali vēḷ ivarmikai cekut tōṉ
(Cēntaṉar tiruvicaippa, tiruvīḻimiḻalai, 10).]]
aruvēḷvi takarttu eccaṉ talai koṇṭāṉai
who removed the head of the god of sacrifice devastating the rare sacrifice of Takkaṉ;
[[PP: talai takkaṉaiyum eccaṉaiyum tūymaikaḷ ceytavā tōḷ nōkkam āṭāmō
(tiruvācakam, tiruttōṇokkam, 11);]]
[[PP: takkaṉaiyum eccaṉaiyum talai aṟuttu
(do. tiruccāḻal, 5)]]
ārūril kaṇṭu aṭiyēṉ ayarttavāṟu (naṉṟu)
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: 1. vaṇ karuppuc cilai]]

meyp pāl veṇṇīṟu aṇinta mēṉiyāṉai
Civaṉ who has a bright colour due to the milk-white sacred ash smeared on the body
[[mēṉi colour;
nīla mēṉi neṭiyōṉ kōyilum (cilappatikāram, 5-172);
mēṉi yeṉṟār āku peyarāl niṟattai (do. aṭiyārkkunallār)]]
veṇ 1. paḷiṅkiṉ uṭpatitta cōtiyāṉai
who placed the brilliance inside the white crystal quarty.
oppāṉai
who can be compared to other god.
oppu ilā oruvaṉ taṉṉai
the unequalled god to whom no one else can be compared.
[[cf. piṟarkku nī vāyiṉ allatu niṉakkup piṟar uvamam ākā oru peruvēntē (patiṟṟuppattu, 73-2-3)]]
uttamaṉai
the supreme god.
nittilattai
who is as precious as the pearl.
ulakam ellām vaippāṉai
who absorbs in him all the worlds;
kaḷaivāṉai
who destroys them.
varuvippāṉai
who creates them again.
[[varuvippāṉai, vaippāṉai, kaḷaivāṉai who creates the worlds, sustains them and destroys them is another meaning]]
valviṉaiyēṉ maṉattu akattē maṉṉiṉāṉai
who stayed permanently inside the mind of mine who has irresistible actions to undergo.
appālaikku appālaikku appālāṉai
who is beyond the life that is beyond the reach of māyai.
ārūril kaṇṭu aṭiyēṉ ayarttavāṟu
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: 1. veṇ paḷiṅkiṉuṭal]]

piṇṭattil piṟantatu oru poruḷai
the object that is born in the body.
maṟṟaip piṇṭattaip 1. paṭaittataṉai
the thing which created the body.
periya vētattuṇṭattiṉ tuṇi poruḷai
ascertained god who is established even by the fragments of the big vētams.
iṟṟaic cuṭutī āki
being the burning fire visible today.
cuḻal kālāy
the wind that is whirling.
nīr āki
being the water.
pārāy
and being the earth.
kaṇṭattil 2. tītiṉ nañcu amutu ceytu (niṟutti)
stationing the harmful poison in the neck having transformed into nectar, and changing its harmful nature.
kaṇ mūṉṟu paṭaittatu oru karumpai
who is as sweet as the sugar-cane which has three eyes.
[[kaṇ also means joints in the sugar-cane;
cf. kaṇkaḷ mūṉṟu uṭaiyatu ōr karumpē (tirumāḷikrittēvar tiruvicaippa, 7)]]
pālai
who is as sweet as the milk.
aṇṭattukku appuṟattār tamakku vittai
the seed for those liberated souls who are above the heaven
ārūril kaṇṭu aṭiyēṉ ayarttavāṟu
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: 1. paṭaittāṉai 2. tītu nañcu]]

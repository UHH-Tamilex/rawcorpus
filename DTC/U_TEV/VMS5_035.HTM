{5:35}__1+
{$}
paruvarāl vayal cūḻnta paḻaṉattāṉ
Civaṉ in Paḻaṉam surrounded by fields abounding in big murrel fish.
aruvaṉāy atti īr uri pōrttu umai uruvaṉāy oṟṟiyūr pati ākilum
Though formless and having Umai on his sacred body covering it with the skin of an elephant wet with blood, his place is Oṟṟiyūr
[[NOTE: place mortgaged with the right of possession]]
ittēvarkku
from this god
tiruviṉāl tiruvēṇṭum
(people) ask for liberation, doing services with the help of wealth.
{5:35}__2+
{$}
cilar ātarkaḷ
some who are mentally blind
vaiyam vantu vaṇaṅki valam koḷum aiyaṉai aṟiyār
do not know the master who is adored and circumambulated by the people of this world having come to paḻaṉam
pai koḷ āṭu aravu ārtta paḻaṉaṉpāl poyyar kālaṅkaḷ pōkkiṭuvārkaḷē
some will waste their lives without worshipping Civaṉ in paḻaṉam who has tied securely a dancing cobra which has a hood
{5:35}__3+
{$}
vaṇṇamāka muṟukkiya vācikai tiṇṇamākat tiruccaṭai cērtti
Having attached securely in the holy catai a garland of flowers strung thickly together and twisted, to appear beautiful.
paṇṇum ākavē pāṭum paḻaṉattāṉ avaṉ āyiram nāmamē nīr eṇṇumiṉ
you think of the thousand names of Civaṉ in paḻaṉam who sings music distinguishing the different melodies. (eṇṇumiṉ may also mean count)
{5:35}__4+
{$}
1. mūrkkap pāmpiṉaip piṭittu atu mūcciṭa
Having caught hold of a wrathful cobra and to make it hiss loudly
vākkap pāmpiṉaik kaṇṭa tuṇimati pākka
the crescent which saw the cobra of perfect shape, to be always gazing at it with fear
pāmpiṉaip paṟṟum paḻaṉattāṉ tārk koḷ mālai caṭaikku 2. aṇintiṭṭatē
what is the reason for Civaṉ in paḻaṉam who holds a cobra to adorn his caṭai with koṉṟai garlands and other garlands?
[[Variant reading: 1. mūkkap 2. caṭaikkarantiṭṭatē]]
{5:35}__5+
{$}
nīlam uṇṭa miṭaṟṟiṉaṉ
Civaṉ has a blue neck
nērntatu ōr kōlam uṇṭa kuṇattāl niṟaintatu ōr pālum uṇṭu
he has on one half a lady who has full of many good qualities and has beauty befitting him
paḻaṉaṉ pāl eṉṉiṭai mālum uṇṭu iṟai eṉ maṉattuḷē
There is also a little bewilderment in my mind towards with Civaṉ in paḻaṉam
{5:35}__6+
{$}
pantamāyiṉa tīrkkum paḻaṉattāṉ
Civaṉ in paḻaṉam who destroys all earthly attachments.
mantamākavaḷar piṟaicūṭi
having as an adornment a crescent growing slowly
ōr cantamākat tiruccaṭai cāttuvāṉ
wears on his holy caṭai a garland
entai tāy tantai em perumāṉumē
is my real father, for my life, and mother and father who gave me this body and brought it up, and my master
{5:35}__7+
{$}
pārkka niṉṟu paravum paḻaṉattāṉ tāṭkaṇ niṉṟu talai vaṇaṅkārkaḷē
those who do not bow their heads at the feet of the Lord in paḻaṉam who grants his grace to those who praise him so that his benign look may fall on them, to be seen by all.
mārkkam oṉṟu aṟiyār matiyillikaḷ
those who have no intelligence do not know any right path
pūkkarattil purikilar mūṭarkaḷ
and some stupid persons will not worship him by carrying flowers in their hands.
{5:35}__8+
{$}
ēṟiṉār imaiyōrkaḷ paṇi kaṇṭu, tēṟuvār alar tīviṉaiyāḷarkaḷ
some sinful persons will not clear thier doubts in their minds even after seeing the tēvar rise in status by their service to Civaṉ
pāṟiṉār paṇi vēṇṭum paḻaṉattāṉ umaiyāḷoṭum kūṭavē kūṟiṉāṉ
Civaṉ in paḻaṉam who desires even the services of low people has one half of his body united with Umai also.
{5:35}__9+
{$}
cuṟṟuvār toḻuvār cuṭar vaṇṇaṉ
devotees will surround and praise with joined hands Civaṉ who has the hue of fire
mēl
in the distant past
teṟṟiṉār tiriyum puram mūṉṟu eytāṉ
destroyed the wandering three cities of the quarrelsome enemies by discharging an arrow.
{5:35}__10+
{$}
poṅku mākaṭal cūḻ ilaṅkaikku iṟai aṅkam āṉa iṟuttu aruḷ ceytavaṉ
Civaṉ who granted his grace after crushing the limbs of the King of ilaṅkai surrounded by the big sea with surging waves
umaiyoṭum eṉṟum paḻaṉaṉ paṅkaṉ
Civaṉ in paḻaṉam has always Umai as his half
aṭiyēṉuṭai ucci 1. tāṉ taṅkaṉ
he placed his feet to stay on his devotee's head.

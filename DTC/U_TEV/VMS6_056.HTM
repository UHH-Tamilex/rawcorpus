poṟai uṭaiya pūmi nīr āṉāy (pōṟṟi)
you are the water and the earth whose quality is forbearance.
pūtap paṭai āḷ puṉitā (pōṟṟi)
the pure god who has in your command an army of pūtam-s.
niṟaiyuṭaiya neñciṉ iṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you dwell in the minds which have complete self-control.
nīṅkātu eṉ uḷḷattu iruntāy (pōṟṟi)
you occupied my mind without leaving it.
maṟai uṭaiya vētam virittāy (pōṟṟi)
you expounded the vētam-s which have a upaniṭatam;
[[PP: vētattu maṟai nī
(Paripātal, 3-66);]]
[[maṟai -- upaniṭatam
(Do. Parimēlaḻakar)]]
[[maṟai may also mean hidden meaning]]
vāṉōr vaṇaṅkap paṭuvāy (pōṟṟi)
you are pid homage by the celestials.
kaṟai uṭaiya kaṇṭam uṭaiyāy (pōṟṟi)
you have a neck which is black.
kayilai malaiyāṉē (pōṟṟi pōṟṟi)
see 1st verse of previous decade. (Civaṉ in Kayilimali!)

you transcended all the seven worlds from time immemorial.
āti purāṇaṉāy niṉṟāy (pōṟṟi)

toḻil nōkki āḷum cuṭarē (pōṟṟi)
the light that admits devotees having an eye on their worship.
kaṇṭam kaṟukkavum vallāy (pōṟṟi)
you were able to consume the poison and to station it in your neck to turn its colour into black.

mēl vaitta vāṉōr perumāṉ (pōṟṟi)
the god of the celestials who are placed in a higher world!
mēl āṭu puram mūṉṟum eytāy (pōṟṟi)
you discharged an arrow to destroy all the three cities which were wandering in the sky.
cīlattāṉ teṉ ilaṅkai maṉṉaṉ cilai eṭukka vāy alaṟa vaittāy (pōṟṟi pōṟṟi)
when the King of the ilaṅki in the south who later worshipped you, lifted the mountin, you cused him to roar by his ten mouths.
kōlattāl kuṟaivu illāṉ taṉṉai aṉṟu koṭitākak kāynta kuḻakā (pōṟṟi)
the youth who burnt in that distant past Kāmaṉ who hadon no defect in beauty, for people to consider it as a cruel act.
kāl attāl kālaṉaiyum kāyntāy (pōṟṟi)
you killed even the god of death by your leg.

[[Note: This decade has been composed as the lamentation of the mother about her dughter who has fallen in love with Civaṉ in Tiruvāṉikkā]]

{2:23}__1+
{$} āy iḻaiyāḷ avaḷ
my dughter who wears choice ornaments.
eṉum
invokes always the names such as,
maḻai ār miṭaṟā
Civaṉ who has a black neck like a sable cloud!.
maḻuvāḷ uṭaiyāy
who has a battle-axe!
uḻai ārkaravā
who holds in his hand a deer!
umaiyāḷ kaṇavā
who is the husband of Umayāḷ!
viḻavu ārum veṇnāvaliṉ mēviya em aḻakā
and who is our beautiful person who dwells under the white jumoon plum tree where festivals are celebrated always.
{2:23}__2+
{$} nēr iḻai
my dughter who wears suitable ornaments
eṉum
unvokes always the name such as,
kolai ārkariyiṉ urimūṭiyaṉē
Civaṉ who covered his body with the skin of an elephant which is capable of killing!
malai ār cilaiyā viḷaivittavaṉē
who bent the mountin as a rare bow!
vilaiyāl eṉai āḷum veṇāmal uḷāy
in who is under the white jumoon plum tree, and who admits me as his protege by purchasing me as his serf
nilaiyā aruḷāy
grant me your grace to stay with me always.
{2:23}__3+
{$} āyiḻai
my dughter who wears choice ornaments
eṉum
invokes the names such as,
kālāl uyir kālaṉai vīṭuceytāy
you who destroyed the life of Kālaṉ by kicking him with your foot!
pālōṭu ney āṭiya pālvaṇaṉē
who are as white as milk and bathed in milk and ghee!
vēl āṭukaiyāy
who has in your hand a trident!
emveṇnāvaluḷāy
our god under the white jumoon plum tree!
āl ārniḻalāy
one who is seated under the shade of the banyan tree!
{2:23}__4+
{$} eṉ āyiḻai
my dughter who is wearing choice ornaments.
cuṟavakkoṭikoṇṭavaṉ nīṟu (atu) āy uṟaneṟṟi viḻitta emuttamaṉē
our Civaṉ who is possessed of all moral attributes and who opened his frontal eye to reduce to ashes one who holds a banner of a shark drawn on it!
viṟal mikkakarikku aruḷceytavaṉē
who granted his grace to the elephant of excessive strength!
aṟam mikkatu eṉum
says that righteousness is superior to everything else.
[[There is no name of veṇṇāval in this verse; but there is a reference to Civaṉ granting his grace to an elephant which worshipped him in this shrine; hence āṉikka, the name of the shrine]]
{2:23}__5+
{$} āyiḻai
(my dughter) who wears choice ornaments (invoking the names such as)
ceṅkaṭpeyar koṇṭavaṉ cempiyarkōṉ aṅkaṇ karuṇai peritāyavaṉē
Civaṉ who granted his grace in a large measure which is revealed by the eyes on the chief of cempiyar (coḻar) ceṅkatcōḻaṉ!
veṅkaṇviṭaiyāy
who has a bull of cruel looks!
veṇvaluḷāy
who is under the white jumoon plum tree.
aṅkattu ayarvu āyiṉaḷ
languished in her mind
[[The reference to ceṅkatcōḻaṉ is worthy of note]]
{2:23}__6+
{$} nēriḻai
(my dughter) who wears appropriate ornaments
eṉum
invokes names such as
kuṉṟē amarvāy
Civaṉ who is seated in the mountin (Kayili)!
kolai ār puliyiṉtaṉ tōl uṭaiyāy
who has a dress of the skin of a tiger whose mature is killing!
caṭaiyāy
who has a cati!
piṟaiyāy
who wears a crescent!
purammūṉṟai veṉṟāy
won a victory over all the three cities!
veṇāvaluḷēniṉṟāy
who is seated under the white jumoon plum!
aruḷāy
grant me your grace!

VMS8 / {2:23}__PIFI7+
{$} āyiḻai
my dughter wearing choice ornaments invoking names such as
malai aṉṟu eṭutta(v) arakkaṉ muṭitōḷ tolaiya(v) viral ūṉṟiya tūmaḻuvā
Civaṉ who has a pure battle-axe, and who fixed firmly a toe to destroy the strength of the heads and shoulders of the arakkaṉ who lifted the mountin (Kayili) in the distant past!
vilaiyāl eṉai āḷum veṇnāvaluḷāy
who is under the white jumoon plum tree and who admitted me as your protege by purchasing me as your serf!
alacāmal nalkāy eṉum
says Please grant me your grace without making me to undergo suffering!
VMS9 / {2:23}__PIFI8+
{$} āyiḻaiyāḷ (avaḷ)
my dughter who wears choice ornaments (avaḷ) has no separate meaning) invoking names such as
tiru ārtaru nāraṇaṉ nāṉ mukaṉum veruvāmaruvā aḻalāy nimirntāy
Civaṉ who rose erect as a pillar of fire near which nāraṇaṉ who has tiru as his wife, and nāṉmukaṉ could not come and who were afrid of it!
virai ārum veṇāvaluḷ mēviya em aravā eṉum
says our Lord who wears cobras and who resides with joy under the white jumoon plum tree which has excessive fragrance!
VMS10 / {2:23}__PIFI9+
{$} puttar palarōṭu amaṇ poyttavarkaḷ otta(v) urai col ivai ōrakilār
without investigating the teachings of many sects of buddhists and amaṇar who have no sincere penance to their credit, which are in agreement with each other,
meyttēvar vaṇaṅkum veṇāvaluḷāy! attā aruḷāy eṉum
says, Civaṉ who is under the white jumoon plum tree, worshipped by the real tēvar! my master! grant me your grace!
VMS11 / {2:23}__PIFI10+
{$} veṇ nāval amarntuṟai vētiyaṉai
on Civaṉ who revealed the vētams and dwells under the white jumoon plum tree desiring it.
kaṇ ār kamaḻkāḻiyartam talaivaṉ
ñaṉacampantaṉ the chief of the residents of Kaḻi whose fame has spread everywhere, and which is a feast to the eye
viṇṇōr (avar) ētta virumpuvar
the celestials will wish to prise (avar has no separate to meaning)
paṇṇōṭu pāṭiya ivaipattum vallār
those who are capable of reciting these ten verses which were composed with melody-type.

Seventh Tirumuṟi (101 patikam-s in 17 paṇ-s)

This is the text as it stands in the PIFI edition. Standard editions only contin 100 patikam-s. The PIFI edition also includes an additional patikam, [7:101], which is found in several manuscripts.

List of paṇ-s

  1. intaḷam: 1 to 12
  2. takkarākam: 13 to 16
  3. naṭṭarākam: 17 to 30 and 82
  4. kolli: 31 to 37
  5. kollikkuvaṇam: 38 to 46
  6. paḻampañcuram: 47 to 53
  7. takkēci: 54 to 70
  8. kāntāram: 71 to 75 and 101
  9. piyantikkāntāram: 76
  10. kāntārapañcamam: 77
  11. naṭṭapāṭi: 78 to 81
  12. puṟanīrmi: 83 to 85
  13. cīkāmaram: 86 to 89
  14. kuṟiñci: 90 to 93
  15. kucikam: 94
  16. centurutti: 95
  17. pañcamam: 96 to 1OO

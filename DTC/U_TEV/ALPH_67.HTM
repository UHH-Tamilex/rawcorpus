[[Note 1 (VMS, 1_111):
This shrine is now known as Kiḻiyūr'. It is a four miles south-east of Tirunaṉṟiyur which is itself two miles north-east of Āṉanta Tāṇtavapuram R.S. South Railway. It is one mile to the north west of Naṉipaḷḷi, the birth-place of the mother of Ñāṉacampantar]]

[[Note 2 (VMS, 1_111):
Kaṭimuṭi: This is commonly believed to be the place now known as Kīḻiyūr, which is five miles to the east of āṉanta tāṇṭavapuram rilway station Tanjore Dt. But M.Raghava Ayyar thinks that this decade of verses is on tirucceṉṉampūṇti, a mile and a half east of Kōvilaṭi, Tanjore Dt. The inscriptions which belong to the 9th and 10th centuries mention the god as tirukkaṭimuṭi makātēvār and the shrine as tirukkaṭimuṭi'. There is another shrine by name tiruccaṭimuṭi which is mentioned in Appar tēvāram where both the names are found in one and the same stanza. This Caṭimuṭi is to the north of Kōvilaṭi. Collection of essays p.280-284, 2nd edition, march 1964]]

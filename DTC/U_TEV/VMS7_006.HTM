{7:6}__1+
{$} vēlaicūḻ veṇkāṭaṉīrē
you who are in Veṇkāṭu surrounded by the sea.
paṭam koḷ nākam ceṉṉi cērtti
having placed a cobra having hood on the head.
pāypulittōl araiyil vīkki
having tied tightly on the waist the skin of a pouncing tiger.
aṭaṅkalār ūr eriyaccīṟi
Having got angry to burn the places of the enemies.
aṉṟu mūvarkku aruḷpurintīr
you bestowed your grace on three people on that day
[[PP: mūveyil ceṟṟa ñāṉṟu uynta mūvaril iruvar niṉ tirukkōyiliṉ vāytal, kāvalāḷar eṉṟu ēviyapiṉṉai oruvaṉ nī karikāṭu araṅkāka, māṉai nōkki ōr mā naṭam makiḻa maṇi muḻā muḻakkav varuḷ ceyta, tēva, tēva (Cuntarar, Tiruppuṉkūr, 8)]]
[[PP: mūvār puraṅkaḷ eritta aṉṟu mūvarkku aruḷ ceytār (Campantar, Tiruvaṇṇāmali, 2nd patikam, 1)]]
[[PP: uyyavallār oru mūvaraik kāval koṇṭu, eyyavallāṉukkē untīpaṟa
(Tiruvācakam, Tiruvuntiyār, 4)]]
maṭaṅkalāṉai ceṟṟu ukantīr
you made the god of death to perish and then granted him his life;
[[PP: tāmā iruvarum tarumaṉum maṭaṅkalum (Paripāṭal, 3-8)]]
[[maṭaṅkalāṉ may mean also the incarnation of Māl, half man and half lion;]]
[[PP: maṭaṅkalāy mā ṟaṭṭāy (Cilappatikāram);]]
[[PP: tarumaṉum maṭaṅkalum (Paripātal, 3-8);]]
[[PP: tarumaṉ-yamaṉ maṭaṅkal-avaṉ ēval ceyyum kūṟṟam'
(Do. Parimēlaḻakar's commentary)]]
talai kai ēnti
holding a skull in the hand.
maṉaikaḷ tōṟum viṭaṅkarākit tirivatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for wandering from house to house, assuming a beauteous form and asking alms.

{$} vēlaicūḻ veṇkāṭaṉīrē
[[same for 1st verse.]]
imaiyavarkkum muṉṉaivēṭam iḻittu ukantīr
you destroyed the previous forms of Māl and Piramaṉ and bore upon your shoulders those forms.
uraikaḷ pēṇātu oḻittu ukantīr
you, without wishing for the fame that you protect all things from death destroyed all things and afterwards desired to create them.
[[The meaning for the first two lines is not very clear]]
amarar vēṇṭa
at the request of the immortals
nīrmuṉ koṇṭa uyar tavattai aḻikkavanta kāmatēvaṉai avaṉuṭaiya tātai kāṇa viḻittu ukanta veṟṟi eṉṉē
what is the reason for desiring the victory when you opened the eye on the forehead to destroy Kāma vēḷ (cupid) and agin granting him his life, when his father was witnessing it helplessly, when he (cupid) came to destroy your superior penance which you undertook?
[[Civaṉ destroyed Cupid and when his wife, Rati, requested Civaṉ, he restored him to regin his beautiful form only to hear eyes; cf.curumpu amar villiyaik kāyntu kātaṟ kārikai māṭṭaruḷi
makarattāṭu koṭiyōṉuṭalam poṭi ceytu avaṉuṭaiya, nikar oppu illāt tēvikku aruḷcey nīlakaṇṭaṉār,
pāṅkuṭai mataṉaṉaip poṭiyā viḻittu avaṉ tēvi vēṇṭa muṉkoṭutta vimalaṉār
kāmaṉai īṭaḻittiṭṭu avaṉ kātali ceṉṟu irappac, cēmamē uṉ taṉakku eṉṟu aruḷ ceytavaṉ (Campantar, Tōṇipuram (3) 1, Canpinakar 3, Tirukkōṇamali, 4; tiruvakkari, 9); Kantapurāṇam, mōṉam nīṅku patalam, 28; tirukkalyamappatalam, 85-90)]]

paṇ uḷīrāyp pāṭṭum āṉīr
you are inside paṇ and also in verses having paṇ.

you placed the crescent on your head]]

what is the reason for you to be prised, approaching you, by the residents of the celestial world?

[[see 1st verse.]]
toṇṭarkuṭam eṭuttu nīrum pūvum koṇṭu ēval ceyya

pulkuvaṇṇam vaṭam eṭutta koṅkai mātu ōr pākamāka vārkaṭal vāyviṭam miṭaṟṟil vaittatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for keeping in the neck the poison that was produced in the long ocean, when you had a lady who wears a chin of necklace on her breasts, to be always with you without leaving you?

[[see 1st verse]]
māṟupaṭṭa vaṉattakattiṉ maruvavantavaṉ kaḷiṟṟaip pīṟi
having torn the strong elephant which was inimical to you and was born to live in the forest.

(Though you are so valourous) yourself and the lady who is like a creeper and who is your half, having mounted on a bull to be close together, with delight.
peypalikku eṉṟu illam tōṟum vēṟupaṭṭuttirivatu eṉṉē
what is the reason for wandering from house to house, being quite different from your nature, for alms that is placed in your bowl?

[[see 1st verse]]
kuravu, koṉṟai, matiyam, mattam, koṅkai mātar kaṅkai, nākam viravukiṉṟa caṭaiyuṭaiyīr
you who have matted locks of hir in which kuravu (bottle-flower), koṉṟi, crescent, dhatura flowers (ūmattam flower), Kaṅki who is a lady having breasts, and cobras are lying mixed!
viruttar āṉīr
you are the most senior to all;
[[viruttaṉāya vētaṉ taṉṉai: (10) of this decade.]]
karuttil ummaip paravum eṉmēl paḻikaḷ pōkkīr
you do not remove from me who is always thinking about you in mind, blemishes.
pākamāya maṅkai añci veruva vēḻam ceṟṟatu eṉṉē
(in addition to that) what is the reason for killing the elephant and covering yourself with its skin, to frighten very much the young lady who is your half?
[[This idea is you do not protect your devotee; you frighten your consort? Is it right on your part to do so?]]
[[añcutal is the fear in the mind. veruvutal the actions born of it in the body.]]

on the vētaṉ who is senior to all and who is in Veṇkāṭu surrounded by sea, and where people who are able to recite the Vētam, expounded by you.
toṇṭaṉ aṭiyaṉ
the servant of devotees.

native of Tirunāvalūr which has extensive gardens.

and one who has the name of Nampi Ārūraṉ

those who are able to recite according to his idea the garland of ten verses which was in the form of questions asked with eagerness.
cemmaiyāḷar vāṉ uḷārē
will be people of moral uprightness and will be in heaven

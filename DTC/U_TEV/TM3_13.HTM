• patikam: [3:13]
  • talam: tirup pūntarāy (_allatu kāḻi)
  • paṇ: kāntārap pañcamam
  • VMS: Vol.2, p.272

*1
miṉ _aṉa _eyiṟu _uṭi viravalōrkaḷtam
tuṉṉiya puram _ukac cuḷinta toṉmiyar---
puṉṉi_ampoḻil _aṇi pūntarāynakar,
_aṉṉa _aṉṉa(n) naṭi _arivi paṅkarē.

*2
mūtaṇi muppurattu _eṇṇilōrkaḷi,
vētu _aṇi carattiṉāl, vīṭṭiṉār_avar---
pōtu _aṇi poḻil _amar pūntarāynakar,
tātu _aṇi kuḻal _umi talivar; kāṇmiṉē!

*3
tarukkiya tiripurattavarkaḷtām _uka,
perukkiya cilitaṉip piṭitta peṟṟiyar---
poru(k) kaṭal puṭi taru pūntarāynakar,
karu(k)kiya kuḻal _umi kaṇavar; kāṇmiṉē!

*4
nākamum variyumē nāṇum villumā,
mākam _ār puraṅkaḷi maṟitta māṇpiṉar---
pūkam _ār poḻil _aṇi pūntarāynakar,
pāku _amar moḻi _umi paṅkar; kāṇmiṉē!

*5
veḷ_eyiṟu _uṭiya _av viravalārkaḷ _ūr
_oḷ_eriyūṭṭiya _oruvaṉār---_oḷir
puḷ _aṇi puṟaviṉil pūntarāynakar,
kaḷ _aṇi kuḻal _umi kaṇavar; kāṇmiṉē!

*6
tuṅku _iyal tāṉavar tōṟṟam mā nakar
_aṅkiyil vīḻtara _āynta _ampiṉar---
poṅkiya kaṭal _aṇi pūntarāynakar,
_am kayal _aṉa kaṇi _arivi paṅkarē.

*7
_aṇṭarkaḷ _uyntiṭa, _avuṇar māytarak
kaṇṭavar; kaṭalviṭam _uṇṭa kaṇṭaṉār---
puṇṭarīka(v) vayal pūntarāynakar,
vaṇṭu_amarkuḻalitaṉ maṇāḷar; kāṇmiṉē!

*8
mā ciṉa _arakkaṉi variyiṉ vāṭṭiya,
kāyciṉa _eyilkaḷik kaṟutta kaṇṭaṉār---
pūcurar politaru pūntarāynakar,
kāci cey kuḻal _umi kaṇavar; kāṇmiṉē!

*9
tām mukam _ākkiya _acurartam pati
vēm mukam _ākkiya vikirtar---kaṇṇaṉum,
pūmakaṉ, _aṟikilā---pūntarāynakark
kōmakaṉ, _eḻilpeṟum_arivi kūṟarē.

*10
muttara _acurarkaḷ moytta muppuram
_at takum _aḻal_iṭi vīṭṭiṉār---_amaṇ
puttarum _aṟivu _oṇāp pūntarāynakar,
kottu _aṇi kuḻal _umi kūṟar; kāṇmiṉē!

*11
puram _ericeytavar, pūntarāynakarp
param mali kuḻal _uminaṅki paṅkarip
paraviya pantaṉ meyp pāṭal vallavar
ciram mali civakati cērtal tiṇṇamē.

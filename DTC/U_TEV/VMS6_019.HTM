{6:19}__1+
{$} ellārkkum muṉṉē tōṉṟi muḷaittāṉai
Civaṉ who became self-existing before all beings came into existence.
mutirum caṭaimuṭimēl mukiḻveṇtiṅkaḷ vaḷaittāṉai
who wore a crescent to surround his old caṭi coiled into a crown.
valacurar puraṅkaḷmūṉṟum varai cilaiyā vācuki mānāṇākkōttut tuḷaittāṉaic cuṭucarattāl tuvaḷa nīṟā
who cused to disappear as dust all the three cities of the strong acurar using the mountin as a bow, fixing vācuki as the bow string, with a burning arrow which pierced them through.
tūmutta veṇmuṟuval umaiyōṭu āṭit tiḷaittāṉai
who sported without ceasing with Umi who has teeth as white as flawless pearls.
teṉkūṭal tiruvālavāyc civaṉaṭiyē cintikkap peṟṟēṉ nāṉ (ē)
I had the good fortune to meditate upon the feet of Civaṉ only at the temple, ālavāy in Kūṭal in the south.
{6:19}__2+
{$} viṇulakiṉ mēlārkaḷ mēlāṉtaṉṉai
Civaṉ who is superior to the celestials in heaven.
mēl āṭu puram mūṉṉum poṭiceytāṉai
who reduced to dust all the three cities which were flying in the sky.
paṇnilavu paimpoḻil cūḻ paḻaṉattāṉai
who is in the shrine, paḻaṉam which is surrounded by the gardens in which the humming of the bees is permanent.
pacumpoṉṉiṉ niṟattāṉai
who has the colour of the fine gold.
pālnīṟṟāṉai
who smears himself with sacred ash as white as milk.
uḷnilavu caṭaikkaṟṟaik (1) kaṅkaiyāṉaik karantu
concealing the Kaṅkiyāḷ in the collection of caṭi which has much space inside.
umaiyōṭu uṭaṉāki iruntāṉtaṉṉai
was at the same time united with Umi.
teṉnilavu teṉkūṭal tiruvālavāyc civaṉaṭiyē cintikkappeṟṟēṉ nāṉ (ē)
I had the good fortune to meditate upon the feet of Civaṉ only at the shrine, tiruvālavāy in Kūṭi in the south where clear knowledge is permanent.
[[Variant reading: (1) kaṅkaiyāṉaik kalantu, kaṅkaiyāṉaik karantu]]
{6:19}__3+
{$} nīrttiraḷai nīrccaṭaimēl niṟaivittāṉai
Civaṉ who cused the collection of water to be full on the long caṭi
nilam maruvi nīr ōṭakkaṇṭataṉṉai
who cused that water to run on the earth joining with it.
pāltiraḷaip payiṉṟu āṭa vallāṉ taṉṉai
who is capable of bathing often in abundant milk.
(1) pakaittu eḻunta veṅkūṟṟaip pāyntāṉ taṉṉai
who pounced upon the cruel god of death who rose with enmity towards mārkkaṇṭēyaṉ.
kāltiraḷāy mēkattiṉ uḷḷē niṉṟu (2) kaṭuṅkuralāy iṭippāṉai
who thunders as a harsh sound being inside the cloud as accumulation of air.
kaṇār neṟṟit tīttiraḷai
who has the mass of fire which is the frontal eye.
teṉkūṭal ... nāṉē
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: (1) pakaitteḻunta (2) kaṭuṅkuralāl]]
{6:19}__4+
{$} vāṉam itu ellām uṭaiyāṉtaṉṉai
Civaṉ who has the heaven and whole of this world.
variaravak kaccāṉai
who wears a girdle of a cobra which has lines.
vaṉpēy cūḻak kāṉamatil naṭamāṭavallāṉtaṉṉai
who is able to dance in the cremation-ground to be surrounded by the strong pēys.
kaṭaikkaṇṇāl (1) maṅkaiyum nōkkā
casting a glance with the outer corner of the eye on the young lady (and myself).
ūṉam (atu) ellām oḻittāṉtaṉṉai
who completely removed all my faults.
uṇarvu āki aṭiyēṉatu uḷḷē niṉṟa tēṉ amutai
who is as sweet as the honey and nectar within me, his slave being the knowledge within me.
teṉkūṭal ... nāṉē
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: (1) maṅkaiyumai]]
{6:19}__5+
{$} ūrāṉai
Civaṉ who is the capital cities
ulaku ēḻāy niṉṟāṉtaṉṉai
who is the seven worlds.
oṟṟai veṇpiṟaiyāṉai
who wears a crescent of a single phase.
umaiyōṭu eṉṟum pērāṉai
who does not leave being united always with Umi.
piṟarkku eṉṟum ariyāṉai
who is difficult to be known by others.
piṇakkāṭṭil pēyōṭu eṉṟum naṭam āṭal ārāṉai
who is never satisfied that dancing along with peys in the cremation-ground which has corpses is enough.
amararkaḷukku amutu īntāṉai
who gave the nectar to the immortals.
arumaṟaiyāl nāṉmukaṉum mālum pōṟṟum cīrāṉai
who has the fame that is prised by Piramaṉ of four faces and Māl, by chanting the abstruse vetams.
teṉkūṭal ... nāṉē
see 1st verse.
{6:19}__6+
{$} mūvaṉai
Civaṉ who is older than all others;
[[mūppāṉ ellār yākkaikkum muṉṉē taṉatu iccaiyāl koḷḷappaṭṭa tirumēṉiyai uṭaiyaṉātaliṉ, ellārkkum tāṉ mūppāṉ (Kōviyār, 312, pērāciriyar)]]
mūvāmēṉi uṭaiyāṉai
who has a body that does not show signs of aging.
mūvulakumtāṉē eṅkum pāvaṉai
who himself pervades everywhere in the three worlds.
pāvam aṟuppāṉ taṉṉai
who cuts at the root of sins.
paṭieḻutal ākāta maṅkaiyōṭu mēvaṉai
who is united with desire with a lady who cannot be drawn on a portrit as a comparison.
viṇṇōr naṭuṅkak kaṇṭu
seeing the celestials tremble with fear.
virikaṭaliṉ nañcuuṇṭu amutam īnta tēvaṉai
the god who gave the nectar, himself consuming the poison that rose in the extensive ocean.
teṉkūṭal ... nāṉē
see 1st verse.
{6:19}__7+
{$} (1) tuṟantārkkut tūneṟiyāy niṉṟāṉtaṉṉai
Civaṉ who is the pure path to those who have renounced all attachments.
tuṉpam tuṭaittu āḷa vallāṉtaṉṉai
who can admit people as his protege, completely removing their sufferings.
iṟantārkaḷ eṉpē aṇintāṉtaṉṉai
who adorned himself only with the bones of celestials who died.
elli naṭam āṭa vallāṉtaṉṉai
who is capable of dancing in the night.
maṟantār matilmūṉṟum māyntāṉtaṉṉai
who destroyed all the three forts of the acurar who had been devotees and later forgot their devotion.
maṟṟu oru paṟṟu illā (2) aṭiyēṟku eṉṟum ciṟantāṉai
who is eminent to me who is his slave and who has no other support.
teṉkūṭal ... nāṉē
see 1st verse.
[[variant readings: (1) tuṟantārkkum (2) aṭiyārkku]]
{6:19}__8+
{$} vāyāṉai
Civaṉ who is in the mouths of devotees when they prise him.
maṉattāṉai
who is in their minds when they meditate on him.
maṉattuḷ niṉṟa karuttāṉai
who is the thoughts that stay in the minds.
karuttu aṟintu muṭippāṉtaṉṉai
who fulfills knowing the thoughts.
tūyāṉai
who is pure.
tūveḷḷai ēṟṟāṉtaṉṉai
who has a spotless white bull.
cuṭarttiṅkaḷ caṭaiyāṉai
who wears on his caṭi the bright crescent.
toṭarntu (1) niṉ(ṟa) eṉ tāyāṉai
who is like the mother and who was such a relation in all my births.
tavam āya taṉmaiyāṉai
who is the fruit of the penance
talaiāya tēvātitēvarkku eṉṟum cēyāṉai
who is remote always from the tēvar such as the rulers of the eight directions, Piramaṉ and Māl who are eminent in their own way.
teṉkūṭal ... nāṉē
see 1st verse.
[[Variant reading: niṉṟa]]
{6:19}__9+
{$} pakaiccuṭarāyp pāvam aṟuppāṉtaṉṉai
Civaṉ who is the light inimical to darkness and completely removes sins.
paḻiiliyāy
who has no reproach.
(1) nañcu uṇṭu amutu īntāṉai
who gave the nectar himself consuming the poison.
vakaiccuṭarāy valacurar puram aṭṭāṉai
who destroyed the cities of the strong acurar being the branching fire.
(2) vaḷaivu iliyāy ellārkkum aruḷ ceyvāṉai
who grants his grace to all without being partial.
(3) mikaiccuṭarai
who is the superior light.
(3) viṇṇavarkaḷmēl appālai mēl āya tēvātitēvarkku eṉṟum tikaiccuṭarai
the light that shows the directions always to the tēvar, who is above all tēvar, and who is above all the celestials.
teṉ ... nāṉē
see 1st verse.
[[variant readings: (1) nañcamuṇṭamirtīntāṉai (2) vaḷaiyiliyāy (3) mikaiccuṭarāy viṇṇavarkaḷ mēlaippālai]]

{$} malaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who is unshakable as the mountin
māmēru maṉṉiṉāṉai
who stays permanently in the big mēru.
vaḷarpuṉcaṭaiyāṉai
who has a ruddy caṭi that is growing.
vāṉōrtaṅkaḷ talaiyāṉai
who is on the heads of the celestials.
eṉtalaiyiṉ ucci eṉṟum tāpittu iruntāṉai
who was permanent establishing himself in the top of my head.
tāṉē eṅkum tulaiyāka oruvaraiyum illātāṉai
who has none as equal to him, himself pervading everywhere.
tōṉṟātār matilmūṉṟum (1) tuvaḷa eyta nilaiyāṉai
who has a bow from which he shot an arrow to make all the three fortifications of the enemies who did not come near him, disappear.
teṉkūṭal ... nāṉē
see 1st verse.
[[variant readings: (1) tuvaḷappeyta, tūḷā eyta]]

{$} tūrttaṉait tōḷmuṭipattu iṟuttāṉ taṉṉai
Civaṉ who crushed the ten shoulders and heads of the debuchee, Irāvaṇaṉ.
tolnarampiṉ iṉṉicaikēṭṭu aruḷceytāṉai
who granted his grace on hearing the sweet music produced in the string of the ancient yāḻ.
pārttaṉaip paṇikaṇṭu parittāṉtaṉṉai
who took compassion on pārttan accepting his service.
parintu avaṟkup pācupatam īntāṉtaṉṉai
who gave the weapon, pācupatam, being affectionate towards him.
(1) āttaṉai
who is a trustworthy person whose words are reliable and authoritative.
aṭiyēṉukku aṉpaṉtaṉṉai
who loves me who is his slave.
aḷavuilāp palūḻi kaṇṭuniṉṟa tīrttaṉai
who is the pure god who has survived having witnessed many and countless aeons.
teṉkūṭal ... nāṉē

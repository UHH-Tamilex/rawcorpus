{2:30}__1+
{$} puṟam payam amarntōy
Civaṉ who is residing in Puṟampayam!
maṟam payam malaintavar matiṟ paricu aṟuttaṉai
you destroyed the flying nature of the forts of those who fought aginst you and struck fear into the minds of those people committing sinful acts.
niṟam pacumai cemmaiyoṭu icaintu
Having combined in you green and red colour
uṉatu nīrmai tiṟam payaṉ uṟum poruḷ terintu uṇarum nālvarkku aṟam uraittāṉai
you expounded the fruitful aṟam to the four sages who made researches into the substance of the works which are beneficial to living beings, by your affable nature.
[[puṟam payam ataṉil aṟam pala aruḷiyum (tiruvācakam, 2-90)]]

arittu oḻuku veḷḷam tiruccaṭaivirittaṉai tarittaṉai
you cused to stay on your holy caṭi by spreading it, the flood that flows washing away by waves on the bank,
atu aṉṟiyum
in addition to that
mikap periya kālaṉ eruttuiṟa utaittaṉai
you kicked the very grean Kālaṉ (god of death) to break his neck
ilaṅkiḻai ōr pākam poruttutal karuttiṉai
you had the idea of fixing on one half a lady who wears brilliant ornaments.

see 1st verse
virintaṉai
you spread yourself into many living beings and worlds
kuvintaṉai
you absorbed them into you at the end of the world

you created all the living beings which had a short respite, to be born agin, in order that they may be get respite for a short while from their Karmams
kuruntu oci peruntakaiyum nīyum pirintaṉai puṇarntaṉai
yourself and the noble-minded Māl who bent the wild lime tree got separated and joined together

{$} puṟam payam amarnta iṟaiyōṉē
the exalted one who is in puṟampayam!
perum piṇi piṟappiṉoṭu iṟappilai
you do not have great disease birth and death
ōr pākam karumpoṭu paṭum coliṉ maṭantaiyai makiḻn tōy
you felt happy in having as a half a lady whose words are as sweet as sugar-cane juice
[[The name of the goddess in this shrine is karumpaṭucollammai]]
curumpu uṇa arumpu aviḻ tirunti eḻukoṉṟaivirumpiṉai
you desired the faultless koṉṟi flowers which loosen their petals for the superior variety of bee to drink its honey.

you are the enemy of the Karmams of those who always think of you though they be engaged in low activities, and the brahmins who mintin the fire with their big hands.

you have a glittering caṭi which is like fire and on which water along with the single phase of the moon are lying

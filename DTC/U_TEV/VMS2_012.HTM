{2:12}__1+
{$} maṟaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who is in the form of Vētam-s.
mācu ilāp puṉcaṭai malku veṇpiṟaiyāṉai
who has a white crescent waxing on his murky cati which has no dirt.
peṇṇoṭu āṇ ākiya pemmāṉai
who is the god who has male as well as female forms in one body.
iṟaiyāṉai
who pervades everywhere.
ēr koḷ kaccit tiruvēkampattu uṟaivāṉai allatu uḷkātu ēṉatu uḷḷamē
my mind would not think of any thing else except the Lord who dwells in tiruvēkampam in beautiful Kacci
{2:12}__2+
{$} nocciyē, vaṉṉi, koṉṟai, mati, kūviḷam, ucciyē puṉaital vēṭam viṭai ūrtiyāṉ
Civaṉ who has as vehicle of a bull, and who has a form, on the head of which he adorns himself with leaves of three-leaved chaste tree, leaves of mesquit, koṉṟi flowers, a crescent, and leaves of bel tree.
kacci ēkampam ēya kaṟaik kaṇṭaṉai
and the Lord with a blackneck who is in Kacci ēkampam.
nacciyē toḻumiṉ
(People of this world!) worship with joined hands, loving him.
num mēl viṉai naiyumē
the acts which are with you will dwindle.
{2:12}__3+
{$} cērātār
those who do not approach.
pār ārum muḻavam montai kuḻalyāḻ oli cīrālē pāṭal āṭal citaivu illatu ōr ēr ār pūṅkacci ēkampaṉai
the Lord in ēkampam in Kacci full of beauty and splendour where the big muḻavu, monti flute and yāḻ are played as accompaniments to vocal songs, and dance which never ceases.
emmāṉai
our god.
(1) iṉpam āya(n) neṟi cērārē
will not reach the path that leads the attinment of supreme bliss.
[[Variant reading: (1) iṉpamāy naṉṉeṟi]]
{2:12}__4+
{$} kuṉṟu ēykkum neṭu veṇ māṭak koṭi kūṭippōy
the flags that are hoisted on the tall and white storeys which resemble hills join together.
miṉ tēykkum mukilkaḷ tōyum viyaṉ kacciyuḷ
in the extensive Kacci where they seem to come into contact with the clouds in which lightnings rub with one another.
maṉṟu ēykkum malku cīrāl mali ēkampam ceṉṟu ēykkum cintaiyār mēl viṉai cērāvē
the acts, good and bad will not approach those who go to ēkampam where the assembly of the earth is situated and is famous by its increasing renown and direct their minds suited to that shrine
{2:12}__5+
{$} eṉatu uḷḷam
my mind.
caṭaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who is prominent by his caṭi
talai kai ēntip palitaruvār tam kaṭaiyē pōy (1) mūṉṟum koṇṭāṉ kalik kacciyuḷ
in Kacci of bustle of Civaṉ who snatched the three things [i.e. body, wealth and life], going to the entrances of houses of those who give alms, holding a skull.
puṭaiyē poṉ malarum kampaik karai ēkampam uṭaiyāṉai allatu uḷkātu
will not think of any thing else except the Lord who has ēkampam temple on the bank of the river, Kampi, where in its banks koṉṟi blossoms like gold.
[[Variant reading: (1) mūṉṟum koṉṟāṉ]]
{2:12}__6+
{$} maḻuvāḷōṭu eḻil koḷ cūlappaṭai vallār
Civaṉ is capable of holding a battle-axe and a beautiful trident.
tam keḻuvāḷ ōr imaiyār ucci
dwells on the top of the imayamali along with Umi who is united with him.
umaiyāḷ kaṅkai
has two wives Umiyāl and Kaṅki.
vaḻuvāmē malku cīrāl vaḷar ēkampam toḻuvārē viḻumiyār mēl
near those who worship with joined hands ēkampam flourishing by increasing fame without any defect are really excellent persons.
viṉai tuṉṉāvē
acts will not come.
{2:12}__7+
{$} viṇ uḷār
Civaṉ is in the cosmic space.
maṟaikaḷ vētam virittu ōtuvār
will expound vētams and upaniṭatam;
[[vētattu maṟai nī (paripāṭal, 3-66);
maṟai - upaniṭatam - (Do. parimēlaḻakar)]]
kaṇ uḷ�r
is in the eyes of wise people.
kaḻaliṉ velvār karikālaṉai
will gin victory over Kālaṉ of black complexion by kicking him with his leg
naṇṇuvār eḻil koḷ kacci ēkampattu aṇṇalār āṭukiṉṟa alaṅkāra(m)mē
How beautiful is the adornment of the god who dances in ēkampam in Kacci, who assumes the nature of those who approach him!
{2:12}__8+
{$} mēvuvār
those who cherish with love.
tūyāṉai
Civaṉ who is pure.
tūya vāya(m) maṟai ōtiya vāyaṉai
who chanted with his mouth the pure vētams.
vāḷ arakkaṉ vali vāṭṭiya tīyāṉai
who is in the form of fire and who destroyed the strength of the cruel arakkaṉ.
tītu il kaccit tiruvēkampam mēyāṉai
and who desires to dwell in tiruvēkampam in Kacci which has no evils.
eṉ talai mēlār
will be seated on my head.
{2:12}__9+
{$} nākam pūṇ īṟu (atu) ēṟal naṟuṅ koṉṟai tār pākam peṇ
Civaṉ has ornaments of cobras; rides on a bull; wears garlands of fragrant koṉṟi on his chest, has on one half a lady.
paliyum ēṟpar
will receive alms [which act is low]
maṟai pāṭuvar
will chant the Vētam-s.
ēkampam mēvi āṭum iṟai
the supreme god who dances in ēkampam desiring it.
iruvarkkum mākampam aṟiyum vaṇṇattavaṉ allaṉē
is not of such a nature that both Māl and Piramaṉ could not know the big pillar of fire.
{2:12}__10+
{$} pōtiyār piṇṭiyār eṉṟivar poynnūlai vātiyā vammiṉ
(People of this world!) please come into our fold without discussing about the works contining lies of the buddhists who esteem the bodhi tree (pipal tree) and the amaṇar who consider the asoka tree, as sacred.
ammā eṉṉum kackiyuḷ
in Kacci in the shrine which got its name from the beautiful mango-tree.
ātiyār mēvi āṭum tiru vēkampam nītiyāl toḻumiṉ
worship with joined hand by right conduct tiruvēkampam where the first cuse of all Kings, dance desiring that place.
num mēl viṉai nillāvē
acts will not stay with you
{2:12}__11+
{$} am taṇ pūṅkacci ēkampaṉai ammāṉai
on the god who dwells in ēkampam in beautiful and cool Kacci full of splendour.
kam taṇ pūṅ kāḻi ūraṉ kalik kōvaiyāl
with the help of words of sweet sounds, the native of Kāḻi which has cool water and is full of splendour.
cantamē pāṭa valla tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ col pāṭi āṭak keṭum pāvamē
sins will perish if one dances and sings the verses of ñāṉacampantaṉ, who has knowledge of tamiḻ and who is capable of composing verses of rhythmic movement.

{5:13}__1+
{$}
eṉ poṉē
Civaṉ who is like gold to me!
imaiyōr toḻu paiṅkaḻal naṉ poṉē
gold of highest degree of fineness whose feet are worshipped by tēvar who do not wink!
nalam tīṅku aṟivu oṉṟu ilēṉ
I do not possess even a small amount of intelligence to discern between good and bad things.
cem poṉē
Civaṉ who has the colour of superior gold.
tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ aṉpaṉē
Civaṉ who is tiruviḻimiḻi and who is the embodiment of love!
aṭiyēṉaikkuṟik koḷ
guard your devotee with care.
{5:13}__2+
{$}
viṇ uḷār toḻum vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ aṇṇalē
god in vīḻimiḻi whom the tēvar in heaven worship!
kaṇṇiṉāṟ kaḷi kūra (kaṇṭu)
seeing you to make my eyes completely satisfied.
kaiyāl toḻutu
worshipping you with joined hands.
eṇṇumāṟu aṟiyātu iḷaip pēṉ taṉai aṭiyēṉaik kuṟik koḷ
guard with care me, your devotee who becomes fatigued without knowing to meditate upon you.

ñālamē
the world!
vicumpē
the sky!
nalam tīmaiyē
the good and bad things!
kālamē
time!
karuttē
mind.
karuttāl toḻum cīlamē
easily approachable god who can be worshipped by the mind.
tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ kōlamē
the beautiful form in tiruvīḻimiḻali!
aṭiyēṉaik kuṟik koḷ
guard your devotee with care.
{5:13}__4+
{$} muttaṉē
Civaṉ who is naturally free from the impurities of the soul and free from the cycle of birth and death!
mutalvā
who is the origin of all things?
mukiḻum muḷai ottaṉē
who is like the shoot that sprouts!
oruvā
the unequalled one!
uru ākiya cittaṉē
who possesses the eight miraculous powers and who has a form.
tiru vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ attaṉē
the father in tiruvīḻimalali?
aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷ
guard your devotee carefully.
{5:13}__5+
{$}
karuvaṉē
Civaṉ who is the embryo of all things.
karuvāyt teḷivārkku elām oruvaṉē
the sole thing for those who have clearly understood him as the embryo
uyirppāy uṇarvāy niṉṟa tiruvaṉē
the wealth who is the breath and intelligence.
tiruvīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ kuruvaṉē
who has the form of the preceptor of tiruvīḻimiḻali!
aṭiyēṉaikkuṟikkoḷ
guard your devotee carefully.
{5:13}__6+
{$}
kātalāl poḻil ēḻaiyum kāttaṉē
Civaṉ who protected the seven worlds out of love!
amararkku āttaṉē
a trustworthy person for the immortals (one whose words are authoritive and reliable)
ayaṉ talai cērttaṉē
who attached in his hand the skull of Ayaṉ (Piramaṉ)
tiruvīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ kūttaṉē
the divine dancer in tiruvīḻimiḻali!
aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷ
guard your devotee carefully.
{5:13}__7+
{$}
vāṉavar nīti nittal niyamam ceytu ōtiyum
though the celestials utter your names performing their dily routines according to good conduct.
vāṉavarum uṇarātatu ōr vētiyā
the brahmin who could not be understood even by those celestials
(vāṉavar is used in the sense of they, in the 2nd line).
vikirtā
Civaṉ who is different from the world!
tiruvīḻiyuḷ ātiyē
the source of all things and who is in tiruvīḻi! (tiruvīḻi is a part of the name tiruvīḻimiḻali)
aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷ
guard your devotee carefully.

{$}
paḻaki niṉ aṭi cūṭiya pālaṉaik kaḻaliṉ mēl vaitta kālaṉaic cāṭiya aḻakaṉē
the beautiful god who killed Kāḻaṉ (the god of death) who treacherously wanted to snatch the life of the boy who wore your feet on his head by the practice of performing worship moving intimately with you.
aṇi vīḻi miḻalaiyuḷ kuḻakaṉē
the youth in beautiful vīḻimiḻali!
aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷ
guard your devotee carefully.

{$}
aṇṭavāṉavar kūṭik kaṭainta nañcu uṇṭa vāṉavaṉē
the god who consumed the poison which rose when the celestials in heaven churned the ocean of milk
[[Though acurar also had their share in churning the ocean, it is sid to be an act of the celestials as they are superior to accurar.]]
uṇarvu oṉṟu ilēṉ
I do not possess even the slightest intelligence.
viṇṭavāṉ poḻil vīḻimiḻalaiyuḷ koṇṭaṉē
Civaṉ who had as his abode vīḻimiḻali which has superior gardens of blossomed flowers.
aṭiyēṉaik kuṟikkoḷ
guard your devotees carefully.

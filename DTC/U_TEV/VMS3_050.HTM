{3:50}__1+
{$} virumpum tiṅkaḷum kaṅkaiyum vimmavē
the crescent desired by all people, and Kanki to increase in excellence.
curumpum tumpiyum cūḻcaṭaiyārkku iṭam
the place of Civaṉ whose caṭi the bees, curumpu and tumpi are surrounding.
karumpum cennelum kāy kamukiṉ vaḷam neruṅkum taṇṭalai nīḷneṟi (kāṇmiṉ) (ē)
(People of this world!) You know that it is taṇṭali nīṇeṟi where the products of sugar-cane, red variety of superior paddy and fruit-bearing areca-palms are crowded.
[[kāṇmiṉ, ē can be left out as expletives.]]
{3:50}__2+
{$} ikaḻum kālaṉ itayattum eṉṉuḷum tikaḻum cēvaṭiyāṉ tiruntum iṭam
is the perfect place of Civaṉ whose red-lotus feet shine in the heart of the god of death who slighted, him and within me.
[[PP: vaḻipāṭu ceyyum pālaṉ micaic ceṉṟu pācam viciṟi maṟinta cintaik kālaṉ aṟintaṉaṉ aṟitaṟku ariyāṉ kaḻalaṭiyē (nāvukkaracar, taṉittiraviruttam, 11)]]
pukaḻum pūmakaḷum puṇar pūcurar nikaḻum taṇṭalai nīḷ neṟi (kāṇmiṉ), (ē)
(People of this world!) you know that it is Taṇṭali Nīṇeṟi where brahmins in whom fame and wealth are united
[[pūmakaḷ the goddess of wealth who is seated in the red lotus flower.]]
{3:50}__3+
{$} paranta nīlappaṭar eri valviṭam karanta kaṇṭattiṉāṉ karutum iṭam
the place which is always desired by Civaṉ who had concealed the strong and burning blue-coloured poison which spread and gave trouble.
curanta mēti tuṟaipaṭintu ōṭaiyil niranta taṇṭalai nīḷ neṟi (kāṇmiṉ) (ē)
is Taṇṭali Nīṇeṟi where in the rivulets the buffaloes stand in a row, having plunged into the ghats, having secreted milk.
{3:50}__4+
{$} tavanta eṉpum tavaḷap poṭiyumē uvanta mēṉiyiṉāṉ uṟaiyum(m) iṭam
the place where Civaṉ who rejoiced in adorning his body with burnt bones, and smearing with white sacred ash resides.
civanta poṉṉum ceḻuntaraḷaṅkaḷum nivanta taṇṭalai nīṇeṟi (kāṇmiṉ, ē)
is taṇṭali nīṇeṟi where superior gold and mature pearls are in large quantities found.

{$} ilaṅkai vēntaṉ irupatu tōḷ iṟa, vilaṅkalil (l) aṭarttāṉ virumpum(m) iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who pressed under the mountin to crush the twenty shoulders of the King of ilaṇki.
calam koḷ ippi, taraḷamum, caṅkamum, nilamkoḷ taṇṭalai nīṇeṟi (kāṇmiṉ, ē)
is taṇṭaali nīṇeṟi where the oysters which are under water, pearls and conches are found on the land.
VMS9 / {3:50}__PIFI6+
{$} karuvaru untiyiṉ nāṉmukaṉ kaṇṇaṉ eṉṟu iruvarum teriyā oruvaṉ(ṉ) iṭam
the place of the unequalled Civaṉ who could not be known by the four-faced Piramaṉ who came into existence in the navel of Māl, and kaṇṇaṉ (Māl)
ceru varuntiya cempiyaṉ kōc ceṅkaṇ nirupar taṇṭalai nīḷneṟi (kāṇmiṉ, ē)
is taṇtali nīṇeṟi built by the King, Kōcceṅkaṇ, who spent all his life in waging battles.
VMS10 / {3:50}__PIFI7+
{$} kalavu cīvarattār kaiyil uṇpavar kulavamāṭṭāk kuḻakaṉ uṟaiviṭam
the place of dwelling of the youth Civaṉ who is not appreciated by the buddhists who cover their bodies with Civaram and amaṇar who eat their food using their hands as eating plates.
culavu māmatilum cutai māṭamum nilavu taṇṭalai nīḷ neṟi (kāṇmiṉ, ē)
is taṇṭali nīṇeṟi where the big wall of fortification surrounding the place and white plastered storeys are conspicuous.
VMS11 / {3:50}__PIFI8+
{$} taṇṭalai nīḷ neṟi nātaṉai
upon the master in taṇṭali nīṇeṟi.
nīṟṟar tōṟṟum mēṉmaiyar tōṇipurattiṟai cāṟṟu ñāṉacampantaṉ tamiḻ valār
those who are able to recite the tamiḻ verses composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who is prised and who is the chief in toṇipuram, where great people appear beautiful having adorned themselves with sacred ash.
māṟṟu il celvar
are people of wealth which never leaves them.
piṟappai maṟappar (ē)
will forget being born agin

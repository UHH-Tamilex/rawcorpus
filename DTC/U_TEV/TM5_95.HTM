• patikam: [5:95]
  • talam: POTU
  • (()): _iliṅka purāṇat tiruk kuṟuntokai
  • VMS: Vol.15, p.127

*1
pukku _aṇaintu purintu _alar _iṭṭilar;
nakku _aṇaintu naṟumalar koytilar;
cokku _aṇainta cuṭar_oḷivaṇṇaṉai
mikkuk kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*2
_alarum nīrum koṇṭu _āṭṭit teḷintilar;
tilakam maṇṭalam tīṭṭit tirintilar;
_ulakamūrtti, _oḷiniṟa-vaṇṇaṉaic
celavu kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*3
_āppi nīrōṭu _alaku kaik koṇṭilar;
pūp pey kūṭai puṉaintu cumantilar;
kāppukkoḷḷi, kapālitaṉ vēṭattai
_ōppik kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*4
neyyum pālum koṇṭu _āṭṭi niṉaintilar;
poyyum pokkamum pōkkip pukaḻntilar;
_aiyaṉ, veyya _aḻalniṟa-vaṇṇaṉai
meyyaik kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*5
_erukku_amkaṇṇikoṇṭu _iṇṭai puṉaintilar;
perukkak kōvaṇam pīṟi _uṭuttilar;
tarukkiṉāl ceṉṟu, tāḻcaṭai _aṇṇalai
nerukki, kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*6
maraṅkaḷ _ēṟi malar paṟittu _iṭṭilar;
nirampa nīr cumantu _āṭṭi niṉaintilar;
_uram porunti, _oḷiniṟa-vaṇṇaṉai
nirampak kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*7
kaṭṭuvāṅkam kapālam kaikkoṇṭilar;
_aṭṭamāṅkam kiṭantu _aṭi vīḻntilar;
ciṭṭaṉ cēvaṭi ceṉṟu _eytik kāṇiya,
paṭṭa kaṭṭam _uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*8
ventanīṟu viḷaṅka _aṇintilar;
kantamāmalar _iṇṭai puṉaintilar;
_entai, _ēṟu _ukantu _ēṟu _erivaṇṇaṉai,
_antam kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*9
_iḷa _eḻunta _iruṅkuvaḷai(m)malar
piḷavu ceytu, piṇaittu _aṭi _iṭṭilar;
kaḷavucey toḻil kāmaṉaik kāyntavaṉ
_aḷavu kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*10
kaṇṭi pūṇṭu kapālam kaik koṇṭilar;
viṇṭa vāṉ caṅkam vimma vāyvaittilar;
_aṇṭamūrtti, _aḻalniṟa-vaṇṇaṉaik
keṇṭik kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*11
ceṅkaṇāṉum piramaṉum tammuḷē
_eṅkum tēṭit tirintavar kāṇkilār;
_iṅku _uṟṟēṉ! _eṉṟu _iliṅkattē tōṉṟiṉāṉ,
poṅku ceñcaṭaip puṇṇiyamūrttiyē.

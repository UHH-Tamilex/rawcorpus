• patikam: [5:95]
  • talam: POTU
  • (()): _iliṅka purāṇat tiruk kuṟuntoki
  • VMS: Vol.15, p.127

*1
pukku _aṇintu purintu _alar _iṭṭilar;
nakku _aṇintu naṟumalar koytilar;
cokku _aṇinta cuṭar_oḷivaṇṇaṉi
mikkuk kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*2
_alarum nīrum koṇṭu _āṭṭit teḷintilar;
tilakam maṇṭalam tīṭṭit tirintilar;
_ulakamūrtti, _oḷiniṟa-vaṇṇaṉic
celavu kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*3
_āppi nīrōṭu _alaku kik koṇṭilar;
pūp pey kūṭi puṉintu cumantilar;
kāppukkoḷḷi, kapālitaṉ vēṭatti
_ōppik kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*4
neyyum pālum koṇṭu _āṭṭi niṉintilar;
poyyum pokkamum pōkkip pukaḻntilar;
_aiyaṉ, veyya _aḻalniṟa-vaṇṇaṉi
meyyik kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*5
_erukku_amkaṇṇikoṇṭu _iṇṭi puṉintilar;
perukkak kōvaṇam pīṟi _uṭuttilar;
tarukkiṉāl ceṉṟu, tāḻcaṭi _aṇṇali
nerukki, kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*6
maraṅkaḷ _ēṟi malar paṟittu _iṭṭilar;
nirampa nīr cumantu _āṭṭi niṉintilar;
_uram porunti, _oḷiniṟa-vaṇṇaṉi
nirampak kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*7
kaṭṭuvāṅkam kapālam kikkoṇṭilar;
_aṭṭamāṅkam kiṭantu _aṭi vīḻntilar;
ciṭṭaṉ cēvaṭi ceṉṟu _eytik kāṇiya,
paṭṭa kaṭṭam _uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*8
ventanīṟu viḷaṅka _aṇintilar;
kantamāmalar _iṇṭi puṉintilar;
_enti, _ēṟu _ukantu _ēṟu _erivaṇṇaṉi,
_antam kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*9
_iḷa _eḻunta _iruṅkuvaḷi(m)malar
piḷavu ceytu, piṇittu _aṭi _iṭṭilar;
kaḷavucey toḻil kāmaṉik kāyntavaṉ
_aḷavu kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*10
kaṇṭi pūṇṭu kapālam kik koṇṭilar;
viṇṭa vāṉ caṅkam vimma vāyvittilar;
_aṇṭamūrtti, _aḻalniṟa-vaṇṇaṉik
keṇṭik kāṇal_uṟṟār---_aṅku _iruvarē.

*11
ceṅkaṇāṉum piramaṉum tammuḷē
_eṅkum tēṭit tirintavar kāṇkilār;
_iṅku _uṟṟēṉ! _eṉṟu _iliṅkattē tōṉṟiṉāṉ,
poṅku ceñcaṭip puṇṇiyamūrttiyē.

• patikam: [3:112]
  • talam: tirup pallavaṉīccuram
  • paṇ: paḻam pañcuram
  • (()): _īraṭi
  • VMS: Vol.1, p.124

*1
paracu pāṇiyar, pāṭal vīṇaiyar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_aracu pēṇi niṉṟār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*2
paṭṭam neṟṟiyar, naṭṭam_āṭuvar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_iṭṭam_āy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*3
pavaḷamēṉiyar, tikaḻum nīṟṟiṉar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_aḻakarāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*4
paṇṇil yāḻiṉar, payilum montaiyar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_aṇṇalāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*5
pal_il_ōṭṭiṉar, pali koṇṭu _uṇpavar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_elli _āṭṭu _ukantār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*6
paccai mēṉiyar, piccai koḷpavar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_iccai_āy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*7
paiṅkaṇ _ēṟṟiṉar, tiṅkaḷ cūṭuvar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_eṅkum_āy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*8
pātam kaitoḻa vētam _ōtuvar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_ātiyāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*9
paṭi koḷ mēṉiyar, kaṭi koḷ koṉṟaiyar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_aṭikaḷāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*10
paṟai koḷ pāṇiyar, piṟai koḷ ceṉṉiyar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟai pallavaṉīccurattu
_iṟaivarāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmai _aṟivār _ār?

*11
vāṉam _āḷvataṟku _ūṉam _oṉṟu _ilai---mātar pallavaṉīccurattāṉai
ñāṉacampantaṉ nal-tamiḻ colla vallavar nallavarē.

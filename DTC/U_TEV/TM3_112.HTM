• patikam: [3:112]
  • talam: tirup pallavaṉīccuram
  • paṇ: paḻam pañcuram
  • (()): _īraṭi
  • VMS: Vol.1, p.124

*1
paracu pāṇiyar, pāṭal vīṇiyar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_aracu pēṇi niṉṟār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*2
paṭṭam neṟṟiyar, naṭṭam_āṭuvar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_iṭṭam_āy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*3
pavaḷamēṉiyar, tikaḻum nīṟṟiṉar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_aḻakarāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*4
paṇṇil yāḻiṉar, payilum montiyar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_aṇṇalāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*5
pal_il_ōṭṭiṉar, pali koṇṭu _uṇpavar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_elli _āṭṭu _ukantār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*6
pacci mēṉiyar, picci koḷpavar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_icci_āy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*7
piṅkaṇ _ēṟṟiṉar, tiṅkaḷ cūṭuvar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_eṅkum_āy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*8
pātam kitoḻa vētam _ōtuvar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_ātiyāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*9
paṭi koḷ mēṉiyar, kaṭi koḷ koṉṟiyar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_aṭikaḷāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*10
paṟi koḷ pāṇiyar, piṟi koḷ ceṉṉiyar, paṭṭiṉattu _uṟi pallavaṉīccurattu
_iṟivarāy _iruppār,---_ivar taṉmi _aṟivār _ār?

*11
vāṉam _āḷvataṟku _ūṉam _oṉṟu _ili---mātar pallavaṉīccurattāṉi
ñāṉacampantaṉ nal-tamiḻ colla vallavar nallavarē.

{1:57}__1+
{$} oḷḷitu uḷḷak katikku ām
if one thinks of good things it will be of help to a higher state of existence
oḷi veḷḷiyāṉ ivaṉ uṟai vēṟkāṭu uḷḷiyār uyarntār, ivvulakil teḷḷiyār avara tēvarē
those who think of vēṟkāṭu of this god who is white in colour, are great people, they have clear knowledge in this world; they will be definitely considered as tēvar in this world.
{1:57}__2+
{$} āṭal nākam acaintu aḷavu illatu ōr vēṭam koṇṭavaṉ vēṟkāṭu pāṭiyum paṇintār
those who made homage by singing and dancing vēṟkātu of Civaṉ who has countless forms, having tied a cobra which can dance.
cēṭar ākiya celvarē
are great persons possessing wealth.
{1:57}__3+
{$} pūtam pāṭap puṟaṅkāṭṭiṭai āṭi, vētavittakaṉ
Civaṉ the dancer in the cremation ground outside the village, when pūtams sing; is well-versed in vētams.
vēṟkāṭu pōtum cāntum pukaiyum koṭuttavarkku ētam eytutal illaiyē
sufferings will not reach those who offer him in vēṟkāṭu flowers about to blossom sandal-paste, and incense.
{1:57}__4+
{$} āḻkaṭal eṉakku kaṅkai karantavaṉ
concealed the Kaṅki, like the deep ocean.
vīḻ caṭaiyiṉaṉ vēṟkāṭu tāḻvu uṭai maṉattāl paṇintu ēttiṭa
if people pay homage to, and prise, with humble minds, vēṟkāṭu of Civaṉ who has a caṭi like serial roots.
avar pāvam pāḻpaṭum
their sins will be ruined.
{1:57}__5+
{$} kāṭṭiṉālum ayarttiru akkālaṉai vīṭṭiṉāṉa, uṟai vēṟkāṭu pāṭṭiṉāl paṇintu ēttiṭa vallavar
those who are able to prise and pay homage to, by singing songs about vēṟkāṭu where Civaṉ who killed that Kālaṉ who forgot the fact though civan ear before him Civaṉ was the sole cuse when mārkaṇteyaṉ worshipped Civaṉ.
ōṭṭiṉār viṉai ollaiyē
drove away the acts quickly.
{1:57}__6+
{$} tōliṉāl uṭai mēva vallāṉ
Civaṉ who is able to make a dress of a skin.
cuṭar vēliṉāṉ uṟai vēṟkāṭu nūliṉāl paṇintu ēttiṭa vallavar
those who are able to prise, and pay homage to, according to rules lid down in ākamams, vēṟkāṭu where Civaṉ who has a bright trident dwells.
māliṉ ār viṉai māyumē
the acts which are born out of confusion, will be destroyed.
{1:57}__7+
{$} mallal mummatil māytara eytatu ōr villiṉāṉa, uṟai vēṟkāṭu collavalla curuṅkā maṉattavar
those who are large hearted and can prise vēṟkāṭu where Civaṉ who has a bow from which he shot an arrow on the fertile three forts to destroy them.
cella vallavar tīrkkamē
will live long in this world.
[[Variant reading: (1) tikkamē]]
{1:57}__8+
{$} mūral veṇmati cūṭum muṭivuṭai vīraṉ mēviya vēṟkāṭu vāramāy vaḻikāṭu niṉaintavar
Those who worshipped with love and intention vēṟkāṭu of the herd who wears on head a smiling white crescent.
cērvar ceykaḻal tiṇṇamē
will definitely reach the feet of Civaṉ.
{1:57}__9+
{$} parakkiṉār paṭuveṇtalaiyil pali virakkiṉāṉ uṟai vēṟkāṭṭūr
in the shrine of vēṟkāṭu where Civaṉ who has the ability to receive alms in the white skull of those who wandered searching his head and feet
[[virakkiṉāṉ-virakiṉāṉ the underlined letter is augmentation for rhyme.]]
arakkaṉ āṇmai aṭarappaṭṭāṉ iṟai nerukkiṉāṉai niṉaimiṉē
meditate on the Lord who pressed down a little to crush the valour of the arakkaṉ.
{1:57}__10+
{$} māṟu ilā malarāṉoṭu mālavaṉ vēṟalāṉ
Civaṉ who gined a victory over Piramaṉ who has no equal and mālavaṉ
[[vēṟalāṉ can be split into vēṟu alāṉ: one who is not dfferent from Piramaṉ and Māl.]]
uṟai vēṟkāṭu īṟu ilā moḻiyē(1) moḻiyā eḻil kūṟiṉārkku illai kuṟṟamē
there will be no defect to those who speak eternal words of the Vētams, upon the beauty of Vēṟkāṭu where noone dwells.
[[Variant reading: (1) moḻivemmoḻi]]
{1:57}__11+
{$} viṇṭamām poḻil (1) poḻilcūḻ tiruvēṟkāṭu kaṇṭu
visiting tiruvēṟkāṭu surrounded by mango-groves which have blossomed.
nampaṉ kaḻalpēṇi
cherishing with love the feet of Civaṉ.
caṇpai (2) ñāṉacampantaṉ centamiḻ koṇṭu pāṭakkuṇam ām
it is a meritorious act to sing the prise of Civaṉ with the help of the songs done in refined Tamiḻ by Ñāṉacampantaṉ of Caṇpi.
[[Variant readings: (1) cūḻtaru (2) ñāṉacampantaṉatiṉṟamiḻ]]

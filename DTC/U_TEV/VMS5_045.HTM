[[This decade has the theme of akapporuḷ. Mother advises her daughter to remain in the house but the daughter who has fallen in love with the Lord refuses to obey the mothers command.]]

{5:45}__1+
{$} mātu iyaṉṟu maṉaikku iru eṉṟakkāl
If I say, ``stay in the house, possessing the qualities of womanhood''
[[maṉaikku-maṉaiyil]]
nītitāṉ cola nī eṉakku ār eṉum
she says, who are you to advise me right conduct?
eṉtaiyal
my young daughter.
cōti ārtaru tōṇipuravarkkut, tāti nāṉ āvaṉ eṉṟum
says, I would become the maid-servant of the effulgent Lord in Tōṇipuram.
[[Variant reading: 1. mātiyeṉṟu]]
{5:45}__2+
{$} nakkam vantu paliiru eṉṟārkku iṭṭam, mikka taiyalai veḷvaḷai koḷvatu
To take away the white bangles of my young daughter who has much desire towards the Lord who asked, give me alms, coming naked.
tokkanīrvayal 1. tōṇi puravarkkut, tamatu perumaikkut takkatu aṉṟu
does not fit with the greatness of the Lord in Tōṇipuram which has fields which have plenty of water,
[[Variant reading:1.tōṇipuravaṉār]]
{5:45}__3+
{$} keṇṭaipōl nayaṉattu imavāṉ makaḷ, vaṇṭu vārkuḻalāḷ uṭaṉākavē kaṇṭu (um)
even after seeing the Lord along with the daughter of Imavāṉ, who has eyes like keṇtai (a fresh-water fish), and who has long tresses of hair on which bees settle.
tōṇi puravaraik kāmuṟukiṉṟaṉaḷ kaṉṉiyē
my unmarried daughter desires the Lord in Tōṇipuram.
{5:45}__4+
{$} pālaiyāḻmoḻiyāḷ avaḷ
that lady (Kaṅkai) who has sweet words like the music produced in pālaiyāḻ
tāḻcaṭai, mēlaḷ āvatu kaṇṭaṉaḷ taiyal
my young daughter saw her to be on the caṭai which falls low.
viṇ uṟac, cōlai ārtaru tōṇipuravarkkuc, cāla nallaḷ ākiṉṟaṉaḷ
has become very strong in love with the Lord in Tōṇipuram which has gardens to touch the sky.
{5:45}__5+
{$} paṇṇiṉ nēr moḻiyāḷ, pali iṭṭa ip, peṇṇai māl koṭu peyvaḷai koḷvatu
snatching away the bangles worn by this girl who speaks as sweet as melody and went to give alms, by making her infatuated with love.
tōṇipurattu uṟai, cuṇṇam āṭiya aṇṇalārukkuc cāla aḻukitē?
is it comely for the eminent god who resides in Tōṇipuram and who smears himself with the sacred ash?
{5:45}__6+
{$} mullai veṇnakai moy kuḻalāy
Girl who has very close tresses of hair, and teeth as white as the flowers of mullai (arabian jasmine)!
uṉakku, allaṉ āvatu nī aṟintilai
you did not understand that the Lord is too great a person to receive you.
kaṉi ārpoḻil, tollait tōṇipuravarkkē naṅkaiyē! nallai āyiṭukiṉṟaṉai
Girl of distinction! you become good only to the Lord in ancient Tōṇipuram which has parks of abundant fruits.
[[This verse is addressed to the daughter in the 2nd person, by the mother]]
{5:45}__7+
{$} ulakattavar oṉṟutāṉ aṟiyār
The people of this world do not know the reason for the melody of the girl, which is different by nature.
niṉṟu colli
standing before the girl infatuated with love, and giving her advice.
1. nikaḻnta niṉaippilar
had no thought why and how it happened to her.
tuṉṟuvār poḻil tōṇipuravar tam koṉṟai cūṭum kuṟippu atu ākum
The reason for her love-sickness is adorning herself with the koṉṟai flowers belonging to the Lord in Tōṇipuram surrounded by long and dense parks.
[[Variant reading: 1.nikaḻntu]]
{5:45}__8+
{$} uṟavu pēykkaṇam
His relations are groups of pēy [[pēy -- goblins]]
uṇpatu veṇtalai
the vessel from which he eats is white skull
uṟaivatu īmam
The place where he resides is the cremation ground.
uṭaliṉ ōr peṇ koṭi
He has in his body a lady who is as tender as a creeper.
tuṟaikaḷ ārkaṭal tōṇipurattu uṟai, iṟaivaṉārkku ivaṉ eṉkaṇṭu aṉpu āvatu
what did this girl find in the Lord who resides in Tōṇipuram which has a sea where there are many ghats, to fall in love with?
[[All the things mentioned are against falling in love with the Lord.]]
{5:45}__9+
{$} māka yāṉai maruppu ērmulaiyiṉar, pōka
when the young ladies who have breasts like the tusk of the elephant which resembles the cloud in colour.
yāṉum avaḷ pukkatē puka
I too who am her friend was going in the way my talaivi went.
tōkai ārtaru tōṇi puravarkkē yāṉum avarkku iṉi āḷatē āka
Let me also become the protege of the Lord in Tōṇipuram which is full of peacocks.
[[This is the words of the friend; āka verb in the optative mood); avarkku is put in apposition with Tōṇipuravar; the demonstrative pronoun means the Lord who has so much greatness which is indescribable]]
{5:45}__10+
{$} kaṭṭam pēciya kār arakkaṉ taṉaittuṭṭu aṭakkiya tōṇipurattu uṟai aṭṭamūrttukku aṉpu (atu) āki
having fallen in love with the Lord who has eight forms who resides in Tōṇipuram and who curbed the mischief of the black arakkaṉ who spoke proud words of no avail.
[[tuṭṭu=tuṭṭam-tuṣṭam]]
eṉ peṇ koṭi, iṭṭam āyiṉa ceyvāḷ
my daughter who is as tender as a creeper will do only, things which she desires.

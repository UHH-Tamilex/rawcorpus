[[Tirumālimāṟṟu: prosody these are called embellishments of words which is a kind of verse that remins identical when its letters are read in the reverse direction. Yapparuṇkaḷam names them as muṟikkavi, ie: `laboured poetry'; such verses were composed by Ñāṉacampantar when he was staying in Cīkāḻi after being visited by Tirunāvukkaracu; cf. periyapurāṇam (tiruñāṉacampantar purāṇam, 273-277);
miṟaikkavippāṭṭē (yāpparunkalam, 94)]]

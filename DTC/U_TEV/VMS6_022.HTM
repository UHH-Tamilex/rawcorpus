in the beautiful and cool nākikkārōṇam surrounded by the roaring sea and clear backwater.
maṉai tuṟanta val amaṇar taṅkaḷ poyyum
the false-hood of the strong amaṇar who have renounced house-holder's life and become ascetics.
māṇpu uraikkum maṉakkuṇṭar taṅkaḷ poyyum
the false-hood of the low-minded house-holders in jinism who dilate upon the greatness of those ascetics.
ciṉai potinta cīvarattar taṅkaḷ poyyum
and the false-hood of the buddhists, who cover their bodies with a cloth called, `Civaram'.
mey eṉṟu karutātē pōta
severing the connection with them and coming out without thinking that their doctrines are truths.

my mind!

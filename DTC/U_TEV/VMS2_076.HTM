{2:76}__1+
{$} vāṭiya veṇtalai mālai cūṭi
having worn the garland of skulls in which the flesh has dried.
mayaṅku iruḷ
in the darkness which cannot be distinguished whether it is day or night.
nīṭu uyarkoḷḷi viḷakkum āka
having as light the very long fire-brand.
nivanta eri āṭiya em perumāṉ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyaip pāṭiya cintaiyiṉārkaṭku
to those who sing the prises of Akattiyāṉpaḷḷi of our Lord who danced in the rising fire.
pāvam (ē) illai (ām)
there will be no sins.
{2:76}__2+
{$} tuṉṉam koṇṭa uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ wears a loin-cloth stitched with several pieces of cloth;
[[cf. eṉ appaṉ empirāṉ ellārkkum tāṉ īcaṉ, tuṉṉam pey kōvaṇamāk koḷḷumatu eṉṉēṭi, maṉṉukalai tuṉṉuporuḷ maṟaināṉkē vāṉ caraṭāt, taṉṉaiyē kōvaṇamāc cāttiṉaṉ kāṇ cāḻalō- (tiruvācakam, tiruccāḻal, 2)]]
tutainta veṇṇīṟṟāṉ
smears his body thickly with a coating of whtie sacred ash.
maṉṉum koṉṟai matamattam cūṭiṉāṉ mānakar
the great city of Civaṉ who adorns his head profusely with koṉṟi and datura flowers
aṉṉam taṅkum poḻil cūḻ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyai
akattiyāṉpaḷḷi which is surrounded by gardens where swans stay.
uṉṉam cey maṉattārkaḷ tam viṉai ōṭum (ē)
the acts good and bad of those who meditate upon the shrine will flee from them.
{2:76}__3+
{$} poḻil cūḻ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyāṉ
the Lord in akattiyāṉpaḷḷi surrounded by gardens.
uṭuttatuvum pulittōl
dresses in tiger`s skin
uṇpatum pali tirintu
eats from alms for which he wanders.
kaṭuttu vanta kālaṉ taṉṉaiyum kālaṉāl 1. aṭarttatuvum
killed with his leg even the god of death who came angrily upon mārkkaṇtēyaṉ
toṭuttatuvum caram muppuram tukaḷ āka(ē)
shot arrow from the bow to reduce the three cities to ash.
[[Variant reading: 1. aṭuttatuvum]]
{2:76}__4+
{$} poḻil cūḻ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyāṉ
Civaṉ in akattiyāṉpaḷḷi surrounded by gardens.
kāyntatuvum aṉṟu kāmaṉai neṟṟik kaṇṇiṉāl
burnt in the past Kamaṉ (cupid) with the frontal eye.
pāyntatuvum kaḻal kālaṉai
sprang upon Kalaṉ and killed him with his leg.
paṇṇiṉ nāṉ maṟai āyntatum
what he investigated the four vētams which had music in them.
ēyntatuvum imavāṉmakaḷ oru pākam (ē)
was united with the dughter of imavāṉ on one half.
{2:76}__5+
{$} poḻil cūḻ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyāṉ
see 3rd verse.
pōrttatuvum kariyiṉ uri
covered himself as a blanket with the skin of an elephant.
pulittōl uṭai
has a dress of a tiger`s skin.
kūrattatu ōr veṇmaḻu ēnti
holding a white battle-axe which is very sharp.
kōḷ aravam araikkum ārttatuvum
tied in his waist a cobra capable of killing.
pārttatuvum (m) araṇam paṭar eri mūḻka (ē)
fixed his gaze on the fortifications to be engulfed by the spreading fire.

terintuvum kaṇai oṉṟu
selected a single arrow
muppuram ceṉṟu uṭaṉ erintatuvum
burnt the three cities at the same time, going there.
muṉ eḻil ār malar uṟaivāṉ talai arintatuvum
cut of the head ofpiramaṉ who dwells in a beautiful flower, long ago.
purintatuvum(m) umaiyāḷ or pākam puṉaital (ē)
desired to adorn himself on one half Umayāḷ
{2:76}__7+
{$} ellām ōti ulakukku 1. ōr oṇ poruḷ āki
being a thing of splendour which is unequalled, the one who revealed all the vētams chanting them!
meyc cōti eṉṟu toḻuvār(avar) tuyar tīrttiṭum āti eṅkaḷ perumāṉ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyai 2. nītiyāl toḻuvār viṉai nīṅkum(ē)
the acts, good and bad of those who worship with joined hand, with a disciplined mine, akattiyāṉ paḷḷi of our Lord who is the first cuse and who definitely removes the sufferings of those who worship him thinking him to be true supreme light, will vanish.
[[Variant reading: 1. oru poruḷāki 2. nītiyiṉāl]]

ceṟuttatuvum takkaṉ vēḷviyai

tiruntārpuram oṟuttatuvum

{$} ciramum, nalla mati, mattamum, tikaḻ koṉṟaiyum, aravum, malaku ceñcaṭaiyāṉ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyaip piramaṉōṭu tirumālum tēṭiya 1. peṟṟimai parava vallār (avar) taṅkaḷ mēl viṉai pāṟum (ē)
the acts, good and bad which are with those who can prise the event of Piramaṉ and tirumāl who searched in vin the head and feet of Civaṉ in akattiyāṉpaḷḷi, who has on his red caṭi a skull, beautiful crescent, datura flowers, shining koṉṟi flowers and a cobra all of which are flourishing, will flee from them.
[[Variant reading: 1. peṟṟiyaip]]
{2:76}__10+
{$} centuvar āṭaiyiṉārum veṟṟaraiyē tiri punti illārkaḷum pēcum pēccu(avai) poymmoḻi
the doctirnes preached by the buddhist who wear a robe soaked in red ochre and the camaṇar who have no intellect and who wander naked, do not contin any truth; they are lies.
antaṇaṉ eṅkaḷ pirāṉ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyaic cintimiṉ
(People of this world!) meditate upon akattiyāṉ palli of our Lord, Civaṉ who is a brahmin among gods.
num viṉai 1. āṉavai citaintu ōṭum (ē)
all your acts good and bad will be destroyed and will run away from you.
[[Variant reading: 1. āyiṉa]]

{$} māmayil ālum cōlai puṭai cūḻ akattiyāṉ paḷḷiyuḷ
in akattiyāṉ paḷḷi which is surrounded on all sides by gardens where the big peacocks dance.
ñālam malkum tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ
-ñāṉacampantaṉ who has knowledge of tamiḻ which is flourishing in this world.
cūlam nalla paṭaiyāṉ aṭi toḻutu ēttiya mālai vallār (avar) taṅkaḷ mēla viṉai māyum (ē)
the acts good and bad which are with those who are capable of reciting the garlands of verses composed in prise of the feet of Civaṉ who has a good weapon of a trident, and worshipping them with joined hands, will perish.

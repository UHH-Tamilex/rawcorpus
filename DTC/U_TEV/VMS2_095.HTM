{2:95}__1+
{$} pāṭal vaṇṭu aṟai koṉṟai pālmati pāy puṉal kaṅkai kōṭal kūviḷamālai mattamum ceñcaṭaik kulāvi
wearing on the red caṭi koṉṟi on which bees hum, a crescent as white as milk, Kanki of flowing water, white malabar glory lily, garland of bel leaves and datura flowers, to be conspicuous.
vāṭal veṇtalaimālai maruviṭa
the garland of dired-up skulls to be united with him.
1. valliyam tōlmēl āṭal mācuṇam acaitta aṭikaḷukku iṭam araciliyē
aracili is the place of the god who tied on the tiger's skin a dancing cobra.
[[Variant reading: 1. valliyan tōḷmēl]]
{2:95}__2+
{$} ēṟu pēṇi atu ēṟi
riding on a bull desiring it
iḷa matakaḷiṟṟiṉai eṟṟi vēṟu ceytu ataṉ urivai veṇpulāl kalakka mey pōrtta ūṟu tēṉavaṉ
Civaṉ is like the secreting sweetness of the honey, who covered his body the white flesh to mix with it, killing a young male elephant of must striking it, killing it and flaying its skin.
umparkku oruvaṉ
the unequalled god to the celestials in heaven.
oḷikoḷ oṇcuṭar ām āṟucērtaru ceṉṉi aṭikaḷukku iṭam araciliyē
aracili is the place of the god who has on his head a river and who is the bright light divine which has brilliance.
[[Variant reading: 1. vempulāl]]
{2:95}__3+
{$} kaṅkai nīr caṭaimēlē katammika
the water of the Kanki to flow swiftly on the caṭi.
katir iḷa aṉam meṉ koṅkaiyāḷ oru pākam maruviya kollai veḷḷēṟṟaṉ
Civaṉ has a white bull which lives in the forest tract, being united with a lady of soft breasts, who is like the young shining swan.
caṅkaiyāyt tiriyāmē taṉ aṭiyārkku aruḷ ceytu
bestowing his grace on his devotees so that they might not be in doubt whether Civaṉ will grant his grace or not.
aṅkaiyāl aṉal ēntum aṭikaḷukku iṭam araciliyē
aracili is the place of the god who holds in his palm, fire.
{2:95}__4+
{$} mikka kālaṉai vīṭṭi
killing the Kālaṉ who is very particular about his duty.
mey keṭak kāmaṉai viḻittu
opening the frontal eye to destroy the body of Kāmaṉ (cupid)
pukka ūr iṭu piccai uṇpatu
what Civaṉ eats is the alms which the people of the village into which he enters, place in his bowl:
poṉ tikaḻ koṉṟai takka nūl tikaḻ mārpil tavaḷa nīṟu aṇintu
smearing the white sacred ash in the chest in which the fitting sacred thread and koṉṟi shining like gold are worn.
āmai akkiṉ āramum pūṇṭa aṭikaḷukku iṭam araciliyē
aracili is the place of the god who adorns himself with the shell of a tortoise and a garland of bones.
{2:95}__5+
{$} māṉ añcum maṭanōkki malaimakaḷ pākamum maruvi
being united with on one half of the body the dughter of the mountin who has beautiful looks of which the deer is afrid
tāṉ añcā araṇ mūṉṟum taḻal eḻac caram atu turantu
discharging an arrow for the fire to rise in all the three forts which had no fear.
vāṉ añcum peruviṭattai uṇṭavaṉ
Civaṉ who consumed the big poison of which the unhabitants of the heaven feared.
māmaṟai ōti
who chants the great vētams.
āṉ añcu āṭiya ceṉṉi aṭikaḷukku iṭam araciliyē
aracili is the place of the god whose head is bathed in the five products of the cow.
{2:95}__6+
{$} pariya mācuṇam kayiṟāp paruppatam ataṟku mattu ākap periya vēlaiyaik kalaṅkap pēṇiya vāṉavar kaṭaiya
when the celestials who were desirous of the nectar, churned the big ocean to get ruffled using the big serpent as the cord wound round the churning staff and the churning rod for that beeing the mountin mēru
[[vaṭavaraiyai mattu ākki vācukiyai nāṇ ākkik kaṭal vaṇṇaṉ paṇṭu oru nāḷ kaṭal vayiṟu kalakkiṉaiyē- (cilappatikaram, 17-32);
pāmpu kayiṟāk kaṭal kaṭainta māyavaṉ- (do.19)]]
kariya nañcu (atu) tōṉṟa
when the black poison appeared from it.
kalaṅkiya avartamaik kaṇṭu ariya ār amutu ākkum aṭikaḷukku iṭam araciliyē
aracili is the place of the god who transformed it into a rare nectar which is difficult to get, seeing those who were agitated in thier minds.

fixing the toe to crush the eyes, shoulders and good mouths of that arakkaṉ who wanted to topple down the big and white mountin, Kayili.

listening to the songs with music sung to the accompaniment of the guts of the hand improvised as a muscial instrument.

aracili is the place of the god who by his superiority granted his grace to the arakkaṉ.

veṟikoḷ tāmarai mēlē virumpiya meyt tavattōṉum
and Piramaṉ who performed sincere penance and is seated with desire on a fragrant lotus flower.
ceṟivu oṇāvakai eṅkum tēṭiyum tiruvaṭikāṇa aṟivu oṇā uruvattu em aṭikaḷukku iṭam araciliyē

VMS11 / {2:95}__PIFI10+
{$} alli nīḷvayal cūḻnta aracili aṭikaḷai
on the god in aracili surrounded by long fields in which lilies grow.
kāḻi nalla ñāṉacampantaṉ naṟṟamiḻ pattu ivai nāḷum colla vallavar tammaic cuḻntu amarar toḻutu ētta
the immortals to prise by worshipping with joined hands going from left to right those who are capable of reciting dily these ten verses done in chaste tamiḻ by good-natured ñāṉacampantaṉ of Kāḻi.
vallavāṉ ulaku eyti vaikalum makiḻntiruppārē
they will dwell with joy always, reaching the heaven which is capable of many things.

munti eyil eyta mutalvaṉār
Civaṉ, the first cuse who destroyed the three forts by shooting an arrow, long ago.
cintippār viṉai tīrttiṭun celvaṉār
-the Lord who completely destroys the acts of those who meditate on him.
antik kōṉ taṉakkē aruḷ ceytavar pantic ceñcaṭaip pācūr aṭikaḷē
the deity in pācūr who has a red caṭi bound and who granted his grace to the moon that rules in the evening.
{5:25}__2+
{$}
maṭantai pākam makiḻnta maṇāḷar
the bridegroom who rejoiced in having a lady on one half.
toṭarnta valviṉai pōkkiṭum cōtiyār
the divine light that removes the irresistible acts which follow the soul in every birth.
kaṭanta kālaṉaik kālkoṭu pāyntavar
pounced on the god of death who was victorious formerly, with his
paṭarnta nākattār pācūr aṭikaḷē
the god in pācūr wears a cobra that spreads its hood.
{5:25}__3+
{$}
nāṟu koṉṟaiyum nākamum tiṅkaḷum āṟum ceñcaṭai vaitta aḻakaṉār
the beautiful god who placed on his red caṭi fragrant koṉṟi, a cobra, a crescent and a river.
kāṟu kaṇṭattavar
has a blackened neck.
kaiyatu ōr cūlattār
holds a trident in his hand.
pāṟiṉ ōṭṭiṉar pācūr aṭikaḷē
the god in pācūr has a ruined skull.
[[Variant reading: 1. mutalvaṉār : amutaṉār]]

{$}
veṟṟiyūr uṟai vētiyar āvar
Civaṉ is the brahmin who dwells in veṟṟiyūr (veṟṟiyūr has no tevaram for it; but is mentioned in tevarams of other shines; it is called in tamiḻ vipputtalam).
nal oṟṟi ēṟu ukantu ēṟum oruvaṉār
the unequalled God who rides with joy on a bull that has no equal.
neṟṟik kaṇṇiṉar
has a frontal eye.
nīḷ aravam taṉaippaṟṟi āṭṭuvar pācūr aṭikaḷē
the god in pācūr will make a cobra to dance catching hold of it.
[[This is a local story about Civaṉ in this shrine, see verse, 9 of this decade]]

maṭṭu aviḻnta malar neṭuṅkaṇṇipāl iṭṭa vēṭkaiyar āki iruppavar
has love placed on Umi who has long eyes like the flower from which honey drips.
tuṭṭarēl ivar cūḻcciyai aṟiyēṉ
if he is a mischievous person I did not know before hand the thought in his mind.
paṭṭam neṟṟiyar pācūr aṭikaḷē
the god in pācūr wears on his forehead a plate of gold.
{5:25}__6+
{$}
pal il ōṭukai ēntip pakal elām
holding a skull without teeth, during the whole of the daytime.
elli niṉṟu iṭupey pali ēṟpavar
receives the alms that is placed in his skull standing in one place in the hot part of the day.
palku nīṟṟiṉar pācūr aṭikaḷē
the god in pācūr smears sacred ash sumptuously.
collip pōyp pukum ūr aṟiyēṉ
I do not know the place where he stays, having gone, telling me about it.
colīr
please tell me (this verse is akapporuḷ)

kaṭṭi viṭṭa caṭaiyar kapāliyar
Civaṉ has a caṭi which he has tied and bundled up; has a skull.
eṭṭi nōkki vantu ilpukuntu (v) avar
who entered into the house coming peeping into it

iṭṭamā ivar ceyvaṉa aṟiyēṉ

vētam ōti vantu ilpukuntār avar
Civaṉ entered into the house chanting the vētams.
kātil veṇ kuḻai vaitta kapāliyār
who holds a skull and wore on the ear a white men's ear-ring.
nīti oṉṟu aṟiyār
does not know right conduct
niṟai koṇṭaṉar
took away complete self control of my mind.
pāti veṇpiṟaip pācūr aṭikaḷē
the god in pācūr who wears a crescent which is half the size of the full moon.

māliṉoṭu maṟaiyavaṉ tāṉum āy mēlum kīḻum aḷappu aritāyavar
Civaṉ could not be measured in the upper region and in the lower region, though Piramaṉ combined with Māl.
āliṉ nīḻal aṟam pakarntār
gave moral instruction under the shade of the banyan tree.
mikap pāl veṇ nīṟṟiṉar pācūr aṭikaḷē
the god in pācūr who smears himself with white sacred ash which is very white like milk.

{3:77}__1+
{$} poṉ iyal poruppu araiyaṉ maṅkai orupaṅkar, puṉal taṅku caṭaimēl vaṉṉiyoṭu mattamalar vaittatiṟal vittakar makiḻntu uṟaiviṭam
the place where Civaṉ who has a lady the dughter of the mountin which is gold, and who is a strong able person, who placed on the caṭi in which water stays, leaves of indian mesquit and datura flowers, dwells with joy.
kaṉṉi iḷavāḷai kutikoḷḷa
when the very young seabbard fish leap.
iḷavaḷḷai paṭar aḷḷal vayalvāy
in the fields which are miry where the tender creeping bindweed is spreading.
iḷamētikaḷ maṉṉippaṭintu
young buffaloes having bathed for a long time.
maṉaicēr utavi māṇikuḻiyē
is utavi māṇikuḻi where they return to their houses.
[[poṉiyal poruppu:
paṉimāl imayap poruppakam cērnta pollāk kaṭuṅkākkaiyum poṉṉiṟamāy irukkumeṉṟu ivvāṟu uraikkumaṉṟō ivvirunilamē (yāpparunkalakkariki, 3);
kāyciṉa āṉai vaḷarum kaṉakamalaiyarukē, pōyiṉa kākkaiyum aṉṟē paṭaittatu poṉvaṇṇamē (poṉvannattanṭāti, 100)]]
{3:77}__2+
{$} cōti miku nīṟu (atu) mey pūci
smearing the sacred ash of excessive brilliance.
orutōl uṭai puṉaintu
putting on beautifully a dress of a skin.
teruvē mātarmaṉaitōṟum icaipāṭi
singing music in every house of the ladies in the streets.
vacipēcum araṉār makiḻviṭam
the place where Araṉ who speaks fascinating words rejoices to dwells.
tātumali tāmarai maṇam kamaḻavaṇṭu mural taṇpaḻaṉammikku
increasing in cool fields where the bees hum to spread the fragrance of the lotus which has plenty of pollen.
ōtamali vēlaipuṭaicūḻ ulakil nīṭu utavi māṇikuḻiyē
is Utavi Māṇikuḻi which has been in existence since very old times, in the world surrounded on all sides by ocean which abounds in waves.
{3:77}__3+
{$} ampu aṉaiya kaṇ umai maṭantai avaḷ añci veruvac ciṉamuṭaik kampa matayāṉai uri ceyta araṉār karuti (1) mēya iṭam
the place which Araṉār who flayed an angry elephant tied to a pillar to make the lady Umi who has eyes like the arrow, to fear and get startled, desired to dwell thinking of its importance.
vampu mali cōlai puṭaicūḻa maṇi (2) māṭam (atu) nīṭi aḻakuār umpar (avar) kōṉ nakarameṉṉa mikamaṉutavi māṇikuḻi ām
is utavi māṇikuḻi which is eternal like the city of the King of the immortals which is beautiful, and where there are tall storeys set with gems, and which is surrounded on all sides by gardens which have abundance of fragrance.
[[Variant reading: (1) mēvumiṭamām (2) matiṉīṭi)]]
{3:77}__4+
{$} nittam niyamat toḻilaṉ āki neṭumāl kuṟaḷaṉ āki mikavum cittam (atu) orukki vaḻipāṭu ceykiṉṟa civalōkaṉ iṭam
the place of Civalōkaṉ whom the tall Māl assuming the form of a dwarf and practising dily routine of religious duties, controlling the mind very strictly.
kottu alar malarp poḻiliṉ nīṭu kula maññai naṭam (1) āṭa atu kaṇṭu vari vaṇṭukaḷ ulāvi otta icaipāṭu utavi māṇikuḻi ām
is utavi māṇikuḻi where, on seeing the superior peacocks dancing in the flower-gardens which blossom in bunches; the bees with lines, moving to and fro hum like the music in agreement with that dance.
[[Variant reading: (1) āṭalatu koṇṭu]]
{3:77}__5+
{$} mācu il maticūṭu caṭai māmuṭiyar
Civaṉ has a big caṭi coiled into a crown on which he wears a crescent without the black spot.
(1) val acurar tol nakaram muṉ nācam (atu) ceytu nalavāṉavarkaḷukku aruḷ ceyta nampaṉ iṭam
the place of nampaṉ (Civaṉ) who bestowed his grace on the good celestials, destroying the old cities of the strong acurar.
vācam malimeṉ kuḻal maṭantaiyarkaḷ māḷikaiyiṉ maṉṉi aḻaku ār ūcal micai ēṟi iṉitāka icaipāṭu utavi māṇikuḻiām
is utavi māṇikuḻi where the ladies who have very fragrant and soft tresses of hir stay in the mansions and climbing on the beautiful swing sing sweet music, appropriate to that.
[[Variant reading: (1) vallavuṇar]]
{3:77}__6+
{$} mantam malar koṇṭu vaḻipāṭu ceyum māṇiuyir vavva maṉamāy vanta oru kālaṉ uyir māḷa utai ceyta maṇikaṇṭaṉ iṭam
the place of Civaṉ who has a blue neck and who kicked the unequalled Kālaṉ (god of death) who came with the idea of taking away the life of the bachelor (mārkkaṇṭeyaṉ) who worshipped Civaṉ with soft and fresh flowers
[[manta malar may also means flowers which have a milk smell]]
cantiṉoṭu kār akil cumantu taṭamā malarkaḷ koṇṭu keṭilam untu puṉal vantu vayal pāyum maṇam ār utavi māṇikuḻi ām
is Utavi Māṇikuḻi full of fragrance where the river, Keṭilam, carrying sandal wood and eagle wood and bringing in its floods very big flowers, whose rising water comes and flows into the fields.
{3:77}__7+
{$} eṇ periya vāṉavarkaḷ niṉṟu tuti ceyya iṟaiyē karuṇaiyāy uṇpu ariya (1) nañcu ataṉai uṇṭu ulakam uyya aruḷ uttamaṉ iṭam
the place of the supreme being possessed of all moral attributes who granted his grace to save the world by consuming the poison which could not be consumed by any other god, showing a little of his grace when the esteemed great celestials like Māl, Piramaṉ and intiraṉ prised him without stopping.
[[uṇpu ariya nañcataṉai uṇṭu: viṇṇōr amutu uṇṭum cāva oruvarum, uṇṇāta nañcu uṇṭum iruntu aruḷ ceykuvāy (cilappatikāram, 12-22); nīlamaṇimiṭaṟṟu oruvaṉ pōla' eṉṟa karuttu, cātaṟkukkāraṇamākiya nañcu uṇṭum nilai peṟṟiruntāṟpōla nīyum cāvātiruttal vēṇṭumeṉpatām (puram, 91-6, old commentary)]]
paṇ payilum vaṇṭu pala keṇṭi matu uṇṭu niṟai paimpoḻiliṉ vāy oṇ palaviṉ iṉkaṉi corintu (2) maṇam nāṟu utavi māṇikuḻi ām
is utavi maṇikuḻi where many bees which practise melody-types, work in the flowers and drink honey, in the fertile gardens where fragrance spreads as the superior jack trees shed their sweet fruits.
[[Variant reading: (1) nañcutaṉai (2) maṇamār]]
{3:77}__8+
{$} eṇṇam (atu) iṉṟi eḻil ār kayilai māmalai eṭutta tiṟal ār tiṇṇiya arakkaṉai nerittu aruḷ purinta civalōkaṉ iṭam
the place of Civalōkam who granted his grace after crushing the valourous and strong arakkaṉ who lifted the great and beautiful mountin, Kayili without forethought
[[eṇṇam may also mean respect]]
paṇ amarum meṉ moḻiyiṉār paṇai mulaippavaḷa vāy aḻaku(atu) ār oḷnutal maṭantaiyar kuṭaintupuṉal āṭu utavi māṇikuḻi ām
is utavi māṇikuḻi where the ladies, whose words are like melody-types, and who have large breasts beautiful lips like coral and bright foreheads, bathe in the water plunging and diving.
{3:77}__9+
{$} nēṭum ayaṉōṭu tirumālum uṇarāvakai nimirntu
shooting up high so that Ayaṉ who searched the feet and Ayaṉ who searched the head could not know them, as a column of fire.
muṭimēl ēṭuulavu tiṅkaḷ matamattam itaḻiccaṭai emīcaṉ iṭam
the place of our Lord of the Universe who has on his caṭi on the head a crescent resembling the petal of the white lotus datura flowers and koṉṟi.
māṭu ulavu mallikai kuruntu koṭi mātavi cerunti, kuraviṉūṭu ulavu puṉṉai (1) virai tātu malicēr utavimāṇi kuḻiyē ām
is Utavimāṇikuḻi where fragrant pollen of the mast wood tree, which moves into the bottle-flower tree, panicled golden blossomed pear-tree, creeper of the common delight of the woods, wild lime trees and jasmine that spreads on the sides, are in an abundant quantity.
[[Variant reading: (1) viritātu]]
{3:77}__10+
{$} moṭṭai amaṇ ātar mututērar matiyillikaḷ muyaṉṟaṉa paṭum muṭṭaikaḷ moḻinta moḻikoṇṭu aruḷ ceyāta mutalvaṉ iṭam
the place of the chief who does not bestow his grace accepting the words spoken by the ignorant amaṇar who have a bald head as the hirs are removed by plucking and senior tērar (buddhists) whose doctrine is that the acts done by taking efforts will themselves bear fruit, who have no firm beliefs, and who have no intelligence.
maṭṭai mali tāḻai iḷanīr mutiya vāḻaiyil viḻunta ataril oṭṭa mali pūka nirai tāṟu utira ēṟu utavi māṇikuḻi ām
is utavi māṇikuḻi where the tender fruit of the cocoanut tree which has many leave stalks falls on the mature plintin tree, strikes aginst dense cluster of fruits of the areca-palm to shed and get stuck in the path.
{3:77}__11+
{$} untivaru taṇ keṭilam ōṭupuṉalcūḻ utavimāṇikuḻimēl
on Utavi Māṇikuḻi surrounded by the flowing water of the cool river Keṭilam which comes pushing many things.
antimati cūṭiya emmāṉai aṭicērum aṇikāḻi nakarāṉ
the native of the city of beautiful Kāḻi who always meditates on the feet of our Lord who wears on his head a crescent that rises at sunset.
cantam niṟai taṇṭamiḻ terintu uṇarum ñāṉacampantaṉatu col munti icai ceytu motivārkaḷ
those who recite with music at the beginning of the day the words of Ñāṉacampantaṉ who has knowledge of refined Tamiḻ full of rhythmic movement, by research.
(1) neṭuvāṉam nilaṉ (ē) uṭaiyārkaḷ
will obtin the extensive heaven and this world as their possession.
[[Variant reading: (1) neṭuvāṉulakamē]]

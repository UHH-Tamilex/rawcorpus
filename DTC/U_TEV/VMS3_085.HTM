{3:85}__1+
{$} maṭṭu oḷi viritaru malar niṟai curi kuḻal maṭavaral paṭṭu oḷi maṇi alkul umai amai uru oru pākamā
having the proper form of Umi who has a waist which wears a silk cloth and has a brilliant girdle set with shining precious stones, the lady with a curly hir in which flowers which have fragrance and spread brilliance are filled.
kaṭṭu oḷir puṉaloṭu kaṭi aravuṭaṉ uṟai muṭimicai viṭṭu oḷir utir pitir matiyavar pati 1. viḻimiḻalaiyē
vīḻimiḻali is the place of Civaṉ on whose head the shining water is arrested along with a fearful cobra that dwells there, has a crescent which sheds its rays intermittently
[[Vīlimiḻali has been shortened into Viḻimiḻali for rhythm; this will apply to the 10 verses also.]]
[[Variant reading: 1. vīḻimiḻalaiyē]]
{3:85}__2+
{$} 1. oḷ niṟa vari vaḷai neṟikuḻal eḻil moḻi iḷa mulaip peṇ uṟum uṭaliṉar
Civaṉ has in his body a lady who has immature breasts, beautiful words, curly tresses of hirs and wears very bright bangles with lines on them.
kaṭal viṭam miṭaṟiṉar
has in his neck poison that rose in the ocean.
kaṇ uṟu nutaliṉar
has a frontal eye
kaṭiyatu or viṭaiyiṉar
has a bull of quick git.
kaṉaliṉar
holds fire in his hand.
viṇ uṟu piṟai aṇi caṭaiyiṉar pati vīḻimiḻalai
vīḻimiḻi is the place of Civaṉ who adorns his head with a crescent that shines in the sky.
[[Variant reading: 1. eṇṇiṟa]]
{3:85}__3+
{$} maittaku matar viḻi malaimakaḷ uru oru pākamā vaittavar
Civaṉ who placed the body of pārvati who has proud eyes, made beautiful being coated with collyrium.
matakariyurivai ceytavar
flayed the frenzied elephant.
tamai maruviṉār tetteṉa icai mural ciritaiyar
has a conduct which is prised with music with measure of time by those who approached him.
[[tetteṉa: onomatopoeia.]]
(1) tikaḻtarum araviṉar
has a glittering cobra.
vittaka nakutalai uṭaiyavar iṭam viḻimiḻalaiyē
vīḻimiḻali is the place of Civaṉ who has a skilful lughing skull.
[[Variant reading: 1. tikaṭarumarāviṉar]]
{3:85}__4+
{$} cevvaḻal eṉa naṉi perukiya uruviṉar
Civaṉ has a form like the red fire which is very much increasing (in size).
ceṟitaru kavvu aḻal araviṉar
has a biting and poisonous cobra which is tied to the waist.
katir mutir maḻuviṉar
has a battle-axe which is exceedingly bright.
toḻu ilā mu aḻal nici cararviṟal avai aḻitara
to destroy the strength of the three angry acurar who did not worship Civaṉ.
mutumatiḷ vevvaḻal koḷa naṉi muṉipavar pati vaḻimiḻalaiyē
vīḻimiḻali is the place of Civaṉ who grew very much enraged so that hot fire might engulf the old forts. (nicicarar acurar who move about during night)
{3:85}__5+
{$} paiṅkaṇatu oru perumaḻalai veḷēṟṟiṉar
Civaṉ has a big white bull tender in age with eyes fuming with anger
[[maḻalai ēṟu: maḻalai ēṟṟu maṇāḷaṉ (nāvukkaracar tiruvīḻimiḻali (3) 11)]]
pali eṉā eṅkaṇum uḻitarvar
will wander everywhere saying give me alms.
amkaṇar
looks with kindness at his devotees.
imaiyavar toḻutu eḻum iyalpiṉar
the devotees who are fit to be worshipped by the celestials who do not wink.
amararkaḷ aṭiyiṇai toḻutu 1. eḻa
and the immortals to worship his two feet.
āramā veṅkaṇa araviṉar uṟaitarupati viḻimiḻalaiyē
Vīḻimiḻali is the place where Civaṉ who wears cobras with fiery eyes as garlands, dwells.
[[ārattaḻai arāp pūṇṭu (Kōviyār, 91);
āramā kiyataḻainta aravaip pūṇṭu (Do. pērāciriyar).]]
{3:85}__6+
{$} poṉ aṉa puritaru caṭaiyiṉar
Civaṉ has a interwining caṭi which is golden in colour.
poṭi aṇi vaṭiviṉar
smears on his body holy ash.
uṉṉiṉar viṉaiyavai kaḷaitalai maruviya oruvaṉār
the unequalled one who is in the habit of completly removing the Karmams which are like weeds, of those who meditate on him.
teṉṉeṉa icai mural caritaiyar
has the conduct of singing with the sound teṉṉeṉa
[[teṉṉeṉa: onomatopeia]]
tikaḻ taru mārpiṉil miṉ eṉa miḷirvatu ōr araviṉar pati viḻimiḻalaiyē
vīḻimiḻali is the place of Civaṉ who has a cobra which is gleaming like lightning in the shining chest.
{3:85}__7+
{$} arai akkiṉoṭu aravu (pūṇṭu)
having adorned his waist with a cobra and conch-beads.
aṇi tikaḻ oḷiyatu ōr āmai pūṇṭu
having adorned his chest with a beautiful and lustrous shell of a tortoise.
[[pūṇṭu should be linked with aravu also;
eṉpoṭu kom poṭu āmai ivai mārpu ilaṅka (campantar, Kōḷaṟu patikam, 2)]]
malitalai kalaṉ eṉa iṭu pali
holding the large skull as the bowls to receive the alms placed in it.
ikku uka ēkuvar
will go speaking such sweet words that even the juice of sugar-cane, appears insipid when compared to them.
kokkarai, kuḻal, muḻa viḻavoṭum icaivator caritaiyar
has a conduct which is combined with festivals in which Kokkari, flute and muḻavu are played.
mikkavar uṟaivatu viraikamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻalaiyē
vīḻimiḻali which has gardens which spreads their fragrance, is the place where Civaṉ who has no superior to him, dwells.
{3:85}__8+
{$} malai pātam ōr viral uṟa
fixing one toe of the foot in the mountin, Kayili.
aṭar pala talai neritara
broke the ten heads which were close to each other.
pūtamoṭu aṭiyavar puṉaikaḻal toḻutu eḻu pukaḻiṉar
has such a fame that his feet which are adorned with Kaḻal are prised by the pūtams and devotees.
ōtamoṭu oli tirai paṭukaṭal viṭam uṭai miṭaṟiṉar
has in his neck poison which rose in the ocean where noisy waves and tides occur.
vētamoṭu uṟutoḻil matiyavar pati viḻimiḻalaiyē
vīḻimiḻali is the place where wise people (brahmins) perform the duties prescribed for them in addition to chanting the vētams.
{3:85}__9+
{$} nīr aṇi malar micai uṟaipavaṉ
Piramaṉ who stays on the lotous which lends beauty to the water (nīr aṇi : a generic epithet)
niṟai kaṭal uṟu tuyil nāraṇaṉ eṉa ivar iruvarum
and nāraṇaṉ who sleeps in the ocean full of water; these two persons.
naṟumalar aṭi muṭi ōr uṇarviṉar celal uṟal arum uruviṉōṭu oḷi tikaḻ vīraṇar uṟaivatu
the place where the brave hero who has a lustrous body which cannot be reached by those two who serched the feet which are like fragrant flowers and the head, though they determined to find them.
veṟi kamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻali which has gardens from which fragrance spreads.
{3:85}__10+
{$} iṭu pali iṉitu eṉa iccaiyar
with an inclination that the alms given is good.
paṭutalai makiḻvatu ōr piccaiyar
receives with joy the alms in the dead skull.
perumaiyai iṟai poḻutu aṟivu eṉa uṇarvu ilar
though he is partial to begging alms his greatness could not be comprehended even for a moment; though one can say I shall measure his greatness.
moccaiya amaṇarum muṭaipaṭu tukilarum aḻivatu ōr viccaiyar uṟaivatu
the place where Civaṉ who has the magic power which cuse destruction of the amaṇar who have a body odour and buddhists wearing a cloth giving an offensive smell.
viraikamaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻalaiyē
is vīḻimiḻali which has gardens from which fragrance spreads.
{3:85}__11+
{$} uṉṉiya arumaṟai oliyiṉai muṟaimiku pāṭal cey iṉṉicaiyavar uṟai eḻil tikaḻ poḻil viḻimiḻalaiyai
about vīḻimiḻali which has beautiful and excellent gardens and where brahmins who, with their knowledge of music, convert into the proper way songs with the notes in chanting vētam which are considered to be revealed by Civaṉ.
maṉṉiya pukaliyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ vaṇ tamiḻ coṉṉavar tuyar ilar viyaṉ ulaku uṟukati peṟuvar
those who recited the tamiḻ verses contining many truths, and composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who is a resident of pukali which knows no destruction, will have no sufferings in the world and attin existence in the extensive world of bliss.

(1) ātippirāṉ vantu cērvu iṭam
the place where the chief who is the origin of all things comes and resides.
vāṉavar etticaiyum niṟaintu

valam ceytu

māmalar punti ceytu iṟaiñcip poḻi pūntarāy pōṟṟutum

{$} kāvi am karuṅkaṇṇiṉāḷ
the lady has black and beautiful eyes like the blue nelumbo flowers.
kaḷittoṇṭai vāykkatir mutta nalvau, nakaittūvi am peṭai aṉṉa naṭaic curimeṉkuḻalāḷ
has lips as red as the fruit of the common creeper of the hedges, good and white teeth like the bright pearls, a gait like the female swan which has down, and curly and soft tresses of hair.
tēviyum tirumēṉi ōrpākamāy
having that tevi on one half of the sacred person.

(1+2+3=6) the place which is sixth in order.

we will worship pūntarāy where brahmins who can be compared to the antaṇaṉ on the lotus flower, live
[[Among the twelve names for cikāḻi, pūntarāy is the sixth in order; this can be known from the following quotations:
piramaṉūr vēṇupuram pukali veṅkurup perunīrttōṇi, puram maṉṉu pūntarāy poṉṉañcirapuram pukali veṅkurup perunīrttōṇipuram maṉṉu pūntarāy poṉṉañcirapuram puṟavam caṇpai, araṉ maṉṉu taṇkāḻi koccaivayam uḷḷiṭṭu aṅku āti āya paramaṉūr paṉṉiraṇṭāy niṉṟa tirukkaḻumalam nām paravum ūrē (campantar, tiruccakkaramaṟṟu, 1);
piramapuram vēṇupuram pukaliperu veṅkuru nīrp poruvil tiruttōṇipuram pūntarāy cirapuram muṉ, varupuṟavam caṇpai nakar, vaḷarkāḻi koccaivayam, paravu tiruk kaḻumām paṉṉiraṇṭu tiruppeyarttāl (periyapurāṇam, tiruñāṉacampaṉtar purāṇam, 14)
oṉṟu iraṇṭu orumūṉṟoṭucērpati, tēviyum tirumēṉiyōr pākamāyccērpati: cērpati should be added with both in this way]]

{$} paiyarā alkul melliyal pañciṉ ēr aṭi vañcikoḷ nuṇṇiṭait taiyalāḷ oru pāluṭai em iṟaicārum iṭam
the place where our god who has on one side a young lady who has a small waist like the glabrous mahua, beautiful feet as soft as cotton, tender nature, and sides resembling the cobra with a hood.
cey elām kaḻunīr kamala(m) malart tēṟal ūṟaliṉ cēṟu ularāta naṟpūntarāy

we will worship pūntarāi where brahmins who do not know falsehood gather together.

{$} muḷḷināḷmukai
the fresh and just appeared bud of the lotus (as the lotus has thron in its stem it is named
muḷḷi:
that which has thorns;
muḷtāḷ tāmaraittuñci (tirumurukāṟṟuppaṭai, 73)
moṭṭu iyal kōṅkiṉ arumpu
the bud of the common caung which has put forth buds in an advanced stage about to blossom.
tēṉkoḷ kurumpai
immature nuts of cocoanut or palmyra which has sweetness,
mūvāmaruntu uḷiyaṉṟa paimpoṟkalacattu (1) iyal ottamūlai
the lady with breasts which are like golden pots into which the nectar which prevents ageing is filled up (mukai, arumpu, kurumpai, kalacam: all the four are compared to the breasts of Umai).
veḷḷi mālvarai aṉṉatu ōr mēṉiyiṉ mēviṉārpati
the city which is desired by the god who has that lady with the above-mentioned description in his body which resembles the big silver mountain (Kailācam) (as the body is smeared with bright and white sacred ash it appears like the silver mountain)
vīmaru taṇpoḻil puḷḷiṉam tuyil malkiya pūntarāy pōṟṟutum
we will worship that city of Pūntarāy where flocks of birds are in deep slumber without any anxiety, in the cool parks of flowers.

{$} paṇ iyaṉṟu eḻu meṉmoḻiyāḷ
the lady with soft words which come out of the mouth combining with melodies music.
pakar kōtai
who is extolled as a garland.
tikaḻ paintaḷir mēṉi ōr peṇ iyaṉṟa moympiṉ perumāṟku iṭam
the place of the great god who has on his shoulders an unequalled lady whose complexion is like the shining green leaves.

we will worship the city of Pūntarai where people of virtuous deeds reside.

{$} kār ulāviya vār kuḻalāḷ kayaṟkaṇṇiṉāḷ
Lady with long tresses of hair like the sable cloud and eyes like carp fish.
puyal kāl oḷi miṉ iṭai vār ulāviya meṉmulaiyāḷ malaimātu uṭaṉāy
joining with a daughter of the mountain, who has soft breasts on which is worn a bodice, a waist as slender as the bright lightning emitted by clouds.

the city which has twelve names which are in the proper order, where the Lord on whose head the water (of the Kaṅkai) is always moving, resides permanently.

we will worship that pūntarāi which has parks and surrounded by a wall of enclosure in which engines of warfare are always on the move.

niḻal veṇṇakaik kovvaivāy, koṭi ēr iṭaiyāḷ

the place where the god resides along with a lady who has a lean and minute waist and eyes resembling fet carnatic carp.

with the words, we will worship beautiful pūntarai where there are flower parks in which there is honey.

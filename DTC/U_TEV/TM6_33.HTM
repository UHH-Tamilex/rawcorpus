*1
porum kai matakari _urivaippōrvaiyāṉai,
pūvaṇamum valañcuḻiyum poruntiṉāṉai,
karumpu taru kaṭṭiyai, _iṉ_amirtai, tēṉai,
kāṇpu _ariya ceḻuñcuṭarai, kaṉakakkuṉṟai,
_iruṅ kaṉakamatil _ārūr mūlaṭṭāṉattu
_eḻuntaruḷi _iruntāṉai, _imaiyōr _ēttum
_aruntavaṉai, _araneṟiyil _appaṉtaṉṉai,---
_aṭaintu _aṭiyēṉ _aruviṉainōy _aṟutta_āṟē!

*2
kaṟpakamum _irucuṭarum _āyiṉāṉai,
kāḷatti kayilāyamalai _uḷāṉai,

vicayaṉukku vēṭuvaṉāy niṉṟāṉtaṉṉai,

poruntiya _emperumāṉai, poruntār cintai
_aṟputaṉai, _araneṟiyil _appaṉtaṉṉai,---
_aṭaintu _aṭiyēṉ _aruviṉainōy _aṟutta_āṟē!

*3

_aṟputaṉai, _araneṟiyil _appaṉtaṉṉai,---
_aṭaintu _aṭiyēṉ _aruviṉainōy _aṟutta_āṟē!

*4
nanti paṇi koṇṭu _aruḷum nampaṉtaṉṉai,

canti malar _iṭṭu _aṇintu vāṉōr _ēttum
tattuvaṉai, cakkaram māṟku _īntāṉtaṉṉai,
_intu nuḻai poḻil _ārūr mūlaṭṭāṉam
_iṭamkoṇṭa perumāṉai, _imaiyōr pōṟṟum
_antaṇaṉai, _araneṟiyil _appaṉtaṉṉai,---
_aṭaintu _aṭiyēṉ _aruviṉainōy _aṟuttavāṟē!

*5
cuṭarp pavaḷattirumēṉi veṇnīṟṟāṉai,

_aṭaintu _aṭiyēṉ _aruviṉainōy _aṟutta_āṟē!

*6

*8

• patikam: [3:19]
  • talam: tiru _amparp peruntirukkōyil
  • paṇ: kāntārap pañcamam
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.168

*1
_eritara _aṉal kiyil _ēnti, _elliyil,
nari tiri kāṉ_iṭi, naṭṭam_āṭuvar---
_aricil _am poru puṉal _ampar mā nakar,
kuricilceṅkaṇṇavaṉ kōyil cērvarē.

*2
miya kaṇ malimakaḷ pākam_āy, _iruḷ,
kiyatu _ōr kaṉal-_eri kaṉal _āṭuvar---
_aiya naṉ poru puṉal _ampar, cempiyar
ceyyakaṇ_iṟi ceyta kōyil cērvarē.

*3
maṟi puṉi pāṭalar, cuṭar ki malka, _ōr
piṟi puṉi caṭimuṭi peyara, _āṭuvar---
_aṟi puṉal niṟi vayal _ampar mā nakar,
_iṟi puṉi _eḻil vaḷar _iṭam_atu _eṉparē.

*4
_iravu malku _iḷamati cūṭi, _īṭu _uyar
parava malku _arumaṟi pāṭi, _āṭuvar---
_aravamōṭu _uyar cemmal _ampar, kompu _alar
maravam malku _eḻil nakar, maruvi vāḻvarē.

*5
caṅku _aṇi kuḻiyiṉar, cāmam pāṭuvar,
veṅkaṉal kaṉaltara vīci _āṭuvar---
_aṅku _aṇi viḻavu _amar _ampar mā nakar,
ceṅkaṇnal_iṟi ceyta kōyil cērvarē.

*6
kaḻal vaḷar kāliṉar, cuṭar ki malka, _ōr
cuḻal vaḷar kuḷirpuṉal cūṭi, _āṭuvar---
_aḻal vaḷar maṟiyavar _ampar, pimpoḻil
niḻal vaḷar neṭu nakar, _iṭam_atu _eṉparē.

*7
_ikal _uṟu cuṭar_eri _ilaṅka vīciyē,
pakal _iṭam pali koḷap pāṭi _āṭuvar---
_akaliṭam mali pukaḻ _ampar, vampu _aviḻ
pukal_iṭam neṭu nakar pukuvarpōlumē.

*8
_eri _aṉa maṇi muṭi _ilaṅkikkōṉtaṉa
kari _aṉa taṭakkikaḷ _aṭartta kāliṉar,
_ariyavar---vaḷa nakar_ampar _iṉpoṭu
puriyavar, pirivu _ilāp pūtam cūḻavē.

*9
veṟi kiḷar malarmiciyavaṉum, ven toḻil
poṟi kiḷar _aravu_aṇip pulku celvaṉum,
_aṟikila _ariyavar---_ampar, cempiyar
ceṟi kaḻal _iṟi ceyta kōyil cērvarē.

*10
vaḻi tali, paṟi tali, _avarkaḷ kaṭṭiya
moḻitalip payaṉ _eṉa moḻiyal! vammiṉō!
_aḻitu _ali poru puṉal _ampar mā nakar,
_uḻitali _oḻintu _uḷar, _umiyum tāmumē.

*11
_aḻakari, _aṭikaḷi, _ampar mēviya
niḻal tikaḻ caṭimuṭi nīlakaṇṭari,
_umiḻ tiri _ulakiṉil _ōtuvīr! koṇmiṉ---
tamiḻ keḻu virakiṉaṉ tamiḻceymāliyē!

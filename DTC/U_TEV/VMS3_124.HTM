viṇ amar paim poḻil veḷḷaṭai mēviya, peṇ amar mēṉi em piññakaṉār
our god, the destroyer, who has a lady on his form and who is in Veḷḷati surrounded by green gardens that seem to touch the sky.
cuṇṇa veṇṇīṟu aṇi mārpil tōl puṉaintu
having adorned on his chest on which is smeared fine powder of white sacred ash, a skin.
eṇ arum pal kaṇam ētta niṉṟu āṭuvar
dances standing to be prised by many groups of countless numbers.
[[pal kaṇam:
patiṉeṭṭut tēvakaṇam, pūtakaṇam, pēykkaṇam, muṉivar kaṇam, uruttira pal kaṇam.]]
{3:124}__2+
{$}
malku virai kamaḻ taṇ poḻil veḷḷaṭai mēviya arai malku vāḷ aravu āṭṭu ukantīrē
you who rejoice in making the killing cobra worn on the waist and thriving to dance (spreading its hood) and who reside eagerly in Veḷḷaṭi which has cool gardens from which much fragrance spreads.
tirai pulku kaṅkai tikaḻ caṭai vaittu
having placed Kaṅki of waves on the bright matted locks of hir.
varai makaḷōṭu uṭaṉ āṭutir
you dance with the dughter of the mountin. (you sport with the dugther of the mountin).
{3:124}__3+
{$}
veḷḷaṭai mēviya, caṭai amar veṇpiṟaic caṅkaraṉīrē
oh! Caṅkara! who resides in veḷḷaṭi with eagerness and has a white escent on the matted locks of hir!
aṭaiyalar tol nakar mūṉṟu erittu
Having burnt the three ancient cities of the enemies.
aṉṉa naṭai maṭa maṅkai ōr pākam nayantu
and having desired as a half a young lady whose git can be compared to that of the swan.
viṭai ukantu ēṟutir
you ride upon a bull with gladness
{3:124}__4+
{$}
viḷaṅkiya taṇ poḻil veḷḷaṭai mēviya (emperumāṉē)
our Lord who is in veḷḷaṭi which has prospering cool gardens!
iḷam piṟai cērcaṭai em perumāṉē
our Lord in whose matted locks of hir there is the crescent moon.
vaḷam kiḷar kaṅkai maṭavaralōṭu kāṭu araṅku kaḷam ākkappaṭa āṭutir
you dance using the cremation ground as a stage, along with the lady, Kaṇki, of increasing beauty.
{3:124}__5+
{$}
viritaru paimpoḻil veḷḷaṭai mēviya (entai pirāṉē)
our Lord and father who is in veḷḷaṭi which has green and entensive gardens!
eri maḻuvāṭpaṭai entai pirāṉē
our Lord and father who has a burning battle-axe as a weapon!
curi kuḻal nalla tuṭiyiṭaiyōṭu pori pulku kāṭṭiṭaip poṅku āṭutir
you dance with enthusiasm in the cremation ground where the trees and other vegetation have become scorched by excessive heat and are in abundance, with a lady who has curly hirs and a waist comparable to tuṭi (uṭukkai)
{3:124}__6+
{$}
tēṉ malar mēviya taṇ poḻil veḷḷaṭai mēviya āviṉil aintu koṇṭu āṭku ukantīrē
you who are pleased to be bathed in the five products of the cow and are in veḷḷaṭi which has cool gardens in which there are flowers with honey.
kāvi amkaṇ maṭavāḷoṭum kāṭṭiṭait tī akal ēnti niṉṟu āṭutir
you dance holding a hollow earthern lamp with fire.

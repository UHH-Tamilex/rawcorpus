*1
taṭa nilaviya mali niṟuvi, _or taḻal _umiḻtaru paṭa_aravukoṭu,
_aṭal _acuraroṭu _amararkaḷ, _alikaṭal kaṭivuḻi _eḻum miku ciṉa
viṭam _aṭitarum miṭaṟu _uṭiyavaṉ; viṭimici varumavaṉ; _uṟi pati---
tiṭam malitaru maṟi muṟi _uṇar maṟiyavar niṟi tiru miḻaliyē.

*2
tariyoṭu tivitalam nalitaru taku tiṟal _uṟu calataraṉatu
vari _aṉa tali viciyoṭu varu tikiriyi _ari peṟa _aruḷiṉaṉ;
_uri malitaru curanati, mati, poti caṭiyavaṉ; _uṟi pati---miku
tiri mali kaṭal maṇal _aṇitaru peṟu tiṭar vaḷar tiru miḻaliyē.

*3
malimakaḷtaṉi _ikaḻvu_atu ceyta mati _aṟu ciṟumaṉavaṉatu _uyar
taliyiṉoṭu _aḻal_uruvaṉa karam _aṟa muṉivu ceytavaṉ _uṟi pati---
kali nilaviya pulavarkaḷ _iṭar kaḷitaru koṭi payilpavar miku,
cili mali matil puṭi taḻuviya, tikaḻ poḻil vaḷar, tiru miḻaliyē.

*4
maruvalar puram _eriyiṉil maṭitara _oru kaṇi cela niṟuviya
peru valiyiṉaṉ, nalam malitaru karaṉ, _uram miku piṇam _amar vaṉa
_iruḷ_iṭi _aṭi _uṟavoṭu naṭa vici _uṟu paraṉ, _iṉitu _uṟi pati---
teruviṉil varu peru viḻavu _oli malitara vaḷar tiru miḻaliyē.

*5
_aṇi peṟu vaṭamaraniḻaliṉil, _amarvoṭum _aṭi_iṇi _iruvarkaḷ
paṇitara, _aṟaneṟi maṟiyoṭum _aruḷiya paraṉ _uṟivu_iṭam---_oḷi
maṇi poruvu _aru marakatanilam mali puṉal _aṇitaru vayal _aṇi,
tiṇi poḻil taru maṇam matu nukar _aṟupatam mural, tiru miḻaliyē.

*6
vaci _aṟu vali vaṉacara_uru_atukoṭu, niṉivu _arutavam muyal
viciyaṉa tiṟal malimakaḷ _aṟivu_uṟu tiṟal _amar miṭalkoṭu ceytu,
_acivu _ila paṭi _aruḷ puritarumavaṉ _uṟi pati_atu---mikutaru
ticiyiṉil malar kulaviya ceṟi poḻil malitaru tiru miḻaliyē.

*7
nalam malitaru maṟimoḻiyoṭu, nati_uṟupuṉal, puki, _oḷi mutal,
malar_avikoṭu, vaḻipaṭu tiṟal maṟiyavaṉ _uyir_atu koḷa varu
calam malitaru maṟalitaṉ _uyirkeṭa, _uticeyta _araṉ _uṟi pati---
tilakam _itu! _eṉa _ulakukaḷ pukaḻtaru, poḻil _aṇi, tiru miḻaliyē.

*8
_araṉ _uṟitaru tayiliyi nilikulivu_atu ceyta tacamukaṉatu
karam_irupatum neritara viral niṟuviya kaḻal_aṭi _uṭiyavaṉ;
varalmuṟi _ulaku_avi taru, malar vaḷar, maṟiyavaṉ vaḻi vaḻuviya
ciram_atukoṭu pali tiritaru civaṉ; _uṟi pati---tiru miḻaliyē.

*9
_ayaṉoṭum _eḻil _amar malarmakaḷ makiḻ kaṇaṉ, _aḷaviṭal _oḻiya, _or
payam _uṟu vaki taḻal nikaḻvatu _or paṭi _uru_atu vara, varalmuṟi,
caya caya! _eṉa miku tuticeya, veḷi _uruviya_avaṉ _uṟi pati---
ceyam nilaviya matil mati_atu tavaḻtara _uyar tiru miḻaliyē.

*10
_ikaḻ _uruvoṭu paṟi talikoṭum _iḻi toḻil mali camaṇvirakiṉar,
tikaḻ tuvar_uṭi _uṭal potipavar, keṭa, _aṭiyavar mika _aruḷiya
pukaḻ _uṭi _iṟi _uṟi pati---puṉal _aṇi kaṭalpuṭi taḻuviya puvi
tikaḻ curartaru nikar koṭiyiṉar ceṟivoṭu tikaḻ tiru miḻaliyē.

*11
ciṉam mali kari _uriceyta civaṉ _uṟitaru tiru miḻaliyi, miku
taṉa maṉar, cirapuranakar _iṟi---tamiḻvirakaṉatu---_uri_orupatum
maṉa makiḻvoṭu payilpavar, _eḻil malarmakaḷ, kalimakaḷ, cayamakaḷ,
_iṉam mali pukaḻmakaḷ, _icitara, _iru nilaṉ_iṭi _iṉitu _amarvarē.

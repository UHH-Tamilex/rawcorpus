{2:54}__1+
{$} uru ārnta melliyal ōr pākam uṭaiyīr
you have on one half a lady of tender nature who has a brilliant form.
aṭaivōrkkuk karu ārnta vāṉ ulakam kāṭṭik koṭuttal karuttāṉīr
you have the idea of showing and bestowing on those who approach you, the heaven which has the seed of your grace.
poru ārnta teṇkaṭal oṇcaṅkam tiḷaikkum pūmpukalit tiruvārnta kōyilē kōyilākattikaḻntīrē
you were eminent in thinking the temple having wealth in beautiful pukali, where the bright conches moving from the clear sea which is always dashing aginst the shore, are always present, as the temple desired by you.
{2:54}__2+
{$} nīr ārnta ceñcaṭaiyīr
you have a red caṭi which is full of water.
nirai ār kaḻal cēr pātattīr
you have in your feet anklets adorned in a row.
ūr ārnta cilpaliyīr
you have some alms collected in many places.
uḻaimāṉ uri tōl āṭaiyīr
you have dressed yourself with the skin of a spotted deer flayed from its body.
pōr ārnta teṇtirai ceṉṟu aṇaiyum kāṉal pūmpukalic cīr ārnta kōyilē kōyilākac cērntīrē
you dwelt in the temple of great fame in beautiful pukali which has sea-shore gardens which are reached by the clear waves which are dashing aginst the shore, as the temple desired by you.
[[When the waves dash aginst the sea-shore it appears as if the water is fighting with the land.]]
{2:54}__3+
{$} aḻimalku pūmpuṉalum aravum caṭaimēl aṭaivu eyta
the beautiful water (of kanki) which has the capacity of complete destruction and cobra are on the caṭi in an order.
moḻimalku mā maṟaiyīr
you are always chanting the great maṟi (vētam)
kaṟai ār kaṇṭattu eṇtōḷīr
you have eight arms and a black neck.
poḻil malku vaṇṭiṉaṅkaḷ aṟaiyum kāṉal pūmpukali eḻil malku kōyilē kōyilāka iruntīrē
you stayed in the temple of increasing beauty in pleasant pukali which has sea-shore gardens where the swarms of bees are flourishing in the garden, as the temple desired by you.
{2:54}__4+
{$} kaiyil ārnta veṇ maḻu oṉṟu uṭaiyīr
you have in your hand a white-hot iron.
[[VMS: I think the word should be kayil which means joint; it will be in keeping with the other three words in the rhyme in the next three lines.]]
kaṭiya kariyiṉ tōl mayil ārnta cāyal maṭa maṅkai veruva mey pōrttīr
you covered your body with the skin of a fearful elephant to make the young lady having the tenderness of a peacock, to be frightened.
payil ārnta vētiyarkaḷ patiyāy viḷaṅkum paimpukali eyil ārnta kōyilē kōyilāka icaintīrē
you agreed to reside in the temple having a wall of enclosure in fertile Pukali which is shining as the place of vētiyar (brahmins) who always trin themselves in Vētam and other arts, as the temple desired by you.
{2:54}__5+
{$} nā ārnta pāṭalīr
you are singing the vētam in your mouth
āṭal aravam araikku ārttīr
you tied round your waist a dancing cobra.
pā ārnta pal poruḷiṉ payaṉkaḷ āṉīr
you were the meaning and the fruit of many songs.
pūvārnta poykaikaḷum vayalum cūḻnta poḻiṟ pukali ayaṉ pēṇum tē ārnta kōyilē kōyilākat tikaḻntīrē
you were brilliant in the temple full of divinity which was worshipped by Ayaṉ in pukali having gardens and surrounded by fields and natural tanks where lotus flowers are growing abundantly.
{2:54}__6+
{$} maṇ ārnta maṇ muḻavam tatumpa
when the kuṭamuḻā is played on which there is a black paste.
[[maṇ-mārccaṉai: black paste smeared on the head of a drum to increase its resonance; paste smeared on the head of a drum for toning it.]]
[[maṇ muḻavam muḻa made of clay; it is called ghatam; in Tamiḻ it is called kuṭamuḻā.]]
malaiyāṉ makaḷ eṉṉum peṇṇārnta maṉam makiḻap pēṇi erikoṇṭu āṭiṉīr
you danced holding fire desiring it, to make the body of your consort who is the dughter of the mountin swell with joy.
viṇ ārnta matiyam miṭai māṭattu ārum viyaṉ pukalik kaṇ ārnta kōyilē kōyilākak kalantīrē
you came into being in the temple in the broad pukali feasting to the eyes where the full moon in the sky rests in the storeys that are close to each other, as the temple desired by you.
{2:54}__7+
{$} kaḷipulku val avuṇar ūr mūṉṟu eriyak kaṇai toṭṭīr
you shot an arrow to burn the three cities of the strong avuṇar (acurar) who were inebriated with power.
aḷi pulku pūmuṭiyīr
you have a head adorned with flowers on which bees swarm.
amarar ētta aruḷ ceytīr
you bestowed your grace on tēvar when they worshipped you
[[amarar is used in the sense of tevar who have a longer lease of life than human beings.]]
teḷi pulku tēṉiṉamum malaruḷ viraicēr tiṇ pukali oḷi pulku kōyilē kōyilāka ukantīrē
you rejoiced to dwell in the temple of great brilliance in strong pukali where the bees of bright colour collect the fragrance which is inside the flowers.
[[tēṉiṉamum: um: expletive.]]
{2:54}__8+
{$} parantu ōṅkum pal pukaḻ cēr arakkar kōṉai varaik kīḻ iṭṭu
having placed the king of arakkar who had many kinds of fame which spread for and wide and increased, under the mountin (Kailācam)
uram tōṉṟum pāṭal kēṭṭu ukavai aḷittīr
you granted boons out of joy, on hearing the songs which came out of his knowledge (of the vētam)
[[It is sid that Civaṉ's anger subsided on hearing Irāvaṇaṉ chanting the musical cāma vētam. Civaṉ granted Irāvaṇaṉ a sword and a lease of long life;]]
[[PP: eṟiyumākaṭal ilaṅkaiyar kōṉait tulaṅka mālvaraikkīḻ aṭarttiṭṭuk kuṟikoḷ pāṭaliṉ iṉṉicai kēṭṭuk kōlavāḷoṭu nāḷatu koṭutta ceṟivu kaṇṭu (cuntarar, tiruppuṉkūr, 9)]]
ukavātār puram tōṉṟum mummatilum eriyac ceṟṟīr
you destroyed the three fortifications which appeared like cities belonging to your enemies.
pūmpukali varam tōṉṟum kōyilē kōyilāka makiḻntīr
you rejoiced to dwell in the eminent temple in beautiful pukali as the temple desired by you.
{2:54}__9+
{$} calantāṅku tāmaraimēl ayaṉum taraṇi aḷantāṉum
Ayaṉ who is seated on the lotus which is supported by water, and (Māl) who measured the world.
kalantu ōṅki vantu iḻintum kāṇāvaṇṇam kaṉal āṉīr
you became a column of fire as not to be seen (by both of them) though they joined and flew high and went descending down the earth.
pulam tāṅki
Having borne in mind knowledge of reality.
aimpulaṉum ceṟṟār vāḻum pūmpukali nalam tāṅkum kōyilē kōyilāka nayantīrē
you desired the temple which has many architectural beauties in beautiful pukali where wise men who have annihilated the five senses of the body, as the temple suitable to you.
{2:54}__10+
{$} neṭitu āyavaṉ camaṇum niṟaivu oṉṟu illāc cākkiyarum
the tall and strong camaṇar and cākkiyar who do not have fullness of knowledge.
kaṭitu āya kaṭṭuraiyāl kaḻaṟa mēl ōr poruḷāṉīr
you were above the abuse of the exceedingly fabricated falsehood (of both of them)
poṭi ārum mēṉiyiṉīr
you have a body on which sacred ash is smeared.
pukali maṟaiyōr purintu ētta vaṭivu ārum kōyilē kōyilāka makiḻntīrē
you dwelt happily in the well-shaped temple in pukali where brahmins prised you with love, as the temple desired by you.
{2:54}__11+
{$} oppu ariya pūmpukali ōṅku kōyil mēyāṉai
on the Lord who gladly resides in the flourishing temple in beautiful pukali which has no equal.
appariciṟ patiyāṉa aṇi koḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ wearing ornaments, who is the chief of such a high honour which cannot be explined in words.
[[Variant reading: appariciṟpatiyāṉai]]
ceppa ariya taṇ tamiḻāl terinta pāṭal ivai vallār
those who are capable of reciting the choice verses composed in pleasant tamiḻ which cannot be recited by those who do not have the grace of the Lord.
epparical iṭar nīṅki imaiyōr ulakattu iruppārē
in whatever they may get sufferings they will be free from them and enjoy the pleasures in the paradise of the tēvar.

{4:25}__1+
{$} veṇṇilā matiyam taṉṉai viricaṭai mēva vaittu
placing the crescent which sheds a white light to be united with the loosened caṭi.
uḷnilap pukuntu niṉṟu
staying having entered into the mind to be permanent
[[nilā is the abbreviation of nilāva]]
aṅku uṇarviṉukku uṇarak kūṟi
revealing himself to the soul which is intelligence itself.
(1) viṇṇil ār mīyaccūrār
Civaṉ is in Mīyaccūr which reaches the sky by its fame.
vēṇṭuvārkkē aṇṇiyār
is near at hand to those who cherish him with love.
vēṇṭātārkkē peritum cēyār atikai vīraṭṭaṉār
the god, Civaṉ in Atiki Vīraṭṭam is very far away only to people who do not cherish him with love.
[[Though there is a Variant reading (vēṇṭuvārkkē), it does not make any sense; vēṇṭātārkkē is a better reading]]
[[Variant reading: (1) viṇṇilā mīyac cūrār (2) vēṇṭilārkkē]]
{4:25}__2+
{$} pāṭiṉār maṟaikaḷ nāṉkum
Civaṉ sang all the four Vētam-s with music.
pāy iruḷ pukuntu eṉ uḷḷam kūṭiṉār
joined in my mind entering in the night of spreading darkness.
kūṭal ālavāyilār
is in the temple Ālavāy in Kūṭal
[[Kūṭal -- Maturi]]
nalla koṉṟai cūṭiṉār
wore on his head koṉṟi of good fragrance.
cūṭalmēvi
desiring wearing that flower.
cūḻ cuṭarc cuṭalai veṇṇīṟu āṭiṉār āṭalmēvi atikai vīraṭṭaṉār
Civaṉ in Atiki Vīraṭṭam performing dance in the cremation-ground which has brightness all round and bathed in the white sacred ash.
{4:25}__3+
{$}
(People of this world?)
(1) ūṉaiyē kaḻikka vēṇṭil uḷḷattuḷḷē uṇarmiṉkaḷ tēṉaiya malarkaḷ koṇṭu
contemplate in your mind about Civaṉ if you want to be rid of the sufferings of birth, worshipping with beautiful flowers having honey.
cintaiyuḷ cintikkiṉṟa ēṉaiya palavum āki
being many other things that you think about in your mind.
imaiyavar ētta niṉṟu
being eternal to be prised by the celestials who do not wink.
āṉaiyiṉ urivai pōrttār atikai vīraṭṭaṉār (ē)
the god in Atiki Vīraṭṭam covered himself with an elephant's skin.
[[Variant reading: (1) ūṉaimey kaḻikka vēṇṭi]]
{4:25}__4+
{$} turutti ām kurampaitaṉṉil
in the body which is like the bellows.
toṇṇūṟṟu (aṅku) aṟuvar niṉṟu
the ninety six realities which are staying there.
virutti (tāṉ) taruka eṉṟu vētaṉai palavum ceyya
when they give pin, saying give us the means of livehood.
varutti (āl) vallavāṟu vantu vantu aṭaiya niṉṟa aruttiyārkku aṉpar (pōlum) atikai vīraṭṭaṉār (ē)
Civaṉ in Atiki Vīraṭṭam bestows love on people who come to him with great avidity according to their capability making strenous efforts, very often.
[[āl expletive]]
{4:25}__5+
{$} atikai vīraṭṭaṉār (ē)
Civaṉ in Atiki Vīraṭṭam
pattiyāl ētti niṉṟu paṇipavar neñcattu uḷḷār
dwells in the hearts of those who pay homage to and prise him, always with devotion.
tutti aintalaiya nākam cūḻcaṭaimuṭimēl vaittu
placing a five headed cobra which has spots on them on the caṭi coiled into a crown which encircles and heads.
uttaramalaiyar pāvai umaiyavaḷ naṭuṅka aṉṟu attiyiṉ urivai pōrttār atikai vīraṭṭaṉār (ē)
Atiki Vīraṭṭaṉār covered himself with an elephant's skin in that distant past, when Umiyavaḷ the dughter of mountin in the North, trembled with fear.
{4:25}__6+
{$} kālattālē
early in life.
varimuṭi pāṭi eṉṟum vallavāṟu kariurimūṭavalla kaṭavuḷai aṭaintum; neñcē
my mind! we approached Civaṉ always to the best of our ability always singing vari and muri, the god who is able to cover himself an elephant skin.
[[vari and muri are secondary melody-types]]
[[aṭaintum=aṭaintōm]]
[[yāṉaiyiṉ tōl piṟar uṭampiṟpaṭṭāl kollumeṉṟu uṇarka. iṉi, vāraṇattu uripōl tappāmaṟkollum valaiyeṉṟum ām (cīvakacintamaṇi 2787, nacciṉāṟkkiṉiyar's commentary)]]
atikai vīraṭṭaṉār
Civaṉ in Atiki Vīraṭṭam.
curipuri virikuḻalāḷ tuṭi iṭaip paravai alkul arivai ōr pākar (pōlum)
has a half a lady having twisted curly and loosened tresses, a waist like the drum tuṭi and broad sides.
{4:25}__7+
{$} neñcē
my mind!
nimalaṉai niṉaicey nittamāka
dily think of the spotless god.
pāti ām umaitaṉṉōṭum pākamāy niṉṟa entai
our father who was half with Umi who is also a half.
cōtiyāy
being the light divine.
cuṭarviḷakkāy
being the lamp that always burns
cuṇṇaveṇṇīṟu(atu)āṭi
smearing himself with the fine powder of sacred ash.
ātiyum īṟum āṉār atikai vīraṭṭaṉār (ē)
the god in Atiki Vīraṭṭam is the source and end of all things.
{4:25}__8+
{$} elliyum pakalum ellām tuñcuvēṟku oruvar vantu (1) pulliya maṉattu kōyil pukkaṉar
the unequalled Civaṉ came to me and entered into my low mind, when I was sleeping always, both at night and day.
kāmaṉ eṉṉum villi aiṅkaṇaiyiṉāṉai ventu uka nōkkiyiṭṭār
fixed his gaze on Kāmaṉ who has a bow of sugarcane and five arrows of flowers are :
[[māmpū, acōkappū, tāmaraippū, mullaippū, tēmpāy tarukuvaḷaic cevvippū mēmpaṭṭa, maintupūṇmārpa mataṉaṉ kaṇaiyāṉa, aintu pū ākum avai (old verse quoted in the commentary of takkayākapparani, 71)]]
alli ām paḻaṉavēli atikaivīraṭṭaṉār
the Lord in Atiki Vīraṭṭam which is surrounded by fields in which water-lily flowers grow.
[[Variant reading: (1) pulliyaṉmaṉattuk]]
{4:25}__9+
{$} veṉṟa aimpulaṉkaḷ tammai vilakkutaṟku uriyīr ellām
all of you who have qualitied yourselves to prevent the five sense perceptions which you have subdued, from inflicting sufferings on you.
oṉṟavē uṇartir ākil ōṅkārattu oruvaṉ ākum
if you realize him, by concentration he will appear before you in omkāram.
naṉṟavaṉ
Civaṉ is all good.
atikai vīraṭṭaṉār (ē) nāraṇaṉum nāṉmukaṉ (um) nāṭik (1) kāṇkuṟṟa aṉṟu avarkku ariyar (pōlum)
the god Atiki Vīraṭṭaṉār was difficult to be seen by Nāraṇaṉ and Piramaṉ of four faces, on that day when they tried to see him by their efforts.
[[Variant reading: (1) kāṇātu aṉṟavarkku ariyar]]
{4:25}__10+
{$} atikai vīraṭṭaṉār (ē)
Civaṉ in Atiki Vīraṭṭam
taṭakkaiyāl eṭuttu vaittut taṭavarai kuluṅka ārttuk kiṭakkaiyāl
as (Irāvaṇaṉ) who lifted the big mountin by this big hand and made it shake, was lying on the ground with roaring.
iṭarkaḷ ōṅka
when his sufferings were increasing
kiḷarmaṇi muṭikaḷ cāya muṭakkiṉār (1) tiruviralāl
bent with his toe to make the crowns set with shining gems to incline.
muruku amar kōtai pākattu aṭakkiṉār
restrined in one half a lady who had tresses of fragrance.
[[Variant reading: (1) tiruviraltāṉ]]

{$}
pūvaṇattavaṉ
Civaṉ has the beauty of a flower.
puṇṇiyaṉ
is the embodiment of all virtues.
naṇṇi aṅku aṭiyārkaḷai āvaṇattu uṭaiyāṉ
admits by a bond as protégés devotees by voluntarily going to them.
[[āvaṇam: bond;
eṉ tāyoṭu eṉ appaṉ ēḻ ēḻ piṟaviyum aṉṟē civaṉukku eḻutiya āvaṇam (tirumantiram, 1876, Kāci muṭṭ edition);
entai tantai tantai tam mūttappaṉ ēḻ paṭi kāl toṭaṅki, vantu vaḻi vaḻi āṭ ceykiṉṟōm (periyāḻvār tirumoḻi, tiruppallāṅtu, 6)]]
tīvaṇam mey tiru nīṟu pūci
smearing holy ash on his body which is red like fire.
ōr kōvaṇattu uṭaiyāṉ kuṭamūkkilē
Kutamūkkil is the shrine of Civaṉ who has a single loin-cloth as his dress.

{$}
vērttu āṭum kāḷitaṉ vicai tīrka eṉṟu kūttāṭi(y) uṟaiyum kuṭa mūkkil
Civaṉ who danced to gin victory over Kāḷi who danced with anger, so that her speed might slow down, dwells in Kutamūkkil,
[[veṉṟi miku tārukaṉatu āruyir maṭaṅkak, kaṉṟi varu kōpa miku kāḷikatam ōva, niṉṟu naṭamāṭi (campantar, tiruviyāṟu - tiruvirākam 5);
curapurattiṉait tuyar ceyta tārukaṉ tuñca veñciṉak kāḷiyaittarum, ciraputtuḷāṉ (campantar, piramāpuram, īraṭivippu 7)]]
pūttu āṭi kaḻiyātu
having been born in this world and wasting your life without any good, and dying
pūmiyīr
People of this world!
tīttu āṭittiṟam cintaiyuḷ vaimmiṉō
keep in your minds the nature of Civaṉ who dances after burning everything at the end of the world.
[[tīttāṭit tiṟam: the doubling of t is for rhyme; it should ordinarily be tīttāṭi tiṟam; kūttāṭi y uṟaiyum;
y is an augmentation for metre.]]
{5:22}__3+
{$}
naṅkaiyāḷ umaiyāḷ uṟai nātaṉār
the chief on whose half Umayaḷ, a lady of distinction dwells.
aṅkaiyāḷoṭu aṟupatam tāḻ caṭaik kaṅkaiyāḷ avaḷ kaṉṉi eṉappaṭum koṅkaiyāḷ uṟaiyum kuṭamūkkilē
the Kaṅkiyāḷ and who dwells in the caṭi where bees of six legs settle, along with a lady of beautiful hand and a lady by name Kaṉṉi prominent by her breasts, stay in Kuṭamūkkil.
{5:22}__4+
{$}
ōtā nāvaṉ tiṟattai uraittirēl
if you speak about the acts of Civaṉ who knew everything without undergoing learning.
ētāṉum iṉitu ākum
however little it may be it will be sweet to hear.
[[ētāṉum may mean also whether it is not a good act.]]
cētā ēṟu uṭaiyāṉ amarnta iṭam
the place where Civaṉ who has a bull from the species of red cows, dwells.
kōtā viri uṟaiyum kuṭamūkkil
is Kutamūkkil where Kōtāviri resides.
{5:22}__5+
{$}
nal neñcē
my good mind!
nakka araiyaṉai vakkarai uṟaivāṉai
Civaṉ who is naked and who dwells in vakkari.
akku araiyōṭu aravu arai ārttavaṉ
one who tied in his waist a cobra in addition to chank beads.
nī nāḷ toṟum vaṇaṅku
you dily bow to
kokkarai uṭaiyāṉ kuṭamūkkil
Kuṭamūkkil of Civaṉ who has the musical instrument of Kokkari.
{5:22}__6+
{$}
tuṟavi neñciṉar ākiya toṇṭarkāḷ
Devotees who have a mind renouncing all attachments!
pittarāy
being crazy after god.
maṟavaṉāy
assuming the form of a warrior,
pārttaṉ mēl kaṇai toṭṭa em kuṟavaṉār uṟaiyum kuṭamūkkil piṟavi nīṅkap pitaṟṟumiṉ
prattle the name of Kuṭamukkil where Civaṉ who assumed the form of a kuṟavaṉ and discharged arrows on pārttaṉ (aruccuṉaṉ), to save you from being born agin.
{5:22}__7+
{$}
paṇṭai valviṉai paṟṟu aṟa vēṇṭuvīr
Those who wish that the accumulated irresistible Karmams should completely leave you!
viṇṭavar puram mūṉṟu oru māttiraik koṇṭavaṉ uṟaiyum kuṭamūkkilē toṇṭar ākit toḻutu
becoming devotees and worshipping with joined hands, bow before Kuṭamūkkil where Civaṉ saw to it that fire consumed the three cities of enemies within the short time taken to wink one's eyes.
{5:22}__8+
{$}
kāmiyam ceytu kālam kaḻiyātē
without wasting your precious life doing all that you wish to do, deriving their fruits.
ōmiyam ceytu
mintining the sacred fire outwardly.
cāmiyōṭu caraccuvatiyavaḷ kōmiyum(m) uṟaiyum kuṭamūkkil aṅku uḷḷattu uṇarmiṉō
realise inwardly in your heart Kuṭamūkkil where Yamuṉi of blue colour, Caracuvati and Kōtāvari dwell;
[[PP: tāvi mutaṟ kāviri nal yamuṉai kaṅkai caracuvati poṟṟāmarai puṭkaraṇi cūḻnta teṇṇīrk, kōviyoṭu kumari varu tīrttam cūḻnta kuṭantai
(appar, Kuṭantikkiḻkkōṭṭam, taṅtakam, 10).]]
{5:22}__9+
{$}
ciramam ceytu civaṉukkup pattarāy
becoming devotees of Civaṉ by practising difficult processes of Yōkam.
piramaṉ māloṭu maṟṟu oḻintārkku elām kuravaṉār uṟaiyum kuṭamūkkilē paramaṉaip pala nāḷum payiṟṟu miṉ
trin yourselves to utter the names of the supreme god for many days in Kuṭamūkkil where the preceptor for Piramaṉ, Māl and others resides.

aṉṟu (tāṉ) arakkaṉ kayilāyattaic ceṉṟu (tāṉ) eṭukka umai añcalum
when Umi was frightened when the arakkaṉ went near the Kayilāyam and lifted it in the past.
naṉṟu (tāṉ) nakku
Civaṉ with a big lugh.
nal viral ūṉṟi

{7:32}__1+
{$}
kōṭik kuḻanīr
Lord in Koṭikkuḻakar!
aṭikēḷ
O Deity!
kaṭitāyk kaṭaṟkāṟṟu vantu eṟṟak karaimēl umakku ār tuṇaiyāka iruntīrē
with whose companionship were you here on the shore when the sea-breeze strikes you with great speed?
koṭiyēṉ kaṇkaḷ kaṇṭaṉa
my cruel eyes witnessed your dwelling here lonely.
kuṭitāṉ ayalē iruntāl kuṟṟam āmō
will there be blemish if you dwell in some other place.
{7:32}__2+
{$}
kuṉṟāp poḻil cūḻtaru kōṭik kuḻakā (empirāṉē)
Civaṉ in Kōtikkuḻakar which is surrounded by gardens which do not decrease! our master!
muṉtāṉ kaṭalnañcam uṇṭa(v) ataṉāl (ō)
as you consumed the poison that rose in the ocean, long ago.
piṉtāṉ paravaikku upakāram ceytāyō
Did you return the help by dwelling on the seashore, to the ocean later?
eṉtāṉ taṉiyē iruntāy; empirāṉē
my master! what is the reason for being alone?
{7:32}__3+
{$}
mattam malicūḻ maṟaikkāṭu ataṉ teṉpāl pattarpalar pāṭa irunta paramā
the supreme god who dwells to be sung and prised by many pious people, to the south of maṟikkāṭu where people of ecstasy are crowded!
kottu ār poḻil cūḻtaru kōṭikkuḻakā
the Lord in Kōṭikkuḻakar which is surrounded by gardens which blossom bunches of flowers!
ettāl taṉiyē iruntāy; empirāṉē
our master! what is the reason for your remining here alone?
[[ettāl - etaṉāl;
ettāl maṟavātē niṉaikkiṉṟēṉ (Cuntarar, Tiruveṇṇinallūr, 1);
The construction of the word should be etaṉāl]]
{7:32}__4+
{$}
kōṭikkuḻakā
O Lord in Kōṭikkuḻakar!
kāṭēl mika vālitu
this forest is very big.
kārikai añca
to make the lady, Umi fear.
kūṭip (1) pontil āntaikaḷ kūkai kuḻaṟal
the crying of the owls and rock horned owls that join together in the hollow of trees (never ceases)
[[The words with a brackets are supplied to complete the sense]]
vēṭit toṇṭar cālavum tīyar
the votaries who have as their livelihood hunting, are very cruel.
caḻakkar
are deceitful persons.
iṭam kōyil koṇṭāy (ē)
you had as your temple this place!
[[variant readings : (1) pottil]]
[[pottil āntaikaḷ pāṭṭu aṟāp pukalūrai (cuntarar, tiruppukalūr)]]
{7:32}__5+
{$}
mai ār taṭaṅkaṇṇipaṅkā
Civaṉ who has on one half a lady who has big eyes coated sumptuously with collyrium!
kaṅkaiyāḷum meyākattu iruntaṉaḷ
Kaṅkiyāḷ too was in your body which does not know destruction.
vēṟu iṭam illai
there is no other place for her.
kai ār vaḷaik kāṭukāḷōṭum uṭaṉāyak, koyār poḻil kōṭiyē kōyil koṇṭāy(ē)
you had as your abode kōṭi only which has gardens where flowers can be plucked, in the company of turkki (kātukāḷ) who has a wrist full of bangles
[[kāṭukāṉ is a corruption of Kāṭukiḻaḷ (kāṭukiḻāṉ);
cf. pōkam koṇṭār kaṭaṟkōṭiyil mōṭiyai (cuntarar, tirunāvalūr, 3)]]
{7:32}__6+
{$}
maravam kamaḻ māmaṟaikkāṭu ataṉ teṉpāl, (1) kuravam, poḻil cūḻtaru kōṭikkuḻakā
O Lord in Kōṭikkuḻakar, which is surrounded by gardens of bottle-flower trees and which is to the south of the great maṟikkāṭu where the saffron trees spread their fragrance!
empirāṉē
O our Lord!
aravu ēr alkulāḷai ōrpākam amarntu
placing with desire on one half a lady who has a waist like the hood of the cobra.
iravē tuṇaiyāy iruntāy
you were there with night as your companion.
[[Variant reading: (1) kuravappoḻil]]
{7:32}__7+
{$}
paṟaiyum kuḻalum olipāṭal iyampa, aṟaiyum kaḻal ārkka niṉṟu āṭum amutē
O Lord, the nectar, who dances standing when the Kaḻal which produces a jingling sound makes a roaring sound and when the drum, flute and songs of sweet sound serve as accompaniments!
kuṟaiyāp poḻil cūḻtaru kōṭikkuḻakā
O Lord in Kōṭikkuḻakar surrounded by gardens which do not decrease.
empirāṉē
our master.

{$}
oṟṟiyūr eṉṟa ūṉattiṉāl (atu) tāṉō
is it due to the fact that oṟṟiyūr has the blemish of being a place mortgaged.
aṟṟappaṭa ārūr(atu) eṉṟu akaṉṟāyō
Did you leave ārūr (a place belonging to somebody else) definitely!
[[There is a pun on the words oṟṟiyūr and ārūr;
ūrumatu oṟṟiyūr; maṟṟai ūr peṟṟavā nām aṟiyōm;
āvaṟkālattu aṭikēḷ ummai oṟṟi vaittu iṅku uṇṇalāmō?;
ārampāmpu; vāḻvatu ārūr; oṟṟiyūrēl ummataṉṟu (cuntarar, mūppatum illi, 3; ōṇakāntaṉtaḷi, 3,9);
This idea is elaborated in a stray verse by Kāḷamēkam;
pār ūr aṟiyap palikku uḻaṉṟīr; paṟṟip pārkkumiṭattu, ōrūrum illai irukka eṉṟālum; uḷḷūrum oṟṟi, pērūr aṟiyat tiyākar eṉṟē perumpērum peṟṟīr; ārūrilē iruppīr? iṉippōyviṭum ampalattē (taṉippālal tiraṭṭu Part I B. irattiṉa nāyakar edition, 1953, page, 165)]]
(muṟṟā mati cūṭiya kōṭikkuḻakā
Civaṉ in Kōtikkuḻakar who wears a crescent on your head!
empirāṉē
our Lord!)
eṟṟāl taṉiyē iruntāy
what is the reason for remining here alone.
{7:32}__9+
{$}
neṭiyāṉoṭu nāṉmukaṉum(m) aṟivu oṇṇāp paṭiyāṉ
Civaṉ whose nature (body) cannot be known by Māl who is tall and Piramaṉ of four faces!
[[paṭiyāṉ: nominative of address]]
palikoḷum iṭam kuṭi illai
there are no inhabitants in the place in which you intend to receive alms.
aṭikēḷ
my god!
koṭiyār palarvēṭarkaḷ vāḻum karaimēl (1) aṉpatāy iṭam koṇṭāyē
you had your abode with love on the seashore where many cruel hunters live; what is the reason?
[[Variant reading: (1) iṉpatāy]]
{7:32}__10+
{$}
pār ūr mali cūḻ maṟaikkāṭu ataṉ teṉpāl, ērār poḻil cūḻtaru kōṭikkuḻakai, ārūraṉ uraittaṉa pattuivai vallār
those who are able to recite these ten verses composed by ārūraṉ on the youth in Kōṭi surrounded by beautiful gardens and situated to the south of maṟikkāṭu where the joy of the places in this world is found.
cīr ūr civalōkattu iruppavartāmē
will certinly dwell in civalōkam whose fame has spread everywhere.

Thus end the shrines of cōḻanātu, both on the northern and southern banks of the river, Kāviri. The decade of campantar 149; the shrines sung by him are 100. The decades of nāvukkaracar number 132; the shrines sung by him are 132. The decades of cuntarar are 35; the shrines sung by him are 27. The total no of decades : 316 the no of shrines : 127 The decades of the shrines in cōḻanāṭu : 555 shrines : 190.

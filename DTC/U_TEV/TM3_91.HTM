• patikam: [3:91]
  • talam: tiru vaṭakuraṅkāṭutuṟi
  • paṇ: cātāri
  • VMS: Vol.4, p.106

*1
kōṅkamē, kuravamē, koḻu malarp puṉṉiyē, kokuṭi, mulli,
vēṅkiyē, ñāḻalē, vimmu pātirikaḷē, viravi _eṅkum
_ōṅku mā kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟi,
vīṅkunīrc caṭimuṭi _aṭikaḷār _iṭam _eṉa virumpiṉārē.

*2
mantam_āy _iḻi matakkaḷiṟṟu _iḷa maruppoṭu poruppiṉ nalla
cantam _ār _akiloṭu cātiyiṉ palaṅkaḷum takiya mōti,
_untu mā kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟi,
_entiyār _iṇi_aṭi _imiyavar toḻutu _eḻum _iyalpiṉārē.

*3
muttum mā maṇiyoṭu muḻi vaḷar _āramum mukantu nunti,
_ettu mā kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟi,
mattamāmalaroṭu mati poti caṭimuṭi _aṭikaḷtammēl
cittam_ām _aṭiyavar civakati peṟuvatu tiṇṇam_aṉṟē.

*4
kaṟiyum mā miḷakoṭu kataliyiṉ palaṅkaḷum kalantu nunti,
_eṟiyum mā kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟi,
maṟi _ulām kiyiṉar malar_aṭi toḻutu _eḻa maruvum _uḷḷak
kuṟiyiṉār_avar mikak kūṭuvār, nīṭuvāṉ_ulakiṉ_ūṭē.

*5
kōṭu_iṭic corinta tēṉ_ataṉoṭum koṇṭal vāyviṇṭa muṉnīr
kāṭu _uṭip pīliyum kaṭaṟu _uṭip paṇṭamum kalantu nunti,
_ōṭu _uṭik kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟi,
pīṭu _uṭic caṭimuṭi _aṭikaḷār _iṭam _eṉap pēṇiṉārē.

*6
kōla mā malaroṭu tūpamum cāntamum koṇṭu pōṟṟi
vāliyār vaḻipaṭap poruntiṉār, tiruntu māṅkaṉikaḷ _unti
_ālum mā kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟi
nīlamāmaṇimiṭaṟṟu _aṭikaḷi, niṉiya, valviṉikaḷ vīṭē.

*7
nīlamāmaṇi niṟattu _arakkaṉi _irupatukarattoṭu _olka
vāliṉāl kaṭṭiya vāliyār vaḻipaṭa maṉṉu kōyil---
_ēlamōṭu, _ili _ilavaṅkamē, _iñciyē, mañcaḷ, _unti,
_āliyā varupuṉal vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟiyē.

*8
porum tiṟal peruṅkimā _urittu, _umi _añcavē, _oruṅki nōkki,
perun tiṟattu _anaṅkaṉi _anaṅkamā viḻittatum perumipōlum---
varun tiṟal kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟi,
_aruntiṟattu _iruvari _allal kaṇṭu _ōṅkiya _aṭikaḷārē!

*9
kaṭṭu _amaṇ tērarum, kaṭukkaḷ tiṉ kaḻukkaḷum, kacivu _oṉṟu _illāp
piṭṭartam _aṟa_uri koḷḷalum! peru varip paṇṭam _unti
_eṭṭum mā kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟic
ciṭṭaṉār _aṭi toḻa, civakati peṟuvatu tiṇṇam_āmē.

*10
tāḻ _iḷaṅ kāviri vaṭakari _aṭi kuraṅkāṭutuṟi,
pōḻ_iḷamati poti puritaru caṭimuṭip puṇṇiyaṉi,
kāḻiyāṉ---_arumaṟi ñāṉacampantaṉa---karutu pāṭal
kōḻiyā _aḻippiṉum, kūṭuvār, nīṭuvāṉ_ulakiṉ_ūṭē.

*1
taḷir _iḷa vaḷar _eṉa _umi pāṭa, tāḷam(m) _iṭa, _ōr kaḻal vīci,
kiḷar _iḷa maṇi _aravu _ari _ārttu, _āṭum vēṭak kiṟimiyār;
viḷar _iḷamuliyavarkku _aruḷ nalki veṇnīṟu _aṇintu, _ōr ceṉṉiyiṉmēl
vaḷar_iḷamatiyamoṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*2
ventaḻal vaṭiviṉar; poṭip pūci, viritaru kōvaṇa_uṭimēl _ōr
pantam ceytu, _aravu _acittu, _oli pāṭi, palapala kaṭitoṟum pali tērvār;
cintaṉi pukuntu, _eṉakku _aruḷ nalki, ceñcuṭarvaṇṇartam _aṭi parava,
vantaṉipala ceya,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*3
paṇṇiṉ polinta vīṇiyar; patiṉeṇkaṇamum _uṇarā nañcu
_uṇṇap polinta miṭaṟṟiṉār; _uḷḷam _urukiṉ _uṭaṉ_āvār;
cuṇṇappoṭi-nīṟu _aṇi mārpar; cuṭarpoṉcaṭimēl-tikaḻkiṉṟa
vaṇṇap piṟiyōṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*4
_eri kiḷar matiyamoṭu _eḻil nutalmēl, _eṟi poṟi_araviṉoṭu, _āṟu mūḻka
viri kiḷar caṭiyiṉar; viṭi_ēṟi; veruva vantu _iṭar ceyta vikirtaṉār;
puri kiḷar poṭi _aṇi tiru_akalam poṉceyta vāymiyar; poṉmiḷirum
vari_aravu _arikku _acittu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*5
_añcaṉa maṇivaṇam _eḻil niṟamā _akammiṭaṟu _aṇi koḷa, _uṭal timila
nañciṉi, _amararkaḷ _amutam _eṉa, naṇṇiya naṟu nutal _umi naṭuṅka
veñciṉa mālkaḷiyāṉiyiṉ tōl veru_uṟap pōrttu, _ataṉ niṟamum _aḵtē,
vañcaṉi vaṭiviṉoṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*6
_alliya malar pulku virikuḻalār kaḻal_iṇi_aṭiniḻal_avi parava,
_elli_ampōtu koṇṭu _eri _ēnti, _eḻiloṭu toḻil_avi _iciya vallār;
colliya _arumaṟi _ici pāṭi, cūṭu _iḷamatiyiṉar; tōṭu peytu,
valliyantōl _uṭuttu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*7
kaṭipaṭu koṉṟinaṉmalar tikaḻum kaṇṇiyar; viṇṇavar kaṉa maṇi cēr
muṭi pilkum _iṟiyavar; maṟukil nallār muṟimuṟi pali peya, muṟuval ceyvār;
poṭi _aṇi vaṭivoṭu, tiru_akalam poṉ _eṉa miḷirvatu _or _araviṉoṭum,
vaṭi nuṉi maḻuviṉoṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*8
kaṭṭu _iṇi putumalar kamaḻ koṉṟikkaṇṇiyar; vīṇiyar; tāmum _aḵtē;
_eṇ-tuṇi cāntamoṭu _umi tuṇiyā, _iṟivaṉār _uṟivatu _or _iṭam viṉavil,
paṭṭu _iṇi _akal_alkul virikuḻalār pāviyar pali _etir koṇarntu peyya,
vaṭṭaṇi _āṭaloṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*9
_ēṉamaruppiṉoṭu _eḻil _āmi _iciyap pūṇṭu, _ōr _ēṟu _ēṟi,
kāṉam_atu _iṭamā _uṟikiṉṟa kaḷvar; kaṉavil-tuyar ceytu,
tēṉ _uṇa malarkaḷ _unti vimmit tikaḻ poṉcaṭimēl-tikaḻkiṉṟa
vāṉanalmatiyiṉoṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*10
cūṭal veṇpiṟiyiṉar; cuṭar muṭiyar; cuṇṇaveṇnīṟṟiṉar; cuṭar maḻuvar;
pāṭal vaṇṭu _ici mural koṉṟi_amtār pāmpoṭu nūl_avi pacintu _ilaṅka,
kōṭal naṉ mukilviral kūppi, nallār kuṟi _uṟu pali _etir koṇarntu peyya,
vāṭalveṇtali piṭittu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*11
tiṅkaḷoṭu _aru varip poḻil-cōlit tēṉ nalam kāṉal _am tiru vāymūr,
_aṅkamoṭu _arumaṟi _olipāṭal _aḻalniṟa vaṇṇartam _aṭi paravi,
naṅkaḷtam viṉi keṭa moḻiya valla ñāṉacampantaṉ tamiḻmāli
taṅkiya maṉattiṉāl-toḻutu _eḻuvār tamar neṟi, _ulakukku _ōr tavaneṟiyē.

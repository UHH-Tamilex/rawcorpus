  • talam: tiru vāymūr
  • paṇ: naṭṭa rākam

*1
taḷir _iḷa vaḷar _eṉa _umai pāṭa, tāḷam(m) _iṭa, _ōr kaḻal vīci,
kiḷar _iḷa maṇi _aravu _arai _ārttu, _āṭum vēṭak kiṟimaiyār;
viḷar _iḷamulaiyavarkku _aruḷ nalki veṇnīṟu _aṇintu, _ōr ceṉṉiyiṉmēl
vaḷar_iḷamatiyamoṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*2
ventaḻal vaṭiviṉar; poṭip pūci, viritaru kōvaṇa_uṭaimēl _ōr
pantam ceytu, _aravu _acaittu, _oli pāṭi, palapala kaṭaitoṟum pali tērvār;
cintaṉai pukuntu, _eṉakku _aruḷ nalki, ceñcuṭarvaṇṇartam _aṭi parava,
vantaṉaipala ceya,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*3
paṇṇiṉ polinta vīṇaiyar; patiṉeṇkaṇamum _uṇarā nañcu
_uṇṇap polinta miṭaṟṟiṉār; _uḷḷam _urukiṉ _uṭaṉ_āvār;
cuṇṇappoṭi-nīṟu _aṇi mārpar; cuṭarpoṉcaṭaimēl-tikaḻkiṉṟa
vaṇṇap piṟaiyōṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*4
_eri kiḷar matiyamoṭu _eḻil nutalmēl, _eṟi poṟi_araviṉoṭu, _āṟu mūḻka
viri kiḷar caṭaiyiṉar; viṭai_ēṟi; veruva vantu _iṭar ceyta vikirtaṉār;
puri kiḷar poṭi _aṇi tiru_akalam poṉceyta vāymaiyar; poṉmiḷirum
vari_aravu _araikku _acaittu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*5
_añcaṉa maṇivaṇam _eḻil niṟamā _akammiṭaṟu _aṇi koḷa, _uṭal timila
nañciṉai, _amararkaḷ _amutam _eṉa, naṇṇiya naṟu nutal _umai naṭuṅka
veñciṉa mālkaḷiyāṉaiyiṉ tōl veru_uṟap pōrttu, _ataṉ niṟamum _aḵtē,
vañcaṉai vaṭiviṉoṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*6
_alliya malar pulku virikuḻalār kaḻal_iṇai_aṭiniḻal_avai parava,
_elli_ampōtu koṇṭu _eri _ēnti, _eḻiloṭu toḻil_avai _icaiya vallār;
colliya _arumaṟai _icai pāṭi, cūṭu _iḷamatiyiṉar; tōṭu peytu,
valliyantōl _uṭuttu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*7
kaṭipaṭu koṉṟainaṉmalar tikaḻum kaṇṇiyar; viṇṇavar kaṉa maṇi cēr
muṭi pilkum _iṟaiyavar; maṟukil nallār muṟaimuṟai pali peya, muṟuval ceyvār;
poṭi _aṇi vaṭivoṭu, tiru_akalam poṉ _eṉa miḷirvatu _or _araviṉoṭum,
vaṭi nuṉai maḻuviṉoṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*8
kaṭṭu _iṇai putumalar kamaḻ koṉṟaikkaṇṇiyar; vīṇaiyar; tāmum _aḵtē;
_eṇ-tuṇai cāntamoṭu _umai tuṇaiyā, _iṟaivaṉār _uṟaivatu _or _iṭam viṉavil,
paṭṭu _iṇai _akal_alkul virikuḻalār pāvaiyar pali _etir koṇarntu peyya,
vaṭṭaṇai _āṭaloṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*9
_ēṉamaruppiṉoṭu _eḻil _āmai _icaiyap pūṇṭu, _ōr _ēṟu _ēṟi,
kāṉam_atu _iṭamā _uṟaikiṉṟa kaḷvar; kaṉavil-tuyar ceytu,
tēṉ _uṇa malarkaḷ _unti vimmit tikaḻ poṉcaṭaimēl-tikaḻkiṉṟa
vāṉanalmatiyiṉoṭu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*10
cūṭal veṇpiṟaiyiṉar; cuṭar muṭiyar; cuṇṇaveṇnīṟṟiṉar; cuṭar maḻuvar;
pāṭal vaṇṭu _icai mural koṉṟai_amtār pāmpoṭu nūl_avai pacaintu _ilaṅka,
kōṭal naṉ mukilviral kūppi, nallār kuṟai _uṟu pali _etir koṇarntu peyya,
vāṭalveṇtalai piṭittu,---_ivarāṇīr---vāymūr _aṭikaḷ varuvārē.

*11
tiṅkaḷoṭu _aru varaip poḻil-cōlait tēṉ nalam kāṉal _am tiru vāymūr,
_aṅkamoṭu _arumaṟai _olipāṭal _aḻalniṟa vaṇṇartam _aṭi paravi,
naṅkaḷtam viṉai keṭa moḻiya valla ñāṉacampantaṉ tamiḻmālai
taṅkiya maṉattiṉāl-toḻutu _eḻuvār tamar neṟi, _ulakukku _ōr tavaneṟiyē.

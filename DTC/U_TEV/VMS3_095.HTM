{3:95}__1+
{$} eṇ ticaikkum pukuḻ iṉṉampar mēviya vaṇṭu icaikkum caṭaiyīrē
Civaṉ who has a caṭi on which bees hum and who is in Iṉṉampar whose fame has spread in the light quarters?
vaṇṭu icaikkum caṭaiyīr
[[see 2nd line]]
umai vāḻttuvār
those who prise you
toṇṭu icaikkum toḻilōrē
will issue commands to Tēvar: ``Do this, Do that!''
{3:95}__2+
{$} yāḻ narampu iṉṉicai iṉṉampar mēviya tāḻtarucaṭai muṭiyīrē
Civaṉ who has a caṭi coiled into a crown hanging downwards, and dwells with pleasure in iṉṉampar where music is played on yāḻ and other sweet instruments.
tāḻ tarucaṭaimuṭiyīr
see 2nd line
umaic cārpavar āḻtuyar aruviṉai ilar
those who approach you will not have sufferings in which they will sink, and irresistible Karmams which are the cuse for those.
{3:95}__3+
{$} iḷamati nutaliyoṭu iṉṉampar mēviya 1. vaḷa mativaḷar caṭaiyīrē
Civaṉ who has on his caṭi a beautiful crescent and who dwells in iṉṉampar with a lady whose forehead is like the crescent!
vaḷamati vaḷar caṭaiyīrē
see 2nd line
umai vāḻttuvār uḷamati mika uṭaiyōr
those who prise you will have a good heart and great knowledge.
[[Variant reading: 1. vaḷar mati]]
{3:95}__4+
{$} iṭikural icaimural iṉṉampar mēviya, kaṭikamaḻ caṭaimuṭiyīrē
Civaṉ who a cati coiled into crown and spreads fragrance and who is in iṉṉampar where musical instruments are played so that their sound is like the roar of the thunder!
kaṭikamaḻ caṭaimuṭiyīr
see 2nd line
uma kamaḻ toḻum aṭiyavar aruviṉai ilarē
Devotees who worship your feet will not have irrestible Karmams.
{3:95}__5+
{$} imaiyavar toḻutu eḻum iṉṉampar mēviya, umai oru kūṟu uṭaiyīr
Civaṉ who has Umi on one half and who is in iṉṉampar which is worshipped by the celestials who do not wink!
umai oru kūṟu uṭaiyīr
see 2nd line
umai uḷkuvār aṉpu amaikilar ākilar
those who fix their thoughts on you have devotion towards you, with which they are satisfied.
{3:95}__6+
{$} eṇ arumpukaḻuṭai iṉṉampar mēviya, 1. taṇ aruñcaṭaimuṭiyīrē
Civaṉ who has a rare and cool cati coiled into a crown, and dwells in iṉṉampar whose fame is countless!
taṇ aruñcaṭaimuṭiyīrē
see 2nd line
umaic cār pavar viṇṇavar aṭaivu uṭaiyōr
those who reach you are the support of the other celestial beings.
[[Variant reading: 1. taṇṇaṟuñcaṭai ; I think this is a better reading]]
{3:95}__7+
{$} eḻil tikaḻum poḻil iṉṉampar mēviya, niḻal tikaḻ mēṉiyiṉīrē
Civaṉ who has a form of shining brightness and who dwells in iṉṉampar having gardens of exquisite beauty!
niḻal tikaḻ mēṉiyiṉīr
see 2nd line
umai niṉaipavar kuḻaṟiya koṭu viṉai ilar
those who think about you will not have cruel Karmam that cuse ruin.
{3:95}__8+
{$} ēttu arumpukaḻ iṉṉampar mēviya, tūrttaṉait tolaivu ceytīrē
Civaṉ who destroyed the strength of the debuchee (Irāvaṇaṉ) and who dwells in iṉṉampar to describe whose fame words are inadequate!
tūrttaṉait tolaivu ceytīr
see 2nd line
umai 1. toḻupavar kūrtta naṟkuṇam uṭaiyōr
Those who worship you will possess in an abundant measure cattuvam, one of the three qualities.
[[Variant reading: 1. toḻumavar]]
{3:95}__9+
{$} iyal uḷōr toḻutu eḻum iṉṉampar mēviya ayaṉum māl (um) aṟivaṟiyīrē
Civaṉ who could not be known by Ayaṉ and Māl and who dwells in iṉṉampar where good natured people fall at his feet and then wake up!
ayaṉum māl (um) aṟivaṟiyīr
see 2nd line
umatu aṭi toḻum 1. iyal uḷār maṟupiṟappu ilarē
those good natured people who worship your feet will not be born agin
[[Variant reading: 1. mayal]]
{3:95}__10+
{$} ēr amar poḻil aṇi iṉṉampar mēviya, tēr amaṇ citaivu ceytīrē
Civaṉ who destroyed the amaṇ and tēr (buddhists) and who dwells in iṉṉampar which is made beautiful by the shrine ēr having gardens. (ēr: may be a shrine which has no separate tevaram of its own but is mentioned in the tēvaram of a nearby shrine; it is called in Tamil vaipputtalam)
tēr amaṇ citaivu ceytīr
see 2nd line
umaic cērpavar 1. ārtuyar aruviṉai ilar
those who think of you always will not have sufferings to undergo and irresistible Karmams which are the cuse for those.
[[Variant reading: 1. āḻtuyar]]
{3:95}__11+
{$} ēṭu amar poḻil aṇi iṉṉampar īcaṉai
on the Lord in iṉṉampar made beautiful by flower-gardens.
nāṭu amar ñāṉacampantaṉ
Ñāṉacampantaṉ who is loved by all the people of the country.
nāṭu amar ñāṉacampantaṉa naṟṟamiḻ pāṭavallār
those who are able to sing the faultless Tamiḻ songs of Ñaṉacampantaṉ who is loved by all the people of the country.
paḻiilar
are without reproach

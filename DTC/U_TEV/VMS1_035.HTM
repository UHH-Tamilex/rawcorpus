{1:35}__1+
{$} uraiyāl
by the words which are mentioned in the Vētams and Ākamams.
arai ār viri kōvaṇa āṭai, narai ār viṭai ūrti nayantāṉ virai ārpoḻil vīḻi(m)miḻalai uṇarvār
those who realise the greatness of vīḻimiḻalai which has fragrant gardens and which is the abode of Civaṉ who desired the vehicle of a bull and a broad loin-cloth worn on the waist.
uyarvār
will become superior persons.
[[(vīḻi(m)miḻalai m is an augmentation for rhythm. This will apply to the following ten verses.]]
{1:35}__2+
{$} puṉaitalpuri puṉcaṭai taṉmēl kaṇaital oru kaṅkai karantāṉ
Civaṉ concealed on his ruddy caṭai decorated (with flowers) Kaṅkai which descended roaring.
viṉai illavar vīḻi(m)miḻalai
viḻimiḻalai where persons of spiritual wisdom who are without Karmams live.
niṉaivu illavar neñcamum neñcē
can the minds of those who do not think of that shrine be called minds?
{1:35}__3+
{$} aḻavallavar āṭiyum
those who weep out of ecstasy and dance.
pāṭi eḻavallavar
fall on the ground singing and then get up
entai aṭimēl viḻavallavar vīḻi(m)miḻalai toḻavallavar nallavar toṇṭē
who can fall at the feet of our father and who can worship vīḻimiḻalai where such devotees live are really good people in their service to Civaṉ.
{1:35}__4+
{$} uravam puri puṉcaṭai taṉmēl, arai aravam ārtta aḻakaṉ
the beautiful Civaṉ who tied on his strong caṭai and waist, cobras.
viravum poḻilcūḻ vīḻi(m)miḻalai paravum aṭiyār aṭiyārē
those serfs who praise vīḻimiḻalai surrounded by gardens of different trees are truly devotees.
{1:35}__5+
{$} karitu ākiya nañcu aṇi kaṇṭaṉ
Civaṉ has a neck which is beautiful by the black poison.
varitākiya vaṇṭuaṟai koṉṟai viritār poḻil vīḻi(m) miḻalai uritā niṉaivār uyarvārē
those who meditate upon viḻimiḻalai as of right with gardens which puts forth blossomed garlands of koṉṟai on which bees which have lines on their bodies hum noisily, are really great people.
[[varitu-varittu; uritu-urittu]]
{1:35}__6+
{$} caṭai ārpiṟaiyāṉ
Civaṉ wears a crescent on his caṭai.
caripūtappaṭaiyāṉ
has an army of pūtam which move about everywhere.
koṭimēlatu ōr paiṅkaṇ viṭaiyāṉ
has a bull with tender eyes on his flag.
uṟaivīḻi(m) miḻalai aṭaivār aṭiyār avar(tām, ē)
those who reach vīḻimiḻalai where he dwells, are really devotees.
{1:35}__7+
{$} ceṟi ārkaḻalum cilampu(um) ārkka, neṟiār kuḻalāḷoṭu niṉṟāṉ
Civaṉ stood with a lady of curly tresses of hair, for the tight fitting Kaḻal and anklet to make a big sound
[[um should be added to cilampu also.]]
veṟiārpoḻil vīḻi(m)miḻalai, aṟivār avalam aṟiyārē
those who meditate upon vīḻimiḻalai which has fragrant gardens, do not know sufferings.
{1:35}__8+
{$} uḷaiyā vali olka arakkaṉ vaḷaiyāviral ūṉṟiya maintaṉ
the strong god who pressed down the straight toe to make the arakkaṉ to suffer pain and to reduce his strength. which did not know defeat before.
viḷaiārvayal vīḻi(m)miḻalai aḷaiyāvaruvār aṭiyār
those who enjoy vīḻimiḻalai which has fields with produce and come there on pilgrimage, are devotees.
{1:35}__9+
{$} iruvar mayal āka maruḷ ceytu
Māl and Piramaṉ to get confused out of ignorance.
aruḷ ceytavaṉ, ār aḻal āki veruḷ ceytavaṉ
Civaṉ granted his grace after frightening them assuming a form of great fire.
vīḻi(m) miḻalai tekuḷ ceytavar tīviṉai tēyvē
the sins of those who clearly realise the greatness of vīḻimiḻalai, will diminish gradually.
{1:35}__10+
{$} tuḷaṅkum neṟiyār avar toṉmai vaḷam koḷḷaṉmiṉ pul amaṇtērai
caman and tēr (buddhists) have no steady and fixed doctrines; therefore do not accept their teachings though they are old.
viḷaṅkum poḻil vīḻi(m)miḻalai uḷaṅkoḷpavar tamviṉai ōyvē
the Karmams of those who fix their thoughts on vīḻimiḻalai which has thriving gardens, will come to an end.
{1:35}__11+
{$} naḷir kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ
Ñāṉacampantaṉ who lives in cool Kāḻi.
miḷir ārpoḻil vīḻi(m)miḻalai, kuḷir ārcaṭaiyāṉ aṭi kūṟakkiḷar pāṭal vallārkku ilaikēṭu
there will be no ruin for those who can recite the exalted songs as they praise the feet of Civaṉ who has a cool caṭai and who dwells in Vīḻimiḻalai which has gleaming gardens.

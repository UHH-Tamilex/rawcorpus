koṭukoṭṭi kaiyala kukaiyiṟkaṇṭēṉ (Nāvukkaracar, tiruvāmāttūr, 6, tiruvārūr taṇṭakam, (6) 3; tiruveṇkāṭu 5, tiruviṉātti-ruttāṇkam, 8);

neñcamē
my mind!
pantam vīṭu avai āya parāparaṉ antam il pukaḻ ārūr araneṟi cinkaiyuḷḷum cirattuḷum taṅka
the shrine of ārūr araneṟi which has unlimited fame and which belongs to Civaṉ who is the hightest and who is himself bondage and salvation, to stay in the mind and head (araneṟi : a temple within the second enclosure of the big temple, tirumulaṭṭaṉam, facing west)
enta mātavam ceytaṉai
what great penance did you perform in previous births?
[[cf. eṉṉa mātavam ceytaṉai neñcamē, miṉṉuvār caṭaivēta viḻup poruḷ, cennel ār vayal cēṟaiyuḷ cenneṟi, maṉṉucōti nampāl vantu vaikavē (nāvukkaracar, tirucceṟi, tirukkuṟuntoki 2)]]

vaṇṭu ulām malar koṇṭu vaḷar caṭaikku iṇṭaimālai puṉaintum
making a garland called circlet to adorn the growing caṭi with flowers around which the bees hum without setting on it.
irāppakal toṇṭarāki
and becoming devotees by performing services night and day.
toṭarntu viṭātavarkku
who worship him continuously without break.
aṇṭam āḷavum vaippar ārūrar
Civaṉ in ārūr will elevate them to rule over the heaven also; in addition to granting knowledge of Civaṉ.

being free completely from all sufferings.
cupattarāy
and possessing auspicious things.
eṉpu elām nekku irāppakal ētti niṉṟu
prising continuously night and day, all the bones to become tender.
iṉparāy

niṉaintu
meditating on god.
iṭai aru aṉparām avarkku aṉpar ārūrar
the Lord in Ārūr reciprocates love to those who cherish him with love without any break.

(People of this world!)
muruṭṭu mettaiyil muṉkiṭattā muṉam
before your bodies are lid in the presence of relations and friends in the rough bed of the funeral pyre
araṭṭar aivarai ācaṟuttiṭṭu
removing quickly the evils that come from the five senses which cuse worries like petty chieftins;.
muraṇ taṭitta attakkaṉ taṉ vēḷviyai araṭṭuaṭakki taṉ ārūr aṭaimiṉ(ē)
reach ārūr of Civaṉ who repressed the hughtiness of the sacrifice performed by that takkaṉ who was unfeeling on account of the perversity he had towards Civaṉ.
[[araṭṭu insolence;

val arakkaṉ araṭṭu iruvaraik kīḻ aṭartta poṉ ampalattu aracē (tiruvicippā, tirumālikitēvar, Koyil, 10)]]

emmaiyār ilai
both my parents are no more.
yāṉum uḷēṉ ilēṉ
as I am young I have no steady mind to live separately.
emmaiyārum itu ceyya vallarē
can my elder sister, tilakavati who was like a mother to me, be a companion to me?
ammaiyār eṉakku eṉṟu eṉṟu araṟṟiṉēṟku
to me who poured out my troubles in lamentation saying many times who will help me like the mother!
ārūr aiyar
the Lord in ārūr.
1. ammai ārat tantār
showed me the path to salvation to my complete satisfaction.

taṇṭa āḷiyai
Civaṉ who punishes people for their misdeeds.
takkaṉ taṉ vēḷviyaik ceṇṭu (atu) āṭiya 1. tēvar akaṇṭaṉai
who cannot be divided by space, time and other things and who ruined the sacrifice of takkaṉ as he woulds do with a bouquet:
[[ceṇṭāṭutal: ceṇṭāṭi avuṇarpuram ceṟṟāṉ kaṇṭāy (navūkkaracar, tirukkōṭika tāṇtakam, 2)]]
[[tēvarakaṇṭaṉ: viṇṇōr tirumāḷātu āṭkoṇṭa tēvarakaṇṭam, perumāḷ tiyākap perumāḷ (tiruvārūrrulā, 27-28)]]
kaṇṭu kaṇṭu ivaḷ kātalittu aṉpu (atu) āy
this girl seeing him always and loving him, and developing devotion towards him.
eṉ taṉ kōtai koṇṭu āyiṉavāṟu
what a change came over my dughter to be insubordinate to me!
[[koṇṭi is like the word paṭṭi which means unblidled woman.;

ivaḷ nammaip pala pēcat toṭaṅkiṉāḷ
This girl, my dugther began to talk many things with me incoherently.
avaṇam aṉṟu eṉil ārūr araṉ eṉum
if we are not in the place where she stays she says `araṉ in ārūr!'
pavaṉi vīti viṭaṅkaṉaik kaṇṭu ivaḷ eṉṟaṉ taiyal tavaṉi āyiṉa vāṟu
this is what this girl, my young dughter has seen infatuated with burning love, having obtined vision of vītiviṭaṅkaṉ who comes in a procession.
[[Vītiviṭantaṉ is the name of tiyākarācar in tiruvārūr as he comes out in procession in the streets; there are seven shrines of tiyākarācār; (1) tiruvārūr (2), nākapaṭṭiṉam, (3) tirunaḷḷāṟu (4) vētāraṇiyam (5) Kāṟāyil (6) tiruvāumūr (7) tirukkōlili.;
viṭaṅkaṉ means a bronze that has not been chiselled. Of the seven the tiyākarācar in ārūr only comes in procession in the streets; so he is called vītiviṭankapperumāṉ.]]

nīraic ceñcaṭai vaitta nimalaṉār
the spotless Civaṉ who placed water on his red caṭi.
kār otta miṭaṟṟar
has a neck which is black like the sable cloud.
kaṉal vāy arā ārattar
wears a garland of cobras which have poison which is like fire.
aṇi ārūrait tūrattē toḻuvār viṉai tūḷiyē
the acts of those who worship with joined hands the beautiful ārūr from a distance, will crumble like powder.

uḷḷamē
my mind!
1. ōr uṟuti uraippaṉ nāṉ
I shall tell you a thing that will do you good.
veḷḷam tāṅku viricaṭai vētiyaṉ
Civaṉ, the brahmin, who bore the floods in his loosened caṭi.
aḷḷal nīr vayal ārūr amarnta em vaḷḷal cēvaṭi vāḻtti vaṇaṅkiṭu
prise, and pay obeisance to, the lotus-red feet of our Lord of unbounded liberality who dwelt in ārūr which has fields full of water and mire. (vaḷḷal : is the translation of tiyākarācar).
[[Variant reading: 1. oṉṟu uṟuti;
This is like the usage in civakacintāmani, oṉṟu tēriṉāl (417)]]

viṇṭa māmalar mēl uṟai vāṉoṭu koṇṭal vaṇṇaṉum kūṭi aṟikilā aṇṭavāṇaṉ taṉ ārūr aṭitoḻa
to worship with joined hands the feet of the god in ārūr who dwells in civalōkam whom Piramaṉ who dwells in a big lotus of unfolded petals and Māl who has the colour of a sable cloud, had not the capacity to know.

our acts which have accumulated in several births will not stand; will vanish.

{2:48}__1+
{$} kaṇkāṭṭum nutalāṉum
the Lord who has on his forehead an eye which is fire?
kaṉal kāṭṭum kaiyāṉum
has on his hand fire,
peṇkāṭṭum uruvāṉum
has a lady on his form,
piṟaikāṭṭum caṭaiyāṉum
has a crescent on his matted locks of hir.
paṇkāṭṭum icaiyāṉum
is in the form of music which has many paṇs.
payirkāṭṭum puyalāṉum
is in the form of cloud which nourishes the vegetation.
veṇkāṭṭil uṟaivāṉum
and who resides in Veṇkāṭu.
viṭaikāṭṭum koṭiyāṉ
has a banner in which the bull is exhibited.
{2:48}__2+
{$} pēy aṭaiyā
Devils will not approach people.
pirivu eytum
if they are possessed by them they will go away from those people.
piḷḷaiyiṉōṭu uḷḷa niṉaivu āyiṉavēvaram peṟuvar
people will be granted boon like getting issued and other boons which they have in their minds.
oṉṟum aiyuṟa vēṇṭā
people should not have even the slightest doubt about these.
vēy aṉa tōḷ umaipaṅkaṉ veṇkāṭṭu mukkuḷanīr tōy viṉaiyār avartammaittīviṉai tōyā
sins will not afflict those who bathe in the water of the three tanks in Veṇkāṭu of the Lord who has Umi whose shoulders resemble the bamboo, on his half,
[[mukkuḷam three tanks: cōma tīrtam, akkiṉi tīrtam, Cūriya tīrtam]]
[[This stanza is connected with the birth of meykaṅtār, the author of Civañāṉapōtam]]
{2:48}__3+
{$} maṇṇoṭu nīr aṉal kālōṭu ākāyam mati iravi eṇṇil varum iyamāṉaṉ ikaparamum eṇ ticaiyum peṇṇiṉoṭu āṇ perumaiyoṭu ciṟumaiyum ām pērāḷaṉ
the renowned personage who is small as well as big, who has male and female halves, who is the eight directions, the life in this world and the life after that, the eight forms, which are the five elements, moon, sun and esteemed person who performs sacrifices.
[[iyamāṉaṉ is the corruption of yajamānaṉ, a sanskrit word]]
viṇṇavar kōṉ vaḻipaṭa veṇkāṭu iṭamā virumpiṉaṉ
the Lord desired Veṇkāṭu as his place, to be worshipped by the king of the residents of heaven
[[cf intiraṉ vaḻipaṭa irunta em miṟai (campantar, veṇkātu, patikam 3-1)]]
{2:48}__4+
{$} viṭam uṇṭa miṭaṟṟaṇṇal veṇkāṭṭiṉ taṇpuṟavil
in the cool jungle of Veṇkāṭu of the Lord who has a neck which drank the poison.
maṭal viṇṭa muṭattāḻai malar niḻalaik kuruku eṉṟu
mistaking the reflection in the water of the flower of the curved fragrant screw-pine, whose petals have unfolded, to be the white crane.
taṭam maṇṭu tuṟaik keṇṭai tāmaraiyiṉ pū maṟaiya
the fish Keṇti, which moves swiftly in the tank, hides under the lotus flower.
kaṭal viṇṭa katir muttam nakaikāṭṭum kāṭciyatē
the lustrous pearl which left the sea seems to be showing its teeth

{$} vēlaimali taṇ kāṉal veṇ kāṭṭāṉ tiruvaṭikkīḻ
at the feet of the Lord in Veṇkāṭu which has cool sea-shore gardens flourishing by the sea.
mālai mali vaṇ cāntalāl vaḻipaṭum naṉmaṟaiyavaṉmēl aṭarkālaṉ uyir viṇṭa piṉai
after the life of Kālaṉ who came to attack upon the good brahmin who worshipped with a great quantity of fragrant sandal-paste and garlands, was separated (by the Lord)
namaṉ tūtar āla miṭaṟṟāṉ aṭiyār eṉṟu aṭara añcuvar
the messagers of Namaṉ will be afrid of attacking people who are devotees of the Lord who has poison in his neck.
[[The story contined in this verse is about Cuvētakētu who worshipped god in this shrine; this is found in the puraṇam about this shrine]]
{2:48}__6+
{$} caṭaiyiṉuṭaṉ taṇ matiyam veyyaravum tāṅkiṉāṉ
the Lord has borne on his matted locks of hir the cool crescent and the cruel cobra together.
[[veyya aravu -- veyyaravu]]
oṇmatiyam nutal umai ōrkūṟu ukantāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple where the Lord who is happy to have on his form Umi who has a forehead resembling the bright crescent, as a half.
paṇ moḻiyāl avaṉ nāmam palaōta
when devotees are chanting his many names with words as sweet as melodies.
pacuṅkiḷḷai veṇmukil cēr karum peṇaimēl vīṟṟirukkum veṇkāṭu
is Veṇkāṭu where the green parrot is perched majestically on the black palmyra tree on which white clouds rest.
[[Variant reading: karumpaṉai]]

{$} cakkaram māṟku īntāṉum
the Lord who gave the discus to Māl
akku araimēl acaittāṉum
who tied round his waist bones.
aṭaintu ayirāvatam paṇiya mikku ataṉukku aruḷ curakkum veṇkāṭum viṉaiturakkum mukkuḷam naṉku uṭaiyāṉum
and who has the good three tanks - which drive away sins and Veṇkāṭu which showered grace in an abundant measure when the white-coloured elephant by name Ayirāvatam reached that place and worshipped the Lord

kaḷ ēntu koṉṟai tūykkālai mūṉṟum ōvāmē niṉṟu tavaṅkaḷ ceyta, veḷḷāṉai vēṇṭum varaṅkoṭuppār (appar, tāṇṭakam, 9)]]
mukkaṇ uṭai iṟaiyavaṉ
is the Lord who has three eyes.

{$} paṇ moytta iṉmoḻiyāḷ payam eyta malai eṭutta uṉmattaṉ uram nerittu aṉṟu aruḷceytāṉ uṟaikōyil
the temple in which the Lord who crushed the chest of the madcap who lifted the mountin to make the lady of sweet words like melodies to be seized with fear, and then bestowed his grace, in the distant past, resides.
kaṇ moytta karumaññai naṭam āṭa
the blue peacock of dense stars in its til to dance.
kaṭal muḻaṅka
and the sea to roar.
viṇ moytta poḻil varivaṇṭu icai muralum veṇkāṭu
is Veṇkāṭu where in the garden which covers the sky bees with lines make a sound.
{2:48}__9+
{$} kaḷ ār ceṅkamalattāṉ kaṭaṟ kiṭantāṉ eṉa ivarkaḷ
these two one of whom is seated in the red lotus of abundant honey and the other who is lying on the (milky) ocean)
oḷḷāṇmai koḷaṟku ōṭi uyarntu āḻntum uṇarvu ariyāṉ
one who is difficult to be known though ran and flew high and went on digging the earth to be climed as his good proteges by Civaṉ.
veḷḷāṉai tavam ceyyum mētaku veṇ kāṭṭāṉ eṉṟu uḷāṭi urukātār uṇarvuṭaimai yuṇarōm
we will not recognise the knowledge of those whose hearts do not become tender and melt out of love thinking of the Lord in eminent Veṇkāṭu where the white-coloured elephant (Airāvatam) did penance, as worthy of being called knowledge.

{$} pōtiyarkaḷ piṇṭiyarkaḷ miṇṭu moḻi poruḷ eṉṉum pētaiyarkaḷ avar
they who are buddhists and jins who talk harshly and arrogantly are ignorant persons who say that their words are real doctrines
piṟimiṉ
leave their company.
aṟivuṭaiyīr
those who are endowed with sense!
kēṇmiṉ
Listen to what I say.
[[pōti is the pipal tree under which Buddha became enlightened; therefore it is sacred for them;
[[piṇṭi-acōku; amarnta nāṉmukaṉ (naṉṉūl, 56)]]
[[piṇṭiyarkaḷ refers to jins]]
vētiyarkaḷ virumpiya cīr viyaṉtiru veṇkāṭṭāṉ eṉṟu ōtiyavar yātu oru tītum ilar eṉṟu uṇarumiṉ
you understand that those who say the Lord is in Veṇkāṭu of great extent, and eminence desired by the vētiyar (brahmins) will not have even the slightest evil.

{$} viṇpoli veṇpiṟaic ceṉṉi vikirtaṉuṟai veṇkāṭṭai
about Veṇkāṭu where the god different from the world has on his head a white crescent which shines in the sky, resides.
taṇpoḻilcūḻ caṇpaiyarkōṉ tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ
(sung by) ñāṉacampantaṉ well-versed in Tamiḻ, and chief of the residents of Caṇpi which is surrounded by cool gardens.
paṇ poli centamiḻ mālai pāṭiya pattu ivai vallār
those who are able to recite these ten verses which are like a garland of centamiḻ which thrives by its melody.
maṇpoliya vāḻntavar pōy vāṉpoliyap pukuvār
will live in this world flourshingly and ascending heaven will reside there to add splendour to the heaven.

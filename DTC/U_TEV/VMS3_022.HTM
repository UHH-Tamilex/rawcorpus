{3:22}__1+
{$}
(People of this world!)
tuñcalum tuñcal ilāta pōḻtiṉum
when you are asleep and when you are awake.
neñcakam naintu nāḷtoṟum niṉaimiṉ
meditate daily on the pañcākkaram, your hearts becoming tender losing its hardness.
vañcam aṟṟu aṭi vāḻtta
when mārkkanṭēyaṉ sang benediction on the feet on Civaṉ without deception.
vanta kūṟṟu añca utaittaṉa añcu eḻuttumē
the mantiram consisting of five letters kicked the god of death who come to take away his life and to be struck with fear.
{3:22}__2+
{$} mantiram nāṉmaṟai āki
being the mantiram and the four vētams (pañcakkaram includes all the things mentioned in the vetams
[[añceḻuttē ākamum aṇṇal arumaṟaiyum (unmai viḷakkam 45)]]
vāṉavar cintaiyuḷ niṉṟu avar tammai āḷvaṉa
admits as proteges the celestials, being permanent in their thoughts.
centaḻal ōmpiya cemmai vētiyarkku antiyuḷ mantiram añcu eḻuttumē
the five letters are the mantiram in the three parts of the day, morning, noon and evening, for the brahmins who are upright and who maintain three kinds of fire.
{3:22}__3+
{$} ūṉil uyirppai oṭukki
restraining the breath in the body.
oṇcuṭar ñāṉa viḷakkiṉai ēṟṟi
lighting the lamp of knowledge of divine wisdom which has a bright flame
naṇpulattu ēṉaivaḻi tiṟantu
opening the other path of the good knowledge.
ēttuvārkku iṭar āṉa keṭuppaṉa añceḻuttumē
the five letters destroy the sufferings of those who praise Civaṉ
{3:22}__4+
{$} nallavar tīyavar eṉātu nacciṉar cellal keṭac civamuttikāṭṭuva
show the path to salvation which is auspicious after destroying the afflictions of those who chant with desire, without any distinction whether they are good people or bad people.
kolla namaṉtamar koṇṭu pōm iṭattu allal keṭuppaṉa añceḻuttumē
the five letters destroy the sufferings when the messengers of the god of death carry you to torture you (kollutal: varuttutal)
[[karumpupōl kollap payaṉpaṭumkīḻ (Kuṟaḷ, 1078)]]
{3:22}__5+
{$} koṅku alarvaṉmataṉ vāḷi aintu
the arrows of the strong maṭaṉ, which have fragrance spreading, are five in number.
akattu aṅku uḷa pūtamum añca
the elements which are inside one self are five in number;
[[vañca maṉattāṉ paṭiṟṟoḻukkam, pūtaṅkaḷ aintum akattē nakum (Kuṟaḷ, 271)]]
aimpoḻil
the divine gardens in heaven are five
cantāṉam, vēṇṭiṟṟellām tarum, aricantaṉam, pū, mantāram pāricātam kaṟpakam maṟṟu ōr aintu ām
(cūṭāmaṇinikaṇṭu 4-2)
taṅku araviṉpaṭam (1) añcum
the hoods of the cobras which stay in Civaṉ's body are five.
tammuṭai aṅkaiyil aiviral añcu eḻuttumē
there are five fingers in the beautiful hands of those who recite mantirams; just like those, the mantiram of Civaṉ consists of five letters.
[[Variant reading: (1)añcu]]
{3:22}__6+
{$} tummal irumal toṭarnta pōḻtiṉum
at times when one sneezes and caughs following each other.
vemmai narakam viḷainta pōḻtiṉum
when we have to experience cruel suffering of the hell.
immai viṉai aṭarttu (1) eytum pōḻtiṉum
when the acts reach us attacking us in this birth.
ammaiyiṉum tuṇai añcu eḻuttumē
the mantiram of five letter is of help to us in the next birth as a result of having chanted that mantiram without ceasing.
[[Variant reading: (1) eytupōḻtiṉum]]
{3:22}__7+
{$} piṟappai aṟuttu vīṭumecciṉar pīṭai keṭuppaṉa
destroys the misery of those who desire salvation getting rid of birth.
piṉṉai nāṭoṟum māṭu koṭuppaṉa
will give wealth daily afterwards
[[cintum valviṉai, celvamum nalkumāl nantināmam namaccivāyavē (campantar, namaccivāyattiruppatikam, 6)]]
maṉṉum mānaṭam āṭi ukappaṉa-añceḻuttumē
the mantiram consisting of five letters is desired by Civaṉ who performs the great dance which is permanent.
{3:22}__8+
{$} añcu eḻuttum
the mantiram consisting of five letters.
vaṇṭu amar ōti maṭantai pēṇiṉa
was cherished with love by the lady who has tresses of hair on which bees settle.
(1)
paṇṭai irāvaṇaṉ pāṭi uyntaṉa
were the cause of irāvaṇan singing them and saving himself, in the past (paṇṭai:ai
expletive)

toṇṭarkaḷ koṇṭu tutittapiṉ avarkku aṇṭam aḷippaṉa
grants rule over the many universe if the devotees recite them as their duty and praise Civaṉ.
[[varient reading: (1) paṇṭa virāvaṇaṉ]]
{3:22}__9+
{$} añcu eḻuttumē
the five letters.
kārvaṇaṉ nāṉmukaṉ kāṇutaṟku oṇāc cīrvaṇaccēvaṭi cevvi nāḷtoṟum pērvaṇam pēcip pitaṟṟum pittarkaṭku
to those devotees who praise many a time daily uttering the name of Civaṉ and nature of the red feet which have the fame of not being seen by Māl who has colour of the sable cloud and Piramaṉ of four faces.
ārvaṇam āvaṉa
have the nature of enjoying them endlessly.
{3:22}__10+
{$} añcu eḻuttumē
see 1st verse.
puttar camaṇ kaḻukkaiyar poy koḷāc cittattavarkaḷ teḷintu tēṟiṉa
those who did not accept the false doctrines of buddhists and camanar who held in their hands stake for impaling, were very clear in their minds.
vittaka nīṟu aṇivār viṉaippakaikku attiramāvaṉa
are weapons to beat with, the enemies of acts of those who adorn their bodies with sacred ash which is capable of destroying all sins

{3:22}__11+
{$} naṟṟamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ who had knowledge of refined tamiḻ.
nāṉmaṟai kaṟṟavaṉ
who learnt the four vētams.
kāḻiyar maṉṉaṉ
and the chief of the inhabitants of Kāḻi.
uṉṉiya añcu eḻuttu uṟṟaṉa aṟṟam il mālai īraintum vallavar
those who are capable of reciting the harmless garlands which contain the greatness of the mantiram of five letters on which he meditated and sang.
umpar āvarē
after their life in this world will become celestials.

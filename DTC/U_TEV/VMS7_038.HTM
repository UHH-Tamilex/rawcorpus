{7:38}__1+
{$} tammāṉai aṟiyāta cātiyār uḷarē
is there any one who by nature does not know his chief?
(there is none)
kaṟai koṇṭa kaṇṭattu ammāṉ taṉ aṭikkoṇṭu eṉ muṭimēl vaittiṭum eṉṉum ācaiyāl vāḻkiṉṟa aṟivilā nāyēṉ nāṉ
I who am as low as a dog, and who am without intelligence, desiring that the god who has a black neck will place his feet on my head.
caṭaimēṟkoḷ piṟaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who wears a crescent on the caṭi.
viṭaimēṟkoḷvikirtaṉ(ai)
who rides on a bull and who is different from the world.
kaimmāviṉ uriyāṉai
who has a skin of an elephant of long trunk.
karikāṭṭil āṭal uṭaiyāṉai
who dances in the scorched cremation ground.
viṭaiyāṉai
who has a flag of a bull.
emmāṉai
who is my Lord.
eṟikeṭilavīraṭṭāṉattu uṟaivāṉai iṟaipōtum ikaḻvak pōl
perhaps I was negligent for a moment about the Lord who dwells in Vīraṭṭam on the north bank of Keṭilam which throws waves, when he came to place his feet on my head, without knowing its greatness; Alas! what a pity my ignorance was so great! perhaps it would not happen at any time here after.

{$} muṉṉē emperumāṉai maṟantu eṉcol maṟavātu oḻintu eṉkol
what did I lose before he admitted me as his protege by forgetting him? what did I gin by always remembering him?
maṟavāta cintaiyāl vāḻvēṉ
I live without forgetting him in my mind after he admitted me as his protege.
poṉṉē, nalmaṇiyē, veṇmuttē, cempavaḷakkuṉṟamē īcaṉ eṉṟu (1) uṉṉaiyē pukaḻvēṉ
I shall prise you only thinking about you, as gold' flawless gem, white pearl, hill made of red coral and the Lord of the universe.
aṉṉē eṉ attā' eṉṟu amararāl amarappaṭuvāṉai
who is desired by the immortals as our mother, our father,
atikai mānakaruḷ vāḻpavaṉai
who dwells in the big city of Atiki.
eṟikeṭila vīraṭṭāṉattu eṉ uṟaivāṉai iṟaipōtum yāṉ ikaḻvaṉpōl
I was perhaps negligent about my Lord who dwells in Vīraṭṭāṉam on the north bank of the Keṭilam which throws waves, for a moment, when he came to place his feet on my head, Alas! what a pity my ignorance was so great. It would not occur at any time hereafter.
[[Variant reading: (1) uṉṉiyē]]

{$} kātal cēr mātarāḷ kaṅkaiyāḷ naṅkaivarum puṉalum caṭaikku aṇintu vaḷarāta piṟaiyum vari aravum uṭaṉtuyila (1) vaittaruḷum entai
my father who adorned his caṭi with the Kaṅki, a lady of distinction who came in the form of water, when there was already a loving lady on one half of the body, was gracious enough to cuse to sleep together a crescent which does not wax, a cobra with lines on it.
irumpuṉal vantu eṟi keṭila vīraṭṭāṉattu uṟaivāṉai
and the Lord who dwells in the Vīraṭṭāṉam on the northern bank of Keṭilam which dashes its floods on the banks.
viṇṇavartam perumāṉē
the Lord of the celestials!
maṉṉavar niṉṟu ēttum karumpē, eṉ kaṭṭi eṉṟu uḷḷattāl uḷki
thinking of him in my mind as the sweet sugar-cane, which is prised without ceasing by the people of this world, my sweet jaggery'.
virumpiṉēṟku
to me who desired him.
eṉatu uḷḷam (1) viṭakilā vitiyē
as my fate did not leave my mind.
iṟaipōtum ikaḻvaṉpōl yāṉ (ē)
I was perhaps negligent for a small moment when he came to adorn my head with his feet. what a pity my ignorance was so great. This will not occur agin here after at anytime.

{$} nāltāṉattu oruvaṉai
the unequalled god who is superior to the three gods, Piramaṉ, Māl and Urattiraṉ.
[[nāl should be taken as an ordinal number and not as a cardinal number]]
nāṉ āyaparaṉai
the supreme being who is united with me without separate identity.
naḷḷāṟṟu nampiyai, veḷḷāṟṟu vitiyai
the perfect soul in Naḷḷāṟu, the righteous conduct in Veḷḷāṟu.
kāṟṟāṉai, tīyāṉai, kaṭalāṉai, malaiyiṉ talaiyāṉai
who is himself the air, the fire, the ocean, who dwells on the peak of the mountin, Kayilāyam.
kaṭuṅkaluḻik kaṅkai nīr veḷḷa āṟṟāṉai
who contined on his head the puddle of swift river of flood of Kaṅki.
[[The words should be arranged in the following order:

piṟaiyāṉai
who wears a crescent.
ammāṉai
who is my father.
emmāṉ tammāṉai
who is the chief of my father also.
yāvarkkum aṟivu ariyaceṅkaṇ ēṟṟāṉai
who has a bull of red eye, and who could not be comprehended by anyone however great he may be.
eṟi keṭila vaṭavīraṭṭāṉattu uṟaivāṉai
and who dwells in Vīraṭṭāṉam on the northern bank of the Keṭilam which hurls waves.
iṟaipōtum ikaḻvaṉpōl yāṉē
I was perhaps negligent for a small moment when he came to adorn my head with his feet, out of ignorance. what a pity my ignorance was so great! This will not occur agin hereafter.

{$} cēntartāy malaimaṅkai tiruniṟamum (1) parivum uṭaiyāṉai
Civaṉ who has the beautiful colour and affection of the dughter of the mountin who is the mother of Murukaṉ.
atikai mānakaruḷ vāḻpavaṉai
who dwells in the great temple of Atiki.
kūntal tāḻpuṉal maṅkai kuyil aṉṉa moḻiyāḷ caṭaiyil
having rejoiced when he received in the cati Kaṅki who is in the form of water, who has tresses of hir hanging low and words as sweet as the voice of the indian cuckoo.
kayal iṉaṅkaḷ kutikoḷḷak kulāvi
shining when the groups of carp fish jump.
vāynta nīr vara
when the floods swell in it.
unti marāmaraṅkaḷ vaṇakki
bending the catampa tree by the rising waves.
malai āramvāri
taking in a sweep sandal wood trees growing in the mountin.
maṟikaṭalai iṭam koḷvāṉ
to occupy the ocean where waves roll up and down.
ēntu nīr eṟi keṭila vīraṭṭāṉattu uṟaivāṉai iṟaipōtum ikaḻvaṉpōl yāṉē
I was perhaps negligent for a small moment about the Lord who dwells in Vīraṭṭāṉam on the north bank of the Keṭilam whose abundant water throws waves, when he came to adorn my head with his feet. What a pity my ignorance was so great! This thing will not happen agin hereafter.

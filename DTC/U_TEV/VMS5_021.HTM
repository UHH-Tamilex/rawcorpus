{5:21}__1+
{$} eṉṉil ārum eṉakku iṉiyār ilai
There is no better one who does good to me than myself.
eṉṉilum iṉiyāṉ oruvaṉ uḷaṉ
But there is one who is more lovable than myself
eṉṉuḷē uyirppāyp puṟam pōntu pukku-eṉṉuḷē niṟkum iṉṉampar īcaṉē
Civaṉ in Iṉṉampar stays within me as my breath doing the twin acts of exhaling and inhaling.
{5:21}__2+
{$} maṭṭu uṇpārkaḷum
People who consume intoxicating drinks
maṭantaiyar vāṭkaṇṇāl kaṭṭuṇpārkaḷum
and those who are bound by the beauty of the eyes of women which are like swords.
taṭṭimuṭṭit taḷḷāṭit taḻukkuḻi
when they lose their eyesight and falter when walking and slip
karutuvatu eṉ kol
what can these people think of as help?
eṭṭumūrttiyar iṉṉampar īcaṉē
except Civaṉ in Iṉṉampar who has eight forms.
{5:21}__3+
{$} kaṉalum kaṇṇiyum taṇmatiyōṭu uṭaṉ puṉalum koṉṟaiyum cūṭum puricaṭai aṉalum cūlamum māṉmaṟikkaiyiṉar (1) eṉalum eṉmaṉattu iṉṉampar īcaṉē
when I say Civaṉ who wears the chaplets, cool crescent, water and koṉṟi worn on the twisted caṭi holds in his hand fire, a trident and a young deer, he immediately enters into my mind and shines there.
[[eṉalum eṉmaṉattu iṉṉampar īcaṉ kaṉalum is the syntactical link; kaṉalum is the predicate]]
[[Variant reading: (1)iṉalum]]
{5:21}__4+
{$} maḻaikkaṇ māmayil ālum makiḻcciyāṉ
with the joy that can be compared to the joy of the peacock that dances during the riny season
aḻaikkum taṉ aṭiyārkaḷ tam aṉpiṉaik kuḻaikkum
melts and blands in union with the devotion of his devotees who invoke him
taṉṉaik kuṟikkoḷa vēṇṭiyē iḻaikkum eṉ maṉattu iṉṉampar īcaṉ
Civaṉ in Iṉṉampar will fix his form in my mind, in order to make me concentrate on him.
{5:21}__5+
{$} teṉṉavaṉ
Civaṉ is beautiful
eṉai āḷum civaṉ avaṉ
he admits me into his grace
maṉṉavaṉ
is the chief of the gods
mati am maṟai ōtiyāṉ
chanted vētams of such great respectability
muṉṉam maṉ avaṉ
he was existing permanently before the creation of the world.
vaṉam cēr pūḻiyāṉ
besmears with the ash in the cremation ground
iṉṉam iṉpuṟṟa iṉṉampar īcaṉ
Civaṉ is form whom the sun-god derived happiness by worshipping here
[[iṉṉanampūr have become iṉṉampar the place where Iṉaṉ (sun-god) worshipped Civaṉ]]
{5:21}__6+
{$} piṟar uḷḷattil vēṟupaṭa viḷakkum
will appear differently in the minds of others than his devotees
taṉ aṭiyār maṉattu aṉpiṉai aḷakkum
will measure the love in the minds of his devotees.
kuṟikkoḷavēṇṭi eṉṉaik kuḷakkum
will coax me to grant me single-minded devotion
[[kuḷakkum -- kuḻakkum: cusal form of kuḻakum is used in the place; kuḻakutal: prattling playfully, coating; kiḷiyoṭum kuḻakēlē, kuḻaki eṅkaḷ kuḻamaṇaṉ koṇṭu (Tiruvāymoḻi, 6-2-5,6)]]
eṉ maṉattu iḷakkum iṉṉampar īcaṉ
Civaṉ in Iṉṉampar melts the hardness of my heart.
{5:21}__7+
{$} caṭaikkaṇāḷ puṉal maṅkai
Kanki in the form of water is on the caṭi
aṉal kaiyatu
fire is on the hand
ōr kaṭaikkaṇāl maṅkai nōkka
when that lady glances at him with the outer corner of her eyes.
maṅkai paṭaik kaṇ nōkkāl parukappaṭuvāṉ
He is looked at by the looks of the lady, Umi whose eyes are like weapon such as lance, arrow and sword, as if she is literally drinking his beauty.
[[parukuvaṉṉa arukā nōkkamoṭu (Porunarāṟṟuppaṭi, 77); kuruku peyark kuṉṟam koṉṟōṉ aṉṉa niṉ, murukac celvi mukantu taṉ kaṇṇāl parukāḷāyiṉ (Maṇimēkali, 5-13-15); irunta kulakkumarartamai irukaṇṇum mukantu aḻaku paruka nōkki (Kampar, Mitilikkāṭcippaṭalam, 157)]]
{5:21}__8+
{$} toḻutu tūmalar tūvittutittu niṉṟu aḻutu kāmuṟṟu araṟṟukiṉṟāraiyum
those cry aloud wishing for him, weaping and standing having scattered faultless flowers for worship
poḻutu pōkkip puṟakkaṇippāraiyum iṉṉampar īcaṉ kīḻkkaṇakku eḻutum
Civaṉ in Iṉṉampar will note down the names of those who do not prising his name but waste their time and neglect him, in small lines in his account book.
{5:21}__9+
{$} viriyum taṇ iḷavēṉilil veṇpiṟai puriyum kāmaṉai vēvap puruvamum tiriyum
will bend his eye-brows to burn Kāmaṉ who desires as his help the white crescent during the expanding portion of the milder part of the summer season.
ellaiyil mummatil tī eḻuntu eriya nōkkiya iṉṉampar īcaṉ
Civaṉ in Iṉṉampar who looked at the three forts which transgressed decency, to be consumed by fire.
[[iṉṉampar īcaṉ kāmaṉaivēvap puruvamum tiriyum is the syntactical link]]
{5:21}__10+
{$} iṉiyaṉāy niṉṟa iṉṉampar īcaṉ
Civaṉ in Iṉṉampar who is a loving person always.
caṉiyum veḷḷiyum tiṅkaḷum ñāyiṟum muṉivaṉāy niṉṟa muṭipattuṭaiyāṉtaṉaik kaṉiya ūṉṟiya kāraṇam eṉ
what is the reason for pressing down Irāvaṇaṉ who had ten heads to become soft with love, and who was angry with planets like Saturn, Venus, Moon and the Sun.

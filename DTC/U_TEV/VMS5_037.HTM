malaik koḷ yāṉai mayakkiya valviṉai nilaik koḷ āṉai niṉaippuḷa neñcamē
my mind which thinks of several things without being controlled like the elephant and being the stand for cruel acts which confuse the mind like the elephant which lives in the mountin.
kolaik kai yāṉaiyum koṉṟiṭum
Civaṉ has the nature of the elephant of (irresistible) acts and other rutting elephants, therefore.
kalaikkai yāṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr is like the elephant which can be reached with good conduct which is the result of studying arts.

veḷḷi māl varai pōlvatu or āṉaiyār
Civaṉ has an elephant by name Ayiravaṇam which is as white as the big silver mountin
[[PP: ayirāvaṇam ēṟātu āṉēṟu ēṟi amarar nāṭu āḷātē ārūr āṇṭa, ayirāvaṇamē
(nāvukkaracar, tiruvārur, tāṇṭakam (2) 1)]]
[[PP: nirai nirai āyiram vakutta māyiru maruppiṉ, veṇṇiṟac ceṅkaṇ vēḻamum
(11th tirumuṟi, Kōyilnāṉmaṇimāli, 4-13-15)]]
uḷḷavāṟu eṉai uḷpukum āṉaiyār
he is like the elephant which enters into my mind in its true nature.
koḷḷam ākiya kōyiluḷ āṉaiyār
is the elephant which occupies the temple which has beauty,
kaḷḷa āṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr is like the elephant which has a deceptive nature.

ñāṉam ākiya naṉku uṇar āṉaiyār
Civaṉ is like the elephant which has well understood which is called spiritual knowledge.
umai añcavē (1) vēṉal āṉai urittu
flaying the elephant which had become emaciated by the hot summer, to make Umi frightened.
[[vēṉal-vēṉil;;
kontār, iḷa vēṉal vantatāl eṉṉām kol iṉṟu, vaḷavēṉaṟ kaṇṇi maṉam.;
vēṉaṟpā ṇikkalantāṉ meṉpūn tirumukattaik kāṉaṟpā ṇikkalantāy kāṇ (Cilappatikāram, canto 8, veṇpa-s at the end)]]
(2) kāṉ al āṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr is the elephant that does not live in the forest.
ūṉai vēva urukkiya āṉaiyār
is the elephant who melted the body to transform it as love itself.

ālam uṇṭu aḻakāyatu ōr āṉaiyār
Civaṉ is the elephant which became beautiful by consuming the poison.
nīlamēṉi neṭum paḷiṅku āṉaiyār
is the elephant which has a body like crystal besides having a body like the saphhire;
[[nīla mēṉi vāliḻai pākattu oruvaṉ (aiṅkuṟunūṟu, invocatory verse)]]
kōlam āya koḻuñcuṭar āṉaiyār
is the elephant which has a beautiful and rich lustre.
kāla āṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr is the elephant to Kālaṉ (god of death).

aḷitta āṉ añcum āṭiya āṉaiyār
the elephant that bathed in the five products of the cow offered by devotees with love.
veḷutta nīḷ koṭi ēṟuṭai āṉaiyār
the elephant who has a long flag on which a white bull is drawn.
eḷitta vēḻattai eḷ kuvitta āṉaiyār
the elephant that flayed the elephant that slighted his might, to be despised by all.
kaḷitta āṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr is the like the rutting elephant.

viṇ uṟa viṭutta māl varai āṉaiyār
Civaṉ is the elephant who has as his abode the big mountin (Kayili) which seems to touch the sky.
māl varai toṭutta tūyatu ōr āṉaiyār
the pure elephant that shot an arrow from the big mountin (that was bent into a bow)
kaṭutta kālaṉaik kāyntatu ōr āṇaiyār
the elephant that destroyed the angry Kālaṉ (god of death)
kaṭutta āṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
Civaṉ in Kaṭavur is like an angry elephant.

maṇṇuḷārai mayakkuṟum āṉaiyār
Civaṉ is the elephant who confuses the people of this world.
eṇ uḷār palar ēttiṭum āṉaiyār
is the elephant who is prised by devotees increasing in number (eṇ uḷār people who have different thoughts)
viṇṇuḷār palarum aṟiyāṉaiyār
is the elephant known to all tēvar in heaven.
kaṇṇuḷ āṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
is the elephant who grants his vision to the apple of the eyes of the devotees (who worship him with love)

ciṉakkum cempavaḷattiraḷ āṉaiyār
Civaṉ is the elephant who is the mass of red coral which seems to be angry by its red colour.
maṉakkum valviṉai tīrttiṭum āṉaiyār
is the elephant who removes the irresistible karmam-s that are in the mind.
[[maṉakkum -- maṉattukkum; attu is dropped;
nalakkuriyār (Kuṟal, 149)]]
aṉaikkum aṉpu uṭaiyār maṉattu āṉaiyār
is the elephant who dwells in the hearts of devotees who lavish on him love, more than the mother.
kaṉaikkum āṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
Civaṉ is the elephant who invites his devotees to come near him by his roaring

[[cuṭar here refers to the illumination that one gets by studying them.]]

is the elephant who understands, distinugishing the many aeons.
kātal āṉai (kaṇṭīr) kaṭavūrar
Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr is the elephant who loves his devotees, reciprocrating their love.

nīṇṭa māloṭu nāṉ mukaṉ tāṉu māyk kāṇṭum eṉṟu pukkārkaḷ
Māl who grew tall and Piramaṉ of four faces proceeded, we shall find out the head and feet.
iruvarum māṇṭa ār aḻal ākiya āṉaiyār

Civaṉ in Kaṭavūr is the elephant that can be seen by all people.

{3:94}__1+
{$} viṇṇavar toḻutu eḻu veṅkuru mēviya, cuṇṇa veṇpoṭi aṇivīrē
Lord who adorn yourself with the sacred white ash which is like the scented powder, and who resides in Veṅkuru where the residents of the heaven worship and then wake up from sleep!
cuṇṇa veṇpoṭi aṇivīr
[[see 2nd line]]
uma toḻukaḻal eṇṇa vallār iṭar ilar
those who are able to meditate upon your feet deserving worship will not have any suffering.
[[uma = um + a: a: is the plural suffix of the genitive case as kaḻal denotes two feet.]]
{3:94}__2+
{$} vētiyar toḻutu eḻu veṅkuru mēviya, ātiya arumaṟaiyīrē
oh! God who is the meaning of the preeminent and abstruse vetam and who is residing in veṅkuru where brahmins worship and then wake up from sleep!
ātiya arumaṟaiyīr
see 2nd line.
umai alarkoṭu ōtiyar uṇarvuṭaiyōrē
they are the people who are endowed with wisdom, who prise you worshipping you with flowers.
{3:94}__3+
{$} viḷaṅku taṇ poḻil aṇi veṅkuru mēviya, iḷam piṟai aṇi caṭaiyīrē
Lord who adorn yourself with a tender crescent moon and who resides in veṅkuru which is made beautiful by cool and thriving parks!
iḷam piṟai aṇi caṭaiyīr
see 2nd line.
umatu iṇai aṭi uḷam koḷa uṟupiṇi ilarē
those who fix your two feet in their minds will not have numerous diseases.
{3:94}__4+
{$} viṇṭu alar poḻil aṇi veṅkuru mēviya, vaṇṭu amar vaḷar caṭaiyīrē
Lord who has lengthening caṭi, on which bees settle and who dwells in veṅkuru which has parks of blossomed flowers!
vaṇṭu amar vaḷar caṭaiyīr
same as 2nd line
umai vāḻttum attoṇṭarkaḷ tuyar piṇi ilar
those famous devotees who prise you will not have mental afflictions and physical diseases
[[tuyar, piṇi -- tuyarum piṇiyum]]
{3:94}__5+
{$} mikkavar toḻu teḻu veṅkuru mēviya, akkiṉoṭu aravu acaittīrē
Lord who fastened cobras along with conch-beads and dwells in veṅkuru which the great persons worship and then rise from sleep!
akkiṉoṭu aravu acaittīr
same as for 2nd line
umatu aṭiyiṇai takkavar uṟuvatu tavamē
The fruit of penance of virtuous persons is to reach your feet
{3:94}__6+
{$} venta veṇpoṭi aṇi veṅkuru mēviya, antam il perumaiyiṉīrē
Lord who is great does not know end, who dwells in veṅkuru, and who smears himself with well burnt white sacred ash!
[[antam il perumai: limitless greatness]]
antam il perumaiyiṉīr
same as 2nd line.
umai alar koṭu cintai ceyvār viṉai citaivē
the sins of those who meditate on you scattering flowers, will be destroyed.
{3:94}__7+
{$} viḻa malku poḻil aṇi veṅkuru mēviya, aḻal malkum aṅkaiyiṉīrē
Lord who has fire in your palm and dwells in veṅkuru made beautiful by festivals conducted in parks!
aḻal malkul aṅkaiyiṉīr
same as 2nd line
umai alar koṭu toḻa allal keṭuvatu tuṇivē
It is beyond doubt that the sufferings will be destroyed when devotes worship you with flowers
{3:94}__8+
{$} vittakam maṟaiyavar veṅkuru mēviya, matta naṉmalar puṉaivīrē
Lord who wears good dhatura flowers and dwells in veṅkuru which is full of skilful brahmins!
matta naṉ malar puṉaivīr
same for 2nd line
umatu aṭitoḻum cittam (atu) uṭaiyavar tiruvē
those who have the mind to worship your feet will obtin the wealth of eternal bliss.
{3:94}__9+
{$} mēlavar toḻutu eḻu veṅkuru mēviya, āla naṉ maṇi miṭaṟṟīrē
Lord who has a neck in which there is poison and therefore is like a flawless sapphire and who dwells in veṅkuru which wise men worship and then get up from sleep!
āla naṉ maṇi maṭaṟṟīr
same as for 2nd line.
umatu aṭi toḻum cīlam (atu) uṭaiyavar tiruvē
Those who have the good conduct of worshipping your feet will obtin eternal bliss.
{3:94}__10+
{$} viraimalku poḻil aṇi veṅkuru mēviya, arai malku puli ataḷīrē
Lord who has fastened a tiger's skin in your waist and dwells in veṅkuru beautiful in having parks of abundant fragrance.
arai malku puli ataḷīr
same for 2nd line
umatu aṭi iṇai urai malku pukaḻvar uyarvē
the greatness of those famous devotees who prise your feet is [the] only true greatness.

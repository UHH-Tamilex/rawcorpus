[[This shrine is now popularly known a Tirunāmanallūr]]

{7:17}__1+
{$} ōr ampiṉāl mēvalar muppuram tī eḻuvittavar
Civaṉ who caused fire to rise in the three cities of the enemies, with a single arrow.
ēvalar
who is praised a clever archer on account of that.
veṇṇey nallūril (1) vaittu eṉaiyum āḷkoṇṭa nāvalaṉārkku
to him who is very clever in arguing his case and admitted even me into his grace making me stand in Veṇṇeynallūr.
kōvalaṉ nāṉmukaṉ vāṉavarkōṉum kuṟṟēval ceyya iṭam āvatu namtiru nāvalūrē
Nāvalūr which is our birth-place is the place for him, where Māl, Piramaṉ of four faces and Intiraṉ, the King of the celestials, do menial service.
[[Variant reading: (1) vaittu eṉṉai; this will apply throughout this decade]]
{7:17}__2+
{$} taṉmaiyiṉāl
due to this nature of unbounded grace.
aṭiyēṉai (um) tām āṭkoṇṭanāḷ
on that day when Civaṉ came to admit even me into his grace me, his slave.
capaimuṉ
before the assembly.
vaṉmaikaḷ pēciṭa
when I spoke rude and harsh words (did not mind them and but only thought of accepting me as his slave).
vaṉ toṇṭaṉ eṉpatu ōrvāḻvutantār
he accepted them as praising words and gave me a title of vaṉṟoṇṭaṉ
puṉmaikaḷ pēcavum poṉṉait tantu eṉṉaip pōkam puṇartta naṉmaiyiṉārkku
even after that when I, on account of great intimacy, I sang many abusive songs he rejoiced hearing them and granted me wordly pleasures giving me gold; To that god who did such good acts.
iṭam āvatu namtirunāvalūrē
Nāvalūr which is our birth place, is the place.
{7:17}__3+
{$} vēkam koṇṭu ōṭiya veḷviṭai ēṟi ōr melliyalai ākam koṇṭār
Civaṉ had in his body a lady of tender nature and rode on a white bull which ran with great speed.
veṇṇey nallūril vaittu eṉṉai āḷum koṇṭār
admitted even me into his grace, making me stand before the assembly in veṇṇainallūr. (eṉai āḷum koṇṭār-eṉaiyum āḷkoṇṭār)
kaṭal kōṭiyil mōṭiyaip pōkam koṇṭār
he had union with the mōṭi (turkkai) in kōṭi on the sea-coast.
[[kai ār vaḷaik kāṭukāḷōṭum uṭaṉāykkoy ārpoḻiṟ kōṭiyē kōyil koṇṭāyē (tirukōtikkuḻakar, (cuntarar, 5)]]
pūṇpatāka nākam koṇṭārkku iṭam āvatu nam tirunāvalūrē
Nāvalūr which is our birth-place is the place of God who had cobras for his ornaments.
{7:17}__4+
{$} (1) āviṉil añcumkoṇṭu āṭuvar
Civaṉ will bathe in the five products of the cow.
cēviṉai āṭci koṇṭār
has the ownership of a bull.
aṭiccaṇṭiyait tañcam koṇṭār tām eṉa vaittu ukantār
rejoiced in placing as equal to him caṇṭi who approached his feet admitting him as a deposit and who sought shelter in god.
veṇṇey nallūril vaittu eṉaiyum āḷ koṇṭu neñcam koṇṭār
captivated my mind, making me stand in veṇṇainallūr and admitting even me as his slave.
nañcam koṇṭārkku iṭam āvatu namtiru nāvalūrē
nāvalūr, our birth-place is place of the Lord who consumed the poison.
[[Variant reading: (1) āviṉaic]]
{7:17}__5+
{$} umparār kōṉait tiṇtōḷ murittār
Civaṉ bent the strong shoulders of Intiraṉ, the King of the celestials,
[[PP: cantiraṉait tēyttaruḷit takkaṉtaṉ vēḷviyiṉil, intiraṉait tōḷ nerittiṭṭu
(tiruvācakam, tiruvammāṉai, 15)]]
urittār kaḷiṟṟai
flayed the skin of an elephant.
cempoṉ ār tīvaṇṇar
has the colour of superior gold and fire.
tūvaṇṇanīṟṟar
smears himself with spotless white-coloured sacred ash.
ōr āvaṇattāl empirāṉār veṇṇey nallūril vaittu eṉaiyum āḷ koṇṭa tampirāṉārkku iṭam āvatu
the place of the Lord who admitted even me also as his slave having made me stand in Veṇṇainallūr by a bond, and who is our master.
namtiru nāvalūrē
is nāvalūr, our birth-place.
{7:17}__6+
{$} kuṭamūkkilum, kōvalum kōttiṭṭaiyum kōṭṭam koṇṭār
Civaṉ had temples in the shrines Kuṭamūkkil, Kōval, and Kōttiṭṭai;
[[kōttiṭṭaiyum kōvalum kōyil toṇṭīr (cuntarar, tirupparaṅkuṉṟam, 1)]]
vēṭṭam koṇṭār
took upon himself hunting, transforming himself into a hunter.
veṇṇey nallūril vaittu eṉaiyum āḷ koṇṭār
admitted even me as his slave making me stand in Veṇṇai Nallūr.
tillaicciṟṟampalattē āṭṭam koṇṭār
danced in ciṟṟampalam in tillai.
arukkaṉai muṉ nāṭṭam koṇṭārkku iṭam āvatu
the place of the Lord who put out the eyes of the sun long ago;
[[PP: uṇṇappukunta pakaṉ oḷittu ōṭāmē kaṇṇaip paṟittavāṟu untīpaṟa
(tiruvācakam, tiruvuntiyār, 12);]]
[[PP: pakaṉ tāmaraik kaṇkeṭak kaṭantōṉ;
cīr arukkaṉ kuruṭṭil pukac ceṟṟa kōṉ
(Kōvaiyār, 184, 270)]]
{7:17}__7+
{$} tāyavaḷāy
being my mother.
tantaiyāki
and being my father.
cātal piṟattal iṉṟi
without death and birth.
pōy akalāmait taṉ poṉ aṭikkē eṉṉai (um) porunta (1) vaitta vēyavaṉār
Civaṉ who manifested in a bamboo in tirunelvēli and who placed even me in his feet valuable as gold, to be united with them without going away leaving them.
veṇṇey nallūril vaittu eṉaiyum āḷkoṇṭa nāyakaṉārkku iṭam āvatu
the place of the chief who admitted even me as his slave making me stand in veṇṇainallūr.
namtirunāvalūrē
is tirunāvalūr which is our birth place.
[[Variant reading: (1) vaittē]]
{7:17}__8+
{$} tī āṭiyār
Civaṉ who dances in fire.
ōr vēṭuvaṉāy ciṉakkēḻaliṉ piṉceṉṟu vēy āṭiyār
who made use of archery assuming the form of a hunter following the angry boar's footprints.
māmaṟai ōti ōr vētiyaṉāki vantu vāyāṭi veṇṇey nallūril vaittu eṉaiyum āṭkoṇṭa nāyaṭiyārkku iṭam āvatu
the place of the hunter who admitted even me as his slave making me stand in veṇṇainallūr, coming there disguised as a brahmin chanting the great vētams; and arguing;
[[nāyaṭiyār hunters; S.I.I Vol.II P.352)]]
namtirunāvalūrē
is nāvalūr, our birth-place.
{7:17}__9+
{$} paṭam āṭu pāmpu aṇaiyāṉukkum pāvai nallāḷ taṉakkum vaṭam āṭu mālviṭai ēṟṟukkum pākaṉāy
Civaṉ who is pākaṉ to [Māl] who lies [[on]] a couch of [[a]] serpent which dances spreading its hood, to [Umai] who is as a beautiful as a portrait and to the bull which wears a string of moving bells
[[āṉēṟu bull of the boving species;]]
[[pākaṉ one who has as a half; and who drives]]
orunāḷvantu iṭam āṭiārtiru veṇṇey nallūril vaittu eṉaiyum āḷ koṇṭa naṭam āṭiyārkku iṭam āvatu
the place of the dancing god who admitted even me as his slave making me stand in veṇṇainallūr, which is his place over which he rules.
nam tirunāvalūrē
is nāvalūr, our birth-place.
{7:17}__10+
{$} miṭukku uṇṭu eṉṟu ōṭi veṟpu eṭuttāṉ valiyai nerittār
Civaṉ crushed the strength of the arakkaṉ who lifted the mountain running towards it, thinking he had strength to do it.
aṭakkam koṇṭu āvaṇam kāṭṭi nalveṇṇeyūr (eṉaiyum) āṭkoṇṭār
admitted even me as his slave in good veṇṇaiyūr showing me the bond, concealing the original document.
taṭukka oṇṇātatu ōr vēḻattiṉai urittiṭṭu umaiyai naṭukkam kaṇṭārkku iṭam āvatu
the place of the god who caused Umai to tremble with fear, having flayed an elephant which could not be stopped from coming towards him.
nam tirunāvalūrē
is nāvalūr, our birth-place.
{7:17}__11+
{$} nātaṉukku ūr
the place of the master.
namakku ūr
our birth place.
naraciṅka muṉaiyaraiyaṉ ātarittu īcaṉukku āṭceyyum ūr
the place where Naracinka Muṉai Araiyaṉ did service to Civaṉ with regards.
aṇi nāvalūr eṉṟu ōta naltakkavaṉ toṇṭaṉ ārūraṉ uraitta tamiḻ kātalittum kaṟṟum kēṭpavar (1) viṉaikkaṭṭaṟum (ē)
the bondage of acts of those who hear the Tamiḻ verses composed by Ārūraṉ, who is fit to sing the fame of beautiful Nāvalūr, Vaṉṟoṇṭaṉ, and learn with love, will be destroyed.
[[Naraciṅka Muṉai Araiyaṉ was a petty chief ruling that region round Nāvalūr; Cuntarar was brought up in his palace till he was married.]]
[[Variant reading: (1) viṉaikaṭṭaṟumē]]

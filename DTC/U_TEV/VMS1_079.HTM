{1:79}__1+
{$} ayil uṟu paṭaiyiṉar
God who has a sharp weapon (of trident)
viṭaiyiṉar
has a bull.
muṭimēl aravamum matiyamum viraviya aḻakar
Has the beauty of cobra and crescent living united on his head.
mayil aṉa cāyala vaṉamulai orupāl makiḻpavar
rejoices in having on one side a lady who has the tenderness of a peacock and beautiful breasts.
vāṉiṭai mukilpulku miṭaṟar
has a neck as black as the cloud in the sky.
payilvuṟu caritaiyar
he is always practising good conduct.
(1) erutu ukantu ēṟip pāṭiyum āṭiyum palikoḷvar
he will receive alms riding rejoicingly on a bull, singing and dancing.
valicēr kayilaiyum potiyilum iṭam eṉa uṭaiyār kaḻumalam niṉaiya
when we think of Kaḻumalam which is the abode of the god who has strong Kayili and Potiyil as his places.
namviṉai, karicu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will become extinct.
[[potiyil is the hill where Akattiyar is supposed to reside; it is also one of the shrines belonging to Civaṉ;]]
[[PP: puliyūrp puṉitaṉ potiyil veṟpil (Kōviyār, 8)]]
[[Variant reading (1) ēṟu]]
{1:79}__2+
{$} koṇṭalum nīlamum purai tiru miṭaṟar
God who has a beautiful neck resembling the sable cloud and blue nelumbo flower.
koṭumuṭi uṟaipavar
he resides in Koṭumuṭi
[[Koṭumuṭi is a shrine in Koṇku country; its full name is Pāṇtikkoṭumuṭi.]]
paṭutalaikkaiyar
holds in his hand a skull which is without life.
paṇṭu alar ayaṉ ciram arintavar
he cut off the head of Ayaṉ in the lotus flower, in the distant past.
paṭarcaṭai aṭikaḷār poruntum pati
the place where the deity who has a spreading matted hir, resides.
ataṉ ayalē
by its side.
vaṇṭalum vaṅkamum caṅkamum cuṟavum maṟitirai kaṭal koṇarntu eṟṟiya karaimēl
on the shore aginst which the sea of rolling waves brings silt, boats, conches and sharks, dashes.
kaṇṭalum kai taiyum neytalum kulavum kaḻumalam niṉaiyanamviṉai karicu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will become extinct if we think of Kaḻumalam where white mangrove, fragrant screw-pine and white indian water-lily plants are thriving.
{1:79}__3+
{$} eṇṇiṭai oṉṟiṉar
when god is formless in the mind he is one
uruvam iraṇṭiṉar
when he assumes a form he has the two forms of Civaṉ and cakti.
eriiṭaimūṉṟiṉar
he is the subtle elements of sound, touch and form in the fire
[[This may mean this way also: he is the three sacrificial fires; ākavaṉīyam, kārukapattiyan and takshiṇakkini.]]
maṇṇiṭai aintiṉar
he is the five subtle elements of sound, touch form, taste and odour.
aṅkam āṟiṉar
he is the six aṅkam of the vētam.
ēḻicai vakuttaṉar
he created the seven notes.
eṭṭu iruṅkalaicēr paṇṇiṭai oṉpatum uṇarntavar
He has knowledge of the nine things in melodies which are united with eight kinds of arts.
[[The meaning of this line is not clear.]]
pattarpāṭi niṉṟu aṭitoḻa mataṉaṉai vekuṇṭa kaṇṇiṭaik kaṉaliṉar karutiya kōyil kaḻumalam niṉaiya
if we meditate upon Kaḻumalam of the god who burnt, out of anger, with the fire in the frontal eye mataṉaṉ (cupid) for the devotees to worship his feet, singing his prise.
nam viṉaikaricu aṟum
our sins will become extinct.
{1:79}__4+
{$} eri oru karattiṉar
he has fire on one hand.
imaiyavarkku iṟaivar
he is the master of the tēvar-s who do not wink.
ēṟu ukantu ēṟuvar
he rides on a bull with joy.
nīṟu meyapūcittiritarum iyalpiṉar
has the nature of wandering besmearing his person with the sacred ash.
ayalavar puraṅkaḷ tī eḻa viḻittaṉar
he opened his frontal eye to set fire to the cities of the enemies.
vēy purai tōḷi, varitaru kaṇiṇai, maṭavāl añca mañcu uṟa nimirntatōr vaṭivoṭum vanta kari uri maruviya aṭikaḷukku iṭamām kaḻumalam niṉaiya
if we meditate upon Kaḻumalam, the abode of the god who covers his person with the skin of an elephant which came upon him with a tall form so as to reach the sky, to frighten the lady who had shoulders like bamboos, and two eyes with lines in them.
namviṉai karicu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will become extinct.
{1:79}__5+
{$} ūr etirntu iṭupalitalaikalaṉ āka uṇpavar
he who eats the alms which is given welcomed by the people of the villages, using the skull as the eating plate.
viṇpolintu ilaṅkiya uruvar
has a brilliant form which is as extensive as the sky.
pār etirntu aṭitoḻa viraitaru mārpil paṭaaravu āmai akku aṇintavarkku iṭam ām (kaḻumalam)
Kaḻumalam which is the abode of the god who adorned himself with chank beads, the shell of the tortoise, a hooded cobra in the fragrant chest, to make the people of this world to worship his feet, submitting to them.
[[akku may mean also rudrāksha.]]
nīr etirntu iḻi maṇi nittila muttam nirai (1) curi caṅkamoṭu oṇmaṇi varaṉṟi
Having scraped off lustrous precious stones along with spiralling conches of several rows with pearls inside them, and pearls which descend down floating upon the water.
kār etirntu ōtam vaṉtirai karaikku eṟṟum kaḻumalam niṉaiya
when we meditate upon Kaḻumalam where the strong waves of the sea which has on it clouds, dashes upon the shore.
nam viṉaikaricu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will become extinct.
[[Variant reading: (1) cori]]
{1:79}__6+
{$} muṉ
at that time when all things are destroyed.
uyirttōṟṟamum iṟutiyum āki
having become the cuse for the creation which unites the souls with the bodies and the end which separates them.
muṭiuṭai(ya) amararkaḷ aṭipaṇintu ētta
to be worshipped by the immortals wearing crowns.
piṉṉiya caṭaimicaip piṟai niṟaivitta pēraruḷāḷaṉār pēṇiya kōyil
the temple desired by the god of abundant grace who made the crescent on his entwined matted hir, to become full in size.
poṉ iyal naṟumalar puṉaloṭu tūpam cāntamum ēntiya kaiyiṉar āki
holding in their hands yellow fragrant flowers like gold, water, incense and sandal-paste.
kaṉṉiyar nāḷ toṟum vēṭamē paravum kaḻumalam niṉaiya
when we meditate upon Kaḻumalam where unmarried girls dily worship the form of the god.
namviṉai karicu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will become extinct.
{1:79}__7+
{$} kolaikku aṇittā varukūṟṟu utai ceytār
God kicked the god of death who came just before impending death (to take the life of mārkaṇtēyaṉ)
kurai kaḻal paṇintavarkku aruḷiya poruḷiṉ nilaikku aṇittāvara niṉaiya vallārkku
for those who are able to think of the stage when he grants it to those who bow before his feet wearing tinkling anklets.
neṭuntuyar tavirtta em nimalarukku iṭamām
the place which is the abode of our spotless god who removed big sufferings.
malaikku aṇittāvara vaṉtirai murala
when the strong waves roar by coming near the artificial hillock which is called tōṇipuram.
matuviri puṉṉaikaḷ muttu eṉa arumpa
when the mastwood trees which open their honey, put forth buds like pearls.
kalaikkaṇam kāṉaliṉ niḻalil vāḻum kaḻumalam niṉaiya
when we meditate upon Kaḻumalam where the herds of stags live under the shade of the sea-side grove.
nam viṉaikaricu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will become extinct.
{1:79}__8+
{$} puyam pala uṭaiya teṉ ilaṅkaiyar vēntaṉ poruvarai eṭuttavaṉ poṉmuṭi tiṇtōḷ
the strong shoulders and golden crowns of the King of southern Ilaṇki who had twenty shoulders and who lifted up the mountin which is compared to other mountins.
payampalapaṭa aṭarttaruḷiya perumāṉ parivoṭum iṉitu uṟaikōyil atu ākum (kaḻumalam)
Kaḻumalam which is the temple where the god pressed down to be frightened in many ways, resides with desire favourably.
viyaṉ pala viṇṇiṉum maṇṇiṉum eṅkum vēṟuvēṟu ukaṅkaḷil peyar uḷatu eṉṉa
well known as having many names in very many different aeons, everywhere in the earthern worlds and many extensive heaven.
[[As there are many universe there are many skies and world.]]
kayampala paṭak (1) kaṭaṟṟirai karaikku eṟṟum kaḻumalam niṉaiya
when we meditate upon Kaḻumalam where the waves of the sea dash aginst the shore to form many pools.
nam viṉai karicu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will become extinct.
[[Variant reading: (1) kaṭaṟirai]]
{1:79}__9+
{$} vilaṅkal oṉṟu ēnti vaṉmaḻai taṭuttōṉum
Māl who averted destruction from torrential rins by uprooting and holding a mountin
[[This is a story of Bhāgavatam; the mountin is called Ghōvardhanam.]]
veṟikamaḻ tāmaraiyōṉum
and Piramaṉ seated in the lotus which spreads its fragrance.
eṉṟu ivar tam palaṅkaḷāl nēṭiyum aṟivu aritāya pariciṉaṉ maruvi niṉṟu iṉitu uṟai kōyil
the temple in which the god whose nature was difficult to be found out by both of them though they tried to do so by their physical strength.
malaṅki vaṉtirai varai eṉap parantu eṅkum maṟikaṭal ōṅki
floating on the rolling sea which has strong waves spreading everywhere like mountin, having been made turbid.
veḷ ippiyum cumantu
carrying white oysters also.
kalaṅkaḷ taṉ carakkoṭu nirakka vantu ēṟum kaḻumalam niṉaiya
when we meditate upon Kaḻumalam where ships enter in a row with their merchandise.
namviṉai karicu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will becme extinct.
[[In the last time it would be better if the reading were kalaṅkaḷ tam carakkoṭu]]

{$} ām pala tavam muyaṉṟu aṟavurai collum amaṇarum
the amaṇar who preach religious instruction having tried to do penance according to their ability.
tērarum kaṇicēr nōmpala tavam aṟiyātavar noṭinta mūturai koḷkilā mutalvar
the cuse of all things who does not regard the old sayings of those who do not know the penance of physical and mental strins of the tērar which should be reckoned with
tam mēṉic cāmpalum pūci veṇtalai kalaṉākattaiyalār iṭupali vaiyakattu ēṟṟu
receiving in the world the alms placed by ladies, carrying the white skull as the begging-bowl, and having besmeared his form with sacred ash.
kāmpu aṉa tōḷiyoṭu iṉitu uṟaikōyil kaḻumalam niṉaiya
when we meditate upon Kaḻumalam where he dwells happily with a lady having shoulders like bamboo.
namviṉai karicu aṟum
our sins and blemishes will become extinct.

{$} kalikeḻupāriṭai ūr eṉa uḷatām kaḻumalam virumpiya kōyil koṇṭavarmēl
on the god who resides in the temple desired by him in Kaḻumalam which exists without destruction to be prised as the important place in this world of great bustle.
vali keḻumaṉam mikavaittavaṉ
he who fixed his resolute mind.
maṟaicēr kalaivarum ñāṉacampantaṉ tamiḻaṉ olikeḻu mālaiyeṉṟu uraiceyta pattum
all the ten verses which are associated with music, composed by ñāṉacampantaṉ who is an adept in the arts and maṟi.
uṇmaiyiṉāl niṉaintu ēttavallārmēl melikeḻu tuyar aṭaiyā
sufferings which are the cuse for the enfeebling of mind and body, will not approach those who can bestow thoughts on them and are able to prise god with sincerity.
viṉai cintum
the sins will be destroyed.
viṇṇavar āṟṟaliṉ mikap peṟuvār
will acquire powers superior to those of the celestials.

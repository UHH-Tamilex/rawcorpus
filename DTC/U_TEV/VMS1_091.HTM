{1:91}__1+
{$} cittam teḷivīrkāḷ
People who wish your minds to be cleared of their impurities of the soul!
attaṉ ārūraip pattimalartūva
if you worship Ārūr of our father scattering the flowers of piety.
mutti ākumē
you will obtin salvation.
{1:91}__2+
{$} piṟavi aṟuppīrkāḷ
People who want to cut at the root of births.
aṟavaṉ ārūrai maṟavātu ēttumiṉ
prise without forgetting Ārūr of Civaṉ who is the embodiment of virtues.
tuṟavi ākumē
it will lead to the way of renunciation.
[[PP: tuṟavi-tuṟavu; vayanta mālaiyum mātavi tuṟavikku, ayarntu mey vāṭiya aḻiviṉaḷ ātaliṉ ātaliṉ;
tuṟavi uḷḷam tōṉṟittoṭarum
(Maṇimēkali, 2-10-11, 26-58);]]
[[PP: tuṟavu tuṟavi eṉa niṉṟāṟpōla aḷavu aḷaviyeṉaniṉṟatu
(Kōviyār, 10, Pērāciriyar)]]
{1:91}__3+
{$} tuṉpam tuṭaippīrkāḷ
people who want to remove sufferings completely.
aṉpaṉ aṇi ārūr naṉpoṉmalartūva
to worship scattering flowers made of gold and silver, Ārūr of Civaṉ who lends beauty to it.
iṉpam ākumē
you will obtin happiness in this world and eternal bliss hereafter.
{1:91}__4+
{$} uyyal uṟuvīrkāḷ
people who want to save yourselves!
aiyaṉ ārūraikkaiyiṉāl toḻa
to worship with joined hands Ārūr belonging to Civaṉ the master.
naiyum viṉaitāṉē
the karmam-s will perish of their own accord.
{1:91}__5+
{$} piṇṭam aṟuppīrkāḷ
people who want to end completely being born in human bodies!
aṇṭam ārūraikkaṇṭu malar tūva
if you scatter flowers obtining sight of Ārūr of the god Civaṉ.
viṇṭuviṉaipōm
Your Karmams will leave you being separated from you.
{1:91}__6+
{$} pācam aṟuppīrkāḷ
People who cut at the root of bondage which has been with you for a long time.
īcaṉ aṇi ārūr vācamalartūva
to worship with fragrant flowers beautiful Ārūr of Civan
nēcam ākum
your piety will increase.
{1:91}__7+
{$} veyyaviṉai tīra
(People of the world!) if you want the cruel Karmams to leave you.
aiyaṉ aṇi ārūr ceyya malartūva
to worship, scattering red flowers, the beautiful Ārūr of our master.
vaiyam umatu ām
you shall become the sole monarch of this world.
{1:91}__8+
{$} arakkaṉ āṇmaiyai nerukkiṉāṉ ārūr karattiṉāl toḻa
to worship with joined hands Ārūr of Civaṉ who squeezed the strength of Arakkaṉ
tiruttam ākum
one will become perfect.
{1:91}__9+
{$} tuḷḷum iruvarkkum vaḷḷal ārūrai uḷḷum avartammēl
on those who always meditate upon Ārūr of Civan who showed his benevolence towards Māl and Piramaṉ who were hughty, each thinking that he was greater than the other.
viḷḷum viṉaitāṉē
Karmams will get separated of their own accord.
{1:91}__10+
{$} kaṭukkoḷ cīvarai aṭakkiṉāṉ ārūr eṭuttu vāḻttuvār
those who prise loudly in a high pitch of voice Ārūr of Civaṉ who controlled camaṇar who eat gall-nuts, and buddhists who cover their bodies with a blanket by name, ciVaram.
vēṭkai viṭuppar
will give up all desires.
{1:91}__11+
{$} cīrūr campantaṉ ārūraic coṉṉa pār ūr pāṭalār pērār iṉpamē
those who are able to recite the songs which have spread throught Tamiḻ Nāṭu and which were composed on Ārūr by Campantaṉ whose eminence has spread everywhere, will obtin happiness which will never leave them.

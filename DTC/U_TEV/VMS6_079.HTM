[[Note: Taliyālaṅkāṭu is the place where Pāṇṭiyaṉ Neṭuñceḻiyaṉ defeated Cōḻaṉ and Cēraṉ and five chieftins when he was a young boy; it is mentioned in Puṟam, 21-16; Maturikkāñci, 127; Akam; 36-14, 175_(10-11)]]

cārātē cāla nāḷ pōkkiṉēṉ
I wasted very many days without going near.
toṇṭarkaṭkut tū neṟiyāy niṉṟāṉ taṉṉai
Civaṉ who stood as the faultless path for his devotees.
cūḻ narakil vīḻāmē kāppāṉ taṉṉai
who protects people from falling into the hell which envelops them.
aṇṭattukku appālaikku appālāṉai
who is beyond life which is beyond the universe.
ātirai nāḷ ātaritta ammāṉ taṉṉai
the god who desired Ātiri as his favourite star.
muṇṭattiṉ muḷaittu eḻunta tīyāṉāṉai
who is the fire that was born, and rose, in his forehead
[[Fire was born in the frontal eye of Civaṉ.]]
mū uruvattu ōr uruvāy mutalāy niṉṟa taṇṭattiṉ talaiyālaṅkāṭaṉ taṉṉai
who is in Taliyālaṅkāṭu, who is in the form of a liṅkam, who is the cuse of the three forms, Ayaṉ, Ari, and Araṉ, and one of the forms distinct as Araṉ.

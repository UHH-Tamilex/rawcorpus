kaṭavūr mayāṉattup periya perumāṉ aṭikaḷ
the supreme god in Kaṭavūr Mayāṉam.
maru ār koṉṟai mati cūṭi
having worn a crescent and fragrant koṉṟi flowers.
māṇikkattiṉ malai pōla varuvār viṭaimēl mātōṭu makiḻntu pūtappaṭai cūḻa
will come riding on a bull like the mountin of a ruby, with a lady, joy fully to be surrounded by an army of putams.
tirumāl piramaṉ intiraṟkum tēvar nākar tāṉavarkkum perumāṉ
is the Lord of ((big deities like)) Tirumāl, Piramaṉ, Intiraṉ, and ((other smaller)) tēvar, inhabitants of the serpent world and tāṉavar (acurar)

naṟaicēr malar aiṅkaṇaiyāṉai nayaṉattīyāl poṭiceyta iṟaiyār āvar
is the supreme god who reduced to ashes by the fire issuing from his frontal eye, the cupid who has arrows of five flowers which have honey in them.
ellārkkum illai eṉṉātu aruḷceyvār
grants to all what they wish without saying I have nothing to give
paṟai, ārmuḻavam pāṭṭōṭu payilum toṇṭar payil kaṭavūrp piṟaiyār caṭaiyār
has a caṭi on which he wears a crescent and dwells in Kaṭavūr where devotees who practise on mudavūr and other leather instruments with vocal music, are crowding.

kottu ārkoṉṟai maticūṭi
wearing koṉṟi which blossoms in bunches, and a crescent.
kōḷ nākaṅkaḷ pūṇāka
wearing as ornaments cobras which are capable of killing.
mattayāṉai uripōrttu
covering himself with the skin of a rutting elephant.

has ornaments of the hog's tusk, and the shell of a tortoise as a tāli.
patticeytu pāriṭaṅkaḷ pāṭiāṭappalikoḷḷum pittar
is Civaṉ who receives alms when the pūtams, cherish him with love, sing and dance.

{$} māṭammalku kaṭavūril maṟaiyōr ēttu mayāṉattup pīṭai tīra aṭiyārukku aruḷum perumāṉ aṭikaḷ cīr nāṭi
desirings earnestly the fame of the supreme god who grants his grace to remove the afflictions of devotees and who dwells in the mayāṉam in Kaṭavūr where mansions are prosperous and the brahmins prise him.
nāval ārūraṉ nampicoṉṉa naṟṟamiḻkaḷ pāṭum aṭiyār kēṭpār mēl pāvam āṉa paṟaiyumē
the sins of those who sing the good Tamiḻ verses composed by the eminent among men, who is a native of Nāvalūr and who has the name of Ārūraṉ, and hear them sung, will disappear by degrees.

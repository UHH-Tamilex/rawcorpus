• patikam: [2:22]
  • talam: tiruk kuṭavāyil
  • paṇ: _intaḷam
  • VMS: Vol.9, p.227

*1
tikaḻum tirumāloṭu nāṉmukaṉum
pukaḻum perumāṉ; _aṭiyār pukala,
makiḻum perumāṉ---kuṭavāyil maṉṉi
nikaḻum peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*2
_ōṭum natiyum, matiyōṭu, _urakam,
cūṭum caṭaiyaṉ; viṭai tolkoṭimēl
kūṭum kuḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
nīṭum peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*3
kalaiyāṉ; maṟaiyāṉ; kaṉal _ēntu kaiyāṉ;
malaiyāḷ_avaḷ pākam makiḻnta pirāṉ;
kolai _ār cilaiyāṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
nilai _ār peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*4
culavum caṭaiyāṉ; cuṭukāṭu _iṭamā,
nala meṉmulaiyāḷ nakaiceyya, naṭam
kulavum kuḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
nilavum peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*5
_eṉtaṉ _uḷam mēvi _irunta pirāṉ;
kaṉṟaṉ; maṇi pōl miṭaṟaṉ; kayilaik-
kuṉṟaṉ; kuḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
niṉṟa peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*6
_alai cēr puṉalaṉ(ṉ); _aṉalaṉ(ṉ); _amalaṉ;
talai cēr paliyaṉ; caturaṉ; vitirum
kolai cēr paṭaiyaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
nilai cēr peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*7
_aṟai _ār kaḻalaṉ(ṉ); _aḻalaṉ(ṉ); _iyaliṉ
paṟai yāḻ muḻavum maṟai pāṭa, naṭam
kuṟaiyā _aḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
niṟai _ār peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*8
varai _ār tiraḷtōḷ _arakkaṉ maṭiya(v)
varai _ār _orkālviral vaitta pirāṉ---
varai _ār matil cūḻ kuṭavāyil maṉṉum
varai _ār peruṅkōyil makiḻntavaṉē.

*9
poṉ _oppavaṉum, puyal _oppavaṉum,
taṉ _oppu _aṟiyāt taḻal_āy nimirntāṉ;
kol-nal paṭaiyāṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
maṉṉum peruṅkōyil makiḻntavaṉē.

*10
veyiliṉ nilaiyār, viri pōrvaiyiṉār,
payilum(m) _uraiyē pakar pāvikaḷpāl
kuyilaṉ; kuḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
_uyarum peruṅkōyil _uyarntavaṉē.

*11
kaṭuvāy mali nīr kuṭavāyiltaṉil
neṭu mā peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉai,
taṭam _ār pukalit tamiḻ _ār virakaṉ,
vaṭam _ār tamiḻ vallavar nallavarē.

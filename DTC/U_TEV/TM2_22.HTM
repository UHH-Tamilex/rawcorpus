  • talam: tiruk kuṭavāyil
  • paṇ: _intaḷam
  • VMS: Vol.9, p.227

*1
tikaḻum tirumāloṭu nāṉmukaṉum
pukaḻum perumāṉ; _aṭiyār pukala,
makiḻum perumāṉ---kuṭavāyil maṉṉi
nikaḻum peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*2
_ōṭum natiyum, matiyōṭu, _urakam,
cūṭum caṭiyaṉ; viṭi tolkoṭimēl
kūṭum kuḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
nīṭum peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*3
kaliyāṉ; maṟiyāṉ; kaṉal _ēntu kiyāṉ;
maliyāḷ_avaḷ pākam makiḻnta pirāṉ;
koli _ār ciliyāṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
nili _ār peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*4
culavum caṭiyāṉ; cuṭukāṭu _iṭamā,
nala meṉmuliyāḷ nakiceyya, naṭam
kulavum kuḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
nilavum peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*5
_eṉtaṉ _uḷam mēvi _irunta pirāṉ;
kaṉṟaṉ; maṇi pōl miṭaṟaṉ; kayilik-
kuṉṟaṉ; kuḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
niṉṟa peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*6
_ali cēr puṉalaṉ(ṉ); _aṉalaṉ(ṉ); _amalaṉ;
tali cēr paliyaṉ; caturaṉ; vitirum
koli cēr paṭiyaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
nili cēr peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*7
_aṟi _ār kaḻalaṉ(ṉ); _aḻalaṉ(ṉ); _iyaliṉ
paṟi yāḻ muḻavum maṟi pāṭa, naṭam
kuṟiyā _aḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
niṟi _ār peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉē.

*8
vari _ār tiraḷtōḷ _arakkaṉ maṭiya(v)
vari _ār _orkālviral vitta pirāṉ---
vari _ār matil cūḻ kuṭavāyil maṉṉum
vari _ār peruṅkōyil makiḻntavaṉē.

*9
poṉ _oppavaṉum, puyal _oppavaṉum,
taṉ _oppu _aṟiyāt taḻal_āy nimirntāṉ;
kol-nal paṭiyāṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
maṉṉum peruṅkōyil makiḻntavaṉē.

*10
veyiliṉ niliyār, viri pōrviyiṉār,
payilum(m) _uriyē pakar pāvikaḷpāl
kuyilaṉ; kuḻakaṉ---kuṭavāyiltaṉil
_uyarum peruṅkōyil _uyarntavaṉē.

*11
kaṭuvāy mali nīr kuṭavāyiltaṉil
neṭu mā peruṅkōyil nilāyavaṉi,
taṭam _ār pukalit tamiḻ _ār virakaṉ,
vaṭam _ār tamiḻ vallavar nallavarē.

• patikam: [7:30]
  • talam: tiruk karuppaṟiyalūr
  • paṇ: naṭṭarākam
  • VMS: Vol.3, p.111

*1
cimmāntu, cimpuḷittu, cintaiyiṉil vaittu _ukantu tiṟampā vaṇṇam-
kaimmāviṉ_urivai pōrttu _umai veruvak kaṇṭāṉai; karuppaṟiyalūr,
koym māviṉ malarccōlaik kuyil pāṭa mayil _āṭum, kokuṭikkōyil
_emmāṉai;-maṉattiṉāl niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*2
nī(ṟ)ṟu _ārum mēṉiyarāy niṉaivārtam _uḷḷattē niṟaintu tōṉṟum
kāṟṟāṉai, tīyāṉai, katirāṉai, matiyāṉai, karuppaṟiyalūr-
kūṟṟāṉai, kūṟṟu _utaittuk kōlvaḷaiyāḷ _avaḷōṭum-kokuṭikkōyil
_ēṟṟāṉai, maṉattiṉāl niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*3
muṭṭāmē nāḷtōṟum nīr mūḻki, pūp paṟittu, mūṉṟupōtum
kaṭṭu _ārnta _iṇṭai koṇṭu, _aṭic cērttum _antaṇartam karuppaṟiyalūr-
koṭṭu _āṭṭup pāṭṭu_āki niṉṟāṉai, kuḻakaṉai,-kokuṭikkōyil
_eṭṭu_āṉamūrttiyai, niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*4
viruntu_āya colmālaikoṇṭu _ētti, viṉai pōka, vēlitōṟum
karun tāḷa vāḻaimēl ceṅkaṉikaḷ tēṉ coriyum karuppaṟiyalūr-
kuruntu_āya muḷ_eyiṟṟuk kōlvaḷaiyāḷ _avaḷōṭum-kokuṭikkōyil
_iruntāṉai maṉattiṉāl niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*5
poṭi _ēṟu tirumēṉip perumāṉai, poṅku _aravakkaccaiyāṉai,
kaṭi nāṟum pūm poykaik kayal vāḷai kutikoḷḷum karuppaṟiyalūr-
_aṭi _ēṟu kaḻalāṉai, niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*6
poyyāta vāymaiyāl, poṭi pūcip pōṟṟu_icaittu, pūcai ceytu,
kaiyiṉāl _eri _ōmpi maṟai vaḷarkkum _antaṇartam karuppaṟiyalūr-
koy _ulām malarccōlaik kuyil kūva mayil _ālum---kokuṭikkōyil
_aiyaṉai _eṉ maṉattiṉāl ṉiṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*7
ceṭi koḷ nōy _uḷḷaḷavum tīviṉaiyum tīrntu_oḻiyac cintaiceymiṉ!
kaṭi koḷ pūn taṭam maṇṭik karumēti kaṇpaṭukkum karuppaṟiyalūr-
koṭi koḷ pū nuṇ_iṭaiyāḷ kōlvaḷaiyāḷ _avaḷōṭum-kokuṭikkōyil
_aṭikaḷai _eṉ maṉattiṉāl niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*8
paṟaiyāta valviṉaikaḷ paṟaintoḻiya, pal-nāḷum pāṭi _āṭi-
kaṟai _ārnta kaṇṭattaṉ, _eṇtōḷaṉ, mukkaṇṇaṉ,-karuppaṟiyalūr,
kuṟaiyāta maṟai nāvar kuṟṟēval _oḻiyāta, kokuṭikkōyil
_uṟaivāṉai, maṉattiṉāl niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*9
caṅku _ēntu kaiyāṉum tāmaraiyiṉmēlāṉum taṉmai kāṇāk
kaṅku _ārnta vārcaṭaikaḷ _uṭaiyāṉai, viṭaiyāṉai, karuppaṟiyalūr-
koṅku _ārnta poḻil-cōlai cūḻ kaṉikaḷpala _utirkkum-kokuṭikkōyil
_em kōṉai, maṉattiṉāl niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*10
paṇ tāḻ _iṉ_icai muralap pal-nāḷum pāvittup pāṭi _āṭik
kaṇṭārtam kaṇ kuḷirum kaḷik kamukam pūñcōlaik karuppaṟiyalūr-
kuṇṭāṭum camaṇarum cākkiyarum puṟamkūṟum-kokuṭikkōyil
_eṇtōḷ _emperumāṉai niṉaintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*11
kalai malinta teṉpulavar kaṟṟōrtam _iṭar tīrkkum karuppaṟiyalūr---
kulai malinta kōḷ-teṅku maṭṭu _oḻukum pūñcōlai---kokuṭikkōyil
_ilai malinta maḻuvāṉai, maṉattiṉāl _aṉpu ceytu, _iṉpam _eyti,
malai malinta tōḷ _ūraṉ---vaṉappakai_appaṉ---_uraitta vaṇtamiḻkaḷē!

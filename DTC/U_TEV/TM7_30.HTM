• patikam: [7:30]
  • talam: tiruk karuppaṟiyalūr
  • paṇ: naṭṭarākam
  • VMS: Vol.3, p.111

*1
cimmāntu, cimpuḷittu, cintiyiṉil vittu _ukantu tiṟampā vaṇṇam-
kimmāviṉ_urivi pōrttu _umi veruvak kaṇṭāṉi; karuppaṟiyalūr,
koym māviṉ malarccōlik kuyil pāṭa mayil _āṭum, kokuṭikkōyil
_emmāṉi;-maṉattiṉāl niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*2
nī(ṟ)ṟu _ārum mēṉiyarāy niṉivārtam _uḷḷattē niṟintu tōṉṟum
kāṟṟāṉi, tīyāṉi, katirāṉi, matiyāṉi, karuppaṟiyalūr-
kūṟṟāṉi, kūṟṟu _utittuk kōlvaḷiyāḷ _avaḷōṭum-kokuṭikkōyil
_ēṟṟāṉi, maṉattiṉāl niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*3
muṭṭāmē nāḷtōṟum nīr mūḻki, pūp paṟittu, mūṉṟupōtum
kaṭṭu _ārnta _iṇṭi koṇṭu, _aṭic cērttum _antaṇartam karuppaṟiyalūr-
koṭṭu _āṭṭup pāṭṭu_āki niṉṟāṉi, kuḻakaṉi,-kokuṭikkōyil
_eṭṭu_āṉamūrttiyi, niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*4
viruntu_āya colmālikoṇṭu _ētti, viṉi pōka, vēlitōṟum
karun tāḷa vāḻimēl ceṅkaṉikaḷ tēṉ coriyum karuppaṟiyalūr-
kuruntu_āya muḷ_eyiṟṟuk kōlvaḷiyāḷ _avaḷōṭum-kokuṭikkōyil
_iruntāṉi maṉattiṉāl niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*5
poṭi _ēṟu tirumēṉip perumāṉi, poṅku _aravakkacciyāṉi,
kaṭi nāṟum pūm poykik kayal vāḷi kutikoḷḷum karuppaṟiyalūr-
_aṭi _ēṟu kaḻalāṉi, niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*6
poyyāta vāymiyāl, poṭi pūcip pōṟṟu_icittu, pūci ceytu,
kiyiṉāl _eri _ōmpi maṟi vaḷarkkum _antaṇartam karuppaṟiyalūr-
koy _ulām malarccōlik kuyil kūva mayil _ālum---kokuṭikkōyil
_aiyaṉi _eṉ maṉattiṉāl ṉiṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*7
ceṭi koḷ nōy _uḷḷaḷavum tīviṉiyum tīrntu_oḻiyac cinticeymiṉ!
kaṭi koḷ pūn taṭam maṇṭik karumēti kaṇpaṭukkum karuppaṟiyalūr-
koṭi koḷ pū nuṇ_iṭiyāḷ kōlvaḷiyāḷ _avaḷōṭum-kokuṭikkōyil
_aṭikaḷi _eṉ maṉattiṉāl niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*8
paṟiyāta valviṉikaḷ paṟintoḻiya, pal-nāḷum pāṭi _āṭi-
kaṟi _ārnta kaṇṭattaṉ, _eṇtōḷaṉ, mukkaṇṇaṉ,-karuppaṟiyalūr,
kuṟiyāta maṟi nāvar kuṟṟēval _oḻiyāta, kokuṭikkōyil
_uṟivāṉi, maṉattiṉāl niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*9
caṅku _ēntu kiyāṉum tāmariyiṉmēlāṉum taṉmi kāṇāk
kaṅku _ārnta vārcaṭikaḷ _uṭiyāṉi, viṭiyāṉi, karuppaṟiyalūr-
koṅku _ārnta poḻil-cōli cūḻ kaṉikaḷpala _utirkkum-kokuṭikkōyil
_em kōṉi, maṉattiṉāl niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*10
paṇ tāḻ _iṉ_ici muralap pal-nāḷum pāvittup pāṭi _āṭik
kaṇṭārtam kaṇ kuḷirum kaḷik kamukam pūñcōlik karuppaṟiyalūr-
kuṇṭāṭum camaṇarum cākkiyarum puṟamkūṟum-kokuṭikkōyil
_eṇtōḷ _emperumāṉi niṉintapōtu _avar namakku _iṉiya_āṟē!

*11
kali malinta teṉpulavar kaṟṟōrtam _iṭar tīrkkum karuppaṟiyalūr---
kuli malinta kōḷ-teṅku maṭṭu _oḻukum pūñcōli---kokuṭikkōyil
_ili malinta maḻuvāṉi, maṉattiṉāl _aṉpu ceytu, _iṉpam _eyti,
mali malinta tōḷ _ūraṉ---vaṉappaki_appaṉ---_uritta vaṇtamiḻkaḷē!

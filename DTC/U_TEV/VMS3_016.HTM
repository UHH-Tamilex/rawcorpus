{3:16}__1+
{$} nam koḷḷikkāṭar (ē)
our Lord in Koḷḷikkāṭu
nīṟu pūci niṉṟu iṇaṅkuvār pēykaḷōṭu
will be in the company of pēy-s having smeared himself with sacred ash.
niṇam paṭu cuṭalaiyil iṭuvar mānaṭam
will perform the great dance in the cremation ground where there is fat.
(1) uṇaṅkal veṇtalaitaṉil uṇpar āyiṉum
though he eats his food in a dried white skull.
kuṇam peritu uṭaiyar
possesses exceedingly good qualities.
[[Variant reading: (1) uṇaṅku]]
{3:16}__2+
{$} koḷḷikkāṭar
[[see 1st verse]]
1. āṟṟa nal aṭiyiṇai 2. alar koṇṭu ēttuvāṉ cāṟṟiya antaṇaṉ takuti kaṇṭu a(n) nāḷ
seeing his fitness to be saved on that day when came the end of the brahmin (mārkaṇtēyaṉ) who came to worship his good feet devoutly with flowers, cried, `grant me protection from the god of death, who is coming near'.
māṟṟalaṉ āki muṉ aṭarttu vantu aṇai kūṟṟiṉai utaittaṉar
Civaṉ kicked and destroyed the god of death who approached near him to kill him, being unpreventable.
[[kūṟṟu māṟṟalaṉ: māṟṟaruṅ kūṟṟam cāṟṟiya perumaiyum tolkāppiyam, puṟattiṇiīyal, 19);
māṟṟēṉ eṉa vantakālaṉai ōlamiṭa aṭartta, kōṟṟēṉ (Kōviyār, 150).]]
[[Variant reading: 1. āṟṟal nal, 2. alarkoṭu]]
{3:16}__3+
{$} koḷḷikkāṭar (ē)
see 1st verse.
attaku vāṉavarkkāka mālviṭam vaittavar maṇipurai kaṇṭattiṉuḷē
placed the big poison inside the neck to resemble the sapphire in order to save such great celestials.
mattamum vaṉṉiyum malinta ceṉṉimēl kottu alar koṉṟaiyar
wears flowers of koṉṟi which blossoms in bunches on the head which has datura flowers and leaves of indian mesquit, profusely.
{3:16}__4+
{$} koḷḷikkāṭar (ē)
see 1st verse.
pāva(ṇ)ṇam mēvu col mālaiyil pala nāvaṇam koḷkaiyiṉ naviṉṟa ceykaiyar
has the act of singing to be appropriate to the tongue, many songs which are like garlands of words according to the rules prosody.
āvaṇam koṇṭu emai āḷvar āyiṉum
though he admits us as his protege by title-deed of bondage.
kōvaṇam koḷkaiyar
has an idea of wearing the men's loin cloth.
{3:16}__5+
{$} koḷḷikkāṭar (ē)
see 1st verse.
vār aṇivaṉa mulai maṅkaiyāḷoṭum
being united with a young lady who wears a bodice on her beautiful breasts.
cīr aṇi tiruvuruttikaḻnta ceṉṉiyar
has a head on which the holy and of famous form of Kanki is staying.
nār aṇi cilaitaṉāl naṇukalār eyil kūr eri koḷuviṉar
set an excessive fire to the forts of the enemies with a bow which was adorned by a bow-string.
{3:16}__6+
{$} koḷḷikkāṭar(ē)
see 1st verse.
pañcutōy mellaṭip pāvaiyāḷoṭum mañcutōy kayilaiyuḷ makiḻvar, nāḷtoṟum
will be happy always in the mountin, Kayili on which clouds settle, along with a beautiful lady who has soft feet on which paste made from red-cotton is pinted.
veñciṉam maruppoṭum viraiya vantu aṭai kuñcaram urittaṉar
flayed an elephant with tusks which came quickly and reached him with cruel anger
{3:16}__7+
{$} koḷḷikkāṭar(ē)
see 1st verse.
iṟai uṟu varivaḷai icaikaḷ pāṭiṭa
as the lady who wears on the wrist bangles with lines sings.
aṟai uṟu kaḻal aṭi ārkka āṭuvar
will dance to make the Kaḻal worn on the leg which can produce sound make a big sound.
ciṟai aṟu viripuṉal ceṉṉiyiṉ micaik kuṟai uṟu matiyiṉar
wears a crescent on the head where the spreading water has been restrined.
{3:16}__8+
{$} koḷḷikkāṭar (ē)
see 1st verse.
eṭuttaṉaṉ kayilaiyai iyal valiyiṉāl; aṭarttaṉar tiruviralāl 1. alaṟiṭa
when irāvāṇaṉ lifted the mountin, Kayili by his natural strength, Civaṉ pressed him down by his holy toe to make him roar.
paṭuttaṉar' eṉṟu avaṉ pāṭal pāṭalum, koṭuttaṉar koṟṟavāḷ
when he sang songs thinking `Civaṉ destroyed me' he granted Irāvaṇaṉ a sword that brings victory.
[[Variant reading: 1. alaṟṟiṭa]]
{3:16}__9+
{$} ayaṉ muṭi tēṭiṉār, mālum cēvaṭi nāṭiṉār; avar eṉṟum 1. naṇukakiṟṟilar
Ayaṉ searched for the head; and the great Māl searched for the lotus-red feet: they had not the capability and could never come anywhere near them.
parivoṭu pāṭiṉār cittamum kūṭiṉār pattarkku aruḷ ceyvār
he will grant his grace to those devotees who sang his prises with love and who had concentrated minds, being very near them.
[[Variant reading: 1. naṇṇukiṟṟilar]]
{3:16}__10+
{$} koḷḷikkāṭar(ē)
see 1st verse.
nāṭi niṉṟu aṟivu il nāṇilikaḷ cākkiyar ōṭi muṉ ōtiya uraikaḷ mey ala
the words of the shameless camaṇar who had no intelligence and the cākkiyar (buddhists) who ran and preached in former times do not contin any truth.
nāṉ maṟai pāṭuvar; payiṉṟa mātoṭum kūṭuvar tiruuru
chanting musically the four vētams he is united in his holy body with a lady with whom he is intimate.
{3:16}__11+
{$} kuṟṟam il perum pukaḻk koḷḷikkāṭarai
on Civaṉ in Koḷḷikkāṭu who has great and faultless fame.
naṟṟavar kāḻiyuḷ ñāṉacampantaṉ col tamiḻ iṉṉicai mālai cōrvu iṉṟik kaṟṟavar
those who have learnt without forgetfulness the garland of Tamiḻ verses of sweet music composed by Ñāṉacamapantaṉ in Kāḻi where people who want to get rid of the cycle of birth and death and attin salvation, live
[[PP: naṟṟavam' - vīṭu peṟac ceyyum tavam (Cīvakacintāmani, 77)]]

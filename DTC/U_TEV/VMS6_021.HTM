ākkūril tāṉ tōṉṟi appaṉār
the father who is self-existing in Ākkūr
muṭittāmarai aṇinta mūrtti (pōlum)
has a body which has adorned its head with the lotus flower
mūvulakum tām āki niṉṟār (pōlum)
has himself pervaded the three worlds.
kaṭittāmarai ēynta kaṇṇār (pōlum)
has eyes that can be compared to the fragrant lotus.
kallalaku pāṇi payiṉṟār (pōlum)
practised dancing to the measurement of time of the instrument Kallalaku
koṭittāmaraikkāṭē nāṭum toṇṭar kuṟṟēval tām makiḻnta kuḻakar (pōlum)
is the youth who felt happy with the menial services rendered by the devotees who search for the abundance of the lotus flower which is a water creeper
[[God accepts the service of the devotees not minding the value of the thing offered, but the love with which it is given.]]
aṭittāmarai malar mēl vaittār (pōlum)
placed the lotus of his feet on the heart which is like the lotus bud.
[[All the pōlum in this verse and the following verses are expletives]]

ōtiṟṟu oru nūlum illai (pōlum)
there is no work that Civaṉ learnt in the ordinary way, tught by a teacher ( He learnt everything without being tught)
uṇarappāṭatatu oṉṟu illai (pōlum)
there is nothing which has not been understood by him (He understands everything without anybody's help).

wore in his ear a men's ear-ring to shine brilliantly.
kavalaip piṟappu iṭumpai kāppār (pōlum)
will save us from the suffering of birth which has cares and anxieties
vētattōṭu āṟu aṅkam coṉṉār (pōlum)
revealed along with the vētams the six aṅkams.
viṭam cūḻntu iruṇṭa miṭaṟṟār(pōlum)
has a neck that is black being surrounded by the poison.
ātikku aḷavu āki niṉṟār pōlum
he is the measure of all things (Civaṉ is the origin of all other things, but he has no origin for himself).

[[ēkācam upper garment;]]
[[PP: ēkācam iṭṭa iruṭikaḷ
(tiruvācakam tiruvuntiyār, 20)]]
mēkācam kaṭṭaḻitta veḷḷi mālai puṉal ār caṭai muṭimēl puṉaintār (pōlum)
adorned on the caṭi coiled into a crown on which there is plenty of water, a garland of white flowers which completely destroyed the brilliance of the cloud.
[[mēkācam = mēka + ācam; ācam is a corruption of ācam, which is itself a corruption of the sanskrit word `hācam'; the smile of the cloud is its lightning; it is compared to the colour of the caṭi]]
mākācam ākiya veṇṇīrum tīyum, matiyum, mati piṟanta viṇṇum, maṇṇum, ākācam eṉṟa ivaiyum āṉār (pōlum)
is all these things i.e. the white water which is born in the sky, fire, moon, the sky where the moon rises, the earth and the space.
[[mākācam the vast sky; in kācam the first letter is elided;
kācam: ākupeyar, vicumpiṟṟuḷivīḻiṉ allāl (Kaiṟaḷ, 16);
vicumpu: ākupeyar (do. parimēlaḻakar);
vaṟitu nilai iya kāyamum (puṟam, 20-4, 30-4)]]

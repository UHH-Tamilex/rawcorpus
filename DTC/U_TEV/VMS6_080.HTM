nāṉē ceṉṟu aṭaintēṉ
I went and approached
pārāṉai
Civaṉ who is the earth
[[nāṉē:
ē means I who could not easily approach god]]
(1) pāriṉatu payaṉ āṉāṉai
who is the produce got(ten) from sowing seeds.
paṭaippu ākip pal uyirkkum parivōṉ taṉṉai
being himself the function of creation and who is affectionate towards all living beings.
aṭiyār taṅkaṭku ārāta iṉṉamutai
who is the sweet and in satiate nectar to his devotees.
aṉaittu ulakum āṉāṉai
who is all the worlds.
amarar kōṉai
the chief of the immortals.
kār ārum kaṇṭaṉai
who has a neck resembling the sable cloud;
[[PP: koṇṭalaṅ kaṇṭaṉ
(Poṉvaṇṇattantāti, 27)]]
kayilai vēntai
who is the ruler of Kayili.
karutuvār maṉattāṉai
who occupies the minds of those who meditate on him.
kālaṟ ceṟṟa cīrāṉai
who has the fame of destroying Kālaṉ (god of death)
celvaṉai
who is the wealth of the devotees.
tirumāṟ pēṟṟu em cempavaḷak kuṉṟiṉai
who is like the hill made of the red coral, belonging to us, and who is in Tirumāṟpēṟu.
[[Variant readings: (1) pāriṉ payaṉāṉāṉai (2) paṭaippāṉaip pal uyirkkum]]

cōti āki
being the light.
tūṇṭu ariya cuṭar āki
and being the lamp that does not require trimming.
tuḷakku il vāṉmēl muḷaikkiṉṟa katir matiyum aravum oṉṟi muḻaṅku oli nīrk kaṅkaiyoṭu mūvātu eṉṟum tiḷak kiṉṟa caṭaiyāṉai
who has a cati in which the crescent of bright rays which rises in the sky which does not quake a cobra both being united, stay always without ageing along with the Kaṅki in the form of water which produces a big sound.

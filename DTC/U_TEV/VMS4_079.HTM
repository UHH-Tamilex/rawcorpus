taiyalār tam māṉam kāppatu āki
to preserve the chastity of women.
valaiyuḷ āḻntu
sinking low into their tricks.
ammāṉai, amutaṉ taṉṉai, ātiyai
Civaṉ, who is the father, the nectar, the cuse of all things.
antam āya cemmāṉa oḷikoḷ mēṉic cintaiyuḷ oṉṟi niṉṟa emmāṉai
and our Lord, who is united with my mind and who has a beautiful body which has the colour of the evening red sky.
niṉaiya māṭṭēṉ
I am incapable of meditating on him.
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
[see 1st verse of previous decade]
[[cemmāṉam: cevvāṉam;
cemmāṉattu oḷiyaṉṉa mēṉiyāṉ kāṇ (nāvukkaracar, tiruvārūr (12) 1) and its notes]]
{4:79}__2+
{$}
makkaḷē, maṇanta (1) tāram avvayiṟṟavarai ōmpum cikkuḷē aḻunti
sunk in the entanglement of protecting wedded wife, children born and their children.
īcaṉ (2) tiṟampaṭēṉ
I do not have any connection with the path of Civaṉ.
tavam atu ōrēṉ
I do not consider attentively doing penance.
koppuḷē pōlattōṉṟi
appearing like bubbles in the water.
ataṉuḷē maṟaiyakkaṇṭum
even after seeing that bubble to vanish into that;
[[cf. paṭumaḻai mokkuḷiṉ, palkālum tōṉṟik keṭum itu ōr yākkai (nālaṭiyār, 27);
miṉṉum mokkuḷum eṉa naṉivīyiṉum vīyum (cīvakacintāmaṇi, 2754)]]
ikkaḷēparattai ōmpa eṉceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
to what purpose was I born to nourish this body?
[[Variant reading: (1) tāram valvayiṟṟavarai (2) tiṟampaṭṭēṉ]]
{4:79}__3+
{$}
kūḻaiyēṉ ākamāṭṭēṉ
I am incapable of doing the duties of a devotee;
[[kūttaṉukku āṭpaṭṭu iruppataṉṟō namtam kūḻaimaiyē (nāvukkaracar, Kōyil (6) 5)]]
koṭuviṉaikkuḻiyil vīḻntu
falling into the pit of cruel acts.
ēḻiṉ iṉṉicaiyiṉālum iṟaivaṉai ēttamāṭṭēṉ
I will not prise, Civaṉ with sweet music having the seven diatonic scales.
māḻai oṇ kaṇṇil nalla maṭantaimār tamakkum pollēṉ
I am ugly looking to the good-natured ladies who have bright and young eyes;
[[māḻai oṇkaṇ paravaiyait tantu āṇṭāṉai (cuntarar, tiruvārūr (4) 10);
cf. see 2nd verse of previous decade]]
ēḻaiyēṉ āki nāḷum
being always an ignorant person.
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.
{4:79}__4+
{$}
eṉ muṉṉaiviṉaiyiṉālē
as I experience the fruits of my acts done in previous births.
nāṉ mūrttiyai niṉaiyamāṭṭēṉ
I am incapable of meditating on Civaṉ who has a visible form.
piṉṉair
after I realised my mistake of not worshipping Civaṉ and knew there is no other benefit in the birth.
pittaṉākip pitaṟṟuvaṉ pētaiyēṉ nāṉ
being attached to the feet of Civaṉ, I who am ignorant will babble incoherantly.
eṉṉuḷē maṉṉi niṉṟa cīrmaiyatu āyiṉāṉai eṉṉuḷē niṉaiya māṭṭēṉ
I am incapable of meditating within myself internally on Civaṉ who has the eminence of remining permanently within me.
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.

piṟainutaṟ pētaimātar peyvaḷaiyārkkum (1) allēṉ
I am not in favour with women who wear bangles and have forehead like the crescent.
kāmaram kaṟṟumillēṉ
I have not learnt music (cf. verse of previous decade).
kaṟai aṇi kaṇṭaṉ taṉṉai, maṟai navil nāviṉāṉai maṉṉi niṉṟu iṟaiñci nāḷum iṟaiyēyum ēttamāṭṭēṉ
I am incapable of prising even for a while dily prostrating before Civaṉ remining steadfast in my devotion, Civaṉ who adds beauty to the poison by keeping it in his neck and who is always practising chanting of the vētams.
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.

aivar kaḷvar
the five organs of sense which are like robbers.
vaḷaittu niṉṟu
remining surrounding me.
vantu eṉai naṭukkam ceyya
draw near me and make me tremble.
taḷaittu vaittu
trying the feet.
ulaiyai ēṟṟi
setting a pot of water on the fire for cooking rice.
taḻal eri maṭutta nīril
in the water which was heated by burning fire.
tiḷaittu niṉṟu āṭukiṉṟa āmaipōl teḷivu ilātēṉ
I who have no clarity like the tortoise which bathes in the water without ceasing.
iḷaittu niṉṟu āṭukiṉṟēṉ
I am enjoying this life, being exhusted.
eṉ ceyvāṉ tōṉṟiṉēṉ (ē)
see 1st verse.

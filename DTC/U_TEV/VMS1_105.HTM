{1:105}__1+
{$} pāṭalaṉ nāṉ maṟaiyaṉ
Civaṉ revealed the four Vētam-s, which are recited with proper accent.
paṭi paṭṭa kōlattaṉ
has a beautiful form which can be compared to others.
tiṅkaḷ cūṭalaṉ
wears on the head a crescent.
mū ilaiya cūlam valaṉ ēnti
holding in the victorious battle-field, a trident of three blades;
[[PP: veḷḷēṟu valavayiṉ uyariyaveṟṟikkaḷattē veḷḷiya ēṟṟiṉai veṟṟikkoṭiyāka eṭutta
(Murukāṟṟuppaṭi, 151-152)]]
valam may also mean victory;
[[PP_EXT: ēṟṟuvalaṉ uyariya:
āṉēṟṟai veṟṟiyāka uyartta
(Puṟam, 56-1, old commentary)]]
kūṭalar mū eyilum eri uṇṇakkūr erikoṇṭu
dischanging an arrow of excessive fire to make the five consume all the three forts of the enemies.
elli āṭalaṉ, ātiraiyaṉ, ārūr amarntāṉē
Civaṉ, who dwells in Ārūr, has as his star Ātiri, and dances at night.
{1:105}__2+
{$} cōlaiyil vaṇṭiṉaṅkaḷ curumpōṭu icaimurala
in the garden the swarms of inferior bees hum like music along with curumpu, a superior Variety.
cūḻnta ālaiyiṉ vempukai pōy mukil tōyum ārūril
in ārūr where the hot smoke of the sugar mills surrounding the gardens, rise above and come into contact with the clouds.
pāloṭu ney tayirum payiṉṟu āṭum paramēṭṭi pātam
the feet of Civaṉ who is in an eralted places and who oftens bathes in milk, ghee and curd.
kālaiyum mālaiyum pōyp paṇital karumamē
paying obeisance to god both in the morning and the evening going to his temple is a specific obligation.
{1:105}__3+
{$} toṇṭīr
Devotees!
uḷḷam ōr iccaiyiṉāl ukantu ēttittoḻumiṉ
worship with joined hands prising Civaṉ with joy by the desire which the mind decided as the proper thing to be done.
meyyē kaḷḷam oḻimiṉ
give up deception sincerely.
veḷḷam ōr caṭaimēṟ karantiṭṭa veḷḷēṟṟāṉ mēya aḷḷal akaṉ kaḻaṉi ārur, karavātu irupoḻutum aṭaivōm
we will approach dily in the morning and evening without fail, ārūr which has broad miry fields, where Civaṉ who has a white bull and who concealed in one of his matted locks the flood(of Kaṅki)
{1:105}__4+
{$} ventu uṟuveṇ maḻuvāḷ paṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ who has the weapon of a white battle-axe which scorches all the Karmams of devotees.
maṇimiṭaṟṟāṉ
who has a neck like the sapphire.
araiyiṉ aintalai āṭu aravam acaittāṉ aṇi ārūr
in ārūr of great beauty belonging to Civaṉ who tied in his waist a dancing cobra of five hoods.
paintaḷirkkoṉṟai amtārpparamaṉ aṭiparavac
if one prised the feet of the supreme god who wears a garland of Koṉṟo flowers which have green leaves.
pāvam naintu aṟum
sins will become extinct, decreasing.
nāḷtoṟum nallaṉavē vantu aṇaiyum
dily only good things will come and join them.
{1:105}__5+
{$} piṟappu vīṭu eḷitām
liberation from birth is easy.
ataṉai viṉavutirēl
if you ask the reason for that.
veyyakāṭu iṭamāka niṉṟu
standing in the hot cremation ground.
kaṉal ēntik kai vīci
holding the fire and swinging the arms.
āṭum avir caṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ who has glittering matted locks, dances.
avaṉ mēya ārūraic ceṉṟu pāṭutal, kaitoḻutal, paṇital karumamē
it is a specific moral duty to sing the prises of ārūr where he wells, to worship with joined hands and to pay obeisance, going there.
{1:105}__6+
{$} kaṅkai ōr vārcaṭaimēl karantāṉ
Civaṉ concluded in one of his long matted locks, Kaṅki.
kiḷi maḻalaik kēṭu il maṅkai ōr kūṟu uṭaiyāṉ
he has on one holl a lady who is without destruction and has gentle speech like the words of the parrot.
maṟaiyāṉ
revealed the vētams which lays down things to be done and things to be avoided.
maḻu ēntum aṅkaiyiṉāṉ aṭiyē paravi
prising only the feet of Civaṉ who holds in his palm a red-hot iron.
araṇmēya ārūr tam kaiyiṉāl toḻuvār taṭumāṟṟu aṟuppār
those who worship with their joined hands ārūr where he dwells, will cut the root of the wavering state of their minds.
{1:105}__7+
{$} nīṟu aṇimēṉiyaṉāy
having a form which is smeared beautifully with sacred ash.
nirampāmaticūṭi
wearing a crescent which has not grown to its full size.
nīṇṭa āṟu aṇi vār caṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ has long matted locks on which he wears a long river.
ārūr iṉitu amar/ntāṉ
stayed in ārūr pleased with that place.
cēṟu aṇi māmalarmēl piramaṉ ciram arinta ceṅkaṇ ēṟuaṇi velkoṭiyāṉ
has a victorious flag on which the form of a red eyed bull adorns; cut off the head of Piramaṉ who is seated on a big lotus which is made beautiful by honey;
[[cēṟu-tēṉ (honey);
cēṟum nāṟṟamum palaviṉ, cuḷaiyum, tēṉum, nāṟṟamum uṭaiyavākiya palāppaḻattiṉ cuḷaiyum (maturikāñci; 527, nacciṉārkkiṉiyar);
(cēṟu may also mean mire; then cēṟu aṇimāmalar; cēṟu will be a generic epithet)]]
avaṉ emperumāṉē
he is our god.

VMS9 / {1:105}__PIFI8+
{$} valliyam tōl uṭaiyāṉ
Civaṉ dresses himself in the skin of a tiger.
vaḷartiṅkaṭ kaṇṇiyaṭaṉāṉ
has a chaplet of flourishing crescent. (The chrse of takkaṉ could not do any harm as the moon took refuge in Civaṉ; so it is called vaḷartiṅkaḷ)
vāytta nalliyal nāṉmukattōṉ talaiyil naṟavu ēṟṟāṉ
received to-day in the skull of the four-faced Piramaṉ who had good qualities appropriate to him.
alliyaṅkōtai taṉṉai ākattu amarntaruḷi
being united with desire the goddess, alliyaṅkōti, in his body.
ārūrppulliya puṇṇiyaṉait toḻuvārum puṇṇiyarē
those people who worship the god who is the embodiment of all virtuous acts and who is very much attached to ārūr, are also people who have performed virtuous acts. (alliyaṅ kōtai: is the name of the goddess in this shrine).
VMS10 / {1:105}__PIFI9+
{$} centuvar āṭaiyiṉār
by buddhists who wear a robe soaked in red ochre.
uṭaiviṭṭu niṉṟu uḻalvār coṉṉa intirañālam oḻintu
Leaving the specious doctrines preached by camaṇar who wander discarding dress and who are both heretics.
iṉpuṟa vēṇṭutirēl
if you want to be happy.
antaramū eyilum araṇam(m) eriyūṭṭi
setting fire to all the three forts which afforded protection to them, and were wandering in the sky.
ārūrt tam tiramā uṭaiyāṉ
has ārūr as his permanent abode.
avaṉ em talaimaiyaṉ
he is our chief.

{$} nalla alli malarkkaḻaṉi ārūr amarntāṉai
on Civaṉ who dwelt with desire in ārūr which has fields in which beautiful water-lilies grow.
nalla puṉaṟpukalit tamiḻ ñāṉacampantaṉ
ñāṉacampantaṉ of possessing knowledge of tamiḻ, a native of pukali of healthy water.
vallatu ōr iccaiyiṉāl vaḻipāṭu ivai pattum vāykkac collutal kēṭṭal vallār tuṉpam tuṭaippār
those who recite, and listen, to the recitation of these ten verses which are in themselves worship by the will of their capabality with concentration, will remove their sufferings without leaving any trace of it.

*1
māṉ _ēṟu karam _uṭaiya varatarpōlum;
mālvarai kāl vaḷai villā vaḷaittārpōlum;
kāṉ _ēṟu kari kataṟa _urittārpōlum;
kaṭṭaṅkam, koṭi, tuṭi, kaik koṇṭārpōlum;
tēṉ _ēṟu tiru_itaḻittārārpōlum;
tiruvīḻimiḻalai _amar celvarpōlum;
_āṉ_ēṟu_atu _ēṟum _aḻakarpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*2
camaram miku calantaraṉ pōr vēṇṭiṉāṉaic
cakkarattāl piḷappitta caturarpōlum;
namaṉai _oru kāl kuṟaitta nātarpōlum;
nāraṇaṉai _iṭappākattu _aṭaittārpōlum;
kumaraṉaiyum makaṉ_āka _uṭaiyārpōlum;
kuḷir vīḻimiḻalai _amar kuḻakarpōlum;
_amararkaḷ piṉ _amutu _uṇa, nañcu _uṇṭārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*3
nīṟu _aṇinta tirumēṉi nimalarpōlum;
nēmi, neṭumāṟku, _aruḷicceytārpōlum;
_ēṟu _aṇinta koṭi _uṭai _em _iṟaivarpōlum;
_eyilmūṉṟum _ericarattāl _eytārpōlum;
vēṟu _aṇinta kōlam _uṭai vēṭarpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalai _uṟai vikirtarpōlum;
_āṟu _aṇinta caṭāmakuṭattu _aḻakarpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*4
kaivēḻamukattavaṉaip paṭaittārpōlum;
kayācuraṉai _avaṉāl kolvittārpōlum;
cey vēḷvit takkaṉai muṉ citaittārpōlum;
ticaimukaṉtaṉ ciram _oṉṟu citaittārpōlum;
mey vēḷvimūrtti talai _aṟuttārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalai _iṭam koṇṭārpōlum;
_aivēḷvi, _āṟu_aṅkam, _āṉārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*5
tuṉṉattiṉ kōvaṇam _oṉṟu _uṭaiyārpōlum;
cuṭarmūṉṟum cōtiyum_āyt tūyārpōlum;
poṉ _otta tirumēṉip puṉitarpōlum;
pūtakaṇam puṭai cūḻa varuvārpōlum;
miṉ _otta ceñcaṭai veṇpiṟaiyārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalai cēr vimalarpōlum;
_aṉṉattēr_ayaṉ muṭi cēr _aṭikaḷpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*6
mālālum _aṟivu _ariya varatarpōlum;
maṟavātār piṟappu _aṟukka vallārpōlum;
nāl_āya maṟaikku _iṟaivar _āṉārpōlum;
nāma _eḻuttu_añcu_āya namparpōlum;
vēl _ār kai vīriyai muṉ paṭaittārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalai _amar vikirtarpōlum;
_ālālam miṭaṟṟu _aṭakki _aḷittārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*7
pañcu _aṭutta melviralāḷ paṅkarpōlum;
painnākam _araikku _acaitta paramarpōlum;
mañcu _aṭutta maṇinīlakaṇṭarpōlum;
vaṭakayilaimalai _uṭaiya maṇāḷarpōlum;
ceñcaṭaikkaṇ veṇpiṟai koṇṭu _aṇintārpōlum;
tiru vīḻimiḻalai _amar civaṉārpōlum;
_añcu _aṭakkum _aṭiyavarkaṭku _aṇiyārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*8
kuṇṭaroṭu pirittu _eṉai _āṭkoṇṭārpōlum;
kuṭamūkkil _iṭam _ākkikkoṇṭārpōlum;
puṇṭarikapputumalar _ātaṉattārpōlum;
puḷ_aracaik koṉṟu _uyir piṉ koṭuttārpōlum;
veṇtalaiyil pali koṇṭa vikirtarpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalainakar _uṭaiyārpōlum;
_aṇṭattu _up puṟattu _appāl _āṉārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*9
muttu _aṉaiya mukiḻ muṟuval _uṭaiyārpōlum;
moy pavaḷakkoṭi _aṉaiya caṭaiyārpōlum;
_ettaṉaiyum patti ceyvārkku _iṉiyārpōlum;
_iru-nāṉkumūrttikaḷum _āṉārpōlum;
mittira vacciravaṇaṟku virupparpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalai _amar vikirtarpōlum;
_attaṉoṭum _ammai _eṉakku _āṉārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*10
kari _uriceytu _umai veruvak kaṇṭārpōlum;
kaṅkaiyaiyum ceñcaṭaimēl karantārpōlum;
_eri_atu _oru kai taritta _iṟaivarpōlum;
_ēṉattiṉ kūṉ_eyiṟu pūṇṭārpōlum;
virikatirōṉ_iruvarai muṉ vekuṇṭārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalai _amar vimalarpōlum;
_aripiramar tuticeya niṉṟu _aḷittārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*11
kayilāyamalai _eṭuttāṉ kataṟi vīḻak
kālviralāl _aṭarttu _aruḷicceytārpōlum;
kuyil _āya meṉmoḻiyāḷ kuḷirntu nōkkak
kūttu_āṭa valla kuḻakarpōlum;
veyil_āya cōtiviḷakku _āṉārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalai _amar vikirtarpōlum;
_ayil _āya mū_ilaivēlpaṭaiyār pōlum---
_aṭiyēṉai _āḷ_uṭaiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

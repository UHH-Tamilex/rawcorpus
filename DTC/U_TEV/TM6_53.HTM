  • talam: tiru vīḻimiḻali
  • (()): tirut tāṇṭakam
  • VMS: Vol.7, p.292

*1
māṉ _ēṟu karam _uṭiya varatarpōlum;
mālvari kāl vaḷi villā vaḷittārpōlum;
kāṉ _ēṟu kari kataṟa _urittārpōlum;
kaṭṭaṅkam, koṭi, tuṭi, kik koṇṭārpōlum;
tēṉ _ēṟu tiru_itaḻittārārpōlum;
tiruvīḻimiḻali _amar celvarpōlum;
_āṉ_ēṟu_atu _ēṟum _aḻakarpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*2
camaram miku calantaraṉ pōr vēṇṭiṉāṉic
cakkarattāl piḷappitta caturarpōlum;
namaṉi _oru kāl kuṟitta nātarpōlum;
nāraṇaṉi _iṭappākattu _aṭittārpōlum;
kumaraṉiyum makaṉ_āka _uṭiyārpōlum;
kuḷir vīḻimiḻali _amar kuḻakarpōlum;
_amararkaḷ piṉ _amutu _uṇa, nañcu _uṇṭārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*3
nīṟu _aṇinta tirumēṉi nimalarpōlum;
nēmi, neṭumāṟku, _aruḷicceytārpōlum;
_ēṟu _aṇinta koṭi _uṭi _em _iṟivarpōlum;
_eyilmūṉṟum _ericarattāl _eytārpōlum;
vēṟu _aṇinta kōlam _uṭi vēṭarpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻali _uṟi vikirtarpōlum;
_āṟu _aṇinta caṭāmakuṭattu _aḻakarpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*4
kivēḻamukattavaṉip paṭittārpōlum;
kayācuraṉi _avaṉāl kolvittārpōlum;
cey vēḷvit takkaṉi muṉ citittārpōlum;
ticimukaṉtaṉ ciram _oṉṟu citittārpōlum;
mey vēḷvimūrtti tali _aṟuttārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻali _iṭam koṇṭārpōlum;
_aivēḷvi, _āṟu_aṅkam, _āṉārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*5
tuṉṉattiṉ kōvaṇam _oṉṟu _uṭiyārpōlum;
cuṭarmūṉṟum cōtiyum_āyt tūyārpōlum;
poṉ _otta tirumēṉip puṉitarpōlum;
pūtakaṇam puṭi cūḻa varuvārpōlum;
miṉ _otta ceñcaṭi veṇpiṟiyārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻali cēr vimalarpōlum;
_aṉṉattēr_ayaṉ muṭi cēr _aṭikaḷpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*6
mālālum _aṟivu _ariya varatarpōlum;
maṟavātār piṟappu _aṟukka vallārpōlum;
nāl_āya maṟikku _iṟivar _āṉārpōlum;
nāma _eḻuttu_añcu_āya namparpōlum;
vēl _ār ki vīriyi muṉ paṭittārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻali _amar vikirtarpōlum;
_ālālam miṭaṟṟu _aṭakki _aḷittārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*7
pañcu _aṭutta melviralāḷ paṅkarpōlum;
pinnākam _arikku _acitta paramarpōlum;
mañcu _aṭutta maṇinīlakaṇṭarpōlum;
vaṭakayilimali _uṭiya maṇāḷarpōlum;
ceñcaṭikkaṇ veṇpiṟi koṇṭu _aṇintārpōlum;
tiru vīḻimiḻali _amar civaṉārpōlum;
_añcu _aṭakkum _aṭiyavarkaṭku _aṇiyārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*8
kuṇṭaroṭu pirittu _eṉi _āṭkoṇṭārpōlum;
kuṭamūkkil _iṭam _ākkikkoṇṭārpōlum;
puṇṭarikapputumalar _ātaṉattārpōlum;
puḷ_aracik koṉṟu _uyir piṉ koṭuttārpōlum;
veṇtaliyil pali koṇṭa vikirtarpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻalinakar _uṭiyārpōlum;
_aṇṭattu _up puṟattu _appāl _āṉārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*9
muttu _aṉiya mukiḻ muṟuval _uṭiyārpōlum;
moy pavaḷakkoṭi _aṉiya caṭiyārpōlum;
_ettaṉiyum patti ceyvārkku _iṉiyārpōlum;
_iru-nāṉkumūrttikaḷum _āṉārpōlum;
mittira vacciravaṇaṟku virupparpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻali _amar vikirtarpōlum;
_attaṉoṭum _ammi _eṉakku _āṉārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*10
kari _uriceytu _umi veruvak kaṇṭārpōlum;
kaṅkiyiyum ceñcaṭimēl karantārpōlum;
_eri_atu _oru ki taritta _iṟivarpōlum;
_ēṉattiṉ kūṉ_eyiṟu pūṇṭārpōlum;
virikatirōṉ_iruvari muṉ vekuṇṭārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻali _amar vimalarpōlum;
_aripiramar tuticeya niṉṟu _aḷittārpōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

*11
kayilāyamali _eṭuttāṉ kataṟi vīḻak
kālviralāl _aṭarttu _aruḷicceytārpōlum;
kuyil _āya meṉmoḻiyāḷ kuḷirntu nōkkak
kūttu_āṭa valla kuḻakarpōlum;
veyil_āya cōtiviḷakku _āṉārpōlum;
viyaṉvīḻimiḻali _amar vikirtarpōlum;
_ayil _āya mū_ilivēlpaṭiyār pōlum---
_aṭiyēṉi _āḷ_uṭiya _aṭikaḷtāmē.

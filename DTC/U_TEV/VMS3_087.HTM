{3:87}__1+
{$} taḷir iḷa vaḷar oḷi taṉatu eḻil taru tikaḻ malaimakaḷ kuḷir iḷa vaḷar oḷi vaṉa mulai iṇai (avai) kulavaliṉ
as the (songs) are intimately connected with the two beautiful, cool, tender and growing lustre of the twin breasts of the dughter of the mountin, whose beauty is like the tender brightness of the growing leaves.
naḷir iḷa vaḷar oḷi maruvu naḷḷāṟar tam nāmamē miḷir iḷa vaḷar eriyiṭil ivai paḻutu ilaiyē
and as these contin the names of the god in naḷḷāṟu, if they are consigned into the fire which is bright like her body, they will suffer no damage.
meymaiyē
it is truth.
{3:87}__2+
{$} pōtu amar taru purikuḻal eḻil malaimakaḷ pūṇ aṇi, cītam (atu) aṇi taru mukiḻ iḷa vaṉamulai ceṟitaliṉ
as the songs are connected with the young, beautiful and bud-like breasts of the beautiful dughter of the mountin who has curly tresses of hir in which flowers are inserted,
nātam atu eḻil uru naḷḷāṟrtam nāmamē
and they contin the names of Civaṉ in naḷḷaṟu who took the body of beautiful nāta tattuvam.
mītamatu eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutilai; meymmaiyē
if they are consigned into fire which is superior and one of the eight forms of Civaṉ, they will suffer no damage; it is truth.
{3:87}__3+
{$} iṭṭuṟu maṇi aṇi iṇar puṇar vaḷar oḷi eḻil vaṭam kaṭṭuṟu katir iḷavaḷam mulai iṇaiyoṭu kalavaliṉ
as these verses are connected with the two tender and beautiful breasts which are adorned with elegant strand of increasing lustre in which are set rows of precious stones like bunches of flowers.
naṭṭu uṟu ceṟivayal maruvu naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē
and these contin the names of the god in naḷḷāṟu where there are fields in which crops are planted and are close to each other.
iṭṭu uṟum eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutu ilai; meymmaiyē
if these are consigned into the small fire they will suffer no damage; it is truth.
{3:87}__4+
{$} maiccu aṇi vari ari nayaṉi tol malaimakaḷ payaṉ uṟu kaccu aṇi katir iḷavaṉamulaiyavaiyoṭu kalavaliṉ
as these verses are connected with the tender, beautiful and bright breasts which were a bodice, which gives knowledge which is not divine wisdom and knowledge of god, of the dughter of the ancient mountin, who has eyes with rows of lives and coated with collyrium.
(maiccu-maittu; maiyaiyuṭaiyatāki)
naccu aṇi miṭaṟu uṭai aṭikaḷ naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē
and they contin the names of the god in naḷḷāṟu who has a neck made beautiful by the poison.
meccu aṇi eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutu ilai; meymmaiyē
if these are consigned into the fire which has wonderful beauty will not suffer any damage; it is truth.
{3:87}__5+
{$} paṇ iyal malaimakaḷ katir viṭu parumaṇi aṇi niṟak kaṇ iyal kalacam (atu) aṉamulai iṇaiyoṭu kalavaliṉ
as these songs are connected with the two bright beautiful breasts which are like pots and wear ornaments with big precious stones which emit rays, belonging to the dughter of the mountin and which are well decorated.
naṇṇiya kuḷir puṉal pukutum naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē
they contin the names of Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu where cool water enters.
viṇ iyal eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutu ilai; meymmaiyē
if these are consigned into the fire which rises high to touch the sky these will not suffer any damage; it is truth.
{3:87}__6+
{$} pōtu uṟu purikuḻal malaimakaḷ iḷavaḷar poṉ aṇi cūtuṟu taḷir niṟa vaṉamulaiyavaiyoṭu tutaitaliṉ
these songs are closely connected with the tender growing and beautiful breasts which have the colour of tender leaves and are like conical pieces in dice-play and wear gold ornaments, of the dughter of the mountin who has curly tresses of hir in which are inserted flowers.
tātu uṟu niṟam uṭai aṭikaḷ naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē
and these contin the names of Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu who has the colour of gold.
mītu uṟum eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutu ilai; meymmaiyē
these suffer no damage if these are consigned into the fire which rises high; it is truth.
{3:87}__7+
{$} kār mali neṟipuri curi kuḻal malaimakaḷ kaviṉ uṟu cīr mali taru maṇi aṇi mulai tikaḻvoṭu ceṟitaliṉ
these songs are closely connected with the breasts which wear ornaments set with famous precious stones, of the dughter of the mountin who has tresses of hir as black as the sable cloud, and wavy and curly and tied into a knot.
tār mali nakutalai uṭaiya naḷḷāṟar tam nāmamē
and they contin the names of Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu who wears a garland of lughing skulls.
ēr mali eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutu ilai; meymmaiyē
if these are consigned into the rising fire they will not suffer any damage; it is truth.
{3:87}__8+
{$} maṉṉiyavaḷar oḷi malaimakaḷ taḷir niṟam matam miku poṉ iyal maṇi aṇi kalacam (atu) aṉamulai puṇartaliṉ
these songs are connected the breasts which are like pots and wear ornaments made of gold set with precious stones and smeared with musk, has the colour of tender leaves, of the dughter of the mountin which has permanent and increasing brightness.
taṉ iyal tacamukaṉ neriya naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē
these contin the names of Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu who pressed down to crush irāvāṇaṉ of ten faces who can be compared to himself in committing cruel acts.
maṉṉiyal eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutu ilai meymmai
if these are consigned into the five which is like lightning, these will not suffer any any damage; it is truth.
{3:87}__9+
{$} kāṉ mukam mayil iyal malaimakaḷ katirviṭu kaṉam miku pāl mukam iyal paṇai iṇaimulai tuṇaiyoṭu payiṟaliṉ
these songs are connected with the two breasts which can be only compared to each other and has heaviness and emit brilliance and swelling in size and has lactation, of the dughter of the mountin, who has the gracefulness of the peacock of the forest.
nāṉ mukaṉ ari aṟivu ariya kaḷḷāṟar tam nāmamē
and these contin the names of Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu who could not be known by Piramaṉ and ari (Māl).
mēṉ muka eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutu ilai; meymmaiyē
if these are consigned into the fire which has the nature of rising up they will suffer no damage, it is truth.
{3:87}__10+
{$} attiranayaṉi tol malaimakaḷ payaṉ uṟum aticayac cittiramaṇi aṇitikaḻ mulai iṇaiyoṭu ceṟitaliṉ
these songs are closely connected with the two breasts which were different and wonderful, precious stones and have lactation of the dughter of the ancient mountin and whose eyes are like the arrow.
puttaroṭu amaṇar poy peyarum naḷḷāṟartam nāmamē
and these contin the names of Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu who is far removed from the lies of buddhists and Camaṇar.
meyttiraḷ eriyiṉil iṭil ivai paḻutu ilai; meymmaiyē
if these are consigned into the ball of fire these will not suffer any damage; it is truth.
{3:87}__11+
{$} koṟṟavaṉ etiriṭai eriyiṉil iṭa avai kūṟiya, ciṟṟiṭai arivaitaṉ vaṉamulai iṇaiyoṭu ceṟitarum naltiṟam uṟu kaḻumala nakar ñāṉacampantaṉa col teri orupatum aṟipavar tuyar ilar tūyarē
the songs were composed in the immediate presence of the King to be placed in the fire, by ñāṉacampantaṉ who had connection with the two breasts of the lady who has a small waist, and who is a native of the city of Kaḻumalam which has good histroy; those who are able to understand the meanings of the ten verses have no sufferings; are pure souls.

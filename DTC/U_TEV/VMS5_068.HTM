uḷ aṟātatu ōr puṇṭarikat tiraḷ teḷ aṟāc civac cōtit tiraḷiṉai
the ball of brilliance of Civaṉ who is not removed from the clarity of the collection of the lotus whose colour is not lost inside it.
kaḷ aṟāta poṟ koṉṟai kamaḻcaṭai naḷḷāṟā eṉa
if we say, `Civaṉ in Naḷḷāṟu on whose caṭi the golden koṉṟi from which honey never leaves'
nam viṉai nācamē
our Karmams will be destroyed.
[[The colour of the collection of lotus flowers is compared to civan's lustre; the lotuses refer to the six cakras or dynamic tattvik centres which are nerve plexuses in the body.]]

āraṇap poruḷ ām aruḷāḷaṉār
Civaṉ who is full of grace and who is the meaning of the āraṇam (vētam)
vāraṇattu uri pōrtta maṇāḷaṉār
the bridegroom who covered himself with the skin of an elephant.
nāraṇaṉ naṇṇi ēttum naḷḷāṟaṉār
Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu who is prised by nāraṇaṉ, having gone there from his world, vikuṇṭam
kāraṇak kalai ñāṉak kaṭavuḷē
is the god who grants knowledge of arts and who is the cuse for all things.
[[kalai ñāṉak kaṭavuḷ may mean also this; the meaning of the knowledge of all arts.]]

mēkam pūṇṭatu ōr mēru viṟkoṇṭu
with the help of the bow of mēru whose peak is covered with clouds.
eyil cōkam pūṇṭu aḻal cōrat toṭṭāṉ avaṉ
he discharged an arrow from which fire dropped for the forts to suffer grief.
pākam pūṇṭa māl paṅkayattāṉoṭu, nākam pūṇṭu kūttāṭum naḷḷāṟaṉē
is Civaṉ in naḷḷaṟu who dances adorning himself with cobras to be worshipped by Māl who is in one half, and Piramaṉ in the lotus flower.

maliyum ceñcaṭai vāḷ aravam moṭu poliyum pūmpuṉal vaitta puṉitaṉār
the immaculate god who placed on his red crowded caṭi, brilliant water along with a shining cobra.
naliyum kūṟṟai nalinta naḷḷāṟartam valiyum kaṇṭu iṟumāntu makiḻvaṉē
I shall rejoice feeling elated seeing also the strength of Civaṉ in nāḷḷaṟu who afflicted the god of death who gives pin to living beings by taking away their lives.

uṟavaṉāy uḷḷam niṟaintu
completely occupying my mind being my relation.
kuḷirppavaṉ
makes me conforted.
iṟaivaṉ āki niṉṟu eṇ niṟaintāṉ avaṉ
he, being the supreme god, filled my thoughts
naṟavam nāṟum poḻil tiru naḷḷāṟaṉ maṟavaṉāyppaṉṟippiṉ ceṉṟa māyamē
Civaṉ in tirunaḷḷāṟu where in the gardens saffron spreads its fragrance, following a pig assuming the form of a hunter is an act of wonder.

cekkar aṅku aḻi ceñcuṭarc cōtiyār
Civaṉ is the sun with red lustre which makes the red sky to perish.
nakkar
is a nude person.
(aṅku) aravu ārtta naḷḷāṟaṉār/
the god in naḷḷāṟu who tied a cobras.
vakkaraṉ uyir vavviya māyaṟkuc cakkaram aruḷ ceyta caturar
is the skilful god who granted a discus to māyaṉ (Māl) who took away the life of vakkaraṉ
[[Vakkaraṉ : an acuraṉ who was born of viruttacarman and curutātevi, the younger sister of Caitevaṉ; he and cicupalaṉ are the incarnation of jayvijayar who were cursed by caṉaka caṉamtaṉar]]

vañca nañciṉ poli kiṉṟa kaṇṭattar
Civaṉ has a neck which is brilliant by the cruel poison.
viñcaiyiṉ celvap pāvaikku vēntaṉār
is the husband of the dear dughter of the mountin by his wonderful acts.
vañca neñcattavarkku vaḻi koṭār
will not show the right path to people of deceitful minds.
nañca neñcarkku aruḷum naḷḷāṟar
the god in naḷḷāṟu will grant his grace to people whose hearts melt.
[[nañcam neñcarkku (um) to those who have hearts as cruel as the poison is another meaning in the former meaning nañca is the word resembling nainta in sound]]

alarkku allaṉ eṉṟum
Civaṉ does not exist to those who are not good by nature.
aruḷ āyiṉa collaṉ eṉṟu (um)
and who initiates in philosophical truths people who beleive in him.
collām maṟaic cōtiyāṉ
is the light of the vētam which is not committed to writing and handed down from teacher to pupil by word of mouth.
vallaṉ eṉṟum vallār
those who are able to prise him as omnipotent.
vaḷam mikkavar
will have abundant riches
naḷḷāṟaṉ eṉṟum nallārkku nallaṉ
Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu is always good-natured to good people.

pāmpu aṇaippaḷḷi koṇṭa paramaṉum
the supreme being who sleeps on a serpent-bed.
pūmpaṇaip polikiṉṟa purāṇaṉum
and the old Piramaṉ who shines in the seat of a lotus-flower.
tām paṇintu aḷappu oṇṇāt taṉittaḻal nāmpaṇintu aṭi pōṟṟum naḷḷāṟaṉ
Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu is the unique fire which could not measured even though they bowed before it, is Civaṉ in naḷḷāṟu whose feet we prise bowing before them.

{2:43}__1+
{$} kaḷ ārnta pūṅkoṉṟai matamattam, katir matiyam uḷ ārnta caṭaimuṭi em perumāṉār uṟaiyum iṭam
the place where our Lord who has inside his matted hir coiled like a crown the shining koṉṟi with profuse honey, purple stramony and a crescent spreading rays.
taḷḷāya campāti caṭāyu eṉpār (tām) iruvar puḷḷāṉārkku araiyaṉ iṭam
and the place of the King for the two birds Campāti and Caṭāyu who could not be slighted becuse of their birth as birds.
puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr
[[Today this shrine is known by the name of Vaittīsvaraṉ Koil]]
{2:43}__2+
{$} taiyalāḷ orupākam
having on one half a lady by name tiyal.
[[tiyal: is the name of the goddess there]]
caṭai mēlāḷ avaḷōṭum
along with a lady (Kaṅki) who is on the matted hir.
aiyam tērntu uḻalvāṉ ōr antaṇaṉār uṟaiyum iṭam
the place where the great antaṇaṉ who wanders begging alms.
[[PP_EXT_(antaṇāṉār):
yāḻ keḻu maṇimiṭaṟṟu antaṇaṉ
(Akam, invocatory verse, 15)]]
mēl ōṭi
running at him
mey collāirāvaṇaṉai īṭu aḻittu
Having destroyed the strength to Irāvaṇaṉ who never spoke truth.
poy collātu uyir pōṉāṉ puḷḷirukku vēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukku Vēḷūr, worshipped by (Caṭāyu) who lost his life as he did not utter lies.
{2:43}__3+
{$} vācam nalam ceytu imaiyōr nāḷ tōṟum malar tūva
as the celestials who do not wink and who live in that place and perform other good acts, scatter flowers dily.
īcaṉ emperumāṉār iṉitāka uṟaiyum iṭam
the place where Civaṉ, our great Lord, dwells with pleasure.
yōcaṉaipōyp pūkkoṇarntu
flying a distance of four kurocam and bringing flowers.
aṅku oru nāḷum oḻiyāmē pūcaṉai ceytu iṉitu iruntāṉ puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukku Vēlūr where Campāti performed pūci without missing even a single day and lived happily.
{2:43}__4+
{$} mākāyam periyatu oru māṉ uritōl uṭaiyāṭai ēkāyam iṭṭu ukanta eri āṭi uṟaiyum iṭam
the place of the god who dances amidst fire and who was pleased to use as upper cloth the skin of an elephant with a huge body.
ākāyam tēr ōṭum irāvaṇaṉai amariṉ kaṇ pōkāmē porutu aḻittāṉ puḷḷirukku vēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukkuvēlūr which was worshipped by Caṭāyu who fought with Irāvaṇaṉ who was travelling in a chariot in air, so that he cannot escape in the fight;
[[Irāvaṇaṉ's chariot is Pushpakavimāṉam]]
{2:43}__5+
{$} kītattai mikap pāṭum aṭiyārkaḷ kuṭiyākap, pātattait toḻa niṉṟa parañcōti payilum iṭam
the place where the supreme light stays to worship his feet which are the ever-lasting abode for devotees who often sing songs of prise.
vētattiṉ mantirattāl veṇmaṇalē civamākap pōtattāl vaḻippaṭṭāṉ puḷḷirukku vēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukku Vēḷūr where Campāti worshipped Civaṉ in the form of white sand with his spiritual wisdom, by chanting mantiram of the Vētam.
{2:43}__6+
{$} tiṟam koṇṭa aṭiyār mēl tīviṉai nōy vārāmē, aṟam koṇṭu civataṉmam uraitta pirāṉ amarum iṭam
the place where the Lord who once gave out Civataṉmam, including aṟam so that the diseases due to evil karmam may not come near the devotees who are of several aptitudes
[[civataṉmam: a work by name Civatarumam]]
maṟam koṇṭu, aṅku irāvaṇaṉ taṉ valikaruti vantāṉaip puṟaṅkaṇṭa caṭāyu eṉpāṉ puḷḷirukku vēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr where Caṭāyu who put to flight Irāvaṇaṉ who come to fight thinking greatly of his might, with valour
[[caṭāyeṉpāṉ-caṭāyu+eṉpāṉ]]
{2:43}__7+
{$} attiyiṉ īr urimūṭi
Having covered himself with the skin of an elephant wet with blood
aḻakāka aṉal ēnti
holding fire (in the hand) beautifully
pittaraippōl pali tiriyum perumāṉār pēṉum iṭam
the place desired by the great one who wanders for alms like mad people.
pattiyiṉāl vaḻipaṭṭu
having worshipped god with devotion.
palakālam tavañceytu
and having done penance for a very long time.
putti oṉṟai vaittu (1) ukantāṉ puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr adored by Campāti who become great by concentrating his mind on god.
[[Variant reading: (1) iruntāṉ]]
[[This stanza is found in some manuscripts after paṇṇoṉṟa]]
{2:43}__8+
{$} paṇ oṉṟa icai pāṭum aṭiyārkaḷ kuṭiyāka, maṇ iṉṟi viṇkoṭukkum maṇikaṇṭaṉ maruvum iṭam
the place where the god with a neck as blue as sapphire, grants his world to the devotees who sing music in unison with melodies, as an ever-lasting abode without being born agin in this world
eṇ iṉṟi mukkōṭi vāṇāḷ(atu) uṭaiyāṉaip puṇ oṉṟap porutu aḻittāṉ puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr which was worshipped by Caṭāyu who fought with Irāvaṇaṉ who had such a long span of life as three crores of years defying numbers and defeated him, to inflict wounds on his body.
{2:43}__9+
{$} vētittār puram mūṉṟum veṅkaṇaiyāl ventu aviyac cātitta villāḷi
one who had a bow by which the three cities of the acurar who were changed in mind being hostile, were made to cease to exist by being burnt, by discharging a cruel arrow.
kaṇṇāḷaṉ cārum iṭam
the place where the god who is the spiritual eye for all, dwells.
ātittaṉ makaṉ eṉṉa akal ñālattavarōṭum pōtitta caṭāyu eṉpāṉ puḷḷirukku vēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukku Vēḷūr where Caṭāyu who was tught all spiritual wisdom and who is extolled as the son of the sun-god, worshipped the god along with people of this wide world.
{2:43}__10+
{$} kaṭuttu varum kaṅkaitaṉaik kamaḻcaṭai oṉṟu āṭāmē taṭuttavar
god who arrested the Kaṅki which was descending with speed and wrath in one of his fragrant matted hir, without wetting it
[[caṭai oṉṟu āṭāmē: without shaking even a single caṭi]]
emperumāṉār(tām) iṉitāy uṟaiyum iṭam
the place where our great Lord dwells with pleasure.
viṭaittu varum ilaṅkaikkōṉ malaṅkac ceṉṟu irāmaṟkāp puṭaittu avaṉaip porutu aḻittāṉ puḷḷirukku vēḷūr
is Puḷḷirukku Vēlūr worshipped by Caṭāyu who fought with the king of Ilaṅki who came bursting into a rage and beat him and destroyed his strength, to make him perturbed in mind, on behalf of Irāmaṉ.
{2:43}__11+
{$} ceṭi āya uṭal tīrppāṉ
God who removes this body of bad odour
[[god who does not create us to be born agin]]
tīviṉaikku ōr maruntu āvāṉ
who is like the cure for the diseases of evil acts.
poṭi aṭikku ōr aṭimai ceyta
one who performed service to the feet of the god who bathes in the holy ash.
kaṭi ārnta poḻil kāḻik kavuṇiyaṉ puḷḷirukku vēḷūraic col
the verses sung about the prises of Puḷḷirukkuvēḷūr by Kavuṇiyaṉ [[Ñāṉacampantaṉ]] born in Kāḻi which has gardens full of fragrance.
[[kaṭi ārttapoḻil: this may also mean: gardens strictly guarded]]
maṭiyātu collavallārkku maṟupiṟappu illai ām
those who are capable of reciting them without indolence will not be born agin.

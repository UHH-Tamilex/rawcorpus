(206) marutam

Lines: (2-3) The horns of buffalo compared to the hands of dancing hermaphrodite with predominating female characteristics (4-6) The urchins sitting on the back of the buffalo resemble monkeys sitting on a boulder, in the place belonging to talivaṉ. (7-12) talivaṉ desires marriage with harlots in their houses (12-6) talivi's shoulders became lean and the ornaments worn on them became loose; talivi comparing that state to the heap of salt dissolved by heavy rins. (1) of what avil are the words of female dancer who pleads on behalf of talivaṉ ?

Translation: Companion (1)! The small urchins doing little errands who sit upon (5) the back which has a black skin to which hirs add beauty (4), of the buffalo which has big twisted and shining horns (3) which resemble the hands that are twisted behind and rise above in curves of a mirthful dancer who dresses himself as a hermaphrodite with predominant female characteristics, (2) appear like female monkeys sitting on boulders (6) to those who look at them from a great distance (5). The chief of such a village (6) is enjoying marriage (12) in the extensive mansion where celebrations take place and the drum is played without stopping (11) Becuse of that, the garland that is worn on the chest of the talivaṉ becomes spoilt and mashy, due to insatiable love, in the chest having broad breasts on which garlands of pearl are worn, of the harlots who wear choice jewels (8) and who have bodies with beautiful mami (māmai) complexion (8) resembling the shining sprouts of leaves of a species of sensitive tree īṅki (īṅkaimaram) in the winter (7) My brod and soft shoulders (16) disliked that (12) and, as a result of that became lean and dropped the ornaments of fire workmanship worn on them, (16) like the numerous heaps of salt which stretch to a great length (14) in the foreshore of vīri (vīrai) which belongs to vēḷir (vēḷir), petty rulers, who are capable of victorious battles, (13), and were dissolved by heavy downpour of rin (15) (This being so) of what avil are the words of viṟil (viṟali) (who intercedes on behalf of talivaṉ)?

Syntactical link :- tōḻi(1)! ūṟaṉ(6) vatuvaimēvalaṉ ākaliṉ(12) meṉtōḷ iḻai nekiḻntaṉa(16); ivvāṉākavum viṟaliyiṉ kūṟṟu) eṉ eṉappaṭum(1)?

Talimakal refused intercession on behalf of talimakaṉ to the female dancer

maturi marutaṉiḷanākaṉār

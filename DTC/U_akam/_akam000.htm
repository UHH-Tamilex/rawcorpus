AKAM 000:
Akanāṉūṟu, Invocation of God

Translation

  • The antaṇaṉ has a neck like sapphire from which is produced the music of yaḻ (Sāma vēda) (15).
  • Everyone including amarar (அமரர்) who do not know old age and muṉivar (முனிவர்) , and others (12)
  • could not know his very old nature (13).
  • He is wearing round his waist (as a cloth) the skin of a strong tiger which has stripes on it (14).
  • He has a garland (தார்) made of fresh and golden laburnum (கொன்றை) flowers which blossom in winter, and another garland (மாலை) and chaplet worn on the head (of the same flower) (1-2).
  • In his chest lies the fine sacred thread which is spotlessly clean (3).
  • In his forehead there is the eye that does not wink (4)
  • In his hand he has an axe which kills enemies, in addition to a battle-axe (கணிச்சி) (4-5).
  • The one who does not know defeat has also a three-forked lance (5-6).
  • He rides on a bull. Umi (உமை) resides in him (7).
  • He has a form resembling the red sky and has sharp, curved, white and shining teeth like the crescent moon that appears in the red sky (8-10).
  • He has on his head matted locks of hirs which resembles fire that has branches, with the crescent which has not attined its full form, shining brightly (10-11).
  • The world stays in the shelter (protection) of his feet which are indestructible (16)

pāratam pāṭiya peruntēvaṉār

(Note) Tār is worn on special occasions, māli worn for beauty; Kaṇṇi is worn when fighting. Some others interpret them as follows: Tār is joining together of flowers; māli is made by knitting them. Kaṇṇi is made with a single flower

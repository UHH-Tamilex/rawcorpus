AKAM 034

(34) mulli

  • (Lines 1-8) Description of pastoral tract during riny season
  • (9-10) Talivaṉ requesting the charioteer to drive the chariot fast.
  • (11-3) Description of the house in which talivi is staying
  • (14-5) State of talivi in the house.

Translation

  • Charioteer who is stronger than others in driving the chariot! (10)
  • In the cool and fragant forest where the white-crested and short bush of small and black bedaly emitic nut (piṭavu) blossoms like a chaplet (1-2),
  • the eminent stag having two twisted horns which resemble the forked stick carried on the head of the hunter who wears slippers (3-4)
  • feeds the young female deer which has its young ones playing by its side (6), with the soft bunches and the red stem of bermuda grass (aṟukampul) which has crowding leaves (5-6)
  • and is keeping guard over them where they sleep in the long sandy banks where clear laving water is surrounding them, with its cheeks moving when chewing the cud (7-8).
  • On seeing that grand sight my mind became weak when I thought, `I was not lucky like these to fondle my wife' (3, 9)
  • Let the chariot go fast (10)
  • in order to make my mind feel happy (9)
  • and to make the lady with shyness to regin her glorious beauty (18).
  • She holds on her forearm a green parrot with a red neck-strip (14),
  • in the precincts of the guarded house (13)
  • where the swans which have pure white feathers (12)
  • like the starch which is sticking on clothes and which is washed from them and rinsed in the ghat by the washerwoman who has big shoulders, and fingers with which she removes it (11-2),
  • play with their mates (13)
  • and, says to it, `in a low tone (16),
  • fearing lest it should be overheard by other inmates in the house (16),
  • teaches to talk prattling sweet words (maḻalaic col.) (17),
  • "If at all you tell something about our talivar who parted from us, please tell me that he will return today' (15)

Syntactical link: peṟun(10)! neñcam ēmuṟa(9) arivai māṇ nalampeṟac(18) celkatēr(10) puṟavil(2) iralai(4) aruttip(6) puṟamkākkum(8) peruntakaikku uṭainta neñcam ēmuṟat(9) tēr celka; varaippil(13) ēnti(14) añcip(16) payiṟṟum(17) arivai.

The talimakaṉ who finished his work spoke to the charioteer.

maturi marutaṉ iḷanākaṉār

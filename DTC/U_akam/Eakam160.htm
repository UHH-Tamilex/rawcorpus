{{Readable Translation}}

  • O you with oil-restrined(?) hir, [is] it thus for you too?
  • My heart in [its] fullness shivered [and] is pitiable.
  • At night came indeed the firm chariot,
  • so that those drooping as high snakes' heads,
  • the blue water-lilies with bundled buds whose tipped faces were shortened
  • by the wheels, when the horses were swimming the cool backwaters
  • with abundant water, [horses] of graceful git that practised [their] arrow task,
  • upon being shown[FNote_57] the lash, for [inducing] the possession of leaping(?)[FNote_58],
  • softly(?), being afrid of it being too much, if they experience the thorn,
  • of the man from the seashore grove coast[FNote_59], where the split-mouthed husband
  • [of the tortoise] protects, as much as the young ones take room,
  • the eggs, smelling of fish, in the shape of horn[FNote_60] pawns,
  • secretly brought forth [and] buried by the fully pregnant tortoise,
  • after having joined the side of heaped white sand
  • by the curved backwaters, bending, so that they are spoiled, the Aṭumpu creepers.
  • Without hiding
  • it has indeed come at midday, superior in leaping motion,
  • for the noisy little village to see,
  • with strong mouths, along with the youths that sound "ol"(?)[FNote_61].

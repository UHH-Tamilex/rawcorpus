NOTES

The loud sound produced by muḻavu when it is played on at a high pitch is compared to the noise of thunder;
""tirumaḻai talaiiya iruḷ niṟa vicumpiṉ, viṇ atir imiḻ icai kaṭuppap, paṇ amaittut, tiṇvār vicitta muḻavoṭu (malaipaṭukatām, 1-3)

For the adjectivel participle poḻinta we have to supply the subject "mēkam'

araviṉ paintalai iṭaṟi: Talaivaṉ coming at night kicking the head of the cobra suggests the dense darkness and his coming unmindful of the dangers in the ways.

paintalai If taken as a changed of form of the word paittalai then it will mean the head of the snake with a hood.

akala vaḷaii - may be split into akala aḷaii; aḷaii will mean having mixed with us.

There is a variant reading in .7-8."muṉitōṟu iṟṟākural'; the meaning for that is everytime he dislikes, it is of this nature!

The new commentators have adopted the reading teṟṟākutal; but teṟṟu will not mean clearness; it should be read as tēṟṟu as there is no difference between short and long letters in palm-leaf manuscripts.

kaṭuñcūl - the first pregnancy; as it is attended with acute labour pains it is called so;
""kaṭuñcūl muṇṭakam mutaṟcūlaiyuṭaiya kaḻimuḷḷi'' (ciṟuppāṇaṟṟuppatai, 148);
"kaṉṉiyāy niṉṟu arumputaliṉ kaṭuñcūl' (special commentary for that line by nacciṉarakkiṉiyar)

The male elephat shampoos with its trunk the female which has given birth to a calf, in order to remove its weakness

muyaṅkutoṟu muyaṅka Instead of splitting it as muyaṅku toṟum uyaṅki - we can split it as muyaṅkutoṟum muyaṅki; it may be interpreted as embracing without stopping.

According to the tuṟai "tōḻi colleṭuppat talaimakaṉ colliyatu will mean: when the friend began "naṟku aṟintaṉamāyiṉ'' and before she could finish it, talaimakal replied thus : ""nāṭaṉ mārpiṉai avaṉ muyaṅkutōṟum mukintu koṇṭu aṭakkuvam''.

The inner meaning of the description of talaivaṉ's mountain is this : The female elephant after giving birth to a calf sleeps in the hill on which clouds traverse, being shampooed by its male, denotes that talaivi desires that she should become a mother in the wealthy mansion of talaivaṉ and should perform hourse hold duties to be praised by talaivaṉ. 

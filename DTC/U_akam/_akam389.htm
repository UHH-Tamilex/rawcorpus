TRANSLATION
  • Friend ! May you flourish ! you talk without knowing the facts (1)
  • Having protected me (8)
  • in the following ways by smearing red pigment in the red, beautiful and small feet (7)
  • which have small ankles (7),
  • drawing figures on the big shoulders (6),
  • sticking inner petals (5),
  • nipping fresh flowers of many petals to be in contrast in colour (4)
  • in the good, young and beautiful breasts, (5),
  • fixing the circular mark on the beautiful forehead which has a sweet smell (3),
  • having dried (2)
  • the curly locks of hir (1)
  • on which a paste has been smeared, by passing the fingers through them (2)
  • and adorning them with flowers (2),
  • and speaking prisingly about you (8)
  • (our talivar) used to be in the house formerly (10)
  • without leaving, even in the daytime, the bed on which many flowers are spread (9),
  • has now gone crossing the paths in the mountins which has tall trees (24)
  • where in the place adjacent to the cruel forest of intense heat (17),
  • which is situated beyond the fertile country of vaṉavarampaṉ (16)
  • of strong vēl (15),
  • the big tiger of bright colour (20)
  • which killed the big male elephant (20)
  • which stays in a different place leaving its own place, desirous of killing (19)
  • to make the new travellers to cry aloud running in different directions (18)
  • by jumping and bending its legs (21),
  • in the ground made red by blood (21),
  • to make it fell on the right side (22),
  • roars its roar to be echoed by the mountin (22)
  • like the thunder produced in the rining cloud (23);
  • to establish good fame in their places (15),
  • and to destroy the affliction born out of the mean and ridiculing words (14)
  • of enemies, (13)
  • thinking of acquiring rare wealth (13),
  • which is the cuse for rejoicing of protectors (11)
  • after having given new things (12)
  • returning quickly (10)
  • with a mind not knowing grief (12)
  • and filling the stretched-out hands of supplicants (11).

NOTES

அணிந்து the clitic உம் should be added to this word also, as that word is found in ll 3, 5 and 7.

If எற்புறந்தந்த is the reading the meaning will be : you who protected me having adorned me in all these ways.

புதுவ wealth that was newly acquired.

புரப்போர்(11) துனைதந்து(10) உவக்கும்(12) is the syntactical order.

தந்து உவக்கும்: rejoicing seeing the gleaming face of the recipients to whom money was given.

Enemies will speak ridiculing words about people who have no money; great souls will not brook such words; cf. ""கறுத்தோர், எள்ளல் நெஞ்சத்து ஏஎச் சொல் நாணி'' (akam, 111-1-2)

வானவரம்பன் : this is the family name of cēra Kings; the meaning is one who royal authority extended upto the celestial world; ""வான வரம்பனை நீயோபெரும'' (puṟam, 2-12); வானவரம்பனெனப் பேரினிது விளக்கி'' (patikam of patiṟṟuppattu, 6th decad)

There was a curam beyond the frontiers of cērantu; this is found in an old vempa that mentions the boundaries of Konkunāṭu; ""வடக்குப் பெரும் பாலை வைகாவூர் தெற்குக், குடக்குப் பொருப்பு வெள்ளிக் குன்று-கிடக்கும், களித் தண்டலை மேவும் காவிரி சூழ் நாட்டுக், குழித்தண்டலையளவும் கொங்கு'' This is mentioned as eighteen in number in the three ulās by oṭṭakūttar; ""பாண்பகலொன்றின் ஈரொன்பது சுரமும், கொண்டு மலை நாடு கொண்டோனும்'' (vikkirama cōḻaṉula, 17); ""ஏறிப்பக லொன்றில் எச்சுரமும் போய் உதகை, நூற்றித்தன் தூதனை நோக்கினான்'' (Kulottunkaṉula, 24); ""மதகயத்தால் ஈரொன்பது சுரமு மட்டித்து உதகையைத் தீத்த உரவோன்'' (rācarācacoḻaṉula, 21)

Talivi replied to the friend who asked her to put up with the separation by telling her, that, when the talivar who did not leave her even during daytime by protecting her in so many ways and speaking prisingly about the friend, had gone into the cruel forest-paths beset with dangers, how could it be possible for talivi to do so ? 

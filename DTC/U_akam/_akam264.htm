TRANSLATION
  • The leather berried birdwood creeper which has rich foliage blossoms white flowers (2)
  • like the cloudless sky which is adorned with stars (1)
  • The cowherds who carry forked sticks (4)
  • adorn themselves abundantly (4)
  • with the white flowers of white malabar glory lily having lines, which has curved bunches (3)
  • return home with many herds of cows, (6)
  • which are wet in the day with rin (5)
  • with their chaplets wet with rin water (6),
  • The clouds which are full of water spread out high in the sky (8),
  • and pour heavily (7)
  • and on account of that the rising swollen water flows swiftly in every street (8)
  • The autumn season has arrived and come to stay (10)
  • with thunders of great noise joining with one another (9),
  • Does our husband (10)
  • who is staying in the war-camp at the battle-field of the cruelly angry king (15)
  • who has stationed his army to strike terror into his enemy (14)
  • where the elephants of beautiful git (12)
  • and burning anger are besieging enemy at night (13)
  • realize (12)
  • his nature (11),
  • though he may not know our nature (11)?

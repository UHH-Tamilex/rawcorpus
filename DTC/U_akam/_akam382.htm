TRANSLATION
  • Friend (8) !
  • You desired and accepted the words of the chief of the hilly tract superior to other mountins (13),
  • having groves that never know drying up (12)
  • where the female monkey of black fingers which plunged into the mountin stream, (9)
  • gets down in the tuṟi (துறை) of the big village situated in the adjacent hill (11)
  • using the fruit of the jack tree which bears fruits abundantly, as a float (10)
  • If others see other's suffering, they will sympathize and try to remove it (1),
  • (not only that); they will think lightly about their own suffering without thinking it to be big (2),
  • you must tell talivaṉ that our relations have slowly (6)
  • desired to perform the vaṟi dance (7)
  • to make the big arena for that to shine (6)
  • to be in unison with the songs prising netuvēḷ (நெடுவேள்: tall murukaṉ) whose abode is the forest (5)
  • the sweet instruments of a particular musical notes tuned of many different sounds played to the measure of time (4),
  • having worn garlands (chaplets) and having tied flags to the Katampu (கடம்பு) tree (3),
  • "when you see him so as to be understood by him.

NOTES

As it is the nature of great souls to sympathize with other's suffering and not mind their own suffering, the friend sid, that if talivaṉ knew our suffering he would try to remove it as quickly as possible.

காடு Here it is used to denote mountin tract, called Kuruñci.

Here தோழி means talivi, as both of them used to address each other as friend ! 

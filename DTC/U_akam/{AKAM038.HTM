AKAM 038

((Originally extracted from master file ^AKAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 2nd))
  • [AN_38-1] viri iṇar vēṅki vaṇṭu paṭu kaṇṇiyaṉ
  • [AN_38-2] teri itaḻ kuvaḷi tēm pāy tāraṉ
  • [AN_38-3] am cili iṭavatu āka ve celal
  • [AN_38-4] kaṇi valam terintu tuṇi paṭarntu uḷḷi
  • [AN_38-5] varutal vāyvatu vāṉ tōy veṟpaṉ
  • [AN_38-6] vantaṉaṉ āyiṉ am taḷir ceyali
  • [AN_38-7] tāḻvu il ōṅku ciṉi toṭutta vīḻ kayiṟṟu
  • [AN_38-8] ūcal māṟiya maruṅku um pāypu uṭaṉ
  • [AN_38-9] āṭāmiyiṉ kaluḻpu ila tēṟi
  • [AN_38-10] nīṭu itaḻ tali_iya kaviṉ peṟu nīlam
  • [AN_38-11] kaṇ eṉa malarnta cuṉi um vaṇ paṟi
  • [AN_38-12] maṭa kiḷi eṭuttal cellā taṭa kural
  • [AN_38-13] kulavu poṟi iṟutta kōl tali iruvi
  • [AN_38-14] koytu oḻi puṉam um nōkki neṭitu niṉintu
  • [AN_38-15] pitalaṉ peyaralaṉ kol ō ai tēyku
  • [AN_38-16] aya veḷ aruvi cūṭiya uyar vari
  • [AN_38-17] kūum kaṇaḵtu em ūr eṉa
  • [AN_38-18] āṅku ati aṟivuṟal maṟanticiṉ yāṉ ē

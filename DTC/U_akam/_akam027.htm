AKAM 27

(27) pāli

  • Lines (1-6) The companion admonishing the talivi
  • (7-8) The beauty of the teeth and looks of the talivi and their power to detin talivaṉ

Translation

  • "(Talivi) you say people in this place talk that (our talivar) will go to the forest not thinking it to be dreadful, in order to acquire wealth which cannot be sid to have stayed permanently in any particular place, out of love of lucre, to make us weep" (3-5)
  • That forest has severe paths where the big and solid bamboos with tossing twigs which are dashed by the west wind, droop and bend so that the big tigers with curved stripes on them (that are in hiding) become visible! (1-2)
  • You are definitely ignorant (6).
  • Though our talivar won't stay here being arrested by your lips which are like coral in which are set sparkling teeth having become great for their smile, (10-11)
  • and which are like the pearls that are produced in the big port of Koṟki (koṟkai) which belongs to Pāṇṭiyar who rule virtuously who are skilled in valourous battles and who possess elephants of white tusks born in the region of Vēṅkaṭam in the north, (6-11),
  • How can the opposing looks of your eyes which are coated with collyrium and which have streaks in them (17),
  • and which, not being content with destroying the beauty of the blue nelumbo (kuvaḷai) flower by vanquishing it which has cylindrical stalk and which has absorbed into it water to produce honey (12-14)
  • resemble the bright lance which was waved after being soaked in blood, and was helpful in gining victory in open fight with kings (in the field), for the kites to surround, allow him to go away from here? (14-17)

Syntactical link: "(tōḻi!) (namtalaivar) kāṉam kaṭiya eṉṉār nām aḻa(3, niṉṟa til poruṭpiṇic ceṉṟu ivaṇ tarumār(4), celpa eṉpa" eṉpōy(5); nīmaṭavaimaṉṟa(6) tuvarvāy(10) takaippat taṅkalarāyiṉum(11) niṉ(16) uṇkaṇ amartta nōkku(17) ikappa(11) yāṅṅamaivakum(12)?

The companion told the talimakaḷ who had some change in her, having known the parting (of talivaṉ).

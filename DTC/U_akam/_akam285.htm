NOTES

The adverbial participle akala in line 3 will modify eṉṟaṉar in line 14.

kāṭu tērpiṇai - the female deer which searches for its stag in the forest.

oṭukuṟaṅku - the thigh which is helpful to fleet fast.

ēṟu-eṟital; pouncing upon; ""kūrucirpparuttiṉ ēṟu kuṟittu ōrīi'' (puṟam 43-5).

The commentators have taken cevi in line 12 as an abbreviation of cevvi and given the meaning accordingly. But in akam 161, line 5 ""eruvai is described as ""ceñcevi eruvaic cēval'' in puṟam, it is described as ""kaḻukoṭu ceñ cevi eruvai tiritarum'', ""ceñcevi eruvai kuḻīi'' (puṟam 370-25-26, 273-3-8) In the old commentary on puṟam, stanza 64, line, 4, the commentator has given the meaning of eruvi as a kind of kite whose head is white and the nest of the body is red. The ear is mentioned in all the three quotations from akam and puṟam. Perhaps this bird had the knack of turning its ears towards the direction where it could find its prey. So the new travellers were keen on noticing which way it turned its ears, to know the direction. So it would be more correct to take cevi to mean ear, as in many quotations the ear is particularly mentioned. 

TRANSLATION
  • (Friend)! Having stood at the gate like the bards who receive female elephants as presents (1),
  • and having opened (5)
  • the strong and firm door (3)
  • of the broad mansion which is well guarded by a wall of enclosure (2)
  • and which appears as if it is inscribed (3),
  • at dead of night when the demons begin to wander, and when the guards are sleeping (4),
  • and having had union with us (5),
  • saying that none was superior to us (5)
  • in this world (6),
  • and rubbing gently our luxuriant tresses with love (7)
  • (our lover) parted from us having made the purpose of his parting clear by words expressing grace (14).
  • Even if he had gone beyond (13)
  • the desert path (13)
  • difficult to travel (12)
  • situated beyond the good country of pulli (12)
  • where in the hot summer, water is dried up and all the four directions are scorching by hot (8),
  • the cowherds, protect the wayfarers who come that way (9)
  • and have with them (11)
  • the rice mixed with sweet tamarind kept in bamboos and tied to the neck of the young bull (10),
  • serving it on the teak leaf (11),
  • to remove their temporary deafness cused by acute hunger (12),
  • will not stay there longer than in necessary (and return without delay) (13).

NOTES

The bards who receive female elephants as presents will stand aside at the gate of the mansions of liberal donors.

It was the custom among cowherds to carry their food in bamboos tied to the neck of bulls; it is mentioned in akam, in a previous verse also;
""துளங்கு இமில் ஏற்றுத், தழூஉப்பிணர் எருத்தம் தாழப் பூட்டிய அம் தூம்பு அகல அமைக்கலம் செலப் பெய்த, துறுகாழ் வல்சிபர்'' (253-13-16).
""கானிலை எருமைக்கழை பெய்தீம் தயிர்'' (malipaṭukaṭān, 523)

செ(வி)யடை the state when the ear loses temporarily the power of hearing due to acute hunger.

புல்லி நன்னாடு:-The country of pulli was situated beyond vEṅkatam, the northern boundary of Tamiḻnātu. It is mentioned in the following quotations;
""மழபுலம் வணக்கிய மாவண் புல்லி, விழவுடை விழுச்சீர் வேங்கடம் பெறினும்'',
""கல்லா இளையர் பெருமகன் புல்லி, வியன்தலை நன்னாட்டு வேங்கடம் கழியினும்'',
""மாஅல்யா*ஷ* மறம் போர்ப்புல்லி, காம்புடை, நெடுவரை வேங்கடத்தும்பர்''
(akam, 61-12-13, 83-9-10/ 209-10);
""புல்லிய, வேங்கட விறல் வரை''
(Puṟam, 385-10-11) 

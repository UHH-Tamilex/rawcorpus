  • [AN_321-11] tuṇiyoṭu tuccil irukkum kol ō
  • [AN_321-12] kaṇiyōr añcā kaṭuṅkaṇ kāḷiyoṭu
  • [AN_321-13] elli muṉṉuṟa cellum kol ō

  • [AN_321-17] vēy uyar piṟaṅkal mali iṟantōḷ ē

  • [AN_321-1] pacitta yāṉai paḻaṅkaṇ aṉṉa
  • [AN_321-2] vaṟum cuṉai mukanta kōṭai teḷ viḷi
  • [AN_321-3] vicittu vāṅku paṟaiyiṉ viṭar akattu iyampa
  • [AN_321-4] katir kāl am piṇai uṇīiya pukal ēṟu
  • [AN_321-5] kutir kāl iruppai veḷ pū uṇṇātu
  • [AN_321-6] āṇ kural viḷikkum cēṇ pāl viyaṉ curai
  • [AN_321-7] paṭu maṇi iṉa nirai uṇīiya kōvalar
  • [AN_321-8] viṭu nilam uṭaitta kaluḻ kaṇ kūval
  • [AN_321-9] kaṉṟu uṭai maṭa piṭi kaḷiṟoṭu taṭavarum
  • [AN_321-10] puṉ talai maṉṟattu am kuṭi cīṟūr
  • [AN_321-11] tuṇaiyoṭu tuccil irukkum kol ō
  • [AN_321-12] kaṇaiyōr añcā kaṭuṅkaṇ kāḷaiyoṭu
  • [AN_321-13] elli muṉṉuṟa cellum kol ō
  • [AN_321-14] e viṉai ceyum kol nōku ō yāṉ ē
  • [AN_321-15] ari peytu potinta teri cilampu kaḻīi
  • [AN_321-16] yāy aṟivuṟutal añci
  • [AN_321-17] vēy uyar piṟaṅkal malai iṟantōḷ ē

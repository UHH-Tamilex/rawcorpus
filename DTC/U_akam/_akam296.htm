TRANSLATION
  • (Chief!) Having enjoyed aquatic sports and bathed in the floods of viyi today too, (5)
  • and having slept in the fragrant grove yesterday also (4)
  • with a self-conceiting harlot of black complexion having cool eyes in the white portion of which there are lines, (3)
  • who has tresses of hir on which were adorned sweet-smelling pollen of fully blossomed buds (2)
  • of river portia tree of short trunk and with bunches of flowers which are like a garland (1),
  • and having returned from there and standing before us, with a chest still wet, (6)
  • can you conceal that incident (7)
  • from us (6) ?
  • The ruler of Koṟki of great fame (10)
  • where the fishermen who catch different kinds of fish (8)
  • also draw from the expanse of the sea oysters contining pearls and pay them as price for the pride they derive from taking the toddy strined by the web of the cocoanut tree (9);
  • pacumpūtceḻiyaṉ has a victorious lance (10),
  • rough and large elephants, tall chariots, (11)
  • When he gesticulated in Kūṭal of high places resembling hills, (10)
  • the clamour was full; like that the incident is on the lips of many people and has become the subject of scandal (14)

NOTES

special notes :- The tree காஞ்சி blossoms flowers in bunches; this is found in Kuṟuntoki also; ""பயறு போலிணர.......காஞ்சியூரன்'' (2-4) (2-4)

Women adorn their tresses with the pollen also; this is found in tirukkōviyār and peruṅkati; ""அளிநீடகைத்தின அதட்டிய தாதும்'' (122); ""அலமரு திருமுகத் தலிகத தப்பிய, செம் பொற்சுண்ணம்'' (1.33-119-120). "காவிரியும் தண்பொருனையும் ஆன்பொருனையும் கையையுமு/ போலும் யாற்றிலும், இருகாமத்திணை யேரிபோலும் குளங்களிலும் திருமருதந்துறைக் காவே போலும் காக்களிலும் விளையாடி, உறை பதியைக் கடந்து போய் நுகர்ச்சி எய்துதலும், தலைவற்கும் காமக்கிழத்தியர்க்கும் தலைவியர்க்கும் உரிய' (tolkāppiyam, poruḷiyal 50, nacciṉārkkiṉiyar's commentary) may be compared with the lines 3-5).

The poet being a native of maturi has mentioned viyi.

புலராமார்பினை: may mean this also; talivaṉ standing before talivi with the marks made by the harlot which are still visible.

இப்பி stands for the pearl inside it.

pacumpūṭpānṭiyaṉ won a victory over Koṅkar; his warriors performed a dance out of joy in his capital, kūṭal; ""அலரே, வாடாப் பூவிற் கொங்கர் ஓட்டி, நாகு பல தந்த பசும் பூண் பாண்டியன், பொன்மலி நெடு நகர்க் கூடல் ஆடிய, இன்னிசை ஆர்ப்பினும் பெரிதே'' (அகம். 253-3-7).

The new commentators have given the name of paṇṭiyaṉ as neṭuñceḻiyaṉ and has given the above lines as a parallel quotation; it is wrong, pacumpūṇ paṇtiyaṉ performed the dance with his warriors to express his joy born of victory

Oysters are found in such abundance in Koṟki that fishermen not knowing their value given them a price for toddy.

In line 14 ஆகின்றது should be split into ஆகின்று அது;

In naṟṟiṇi in two places the word "ஆகின்றது' is found in print 227-9, 354-11, but "ஆகின்றது' is a later usage; in those two places also the form of the word should be only ஆகின்று 

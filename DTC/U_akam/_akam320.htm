(1-5). Description of the maritime tract of talivaṉ where girls of the fishermen caste sell fish cught by their brothers in their boats.
(6-7). talivaṉ became unfaithful to his word as not to remove the distress of talivi and the scandal of the village.
(8-10). The description of the big ghat on the sea-shore.
(11-12). talivaṉ coming in his chariot so as to destroy the doll made on the sand during talivi's play.
(13-14). talivaṉ swearing before the god of the sea on the first day of meeting. Friend questioning whether it was also a false one.

TRANSLATION
  • Chief of the maritime tract having small hamlets surrounded by sea-shore groves (5)
  • where the petted sisters who wear on their waist a garment of leaves (3)
  • sell in the streets where festivals are celebrated (4)
  • the ferocious and strong fish cught by (their brothers) in their boats (2)
  • dragging the fishing nets with great force in the expanse of the rising waves in the ocean (1)!
  • Even if you become unfaithful to your word by not bestowing your grace on my friend (7),
  • who has eyes ((that are)) as beautiful as flowers and ((that are)) taking in collyrium (6),
  • to remove her distress (6)
  • and the scandal of the village folk (7),
  • Is the oath that you swore (14)
  • on that day when you had first union with her (13),
  • coming in your tall chariot having a part resembling lotus bud, cutting the loose fine sand (11)
  • so as to destroy the doll made of sand during play (12)
  • in the big ghat which was made beautiful by the pollen of the puṉṉi tree (புன்னை; mast wood tree) of curved stature (10),
  • where the swan of short legs (18)
  • adjusts its feather with its beak and dries them (9)
  • (eating the fish) after stirring the long back-water (for fish), (8)
  • sitting on the mound of sand on which the creeper of aṭumpu (அடும்பு) is resting (9),
  • also false (14)
  • for which the sea bore a witness (14) ?

NOTES

திமிலோன் - here means the brothers who own fishing boats, as in the 3rd line their sisters are mentioned.

கடுங்கண் வயமீன்

- may mean shark. After stirring the back-water for fish the feathers become wet; so the swan adjusts its feathers and makes them dry.

Talivaṉ coming to meet talivi when she was playing with her mates is mentioned in akam, 110-7-9.

தோன்புதிது உண்டரான்றை - a polished of saying `having had union!;

புதிது உண்ணல் - enjoying the fresh thing;
""உழாஅது வித்திய பரூஉக்குரற் சிறுதினை, முந்து வினையாணர் நாட்பு திது உண்மார்'' (puṟam, 168-6-7);
""உய்யாமல் மலைந்து உமர்'' ஆருயிரை, மெய்யாக இராமன் விகந்திடவே, கையாரமுகந்து கொடந்தகனார், ஐயாபுதிது உண்டது அறிந்திலையோ'' (Kamparāmāyaṇam, catāyu uyir nītta patalam, 96).

சூள்: the oath that talivaṉ swore; He banished talivi's doubts by saying, `I won't part from you; if I do that I shall be committing sinful acts". This is mentioned in akam, 5-16-17 as ""அறத்தாறு அன்றென மொழிந்த தொன்று படுகிளவி''.

கடல் here means the deity that resides in the ocean;
""கரியமலர் நெடுங்கட் காரிகை முன் கடல் தெய்வம் காட்டிக் காட்டி, அரிய சூள் பொய்த்ததார் அறனிலர் என்று ஏழையம் யாங்கு அறிகோ ஐய'' (cilappatikāram, 6-5, ll.1-2) also substantiates this.

The oath may be this also; `Talivaṉ sid at the time when he had the union with talivi (இயற்கைப் புணர்ச்சி) "I shall marry you and lead a house-holder's life"

As it is a grave error to go back on one's oath the friend urged talivaṉ to marry talivi as early as possible.

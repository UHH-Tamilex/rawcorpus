TRANSLATION
  • Companion ! (13)
  • Our talivar crossed the forest where the stag with twisted horns (18)
  • and its young female nibble the bowstring hemp with lines on it (17)
  • which grows in abundance on the wet high level lands (in the paths of the desert tract in miḷināṭu (மிளைநாடு) (4)
  • (There) the flowers of scarlet ixora (வெட்சிமலர்) which have mature buds and which resemble the hooked claw of (இதல்): (patridge or rin-quail) drops on the stony rocks and spreads with flowers of wild lime tree (குருந்தமரம்) which grows in the millet farm (தினைப்புனம்) which has been burnt and brought under cultivation (14-15)
  • In that sylvan region which has green bushes becuse of the riny season that has set in (5)
  • the flowers of east indian kino tree (வேங்கைமலர்) which blossom to indicate the day on which marriagess could be celebrated, (4)
  • drop on the dust in the mound of gravel which contins red pebbles, (3)
  • and which was scratched by the male of the white rats which has slender legs, (2)
  • a chin, hirs of bright colour, and eyes comparable to the red seed of a crab's eye (1);
  • (Those flowers) add beauty to the place as to appear like a stage where the superior dance of veṟi (வெறி) is performed (4)
  • (In that sylvan region) of the small hillocks which have plots of ground newly brought under cultivation for dry crops, the white clouds seem to traverse gently in the small adjoining hills like the cotton which has been pulled thinly with a contrivance called bow and carded (6-7).
  • Our talivar put me a big question sometimes back, "Are you capable of coming along with me and would you drink only the tiny particules of water in the forest river which are rised high by the wind?" (10-13)
  • The forest river of sweet water carries on its surface the old flowers of iron wood tree (காயாமரம்) which resembles charcoal that is spread out combining with the flowers of silk-cotton having thorns, (இலவு) resembling fire that is spread out and flowing with those flowers (8-10)
  • (But he has not since carried out his words into practice)

  • [AN_37-11] kayam maṇṭu pakaṭṭiṉ paruki kāṇvara
  • [AN_37-12] koḷḷoṭu payaṟu pāl virai_i veḷḷi
  • [AN_37-13] kōl varaintaṉṉa vāl aviḻ mitavai

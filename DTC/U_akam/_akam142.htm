    NOTES

    Miñili fed the devils by performing a sacrifice on the battle field which is known by the name of Kaḷavēḷvi (kaḷavēḷvi) in Tamil. As the name of chieftin with whom Miñili fought is found as Āy Eyiṉaṉ (āy eyiṉaṉ) in stanzas 148: 7-8, 181 : 5-7, 208: 5-7, 296 : 2-6, we have to infer that Atikaṉ may be another name for eyiṉaṉ. We may adopt the reading (eyiṉaṉ) as the author of all the stanzas is paraṇar, taking it to be a scribal error.

    That fish sleep at night is also found in Naṟṟiṇi (219-10)

    (niṟaiyiruntāṉai may be mistaken for niṟaiyaruntāṉai; cf. puṟam (156-5).

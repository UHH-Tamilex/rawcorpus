TRANSLATION
  • Friend ! May you live long (3)
  • Do not get immersed in grief (3)
  • thinking our lover will forget us and stay there, having the strength of mind to part from us (1)
  • and has left us (1)
  • Our lover has crossed (9)
  • the long desert region which is devoid of water and has tooth brush trees which have no shade (8)
  • where the big boulders (5)
  • on which the branches of coral tree of ripe bud (5)
  • and raw long fruits with stem which resemble in shape the bending tooth (4)
  • of the boar (3)
  • appear like an elephant in the forest (16)
  • surrounded by fire (7)
  • which is kindled by the wind of very high velocity, makes one wonder-struck. How can our lover (9)
  • get sleep staying there and forgetting us (10).
  • He slept on your tresses which were washed (16)
  • and adorned with the cool and fragrant garlands made of blue nelumbo flowers (14)
  • together with the wild jasmine flowers which grow in the caves (14),
  • having completely combed that curly round of minute black colour which resembles the swarms of tumpi (a kind of bee) (12-13)
  • and falls low on the back, (10)
  • and rests on your shoulders resembling bamboo (11)

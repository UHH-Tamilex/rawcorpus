TRANSLATION
  • The mother will get angry without any use (12)
  • even if she sees the talivi playing with her mates wearing garlands, building toy houses in the shore having sand-dunes heaped by the northern wind (9-10)
  • saying, `your brightness will emaciate! (11)
  • If mother sees the marks made by the bangles (12)
  • she who knows no consideration of virtue (15),
  • will place our talivi under more severe guard (15)
  • than the wealth that was pid by the enemies' countries (14)
  • and which was kept guarded at Kuṭanti (kuṭantai-kuṭavāyil) by the victorious Cōḻar (13)
  • who had a victorious lance (12).
  • Please do not press into close contact the bright bangles of ours (7)
  • who can be compared to Toṇti belonging to Poṟayaṉ of strong and well built chariots (7);
  • where the young girl serves (6)
  • her father who had been engaged in the business of catching fish (3)
  • seated in the long fishing boat (1)
  • with the cruel nets to which are fixed stright sticks (2),
  • and to catch fish to their maximum capacity (2)
  • to make the red prawn to tremble in the expanse of the big ocean (1),
  • with boiled rice resembling teeth (4)
  • got by exchanging salt for paddy (4),
  • by mixing with it the curry contining tamarind juice with well cooked loach ayilai (5)
  • fish and, salted and dried Koḻumīṉ (koḻumiṉ) (6).

TRANSLATION
  • (Charioteer!) In the darkness at dead of night (3)
  • the white flowers of leather berried birdweed (முசுண்டை-முசுட்டை) which has leaves heaping at the end (2)
  • and which grows luxuriantly (1)
  • in the adjoining spaces in great quantity near the peak where honey-combs are built (by bees), (1)
  • blossom like the stars in the sky (3)
  • The shepherd who has on his back a mat of palmyra leaves and who has collected the young kids of sheep has adorned himself with a chaplet from which water is dripping, on which bees hum swarming (6)
  • and which is made by him in combination with the cool and fragrant arabian jasmine (முல்லைப்பூ,) with white species of gloriosa superba (தோன்றிப்பூ) flowers, (5)
  • warms his palms in the firebrand which is soft and makes a long whistling sound to scare away the small foxes in the thick darkness (8).
  • That sound combines with the sound of the big black horn (11)
  • which the people who keep guard over the ripe millet farm (10) (தினைப்புனம்)
  • make to drive away the big herd of wild pigs (9)
  • of small eyes, anticipating the time of their coming into the fields, (10),
  • and sounds simultaneously (11),
  • in the region consisting of hard soil of jungle tracts (12)
  • The dwelling place of the talivi who wears flawless ornaments and has black, luxuriant tresses (14),
  • exceedingly gentle nature, affection (13)
  • born of desire (12)
  • for a thing one wishes to have, is situated inside that country, (12)
  • (So, drive the chariot speedily)

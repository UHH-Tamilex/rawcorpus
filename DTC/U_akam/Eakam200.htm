{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if it [is] not a pleasant abode,
  • encircled[FNote_77] by water, of a smallness as not to be perceived as a village,
  • with [its] grass-covered huts in a quarter reeking of fish,
  • in a street where sand is much, shining like moonlight, -
  • up, o you who have come[FNote_78] to our place, a pleasure to whoever have
  • stid a single day, that makes [them] forget their own place the other day!
  • After spending the day time with us,
  • having appreciated many things in a spot high-grown with trees,
  • where the waves of the extension grazed by shells[FNote_79],
  • coming in thick, keep breaking quietly(?)[FNote_80],
  • [now] that night comes you have to yoke[FNote_81] [your] good chariot.
  • If it is not so [and] you were to go [and] join [us][FNote_82], we all of us(?)[FNote_83]
  • will protect [you], according to our limits.
  • Is [this] agreeable to you? Tell us!

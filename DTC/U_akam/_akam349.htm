TRANSLATION
  • Friend (3)!
  • Our lover who went beyond the curaṉ which is situated in a country where a different languaged is spoken (14),
  • where the curved and emanciated trunk (12)
  • of the male elephant (11)
  • which is placed on its spotted forehead (12),
  • appears like the snake which crawls on the rock (13),
  • after eating the mouthful of flowers resembling fire (11),
  • of the vēṅki (வேங்கை: east indian kino tree) of black trunk which is tall and perfect (10),
  • and which are of bright colour growing in the middle portion of the slopes of the ēḻil hill (9)
  • which is situated in the good country of naṉṉaṉ (8)
  • who, without thinking whether they deserve it, or not, gives liberally (8)
  • big ornaments of high value, after destroying the difficult fortresses of enemies (7)
  • devastating their battle-fronts with a mind of very great strength, when minstrels who have impoverished relations, approach him (5),
  • and who speaks faultless true words (4)
  • which can be compared to the pointer in the balance (3)
  • which correctly measure things (4);
  • what was the idea he had in his mind to make the former beauty which we had by his marriage when he cught hold of my forearm (2)
  • which wears tight-fitting beautiful beangles which were made by filing (conches) (1)?

NOTES

பிணித்த cught hold cf. The bridegroom catches hold of the right hand of the bride during wedding. That is called `pāṇigrahaṇam in sanskrit;
cf. ""நேரிறை முன்கை பற்றி நுமர்தர, நாடு அறிநன்மணம் அயர்கம்'' (Kuṟiñcuppāṭṭu, 231-232).

முன்கைவரைந்து தாம் பிணித்த
- the order of words should be changed into முன்கையைப் பிணித்து ரைந்த- to be correct to the sense.

தொல்கவின்

- the beauty that talivi had on the occasion of her marriage with talivaṉ.

செஞம்ன்

- here it is அளபெடை: the meaning is pointer in the balance; it is called சமன்வாய் in tamiḻ
""தெரிகோல் ஞமன்ன் போல ஒருதிறம் பற்றல் இலியரோ'' (puṟam 6-9).
""துலைத்தாலம் அன்னதினி நிலை'' (Kampārāyaṇam 2 - mantarippaṭalam, 19).

செம்மொழி - is an epithet qualifying naṉṉaṉ.

சுரக்கும்: adjectival participle qualifying naṉaṉ; The ēḻil hill belonging to naṉṉaṉ is mentioned in akam in other stanzas also
""நன்னன் ஏழி ல் நெடுவரைப் பாழிச் சிலம்பில்'' (152-12-13);
see also the notes for akam 345-7l ll.10-12.

The elephant eats the flowers of vēṅki for want of better food and its hunger has not decreased and therefore is diminishing in strenghth.

The elephants trunk is compared to the snake;
""யானைதன், வாய் நிறை கொண்ட வலிதேம்பு தடக்கை, குன்று புகுபாம்பின் தோன்றும்'' (akam, 391-11-13);
""நிரை கதிர் நீள் எஃகம் நீட்டி வயவர், வரை புரை யானைக்கை நூறவரைமேல் உரும் எறி, பாம்பின் புரளும்'' (Kaḷavaḻi nāṟpatu, 13);
""பாந்தள் ""அன்ன பரேரெறுழ்த்தடக் கையின்'' (peruṅkati, 1-58-8);
""அந்தன் அன்னகை யானை'' (cīvaka cintāmaṇi, 2521). 

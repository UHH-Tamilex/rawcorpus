Table of contents (by lines):
1-6 Talivaṉ feeling for the pitiable condition of talivi in her house.
7-10 The desert tract having lost its beauty compared to the eye of the Kiṇi belonging to atiyaṉ which became silent after his death.
10-15 Talivaṉ advising talivi to put up with the troubles and assuring her of elopement.
16-20 Talivaṉ going to the country of pāṇaṉ, to shatter all hopes of escape from troubles.
21-22. Talivi afrid of blame attaching to talivaṉ by cusing distemper to her.
Colophon(s):

NOTES

In lines 2-3. talivaṉ meeting talivi at night at the secretly appointed place for union is mentioned. As the mother suspected talivi of clandestine love she interned her inside the house and guarded her without a moment separation.

In the 3rd line the words kaṅkuṟpuṇarkuṟi vāytta may arranged like this; kaṅkuṟkuṟipuṇar vāytta

Lines 2-6 are the thoughts expressed by talivaṉ about talivi's condition;

aḷḷaṉ may be the commander of atiyaṉ's forces. Atiyaṉ should have met with his death in that battle.

eyiṟu here stands for the sweet water that secretes from the teeth;

Talivaṉ tasting and enjoying it may be found in the following quotations :
""maṭantai niṉ! kūr eyiṟu uṇku eṉa (naṟṟiṇi 204-6;
""kāl eṟi kaṭikaikkaṇ ayiṉṟaṉṉa, vāy eyiṟu ūṟiya vacaiil tīnīr, muṉḷeyiṟṟu amiḻtam ūṟum'', (kuṟuntoki, 263-3-4, 286-1-2);
""vāṉ nakai ilaṅku eyiṟṟu, cevvāy ""amiḻtu potituvarvāy'' (Patiṟṟuppattu, 51-20-21);
""muṉ uṟaḻ muḷaieyiṟṟu amiḻtu ūṟum tīnīraik, kaḷḷiṉum makiḻ ceyyum eṉa uraittumu/ amaiyār'',
""mella, muyaṅkuniṉ muṉ eyiṟu uṇkum'' (Kalittoaki, 4-13, 112-19-20);
""vai ēr vāl eyiṟu ūṟiya nīrē'',
""mūvāp pacuṅkāy, nīriṉum iṉiya vākik kūr eyiṟṟu, amiḻtam ūṟum cevvāy.... kuṟumakaḷ'' (akam, 237-17,335-23-26);
""pāloṭu tēṉ kalantaṟṟē paṇimoḻi, vāl eyiṟu ūṟayanīr'' (Kuṟaḷ 1121).

vāṭkaṇ vāṉattu: The order of these words has been changed by the new commentators as vāṉ vāṉattukkaṇ; The words may be in the order in which they are found in the poem;

kaṇ here means space; ""kaṇ akal ñālam'' (tirikaṭukam, invocatory verse)

perum piṟitu: death;
""iṉṉuyir perum piṟitu āyiṉum'' (naṟṟiṇi, 219-3);
""karuṅkaṇṭākkalai perumpiṟitu uṟṟeṉa'',
""aruntuyar uḻattalum āṟṟām; ataṉ talaip, perum piṟitu ākal ataṉiṉum añcutum'' (Kuṟuntoki, 69-1, 302-2-3);
""pēḻ vāy uḻuvaiyaip perum piṟitu uṟīi'' (puranāṉūṟu, 152-2)
""piḷḷai nakulam perumpiṟitu āka'' (cilappatikāram, 15-54)

pāṇaṉ the name of a chieftin; his country has been mentioned previously in akam
""eḻanaappāṇaṉ naṉṉāṭṭumpar'' ""valimikumuṉpiṉ pāṇaṉoṭu''
""pāṇar, mal aṭumārpiṉ valiyuṟa varunti'' (113-17, 226-13, 286-3-4)

Does this pāṇaṉ belong to the dynasty of bānas who later figure in South Indian History?

āṭkol yāṉai atar pārttu alkutal:
""kāṭu mīk kūṟum kōṭu ēntu oruttal, āṭi kaṭi koḷḷum aruñcuram'' (akam, 65-16-17);
""iṭunīṟu āṭiya kaṭu naṭai oruttal, āṭpeṟal nacaii nāṭkaram vilaṅkit, tuṉaitarum, vampalark kāṇātu acciṉam, pāṉaikkāṉṟu āṟum'' (naṟṟiṇi, 126-2-5);
""ciṟukaṇ yāṉai ḷ viḻttut tiritarum nīṉ iṭai aruñcuram'' (ainkuṟunūṟu, 314-3-4)

For the 22nd line it can be interpreted an in this way also;
Talivi sid, "What will be the blame that will attach to us". 

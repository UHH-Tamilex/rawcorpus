Table of contents (by lines):
1-6 Talivaṉ feeling for the pitiable condition of talivi in her house.
7-10 The desert tract having lost its beauty compared to the eye of the Kiṇi belonging to atiyaṉ which became silent after his death.
10-15 Talivaṉ advising talivi to put up with the troubles and assuring her of elopement.
16-20 Talivaṉ going to the country of pāṇaṉ, to shatter all hopes of escape from troubles.
21-22. Talivi afrid of blame attaching to talivaṉ by cusing distemper to her.

TRANSLATION
  • Friend May you live long ! Hear attentively what I say (1)
  • our lover (1)
  • was gracious (6)
  • when he sid (6),
  • "The clandestine love which developed between us by meeting in the secretly appointed place for Union during the long pitch dark night (3)
  • at a big house having a gate of grand appearance where white sand is spread to a great height (2),
  • has outgrown its limits (4).
  • The mother too (4),
  • bearing this in her mind keeps guarding you without a moment of separation; (5)
  • How is it possible to stay here long hereafter ? She is to be pitied" (6)
  • "Arivi (Lady) with a bright forehead and gentle nature! we shall go along with you (14);
  • you put up with the troubles for some days (14)
  • The ways in the difficult desert tract (curam) of dense forest in the mountin (10)
  • which lost its beauty (10)
  • like the eye of the big Kiṇi producing a clear sound, which became silent (9)
  • after the death of Atiyaṉ who commanded aḷḷaṉ (8)
  • to annexe adjoining tracts with the great desire of always being victorious (7),
  • are certinly very hot (11).
  • Let the cool rins pour on one day (12)
  • to enjoy the sweet water secreting in your sharp teet (11)
  • "Thus speaking he emphasized on many days taking me along with him, with mind unduted by anything (15).
  • He went away leaving us to stay here (21)
  • starting in the evening in the paths having grove (20)
  • where the elephants which kill travellers are on the look out for them in the ways (19)
  • in the long curam where the severity of the sun which shines in the bright space of the sky is at is height (18)
  • and which is situated in the good country of Paṇaṉ of good lance (17),
  • to the north of (tamiḻ nātu) (16),
  • for all the foregoing assurance to come to nought. (16)

NOTES

In lines 2-3. talivaṉ meeting talivi at night at the secretly appointed place for union is mentioned. As the mother suspected talivi of clandestine love she interned her inside the house and guarded her without a moment separation.

In the 3rd line the words கங்குற்புணர்குறி வாய்த்த may arranged like this; கங்குற்குறிபுணர் வாய்த்த

Lines 2-6 are the thoughts expressed by talivaṉ about talivi's condition;

aḷḷaṉ may be the commander of atiyaṉ's forces. Atiyaṉ should have met with his death in that battle.

எயிறு here stands for the sweet water that secretes from the teeth;

Talivaṉ tasting and enjoying it may be found in the following quotations :
""மடந்தை நின்! கூர் எயிறு உண்கு என (naṟṟiṇi 204-6;
""கால் எறி கடிகைக்கண் அயின்றன்ன, வாய் எயிறு ஊறிய வசைஇல் தீநீர், முன்ளெயிற்று அமிழ்தம் ஊறும்'', (kuṟuntoki, 263-3-4, 286-1-2);
""வான் நகை இலங்கு எயிற்று, செவ்வாய் ""அமிழ்து பொதிதுவர்வாய்'' (Patiṟṟuppattu, 51-20-21);
""முன் உறழ் முளைஎயிற்று அமிழ்து ஊறும் தீநீரைக், கள்ளினும் மகிழ் செய்யும் என உரைத்துமு/ அமையார்'',
""மெல்ல, முயங்குநின் முன் எயிறு உண்கும்'' (Kalittoaki, 4-13, 112-19-20);
""வை ஏர் வால் எயிறு ஊறிய நீரே'',
""மூவாப் பசுங்காய், நீரினும் இனிய வாகிக் கூர் எயிற்று, அமிழ்தம் ஊறும் செவ்வாய்.... குறுமகள்'' (akam, 237-17,335-23-26);
""பாலொடு தேன் கலந்தற்றே பணிமொழி, வால் எயிறு ஊறயநீர்'' (Kuṟaḷ 1121).

வாட்கண் வானத்து: The order of these words has been changed by the new commentators as வான் வானத்துக்கண்; The words may be in the order in which they are found in the poem;

""பேழ் வாய் உழுவையைப் பெரும் பிறிது உறீஇ'' (puranāṉūṟu, 152-2)
""பிள்ளை நகுலம் பெரும்பிறிது ஆக'' (cilappatikāram, 15-54)

AKAM 034

((Originally extracted from master file ^AKAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 2nd))
  • [AN_34-1] ciṟu karum piṭaviṉ veḷ tali kuṟum putal
  • [AN_34-2] kaṇṇiyiṉ malarum taṇ naṟum puṟavil
  • [AN_34-3] toṭutōl kāṉavaṉ kavi poṟuttaṉṉa
  • [AN_34-4] iru tiri maruppiṉ aṇṇal irali
  • [AN_34-5] ceṟi ili pataviṉ ce kōl mel kural
  • [AN_34-6] maṟi āṭu maruṅkiṉ maṭa piṇi arutti
  • [AN_34-7] teḷ aṟal taḻīiya vār maṇal aṭi kari
  • [AN_34-8] melkiṭu kavuḷa tuñcu puṟam kākkum
  • [AN_34-9] peruntakikku uṭinta neñcam ēmuṟa
  • [AN_34-10] celka tēr ē nal valam peṟuna
  • [AN_34-11] paci kol mel viral perum tōḷ pulitti
  • [AN_34-12] tuṟi viṭṭaṉṉa tū mayir ekiṉam
  • [AN_34-13] tuṇiyoṭu tiḷikkum kāppu uṭi varippil
  • [AN_34-14] ce tār pim kiḷi muṉki ēnti
  • [AN_34-15] iṉṟu varal urimō ceṉṟiciṉōr tiṟattu eṉa
  • [AN_34-16] illavar aṟital añci melleṉa
  • [AN_34-17] maḻali iṉ col payiṟṟum
  • [AN_34-18] nāṇ uṭi arivi māṇ nalampeṟa ē

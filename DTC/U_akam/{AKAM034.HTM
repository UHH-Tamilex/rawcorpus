AKAM 034

((Originally extracted from master file ^AKAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 2nd))
  • [AN_34-1] ciṟu karum piṭaviṉ veḷ talai kuṟum putal
  • [AN_34-2] kaṇṇiyiṉ malarum taṇ naṟum puṟavil
  • [AN_34-3] toṭutōl kāṉavaṉ kavai poṟuttaṉṉa
  • [AN_34-4] iru tiri maruppiṉ aṇṇal iralai
  • [AN_34-5] ceṟi ilai pataviṉ ce kōl mel kural
  • [AN_34-6] maṟi āṭu maruṅkiṉ maṭa piṇai arutti
  • [AN_34-7] teḷ aṟal taḻīiya vār maṇal aṭai karai
  • [AN_34-8] melkiṭu kavuḷa tuñcu puṟam kākkum
  • [AN_34-9] peruntakaikku uṭainta neñcam ēmuṟa
  • [AN_34-10] celka tēr ē nal valam peṟuna
  • [AN_34-11] pacai kol mel viral perum tōḷ pulaitti
  • [AN_34-12] tuṟai viṭṭaṉṉa tū mayir ekiṉam
  • [AN_34-13] tuṇaiyoṭu tiḷaikkum kāppu uṭai varaippil
  • [AN_34-14] ce tār paim kiḷi muṉkai ēnti
  • [AN_34-15] iṉṟu varal uraimō ceṉṟiciṉōr tiṟattu eṉa
  • [AN_34-16] illavar aṟital añci melleṉa
  • [AN_34-17] maḻalai iṉ col payiṟṟum
  • [AN_34-18] nāṇ uṭai arivai māṇ nalampeṟa ē

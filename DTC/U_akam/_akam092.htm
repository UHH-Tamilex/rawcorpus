Table of contents (by lines):
(1-5) The companion requesting talivaṉ not to come at midnight
(6-7) companion informing talivaṉ that both talivi and herself intend to keep guard over the millet farm (தினைப்புனம்)
(7-12) Assuring talivaṉ that he can sleep happily on the tresses of talivi if he comes by day.
Colophon(s):
The companion urged the talimakaṉ to think of marriage when he went to the appointed secret place at night and was leaving after meeting talimakal, by words indicating consent to meet at the appointed secret place by day.

TRANSLATION
  • Oh, Chief! May you live long! (5)
  • Please do not come (5)
  • in the slopes of the mountin when the big tiger (4)
  • with bended stripes and red eyes (4)
  • attacks the elephant for the latter to tremble, roars (3)
  • at dead of night when the clouds had poured excessive rins (2)
  • in the sides of high mountins emitting lightning to deprive the eyes of their power of vision (1)
  • This lady (6)
  • who has a soft forearm, (5)
  • long, soft and big shoulders, and myself (6)
  • will be keeping guard over millet (7)
  • Tomorrow (7)
  • at a place where there is a thicket where trees grows thickly and which are unknown to even female monkeys skilled in climbing high (8),
  • where the bright and malabar glory lily has blossomed (9)
  • and where the many cool streams pour their water down into the mountin springs on one side (10)
  • you will enjoy (12)
  • the pleasant sleep in the luxurious black and soft tresses (13)
  • in the light of the beautiful jewel in (12)
  • the hood of the serpent (11)
  • which lost its strength (11)
  • having been attacked by the thunder having become angry (11).

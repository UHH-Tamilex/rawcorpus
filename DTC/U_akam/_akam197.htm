(197) pāli

Lines: (1-3) The present state of talivi having lost her old beauty (4-5) Companion consoling her not to lament (6-9) talivaṉ would not stay longer even if he crosses the ancient hill belonging to Kaṇṇaṉ eḻiṉi (10-15) The scene in the desert of the male elephant, pregnant female one and calf compared to husband, wife and son (16) The purposes for which talivaṉ departed from talivi, in the mountinous path where indian laburnam (koṉṟaimaram) trees grow.

Translation: Friend (5)! Let you be blessed with long life (5)! Do not feel even a little bit of grief (4-5) due to having lost your former beauty (4) which was done with minute care by your circle of mates (3) You have at present eyes that have lost their beauty and which were formerly, comparable to blue nelumbo (kuvaḷaimalar) flowers (1) and lean shoulders which appear like the pillows that have become loose and contracted (2) In the desert which makes people tremble with fear (15) the male elephant which is the big chief of the group of white tuskers (14) and which is in the company of its young and female one of first pregnancy (13), is sleeping (15) and allowing its calf to play by climbing up and down on its body (14). It is just like the son wiht a tender head (12) climbing up and down (12) the chest of the valourous and eminent father (11) who is in the company of his young wife (11) who has a big collection of tresses luxuriant, and dark that fall low on her shoulders (10), Our talivan has become in this manner without grace (18), departed from us, (18) crossed that desert (15) and went in the paths adjacent to the high mountin (16) where indian laburnum (koṉṟaimaram) trees of bright bunches of flowers grow, (16) with a mind dedicated to the task of acquiring wealth, (17) so that your bangles arranged in a row become loose, and stip off (9) Though he crossed and went beyond the ancient hill where honey can be had in plenty (8) which has ruled by Kaṇṇaṉ eḻiṉi (7) who an army of excessive valour (7), and who himself possessed the wealth of valour and strenght, (6) and drove away those who came iwth the intention of fighting aginst him (6), he would not prolong his stay (10) (and he will return soon)

Syntactical lin :- tōḻi! vāḻi!(5); toṉṉalam iḻanta tuyaramoṭu(4) iṉaiyal(5); paṉiccuram nīnti(15), ōṅkumalai attam(16) viṉai valiyuṟūum neñca moṭu(17) niṉnirai nekiḻa(9) iṉaiyarāki nappirinticiṉōr(18) kaṇṇaṉ eḻiṉi(7) mutukuṉṟam iṟantaṉarāyiṉum(8) nīṭalar(9).

The companion infused courage into talimakaḷ who became chaned during separation.

māmūlaṉār

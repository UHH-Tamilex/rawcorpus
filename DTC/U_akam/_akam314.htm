NOTES

āli here does not mean hils but only used in the sense of water.

iṉam tērtal - the farmers select people helpful to plough the land, and bullocks to plough it. Their voice is heard when they plough and sow; or when they sing to forget the tediousness of their work. As they do it with joy it is called sweet voice.

The word kār should be added before ār peyal also.

cevvaḻi is a tune appropriate for the evening.

tam nilai here means talivaṉ's truthfulness.

cey viṉai here means eating and adornment.

kaṇṇi vāḻka one way of blessing anybody;
""yār kol vāḻka avaṉ kaṇṇi'' (puṟam, 77-6);
""vāḻka niṉ kaṇṇi vāy vāḷ vēntē'' (maṇimēkali, 18-62) 

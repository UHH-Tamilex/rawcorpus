TRANSLATION
  • The northern wind has arrived quickly and stayed with no pity on us (8)
  • and with a sound resembling `Kal' after having stirred and swept over the green ponds where the paddy crop is making a noise (7)
  • in the field where ripe ears of corn being prominent are bent after blooming from the embryo (6)
  • resembling the embryo of the whip snake (5)
  • though the new arrival of rin (5)
  • has stopped to make the indian jalap which spreads on the bushes which are wet, which blossom in the evening (3)
  • to adorn the sides near villages, by the noisy and fine drops of rin water falling on them (4),
  • to make the īṅki tree to put forth flowers like the red coral which has curved holes (1-2)
  • and to make the beautiful flowers of field-beans to drop (1)
  • It should go there without stopping here and tell talivar (9),
  • ``you have stayed too long,'' (9)
  • and should add about our life without our partners, hating many things (10)
  • and our strength of will in putting up with distress without dying (1),''
  • and know his state of mind (11);
  • it would be good; it is my wish (12).
  • Now that wind (13)
  • inflicts distress on us only to make us shed tears (13).
  • Such is the fruit of our actions. done in previous births. Friend (15)!

NOTES

ciṉai nērpu has been taken to mean kiḷaittal porunti But I have taken it to mean the embryo of the whip-snake which is commonly called paccaip pāmpu in Tamil.
civaka-cintamani verse 73. has employed this simile for paddy crop when the grins are concealed inside the plant.

talivi is alread suffering on account of separation. Over and about that the northern wind also adds its quota of pin. So she qualifies it by the epithet pitiless. The northern wind has arrived earlier than its usual time is expressed by the word kaṭitu vantu iṟutta. The strength of talivi is putting up with all sufferings and yet she is not dead. If the northern wind which has cused so much suffering staying in our place is also at the place where talivaṉ stays, he will not bear it and return soon to be with talivi.
avar nilai aṟiyumāyiṉ can be interpreted in this way also; If the northern wind can tell talivan about the suffering we undergo on account of the separation, as a messenger and return to us to tell about the state of mind of talivaṉ.

nam valattu aṉmai can be translated as `not within in our power' 

TRANSLATION
  • My mind (17) !
  • We thought several times whether we should give vent (1)
  • to the longing (காமம்) concealed in us (2)
  • or not (1),
  • and at the end came to the conclusion that we openly beg talivi by so many sweet words, (3)
  • as we could not keep it under control (2).
  • She also desired intensely our words (2)
  • (and came out of her house) concealing her in the heavy downpour of rin (4)
  • at midnight (5).
  • She came with her tresses having the fragrance peculiar to the riny season workmanship (6)
  • and which was sticking to her body as it was drenched in rin (6)
  • She came like a young peacock surrounded by forming clouds in the mountin (7)
  • She was adorned with cool flowers to be followed by bees, (8)
  • wore tightly her beautiful anklets (10)
  • which are hollow and have many sections in the shape of a bow, (9)
  • without making sound; (10)
  • came with fear (10)
  • in the midnight when the whole village was in sound sleep (11),
  • she embraced us and returned home (11)
  • She is a not girl of this world, (13)
  • who has a pre-eminence born of her excellent chastity (12),
  • and beautiful complexion of māmi (மாமை) (13)
  • We would like to say that she is a fearful water-nymph (17)
  • who resides in the mountin-pool full of water and soft flowers (16)
  • in the adjecent awe-inspiring hill which has the name of kaviram (கவிரம்) (15)
  • which has got residing deities, and is situated in the good country of āy, (14)
  • in the South (13)

SYNTACTICAL LINK

என் நெஞ்சே(17)! நாம் விட்ட நன் மொழி நமபி(3), தண்மலர்வேய்ந்து(8) அஞ்சிலம்பு ஒடுக்கி(10) மடமயில்போல(7), அஞ்சினள் வந்து(10), யாமத்து முயங்கினள் பெயர்வோள்(11) அரிவையோ அல்லள்(13); சுனையுறையும் சூர்மகள் என்னும்(17).

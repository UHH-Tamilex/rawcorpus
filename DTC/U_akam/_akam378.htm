TRANSLATION
  • Friend (17)'.
  • You say "The solitude cused by the parting of chief of the big mountinous tract (11)
  • where the peacock of beautiful til which has feathers made more beautiful (5),
  • by the shedding of fragrant pollen which is gold in colour, (4)
  • of the vēṅki (வேங்கை மரம்: east indian Kino tree) of beautiful leaves (3)
  • which were shaken by the winds to absorb fragrance (2)
  • like the tresses of hir of the girls who were wedded in the tall house where great quantity of wealth lies buried (1),
  • perches on the waving bamboo of big joints, to make itself shine (10),
  • rising high in the sky, (9),
  • making a sweet sound like the vayir (வயிர் : bugle) blown in the dance arena (8),
  • as it runs being frightened by the bleating of the young ram which has full grown horns (7)
  • belonging to the species of the mountin sheep which are of different groups (6)
  • the angry looks of the hostile mother, (12)
  • and the evening of intense distress when the sun disappears in the western sea (15)
  • when its bright orbit becomes dim (14),
  • even when all the three combines together and inflict pin on you (16)
  • how are you able to live ?" Though our talivar who made an oath not to leaves us, parted from us (18)
  • does not think of us, I still exist (19),
  • as the peak (belonging to talivaṉ) which seems to reach the sky (24)
  • is visible, having worn the stream of sweet sound in the big and high mountin where deities of great powers, reside, and where the female monkey which has not learnt anything except jumping from branch to branch, which has in its hands the fruit of the jack tree of crowed leaves, having siezed it (20),
  • growing in his country, (19)
  • and loiters with its male :

வங்கூழ் wind, ""விரை செலலியற்கை வங்கூழாட்ட'' (akam, 255-4)

As the pollen sheds on the feathers they look more attractive

வருடை mountin sheeps, as there are severl kinds among them, the author says "வேறு வேறு இனத்த வருடை';

in the commentary on cūttiram 32 of marapiyal, pēraciriyar says, "கிளை என்பன எண் கால் வருடையும் குரங்கும் போல்வன; எண் கால ஆயினும் மா வெனப்படுதலின் வருடை கிளை ஆயிற்று'

In the commentary on ll 502-503 of malipuṭukatām nacciṉārkkiṉiyar writes, "முது கிடத்தே கொண்ட, நிலத்தைக் கைக் கொள்ளும் செலவினையுடைய வளைந்த காலையுடைய வரையிடத்தே வாழும் எண்கால் வருடை'

விறந்து being frigtened; "அவற்றுள், விறப்பே வெரூ உப் பொருட்கும் ஆகும்'' (tolkappiyam II - uriiyal 49).

The subjects for நலிய are புலம்பும், நோக்கமும்; மாலையும்

For the variant reading "உஞற்றி' the meaning is `having endured them' `Though our talivar who parted from us has not returned thinking of us I am able to bear my distress as the peak of his mountin is visible this was the reply given by talivi to her friend cf. ""அவர் நாட்டுக்குன்றம் நோக்கினென் தோழி, பண்டையற்றோ கண்டிசின் நுதலே'' (Kuṟuntoki 249-3-5); ""தோழி என், நெஞ் சிற் பிரிந்த தூ உ மிலரேதம், குன்ற நோக்கம் கடிந்ததும் இலரே'' (tolkappiyam, Kaḷaviyal, 20, quoted by nacciṉārkkiṉiyar.

In line 19, the new commentators have adopted the reading உளளே and added a note that the verbal suffix அன் is used for 1st person singular; I think it to be scribal error; so I have adopted உளென் as the correct reading. 

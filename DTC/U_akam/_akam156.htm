TRANSLATION
  • Great one (12)!
  • Chief of the village which has abundant sweet water (7)!
  • There the farmers who keep watch over the paddy-fields (4)
  • are afrid of the old cow eating (4)
  • the young ears of superior paddy of red colour, growing in the fertile fields (3),
  • which resemble the yak-til fan which are joined together and adorned and fixed upright on the forehead of horses belonging to the wealthy kings who have muracu (muracu) as one of the royal insignia (1)
  • They cut the superior creeper of balsam-pear (pākal) along with creeper of indian jalap (pakaṉṟaikkoṭi) (5)
  • and with them tether the cow to the trunk of the river portia (kāñcimaram) tree, after feeding it with sugar-cane (6)
  • our mother (16)
  • even after having offered as presennts (13)
  • to the deity which stands permanently in the ghat (tuṟai) (15)
  • liquor, chaplets (13)
  • including a he-goat which has upright horns and drooping ears (14),
  • does not know any other remedy to cure her dughter's illness (16)
  • and therefore weeps (16)
  • as talivi's complexion resembling sapphire changes into sallowness erroneously, of smiling with you and enjoying your company, (12)
  • in the early morning when the sun was mild when myself and this lady with eyes resembling blue nelumbo (kuvaḷaimalar) coated with collyrium (8)
  • dressing ourselves in the proper way (7)
  • with a green garment made of water lily (āmpal) growing in the fields without removing its outer petals, (9)
  • which was moving on our bodies (10),
  • and played with our mates, (11)
  • and met you (11)
  • and blessed you, `May you live long' (11).

NOTES

கோதையர் - should be put in apposition with இளையோர்: here refers to playmates other than the confidente.

The statement that the day has become dark hinted that they should also return home. As a reply to the words of the friend that talivi who played with her playmates during day should return home at night, talivi sid that if she stayed in the house with distemper, she would be placed under strict guard by her father.

"As the friend is reporting the words of talivi, she used the words, "வைகுதியாயின்! நுந்தை! படுவலும்

In this word உம் is an expletive.

The words செல்வல் என்றலும் ஆற்றாய் explins that talivi will not live if she returned home

வெறி கொள் பாவை the priestess who performs a dance being possessed by murukaṉ.

ஆடுமகள் in line 15 is used in the sense of அவள் (a demonstrative pronoun)

தெய்ய (16) is also an expletive.

I think that the reading found in line 11. "தண்ணரும் பைந்தார்' should be தண்ணறும் பைந்தார்; it may be a scribal error.

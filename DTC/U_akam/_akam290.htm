NOTES

kuṭumik kokku

- a special kind of crane that has a crest on its head.

koḻumiṉ: a kind of fish; perhaps it got its name by its resemblance to plough share which is called "koḻu' (Koḻu) in tamiḻ;
""koḻu mī eṉpatu orucāti'', (tirucciṟṟampalak kōvai yār, 188, pērāciriyar.)

melku irai: food that is well chewed;
""melkupu peyarā'' (ciṟupāṇāṟṟuppaṭai, 45.)

paitaṟpiḷḷai: young one which cannot fly on account of the suffering; or young one which fears on seeing other things.

aṉpu: the love that it has towards its male;

aṉpuuṟa - may mean this also; the female crane cries to excite the pity of people who look at it. talivi was certin that talivaṉ would come alone to the sea-shore grove and have union with her; so she says "maṇavā muṉṉum'

- Talivi's eyes changed into pale yellow colour out of fear for talivaṉ's safety thinking, "What obstacles there would be when talivaṉ comes in the night to meet me at the appointed secret place". This change in the colour of the eyes is called in Kuṟaḷ as "pacappuṟu paruvaral' The idea of talivi saying that the eyes changed into pale yellow colour even before talivaṉ had physical contact with her, is to make talivaṉ think, "talivi will not bear our separation even for a short while; therefore we must marry her early"

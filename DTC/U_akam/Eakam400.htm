{{Readable Translation}}

  • Laughter [is] something good, alas, in itself(?)[FNote_155],
  • in our noisy village with long dark palmyra palms
  • by the three-watered [sea], where singing rises [and] sounds:
  • an old victory[FNote_156], alas, has become new
  • in a good way, seeing, while buds open [and] blossom,
  • many together, so that the unripe young nectar gives fragrance,
  • on the blue-water-lilies in the long water given by the ocean,
  • with leaves coming into swinging in the dark backwaters abounding in flowers,
  • the day the soft man from a land of loneliness came,
  • with ghats, swimming the desert placed(?)[FNote_157] in the waters of darkness
  • there in the depth of muddy mire with fine sand,
  • going setting off, as if one had seen wind,
  • through the white sand in the cold wind, as if one had seen milk,
  • with the strong motion of sturdy legs, as arrows passing by,
  • on the firm chariot with horses, being as if the flood had sprung up,
  • in the breaking ford, while the mindful/spirited(?) charioteer directs [them],
  • having fastened many layers/weapons with flower dots,
  • joining the yoke [for them] to bear swarmingly(?) on the swelling necks
  • of the good four with curved head[gear?] as if the ears were overflowing
  • on the red millet, while going as one harmoniously(?) in a row,
  • becoming full of rich cooked rice, while they hated ghee[-soaked] lumps,
  • becoming big, for [their] necks with plucked hair to become rough,
  • by blue head-ornaments, of a birth befitting the śāstra,
  • for good work that they had here, while they were driven, enjoyed by many(?),
  • so that our secret love departs(?) that abided in the seashore-grove,
  • longingly, [meaning] gossip even if [we] crossed the house [and] staid with him.

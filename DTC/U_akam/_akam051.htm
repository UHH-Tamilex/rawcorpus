TRANSLATION
  • In the desert there is no movement of people; as the sun's rays are scorching, excessive heat spreads everywhere (1-2)
  • In the tall and ugly-looking branch of the yā (yā) tree of long trunk through which the wind blows rushing fast with a loud sound, as if it is splitting it (2-3).
  • In that branch the male kite (eruvai) which has red ears which resemble pieces of flesh fixed in its body, and strikes fear, on seeing the face of its female which is perched on that branch and desirous of flesh leaves it, gets its wings burnt and charned by the intense heat of the summer in the extensive forest which has tall bamboos (4-7)
  • My glorious mind! (14)
  • If you can acquire wealth which you can do only with great suffering and strenous effort, by parting from the talivi who has the complexion of māmi (māmai), and eyes as cool as a flower (8-10),
  • try to forget parting from her (14).
  • Without parting, think of making our talivi perform cheerfully household duties dily, (12-14)
  • Let the expanse of her rising breasts which are not separated from each other, swell in size and let her embrace (me) many times (11-12)
  • in order to make her red ornaments produce a sweet sound (12)

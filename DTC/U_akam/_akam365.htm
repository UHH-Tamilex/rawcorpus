Table of contents (by lines):
1-3. The description of the dull evening.
4-6. The elephants injures its nil by kicking the hero-stones, thinking them to be actual human being.
7-8. The hot curam where highway robbers, find no means to remove their poverty as none travels that way, in which they lie in ambush with an effort to discharge arrows.
9-11. Talivaṉ rebuking his mind for thinking of talivi in the curam.
12-15. The tresses of hir of talivi which are as fragrant as the flowers of venki, which was donated to Kaḻuvuḷ by a pūtam and growing in Kāmūr.

TRANSLATION
  • My mind (11)!
  • May you flourish (11)!
  • you brought me in the severly hot and cruel way of the curam, (9)
  • which is the place where the merciless highway robbers (7)
  • do not find anybody who can remove their poverty as nobody travels, that way (8)
  • when they are hiding to discharge arrows with effort (7),
  • in the spot where the sharp nil of the wild elephant (5)
  • which kicked the hero-stone thinking it to be a human being (4)
  • is injured (5),
  • and breaks like the cover of the pulpy kernel of tender fruit of a big palmyra tree (6)
  • in the very angry evening which inflicts suffering (3)
  • with a dull appearance having despatched the sun (2)
  • to make the thick darkness spread in the widening sky (1),
  • and made not only yourslef unable to bear and made me also suffer; and having gone back from your prupose (10),
  • began to think about (11)
  • the beautiful artlessness of our talivi who has a few tresses of hir (15)
  • on which the cool and fresh flowers of the vēṅki (வேங்கை: east indian Kino tree) having a trunk of rough cracked surface which was given by a pūtam; (பூதம்: demon) in the city of Kāmūr belonging to Kaḻuvuḷ of great munificence, having a victorious vēl.

NOTES

ஆல் இருள் - increasing darkness. It can be split into s வானம் இருள் போகிய increased

மாலையல் வெஞ்சுரம் தந்த

- is the order of words. It can be interpreted in this way also;

மாலையில் துயர் செய்து ஆற்றாயாகி,

கதுவாய் scar; ""கதுவாய் போகிய துதி வாய் எஃகமொடு'' ""காத்துயர் தொல் குடிச் கதுவாயாக'' (peruṅkati, 1-35-166 foot notes) ""அரக்கன் கதுவாய்த்தலைகள் பத்து அலற யிடக்கண்டான்'', ""கதுவாய்த்தலையிற் பலநீ கொள்ளக் கண்டால் அடியார் கவலாரோ'' (tēvāram) ""கடல் வாய்ச் சென்று மேகம் கவிழ்ந்து இறங்கிக்கதுவாய்ப்பட நீர் முகந்து ஏறி'' (periyāḻvār, 3-5-4). The cuse for the elephants nil becoming injured is its kicking the hero-stone. From this we can infer that the hero-stones were of human size.

இதக்கை - this is a rare word.

வருநர் இன்மையின் - this means that there are no people who travel that way on account of the cruel nature of the forest. Talivaṉ rebuked his mind in the following manner: "You yourself who brought me without thinking of the cruel nature of the forest, go back on your resolve thinking about the beauty of talivi. How is your idea ?

""கழுவுள் the name of a petty chieftin, he is mentioned to be strong as to require the combined efforts of fourteen petty chieftins to overthrow him; akam, 135-11-13). He is mentioned in two places in patiṟṟuppattu; ""ஆன்பயன் வாழ்நர் கழுவுள் தலைமங்க'' ""பொரு முரண் எய்திய கழுவுள் புறம் பெற்று'' (71-17, 88-7). From the old commentary on pattiṟṟuppattu we learn that he was chief of the shepherds, giving trouble to the cera Kings. 

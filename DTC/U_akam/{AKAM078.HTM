  • [AN_78-8] muntūḻ āy malar utira kāntaḷ

  • [AN_78-16] vāymoḻi kapilaṉ cūḻa cēy niṉṟu
  • [AN_78-17] ceḻum ceym nelliṉ viḷai katir koṇṭu
  • [AN_78-18] taṭam tāḷ āmpal malaroṭu kūṭṭi
  • [AN_78-19] yāṇṭu pala kaḻiya vēṇṭu vayiṉ piḻaiyātu
  • [AN_78-20] āḷ iṭūu kaṭantu vāḷ amar uḻakki
  • [AN_78-21] ēntu kōṭṭu yāṉai vēntar ōṭṭiya
  • [AN_78-22] kaṭum pari puravi kai vaṇ pāri
  • [AN_78-23] tīm perum paim cuṉai pūtta
  • [AN_78-24] tēm kamaḻ putu malar nāṟum ivaḷ nutal ē

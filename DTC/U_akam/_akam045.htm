TRANSLATION
  • Our beloved one went crossing the forest (6)
  • where the tiger roams about in the desert where there is no movement of (5)
  • people, in the wide and big hill where summer stays with its severity in the difficult path which has no water (3-4)
  • having killed a tall male elephant (4).
  • The beautiful and dry bunches of mature seeds of sirissa tree (உழிஞ்சில்) (1)
  • make a sound intermittently like the drum (கரடிகை) (Karatiki) which is played on the dance stage (2).
  • Let the māmi (மாமை change its colour like the beautiful flowers of sponge gourd (பீரம்) by the spreading of beautiful and minute sallowness (பசலை) (6-7).
  • The noise of scandal born out of it is like the tumultuous uproar by the dancers in singing the fame on the battle field of Kurukki (குறுக்கை) when Aṉṉi (a chieftin) felled down and cut into pieces the big trunk of the old mast wood tree (புன்னை) belonging to Titiyaṉ (8-12)
  • Shall I, who is without sleep becuse of the fearful sorrow like the people inside a single battered defence of a fortress which became possessed with fear in the valourous battle front of Vāṉavarampaṉ who has a good lance by which he made the sea recede, and a garland made of gold, wander like Ātimanti and search for my husband whom I could not find?

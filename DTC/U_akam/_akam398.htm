Table of contents (by lines):
(1-6) The pitiable condition of talivi
(7-12) Talivi afrid of admonishing and being sulky with, the river that flows from talivaṉ's country.
(13-15) Talivi thinking the river to be a messenger of talivaṉ.
(15-16) Talivaṉ strong to forsake people whom he loved.
(17-21) Talivi requesting the river to stay for that day in her place and then proceed further.
(22-25) Description of the talivaṉ's mountinous country.
Colophon(s):
Talimakaḷ spoke sulkily to the river which came from the mountin belonging to talimakaṉ, employing words expressive of acute distress as her longing increased.

TRANSLATION
  • Oh river with floods dashing aginst the banks ! (11)
  • you who are coming from the high mountinous country of our talivar (25)
  • where the male elephant of decaying strength (23)
  • and having many wounds as it was attacked by the big tiger having stripes like the sickle (22)
  • and which was embraced by the suffering female elephant (23);
  • breathes like the sound produced by the musical instrument tūmpu, made of bending bamboo (24)
  • As the distress which is the cuse for the ornaments to slip from their positions (1),
  • and having stayed disliking many things with the thoughts about suffering (2),
  • having lost the former state as the soft shoulders became lean (3)
  • and looking at the body on which sallowness (5)
  • has spread completely to spoil the beauty (4),
  • like the fladed flowers of (4)
  • Koṉṟi (கொன்றை: indian laburnum) (3)
  • the forehead too to turn sallow (5)
  • on account of the cruel act which was committed by your chief (7)
  • without bestowing grace upon us here who are in this state (6)
  • the cool and confused eyes got filled up with clear tears (8)
  • quickly (7)
  • Being afrid of my being sulky with you (12)
  • admonishing you How will this going away leaving righteousness reflect on your chief of the mountinous country (11)?
  • the stranger of a talivar (16)
  • has the strength to forsake us without any affection (15)
  • having brought and directed you who will pass away concealing (14)
  • yourself (14)
  • having shrunk with shyness, covering yourself with many flowers (13)
  • in his mountin (12)
  • without taking notice of it (16),
  • staying in the shade of the vēṅki (வேங்கைமரம்: east indian kind tree) which has branches full of flowers, resembling fire, having put away your journey (17),
  • and stopping at the forest of many flowers (20)
  • which belongs to my father (19)
  • which can be compared to the high mountin where gold is found (19)
  • belonging to āriyar (18)
  • becoming swollen fed by the rins (18)
  • If you proceed after staying (21)
  • today here (20),
  • will it harm your business ? (21)

NOTES

The flowers of Koṉṟi compared to sallowness:
""சென்ற நாட்ட கொன்றையம் பசுவீ, நம் போற் பசக்கும் காலை'' (Kuṟuntoki, 183-1-2)

நும்மோன் one who is your chief.

குன்று கெழு நாடன் - in line 10 is used in the sense of a demonstrative pronoun as talivaṉ has been mentioned by the name of நும்மோன் - As talivi sid your chief and chief of the mountinous country her admonishing and being sulky are with regard to the river.

மறைந்து கழிதல் - covering itself with flowers so that water cannot be seen. Being afrid of being sulky with you, you covered yourself with flowers and passed away shrinking yourself; that being your case the stranger of a lover having brought and directed you, is strong enough to forsake me" These are the ideas of talivi

இன்று இவண் சேந்தனை செலின் சிதைவது உண்டோ - talivi spoke these words, "As there is no harm that may befall you and as you are coming from the mountinous country of talivaṉ. I can stay with you for a day and thereby asssuage my distress" to give vent to her excessive distress. The emaciated elephant will breathe like the musical instrument, `tūmpu';
""ஓய்களிறு எடுத்த நோயுடை நெடுங்கை, தொகு சொற் கோடியர் தூம்பின் உயிர்க்கும்'' (akam, 111-8-9); ""செறி நடைப் பிடியொடு களிறு புணர்ந்தென்னக், குறு நெடுந்தூ ம் பொடு முழவுப் புணர்ந்திசைப்ப'' (akam, 301-16-17)

கழை here stands for `tūmpu' (தூம்பு) a musical instrument made of bamboo.

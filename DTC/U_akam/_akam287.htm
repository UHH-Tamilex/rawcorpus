TRANSLATION
  • In the vast expanse where rin has completely failed (9),
  • is the temple on which the creeper calabash (சுரைக்கொடி) spreads, (5)
  • and which has a pillar of black bottom, and shaking position (3)
  • as a result of the male of the Indian elk rubbing its body aginst it (4);
  • the inhabitants of the small villages of beautiful dwelling places (5)
  • forgot to give offerings in the early morning (6)
  • and as a result of that the altar constructed of brick has become clean white (6);
  • it is surrounded by a banyan tree having a trunk of rough cracked surface like a wall of enclosure (7).
  • The solitary large aerial root of that tree dashed by the western wind (8)
  • the sound of which makes the twin pigeons, male and female, to flee from there (9).
  • The stag licks the back of the beautiful female deer of cylindrical legs, (10).
  • We are looking at it staying in the diminishing shade of lines cast by the tall branches of the yā tree of shaking nature (11)
  • In the evening which appears like petty chieftins and inflicts pin, (13)
  • we are alone and suffer in this never-ending long way (14);
  • Will (our talivi), having collected and closed up all her fingers as in prayer in the wrist on which are worn bangles (1),
  • be suffering (3)
  • with the daytime only as her companion with a mind performing penance (12)
  • (to see us back) ? She has to be pitied.

VARIANT READINGS
  • .9. துணைப்புறா இகுக்கும்.
    There is an old gloss for நரைக்கணிட்டிகை என்பது பலிபீடம். அரைக்கணிட்டிகை is a variant reading for that.

NOTES

தொகுக விரல் குவைஇ - This is what women do when they are very sad.

பகல் துணையாக: means that there was no other companion.

talivan sid that the talivi was pitiable becuse he could not return quickly and remove her sorrow.

கந்து:- a pillar set up in the bottom portion is black it is called மாத்தாள் The following quotations will explin it better; ""கலிகெழுகடவுள் கந்தம் கைவிடப ""புற்றுடைச் சுவர புதலிவர் பொதியற், கடவுள் போகி கருந்தாட் கந்தத்து'' (அகம். 207-11-12); ""அருந்திறற் கடவுள் திருந்துபலிக்கந்தமும்'', ""இலகொளிக் கந்தமும்'' (மணி மேகலை, 6-60, 24-162).

அம் குடிச் சீறூர் This refers to its former flourishing state when it had beautiful houses. As the inhabitants had deserted that place the common place became dilapidated as there was none to give offerings in the early morning. So it had become the abode of the Indian elk and calabash also spread over it,

இட்டிகை brick; as the altar is constructed of bricks, it is called இட்டிகை

தூமழை- should be changed into மழைதூ; தூ means failure.

The stag licked the back of the deer to lessen the heat. This sight brought back memories of talivi to talivaṉ.

Petty chieftins who suddenly appear on the scene after the death of a great king and before the ascendancy of his successive, and harass the people for a while, are compared to the evening; ""புன் கண் மாலைக் குறும் பெறிந் தோட்டி'' (சிலப்.4-24); நிறைகடல் மன்டலம் நேமி யுருட்டிய, இறை கெழு பெருவிறல் எஞ்சிய பின்றைக், கடம்கண் அரிந்த கையராகி, இடம் தொறும் பல்கிய மன்னர் போல'' (பெருங்கதை, 1. 33-45-48). 

AKAM 042

(42) Kuṟiñci

  • Lines 1-4 Description of the beauty of talivi
  • (5-10) Rain in the scorching summer
  • (11-14) Rejoicing of talivi on prospects of marriage.
  • Lady with a complexion called māmi (māmai) resembling tender leaves (4)
  • and beautiful outer corners of cool eyes which resemble the red back side of the fragrant bud of large flowered jasmine (pittikam) which grow luxuriantly and is difficult to be plucked on account of its abundance, having received copious supply of water due to heavy downpour of rin, and which puts forth flowers in the riny season, (1-3)
  • The talivaṉ of the mountin which touches the sky, which is surrounded by hills on its sides and which can be seen from at a distance becuse of its height, came with proposals of marriage (12-14).
  • On seeing his coming I rejoiced. Many people feel happy
  • at the pleasant daybreak (10)
  • after a long spell of very hot summer, when even the moisture disappears (6);
  • [and when] the plough remins idle to make the country suffer from scarcity (5),
  • the clouds pour heavily so that it helps empty extensive and scorched tanks to be filled, which have embankments as high as hills, where birds did not stay previously as there was no water (7-10)
  • It is like the rejoicings of all those people poured into me (11-12)

Syntactical link :- māyōy(4)! veṟpaṉ vantamāṟu(14) (eṉ makiḻcci) perum peyal poḻinta vaikaṟai(10) pallōr uvanta uvakai ellām(11) eṉṉuṭpeytantaṟṟu(12)

The companion informed talimakal that the talimakaṉ had come with proposals of marriage.

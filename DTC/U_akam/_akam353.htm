Table of contents (by lines):
1-2. Talivaṉ telling his mind to listen to some important things.
3-6. The three important things that talivaṉ wanted to tell, though his mind could not know them at present.
7-9. The west wind whirling, having drewn the dust.
10-14. The stag and its mate become weary as there is no drinkable water in the dried up tank, to quench its thirst.
15-16. The curaṉ has trees devoid of shade.
16. Talivaṉ rebuking his mind that it will think of talivi, becoming irresolute.
17-23. The state of talivi when talivaṉ parted from her.
Colophon(s):
Talivaṉ who returned once before parted for acquiring wealth and stayed in his house returning with it spoke to his mind which agin gooded him to acquire wealth.

TRANSLATION
  • My mind! May you live long (2) !
  • Is it possible to part from talivi for acquiring wealth ? (1)
  • Even if we part (1)
  • please listen to what I say now (2)
  • Even if you do not realise now (6)
  • such things as the passing of days (4)
  • in reality (3)
  • like things that appear and disappear quickly in a dream (3),
  • the coming of old age (4)
  • and the returns of Kāmam of rare eminence difficult to obtin (5);
  • you will cross the curaṉ having ways where the trees are devoid of shade (15),
  • in the hot during summer when the scorching sunshine is at its zenith (10)
  • to make the hot west wind which made to drop all the leaves of ñemi tree (ஞெமைமரம்) (9)
  • to make a sound like `Kal' (7)
  • after dashing aginst the moving twigs in the stright bamboos (7),
  • to whirl, having drawn the dust in the long stretch of white saline soil (9),
  • where the beautiful (12)
  • spotted (11)
  • stag (12)
  • with wavy horns (12)
  • which embraced its young mate of weary git (11),
  • goes towards the clear water to drink, forgets eating the leaves (13),
  • and as there is no water becomes weary, having become reduced (14),
  • will you not remember the qualities of (our talivi) who wears beautiful ornaments and has luxuriant locks of hir, (23)
  • who speaks good words in our company (22)
  • sitting on the glorious bed with a smile expressing joy (21),
  • after having tucked in the petals of desirable flowers (20)
  • in the plits on which was shed the fine pollen of tāḻi (தாழை : fragrant screw pine) (19)
  • to make it cool, (10),
  • entertining guests who come suffering from poverty (18)
  • in the big, cool pantar where there is a never-ceasing stream of guests (17)
  • who come with the idea of being fed sumptuously (16).

NOTES

The idea of things appearing and disappearing in dreams is passing away as soon as they appear; The day also pass away quickly.

The ll 4-5 remind one of the lines in
""நாளது சின்மையும் இளமையது அருமையும்.....அன்பிதைகலமும்'' (tolkappiyam, akattiṇi iyal, 41-7-10)
cf. ""இளமையும் காமமும் நின்பாணி நில்லா'',
""இளமையும் காமமும் ஓராங்குப் பொற்றார், வளமை விழை தக்கது உண்டோ'' (Kalittoki, 11-12; 18-7-8)
""இளமையிற் சிறந்த வளமை இல்லை, இளமை கழிந்த பின்றை வளமை, காமம் தருதலும் இன்றே'' (naṟṟiṇi, 126-8-10).

கோடை சுழல, கலை ஒல்குவன தளர, மரன் நிழல் அற்ற இயவின் சுரன் - is the order of words.

As the qualities of talivi performing household duties including entertining guests, her physical beauty by adorning her tresses with flowers, and cheerful looks, speaking sweet words, staying in the cot, produce great pleasure to talivaṉ, so he says to his mind, "If you go to the forest, you will return remembering those pleasures" 

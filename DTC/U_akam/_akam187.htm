Lines: (1-5) The beloved one thought of our greatness when he went determined to acquire wealth (5-10) The front-yard in the house will look lonely, which was formerly like the first day of the festival of garlands celebrated by maḻavar (10-13) talivaṉ staying at night in the pākkam in the mountin (14-8) The elephant eating the inner part of palmyra in the forest (19-20) The elephant asleep without moving its trunk. (21-24) That elephant compared to a fishing boat in the sea.

Companion (10)! Our lover slept with me to rid my shoulders of their lonliness (1) and he gave up the clandestine union which had lasted for many days (2), being ashamed of remining idle without any effort (3) resolved to acquire great wwealth in a distant country (3) and he hass gone there with a determined mind (4) The forest in the mountin is scorched by the heat of summer (16) It has yā () trees of black trunks and bright cleft leaves (15) which can be compared to the beard-like thing which hangs in

the neck of the domestic fowl (14) The inhabitants in the fortress of the forest abandoned it and it has become desolate and difficult to travel (17) In that place the elephant desolate and difficult to travel (17) In that place the elephant with greenish eyes (18), sleeps without any action and is moving its body (2) in the morning when the sun's rays are not vehement and are only mild (19).

  • [AN_47-13] pulampoṭu vanta puṉkaṇ mālai

  • [AN_47-15] iḻai aṇi neṭum tēr kai vaṇ ceḻiyaṉ
  • [AN_47-16] maḻai viḷaiyāṭum vaḷam keḻu ciṟumalai

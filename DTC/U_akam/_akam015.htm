AKAM 015

(15) pāli

  • Lines 1-8 Wish of the mother
  • Lines 9-19 Description of the path in which the dughter went.

Translation

  • Having escaped the rigourous guard of the well-protected extensive mansion which can be compared to Pāḻi of Naṉṉaṉ who wears bright jewels and has state elephants adorned with ornamental coverings on the face (10-12),
  • in order to make myself and (her) friends grieve on account of her absence (9),
  • my mother of soft shoulders which are cylindrical like the bamboos growing on hills, went in a path, having joined her sweet company thinking about him, with attachment. (17-19)
  • (In that path) the powerful group of bears with strong hands, having eaten with their mouths the fresh flowers of mahua trees (iruppaimaram.) in the difficult desert tract, which have dropped from the stems, go from there for the dust to rise from the soil and cull out the contents of the fruits on the beautiful branches of indian laburnum (koṉṟaimaram) which resemble flute by their shape, and spread themselves. (13-16)
  • If what we desire much is to become possible of achievement (1)
  • let the superior old place which has many streets (cērikaḷ) close to one another and which has the good nature of helping strangers who come there empty-handed, have people of familiar faces; it is my wish (6-8)
  • (The old place) is like the tuḷu country which belongs to superior Kōcar famous for their truthfulness who wear large-sized ornaments, where in the garden the peacocks, which have feathers, with stars in their tils like the drum, eat the round matured fruits of balsam pears (pākal) which becomes ripe after leaving the unripe stage, and which has a stem on its head (2-5).

Syntactical link :- em veṅkāmam iyaivatāyiṉ(1) avaṉoṭu pōki(12) eṉ aññai ceṉṟa āṟu(19) tuḷu nāṭṭaṉṉa(5) cemmalmūtūr(7) aṟintamākkaṭṭu ākukatilla

The mother who was the cuse for the dughter to go (to the desert) spoke (thus)

māmūlaṉār

(Note : The dughter is spoken as "mother' which is a term of endearment which is allowed in Tamil grammar)

AKAM 035

((Last updated on 2004 April 2nd))
  • [AN_35-1] īṉṟu puṟantanta em um uḷḷāḷ
  • [AN_35-2] vāṉ tōy iñci nal nakar pulampa
  • [AN_35-3] taṉi maṇi iraṭṭum tāḷ uṭai kaṭikai
  • [AN_35-4] nuḻai nuti neṭu vēl kuṟum paṭai maḻavar
  • [AN_35-5] muṉai ā tantu murampiṉ vīḻtta
  • [AN_35-6] vil ēr vāḻkkai viḻu toṭai maṟavar
  • [AN_35-7] val āṇ patukkai kaṭavuḷ pēṇmār
  • [AN_35-8] naṭukal pīli cūṭṭi tuṭippaṭuttu
  • [AN_35-9] tōppi kaḷḷoṭu turūu pali koṭukkum
  • [AN_35-10] pōkku arum kavalaiya pulavu nāṟu arum curam
  • [AN_35-11] tuṇintu piṟaḷ āyiṉaḷ āyiṉum aṇintu aṇintu
  • [AN_35-12] ārva neñcamoṭu āy nalaṉ aḷai_i taṉ
  • [AN_35-13] mārpu tuṇai āka tuyiṟṟuka tilla
  • [AN_35-14] tuñcā muḻaviṉ kōval kōmāṉ
  • [AN_35-15] neṭum tēr kāri koṭum kāl muṉtuṟai
  • [AN_35-16] peṇṇai am pēr yāṟṟu nuṇ aṟal kaṭukkum
  • [AN_35-17] neṟi irum katuppiṉ eṉ pētaikku
  • [AN_35-18] aṟiyā tēettu āṟṟiya tuṇai ē

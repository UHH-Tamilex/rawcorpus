NOTES

The fragrance is due to the pollen of mast-wood tree.

The adverbial participle (viḷaiyāṭa) denotes time and place (3)

Lines 8 and 9 indicate that the talivaṉ prised their house drawn on sand to get a word as reply from talivi. As they did not speak even a single word as reply out of rudeness he returned silently.

"ataṟkoṇṭu(9) nammoṭu'(13) means that the cuse for our malady is, that they were separated from him though our heart was with him. That even the harbour does not sleep means that as they did not have break throughout the night. So she sympathizes with the sea as it too did not sleep at night. The only remedy for the removal of our distress and the stopping of scandal is to accede to the request of talimakaṉ; there is no other way out

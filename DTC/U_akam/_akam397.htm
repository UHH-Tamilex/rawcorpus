Table of contents (by lines):
1-4. The foster-mother feeling sorry for the elopement of her dughter.
5-9. The season, in the opinion of the foster mother, is not suitable for talivi.
10-11. The male elephants pierce the ōmi tree and peels off the bark.
12-13. The Kite perching on the yā tree comes and stays in the forest, mistaking the red trunk to be flesh.

TRANSLATION
  • The cruel chief of the desert tract who is hastening (9)
  • not receiving talivi though the means of sacramental marriage (4)
  • when her own mother of talivi would perform her duties knowing talivaṉ's eminence I would prisse the big youngness of my dughter (1)
  • in the broad mansion where muḻavu never ceases from being played, as there is no end to festivities (3),
  • not going in the season in which heat would have decreased (8)
  • thinking that the expansive tracts of dense forests where rocks have been split as the rins failed (5)
  • are easy to travel (6)
  • for the girl who has māmi complexion and a waist with dots and spreading lines (7)
  • resembling superior creepers (6),
  • in the forest where the grins of bamboos of dull coloured leaves (16)
  • are produced which wave by the hot west wind (15)
  • in the high peak rising into the sky (14)
  • on which the Kites which are on the yā (யாமரம்) trees of black trunk (13)
  • which grow tall to a great height (14),
  • fly and perch mistaking as flesh seeing the red trunk (12)
  • from which the bark has been peeled off of the Omi (ஓமைமரம்: tooth-brush tree) with a rough cracked trunk (11),
  • which has been pierced and split to cuse a wound (11)
  • by the rough and great male elephant has decided positively (9)
  • to take talivi along with him (8).

NOTES

தன்செம்மல் - here denotes talivaṉ's eminence

ஏந்து கொடி பரந்தி can be added along with கான்; the meaning will be the forest in which exalted creepers have spread

மாயோட்கு(7) எளியவாக(6) is the order of words

துனைதல் - being hasty, being impatient. The elephant peels off the bark of the ஓமை (tooth-brush tree) and eats it; this is mentioned in the following quotations;
""அருவி ஆன்ற நீர் இல் நீளிடைக், கயந்தலை மடப்பிடி உயங்கு பசிகளைஇயர், பெருங்களிறு தொலைத்த முடத்தாள் ஓமை'' (naṟṟiṇi 137-5-7);
""கான யானை தோல் நயந்து உண்ட, பொரிதான் ஓமை வளிபொரு நெடுஞ்சினை'',
""முளிசினை ஓமைக் குத்திய உயர்கோட்டு ஓருத்தல்'' (Kuṟuntoki 79-1-2, 396-3-4).

ஓங்கிய this adjectival participle qualifies யா;

இறுக்கும் qualifies காடு நெடுங்கோட்டு வெதிரகாடு the forest which has bamboos growing in the high peaks. 

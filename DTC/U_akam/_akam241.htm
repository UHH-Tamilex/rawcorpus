TRANSLATION
  • Friend ! May you live long ! (5)
  • Though one who loves me without having sid good words (3)
  • prising talivaṉ's sweet nature (4-3)
  • ``He is a kind person having friendship with us, which cannot be broken, and which has been formed without dislike (1);
  • our lover (5)
  • who was hitherto dily by my side with love that did not know separation, (4)
  • has gone crossing the mountin which has on its sides well-grown trees (16)
  • The young shoots of the bamboo which grows on a great height withers and shrinks (5-6)
  • The extensive rocks where the twigs of the bamboo make a creaking sound gets scorched (6-7)
  • As the clouds have not poured rin, the mountin streams have dried up; (7-8)
  • the expansive area strikes fear in the minds of intending travellers (8)
  • In that area, the stag which is full-grown, solitary and big (10)
  • runs towards the mirage mistaking it to be rin water (9),
  • stays with sorrow in that new hot forest where the mirage is always in a wavy motion without any idea of leaving that place (11)
  • There the red-faced female monkey which has soft hirs on its head like cotton fibres (14)
  • jumps about and plays (15)
  • with the sweet fruits of emblic myrobalam which grows in the paths of the desert (13)
  • as if it is playing with molucca beans of spherical shape (14)
  • (Therefore) I am not able to bear)

NOTES

Translation:

முனியாது நல்குவர் is a hint at the friend who is spoken as a stranger in the third person.

""அவர் நட்பின் இனியர்; முனியாது நல்குவர்'' These words can be taken as the friend prising talivaṉ's qualities.

Mirage is called in Tamil as "ப்த்தேர்'

தாஅம் is a corruption of the Samskrit word "தாகம்'. This word is used in Kalittoki verse 12, line 5 and in a verse quoted in Yāpparunkalavirutti
""தா அம்படு நர்க்குத்தண்ணீருள் கொல்லோ'' (Bhavānantar edition p.383

புலம் can be taken to mean some other place. :

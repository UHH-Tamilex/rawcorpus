{{Readable Translation}}

  1. May the day come when [you] let go [of me] just like that[FNote_964].
  2. If you have consented to that, you have given [back][FNote_965]
  3. my integrity which you had eaten, o man from the coast of the cool sea,
  4. where the young(?) herons rest
  5. on the twig giving way to the ground of the Puṉṉai tree standing
  6. on the shore where sand settles in heaps [big] as hills. Go!

Altern: T.V.G.

2b {--!}The day might come when [you] let go [of her] finally. If you were to consent to that, give [back] ... [and] go.

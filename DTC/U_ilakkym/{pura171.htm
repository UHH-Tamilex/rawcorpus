  • {PU 171-1} iṉṟu celiṉum tarum ē ciṟu vari
  • {PU 171-2} niṉṟu celiṉum tarum ē piṉ um
  • {PU 171-3} muṉ ē tantaṉeṉ eṉṉātu tuṉṉi
  • {PU 171-4} vikal um celiṉum poyyalaṉ āki
  • {PU 171-5} yām vēṇṭiyāṅku em vaṟum kalam niṟippōṉ
  • {PU 171-6} tāṉ vēṇṭiyāṅku taṉ iṟi uvappa
  • {PU 171-7} arum toḻil muṭiyar ō tiruntu vēl koṟṟaṉ
  • {PU 171-8} iṉam mali kata cē kaḷaṉoṭu vēṇṭiṉum
  • {PU 171-9} kaḷam mali nelliṉ kuppi vēṇṭiṉum
  • {PU 171-10} arum kalam kaḷiṟṟoṭu vēṇṭiṉum peruntaki
  • {PU 171-11} piṟarkku um aṉṉa aṟam takiyaṉ ē
  • {PU 171-12} aṉṉaṉ ākaliṉ enti uḷ aṭi
  • {PU 171-13} muḷ um nōva uṟāṟka tilla
  • {PU 171-14} īvōr ariya i ulakattu
  • {PU 171-15} vāḻvōr vāḻa avaṉ tāḷ vāḻiya ē

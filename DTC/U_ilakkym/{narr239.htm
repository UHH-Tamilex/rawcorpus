((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {NT 239-1} ñāṉṟa ñāyiṟu kuṭa malai maṟaiya
  • {NT 239-2} māṉṟa mālai makiḻnta paratavar
  • {NT 239-3} iṉitu peṟu peru mīṉ eḷitiṉiṉ māṟi
  • {NT 239-4} alavaṉ āṭiya pulavu maṇal muṉṟil
  • {NT 239-5} kāmar ciṟukuṭi cel neṟi vaḻiyiṉ
  • {NT 239-6} āy maṇi poti aviḻntāṅku neytal
  • {NT 239-7} pul itaḻ potinta pū tapa mitikkum
  • {NT 239-8} mallal irum kaḻi mali nīr cērppaṟku
  • {NT 239-9} amaintu toḻil kēṭṭaṉṟu ō ilam ē muṉkai
  • {NT 239-10} vār kōl el vaḷai uṭaiya vāṅki
  • {NT 239-11} muyaṅku eṉa kaluḻnta i ūr
  • {NT 239-12} eṟṟu āvatu kol yām maṟṟu oṉṟu ceyiṉ ē

  5. yoḻukunuṇ ṇuccuppi ṉakaṉṟa valkuṟ ((5))

  7. ṉaritupuṇa riṉṉuyir vuviya nīyeṉap
  8. pūṇmali neṭuntērp puṟavi tāṅkit
  9. tāṉam maṇaṅkuta laṟiyā ṉammiṟ

{{Readable Translation}}

  • A smile came of it, friend,
  • [he] standing in worship, the man from the coast of the great sea,
  • looking at [my] glowing forehead where bees hum
  • in the seashore grove, talking of his being tormented
  • by us, not knowing he himself is tormenting us,
  • stopping the horses with the long chariot abounding in ornaments,
  • [with the words] "you who have snatched my sweet life away, difficult to join,
  • who [are] you with clear sweet words,
  • with wide hips to a fleating fine waist,
  • playing with [your] companions in the ocean with clear water,
  • knitting, for the bees to come to [your] sapphire-like [hir put] into five parts,
  • garlands of choice flowers from the Muṇṭakam with fitly adorning blossoms?"

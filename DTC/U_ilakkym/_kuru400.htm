{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As one with a mind[FNote_1592], who considered, desirous of the good:
  2. 'if we don't go the way through the distance without impediment,
  3. we won't alleviate the desire for the one with big shoulders',
  4. you charioteer with a mind that effects new paths
  5. in the wasteland, setting of to break into the area[FNote_1593] of mounds of gravel,
  6. have you given [just] the chariot today?
  7. With this you grant[FNote_1594] [also] her, who constantly endures pain[FNote_1595].

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Foolish indeed [are] the broad-trunked(?) Laburnum trees,
  2. having taken the new showers for the rainy season
  3. when trailing clusters joined to twigs have blown
  4. so that time crushes [them] without the season having come
  5. that he named, who has gone the road glistening with stones.

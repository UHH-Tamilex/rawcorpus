  6. neṭumperuṅ kuṉṟa nīnti namvayiṉ
  7. vantaṉar vāḻi tōḻi kaitai
  8. cempoṟ kaḻaṟoṭi nōkki māmakaṉ
  9. kavavukko ḷiṉkural kēṭṭoṟum
  10. vayavukkoṇ maṉattē mākiya namakkē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • He has come to us, live, friend,
  • crossing the long big hills
  • of the Vaṭukar with angry dogs [and] fierce-voiced Pampai drums,
  • there on the road, where the clear sound full of loneliness
  • of the lon-legged Kaṇantuḷ bird after being frightened by the net
  • laid out by the [sharp-]sighted[FNote_670] hunter takes up[FNote_671] the [bow]string
  • that the desert-going cruel ones[FNote_672] sound quickly,
  • - to us[FNote_673] that have become of a mind taken up with love
  • whenever we hear the sweet voice with an embrace
  • of [our] big son after looking at the redgolden loosening armlets fixed on [our] hands.[FNote_674]

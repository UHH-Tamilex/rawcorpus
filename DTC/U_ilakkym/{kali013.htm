  • {KALI 13-1} ceru miku ciṉa vēntaṉ civantu iṟutta pulam pōla
  • {KALI 13-2} eri mēynta kari vaṟal vāy pukavu kāṇā āy
  • {KALI 13-3} pori malarntaṉṉa poṟiya maṭa māṉ
  • {KALI 13-4} tiri maruppu ēṟoṭu tēr aṟaṟku ōṭa
  • {KALI 13-5} maral cāya malai vempa manti uyaṅka
  • {KALI 13-6} ural pōl aṭiya uṭampu uyaṅku yāṉai
  • {KALI 13-7} ūṟu nīr aṭaṅkaliṉ uṇ kayam kāṇātu
  • {KALI 13-8} cēṟu cuvaittu tam cel uyir tāṅkum
  • {KALI 13-9} puyal tuḷi māṟiya pōkku aru ve curam
  • {KALI 13-10} el vaḷai emmoṭu nī variṉ yāḻa niṉ
  • {KALI 13-11} mel iyal mēvanta cīṟu aṭi tāmarai
  • {KALI 13-12} alli cēr āy itaḻ arakku tōyntavai pōla
  • {KALI 13-13} kal uṟiṉ a aṭi kaṟukkuna alla ō
  • {KALI 13-14} nalampeṟu cuṭar nutāl emmoṭu nī variṉ
  • {KALI 13-15} ilaṅku māṇ avir tūvi aṉṉa mel cēkkai uḷ
  • {KALI 13-16} tulaṅku māṉ mēl ūrti tuyil ēṟpāy maṟṟu āṇṭai
  • {KALI 13-17} vilaṅku māṉ kural kēṭpiṉ veruvuvai allai ō
  • {KALI 13-18} kiḷi purai kiḷaviyāy emmoṭu nī variṉ
  • {KALI 13-19} taḷi poḻi taḷir aṉṉa eḻil mēṉi takai vāṭa
  • {KALI 13-20} muḷi aril pottiya muḻaṅku aḻal iṭai pōḻnta
  • {KALI 13-21} vaḷi uṟiṉ a eḻil vāṭuvai allai ō
  • {KALI 13-22} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 13-23} aṉaiyavai kātalar kūṟaliṉ viṉai vayiṉ
  • {KALI 13-24} pirikuvar eṉa peritu aḻiyāti tiripu uṟīi
  • {KALI 13-25} kaṭum kurai arumaiya kāṭu eṉiṉ allatu
  • {KALI 13-26} koṭum kuḻāy tuṟakkunar allar
  • {KALI 13-27} naṭuṅkutal kāṇmār nakai kuṟittaṉar ē

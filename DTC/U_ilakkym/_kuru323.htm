{{Readable Translation}}

  1. What of all [that]? Chaff [are] the days.[FNote_1315]
  2. When [we?] slept as a pair, [me] by the shoulder of the young woman
  3. with fragrant forehead smelling of the pollen [and] green buds
  4. of the jasmine, which flowered on the field on which it rained,
  5. so that good sound descended from the [cloud] wall rising to the sky
  6. like[FNote_1316] the notes produced in Paṭumalai[FNote_1317] by the bards
  7. - of [all] the days that passed by [this is] the day which lives here.

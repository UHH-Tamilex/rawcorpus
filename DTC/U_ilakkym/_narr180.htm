{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from a village, where the eggs(?)[FNote_584] of the red ants
  • by the jack tree near the pond, spread from the nest,
  • when the field heron grates(?)[FNote_585] [them], as red rice mixed with white grains,
  • he does not come to our house, which many yearn to obtain.
  • The dark one, trusting in [her? his?] integrity, is not capable of letting [him].
  • As the distress of the Puṉṉai tree
  • that had been destroyed on the battlefield of the great king
  • who was superior to Periyaṉ himself, Aṉṉi,[FNote_586]
  • the hostility of these two will pass away with me.

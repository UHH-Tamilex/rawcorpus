  1. The breasts! The have brought forth buds. The head!
  2. Multiplied soft bunches have come to descend to the bottom.[FNote_1357]
  3. In dense rows the white teeth become complete[FNote_1358] in the way of sprouting(?)[FNote_1359]
  4. and beauty spots, a few, have appeared. A plague [to me]
  5. I know [this] of her.[FNote_1360] She herself doesn't know.
  6. What will become of her, she,
  7. the single, inexperienced dughter of great, old, prosperous people?

  1. kaṭuntē rēṟiyuṅ kāṉaliṟ ceṉṟuṅ
  2. koṭuṅkaḻi maruṅki ṉaṭumpumalar koytuṅ
  3. kiti tūkkiyu neytal kuṟṟum
  4. puṇarntām pōla vuṇarnta neñcamoṭu
  5. vikalu miṉiya mākavuñ ceytārp ((5))
  6. pacumpūṇ vēnta raḻinta pācaṟi
  7. yoḷiṟuvē laḻuvattuk kaḷiṟupaṭa poruta
  8. perumpuṇ ṇuṟunarkkup pēey pōlap
  9. piṉṉili muṉiyā namvayi
  10. ṉeṉṉeṉa niṉiyuṅkol paratavar makaḷē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Mounting the quick chariot and going to the seashore grove
  • and plucking Aṭumpu blossoms by the side of the curved backwaters
  • and lifting the [branches of] Kaiti and plucking blue water-lilies,
  • with a heart that perceives [this] as if we were united,
  • even if we [are] like this dily,
  • will she think "what [is this]", the fishermens' dughter,
  • about us who are not averse to standing behind [her],
  • as the evil spirits[FNote_993] for those who have big wounds
  • beaten when the elephants came in a thicket of bright spears
  • into the encampment ruined by the king with green ornaments
  • [and] a made(?)[FNote_994] garland?

{{Readable Translation}}

  • When the red-beamed disk that climbed the distant sky
  • had hidden behind the big mountains the ghat became lonely.
  • The black-legged white egrets that had risen to eat prawn
  • set on to there white-tipped wings [and] took [their] seat
  • in the Puṉṉai tree with black branches on that shore.
  • And our people have entered the hunt,
  • taking light-emitting[FNote_246] lamps to increase [their sight of] the dewy sea
  • to that place, where sharks in company are wandering [their] route[FNote_247]
  • in the backwaters that rise to hide the tube-stalked big blossoms.
  • Therefore,
  • what if [you] stayed in the village[FNote_248] of our residence
  • with gardens by the breaking sea, where waves rise up
  • with drum-sound [and with] foaming spray?

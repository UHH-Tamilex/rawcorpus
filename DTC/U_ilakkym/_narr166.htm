  1. poṉṉu maṇiyum pōlum yāḻani
  2. ṉaṉṉar mēṉiyu nāṟiruṅ katuppum
  3. pōtu paṇaiyum pōlum yāḻaniṉ
  4. māta ruṇkaṇum vaṉappiṉ ṟōḷu
  5. mivaikāṇ ṭōṟuma kaliṉiti yāṉu ((5))
  6. maṟanilai peṟṟō raṉaiyē ṉataṉṟalaip
  7. polantoṭip putalvaṉum poytal kaṟṟaṉaṉ
  8. viṉaiyum vēṟupulat tilaṉē niṉaiyiṉ
  9. yātaṉiṟ pirivā maṭantai
  10. kāta ṟāṉuṅ kaṭaliṉum peritē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Like gold and sapphire your
  • good body and fragrant dark hair.
  • Like buds and bamboo your
  • beautiful collyrium eyes and beatiful body.
  • Whenever seeing these departing is pleasing.(?)[FNote_541] And I
  • am one of those who have obtained the state of virtue. And moreover
  • [my] son with golden armlets has [just] learned playing(?)[FNote_542]
  • and work is not for him in other places.(?) If I think [of it],
  • we will not separate for whatever reason, girl.
  • And love itself [is] greater than the sea.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Are there things more pleasing than joining [someone][FNote_1311],
  2. like the pretty-eared calf of the wild cow with longing eyes[FNote_1312],
  3. frightened because the forest people rouse it, leaving the herd,
  4. [and], because [it] has come across the little homes situated in the forest,
  5. joining the children [for them] to take care [and then] loving that place,
  6. making a living [by] staying at the house?[FNote_1313]
  7. We are going, friend, as far as it is possible on foot.(?)[FNote_1314]

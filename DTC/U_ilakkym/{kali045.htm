Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 45-1} viṭiyal ve katir kāyum vēy amal akal aṟi
  • {KALI 45-2} kaṭi cuṉi kaviṉiya kāntaḷ am kuliyiṉi
  • {KALI 45-3} aru maṇi avir utti aravu nīr uṇal cettu
  • {KALI 45-4} peru mali miḷirppaṉṉa kāṟṟu uṭi kaṉi peyal
  • {KALI 45-5} urumu kaṇṇuṟutaliṉ uyar kural oli ōṭi
  • {KALI 45-6} naṟu vīya naṉam cāral cilampaliṉ katumeṉa
  • {KALI 45-7} ciṟukuṭi tuyil eḻūum cēṇ uyar viṟal veṟpa
  • {KALI 45-8} kāl pora nuṭaṅkala kaṟaṅku ici aruvi niṉ
  • {KALI 45-9} māl vari mali cuṉi malar ēykkum eṉpatu ō
  • {KALI 45-10} pul ārā puṇarcciyāl pulampiya eṉ tōḻi
  • {KALI 45-11} pal itaḻ malar uṇ kaṇ pacappa nī citittatu ai
  • {KALI 45-12} pukar muka kaḷiṟoṭu puli porutu uḻakkum niṉ
  • {KALI 45-13} akal mali aṭukkatta ami ēykkum eṉpatu ō
  • {KALI 45-14} kaṭi eṉa kaluḻum nōy kimmika eṉ tōḻi
  • {KALI 45-15} taṭiyiṉa tiraṇṭa tōḷ taki vāṭa citittatu ai
  • {KALI 45-16} cuṭar uṟa uṟa nīṇṭa curumpu imir aṭukkatta
  • {KALI 45-17} viṭar vari eri vēṅki iṇar ēykkum eṉpatu ō
  • {KALI 45-18} yāmattu um tuyilalaḷ alamarum eṉ tōḻi
  • {KALI 45-19} kāmaru nal eḻil kaviṉ vāṭa citittatu ai
  • {KALI 45-20} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 45-21} taṉ tīmi pala kūṟi kaḻaṟaliṉ eṉ tōḻi
  • {KALI 45-22} maṟiyil tāṉ maruvuṟa maṇanta naṭpu arukalāṉ
  • {KALI 45-23} piṟi puri nutal avar pēṇi nam
  • {KALI 45-24} uṟi varintaṉar avar uvakkum nāḷ ē

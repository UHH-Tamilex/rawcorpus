{{Readable Translation}}

  1. How has he known[FNote_615], friend,
  2. he who is able to remain distant, separated,
  3. in the forest of the difficult desert region,
  4. where the short-gaited hen, with spotted plumage on the neck,
  5. is cooing while she is lonely, perched[FNote_616] so that she shines[FNote_617]
  6. on the broad twig with unripe fruits of the Kaḷḷi tree with parched trunk,
  7. having remembered the cock who had risen for prey
  8. in a time of heat shining like the outstretched[FNote_618] skin of a snake?

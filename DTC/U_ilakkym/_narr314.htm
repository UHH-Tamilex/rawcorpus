{{Metrical text}}

  1. mutirntō riḷamai yaḻintu meytār
  2. vāḻnāḷ vakaiyaḷa vaṟiñaru millai
  3. mārip pittikat tīrita ḻalari
  4. naṟuṅkā ḻāramoṭu miṭainta mārpiṟ
  5. kuṟumpoṟik koṇṭa kommaiyam pukarppiṟ ((5))
  6. karuṅkaṇ vemmulai ñemuṅkap pullik
  7. kaḻiva tāka kaṅku leṉṟu
  8. tāmoḻi vaṉmaiyiṟ poyttaṉar vāḻiya
  9. noṭiviṭu vaṉṉa kāyviṭu kaḷḷi
  10. yalaṅkalam pāvai yēṟip pulampukoḷ ((10))
  11. puṉpuṟā vīḻpeṭaip payiru
  12. meṉṟūḻ nīḷiṭaic ceṉṟici ṉōrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • He lied with the strength of his words, saying
  • "Old people won't attain youth once it is perished.
  • No one knows the measure of the amount of days to live.
  • May the night pass
  • in embracing, so that [your] hot breasts with black nipples are pressed,
  • bright [and] round, with small spots,
  • to [my] chest full of sandal from the fragrant core,
  • wet petals of the Pittikam in the shower bloosoming,
  • - the one who has gone the long way in the sun,
  • where the red dove calls [its] desirable female,
  • conveying loneliness, rising to the swaying image(?)
  • of the Kaḷḷi tree that lets [its] unripe fruits go like a finger-snapping.

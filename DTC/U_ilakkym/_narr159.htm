{{Metrical text}}

  1. maṇituṇin taṉṉa māyirum parappiṉu
  2. ṉuravuttiri keḻīiya pūmali peruntuṟi
  3. nilavukkuvit taṉṉa mōṭṭumaṇa liṭikarik
  4. kōṭutuṇarn taṉṉa kurukoḻuk keṇṇi
  5. yelli kaḻippaṉa māyiṉ mella ((5))
  6. vaḷicīttu varitta puṉṉi muṉṟiṟ
  7. koḻumī ṉārkic ceḻunakarc celīiya
  8. veḻuveṉi ṉavaḷu mollāḷ yāmu
  9. moḻiyeṉa valla māyiṉam yāmat
  10. tuṭitiri yoliyiṟ ṟuñcu malikaṭaṟ ((10))
  11. cilkuṭip pākkaṅ kalleṉa
  12. valkuva tākanī yayarnta tērē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • When we spent the daytime,
  • counting flocks of herons clustered like conches
  • on the pounding shore with sand in peaks heaped like moonlight[FNote_510]
  • on the big ghat abounding in flowers embraced by the strong waves
  • of the big dark extension clear like a jewel,
  • when [I] sid: 'up! let [us] go [back] to the mansion abundant in eating rich fish
  • [and] a frontyard with Puṉṉi trees softly drawn (?)[FNote_511] by the wind'
  • she did not consent and we
  • turned out to be not those to speak a word.
  • Let the chariot you ride stay
  • openly(?)[FNote_512] in the village of small hamlets
  • by the great sea where they sleep with the sound of the breaking waves at midnight.

  5. notuma lāḷaṉ katumeṉat tākkaliṟ 5
  6. kēṭṭpō ruḷarko lillikol pōṟṟeṉa

  8. nāṇili yeḻuva veṉṟuvan ticiṉē

  10. naṟunuta larivi pōṟṟeṉ ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Fearing mother for [her] not knowing
  • we didn`t go to embrace [him] in the Tuṇaṅki danced at the festival,
  • him with earrings, a garland [and] small green armlets.
  • By the way of assulting [us] suddenly(?) as a stranger
  • in the bent within reach of the long broad(?) street,
  • he sid,
  • protectively [in the thought] 'are there those who listen, are there not',
  • 'from whence [these fits of] pallor?'[FNote_195] Aginst that
  • I came with 'you have no shame, friend'.
  • I didn`t/don`t spare the young woman with fragrant forehead,
  • saying 'he [is] a superior one whom even [his] enemies desire',
  • resolved not to see smallness in the greatness.

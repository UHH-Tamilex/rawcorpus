  • {KALI 46-3} vāy iḻi kaṭātta vāl maruppu oruttalōṭu

  • {KALI 46-5} vēṅkai am ciṉai eṉa viṟal puli muṟṟi um

  • {KALI 46-7} vali miku vekuḷiyāṉ vāḷ uṟṟa maṉṉarai
  • {KALI 46-8} nayaṉ nāṭi naṭpu ākkum viṉaivar pōl maṟitarum
  • {KALI 46-9} ayam iḻi aruviya aṇi malai nal nāṭa
  • {KALI 46-10} ēṟu iraṅku iruḷ iṭai iraviṉil patam peṟāaṉ
  • {KALI 46-11} māṟiṉeṉ eṉa kūṟi maṉam koḷḷum tāṉ eṉpa
  • {KALI 46-12} kūṭutal vēṭkaiyāṉ kuṟi pārttu kural nocci
  • {KALI 46-13} pāṭu ōrkkum ceviyōṭu paitalēṉ yāṉ āka
  • {KALI 46-14} arum celavu ār iṭai aruḷi vantu aḷi peṟāaṉ
  • {KALI 46-15} varuntiṉeṉ eṉa pala vāyviṭūum tāṉ eṉpa
  • {KALI 46-16} nilai uyar kaṭavuṭku kaṭam pūṇṭu taṉ māṭṭu
  • {KALI 46-17} pala cūḻum maṉattōṭu paitalēṉ yāṉ āka
  • {KALI 46-18} kaṉai peyal naṭunāḷ yāṉ kaṇ māṟa kuṟi peṟāaṉ
  • {KALI 46-19} puṉai iḻāy eṉ paḻi niṉakku uraikkum tāṉ eṉpa
  • {KALI 46-20} tuḷi nacai vēṭkaiyāṉ micai pāṭum puḷḷiṉ taṉ
  • {KALI 46-21} aḷi nacaii ārvuṟṟa aṉpiṉēṉ yāṉ āka
  • {KALI 46-22} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 46-23} kalanta nōy kaimmika kaṇ paṭā eṉ vayiṉ
  • {KALI 46-24} pulantāy um nī āyiṉ poyyāṉ ē velkuvai
  • {KALI 46-25} ilaṅku tāḻ aruviyōṭu aṇi koṇṭa niṉ malai
  • {KALI 46-26} cilampu pōl kūṟuva kūṟum

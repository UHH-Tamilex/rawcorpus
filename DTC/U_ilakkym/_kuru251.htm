{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Ignorantly they [behave], oh [friend][FNote_1012], the peacocks in big groups,
  2. upon hearing the thundering voice of the indifferent sky
  3. which sheds, in order to take in new water,
  4. the old water that stayed behind from passed showers.
  5. At [the thought] that: 'a timely shower has rained'[FNote_1013]
  6. they have danced [their] dance and the Piṭavu plants have begun to flower.
  7. It is not the rainy season, friend, may your affliction end.

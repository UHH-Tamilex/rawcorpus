  • {PARI 2-5} karu vaḷar vāṉattu iciyiṉ tōṉṟi
  • {PARI 2-6} uru aṟivārā oṉṟaṉ ūḻi um
  • {PARI 2-7} untu vaḷi kiḷarnta ūḻ ūḻ ūḻi um
  • {PARI 2-8} ce tī cuṭariya ūḻi um paṉiyoṭu
  • {PARI 2-9} taṇ peyal taliiya ūḻi um aviyiṟṟu
  • {PARI 2-10} uḷ muṟi veḷḷam mūḻki ārtarupu
  • {PARI 2-11} mīṇṭu um pīṭu uyarpu īṇṭi avaṟṟiṟku um
  • {PARI 2-12} uḷḷīṭu ākiya iru nilattu ūḻi um
  • {PARI 2-13} neytal um kuvaḷi um āmpal um caṅkam um
  • {PARI 2-14} mi il kamalam um veḷḷam um nutaliya
  • {PARI 2-15} cey kuṟi īṭṭam kaḻippiya vaḻimuṟi
  • {PARI 2-16} kēḻal tikaḻvara kōlamoṭu peyariya
  • {PARI 2-17} ūḻi oru viṉi uṇarttaliṉ mutumikku
  • {PARI 2-18} ūḻi yāvar um uṇarā
  • {PARI 2-19} āḻimutalva niṉ pēṇutum toḻutu
  • {PARI 2-20} nī ē vaḷiyoṭu puriyum vāliyōṟku avaṉ
  • {PARI 2-21} iḷiyaṉ eṉpōrkku iḷiyi ātal um
  • {PARI 2-22} puti iruḷ uṭukki polam paṉikkoṭiyōṟku
  • {PARI 2-23} mutiyi eṉpōrkku mutumi tōṉṟal um
  • {PARI 2-24} vaṭu il koḷkiyiṉ uyarntōr āynta
  • {PARI 2-25} keṭu il kēḷvi uḷ naṭu ākutal um
  • {PARI 2-26} i nili teri poruḷ tēriṉ i nili
  • {PARI 2-27} niṉ nili tōṉṟum niṉ tol nili ciṟappu ē
  • {PARI 2-28} ōṅku uyar vāṉiṉ vāṅku vil puriyum
  • {PARI 2-29} pūṇ aṇi kaviiya ār aṇi nittila
  • {PARI 2-30} nittila matāṇi a taku mati maṟu
  • {PARI 2-31} ceyyōḷ cērnta niṉ mācu il akalam
  • {PARI 2-32} vaḷar tiri maṇṇiya kiḷar poṟi nāppaṇ
  • {PARI 2-33} vi vāl maruppiṉ kaḷiṟu maṇaṉ ayarpu
  • {PARI 2-34} puḷḷi nilaṉ um puri paṭal aritu eṉa
  • {PARI 2-35} uḷḷunar urippōr uriyoṭu ciṟantaṉṟu
  • {PARI 2-36} oṭiyā uḷḷamoṭu uruttu oruṅku uṭaṉ iyintu
  • {PARI 2-37} iṭi etir kaḻaṟum kāl uṟaḻpu eḻuntavar
  • {PARI 2-38} koṭi aṟupu iṟupu cevi ceviṭu paṭupu
  • {PARI 2-39} muṭikaḷ atira paṭi nili taḷara
  • {PARI 2-40} naṉi mural vaḷi muṭi aḻipu iḻipu
  • {PARI 2-41} tali iṟupu tāroṭu puraḷa
  • {PARI 2-42} nili tolipu vēr tūr maṭal
  • {PARI 2-43} kuruku paṟiyā nīḷ irum paṉi mici
  • {PARI 2-44} pala patiṉāyiram kuli tari utirva pōl
  • {PARI 2-45} nillātu oru muṟi koypu kūṭi
  • {PARI 2-46} oruṅku uruṇṭu piḷantu nerintu uruḷpu citaṟupu
  • {PARI 2-47} aḷaṟu coripu nilam cōra
  • {PARI 2-48} cērār iṉ uyir cekukkum
  • {PARI 2-49} pōr aṭu kuricil nī ēntiya paṭi ē
  • {PARI 2-50} oṉṉār uṭaṅku uṇṇum kūṟṟam uṭal ē
  • {PARI 2-51} poṉ ērpu avir aḻal nuṭakku ataṉ niṟaṉ ē
  • {PARI 2-52} niṉṉatu tikaḻ oḷi ciṟappu iruḷ tiru maṇi
  • {PARI 2-53} kaṇ ē pukaḻ cāl tāmari alar iṇi piṇiyal
  • {PARI 2-54} vāymi vayaṅkiya vikal ciṟanta
  • {PARI 2-55} nōṉmi nāṭiṉ iru nilam yāvarkku um
  • {PARI 2-56} cāyal niṉatu vāṉ niṟi eṉṉum
  • {PARI 2-57} nā val antaṇar aru maṟi poruḷ ē
  • {PARI 2-58} a um piṟa um ottaṉi u um
  • {PARI 2-59} e vayiṉōy um nī ē
  • {PARI 2-60} ce vāy uvaṇattu uyar koṭiyōy ē
  • {PARI 2-61} kēḷvi uḷ kiḷanta ācāṉ uri um
  • {PARI 2-62} paṭi nili vēḷvi uḷ paṟṟi āṭu koḷal um
  • {PARI 2-63} pukaḻ iyintu ici maṟi uṟu kaṉal muṟi mūṭṭi
  • {PARI 2-64} tikaḻ oḷi oḷ cuṭar vaḷappāṭu koḷal um
  • {PARI 2-65} niṉ urupu uṭaṉ uṇṭi
  • {PARI 2-66} piṟar uṭampaṭuvārā
  • {PARI 2-67} niṉṉoṭu puriya
  • {PARI 2-68} antaṇar kāṇum varavu
  • {PARI 2-69} vāyaṭi amirtam niṉ maṉattu akattu aṭittara
  • {PARI 2-70} mūvā marapu um ōvā nōṉmi um
  • {PARI 2-71} cāvā marapiṉ amararkku ā ceṉṟa niṉ
  • {PARI 2-72} marapiṉōy niṉ aṭi
  • {PARI 2-73} tali uṟa vaṇaṅkiṉēm pal māṇ yām um
  • {PARI 2-74} kali il neñciṉēm ēttiṉēm vāḻttiṉēm
  • {PARI 2-75} kaṭumpoṭu um kaṭumpoṭu um paravutum
  • {PARI 2-76} koṭumpāṭu aṟiyaṟka em aṟivu eṉa ē

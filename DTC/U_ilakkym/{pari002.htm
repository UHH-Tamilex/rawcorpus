Paripāṭal 2

  • {PARI 2-1} tol muṟai iyaṟkaiyiṉ mati ō
  • {PARI 2-2} marapiṟṟu āka
  • {PARI 2-3} pacum poṉ ulakam um maṇ um pāḻpaṭa
  • {PARI 2-4} vicumpil ūḻi ūḻ ūḻ cella
  • {PARI 2-5} karu vaḷar vāṉattu icaiyiṉ tōṉṟi
  • {PARI 2-6} uru aṟivārā oṉṟaṉ ūḻi um
  • {PARI 2-7} untu vaḷi kiḷarnta ūḻ ūḻ ūḻi um
  • {PARI 2-8} ce tī cuṭariya ūḻi um paṉiyoṭu
  • {PARI 2-9} taṇ peyal talaiiya ūḻi um avaiyiṟṟu
  • {PARI 2-10} uḷ muṟai veḷḷam mūḻki ārtarupu
  • {PARI 2-11} mīṇṭu um pīṭu uyarpu īṇṭi avaṟṟiṟku um
  • {PARI 2-12} uḷḷīṭu ākiya iru nilattu ūḻi um
  • {PARI 2-13} neytal um kuvaḷai um āmpal um caṅkam um
  • {PARI 2-14} mai il kamalam um veḷḷam um nutaliya
  • {PARI 2-15} cey kuṟi īṭṭam kaḻippiya vaḻimuṟai
  • {PARI 2-16} kēḻal tikaḻvara kōlamoṭu peyariya
  • {PARI 2-17} ūḻi oru viṉai uṇarttaliṉ mutumaikku
  • {PARI 2-18} ūḻi yāvar um uṇarā
  • {PARI 2-19} āḻimutalva niṉ pēṇutum toḻutu
  • {PARI 2-20} nī ē vaḷaiyoṭu puraiyum vāliyōṟku avaṉ
  • {PARI 2-21} iḷaiyaṉ eṉpōrkku iḷaiyai ātal um
  • {PARI 2-22} putai iruḷ uṭukkai polam paṉaikkoṭiyōṟku
  • {PARI 2-23} mutiyai eṉpōrkku mutumai tōṉṟal um
  • {PARI 2-24} vaṭu il koḷkaiyiṉ uyarntōr āynta
  • {PARI 2-25} keṭu il kēḷvi uḷ naṭu ākutal um
  • {PARI 2-26} i nilai teri poruḷ tēriṉ i nilai
  • {PARI 2-27} niṉ nilai tōṉṟum niṉ tol nilai ciṟappu ē
  • {PARI 2-28} ōṅku uyar vāṉiṉ vāṅku vil puraiyum
  • {PARI 2-29} pūṇ aṇi kavaiiya ār aṇi nittila
  • {PARI 2-30} nittila matāṇi a taku mati maṟu
  • {PARI 2-31} ceyyōḷ cērnta niṉ mācu il akalam
  • {PARI 2-32} vaḷar tirai maṇṇiya kiḷar poṟi nāppaṇ
  • {PARI 2-33} vai vāl maruppiṉ kaḷiṟu maṇaṉ ayarpu
  • {PARI 2-34} puḷḷi nilaṉ um purai paṭal aritu eṉa
  • {PARI 2-35} uḷḷunar uraippōr uraiyoṭu ciṟantaṉṟu
  • {PARI 2-36} oṭiyā uḷḷamoṭu uruttu oruṅku uṭaṉ iyaintu
  • {PARI 2-37} iṭi etir kaḻaṟum kāl uṟaḻpu eḻuntavar
  • {PARI 2-38} koṭi aṟupu iṟupu cevi ceviṭu paṭupu
  • {PARI 2-39} muṭikaḷ atira paṭi nilai taḷara
  • {PARI 2-40} naṉi mural vaḷai muṭi aḻipu iḻipu
  • {PARI 2-41} talai iṟupu tāroṭu puraḷa
  • {PARI 2-42} nilai tolaipu vēr tūr maṭal
  • {PARI 2-43} kuruku paṟiyā nīḷ irum paṉai micai
  • {PARI 2-44} pala patiṉāyiram kulai tarai utirva pōl
  • {PARI 2-45} nillātu oru muṟai koypu kūṭi
  • {PARI 2-46} oruṅku uruṇṭu piḷantu nerintu uruḷpu citaṟupu
  • {PARI 2-47} aḷaṟu coripu nilam cōra
  • {PARI 2-48} cērār iṉ uyir cekukkum
  • {PARI 2-49} pōr aṭu kuricil nī ēntiya paṭai ē
  • {PARI 2-50} oṉṉār uṭaṅku uṇṇum kūṟṟam uṭal ē
  • {PARI 2-51} poṉ ērpu avir aḻal nuṭakku ataṉ niṟaṉ ē
  • {PARI 2-52} niṉṉatu tikaḻ oḷi ciṟappu iruḷ tiru maṇi
  • {PARI 2-53} kaṇ ē pukaḻ cāl tāmarai alar iṇai piṇaiyal
  • {PARI 2-54} vāymai vayaṅkiya vaikal ciṟanta
  • {PARI 2-55} nōṉmai nāṭiṉ iru nilam yāvarkku um
  • {PARI 2-56} cāyal niṉatu vāṉ niṟai eṉṉum
  • {PARI 2-57} nā val antaṇar aru maṟai poruḷ ē
  • {PARI 2-58} a um piṟa um ottaṉai u um
  • {PARI 2-59} e vayiṉōy um nī ē
  • {PARI 2-60} ce vāy uvaṇattu uyar koṭiyōy ē
  • {PARI 2-61} kēḷvi uḷ kiḷanta ācāṉ urai um
  • {PARI 2-62} paṭi nilai vēḷvi uḷ paṟṟi āṭu koḷal um
  • {PARI 2-63} pukaḻ iyaintu icai maṟai uṟu kaṉal muṟai mūṭṭi
  • {PARI 2-64} tikaḻ oḷi oḷ cuṭar vaḷappāṭu koḷal um
  • {PARI 2-65} niṉ urupu uṭaṉ uṇṭi
  • {PARI 2-66} piṟar uṭampaṭuvārā
  • {PARI 2-67} niṉṉoṭu puraiya
  • {PARI 2-68} antaṇar kāṇum varavu
  • {PARI 2-69} vāyaṭai amirtam niṉ maṉattu akattu aṭaittara
  • {PARI 2-70} mūvā marapu um ōvā nōṉmai um
  • {PARI 2-71} cāvā marapiṉ amararkku ā ceṉṟa niṉ
  • {PARI 2-72} marapiṉōy niṉ aṭi
  • {PARI 2-73} talai uṟa vaṇaṅkiṉēm pal māṇ yām um
  • {PARI 2-74} kali il neñciṉēm ēttiṉēm vāḻttiṉēm
  • {PARI 2-75} kaṭumpoṭu um kaṭumpoṭu um paravutum
  • {PARI 2-76} koṭumpāṭu aṟiyaṟka em aṟivu eṉa ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if she is united with the lover she longed for
  • on the hot dusty way where the red(?) dove suffers,
  • whirling so as to fling its lustruous spotted neck
  • deliberately(?)[FNote_244], mounting lonelily(?) a long branch,
  • when it had eaten[FNote_245], hacking bits(?) from the Ukāy tree with withered head,
  • [that is,] from [its] red unripe fruits that tasted as if poured with pepper,
  • - have they not been at strife, dimming in the brightness [and] reddening,
  • the blossom-like eyes of my charming(?) little daughter,
  • whose glorious integrity dims to motionlessness,
  • even when [her] garland is confused, even when [her] small bangles become
  • lose, even when the order of coins is twisted on [her] hip that wears a girdle.

  • Without talking of the crossroads in the difficult desert,
  • there without shade, when the water has subsided,
  • so that the swift-gaited elephant with [its] calf suffers,
  • at the rustling side of bamboo, where hot wind wanders
  • noisily, dashing at so that they shake the resplendent branches
  • of the thorn-trunked silk-cotton tree wrapped by dry creepers,
  • you have experienced the long distance, heart,
  • - so that [she] of pretty few hair, that smells of the sky buds
  • frequented by bees of the dark-petalled blue water-lily from the mountain pools of the western mountain of Kuṭṭuvaṉ, undergoes difficult affliction.

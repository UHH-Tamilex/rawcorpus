  • {PATI 51-1} tuḷaṅku nīr viyal akam kalaṅka kāl pora
  • {PATI 51-2} viḷaṅku irum puṇari urum eṉa muḻaṅkum
  • {PATI 51-3} kaṭal cēr kāṉal kuṭa pulam muṉṉi
  • {PATI 51-4} kūval tuḻanta taṭam tāḷ nāri
  • {PATI 51-5} kuvi iṇar ñāḻal mā ciṉi cēkkum
  • {PATI 51-6} vaṇṭu iṟikoṇṭa taṇ kaṭal parappiṉ
  • {PATI 51-7} aṭumpu amal aṭi kari alavaṉ āṭiya
  • {PATI 51-8} vaṭu aṭu nuṇ ayir ūti uñaṟṟum
  • {PATI 51-9} tū irum pōnti poḻil aṇi politantu
  • {PATI 51-10} iyaliṉaḷ olkiṉaḷ āṭum maṭa makaḷ
  • {PATI 51-11} veṟi uṟu nuṭakkam pōla tōṉṟi
  • {PATI 51-12} peru mali vayiṉ vayiṉ vilaṅkum aru maṇi
  • {PATI 51-13} ara vaḻaṅkum perum teyvattu
  • {PATI 51-14} vaḷi ñaralum paṉi puvattu
  • {PATI 51-15} kuṇa kuṭa kaṭalōṭu āyiṭi maṇanta
  • {PATI 51-16} pantar antaram vēyntu
  • {PATI 51-17} vaṇ piṇi aviḻnta kaṇ pōl neytal
  • {PATI 51-18} naṉi uṟu naṟaviṉ nāṭu uṭaṉ kamaḻa
  • {PATI 51-19} cuṭar nutal maṭa nōkkiṉ
  • {PATI 51-20} vāḷ naki ilaṅku eyiṟṟu
  • {PATI 51-21} amiḻtu poti tuvar vāy aci naṭi viṟaliyar
  • {PATI 51-22} pāṭal cāṉṟu nīṭiṉi uṟitaliṉ
  • {PATI 51-23} veḷ vēl aṇṇal melliyaṉ pōṉm eṉa
  • {PATI 51-24} uḷḷuvar kol ō niṉ uṇarātōr ē
  • {PATI 51-25} maḻi tavaḻum perum kuṉṟattu
  • {PATI 51-26} ceyir uṭiya aravu eṟintu
  • {PATI 51-27} kaṭum ciṉatta miṭal tapukkum
  • {PATI 51-28} perum ciṉa puyal ēṟu aṉiyi
  • {PATI 51-29} tāṅkunar taṭa ki yāṉi toṭi kōṭu tumikkum
  • {PATI 51-30} eḵku uṭi valattar niṉ paṭi vaḻi vāḻnar
  • {PATI 51-31} maṟam keḻu pōnti veḷ tōṭu puṉintu
  • {PATI 51-32} niṟam peyar kaṇṇi paruntu ūṟu aḷappa
  • {PATI 51-33} tū kaṇi kiḻitta mā kaṇ taṇṇumi
  • {PATI 51-34} ki val iḷiyar ki ali aḻuṅka
  • {PATI 51-35} māṟṟu arum cīṟṟattu mā irum kūṟṟam
  • {PATI 51-36} vali virittaṉṉa nōkkali
  • {PATI 51-37} kaṭiyi āl neṭuntaki ceruvattāṉ ē

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 51-1} tuḷaṅku nīr viyal akam kalaṅka kāl pora
  • {PATI 51-2} viḷaṅku irum puṇari urum eṉa muḻaṅkum
  • {PATI 51-3} kaṭal cēr kāṉal kuṭa pulam muṉṉi
  • {PATI 51-4} kūval tuḻanta taṭam tāḷ nārai
  • {PATI 51-5} kuvi iṇar ñāḻal mā ciṉai cēkkum
  • {PATI 51-6} vaṇṭu iṟaikoṇṭa taṇ kaṭal parappiṉ
  • {PATI 51-7} aṭumpu amal aṭai karai alavaṉ āṭiya
  • {PATI 51-8} vaṭu aṭu nuṇ ayir ūtai uñaṟṟum
  • {PATI 51-9} tū irum pōntai poḻil aṇi politantu
  • {PATI 51-10} iyaliṉaḷ olkiṉaḷ āṭum maṭa makaḷ
  • {PATI 51-11} veṟi uṟu nuṭakkam pōla tōṉṟi
  • {PATI 51-12} peru malai vayiṉ vayiṉ vilaṅkum aru maṇi
  • {PATI 51-13} ara vaḻaṅkum perum teyvattu
  • {PATI 51-14} vaḷai ñaralum paṉi pauvattu
  • {PATI 51-15} kuṇa kuṭa kaṭalōṭu āyiṭai maṇanta
  • {PATI 51-16} pantar antaram vēyntu
  • {PATI 51-17} vaṇ piṇi aviḻnta kaṇ pōl neytal
  • {PATI 51-18} naṉai uṟu naṟaviṉ nāṭu uṭaṉ kamaḻa
  • {PATI 51-19} cuṭar nutal maṭa nōkkiṉ
  • {PATI 51-20} vāḷ nakai ilaṅku eyiṟṟu
  • {PATI 51-21} amiḻtu poti tuvar vāy acai naṭai viṟaliyar
  • {PATI 51-22} pāṭal cāṉṟu nīṭiṉai uṟaitaliṉ
  • {PATI 51-23} veḷ vēl aṇṇal melliyaṉ pōṉm eṉa
  • {PATI 51-24} uḷḷuvar kol ō niṉ uṇarātōr ē
  • {PATI 51-25} maḻai tavaḻum perum kuṉṟattu
  • {PATI 51-26} ceyir uṭaiya aravu eṟintu
  • {PATI 51-27} kaṭum ciṉatta miṭal tapukkum
  • {PATI 51-28} perum ciṉa puyal ēṟu aṉaiyai
  • {PATI 51-29} tāṅkunar taṭa kai yāṉai toṭi kōṭu tumikkum
  • {PATI 51-30} eḵku uṭai valattar niṉ paṭai vaḻi vāḻnar
  • {PATI 51-31} maṟam keḻu pōntai veḷ tōṭu puṉaintu
  • {PATI 51-32} niṟam peyar kaṇṇi paruntu ūṟu aḷappa
  • {PATI 51-33} tū kaṇai kiḻitta mā kaṇ taṇṇumai
  • {PATI 51-34} kai val iḷaiyar kai alai aḻuṅka
  • {PATI 51-35} māṟṟu arum cīṟṟattu mā irum kūṟṟam
  • {PATI 51-36} valai virittaṉṉa nōkkalai
  • {PATI 51-37} kaṭiyai āl neṭuntakai ceruvattāṉ ē

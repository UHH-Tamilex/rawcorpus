{{Readable Translation}}

  • Little white egret! Little white egret!
  • Feathers resplendent in colour as a clean garment
  • from cloth gone to the ghat, little white egret!
  • Coming to our village, stirring our bright ghat
  • [and] when full of the finned Keḷiṟu fish returning to his village,
  • - do you have that much love or are you of great forgetfulness,
  • that you don`t talk of [my] jewel-loosening suffering
  • to the delightful man from the good village of fields,
  • the sweet flood from there spreading [even] here.

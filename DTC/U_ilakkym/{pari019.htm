Paripāṭal 19

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 19-1} nila vari aḻuvattāṉ vāṉ uṟi pukal tantu
  • {PARI 19-2} pula vari aṟiyāta pukaḻ pūtta kaṭampu amarntu
  • {PARI 19-3} aru muṉi marapiṉ āṉṟavar nukarcci maṉ
  • {PARI 19-4} iru nilattōr um iyika eṉa ītta niṉ
  • {PARI 19-5} taṇ paraṅkuṉṟattu iyal aṇi niṉ maruṅku
  • {PARI 19-6} cāṟu koḷ tuṟakkattavaḷoṭu
  • {PARI 19-7} māṟu koḷvatu pōlum mayil koṭi vatuvi
  • {PARI 19-8} pulattiṉum pōriṉum pōr tōlā kūṭal
  • {PARI 19-9} kalappōṭu iyinta iravu tīr elli
  • {PARI 19-10} aṟam peritu āṟṟi ataṉ payaṉ koṇmār
  • {PARI 19-11} ciṟantōr ulakam paṭarunar pōla
  • {PARI 19-12} uri māṇ puṉi kalam oḷ tukil tāṅki
  • {PARI 19-13} puri māṇ puraviyar pōkku ami tērar
  • {PARI 19-14} teri malar tārar teru iruḷ cīppa niṉ
  • {PARI 19-15} kuṉṟoṭu kūṭal iṭi ellām oṉṟupu
  • {PARI 19-16} nēr pū niṟi peytu iru nilam pūṭṭiya
  • {PARI 19-17} tār pōlum māli tali niṟiyāl taṇ maṇal
  • {PARI 19-18} ār vēli yāttiri cel yāṟu
  • {PARI 19-19} cuṭaroṭu cūḻ varu tāraki mēru
  • {PARI 19-20} puṭi varu cūḻal pulam māṇ vaḻuti
  • {PARI 19-21} maṭa mayil ōrum maṉiyavarōṭu um
  • {PARI 19-22} kaṭaṉ aṟi kāriya kaṇṇavarōṭu um niṉ
  • {PARI 19-23} cūr uṟi kuṉṟiṉ taṭa vari ēṟi mēl
  • {PARI 19-24} pāṭu valam tiri paṇpiṉ paḻa mati
  • {PARI 19-25} cūṭi aciyum cuval mici tāṉiyiṉ
  • {PARI 19-26} pāṭiya nāviṉ paranta uvakiyiṉ
  • {PARI 19-27} nāṭu um nakar um aṭiya aṭintaṉittu ē
  • {PARI 19-28} paṭu maṇi yāṉi neṭiyāy nī mēya
  • {PARI 19-29} kaṭi nakar cūḻ nuvalum kāl
  • {PARI 19-30} tumpi toṭar katuppa tumpi toṭar āṭṭi
  • {PARI 19-31} vampu aṇi pūm kayiṟṟu vāṅki maraṉ acippār
  • {PARI 19-32} vaṇ tār puravi vaḻi nīṅka vāṅkuvār
  • {PARI 19-33} tiṇ tēr vaḻiyiṉ cela niṟuppār kaṇṭa
  • {PARI 19-34} karumpu kavaḻam maṭuppār nirantu
  • {PARI 19-35} pari nimir tāṉiyāṉ pācaṟi nīrttu ē
  • {PARI 19-36} kuruku eṟi vēlōy niṉ kuṉṟa kīḻ niṉṟa
  • {PARI 19-37} iṭi nilam yām ēttum āṟu
  • {PARI 19-38} kuraṅku aruntu paṇṇiyam koṭuppōr um
  • {PARI 19-39} karumpu karumukam kaṇakku aḷippōr um
  • {PARI 19-40} teyva piramam ceykuvōr um
  • {PARI 19-41} ki vittu imirpu kuḻal kāṇkuvōr um
  • {PARI 19-42} yāḻiṉ iḷi kural camam koḷvōr um
  • {PARI 19-43} vēḷviyiṉ aḻaku iyal viḷampuvōr um
  • {PARI 19-44} kūra nāṇ kural kommeṉa olippa
  • {PARI 19-45} ūḻ uṟa muraciṉ oli ceyvōr um
  • {PARI 19-46} eṉṟūḻ uṟa varum iru cuṭar nēmi
  • {PARI 19-47} oṉṟiya cuṭarnili uḷpaṭuvōr um
  • {PARI 19-48} irati kāmaṉ ivaḷ ivaṉ eṉāa
  • {PARI 19-49} virakiyar viṉava viṉā iṟuppōr um
  • {PARI 19-50} intiraṉ pūci ivaḷ akaliki ivaṉ
  • {PARI 19-51} ceṉṟa kavutamaṉ ciṉaṉ uṟa kal uru
  • {PARI 19-52} oṉṟiya paṭi itu eṉṟu uriceyvōr um
  • {PARI 19-53} iṉṉa pala pala eḻuttu nili maṇṭapam
  • {PARI 19-54} tuṉṉunar cuṭṭa um cuṭṭu aṟivuṟutta um
  • {PARI 19-55} nēr vari viri aṟi viyal iṭattu iḻikka
  • {PARI 19-56} cōpaṉa niliyatu tuṇi paraṅkuṉṟattu
  • {PARI 19-57} māal marukaṉ māṭa maruṅku
  • {PARI 19-58} piṟanta tamariṉ peyarntu oru pēti
  • {PARI 19-59} piṟaṅkal iṭi iṭi pukku piṟaḻntu yāṉ
  • {PARI 19-60} vanta neṟi um maṟantēṉ ciṟantavar
  • {PARI 19-61} ēe ōo eṉa viḷi ēṟpikka
  • {PARI 19-62} ēe ōo eṉṟu ēlā a viḷi
  • {PARI 19-63} a ici muḻi ēṟṟu aḻippa aḻittuḻi
  • {PARI 19-64} celkuvaḷ āṅku tamar kāṇāmi
  • {PARI 19-65} mīṭci um kūu kūu mēvum maṭamittu ē
  • {PARI 19-66} vāḻttu uvappāṉ kuṉṟiṉ vaki
  • {PARI 19-67} naṉi nuṉi nayavaru cāyppiṉ nāṟu iṇar
  • {PARI 19-68} ciṉi pōḻ pallavam tīm cuṉi utirppa
  • {PARI 19-69} utirtta cuṉiyiṉ eṭutta taliya
  • {PARI 19-70} alar mukiḻ uṟa avi kiṭappa
  • {PARI 19-71} teri malar naṉi uṟuva
  • {PARI 19-72} ai tali avir poṟi aravam mūtta
  • {PARI 19-73} mintaṉ aruku oṉṟu maṟṟu iḷam pārppu eṉa
  • {PARI 19-74} āṅku iḷa makaḷir maruḷa pāṅkar
  • {PARI 19-75} pacumpiṭi iḷa mukiḻ nekiḻnta vāy āmpal
  • {PARI 19-76} ki pōl pūtta kamaḻ kuli kāntaḷ
  • {PARI 19-77} eruvi naṟum tōṭu eri iṇar vēṅki
  • {PARI 19-78} uruvam miku tōṉṟi ūḻ iṇar naṟavam
  • {PARI 19-79} paruvam il kōṅkam paki malar ilavam
  • {PARI 19-80} niṇantavi kōttavi neytavi tūkka
  • {PARI 19-81} maṇantavi pōla vari mali ellām
  • {PARI 19-82} niṟintu um uṟaḻntu um nimirntu um toṭarntu um
  • {PARI 19-83} viṭiyal viyal vāṉam pōla poliyum
  • {PARI 19-84} neṭiyāy niṉ kuṉṟiṉ mici
  • {PARI 19-85} niṉa yāṉi ceṉṉi niṟam kuṅkumattāl
  • {PARI 19-86} puṉiyā pū nīr ūṭṭi puṉi kavari cārttā
  • {PARI 19-87} poṉ pavaḻa pūm kāmpiṉ poṉ kuṭi ēṟṟi
  • {PARI 19-88} malivu uṭi uḷḷattāṉ vantu cey vēḷvi uḷ
  • {PARI 19-89} pal maṇam maṉṉu piṉ irum kūntalar
  • {PARI 19-90} kaṉṉimi kaṉinta kālattār niṉ
  • {PARI 19-91} koṭi ēṟṟu vāraṇam koḷ kavaḻa miccil
  • {PARI 19-92} maṟu aṟṟa mintar tōḷ eytār maṇantār
  • {PARI 19-93} muṟuval taliyaḷi eytār niṉ kuṉṟam
  • {PARI 19-94} kuṟuki ciṟappu uṇā kāl
  • {PARI 19-95} kuṟa piṇā koṭiyi kūṭiyōy vāḻttu
  • {PARI 19-96} ciṟappu uṇā kēṭṭi cevi
  • {PARI 19-97} uṭi um oliyal um ceyyi maṟṟu āṅku ē
  • {PARI 19-98} paṭi um pavaḻakkoṭi niṟam koḷḷum
  • {PARI 19-99} uru um uruva tī otti mukaṉ um
  • {PARI 19-100} viri katir muṟṟā viri cuṭar otti
  • {PARI 19-101} evvattu ovvā mā mutal taṭintu
  • {PARI 19-102} tevvu kuṉṟattu tiruntu vēl aḻutti
  • {PARI 19-103} a vari uṭittōy nī i vari maruṅkil
  • {PARI 19-104} kaṭampu amar aṇi nili pakarntēm
  • {PARI 19-105} uṭaṅku amar āyamoṭu ēttiṉam toḻutu ē

Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 77-1} iṇai iraṇṭu iyaintu otta mukai nāppaṇ piṟitu yātu um
  • {KALI 77-2} tuṇai iṉṟi taḷai viṭṭa tāmarai taṉi malar
  • {KALI 77-3} tiru mukam iṟaiñciṉaḷ vīḻpavaṟku iṉaipavaḷ
  • {KALI 77-4} ari matar maḻai kaṇ nīr alar mulai mēl teṟippa pōl
  • {KALI 77-5} takai malar paḻaṉatta puḷ oṟṟa ocintu olki
  • {KALI 77-6} mika naṉi cērnta a mukai micai a malar
  • {KALI 77-7} aka itaḻ taṇ paṉi uṟaittarum ūra kēḷ
  • {KALI 77-8} taṇ taḷir takai pūtta tātu eḻil nalam cela
  • {KALI 77-9} koṇṭu nī māṟiya kaviṉ peṟal vēṇṭēṉ maṉ
  • {KALI 77-10} uṇṭātal cālā eṉ uyir cātal uṇarntu niṉ
  • {KALI 77-11} peṇṭu eṉa piṟar kūṟum paḻi māṟa peṟukaṟpiṉ
  • {KALI 77-12} poṉ eṉa pacanta kaṇ pōtu eḻil nalam cela
  • {KALI 77-13} tol nalam iḻanta kaṇ tuyil peṟal vēṇṭēṉ maṉ
  • {KALI 77-14} niṉ aṇaṅku uṟṟavar nī ceyyum koṭumaikaḷ
  • {KALI 77-15} eṉ uḻai vantu nontu uraiyāmai peṟukaṟpiṉ
  • {KALI 77-16} mācu aṟa maṇṇuṟṟa maṇi ēcum irum kūntal
  • {KALI 77-17} vī cērntu vaṇṭu ārkkum kaviṉ peṟal vēṇṭēṉ maṉ
  • {KALI 77-18} nōy cērnta tiṟam paṇṇi niṉ pāṇaṉ em maṉai
  • {KALI 77-19} nī cērnta il viṉāy vārāmai peṟukaṟpiṉ
  • {KALI 77-20} āṅka
  • {KALI 77-21} kaṭaiiya niṉ mārpu tōyalam eṉṉum
  • {KALI 77-22} iṭai um niṟai um eḷitu ō niṉ kāṇiṉ
  • {KALI 77-23} kaṭavupu kai taṅkā neñcu eṉṉum tammōṭu
  • {KALI 77-24} uṭaṉ vāḻ pakai uṭaiyārkku

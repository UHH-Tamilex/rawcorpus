  1. kēḷā yallai tōḻi yalkal
  2. vēṇavā naliya veyya vuyirā
  3. vēmāṉ piṇaiyiṉ varuntiṉe ṉākat
  4. tuyarmaruṅ kaṟintaṉaḷ pōla vaṉṉai
  5. tuñcāyō yōveṉ kuṟumak ḷeṉṟaliṟ ((5))
  6. colveḷip paṭāmai mellave ṉeñciṟ
  7. paṭumaḻai poḻinta pāṟai maruṅkiṟ
  8. ciralvā yuṟṟa taḷaviṟ paralavaṟ
  9. kāṉkeḻu nāṭaṟ paṭarntōrkkuk
  10. kaṇṇum paṭumō veṉṟiciṉ yāṉē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Listen, won't you, friend: at night,
  • as I suffered like the doe of a stag [pierced by] an arrow(?),
  • sighing hotly while afflicted by increasing desire,
  • when mother, as if she knew the order(?)[FNote_231] of [my] misery,
  • said "don't you sleep, my little daughter?",
  • Without the words coming out even softly from my heart
  • I said: "will even the eyes close
  • for those that have been thinking of the man from the forested land,
  • with pebbly depression of golden jasmine that had the beak of the kingfisher(?)[FNote_232]
  • by the side of the rocks on which continuous rain had flown?

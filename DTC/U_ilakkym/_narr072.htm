  1. pēṇupa pēṇār periyō reṉpatu
  2. nāṇut takkaṉṟatu kāṇuṅ kālē
  3. yuyirō raṉṉa ceyirtīr naṭpi
  4. ṉiṉakkiyāṉ maṟaittal yāvatu mikapperi
  5. taḻitak kaṉṟāṟ ṟāṉē koṇkaṉ ((5))
  6. yāṉyā yañcuva leṉiṉun tāṉē
  7. pirital cūḻāṉ maṉṉē yiṉiyē
  8. kāṉa lāya maṟiyiṉu māṉā
  9. talarva taṉṟuko leṉṉu mataṉāṟ
  10. pularvatu kollavar naṭpeṉa ((10))
  11. vañcuva ṟōḻiyeṉ ṉeñcat tāṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • For saying "great ones may either value it or not"[FNote_259]
  • shame is fitting, at the time one looks at it.
  • Very great perishing itself has been fit indeed
  • for my not concealing anything from you.
  • Even if I said[FNote_260] to the man from the sea "I fear mother",
  • he himself certainly wouldn't consider separating. Now,
  • even if the attendants know of the seashore-grove,
  • he(?) says "that [is] unending gossip, isn't it?" Therefore,
  • "is it drying, his friendship?",
  • [thus] I fear, friend, in my heart.

  • {KALI 114-3} putuva malar taii emar eṉ peyarāl

  • {KALI 114-5} ēḻaiyai eṉṟu akala nakku vantīyāy nī
  • {KALI 114-6} tōḻi avaṉ uḻai ceṉṟu
  • {KALI 114-7} ceṉṟu yāṉ aṟivēṉ kūṟuka maṟṟu iṉi
  • {KALI 114-8} col aṟiyā pētai maṭavai maṟṟu ellā
  • {KALI 114-9} niṉakku orūum maṟṟu eṉṟu akal akalum nīṭu iṉṟu
  • {KALI 114-10} niṉakku varuvatu ā kāṇpāy aṉaittu āka
  • {KALI 114-11} colliya collum viyam koḷa kūṟu

  • {KALI 114-14} peru maṇam ellām taṉittu ē oḻiya
  • {KALI 114-15} vari maṇal muṉtuṟai ciṟṟil puṉainta
  • {KALI 114-16} tiru nutal āyattār tam muḷ puṇarnta
  • {KALI 114-17} oru maṇam tāṉ aṟiyum āyiṉ eṉaittu um
  • {KALI 114-18} terumaral kaiviṭṭu irukku ō alarnta
  • {KALI 114-19} viri nīr uṭukkai ulakam peṟiṉum
  • {KALI 114-20} aru neṟi āyar makaḷirkku
  • {KALI 114-21} iru maṇam kūṭutal il iyalpu aṉṟu ē

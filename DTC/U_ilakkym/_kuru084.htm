{{Readable Translation}}

  1. I refused to embrace [her], I was upset about her who had spoken;[FNote_342]
  2. now I've recognized[FNote_343] that as the beginnings[FNote_344] of disgust.
  3. She is cooler than water-lily blossoms,
  4. Smelling of Vēṅkai and of Malabar lily(?)[FNote_345]
  5. from the rain-covered mountain Potiyil of Āy with [his] anklets [and] armlets[FNote_346].

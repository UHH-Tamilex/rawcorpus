{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As one that only[FNote_1255] gives desire the hand[FNote_1256] of friendship
  2. has sunk down of the man from the land of big mountins,
  3. where the proud(?) elephant bull rarely(?)[FNote_1257] closes [his] eyes,
  4. at the mountin slope where the water is falling, beset by confusing misery
  5. after [his] rutting vigour has perished from a curved leaf of the banana tree in the woods, stroking [his] desperately plagued, difficult(?)[FNote_1258] head,
  6. while the youthful female with weakened breath[FNote_1259] rubs [his] ruined back.[FNote_1260]

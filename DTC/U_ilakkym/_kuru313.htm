{{Readable Translation}}

  1. To the man from the ghat, where in the flower-smelling grove
  2. the little white crow[FNote_1279] of the great sea shore rests,
  3. having searched for prey [and] eaten in the swimming dark backwaters,
  4. we are bound. Bound is the friendship.
  5. One difficult to undo, it has become perfect.[FNote_1280]

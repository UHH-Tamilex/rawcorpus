{PUṞAM 336}

((Originally extracted from master file ^PURAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PU 336-1} vēṭṭa vēntaṉ um ve ciṉattiṉaṉ ē
  • {PU 336-2} kaṭavaṉa kaḻippu ivaḷ tanti um ceyyāṉ
  • {PU 336-3} oḷiṟu mukattu ēntiya viṅku toṭi maruppiṉ
  • {PU 336-4} kaḷiṟu um kaṭimaram cērā cērnta
  • {PU 336-5} oḷiṟu vēl maṟavar um vāy mūḻttaṉar ē
  • {PU 336-6} iyavar um aṟiyā pal iyam kaṟaṅka
  • {PU 336-7} aṉṉō perum pētuṟṟaṉṟu i arum kaṭi mūtūr
  • {PU 336-8} aṟaṉ ilaḷ maṉṟa tāṉ ē viṟal mali
  • {PU 336-9} vēṅki veṟpiṉ virinta kōṅkiṉ
  • {PU 336-10} muki vaṉappu ēntiya muṟṟā iḷa muli
  • {PU 336-11} taki vaḷarttu eṭutta nakiyoṭu
  • {PU 336-12} paki vaḷarttirunta i paṇpu il tāy ē

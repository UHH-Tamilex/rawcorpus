{{Readable Translation}}

  1. If I resolve,
  2. that the suffering should be destroyed, [which results] from the lack of action
  3. of the man from the hills, where the fresh-eyed elephant,
  4. chased away, since he ate from the fields, by the man of the forest
  5. with bent bow [and] arrows, short weapons[FNote_1348], lifts [his] head to the short height,
  6. together with parakeets, which women with fragrant foliage scare away,
  7. {--!}- what, friend, [will become] of our secret?

Altern

5+6b {--!}What [would happen], friend, if I resolved [to give away] the secret, {--!}so that the suffering is destroyed ...

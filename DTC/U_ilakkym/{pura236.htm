  • {PU 236-8} iṉiyi ātaliṉ niṉakku maṟṟu yāṉ
  • {PU 236-9} mēyiṉēṉ aṉmiyāṉ ē āyiṉum
  • {PU 236-10} immi pōla kāṭṭi ummi
  • {PU 236-11} iṭi il kāṭci niṉṉōṭu
  • {PU 236-12} uṭaṉ uṟivu ākkuka uyarnta pāl ē

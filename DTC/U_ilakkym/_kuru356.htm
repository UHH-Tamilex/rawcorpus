{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In a difficult place without water, devoid of shade,
  2. to go hastily under the protection of the man with anklets,
  3. [and] drink, 'tav'[FNote_1426], hot, hot muddy water,
  4. evaporated in swirls(?)[FNote_1427] from subsiding mountain pools
  5. - how is she able, she,
  6. [tender] as a sprout, with broad(?)[FNote_1428] tight armlets,
  7. who, saying '[too] much', does not eat even milk
  8. mixed with nice(?)[FNote_1429] rice from a pot decorated with red gold, which she received[FNote_1430]?

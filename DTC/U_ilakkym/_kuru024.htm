{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The freshness of the bright flowers from the black-trunked Neem tree,
  2. will it also pass without my lord?
  3. [For me] to become mushy,
  4. like one fig trampled by seven crabs(?)[FNote_114]
  5. from the white-branched tree near the river,
  6. {--!}the tongues of the cruel,
  7. they have foretold(?)[FNote_115] that the lover would leave.

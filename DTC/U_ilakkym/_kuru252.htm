{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Don't be angry, oh friend,
  2. urging [me] 'you [are] foolish indeed,
  3. being courteous in front of him in [your] divine fidelity[FNote_1015],
  4. the pleasant face not turned away in the morning from the one who is coming,[FNote_1016]
  5. the man from the hilly land, who has become cruel/faithless,
  6. who has made the bangles loose on [your] long, round upper arms.'
  7. Noble ones are ashamed who are prised in their presence[FNote_1017];
  8. how would blame be bearable to them while [one is actually] looking [at them][FNote_1018]?[FNote_1019]

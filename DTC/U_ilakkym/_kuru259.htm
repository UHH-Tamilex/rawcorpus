{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Black one with many-petalled[FNote_1049] rin-eyes,
  2. with a forehead which emits infinite fragrance of the buds having opened
  3. on the cool fragrant Malabar lily, become full by the waterfall
  4. on the hill in the showers which come up after clouds have joined!
  5. If you consent or if you dissent,[FNote_1050]
  6. you have measured [and] know your greatness.
  7. Speaking words that lie as if [they were] truth - what [is] this?
  8. The heart is good, on your side.[FNote_1051]

Altern: T.V.G.

8b {--!}[His] heart (i.e. his intentions) is good, with respect to you.

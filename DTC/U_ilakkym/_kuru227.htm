{{Readable Translation}}

  1. This place possesses only[FNote_924] short blue water-lilies
  2. without full petals, for the sword faces[FNote_925] have cut [them]
  3. of the gold-rimmed[FNote_926] wheels formed like an ornament,
  4. in the seashore grove the chariot had gone to.

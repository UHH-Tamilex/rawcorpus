{PUṞAM 227}

((Originally extracted from master file ^PURAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PU 227-1} naṉi pētai ē nayaṉ il kūṟṟam
  • {PU 227-2} viraku iṉmaiyiṉ vittu aṭṭu uṇṭaṉai
  • {PU 227-3} iṉṉum kāṇkuvai nal vāy ākutal
  • {PU 227-4} oḷiṟu vāḷ maṟavar um kaḷiṟu um mā um
  • {PU 227-5} kuruti am kurūu puṉal porukaḷattu oḻiya
  • {PU 227-6} nāḷ um āṉāṉ kaṭantu aṭṭu eṉṟum niṉ
  • {PU 227-7} vāṭu paci aruttiya vacai tīr āṟṟal
  • {PU 227-8} niṉ ōraṉṉa poṉ iyal perum pūṇ
  • {PU 227-9} vaḷavaṉ eṉṉum vaṇṭu mūcu kaṇṇi
  • {PU 227-10} iṉaiyōṉ koṇṭaṉai āyiṉ
  • {PU 227-11} iṉi yār maṟṟu niṉ paci tīrppōr ē

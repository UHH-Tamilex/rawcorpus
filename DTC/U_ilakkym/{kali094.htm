  • {KALI 94-1} eṉ nōṟṟaṉai kol ō

  • {KALI 94-3} īṅku uru curuṅki
  • {KALI 94-4} iyaluvāy niṉṉōṭu ucāvuvēṉ niṉṟīttai
  • {KALI 94-5} aṉṉai ō kāṇ takai illā kuṟaḷ nāḻi pōḻtiṉāṉ
  • {KALI 94-6} āṇṭalaikku īṉṟa paṟaḻ makaṉ ē nī emmai
  • {KALI 94-7} vēṇṭuval eṉṟu vilakkiṉai niṉ pōlvār
  • {KALI 94-8} tīṇṭa peṟupa ō maṟṟu
  • {KALI 94-9} māṇṭa eṟitta paṭai pōl muṭaṅki maṭaṅki
  • {KALI 94-10} neṟittuviṭṭaṉṉa niṟai ērāl eṉṉai
  • {KALI 94-11} poṟukkallā nōy ceytāy poṟīi niṟukkallēṉ
  • {KALI 94-12} nī nalkiṉ uṇṭu eṉ uyir
  • {KALI 94-13} kuṟippu kāṇ valluppalakai eṭuttu niṟuttaṉṉa
  • {KALI 94-14} kallā kuṟaḷa kaṭum pakal vantu emmai
  • {KALI 94-15} illattu vā eṉa mey koḷīi ellā niṉ
  • {KALI 94-16} peṇṭir uḷar maṉ ō kūṟu
  • {KALI 94-17} nallāy kēḷ ukkattu mēl um naṭu uyarntu vāḷ vāya
  • {KALI 94-18} kokku urittaṉṉa koṭu maṭāy niṉṉai yāṉ
  • {KALI 94-19} pukku akalam pulliṉ neñcu ūṉṟum puṟam pulliṉ
  • {KALI 94-20} akkuḷuttu pullal um āṟṟēṉ aruḷīm ō
  • {KALI 94-21} pakkattu pulla ciṟitu
  • {KALI 94-22} pō cīttai makkaḷ muri ē nī māṟu iṉi tokka
  • {KALI 94-23} mara kōṭṭam cērntu eḻunta pūm koṭi pōla
  • {KALI 94-24} nirappam il yākkai taḻīiyiṉar emmai
  • {KALI 94-25} purappēm eṉpār um palar āl parattai eṉ
  • {KALI 94-26} pakkattu pullīyāy eṉṉumāl tokka
  • {KALI 94-27} uḻuntiṉum tuvvā kuṟu vaṭṭā niṉṉiṉ
  • {KALI 94-28} iḻintatu ō kūṉiṉ piṟappu kaḻintu āṅku ē
  • {KALI 94-29} yām vīḻtum eṉṟu taṉ piṉ celavu um uṟṟīyā
  • {KALI 94-30} kūṉi kuḻai um kuḻaivu kāṇ
  • {KALI 94-31} yāmai eṭuttu niṟuttaṟṟu āl tōḷ iraṇṭu um vīci
  • {KALI 94-32} yām vēṇṭēm eṉṟu vilakka um em vīḻum
  • {KALI 94-33} kāmar naṭakkum naṭai kāṇ kavar kaṇai
  • {KALI 94-34} cāmaṉār tam muṉ celavu kāṇ
  • {KALI 94-35} ōo kāṇ nam uḷ nakutal toṭīiyar nam uḷ nām
  • {KALI 94-36} ucāvuvam kōṉ aṭi toṭṭēṉ
  • {KALI 94-37} āṅku āka cāyal iṉ mārpa aṭaṅkiṉēṉ ēe
  • {KALI 94-38} pēy um pēy um tuḷḷal uṟum eṉa
  • {KALI 94-39} kōyil uḷ kaṇṭār nakāmai vēṇṭuval
  • {KALI 94-40} taṇṭā takaṭu uruva vēṟu āka kāviṉ kīḻ
  • {KALI 94-41} pōtar akaṭu āra pulli muyaṅkuvēm
  • {KALI 94-42} tukaḷ tapu kāṭci avaiyattār ōlai
  • {KALI 94-43} mukaṭu kāppu yāttuviṭṭāṅku

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. 1.
  2. Pitiable it [is], desire,
  3. that comes to see [and] goes ashamed,[FNote_847]
  4. when the clear bell sounds on the set shore of the clear sea,
  5. of the long chariot with a handle(?), mounted by the man from the sea.
  6. Certainly it will perish.[FNote_848] Ah, I ache.[FNote_849]

  7. 2. (Alternate translation)

    • Pitiable it [is, this] desire,
    • when [he] comes to see [and] goes ashamed,
    • the clear bell sounding on the settling shore of the clear sea,
    • of the long chariot with a handle(?), mounted by the man from the sea.
    • Certainly it will perish. Do I ache?[FNote_850]

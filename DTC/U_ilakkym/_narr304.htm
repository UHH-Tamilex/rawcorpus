{{Metrical text}}

  1. vāraṉ meṉṟiṉaip pularvukkuraṉ māntic
  2. cāral varaiya kiḷaiyuṭaṉ kuḻīi
  3. vaḷiyeṟi vayiriṟ kiḷiviḷi payiṟṟu
  4. naḷiyiruñ cilampi ṉaṉmalai nāṭaṉ
  5. puṇariṟ puṇarumā reḻilē piriyiṉ ((5))
  6. maṇiyiṭai poṉṉiṉ māmai cāyave
  7. ṉaṇinalañ citaikkumār pacalai yataṉā
  8. lacuṇaṅ kolpavar kaipō ṉaṉṟu
  9. miṉpamun tuṉpamu muṭaittē
  10. taṇkamaḻ naṟuntār viṟalōṉ māṟpē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • When the man from a land of good mountains with vast big slopes,
  • where the parrot repeat [its] sound as a horn blown by the wind,
  • crowding together with [its] relations from the slopy(?) mountains,
  • feeding on the drying ear of the ripening(?)[FNote_910] soft millet,
  • unites [with me], grace itself unites. When he separates,
  • [my] darkness, as gold inlaid with sapphires, being exhausted,
  • pallor wastes my adorning integrity. Therefore,
  • it well possesses pleasure and distress,
  • as the hands of those who take the Acuṇam,[FNote_911]
  • the chest of the victorious man with cool fragrant scented garland.

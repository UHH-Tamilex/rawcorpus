  1. Who remembers neither us nor the little green image[FNote_1141]
  2. with small feet, tender as if [one] had seen the shoot
  3. of a mango tree with pretty sprouts, blown by the going wind[FNote_1142],
  4. cruel [is] he, oh friend,
  5. who traversed the mountain of she-monkeys with young ones
  6. who, while the male drops down the ripe sweet fruit,
  7. are below [and] receive [and] receive [them and] eat.(?)[FNote_1143]

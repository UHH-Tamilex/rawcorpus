{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Will he traverse, oh friend,
  2. [and] overcome the forest of sharp-eyed elephants,
  3. where the valorous ones with bent bows eat together[FNote_1343] in the wilderness,
  4. after travellers going the way have stood [them] opposition [only] to get lost,
  5. at a waterless difficult place where the long bamboo is dried up,
  6. - to give wealth difficult [to obtin], which was more important than we,
  7. so that the good dark body becomes pale
  8. like a fresh-stemmed mango sprout of the fragrant green mango[FNote_1344]?

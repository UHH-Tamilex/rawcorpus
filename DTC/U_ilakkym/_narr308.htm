  1. celaviri vuṟṟa varavam pōṟṟi
  2. malarē ruṇkaṇ paṉivara vāyiḻi
  3. yāntaṟ kariyavu nāṇiṉaḷ varuvōḷ
  4. vēṇṭā miyiṉ meṉmel vantu
  5. viṉavalun takittaluñ cellā ḷāki ((5))
  6. veṟikamaḻ tuṟumuṭi tayaṅka nalviṉip
  7. poṟiyaḻi pāviyiṟ kalaṅki neṭituniṉin
  8. tāka maṭitan tōḷē yatukaṇ
  9. ṭīrmaṭ ceyki nīrpaṭu pacuṅkalam
  10. perumaḻip peyaṟkēṟ ṟāṅkumeṉ ((10))
  11. poruṇmali neñcam puṇarntuvan taṉṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • When she noticed the noice produced by the hurry to go,
  • even as we call her of choice jewels, while dew comes into
  • [her] blossom-like collyrium eyes, she come ashamed.
  • Coming slowly slowly, as if not wanting to,
  • thinking long, agitated as an image with dissolving form(?)[FNote_918],
  • well done, while [her] thick hir knot fragrant with toddy glitters,
  • she sunk down on [my] bosom. Seing that,
  • as a fresh pot exposed to the great rins,
  • suffering the water in the action(?) on the wet earth,
  • [my] soft heart full of wealth came to unite [with her].

{PUṞAM 9}

((Originally extracted from master file ^PURAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PU 9-1} ā um āṉ iyal pārppaṉa mākkaḷ um
  • {PU 9-2} peṇṭir um piṇi uṭiyīr um pēṇi
  • {PU 9-3} teṉ pula vāḻnarkku arum kaṭaṉ iṟukkum
  • {PU 9-4} poṉ pōl putalvar peṟātīr um
  • {PU 9-5} em ampu kaṭi viṭutum num araṇ cērmiṉ eṉa
  • {PU 9-6} aṟattu āṟu nuvalum pūṭki maṟattiṉ
  • {PU 9-7} kol kaḷiṟṟu mīmici koṭi vicumpu niḻaṟṟum
  • {PU 9-8} em kō vāḻiya kuṭumi tam kō
  • {PU 9-9} ce nīr pacum poṉ vayiriyarkku ītta
  • {PU 9-10} munnīr viḻaviṉ neṭiyōṉ
  • {PU 9-11} nal nīr paḵṟuḷi maṇaliṉum pala ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The one who used[FNote_337] to smooth [our] full curly hair, pressing it to [our] back(?)[FNote_338]
  2. [and] to wipe our tear-stained eyes, saying 'don't cry',
  3. what will become of him, friend,
  4. who hasn't come for the cold season with heavy dew,
  5. when the field-bean blossoms with rich creepers
  6. in amongst the second harvest[FNote_339] of small millet of the hill-dweller with big fields on the slope.

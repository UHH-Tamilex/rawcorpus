  • {PATI 50-1} mā mali muḻakkiṉ māṉ kaṇam paṉippa
  • {PATI 50-2} kāl mayaṅku kataḻ uṟi āliyoṭu citaṟi
  • {PATI 50-3} karumpu amal kaḻaṉiya nāṭu vaḷam poḻiya
  • {PATI 50-4} vaḷam keḻu ciṟappiṉ ulakam purii
  • {PATI 50-5} ce kuṇakku oḻukum kaluḻi malir niṟi
  • {PATI 50-6} kāviri aṉṟi um pū viri puṉal oru
  • {PATI 50-7} mūṉṟu uṭaṉ kūṭiya kūṭal aṉiyi
  • {PATI 50-8} kol kaḷiṟṟu uravu tiri piṟaḻa a vil picira
  • {PATI 50-9} puri tōl varippiṉ eḵku mīṉ avirvara
  • {PATI 50-10} viravu paṇi muḻaṅku oli verīiya vēntarkku
  • {PATI 50-11} araṇam ākiya veruvaru puṉal tār
  • {PATI 50-12} kal miciya um kaṭala um piṟa um
  • {PATI 50-13} aruppam amiiya amar kaṭantu urutta
  • {PATI 50-14} āḷ mali maruṅkiṉ nāṭu akappaṭuttu
  • {PATI 50-15} nal ici naṉam tali iriya oṉṉār
  • {PATI 50-16} uruppu aṟa nirappiṉi ātaliṉ cāntu pularpu
  • {PATI 50-17} vaṇṇam nīvi vaki vaṉappuṟṟa
  • {PATI 50-18} vari ñimiṟu imirum mārpu piṇi makaḷir
  • {PATI 50-19} viri mel kūntal mel aṇi vatintu
  • {PATI 50-20} kol piṇi tirukiya mārpu kavar muyakkattu
  • {PATI 50-21} poḻutu koḷ marapiṉ mel piṇi aviḻa
  • {PATI 50-22} evaṉ pala kaḻiyum ō peruma pal nāḷ
  • {PATI 50-23} paki vemmiyiṉ pācaṟi marīi
  • {PATI 50-24} pāṭu aritu iyinta ciṟu tuyil iyalātu
  • {PATI 50-25} kōṭu muḻaṅku imiḻ ici eṭuppum
  • {PATI 50-26} pīṭu keḻu celvam marīiya kaṇ ē

  • {PATI 50-6} kāviri aṉṟi um pū viri puṉal oru
  • {PATI 50-7} mūṉṟu uṭaṉ kūṭiya kūṭal aṉaiyai
  • {PATI 50-8} kol kaḷiṟṟu uravu tirai piṟaḻa a vil picira
  • {PATI 50-9} purai tōl varaippiṉ eḵku mīṉ avirvara
  • {PATI 50-10} viravu paṇai muḻaṅku oli verīiya vēntarkku
  • {PATI 50-11} araṇam ākiya veruvaru puṉal tār
  • {PATI 50-12} kal micaiya um kaṭala um piṟa um

  • {PATI 50-15} nal icai naṉam talai iriya oṉṉār
  • {PATI 50-16} uruppu aṟa nirappiṉai ātaliṉ cāntu pularpu
  • {PATI 50-17} vaṇṇam nīvi vakai vaṉappuṟṟa
  • {PATI 50-18} vari ñimiṟu imirum mārpu piṇi makaḷir
  • {PATI 50-19} viri mel kūntal mel aṇai vatintu
  • {PATI 50-20} kol piṇi tirukiya mārpu kavar muyakkattu
  • {PATI 50-21} poḻutu koḷ marapiṉ mel piṇi aviḻa
  • {PATI 50-22} evaṉ pala kaḻiyum ō peruma pal nāḷ
  • {PATI 50-23} pakai vemmaiyiṉ pācaṟai marīi
  • {PATI 50-24} pāṭu aritu iyainta ciṟu tuyil iyalātu

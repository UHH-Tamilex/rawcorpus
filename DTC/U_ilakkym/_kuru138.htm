{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if the big(?) village sleeps, we don't sleep
  2. having heard the plentiful falling of the sapphire-like flowers,
  3. which have withered on the many soft twigs, the decoration
  4. of the Nocci tree with peacockfoot-shaped big leaf-clusters,[FNote_561]
  5. beyond the Īḻil mountin near our house.

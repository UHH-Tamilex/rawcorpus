{{Metrical text}}

  1. perunaki kēḷāy tōḻi kātala
  2. rorunāḷ kaḻiyalu muyirvēṟu paṭūum
  3. pomma lōti nammiva ṇoḻiyac
  4. celpa veṉpa tāmē ceṉṟu
  5. tamviṉi muṟṟi vaṟūumvari nammaṉi ((5))
  6. vāḻtu meṉpa nāmē yataṉṟalik
  7. kēḻkiḷa ruttiyara vuttali paṉippap
  8. paṭumaḻi yurumi ṉuraṟṟu
  9. kuraṇaṭunāḷ yāmattun tamiyaṅ kēṭṭē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Hear the great joke[FNote_415], friend. [Our] lover,
  • he himself says he will go
  • so that we with thick hir stay behind here,
  • where even if one day passes by life will be different.
  • We ourselves say we will be living in our house
  • until he comes when his work is completed. On top of that
  • we [are] alone even at midnight, hearing in the middle of the night the voice:
  • thunder roaring in the falling rin,
  • so that the head of the snake with bright lustrous spots on its hood shivers.

Paripāṭal 9

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 9-1} iru nilam tuḷaṅkāmi vaṭa vayiṉ nivantu ōṅki
  • {PARI 9-2} aru nili uyar teyvattu aṇaṅku cāl tali kākkum
  • {PARI 9-3} urumu cūḻ cēṇ cimi uyarntavar uṭampaṭa
  • {PARI 9-4} eri malar tāmari iṟi vīḻtta peru vāri
  • {PARI 9-5} viri caṭi poṟi ūḻttu viḻu nikar malar ēyppa
  • {PARI 9-6} taṇivuṟa tāṅkiya taṉi nili calatāri
  • {PARI 9-7} maṇi miṭaṟṟu aṇṇaṟku mati āral piṟantōy nī
  • {PARI 9-8} mi iru nūṟṟu imi uṇ kaṇ māṉ maṟi tōḷ maṇanta ñāṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-9} ai iru nūṟṟu meym nayaṉattavaṉ makaḷ malar uṇ kaṇ
  • {PARI 9-10} maṇi maḻi taliiyeṉa mā vēṉil kār ēṟṟu
  • {PARI 9-11} taṇi maḻi taliyiṉṟu taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-12} nāṉmaṟi virittu nal ici viḷakkum
  • {PARI 9-13} vāymoḻi pulavīr kēṇmiṉ ciṟantatu
  • {PARI 9-14} kātal kāmam kāmattu ciṟantatu
  • {PARI 9-15} viruppōr ottu meyyuṟu puṇarcci
  • {PARI 9-16} pulattaliṉ ciṟantatu kaṟpu ē atu tāṉ
  • {PARI 9-17} irattal um ītal um ivi uḷḷīṭu ā
  • {PARI 9-18} paratti uḷḷatu ē paṇpuṟu kaḻaṟal
  • {PARI 9-19} tōḷ putitu uṇṭa paratti il civappuṟa
  • {PARI 9-20} nāḷ aṇintu uvakkum cuṇaṅkaṟiyatu ē
  • {PARI 9-21} kēḷ aṇaṅkuṟa maṉi kiḷantu uḷa cuṇaṅkaṟi
  • {PARI 9-22} cuṇaṅkaṟi payaṉ um ūṭal uḷḷatu ē
  • {PARI 9-23} ataṉāl akaṟal aṟiyā aṇi iḻi nallār
  • {PARI 9-24} ikal talikkoṇṭu tuṉikkum tavaṟu ilar i
  • {PARI 9-25} taḷḷā poruḷ iyalpiṉ taṇ tamiḻ āyvantilār
  • {PARI 9-26} koḷḷār i kuṉṟu payaṉ
  • {PARI 9-27} ūḻ ārattu ōy kari nūkki puṉal tanta
  • {PARI 9-28} kāḻ ārattu am puki cuṟṟiya tār mārpiṉ
  • {PARI 9-29} kēḻ āram poṟpa varuvāṉi toḻāa
  • {PARI 9-30} vāḻiya māyā niṉ tavaṟu ili em pōlum
  • {PARI 9-31} kēḻ ilār māṇ nalam uṇku ō tiru uṭiyār
  • {PARI 9-32} mel tōḷ mēl alki nalkal um iṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-33} vi eyiṟṟu eyyā makaḷir tiṟam iṉi
  • {PARI 9-34} peyya uḻakkum maḻi kā maṟṟu aiya
  • {PARI 9-35} kariyā ve nōkkattāṉ ki cuṭṭi peṇṭiṉ
  • {PARI 9-36} ikaliṉ ikantāḷi a vēḷ tali kaṇṇi
  • {PARI 9-37} tiruntu aṭi tōya tiṟi koṭuppāṉi
  • {PARI 9-38} varuntal eṉa avaṟku mārpu aḷippāḷi
  • {PARI 9-39} kuṟukal eṉṟu oḷ iḻi kōti kōl āka
  • {PARI 9-40} iṟuka iṟuka yāttu puṭippa
  • {PARI 9-41} oruvar mayil oruvar oḷ mayilōṭu ēla
  • {PARI 9-42} iruvar vāṉ kiḷi ēṟpil maḻali
  • {PARI 9-43} ceṟi koṇṭi mēl vaṇṭu ceṉṟu pāyntaṉṟu ē
  • {PARI 9-44} veṟi koṇṭāṉ kuṉṟattu vaṇṭu
  • {PARI 9-45} tār tār piṇakkuvār kaṇṇi ōcci taṭumāṟuvār
  • {PARI 9-46} mārpu aṇi koṅki vār mattiki ā puṭippār
  • {PARI 9-47} kōti vari pantu koṇṭu eṟivār
  • {PARI 9-48} pēti maṭa nōkkam piṟitu āka ūta
  • {PARI 9-49} nuṭaṅku noci nucuppār nūḻil talikkoḷḷa
  • {PARI 9-50} kayampaṭu kamaḻ ceṉṉi kaḷiṟṟu iyal kimmāṟuvār
  • {PARI 9-51} vayampaṭu pari puravi mārkkam varuvār
  • {PARI 9-52} tēr aṇi aṇi kayiṟu teripu varuvār
  • {PARI 9-53} vari cili vaḷiya mārpu uṟa vāṅkuvār
  • {PARI 9-54} vāḷi vāḷikaḷ nilipeṟa maṟaluvār
  • {PARI 9-55} tōḷ vaḷi āḻi cuḻaṟṟuvār
  • {PARI 9-56} mel cīr mayil iyalavar
  • {PARI 9-57} vāḷ miku vaya moympiṉ
  • {PARI 9-58} vari akalattavaṉi vāṉavaṉ makaḷ
  • {PARI 9-59} māṇ eḻil malar uṇ kaṇ
  • {PARI 9-60} maṭa moḻiyavar uṭaṉ cuṟṟi
  • {PARI 9-61} kaṭi cuṉi uḷ kuḷittu āṭunar um
  • {PARI 9-62} aṟi aṇinta arum cuṉiyāṉ
  • {PARI 9-63} naṟa uṇ vaṇṭu āy narampu uḷarnar um
  • {PARI 9-64} ciki mayil āy tōki virittu āṭunar um
  • {PARI 9-65} kōkulam āy kūvunar um
  • {PARI 9-66} ākulam ākunar um
  • {PARI 9-67} kuṟiñci kuṉṟavar maṟam keḻu vaḷḷi tamar
  • {PARI 9-68} vittaka tumpi viḷittalāṉ veṉ vēlāṟku
  • {PARI 9-69} ottaṉṟu taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-70} kaṭum cūr mā mutal taṭintu aṟutta vēl
  • {PARI 9-71} aṭum pōrāḷa niṉ kuṉṟiṉ mici
  • {PARI 9-72} āṭal naviṉṟōr avar pōr ceṟuppa um
  • {PARI 9-73} pāṭal payiṉṟōri pāṇar ceṟuppa um
  • {PARI 9-74} vallāri vallār ceṟuppa um
  • {PARI 9-75} allāri allār ceṟuppa um ōr col āy
  • {PARI 9-76} cemmi putu puṉal
  • {PARI 9-77} taṭākam ēṟṟa taṇ cuṉi pāṅkar
  • {PARI 9-78} paṭāki niṉṟaṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-79} mēe eḵkiṉavi
  • {PARI 9-80} veṉṟu uyartta koṭi viṟal cāṉṟavi
  • {PARI 9-81} kaṟpu iṇi neṟi ūṭu aṟpu iṇi kiḻami
  • {PARI 9-82} naya taku marapiṉ viya taku kumara
  • {PARI 9-83} vāḻttiṉēm paravutum tāḻttu tali niṉi yām
  • {PARI 9-84} nayattaliṉ ciṟanta em aṭiyuṟi
  • {PARI 9-85} payattaliṉ ciṟakka nāḷ toṟum polintu ē

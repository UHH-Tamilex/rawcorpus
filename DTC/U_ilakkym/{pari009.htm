Paripāṭal 9

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 9-1} iru nilam tuḷaṅkāmai vaṭa vayiṉ nivantu ōṅki
  • {PARI 9-2} aru nilai uyar teyvattu aṇaṅku cāl talai kākkum
  • {PARI 9-3} urumu cūḻ cēṇ cimai uyarntavar uṭampaṭa
  • {PARI 9-4} eri malar tāmarai iṟai vīḻtta peru vāri
  • {PARI 9-5} viri caṭai poṟai ūḻttu viḻu nikar malar ēyppa
  • {PARI 9-6} taṇivuṟa tāṅkiya taṉi nilai calatāri
  • {PARI 9-7} maṇi miṭaṟṟu aṇṇaṟku mati āral piṟantōy nī
  • {PARI 9-8} mai iru nūṟṟu imai uṇ kaṇ māṉ maṟi tōḷ maṇanta ñāṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-9} ai iru nūṟṟu meym nayaṉattavaṉ makaḷ malar uṇ kaṇ
  • {PARI 9-10} maṇi maḻai talaiiyeṉa mā vēṉil kār ēṟṟu
  • {PARI 9-11} taṇi maḻai talaiyiṉṟu taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-12} nāṉmaṟai virittu nal icai viḷakkum
  • {PARI 9-13} vāymoḻi pulavīr kēṇmiṉ ciṟantatu
  • {PARI 9-14} kātal kāmam kāmattu ciṟantatu
  • {PARI 9-15} viruppōr ottu meyyuṟu puṇarcci
  • {PARI 9-16} pulattaliṉ ciṟantatu kaṟpu ē atu tāṉ
  • {PARI 9-17} irattal um ītal um ivai uḷḷīṭu ā
  • {PARI 9-18} parattai uḷḷatu ē paṇpuṟu kaḻaṟal
  • {PARI 9-19} tōḷ putitu uṇṭa parattai il civappuṟa
  • {PARI 9-20} nāḷ aṇintu uvakkum cuṇaṅkaṟaiyatu ē
  • {PARI 9-21} kēḷ aṇaṅkuṟa maṉai kiḷantu uḷa cuṇaṅkaṟai
  • {PARI 9-22} cuṇaṅkaṟai payaṉ um ūṭal uḷḷatu ē
  • {PARI 9-23} ataṉāl akaṟal aṟiyā aṇi iḻai nallār
  • {PARI 9-24} ikal talaikkoṇṭu tuṉikkum tavaṟu ilar i
  • {PARI 9-25} taḷḷā poruḷ iyalpiṉ taṇ tamiḻ āyvantilār
  • {PARI 9-26} koḷḷār i kuṉṟu payaṉ
  • {PARI 9-27} ūḻ ārattu ōy karai nūkki puṉal tanta
  • {PARI 9-28} kāḻ ārattu am pukai cuṟṟiya tār mārpiṉ
  • {PARI 9-29} kēḻ āram poṟpa varuvāṉai toḻāa
  • {PARI 9-30} vāḻiya māyā niṉ tavaṟu ilai em pōlum
  • {PARI 9-31} kēḻ ilār māṇ nalam uṇku ō tiru uṭaiyār
  • {PARI 9-32} mel tōḷ mēl alki nalkal um iṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-33} vai eyiṟṟu eyyā makaḷir tiṟam iṉi
  • {PARI 9-34} peyya uḻakkum maḻai kā maṟṟu aiya
  • {PARI 9-35} karaiyā ve nōkkattāṉ kai cuṭṭi peṇṭiṉ
  • {PARI 9-36} ikaliṉ ikantāḷai a vēḷ talai kaṇṇi
  • {PARI 9-37} tiruntu aṭi tōya tiṟai koṭuppāṉai
  • {PARI 9-38} varuntal eṉa avaṟku mārpu aḷippāḷai
  • {PARI 9-39} kuṟukal eṉṟu oḷ iḻai kōtai kōl āka
  • {PARI 9-40} iṟuka iṟuka yāttu puṭaippa
  • {PARI 9-41} oruvar mayil oruvar oḷ mayilōṭu ēla
  • {PARI 9-42} iruvar vāṉ kiḷi ēṟpil maḻalai
  • {PARI 9-43} ceṟi koṇṭai mēl vaṇṭu ceṉṟu pāyntaṉṟu ē
  • {PARI 9-44} veṟi koṇṭāṉ kuṉṟattu vaṇṭu
  • {PARI 9-45} tār tār piṇakkuvār kaṇṇi ōcci taṭumāṟuvār
  • {PARI 9-46} mārpu aṇi koṅkai vār mattikai ā puṭaippār
  • {PARI 9-47} kōtai vari pantu koṇṭu eṟivār
  • {PARI 9-48} pētai maṭa nōkkam piṟitu āka ūta
  • {PARI 9-49} nuṭaṅku noci nucuppār nūḻil talaikkoḷḷa
  • {PARI 9-50} kayampaṭu kamaḻ ceṉṉi kaḷiṟṟu iyal kaimmāṟuvār
  • {PARI 9-51} vayampaṭu pari puravi mārkkam varuvār
  • {PARI 9-52} tēr aṇi aṇi kayiṟu teripu varuvār
  • {PARI 9-53} vari cilai vaḷaiya mārpu uṟa vāṅkuvār
  • {PARI 9-54} vāḷi vāḷikaḷ nilaipeṟa maṟaluvār
  • {PARI 9-55} tōḷ vaḷai āḻi cuḻaṟṟuvār
  • {PARI 9-56} mel cīr mayil iyalavar
  • {PARI 9-57} vāḷ miku vaya moympiṉ
  • {PARI 9-58} varai akalattavaṉai vāṉavaṉ makaḷ
  • {PARI 9-59} māṇ eḻil malar uṇ kaṇ
  • {PARI 9-60} maṭa moḻiyavar uṭaṉ cuṟṟi
  • {PARI 9-61} kaṭi cuṉai uḷ kuḷittu āṭunar um
  • {PARI 9-62} aṟai aṇinta arum cuṉaiyāṉ
  • {PARI 9-63} naṟa uṇ vaṇṭu āy narampu uḷarnar um
  • {PARI 9-64} cikai mayil āy tōkai virittu āṭunar um
  • {PARI 9-65} kōkulam āy kūvunar um
  • {PARI 9-66} ākulam ākunar um
  • {PARI 9-67} kuṟiñci kuṉṟavar maṟam keḻu vaḷḷi tamar
  • {PARI 9-68} vittaka tumpai viḷaittalāṉ veṉ vēlāṟku
  • {PARI 9-69} ottaṉṟu taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-70} kaṭum cūr mā mutal taṭintu aṟutta vēl
  • {PARI 9-71} aṭum pōrāḷa niṉ kuṉṟiṉ micai
  • {PARI 9-72} āṭal naviṉṟōr avar pōr ceṟuppa um
  • {PARI 9-73} pāṭal payiṉṟōrai pāṇar ceṟuppa um
  • {PARI 9-74} vallārai vallār ceṟuppa um
  • {PARI 9-75} allārai allār ceṟuppa um ōr col āy
  • {PARI 9-76} cemmai putu puṉal
  • {PARI 9-77} taṭākam ēṟṟa taṇ cuṉai pāṅkar
  • {PARI 9-78} paṭākai niṉṟaṉṟu
  • {PARI 9-79} mēe eḵkiṉavai
  • {PARI 9-80} veṉṟu uyartta koṭi viṟal cāṉṟavai
  • {PARI 9-81} kaṟpu iṇai neṟi ūṭu aṟpu iṇai kiḻamai
  • {PARI 9-82} naya taku marapiṉ viya taku kumara
  • {PARI 9-83} vāḻttiṉēm paravutum tāḻttu talai niṉai yām
  • {PARI 9-84} nayattaliṉ ciṟanta em aṭiyuṟai
  • {PARI 9-85} payattaliṉ ciṟakka nāḷ toṟum polintu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If he knew [our] good house
  • in the small village in the vicinity of the palmyra fence,
  • where the red-headed kids left behind crying in the house,
  • since [their] bent-legged[FNote_1062] father who threw the fierce shark
  • went without taking [them] to the big sea buzzing with birds,
  • rejoice in getting, singing with hot breasts, with [the help of] a bamboo [stick]
  • the palmyra fruit with sweet core(?) for which they had made an unhasty effort(?),
  • it would be[FNote_1063] good,
  • - [our] lover who has very great love.
  • Even if hateful affliction destroyed [him][FNote_1064] for many days,
  • when he seemed to be perishing along with the seashore grove to which he came [and] went with a superior heart, he will remove [it].

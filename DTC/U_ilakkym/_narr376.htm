{{Readable Translation}}

  • O green group with curved beak that eats with [its] relations,
  • many together, as if from the liberality of one without limit,
  • of the red millet with greens stalks [and] bowed ears,
  • bending down as the broad trunk of a winnow-eared elephant
  • if you catch sight of him with a good garland on his chest,
  • who appears by the Aśoka tree with a bow tie close around [him]
  • [and] with a wet cool chaplet in which with the Illam
  • hemp, wild jasmine, Kūtaḷ [and] blue water-lily are set close,
  • talk to him a little, so that he knows well:
  • Haven't you understood that [our] dutiful/virtueless mother
  • won't let [us] guard the poor field afterwards,
  • plagueing [us] as if all the plagues together(?)?

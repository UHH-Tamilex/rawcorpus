{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The man from the coast of pure sands spreading in every ghat
  2. where the full dewy big blossoms of the curvy-thorned Muṇṭakam
  3. are scattered by the wind like pearls from a broken thread,
  4. him I love and mother has warmed [to him]
  5. and father must give [him to me][FNote_217]
  6. and even the prattling village speaks for him.

Altern: T.V.G.

6b {--!}and even the village talks about [my connection] with him.

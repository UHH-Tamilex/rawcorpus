{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Dark one with beauty spots fine [and] many
  2. as the pollen of the red lotus in deep water,
  3. with a honey-hoarding coral mouth redolent of white water-lilies,
  4. with massed dark tresses, redolent of blue water-lilies!
  5. You,
  6. don't fear my saying 'don't you fear!'[FNote_1230] -
  7. As for me,
  8. even if I obtained the sea-girt orb,
  9. where short-legged geese are resting upon heaped sand[FNote_1231],
  10. I won't think of[FNote_1232] letting go of your[FNote_1233] friendship.{(_|)}

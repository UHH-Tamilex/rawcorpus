{{Readable Translation}}

  1. If he leaves [us], the man from the coast of the broad water
  2. with many-flowered seashore groves, where the red-mouthed big flock
  3. of little white crows(?)[FNote_1349] permanently[FNote_1350] sulks with the coldness(?)[FNote_1351],
  4. since it wets [their] backs [already] moist from the drops thrown by the waves
  5. - what else, friend, is it that we can loose
  6. than our one sweet life?

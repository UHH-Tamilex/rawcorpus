{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As for its not coming, that is hardly possible.[FNote_1003]
  2. The day which means you may have his chest
  3. has come so close, friend.
  4. My mother has understood [and] talks great things
  5. to the man from the ghat, where the long Palmyras
  6. become short, as sand heaps crawl, which are climbed by Aṭumpu plants
  7. [and] placed by the west wind, to bury [their] swaying trunks, in the seashore grove.[FNote_1004]

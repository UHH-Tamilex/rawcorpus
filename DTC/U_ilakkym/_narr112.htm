  8. maliyimip patupōṉ miṉṉic
  9. ciliva lēṟṟoṭu ceṟintavim maḻikkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • How can we give a festival, friend,
  • for this rin, dense with resounding strong strokes [of thunder],
  • flashing as if [it were] the mountin's twinkling,
  • spreading, so that depths arise in the vast area, water that comes down
  • from sapphire-coloured waterfalls, drawing from the great sea,
  • while [we] long for [and] know of the coming of the man from the land of big stones, where the lion wanders with unaffected mind, killing the elephant bull so that the mountin side is lonely, humming with bees in the black-trunked Vēṅki trees come to bloom so that the buds break open on the slope?

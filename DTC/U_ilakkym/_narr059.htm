{{Readable Translation}}

  • It [lies] in a land of wilderness with hard fields,
  • where the hunter who had killed the leguan, excavated(?) the frog,
  • dug white ant from the long-peaked anthill
  • [and] thrown down the hare even at night(?) forgets the bundle
  • of many different stores on [his] pretty shoulder
  • [and] is elated by sweet intoxication with toddy at a great drinking,
  • - the village where she remins [and] remembers
  • in a heart with the vow(?) of desiring us, mingling in love.
  • Now, in the woodland where fine buds have opened on the jasmine,
  • patience has been completely united with suffering.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. muṉṉiya muṭittaṉa māyi ṉaṉṉutal
  2. varuva meṉṉum paruvara ṟīrap
  3. paṭuṅkol vāḻi neṭuñcuvarp palli
  4. paraṟṟalip pōkiya ciṟaṟṟalik kaḷḷi
  5. mīmicik kalitta vīnaṟu mulli ((5))
  6. yāṭutalit taṭuviṉ ṟōṭutalip peyarkkum
  7. vaṉki yiṭiya ṉellip parīiya
  8. veṇpōḻ tiiya valaṅkalan toṭali
  9. maṟukuṭaṉ kamaḻu mālic
  10. ciṟukuṭip pākkattem perunaka rāṉē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Will a lizard appear - live - on the long wall
  • to end the suffering by saying "we will come,
  • [you of] good forehead, when [our] plans are completed",
  • - in our big mansion in the village of small homes,
  • at evening when swaying pretty garlands emit
  • fragrance to all sides, knitted from white palmyra leaves(?),
  • swept together[FNote_549] at daytime by the strong-handed herdsman,
  • who drives the heads in masses for searching [pasture], the heads of sheep,
  • in the jasmine fragrant with blossoms that flourished on the heights
  • of spurges with kingfisher[-like] heads, gone over the pebble-heads(?)[FNote_550]?

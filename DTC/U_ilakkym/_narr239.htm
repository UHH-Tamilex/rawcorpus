{{Metrical text}}

  1. ñāṉṟa ñāyiṟu kuṭamalai maṟaiya
  2. māṉṟa mālai makiḻnta paratava
  3. riṉitupeṟu perumī ṉeḷitaṉiṉ māṟi
  4. yalava ṉāṭiya pulavumaṇal muṉṟiṟ
  5. kāmar ciṟukuṭic ceṉṉeṟi vaḻiyi ((5))
  6. ṉaymaṇi potiyaviḻn tāṅku neytaṟ
  7. pullitaḻ potinta pūnta mitikku
  8. malla liruṅkaḻi malinīrc cērppaṟ
  9. kamaintutoḻil kēṭṭaṉṟō vilamē muṉkai
  10. vārkō lelvaḷai yuṭaiya vāṅki ((10))
  11. muyaṅkeṉak kaliḻnta vivvū
  12. reṟṟā vatukolyāṉ maṟṟoṉṟu ceyiṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • As for listening to the request(?)[FNote_754], consenting
  • to the man from the coast of abundant waters, with excellent dark backwaters,
  • where, when the sun that declined vanishes behind the western mountain,
  • the fishermen, delighted[FNote_755] in diffuse evening,
  • since they had exchanged easily a big fish pleasantly obtained,[FNote_756]
  • on [their] way on the path to the desirable small hamlet
  • with frontyards to the fish-reeking sand where crabs have been playing,
  • trample the flowers themselves[FNote_757], that bundled [their] low(?)[FNote_758] petals,
  • of the water-lilies blue as an opened hoard of choice sapphires,
  • we didn't.
  • What will become
  • of this village that has started weeping "embrace [him]
  • bending the bright bangles in long row on [your] forearm up to breaking",
  • if I do just the one thing?

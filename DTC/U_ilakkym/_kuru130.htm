KT 130 Veḷḷivītiyār (-vīti): the friend [SHE]

{{kiḷavi}}: (1. Encouraged by the friend HER(t.) who is broken down in the time of separation. Strength given to HER(t.) by the friend, saying 'what happened that you don't have the strength becuse he has separated? I, knowing his interior (or: his usual path) will bring him here by sending a messenger. May your lack of strength pass by.' 2. Spoken becuse of her lack of strength by HER(t.), so that the friend would send a messenger.){{/kiḷavi}}

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. [Since] he has not disappeared inside the earth, not climbed the sky,
  2. not gone[FNote_524] by foot across the transverse, dark three waters,
  3. if we search land by land, village by village,
  4. every home row by row,[FNote_525]
  5. is he one to get lost[FNote_526], our lover?

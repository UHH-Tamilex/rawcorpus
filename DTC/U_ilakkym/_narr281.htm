{{Readable Translation}}

  • He (is) devoid of love, friend, our lover,
  • he who knows we will be sleepless ,
  • being very much troubled by the fierceness of our(?) dew,
  • even when he himself (is) near us
  • at midnight in great darkness that quietly(?) had rin
  • while the offering-eating crows of the flawless tree,
  • hanging with the drops on the long branches beaten by the wind,
  • are perched, remembering the great cooked rice with meat
  • from the nice much(?) yield of endless boiled rice
  • that is shed along with the giving of much offering of good sort,
  • when the Cōḻar victorious in battle takes Kaḻāar-ghat.(?!)

{{Metrical text}}

  1. vēṭṭam poyyātu valaivaḷañ ciṟappap
  2. pāṭṭam poyyātu paratavar pakara
  3. virumpaṉan tīmpiḻi yuṇpōr makiḻu
  4. mārkali yāṇart tāyiṉun tōṭukeḻu
  5. mellam pulampaṉ piriyiṟ pulleṉap ((5))
  6. pulampā kiṉṟē tōḻi kalaṅkunīrk
  7. kaḻicūḻ paṭappaik kāṇṭa vāyi
  8. lolikā vōlai muṇmiṭai vēlip
  9. peṇṇai yivaru māṅkaṇ
  10. veṇmaṇaṟ paṭappaiyem maḻuṅka lūrē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if the loudly roaring [sea?][FNote_161] gives fertility,
  • where the eaters are delighted, pressing out the sweet of the dark palmyra leaf,
  • while the fishermen call out ?,
  • when the income of [their] nets is excellent ?,
  • when the wealthy(?)[FNote_162], tender bringer of loneliness separated,
  • empty, loneliness has become, friend,
  • our noisy village with gardens in the white sands,
  • in that place, where palmyra palms climb,
  • hedges where thorns noisy ripe palm-leaves,
  • in Kāṇṭavāyil, gardens surrounded by backwaters with stirring water.

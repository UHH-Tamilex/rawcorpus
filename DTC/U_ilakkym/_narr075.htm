{{Readable Translation}}

  • Without [even] saying 'this [is] gin, in the absence of longing',
  • [wounding me] like[FNote_270] the lethal wound of the snake,
  • gently, with fire-spitting interior, glittering with flower[-like] spots,
  • this here is not fit, may you live, little woman.
  • Talk [to me] without smiling,
  • so that(?)[FNote_271] my suffering heart survive,
  • which had a look at, [however] without having
  • [her of] big-petalled rin eyes spread with red lines,
  • as arrows pulled(?) from the fresh flesh,
  • after the forest men with bent bows have killed the boar on the slope,
  • in my breaking mind.[FNote_272]

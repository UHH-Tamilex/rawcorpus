{{Readable Translation}}

  • O heart, while looking, for longing to arise, with our eyes, [at her,]
  • as at Āṟēṟu[FNote_827] of Ceṉṉi with small staff,
  • with sandal[FNote_828] chest, as at Pāram,
  • which is guarded by Miñili with [his] binding of flowers on the shoulders(?),[FNote_829]
  • the great son of bowmen with whistling arrows
  • in the forest woodland, where the confused[FNote_830] doe [and] the stag
  • with dots [and] lines that have played in the mud, that ripens
  • the mangled poor tips of hard grass[FNote_831], are frightened,
  • - her flourishing soft tresses [are] with us,
  • as the feathers of a proud peacock
  • on Kolli hill of delightful Ōri, generous [as] the showers.

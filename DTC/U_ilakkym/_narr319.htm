{{Metrical text}}

  1. ōtamu moliyō viṉṟē yūtiyun
  2. tātuḷar kāṉaṟ ṟavveṉ ṟaṉṟē
  3. maṇaṉmali mūtū rakaṉeṭun teruviṟ
  4. kūkic cēval kurālō ṭēṟi
  5. yāriruñ catukkat tañcuvarak kuḻaṟu ((5))
  6. maṇaṅkukāl kiḷaṟu mayaṅkiru ṇaṭunāṭ
  7. pāvi yaṉṉa palarāy vaṉappiṟ
  8. ṟaṭameṉ paṇittōṇ maṭamiku kuṟumakaḷ
  9. cuṇaṅkaṇi vaṉamuli muyaṅka luḷḷi
  10. mīṉkaṇ ṭuñcum poḻutum ((10))
  11. yāṉkaṇ ṭuñcēṉ yātuko ṉiliyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The sound of the flood has stopped and the cold wind
  • whiffling pollen has come down intensily[FNote_932] on the seashore grove.
  • At confusing dark midnight, when the time comes up,
  • where the cock of the owl rises rises with [its] female(?)[FNote_933]
  • in the wide long streets of the old village full of sand
  • [and] shrieks on the difficult dark junctions for fear to come,
  • my[FNote_934] eyes don't sleep,
  • in the rememberance of embracing the beautiful breast adorned with spots
  • of the little woman full of inexperience, with broad soft bamboo shoulders,
  • of a beauty appreciated by many, like an image.
  • What [is this] state?

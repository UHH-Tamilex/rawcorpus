{{Readable Translation}}

  • At the dimming of [my] rain eyes, graceful [as] buds,
  • water rolling ceaselessly to the limit of [my] checking, although I suffered,
  • having looked at the dark row[FNote_73] of wide dark forests
  • with waterfall, which descend like the waves of the big sea
  • from the good long hills, which were offered rain in the day,
  • - when mother spoke such soft [and] pleasing [words]:
  • 'what have you done? let me kiss your shining teeth.',
  • I [just] escaped telling, friend,
  • completely forgotten also shame, superior to life [itself], in great haste,
  • that [my] plague [is] the chest of the man from the sky-touching mountains,
  • where the sappire-coloured bee filled up with Malabar lilies on the slope
  • is humming like the sweet string of a lute.

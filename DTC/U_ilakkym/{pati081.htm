  • {PATI 81-1} ulakam purakkum uru keḻu ciṟappiṉ
  • {PATI 81-2} vaṇṇa karuviya vaḷam keḻu kamam cūl
  • {PATI 81-3} akal iru vicumpiṉ atir ciṉam ciṟantu
  • {PATI 81-4} kaṭum cili kaḻaṟi vicumpu aṭiyū nivantu
  • {PATI 81-5} kāli icikkum poḻutoṭu pulampu koḷa
  • {PATI 81-6} kaḷiṟu pāyntu iyala kaṭu mā tāṅka
  • {PATI 81-7} oḷiṟu koṭi nuṭaṅka tēr tirintu koṭpa
  • {PATI 81-8} aracu puṟattu iṟuppiṉum atirvu ilar tirintu koṭpa
  • {PATI 81-9} vāyil koḷḷā mintiṉar vayavar
  • {PATI 81-10} mā irum kaṅkul um viḻu toṭi cuṭarvara
  • {PATI 81-11} tōḷ piṇi mīkiyar pukal ciṟantu nāḷ um
  • {PATI 81-12} muṭital vēṭkiyar neṭiya moḻiyūu
  • {PATI 81-13} keṭāa nal ici tam kuṭi niṟumār
  • {PATI 81-14} iṭāa ēṇi viyal aṟi koṭpa
  • {PATI 81-15} nāṭu aṭippaṭuttaliṉ koḷḷi māṟṟi
  • {PATI 81-16} aḻal viṉi aminta niḻal viṭu kaṭṭi
  • {PATI 81-17} kaṭṭaḷi valippa niṉ tāṉi utavi
  • {PATI 81-18} vēṟu pulattu iṟutta vel pōr aṇṇal
  • {PATI 81-19} muḻaviṉ aminta perum paḻam micintu
  • {PATI 81-20} cāṟu ayarntaṉṉa kār aṇi yāṇar
  • {PATI 81-21} tūmpu akam paḻuṉiya tīm piḻi mānti
  • {PATI 81-22} kāntaḷ am kaṇṇi ceḻum kuṭi celvar
  • {PATI 81-23} kali makiḻ mēvalar iravalarkku īyum
  • {PATI 81-24} curumpu ār cōli perum peyal kolli
  • {PATI 81-25} peru vāy malaroṭu pacumpiṭi makiḻntu
  • {PATI 81-26} miṉ umiḻntaṉṉa cuṭar iḻi āyattu
  • {PATI 81-27} taṉ niṟam karanta vaṇṭu paṭu katuppiṉ
  • {PATI 81-28} oṭuṅku īr ōti oḷ nutal aṇi koḷa
  • {PATI 81-29} koṭum kuḻikku amartta nōkkiṉ nayavara
  • {PATI 81-30} peruntakikku amarnta mel col tiru mukattu
  • {PATI 81-31} māṇ iḻi arivi kāṇiya oru nāḷ
  • {PATI 81-32} pūṇka māḷa niṉ puravi neṭum tēr
  • {PATI 81-33} muṉi kiviṭṭu muṉṉili cellātu
  • {PATI 81-34} tū etirntu peṟāa tā il maḷḷaroṭu
  • {PATI 81-35} tol maruṅku aṟuttal añci araṇ koṇṭu
  • {PATI 81-36} tuñcā vēntar um tuñcuka
  • {PATI 81-37} viruntu um āka niṉ perum tōṭku ē

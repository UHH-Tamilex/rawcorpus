{{Readable Translation}}

  1. While destructive affliction [and] inner pin increase steadily[FNote_694],
  2. [my] shame killed, mounting, so that [its] bells ring,
  3. the proud(?) horse of Palmyra with a many-threaded garland
  4. close-set with new, gold-like Āviri blossoms,
  5. - I am [prepared][FNote_695] to go up to this point, for this village to realise,
  6. so that it confronts [her] saying: this is the doing of such a one[FNote_696]
  7. [and] talks of her blame.

1b: set close with new blossoms of Āviri [and] fine gold,

5-7b {--!}- I am [prepared] to go thus far, if this village then at last realises, {--!}[and] confronts [her] saying: this is the doing of such a one, {--!}[and] talks of her blame.

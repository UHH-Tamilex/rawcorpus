Paripāṭal 16

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 16-1} karai ē kai vaṇ tōṉṟal īkai pōṉm eṉa
  • {PARI 16-2} mai paṭu cilampiṉ kaṟiyoṭu um cāntoṭu um
  • {PARI 16-3} ney kuṭai tayiriṉ nuraiyoṭu um piṟavoṭu um
  • {PARI 16-4} e vayiṉāṉ um mītu mītu aḻiyum
  • {PARI 16-5} tuṟai ē muttu nērpu puṇar kāḻ mattaka nittilam
  • {PARI 16-6} polam puṉai avir iḻai kalaṅkal am puṉal maṇi
  • {PARI 16-7} valam cuḻi untiya tiṇai piri putalvar
  • {PARI 16-8} kayam talai mucciya muñcamoṭu taḻīi
  • {PARI 16-9} tam tam tuṇaiyōṭu oruṅku uṭaṉ āṭum
  • {PARI 16-10} tattu ari kaṇṇār talaitalai varum ē
  • {PARI 16-11} ceṟu ē viṭu malar cumantu pū nīr niṟaitaliṉ
  • {PARI 16-12} paṭu kaṇ imiḻ koḷai payiṉṟaṉar āṭum
  • {PARI 16-13} kaḷi nāḷ araṅkiṉ aṇi nalam puraiyum
  • {PARI 16-14} kā ē curumpu imir tātoṭu talaitalai mikūum
  • {PARI 16-15} naranta naṟu malar naṉku aḷikkum ē
  • {PARI 16-16} karaipu oḻuku tīm puṉaṟku etir viruntu ayarva pōl
  • {PARI 16-17} kāṉ al am kā um kayam um turutti um tēṉ
  • {PARI 16-18} tēṉ uṇṭu pāṭa ticai ticai pū nalam
  • {PARI 16-19} pūttaṉṟu vaiyai varavu
  • {PARI 16-20} curuṅkaiyiṉ āyattār cuṟṟum eṟintu
  • {PARI 16-21} kurumpai mulai paṭṭa pū nīr tuṭaiyāḷ
  • {PARI 16-22} peruntakai mīḷi varuvāṉai kaṇṭu ē
  • {PARI 16-23} irum tukil tāṉaiyiṉ oṟṟi poruntalai
  • {PARI 16-24} pūttaṉaḷ nīṅku eṉa poy āṟṟāl tōḻiyar
  • {PARI 16-25} tōṟṟam ōr otta malar kamaḻ taṇ cāntiṉ
  • {PARI 16-26} nāṟṟattiṉ pōṟṟi nakaiyoṭu um pōttantu
  • {PARI 16-27} irum kaṭaṟku ūṅku ivarum yāṟu eṉa taṅkāṉ
  • {PARI 16-28} makiḻa kaḷi paṭṭa tēṉ tēṟal māṟṟi
  • {PARI 16-29} kuruti tuṭaiyā kuṟuki maruva iṉiyar
  • {PARI 16-30} pūttaṉaḷ naṅkai polika eṉa nāṇutal
  • {PARI 16-31} vāyttaṉṟu āl vaiyai varavu
  • {PARI 16-32} malaiyiṉ iḻi aruvi malku iṇar cār cār
  • {PARI 16-33} karai maram cērntu kaviṉi maṭavār
  • {PARI 16-34} naṉai cēr katuppiṉ uḷ taṇ pōtu maintar
  • {PARI 16-35} malar mārpiṉ cōrnta malar itaḻ tāay
  • {PARI 16-36} mīṉ āram pūtta viyaṉ kaṅkai nantiya
  • {PARI 16-37} vāṉam peyarnta maruṅku ottal e ñāṉṟu um
  • {PARI 16-38} tēṉ imir vaiyaikku iyalpu
  • {PARI 16-39} puḷ ē puṉal ē pulavi i mūṉṟiṉum
  • {PARI 16-40} oḷ oḷi ceytu ā oḷi kiḷar uṇ kaṇ keṇṭai
  • {PARI 16-41} pal vari vaṇṭu iṉam vāy cūḻ kaviṉoṭu um
  • {PARI 16-42} cel nīr vī vayiṉ tēṉ cōra pal nīr
  • {PARI 16-43} aṭuttu aṭuttu āṭuvār pulla kuḻaintu
  • {PARI 16-44} vaṭu paṭu māṉmata cāntu ār akalattāṉ
  • {PARI 16-45} eṭutta vēy ekki nūkku uyarpu tākka
  • {PARI 16-46} toṭutta tēṉ cōrum varai pōlum tōṟṟam
  • {PARI 16-47} koṭi tērāṉ vaiyaikku iyalpu
  • {PARI 16-48} varai ārkkum puyal karai
  • {PARI 16-49} tirai ārkkum i tīm puṉal
  • {PARI 16-50} kaṇṇiyar tārar kamaḻ naṟum kōtaiyar
  • {PARI 16-51} paṇṇiya īkai payaṉ koḷvāṉ āṭalāl
  • {PARI 16-52} nāḷ nāḷ uṟai um naṟum cāntu um kōtai um
  • {PARI 16-53} pūtta pukai um avi um pularāmai
  • {PARI 16-54} maṟāaṟka vāṉam malitantu nīttam
  • {PARI 16-55} vaṟāaṟka vaiyai niṉakku

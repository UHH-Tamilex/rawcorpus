Paripāṭal 16

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 16-1} kari ē ki vaṇ tōṉṟal īki pōṉm eṉa
  • {PARI 16-2} mi paṭu cilampiṉ kaṟiyoṭu um cāntoṭu um
  • {PARI 16-3} ney kuṭi tayiriṉ nuriyoṭu um piṟavoṭu um
  • {PARI 16-4} e vayiṉāṉ um mītu mītu aḻiyum
  • {PARI 16-5} tuṟi ē muttu nērpu puṇar kāḻ mattaka nittilam
  • {PARI 16-6} polam puṉi avir iḻi kalaṅkal am puṉal maṇi
  • {PARI 16-7} valam cuḻi untiya tiṇi piri putalvar
  • {PARI 16-8} kayam tali mucciya muñcamoṭu taḻīi
  • {PARI 16-9} tam tam tuṇiyōṭu oruṅku uṭaṉ āṭum
  • {PARI 16-10} tattu ari kaṇṇār talitali varum ē
  • {PARI 16-11} ceṟu ē viṭu malar cumantu pū nīr niṟitaliṉ
  • {PARI 16-12} paṭu kaṇ imiḻ koḷi payiṉṟaṉar āṭum
  • {PARI 16-13} kaḷi nāḷ araṅkiṉ aṇi nalam puriyum
  • {PARI 16-14} kā ē curumpu imir tātoṭu talitali mikūum
  • {PARI 16-15} naranta naṟu malar naṉku aḷikkum ē
  • {PARI 16-16} karipu oḻuku tīm puṉaṟku etir viruntu ayarva pōl
  • {PARI 16-17} kāṉ al am kā um kayam um turutti um tēṉ
  • {PARI 16-18} tēṉ uṇṭu pāṭa tici tici pū nalam
  • {PARI 16-19} pūttaṉṟu viyi varavu
  • {PARI 16-20} curuṅkiyiṉ āyattār cuṟṟum eṟintu
  • {PARI 16-21} kurumpi muli paṭṭa pū nīr tuṭiyāḷ
  • {PARI 16-22} peruntaki mīḷi varuvāṉi kaṇṭu ē
  • {PARI 16-23} irum tukil tāṉiyiṉ oṟṟi poruntali
  • {PARI 16-24} pūttaṉaḷ nīṅku eṉa poy āṟṟāl tōḻiyar
  • {PARI 16-25} tōṟṟam ōr otta malar kamaḻ taṇ cāntiṉ
  • {PARI 16-26} nāṟṟattiṉ pōṟṟi nakiyoṭu um pōttantu
  • {PARI 16-27} irum kaṭaṟku ūṅku ivarum yāṟu eṉa taṅkāṉ
  • {PARI 16-28} makiḻa kaḷi paṭṭa tēṉ tēṟal māṟṟi
  • {PARI 16-29} kuruti tuṭiyā kuṟuki maruva iṉiyar
  • {PARI 16-30} pūttaṉaḷ naṅki polika eṉa nāṇutal
  • {PARI 16-31} vāyttaṉṟu āl viyi varavu
  • {PARI 16-32} maliyiṉ iḻi aruvi malku iṇar cār cār
  • {PARI 16-33} kari maram cērntu kaviṉi maṭavār
  • {PARI 16-34} naṉi cēr katuppiṉ uḷ taṇ pōtu mintar
  • {PARI 16-35} malar mārpiṉ cōrnta malar itaḻ tāay
  • {PARI 16-36} mīṉ āram pūtta viyaṉ kaṅki nantiya
  • {PARI 16-37} vāṉam peyarnta maruṅku ottal e ñāṉṟu um
  • {PARI 16-38} tēṉ imir viyikku iyalpu
  • {PARI 16-39} puḷ ē puṉal ē pulavi i mūṉṟiṉum
  • {PARI 16-40} oḷ oḷi ceytu ā oḷi kiḷar uṇ kaṇ keṇṭi
  • {PARI 16-41} pal vari vaṇṭu iṉam vāy cūḻ kaviṉoṭu um
  • {PARI 16-42} cel nīr vī vayiṉ tēṉ cōra pal nīr
  • {PARI 16-43} aṭuttu aṭuttu āṭuvār pulla kuḻintu
  • {PARI 16-44} vaṭu paṭu māṉmata cāntu ār akalattāṉ
  • {PARI 16-45} eṭutta vēy ekki nūkku uyarpu tākka
  • {PARI 16-46} toṭutta tēṉ cōrum vari pōlum tōṟṟam
  • {PARI 16-47} koṭi tērāṉ viyikku iyalpu
  • {PARI 16-48} vari ārkkum puyal kari
  • {PARI 16-49} tiri ārkkum i tīm puṉal
  • {PARI 16-50} kaṇṇiyar tārar kamaḻ naṟum kōtiyar
  • {PARI 16-51} paṇṇiya īki payaṉ koḷvāṉ āṭalāl
  • {PARI 16-52} nāḷ nāḷ uṟi um naṟum cāntu um kōti um
  • {PARI 16-53} pūtta puki um avi um pularāmi
  • {PARI 16-54} maṟāaṟka vāṉam malitantu nīttam
  • {PARI 16-55} vaṟāaṟka viyi niṉakku

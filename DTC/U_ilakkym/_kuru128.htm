{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Like a cormorant(?)[FNote_515] with failing wings in(?)[FNote_516] the waves of the eastern sea
  2. lifts [its] head towards prey difficult [to obtin], the Ayiri fish,
  3. [near] the ghat in front[FNote_517] of Toṇṭi, [the city] of Poṟiyaṉ of firm chariot,[FNote_518]
  4. [so too] you think of her who is distant, difficult [to obtin],
  5. full of pin you [are], heart, pin your share.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Since [my] yearning became false with the not granting
  • of the man from the ghat, where the red-beaked male
  • of the sea crow stirs the flowery pond
  • by the clear backwaters, searching for Ayiri fish in the dark mud
  • for [its] desirable female in high pregnancy
  • on one side of the white sands with thick Aṭumpus,
  • ruined since women [engaged in] austerity plucked the creepers,
  • the calumnous old village's knowing it,
  • with mouths gossiping about [my] lowness, turned to utter motionlessness,
  • became pin greater than pin.

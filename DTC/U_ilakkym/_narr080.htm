{{Metrical text}}

  1. maṉṟa verumai malartalaik kārā
  2. ṉiṉṟīm pāṟpayaṅ koṇmār kaṉṟuviṭ
  3. ṭūrkkuṟu mākkaṇ mēṟkoṇṭu kaḻiyum
  4. perumpular viṭiyaliṉ virumpip pōttantu
  5. taḻaiyun tāṟun tantaṉa ṉivaṉeṉa ((5))
  6. viḻaiyaṇi yāyamoṭu tarunāṇ taṭaiit
  7. taiit tiṅkaṭ ṭaṇkayam paṭiyum
  8. peruntōṭ kuṟumaka ḷallatu
  9. maruntil laiyā ṉuṟṟa nōykkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • There is no remedy for the pain I had
  • besides the little woman with big shoulders
  • who immerses herself in the cool pond during the month of Tai,[FNote_291]
  • bent down with shame brought on by [her] jewel-adorned companions,
  • by saying "he gave [her] foliage and a garland,
  • going longingly at progressing(?)[FNote_292] dawn,
  • when the little people from the village passed by, mounted [on their animals?],
  • the calfs set free to take the yield of the pleasing sweet milk
  • of the flower-headed cows of the buffalos in the village common."

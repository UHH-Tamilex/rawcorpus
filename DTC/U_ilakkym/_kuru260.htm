{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Herons are climbing the dark sky, and[FNote_1052] shrubs have opened
  2. [their blossom] mouths so that striped bees fill themselves [with pollen][FNote_1053].
  3. While also [my] upper arms glittering with winding bangles are resisting,[FNote_1054]
  4. will he come, oh friend,
  5. who traversed the desert of low-based Ōmi trees,
  6. across which lies[FNote_1055] a single cow without calf
  7. from the mountinside thick with Vaḻi trees, of the Toṇṭiyar with strong chariots
  8. [and] superior elephants, who take over the land of [enemy] fighters?[FNote_1056]

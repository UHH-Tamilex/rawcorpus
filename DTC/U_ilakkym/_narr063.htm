{{Readable Translation}}

  • [Our] noisy village,
  • where the Puṉṉi trees emit fragrance all together, shining clusters
  • spread, smelling of festival, so that the noisy street don't smell of fish,
  • there by the new sand where much fish is put to dry
  • by the fishermen with big nets that had ploughed the strong sea?
  • - It is devoid of duty. Therefore,
  • Having turned into pallor, while [our] dutyless mother
  • cuses difficult protection, will it perish,
  • our attchment settled with the man from the coast of growing waves,
  • where the horses yoked in darkness that came up through the backwater desert,
  • in perturbed mire with flowers broken up when the bees come(?),
  • wash in the wave-bringing ocean?

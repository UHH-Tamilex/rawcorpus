{{Metrical text}}

  1. uḷḷār kollō tōḻi tuṇiyoṭu
  2. vēṉi lōtip pāṭunaṭi vaḻali
  3. varimā ṉukumpiṉ vāṭi yavaṇa
  4. vaṟaṉporuntu kuṉṟat tucci kavāaṉ
  5. vēṭṭac cīṟū rakaṉkaṭ kēṇip ((5))
  6. payanirik keṭunta maṇinīrp pantar
  7. puṉṟali maṭappiṭi kaṉṟō ṭāra
  8. vilkaṭin tūṭṭiṉa peyaruṅ
  9. kolkaḷi ṟoruttal curaniṟan tōrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Won't he remember, friend,
  • the one who traversed the desert of the killing elephant bull that returns
  • to those that are fed after having been chased away by the bow(?)[FNote_317],
  • so that the red-headed inexperienced she elephant with [her] calf become full
  • from the trough of sapphire water rised[FNote_318] for the yielding herd
  • from the wide-eyed well in the desired small village
  • on the slope near the summit of the hill approached by drught
  • the striped animals there(?) fading like banana leaves,
  • the sleepy-gited serpent, the chameleon in summer, with [its] mate[FNote_319]?

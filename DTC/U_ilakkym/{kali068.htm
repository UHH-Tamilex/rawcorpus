Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 68-1} potu moḻi piṟarkku iṉṟi muḻutu āḷum celvarkku
  • {KALI 68-2} mati moḻi iṭal mālai viṉaivar pōl vallavar
  • {KALI 68-3} cetu moḻi cītta cevi ceṟu āka
  • {KALI 68-4} mutu moḻi nīr ā pulaṉ nā uḻavar
  • {KALI 68-5} putu moḻi kūṭṭuṇṇum puricai cūḻ puṉal ūra
  • {KALI 68-6} ūraṉ maṉ uraṉ allaṉ namakku eṉṉa uṭaṉ vāḷātu
  • {KALI 68-7} ōr ūr tokku irunta niṉ peṇṭir uḷ nēr āki
  • {KALI 68-8} kaḷaiyā niṉ kuṟi vantu em katavam cērntu acaitta kai
  • {KALI 68-9} vaḷaiyiṉ vāyviṭal mālai makaḷirai nōvēm ō
  • {KALI 68-10} kēḷ alaṉ namakku avaṉ kuṟukaṉmiṉ eṉa maṟṟu em
  • {KALI 68-11} tōḷoṭu pakaipaṭṭu niṉai vāṭu neñcattēm
  • {KALI 68-12} ūṭiyār nalam tēmpa oṭiyeṟintu avar vayiṉ
  • {KALI 68-13} māl tīrkkum avaṉ mārpu eṉṟu eḻunta col nōvēm ō
  • {KALI 68-14} mukai vāytta mulai pāya kuḻainta niṉ tār eḷḷa
  • {KALI 68-15} vakai vari ceppiṉ uḷ vaikiya kōtaiyēm
  • {KALI 68-16} cēriyāl ceṉṟu nī cērnta il viṉāyiṉaṉ
  • {KALI 68-17} tēroṭu tiritarum pākaṉai paḻippēm ō
  • {KALI 68-18} oli koṇṭa cummaiyāṉ maṇa maṉai kuṟittu em il
  • {KALI 68-19} polika eṉa pukunta niṉ pulaiyaṉai kaṇṭa yām
  • {KALI 68-20} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 68-21} naṉaviṉāṉ vēṟu ākum vēḷā muyakkam
  • {KALI 68-22} maṉai variṉ peṟṟu uvantu maṟṟu em tōḷ vāṭa
  • {KALI 68-23} iṉaiyar eṉa uṇarntār eṉṟu ēkkaṟṟu āṅku
  • {KALI 68-24} kaṉaviṉāṉ eytiya celvattu aṉaiyatu ē
  • {KALI 68-25} aiya emakku niṉ mārpu

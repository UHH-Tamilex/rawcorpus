{{Readable Translation}}

  1. It is not[FNote_1587], friend, that we don't know someone
  2. who would wipe away the dew shed by crying eyes,
  3. arising from much joy of the body[FNote_1588],
  4. when we immerse ourselves in festival, becuse the lover difficult to obtin has come, on sorrowful evening, which rises misery with the glow
  5. kindled, after oil has been poured, as a support[FNote_1589] for the hands
  6. of women with thick ear-rings [and] carp-like collyrium eyes,
  7. at a time abounding in misery with spray spreading 'coolness'.

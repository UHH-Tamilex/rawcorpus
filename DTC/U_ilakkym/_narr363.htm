{{Readable Translation}}

  • If you were to take the order [and] go,
  • saying "I go to the good land by the clear sea,
  • with gardens surrounded by backwaters with mangrove fences",
  • come, live, o man from the coast of abundant water,
  • bringing sand from the ghat, for when[FNote_1018] the artisan puts the spotless
  • links together [again] on [his] furnace, languishing not on the work to be had.
  • The anklets have been crushed of her who has chased crabs,
  • so that [her] few shining light bangles become lose,
  • with you yesterday, in the good evening,
  • so that the fresh foliage was spoiled [and] the garland faded.

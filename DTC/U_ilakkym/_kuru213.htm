{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Pretty yearning he is,[FNote_853] friend, [so] they say,
  2. on the path that he, who is fed up with sweet sleep with us, went down,
  3. where the fork-headed old stag quickly scrapes with [its] leg,
  4. [and], if [something] is left of the great big [branch] being squashed[FNote_854]
  5. that he had bowed because of the fetter of hunger, eats, [and] for his calf
  6. with faultless heart[FNote_855] and naturally(?)[FNote_856] leaping gait he will be a shade
  7. [and] standing blocks off the heat.

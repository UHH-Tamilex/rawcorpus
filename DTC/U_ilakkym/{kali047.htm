  • {KALI 47-1} oṉṟu irappāṉ pōl eḷivantu um collum ulakam
  • {KALI 47-2} purappāṉ pōlvatu ōr matuki um uṭiyaṉ
  • {KALI 47-3} vallāri vaḻipaṭṭu oṉṟu aṟintāṉ pōl
  • {KALI 47-4} nallār kaṇ tōṉṟum aṭakkam um uṭiyaṉ
  • {KALI 47-5} illōr puṉkaṇ īkiyiṉ taṇikka
  • {KALI 47-6} vallāṉ pōlvatu ōr vaṉmi um uṭiyaṉ
  • {KALI 47-7} aṉṉāṉ oruvaṉ taṉ āṇtaki viṭṭu eṉṉi
  • {KALI 47-8} collum col kēṭṭī cuṭar iḻāy pal māṇum
  • {KALI 47-9} niṉ iṉṟi amiyalēṉ yāṉ eṉṉum avaṉ āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-10} aṉṉāṉ col nampuṇṭal yārkku um iṅku aritu āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-11} eṉ uṟṟa piṟarkku um āṅku uḷa kol ō naṟu nutāl
  • {KALI 47-12} aṟiyāy nī varuntuval yāṉ eṉṉum avaṉ āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-13} tamiyar ē tuṇikiṟṟal peṇṭirkku um aritu āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-14} aḷiyar ō em pōla īṅku ivaṉ valippaṭṭār
  • {KALI 47-15} vāḻalēṉ yāṉ eṉṉum nī nīppiṉ avaṉ āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-16} ēḻiyar eṉa palar kūṟum col paḻi āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-17} cūḻum kāl niṉippatu oṉṟu aṟikalēṉ varuntuval
  • {KALI 47-18} cūḻum kāl naṟu nutāl nam uḷ ē cūḻkuvam
  • {KALI 47-19} avaṉi
  • {KALI 47-20} nāṇ aṭa peyarttal namakku um āṅku ollātu
  • {KALI 47-21} pēṇiṉar eṉappaṭutal peṇmi um aṉṟu avaṉ
  • {KALI 47-22} vuviṉaṉ muyaṅkum māttiram vā eṉa
  • {KALI 47-23} kūṟuveṉ pōla kāṭṭi
  • {KALI 47-24} maṟṟu avaṉ mēe vaḻi mēvāy neñcu ē

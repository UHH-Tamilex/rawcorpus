Kalittokai

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 47-1} oṉṟu irappāṉ pōl eḷivantu um collum ulakam
  • {KALI 47-2} purappāṉ pōlvatu ōr matukai um uṭaiyaṉ
  • {KALI 47-3} vallārai vaḻipaṭṭu oṉṟu aṟintāṉ pōl
  • {KALI 47-4} nallār kaṇ tōṉṟum aṭakkam um uṭaiyaṉ
  • {KALI 47-5} illōr puṉkaṇ īkaiyiṉ taṇikka
  • {KALI 47-6} vallāṉ pōlvatu ōr vaṉmai um uṭaiyaṉ
  • {KALI 47-7} aṉṉāṉ oruvaṉ taṉ āṇtakai viṭṭu eṉṉai
  • {KALI 47-8} collum col kēṭṭī cuṭar iḻāy pal māṇum
  • {KALI 47-9} niṉ iṉṟi amaiyalēṉ yāṉ eṉṉum avaṉ āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-10} aṉṉāṉ col nampuṇṭal yārkku um iṅku aritu āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-11} eṉ uṟṟa piṟarkku um āṅku uḷa kol ō naṟu nutāl
  • {KALI 47-12} aṟiyāy nī varuntuval yāṉ eṉṉum avaṉ āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-13} tamiyar ē tuṇikiṟṟal peṇṭirkku um aritu āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-14} aḷiyar ō em pōla īṅku ivaṉ valaippaṭṭār
  • {KALI 47-15} vāḻalēṉ yāṉ eṉṉum nī nīppiṉ avaṉ āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-16} ēḻaiyar eṉa palar kūṟum col paḻi āyiṉ
  • {KALI 47-17} cūḻum kāl niṉaippatu oṉṟu aṟikalēṉ varuntuval
  • {KALI 47-18} cūḻum kāl naṟu nutāl nam uḷ ē cūḻkuvam
  • {KALI 47-19} avaṉai
  • {KALI 47-20} nāṇ aṭa peyarttal namakku um āṅku ollātu
  • {KALI 47-21} pēṇiṉar eṉappaṭutal peṇmai um aṉṟu avaṉ
  • {KALI 47-22} vauviṉaṉ muyaṅkum māttiram vā eṉa
  • {KALI 47-23} kūṟuveṉ pōla kāṭṭi
  • {KALI 47-24} maṟṟu avaṉ mēe vaḻi mēvāy neñcu ē

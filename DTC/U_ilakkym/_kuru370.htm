{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The man from a village by the cool ghat, where bee mouths open
  2. the rich buds in adorning colours on the water-lilies in the pond,
  3. - when being with him, we [are] two bodies. When resting,
  4. when, come together like the fingers in the centre of a bow(?)[FNote_1477],
  5. joining with his good mind(?)[FNote_1478], we [are] one body.

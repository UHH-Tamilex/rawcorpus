{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Midday on the slope of the long mountin, high with peaks,
  2. for a raft the chest of the man from the noisy(?)[FNote_1418] mountins.
  3. Sweet to play in the singing sweet waterfall[FNote_1419].
  4. Not sweet, in sleep to embrace the mother,
  5. embraced by [her upon whose] neck brids(?) are descending,[FNote_1420]
  6. in the good house with red glow on the white cotton wick,
  7. at night, with eyes of which the rows of petals don't come close.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Before I see him, friend, I know
  2. base[FNote_1416] empty evening [will be] there,
  3. when the sharp-nailed bats with soft wings, curved
  4. like the undersides of the lily-leaves in the long water,
  5. go, approaching the slope with [its] broad-leaved jackfruit trees,
  6. for the old tree, where they stay at midday, to be lonely.

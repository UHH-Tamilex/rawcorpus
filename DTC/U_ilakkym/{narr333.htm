  • {NT 333-7} arum poruṭku akaṉṟa kātalar muyakku etirntu
  • {NT 333-8} tiruntu iḻi paṇi tōḷ peṟunar pōlum
  • {NT 333-9} nīṅkuka mātō niṉ avalam ōṅku mici
  • {NT 333-10} uyar pukaḻ nal il oḷ cuvar porunti
  • {NT 333-11} nayavaru kurala palli

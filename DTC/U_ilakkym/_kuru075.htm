{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Did you see [it] or have you heard from those who have seen?
  2. One thing we longed[FNote_305] to be clear: speak
  3. - may you obtain (the town) Pāṭali[FNote_306], full of gold,
  4. settling on the [river] Cōṇai with her white-tusked elephants -
  5. from whose mouth have you heard that [our/her] lover is coming?

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. If my friend had given you a fresh, unripe Neem fruit,
  2. you would have said[FNote_787]: honey-flower-sweets! Now,
  3. even if she gave you cool [draughts], in the Tai month,
  4. of clear water from a dewy pool of Pāri's Paṟampu mountain,
  5. you would say: hot[FNote_788], brackish!
  6. O sir, so that's how [her] share in [your] love is[FNote_789].

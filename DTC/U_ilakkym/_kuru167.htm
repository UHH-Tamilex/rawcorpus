{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The face of [her with] bright forehead was delighted a little(?)[FNote_669],
  2. when, on eating[FNote_670] the sweet-sour dish(?) she herself had stirred
  3. [and] cooked, the husband said 'it is delicious',
  4. while the spicy smoke spread(?) into [her] water-lily eyes [lined with] collyrium
  5. [and she] wore an unwashed garment, touched[FNote_671]
  6. by fingers tender as Malabar lilies, which had mixed drying curd.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land, where the red-faced female monkey becomes full
  • of the sweet water from the waterfall that came from the pretty stems
  • of the plantain tree with rich stems that touch the clustered [flower-]mouths
  • of the Kāntaḷ, as the hand that guides the mouth to the breast
  • of a flower-eyed girl who has given birth to a son,
  • if [their] friends are in front [of them(?) and] give it [to them],
  • courteous people will even absorb poison, abundantly(?).[FNote_1006]
  • Even if [your] heart has no pleasure
  • in sleeping by the shoulder of my friend with pretty few hair,
  • turn to(?) it for my sake[FNote_1007] [and] have pity.
  • Apart from you she has nothing else.

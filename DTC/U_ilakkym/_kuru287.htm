{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, oh friend, [my] lover,
  2. will he give [me] up, even now that he has seen
  3. the time when a big bustle is rising in the sky,
  4. facing[FNote_1182] the very many hills,
  5. after [clouds][FNote_1183] have united, enduring without learning to climb the sky(?),
  6. with water, like women in heavy pregnancy
  7. demanding fresh, sour [things], unable to step aside[FNote_1184],
  8. wearied after having borne the fullness for twelve months[FNote_1185]?

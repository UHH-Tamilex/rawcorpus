{{Readable Translation}}

  • Midnight passes by for a long time and desire is
  • increasing without granting the eyes to close. The thundering waves[FNote_1043]
  • of the clear sea are playing in the expanse, gently
  • as the beat of a drum, as those having an old wound
  • - even while those are thus afflicting [me], the daylight won't appear,
  • as though it had left, having crossed the night. While gossip-mouthed
  • women from the neighbourhood sing [my] pallor,
  • it has indeed become thus, friend:
  • the limit of the friendship enjoyed without examination
  • with the man from the coast of dewy waters buzzing with singing,
  • trusting [his] humble words that touched [me] movingly(?),
  • coming [and] wasting the small houses full of lines in the high sand.

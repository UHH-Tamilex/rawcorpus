{{Readable Translation}}

  1. At a time, that it set out for the lover when desire increased
  2. [and] was with us, when we lamented him,
  3. without [however] going [over] to the happenings of one party(?)[FNote_1366],
  4. my heart he has been with[FNote_1367], is troubled, friend,
  5. like the flood moving [just] to move [again][FNote_1368]
  6. [and] becoming weak on the side where the backwaters stir
  7. with blossom-covered mangroves standing[FNote_1369] in the depth.

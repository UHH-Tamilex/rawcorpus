{{Readable Translation}}

  • I had one dughter, verily. And she
  • has gone yesterday to the difficult desert of big mountins
  • with a young man with sharp spear, very valorous in fight.
  • Now,
  • endure your affliction, you may say. How is that, of all things,
  • possible to us who are thus, o you who possess knowledge?
  • When I remember [my] inside burns
  • - seeing the sapphire-bearing Nocci tree and the verandah
  • where my decorated little dughter played,
  • as if the pupil, living sapphire of [my] collyrium eye, had learned to walk.

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 49-1} yām um cēṟukam nīyir um vammiṉ
  • {PATI 49-2} tuyalum kōti tuḷaṅku iyal viṟaliyar
  • {PATI 49-3} koḷi val vāḻkki num kiḷi iṉitu uṇīiyar
  • {PATI 49-4} kaḷiṟu parantu iyala kaṭu mā tāṅka
  • {PATI 49-5} oḷiṟu koṭi nuṭaṅka tēr tirintu koṭpa
  • {PATI 49-6} eḵku turantu eḻutarum ki kavar kaṭum tār
  • {PATI 49-7} vel pōr vēntar um vēḷir um oṉṟumoḻintu
  • {PATI 49-8} moy vaḷam cerukki mocintu varu mōkūr
  • {PATI 49-9} valam paṭu kuḻūu nili atira maṇṭi
  • {PATI 49-10} neyttōr toṭṭa ce ki maṟavar
  • {PATI 49-11} niṟam paṭu kuruti nilam paṭarntu ōṭi
  • {PATI 49-12} maḻi nāḷ puṉaliṉ aval parantu oḻuka
  • {PATI 49-13} paṭu piṇam piṟaṅka pāḻ pala ceytu
  • {PATI 49-14} paṭu kaṇ muracam naṭuvaṇ cilippa
  • {PATI 49-15} vaḷaṉ aṟa nikaḻntu vāḻunar palar paṭa
  • {PATI 49-16} karum ciṉi viṟal vēmpu aṟutta
  • {PATI 49-17} perum ciṉa kuṭṭuvaṉ kaṇṭaṉam varaṟku ē

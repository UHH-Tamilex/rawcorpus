{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Come[FNote_1117], friend, we come to see[FNote_1118],
  2. climbing the stony height, crawled over by jasmine.
  3. [Is that] the bells worn by the good cows eating grass
  4. in a group with the bull coming to join[FNote_1119] in the night[-time][FNote_1120] village?
  5. Is that the bells of the chariot sounding up there,
  6. which comes on wild ways[FNote_1121] across the wet sand
  7. while the young man[FNote_1122] with strong bow protects the wings
  8. with a satisfied(?) mind that completed the work to be done?

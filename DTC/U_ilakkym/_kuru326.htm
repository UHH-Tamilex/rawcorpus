{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The friendship, friend, bound in the little house
  2. made in the seashore grove by women playing in the sea,
  3. with big bamboo shoulders[FNote_1325] [and] wound garlands,
  4. if the man from the ghat gives [it] up for one day[FNote_1326]
  5. is an unsweetness[FNote_1327] coming for many days.

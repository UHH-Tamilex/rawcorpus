{{Readable Translation}}

  • The forest is silent(?)[FNote_491], and in the sky
  • the many-voiced clouds cannot sleep,
  • having spread collyrium darkness as if lowered on the mountin.
  • Can you sleep, friend,
  • without hearing the fearful roaring sound
  • of the vast-mouthed male of the tiger in hot anger
  • that had brought on the strength(?)[FNote_492] of the elephant-bull in a cloud-covered thicket?
  • If he won't come today, so that as fire is killed by water
  • [my] stined(?)[FNote_493] heart, struck by the great grief
  • of a woman in the pure house is appeased, it [is] good.
  • My unchecked heart flows along the ground[FNote_494]
  • whenever I remember [his] difficlut path on the slopy transverse mountin.

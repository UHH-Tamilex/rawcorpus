Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 31-1} kuṉṟu talaimaṇantu kuḻūu kaṭal uṭutta
  • {PATI 31-2} maṇ keḻu ñālattu māntar orāṅku
  • {PATI 31-3} kai cumantu alaṟum pūcal mātirattu
  • {PATI 31-4} nāl vēṟu naṉam talai oruṅku eḻuntu olippa
  • {PATI 31-5} teḷ uyar vaṭi maṇi eṟiyunar kalleṉa
  • {PATI 31-6} uṇṇā paiññilam paṉi tuṟai maṇṇi
  • {PATI 31-7} vaṇṭu ūtu poli tār tiru ñemar akalattu
  • {PATI 31-8} kaṇ poru tikiri kamaḻ kural tuḻāay
  • {PATI 31-9} alaṅkal celvaṉ cē aṭi paravi
  • {PATI 31-10} neñcu mali uvakaiyar tuñcu pati peyara
  • {PATI 31-11} maṇi niṟa mai iruḷ akala nilā viripu
  • {PATI 31-12} kōṭu kūṭu matiyam iyaluṟṟāṅku
  • {PATI 31-13} tuḷaṅku kuṭi viḻu tiṇai tirutti muracu koṇṭu
  • {PATI 31-14} āṇ kaṭaṉ iṟutta niṉ pūṇ kiḷar viyal mārpu
  • {PATI 31-15} karuvi vāṉam taṇ taḷi talaiiya
  • {PATI 31-16} vaṭa teṟku vilaṅki vilaku talaittu eḻiliya
  • {PATI 31-17} paṉi vār viṇṭu viṟal varai aṟṟu ē
  • {PATI 31-18} kaṭavuḷ añci vāṉattu iḻaitta
  • {PATI 31-19} tūṅku eyil katavam kāval koṇṭa
  • {PATI 31-20} eḻūu nivantaṉṉa parēr eṟuḻ muḻavu tōḷ
  • {PATI 31-21} veḷ tirai munnīr vaḷaiiya ulakattu
  • {PATI 31-22} vaṇ pukaḻ niṟutta vakai cāl celvattu
  • {PATI 31-23} vaṇṭaṉ aṉaiyai maṉ nī ē vaṇṭu paṭa
  • {PATI 31-24} olinta kūntal aṟam cāl kaṟpiṉ
  • {PATI 31-25} kuḻaikku viḷakku ākiya oḷ nutal poṉṉiṉ
  • {PATI 31-26} iḻaikku viḷakku ākiya a vāṅku unti
  • {PATI 31-27} vicumpu vaḻaṅku makaḷir uḷ um ciṟanta
  • {PATI 31-28} cemmīṉ aṉaiyaḷ niṉ tol nakar celvi
  • {PATI 31-29} nilaṉ atirpu iraṅkala āki valaṉ ērpu
  • {PATI 31-30} viyaṉ paṇai muḻaṅkum vēl mūcu aḻuvattu
  • {PATI 31-31} aṭaṅkiya puṭaiyal polam kaḻal nōṉ tāḷ
  • {PATI 31-32} oṭuṅkā tevvar ūkku aṟa kaṭaii
  • {PATI 31-33} puṟakkoṭai eṟiyār niṉ maṟa paṭai koḷḷunar
  • {PATI 31-34} nakaivarkku araṇam āki pakaivarkku
  • {PATI 31-35} cūr nikaḻntaṟṟu niṉ tāṉai
  • {PATI 31-36} pōr miku kurucil nī māṇṭaṉai pala ē

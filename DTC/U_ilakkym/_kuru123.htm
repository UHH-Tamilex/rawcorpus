{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Such that the grove of black-branched Puṉṉai trees grow lonely[FNote_497] -
  2. [on] one wing white sand like heaped moonlight,
  3. [on the other] cool, moist, dense shadow like solid darkness -
  4. even now he doesn't come. [There] comes
  5. the boat of my people, hunting for many fish.

  • {PU 10-5} vantu aṭi porunti munti niṟpiṉ

  • {PU 10-8} varunarkku variyā vaci il vāḻkki

  • {PU 10-10} malittal pōkiya cili tār mārpa
  • {PU 10-11} ceytu iraṅkā viṉi cēṇ viḷaṅkum pukaḻ
  • {PU 10-12} neytal am kāṉal neṭiyōy
  • {PU 10-13} eyta vantaṉam yām ēttukam pala ē

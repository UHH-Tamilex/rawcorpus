  9. kuṉṟa vippiṉ kāṉañ

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If you have considered separating
  • from the good one with broad soft bamboo shoulders
  • [and] hanging dark tresses that smell coolly,
  • you have achieved something difficult, may you live, heart,
  • - you who have the strength to leave for the distance, going
  • through the forest of the hill region, where the bent-footed Ōmi tree, destroyed by the big elephant bull in order to remove
  • the hunger weakening the inexperienced elephant cow with soft head
  • on the waterless long way left by the waterfalls from the red mountin,
  • becomes shade to abide in for those going through the difficult desert.

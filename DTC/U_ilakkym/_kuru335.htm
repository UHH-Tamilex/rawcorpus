{{Readable Translation}}

  1. It is situated in midst the mountains,
  2. where, after fine-jewelled women of forearms with bangles in row
  3. have spread red millet near the wide rocks of dark stone,
  4. the green-eyed she-monkey with [her] young one seizes [the opportunity],
  5. descended from the branch, as she observed the occasion of negligence, [namely] jumping into the mountain pool [on the part of the women],[FNote_1352]
  6. - the village, where the big-shouldered creeper woman was,
  7. the younger sister of the men from the forest with strong bows [and] long arrows.

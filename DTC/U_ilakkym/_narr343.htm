{{Readable Translation}}

  • Are there not, live, friend,
  • sorrowful evenings, come with [all their] weapons,
  • when the crows with greedy(?)[FNote_982] fingers that had eaten from the offering shed
  • at the Banyan tree for the god placed in the long aerial roots
  • touching the back of the cows by the street in the fine(?) dung[FNote_983]ots
  • Banyan tree for the god placed in the long arial its habits.lose-set toes of the bird, but to me it seems more plausible
  • in the small village with pretty homes [he] traversed without joy,
  • on the small path of stones [and] fine sand spread with jasmine,
  • are close to [their] relations in the sorrowful twilight,
  • - in the land [our] lover has gone to, remaining separate,
  • wanting union[FNote_984] with difficult wealth, abandoning us.

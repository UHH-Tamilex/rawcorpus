{{Readable Translation}}

  1. So[FNote_1434] [much] sobbing that tight jewels loosen.
  2. Don't be confused, [you] with [tear-]shedding eyes[FNote_1435]:
  3. see there, the season, of which [he] said 'we will come',
  4. so that your affliction leaves into the distance, grasping for the wall,
  5. after having placed special signs(?) [on it](?)[FNote_1436],
  6. [see] the tender buds[FNote_1437] of the jasmine, as if speaking,
  7. in the chaplets of the cowherds with many cows,
  8. in the evening abundant in dew, when those who are alone, weep.

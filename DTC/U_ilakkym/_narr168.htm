{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land of good mountins,
  • where the strong young one of the red-headed female monkey licks
  • the rest of the sweet honey that has been eaten by the youths
  • of the forest people from a stone hole, oozed out from the bees(?)[FNote_546]
  • of the large(?)[FNote_547] honeycomb fastened to a big branch
  • of the dark-trunked Vēṅki tree that spreads [flowers] for the bees to eat,
  • - will it be called righteous,
  • your coming here to the small hamlets on the slope,
  • you with a chest fragrant of sandal,
  • [your only] companion [your] lance on the small path suffused in dark
  • at midnight of singular darkness, with dangerous snakes,
  • you not remembering the sweet life of her who remins longing for you?

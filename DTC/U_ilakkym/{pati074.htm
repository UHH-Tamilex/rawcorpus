  • {PATI 74-1} kēḷvi kēṭṭu paṭivam oṭiyātu
  • {PATI 74-2} vēḷvi vēṭṭaṉi uyarntōr uvappa
  • {PATI 74-3} cāy aṟal kaṭukkum tāḻ irum kūntal
  • {PATI 74-4} vēṟupaṭu tiruviṉ niṉ vaḻi vāḻiyar
  • {PATI 74-5} koṭumaṇam paṭṭa viṉi māṇ arum kalam
  • {PATI 74-6} pantar payanta palar pukaḻ muttam
  • {PATI 74-7} vari akam naṇṇi kuṟum poṟi nāṭi
  • {PATI 74-8} teriyunar koṇṭa ciraṟu uṭi pim poṟi
  • {PATI 74-9} kavi maram kaṭukkum kavaliya maruppiṉ
  • {PATI 74-10} puḷḷi irali tōl ūṉ utirttu
  • {PATI 74-11} tītu kaḷintu eñciya tikaḻ viṭu pāṇṭil
  • {PATI 74-12} paruti pōkiya puṭi kiḷi kaṭṭi
  • {PATI 74-13} eḵku uṭi irumpiṉ uḷ amittu vallōṉ
  • {PATI 74-14} cūṭu nili uṟṟu cuṭarviṭu tōṟṟam
  • {PATI 74-15} vicumpu āṭu marapiṉ paruntu ūṟu aḷappa
  • {PATI 74-16} nalampeṟu tiru maṇi kūṭṭum nal tōḷ
  • {PATI 74-17} oṭuṅku īr ōti oḷ nutal karuvil
  • {PATI 74-18} eṇ iyal muṟṟi īr aṟivu purintu
  • {PATI 74-19} cālpu um cemmi um uḷappaṭa piṟa um
  • {PATI 74-20} kāvaṟku aminta aracu tuṟipōkiya
  • {PATI 74-21} vīṟu cāl putalvaṉ peṟṟaṉi ivaṇarkku
  • {PATI 74-22} arum kaṭaṉ iṟutta ceru pukal muṉpa
  • {PATI 74-23} aṉṉavi maruṇṭaṉeṉ allēṉ niṉ vayiṉ
  • {PATI 74-24} muḻutu uṇarntu oḻukkum nari mūtāḷaṉi
  • {PATI 74-25} vaṇmi um māṇpu um vaḷaṉ um eccam um
  • {PATI 74-26} teyvam um yāvatu um tavam uṭiyōrkku eṉa
  • {PATI 74-27} vēṟupaṭu naṉam tali peyara
  • {PATI 74-28} kūṟiṉi peruma niṉ paṭimiyāṉ ē

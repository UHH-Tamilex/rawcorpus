Patiṟṟup pattu

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 74-1} kēḷvi kēṭṭu paṭivam oṭiyātu
  • {PATI 74-2} vēḷvi vēṭṭaṉai uyarntōr uvappa
  • {PATI 74-3} cāy aṟal kaṭukkum tāḻ irum kūntal
  • {PATI 74-4} vēṟupaṭu tiruviṉ niṉ vaḻi vāḻiyar
  • {PATI 74-5} koṭumaṇam paṭṭa viṉai māṇ arum kalam
  • {PATI 74-6} pantar payanta palar pukaḻ muttam
  • {PATI 74-7} varai akam naṇṇi kuṟum poṟai nāṭi
  • {PATI 74-8} teriyunar koṇṭa ciraṟu uṭai paim poṟi
  • {PATI 74-9} kavai maram kaṭukkum kavalaiya maruppiṉ
  • {PATI 74-10} puḷḷi iralai tōl ūṉ utirttu
  • {PATI 74-11} tītu kaḷaintu eñciya tikaḻ viṭu pāṇṭil
  • {PATI 74-12} paruti pōkiya puṭai kiḷai kaṭṭi
  • {PATI 74-13} eḵku uṭai irumpiṉ uḷ amaittu vallōṉ
  • {PATI 74-14} cūṭu nilai uṟṟu cuṭarviṭu tōṟṟam
  • {PATI 74-15} vicumpu āṭu marapiṉ paruntu ūṟu aḷappa
  • {PATI 74-16} nalampeṟu tiru maṇi kūṭṭum nal tōḷ
  • {PATI 74-17} oṭuṅku īr ōti oḷ nutal karuvil
  • {PATI 74-18} eṇ iyal muṟṟi īr aṟivu purintu
  • {PATI 74-19} cālpu um cemmai um uḷappaṭa piṟa um
  • {PATI 74-20} kāvaṟku amainta aracu tuṟaipōkiya
  • {PATI 74-21} vīṟu cāl putalvaṉ peṟṟaṉai ivaṇarkku
  • {PATI 74-22} arum kaṭaṉ iṟutta ceru pukal muṉpa
  • {PATI 74-23} aṉṉavai maruṇṭaṉeṉ allēṉ niṉ vayiṉ
  • {PATI 74-24} muḻutu uṇarntu oḻukkum narai mūtāḷaṉai
  • {PATI 74-25} vaṇmai um māṇpu um vaḷaṉ um eccam um
  • {PATI 74-26} teyvam um yāvatu um tavam uṭaiyōrkku eṉa
  • {PATI 74-27} vēṟupaṭu naṉam talai peyara
  • {PATI 74-28} kūṟiṉai peruma niṉ paṭimaiyāṉ ē

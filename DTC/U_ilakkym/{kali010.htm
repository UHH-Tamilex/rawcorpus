Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 10-1} vaṟiyavaṉ iḷamai pōl vāṭiya ciṉaiya āy
  • {KALI 10-2} ciṟiyavaṉ celvam pōl cērntārkku niḻal iṉṟi
  • {KALI 10-3} yār kaṇ um ikantu ceytu icai keṭṭāṉ iṟuti pōl
  • {KALI 10-4} vēroṭu maram vempa viri katir teṟutaliṉ
  • {KALI 10-5} alavuṟṟu kuṭi kūva āṟu iṉṟi poruḷ veḵki
  • {KALI 10-6} kolai añcā viṉaivarāl kōl kōṭiyavaṉ niḻal
  • {KALI 10-7} ulaku pōl ulaṟiya uyar mara ve curam
  • {KALI 10-8} iṭai koṇṭu poruḷ vayiṉ iṟatti nī eṉa kēṭpiṉ
  • {KALI 10-9} uṭaipu neñcu uka āṅku ē oḷi ōṭaṟpāḷ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 10-10} paṭai amai cēkkai uḷ pāyaliṉ aṟiyāy nī
  • {KALI 10-11} puṭai peyarvāy āyiṉum pulampu koṇṭu iṉaipavaḷ
  • {KALI 10-12} muṉivu iṉṟi muyal poruṭku iṟatti nī eṉa kēṭpiṉ
  • {KALI 10-13} paṉiya kaṇ paṭal ollā paṭar kūrkiṟpāḷ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 10-14} naṉi koṇṭa cāyalāḷ nayantu nī nakai āka
  • {KALI 10-15} tuṉi ceytu nīṭiṉum tuṟappu añci kaluḻpavaḷ
  • {KALI 10-16} poruḷ nōkki pirintu nī pōkuti eṉa kēṭpiṉ
  • {KALI 10-17} maruḷ nōkkam maṭintu āṅku ē mayal kūrkiṟpāḷ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 10-18} iruḷ nōkkam iṭaiyiṉṟi īrattiṉ iyaṉṟa niṉ
  • {KALI 10-19} aruḷ nōkkam aḻiyiṉum avalam koṇṭu aḻipavaḷ
  • {KALI 10-20} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 10-21} viṉai veḵki nī celiṉ viṭum ivaḷ uyir eṉa
  • {KALI 10-22} puṉai iḻāy niṉ nilai yāṉ kūṟa paiyeṉa
  • {KALI 10-23} nilavu vēl neṭuntakai nīḷ iṭai
  • {KALI 10-24} celavu oḻintaṉaṉ āl ceṟika niṉ vaḷai ē

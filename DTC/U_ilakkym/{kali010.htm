Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 10-1} vaṟiyavaṉ iḷami pōl vāṭiya ciṉiya āy
  • {KALI 10-2} ciṟiyavaṉ celvam pōl cērntārkku niḻal iṉṟi
  • {KALI 10-3} yār kaṇ um ikantu ceytu ici keṭṭāṉ iṟuti pōl
  • {KALI 10-4} vēroṭu maram vempa viri katir teṟutaliṉ
  • {KALI 10-5} alavuṟṟu kuṭi kūva āṟu iṉṟi poruḷ veḵki
  • {KALI 10-6} koli añcā viṉivarāl kōl kōṭiyavaṉ niḻal
  • {KALI 10-7} ulaku pōl ulaṟiya uyar mara ve curam
  • {KALI 10-8} iṭi koṇṭu poruḷ vayiṉ iṟatti nī eṉa kēṭpiṉ
  • {KALI 10-9} uṭipu neñcu uka āṅku ē oḷi ōṭaṟpāḷ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 10-10} paṭi ami cēkki uḷ pāyaliṉ aṟiyāy nī
  • {KALI 10-11} puṭi peyarvāy āyiṉum pulampu koṇṭu iṉipavaḷ
  • {KALI 10-12} muṉivu iṉṟi muyal poruṭku iṟatti nī eṉa kēṭpiṉ
  • {KALI 10-13} paṉiya kaṇ paṭal ollā paṭar kūrkiṟpāḷ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 10-14} naṉi koṇṭa cāyalāḷ nayantu nī naki āka
  • {KALI 10-15} tuṉi ceytu nīṭiṉum tuṟappu añci kaluḻpavaḷ
  • {KALI 10-16} poruḷ nōkki pirintu nī pōkuti eṉa kēṭpiṉ
  • {KALI 10-17} maruḷ nōkkam maṭintu āṅku ē mayal kūrkiṟpāḷ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 10-18} iruḷ nōkkam iṭiyiṉṟi īrattiṉ iyaṉṟa niṉ
  • {KALI 10-19} aruḷ nōkkam aḻiyiṉum avalam koṇṭu aḻipavaḷ
  • {KALI 10-20} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 10-21} viṉi veḵki nī celiṉ viṭum ivaḷ uyir eṉa
  • {KALI 10-22} puṉi iḻāy niṉ nili yāṉ kūṟa piyeṉa
  • {KALI 10-23} nilavu vēl neṭuntaki nīḷ iṭi
  • {KALI 10-24} celavu oḻintaṉaṉ āl ceṟika niṉ vaḷi ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Out of time(?)[FNote_1518] indeed rins the rin,
  2. so that the jasmine in fresh creepers, exposed to the cool drops,
  3. is dense [and] smells of honey, together with the red jasmine full of flowers,
  4. on(?)[FNote_1519] the fragrant shrubs, where the bud heads have opened,
  5. if it is not out of time [and] this [is] the riny season
  6. won't he come, our lover?

{{Metrical text}}

  1. nayaṉu naṇpu nāṇunaṉ kuṭaimaiyum
  2. payaṉum paṇpum pāṭaṟin toḻukalu
  3. nummiṉu maṟikuveṉ maṉṉē kammeṉa
  4. vetirtta titti yēriḷa vaṉamulai
  5. vitirttuviṭ ṭaṉṉa vannuṇ cuṇaṅki ((5))
  6. ṉaimpāl vakutta kūntaṟ cempoṟ
  7. ṟirunutaṟ polinta tēmpā yōti
  8. mutunī rilañcip pūtta kuvaḷai
  9. yetirmalarp piṇaiya laṉṉaviva
  10. ḷarimatar maḻaikkaṇ kāṇā vūṅkē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even more than you, verily, I knew, nicely(?)[FNote_513],
  • of longing and affection and shame and possession of the good
  • and yield and quality and behaving with knowledge of propriety[FNote_514]
  • - before having seen her red-streaked luxuriant rain eyes,
  • like opposed blossoms in a garland
  • of blue water-lilies come to bloom in a pond of ancient[FNote_515] water,
  • [her] hair spreading fragrance[FNote_516] in which [her] forehead shines brilliant
  • [as] red gold, [her] tresses divided into five parts,
  • pretty fine beauty[FNote_517] spots as if scattered at random(?)[FNote_518],
  • [her] rising young beautiful breast with resistant beauty spots.

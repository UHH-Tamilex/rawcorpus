{{Readable Translation}}

  • She encircled [me], live friend, to slap [me],
  • mother who has the strength of a little rod,
  • not spreading[FNote_476] the calumny of the women in the street,
  • putting [her] index finger to the tip of [her] nose(?),[FNote_477]
  • looking from the corner of [her] eye[FNote_478] at the few and the many(?).
  • I am indeed[FNote_479] immersed in going
  • with the man from the sea with [his] moving chariot, that comes
  • at midnight, inciting the long chariot that has yoked quick horses
  • with brilliant(?) manes fragrant of flowers that gripped the new blossoms in the seashore grove.
  • May this noisy village stay behind, burdened with gossip.

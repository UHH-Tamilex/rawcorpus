{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Look, friend, he who has separated because of wealth,
  2. even in the evening he has not come, when the bees gather
  3. in the woodland of fragrant blossoms, the little buds opening
  4. [their] tender fetters into fresh blooms, like the laugh of the wild cat,
  5. on the jasmine, flowering beside[FNote_890] the stubble
  6. - an image[FNote_891] of missing heads[FNote_892], grazed by the Iralai -
  7. of the millet on the new field, swollen in the old rain[FNote_893].

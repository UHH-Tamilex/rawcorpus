{{Readable Translation}}

  1. I don't feel pity, friend
  2. for the man from the coast of the big water with flowers in bloom
  3. where the many-winged swarm [of bees], having yearned for seasonal honey,[FNote_702]
  4. gather on the big twigs of the Puṉṉi tree, wet
  5. on the shore where firm sands have settled, dashed by the strong waves.
  6. May gossip subside as if silenced,
  7. [this] talking [to let] more [and] more people know,
  8. saying 'what [is this] here?' - What [is] it [after all]?[FNote_703]

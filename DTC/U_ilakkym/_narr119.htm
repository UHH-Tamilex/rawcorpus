{{Metrical text}}

  1. tiṉaiyuṇ kēḻa liriyap puṉavaṉ
  2. ciṟupoṟi māṭṭiya peruṅkal laṭāa
  3. roṇkēḻ vayappuli paṭūu nāṭa
  4. ṉāṟtara vantaṉa ṉāyiṉum paṭappai
  5. yiṉmucup peruṅkalai naṉmēya lārum ((5))
  6. paṉmalark kāṉyāṟ ṟumpark karuṅkalai
  7. kaṭumpāṭṭu varuṭaiyoṭu tāvaṉa vukaḷum
  8. peruvarai nīḻal varukuvaṉ kuḷaviyoṭu
  9. kūviḷan tatainta kaṇṇiyaṉ yāvatu
  10. muyaṅkal peṟukuva ṉallaṉ ((10))
  11. pulavi koḷīiyartaṉ malaiyiṉum peritē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from a land, where the strong tiger of bright lustre is caught,
  • without being killed(?)[FNote_383], by the big stone attached to a small ?(?)[FNote_384]
  • by the field owner, so that the boar that eats the millet retreats,
  • even if he came to get full satisfaction(?),[FNote_385]
  • he who comes into the shade of the big mountain,
  • where goats [and their] relations leaping with the mountain sheep jump about
  • with the black male monkey[FNote_386] beyond the many-flowered forest river,
  • where the big male of the pleasant(?)[FNote_387] langur becomes full with good grazing
  • in the garden, he with a chaplet full of Bael plants with wild jasmine,
  • he will not obtain any embrace at all.
  • May he get vexation bigger than his mountain.

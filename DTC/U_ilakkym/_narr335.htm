{{Readable Translation}}

  • The moon is rising in the glittering sky and the ocean
  • with [its] foaming waves of young water does not stop singing.
  • Apart from the Aṉṟil too calling in my back,
  • suffering [its] miseries(?) high on the collyrium-dark palmyra palm
  • with an undestructable fragrance that feeds [me], the wind spreading [it],
  • while the closed buds, as cups from which rice is poured forth,
  • of the Tāḻai tree with thorny leaves in the many-flowered seashore grove open,
  • in the torrent much when the flood too is moving in, overwhelming in sound, the good lute sweet with desire, skillfully(?) seized by fingers,
  • does not stand [still], unescapable(?)[FNote_965] at midnight.
  • Desire [is] great. Ah, no one there to remove [it].

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As choice bangles become loose and the body weighs forgetfulness[FNote_1285],
  2. if mother gets to know of [this] painful, great suffering,
  3. am I [still], oh friend?
  4. The words of the man from the ghat,
  5. where the broad waves that come in high snatch away
  6. the distressed hasty motion of crabs selected
  7. to be chased together[FNote_1286] by women in play,
  8. on the shore where mixed sands settle, incessantly beaten by the powerful sea?
  9. {--!}They have become different.

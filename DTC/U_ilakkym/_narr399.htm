{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from a land of big mountins,
  • where the inexperienced she elephant, who gave birth in the brightly glittering light of the brilliant sapphires that are many, come up on the field border
  • dug up by the boar on the plantin mountin side,
  • where the Malabar lily in fragrant flowers resembling blood
  • blossoms for the bees with wings [and] adorned with stripes to eat
  • on the side of the big mouuntin, where the waterfall roars,
  • resides with [her] calf, while the bull stands guard,
  • has begun to long [for you]. When he comes,[FNote_1083]
  • will [his] saying "she possesses greatness"
  • give [back], friend, your brilliant forehead's beauty?

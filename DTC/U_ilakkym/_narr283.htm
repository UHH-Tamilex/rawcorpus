{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a ghat, where women with bright foreheads decorate
  • small houses with broad lines of fragrantly smelling blue water-lilies
  • that are like eyes, which they have plucked in the high backwaters,
  • you are no good,
  • is it suitable to be like this,
  • so that the old beauty which was sought out by able ones gets lost
  • in our [woman] of few soft hair,
  • - against those who longed a word from you who were worthy of truthfullness
  • more than the glow [of the sun] that has come to shine for having joy,
  • appearing for many to worship high on the height of the high-waved three-water.

  • {PATI 19-21} nelliṉ ceṟuvil neytal pūppa
  • {PATI 19-22} arinar koy vāḷ maṭaṅka aṟainar
  • {PATI 19-23} tīm piḻi entiram pattal varunta
  • {PATI 19-24} iṉṟu ō aṉṟu ō toṉṟu ōr kālai

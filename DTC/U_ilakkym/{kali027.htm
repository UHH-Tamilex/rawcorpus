Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 27-1} ītalil kuṟi kāṭṭātu aṟaṉ aṟintu oḻukiya
  • {KALI 27-2} tītu ilāṉ celvam pōl tīm kari maram nanta
  • {KALI 27-3} pētuṟu maṭa moḻi piṇi eḻil māṉ nōkkiṉ
  • {KALI 27-4} mātarār muṟuval pōl maṇa muval muki ūḻppa
  • {KALI 27-5} kātalar puṇarntavar katuppu pōl kaḻalkupu
  • {KALI 27-6} tātoṭu um taḷiroṭu um taṇ aṟal takipeṟa
  • {KALI 27-7} pētiyōṉ viṉi vāṅka pīṭu ilā aracaṉ nāṭṭu
  • {KALI 27-8} ētilāṉ paṭi pōla iṟuttantatu iḷavēṉil
  • {KALI 27-9} nilam pūtta mara mici nimirpu ālum kuyil eḷḷa
  • {KALI 27-10} nalam pūtta niṟam cāya nammi ō maṟantikka
  • {KALI 27-11} kalam pūtta aṇiyavar kāriki makiḻ ceyya
  • {KALI 27-12} pulam pūttu pukaḻpu āṉā kūṭal um uḷḷār kol
  • {KALI 27-13} kal mici mayil āla kaṟaṅki ūr alar tūṟṟa
  • {KALI 27-14} tol nalam naṉi cāya nammi ō maṟantikka
  • {KALI 27-15} oṉṉātār kaṭantu aṭūum uravu nīr mā koṉṟa
  • {KALI 27-16} veṉ vēlāṉ kuṉṟiṉ mēl viḷiyāṭṭu um virumpār kol
  • {KALI 27-17} mi eḻil malar uṇ kaṇ maru ūṭṭi makiḻ koḷḷa
  • {KALI 27-18} poyyiṉāl purivuṇṭa nammi ō maṟantikka
  • {KALI 27-19} tiiya makaḷir tam āyamōṭu amarntu āṭum
  • {KALI 27-20} viyi vār uyar ekkar nukarcci um uḷḷār kol
  • {KALI 27-21} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 27-22} nōy mali neñcamōṭu iṉiyal tōḻi
  • {KALI 27-23} nām illā pulampu āyiṉ naṭukkam cey poḻutu āyiṉ
  • {KALI 27-24} kāmavēḷ viḻavu āyiṉ kalaṅkuvaḷ peritu eṉa
  • {KALI 27-25} ēmuṟu kaṭum tiṇ tēr kaṭavi
  • {KALI 27-26} nām amar kātalar tuṇi tantār virintu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. On a day with cool dew, when the decreasing rin sets out
  2. for the southern fields before the wind from the northern fields[FNote_1287],
  3. overmuch, will he really[FNote_1288] let go of us once and for all[FNote_1289],
  4. the man from a land, where there drinks from the fresh pool on the high mountin
  5. the black-and-white bull of the desirous-youthful wild cow,
  6. after feeding on sweet-sour Nelli fruit [and] nearby
  7. breathing hot for big blossoms spread with honey to tremble[FNote_1290]?

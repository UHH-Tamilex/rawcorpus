  9. marumpeṟaṟ perumpayaṅ koḷḷātu

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O the foremost one of the time the world was created,
  • has he forgotten, he who was excellent,
  • - he who created wealth [along] with the custom of remining separate,
  • without [however] taking big yield difficult to obtin,
  • when it gently thunders near(?) the fragrant hir that slackens
  • in five parts resembling sapphire taken as an adorning colour,
  • gathering together the fragrance as if a vessel were upturned
  • in which were inclosed, all thrown together, with the fragrant Mōrōṭam
  • the furry dark flowers of the large-trunked trumpet flower tree
  • [and] wild jasmine in the face of summer not yet there?

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What can be sid, friend?
  • Does he not have [any] consideration for us,
  • coming along [this] bad difficult way at midnight in deep darkness
  • that spread [and] sealed the eyes, while the clouds with a shaking voice
  • fulfill there age-old duty, flashes[FNote_722] splitting?
  • - the master of the mountin of waterfalls descending from high,
  • where the elephant bull with large trunk, resembling the small back of a forest man, sounds inside the clefts (aḻuntu paṭa?),
  • being frightened by the arrows thrown by the frenzied(?) bow.

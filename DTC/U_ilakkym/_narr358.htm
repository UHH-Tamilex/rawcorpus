{{Readable Translation}}

  • Up, friend, let us do reverence, strewing
  • high offerings to the gods in groups, [and] come [back],
  • saying "may you live, beware of plageuing him
  • in any way, even if he has sworn on you,
  • ashamed at seeing me thus:
  • while [my] big shoulders are emaciated, while those lines fade,
  • while big pallor creeps up my soft little bosom,
  • - him who is strong [enough] to stay there, separated,
  • so that my choice beauty difficult to obtin is lost,
  • like Maruṅki of Vaḻuti with green ornaments,
  • where the short white crow obtins as dily prey
  • something bent with soft head in the big red prawn.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Did she herself know or didn't she,
  2. the flower-eyed innocence who chases parakeets from the field,
  3. plucking flowers from mountain pools, knitting garlands?
  4. Having breathed like an elephant at rest, at midnight,
  5. my mind [is] close to her even afterwards.

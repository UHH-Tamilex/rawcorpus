{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When [we] danced together in the sea [and] dwelled in the seashore grove
  2. and immersed [ourselves] in embracing [and] adorning, with companions [wearing] chaplets,
  3. [and] he came quickly(?) like a stranger
  4. [and] embraced [me and] went[FNote_1207], of course there was gossip.
  5. He, who doesn't go from [my] side any more than the foliage
  6. of green sprouts removed from swaying branches [and] the perfect jewels
  7. [go from my] broad hips that are spread freshly(?)[FNote_1208] with beauty spots,
  8. has himself instilled into my mother watchful[FNote_1209] vigilance.

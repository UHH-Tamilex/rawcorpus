  9. maṭupā laṉṉaveṉ pacalai meyyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Was it known[FNote_567]
  • to mother herself, [our connection?][FNote_568] with the man from the ghat
  • where the Puṉṉai is high, with fragrant blossoms, where the fishermen,
  • with [their] bent boats that disturbed the long sea,
  • heap in the soft(?)[FNote_569] sand [their] catch of rich fish [and] sleep
  • by small-flamed lamps that are lighted in shells by pouring fish oil?
  • Not to her. At midday,
  • trusting in the belittling words[FNote_570] of the women in the streets,
  • she looked, as if with glowing swords,
  • at my pallorous body, resembling boiled milk.

  5. pulipārt tuṟiyum pullatarc ciṟuneṟi 5

  7. vaṉintēn tiḷamuli nōva kolleṉa

{{Readable Translation}}

  • How is she able [to go], she,
  • on the little grassy [and] sandy path where the doe, having [just] given birth,
  • feet drooping, stays after seeing the tiger, that has destroyed
  • wanderers in confused evening becuse [its] hunger is waxing,
  • without obtining a shady place on the long way of shining fire,
  • with Malabar lilies dead from permanent summer?
  • - [she who] is fit for great folly, of collyrium[-coloured] moist hir,
  • the graceful [one](?)[FNote_124], who breathes hot [sighs]
  • while her large, beautiful rin eyes dim so as to become wet
  • about the embrace of [my] hand becoming loose in the thought
  • 'do [her] emminent young breasts ache, after I had embraced them?'

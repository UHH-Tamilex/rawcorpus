  2. ḷaḻalavir nīḷiṭai niḻaliṭam peṟāa
  3. tīṉṟukāṉ maṭinta piṇavuppaci kūrnteṉa
  4. māṉṟa mālai vaḻaṅkunarc cekīiya
  5. pulipārt tuṟaiyum pullatarc ciṟuneṟi 5
  6. yāṅkuval lunaḷko ṟāṉē yāṉṟaṉ
  7. vaṉaintēn tiḷamulai nōva kolleṉa
  8. niṉaintukain nekiḻnta vaṉaittaṟkut tāṉṟaṉ
  9. pēramar maḻaikka ṇīriya kaluḻa
  10. veyya vuyirkkuñ cāyaṉ ((10))
  11. maiyī rōtip perumaṭat takaiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • How is she able [to go], she,
  • on the little grassy [and] sandy path where the doe, having [just] given birth,
  • feet drooping, stays after seeing the tiger, that has destroyed
  • wanderers in confused evening because [its] hunger is waxing,
  • without obtaining a shady place on the long way of shining fire,
  • with Malabar lilies dead from permanent summer?
  • - [she who] is fit for great folly, of collyrium[-coloured] moist hair,
  • the graceful [one](?)[FNote_124], who breathes hot [sighs]
  • while her large, beautiful rain eyes dim so as to become wet
  • about the embrace of [my] hand becoming loose in the thought
  • 'do [her] emminent young breasts ache, after I had embraced them?'

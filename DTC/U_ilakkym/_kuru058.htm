{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Friends, [you] who admonish, if you considered
  2. that you have a task(?)[FNote_243], [that] would be good.
  3. Like butter shrinking,
  4. guarded with the eyes by the handless dumb one,
  5. on a hot rock heated by the sun,
  6. this pain has spread, hard to endure.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • That dark young woman, she in the kitchen,[FNote_390]
  • being vexed with us, she who wiped off with the top of [her] pretty cloth
  • the perspiration in many minute [drops], impressed on her crescent forehead
  • befittingly(?), she with eyes at strife with the touch of smoke,
  • distributing skillfully(?) the moist flesh on plantin leaves,
  • so that [her] soft finger become red, on which small rings(?)[FNote_391] were tight,
  • from a richly made(?!) box into which [she] put(?) curved earrings,
  • in the good house fit to be seen, where to every pillar is bound
  • a calf with inexperienced git of the broad-horned buffalo cow
  • - as for us[FNote_392],
  • may a festival come. May we see [her] face that has taken up a smile
  • so that [her] thorn teeth appear a little ... (?)[FNote_393].

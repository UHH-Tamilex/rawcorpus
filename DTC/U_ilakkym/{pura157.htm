  • {PU 157-7} kōṭal kaṇṇi kuṟavar perumakaṉ
  • {PU 157-8} āṭu maḻai tavirkkum payam keḻu mīmicai
  • {PU 157-9} el paṭu poḻutiṉ iṉam talai mayaṅki
  • {PU 157-10} kaṭci kāṇā kaṭamāṉ nal ēṟu
  • {PU 157-11} maṭam māṉ nāku piṇai payiriṉ viṭar muḻai
  • {PU 157-12} irum puli pukar pōttu ōrkkum

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 41-1} puṇar puri narampiṉ tīm toṭi paḻuṉiya
  • {PATI 41-2} vaṇar ami nal yāḻ iḷiyar poṟuppa
  • {PATI 41-3} paṇ ami muḻavu um patali um piṟa um
  • {PATI 41-4} kaṇ aṟuttu iyaṟṟiya tūmpoṭu curukki
  • {PATI 41-5} kāvil takitta tuṟi kūṭu kala piyar
  • {PATI 41-6} ki val iḷiyar kaṭavuḷ paḻicca
  • {PATI 41-7} maṟa puli kuḻūu kural cettu vaya kaḷiṟu
  • {PATI 41-8} vari cērpu eḻunta cuṭar vī vēṅki
  • {PATI 41-9} pū uṭi perum ciṉi vāṅki piḷantu taṉ
  • {PATI 41-10} mā irum ceṉṉi aṇipeṟa milicci
  • {PATI 41-11} cēer uṟṟa cel paṭi maṟavar
  • {PATI 41-12} taṇṭu uṭi valattar pōr etirntāṅku
  • {PATI 41-13} vaḻi amal viyaṉ kāṭu cilampa piḷiṟum
  • {PATI 41-14} maḻi peyal māṟiya kaḻi tiraṅku attam
  • {PATI 41-15} oṉṟu iraṇṭu ala pala kaḻintu tiṇ tēr
  • {PATI 41-16} vaci il neṭuntaki kāṇku vanticiṉ ē
  • {PATI 41-17} tāval uyyum ō maṟṟu ē tāvātu
  • {PATI 41-18} vañciṉam muṭitta oṉṟumoḻi maṟavar
  • {PATI 41-19} muracu uṭi perum camattu aracu paṭa kaṭantu
  • {PATI 41-20} vevvar ōccam peruka tevvar
  • {PATI 41-21} miḷaku eṟi ulakkiyiṉ irum tali iṭittu
  • {PATI 41-22} viku ārppu eḻunta mi paṭu parappiṉ
  • {PATI 41-23} eṭuttēṟu ēya kaṭippu uṭi viyaṉ kaṇ
  • {PATI 41-24} valam paṭu cīrtti oruṅku uṭaṉ iyintu
  • {PATI 41-25} kāl uḷi kaṭum picir uṭiya vāl uḷi
  • {PATI 41-26} kaṭum pari puravi ūrnta niṉ
  • {PATI 41-27} paṭum tiri paṉi kaṭal uḻanta tāḷ ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the fertile village,
  • where the dark male with moving(?) nape of the broad-horned bufallo cow
  • springs with a splash(?) into the cool pond of long water,
  • so that the many groups of herons with inexperienced(?) gait retreat,
  • [and] stays in the pleasing shade of the Marutu tree decorated with darkness,
  • with lofty branches, to repell the suffering from the daily task,
  • even if you bring to our house [and] embrace [there]
  • your women with glorious jewels,
  • as for their sincerity(?), in their low minds, it [is] rare.
  • Rarer than that is their[FNote_956] becoming of our propriety,
  • with chastity worthy of kindness,
  • bringing forth children with [the help of?] women with fresh armlets.

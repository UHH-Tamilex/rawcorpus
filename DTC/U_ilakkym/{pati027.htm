  • {PATI 27-7} el vaḷai makaḷir teḷ viḷi icaippiṉ
  • {PATI 27-8} paḻaṉa kāvil pacu mayil ālum
  • {PATI 27-9} poykai vāyil puṉal poru putaviṉ
  • {PATI 27-10} neytal marapiṉ nirai kaḷ ceṟuviṉ
  • {PATI 27-11} val vāy uruḷi katumeṉa maṇṭa
  • {PATI 27-12} aḷḷal paṭṭu tuḷḷupu turappa
  • {PATI 27-13} nal erutu muyalum aḷaṟu pōku viḻumattu
  • {PATI 27-14} cākāṭṭāḷar kampalai allatu

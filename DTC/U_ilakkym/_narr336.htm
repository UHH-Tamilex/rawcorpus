{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the land of long mountains, where woman of the house
  • with decorative dark hair cuts [and] distributes, according to the home,
  • the white-tusked male given by the bow
  • of the forest man, staying[FNote_966] on the difficult forest path of stone [and] fine sand
  • for seizing exceedingly of the round-stemmed millet
  • with the skinny-breasted female of the rough-naped pig,
  • You don't fear the night,
  • when the elephant bull of strong anger looks out for a tiger to attack,
  • I fear [it].
  • Don't come along the small path joining the mountain,
  • where a group bear searching for prey digs out, the rainy season
  • itself being well on(?)[FNote_967], the anthill with wet holes for the snake.

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 44-1} nilam puṭaippaṉṉa ārppoṭu vicumpu tuṭaiyū
  • {PATI 44-2} vāṉ tōy vel koṭi tēr micai nuṭaṅka
  • {PATI 44-3} periya āyiṉum amar kaṭantu peṟṟa
  • {PATI 44-4} ariya eṉṉātu ōmpātu vīci
  • {PATI 44-5} kalam cela curattal allatu kaṉaviṉum
  • {PATI 44-6} kaḷaika eṉa aṟiyā kacaṭu il neñcattu
  • {PATI 44-7} āṭu naṭai aṇṇal niṉ pāṭu makaḷ kāṇiyar
  • {PATI 44-8} kāṇiliyar ō niṉ pukaḻnta yākkai
  • {PATI 44-9} muḻu vali tuñcum nōy tapu nōṉ toṭai
  • {PATI 44-10} nuṇ koṭi uḻiñai vel pōr aṟukai
  • {PATI 44-11} cēṇaṉ āyiṉum kēḷ eṉa moḻintu
  • {PATI 44-12} pulam peyarntu oḷitta kaḷaiyā pūcaṟku
  • {PATI 44-13} araṇkaḷ tāvuṟīi aṇaṅku nikaḻntaṉṉa
  • {PATI 44-14} mōkūr maṉṉaṉ muracam koṇṭu
  • {PATI 44-15} neṭumoḻi paṇittu avaṉ vēmpu mutal taṭintu
  • {PATI 44-16} muracu ceya muracci kaḷiṟu pala pūṭṭi
  • {PATI 44-17} oḻukai uyttōy koḻu il paim tuṇi
  • {PATI 44-18} vai talai maṟanta tuy talai kūkai
  • {PATI 44-19} kavalai kavaṟṟum kurāl am paṟantalai
  • {PATI 44-20} muracu uṭai tāyattu aracu pala ōṭṭi
  • {PATI 44-21} tuḷaṅku nīr viyal akam āṇṭu iṉitu kaḻinta
  • {PATI 44-22} maṉṉar maṟaitta tāḻi
  • {PATI 44-23} vaṉṉi maṉṟattu viḷaṅkiya kāṭu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • While the blue water-lilies close, while the shadow falls eastward,
  • while the stone-joining [sun-]disk reddens [and] the ground becomes lonely
  • the many-flowered seashore-grove rested[FNote_597]
  • and, [horses] yoked, as the [day]time is fading, so that grouped bells sound,
  • for us with desire much in [our] body to stay behind in reverence
  • the moving back of the chariot too is vanishing.
  • What will become ot it, with the village,
  • - of the grove where we played, when [he?] wished for a sweet smile,
  • with the man from the sea with bent ornaments, on which rise flashes,
  • on [his] chest with a garland where bees are humming, filling themselves up
  • so that there will be honey.

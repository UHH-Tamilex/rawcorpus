  • {PU 54-1} em kōṉ irunta kampali mūtūr
  • {PU 54-2} uṭiyōr pōla iṭi iṉṟu kuṟuki
  • {PU 54-3} cemmal nāḷavi aṇṇāntu pukutal
  • {PU 54-4} em aṉa vāḻkki iravalarkku eḷitu ē
  • {PU 54-5} iravalarkku eṇmi allatu puravu etirntu
  • {PU 54-6} vāṉam nāṇa variyātu ceṉṟōrkku
  • {PU 54-7} āṉātu īyum kavi ki vaṇmi
  • {PU 54-8} kaṭu māṉ kōti tuppu etirntu eḻunta
  • {PU 54-9} neṭumoḻi maṉṉar niṉikkum kāli
  • {PU 54-10} pācu ili toṭutta uvali kaṇṇi
  • {PU 54-11} mācu uṇ uṭukki maṭi vāy iṭiyaṉ
  • {PU 54-12} ciṟu tali āyamoṭu kuṟukal cellā
  • {PU 54-13} puli tuñcu viyaṉ pulattu aṟṟu ē
  • {PU 54-14} vali tuñcu taṭam ki avaṉ uṭi nāṭu ē

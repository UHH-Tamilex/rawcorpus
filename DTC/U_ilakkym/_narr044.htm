  9. kuṭakkā yāciṉip paṭappai nīṭiya

  11. ceṉmaḻai yiyakkaṅ kāṇu

{{Readable Translation}}

  • As for you[FNote_178],
  • who have been thinking of [her since] the time she moved to the house,
  • having granted [you] a smile with an unintentional look
  • from her big, beautiful rain-eyes, reddened by water dashing against [them]
  • when she played in the waterfall with [her] incomparable attendants,
  • - she knows [you], heart,
  • the beloved daughter of the man from the land of good mountains,
  • where the hill inhabitants with many, many(?)[FNote_179] relations,
  • [wearing] chaplets of the rich-eared white Malabar lily,
  • see[FNote_180] in the motion of the falling rain
  • the lights of the fireflies in the high branches of many trees,
  • reaching into the garden of bread-fruit trees with pot[-shaped] unripe fruits
  • in the frontyard immersed in night, while the bent stalks
  • of the ripening long-leaved millet stand upright in [their] fields.

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 66-1} vāṅku iru maruppiṉ tīm toṭi paḻuṉiya
  • {PATI 66-2} iṭaṉ uṭi pēriyāḻ pāli paṇṇi
  • {PATI 66-3} paṭarntaṉi cellum mutu vāy iravala
  • {PATI 66-4} iṭi ici muracamoṭu oṉṟumoḻintu oṉṉār
  • {PATI 66-5} vēl uṭi kuḻūu camam tatiya nūṟi
  • {PATI 66-6} koṉṟu puṟampeṟṟa piṇam payil aḻuvattu
  • {PATI 66-7} toṉṟu tiṟi tanta kaḷiṟṟoṭu nelliṉ
  • {PATI 66-8} ampaṇa aḷavi virintu uṟi pōkiya
  • {PATI 66-9} ār patam nalkum eṉpa kaṟuttōr
  • {PATI 66-10} uṟu muraṇ tāṅkiya tār arum takippiṉ
  • {PATI 66-11} nāḷ maḻi kuḻūu cimi kaṭukkum tōṉṟal
  • {PATI 66-12} tōl micittu eḻutarum virintu ilaṅku eḵkiṉ
  • {PATI 66-13} tār purintaṉṉa vāḷ uṭi viḻaviṉ
  • {PATI 66-14} pōr paṭu maḷḷar pōntoṭu toṭutta
  • {PATI 66-15} kaṭavuḷ vāki tuy vī ēyppa
  • {PATI 66-16} pūtta mulli putal cūḻ paṟavi
  • {PATI 66-17} kaṭattu iṭi piṭaviṉ toṭi kuli cēkkum
  • {PATI 66-18} vāṉ paḷiṅku viriiya ce paral murampiṉ
  • {PATI 66-19} ilaṅku katir tiru maṇi peṟūum
  • {PATI 66-20} akal kaṇ vippiṉ nāṭu kiḻavōṉ ē

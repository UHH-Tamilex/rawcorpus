Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 66-1} vāṅku iru maruppiṉ tīm toṭai paḻuṉiya
  • {PATI 66-2} iṭaṉ uṭai pēriyāḻ pālai paṇṇi
  • {PATI 66-3} paṭarntaṉai cellum mutu vāy iravala
  • {PATI 66-4} iṭi icai muracamoṭu oṉṟumoḻintu oṉṉār
  • {PATI 66-5} vēl uṭai kuḻūu camam tataiya nūṟi
  • {PATI 66-6} koṉṟu puṟampeṟṟa piṇam payil aḻuvattu
  • {PATI 66-7} toṉṟu tiṟai tanta kaḷiṟṟoṭu nelliṉ
  • {PATI 66-8} ampaṇa aḷavai virintu uṟai pōkiya
  • {PATI 66-9} ār patam nalkum eṉpa kaṟuttōr
  • {PATI 66-10} uṟu muraṇ tāṅkiya tār arum takaippiṉ
  • {PATI 66-11} nāḷ maḻai kuḻūu cimai kaṭukkum tōṉṟal
  • {PATI 66-12} tōl micaittu eḻutarum virintu ilaṅku eḵkiṉ
  • {PATI 66-13} tār purintaṉṉa vāḷ uṭai viḻaviṉ
  • {PATI 66-14} pōr paṭu maḷḷar pōntoṭu toṭutta
  • {PATI 66-15} kaṭavuḷ vākai tuy vī ēyppa
  • {PATI 66-16} pūtta mullai putal cūḻ paṟavai
  • {PATI 66-17} kaṭattu iṭai piṭaviṉ toṭai kulai cēkkum
  • {PATI 66-18} vāṉ paḷiṅku viraiiya ce paral murampiṉ
  • {PATI 66-19} ilaṅku katir tiru maṇi peṟūum
  • {PATI 66-20} akal kaṇ vaippiṉ nāṭu kiḻavōṉ ē

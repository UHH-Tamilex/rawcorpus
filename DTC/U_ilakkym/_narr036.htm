{{Readable Translation}}

  • Believing the word of the man from the mountain with a mind of stone[FNote_154],
  • where the male of the short-handed big tiger, strong in murdering,
  • attacks [and] kills the big elephant bull in the vast area of descending water, for the female with [her] flower forehead to be lonely,
  • we lost our integrity. At midnight,
  • while [to me] it is unending affliction,
  • rousing comments that repeat ungenerous, fiery words,
  • gossip-mouthed women becoming one in calumny,
  • what loss is it[FNote_155] to this noisy village?

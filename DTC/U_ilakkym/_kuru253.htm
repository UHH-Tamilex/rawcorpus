{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He will not hear [it][FNote_1021], friend. If he hears [it],
  2. even if something excellent passes by,
  3. is he actually[FNote_1022] one not to delay further, while your days get lost in
  4. misery, the great beauty lost on the common[FNote_1023] bed, a flower on a thread, wasting away[FNote_1024]
  5. - he who traversed the mountins, glistening high on the peaks,
  6. where people going [their] way rest
  7. in stony holes smelling of the flesh the tiger has stored for food
  8. on the high mountin slope where sounding bamboo has grown high?

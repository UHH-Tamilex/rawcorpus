{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if he doesn't come right now,
  • being considerate [and] longing for us who don't exist without him,
  • will he go by ship to the Gangā,
  • with herons(?)[FNote_599], [their] voices sweet as if the strings were sounded
  • on the small lute, in front of the god rare in wrath, by excellent [bards]?
  • What work will he do, he,
  • who has gone to traverse the wilderness of swaying branches[FNote_600],
  • where the cock of the forest dove, gone after it had broken free from the net
  • of the hunter strong in the cruel work of killing,
  • [now] is frightened [even] by the spider that [makes] threads from [its] mouth?

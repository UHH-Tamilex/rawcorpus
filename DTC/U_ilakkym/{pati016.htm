Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 16-1} kōṭu uṟaḻntu eṭutta koṭum kaṇ iñci
  • {PATI 16-2} nāṭu kaṇṭaṉṉa kaṇi tuñcu vilaṅkal
  • {PATI 16-3} tuñcu mara kuḻāam tuvaṉṟi puṉiṟṟu makaḷ
  • {PATI 16-4} pūṇā aiyavi tūkkiya matila
  • {PATI 16-5} nal eḻil neṭum putavu murukki kollupu
  • {PATI 16-6} ēṉam ākiya nuṉi muri maruppiṉ
  • {PATI 16-7} kaṭāam vārntu kaṭum ciṉam potti
  • {PATI 16-8} maram kol maḻa kaḷiṟu vaḻaṅkum pācaṟi
  • {PATI 16-9} nīṭiṉi ākaliṉ kāṇku vanticiṉ ē
  • {PATI 16-10} āṟiya kaṟpiṉ aṭaṅkiya cāyal
  • {PATI 16-11} ūṭiṉum iṉiya kūṟum iṉ naki
  • {PATI 16-12} amirtu poti tuvar vāy amartta nōkkiṉ
  • {PATI 16-13} cuṭar nutal aci naṭi uḷḷal um uriyaḷ
  • {PATI 16-14} pāyal uyyum ō tōṉṟal tā iṉṟu
  • {PATI 16-15} tiru maṇi poruta tikaḻ viṭu pacum poṉ
  • {PATI 16-16} vayaṅku katir vayiramōṭu uṟaḻntu pūṇ cuṭarvara
  • {PATI 16-17} eḻu muṭi keḻīiya tiru ñemar akalattu
  • {PATI 16-18} puriyōr uṇ kaṇ tuyil iṉ pāyal
  • {PATI 16-19} pāl um koḷāl um vallōy niṉ
  • {PATI 16-20} cāyal mārpu naṉi alittaṉṟu ē

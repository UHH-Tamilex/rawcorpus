{{Readable Translation}}

  • He who is constant in speaking, who is pleasing apparently a long time,
  • he will never know of separation from my shoulder.
  • Like the sweet honey collected in the sandal tree
  • on the height[FNote_1], after [bees] have filled themselves with the cool pollen of the day lotus,
  • great, certinly, are[FNote_2] the friendships of great people.
  • Since [he] is considerate, longing for us who are not content without him,
  • as the world is not content without water,
  • since [he] fears [our] fragrant forehead turning pale,
  • will he have baseness? He won`t know of [such] doing.

  • 9b will they (the friendships) have baseness? They don`t know of [such] doing.[FNote_3]

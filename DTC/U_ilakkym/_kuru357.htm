{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Such that they came close[FNote_1431] to the word that says: 'good [are] they
  2. [and] round so that wishes arise, because they [are] soft',
  3. [were] the shoulders [now] emaciating, having crossed the time of dew[FNote_1432],
  4. [and the] collyrium eyes without sleeping, enduring hateful affliction
  5. - before he united with them, the man from the sky-touching mountains,
  6. where the tall good elephant, [once] moved by the brand
  7. of the watchman who guards the little Īṉal millet(?)[FNote_1433],
  8. is frightened at the glowing brightness falling from a star.

  1. iḻiyaṇi makaḷiriṉ viḻitaka pūtta
  2. nīṭucuri yiṇara cuṭarvīk koṉṟik
  3. kāṭukaviṉ pūtta vāyiṉu naṉṟum
  4. varumaḻik ketiriya maṇiniṟa virumputa
  5. ṉariniṟam paṭutta nalliṇart teṟuḻvī ((5))
  6. tāan tēralar kollō cēynāṭṭuk
  7. kaḷiṟutit tāṭiya kaviḻaka niṭunīṟu
  8. veḷiṟil kāḻa vēla nīṭiya
  9. paḻaṅkaṇ mutuneṟi maṟikkum
  10. vaḻaṅkaruṅ kāṉa miṟantici ṉōrē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Although they have [already] flowered - the beauty of the wilderness -
  • the Koṉṟi tress with glowing blossoms in long curly clusters
  • that flowered to rise evoke desire as jewel-adorned women,
  • does not he make out
  • the Teṟuḻ blossoms in good clusters that turned to white colour
  • on the sapphire-coloured big bushes in the face of the abundantly coming rin,
  • - he who traversed the forest difficult to wander,
  • where the distressful old path, on which Vēlam trees
  • with cores without whiteness have grown long, is concealed
  • by dust placed on the upturned inside which the elephant had kicked in play(?)
  • in a distant land?

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The noisy old village,
  • where the sweet song of the thundering waves lingers(?)
  • with the women dancing for a festival, [their] hips adorned with jewels
  • of fine workmanship, apart from seeing with the eye
  • that[FNote_441] the man from the cool ghat, where the white heron gives birth
  • on an old [cart] bar ...?, cast off in a weak(?) place
  • by the saltmerchants going in groups of unstable living,
  • that bring to the mountins heaps [large] as hills
  • of salt ripened in brackish soil(?) [and] announce [the price],
  • tied garlands with us on the previous day, stripping with [other] flowers
  • green-stalked blue water-lilies the leaves of which are swollen,
  • didn't know anything, did it?

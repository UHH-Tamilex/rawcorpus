{{Readable Translation}}

  • Ah, I ache, aches my heart, [at the thought]
  • what will become of her, because of the great dew [and] the northwind,
  • she who is shivering [even] in the month of westwind,
  • she of immature breasts with spots that came to oppose [it].
  • Even when we are near she herself has become weary(?)
  • with the maddening(?) cold season that stood in showers,
  • while the hunter considerately removes the core(?)[FNote_926] upon seeing
  • the suffering white egrets that was perched in heaps on the branches,
  • while the pretty sprouts of the mimosa in dewy bushes touches them.

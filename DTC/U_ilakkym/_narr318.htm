  1. niṉaikkalu niṉaitirō vaiya vaṉṟunām
  2. paṇaittā ḷōmaip paṭuciṉai payanta
  3. poruntāp pukarniḻa liruntaṉa māka
  4. naṭukkañ ceyyātu naṇṇuvaḻit tōṉṟi
  5. yoṭittumicai koṇṭa yōṅkumarup piyāṉai ((5))
  6. poṟipaṭu taṭakkaic curukkip piṟitō
  7. raṟiyiṭai yiṭṭa vaḷavaikku vēṟuṇarn
  8. teṉṟūḻ viṭarakañ cilampap
  9. puṉṟalai maṭappiṭi pulampiya kuralē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Will you think too, o lord,
  • of the lonely voice of the youthful she-elephant with low head,
  • so that the sunny(?) inside of the cave resounded,
  • realising differently to the measure that knowing one other thing
  • intervened(?)[FNote_931]: the elephant with high tusks folding
  • [its] broad trunk showing spots, that had taken food, breaking [it] off,
  • appearing on the near(?) way without making [us] tremble,
  • while we were in the unlinking dappled shade
  • spread by the branches coming from the bamboo-trunked Ōmai tree, that day?

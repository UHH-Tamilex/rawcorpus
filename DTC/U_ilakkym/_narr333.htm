{{Metrical text}}

  1. maḻitoḻi ṉuḻantu māvicum pukanteṉak
  2. kaḻikavi ṉaḻinta kallatarc ciṟuneṟip
  3. paralava lūṟaṟ ciṟunīr maruṅkiṟ
  4. pūnutal yāṉiyoṭu puliporu tuṇṇuñ
  5. curaṉiṟan tariya veṉṉā ruraṉaḻin ((5))
  6. tuṇmali neñcamoṭu vaṇmi vēṇṭi
  7. yarumporuṭ kakaṉṟa kātalar muyakketirntu
  8. tiruntiḻip paṇittōḷ peṟuṉar pōlu
  9. nīṅkuka mātōniṉ ṉavala mōṅkumici
  10. yuyarpukaḻ nalli loṇcuvarp porunti ((10))
  11. nayavaru kurala palli
  12. naḷḷeṉ yāmat tuḷḷutoṟum paṭumē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The lover who departed for difficult wealth,
  • needing strength, with a heart full inside, strength perishing,
  • not saying "difficult to traverse[FNote_961] [are] the deserts,
  • where the tiger eats, having fought with the elephant of flower-forehead
  • by the side of a little water oozing from the pebbly depression
  • by the small path with stones [and] fine sand where the bamboo's beauty is perished, becuse the clouds have ascended the great sky, task finished,
  • he will obtin the bamboo shoulder with perfect jewels, faced with embrace,
  • it seems.
  • May not your affliction leave?
  • The lizard with a voice from which longing comes,
  • joining the bright wall in the highly prised good house on the high elevation,
  • falls in whenever it remembers deep midnight.

{PUṞAM 46}

((Originally extracted from master file ^PURAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PU 46-1} nī ē puṟaviṉ allal aṉṟi um piṟa um
  • {PU 46-2} iṭukkaṇ pala um viṭuttōṉ marukaṉi
  • {PU 46-3} ivar ē pulaṉ uḻutu uṇmār puṉkaṇ añci
  • {PU 46-4} tamatu pakuttu uṇṇum taṇ niḻal vāḻnar
  • {PU 46-5} kaḷiṟu kaṇṭu aḻūum aḻāal maṟanta
  • {PU 46-6} puṉ tali ciṟāar maṉṟu maruṇṭu nōkki
  • {PU 46-7} viruntiṉ puṉkaṇ ō uṭiyar
  • {PU 46-8} kēṭṭaṉi āyiṉ nī vēṭṭatu ceym ē

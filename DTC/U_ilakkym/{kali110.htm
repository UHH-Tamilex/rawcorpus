  • {KALI 110-3} iṭu tēḷ maruntu ō niṉ vēṭkai toṭutara

  • {KALI 110-7} āṅku nī kūṟiṉ aṉaittu āka niṅkuka
  • {KALI 110-8} accattāṉ māṟi acaiviṉāṉ pōttantu
  • {KALI 110-9} niccam taṭumāṟum mel iyal āy makaḷ
  • {KALI 110-10} mattam piṇitta kayiṟu pōl niṉ nalam
  • {KALI 110-11} cuṟṟi cuḻalum eṉ neñcu
  • {KALI 110-12} viṭinta poḻutiṉum il vayiṉ pōkātu
  • {KALI 110-13} koṭum toḻuviṉ uḷ paṭṭa kaṉṟiṟku cūḻum
  • {KALI 110-14} kaṭum cūl ā nāku pōl niṉ kaṇṭu nāḷ um
  • {KALI 110-15} naṭuṅku añar uṟṟatu eṉ neñcu
  • {KALI 110-16} evvam mikutara em tiṟattu e ñāṉṟu um
  • {KALI 110-17} ney kaṭai pāliṉ payaṉ yātu um iṉṟu āki
  • {KALI 110-18} kai tōyal māttirai allatu ceyti
  • {KALI 110-19} aṟiyātu aḷittu eṉ uyir
  • {KALI 110-20} aṉṉai ō maṉṟattu kaṇṭāṅku ē cāṉṟār makaḷirai
  • {KALI 110-21} iṉṟi amaiyēṉ eṉṟu iṉṉa um colluvāy
  • {KALI 110-22} niṉṟāy nī ceṉṟī emar kāṇpar nāḷai um
  • {KALI 110-23} kaṉṟoṭu cēṟum pulattu

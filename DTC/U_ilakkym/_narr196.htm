{{Readable Translation}}

  • O fullmoon who does not know of the afflictions of the bamboo ladder[FNote_624],
  • with white moonlight fresh like milked milk,
  • within all beams as if crystals bundeled,
  • whereas in the world there is nothing that stays hidden from you,
  • because of [your] possessing excellent impartiality,
  • you don't show me the way to the place of his staying, hidden from me.
  • Being emaciated as my shoulder that lost [its] good integrity,
  • rolling sideways, becoming ever smaller[FNote_625]
  • - that, is it because of a knowing witness's lying?

  1. vārāy pāṇa nakukam nēriḻai
  2. kaṭumpuṭaik kaṭuñcū ṉaṅkuṭik kutavi
  3. neyō ṭimaikku maiyavit tiraḷkāḻ
  4. viḷaṅkunakar viḷaṅkak kiṭantōṭ kuṟukip
  5. putalva ṉīṉṟeṉap peyarpeyart tavvarit ((5))
  6. titalai yalkuṉ mutupeṇ ṭākit
  7. tuñcuti yōmel lañci lōtiyeṉap
  8. paṉmā ṇakaṭṭiṟ kuvaḷai yoṟṟi
  9. yuḷḷiṉe ṉuṟaiyu meṟkaṇṭu mella niṉṟaṉe ṉallaṉō yāṉē yeṟkaṇṭu
  10. mukaināṇ muṟuva ṟōṉṟit ((10))
  11. takaimalar neṭuṅkaṇ kaiputait tatuvē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Don't come, bard, we must smile
  • at [her] burying [her] hand, [she with] long eyes, befitting blossoms,
  • while a smile fresh(?) [as] a bud appeared,
  • softly, upon seeing me staying [there] lost in memory(?)[FNote_1030],
  • pressing [her] belly with a blue water-lily many times,
  • saying "Do you sleep, [you of] soft pretty few hair?",
  • having become an old woman with hips with beauty spots
  • [and] pretty lines, changing [her] name since she had given birth to a son,
  • [me] coming near to her who rested, while the bright mansion was shining
  • with the round seeds of white mustard that twinkled with ghee,
  • contributing to our house with the heavy pregnancy of fine-jewelled relations(?).[FNote_1031]

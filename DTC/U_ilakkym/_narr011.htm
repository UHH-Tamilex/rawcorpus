{{Metrical text}}

  1. peyyātu vaikiya kōtai pōla
  2. meycā yiṉaiyavar ceykuṟi piḻaippa
  3. vuḷḷi notumalar nērpurai teḷḷitiṉ
  4. vārār eṉṉum pulavi yuṭkoḷa
  5. loḻika māḷaniṉ neñcat tāṉē ((5))
  6. puṇari poruta pūmaṇa laṭaikarai
  7. yāḻi maruṅki ṉalava ṉōmpi
  8. valavaṉ vaḷpāyn tūra
  9. nilavu virintaṉṟāṟ kāṉa lāṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O you emaciated of body like a garland kept unworn[FNote_37]
  • may pass by in your heart
  • the taking inside of vexation that says 'he won`t come,
  • for sure, [since] strangers talk of [our] meeting', remembering
  • how [you] failed the sign he made.
  • Moonlight indeed has spread in the seashore grove,
  • while the charioteer checks the reigns [and] moves slowly,
  • protecting crabs on the side of the wheels
  • on the shore where sand sets, flowers struck by the ocean.
  • 3b for sure, [since they] talk of [my parents] consenting to a stranger,

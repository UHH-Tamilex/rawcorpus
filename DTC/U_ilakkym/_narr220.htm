{{Readable Translation}}

  • All too[FNote_698] noble indeed
  • [are] the young(?) little people[FNote_699] who turn[FNote_700] together in the street,
  • together with us on the good horse that does not eat,[FNote_701]
  • wearing a chaplet of short-budded Yārkkum,
  • linking the small bells [and] placing the big belt.
  • They themselves [are] those that have this great confusion, saying
  • "we [are] from the village in festival there, where the eye of the leather-tied drum does not fail".
  • - What happens with us means [their] becoming neighbours
  • to the little woman with carp-like collyrium eyes [and] sweet words.[FNote_702]

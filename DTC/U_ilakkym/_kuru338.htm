{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In the cool, dewy cold season, on the last day of the late dews
  2. at sorrowful evening, when the superior good bull
  3. of the Irali with twisted horns chews abundant corn,
  4. sleeps, while time passes, in the wide thicket full of blossoms
  5. [and] rests in the lasting shade with [his] soft, youthful doe,
  6. it has come, the big, victorious chariot, for [her] with the bamboo shoulder
  7. to let go of the languor of loneliness, the young woman lustrous with integrity,
  8. in fidelity quieted in the shining house.[FNote_1361]

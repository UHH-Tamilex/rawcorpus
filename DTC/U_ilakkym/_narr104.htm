{{Readable Translation}}

  • I enjoying permanently the chest of the man from the bustling(?) mountin[FNote_348],
  • where the rhythm of the small Toṇṭakam drum,
  • beaten desirously(?) by the little people of the hills,
  • without fearing affliction on the heights of thick stones,
  • when[FNote_349] the vast-mouthed male of the flower-dotted tiger
  • fights with the elephant bull on the mountin side fragrant with honey,
  • chases away the parakeets falling into the big-stalked red millet nearby
  • - even apart from that[FNote_350], is he [still] thinking[FNote_351]
  • of the small path difficult to look at on the slope
  • that is obstructed, so that going is cut off, by great water
  • with the time when the thunder dashes angrily so that
  • the high mountins with caves that possess snakes are upset at midnight.

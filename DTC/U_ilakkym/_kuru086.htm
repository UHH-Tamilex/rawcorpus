{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Are there other listeners
  2. - stirred up, struck by loneliness, with pain difficult to bear,
  3. with red-streaked rain-eyes, the long-checked dew broken out -
  4. to the curved bell's poor voice[FNote_351] in which the tongue is sounding,
  5. stirring each time a fly buzzes around the cow,[FNote_352]
  6. at midnight in the cold season, when the rain-laden cold wind spreads?

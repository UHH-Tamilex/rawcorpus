{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land, where the cotton-headed she-monkey sneezes,
  • since impossible to endure is the fragrance of the catch(?)[FNote_950] of fish
  • of the heron on the big branch, bent under the abundant [fruit-] bunches
  • on the mountain slope with a yield of jacktrees with rich pulp,
  • even of talking to you[FNote_951] on her behalf I am ashamed.
  • Pallor, [recalling] the whithered flower of the Pīram in fine creepers,
  • is creeping up, alas, for many days
  • in her eyes, on which, taking [them] for blossoms, the bees
  • come joining together, being completely unaware,
  • when they come near our forest, in [their] read-streaked desirable beauty.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In the land where he has gone, becoming hard,
  2. he who abandoned [me] for that line to fade,
  3. on [my] hip - wealth! Has he reached [it],
  4. having traversed the desert where a suffering[FNote_724], single bamboo has grown
  5. as long as [the gap] between the nodes of sugarcane, drooping on the rocks,
  6. perishing when the head of the group, the dark elephant bull comes,
  7. broad feet with nails big as devil's teeth?

{{Metrical text}}

  1. peruṅkaḷi ṟuḻuvai tākkali ṉirumpiṭik
  2. karuvi māmaḻai yarava mañcum
  3. vāṅkamaik kaḻaiyi ṉaralum pūcaṟkup
  4. pōtē ruṇkaṇ kaliḻavu mētil
  5. pētai neñcaṅ kavalai kavaṟṟa
  6. vaṅkā kiṉṟāṟ ṟōḻi pakuvāyp ((5))
  7. piṇavuppuli vaḻaṅku maṇaṅkaruṅ kavalai piṇavuppaci tīriya pēḻvā yirumpuli
  8. piṇaṅkari loṭuṅku maṇaṅkaruṅ kavalaiya
  9. yaviraṟa loḻukum viraicelaṟ kāṉyāṟṟuk
  10. karaiyaruṅ kuṭṭan tamiyar nīnti
  11. viravumalar poṟitta tōḷa
  12. riraviṉ varuta laṟiyā tōṟkē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Thus it has become, friend,
  • while my strange foolish heart causes distress
  • and my budlike collyrium eyes are weeping,
  • fearing the sound of rain great in amount[FNote_459]
  • in [the voice of] the dark elephant cow, when the big elephant bull attacks the tiger,
  • - for the one who does not know of coming at night,
  • he with a shoulder imprinted by mixed blossoms,
  • swimming all alone in the difficult depth to the shore[FNote_460]
  • of the swift-going forest river where shimmering water flows,
  • by crossroads difficult with plagues(?)[FNote_461], where the broad-mouthed female tiger roams.

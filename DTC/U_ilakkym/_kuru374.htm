{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As my father and mother, after having shown that they had come to know,
  2. [and] after it was expressed clearly, that [my] secret action had become public,
  3. [and] since [the thing] with the man from the mountins had come to pass [anyhow][FNote_1491], begged [him to marry me],[FNote_1492]
  4. [the village] became one in the principle of desiring the good (the right thing?),
  5. the blinded village, more confused [about that] than over the nest
  6. of the Tūṅkanam bird with bent wings,[FNote_1493]
  7. connected with the long, dark Palmyra tree.

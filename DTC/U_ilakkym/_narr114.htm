{{Readable Translation}}

  • I am distressed on hearing, being kept [at home?][FNote_375],
  • the sound from the small hamlet, reeking of flesh in every street,
  • when they take the white tusk [and] place it on the wide rock
  • and when they cut off the fresh flesh [and] draw the sharp nails.
  • Ah, pitiable [is] it, friend, the night.(?)[FNote_376]
  • [At that time] he came indeed, the man from a land of hills.
  • I fear the river that comes when the dashing waves grow
  • over the marked(?) old shore, impressed by the dripping rain.
  • They threw down the elephant bull that comes down, trunk fixed,
  • for the cloud-like inexperienced elephant cow to despair,
  • covering the high mountain, where the bustling good stroke
  • of thunder with [its] wet voice destroys the beauty of snakes.

  • {KALI 98-1} yāri nī em il pukutarvāy ōrum
  • {KALI 98-2} putuva malar tērum vaṇṭu ē pōl yāḻa
  • {KALI 98-3} vatuvi viḻavu aṇi vikal um kāṭṭiṉi āy
  • {KALI 98-4} māṭṭu māṭṭu ōṭi makaḷir tara tara
  • {KALI 98-5} pūṭṭu māṉ tiṇ tēr puṭitta maṟuku ellām
  • {KALI 98-6} pāṭṭu ātal cāṉṟa niṉ māya parattimi
  • {KALI 98-7} kāṭṭiya vantami kippaṭuttēṉ paṇṭu elām
  • {KALI 98-8} kēṭṭu um aṟivēṉ maṉ yāṉ
  • {KALI 98-9} teri kōti am nallāy tēṟīyal vēṇṭum
  • {KALI 98-10} poru kari vāy cūḻnta pū mali viyi
  • {KALI 98-11} varu puṉal āṭa tavirntēṉ peritu eṉṉi
  • {KALI 98-12} ceyyā moḻivatu evaṉ
  • {KALI 98-13} ōo puṉal āṭiṉāy eṉa um kēṭṭēṉ puṉal āṅku ē
  • {KALI 98-14} nīḷ nīr neṟi katuppu vārum aṟal āka
  • {KALI 98-15} māṇ eḻil uṇ kaṇ piṟaḻum kayal āka
  • {KALI 98-16} kār malar vēynta kamaḻ pūm parappu ākam
  • {KALI 98-17} nāṇu ciṟi aḻittu naṉpakal vanta a
  • {KALI 98-18} yāṇar putu puṉal āṭiṉāy muṉ māli
  • {KALI 98-19} pāṇaṉ puṇi āka pukku
  • {KALI 98-20} āṉātu aḷittu amar kātalōṭu a puṉal āṭi
  • {KALI 98-21} veḷippaṭu kavviyi yāṉ aṟital añci
  • {KALI 98-22} kuḷittu oḻukiṉāy eṉa um kēṭṭēṉ kuḷittāṅku ē
  • {KALI 98-23} pōrtta ciṉattāṉ puruva tiri iṭā
  • {KALI 98-24} ārkkum ñekiḻattāṉ nal nīr naṭi taṭpa
  • {KALI 98-25} cīr taka vanta putu puṉal niṉṉi koṇṭu
  • {KALI 98-26} īrttu uyppa kaṇṭār uḷar
  • {KALI 98-27} īrttatu uri cāl ciṟappiṉ niṉ nīr uḷḷam vāṅka
  • {KALI 98-28} puri tīr putu puṉal veḷḷattiṉ iṉṉum
  • {KALI 98-29} kari kaṇṭatū um ili
  • {KALI 98-30} niri toṭīi poyyā vāḷ tāṉi puṉi kaḻal kāl teṉṉavaṉ
  • {KALI 98-31} viyi putu puṉal āṭa tavirntatu ai
  • {KALI 98-32} teyvattiṉ tēṟṟi teḷippēṉ peritu eṉṉi
  • {KALI 98-33} ceyyā moḻivatu evaṉ
  • {KALI 98-34} meyyatu ai malku malar vēynta māya putu puṉal
  • {KALI 98-35} pal kāl um āṭiya celvuḻi olki
  • {KALI 98-36} kaḷiñar um il vaḻi kāl āḻntu tērōṭu
  • {KALI 98-37} iḷa maṇal uḷ paṭal ōmpu muḷi nēr
  • {KALI 98-38} muṟuvalārkku ōr naki ceytu

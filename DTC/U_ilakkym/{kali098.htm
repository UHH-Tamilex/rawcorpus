Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 98-1} yārai nī em il pukutarvāy ōrum
  • {KALI 98-2} putuva malar tērum vaṇṭu ē pōl yāḻa
  • {KALI 98-3} vatuvai viḻavu aṇi vaikal um kāṭṭiṉai āy
  • {KALI 98-4} māṭṭu māṭṭu ōṭi makaḷir tara tara
  • {KALI 98-5} pūṭṭu māṉ tiṇ tēr puṭaitta maṟuku ellām
  • {KALI 98-6} pāṭṭu ātal cāṉṟa niṉ māya parattaimai
  • {KALI 98-7} kāṭṭiya vantamai kaippaṭuttēṉ paṇṭu elām
  • {KALI 98-8} kēṭṭu um aṟivēṉ maṉ yāṉ
  • {KALI 98-9} teri kōtai am nallāy tēṟīyal vēṇṭum
  • {KALI 98-10} poru karai vāy cūḻnta pū mali vaiyai
  • {KALI 98-11} varu puṉal āṭa tavirntēṉ peritu eṉṉai
  • {KALI 98-12} ceyyā moḻivatu evaṉ
  • {KALI 98-13} ōo puṉal āṭiṉāy eṉa um kēṭṭēṉ puṉal āṅku ē
  • {KALI 98-14} nīḷ nīr neṟi katuppu vārum aṟal āka
  • {KALI 98-15} māṇ eḻil uṇ kaṇ piṟaḻum kayal āka
  • {KALI 98-16} kār malar vēynta kamaḻ pūm parappu ākam
  • {KALI 98-17} nāṇu ciṟai aḻittu naṉpakal vanta a
  • {KALI 98-18} yāṇar putu puṉal āṭiṉāy muṉ mālai
  • {KALI 98-19} pāṇaṉ puṇai āka pukku
  • {KALI 98-20} āṉātu aḷittu amar kātalōṭu a puṉal āṭi
  • {KALI 98-21} veḷippaṭu kavvaiyai yāṉ aṟital añci
  • {KALI 98-22} kuḷittu oḻukiṉāy eṉa um kēṭṭēṉ kuḷittāṅku ē
  • {KALI 98-23} pōrtta ciṉattāṉ puruva tirai iṭā
  • {KALI 98-24} ārkkum ñekiḻattāṉ nal nīr naṭai taṭpa
  • {KALI 98-25} cīr taka vanta putu puṉal niṉṉai koṇṭu
  • {KALI 98-26} īrttu uyppa kaṇṭār uḷar
  • {KALI 98-27} īrttatu urai cāl ciṟappiṉ niṉ nīr uḷḷam vāṅka
  • {KALI 98-28} purai tīr putu puṉal veḷḷattiṉ iṉṉum
  • {KALI 98-29} karai kaṇṭatū um ilai
  • {KALI 98-30} nirai toṭīi poyyā vāḷ tāṉai puṉai kaḻal kāl teṉṉavaṉ
  • {KALI 98-31} vaiyai putu puṉal āṭa tavirntatu ai
  • {KALI 98-32} teyvattiṉ tēṟṟi teḷippēṉ peritu eṉṉai
  • {KALI 98-33} ceyyā moḻivatu evaṉ
  • {KALI 98-34} meyyatu ai malku malar vēynta māya putu puṉal
  • {KALI 98-35} pal kāl um āṭiya celvuḻi olki
  • {KALI 98-36} kaḷaiñar um il vaḻi kāl āḻntu tērōṭu
  • {KALI 98-37} iḷa maṇal uḷ paṭal ōmpu muḷai nēr
  • {KALI 98-38} muṟuvalārkku ōr nakai ceytu

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. We are without any blame[FNote_1301] of having lughed a single day
  2. with the man from the ghat, where on every dune spreads,
  3. drying[FNote_1302] fish from[FNote_1303] the fishermen of the great sea
  4. together with fading prawns from the difficult(?)[FNote_1304] backwaters,
  5. many together, so that the moonlight-coloured sand reeks [of it].
  6. This village of streets [planted] with Puṉṉi,
  7. with branches buzzing with birds joined[FNote_1305] with golden clusters of opening buds,
  8. vinly spreads gossip in its cruelty.

Altern

8b: vinly spreads gossip about his cruelty.

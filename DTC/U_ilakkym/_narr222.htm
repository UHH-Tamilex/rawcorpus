{{Readable Translation}}

  • Shall I let go of [you] alltogether, friend, into movement,
  • swinging as I grasp [your] hip with the green girdle
  • today[FNote_708], [for you] to resemble as peacock dancing in the sky,
  • gently, bending the small [swing-]curve[FNote_709] decorated by hand,
  • on the cord with way-giving bends(?) fettered so that it is injured
  • to the red-blossomed bending branch of the black-trunked Vēṅki tree?
  • - for you to see his distant long hill
  • with groves, where the big elephant bull trumpets,
  • not seeing, since clouds come up, the sleeping female by [his] side
  • on the mountin side joined by Vaḻi trees where the plantins have become high, many together.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. aṟitalu maṟitiyō pāka peruṅkaṭa
  2. leṟitirai kōḻīiya vekkar veṟikoḷa
  3. vāṭuva riyalava ṉōṭuvayi ṉāṟṟā
  4. tacaiyuḷ ḷoḻinta vacaitīr kuṟumakaṭ
  5. kuyaviṉeṉ ceṉṟiyā ṉuṇṇō vuraippa ((5))
  6. maṟumoḻi peyartta lāṟṟā ṇaṟumalar
  7. ñāḻa lañciṉait tāḻiṇar koḻuti
  8. muṟitimirn tutirtta kaiya
  9. ṉaṟivaña ruṟuvi yāymaṭa nilaiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Do you have any knowledge at all[FNote_354], charioteer
  • of the state of choice innocence of her who has the grief of knowledge,
  • she with a hand that rubbed [and] dropped a shoot,
  • picking at the trailing clusters on the pretty Ñāḻal branches
  • with fragrant blossoms, she who is unable to drive away(?) the words exchanged, when I talk of the inner pain, going as one who suffered(?)[FNote_355]
  • for the faultless little woman who stayed behind with a shaking(?) mind,
  • without strength(?) at the place of running after the lined crab in play(?),
  • when they take up the Veṟi dance on the dune, the dashing waves being full in the great sea.(?)

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The glow go[es], affliction abound[s],
  2. at the time the light subsides the jasmine blossom[s] -
  3. 'evening' - those who say so are confused.
  4. When in the wide city[FNote_959] the crested[FNote_960] cockerel sounds,
  5. even quickly[FNote_961] falling dawn [is] evening
  6. and midday [is] evening for those without mates.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As shining bangles cut from conches(?) loosen, day by day,
  2. with eyes crying without sleeping, lonely
  3. from staying here like this we will escape. There
  4. rise now, live, my heart!
  5. [Just] think [about it]: departing for his land
  6. even if he were in a country with different(?) language[FNote_62],
  7. beyond the good land of Kaṭṭi with many spears,
  8. in front of [that of] the Vaṭukars with basil/hemp chaplets, over there(?)[FNote_63].

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When [you] consider that she has made the one[FNote_1396] union
  2. with us, also she of herself(?)[FNote_1397], on the long forest way[FNote_1398],
  3. where the stalked(?)[FNote_1399] fragrant blossom of the maiden Sirissa
  4. appears like the crest of a youthful big peacock,
  5. on the bottom of the poor desert, where plentiful mountain pools have dried up,
  6. - good [is], heart, your decision that you longed for[FNote_1400].

Altern: T.V.G.

If you considered that she would make the one union with us[FNote_1401]

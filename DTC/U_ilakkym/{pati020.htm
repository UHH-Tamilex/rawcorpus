  • {PATI 20-3} muraṇiyōr talai ceṉṟu
  • {PATI 20-4} kaṭampu mutal taṭinta kaṭum ciṉa muṉpiṉ
  • {PATI 20-5} neṭum cēralātaṉ vāḻka avaṉ kaṇṇi
  • {PATI 20-6} vāyppu aṟiyalaṉ ē veyil tukaḷ aṉaittu um
  • {PATI 20-7} māṟṟōr tēettu māṟiya viṉai ē
  • {PATI 20-8} kaṇṇiṉ uvantu neñcu aviḻpu aṟiyā
  • {PATI 20-9} naṇṇār tēettu um poyppu aṟiyalaṉ ē
  • {PATI 20-10} kaṉaviṉum oṉṉār tēya ōṅki naṭantu
  • {PATI 20-11} paṭiyōr tēyttu vaṭi maṇi iraṭṭum
  • {PATI 20-12} kaṭāa yāṉai kaṇa nirai alaṟa
  • {PATI 20-13} viyal irum parappiṉ mā nilam kaṭantu
  • {PATI 20-14} pulavar ētta ōṅku pukaḻ niṟīi
  • {PATI 20-15} viri uḷai mā um kaḷiṟu um tēr um
  • {PATI 20-16} vayiriyar kaṇṇuḷarkku ōmpātu vīci
  • {PATI 20-17} kaṭi miḷai kuṇṭu kiṭaṅkiṉ
  • {PATI 20-18} neṭu matil nilai ñāyil
  • {PATI 20-19} ampu uṭai ār eyil uḷ aḻittu uṇṭa
  • {PATI 20-20} aṭāa aṭu pukai aṭṭu malar mārpaṉ
  • {PATI 20-21} emarkku um piṟarkku um yāvar āyiṉum
  • {PATI 20-22} paricil mākkaḷ vallār āyiṉum
  • {PATI 20-23} koṭai kaṭaṉ amarnta kōṭā neñciṉaṉ
  • {PATI 20-24} maṉ uyir aḻiya yāṇṭu pala māṟi
  • {PATI 20-25} taṇ iyal eḻili talaiyātu āyiṉum
  • {PATI 20-26} vayiṟu paci kūra īyalaṉ
  • {PATI 20-27} vayiṟu mācu ilīyar avaṉ īṉṟa tāy ē

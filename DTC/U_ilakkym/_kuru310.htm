{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The birds began to lament and the flowers closed
  2. and the seashore grove is filled with loneliness. The sky too,
  3. just like us[FNote_1267], has become deluded
  4. [and] empty as the daylight passes.
  5. Even now I am, friend. This state,
  6. if only[FNote_1268] someone told it the man from the cool ghat
  7. of cool[FNote_1269] [and] fragrant Ñāḻal trees.

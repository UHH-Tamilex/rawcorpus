{{Readable Translation}}

  • In case you desired[FNote_886] the attachment
  • to the man from the village, where a bull with weary gait feeds on
  • the rest of what was eaten by a newly calfed cow with [her] calf
  • from the new flowers, given to be worn(?)[FNote_887], on the white water-lilies in the paddy field,
  • don't you take my word [for it][FNote_888], you with thorn teeth.
  • You, [you are] suitable with great beauty/integrity. He,
  • those who don't call him "son" say "[he is] a bee,
  • filling himself on cool fragrant new blossoms
  • when reaching the pond of long water at midday".

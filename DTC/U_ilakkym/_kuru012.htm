{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The way he has gone, they say, has crossroads
  2. where hunters with bent bows sharpen arrows,
  3. climbed on rocks [hot] as the stone of a smithy
  4. with many pools tiny as ant holes.
  5. Without taking in just that is troubling,
  6. this noisy village murmurs indifferent things[FNote_70].
  7. 6b {--!}this noisy village, declaring indifference!

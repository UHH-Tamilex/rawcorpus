{{Metrical text}}

  1. paṭukāḻ nāṟiya parāarip puṉṉi
  2. yaṭumaraṉ mokkuḷi ṉirumpu vāyaviḻap
  3. poṉṉi ṉaṉṉa tātupaṭu paṉmalar
  4. cūṭunar toṭutta miccil kōṭutoṟu
  5. neykaṉi pacuṅkāy tūṅkun tuṟivaṉi ((5))
  6. yiṉiyaṟin ticiṉē koṇka ṉākutal
  7. kaḻiccē ṟāṭiya kaṇikkā lattiri
  8. kuḷampiṉuñ cēyiṟā voṭuṅkiṉa
  9. kōtiyu mellā mūtiveṇ maṇalē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Know[FNote_866] now that he will become [your] husband,
  • the man from the ghat, where green unripe fruits becoming soft with oil hang
  • on every branch, the rest after those wearing [garlands] had linked
  • many blossoms full of gold-like pollen,
  • when the large-trunked Puṉṉi tree that had become hard at core
  • openend [its] iron mouths, as the flower-buds of the near bowstring hemp.
  • More than the prawn shrank under the hoofs
  • of the mule with round legs that played in the the mud of the backwaters
  • all the garlands[FNote_867] [shrank] under the sand [blown] by the cold wind.[FNote_868]

{{Metrical text}}

  1. neyyuṅ kuyyu māṭi miyoṭu
  2. mācupaṭ ṭiṉṟē kaliṅkamun tōḷuṅ
  3. titali meṉmulit tīmpāl piliṟṟāp
  4. putalvaṟ pullip puṉiṟu nāṟummē
  5. vāliḻi makaḷir cērit tōṉṟun ((5))
  6. tērōrk kottaṉe mallē mataṉāṟ
  7. poṉpuri narampi ṉiṉkuraṟ cīṟiyā
  8. ḻeḻāal valli yāyiṉun toḻāal
  9. puriyō raṉṉa puriyi ṉaṭpi
  10. ṉiḷiyōr kūmpuki maruḷvō rāṅkuk
  11. koṇṭucel pāṇaniṉ ṟaṇṭuṟi yūraṉip
  12. pāṭumaṉip pāṭal kūṭātu nīṭunilip ((10))
  13. puraviyum pūṇili muṉikuva
  14. virakila moḻiyalyām vēṭṭatil vaḻiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Fumbling with ghee and curry [our] cloth
  • has been stined with dirt(?) and [our] shoulders,
  • embracing [our] son, while sweet milk drizzles
  • from [our] soft breasts with beauty spots, smell of the newly born.
  • We ar not equal to the one with a chariot
  • that appears in the street of women with pure jewels.
  • Therefore,
  • though you are strong in rising
  • the sweet-voiced small lute with gold-like strings,
  • don't worship [us].
  • Take him [and] go, o bard - the man from the village by the cool ghat.
  • In the prolonged state of not meeting singing in the singing house(?!)
  • the horses too hate the state of being yoked.
  • Don't speak useless [words] in a way that is not wanted by us.

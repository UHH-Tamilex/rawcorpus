{{Readable Translation}}

  1. We are worried; rather don't come
  2. at midday, man of the slope
  3. where the love-struck female monkey who did not escape widowhood,
  4. after uniting with the group [her] inexperienced strong young,
  5. destroys [her] life by jumping from the high mountain's side
  6. because the black-eyed restless(?) male had attained the great other.[FNote_290]

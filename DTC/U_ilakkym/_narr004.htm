  6. yariya vāku namakkeṉa kūṟiṟ
  7. koṇṭuñ celvarko ṟōḻi yumaṇar
  8. veṇka luppiṉ koḷḷi cāṟṟik
  9. kaṇaniri kiḷarkku neṭuneṟic cakaṭa
  10. maṇaṉmaṭut turaṟu mōci kaḻaṉik ((10))
  11. karuṅkāl veṇkuruku verūu
  12. miruṅkaḻic cērppiṟṟa muṟivi ṉūrkkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • 'The gossip
  • about the man from the ghat, where fishermen, attached to the sea,
  • [their] little homes by the seashore grove, stay in the rich shade of the
  • blue-coloured Puṉṉi tree, look over the bright water[FNote_13] of cool, vast expanse,
  • [and] let [their] twisted nets with pretty holes dry,
  • - if mother gets to know, living here
  • will be difficult[FNote_14] for us' - if we tell [him] so,
  • will he take [us] along[FNote_15], friend,
  • to the village where his [people] stay[FNote_16], on the coast with dark backwaters,
  • where the black-legged white egret is frightened
  • in the fields by the sound, which rumbles when the cart [wheel] got stuck
  • in the sands, on the long way, where cattle in groups are roused,
  • when the salt-merchants proclim the price of the white salt stones.

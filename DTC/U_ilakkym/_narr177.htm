{{Readable Translation}}

  • He has but gone to traverse[FNote_574] the hot desert, difficult to get away from(?)[FNote_575],
  • of the [once] delightful pretty wilderness where the trees have cught fire,
  • while sharp flames, coming up [and] spreading, crush the forest.
  • I see his intention.[FNote_576] Orderly
  • he wipes the shining leaf of [his] spear and he puts
  • peacock-feathers on [his] shield [and] adorns it with bells.
  • And now he takes ever so much care of [his] wife.
  • It seems to have come, friend,
  • the day you swim the flood, the pupils of [your] collyrium eyes,
  • graceful [as if] drawn, going to waste, aching, aching.

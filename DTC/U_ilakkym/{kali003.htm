Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 3-1} aṟaṉ iṉṟi ayal tūṟṟum ampali nāṇi um
  • {KALI 3-2} vaṟaṉ nīnti nī cellum nīḷ iṭi niṉippa um
  • {KALI 3-3} iṟi nillā vaḷi ōṭa itaḻ cōrpu paṉi malka
  • {KALI 3-4} poṟi nillā nōyōṭu pulleṉṟa nutal ivaḷ
  • {KALI 3-5} viṟal nalaṉ iḻappa um viṉi vēṭṭāy kēeḷ iṉi
  • {KALI 3-6} uṭi ivaḷ uyir vāḻāḷ nī nīppiṉ eṉa pala
  • {KALI 3-7} iṭi koṇṭu yām irappa um ema koḷḷāy āyiṉi
  • {KALI 3-8} kaṭiiya āṟṟu iṭi nīr nītta vaṟum cuṉi
  • {KALI 3-9} aṭiyoṭu vāṭiya aṇi malar takippaṉa
  • {KALI 3-10} valli nī tuṟappāy ēl vaki vāṭum ivaḷ eṉa
  • {KALI 3-11} ol āṅku yām irappa um uṇarntīyāy āyiṉi
  • {KALI 3-12} cellu nīḷ āṟṟu iṭi cērntu eḻunta maram vāṭa
  • {KALI 3-13} pullu viṭṭu iṟiñciya pūm koṭi takippaṉa
  • {KALI 3-14} piṇipu nī viṭal cūḻiṉ piṟaḻtarum ivaḷ eṉa
  • {KALI 3-15} paṇipu vantu irappa um pala cūḻvāy āyiṉi
  • {KALI 3-16} tuṇipu nī cela kaṇṭa āṟṟu iṭi a marattu
  • {KALI 3-17} aṇi cela vāṭiya am taḷir takippaṉa
  • {KALI 3-18} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 3-19} yām niṉ kūṟa um ema koḷḷāy āyiṉi
  • {KALI 3-20} āṉātu ivaḷ pōl aruḷ vantavi kāṭṭi
  • {KALI 3-21} mēl niṉṟu mey kūṟum kēḷir pōl nī cellum
  • {KALI 3-22} kāṉam takippa celavu

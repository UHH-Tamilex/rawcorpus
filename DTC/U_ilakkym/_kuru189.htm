{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Setting out today, the arrival will be tomorrow,
  2. so that the white chariot advances like a waterfall falling from the hill,
  3. so that the glowing wheel, shining like the young crescent moon,
  4. cuts down fresh corn like a brand descending from the sky,
  5. by going as [swift] as the wind reaching [her] by evening,
  6. the little woman with pure bangles in few rows,
  7. [and] uniting [with her] bosom of many charms(?)[FNote_751], we will rejoice.

Altern

7b: [and] uniting [with her], so that it will be very glorious, we will rejoice.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O young woman of soft nature,
  • with shining armlets in a choice row [and] with good forehead as
  • Arumaṉ's famous Ciṟukuṭi with old houses,
  • where the sharp-beaked female of the cruel-eyed crow resides(?),
  • embracing [its] young ones with shivering wings, calling [its] relatives,
  • [all of them][FNote_1023] crowding the good house rich in food,
  • on short stilts, in order to seize along with the offering of the fearful deity(?)[FNote_1024],
  • [His] young [attendants] have come, wearing
  • cool fragrant garlands of blossom [and] sprouts unfolding
  • of the jasmine with fragrant blossoms, linked with blue water-lilies,
  • [just] like [those] in your rich dark hair,
  • and our man
  • will come too, speedily[FNote_1025],
  • impelling [his] broad-maned good horses,
  • on this day of falling dews.

Kalittoki

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 121-1} oḷ cuṭar kal cēra ulaku ūrum takiyatu
  • {KALI 121-2} teḷ kaṭal aḻuvattu tiri nīkkā eḻutarūum
  • {KALI 121-3} taṇ katir matiyattu aṇi nilā niṟittara
  • {KALI 121-4} puḷ iṉam iri mānti pukal cēra oli āṉṟu
  • {KALI 121-5} vaḷ itaḻ kūmpiya maṇi maruḷ irum kaḻi
  • {KALI 121-6} paḷḷi pukkatu pōlum parappu nīr taṇ cērppa
  • {KALI 121-7} tāṅka arum kāmatti taṇantu nī puṟam māṟa
  • {KALI 121-8} tūṅku nīr imiḻ tiri tuṇi ākīi olikkum ē
  • {KALI 121-9} uṟiyoṭu vikiya pōtu pōl oyyeṉa
  • {KALI 121-10} niṟi āṉātu iḻitarūum nīr nīntu kaṇṇāṭku
  • {KALI 121-11} vārāy nī puṟam māṟa varuntiya mēṉiyāṭku
  • {KALI 121-12} ār iruḷ tuṇi āki aci vaḷi alikkum ē
  • {KALI 121-13} kamaḻ taṇ tātu utirntu uka ūḻ uṟṟa kōṭal vī
  • {KALI 121-14} itaḻ cōrum kuli pōla iṟi nīvu vaḷiyāṭku
  • {KALI 121-15} iṉ tuṇi nī nīppa iraviṉ uḷ tuṇi āki
  • {KALI 121-16} taṉ tuṇi pirintu ayāam taṉi kuruku ucāvum ē
  • {KALI 121-17} oḷ cuṭar ñāyiṟṟu viḷakkattāṉ oḷi cāmpum
  • {KALI 121-18} naṇpakal matiyam pōl nalam cāynta aṇiyāṭku
  • {KALI 121-19} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 121-20} eṟi tiri tantiṭa iḻinta mīṉ iṉ tuṟi
  • {KALI 121-21} maṟi tiri varuntāmal koṇṭāṅku neṟi tāḻntu
  • {KALI 121-22} cāyiṉaḷ varuntiyāḷ iṭumpi
  • {KALI 121-23} pāy pari kaṭum tiṇ tēr kaḷiyiṉ ō iṭaṉ ē

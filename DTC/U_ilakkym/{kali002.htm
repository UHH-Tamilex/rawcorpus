  • {KALI 2-13} niliiya kaṟpiṉāḷ nī nīppiṉ vāḻātāḷ

  • {KALI 2-15} il eṉa irantōrkku oṉṟu īyāmi iḻivu eṉa
  • {KALI 2-16} kal iṟantu ceyal cūḻnta poruḷ poruḷ ākum ō
  • {KALI 2-17} tol iyal vaḻāami tuṇi eṉa puṇarntavaḷ
  • {KALI 2-18} pul ākam piriyāmi poruḷ āyiṉ allati
  • {KALI 2-19} iṭaṉ iṉṟi irantōrkku oṉṟu īyāmi iḻivu eṉa
  • {KALI 2-20} kaṭaṉ iṟantu ceyal cūḻnta poruḷ poruḷ ākum ō

  • {KALI 2-23} eṉa ivaḷ

  • {KALI 2-28} tol kaviṉ tolital añci eṉ

{{Readable Translation}}

  • It [is] in the woodlands,
  • where the milk-selling shepherd, palm-mat put on [his] back,
  • folding together with skin the bag(?) [and] churning-wood,
  • the many-threaded soft net taken in hand,
  • on the last days of resplendent rining poured forth broken from the clouds,
  • abides[FNote_449], securing the multitude of small heads
  • by [his] drooping sound[FNote_450] in a restrined state, [his] leg placed on the staff,
  • while one side becomes wet with many fine drops,
  • - the village where the soft-natured little woman resides,
  • with [her] fidelity worthy of jasmine[FNote_451],
  • that rejoices[FNote_452] when a guest comes, be it even at night,
  • in unfailing fertility.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. kavitali yeṇkiṉ parumayi rēṟṟi
  2. yiritēr vēṭkiyi ṉiraviṟ pōki
  3. nīṭuceyaṟ citalit tōṭupuṇin teṭutta
  4. varavāḻ puṟṟa moḻiya voyyeṉa
  5. muravāy vaḷḷuki riṭappa vāṅku ((5))
  6. mūkkaruṅ kavali nīnti maṟṟivaḷ
  7. pūppō luṇkaṇ putunalañ citiya
  8. vilaṅkunīr vārak kaṇṭun
  9. takumō peruma tavirkanuñ celavē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Crossing[FNote_948] the crossroad difficult to pass(?),
  • where the huge-furred male of the bear with overhanging head,
  • going at night in desire to search for prey,
  • bends to root up[FNote_949] with [its] sharp nils with broken tips,
  • suddenly(?), so that the anthill, inhabited by a snake, perishes,
  • rised decoratively by the multitude of white ants in long action,
  • - is it fitting, o great one
  • though having seen that obstructing water overflows,
  • so that [her] new integrity is wasted, her flower-like collyrium eyes?
  • Let your going be restrined.

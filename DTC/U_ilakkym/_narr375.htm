  1. nīṭuciṉaip puṉṉai naṟuntā tutirak
  2. kōṭupuṉai kurukiṉ ṟōṭutalaip peyarum
  3. palpūṅ kāṉaṉ malkunīrc cērppa
  4. vaṉpilai yātaliṟ ṟaṉpula ṉayanta
  5. veṉṉu nāṇu naṉṉuta luvappa ((5))
  6. varuvai yāyiṉō naṉṟē peruṅkaṭa
  7. liravuttalai maṇṭilam peyarnteṉa vuravuttirai
  8. yeṟivaṉa pōla varūu
  9. muyarmaṇaṟ paṭappaiyem muṟaivi ṉūrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Since you have no love,
  • o man from the coast of rising water by the seashore grove of many flowers,
  • where the mass of herons decorating the branches moves [their] heads[FNote_1040],
  • while fragrant pollen drops from the long-branched Puṉṉai tree,
  • if you come [nevertheless?], so that [she with] good forehead rejoices,
  • who is ashamed even with me that longed for her opinion(?),
  • it [will be] good,
  • - to the village of our residence in the garden with high sand,
  • where the strong waves are coming as if they were dashing,
  • because the [moon] disk is moving in the night[FNote_1041], in the great sea.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. This[FNote_584] little home with [its] ghat is no place to stay,
  2. with its long nights, with sleepy-eyed people
  3. who do not inquire after those[FNote_585] that remin sleepless[FNote_586]
  4. at midnight, troubled by unending grief,
  5. struck with the cruelty of the man from the coast of shore groves.

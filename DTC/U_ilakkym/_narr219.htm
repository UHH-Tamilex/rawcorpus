{{Metrical text}}

  1. kaṇṇun tōḷun taṇṇaṟuṅ katuppum
  2. paḻanala miḻantu pacali pāya
  3. viṉṉuyir perumpiṟi tāyiṉu meṉṉatūm
  4. pulaveṉ vāḻi tōḻi ciṟukā
  5. lalavaṉoṭu peyarum pulavuttiri naḷikaṭaṟ ((5))
  6. perumīṉ koḷḷuñ ciṟukuṭip paratavar
  7. kaṅkuṉ māṭṭiya kaṉikati roṇcuṭar
  8. mutirā ñāyir ṟetiroḷi kaṭukkuṅ
  9. kāṉalam peruntuṟic cērppaṉ
  10. ṟāṉē yāṉē puṇarnta māṟē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if [my] sweet life becomes greatly different,[FNote_696]
  • when eyes and shoulder and cool fragrant hir
  • have lost [their] olf integritiy [and] pallor has spread,
  • I won't be vexed at all, live, friend,
  • becuse he and I[FNote_697] have united,
  • the man from the coast of big ghats with seashore groves,
  • where the bright light with intense beam kindled at night
  • by the fishermen in [their] small hamlets, that take big fish
  • from the vast sea with fish-reeking waves that returns with small-legged crabs,
  • resembles the reflected brightness of the rising sun.

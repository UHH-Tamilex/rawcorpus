{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Very few [were] the days the man from the ghat granted,
  2. where the ocean sounds like a drum, the mouth to a drumstick,
  3. so that the little houses of crabs are destroyed,
  4. sleeping places in the root holes of small-blossomed Ñāḻal trees. Gossip!
  5. It is louder than the roaring of bustling Kuṟumpūr[FNote_1334],
  6. which has seen the state, resembling the tiger looks [exchanged]
  7. by bards[FNote_1335], at a time when the great son of the Vicciyar[FNote_1336]
  8. with the bow-armed army fought with the kings.

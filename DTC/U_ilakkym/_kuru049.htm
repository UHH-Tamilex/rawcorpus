{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O man from a coast of great waters like the lustre of sapphires
  2. where [are] Muṇṭakam shrubs, [their] pollen ripening, like squirrels' teeth,
  3. even when, after this state has changed, another state should come,[FNote_213]
  4. may you be my husband,
  5. let me be the one to suit to your heart.

Altern

5b: let me be the one to whom your heart consents.

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 18-1} uṇmiṉ kaḷ ē aṭumiṉ cōṟu ē
  • {PATI 18-2} eṟika tiṟṟi ēṟṟumiṉ puḻukku ē
  • {PATI 18-3} varunarkku variyātu polam kalam teḷirppa
  • {PATI 18-4} iruḷ vaṇar olivarum puri aviḻ aimpāl
  • {PATI 18-5} entu kōṭṭu alkul mukiḻ naki maṭavaral
  • {PATI 18-6} kūntal viṟaliyar vaḻaṅkuka aṭuppu ē
  • {PATI 18-7} peṟṟatu utavumiṉ tappu iṉṟu piṉ um
  • {PATI 18-8} maṉ uyir aḻiya yāṇṭu pala tuḷakki
  • {PATI 18-9} maṇ uṭi ñālam puravu etirkoṇṭa
  • {PATI 18-10} taṇ iyal eḻili taliyātu māṟi
  • {PATI 18-11} māri poykkuvatu āyiṉum
  • {PATI 18-12} cēralātaṉ poyyalaṉ naci ē

{{Metrical text}}

  1. pullēṉ makiḻna pulattalu milaṉē
  2. kallā yāṉik kaṭuntērc ceḻiyaṉ
  3. paṭimāṇ peruṅkuḷa maṭinīr viṭṭeṉak
  4. kālaṇin tetiriya kaṇikkōṭṭu vāḷi
  5. yaḷḷalaṅ kaḻaṉi yuḷvā yōṭip ((5))
  6. pakaṭucē ṟutitta puḷḷiveṇ puṟattuc
  7. ceñcā luḻavar kōṟpuṭi matarip
  8. piṅkāṟ ceṟuvi ṉaṉimutaṟ puraḷum
  9. vāṇaṉ ciṟukuṭi yaṉṉaveṉ
  10. kōṉē relvaḷi ñekiḻtta nummē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • I won't embrace [you], delightful man, and I'm not at all vexed
  • with you who made my fine light bangles in a row(?) lose,
  • like Vāṇaṉ's Ciṟukuṭi,
  • where the wet base of the field with fresh [water-]channels(?) is deranged,
  • flourishing(?) when struck(?) by the stick of the ploughmen in the red furrows,
  • with [their] white backs full of dots kicked [up] from the mud by the bulls,
  • the round-finned Vāḷi fish faced with [and] touched by [their] legs
  • running into the inner parts(?) of the miry field,
  • since drinking(?) water had been let out from the big pond of one who is glorious for [his] weapons(?), Ceḻiyaṉ with quick chariot [and] untught elephants(?).[FNote_974]

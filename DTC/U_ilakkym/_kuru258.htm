{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Don't come to our street! Don't give your garland!
  2. It seems that gossip has arisen, o great one,
  3. at the sight of her blameless, glorious integrity being lost,
  4. like [the loss? of] Ārkāṭu, [the town of] Aḻici,[FNote_1045]
  5. the great son[FNote_1046] with young [warriors?] with bright swords placed in a row,
  6. on the hunt for good things(?) for food furnished with toddy[FNote_1047],
  7. father of Cēntaṉ with high-tusked elephants,
  8. fettered to the Arjuna trees[FNote_1048] of the big Kāveri ghat where many play.

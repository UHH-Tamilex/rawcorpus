{{Metrical text}}

  1. maṟattaṟ karitāṟ pāka paṉṉāḷ
  2. vaṟattoṭu varuntiya valkutoḻiṟ koḷīiya
  3. paḻamaḻai poḻinta putunī raravattu
  4. nānavil palkiḷai kaṟaṅka nāvuṭai
  5. maṇiyoli kēḷāḷ vāṇuta lataṉā ((5))
  6. leḵkumi ṉeṉṟa viḷaiyar vallē
  7. yilakat taṟiyuna rāka melleṉa
  8. maṇṇuk kūntaṉ mācaṟak kaḻīic
  9. cilpōtu koṇṭu palkura laḻuttiya
  10. vaṉṉilai pukutaliṉ meyvarut tuṟāa ((10))
  11. vaviḻpū muṭiyiṉaḷ kavaiiya
  12. maṭamā varivai makiḻntayar nilaiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Difficult to forget indeed, charioteer,
  • the state of immersing in delight of the youthful dark woman,
  • who embraced [me], she with a hairknot with opening flowers,
  • [her] body having not suffered, at [my] entering [during] the hasty state[FNote_175]
  • when she inlayed [her] many locks with few flowers,
  • having washed free from dust [her] softly bathed tresses,
  • while because of that the youngsters inside the house,
  • who had been told 'get up' knew [it] quickly(?),[FNote_176]
  • - [she of] pure forehead who could not hear the tongued bell,
  • while the many tongue-uttering relations of the snake
  • were sounding in the new water come from the old rains(?),
  • having taken up [their] nightly work after suffering with drought for many days.

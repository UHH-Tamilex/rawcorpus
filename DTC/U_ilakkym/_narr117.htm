{{Readable Translation}}

  • I won't live, friend,
  • for many days, if they pass by without his thinking that he will be thus [too?],
  • on sorrowful evening that has come with loneliness
  • so that one shivers, affliction settling, since the curved disk is situated[FNote_381]
  • in the abode of stones, having become red so that the going glow is [but] blunt,
  • while the great sea thunders, the seahore grove is blossoming,
  • while the flood from the dark backwaters traverses [and] floods the houses,
  • while the strong-petalled blue water-lilies close, while the birds together
  • join [their] nests in the grove of fragrant flowers.
  • The say that the illness in me [is] something different.
  • It is not, however(?), of a kind that has an other cuse.

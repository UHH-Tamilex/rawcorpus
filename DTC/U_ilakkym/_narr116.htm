{{Metrical text}}

  1. tīmai kaṇṭōr tiṟattum periyōr
  2. tāmaṟin tuṇarka veṉpa mātō
  3. vaḻuvap piṇṭa nāppa ṇēmuṟ
  4. ṟiruveti rīṉṟa vēṟṟalaik koḻumuḷai
  5. cūṉmutir maṭappiṭi nāṇmēya lāru ((5))
  6. malaikeḻu nāṭaṉ kēṇmai palaviṉ
  7. mācciṉai tuṟanta kōṇmutir perumpaḻam
  8. viṭaraḷai vīḻntuk kāṅkut toṭarpaṟac
  9. cēṇuñ ceṉṟuk kaṉṟē yaṟiyā
  10. tēka laṭukkat tiruṇmukai yirunta ((10))
  11. kuṟiñci nallūrp peṇṭi
  12. riṉṉu mōvā reṉṟiṟat talarē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Great ones, even when they have seen mischief/evil(?)
  • will know [and] perceive for themselves" - don't they say so?
  • The intimacy with the man from the mountainous land,
  • where the highly pregnant youthful elephant cow becomes full on grazing by day on the rich shoots on top of the spears brought forth by the big bamboo,
  • ...?,
  • as the big fruit ripe to be taken abandoned by the big brach of the jackfruit tree that is shed, falling into a cleft[FNote_379], it has gone into the distance[FNote_380],
  • so that attachment subsides, [and] been dropped. Without knowing
  • the women of the good village with Kuṟiñci shrubs,
  • on which there were dark buds, on the mountain side with big stones,
  • even now don't stop [their] gossip on my account.

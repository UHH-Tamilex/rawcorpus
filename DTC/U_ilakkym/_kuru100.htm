{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Difficult to unite with [were] the big bamboo shoulders.[FNote_405]
  2. She came, inexperience [herself][FNote_406], like the image
  3. on the western [side of] mount Kolli, [belonging to] Ōri[FNote_407] with strong bow,
  4. where [people] eat having sold the tooth of the swift-eyed elephant
  5. when they are hungry in the small homes with fences of Malabar lilies,
  6. plucking green bowstring hemp with large-leaved wild jasmine[FNote_408]
  7. [and] sowing wild rice in the area(?) of waterfalls.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the coast of the strong water, where the cold wind throws up,
  • groupwise, like fine cloth waving [in the air],
  • the great dunes rich with thundering waves,
  • while you have not realised [it],[FNote_59]
  • haven newly[FNote_60] eaten our flowerlike goodness, - as for us,
  • we have let go also of shame,
  • which came with us all this way[FNote_61],
  • like an inexperienced [woman] of spotless fidelity
  • lets go of [her] infant in order to appease an evil spirit[FNote_62]
  • enduring while [our] consenting heart endures.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Besides [our] having to bear that he isn't close,
  2. when [we are] exhusted, having become like a bruised sprout,
  3. with troubling pin, which grew bit by bit like the waxing crescent moon
  4. [and] loosened the bangles from [our] joints,
  5. - the rin too, friend, has got confused.[FNote_1188]
  6. This noisy village feels pity for us,
  7. more than we feel pity for him,
  8. even with eyes[FNote_1189] not closing in the fallen shower[FNote_1190].

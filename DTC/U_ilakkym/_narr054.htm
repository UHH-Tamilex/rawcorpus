{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if you, after having grazed the curved waters(?)[FNote_209], moving amon [your] flock, are longing for leaping flight, [you with] pure wings,
  • stay a little [longer] with your flock to eat big flesh,
  • blacke-legged white heron, [and] listen to what [I] say:
  • very lonely is this small empty evening. When you know that you really(?)[FNote_210]
  • do possess love. Without taking on an indifferent heart
  • speak so that [he] realises (?)[FNote_211] my request,
  • to the lord of your ghat with mangrove fences,
  • where the sapphire backs of the clear waves rub
  • on the miden Ñāḻal[FNote_212] trees that have budded sprouts to be plucked by those who wear foliage.

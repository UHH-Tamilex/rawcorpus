{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Like flowers, like arrows that are fit to flit
  2. - they have cused pin for all to know,
  3. the big rin-eyes of her who chases birds
  4. away from the millet into which cotton is sown[FNote_296]
  5. on the big mountin, [she with] honey[-sweet] words [and] round tender shoulders.

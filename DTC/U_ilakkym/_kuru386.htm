{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The man from the cool ghat by the seashore grove full of blossoms,
  2. spread out in the white sand, before[FNote_1533] he departed,
  3. I was knowing[FNote_1534] certinly [the kind of] evening[FNote_1535], when women with pure jewels
  4. combine the festive ornaments. That[FNote_1536] evening
  5. possesses loneliness, together with sorrow
  6. as if it covered the earth, I didn't know.

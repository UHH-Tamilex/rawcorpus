{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Earlier when [he] said 'we are going, we are going!'[FNote_1323],
  2. although thinking that his going would be fake,
  3. I said 'may that side [of him] pass permanently.'(?)[FNote_1324] Alas!
  4. Where is he, my father who would be [my] support?
  5. A big pond, where black-legged white egrets are grazing,
  6. came into being, as [the place] between my breasts filled [with tears].

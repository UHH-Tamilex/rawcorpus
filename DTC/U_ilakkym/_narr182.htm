{{Readable Translation}}

  • Moonlight has been hidden and darkness has come
  • and mother has fallen asleep in excellent prosperity,
  • guarding us like [her] pupil[FNote_589]
  • within the borders of [this] picture-like place.
  • Up, friend, softly-softly, let us see[FNote_590],
  • the dew-afflicted state of him who has come alone
  • as an elephant bull with poor head,[FNote_591]
  • left by [his] guards below and above,[FNote_592]
  • embrace [him] so as to settle on [his] good chest,
  • accepted [back] like a good ornament that had been destroyed,
  • [and] come [back].

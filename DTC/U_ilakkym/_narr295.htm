{{Readable Translation}}

  • The attendants were upset(?),[FNote_895]
  • with [their] hanging dark tresses coming into sprout, perishing outside
  • as the crushed Vaḷḷi creeper on the broken(?) mountin side,
  • and mother knew it.
  • In guarding she immersed, with difficult protection.
  • Let us go - may you live - [and] grow old,
  • so that our young integrity stays behind within the house,
  • as a jar of toddy, bustlingly drunk(?)
  • from the big ghat, where the ships with many tasks appear,
  • come with the wind from many other lands of my father.[FNote_896]

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 75-1} irum puli koṉṟu perum kaḷiṟu aṭūum
  • {PATI 75-2} arum poṟi vaya māṉ aṉiyi pal vēl
  • {PATI 75-3} polam tār yāṉi iyal tēr poṟiya
  • {PATI 75-4} vēntar um vēḷir um piṟar um kīḻppaṇintu
  • {PATI 75-5} niṉ vaḻippaṭāar āyiṉ nel mikku
  • {PATI 75-6} aṟi uṟu karumpiṉ tīm cēṟṟu yāṇar
  • {PATI 75-7} varunar variyā vaḷam vīṅku irukki
  • {PATI 75-8} vaṉpulam taḻīi meṉpāl tōṟum
  • {PATI 75-9} arum paṟi viṉiñar pul ikal paṭuttu
  • {PATI 75-10} kaḷ uṭi niyamattu oḷ vili koṭukkum
  • {PATI 75-11} veḷ varaku uḻuta koḷ uṭi karampi
  • {PATI 75-12} cennel valci aṟiyār tam tam
  • {PATI 75-13} pāṭal cāṉṟa vippiṉ
  • {PATI 75-14} nāṭu uṭaṉ āḷtal yāvaṇatu avarkku ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The chariot of the man from the cool ghat,
  • where the poor female heron whose virginity had been eaten
  • by the broad-footed male, feathers dense in a mass
  • just as the sweet mass kneaded from the content of the dancing bamboo,
  • without eating the small fish from the sides of the stirring backwater
  • abides with loneliness in the swaying Kaiti branch,
  • certinly it was enjoyable just to see it with eyes. Full of shame,
  • I do not obtin sleep even at deepest midnight.
  • My heart that has gined complete[FNote_577] clarity has come to breaking
  • while listening to the sound of the birds, thinking of [his chariot's] bells.

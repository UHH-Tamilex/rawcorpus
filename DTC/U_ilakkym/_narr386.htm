{{Readable Translation}}

  • At the time he came to say "marriage", humbly,
  • - the man from a land, where the wrathful male
  • of the small-eyed pig is sleeping on the bamboo-grown slope,
  • without fear, by the Vēṅki tree[FNote_1056] in [its] resting place within a cave,
  • there, after having fed on the millet with gleaming ears
  • ploughed for by the forest people with chaplets with thick nectar, -
  • while our stony little hamlet glittered,
  • I wondered at what I had understood, friend, saying:
  • "O you, saying "I give a rare oath possessing the plague(?)[FNote_1057]",
  • your people are such as not to adhere to these [words]".

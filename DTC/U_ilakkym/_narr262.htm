{{Readable Translation}}

  • Severe indeed, alas, [is] the abuse(?) of youth[FNote_822],
  • when the heart speaks of separation,[FNote_823]
  • as male work is compelling, even leaving her
  • with honey words [and] tresses that are fragrant of round-stemmed
  • blue water-lilies, with beauty spots that budded on [her] bamboo shoulder,
  • who will bear hateful affliction, her mind abounding in disgust,
  • [her] good grace being exhusted while afflicted by the shivering disease,
  • at midnight when the village sleeps, perturbed by drops,
  • so that the eye-like dark blossoms of the Karuviḷi in the cool fields
  • come into swinging with the northwind as the feathers of a dancing peacock.

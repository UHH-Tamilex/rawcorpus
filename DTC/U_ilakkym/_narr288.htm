{{Readable Translation}}

  • After mother, having seen[FNote_881] pallor spread on [my] good forehead,
  • since he had gone into separation, the man from the hilly land,
  • where the fan[-tailed] peacock is dancing with [its] female
  • on a high branch, enjoying the young sunlight above
  • on the long awe-inspiring peak where the waterfall sounds,
  • tarries in front of rice[FNote_882] along with straight old women,
  • when we'll hear the divination,
  • though we [just] chased away the little parrots
  • from the milky bent ears of the red millet on the mountain,
  • will it say "that [is] a plague of this great Murukaṉ"?

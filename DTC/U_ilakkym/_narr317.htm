{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the land of high mountins,
  • where the green parrot with a coral-red beak seizes
  • the fresh millet with lengthening ears bending the heads to [their] leaves,
  • resembling the rised trunk of the flower-dotted male [elephant]
  • united with [its] female on the long big mountin side,
  • if mother should know of [your] intimacy with her you longed for,
  • what will become of them[FNote_930], mingling with dew,
  • - the eyes that resembled the big blossoms with dark petals
  • of the blue water-lilies plucked with the companions,
  • playing in the sweet mountin spring on the slope of my father's high mountin.

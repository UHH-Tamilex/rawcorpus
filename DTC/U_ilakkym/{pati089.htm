Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 89-1} vāṉam poḻutoṭu curappa kāṉam
  • {PATI 89-2} tōṭu uṟu maṭa māṉ ēṟu puṇarntu iyala
  • {PATI 89-3} puḷ um miñiṟu um mā ciṉai ārppa
  • {PATI 89-4} paḻaṉ um kiḻaṅku um micaiyaṟavu aṟiyātu
  • {PATI 89-5} pal āṉ nal nirai pul aruntu ukaḷa
  • {PATI 89-6} payam kaṭai aṟiyā vaḷam keḻu ciṟappiṉ
  • {PATI 89-7} perum pal yāṇar kūlam keḻuma
  • {PATI 89-8} nal pal ūḻi naṭuvu niṉṟu oḻuka
  • {PATI 89-9} pal vēl irum poṟai niṉ kōl cemmaiyiṉ
  • {PATI 89-10} nāḷiṉ nāḷiṉ nāṭu toḻutu ētta
  • {PATI 89-11} uyarnilai ulakattu uyarntōr parava
  • {PATI 89-12} araciyal piḻaiyātu ceru mēntōṉṟi
  • {PATI 89-13} nōy ilai ākiyar nī ē niṉ māṭṭu
  • {PATI 89-14} aṭaṅkiya neñcam pukarpaṭupu aṟiyātu
  • {PATI 89-15} kaṉaviṉum piriyā uṟaiyuḷoṭu taṇṇeṉa
  • {PATI 89-16} takaram nīviya tuvarā kūntal
  • {PATI 89-17} vatuvai makaḷir nōkkiṉar peyarntu
  • {PATI 89-18} vāḻ nāḷ aṟiyum vayaṅku cuṭar nōkkattu
  • {PATI 89-19} mīṉoṭu puraiyum kaṟpiṉ
  • {PATI 89-20} vāḷ nutal arivaiyoṭu kāṇvara polintu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from the fertile mountin,
  • where the bee that played in the fragrant pollen
  • of the Naṟavu, odorous [even] at a distance, with red-lined petals,
  • as if drawn with vermilion on the back of a pretty conch,
  • obtins the good colour of a stone rubbed with fresh brilliant gold[FNote_100],
  • after staying [and] chasing away with us yesterday
  • the parakeets from the little millet, coming in flocks[FNote_101],
  • he turned away without getting an opportunity to speak.
  • This turns[FNote_102] to love[FNote_103], nothing else, friend.
  • The thought of my doing something unnatural has come up,
  • compelled by [the one with] anklets and armlets,[FNote_104]
  • even[FNote_105] when he had an unobstructed sight [of me], just like a bee
  • that is not filled up after searching a bud in the desire to eat pollen.

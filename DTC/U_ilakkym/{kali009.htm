Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 9-1} eṟittaru katir tāṅki ēntiya kuṭai nīḻal
  • {KALI 9-2} uṟi tāḻnta karakam um urai cāṉṟa mukkōl um
  • {KALI 9-3} neṟippaṭa cuval acaii vēṟu ōrā neñcattu
  • {KALI 9-4} kuṟippu ēval ceyal mālai koḷai naṭai antaṇīr
  • {KALI 9-5} ve iṭai celal mālai oḻukkattīr i iṭai
  • {KALI 9-6} eṉ makaḷ orutti um piṟaḷ makaṉ oruvaṉ um
  • {KALI 9-7} tam uḷē puṇarnta tām aṟi puṇarcciyar
  • {KALI 9-8} aṉṉār iruvarai kāṇir ō peruma
  • {KALI 9-9} kāṇēm allēm kaṇṭaṉam kaṭattu iṭai
  • {KALI 9-10} āṇ eḻil aṇṇalōṭu arum curam muṉṉiya
  • {KALI 9-11} māṇ iḻai maṭavaral tāyir nīr pōṟir
  • {KALI 9-12} pala uṟu naṟum cāntam paṭuppavarkku allatai
  • {KALI 9-13} malai uḷē piṟappiṉum malaikku avai tām eṉ ceyyum
  • {KALI 9-14} niṉaiyum kāl num makaḷ numakku um āṅku aṉaiyaḷ ē
  • {KALI 9-15} cīr keḻu veḷ muttam aṇipavarkku allatai
  • {KALI 9-16} nīr uḷē piṟappiṉum nīrkku avai tām eṉ ceyyum
  • {KALI 9-17} tērum kāl num makaḷ numakku um āṅku aṉaiyaḷ ē
  • {KALI 9-18} ēḻ puṇar iṉ icai muralpavarkku allatai
  • {KALI 9-19} yāḻ uḷē piṟappiṉum yāḻkku avai tām eṉ ceyyum
  • {KALI 9-20} cūḻum kāl num makaḷ numakku um āṅku aṉaiyaḷ ē
  • {KALI 9-21} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 9-22} iṟanta kaṟpiṉāṭku evvam paṭaraṉmiṉ
  • {KALI 9-23} ciṟantāṉai vaḻipaṭīi ceṉṟaṉaḷ
  • {KALI 9-24} aṟam talai piriyā āṟu um maṟṟu atu ē

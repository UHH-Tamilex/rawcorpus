Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 9-1} eṟittaru katir tāṅki ēntiya kuṭi nīḻal
  • {KALI 9-2} uṟi tāḻnta karakam um uri cāṉṟa mukkōl um
  • {KALI 9-3} neṟippaṭa cuval acii vēṟu ōrā neñcattu
  • {KALI 9-4} kuṟippu ēval ceyal māli koḷi naṭi antaṇīr
  • {KALI 9-5} ve iṭi celal māli oḻukkattīr i iṭi
  • {KALI 9-6} eṉ makaḷ orutti um piṟaḷ makaṉ oruvaṉ um
  • {KALI 9-7} tam uḷē puṇarnta tām aṟi puṇarcciyar
  • {KALI 9-8} aṉṉār iruvari kāṇir ō peruma
  • {KALI 9-9} kāṇēm allēm kaṇṭaṉam kaṭattu iṭi
  • {KALI 9-10} āṇ eḻil aṇṇalōṭu arum curam muṉṉiya
  • {KALI 9-11} māṇ iḻi maṭavaral tāyir nīr pōṟir
  • {KALI 9-12} pala uṟu naṟum cāntam paṭuppavarkku allati
  • {KALI 9-13} mali uḷē piṟappiṉum malikku avi tām eṉ ceyyum
  • {KALI 9-14} niṉiyum kāl num makaḷ numakku um āṅku aṉiyaḷ ē
  • {KALI 9-15} cīr keḻu veḷ muttam aṇipavarkku allati
  • {KALI 9-16} nīr uḷē piṟappiṉum nīrkku avi tām eṉ ceyyum
  • {KALI 9-17} tērum kāl num makaḷ numakku um āṅku aṉiyaḷ ē
  • {KALI 9-18} ēḻ puṇar iṉ ici muralpavarkku allati
  • {KALI 9-19} yāḻ uḷē piṟappiṉum yāḻkku avi tām eṉ ceyyum
  • {KALI 9-20} cūḻum kāl num makaḷ numakku um āṅku aṉiyaḷ ē
  • {KALI 9-21} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 9-22} iṟanta kaṟpiṉāṭku evvam paṭaraṉmiṉ
  • {KALI 9-23} ciṟantāṉi vaḻipaṭīi ceṉṟaṉaḷ
  • {KALI 9-24} aṟam tali piriyā āṟu um maṟṟu atu ē

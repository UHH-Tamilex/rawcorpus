{{Readable Translation}}

  1. [Would it be] a mistake, then[FNote_1385], if you stayed[FNote_1386],
  2. restraining [and] bringing to a stop(?)[FNote_1387] by the sand [dune] long as a mountain
  3. the long chariot with the handle, coming forward adorned with jewels(?)[FNote_1388],
  4. in our little good village with the great water for a fence,
  5. there, where in the winding(?) backwaters
  6. the glittering wave sounds sweetly(?)[FNote_1389], close to(?)[FNote_1390] the Tāḻai tree,
  7. so that loneliness may leave her with a hip hung with foliage?

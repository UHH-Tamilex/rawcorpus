{{Readable Translation}}

  • When it is appropriate for the male(?)[FNote_324] to speak of desire
  • at a time where [I] perish from passion[FNote_325] that was stirred up, slapped by pain,
  • while I just(?)[FNote_326] endure the coolness of my womanhood,
  • the man from the coast of fish-reeking water where the Puṉṉai tree has budded
  • in heaped clusters that resemble unbathed green pearls,
  • not washed to obtain beauty by the artisan skilled in [his] hands,
  • what kind of man is he, friend,
  • - who does not know of those who are plagued by [his] chest,
  • having become full of affection for him?

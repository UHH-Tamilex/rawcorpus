  2. pāmpūr matiyi ṉutaloḷi karappavu

  4. ṉuyirpakut taṉṉa māṇpiṉē ṉākali
  5. ṉatukaṇ ṭiciṉāl yāṉē yeṉṟunaṉi ((5))
  6. yaḻuta lāṉṟici ṉāyiḻai yolikura
  7. lēṉal kāvali ṉiṭaiyuṟ ṟoruvaṉ
  8. kaṇṇiyaṉ kaḻalaṉ ṟāraṉ ṟaṇṇeṉac
  9. ciṟupuṟaṅ kavaiyiṉa ṉāka vataṟkoṇ
  10. ṭaḵtē niṉainta neñcamo ((10))
  11. ṭiḵtā kiṉriyā ṉuṟṟa nōyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Although [my] body is emaciated as the glow of a flame at midday,
  • although the brightness of [my] forehead is hidden as the moon crept over by the snake, you have become one who does not talk to me. While
  • I am glorious as if I had distributed life to you,
  • I see that indeed." Leave off saying [thus
  • and] crying abundantly, o you of choice jewels. When someone,
  • having slipped between the watch of the millet with sprouting ears,
  • one with a chaplet, one with an anklet, one with a garland
  • embraced [my] waist[FNote_413] coolly - taking in that,
  • with a heart that tought "that [is it]" -
  • this has become the pain I had.[FNote_414]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. For noble men there is no lying on the part of a knowing witness.[FNote_738]
  2. Beware coming near the little homes when going[FNote_739]!
  3. At the seashore grove where the inexperienced[FNote_740] dughters
  4. of fishermen with fine nets throw out the nets of [their] eyes
  5. - [the dughters who are the very] images[FNote_741] of [women with] hir knots, glorious like the eyes in a peacock's [til] -
  6. there it has stayed behind, the heart for which glory is suitable(?)[FNote_742],
  7. after that has happened [to it][FNote_743] without [my] thinking: glorious [is] this for it.

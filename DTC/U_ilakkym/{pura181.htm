((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PU 181-1} maṉṟa viḷaviṉ maṉai vīḻ veḷḷil
  • {PU 181-2} karum kaṇ eyiṟṟi kātal makaṉoṭu
  • {PU 181-3} kāṉa irum piṭi kaṉṟu talaikkoḷḷum
  • {PU 181-4} perum kuṟumpu uṭutta vaṉpulam irukkai
  • {PU 181-5} pulāa ampiṉ pōr arum kaṭi miḷai
  • {PU 181-6} valāarōṉ ē vāy vāḷ paṇṇaṉ
  • {PU 181-7} uṇṇā vaṟum kaṭumpu uytal vēṇṭiṉ
  • {PU 181-8} iṉṉē ceṉmati nī ē ceṉṟu avaṉ
  • {PU 181-9} pakai pulam paṭarā aḷavai niṉ
  • {PU 181-10} paci pakai paricil kāṭṭiṉai koḷaṟku ē

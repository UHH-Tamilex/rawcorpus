{{Readable Translation}}

  1. If fearing scandal desire will grow weary.
  2. If letting censure subside there is shame.[FNote_452]
  3. It [is] like the splintering [of the branch][FNote_453] full of bark[FNote_454],
  4. bent without reaching the ground when the elephant bull bows [it]
  5. - see, friend, my integrity he has eaten.

2b: if letting go of it (the desire) for censure to subside ...

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even though he does live in the same village, he doesn't come to [my] street,
  2. even when he comes to the street he doesn't embrace [me] fully
  3. - he[FNote_943] passes [me] by indeed without seeing,
  4. as if [passing] a crematory ground for strangers[FNote_944],
  5. so that [my] desire, which has killed shame [and] lost good knowledge[FNote_945],
  6. gets into a distance, gone like an arrow shot from the bow.

Altern: T.V.G.

4-6b {--!}while it gets into a distance, gone like an arrow shot from the bow, {--!}[his] desire which has killed [his] shame [and] lost good knowledge.

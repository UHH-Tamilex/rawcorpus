{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In the long white light of the old moon
  2. the man from a land, where the wide-mouthed frog sounds
  3. like the bird-chasing drum, in the narrow-mouthed mountain pool
  4. like a sapphire pot filled with toddy[FNote_771],
  5. indeed united[FNote_772] with [her of] long shoulders.
  6. Today the jasmine buds are fragrant.

Altern: T.V.G.

4-6b {--!}In the previous month, in the long white moonlight {--!}he united [with me] of long shoulders. {--!}Even today[FNote_773] they (my shoulders) are fragrant with jasmine buds.[FNote_774]

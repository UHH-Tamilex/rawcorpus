  • {KALI 23-1} ilaṅku oḷi maruppiṉ kimmā uḷampunar
  • {KALI 23-2} pulam kaṭi kavaṇiyiṉ pūm ciṉi utirkkum
  • {KALI 23-3} vilaṅku mali vempiya pōkku aru ve curam
  • {KALI 23-4} taṉi ē iṟappa yāṉ oḻintu iruttal
  • {KALI 23-5} nakutakkaṉṟu i aḻuṅkal ūrkku ē
  • {KALI 23-6} iṉi yāṉ
  • {KALI 23-7} uṇṇal um uṇṇēṉ vāḻal um vāḻēṉ
  • {KALI 23-8} tōḷ nalam uṇṭu tuṟakkappaṭṭōr
  • {KALI 23-9} vēḷ nīr uṇṭa kuṭi ōraṉṉar
  • {KALI 23-10} nalkunar purintu nalaṉ uṇappaṭṭōr
  • {KALI 23-11} alkunar pōkiya ūr ōraṉṉar
  • {KALI 23-12} kūṭiṉar purintu kuṇaṉ uṇappaṭṭōr
  • {KALI 23-13} cūṭiṉar iṭṭa pū ōraṉṉar
  • {KALI 23-14} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 23-15} yāṉ um niṉ akattu aṉiyēṉ āṉātu
  • {KALI 23-16} koli ve koḷkiyoṭu nāy akappaṭuppa
  • {KALI 23-17} valivarkku amarnta maṭa māṉ pōla
  • {KALI 23-18} niṉ āṅku varūum eṉ neñciṉi
  • {KALI 23-19} eṉ āṅku vārātu ōmpiṉi koṇmē

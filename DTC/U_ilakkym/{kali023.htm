Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 23-1} ilaṅku oḷi maruppiṉ kaimmā uḷampunar
  • {KALI 23-2} pulam kaṭi kavaṇaiyiṉ pūm ciṉai utirkkum
  • {KALI 23-3} vilaṅku malai vempiya pōkku aru ve curam
  • {KALI 23-4} taṉi ē iṟappa yāṉ oḻintu iruttal
  • {KALI 23-5} nakutakkaṉṟu i aḻuṅkal ūrkku ē
  • {KALI 23-6} iṉi yāṉ
  • {KALI 23-7} uṇṇal um uṇṇēṉ vāḻal um vāḻēṉ
  • {KALI 23-8} tōḷ nalam uṇṭu tuṟakkappaṭṭōr
  • {KALI 23-9} vēḷ nīr uṇṭa kuṭai ōraṉṉar
  • {KALI 23-10} nalkunar purintu nalaṉ uṇappaṭṭōr
  • {KALI 23-11} alkunar pōkiya ūr ōraṉṉar
  • {KALI 23-12} kūṭiṉar purintu kuṇaṉ uṇappaṭṭōr
  • {KALI 23-13} cūṭiṉar iṭṭa pū ōraṉṉar
  • {KALI 23-14} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 23-15} yāṉ um niṉ akattu aṉaiyēṉ āṉātu
  • {KALI 23-16} kolai ve koḷkaiyoṭu nāy akappaṭuppa
  • {KALI 23-17} valaivarkku amarnta maṭa māṉ pōla
  • {KALI 23-18} niṉ āṅku varūum eṉ neñciṉai
  • {KALI 23-19} eṉ āṅku vārātu ōmpiṉai koṇmē

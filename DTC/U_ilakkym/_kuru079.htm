{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Did he himself stay
  2. in the little village with pretty homes near the road
  3. where female doves with voices[FNote_324] conveying loneliness call out
  4. 'oy' after having ascended the swaying twig
  5. of a wind-beaten long branch in an Ōmai tree with parched trunk
  6. eaten for its bark by the forest elephant
  7. - he who was able to leave without speaking
  8. [because of his] erroneous idea that it would be impossible to us?

6+7b {--!}[because of our] error to have said: we don't consent.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Whether you remember the great many hills
  • in the vast area, where fear comes on the crossroads of the difficult desert,
  • when the male kite moves over to [its] relations,
  • hearing the resounding voice of the Taṇṇumai drum with upturned(?) mouth
  • of men with angry looks who have linked [their] straight arrows,
  • looking to the side where new people would come,
  • or whether you look just at her bamboo shoulder with sugarcane [drawing],
  • you won't cling to one side, live, my heart.
  • Even when adopting[FNote_899] the big mountains, will it really be enjoyable to look
  • at [her] tresses that are fragrant with dark buds
  • [and] bright buds of a rareness that speaks of the unity(?)[FNote_900]
  • there in Kūṭal of Ceḻiyaṉ with golden chariot [and] good garland?

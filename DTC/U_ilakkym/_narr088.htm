{{Metrical text}}

  1. yāñcey tolviṉaik kevaṉpē tuṟṟaṉai
  2. varuntal vāḻi nīyeṉa yāñceṉ
  3. ṟuraittaṉam varuka meḻumati puṇartiraik
  4. kaṭalviḷai yamutam peyaṟkēṟ ṟāaṅ
  5. kuruki yalkuta lañcuva lutukāṇ ((5))
  6. ṭammōṉ koṭumai namvayi ṉēṟṟi
  7. nayamperi tuṭaimaiyiṟ ṟāṅkal cellātu
  8. kaṇṇī raruvi yāka
  9. vaḻumē tōḻiyavar paḻamutir kuṉṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What confusion did you have for the old work we do?
  • Rise, let's go, talk [to him and] come [back],
  • saying "don't suffer, may you live". I fear
  • [our] staying [there], melting as the ambrosia ripened by the sea
  • of uniting waves when subjected(?) to the rain. See there:
  • His fruit-ripening hill is crying, friend,
  • with waterfalls for tears,
  • unable to bear[FNote_306] because it greatly possesses longing,
  • while its master strikes at us with [his] cruelty.(?)

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In bondless[FNote_478] evening that has come just like that(?)[FNote_479]
  2. for birds and beasts to stay with loneliness,
  3. even when they urge, before locking the door that many have entered,
  4. 'Is there anybody [there still] coming?'
  5. he doesn't come, friend, our lover[FNote_480].

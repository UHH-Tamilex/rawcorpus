  • {PU 164-1} āṭu naṉi maṟanta kōṭu uyar aṭuppiṉ
  • {PU 164-2} āmpi pūppa tēmpu paci uḻavā
  • {PU 164-3} pāal iṉmiyiṉ tōloṭu tiraṅki
  • {PU 164-4} illi tūrnta pollā vaṟu muli
  • {PU 164-5} cuvittoṟu aḻūum taṉ makattu mukam nōkki
  • {PU 164-6} nīroṭu niṟinta īr itaḻ maḻi kaṇ eṉ
  • {PU 164-7} maṉiyōḷ evvam nōkki niṉii
  • {PU 164-8} niṉ paṭarnticiṉ ē nal pōr kumaṇa
  • {PU 164-9} eṉ nili aṟintaṉi āyiṉ i nili
  • {PU 164-10} toṭuttu um koḷḷātu amiyaleṉ aṭukkiya
  • {PU 164-11} paṇ ami narampiṉ pacci nal yāḻ
  • {PU 164-12} maṇ ami muḻaviṉ vayiriyar
  • {PU 164-13} iṉmi tīrkkum kuṭi piṟantōy ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What will stop[FNote_897] [it], friend?
  • He will go, they say, [our] lover,
  • hastening with a mind thinking just "work",
  • even at a time difficult to obtain, when those that are seprated are aggrieved,
  • when eminently on the height of big stones the trailing clusters
  • adorning the branches of the Koṉṟai with hollow unripe fruit blossom,
  • resembling the adornments of golden metal plates
  • that decorated the spotted face of the elephant strong in work for the king.
  • We will stay behind, they say,[FNote_898] bearing the affliction we suffer.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. uḷḷūr marāatta muḷeyiṟṟu vāva
  2. lōṅka lañciṉit tūṅkutuyil poḻutiṉ
  3. velpōrc cōḻa raḻiciyam peruṅkāṭṭu
  4. nelliyam puḷiccuvik kaṉavi yāaṅ
  5. katukaḻin taṉṟē tōḻi yavarnāṭṭup ((5))
  6. paṉiyarum puṭinta peruntāṭ puṉṉi
  7. tuṟimē yippi yīrmpuṟat tuṟikkuñ
  8. ciṟukuṭi paratavar makiḻcciyum
  9. peruntaṇ kāṉalu niṉintavap pakalē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • It passed like a dream, friend,
  • that has the sour taste of the Nelli
  • from the great wildernes of Aḻici, the Cōḻa, victorious in battle,
  • at a time when the thorn-toothed bat from the tree
  • of the inner village is in sleep, hanging on a high pretty branch,
  • - that day of thinking of the big cool seashore grove
  • and of the delight of the fishermen from the small hamlet,
  • where large-trunked Puṉṉi tree from which dewy buds have broken
  • drips on the moist backs of the shell grazing the ghat
  • in his land.

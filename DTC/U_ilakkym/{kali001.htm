Kalittoki 1: kaṭavuḷ vāḻttu

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 1-1} āṟu aṟi antaṇarkku aru maṟi pala pakarntu
  • {KALI 1-2} tēṟu nīr caṭi karantu tiripuram tī maṭuttu
  • {KALI 1-3} kūṟāmal kuṟittataṉ mēl cellum kaṭum kūḷi
  • {KALI 1-4} māṟā pōr maṇi miṭaṟṟu eṇkiyāy kēḷ iṉi
  • {KALI 1-5} paṭu paṟi pala iyampa pal uruvam peyarttu nī
  • {KALI 1-6} koṭukoṭṭi āṭum kāl kōṭu uyar akal alkul
  • {KALI 1-7} koṭi puri nucuppiṉāḷ koṇṭa cīr taruvāḷ ō
  • {KALI 1-8} maṇṭu amar pala kaṭantu matukiyāl nīṟu aṇintu
  • {KALI 1-9} paṇṭaraṅkam āṭum kāl paṇi eḻil aṇi mel tōḷ
  • {KALI 1-10} vaṇṭu araṟṟum kūntalāḷ vaḷar tūkku taruvāḷ ō
  • {KALI 1-11} koli uḻuvi tōl acii koṉṟi tār cuval puraḷa
  • {KALI 1-12} tali aṅki koṇṭu nī kāpālam āṭum kāl
  • {KALI 1-13} muli aṇinta muṟuvalāḷ muṉ pāṇi taruvāḷ ō
  • {KALI 1-14} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 1-15} pāṇi um tūkku um cīr um eṉṟu ivi
  • {KALI 1-16} māṇ iḻi arivi kāppa
  • {KALI 1-17} āṇam il poruḷ emakku amarntaṉi āṭi

Kalittokai 1: kaṭavuḷ vāḻttu

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 1-1} āṟu aṟi antaṇarkku aru maṟai pala pakarntu
  • {KALI 1-2} tēṟu nīr caṭai karantu tiripuram tī maṭuttu
  • {KALI 1-3} kūṟāmal kuṟittataṉ mēl cellum kaṭum kūḷi
  • {KALI 1-4} māṟā pōr maṇi miṭaṟṟu eṇkaiyāy kēḷ iṉi
  • {KALI 1-5} paṭu paṟai pala iyampa pal uruvam peyarttu nī
  • {KALI 1-6} koṭukoṭṭi āṭum kāl kōṭu uyar akal alkul
  • {KALI 1-7} koṭi purai nucuppiṉāḷ koṇṭa cīr taruvāḷ ō
  • {KALI 1-8} maṇṭu amar pala kaṭantu matukaiyāl nīṟu aṇintu
  • {KALI 1-9} paṇṭaraṅkam āṭum kāl paṇai eḻil aṇai mel tōḷ
  • {KALI 1-10} vaṇṭu araṟṟum kūntalāḷ vaḷar tūkku taruvāḷ ō
  • {KALI 1-11} kolai uḻuvai tōl acaii koṉṟai tār cuval puraḷa
  • {KALI 1-12} talai aṅkai koṇṭu nī kāpālam āṭum kāl
  • {KALI 1-13} mulai aṇinta muṟuvalāḷ muṉ pāṇi taruvāḷ ō
  • {KALI 1-14} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 1-15} pāṇi um tūkku um cīr um eṉṟu ivai
  • {KALI 1-16} māṇ iḻai arivai kāppa
  • {KALI 1-17} āṇam il poruḷ emakku amarntaṉai āṭi

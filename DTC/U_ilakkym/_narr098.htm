  7. kallaḷip paḷḷi vatiyu nāṭa
  8. ṉenti yōmpuṅ kaṭiyuṭi viyaṉakart
  9. tuñcāk kāvala rikaḻpata nōkki
  10. yiraviṉ varūu mataṉiṉuṅ koṭitē ((10))
  11. vikalum poruta lollāk
  12. kaṇṇoṭu vārāveṉ ṉāri neñcē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Crueller than that
  • - that the man of the land, where the small-eyed pig desirous of the wet field,
  • with [its] big furry neck as the thorn of the porcupine,
  • does not fall, at the time of getting into the opening,
  • into the large trap to catch [it] by the wide field on the high mountin
  • [and], since the lizard happened [to call] on the beneighboured side
  • moves softly softly, going backwards(?)[FNote_336],
  • [and] abides in its resting place in a hole in the stones,
  • comes at night,
  • looking for the occasion when the sleepless watchmen are negligent
  • in the protected wide mansion shielded by my father,
  • [is] my bondless heart that does not come along with [my] eyes
  • that are incapable of closing even at dawn.

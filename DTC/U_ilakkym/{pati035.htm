Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 35-1} purai cāl mainta nī ōmpal māṟu ē
  • {PATI 35-2} urai cāṉṟaṉa āl perumai niṉ veṉṟi
  • {PATI 35-3} irum kaḷiṟṟu yāṉai ilaṅku vāl maruppoṭu
  • {PATI 35-4} neṭum tēr tikiri tāya viyaṉ kaḷattu
  • {PATI 35-5} aḷaku uṭai cēval kiḷai pukā āra
  • {PATI 35-6} talai tumintu eñciya mey āṭu paṟantalai
  • {PATI 35-7} anti mālai vicumpu kaṇṭaṉṉa
  • {PATI 35-8} ce cuṭar koṇṭa kuruti maṉṟattu
  • {PATI 35-9} pēey āṭum vel pōr
  • {PATI 35-10} vīyā yāṇar niṉ vayiṉāṉ ē

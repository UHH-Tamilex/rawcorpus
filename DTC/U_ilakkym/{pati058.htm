  • {PATI 58-1} āṭuka viṟaliyar pāṭuka paricilar
  • {PATI 58-2} veḷ tōṭṭu acaitta oḷ pūm kuvaḷaiyar
  • {PATI 58-3} vāḷ mukam poṟitta māṇ vari yākkaiyar
  • {PATI 58-4} cel uṟaḻ maṟavar tam kol paṭai tarīiyar
  • {PATI 58-5} iṉṟu iṉitu nukarntaṉam āyiṉ nāḷai
  • {PATI 58-6} maṇ puṉai iñci matil kaṭantu allatu
  • {PATI 58-7} uṇkuvam allēm pukā eṉa kūṟi
  • {PATI 58-8} kaṇṇi kaṇṇiya vayavar perumakaṉ
  • {PATI 58-9} poy paṭupu aṟiyā vayaṅku ce nāviṉ
  • {PATI 58-10} eyil eṟi val vil ē viḷaṅku taṭa kai
  • {PATI 58-11} ēntu eḻil ākattu cāṉṟōr meymmaṟai
  • {PATI 58-12} vāṉavarampaṉ eṉpa kāṉattu
  • {PATI 58-13} kaṟaṅku icai citaṭi pori arai poruntiya
  • {PATI 58-14} ciṟu ilai vēlam periya tōṉṟum
  • {PATI 58-15} puṉpulam vittum vaṉ kai viṉaiñar
  • {PATI 58-16} cīr uṭai pal pakaṭu olippa pūṭṭi
  • {PATI 58-17} nāñcil āṭiya koḻu vaḻi maruṅkiṉ
  • {PATI 58-18} alaṅku katir tiru maṇi peṟūum
  • {PATI 58-19} akal kaṇ vaippiṉ nāṭu kiḻavōṉ ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • While the big village is sleeping noone is there.
  • Even the neighbourhood has glorius defences,
  • with [its] beds of many flowers that have come up to rustle(?)[FNote_423]
  • by the good mansion, where the sharp-toothed dog shivers,
  • while the cool wind endorsed with raining that drips on the big street,
  • oy, [as] the vomiting water of the shark with perfect mouth,
  • spatters through every hole in the doors that are permanently(?) wrapped.
  • On top of that, [is] today,
  • when the longe-tongued bell of the midnight guards,
  • that says "protect [your] guarded doors!",
  • sounds as the rhythm beaten one [by one],
  • the day I perish, pitiable me.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "[My] shoulders, the armlets have become lose. [My] forehead,
  • pallor crept up like the climbing blossoms of the Pīr.
  • And [my] eyes,[FNote_627] they were kept in cool dew, alas.
  • We have understood indeed that my life is dwindling."
  • - don't be overwhelmed, live you[FNote_628], friend.
  • His land's good mountins are covered with moving clouds,
  • as the many shields of the king's fortified village,
  • sounding with the groupwise pleasing sounds of the drum,
  • flashing as the golden armlets in small rows(?)
  • on the forearms of women of great inexperience,
  • so as to bring new blossom visited by bees to the bright ghat,
  • together with wind that descended into your hanging dense hir.

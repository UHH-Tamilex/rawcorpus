{{Metrical text}}

  1. vīḻnta mārip peruntaṇ cāraṟ
  2. kūtirk kūtaḷat talari nāṟu
  3. mātar vaṇṭi ṉayavarun tīṅkuraṉ
  4. maṇanāṟu cilampi ṉacuṇa mōrkku
  5. muyarmali nāṭaṟ kuritta loṉṟō ((5))
  6. tuyarmaruṅ kaṟiya vaṉṉik kinnōy
  7. taṇiyumā ṟituveṉa vuritta loṉṟō
  8. ceyyā yātaliṟ koṭiyi tōḻi
  9. maṇikeḻu neṭuvari yaṇipeṟa nivanta
  10. ceyali yantaḷi raṉṉaveṉ ((10))
  11. mataṉiṉ māmip pacaliyuṅ kaṇṭē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • As for telling one thing to the man from the land of high mountins,
  • where the Acuṇam(?)[FNote_772] on the mountinside frangrant with scents listens
  • to the desiring(?)[FNote_773] sweet voice of the beautiful bees,
  • when the flowers of the Kūṭalam emit frangrance in the cool season
  • on the big cool slope in the shower that came down[FNote_774],
  • as for telling one thing, "this is the way to decrease
  • this pin", to mother so that she may know of [my] misery
  • - becuse you don't do it you [are] cruel, friend,
  • having seen the pallor of] dark complexion too, my strength failing,
  • as the pretty Ceyali sprouts
  • come up for the long mountin to be decorated, having sapphires.

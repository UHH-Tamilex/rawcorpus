  1. vīḻnta mārip peruntaṇ cāraṟ
  2. kūtirk kūtaḷat talari nāṟu
  3. mātar vaṇṭi ṉayavarun tīṅkuraṉ
  4. maṇanāṟu cilampi ṉacuṇa mōrkku
  5. muyarmalai nāṭaṟ kuraitta loṉṟō ((5))
  6. tuyarmaruṅ kaṟiya vaṉṉaik kinnōy
  7. taṇiyumā ṟituveṉa vuraitta loṉṟō
  8. ceyyā yātaliṟ koṭiyai tōḻi
  9. maṇikeḻu neṭuvarai yaṇipeṟa nivanta
  10. ceyalai yantaḷi raṉṉaveṉ ((10))
  11. mataṉiṉ māmaip pacalaiyuṅ kaṇṭē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • As for telling one thing to the man from the land of high mountains,
  • where the Acuṇam(?)[FNote_772] on the mountainside frangrant with scents listens
  • to the desiring(?)[FNote_773] sweet voice of the beautiful bees,
  • when the flowers of the Kūṭalam emit frangrance in the cool season
  • on the big cool slope in the shower that came down[FNote_774],
  • as for telling one thing, "this is the way to decrease
  • this pain", to mother so that she may know of [my] misery
  • - because you don't do it you [are] cruel, friend,
  • having seen the pallor of] dark complexion too, my strength failing,
  • as the pretty Ceyalai sprouts
  • come up for the long mountain to be decorated, having sapphires.

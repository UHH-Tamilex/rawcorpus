  2. ciṟukiḷi kaṭital piṟakkiyā vaṇatō
  3. kuḷirpaṭu kaiyaḷ koṭicci celkeṉa

  5. koyaṟoṭaṅ kiṉarē kāṉavar koṭuṅkuraṟ ((5))
  6. kulavuppoṟai yiṟutta koṟṟalai yiruvi
  7. viḻavoḻi viyaṉkaḷam kaṭuppat teṟuvarap
  8. paita lorunilaik kāṇa vaikal
  9. yāṅku varuvatu kollō tīñcoṟ
  10. ceṟittōṭ ṭelvaḷaik kuṟumakaḷ ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Where [is] afterwards the chasing away of small parrots,
  • gently, from the soft millet sown by father?
  • "The creeper woman, in her hand the kettle-drum stirring, may go",
  • [thus] talking good [and] pleasing words, softly,
  • the foresters have begun to pluck [the corn].
  • How [will there be] coming at dawn,
  • to see the one state of suffering, scorchingly(?),
  • like the wide gound left behind from a festival,
  • of stubbles, with heads taken, brought down to a gleaming burden of bent ears,
  • [To see][FNote_917] the state of the large back that abided in the small field
  • of the little woman with light bangles in a tight multitude, with sweet words?

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Foolish woman, opposed[FNote_923] to giving [your] daughter away
  • tomorrow to the man from the village, where the Vāḷai fish are leaping
  • in the deep water so that the women retreat at the drinking[-water] ghat,
  • won't you speak [favourably] at once,
  • staying behind with the mother, becoming one in agreement,
  • with agitated short words from exhausted(?) tongues?
  • Just covering [are your] words, nothing inside,
  • as the sharp-toned Taṇṇumai drum,
  • wonderful(?), in the hand of the bard with food in obtaining a calf, skilfully.[FNote_924]

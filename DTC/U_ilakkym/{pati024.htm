Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 24-1} neṭu vayiṉ oḷiṟu miṉṉu parantāṅku
  • {PATI 24-2} puli uṟi kaḻitta pulavu vāy eḵkam
  • {PATI 24-3} ēval āṭavar valaṉ uyarttu ēnti
  • {PATI 24-4} ār araṇ kaṭanta tār arum takippiṉ
  • {PATI 24-5} pīṭu koḷ māli perum paṭi taliva
  • {PATI 24-6} ōtal vēṭṭal avi piṟar ceytal
  • {PATI 24-7} ītal ēṟṟal eṉṟu āṟu purintu oḻukum
  • {PATI 24-8} aṟam puri antaṇar vaḻimoḻintu oḻuki
  • {PATI 24-9} ñālam niṉ vaḻi oḻuka pāṭal cāṉṟu
  • {PATI 24-10} nāṭu uṭaṉ viḷaṅkum nāṭā nal ici
  • {PATI 24-11} tiruntiya iyal moḻi tiruntu iḻi kaṇava
  • {PATI 24-12} kuli iḻipu aṟiyā cāpattu vayavar
  • {PATI 24-13} ampu kaḷivu aṟiyā tūṅku tuḷaṅku irukki
  • {PATI 24-14} iṭāa eṇi iyal aṟi kurucil
  • {PATI 24-15} nīr nilam tī vaḷi vicumpōṭu aintu um
  • {PATI 24-16} aḷantu kaṭi aṟiyiṉum aḷappu arum kuriyi niṉ
  • {PATI 24-17} vaḷam vīṅku perukkam iṉitu kaṇṭikum ē
  • {PATI 24-18} uṇmar um tiṉmar um vari kōḷ aṟiyātu
  • {PATI 24-19} kuri toṭi maḻukiya ulakki vayiṉ tōṟu
  • {PATI 24-20} aṭi cēmpu eḻunta āṭuṟum maṭāviṉ
  • {PATI 24-21} eḵku uṟa civanta ūṉattu yāvar um
  • {PATI 24-22} kaṇṭu mati maruḷum vāṭā coṉṟi
  • {PATI 24-23} vayaṅku katir virintu vāṉakam cuṭarvara
  • {PATI 24-24} vaṟitu vaṭakku iṟiñciya cīr cāl veḷḷi
  • {PATI 24-25} payam keḻu poḻutōṭu āniyam niṟpa
  • {PATI 24-26} kaliḻum karuviyoṭu ki uṟa vaṇaṅki
  • {PATI 24-27} maṉ uyir puriiya valaṉ ērpu iraṅkum
  • {PATI 24-28} koṇṭal taṇ taḷi kamam cūl mā maḻi
  • {PATI 24-29} kār etir paruvam maṟappiṉ um
  • {PATI 24-30} pērā yāṇarttāl vāḻka niṉ vaḷaṉ ē

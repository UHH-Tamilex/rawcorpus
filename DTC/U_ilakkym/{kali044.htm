((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 44-1} katir viri kaṉi cuṭar kaviṉ koṇṭa naṉam cāral
  • {KALI 44-2} etir etir ōṅkiya māl vari aṭukkattu
  • {KALI 44-3} atir ici aruvi taṉ am ciṉi mici vīḻa
  • {KALI 44-4} mutir iṇar ūḻ koṇṭa muḻavu tāḷ eri vēṅki
  • {KALI 44-5} vari nutal eḻil vēḻam pū nīr mēl coritara
  • {KALI 44-6} puri nekiḻ tāmari malar am kaṇ vīṟu eyti
  • {KALI 44-7} tiru nayantu iruntaṉṉa tēm kamaḻ viṟal veṟpa
  • {KALI 44-8} taṉ evvam kūriṉum nī ceyta aruḷ iṉmi
  • {KALI 44-9} eṉṉi um maṟittāḷ eṉ tōḻi atu kēṭṭu
  • {KALI 44-10} niṉṉi yāṉ piṟar muṉṉar paḻi kūṟal tāṉ nāṇi
  • {KALI 44-11} kūrum nōy ciṟappu um nī ceyta aruḷ iṉmi
  • {KALI 44-12} cēri um maṟittāḷ eṉ tōḻi atu kēṭṭāṅku
  • {KALI 44-13} ōrum nī niliyali eṉa kūṟal tāṉ nāṇi
  • {KALI 44-14} nōy aṭa varunti um nī ceyta aruḷ iṉmi
  • {KALI 44-15} āyam um maṟittāḷ eṉ tōḻi atu kēṭṭu
  • {KALI 44-16} māya niṉ paṇpu iṉmi piṟar kūṟal tāṉ nāṇi
  • {KALI 44-17} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 44-18} iṉiyaṉa tīmi niṉivaṉaḷ kāttāṅku
  • {KALI 44-19} aṉi arum paṇpiṉāṉ niṉ tīmi kāttavaḷ
  • {KALI 44-20} arum tuyar ār añar tīrkkum
  • {KALI 44-21} maruntu āki celkam peruma nām virintu ē

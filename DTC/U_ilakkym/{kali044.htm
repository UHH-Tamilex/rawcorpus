Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 44-1} katir viri kaṉai cuṭar kaviṉ koṇṭa naṉam cāral
  • {KALI 44-2} etir etir ōṅkiya māl varai aṭukkattu
  • {KALI 44-3} atir icai aruvi taṉ am ciṉai micai vīḻa
  • {KALI 44-4} mutir iṇar ūḻ koṇṭa muḻavu tāḷ eri vēṅkai
  • {KALI 44-5} vari nutal eḻil vēḻam pū nīr mēl coritara
  • {KALI 44-6} puri nekiḻ tāmarai malar am kaṇ vīṟu eyti
  • {KALI 44-7} tiru nayantu iruntaṉṉa tēm kamaḻ viṟal veṟpa
  • {KALI 44-8} taṉ evvam kūriṉum nī ceyta aruḷ iṉmai
  • {KALI 44-9} eṉṉai um maṟaittāḷ eṉ tōḻi atu kēṭṭu
  • {KALI 44-10} niṉṉai yāṉ piṟar muṉṉar paḻi kūṟal tāṉ nāṇi
  • {KALI 44-11} kūrum nōy ciṟappu um nī ceyta aruḷ iṉmai
  • {KALI 44-12} cēri um maṟaittāḷ eṉ tōḻi atu kēṭṭāṅku
  • {KALI 44-13} ōrum nī nilaiyalai eṉa kūṟal tāṉ nāṇi
  • {KALI 44-14} nōy aṭa varunti um nī ceyta aruḷ iṉmai
  • {KALI 44-15} āyam um maṟaittāḷ eṉ tōḻi atu kēṭṭu
  • {KALI 44-16} māya niṉ paṇpu iṉmai piṟar kūṟal tāṉ nāṇi
  • {KALI 44-17} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 44-18} iṉaiyaṉa tīmai niṉaivaṉaḷ kāttāṅku
  • {KALI 44-19} aṉai arum paṇpiṉāṉ niṉ tīmai kāttavaḷ
  • {KALI 44-20} arum tuyar ār añar tīrkkum
  • {KALI 44-21} maruntu āki celkam peruma nām viraintu ē

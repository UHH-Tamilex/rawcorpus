  5. vampuvirit taṉṉa cempulap puṟavi ((5))
  6. ṉīraṇi peruvaḻi nīḷiṭip pōḻac
  7. celka pākaniṉ ceyviṉi neṭuntēr
  8. viruntu viṟuppuṟūum peruntōṭ kuṟumakaṇ
  9. miṉṉoḷi raviriḻi naṉṉakar viḷaṅka
  10. naṭināṭ ceyta navilāc cīṟaṭip ((10))
  11. pūṅkaṭ putalvaṉ ṟūṅkuvayi ṉolki
  12. vantī kenti yeṉṉu

{{Readable Translation}}

  • While Mussel-shell creepers of dark colour looking like sapphires
  • adorn together with bright-flowered Malabar lilies the cool bushes,
  • while good flowers wound as linked gold
  • are hanging in every bright-clustered branch of the Koṉṟis.
  • as if a news had spread(?),[FNote_704] in the red-soiled woodlands,
  • - to cross the long ways, the great route adorned with water
  • may your long chariot for the work to be done(?)[FNote_705] go, charioteer.
  • Let us hear the pretty sweet words
  • "let my father come!" that are sid,[FNote_706]
  • bending hanginly to one side(?) by [my] flower-eyed son
  • with [his] unpractised small feet that have done walking in the morning(?),
  • while the good mansion shines with the shining jewels, resplendent with lightening, of the broad-shouldered little woman who has longing for a festival.[FNote_707]

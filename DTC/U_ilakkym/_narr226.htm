{{Metrical text}}

  1. marañcā maruntuṅ koḷḷār mānta
  2. rurañcāc ceyyā ruyartavam vaḷaṅkoṭ
  3. poṉṉuṅ koḷḷār maṉṉar naṉṉuta
  4. ṉānta muṇmiyi ṉuḷamē yataṉāṟ
  5. ṟāñcey poruḷaḷa vaṟiyā tāṅkacin ((5))
  6. teṉṟūḻ niṟuppa nīḷiṭi yoḻiyac
  7. ceṉṟōr maṉṟanam kātala reṉṟu
  8. miṉṉa nilimit teṉpa
  9. veṉṉōru maṟipaviv vulakat tāṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • People won't not take away medicine for the tree to die
  • and they won't do high penance for the strength to be exhusted
  • and the king won't take gold to take away [all] the wealth, o you of good forehead.[FNote_716]
  • We are [just] becuse of his existence. Therefore,
  • even when it is sid that certinly our lover is one who has gone
  • for the long way to stay behind, while the sun is balancing,
  • he himself melting, not knowing the measure of the wealth to be made by him,
  • thus [is] the state of affairs, they say.
  • Whoever [there is] knows it, in this world.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if [it] received seven pots
  2. of a hot boiled dish of ripe white rice together with the whole
  3. of Toṇṭi's[FNote_838] ghee, given by the many cows
  4. of the herdsmen in the forest of Naḷḷi with firm chariot
  5. - a trifle
  6. as offering for the crow who cried [he][FNote_839] will come for the festival,
  7. becuse of the distress which emaciated the big shoulders of my friend.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Since she who does not tell the time because of the greatness of her own desire
  • needs pitying because you possess so much love,[FNote_710]
  • you want to come even at midday. When she says "[it] is sweet indeed
  • that your are like that, in the many-flowered seashore grove",
  • because of the gossip she will be under severe protection. Therefore,
  • please come at night, o tender man from the seas.
  • More than the ghat with [its] full dark extension
  • where groups of sharks have been flourishing
  • this talkative village possesses women too, with sleepless eyes.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O lord, we`ve seen[FNote_85] the little woman,
  • unembraced, a creeper in a withered garland,
  • [her] shoulder pair kept in the old fetter,
  • earrings slipped down, crushed in the middle of an embrace by [his] chest,
  • she of shining ornaments, who had been adorned with fine [and] many beauty spots.
  • [Once] she went - may the girl live - discharging looks from [her] flower-like collyrium eyes
  • in the street, flinging [her arms] so that [her] dense armlets sparkled,
  • while [her] garment swayed so that [her] tresses came into swaying movement,
  • smelling of the honey springing Marāam tree with opening [flower] clusters,
  • having slept tight[FNote_86] on the chest of the delightful man.

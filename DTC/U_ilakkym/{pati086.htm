Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 86-1} uṟal uṟu kuruti cerukkaḷam pulava
  • {PATI 86-2} koṉṟu amar kaṭanta ve tiṟal taṭa kai
  • {PATI 86-3} veṉ vēl poṟaiyaṉ eṉṟaliṉ veruvara
  • {PATI 86-4} veppu uṭai āṭūu cettaṉeṉ maṉ yāṉ
  • {PATI 86-5} nal icai nilaiiya naṉam talai ulakattu
  • {PATI 86-6} illōr puṉkaṇ tīra nalkum
  • {PATI 86-7} nāṭal cāṉṟa nayaṉ uṭai neñciṉ
  • {PATI 86-8} pāṭunar puravalaṉ āṭu naṭai aṇṇal
  • {PATI 86-9} kaḻai nilai peṟāa kuṭṭattu āyiṉum
  • {PATI 86-10} puṉal pāy makaḷir āṭa oḻinta
  • {PATI 86-11} poṉ cey pūm kuḻai mīmicai tōṉṟum
  • {PATI 86-12} cāntu varu vāṉi nīriṉum
  • {PATI 86-13} tīm taṇ cāyalaṉ maṉṟa tāṉ ē

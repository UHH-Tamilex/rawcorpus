{{Readable Translation}}

  • "This [is] the grove abounding in flowers where we newly united,
  • on one wing of the white sand brought to the Puṉṉai tree
  • spread with big blossoms of the Ñāḻal with fragrant flowers.
  • Over there [is] the ghat with pure water where he curled, when he came,[FNote_329]
  • the five parts of [my] full-grown [hair-] bushels that were dark [and] hung down on [my] back when playing with us in the thick inrolling waves.
  • That [is] the seashore grove where he had gone on his own,
  • knitting, to obtain a decoration, calyxes [and] foliage in pretty contrast
  • of the long-stalked blue water-lily that came up by the bent bamboo." As if speaking thus[FNote_330], whenever whenever you remember, melting,
  • [your] pallor has gently gently faded.

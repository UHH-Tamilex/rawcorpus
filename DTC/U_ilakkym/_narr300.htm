{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from the village by the cool ghat, where the white water-lilies,
  • become weak when pressed by a little wind, bow to the lotus,
  • as if, since the king's daughter with glowing armlets is in rage, the attendants
  • to whom ignorance is befitting, [would stand] in front of this, worshipping,
  • with [joined] hands,
  • he prepared [his] big chariot [with the words] "the price [is she] with small bangles"(?)[FNote_905], put [it] at our front-entrance [and] went [away].
  • And you,
  • having come with the chariot, without talking of going,
  • as master[FNote_906] of raising great melodies stilled by cotton(?) as ghee overflowing,
  • touch the palm-leaf [thatching] our kitchen,
  • as the big elephant bull going round for alms
  • there in the Ūṇūr of Taṉumpaṉ beautiful with [his] big wound,
  • [and] stand [there]!

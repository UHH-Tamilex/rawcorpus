{{colophon}}: itu pakaṟkuṟi vantu nīṅkun talaimakaṉ pōkku nōkkit (EN, EV: nīkkit) tōḻi yiraviṉmēl (EN, ER, EV: māviṉmēl) vaittuc colliyatu.{{/colophon}}

{{Metrical text}}

  1. peṟumutu celvar poṉṉuṭaip putalvar ciṟutōṭ kōtta cevvarip paṟaiyiṉ
  2. kaṇṇakat teḻutiya kuṟiyē pōlak
  3. kōlkoṇ ṭalaikkap paṭīiyar mātō
  4. vīrai vēṇmāṉ veḷiyaṉ ṟittaṉ ((5))
  5. muracumutaṟ koḷīiya mālai viḷakkiṉ
  6. veṇkō ṭiyampa nuṇpaṉi yarumpak
  7. kaiyaṟa vanta poḻutoṭu meycōrn
  8. tavala neñciṉam peyara vuyartirai
  9. nīṭunīrp paṉittuṟaic cērppa ((10))
  10. ṉōṭutēr nuṇṇuka nuḻainta māvē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Should they be slapped[FNote_219] with a stem
  • similar to the sign[FNote_220] drawn into the eye
  • of the red-lined drum stringed to the small shoulder
  • of the gold-adorned(?)[FNote_221] son of great old[FNote_222] wealthy people,
  • - the horses harnessed to the fine yoke of the running chariot
  • of the man from the coast of dewy ghats by the long water
  • with high waves, while we with an unhappy heart are agitated,
  • [our] body languishing with the time that has come to end [all] action,
  • while fine dew buds, while white conches sound,
  • when they announce evening by beating the bottom of the drum(?)[FNote_223]
  • of Veḷiyaṉ Tittaṉ, the son of Virai Vēṇ?

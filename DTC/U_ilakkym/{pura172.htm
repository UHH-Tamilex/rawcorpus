  • {PU 172-1} ēṟṟuka uli ē ākkuka cōṟu ē
  • {PU 172-2} kaḷ um kuṟipaṭal ōmpuka oḷ iḻi
  • {PU 172-3} pāṭu val viṟaliyar kōti um puṉika
  • {PU 172-4} aṉṉavi piṟa um ceyka eṉṉatūum
  • {PU 172-5} pariyal vēṇṭā varu patam nāṭi
  • {PU 172-6} aivaṉam kāvalar pey tī nantiṉ
  • {PU 172-7} oḷi tikaḻ tiruntu maṇi naḷi iruḷ akaṟṟum
  • {PU 172-8} vaṉpulam nāṭaṉ vayam māṉ piṭṭaṉ
  • {PU 172-9} ār amar kaṭakkum vēl um avaṉ iṟi
  • {PU 172-10} mā vaḷ īki kōti um
  • {PU 172-11} māṟu koḷ maṉṉar um vāḻiyar neṭitu ē

  4. ciṟuveṇ kākkai palavuṭa ṉāṭun
  5. tuṟaipulam puṭaittē tōḻi paṇṭu ((5))
  6. muḷḷūrk kurīik karuvuṭait taṉṉa
  7. perumpō taviḻnta karuntāṭ puṉṉaik
  8. kāṉalaṅ koṇkaṉ ṟanta
  9. kāta ṉammoṭu nīṅkā māṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The ghat,
  • where the little white crows, many together, search,
  • so that [their] big backs get wet, the extension of the dark sea,
  • as the seven stars customarily worshipped in the sapphire-coloured
  • sky that has begun to shine when the collyrium[-clouds] subside,[FNote_729]
  • possesses loneliness, friend,
  • because the love
  • given by the man from the land of seashore groves
  • with black-trunked Puṉṉai trees that have opened big buds
  • as the broken egg of the bird in the inner village, formerly,
  • does not stay behind with us.[FNote_730]

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the land of great mountains,
  • where the large-trunked pair of small-eyed elephants,
  • after taking possession of the coold pond with wild jasmine,
  • until they are tired, dislike the bananas in the grove
  • [and] taste the sweet fruit of the red-trunked jack-tree,
  • so that the small bamboo-fenced hamlet cries out, in the neighbourhood,
  • - we entreat [you], may you live, to grant [us your] string,
  • you who have abided in the stony village in the frontyard
  • spread with blossoms shed by the Vēṅkai tree of my father,
  • when you go.[FNote_731]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. One who understands well the dutiful taking
  2. by the god of death, according to law(?)[FNote_1083], according to fate[FNote_1084],
  3. on any day, [such] a one doesn't separate for manly work,
  4. so that the little woman with broad(?)[FNote_1085] tight bangles stays behind,
  5. [the woman of] defectless sweet water which wetted [her] pure teeth,
  6. as if [she were] eating a piece hacked from the foot
  7. of the sugarcane - even if the gin of the whole[FNote_1086] wide world
  8. together with great wealth could be enjoyed [by him].[FNote_1087]

Altern

8b: One who knows well of the merciless creed[FNote_1088]

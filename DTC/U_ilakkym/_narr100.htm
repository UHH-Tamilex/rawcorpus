{{Metrical text}}

  1. uḷḷutoṟu nakuveṉ ṟōḻi vaḷḷukir
  2. mārik kokkiṉ kūrala kaṉṉa
  3. kuṇtunī rāmpaṟ ṟaṇṭuṟi yūraṉ
  4. ṟēṅkama ḻimpāl paṟṟi yemvayiṉ
  5. vāṉkō lelvaḷi vuviya pūcal ((5))
  6. ciṉaviya mukattuc ciṉavātu ceṉṟuniṉ
  7. maṉiyōṭ kurippa leṉṟaliṉ muṉiyūrp
  8. palāneṭu neṟiniri villi ṉoyyun
  9. tērvaṇ maliyaṉ muntip pēricip
  10. pulampuri vayiriyar nalampuri muḻaviṉ ((10))
  11. maṇṇār kaṇṇi ṉatiru
  12. naṉṉa rāḷa ṉaṭuṅkañar niliyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • I smile, friend, whenever I remember
  • the afflicted state of the good man,
  • who shakes likes the eye full of earth
  • of the drum that does good for the dancers that do good to the field(?)
  • of great fame in front of generous Maliyaṉ with the chariot,
  • who dragged along with [his] bow a herd on the long path of many cows
  • from the front village(?), - when I sid: "I will tell to her in your house,
  • going, without being angry, with an angry face,
  • of the fight where he snatched the light bangles in ...(?)
  • on us, grasping our honey-scented five parts [of hir],
  • the man from the village by the cool ghat with white lotuses in the deep water,
  • as the sharp beaks of cranes in the shower, with sharp nils[FNote_339].

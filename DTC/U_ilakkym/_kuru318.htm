{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As for me, will I tell him who does not know
  2. that one thing is different[FNote_1292], whether he intends it or not[FNote_1293],
  3. the man from the ghat, where appears like on the floor, where [one] immerses in the Veṟi dance,
  4. after Puṉṉai together with Ñāḻal has been spread in fragrant blossoms,
  5. in the expanse the shining water[FNote_1294], coming up with sharks dashing [against each other?],
  6. him, who [is] robber and dutiful one and support,
  7. who has made an unfailing oath
  8. on that day he came close to these [my] soft shoulders with the grace of bamboo?

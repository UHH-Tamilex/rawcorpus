{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even in the evening full of loneliness[FNote_1281], darkened by rain hanging down,
  2. while bright glow flickers from the cool-voiced cloud,
  3. fully pregnant with water in the distant high sky,
  4. he has not come, oh friend, - [there] comes[FNote_1282]
  5. the lightning, for my close young breast to be pressed -
  6. he, who traversed the desert of unpleasant regions.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. So that gossip arise in the village, so that the streets resound,
  2. let [my] dutiful[FNote_1060] mother, who slaps [me] unendingly,
  3. be [alone] in her house. As for me,
  4. with [my] thorn teeth glittering after they have eaten Nelli [fruit],
  5. I have chosen to drink
  6. the water which has stood in the footprints(?)[FNote_1061] of big elephant bulls,
  7. [big] as the [irrigation] troughs in the middle of sugarcane [fields?],[FNote_1062]
  8. on the slopes of transverse mountains that have come up to touch the sky,
  9. {--!}in a distant land with him.

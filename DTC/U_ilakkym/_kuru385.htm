{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He [is] full of friendship[FNote_1529] like on that day,
  2. always, the master of the big mountain side,
  3. where the male of the fruit-eating monkey group, joined under the jackfruit trees, frightened by the red arrow[FNote_1530] of the forest inhabitant with [his] bow,
  4. leaps[FNote_1531], so that the long bamboo[-stem] of the big bamboo glitters[FNote_1532]
  5. on the slope, pranced like a horse in fight.
  6. Newcomers [has], alas, this noisy village.

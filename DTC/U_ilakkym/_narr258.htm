{{Readable Translation}}

  • Having come to the meeting-place at midday, in the many-flowered seashore grove, I go [back], man from the sea. Mother has confined [her],
  • seeing the tight beautiful bright bangles slip down
  • from her [beautiful] as Maruṅkūr Paṭṭiṉam[FNote_815],
  • where the the green-eyed crow, that had fed on the boiled rice [long as] as
  • cranes nil[FNote_816], dropped behind by women with golden armlets,
  • while the prosperous wide mansion is immersed in the coming festival,
  • in the sun hot on the stones, while the foot of [its] rays scorches,
  • [and], when the light set, had seized the fresh prawn
  • heaped in the moving shade of the wide bazaar,
  • dwells on the mast of a vehicle in suspension.

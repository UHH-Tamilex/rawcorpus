{{Metrical text}}

  1. aṇivarai maruṅki ṉaituvaḷarn tiṭṭa
  2. maṇiyēr tōṭṭa maiyā rēṉa
  3. lirumpiṭit taṭakkaiyiṟ ṭaṭaiiya perumpuṇaṅ
  4. kāval kaṇṇiṉa māyi ṉāyiḻai
  5. nannilai yiṭaiterin tuṇarāṉ ṟaṉmalai ((5))
  6. yāra nīviya vaṇikiḷa rākañ
  7. cāra ṉīḷiṭaic cālavaṇ ṭarppac
  8. celvañ celluṅko ṟāṉē yuyarvaraip
  9. peruṅkal viṭarakañ cilampa virumpuli
  10. kaḷiṟutolait turaṟuṅ kaṭiyiṭi maḻaicettuc ((10))
  11. centiṉai yuṇaṅka ṟokukku
  12. miṉkaḷ yāṇarttam muṟaivi ṉūrkkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If our eyes [are] guarding, [o you of] choice jewels,
  • the big field, plump[FNote_986] as the broad trunk of the large she-elephant,
  • of millet, full of collyrium, with leaves resembling sapphires(?),
  • grown [and] planted wonderfully on the decorative mountain side,
  • will he be going[FNote_987] himself,
  • while the bees are humming very much, on the long way on the slope,
  • on [his] adorned lustrious breast smeared with sandal
  • from his mountain, he who does not perceive [and] distinguish our state,
  • - to the village of their(?) residence, fertile with sweet toddy,
  • where they gather the dried [corn] of the red millet,
  • thinking it speedily thundering rain, when the big tiger, having destroyed
  • the elephant bull, roars so that the inside of the cave of big stones resounds on the hight mountain. (?)

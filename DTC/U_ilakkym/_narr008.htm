{{Metrical text}}

  1. alkupaṭa ruḻanta varimatar maḻaikkaṭ
  2. palpūm pakaittaḻai nuṭaṅku malkuṟ
  3. tirumaṇi puraiyu mēṉi maṭavōḷ
  4. yārmakaḷ kolliva ṭantai vāḻiyar
  5. tuyara muṟīiyiṉa ḷemmē yakalvaya ((5))
  6. larivaṉa rarintun taruvaṉar peṟṟun
  7. taṇcēṟu tāay mataṉuṭai nōṉṟāṭ
  8. kaṇpō ṉeytal pōrviṟ pūkkun
  9. tiṇtērp poṟaiyaṉ ṟoṇṭi
  10. taṉṟiṟam peṟukaviva ḷīṉṟa tāyē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The inexperienced girl of a body resembling a radiant sapphire,
  • with hips where foliage contrasting[FNote_26] with many flowers is fine,
  • with rain eyes abounding in red lines, which have suffered lasting distress,
  • - whose daughter is she?[FNote_27] May [her] father live.
  • She has caused us grief.
  • May the mother who begot her obtain the opulence
  • of Toṇṭi itself[FNote_28], [the city] of Poṟaiyaṉ with firm chariot,
  • where, the cutters cutting of [and] the bringers receiving [the harvest],
  • blue water-lilies flower in [hay-]heaps like eyes,
  • with beautiful strong stems spread in the cool mud.

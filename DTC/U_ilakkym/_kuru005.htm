{{Readable Translation}}

  1. [Is] that, friend, the pain of desire?
  2. Because of separation from the tender man
  3. of the sweet waters, budding with spray of breaking waves
  4. near the Puṉṉai tree with pleasant shade where the heron slumbers in [its] nest
  5. sleeping is impossible to [my] many-petaled collyrium eyes.

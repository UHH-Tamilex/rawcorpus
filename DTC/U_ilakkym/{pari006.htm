Paripāṭal 6

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 6-1} niṟi kaṭal mukantu urāy niṟintu nīr tuḷumpum tam
  • {PARI 6-2} poṟi tavirpu aciviṭa poḻintaṉṟu vāṉam
  • {PARI 6-3} nilam maṟivatu pōl malir puṉal tali talii
  • {PARI 6-4} maliya iṉam kalaṅka maliya mayil akava
  • {PARI 6-5} mali mācu kaḻiya kataḻum aruvi iḻiyum
  • {PARI 6-6} mali nīr atar pala keḻuvu tāḻ vari
  • {PARI 6-7} mācu il paṉuval pulavar pukaḻ pula
  • {PARI 6-8} nāviṉ puṉinta nal kaviti māṟāmi
  • {PARI 6-9} mēvi parantu virintu viṉi nanta
  • {PARI 6-10} tāyiṟṟu ē taṇṇam puṉal
  • {PARI 6-11} puki pū avi ārātaṉi aḻal pala ēnti
  • {PARI 6-12} naki amar kātalari nāḷaṇi kūṭṭum
  • {PARI 6-13} vaki cālum viyi varavu
  • {PARI 6-14} toṭi tōḷ ceṟippa tōḷvaḷi iyaṅka
  • {PARI 6-15} koṭi cērā tiru kōvi kāḻ koḷa
  • {PARI 6-16} toku katir muttu toṭi kaliḻpu maḻuka
  • {PARI 6-17} ukir um koṭiṟu um uṇṭa ce pañci um
  • {PARI 6-18} nakil aṇi aḷaṟu naṉi vaṇṭal maṇṭa
  • {PARI 6-19} ili um mayir um īrm cāntu niḻatta
  • {PARI 6-20} muli um mārpu um muyaṅku aṇi mayaṅka
  • {PARI 6-21} viruppu oṉṟupaṭṭavar uḷam niṟi uṭitteṉa
  • {PARI 6-22} vari ciṟi uṭittatu ai viyi viyi
  • {PARI 6-23} tiri ciṟi uṭittaṉṟu kari ciṟi aṟika eṉum
  • {PARI 6-24} uri ciṟi paṟi eḻa ūr olittaṉṟu
  • {PARI 6-25} aṉṟu pōr aṇi aṇiyiṉ pukar mukam ciṟanteṉa
  • {PARI 6-26} nīr aṇi aṇiyiṉ niri niri piṭi cela
  • {PARI 6-27} ēr aṇi aṇiyiṉ iḷiyar um iṉiyar um
  • {PARI 6-28} īraṇi aṇiyiṉ ikal mika naviṉṟu
  • {PARI 6-29} taṇi puṉal āṭum taki miku pōr kaṇ
  • {PARI 6-30} tuṇi puṉal āka tuṟi vēṇṭum mintiṉ
  • {PARI 6-31} aṇi aṇi ākiya tārar karuviyar
  • {PARI 6-32} aṭu puṉalatu cela avaṟṟi iḻivar
  • {PARI 6-33} kimmāṉ eruttar kali maṭa māviṉar
  • {PARI 6-34} neym māṇ civiṟiyar nīr maṇa kōṭṭiṉar
  • {PARI 6-35} veḷ kiṭi mitaviyar nal kiṭi tēriṉar
  • {PARI 6-36} cāriki maṟuttu taṇṭā uṇṭiki
  • {PARI 6-37} ōr iyavu uṟuttara ūr ūrpu iṭam tirīi
  • {PARI 6-38} cēri iḷiyar cela aru niliyar
  • {PARI 6-39} valiyar allōr tuṟi tuṟi ayara
  • {PARI 6-40} meliyar allōr viruntu puṉal ayara
  • {PARI 6-41} cāṟu um cēṟu um ney um malar um
  • {PARI 6-42} nāṟupu nikaḻum yāṟu varal āṟu
  • {PARI 6-43} nāṟupu nikaḻum yāṟu kaṇṭu aḻintu
  • {PARI 6-44} vēṟupaṭu puṉal eṉa viri maṇṇu kaliḻi
  • {PARI 6-45} pulam puri antaṇar kalaṅkiṉar maruṇṭu
  • {PARI 6-46} māṟu mel malar um tār um kōti um
  • {PARI 6-47} vēr um tūr um kāy um kiḻaṅku um
  • {PARI 6-48} pūriya mākkaḷ uṇpatu maṇṭi
  • {PARI 6-49} nār ari naṟavam ukuppa nalaṉ aḻintu
  • {PARI 6-50} vēṟu ākiṉṟu i viri puṉal varavu eṉa
  • {PARI 6-51} cēṟu āṭu puṉalatu celavu
  • {PARI 6-52} vari aḻi vāl aruvi vā tālāṭṭa
  • {PARI 6-53} kari aḻi vāl aruvi kāl pārāṭṭa
  • {PARI 6-54} iravil puṇarntōr iṭi muli alkal
  • {PARI 6-55} purivatu pūm tārāṉ kuṉṟu eṉa kūṭārkku
  • {PARI 6-56} uriyōṭu iḻintu urāy ūr iṭi ōṭi
  • {PARI 6-57} calappaṭiyāṉ iravil tākkiyatu ellām
  • {PARI 6-58} pulappaṭa puṉ am pulariyiṉ nilappaṭa
  • {PARI 6-59} tāṉ malarntaṉṟu ē
  • {PARI 6-60} tamiḻ viyi taṇṇam puṉal
  • {PARI 6-61} viḷiyā viruntu viḻuvārkku koytōy
  • {PARI 6-62} taḷir aṟintāy tām ivi
  • {PARI 6-63} paṇipu oci paṇpa paṇṭu ellām naṉi uruvattu
  • {PARI 6-64} eṉṉō tuvaḷ kaṇṭī
  • {PARI 6-65} eytum kaḷavu iṉi niṉ mārpiṉ tār vāṭa
  • {PARI 6-66} koytatu um vāyāḷ ō koy taḻi ki paṟṟi
  • {PARI 6-67} ceytatu vāyāḷ ō ceppu
  • {PARI 6-68} puṉi puṇi ēṟa tāḻttatu ai taḷir ivi
  • {PARI 6-69} nīriṉ tuvaṇṭa cēey kuṉṟam kāmar
  • {PARI 6-70} perukku aṉṟu ō viyi varavu
  • {PARI 6-71} ām ām atu okkum kātal am kāmam
  • {PARI 6-72} orukka oru taṉmi niṟkum ō olli
  • {PARI 6-73} curukkam um ākkam um cūḷ uṟal viyi
  • {PARI 6-74} perukku aṉṟu ō peṟṟāy piḻi
  • {PARI 6-75} aruku pati āka ampiyiṉ tāḻppikkum
  • {PARI 6-76} kuruku iri tēra kiṭakkum poḻi kāril
  • {PARI 6-77} iṉ iḷavēṉil itu aṉṟu ō viyi niṉa
  • {PARI 6-78} viyi vayam āka vi
  • {PARI 6-79} cel yāṟṟu tīm puṉalil cel maram pōla
  • {PARI 6-80} vavvu vallār puṇi ākiya mārpiṉi
  • {PARI 6-81} eṉṉum paṉiyāy iravu ellām vikiṉi
  • {PARI 6-82} viyi uṭinta maṭi aṭitta kaṇ um
  • {PARI 6-83} piṉ um malirum picir pōla iṉṉum
  • {PARI 6-84} aṉaṟṟiṉi tuṉpu aviya nī aṭinta kaṇ um
  • {PARI 6-85} paṉittu paṉi vārum kaṇṇavar neñcam
  • {PARI 6-86} kaṉaṟṟupu kātti varavu
  • {PARI 6-87} nallāḷ kari niṟpa nāṉ kuḷitta pim taṭattu
  • {PARI 6-88} nillāḷ tiri mūḻki nīṅki eḻuntu eṉ mēl
  • {PARI 6-89} allā viḻuntāṉi eyti eḻuntu ēṟṟu yāṉ
  • {PARI 6-90} koḷḷā aḷavi eḻum tēṟṟāḷ kōtiyiṉ
  • {PARI 6-91} uḷ aḻuttiyāḷ evaḷ ō tōyntatu yātu eṉa
  • {PARI 6-92} tēṟi teriya uṇar nī piṟitu um ōr
  • {PARI 6-93} yāṟu uṇṭu ō i viyi yāṟu
  • {PARI 6-94} i viyi yāṟu eṉṟa māṟu eṉṉi kiyāl
  • {PARI 6-95} tali toṭṭēṉ taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 6-96} ciṉaval niṉ uṇ kaṇ civappu añcuvāṟku
  • {PARI 6-97} tuṉi nīṅki āṭal toṭaṅku tuṉi naṉi
  • {PARI 6-98} kaṉṟiṭiṉ kāmam keṭūum makaḷ ivaṉ
  • {PARI 6-99} allā neñcam uṟa pūṭṭa kāyntu ē
  • {PARI 6-100} val iruḷ nīyal atu piḻi ākum eṉa
  • {PARI 6-101} illavar āṭa irantu parantu uḻantu
  • {PARI 6-102} vallavar ūṭal uṇarttara nallāy
  • {PARI 6-103} kaḷippar kuḷippar kāmam koṭi viṭa
  • {PARI 6-104} aḷippa tuṉippa āṅku āṅku āṭupa
  • {PARI 6-105} āṭuvār neñcattu alarntu aminta kāmam
  • {PARI 6-106} vāṭaṟka viyi niṉakku

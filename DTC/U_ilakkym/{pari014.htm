Paripāṭal 14

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 14-1} kār mali kataḻ peyal talii ēṟṟa
  • {PARI 14-2} nīr mali niṟi cuṉi pū malarntaṉa ē
  • {PARI 14-3} taṇ naṟum kaṭampiṉ kamaḻ tātu ūtum
  • {PARI 14-4} vaṇṇa vaṇṭu imir kural paṇṇi pōṉṟaṉa ē
  • {PARI 14-5} aṭiyuṟi makaḷir āṭum tōḷ ē
  • {PARI 14-6} neṭu vari aṭukkattu vēy pōṉṟaṉa ē
  • {PARI 14-7} vāki oḷ pū puriyum mucciya
  • {PARI 14-8} tōki ār kural maṇantu taṇantōri
  • {PARI 14-9} nīṭaṉmiṉ vārum eṉpavar col pōṉṟaṉa ē
  • {PARI 14-10} nāḷ malar koṉṟi um polam tār pōṉṟaṉa
  • {PARI 14-11} mel iṇar vēṅki viyal aṟi tāyiṉa
  • {PARI 14-12} aḻuki makaḷirkku uḻuvi ceppa
  • {PARI 14-13} nīr ayal kalitta neri muki kāntaḷ
  • {PARI 14-14} vār kuli aviḻnta vaḷ itaḻ niri toṟum
  • {PARI 14-15} viṭu koṭi piṟanta mel taki tōṉṟi
  • {PARI 14-16} pavaḻattu aṉṉa ce pū tāay
  • {PARI 14-17} kār malintaṉṟu niṉ kuṉṟu pōr malintu
  • {PARI 14-18} cūr maruṅku aṟutta cuṭar paṭiyōy ē
  • {PARI 14-19} kaṟi il kār maḻi poṅki aṉṉa
  • {PARI 14-20} naṟiyiṉ naṟum puki naṉi amarntōy ē
  • {PARI 14-21} aṟu mukattu āṟu iru tōḷāl veṉṟi
  • {PARI 14-22} naṟu malar vaḷḷi pū nayantōy ē
  • {PARI 14-23} keḻīi kēḷir cuṟṟa niṉṉi
  • {PARI 14-24} eḻīi pāṭum pāṭṭu amarntōy ē
  • {PARI 14-25} piṟanta ñāṉṟu ē niṉṉi uṭki
  • {PARI 14-26} ciṟantōr añciya cīr uṭiyōy ē
  • {PARI 14-27} iru piṟappu iru peyar īra neñcattu
  • {PARI 14-28} oru peyar antaṇar aṟaṉ amarntōy ē
  • {PARI 14-29} aṉṉi ākaliṉ amarntu yām niṉṉi
  • {PARI 14-30} tuṉṉi tuṉṉi vaḻipaṭuvataṉ payam
  • {PARI 14-31} iṉṉum iṉṉum avi ākuka
  • {PARI 14-32} toṉ mutir marapiṉ niṉ pukaḻiṉum pala ē

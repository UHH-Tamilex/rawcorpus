Paripāṭal 14

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 14-1} kār mali kataḻ peyal talaii ēṟṟa
  • {PARI 14-2} nīr mali niṟai cuṉai pū malarntaṉa ē
  • {PARI 14-3} taṇ naṟum kaṭampiṉ kamaḻ tātu ūtum
  • {PARI 14-4} vaṇṇa vaṇṭu imir kural paṇṇai pōṉṟaṉa ē
  • {PARI 14-5} aṭiyuṟai makaḷir āṭum tōḷ ē
  • {PARI 14-6} neṭu varai aṭukkattu vēy pōṉṟaṉa ē
  • {PARI 14-7} vākai oḷ pū puraiyum mucciya
  • {PARI 14-8} tōkai ār kural maṇantu taṇantōrai
  • {PARI 14-9} nīṭaṉmiṉ vārum eṉpavar col pōṉṟaṉa ē
  • {PARI 14-10} nāḷ malar koṉṟai um polam tār pōṉṟaṉa
  • {PARI 14-11} mel iṇar vēṅkai viyal aṟai tāyiṉa
  • {PARI 14-12} aḻukai makaḷirkku uḻuvai ceppa
  • {PARI 14-13} nīr ayal kalitta neri mukai kāntaḷ
  • {PARI 14-14} vār kulai aviḻnta vaḷ itaḻ nirai toṟum
  • {PARI 14-15} viṭu koṭi piṟanta mel takai tōṉṟi
  • {PARI 14-16} pavaḻattu aṉṉa ce pū tāay
  • {PARI 14-17} kār malintaṉṟu niṉ kuṉṟu pōr malintu
  • {PARI 14-18} cūr maruṅku aṟutta cuṭar paṭaiyōy ē
  • {PARI 14-19} kaṟai il kār maḻai poṅki aṉṉa
  • {PARI 14-20} naṟaiyiṉ naṟum pukai naṉi amarntōy ē
  • {PARI 14-21} aṟu mukattu āṟu iru tōḷāl veṉṟi
  • {PARI 14-22} naṟu malar vaḷḷi pū nayantōy ē
  • {PARI 14-23} keḻīi kēḷir cuṟṟa niṉṉai
  • {PARI 14-24} eḻīi pāṭum pāṭṭu amarntōy ē
  • {PARI 14-25} piṟanta ñāṉṟu ē niṉṉai uṭki
  • {PARI 14-26} ciṟantōr añciya cīr uṭaiyōy ē
  • {PARI 14-27} iru piṟappu iru peyar īra neñcattu
  • {PARI 14-28} oru peyar antaṇar aṟaṉ amarntōy ē
  • {PARI 14-29} aṉṉai ākaliṉ amarntu yām niṉṉai
  • {PARI 14-30} tuṉṉi tuṉṉi vaḻipaṭuvataṉ payam
  • {PARI 14-31} iṉṉum iṉṉum avai ākuka
  • {PARI 14-32} toṉ mutir marapiṉ niṉ pukaḻiṉum pala ē

{{Metrical text}}

  1. yāri yeluva yāmē nīyemak
  2. kiyāriyu malli notuma lālaṉi
  3. yaṉittāṟ koṇkavem miṭiyē niṉippiṉ
  4. kaṭumpakaṭ ṭiyāṉi neṭuntērk kuṭṭuvaṉ
  5. vēntaṭu kaḷattiṉ muracatirn taṉṉa ((5))
  6. vōṅkaṟ puṇari pāyntāṭu makaḷi
  7. raṇintiṭu palpū marīi yārnta
  8. vāpulam pukutaru pērici mālik
  9. kaṭalkeḻu mānti yaṉṉavem
  10. vēṭṭaṉi yalliyā ṉalantantu ceṉmē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Who [are] you, friend(?)? We, for us you
  • are nobody at all, you [are] a stranger.
  • Thus it [is] indeed, man from the sea, in between us. If you think so,
  • you didn't want us indeed,
  • like Mānti, having the sea at famous/noisy(?) evening,
  • when the cows enter the field(?)[FNote_1075] that have approached [and] become full of the many flowers, used for decoration [and] cast away
  • by women jumping [and] playing in the rising ocean,
  • resounding like a drum on the field where kings are killed
  • by Kuṭṭuvaṉ with long chariot [and] fierce bull elephants.
  • Give [back our] integrity [and] go![FNote_1076]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, o friend, for mother
  2. indeed[FNote_1443] also the upper[FNote_1444] world would be a small [return],
  3. she who did not prevent the setting up[FNote_1445] in the house
  4. and the embracing, softly[FNote_1446], of the tender leaves
  5. of the whole stem of the Malabar lily, come in the morning
  6. with the river, fragrant with the smell[FNote_1447] rained in the evening from his mountain.

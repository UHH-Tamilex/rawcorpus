{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a village with a pond where the carps have been flourishing,
  • where the ambrosia cool fragrant buds of the tube-stemmed white water-lily,
  • buds when closing as the herons in groups in a pond,
  • green leaves as the ear of the ...(?) female elephant,
  • expand themselves to dispel the darkness like Venus appearing to the east,
  • having another woman without dislike [for you] you have abandoned me without consideration.
  • Pleasant is the time of seeing you,
  • as if an abundant flood were to spread, [bringing] coolness,
  • on the fields abounding in new drought,
  • so that summer(?) leaves that has afflicted [them] with much loneliness.

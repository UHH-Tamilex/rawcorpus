{{Readable Translation}}

  1. We, who traversed the hills difficult to traverse,
  2. where the fruits, still green, of the Nelli tree which has taken up duty(?)[FNote_833]
  3. are sounding(?)[FNote_834] when taken by the young of the brave tiger,
  4. we don't remember many things, o you of short steps(?)[FNote_835],
  5. [only] the intimacy with the girl with collyrium-dark tresses,
  6. which smell of the bent-twigged Veṭci tree's
  7. many bond-bursting buds, swollen in the wilderness by the way.

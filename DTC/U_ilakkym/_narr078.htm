{{Metrical text}}

  1. kōṭcuṟā vaḻaṅkum vāṭkē ḻiruṅkaḻi
  2. maṇiyēr neytaṉ māmalar niṟaiyap
  3. poṉṉēr nuṇṭātu puṉṉai tāam
  4. vīḻtāṭ ṭāḻaip pūkkamaḻ kāṉaṟ
  5. paṭarvantu naliyuñ cuṭarceṉ mālai ((5))
  6. nōymali paruvara ṉāmiva ṇuykaṅ
  7. kēṭṭiciṉ vāḻi tōḻi teṇkaḻi
  8. vaḷvā yāḻi yuḷvāy tōyiṉum
  9. puḷḷunimirn taṉṉa polampuṭaik kalimā
  10. valavaṉ kōluṟa vaṟiyā ((10))
  11. vuravunīrc cērppaṉ ṟērmaṇik kuralē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • We will escape suffering here, with increasing pain,
  • at evening when the glow goes, when affliction comes [and] assaults [us]
  • in the seashore grove fragrant with the flowers of the Tāḻai tree, aerial roots
  • down to [its] foot, where the Puṉṉai tree spreads gold-like fine pollen,
  • while the big blossoms of the sapphire-like water-lilies fill
  • the big backwaters full of light, where killing sharks are wandering.
  • Listen, live, friend:
  • the voice of the chariot bells of the man from the coast of strong waters,
  • [his] proud horses with gold on [their] sides(?)[FNote_282], stretching out like birds,
  • not knowing of getting the stick by the charioteer,
  • even when inner part[FNote_283] of the sharp-mouthes wheels touch
  • the clear backwaters.

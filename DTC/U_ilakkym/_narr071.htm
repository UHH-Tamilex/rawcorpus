  1. maṉṉāp poruṭpiṇi muṉṉi yiṉṉatai
  2. vaḷaiyaṇi muḷikai niṉikuḷaik kuṇartteṉap
  3. paṉmā ṇiratti rāyiṟ ceṉmeṉa
  4. viṭuna ḷātalu muriyaḷ viṭiṉē
  5. kaṇṇu nutalu nīvi muṉṉiṉṟu ((5))
  6. pirital vallirō vaiya cellā
  7. vakaiyamar nalli lakaviṟai yuṟaiyum
  8. vaṇṇap puṟaviṉ ceṅkāṟ cēval
  9. vīḻtuṇai payiruṅ kaiyaṟu muralkuraṉu
  10. mayilaḷ pulampak kēṭṭoṟum ((10))
  11. pomma lōti peruvitup puṟavē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If she lets go of [you], she for whom it is suitable that she also lets go of [you],
  • saying "go", when you have begged for many times,
  • since you made your friend[FNote_256] with bangle-adorned forearms realise[FNote_257]
  • thus, thinking of the fetter of impermanent wealth,
  • - when you stand in front of [her and] stroke [her] eyes and forehead,
  • will you be strong [enough] to separate [from her], sir,
  • so that [she with] thick hair has great longing,
  • whenever she hears, while your peacock-woman is lonely,
  • the deed-ending sounding voice with which the red-legged cock
  • of the coloured dove, that remains on [its] seat inside the good house,
  • where it has the habit of abiding without going, calls [its] descending mate?

{{Metrical text}}

  1. ulakiṟ kāṇi yākap palartoḻap
  2. palavayi ṉiliiya kuṉṟiṟ kōṭutō
  3. ṟēyiṉi yuriyarō peruṅkali yeḻili
  4. paṭumali niṉṟa nalyāḻ vaṭinaram
  5. peḻīi yaṉṉa yuṟiyiṉi muḻaviṉ ((5))
  6. maṇṇāṟ kaṇṇi ṉimmeṉa vimirum
  7. vaṇarntoli kūntaṉ māa yōḷoṭu
  8. puṇarntiṉitu nukarnta cāra ṉallūr
  9. viravumala rukira vīci
  10. yiravuppeyal poḻinta vutavi yōyē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You who seem to be
  • on every hill peak where you remined in many places
  • for many to worship as a support to the world,
  • may you talk, o big bustling cloud,
  • you who have been of help when rin flowed [from you] at night,
  • throwing [water] so that the mixed blossoms dropped off
  • on the good village on the slope that enjoyed[FNote_443] something sweet, being united
  • with the dark one with tresses that sprout curly,
  • humming[FNote_444] 'im' like the eye full of paste
  • of the drum, you who reside[FNote_445] as if risen
  • from the tinkling(?) strings of the good lute persisting in the Paṭumali melody.

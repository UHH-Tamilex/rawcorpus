{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Spread, so that the sky's eye is buried, [and] thundering
  2. good many [beats] like the drum beaten by a victorious king,
  3. rining [is] unending. The heavenly(?)[FNote_1514] lover,
  4. being in a very distant land, doesn't approach us.
  5. What will we do, friend,
  6. in the harsh days of frost, appearing before[FNote_1515] [us],
  7. so that blossoms drop off in colourful filaments?

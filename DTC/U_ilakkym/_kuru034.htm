{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Those who don't stop scolding don't understand that they reject.[FNote_161]
  2. May this village have joy on hearing the sweet [news],
  3. there being[FNote_162] no affliction in slumber for the lonely[FNote_163].
  4. He alone(?)[FNote_164] is master of our choice forehead, shining among [its] locks(?)[FNote_165],
  5. [beautiful] like Maṟantai [the town of] Kuṭṭuvaṉ
  6. that is frightened by the roaring noise of the death-wielding warriors
  7. many in numbers like the elephant herons(?) in the seashore grove(?)[FNote_166], near the front line.

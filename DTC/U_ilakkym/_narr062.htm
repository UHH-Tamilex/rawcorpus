  1. vērpiṇi vetirattuk kālporu naralicai
  2. kantupiṇi yāṉai yayarvuyirt tāaṅ

  4. kuṉṟūr matiya nōkki niṉṟuniṉain
  5. tuḷḷiṉe ṉallaṉō yāṉē muḷḷeyiṟṟut ((5))
  6. tilakan taiiya tēṅkamaḻ tirunuta
  7. lematu muṇṭōr matināṭ ṭiṅka
  8. ḷuraṟukural vevvaḷi yeṭuppa niḻaṟtapa
  9. vulavai yākiya maratta
  10. kalpiṟaṅku māmalai yumparaḵ teṉavē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Standing in thought looking at the moon creeping up the hill
  • with a rustling depth of bamboo when the sun lengthened,
  • sighing like an elephant fettered to a post lying down
  • to the calling sound of wind dashing against the tangle(?) of roots,
  • did I not remember?: "There is a new-moon
  • to the knowing mind, all ours - the honey-scented
  • fragrant forehead wearing the Tilakam of [her with] thorn teeth.
  • It [is] beyond the stone-glittering great mountain
  • of trees that have become [just] bare twigs
  • so that [their] shade fails when the hot wind rouses [them] with muttering voice."

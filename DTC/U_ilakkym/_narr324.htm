{{Readable Translation}}

  • Alas for it. Pitiable mother.
  • Aching, with destructive affliction - what will become of her?
  • The one she longed for as her own dughter, with gold-like body,
  • - the cotton-soft feet repeat steps,
  • that she of pretty few hir has,
  • running as if she were rolling a ball in play
  • on the ridge of the land of [her] wealthy father
  • with enduring hard blades, as if smeared with ghee,[FNote_944]
  • while elephants whose tusks are mature fill up together the wilderness.[FNote_945]

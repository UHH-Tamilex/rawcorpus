{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Pain he gave, friend,
  2. the man from a land where rest in the single(?) cool spot
  3. dark, rough, thick stones that endured great rining
  4. like elephants fully washed of dust.
  5. With pallor filled [these] eyes pretty as water-lilies.

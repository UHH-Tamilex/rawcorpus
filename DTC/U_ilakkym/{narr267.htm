  • {NT 267-1} nocci mā arumpu aṉṉa kaṇṇa
  • {NT 267-2} ekkar ñeṇṭiṉ irum kiḷai toḻuti
  • {NT 267-3} ilaṅku eyiṟṟu ēer iṉ nakai makaḷir
  • {NT 267-4} uṇaṅku tiṉai tuḻavum kai pōl ñāḻal
  • {NT 267-5} maṇam kamaḻ naṟu vī varikkum tuṟaivaṉ
  • {NT 267-6} taṉṉoṭu puṇartta iṉ amar kāṉal
  • {NT 267-7} taṉi ē varutal naṉi pulampu uṭaittu eṉa
  • {NT 267-8} vārēṉ maṉ yāṉ vantaṉeṉ teyya
  • {NT 267-9} ciṟu nā oḷ maṇi teḷ icai kaṭuppa
  • {NT 267-10} iṉa mīṉ ārkai īṇṭu puḷ oli kural
  • {NT 267-11} ivai makaṉ eṉṉā aḷavai
  • {NT 267-12} vaya māṉ tōṉṟal vantu niṉṟaṉaṉ ē

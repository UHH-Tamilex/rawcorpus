{{Readable Translation}}

  • The chariot of the man from the sea is coming, friend,
  • noisily, to the village by the great backwaters,
  • where after having mounted the boats [and] crossed the desert of waves,
  • the boatsmen descend on the sand, cutting strong fish
  • together with sword-mouthed sharks(?)[FNote_367] [and] pouring fat,
  • as if youths of hot strength having mounted [their] horses(?)[FNote_368]
  • had risen desirously to seize groups of animals,
  • - the little ones of dark deeds of the fishermen with lined(?)[FNote_369] nets,
  • obtaining fish in masses together with soft-headed prawns,
  • as the flowers of the Iruppai tree by the road.[FNote_370]

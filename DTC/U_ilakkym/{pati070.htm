  • {PATI 70-14} peṇmai cāṉṟu peru maṭam nilaii

  • {PATI 70-18} vēḷviyil kaṭavuḷ aruttiṉai kēḷvi

  • {PATI 70-20} vaṇaṅkiya cāyal vaṇaṅkā āṇmai
  • {PATI 70-21} iḷam tuṇai putalvariṉ mutiyar pēṇi
  • {PATI 70-22} tol kaṭaṉ iṟutta vel pōr aṇṇal
  • {PATI 70-23} māṭōr uṟaiyum ulakam um kēṭpa
  • {PATI 70-24} iḻumeṉa iḻitarum paṟai kural aruvi
  • {PATI 70-25} muḻu mutal micaiya kōṭu toṟum tuvaṉṟum
  • {PATI 70-26} ayirai neṭu varai pōla
  • {PATI 70-27} tolaiyātu āka nī vāḻum nāḷ ē

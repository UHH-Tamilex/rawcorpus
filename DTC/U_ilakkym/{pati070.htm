  • {PATI 70-4} kal alitta tōḷ

  • {PATI 70-6} vaṇṭu ici kaṭāvā taṇ paṉam pōnti
  • {PATI 70-7} kuvi mukiḻ ūci veḷ tōṭu koṇṭu
  • {PATI 70-8} tīm cuṉi nīr malar malintu matam cerukki
  • {PATI 70-9} uṭi nili nal amar kaṭantu maṟam keṭuttu
  • {PATI 70-10} kaṭum ciṉa vēntar cemmal tolitta
  • {PATI 70-11} valam paṭu vāṉ kaḻal vayavar peruma
  • {PATI 70-12} nakiyiṉum poyyā vāymi pakivar
  • {PATI 70-13} puṟañcol kēḷā puri tīr oṇmi
  • {PATI 70-14} peṇmi cāṉṟu peru maṭam nilii
  • {PATI 70-15} kaṟpu iṟikoṇṭa kamaḻum cuṭar nutal
  • {PATI 70-16} puriyōḷ kaṇava pūṇ kiḷar mārpa
  • {PATI 70-17} toliyā koḷki cuṟṟam cuṟṟa
  • {PATI 70-18} vēḷviyil kaṭavuḷ aruttiṉi kēḷvi
  • {PATI 70-19} uyarnili ulakattu aiyar iṉpuṟuttiṉi
  • {PATI 70-20} vaṇaṅkiya cāyal vaṇaṅkā āṇmi
  • {PATI 70-21} iḷam tuṇi putalvariṉ mutiyar pēṇi
  • {PATI 70-22} tol kaṭaṉ iṟutta vel pōr aṇṇal
  • {PATI 70-23} māṭōr uṟiyum ulakam um kēṭpa
  • {PATI 70-24} iḻumeṉa iḻitarum paṟi kural aruvi
  • {PATI 70-25} muḻu mutal miciya kōṭu toṟum tuvaṉṟum
  • {PATI 70-26} ayiri neṭu vari pōla
  • {PATI 70-27} toliyātu āka nī vāḻum nāḷ ē

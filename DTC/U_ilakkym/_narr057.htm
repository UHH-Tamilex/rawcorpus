{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land of big mountains,
  • where, because the innocent-eyed long row of broad-horned wild cows
  • abided with the calves by the Vēṅkai trees on the hill,
  • while the cotton-headed she-monkey, that had taken the opportunity [they] were sleeping,
  • comes near quietly without passing the group(?)[FNote_216] of [her] kinsfolk
  • [and] bends in a pressing way[FNote_217] the swelling udder, the untaught
  • strong young [monkey] presses out a handful of sweet milk,
  • - it is bewildering,
  • the loss of the glorious integrity of our collyrium[-dark] moist hair
  • at the time, when occasion of harvesting approaches
  • on the wide fields of small millet with red stalks [and] bent ears.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Where [is] he today, friend,
  2. who stroked [my] thick hair,
  3. saying 'you come to our house,
  4. [you with] dense bangles, when knowledge increases[FNote_1511]',
  5. the man from a land, where the forest inhabitant, who dug a pit for fruit[FNote_1512] on the hill,
  6. obtains, together with the bulbous roots a jewel of eye-widening[FNote_1513] purity.

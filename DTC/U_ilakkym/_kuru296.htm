{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, oh friend, if you see the man from the cool ghat,
  2. where the pretty-winged heron, perched on the swaying Puṉṉi twig,
  3. longs for the blue water-lily fragrant with nectar together with ears [of corn]
  4. from the field, when it is fed up with the little fish in the full(?)[FNote_1216] backwaters,
  5. beware of confronting[FNote_1217] [him and] murmuring rash things[FNote_1218],
  6. having resolved to say such things as 'is it fitting for you
  7. to abandon [her], so that she with armlets becomes like that?'

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Alas, live, friend, [our] good forehead,
  • how has it become [a place of] pallor?
  • The man from the ghat, where the fishermen with firm boats
  • [and] throwing hooks(?) of quick gait, tied to the end of a rope
  • with enduring strands, take bright lights [and] go to hunt
  • at midnight, bring fish from the ocean at dawn,
  • heap [them] in the seashore grove [and then] enjoy themselves greatly,
  • drinking with [their] relatives of the toddy fragrant of honey,
  • being in the dappled shade of the high dark Puṉṉai tree,
  • he does not know of departing from our heart [even] a little[FNote_1059].

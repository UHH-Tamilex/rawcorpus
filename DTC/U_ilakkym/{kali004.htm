((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 4-1} vali muṉpiṉ valleṉṟa yākkai puli nōkkiṉ
  • {KALI 4-2} cuṟṟu amai villar curi vaḷar pittaiyar
  • {KALI 4-3} aṟṟam pārttu alkum kaṭuṅkaṇ maṟavar tām
  • {KALI 4-4} koḷḷum poruḷ ilar āyiṉum vampalar
  • {KALI 4-5} tuḷḷunar kāṇmār toṭarntu uyir vauvaliṉ
  • {KALI 4-6} puḷ um vaḻaṅkā pulampu koḷ ār iṭai
  • {KALI 4-7} veḷ vēl valattir poruḷ taral vēṭkaiyiṉ
  • {KALI 4-8} uḷḷiṉir eṉpatu aṟintaṉaḷ eṉ tōḻi
  • {KALI 4-9} kāḻ viri kavai āram mī varum iḷa mulai
  • {KALI 4-10} pōḻtu iṭaippaṭāamal muyaṅki um amaiyār eṉ
  • {KALI 4-11} tāḻ katuppu aṇikuvar kātalar maṟṟu avar
  • {KALI 4-12} cūḻvatu ai evaṉ kol aṟiyēṉ eṉṉum
  • {KALI 4-13} muḷ uṟaḻ muḷai eyiṟṟu amiḻtu ūṟum tī nīrai
  • {KALI 4-14} kaḷḷiṉum makiḻ ceyum eṉa uraittu um amaiyār eṉ
  • {KALI 4-15} oḷ iḻai tiruttuvar kātalar maṟṟu avar
  • {KALI 4-16} uḷḷuvatu evaṉ kol aṟiyēṉ eṉṉum
  • {KALI 4-17} nuṇ eḻil māmai cuṇaṅku aṇi ākam tam
  • {KALI 4-18} kaṇṇoṭu toṭutteṉa nōkki um amaiyār eṉ
  • {KALI 4-19} oḷ nutal nīvuvar kātalar maṟṟu avar
  • {KALI 4-20} eṇṇuvatu evaṉ kol aṟiyēṉ eṉṉum
  • {KALI 4-21} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 4-22} kaḻi peru nalkal oṉṟu uṭaittu eṉa eṉ tōḻi
  • {KALI 4-23} aḻivoṭu kalaṅkiya evvattaḷ oru nāḷ nīr
  • {KALI 4-24} poḻutu iṭaippaṭa nīppiṉ vāḻvāḷ ō
  • {KALI 4-25} oḻika iṉi peruma niṉ poruḷ piṇi celavu ē

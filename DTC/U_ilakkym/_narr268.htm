{{Readable Translation}}

  • "What if you sid, friend?" we ask:
  • "although making love
  • to the man from a land, where fragrant honeycombs are oozing,
  • made within the limit of houses as if one saw a picture(?),[FNote_840]
  • with the sky flowers of the fertile soil from the black-trunked Kuṟiñci
  • on the rich long hills on which great rin had poured
  • so that the water increases in the mountin-springs of fearful vast area[FNote_841],
  • [he] has no love for us."[FNote_842]
  • - since the Vēlaṉ has been brought[FNote_843], with [his] truthful molluca beans,
  • the courtyard strewn with sand having taken scent.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O you bangled one[FNote_1415], rejoice:
  2. Our people granted [your] possession
  3. to the man from the ghat, where thunder-sounding waves break
  4. with [the sound] 'iḻum', so that they destroy, by flooding the moist sand, the ways
  5. scratched by the sharp nails of quick, bent-legged crabs living in holes.
  6. This noisy village, [is it] like that even now,
  7. with pleasantly jesting attendants,
  8. by the fish-reeking street, where broad-blossomed Puṉṉai trees have become high?

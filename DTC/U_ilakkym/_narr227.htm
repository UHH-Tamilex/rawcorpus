  4. piṉṉī rōtiyeṉ ṟōḻi yaṉṉō

  8. tērvaḻaṅku teruvi ṉaṉṉa
  9. kuviyā kiṉṟa tiyaniṉ ṉaruḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if [her] sweet life passes by that was above saying
  • "the knowledgeable ones [are] not devoid of virtue", it is not very pleasant.
  • Alas, my friend with brided moist hir
  • who has reached the sign of union in the seashore grove of Puṉṉi trees
  • - scandal has become, sir, your consideration,
  • [loud] as in the streets where the chariot passes,
  • the birds unceasingly calling in the trees full of nectar,[FNote_718]
  • there in Ārkkāṭu, in the streets with swaying banners,
  • of the Cōḻa king with green ornaments, [his] elephant with a sounding bell.[FNote_719]

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 66-1} vīṅku nīr aviḻ nīlam pakarpavar vayal koṇṭa
  • {KALI 66-2} ñāṅkar malar cūḻtantu ūr pukunta vari vaṇṭu
  • {KALI 66-3} ōṅku uyar eḻil yāṉai kaṉai kaṭām kamaḻ nāṟṟam
  • {KALI 66-4} āṅku avai viruntu āṟṟa pakal alki kaṅkulāṉ
  • {KALI 66-5} vīṅku iṟai vaṭu koḷa vīḻunar puṇarntavar
  • {KALI 66-6} tēm kamaḻ katuppiṉ uḷ arumpu aviḻ naṟu mullai
  • {KALI 66-7} pāyntu ūti paṭar tīrntu paṇṭu tām marīiya
  • {KALI 66-8} pūm poykai maṟantu uḷḷā puṉal aṇi nal ūra
  • {KALI 66-9} aṇai mel tōḷ yām vāṭa amar tuṇai puṇarntu nī
  • {KALI 66-10} maṇa maṉaiyāy eṉa vanta mallaliṉ māṇpu aṉṟu ō
  • {KALI 66-11} potu koṇṭa kavvaiyiṉ pū aṇi polinta niṉ
  • {KALI 66-12} vatuvai am kamaḻ nāṟṟam vaikaṟai peṟṟatu ai
  • {KALI 66-13} kaṉal um nōy talai um nī kaṉam kuḻai avaroṭu
  • {KALI 66-14} puṉal uḷāy eṉa vanta pūcaliṉ peritu aṉṟu ō
  • {KALI 66-15} tār koṇṭāḷ talai kōtai taṭumāṟi pūṇṭa niṉ
  • {KALI 66-16} īr aṇi citaiyātu em il vantu niṉṟatu ai
  • {KALI 66-17} taṇantataṉ talai um nī taḷar iyal avaroṭu
  • {KALI 66-18} tuṇaṅkaiyāy eṉa vanta kavvaiyiṉ kaṭappu aṉṟu ō
  • {KALI 66-19} oḷi pūtta nutalārōṭu ōr aṇi polinta niṉ
  • {KALI 66-20} kaḷi taṭpa vanta i kaviṉ kāṇa iyaintatu ai
  • {KALI 66-21} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 66-22} aḷi peṟṟēm emmai nī aruḷiṉai viḷiyātu
  • {KALI 66-23} vēṭṭōr tiṟattu virumpiya niṉ pākaṉ um
  • {KALI 66-24} nīṭṭittāy eṉṟu kaṭāam kaṭum tiṇ tēr
  • {KALI 66-25} pūṭṭu viṭāa niṟuttu

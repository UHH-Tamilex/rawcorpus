{{colophon}}: itu talaivi pirivuṇarntu (ET: pirivukuṟippu uṇarntu talaivi) vēṟupaṭṭamai collit tōḻi celavu aḻuṅkuvittatu.{{/colophon}}

{{Metrical text}}

  1. nōkō yāṉē nekiḻtaṉa vaḷaiyē
  2. cevvi cērnta puḷḷi veḷḷarai
  3. viṇṭup puraiyum puṇarnilai neṭuṅkūṭṭup
  4. piṇṭa nelliṉ ṟāymaṉai yoḻiyac
  5. cuṭarmuḻu teṟippat tiraṅkic ceḻuṅkāy ((5))
  6. muṭamutir palavi ṉatta nummoṭu
  7. keṭutuṇai yākiya tavaṟō vaiyeyiṟṟup
  8. poṉpotin taṉṉa cuṇaṅki
  9. ṉiruñcū ḻōtip peruntō ḷāṭkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Ah, I ache. Loosened have [my] bangles.
  • - for her of big shoulders, with dark hair all around,
  • with beauty spots like bundeled gold, with sharp teeth.
  • [Would it be] a mistake when [she] became[FNote_109] the companion to get lost
  • with you on the road of jack-fruit trees where the bends[FNote_110] of abundant
  • unripe fruits ripen, having dried up while the [sun`s] glow shines on all(?)[FNote_111],
  • so that [her] mother`s house of rice bursting in balls[FNote_112] stays behind,
  • [after] being together for long in a state of union[FNote_113], which resembles a
  • mountain with white base [and] marks which have turned red(?)[FNote_114]?

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Whether he doesn't come, whether he does,
  2. what is he to us now[FNote_444], friend?
  3. - he who didn't say: what has become of her
  4. with the north wind tossing about unsweetly,
  5. cold, for blossoms to drop off in colourful filaments
  6. that have ēp
  7. withered as red buds on the fine-thorned mimosa,
  8. after it has shaken the Karuviḷi creepers, bright eyes of peacock feathers in the shrubs,
  9. [and] parted the green buds of the blue water[-lilies][FNote_445].

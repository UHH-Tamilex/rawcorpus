  5. kival viṉivaṉ ṟiyupu corinta ((5))
  6. curitaka vuruviṉa vākip periya
  7. kōṅkaṅ kuvimuki yaviḻu vīṅki
  8. naṟṟaḷir nayavara nuṭaṅku
  9. muṟṟā vēṉiṉ muṉṉivan tōrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Dutiful [is] he[FNote_303], live, friend,
  • who has come back before summer is at an end,
  • when the good sprouts of the mimosa are fine to rise longing,
  • when the piled buds of the Kōṅkam are opening,
  • big ones having the form of hir-ornaments
  • cast [and] inlid by a craftsman skilful with [his] hands in a way fit to be seen,
  • while [we] are shivering in the cold season with fierce dew,
  • when the Pakaṉṟi blossoms, resembling disks
  • of hiry hide spread like weapons(?)[FNote_304] by the bold ones.

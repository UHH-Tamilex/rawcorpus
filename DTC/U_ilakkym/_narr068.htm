  3. laṟaṉu maṉṟē yākkamun tēymeṉak
  4. kuṟunurai cumantu naṟumala runtip
  5. poṅkivaru putunīr neñcuṇa vāṭukam ((5))
  6. vallitiṉ vaṇaṅkic collunarp peṟiṉē (4/6)
  7. celkeṉa viṭunaṇmaṟ kollō vellumiḻn
  8. turavuru muraṟu maraiyiru ṇaṭunāṭ
  9. koṭinuṭaṅ kilaṅkiya miṉṉi
  10. yāṭumaḻai yiṟuttaṉṟavaṟ kōṭuyar kuṉṟē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "For youths to be confined to the house, to this place
  • without playing games with [their] playing attendants
  • is not at all duty. We want to dance for [our] hearts to enjoy
  • the new waters that rise foaming in the river with fragrant blossoms,
  • being burdened[FNote_249] with bits of foam [meaning] "all the gain is dwindling"(?)."
  • If [we] obtained someone would bow submissively [and] speak [thus],
  • she would certainly let go of saying "I will go"? Spitting light,
  • at midnight in half darkness when strong thunder murmurs,
  • flashing so that the creepers shine in a flicker,
  • playing rain has been tarrying on his hill high with peaks.

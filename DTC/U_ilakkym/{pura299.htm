{PUṞAM 299}

((Originally extracted from master file ^PURAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PU 299-1} parutti vēli cīṟūr maṉṉaṉ
  • {PU 299-2} uḻuttatar uṇṭa ōy naṭai puravi
  • {PU 299-3} kaṭal maṇṭu tōṇiyiṉ paṭai mukam pōḻa
  • {PU 299-4} neym miti aruntiya koy cuval eruttiṉ
  • {PU 299-5} taṇṇaṭai maṉṉar tār uṭai puravi
  • {PU 299-6} aṇaṅku uṭai murukaṉ kōṭṭattu
  • {PU 299-7} kalam toṭā makaḷiriṉ ikantu niṉṟa ē

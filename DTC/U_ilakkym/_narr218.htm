{{Readable Translation}}

  • With the sun declining [its] beams became blunt.
  • And with night
  • settled loneliness settled on the creepers in full bloom.
  • And the bats fly on every side. And the cock,
  • [its] mouth taking a smile, calls whenever it smiles.(?)[FNote_694]
  • The season he talked of, making clear that it would happen,
  • with unfailing love, has passed by. And the tawny owl,
  • perched on the way-giving branch of the Neem tree
  • with broad trunk is sounding at night.
  • And besides suffering so as to be killed by unending pin
  • I who am alone, shall I hear
  • the voice of the Aṉṟil in the large-trunked palmyra palm?

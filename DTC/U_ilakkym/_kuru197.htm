{{Readable Translation}}

  1. What [can] we do, friend? Word comes
  2. in the form of the cold season
  3. thoroughly disturbed[FNote_793] with the coldness of the cold wind,
  4. more rin than in the riny season, more abundant than water(?)[FNote_794],
  5. fitting for pin(?), [and] marks me who is separated from the lover.

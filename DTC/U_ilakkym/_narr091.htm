{{Metrical text}}

  1. nīyuṇarn taṉiyē tōḻi vīyuka
  2. puṉṉi pūtta viṉṉiḻa luyarkarip
  3. pāṭimiḻ paṉikkaṭa ṟuḻiip peṭiyō
  4. ṭuṭaṅkiri tērun taṭantā ṇāri
  5. yiya ciṟukaṭ ceṅkaṭic ciṟumīṉ ((5))
  6. mēkkuyar ciṉiyiṉ mīmicik kuṭampit
  7. tāyppayir piḷḷi vāyppaṭac coriyuṅ
  8. kāṉalam paṭappi yāṉā vaṇmakiḻp
  9. perunal līkinañ ciṟukuṭip poliyap
  10. puḷḷuyirk koṭpiṉ vaḷḷuyir maṇittārk ((10))
  11. kaṭumāp pūṇṭa vaḷḷuraṭa neṭuntēr
  12. neṭunīrc cērppaṉ pakalivaṇ varavē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You have realised, friend,
  • the coming here by day of the man from the coast of the long water
  • in the long chariot yoked with swift horses
  • with bell garlands [and] abundant breath, to the lively agitation of the birds,
  • while our small hamlet glitters with great good gifts,
  • delightful in unending generosity in the gardens by the seashore grove,
  • where the mother pours forth into the mouth of [her] calling young one,
  • in the nest on the height of the high high branch,[FNote_315]
  • the beautiful small-eyed red-rimmed little fish,
  • the broad-footed heron[FNote_316] that searches prey together
  • with [its] female, stirring the dewy sea, buzzing with singing
  • on the high shore with pleasing shade where the Puṉṉi has flowered to shed [its] blossoms.

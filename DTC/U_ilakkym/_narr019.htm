{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the coast of the strong water, which smells [like] a festival ground,
  • since the buds, like the tusks of a big elephant bull, have ripened,
  • appearing separate like good stag [and] doe,[FNote_79]
  • on the tāḻai trees with leaves as thorny as a swordfish`s sword(?)[FNote_80]
  • [and] a broad base becoming rough like prawns` backs,
  • - if you are going to go,
  • while the charioteer impells the long chariot with different[FNote_81] bells,
  • go as one who has understood well that she, she would not live[FNote_82]
  • the few days until[FNote_83] you come.

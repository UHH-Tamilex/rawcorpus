{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He,
  2. he is one who, in order to bring imperishable excellent wealth,
  3. traversed the wilderness of evergreen(?)[FNote_867] creepers.
  4. I,
  5. so that my bright(?)[FNote_868] shining bangles become loose, I lament
  6. [and] suffer affliction in abundance on a sleepless[FNote_869] bed.
  7. Without saying: 'as she [is] she [is] pitiable!' the black cloud,
  8. thundering, so that it will rin now too,
  9. flashes lightning, friend, intent on my sweet life.[FNote_870]

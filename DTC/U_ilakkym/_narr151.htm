{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if [your] good forehead fades, even if [your] big shoulders become emaciated,
  • let him not come at night, friend,
  • on the slope where the elephant washes in the waterfall on stony height,
  • with [his] white tusk that has taken red stains
  • since the big tiger had attacked[FNote_484] [him] in murderous antagony on the difficult forest path,
  • - the man from a land within the hills,
  • where the red-faced she-monkey,
  • that had united in secret meeting on the pepper-grown mountain side,
  • fearing that the big horde filling on sprouts should know, with the male monkey, goes[FNote_485] on a flowering branch of the gol-clustered Vēṅkai tree
  • with dark trunk [and] fading sign(?)[FNote_486], looks into the the long mountain pool with deep water, turn completely(?)[FNote_487] upside down
  • [and] rearranges the scattered hair on [her] ruddish head.

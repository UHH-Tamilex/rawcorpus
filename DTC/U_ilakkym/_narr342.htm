{{Readable Translation}}

  • "It is necessary to be considerate, o loving one,
  • to the one who comes dily to join [our] street
  • on your behalf. I cannot [convey to him?] yours words with my mouth,
  • he mounting [his] white chariot,[FNote_978] taking it as a fortification,
  • as if he were mounted the palmyra stems, taking them for a horse."
  • - Although [I] bend to be pleasing to the eye, she does not understand.
  • Is there [her][FNote_979] saying, too, "what is going on?",{X}[FNote_980]
  • {/X}if I, for one, join to [her] red feet [my] head
  • lined finely with fragrant blossoms eaten be bees
  • from the seashore grove bound with light bangles[FNote_981]?

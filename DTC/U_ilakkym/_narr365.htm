{{Readable Translation}}

  • Passing beyond severly protective mother's guarding,
  • crossing the big gate(?), going to the village common,
  • - at midday! - for many to see letting go of shame,
  • asking for his village with gardens [and] wide paddy fields,
  • up, live, friend,
  • in order to come after we have sid[FNote_1019]: "you are not worthy"
  • to the man from a land of mountins big as to touch the sky,
  • with water glittering sides where waterfalls roar,
  • even if the sky with masses [of clouds] does not rin for many days.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Talk as if you had seen it yourself, bard,
  • to the man from the ghat by the water of the cool big ocean,
  • where the herons in groups, mounted on the heaped white sand,
  • when they eat the rich fish with oily heads
  • in the dense muddy mire that has been changed as the water has moved,
  • appear shining as a collection of bright king's weapons,[FNote_889]
  • of the quality[FNote_890] of her integrity that perished, as the owner of many cows
  • in a grouped row, when the king mounts [his] horse
  • in the big dark inner village [and] takes[FNote_891] [them] at night.

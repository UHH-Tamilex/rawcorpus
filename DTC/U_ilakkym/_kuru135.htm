{{Readable Translation}}

  1. 'Work alone[FNote_546] [is] life for men; for women
  2. with bright foreheads, staying at home, the man [is] life.'
  3. [Thus] he himself told us;
  4. don't cry, friend, he is one to give up going.

Altern

2-4b[FNote_547] {--!}'... men [are] life' {--!}[so] also they themselves told us, {--!}don't cry, friend, they are those who dispense with going.

3+4c[FNote_548] Even if they have told us: '...', {--!}don't cry, friend, he himself is one to give up going.

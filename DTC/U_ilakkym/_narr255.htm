  1. kaḻutukāl kiḷara vūrmaṭin taṉṟē
  2. yurukeḻu marapiṟ kuṟiñci pāṭik
  3. kaṭiyuṭi viyaṉakark kāṉavar tuñcār
  4. vayakkaḷiṟu poruta vāḷvari yuḻuvi
  5. kaṉmukic cilampiṟ kuḻumu maṉṉō ((5))
  6. meṉṟō ṇekiḻntunām varuntiṉu miṉṟavar
  7. vārā rāyiṉō naṉṟumaṟ ṟilla
  8. vuyarvari yaṭukat toḷiṟupu miṉṉip
  9. peyalkāṉ mayaṅkiya yoḻutukaḻi pāṉāṭ
  10. ṭirumaṇi yaravuttērn tuḻala ((10))
  11. vurumuccivan teṟiyu mōṅkuvari yāṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • While the time of the demons comes up, the village has become inactive.
  • Singing [their] fearful Kuṟiñci [tune] in the customary way
  • the forest people(?)[FNote_807] in [their] protected wide mansion don't sleep.
  • Does not[FNote_808] the bright-striped tiger that has beaten the strong elephant bull
  • growl on the mountin side in [its] stone cave?
  • Even if we will suffer, soft shoulders emaciating, when he
  • does not come today? [That] would be good indeed
  • - along the high mountin path, where [lightning] flashes brightly
  • on the flank of the high mountin [and] thunder dashes angrily(?),
  • so that the snake roams about in search of [its] brilliant gem,[FNote_809]
  • many days, as time passes by, confused by wind [and] rining.

  1. aḻuntupaṭu viḻuppuṇ vaḻumpuvāy pularā

  3. pirivila pulampi nuvaluṅ kuyiliṉun
  4. tēṟunīr keḻīiya yāṟunaṉi koṭitē

  7. pittikai viravumalar koḷḷī rōveṉa
  8. vaṇṭucūḻ vaṭṭiya ṭiritarun
  9. taṇṭalai yuḻavar taṉimaṭa makaḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Much crueller
  • as a lance thrown into a troubled heart,
  • the wet mouth of the terrible(?)[FNote_332] wound not drying, reaching deep,
  • than the Coels that lament [and] talk, they not separated,
  • [is] the river filled with clear water.
  • Crueller than that [is] she herself, excessively(?)[FNote_333],
  • the sole innocent daughter of the ploughman from the cool area,
  • who turns about the basket she holds, surrounded by bees,
  • [with the words] "won't you take the mixed blossoms of the large-flowered jasmine [and] the fresh Kurukkatti in cotton-headed clusters?"[FNote_334]

  1. cuṉippūk kuṟṟun toṭali tiiyu
  2. malicceṅ kāntaṭ kaṇṇi tantun
  3. taṉvaḻip paṭūu nannayan taruḷi
  4. veṟiyeṉa vuṇarnta variya vaṉṉiyik
  5. kaṇṇiṉuṅ kaṉaviṉuṅ kāṭṭi vinnō ((5))
  6. yeṉṉiṉum vārātu maṇiyiṟ ṟōṉṟu
  7. mammali kiḻavōṉ ceytaṉa ṉituveṉiṟ
  8. paṭuvaṇ ṭārkkum pintār māṟpi
  9. ṉeṭuvēṭ kēta muṭittō
  10. toṭiyōy kūṟumati viṉavuval yāṉē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if we(?) talk of this pin, he(?)[FNote_561] does not come,
  • being favorable(?)[FNote_562] [and] granting grace to us who are reverencing him,
  • plucking flowers in the mountin pool and knitting garlands
  • and giving chaplets of red Malabar-lilies from the mountin,
  • [and] showing mother by eye [or] in a dream
  • the difficulties, who had realised "[it is time for] the Veṟi dance".
  • "The lord of that mountin which appears like a sapphire has done it."
  • - if he sid this, would it afflict the great Vēḷ
  • with a chest with fresh garlands on which moving(?) bees are humming?
  • - O you with armlets, speak! I ask [you].

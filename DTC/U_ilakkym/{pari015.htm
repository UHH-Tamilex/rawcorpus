Paripāṭal 15

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 15-1} pula vari aṟiyā pukaḻoṭu polintu
  • {PARI 15-2} nila vari tāṅkiya nilimiyiṉ peyarā
  • {PARI 15-3} toliyā nēmi mutal tol ici amiyum
  • {PARI 15-4} pulavar āypu uritta puṉi neṭum kuṉṟam
  • {PARI 15-5} pala eṉiṉ āṅku avi pala ē palaviṉum
  • {PARI 15-6} nila vari āṟṟi niṟi payaṉ oruṅku uṭaṉ
  • {PARI 15-7} niṉṟu peṟa nikaḻum kuṉṟu avi cila ē
  • {PARI 15-8} cilaviṉum ciṟantaṉa teyvam peṭpuṟum
  • {PARI 15-9} malar akal mārpiṉ mi paṭi kuṭumiya
  • {PARI 15-10} kula vari cila ē kula vari cilaviṉum
  • {PARI 15-11} ciṟantatu kal aṟi kaṭal um kāṉal um pōla um
  • {PARI 15-12} pulliya col um poruḷ um pōla um
  • {PARI 15-13} ellām vēṟu vēṟu uruviṉ oru toḻil iruvar
  • {PARI 15-14} tāṅkum nīḷ nili ōṅku irum kuṉṟam
  • {PARI 15-15} nāṟu iṇar tuḻāyōṉ nalkiṉ allati
  • {PARI 15-16} ēṟutal eḷitu ō vīṟu peṟu tuṟakkam
  • {PARI 15-17} aritiṉ peṟu tuṟakkam mālirumkuṉṟam
  • {PARI 15-18} eḷitiṉ peṟal urimi ēttukam cilampa
  • {PARI 15-19} arā aṇar kayam tali tam muṉ mārpiṉ
  • {PARI 15-20} marā malar tāriṉ māṇ vara tōṉṟi
  • {PARI 15-21} alaṅkum aruvi ārttu imiḻpu iḻiya
  • {PARI 15-22} cilampāṟu aṇinta cīr keḻu tiruviṉ
  • {PARI 15-23} cōliyoṭu toṭar moḻi māliruṅkuṉṟam
  • {PARI 15-24} tām vīḻ kāmam vittupu viḷikkum
  • {PARI 15-25} nāma taṉmi naṉkaṉam paṭi eḻa
  • {PARI 15-26} yāma taṉmi i ai iruṅkuṉṟattu
  • {PARI 15-27} maṉ puṉal iḷa veyil vaḷāva iruḷ vaḷarvu eṉa
  • {PARI 15-28} poṉ puṉi uṭukkiyōṉ puṇarntu amar nili ē
  • {PARI 15-29} niṉimiṉ māntīr kēṇmiṉ kamaḻ cīr
  • {PARI 15-30} cuṉi elām nīlam malara cuṉi cūḻ
  • {PARI 15-31} ciṉi elām ceyali malara kāy kaṉi
  • {PARI 15-32} uṟaḻa naṉi vēṅki oḷ iṇar malara
  • {PARI 15-33} māyōṉ otta iṉ nilittu ē
  • {PARI 15-34} ceṉṟu toḻukallīr kaṇṭu paṇimiṉ ē
  • {PARI 15-35} iruṅkuṉṟu eṉṉum peyar parantatu ē
  • {PARI 15-36} perum kali ñālattu toṉṟu iyal pukaḻatu
  • {PARI 15-37} kaṇṭu mayar aṟukkum kāmakkaṭavuḷ
  • {PARI 15-38} maka muyaṅku manti vari vari pāya
  • {PARI 15-39} mukiḻ mayaṅku mulli muṟi nikaḻvu kāṭṭa
  • {PARI 15-40} maṇi maruḷ nal nīr ciṉi maṭa mayil akava
  • {PARI 15-41} kuruku ili utira kuyil iṉam kūva
  • {PARI 15-42} pakar kuḻal pāṇṭil iyampa akavunar
  • {PARI 15-43} nā navil pāṭal muḻavu etirntaṉṉa
  • {PARI 15-44} cilampiṉ cilampu ici ōvātu oṉṉār
  • {PARI 15-45} kaṭantu aṭṭāṉ kēḻ iruṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 15-46} tiyal avaroṭu um tantār avaroṭu um
  • {PARI 15-47} kimmakavoṭu um kātal avaroṭu um
  • {PARI 15-48} teyvam pēṇi tici toḻutaṉir celmiṉ
  • {PARI 15-49} puvva tāmari puriyum kaṇṇaṉ
  • {PARI 15-50} vuval kār iruḷ mayaṅku maṇi mēṉiyaṉ
  • {PARI 15-51} e vayiṉ ulakattu um tōṉṟi a vayiṉ
  • {PARI 15-52} maṉpatu maṟukka tuṉpam kaḷivōṉ
  • {PARI 15-53} aṉpu atu mēey iruṅkuṉṟattāṉ
  • {PARI 15-54} kaḷ aṇi pacum tuḷaviṉavi karum kuṉṟaṉiyavi
  • {PARI 15-55} oḷ oḷiyavi oru kuḻiyavi
  • {PARI 15-56} puḷ aṇi polam koṭiyavi
  • {PARI 15-57} vaḷ aṇi vaḷi nāñcilavi
  • {PARI 15-58} calam puri taṇṭu ēntiṉavi
  • {PARI 15-59} valampuri vaya nēmiyavi
  • {PARI 15-60} vari cili vaya ampiṉavi
  • {PARI 15-61} pukar iṇar cūḻ vaṭṭattavi pukar vāḷavi
  • {PARI 15-62} eṉa āṅku
  • {PARI 15-63} nalam purīi am cīr nāma vāymoḻi
  • {PARI 15-64} itu eṉa urittaṉ em uḷ amarntu icittu iṟi
  • {PARI 15-65} iruṅkuṉṟattu aṭiyuṟi iyika eṉa
  • {PARI 15-66} perum peyar iruvari paravutum toḻutu ē

Paripāṭal 15

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 15-1} pula varai aṟiyā pukaḻoṭu polintu
  • {PARI 15-2} nila varai tāṅkiya nilaimaiyiṉ peyarā
  • {PARI 15-3} tolaiyā nēmi mutal tol icai amaiyum
  • {PARI 15-4} pulavar āypu uraitta puṉai neṭum kuṉṟam
  • {PARI 15-5} pala eṉiṉ āṅku avai pala ē palaviṉum
  • {PARI 15-6} nila varai āṟṟi niṟai payaṉ oruṅku uṭaṉ
  • {PARI 15-7} niṉṟu peṟa nikaḻum kuṉṟu avai cila ē
  • {PARI 15-8} cilaviṉum ciṟantaṉa teyvam peṭpuṟum
  • {PARI 15-9} malar akal mārpiṉ mai paṭi kuṭumiya
  • {PARI 15-10} kula varai cila ē kula varai cilaviṉum
  • {PARI 15-11} ciṟantatu kal aṟai kaṭal um kāṉal um pōla um
  • {PARI 15-12} pulliya col um poruḷ um pōla um
  • {PARI 15-13} ellām vēṟu vēṟu uruviṉ oru toḻil iruvar
  • {PARI 15-14} tāṅkum nīḷ nilai ōṅku irum kuṉṟam
  • {PARI 15-15} nāṟu iṇar tuḻāyōṉ nalkiṉ allatai
  • {PARI 15-16} ēṟutal eḷitu ō vīṟu peṟu tuṟakkam
  • {PARI 15-17} aritiṉ peṟu tuṟakkam mālirumkuṉṟam
  • {PARI 15-18} eḷitiṉ peṟal urimai ēttukam cilampa
  • {PARI 15-19} arā aṇar kayam talai tam muṉ mārpiṉ
  • {PARI 15-20} marā malar tāriṉ māṇ vara tōṉṟi
  • {PARI 15-21} alaṅkum aruvi ārttu imiḻpu iḻiya
  • {PARI 15-22} cilampāṟu aṇinta cīr keḻu tiruviṉ
  • {PARI 15-23} cōlaiyoṭu toṭar moḻi māliruṅkuṉṟam
  • {PARI 15-24} tām vīḻ kāmam vittupu viḷaikkum
  • {PARI 15-25} nāma taṉmai naṉkaṉam paṭi eḻa
  • {PARI 15-26} yāma taṉmai i ai iruṅkuṉṟattu
  • {PARI 15-27} maṉ puṉal iḷa veyil vaḷāva iruḷ vaḷarvu eṉa
  • {PARI 15-28} poṉ puṉai uṭukkaiyōṉ puṇarntu amar nilai ē
  • {PARI 15-29} niṉaimiṉ māntīr kēṇmiṉ kamaḻ cīr
  • {PARI 15-30} cuṉai elām nīlam malara cuṉai cūḻ
  • {PARI 15-31} ciṉai elām ceyalai malara kāy kaṉi
  • {PARI 15-32} uṟaḻa naṉai vēṅkai oḷ iṇar malara
  • {PARI 15-33} māyōṉ otta iṉ nilaittu ē
  • {PARI 15-34} ceṉṟu toḻukallīr kaṇṭu paṇimiṉ ē
  • {PARI 15-35} iruṅkuṉṟu eṉṉum peyar parantatu ē
  • {PARI 15-36} perum kali ñālattu toṉṟu iyal pukaḻatu
  • {PARI 15-37} kaṇṭu mayar aṟukkum kāmakkaṭavuḷ
  • {PARI 15-38} maka muyaṅku manti varai varai pāya
  • {PARI 15-39} mukiḻ mayaṅku mullai muṟai nikaḻvu kāṭṭa
  • {PARI 15-40} maṇi maruḷ nal nīr ciṉai maṭa mayil akava
  • {PARI 15-41} kuruku ilai utira kuyil iṉam kūva
  • {PARI 15-42} pakar kuḻal pāṇṭil iyampa akavunar
  • {PARI 15-43} nā navil pāṭal muḻavu etirntaṉṉa
  • {PARI 15-44} cilampiṉ cilampu icai ōvātu oṉṉār
  • {PARI 15-45} kaṭantu aṭṭāṉ kēḻ iruṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 15-46} taiyal avaroṭu um tantār avaroṭu um
  • {PARI 15-47} kaimmakavoṭu um kātal avaroṭu um
  • {PARI 15-48} teyvam pēṇi ticai toḻutaṉir celmiṉ
  • {PARI 15-49} puvva tāmarai puraiyum kaṇṇaṉ
  • {PARI 15-50} vauval kār iruḷ mayaṅku maṇi mēṉiyaṉ
  • {PARI 15-51} e vayiṉ ulakattu um tōṉṟi a vayiṉ
  • {PARI 15-52} maṉpatu maṟukka tuṉpam kaḷaivōṉ
  • {PARI 15-53} aṉpu atu mēey iruṅkuṉṟattāṉ
  • {PARI 15-54} kaḷ aṇi pacum tuḷaviṉavai karum kuṉṟaṉaiyavai
  • {PARI 15-55} oḷ oḷiyavai oru kuḻaiyavai
  • {PARI 15-56} puḷ aṇi polam koṭiyavai
  • {PARI 15-57} vaḷ aṇi vaḷai nāñcilavai
  • {PARI 15-58} calam puri taṇṭu ēntiṉavai
  • {PARI 15-59} valampuri vaya nēmiyavai
  • {PARI 15-60} vari cilai vaya ampiṉavai
  • {PARI 15-61} pukar iṇar cūḻ vaṭṭattavai pukar vāḷavai
  • {PARI 15-62} eṉa āṅku
  • {PARI 15-63} nalam purīi am cīr nāma vāymoḻi
  • {PARI 15-64} itu eṉa uraittaṉ em uḷ amarntu icaittu iṟai
  • {PARI 15-65} iruṅkuṉṟattu aṭiyuṟai iyaika eṉa
  • {PARI 15-66} perum peyar iruvarai paravutum toḻutu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Young woman of tender nature,
  2. if I were to be content to abandon[FNote_558] you,
  3. for your good heart to become lonely,
  4. may the days that beggars abandon [and] don't come [to me]
  5. become many, in consequence[FNote_559] of my going.

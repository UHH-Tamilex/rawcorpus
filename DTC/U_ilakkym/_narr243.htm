{{Metrical text}}

  1. tēmpaṭu cilampiṟ ṟeḷḷaṟa ṟaḻīiya
  2. tuṟuka layala tūmaṇa laṭaikarai
  3. yalaṅkuciṉai potuḷiya naṟuvaṭi marāattup
  4. potumputō ṟalkum pūṅka ṇiruṅkuyil
  5. kavaṟupeyart taṉṉa nillā vāḻkkaiyiṭ ((5))
  6. ṭakaṟa lōmpumi ṉaṟivuṭai yīreṉak
  7. kaiyaṟat tuṟappōrk kaḻaṟuva pōla
  8. meyyuṟu viruntu mēvara nuvala
  9. viṉṉā tākiya kālaip poruḷvayiṟ
  10. pirita lāṭavaṟ kiyalpeṉi ((10))
  11. ṉaritumaṉ ṟamma vaṟattiṉum poruḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • At [this] time that is unpleasant,
  • since the flower-eyed dark Coels, that are abiding in every grove
  • of fragrant mango trees full of swaying branches
  • on the shore where pure sand settles near thick stones
  • enveloped by clear water from the honey-flowing mountain-side,
  • talk desirously of the festival held by bodies[FNote_768]
  • as if they were mumuring: "beware of departing, o you who possess knowledge,
  • for(?)[FNote_769] a livelihood[FNote_770] as impermanent as movement of dice",
  • if [you] say
  • it [is] natural to men to separate for wealth",
  • more difficult [to bear] than drought indeed [is] wealth.
{{s.trsl}}
  • 11b rarer than duty indeed [is] wealth.[FNote_771]

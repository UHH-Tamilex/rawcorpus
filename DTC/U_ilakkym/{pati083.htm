Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 83-1} kār maḻi muṉpiṉ ki parintu eḻutarum
  • {PATI 83-2} vāṉ paṟi kurukiṉ neṭu vari poṟpa
  • {PATI 83-3} kol kaḷiṟu miṭinta pal tōl toḻutiyoṭu
  • {PATI 83-4} neṭum tēr nuṭaṅku koṭi avirvara polintu
  • {PATI 83-5} celavu peritu iṉitu niṉ kāṇumōrkku ē
  • {PATI 83-6} iṉṉātu amma atu tāṉ ē pal mā
  • {PATI 83-7} nāṭu keṭa erukki nal kalam tarūum niṉ
  • {PATI 83-8} pōr arum kaṭum ciṉam etirntu
  • {PATI 83-9} māṟu koḷ vēntar pācaṟiyōrkku ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Becuse the newly calfed cow had eaten the Vayali rising by the house,
  • my apt(?) little dughter, who threw [her] ball to the ground,
  • rejected [her] doll [and] slapped that belly,[FNote_579]
  • did not eat the sweet milk with honey, while [her] mother and I
  • were feeding [her], with a mad look as if being at strife with the animal(?)[FNote_580].
  • As one swelling sobbingly(?)
  • even yesterday indeed she was like that. Today,
  • since she rejoices in the lies of the black-bearded young man,
  • she has gone to traverse the difficult desert, they say,
  • her teeth, white as quills, opened in a budding smile.

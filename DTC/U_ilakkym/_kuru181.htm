{{Readable Translation}}

  1. What is this after all, friend, the unpleasant word
  2. that says: 'that's what he's like', in midst of disgust[FNote_725]?
  3. [What is it] to us who have become respectable grown women[FNote_726]
  4. that have taken on many duties in the auspicious house
  5. of the man from the village where the buffalo cow, adorned(?)[FNote_727]
  6. after having given birth to a dark-horned buffalo,
  7. feeds on fresh corn, one part[FNote_728],
  8. without leaving the calf tied up by the ploughman?

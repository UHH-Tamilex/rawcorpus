{{Readable Translation}}

  • We [are] in [our] small village, that place wide inside,
  • where the sky flowers of the short-trunked Kura-tree bloom
  • in heaped clusters, close to the short fields of the woodland
  • cowherds,[FNote_832] to wear for the shepherd-son with [his] sheep.
  • That alone is worthy[FNote_833], even apart from desire:
  • If you depart, letting go of us, I tell
  • you one thing[FNote_834], may you live, lord: if the time
  • that made us wait, changing[FNote_835], retreats,
  • won't be the state of the great ones great?[FNote_836]

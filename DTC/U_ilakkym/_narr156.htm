{{Readable Translation}}

  • You, you have big love, o man from land of big stones,
  • coming through the difficult darkness when [movement] is restrained to knowing by foot and passing by the watch of our well-protected wide mansion.
  • We, singing you and your mountain for many days
  • we go and guard the small millet. Therefore
  • please come at midday. While many afflictions grow wide,
  • tarries the playing rain on our high-peaked hills
  • so that it thunders in clefts [and] caves, humming from the singing
  • of [our?] big one, even if they have become full of toddy
  • in the small hamlet on the big mountain where the reeads have grown long.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. muḻaṅkutirai keḻīiya mūri yekkart
  2. taṭantāṭ tāḻai muḷḷuṭai neṭuntōṭ
  3. ṭakamaṭaṟ potuḷiya mukaimutir vāṉpūk
  4. kōṭuvārn taṉṉa veṇpūt tāḻai
  5. yeṟitirai yutaittaliṟ poṅkit tātucōrpu ((5))
  6. ciṟukkuṭip pākkattu maṟukupulā maṟukku
  7. maṇaṅkamaḻ kāṉa liyaintanaṅ kēṇmai
  8. yorunāḷ piriyiṉu muyvari teṉṉātu
  9. kataḻpari neṭuntēr varavāṇ ṭaḻuṅkac
  10. ceytataṉ ṟappa laṉṟiyu ((10))
  11. muyavuppuṇarn taṉṟiv vaḻuṅka lūrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Without saying "escaping is difficult, even if separated for one day,
  • from our intimacy that was enjoyable in the seashore-grove fragrant with scent,
  • where the white flowers of the Tāḻai tree, as upright conches,
  • sky flowers ripening from buds that were dense on the inner stems(?)
  • in in thorny long masses on the broad-trunked Tāḻai tree
  • in the great dunes that were full of thundering waves,
  • [their] pollen dripping down as the dashing waves foam while striking(?),[FNote_642]
  • defy the stench of fish in the streets of the village with small homes",
  • apart from their mistake that was done
  • to spoil the coming there of the long chariot of hasty motion
  • this noisy village is united in suffering.

  4. ṭāṭiṉam varutali ṉiṉiyatu uṇṭō
  5. neṟipaṭu kūḻaik kārmutir pirunta ((5))

  7. pacalai pāytaru nutalu nōkki
  8. vaṟituku neñciṉaḷ piṟitoṉṟu cuṭṭi
  9. veyya vuyirttaṉaḷ yāyē
  10. yaiya vañciṉa maḷiyam yāmē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • We played[FNote_1027]
  • with you, adorning [our] hips of eminent curves with foliage,
  • swaying in the swing on the black-trunked Vēṅkai tree,
  • chasing away the small parrots that seizes the big field.
  • Is there anything sweeter[FNote_1028] than [your] coming?
  • Looking at [our] forehead where pallor spreads slightly
  • and at the fragrance brought by the fragrant scent(?)[FNote_1029],
  • that was, black colour ripening(?), in [our] curly short [hair],
  • mother with a vainly languishing heart sighed
  • hotly [and] pointed out something else,
  • o lord, we have come to fear, pitiable we.

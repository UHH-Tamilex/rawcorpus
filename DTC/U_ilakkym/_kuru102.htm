{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Remembering, it burns [us] inside;
  2. Not remembering, it is measureless[FNote_412] to us[FNote_413];
  3. Causing suffering it is such as to touch the skies - [that] desire.
  4. He is not noble(?)[FNote_414], the man with whom we joined.

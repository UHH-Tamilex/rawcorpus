  1. kuṟunilaik kuraviṉ ciṟunaṉai naṟuvī
  2. vaṇṭutaru nāṟṟam vaḷikalan tīyak
  3. kaṇkaḷi peṟūuṅ kaviṉpeṟu kālai
  4. yelvaḷai ñekiḻttōrk kalla luṟīiyar
  5. ceṉṟa neñcañ ceyviṉai kucāvā ((5))
  6. voruṅkuvara ṉacaiyoṭu varuntuṅ kollō
  7. aruḷā ṉātali ṉaḻintivaṇ vantu
  8. toṉṉala ṉiḻantaveṉ poṉṉiṟa nōkki
  9. yēti lāṭṭi yivaḷeṉap
  10. pōyiṉṟu kollō nōytalai maṇantē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • [My] heart, gone
  • to make [him] unhappy to the one who made loose [my] light bangles
  • at a time that obtains beauty, when the eye obtains delight
  • by trumpet-flower trees that mingled in the wind with the fragrance, attracting bees, of the small-budded fragrant blossoms of the short-trunked Kura tree,
  • does it suffer with yearning
  • of coming [back] together, without inquiring after the work to be done?
  • Is it gone [for good], completely united with pain,
  • perishing because he has not been considerate, coming [back] here,
  • looking at my gold colour that that has lost [its] old integrity,
  • saying "she is a stranger"?

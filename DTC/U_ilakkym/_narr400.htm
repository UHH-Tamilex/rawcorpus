  1. vāḻai meṉṟōṭu vārpuṟu pūkku
  2. nelviḷai kaḻaṉi nērkaṭ ceṟuvi
  3. ṉarivaṉa riṭṭa cūṭṭayaṟ periya
  4. viruñcuval vāḷai piṟaḻu mūra
  5. niṉṉiṉ ṟamaikuve ṉāyi ṉivaṇiṉ ((5))
  6. ṟiṉṉā nōkkamō ṭevaṉpiḻaip puṇṭō
  7. maṟaṅkeḻu cōḻa ruṟantai yavaiyat
  8. taṟaṅkeṭa vaṟiyā tāṅkuc ciṟanta
  9. kēṇmaiyō ṭaḷaii nīyē
  10. keṭuvaṟi yāyeṉ ṉeñcat tāṉē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a village, where the dark-shouldered Vāḷai fish are leaping,
  • big ones(?), near the ornaments(?) put [there] by the cutters
  • of the fields with fine sections(?)[FNote_1084] of paddy where the corn is ripening,
  • where the soft platain stems, having grown overlong[FNote_1085], are swinging,
  • if I be content without you, standing here,
  • with unbpleasing look, what mistake is [this]?
  • Mingled with superior intimacy, you,
  • as virtue does not know of getting lost
  • in the assembly in Uṟantai of the brave Cōḻars,
  • you do not know of getting lost from my heart.

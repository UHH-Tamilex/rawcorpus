{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Resembling the sandal on the slope possessed by [fearful] deities
  2. of mount Potiyil, difficult to approach, not known to those of stable life(?)[FNote_1498],
  3. in summer itself she [is] cool[FNote_1499] In the [time of] dew
  4. she is hot a little like in the inside
  5. the lotus, which has bundled[FNote_1500], ah[FNote_1501], the swaying sunlight
  6. [and] closes to hold together the subsiding[FNote_1502] [sun-]beams.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • When the many-beamed disk, having ably made the day,
  • goes to the great mountains in distant height [and] hides there,
  • while the birds settle with [their] young ones, in the woodland,
  • while the black-necked Iralai embraces [his] youthful doe
  • without(?)[FNote_250] the shrubs having lamps, since the Malabar lilies appeared,
  • when the flawless clear bell of the exhilarated good cows
  • becomes one with the flute of the cowherds with bent sticks,
  • comes over beautifully [and] sounds - evenings without consideration,
  • if they appear to be like that
  • also in the land where he has gone, who has departed for manly work,
  • he really cannot be content, compelled by work.

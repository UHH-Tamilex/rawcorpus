Patiṟṟup pattu

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 33-1} iṟumpūtu āl peritu ē koṭi tēr aṇṇal
  • {PATI 33-2} vaṭi maṇi aṇitta paṇi maruḷ nōṉ tāḷ
  • {PATI 33-3} kaṭi marattāṉ kaḷiṟu aṇittu
  • {PATI 33-4} neṭu nīra tuṟi kalaṅka
  • {PATI 33-5} mūḻttu iṟutta viyaṉ tāṉiyoṭu
  • {PATI 33-6} pulam keṭa neritarum varampu il veḷḷam
  • {PATI 33-7} vāḷ matil āka vēl miḷi uyarttu
  • {PATI 33-8} vil vici umiḻnta vim muḷ ampiṉ
  • {PATI 33-9} ce vāy eḵkam vaḷiiya akaḻiṉ
  • {PATI 33-10} kār iṭi urumiṉ uraṟu muraciṉ
  • {PATI 33-11} kāl vaḻaṅku ār eyil karutiṉ
  • {PATI 33-12} pōr etir vēntar orūupa niṉ ē

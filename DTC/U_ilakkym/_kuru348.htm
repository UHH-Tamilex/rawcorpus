  1. They themselves[FNote_1402], if they go[FNote_1403],
  2. don't they see, our men with glorious jewellery,
  3. also the wetting of the beautiful breast, the inside of the ornaments(?)[FNote_1404]
  4. of flourishing grace, by the dew of the eyes, which oozes destructively(?)[FNote_1405]
  5. through petal [lids][FNote_1406],
  6. spread like jasmine twigs[FNote_1407] with little blossoms,
  7. staying behind between the tusks of an elephant searching the fields of the forest?

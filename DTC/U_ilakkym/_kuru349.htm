{{Readable Translation}}

  1. After having linked [it] to the man from the cool ghat
  2. with dunes, where broad-footed herons are perched,
  3. which eat[FNote_1408] fish [and] waste the opening adorning blossoms of the Aṭumpu,
  4. we'll take our integrity, you say, friend, we'll take [it back].
  5. Even if [we] said 'give those things [back]', having offered
  6. them as they were needed by one who begged, for fear of [his] distress,
  7. [is that] less undear than the loss of our dear life?

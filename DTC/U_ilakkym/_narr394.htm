{{Metrical text}}

  1. marantali maṇanta naṉantalik kāṉat
  2. talantali ñemiyat tirunta kuṭiñi
  3. poṉcey kalaṉi ṉiṉiya teḷirppap
  4. peymaṇi yārkku miḻikiḷar neṭuntēr
  5. vaṉparaṉ murampi ṉēmi yatira ((5))
  6. ceṉṟiciṉ vāḻiyō paṉikaṭu nāḷē
  7. yiṭiccurat teḻili yuṟitteṉa mārpiṟ
  8. kuṟumpoṟik koṇṭa cāntamoṭu
  9. naṟuntaṇ ṇiyaṉko ṉōkō yāṉē.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. marantalai maṇanta naṉantalaik kāṉat
  2. talantalai ñemaiyat tirunta kuṭiñai
  3. poṉcey kalaṉi ṉiṉiya teḷirppap
  4. peymaṇi yārkku miḻaikiḷar neṭuntēr
  5. vaṉparaṉ murampi ṉēmi yatira ((5))
  6. ceṉṟiciṉ vāḻiyō paṉikaṭu nāḷē
  7. yiṭaiccurat teḻili yuṟaitteṉa mārpiṟ
  8. kuṟumpoṟik koṇṭa cāntamoṭu
  9. naṟuntaṇ ṇiyaṉko ṉōkō yāṉē.

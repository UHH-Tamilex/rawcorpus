{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, oh friend, [our] lover
  2. has separated[FNote_419] on a day when dew falls,
  3. where cows graze on the cool Tāḷi creeper [early in] the day,
  4. so that cool drops drip like pearls from a broken thread.
  5. All the days of separation are becoming many.

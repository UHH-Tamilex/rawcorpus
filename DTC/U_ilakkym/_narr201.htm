{{Readable Translation}}

  • The beloved [and] inexperienced dughter of the Kuṟavaṉ staying in the mountins, she [is] difficult to obtin, she [is] guarded by difficult protection,
  • she does not receive words - such is she.[FNote_638b]
  • Thinking of her is not appropriate is what you sid,[FNote_639b]
  • - of her who [is] in my heart [and] does not consent to going away,
  • just as the illusionary image
  • of good form more brilliant than saline soil(?) on the vast ground,
  • even if it thunders, wind dashing furiously, even if the hasty rin is rushing,
  • even if the thunders strike [all] together(?), even if obstacle appear in number,
  • on the side of the western mountin with pretty white waterfalls,
  • on the long evil-ending peak that the god guards,
  • on Kolli, fertile as a red-rooted jack-tree.

  1. urukeḻu yāṉi yuṭikoṇ ṭaṉṉa

  3. tayaṅkiruṅ kōta ṟūkkali ṉuṇṭātu
  4. vayaṅkiḻi makaḷir vaṇṭiṟ ṟāaṅ
  5. kāmar ciṟukuṭip pulampiṉu mavarkā ((5))
  6. ṇāmila mākuta laṟitu maṉṉō
  7. villeṟi pañci pōla malkutiri
  8. vaḷiporu vayaṅkupicir poṅku
  9. naḷikaṭaṟ cērppaṉoṭu nakāa vūṅkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • See him, more than(?) the loneliness in [our] desirable small hamlet,
  • where, as the bracelets of women with glowing jewels, the fine pollen
  • spreads, when [the wind] lifts the glittering big white Malabar lilies
  • on the sky bushes by the Tāḻi trees that opened [their] dense fetters,
  • as garments worn by beautiful elephants,
  • - did we really know we would become nothing
  • before we lughed with the man from the coast of the vast sea,
  • where glowing spray foams as the wind dashes at
  • the increasing waves, as cotton cleaned by an instrument[FNote_902]?

{{Metrical text}}

  1. eḻāa vākali ṉeḻiṉalan toliya
  2. vaḻāa tīmō notumalar taliyē
  3. yēṉal kāvalar māvīḻttup paṟitta
  4. pakaḻi yaṉṉa cēyari maḻikka
  5. ṇalla peruntō ḷōyē kolla ((5))
  6. ṉeṟipoṟ pitiriṟ ciṟupal kāya
  7. vēṅki vīyuku mōṅkumalik kaṭci
  8. mayilaṟi paṟiyā maṉṉō
  9. payilkural kavarum pimpuṟak kiḷiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Since [you] will not rise, don`t cry, before strangers,[FNote_47]
  • that [you] will loose [your] graceful goodness!
  • You of good big shoulders
  • [and] red-lined rin eyes like the arrows
  • pulled out, after the animal has been thrown down, by the millet guards!
  • Doesn`t it know of the peacock`s knowing,
  • in the nest on the high mountin where the Vēṅki tree sheds [its] blossoms,
  • burning like[FNote_48] many little[FNote_49] sparks from the iron beaten by the smith
  • - the green-backed parakeet which seizes the dense [millet] ears?[FNote_50]

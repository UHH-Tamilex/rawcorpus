{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Today, when the glowing sun, which sets out, slowly moves [on],
  2. [and] vanishes behind the great mountains,
  3. will he come, oh friend,
  4. who traversed the desert alongside of the long mountains,
  5. where the shiny-tusked elephant which stirs the waterless poor pond
  6. embraces his chosen mate by the low hill
  7. [and] guards [her] from the big tiger with curved stripes?[FNote_863]

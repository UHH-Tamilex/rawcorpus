  1. yāṉaḵ tañciṉeṉ karappavun tāṉaḵ

  3. vevaṉko ṟōḻi yaṉṉi kaṇṇiyatu
  4. vāṉuṟa nivanta perumalik kavāaṉ
  5. ṉārkali vāṉan talii naṭunāṭ ((5))
  6. kaṉipeyal poḻinteṉak kāṉak kalyāṟṟu
  7. muḷiyili kaḻittaṉa mukiṇar malaroṭu
  8. varuvirun tiṟṟīnīr maruntu mākun
  9. taṇṇeṉa vuṇṭu kaṇṇi ṉōkki
  10. muṉiyā tāṭa peṟiṉivaḷ ((10))
  11. paṉiyun tīrkuvaḷ celkeṉ ṟōḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • I was afrid of that, even though I hid [it]: has she herself
  • understood it? Is she considerate?
  • What is the thing mother has seen,
  • who sid: 'she may go, ending any fever,
  • if she could dance in front of it,
  • seeing with [her] eyes: it is cool,[FNote_207]
  • the water sweet as a coming festival being also a remedy,
  • with flowers [and] clusters of buds that have removed the dry leaves
  • in the river inside the forest, becuse intense rin has flown
  • at midnight, being offered from a fully bustling sky
  • on the slope of the big mountin come up to reach the sky.'[FNote_208]

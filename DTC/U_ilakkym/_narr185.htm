{{Readable Translation}}

  • With unending pin, perturbed by destructive affliction,
  • deed-ending misery, while desire exceeds
  • - if you don't grant even seeing [her],
  • she who is as the gloriously crafted image
  • drawn by the god on the long mountin full of honey,
  • in many-eyed honeycombs made by bees,
  • spreading on the swaying bunches of wide-leaved Malabar-lilies,
  • on the west sid of Kolli, the high mountin worthy of prise
  • of Poṟiyaṉ, where the beggers ascend without weakening
  • on the small paths in stony height struck by the broad hoofs
  • of broad-maned good horses, obtined as a gift by the bards,
  • she has indeed planned to kill [me]. Ah, I ache.

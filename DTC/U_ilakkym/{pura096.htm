  • {PU 96-1} alar pūm tumpi am pakaṭṭu mārpiṉ
  • {PU 96-2} tiraṇṭu nīṭu taṭam ki eṉṉi iḷiyōṟku
  • {PU 96-3} iraṇṭu eḻuntaṉa āl paki ē oṉṟu ē
  • {PU 96-4} pū pōl uṇ kaṇ pacantu tōḷ nuṇuki
  • {PU 96-5} nōkkiya makaḷir piṇittaṉṟu oṉṟu ē
  • {PU 96-6} viḻavu iṉṟu āyiṉum paṭu patam piḻiyātu
  • {PU 96-7} mi ūṉ mocitta okkaloṭu tuṟi nīr
  • {PU 96-8} kimāṉ koḷḷum ō eṉa
  • {PU 96-9} uṟiyuḷ muṉiyum avaṉ cellum ūr ē

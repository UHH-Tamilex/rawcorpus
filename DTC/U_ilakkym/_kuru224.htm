{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Sleepless, struck with the cruelty of him who has gone
  2. the long, afflicted way of Yām trees on crossroads[FNote_914],
  3. [her] pin has become more than pin.
  4. Like the dumb man from a high family,[FNote_915]
  5. who has seen at night the misery that has befallen the tawny cow in a well,
  6. I cannot bear the misery for [my] friend's pin.

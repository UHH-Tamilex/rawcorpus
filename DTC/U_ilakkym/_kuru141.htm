{{Readable Translation}}

  1. What if you sid[FNote_567], friend:
  2. 'mother sid: go chasing away from the
  3. ripening millet little hook-beaked parakeets'
  4. [and] 'don't come, man from the slope
  5. - you come in the midst of difficult darkness,
  6. where the male of the short-handed big tiger, strong in killing,
  7. who bore the enmity of the long-handed mighty elephant[FNote_568] in the woodlands,
  8. looks for the green-eyed red dog as [its] proper prey'?

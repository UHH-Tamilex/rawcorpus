{{Readable Translation}}

  • The love of great people is something permanent.[FNote_712] On top of that
  • the Kura trees have budded, made to come as the fore-front
  • of the early dewy season [which means] the time of the later dewy season is coming.
  • And summer[FNote_713] has come with pleasure,
  • when the voice of the red-eyed dark Coels echoes each other
  • on top of the clusters, saying "[now] unite, you who belong together".
  • What shall I say now
  • to him to clearly sid to us "we won't separate"?
  • - to him who has approached the difficult desert with hot fronts
  • across the crossroads swarming with work(?),
  • on the long paths with many trees that have whithered since the area perishes
  • while the ponds are subsiding.

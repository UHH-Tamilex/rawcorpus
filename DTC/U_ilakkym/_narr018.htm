{{Readable Translation}}

  • As many worries extend, with [my] heart being afflicted,
  • will he come, oh friend?
  • - the one who traversed the hills with a single shining waterfall,
  • like the broad-based one tusk
  • of the elephant whose fury is subdued when [his] must is washed off,
  • so that the sleepless warriors[FNote_77], whose hearts [he] made tremble,
  • pleasantly close [their] eyes, as if the waves subside in the sea,
  • in the encampment of Poṟaiyaṉ with an army of rare heat
  • [and] hot spears, the leader of Toṇṭi by the seashore grove,
  • in anger over Mūvaṉ pressing together [his] fully strong thorn teeth.[FNote_78]

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Those who separate for the northwind from the northern fields,
  • that presses, so that the pretty shafts of the bamboo sway,
  • on the nests made with effort by the birds ripe in wisdom(?),
  • seizing to unfold them the lance-like white buds
  • of the sugarcane, while the bed of dark many soft [tresses][FNote_1021] stays behind,
  • that are washed spotlessly in five sapphire-like parts
  • [and] accomplished, for bees with beautiful bees to come,
  • with short-stemmed blossoms of jasmine of the cool season,
  • by the little woman with big shoulders [and] hips with perfect jewels,
  • where, when the gliding fine cloth comes down from [her] waist,
  • many mixed pearl-strings glitter, as a snake emerging.[FNote_1022]

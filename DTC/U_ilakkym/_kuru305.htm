{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if the bright flame of desire, come since the eye gives [it](?),
  2. afflicts [me] to the bone, we [are] a rare sight[FNote_1244] for embracing,
  3. having gone tenderly with him(?)[FNote_1245].
  4. He is not able to come [and] remove [my] sorrow.
  5. No one is there to remove the pain I have,
  6. if not it perishes[FNote_1246], [then it means for me] perishing thus,[FNote_1247]
  7. like cocks in a lone battle over a heap:
  8. he who has sent [them and] does not let [them](?) doesn't separate [and] in between remove [them].[FNote_1248]

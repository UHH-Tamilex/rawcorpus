{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Jasmine, standing up to the rain[FNote_646], in the wide fields(?) full of water,
  2. on an empty evening, when many go home[FNote_647],
  3. live, jasmine, you took on
  4. a smile with [your] little white buds.
  5. To show yourself as if laughing
  6. - is this what is fitting[FNote_648] towards those who are alone?

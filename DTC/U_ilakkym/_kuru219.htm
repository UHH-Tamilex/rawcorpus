{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Pale is my body. The place of longing in his
  2. bond-free heart is difficult [to obtain].[FNote_883]
  3. Even restraint(?) stayed aside in the distance. Knowledge.[FNote_884]
  4. To say here: 'we'll go there, rise!'[FNote_885]
  5. If I were to say[FNote_886]: 'of what kind [are] you?', friend, [that] indeed [would be] the opportunity
  6. for the man from the coast of thorny-leafed, broad-based Tāḻai trees,
  7. who permanently[FNote_887] talks without being able [to act].(?!)[FNote_888]

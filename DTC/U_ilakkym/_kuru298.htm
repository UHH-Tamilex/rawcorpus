{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Think of the suffering look, friend,
  2. of him who comes agin and agin, gently, to visit [our] street,
  3. rarely opens [his] mouth, speaks pleasant [things],
  4. [and] stays every day, [his] colour changed.[FNote_1225]
  5. His long standing at [my] back means something different,
  6. like the gift of a youthful[FNote_1226] elephant cow
  7. to the sooth-saying woman with the white-tipped small staff,
  8. [standing] behind[FNote_1227] Akuti of sweet, strong toddy.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You may go, girl,
  • so that the dashing wind whiffles your flower-wearing tresses,
  • as a peacock with a sapphire-like neck,
  • that moves forward, spreading [its] adorning shining feathers wonderfully
  • at the time of seeing when the drops flowed[FNote_824] from the sky,
  • forcefully risen [and] thundering for the snake to keep to [its] hole.
  • In the time when light fails
  • see yonder: there appears our small good village,
  • where the ornamental clear bells of the cows tied by the cowherds
  • make sound in the rustling bamboo thicket.

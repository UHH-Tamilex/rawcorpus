{{Readable Translation}}

  • We knew of the non-existence of oneness in birth[FNote_952]
  • with the man from a land of big stones, where they chase away the parrots from the millet,
  • where bulbous roots are extending below[FNote_953], honey[-combs] hanging above,
  • where they sow little little [and] reap very much. Therefore,
  • That now, live, friend, [is] the one day:
  • being of small many amounts let it rise with strength
  • [and] let great rain fall liberally on the fields. Now,
  • in this village the dancing girl will dance, obtaining ornaments
  • on all sides(?) of the transverse mountain,
  • on the high huge slope, grown abundantly with sandal,
  • not needing the white cloth with oil pressed out from sesame.(?)[FNote_954]

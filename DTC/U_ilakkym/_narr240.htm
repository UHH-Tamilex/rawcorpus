{{Readable Translation}}

  • Let him go easily, him who created this world.
  • Even if it is of a kind that sleep comes at intervalls
  • to the bosom with swelling breasts that sighed lamentingly,
  • wrung [by desire] to bodily have [his] embrace, seized by [his] hands,
  • of the little woman with bright forehead [and] sharp upright pure teeth,
  • - it is like the firm forest mountains,
  • where the row of a group of elephants snatches
  • the hollowed bucket of those that are on the way to the cows,
  • situated at the well hollowed out by the chisel
  • in a separate spot of the pebbly depression become hot in the sunlight.

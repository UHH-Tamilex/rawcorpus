{{Readable Translation}}

  1. 1.
  2. Believing the man from the land where the river voices [its] fullness
  3. [and] scatters big drops like a waterfall,
  4. it really happened.[FNote_1097] One day only [was] that.
  5. Very many days have been [my] shoulders[FNote_1098] confused
  6. [and that] of a nature which attracts[FNote_1099] became pin.

Altern

5b: [and] it became pin of the gripping kind.

Altern

2.

  • Believing the man from the land where the river voices [its] fullness
  • [and] scatters big drops like a waterfall,
  • it really happened one day. But that [same thing],
  • after distressing [my] shoulders for many days,
  • became a pin of a nature that devours.[FNote_1100]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Here[FNote_604] [is] the end for youth - to say: I traverse[FNote_605],
  2. while [my] love, who is difficult to forget, stays behind,
  3. without saying they are difficult, the small hilly ways
  4. where the red-legged female left behind by the Vaṅkā bird
  5. is calling many short [notes] in flute-sounding tones(?)[FNote_606],
  6. without seeing [her] husband, becuse the Eḻāl bird has descended to take her.

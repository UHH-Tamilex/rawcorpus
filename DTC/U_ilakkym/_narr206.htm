{{Readable Translation}}

  • When my father came,
  • [thinking] "the millet [plants] have come to sway in masses,
  • [their] cotton-headed newly born ears bowing, overflowing with milk"[FNote_655]
  • [and] "crowding on the height of thick stomes, [ready] to pluck
  • the green group of red-beaks will seize [our crop]"
  • [and] said "in that place
  • you may put together the sling [and] thrash the Taṭṭai"
  • and mother
  • looked at my face, saying "may the Vēṅkai too blossom
  • on a good day now". What [of this], friend?
  • Does it say she will go? Does it say I shall keep her back?
  • [Our] intimacy with the man from the stony land,
  • has she understood it? That I do not know.

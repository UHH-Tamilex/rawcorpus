{{Readable Translation}}

  1. On the way that the lover has gone on whom we [are] keen,
  2. who has set out bound by desired wealth
  3. somewhere, intending to fulfill his duty,
  4. he will have seen[FNote_1029], friend, an elephant with broad tusks,
  5. who puts an end to the hunger suffered by a big row of small-eyed [elephants]
  6. with weakened git, piercing [and] bending with [its] brave broad trunk,
  7. so that the whole base is peeled, the parched trunk
  8. of the Yām tree by the road, the core of which has no hollow.[FNote_1030]

Altern

6b: reckoning to be broken by his duty[FNote_1031]

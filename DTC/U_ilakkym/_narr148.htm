{{Metrical text}}

  1. vaṇṇa nōkkiyu meṉmoḻi kūṟiyu
  2. nīyavaṇ varuta lāṟṟā yeṉattān
  3. toṭaṅki yāḷviṉaip pirintō riṉṟē
  4. neṭuṅkayam pularnta nīri ṉīḷiṭaic
  5. ceṅkāṉ marāat tampuṭai porunti ((5))
  6. vāṅkucilai maṟavar vīṅkunilai yañcātu
  7. kallaḷaic ceṟinta vaḷḷukirp piṇavi
  8. ṉiṉpuṉiṟ ṟiṭumpai tīrac ciṉañciṟantu
  9. ceṅka ṇirumpulik kōlva lēṟṟai
  10. yuyarmarup poruttaṟ pukarmukam pāyu ((10))
  11. maruñcura miṟappa veṉpa
  12. varuntēṉ ṟōḻi vāykavar colavē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even when looking at [my] colour, even when talking soft words,
  • he himself began saying "you cannot come there"
  • [and] separated for male work today.
  • While he traverses the difficult desert,
  • where the male strong in killing of the red-eyed tiger
  • jumps into the spotted face of the high-tusked male [elephant],
  • [his] anger superior in order to end the distress of the sweet newly born one
  • of [his] sharp-nailed female kept within the stone hole,
  • without fear of the copious state[FNote_473] of the bold ones with bent bows,
  • coming together with [their] arrows[FNote_474] by the red-trunked Marā tree
  • on the waterless long way where the long tanks have dried up, they talk.[FNote_475]
  • I do not suffer, friend. Let [their] speaking of him prevail(?).

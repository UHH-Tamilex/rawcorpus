{{Metrical text}}

  1. uraiyāy vāḻi tōḻi yiruṅkaḻi
  2. yiraiyār kuruki ṉiraippaṟait toḻuti
  3. vāṅkumaṭaṟ kuṭampait tūṅkiruṭ ṭuvaṉṟum
  4. peṇṇai yōṅkiya veṇmaṇaṟ paṭappaik
  5. kāṉa lāyamoṭu kālaik kuṟṟa ((5))
  6. kaṭkama ḻalara taṇṇaṟuṅ kāvi
  7. yampakai neṟittaḻai yaṇipeṟat taii
  8. varipuṉai ciṟṟiṟ pariciṟan tōṭip
  9. pulavuttirai yutaitta koṭuntāṭ kaṇṭaṟ
  10. cempē riraṇai yalavaṟ pārkkuñ ((10))
  11. ciṟuviḷai yāṭalu maḻuṅka
  12. niṉakkuperun tuyara mākiya nōyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You don't talk, friend,
  • of the pain that has become a great misery for you
  • so that even a playing a little is spoiled: when [you][FNote_403]
  • when [you] run, faster in motion, to the small houses adorned with lines,
  • when [you] peer out for the crabs in red big pairs(?)
  • by the mangroves with bent trunks, kicked by the fish-reeking waves knit together, to get a decoration, the foliage [and] calyxes, in pretty enmity,
  • of the cool fragrant blue water-lily with blossoms fragrant of nectar
  • plucked in the morning with the companions in the seashore grove,
  • in the garden of white sand grown high with where the plamyra palms,
  • where the hanging darkness of the nests on the bent palmyra stems is filled up
  • by a multitude of wings in a row of the herons feeding on prey
  • in the dark backwaters.

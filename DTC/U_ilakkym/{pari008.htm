Paripāṭal 8

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 8-1} maṇ micai aviḻ tuḻāy malar taru celvattu
  • {PARI 8-2} puḷmicaikoṭiyōṉ um puṅkavamūrvōṉ um
  • {PARI 8-3} malar micai mutalvaṉ um maṟṟu avaṉ iṭai tōṉṟi
  • {PARI 8-4} ulaku iruḷ akaṟṟiya patiṉmar um iruvar um
  • {PARI 8-5} maruntu urai iruvar um tiruntu nūl eṇmar um
  • {PARI 8-6} ātirai mutalvaṉiṉ kiḷanta
  • {PARI 8-7} nātar paṉṉoruvar um nal ticai kāppōr um
  • {PARI 8-8} yāvar um piṟar um amarar um avuṇar um
  • {PARI 8-9} mēvaru mutumoḻi viḻu tava mutalvar um
  • {PARI 8-10} paṟṟu ākiṉṟu niṉ kāraṇam āka
  • {PARI 8-11} paraṅkuṉṟu imaya kuṉṟam nikarkkum
  • {PARI 8-12} imaya kuṉṟiṉil ciṟantu
  • {PARI 8-13} niṉ īṉṟa nirai itaḻ tāmarai
  • {PARI 8-14} miṉ īṉṟa viḷaṅku iṇar ūḻā
  • {PARI 8-15} oru nilai poykaiyōṭu okkum niṉ kuṉṟiṉ
  • {PARI 8-16} aruvi tāḻ mālai cuṉai
  • {PARI 8-17} mutalva niṉ yāṉai muḻakkam kēṭṭa
  • {PARI 8-18} katiyiṟṟu ē kāriṉ kural
  • {PARI 8-19} kural kēṭṭa kōḻi kuṉṟu atira kūva
  • {PARI 8-20} mata naṉi vāraṇam māṟu māṟu atirppa
  • {PARI 8-21} etirkutir ākiṉṟu atirppu malai muḻai
  • {PARI 8-22} ēḻ puḻai aim puḻai yāḻ icai kēḻttu aṉṉa iṉam
  • {PARI 8-23} vīḻ tumpi vaṇṭoṭu miñiṟu ārppa cuṉai malara
  • {PARI 8-24} koṉṟai koṭi iṇar ūḻppa koṭi malar
  • {PARI 8-25} maṉṟala malara malar kāntaḷ vāy nāṟa
  • {PARI 8-26} naṉṟu aviḻ pal malar nāṟa naṟai paṉippa
  • {PARI 8-27} teṉṟal acaivarūum cemmaṟṟu ē amma niṉ
  • {PARI 8-28} kuṉṟattāṉ kūṭal varavu
  • {PARI 8-29} kuṉṟam uṭaitta oḷir vēlōy kūṭal
  • {PARI 8-30} maṉṟal kalanta maṇi muraciṉ ārppu eḻa
  • {PARI 8-31} kāloṭu mayaṅkiya kaliḻ kaṭal eṉa
  • {PARI 8-32} māl kaṭal kuṭikkum maḻai kural eṉa
  • {PARI 8-33} ēṟu atirkkum intiraṉ irum urum eṉa
  • {PARI 8-34} maṉṟal atira atira māṟu māṟu atirkkum niṉ
  • {PARI 8-35} kuṉṟam kumuṟiya urai
  • {PARI 8-36} tūtu ēya vaṇṭiṉ toḻuti muralvu avar
  • {PARI 8-37} kātal mūtūr matil kampalaittaṉṟu
  • {PARI 8-38} vaṭu vakir veṉṟa kaṇ mā taḷir mēṉi
  • {PARI 8-39} neṭu mel paṇai tōḷ kuṟum toṭi makaḷir
  • {PARI 8-40} ārā kāmam ār poḻil pāyal
  • {PARI 8-41} varai akattu iyaikkum varaiyā nukarcci
  • {PARI 8-42} muṭiyā nukarcci muṟṟā kātal
  • {PARI 8-43} aṭiyōr maintar akalattu akalā
  • {PARI 8-44} alar ñemal makaṉṟil naṉṉar puṇarcci
  • {PARI 8-45} pularā makiḻ maṟappu aṟiyātu nalkum
  • {PARI 8-46} ciṟappiṟṟu ē taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 8-47} iṉi maṉṉum ētilar nāṟuti āṇṭu
  • {PARI 8-48} paṉi malar kaṇṇārōṭu āṭa nakai malar
  • {PARI 8-49} mālaikku mālai varūum varai cūḷ nil
  • {PARI 8-50} kālai pōy mālai varavu
  • {PARI 8-51} iṉi maṇal vaiyai irum poḻil um kuṉṟa
  • {PARI 8-52} paṉi poḻi cāral um pārppār um
  • {PARI 8-53} tuṉiyal malar uṇ kaṇ col vēṟu nāṟṟam
  • {PARI 8-54} kaṉiyiṉ malariṉ malir kāl cīppu iṉṉatu
  • {PARI 8-55} tuṉiyal naṉi nī niṉ cūḷ
  • {PARI 8-56} eṉ pāṇi nil nil elāa pāṇi nī niṉ cūḷ
  • {PARI 8-57} cāṉṟāḷar īṉṟa takāa takāa makāaṉ
  • {PARI 8-58} īṉṟāṭku oru peṇ ivaḷ
  • {PARI 8-59} iruḷ mai īr uṇ kaṇ ilaṅku iḻai īṉṟāṭku
  • {PARI 8-60} ariyaḷ ō āvatu aṟintilēṉ ītā
  • {PARI 8-61} varu puṉal vaiyai maṇal toṭṭēṉ taru maṇa vēḷ
  • {PARI 8-62} taṇ paraṅkuṉṟattu aṭi toṭṭēṉ eṉpāy
  • {PARI 8-63} kēḷir maṇaliṉ keḻuvu um itu ō
  • {PARI 8-64} ēḻ ulaku um āḷi tiru varai mēl aṉpu aḷitu ō
  • {PARI 8-65} eṉṉai aruḷi aruḷ muruku cūḷ cūḷiṉ
  • {PARI 8-66} niṉṉai aruḷ il aṇaṅkāṉ mey vēl tiṉṉum
  • {PARI 8-67} viṟal veyyōṉ ūr mayil vēl niḻal nōkki
  • {PARI 8-68} aṟavar aṭi toṭiṉum āṅku avai cūḷēl
  • {PARI 8-69} kuṟavaṉ makaḷ āṇai kūṟu ēlā kūṟēl
  • {PARI 8-70} aiya cūḷiṉ toṭu kuṉṟoṭu
  • {PARI 8-71} vaiyaikku takka maṇal cīr cūḷ kūṟal
  • {PARI 8-72} yār piriya yār vara yār viṉava yār ceppu
  • {PARI 8-73} nīr uraicey nīrmai il cūḷ eṉṟi nēr iḻāy
  • {PARI 8-74} kaya vāya neytal alar kamaḻ mukai maṇa nakai
  • {PARI 8-75} nayavaru naṟavu itaḻ matar uṇ kaṇ vāḷ nutal
  • {PARI 8-76} mukai mullai veṉṟu eḻil muttu ēykkum veḷ pal
  • {PARI 8-77} nakai cāṉṟa kaṉavu aṉṟu naṉavu aṉṟu naviṉṟatu ai
  • {PARI 8-78} iṭu tuṉi kai āṟā eṉ tuyar kūra
  • {PARI 8-79} cuṭum iṟai āṟṟiciṉ aṭi cērntu cāṟṟumiṉ
  • {PARI 8-80} mika eṟṟutum malar ūṭṭutum avi
  • {PARI 8-81} kēṭṭutum pāṇi eḻutum kiṇai murukaṉ
  • {PARI 8-82} tāḷ toḻu taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 8-83} teri iḻāy celka eṉṟāy ellā yām peṟṟēm
  • {PARI 8-84} oruvarkku um poyyā niṉ vāy il cūḷ vauval
  • {PARI 8-85} paruvattu pal māṇ nī cēṟaliṉ kāṇṭai
  • {PARI 8-86} erumai irum tōṭṭi eḷḷīyum kāḷai
  • {PARI 8-87} ceruvam ceyaṟku eṉṉai muṉṉai taṉ ceṉṉi
  • {PARI 8-88} aruḷ vayiṉāṉ tūṅku maṇi kaiyāl tākki
  • {PARI 8-89} nirai vaḷai āṟṟu irum cūḷ
  • {PARI 8-90} vaḷi poru cēṇ cimai varai akattāl
  • {PARI 8-91} taḷi perukum taṇ ciṉaiya
  • {PARI 8-92} poḻil koḷa kuṟaiyā malara
  • {PARI 8-93} kuḷir poykai aḷaṟu niṟaiya
  • {PARI 8-94} marutam naḷi maṇal ñemarnta
  • {PARI 8-95} naṉi malar peru vaḻi
  • {PARI 8-96} cīṟaṭiyavar cāṟu koḷa eḻuntu
  • {PARI 8-97} vēṟupaṭu cāntam um vīṟupaṭu pukai um
  • {PARI 8-98} āṟu cel vaḷiyiṉ aviyā viḷakkam um
  • {PARI 8-99} nāṟu kamaḻ vī um kūṟum icai muḻavam um
  • {PARI 8-100} maṇi um kayiṟu um mayil um kuṭāri um
  • {PARI 8-101} piṇimukam uḷappaṭa piṟa um ēnti
  • {PARI 8-102} aru varai cērā toḻunar
  • {PARI 8-103} kaṉaviṉ toṭṭatu kai piḻai ākātu
  • {PARI 8-104} naṉaviṉ cēeppa niṉ naḷi puṉal vaiyai
  • {PARI 8-105} varu puṉal aṇika eṉa varam koḷvōr um
  • {PARI 8-106} karu vayiṟu uṟuka eṉa kaṭampaṭuvōr um
  • {PARI 8-107} cey poruḷ vāykka eṉa cevi cārttuvōr um
  • {PARI 8-108} ai amar aṭuka eṉa arucci pōr um
  • {PARI 8-109} pāṭuvār pāṇi cīr um āṭuvār araṅka tāḷam um
  • {PARI 8-110} mañcu āṭu malai muḻakku um
  • {PARI 8-111} tuñcā kampalai
  • {PARI 8-112} paim cuṉai pāay eḻu pāvaiyar
  • {PARI 8-113} āy itaḻ uṇ kaṇ alar muka tāmarai
  • {PARI 8-114} tāḷ tāmarai tōḷ tamaṉiya kaya malar
  • {PARI 8-115} em kai patumam koṅkai kaya mukai
  • {PARI 8-116} ce vāy āmpal cel nīr tāmarai
  • {PARI 8-117} puṉal tāmaraiyoṭu pulam vēṟupāṭuṟā
  • {PARI 8-118} kūr ēyiṟṟār kuvi mulai pūṇoṭu
  • {PARI 8-119} māraṉ oppār mārpu aṇi kalavi
  • {PARI 8-120} arivaiyar amir tapāṉam
  • {PARI 8-121} urimai mākkaḷ uvakai amirtu uyppa
  • {PARI 8-122} maintar mārvam vaḻi vanta
  • {PARI 8-123} ce taḷir mēṉiyār cellal tīrppa
  • {PARI 8-124} eṉa āṅku
  • {PARI 8-125} uṭam puṇar kātalar um allār um kūṭi
  • {PARI 8-126} kaṭampu amar celvaṉ kaṭi nakar pēṇa
  • {PARI 8-127} maṟu miṭaṟṟu aṇṇaṟku mācilōḷ tanta
  • {PARI 8-128} neṟi nīr aruvi acumpu uṟu celvam
  • {PARI 8-129} maṇ pariya vāṉam vaṟappiṉ um maṉṉuka mā
  • {PARI 8-130} taṇ paraṅkuṉṟam niṉakku

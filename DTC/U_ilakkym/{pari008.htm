Paripāṭal 8

  • {PARI 8-1} maṇ mici aviḻ tuḻāy malar taru celvattu

  • {PARI 8-12} imaya kuṉṟiṉil ciṟantu
  • {PARI 8-13} niṉ īṉṟa niri itaḻ tāmari
  • {PARI 8-14} miṉ īṉṟa viḷaṅku iṇar ūḻā

  • {PARI 8-20} mata naṉi vāraṇam māṟu māṟu atirppa
  • {PARI 8-21} etirkutir ākiṉṟu atirppu mali muḻi
  • {PARI 8-22} ēḻ puḻi aim puḻi yāḻ ici kēḻttu aṉṉa iṉam
  • {PARI 8-23} vīḻ tumpi vaṇṭoṭu miñiṟu ārppa cuṉi malara
  • {PARI 8-24} koṉṟi koṭi iṇar ūḻppa koṭi malar
  • {PARI 8-25} maṉṟala malara malar kāntaḷ vāy nāṟa
  • {PARI 8-26} naṉṟu aviḻ pal malar nāṟa naṟi paṉippa
  • {PARI 8-27} teṉṟal acivarūum cemmaṟṟu ē amma niṉ
  • {PARI 8-28} kuṉṟattāṉ kūṭal varavu
  • {PARI 8-29} kuṉṟam uṭitta oḷir vēlōy kūṭal
  • {PARI 8-30} maṉṟal kalanta maṇi muraciṉ ārppu eḻa
  • {PARI 8-31} kāloṭu mayaṅkiya kaliḻ kaṭal eṉa
  • {PARI 8-32} māl kaṭal kuṭikkum maḻi kural eṉa
  • {PARI 8-33} ēṟu atirkkum intiraṉ irum urum eṉa
  • {PARI 8-34} maṉṟal atira atira māṟu māṟu atirkkum niṉ
  • {PARI 8-35} kuṉṟam kumuṟiya uri
  • {PARI 8-36} tūtu ēya vaṇṭiṉ toḻuti muralvu avar
  • {PARI 8-37} kātal mūtūr matil kampalittaṉṟu
  • {PARI 8-38} vaṭu vakir veṉṟa kaṇ mā taḷir mēṉi
  • {PARI 8-39} neṭu mel paṇi tōḷ kuṟum toṭi makaḷir
  • {PARI 8-40} ārā kāmam ār poḻil pāyal
  • {PARI 8-41} vari akattu iyikkum variyā nukarcci
  • {PARI 8-42} muṭiyā nukarcci muṟṟā kātal
  • {PARI 8-43} aṭiyōr mintar akalattu akalā
  • {PARI 8-44} alar ñemal makaṉṟil naṉṉar puṇarcci
  • {PARI 8-45} pularā makiḻ maṟappu aṟiyātu nalkum
  • {PARI 8-46} ciṟappiṟṟu ē taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 8-47} iṉi maṉṉum ētilar nāṟuti āṇṭu
  • {PARI 8-48} paṉi malar kaṇṇārōṭu āṭa naki malar
  • {PARI 8-49} mālikku māli varūum vari cūḷ nil
  • {PARI 8-50} kāli pōy māli varavu
  • {PARI 8-51} iṉi maṇal viyi irum poḻil um kuṉṟa
  • {PARI 8-52} paṉi poḻi cāral um pārppār um

  • {PARI 8-54} kaṉiyiṉ malariṉ malir kāl cīppu iṉṉatu
  • {PARI 8-55} tuṉiyal naṉi nī niṉ cūḷ
  • {PARI 8-56} eṉ pāṇi nil nil elāa pāṇi nī niṉ cūḷ
  • {PARI 8-57} cāṉṟāḷar īṉṟa takāa takāa makāaṉ
  • {PARI 8-58} īṉṟāṭku oru peṇ ivaḷ
  • {PARI 8-59} iruḷ mi īr uṇ kaṇ ilaṅku iḻi īṉṟāṭku
  • {PARI 8-60} ariyaḷ ō āvatu aṟintilēṉ ītā
  • {PARI 8-61} varu puṉal viyi maṇal toṭṭēṉ taru maṇa vēḷ
  • {PARI 8-62} taṇ paraṅkuṉṟattu aṭi toṭṭēṉ eṉpāy
  • {PARI 8-63} kēḷir maṇaliṉ keḻuvu um itu ō
  • {PARI 8-64} ēḻ ulaku um āḷi tiru vari mēl aṉpu aḷitu ō
  • {PARI 8-65} eṉṉi aruḷi aruḷ muruku cūḷ cūḷiṉ
  • {PARI 8-66} niṉṉi aruḷ il aṇaṅkāṉ mey vēl tiṉṉum
  • {PARI 8-67} viṟal veyyōṉ ūr mayil vēl niḻal nōkki
  • {PARI 8-68} aṟavar aṭi toṭiṉum āṅku avi cūḷēl
  • {PARI 8-69} kuṟavaṉ makaḷ āṇi kūṟu ēlā kūṟēl
  • {PARI 8-70} aiya cūḷiṉ toṭu kuṉṟoṭu
  • {PARI 8-71} viyikku takka maṇal cīr cūḷ kūṟal
  • {PARI 8-72} yār piriya yār vara yār viṉava yār ceppu
  • {PARI 8-73} nīr uricey nīrmi il cūḷ eṉṟi nēr iḻāy
  • {PARI 8-74} kaya vāya neytal alar kamaḻ muki maṇa naki
  • {PARI 8-75} nayavaru naṟavu itaḻ matar uṇ kaṇ vāḷ nutal
  • {PARI 8-76} muki mulli veṉṟu eḻil muttu ēykkum veḷ pal
  • {PARI 8-77} naki cāṉṟa kaṉavu aṉṟu naṉavu aṉṟu naviṉṟatu ai
  • {PARI 8-78} iṭu tuṉi ki āṟā eṉ tuyar kūra
  • {PARI 8-79} cuṭum iṟi āṟṟiciṉ aṭi cērntu cāṟṟumiṉ
  • {PARI 8-80} mika eṟṟutum malar ūṭṭutum avi
  • {PARI 8-81} kēṭṭutum pāṇi eḻutum kiṇi murukaṉ
  • {PARI 8-82} tāḷ toḻu taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 8-83} teri iḻāy celka eṉṟāy ellā yām peṟṟēm
  • {PARI 8-84} oruvarkku um poyyā niṉ vāy il cūḷ vuval
  • {PARI 8-85} paruvattu pal māṇ nī cēṟaliṉ kāṇṭi
  • {PARI 8-86} erumi irum tōṭṭi eḷḷīyum kāḷi
  • {PARI 8-87} ceruvam ceyaṟku eṉṉi muṉṉi taṉ ceṉṉi
  • {PARI 8-88} aruḷ vayiṉāṉ tūṅku maṇi kiyāl tākki
  • {PARI 8-89} niri vaḷi āṟṟu irum cūḷ
  • {PARI 8-90} vaḷi poru cēṇ cimi vari akattāl
  • {PARI 8-91} taḷi perukum taṇ ciṉiya
  • {PARI 8-92} poḻil koḷa kuṟiyā malara
  • {PARI 8-93} kuḷir poyki aḷaṟu niṟiya
  • {PARI 8-94} marutam naḷi maṇal ñemarnta
  • {PARI 8-95} naṉi malar peru vaḻi
  • {PARI 8-96} cīṟaṭiyavar cāṟu koḷa eḻuntu

  • {PARI 8-99} nāṟu kamaḻ vī um kūṟum ici muḻavam um

  • {PARI 8-103} kaṉaviṉ toṭṭatu ki piḻi ākātu
  • {PARI 8-104} naṉaviṉ cēeppa niṉ naḷi puṉal viyi
  • {PARI 8-105} varu puṉal aṇika eṉa varam koḷvōr um
  • {PARI 8-106} karu vayiṟu uṟuka eṉa kaṭampaṭuvōr um

  • {PARI 8-115} em ki patumam koṅki kaya muki
  • {PARI 8-116} ce vāy āmpal cel nīr tāmari

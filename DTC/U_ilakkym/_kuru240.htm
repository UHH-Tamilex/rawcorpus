{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The north wind has come[FNote_977],
  2. so that the many blossoms sending out brightness[FNote_978], resembling parakeet mouths,
  3. of the field bean whose fresh creepers have climbed the dewy shrubs[FNote_979]
  4. crowd together with the jasmine [buds] of the form of the teeth of a wild cat,
  5. and apart from that so that pin strikes [me]
  6. his long hill, a sapphire[FNote_980], vanishes in the evening[FNote_981],
  7. appearing like a ship sinking into the sea
  8. of clear waves: see, oh friend.

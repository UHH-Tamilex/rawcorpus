{{Readable Translation}}

  1. [Is it] the time[FNote_619], when the small seed-baskets are full with buds
  2. of the busy(?)[FNote_620] ploughmen[FNote_621] who have cleared the old fields(?)[FNote_622]?
  3. It has come; word has not come that the chariot is coming,
  4. in order to become immersed in abundant festival in the evening,
  5. coming down from the desert so that in the thicket of rustling trees
  6. the clear wide-mouthed bells[FNote_623] are ringing, [that were] poured(?) [from/in] the furnace [while the bellows were] blowing [into a mould] cusing the wax to disappear(?).[FNote_624]

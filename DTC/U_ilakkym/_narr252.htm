{{Readable Translation}}

  • It seems they do not obstruct the way of work,
  • that has been considered when the heart, that was not hard, became hard,
  • saying "for those who once were[FNote_795] in union with difficult wealth there is nothing
  • apart from making it [afresh], traversing, with the principle of acting by reason,
  • the roads to distant lands, where the fierce bee(?) is sounding,
  • being confined to the weakened state of the dry-twigged Ōmi tree,
  • is not obstructed
  • by the qualities of [her with] jewels decorating [her] thick hir,
  • [her] small feet resembling the good tongue[FNote_796]
  • of the angry dog of advancing speed that has risen in desire for the hare,
  • [her] large-petalled rin eyes as blossoms interlinked,
  • [her] sight faultless as an image on a decorated wall.

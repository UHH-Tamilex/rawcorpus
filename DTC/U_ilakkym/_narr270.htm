{{Readable Translation}}

  • O friend,[FNote_845] you cared more for me
  • than for her with the richness of big shoulders,
  • who did not make clear [to me her] fettering [you], apart from [her] head being adorned, [and her] suffering in front of us, as a grin heap turned about(?),[FNote_846]
  • applying foaming paste[FNote_847] to her night-like tresses
  • with a scent for bees to come, smelling of cool places[FNote_848]
  • that don't bear nests in the broad-trunked Tāḻi trees.[FNote_849]
  • It is crueller than the cord of tresses[FNote_850]
  • of Naṉṉaṉ with rised spear who drove away kings
  • with speedy good horses whose broad manes glittered.
  • Will I ever forget your aptness in victory?

  • {NT 298-1} vampa mākkaḷ varu tiṟam nōkki
  • {NT 298-2} ce kaṇi toṭutta ceyir nōkku āṭavar
  • {NT 298-3} maṭi vāy taṇṇumi taḻaṅku kural kēṭṭa
  • {NT 298-4} eruvi cēval kiḷi vayiṉ peyarum
  • {NT 298-5} arum cura kavali añcuvaru naṉam tali
  • {NT 298-6} perum pal kuṉṟam uḷḷi um maṟṟu ivaḷ
  • {NT 298-7} karumpu uṭi paṇi tōḷ nōkki um oru tiṟam
  • {NT 298-8} paṟṟāy vāḻi em neñcu ē nal tār
  • {NT 298-9} poṉ tēr ceḻiyaṉ kūṭal āṅkaṇ
  • {NT 298-10} orumi ceppiya arumi vāṉ muki
  • {NT 298-11} irum pōtu kamaḻum kūntal
  • {NT 298-12} peru mali taḻīi um nōkku iyiyum ō maṟṟu ē

Naṟṟiṇai

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {NT 298-1} vampa mākkaḷ varu tiṟam nōkki
  • {NT 298-2} ce kaṇai toṭutta ceyir nōkku āṭavar
  • {NT 298-3} maṭi vāy taṇṇumai taḻaṅku kural kēṭṭa
  • {NT 298-4} eruvai cēval kiḷai vayiṉ peyarum
  • {NT 298-5} arum cura kavalai añcuvaru naṉam talai
  • {NT 298-6} perum pal kuṉṟam uḷḷi um maṟṟu ivaḷ
  • {NT 298-7} karumpu uṭai paṇai tōḷ nōkki um oru tiṟam
  • {NT 298-8} paṟṟāy vāḻi em neñcu ē nal tār
  • {NT 298-9} poṉ tēr ceḻiyaṉ kūṭal āṅkaṇ
  • {NT 298-10} orumai ceppiya arumai vāṉ mukai
  • {NT 298-11} irum pōtu kamaḻum kūntal
  • {NT 298-12} peru malai taḻīi um nōkku iyaiyum ō maṟṟu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if we do not tell him a word,
  • out of shame for [our] forehead that has lost [its] crescent moon beauty
  • and [our] bangles that won't stay above the joints and the calumny
  • that spreads gossip in the village without concealing,
  • upon seeing the soft man from the shore,
  • where the lame aging heron carries fish from the sea
  • to [its] female with bent beak that stayed behind in the fields,
  • not reaching the seashore grove because of the heaviness of advanced pregnancy, craving for prey,
  • though hiding and hiding [this] untenable state
  • the waters in [our] collyrium eyes exceeded [and] told him, friend.

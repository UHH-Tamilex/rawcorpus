{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O man from the village of Kāñci trees in the gardens near the fields(?)[FNote_509],
  2. where the barb(?), diving becuse it takes [it] for a heron,
  3. is startled by the sky[-bright] bud of the lotus of [similar? beautiful?] form,[FNote_510]
  4. - if your one bard is a liar,[FNote_511]
  5. all bards are[FNote_512] in fact[FNote_513].
  6. They are like robbers to those you have left.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. toṭipaḻi maṟaittaliṟ ṟōḷuyn taṉavē
  2. vaṭikkoḷ kūḻai yāyamō ṭāṭali
  3. ṉiṭippu meyyatoṉ ṟuṭaittē kaṭikkoḷa
  4. vaṉṉai kākkun toṉṉalañ citaiyak
  5. kāṇṭoṟuṅ kaluḻta laṉṟiyu mīṇṭunīr ((5))
  6. muttuppaṭu parappiṟ koṟkai muṉṟuṟaic
  7. ciṟupā caṭaiya ceppūr neytaṟ
  8. ṟeṇṇīr malariṟ ṟolainta kaṉṉē kāmaṅ karappari yavvē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The shoulders lived while concealing the blame of the armlets.
  • While dancing with companions with beautified(?) short [hair?],
  • the noise possesses one ...?
  • The eyes, lost like blossoms in the clear water
  • of the blue water-lily creeping from the casket of little green leaves
  • in the front ghat of Koṟkai of an expanse where pearls come
  • from the water here, even apart from dimming every time they look,
  • it is difficult for them to hide desire,
  • the old integrity being wasted, that mother guards with [her] protection.

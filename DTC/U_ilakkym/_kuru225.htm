{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O man from a land of big stones, where an elephant cow, from whose
  2. yielding[FNote_918] breast a calf is fed, eats from the millet in the front-yard,
  3. if you do not totally forget[FNote_919] [her] kindness,
  4. like a king who, having obtined distinction on the throne,
  5. forgets the aid which delighted [him] when he was lost,
  6. her lush, soft tresses, like the soft, excellent feathers
  7. of the bustling peacock, are worthy[FNote_920] of you.

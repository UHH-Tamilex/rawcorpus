{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Near the village [is] the pond, and from the pond
  2. not far is the little forest river.[FNote_457]
  3. Nothing but the white egret(?) searching for prey
  4. ever goes near the grove. We
  5. will go fetch dung [and] earth for [our] clay.[FNote_458]
  6. There too she will come, foolishness[FNote_459] herself.

  1. kaḻaipā ṭiraṅkap palliyaṅ kaṟaṅka
  2. vāṭumaka ṇaṭanta koṭumpuri nōṉkayiṟ
  3. ṟatavat tīṅkaṉi yaṉṉa cemmukat
  4. tuyttalai manti vaṉpaṟaḻ tūṅkak
  5. kaḻaikka ṇirumpoṟai yēṟi niṉṟu ((5))
  6. kuṟakkuṟu mākka ṭāḷaṅ koṭṭumak
  7. kuṉṟakat tatuvē kuḻumiḷaic cīṟūr
  8. cīṟū rōḷē nāṟumayirk koṭicci
  9. koṭicci kaiyakat tatuvēpiṟar
  10. viṭuttaṟ kākātu piṇittevaṉ ṉeñcē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • It [is] inside those hills,
  • where the little people of the Kuṟa, mounting the big height
  • of the bamboo area, standing [there and] clapping the tact,
  • when the strong young one of the cotton-headed she-monkey,
  • [its] face as red as the sweet ripe fruit of the country fig, hangs on
  • the durable rope of twisted strands that has been walked by the dancing girl,
  • while the singing of the bamboo [flute] sounded [and] many instruments sounded - the little village with an enclosure for [their] herd(?)[FNote_327].
  • She [is] from the little village, the creeper woman with fragrant body hair.
  • It [is] in the palm of the hand of the creeper woman,
  • my heart that has been fettered, being not to be set free by others.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When we were to go, we would remember the bosom with adorning breasts
  2. of the girl with hips, where sapphires are set close
  3. with gold - pleasing [all these][FNote_1115] - and the unpleasant forest,
  4. where the sharp-eyed man looking out for those who are coming
  5. decreases by chewing bark the yearning demand for water,
  6. having climbed a branch(?), grasping together with the bow a loose arrow,
  7. so that the dense ripe fruits [that are] like coins drop
  8. from the Ukāay tree with a trunk low as a dove back(?)[FNote_1116].

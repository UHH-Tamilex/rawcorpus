{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Worse than that my integrity should be lost,
  2. choicest among [my] companions in the seashore grove,
  3. [would be] if [word of] the cruelty of the tender man from the sea(?),
  4. where the Tāḻai trees with rich fronds with swordlike blades
  5. become hedges of raised spears in the evening,
  6. comes out [and] spreads [abroad] for many to know.

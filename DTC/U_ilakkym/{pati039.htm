Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 39-1} piṟarkku eṉa vāḻti nī ākaṉ māṟu ē
  • {PATI 39-2} emakku il eṉṉār niṉ maṟam kūṟu kuḻāttar
  • {PATI 39-3} tuppu tuṟipōkiya veppu uṭi tumpi
  • {PATI 39-4} kaṟutta tevvar kaṭi muṉi alaṟa
  • {PATI 39-5} eṭuttu eṟintu iraṅkum ēval viyaṉ paṇi
  • {PATI 39-6} urum eṉa atirpaṭṭu muḻaṅki ceru mikku
  • {PATI 39-7} aṭaṅkār ār araṇ vāṭa cellum
  • {PATI 39-8} kālaṉ aṉiya kaṭum ciṉa muṉpa
  • {PATI 39-9} vālitiṉ nūliṉ iḻiyā nuṇ mayir iḻiya
  • {PATI 39-10} poṟitta pōlum puḷḷi eruttiṉ
  • {PATI 39-11} puṉ puṟa puṟaviṉ kaṇa niri alaṟa
  • {PATI 39-12} alantali vēlattu ulavi am ciṉi
  • {PATI 39-13} cilampi kōliya alaṅkal pōrviyiṉ
  • {PATI 39-14} ilaṅku maṇi miṭinta pacum poṉ paṭalattu
  • {PATI 39-15} avir iḻi tii miṉ umiḻpu ilaṅka
  • {PATI 39-16} cīr miku muttam tiiya
  • {PATI 39-17} nār muṭi cēral niṉ pōr niḻal pukaṉṟu ē

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 39-1} piṟarkku eṉa vāḻti nī ākaṉ māṟu ē
  • {PATI 39-2} emakku il eṉṉār niṉ maṟam kūṟu kuḻāttar
  • {PATI 39-3} tuppu tuṟaipōkiya veppu uṭai tumpai
  • {PATI 39-4} kaṟutta tevvar kaṭi muṉai alaṟa
  • {PATI 39-5} eṭuttu eṟintu iraṅkum ēval viyaṉ paṇai
  • {PATI 39-6} urum eṉa atirpaṭṭu muḻaṅki ceru mikku
  • {PATI 39-7} aṭaṅkār ār araṇ vāṭa cellum
  • {PATI 39-8} kālaṉ aṉaiya kaṭum ciṉa muṉpa
  • {PATI 39-9} vālitiṉ nūliṉ iḻaiyā nuṇ mayir iḻaiya
  • {PATI 39-10} poṟitta pōlum puḷḷi eruttiṉ
  • {PATI 39-11} puṉ puṟa puṟaviṉ kaṇa nirai alaṟa
  • {PATI 39-12} alantalai vēlattu ulavai am ciṉai
  • {PATI 39-13} cilampi kōliya alaṅkal pōrvaiyiṉ
  • {PATI 39-14} ilaṅku maṇi miṭainta pacum poṉ paṭalattu
  • {PATI 39-15} avir iḻai taii miṉ umiḻpu ilaṅka
  • {PATI 39-16} cīr miku muttam taiiya
  • {PATI 39-17} nār muṭi cēral niṉ pōr niḻal pukaṉṟu ē

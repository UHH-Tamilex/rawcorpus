{{Readable Translation}}

  • If uniting [with her it means] not uniting with wealth. If separating
  • for wealth [it means] not having union [with her]. Whether you
  • go to that place or whether you don`t go, you are worthy
  • of the good, live, my heart. Wealth,
  • it gets[FNote_67] lost like the ways of the fish
  • in the middle of the pond with unfading flowers. As for me,
  • even if I obtained an excellent hoard measuring seven times[FNote_68]
  • what is the measure of the wide interior of the excellent water,
  • by the red-lined rain eyes, which were at strife with the thick earrings,
  • I have been destroyed, with a sweetly lingering look.
  • What might become of it? - let it live, wealth.

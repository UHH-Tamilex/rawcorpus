{{Readable Translation}}

  • Sweet [was] the ghat too[FNote_132], with [its] small-blossomed Ñāḻal trees,
  • where the little white crow, that has thrown up red prawns
  • after peering to discern the inside of the great dark surface,
  • calls out to [his] female [and] gives [her] abundantly, descending [on her]
  • himself with [his] green feet[FNote_133], having stirred the extensive(?) dark dewy backwaters
  • - before [I] united with the man from the coast of strong waters,
  • where the highly pregnant white heron is frightened
  • by the flood with successsive(?) waves, on the long-branched Puṉṉi tree
  • in the moonlight[-coloured] sand on which various(?)[FNote_134] goods are lost(?),
  • come[FNote_135], when the wind blows, from many different lands,
  • unendingly, when I [am] thus,
  • thinking of many things with a heart filled by great loneliness.

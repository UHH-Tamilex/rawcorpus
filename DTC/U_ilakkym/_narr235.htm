{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if he has come to the sign by day in the many-flowered seashore grove,
  • where the Tāḻai tress with thorny leaves that are not surpassed by snakes(?)[FNote_741],
  • with broad base become rough as it is beaten by strong waves,
  • gives frangrance along with the Puṉṉai tree with gold-like pollen,
  • [and] has gone [back] so that the beauty of our body is wasted,
  • - up, friend, let us see[FNote_742] [and] come [back],
  • mounting the sand in heaps that appear like hills:
  • the route the man from the coast of long waters is coming,
  • impelling the proud horses with ringing bells.

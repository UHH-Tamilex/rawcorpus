{{Readable Translation}}

  • While the wet blossom of the leafless Piṭavam bud,
  • while the flower creeper of the golden jasmine open, climbing the bushes,
  • while the gold-like Koṉṟi blossoms, while the short twigs
  • of the ironwood-tree are dense with many sapphire-like blossoms,
  • the riny season has begun. In great hurry this time[FNote_765]
  • let, charioteer your chariot go. See there:
  • the Irali stag that stood seeking,
  • not parting with [its] desirous heart,
  • when the group[FNote_766] of youthful deer retreats [and] runs with the
  • openen-eyed young one[FNote_767] from the saline soil where backwaters are moving.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land of high mountains, let your truthfullness stay behind.
  • Looking as with a cluster of eyes
  • at the swarming of countless six-legged wing[ed ones][FNote_213],
  • having joined[FNote_214] [her] shoulders when [you] came at night without regard
  • for the enmity there on the stony sandy small path overgrown by bamboo
  • [and] embraced her bosom lustrous with spots,
  • mother said: "[have] you [been] thus formerly?"
  • As one who did not answer to that
  • she looked at my face distressedly - mother!
  • [Thinking] "how will she live when [mother] realises"
  • I pointed out the firebrand of kindled sandal
  • [and] said "here indeed have they been".

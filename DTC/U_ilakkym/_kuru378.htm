{{Readable Translation}}

  1. That today it be a place of cool rain
  2. [and] sand spread[FNote_1509] much on the small paths on the mountain
  3. [and] shade fall from a tree without the sun being hot
  4. - the desert, where the youthful dark young woman has gone
  5. with the young man [bearing] a glow-mouthed[FNote_1510] long spear, after she left us.

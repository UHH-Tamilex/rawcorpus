{{Readable Translation}}

  • The black-stalked red millet [grains] have been harvested(?)[FNote_399],
  • [on the field] ploughed by my lord on the mountainside with brilliant stones,
  • and the wild jasmines have budded at the side of the abundance(?)
  • of the small hamlets by the forest on the border within the stones
  • and mother who has a face that is not pleased(?) with you[FNote_400]:
  • "is it said that the man from the land inside the mountains is coming
  • in the fearful dense darkness when the smiling(?) thunder roars?
  • Is it not? Just what [is the case]?" standing [thus],
  • a mirror[FNote_401] of what is hidden inside by a strong mind -
  • as for you, it is necessary to consider [the situation][FNote_402],
  • o you with flower-like eyes, the way it is appropriate.

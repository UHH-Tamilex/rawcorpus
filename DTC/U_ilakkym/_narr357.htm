{{Readable Translation}}

  • What [was] your intention, friend? My intention,
  • even if it is not permanent with me, always,
  • it does not know of getting lost, so that it will reproach the heart
  • - on the day [we?] played with the man from a land of slopes,
  • with a chaplet that dispersed when hit by the water,
  • having knitted together blue [lotuses] resembling collyrium eyes
  • from the rocky wide-mouthed fresh mountin spring, a pretty place
  • in a grove where the fanned(?) peacocks dance,
  • with [their] sapphire-spotted crests that dried after having borne the rin
  • on the slope of the hill that appears in the distance[FNote_1009].

{{Readable Translation}}

  • In the time of the cold season too - when the busy clouds are sounding,
  • risen to the South, which poured great rin
  • for the earth to fill up with water, for the hills to be sprouting,
  • for the crops to take root by wide-mouthed fresh mountin pools,
  • for odourous creepers to shoot forth, surrounding the sandal tree
  • with fragrant core, deficient creepers weeded by the hill people,[FNote_19] -
  • separating from the lover is difficult:
  • rin eyes with dishelleved petals have turned messengers,
  • together with the north-wind, which brings [back] the friends, going today.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. ilaimāṇ pakaḻic cilaimā ṇirīiya
  2. vaṉpi lāṭava ralaittaliṟ palaruṭaṉ
  3. vampalar tolainta varuñcurak kavalai
  4. yaḻalpōl ceviya cēva lāṭṭi
  5. niḻaloṭu katikku niṇampuri mutunari ((5))
  6. paccūṉ koḷḷai māntivey tuṟṟut
  7. tērtikaḻ vaṟumpulan tuḻaiiya nīrnayantu
  8. patukkai nīḻa lotukkiṭam peṟāa
  9. vañcuvaru kavalai varutaliṉ varuntiya
  10. namakku mariya vāyiṉa vamaittōṇ ((10))
  11. māṇpuṭaik kuṟumaka ṇīṅki
  12. yāṅkuvan taṉaḷko laḷiya ṭāṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The have become difficult for us
  • who suffered coming along the fearful crossroads,
  • obtaining(?) a single(?) place of shade by the stone heap,
  • longing for the water that has been stirred, on the poor field, where the mirage(?)[FNote_999] glitters, since he felt hot after having eaten the plunder of fresh flesh, by the old jackal[FNote_1000] desirous of fat, that moves with the shade[FNote_1001],
  • chasing away the cock with ears like fire(?),
  • on the crossroads of the difficult desert where travellers are lost,
  • many together, to the beating of the loveless men
  • that put into the glory of [their] bows arrows glorious for [their] blade.
  • Having left the glorious little woman with bamboo shoulders,
  • what has become of her, pitiable she?

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Become mother, a woman [who is] cuse for feasting:
  2. becuse she has hidden
  3. the faithlessness of the man from the village of Kāñci trees
  4. whose tender twigs with fragrant flowers the ploughmen bend
  5. so that the fresh pollen from [flower] clusters like green gram/[ears of] corn
  6. settle [on them][FNote_45]
  7. {--!}she usually[FNote_46] comes [along] ashamed.

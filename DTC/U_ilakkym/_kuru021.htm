KT 21 Ōtalāntiyār: SHE

{{kiḷavi}}: (Uttered to make clear: I myself have the strength [with the words]: 'even if the forest has indicated the time of his coming I don't believe this to be the riny season, becuse he doesn't tell lies', by HER(t.) who comprehended the inner emotion of the one who was anxious in herself: 'now, at the coming of the season, how can we give [her] strength?', [i.e.] the friend who gave strength even up to the coming of the season.){{/kiḷavi}}

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Just as a broken bangle, for many to worship,
  2. appearing, all of a sudden(?)[FNote_1253], in the red mouth, the sky,
  3. now also the crescent moon has been born.[FNote_1254] Alas!
  4. Did he forget, he,
  5. who separated, so that [I] cry, for the long way on the road,
  6. where the elephant bull thunders with an afflicted heart,
  7. not enduring the suffering of his youthful female with weakened gait,
  8. piercing to death the Yām tree high of base,
  9. taking the white bark, tasting it with the hand [and] looking up?

  • {PATI 82-1} pakai perumaiyiṉ teyvam ceppa
  • {PATI 82-2} ār iṟai añcā veruvaru kaṭṭūr
  • {PATI 82-3} pal koṭi nuṭaṅkum muṉpiṉ ceṟunar
  • {PATI 82-4} cel camam tolaitta viṉai navil yāṉai
  • {PATI 82-5} kaṭāam vārntu kaṭum ciṉam potti
  • {PATI 82-6} vaṇṭu paṭu ceṉṉiya piṭi puṇarntu iyala
  • {PATI 82-7} maṟavar maṟala mā paṭai uṟuppa
  • {PATI 82-8} tēr koṭi nuṭaṅka tōl puṭai ārppa
  • {PATI 82-9} kāṭukai kāyttiya nīṭu nāḷ irukkai
  • {PATI 82-10} iṉṉa vaikal pal nāḷ āka
  • {PATI 82-11} pāṭi kāṇku vanticiṉ peruma
  • {PATI 82-12} pāṭunar koḷa koḷa kuṟaiyā celvattu ceṟṟōr
  • {PATI 82-13} kola kola kuṟaiyā tāṉai cāṉṟōr
  • {PATI 82-14} vaṇmai um cemmai um cālpu um maṟaṉ um
  • {PATI 82-15} pukaṉṟu pukaḻntu acaiyā nal icai
  • {PATI 82-16} nilam taru tiruviṉ neṭiyōy niṉ ē

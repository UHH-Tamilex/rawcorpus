  • {PATI 82-5} kaṭāam vārntu kaṭum ciṉam potti
  • {PATI 82-6} vaṇṭu paṭu ceṉṉiya piṭi puṇarntu iyala
  • {PATI 82-7} maṟavar maṟala mā paṭi uṟuppa
  • {PATI 82-8} tēr koṭi nuṭaṅka tōl puṭi ārppa
  • {PATI 82-9} kāṭuki kāyttiya nīṭu nāḷ irukki
  • {PATI 82-10} iṉṉa vikal pal nāḷ āka
  • {PATI 82-11} pāṭi kāṇku vanticiṉ peruma
  • {PATI 82-12} pāṭunar koḷa koḷa kuṟiyā celvattu ceṟṟōr
  • {PATI 82-13} kola kola kuṟiyā tāṉi cāṉṟōr
  • {PATI 82-14} vaṇmi um cemmi um cālpu um maṟaṉ um
  • {PATI 82-15} pukaṉṟu pukaḻntu aciyā nal ici
  • {PATI 82-16} nilam taru tiruviṉ neṭiyōy niṉ ē

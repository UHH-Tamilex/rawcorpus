{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from a land of hills, where the forest inhabitant,
  • thinking of the move of [his] arrow that failed,
  • not entering the vital spot[FNote_538] difficult [to hit] of the pretty-eyed wild cow,
  • rejoices with [his] relatives, rising in the wish to worship the high mountin
  • of the god, saying "may the mountin take the sky[FNote_539], standing with plague",
  • - while [we] talk to him about the difficulties whenever he arrives(?),
  • he does not say: "let [this] be as
  • the intimacy of those who have no longing", without uniting with us.[FNote_540]
  • Stay behind without weakening. The messages have become very many.

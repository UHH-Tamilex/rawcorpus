{{Readable Translation}}

  1. His chest united [with mine][FNote_285], nothing else is the remedy
  2. to end the cold season with dew[FNote_286] hard [to bear]
  3. when a herd of deer seizes the old fruits rotting unripe[FNote_287]
  4. of the Uḻuntu, whose trunk [is] as red as quails' legs.

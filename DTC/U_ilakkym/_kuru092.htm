{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Pitiable [are] they, those birds(?)[FNote_379] with curved wings,
  2. in the broad-mouthed sky where the sun has [just] risen/set,
  3. [their] going so hurried when [their] children become quiet
  4. with food inserted into the mouths,
  5. in the tree near the path grown high [enough for them] to have [their] seat [there](?)[FNote_380].[FNote_381]

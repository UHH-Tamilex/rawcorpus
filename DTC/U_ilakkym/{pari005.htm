Paripāṭal 5

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 5-1} pāy irum paṉi kaṭal pār tukaḷ paṭa pukku
  • {PARI 5-2} cēy uyar piṇimukam ūrntu amar uḻakki
  • {PARI 5-3} tī aḻal tuvaippa tiriya viṭṭeṟintu
  • {PARI 5-4} nōy uṭai nuṭaṅku cūr mā mutal taṭintu
  • {PARI 5-5} veṉṟiyiṉ makkaḷ uḷ orumaiyoṭu peyariya
  • {PARI 5-6} koṉṟu uṇal añcā koṭu viṉai kol takai
  • {PARI 5-7} māya avuṇar maruṅku aṟa taputta vēl
  • {PARI 5-8} nāval am taṇ poḻil vaṭa poḻil āyiṭai
  • {PARI 5-9} kurukoṭu peyar peṟṟa māl varai uṭaittu
  • {PARI 5-10} malai āṟṟuppaṭutta mū iru kayam talai
  • {PARI 5-11} mū iru kayam talai mu nāṉku muḻavu tōḷ
  • {PARI 5-12} ñāyiṟṟu ēr niṟa takai naḷiṉattu piṟaviyai
  • {PARI 5-13} kāay kaṭavuḷ cēey cevvēḷ
  • {PARI 5-14} cālva talaiva eṉa pēe viḻaviṉ uḷ
  • {PARI 5-15} vēlaṉ ēttum veṟi um uḷa ē
  • {PARI 5-16} avai vāy um alla poy um alla
  • {PARI 5-17} nī ē varampiṟṟu i ulakam ātaliṉ
  • {PARI 5-18} ciṟappōy ciṟappu iṉṟi peyarkuvai
  • {PARI 5-19} ciṟappiṉ uḷ uyarvu ākal um
  • {PARI 5-20} piṟappiṉ uḷ iḻipu ākal um
  • {PARI 5-21} ēṉōr niṉ valattiṉatu ē
  • {PARI 5-22} āti antaṇaṉ aṟintu pari koḷuva
  • {PARI 5-23} vēta mā pūṇ vaiya tēr ūrntu
  • {PARI 5-24} nākam nāṇā malai vil āka
  • {PARI 5-25} mū vakai ār eyil ōr aḻal ampiṉ muḷiya
  • {PARI 5-26} mātiram aḻala eytu amarar vēḷvi
  • {PARI 5-27} pākam uṇṭa paim kaṇ pārppāṉ
  • {PARI 5-28} umaiyoṭu puṇarntu kāma vatuvai uḷ
  • {PARI 5-29} amaiyā puṇarcci amaiya neṟṟi
  • {PARI 5-30} imaiyā nāṭṭattu oru varam koṇṭu
  • {PARI 5-31} vilaṅku eṉa viṇṇōr vēḷvi mutalvaṉ
  • {PARI 5-32} viri katir maṇi pūṇavaṟku tāṉ īttatu
  • {PARI 5-33} aritu eṉa māṟṟāṉ vāymaiyaṉ ātaliṉ
  • {PARI 5-34} eri kaṉaṉṟu āṉā kuṭāri koṇṭu avaṉ uruvu
  • {PARI 5-35} tirittiṭṭōṉ i ulaku ēḻu um maruḷa
  • {PARI 5-36} karu peṟṟukoṇṭōr kaḻinta cēy yākkai
  • {PARI 5-37} nocippiṉ ēḻuṟu muṉivar naṉi uṇarntu
  • {PARI 5-38} vacittatai kaṇṭam āka mātavar
  • {PARI 5-39} maṉaiviyar niṟai vayiṉ vaci taṭi camaippiṉ
  • {PARI 5-40} cālār tāṉ ē tarikka eṉa avar avi
  • {PARI 5-41} uṭaṉ peytōr ē aḻal vēṭṭu a avi
  • {PARI 5-42} taṭavu nimir muttī pēṇiya maṉ eccil
  • {PARI 5-43} vaṭa vayiṉ viḷaṅku āl uṟai eḻu makaḷir uḷ
  • {PARI 5-44} kaṭavuḷ oru mīṉ cāliṉi oḻiya
  • {PARI 5-45} aṟuvar maṟṟaiyōr um a nilai ayiṉṟaṉar
  • {PARI 5-46} maṟu aṟu kaṟpiṉ mātavar maṉaiviyar
  • {PARI 5-47} niṟai vayiṉ vaḻāatu niṉ cūliṉar ē
  • {PARI 5-48} nivantu ōṅku imayattu nīla paim cuṉai
  • {PARI 5-49} payantōr eṉpa patumattu pāyal
  • {PARI 5-50} perum peyar muruka niṉ payanta ñāṉṟu ē
  • {PARI 5-51} aritu amar ciṟappiṉ amarar celvaṉ
  • {PARI 5-52} eri umiḻ vacciram koṇṭu ikantu vantu eṟinteṉa
  • {PARI 5-53} aṟu vēṟu tuṇi um aṟuvar āki
  • {PARI 5-54} oruvaṉai vāḻi ōṅku viṟal cēey
  • {PARI 5-55} ārā uṭampiṉ nī amarntu viḷaiyāṭiya
  • {PARI 5-56} pōrāl vaṟum kaikku purantaraṉ uṭaiya
  • {PARI 5-57} allal il aṉalaṉ taṉ meyyiṉ pirittu
  • {PARI 5-58} celva vāraṇam koṭuttōṉ vāṉattu
  • {PARI 5-59} vaḷam keḻu celvaṉ taṉ meyyiṉ pirittu
  • {PARI 5-60} tikaḻ poṟi pīli aṇi mayil koṭuttōṉ
  • {PARI 5-61} tiruntu kōl ñamaṉ taṉ meyyiṉ pirivittu
  • {PARI 5-62} irum kaṇ veḷ yāṭṭu eḻil maṟi koṭuttōṉ
  • {PARI 5-63} āaṅku avar um piṟar um amarntu paṭai aḷitta
  • {PARI 5-64} maṟi um maññai um vāraṇaccēval um
  • {PARI 5-65} poṟi vari cāpam um maraṉ um vāḷ um
  • {PARI 5-66} ceṟi ilai īṭṭi um kuṭāri um kaṇicci um
  • {PARI 5-67} teṟu katir kaṉali um mālai um maṇi um
  • {PARI 5-68} vēṟu vēṟu uruviṉ i āṟu iru kaikkoṇṭu
  • {PARI 5-69} maṟu il tuṟakkattu amarar celvaṉ taṉ
  • {PARI 5-70} poṟi vari koṭṭaiyoṭu pukaḻ varampu ikantōy
  • {PARI 5-71} niṉ kuṇam etirkoṇṭōr aṟam koṇṭōr allatai
  • {PARI 5-72} maṉ kuṇam uṭaiyōr mātavar vaṇaṅkiyōr allatai
  • {PARI 5-73} ceṟu tī neñcattu ciṉam nīṭiṉōr um
  • {PARI 5-74} cērā aṟattu cīr ilōr um
  • {PARI 5-75} aḻi tava paṭivattu ayariyōr um
  • {PARI 5-76} maṟu piṟappu il eṉum maṭavōr um cērār
  • {PARI 5-77} niṉ niḻal aṉṉōr allatu iṉṉōr
  • {PARI 5-78} cērvār ātaliṉ yāam irappavai
  • {PARI 5-79} poruḷ um poṉ um pōkam um alla niṉ pāl
  • {PARI 5-80} aruḷ um aṉpu um aṟaṉ um mūṉṟu um
  • {PARI 5-81} uruḷ iṇar kaṭampiṉ oli tārōy ē

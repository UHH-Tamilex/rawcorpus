{{Readable Translation}}

  • If he traverses the difficult desert with many mountins, having let go of us,
  • the man from a land,
  • where the soft-headed calf, un[able to] go when the suffering [mother] elephant, that swung [from side to side] from yearning for water, goes from the ground to the slopes of the hot mountins of hills(?)[FNote_554] in summer,
  • enters the village with the calfs of the cows,
  • so that [compassion? fear?] hits the hearts of the women in the streets,
  • - how will [my] eyes be strong[FNote_555] [enough] henceforth
  • that joined together in sleeping(?)[FNote_556] when they had [his] chest,
  • mingling with affectionate hearts
  • at midnight, taken as the time when demons move about(?),[FNote_557]
  • so that they (the eyes) become as brilliant as the clear sapphires in crafted ornaments(?)[FNote_558]?

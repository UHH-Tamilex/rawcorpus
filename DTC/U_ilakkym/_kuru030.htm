{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Listen, o friend, in the night
  2. I arose [and] stroked the bed
  3. charmed by a false dream seeming like true
  4. combined with the bodily presence of the man with the power to lie.
  5. Exhausted like the water-lily blossom visited by bees,
  6. alone, verily, poor me.

Altern

6b alone me, verily, loving one.

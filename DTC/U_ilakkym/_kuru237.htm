{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if the heart has separated[FNote_966] from us
  2. to embrace the desirable mate without knowing of something fearful,
  3. if [still] the bond becomes loose on the hand [of her] who has been left aside - what [good is] it?
  4. Much[FNote_967] the distances[FNote_968], alas, between us.
  5. How many do I count -
  6. woods where killing tigers are roaming,
  7. rising powerfully[FNote_969] [and] thundering like the waves of the great sea,
  8. {--!}obstacles between [our] embrace?

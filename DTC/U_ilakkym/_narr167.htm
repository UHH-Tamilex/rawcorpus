{{Readable Translation}}

  • O bard that ends suffering by [so] many words that understand [just your] profit, come with a message from the man of the cool ghat,
  • where the [well]prepared long chariot that has been obtined
  • as a gift in the assembly on a day of great joy from Āy[FNote_544]
  • sounds in rhythm as the din of the white herons when they are in distress
  • on the big branches bend to the west of the dark-trunked Puṉṉi tree,
  • - the humble words from your mouth won't remove
  • the pallor, this having spread on the brilliant forehead resembling the crescent moon
  • of the small woman with bright bangles rising on [her] forearms
  • who lost her glorious integrity in the seashore-grove giving frangrance,
  • where the most glorious[FNote_545] new blossoms of the Puṉṉi together with the Ñāḻal are spreading.

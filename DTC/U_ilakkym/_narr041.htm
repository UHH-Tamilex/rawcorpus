{{Readable Translation}}

  • Is he really suffering, going the long distance,
  • besmearing himself with the loose ashes[FNote_174] of white-backed saline soil,
  • that is kicked by the big foot of the green-eyed elephant,
  • gently alleviating the suffering experienced
  • in the desert in the well sprinkling the rocks?
  • - he who wanted union with [her of] short steps,
  • bespattered with perspiration in many little fine [drops],
  • [her] front stung by the smoke of whitening meat,
  • stired [and] cooked in ghee by the young woman of shining jewels
  • for the famed(?) guests come at night.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "You who do neither join in play with bright-jewelled women
  • nor string a garland of pure-petalled blue water-lilies,
  • you who is posed on one side of the wide-flowered seashore-grove,
  • who [are] you? We worship you.[FNote_495] We want to ask you
  • that have goodness unmeasured(?)[FNote_496] by those who have seen [you]:
  • [are you] a plague[FNote_497] abiding permanently by the extension of the great sea
  • or have you took a station near the dark backwaters?
  • Speak now, girl." I said. In answer to that
  • [her] thorn-teeth openened in a smile
  • and [her] many-petalled collyrium eyes spread dew indeed.

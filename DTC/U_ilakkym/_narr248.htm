{{Metrical text}}

  1. ciṟuvī mullait tēṅkamaḻ pacuvī
  2. poṟivari naṉmāṉ pukarmukaṅ kaṭuppat
  3. taṇputa laṇipeṟa malara vaṇpeyaṟ
  4. kārvaru paruva meṉṟaṉar maṉṉiṉip
  5. pēraña ruḷḷa naṭuṅkal kāṇiya ((5))
  6. raṉpiṉ maiyiṟ paṇpila payiṟṟum
  7. poyyiṭi yatirkural vāycet tālu
  8. miṉmayiṉ maṭakkaṇam pōla
  9. niṉaimaruḷ vēṉō vāḻiyar maḻaiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • He certainly said "in the season when the clouds come,
  • generous raining so that the cool bushes flower in order to get adorned
  • with green blossoms fragrant with honey on the small-blossomed jasmine,
  • resembling the spotted faces of the good deer with dots [and] lines"[FNote_783].
  • Now,
  • may you live, rain, will I confuse you,
  • as [does] the foolish group of pleasing peacocks
  • that dances, thinking true the shaking voice of the false thunder
  • that repeats bad [tones], for absence of love [for me],
  • in order to see [me] shivering inside with big grief?

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O dream, you arouse [me] from sweet sleep,
  2. as if you had given[FNote_591] the girl with fine ornaments,
  3. of beautiful, [now] dimming darkness, who has flown, body-hir rised(?)[FNote_592],
  4. like on the curved blossom of the trumpet flower tree in summer.[FNote_593]
  5. They won't censure [you], alas, those who are separated from [their] mates.

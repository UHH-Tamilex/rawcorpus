{{Readable Translation}}

  • While long-tongued bright bells sound in the protected house,
  • because[FNote_167] protection has been made by the great Pāṇ(-melody)
  • in the arbour [spread with] mixed sand where rustling(?) leaves are
  • moving(?)[FNote_168], in one side(?)
  • women with perfect jewels are standing [listening to] auspicious voices.
  • The man from the village with wide ghats has come too,
  • like a robber in deepest night,
  • when the weighty[FNote_169] girl joined [her] moist lids,
  • with [her] soft body cooled with green oil,
  • adorned with moistness in an oil bath, adorned with white mustard,
  • while [her] son sleeps with [his] foster-mother, fragrant of new birth,
  • on the bed with soft cloth spread out to have fragrance(?)[FNote_170],
  • - because[FNote_171] one is born who bears the name of [his] elder[FNote_172].

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even yesterday he [was] here indeed,
  • prised in many [words my] eyes and shoulders
  • and cool fragrant hir and beauty-spotted hips.
  • Today,
  • he himself say he will go[FNote_301]
  • through the difficult desert afflicted by heat where the sunlight has been enormous on the wilderness of Ōmi trees in the saline soil, where the
  • saltishness rises as within the heat of unfinished butter
  • eaten by the churning stick in the earthern pot,
  • where the mirage of an evil spirit makes the deer yearning
  • on the treeless long way with the resemblance of great water,
  • - he who hasn't the strength for a living in the house
  • without being able to refuse beggers [come] to his place.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the cool coast, where they count the firm boats
  • of [their] relatives gone to hunt in the curved water,
  • saying "this [is] ma father's boat, [this is] your father's boat",
  • mounting together the salt[-mounts], with the attendant of inexperienced look,
  • scaring away the birds coming onto the drying of reeking fish
  • in heaps, to be guarded, placed in the seashore grove
  • by the ploughmen that do not plough for the brackish ripening salt,
  • looking at for proper time the delighted saltmerchants come,
  • pleasing it [is], our unafflicted good village,
  • and if [you] come now it is not a mistake,
  • and it has streets of people not knowing each other
  • - how [could there be] knowing of all the others[FNote_957], even a little bit?[FNote_958]

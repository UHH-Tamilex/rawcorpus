{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Think, oh friend,
  2. of the way [you][FNote_1376] will move with the man from the land of high mountains,
  3. where rests, like a twig with fading flowers
  4. of the black-trunked Vēṅkai[FNote_1377] broken by the west wind,
  5. the split-mouthed[FNote_1378] male of the strong dark/big tiger
  6. in a rock cave, after it had made the white tusks take red spots,
  7. because it sprung into the adorned[FNote_1379] face of a superior elephant with wet temple[FNote_1380].

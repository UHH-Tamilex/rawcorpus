{{Metrical text}}

  1. tolkaviṉ ṟolaiyat tōṇalam cāya
  2. nalkā nīttaṉa rāyiṉu nalkuvar
  3. nāṭiṉar vāḻi tōḻi kuṭṭuva
  4. ṉakappā vaḻiya nūṟic cempiyaṉ
  5. pakaṟṟī vēṭṭa ñāṭpiṉu mikapperi ((5))
  6. talareḻac ceṉṟaṉa rāyiṉu malarkavintu
  7. māmaṭa laviḻnta kāntaḷañ cāraliṉ
  8. ñālvāyk kaḷiṟu pāntaṭ paṭṭeṉat
  9. tuñcāt tuyarat tañcupiṭip pūca
  10. ṉeṭuvarai viṭarakat tiyampuṅ ((10))
  11. kaṭumāṉ pulliya kāṭiṟan tōrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if he left, not granting [his presence],
  • for [my] old beauty to be lost, for [my] shoulders` goodness to be exhausted,
  • he is granting in [that other] land, oh friend,
  • he who traversed the wilderness of Pulli with fast horses,
  • where the clamour of the fearful she-elephant, sleepless with misery,
  • resounds inside the caves of the long montain,
  • because the serpent has come over[FNote_55] the elephant bull with hanging mouth,
  • on the slope of Malabar lilies which opened on big stems
  • [their] covering blossoms[FNote_56], - even if he went so that gossip arose,
  • much louder than the battle, where Cempiyaṉ completely[FNote_57] demolished
  • Kuṭṭuvaṉ`s [fort] Akappā [and] made [it] a sacrifice of the fire on midday.

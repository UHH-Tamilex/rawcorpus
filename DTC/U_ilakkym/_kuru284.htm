{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The man from a land, where bright red Malabar lilies open,
  2. many together, on an elevation of thick stones in the open(?)[FNote_1170],
  3. so that it resembles the spotted face of a slain elephant[FNote_1171],
  4. whether he is dutiful or not
  5. are they abusing us,
  6. the [people] from these little homes to whom it wasn't right[FNote_1172],
  7. where the pure white waterfall hanging down from the mountain
  8. comes down upon the pointlessly[FNote_1173] leaf[-thatched] huts?
  9. {--!}Do they have nothing [to abuse] themselves [with]?[FNote_1174]

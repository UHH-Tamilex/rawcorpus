  2. ṉiṟampeṟu mīritaḻp polinta vuṇka

  4. cella ṟīrkkañ celvā meṉṉuñ

  6. leyyā maiyō ṭiḷivutalait tarumeṉa
  7. vuṟuti tūkkāt tūṅki yaṟivē
  8. ciṟitunaṉi viraiya leṉṉu māyiṭai
  9. yoḷiṟēntu maruppiṟ kaḷiṟumāṟu paṟṟiya
  10. tēypurip paḻaṅkayiṟu pōla ((10))
  11. vīvatu kolleṉ varuntiya vuṭampē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • While [my] heart, on the side of her who has taken [my] mind into fetter
  • with collyrium eyes that glittered [as] moist petal which reach the colour
  • of buds [and] darkened tresses hanging down on [her] back,
  • says "we will go and end [her] distress",
  • but[FNote_876] [my] knowledge, suspended(?) without weighing the firmness of
  • "making trouble without completing the work to be done
  • will give disgrace along with irgnorance",
  • is saying "don't hurry much for a little [while](?)[FNote_877]",
  • - will my body perish that has suffered
  • as an old rope with wornout strands,
  • seized by opposite elephants with bright high tusks in that place?

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The curly dark hir and the long shoulders,
  • alas, while [their] old integrity is dily wasted,
  • will he come, live, friend,
  • he who traversed the difficult desert with crossroads difficult to near,
  • obstructed in between(?) by the bows of the untught bold ones
  • with chaplet, who regardless abandoned stright arrows?
  • Flashing like a sword drawn from the sheath
  • in the encampment of Ceṟiyaṉ with an army full of spears
  • that tarried for fear to come to the Banyan forest, crossing a conflict,
  • see there:
  • the bustling sky that flows with fierce rin,
  • after ripening in the long big hills.

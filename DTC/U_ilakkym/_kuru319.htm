{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Evening has come[FNote_1295] with great rain in showers,
  2. while inside(?) the mountain, on the sides adorned with collyrium[FNote_1296]
  3. the good animal with the hand[FNote_1297] comes together [and] joins with [his] female
  4. and the stag embraces the youthful doe [and] hides completely[FNote_1298]
  5. in the shrubs situated in the forest, perfectly deluding(?)[FNote_1299].
  6. The one who wasted the good integrity of [our] gold-fine/gold-like body,
  7. if he doesn't come even now,
  8. what will be the state of our sweet life?

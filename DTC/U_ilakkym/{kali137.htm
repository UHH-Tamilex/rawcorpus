  • {KALI 137-8} melliya neñcu piyuḷ kūra tam

  • {KALI 137-11} villiṉāṉ eytal ō ilar maṉ āy iḻi
  • {KALI 137-12} villiṉum kaṭitu avar colliṉ uḷ piṟanta nōy
  • {KALI 137-13} naki mutal āka naṭpiṉ uḷ eḻunta
  • {KALI 137-14} takimiiṉ nalital allatu avar nammi
  • {KALI 137-15} vakimiyiṉ eḻunta tol muraṇ mutal āka
  • {KALI 137-16} pakimiiṉ nalital ō ilar maṉ āy iḻi
  • {KALI 137-17} pakimiiṉ kaṭitu avar takimiiṉ naliyum nōy
  • {KALI 137-18} nīyalēṉ eṉṟu eṉṉi aṉpiṉāl piṇittu tam
  • {KALI 137-19} cāyaliṉ cuṭutal allatu avar nammi
  • {KALI 137-20} pāy iruḷ aṟa nīkkum nōy tapu neṭum cuṭar
  • {KALI 137-21} tīyiṉāl cuṭutal ō ilar maṉ āy iḻi
  • {KALI 137-22} tīyiṉum kaṭitu avar cāyaliṉ kaṉalum nōy
  • {KALI 137-23} āṅku
  • {KALI 137-24} aṉṉar kātalar āka avar namakku
  • {KALI 137-25} iṉ uyir pērttaruma maruttuvar āyiṉ
  • {KALI 137-26} yāṅku āvatu kol tōḻi eṉiyatūum
  • {KALI 137-27} tāṅkutal valittaṉṟu āyiṉ
  • {KALI 137-28} nīṅka aritu uṟṟa aṉṟu avar uṟīiya nōy ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Into the forest where the west wind lifts
  2. the noisy[FNote_1476] light nuts on the Sirissa trees by the road,
  3. to make the grins roar like the metal in anklets,
  4. we are going, friend. Our man has granted [it].

Altern

4b: Our people have granted [it].

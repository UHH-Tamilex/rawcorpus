{{Metrical text}}

  1. vēṅkai yumpuli yīṉṟaṉa varuviyun
  2. tēmpaṭu neṭuvarai maṇiyiṉ māṉu
  3. maṉṉaiyu mamarntunōk kiṉaḷē yeṉṉaiyuṅ
  4. kaḷiṟṟumukan tiṟanta kallā viḻuttoṭai
  5. yēva liḷaiyaroṭu māvaḻip paṭṭeṉac ((5))
  6. ciṟukiḷi muraṇiya peruṅkura lēṉal
  7. kāva liṉiyeṉ ṟōḷē cēvaloṭu
  8. cilampiṟ pōkiya citarkāl vāraṇa
  9. mutaiccuvaṟ kiḷaitta pūḻi mikappala
  10. naṉpo ṉimaikku nāṭaṉō ((10))
  11. ṭaṉpuṟu kāma mamaikanan toṭarpē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The Vēṅkai trees have brought forth tigers and the waterfalls
  • resemble sapphires on the long mountain flowing with honey
  • and mother stood still [and] looked [at it]. And since father
  • is on the way for the animals with the youths [he] commands,
  • with arrows excellent in training(?) that opened the face of the elephant bull,
  • she has said "[yours is] the guarding now
  • of the big-eared millet on which the small parrots have become many".
  • May our attachment: desire that has love, be close
  • with the man from the land, where good gold twinkles,
  • very many [bits] in the dust dug up on the old height
  • by the hen(?) with spotted legs who had gone to the slope with [her] cock.

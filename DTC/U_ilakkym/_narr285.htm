{{Readable Translation}}

  • The intimacy with the man from a land of hills,
  • where the strong-handed forest man with strong arrow bends [his] cruel bow
  • [and] comes back to his home, a hut with erect legs,
  • to show himself joyful as the hunting had been successfull[FNote_878],
  • while the dogs play together in a site at the house,
  • with a male of the porcupine hit(?) in the breast,
  • who, besides coming at night,
  • at midnight by difficult darkness when the snake searches for prey,
  • comes at midday to the field shielded [by us],
  • not one to leave off because of the rumours among the neighbours,
  • daily, for us it [is] good indeed, live, friend.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Did they become weary, bearing the fine sand
  • on the route, where the red trunk of the ōmi tree,
  • kicked by the hungry she-elephant, twinkles at the time the sunlight is hot,
  • resembling the wounded leg of an elephant that was beaten
  • by the male of bright lustrous [stripes] that has borne the desert [early] in the day, when cool dew drips as a wick soaked in ghee
  • on every brach [ful of][FNote_869] bats that bore the tarrying dew,
  • hating the bright fruits of the Neem [and] yearning for
  • the sweet fruits subdued(?) in honey milk of the Iruppi,
  • - the feet of my [girl of] choice jewels who passed through, for others to perceive, the pleasure of removing [her] anklets, [their] mouths being squashed?[FNote_870]

{{Readable Translation}}

  • While [we?] are coming to see jointly(?) the cave in the clefts of the big mountain, where the broad-trunked elephant bull, embraced by [his] youthful female with a calf, feeds out protectively gold-like morsels from the Vēṅkai-tree from which he had driven away the large swarms of [those that] make honey,
  • after he had ruined the big trunk of strong height,
  • since [his] strength was superior, while the sounding many pearls made noise[FNote_640]
  • in [his] red tusks reeking of flesh, reddened when he beat the tiger,
  • - see, live, little woman:
  • the wilderness, adorned with many-flowered Kōṅkam trees,
  • as the long chain of moving lights
  • in the month to do duty when the six stars have spread,[FNote_641]
  • of your father.

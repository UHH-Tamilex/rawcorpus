{{Readable Translation}}

  1. I realised before he did [what actually deserves to be called] 'wealth',
  2. [namely] that he would speak good [words], unite [with her
  3. and] go away together[FNote_1221], to the wide places in stony height,
  4. where like a village appear the [stone-]heaps[FNote_1222] piled up with leaves
  5. for the travellers going the way who died after having resisted
  6. those who don't like opposition[FNote_1223] in the victory of [their] sharp, long arrows,
  7. the bold ones with bent bows drawn on that border.[FNote_1224]

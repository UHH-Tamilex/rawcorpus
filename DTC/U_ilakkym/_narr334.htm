{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from the land inside the mountins, where the black-fingered female monkey, [her] red-faced big[FNote_962] relations[FNote_963] playing in the waterfall
  • on the side of the big mountin, hangs on the high bamboo as a swing [and]
  • sports with [its] male, so that the mountin-adorning fragrant blossoms
  • of the Vēṅki tree drop into the stony mountin pool,
  • if you [say] he is coming with the edge of lightning for a lamp,
  • one spear rised on the mountinside with [its] waterfall,
  • at midnight in difficult darkness when the showers have stopped[FNote_964],
  • what, friend, [will be] the state of our sweet life?

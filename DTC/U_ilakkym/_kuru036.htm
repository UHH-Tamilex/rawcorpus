{{Readable Translation}}

  1. His saying as an imperishable oath,
  2. when he united with [me of] good shoulders:
  3. 'I won't leave [you], because [your] heart [is my] place',
  4. the man from the hills where a sleeping elephant
  5. is climbed by big Māṇai creepers, close to the thick stone(?)[FNote_172],
  6. [is it] pain on your side, friend?

{{Readable Translation}}

  • How will we become [attinable], friend,
  • for the keen one of exceeding love, the one who saw [us]
  • decorating, to obtin beauty, [our] sprouting many tresses,
  • ...........? that had befitting beauty,
  • resembling the handicraft of the artisan working with gold,
  • with the day-fresh new blossom of the balck-trunked Vēṅki?
  • The millet we shouted in is taking plucking consistency,
  • endless the sounding of the waterfall,
  • [its] leaves become dry, when it appears we seem to have to return
  • to the village, so that the fragrant Kūṭalam grove will be lonely,
  • on the slope that evokes longing, where the fragrant bunches of Malabar lilies have opened.

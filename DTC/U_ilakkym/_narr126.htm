{{Metrical text}}

  1. paiṅkāy naliṭa morīiya ceṅkāyk
  2. karuṅkaḷi yīntiṉ veṇpuṟak kaḷari
  3. yiṭunī ṟāṭiya kaṭunaṭai yorutta
  4. lāṭpeṟa ṉacaii nāṭcuram vilaṅkit
  5. tuṇaitaru vampalark kāṇā tacciṉam ((5))
  6. paṉaikkāṉ ṟāṟum pāḻnāṭ ṭatta
  7. miṟantucey poruḷu miṉpan tarumeṉi
  8. ṉiḷamaiyiṟ ciṟanta vaḷamaiyu millai
  9. yiḷamai kaḻinta piṉṟai vaḷamai
  10. kāman tarutalu miṉṟē yataṉā ((10))
  11. ṉillāp poruṭpiṇic cēṟi
  12. vallē neñcam vāykaniṉ viṉaiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If they say wealth too gives joy that is made traversing
  • the road through the ruined lands, where the quick-gaited male
  • that played with the dust put in the white-backed saline soil
  • near the date palm with black toddy from the red unripe fruits abandoned
  • in a good place with fresh unripe fruits(?)[FNote_409], without seeing strangers that bring companions, alleviates that anger by attacking a palmyra palm,
  • there is no prosperity superior to youth
  • and after youth has passed by desire
  • doesn't give prosperity. Therefore,
  • you want to go in the fetter of unstable wealth.
  • Speedily, heart, may your work be achieved.

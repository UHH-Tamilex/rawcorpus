  1. tīyum vaḷiyum vicumpupayan tāṅku
  2. nōyu miṉpamu mākiṉṟu mātō
  3. māya maṉṟu tōḻi vēypayiṉ
  4. ṟeruvi nīṭiya peruvari yakantoṟun
  5. toṉṟuṟi tuppoṭu muraṇmikac ciṉiik ((5))
  6. koṉṟa yāṉik kōṭukaṇ ṭaṉṉa
  7. cempuṭik koḻumuki yaviḻnta kāntaḷ
  8. cilampuṭaṉ kamaḻuñ cāra
  9. lilaṅkumali nāṭaṉ malarnta mārpē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • As the sky's spreading fire and wind
  • has it really become pin and pleasure?
  • - the blooming chest of the man from a land of shining mountins
  • with slopes, where the Malabar-lily that opened [its] rich buds
  • with red sides spreads fragrance all along the mountin slopes,
  • as if seeing the tusks of an elephant that has killed,
  • since antagony had greatly arisen, with vigour reming of old,
  • in every inside of the great mountins where the reeds have grown long
  • [and] the bamboo rustled. It is no illusion, friend.

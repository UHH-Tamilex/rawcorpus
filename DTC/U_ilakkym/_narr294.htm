{{Metrical text}}

  1. tīyum vaḷiyum vicumpupayan tāṅku
  2. nōyu miṉpamu mākiṉṟu mātō
  3. māya maṉṟu tōḻi vēypayiṉ
  4. ṟeruvai nīṭiya peruvarai yakantoṟun
  5. toṉṟuṟai tuppoṭu muraṇmikac ciṉaiik ((5))
  6. koṉṟa yāṉaik kōṭukaṇ ṭaṉṉa
  7. cempuṭaik koḻumukai yaviḻnta kāntaḷ
  8. cilampuṭaṉ kamaḻuñ cāra
  9. lilaṅkumalai nāṭaṉ malarnta mārpē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • As the sky's spreading fire and wind
  • has it really become pain and pleasure?
  • - the blooming chest of the man from a land of shining mountains
  • with slopes, where the Malabar-lily that opened [its] rich buds
  • with red sides spreads fragrance all along the mountain slopes,
  • as if seeing the tusks of an elephant that has killed,
  • since antagony had greatly arisen, with vigour remaing of old,
  • in every inside of the great mountains where the reeds have grown long
  • [and] the bamboo rustled. It is no illusion, friend.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Risen in the morning, after preparing the swift chariot,
  2. [he's] gone to embrace women with pure ornaments -
  3. 'Very glorious [is] the man from the prosperous village'(?)[FNote_201], [thinking] thus
  4. the little one's mother is agitated.
  5. Painful, alas, to be born into this family.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Foolish indeed [are] you, may you live, Murukaṉ,
  • since you have come, even though a god,
  • to the house [prepared for] the Veṟi dance that requested the Vēlaṉ [priest],
  • wearing a chaplet from the Kaṭampa tree smelling of the riny season,
  • head held erect although you know of your not being the plague
  • in difficult pin abundant from distress come, as given by the chest
  • of the man from a land, where the fearful damsels are dancing beautifully(?)
  • to the sweet sound of the waterfall on the side of the big mountin,
  • having tied, in order to achieve a form of bright flowers [and] blood(?)[FNote_147],
  • Malabar lilies together with the blossoms of blue water-lilies, unplucked,
  • traversing the leaves[FNote_148] [and] opening in the mountin pools [between?] the stones of the god.

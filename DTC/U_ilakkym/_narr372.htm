{{Metrical text}}

  1. aḻitak kaṉṟē tōḻi kaḻicērpu
  2. kāṉaṟ peṇṇait tēṉuṭai yaḻipaḻam
  3. vaḷḷitaḻ neytal varunta mūkkiṟu
  4. paḷḷa liruñcēṟ ṟāḻap paṭṭeṉak
  5. kiḷaikkuru kiriyun tuṟaivaṉ vaḷaikōṭ ((5))
  6. ṭaṉṉa veṇmaṇaṟ ṟakavayiṉ vēṭṭa
  7. vaṇṇa luḷḷamō ṭamarntiṉitu nōkki
  8. yaṉṉai tanta valaṅkal vāṉkō
  9. ṭulaintāṅku nōta lañci yaṭaintataṟ
  10. kiṉaiya leṉṉu meṉpa maṉaiyirun ((10))
  11. tiruṅkaḻi tuḻavum paṉittalaip paratavar
  12. tiṇṭimil viḷakka meṇṇuṅ
  13. kaṇṭal vēlik kaḻinal lūrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Ruin has become fit, friend,
  • for the good village in the backwaters with mangrove fences,
  • where they count the lamps in the firm boats
  • of the fishermen with bedewed heads who stir the dark backwaters,
  • remaining at home. "Do not fret at all", they say,
  • for what has come(?), fearing to be pained, as if the swaying
  • white twig given by mother had been ruined(?)[FNote_1036],
  • looking at what is pleasing(?), abiding with exalted mind,
  • that is full of desire within(?), in the sand white as curved conches,
  • for the man from the ghat[FNote_1037], where the herons with [their] relatives retreat,
  • as a honey-filled decaying fruit form the palmyra in the seashore-grove adjoining the backwaters happened to sink into the miry dark mud,
  • breaking [its] nose, so that the many-petalled blue water-lilies suffer.

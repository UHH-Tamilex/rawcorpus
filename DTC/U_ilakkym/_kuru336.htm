{{Readable Translation}}

  1. That it scorches[FNote_1353] [her], being a pleasure to those who slander [her][FNote_1354],
  2. will they come[FNote_1355] here, o man from the ghat spread with[FNote_1356] honey?
  3. Like blue water-lilies in the dark backwaters,
  4. run over by the wheel of the long chariot with swift horses,
  5. while the small-tongued bright bells boom the Viḷari tune,
  6. she was troubled, the pitiable one you had separated from.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. We're not ashamed of the reproach, friend,
  2. that will come with [his] coming even at midday(?)[FNote_357],
  3. from the slope difficult to approach, where the old antagony languishes[FNote_358]
  4. after the small-eyed big elephant bull has attacked the strong tiger,[FNote_359]
  5. - the man from a land of high mountains with sounding white waterfalls.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. [In response to this] pain is fitting[FNote_1064], friend,
  2. upon the event[FNote_1065] of [their] saying: 'this one is possessed by a spirit!',
  3. cutting a kid's throat [and] preparing millet offerings
  4. [and] praising with many [songs?] different great gods[FNote_1066],
  5. without [that] being a remedy for the pain apart from appearance[FNote_1067],
  6. while many instruments are sounding on the crossing of moving rivers.
  7. That this [should] happen to us who haven't wronged[FNote_1068]
  8. the man from a land, where rain is playing[FNote_1069] on the great mountain!

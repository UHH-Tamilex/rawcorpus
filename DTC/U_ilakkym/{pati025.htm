Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 25-1} mā āṭiya pulaṉ nāñcil āṭā
  • {PATI 25-2} kaṭāam ceṉṉiya kaṭuṅkaṇ yāṉai
  • {PATI 25-3} iṉam paranta pulam vaḷam parappu aṟiyā
  • {PATI 25-4} niṉ paṭaiñar cērnta maṉṟam kaḻutai pōki
  • {PATI 25-5} nī uṭaṉṟōr maṉ eyil tōṭṭi vaiyā
  • {PATI 25-6} kaṭum kāl oṟṟaliṉ cuṭar ciṟantu uruttu
  • {PATI 25-7} pacum picir oḷ aḻal āṭiya maruṅkiṉ
  • {PATI 25-8} āṇṭalai vaḻaṅkum kāṉ uṇaṅku kaṭu neṟi
  • {PATI 25-9} muṉai akal perum pāḻ āka maṉṉiya
  • {PATI 25-10} urum uṟaḻpu iraṅku muraciṉ peru malai
  • {PATI 25-11} varai iḻi aruviyiṉ oḷiṟu koṭi nuṭaṅka
  • {PATI 25-12} kaṭum pari kataḻ ciṟaku akaippa nī
  • {PATI 25-13} neṭum tēr ōṭṭiya piṟar akal talai nāṭu ē

  • {KALI 30-1} arum tavam āṟṟiyār nukarcci pōl aṇi koḷa
  • {KALI 30-2} virintu āṉā ciṉi toṟūum vēṇṭum tātu amarntu āṭi
  • {KALI 30-3} purintu ārkkum vaṇṭoṭu pulampu tīrntu e vāy um
  • {KALI 30-4} irum tumpi iṟikoḷa etiriya vēṉilāṉ
  • {KALI 30-5} tuyil iṉṟi yām nīnta toḻuvi am puṉal āṭi
  • {KALI 30-6} mayil iyalār maru uṇṭu maṟantu amikuvāṉ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 30-7} veyil oḷi aṟiyāta viri malar taṇ kāvil
  • {KALI 30-8} kuyil ālum poḻutu eṉa kūṟunar uḷar āyiṉ
  • {KALI 30-9} pāṉāḷ yām paṭar kūra paṇi eḻil aṇi mel tōḷ
  • {KALI 30-10} māṉ nōkkiṉavaroṭu maṟantu amikuvāṉ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 30-11} āṉā cīr kūṭal uḷ arumpu aviḻ naṟu mulli
  • {KALI 30-12} tēṉ ārkkum poḻutu eṉa teḷikkunar uḷar āyiṉ
  • {KALI 30-13} uṟal yām oḷi vāṭa uyarntavaṉ viḻaviṉ uḷ
  • {KALI 30-14} viṟal iḻiyavarōṭu viḷiyāṭuvāṉ maṉ ō
  • {KALI 30-15} peṟal arum poḻutōṭu piṟaṅku iṇar turutti cūḻntu
  • {KALI 30-16} aṟal vārum viyi eṉṟu aṟiyunar uḷar āyiṉ
  • {KALI 30-17} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 30-18} taṇiyā nōy uḻantu āṉā takiyavaḷ takipeṟa
  • {KALI 30-19} aṇi kiḷar neṭum tiṇ tēr ayarmati paṇipu niṉ
  • {KALI 30-20} kāmar kaḻal aṭi cērā
  • {KALI 30-21} nāmam cāl tevvariṉ naṭuṅkiṉaḷ peritu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The sea ceased singing, the seashore groves [fell] enchanted,
  2. the ghat waters [have become] dark backwaters - it is empty.[FNote_711]
  3. Today, as the Aṉṟil is calling gently,
  4. whose living is amongst the Palmyra stems of the village common,[FNote_712]
  5. will he come, oh friend,
  6. he who has captured [our] desire, difficult to escape,
  7. [which we] fear just like separation, also when we sulk with him? (?)[FNote_713]

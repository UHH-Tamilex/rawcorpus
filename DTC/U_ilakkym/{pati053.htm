Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 53-1} veṉṟu kalam tarīiyar vēṇṭu pulattu iṟuttu avar
  • {PATI 53-2} vāṭā yāṇar nāṭu tiṟai koṭuppa
  • {PATI 53-3} nalkiṉai ākumati em eṉṟu aruḷi
  • {PATI 53-4} kal piṟaṅku vaippiṉ kaṭaṟu arai yātta niṉ
  • {PATI 53-5} tol pukaḻ mūtūr celkuvai āyiṉ
  • {PATI 53-6} ce poṟi cilampoṭu aṇi taḻai tūṅkum
  • {PATI 53-7} entira takaippiṉ ampu uṭai vāyil
  • {PATI 53-8} kōḷ val mutalaiya kuṇṭu kaṇ akaḻi
  • {PATI 53-9} vāṉ uṟa ōṅkiya vaḷaintu cey puricai
  • {PATI 53-10} oṉṉā tevvar muṉai keṭa vilaṅki
  • {PATI 53-11} niṉṉiṉ tanta maṉ eyil allatu
  • {PATI 53-12} muṉ um piṉ um niṉ muṉṉōr ōmpiya
  • {PATI 53-13} eyil mukappaṭuttal yāvatu vaḷaiyiṉum
  • {PATI 53-14} piṟitu āṟu ceṉmati ciṉam keḻu kurucil
  • {PATI 53-15} eḻūu puṟantarīi poṉ piṇi palakai
  • {PATI 53-16} kuḻūu nilai putaviṉ katavu mey kāṇiṉ
  • {PATI 53-17} tēm pāy kaṭāttoṭu kāḻ kai nīvi
  • {PATI 53-18} vēṅkai veṉṟa poṟi kiḷar pukar nutal
  • {PATI 53-19} ēntu kai curuṭṭi tōṭṭi nīvi
  • {PATI 53-20} mēmpaṭu vel koṭi nuṭaṅka
  • {PATI 53-21} tāṅkal ākā āṅku niṉ kaḷiṟu ē

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 53-1} veṉṟu kalam tarīiyar vēṇṭu pulattu iṟuttu avar
  • {PATI 53-2} vāṭā yāṇar nāṭu tiṟi koṭuppa
  • {PATI 53-3} nalkiṉi ākumati em eṉṟu aruḷi
  • {PATI 53-4} kal piṟaṅku vippiṉ kaṭaṟu ari yātta niṉ
  • {PATI 53-5} tol pukaḻ mūtūr celkuvi āyiṉ
  • {PATI 53-6} ce poṟi cilampoṭu aṇi taḻi tūṅkum
  • {PATI 53-7} entira takippiṉ ampu uṭi vāyil
  • {PATI 53-8} kōḷ val mutaliya kuṇṭu kaṇ akaḻi
  • {PATI 53-9} vāṉ uṟa ōṅkiya vaḷintu cey purici
  • {PATI 53-10} oṉṉā tevvar muṉi keṭa vilaṅki
  • {PATI 53-11} niṉṉiṉ tanta maṉ eyil allatu
  • {PATI 53-12} muṉ um piṉ um niṉ muṉṉōr ōmpiya
  • {PATI 53-13} eyil mukappaṭuttal yāvatu vaḷiyiṉum
  • {PATI 53-14} piṟitu āṟu ceṉmati ciṉam keḻu kurucil
  • {PATI 53-15} eḻūu puṟantarīi poṉ piṇi palaki
  • {PATI 53-16} kuḻūu nili putaviṉ katavu mey kāṇiṉ
  • {PATI 53-17} tēm pāy kaṭāttoṭu kāḻ ki nīvi
  • {PATI 53-18} vēṅki veṉṟa poṟi kiḷar pukar nutal
  • {PATI 53-19} ēntu ki curuṭṭi tōṭṭi nīvi
  • {PATI 53-20} mēmpaṭu vel koṭi nuṭaṅka
  • {PATI 53-21} tāṅkal ākā āṅku niṉ kaḷiṟu ē

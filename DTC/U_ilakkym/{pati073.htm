Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 73-1} uravōr eṇṇiṉum maṭavōr eṇṇiṉum
  • {PATI 73-2} piṟarkku nī vāyiṉ allatu niṉakku
  • {PATI 73-3} piṟar uvamam ākā oru peru vēntu ē
  • {PATI 73-4} kūntal oḷ nutal polinta
  • {PATI 73-5} niṟam tikaḻ pācu iḻai uyar tiṇai makaḷir um
  • {PATI 73-6} teyvam tarūu neñcattu āṉṟōr
  • {PATI 73-8} marutam cāṉṟa malar talai viḷai vayal
  • {PATI 73-9} cey uḷ nārai oyyum makaḷir
  • {PATI 73-10} iravu um pakal um pācu iḻai kaḷaiyār
  • {PATI 73-11} kuṟum pal yāṇar kuravai ayarum
  • {PATI 73-12} kāviri maṇṭiya cēy viri vaṉappiṉ
  • {PATI 73-13} pukāar celva pūḻiyar meymmaṟai
  • {PATI 73-14} kaḻai virintu eḻutaru maḻai tavaḻ neṭum kōṭṭu
  • {PATI 73-15} kolli poruna koṭi tēr poṟaiya niṉ
  • {PATI 73-16} vaḷaṉ um āṇmai um kaivaṇmai um
  • {PATI 73-17} māntar aḷavu iṟantaṉa eṉa pal nāḷ
  • {PATI 73-18} yāṉ ceṉṟu uraippa um tēṟār piṟar um
  • {PATI 73-19} cāṉṟōr uraippa teḷikuvar kol eṉa
  • {PATI 73-20} āṅku um mati maruḷa kāṇkuval
  • {PATI 73-21} yāṅku uraippēṉ eṉa varuntuval yāṉ ē

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 73-1} uravōr eṇṇiṉum maṭavōr eṇṇiṉum
  • {PATI 73-2} piṟarkku nī vāyiṉ allatu niṉakku
  • {PATI 73-3} piṟar uvamam ākā oru peru vēntu ē
  • {PATI 73-4} kūntal oḷ nutal polinta
  • {PATI 73-5} niṟam tikaḻ pācu iḻi uyar tiṇi makaḷir um
  • {PATI 73-6} teyvam tarūu neñcattu āṉṟōr
  • {PATI 73-8} marutam cāṉṟa malar tali viḷi vayal
  • {PATI 73-9} cey uḷ nāri oyyum makaḷir
  • {PATI 73-10} iravu um pakal um pācu iḻi kaḷiyār
  • {PATI 73-11} kuṟum pal yāṇar kuravi ayarum
  • {PATI 73-12} kāviri maṇṭiya cēy viri vaṉappiṉ
  • {PATI 73-13} pukāar celva pūḻiyar meymmaṟi
  • {PATI 73-14} kaḻi virintu eḻutaru maḻi tavaḻ neṭum kōṭṭu
  • {PATI 73-15} kolli poruna koṭi tēr poṟiya niṉ
  • {PATI 73-16} vaḷaṉ um āṇmi um kivaṇmi um
  • {PATI 73-17} māntar aḷavu iṟantaṉa eṉa pal nāḷ
  • {PATI 73-18} yāṉ ceṉṟu urippa um tēṟār piṟar um
  • {PATI 73-19} cāṉṟōr urippa teḷikuvar kol eṉa
  • {PATI 73-20} āṅku um mati maruḷa kāṇkuval
  • {PATI 73-21} yāṅku urippēṉ eṉa varuntuval yāṉ ē

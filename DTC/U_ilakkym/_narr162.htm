{{Metrical text}}

  1. maṇaiyuṟai puṟaviṉ ceṅkāṟ pēṭaik
  2. kāmar tuṇaiyoṭu cēval cērap
  3. pulampiṉ ṟeḻutarum puṉkaṇ mālait
  4. taṉiyē yirutta lāṟṟē ṉeṉṟuniṉ
  5. paṉivā ruṇkaṇ paitala kaluḻa ((5))
  6. nummoṭu varuta leṉṟi yemmoṭu
  7. perumpeyart tantai nītupukaḻ neṭunakar
  8. yāyoṭu naṉimika maṭavai muṉātu
  9. vēli ṉiṟṟit tōyā neṭunvīḻ
  10. vaḻinā ḷūcaliṟ kōṭai tūkkutoṟun ((10))
  11. tuñcumpiṭi varuṭu mattam
  12. vallai yākuta lollumō niṉakkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Lonely on sorrowful evening, rising irresistably[FNote_524],
  • endowed with loneliness[FNote_525], when the cock joins with [its] desired companion,
  • the reg-legged female of the dove staying in the house.
  • I cannot bear [it]"[FNote_526] saying so while your
  • collyrium eyes, overflowing with dew, dimned from sufferings,
  • you said [you would] come with us.
  • With us[FNote_527], is it possible to you to be strong
  • for the road where the long aerial roots that doesn't touch [the ground]
  • of the fig tree, spearlike, rubs the sleeping elephant cow
  • whenever the west wind lifts [it], on a swing on suceeding days,
  • to you who have had no experience at all before(?)[FNote_528], with [your] mother
  • in the long-praised large mansion of [your] great-named father?

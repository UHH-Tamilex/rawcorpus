Kalittokai

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 52-1} muṟam cevi maṟai pāypu muraṇ ceyta puli ceṟṟu
  • {KALI 52-2} maṟam talaikkoṇṭa nūṟṟuvar talaivaṉai
  • {KALI 52-3} kuṟaṅku aṟuttiṭuvāṉ pōl kūr nuti maṭuttu ataṉ
  • {KALI 52-4} niṟam cāṭi muraṇ tīrnta nīḷ maruppu eḻil yāṉai
  • {KALI 52-5} mallarai maṟam cāytta māl pōl taṉ kiḷai nāppaṇ
  • {KALI 52-6} kal uyar naṉam cāral kalantu iyalum nāṭa kēḷ
  • {KALI 52-7} tāmarai kaṇṇiyai taṇ naṟum cāntiṉai
  • {KALI 52-8} nēr itaḻ kōtaiyāḷ cey kuṟi nī variṉ
  • {KALI 52-9} maṇam kamaḻ nāṟṟatta malai niṉṟu pali peṟūum
  • {KALI 52-10} aṇaṅku eṉa añcuvar ciṟukuṭiyōr ē
  • {KALI 52-11} īrm taṇ āṭaiyai elli mālaiyai
  • {KALI 52-12} cōrntu vīḻ katuppiṉāḷ cey kuṟi nī variṉ
  • {KALI 52-13} oḷi tikaḻ ñekiḻiyar kavaṇaiyar villar
  • {KALI 52-14} kaḷiṟu eṉa ārppavar ēṉal kāvalar ē
  • {KALI 52-15} āra mārpiṉai aṇṇalai aḷiyai
  • {KALI 52-16} aitu akal alkulāḷ cey kuṟi nī variṉ
  • {KALI 52-17} kaṟi vaḷar cilampil vaḻaṅkal āṉā
  • {KALI 52-18} puli eṉṟu ōrkkum i kali kēḻ ūr ē
  • {KALI 52-19} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 52-20} vilaṅku ōrār mey ōrppiṉ ivaḷ vāḻāḷ ivaḷ aṉṟi
  • {KALI 52-21} pulam pukaḻ oruva yāṉ um vāḻēṉ
  • {KALI 52-22} ataṉāl poti aviḻ vaikaṟai vantu nī kuṟai kūṟi
  • {KALI 52-23} vatuvai ayartal vēṇṭuval āṅku
  • {KALI 52-24} putuvai pōlum niṉ varavu um ivaḷ
  • {KALI 52-25} vatuvai nāṇ oṭukkam um kāṇkuval yāṉ ē

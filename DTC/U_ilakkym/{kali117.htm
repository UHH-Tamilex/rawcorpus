  • {KALI 117-1} māṇa urukkiya nal poṉ maṇi uṟīi
  • {KALI 117-2} pēṇi tuṭaittaṉṉa mēṉiyāy kōṅkiṉ
  • {KALI 117-3} mutirā iḷa mukai oppa etiriya
  • {KALI 117-4} toyyil poṟitta vaṉa mulaiyāy maṟṟu niṉ
  • {KALI 117-5} kaiyatu evaṉ maṟṟu urai
  • {KALI 117-6} kaiyatu ai cēri kiḻavaṉ makaḷēṉ yāṉ maṟṟu iḵtu ōr
  • {KALI 117-7} mātar pulaitti vilai āka ceytatu ōr
  • {KALI 117-8} pōḻiṉ puṉainta vari puṭṭil puṭṭil uḷ eṉ uḷa
  • {KALI 117-9} kāṇtakkāy eṉ kāṭṭi kāṇ
  • {KALI 117-10} kāṇ iṉi tōṭṭār katuppiṉ eṉ tōḻi avaroṭu
  • {KALI 117-11} kāṭṭu cār koyta ciṟu mullai maṟṟu ivai
  • {KALI 117-12} mullai ivai āyiṉ muṟṟiya kūḻaiyāy
  • {KALI 117-13} elliṟṟu pōḻtu āyiṉ ītōḷi kaṇṭēṉ āl
  • {KALI 117-14} cel eṉṟu niṉṉai viṭuvēṉ yāṉ maṟṟu eṉakku
  • {KALI 117-15} melliyatu ōrātu aṟivu

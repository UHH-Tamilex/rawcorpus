  • {NT 348-1} nilavu ē nīl niṟa vicumpil pal katir parappi
  • {NT 348-2} pāl mali kaṭaliṉ parantu paṭṭaṉṟu ē
  • {NT 348-3} ūr ē olivarum cummiyoṭu malipu tokupu īṇṭi
  • {NT 348-4} kali keḻu maṟukiṉ viḻavu ayarum ē
  • {NT 348-5} kāṉ ē pū malar kañaliya poḻil akam tōṟum
  • {NT 348-6} tām amar tuṇiyoṭu vaṇṭu imirum ē
  • {NT 348-7} yāṉ ē puṉi iḻi ñekiḻtta pulampu koḷ avalamoṭu
  • {NT 348-8} kaṉi irum kaṅkul um kaṇpaṭi ileṉ ē
  • {NT 348-9} ataṉāl eṉṉoṭu porum kol i ulakam
  • {NT 348-10} ulakamoṭu porum kol eṉ avalam uṟu neñcu ē

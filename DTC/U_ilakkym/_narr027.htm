{{Readable Translation}}

  • You and me, yesterday, besides driving away groups of bees,
  • that were crowding on the fine pollen of the flowers,
  • [and] dancing in the seashore grove surrounded by backwaters
  • on the settling shore in the white sand marked by subsiding(?)[FNote_117] waves
  • there is not a thing done by us secretly. If they say it is,
  • there are no others to have spread [this] from knowledge(?)[FNote_118].
  • What was well(?)[FNote_119] on mother`s mind?
  • - who did not tell [us] to go [and] pluck[FNote_120]
  • blue water-lilies with tiny green leaves,
  • fine [and] many[FNote_121], at the sight of [their] blossoming just like eyes,
  • grown on tubular stems at a side where sharks join in the backwaters,
  • while the herons are clamouring, flocks feeding on prawn in every pond.

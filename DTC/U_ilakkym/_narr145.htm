{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even before there was reason, our being attached
  • in intimacy to the desirous man from the sea,
  • where the women join garlands with [their] tresses
  • from the big-petalled flowers of the Aṭumpu in large creepers,
  • in the moist white sand beaten by the dark backwaters,
  • [our] dutiless/dutifull mother says: "where [is] he?",
  • talking while she regards [me](?)[FNote_462] as if he had united with us.
  • [line 8 ???!]
  • Will he come, even at deepest night,
  • softly, in the street of large-trunked Puṉṉi trees?
  • Alas, may the voice of his chariot bells live.

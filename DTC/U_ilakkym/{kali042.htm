  • {KALI 42-1} maṟam koḷ irum puli tol muraṇ tolitta
  • {KALI 42-2} muṟam cevi vāraṇam muṉ kuḷaku arunti
  • {KALI 42-3} kaṟaṅku veḷ aruvi ōliṉ tuñcum
  • {KALI 42-4} piṟaṅku irum cōli nal mali nāṭaṉ
  • {KALI 42-5} maṟantāṉ maṟakka iṉi ellā namakku
  • {KALI 42-6} ciṟantami nām naṟku aṟintaṉam āyiṉ avaṉ tiṟam
  • {KALI 42-7} kol yāṉi kōṭṭāl vetir nel kuṟuvām nām
  • {KALI 42-8} vaḷḷi akavuvam vā ikuḷi nām
  • {KALI 42-9} vaḷḷi akavuvam vā
  • {KALI 42-10} kāṇiya vā vāḻi tōḻi vari tāḻpu
  • {KALI 42-11} vāḷ niṟam koṇṭa aruvittu ē nam aruḷā
  • {KALI 42-12} nāṇ ili nāṭṭu mali
  • {KALI 42-13} ārvuṟṟār neñcam aḻiya viṭuvāṉ ō
  • {KALI 42-14} ōrvu uṟṟu oru tiṟam olkāta nērkōl
  • {KALI 42-15} aṟam puri neñcattavaṉ
  • {KALI 42-16} taṇ naṟum kōṅkam malarnta vari ellām
  • {KALI 42-17} poṉ aṇi yāṉi pōl tōṉṟum ē nam aruḷā
  • {KALI 42-18} koṉṉāḷaṉ nāṭṭu mali
  • {KALI 42-19} kūru nōy ēyppa viṭuvāṉ ō taṉ mali
  • {KALI 42-20} nīriṉum cāyal uṭiyaṉ nayantōrkku
  • {KALI 42-21} tēr īyum vaṇ kiyavaṉ
  • {KALI 42-22} vari mici mēl toṭutta ney kaṇ iṟāal
  • {KALI 42-23} maḻi nuḻi tiṅkaḷ pōl tōṉṟum iḻi nekiḻa
  • {KALI 42-24} evvam uṟīiyiṉāṉ kuṉṟu
  • {KALI 42-25} eñcātu ellā koṭumi nuvalāti
  • {KALI 42-26} añcuvatu añcā aṟaṉ ili allaṉ eṉ
  • {KALI 42-27} neñcam piṇikkoṇṭavaṉ
  • {KALI 42-28} eṉṟu yām pāṭa maṟi niṉṟu kēṭṭaṉaṉ
  • {KALI 42-29} tāḻ irum kūntal eṉ tōḻiyi ki kaviyā
  • {KALI 42-30} cāyal iṉ mārpaṉ ciṟupuṟam cārtara
  • {KALI 42-31} ñāyiṟṟu muṉṉar iruḷ pōla māyntatu eṉ
  • {KALI 42-32} āy iḻi mēṉi pacappu

  • {KALI 42-1} maṟam koḷ irum puli tol muraṇ tolaitta
  • {KALI 42-2} muṟam cevi vāraṇam muṉ kuḷaku arunti
  • {KALI 42-3} kaṟaṅku veḷ aruvi ōliṉ tuñcum
  • {KALI 42-4} piṟaṅku irum cōlai nal malai nāṭaṉ
  • {KALI 42-5} maṟantāṉ maṟakka iṉi ellā namakku
  • {KALI 42-6} ciṟantamai nām naṟku aṟintaṉam āyiṉ avaṉ tiṟam
  • {KALI 42-7} kol yāṉai kōṭṭāl vetir nel kuṟuvām nām
  • {KALI 42-8} vaḷḷai akavuvam vā ikuḷai nām
  • {KALI 42-9} vaḷḷai akavuvam vā
  • {KALI 42-10} kāṇiya vā vāḻi tōḻi varai tāḻpu
  • {KALI 42-11} vāḷ niṟam koṇṭa aruvittu ē nam aruḷā
  • {KALI 42-12} nāṇ ili nāṭṭu malai
  • {KALI 42-13} ārvuṟṟār neñcam aḻiya viṭuvāṉ ō
  • {KALI 42-14} ōrvu uṟṟu oru tiṟam olkāta nērkōl
  • {KALI 42-15} aṟam puri neñcattavaṉ
  • {KALI 42-16} taṇ naṟum kōṅkam malarnta varai ellām
  • {KALI 42-17} poṉ aṇi yāṉai pōl tōṉṟum ē nam aruḷā
  • {KALI 42-18} koṉṉāḷaṉ nāṭṭu malai
  • {KALI 42-19} kūru nōy ēyppa viṭuvāṉ ō taṉ malai
  • {KALI 42-20} nīriṉum cāyal uṭaiyaṉ nayantōrkku
  • {KALI 42-21} tēr īyum vaṇ kaiyavaṉ
  • {KALI 42-22} varai micai mēl toṭutta ney kaṇ iṟāal
  • {KALI 42-23} maḻai nuḻai tiṅkaḷ pōl tōṉṟum iḻai nekiḻa
  • {KALI 42-24} evvam uṟīiyiṉāṉ kuṉṟu
  • {KALI 42-25} eñcātu ellā koṭumai nuvalāti
  • {KALI 42-26} añcuvatu añcā aṟaṉ ili allaṉ eṉ
  • {KALI 42-27} neñcam piṇikkoṇṭavaṉ
  • {KALI 42-28} eṉṟu yām pāṭa maṟai niṉṟu kēṭṭaṉaṉ
  • {KALI 42-29} tāḻ irum kūntal eṉ tōḻiyai kai kaviyā
  • {KALI 42-30} cāyal iṉ mārpaṉ ciṟupuṟam cārtara
  • {KALI 42-31} ñāyiṟṟu muṉṉar iruḷ pōla māyntatu eṉ
  • {KALI 42-32} āy iḻai mēṉi pacappu

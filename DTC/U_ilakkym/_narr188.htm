{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the sky-touching mountin, where the piled bud with sharp edge
  • brought forth by the bent stem of the plantin swollen the water-rich
  • mountin-side touches the pointed petals of the Malabar-lily,
  • as the ornament on the soft fingers
  • joins with the shining bangles of bright-jewelled women,
  • - if she she understood well that day
  • that as the result of also kindness something evil arises,
  • she would not perish indeed, bamboo shoulder resembling bamboo,
  • plump on the honey-ripening slopes up the hill.

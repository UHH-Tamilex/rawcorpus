{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Will he ever forget your fragrant forehead,
  2. smelling of wild jasmine tied with water-lilies
  3. from the deep broad-mouthed pool on Aralai hill
  4. [belonging to] the king of suppliants with the beat of drums[FNote_245]?
  5. Because, even if he makes an effort,
  6. the wealth difficult [to attain] lying athwart in many deserts
  7. has not been attained, will there be prolongation?[FNote_246] No.

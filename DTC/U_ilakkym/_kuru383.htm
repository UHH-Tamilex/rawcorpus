{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When I gave [him] your agreement, he came
  2. [and] stood at the sign/meeting place, the man from the hills.
  3. 'May the span of today go', you said.[FNote_1522]
  4. Nothing have I to do
  5. than trembling like a sprout fallen into the fire
  6. so that hands and feet growing tired(?) will be spoiled.(?)[FNote_1523]

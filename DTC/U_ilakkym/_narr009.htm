{{Readable Translation}}

  • When, like people, desirous to exert themselves without causing loss,
  • upon seeing with [their] eyes the god they had set out for,
  • we will have attained your good soft bamboo shoulders,
  • so that the whirling misery ends,
  • - when the plague increases in your beautiful breast adorned with beauty spots,
  • [like] the graceful bright sprouts on the Puṉku tree with parched-rice flowers,[FNote_32]
  • whenever [you] see shade you may stay long,
  • whenever [you] see sand you may join it in play,
  • you may go without worry, o you of pure teeth.
  • The way we will go has many small villages
  • in forests with fragrant cool groves,
  • where the joyous Coel dance, picking at big buds[FNote_33].

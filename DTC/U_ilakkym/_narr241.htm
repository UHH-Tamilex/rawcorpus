{{Metrical text}}

  1. uḷḷār kollō tōḻi koṭuñciṟai
  2. yuḷḷaṭi poṟitta variyuṭait talaiya
  3. nīraḻi maruṅki ṉīrayir tōṉṟa
  4. vaḷarā vāṭai yuḷarpunaṉi tīṇṭaliṉ
  5. vēḻa veṇpū virivaṉa palavuṭaṉ ((5))
  6. vēntuvīcu kavariyiṟ pūmputa laṇiya
  7. maḻaikaḻi vicumpiṉ māṟi ñāyiṟu
  8. viḻittimaip patupōl viḷaṅkupu maṟaiya
  9. vellai pōkiya poḻuti ṉelluṟap
  10. paṉikkāṟ koṇṭa paiyuḷ yāmattup ((10))
  11. pallita ḻuṇkaṇ kaluḻa
  12. nillāp poruṭpiṇi pirintici ṉōrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Won't he remember, friend,
  • the one who has separated in the fetter of transient wealth,
  • while [my] many-petalled collyrium eyes are dimming
  • at sorrowful midnight taken by the dewy wind,
  • when night falls(?) at the time the daylight is gone,
  • when the sun after shining vanishes as a [eye] twinkling in
  • opening, having changed the sky as the rain is passing,
  • while the bushes adorn themselves with flowers as a ? ?[FNote_761] for a king,
  • many together, the Vēḻam's white flowers expanding(?)[FNote_762]
  • for the ungrown northwind whiffling [and] coming much into contact [with them](?)[FNote_763],
  • while in the wet sand at a side where the water is spent appear
  • lined(?) [the traces?] impressed by the inner feet of those with bent wings(?)[FNote_764]?

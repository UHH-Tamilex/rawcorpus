{{Readable Translation}}

  1. It [was] good that she plucked lotuses in the backwaters and
  2. played in the white-headed sea waves.
  3. She who would on the one hand[FNote_576] not have[FNote_577]
  4. the inseparable attendants forget themselves in something[FNote_578] [without her],[FNote_579]
  5. on the other hand she actually[FNote_580] went, so that she suffered the pebbles ruining [her feet],
  6. into the land of transverse mountains, glistening high as the sky,
  7. [their] tops covered in wandering clouds[FNote_581]?

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The chest with drying sandal paste of the man from a land of high mountains,
  2. where flash forth in place after place like stars in the sky
  3. the fire-brands with fragrant smoke, kindled by the [field-]guardian[FNote_601];
  4. remembering [it], [my] inner pain increases,
  5. embracing, it vanishes. What [is this], mother?[FNote_602]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. I won't remember, friend,
  2. the man from the coast of the great sea buzzing with birds,
  3. where women with bright armlets immerse themselves in play
  4. after having picked flowers bright as sapphires for a garland[FNote_989]
  5. of the Aṭumpu plants [forked] like a stag's foot.
  6. {--!}May my eyes close.

Altern

5b: ... {--!}, while my eyes suffer.[FNote_990]

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the sky-touching mountain,
  • where the beloved inexperienced daughter of the stone-ploughing hill people
  • gives[FNote_1002] a festival to come to the black-fingered female monkey:
  • a pot-like big fruit of the jacktree grown old(?)
  • on the high long mountain with peaks that are covered in playing clouds
  • in groups, as very fine cotton
  • made by the effort of manless women,
  • you are not worthy,
  • for coming by the fearful small path,
  • where the large-trunked elephant that had destroyed a tiger
  • roars like thunder in hot anger,
  • at midnight, even if our desire is at melting point.

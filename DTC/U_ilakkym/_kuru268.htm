{{Readable Translation}}

  1. We cannot say: 'are you going?'
  2. and we don't ask [him] urgently[FNote_1089]: 'are you coming?'
  3. the one who had enjoyed the long soft bamboo shoulders,
  4. coming without saying: 'midnight,
  5. with thundering which severs the big/dark hooded head
  6. of the snake'. What can we do, friend?

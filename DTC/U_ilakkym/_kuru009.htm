{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Become mother[FNote_40], the dark one!
  2. Like flowers kept alone in a vessel famed for[FNote_41] its clasp,
  3. unworn, her body is emaciated.
  4. Ashamed even of us she plays hiding[FNote_42]
  5. the faithlessness of the man from the cool ghat
  6. where the round-stemmed blue water-lilies risen up from green leaves
  7. whenever the flood rises in the dark backwaters with all varieties of fish[FNote_43]
  8. resemble the eyes of women diving in tanks.

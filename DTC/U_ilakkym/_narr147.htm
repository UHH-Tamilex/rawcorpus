{{Metrical text}}

  1. yāṅkā kuvamō vaṇinutaṟ kuṟumaka
  2. ṭēmpaṭu cāraṟ ciṟutiṉaip perunkural
  3. cevvāyp paiṅkiḷi kavara nīmaṟ
  4. ṟevvāyc ceṉṟaṉai yavaṇeṉak kūṟi
  5. yaṉṉai yāṉāḷ kaḻaṟa muṉṉiṉ ((5))
  6. ṟaruvi yārkkum peruvarai nāṭaṉai
  7. yaṟiyalu maṟiyēṉ kāṇṭalu milaṉē
  8. vetirpuṉai taṭṭaiyēṉ malarppūk koytu
  9. cuṉaippāyn tāṭiṟṟu milaṉeṉa niṉaivilai, kaṉaviṉ
  10. poyya lantō vāyttaṉai yatukēṭṭut ((10))
  11. talaiyiṟaiñ ciṉaḷē yaṉṉai
  12. celavoḻin taṉaiyā laḷiyainī puṉattē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What will become of us, little woman with decorated forehead ?
  • When mother murmured unendingly(?)[FNote_468] and said
  • "to which place did you go from there,
  • just you[FNote_469], while the red-mouthed green parakeets seized
  • the big ears of the small millet on the honey-having slope?",
  • you stood in fornt [of her and] did not think[FNote_470], saying "I don't know at all
  • the man from a land of great mountains where the waterfalls sound.
  • I have not seen [him] at all. I had a Taṭṭai fashioned out of bamboo.
  • I did not play, plucking blossoming flowers [and] jumping into mountain pools."
  • Don't lie[FNote_471], alas. You have achieved it. Having heard that
  • mother bent [her] head.
  • You have [forthwith] ceased going to the fields, pitiable you.

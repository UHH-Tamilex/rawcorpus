{{Metrical text}}

  1. yāṅkā kuvamō vaṇinutaṟ kuṟumaka
  2. ṭēmpaṭu cāraṟ ciṟutiṉip perunkural
  3. cevvāyp piṅkiḷi kavara nīmaṟ
  4. ṟevvāyc ceṉṟaṉi yavaṇeṉak kūṟi
  5. yaṉṉi yāṉāḷ kaḻaṟa muṉṉiṉ ((5))
  6. ṟaruvi yārkkum peruvari nāṭaṉi
  7. yaṟiyalu maṟiyēṉ kāṇṭalu milaṉē
  8. vetirpuṉi taṭṭiyēṉ malarppūk koytu
  9. cuṉippāyn tāṭiṟṟu milaṉeṉa niṉivili, kaṉaviṉ
  10. poyya lantō vāyttaṉi yatukēṭṭut ((10))
  11. taliyiṟiñ ciṉaḷē yaṉṉi
  12. celavoḻin taṉiyā laḷiyinī puṉattē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What will become of us, little woman with decorated forehead ?
  • When mother murmured unendingly(?)[FNote_468] and sid
  • "to which place did you go from there,
  • just you[FNote_469], while the red-mouthed green parakeets seized
  • the big ears of the small millet on the honey-having slope?",
  • you stood in fornt [of her and] did not think[FNote_470], saying "I don't know at all
  • the man from a land of great mountins where the waterfalls sound.
  • I have not seen [him] at all. I had a Taṭṭi fashioned out of bamboo.
  • I did not play, plucking blossoming flowers [and] jumping into mountin pools."
  • Don't lie[FNote_471], alas. You have achieved it. Having heard that
  • mother bent [her] head.
  • You have [forthwith] ceased going to the fields, pitiable you.

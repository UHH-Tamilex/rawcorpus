  • {NT 384-3} muḷari am kuṭampi īṉṟu iḷippaṭṭa
  • {NT 384-4} uyavu naṭi pēṭi uṇīiya maṉṉar
  • {NT 384-5} muṉi kavar mutu pāḻ uku nel peṟūum
  • {NT 384-6} araṇ il cēy nāṭṭu atar iṭi malarnta
  • {NT 384-7} naṉṉāḷ vēṅki poṉ maruḷ putu pū
  • {NT 384-8} parantaṉa naṭakka yām kaṇṭaṉam mātō
  • {NT 384-9} kāṇ iṉi vāḻi eṉ neñcu ē nāṇ viṭṭu
  • {NT 384-10} arum tuyar uḻanta kāli
  • {NT 384-11} maruntu eṉappaṭūum maṭavōḷi ē

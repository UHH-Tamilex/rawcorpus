Naṟṟiṇai

((Originally extracted from master file ^NARRI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {NT 384-1} paim puṟa puṟaviṉ ce kāl cēval
  • {NT 384-2} kaḷari ōṅkiya kavai muṭa kaḷḷi
  • {NT 384-3} muḷari am kuṭampai īṉṟu iḷaippaṭṭa
  • {NT 384-4} uyavu naṭai pēṭai uṇīiya maṉṉar
  • {NT 384-5} muṉai kavar mutu pāḻ uku nel peṟūum
  • {NT 384-6} araṇ il cēy nāṭṭu atar iṭai malarnta
  • {NT 384-7} naṉṉāḷ vēṅkai poṉ maruḷ putu pū
  • {NT 384-8} parantaṉa naṭakka yām kaṇṭaṉam mātō
  • {NT 384-9} kāṇ iṉi vāḻi eṉ neñcu ē nāṇ viṭṭu
  • {NT 384-10} arum tuyar uḻanta kālai
  • {NT 384-11} maruntu eṉappaṭūum maṭavōḷai ē

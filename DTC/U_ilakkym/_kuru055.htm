{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, [just] a few days this little good village has,
  2. [before] becoming an unpleasant abode
  3. with abrasive cold wind, come for hands to part,
  4. the clouds enmeshed with sprays of foam[FNote_232]
  5. from the pure waves, so that sapphire flowers close in the dark backwaters.

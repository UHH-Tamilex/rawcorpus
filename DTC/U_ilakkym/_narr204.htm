  1. taḷircēr taṇṭaḻi tii nunti
  2. kuḷirvāy viyaṉpuṉat teṟpaṭa varukō
  3. kuṟuñcuṉik kuvaḷi yaṭiccinām puṇariya
  4. naṟuntaṇ kāṉa lāṭukam varukō
  5. viṉcoṉ mēvalip paṭṭave ṉeñcuṇak ((5))
  6. kūṟiṉi maṭantiniṉ kūreyi ṟuṇkeṉa
  7. yāṉṟaṉ moḻitaliṉ moḻiyetir vantu
  8. tāṉcey kuṟinili yiṉiya kūṟi
  9. yēṟupiri maṭappiṇi pōla vērupaṭ
  10. ṭuṟukaḻi nivappiṟ ciṟukuṭip peyaruṅ ((10))
  11. koṭicci celpuṟa nōkki
  12. viṭutta neñcam viṭalol lāvē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Shall I come when the light comes[FNote_644] to the field fit for the drum
  • of your father, knitting cool foliage joining with sprouts?
  • Shall I come for us to play[FNote_645] in the fragrant cool seashore grove
  • for us to unite [and] put in blue water-lilies from the little mountin spring?
  • Speak now, girl,
  • for my heart to enjoy that is tormented by the wish for sweet words.
  • let me eat your sharp teeth[FNote_646]",[FNote_647]
  • when I thus speak to her, she comes up [to me] with an answer,
  • talks sweetly about the position of the sign she herself would make,
  • [suddenly] changes [her mind](?)[FNote_648] as a youthful doe separated from the bull
  • [and] goes back to the small hamlet on the bamboo-grown height.[FNote_649]
  • Looking at the moving back of the creeper-woman
  • [my] heart that had been let go off cannot let [her] go.

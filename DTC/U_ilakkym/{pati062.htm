Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 62-1} iḻi aṇintu eḻutarum pal kaḷiṟṟu toḻutiyoṭu
  • {PATI 62-2} maḻi eṉa maruḷum mā irum pal tōl
  • {PATI 62-3} eḵku paṭi aṟutta koy cuval puraviyoṭu
  • {PATI 62-4} mintu uṭi ār eyil puṭi paṭa vaḷii
  • {PATI 62-5} vantu puṟattu iṟukkum pacum picir oḷ aḻal
  • {PATI 62-6} ñāyiṟu palkiya māyamoṭu cuṭar tikaḻpu
  • {PATI 62-7} ollā mayaloṭu pāṭu imiḻpu uḻitarum
  • {PATI 62-8} maṭaṅkal vaṇṇam koṇṭa kaṭum tiṟal
  • {PATI 62-9} tuppu tuṟipōkiya koṟṟa vēntu ē
  • {PATI 62-10} puṉal poru kiṭaṅkiṉ vari pōl iñci
  • {PATI 62-11} aṇaṅku uṭi taṭa kiyar tōṭṭi ceppi
  • {PATI 62-12} paṇintu tiṟi tarupa niṉ pakivar āyiṉ
  • {PATI 62-13} pul uṭi viyaṉ pulam pal ā parappi
  • {PATI 62-14} vaḷaṉ uṭi ceṟuviṉ viḷintavi utirnta
  • {PATI 62-15} kaḷaṉ aṟu kuppi kāñci cērtti
  • {PATI 62-16} ariyal ārki vaṉ ki viṉinar
  • {PATI 62-17} aruvi āmpal malinta ceṉṉiyar
  • {PATI 62-18} āṭu ciṟi vari vaṇṭu ōppum
  • {PATI 62-19} pāṭal cāṉṟa avar akal tali nāṭu ē

{{Metrical text}}

  1. neṭuvāṉ miṉṉik kuṟuntuḷit taliip
  2. paṭumaḻi poḻinta pakuvāyk kuṉṟat
  3. tuḻimā ṉampiṇi tīṇṭali ṉiḻimakaḷ
  4. poṉcey kāci ṉoṇpaḻan tāaṅ
  5. kumiḻtali mayaṅkiya kuṟumpa latta ((5))
  6. memmoṭu varutiyō pomma lōtiyeṉak
  7. kūṟiṉṟu muṭiyarō maṟṟē vēṟupaṭ
  8. ṭirumpuli vaḻaṅkuñ cōlip
  9. peruṅkal vippiṟ curaniṟan tōrē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Didnōt he use to say[FNote_857]
  • "will you come with us, [you of] thick hir,
  • on the short many routes confused[FNote_858] with the tops of Kumiḻ trees,
  • where the fruits bright as golden coins from a jewel[-wearing] woman
  • are spreading, when the pretty doe of the male deer touches [the trees],[FNote_859]
  • on the split-mouthed hills on which coming rin has flown,
  • the long sky having flashed [and] offered small drops",
  • - the one who traversed the desert of stony regions
  • with groves where the big tiger roams, having changed [its mood][FNote_860]?

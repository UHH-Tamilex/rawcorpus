{{Metrical text}}

  1. yāṅkuc ceyvāṅko ṟōḻi yōṅkukaḻik
  2. kāmpuṭi viṭarakañ cilampap pāmpuṭaṉ
  3. ṟōṅkuvari miḷira vāṭṭi vīṅkucelaṟ
  4. kaṭuṅkura lēṟoṭu kaṉituḷi taliip
  5. peyalā ṉātē vāṉam peyaloṭu ((5))
  6. miṉṉunimirn taṉṉa vēlaṉ vanteṉap
  7. piṉṉuviṭu mucci yaḷippā ṉātē
  8. peruntaṇ kuḷavi kuḻitta pāvaṭi
  9. yiruñcē ṟāṭiya nutala kolkaḷiṟu
  10. pēti yāciṉi yocitta ((10))
  11. vītā vēṅkiya malikiḻa vōṟkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What can we do, friend?
  • Offering ripe drops with harsh-voiced [thunder-]strokes
  • ... ? so that the high mountins turn [and]
  • upset snakes, while the bamboo, shafts snapping, resound inside caves,
  • rining [is] unending. With the sky rining,
  • since the [priest] came, spear extended like a flashing,
  • care for [my] tuft, which lets go the brids, [is] unending.
  • - about the lord from the mountin of flower-spreading Vēṅki trees,
  • where the deadly elephant bull with broad feet that mashed
  • the big cool wild jasmine [and] a forehead that played in the dark mud
  • has shaken(?)[FNote_200] the innocent(?)[FNote_201] breadfruit tree.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In the land where he has gone,
  2. where the bud-opening sky flowers of the big-leaved Pakaṉṟi
  3. emit [their] smell(?)[FNote_1339] like sweet, strong toddy,
  4. resembling the big twists [of cloth][FNote_1340]: dipped in the paste [and] rised,
  5. beaten[FNote_1341] (on stones) with the end [and] put in the cool tank
  6. unseparated from the water, by the washerwoman fit for goodness(?)[FNote_1342],
  7. aren't there, friend, sorrowful evenings and loneliness?

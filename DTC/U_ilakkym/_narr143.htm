{{Metrical text}}

  1. aitē kamma yāṉē yoyyeṉat
  2. tarumaṇaṉ ñemiriya tirunakar muṟṟat
  3. tōrai yāyamu nocciyuṅ kāṇṭoṟum
  4. nīrvār kaṇṇeṉ kaliḻu meṉṉiṉuṅ
  5. kiḷḷaiyuṅ kiḷaiyeṉak kūu miḷaiyōḷ ((5))
  6. vaḻuvila ḷamma tāṉē kuḻīi
  7. yampaṉ mūtū ralarvāyp peṇṭi
  8. riṉṉā viṉṉurai kēṭṭa ciṉṉā
  9. ḷaṟiyēṉ pōla vuyirē
  10. ṉaṟiya nāṟuniṉ katuppeṉ ṟēṉē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Beautiful, go, alas, [is] it. I, oy[FNote_454],
  • even more than me who weeps, I with eyes overflowing with water
  • whenever I see the Nocci tree and the playmates
  • in the courtyard of the brilliant mansion, spread with sea-given sand,
  • she herself is without fault, alas,
  • the young one who crows intimately(?)[FNote_455] even to the parakeet
  • - I who said "fragrant is your fragrant hair"[FNote_456],
  • I who did not sigh, as if I didn't know,
  • for the few days that I heard the unpleasant pleasing talk
  • of gossip-mouthed women in the old village [driven by] calumny, crowding together.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When he sees the cool fields in the face of the rainy season,
  2. where the day[-old] calf[FNote_1159] of the doe rejects duty(?)[FNote_1160] seizing
  3. one leaf in the dark of the rainy season from the afflicted seedling
  4. swollen on the red height on the reddening millet,
  5. does our man say [to himself?]: 'the bangles on [her] hand don't slip off
  6. like the pretty-tubed new blossoms of the white Kūtāḷam tree,
  7. opened like one with the slopes glistening with water,
  8. are loosened from [their] stems[FNote_1161] and shed'?

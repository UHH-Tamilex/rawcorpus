{PUṞAM 263}

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PU 263-1} perum kaḷiṟṟu aṭiyiṉ tōṉṟum oru kaṇ
  • {PU 263-2} irum paṟai iravala cēṟi āyiṉ
  • {PU 263-3} toḻātaṉai kaḻital ōmpumati vaḻātu
  • {PU 263-4} vaṇṭu mēmpaṭūum i vaṟam nilai āṟu ē
  • {PU 263-5} pal ā tiraḷ nirai peyartara peyartantu
  • {PU 263-6} kallā iḷaiyar nīṅka nīṅkāṉ
  • {PU 263-7} vil umiḻ kaṭum kaṇai mūḻka
  • {PU 263-8} kol puṉal ciṟaiyiṉ vilaṅkiyōṉ kal ē

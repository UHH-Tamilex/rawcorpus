Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 45-1} polam pūm tumpi poṟi kiḷar tūṇi
  • {PATI 45-2} puṟṟu aṭaṅku araviṉ oṭuṅkiya ampiṉ
  • {PATI 45-3} ocivu uṭi villiṉ ociyā neñciṉ
  • {PATI 45-4} kaḷiṟu eṟintu murinta katuvāy eḵkiṉ
  • {PATI 45-5} viḻumiyōr tuvaṉṟiya akal kaṇ nāṭpiṉ
  • {PATI 45-6} eḻu muṭi mārpiṉ eytiya cēral
  • {PATI 45-7} kuṇṭu kaṇ akaḻiya matil pala kaṭantu
  • {PATI 45-8} paṇṭu um paṇṭu um tām uḷ aḻittu uṇṭa
  • {PATI 45-9} nāṭu keḻu tāyattu naṉam tali aruppattu
  • {PATI 45-10} katavam kākkum kaṇi eḻu aṉṉa
  • {PATI 45-11} nilam peṟu tiṇi tōḷ uyara ōcci
  • {PATI 45-12} piṇam piṟaṅku aḻuvattu tuṇaṅki āṭi
  • {PATI 45-13} cōṟu vēṟu eṉṉā ūṉ tuvi aṭicil
  • {PATI 45-14} ōṭā pīṭar uḷ vaḻi iṟuttu
  • {PATI 45-15} muḷ iṭupu aṟiyā ēṇi tevvar
  • {PATI 45-16} cili vici aṭakkiya mūri veḷ tōl
  • {PATI 45-17} aṉiya paṇpiṉ tāṉi maṉṉar
  • {PATI 45-18} iṉi yār uḷar ō muṉ um illi
  • {PATI 45-19} maḻi koḷa kuṟiyātu puṉal puka niṟiyātu
  • {PATI 45-20} vilaṅku vaḷi kaṭavum tuḷaṅku irum kamam cūl
  • {PATI 45-21} vayaṅku maṇi imippiṉ vēl iṭupu
  • {PATI 45-22} muḻaṅku tiri paṉi kaṭal maṟutticiṉōr ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Won't he remember, friend,
  2. he who has traversed[FNote_87] the wilderness of fine-trunked Kaḷḷi trees
  3. where the red-legged gecko is calling its mate
  4. with a sound like that of robbers turning(?)[FNote_88] between the tips of [their] nils
  5. their arrows adorned with gold(?)[FNote_89] to get them ready?

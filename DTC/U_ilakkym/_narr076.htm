{{Metrical text}}

  1. varumaḻai karanta vāṉiṟa vicumpi
  2. ṉuṇṭuḷi māṟiya vulavai yaṅkāṭ
  3. ṭāla nīḻa lacaivu nīkki
  4. yañcuvaḻi yañcā tacaivaḻi yacaii
  5. varuntā tēkumati vāliḻaik kuṟumaka ((5))
  6. ḷimmeṉ pēralar nummūrp puṉṉai
  7. vīmala rutirnta tēṉāṟu pulaviṟ
  8. kāṉa lārmaṇaṉ marīik
  9. kalluṟac civantaniṉ mellaṭi yuyaṟkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Repelling the weariness in the Banyan shade in the pretty forest
  • with green twigs that have become changed with the fine drops
  • from the pure-coloured sky that is hidden by oncoming rain,
  • resting on the moving way without fear for the fearful way,
  • go without suffering, little woman with pure jewels,
  • so that your soft feet reddened by the stone may escape,
  • joining together in the full sand of the seashore grove
  • with [its] smell of fish, fragrant with the honey dropped from the many blossoms(?)[FNote_275] of the Puṉṉai trees by your village of resounding great gossip.

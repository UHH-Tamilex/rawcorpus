{{Readable Translation}}

  1. I looked, friend, at the hill
  2. of his land with slopes from which flows[FNote_1005] falling rain,
  3. so that the white-faced black monkey with [its] young one shivers
  4. in the forest, where trees rustle [and] peacocks call out in groups.
  5. [Was] it like that before? Look, [my] forehead.

Altern: T.V.G.

5b {--!}[Is] it [again] such as it [was] before? Look, [my] forehead.[FNote_1006]

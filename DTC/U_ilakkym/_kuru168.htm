{{Readable Translation}}

  1. We can neither depart from nor unite with [her]:
  2. bamboo shoulder with supple joints, like a raft on the flood[FNote_672],
  3. good dark body - fragrant [and] cool she [is],
  4. like the water-dripping rich buds of the large-flowered jasmine in the shower,
  5. bundled - many together - in a green basket of dark Palmyra leaves
  6. [and] spread again[FNote_673] in the dusk with great raining.
  7. And moreover(?)[FNote_674] we cannot live when separated.[FNote_675]

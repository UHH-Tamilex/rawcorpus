{{Metrical text}}

  1. viḻavumuḻan taṉṟu muḻavun tūṅkiṉ
  2. ṟevaṉkuṟit taṉaḷko leṉṟi yāyiṟ
  3. ṟaḻiyaṇin talamvaru malkuṟ ṟeruvi
  4. ṉiḷiyō ḷiṟanta vaṉittaṟkup paḻaviṟa
  5. lōrik koṉṟa voruperun teruviṟ ((5))
  6. kāri pukka nērār pulampōṟ
  7. kalleṉ ṟaṉṟā lūrē yataṟkoṇṭu
  8. kāval ceṟiya māṭṭi yāytoṭi
  9. yeḻiṉmā mēṉi makaḷir
  10. viḻumān taṉartaṅ koḻunarik kāttē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The festival is gone through and the drums are hung up.
  • If you should ask "what did she intend?",
  • the village really was in uproar becuse of the like[FNote_936],
  • as the field of the enemies entered by Kāri,
  • on the one big street where he killed Ōri of old victory,
  • when the youthful one traversed the street,
  • with [her] hips which swung, being adorned with foliage.
  • Taking in that the women with graceful dark bodies
  • [and] choice armlets fastened tightly the guard
  • [and] were excellent in guarding their husbands.

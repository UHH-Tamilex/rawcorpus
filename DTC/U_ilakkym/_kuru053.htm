{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The oaths made in front of(?)[FNote_225] dreadful celestial women(?),
  2. plagued us, delightful man,
  3. when you had grasped the tender-jointed forearm
  4. at the wide ghat of our village close to the dunes
  5. with white sand, on which hang down the flowers of the Puṉku with ripe buds
  6. in the front-yard, like bright corns together with red rice[FNote_226], strewn
  7. in all grounds where [they] dance the Veṟi[FNote_227], practised by Murukaṉ's priest.

3b: in all grounds where [they] dance the Veṟi, adorned by Murukaṉ's priest.[FNote_228]

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 14-1} nilam nīr vaḷi vicumpu eṉṟa nāṉkiṉ
  • {PATI 14-2} aḷappu ariyi ē
  • {PATI 14-3} nāḷ kōḷ tiṅkaḷ ñāyiṟu kaṉi aḻal
  • {PATI 14-4} aintu oruṅku puṇarnta viḷakkattu aṉiyi
  • {PATI 14-5} pōr talimikutta īr aimpatiṉmaroṭu
  • {PATI 14-6} tuppu tuṟipōkiya tuṇivu uṭi āṇmi
  • {PATI 14-7} akkuraṉ aṉiya kivaṇmiyi ē
  • {PATI 14-8} amar kaṭantu malinta tumpi pakivar
  • {PATI 14-9} pōr pīṭu aḻitta ceru pukal muṉpa
  • {PATI 14-10} kūṟṟu vekuṇṭu variṉum māṟṟum āṟṟali ē
  • {PATI 14-11} eḻu muṭi keḻīiya tiru ñemar akalattu
  • {PATI 14-12} nōṉ puri taṭa ki cāṉṟōr meymmaṟi
  • {PATI 14-13} vāṉ uṟi makaḷir nalaṉ ikal koḷḷum
  • {PATI 14-14} vayaṅku iḻi karanta vaṇṭu paṭu katuppiṉ
  • {PATI 14-15} oṭuṅku īr ōti koṭum kuḻi kaṇava
  • {PATI 14-16} pal kaḷiṟṟu toḻutiyoṭu vel koṭi nuṭaṅkum
  • {PATI 14-17} paṭi ēr uḻava pāṭiṉi vēntu ē
  • {PATI 14-18} ilaṅku maṇi miṭinta polam kala tikiri
  • {PATI 14-19} kaṭal aka varippiṉ i poḻil muḻutu āṇṭa niṉ
  • {PATI 14-20} muṉ tiṇi mutalvar pōla niṉṟu nī
  • {PATI 14-21} keṭāa nal ici nilii
  • {PATI 14-22} tavāaliyar ō i ulakamōṭu uṭaṉ ē

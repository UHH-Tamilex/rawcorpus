Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 14-1} nilam nīr vaḷi vicumpu eṉṟa nāṉkiṉ
  • {PATI 14-2} aḷappu ariyai ē
  • {PATI 14-3} nāḷ kōḷ tiṅkaḷ ñāyiṟu kaṉai aḻal
  • {PATI 14-4} aintu oruṅku puṇarnta viḷakkattu aṉaiyai
  • {PATI 14-5} pōr talaimikutta īr aimpatiṉmaroṭu
  • {PATI 14-6} tuppu tuṟaipōkiya tuṇivu uṭai āṇmai
  • {PATI 14-7} akkuraṉ aṉaiya kaivaṇmaiyai ē
  • {PATI 14-8} amar kaṭantu malainta tumpai pakaivar
  • {PATI 14-9} pōr pīṭu aḻitta ceru pukal muṉpa
  • {PATI 14-10} kūṟṟu vekuṇṭu variṉum māṟṟum āṟṟalai ē
  • {PATI 14-11} eḻu muṭi keḻīiya tiru ñemar akalattu
  • {PATI 14-12} nōṉ puri taṭa kai cāṉṟōr meymmaṟai
  • {PATI 14-13} vāṉ uṟai makaḷir nalaṉ ikal koḷḷum
  • {PATI 14-14} vayaṅku iḻai karanta vaṇṭu paṭu katuppiṉ
  • {PATI 14-15} oṭuṅku īr ōti koṭum kuḻai kaṇava
  • {PATI 14-16} pal kaḷiṟṟu toḻutiyoṭu vel koṭi nuṭaṅkum
  • {PATI 14-17} paṭai ēr uḻava pāṭiṉi vēntu ē
  • {PATI 14-18} ilaṅku maṇi miṭainta polam kala tikiri
  • {PATI 14-19} kaṭal aka varaippiṉ i poḻil muḻutu āṇṭa niṉ
  • {PATI 14-20} muṉ tiṇai mutalvar pōla niṉṟu nī
  • {PATI 14-21} keṭāa nal icai nilaii
  • {PATI 14-22} tavāaliyar ō i ulakamōṭu uṭaṉ ē

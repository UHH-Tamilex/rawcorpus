Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 55-1} āṉṟōḷ kaṇava cāṉṟōr puravala
  • {PATI 55-2} niṉ nayantu vantaṉeṉ aṭu pōr koṟṟava
  • {PATI 55-3} iṉ icai puṇari iraṅkum pauvattu
  • {PATI 55-4} nal kala veṟukkai tuñcum pantar
  • {PATI 55-5} kamaḻum tāḻai kāṉal am perum tuṟai
  • {PATI 55-6} taṇ kaṭal paṭappai nal nāṭṭu poruna
  • {PATI 55-7} ce ūṉ tōṉṟā veḷ tuvai mutirai
  • {PATI 55-8} vāl ūṉ valci maḻavar meymmaṟai
  • {PATI 55-9} kuṭavar kō ē koṭi tēr aṇṇal
  • {PATI 55-10} vārār āyiṉum iravalar vēṇṭi
  • {PATI 55-11} tēriṉ tantu avarkku ār pataṉ nalkum
  • {PATI 55-12} nacai cāl vāymoḻi icai cāl tōṉṟal
  • {PATI 55-13} vēṇṭuva aḷavai uḷ yāṇṭu pala kaḻiya
  • {PATI 55-14} peytu puṟantantu poṅkal āṭi
  • {PATI 55-15} viṇṭu cērnta veḷ maḻai pōla
  • {PATI 55-16} ceṉṟāliyar ō peruma alkal um
  • {PATI 55-17} naṉam talai vēntar tār aḻintu alaṟa
  • {PATI 55-18} nīṭu varai aṭukkatta nāṭu kaikkoṇṭu
  • {PATI 55-19} porutu ciṉam taṇinta ceru pukal āṇmai
  • {PATI 55-20} tāṅkunar takaitta oḷ vāḷ
  • {PATI 55-21} ōṅkal uḷḷattu kurucil niṉ nāḷ ē

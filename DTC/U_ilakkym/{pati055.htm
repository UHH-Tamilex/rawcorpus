  • {PATI 55-1} āṉṟōḷ kaṇava cāṉṟōr puravala
  • {PATI 55-2} niṉ nayantu vantaṉeṉ aṭu pōr koṟṟava
  • {PATI 55-3} iṉ ici puṇari iraṅkum puvattu
  • {PATI 55-4} nal kala veṟukki tuñcum pantar
  • {PATI 55-5} kamaḻum tāḻi kāṉal am perum tuṟi
  • {PATI 55-6} taṇ kaṭal paṭappi nal nāṭṭu poruna
  • {PATI 55-7} ce ūṉ tōṉṟā veḷ tuvi mutiri
  • {PATI 55-8} vāl ūṉ valci maḻavar meymmaṟi
  • {PATI 55-9} kuṭavar kō ē koṭi tēr aṇṇal
  • {PATI 55-10} vārār āyiṉum iravalar vēṇṭi
  • {PATI 55-11} tēriṉ tantu avarkku ār pataṉ nalkum
  • {PATI 55-12} naci cāl vāymoḻi ici cāl tōṉṟal
  • {PATI 55-13} vēṇṭuva aḷavi uḷ yāṇṭu pala kaḻiya
  • {PATI 55-14} peytu puṟantantu poṅkal āṭi
  • {PATI 55-15} viṇṭu cērnta veḷ maḻi pōla
  • {PATI 55-16} ceṉṟāliyar ō peruma alkal um
  • {PATI 55-17} naṉam tali vēntar tār aḻintu alaṟa
  • {PATI 55-18} nīṭu vari aṭukkatta nāṭu kikkoṇṭu
  • {PATI 55-19} porutu ciṉam taṇinta ceru pukal āṇmi
  • {PATI 55-20} tāṅkunar takitta oḷ vāḷ
  • {PATI 55-21} ōṅkal uḷḷattu kurucil niṉ nāḷ ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a village, where the black-legged buffalo cow
  • that grazed the fields, disliking the dewy blossom of the lotus
  • in the pond moves forward as a warrior with [his] club
  • [and] sleeps in the white sand joined to hills in the neighbourhood,
  • you embrace [me] as if you were keen.
  • He [is] an enemy indeed, who made the garland
  • with opening buds fade that decorated for obtining goodness
  • my flourishing many tresses, as wealthy(?) Puṉalvāyil[FNote_817] Iruppi
  • of the strong one with stright spear who reduced [his] foes,
  • when the front rose. I have not completely forgotten.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Having confusion [and] being agitated becuse [her] lover departed,
  • in intimacy bound of old that sid 'we will be one',
  • [is] thus the state of this good forehead",
  • I do ask, o [you of] unendingly(?) adorning jewels, listen now
  • - at the drooping time of midday that passes by to be perturbed,
  • since I am one saying "the state
  • of the short cow tied by the head(?)[FNote_362]
  • [is] one of destruction of resolution(?), in a manger full of mire,
  • at a time abounding in darkness [and] north wind that cries out
  • 'im', with trouble that is not told.

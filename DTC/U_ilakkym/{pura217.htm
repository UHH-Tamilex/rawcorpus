  • {PU 217-3} ataṉiṉum maruṭki uṭittu ē piṟaṉ nāṭṭu
  • {PU 217-4} tōṟṟam cāṉṟa cāṉṟōṉ pōṟṟi
  • {PU 217-5} ici marapu āka naṭpu kantu āka
  • {PU 217-6} iṉiyatu ōr kāli īṅku varutal
  • {PU 217-7} varuvaṉ eṉṟa kōṉatu perumi um
  • {PU 217-8} atu paḻutu iṉṟi vantavaṉ aṟivu um
  • {PU 217-9} viyattoṟum viyattoṟum viyappu iṟantaṉṟu ē
  • {PU 217-10} ataṉāl taṉ kōl iyaṅkā tēyattu uṟiyum
  • {PU 217-11} cāṉṟōṉ neñcuṟa peṟṟa toṉṟu ici
  • {PU 217-12} aṉṉōṉi iḻanta i ulakam
  • {PU 217-13} eṉ āvatu kol aḷiyatu tāṉ ē

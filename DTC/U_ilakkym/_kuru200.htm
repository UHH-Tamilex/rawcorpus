{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He has not come, friend, even with the descending flood,
  2. coming laden with blossoms [that were] spread on the heights[FNote_802],
  3. cool [and] muddy, fragrant from the flowers on the rain-wet hills:
  4. surely he has forgotten,
  5. he who abandoned [us] after having promised(?)[FNote_803] to come before
  6. the noises of thunder would sound sweetly
  7. in the big rain in the evening of timely showers.
  8. {--!}We don't forget.

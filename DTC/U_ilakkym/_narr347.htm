{{Readable Translation}}

  • "The man from a land of great mountains, where the waterfalls descend
  • as if the drummer touched the place of the large-mouthed Taṇṇumai drum,
  • at a time that the fully pregnant dark clouds, that drew [water]
  • from the thundering sea, spread, for the vast area of the great directions
  • to be buried, driving [along] for the high mountains to be turned [upside down],
  • troubling snakes, maturing in the hills with heads that enter the sky,
  • have begun to offer dwindling drops,
  • [is] in such a state, he [is] of great loving"
  • - [these] long words of solicitors talking of [his] many glories
  • [are] pitiable as frogs in the summer.
  • Will it let go of seeing [him], friend, my integrity?[FNote_990]

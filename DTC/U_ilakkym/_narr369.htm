{{Metrical text}}

  1. cuṭarciṉan taṇintu kuṉṟañ cēra
  2. niṟipaṟi kurukiṉam vicumpukan toḻuka
  3. velli pipayak kaḻippi mulli
  4. yarumpuvā yaviḻum perumpuṉ māli
  5. yiṉṟu malarva tāyi ṉaṉṟu ((5))
  6. maṟiyēṉ vāḻi tōḻi yaṟiyēṉ
  7. ñemiyōṅ kuyarvari yimiyat tucci
  8. vāṉa miḻitarum vayaṅkuveḷ ḷaruvi
  9. kaṅkiyam pēryāṟṟuk kariyiṟan tiḻitaruñ
  10. ciṟiyaṭuṅ kaṭumpuṉa laṉṉave ((10))
  11. ṉiṟiyaruṅ kāma nīntu māṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If it blossoms even today,
  • great vin evening, when the bud mouths of the jasmine
  • open, daylight passing gently,
  • while a group of full-winged herons ascend the sky [and] pass,
  • while the glow's anger decreases [and] joins the hills,
  • I don't know well, friend, I don't know
  • a way of swimming the difficult desire that fills me,
  • as the quick flood that breaks the banks,
  • when the glowing white waterfall that descends from the sky,
  • the summit of the Himālāya, [its] mountin high [and] high with Ñemi tress,
  • descends, traversing the shores of the big Gangā river.

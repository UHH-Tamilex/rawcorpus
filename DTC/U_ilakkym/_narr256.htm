{{Readable Translation}}

  • You,
  • you have eyes that are at strife [with your ears], increasingly[FNote_811] great beauty,
  • faultless small feet worthy of song.
  • The wilderness,
  • [its] excellences were wasted, [their] distinction having been[FNote_812] loneliness,
  • with [its] fire-seized trees, the beauty of shade perished.
  • In this state we refrined from going.
  • And we have refrined from going to the forest as it becomes cool,
  • where the big-necked Irali, embraced by [its] youthful doe,
  • abides in the eye-seizing dappled shade
  • spread by the sinking branches of the hard-cored Vēlam tree,
  • at the desirable time when the riny season has done [its] work,
  • for the Piṭavu to open [its] bonds, for the sharp-tipped Kaḷavu to be fragrant.

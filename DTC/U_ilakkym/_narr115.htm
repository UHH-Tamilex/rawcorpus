{{Metrical text}}

  1. malarnta poykaip pūkkuṟ ṟaḻuṅka
  2. vayarnta vāyaṅ kaṇṇi ṉitupaṭīiya
  3. raṉṉaiyuñ ciṟitutaṇin tuyiriṉa ḷiṉṉīrt
  4. taṭaṅkal vāyilu muṇṭuci ṉīreṉa
  5. mayilaṭi yilaiya mākkura ṉocci ((5))
  6. maṉainaṭu mauvalō ṭūḻmukai yaviḻaka
  7. nāretirn taṉṟāṟ kālai kātalar
  8. tavaccēy nāṭṭā rāyiṉu mikappē
  9. raṉpiṉar vāḻi tōḻi naṉpuka
  10. ḻulappiṉṟu peṟiṉun taviralar ((10))
  11. kēṭṭici ṉallaṉō vicumpiṉ ṟakavē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "While the eyes of the attendants, plucking flowers in the pond that bloomed
  • [and] being immersed to the point of exhaustion(?), close pleasantly
  • mother is one who sighs, a little appeased, and in the opening
  • of broad stones of sweet water there is some water", [with these toughts][FNote_377]
  • the bonds were opposed indeed
  • of the opening inside of the aged buds on the big-eared Nocci tree
  • with peacock-foot leaves along with [those of] the wild jasmine in the house.
  • At [that] time[FNote_378], even if [my] lover
  • is in a very distant land, he has very great
  • love, live, friend. Even if he obtains today
  • the wasting of good praise(?!), he won't restrain.
  • Don't I hear the call of the sky today?

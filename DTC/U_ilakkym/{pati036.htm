Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 36-1} vīyā yāṇar niṉ vayiṉāṉ ē
  • {PATI 36-2} tāvātu ākum mali peṟu vayavu ē
  • {PATI 36-3} mallal uḷḷamoṭu vampu amar kaṭantu
  • {PATI 36-4} ceru miku muṉpiṉ maṟavaroṭu tali ceṉṟu
  • {PATI 36-5} paṉi taṭi puṉattiṉ ki taṭipu pala uṭaṉ
  • {PATI 36-6} yāṉi paṭṭa vāḷ mayaṅku kaṭum tār
  • {PATI 36-7} mā um mākkaḷ um paṭu piṇam uṇīiyar
  • {PATI 36-8} poṟitta pōlum puḷḷi eruttiṉ
  • {PATI 36-9} puṉ puṟa eruvi peṭi puṇar cēval
  • {PATI 36-10} kuṭumi eḻāloṭu koṇṭu kiḻakku iḻiya
  • {PATI 36-11} nilam iḻi nivappiṉ nīḷ niri pala cumantu
  • {PATI 36-12} uru eḻu kūḷiyar uṇṭu makiḻntu āṭa
  • {PATI 36-13} kuruti ce puṉal oḻuka
  • {PATI 36-14} ceru pala ceykuvi vāḻka niṉ vaḷaṉ ē

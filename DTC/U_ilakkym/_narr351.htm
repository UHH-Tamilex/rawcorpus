{{Metrical text}}

  1. iḷamai tīrntaṉa ḷivaḷeṉa vaḷamaṉai
  2. aruṅkaṭip paṭuttaṉai yāyiṉuñ ciṟantivaḷ
  3. pacantaṉa ḷeṉpa tuṇarāy paṉṉā
  4. ḷevva neñcamoṭu teyvam pēṇi
  5. varuntal vāḻivēṇ ṭaṉṉai karuntāḷ ((5))
  6. vēṅkaiyaṅ kuvaṭṭiṭaic cāntiṟ ceyta
  7. kaḷiṟṟuttup pañcāp puliyata ḷitaṇattuc
  8. ciṟutiṉai viyaṉpuṉaṅ kāppiṟ
  9. peṟukuvaṇ maṉṉōveṉ ṟōḻitaṉ ṉalaṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if you have put [her] under heavy protection
  • in the wealthy house, saying "she has ended youth[FNote_996]",
  • you, not aware[FNote_997] of the saying that she has become very pale,
  • many days, honouring the god with a troubled heart,
  • don't suffer, live, please try(?)[FNote_998], mother.
  • If she guards the wide field of little millet
  • from the platform with the skin of a tiger without fear of the elephant bull's vigour, made of sandal wood(?!) on a Vēṅkai peak with black trunk,
  • won't my friend get [back] her integrity?

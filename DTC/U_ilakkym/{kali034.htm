  • {KALI 34-1} maṉ uyir ēmuṟa malar ñālam puravu īṉṟu
  • {KALI 34-2} pal nīrāl pāy puṉal parantu ūṭṭi iṟanta piṉ
  • {KALI 34-3} cil nīrāl aṟal vāra akal yāṟu kaviṉ peṟa
  • {KALI 34-4} muṉ oṉṟu tamakku āṟṟi muyaṉṟavar iṟuti kaṇ
  • {KALI 34-5} piṉ oṉṟu peyarttu āṟṟum pīṭu uṭaiyāḷar pōl
  • {KALI 34-6} pal malar ciṉai uka curumpu imirntu vaṇṭu ārppa
  • {KALI 34-7} iṉ amar iḷavēṉil iṟuttanta poḻutiṉāṉ
  • {KALI 34-8} viri kāñci tātu āṭi irum kuyil viḷippa um
  • {KALI 34-9} pirivu añcātavar tīmai maṟaippeṉ maṉ maṟaippa um
  • {KALI 34-10} kari poyttāṉ kīḻ irunta maram pōla kaviṉ vāṭi
  • {KALI 34-11} eri potti eṉ neñcam cuṭum āyiṉ evaṉ ceyku ō
  • {KALI 34-12} poṟai taḷar kompiṉ mēl citar iṉam iṟaikoḷa
  • {KALI 34-13} niṟai taḷarātavar tīmai maṟaippeṉ maṉ maṟaippa um
  • {KALI 34-14} muṟai taḷarnta maṉṉavaṉ kīḻ kuṭi pōla kalaṅkupu
  • {KALI 34-15} poṟai taḷarpu paṉi vārum kaṇ āyiṉ evaṉ ceyku ō
  • {KALI 34-16} taḷai aviḻ pūm ciṉai curumpu yāḻ pōla icaippa um
  • {KALI 34-17} koḷai taḷarātavar tīmai maṟaippeṉ maṉ maṟaippa um
  • {KALI 34-18} kiḷai aḻiya vāḻpavaṉ ākkam pōl pulleṉṟu
  • {KALI 34-19} vaḷai āṉā nekiḻpu ōṭum tōḷ āyiṉ evaṉ ceyku ō
  • {KALI 34-20} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 34-21} niṉ uḷ nōy nī uraittu alamaral ellā nām
  • {KALI 34-22} eṇṇiya nāḷ varai iṟavātu kātalar
  • {KALI 34-23} paṇṇiya māviṉar pukutantār
  • {KALI 34-24} kaṇ uṟu pūcal kai kaḷaintāṅku ē

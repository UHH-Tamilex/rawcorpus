  • {KALI 34-6} pal malar ciṉi uka curumpu imirntu vaṇṭu ārppa
  • {KALI 34-7} iṉ amar iḷavēṉil iṟuttanta poḻutiṉāṉ
  • {KALI 34-8} viri kāñci tātu āṭi irum kuyil viḷippa um
  • {KALI 34-9} pirivu añcātavar tīmi maṟippeṉ maṉ maṟippa um
  • {KALI 34-10} kari poyttāṉ kīḻ irunta maram pōla kaviṉ vāṭi
  • {KALI 34-11} eri potti eṉ neñcam cuṭum āyiṉ evaṉ ceyku ō
  • {KALI 34-12} poṟi taḷar kompiṉ mēl citar iṉam iṟikoḷa
  • {KALI 34-13} niṟi taḷarātavar tīmi maṟippeṉ maṉ maṟippa um
  • {KALI 34-14} muṟi taḷarnta maṉṉavaṉ kīḻ kuṭi pōla kalaṅkupu
  • {KALI 34-15} poṟi taḷarpu paṉi vārum kaṇ āyiṉ evaṉ ceyku ō
  • {KALI 34-16} taḷi aviḻ pūm ciṉi curumpu yāḻ pōla icippa um
  • {KALI 34-17} koḷi taḷarātavar tīmi maṟippeṉ maṉ maṟippa um
  • {KALI 34-18} kiḷi aḻiya vāḻpavaṉ ākkam pōl pulleṉṟu
  • {KALI 34-19} vaḷi āṉā nekiḻpu ōṭum tōḷ āyiṉ evaṉ ceyku ō
  • {KALI 34-20} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 34-21} niṉ uḷ nōy nī urittu alamaral ellā nām
  • {KALI 34-22} eṇṇiya nāḷ vari iṟavātu kātalar
  • {KALI 34-23} paṇṇiya māviṉar pukutantār
  • {KALI 34-24} kaṇ uṟu pūcal ki kaḷintāṅku ē

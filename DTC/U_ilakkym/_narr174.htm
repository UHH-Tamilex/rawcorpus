{{Metrical text}}

  1. kaṟṟi yīntiṉ muṟṟukkuli yaṉṉa
  2. vāḷi lattat tāḷam pōntik
  3. kōḷuṭi neṭuñciṉi yāṇkural viḷippiṟ
  4. puliyetir vaḻaṅkum vaḷivaḻaṅ kāriṭic
  5. ceṉṟa kātalar vantiṉitu muyaṅkip ((5))
  6. piriyā toruvaḻi yuṟiyiṉum peritaḻin
  7. tuyaṅkiṉi maṭanti yeṉṟi tōḻi
  8. yaṟṟu mākumaḵ taṟiyā tōrkkē
  9. vīḻāk koḷki vīḻnta koṇṭi
  10. pularpukaḻ mūtūrp paṉittuṟi yaṉṉa
  11. mallaṉ mārpu maṭuttaṉaṉ ((10))
  12. pullumaṟ ṟevaṉō vaṉpilaṉ kaṭiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Although [your] lover who had gone
  • the wind-blown difficult way where the tiger faces [anyone][FNote_564]
  • to the sound of the male [bird] on the clustered long branch
  • of the [long-]legged plamyra palm of a road devoid of men,
  • resembling the ripening bunches on the bundled date palm,
  • has come [and] embraces [you] sweetly,
  • not separating and staying the same way, you are greatly unsettled
  • [and] become weak, girl" you sid, friend.
  • And thus it is to those who don't know that.
  • He united [his] broad chest
  • [as the dewy ghat in an old village [full of] prise when it dawns]
  • with a harlot, lowering [his] unyielding[FNote_565] principles.
  • What of but embracing, when there is no love with us?[FNote_566]

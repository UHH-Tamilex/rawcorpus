{{Metrical text}}

  1. nīṇmalaik kalitta peruṅkōṟ kuṟiñci
  2. nāṇmalar puraiyu mēṉip peruñcuṉai
  3. malarpiṇait taṉṉa māyitaḻ maḻaikkaṇ
  4. mayilō raṉṉa cāyaṟ centārk
  5. kiḷiyō raṉṉa kiḷavip paṇaittōṭ ((5))
  6. pāvai yaṉṉa vaṉappiṉa ḷivaḷeṉak
  7. kāmar neñcamoṭu palapā rāṭṭi
  8. yāymaṟap paṟiyā maṭantai
  9. tēmaṟap paṟiyāk kamaḻkūn talaḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • [This] girl whom mother never[FNote_908] forgets,
  • praising [her] in many [words] with a desirous heart:
  • "a body as the day blossom
  • of the large-stemmed Kuṟiñci swollen on the long mountain,
  • rain eyes with choice petals like linked blossoms
  • from the big mountain-pool, grace just like a peacock,
  • words just like a parrot with red neck-stripe, bamboo shoulders
  • - she [is] pretty as an image."
  • she has fragrant tresses never without [the scent of] honey.

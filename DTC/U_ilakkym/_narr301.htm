{{Metrical text}}

  1. nīṇmalik kalitta peruṅkōṟ kuṟiñci
  2. nāṇmalar puriyu mēṉip peruñcuṉi
  3. malarpiṇit taṉṉa māyitaḻ maḻikkaṇ
  4. mayilō raṉṉa cāyaṟ centārk
  5. kiḷiyō raṉṉa kiḷavip paṇittōṭ ((5))
  6. pāvi yaṉṉa vaṉappiṉa ḷivaḷeṉak
  7. kāmar neñcamoṭu palapā rāṭṭi
  8. yāymaṟap paṟiyā maṭanti
  9. tēmaṟap paṟiyāk kamaḻkūn talaḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • [This] girl whom mother never[FNote_908] forgets,
  • prising [her] in many [words] with a desirous heart:
  • "a body as the day blossom
  • of the large-stemmed Kuṟiñci swollen on the long mountin,
  • rin eyes with choice petals like linked blossoms
  • from the big mountin-pool, grace just like a peacock,
  • words just like a parrot with red neck-stripe, bamboo shoulders
  • - she [is] pretty as an image."
  • she has fragrant tresses never without [the scent of] honey.

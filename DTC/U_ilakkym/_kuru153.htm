{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When the owl shrieks from the hills and in the front-yard
  2. the monkey jumps to and fro[FNote_613] on the jackfruit tree branches
  3. fearful indeed [and] pitiable [is] my heart now[FNote_614]:
  4. in difficult darkness, at night for him
  5. on the slope [it is] a long way unending to go.

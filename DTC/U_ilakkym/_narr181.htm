{{Readable Translation}}

  • So that the cool corn is cut off by the horses adorned with garlands
  • [of bells] that have lost [their] pleasing sound because it was called to a halt
  • by mad evening, come[FNote_588] to end [all] action,
  • when the female bird,
  • having watched the occasion of the coming
  • of the black-chinned cock of the birds staying inside [the house],
  • after he had abided in the field, staying with a companion from other fields,
  • after chasing [him] away from the nest together with [her] small many young ones, as nectarous minosa flowers by the path,
  • took mery on the state of the feathers of him who was nearby, [his] back
  • wetted from the spray, thought for a long time
  • and calls out to him with a merciful(?) heart,
  • - the big victorious chariot has come.
  • The beauty of her choice is one that has survived.

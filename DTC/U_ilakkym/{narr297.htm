Naṟṟiṇai

((Originally extracted from master file ^NARRI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {NT 297-1} poṉ cey vaḷḷattu pāl kiḻakku iruppa
  • {NT 297-2} niṉ oḷi eṟiya cē aṭi otuṅkāy
  • {NT 297-3} pal māṇ cēkkai pakai koḷa niṉaii
  • {NT 297-4} makiḻā nōkkam makiḻntaṉai pōṉṟaṉai
  • {NT 297-5} evaṉ kol eṉṟu niṉaikkal um niṉaittilai
  • {NT 297-6} niṉ uḷ tōṉṟum kuṟippu naṉi peritu ē
  • {NT 297-7} citar naṉai muṇaiiya citar kāl vāraṇam
  • {NT 297-8} mutir kaṟi yāppiṉ tuñcum nāṭaṉ
  • {NT 297-9} mella vantu nal akam peṟṟamai
  • {NT 297-10} maiyal uṟukuvaḷ aṉṉai
  • {NT 297-11} aiyam iṉṟi kaṭum kavaviṉaḷ ē

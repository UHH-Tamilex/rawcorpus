{{Readable Translation}}

  1. 1.
  2. 'Waterfall, Vēṅki on the big mountin, for the man from there,
  3. what can I do?' That
  4. [was] a joke, if I don't realise so,
  5. what will become of you, you with good forehead?
  6. 2.
  7. 'Waterfall, Vēṅki on the big mountin -
  8. what can I do for the man from that place?'
  9. If I don't realise that was a joke,
  10. will you be mine, you of good forehead?

Remarks

Or, as the kiḷavi affirms, SHE is talking, but then HE would be of good forehead:[FNote_396]

Alternate translation

  • As I sid: 'what can I do
  • for the man from the big mountin with Vēṅki [and] waterfalls?'
  • - if I don't mean that to be a joke,

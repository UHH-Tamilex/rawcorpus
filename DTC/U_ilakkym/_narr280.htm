{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Are you not vexed", you said, friend, "about the going astray, unpreventable(?), of the man from the village by the cool ghat,
  • where the sweet fruits left to drop by the mango tree fall with
  • a splash(?) into the depth of water on which are suspended
  • white water-lilies with buds as the closed[-wings] state of a heron?"
  • I am vexed,
  • [it is just] because I donōt get [him], for there [is] no leisure,
  • while my good house is well immersed in festival,
  • as the hill village of the venerable[FNote_871] Vēḷir,
  • where the field guards are breaking roasted snails
  • on the fortified backs of the pond tortoises.

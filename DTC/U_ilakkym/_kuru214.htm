{{Readable Translation}}

  1. This noisy village has fallen into confusion
  2. [and] adorns [itself] with chaplets of bowstring hemp
  3. from the neighbourhood, while the whole Aśoka stump stays behind
  4. which contributed [its] plentiful foliage for the hips, as a perfect decoration,[FNote_859]
  5. of the creeper woman with swinging gait(?)[FNote_860] [and] pretty, thin hair,
  6. hanging down [her] back, who guards the bright-eared millet,
  7. sown after the field was hoed[FNote_861] by the tree-felling man from the wood.

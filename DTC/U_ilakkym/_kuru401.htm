{{Readable Translation}}

  1. One day with the man from the ghat, where moist crabs
  2. are running to the sea for fear of women in the Ōrai game,
  3. with tresses overflowing from water, covered by long wreaths
  4. of blue water-lily, smelling from choice Aṭumpu blossoms,
  5. has chased away laughing [and] playing.[FNote_1597]
  6. A beautiful thing, alas[FNote_1598], [is] the intimacy in which [he] touches[FNote_1599] [my] body.

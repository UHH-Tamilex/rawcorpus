{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Who troubled you, ocean?[FNote_650]
  2. We hear your voice all through the deep night,
  3. when waves slap[FNote_651] the white-blossomed Tāḻai trees
  4. in the big ghat of seashore groves with fish-eating herons,
  5. spread like a flock of small-headed goats
  6. of the Pūḻiyar.

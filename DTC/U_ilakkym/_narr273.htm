{{Metrical text}}

  1. iḵtevaṉ kollō tōḻi meyparan
  2. tevvaṅ kūrnta vēmuṟu tuyaram
  3. vemmaiyiṟ ṟāṉvarut tuṟīi namvayi
  4. ṉaṟiyā tayarnta vaṉṉaikku veṟiyeṉa
  5. vēla ṉuraikku meṉpava rākaliṉ ((5))
  6. vaṇṇa mikutta vaṇṇal yāṉai
  7. nīrko ṇeṭuñcuṉai yamaintuvārn tuṟainteṉ
  8. kaṇpō ṉīlan taṇkamaḻ ciṟakkuṅ
  9. kuṉṟa nāṭaṉai yuḷḷutoṟu
  10. neñcunaṭuk kuṟūumavar paṇputaru paṭarē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What [is] this, friend,
  • [that] affliction his nature gives [us], which makes the heart tremble
  • whenever it remembers the man from a land of hills,
  • where the blue [lotus], resembling my eyes, is superiorly cool [and] fragrant,
  • the long mountain spring being in a quiet state of overflowing(?)[FNote_855] as the superior elephant whose colour increased[FNote_856] takes water,
  • since they say the Vēlaṉ will talk
  • of the Veṟi dance to mother who is [still] held in ignorance
  • as far as we are concerned, he having made us suffer the heat
  • of confused misery abounding in trouble, [pallor] spreading on [our] body?

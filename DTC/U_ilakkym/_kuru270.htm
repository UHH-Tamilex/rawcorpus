{{Readable Translation}}

  1. May you live, great sky, now that you've rined
  2. under rumbling thunder[FNote_1094] like a drum beaten with the stick
  3. at one turn(?)[FNote_1095] pouring out the descending, dripping sweetness
  4. which means coolness, lightning so that the hanging dark is severed.
  5. With a satisfied(?) mind that, since we wished for her,
  6. has completed the work to be done,
  7. we [are now] on a soft bed with [her who has] fragrant, soft tresses[FNote_1096]
  8. smelling of the short-stemmed day blossom of the water-lily.

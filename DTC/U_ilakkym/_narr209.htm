{{Metrical text}}

  1. malaiyiṭam paṭuttuk kōṭṭiya kollait
  2. taḷipatam peṟṟa kāṉuḻu kuṟavar
  3. cilavit takala viṭṭuṭaṉ palaviḷain
  4. tiṟaṅkukural piṟaṅkiya vēṉa luḷḷāṇ
  5. maḻalaiyaṅ kuṟumakaṇ miḻalaiyan tīṅkural ((5))
  6. kiḷiyun tāmaṟi pavvē yemakkē
  7. paṭuṅkāṟ paiyu ṭīrum paṭātu
  8. taviruṅ kālai yāyiṉeṉ
  9. ṉuyirō ṭellā muṭaṉvāṅ kummē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • She who doesn't remember the millet that glistened, ears coming down,
  • ripening many together, after few seeds had been let go off widely
  • by the forest-plouging hill people when the woodland had obtained the right
  • state of wetness, brought under control [and] fenced(?)[FNote_664] in the mountain,
  • - the parakeets themselves know
  • the prattling sweet voice of the prattling little woman. To us,
  • the time it comes [to us] sorrow ends,
  • if there is a time it refrains from coming,
  • along with my life it will take away all.

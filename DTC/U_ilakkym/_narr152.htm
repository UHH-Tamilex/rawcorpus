{{Metrical text}}

  1. maṭalē kāman tanta talarē
  2. miṭipū verukki ṉalartan taṉṟē
  3. yilaṅkukatir maḻuṅki yelvicumpu paṭarap
  4. pulamputan taṉṟē pukaṉṟucey maṇṭila
  5. mellām tantataṉ ṟaliyum piyeṉa ((5))
  6. vaṭanti tuvali tūvak kuṭampip
  7. peṭiyuṇa raṉṟi liyaṅkukura laḷiik
  8. kaṅkuluṅ kiyaṟa vantaṉṟi
  9. yāṅkā kuveṉko laḷiyeṉ yāṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Desire has given [me] the palmyra horse. The blossom
  • of Yarcum in close-set flowers has given [me] gossip.
  • The desired(?)[FNote_488] [sun]-disk has given [me] loneliness,
  • the shining beams having become dim while the light pervades in the sky,
  • Above all that has been given by all [of these]
  • night too has come to stop [all] doing,
  • the stirring voice of the Aṉṟil mingling in that has perceived [its] female
  • in the nest, while the northern wind spatters spray, gently.
  • What will become of me, pitiable I?

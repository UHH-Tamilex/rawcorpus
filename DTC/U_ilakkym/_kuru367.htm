{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if he [is] a cruel and ungranting[FNote_1462] one,
  2. so that your shoulders with joints where armlets are shining obtain beauty,
  3. come there[FNote_1463] [and] see, friend, rejoicing[FNote_1464]:
  4. the mountain heightened[FNote_1465] by thick stones, cool [and] fragrant,
  5. where the fighting girl companions in the merrily[FNote_1466] falling waterfall
  6. appear like jewels taking a bath[FNote_1467],
  7. in his land, at the beginning of the muddy(?)[FNote_1468] great rain.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Live, north wind, beware
  2. of the village with grass-thatched huts of the good[FNote_962] woman
  3. [and] front-yards, where groups of wild cows feed
  4. on Nelli [fruits]. It lies in stony height
  5. by the pure white waterfall, resembling the hanging slough of a snake.

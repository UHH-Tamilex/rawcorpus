KT 121 Kapilar: SHE

{{kiḷavi}}: (Spoken by HER(t.) to the friend, who made her realise that he would come on another day, [that is,] when she had got confused becuse she didn't see him there [where she had awaited him]: she had gone there, becuse another sign had been given which had been similar to that which he used to give when coming to the night tryst, so that she had mistaken the similarity for the real sign.){{/kiḷavi}}

  1. [Is it] truth[FNote_486], oh friend?
  2. Becuse of the mistake that he didn't reach the sign,[FNote_487]
  3. the man from a mountin land, like the male langur on the slope,
  4. black in the face as if full of collyrium[FNote_488], who went down with a branch
  5. that received [him][FNote_489], [becuse if] the mistake of not jumping [one] that bears [him],
  6. they have become pale, my full soft shoulders.

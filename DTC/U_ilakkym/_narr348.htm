{{Readable Translation}}

  • The moonlight!
  • Spreading [its] many rays in the blue-coloured sky
  • it has expanded[FNote_992] as the great milk ocean.
  • The village!
  • crowding, gathering happily, with the burden of noise increasing(?),
  • immersing in festival in the bustling streets.
  • The forest!
  • Inside every grove dense with flowers blossoming
  • bees are humming with companions they desire.
  • I!
  • With the affliction brought by loneliness that made lose [my] decorative jewels
  • even in the full dark of night I do not sleep.
  • Therefore, does this world make wore on me?
  • Does my afflicted heart make war on this world?

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Penance have they done, indeed, friend,
  2. who see the long[FNote_1381]-separated lover coming,
  3. having gone in the fetter of wealth difficult to obtin,
  4. at sorrowful evening, when those with dewlaps[FNote_1382] hanging to the ground,
  5. traversing the row[FNote_1383] in remembrance of the calf, milk flowing over
  6. from udders with swelling teats, return to the village
  7. with [their] superior bull to eat of the corn from the fields,
  8. in the month of heavy dew with drops spreading 'coolness'.

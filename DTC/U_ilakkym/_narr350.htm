{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if my old beauty is lost,
  • like Iruppai of generous Virāṉ [who has many?] chariots,
  • where the [flower-]bunches hanging on the Marutam trees with bent branches
  • drop off by the fields, when many birds retreat to the pond,
  • frightened by the Taṇṇumai drum of the cutters of white paddy,
  • let it be lost.
  • I will not let [you] come near. If I let you,
  • you have a garland that is faded, you have sandal-paste spoiled(?)
  • by strong plump breasts that endure [your] hands in copulation, urgently(?).
  • It [is] as[FNote_995] removing an unfit pot.
  • Don't come, live, with your shoulders having embraced [somone else].

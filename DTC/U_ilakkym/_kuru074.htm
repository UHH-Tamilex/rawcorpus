{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The man from the hills of green bamboo that touches the sky,
  2. as if a horse set free leaping up,
  3. he didn't know we were thinking of him[FNote_302]
  4. and like a bull in the summer heat
  5. he is emaciated, they say[FNote_303], longing for our precious[FNote_304] goodness.

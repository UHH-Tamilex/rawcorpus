{{colophon}}: itu variviṭi vittup piriviṉkaṇ tōḻi (G1+2: -kaṭ ṭōḻi; ET: piriyak karutum talimakaṟkut tōḻi) colliyatu.{{/colophon}}

{{Metrical text}}

  1. piṇaṅkaril vāṭiya paḻaviṟa ṉaṉantali
  2. yuṇaṅkū ṇāyat tōrāṉ ṟeṇmaṇi
  3. piya vicikku mattam viyeyiṟ
  4. ṟivaḷoṭuñ celiṉō naṉṟē kuvaḷi
  5. nīrcūḻ māmala raṉṉa kaṇṇaḻak ((5))
  6. kaliyoḻi piṇiyiṟ kalaṅki māṟi
  7. yaṉpili rakaṟi rāyi ṉeṉpara
  8. mākuva taṉṟiva ḷavala nākat
  9. taṇaṅkuṭi yaruntali yuṭali valaṉēr
  10. pārkali nallēṟu tiritaruṅ ((10))
  11. kārcey māli varūum pōḻtē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • It [would be] good if you go with her
  • of sharp teeth on the road, where the clear bell
  • of the one cow of the attendant(?)[FNote_156] gently sounds, [eating] dry food
  • in the vast area of the old victory(?)[FNote_157] where the intertwined thicket has faded.
  • If you are devoid of love [and] leave [her],
  • changing as the doe stirs while the stag stays behind(?)[FNote_158],
  • it won`t be in our capacity [to hinder you],
  • - in a time[FNote_159] when evening comes, making(?) the riny season,
  • when a loudly clamouring good [thunder]clap, risen with strength,
  • attacks the difficult, plagued head of a snake [and] twists [it].

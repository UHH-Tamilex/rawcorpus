{{Metrical text}}

  1. paṭucuṭa raṭinta pakuvāy neṭuvari
  2. murampucēr ciṟukuṭip paranta mālip
  3. pulampukūṭ ṭuṇṇum pulleṉ maṉṟattuk
  4. kalluṭip paṭuviṟ kaluḻi tantu
  5. niṟipeya laṟiyāk kuṟikkū ṇalliṟ ((5))
  6. ṟuvarcey yāṭic centoṭi maṟava ratarpārt talku mañcuvaru neṟiyiṭi
  7. yiṟappa veṇṇuva ravareṉiṉ maṟuttal
  8. valluvaṅ kollō melliya ṉāmeṉa
  9. vimmuṟu kiḷaviya ḷeṉmuka nōkki ((10))
  10. nallaka vaṉamulik karicērpu
  11. malkupuṉal paranta malarēr kaṇṇē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • An increasing flood spread[FNote_141] from [her] blossom-like eyes,
  • joining the shore of [her] beautiful breasts with good inside(?)[FNote_142],
  • as [she] looked into my face, she of heavy words:
  • 'if he says he considers to traverse
  • all the pathes(?)[FNote_143], where fear of staying arises at the sight of the track(?)
  • of the bold ones with red arrows [and] dresses made with coral,
  • in [their] good houses, crowding together to [form] a small island(?)[FNote_144], without knowing of full rin,
  • muddy water being brought[FNote_145] into the cooking pit consisting of stones
  • in the village common, empty, where evening enjoys loneliness to the full,
  • spread with little homes joining the mounts of gravel
  • on the long mountin with split mouth reached by the glow of the setting [sun],
  • will we be able to refuse, [you of] soft nature?'

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Having mounted the white chariot glittering with golden weapons,
  2. so that the spray from the agitated sea wets the wheels,
  3. he has gone now, the man from the coast of set sand[FNote_822],
  4. like a goose(?) risen in flight[FNote_823] dancing in the sky,
  5. while raining clouds hang [there] in masses, making lightning.
  6. How did it know, friend,
  7. the pallor, creeping up on my honey-scented, radiant forehead?

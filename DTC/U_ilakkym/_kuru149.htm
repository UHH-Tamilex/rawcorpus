{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Pitiable[FNote_598] it [is], shame. With us
  2. it has indeed borne [it] a fairly long time. [But] now
  3. it doesn't stand ground to the hand[FNote_599], as desire crushes [it],
  4. having endured as long as it could,
  5. like the little wings of high sand near the sky-flowered sugarcane,
  6. which give way, destroyed as the sweet flood crushes them.

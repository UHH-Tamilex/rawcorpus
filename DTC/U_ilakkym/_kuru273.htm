{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O you with bright forehead smelling coolly[FNote_1112]
  2. like the moving wind which blows in the great wilderness,
  3. so that buds shimmer with pollen at night time!
  4. If you were to ache, I speak of what [I have] seen:
  5. confused is this world,
  6. like an ignorant person who mounted without knowing
  7. an old bamboo ladder on the mountain, where a great honey[comb] is visible to the eye.
  8. Let it be clear: he won't separate while we are here.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You may go, great one,
  • to obtain the soft shoulder of women that give today,
  • snatching the new integrity of those [you] united with yesterday,
  • as the dolls of the floor[FNote_1011] staying behind after the festival,
  • who danced in [various?] ways, the faces of the drums drying.
  • May your other woman be excellent.
  • I rejoice at your distress that you had
  • in abundance - as the disgusting rice-ball [the young elephant?][FNote_1012]
  • smears over [its] body from what it put in [its] trunk,
  • when those relenting after piercing with a core slap [it] -
  • being strongly ashamed that many blame [you].

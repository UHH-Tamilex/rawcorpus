{PUṞAM 45}

((Originally extracted from master file ^PURAM.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PU 45-1} irum paṉi veḷ tōṭu malintōṉ allaṉ
  • {PU 45-2} karum ciṉi vēmpiṉ teriyalōṉ allaṉ
  • {PU 45-3} niṉṉa kaṇṇi um ār miṭintaṉṟu ē niṉṉoṭu
  • {PU 45-4} poruvōṉ kaṇṇiyum ār miṭintaṉṟu ē
  • {PU 45-5} oruvīr tōṟpiṉ um tōṟpa num kuṭi ē
  • {PU 45-6} iruvīr vēṟal iyaṟki um aṉṟu ē ataṉāl
  • {PU 45-7} kuṭi poruḷ aṉṟu num ceyti koṭi tēr
  • {PU 45-8} num ōraṉṉa vēntarkku
  • {PU 45-9} meym mali uvaki ceyyum i ikal ē

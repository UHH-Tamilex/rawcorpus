{{Readable Translation}}

  • Is there someone, friend, who begged
  • the man from a land of big mountins, where
  • the sharp upright rich bud brought forth by the plantin,
  • resembling the white tusk of the elephant that had fought,
  • elated by fierce anger, with a strength close to [that of] Murukaṉ,
  • coms into swinging together with flowers as the hir of softnatured women:[FNote_714]
  • for [his] bondless chest that doesn't help those that long [for it]
  • to end the fading [and] the rising suffering,
  • while the beautiful breasts with sandal paste[FNote_715] [and] perfect jewels
  • lose the beauty of [their] streaks.

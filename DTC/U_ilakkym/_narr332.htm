  3. nāḷu nāḷuṭaṉ kalavun tōḷē
  4. toṉṉili vaḻiiyaniṉ ṟoṭiyeṉap paṉmā

  7. yirinaciip parikku malimutaṟ ciṟuneṟi
  8. talinā ḷaṉṉa pēṇalaṉ palanā

  10. kiyāṅkā kummē yilaṅkiḻi ceṟippē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Friend, friend, what is to be sid of this?
  • You leave off telling many times:
  • "[Your] shoulder that is blended together [with his] day by day,
  • as if those plucking water-lilies wanted water![FNote_959]
  • [Yet] your armlet that have slipped from the old state!"[FNote_960]
  • How can there be tightness of [my] shining jewels,
  • for me who see [him] coming by difficult darkness
  • many days, the one who does not care, like the first day,
  • about the small path at the mountin base, where the big killing strong tiger
  • runs yearning for prey, subjugated by [its] female gicing birth in a clefty cave.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You, you see [me][FNote_793] protecting you too,
  • we, who are longing for the man from the land of good mountains,
  • where the she-monkey seizes the choice unripe fruit with rich base[FNote_794]
  • of the plantain on the big stones, [its] trunk at the base fettered to
  • that place of fierce songs from the waterfall of the long water,
  • scaring [the birds] away from your ears, in very great love for him.
  • Ascend as one that does not bend in the leaves, [a time]
  • when, while the bustling is great in honouring the god who obtains offerings,
  • they will seize [your] ears immediately to the image in raw state(?),
  • so that the old beauty of my sprout-like body will perish,
  • - take a long time to ripen, may you live, millet.

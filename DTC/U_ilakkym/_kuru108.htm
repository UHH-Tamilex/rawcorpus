{{Readable Translation}}

  1. For the cows to set out[FNote_436] for the calves,
  2. from the little homes[FNote_437] where hills join on which rain is playing,
  3. in the woodland the flawless sky[-bright] flower of jasmine
  4. has taken the redness of the red sky.[FNote_438]
  5. I don't seem to live, friend.

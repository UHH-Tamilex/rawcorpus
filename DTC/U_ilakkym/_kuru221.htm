{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He?[FNote_894] He has not come, and the jasmines[FNote_895] have flowered.
  2. All[FNote_896] the son of the shepherd with [his] sheep
  3. has put[FNote_897] on his head,
  4. who has come [to the village] with milk [and] goes with gruel[FNote_898],
  5. while those with palm mats in hand[FNote_899] stay behind with the groups of lambs,
  6. [are these] little fresh buds.

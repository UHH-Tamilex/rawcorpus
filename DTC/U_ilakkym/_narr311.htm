{{Readable Translation}}

  • If it rins,
  • it [has] the yield of big-eared rice,
  • an abundance of deers let loose appearing.
  • It if gets dry,
  • when white salt ripens in the fields of the dark backwaters,
  • mus dried, Muṇṭakam[-blossoms] spread by the great water,
  • our old village of unchanging custom [is] good.
  • Pleasing [is] also the ghat with small-blossomed Ñāḻal trees,
  • the inside of streets diffused with the glowing smoke of rich fish.
  • One only, friend, [is] the blame of our seashore grove.
  • As for hearing the sweet sound of his long chariot,
  • while the bees[FNote_925] are filling themselves on great nectar,
  • eating the pollen from the blossoms of the black-branched Puṉṉi tree,
  • it [is] difficult.

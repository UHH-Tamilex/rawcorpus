Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 40-1} pōr niḻal pukaṉṟa cuṟṟamoṭu ūr mukattu
  • {PATI 40-2} iṟāaliyar ō peruma niṉ tāṉi
  • {PATI 40-3} iṉ ici imiḻ muracu iyampa kaṭippu ikūu
  • {PATI 40-4} puṇ tōḷ āṭavar pōr mukattu iṟuppa
  • {PATI 40-5} kāytta karanti mā koṭi viḷi vayal
  • {PATI 40-6} vantu iṟikoṇṭaṉṟu tāṉi antil
  • {PATI 40-7} kaḷinar yār iṉi piṟar eṉa pēṇi
  • {PATI 40-8} maṉ eyil maṟavar oli avintu aṭaṅka
  • {PATI 40-9} oṉṉār tēya pū malintu urii
  • {PATI 40-10} veḷ tōṭu niriiya vēntu uṭi arum camam
  • {PATI 40-11} koṉṟu puṟampeṟṟu maṉpati nirappi
  • {PATI 40-12} veṉṟi āṭiya toṭi tōḷ mī ki
  • {PATI 40-13} eḻu muṭi keḻīiya tiru ñemar akalattu
  • {PATI 40-14} poṉ am kaṇṇi polam tēr naṉṉaṉ
  • {PATI 40-15} cuṭar vī vāki kaṭi mutal taṭinta
  • {PATI 40-16} tār miku mintiṉ nār muṭi cēral
  • {PATI 40-17} puṉ kāl uṉṉam cāya teḷ kaṇ
  • {PATI 40-18} vaṟitu kūṭṭu ariyal iravalar taṭuppa
  • {PATI 40-19} tāṉ tara uṇṭa naṉi naṟavu makiḻntu
  • {PATI 40-20} nīr imiḻ cilampiṉ nēriyōṉ ē
  • {PATI 40-21} cellāy ō til cil vaḷi viṟali
  • {PATI 40-22} malarnta vēṅkiyiṉ vayaṅku iḻi aṇintu
  • {PATI 40-23} mel iyal makaḷir eḻil nalam ciṟappa
  • {PATI 40-24} pāṇar pim pū maliya iḷiyar
  • {PATI 40-25} iṉ kaḷi vaḻāa mel col amarntu
  • {PATI 40-26} neñcu mali uvakiyar viyaṉ kaḷam vāḻtta
  • {PATI 40-27} tōṭṭi nīvātu toṭi cērpu niṉṟu
  • {PATI 40-28} pākar ēvaliṉ oḷ poṟi picira
  • {PATI 40-29} kāṭu talikkoṇṭa nāṭu kāṇ avir cuṭar
  • {PATI 40-30} aḻal viṭupu marīiya mintiṉ
  • {PATI 40-31} toḻil pukal yāṉi nalkuvaṉ pala ē

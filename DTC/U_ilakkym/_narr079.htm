  7. veṉṟunāṅ kūṟik kāmañ cepputuñ
  8. ceppātu viṭiṉē yuyiroṭum vantaṉ

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "It is difficult when we consider the separating
  • of those that are united, to be [re-]united after the one who separated has come from the crossroads difficult to march, which had the bringing of beauty(?)[FNote_284],
  • where the dripping[FNote_285] very round blossom of the mimosa[FNote_286] closed by day(?)[FNote_287] spread on the rocky heights like the Molucca-beans spread in play in the sand
  • by women with small armlets from the good houses with curvy bark[FNote_288]",
  • [thus] we shall speak and talk of desire.
  • If we let go of speaking, it will have come [to pass] along with [our] life too.[FNote_289]
  • Alas, live, friend,
  • how do we prevent [our] lover from going?

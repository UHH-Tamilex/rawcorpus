{{colophon}}: itu variviṭivittup pirivāṟṟāḷāy (EN, ER, ET: -āya) talivi vaṟpuṟukkun tōḻi (EN, ER, ET: tōḻikkuc) colliyatu.{{/colophon}}

{{Metrical text}}

  1. tōḷē toṭikoṭ pāṉā kaṇṇē
  2. vāḷīr vaṭiyiṉ vaṭiviḻan taṉavē
  3. nutalum pacali pāyiṉṟu titalic
  4. cilpoṟi yaṇinta palkā ḻalkuṉ
  5. maṇiyē rimpāṉ māyōṭ keṉṟu ((5))
  6. vevvāyp peṇṭir kuvi tūṟṟa
  7. nāmuṟu tuyarañ ceyyala reṉṉuṅ
  8. kāmuṟu tōḻi kātalaṅ kiḷavi
  9. yirumpucey kollaṉ vevvulit teḷitta
  10. tōymaṭaṟ ciṉṉīr pōla ((10))
  11. nōymali neñciṟ kēmamāñ ciṟitē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The word full of desire [and] love[FNote_424], friend,
  • that says "he does not cuse the misery we have",
  • so that hot-mouthed women spread scandal
  • "The shoulders! Unending instability of the armlets. The eyes!
  • They have lost [their] shape of green mangos cut by a sword[FNote_425].
  • Even[FNote_426] the forehead has become pallor
  • on the dark one[FNote_427] with jewel-like five parts [of hir],
  • [her] hips with many pearl-strings adorned with few spots of beauty."
  • - [it is] small as a protection for the heart abounding in pin,
  • as a little water touches the earth[FNote_428],
  • sprinkled on the hot furnace of the iron working smith.

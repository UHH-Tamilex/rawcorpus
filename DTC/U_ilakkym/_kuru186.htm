{{Readable Translation}}

  1. My eyes, friend, have actually given up sleep
  2. for the man from a land where the soft creepers of the field jasmine
  3. bud, like teeth, in the forest area
  4. where rain clouds have joined with heavily clamouring[FNote_746] thunder.

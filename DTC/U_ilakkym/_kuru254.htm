{{Readable Translation}}

  1. On the Kōṅku which has opened its tender buds, resembling breasts,
  2. so that bees in swarms are humming around [its] leafless pretty twigs,
  3. the min blossoms have come. [What has] not come, friend,
  4. is the message[FNote_1025] [that should be] coming to say 'he is about to arrive'[FNote_1026],
  5. he who has gone yearning to bring wealth that has to be made [first],
  6. who does not remember even the bed of dense, fragrant hir,
  7. he who [even used to] forget sleep in sleepless nights.[FNote_1027]

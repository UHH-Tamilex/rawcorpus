{{Readable Translation}}

  • Strenght-vanquishing evening, come on the desert,
  • in a little village with the hot battle-fronts of ploughmen [who have] bows for ploughs,[FNote_7]
  • where the unstudied children are playing with Nelli [fruits] for pawns,
  • having made a play-field with pawns[FNote_8] like a touchstone[FNote_9],
  • in the dappled shade of a Neem tree with parched bottom
  • [and] long branches struck by the wind[FNote_10], where a kite giving birth is suffering,
  • I still remember[FNote_11] [it], don`t I?,
  • thinking '[this is] the time of distress, with glorious light in the house,
  • for her who is sweet like a fulfilled task that is remembered.

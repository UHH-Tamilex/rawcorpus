{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if the ground moved to the side, even if water became fire,
  2. even if there appeared [another] border to the great sea with shining waves(?)[FNote_1487]
  3. - what loss [would] that [be], friend,
  4. fearing scandal by hot-mouthed[FNote_1488] women,
  5. for our attachment, established with the man from a land of high mountains,
  6. where the ripe jackfruit from the flower-smelling tree, touched[FNote_1489] on one side
  7. [and] broken by the spotty-fingered male of the sharp-toothed black monkey,
  8. smells near the little homes with Malabar lilies?[FNote_1490]

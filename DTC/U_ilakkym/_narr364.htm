{{Readable Translation}}

  • The season [he] had spoken of has passed by.
  • If a few [more] pleasing days pass by,
  • while the water is bundled [and] stirs in the fully bustling sky,
  • become one with the clouds at midday of confusing darkness even by daylight,
  • while disgust come with the dewy northwind [and] tarries,
  • I won't live for many days, friend,
  • - when together with the blemishless(?) showers hasty(?)
  • evening takes place, for [my] life to go,
  • while on every village common sounds the sweet Koṉṟi flute
  • of the untught cowherds who have brought [back] many cows,
  • while in the village the sweet voices of [their] wet bells with small red tongues
  • that don't know of the sound of thunder sound abundantly in turn.

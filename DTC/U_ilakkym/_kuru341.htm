{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if the lover won't esteem [me], also at a time become pleasant with twigs,
  2. taken[FNote_1370] for decoration by the grove, of the Kuravam tree with green buds
  3. from which have come forth many blossoms, together with the parched-flowered[FNote_1371] Puṉku tree,
  4. I will live, friend, by my strength
  5. to compel the heart which is not compelled:
  6. 'the courage purposed by the heart of the great one is not duty'[FNote_1372].

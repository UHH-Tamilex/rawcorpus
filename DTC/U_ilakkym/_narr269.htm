{{Metrical text}}

  1. kurumpaimaṇip pūṭṭu paruñceṅk kiṇkiṇip
  2. pālār tuvarvāyp paimpūt putalvaṉ
  3. mālaik kaṭṭiṉ mārpūr piḻiya
  4. vavveyi ṟoḻukiya vavvāy māṇakaic
  5. ceyirtīr koḷkainam muyirveṅ kātali ((5))
  6. tirumukat talamvaruṅ kaṇṇiṉain talkalum
  7. perumara vaḷḷiyiṟ piṇikku meṉṉā
  8. ciṟupal kuṉṟa miṟappō
  9. raṟivār yāravar muṉṉi yavvē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The one who traverses many small hills
  • without saying "she fetters[FNote_844] [me] like a creeper a big tree",
  • all day thinking of the eyes, moving agitatedly in the brilliant face,
  • "[our] hot(?) love, our life, with faultless principles,
  • a glorious smile on that mouth on which flowed [saliva] from those teeth,
  • while the son with green ornaments [and] a coral mouth feeding on milk,
  • with big red tinkling anklets, in which unripe coconut-kernels are put as gems(?),
  • ascends [and] descends on [her] sweet chest on which garlands are tied",
  • - who knows what he is about?

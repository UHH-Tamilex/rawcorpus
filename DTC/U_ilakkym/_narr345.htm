{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the ghat, where the white ones are expanding,
  • as if the little white crow yawned,
  • the white water-lilies, hanging(?) while the coming wind lifts [them],
  • as[FNote_988] the fresh unripe fruits shed loose from the mangrove in the seashore grove
  • falls so that it plunges into the big backwaters of blue colour,
  • Pitiable [are] the intimacies of great people like you.(?)
  • If they do not long for a long time,
  • the unsought heart, that knows the straightness
  • received by quality, fading in motionlessness,(?)
  • what of but living? May the great clarity dwindle.

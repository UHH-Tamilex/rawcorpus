{{Readable Translation}}

  1. A double-faced robberess[FNote_1275] our love!
  2. As if she [were] just ours in soundless night,
  3. come to be fragrant in the forest on the Muḷḷūr mountain
  4. of Malaiyaṉ with red spear [and] energy to take on [any] enmity,
  5. but[FNote_1276] at dawn as if she [were] just theirs,
  6. one with settled face[FNote_1277],
  7. thrown off mixed blossoms that decked [her] tresses,
  8. sesame oil applied to the fragrant hair, smoothed with sandal paste.

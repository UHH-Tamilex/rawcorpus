{{Readable Translation}}

  • Negligent of pain, since I donōt feel pity, thinking of
  • the man from the coast of the big water with foolish blue water-lilies,
  • that open [their] green mouths from sweet sleep in the scorching rays,
  • [their] minds soft, without knowing of the distress they have[FNote_861]
  • on [their] bed, mixed up with the [cutting] tools and with the ears
  • - flowers, gone with the base [because of][FNote_862] the cutters of red paddy
  • not seeing that they are wounded by [their] sharp sickles -
  • even if I get, to get praise, [that] virtueless man,
  • I donōt have the strength [anymore], friend.

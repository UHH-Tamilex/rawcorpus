{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When[FNote_188] [my] lover is close, greatly delighted[FNote_189],
  2. like a village taking a festival, I rejoice, certainly.
  3. Near the road, in a small village with pretty homes[FNote_190]
  4. people gone, squirrels playing in the front-yard,
  5. a house left lonely - like that, empty,
  6. I am in distress, friend, since he left.

Altern: Srin.

{--!}Because [my] lover is a tough worker (cf. DED 586; āka as bhavati), I will rejoice like the people of an ūr who are having a festival, for sure (i.e. when he comes back as a wealthy man who is able to marry me), (but for the time being,) while he is away I will be afflicted ...

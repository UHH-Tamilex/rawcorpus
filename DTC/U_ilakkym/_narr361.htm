{{Readable Translation}}

  • Very fragrant blossom of small-blossomed jasmine
  • he himself wore and [his] young [attendants] put [them] on too.
  • The proud horses with golden weapons, as if passing over the sky
  • have given the long, befitting chariot
  • to the wide mansion filled with sand that is perturbed by evening,
  • while the singing of the long-tongues clear bells sounded excellently
  • through the cool fragrant woodland on which the passing rin had flown.
  • Setting aside [for it] to depart the difficult affliction that was permanent
  • she with perfect jewels longed to immerse in festival.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If they say [it is] blame to trust in the noble one searching [me],
  • exhausted, with eyes that weep without sleeping,
  • dying [is] sweet, loving friend.
  • Even if those states are not so,
  • since it is the talk of the world, staying together
  • saying "noble ones do not at all shrink from a state of duty(?)",
  • it is suitable that [I] possess permanently
  • the graceful chest of the man from the cool ghat
  • by the seashore grove where the Puṉṉai trees have grown high,
  • buds opening.

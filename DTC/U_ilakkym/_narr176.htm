{{Readable Translation}}

  • When she remained longing for us, we, longing [for her]
  • have granted it.[FNote_571] What has been let off on that side, by the good one?(?)[FNote_572]?
  • Does it say "she is full of love for her
  • who doesn't know how to take care of [her] status?" - speak, friend.[FNote_573]
  • - the village of their abode, with fences, in the hills,
  • on a slope, where soft soft [tones] are sounding:
  • the bees in groups with pleasing voices, as if one listened to the lute,
  • singing as one with the drum of the waterfall,
  • while heaped so as to be unstable on the banana[-grown] mountainside
  • are the bright red Malabar-lilies that have broken the pack of [their] buds,
  • resembling the lines in the faces of elephants put in a row.

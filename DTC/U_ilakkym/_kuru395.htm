{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The heart, stopping is impossible [to it]. He,
  2. without saying 'when there is no love, consideration [is] wealth',
  3. he is one who has the strength [and] forces by strength.[FNote_1576]
  4. And no one is there who says 'don't you fear',
  5. while they pleasantly close [their] eyes, even if they don't remove [the evil][FNote_1577],
  6. like the people here for the moon which the snake swallows[FNote_1578].
  7. Pitiable [is] it, shame,
  8. if [we] happen to leave for the place he abides there.

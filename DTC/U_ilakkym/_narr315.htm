{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the ghat, where they fetter
  • the boat with curved mouth that had gone to work of old(?),
  • taking the blows of the waves slapping aginst the expansive ghat,
  • since many of years of the great god here had passed by,
  • to the drum-trunk
  • with rich shade of the Puṉṉi tree joined with small-blossomed Ñāḻal,
  • without giving [it] good smoke of swift fragrance,
  • as the ploughmen, becuse the good bull had reached the wealth of git(?)[FNote_927],
  • let [it] go from [its] task in the grassy grove,
  • if you do not know well the mistake, thinly(?),
  • in the intimacy taken excellently and well(?), like us,
  • those that longed for you might be as blossom burned,
  • their eyes dimmed [and] shoulder emaciating.

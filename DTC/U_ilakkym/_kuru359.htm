{{Readable Translation}}

  1. See, bard, it is like that, alas:
  2. he breathed, like an elephant on [its] resting place[FNote_1438],
  3. on the bed fragrant with flowers near the short-legged cot[FNote_1439]
  4. in the fresh white moonlight expanded at evening.
  5. When he was full of yearning, the victorious one embraced [his] son.
  6. The mother of [his] son, she was parted from his back(?).[FNote_1440]

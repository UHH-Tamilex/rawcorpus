Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 37-1} vāḻka niṉ vaḷaṉ ē niṉ uṭi vāḻkki
  • {PATI 37-2} vāymoḻi vāyar niṉ pukaḻ ētta
  • {PATI 37-3} pakivar āra paḻaṅkaṇ aruḷi
  • {PATI 37-4} nakivar āra nal kalam citaṟi
  • {PATI 37-5} āṉṟu avintu aṭaṅkiya ceyir tīr cemmāl
  • {PATI 37-6} vāṉ tōy nal ici ulakamoṭu uyirppa
  • {PATI 37-7} tuḷaṅku kuṭi tiruttiya valam paṭu veṉṟi um
  • {PATI 37-8} mā irum puṭiyal mā kaḻal puṉintu
  • {PATI 37-9} maṉ eyil eṟintu maṟavar tarīi
  • {PATI 37-10} tol nili ciṟappiṉ niṉ niḻal vāḻnarkku
  • {PATI 37-11} kōṭu aṟa vitta kōṭā koḷki um
  • {PATI 37-12} naṉṟu peritu uṭiyiyāl nī ē
  • {PATI 37-13} ve tiṟal vēntu ē i ulakattōrkku ē

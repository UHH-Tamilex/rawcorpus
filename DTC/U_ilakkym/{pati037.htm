Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 37-1} vāḻka niṉ vaḷaṉ ē niṉ uṭai vāḻkkai
  • {PATI 37-2} vāymoḻi vāyar niṉ pukaḻ ētta
  • {PATI 37-3} pakaivar āra paḻaṅkaṇ aruḷi
  • {PATI 37-4} nakaivar āra nal kalam citaṟi
  • {PATI 37-5} āṉṟu avintu aṭaṅkiya ceyir tīr cemmāl
  • {PATI 37-6} vāṉ tōy nal icai ulakamoṭu uyirppa
  • {PATI 37-7} tuḷaṅku kuṭi tiruttiya valam paṭu veṉṟi um
  • {PATI 37-8} mā irum puṭaiyal mā kaḻal puṉaintu
  • {PATI 37-9} maṉ eyil eṟintu maṟavar tarīi
  • {PATI 37-10} tol nilai ciṟappiṉ niṉ niḻal vāḻnarkku
  • {PATI 37-11} kōṭu aṟa vaitta kōṭā koḷkai um
  • {PATI 37-12} naṉṟu peritu uṭaiyaiyāl nī ē
  • {PATI 37-13} ve tiṟal vēntu ē i ulakattōrkku ē

  1. ceṉṉilap puṟaviṉ puṉmayirp puruvi
  2. pāṭiṉ ṟeṇmaṇit tōṭutalip peyarak
  3. kāṉa mullik kayavā yalarip
  4. pārppaṉa makaḷir cāraṟpuṟat taṇiyak
  5. kalcuṭar cēruṅ katirmāy mālip ((5))
  6. pulleṉ vaṟumaṉi nōkki mella
  7. varuntuṅ kollō tiruntiḻi yarivi
  8. vallik kaṭavumati tērē ceṉṟika
  9. kuruntaviḻ kuṟumpoṟi payiṉṟap
  10. peruṅkali mūtūr marantōṉ ṟummē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • In the evening when the rays disappear, when the glow joins the stones[FNote_938]
  • to adorn the backside of the slopes of brahmin women[FNote_939]
  • with deep-mouthed flowers of the forest jasmine,
  • while the numerous heads with bells clear in song
  • of the short-hired sheep in the woodland with red soil return,
  • is she suffering,
  • softly, looking about the empty poor house,
  • the young women with perfect jewels?
  • You strong one, urge the chariot! Go!
  • There appear the trees of our old village in big bustle,
  • dense with short hills where the wild limes are opening.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • He [is] without consideration, live, friend,
  • for coming by the small path in the great mountins,
  • where the fragrant flowers on the reed have grown long
  • by the clefts [and] caves fragrant with honey come from(?)[FNote_819] the sandal,
  • where the big angry rock-snake that had swallowed an elephant bull
  • coils [itself] much, fetteringly, around a tree with a core that is not soft(?),[FNote_820]
  • at midnight that offered faultless great rin,
  • the short many of fully pregnant clouds whirling
  • on the long big hills, hiding the hot glow
  • with [thunder-]strokes that shake the sky hung in darkness, split by lightning.

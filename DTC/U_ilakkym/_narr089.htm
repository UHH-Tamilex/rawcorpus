{{Metrical text}}

  1. koṇṭa lāṟṟi viṇṭalaic ceṟiyar
  2. tiraipitir kaṭuppa mukaṭukan tēṟi
  3. niraittunirai koṇṭa kamañcūṉ māmaḻai
  4. yaḻittuḷi kaḻippiya vaḻipeyaṟ kaṭainā
  5. ḷirumpaṉip paruvatta mayiṟkā yuḻunti ((5))
  6. ṉakalilai yakala vīci yakalā
  7. talkalu malaikku nalkā vāṭaip
  8. paruma yāṉai yayara vuyirttāṅ
  9. kiṉṉum varumē tōḻi vārā
  10. vaṉka ṇāḷarō ṭiyainta ((10))
  11. puṉkaṇ mālaiyum pulampumun tuṟuttē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • On the last days of the successive(?) rains when the dwindling drops
  • of the fully pregnant clouds that formed an orderly row are spent,
  • having risen upwards to the top so that the spray of the waves is spread(?),
  • while the heads are close to the sky, bearing the eastwind,
  • now too the ungranting northwind comes, friend,
  • sighing like an elephant that lowers itself to a seat,
  • that slaps though it still abides the black-gram with hairy unripe fruits
  • of the great dewy season, dashing [at it] incessantly
  • so that the wide leaves take off(?)[FNote_308] - what does not come [is][FNote_309]
  • even at sorrowful evening
  • that was enjoyable with the [our] man, then,
  • having brought loneliness into appearance.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The dreadful god in the Marām tree of the village common
  2. torments the faithless ones, they say.
  3. Yearning for our man from a land full of hills,
  4. who is not at all faithless, [my] forehead has become pale.
  5. [My] full tender shoulder has wasted and wasted away.[FNote_355]

Altern

3b Not at all faithless is our man from a land full of hills.[FNote_356]

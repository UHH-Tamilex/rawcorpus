  1. She is like a sprout, on which drops have been offered(?)[FNote_902],
  2. rin-eyes with beautiful corners(?)[FNote_903], resembling the red backs
  3. of the water-dripping rich buds of the large-flowered jasmine in an abundant
  4. shower, similar to her[FNote_904], who:
  5. if [one] takes the float at the head, [the other also] does so,
  6. if [one] takes the float at the end, [the other also] does so
  7. if [one] lets go of the float [and] it flows with the flood
  8. {--!}comes also there.[FNote_905]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The old beauty lost, the shoulders' integrity exhausted,
  2. with unhappy heart sleepless even at night,
  3. pallor come up - perishing [like this],
  4. [is that] the gain of having laughed, so that shining teeth appeared,
  5. with the man from the ghat, where transverse waves are breaking,
  6. disturbing(?)[FNote_1516] the day-blossoms in the bushes full of flowers
  7. of the seashore grove smelling coolly, where white egrets call out?

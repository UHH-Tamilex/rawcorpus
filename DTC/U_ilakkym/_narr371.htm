{{Metrical text}}

  1. kāyāṅ[FNote_1032] kuṉṟattuk koṉṟi pōla
  2. māmali viṭarakam viḷaṅka miṉṉi
  3. māyō ḷirunta tēe nōkki
  4. viyaliru vicumpakam putiyap pāayp
  5. peyaṟuṭaṅ kiṉavē peyyā vāṉa ((5))
  6. niḻaṟikaḻ cuṭarttoṭi ñekiḻa vēṅki
  7. yaḻaṟuṭaṅ kiṉaḷē yāyiḻi yataṉetir
  8. kuḻaṟuṭaṅ kiṉarē kōvalar
  9. taḻaṅkukura lurumiṉ kaṅku lāṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Flashing so that the insides of caves shine in the dark mountins,
  • as a Koṉṟi tree on a hill of ironwood,
  • looking at the land where [my] dark one was,
  • spreading so that the inside of the wide dark sky is buried
  • the unrining clouds have begun to rin.
  • Lamenting so that [her] glowing armlets, glittering in the shade[FNote_1033], became lose
  • the [one of] choice jewels began to cry. In the face of that[FNote_1034]
  • the cowherds began [to play their] flutes,
  • - in the night of thunder with resounding voice.

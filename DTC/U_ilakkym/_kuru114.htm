{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Having lid down [my] doll on a spread of blue water-lilies
  2. I came to your sign, o man from the sea with speedy(?) chariot.
  3. We go; obey the order to go.[FNote_464]
  4. The one with a belly to eat[FNote_465] Āral nightly[FNote_466],
  5. the heron treads on my dughter's forehead.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • After the daylight is gone, the blossoms have closed
  • and the crabs have come to keep to [their] holes with the young ones of the tortoise on the settling shore of the fish-reeking water and the birds
  • have come to stay with [their] young ones on the trees to destroy their yearning suffering for prey from the curved backwaters. Therefore,
  • since this is not the time, do you come solitary,
  • o you of rin eyes with [collyrium-]drawn grace ...

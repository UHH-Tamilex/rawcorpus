  • {KALI 150-1} ayam tikaḻ naṟum koṉṟi alaṅkal am teriyalāṉ

  • {KALI 150-3} kaṉi katir teṟutaliṉ kaṭuttu eḻunta kāmpu tī
  • {KALI 150-4} mali parantu tali koṇṭu muḻaṅkiya muḻaṅku aḻal
  • {KALI 150-5} mayaṅku atar maṟukaliṉ mali talikkoṇṭeṉa
  • {KALI 150-6} vicumpu uṟa nivantu aḻalum vilaṅku aru ve curam
  • {KALI 150-7} iṟantu tām eṇṇiya eytutal vēṭkiyāl
  • {KALI 150-8} aṟam tuṟantu āy iḻāy ākkattil pirintavar
  • {KALI 150-9} piṟaṅku nīr caṭi karantāṉ aṇi aṉṉa niṉ niṟam
  • {KALI 150-10} pacantu nī iṉiyi āy nīttal um nīppa ō
  • {KALI 150-11} kari kāynta kavalittāy kal kāynta kāṭṭu akam
  • {KALI 150-12} veru vanta āṟu eṉṉār viḻu poruṭku akaṉṟavar
  • {KALI 150-13} uruva ēṟṟu ūrtiyāṉ oḷ aṇi nakkaṉṉa niṉ
  • {KALI 150-14} uru iḻantu iṉiyi āy uḷḷal um uḷḷupa ō
  • {KALI 150-15} kotittu urāy kuṉṟu ivarntu koṭi koṇṭa kōṭiyāl
  • {KALI 150-16} otukku ariya neṟi eṉṉār oḷ poruṭku akaṉṟavar
  • {KALI 150-17} putu tiṅkaḷ kaṇṇiyāṉ poṉ pūṇ ñāṉṟu aṉṉa niṉ
  • {KALI 150-18} katuppu ulaṟum kaviṉi āy kāṇṭal um kāṇpa ō
  • {KALI 150-19} āṅku
  • {KALI 150-20} arum peṟal ātiriyāṉ aṇipeṟa malarnta
  • {KALI 150-21} perum taṇ caṇpakam pōla oruṅku avar
  • {KALI 150-22} poyyār ākutal teḷintaṉam
  • {KALI 150-23} mi īr ōti maṭa moḻiyōy ē

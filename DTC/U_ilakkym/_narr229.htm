{{Readable Translation}}

  • When he says "we will go, we will go", being vexed
  • I fear to say "go".
  • If I say "don't go", I am afraid
  • because of the base words many people will throw on [his] breast.
  • Therefore,
  • go, having gone accomplish [your] work, having gone
  • beware not to prolong there itself(?)[FNote_723].
  • Even if you are close [to her], embracing [her] so that you take the impression[FNote_724]
  • of [her] jewel-adorned bosom at midnight, she shivers alone.
  • After the young rain that played, [bringing] coolness,
  • gathering in the area of the softening sky(?),
  • have you seen the northwind too that came to stay?

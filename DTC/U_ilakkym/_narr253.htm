  5. yuḷḷiṉum paṉikku moḷḷiḻik kuṟumakaḷ ((5))
  6. pērici yurumoṭu māri muṟṟiya
  7. palkuṭik kaḷḷiṉ vaṇmakiḻp pāri
  8. palavuṟu kuṉṟam pōlap
  9. peruṅkavi ṉeytiya varuṅkāp piṉaḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You who sigh hotly, as an elephant in [its] resting place,
  • when the birds join [their] abodes and when you see those that are united,
  • greatly lovelorn, with a trouble that suffered in thinking much of [her],
  • you don't listen [to words] of my mouth, you who are thinking abundantly.[FNote_800]
  • The bright-jewelled little woman who shivers also when she remembers [you]
  • has severe guards, since she has attined beauty great
  • as the hill full of jack-trees
  • of Pāri who delights in generosity, [giving] toddy in many cups
  • that has matured in showers with great noisy thunder.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Like ambrosia pretty [and] sweet [her] words,
  2. just as pleasing she and pleasing [her] qualities.
  3. Unpleasant, when she brings heavy affliction:
  4. when staying together, difficult [is] desire.[FNote_824]
  5. Beware of coming near, you who possess knowledge.

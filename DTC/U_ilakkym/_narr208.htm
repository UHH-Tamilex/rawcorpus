{{Readable Translation}}

  • Sobbing, while the shining jewels become lose, [your] bravery exhusted,
  • perishing greatly, with eyes that weep unceasingly,
  • so that [your] breasts become wet in between with sapphires clear as water,
  • what thinking do you fade away, little woman with glowing forehead?
  • He is not one to go, our lover, [and] even if he goes
  • he is not on to suffer such pin. He himself
  • is one who rejoices(?), trusting greatly(?)[FNote_662] in you(?)[FNote_663].
  • As one of superior love and one bound to be exhusted (from lovesickness),
  • being grieved even more than we when separated,
  • he will come, even if the rare wealth is not accomplished. Moreover,
  • the voice of the great rin, it seems, will bring [him],
  • searching for those who are separated from [their] dear mates.

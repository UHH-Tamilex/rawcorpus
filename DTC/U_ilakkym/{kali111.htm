Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 111-1} tīm pāl kaṟanta kalam māṟṟi kaṉṟu ellām
  • {KALI 111-2} tāmpiṉ piṇittu maṉai niṟīi yāy tanta
  • {KALI 111-3} pūm karai nīlam puṭai tāḻa mey acaii pāṅkar um
  • {KALI 111-4} mullai um tāya pāṭṭam kāl tōḻi nam
  • {KALI 111-5} pul iṉattu āyar makaḷirōṭu ellām
  • {KALI 111-6} oruṅku viḷaiyāṭa a vaḻi vanta
  • {KALI 111-7} kuruntu am pūm kaṇṇi potuvaṉ maṟṟu eṉṉai
  • {KALI 111-8} muṟṟu iḻai ēer maṭa nallāy nī āṭum
  • {KALI 111-9} ciṟṟil puṉaiku ō ciṟitu eṉṟāṉ ellā nī
  • {KALI 111-10} peṟṟēm yām eṉṟu piṟar ceyta il iruppāy
  • {KALI 111-11} kaṟṟatu ilai maṉṟa kāṇ eṉṟēṉ muṟṟu iḻāy
  • {KALI 111-12} tātu cūḻ kūntal takaipeṟa taiiya
  • {KALI 111-13} kōtai puṉaiku ō niṉakku eṉṟāṉ ellā nī
  • {KALI 111-14} ētilār tanta pū koḷvāy naṉi mika
  • {KALI 111-15} pētaiyai maṉṟa peritu eṉṟēṉ mātarāy
  • {KALI 111-16} aiya pitirnta cuṇaṅku aṇi mel mulai mēl
  • {KALI 111-17} toyyil eḻutuku ō maṟṟu eṉṟāṉ yām piṟar
  • {KALI 111-18} cey puṟam nōkki iruttum ō nī peritu
  • {KALI 111-19} maiyalai mātō viṭuka eṉṟēṉ taiyalāy
  • {KALI 111-20} colliya āṟu ellām māṟu māṟu yāṉ peyarppa
  • {KALI 111-21} allāntāṉ pōla peyarntāṉ āvaṉai nī
  • {KALI 111-22} āyar makaḷir iyalpu uraittu entai um
  • {KALI 111-23} yāy um aṟiya uraittīyiṉ yāṉ uṟṟa
  • {KALI 111-24} nōy um kaḷaikuvai maṉ

{{Readable Translation}}

  • At dreadful midnight, when the strong tiger is roaring on the beautiful height,
  • having killed the elephant bull, attacking so that [its] spotted face was spoiled,
  • since the dark female [tiger] was hungry, with [its] strong newly born one,[FNote_1052]
  • the young one, as a swaying pretty garland
  • of the black-trunked Vēṅkai tree grown near the stones,
  • even if it seems you [are] considerate, you are not considerate, no[FNote_1053],
  • - for your coming, o man from a land of high mountains,
  • with the thunder that furiously drives away the snakes,
  • at fearful night buried in ripe darkness.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Ambrosia may eat the woman from the neighbouring house,
  2. who sid he would come, the man from a land full of mountins
  3. with woods of bamboo grown high,
  4. where the bats with sharp nils [and] blue soft wings,
  5. after eating mangos, [sweet] as honey in a milk mixture[FNote_807],
  6. feed on the sour Nelli fruit [and] nearby
  7. hang in the thornless pretty bamboos.[FNote_808]

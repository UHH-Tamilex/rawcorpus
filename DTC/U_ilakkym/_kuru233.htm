{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The village of the father of [her with] tight[FNote_951] armlets worn in a row
  2. [means] cooked rice that knows no measure[FNote_952]
  3. for anyone, as a rest of what is poured forth with water[FNote_953]
  4. for the high ones[FNote_954]. It [lies] in the woodland, in the face of the rainy season,
  5. as if the lid is opened of the gold-raining baskets of prosperous people,
  6. since the bright blossoms of the Laburnum are spread
  7. in small pits[FNote_955] with wide mouths dug for the Kavalai plants.[FNote_956]

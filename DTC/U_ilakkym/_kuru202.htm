{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Aches my heart. Aches my heart.
  2. Since, just like the new flowers, sweet to the eye, bear thorns
  3. on the small-leaved Neruñci[FNote_810], grown dense in the grassy fields,
  4. our lover who did sweet things [to us],
  5. [now] does unsweet ones, aches my heart.

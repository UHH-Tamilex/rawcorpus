  1. If, [as] you said, [it is] unsweet,
  2. the big wilderness of Ōmai trees, like a ruined village
  3. in the open, the side of which have all[FNote_499] passed by the salt merchants[FNote_500]
  4. - are the houses sweet, o great one, for those left alone?

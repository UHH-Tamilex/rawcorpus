  1. vika ṟōṟu miṉpamu miḷamiyu
  2. meykaṇi niḻaliṟ kaḻiyumiv vulakattuk
  3. kāṇī reṉṟalō varitē yatunaṉi
  4. pēṇī rāku viriyaveṉ ṟōḻi
  5. pūṇaṇi yākam pulampap pāṇa ((5))
  6. rayirppukkoṇ ṭaṉṉa koṉṟiyan tīṅkaṉi ruyirppiṉ ṉalyā ḻuṭimarup paṉṉa
  7. vūḻuṟupu viḷinta koṉṟiyan tīṅkaṉi
  8. paṟiyaṟi kaṭippi ṉaṟiyaṟiyāt tuyalvara
  9. vevvaḷi vaḻaṅkum vēypayi laḻuvat
  10. tevva mikūu maruñcura miṟantu
  11. naṉvā yallā vāḻkki ((10))
  12. maṉṉāp poruṭpiṇip piritumyā meṉavē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Is it difficult to say: 'you don`t see [it]
  • in this world, where each day pleasure and youth
  • pass by like the shadow of an aiming arrow?'
  • You are not one to pay much heed to that, sir,
  • saying 'we separate', in the fetter of impermanent wealth,
  • a living without good truth(?)[FNote_185],
  • traversing the difficult desert where distress abounds,
  • in the hot rustling depth, where hot winds are wandering
  • so that the sweet ripe Laburnum fruits come into swaying
  • [and] not hitting the rock like a drumstick beating a drum
  • as if the bard had been seeized by doubt[FNote_186],
  • while my friend, [her] breast decked with ornaments, will be lonely.

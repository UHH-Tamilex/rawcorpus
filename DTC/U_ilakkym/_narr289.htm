{{Readable Translation}}

  • Alas, live, friend,
  • even if the ground turned upside down
  • [my] lover would not overturn the word he spoke.[FNote_883]
  • The sky [is now] close to me[FNote_884], making rain clouds,
  • drawing [water] from the vast sea, becoming densely dark,
  • pouring force the ripe rain, repeating [thunder] with fierce voice.
  • Just as the big animals running
  • when the cowherds have kindled light in the woodlands,
  • I am without [his] consideration, alas, pitiable me.

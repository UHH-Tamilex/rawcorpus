{{Readable Translation}}

  • Foolish, alas, [is] the sapphire-coloured cloud:
  • It has really given sound in the wide area,
  • offering cool rin bringing drops,
  • letting the wind fall in, while the mountin side is buried on stony elevation,
  • on the road, before comes the reaching [and] coming
  • to [the place of] his yearning, of him who has gone stroking your forehead,
  • perceiving it as moon in the eye-widening[FNote_928] sky,
  • saying "we will be back at the time these resemble your teeth,
  • [o you of] thick earrings [and] carp-like collyrium eyes",
  • pointing at the blossomless wild jasmine, to attin maturity(?)[FNote_929].

{{Metrical text}}

  1. yārkkunon turaikkō yāṉē yūrkaṭa
  2. lōtañ ceṉṟa vuppuṭaic ceṟuviṟ
  3. koṭuṅkaḻi maruṅki ṉiraivēṭ ṭeḻunta
  4. karuṅkāṟ kurukiṉ kōḷuyntu pōkiya
  5. muṭaṅkupuṟa viṟaviṉ mōvā yēṟṟāi ((5))
  6. yeṟitirai tokutta vekkar neṭuṅkōṭṭut
  7. tuṟukaṟ ṟalaiya tōṭupoti tāḻai
  8. vaṇṭupaṭu vāṉpōtu verūun
  9. tuṟaikeḻu koṇkaṟ ṟuṟantaṉa ṉeṉavē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • To whom shall I speak achingly
  • "he has abandoned [me], the man from the sea with ghats,
  • where the bearded male[FNote_668] of the bend-backed Iṟavu fish,
  • escaped [and] gone from the hold of the black-legged heron
  • that had risen for the hunt of prey on the side of the curvy backwaters
  • by the salt fields from which the flood had gone with the creeping sea,
  • is frightened by the sky bud frequented by bees
  • of the Tāḻai trees with bundles of petals(?) exalted on thick stones
  • in the long mounts of sand gathered by the dashing waves"?

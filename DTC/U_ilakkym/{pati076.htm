  • {PATI 76-10} karuvi vāṉam taṇ taḷi corinteṉa
  • {PATI 76-11} pal vitai uḻaviṉ cil ērāḷar
  • {PATI 76-12} paṉi tuṟai pakaṉṟai pāṅku uṭai teriyal

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. You, may you obtain in a vessel set aside(?)
  2. hot fresh water[FNote_1136], hot [draughts] in the cold season
  3. [and] feed to become full of the alms of a single house,
  4. very white ghee with boiled red rice
  5. at a wide gate without dogs in a flawless street![FNote_1137]
  6. When do you say it will come,
  7. the north wind with the last[FNote_1138] raining, when [one] shivers in midst of lightning?[FNote_1139] At that time he will come, our lover.

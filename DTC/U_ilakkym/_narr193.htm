{{Readable Translation}}

  • O great cool north-wind that blocks the vast fields,
  • you unendingly toiling, vexed with what stands newly generated,
  • so that the tops of the cotton-headed new blossom are perturbed by drops
  • on the mimosa with buds as round as the form of melted(?)[FNote_612] lac,
  • - nothing evil to you has been known to us.[FNote_613]
  • Don't make suffer the one who [already] has big grief[FNote_614],
  • being on one side where there is nobody,
  • since our lover who has made loose the light bangles on [her] bamboo arms
  • has separated in the fetter of wealth difficult to make.

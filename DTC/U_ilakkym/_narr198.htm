  1. cēyiṉ varūu matavali yāvuyarn
  2. tōmai nītiya kāṉiṭai yatta
  3. muṉṉā ḷumpark kaḻinta veṉmakaḷ
  4. kaṇpaṭa nīrāḻn taṉṟē tantai aṉṉāy kaṇpaṭu pāṉṟaṉi raṉṟē
  5. taṉṉū riṭavayiṟ ṟoḻuvē ṉuṇpal ((5))
  6. kōṭēn talku larumpiya titalai
  7. vāarn tilaṅkuvā leyiṟṟup polintatārc
  8. cilvaḷaip palkūn talaḷē yavaḷē
  9. maiyaṇa leruttiṉ muṉpiṟ ṟaṭakkai
  10. valvil lampi ṉeyyā vaṇmakiḻt ((10))
  11. tantai taṉṉū rituvē
  12. yīṉṟēṉ yāṉē polikanum peyarē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O strong man that comes from a distance,
  • water sank so as to fill [my] eyes[FNote_630]
  • [because of] my daughter who passed by over there yesterday
  • on the road in the midst of the forest where Ōmai trees have grown long
  • [and] Yām trees have become high.
  • I pay homage to the father at the place of his village.[FNote_631]
  • She, she has many tresses [and] few bangles,
  • a garland that glittered [and] upright shining sharp teeth,
  • beauty spots that budded on [her] hips with eminent bulk?, fine [and] many.[FNote_632]
  • This village is the one of [her] father,
  • unwearyingly generous with [his] toddy(?), with arrow in [his] strong bow,
  • with broad hands, with strength in [his] neck [and a black beard.
  • I brought her forth. May your name shine.
{{s.trsl}}
  • 9b with broad hands of the strength of a black-dewlapped bull.

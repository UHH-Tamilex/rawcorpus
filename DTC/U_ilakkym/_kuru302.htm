{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Tell [me], friend, [is] that great? No![FNote_1236]
  2. We cannot bear the heavy misery and apart from that
  3. we fear more than that the coming of the great other[FNote_1237].
  4. Alas! Even now the man from a good mountin land
  5. has come to fear[FNote_1238], becuse of that, gossip,
  6. which says 'the two of them [are] of inseparable friendship'?
  7. At midnight too, into the village where many sleep together,
  8. he knows nothing of coming[FNote_1239], except into my heart.

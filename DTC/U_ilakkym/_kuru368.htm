{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O you with tender nature. O you with tender nature.
  2. Of the blameless[FNote_1470] darkness[FNote_1471], which has left [us] on a good day,
  3. besides bearing [it] with strength, with words
  4. we cannot speak [of it], o you with tender nature.
  5. To the village [only], where small ones and great ones are living.[FNote_1472]
  6. Like a big tree the firm shore[FNote_1473]
  7. in the swimming of abundant waters, without interruption[FNote_1474],
  8. in a faultless state we will embrace for many [days].(?)[FNote_1475]

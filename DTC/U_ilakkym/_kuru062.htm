{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The body of [my] good lady, fragrant[FNote_257],
  2. like a garland glorious in a beautiful winding [of flowers]
  3. - jasmine in fresh bloom [and] opening buds of the white Malabar lily
  4. fragrantly interwoven with water-lilies of scented petals[FNote_258],
  5. it is excellent, even if it will pass, and sweet to embrace.[FNote_259]

Altern

5b: it surpasses a shoot [in tenderness] and [is] sweet/sweeter to embrace.[FNote_260]

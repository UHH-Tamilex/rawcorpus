Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 15-1} yāṇṭu talaippeyara vēṇṭu pulattu iṟuttu
  • {PATI 15-2} muṉai eri parappiya tuṉ arum cīṟṟamoṭu
  • {PATI 15-3} maḻai tavaḻpu talaiiya matil maram murukki
  • {PATI 15-4} nirai kaḷiṟu oḻukiya niraiya veḷḷam
  • {PATI 15-5} parantu āṭu kaḻaṅku aḻi maṉ maruṅku aṟuppa
  • {PATI 15-6} koṭi viṭu kurūu pukai picira kāl pora
  • {PATI 15-7} aḻal kavar maruṅkiṉ uru aṟa keṭuttu
  • {PATI 15-8} tol kaviṉ aḻinta kaṇ akal vaippiṉ
  • {PATI 15-9} veḷ pū vēḷaiyoṭu paim curai kalittu
  • {PATI 15-10} pīr ivarpu paranta nīr aṟu niṟai mutal
  • {PATI 15-11} civanta kāntaḷ mutal citai mūtil
  • {PATI 15-12} pulavu vil uḻaviṉ pullāḷ vaḻaṅkum
  • {PATI 15-13} pul ilai vaippiṉ pulam citai arampiṉ
  • {PATI 15-14} aṟiyāmaiyāṉ maṟantu tuppu etirnta niṉ
  • {PATI 15-15} pakaivar nāṭu um kaṇṭu vanticiṉ ē
  • {PATI 15-16} kaṭala um kalla um yāṟṟa um piṟa um
  • {PATI 15-17} vaḷam pala nikaḻtaru naṉam talai nal nāṭṭu
  • {PATI 15-18} viḻavu aṟupu aṟiyā muḻavu imiḻ mūtūr
  • {PATI 15-19} koṭi niḻal paṭṭa poṉ uṭai niyamattu
  • {PATI 15-20} cīr peṟu kali makiḻ iyampum muraciṉ
  • {PATI 15-21} vayavar vēntu ē paricilar veṟukkai
  • {PATI 15-22} tār aṇintu eḻiliya toṭi citai maruppiṉ
  • {PATI 15-23} pōr val yāṉai cēralāta
  • {PATI 15-24} nī vāḻiyar i ulakattōrkku eṉa
  • {PATI 15-25} uṇṭu urai māṟiya maḻalai nāviṉ
  • {PATI 15-26} mel col kala paiyar tiruntu toṭai vāḻtta
  • {PATI 15-27} veytuṟavu aṟiyātu nantiya vāḻkkai
  • {PATI 15-28} ceyta mēval amarnta cuṟṟamōṭu
  • {PATI 15-29} oṉṟumoḻintu aṭaṅkiya koḷkai eṉṟum
  • {PATI 15-30} pati piḻaippu aṟiyātu tuyttal eyti
  • {PATI 15-31} niraiyam orīiya vēṭkai puraiyōr
  • {PATI 15-32} mēyiṉar uṟaiyum palar pukaḻ paṇpiṉ
  • {PATI 15-33} nī puṟantarutaliṉ nōy ikantu orīiya
  • {PATI 15-34} yāṇar nal nāṭu um kaṇṭu mati maruṇṭaṉeṉ
  • {PATI 15-35} maṇ uṭai ñālattu maṉ uyirkku eñcātu
  • {PATI 15-36} īttu kai taṇṭā kai kaṭum tuppiṉ
  • {PATI 15-37} purai vayiṉ purai vayiṉ periya nalki
  • {PATI 15-38} ēmam ākiya cīr keḻu viḻaviṉ
  • {PATI 15-39} neṭiyōṉ aṉṉa nal icai
  • {PATI 15-40} oṭiyā mainta niṉ paṇpu pala nayantu ē

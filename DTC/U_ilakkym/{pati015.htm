Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 15-1} yāṇṭu talippeyara vēṇṭu pulattu iṟuttu
  • {PATI 15-2} muṉi eri parappiya tuṉ arum cīṟṟamoṭu
  • {PATI 15-3} maḻi tavaḻpu taliiya matil maram murukki
  • {PATI 15-4} niri kaḷiṟu oḻukiya niriya veḷḷam
  • {PATI 15-5} parantu āṭu kaḻaṅku aḻi maṉ maruṅku aṟuppa
  • {PATI 15-6} koṭi viṭu kurūu puki picira kāl pora
  • {PATI 15-7} aḻal kavar maruṅkiṉ uru aṟa keṭuttu
  • {PATI 15-8} tol kaviṉ aḻinta kaṇ akal vippiṉ
  • {PATI 15-9} veḷ pū vēḷiyoṭu pim curi kalittu
  • {PATI 15-10} pīr ivarpu paranta nīr aṟu niṟi mutal
  • {PATI 15-11} civanta kāntaḷ mutal citi mūtil
  • {PATI 15-12} pulavu vil uḻaviṉ pullāḷ vaḻaṅkum
  • {PATI 15-13} pul ili vippiṉ pulam citi arampiṉ
  • {PATI 15-14} aṟiyāmiyāṉ maṟantu tuppu etirnta niṉ
  • {PATI 15-15} pakivar nāṭu um kaṇṭu vanticiṉ ē
  • {PATI 15-16} kaṭala um kalla um yāṟṟa um piṟa um
  • {PATI 15-17} vaḷam pala nikaḻtaru naṉam tali nal nāṭṭu
  • {PATI 15-18} viḻavu aṟupu aṟiyā muḻavu imiḻ mūtūr
  • {PATI 15-19} koṭi niḻal paṭṭa poṉ uṭi niyamattu
  • {PATI 15-20} cīr peṟu kali makiḻ iyampum muraciṉ
  • {PATI 15-21} vayavar vēntu ē paricilar veṟukki
  • {PATI 15-22} tār aṇintu eḻiliya toṭi citi maruppiṉ
  • {PATI 15-23} pōr val yāṉi cēralāta
  • {PATI 15-24} nī vāḻiyar i ulakattōrkku eṉa
  • {PATI 15-25} uṇṭu uri māṟiya maḻali nāviṉ
  • {PATI 15-26} mel col kala piyar tiruntu toṭi vāḻtta
  • {PATI 15-27} veytuṟavu aṟiyātu nantiya vāḻkki
  • {PATI 15-28} ceyta mēval amarnta cuṟṟamōṭu
  • {PATI 15-29} oṉṟumoḻintu aṭaṅkiya koḷki eṉṟum
  • {PATI 15-30} pati piḻippu aṟiyātu tuyttal eyti
  • {PATI 15-31} niriyam orīiya vēṭki puriyōr
  • {PATI 15-32} mēyiṉar uṟiyum palar pukaḻ paṇpiṉ
  • {PATI 15-33} nī puṟantarutaliṉ nōy ikantu orīiya
  • {PATI 15-34} yāṇar nal nāṭu um kaṇṭu mati maruṇṭaṉeṉ
  • {PATI 15-35} maṇ uṭi ñālattu maṉ uyirkku eñcātu
  • {PATI 15-36} īttu ki taṇṭā ki kaṭum tuppiṉ
  • {PATI 15-37} puri vayiṉ puri vayiṉ periya nalki
  • {PATI 15-38} ēmam ākiya cīr keḻu viḻaviṉ
  • {PATI 15-39} neṭiyōṉ aṉṉa nal ici
  • {PATI 15-40} oṭiyā minta niṉ paṇpu pala nayantu ē

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 42-1} irum paṉam puṭaiyal īkai vāṉ kaḻal
  • {PATI 42-2} mīṉ tēr koṭpiṉ paṉi kayam mūḻki
  • {PATI 42-3} ciral peyarntaṉṉa neṭu veḷ ūci
  • {PATI 42-4} neṭu vaci paranta vaṭu vāḻ mārpiṉ
  • {PATI 42-5} ampu cēr uṭampiṉar cērntōr allatu
  • {PATI 42-6} tumpai cūṭātu malainta māṭci
  • {PATI 42-7} aṉṉōr peruma nal nutal kaṇava
  • {PATI 42-8} aṇṇal yāṉai aṭu pōr kuṭṭuva
  • {PATI 42-9} maintu uṭai nal amar kaṭantu valam tarīi
  • {PATI 42-10} iñci vī virāya paim tār pūṭṭi
  • {PATI 42-11} cāntu puṟattu eṟinta tacumpu tuḷaṅku irukkai
  • {PATI 42-12} tīm cēṟu viḷainta maṇi niṟa maṭṭam
  • {PATI 42-13} ōmpā īkaiyiṉ vaṇ makiḻ curantu
  • {PATI 42-14} kōṭiyar perum kiḷai vāḻa āṭu iyal
  • {PATI 42-15} uḷai avir kali mā poḻintavai eṇṇiṉ
  • {PATI 42-16} maṉpatai maruḷa aracu paṭa kaṭantu
  • {PATI 42-17} muntu viṉai etir vara peṟutal kāṇiyar
  • {PATI 42-18} oḷiṟu nilai uyar maruppu ēntiya kaḷiṟu ūrntu
  • {PATI 42-19} māṉa maintaroṭu maṉṉar ētta niṉ
  • {PATI 42-20} tēroṭu cuṟṟam ulaku uṭaṉ mūya
  • {PATI 42-21} mā irum teḷ kaṭal mali tirai pauvattu
  • {PATI 42-22} veḷ talai kurūu picir uṭaiya
  • {PATI 42-23} taṇ pala varūum puṇariyiṉ pala ē

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 42-1} irum paṉam puṭiyal īki vāṉ kaḻal
  • {PATI 42-2} mīṉ tēr koṭpiṉ paṉi kayam mūḻki
  • {PATI 42-3} ciral peyarntaṉṉa neṭu veḷ ūci
  • {PATI 42-4} neṭu vaci paranta vaṭu vāḻ mārpiṉ
  • {PATI 42-5} ampu cēr uṭampiṉar cērntōr allatu
  • {PATI 42-6} tumpi cūṭātu malinta māṭci
  • {PATI 42-7} aṉṉōr peruma nal nutal kaṇava
  • {PATI 42-8} aṇṇal yāṉi aṭu pōr kuṭṭuva
  • {PATI 42-9} mintu uṭi nal amar kaṭantu valam tarīi
  • {PATI 42-10} iñci vī virāya pim tār pūṭṭi
  • {PATI 42-11} cāntu puṟattu eṟinta tacumpu tuḷaṅku irukki
  • {PATI 42-12} tīm cēṟu viḷinta maṇi niṟa maṭṭam
  • {PATI 42-13} ōmpā īkiyiṉ vaṇ makiḻ curantu
  • {PATI 42-14} kōṭiyar perum kiḷi vāḻa āṭu iyal
  • {PATI 42-15} uḷi avir kali mā poḻintavi eṇṇiṉ
  • {PATI 42-16} maṉpati maruḷa aracu paṭa kaṭantu
  • {PATI 42-17} muntu viṉi etir vara peṟutal kāṇiyar
  • {PATI 42-18} oḷiṟu nili uyar maruppu ēntiya kaḷiṟu ūrntu
  • {PATI 42-19} māṉa mintaroṭu maṉṉar ētta niṉ
  • {PATI 42-20} tēroṭu cuṟṟam ulaku uṭaṉ mūya
  • {PATI 42-21} mā irum teḷ kaṭal mali tiri puvattu
  • {PATI 42-22} veḷ tali kurūu picir uṭiya
  • {PATI 42-23} taṇ pala varūum puṇariyiṉ pala ē

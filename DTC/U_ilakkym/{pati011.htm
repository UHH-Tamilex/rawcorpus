Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 11-1} varai maruḷ puṇari vāṉ picir uṭaiya
  • {PATI 11-2} vaḷi pāyntu aṭṭa tuḷaṅku irum kamam cūl
  • {PATI 11-3} naḷi irum parappiṉ mā kaṭal muṉṉi
  • {PATI 11-4} aṇaṅku uṭai avuṇar ēmam puṇarkkum
  • {PATI 11-5} cūr uṭai muḻumutal taṭinta pēr icai
  • {PATI 11-6} kaṭum ciṉa viṟal vēḷ kaḷiṟu ūrntāṅku
  • {PATI 11-7} ce vāy eḵkam vilaṅkunar aṟuppa
  • {PATI 11-8} aru niṟam tiṟanta puṇ umiḻ kurutiyiṉ
  • {PATI 11-9} maṇi niṟa irum kaḻi nīr niṟam peyarntu
  • {PATI 11-10} maṉāla kalavai pōla araṇ koṉṟu
  • {PATI 11-11} muraṇ miku ciṟappiṉ uyarnta ūkkalai
  • {PATI 11-12} palar mocintu ōmpiya tiraḷ pūm kaṭampiṉ
  • {PATI 11-13} kaṭi uṭai muḻumutal tumiya ēey
  • {PATI 11-14} veṉṟu eṟi muḻaṅku paṇai ceyta vel pōr
  • {PATI 11-15} nār ari naṟaviṉ āra mārpiṉ
  • {PATI 11-16} pōr aṭu tāṉai cēralāta
  • {PATI 11-17} mārpu mali paim tār ōṭaiyoṭu viḷaṅkum
  • {PATI 11-18} valaṉ uyar maruppiṉ paḻi tīr yāṉai
  • {PATI 11-19} polaṉ aṇi erutta mēl koṇṭu polinta niṉ
  • {PATI 11-20} palar pukaḻ celvam iṉitu kaṇṭikum ē
  • {PATI 11-21} kavir tatai cilampil tuñcum kavari
  • {PATI 11-22} parantu ilaṅku aruviyoṭu narantam kaṉavum
  • {PATI 11-23} āriyar tuvaṉṟiya pēr icai imayam
  • {PATI 11-24} teṉ am kumariyoṭu āyiṭai
  • {PATI 11-25} maṉ mīkkūṟunar maṟam tapa kaṭantu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. How will gossip cease[FNote_1271], friend? By the great sea,
  2. I have seen, didn't I, the rattling, quick chariot
  3. which passed by without stopping,
  4. although(?)[FNote_1272] the charioteer restrained [it] in the wide ghat smelling of fish?
  5. On midday it was visible[FNote_1273] for all the attendants,
  6. plucking lustrous blossoms joined with pollen
  7. of the Puṉṉai tree which hung down into the rising white sands.

  • {PATI 59-3} paṉi curam paṭarum pāṇmakaṉ uvappa

  • {PATI 59-5} pāy iruḷ nīṅka pal katir parappi

  • {PATI 59-8} ulakam tāṅkiya mēmpaṭu kaṟpiṉ

  • {PATI 59-10} celvar celva cērntōrkku araṇam
  • {PATI 59-11} aṟiyātu etirntu tuppil kuṟaiyuṟṟu
  • {PATI 59-12} paṇintu tiṟai tarupa niṉ pakaivar āyiṉ
  • {PATI 59-13} ciṉam cela taṇiyum ō vāḻka niṉ kaṇṇi
  • {PATI 59-14} pal vēṟu vakaiya naṉam talai īṇṭiya
  • {PATI 59-15} malaiya um kaṭala um paṇṇiyam pakukkum
  • {PATI 59-16} āṟu muṭṭuṟāatu aṟam purintu oḻukum
  • {PATI 59-17} nāṭal cāṉṟa tuppiṉ paṇai tōḷ
  • {PATI 59-18} pāṭu cāl nal kalam tarūum
  • {PATI 59-19} nāṭu puṟantarutal niṉakku um ār kaṭaṉ ē

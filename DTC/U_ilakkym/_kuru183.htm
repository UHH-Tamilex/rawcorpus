{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In a time when like us the fresh Laburnum flowers
  2. are fading in the land where he has gone,
  3. does he, our man, also see an Irali stag
  4. with curled horns that, like himself, has left [his] small-headed doe,
  5. in the grassy fields(?)[FNote_736] in the forest region,
  6. where the big twigs full of flowers of the ironwood tree, plin[FNote_737],
  7. appear like tender peacock necks.

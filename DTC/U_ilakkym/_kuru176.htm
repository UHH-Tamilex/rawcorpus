{{Readable Translation}}

  1. For one day he didn't come, for two days he didn't come,
  2. after he had come for many days, repeating humble words,
  3. [and] made my good heart soft -
  4. like honey ripening on the mountain,[FNote_707] he is gone.
  5. My father who is[FNote_708] a prop to [me], where is he?
  6. Like thundering rain[FNote_709] rained on good land
  7. with different fields(?)[FNote_710] my heart cries.

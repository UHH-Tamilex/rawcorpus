{{Readable Translation}}

  • When saying, o you of red jewels[FNote_95] 'we will go
  • a difficult way to a different land, with woodapple for food,
  • where the fresh crops spread like a blanket,
  • while the noses[FNote_96] on the branches perish like balls staying behind after play,
  • of the long, parched woodapple set(?) like Uṭumpu trees
  • with excellent branches possessing roots descending to ...?',
  • you did well [and] you have done a good thing.
  • Men are such as depart for wealth to be made,
  • [their] minds set on action. That`s its[FNote_97] nature.

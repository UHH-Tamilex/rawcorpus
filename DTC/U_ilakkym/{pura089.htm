  • {PU 89-1} iḻi aṇi polinta ēntu kōṭṭu alkul
  • {PU 89-2} maṭavaral uṇ kaṇ vāḷ nutal viṟali
  • {PU 89-3} porunar um uḷar ō num akal tali nāṭṭu eṉa
  • {PU 89-4} viṉaval āṉā poru paṭi vēntu ē
  • {PU 89-5} eṟi kōl añcā araviṉ aṉṉa
  • {PU 89-6} ciṟu val maḷḷar um uḷar ē atu aṉṟu
  • {PU 89-7} potuvil tūṅkum viciyuṟu taṇṇumi
  • {PU 89-8} vaḷi poru teḷ kaṇ kēṭpiṉ
  • {PU 89-9} atu pōr eṉṉum eṉṉi um uḷaṉ ē

  • {PATI 48-1} pim poṉ tāmari pāṇar cūṭṭi
  • {PATI 48-2} oḷ nutal viṟaliyarkku aram pūṭṭi
  • {PATI 48-3} keṭal arum pal pukaḻ nilii nīr pukku
  • {PATI 48-4} kaṭaloṭu uḻanta paṉi tuṟi paratava
  • {PATI 48-5} āṇṭu nīr peṟṟa tāram īṇṭu ivar
  • {PATI 48-6} koḷḷā pāṭaṟku eḷitiṉiṉ īyum
  • {PATI 48-7} kallā vāymiyaṉ ivaṉ eṉa tam tam
  • {PATI 48-8} ki val iḷiyar nēr ki nirippa
  • {PATI 48-9} vaṇaṅkiya cāyal vaṇaṅkā āṇmi
  • {PATI 48-10} muṉi cuṭu kaṉi eri erittaliṉ peritu um
  • {PATI 48-11} itaḻ kaviṉ aḻinta māliyoṭu cāntu pular
  • {PATI 48-12} pal poṟi mārpa niṉ peyar vāḻiyar ō
  • {PATI 48-13} niṉ mali piṟantu niṉ kaṭal maṇṭum
  • {PATI 48-14} mali puṉal nikaḻtarum tīm nīr viḻaviṉ
  • {PATI 48-15} poḻil vati vēṉil pēr eḻil vāḻkki
  • {PATI 48-16} mēvaru cuṟṟamōṭu uṇṭu iṉitu nukarum
  • {PATI 48-17} tīm puṉal āyam āṭum
  • {PATI 48-18} kāñci am perum tuṟi maṇaliṉum pala ē

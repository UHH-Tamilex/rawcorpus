{{Readable Translation}}

  • The shoulders are perishing and, since all the days go by,
  • looking at the road, a long way, while light subsides[FNote_1080],
  • [my] eyes have lessened in sight, and, leaving me,
  • knowledge has become something confusingly different.
  • Pain too is becoming big and evening has come.
  • What will become of me? As for that(?),
  • I don't fear dying. I fear, if I die,
  • if birth becomes something other,
  • will I forget that he [is] my lover?

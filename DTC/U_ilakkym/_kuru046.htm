{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In the country where he has gone,
  2. aren't there, friend, sorrowful evenings and loneliness
  3. - when the birds that stay in the house, wings folded
  4. like the withered flowers of the water-lily,
  5. after picking corn in the front-yard [and] playing while
  6. working through the particles of dung in the village common
  7. roost with their children in a nest beneath the eaves of the roof?

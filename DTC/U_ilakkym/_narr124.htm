{{Metrical text}}

  1. oṉṟil kālai yaṉṟil pōlap
  2. pulampukoṇ ṭuṟaiyum puṉkaṇ vāḻkkai
  3. yāṉumāṟ ṟēṉatu tāṉumvan taṉṟu
  4. nīṅkal vāḻi yaraiya vīṅkai
  5. mukaivī yatiraṉ mōṭṭumaṇa lekkar ((5))
  6. navvi nōṉkuḷam paḻinteṉa veḷḷi
  7. yurukkuṟu koḷkalaṅ kaṭuppa viruppuṟat
  8. teṇṇīrk kumiḻi yiḻitarun
  9. taṇṇīrt tataii niṉṟa poḻutē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • I cannot bear
  • the sorrowful life of staying in the grip of loneliness,
  • as the Aṉṟil bird the time when it is not one [with its mate],
  • and it has come by itself
  • - don't leave, may you live, lord -
  • the time that stood(?)[FNote_404], full of cool water,
  • when the clear water descends in bubbles,
  • to arouse longing, as a holding vessel in which silver
  • is molten, because(?)[FNote_405] the wild jasmine in blossoms [and] the mimosa buds
  • on the high sand dune perish under the strong hooves of the doe.

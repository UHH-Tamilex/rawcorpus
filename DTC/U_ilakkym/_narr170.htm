{{Readable Translation}}

  • Modest eyes, pasted tresses, bamboo shoulders,
  • with trickling pure teeth, with thighs joined close together(?),[FNote_552]
  • gathering pretty foliage in [her] garland, she without companion,
  • she came [and] stood for the festival floor to glitter.
  • Rise, let's rise to protect [our] husband!
  • If [he] meets her beauty(?)[FNote_553], what of our being many,
  • as [those who] ran from the one spear
  • of Malaiyaṉ with bright sword who did away with many together
  • in famous Muḷḷūr filled by Āriyar?

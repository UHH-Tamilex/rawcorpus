{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land, where the hill people with [their] pretty hamlets,
  • who have seen a quick-eyed elephant, that quitted [his] companions, approach
  • the millet becoming full by the collyrium [clouds] that had swollen on the nearby mountin, roar by [their] outer homes(?)[FNote_358], some with arrows,
  • with Kiṇi drums, with slings worn in the hand, some making noise,
  • enmity of old and separation [are] unpleasant.(?)[FNote_359]
  • How could you let go of [her] attachment,
  • so that the sunlike(?)[FNote_360] brilliance faded on the forehead
  • of the girl with pleasing smile and budlike shining teeth?
  • Ah, I ache.

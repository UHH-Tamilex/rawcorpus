{{Readable Translation}}

  • If only [we] could get someone going to reach [her and] saying,
  • to the little woman with big shoulders [and] hips with beauty spots,
  • rin eyes of eminent grace like the blue water-lily,
  • darkness, a seat of beauty like the tubular round stem,
  • stripped of bark, of the white lily growing in the water:
  • 'we have come', - she would be very bewildered,[FNote_21]
  • with many dark sprouting tresses,
  • fragrant of the forest of strong-bowed Ōri,
  • where the bent-nosed ripe fruit of the Kumiḻ tree by the road
  • becomes the food for the inexperienced animal that tosses [it about],
  • without resenting [it][FNote_22], she who doesn`t say 'who is he?'

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. For the tryst with the one who comes at night
  2. when a thick stone strewn with blossoms of the black-trunked Vēṅki
  3. appears like a big tiger cub, in the middle of the wilderness,
  4. to meet the one coming at night
  5. you are no good[FNote_206], long white moonlight!

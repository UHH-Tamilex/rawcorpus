{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Which is [your] small hamlet with a fence of big stones,
  • lying in the great mountains where the waterfall roars,
  • where the red cow with calf feeds on the big fruit
  • with rich pulp that hangs down to the root of the jack-tree
  • on the village common where the calf is bound by [its] leg [and] drinks
  • fine water in a beneighboured bambo-rustling thicket?"
  • If you don't speak, even if spoke to
  • - is the watch of the field your's too, where they pluck
  • the small millet with abundant ears on which red lustre has played[FNote_677]
  • that had risen since rain had descended in great amount, noisily,
  • of you of long shoulder [and] hips with eminent protusions?

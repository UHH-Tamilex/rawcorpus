{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O man from the ghat, where the black-legged white egrets,
  2. who have come to rest after searching the backwaters, are sleeping
  3. with the sound of breaking waves from the great sea,
  4. crowded on the Tāḻai trees on the settling shore,
  5. she with bangles which have loosened from [their] old state here,
  6. my friend, became pale indeed,
  7. since she chased golden-striped crabs with me
  8. in the spotty shade of the Puṉṉai trees with flashing clusters.

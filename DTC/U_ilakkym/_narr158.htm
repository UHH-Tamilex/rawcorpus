{{Metrical text}}

  1. amma vāḻi tōḻi namvayiṉ
  2. yāṉō kāṇē ṉatutāṉ karantē
  3. vantaṉṟu maṉṉō kaṇṇē kaṇṇiṟ
  4. kallatar maṉṉuṅ kāṉkol lummē
  5. kaṉiyiruḷ maṉṉuṅ kaṇkol lummē
  6. viṭarmukic ceṟinta veñciṉa virumpuli ((5))
  7. pukarmuka vēḻam pulampat tākkik
  8. kuruti parukiya koḻuṅkavuṭ kayavāy
  9. vēṅki mutaloṭu tuṭikku
  10. mōṅkumali nāṭaṉ varūu māṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Alas, live, friend, among us(?)[FNote_503]
  • I, on my part, don't see it. It has hidden itself.
  • It has certinly come to the eye? For the eye
  • the forest, where the stony road stays, is killing.
  • The eye, where intense darkness stays, is killing. (?!)[FNote_504]
  • - the path along which the man from a land of high mountins is coming,
  • where the hot-angered big tiger, that keeps to clefted caves,
  • has attacked, for [its female?][FNote_505] to be lonely, the spotted-faced elephant bull
  • [and] wipes [its] deep mouth [and] fleshy(?)[FNote_506] cheek, that have drunken blood,
  • with the base of the Vēṅki tree.

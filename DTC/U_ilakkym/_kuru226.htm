{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Very[FNote_921] good certinly, oh friend,
  2. [was] the forehead, which confuses the mind into saying 'crescent moon',
  3. and the shoulder, which has reached victorious beauty
  4. so that [it] says 'bamboo', and the eyes, which are like flowers[FNote_922]
  5. - before having lughed with the man from the coast of the broad water
  6. with great ghats, where the white flowers of the Tāḻi tree are blossoming
  7. so that it says 'egrets', while the glittering waves dash aginst [it] all through the night.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As the soft-headed calf of a dark she-elephant with yard[wide?] feet
  2. [is] pleasing on a former day,
  3. (walking on the slope to all the huts,)
  4. running about with the short-fingered(?) sons
  5. brought forth by the Kuṟava daughters in a village abounding in toddy,
  6. {--!}[but] on a later day
  7. grazes away on their millet,
  8. [thus] became enmity his jesting play[FNote_1574].

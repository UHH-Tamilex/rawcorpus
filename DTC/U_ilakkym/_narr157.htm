{{Metrical text}}

  1. iruṅkaṇ ñālat tīṇṭutoḻi lutavip
  2. perumpeyal poḻinta vaḻinā ḷamaiyattup
  3. palpoṟi yaraviṉ celpuṟaṅ kaṭuppa
  4. yāṟṟaṟa ṉuṇaṅkiya nāṭpata vēṇi
  5. liṇartutai maāatta puṇarkoyil viḷittoṟu ((5))
  6. namvayi ṉiṉaiyu neñcamoṭu kaimmikak
  7. kēṭṭoṟuṅ kaliḻumāṟ peritē kāṭṭa
  8. kuṟumpoṟai yayala neṭuntāḷ vēṅkai
  9. yampūn tātuk kaṉṉa
  10. nuṇpaḵ ṟitti māa yōḷē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • At the time of subsequent days when great raining has flown,
  • having helped here in the task of the world of vast area,
  • in summer, right on the day(?)[FNote_501] when the water in the river has thinned,
  • resembling the moving back of the many-spotted snake,
  • whenever the Coels call, uniting in the mango-tree dense with clusters,
  • whenever she hears [it] she will weep greatly,
  • exceedingly [vexed](?)[FNote_502], with a heart thinking of us,
  • - the dark one with fine many beauty spots,
  • as if the pollen of pretty flowers had been shed
  • from the long-trunked Vēṅkai trees near the short forest height.

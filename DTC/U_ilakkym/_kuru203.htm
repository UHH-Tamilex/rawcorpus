{{Readable Translation}}

  1. One time, formerly, I was indeed in distress[FNote_812]
  2. for my lord who abandons [me] completely(?)[FNote_813],
  3. like one whose fate is placed in god,[FNote_814]
  4. although there was a way placed for the eye to see,
  5. and he is not from a village the tree-tops of which don't appear[FNote_815],
  6. and he is not from a country cut off by mountins.[FNote_816]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Won't he remember, friend,
  2. he who has traversed[FNote_281] the wilderness of Kaḷḷi trees on scorched ground
  3. where the bright Neem fruit in the curved beak of a parakeet
  4. looks like a coin of a golden ornament,
  5. which the sharp nil with a famous tip[FNote_282] inserts on a new thread?

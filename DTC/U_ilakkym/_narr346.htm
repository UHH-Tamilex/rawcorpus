  1. kuṇakaṭaṉ mukantu kuṭakkēr piruḷit
  2. taṇkār taliiya nilantaṇi kāli
  3. yaracupaki nuvalu marumuṉi yiyavi
  4. ṉaḻinta vēli yaṅkuṭic cīṟū
  5. rāḷiṉ maṉṟat talkuvaḷi yāṭṭat ((5))
  6. tāḷvali yākiya vaṉka ṇirukki
  7. yiṉṟunak kaṉimaṉ pōlā veṉṟu
  8. niṟivuṟu matiyi ṉilaṅkum poṟiyaṉ
  9. peruntaṇ kollic ciṟupacuṅ kuḷavik
  10. kaṭipataṅ kamaḻuṅ kūntaṉ ((10))
  11. maṭamā varivi taṭameṉ ṟōḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Staying by strength, feet having become strong,[FNote_989]
  • while the abiding wind drives over the village common devoid of men,
  • in the the small village with pretty homes [and] ruined fences
  • on the way of the difficult front that tells of the king's enmity,
  • at a time of coolness in the grounds that had been offered cool rin,
  • having drawn [water] from the eastern sea, having risen to the west, having become dark,
  • - today it does not seem as if you smiled
  • at the broad soft shoulders of the youthful dark woman
  • with tresses that are fragrant from the height of scent
  • of the little green wild jasmine from big cool Kolli hill
  • of Poṟiyaṉ who shines as the fullmoon, dily.

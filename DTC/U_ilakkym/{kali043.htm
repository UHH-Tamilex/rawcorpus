Kalittokai

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 43-1} vēṅkai tolaitta veṟi poṟi vāraṇattu
  • {KALI 43-2} ēntu maruppiṉ iṉa vaṇṭu imirpu ūtum
  • {KALI 43-3} cānta marattiṉ iyaṉṟa ulakkaiyāl
  • {KALI 43-4} aivaṉa veḷ nel aṟai ural uḷ peytu iruvām
  • {KALI 43-5} aiyaṉai ēttuvām pōla aṇipeṟṟa
  • {KALI 43-6} mai paṭu ceṉṉi paya malai nāṭaṉai
  • {KALI 43-7} taiyalāy pāṭuvām nām
  • {KALI 43-8} takaiyavar kai ceṟitta tāḷ pōla kāntaḷ
  • {KALI 43-9} mukaiyiṉ mēl tumpi irukkum pakai eṉiṉ
  • {KALI 43-10} kūṟṟam variṉum tolaiyāṉ taṉ naṭṭārkku
  • {KALI 43-11} tōṟṟalai nāṇātōṉ kuṉṟu
  • {KALI 43-12} veruḷpu uṭaṉ nōkki viyal aṟai yūkam
  • {KALI 43-13} iruḷ tūṅku iṟu varai ūrpu iḻipu āṭum
  • {KALI 43-14} varuṭaimāṉ kuḻaviya vaḷa malai nāṭaṉai
  • {KALI 43-15} teruḷa teri iḻāy nī oṉṟu pāṭittai
  • {KALI 43-16} nuṇ poṟi māṉ cevi pōla vetir muḷai
  • {KALI 43-17} kaṇ poti pāḷai kaḻaṉṟu ukum paṇpiṟṟu ē
  • {KALI 43-18} māṟu koṇṭu āṟṟār eṉiṉum piṟar kuṟṟam
  • {KALI 43-19} kūṟutal tēṟṟātōṉ kuṉṟu
  • {KALI 43-20} puṇar nilai vaḷakiṉ kuḷaku amarntu uṇṭa
  • {KALI 43-21} puṇar maruppu eḻil koṇṭa varai purai celaviṉ
  • {KALI 43-22} vayaṅku eḻil yāṉai paya malai nāṭaṉai
  • {KALI 43-23} maṇam nāṟu katuppiṉāy maṟuttu oṉṟu pāṭittai
  • {KALI 43-24} kaṭum kaṇ uḻuvai aṭi pōla vāḻai
  • {KALI 43-25} koṭum kāy kulai toṟūum tūṅkum iṭumpaiyāl
  • {KALI 43-26} iṉmai uraittārkku atu niṟaikkal āṟṟā kāl
  • {KALI 43-27} taṉ mey tuṟappāṉ malai
  • {KALI 43-28} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 43-29} kūṭi avar tiṟam pāṭa eṉ tōḻikku
  • {KALI 43-30} vāṭiya mel tōḷ um vīṅkiṉa
  • {KALI 43-31} āṭu amai veṟpaṉ aḷitta kāl pōṉṟu ē

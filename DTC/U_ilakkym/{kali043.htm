  • {KALI 43-1} vēṅki tolitta veṟi poṟi vāraṇattu
  • {KALI 43-2} ēntu maruppiṉ iṉa vaṇṭu imirpu ūtum
  • {KALI 43-3} cānta marattiṉ iyaṉṟa ulakkiyāl
  • {KALI 43-4} aivaṉa veḷ nel aṟi ural uḷ peytu iruvām
  • {KALI 43-5} aiyaṉi ēttuvām pōla aṇipeṟṟa
  • {KALI 43-6} mi paṭu ceṉṉi paya mali nāṭaṉi
  • {KALI 43-7} tiyalāy pāṭuvām nām
  • {KALI 43-8} takiyavar ki ceṟitta tāḷ pōla kāntaḷ
  • {KALI 43-9} mukiyiṉ mēl tumpi irukkum paki eṉiṉ
  • {KALI 43-10} kūṟṟam variṉum toliyāṉ taṉ naṭṭārkku
  • {KALI 43-11} tōṟṟali nāṇātōṉ kuṉṟu
  • {KALI 43-12} veruḷpu uṭaṉ nōkki viyal aṟi yūkam
  • {KALI 43-13} iruḷ tūṅku iṟu vari ūrpu iḻipu āṭum
  • {KALI 43-14} varuṭimāṉ kuḻaviya vaḷa mali nāṭaṉi
  • {KALI 43-15} teruḷa teri iḻāy nī oṉṟu pāṭitti
  • {KALI 43-16} nuṇ poṟi māṉ cevi pōla vetir muḷi
  • {KALI 43-17} kaṇ poti pāḷi kaḻaṉṟu ukum paṇpiṟṟu ē
  • {KALI 43-18} māṟu koṇṭu āṟṟār eṉiṉum piṟar kuṟṟam
  • {KALI 43-19} kūṟutal tēṟṟātōṉ kuṉṟu
  • {KALI 43-20} puṇar nili vaḷakiṉ kuḷaku amarntu uṇṭa
  • {KALI 43-21} puṇar maruppu eḻil koṇṭa vari puri celaviṉ
  • {KALI 43-22} vayaṅku eḻil yāṉi paya mali nāṭaṉi
  • {KALI 43-23} maṇam nāṟu katuppiṉāy maṟuttu oṉṟu pāṭitti
  • {KALI 43-24} kaṭum kaṇ uḻuvi aṭi pōla vāḻi
  • {KALI 43-25} koṭum kāy kuli toṟūum tūṅkum iṭumpiyāl
  • {KALI 43-26} iṉmi urittārkku atu niṟikkal āṟṟā kāl
  • {KALI 43-27} taṉ mey tuṟappāṉ mali
  • {KALI 43-28} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 43-29} kūṭi avar tiṟam pāṭa eṉ tōḻikku
  • {KALI 43-30} vāṭiya mel tōḷ um vīṅkiṉa
  • {KALI 43-31} āṭu ami veṟpaṉ aḷitta kāl pōṉṟu ē

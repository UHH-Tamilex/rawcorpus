{{Readable Translation}}

  • Playing small [games] when she refuses the command,
  • running through the arbour so that [her] upright(?) old foster-mother
  • with soft white tresses stays behind, [her] motion become weak,
  • leaping forward so that the golden anklets with pearls for tinkling pieces sound,[FNote_364]
  • ... (?), lifting [with the words] "eat"
  • the little stem with flower head, revolving it for a stroke(?),
  • clutching with one hand the broad-beaming golden pot
  • with sweet milk hot to the taste, mixed with honey,
  • - how did she perceive the knowledge and the custom,
  • she who has little strength, who eats [now], refusing the [right] time,
  • what resembles[FNote_365] fine black sand in flowing water,
  • she who doesn't remember the rich boiled rice of [her] father that was given [to her], because the hamlet of the husband she had taken was in drought?

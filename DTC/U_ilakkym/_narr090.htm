{{Metrical text}}

  1. āṭiyal viḻavi ṉaḻuṅkaṉ mūtū
  2. ruṭiyōr paṉmiyiṟ peruṅki tūvā
  3. vaṟaṉil pulatti yellit tōytta
  4. pukarppukark koṇṭa puṉpūṅ kaliṅkamoṭu
  5. vāṭā māli tuyalvara vōṭip ((5))
  6. peruṅkayiṟu nālu mirumpaṉam piṇiyaṟ
  7. pūṅka ṇāya muyaṅka vūṅkā
  8. ḷaḻutaṉaḷ peyaru mañci lōti
  9. nalkūr peṇṭiṉ cilvaḷik kuṟumaka
  10. ḷūca luṟutoḻiṟ pūcaṟ kūṭṭā ((10))
  11. nayaṉiṉ mākkaḷoṭu kuḻīip
  12. payaṉiṉ ṟammaviv vēntuṭi yaviyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Of no use, alas, is this assembly with a king,[FNote_310]
  • crowded with people without longing,[FNote_311]
  • since the small dughter with few bangles of a poor woman
  • does not join in the bustle of the task on the swings,
  • [she with] pretty few hir who moves about(?) crying[FNote_312],
  • not swinging, while [her] flower-eyed companions embrace each other
  • on the fusion of big palmyra leaves that is hanging on big ropes,
  • as the dutiless washerwoman runs, so that [her] unfaded garland
  • comes into swinging, with the cloth with red(?) flowers
  • that had absorbed the paste(?)[FNote_313], soaked over night,
  • [her] big hands not resting in the multitude, examining the dresses,[FNote_314]
  • in the noisy old village in dance festival.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • It is certinly necessary to go to the festival,
  • fastening to obtin adornment on the wonderful broad hips
  • foliage [and] calyxes, while stripping the white water-lilies from the paddy fields
  • that surround Veṇṇi of Kiḷḷi, generous of hand,
  • where the otter awakens from the sleep he keeps by the pond
  • dily, while sword-like Vāḷi fish are changing?
  • If the man from the fertile village sees [us],
  • it [is] difficult not to marry [us]. If he marries,
  • many [will be] the good shoulder getting lost, friend,
  • pitiable ones that resemble the bamboo on the mountin
  • of Muṭiyaṉ with true words [and] mountin-like elephants.

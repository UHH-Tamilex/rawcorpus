{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Gossip rose in this noisy village
  2. before the heaps reach the dry consistency,
  3. assembled in the dune, [such that it can be] applied to the hir(?)[FNote_1482],
  4. from the river mouth(?)[FNote_1483] with mud [and] fine sand thrown by the sea heads[FNote_1484],
  5. in the seashore grove, where the peaks in flocks are plagued(?)[FNote_1485]
  6. by the long white heaps piled up by the quick wind,
  7. so that with the sprouts the saw-edged Palmyra stems disappear at the head of the Palmyras.(?!)[FNote_1486]

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land of good mountains,
  • where the gold-like fragrant blossoms of the black-trunked Vēṅkai
  • that had opened [their] bud mouths spread on the stony height,
  • on the wide mountain slope with shining white waterfalls
  • on the long peaks, sky-high, on which bamboo has grown thick [and] long,
  • on the mountain-side hung with cold, on which clouds rise [and] tarry,
  • talking abounding in drops with endless sound,
  • you have longed [for her]. Have consideration:
  • you would come at midnight,
  • knowing that fierce animals roam [there], on the
  • small path glittering with water, where wanderers have become inactive(?)[FNote_813]. Ah, I ache.

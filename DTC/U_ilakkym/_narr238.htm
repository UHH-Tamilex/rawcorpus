  1. vaṟaṅkola vīnta kāṉattuk kuṟumpūṅ

  3. vaṇṭuvāy tiṟappa viṇṭa piṭava
  4. mālai yanti mālatar naṇṇiya
  5. paruvañ ceyta karuvi māmaḻai ((5))
  6. yavarnilai yaṟimō vīṅkeṉa varutal
  7. cāṉṟōrp puraivatō vaṉṟē māṉṟuṭa
  8. ṉuravuru muraṟu nīriṟ paranta
  9. pāmpuvai maḻuṅka laṉṟiyu māṇṭa
  10. kaṉiyā neñcat tāṉu ((10))
  11. miṉiya vallaniṉ ṉiṭinavil kuralē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O great big rain made in season,
  • situated[FNote_750] on the big road in evening twilight,
  • with Piṭavus that unfolded when the bees opened [their bud-]mouths
  • as a gathering row of women [wearing] garlands
  • with all short flowers in the forest that was perished, afflicted by drought,
  • please find out(?) about his state. Is it great for a noble
  • to come just here[FNote_751]? It is not,
  • - with a voice that utters your thunder, sweet [and] strong,
  • into [his] glorious [but] unmelting heart[FNote_752],
  • besides spoiling the snake's hood, sprawled[FNote_753]
  • in the water when the strong thunder roars, all mixed up together(?).

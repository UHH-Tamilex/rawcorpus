  8. liruntiṟai kūṭiya pacalaikku
  9. maruntupiṟi tiṉmainaṟ kaṟintaṉai ceṉmē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land, where the sweet-voiced clouds,
  • from which the dwindling drops have flown
  • without washing[FNote_778] the red horn of the elephant that killed,
  • [its] strength having greatly risen, along with [its] vigour happening of old(?)[FNote_779],
  • are playing on the long mountains on peak-height
  • at dawn, having become edged cotton[FNote_780],
  • - you have understood well that there is no other remedy
  • for the pallor that met the joints, having been [first only][FNote_781]
  • on the good forehead of my friend who follows your way,
  • even if you don't grant [your presence], even if you act unlongingly,
  • go!

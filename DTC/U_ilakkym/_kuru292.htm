{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Mother, who, like a village facing the enemy, doesn't sleep at all[FNote_1197]
  2. becuse a guest with smiling face has come one day
  3. - may she forever go to hell
  4. like Naṉṉaṉ, who wanted the murder of a woman,
  5. without taking, even when [they] offered [him], a gold-made image
  6. of her full weight together with nine [times] nine elephant bulls,
  7. [becuse of] her error that the young woman with bright forehead,
  8. who had gone to bath, had eaten a green unripe fruit given by the flood.[FNote_1198]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. May mother obtain the world of great name
  2. [for her] to have rare food of ambrosia, hard to obtain,
  3. she who said: he will come[FNote_340], the man from the high mountain
  4. with jackfruit trees hung with sweet fruit
  5. on every twig, eaten like one's own in one's own house.

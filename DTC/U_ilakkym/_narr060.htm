{{Metrical text}}

  1. malaikaṇ ṭaṉṉa nilaipuṇar nivappiṟ
  2. peruneṟ palkūṭ ṭerumai yuḻava
  3. kaṇpaṭai peṟātu taṇpular viṭiyaṟ
  4. karuṅkaṇ varāaṟ peruntaṭi miḷirvaiyoṭu
  5. pukarvai yaricip pommaṟ peruñcōṟu ((5))
  6. kavarpaṭu kaiyai kaḻuma mānti
  7. nīruṟuc ceṟuvi ṉāṟumuṭi yaḻuttaniṉ
  8. ṉaṭuna roṭunī cēṟi yāyiṉvaṇ
  9. cāyu neytalu mōmpumati yemmiṉ
  10. māyiruṅ kūntaṉ maṭantai ((10))
  11. kāyvaḷaik kūṭṭu maṇiyumā ravaiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O ploughman with [your] buffalos by the granary of great rice,
  • of a height combining with [that of] the base as if one saw a mountain,
  • at cool dawning dawn where one doesn't obtain sleep,
  • o you with a hand that happens to(?) seize
  • along with the turning abundance(?) of the big flesh of the black-eyed murrel
  • the great cooked rice thick with grains [and] sharpness in spots(?),
  • if you, having fed on fault(?), go with your (?)
  • who have put down [their] fragrant bundles in the water-rich fields,
  • beware of the blue water-lilies that are strongly emaciated[FNote_227].
  • In our house those indeed adorn, in assistance with shining bangles,
  • the girls with long dark tresses.

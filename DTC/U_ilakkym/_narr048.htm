{{Readable Translation}}

  • Being such on that day, are they [still] spinning
  • today, as if inside our eyes?
  • - the wild places where you were concealed when our people occurred in
  • between, having left so that ...?
  • without fearing those whose occupation are the sharp, sounding arrows,
  • the bold ones with armlets in a row clinking 'kiṭiṉ'
  • in the middle of the wilderness fragrant with flowers which are taken as adornment of the woodland,
  • appearing, when one thinks [of them](?)[FNote_189] like stars at dawn,
  • the flowers with parasol of soft petals of the low-petalled(?) Kōṅku tree.{X}

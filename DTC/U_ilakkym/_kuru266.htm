{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if he didn't tell us a thing[FNote_1080],
  2. did he forget the message in a bird's mouth[FNote_1081],
  3. for the Vēṅki tree in the garden which was the one dear companion
  4. to him in undear night, he who was able to abandon [us],
  5. (after joining with [my] bamboo shoulders [that are] difficult to forget)?

Paripāṭal 7

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 7-1} tiri irum paṉi puvam cevvitu ā aṟa mukantu
  • {PARI 7-2} ura urum uṭaṉṟu ārppa ūr poṟi koḷḷātu
  • {PARI 7-3} kari uṭi kuḷameṉa kaḻaṉṟu vāṉ vayiṟu aḻipu
  • {PARI 7-4} vari vari toṭutta vayaṅku veḷ aruvi
  • {PARI 7-5} iravu iruḷ pakal āka iṭam aritu celavu eṉṉātu
  • {PARI 7-6} valaṉ iraṅku muraciṉ teṉṉavar uḷḷiya
  • {PARI 7-7} nilaṉ uṟa nimir tāṉi neṭu niri nivappu aṉṉa
  • {PARI 7-8} peyalāṉ polintu perum puṉal pala nanta
  • {PARI 7-9} nalaṉ nanta nāṭu aṇi nanta pulaṉ nanta
  • {PARI 7-10} vantaṉṟu viyi puṉal
  • {PARI 7-11} naḷi irum cōli narantam tāay
  • {PARI 7-12} oḷir ciṉi vēṅki virinta iṇar utiraloṭu
  • {PARI 7-13} tuḷiyiṉ uḻanta tōyvu arum cimi toṟum
  • {PARI 7-14} vaḷi vāṅku ciṉiya mā maram vēr kīṇṭu
  • {PARI 7-15} uyarntuḻi uḷḷaṉa payampu iṭi parappi
  • {PARI 7-16} uḻavar kaḷi tūṅka muḻavu paṇi murala
  • {PARI 7-17} āṭal aṟiyā arivi pōla um
  • {PARI 7-18} ūṭal aṟiyā uvakiyaḷ pōla um
  • {PARI 7-19} vēṇṭu vaḻi naṭantu tāṅku taṭi porutu
  • {PARI 7-20} viti āṟṟāṉ ākkiya mey kalavi pōla
  • {PARI 7-21} potu nāṟṟam uḷ uḷ karantu putu nāṟṟam
  • {PARI 7-22} ceykiṉṟu ē ce pūm puṉal
  • {PARI 7-23} kaviḻnta puṉaliṉ kayam taṇ kaḻunīr
  • {PARI 7-24} aviḻnta malar mītuṟṟeṉa oru cār
  • {PARI 7-25} mātar maṭa nallār maṇaliṉ eḻutiya
  • {PARI 7-26} pāvi citittatu eṉa aḻa oru cār
  • {PARI 7-27} aka vayal iḷa nel arikāl cūṭu
  • {PARI 7-28} toku puṉal paranteṉa tuṭi paṭa oru cār
  • {PARI 7-29} ōtam cuṟṟiyatu ūr eṉa oru cār
  • {PARI 7-30} kār tūmpu aṟṟatu vāṉ eṉa oru cār
  • {PARI 7-31} pāṭuvār pākkam koṇṭeṉa
  • {PARI 7-32} āṭuvār cēri aṭinteṉa
  • {PARI 7-33} kaḻaṉi vantu kāl kōtteṉa
  • {PARI 7-34} paḻaṉa vāḷi pāḷi uṇṭeṉa
  • {PARI 7-35} vittu iṭu pulam mēṭu āyiṟṟeṉa
  • {PARI 7-36} uṇartta uṇarā oḷ iḻi mātari
  • {PARI 7-37} puṇarttiya iccattu perukkattiṉ tuṉintu
  • {PARI 7-38} ciṉi vaḷar vāḷiyiṉ kiḷiyoṭu keḻīi
  • {PARI 7-39} paḻaṉa uḻavar pāy puṉal parattantu
  • {PARI 7-40} iṟu vari puriyum āṟu iru kari ēmattu
  • {PARI 7-41} vari puri uruviṉ nuri pala cumantu
  • {PARI 7-42} pū vēyntu poḻil parantu
  • {PARI 7-43} tuṉintu āṭuvār āy kōtiyar
  • {PARI 7-44} alar taṇ tāravar kātil
  • {PARI 7-45} taḷir cerīi kaṇṇi paṟittu
  • {PARI 7-46} ki vaḷi āḻi toyyakam puṉi tukil
  • {PARI 7-47} mēkali kāñci vākuvalayam
  • {PARI 7-48} ellām kavarum iyalpiṟṟu āy teṉṉavaṉ
  • {PARI 7-49} oṉṉār uṭi pulam pukkaṟṟu āl māṟu aṭṭa
  • {PARI 7-50} tāṉiyāṉ viyi vaṉappu
  • {PARI 7-51} purinta takiyiṉāṉ yāṟu āṭuvār uḷ
  • {PARI 7-52} turantu puṉal tūva tū malar kaṇkaḷ
  • {PARI 7-53} amintaṉa āṅkaṇ avar uḷ orutti
  • {PARI 7-54} ki putiiya vaḷi
  • {PARI 7-55} ē kaḻuttu nāṇāṉ karumpiṉ aṇi mel tōḷ
  • {PARI 7-56} pōkki ciṟi piṭittāḷ ōr poṉ am kompu
  • {PARI 7-57} parintu avaḷi ki piṇi nīkkuvāṉ pāyvāḷ
  • {PARI 7-58} irumpu īr vaṭi ottu mi viḷaṅkum kaṇ oḷiyāl
  • {PARI 7-59} cemmi putu puṉal ceṉṟu iruḷ āyiṟṟu ē
  • {PARI 7-60} viyi perukku vaṭivu
  • {PARI 7-61} virumpiya īraṇi mey īram tīra
  • {PARI 7-62} curumpu ārkkum cūr naṟā ēntiṉāḷ kaṇ neytal
  • {PARI 7-63} pēr makiḻ ceyyum peru naṟā pēṇiya ē
  • {PARI 7-64} kūr naṟā ārntavaḷ kaṇ
  • {PARI 7-65} kaṇ iyal kaṇṭu ētti kāriki nīr nōkkiṉi
  • {PARI 7-66} pāṇ ātarittu pala pāṭa a pāṭṭu
  • {PARI 7-67} pēṇātu orutti pētuṟa āyiṭi
  • {PARI 7-68} eṉṉi varuvatu eṉakku eṉṟu iṉiyā
  • {PARI 7-69} nal ñemar mārpaṉ naṭukkuṟa naṇṇi
  • {PARI 7-70} ciki kiṭanta ūṭaliṉ ce kaṇ cēppu ūra
  • {PARI 7-71} vaki toṭarnta āṭal uḷ nallavar tam uḷ
  • {PARI 7-72} paki toṭarntu kōti pariyūu naṉi vekuṇṭu
  • {PARI 7-73} yāṟu āṭu mēṉi aṇi kaṇṭa taṉ aṉpaṉ
  • {PARI 7-74} cēṟu āṭu mēṉi tiru nilattu uyppa ciram mitittu
  • {PARI 7-75} tīrvu ilātu āka ceruvuṟṟāḷ ce puṉal
  • {PARI 7-76} ūr uṭaṉ āṭum kaṭi
  • {PARI 7-77} puri narampu iṉ koḷi pukal pāli ēḻu um
  • {PARI 7-78} eḻūu puṇar yāḻ um ici um kūṭa
  • {PARI 7-79} kuḻal aḷantu niṟpa muḻavu eḻuntu ārppa
  • {PARI 7-80} maṉ makaḷir ceṉṉiyar āṭal toṭaṅka
  • {PARI 7-81} porutu iḻi vār puṉal poṟpu aḵtu
  • {PARI 7-82} urum iṭi cērnta muḻakkam puriyum
  • {PARI 7-83} tirumarutamuṉṟuṟi cēr puṉal kaṇ tuyppār
  • {PARI 7-84} tāmam tali puṉi pēem nīr viyi
  • {PARI 7-85} niṉ payam pāṭi viṭivuṟṟu ēmākka
  • {PARI 7-86} niṉ paṭintu nīṅkāmi iṉṟu puṇarnteṉa ē

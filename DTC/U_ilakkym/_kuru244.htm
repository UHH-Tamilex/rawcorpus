{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Not that we didn't hear the night attempt on the door,
  2. at deepest midnight when many sleep,
  3. - [you] having come like a strong elephant bull - we did hear[FNote_992], great man.
  4. Oh, my dutiful[FNote_993] mother who embraces [us] every time we were troubled
  5. like a good peacock cught in a net,
  6. so that [its] feathers thin [and its] crest(?) is ruined.

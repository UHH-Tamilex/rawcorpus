{{Metrical text}}

  1. ciṟippuṟamākat tōḻi ceṟippaṟivuṟīi varivu kaṭāyatu.
  3. tōṭami ceṟippi ṉiḷaṅkuvaḷi ñekiḻak
  4. kōṭēn talku lavvari vāṭa
  5. naṉṉutaṟ pāya paṭarmali yarunōy
  6. kātalaṉ ṟantami yaṟiyā tuṇartta
  7. vaṇaṅkuṟu kaḻaṅkiṉ mutuvāy vēlaṉ ((5))
  8. kiḷaviṟ ṟaṇiyi ṉaṉṟumaṉ cāra
  9. lakilcuṭu kāṉava ṉuvalcuṭu kamaḻpuki
  10. yāṭumaḻi maṅkuliṉ maṟikku
  11. nāṭukeḻu veṟpaṉō ṭamintanan toṭarpē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Good, certinly, if by the word
  • of the spear-bearer of ancient truth, with awe-inspiring(?)[FNote_872] molucca beans,
  • so that he informs without knowing it of [our] lover's having given ]it],
  • the difficult pin abounding in affliction would be alleviated,
  • while [pallor] spreads on [my] good forehead,
  • while those lines are fading on [my] hip with eminent rounds,
  • while tight(?)[FNote_873] shining bangles attached in a group become lose
  • - becuse of our attachment that was settled with the man from a land of mountins, where the fragrant smoke of foliage being burned by the forest man
  • who burns eagelwood hides the slope as a dancing rin cloud.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • May they revive[FNote_529] - the pitiful ones are indeed toiling[FNote_530] daily,
  • much [and] of old(?), just as my heart today,
  • while going [and] coming, mounting the sand peakes covered with moonlight,
  • while the bells, joined(?)[FNote_531] in groups are sounding musically(?)[FNote_532],
  • 'kal', without telling day from night,
  • with unending cold wind drawing fine sand [and] dust.
  • Pitiful [are] they.
  • - the horses of the man from the ghat by the Kaitai grove,
  • where the solitary bright Kaitai tree among(?) the blue-coloured Punnai-trees by the great sea(?)[FNote_533] appears like the dawn beauty
  • of the sun, fiery inside, rising [and] growing hot with rays
  • of long glow, glowing brightness that submerged the sky.[FNote_534]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He with the bow, anklets on [his] legs. And she with armlets,
  2. tinkling rings(?)[FNote_32] over [her] tender feet. Good ones,
  3. who [are they]? Pitiable they,
  4. approaching the rustling bamboo thicket,
  5. where, like the rope-dance drums of the Āriyar,
  6. stirred up by the wind white Sirissa nuts are sounding.

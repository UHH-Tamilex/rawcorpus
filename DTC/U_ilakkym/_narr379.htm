{{Readable Translation}}

  • The eyes that cried, for [their] drawn grace to be wasted,
  • of the creeper woman, when the untaught strong young one of the red-headed
  • she-monkey snatched the sweet milk with honey
  • from [her] hands, not going from the frontyard situated near the hills,
  • joining the low(?) branches of the Vēṅkai tree
  • with clusters full of blossoms as if burning with flames,
  • - they were like blue [lotuses] subjected to the rain
  • that blossomed while the pretty ditches became wet in the shower
  • near Kuṭantai of the Cōḻar generous with chariots. The fingers,
  • turned red they were like the rich buds of the Malabar lily
  • that flowered on the high dark mountain side
  • of Potiyil high with peaks that are covered by playing rain,
  • unendingly slapping [her] stomach, widely(?).

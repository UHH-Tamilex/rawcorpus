{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The man from mountins of abundant stones(?)[FNote_1073], where petals have opened [their] bonds,
  2. giving way(?)[FNote_1074] like people knowing [their] duty
  3. who have seen noble ones of an impartiality known to themselves(?)[FNote_1075],
  4. even before, at a time when bees open [their] mouths,
  5. unguardable(?)[FNote_1076], on the rich buds of the Malabar lily,
  6. he has a good heart, friend.[FNote_1077]
  7. When I told him about your state
  8. he himself was ashamed: 'this is no way.'

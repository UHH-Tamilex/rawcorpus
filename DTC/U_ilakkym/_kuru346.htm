{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The man from a land, friend, where a young elephant bull with sprouting[FNote_1391]
  2. tusks, who longed for the young female, [usually] staying in the hills(?)[FNote_1392]
  3. crosses the village common, so that the hill people shout,
  4. - giving a water-lily chaplet of flowers from the mountin pool
  5. and scaring away the parakeets falling into[FNote_1393] the millet field
  6. he came in time(?)[FNote_1394] [and] at evening time
  7. he longed in [his] good mind, became weak himself
  8. [and] even without[FNote_1395] speaking he [was] dejected.

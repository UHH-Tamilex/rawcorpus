{{Readable Translation}}

  1. After [he] said: 'if we speak [to her and] go[FNote_825], going will be difficult!'[FNote_826],
  2. many [are] those with affection for us who have heard that he has gone[FNote_827],
  3. set out[FNote_828] to imprint the good foot
  4. on the small way, frequented of old, in the neighbourhood of stony mountains,
  5. where the clear sound filled with loneliness[FNote_829] of the kite without flock,
  6. perched[FNote_830] on a twig of the Ōmai tree on the road,
  7. becomes a companion in suffering for the people going through the desert.

  • {PU 136-20} emakku īvōr piṟarkku īvōr
  • {PU 136-21} piṟarkku īvōr tamakku īpa eṉa
  • {PU 136-22} aṉittu urittaṉaṉ yāṉ āka
  • {PU 136-23} niṉakku ottatu nī nāṭi
  • {PU 136-24} nalkiṉi viṭumati paricil alkal um
  • {PU 136-25} taṇ puṉal vāyil tuṟiyūr muṉtuṟi
  • {PU 136-26} nuṇ pala maṇaliṉum ētti
  • {PU 136-27} uṇkuvam peruma nī nalkiya vaḷaṉ ē

  1. amma vāḻi tōḻi kaimmāṟu
  2. yātucey vāṅko ṟāmē kayavāyk
  3. kaṉṟuṭai maruṅkiṟ piṭipuṇarn tiyalum
  4. valaṉuyar maruppi ṉilanīrt taṭakkai
  5. yaṇṇal yāṉaik kaṉṟiyuṅ kaṉmicait ((5))
  6. taṉinilai yitaṇam pulampap pōki
  7. mantiyu maṟiyā marampayi loruciṟaik
  8. kuṉṟa veṟpaṉoṭu nāmviḷai yāṭa
  9. virumpukavar koṇṭa vēṉaṟ
  10. peruṅkural koḷḷāc ciṟupacuṅ kiḷikkē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Alas, live, friend -
  • what can we do in exchange
  • for the small green parakeet that didn't take the big ear
  • of the millet which arouses[FNote_616] desire,
  • while we were playing[FNote_617] with the man from the hilly mountains
  • on a site dense with trees not even known to the she-monkey,
  • being gone for the solitary standing platform to be lonely on stony height,
  • and besides for the excellent elephant
  • with fresh water in [his] long trunk, with strong upright tusks, that moves
  • forward together with [his] female which has at [her] side a soft-mouthed calf.

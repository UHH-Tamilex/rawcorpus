{{Readable Translation}}

  • As if with a heavy sigh, since [their] rare stronghold has been harassed,
  • people proceed to other places, while the drummer sounds the big
  • Tuṭi drum, going mounted on [their] mountain animals,
  • it has sent [me away], o heart
  • - the delightful innocent glance from the water-lily collyrium eyes
  • of the little woman, [her] hip with beauty-spots,
  • forehead bright, tightened on [her] forearm wide armlets:(?)[FNote_276]
  • fine light bangles ...(?)[FNote_277], cut with a saw(?)[FNote_278]
  • by the skilful [artisan] in the little village with pretty streets ...(?)[FNote_279],
  • where she in the house sleeps at night to the sound
  • of the sounding white waterfall, in the frontyard with pulp
  • from the jack-trees with rich yield hanging in every branch
  • down to the red roots.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The sky, grumbling with loud-voiced thunder,
  • for waters in mountin pools to swell, in the fearful vast area,
  • for waterfalls to roar on the side of the big mountin,
  • for the bamboo-hiding[FNote_24] flood to roar, shaking the wilderness,
  • in the quickly coming forest river, which descends hitting stones,
  • - just now it flashes indeed, friend, so that it rins
  • on the whithering great wilderness of small-leaved sandal trees,
  • where the elephant with lined forehead, that has eaten white rice[FNote_25],
  • sleeps on the cool fragrant mountin side.

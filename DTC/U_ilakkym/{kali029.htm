Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 29-1} tol eḻil varittu aṉṟi vayavu nōy nalitaliṉ
  • {KALI 29-2} allāntār alavuṟa īṉṟavaḷ kiṭakki pōl
  • {KALI 29-3} pal payam utaviya pacumi tīr akal ñālam
  • {KALI 29-4} pulliya puṉiṟu orīi putu nalam ērtara
  • {KALI 29-5} vaḷiyavar vaṇṭal pōl vār maṇal vaṭu koḷa
  • {KALI 29-6} iḷiyavar aimpāl pōl ekkar pōḻntu aṟal vāra
  • {KALI 29-7} mā īṉṟa taḷir mici māyavaḷ titali pōl
  • {KALI 29-8} āy itaḻ pal malar aiya koṅku uṟittara
  • {KALI 29-9} mētaka iḷavēṉil iṟuttanta poḻutiṉ kaṇ
  • {KALI 29-10} cēyār kaṇ ceṉṟa eṉ neñciṉi ciṉ moḻi
  • {KALI 29-11} nī kūṟum varittu aṉṟi niṟuppeṉ maṉ niṟi nīvi
  • {KALI 29-12} vāy viripu paṉi ēṟṟa viravu pal malar tīṇṭi
  • {KALI 29-13} nōy cērnta vikalāṉ vāṭi vantu alittarūum
  • {KALI 29-14} pōḻtu uḷḷār tuṟantār kaṇ puri vāṭum koḷkiyi
  • {KALI 29-15} cūḻpu āṅku ē cuṭar iḻāy karappeṉ maṉ ki nīvi
  • {KALI 29-16} vīḻ katir viṭutta pū viruntu uṇṇum irum tumpi
  • {KALI 29-17} yāḻ koṇṭa imiḻ ici iyal māli alittarūum
  • {KALI 29-18} toṭi nili nekiḻttār kaṇ tōyum eṉ ār uyir
  • {KALI 29-19} vaṭu nīṅku kiḷaviyāy valippeṉ maṉ valippa um
  • {KALI 29-20} neṭu nilā tiṟantu uṇṇa niri itaḻ vāyviṭṭa
  • {KALI 29-21} kaṭi malar kamaḻ nāṟṟam kaṅkul vantu alittarūum
  • {KALI 29-22} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 29-23} varuntiṉi vatinta niṉ vaḷi nīṅka cēy nāṭṭu
  • {KALI 29-24} pirintu cey poruḷ piṇi piṉ nōkkātu ēki nam
  • {KALI 29-25} arum tuyar kaḷiñar vantaṉar
  • {KALI 29-26} tiruntu eyiṟu ilaṅku niṉ tē moḻi paṭarntu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Whether she has consideration or whether she has no consideration,
  • the emotionless woman that moves with a ball
  • over the expanse of moonlit sand, where the long chariot of [her] father
  • wanders, so that [her] large-eyed companions rejoice,
  • with short hir, into which the big cool sandal in small-stemmed bunches,
  • that sprouted when great rin is shed in agitation by the cloud,
  • is combed to obtin with the hand the five parts [of hirstyle] joining in a [certin] way [and] becomes dust that dropped in places where it is dry(?),
  • don't feel disgust, heart, for standing behind [her], greatly heart-broken.
  • There is not other remedy
  • that ends the affliction of difficult misery at all,
  • for the pin I had.

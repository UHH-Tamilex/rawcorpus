{{Metrical text}}

  1. varaiyar nayaviṉar niraiyam pēṇār
  2. koṉṟāṟṟut tuṟanta mākkali ṉaṭupiṇa
  3. ṉiṭumuṭai maruṅkiṟ ṟoṭumiṭam peṟātu
  4. puṉiṟṟunirai katitta poṟiya mutupā
  5. ṟiṟakuputait tiṟṟa paṟaippuṉ ṟūvi ((5))
  6. ceṅkaṇaic ceṟitta vaṉka ṇāṭava
  7. rāṭuko ṇeñcamō ṭatarpārt talku
  8. matta miṟantaṉa rāyiṉun nattuṟan
  9. talkalar vāḻi ṭōḻi yutukkā
  10. ṇiruvicum patira miṉṉik ((10))
  11. karuvi māmaḻai kaṭaṉmukan taṉāvē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The one of limitless longing who does not care for hell,
  • even if he traversed the road,
  • where strong[FNote_955] men who tightened [their] straight arrows
  • with the small(?) feathers from the wing that was severed, quill flapping(?),
  • from the dotted old kite that hastened to [its] row of newly born ones,
  • without getting a place where it would touch the side with the flesh put there
  • from a killed corpse of people abandoned by the ? way
  • stay peering at the route, with a heart that takes it for play,
  • he won't stay [long], live, friend, abandoning us. See there,
  • the [clouds] have drawn rain great in amount from the sea,
  • flashing so that the dark sky shakes.

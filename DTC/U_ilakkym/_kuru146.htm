{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, oh friend, in our village,
  2. have there [once] been those who united the separated?[FNote_588]
  3. There, that [is] an assembly saying 'today [is] a big [day]',
  4. with people saying 'good, good',[FNote_589]
  5. staffs in hand, well-worn clothes on [their] white heads.[FNote_590]

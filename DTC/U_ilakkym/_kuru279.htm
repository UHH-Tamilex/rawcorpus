{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When with every motion, at midnight filled with loneliness[FNote_1144],
  2. the slit-mouthed clear bell sounds, tied to the protruding(?)[FNote_1145] neck
  3. of the night-coloured buffalo, the buffalo[FNote_1146] with twisted horns
  4. - even as this time is arising, doesn't he come,
  5. who doesn't remember the bamboo shoulder with perfect joints
  6. [and] has gone [across] many dark hills
  7. where the big, dark, thick stones the rin has forgotten to wash
  8. seem to glitter like elephants covered in dust?

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. A chariot came [and] went[FNote_995], they say,
  2. solitary, at midday, when the little white crow[FNote_996]
  3. from the shore of the great sea disturbs the green leaves
  4. like elephant ears, [and] stirs the misty backwaters,
  5. There are young and inexperienced
  6. women with lightning jewels in brids coming loose(?)[FNote_997]
  7. - they [have] the better part, with mothers who don't slap [them] -
  8. and others with mothers who, taking that [as a cuse], interrogate [and] slap [them].[FNote_998]

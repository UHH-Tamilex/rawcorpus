{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He with a chest full of red sandal paste from the mountins,
  2. he with a chaplet full of bees on water-lilies, flowers from the mountin pool,
  3. comes at midnight [and] leaves our house.[FNote_1306]
  4. Unwise to come(?)[FNote_1307] [like that], o young woman, [is] the dear companion fit for your chest[FNote_1308].
  5. Therefore(?)[FNote_1309], when the red-eyed dark tiger roars,
  6. having killed the bull so that the wild cows retreat to the village common
  7. [is this] a time for hiding? No!
  8. I open - live, will you [allow it](?)[FNote_1310], mother? - our door.

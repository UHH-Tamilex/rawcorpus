{{Readable Translation}}

  • In sorrowful evening, when a strong-mouthed evil spirit
  • with fingers ugly, as the ripening bunches
  • of the silk-cotton tree in summer, crosses the village common[FNote_261]
  • to eat the offering of flowers in the fertile old village
  • he himself says he will go[FNote_262],
  • so that we[FNote_263] stay behind here, fearing for him.[FNote_264]
  • It is fit for calumny too that the neighbours spread
  • and for pallor that destroys the beauty of my forehead,
  • as if it showed[FNote_265] the brilliance of a flowered garden,
  • where the white birds are sleeping in the fields of red rice,
  • bowing ears on long stems as hir put stright into red lines.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. If [you] were[FNote_372] to become the wife of the man from the village near the cool ghat,{(|_)}{(_|)}
  2. where Keṇṭi fishes in the tank with deep water seize[FNote_373]
  3. the ripe fruit with striped outside(?)[FNote_374] from the reed, [forming] a thicket with creepers[FNote_375],
  4. the affliction of your heart might become much.
  5. As in a village at night, near the terrible front line
  6. of(?) Añci with long chariots [and] swift high elephants,
  7. of generous hand, a cloud that gives unceasingly,
  8. the days that you sleep might become few.

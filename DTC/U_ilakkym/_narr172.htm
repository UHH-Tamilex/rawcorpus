{{Metrical text}}

  1. viḷaiyā ṭāyamoṭu veṇmaṇa laḻutti
  2. maṟantaṉan tuṟanta kāḻmuḷai yakaiya
  3. neypey tīmpāl peytaṉam vaḷarppa
  4. nummiṉuñ ciṟantatu nuvvai yākumeṉa
  5. ṟaṉṉai kūṟiṉaḷ puṉṉaiyatu ciṟappē ((5))
  6. yamma nāṇutu nummoṭu nakaiyē
  7. viruntiṟ pāṇar viḷaricai kaṭuppa
  8. valampuri vāṉkōṭu naralu milaṅkunīrt
  9. tuṟaikeḻu koṇkanī nalki
  10. ṉiṟaipaṭu nīḻal piṟavumā ruḷavē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Mother talked of the distinctiveness of [this] Puṉṉai tree:
  • "this being superior to you it becomes [your] younger sister",
  • when a sprout came out from the core that had been abandoned,
  • forgotten by us[FNote_559] [and] pressed into the white sand, together with the attendants in play,
  • [and] when we made it grow by pouring sweet milk soaked with ghee [on it].
  • Alas, we would be ashamed of smiling[FNote_560] with you [by its side].
  • If you allow it, man from the coast of ghats
  • with shining water, where the right-twisted white conches are sounding
  • as the sounding music of the bards in festival,
  • - there are also other [trees] with a full shade.

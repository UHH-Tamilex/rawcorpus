  • {PU 102-1} erutu ē iḷaiya nukam uṇarā ē
  • {PU 102-2} cakaṭam paṇṭam peritu peytaṉṟu ē
  • {PU 102-3} aval iḻiyiṉum micai ēṟiṉum
  • {PU 102-4} avaṇatu aṟiyunar yār eṉa umaṇar
  • {PU 102-5} kīḻ marattu yātta cēma accu aṉṉa
  • {PU 102-6} icai viḷaṅku kavi kai neṭiyōy tiṅkaḷ
  • {PU 102-7} nāḷ niṟai matiyattu aṉaiyai iruḷ
  • {PU 102-8} yāvaṇatu ō niṉ niḻal vāḻvōrkku ē

  • {PU 260-23} kampamoṭu tuḷaṅkiya ilakkam pōla
  • {PU 260-24} ampoṭu tuḷaṅki āṇṭu oḻintaṉṟu ē

  • {PU 260-27} iṭam piṟar koḷḷā ciṟu vaḻi
  • {PU 260-28} paṭam cey pantar kal micaiyatu ē

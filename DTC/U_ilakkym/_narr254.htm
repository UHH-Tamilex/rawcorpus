  4. colletir peṟāa yāki mellac
  5. celīiya cellu moliyirum parappa ((5))
  6. vumaṇar tanta vuppunoṭi nelli
  7. ṉayiṉi māviṉ ṟarunta nīvīk
  8. kaṇanāṟu peruntoṭi puraḷu mārpiṟ
  9. ṟuṇiyili tamiyi cēkkuvi yalli
  10. nērkaṭ ciṟutaṭi nīriṉ māṟṟi ((10))
  11. vāṉam vēṇṭā vuḻaviṉeṅ
  12. kāṉalañ ciṟukuṭic cērntaṉi celiṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the sounding dark extension that goes to go
  • softly, being one that did not obtin an answer,
  • even when you engaged in play and kicked the coming waves
  • and plucked Aṭumpu in creepers in the white sand that has grown into hills
  • and talked good words free from offence(?) in a pleasing way,
  • - if you go after you have joined the small home in the seashore grove
  • of us whose ploughing does not depend on the sky,
  • changing the water in the small plots of suitable area[FNote_802],
  • you will not rest alone, as one without companion
  • on [your] chest where the big wreath fragrant from groups of blossoms
  • is deranged, you who has not eaten much of [our] food
  • of rice [that is] the price of salt, given by the salt-merchants.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Since he has set out to traverse[FNote_1338] the difficult desert of glittering mountins,
  2. where the summery white flowers of the Iruppi tree in the forest spread,
  3. loosened from [their] stems when shed by the long twigs struck by the wind,
  4. so that the small path wandered by elephant bulls is buried,
  5. [and] sleep has become difficult at midnight, in the rustling darkness
  6. - dew[drops] are easy indeed to the rin-eyes, good blossoms
  7. resembling lustrous blossoms in clear water.

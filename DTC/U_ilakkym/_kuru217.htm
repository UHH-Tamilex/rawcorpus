  1. 'If they said: "go chasing parakeets from the millet", it would be possible at midday too.[FNote_874]
  2. If you come at night, I fear obstacles too.
  3. What do we do against the pain that afflicts us?'
  4. when I spoke thus, he thought of another [thing to do] in such [a state of affairs],[FNote_875]
  5. the man from the land of high mountains, [and] he sighed indeed:
  6. 'desire is [all very] well!' But I,
  7. I said: '[this is] a very old short-cut, and [it is] blame.'

{{Metrical text}}

  1. āymalar maḻaikkaṇ ṭeṇpaṉi yuṟaippavum
  2. vēymaruḷ paṇaittōḷ viṟaliḻai nekiḻavu
  3. mampaṉ mūtū rarava māyiṉuṅ
  4. kuṟuvari yirumpuli yañcik kuṟunaṭaik
  5. kaṉṟuṭai vēḻa niṉṟukāt talku ((5))
  6. māriruḷ kaṭukiya vañcuvaru ciṟuneṟi
  7. vāraṟka tilla tōḻi cāraṟ
  8. kāṉava ṉeyta muḷavumāṉ koḻuṅkuṟai
  9. tēṅkamaḻ katuppiṟ koṭicci makiḻaṅkoṭuṅ
  10. kāntaḷañ ciṟukuṭip pakukku ((10))
  11. mōṅkumalai nāṭaṉiṉ ṉacaiyi ṉāṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even when clear dew drops from [your] rain eyes, choice flowers,
  • even when the victorious jewels on [your] bamboo-like bamboo shoulders become loose, even if the old village resounds in calumny,
  • please let him not come, friend,
  • on the fearful small path he hastened in difficult darkness,
  • where the elephant bull remains standing [and] guarding, with [his] calf
  • of short steps, fearing the short-striped big tiger,
  • - in [his] yearning for you, the man from a land of high mountains,
  • were they distribute in the small Malabar lily hamlet,
  • that gives delight to creeper-women with hair fragrant of honey,
  • the rich flesh of the male porcupine shot by the forest man.

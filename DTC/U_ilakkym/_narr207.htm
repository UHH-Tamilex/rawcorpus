{{Readable Translation}}

  • Unending [is his] coming, the long chariot prepared,
  • noisily, to the village of those who are taking rich fish,
  • in huts with short-eaved [roofs] covered in Muṇṭakam,
  • [and] gardens surrounded by backwaters with mangrove fences.
  • Has he come, crossing the sands heaped like hills,
  • [and now] goes [back again all by] himself? The little daughter
  • with innocent words of the fishermen, re-fashioning the knots [of their nets]
  • with shrunken fibre(?)[FNote_658], because they had thrown up the killing shark,
  • crowding at night with [their] relations, young [and] old,
  • the fate of [their] children, keen on killing,
  • approaching[FNote_659] the ocean where waves rise in the great wind,
  • [their] fishing net and hook tight in hand, has befallen her [too].

  • {KALI 17-5} tol eḻil tolipu ivaḷ tuyar uḻappa tuṟantu nī
  • {KALI 17-6} val viṉi vayakkutal valitti maṉ valippaḷavi
  • {KALI 17-7} nīḷ katir avir mati niṟivu pōl niliyātu
  • {KALI 17-8} nāḷiṉum nekiḻpu ōṭum nalaṉ uṭaṉ niliyum ō
  • {KALI 17-9} āṟṟal nōy aṭa ivaḷ aṇi vāṭa akaṉṟu nī
  • {KALI 17-10} tōṟṟam cāl toku poruḷ muyaṟi maṉ muyalvaḷavi
  • {KALI 17-11} nāṟṟam cāl naḷi poyki aṭi mutir mukiyiṟku
  • {KALI 17-12} kūṟṟu ūḻ pōl kuṟipaṭūum vāḻ nāḷ um niliyum ō
  • {KALI 17-13} vaki eḻil vaṉappu eñca vari pōka valittu nī
  • {KALI 17-14} paki aṟu paya viṉi muyaṟi maṉ muyalvaḷavi
  • {KALI 17-15} taki vaṇṭu putitu uṇṇa tātu aviḻ taṇ pōtiṉ
  • {KALI 17-16} muki vāytta taṭam pōlum iḷami um niliyum ō
  • {KALI 17-17} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 17-18} porunti yāṉ tāṉ vēṭṭa poruḷ vayiṉ niṉinta col
  • {KALI 17-19} tiruntiya yākki uḷ maruttuvaṉ ūṭṭiya
  • {KALI 17-20} maruntu pōl maruntu āki maṉaṉ uvappa
  • {KALI 17-21} perum peyar mīḷi peyarntaṉaṉ celavu ē

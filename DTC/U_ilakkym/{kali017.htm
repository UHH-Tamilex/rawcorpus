Kalittokai

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 17-1} paṭai paṇṇi puṉaiya um pā māṇṭa pala aṇai
  • {KALI 17-2} puṭai peyarntu oṭuṅka um puṟam cēra uyirppa um
  • {KALI 17-3} uṭaiyatu ai evaṉ kol eṉṟu ūṟu aḷantavar vayiṉ
  • {KALI 17-4} naṭai cellāy naṉi ēṅki naṭuṅkal kāṇ naṟu nutāl
  • {KALI 17-5} tol eḻil tolaipu ivaḷ tuyar uḻappa tuṟantu nī
  • {KALI 17-6} val viṉai vayakkutal valitti maṉ valippaḷavai
  • {KALI 17-7} nīḷ katir avir mati niṟaivu pōl nilaiyātu
  • {KALI 17-8} nāḷiṉum nekiḻpu ōṭum nalaṉ uṭaṉ nilaiyum ō
  • {KALI 17-9} āṟṟal nōy aṭa ivaḷ aṇi vāṭa akaṉṟu nī
  • {KALI 17-10} tōṟṟam cāl toku poruḷ muyaṟi maṉ muyalvaḷavai
  • {KALI 17-11} nāṟṟam cāl naḷi poykai aṭai mutir mukaiyiṟku
  • {KALI 17-12} kūṟṟu ūḻ pōl kuṟaipaṭūum vāḻ nāḷ um nilaiyum ō
  • {KALI 17-13} vakai eḻil vaṉappu eñca varai pōka valittu nī
  • {KALI 17-14} pakai aṟu paya viṉai muyaṟi maṉ muyalvaḷavai
  • {KALI 17-15} takai vaṇṭu putitu uṇṇa tātu aviḻ taṇ pōtiṉ
  • {KALI 17-16} mukai vāytta taṭam pōlum iḷamai um nilaiyum ō
  • {KALI 17-17} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 17-18} porunti yāṉ tāṉ vēṭṭa poruḷ vayiṉ niṉainta col
  • {KALI 17-19} tiruntiya yākkai uḷ maruttuvaṉ ūṭṭiya
  • {KALI 17-20} maruntu pōl maruntu āki maṉaṉ uvappa
  • {KALI 17-21} perum peyar mīḷi peyarntaṉaṉ celavu ē

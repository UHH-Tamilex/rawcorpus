{{Readable Translation}}

  1. There, see that![FNote_756] What do I say to this?
  2. If the strange woman[FNote_757] comes here,
  3. who has left [us], having heard the sweet voices
  4. that don't remember those from whom they had separated when they are content[FNote_758] in a [new] match,
  5. [the voices] of the flock of birds, in a dark mass, perched on supporting branches[FNote_759],
  6. [if she comes] we will indeed say: don't touch us,
  7. [and] don't adorn [our] thick hair with buds.

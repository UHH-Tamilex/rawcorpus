{{Readable Translation}}

  • O travellers with high umbrellas brought over [their] tops,
  • while removing hunger with the unripe fruit of the tamarind in the saline soil
  • of the small hamlet where the saltmerchants prosper, that appears high
  • on the unending(?) way full of heaps of gravel,
  • - did we have [this] also before(?!),
  • [this] state of the sweet words of the young woman with perfect jewels,
  • who suffers from her desire to immerse in festival,
  • while [her] bosom becomes wet with overflowing drops of water,
  • [she of] descending dark sapphire[-like] decorated long tresses,
  • afterwards?

{{Metrical text}}

  1. āḻaṉ maṭantai yaḻuṅkuvar celavē
  2. pulippoṟi yaṉṉa puḷḷiyam potumpiṟ
  3. paṉippavar mēynta māyiru maruppiṉ
  4. malartalaik kārā ṉakaṟṟiya taṇṇaṭai
  5. yoṇṭoṭi makaḷi riḻaiyaṇik kūṭṭum ((5))
  6. poṉpaṭu koṇkāṉa naṉṉa ṉaṉṉāṭ
  7. ṭēḻiṟ kuṉṟam peṟiṉum poruḷvayiṉ
  8. yārō pirikiṟ pavarē kuvaḷai
  9. nīrvār nikarmala raṉṉaniṉ
  10. pēramar maḻaikkaṇ ṭeṇpaṉi koḷavē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Don't be overwhelmed, girl, he will refrain from going.
  • Even if he were to get the Ēḻil hill
  • in Naṉṉaṉ's good land in the gold-yielding Koṇkāṉam mountains,
  • where bright-bangled women compund [their] jewel decoration,
  • with cool(?) gait that repelled the flower-headed buffalo cow
  • with big dark horns that grazed on the dewy creepers
  • of the grove dotted like tiger-spots,
  • who will be able to separate [from you] for wealth,
  • so that your big beautiful eyes take clear dew,
  • as lustrous blossoms flowing over with water of the blue water-lily?

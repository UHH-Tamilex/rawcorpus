((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 24-1} neñcu naṭukkuṟa kēṭṭu um kaṭuttu um tām
  • {KALI 24-2} añciyatu āṅku ē aṇaṅku ākum eṉṉum col
  • {KALI 24-3} iṉ tīm kiḷaviyāy vāy maṉṟa niṉ kēḷ
  • {KALI 24-4} putuvatu pal nāḷ um pārāṭṭa yāṉ um
  • {KALI 24-5} itu oṉṟu uṭittu eṉa eṇṇi atu tēra
  • {KALI 24-6} mācu il vaṇ cēkki maṇanta puṇarcci uḷ
  • {KALI 24-7} pāyal koṇṭu eṉ tōḷ kaṉavuvār āy kōl
  • {KALI 24-8} toṭi niri muṉkiyāḷ kiyāṟu koḷḷāḷ
  • {KALI 24-9} kaṭi maṉi kāttu ōmpa valluvaḷ kol ō
  • {KALI 24-10} iṭu maruppu yāṉi ilaṅku tērkku ōṭum
  • {KALI 24-11} neṭu mali ve curam pōki naṭu niṉṟu
  • {KALI 24-12} cey poruḷ muṟṟum aḷavu eṉṟār āy iḻāy
  • {KALI 24-13} tām iṭi koṇṭatu atu āyiṉ tam iṉṟi
  • {KALI 24-14} yām uyir vāḻum matuki ilēm āyiṉ
  • {KALI 24-15} toyyil tuṟantār avar eṉa tam vayiṉ
  • {KALI 24-16} noyyār nuvalum paḻi niṟpa tammoṭu
  • {KALI 24-17} pōyiṉṟu col eṉ uyir

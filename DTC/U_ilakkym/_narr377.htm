{{Metrical text}}

  1. maṭaṉmā vūrntu māli cūṭik
  2. kaṇṇakaṉ vippi ṉāṭu mūru
  3. moṇṇuta larivi nalampā rāṭṭi
  4. paṇṇaṉ mēvala māki yarituṟ
  5. ṟatupiṇi yāka viḷiyalaṅ kollō ((5))
  6. vakaliru vicumpi ṉaravukkuṟip paṭutta
  7. pacuṅkatir matiyat takaṉilāp pōla
  8. vaḷakañ cērnta ciṟunutal
  9. kaḻaṟum mellikku nōyā kiṉṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Won't we perish, since that [is] a fetter,
  • having become(?) difficult, as ones who do not wish to prepare for
  • mounting the palmyra horse, wearing a garland
  • [and] prising the integrity of the young woman of bright forehead
  • in every village, land and region of wide area[FNote_1042]?
  • The small forehead that has joined the hir,
  • as the wide moonlight of the fresh-rayed moon
  • that had been curtiled by the snake in the wide dark sky,
  • has become weakening pin that is murmuring.

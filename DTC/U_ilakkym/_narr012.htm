{{Metrical text}}

  1. viḷampaḻaṅ kamaḻuṅ kamañcūṟ kuḻicip
  2. pācan tiṉṟa tēykāṉ matta
  3. neyteri yiyakkam veḷiṉmutaṉ muḻaṅkum
  4. vaikupular viṭiyaṉ meykarantu taṉkā
  5. lariyamai cilampu kaḻīip paṉmāṇ ((5))
  6. varippuṉai pantoṭu vaiiya celvō
  7. ḷivaikāṇ ṭōṟu nōvar mātō
  8. vaḷiyarō vaḷiyareṉ ṉāyat tōreṉa
  9. nummoṭu varavutā ṉayaravun
  10. taṉvarait taṉṟiyuṅ kaluḻntaṉa kaṇṇē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • When the base of the moving churning stick thunders,[FNote_41] testing the butter,
  • in the milk-mixture(?), [its] stem diminuished, eaten away by the cord,
  • in the pot filled to the brim[FNote_42], smelling of wood-apple,[FNote_43]
  • in the oncoming dawn that remains she would go, body hidden,
  • to unfasten(?)[FNote_44] the anklets, so that the [tinkling] pieces [in them] be still,
  • [and] put [them away] with the ball tied many times[FNote_45] with [cloth-]stripes
  • - [when thinking] 'whenever they see these they will certainly[FNote_46] ache.
  • Ah, pitiable, pitiable they, my companions',
  • even when she immersed herself in coming with you,
  • and in spite of controlling herself [her] eyes cried.

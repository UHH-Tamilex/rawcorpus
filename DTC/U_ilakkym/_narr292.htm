{{Metrical text}}

  1. neṭuṅka ṇārak kalaṅkuciṉi valanta
  2. pacuṅkē ḻiliya naṟuṅkoṭit tamālan
  3. tīntēṉ koḷpavar vāṅkupu pariyum
  4. yāṇar vippiṟ kāṉa meṉṉāy
  5. kaḷiṟuporak karinta kayavāyk kuṇṭukari ((5))
  6. yoḷiṟuvāṉ paḷiṅkoṭu cempoṉ miṉṉuṅ
  7. karuṅkaṟ kāṉyāṟ ṟaruñcuḻi vaḻaṅkuṅ
  8. karāam pēṇā yiravuvariṉ
  9. vāḻē ṉiya mikūr paṉiyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You who don't think of the forest with fertile regions,
  • where the Tamālam in fragrant creepers with green lustrous leaves
  • that encircled the stirring branches of long-eyed sandal
  • is in distress when bent by those who take sweet honey,
  • you who don't care for crocodiles,
  • that wander the difficult whirling of the forest river with black stones,
  • where red gold flashes together with bright white crystals
  • on the deep shore in the depth worn away when the elephant dash along,
  • if you come at night
  • I won't live, sir, in the dark abundant dew.

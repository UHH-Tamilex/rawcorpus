{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The eyes of the one you longed for, o man from a land of big stones
  2. with jackfruit trees which cling to the sides(?)[FNote_1454],
  3. where the waterfall, which resounds[FNote_1455] from the long mountain difficult to approach
  4. gives the rumbling sound of the drum promising coolness[FNote_1456]
  5. - they are unending in dew, having cried without sleeping
  6. day per day, so that [her] conch-cut shining bangles become loose.

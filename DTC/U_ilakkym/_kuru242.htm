{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The girl [is] from a little village[FNote_986]
  2. in the woodlands, fragrant from flowers dripping with water in the shrubs,
  3. so that cool drops drip in the bright-spotted neck
  4. of the forest cockerel with arresting voice.
  5. Even if he goes with a task, sent by the king, to another village,
  6. [his] superior chariot knows nothing of resting [before] coming.[FNote_987]

{{Metrical text}}

  1. kallāk kaṭuva ṉaṭuṅkamuḷ ḷeyiṟṟu
  2. maṭamā manti māṇā vaṉpaṟaḻ
  3. kōṭuya raṭukkat tāṭumaḻi yoḷikkum
  4. peruṅka ṉāṭaṉi yaruḷiṉi yāyi
  5. ṉiṉiyeṉa koḷḷali maṉṉē koṉṉoṉṟu ((5))
  6. kūṟuvaṉ vāḻi tōḻi muṉṉuṟa
  7. nāruṭi nañcat tīram potti
  8. yāṉṟōr ceṉṉeṟi vāḻāc
  9. cāṉṟō ṉātaṉaṟ kaṟintaṉi terimē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If you have consideration for the man from the land of big stones,
  • where the rin[FNote_732] playing on the side of the peak height conceal
  • the thorn-toothed youthful dark she monkey [and its] unglorious(?)[FNote_733]
  • strong young one, so that the ininformed male monkey shivers,
  • you do indeed not take it in right[FNote_734] now. I will
  • tell [you] one thing, live, friend.
  • You have well realised that he is a noble one
  • who does not live according to the path followed by noble men,
  • having buried the pliability of [his] previously loving[FNote_735] heart.
  • Understand [this].[FNote_736]

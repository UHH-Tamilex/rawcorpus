{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Urge, charioteer, the horses, [who are] fast
  2. the way wind [is], before[FNote_1007] evening comes,
  3. when the good Irali stag bounces about with [its] mate
  4. on the way[FNote_1008] [and] feasts on the water coming into the pebbly depression,
  5. so that [the one with] collyrium eyes, opposed carps united in the long water,
  6. escapes anxiety [expressed by] words sweet with understanding[FNote_1009].

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O spear-carrying [priest] with old truth, come in worship of Murukaṉ
  2. beware of getting angry. I have something to ask [you]:
  3. if you, having killed the little lamb [and] smeared her fragrant forehead [with its blood],
  4. bow [and] give [it], together with a gift of a few grins in many different colours,
  5. will [his] chest also eat the offering - [the chest] with a bright garland
  6. of the man from the slope of mountins big as to touch the sky, who plagued [her]?

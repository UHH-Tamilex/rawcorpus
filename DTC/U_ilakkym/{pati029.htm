Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 29-1} aval eṟi ulakki vāḻi cērtti
  • {PATI 29-2} vaḷi ki makaḷir vaḷḷi koyyum
  • {PATI 29-3} muṭanti nelliṉ viḷi vayal paranta
  • {PATI 29-4} taṭam tāḷ nāri iriya ayiri
  • {PATI 29-5} koḻu mīṉ ārkiya maram toṟum kuḻāaliṉ
  • {PATI 29-6} veḷ ki makaḷir veḷ kuruku ōppum
  • {PATI 29-7} aḻiyā viḻaviṉ iḻiyā tivaviṉ
  • {PATI 29-8} vayiriya mākkaḷ paṇ amittu eḻīi
  • {PATI 29-9} maṉṟam naṇṇi maṟuku ciṟi pāṭum
  • {PATI 29-10} akal kaṇ vippiṉ nāṭu maṉ aḷiya
  • {PATI 29-11} viravu vēṟu kūlamoṭu kuruti vēṭṭa
  • {PATI 29-12} mayir puti mā kaṇ kaṭiya kaḻaṟa
  • {PATI 29-13} amar kōḷ nēr ikantu ār eyil kaṭakkum
  • {PATI 29-14} perum pal yāṉi kuṭṭuvaṉ
  • {PATI 29-15} varampu il tāṉi paravā ūṅku ē

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 29-1} aval eṟi ulakkai vāḻai cērtti
  • {PATI 29-2} vaḷai kai makaḷir vaḷḷai koyyum
  • {PATI 29-3} muṭantai nelliṉ viḷai vayal paranta
  • {PATI 29-4} taṭam tāḷ nārai iriya ayirai
  • {PATI 29-5} koḻu mīṉ ārkaiya maram toṟum kuḻāaliṉ
  • {PATI 29-6} veḷ kai makaḷir veḷ kuruku ōppum
  • {PATI 29-7} aḻiyā viḻaviṉ iḻiyā tivaviṉ
  • {PATI 29-8} vayiriya mākkaḷ paṇ amaittu eḻīi
  • {PATI 29-9} maṉṟam naṇṇi maṟuku ciṟai pāṭum
  • {PATI 29-10} akal kaṇ vaippiṉ nāṭu maṉ aḷiya
  • {PATI 29-11} viravu vēṟu kūlamoṭu kuruti vēṭṭa
  • {PATI 29-12} mayir putai mā kaṇ kaṭiya kaḻaṟa
  • {PATI 29-13} amar kōḷ nēr ikantu ār eyil kaṭakkum
  • {PATI 29-14} perum pal yāṉai kuṭṭuvaṉ
  • {PATI 29-15} varampu il tāṉai paravā ūṅku ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Like the union of the Makaṉṟil birds staying in the water,
  2. as if a year had passed even if [only] a flower comes between them,
  3. with desire unchecked for which separation is difficult,
  4. may we go together from life[FNote_239]
  5. so that we live[FNote_240], having become one in sorrow,
  6. having become two in the world, knowing duty.

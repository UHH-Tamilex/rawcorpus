{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The man from the land, where the dancing peacock is calling,
  2. moving forward so that [its] full, long feathers come to swing,
  3. on the crumbling shore of the forest river filled with clamouring rin,
  4. he longed[FNote_1070] with us for the intimacy he had taken.[FNote_1071]
  5. Even at a time when [we] have paled, pallor is impossible. (?)[FNote_1072]

Altern

3-5b {--!}For the intimacy that he who longed for us has taken, the man from the land ..., pallor is impossible, even at a time when [we] have paled.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. 'The one who doesn't see [your] youth, who has gone yearning for wealth,
  2. who also hasn't come here - where [is] he?'
  3. [Thus] lughs, friend, the fragrant cool riny season,
  4. so that on the jasmine, in flower clusters defying[FNote_508] the rin,
  5. gathering buds become shining teeth.

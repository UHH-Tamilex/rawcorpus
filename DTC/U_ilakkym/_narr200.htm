  5. vituvu māṅka ṇuvaṉṟiciṉ mātō ((5))
  6. vāmpa lamaṉṟa tīmperum paḻaṉattup

  9. ceyta vallal palkuva vaiyeyiṟ
  10. ṟaitaka lalkuṉ makaḷirivaṉ ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O potter of old wisdom who talks about [the coming of] a festival
  • in the streets wide [and] long as river gone to rest,
  • wearing the garland of Nocci in bright ears
  • resembling ears of grain tied in a chaplet(?)[FNote_637],
  • shouldn't you talk[FNote_638] there also this,
  • you being one that goes to the village with [its] pond
  • in the sweet big paddy fields, thickly grown with white water-lilies,
  • [that is], "the much-talking bard with [his] stings seized by the hands,
  • o you women with sharp teeth [and beautifully wide hips,
  • beware of his cruel words, a hoard of lies,
  • that are increasing the unhappiness [already] caused."[FNote_639]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if he is content not to come[FNote_475],
  2. the man from the ghat where, like an ox cut loose from the rope
  3. of the herdsmen, the wet crab hastens to go into the hole
  4. of a mangrove root, suffering for fear of the look[FNote_476]
  5. of the crane(?) like a water-lily in the shower
  6. {--!}- I will be content:
  7. With the merchants here there are small bangles for the arms.

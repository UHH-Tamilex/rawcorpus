{{Metrical text}}

  1. āṅkaṉan taṇikuva tāyiṉ yāṅku
  2. mitaṉiṟ koṭiyatu piritoṉ ṟilli
  3. vāykol vāḻi tōḻi vēyuyarn
  4. teṟintuceṟit taṉṉa piṇaṅkaril viṭarmuki
  5. yūṉṟiṉ piṇaviṉ uṭkupaci kaḷiiya ((5))
  6. rāḷiyaṅ karumpuḻi yoṟṟi vāḷvarik
  7. kaṭuṅkaṇ vayappuli yoṭuṅku nāṭaṉ
  8. ṟaṇkamaḻ viyaṉmār puritiṉiṟ peṟātu
  9. naṉṉutaṟ pacanta paṭarmali yarunō
  10. yaṇaṅkeṉa vuṇarak kūṟi vēla ((10))
  11. ṉiṉṉiyaṅ kaṟaṅkap pāṭi
  12. paṉmalar citaṟiya paravuṟu palikkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If it decrease in that way, alltogether
  • there is nothing else as cruel as this.
  • [Is it] true, live, friend?
  • - For the reverential(?) offering when many flowers are scattered,
  • the Vēlaṉ singing while sweet instruments are sounding,
  • declaring so that it is realised "a plague[FNote_940] [is this]
  • sever pin abounding in affliction", when the good forehead has become pale,
  • not obtining in the suitable way the cool fragrant broad chest
  • of the man from a land, where the fierce-eyed strong tiger with bright stripes
  • is hidden, closing in on the forestpath difficult to move on for men,
  • in order to remove the dreadful hunger of [its] flesh-eating female
  • in a cave in the clefts in the thicket intertwining as if the bamboo had
  • grown, cut down [and then] packed close.

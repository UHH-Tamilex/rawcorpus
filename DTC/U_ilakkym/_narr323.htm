{{Metrical text}}

  1. ōṅkit tōṉṟun tīṅkaṭ peṇṇi
  2. naṭuva ṇatuvē teyya maṭavara
  3. lāyamum yāṉu maṟiyā tavaṇa
  4. māya naṭpiṉ māṇala moḻintuniṉ
  5. kiḷimi koṇṭa vaḷiyār muṉki ((5))
  6. nallō ṭanti ciṟukuṭip pākkam
  7. pulivari yekkarp puṉṉi yutirtta
  8. malitā tūtun tēṉō ṭoṉṟi
  9. vaṇṭimi riṉṉici kaṟaṅkat tiṇṭērt
  10. terimaṇi kēṭṭalu maritē ((10))
  11. varumā ṟītavaṇ maṟavā tīmē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • It is in the middle
  • of palmyra palms with sweet toddy that appear high.
  • It will be difficult to hear at all the clear bell
  • of the firm chariot, while the humming sweet sounds of the bees are sounding,
  • becoming one with the honey[FNote_942], filling themselves on the abundant pollen
  • that dropped by the Puṉṉi trees in the tiger-lined dunes
  • by the village with small homes of the father of the good one
  • with forearms full of bangles who has taken up relationship
  • with you, [her] glorious integrity staying behind in the intimacy
  • that came with us there, the companions and I not knowing.[FNote_943]
  • This [is] the way to come there. Don't forget.

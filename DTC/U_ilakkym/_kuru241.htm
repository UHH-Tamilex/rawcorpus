{{Readable Translation}}

  1. We, even while we endure desire, these [our eyes]
  2. have cried for their intimacy
  3. - our eyes which had seen the man from the hills,
  4. where inside the clefts touching the sky
  5. the joyous clamour [re]sounds, taken up(?)[FNote_983], without climbing it,
  6. by the little ones with low heads[FNote_984] leading the calves on the way,
  7. on beholding the ripeness of the blossoms on the Vēṅki tree on the village common.[FNote_985]

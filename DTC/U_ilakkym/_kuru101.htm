{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Weighing both:
  2. the [whole] world surrounded by the broad-waved big sea
  3. and the country of the gods, of an excellence hard to obtin,
  4. {--!}for us they are not equal in worth
  5. to the day that comes and changes the shoulder
  6. of the short girl with hips of grand shape(?),
  7. a body like gold [and] collyrium eyes like flowers[FNote_410].

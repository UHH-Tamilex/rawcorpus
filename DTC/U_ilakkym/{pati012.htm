Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 12-1} vayavar vīḻa vāḷ aril mayakki
  • {PATI 12-2} iṭam kavar kaṭumpiṉ aracu talai paṉippa
  • {PATI 12-3} kaṭampu mutal taṭinta kaṭum ciṉa vēntu ē
  • {PATI 12-4} tār aṇi eruttiṉ vāral vaḷ ukir
  • {PATI 12-5} arimāṉ vaḻaṅkum cāral piṟa māṉ
  • {PATI 12-6} tōṭu koḷ iṉa nirai neñcu atirntāṅku
  • {PATI 12-7} muracu muḻaṅku neṭu nakar aracu tuyilīyātu
  • {PATI 12-8} mātiram paṉikkum maṟam vīṅku pal pukaḻ
  • {PATI 12-9} kēṭṭaṟku iṉitu niṉ celvam kēṭṭoṟum
  • {PATI 12-10} kāṇṭal viruppoṭu kamaḻum kuḷavi
  • {PATI 12-11} vāṭā paim mayir iḷaiya āṭu naṭai
  • {PATI 12-12} aṇṇal maḻa kaḷiṟu ari ñimiṟu ōppum
  • {PATI 12-13} kaṉṟu puṇar piṭiya kuṉṟu pala nīnti
  • {PATI 12-14} vantu avaṇ iṟutta irum pēr okkal
  • {PATI 12-15} tol paci uḻanta paḻaṅkaṇ vīḻa
  • {PATI 12-16} eḵku pōḻntu aṟutta vāl niṇa koḻum kuṟai
  • {PATI 12-17} mai ūṉ peyta veṇṇel veḷ cōṟu
  • {PATI 12-18} naṉai amai kaḷḷiṉ tēṟaloṭu mānti
  • {PATI 12-19} nīr paṭu paruntiṉ irum ciṟaku aṉṉa
  • {PATI 12-20} nilam tiṉ citāar kaḷainta piṉṟai
  • {PATI 12-21} nūlā kaliṅkam vāl arai koḷīi
  • {PATI 12-22} vaṇar irum katuppiṉ vāṅku amai mel tōḷ
  • {PATI 12-23} vacai il makaḷir vayaṅku iḻai aṇiya
  • {PATI 12-24} amarpu mey ārtta cuṟṟamoṭu
  • {PATI 12-25} nukartaṟku iṉitu niṉ perum kali makiḻvu ē

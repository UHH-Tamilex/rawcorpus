  1. muṉṟiṟ palaviṉ paṭucuḷai marīip

  3. maipaṭu mālvarai pāṭiṉaḷ koṭicci
  4. yaivaṉa veṇṇeṟ kuṟūu nāṭanoṭu
  5. cūruṭaic cilampi ṉaruvi yāṭik ((5))
  6. kārarum paviḻnta kaṇivāy vēṅkaip
  7. pāvamai yitaṇa mēṟip pāciṉam
  8. vaṇarkuraṟ ciṟutiṉai kaṭiyap
  9. puṇarvatu kollō nāḷaiyu namakkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Will [there be] uniting, friend, also tomorrow for us,
  • when chasing away from the little millet with curved ears
  • the green groups, having mounted the broad bamboo platform
  • on the Vēṅkai, the mouth of an astrologer, who opened [its] black(?)[FNote_1038] buds,
  • [when] playing in the waterfall on the fearful mountain-side
  • with the man from a land, where the creeper woman, who sang(?)[FNote_1039] of the mountain black with collyrium [clouds] of [her] father, is pounding the white paddy of the wild rice, while the low-headed she-monkey approaches the pulp
  • coming from the jacktree in the frontyard [and] chokes [it] up?

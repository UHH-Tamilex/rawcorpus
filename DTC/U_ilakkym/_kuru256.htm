{{Readable Translation}}

  1. 'We come when work has turned out well,
  2. afterwards[FNote_1033], through the forest where the stag[FNote_1034] bounces about
  3. who has become full even with the doe from the tender twigs with linking stalks(?)[FNote_1035]
  4. of the Aṟuki, the big creepers as if dripping with jewels.[FNote_1036]
  5. Is it possible to endure that much, o you with the flower earring?'
  6. [Even] before [that] was sid - without standing [still],
  7. by crying obstructive[FNote_1037] waters, unending,
  8. they actually hindered the chariot, the eyes of the woman.

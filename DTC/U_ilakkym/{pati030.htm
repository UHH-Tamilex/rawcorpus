Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 30-1} iṇar tatai ñāḻal karai keḻu perum tuṟai
  • {PATI 30-2} maṇi kalattu aṉṉa mā itaḻ neytal
  • {PATI 30-3} pācu aṭai paṉi kaḻi tuḻaii puṉṉai
  • {PATI 30-4} vāl iṇar paṭu ciṉai kuruku iṟaikoḷḷum
  • {PATI 30-5} alkuṟu kāṉal ōṅku maṇal aṭai karai
  • {PATI 30-6} tāḻ aṭumpu malainta puṇari vaḷai ñarala
  • {PATI 30-7} ilaṅku nīr muttamoṭu vār tukir eṭukkum
  • {PATI 30-8} taṇ kaṭal paṭappai meṉpālaṉa um
  • {PATI 30-9} kāntaḷ am kaṇṇi kolai vil vēṭṭuvar
  • {PATI 30-10} ce kōṭṭu āmāṉ ūṉoṭu kāṭṭa
  • {PATI 30-11} mataṉ uṭai vēḻattu veḷ kōṭu koṇṭu
  • {PATI 30-12} poṉ uṭai niyamattu piḻi noṭai koṭukkum
  • {PATI 30-13} kuṉṟu talaimaṇanta puṉpula vaippu um
  • {PATI 30-14} kālam aṉṟi um karumpu aṟuttu oḻiyātu
  • {PATI 30-15} ari kāl avittu pala pū viḻaviṉ
  • {PATI 30-16} tēm pāy marutam mutal paṭa koṉṟu
  • {PATI 30-17} veḷ talai ce puṉal parantu vāy mikukkum
  • {PATI 30-18} pala cūḻ patappar pariya veḷḷattu
  • {PATI 30-19} ciṟai koḷ pūcaliṉ pukaṉṟa āyam
  • {PATI 30-20} muḻavu imiḻ mūtūr viḻavu kāṇūu peyarum
  • {PATI 30-21} ceḻum pal vaippiṉ paḻaṉa pāl um
  • {PATI 30-22} ēṉal uḻavar varaku mītu iṭṭa
  • {PATI 30-23} kāṉ miku kuḷaviya vaṉpu cēr irukkai
  • {PATI 30-24} mel tiṉai nuvaṇai muṟai muṟai pakukkum
  • {PATI 30-25} puṉpulam taḻīiya puṟavu aṇi vaippu um
  • {PATI 30-26} pal pūm cemmal kāṭu payam māṟi
  • {PATI 30-27} arakkattu aṉṉa nuṇ maṇal kōṭu koṇṭu
  • {PATI 30-28} oḷ nutal makaḷir kaḻaloṭu maṟukum
  • {PATI 30-29} viṇ uyarntu ōṅkiya kaṭaṟṟa um piṟa um
  • {PATI 30-30} paṇai keḻu vēntar um vēḷir um oṉṟumoḻintu
  • {PATI 30-31} kaṭala um kāṭṭa um araṇ valiyār naṭuṅka
  • {PATI 30-32} muraṇ miku kaṭum kural vicumpu aṭaipu atira
  • {PATI 30-33} kaṭum ciṉam kaṭāay muḻaṅkum mantirattu
  • {PATI 30-34} arum tiṟal marapiṉ kaṭavuḷ pēṇiyar
  • {PATI 30-35} uyarntōṉ ēntiya arum peṟal piṇṭam
  • {PATI 30-36} karum kaṇ pēy makaḷ kai puṭaiyūu naṭuṅka
  • {PATI 30-37} neyttōr tūuya niṟai makiḻ irum pali
  • {PATI 30-38} eṟumpu um mūcā iṟumpūtu marapiṉ
  • {PATI 30-39} karum kaṇ kākkaiyoṭu paruntu iruntu āra
  • {PATI 30-40} ōṭā pūṭkai oḷ poṟi kaḻal kāl
  • {PATI 30-41} perum camam tatainta ceru pukal maṟavar
  • {PATI 30-42} urumu nilaṉ atirkkum kuraloṭu koḷai puṇarntu
  • {PATI 30-43} perum cōṟu ukuttaṟku eṟiyum
  • {PATI 30-44} kaṭum ciṉa vēntu ē niṉ taḻaṅku kural muracu ē

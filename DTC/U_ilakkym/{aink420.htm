  • {AING 420-1} poṉ eṉa malarnta koṉṟai maṇi eṉa
  • {AING 420-2} tēm paṭu kāyā malarnta tōṉṟiyoṭu
  • {AING 420-3} nal nalam eytiṉai puṟavu ē niṉṉai
  • {AING 420-4} kāṇiya varutum yām ē
  • {AING 420-5} vāḷ nutal arivaiyoṭu āy nalam paṭarntu ē

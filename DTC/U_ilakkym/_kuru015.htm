{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Like the good word of the Kōcars from the four villages,
  2. the intimacy of the girl with many bangles on [her] forearm
  3. with the warrior [carrying] a white spear with red blade [and] choice anklets
  4. has come true, friend,
  5. taking [their] seats while drums are sounding [and] conches are roaring
  6. in the assembly near the age-old(?) banyan tree.

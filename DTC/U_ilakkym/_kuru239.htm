{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The armlets have become loose, the shoulders are emaciated
  2. for the master of the mountin with bamboo hedges,
  3. where appears like a jewel[FNote_975] spit up by a snake
  4. the short-winged bee, filling itself up with fragrant pollen
  5. from the Malabar lily in swaying bunches, all fragrant[FNote_976], on the mountin-side
  6. with clefts [and] caves. Is there [still any] shame to let go of, friend?

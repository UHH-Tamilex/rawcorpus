{{Readable Translation}}

  1. To the victorious Cūli(?) at the mountainside with clefts [and] caves[FNote_878]
  2. we don't take up duty [and] don't bind the thread on [our] hand[FNote_879]
  3. and we don't know about omens and we don't depend on overheard voices(?)
  4. and [still] it is not that we don't think and think[FNote_880], friend,
  5. about him who was able to become still there, having forgotten
  6. us who have not been still for the blink [of an eye]
  7. without him, because he [is] life for [our] life.

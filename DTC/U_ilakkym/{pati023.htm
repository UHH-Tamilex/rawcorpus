Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 23-1} alantali uṉṉattu am kavaṭu porunti
  • {PATI 23-2} citaṭi kariya peru vaṟam kūrntu
  • {PATI 23-3} nilam pitu aṟṟa pulam keṭu kāli um
  • {PATI 23-4} vāṅkupu takitta kala piyar āṅkaṇ
  • {PATI 23-5} maṉṟam pōntu maṟuku ciṟi pāṭum
  • {PATI 23-6} vayiriya mākkaḷ kaṭum paci nīṅka
  • {PATI 23-7} poṉ cey puṉi iḻi olippa peritu uvantu
  • {PATI 23-8} neñcu mali uvakiyar uṇṭu malintu āṭa
  • {PATI 23-9} ciṟu makiḻāṉ um perum kalam vīcum
  • {PATI 23-10} pōr aṭu tāṉi polam tār kuṭṭuva
  • {PATI 23-11} niṉ nayantu varuvēm kaṇṭaṉam pul mikku
  • {PATI 23-12} vaḻaṅkunar aṟṟeṉa maruṅku keṭa tūrntu
  • {PATI 23-13} perum kaviṉ aḻinta āṟṟa ēṟu puṇarntu
  • {PATI 23-14} aṇṇal mariyā amarntu iṉitu uṟiyum
  • {PATI 23-15} viṇ uyar vippiṉa kāṭu āyiṉa niṉ
  • {PATI 23-16} mintu mali perum pukaḻ aṟiyār malinta
  • {PATI 23-17} pōr etir vēntar tār aḻintu orāliṉ
  • {PATI 23-18} marutu imiḻntu ōṅkiya naḷi irum parappiṉ
  • {PATI 23-19} maṇal mali perum tuṟi tatinta kāñciyoṭu
  • {PATI 23-20} murukku tāḻpu eḻiliya neruppu uṟaḻ aṭi kari
  • {PATI 23-21} nantu nāriyoṭu ce vari ukaḷum
  • {PATI 23-22} kaḻaṉi vāyil paḻaṉa paṭappi
  • {PATI 23-23} aḻal maruḷ pūviṉ tāmari vaḷi makaḷ
  • {PATI 23-24} kuṟāatu malarnta āmpal
  • {PATI 23-25} aṟāa yāṇar avar akal tali nāṭu ē

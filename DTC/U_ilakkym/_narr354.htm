{{Readable Translation}}

  • How can one bear that?[FNote_1003]
  • Slandour, o lord, became your intimacy,
  • like the agitation going through the salt-pan
  • of those who see, when they go, the rows of carts on the long path,
  • with stony ripe salt boiled by the fierce sunlight
  • on the drizzling coast[FNote_1004] with boats hanging,
  • linked to the whole base of the good trunks
  • of the Puṉṉi trees coloured dark as night
  • in the frontyards with light sand situated by the seashore grove,
  • bound to(?) a surrounding fence(?) of sheltering palm-leaves that descend
  • from the long dark palmyra palms that cast off(?) swaying trunks
  • in the seashore grove.

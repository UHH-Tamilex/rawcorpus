{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from a land, where the peacock, obtining beauty when it played with the fragrant pollen after stirring the branches with gold-like flowers
  • on the good day of the mountin's decoration given by the Vēṅki tree,
  • enjoys the young sunlight of rays the sun has,
  • with [its] group on the elevation of green rocks,
  • while the clouds, going after rining, join the mountins to be kept [there], while the waterfalls roar on the high mountins hung with honey[-combs],
  • - to whom shall I achingly tell
  • of the difficult pin of distress with which your chest has plagued me?
  • After speaking for many days many[FNote_1077] desirous words,
  • you [are] not considerate [in a way that gives] joy.[FNote_1078] You are changed.[FNote_1079]

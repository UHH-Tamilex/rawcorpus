{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Like white mustard the small-blossomed Ñāḻal
  2. spread together with withered red Arjuna blossoms
  3. adorned[FNote_215] the ghat in his village. Wasted away
  4. so that the shining bangles become loose [and] slip over the joints[FNote_216]
  5. the arms he clung to are adorned by loneliness.

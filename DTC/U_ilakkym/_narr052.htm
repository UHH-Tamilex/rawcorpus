{{Metrical text}}

  1. mākko ṭiyatiraṟ pūvoṭu pātiri
  2. tūttakaṭ ṭetirmalar vēynta kūntaṉ
  3. maṇaṅkamaḻ nāṟṟa marīi yāmivaḷ
  4. cuṇaṅkaṇi yāka maṭaiya muyaṅki
  5. vīṅkuvar kavavi ṉīṅkal cellē ((5))
  6. nīyē
  7. yāḷviṉai ciṟappa veṇṇi nāḷum
  8. pirintuṟai vāḻkkai purintamai yalaiyē
  9. yaṉpiṉai vāḻiyeṉ ṉēñcē vempōr
  10. maḻavar perumakaṉ māvā ḷōri
  11. kaivaḷa miyaiva tāyiṉu ((10))
  12. maitē kamma viyaintucey poruḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • We won`t go, leaving the swelling salty[FNote_202] embraces,
  • we embracing to be joined with the beauty-spot-adorned bosom
  • of her connected with odorous fragrance of perfumes(?)[FNote_203]
  • in [her] tresses covered with alternating blossoms, the pure plates
  • of the trumpet-flower tree with the flowers of the big wild jasmine creeper.
  • You, considering manly work to be superior, daily,
  • you aren`t content, desiring a living of lasting separation,
  • you full of love[FNote_204], live, my heart. Even if
  • there were to enjoy the riches in the hand
  • of Ōri, great son of warriors in hot battle,
  • light would be - you [just] go[FNote_205] - the wealth to be made [and] enjoyed[FNote_206].

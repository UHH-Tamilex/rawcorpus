  4. mērtaru teruvi ṉetircci nōkkiniṉ
  5. mārputalik koṇṭa māṇiḻi makaḷir 5

  7. kālē muṟṟap pitaru kālik

{{Readable Translation}}

  • I saw, o delightful one - and having seen [it], what can I do? -
  • your earlier state of distress, when you were dragged to and fro,
  • like a plank grabbed by many, since they fell,
  • agitated becuse [their] wooden [ship] had been turned upside down in the sea,
  • in sorrow-awakening morning which had confusion: a time
  • of itching dew hotly descending which had confusion from the concern[FNote_128]
  • of women with glorious jewels who took your chest in possession
  • with a look at [their] rivals(?) in the rising(?)[FNote_129] street,
  • where the excellent little lute in the hand of a bard
  • sounds 'im' like the fertile bee.

Naṟṟiṇai

((Originally extracted from master file ^NARRI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {NT 311-1} peyiṉ ē viṭu māṉ uḷaiyiṉ veṟuppa tōṉṟi
  • {NT 311-2} irum katir nelliṉ yāṇaraḵtu ē
  • {NT 311-3} vaṟappiṉ mā nīr muṇṭakam tāay cēṟu pularntu
  • {NT 311-4} irum kaḻi ceṟuviṉ veḷ uppu viḷaiyum
  • {NT 311-5} aḻiyā marapiṉ nam mūtūr naṉṟu ē
  • {NT 311-6} koḻu mīṉ cuṭu pukai maṟukiṉ uḷ mayaṅki
  • {NT 311-7} ciṟu vī ñāḻal tuṟai um ār iṉitu ē
  • {NT 311-8} oṉṟu ē tōḻi nam kāṉalatu paḻi ē
  • {NT 311-9} karum kōṭṭu puṉṉai malar tātu arunti
  • {NT 311-10} irum kaḷi piracam ūta avar
  • {NT 311-11} neṭum tēr iṉ oli kēṭṭal ō aritu ē

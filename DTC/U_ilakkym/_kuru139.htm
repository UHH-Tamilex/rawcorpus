{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Don't come, sir, may you live, to our street
  2. with the gossip sounding unpleasantly
  3. as when the short-legged hens staying at the house
  4. call [their] troubled flock of children,
  5. so that they crowd all together without knowing which place to enter,
  6. becuse in the evening there is a family of wild cats at the hedge[FNote_563].

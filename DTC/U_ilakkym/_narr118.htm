{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Even at a time abounding in flowers, when the red-legged dark Coel,
  • united with [its] cock rejoices [and] dances face to face(?),
  • in the cool fragrant grove where the sprouts have attined beauty,
  • while the swaying branches of the Marā tree on the set shore flourish,
  • he has departed indeed, he who has forgotten us", on top of
  • breaking [at this thought], perceiving the [flower] clusters,
  • my heart aches becuse of the strange woman
  • talking in every street of the new blossoms,
  • offering(?) for the bees to come pure-petalled oleander
  • [and] cotton-headed [blossoms of] the trumpet-flower trees, as brushes
  • dyed with bright vermilion by people ...?.

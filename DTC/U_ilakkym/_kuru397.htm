{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O man from the coast of the strong water, where the cold wind spreads
  2. the egg-like[FNote_1584] round blossoms of the Ñāḻal, on which buds are ripening,
  3. on the big blossoms of the blue water-lily, as if raining:
  4. like a child, which opens [its] mouth [and] calls 'mummy'
  5. even at a time, when the mother slaps [it] in quarrel,
  6. whether you do what is unsweet or sweetly protect [her],
  7. my friend [is] one with you as a border.[FNote_1585]
  8. She doesn't have someone to remove[FNote_1586] the distress she has.

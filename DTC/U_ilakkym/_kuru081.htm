{{Readable Translation}}

  1. This one,
  2. after my words that had taken up your words became clear [to her][FNote_330],
  3. after[FNote_331] losing [her] virginity[FNote_332] to one side of the many-branched Ñāḻal[FNote_333]
  4. with green buds, is possessed, alas(?), by loneliness
  5. - just[FNote_334] look over there! Our good little village[FNote_335],
  6. [among] the Palmyra trees with hanging stems, must be thought of,
  7. where seashore grove and sea with waves reeking of fish
  8. appear like darkness and moonlight.

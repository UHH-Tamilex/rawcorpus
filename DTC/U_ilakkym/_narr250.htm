{{Readable Translation}}

  • We had to laugh[FNote_787], come, bard,
  • at [her] standing aside, saying "who [are] you?",
  • renouncing [us] as the doe the stag when it is frightened,
  • perceiving something else[FNote_788], [our] love with fragrant dark hair
  • [and] flawless brilliant forehead with the beauty of the crescent moon,
  • when we came near with longing to embrace
  • her, while a desirous heart was driving [us],
  • with the sandal paste [on our chest][FNote_789] spoiled by the flower-smelling red mouth
  • of [our] son with honey words, repeating the gait of a chariot[FNote_790]
  • in the street, while [his] slit-mouthed metal-filled anklets are tinkling.

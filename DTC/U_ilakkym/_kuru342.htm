{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Is it fitting, o man from a land of hills -
  2. where the forest man who forgot to watch
  3. the big jackfruit with fragrant flesh the male monkey touched[FNote_1374] with the hand,
  4. thereafter attaches catching nets on every scented tree
  5. - if it is said: 'you are of the kind not to take the consequences(?)[FNote_1375],
  6. who ends the sorrow of those who are yearning,
  7. since she with cool foliage [and] water-lilies from the fresh mountain pool, is troubled here'?

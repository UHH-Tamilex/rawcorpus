  • {PATI 88-1} viyakam malarnta toḻil muṟi oḻiyātu
  • {PATI 88-2} kaṭavuḷ peyariya kāṉamoṭu kal uyarntu
  • {PATI 88-3} teḷ kaṭal vaḷiiya malar tali ulakattu
  • {PATI 88-4} tam peyar pōkiya oṉṉār tēya
  • {PATI 88-5} tuḷaṅku irum kuṭṭam toliya vēl iṭṭu
  • {PATI 88-6} aṇaṅku uṭi kaṭampiṉ muḻumutal taṭintu
  • {PATI 88-7} poru muraṇ eytiya kaḻuvuḷ puṟam peṟṟu
  • {PATI 88-8} nāma maṉṉar tuṇiya nūṟi
  • {PATI 88-9} kāl val puravi aṇṭar ōṭṭi
  • {PATI 88-10} cuṭar vī vāki naṉṉaṉ tēyttu
  • {PATI 88-11} kuruti vitirtta kuvavu cōṟṟu kuṉṟōṭu
  • {PATI 88-12} uru keḻu marapiṉ ayiri parii
  • {PATI 88-13} vēntar um vēḷir um piṉ vantu paṇiya
  • {PATI 88-14} koṟṟam eytiya periyōr maruka
  • {PATI 88-15} viyal uḷi arimāṉ maṟam keḻu kurucil
  • {PATI 88-16} viravu paṇi muḻaṅkum niri tōl varippiṉ
  • {PATI 88-17} uravu kaḷiṟṟu vel koṭi nuṭaṅkum pācaṟi
  • {PATI 88-18} ār eyil alitta kal kāl kavaṇi
  • {PATI 88-19} nār ari naṟaviṉ koṅkar kō ē
  • {PATI 88-20} uṭalunar taputta pola tēr kurucil
  • {PATI 88-21} vaḷi kaṭal muḻaviṉ toṇṭiyōr poruna
  • {PATI 88-22} nī nīṭu vāḻiya peruma niṉ vayiṉ
  • {PATI 88-23} tuvitta tumpi naṉavuṟṟu viṉavum
  • {PATI 88-24} māṟṟu arum teyvattu kūṭṭam muṉṉiya
  • {PATI 88-25} puṉal mali pēr yāṟu iḻitantāṅku
  • {PATI 88-26} varunar variyā ceḻum pal tāram
  • {PATI 88-27} koḷa koḷa kuṟiyātu talittali ciṟappa
  • {PATI 88-28} ōvattu aṉṉa uru keḻu neṭu nakar
  • {PATI 88-29} pāvi aṉṉa makaḷir nāppaṇ
  • {PATI 88-30} pukaṉṟa māṇ poṟi polinta cāntamoṭu
  • {PATI 88-31} taṇ kamaḻ kōti cūṭi pūṇ cumantu
  • {PATI 88-32} tiruvil kulii tiru maṇi puriyum
  • {PATI 88-33} uru keḻu karuviya peru maḻi cērntu
  • {PATI 88-34} vēṅki virintu vicumpuṟu cēṇ cimi
  • {PATI 88-35} aruvi aru vari aṉṉa mārpiṉ
  • {PATI 88-36} cēṇ nāṟu nal ici cē iḻi kaṇava
  • {PATI 88-37} mākam cuṭara mā vicumpu ukakkum
  • {PATI 88-38} ñāyiṟu pōla viḷaṅkuti pal nāḷ
  • {PATI 88-39} īṅku kāṇku vantaṉeṉ yāṉ ē
  • {PATI 88-40} uṟu kāl eṭutta ōṅku varal puṇari
  • {PATI 88-41} nuṇ maṇal aṭi kari uṭitarum
  • {PATI 88-42} taṇ kaṭal paṭappi nāṭu kiḻavōy ē

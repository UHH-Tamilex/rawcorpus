Patiṟṟup pattu

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 88-1} vaiyakam malarnta toḻil muṟai oḻiyātu
  • {PATI 88-2} kaṭavuḷ peyariya kāṉamoṭu kal uyarntu
  • {PATI 88-3} teḷ kaṭal vaḷaiiya malar talai ulakattu
  • {PATI 88-4} tam peyar pōkiya oṉṉār tēya
  • {PATI 88-5} tuḷaṅku irum kuṭṭam tolaiya vēl iṭṭu
  • {PATI 88-6} aṇaṅku uṭai kaṭampiṉ muḻumutal taṭintu
  • {PATI 88-7} poru muraṇ eytiya kaḻuvuḷ puṟam peṟṟu
  • {PATI 88-8} nāma maṉṉar tuṇiya nūṟi
  • {PATI 88-9} kāl val puravi aṇṭar ōṭṭi
  • {PATI 88-10} cuṭar vī vākai naṉṉaṉ tēyttu
  • {PATI 88-11} kuruti vitirtta kuvavu cōṟṟu kuṉṟōṭu
  • {PATI 88-12} uru keḻu marapiṉ ayirai paraii
  • {PATI 88-13} vēntar um vēḷir um piṉ vantu paṇiya
  • {PATI 88-14} koṟṟam eytiya periyōr maruka
  • {PATI 88-15} viyal uḷai arimāṉ maṟam keḻu kurucil
  • {PATI 88-16} viravu paṇai muḻaṅkum nirai tōl varaippiṉ
  • {PATI 88-17} uravu kaḷiṟṟu vel koṭi nuṭaṅkum pācaṟai
  • {PATI 88-18} ār eyil alaitta kal kāl kavaṇai
  • {PATI 88-19} nār ari naṟaviṉ koṅkar kō ē
  • {PATI 88-20} uṭalunar taputta pola tēr kurucil
  • {PATI 88-21} vaḷai kaṭal muḻaviṉ toṇṭiyōr poruna
  • {PATI 88-22} nī nīṭu vāḻiya peruma niṉ vayiṉ
  • {PATI 88-23} tuvaitta tumpai naṉavuṟṟu viṉavum
  • {PATI 88-24} māṟṟu arum teyvattu kūṭṭam muṉṉiya
  • {PATI 88-25} puṉal mali pēr yāṟu iḻitantāṅku
  • {PATI 88-26} varunar varaiyā ceḻum pal tāram
  • {PATI 88-27} koḷa koḷa kuṟaiyātu talaittalai ciṟappa
  • {PATI 88-28} ōvattu aṉṉa uru keḻu neṭu nakar
  • {PATI 88-29} pāvai aṉṉa makaḷir nāppaṇ
  • {PATI 88-30} pukaṉṟa māṇ poṟi polinta cāntamoṭu
  • {PATI 88-31} taṇ kamaḻ kōtai cūṭi pūṇ cumantu
  • {PATI 88-32} tiruvil kulaii tiru maṇi puraiyum
  • {PATI 88-33} uru keḻu karuviya peru maḻai cērntu
  • {PATI 88-34} vēṅkai virintu vicumpuṟu cēṇ cimai
  • {PATI 88-35} aruvi aru varai aṉṉa mārpiṉ
  • {PATI 88-36} cēṇ nāṟu nal icai cē iḻai kaṇava
  • {PATI 88-37} mākam cuṭara mā vicumpu ukakkum
  • {PATI 88-38} ñāyiṟu pōla viḷaṅkuti pal nāḷ
  • {PATI 88-39} īṅku kāṇku vantaṉeṉ yāṉ ē
  • {PATI 88-40} uṟu kāl eṭutta ōṅku varal puṇari
  • {PATI 88-41} nuṇ maṇal aṭai karai uṭaitarum
  • {PATI 88-42} taṇ kaṭal paṭappai nāṭu kiḻavōy ē

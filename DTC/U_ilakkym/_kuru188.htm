{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The jasmine buds are fully grown. With the jasmine,
  2. the splendour[FNote_749] is fully grown on the wide fields in the rainy season.
  3. He who has loosened [my] pure ornaments has not come.
  4. Evening has come for[FNote_750] my glorious integrity.

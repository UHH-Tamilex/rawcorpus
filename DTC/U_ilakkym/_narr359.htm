{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from a land of hills, where the calf is confused by [its] mother,
  • when pollen was shed, after coming into contect with the Malabar lily in swaying bunches,
  • on the red cow with small horns that grazed on the mountin side,
  • he gave [us] foliage that one puts on. If we put on
  • those, I fear mother. If we give [them back],
  • I fear the loss of a friend[FNote_1010]. In between(?),
  • won't they themselves fade,
  • the leaves difficult to pluck from the fearful mountin-side,
  • where even the wehrhaft(?) Varuṭi sheep do not jump,
  • on his mountin?

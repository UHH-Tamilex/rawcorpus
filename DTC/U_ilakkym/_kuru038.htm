{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The intimacy with the man from the hills,
  2. where the young of the langur, playing in the sunlight,
  3. is rolling the egg laid on a rock by a peahen,
  4. [is] always good, certainly, oh friend,
  5. - for those able to endure without remembering
  6. when [he] departs, as one with[FNote_181] water in [my] collyrium eyes.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. You have become all a-quiver, laid down between her breasts,
  2. like people wanting water while plucking water-lilies
  3. with tubular, round stems [and] blossoms[FNote_715] of exceeding grace
  4. in the nice cool pond where Ayirai fish have spread(?)[FNote_716].
  5. At that time when we were difficult for you [to obtain],
  6. appearing [as we did] like the crescent moon, seen [and] worshipped,
  7. you suffered great things. Ah, I ache.[FNote_717]

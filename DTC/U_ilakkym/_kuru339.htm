{{Readable Translation}}

  1. To embrace the good chest with a garland of different blossoms
  2. of the man from a land, where fragrant smoke from the dense fields,
  3. on which fragrant eagle wood has glowed up, goes like dropless clouds[FNote_1362]
  4. [and] comes down into the village of the hill people on the slope[FNote_1363],
  5. [that is] sweet indeed, oh friend,
  6. before pallor comes, at the end of night[FNote_1364],
  7. for collyrium eyes to dim, black-petalled[FNote_1365] water-lilies.

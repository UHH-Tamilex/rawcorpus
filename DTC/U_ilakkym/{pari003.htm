Paripāṭal 3

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 3-1} māayōy ē māayōy ē
  • {PARI 3-2} maṟu piṟappu aṟukkum mācu il cē aṭi
  • {PARI 3-3} maṇi tikaḻ urupiṉ māayōy ē
  • {PARI 3-4} tī vaḷi vicumpu nilaṉ nīr aintu um
  • {PARI 3-5} ñāyiṟu um tiṅkaḷ um aṟaṉ um aivar um
  • {PARI 3-6} titiyiṉ ciṟār um vitiyiṉ makkaḷ um
  • {PARI 3-7} mācu il eṇmar um patiṉoru kapilar um
  • {PARI 3-8} tā mā iruvar um tarumaṉ um maṭaṅkal um
  • {PARI 3-9} mū ēḻ ulakam um ulakiṉ uḷ maṉpatu um
  • {PARI 3-10} māyōy niṉ vayiṉ parantavai uraittēm
  • {PARI 3-11} māyā vāymoḻi uraitara valantu
  • {PARI 3-12} vāymoḻi ōṭai malarnta
  • {PARI 3-13} tāmarai pūviṉ uḷ piṟantōṉ um tātai um
  • {PARI 3-14} nī eṉa moḻiyum āl antaṇar aru maṟai
  • {PARI 3-15} ēer vayaṅku pūṇ amararai vauviya amiḻtiṉ
  • {PARI 3-16} payantōḷ iṭukkaṇ kaḷainta puḷḷiṉai
  • {PARI 3-17} payantōḷ iṭukkaṇ kaḷainta puḷḷiṉ
  • {PARI 3-18} nivantu ōṅku uyar koṭi cēvalōy niṉ
  • {PARI 3-19} cē aṭi toḻār um uḷar ō avaṟṟu uḷ
  • {PARI 3-20} kīḻ ēḻ ulakam um uṟṟa aṭiyiṉai
  • {PARI 3-21} tī ce kaṉali um kūṟṟam um ñamaṉ um
  • {PARI 3-22} mācu il āyiram katir ñāyiṟu um tokūum
  • {PARI 3-23} ūḻi āḻi kaṇ iru nilam uru keḻu
  • {PARI 3-24} kēḻal āy maruppiṉ uḻutōy eṉa um
  • {PARI 3-25} mā vicumpu oḻuku puṉal vaṟaḷa aṉṉa
  • {PARI 3-26} cēval āy ciṟakar pularttiyōy eṉa um
  • {PARI 3-27} ñālattu uṟaiyuḷ tēvar um vāṉattu
  • {PARI 3-28} nāl eṇ tēvar um nayantu niṉ pāṭuvōr
  • {PARI 3-29} pāṭum vakai ē em pāṭal tām a
  • {PARI 3-30} pāṭuvār pāṭum vakai
  • {PARI 3-31} kūntal eṉṉum peyaroṭu kūntal
  • {PARI 3-32} eri ciṉam koṉṟōy niṉ pukaḻ uruviṉa kai
  • {PARI 3-33} nakai accu āka nal amirtu kalanta
  • {PARI 3-34} naṭuvunilai tiṟampiya nayam il oru kai
  • {PARI 3-35} iru kai māal
  • {PARI 3-36} mu kai muṉiva nāl kai aṇṇal
  • {PARI 3-37} aim kai mainta aṟu kai neṭu vēḷ
  • {PARI 3-38} eḻu kaiyāḷa eṇ kai ēntal
  • {PARI 3-39} oṉpatiṟṟu taṭa kai maṉ pērāḷa
  • {PARI 3-40} patiṟṟu kai matavali nūṟṟu kai āṟṟal
  • {PARI 3-41} āyiram viritta kaim māya maḷḷa
  • {PARI 3-42} patiṉāyiram kai mutumoḻi mutalva
  • {PARI 3-43} nūṟāyiram kai āṟu aṟi kaṭavuḷ
  • {PARI 3-44} aṉaittu um alla pala aṭukkal āmpal
  • {PARI 3-45} iṉaittu eṉa eṇ varampu aṟiyā yākkaiyai
  • {PARI 3-46} niṉṉai purai niṉaippiṉ nī alatu uṇarti ō
  • {PARI 3-47} muṉṉai marapiṉ mutumoḻi mutalva
  • {PARI 3-48} niṉakku virintu akaṉṟa kēḷvi aṉaittiṉum
  • {PARI 3-49} valiyiṉum maṉattiṉum uṇarviṉum ellām
  • {PARI 3-50} vaṉappu varampu aṟiyā marapiṉōy ē
  • {PARI 3-51} aṇi niḻal vayaṅku oḷi īr eṇ tīm katir
  • {PARI 3-52} piṟai vaḷar niṟai mati uṇṭi
  • {PARI 3-53} aṇi maṇi paim pūṇ amararkku mutalvaṉ nī
  • {PARI 3-54} tiṇi nilam kaṭanta kāl tirintu ayarntu akaṉṟu ōṭi
  • {PARI 3-55} niṉ añci kaṭal pāynta piṇi nekiḻpu aviḻ taṇ tār
  • {PARI 3-56} aṉṉavar paṭa allā avuṇarkku um mutalvaṉ nī
  • {PARI 3-57} ataṉāl pakaivar ivar ivar naṭṭōr eṉṉum
  • {PARI 3-58} vakai um uṇṭu ō niṉ marapu aṟivōrkku ē
  • {PARI 3-59} āyira aṇar talai aravu vāy koṇṭa
  • {PARI 3-60} cēval ūrti um ce kaṇ māal
  • {PARI 3-61} ō eṉa kiḷakkum kāla mutalvaṉai
  • {PARI 3-62} ēe iṉa kiḷattaliṉ iṉaimai naṟku aṟintaṉam
  • {PARI 3-63} tīyiṉ uḷ teṟal nī pūviṉ uḷ nāṟṟam nī
  • {PARI 3-64} kalliṉ uḷ maṇi um nī colliṉ uḷ vāymai nī
  • {PARI 3-65} aṟattiṉ uḷ aṉpu nī maṟattiṉ uḷ maintu nī
  • {PARI 3-66} vētattu maṟai nī pūtattu mutal um nī
  • {PARI 3-67} ve cuṭar oḷi um nī tiṅkaḷ uḷ aḷi um nī
  • {PARI 3-68} aṉaittu um nī aṉaittiṉ uḷ poruḷ um nī ātaliṉ
  • {PARI 3-69} uṟaiyum uṟaivatu um ilai ē uṇmai um
  • {PARI 3-70} maṟaviyil ciṟappiṉ māyam ār aṉaiyai
  • {PARI 3-71} mutal muṟai iṭai muṟai kaṭai muṟai toḻilil
  • {PARI 3-72} piṟavā piṟappu ilai piṟappittōr ilai ē
  • {PARI 3-73} paṟavā pūvai pūviṉōy ē
  • {PARI 3-74} aruḷ kuṭai āka aṟam kōl āka
  • {PARI 3-75} iru niḻal paṭāmai mū ēḻ ulakam um
  • {PARI 3-76} oru niḻal ākkiya ēmattai mātō
  • {PARI 3-77} pāḻ eṉa kāl eṉa pāku eṉa oṉṟu eṉa
  • {PARI 3-78} iraṇṭu eṉa mūṉṟu eṉa nāṉku eṉa aintu eṉa
  • {PARI 3-79} āṟu eṉa ēḻu eṉa eṭṭu eṉa toṇṭu eṉa
  • {PARI 3-80} nālvakai ūḻi eṇ naviṟṟum ciṟappiṉai
  • {PARI 3-81} ce kaṇ kāri karum kaṇ veḷḷai
  • {PARI 3-82} poṉ kaṇ paccai paim kaṇ māal
  • {PARI 3-83} iṭa vala kuṭa ala kōvala kāvala
  • {PARI 3-84} kāṇā marapa nīyā niṉaiva
  • {PARI 3-85} māyā maṉṉa ulaku āḷ maṉṉava
  • {PARI 3-86} tol iyal pulava nal yāḻ pāṇa
  • {PARI 3-87} mālai celva tōlā kōṭṭa
  • {PARI 3-88} polam puri āṭai valampuri vaṇṇa
  • {PARI 3-89} paruti valava poru tiṟal malla
  • {PARI 3-90} tiruviṉ kaṉava peru viṟal maḷḷa
  • {PARI 3-91} mā nilam iyalā mutal muṟai amaiyattu
  • {PARI 3-92} nāma veḷḷattu naṭuvaṇ tōṉṟiya
  • {PARI 3-93} vāymoḻi makaṉoṭu malarnta
  • {PARI 3-94} tāmarai pokuṭṭu niṉ nēmi niḻal ē

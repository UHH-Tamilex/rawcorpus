Naṟṟiṇai

((Originally extracted from master file ^NARRI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {NT 227-1} aṟintōr aṟaṉ ilar eṉṟaliṉ ciṟanta
  • {NT 227-2} iṉ uyir kaḻiyiṉum naṉi iṉṉātu ē
  • {NT 227-3} puṉṉai am kāṉal puṇar kuṟi vāytta
  • {NT 227-4} piṉ īr ōti eṉ tōḻikku aṉṉō
  • {NT 227-5} paṭu maṇi yāṉai pacum pūṇ cōḻar
  • {NT 227-6} koṭi nuṭaṅku maṟukiṉ ārkkāṭṭu āṅkaṇ
  • {NT 227-7} kaḷ uṭai taṭavil puḷ olittu ōvā
  • {NT 227-8} tēr vaḻaṅku teruviṉ aṉṉa
  • {NT 227-9} kauvai ākiṉṟatu aiya niṉ aruḷ ē

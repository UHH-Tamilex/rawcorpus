{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He who is full of[FNote_176] yearning [is] also a granting one.[FNote_177]
  2. The path he went, friend, is one of lovers[FNote_178]
  3. where the tender-twigged Yām tree[FNote_179] is pierced
  4. by the elephant bull to remove the hunger of [his] female.

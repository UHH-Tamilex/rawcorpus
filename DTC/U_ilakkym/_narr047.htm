{{colophon}}: itu ciṟippuṟamākat tōḻi talimakaḷukkurippāḷāyc (G1+2, ET: talimakaṭkurippāḷāyc) colliyatu.{{/colophon}}

{{Metrical text}}

  1. peruṅkaḷi ṟuḻuvi yaṭṭeṉa virumpiṭi
  2. yuyaṅkupiṇi varuttamo ṭiyaṅkal cellātu
  3. neytaṟ pācaṭi puriyu mañcevip
  4. pitalaṅ kuḻavi taḻīi yoyyeṉa
  5. varumpuṇ ṇuṟunariṉ varunti vikuṅ ((5))
  6. kāṉaka nāṭaṟ kituveṉa yāṉatu
  7. kūṟi ṉevaṉō tōḻi vēṟuṇarn
  8. taṇaṅkaṟi kaḻaṅkiṟ kōṭṭaṅ kāṭṭi
  9. veṟiyeṉa vuṇarnta vuḷḷamoṭu maṟiyaṟut
  10. taṉṉi yayaru murukuniṉ ((10))
  11. poṉṉēr pacalik kutavā māṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What if I told that, friend,
  • [with the words] 'this [is it]' to the man from the land inside the forests,
  • where the dark elephant cow, becuse a tiger has killed the big bull,
  • doesn`t get to move(?)[FNote_188] from suffering, a weakening fetter(?),
  • embraces [her] sorrowful pretty calf
  • with ears pretty as the green leaves of the blue Nelumbo
  • [and] is transfixed in suffering', 'oy', like those having a heavy wound,
  • - becuse Murukaṉ, in whom mother resorts(?),
  • after having realised [something is] different [and] the crookedness of the plague-knowing Molucca beans has been shown [and] she had a lamb[`s throat] cut off with a mind that realised 'the Veṟi dance [is the remedy]',
  • did not contribute to your goldlike pallor?{X}

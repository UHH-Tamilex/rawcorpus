  8. puṉka ṇañcum paṇpiṉ
  9. meṉkaṭ celvañ celvameṉ patuvē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the fertile village, where in the wide paddy fields of fine area,
  • changed since the time of harvest, the baskets that had gone
  • with seed to the wet fields that were ploughed
  • at another time come [back] with many many fish,
  • talking at length and mounting [your horse] quickly
  • is not wealth, [is is] the yield of the work one does.
  • What is called wealth by the noble ones
  • is called the wealth of wealth: tenderness
  • of a kind that fears sorrow in those that are close.[FNote_666]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. In the distress the wound in my heart had,
  2. troubled becuse of the special manner of [time] calculation
  3. of the [night] watchmen, my eyes don't sleep, oh friend,
  4. even at the time when fear comes,
  5. when at deepest midnight the red-eyed buffalo cow,
  6. to whom standing in the mud is disgusting, roars 'ai',
  7. on the last days of little rin, when she considers the hollow, unripe fruit,
  8. which have become soft and rotting, becuse rin has fallen on the fruit.

1+2b {--!}on the last days of the little rin on the Eḷ corn without grins, {--!}which have become soft [and] rotting becuse the old rins have drenched it.

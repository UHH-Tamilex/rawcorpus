  4. kaḷvar pōlak koṭiyaṉ mātō

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Pressing my hands to his eyes[FNote_122]
  • and stroking my forehead with his hands and speaking sweet things like mother
  • - [is] he really cruel like a robber,
  • the lord of the mountains glistening high on the peaks,
  • with tops, where the running rain tears
  • at the bamboo with fresh eyes from which dancing stems have sprung up(?)[FNote_123]
  • on the side of the high mountain spread with gold-like Vēṅkai
  • [and] sapphire-like descending waterfalls?

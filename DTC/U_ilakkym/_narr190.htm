{{Readable Translation}}

  • Ache now - may you live, heart -
  • you who were delighted with the smile of the coral mouth with few words
  • of the little woman with rain eyes, [as] a deer in the net,
  • who has reached distinction in the goodness of [her] desirable bamboo shoulder,
  • as Ārkāṭu with [its] toddy[FNote_601] fields,
  • where bee-swarmed blue water-lilies are blossoming between the rice,
  • [the town] of Aḻici with swift(?) chariot [and] broad garlands fragrant with honey, father of Cēntaṉ with a [red-]spotted[FNote_602] lance,
  • a delight[FNote_603], generous [like] the clouds, who surmounted the difficult fortress of [his] foes.

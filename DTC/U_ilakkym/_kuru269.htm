{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Oh, it would be good if one could get someone who would go the way into the distance
  2. without tarrying in [his] hasty motion [and] inquire [after him]:
  3. - My father, now that the wound torn by the strong shark has healed,
  4. has entered the blue-coloured great sea, and my mother,
  5. in order to exchange white rice for salt,[FNote_1091]
  6. has gone to the salt[FNote_1092] fields. Therefore -
  7. a messenger who says 'if [you] come now, she [is] easy [to have]!'
  8. to the man from the coast of dewy, dark expands(?).

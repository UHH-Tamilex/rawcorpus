{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He who has stroked the big shoulder along with the curly dark hair
  2. [and] left [me] to make wealth[FNote_752], for tight bangles to become loose,
  3. does he know, oh friend,
  4. of the one bell's voice in the manger with many cows
  5. that sounds each time the good bull stirs
  6. at midnight [after] half of the darkness[FNote_753], when strong thunder roars,
  7. so that the hooded(?)[FNote_754] head of the striped [and] spotted snake, hotly furious[FNote_755], is severed?

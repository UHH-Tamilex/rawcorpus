{{Readable Translation}}

  1. We have left behind, oh friend, the fear
  2. for the one who left [us], who has no consideration for us, having consented
  3. to choice bangles becoming loose [on her] with pretty, thin hair,[FNote_841]
  4. the one who traversed the desert in a waterless region
  5. with bees that move without being satiated,
  6. filling themselves[FNote_842] with the honey[FNote_843] of one [single] cluster, in summer,
  7. on the rising(?)[FNote_844] hot twig of the scorched Marām tree, which they do not forsake.[FNote_845]

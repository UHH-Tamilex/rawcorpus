{{Metrical text}}

  1. aḻuntupaṭa vīḻnta peruntaṇ kuṉṟat
  2. toliva līnti ṉulavai yaṅkāṭ
  3. ṭāṟuceṉ mākkaḷ ceṉṉi yeṟinta
  4. cemmaṟut talaiya neyttōr vāya
  5. valliyam peruntalaik kuruḷai mālai ((5))
  6. maraṉōkku miṇṭiva rīṅkaiya curaṉē
  7. vaiyeyiṟ ṟaiyaṉ maṭantai muṉṉuṟ
  8. ṟelliṭai nīṅku miḷaiyō ṉuḷḷaṅ
  9. kāloṭu paṭṭa māri
  10. mālvarai miḷirkku murumiṉuṅ koṭitē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The desert of Īṅkai trees climbing Iṇṭu trees, where in the evening
  • the big-headed cub of the tiger peers out of the hemp,
  • bloody-mouthed so as to offer red stains,
  • as it has attacked the heads of people going the way
  • through the wilderness of dry branches on the thriving, strong date-palms
  • by the cool hills, which descend so that valleys arise,[FNote_6]
  • - to leave [there] in the middle of daylight
  • having in front [of him] the wonderful girl with sharp teeth,
  • crueller than the thunder, which upsets the big mountains
  • with showers come with wind, is the mind of [that] youth.

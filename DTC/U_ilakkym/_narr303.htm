{{Readable Translation}}

  • Deep at midnight when sound has ceased [and] become still
  • the bustling village has sunk into sleep.
  • Is there word of [her of] good forehead, suffering at our side,
  • exhusted for misery kills [her], her eyes not sleeping,
  • whenever she hears the suffering voice of the Aṉṟil, united with [its] mate
  • in the nest on the bending stems of the palmyra tree on the village common,
  • with [its] large trunk joined of old by the god
  • - in the heart of the man from the coast of the long water himself,
  • where the dashing shark wanders, of fierce antagony,
  • let go when the pretty with bent knots net was torn
  • from the stright rod put by the strong-handed fishermen?

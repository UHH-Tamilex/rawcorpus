{{Readable Translation}}

  • If you say we are women of the men strong in
  • hunting the strong stag that yearn for searching the wilderness
  • with [their] dogs with seizing mouth, sounding the horn noisily,
  • - we are women the hill people, we [are] creeper-women from the hills.
  • Our village [is] inside the stones,
  • where the forest peacock rests in [its] nest
  • by the long-legged platform rised by the [field-]guardian.
  • Rest without going [and] go you, having tasted
  • the toddy become mellow in the bent bamboo on the big mountin and
  • having seen the Kuravi dance in the Vēṅki tree frontyard.

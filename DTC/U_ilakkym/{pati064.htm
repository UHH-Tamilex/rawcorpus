Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 64-1} valam paṭu muraciṉ vāy vāḷ koṟṟattu
  • {PATI 64-2} polam pūṇ vēntar palar til amma
  • {PATI 64-3} aṟam karaintu vayaṅkiya nāviṉ piṟaṅkiya
  • {PATI 64-4} urai cāl vēḷvi muṭitta kēḷvi
  • {PATI 64-5} antaṇar arum kalam ēṟpa nīr paṭṭu
  • {PATI 64-6} irum cēṟu āṭiya maṇal mali muṟṟattu
  • {PATI 64-7} kaḷiṟu nilai muṇaiiya tār arum takaippiṉ
  • {PATI 64-8} puṟam ciṟai vayiriyar kāṇiṉ vallē
  • {PATI 64-9} eḵku paṭai aṟutta koy cuval puravi
  • {PATI 64-10} alaṅkum pāṇṭil iḻai aṇintu īm eṉa
  • {PATI 64-11} āṉā koḷkaiyai ātaliṉ a vayiṉ
  • {PATI 64-12} mā iru vicumpil pal mīṉ oḷi keṭa
  • {PATI 64-13} ñāyiṟu tōṉṟiyāṅku māṟṟār
  • {PATI 64-14} uṟu muraṇ citaitta niṉ nōṉ tāḷ vāḻtti
  • {PATI 64-15} kāṇku vanticiṉ kaḻal toṭi aṇṇal
  • {PATI 64-16} mai paṭu malar kaḻi malarnta neytal
  • {PATI 64-17} itaḻ vaṉappu uṟṟa tōṟṟamoṭu uyarnta
  • {PATI 64-18} maḻaiyiṉum perum payam poḻiti ataṉāl
  • {PATI 64-19} paci uṭai okkalai orīiya
  • {PATI 64-20} icai mēm tōṉṟal niṉ pācaṟaiyāṉ ē

  1. urukeḻu teyvamuṅ karantuṟi yiṉṟē
  2. virikatir ñāyiṟuṅ kuṭakkuvāṅ kummē
  3. nīralik kaliiya kūḻi vaṭiyāc
  4. cāa yavvayi ṟalippa vuṭaṉiyin
  5. tōri makaḷiru mūrey tiṉarē ((5))
  6. paṉmalar naṟumpoḻil paḻicci yāmuṉ
  7. ceṉmō cēyiḻi yeṉṟaṉa mataṉetir
  8. collāṇ melliyal cilavē nallakat
  9. tiyāṇa riḷamuli naṉiya
  10. māṇeḻiṉ maḻikkaṇ ṭeṇpaṉi koḷavē. ((10))

  • [she of] soft nature didn't say [even] a few [words],
  • while the flourishing young breasts on [her] good chest became wet,
  • while [her] rin eyes of glorious grace took clear dew.

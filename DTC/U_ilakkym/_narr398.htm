{{Metrical text}}

  1. urukeḻu teyvamuṅ karantuṟai yiṉṟē
  2. virikatir ñāyiṟuṅ kuṭakkuvāṅ kummē
  3. nīralaik kalaiiya kūḻai vaṭiyāc
  4. cāa yavvayi ṟalaippa vuṭaṉiyain
  5. tōrai makaḷiru mūrey tiṉarē ((5))
  6. paṉmalar naṟumpoḻil paḻicci yāmuṉ
  7. ceṉmō cēyiḻai yeṉṟaṉa mataṉetir
  8. collāṇ melliyal cilavē nallakat
  9. tiyāṇa riḷamulai naṉaiya
  10. māṇeḻiṉ maḻaikkaṇ ṭeṇpaṉi koḷavē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Fearful gods won't remain hidden
  • and the sun with broad beams bends itself to the west
  • and the women have reached the village, [after] the game they had enjoyed together(?)[FNote_1082], while they slap [their] pretty bellies exhaustedly, [their] short [hair] no [longer] trickling that was disarrayed from being dashed by the water.
  • Adoring the grove fragrant with many blossoms we said:
  • "we in front. up, [o you with] red jewels! Against that
  • [she of] soft nature didn't say [even] a few [words],
  • while the flourishing young breasts on [her] good chest became wet,
  • while [her] rain eyes of glorious grace took clear dew.

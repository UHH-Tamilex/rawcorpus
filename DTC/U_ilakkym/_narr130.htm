{{Metrical text}}

  1. vaṭuviṉṟu niṟinta māṉṟērt teṇkaṇ
  2. maṭivāyt taṇṇumi naṭuva ṇārppak
  3. kōli ṉeṟintu kālit tōṉṟiya
  4. ceṉṉīrp potuviṉic cemmaṉ mūtūrt
  5. tamatucey vāḻkiyi ṉiṉiya tūṇṭō ((5))
  6. veṉiyiṉ viruppuṭiya rāyiṉu niṉivilar
  7. tēenta
  8. nērnta neñcu nekiḻnta tōḷum
  9. vāṭiya variyu nōkki nīṭā
  10. tevaṉcey taṉaḷip pēraña ruṟuviyeṉ
  11. ṟorunāḷ kūṟiṉṟu milarē virinīr ((10))
  12. viyaka variyaḷa viṟanta
  13. vevva nōypiṟi tuyavuttuṇi yiṉṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Is there anything as pleasing as a living made by oneself(?)[FNote_417]
  • in this superior(?) old village with public work of an upright kind(?)[FNote_418],
  • that has appeared (= begun?) in the morning, the clear-eyed leather-mouthed(?)
  • Taṇṇumi drum being beaten by the stick so that it roars in the centre
  • for(?) the chariot [drawn by] horses ...(?)[FNote_419]
  • Even if he possesses however much longing, he doesn't think
  • he is not one of saying for a single day,
  • looking at [her] heart that consented and [her] shoulder that is emaciated
  • and [her] lines which have faded, not prolonging:
  • "what has she done, she who has this big grief?"
  • There is no companion in suffering other than troubled pin
  • that has gone beyond the limit of the world of expanding waters.

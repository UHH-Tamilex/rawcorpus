{{Metrical text}}

  1. kāṉaṉ mālaik kaḻinīr malka
  2. nīṉiṟa neyta ṉiraiyitaḻ porunta
  3. vāṉā talaikkuṅ kaṭaṉmī ṉaruntip
  4. puḷḷiṉaṅ kuṭampai yuṭaṉcēr puḷḷār
  5. tuṟantōr tēet tiruntunaṉi varunti ((5))
  6. yāruyi raḻiva tāyiṉu nēriḻai
  7. karattal vēṇṭumāṉ maṟṟē parappunīrt
  8. taṇṇan tuṟaiva ṉāṇa
  9. naṇṇār tūṟṟum paḻitā ṉuṇṭē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if [our] difficult lifes goes to ruin, [you of] fine jewels,
  • suffering much urpon being in the land of the one who abandoned [us],
  • where the birds joining together in the nest with groups of birds become full,
  • eating fish from the sea that dashes unendingly,
  • while the water increases in the backwaters at evening in the forest,
  • while the petals fold themselves in rows on the blue-coloured water-lilies,
  • - it is just necessary indeed to hide [this].
  • Blame itself it is which the foes are spreading,
  • for the man from the cool ghat of extensive waters to be ashamed.

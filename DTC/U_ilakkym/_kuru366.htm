{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Besides what is fixed [and] appointed by fate,
  2. who [are] we to measure [and] know the qualities on his side?
  3. She is not quiet even when they say, 'she [is] one for the Veṟi dance', [and] moreover, being one with tearful eyes,
  4. suffering in [her] mind, looking at the blue of the full petals,
  5. which opened many fetters[FNote_1457] in the fresh-eyed(?)[FNote_1458], big mountin pool,
  6. it is not a mistake, alas, the decision of this[FNote_1459] [woman] with choice jewels.[FNote_1460]

  • {PATI 21-3} evvam cūḻātu viḷaṅkiya koḷkai
  • {PATI 21-4} kālai aṉṉa cīr cāl vāymoḻi
  • {PATI 21-5} uru keḻu marapiṉ kaṭavuḷ pēṇiyar
  • {PATI 21-6} koṇṭa tīyiṉ cuṭar eḻutōṟum
  • {PATI 21-7} virumpu mey paranta perum peyar āvuti

  • {PATI 21-9} viruntu kaṇ māṟātu uṇīiya pācavar
  • {PATI 21-10} ūṉattu aḻitta vāl niṇa koḻum kuṟai
  • {PATI 21-11} kuy iṭutōṟum āṉātu ārppa
  • {PATI 21-12} kaṭal oli koṇṭu ceḻu nakar varaippiṉ
  • {PATI 21-13} naṭuvaṇ eḻunta aṭu ney āvuti
  • {PATI 21-14} iraṇṭu uṭaṉ kamaḻum nāṟṟamoṭu vāṉattu
  • {PATI 21-15} nilaipeṟu kaṭavuḷ um viḻaitaka pēṇi
  • {PATI 21-16} ār vaḷam paḻuṉiya aiyam tīr ciṟappiṉ
  • {PATI 21-17} māri am kaḷḷiṉ pōr val yāṉai
  • {PATI 21-18} pōrppu uṟu muracam kaṟaṅka ārppu ciṟantu
  • {PATI 21-19} nal kalam tarūum maṇ paṭu mārpa
  • {PATI 21-20} mullai kaṇṇi pal āṉ kōvalar
  • {PATI 21-21} pul uṭai viyaṉ pulam pal ā parappi
  • {PATI 21-22} kal uyar kaṭattu iṭai katir maṇi peṟūum
  • {PATI 21-23} miti al ceruppiṉ pūḻiyar kō ē
  • {PATI 21-24} kuviyal kaṇṇi maḻavar meymmaṟai
  • {PATI 21-25} pal payam taḻīiya payam keḻu neṭum kōṭṭu
  • {PATI 21-26} nīr aṟal maruṅku vaḻippaṭā pākuṭi
  • {PATI 21-27} pārval kokkiṉ parivēṭpu añcā
  • {PATI 21-28} cīr uṭai tēetta muṉai keṭa vilaṅkiya
  • {PATI 21-29} nēr uyar neṭu varai ayirai poruna
  • {PATI 21-30} yāṇṭu piḻaippu aṟiyātu paya maḻai curantu
  • {PATI 21-31} nōy il māntarkku ūḻi āka
  • {PATI 21-32} maṇṇā āyiṉ maṇam kamaḻ koṇṭu
  • {PATI 21-33} kār malar kamaḻum tāḻ irum kūntal
  • {PATI 21-34} orīiyiṉa pōla iravu malar niṉṟu
  • {PATI 21-35} tiru mukattu alamarum peru matar maḻai kaṇ
  • {PATI 21-36} alaṅkiya kāntaḷ ilaṅku nīr aḻuvattu
  • {PATI 21-37} vēy uṟaḻ paṇai tōḷ ivaḷōṭu

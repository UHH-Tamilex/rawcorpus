  3. peruṅki yāṉi piṭiyeti rōṭuṅ
  4. kāṉam vempiya vaṟaṅkūr kaṭattiṭi
  5. vēṉi lōti niṟampeyar mutupōttup ((5))
  6. pāṇyāḻ kaṭiya vāṅkip pāṅkar
  7. neṭunili yāa yēṟun toḻila
  8. piṟarkkeṉa muyalum pērā ṇeñcamoṭu
  9. kāmar poruṭpiṇip pōkiya
  10. nāmvēṅ kātalar ceṉṟa vāṟē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Full of toil
  • [are] the paths taken by the lover whom we are burning for,
  • who has gone in the fetter of desired wealth,
  • with the heart of a great man that exhorts itself saying "for others",
  • - where the colour-changing old male of the chameleon in summer
  • ascends the Yām tree with broad base
  • nearby, greedily(?)[FNote_596] taking in the rhytmic lute,
  • in the midst of the wilderness abounding in drought pressing hot on the forest,
  • where the large-trunked elephant runs towards [its] female
  • with water, stretching out [its] broad trunk of dark roughness,
  • having taken in the gathering [drops?] from the rocky spring, so that the pond subsides.

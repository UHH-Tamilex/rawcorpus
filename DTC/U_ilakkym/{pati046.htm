Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 46-1} iḻaiyar kuḻaiyar naṟum taṇ mālaiyar
  • {PATI 46-2} cuṭar nimir avir toṭi ceṟitta muṉkai
  • {PATI 46-3} tiṟal viṭu tiru maṇi ilaṅku mārpiṉ
  • {PATI 46-4} vaṇṭu paṭu kūntal muṭi puṉai makaḷir
  • {PATI 46-5} toṭai paṭu pēriyāḻ pālai paṇṇi
  • {PATI 46-6} paṇiyā marapiṉ uḻiñai pāṭa
  • {PATI 46-7} iṉitu puṟantantu avarkku iṉ makiḻ curattaliṉ
  • {PATI 46-8} curam pala kaṭavum karai vāy paruti
  • {PATI 46-9} ūr pāṭṭu eṇ il paim talai tumiya
  • {PATI 46-10} pal ceru kaṭanta kol kaḷiṟṟu yāṉai
  • {PATI 46-11} kōṭu naral pauvam kalaṅka vēl iṭṭu
  • {PATI 46-12} uṭai tirai parappiṉ paṭu kaṭal ōṭṭiya
  • {PATI 46-13} vel pukaḻ kuṭṭuvaṉ kaṇṭōr
  • {PATI 46-14} celkuvam eṉṉār pāṭupu peyarntu ē

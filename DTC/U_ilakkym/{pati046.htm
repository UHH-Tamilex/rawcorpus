Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 46-1} iḻiyar kuḻiyar naṟum taṇ māliyar
  • {PATI 46-2} cuṭar nimir avir toṭi ceṟitta muṉki
  • {PATI 46-3} tiṟal viṭu tiru maṇi ilaṅku mārpiṉ
  • {PATI 46-4} vaṇṭu paṭu kūntal muṭi puṉi makaḷir
  • {PATI 46-5} toṭi paṭu pēriyāḻ pāli paṇṇi
  • {PATI 46-6} paṇiyā marapiṉ uḻiñi pāṭa
  • {PATI 46-7} iṉitu puṟantantu avarkku iṉ makiḻ curattaliṉ
  • {PATI 46-8} curam pala kaṭavum kari vāy paruti
  • {PATI 46-9} ūr pāṭṭu eṇ il pim tali tumiya
  • {PATI 46-10} pal ceru kaṭanta kol kaḷiṟṟu yāṉi
  • {PATI 46-11} kōṭu naral puvam kalaṅka vēl iṭṭu
  • {PATI 46-12} uṭi tiri parappiṉ paṭu kaṭal ōṭṭiya
  • {PATI 46-13} vel pukaḻ kuṭṭuvaṉ kaṇṭōr
  • {PATI 46-14} celkuvam eṉṉār pāṭupu peyarntu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, may you live, bee with adorning wings,
  2. fear [to utter] good words is not [necessary].[FNote_1562]
  3. If you go to the majestic long mountain of his land,
  4. say to the master of the mountain, where honeycombs are hanging
  5. like fine shields, going in a row for the king:
  6. 'she won't end with her people,
  7. the younger sister of the pickers(?) in the fine dust, where they hack weeds[FNote_1563]
  8. from the little millet by the wood[FNote_1564] crowded with wild animals.'

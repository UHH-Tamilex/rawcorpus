{{Readable Translation}}

  • They have come to reach [us] indeed, traversing the big wilderness,
  • where the bundle-backed(?)[FNote_371] green unripe fruits of the low-trunked Jujube tree
  • from the high branch, bent down a little[FNote_372] when eaten, head lifted, by the deer,
  • spread increasingly on the small path joined with stones,
  • - the looks full of loneliness of her who has agitating grief,
  • lamenting as the flute [from the stem of?] the white water-lily
  • that is blown by the musician(?)[FNote_373] on the resounding(?) big field
  • of the noisy main(?) battle that rushed against[FNote_374] Utiyaṉ,
  • greatly heart-broken, hiding herself in her braided dark tresses,
  • while suffering was abundant in her water-lily collyrium eyes,
  • when [we] said: "we, we will go, girl,"
  • thinking in the fetter of wealth difficult to make.

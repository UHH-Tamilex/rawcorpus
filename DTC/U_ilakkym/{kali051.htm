  • {KALI 51-1} cuṭar toṭīi kēḷāy teruvil nām āṭum
  • {KALI 51-2} maṇal ciṟṟil kāliṉ citiyā aṭicciya
  • {KALI 51-3} kōti parintu vari pantu koṇṭu ōṭi
  • {KALI 51-4} nōtakka ceyyum ciṟu paṭṭi mēl ōr nāḷ
  • {KALI 51-5} aṉṉi um yāṉ um iruntēm ā illir ē
  • {KALI 51-6} uṇṇu nīr vēṭṭēṉ eṉa vantāṟku aṉṉi
  • {KALI 51-7} aṭar poṉ cirakattāl vākki cuṭar iḻāy
  • {KALI 51-8} uṇṇu nīr ūṭṭi vā eṉṟāḷ eṉa yāṉ um
  • {KALI 51-9} taṉṉi aṟiyātu ceṉṟēṉ maṟṟu eṉṉi
  • {KALI 51-10} vaḷi muṉki paṟṟi naliya terumantiṭṭu
  • {KALI 51-11} aṉṉāy ivaṉ oruvaṉ ceytatu kāṇ eṉṟēṉ ā
  • {KALI 51-12} aṉṉi alaṟi paṭartara taṉṉi yāṉ
  • {KALI 51-13} uṇṇu nīr vikkiṉāṉ eṉṟēṉ ā aṉṉi um
  • {KALI 51-14} taṉṉi puṟampu aḻittu nīva maṟṟu eṉṉi
  • {KALI 51-15} kaṭi kaṇṇāl kolvāṉ pōl nōkki naki kūṭṭam
  • {KALI 51-16} ceytāṉ a kaḷvaṉ makaṉ

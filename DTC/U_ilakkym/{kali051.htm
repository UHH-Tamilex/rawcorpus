Kalittokai

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 51-1} cuṭar toṭīi kēḷāy teruvil nām āṭum
  • {KALI 51-2} maṇal ciṟṟil kāliṉ citaiyā aṭaicciya
  • {KALI 51-3} kōtai parintu vari pantu koṇṭu ōṭi
  • {KALI 51-4} nōtakka ceyyum ciṟu paṭṭi mēl ōr nāḷ
  • {KALI 51-5} aṉṉai um yāṉ um iruntēm ā illir ē
  • {KALI 51-6} uṇṇu nīr vēṭṭēṉ eṉa vantāṟku aṉṉai
  • {KALI 51-7} aṭar poṉ cirakattāl vākki cuṭar iḻāy
  • {KALI 51-8} uṇṇu nīr ūṭṭi vā eṉṟāḷ eṉa yāṉ um
  • {KALI 51-9} taṉṉai aṟiyātu ceṉṟēṉ maṟṟu eṉṉai
  • {KALI 51-10} vaḷai muṉkai paṟṟi naliya terumantiṭṭu
  • {KALI 51-11} aṉṉāy ivaṉ oruvaṉ ceytatu kāṇ eṉṟēṉ ā
  • {KALI 51-12} aṉṉai alaṟi paṭartara taṉṉai yāṉ
  • {KALI 51-13} uṇṇu nīr vikkiṉāṉ eṉṟēṉ ā aṉṉai um
  • {KALI 51-14} taṉṉai puṟampu aḻittu nīva maṟṟu eṉṉai
  • {KALI 51-15} kaṭai kaṇṇāl kolvāṉ pōl nōkki nakai kūṭṭam
  • {KALI 51-16} ceytāṉ a kaḷvaṉ makaṉ

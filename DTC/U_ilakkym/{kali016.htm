  • {KALI 16-1} pāṭu iṉṟi pacanta kaṇ pitala paṉi malka
  • {KALI 16-2} vāṭupu vaṉappu ōṭi vaṇaṅku iṟi vaḷi ūra
  • {KALI 16-3} āṭu eḻil aḻivu añcātu akaṉṟavar tiṟattu iṉi
  • {KALI 16-4} nāṭum kāl niṉippatu oṉṟu uṭiyēṉ maṉ atu um tāṉ
  • {KALI 16-5} tol nalam tolipu īṅku yām tuyar uḻappa tuṟantu uḷḷār
  • {KALI 16-6} tuṉṉi nam kātalar tuṟantu ēkum ār iṭi
  • {KALI 16-7} kal mici uruppu aṟa kaṉi tuḷi citaṟu eṉa
  • {KALI 16-8} iṉ ici eḻiliyi irappa um iyivatu ō
  • {KALI 16-9} puṉi iḻāy īṅku nām pulampuṟa poruḷ veḵki
  • {KALI 16-10} muṉi eṉṉār kātalar muṉṉiya ār iṭi
  • {KALI 16-11} ciṉi vāṭa ciṟakkum niṉ ciṉam taṇintīka eṉa
  • {KALI 16-12} kaṉi katir kaṉaliyi kāmuṟal iyivatu ō
  • {KALI 16-13} oḷi iḻāy īṅku nām tuyar kūra poruḷ vayiṉ
  • {KALI 16-14} aḷi orīi kātalar akaṉṟu ēkum ār iṭi
  • {KALI 16-15} muḷi mutal mūḻkiya vemmi tīrntu uṟuka eṉa
  • {KALI 16-16} vaḷi tarum celvaṉi vāḻtta um iyivatu ō
  • {KALI 16-17} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 16-18} cey poruḷ ciṟappu eṇṇi celvār māṭṭu iṉiyaṉa
  • {KALI 16-19} teyvattu tiṟaṉ nōkki terumaral tē moḻi
  • {KALI 16-20} vaṟaṉ ōṭiṉ viyakattu vāṉ tarum kaṟpiṉāḷ
  • {KALI 16-21} niṟaṉ ōṭi pacappu ūrtal uṇṭu eṉa
  • {KALI 16-22} aṟaṉ ōṭi vilaṅkiṉṟu avar āḷviṉi tiṟattu ē

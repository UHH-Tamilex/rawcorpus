{{Metrical text}}

  1. emmūr vāyi loṇṭuṟait taṭaiiya
  2. kaṭavuṇ mutumarat tuṭaṉuṟai paḻakiya
  3. tēyā vaḷaivāyt teṇkaṭ kūrukir
  4. vāyppaṟai yacāvum valimuntu kūkai
  5. maiyūṉ ṟerinta neyyuṇ puḻukka ((5))
  6. lelivāṉ cūṭṭoṭu maliyap peṇutu
  7. meñcāk koḷkaiyeṅ kātalar varanacaiit
  8. tuñcā talamvarum poḻuti
  9. ṉañcuvarak kaṭuṅkural payiṟṟā tīmē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O strong [and] old(?)[FNote_299] owl whose drum of a mouth languishes,
  • [you with] sharp nail, clear eyes [and] untiring(?) curved beak,
  • familiar for staying together in the plump god[-inhabited] old tree
  • by the bright ghat at the gate of our village,
  • we will honour you to [your] delight with a rat roasted white
  • [and] a boil of food with choice ghee [and] ghoat's flesh
  • - don't use again [your] fierce voice for fears to come up
  • at a time when [she] is sleepless [and] agitated,
  • yearning for our lover of unswerving principles to come.

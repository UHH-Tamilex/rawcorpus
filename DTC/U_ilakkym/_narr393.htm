{{Metrical text}}

  1. neṭuṅkaḻai nivanta niḻalpaṭu cilampiṟ
  2. kaṭuñcūl vayappiṭi kaṉṟīṉ ṟuyaṅkap
  3. pālār pacumpuṉiṟu tīriya kaḷiciṟantu
  4. vālā vēḻam vaṇarkural kavartaliṟ
  5. kāṉava ṉeṟinta kaṭuñcelaṉ ñekiḻi ((5))
  6. vēypayi laṭukkañ cuṭara miṉṉi
  7. nilaikiḷar miṉṉiṟ ṟōṉṟu nāṭa
  8. ṉiraviṉ varūu miṭumpai nāmuya
  9. varaiya vanta vāymaik kēṟpa
  10. namarkoṭai nērntaṉa rāyi ṉavaruṭa ((10))
  11. ṉērvarkol vāḻi tōḻinaṅ kātalar
  12. putuva rākiya varavuniṉ
  13. vatuvainā ṇoṭukkamuṅ kāṇuṅ kālē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • If our people were to consent to give [you],
  • as is suitable to the truth[FNote_1066] that he has come to marry,
  • so that we escape distress when he comes at night,
  • the man from a land, where the steady(?)[FNote_1067] lustre appears like lightning,
  • the mountainside rustling with bamboo flashing brightly
  • from the swift-moving fire-brand thrown by the forest man,
  • when the impure elephant bull seizes the bent ears,
  • superior in rut(?)[FNote_1068], so that state of having freshly given birth, full of milk,
  • while being weak having brought forth a calf, ends in [his] highly pregnant strong female(?)[FNote_1069] on the slope giving shade since long bamboo has come up,
  • - will they consent to him, live, friend,
  • at the time they see your control of shame at the marriage,
  • when our lover comes(?)[FNote_1070] as a new man?

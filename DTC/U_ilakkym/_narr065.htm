{{Readable Translation}}

  • May she eat ambrosia, our woman of peacock nature,
  • she who said that the man from the land of big mountains will come,
  • where the elephant with a number of wounds that had fought with a tiger,
  • after taking drink at the collyrium ghat with [its] bright white waterfall,
  • while the leafs are perturbed in the water with lichen that is stirred
  • by the forest river with a shore of stranded [goods] as in a storehouse(?)[FNote_242],
  • cries out thunderingly in [self]protection(?) in the thunder(?)
  • of the fearful fight that came from the drawn(?) bow
  • of the loveless forester who longed for [its] horn.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, oh friend, the man from the sea,
  2. he does not decide[FNote_937] on this. Do I,
  3. with my folly, filled by a great [sense of] being suited[FNote_938],
  4. possess something which gave [him] pain?[FNote_939]
  5. On the road by the water frequented by strong sharks,
  6. as if[FNote_940] [it were only] a few days, he just didn't come[FNote_941].

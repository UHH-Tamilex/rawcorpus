{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The traversing of the undear, difficult desert,
  2. where the superior good bull with horns for a chaplet[FNote_1448]
  3. looks permanently [at her of][FNote_1449] inexperienced eyes,
  4. who chews abundant bunches of red-stemmed Patavu grass,
  5. [and] get hot [and] abides in the striped shade of the low-trunked Ukāay tree,
  6. - is it dear, o great one, separated from the dear companion?

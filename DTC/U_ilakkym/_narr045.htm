{{Readable Translation}}

  • She, she [is] the dughter of fishermen, hurling fish,
  • entering the stirring inside of the blue-coloured great sea,
  • with desirable small homes situated by the seashore grove.
  • You, you [are] the beloved son of a wealthy man with quick chariot,
  • from an old village with a fine market place(?) with long flags.
  • What good [is there] to us who are chasing away birds in flocks,
  • wanting the dryings that have been killed by the fat shark?
  • We are smelling of fish. Stay away![FNote_183]
  • Our little good living from the produce of the great water
  • is nothing big with you.
  • For us(?), [our] one house possesses all the excellence.

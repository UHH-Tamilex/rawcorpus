{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Our [man], o red-jewel[led one][FNote_1149], has he gone
  2. to the forest, where on[FNote_1150] the hills united,
  3. worn for [their] heads[FNote_1151] with curly full mane to shine[FNote_1152],
  4. the dense sky(light) flower of the Neem tree by the path,
  5. with the white mass of crests of the rounded Palmyra
  6. with sharp edges [and] fresh stems[FNote_1153], thriving in the white sands[FNote_1154]?

Altern

(Cam.T.V.G.: HE also as the subject of the abs. cūṭi)[FNote_1155] Has he gone, o red-jewel[led one], our [man], to the forest united with hills[FNote_1156], having worn, for [his] curly full tuft of hair on the head to shine,[FNote_1157] the sky(light) flowers of the Neem by the path together with white masses of crests of the rounded Palmyra with sharp edges [and] fresh stems, thriving in the white sands?[FNote_1158]

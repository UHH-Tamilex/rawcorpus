{{Metrical text}}

  1. kuṇakaṭaṉ mukantu kuṭakkēr piruḷi
  2. maṇṭiṇi ñālam viḷaṅka kammiyar
  3. cempucori pāṉiyiṉ miṉṉiyev vāyun
  4. taṉṟoḻil vāytta viṉkura leḻili
  5. teṉpula maruṅkiṟ ceṉṟaṟ ṟāṅku ((5))
  6. neñcama varvayiṟ ceṉṟeṉa vīṇṭoḻin
  7. tuṇṭa laḷitte ṉuṭampē viṟaṟpōr
  8. veñciṉa vēntaṉ pakiyalik kalaṅki
  9. vāḻvōr pōkiya pērūrp
  10. pāḻkāt tirunta taṉimakaṉ pōṉṟē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Becuse [my] heart has gone to him
  • - like the sweet-voiced cloud, that has achieved its task anywhere(?)[FNote_489],
  • drawing [water] from the eastern sea, rising [and] darkening to the west,
  • [and] flashing like a polished(?)[FNote_490] pot of copper [made] by artisans,
  • so that the firm world of earth shines,
  • goes to the southern fields [and] subsides -
  • my body is pitiful in staying behind here [and] eating,
  • like a single man who stays protecting the ruin
  • of a big village from which the inhabitants have gone,
  • agitated by the oppression of enmity from a king in hot anger, in victorious battle.

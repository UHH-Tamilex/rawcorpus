Naṟṟiṇai

((Originally extracted from master file ^NARRI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {NT 207-1} kaṇṭal vēli kaḻi cūḻ paṭappai
  • {NT 207-2} muṇṭakam vēynta kuṟu iṟai kurampai
  • {NT 207-3} koḻu mīṉ koḷpavar pākkam kalleṉa
  • {NT 207-4} neṭum tēr paṇṇi varal āṉātu ē
  • {NT 207-5} kuṉṟattu aṉṉa kuvavu maṇal nīnti
  • {NT 207-6} vantaṉar peyarvar kol tām ē alkal
  • {NT 207-7} iḷaiyar um mutiyar um kiḷai uṭaṉ kuḻīi
  • {NT 207-8} kōḷ cuṟā eṟinteṉa curuṅkiya nāmpiṉ
  • {NT 207-9} muṭi mutir paratavar maṭa moḻi kuṟumakaḷ
  • {NT 207-10} valai um tūṇṭil um paṟṟi perum kāl
  • {NT 207-11} tirai eḻu pauvam muṉṉiya
  • {NT 207-12} kolai ve ciṟāar pāl paṭṭaṉaḷ ē

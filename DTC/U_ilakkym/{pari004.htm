Paripāṭal 4

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 4-1} aintu iruḷ aṟa nīkki nāṉkiṉ uḷ tuṭittu tam
  • {PARI 4-2} oṉṟu āṟṟuppaṭutta niṉ ārvalar toḻutu ētti
  • {PARI 4-3} niṉ pukaḻ virittaṉar kiḷakkum kāl avi niṉakku
  • {PARI 4-4} iṟumpūtu aṉmi naṟku aṟintēm āyiṉum
  • {PARI 4-5} nakutal um takuti īṅku ūṅku niṉ kiḷappa
  • {PARI 4-6} tiru maṇi tiri pāṭu avinta munnīr
  • {PARI 4-7} varu maḻi irum cūl mūṉṟu um puriyum mā mey
  • {PARI 4-8} māa meyyoṭu muraṇiya uṭukkiyi
  • {PARI 4-9} nōṉār uyiroṭu muraṇiya nēmiyi
  • {PARI 4-10} ceyir tīr ce kaṇ celva niṉ pukaḻa
  • {PARI 4-11} pukinta neñciṉ pularnta cāntiṉ
  • {PARI 4-12} piruṅkalātaṉ pala pala piṇi paṭa
  • {PARI 4-13} valantuḻi malarnta nōy kūr kūmpiya naṭukkattu
  • {PARI 4-14} alarnta pukaḻōṉ tāti ākaliṉ
  • {PARI 4-15} ikaḻvōṉ ikaḻā neñciṉaṉ āka nī ikaḻā
  • {PARI 4-16} naṉṟu ā naṭṭa avaṉ nal mārpu muyaṅki
  • {PARI 4-17} oṉṟu ā naṭṭavaṉ uṟu vari mārpiṉ
  • {PARI 4-18} paṭimatam cāmpa otuṅki
  • {PARI 4-19} iṉṉal iṉṉaroṭu iṭi muracu iyampa
  • {PARI 4-20} veṭi paṭā oṭi tūṇ taṭiyoṭu
  • {PARI 4-21} taṭi taṭi pala paṭa vakir vāytta ukiriṉi
  • {PARI 4-22} puruvattu karu val kantarattāl
  • {PARI 4-23} tāṅki i ulakam tantu aṭippaṭuttatu ai naṭuvaṇ
  • {PARI 4-24} ōṅkiya palar pukaḻ kuṉṟiṉōṭu okkum
  • {PARI 4-25} niṉ vemmi um viḷakkam um ñāyiṟṟu uḷa
  • {PARI 4-26} niṉ taṇmi um cāyal um tiṅkaḷ uḷa
  • {PARI 4-27} niṉ curattal um vaṇmi um māri uḷa
  • {PARI 4-28} niṉ purattal um nōṉmi um ñālattu uḷa
  • {PARI 4-29} niṉ nāṟṟam um oṇmi um pūvi uḷa
  • {PARI 4-30} niṉ tōṟṟam um akalam um nīriṉ uḷa
  • {PARI 4-31} niṉ uruvam um oli um ākāyattu uḷa
  • {PARI 4-32} niṉ varutal um oṭukkam um maruttiṉ uḷa
  • {PARI 4-33} ataṉāl i um u um a um piṟa um
  • {PARI 4-34} ēmam ārnta niṉ pirintu
  • {PARI 4-35} mēval cāṉṟaṉa ellām
  • {PARI 4-36} cēval ōṅku uyar koṭiyōy ē
  • {PARI 4-37} cēval ōṅku uyar koṭi
  • {PARI 4-38} niṉ oṉṟu uyar koṭi paṉi
  • {PARI 4-39} niṉ oṉṟu uyar koṭi nāñcil
  • {PARI 4-40} niṉ oṉṟu uyar koṭi yāṉi
  • {PARI 4-41} niṉ oṉṟā uyar koṭi oṉṟu iṉṟu
  • {PARI 4-42} viṭam uṭi araviṉ uṭal uyir uruṅku uvaṇam
  • {PARI 4-43} avaṉ maṭi mēl valantatu pāmpu
  • {PARI 4-44} pāmpu toṭi pāmpu muṭi mēlaṉa
  • {PARI 4-45} pāmpu pūṇ pāmpu tali mēlatu
  • {PARI 4-46} pāmpu ciṟi taliyaṉa
  • {PARI 4-47} pāmpu paṭimatam cāyttōy pacum pūṇavi
  • {PARI 4-48} koṭi mēl iruntavaṉ tākku iriyatu pāmpu
  • {PARI 4-49} kaṭu navi aṇaṅku um kaṭuppu um nalkal um
  • {PARI 4-50} koṭumi um cemmi um vemmi um taṇmi um
  • {PARI 4-51} uḷ vaḻi uṭiyi il vaḻi ili ē
  • {PARI 4-52} pōṟṟār uyiriṉum pōṟṟunar uyiriṉum
  • {PARI 4-53} māṟṟu ēm āṟṟal ili ē niṉakku
  • {PARI 4-54} māṟṟōr um ilar kēḷir um ilar eṉum
  • {PARI 4-55} vēṟṟumi iṉṟu atu pōṟṟunar peṟiṉ ē
  • {PARI 4-56} maṉa kōḷ niṉakku eṉa vaṭivu vēṟu ili ē
  • {PARI 4-57} kōḷ iruḷ irukki āy maṇi mēṉi
  • {PARI 4-58} nakku alar tuḻāay nāṟu iṇar kaṇṇiyi
  • {PARI 4-59} poṉṉiṉ tōṉṟiya puṉi maṟu mārpa
  • {PARI 4-60} niṉṉil tōṉṟiya niri itaḻ tāmari
  • {PARI 4-61} aṉṉa nāṭṭattu aḷappariyavi
  • {PARI 4-62} niṉṉiṉ ciṟanta niṉ tāḷ iṇiyavi
  • {PARI 4-63} niṉṉil ciṟanta niṟi kaṭavuḷavi
  • {PARI 4-64} aṉṉōr allā vēṟu um uḷa avi
  • {PARI 4-65} niṉ ōraṉ ōr antaṇar aru maṟi
  • {PARI 4-66} aḻal puri kuḻi koḻu niḻal tarum pala ciṉi
  • {PARI 4-67} ālam um kaṭampu um nal yāṟṟu naṭu um
  • {PARI 4-68} kāl vaḻakku aṟu nili kuṉṟam um piṟa um
  • {PARI 4-69} a avi mēviya vēṟu vēṟu peyarōy
  • {PARI 4-70} e vayiṉōy um nī ē niṉ ārvalar
  • {PARI 4-71} toḻuta ki amitiyiṉ amarntōy um nī ē
  • {PARI 4-72} avaravar ēvalāḷaṉ um nī ē
  • {PARI 4-73} avaravar cey poruṭku araṇam um nī ē

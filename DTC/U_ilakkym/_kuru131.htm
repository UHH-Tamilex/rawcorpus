{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The village where she with very beautiful[FNote_528] eyes was[FNote_529],
  2. [her] bamboo shoulder of a beauty resembling dancing bamboo,
  3. far away, a place difficult [to attain] it [is], heart.[FNote_530]
  4. Like a ploughman with one plough
  5. on a fresh field in the time wetness has come,
  6. it was in headlong haste.[FNote_531] Ah, I ache.[FNote_532]

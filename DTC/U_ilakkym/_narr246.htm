  1. iṭūuṅkaṇ ṇiṉiya paṭūun neṭuñcuvar
  2. palliyum pāṅkiṟ ṟēṟṟu maṉṟē
  3. maṉimara nocci mīmici mācciṉi
  4. viṉimā ṇiruṅkuyil payiṟṟalum payiṟṟu
  5. murampuri yuḷḷamoṭu curampala nīntic ((5))
  6. ceyporuṭ kakaṉṟaṉa rāyiṉum poyyalar
  7. varuvar vāḻi tōḻi puṟaviṟ
  8. poṉvīk koṉṟiyoṭu piṭavuttaḷi yaviḻa
  9. viṉṉici vāṉa miraṅkumavar
  10. varutu meṉṟa paruvamō vituvē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The gecko too in the neighbourhood makes it clear, doesn't it,
  • on the long wall when [its] sweet [sounds] come forth in between(?!).[FNote_777]
  • While the dark Coels glorious in [their] work repeat one another
  • on the high big Nocci branches of the tree by the house,
  • even if he has departed for wealth to be made,
  • crossing the deserts, many, with a mind of strong desire,
  • he does not lie, he will come, o friend.
  • As for the season he sid "we will come",
  • when the sky roars in sweet sounds for the Piṭavu to open
  • [its] bonds along with the gold-blossomed Koṉṟi in the woodland,
  • [it is] this.

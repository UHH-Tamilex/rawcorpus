Kalittoki

  • {KALI 84-1} uṟu vaḷi tūkkum uyar ciṉi māviṉ
  • {KALI 84-2} naṟu vaṭi ār iṟṟavi pōl aḻiya
  • {KALI 84-3} karantu yāṉ arakka um ki nillā vīṅki
  • {KALI 84-4} curanta eṉ mel muli pāl paḻutu āka nī
  • {KALI 84-5} nal vāyil pōttanta poḻutiṉāṉ ellā
  • {KALI 84-6} kaṭavuḷ kaṭi nakar tōṟum ivaṉi
  • {KALI 84-7} valam koḷīi vā eṉa ceṉṟāy vilaṅkiṉi
  • {KALI 84-8} īram ilāta ivaṉ tanti peṇṭir uḷ
  • {KALI 84-9} yār il tavirntaṉi kūṟu
  • {KALI 84-10} nīr uḷ aṭi maṟi āy itaḻ pōtu pōl koṇṭa
  • {KALI 84-11} kuṭi niḻal tōṉṟum niṉ cemmali kāṇūu
  • {KALI 84-12} ivaṉ maṉṟa yāṉ nōva uḷḷam koṇṭu uḷḷā
  • {KALI 84-13} makaṉ allāṉ peṟṟa makaṉ eṉṟu akal nakar
  • {KALI 84-14} vāyil vari iṟantu pōttantu tāyar
  • {KALI 84-15} teruvil tavirppa tavirntaṉaṉ maṟṟu avar
  • {KALI 84-16} tam tam kalaṅkaḷ uḷ kiyuṟi eṉṟu ivaṟku
  • {KALI 84-17} ottavi ārāyntu aṇintār piṟaṉ peṇṭir
  • {KALI 84-18} īttavi koḷvāṉ ām iḵtu ottaṉ cītti
  • {KALI 84-19} ceṟu takkāṉ maṉṟa peritu
  • {KALI 84-20} ciṟu paṭṭi ētilār ki emmi eḷḷupu nī toṭṭa
  • {KALI 84-21} mōtiram yā ō yām kāṇku
  • {KALI 84-22} avaṟṟu uḷ naṟā itaḻ kaṇṭaṉṉa ce viraṟku ēṟpa
  • {KALI 84-23} cuṟā ēṟu eḻutiya mōtiram toṭṭāḷ
  • {KALI 84-24} kuṟi aṟintēṉ kāmaṉ koṭi eḻuti eṉṟum
  • {KALI 84-25} ceṟiyā paratti ivaṉ tanti mārpil
  • {KALI 84-26} poṟi oṟṟi koṇṭu āḷval eṉpatu taṉṉi
  • {KALI 84-27} aṟīiya ceyta viṉi
  • {KALI 84-28} aṉṉi ō iḵtu oṉṟu
  • {KALI 84-29} muntiya kaṇṭu um eḻukallāta eṉ muṉṉar
  • {KALI 84-30} venta puṇ vēl eṟintaṟṟu ā iḵtu oṉṟu
  • {KALI 84-31} tanti iṟi toṭi maṟṟu ivaṉ taṉ ki kaṇ
  • {KALI 84-32} tantār yār ellāa itu
  • {KALI 84-33} iḵtu oṉṟu eṉ ottu kāṇka piṟar um ivaṟku eṉṉum
  • {KALI 84-34} taṉ nalam pāṭuvi tantāḷ ā niṉṉi
  • {KALI 84-35} itu toṭuka eṉṟavar yār
  • {KALI 84-36} añcāti nī um tavaṟu ili niṉ ki itu tanta
  • {KALI 84-37} pū eḻil uṇ kaṇ avaḷ um tavaṟu ilaḷ
  • {KALI 84-38} vēṉil puṉal aṉṉa nuntiyi nōvār yār
  • {KALI 84-39} mēl niṉṟu um eḷḷi itu ivaṉ ki tantāḷ
  • {KALI 84-40} tāṉ yār ō eṉṟu viṉaviya nōyppālēṉ
  • {KALI 84-41} yāṉ ē tavaṟu uṭiyēṉ

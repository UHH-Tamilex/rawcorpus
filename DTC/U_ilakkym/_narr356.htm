{{Metrical text}}

  1. nilantāḻ maruṅkiṉ ṟeṇkaṭaṉ mēynta
  2. vilaṅkumeṉ ṟūvic ceṅkā laṉṉam
  3. poṉpaṭu neṭuṅkōṭ ṭimayat tucci
  4. vāṉara makaḷirkku mēva lākum
  5. vaḷarāp pārppiṟ kalkirai yoyyu ((5))
  6. macaivi ṉōṉpaṟai pōlac celvara
  7. varuntiṉai vāḻiyeṉ ṉuḷḷa morunāṭ
  8. kātali yuḻaiya ḷākak
  9. kuṇakkuktōṉṟu veḷḷiyi ṉemakkumār varumē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • You suffered, live, my mind,
  • in going [and] coming, as the unwearying enduring wing
  • of the red-legged crane with shining soft feathers
  • that grazed the clear sea on a side hanging down to the ground,[FNote_1008]
  • which carries permanently(?) prey to [its] ungrown young one,
  • that is dear to celestials damsels in heaven(?),
  • on the summit of the Himālāya with long peaks where gold is found.
  • A day will certainly come for us, as the Venus appearing to the east,
  • when [our] beloved will be close [to us].

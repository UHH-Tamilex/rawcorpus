{{Readable Translation}}

  1. My eyes, they have verily given up sleep, friend,
  2. with the note ringing in the ear
  3. as if it came, even when it doesn't come,
  4. the long chariot with grouped(?) bells, crossing the village common
  5. deep at night, when the she-bird[FNote_1234] with pregnancy pangs calls,
  6. heavily pregnant, desiring the black-legged Aṉṟil bird,
  7. in the nest of small stalks made from the rich stems
  8. of the broad-based Palmyra palm, trunk [like] the base of a drum.[FNote_1235]

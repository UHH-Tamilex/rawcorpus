{{Readable Translation}}

  • Just see! Like this [was] ever before
  • her integrity, like Maranti with [its] ghat,
  • where the prey-searching heron gets [and] lets go
  • the many-legged crab [becuse of?] the clamour,
  • which sounds like the string(?) bent for taking [and] not taken(?)[FNote_149],
  • [arising] when the swarming bee thinks of fruit, thinks of [its] relations[FNote_150],
  • on the dark ripe fruits with full backs on the low-trunked Nāval tree,
  • on the shore where sand settles, overflown when dashed by foaming waves.
  • Even if she is pitiful without going near [her][FNote_151],
  • [is this her] beautiful integrity, slightly(?)[FNote_152] emaciated?
  • Like the passing intoxication by toddy in those who are delighted [by it],
  • [is this] desire? Her eye became pale[FNote_153].

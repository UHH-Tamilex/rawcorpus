{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Won't he remember, friend? And if he remembers,
  2. will he not come, because he doesn't take the opportunity[FNote_946],
  3. he who traversed the mountains with big dark[FNote_947] woods,
  4. where the large-necked Iralai, which has eaten[FNote_948] bowstring hemp for [its] food,
  5. sleeps in the dappled shade of the Yām tree[FNote_949]
  6. broken off, eaten [and] left aside by the mortar-leg elephant?

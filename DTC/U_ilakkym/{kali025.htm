Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 25-1} vayakkuṟu maṇṭilam vaṭamoḻi peyar peṟṟa
  • {KALI 25-2} mukattavaṉ makkaḷ uḷ mutiyavaṉ puṇarppiṉāl
  • {KALI 25-3} aivar eṉṟu ulaku ēttum aracarkaḷ akattar ā
  • {KALI 25-4} kai puṉai arakku illai kataḻ eri cūḻntāṅku
  • {KALI 25-5} kaḷi tikaḻ kaṭāatta kaṭum kaḷiṟu akatta ā
  • {KALI 25-6} muḷi kaḻai uyar malai muṟṟiya muḻaṅku aḻal
  • {KALI 25-7} oḷ uru arakku illai vaḷimakaṉ uṭaittu taṉ
  • {KALI 25-8} uḷḷattu kiḷaikaḷōṭu uya pōkuvāṉ pōla
  • {KALI 25-9} eḻu uṟaḻ taṭam kaiyiṉ iṉam kākkum eḻil vēḻam
  • {KALI 25-10} aḻuvam cūḻ pukai aḻal atarpaṭa mitittu tam
  • {KALI 25-11} kuḻuvoṭu puṇarntu pōm kuṉṟu aḻal ve curam
  • {KALI 25-12} iṟattirāl aiya maṟṟu ivaḷ nilaimai kēṭṭīmiṉ
  • {KALI 25-13} maṇakkum kāl malar aṉṉa takaiya āy ciṟitu nīr
  • {KALI 25-14} taṇakkum kāl kaluḻpu āṉā kaṇ eṉa um uḷa aṉṟu ō
  • {KALI 25-15} ciṟappu ceytu uḻaiyar ā pukaḻpu ētti maṟṟu avar
  • {KALI 25-16} puṟakkoṭai ē paḻi tūṟṟum pulliyār toṭarpu pōl
  • {KALI 25-17} īṅku nīr aḷikkum kāl iṟai ciṟantu oru nāḷ nīr
  • {KALI 25-18} nīṅkum kāl nekiḻpu ōṭum vaḷai eṉa um uḷa aṉṟu ō
  • {KALI 25-19} celvattu uḷ cērntavar vaḷaṉ uṇṭu maṟṟu avar
  • {KALI 25-20} olkattu nalkilā uṇarvu ilār toṭarpu pōl
  • {KALI 25-21} oru nāḷ nīr aḷikkum kāl oḷi ciṟantu oru nāḷ nīr
  • {KALI 25-22} pārāṭṭā kāl pacakkum nutal eṉa um uḷa aṉṟu ō
  • {KALI 25-23} poruntiya kēṇmaiyiṉ maṟai uṇarntu a maṟai
  • {KALI 25-24} pirinta kāl piṟarkku uraikkum pīṭu ilār toṭarpu pōl
  • {KALI 25-25} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 25-26} yām niṉ kūṟuvatu evaṉ uṇṭu emmiṉum
  • {KALI 25-27} nī naṟku aṟintaṉai neṭuntakai vāṉam
  • {KALI 25-28} tuḷi māṟu poḻutiṉ i ulakam pōlum niṉ
  • {KALI 25-29} aḷi māṟu poḻutiṉ i āy iḻai kaviṉ ē

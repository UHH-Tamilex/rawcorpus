  • {PU 257-7} kāṭu kaikkoṇṭaṉṟu um ilaṉ ē kālai
  • {PU 257-8} pullār iṉam nirai cel puṟam nōkki
  • {PU 257-9} kaiyiṉ cuṭṭi paiyeṉa eṇṇi
  • {PU 257-10} cilaiyiṉ māṟṟiyōṉ ē avai tām
  • {PU 257-11} mika pala āyiṉum eṉ ām eṉaittu um
  • {PU 257-12} veḷ kōḷ tōṉṟā kuḻiciyoṭu

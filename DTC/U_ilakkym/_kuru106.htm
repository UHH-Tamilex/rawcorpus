{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Word has come to us, o friend,
  2. from[FNote_426] the faultless heart
  3. of the man from a land where, like the waterfall descending the mountin
  4. appears the stone-climbing white root of the talbot fig with low aerial roots.[FNote_427]
  5. After receiving [his words] like fire into which ghee pours,[FNote_428]
  6. we too send a message saying 'we are [still] those he united with[FNote_429]'.

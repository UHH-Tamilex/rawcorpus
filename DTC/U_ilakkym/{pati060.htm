Patiṟṟup pattu

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 60-1} koli viṉi mēvaṟṟu tāṉi tāṉ ē
  • {PATI 60-2} ikal viṉi mēvalaṉ taṇṭātu vīcum
  • {PATI 60-3} cellāmō til pāṇmakaḷ kāṇiyar
  • {PATI 60-4} miñiṟu puṟam mūca um tīm cuvi tiriyātu
  • {PATI 60-5} aram pōḻkallā maram paṭu tīm kaṉi
  • {PATI 60-6} am cēṟu aminta muṇṭi viḷi paḻam
  • {PATI 60-7} āṟu cel mākkaṭku ōy taki taṭukkum
  • {PATI 60-8} maṟāa viḷiyuḷ aṟāa yāṇar
  • {PATI 60-9} toṭi maṭi kaḷinta cili uṭi maṟavar
  • {PATI 60-10} poṅku picir puṇari maṅkuloṭu mayaṅki
  • {PATI 60-11} varum kaṭal ūtiyiṉ paṉikkum
  • {PATI 60-12} tuvvā naṟaviṉ cāy iṉattāṉ ē

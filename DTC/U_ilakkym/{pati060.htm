Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 60-1} kolai viṉai mēvaṟṟu tāṉai tāṉ ē
  • {PATI 60-2} ikal viṉai mēvalaṉ taṇṭātu vīcum
  • {PATI 60-3} cellāmō til pāṇmakaḷ kāṇiyar
  • {PATI 60-4} miñiṟu puṟam mūca um tīm cuvai tiriyātu
  • {PATI 60-5} aram pōḻkallā maram paṭu tīm kaṉi
  • {PATI 60-6} am cēṟu amainta muṇṭai viḷai paḻam
  • {PATI 60-7} āṟu cel mākkaṭku ōy takai taṭukkum
  • {PATI 60-8} maṟāa viḷaiyuḷ aṟāa yāṇar
  • {PATI 60-9} toṭai maṭi kaḷainta cilai uṭai maṟavar
  • {PATI 60-10} poṅku picir puṇari maṅkuloṭu mayaṅki
  • {PATI 60-11} varum kaṭal ūtaiyiṉ paṉikkum
  • {PATI 60-12} tuvvā naṟaviṉ cāy iṉattāṉ ē

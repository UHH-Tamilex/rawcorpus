  2. miṉitō kāṇunark kāṇpuḻi vāḻtal
  3. kaṇṇuṟu viḻumaṅ kipō lutavi
  4. nammuṟu tuyaraṅ kaḷiyā rāyiṉu
  5. miṉṉā taṉṟē yavari lūrē ((5))
  6. yerimaruḷ vēṅkik kaṭavuḷ kākkuṅ
  7. kurukār kaḻaṉiyi ṉitaṇat tāṅka
  8. ṇēti lāḷaṉ kavali kavaṟṟa
  9. vorumuli yaṟutta tirumā vuṇṇik
  10. kēṭṭō raṉiyā rāyiṉum ((10))
  11. vēṭṭō rallatu piṟariṉ ṉārē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if he doesn't approach [us] with union to end the disgust,
  • is it pleasing to live while seeing the one that sees(?)[FNote_682]?
  • Even if he doesnōt remove the misery we have,
  • helping as a hand would [help][FNote_683] the distress of the eye,
  • not unpleasant is the village without him.
  • Even if he is one that has heard
  • of Tirumāvuṇṇi who tore off one of [her] breasts,[FNote_684]
  • greatly distressed[FNote_685] by a stranger
  • there on the platform in the fields where the herons become full,
  • where the god guards the flame-like Vēṅki tree,
  • besides wanting others(?)[FNote_686], he is unpleasing.

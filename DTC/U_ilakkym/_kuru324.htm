{{Readable Translation}}

  1. By the big seashore grove ghat, where the murderous, strong male
  2. of the bent-legged crocodile tears apart what wanders on the way[FNote_1319],
  3. you swim the dark backwaters with fishes in swarms
  4. [and] come, possessed by longing.
  5. While she, possessed by her inexperience, is weakened -
  6. as if my twin[FNote_1320] children had eaten poison,
  7. I fear that, o great one, in my heart.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. iṟaiyu maruntoḻiṉ muṭitteṉap poṟaiya
  2. kaṇpō ṉīlañ cuṉaitoṟu malara
  3. vītā vēṅkaiya viyaṉeṭum puṟavi
  4. ṉimmeṉ paṟavai yīṇṭukiḷai yiriya
  5. neṭunteru vaṉṉa nērko ṇeṭuvaḻi ((5))
  6. yiḷaiya rēkuvaṉar parippa vaḷaiyeṉak
  7. kāntaḷ vaḷḷitaḻ kavikuḷam paṟuppat
  8. tōḷvali yāppa vīṇṭunam varaviṉaip
  9. puḷḷaṟi vuṟīiyiṉa kollō teḷḷitiṟ
  10. kātal keḻumiya nalatta ḷētil ((10))
  11. putalvaṟ kāṭṭip poykkun
  12. titalai yalkuṟ ṟēmoḻi yāṭkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Have the birds informed [her], clearly,
  • of our coming here, so that the strength of [her] arms shall tie [us][FNote_519],
  • while the covering hoofs cuts off the pure petals of Malabar-lilies,
  • as if [they were] conches, while [our] young [attendants] on the move are
  • hurrying on the long way taken as straight as a long street[FNote_520],
  • so that the gathering swarms of hummning wings[FNote_521] retreat
  • in the wide long woodland of Vēṅkai trees spreading blossoms,
  • while in every mountain pool blue [lotusses] resembling eyes flower
  • on the heights(?)[FNote_522], because even the monarch has completed [his] difficult task,
  • - her of sweet words, [her] hips with beauty spots,
  • who is playing(?)[FNote_523], showing [her] son
  • something strange, the good one full of love?

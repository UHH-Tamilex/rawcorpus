{{Readable Translation}}

  • Pleasing indeed, alas, [was] it,
  • the little village with pretty homes for those who live in cool(?)[FNote_435] homes,
  • where they immerse in the coming festival with unlimited musical modes
  • in the frontyard abounding in sand that has buried the big trunks
  • of the palmyra palms with high stems [and] hanging leaves
  • - before he had smiled with us, the one with the chariot
  • with horses the pure manes of which glittered,
  • that revolved so that the new sand by the thundering waves was impressed,
  • with poor motion, fatigued by many deserts, when the dew fell.[FNote_436]

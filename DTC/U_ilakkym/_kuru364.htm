{{Readable Translation}}

  1. One who has on herself golden, broad, shining bangles, fitting
  2. to the man from the village, where the Vāḷi fish is obtined as dily prey
  3. by an otter with a back striped like the reed with creepers in a thicket,
  4. talks [about me] behind my back, [so] they say. So that it be clear
  5. also the days of the Tuṇaṅki dance are come[FNote_1450]
  6. for women with bright bangles [and] bamboo shoulders with flexible joints
  7. - so(?), a battle for warriors who ascend to taking union[FNote_1451]
  8. with them(?) at just that [occasion?], while [their] eyes fight?![FNote_1452]

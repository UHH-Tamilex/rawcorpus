Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 31-1} kaṭum puṉal kāl paṭṭu kaluḻ tēṟi kaviṉ peṟa
  • {KALI 31-2} neṭum kayattu ayal ayal ayir tōṉṟa a maṇal
  • {KALI 31-3} vaṭuttu ūra varippa pōl īṅki vāṭu utirpu uka
  • {KALI 31-4} pirintavar nutal pōla pīr vīya kātalar
  • {KALI 31-5} puṇarntavar mukam pōla poyki pū putitu īṉa
  • {KALI 31-6} mey kūrnta paṉiyoṭu mēl niṉṟa vāṭiyāl
  • {KALI 31-7} kiyāṟu kaṭikkūṭṭa kalakkuṟūum poḻutu maṉ
  • {KALI 31-8} poyyēm eṉṟu āy iḻāy puṇarntavar urittatu ai
  • {KALI 31-9} mayaṅku amar māṟu aṭṭu maṇ vuvi varupavar
  • {KALI 31-10} tayaṅkiya kaḷiṟṟiṉ mēl taki kāṇa viṭuvatu ō
  • {KALI 31-11} payam keḻu pal katir pāl pōlum poḻutoṭu
  • {KALI 31-12} vayaṅku iḻi taṇṇeṉa vanta i aci vāṭi
  • {KALI 31-13} tāḷ valampaṭa veṉṟu taki nal mā mēl koṇṭu
  • {KALI 31-14} vāḷ veṉṟu varupavar vaṉappu āra viṭuvatu ō
  • {KALI 31-15} nīḷ kaḻi nivanta pū niṟam vāṭa tūṟṟupu
  • {KALI 31-16} tōḷ atirpu akam cēra tuvaṟṟum i cil maḻi
  • {KALI 31-17} paki veṉṟu tiṟi koṇṭa pāy tiṇ tēr miciyavar
  • {KALI 31-18} vaki koṇṭa cemmal nām vaṉappu āra viṭuvatu ō
  • {KALI 31-19} puki eṉa putal cūḻntu pū am kaḷ poti ceyyā
  • {KALI 31-20} muki veḷ pal nuti pora muṟṟiya kaṭum paṉi
  • {KALI 31-21} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 31-22} vāḷāti vayaṅku iḻāy varuntuvaḷ ivaḷ eṉa
  • {KALI 31-23} nāḷ vari niṟuttu tām colliya poy aṉṟi
  • {KALI 31-24} mīḷi vēl tāṉiyar pukutantār
  • {KALI 31-25} nīḷ uyar kūṭal neṭum koṭi eḻa ē

  • {PU 76-8} pāṭu iṉ teḷ kiṇai kaṟaṅka kāṇtaka
  • {PU 76-9} nāṭu keḻu tiruviṉ pacum pūṇ ceḻiyaṉ
  • {PU 76-10} pīṭu um cemmal um aṟiyār kūṭi
  • {PU 76-11} porutum eṉṟu taṉ talai vanta
  • {PU 76-12} puṉai kaḻal eḻuvar nal valam aṭaṅka

  • {PU 76-2} putuvatu aṉṟu i ulakattu iyaṟki

  • {PU 76-4} maṉṟa vēmpiṉ mā ciṉi oḷ taḷir
  • {PU 76-5} neṭum koṭi uḻiñi pavaroṭu miṭintu
  • {PU 76-6} ceṟiya toṭutta tēm pāy kaṇṇi
  • {PU 76-7} oliyal māliyoṭu poliya cūṭi
  • {PU 76-8} pāṭu iṉ teḷ kiṇi kaṟaṅka kāṇtaka
  • {PU 76-9} nāṭu keḻu tiruviṉ pacum pūṇ ceḻiyaṉ
  • {PU 76-10} pīṭu um cemmal um aṟiyār kūṭi
  • {PU 76-11} porutum eṉṟu taṉ tali vanta
  • {PU 76-12} puṉi kaḻal eḻuvar nal valam aṭaṅka
  • {PU 76-13} oru tāṉ āki porutu kaḷattu aṭal ē

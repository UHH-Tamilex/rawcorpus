  1. Pitiable me, when the [one with] red jewels there
  2. sees [my] husband[FNote_1200]: she comes,
  3. so that the contrasting(?)[FNote_1201] calyx [and] foliage of the white water lily
  4. slap aginst [each of her] beauty-spot thighs alternately(?)[FNote_1202],
  5. like the old village of Ātiyarumaṉ,
  6. where inside the village on a journey[FNote_1203] to the toddy house the friends
  7. come back(?) with the kernels from the high dark Palmyra palm,
  8. of small unripe fruits with fibre, given by the spaths.(?)[FNote_1204]

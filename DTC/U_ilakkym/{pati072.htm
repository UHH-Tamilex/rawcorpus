Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 72-1} ikal perumiyiṉ paṭi kōḷ añcār
  • {PATI 72-2} cūḻātu tuṇital allatu vaṟitu uṭaṉ
  • {PATI 72-3} kāval etirār kaṟuttōr nāṭu niṉ
  • {PATI 72-4} muṉ tiṇi mutalvarkku ōmpiṉar uṟintu
  • {PATI 72-5} maṉpati kāppa aṟivu valiyuṟuttum
  • {PATI 72-6} naṉṟu aṟi uḷḷattu cāṉṟōr aṉṉa niṉ
  • {PATI 72-7} paṇpu naṉku aṟiyār maṭam perumiyiṉ
  • {PATI 72-8} tuñcal uṟūum pakal puku māli
  • {PATI 72-9} nilam poṟi orāa nīr ñemara vantu īṇṭi
  • {PATI 72-10} uravu tiri kaṭukiya uruttu eḻu veḷḷam
  • {PATI 72-11} variyā mātirattu iruḷ cērpu parantu
  • {PATI 72-12} ñāyiṟu paṭṭa akaṉṟu varu kūṭṭattu
  • {PATI 72-13} am cāṟu puriyum niṉ toḻil oḻittu
  • {PATI 72-14} poṅku picir nuṭakkiya ce cuṭar nikaḻviṉ
  • {PATI 72-15} maṭaṅkal tīyiṉ aṉiyi
  • {PATI 72-16} ciṉam keḻu kurucil niṉ uṭaṟṟiciṉōrkku ē

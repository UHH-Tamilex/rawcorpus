Paripāṭal 17

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 17-1} tēm paṭu malar kuḻi pūm tukil vaṭi maṇi
  • {PARI 17-2} ēntu ili cumantu cāntam virii
  • {PARI 17-3} viṭi ari acitta vēlaṉ kaṭimaram
  • {PARI 17-4} paraviṉar uriyoṭu paṇṇiya iciyiṉar
  • {PARI 17-5} viri malar matuviṉ maram naṉi kuṉṟattu
  • {PARI 17-6} kōl eri koḷi naṟi puki koṭi oruṅku eḻa
  • {PARI 17-7} māli māli aṭiyuṟi iyinar
  • {PARI 17-8} mēlōr uṟiyuḷ um vēṇṭunar yāar
  • {PARI 17-9} oru tiṟam pāṇar yāḻiṉ tīm kural eḻa
  • {PARI 17-10} oru tiṟam yāṇar vaṇṭiṉ imir ici eḻa
  • {PARI 17-11} oru tiṟam kaṇ ār kuḻaliṉ karipu eḻa
  • {PARI 17-12} oru tiṟam paṇ ār tumpi parantu ici ūta
  • {PARI 17-13} oru tiṟam maṇ ār muḻaviṉ ici eḻa
  • {PARI 17-14} oru tiṟam aṇṇal neṭu vari aruvi nīr tatumpa
  • {PARI 17-15} oru tiṟam pāṭal nal viṟaliyar olkupu nuṭaṅka
  • {PARI 17-16} oru tiṟam vāṭi uḷar vayiṉ pūm koṭi nuṭaṅka
  • {PARI 17-17} oru tiṟam pāṭiṉi muralum pāli am kuraliṉ
  • {PARI 17-18} nīṭu kiḷar kiḻami niṟi kuṟi tōṉṟa
  • {PARI 17-19} oru tiṟam āṭu cīr maññi ari kural tōṉṟa
  • {PARI 17-20} māṟu māṟu uṟṟaṉa pōl māṟu etir kōṭal
  • {PARI 17-21} māṟu aṭṭāṉ kuṉṟam uṭittu
  • {PARI 17-22} pāṭal cāṉṟu pal pukaḻ muṟṟiya
  • {PARI 17-23} kūṭaloṭu paraṅkuṉṟiṉ iṭi
  • {PARI 17-24} kamaḻ naṟum cāntiṉ avar avar tiḷippa
  • {PARI 17-25} naṇi naṇittu āyiṉum cēey cēyttu
  • {PARI 17-26} makiḻ miku tēem kōtiyar kūntal kuñciyiṉ
  • {PARI 17-27} cōrntu aviḻ itaḻiṉ iyaṅkum āṟu iṉṟu
  • {PARI 17-28} vaci nīṅkiya vāymiyāl vēḷviyāl
  • {PARI 17-29} tici nāṟiya kuṉṟu amarntu āṇṭu āṇṭu
  • {PARI 17-30} āvi uṇṇum akil keḻu kamaḻ puki
  • {PARI 17-31} vāy vāy mī pōy umpar imipu iṟappa
  • {PARI 17-32} tēyā maṇṭilam kāṇum āṟu iṉṟu
  • {PARI 17-33} vaḷi muṉki vaṇaṅku iṟiyār
  • {PARI 17-34} aṇi mel tōḷ acipu ottār
  • {PARI 17-35} tār mārpiṉ taki iyalār
  • {PARI 17-36} īra māli iyal aṇiyār
  • {PARI 17-37} maṉam makiḻ tūṅkunar pāypu uṭaṉ āṭa
  • {PARI 17-38} cuṉi malar tātu ūtum vaṇṭu ūtal eytā
  • {PARI 17-39} aṉiya paraṅkuṉṟiṉ aṇi
  • {PARI 17-40} kīḻōr vayal parakkum vār veḷ aruvi parantu āṉātu arō
  • {PARI 17-41} mēlōr iyaṅkutalāl vīḻ maṇi nīlam ceṟu uḻakkum arō
  • {PARI 17-42} teyva viḻavu um tiruntu viruntu ayarvu um
  • {PARI 17-43} a veḷ aruvi aṇi paraṅkuṉṟiṟku um
  • {PARI 17-44} toyyā viḻu cīr vaḷam keḻu viyikku um
  • {PARI 17-45} koy uḷi māṉ tēr koṭi tērāṉ kūṭaṟku um
  • {PARI 17-46} ki ūḻ taṭumāṟṟam naṉṟu
  • {PARI 17-47} eṉa āṅku
  • {PARI 17-48} maṇi niṟa maññi ōṅkiya puḷ koṭi
  • {PARI 17-49} piṇimukam ūrnta vel pōr iṟiva
  • {PARI 17-50} paṇi orīi niṉ pukaḻ ētti
  • {PARI 17-51} aṇi neṭum kuṉṟam pāṭutum toḻutum
  • {PARI 17-52} avi yām um em cuṟṟam um paravutum
  • {PARI 17-53} ēma vikal peṟuka yām eṉa ē

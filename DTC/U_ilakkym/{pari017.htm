Paripāṭal 17

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 17-1} tēm paṭu malar kuḻai pūm tukil vaṭi maṇi
  • {PARI 17-2} ēntu ilai cumantu cāntam viraii
  • {PARI 17-3} viṭai arai acaitta vēlaṉ kaṭimaram
  • {PARI 17-4} paraviṉar uraiyoṭu paṇṇiya icaiyiṉar
  • {PARI 17-5} viri malar matuviṉ maram naṉai kuṉṟattu
  • {PARI 17-6} kōl eri koḷai naṟai pukai koṭi oruṅku eḻa
  • {PARI 17-7} mālai mālai aṭiyuṟai iyainar
  • {PARI 17-8} mēlōr uṟaiyuḷ um vēṇṭunar yāar
  • {PARI 17-9} oru tiṟam pāṇar yāḻiṉ tīm kural eḻa
  • {PARI 17-10} oru tiṟam yāṇar vaṇṭiṉ imir icai eḻa
  • {PARI 17-11} oru tiṟam kaṇ ār kuḻaliṉ karaipu eḻa
  • {PARI 17-12} oru tiṟam paṇ ār tumpi parantu icai ūta
  • {PARI 17-13} oru tiṟam maṇ ār muḻaviṉ icai eḻa
  • {PARI 17-14} oru tiṟam aṇṇal neṭu varai aruvi nīr tatumpa
  • {PARI 17-15} oru tiṟam pāṭal nal viṟaliyar olkupu nuṭaṅka
  • {PARI 17-16} oru tiṟam vāṭai uḷar vayiṉ pūm koṭi nuṭaṅka
  • {PARI 17-17} oru tiṟam pāṭiṉi muralum pālai am kuraliṉ
  • {PARI 17-18} nīṭu kiḷar kiḻamai niṟai kuṟai tōṉṟa
  • {PARI 17-19} oru tiṟam āṭu cīr maññai ari kural tōṉṟa
  • {PARI 17-20} māṟu māṟu uṟṟaṉa pōl māṟu etir kōṭal
  • {PARI 17-21} māṟu aṭṭāṉ kuṉṟam uṭaittu
  • {PARI 17-22} pāṭal cāṉṟu pal pukaḻ muṟṟiya
  • {PARI 17-23} kūṭaloṭu paraṅkuṉṟiṉ iṭai
  • {PARI 17-24} kamaḻ naṟum cāntiṉ avar avar tiḷaippa
  • {PARI 17-25} naṇi naṇittu āyiṉum cēey cēyttu
  • {PARI 17-26} makiḻ miku tēem kōtaiyar kūntal kuñciyiṉ
  • {PARI 17-27} cōrntu aviḻ itaḻiṉ iyaṅkum āṟu iṉṟu
  • {PARI 17-28} vacai nīṅkiya vāymaiyāl vēḷviyāl
  • {PARI 17-29} ticai nāṟiya kuṉṟu amarntu āṇṭu āṇṭu
  • {PARI 17-30} āvi uṇṇum akil keḻu kamaḻ pukai
  • {PARI 17-31} vāy vāy mī pōy umpar imaipu iṟappa
  • {PARI 17-32} tēyā maṇṭilam kāṇum āṟu iṉṟu
  • {PARI 17-33} vaḷai muṉkai vaṇaṅku iṟaiyār
  • {PARI 17-34} aṇai mel tōḷ acaipu ottār
  • {PARI 17-35} tār mārpiṉ takai iyalār
  • {PARI 17-36} īra mālai iyal aṇiyār
  • {PARI 17-37} maṉam makiḻ tūṅkunar pāypu uṭaṉ āṭa
  • {PARI 17-38} cuṉai malar tātu ūtum vaṇṭu ūtal eytā
  • {PARI 17-39} aṉaiya paraṅkuṉṟiṉ aṇi
  • {PARI 17-40} kīḻōr vayal parakkum vār veḷ aruvi parantu āṉātu arō
  • {PARI 17-41} mēlōr iyaṅkutalāl vīḻ maṇi nīlam ceṟu uḻakkum arō
  • {PARI 17-42} teyva viḻavu um tiruntu viruntu ayarvu um
  • {PARI 17-43} a veḷ aruvi aṇi paraṅkuṉṟiṟku um
  • {PARI 17-44} toyyā viḻu cīr vaḷam keḻu vaiyaikku um
  • {PARI 17-45} koy uḷai māṉ tēr koṭi tērāṉ kūṭaṟku um
  • {PARI 17-46} kai ūḻ taṭumāṟṟam naṉṟu
  • {PARI 17-47} eṉa āṅku
  • {PARI 17-48} maṇi niṟa maññai ōṅkiya puḷ koṭi
  • {PARI 17-49} piṇimukam ūrnta vel pōr iṟaiva
  • {PARI 17-50} paṇi orīi niṉ pukaḻ ētti
  • {PARI 17-51} aṇi neṭum kuṉṟam pāṭutum toḻutum
  • {PARI 17-52} avai yām um em cuṟṟam um paravutum
  • {PARI 17-53} ēma vaikal peṟuka yām eṉa ē

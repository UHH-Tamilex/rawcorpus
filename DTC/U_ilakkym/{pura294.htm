  • {PU 294-2} kaṇ kūṭu iṟutta kaṭal maruḷ pācaṟai
  • {PU 294-3} kumari paṭai taḻīiya kūṟṟu viṉai āṭavar
  • {PU 294-4} tamar piṟar aṟiyā amar mayaṅku aḻuvattu
  • {PU 294-5} iṟai um peyar um tōṟṟi numar uḷ
  • {PU 294-6} nāḷ muṟai taputtīr vammiṉ īṅku eṉa
  • {PU 294-7} pōr malaintu oru ciṟai niṟpa yāvar um
  • {PU 294-8} aravu umiḻ maṇiyiṉ kuṟukār
  • {PU 294-9} nirai tār mārpiṉ niṉ kēḷvaṉai piṟar ē

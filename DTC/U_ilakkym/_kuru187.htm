{{Readable Translation}}

  1. He is harder than stone, friend,
  2. the man from a land, where the male lamb of the mountin sheep(?)
  3. resting on the red mountin leaps about in the shadow of the big mountin
  4. [and] feeds to satisfaction on the sweet milk flowing from the udder.
  5. Without saying 'he is hard' my heart is softening.

Altern: Cam.

5b {--!}Without thinking 'he [is] strong (and accordingly will soon have finished his {--!}work and come back)' my heart is softening.

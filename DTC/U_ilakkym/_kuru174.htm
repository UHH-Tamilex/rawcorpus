{{Readable Translation}}

  1. If he separates because of wealth
  2. [and] abandons us without thinking: difficult [are] the roads,
  3. where the pair of doves with dense, soft feathers is driven away
  4. by the sharp sound of falling unripe fruits of the fork-thorned Kaḷḷi tree
  5. in the lonely[FNote_699] wilderness abandoned [even] by pouring rain,
  6. - in this world really just[FNote_700] wealth [is] wealth,
  7. consideration really is not enough[FNote_701].

Altern

7b: consideration really doesn't exist for anybody.

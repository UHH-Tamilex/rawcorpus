{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The one who has abandoned [us] here[FNote_687], so that we are by ourselves[FNote_688]
  2. in sorrowful evening, when the bats with light flight
  3. [and] rushing, fine wings set out for trees with ripening [fruit],
  4. is he happy to be alone?
  5. Like the bellows pressed in the smithy
  6. of one village bound to the public work of seven villages,[FNote_689]
  7. without [my] head[FNote_690] knowing [any] limit my heart suffers.

Altern

7b: without knowing an upper limit my heart suffers.

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 17-1} puraivatu niṉaippiṉ puraivatu ō iṉṟu ē
  • {PATI 17-2} periya tappunar āyiṉum pakaivar
  • {PATI 17-3} paṇintu tiṟai pakara koḷḷunai ātaliṉ
  • {PATI 17-4} tuḷaṅku picir uṭaiya mā kaṭal nīkki
  • {PATI 17-5} kaṭampu aṟuttu iyaṟṟiya valampaṭu viyaṉ paṇai
  • {PATI 17-6} āṭunar peyarntu vantu arum pali tūuy
  • {PATI 17-7} kaṭippu kaṇ uṟūum toṭi tōḷ iyavar
  • {PATI 17-8} araṇam kāṇātu mātiram tuḻaiiya
  • {PATI 17-9} naṉam talai paim ñilam varuka i niḻal eṉa
  • {PATI 17-10} ñāyiṟu pukaṉṟa tītu tīr ciṟappiṉ
  • {PATI 17-11} amiḻtu tikaḻ karuviya kaṇa maḻai talaii
  • {PATI 17-12} kaṭum kāl koṭkum nal perum parappiṉ
  • {PATI 17-13} vicumpu tōy veḷ kuṭai nuvalum
  • {PATI 17-14} pacum pūṇ mārpa pāṭiṉi vēntu ē

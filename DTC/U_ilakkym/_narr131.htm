{{Readable Translation}}

  • Do we possess also being vexed, o man from the coast of high sands,
  • with a suffering heart that does not remember
  • the task(?)[FNote_420] when we played and the grove where we stayed?
  • - in order to forget you,[FNote_421] while my good shoulders gets emaciated,
  • as the high river smelling of nectar
  • of the great one with good chariot from a residence(?) delighting in toddy,
  • where the heron from a group becoming full of prawn perches itself, by the way of taking a seat, so that the palm leaf, thorny as a shark's horn, is broken
  • on the broad-based Tāḻai tree with a trunk saturated(?) by the waves[FNote_422].

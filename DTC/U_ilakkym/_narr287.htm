{{Metrical text}}

  1. vicumpuṟaḻ purici vempa muṟṟip
  2. piṅkaṇ yāṉi vēntupuṟat tiṟutta
  3. nalleyi luṭiyō ruṭiya meṉṉum
  4. peruntaki maṟavaṉ pōlak koṭuṅkaḻip
  5. pācaṭi neytaṟ paṉinīrc cērppa ((5))
  6. ṉāma mutali naṭuṅkupaki yañcāṉ
  7. kāmam perumiyiṉ vanta ñāṉṟi
  8. yarukā tākiya vaṉka ṇeñca
  9. naḷḷeṉ kaṅkuṟ puḷḷoli kēṭṭoṟun
  10. tērmaṇit teḷḷicik kolleṉa ((10))
  11. vūrmaṭi kaṅkulun tuyiṉmaṟan tatuvē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Whenever [my] strong heart that was not afrid
  • at the time he came, for desire [is] greast],
  • without fear of the shivering[FNote_879] enmity of the fear[-ful] crocodile,
  • the man from the coast of dewy waters with green-leaved blue water-lilies,
  • saying "we have those that have a good fortress,
  • where a king with green-eyed elephants tarried outside,
  • the fortifications up to the sky furiously[FNote_880] completed",
  • hears the birds' sound deep at night,
  • [asking] "[is it] the clear sound of [his] chariot's bell?",
  • all [this] night when the village droops moon it has fogotten sleep.

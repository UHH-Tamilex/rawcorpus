  1. naṉimikap pacantu tōḷuñ cāayp

  3. viṉṉuyi raṉṉa pirivaruṅ kātalar
  4. nīttu nīṭiṉa reṉṉuma pulavi
  5. yuṭkoṇ ṭūṭiṉṟu milaiyō maṭantai ((5))
  6. yuvakkāṇ ṭōṉṟuva vōṅki viyappuṭai
  7. yiravalar varūu maḷavai yaṇṭiraṉ
  8. puravetirntu tokutta yāṉai pōla
  9. vulaka muvappa vōtarum
  10. vēṟuppal luruvi ṉērtaru maḻaiyē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The vexation that says: "[our] lover from whom separation is difficult,
  • as from [our] sweet life has left [and] prolonged [his absence],
  • [our] shoulder, very much faded[FNote_745], becoming emaciated,
  • [our] eyes abounding in dew unlike [they were] formerly",
  • it didn't take it to heart and pick quarrel at all, girl?
  • See these[FNote_746]: there appear clouds that rise
  • in many different forms, difficult to count(?)[FNote_747],
  • for the world to rejoice, just like the elephants
  • gathered, when facing a grant[FNote_748], by Aṇṭiraṉ at the time
  • the beggers are coming [to him] in high amazement.[FNote_749]

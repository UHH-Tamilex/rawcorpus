Paripāṭal 22

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 22-1} oḷiṟu vāḷ poruppaṉ uṭal camattu iṟutta
  • {PARI 22-2} kaḷiṟu nirittavi pōl koṇmū neritara
  • {PARI 22-3} aracu paṭa kaṭanta āṉā cīṟṟattavaṉ
  • {PARI 22-4} muracu atirpavi pōl muḻaṅku iṭi payiṟṟi
  • {PARI 22-5} oṭuṅkār uṭaṉṟavaṉ tāṉi vil vici
  • {PARI 22-6} viṭum kaṇi oppiṉ kataḻ uṟi citaṟūu
  • {PARI 22-7} kaṇ oḷir eḵkiṉ kaṭiya miṉṉi avaṉ
  • {PARI 22-8} vaṇmi pōl vāṉam poḻinta nīr maṇ mici
  • {PARI 22-9} āṉātu vantu tokupu īṇṭi maṟṟu avaṉ
  • {PARI 22-10} tāṉiyiṉ ūḻi tāvū kattiṉ
  • {PARI 22-11} pōṉa nilam ellām pōr ār vayal pukuta
  • {PARI 22-12} nīkki
  • {PARI 22-13} kāṉa malittari koṉṟu maṇala piṉaṟī
  • {PARI 22-14} vāṉa malitta
  • {PARI 22-15} maṇa muracu eṟitara
  • {PARI 22-16} tāṉi talitali vantu mintu uṟṟu
  • {PARI 22-17} poṟivi yāṟṟuṟi tuvar puki cāntam
  • {PARI 22-18} eṟivaṉa ekkuva īraṇikku ēṟṟa
  • {PARI 22-19} naṟavu aṇi pūm tukil nal pala ēnti
  • {PARI 22-20} piṟa toḻiṉa piṉ piṉ toṭara
  • {PARI 22-21} ceṟi viṉi polinta ce pūm kaṇṇiyar
  • {PARI 22-22} īr ami veṭci itaḻ puṉi kōtiyar
  • {PARI 22-23} tār ār muṭiyar taki keḻu mārpiṉar
  • {PARI 22-24} mā um kaḷiṟu um maṇi aṇi vēcari
  • {PARI 22-25} kāvu niṟiya kari neripu īṇṭi
  • {PARI 22-26} vēl āṟṟum moympaṉiṉ viri malar ampiṉōṉ
  • {PARI 22-27} pōl āṟṟu muṉpiṉ puṉi kaḻal mintaroṭu
  • {PARI 22-28} tār aṇi mintar tava payaṉ cāṉm eṉa
  • {PARI 22-29} kār aṇi kūntal kayal kaṇ kavir itaḻ
  • {PARI 22-30} vār aṇi kommi vaki ami mēkali
  • {PARI 22-31} ēr aṇi ilaṅku eyiṟṟu iṉ nakiyavar
  • {PARI 22-32} cīr aṇi viyikku aṇi kol ō viyi taṉ
  • {PARI 22-33} nīr aṇi nīttam ivarkku aṇi kol eṉa
  • {PARI 22-34} tērunar tērum kāl tērtaṟku aritu kāṇ
  • {PARI 22-35} tīram um viyi um cērkiṉṟa kaṇ kaviṉ
  • {PARI 22-36} maṇ kaṇi muḻaviṉ iṉ kaṇ imiḻviṟku
  • {PARI 22-37} etirva poruvi mēṟu māṟu imiḻppa
  • {PARI 22-38} kavar toṭi nal yāḻ imiḻa kāvil
  • {PARI 22-39} pukar vari vaṇṭu iṉam pūm ciṉi imira
  • {PARI 22-40} ūtu cīr tīm kuḻal iyampa malar mici
  • {PARI 22-41} tātu ūtu tumpi tavirpu ala iyampa
  • {PARI 22-42} tuṭi cīr naṭatta vaḷi naṭaṉ
  • {PARI 22-43} mel iṇar pūm koṭi mēvara nuṭaṅka
  • {PARI 22-44} āṅku avi tam tam toḻil māṟu koḷḷum
  • {PARI 22-45} tīm puṉal viyi tirumaruta muṉtuṟiyāl
  • {PARI 22-46} kōṭu uḷar kural poli oli tuyal irum kūntal
  • {PARI 22-47} puri tīr neṭu mel
  • {PARI 22-48} tōḷ tāḻpu taḻi malar tuvaḷā valliyiṉ
  • {PARI 22-49} nīḷ tāḻpu tōkki nittila ari cilampu

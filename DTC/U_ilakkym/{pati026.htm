  • {PATI 26-1} tēer paranta pulam ēer paravā
  • {PATI 26-2} kaḷiṟu āṭiya pulam nāñcil āṭā
  • {PATI 26-3} mattu uraṟiya maṉai iṉ iyam imiḻā
  • {PATI 26-4} āṅku paṇṭu naṟku aṟiyunar ceḻu vaḷam niṉaippiṉ
  • {PATI 26-5} nōku ō yāṉ ē nōtaka varum ē
  • {PATI 26-6} peyal maḻai puravu iṉṟu āki veytuṟṟu
  • {PATI 26-7} valam iṉṟu amma kālaiyatu paṇpu eṉa
  • {PATI 26-8} kaṇ paṉi malir niṟai tāṅki kai puṭaiyū
  • {PATI 26-9} melivu uṭai neñciṉar ciṟumai kūra
  • {PATI 26-10} pīr ivar vēli pāḻ maṉai neruñci
  • {PATI 26-11} kāṭuṟu kaṭu neṟi āka maṉṉiya
  • {PATI 26-12} muruku uṭaṉṟu kaṟutta kali aḻi mūtūr
  • {PATI 26-13} urumpu il kūṟṟattu aṉṉa niṉ
  • {PATI 26-14} tiruntu toḻil vayavar cīṟiya nāṭu ē

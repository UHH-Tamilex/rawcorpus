{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Cock with your troupe [and your] forehead gathering red
  2. like the bright flower of the Malabar lily[FNote_432] in overlapping clusters,
  3. may you suffer severe punishment,
  4. becoming lasting[FNote_433] prey for the young wild cat
  5. that looks for rats in the house at midnight in the deep of darkness
  6. - you who aroused me from the delightful, the pleasurable sleep
  7. I dwelt in with him, the man from the fertile village at the long water.

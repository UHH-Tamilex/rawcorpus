  1. Very few [were] the days the delightful man embraced,
  2. for the garland of intertwined blossoms to soften. The gossip,
  3. it [is] louder than the roaring of the Koṅkars with bright swords,
  4. when the energetic Atikaṉ, the Pāṇṭiya with green ornaments[FNote_1569],
  5. fell with the elephant bull,
  6. in the desert place of Vākai [full of] owls [and] fowl[FNote_1570].

{{Metrical text}}

  1. uḷḷutoṟu nakuvēṉ tōḻi vaḷḷukirp
  2. piṭipiḷan tiṭṭa nārilveṇ kōṭṭuk
  3. koṭiṟupōl kāya vāliṇarp pālaic
  4. celvaḷi tūkkali ṉilaitīr neṟṟaṅ
  5. kalliḻi yaruviyi ṉolleṉa volikkum ((5))
  6. pullilai yōmaiya pulivaḻaṅ kattañ
  7. ceṉṟa kātalar vaḻivaḻip paṭṭa
  8. neñcē nalviṉaip pāṟṟē yīṇṭoḻin
  9. tāṉāk kauvai malainta
  10. yāṉē tōḻi nōyppā lēṉē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Whenever remembering I smile, friend:[FNote_356]
  • [My] heart, that set out to the lover who had gone
  • on the road where tigers roam, with low-leaved Ōmai trees,
  • where the leafless[FNote_357] nuts, when the moving wind lifts [them]
  • on the Pālai trees with pure clusters [and] unripe fruits, as pincers,
  • on the barkless white branches split [and] put aside by the sharp-nailed she-elephant, sound "ol" as the waterfall descending from the stones,
  • good work [was] its part. I,
  • who was opposed by unending scandal, staying behind here,
  • my part, friend, [was] pain.

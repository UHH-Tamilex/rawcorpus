{{Metrical text}}

  1. uḷḷutoṟu nakuvēṉ tōḻi vaḷḷukirp
  2. piṭipiḷan tiṭṭa nārilveṇ kōṭṭuk
  3. koṭiṟupōl kāya vāliṇarp pālic
  4. celvaḷi tūkkali ṉilitīr neṟṟaṅ
  5. kalliḻi yaruviyi ṉolleṉa volikkum ((5))
  6. pullili yōmiya pulivaḻaṅ kattañ
  7. ceṉṟa kātalar vaḻivaḻip paṭṭa
  8. neñcē nalviṉip pāṟṟē yīṇṭoḻin
  9. tāṉāk kuvi malinta
  10. yāṉē tōḻi nōyppā lēṉē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Whenever remembering I smile, friend:[FNote_356]
  • [My] heart, that set out to the lover who had gone
  • on the road where tigers roam, with low-leaved Ōmi trees,
  • where the leafless[FNote_357] nuts, when the moving wind lifts [them]
  • on the Pāli trees with pure clusters [and] unripe fruits, as pincers,
  • on the barkless white branches split [and] put aside by the sharp-niled she-elephant, sound "ol" as the waterfall descending from the stones,
  • good work [was] its part. I,
  • who was opposed by unending scandal, staying behind here,
  • my part, friend, [was] pin.

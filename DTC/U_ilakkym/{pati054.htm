Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 54-1} vaḷḷiyai eṉṟaliṉ kāṇku vanticiṉ ē
  • {PATI 54-2} uḷḷiyatu muṭitti vāḻka niṉ kaṇṇi
  • {PATI 54-3} vīṅku iṟai taṭaiiya amai maruḷ paṇai tōḷ
  • {PATI 54-4} ēntu eḻil maḻai kaṇ vaṉaintu varal iḷa mulai
  • {PATI 54-5} pūm tukil alkul tēm pāy kūntal
  • {PATI 54-6} miṉ iḻai viṟaliyar niṉ maṟam pāṭa
  • {PATI 54-7} iravalar puṉkaṇ tīra nāḷ toṟum
  • {PATI 54-8} urai cāl nal kalam varaivu ila vīci
  • {PATI 54-9} aṉaiyai ākaṉ māṟu ē eṉaiyatūum
  • {PATI 54-10} uyarnilai ulakattu cellātu ivaṇ niṉṟu
  • {PATI 54-11} iru nila maruṅkiṉ neṭitu maṉṉiyar ō
  • {PATI 54-12} nilam tapa iṭūum ēṇi pulam paṭarntu
  • {PATI 54-13} paṭu kaṇ muracam naṭuvaṇ cilaippa
  • {PATI 54-14} tōmara valattar nāmam ceymmār
  • {PATI 54-15} ēval viyaṅkoṇṭu iḷaiyaroṭu eḻutarum
  • {PATI 54-16} ollār yāṉai kāṇiṉ
  • {PATI 54-17} nillā tāṉai iṟai kiḻavōy ē

Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 54-1} vaḷḷiyi eṉṟaliṉ kāṇku vanticiṉ ē
  • {PATI 54-2} uḷḷiyatu muṭitti vāḻka niṉ kaṇṇi
  • {PATI 54-3} vīṅku iṟi taṭiiya ami maruḷ paṇi tōḷ
  • {PATI 54-4} ēntu eḻil maḻi kaṇ vaṉintu varal iḷa muli
  • {PATI 54-5} pūm tukil alkul tēm pāy kūntal
  • {PATI 54-6} miṉ iḻi viṟaliyar niṉ maṟam pāṭa
  • {PATI 54-7} iravalar puṉkaṇ tīra nāḷ toṟum
  • {PATI 54-8} uri cāl nal kalam varivu ila vīci
  • {PATI 54-9} aṉiyi ākaṉ māṟu ē eṉiyatūum
  • {PATI 54-10} uyarnili ulakattu cellātu ivaṇ niṉṟu
  • {PATI 54-11} iru nila maruṅkiṉ neṭitu maṉṉiyar ō
  • {PATI 54-12} nilam tapa iṭūum ēṇi pulam paṭarntu
  • {PATI 54-13} paṭu kaṇ muracam naṭuvaṇ cilippa
  • {PATI 54-14} tōmara valattar nāmam ceymmār
  • {PATI 54-15} ēval viyaṅkoṇṭu iḷiyaroṭu eḻutarum
  • {PATI 54-16} ollār yāṉi kāṇiṉ
  • {PATI 54-17} nillā tāṉi iṟi kiḻavōy ē

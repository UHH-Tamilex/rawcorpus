Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 65-1} eṟi piṇam iṭaṟiya ce maṟu kuḷampiṉ
  • {PATI 65-2} pari uṭai nal mā viri uḷai cūṭṭi
  • {PATI 65-3} malaitta tevvar maṟam tapa kaṭanta
  • {PATI 65-4} kāñci cāṉṟa vayavar peruma
  • {PATI 65-5} villōr meymmaṟai cērntōr celva
  • {PATI 65-6} pūṇ aṇintu eḻiliya vaṉaintu varal iḷa mulai
  • {PATI 65-7} māṇ vari alkul malarnta nōkkiṉ
  • {PATI 65-8} vēy puraipu eḻiliya viḷaṅku iṟai paṇai tōḷ
  • {PATI 65-9} kāmar kaṭavuḷ um āḷum kaṟpiṉ
  • {PATI 65-10} cēṇ nāṟu naṟu nutal cē iḻai kaṇava
  • {PATI 65-11} pāṇar puravala paricilar veṟukkai
  • {PATI 65-12} pūṇ aṇintu viḷaṅkiya pukaḻ cāl mārpa niṉ
  • {PATI 65-13} nāḷ makiḻ irukkai iṉitu kaṇṭikum ē
  • {PATI 65-14} tīm toṭai narampiṉ pālai vallōṉ
  • {PATI 65-15} paiyuḷ uṟuppiṉ paṇṇu peyarttāṅku
  • {PATI 65-16} cēṟu cey māriyiṉ aḷikkum niṉ
  • {PATI 65-17} cāṟu paṭu tiruviṉ naṉai makiḻāṉ ē

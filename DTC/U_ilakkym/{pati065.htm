Patiṟṟup pattu

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 65-1} eṟi piṇam iṭaṟiya ce maṟu kuḷampiṉ
  • {PATI 65-2} pari uṭi nal mā viri uḷi cūṭṭi
  • {PATI 65-3} malitta tevvar maṟam tapa kaṭanta
  • {PATI 65-4} kāñci cāṉṟa vayavar peruma
  • {PATI 65-5} villōr meymmaṟi cērntōr celva
  • {PATI 65-6} pūṇ aṇintu eḻiliya vaṉintu varal iḷa muli
  • {PATI 65-7} māṇ vari alkul malarnta nōkkiṉ
  • {PATI 65-8} vēy puripu eḻiliya viḷaṅku iṟi paṇi tōḷ
  • {PATI 65-9} kāmar kaṭavuḷ um āḷum kaṟpiṉ
  • {PATI 65-10} cēṇ nāṟu naṟu nutal cē iḻi kaṇava
  • {PATI 65-11} pāṇar puravala paricilar veṟukki
  • {PATI 65-12} pūṇ aṇintu viḷaṅkiya pukaḻ cāl mārpa niṉ
  • {PATI 65-13} nāḷ makiḻ irukki iṉitu kaṇṭikum ē
  • {PATI 65-14} tīm toṭi narampiṉ pāli vallōṉ
  • {PATI 65-15} piyuḷ uṟuppiṉ paṇṇu peyarttāṅku
  • {PATI 65-16} cēṟu cey māriyiṉ aḷikkum niṉ
  • {PATI 65-17} cāṟu paṭu tiruviṉ naṉi makiḻāṉ ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, oh friend,
  2. would he have gone, if we had said[FNote_1411], confronting [him],
  3. 'Don't go. We are one who cries out quick with [eye] dew.[FNote_1412]',
  4. he, who has separated in the fetter of impermanent wealth,
  5. passing mountainous forests,
  6. where the travellers going the way bring [their] weapons in position,[FNote_1413]
  7. informed about [the coming] of men by long-legged Kaṇantuḷ birds
  8. with pretty wings, in a big mass perched by the way?

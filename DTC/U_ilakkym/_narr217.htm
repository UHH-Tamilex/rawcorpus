{{Metrical text}}

  1. icaipaṭa vāḻpavar celvam pōlak
  2. kāṇṭoṟum poliyuṅ kataḻvāy vēḻa
  3. miruṅkēḻ vayapuli verīi yayalatu
  4. karuṅkāl vēṅkai yūṟupaṭa maṟalip
  5. peruñciṉan taṇiyuṅ kuṉṟa nāṭa ((5))
  6. ṉaṉiperi tiṉiya ṉāyiṉun tuṉipaṭarn
  7. tūṭa luṟuveṉ ṟōḻi nīṭu
  8. pulampucē ṇakala nīntip
  9. pulavi yuṇartal vaṇmai yāṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from the hilly land, where the great anger of
  • the quick-trunked(?)[FNote_688] elephant bull that shines whenever seen,
  • as the prosperity of those who live to acquire fame,
  • being frightened by big lustruous strong tiger, decreases after giving fight,
  • when it happens to be in the way, to the flame-like Vēṅkai tree nearby,
  • - even if he [is] very very pleasing, thinking of disgust
  • I will quarrel [with him], friend,
  • so that he realises to the full(?)[FNote_689] [my] vexation
  • [and] crosses the distant width of prolonged loneliness.

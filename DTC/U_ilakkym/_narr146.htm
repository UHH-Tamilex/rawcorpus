{{Metrical text}}

  1. villāp pūviṉ kaṇṇi cūṭi
  2. nallē muṟuvaleṉap pallūr tiritaru
  3. neṭumāp peṇṇi
  4. maṭaṉmā ṉōyē kaṭaṉaṟi maṉṉar
  5. kuṭiniḻaṟ pōlap peruntaṇ ṇeṉṟa
  6. maraniḻaṟ ciṟitaḻin tiruntaṉi ceṉmō ((5))
  7. vaḻaṅkuka cuṭareṉa varuḷik kūṭumārva
  8. mākka ṇallē meṉṉuṅ kiḷavivallō
  9. ṉeḻuti yaṉṉa kāṇṭaku vaṉappi
  10. ṉiyaṇ māyō ḷaṇaṅkiya
  11. miya ṉeñca meṉmoḻik koḷiṉē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O you on the palmyra horse,
  • turning about in many villages, saying "I have great[FNote_463] confusion",
  • wearing a chaplet of flowers that are not sold,
  • go, you that have been[FNote_464], slightly heart-broken[FNote_465], in the shade of the tree
  • that brings great coolness as the parasol of a king knowing [his] duty,
  • - if you take my word, [you with] an infatuated heart
  • plagued by the dark one, the beautiful one,
  • of a beauty fit to be seen, as if drawn by
  • a capable [pinter], the word[FNote_466] that says: "we are good people,
  • with affection that is worthy(?) [and] considerate, saying 'may the glow wander'[FNote_467]"

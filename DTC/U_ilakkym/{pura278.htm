  • {PU 278-4} maṇṭu amarkku uṭintaṉaṉ āyiṉ uṇṭa eṉ

  • {PU 278-6} koṇṭa vāḷoṭu paṭu piṇam peyarā
  • {PU 278-7} ce kaḷam tuḻavuvōḷ citintu vēṟu ākiya
  • {PU 278-8} paṭu makaṉ kiṭakki kāṇūu
  • {PU 278-9} īṉṟa ñāṉṟiṉum peritu uvantaṉaḷ ē

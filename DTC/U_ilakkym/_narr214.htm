{{Readable Translation}}

  • Doesn't he hear it, friend,
  • [our] blemishless lover who had departed for wealth,
  • traversing mountins, many [of them], of collyrium-covered mountins,
  • uttering, for [my] heart to absorb, unviolable oaths:
  • "we shall come to adorn your sapphire-dark hir
  • with many buds [full of] bees from the bud-opening oleander",
  • with principle different [from ours] that separated for work
  • [in the thought]: "sweet [is] the difficult union for those who kept struggling
  • for all three [aims]: fame and pleasure and [alms]giving."
  • - the voice of this monsoon rin that seems to be roaring,
  • flashing as if it smiles, censuring
  • the agitated grief that loosened the shining bangles in [my] upper arms?

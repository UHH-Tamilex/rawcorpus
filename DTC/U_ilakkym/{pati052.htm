Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 52-1} koṭi nuṭaṅku nilaiya kol kaḷiṟu miṭaintu
  • {PATI 52-2} vaṭi maṇi neṭum tēr vēṟu pulam parappi
  • {PATI 52-3} arum kalam tarīiyar nīr micai nivakkum
  • {PATI 52-4} perum kali vaṅkam ticai tirintāṅku
  • {PATI 52-5} mai aṇintu eḻutaru mā irum pal tōl
  • {PATI 52-6} mey putai araṇam eṇṇātu eḵku cumantu
  • {PATI 52-7} muṉ camattu eḻutarum vaṉkaṇ āṭavar
  • {PATI 52-8} tolaiyā tumpai tev vaḻi viḷaṅka
  • {PATI 52-9} uyarnilai ulakam eytiṉar palar paṭa
  • {PATI 52-10} nal amar kaṭanta niṉ cel uṟaḻ taṭa kai
  • {PATI 52-11} irappōrkku kavital allatu ai iraiiya
  • {PATI 52-12} malarpu aṟiyā eṉa kēṭṭikum iṉi ē
  • {PATI 52-13} cuṭarum pāṇṭil tiru nāṟu viḷakkattu
  • {PATI 52-14} muḻā imiḻ tuṇaṅkaikku taḻūu puṇai āka
  • {PATI 52-15} cilaippu val ēṟṟiṉ talai kai tantu nī
  • {PATI 52-16} naḷintaṉai varutal uṭaṉṟaḷaḷ āki
  • {PATI 52-17} uyalum kōtai ūral am titti
  • {PATI 52-18} īr itaḻ maḻai kaṇ pēr iyal arivai
  • {PATI 52-19} oḷ itaḻ aviḻ akam kaṭukkum cīṟu aṭi
  • {PATI 52-20} pal cila kiṇkiṇi ciṟu paraṭu alaippa
  • {PATI 52-21} kol puṉal taḷiriṉ naṭuṅkuvaṉaḷ niṉṟu niṉ
  • {PATI 52-22} eṟiyar ōkkiya ciṟu ceṅkuvaḷai
  • {PATI 52-23} ī eṉa irappa um ollāḷ nī emakku
  • {PATI 52-24} yārai ō eṉa peyarvōḷ kaiyatu ai
  • {PATI 52-25} katumeṉa urutta nōkkamoṭu atu nī
  • {PATI 52-26} pāal vallāy āyiṉai pāal
  • {PATI 52-27} yāṅku vallunai ō vāḻka niṉ kaṇṇi
  • {PATI 52-28} akal iru vicumpil pakal iṭam tarīiyar
  • {PATI 52-29} teṟu katir tikaḻtarum uru keḻu ñāyiṟṟu
  • {PATI 52-30} urupu kiḷar vaṇṇam koṇṭa
  • {PATI 52-31} vāṉ tōy veḷ kuṭai vēntar tam eyil ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The fast going young [warriors], who suffered from the hasty motion,
  • having tied [their] belts tight to [their] bodies [and] rested utterly exhusted[FNote_91],
  • may they come slowly, slowly at the pace they wish for.
  • For our going, so that [the horses] taste the untasted sharp thorn,
  • go on with the chariot, charioteer. Look there:
  • the very befitting state,
  • when the desirable, lovely forest cock,
  • with many pretty fine spots in its neck [and] a voice cutting
  • as milk dropped into fragrant ghee which is melted,
  • digs, while it is flooded, the undried moist sand
  • in the wide long woodland, from which the rin water has gone,
  • [and] hooks [for her] to seize the dily prey
  • [and] looks for its female.

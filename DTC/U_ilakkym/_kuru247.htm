{{Readable Translation}}

  1. As one possessing much grace the faultless intimacy
  2. here grows close[FNote_1000], which is one of possessing the chest
  3. of the man from the land, where the elephant bull rumbles in [his] rare sleep,
  4. so that the blossoms fall from soft twigs without the Vēṅkai being destroyed.[FNote_1001]
  5. 'That the work to be done by a able man is according to duty,
  6. this [is] also a support for people who have relatives'[FNote_1002]
  7. - thus you may know, friend.

Altern

4b: thus I have understood, friend.

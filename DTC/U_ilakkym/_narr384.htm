{{Readable Translation}}

  • We surely saw [her] while walking over the gold-like
  • new blossoms that had spread[FNote_1054] from the Vēṅkai tree on a good day
  • that blossomed in the middle of the road to distant lands of glorious ground,
  • where the red-legged cock of the beatiful(?) dove
  • obtains the rice shed, the old grains, seized from the house
  • of the king, for [its] female with the gait of pregnancy cravings to eat,
  • that had a young one brought forth in the nest of thorny twigs
  • from the Kaḷḷi tree with forked thorns grown high in the saline soil?
  • See now, live, my heart: the inexperienced one who is called "remedy",
  • in a time she bore difficult suffering, letting go of shame.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. iruñcem muṭiya koṭuṅkavuṭ kayavāy
  2. māri yāṉaiyiṉ maruṅku ṟīṇṭip
  3. poriyarai ñemirnta puḻaṟkāyk koṉṟai
  4. nīṭiya caṭaiyō ṭāṭā mēṉik
  5. kuṉṟuṟai tavaciyar pōlap palavuṭa ((5))
  6. ṉeṉṟūḻ nīḷiṭaip poṟpat tōṉṟu
  7. maruñcura meḷiyamaṉ ṉiṉakkē paruntupaṭap
  8. pāṇṭiloṭu poruta palpiṇart taṭakkai
  9. yēntukōṭ ṭiyāṉai yicaiveṅ kiḷḷi
  10. vampaṇi yuyarkoṭi yampar cūḻnta ((10))
  11. varicilan teḷḷaṟa laṉṉavivaḷ
  12. viriyoli kūntal viṭṭamai kalaṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • To you the difficult deserts [may be] easy,
  • where the Koṉṟai trees with hollow unripe fruits that were crushed on the parched trunk, rubbing(?) the stomach of the cloud[like?] elephant
  • with deep mouth [and] bent cheek on [its] big red head,
  • appear beautifully on the long way in the sun,
  • many together, as ascetics dwelling in the hills,
  • bodys motionless, with long-grown matted locks.
  • I won't be quiet, having let go off the broadly sprouting tresses
  • of her who [is] like the clear water of the Aricil river
  • that surrounded Ampar, with high flags as a fresh decoration,
  • [belonging to] Kiḷḷi, desirous of fame, with high-tusked elephants
  • with broad trunks with many roughnesses that had fought with chariots(?!)[FNote_447],
  • so that kites were coming (to take the prey).

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O [you] of choice jewels, leave off despiring,
  • aching [and] aching, sobbing for [your] adorning jewels to become lose,
  • saying "may my life go: he has gone indeed
  • to the empty hills, where the choice petals of the Kumiḻ tree
  • by the road adorn(?) the stony rocks,
  • as if it were a way(?) possessing swinging bright earrings."
  • If [you] think, is not the reason he has gone, friend,
  • needing gin for those who are near [to him]
  • and coming so that your shoulder that has shrunk obtins adornment?

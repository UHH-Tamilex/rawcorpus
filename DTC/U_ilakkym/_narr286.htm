{{Metrical text}}

  1. ūca loṇkuḻai yuṭaiviyat taṉṉa
  2. vattak kumiḻi ṉāyita ḻalari
  3. kallaṟai varikkum pulleṉ kuṉṟañ
  4. ceṉṟōr maṉṟa celīiyareṉ ṉuyireṉap
  5. puṇaiyiḻai nekiḻa vimmi nontunon ((5))
  6. tiṉaita lāṉṟici ṉāyiḻai niṉaiyi
  7. ṉaṭṭō rākkam vēṇṭiyu moṭṭiya
  8. niṉṟō ḷaṇipeṟa varaṟkku
  9. maṉṟō tōḻi yavarceṉṟa tiṟamē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O [you] of choice jewels, leave off despairing,
  • aching [and] aching, sobbing for [your] adorning jewels to become lose,
  • saying "may my life go: he has gone indeed
  • to the empty hills, where the choice petals of the Kumiḻ tree
  • by the road adorn(?) the stony rocks,
  • as if it were a way(?) possessing swinging bright earrings."
  • If [you] think, is not the reason he has gone, friend,
  • needing gain for those who are near [to him]
  • and coming so that your shoulder that has shrunk obtains adornment?

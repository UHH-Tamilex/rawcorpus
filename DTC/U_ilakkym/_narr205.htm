{{Readable Translation}}

  • O you who do not say that the forest is difficult to approach,
  • where the good bull(?) of the Āḷi[FNote_650] drags along the strong elephant bull
  • with raised white tusks with sharp tips that had destroyed
  • the flower-dotted(?)[FNote_651] tiger with murderous nail on the rise for a hunt
  • on the side of the big mountain where the waterfall roars,
  • - if you were to depart to [your] male work
  • for her with water-lily collyrium eyes to stay behind,
  • would it not be gone with the very day,
  • her dark beauty that is like the choice colour,
  • that has been offered quick rain, abounding in water,[FNote_652]
  • of the long dark pretty sprouts of the mimosa with curve thorns?

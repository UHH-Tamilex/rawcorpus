{{Metrical text}}

  1. nīraṟa vaṟanta nirampā nīḷiṭit
  2. tukilvirit taṉṉa vēyil viruppi
  3. ṉañcuvarap parikkum veñcura miṟantōr
  4. tāmvarat teḷitta paruvaṅ kāṇvara
  5. vituvō veṉṟiciṉ maṭanti matiyiṉṟu ((5))
  6. maṟantu kaṭaṉmukanta kamañcūṉ māmaḻi
  7. poṟuttal cellā tiṟutta vaṇpeyal
  8. kāreṉa vayarnta vuḷḷamoṭu tērvila
  9. piṭavuṅ koṉṟiyuṅ kōṭalu
  10. maṭava vākaliṉ malarnta peritē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "[Is] this", you say, girl,
  • "while it comes to be seen, the season of which he made clear he would come,
  • the one who traversed the hot desert, where one hastens, for fear comes up,
  • through the heat of the sunlight [whitehot] as cloth spread out [to dry]
  • on the endless long way that has fallen dry as the water subsides?"
  • - Becuse they [are] foolish they have flowered greatly,
  • the Piṭavu and the Koṉṟi and the white Malabar lily
  • that have no discrimination, with [their] minds immersed in [the thought] "riny season", with liberal rining, when the fully pregnant dark clouds
  • that have drawn [water] from the sea, having forgotten ...(?)[FNote_337]
  • tarry, [full to] the breaking point[FNote_338].

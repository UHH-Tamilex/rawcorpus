  • {KALI 72-1} iṇipaṭa nivanta nīla mel cēkki uḷ
  • {KALI 72-2} tuṇi puṇar aṉṉattiṉ tūvi mel aṇi acii
  • {KALI 72-3} cēṭu iyal vaḷḷattu peyta pāl cila kāṭṭi
  • {KALI 72-4} ūṭum mel ciṟu kiḷi uṇarppavaḷ mukam pōla
  • {KALI 72-5} putu nīra putal oṟṟa puṇar tiri pitir malka
  • {KALI 72-6} mati nōkki alar vītta āmpal vāl malar naṇṇi
  • {KALI 72-7} kaṭi kaya tāmari kamaḻ muki kari māviṉ
  • {KALI 72-8} vaṭi tīṇṭa vāy viṭūum vayal aṇi nal ūra
  • {KALI 72-9} kaṇṇi nī kaṭi koṇṭār kaṉitoṟum yām aḻa
  • {KALI 72-10} paṇṇiṉāl kaḷippikkum pāṇaṉ kāṭṭu eṉṟāṉ ō
  • {KALI 72-11} pēṇāṉ eṉṟu uṭaṉṟavar ukir ceyta vaṭuviṉāṉ
  • {KALI 72-12} mēl nāḷ niṉ tōḷ cērntār naki cērnta itaḻiṉi
  • {KALI 72-13} nāṭi niṉ tūtu āṭi tuṟi cellāḷ ūravar
  • {KALI 72-14} āṭi koṇṭu olikkum niṉ pulitti kāṭṭu eṉṟāḷ ō
  • {KALI 72-15} kūṭiyār puṉal āṭa puṇi āya mārpiṉil
  • {KALI 72-16} ūṭiyār eṟitara oḷi viṭṭa arakkiṉi
  • {KALI 72-17} veṟitu niṉ pukaḻkaḷi vēṇṭār il eṭuttu ēttum
  • {KALI 72-18} aṟivu uṭi antaṇaṉ avaḷi kāṭṭu eṉṟāṉ ō
  • {KALI 72-19} kaḷi paṭṭār kamaḻ kōti kayampaṭṭa uruviṉ mēl
  • {KALI 72-20} kuṟi peṟṟār kural kūntal kōṭu uḷarnta tukaḷiṉi
  • {KALI 72-21} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 72-22} ceṟivuṟṟēm emmi nī ceṟiya aṟivuṟṟu
  • {KALI 72-23} aḻintu uku neñcattēm allal uḻappa
  • {KALI 72-24} kaḻintavi uḷḷātu kaṇṭa iṭattu ē
  • {KALI 72-25} aḻintu niṉ pēṇi koḷaliṉ iḻintatu ō
  • {KALI 72-26} i nōy uḻattal emakku

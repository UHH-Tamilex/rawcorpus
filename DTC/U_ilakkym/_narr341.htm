{{Readable Translation}}

  • The girl well possesses a companion,
  • who, if she has enough of [her] little sleep on the silver-lined rock,
  • drinks the unspoiled milk[FNote_975], with a playful sweet smile,
  • that she cuses to flow from the round tongue of red-spotted vermilion,
  • rises, without slapping, a green twig, deals [him] a few [mock blows](?)[FNote_976]
  • and repeats short phrases with the man from the hills.[FNote_977]
  • We,
  • while the north wind from other fields dashes along
  • with the cold season, half-days being confused with water wailing(?),
  • becuse cool rin has flown on the difficult front of hot enmity
  • we are without companion, we [are] solitary, we [are] in the encampment.

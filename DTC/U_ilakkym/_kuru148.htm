{{Readable Translation}}

  1. If, [as] you sid, [this] is not the riny season,
  2. even at times when along with[FNote_596] the wild lemon
  3. the Laburnum tree is agitated, bringing forth buds, like coins
  4. on the golden anklets with frogs' voices[FNote_597]
  5. which glittered on the feet of prosperous children
  6. - I ask: [is] this just a dream?

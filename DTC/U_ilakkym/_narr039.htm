{{Readable Translation}}

  • If [I] speak you don`t receive [my] words. Bowing
  • your brillant face you are ashamed. Is it easy
  • to endure when desire exceeds the limit, harsh(?)[FNote_164]?
  • Bordered jewels become red like the tusks of the elephant, that smells of flesh since he played with the tiger
  • [and] gored, [for him] to shiver, [his] dark back with curved, lustrous [stripes][FNote_165],
  • your eyes, they are not angry.
  • Like the famous(?) meeting
  • of Ceḻiyaṉ, in deathly battle, who took [his] drum [and] left the protected
  • fortress, even when the defenders(?) didn`t reach the fortress,
  • your shoulders too, [drawn][FNote_166] with sugarcane are indeed full of plague.

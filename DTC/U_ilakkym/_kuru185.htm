{{Readable Translation}}

  1. 'Pallor spread on the forehead, beauty spots faded,
  2. long soft bamboo shoulders emaciated, armlets became loose
  3. - that she is like this happens because of you!',
  4. if someone speaks [like this], what [might] happen, friend?
  5. The state of destructive affliction in my good dark body
  6. [is] because of the man from a land where the stony height
  7. is covered by bright red Malabar lilies, lost in the rain[FNote_744]
  8. [and] closing, shrunk like the hood of the many-striped snake.

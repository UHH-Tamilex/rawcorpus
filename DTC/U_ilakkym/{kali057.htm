Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 57-1} vēy eṉa tiraṇṭa tōḷ veṟi kamaḻ vaṇar aimpāl
  • {KALI 57-2} mā veṉṟa maṭa nōkkiṉ mayil iyal taḷarpu olki
  • {KALI 57-3} āy cilampu ari ārppa avir oḷi iḻai imaippa
  • {KALI 57-4} koṭi eṉa miṉ eṉa aṇaṅku eṉa yātu oṉṟu um
  • {KALI 57-5} terikallā iṭaiyiṉ kaṇ kaṇ kavarpu oruṅku ōṭa
  • {KALI 57-6} vaḷamai cāl uyar ciṟappiṉ nuntai tol viyal nakar
  • {KALI 57-7} iḷamaiyāṉ eṟi pantoṭu ikattantāy kēḷ iṉi
  • {KALI 57-8} pūm taṇ tār pular cāntiṉ teṉṉavaṉ uyar kūṭal
  • {KALI 57-9} tēm pāya aviḻ nīlattu alar veṉṟa amar uṇ kaṇ
  • {KALI 57-10} ēntu kōṭṭu eḻil yāṉai oṉṉātārkku avaṉ vēliṉ
  • {KALI 57-11} cēntu nī iṉaiyai āl ottatu ō ciṉ moḻi
  • {KALI 57-12} poḻi peyal vaṇmaiyāṉ acōkam taṇ kāviṉ uḷ
  • {KALI 57-13} kaḻi kaviṉ iḷa māviṉ taḷir aṉṉāy ataṉ talai
  • {KALI 57-14} paṇai amai pāy māṉ tēr avaṉ ceṟṟār niṟam pāynta
  • {KALI 57-15} kaṇaiyiṉum nōy ceytal kaṭappu aṉṟu ō kaṉam kuḻāy
  • {KALI 57-16} vakai amai taṇ tārāṉ kōṭu uyar poruppiṉ mēl
  • {KALI 57-17} takai iṇar iḷa vēṅkai malar aṉṉa cuṇaṅkiṉāy
  • {KALI 57-18} mata vali miku kaṭāattu avaṉ yāṉai maruppiṉum
  • {KALI 57-19} katava āl takkatu ō kāḻ koṇṭa iḷa mulai
  • {KALI 57-20} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 57-21} iṉaiyaṉa kūṟa iraiñcupu nilam nōkki
  • {KALI 57-22} niṉaiyupu neṭitu oṉṟu niṉaippāḷ pōl maṟṟu āṅku ē
  • {KALI 57-23} tuṇai amai tōḻiyarkku amartta kaṇṇaḷ
  • {KALI 57-24} maṉai āṅku peyarntāḷ eṉ aṟivu akappaṭuttu ē

  1. Black cloud, fully pregnant, come with the wind,
  2. mingled with the murmuring voice of heavy[FNote_632] thunder[FNote_633],
  3. roaring so that snakes suffer(?)[FNote_634] on the long mountain side:

  5. shaker even of the wide-famed(?) Himalāya?

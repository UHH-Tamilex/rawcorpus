  • {KALI 11-1} aritu āya aṟaṉ eyti aruḷiyōrkku aḷittal um
  • {KALI 11-2} peritu āya paki veṉṟu pēṇāri teṟutal um
  • {KALI 11-3} purivu amar kātaliṉ puṇarcci um tarum eṉa
  • {KALI 11-4} pirivu eṇṇi poruḷ vayiṉ ceṉṟa nam kātalar
  • {KALI 11-5} varuvar kol vayaṅku iḻāay valippal yāṉ kēeḷ iṉi
  • {KALI 11-6} aṭi tāṅkum aḷavu iṉṟi aḻal aṉṉa vemmiyāl
  • {KALI 11-7} kaṭiya ē kaṉam kuḻāay kāṭu eṉṟār a kāṭṭu uḷ
  • {KALI 11-8} tuṭi aṭi kayam tali kalakkiya ciṉ nīri
  • {KALI 11-9} piṭi ūṭṭi piṉ uṇṇum kaḷiṟu eṉa um urittaṉar ē
  • {KALI 11-10} iṉpattiṉ ikantu orīi ili tīnta ulaviyāl
  • {KALI 11-11} tuṉpuṟūum takiya ē kāṭu eṉṟār a kāṭṭu uḷ
  • {KALI 11-12} aṉpu koḷ maṭa peṭi aciiya varuttatti
  • {KALI 11-13} mel ciṟakarāl āṟṟum puṟavu eṉa um urittaṉar ē
  • {KALI 11-14} kal mici vēy vāṭa kaṉi katir teṟutalāṉ
  • {KALI 11-15} tuṉṉarūum takiya ē kāṭu eṉṟār a kāṭṭu uḷ
  • {KALI 11-16} iṉ niḻal iṉmiyāṉ varuntiya maṭa piṇikku
  • {KALI 11-17} taṉ niḻali koṭuttu aḷikkum kali eṉa um urittaṉar ē
  • {KALI 11-18} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 11-19} iṉi nalam uṭiya kāṉam ceṉṟōr
  • {KALI 11-20} puṉi nalam vāṭṭunar allar maṉi vayiṉ
  • {KALI 11-21} palli um pāṅku ottu icittaṉa
  • {KALI 11-22} nal eḻil uṇ kaṇ um āṭumāl iṭaṉ ē

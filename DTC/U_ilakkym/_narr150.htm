{{Metrical text}}

  1. nakainaṉ kuṭaiyaṉ pāṇanum perumakaṉ
  2. miḷaivali citaiyak kaḷiṟupala parappi
  3. yaraṇpala kaṭantamuraṇko ṭāṉai
  4. vaḻuti vāḻiya palaveṉat toḻutīṇṭu
  5. maṉṉeyi luṭaiyōr pōla vaḵtiyā ((5))
  6. meṉṉalum pariyalō vilameṉat taṇṭaik
  7. kalimāk kaṭaiiya vanteñ cērit
  8. tāruṅ kaṇṇiyuṅ kāṭṭi yorumaiya
  9. neñcaṅ koṇṭamai viṭumō vañcak
  10. kaṇṇuṭaic ciṟukōl paṟṟik ((10))
  11. katamperi tuṭaiyaḷyā yaḻuṅkalō vilaḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • He [is] smiling well, bard, your great man.
  • Does he let go of having taken [our] heart
  • that was one [with him] since he showed a garland and a chaplet
  • in our street, having come while impelling the proud horses
  • with shields(?)[FNote_480], saying "as for the distress even of our talking like that,
  • we have none", as those who possess a great fortress(?)
  • here worship [with the words] "may Vaḻuti live many [days],
  • with [his] enemy-taking army that has surmounted many a stronghold,
  • having spread a number of(?)[FNote_481] elephant bulls to waste [their] strength on the
  • enclosure.
  • Seizing the small jointed rod of the bamboo(?)
  • she has great anger: mother, as for being [just] noisy - not she.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, o friend, if [you] say:
  2. somehow they are mistaken,
  3. [my] broad, tender shoulders that have become thin
  4. for him who has gone to the difficult forests
  5. where the stone-heap piled with leaves for the dead traveller(?)
  6. gives some shade to the large good elephant
  7. in the hot desert - they are rather not mistaken.

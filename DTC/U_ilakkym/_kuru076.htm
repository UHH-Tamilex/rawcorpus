{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Do they say he with the chest of a stony mountain will go[FNote_309]
  2. to a good land with high mountains [and] fences of Malabar lilies,
  3. for me to have trembling sorrow in the cold season with heavy dew
  4. when the north wind, the coming of coolness, lifts
  5. the many swaying leaves, like[FNote_310] the ears of big elephant bulls,
  6. of the Cēmpu on the mountain-side [and] sticks [to them]?

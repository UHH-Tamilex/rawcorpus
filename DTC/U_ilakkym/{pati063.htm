Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 63-1} pārppārkku allatu paṇipu aṟiyali ē
  • {PATI 63-2} paṇiyā uḷḷamoṭu aṇivara keḻīi
  • {PATI 63-3} naṭṭōrkku allatu kaṇ añcali ē
  • {PATI 63-4} vaṇaṅku cili poruta niṉ maṇam kamaḻ akalam
  • {PATI 63-5} makaḷirkku allatu malarppu aṟiyali ē
  • {PATI 63-6} nilam tiṟam peyarum kāli āyiṉum
  • {PATI 63-7} kiḷanta col nī poyppu aṟiyali ē
  • {PATI 63-8} ciṟu ili uḻiñi teriyal cūṭi
  • {PATI 63-9} koṇṭi mikipaṭa taṇ tamiḻ ceṟittu
  • {PATI 63-10} kuṉṟu nili taḷarkkum urumiṉ cīṟi
  • {PATI 63-11} oru muṟṟu iruvar ōṭṭiya oḷ vāḷ
  • {PATI 63-12} ceru miku tāṉi vel pōrōy ē
  • {PATI 63-13} āṭu peṟṟu aḻinta maḷḷar māṟi
  • {PATI 63-14} nī kaṇṭaṉiyēm eṉṟaṉar nī um
  • {PATI 63-15} num nukam koṇṭu iṉum veṉṟōy ataṉāl
  • {PATI 63-16} celva kō ē cēralar maruka
  • {PATI 63-17} kāl tiri eṭutta muḻaṅku kural vēli
  • {PATI 63-18} naṉam tali ulakam ceyta naṉṟu uṇṭu eṉiṉ
  • {PATI 63-19} aṭi aṭuppu aṟiyā aruvi āmpal
  • {PATI 63-20} āyira veḷḷa ūḻi
  • {PATI 63-21} vāḻiyāta vāḻiya pala ē

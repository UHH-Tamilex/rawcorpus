{{Metrical text}}

  1. tōlāk kātalar tuṟantuna maruḷā
  2. ralarva taṉṟuko lituveṉṟu naṉṟum
  3. pularā neñcamoṭu putuva kūṟi
  4. yiruvē nīntum paruvaral veḷḷa
  5. maṟintaṉaḷ pōlu maṉṉai ciṟanta ((5))
  6. cīrkeḻu viyaṉakar varuvaṉaṇ muyaṅki
  7. nīralaik kalaiiya vīritaḻt toṭaiya
  8. loṇṇutaṟ petumpai naṉṉalam peṟīi
  9. miṉṉē rōti yivaḷoṭu nāḷaip
  10. paṉmalar kañaliya veṟikamaḻ vēlit ((10))
  11. teṇṇīr maṇiccuṉai yāṭi
  12. ṉeṉṉō makaḷirtam paṇpeṉ ṟōḷē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "[Our] undefeated lover, abandoning [us], [is] not considerate.
  • Is there no gossip?" saying this, talking
  • well in a new way with a heart not [remaining] dry,
  • mother, as if she knew of the flood of suffering
  • we two are swimming, coming[FNote_972] [to me]
  • in the wide mansion with superior excellence, embraced [me]
  • [and] said:
  • "if [you] play in the sapphire[-like] mountain spring with clear water
  • [and] hedges fragrant with toddy, dense with many blossoms,
  • tomorrow with her of lightning-like hair,
  • obtaining the good wellness of a girl with bright forehead
  • [and] a wreath of wet petals, dispersing ripples of water,
  • what of the own quality of women?"

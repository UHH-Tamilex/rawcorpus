  1. ōṅkumaṇa luṭutta neṭumāp peṇṇi
  2. vīṅkumaṭaṟ kuṭampip pital veṇkuruku
  3. naḷḷeṉ yāmat tuyavutō ṟuruki
  4. yaḷḷa ḷaṉṉave ṉuḷḷamo ṭuḷḷuṭin
  5. tuḷḷeṉ vāḻi tōḻi vaḷinīrk ((5))
  6. kaṭuñcuṟā veṟinta koṭuntimiṟ paratavar
  7. vāṅkuvicit tūṇṭi lūṅkūṅ kāki
  8. vaḷiporak kaṟi tāya navicuṭar
  9. nīṉiṟa vicumpiṉ mīṉoṭu puriyap
  10. pipaya vimikkun tuṟivaṉ ((10))
  11. meytōy muyakkaṅ kāṇā vūṅkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • I won't remember[FNote_633], live, friend,
  • broken inside, with my mind like mire,
  • melting with every [sound of] suffering at deepest midnight
  • from the distressed white heron[FNote_634] in [its] nest in the swelling palmyra stems,
  • on the long big palmyra palm encircled by high sand,
  • - before I see the embrace touch [my] body
  • of the man from the ghat, where the big lights,
  • on which blemishes are spread[FNote_635] when the wind dashes at them,
  • being here [and] there on the fish-hooks speedily pulled
  • by the fishermen in [their] bent boats who had thrown up the fierce sharks,
  • are gently twinkling as[FNote_636] stars in the water-coloured sky.

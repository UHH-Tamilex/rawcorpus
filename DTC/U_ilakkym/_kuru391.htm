{{Readable Translation}}

  1. On the woodlands left by clouds which was sultry for the deer(?),
  2. while the bull droops without ploughing, from loathing(?)[FNote_1559],
  3. sweets have descended, mixed with thunder,
  4. for the snake hood to shrink in heavily thundering thunder.
  5. [These] very foolish [birds] are cooing, friend
  6. while the wide place becomes lonely in the rushing water,
  7. the peacocks with wide open [tails'] eyes, perched on flowering branches,
  8. in sorrowful evening, so that action subsides
  9. in those who are separated, when the fallen big rain embraces [them].

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if [our] dear life is passing,
  2. since[FNote_384] [our] virginity(?)[FNote_385] is lost [and] integrity wasting away fast,
  3. {--!}don't speak:
  4. isn't he mother and father [to us], friend?
  5. What [is] the [good] of being vexed[FNote_386], [if his] love is not with us?

Altern

4b: What [is] the [good] of being vexed? With us is no love [for him anymore].

Altern

4c: What [is] the [good] of being vexed? We are boundless in [our] love [for him].

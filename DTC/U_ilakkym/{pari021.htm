Paripāṭal 21

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 21-1} ūrntatu ai eri purai ōṭai iṭai imaikkum ceṉṉi
  • {PARI 21-2} poru camam kaṭanta pukaḻ cāl vēḻam
  • {PARI 21-3} toṭṭatu ai taippu amai carumattiṉ tāḷ iyai tāmarai
  • {PARI 21-4} tuppu amai tuvar nīr tuṟai maṟai aḻuttiya
  • {PARI 21-5} verina tōloṭu muḻu mayir miṭainta
  • {PARI 21-6} vari mali ara uri vaḷpu kaṇṭaṉṉa
  • {PARI 21-7} puri mel pīli pōḻ puṉai aṭaiyal
  • {PARI 21-8} kaiyatu ai koḷḷā tevvar koḷ mā mutal taṭintu
  • {PARI 21-9} puḷḷoṭu peyariya poruppu puṭai tiṟanta vēl
  • {PARI 21-10} pūṇṭatu ai curuḷ uṭai vaḷḷi iṭai iṭupu iḻaitta
  • {PARI 21-11} uruḷ iṇar kaṭampiṉ oṉṟupaṭu kamaḻ tār
  • {PARI 21-12} amarntatu ai puraiyōr nāvil pukaḻ nalam muṟṟi
  • {PARI 21-13} nirai ēḻ aṭukkiya nīḷ ilai pālai
  • {PARI 21-14} arai varai mēkalai aṇi nīr cūḻi
  • {PARI 21-15} tarai vicumpu ukanta taṇ paraṅkuṉṟam
  • {PARI 21-16} kuṉṟattu aṭi uṟai iyaika eṉa paravutum
  • {PARI 21-17} veṉṟi koṭi aṇi celva niṉ toḻutu
  • {PARI 21-18} cuṭu poṉ ñekiḻattu muttu ari ceṉṟu ārppa
  • {PARI 21-19} tuṭiyiṉ aṭi peyarttu tōḷ acaittu tūkki
  • {PARI 21-20} aṭu naṟā makiḻ taṭpa āṭuvāḷ takaimaiyiṉ
  • {PARI 21-21} nuṉai ilaṅku eḵku eṉa civanta nōkkamoṭu
  • {PARI 21-22} tuṇai aṇai kēḷvaṉai tuṉippavaḷ nilai um
  • {PARI 21-23} niḻal kāṇ maṇṭilam nōkki
  • {PARI 21-24} aḻal puṉai avir iḻai tiruttuvāḷ kuṟippu um
  • {PARI 21-25} potirtta mulai iṭai pūci cantaṉam
  • {PARI 21-26} utirttu piṉ uṟa ūṭṭuvāḷ viruppu um
  • {PARI 21-27} pal ūḻ ivai ivai niṉaippiṉ vallōṉ
  • {PARI 21-28} ōvattu eḻutu eḻil pōlum mā
  • {PARI 21-29} tiṭṭōy niṉ kuṉṟiṉ micai
  • {PARI 21-30} micai paṭu cāntāṟṟi pōla eḻili
  • {PARI 21-31} icai paṭu pakkam iru pāl um kōli
  • {PARI 21-32} viṭu poṟi maññai peyarpu uṭaṉ āṭa
  • {PARI 21-33} viral ceṟi tūmpiṉ viṭu tuḷaikku ēṟpa
  • {PARI 21-34} mural kural tumpi aviḻ malar ūta
  • {PARI 21-35} yāṇar vaṇṭu iṉam yāḻ icai piṟakka
  • {PARI 21-36} pāṇi muḻavu icai aruvi nīr tatumpa
  • {PARI 21-37} oruṅku parantavai ellām olikkum
  • {PARI 21-38} iraṅku muraciṉāṉ kuṉṟu
  • {PARI 21-39} tāḻ nīr imiḻ cuṉai nāppaṇ kuḷittu avaṇ
  • {PARI 21-40} mī nīr nivanta viṟal iḻai kēḷvaṉai
  • {PARI 21-41} vēy nīr aḻuntu taṉ kaiyiṉ viṭuka eṉa
  • {PARI 21-42} pū nīr pey vaṭṭam eṟiya puṇai peṟātu
  • {PARI 21-43} aru nilai nīriṉ avaḷ tuyar kaṇṭu
  • {PARI 21-44} koḻunaṉ makiḻ tūṅki koy pūm puṉal vīḻntu
  • {PARI 21-45} taḻuvum takai vakaittu taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 21-46} vaṇṭu ār piṟaṅkal maintar nīviya
  • {PARI 21-47} taṇ kamaḻ cāntam taiiya vaḷi um
  • {PARI 21-48} kayal purai kaṇṇiyar kamaḻ tukaḷ utirtta
  • {PARI 21-49} puyal purai katuppu akam uḷariya vaḷi um
  • {PARI 21-50} uruḷ iṇar kaṭampiṉ neṭuvēṭku eṭutta
  • {PARI 21-51} muruku kamaḻ pukai nuḻainta vaḷi um
  • {PARI 21-52} acumpum aruvi aru viṭar paranta
  • {PARI 21-53} pacum pūṇ cēey niṉ kuṉṟam naṉku uṭaittu
  • {PARI 21-54} kaṇ oḷir tikaḻ aṭar iṭu cuṭar paṭar koṭi miṉṉu pōl
  • {PARI 21-55} oḷ nakai takai vakai neṟi peṟa iṭai iṭai iḻaittu yātta
  • {PARI 21-56} ceṇṇikai kōtai katuppōṭu iyala
  • {PARI 21-57} maṇi maruḷ tēṉ makiḻ taṭpa olki
  • {PARI 21-58} piṇi nekiḻa paim tukil nōkkam civappu ūra
  • {PARI 21-59} pūm koṭi pōla nuṭaṅkuvāḷ āṅku taṉ
  • {PARI 21-60} cīr taku kēḷvaṉ uruṭṭum tuṭi cīrāṉ
  • {PARI 21-61} kōṭu aṇinta muttu āram olka ocipavaḷ ēr
  • {PARI 21-62} āṭai acaiya aṇi acaiya tāṉ acaiyum
  • {PARI 21-63} vāṭai uḷar kompar pōṉm
  • {PARI 21-64} vāḷi puraḷpavai pōlum tuṭi cīrkku
  • {PARI 21-65} tōḷ ūḻ peyarppavaḷ kaṇ
  • {PARI 21-66} māṟu amar aṭṭavai maṟa vēl peyarppavai
  • {PARI 21-67} āṟu iru tōḷavai aṟu mukam virittavai
  • {PARI 21-68} naṉṟu amar āyamōṭu oruṅku niṉ aṭiyuṟai
  • {PARI 21-69} iṉṟu pōl iyaika eṉa paravutum
  • {PARI 21-70} oṉṟār tēytta celva niṉ toḻutu ē

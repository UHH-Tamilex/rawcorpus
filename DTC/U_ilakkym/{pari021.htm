Paripāṭal 21

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 21-1} ūrntatu ai eri puri ōṭi iṭi imikkum ceṉṉi
  • {PARI 21-2} poru camam kaṭanta pukaḻ cāl vēḻam
  • {PARI 21-3} toṭṭatu ai tippu ami carumattiṉ tāḷ iyi tāmari
  • {PARI 21-4} tuppu ami tuvar nīr tuṟi maṟi aḻuttiya
  • {PARI 21-5} verina tōloṭu muḻu mayir miṭinta
  • {PARI 21-6} vari mali ara uri vaḷpu kaṇṭaṉṉa
  • {PARI 21-7} puri mel pīli pōḻ puṉi aṭiyal
  • {PARI 21-8} kiyatu ai koḷḷā tevvar koḷ mā mutal taṭintu
  • {PARI 21-9} puḷḷoṭu peyariya poruppu puṭi tiṟanta vēl
  • {PARI 21-10} pūṇṭatu ai curuḷ uṭi vaḷḷi iṭi iṭupu iḻitta
  • {PARI 21-11} uruḷ iṇar kaṭampiṉ oṉṟupaṭu kamaḻ tār
  • {PARI 21-12} amarntatu ai puriyōr nāvil pukaḻ nalam muṟṟi
  • {PARI 21-13} niri ēḻ aṭukkiya nīḷ ili pāli
  • {PARI 21-14} ari vari mēkali aṇi nīr cūḻi
  • {PARI 21-15} tari vicumpu ukanta taṇ paraṅkuṉṟam
  • {PARI 21-16} kuṉṟattu aṭi uṟi iyika eṉa paravutum
  • {PARI 21-17} veṉṟi koṭi aṇi celva niṉ toḻutu
  • {PARI 21-18} cuṭu poṉ ñekiḻattu muttu ari ceṉṟu ārppa
  • {PARI 21-19} tuṭiyiṉ aṭi peyarttu tōḷ acittu tūkki
  • {PARI 21-20} aṭu naṟā makiḻ taṭpa āṭuvāḷ takimiyiṉ
  • {PARI 21-21} nuṉi ilaṅku eḵku eṉa civanta nōkkamoṭu
  • {PARI 21-22} tuṇi aṇi kēḷvaṉi tuṉippavaḷ nili um
  • {PARI 21-23} niḻal kāṇ maṇṭilam nōkki
  • {PARI 21-24} aḻal puṉi avir iḻi tiruttuvāḷ kuṟippu um
  • {PARI 21-25} potirtta muli iṭi pūci cantaṉam
  • {PARI 21-26} utirttu piṉ uṟa ūṭṭuvāḷ viruppu um
  • {PARI 21-27} pal ūḻ ivi ivi niṉippiṉ vallōṉ
  • {PARI 21-28} ōvattu eḻutu eḻil pōlum mā
  • {PARI 21-29} tiṭṭōy niṉ kuṉṟiṉ mici
  • {PARI 21-30} mici paṭu cāntāṟṟi pōla eḻili
  • {PARI 21-31} ici paṭu pakkam iru pāl um kōli
  • {PARI 21-32} viṭu poṟi maññi peyarpu uṭaṉ āṭa
  • {PARI 21-33} viral ceṟi tūmpiṉ viṭu tuḷikku ēṟpa
  • {PARI 21-34} mural kural tumpi aviḻ malar ūta
  • {PARI 21-35} yāṇar vaṇṭu iṉam yāḻ ici piṟakka
  • {PARI 21-36} pāṇi muḻavu ici aruvi nīr tatumpa
  • {PARI 21-37} oruṅku parantavi ellām olikkum
  • {PARI 21-38} iraṅku muraciṉāṉ kuṉṟu
  • {PARI 21-39} tāḻ nīr imiḻ cuṉi nāppaṇ kuḷittu avaṇ
  • {PARI 21-40} mī nīr nivanta viṟal iḻi kēḷvaṉi
  • {PARI 21-41} vēy nīr aḻuntu taṉ kiyiṉ viṭuka eṉa
  • {PARI 21-42} pū nīr pey vaṭṭam eṟiya puṇi peṟātu
  • {PARI 21-43} aru nili nīriṉ avaḷ tuyar kaṇṭu
  • {PARI 21-44} koḻunaṉ makiḻ tūṅki koy pūm puṉal vīḻntu
  • {PARI 21-45} taḻuvum taki vakittu taṇ paraṅkuṉṟu
  • {PARI 21-46} vaṇṭu ār piṟaṅkal mintar nīviya
  • {PARI 21-47} taṇ kamaḻ cāntam tiiya vaḷi um
  • {PARI 21-48} kayal puri kaṇṇiyar kamaḻ tukaḷ utirtta
  • {PARI 21-49} puyal puri katuppu akam uḷariya vaḷi um
  • {PARI 21-50} uruḷ iṇar kaṭampiṉ neṭuvēṭku eṭutta
  • {PARI 21-51} muruku kamaḻ puki nuḻinta vaḷi um
  • {PARI 21-52} acumpum aruvi aru viṭar paranta
  • {PARI 21-53} pacum pūṇ cēey niṉ kuṉṟam naṉku uṭittu
  • {PARI 21-54} kaṇ oḷir tikaḻ aṭar iṭu cuṭar paṭar koṭi miṉṉu pōl
  • {PARI 21-55} oḷ naki taki vaki neṟi peṟa iṭi iṭi iḻittu yātta
  • {PARI 21-56} ceṇṇiki kōti katuppōṭu iyala
  • {PARI 21-57} maṇi maruḷ tēṉ makiḻ taṭpa olki
  • {PARI 21-58} piṇi nekiḻa pim tukil nōkkam civappu ūra
  • {PARI 21-59} pūm koṭi pōla nuṭaṅkuvāḷ āṅku taṉ
  • {PARI 21-60} cīr taku kēḷvaṉ uruṭṭum tuṭi cīrāṉ
  • {PARI 21-61} kōṭu aṇinta muttu āram olka ocipavaḷ ēr
  • {PARI 21-62} āṭi aciya aṇi aciya tāṉ aciyum
  • {PARI 21-63} vāṭi uḷar kompar pōṉm
  • {PARI 21-64} vāḷi puraḷpavi pōlum tuṭi cīrkku
  • {PARI 21-65} tōḷ ūḻ peyarppavaḷ kaṇ
  • {PARI 21-66} māṟu amar aṭṭavi maṟa vēl peyarppavi
  • {PARI 21-67} āṟu iru tōḷavi aṟu mukam virittavi
  • {PARI 21-68} naṉṟu amar āyamōṭu oruṅku niṉ aṭiyuṟi
  • {PARI 21-69} iṉṟu pōl iyika eṉa paravutum
  • {PARI 21-70} oṉṟār tēytta celva niṉ toḻutu ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Very pleasing indeed it [was] - pitiful [is] it [now] -
  • to stay in the village too that was near the ghat,
  • where they(?)[FNote_341] look at the drying opulence of grouped heaps
  • of prawn from the surrounding backwaters, in coiled roughness[FNote_342]
  • similar to the green back of unripe Tumeric,
  • bring them in front of the rich Puṉṉi shade [and] spread [them],
  • - before seeing the deer-like look
  • of the innocent dughter of the fish-catching fishermen,
  • with a waist fitting beautifully to [her] wide hips, ending disgust.[FNote_343]

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O red-mouthed green parakeets that shortened the bent ears,
  • after you have fulfilled your need,
  • taking the opportunity of becoming full, avoiding fear,
  • it is necessary indeed to speak of my need. I beg [you] imploringly.
  • When you go to your relations
  • in his land of jack trees with many [flower-]clusters,
  • talk to the lord of that mountin, to say
  • the inexperienced dughter of the Kuṟa people from the forest of this mountin
  • has become one to guard the millet.

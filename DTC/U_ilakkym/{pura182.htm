{PUṞAM 182}

((Originally extracted from master file ^PURAM.OCP))

  • {PU 182-1} uṇṭāl amma i ulakam intirar
  • {PU 182-2} amiḻtam iyivatu āyiṉum iṉitu eṉa
  • {PU 182-3} tamiyar uṇṭal um ilar ē muṉivu ilar
  • {PU 182-4} tuñcal um ilar piṟar añcuvatu añci
  • {PU 182-5} pukaḻ eṉiṉ uyir um koṭukkuvar paḻi eṉiṉ
  • {PU 182-6} ulaku uṭaṉ peṟiṉum koḷḷalar ayarvu ilar
  • {PU 182-7} aṉṉa māṭci aṉiyar āki
  • {PU 182-8} tamakku eṉa muyalā nōṉ tāḷ
  • {PU 182-9} piṟarkku eṉa muyalunar uṇmiyāṉ ē

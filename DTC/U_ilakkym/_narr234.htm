  1. nerunalum muṉṉā ḷellaiyu moruciṟaip
  2. putuvai yākali ṉataṟkeyta nāṇi
  3. nēriṟai vaḷaittō ṇiṉtōḻi ceyta
  4. vāruyir varuttaṅ kaḷaiyā yōveṉa
  5. veṟkuṟai yuṟuti rāyiṉ coṟkuṟai ((5))
  6. yempatat teḷiya ḷaḷḷa ḷemakkōr
  7. kaṭkāṇ kaṭavuḷ allaḷō peruma
  8. vāykōṉ miḷakiṉ malaiyaṅ koḻuṅkoṭi
  9. tuñcupuli varippuṟan taivaru
  10. mañcucūḻ maṇivarai maṉṉavaṉ makaḷē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Having been ashamed to reach out for that[FNote_738] since you are a newcomer,
  • on one side yesterday and during the daylight of the previous days,
  • if should have a request to me, saying
  • "won't you remove the suffering of [my] difficult life
  • that has been caused by your friend with fine-jointed bangled arms",
  • - she is not easy of approach for us[FNote_739] for worded requests.
  • Is she not like a[FNote_740] goddess seen with the eyes to us, o great one?
  • [She is] the daughter of the king of the sapphire mountain, surrounded by
  • clouds,
  • where the rich mountain creeper of the pretty-stemmed black pepper
  • rubs the striped back of a sleeping tiger.

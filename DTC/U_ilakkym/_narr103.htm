{{Readable Translation}}

  • Having understood one thing, heart, speak
  • of what you have resolved, whether we say we return,
  • whether we say we depart for male work:
  • we will suffer in the newly(?)[FNote_344] hot wilderness,
  • where the green-eyed red dog, bent down to kill the hunger,
  • pressing to the ground [its] belly with skinny breasts, milk ceased,
  • for the moistness that is not water coming in standing[FNote_345] from the elephant bull,
  • formerly with fierce anger, elated by must,
  • felling the big [branches] of the small-leaved Neem tree with red trunk,
  • feels pity, the husband gone for the unfailing hunt[FNote_346]
  • thinking of [its] female after ther custom not to be betrayed.

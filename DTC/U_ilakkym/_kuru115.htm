{{Readable Translation}}

  1. If [someone] does something very good, there are also those who don't value [it].[FNote_467]
  2. Even if she possesses only one good, take care of [her]
  3. to end the sulking, as desire has grown.
  4. O man from the land of good mountins,
  5. where wild cows with tender git seize leaves [and] sleep
  6. on the cool, fragrant slope on which the dancing bamboo has grown long,
  7. {--!}without you she doesn't exist.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. nōyuṅ kaimikap peritē meyyun
  2. tīyumiḻ teṟaliṉ veytā kiṉṟē
  3. yoyyeṉac ciṟitāṅ kuyiriyar paiyeṉa
  4. muṉṟiṟ koḷiṉē nantuvaḷ periteṉa
  5. niraiya neñcat taṉṉaik kuyttāṇ ((5))
  6. ṭuraiyiṉi vāḻi tōḻi puraiyi
  7. ṉuṇṇē relvaḷai ñekiḻttōṉ kuṉṟat
  8. taṇṇa ṉeṭuvarai yāṭit taṇṇeṉa
  9. viyalaṟai mūḻkiya vaḷiyeṉ
  10. pacalai yākan tīṇṭiya ciṟitē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Exceedingly great [is] the pain and my body
  • became hot as if being shot [and] scorched by fire.
  • "If I take [her] to the frontyard, gently, so that she
  • breathes there a little, deeply(?), she will flourish[FNote_743]",
  • [thus] tell now, o friend,
  • to mother with [her] heart of hell, after having sent [me] there
  • - so that the wind diving between the wide rocks
  • cooly [and] playing on the superior long mountain
  • in the hills of him who loosened my holeless(?)[FNote_744] fine fine bangles,
  • will grip for a little [while] my pallorous bosom.

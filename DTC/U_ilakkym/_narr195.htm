{{Readable Translation}}

  • That you should not be considerate? It [is] cruel,
  • o tender man from the sea,
  • where the young otters feeds on rich fish in the dark backwaters
  • [and] takes a resting place in the grove of Tillai trees,
  • - I saw:
  • The eyes of her you longed for are dimming, wet
  • as the water-wrought appearance
  • of the blue waterlilies that don't close, [their] petals glittering[FNote_621],
  • when they fall[FNote_622] under the sharp blades of the rice-cutting workmen.

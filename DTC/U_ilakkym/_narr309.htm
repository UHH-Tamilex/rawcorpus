{{Readable Translation}}

  • Don't cry out of love, live, friend,
  • looking at [my] emaciated shoulders and [my] faded lines
  • and my colour that lost the beauty of sprouts,
  • saying "her misery has been made by me".
  • Won't you say, for sure, that they are not those
  • who know[FNote_920] that it is becoming misery to us[FNote_921],
  • the intimacy with the man from a land of great rins, where the dripping
  • tips of the wide leaves are perturbed on the rich stems of the plantin?
  • I do believe in the friendship he has.

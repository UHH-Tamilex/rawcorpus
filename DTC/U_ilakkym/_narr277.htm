  3. meyyē karumi yaṉṟiyuñ cevva
  4. ṉaṟivuṅ karitō vaṟaṉilōy niṉakkē
  5. maṉippuṟaṅ kākku māṇperuṅ kiṭakki ((5))
  6. nuṇmuḷ vēlit tātoṭu potuḷiya
  7. tāṟupaṭu pīra mūti vēṟupaṭa
  8. nāṟṟa miṉmiyiṟ pacali yūtāy
  9. ciṟukuṟum paṟavik kōṭi virivuṭa
  10. ṉeñcunekiḻ ceytataṉ payaṉō vaṉpilar ((10))
  11. vemmali yaruñcura miṟantōrk
  12. keṉṉili yuriyāy ceṉṟavaṇ varavē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Cruel you [are], live, bee, for going [and] coming there:[FNote_864]
  • since I told you "would that this pin passed by",
  • [your] body, besides it being black, [is your] knowledge
  • completely black too, o you of no virtue?
  • For you,
  • who have filled yourself on the Pīram springing up in bunches
  • that have become full of pollen on the fine-thorned hedges
  • of glorious great expanse that guard the backside of [our] house
  • [and] donōt fill yourself with [my] pallor becuse doesnōt give fragrance,
  • so that it changes, [is there any] yield in doing what weakens [my] heart
  • in a hurry, running off to [your] small short bee[-companion]?
  • - you who donōt talk of my state to the one who traversed
  • the difficult desert of hot mountins, the loveless one.

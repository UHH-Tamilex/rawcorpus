{{Readable Translation}}

  • Can she really have great haste,[FNote_893]
  • like us whenever we see [her] flower-eyed companions
  • in the old village of old victory that has [always] conducted festivals
  • on the village common of vast area, where the bulky old potter,
  • becoming full on cool(?) toddy, wearing a garland
  • from the sapphire-eared Nocci tree, declares the offerings that are placed,
  • - the mother that brought forth the strong young man
  • who compelled [her] to go together, taking [her],
  • making the one with thick hir believe his words?

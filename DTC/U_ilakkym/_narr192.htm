{{Metrical text}}

  1. kuruti vēṭki yurukeḻu vayamāṉ
  2. valimiku muṉpiṉ maḻakaḷiṟu pārkku
  3. marampayil cōli maliyap pūḻiya
  4. ruruvat turuvi ṉāṇmēya lāru
  5. māri yeṇkiṉ maliccura nīḷiṭi ((5))
  6. nīnayantu varuta levaṉeṉap palapulan
  7. taḻutaṉi yuṟiyu mammā varivi
  8. payaṅkeḻu palaviṉ kollik kuṭavarip
  9. pūtam puṇartta putitiyal pāvi
  10. virikati riḷaveyiṟ ṟōṉṟi yaṉṉaniṉ ((10))
  11. ṉāynala muḷḷi variṉemak
  12. kēma māku malimuta lāṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O you pretty dark young woman who remins crying(?)[FNote_607],
  • very vexed [by the thought]: "what of your longing [for me and] coming
  • the long way through the mountinous desert of the bear in the shower
  • that becomes full preying early in the day on the beautiful sheep
  • of the Pūḻiyar(?)[FNote_608], while there are many grove dense with trees,
  • where the beautiful strong animal with a demand for blood[FNote_609]
  • peers out for the infant elephant bull of much strong strength(?)[FNote_610],
  • joyful is for us the way along the foot of the mountin,
  • remembering [your] choice goodness,
  • as the appearance of the young sunlight with unfolding rays,
  • as the newly arising doll put together by the spirits(?)[FNote_611]
  • on the west mountin of Kolli with richly yielding jack-trees.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Alas, o friend, if there were
  2. no separation from us - good indeed [would be]
  3. the intimacy, which has mixed [us] with the man from the transverse mountains,
  4. where there falls down like a snake taking over(?)[FNote_545] the earth
  5. the hastily descending waterfall, which resounds, dashing against the stones,
  6. attacking, so that the swaying twigs full of flowers
  7. of the long-trunked Vēṅkai, thick on the low height(?) become lonely.

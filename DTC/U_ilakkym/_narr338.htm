{{Metrical text}}

  1. kaṭuṅkatir ñāyiṟu malaimaṟain taṉṟē
  2. vaṭumpukoṭi tumiya vāḻi pōḻntavar
  3. neṭuntē riṉṉoli yiravun tōṉṟā
  4. viṟappa vevva naliyu niṉṉilai
  5. niṟuttal vēṇtu meṉṟi nilaippa ((5))
  6. yāṅṅaṉam viṭumō maṟṟē mālkoḷa
  7. viyalirum parappi ṉiraiyeḻun taruntupu
  8. pulavunāṟu ciṟukuṭi maṉṟat tōṅkiya
  9. vāṭaraip peṇṇait tōṭumaṭa lēṟik
  10. koṭuvāyp pēṭai kuṭampaic cēriya ((10))
  11. vuyircelak kaṭaiip puṇartuṇaip
  12. payirta lāṉāp paitalaṅ kurukē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The sun with swift rays has vanished behinnd the mountain.
  • Even at night the sweet sound of his chariot does not appear,
  • crossing with [its] wheels so that they are severed the Aṭumpu creeper.
  • "In your state that is exceedingly afflicted by trouble,
  • considering is necessary", you may say. How
  • will it let go off [me] permanently,
  • - the suffering of the crane(?)[FNote_970], with [its] calling unendingly
  • for [its] companion to unite, impelling [my] life to go,
  • while the she-bird with curved beak joins the nest,
  • mounting the massed stems on the palmyra palm with swaying trunk,
  • grown high on the village common of the small hamlet reeking of fish,
  • risen for prey in the wide dark extension [and] eating,
  • while blackness[FNote_971] takes [me]?

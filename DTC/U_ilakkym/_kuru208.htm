{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Not that I am not one; I am one
  2. - I am not one, friend, becuse of one thing[FNote_831]
  3. with the man from the land where the Vēṅki flowers, to be plucked
  4. while standing(?)[FNote_832] to be worn in the tresses by the women of the hill people,
  5. [its] crushed trunk trampled by the fighting elephant bulls of the hills.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Becuse we are not able to be content without you,
  2. even if you do to us many unsweet [things],
  3. o great one, we [are] like the blue water-lily in your village,
  4. flowering in the field, chased away [ever] agin,
  5. without saying 'as for [these] cruel ones, we move away[FNote_1264] from [their] ground.',
  6. even if [they] let it fade on the long [field] borders,
  7. so that the fragrance gives way, flourished for bees to eat,
  8. when the busy(?)[FNote_1265] people complete [their] dily work[FNote_1266].

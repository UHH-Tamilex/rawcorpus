{{Readable Translation}}

  • Listen, live, friend, this [is] one thing:
  • May our lord live for many [days],
  • [he who was] as a doctor that selected [and] gave a remedy,
  • without giving the thing wanted, to one who has a difficult disease[FNote_439],
  • when I cried wanting bright bangles in perfect row,
  • - he who tightened a golden armlet that doesn't move [on my arm],
  • a help that hides the blame of [my] shoulder,
  • controlling(?) the limits, when he comes without leaving, in order to leave(?),
  • as if he knew of the existence of the separation ...?
  • ...? on our part(?) with the man from a land of well-praised mountains.

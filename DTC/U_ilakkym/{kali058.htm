Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 58-1} vār uṟu vaṇar aimpāl vaṇaṅku iṟi neṭu mel tōḷ
  • {KALI 58-2} pēr eḻil malar uṇ kaṇ piṇi eḻil māṉ nōkkiṉ
  • {KALI 58-3} kār etir taḷir mēṉi kaviṉ peṟu cuṭar nutal
  • {KALI 58-4} kūr eyiṟṟu muki veḷ pal koṭi puriyum nucuppiṉāy
  • {KALI 58-5} nēr cilampu ari ārppa niri toṭi ki vīciṉi
  • {KALI 58-6} ār uyir vuvikkoṇṭu aṟintīyātu iṟappāy kēḷ
  • {KALI 58-7} uḷaṉ ā eṉ uyiri uṇṭu uyavu nōy kimmika
  • {KALI 58-8} iḷamiyāṉ uṇarātāy niṉ tavaṟu illāṉ um
  • {KALI 58-9} kaḷinar il nōy ceyyum kaviṉ aṟintu aṇintu tam
  • {KALI 58-10} vaḷamiyāṉ pōttanta numar tavaṟu il eṉpāy
  • {KALI 58-11} naṭi melintu ayarvu uṟīi nāḷ um eṉ naliyum nōy
  • {KALI 58-12} maṭamiyāṉ uṇarātāy niṉ tavaṟu illāṉ um
  • {KALI 58-13} iṭi nillātu eykkum niṉ uru aṟintu aṇintu tam
  • {KALI 58-14} uṭimiyāl pōttanta numar tavaṟu il eṉpāy
  • {KALI 58-15} allal kūrntu aḻivuṟa aṇaṅku āki aṭarum nōy
  • {KALI 58-16} colliṉum aṟiyātāy niṉ tavaṟu illāṉ um
  • {KALI 58-17} olli ē uyir vuvum uru aṟintu aṇintu tam
  • {KALI 58-18} celvattāl pōttanta numar tavaṟu il eṉpāy
  • {KALI 58-19} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 58-20} oṟuppiṉ yāṉ oṟuppatu numari yāṉ maṟṟu i nōy
  • {KALI 58-21} poṟukkalām varittu aṉṟi peritu āyiṉ polam kuḻāy
  • {KALI 58-22} maṟuttu i ūr maṉṟattu maṭal ēṟi
  • {KALI 58-23} niṟukkuveṉ pōlval yāṉ nī paṭu paḻi ē

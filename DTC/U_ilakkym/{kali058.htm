Kalittokai

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 58-1} vār uṟu vaṇar aimpāl vaṇaṅku iṟai neṭu mel tōḷ
  • {KALI 58-2} pēr eḻil malar uṇ kaṇ piṇai eḻil māṉ nōkkiṉ
  • {KALI 58-3} kār etir taḷir mēṉi kaviṉ peṟu cuṭar nutal
  • {KALI 58-4} kūr eyiṟṟu mukai veḷ pal koṭi puraiyum nucuppiṉāy
  • {KALI 58-5} nēr cilampu ari ārppa nirai toṭi kai vīciṉai
  • {KALI 58-6} ār uyir vauvikkoṇṭu aṟintīyātu iṟappāy kēḷ
  • {KALI 58-7} uḷaṉ ā eṉ uyirai uṇṭu uyavu nōy kaimmika
  • {KALI 58-8} iḷamaiyāṉ uṇarātāy niṉ tavaṟu illāṉ um
  • {KALI 58-9} kaḷainar il nōy ceyyum kaviṉ aṟintu aṇintu tam
  • {KALI 58-10} vaḷamaiyāṉ pōttanta numar tavaṟu il eṉpāy
  • {KALI 58-11} naṭai melintu ayarvu uṟīi nāḷ um eṉ naliyum nōy
  • {KALI 58-12} maṭamaiyāṉ uṇarātāy niṉ tavaṟu illāṉ um
  • {KALI 58-13} iṭai nillātu eykkum niṉ uru aṟintu aṇintu tam
  • {KALI 58-14} uṭaimaiyāl pōttanta numar tavaṟu il eṉpāy
  • {KALI 58-15} allal kūrntu aḻivuṟa aṇaṅku āki aṭarum nōy
  • {KALI 58-16} colliṉum aṟiyātāy niṉ tavaṟu illāṉ um
  • {KALI 58-17} ollai ē uyir vauvum uru aṟintu aṇintu tam
  • {KALI 58-18} celvattāl pōttanta numar tavaṟu il eṉpāy
  • {KALI 58-19} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 58-20} oṟuppiṉ yāṉ oṟuppatu numarai yāṉ maṟṟu i nōy
  • {KALI 58-21} poṟukkalām varaittu aṉṟi peritu āyiṉ polam kuḻāy
  • {KALI 58-22} maṟuttu i ūr maṉṟattu maṭal ēṟi
  • {KALI 58-23} niṟukkuveṉ pōlval yāṉ nī paṭu paḻi ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Day by day being kept back he does not come
  2. and[FNote_1175] at the edge of all daylights he does not appear -
  3. where is he, friend,
  4. who traversed the mountains glittering up into the sky,
  5. where a single kite yearning for flesh is perched
  6. on the top of a Ñemai tree? What became, in [that] moment,
  7. of the lovely dove calling for [its] low-backed[FNote_1176] female,
  8. many times? This is, isn't it,[FNote_1177] the season he had spoken of here?[FNote_1178]

  8. viriyuḷaip polinta vīṅkucelaṟ kalimā
  9. vaṉpari tayaṅka veḻīit taṇpeyaṟ

  11. velvirun tayaru maṉaivi
  12. melliṟaip paṇaittōṭ ṭuyilamar vōyē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "In the woodlands, alas, [is] the village of her I longed for,
  • where the desirable inexperienced doe, that chewed the forked ear
  • of the wet-leaved millet filled with ...?
  • of dust from the ancient forest felled by the sowers,
  • abides with the male Iralai in the pretty hemp(?) wilderness.
  • [This] night the king has let go [of us]"[FNote_396], speaking [thus]
  • don't be distressed, may your chaplet live: while they come to see(?)[FNote_397],
  • while the rear of the shore of sand brought by the forest river in the cool rain stays behind, making rise so that they glitter in strong motion
  • the proud horses with swelling gait on which the broad mane is glittering,
  • you will abide in sleep on the bamboo shoulder with soft joints[FNote_398]
  • of her in the house who will immerse in a festival of lights.

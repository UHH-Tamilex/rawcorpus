  • {KALI 61-19} peṇṭir aruḷa kiṭantatu evaṉ kol ō

  • {KALI 61-23} mallal ūr āṅkaṇ paṭum ē naṟu nutal
  • {KALI 61-24} nalkāḷ kaṇmāṟiviṭiṉ eṉa celvāṉ ām
  • {KALI 61-25} eḷḷi nakiṉum varūum iṭai iṭai

  • {KALI 61-28} uḷḷam kuṟaipaṭā āṟu

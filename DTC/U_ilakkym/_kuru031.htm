{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Be it on the festival crowded by warriors
  2. or at the Tuṇaṅki dance where women embrace [each other],
  3. nowhere do I see the one famously fit [for me][FNote_147].
  4. I am but one girl on the dance floor[FNote_148], and he
  5. who made my shining bangles cut(?) from conches loose,
  6. [that] great master is but one boy on the dance floor.(?)[FNote_149]

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. O man from the shining mountains where tumbles from the mountain-side,
  2. resounding like the drum of dancers with wise words,
  3. the long white waterfall high on the big mountain,
  4. pain is fitting. To say: desire
  5. [is] something good - on the part of the ignorant
  6. [that is] lasting great folly.

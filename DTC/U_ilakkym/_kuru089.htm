{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Let other people talk
  2. - when pounding[FNote_362] at the split mouth of the broad-based mortar
  3. this tender-natured little woman sang the Vaḷḷi[FNote_363],
  4. like a good-natured image
  5. of the black-eyed god drawn on the western [side of] the mountin,[FNote_364]
  6. the dreadful Kolli of Poṟiyaṉ with big ornaments.
  7. What harm is it to this foolish village?

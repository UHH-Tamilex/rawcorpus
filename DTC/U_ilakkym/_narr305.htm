{{Metrical text}}

  1. variyaṇi pantum vāṭiya vayaliyu
  2. mayilaṭi yaṉṉa mākkura ṉocciyuṅ
  3. kaṭiyuṭi viyaṉakark kāṇvarat tōṉṟat
  4. tamiyēṉ kaṇṭataṇ ṭaliyun teṟuvara
  5. nōyā kiṉṟē makaḷiniṉ ṟōḻi ((5))
  6. yericiṉan taṇinta viliyi lañciṉi
  7. varippuṟap puṟaviṉ pulampuko ṭeḷviḷi
  8. yuruppavi ramiyat tamarppaṉa ṇōkki
  9. yilaṅkili veḷvēl viṭaliya
  10. vilaṅkumali yāriṭi naliyuṅko leṉavē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The line-adorned ball and the faded Vayali
  • and the Nocci with big ears as a pecock's foot,
  • when these appear for [my eyes] to see[FNote_912] in the well-protected wide mansion,
  • and the garden seen by me who am solitary, scorchingly(?)
  • they have become pin, o dughter[FNote_913] - "your friend,
  • will she suffer on the difficult way through the transverse mountins
  • of the young man[FNote_914] with white spear with a shining leaf,
  • looking like[FNote_915] one who has come to be at strife on the occasion of shining heat
  • with the clear sound conveying loneliness of the dove with striped back
  • on the leaf-less pretty branch as the anger of the flame has come down?"

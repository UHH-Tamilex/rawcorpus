{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Don't worry, [you of] of choice jewels; very anxious is he,
  2. the man from the yielding mountain land, who also fears blame,[FNote_571]
  3. [because] of the pallor sprung up on your beautiful, [now] dimmed body
  4. which is without permanence,
  5. like wealth belonging to a man firm in duty
  6. with a longing heart[FNote_572], which wanted good fame[FNote_573],
  7. because only transience has permanence.[FNote_574]

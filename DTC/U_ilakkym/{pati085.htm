Patiṟṟup pattu

((Originally extracted from master file ^PATIRRU.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PATI 85-1} nal maram tuvaṉṟiya nāṭu pala tarīi
  • {PATI 85-2} poṉ avir puṉi ceyal ilaṅkum perum pūṇ
  • {PATI 85-3} oṉṉā pūṭki ceṉṉiyar perumāṉ
  • {PATI 85-4} iṭṭa veḷ vēl mutti tam eṉa
  • {PATI 85-5} muṉ tiṇi mutalvar pōla niṉṟu
  • {PATI 85-6} tīm cuṉi niliiya tiru mā maruṅkiṉ
  • {PATI 85-7} kōṭu pala virinta nāṭu kāṇ neṭu vari
  • {PATI 85-8} cūṭā naṟaviṉ nāḷ makiḻ irukki
  • {PATI 85-9} aracavi paṇiya aṟam purintu vayaṅkiya
  • {PATI 85-10} maṟam puri koḷki vayaṅku ce nāviṉ
  • {PATI 85-11} uvali kūrā kavali il neñciṉ
  • {PATI 85-12} naṉavil pāṭiya nal ici
  • {PATI 85-13} kapilaṉ peṟṟa ūriṉum pala ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Even if we endure today the trouble of heavy affliction,
  2. for mother to see that[FNote_1441] the spear-carrying [priest],
  3. who comes up(?) with the Veṟi dance doesn't know a remedy for the pin,
  4. - may he not come, friend,
  5. in the night, the man from a land of shining mountins
  6. with groves, where it is ringing like anklets:
  7. the drum[FNote_1442] in the hand of the creeper woman chasing away parakeets
  8. eating from the millet with ears big as the hand of a female elephant, on the slope.

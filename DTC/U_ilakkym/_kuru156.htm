{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Brahmin's son! Brahmin's son![FNote_627]
  2. Holding a dangling water jar by means of a stick
  3. of the red-flowered coral tree after having stripped it of the good bark,
  4. [eating] austere food, brahmin's son!
  5. Is there also a remedy of the kind
  6. that unites those who are separated, among all[FNote_628] your words
  7. of unwritten teachings? Is this madness?

Altern

7b: Is there also a remedy ..., or [is] this illusion?[FNote_629]

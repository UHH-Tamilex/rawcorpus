{{Metrical text}}

  1. ciṟuvī ñāḻaṟ ṟēṉṟō yoḷḷiṇar
  2. nēriḻai makaḷir vārmaṇa liḻaitta
  3. vaṇṭaṟ pāvai vaṉamulai muṟṟat
  4. toṇpoṟic cuṇaṅki ṉaitupaṭat tāaṅ
  5. kaṇṭal vēlik kāmar ciṟukuṭi ((5))
  6. yellivan taṉṟō tēreṉac colli
  7. yalareḻun taṉṟiv vūrē palaruḷu
  8. meṉṉōk kiṉarē yaṉṉai nāḷai
  9. maṇippū muṇṭakaṅ koyyē ṉāyi
  10. ṉaṉikkavi ṉuṇmaiyō varitē maṇikkaḻi ((10))
  11. naṟumpūṅ kāṉal vantavar
  12. vaṟuntēr pōta lataṉiṉu maritē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Did a chariot come at night
  • to [our] desirable small hamlet with mangrove fences,
  • where the honey-soaked bright clusters of the small-blossomed Ñāḻal
  • are spreading, to be beautiful as bright beauty spots[FNote_604],
  • in the courtyard, on the beautiful breast of the play[FNote_605] dolls
  • made of wet(?) sand by women with fine jewels?" speaking thus
  • gossip arose in this village. Among many
  • mother looked at me. Tomorrow,
  • if I don't pluck the Muṇṭakam's sapphire flowers,
  • the [further] being of [my] abundant beauty [is] difficult.
  • More difficult than that [is] the going of the chariot in which he comes,
  • taking the way(?)[FNote_606] through the seashore grove with fragrant flowers by the sapphire[-coloured] backwaters.

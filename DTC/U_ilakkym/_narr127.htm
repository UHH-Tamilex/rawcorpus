{{Readable Translation}}

  • What, bard, if that man comes
  • to the village, where the heron with wet back, that stirred
  • the dark backwaters, shivers from the spray thrown on [its] quills?
  • Folly says "we will go to the seashore-grove
  • even without the soft man who gives loneliness",
  • where she puts a head[FNote_410] on the doll not brought forth [yet[FNote_411] and] regards [it],[FNote_412]
  • when she herself formerly played games with [her] companions,
  • even while her people are uneducated angry men
  • that filled the rich mansion with the food of rich fish.

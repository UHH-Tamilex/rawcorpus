{{Readable Translation}}

  1. She who wouldn't even drink milk, who wouldn't wish more than(?)[FNote_1579] a ball,
  2. who would immerse herself with [her] companions in play - now!
  3. Did she feel it would be easy,
  4. going with him to a difficult place, where the bamboo dries up,
  5. where the bull with high tusks[FNote_1580], who pierced the dry-leaved Ōmai tree,
  6. listens to the thundering, heavy voice of rain
  7. on the slope of the hot mountain in the hills[FNote_1581] in summer?

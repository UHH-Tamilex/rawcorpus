Paripāṭal 1

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 1-1} āyiram viritta aṇaṅku uṭai arum talai
  • {PARI 1-2} tī umiḻ tiṟaloṭu muṭi micai aṇavara
  • {PARI 1-3} mā uṭai malar mārpiṉ mai il vāl vaḷai mēṉi
  • {PARI 1-4} cēy uyar paṇai micai eḻil vēḻam ēntiya
  • {PARI 1-5} vāy vāṅkum vaḷai nāñcil oru kuḻai oruvaṉai
  • {PARI 1-6} erimalar ciṉaiiya kaṇṇai pūvai
  • {PARI 1-7} viri malar puraiyum mēṉiyai mēṉi
  • {PARI 1-8} tiru ñemirntu amarnta mārpiṉai mārpil
  • {PARI 1-9} teri maṇi piṟaṅkum pūṇiṉai māl varai
  • {PARI 1-10} eri tirintaṉṉa poṉ puṉai uṭukkaiyai
  • {PARI 1-11} cēval am koṭiyōy niṉ vala vayiṉ niṟuttum
  • {PARI 1-12} ēval uḻantamai kūṟum
  • {PARI 1-13} nā val antaṇar aru maṟai poruḷ ē
  • {PARI 1-14} iṇai piri aṇi tuṇi paṇi eri puraiya
  • {PARI 1-15} viṭar iṭu cuṭar paṭar polam puṉai viṉai malar
  • {PARI 1-16} neri kiṭar eri purai taṉa miku taṉa muraṇ miku
  • {PARI 1-17} kaṭaṟaru maṇiyoṭu um muttu yātta nēraṇi
  • {PARI 1-18} neṟi ceṟi veṟi uṟu mural viṟal vaṇaṅku aṇaṅku vil
  • {PARI 1-19} tār aṇi tuṇi maṇi veyil uṟaḻ eḻil pukaḻ alar mārpiṉ
  • {PARI 1-20} eri vayira nuti eṟi paṭai eruttu malai ivar navaiyiṉil
  • {PARI 1-21} tuṇi paṭal ila maṇi veyil uṟaḻ eḻil nakku
  • {PARI 1-22} imai iruḷ akala muṟu kiṟuku puri oru puri nāḷ malar
  • {PARI 1-23} malar ilakiṉa vaḷar paritiyiṉ oḷi maṇi mārpu aṇi
  • {PARI 1-24} maṇam mika nāṟu uruviṉa virai vaḷi miku kaṭu vicai
  • {PARI 1-25} uṭuvuṟu talai nirai itaḻ aṇi vayiriya amararai
  • {PARI 1-26} por eḻuntu uṭaṉṟu iraittu uraiiya tāṉavar
  • {PARI 1-27} ciram umiḻ puṉal poḻipu iḻintu uram
  • {PARI 1-28} utirpu atirpu alam toṭā amar veṉṟa kaṇai
  • {PARI 1-29} poruvēm eṉṟavar matam tapa kaṭantu
  • {PARI 1-30} ceru mēmpaṭṭa ceyir tīr aṇṇal
  • {PARI 1-31} iruvar tātai ilaṅku pūṇ māal
  • {PARI 1-32} teruḷa niṉ varavu aṟital
  • {PARI 1-33} maruḷ aṟu tērcci muṉaivarkku um aritu ē
  • {PARI 1-34} aṉṉa marapiṉ aṉaiyōy niṉṉai
  • {PARI 1-35} iṉṉaṉ eṉṟu uraittal emakku evaṉ eḷitu
  • {PARI 1-36} arumai naṟku aṟiyiṉum ārvam niṉ vayiṉ
  • {PARI 1-37} perumaiyiṉ vallā yām ivaṇ moḻipavai
  • {PARI 1-38} melliya eṉāa veṟāatu alli am
  • {PARI 1-39} tiru maṟu mārpa nī aruḷal vēṇṭum
  • {PARI 1-40} viṟal miku viḻu cīr antaṇar kākkum
  • {PARI 1-41} aṟaṉ um ārvalarkku aruḷ um nī
  • {PARI 1-42} tiṟaṉ ilōr tiruttiya tītu tīr koḷkai
  • {PARI 1-43} maṟaṉ um māṟṟalarkku aṇaṅku um nī
  • {PARI 1-44} am kaṇ vāṉattu aṇi nilā tikaḻtarum
  • {PARI 1-45} tiṅkaḷ um teṟu katir kaṉali um nī
  • {PARI 1-46} ai talai uyiriya aṇaṅku uṭai arum tiṟal
  • {PARI 1-47} maintu uṭai oruvaṉ um maṭaṅkal um nī
  • {PARI 1-48} nalam muḻutu aḷaiiya pukar aṟu kāṭci
  • {PARI 1-49} pulam um pūvaṉ um nāṟṟam um nī
  • {PARI 1-50} valaṉ uyar eḻili um māka vicumpu um
  • {PARI 1-51} nilaṉ um nīṭiya imayam um nī
  • {PARI 1-52} ataṉāl
  • {PARI 1-53} iṉṉōr aṉaiyai iṉaiyai al eṉa
  • {PARI 1-54} aṉṉōr yām ivaṇ kāṇāmaiyiṉ
  • {PARI 1-55} poṉ aṇi nēmi valam koṇṭu ēntiya
  • {PARI 1-56} maṉṉiya mutalvaṉai ātaliṉ
  • {PARI 1-57} niṉṉōr aṉaiyai niṉ pukaḻoṭu um polintu ē
  • {PARI 1-58} niṉ okkum pukaḻ niḻalavai
  • {PARI 1-59} poṉ okkum uṭaiyavai
  • {PARI 1-60} puḷḷiṉ koṭiyavai puri vaḷaiyiṉavai
  • {PARI 1-61} eḷḷunar kaṭantu aṭṭa ikal nēmiyavai
  • {PARI 1-62} maṇṇuṟu maṇi pāy uruviṉavai
  • {PARI 1-63} eṇ iṟanta pukaḻavai eḻil mārpiṉavai
  • {PARI 1-64} āṅku
  • {PARI 1-65} kāmaru cuṟṟamoṭu oruṅku niṉ aṭiyuṟai
  • {PARI 1-66} yām īyaintu oṉṟupu vaikal um polika eṉa
  • {PARI 1-67} ēmuṟu neñcattēm paravutum
  • {PARI 1-68} vāymoḻi pulava niṉ tāḷ niḻal toḻutu ē

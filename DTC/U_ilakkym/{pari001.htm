Paripāṭal 1

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 1-1} āyiram viritta aṇaṅku uṭi arum tali
  • {PARI 1-2} tī umiḻ tiṟaloṭu muṭi mici aṇavara
  • {PARI 1-3} mā uṭi malar mārpiṉ mi il vāl vaḷi mēṉi
  • {PARI 1-4} cēy uyar paṇi mici eḻil vēḻam ēntiya
  • {PARI 1-5} vāy vāṅkum vaḷi nāñcil oru kuḻi oruvaṉi
  • {PARI 1-6} erimalar ciṉiiya kaṇṇi pūvi
  • {PARI 1-7} viri malar puriyum mēṉiyi mēṉi
  • {PARI 1-8} tiru ñemirntu amarnta mārpiṉi mārpil
  • {PARI 1-9} teri maṇi piṟaṅkum pūṇiṉi māl vari
  • {PARI 1-10} eri tirintaṉṉa poṉ puṉi uṭukkiyi
  • {PARI 1-11} cēval am koṭiyōy niṉ vala vayiṉ niṟuttum
  • {PARI 1-12} ēval uḻantami kūṟum
  • {PARI 1-13} nā val antaṇar aru maṟi poruḷ ē
  • {PARI 1-14} iṇi piri aṇi tuṇi paṇi eri puriya
  • {PARI 1-15} viṭar iṭu cuṭar paṭar polam puṉi viṉi malar
  • {PARI 1-16} neri kiṭar eri puri taṉa miku taṉa muraṇ miku
  • {PARI 1-17} kaṭaṟaru maṇiyoṭu um muttu yātta nēraṇi
  • {PARI 1-18} neṟi ceṟi veṟi uṟu mural viṟal vaṇaṅku aṇaṅku vil
  • {PARI 1-19} tār aṇi tuṇi maṇi veyil uṟaḻ eḻil pukaḻ alar mārpiṉ
  • {PARI 1-20} eri vayira nuti eṟi paṭi eruttu mali ivar naviyiṉil
  • {PARI 1-21} tuṇi paṭal ila maṇi veyil uṟaḻ eḻil nakku
  • {PARI 1-22} imi iruḷ akala muṟu kiṟuku puri oru puri nāḷ malar
  • {PARI 1-23} malar ilakiṉa vaḷar paritiyiṉ oḷi maṇi mārpu aṇi
  • {PARI 1-24} maṇam mika nāṟu uruviṉa viri vaḷi miku kaṭu vici
  • {PARI 1-25} uṭuvuṟu tali niri itaḻ aṇi vayiriya amarari
  • {PARI 1-26} por eḻuntu uṭaṉṟu irittu uriiya tāṉavar
  • {PARI 1-27} ciram umiḻ puṉal poḻipu iḻintu uram
  • {PARI 1-28} utirpu atirpu alam toṭā amar veṉṟa kaṇi
  • {PARI 1-29} poruvēm eṉṟavar matam tapa kaṭantu
  • {PARI 1-30} ceru mēmpaṭṭa ceyir tīr aṇṇal
  • {PARI 1-31} iruvar tāti ilaṅku pūṇ māal
  • {PARI 1-32} teruḷa niṉ varavu aṟital
  • {PARI 1-33} maruḷ aṟu tērcci muṉivarkku um aritu ē
  • {PARI 1-34} aṉṉa marapiṉ aṉiyōy niṉṉi
  • {PARI 1-35} iṉṉaṉ eṉṟu urittal emakku evaṉ eḷitu
  • {PARI 1-36} arumi naṟku aṟiyiṉum ārvam niṉ vayiṉ
  • {PARI 1-37} perumiyiṉ vallā yām ivaṇ moḻipavi
  • {PARI 1-38} melliya eṉāa veṟāatu alli am
  • {PARI 1-39} tiru maṟu mārpa nī aruḷal vēṇṭum
  • {PARI 1-40} viṟal miku viḻu cīr antaṇar kākkum
  • {PARI 1-41} aṟaṉ um ārvalarkku aruḷ um nī
  • {PARI 1-42} tiṟaṉ ilōr tiruttiya tītu tīr koḷki
  • {PARI 1-43} maṟaṉ um māṟṟalarkku aṇaṅku um nī
  • {PARI 1-44} am kaṇ vāṉattu aṇi nilā tikaḻtarum
  • {PARI 1-45} tiṅkaḷ um teṟu katir kaṉali um nī
  • {PARI 1-46} ai tali uyiriya aṇaṅku uṭi arum tiṟal
  • {PARI 1-47} mintu uṭi oruvaṉ um maṭaṅkal um nī
  • {PARI 1-48} nalam muḻutu aḷiiya pukar aṟu kāṭci
  • {PARI 1-49} pulam um pūvaṉ um nāṟṟam um nī
  • {PARI 1-50} valaṉ uyar eḻili um māka vicumpu um
  • {PARI 1-51} nilaṉ um nīṭiya imayam um nī
  • {PARI 1-52} ataṉāl
  • {PARI 1-53} iṉṉōr aṉiyi iṉiyi al eṉa
  • {PARI 1-54} aṉṉōr yām ivaṇ kāṇāmiyiṉ
  • {PARI 1-55} poṉ aṇi nēmi valam koṇṭu ēntiya
  • {PARI 1-56} maṉṉiya mutalvaṉi ātaliṉ
  • {PARI 1-57} niṉṉōr aṉiyi niṉ pukaḻoṭu um polintu ē
  • {PARI 1-58} niṉ okkum pukaḻ niḻalavi
  • {PARI 1-59} poṉ okkum uṭiyavi
  • {PARI 1-60} puḷḷiṉ koṭiyavi puri vaḷiyiṉavi
  • {PARI 1-61} eḷḷunar kaṭantu aṭṭa ikal nēmiyavi
  • {PARI 1-62} maṇṇuṟu maṇi pāy uruviṉavi
  • {PARI 1-63} eṇ iṟanta pukaḻavi eḻil mārpiṉavi
  • {PARI 1-64} āṅku
  • {PARI 1-65} kāmaru cuṟṟamoṭu oruṅku niṉ aṭiyuṟi
  • {PARI 1-66} yām īyintu oṉṟupu vikal um polika eṉa
  • {PARI 1-67} ēmuṟu neñcattēm paravutum
  • {PARI 1-68} vāymoḻi pulava niṉ tāḷ niḻal toḻutu ē

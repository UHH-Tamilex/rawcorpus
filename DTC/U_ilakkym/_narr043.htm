{{Readable Translation}}

  • Traversing the hot [and] difficult places,
  • where for those who are going through the difficult desert in a distant land
  • food is the leavings that stayed behind from those who had filled themselves[FNote_177]
  • having killed an exhausted wild cow: the red dogs in painful hunger,
  • on the hilly slopes where the sun has been lasting,
  • in the form of blazing heat like fine cloth spread out,
  • - to you it has become a pleasure in which your body rejoices. To her,
  • having heard of [her] staying behind, it has come as ruin,
  • as to the king with one fortress
  • of breaking fortifications, agitated since he doesn`t see someone to relieve him
  • of the king waiting outside with green-eyed elephants,
  • because the monarch who said 'don`t fear' has let go [of him].

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When you shielded [your] eyes [from] the rain, you didn't see the sky.
  2. When water spread [and] flowed, you didn't see the ground.
  3. When daylight went, a great darkness fell.[FNote_1424]
  4. While many are sleeping, at midnight
  5. how did you come, man from the high[FNote_1425] mountains?
  6. Of our little home smelling of Vēṅkai,
  7. how did you know? Ah, I ache.

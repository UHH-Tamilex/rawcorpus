{{Readable Translation}}

  • When the salt-merchants are all gone(?)[FNote_593]
  • leaving [their] soil along with [their] kinsfolk, who hated to stay there,
  • crossing the moonlit sand on carts by small paths,
  • procliming the price of the salt in other lands,
  • given [in exchange] for white rice, ripened in their land[FNote_594],
  • [the place] will be unpleasant.
  • Foolish indeed, alas, [are] you, man from the sea:
  • your house has evenings of loneliness too, to which one listens(?)[FNote_595],
  • along with the cold wind that dashes unpleasantly on all sides,
  • - you who do not consider that she won't live,
  • as a water-lily in scanty water,
  • trampled by the white herons that have fed on fish in groups.

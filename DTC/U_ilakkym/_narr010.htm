{{Metrical text}}

  1. aṇṇān tēntiya vaṉamulai taḷariṉum
  2. poṉṉēr mēṉi maṇiyiṟ ṟāḻnta
  3. naṉṉeṭuṅ kūnta ṉaraiyoṭu muṭippiṉu
  4. nītta lōmpumati pūkkē ḻūra
  5. viṉkaṭuṅ kaḷḷi ṉiḻaiyaṇi neṭuntērk ((5))
  6. koṟkaic cōḻar koṅkarp paṇīiyar
  7. veṇkōṭ ṭiyāṉaip pōor kiḻavōṉ
  8. paḻaiyaṉ vēlvāyt taṉṉaniṉ
  9. piḻaiyā naṉmoḻi tēṟiya vivaṭkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even when [her] beautiful breasts which look upwards [and] are eminent slacken
  • [and] if [she] fastens because of whiteness [her] good long tresses[FNote_34]
  • which hang down without jewels on her gold-like body
  • - beware of leaving [her], o man from the flowerful village,
  • her[FNote_35], who has come to believe in your unfailing good words
  • like in the success of the spear of Paḻaiyaṉ,
  • lord of Pōor with white-tusked elephants,
  • when the Koṅkars were humbled by the Cōḻars of Koṟkai
  • with jewel-adorned long chariots [and] with sweet strong toddy.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. aṇṇān tēntiya vaṉamuli taḷariṉum
  2. poṉṉēr mēṉi maṇiyiṟ ṟāḻnta
  3. naṉṉeṭuṅ kūnta ṉariyoṭu muṭippiṉu
  4. nītta lōmpumati pūkkē ḻūra
  5. viṉkaṭuṅ kaḷḷi ṉiḻiyaṇi neṭuntērk ((5))
  6. koṟkic cōḻar koṅkarp paṇīiyar
  7. veṇkōṭ ṭiyāṉip pōor kiḻavōṉ
  8. paḻiyaṉ vēlvāyt taṉṉaniṉ
  9. piḻiyā naṉmoḻi tēṟiya vivaṭkē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even when [her] beautiful breasts which look upwards [and] are eminent slacken
  • [and] if [she] fastens becuse of whiteness [her] good long tresses[FNote_34]
  • which hang down without jewels on her gold-like body
  • - beware of leaving [her], o man from the flowerful village,
  • her[FNote_35], who has come to believe in your unfailing good words
  • like in the success of the spear of Paḻiyaṉ,
  • lord of Pōor with white-tusked elephants,
  • when the Koṅkars were humbled by the Cōḻars of Koṟki
  • with jewel-adorned long chariots [and] with sweet strong toddy.

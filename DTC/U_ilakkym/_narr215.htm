{{Readable Translation}}

  • Since you [are] here today,
  • in the noisy village that lies close to the shore,
  • while the glittering are dashing in the blue-coloured extension,
  • with bright lights of fleshy[FNote_679] oil collected from the fat of the fish,
  • when the women with ahining bangles occupy themselves in the wide mansions
  • in [the works of] sorrowful evening that has come [and] tarries with loneliness,
  • when the day-holding goer[FNote_680], after having climbed over the east sea
  • [and] spreading his brilliant beams, vanishes behind the western mountains,
  • - what if [you] stay with us?
  • Our people won't come unless the hunt succeeds,
  • with a strength that has put [its] intension on the killing shark
  • that was gone so that the pretty net with red-stemmed(?)[FNote_681] bent knots had been damaged.

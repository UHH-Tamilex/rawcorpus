{{Metrical text}}

  1. viṉaiyamai pāvaiyi ṉiyali nuntai
  2. maṉaivarai yiṟantu vantaṉai yāyiṟ
  3. ṟalaināṭ ketiriya taṇpata veḻili
  4. yaṇimiku kāṉat takaṉpuṟam paranta
  5. kaṭuñcem mūtāy kaṇṭuṅ koṇṭu ((5))
  6. nīviḷai yāṭuka ciṟitē yāṉē
  7. maḻakaḷi ṟuriñciya parārai vēṅkai
  8. maṇaliṭu maruṅki ṉirumpuṟam porunti
  9. yamarvari ṉañcēṉ peyarkkuva
  10. ṉumarvariṉ maṟaikuvaṉ māa yōḷē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • When you were to come[FNote_1014], traversing the border of your father's
  • house, moving forward as an artificial doll(?)[FNote_1015],
  • you might play a little,
  • seeing and taking the quick red insects
  • spread on the wide back of the forest much decorated
  • by properly cool clouds that faced the first day.
  • I,
  • Having approached the dark back of the side spread with sand
  • of the large.trunked Vēṅkai on which the young elephant bull has rubbed itself,
  • if battle(?) comes I am not afraid, I will dispell [them],
  • if your people come, I will hide myself, o dark one.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. When playing in water for long the eyes will redden
  2. and in the mouth of one satisfied even[FNote_1421] honey will turn sour.[FNote_1422]
  3. When you are departed, send [us] back to our house,
  4. for whom [you once] had removed the trouble of trembling sorrow,
  5. in a street where quick snakes wander,[FNote_1423]
  6. in our village, at my father's, by the pretty cool pond.

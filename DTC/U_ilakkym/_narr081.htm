{{Metrical text}}

  1. irunilaṇ kuṟiyak koṭṭip parintiṉ
  2. ṟāti pōkiya vacivi ṉōṉṟāṇ
  3. maṉṉar matikku māṇviṉip puravi
  4. koymmayi reruttir peymmaṇi yārppap
  5. pūṇkatil pākaniṉ ṟērē pūṇṭā ((5))
  6. ḻāka vaṉamulik karivalan teṟippa
  7. vaḻutaṉa ḷuṟiyu mammā varivi
  8. viruntayar viruppoṭu vantaṉa ḷaciiya
  9. muṟuva liṉṉaki kāṇka
  10. muṟupaki taṇittaṉa ṉuravuvāḻ vēntē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • May the horse of glorious work that the king values
  • be yoked to the chariot, charioteer,
  • so that the bells they wear on [their] neck with trimmed hir roar,
  • with defectless enduring legs that have gone galopping
  • speedily(?), pounding so that it is worn of the dark ground.
  • May we see the smiling sweet smile that became weary
  • in her who came up(?)[FNote_294] with longing to immerse in a feast,
  • the pretty dark young woman who remined crying(?)
  • so that [her] beautiful breasts on the bosom on which ornaments hang down
  • is heaving, strength wearing away.(?)
  • The king in whom strength lives has [just] appeased the enmity he met with.

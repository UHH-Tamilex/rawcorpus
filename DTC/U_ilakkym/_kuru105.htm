{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Thought[FNote_424] arose, with eyes full of tears,
  2. of the intimacy with the man from the awe-inspiring[FNote_425] mountin
  3. where trembles with heat, beautiful like a woman dancing the Veṟi,
  4. the peacock that ate without knowing
  5. from the rich ears, lid out for the god to enjoy the fragrance,
  6. of the gold-like small millet near(?) the grove of the man from the high ground.

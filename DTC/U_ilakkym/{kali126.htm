Kalittoki

((Originally extracted from master file ^KALI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 126-1} poṉ mali cuṭar cēra pulampiya iṭaṉ nōkki
  • {KALI 126-2} taṉ malintu ulaku ētta taki mati ērtara
  • {KALI 126-3} cekkar koḷ poḻutiṉāṉ oli nīvi iṉa nāri
  • {KALI 126-4} mukkōl koḷ antaṇar mutu moḻi niṉivār pōl
  • {KALI 126-5} ekkar mēl iṟikoḷḷum ilaṅku nīr taṇ cērppa
  • {KALI 126-6} aṇi ciṟi iṉa kuruku olikkum kāl niṉ tiṇ tēr
  • {KALI 126-7} maṇi kural eṉa ivaḷ matikkum maṉ matittāṅku ē
  • {KALI 126-8} uḷ āṉṟa oliya āy iruppa kaṇṭu avi kāṉal
  • {KALI 126-9} puḷ eṉa uṇarntu piṉ pulampu koṇṭu iṉiyum ē
  • {KALI 126-10} nīr nīvi kañaṉṟa pū kamaḻum kāl niṉ mārpil
  • {KALI 126-11} tār nāṟṟam eṉa ivaḷ matikkum maṉ matittāṅku ē
  • {KALI 126-12} alar patattu aci vaḷi vantu olka kaḻi pūtta
  • {KALI 126-13} malar eṉa uṇarntu piṉ mammar koṇṭu iṉiyum ē
  • {KALI 126-14} nīḷ nakar niṟi āṟṟāḷ niṉiyunaḷ vatinta kāl
  • {KALI 126-15} tōḷ mēlāy eṉa niṉṉi matikkum maṉ matittāṅku ē
  • {KALI 126-16} naṉavu eṉa pullum kāl kāṇāḷ āy kaṇṭatu
  • {KALI 126-17} kaṉavu eṉa uṇarntu piṉ kiyaṟṟu kalaṅkum ē
  • {KALI 126-18} eṉa āṅku
  • {KALI 126-19} pala niṉintu iṉiyum pital neñciṉ
  • {KALI 126-20} alamaral nōy uḷ uḻakkum eṉ tōḻi
  • {KALI 126-21} mati maruḷ vāḷ mukam viḷaṅka
  • {KALI 126-22} putu nalam ērtara pūṇka niṉ tēr ē

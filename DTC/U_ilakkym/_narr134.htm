{{colophon}}: iḵtu (ET: itu tamar) iṟceṟippāreṉa vāṟṟāḷāy (EN, ER, ET: -āya) taliviyi yaḵtilar eṉapaṭat (EN, ER, ET: eṉpatupaṭat) tōḻi colliyatu.{{/colophon}}

{{Metrical text}}

  1. iṉiti ṉiṉitu talippaṭutu meṉpa
  2. titukol vāḻi tōḻi kātalar
  3. varukuṟi ceyta variyakac ciṟutiṉic
  4. cevvāyp pāciṉaṅ kaṭiyar koṭicci
  5. yavvāyt taṭṭiyo ṭavaṇi yākeṉa ((5))
  6. vēyaṇmaṉ yāyu nunti vāḻiya
  7. rammā mēṉi niritoṭik kuṟumakaḷ
  8. cellā yōniṉ muḷḷeyi ṟuṇkeṉa
  9. melliya viṉiya kūrali ṉiyāṉaḵ[FNote_429]
  10. tollēṉ pōla vuriyā ṭuvalē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Does this say, live, friend,
  • that something sweeter than sweet does happen to us?
  • Your mother, who doesn't resemble herself,
  • saying "to chase away the red-beaked green group from the little millet
  • inside the mountins, where the sign has been made by [our] lover,
  • creeper-gir{X}l[FNote_431]{/X}, be at that place with your pretty-mouthed Taṭṭi"
  • and your father may live,[FNote_432]
  • saying "little dughter with armlet in a row [and] pretty dark body,
  • please go. Let me eat your thorn teeth![FNote_433]"
  • While talking softly [and] pleasantly I
  • played telling as if I were not content with that.

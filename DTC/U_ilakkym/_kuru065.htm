{{Readable Translation}}

  1. So that the Irali deer, that drank the water clear on hard pebbles,
  2. bounces about full of joy with his mate,
  3. the riny season itself has come, cool [and] dripping,
  4. to say: have you stayed[FNote_274] full of sorrow [and] affliction,
  5. having longed for the coming of him who is staying away[FNote_275]?

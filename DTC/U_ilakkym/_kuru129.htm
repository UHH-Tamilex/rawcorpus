{{Readable Translation}}

  1. You [strong like a] bear, companion [prone] to be confused by small [foreheads/women][FNote_522],
  2. friend of scholars(?), listen:
  3. The small forehead close to the hair,
  4. appearing like the fresh white moon
  5. on the eighth day over the black sea,
  6. has actually fettered us like a newly caught elephant.

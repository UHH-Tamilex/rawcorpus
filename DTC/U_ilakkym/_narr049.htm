{{Readable Translation}}

  • The ghat became lonely because those with armlets have left(?)
  • the many-coloured dunes that have ... the coming waves.
  • Midday passed by in chasing away the birds falling
  • into the images of Iṟā fish drawn with last strength.
  • What, friend, if it were known that we have gone(?)[FNote_191]
  • saying 'we have enough(?)' to our people who have stayed(?)[FNote_192],
  • having left off the hunt, full of pleasure for the sword fish they had thorwn up
  • - to the thriving little good village of the man from the coast of the clear sea,
  • where the fragrant blossoms on the big branches of the Puṉṉai tree on the village common emit fragrance together with the Tāḻai tree in [his] frontyard?

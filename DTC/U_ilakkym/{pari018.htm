Paripāṭal 18

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 18-1} pōr etirntu ēṟṟār matuki matam tapa
  • {PARI 18-2} kār etirntu ēṟṟa kamam cūl eḻili pōl
  • {PARI 18-3} nīr nirantu ēṟṟa nilam tāṅku aḻuvattu
  • {PARI 18-4} cūr nirantu cuṟṟiya mā taputta vēlōy niṉ
  • {PARI 18-5} cīr nirantu ēntiya kuṉṟoṭu nēr nirantu
  • {PARI 18-6} ēṟumāṟu ēṟkum i kuṉṟu
  • {PARI 18-7} oḷ oḷi maṇi poṟi āl maññi nōkki taṉ
  • {PARI 18-8} uḷḷattu niṉippāṉi kaṇṭaṉaḷ tiru nutal um
  • {PARI 18-9} uḷḷiyatu uṇarntēṉ aḵtu uri iṉi nī emmi
  • {PARI 18-10} eḷḷutal maṟittal ōmpu eṉpāḷi peyarttu avaṉ
  • {PARI 18-11} kātalāy niṉ iyal kaḷavu eṇṇi kaḷi makiḻ
  • {PARI 18-12} pētuṟṟa itaṉi kaṇṭu yāṉ nōkka nī emmi
  • {PARI 18-13} ētilā nōkkuti eṉṟu āṅku uṇarppittal
  • {PARI 18-14} āy tērāṉ kuṉṟa iyalpu
  • {PARI 18-15} ai vaḷam pūtta aṇi tikaḻ kuṉṟiṉ mēl
  • {PARI 18-16} mi vaḷam pūtta malar ēr maḻi kaṇṇār
  • {PARI 18-17} ki vaḷam pūtta vaṭuvoṭu kāṇāy nī
  • {PARI 18-18} moy vaḷam pūtta muyakkam yām kippaṭuttēm
  • {PARI 18-19} mey vaḷam pūtta viḻitaku poṉ aṇi
  • {PARI 18-20} ni vaḷi pūtta narampu iyi cīr poy vaḷam
  • {PARI 18-21} pūttaṉa pāṇā niṉ pāṭṭu
  • {PARI 18-22} taṇ taḷir taruppaṭuttu eṭuttu urii
  • {PARI 18-23} maṅkul maḻi muḻaṅkiya viṟal variyāl
  • {PARI 18-24} kaṇ porupu cuṭarntu aṭarntu iṭantu
  • {PARI 18-25} iruḷ pōḻum koṭi miṉṉāl
  • {PARI 18-26} veḷ cuṭar vēl vēḷ viri mayil mēl ñāyiṟu niṉ
  • {PARI 18-27} oḷ cuṭar ōṭi kaḷiṟu ēykkum niṉ kuṉṟattu
  • {PARI 18-28} eḻutu eḻil ampalam kāmavēḷ ampiṉ
  • {PARI 18-29} toḻil vīṟṟirunta nakar
  • {PARI 18-30} ār tatumpum ayil ampu niṟi nāḻi
  • {PARI 18-31} cūr tatumpu variya kā āl
  • {PARI 18-32} kār tatumpu nīr tatumpuvaṉa cuṉi
  • {PARI 18-33} ēr tatumpuvaṉa pū aṇi ceṟivu
  • {PARI 18-34} pōr tōṟṟu kaṭṭuṇṭār ki pōlva kār tōṟṟum
  • {PARI 18-35} kāntaḷ ceṟinta kaviṉ
  • {PARI 18-36} kaviṉ muki kaṭṭu aviḻppa tumpi kaṭṭu yāḻiṉ
  • {PARI 18-37} puri nekiḻppār pōṉṟaṉa ki
  • {PARI 18-38} acciram kāl ārttu aṇi maḻi kōliṉṟu ē
  • {PARI 18-39} vaccirattāṉ vāṉa villu
  • {PARI 18-40} villu cori pakaḻiyiṉ mel malar tāyiṉa
  • {PARI 18-41} vallu pōr vallāy mali mēl maram
  • {PARI 18-42} vaṭṭu uruṭṭu vallāy maliya neṭṭuruṭṭu
  • {PARI 18-43} cīr tatumpum aravam uṭaṉ ciṟantu
  • {PARI 18-44} pōr tatumpum aravam pōla
  • {PARI 18-45} karuvi ārppa karuvi niṉṟaṉa kuṉṟam
  • {PARI 18-46} aruvi ārppa muttu aṇintaṉa vari
  • {PARI 18-47} kuruvi ārppa kural kuvintaṉa tiṉi
  • {PARI 18-48} eruvi kōppa eḻil aṇi tiruvil
  • {PARI 18-49} vāṉil aṇitta vari ūtum pal malarāl
  • {PARI 18-50} kūṉi vaḷitta cuṉi
  • {PARI 18-51} puri uṟu narampu um iyal um puṇarntu
  • {PARI 18-52} curuti um pū um cuṭar um kūṭi
  • {PARI 18-53} eri uruku akilōṭu āram um kamaḻum
  • {PARI 18-54} ceru vēl tāṉi celva niṉ aṭiyuṟi
  • {PARI 18-55} uritiṉiṉ uṟi pati cērntu āṅku
  • {PARI 18-56} piriyātu irukka em cuṟṟamōṭu uṭaṉ ē

Paripāṭal 18

((Originally extracted from master file ^PARI.OCP))
((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {PARI 18-1} pōr etirntu ēṟṟār matukai matam tapa
  • {PARI 18-2} kār etirntu ēṟṟa kamam cūl eḻili pōl
  • {PARI 18-3} nīr nirantu ēṟṟa nilam tāṅku aḻuvattu
  • {PARI 18-4} cūr nirantu cuṟṟiya mā taputta vēlōy niṉ
  • {PARI 18-5} cīr nirantu ēntiya kuṉṟoṭu nēr nirantu
  • {PARI 18-6} ēṟumāṟu ēṟkum i kuṉṟu
  • {PARI 18-7} oḷ oḷi maṇi poṟi āl maññai nōkki taṉ
  • {PARI 18-8} uḷḷattu niṉaippāṉai kaṇṭaṉaḷ tiru nutal um
  • {PARI 18-9} uḷḷiyatu uṇarntēṉ aḵtu urai iṉi nī emmai
  • {PARI 18-10} eḷḷutal maṟaittal ōmpu eṉpāḷai peyarttu avaṉ
  • {PARI 18-11} kātalāy niṉ iyal kaḷavu eṇṇi kaḷi makiḻ
  • {PARI 18-12} pētuṟṟa itaṉai kaṇṭu yāṉ nōkka nī emmai
  • {PARI 18-13} ētilā nōkkuti eṉṟu āṅku uṇarppittal
  • {PARI 18-14} āy tērāṉ kuṉṟa iyalpu
  • {PARI 18-15} ai vaḷam pūtta aṇi tikaḻ kuṉṟiṉ mēl
  • {PARI 18-16} mai vaḷam pūtta malar ēr maḻai kaṇṇār
  • {PARI 18-17} kai vaḷam pūtta vaṭuvoṭu kāṇāy nī
  • {PARI 18-18} moy vaḷam pūtta muyakkam yām kaippaṭuttēm
  • {PARI 18-19} mey vaḷam pūtta viḻaitaku poṉ aṇi
  • {PARI 18-20} nai vaḷai pūtta narampu iyai cīr poy vaḷam
  • {PARI 18-21} pūttaṉa pāṇā niṉ pāṭṭu
  • {PARI 18-22} taṇ taḷir taruppaṭuttu eṭuttu uraii
  • {PARI 18-23} maṅkul maḻai muḻaṅkiya viṟal varaiyāl
  • {PARI 18-24} kaṇ porupu cuṭarntu aṭarntu iṭantu
  • {PARI 18-25} iruḷ pōḻum koṭi miṉṉāl
  • {PARI 18-26} veḷ cuṭar vēl vēḷ virai mayil mēl ñāyiṟu niṉ
  • {PARI 18-27} oḷ cuṭar ōṭai kaḷiṟu ēykkum niṉ kuṉṟattu
  • {PARI 18-28} eḻutu eḻil ampalam kāmavēḷ ampiṉ
  • {PARI 18-29} toḻil vīṟṟirunta nakar
  • {PARI 18-30} ār tatumpum ayil ampu niṟai nāḻi
  • {PARI 18-31} cūr tatumpu varaiya kā āl
  • {PARI 18-32} kār tatumpu nīr tatumpuvaṉa cuṉai
  • {PARI 18-33} ēr tatumpuvaṉa pū aṇi ceṟivu
  • {PARI 18-34} pōr tōṟṟu kaṭṭuṇṭār kai pōlva kār tōṟṟum
  • {PARI 18-35} kāntaḷ ceṟinta kaviṉ
  • {PARI 18-36} kaviṉ mukai kaṭṭu aviḻppa tumpi kaṭṭu yāḻiṉ
  • {PARI 18-37} puri nekiḻppār pōṉṟaṉa kai
  • {PARI 18-38} acciram kāl ārttu aṇi maḻai kōliṉṟu ē
  • {PARI 18-39} vaccirattāṉ vāṉa villu
  • {PARI 18-40} villu cori pakaḻiyiṉ mel malar tāyiṉa
  • {PARI 18-41} vallu pōr vallāy malai mēl maram
  • {PARI 18-42} vaṭṭu uruṭṭu vallāy malaiya neṭṭuruṭṭu
  • {PARI 18-43} cīr tatumpum aravam uṭaṉ ciṟantu
  • {PARI 18-44} pōr tatumpum aravam pōla
  • {PARI 18-45} karuvi ārppa karuvi niṉṟaṉa kuṉṟam
  • {PARI 18-46} aruvi ārppa muttu aṇintaṉa varai
  • {PARI 18-47} kuruvi ārppa kural kuvintaṉa tiṉai
  • {PARI 18-48} eruvai kōppa eḻil aṇi tiruvil
  • {PARI 18-49} vāṉil aṇitta vari ūtum pal malarāl
  • {PARI 18-50} kūṉi vaḷaitta cuṉai
  • {PARI 18-51} puri uṟu narampu um iyal um puṇarntu
  • {PARI 18-52} curuti um pū um cuṭar um kūṭi
  • {PARI 18-53} eri uruku akilōṭu āram um kamaḻum
  • {PARI 18-54} ceru vēl tāṉai celva niṉ aṭiyuṟai
  • {PARI 18-55} uritiṉiṉ uṟai pati cērntu āṅku
  • {PARI 18-56} piriyātu irukka em cuṟṟamōṭu uṭaṉ ē

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. [To and from] the front-yard of the little home situated by the seashore grove,
  2. where, spread out like the quills [which] a heron smoothes, the mass
  3. of ripened rich buds open on the Tāḻi trees with hanging aerial roots,
  4. the waves are coming [and] going[FNote_927], [so] they say,
  5. to the land of the cool sea of him who is close to [our] heart,
  6. even if he has abandoned us [and is] in a far away land[FNote_928].[FNote_929]

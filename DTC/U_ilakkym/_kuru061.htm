{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Like boys who have pleasure pulling with the hand
  2. the little horse-cart made by the carpenter,
  3. even if they can't have the pleasure of mounting [it],
  4. we had the pleasure of having made friendship with the man from a pond village
  5. with good chariot, even if we don't have the pleasure we had[FNote_254].
  6. [Our] bangles have stayed tight.

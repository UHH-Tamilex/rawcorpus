{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Now, what [is] this, friend?
  2. At the time [I] reached the meeting point[FNote_1228]
  3. in the shade of the dunes, by the Puṉṉi tree, clusters opening,
  4. in the seashore grove buzzing with birds, where the waves of the old water are jostling,
  5. our eyes indeed saw the man from the sea,
  6. our ears indeed heard his words.
  7. Now if [he] unites with my broad, soft shoulders,
  8. they reach glorious integrity; if he departs, they slacken.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from a land, where the she-monkey that has taken
  • the big ears ripening first on the fresh millet on the mountin-side,
  • which is guarded by the creeper-woman, climbing the good mountin
  • with its unlearned male [and] pressing [the ear] out for herself(?)[FNote_92]
  • so that its palm is filled [and] gulping [it down] for [its] wrinkled warm curved
  • cheek to become full, appears like an ascetics sitting food in hand[FNote_93],
  • backs become wet by the rining from the sky,
  • - he came, oh friend,
  • like rin pouring at deepest midnight
  • on the Nelli tree that had withered with [its] shoots,
  • in a time when the green subsides, [water?] having subsided in the ponds of the world.

KT 26 Veḷḷivītiyār: the friend [SHE]

{{kiḷavi}}: (Stood up to duty by the friend after she heard say another god (i.e. not the lover) was the cause of HER(t.) changes at the time the divination came to pass [and] the soothsayer was asked 'what has this come from', after the actual mother and the foster-mother had seen the changes in HER(t.).){{/kiḷavi}}

{{Readable Translation}}

  • What do we do, friend?
  • I see they seem [to become] small [and] very rare,
  • the intimacies, enjoyable [and] greatly desired,
  • of abiding in the vast fields with the man from the land of big stones,
  • on the slope fragrant with honey high with gold-blossomed Vēṅki trees,
  • scaring away the red-beaked green parakeets, playing
  • there in the waterfall on the big mountin side,
  • smearing [on his? on our chest?] for bees to come the sandal from the slope.
  • Being as if the broad-waved sea had retreated,
  • the ears of the millet have taken [their] dry state.

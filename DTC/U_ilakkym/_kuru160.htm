{{Readable Translation}}

  1. [Is] this, friend, the marriage to our lover,
  2. who has not come even with the great, cool north wind
  3. at deepest midnight, where the Aṉṟil bird, [its] head red like fire,{X} {/X}
  4. with[FNote_642] its female, [its] beak bent like a prawn,
  5. calls out, such that activity ends
  6. in those who are separated, in the nest on a twig high in the tree?

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. So exhusted [am] I that shining bangles become loose.
  2. I am [still], o friend. On the slope,[FNote_503]
  3. among all the women with leaf-adorned hips,
  4. at the festival, my integrity, which excelled.
  5. A glance has been exchanged with the man from the cool ghat,
  6. where the suffering heron(?) whose wings have failed in strength [becuse of]
  7. an old victory(?)[FNote_504] is perched on a twig bowed to touch the waves.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • O you with a shoulder that had the beauty of bamboo,
  • excellent, while it is destroyed by pain and emaciation[FNote_296],
  • will you come to me, o creeper-woman of good gait?
  • I am unable to look [at you], while your beautiful eyes shine
  • as [those] of Vaḷḷi who moved forward united with Murukaṉ.
  • - to [my] little hamlet, where the forest people take away
  • the plunder taken by the killing dog,
  • having destroyed [their] lashes, that swarmed out[FNote_297],
  • when the greatly angered male of the small-eyed pig was caught
  • in the empty trap when going for water, [its] dark back playing in the mud,
  • by the crossroad difficult for going, with large[FNote_298] Nākam trees.

{{Readable Translation}}

  1. The water-lily with petals in row, [its] foot tied to the water,
  2. even if the west wind presses [it], doesn't fade,
  3. [and][FNote_1539], if [she] comes with you, even the forests will be pleasing,[FNote_1540]
  4. where elephants are suffering, hands folded[FNote_1541],
  5. from the non-existence of [their] former [strength], which split dry twigs,
  6. standing[FNote_1542] in row - like a mass of salt-merchants' ox-carts,
  7. struck[FNote_1543] by languor [and] fastened as if in a sling[FNote_1544].[FNote_1545]

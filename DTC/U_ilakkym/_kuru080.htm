{{Readable Translation}}

  1. Whole calyxes of water-lily put in [our] tresses,
  2. desirous of the dark ghat where the great flood has come
  3. we go to immerse ourselves there.
  4. If she is possessed by fear of that
  5. she may guard her husband, together with [her] relatives too,
  6. like many-speared Eḻiṉi, with the big rows on the [battle-]front,
  7. who conquers [enemies] in hot battle to bear [his] yoke.

5+6b {--!}just as many-speared Eḻiṉi who conquers ... [guards] a big herd of cows on the [battle-]front.

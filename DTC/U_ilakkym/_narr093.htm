{{Readable Translation}}

  • O man from the land of big stones, where those separated are aggrieved:
  • "when the honeycombs are hanging, when big fruits are clustering,
  • when white waterfalls descend from the mountain in the evening,
  • when all the grains enter the fields daily,
  • abundance has, alas, this mountainous region."[FNote_321],
  • - we'll go, rise - may your lifetime be excellent.
  • It is difficult[FNote_322] to obtain an equivalent to [her] life
  • before listening to the fur-eyed drum
  • with resounding tones that possess the blackness of distress too
  • that was suffered, for [her] shame to be killed, by the ornament-hung bosom
  • of the little woman of tender nature, with narrow(?)[FNote_323] waist,
  • bamboo shoulders [and] perfect jewels who is hidden on the side.

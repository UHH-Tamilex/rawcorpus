{{Readable Translation}}

  1. What if you spoke, friend,
  2. that you longed[FNote_1345] to end the difficult affliction, swum by pain,
  3. on the end day of the few rain, the north wind come,
  4. - to the master of the mountain, situated by the village common(?)[FNote_1346],
  5. where the broad-trunked elephant, having embraced the youthful female with fragrant breath(?)[FNote_1347], descends upon the little homes on the hills?

Kalittoki

((Last updated on 2004 April 14th))

  • {KALI 7-1} vēṉil uḻanta vaṟitu uyaṅku ōy kaḷiṟu
  • {KALI 7-2} vāṉ nīṅku vippiṉ vaḻaṅkā tēr nīrkku avāam
  • {KALI 7-3} kāṉam kaṭattir eṉa kēṭpiṉ yāṉ oṉṟu
  • {KALI 7-4} ucāvuku ō aiya ciṟitu
  • {KALI 7-5} nī ē cey viṉi maruṅkil celavu ayarntu yāḻa niṉ
  • {KALI 7-6} ki puṉi val vil ñāṇ uḷartī ē
  • {KALI 7-7} ivaṭku ē ceyvu uṟu maṇṭilam miyāppatu pōl
  • {KALI 7-8} mi il vāḷ mukam pacappu ūrum ē
  • {KALI 7-9} nī ē viṉi māṇ kāḻakam vīṅka kaṭṭi
  • {KALI 7-10} puṉi māṇ marīiya ampu teriti ē
  • {KALI 7-11} ivaṭku ē cuṉi māṇ nīlam kār etirpavi pōl
  • {KALI 7-12} iṉi nōkku uṇ kaṇ nīr nillā ē
  • {KALI 7-13} nī ē pulampu il uḷḷamoṭu poruḷ vayiṉ celīiya
  • {KALI 7-14} valam paṭu tikiri vāy nīvuti ē
  • {KALI 7-15} ivaṭku ē alaṅku itaḻ kōṭal vī ukupavi pōl
  • {KALI 7-16} ilaṅku ēr el vaḷi iṟi ūrum ē
  • {KALI 7-17} eṉa niṉ
  • {KALI 7-18} cel navi aravattu um iṉiyavaḷ nī nīppiṉ
  • {KALI 7-19} taṉ nalam kaṭikoḷappaṭutaliṉ maṟṟu ivaḷ
  • {KALI 7-20} iṉ uyir tarutal um āṟṟum ō
  • {KALI 7-21} muṉṉiya tēettu muyaṉṟu cey poruḷ ē

  • {PU 288-2} aṇṇal nal ēṟu iraṇṭu uṭaṉ maṭuttu
  • {PU 288-3} veṉṟataṉ paccai cīvātu pōrtta
  • {PU 288-4} tiṇ piṇi muracam iṭai pulattu iraṅka
  • {PU 288-5} ār amar mayaṅkiya ñāṭpiṉ teṟuvara
  • {PU 288-6} neṭu vēl pāynta nāṇ uṭai neñcattu
  • {PU 288-7} arukukai maṉṟa
  • {PU 288-8} kurutiyoṭu tuyalvaru mārpiṉ
  • {PU 288-9} muyakku iṭai īyātu moyttaṉa paruntu ē

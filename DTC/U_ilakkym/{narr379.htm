  • {NT 379-9} peyal uṟu nīlam pōṉṟaṉa viral ē
  • {NT 379-10} pāay a vayiṟu alaittaliṉ āṉātu
  • {NT 379-11} āṭu maḻai tavaḻum kōṭu uyar potiyil
  • {NT 379-12} ōṅku irum cilampil pūtta
  • {NT 379-13} kāntaḷ am koḻu mukai pōṉṟaṉa civantu ē

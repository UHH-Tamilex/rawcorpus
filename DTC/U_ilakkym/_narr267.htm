  4. ruṇaṅkutiṉi tuḻavuṅ kipōṉ ñāḻaṉ

  6. ṟaṉṉoṭu puṇartta viṉṉamar kāṉaṟ
  7. ṟaṉiyē varuta ṉaṉipulam puṭitteṉa
  8. vārēṉ maṉyāṉ vantaṉeṉ ṟeyya
  9. ciṟunā voṇmaṇit teḷḷici kaṭuppa
  10. viṉamī ṉārki yīṇṭupuḷ ḷolikkura ((10))
  11. livimaka ṉeṉṉā vaḷavi
  12. vayamāṉ ṟōṉṟal vantuniṉ ṟaṉṉē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Coming all alone
  • to the seashore-grove where it is pleasing to reside, where was united to her
  • the man from the ghat, where the multitude of big relatives of the crabs
  • in the dunes, their eyes as the dark buds of the Nocci-tree,
  • are drawing lines among the scented blossoms emitting fragrance
  • of the Ñāḻal tree, as the hands stirring the drying millet
  • of women with beautiful sweet smile [and] shining teeth,
  • is very lonely."
  • - I won't come indeed. [Yet] I came.
  • Before [even] saying "these, the sounding voices
  • of birds gathering to feed on various fish, so like the clear sound
  • of the small tongued bright bells, [are] his[FNote_837]",
  • he came, [his] strong horses appearing] [and] stood there.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • "Even if he has gone
  • in a time of forgetfullness, the world suffering much,
  • while the earth splits from the scorching of the one who is rich in his(?)[FNote_535] red
  • beams on the woodland that lay abandoned by rin,
  • he has done a good thing",
  • even when [you] sid [so] in order to make it clear[FNote_536], you did not say it so as to be clear.[FNote_537]
  • - becuse [you?] don't remember that he has no obstacle
  • on the road, where,
  • since the men with bent bows [and] red-stemmed arrows
  • took away the constituents of life of travelling people,
  • the hungry short jackal, enveloped by the stench coming from the body [covered in] leaves in the hot jungle, doesn't move [its] short legs
  • [and] turns [its] back on it.

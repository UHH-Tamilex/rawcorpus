{{Metrical text}}

  1. irumpuṉiṟ ṟerumaip peruñcevik kuḻavi
  2. paintā teruviṉ vaikutuyiṉ maṭiyuñ
  3. ceḻuntaṇ maṉaiyō ṭemmiva ṇoḻiyac
  4. celperuṅ kāḷai poymaruṇṭu cēynāṭṭuc
  5. cuvaikkāy nellip pōkkarum poṅkar ((5))
  6. vīḻkaṭait tiraḷkā yoruṅkuṭaṉ ṟiṉṟu
  7. vīcuṉaic ciṟunīr kuṭiyiṉaḷ kaḻinta
  8. kuvaḷai yuṇkaṇeṉ makaḷō raṉṉa
  9. ceypōḻ veṭṭip peyta lāya ceypāḻ paṭuppa veytuyirt tayarun
  10. mālaiviri nilaviṟ peyarpupuṟaṅ kāṇṭaṟku 10
  11. māyirun tāḻi kavippat
  12. tāviṉṟu kaḻikaveṟ koḷḷāk kūṟṟē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • May Yama who didn't take me pass away without strength,
  • to be buried by a big large pot[FNote_851],
  • for [my] seeing the backs, moving in the moonlight spreading at evening,
  • of the [girl] companions[FNote_852] cutting [and] wearing of red(?)[FNote_853] palmyra leaves
  • just as [my] daughter with collyirium eyes [as] blue water-lilies,
  • who passed away: she drank the little water from the subsiding mountain spring,
  • having eaten together the globular unripe fruits within the area of falling
  • in the grove difficult to approach, from the Nelli tree with tasty unripe fruits,
  • in a distant land, bewitched by the lies of the [fast-?]going big young man,
  • so that we[FNote_854] stay behind here with the prosperous cool house,
  • where the large-eared calf of the buffalo, newly born [and] dark,
  • drowses into sleep, being kept in the dung [spread] with fresh pollen.

{{Metrical text}}

  1. aruntuya ruḻattali ṉuṇmai cāṉmeṉap
  2. perumpiṟi tiṉmaiyi ṉilēṉu mallēṉ
  3. karaiporu tiḻitaruṅ kāṉyāṟ ṟikukarai
  4. vērkilar māat tantaḷir pōla
  5. naṭuṅka lāṉā neñcamō ṭiṭumpai ((5))
  6. yāṅkaṉan tāṅkuveṉ maṟṟē yōṅkucelaṟ
  7. kaṭumpakaṭ ṭiyāṉai neṭumā ṉañci
  8. yīra neñcamō ticaicēṇ viḷaṅkat
  9. tērvī cirukkai pōla
  10. māri yirīi māṉṟaṉṟāṉ maḻaiyē. ((10))

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Saying[FNote_1050] "be worthy of the existence of bearing difficult misery!"
  • I stand not for the non-existence of the great other.[FNote_1051]
  • How will I endure
  • the distress, with a heart shivering unendingly,
  • as the pretty sprout of the mangotree the roots of which emerge
  • on the breaking shore of the forest river that descends beating the shore?
  • The rain has been profuse indeed, creating showers
  • as [liberal] as the chariot-giving court(?),
  • while [his] fame shines wide, with an affected heart,
  • of Añci with quick male elephants of high going [and] big horse.

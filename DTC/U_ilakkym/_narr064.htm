{{Metrical text}}

  1. eṉṉa rāyiṉu miṉiniṉai voḻika
  2. vaṉṉa vāka vuraiyaṟ ṟōḻiyā
  3. miṉṉa mākat tuṟantōr naṭpeṉa
  4. maraṉā ruṭukkai malaiyuṟai kuṟava
  5. raṟiyā taṟutta ciṟiyilaic cāntam ((5))
  6. vaṟaṉuṟ ṟāra murukkip paiyeṉa
  7. maramvaṟi tākac cōrntuk kāṅke
  8. ṉaṟivu muḷḷamu mavarvayiṟ ceṉṟeṉa
  9. vaṟitā likuḷaiyeṉ yākkai yiṉiyavar
  10. variṉu nōymarun tallar vārā ((10))
  11. tavaṇa rākuka kātala rivaṇaṅ
  12. kāmam paṭaraṭa varuntiya
  13. nōymali varuttaṅ kāṇaṉmā remarē.

{{Readable Translation}}

  • Even if he is of whatever kind[FNote_239], let the thought stay behind now.
  • Since things are thus, don't talk friend. [Thinking]
  • "he is one that has abandoned [our] intimacy, for us to become thus",
  • my body is empty in fact,
  • because knowledge and mind have gone to him,
  • as a sandal tree with small leaves, that have been cut off unknowingly
  • by the mountain-dwelling hill people, with [their] garments of hemp fibre,
  • is fully ruined by draught, languishes [and] sheds [its foliage],
  • so that the tree slowly becomes empty. Now, even if
  • he comes, he is not a remedy for the ill.
  • Let him be there without coming, [our] lover.
  • Let our [man] not see the suffering increased by pain
  • suffered for affliction to kill our desire, here.

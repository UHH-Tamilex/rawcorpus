{{Readable Translation}}

  • The man from the coast of overflowing sands is coming, friend,
  • in order to praise the goodness of [our] beauty-spotted hips
  • that glittered with big decorations in the wide place of playing in the sea,
  • for [his] youths being noisy, who travelled to achieve a name(?),
  • when also the bell of the long chariot with seizing motion sounds.
  • Come, let us hide behind the big trunk
  • of the drum-based Puṉṉai tree, bent to touch the house(?).
  • Let us see a little of the severe affliction of unhappiness
  • in him who does not see us in the grove fragrant with soft clusters,
  • come to the sign of uniting in the seashore grove pread with flowers,
  • at midday.

  • {PATI 80-1} vāl maruppiṉ kaḷiṟṟu yāṉai
  • {PATI 80-2} mā malaiyiṉ kaṇam koṇṭu avar
  • {PATI 80-3} eṭuttu eṟinta viṟal muracam
  • {PATI 80-4} kār maḻaiyiṉ kaṭitu muḻaṅka
  • {PATI 80-5} cāntu pularnta viyal mārpiṉ
  • {PATI 80-6} toṭi cuṭar varum vali muṉkai
  • {PATI 80-7} puṇ uṭai eṟuḻ tōḷ puṭaiyal am kaḻal kāl
  • {PATI 80-8} piṟakku aṭi otuṅkā pūṭkai oḷ vāḷ
  • {PATI 80-9} oṭivu il tevvar etir niṉṟu uraii
  • {PATI 80-10} iṭuka tiṟai ē puravu etirntōṟku eṉa
  • {PATI 80-11} ampu uṭai valattar uyarntōr parava
  • {PATI 80-12} aṉaiyai ākaṉ māṟu ē pakaivar
  • {PATI 80-13} kāl kiḷarntaṉṉa kataḻ pari puravi
  • {PATI 80-14} kaṭum pari neṭum tēr mīmicai nuṭaṅku koṭi
  • {PATI 80-15} pula varai tōṉṟal yāvatu ciṉa pōr
  • {PATI 80-16} nila varai niṟīiya nal icai
  • {PATI 80-17} tolaiyā kaṟpa niṉ temmuṉaiyāṉ ē

{C URAI} |ivvōttu |eṉṉa peyarttō |eṉiṉ, |uvamaiyiyal |eṉṉum peyarttu |orupuṭai |oppumaipaṟṟi yuvamai |uṇarttiṉamaiyāṟ peṟṟa peyar. meyppāṭu paṟṟit tōṉṟi vaḻaṅkuvatu.

|itaṉāṟ payaṉ |eṉṉai matippatōveṉiṉ, pulaṉ |allātaṉapulaṉātalum |alaṅkāramākik kēṭṭārkkiṉpam payattalum. |āp pōlum |āmā |eṉa vuṇarttiyavaḻi, |ataṉaik kāṭṭakattuk kaṇṭāṉ muṉ kēṭṭa |oppumaipaṟṟi |iḵtu |āmāveṉṟu |aṟiyum. ``tāmarai pōl vāḷmukattut taiyalīr'' |eṉṟavaḻi |alaṅkāramākik kēṭṭārkku |iṉpam payakkum. |aḵtāvatu mēṟcollappaṭṭa |eḻutiṇaiyiṉum yātaṉuḷ |aṭaṅkum |eṉiṉ, |avaiyellāvaṟṟiṟkum potuvākip perumpāṉmaiyum |akapporuḷ paṟṟi varum.

mēṟ kuṟippup paṟṟi varum meyppāṭu kūṟiṉār; |itu paṇpuntoḻilum paṟṟi varutaliṉ |ataṉpiṉ kūṟappaṭṭatu.

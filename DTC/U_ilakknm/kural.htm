((atikāram 1: kaṭavuḷ vāḻttu))
Kuṟaḷ_001 [comm.Pari_001]
akara mutala veḻuttellā māti
pakavaṉ mutaṟṟē yulaku.
Kuṟaḷ_002 [comm.Pari_002]
kaṟṟataṉā lāya payaṉeṉkol vālaṟiva
ṉaṟṟā ṭoḻāa reṉiṉ.
Kuṟaḷ_003 [comm.Pari_003]
malarmici yēkiṉāṉ māṇaṭi cērntār
nilamici nīṭuvāḻ vār.
Kuṟaḷ_004 [comm.Pari_004]
vēṇṭutalvēṇ ṭāmi yilāṉaṭi cērntārk
kiyāṇṭu miṭumpi yila.
Kuṟaḷ_005 [comm.Pari_005]
iruḷcē riruviṉiyuñ cērā viṟivaṉ
poruḷcēr pukaḻpurintār māṭṭu.
Kuṟaḷ_006 [comm.Pari_006]
poṟivāyi lintavittāṉ poytī roḻukka
neṟiniṉṟār nīṭuvāḻ vār.
Kuṟaḷ_007 [comm.Pari_007]
taṉakkuvami yillātāṉ ṟāḷcērntārk kallāṉ
maṉakkavali māṟṟa laritu.
Kuṟaḷ_008 [comm.Pari_008]
aṟavāḻi yantaṇaṉ ṟāḷcērntārk kallāṟ
piṟavāḻi nīnta laritu.
Kuṟaḷ_009 [comm.Pari_009]
kōḷil poṟiyiṟ kuṇamilavē yeṇkuṇattāṉ
ṟāḷi vaṇaṅkāt tali.
Kuṟaḷ_010 [comm.Pari_010]
piṟavip peruṅkaṭa ṉīntuvar nīntā
riṟiva ṉaṭicērā tār

((atikāram 2: vāṉciṟappu))
Kuṟaḷ_011 [comm.Pari_011]
vāṉiṉ ṟulakam vaḻaṅki varutalāṟ
ṟāṉamiḻta meṉṟuṇaraṟ pāṟṟu
Kuṟaḷ_012 [comm.Pari_012]
tuppārkkut tuppāya tuppākkit tuppārkkut
tuppāya tūu maḻi.
Kuṟaḷ_013 [comm.Pari_013]
viṇṇiṉṟu poyppiṉ virinīr viyaṉulakat
tuṇṇiṉ ṟuṭaṟṟum paci.
Kuṟaḷ_014 [comm.Pari_014]
ēri ṉuḻāa ruḻavar puyaleṉṉum
vāri vaḷaṅkuṉṟik kāl.
Kuṟaḷ_015 [comm.Pari_015]
keṭuppatūuṅ keṭṭārkkuc cārvāymaṟ ṟāṅkē
eṭuppatūu mellā maḻi.
Kuṟaḷ_016 [comm.Pari_016]
vicumpiṟ ṟuḷivīḻi ṉallāṉmaṟ ṟāṅkē
pacumpuṟ ṟalikāṇ paritu.
Kuṟaḷ_017 [comm.Pari_017]
neṭuṅkaṭalun taṉṉīrmi kuṉṟun taṭinteḻili
tāṉalkā tāki viṭiṉ.
Kuṟaḷ_018 [comm.Pari_018]
ciṟappoṭu pūcaṉi cellātu vāṉam
vaṟakkumēl vāṉōrkku mīṇṭu.
Kuṟaḷ_019 [comm.Pari_019]
tāṉan tavamiraṇṭun taṅkā viyaṉulakam
vāṉam vaḻaṅkā teṉiṉ.
Kuṟaḷ_020 [comm.Pari_020]
nīriṉ ṟamiyā tulakeṉiṉ yāryārkkum
vāṉiṉ ṟamiyā toḻukku.

((atikāram 3: nīttārperumi))
Kuṟaḷ_021 [comm.Pari_021]
oḻukkattu nīttār perumi viḻuppattu
vēṇṭum paṉuvaṟ ṟuṇivu.
Kuṟaḷ_022 [comm.Pari_022]
tuṟantār perumi tuṇikkūṟiṉ viyat
tiṟantāri yeṇṇikkoṇ ṭaṟṟu.
Kuṟaḷ_023 [comm.Pari_023]
irumi vakiterin tīṇṭaṟam pūṇṭār
perumi piṟaṅkiṟ ṟulaku
Kuṟaḷ_024 [comm.Pari_024]
uraṉeṉṉun tōṭṭiyā ṉōrintuṅ kāppāṉ
varaṉeṉṉum vippiṟkōr vittu.
Kuṟaḷ_025 [comm.Pari_025]
aintavittā ṉāṟṟa lakalvicumpu ḷārkōmā
ṉintiraṉē cāluṅ kari.
Kuṟaḷ_026 [comm.Pari_026]
ceyaṟkariya ceyvār periyar ciṟiyar
ceyaṟkariya ceykalā tār
Kuṟaḷ_027 [comm.Pari_027]
cuviyoḷi yūṟōci nāṟṟameṉ ṟintiṉ
vakiterivāṉ kaṭṭē yulaku.
Kuṟaḷ_028 [comm.Pari_028]
niṟimoḻi māntar perumi nilattu
maṟimoḻi kāṭṭi viṭum.
Kuṟaḷ_029 [comm.Pari_029]
kuṇameṉṉuṅ kuṉṟēṟi niṉṟār vekuḷi
kaṇamēyuṅ kātta laritu.
Kuṟaḷ_030 [comm.Pari_030]
antaṇa reṉpō raṟavōrmaṟ ṟevvuyirkkuñ
centaṇmi pūṇṭoḻuka lāṉ.

((atikāram 4: aṟaṉvaliyuṟuttal))
Kuṟaḷ_031 [comm.Pari_031]
ciṟappīṉuñ celvamu mīṉu maṟattiṉūuṅ
kākka mevaṉō vuyirkku.
Kuṟaḷ_032 [comm.Pari_032]
aṟattiṉūuṅ kākkamu milli yataṉi
maṟattali ṉūṅkilli kēṭu.
Kuṟaḷ_033 [comm.Pari_033]
ollum vakiyā ṉaṟaviṉi yōvātē
cellumvā yellāñ ceyal.
Kuṟaḷ_034 [comm.Pari_034]
maṉattukkaṇ mācila ṉāta laṉittaṟa
ṉākula nīra piṟa.
Kuṟaḷ_035 [comm.Pari_035]
aḻukkā ṟavāvekuḷi yiṉṉāccoṉ ṉāṉku
miḻukkā viyaṉṟa taṟam.
Kuṟaḷ_036 [comm.Pari_036]
aṉṟaṟivā meṉṉā taṟañceyka maṟṟatu
poṉṟuṅkāṟ poṉṟāt tuṇi.
Kuṟaḷ_037 [comm.Pari_037]
aṟattā ṟituveṉa vēṇṭā civiki
poṟuttāṉō ṭūrntā ṉiṭi.
Kuṟaḷ_038 [comm.Pari_038]
vīḻnāḷ paṭāami naṉṟāṟṟi ṉaḵtoruvaṉ
vāḻnāḷ vaḻiyaṭikkuṅ kal.
Kuṟaḷ_039 [comm.Pari_039]
aṟantāṉ varuvatē yiṉpamaṟ ṟellām
puṟatta pukaḻu mila.
Kuṟaḷ_040 [comm.Pari_040]
ceyaṟpāla tōru maṟaṉē yoruvaṟ
kuyaṟpāla tōrum paḻi.

((atikāram 5: ilvāḻkki))
Kuṟaḷ_041 [comm.Pari_041]
ilvāḻvā ṉeṉpā ṉiyalpuṭiya mūvarkku
nallāṟṟi ṉiṉṟa tuṇi.
Kuṟaḷ_042 [comm.Pari_042]
tuṟantārkkun tuvvā tavarkku miṟantārkku
milvāḻvā ṉeṉpāṉ ṟuṇi.
Kuṟaḷ_043 [comm.Pari_043]
teṉpulattār teyvam viruntokka ṟāṉeṉṟāṅ
kimpulattā ṟōmpa ṟali.
Kuṟaḷ_044 [comm.Pari_044]
paḻiyañcip pāttū ṇūṭittāyiṉ vāḻkki
vaḻiyeñca leññāṉṟu mil.
Kuṟaḷ_045 [comm.Pari_045]
aṉpu maṟaṉu muṭittāyi ṉilvāḻkki
paṇpum payaṉu matu.
Kuṟaḷ_046 [comm.Pari_046]
aṟattāṟṟi ṉilvāḻkki yāṟṟiṟ puṟattāṟṟiṟ
pōoyp peṟuva tevaṉ
Kuṟaḷ_047 [comm.Pari_047]
iyalpiṉā ṉilvāḻkki vāḻpava ṉeṉpāṉ
muyalvāru ḷellān tali.
Kuṟaḷ_048 [comm.Pari_048]
āṟṟi ṉoḻukki yaṟaṉiḻukkā vilvāḻkki
nōṟpāri ṉōṉmi yuṭittu.
Kuṟaḷ_049 [comm.Pari_049]
aṟaṉeṉap paṭṭatē yilvāḻkki yaḵtum
piṟaṉpaḻippa tillāyi ṉaṉṟu.
Kuṟaḷ_050 [comm.Pari_050]
viyattuḷ vāḻvāṅku vāḻpavaṉ vāṉuṟiyun
teyvattuḷ vikkap paṭum.

((atikāram 6: vāḻkkittuṇinalam))
Kuṟaḷ_051 [comm.Pari_051]
maṉittakka māṇpuṭiya ḷākittaṟ koṇṭāṉ
vaḷattakkāḷ vāḻkkit tuṇi.
Kuṟaḷ_052 [comm.Pari_052]
maṉimāṭci yillāḷka ṇillāyiṉ vāḻkki
yeṉimāṭcit tāyiṉu mil.
Kuṟaḷ_053 [comm.Pari_053]
illate ṉillavaṇ māṇpāṉā luḷḷate
ṉillavaṇ māṇāk kaṭi.
Kuṟaḷ_054 [comm.Pari_054]
peṇṇiṟ peruntakka yāvuḷa kaṟpeṉṉun
tiṇmiyuṇ ṭākap peṟiṉ.
Kuṟaḷ_055 [comm.Pari_055]
teyvan toḻāaḷ koḻunaṟ ṟoḻuteḻuvāḷ
peyyeṉap peyyu maḻi.
Kuṟaḷ_056 [comm.Pari_056]
taṟkāttut taṟkoṇṭāṟ pēṇit takicāṉṟa
coṟkāttuc cōrvilāḷ peṇ.
Kuṟaḷ_057 [comm.Pari_057]
ciṟikākkuṅ kāppevaṉ ceyyu makaḷir
niṟikākkuṅ kāppē tali.
Kuṟaḷ_058 [comm.Pari_058]
peṟṟāṟ peṟiṟpeṟuvar peṇṭir peruñciṟappup
puttēḷir vāḻu mulaku.
Kuṟaḷ_059 [comm.Pari_059]
pukaḻpurin tillilōrk killi yikaḻvārmuṉ
ṉēṟupōṟ pīṭu naṭi.
Kuṟaḷ_060 [comm.Pari_060]
maṅkala meṉpa maṉimāṭci maṟṟata
ṉaṉkala naṉmakkaṭ pēṟu.

((atikāram 7: putalvarip peṟutal))
Kuṟaḷ_061 [comm.Pari_061]
peṟumavaṟṟuḷ yāmaṟiva tilli yaṟivaṟinta
makkaṭpē ṟalla piṟa.
Kuṟaḷ_062 [comm.Pari_062]
eḻupiṟappun tīyavi tīṇṭā paḻippiṟaṅkāp
paṇpuṭi makkaṭ peṟiṉ.
Kuṟaḷ_063 [comm.Pari_063]
tamporu ḷeṉpatam makka ḷavarporu
ṭantam viṉiyāṉ varum.
Kuṟaḷ_064 [comm.Pari_064]
amiḻtiṉu māṟṟa viṉitētam makkaḷ
ciṟuki yaḷāviya kūḻ.
Kuṟaḷ_065 [comm.Pari_065]
makkaṇmey tīṇṭa luṭaṟkiṉpa maṟṟavar
coṟkēṭṭa liṉpañ cevikku.
Kuṟaḷ_066 [comm.Pari_066]
kuḻaliṉi tiyāḻiṉi teṉpatam makkaṇ
maḻalikcoṟ kēḷā tavar.
Kuṟaḷ_067 [comm.Pari_067]
tanti makaṟkāṟṟu naṉṟi yaviyattu
munti yiruppac ceyal.
Kuṟaḷ_068 [comm.Pari_068]
tammiṟṟam makka ḷaṟivuṭimi mānilattu
maṉṉuyirk kellā miṉitu.
Kuṟaḷ_069 [comm.Pari_069]
īṉṟa poḻutiṟ perituvarkkun taṉmakaṉic
cāṉṟō ṉeṉakkēṭṭa tāy.
Kuṟaḷ_070 [comm.Pari_070]
makaṉṟantik kāṟṟu mutavi yivaṉṟanti
yeṉṉōṟṟāṉ kolleṉuñ col.

((atikāram 8: aṉpuṭimi))
Kuṟaḷ_071 [comm.Pari_071]
aṉpiṟku muṇṭō vaṭikkuntā ḻārvalar
puṇkaṇīr pūca ṟarum
Kuṟaḷ_072 [comm.Pari_072]
aṉpilā rellān tamakkuriya raṉpuṭiyā
reṉpu muriyar piṟarkku.
Kuṟaḷ_073 [comm.Pari_073]
aṉpō ṭiyinta vaḻakkeṉpa vāruyirk
keṉpō ṭiyinta toṭarpu.
Kuṟaḷ_074 [comm.Pari_074]
aṉpīṉu mārva muṭimi yatuvīṉu
naṇpeṉṉu nāṭāc ciṟappu.
Kuṟaḷ_075 [comm.Pari_075]
aṉpuṟ ṟamarnta vaḻakkeṉpa viyakat
tiṉpuṟṟā reytuñ ciṟappu.
Kuṟaḷ_076 [comm.Pari_076]
aṟattiṟkē yaṉpucār peṉpa vaṟiyār
maṟattiṟku maḵtē tuṇi.
Kuṟaḷ_077 [comm.Pari_077]
eṉpi lataṉi veyilpōlak kāyumē
yaṉpi lataṉi yaṟam.
Kuṟaḷ_078 [comm.Pari_078]
aṉpakat tillā vuyirvāḻkki vaṉpāṟkaṇ
vaṟṟaṉ marantaḷirttaṟṟu.
Kuṟaḷ_079 [comm.Pari_079]
puṟattuṟup pellā mevaṉ ceyyum yākki
yakattuṟup paṉpi lavarkku.
Kuṟaḷ_080 [comm.Pari_080]
aṉpiṉ vaḻiya tuyirnili yaḵtilārk
keṉputōl pōrtta vuṭampu.

((atikāram 9: viruntōmpal))
Kuṟaḷ_081 [comm.Pari_081]
iruntōmpi yilvāḻva tellām viruntōmpi
vēḷāṇmi ceytaṟ poruṭṭu.
Kuṟaḷ_082 [comm.Pari_082]
viruntu puṟattatāt tāṉuṇṭal cāvā
marunteṉiṉum vēṇṭaṟpāṟ ṟaḷṟu.
Kuṟaḷ_083 [comm.Pari_083]
varuviruntu vikalu mōmpuvāṉ vāḻkki
paruvantu pāḻpaṭuta liṉṟu.
Kuṟaḷ_084 [comm.Pari_084]
akaṉamarntu ceyyā ḷuṟiyu mukaṉamarntu
nalvirun tōmpuvā ṉil.
Kuṟaḷ_085 [comm.Pari_085]
vittu miṭalvēṇṭuṅ kollō viruntōmpi
micciṉ micivāṉ pulam.
Kuṟaḷ_086 [comm.Pari_086]
celvirun tōmpi varuviruntu pārttiruppā
ṉalviruntu vāṉat tavarkku.
Kuṟaḷ_087 [comm.Pari_087]
iṉittuṇat teṉpatoṉ ṟilli viruntiṉ
ṟuṇittuṇi vēḷvip payaṉ.
Kuṟaḷ_088 [comm.Pari_088]
parittōmpip paṟṟaṟṟē meṉpar viruntōmpi
vēḷvi talippaṭā tār.
Kuṟaḷ_089 [comm.Pari_089]
uṭimiyu ḷiṉmi viruntōmpa lōmpā
maṭami maṭavārka ṇuṇṭu.
Kuṟaḷ_090 [comm.Pari_090]
mōppak kuḻiyu maṉicca mukantirintu
nōkkak kuḻiyum viruntu

((atikāram 10: iṉiyavikūṟal))
Kuṟaḷ_091 [comm.Pari_091]
iṉcollā līra maḷiip paṭiṟilavāñ
cemporuḷ kaṇṭārvāyc col.
Kuṟaḷ_092 [comm.Pari_092]
akaṉamarn tītali ṉaṉṟē mukaṉamarn
tiṉcola ṉākap peṟiṉ.
Kuṟaḷ_093 [comm.Pari_093]
mukattā ṉamarntiṉitu nōkki yakattāṉā
miṉco liṉatē yaṟam.
Kuṟaḷ_094 [comm.Pari_094]
tuṉpuṟūun tuvvāmi yillārum yārmāṭṭu
miṉpuṟūu miṉco lavarkku.
Kuṟaḷ_095 [comm.Pari_095]
paṇivuṭiya ṉiṉcola ṉāta loruvaṟ
kaṇiyalla maṟṟup piṟa.
Kuṟaḷ_096 [comm.Pari_096]
allavi tēya vaṟamperuku nallavi

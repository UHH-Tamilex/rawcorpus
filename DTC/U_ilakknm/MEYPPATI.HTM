{C URAI} |eṉpatu cūttiram.

|ic cūttiram |eṉṉutaliṟṟōveṉiṉ, piṟar vēṇṭumāṟṟāl cuvaiyuñ cuvaikkuṟippum |uṇarttutal nutaliṟṟu.

(|i-ḷ.) paṇṇait tōṉṟiya |eṉpatu-viḷaiyāṭṭāyattiṉkaṇ tōṉṟiya |eṉṟavāṟu. paṇṇaiyuṭaiyatu paṇṇai |eṉṟāyiṟṟu.

|eṇṇāṉku poruḷum kaṇṇiya puṟaṉē nāṉāṉ keṉpa |eṉpatu--muppattiraṇṭu poruḷaiyuṅ kuṟittataṉ puṟattu nikaḻum poruḷ patiṉāṟu |eṉṟu colluvar |eṉṟavāṟu.

puṟattu nikaḻvataṉaip puṟam |eṉṟār.

paṇṇait tōṉṟiya kaṇṇiya puṟaṉ |eṉap peyarecca |aṭukkākak kūṭṭuka. |aṉṟiyum, |eṇṇāṉku poruḷuṅ kaṇṇiya puṟaṉ |eṉa |orucol naṭaiyāka |oṭṭit tōṉṟiya |eṉṉum peyareccattiṟku muṭipākkiṉum |amaiyum. puṟaṉ |eṉṉum |eḻuvāy nāṉāṉ keṉṉum payaṉilaikoṇṭu muṭintatu. |īṇṭuc collappaṭukiṉṟa patiṉāṟu poruḷum kaṟṟu nalloḻukku |oḻukum |aṟivuṭaiyār |avaikkaṇ tōṉṟāmaiyāṟ paṇṇait tōṉṟiya |eṉṟār. |eṉṉai? nakaikkuk kāraṇamākiya |eḷḷal |avar kaṇ tōṉṟāmaiyiṉ. piṟavum |aṉṉa.

muppattiraṇṭāvaṉa :- nakai mutalāṉavaṟṟiṟ kētuvām |eḷḷal mutalāka viḷaiyāṭṭīṟāka muṉṉeṭut tōtappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. |avaṟṟaik kuṟitta puṟaṉāvaṉa cuvaiyum kuṟippum. vīram, |accam, |iḻippu, viyappu, kāmam, |avalam, |uruttiram, nakai, naṭuvunilaimai |eṉṟum, vīrakkuṟippu, |accak kuṟippu, |iḻippukkuṟippu, viyappukkuṟippu, kāmakkuṟippu, |avalakkuṟippu, |uruttirak kuṟippu, nakaik kuṟippu, naṭuvu nilaimaikkuṟippu |eṉṟum collappaṭṭa patiṉeṭṭiṉum, naṭuvunilaimaiyum |ataṉ kuṟippum |oḻittu |ēṉaiya patiṉāṟumām. viyappeṉiṉum |aṟputameṉiṉum |okkum. kāmameṉiṉuñ ciṅ kārameṉiṉum |okkum. |avalam |eṉiṉum karuṇaiyeṉiṉum |okkum. |uruttiram |eṉiṉum vekuḷiyeṉiṉum |okkum. naṭuvu nilaimai |eṉiṉum mattimam |eṉiṉum cāntam |eṉiṉum |okkum. vīram |eṉpatu māṟṟāraik kuṟittu nikaḻvatu. |accam |eṉpatu |añcattakuvaṉa kaṇṭa vaḻi nikaḻva. |iḻippeṉpatu |iḻikkattakkaṉa kaṇṭuḻi nikaḻvatu. viyappeṉpatu viyakkattakkaṉa kaṇṭuḻi nikaḻvatu. kāmam |eṉpatu |iṉpa nikaḻcciyāṉ nikaḻvatu. |avalam |eṉpatu |iḻavupaṟṟip piṟappatu. |uruttiram |eṉpatu |avamatippāṟ piṟappatu. nakaiyeṉpatu |ikaḻcci mutalāyiṉavaṟṟāṟ piṟappatu. naṭuvunilaimai yeṉpatu yātoṉṟāṉum vikārappaṭāmai.

|avai |iṟṟāka, mattimameṉpataṉai |īṇṭoḻittatu |eṉṉai yeṉiṉ, ``mattimam |eṉpatu mācaṟat teriyiṟ collap paṭṭa |ellāc cuvaiyoṭu pullā tākiya poliviṟ ṟeṉpa.''

``nayaṉuṭai marapiṉ |itaṉpayam yāteṉiṟ certti yōrkkuñ cār/tupaṭup pōrkakum |oppa niṟkum nilaiyiṟ ṟeṉpa.''

``|uyppō ritaṉai yāreṉiṉ mikkatu payakkun tāpatar cāraṇar camaṇar kayakkaṟu muṉivar |aṟivaroṭu piṟaruṅ kāmam vekuḷi mayakkam nīṅkiya vāymai yāḷar vakuttaṉar piṟarum |accuvai yeṭṭum |avarkkila |ātaliṉ |accuvai |orutalai |ātaliṉ |ataṉai meyttalaip paṭukka|itaṉ mikavaṟin tōrē.'' |eṉpatu ceyiṟṟiyac cūttiram. |itaṉāṉē |itu vaḻakkilakkaṇam |aṉṟu |eṉa |uṇarka.

|iṉi, cuvai |eṉpatu kāṇappaṭu poruḷāṟ kāṇpōrakattiṉ varuvatōr vikāram. ``|iruvakai nilattiṉ |iyalvatu cuvaiyē.'' |eṉṟum, ``niṉṟa cuvai ... ... |oṉṟiya nikaḻcci cattuvam |eṉpa'' |eṉṟum, ``cattuvam |eṉpatu cāṟṟuṅ kālai meymmayir kuḷirttal kaṇṇīr vārtal naṭukkaṅ kaṭuttal viyarttal tēṟṟam kaḷittal, viḻittal, vetumpal, cākkāṭu, koṭuṅkuraṟ citaivoṭu niralpaṭa vanta patteṉa moḻipa cattuvan tāṉē'' |eṉṟum cārporuḷ |uraippa. |avai varumāṟu : pēyāṉum puliyāṉum kaṇṭāṉoruvaṉ |añciyavaḻi mayakkamuṅkarattalum naṭukkamum viyarppu muḷavākiṉṟē. |avaṟṟuḷ |accattiṟkē tuvākiya puliyum pēyum cuvaippaṭuporuḷ. |avaṟṟaik kaṇṭa kālantoṭṭu nīṅkātu niṉṟa |accam cuvai. |ataṉkaṇ mayakkamum karattaluṅ kuṟippu. naṭukkamum viyarppuñ cattuvam. |ivaṟṟuḷ naṭukkamum viyarppum piṟarkkum pulaṉāvatu |eṉṟu koḷka; |ēṉaiya maṉanikaḻcci. piṟavu maṉṉa. |ivaṟṟiṉ pirivai nāṭaka nūliṟ kāṇka.

((naṉṉūṟ ciṟappuppāyiram))

[[Naṉṉūl_0]] [[Mayilai_0]]
[[Caṅkaranamaccivāyar_0]] [[Virutti_0]]

malartalai yulakiṉ malkiru ḷakala
ilakoḷi parappi yāvaiyum viḷakkum
parutiyi ṉorutā ṉāki mutalī
ṟoppaḷa vācai muṉivikan tuyarnta
aṟputa mūrttitaṉ ṉalartaru taṉmaiyiṉ {{5}}
maṉaviru ḷiriya māṇporuṇ muḻuvatum
muṉivaṟa varuḷiya mūvaṟu moḻiyuḷum
kuṇakaṭal kumari kuṭakam vēṅkaṭam
eṉunāṉ kellaiyi ṉiruntamiḻk kaṭaluḷ
arumporu ḷaintaiyum yāvaru muṇarat {{10}}
tokaivakai viriyiṟ ṟarukeṉat tuṉṉār
ikalaṟa nūṟi yirunila muḻuvatum
taṉateṉak kōlit taṉmata vāraṇam
ticaitoṟu niṟuviya tiṟaluṟu tolcīrk
karuṅkaḻal veṇkuṭaik kārnikar vaṇkait {{15}}
tiruntiya ceṅkōṟ cīya kaṅkaṉ
aruṅkalai vinōta ṉamarā paraṇaṉ
moḻintaṉa ṉāka muṉṉōr nūliṉ
vaḻiyē naṉṉūṟ peyariṉ vakuttaṉaṉ
poṉmatiṟ caṉakaic caṉmati muṉiyaruḷ {{20}}
paṉṉaruñ ciṟappiṟ pavaṇanti
eṉṉu nāmat tiruntavat tōṉē.

muṉmoḻintu kōṭal piṉṉatu niṟuttal
10. vikaṟpattiṉ muṭittaṉ muṭintavai muṭittal

orutalai tuṇita leṭuttuk kāṭṭal
eṭutta moḻiyi ṉeyta vaittal
iṉṉa talla tituveṉa moḻital
15. eñciya colli ṉeytak kūṟal
piṟanūṉ muṭintatu tāṉuṭam paṭutal

Latest modification: 2004/03/03

Sandhi rules for words ending in "ṇ"

  • TEi148
    NORMAL CASE in alvaḻi: invariance (iyalpu):
    {{maṇ, poṉ}} eṉa niṟutti, {{kaṭitu, ciṟitu, tītu, peritu}} eṉa vaṉkaṇattōṭu oṭṭuka.
    EXCEPTIONAL CASE: change (tiripu): {{cāṭkōl}}
  • TEi309 SPECIAL CASE: eṇ
    {e-ṭu: {{eṭkaṭitu; ciṟitu, tītu, peritu}} eṉa varum. ummaiyāl, {{eṇkaṭitu}} eṉṟu iyalpu* ātal ē perumpāṉmai}
  • TE303i
    NORMAL CASE in vēṟṟumai: change (tiripu)
    {ṇakāravīṟṟu+ peyar valleḻuttu mutaṉmoḻi iyaiyiṉ ṭakāram āy muṭiyum vēṟṟumaipporuṭ puṇarccikkaṇ}
    {e-ṭu: {{maṭkuṭam; cāṭi, tūtai, pāṉai}} eṉa varum}
  • TE304i EXCEPTION: āṇ & peṇ
  • TEi307EXCEPTION: toḻiṟ peyar
    {e-ṭu: {{maṇṇukkaṭitu ((alvaḻi))}} eṉa ~um; {{maṇṇukkaṭumai ((vēṟṟumai))}} eṉa ~um; {{maṇṇuñāṉṟatu, ñāṟci}} eṉa ~um; {{maṇṇuvalitu, valimai}} eṉa ~um iruvaḻi ~um oṭṭuka.}
  • TE308iEXCEPTION: kiḷaippeyar
    {e-ṭu: {{umaṇkuṭi; cēri, tōṭṭam, pāṭi}} eṉa varum}
    {ADDITIONAL INTERPRETATION: `koḷa' eṉṟataṉāṉ, iv= īṟṟu ēḻām vēṟṟumai+ poruṇmai uṇaraniṉṟa iṭaiccol tirintu muṭivaṉa koḷka. {{aṅkaṭkoṇṭāṉ, iṅkaṭkoṇṭāṉ, uṅkaṭkoṇṭāṉ}} eṉa ~um; {{āṅkaṭkoṇṭāṉ, īṅkaṭkoṇṭāṉ, ūṅkaṭkoṇṭāṉ}} eṉa ~um; {{avaṭkoṇṭāṉ, ivaṭkoṇṭāṉ, uvaṭkoṇṭāṉ}} eṉa ~um oṭṭuka.}
  • TE310iSPECIAL CASE: muraṇ (toḻiṟ peyar)
    {muraṇ eṉṟu kūṟappaṭum toḻiṟpeyar iv= īṟṟiṟku iruvaḻi ~um muṉ kūṟiya iyalpu* um tiripu* um ākiya iyalpiṉ kaṇ= ē niṉṟu muṭiyum.}
    {e-ṭu: {{muraṇkaṭitu; ciṟitu, tītu, peritu ((alvaḻi))}} eṉa ~um; {{muraṭkaṭumai; cēṉai, tāṉai, paṟai ((vēṟṟumai))}} eṉa ~um varum}
    {itaṉai+ {T}toḻiṟpeyar ellām{/T} (([E307i])) eṉpataṉ piṉ vaiyāta muṟai ~aṉṟiya kūṟṟiṉāṉ, {{muraṇkaṭumai}} eṉṉum iyalpu* um {{araṇkaṭumai, araṭkaṭumai}} eṉṉum uṟaḻcci ~um koḷka.}

TERMIN_G_0007: ilēcu

Latest modification: 2004/02/29

MTL: ilEcu (1. "Lightness in weight", 2. "that which is easy to obtin or attin", 3. "that which is very small", 4. (Gram.) "short, significant and very suggestive word"
connected with sanskrit ileza- (i.e. ile'sa "petit morceau, particule, atome")
But, in the case of sanskrit, the technical meanings obtined in Renou (Terminologie Grammaticale du Sanskrit) and in Abhyankar (A Dictionnary of Sanskrit Grammar) seem circumscribed to the sphere of pronunciation. So, that might be (or might not be?) a Tamil innovation, unless they follow some sanskrit texts which Renou and Abhyankar did not explore.

Sanskrit Woerterbuch (Kuerzerer Fassung).

    le'sa:
  • 1) Partikel, Minimum, ein Bischen, ...
  • 2) ein best, kleiner Zeittheil
  • 3) ...
  • 4) in der Rhetorik:
    • a) anwendung der Vergleichung statt des directen Ausspruches
    • b) Darstellung als Nachtheil, was sonst als Vorzug gilt, und umgekehrt ("pr'esenter comme un inconv'enient ce qui est un avantage et r'eciproquement")
  • 5) ..

((2. |puṟattiṇi iyal))

|ivvōttu |eṉṉa peyarttō |eṉiṉ, puṟattiṇiyiyal |eṉṉumpeyarttu. |itu puṟapporuḷ |uṇarttutalāṟ peṟṟa peyar.

|aḵtu yāṅṅaṉam |uṇarttiṉārō |eṉiṉ, mēl |akattiṇiyākiya|eḻutiṇiyum cāṟṟi, |avaṟṟiṉ puṟattu nikaḻvaṉa |eḻu ṭiṇi |uṇarttiṉār |eṉṟu koḷka. |avi :-

maliyākiya kuṟiñcittiṇippuṟam nirikōṭalum niri mīṭṭalum|eṉṉu vēṟupāṭu kuṟittu veṭci |eṉavum karanti |eṉavum |iraṇṭumkuṟi peṟutalum,

kāṭuṟiyulakākiya mullippuṟam maṇnaci vēṭkiyāl |eṭuttuccelavupurinta vēntaṉmēl |aṭal kuṟittuc celavu puritalāṉ, |avviruperuvēntarum |oru viṉiyākiya celavu puritaliṉ |atu vañci |eṉakuṟi peṟutalum.

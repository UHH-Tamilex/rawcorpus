Cūḷāmaṇi - “Crest Jewel”
Author : Tōlāmoḻi-t-tēvar, Jain, c.Late 9th Century A.D.
Contains 12 Carukkams (Skt.sarga) (chapters); the last one deals with “mukthi” – “final emancipation”
Deals with Dharma, Artha, Kāma and Mōkṣa
Source: Mahāpurāṇa > abb.Srī Purāṇa > 11th Purāṇa - Śreyāṃśa Swāmi Purāṇa
aṅkaṇ ulakiṟku aṇivāṉ cuṭarāki niṉṟāṉ

paiṅkaṇ matarvaip pakuvāy ariēṟu pōḻnta
ceṅkaṇ neṭiyāṉ caritam itu ceppal uṟṟēṉ

nā māṇpu uraikkum kuṟai eṉṉiṉum nāma veṉvēl
tē māṇ alaṅkal tirumāl neṭuñ cēntaṉ eṉṉum
tū māṇ tamiḻiṉ kiḻavaṉ cuṭar āra mārpil
kōmāṉ avaiyuḷ teruṇṭār koḷappaṭṭatu aṉṟē
In every era, Jainism believes the birth and involvement of 63 enriched souls who are categorised as 24 Tīrtaṅkaras - 12 Chakravarthins- 9 Baladevas- 9 Vāsudevas and 9 prati-Vāsudevas as incarnators on the Earth who taught the mankind to attain the heavenly abode for each soul by virtue and wisdom.
Story in a nutshell:
The hero Tiviṭṭaṉ is incarnation of Tirumāl. He performs all miracles of Viṣṇu. He lifts a hill on his single hand. Conch and disc come to him from the Heaven. He annihilates the evil doers and establishes virtues. The hero’s father King Payāpati renounces the world and all worldly pleasures. He is praised as the precious gem Cuḷāmaṇi.

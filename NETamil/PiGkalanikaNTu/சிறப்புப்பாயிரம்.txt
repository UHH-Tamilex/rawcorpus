ciṟappuppāyiram
iruṅkaṭa luṭuttavim mānilam viḷaṅka
varumporu ṭeritta vakaṉṟamiḻ varaippi
ṉiyaṟcoṟ ṟiricoṟ ṟicaiccol vaṭacoleṉ
ṟaṉaittē ceyyuṭ kaṇikala ṉātaliṟ

tamporuḷ vaḻāma licaikku miyaṟco
loruporuḷ kuṟitta vēṟucol lākiyum
vēṟuporuḷ kuṟitta vorucol lākiyu
mirupāṟ ṟeṉpa tiricoṟ kiḷavi
centamiḻ cērnta ticaitoṟun ticaitoṟun
taṅkuṟip piṉavē ticaicoṟ kiḷavi

yeḻuttoṭu puṇarnta coṟṟā ṉākuñ

rannāṟ coṟṟiṟa mariṟapa muḻutuñ

piṅkala muṉiva ṉeṉattaṉ peyarniṟīi

mavaṉi vakaiyu māṭavar vakaiyu

mavaiyavat taṭaitta vāṉā viyalpiṟ

morucoṟ palporu ḷōṅkiya vakaiyumeṉ

maruḷaṟa vakuttaṉaṉ matiyiṉiṉ viraintē.
(itaṉ poruḷ.) iruṅkaṭal uṭutta i mā nilam viḷaṅka arum poruḷ teritta akaṉ tamiḻ varaippu iṉ – mikka kaṭal nīr puṭai cūḻnta intap periya nilavulaku viḷaṅkap peṟutaṟkariya aṟam, poruḷ, iṉpam, vīṭu eṉṉum nāṉku poruḷkaḷaiyum āṉmākkaḷukkup pulappaṭuttiya iṭam (paranta) tamiḻ vaḻaṅku mellaiyiṉkaṇ,
iyaṟcol ticaiccol vaṭacol eṉṟa aṉaittu ē uriccoṟkiḷavi – iyaṟcoleṉṟum tiricolleṉṟum ticaiccolleṉṟum vaṭacolleṉṟuñ collappaṭṭa avvaḷaviṉatē yuriccol,
ceyyuṭku aṇikalaṉ ātaliṉ – atu pulavarāl toṭukkappaṭuñ ceyyuḷukku ōraṇikalamātalāl,
centamiḻ nilattu vaḻakku oṭu civaṇi tam poruḷ vaḻāmal icaikkum iyaṟcol – uriccolliṉ pākupāṭāyc centamiḻ nilattil vaḻaṅkum vaḻakkātaṟkup poruntik koṭuntamiḻ nilattun tam poruḷ vaḻuvāma luṇarttu miyaṟcollum,
oruporuḷ kuṟitta vēṟu col ākiyum vēṟuporuḷ kuṟitta orucol ākiyum irupāṟṟu eṉpa tiricol kiḷavi – oruporuḷaik karutiya vēṟupeyarp pala collum pala poruḷaik karutiya oru collumā yiruvakaippaṭun tiricollum,
centamiḻ cērnta ticaitoṟum ticaitoṟum tam kuṟippiṉa ticaicol kiḷavi – centamiḻ nilattaic cērnta paṉṉiraṇṭu nilantōṟum tāṅkuṟitta poruḷai viḷakkun ticaiccollum,
vaṭaveḻuttu oruvi eḻuttu oṭu puṇarnta col tāṉākum citaintaṉa variṉ um iyaintaṉa varaiyār vaṭacol kiḷavi – vaṭacollukkē yuriyaveṉappaṭuñ ciṟappeḻuttiṉīṅki yiruvakaippaṭu moḻikaḷukkum potuvākiya eḻuttāliyaṉṟa collum vaṭaveḻuttāliyaṉṟa vaṭacol citaintu vantālum ceyyuḷiṭattup porutta muṭaiyaṉaveṉṟu kaḷaiyātu pulavar koḷḷuñcollumākiya vaṭacollumeṉṟa,
a nāṟcol tiṟam ariltapa muḻutum – anta nāṉku coṟkaḷiṉ pākupāṭṭiluḷḷa kuṟṟaṅkaḷ nīṅka accoṟkaḷ muṟṟum (av vuriccol ātalāl),
ceṅkatir varattāl tivākaraṉ payanta piṅkala muṉivaṉ eṉṉa taṉ peyar niṟuvi viruppuṭaṉ virikkum kālai – cūriyaṉ varattāl vanta tivākaramuṉivar peṟṟa piṅkalamuṉivaṉāṉavaṉ piṅkalam eṉṟu taṉ peyarait taṉṉūlukkut tantu makkaḷiṭattu aṉpukūrntu nūl ceyyuṅ kālattil,
nilaiiya vīṟucāl ciṟappiṉ vāṉ vakaiyum vāṉavar vakaiyum vāṉiṉum maṇṇiṉum vaḻaṅkal vaḻāta īṉam il aiyar iyalpuḷi vakaiyum – nilaipeṟṟa perumai mikka ciṟappiṉaiyuṭaiya vāṉ vakaiyum vāṉavar vakaiyum vāṉulakilum nilavulakilum tanta neṟiyiṉiṉṟum vaḻuvāta kuṟṟattiṉiṉṟu nīṅkiya aiyar vakaiyum,
avaṉi vakaiyum āṭavar vakaiyum avar tiṟattu amainta aṉupōka vakaiyum avaiyavattu aṭaitta āṉāta iyalpiṉ paṇpiṉ ceyaliṉ pakuti vakaiyum māppeyar vakaiyum marappeyar vakaiyum orucol palporuḷ ōṅkiya vakaiyum eṉṟu iruḷ aṟa īraintu ākki – avaṉi vakaiyum āṭavar vakaiyum av vāṭavar pākupāṭṭi lamainta aṉupōka vakaiyum taṉatakattu uḷḷa nalvakaiyum mutaliya uṟuppukaḷākac ceṟittaliṉiṉṟu nīṅkāta iyalpiṉaiyuṭaiya paṇpiṟ ceyaliṟ pakuti vakaiyum māppeyar vakaiyum marappeyar vakaiyum orucoṟ palporuḷ vakaiyumeṉṟu aiyamaṟap pattu vakaikaḷākap pakuttu amaittu,
matiyiṉiṉ viraintu maruḷ aṟa vakuttaṉaṉ – taṉatu nuṇṇaṟivāl viraintu ārāyvōrkkut tiripaṟa innūlai vakuttamaittaṉaṉ eṉka.
(viḷakkavurai.) ulaku puṟappāṭṭellaiyātalāl ‘immānilam’ eṉavum, āṉmākka ḷaṭaitaṟkuriya poruḷkaḷ aṟa mutaliya poruḷkaḷ ātalāl ‘arumporuḷ’ eṉavum, kiḻakkē kaṭalum teṟkē kumariyāṟum mēṟkē kuṭakanāṭum vaṭakkē tiruvēṅkaṭamumākiya innāṉkum tamiḻ vaḻaṅkutaṟkuriya akappāṭ ṭellaikaḷātalāl tokuttut ‘tamiḻvaraippiṉ’ eṉavum, iyaṟcol mutaliya nāṉkum ceyyuṭku urimaipūṇṭu niṟṟalāl ‘uriccol kiḷavi’ eṉavum, ceyyuḷīṭṭutaṟkuriya col atuvātalāl ‘aṉaittē ceyyuṭkaṇikala ṉātaliṉ’ eṉavum vaikaiyāṟṟiṉ vaṭakkum marutayāṟṟiṉ teṟkum karuvūriṉ mēṟkum tamiḻ vaḻaṅkum nilaṅkaḷātalāl ‘centamiḻ nilattu’ eṉavum, koṭuntamiḻ nilattuñ ceṉṟu tattamporuḷkaḷai annilatti luḷḷōrkku vaḻuvā tuṇarttutalāl ‘tamporuḷ vaḻāma licaikkum’ eṉavum, tiricoṟpōlātu kaṟṟōrkkaṉṟi yēṉaiyōrkkum poruḷ viḷaṅkiniṟṟalāl ‘iyaṟcol’ eṉavum, oruporuḷ karutiyum vēṟu poruḷ karutiyum varun tiricol: kiḷi – kiḷḷai, mayil – maññaiyeṉat tiriyum uṟupputtiripum, malai – viṇṭu, vilaṅkal eṉat tiriyum muḻutun tiripum eṉa iruvakaippaṭutalāl ivaṟṟait ‘tiricoṟ kiḷavi’ eṉavum, koṭuntamiḻ vaḻaṅkum nilam centamiḻ vaḻaṅkum nilattaic cērntatātalāl ‘centamiḻ cērnta’ eṉavum, koṭuntamiḻ vaḻaṅku nilaṅkaḷ: poṅkarnāṭu, oḷināṭu, teṉpāṇṭināṭu, kuṭṭanāṭu, kuṭanāṭu, paṉṟināṭu, kaṟkānāṭu, cītanāṭu, pūḻināṭu, malaināṭu, aruvānāṭu, aruvāvaṭatalai yeṉap paṉṉiraṇṭu vakaippaṭutalāl ‘ticaitoṟun ticaitoṟum’ eṉap paṉmaip poruḷpaṭa vaṭukkiyum, eḻuttu eṉṟatu āriyattiṟkun tamiḻiṟkum potuveḻutteṉavum, atu – mēru, maṇi mutaliya pōlvaṉavum eṉpār ‘vaṭaveḻut torīi, eḻuttoṭu puṇarnta’ eṉavum vaṭacoṟ citaintu varutalumaṉṟip pākatamāyc citaintu varuvaṉavum uḷavātalāl ‘citaintaṉa variṉum’, eṉavum, piṅkalaṉāṟ ceyyappaṭṭatu piṅkalamātalāl ‘piṅkalamuṉivaṉeṉat taṉpeyar niṟīi’ eṉavum, ēṉaip pūtaṅkaḷ tōṉṟa nilaipeṟutalāl ‘nilaiiya vīṟucāl ciṟappiṉ’ eṉavum, ivvakaikaḷ oṉṟōṭoṉ ṟiyaipuṭaimaiyil iṭainilait tīvakamāka ‘iyalpuḷi’ eṉavum, muṉṉuḷḷa tivākaram pākupāṭillāmal kaṟpōrkku aiyamun tiripum viḷaittalāl ‘iruḷaṟa maruḷaṟa’ eṉavum, ennikaṇṭiṉ poruḷum innikaṇṭiṉuṭpaṭa vakuttamaiyāl ‘matiyiṉiṉ vakuttaṉaṉ’ eṉavuṅ kūṟiṉār.
ciṟappuppāyiram muṟṟiṟṟu.

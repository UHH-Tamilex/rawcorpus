mukavurai
——
tamiḻneṟiviḷakkameṉpatu tamiḻiṉatu oḻukalāṟṟai viḷakkum viḷakkuppōṉṟa nūleṉṉum poruḷuṭaiyatu. iḵtu akapporuḷ viḷakkam, ilakkaṇaviḷakkam, toṉṉūlviḷakkameṉṉum ilakkaṇa nūṟpeyarkaḷaiyum, uṇmaiviḷakkam, nītineṟiviḷakkameṉṉum ilakkiyanūṟpeyarkaḷaiyum pōṉṟatu. tamiḻeṉṟatu palapakutiyākiya tamiḻnūṟparappai. neṟiyeṉṟatu pulaṉeṟivaḻakkiṉai. alaṅkārattil neṟiyeṉpatu ceyyuḷiṉ naṭaiyaik kuṟittu niṟṟal kāṇka. ilakkiyaṅkaṇṭataṟku ilakkaṇam iyampuvatu marapātaliṉ nallicaip pulavarum piṟarum ilakkiyaṅkaḷil amaittuk kāṭṭiyavaṟṟaiyum, ilakkaṇaṅkaḷil vitittuk kāṭṭiyavaṟṟaiyum ārāyntu avar kaḷuṭaiya tamiḻnūlkaḷiṉ neṟi ittakaiyateṉpatai viḷakkuvatu innūl.
iṟaiyaṉārakap poruḷuraiyāciriyar annūl nutaliyatu iṉṉateṉak kūṟumiṭattu,
'innūl, eṉṉutaliṟṟō veṉiṉ tamiḻnutaliya teṉpatu'
eṉṟum, piṟitōriṭattil
'tamiḻtāṉ nāṉkuvakaippaṭum, eḻuttum collum 
poruḷum yāppumeṉa'
eṉṟum amaikkiṉṟār. ivviṭaṅkaḷil tamiḻeṉpataṟku avar tamiḻilakkaṇameṉṟa poruḷ koṇṭāreṉṟu tōṟṟutaliṉ, tamiḻ ilakkaṇa neṟiyai viḷakkuvateṉṉum poruḷum in nūṟpeyarukkup poruttamuṭaiyatākum.
"tamiḻiyal vaḻakkameṉat taṉṉaṉpu mikaiperukiya
kaḷaveṉap paṭuvatu kantaruva maṇamē" (yā. vi. cū. 96, mēṟ.)
eṉṉum avinayac cūttiramum,
"tamiḻneṟi vaḻakka maṉṉa taṉiccilai vaḻakku" (kaḷaṅkāṇ. 61) eṉṉum kampar vākkum tamiḻ ilakkaṇavaḻakkaic ciṟappikkiṉṟaṉa. 'tamiḻiyal vaḻakkam', 'tamiḻneṟi vaḻakkam' eṉṟa toṭarmoḻikaḷukkum innūṟpeyarukkum poruḷoppumaiyiruttal kāṇka.
innūl tamiḻilakkaṇappakutiyaṉaittaiyum uṭaiyatākat tōṟṟu kiṉṟatu. itaṉ mutaṟcūttira uraiyil,
'niṟutta muṟaiyāṉē poruḷilakkaṇam āmāṟu
uṇarttutal nutaliṟṟu'
eṉṟu kāṇappaṭum vākkiyamum, tamiḻ neṟiviḷakkam poruḷiya leṉṟa atikārap peyarum itaṟku muṉ eḻuttiyalum moḻiyiyalumākiya iraṇṭu pirivukaḷ uṇṭeṉpataip pulappaṭuttukiṉṟaṉa. poruḷiyaliṟkup piṉpu yāppiyaloṉṟum iruntateṉṟu karuta iṭamuṇṭu.
ipporuḷiyalum akam, puṟam, akappuṟameṉṉum mūṉṟu pirivāka iruttal vēṇṭum; ikkaruttai mutaṟ cūttiram tōṟṟac ceykiṉṟatu. poruḷiyaleṉṟa potuppeyarē itaṉkaṇ akapporuḷ, puṟapporuḷeṉṉum iraṇṭupakutiyēṉum irukkavēṇṭumeṉṟu niṉaikka ātāramākiṉṟatu. ippoḻutu innūliṟ kiṭaippavai akapporuḷaippaṟṟik kūṟum pakutiyākiya 25 cūttiraṅkaḷē. avaṟṟuḷḷum, 25-āvatu cūttira uraiyiṉ piṟpakuti kiṭaikkavillai. ivvirupattaintu cūttiraṅkaḷil innūlāciriyar vakuttuk koṇṭa akapporuḷilakkaṇam muṭivuṟukiṉṟatu.
tamiḻilakkaṇaṅkaḷil tolkāppiyam, avinayam, vīracōḻiyam, ilakkaṇaviḷakkam, toṉṉūl viḷakkameṉpaṉa eḻuttu mutaliya ilakkaṇap pakutikaḷaṉaittaiyum paṟṟik kūṟuvaṉa. avaṟṟiṉ varicaiyilē oṉṟāka innūlum cērttu eṇṇat takuvatu. innūlai iyaṟṟiya āciriyariṉ peyarum varalāṟum aṟiyakkūṭavillai.
tamiḻilakkaṇattiṉ ovvoru pakutiyum nāḷaṭaivil virivaṭaiyavē ovvoru pakutiyilum palanūlkaḷ uṇṭāyiṉa. ippoḻutu aṟiyappaṭum ilakkaṇa nūlkaḷuḷ mikap paḻaiyatākiya akattiyam muttamiḻukkum ilakkaṇam vakuttatu. avaṟṟil oṉṟākiya iyaṟṟamiḻukkut tolkāppiyam mutaliyaṉa ilakkaṇam vakuttaṉa. tolkāppiyattiluḷḷa eḻuttu, col eṉṉum iraṇṭaiyum taṉiyē virittuk kūṟum nūlkaḷ cila piṟkālattil uṇṭāyiṉa. poruḷatikārattiṟ kaṇṭa ilakkaṇaṅkaḷ akapporuḷ, puṟapporuḷ, yāppu, aṇi, pāṭṭiyaleṉṟu taṉittaṉiyāka virintu pala nūlkaḷiṟ collappaṭṭaṉa. yāppilakkaṇa nūlkaḷ evvaḷavō iruntaṉa veṉpatai yāpparuṅkalaviruttiyuraiyāl uṇaralām. akap poruḷilakkaṇa nūlkaḷum pala iruntaṉa. ippoḻutu iṟaiyaṉārakapporuḷ, nāṟkavirāca nampi iyaṟṟiya akapporuḷ viḷakkam, kaḷaviyaṟ kārikai yeṉṉum puṉaipeyaraiyuṭaiya torunūl, māṟaṉakapporuḷeṉpaṉa namakkuk kiṭaippavai. tolkāppiya uraikaḷ, iṟaiyaṉārakapporuḷurai, yāpparuṅkala viruttiyurai, vīracōḻiya urai mutaliyavaṟṟil mēṟkōḷāka eṭuttāḷappaṭum cila cūttiraṅkaḷum veṇpākkaḷum vēṟupala akapporuḷilakkaṇa nūlkaḷiṉ pakutikaḷeṉṟē niṉaikkattakum.
tirucciṟṟampalak kōvaiyāruraiyil, pērāciriyar annūlukkēṟpak kāṭṭum cūttiraṅkaḷ ōr akapporuḷilakkaṇa nūliluḷḷaṉavō, aṉṟi avarāka amaittukkoṇṭa uraic cūttiraṅkaḷō iṉṉaveṉṟu tuṇiya muṭiyavillai.
iṅṅaṉam amainta nūlkaḷ oṉṟaṟ koṉṟu palavakai vēṟupāṭukaḷ uṭaiyaṉavāka irukkum. ittamiḻneṟi viḷakkattiṉ akapporuṭpakutikkum vēṟu akapporuḷilakkaṇa nūlkaḷukkum vēṟupāṭukaḷ pala uṇṭu. innūl kaḷavukku muṉ kaikkiḷaiyaik kūṟavillai. kaḷaviṉ pakutiyākap perumpālōrāl amaikkappaṭum aṟattoṭunilai, uṭaṉpōkku eṉṉum iraṇṭaiyum innūl kaṟpiṉuḷ amaikkiṉṟatu. tolkāppiyattil, kūṟṟiṟkuriyavaruḷ oruvarākac collappaṭāta, talaivaṉuṭaiya naṟṟāyiṉ kūṟṟoṉṟu itiṟ kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. aṅkaṅkē arukik kāṇappaṭum cila tuṟaikaḷukku itil ilakkaṇamum ilakkiyamum uḷḷaṉa (mēṟ. 41, 96, 108.)
innūlāciriyar poruḷiyalai akam, puṟam, akappuṟameṉa mūṉṟākap pakuttuk kūṟi, piṉ akapporuḷiṉ vakaiyākiya mutal karu uriyeṉṉum mūṉṟai uṇarttukiṉṟār. karupporuḷiṉ vakaiyākac cūttirattiṟ kūṟappaṭuvaṉa teyvam, māṉiṭam, toḻil, uṇavu, icai, vilaṅku eṉpaṉa. ēṉaiyavai 'piṟavum' eṉpataṉāṟ koḷḷakkiṭakkiṉṟaṉa. teyvak karupporuḷ kūṟumpoḻutu pālaikku iraviyai amaikkiṉṟār;
'ñāyiṟṟait teyvamākki avaṉiṟ ṟōṉṟiya
maḻaiyiṉaiyum kāṟṟiṉaiyum atteyvap pakutiyākkik kūṟupa'
(tol. akat. 5, na.)
eṉṟu nacciṉārkkiṉiyar cuṭṭum āciriyaruḷ ivarum oruvareṉṟē koḷḷattakum. iṟaiyaṉārakapporuḷ uraiyāciriyarum, takkayākap paraṇiyuraiyāciriyarum ittakaiya orucārār koḷkaiyai eṭuttuk kūṟuvar.
kaḷavaippaṟṟik kūṟappukunta āciriyar atu kantaruva maṇattōṭoppateṉpar. iyaṟkaip puṇarcci, pāṅkaṟkūṭṭam, tōḻiyiṟ kūṭṭattup pakaṟkuṟi, iravukkuṟi, varaivu kaṭātal, uṭaṉpōkku valittaleṉṉum 1āṟupirivākak kaḷavoḻukkam pakukkappaṭukiṉṟatu. avaṟṟuḷ iyaṟkaip puṇarcci taṉṉaya muṇarttalmutal āṟṟiṉaṉ peyartalīṟākiya ēḻu tuṟaikaḷaiyum, pāṅkaṟkūṭṭam viṉātal mutal paḻavarai viṭuttalīṟākiya paṉṉiraṇṭu tuṟaikaḷaiyum, pakaṟkuṟi pāṅkuṇarvu mutal pāṅki koṇṭikattalīṟākiya muppattiraṇṭu tuṟaikaḷaiyum, iravukkuṟi kātaṉmikaviṉ vāyviṭukiḷavi mutal nōtalīṟākiya patiṉaintu tuṟaikaḷaiyum, varaivukaṭātal varuneṟi niṉaital mutal aṟintēṉeṉṟalīṟākiya eṭṭum piṟavumākiya tuṟaikaḷaiyum, uṭaṉpōkku valittal alar periteṉṟal mutal virumpiṉaṉ nērnta pāvakkiḷavi yīṟākiya ēḻu tuṟaikaḷaiyum uṭaiyaṉa.
kaṟpoḻukkam aṟattoṭunilai, uṭaṉ celavu, cēyiṭaip pirivu, āyiṭaip piriveṉṉum nāṉku pirivukaḷai uṭaiyatu. avaṟṟuḷ aṟattoṭunilai talaivi tōḻikku aṟattoṭuniṟṟal mutal iḷaiyōṟ ketirtalīṟākiya patiṉēḻu tuṟaikaḷaiyum, uṭaṉcelavu kaiyaṭai mutal talaimakaṉ moḻiyīṟākiya patiṉaintum piṟavumākiya tuṟaikaḷaiyum, cēyiṭaip pirivu pirivakaiyuṇarttal mutal varuvōṉ kūṟṟīṟākiya patiṉāṉku tuṟaikaḷaiyum, āyiṭaip pirivu vāyiṉmaṟuttal mutal cevili kūṟṟīṟākiya eṭṭum piṟavumākiya tuṟaikaḷaiyum uṭaiyaṉa.
ittuṟaikaḷiṟ cila uraiyil iraṇṭu mutaṟ pala tuṟaikaḷāka virikkappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ovvoru tuṟaikkum kāṭṭappaṭṭuḷḷa utāraṇaṅkaḷuḷ kuṟuntokai, aiṅkuṟunūṟu, ciṟṟaṭṭakam eṉṉum nūlkaḷiluḷḷa ceyyuṭkaḷum vēṟu pala ceyyuṭkaḷum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.
innūlaiyum uraiyaiyum pārkkaiyil iraṇṭum ōrāciriyarālēyē iyaṟṟappaṭṭaṉa veṉṟu tōṟṟukiṉṟatu. kaḷaviyaṟ kārikaiyurai yāciriyar,
'tamiḻneṟi viḷakkattiṟ poruḷiyaluṭaiyārum kaḷavoḻukkam āṟuvakaip paṭumeṉṟār. avai yaṟivittalaik karutiṟṟu......... teyvappuṇarcciyum pāṅkaṟkūṭṭamum tōḻiyālāya kūṭṭattup pakaṟkuṟiyum iravukkuṟiyum varaivukaṭātalum uṭaṉpōkku valittalumeṉa ...........'
eṉṟu innūlai mēṟkōḷ kāṭṭukiṉṟaṉar. iṅṅaṉamē "mukkaṭkūṭṭam" (21) eṉṟa cūttirattiṉ mūlamum uraiyum itiluḷḷapaṭiyē mēṟkōḷāka avarāṟ kāṭṭappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. kuṟaḷ, kaṟpiyal, avatārikaiyil parimēlaḻakar,
'varainteytiya piṉ talaimakaṉ aṟam poruḷiṉpaṅkaḷiṉ poruṭṭuc cēyiṭaiyiṉum āyiṭaiyiṉum talaimakaḷaip pirintu cellum'
eṉṟeḻutum pakutiyil cēyiṭaip pirivu, āyiṭaip pirivu eṉa innūluṭ kuṟikkappeṟuvaṉa kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ivaṟṟāl ivviraṇṭu uraiyāciriyarkaḷukkum muṟpaṭṭa kālattiṉar innūlāciriyareṉṟu koḷḷalākum.
ilakkaṇa nūlkaḷil tollāciriyar karuttukkaḷait tām mēṟkoṇṭataip pulappaṭuttumporuṭṭu an nūlkaḷiṉ āciriyarkaḷ 'eṉpa', 'eṉmaṉār' eṉpaṉa pōṉṟa coṟkaḷai amaippatu marapu. kuṟiñciyaik kuṟuñci yeṉṟum (cū. 3), makiṇaṉai makuṇaṉeṉṟum (cū. 8) innūl kuṟikkiṉṟatu. kuricil kurucileṉṟum, pariti paruti yeṉṟum vaḻaṅkutal iṅkē niṉaittaṟkuriyatu.
kaṟpoḻukkattaip paṟṟik kūṟum cūttirattiluḷḷa "mukkaṭ kūṭṭam" eṉpatu iṉṉateṉṟu ippoḻutu viḷaṅkavillai; ataṉ uraiyil 'mukkaṭ kūṭṭamuṭaiya kaṟpu' eṉṟa pakuti kāṇappaṭukiṉṟatu.
"......kūṭṭak kūṭal
evviṭat tāṉuṅ kaḷaviṟ killai" (iṟai. 4)
eṉṟa cūttiramum,
'allatūum, piṟarum makkaṭ kūṭṭam kaḷaviṟkillai yeṉṟamaiyāṉ makkaṭkūṭṭam kaṟpiṟkuṇṭeṉpatu peṟappaṭṭateṉṟu viritturaittārākalāṉu meṉka' (yā. vi. 22, urai)
eṉpatum appakuti makkaṭkūṭṭam eṉṟu iruttal kūṭumeṉṟu ūkikkac ceykiṉṟaṉa. āyiṉum, iccūttirattaiyum uraiyaiyum appaṭiyē eṭuttu mēṟkōḷ kāṭṭum kaḷaviyaṟkārikaiyuraiyil mukkaṭ kūṭṭameṉṟa toṭarē irukkiṉṟatu. cūttirattai maṭṭum nōkkukaiyil mukkaṭkūṭṭameṉpataṟku aṟattoṭunilaiyeṉṉum poruḷ koḷḷak kiṭakkiṉṟatu. aṅṅaṉamāyiṉ yāpparuṅkala viruttiyāciriyar kāṭṭum mēṟkōḷil mukkaṭkūṭṭameṉpatē ēṟṟa pāṭamātal vēṇṭum. inta aiyamum vēṟu piṟavum nāḷaṭaivil teḷivākumeṉṟu eṇṇukiṟēṉ.
in nūluraiyiluḷḷa utāraṇac ceyyuṭkaḷil iṭam viḷaṅkāta 173 ceyyuṭkaḷukkut taṉiyeṇkaḷ koṭukkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟiṟ pala ceyyuṭkaḷ 0kaḷaviyaṟ kārikaiyuraiyil 'poruḷiyal' eṉṉum peyaruṭaṉ kāṭṭappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. avvuraiyāciriyar in nūṟ cūttirattaiyum, uraiyaiyum, mēṟkōḷaiyum poruḷiya leṉṟē kuṟittalai nōkkumpoḻutu im mūṉṟaiyum oruvarē iyaṟṟiyiruttal kūṭumeṉṟa karuttu uṇṭākiṉṟatu. utāraṇac ceyyuḷiṟ cila tolkāppiya urai, nampiyakapporuḷurai, yāpparuṅkalaviruttiyurai mutaliyavaṟṟil vantuḷḷaṉa. pala uraikaḷil mēṟkōḷākak kāṭṭappeṟum "pūttavēṅkai" eṉṉum ceyyuḷum "ēva liḷaiyar tāyvayiṟu karippa" eṉpatum itil utāraṇaṅkaḷāka vantuḷḷaṉa.
ivvutāraṇac ceyyuṭkaḷ ilakkaṇa amaitiyaik kāṭṭutaṟkeṉa iyaṟṟappaṭṭaṉa pōlac cila aṭikaḷāl iyaṉṟuḷḷaṉa. palavaṭikaḷāl iyaṉṟa akanāṉūṟu mutaliya nūṟceyyuṭkaḷai ivvuraiyāciriyar utāraṇamākak kāṭṭavillai. akapporuḷ ilakkaṇa vamaitiyait terivikkac cila aṭikaḷaiyuṭaiya ceyyuṭkaḷē pōtiyaṉaveṉpatu ivar karuttup pōlum. caṅkamaruviya nūlkaḷiluḷḷa collum poruḷum utāraṇac ceyyuṭkaḷiṟ pala viṭaṅkaḷil eṭuttāḷappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
icceyyuṭkaḷil vaḻuttū reṉpatōr ūriṉ peyarum, atilirunta matitara reṉpavar ciṟappum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa:
"uṭaiyai vāḻi neñcē yiṭaikoṇ
ṭaḻuṅka lōmpumati taḻaṅkoli mikunīr
vaḻuttūr kākkum puṇaiyiṉ
viḻuttuṇai cāṉṟa mikuperuṅ kiḷaiyē" (6)
"tūymai cāṉṟa tolkuṭit tōṉṟiya
vāymai nāviṉ matitaraṉ pōla
uyartava muṉivar cārap
peyarā nilaiyatip piṟaṅkuperu malaiyē" (32)
"tantunī yaḷitta taṇṭaḻai kāṇṭalum
vantaṉa ḷetirnta maṭantai neñcam
maṇmicai viḷaṅkiya vaḻuttūr matitaraṉ
nuṇṇiyaṟ paṉuva ṉuḻaiporu ṇuṉitta
vāymoḻi yamirta maṭuttavar maṉameṉa
āṉilai peṟṟaṉṟi yāṉaṟin tilaṉē." (52)
vaḻuttūreṉṉum peyaruṭaiya ūroṉṟu tañcai jillāvil aiyampēṭṭaikku vaṭakkē kuṭamuruṭṭiyāṟṟiṉ teṉkaraiyil uḷḷatu. ivvaḻuttūr atuvēpōlum. 'vaḻuttūr kākkum puṇaiyiṉ' eṉṟatu veḷḷam perukiya kālattil avvūriṉar ōṭattilēṟik karaicērntaṉareṉṟu niṉaikka iṭantarukiṉṟatu. aṉṟip puṇaiyeṉpatu piṟarukkup paṟṟukkōṭākiya oru talaivaṉaik kuṟippāṟ cuṭṭiyatākak koḷḷalum ām; "kaḷvaṉuṅ kaṭavaṉum puṇaivaṉun tāṉē" (kuṟun. 318) vaḻuttūril 'araṇmaṉai mēṭu' eṉṟatōriṭam irukkiṟateṉpar. aṅkē ōr araṇmaṉai yiruntateṉṟum avvaraṇmaṉaiyil ōratikāriyō ciṟṟaracaṉō vāḻntiruntaṉaṉeṉṟum kūṟuvar.
vaḻuttūril vāḻntirunta matitarareṉpār naṟkuṭiyiṟ ṟōṉṟiyavar; vāymai tavaṟā māṇpiṉar; tavavoḻukkiṉar; pala nūlārāycci yuṭaiyavar; nallupatēcam puripavar. ivai mēṟ kaṇṭa ceyyuṭkaḷāl aṟiyappaṭuvaṉa. icceyyuṭkaḷai iyaṟṟiya āciriyarukku avar āciriyarāka iruttaluṅkūṭum. nuṇ iyaṟpaṉuval nuḻaiporuḷ nuṉitta eṉpatu nuṇṇiya ilakkaṇa nūlkaḷiṉ ariya poruḷkaḷai ārāyntaveṉṟu poruḷpaṭṭu, am matitarar, ilakkaṇa nūlkaḷiṟ ciṟanta ārāycciyuṭaiyavareṉpataip pulappaṭuttum. matitarareṉpatu civaperumāṉ tirunāmaṅkaḷil oṉṟātaliṉ avarum avaraic cārntu payiṉṟa ivvutāraṇac ceyyuṭkaḷai iyaṟṟiyavarum civapaktic celvarkaḷeṉṟu koḷḷalām.

5. "iṉaiyal vāḻi yemmūr malarnta
paḻaṉat tāmaraik keḻīiya vaṇṭuniṉ
kaṇṇeṉa malarnta kāmar cuṉaimalar
naṇṇi nāḷu nalaṉuka rummē" (ciṟṟaṭṭakam)
ituvumatu.
6. "maṉavē ralkuṉ maṭantai kūṭṭam
kaṉavō teriyiṉ yāvatum
naṉavē yeṉiṉu naṇṇalō varitē" 
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 25, nampi. 133 mēṟ.) (3)
eṉpatu eytuta larumai.
7. "0pulaṉaṉ ṟeṉpa viṉṉuyi ratuvē
alarmulai yākan tāṅki
nilavoḷi 0matiyi ṉilavume ṉuyirē" (4)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 25, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu uyireṉak kūṟal.
8. "kaṉavē yāyiṉu naṉavē yāyiṉum
nalviṉai tantatoṉ ṟaṉṟē yataṉāl
melliya lākam mēvavum peṟumē" (5)
eṉpatu teyvan tuṇaiyākak koṇṭu āṟṟippeyarntatu.
9. "uṭaiyai vāḻi neñcē yiṭaikoṇ
ṭaḻuṅka lōmpumati taḻaṅkoli mikunīr
vaḻuttūr kākkum puṇaiyiṉ
viḻuttuṇai cāṉṟa mikuperuṅ kiḷaiyē" (6)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 25, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu pāṅkaṉai niṉaittu āṟṟip peyarntatu. (15)
2. pāṅkaṟkūṭṭam
16. viṉātalum viṭuttaluṅ kaḻaṟalu maṟuttalum
nōtalum yāṇṭeṉa viṉṉuḻi yeṉṟalum
mātaraik kāṇṭalu maṉṉiya vakuttalum
kiḻavōṟ kicaittalu māṟṟāṉ kiḷattalum
5. 	paḻavarai viṭuttalum pāṅkaṟ kūṭṭam.
e-tu. pāṅkaṟkūṭṭam āmāṟu uṇarttutal nutaliṟṟu.
(i – ḷ.) pāṅkaṉ talaimakaṟkuṟṟa viṉātalum, talaimakaṉ iṉṉateṉṟalum, pāṅkaṉ nōtalum, evviṭat tevvuruveṉṟalum, talaimakaṉ iṉṉaviṭattu iṉṉavuru veṉṟalum, pāṅkaṉ avviṭattu avvuruk kāṇṭalum, talaimakaṉaip perumai kūṟalum, peyarntu vantu talaimakaṟku uraittalum, talaimakaṉ talaimakaḷaik kaṇṭu aṇaitalum, uṟaviṉāṟṟāṉ kūṟalum, viṭuttalum pāṅkaṟkūṭṭamā meṉṟavāṟu.
1. "muṉṉiṉai muṭippatoṉ ṟuḷatukoṉ 0mūvakai
nuṇṇiya paṉuvali ṉuḻaintakol
eṉṉaikol vāṭiya taṇṇaṉiṉ ṉeḻilē" (7)
eṉpatu viṉātal.
2. "ciṟuveḷ ḷaravi ṉavvarik kuruḷai
kāṉa yāṉai yaṇaṅki yāaṅ
kiḷaiyaṇ muḷaivā ḷeyiṟṟaḷ
vaḷaiyuṭaik kaiyaḷem maṇaṅki yōḷē" (kuṟun. 119)
eṉpatu pāṅkaṟkut talaimakaṉ kūṟiyatu.
3. "0poruntā tamma puṉaiyiḻai yarivai
muruntēr muṟuva ṉōkkiṉ
varuntiṉa ṉeṉpatu peruntakai peritē" (8)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 26, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu pāṅkaṉ kaḻaṟiyatu.
4. "0kāṇkuvi rāyiṟ kaḻaṟalir maṉṉō
pūṇpuṉai vaḷariḷa vaṉamulai
māṇkuḻai mātar maṭaṅkeḻu nōkkē" (9)
eṉpatu kaḻaṟṟetir maṟuttatu.
5. "pulantuṟai pōkiya neñcuniṟai yaḻintu
kalaṅkuva tāyiṉ mātō
cilampaṉai yārō teḷikkunar piṟarē." (10)
eṉpatu pāṅkaṉ piḻaittaṉaṉeṉṟu āṟṟāṉāyiṟṟu.
6. "vāḻvati yāvatu kollō vāṉpukaḻc
cūḻkaḻa laṇṇa ṉeñcam
āḻtuya reyta vaṇaṅkiya vaṇaṅkē" (11)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 26, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu pāṅkaṉ iṭam viṉāyiṟṟu.
7. "kaṇṇē kāviyaṅ kayamalar nutalē
taṇṇiḷa matiyeṉat takumē patiyum
perumalaic cāraṟ cīṟūr
arunilai vāṅkiya vāyiḻai taṉakkē" (12)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ kuṟiyuraittatu.
8. "vēṅkai yoḷvī kāntaḷoṭu kamaḻum
pūntaṇ cāralu mituvē yēntilai
ayilvē laṇṇal kūṟiya
mayilēr cāyal vaṇṇamu mituvē" (13)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 27, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu pāṅkaṉ etirppaṭṭatu.
9. "nōtaka vuṭaittē neñca matimicai
mātark kuvaḷai malarmalarn taṉṉa
karuntaṭaṅ kaṇkaṇ ṭāṟṟiya
peruntakai yaṇṇalaik kaḻaṟiṉaṉ peritē" (14)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 27, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉ āṟṟal kūṟiyatu.
10. "āytaḷir potuḷiya vītatai taṇciṉaik
0kāykatir nuḻaiyāk kaṭipoḻil
yāvayi ṉōrum viḻaivuṟun takaittē" (15)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 28, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu pāṅkaṉ peyarntu vantu talaimakaṟ kuraittatu.
11. "0taṉimai neñcattu muṉivukaṇ ṇakaṟṟaliṉ
viṉaimāṇ pāvai yaṉṉa
puṉaiyiḻai mātarum pōṉṟatip poḻilē" (16)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 28, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉ avviṭattuc ceṉṟatu.
12. "0teyva māka vaiyuṟu neñcam
poyyā tāyiṉiṉ cevvāy tiṟantu
kiḷipurai kiḷaviyām peṟuka
oḷiyiḻai maṭantai yuyirpeyarp paritē" (17)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 28, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ aṇaitaluṟaviṉāṟṟāṉ kūṟiyatu.
13. "0kāviyaṅ karuṅkaṭ cevvāyp paintoṭi
pūviri curimeṉ kūntalum
vēypurai tōḷu maṇaṅkumā lemmē" (18)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 28, yā. vi. 39, mēṟ.)
ituvumatu.
14. "perumalarp pirinta tirumaka ḷammalar
aruṅkavi ṉeytac ceṉṟu cērntāṅ
kiruṅkalat toṉmaṉai poliyap
peruntakait tōḷi peyarntaṉai ceṉmē" (19)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷai viṭuttal. (16)
3. tōḻiyiṟ kūṭṭam—pakaṟkuṟi
17. pāṅkuṇar vaiyuṟa virantukuṟai yuṟutalum
āṅkavaḷ viṉātalu matiyuṭa ṉākkalum
tuṇitaluñ cēṭpaṭa niṟuttaluñ cuṭṭiya
taṟiyēṉaṟivaṉ maṟaiyē leṉṟalum
5. kuṟiyā kūṟalum paṭaittaṉa moḻitalum
ariya ḷeṉṟalu nīkū ṟeṉṟalum
nakaikoṇ ṭāṟṟalu nakāturai yeṉṟalum
kaiyuṟai 0yeḷimai kāṭṭalu meyyuṟa
ovvā teṉṟalu mavaṇilai yuṇarntu
10. koḷkuva ṉeṉṟalu maṭaṉmā kūṟalum
maṟṟatu vilakkaluṅ kaiyuṟai kōṭalum
viḻaiya vuṇarttalu nāṇoṭu nīṅkalum
valitākac collik kuṟainayap pittalum
taḻīik kōṭalun taḻaiyēṟ pittalum
15. kaiyuṟai yetirntamai meypeṟa vicaittalum
virumpiṭa muraittaluṅ kuṟiyoṭu nīṅkalum
puṉaintu nilaiyalu nilaimai kūṟalum
pāṅkikoṇ ṭikattalum pakaṟkuṟi yākum.
e-tu. tōḻiyiṟ kūṭṭattup pakaṟkuṟiyāmāṟu uṇarttutal nutaliṟṟu.
(i – ḷ.) pāṅkiyai uṇarntatu mutalākak koṇṭu peyarnta tīṟākac collap paṭṭaṉa vellām tōḻiyiṉākiya pakaṟkuṟik kūṭṭamā meṉṟavāṟu.
1. "nōkkiṉum piṟarmuka nōkkāḷ cāriṉum
pūkkuḻaṉ maṭantai tōḷcā rummē
aṉṉa talaiyaḷi yuṭaimaiyiṉ
iṉṉuyirt tōḻiyiv vēntiḻai yivaṭkē" (20)
(nampi. 133, mēṟ; kaḷaviyaṟ. 25, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu pāṅkiyai uṇarntatu.
2. "0puṉaiyiḻai yāyamoṭu pūmpan teṟiyavum
0naṉaimalar ñāḻa loḷvī koyyavum
varuntiṉaḷ kollō maṭantai
parantaṉṟu mātō paṇpukeḻu niṟaṉē" (21)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 31, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu vēṟpāṭukaṇṭu pāṅki aiyuṟṟatu.
3. "kaṇṇi takaiciṟan taṉavē taṇṇeṉ
pūntaḻai cevviya pōlum vāṅkiruṅ
kūntalu malkulum poliya
ēntiṉir koṇmiṉyām viḻaikuvam peritē" (22)
(nampi. 144, mēṟ; kaḷaviyaṟ. 29. mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉ irantu kuṟaiyuṟṟatu.
4. "vaikal vaika laiya vivvaḻik
kaiyuṟai yēntiṉir varutir maṟṟaḵ
taṟikalam yāmemark kuraippiṟ
peṟukuvi rentai peruvaṇ maiyaṉē" (23)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉait tōḻi viṉāyiṟṟu.
5. "0ampumukaṅ kiḻitta vempuṇ vāya
kalaimāṉ pōntaṉa vuḷavōnum
maṭamā ṉōkka marīiyiṉa paṭarntē" (24)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 30, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu kavarntuniṉṟa tōḻiyai matiyuṭampaṭuppāṉuṟṟut tōḻiyum talaimakaḷum uṭaṉāyavaḻit talaimakaṉ viṉāyatu.
6. "0uraimi ṉīrma ṉemakkē varaiyiṭai
arumpaṭark kavalai nīntip
peruntuya raruppam peyartaru neṟiyē" (25)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 30, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu vaḻiviṉāyatu.
7. "0kaiyatu ceyalaiyan taḻaiyē viṉāya
teypuṇ vāya māvē kaiviṭ
ṭakalā ṉammaviv vakaṉpuṉam
takaiyō ṉuḷḷiya taṟiyalam peritē" (26)
(nampi. 140, mēṟ; kaḷaviyaṟ. 33, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉait tōḻi aiyuṟṟatu.
8. "pūtta vēṅkai viyaṉciṉai yēṟi
mayiliṉa makavu nāṭaṉ
naṉṉutaṟ koṭicci maṉattakat tōṉē" (27)
(yā. vi. mēṟ; kaḷaviyaṟ. 31, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu tōḻi talaimakaṉait tuṇintatu.
9. "0naṇṇuta laritē taṇṇeṉ cāral
eṇṇiya kuṟaiyoṉ ṟuḷateṉiṉ
naṇṇātu muṭitti yaṇṇa ṉīyē" (28)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 33, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉaic cēṭpaṭa niṟuttatu.
10. "0vāṅkiruñ cilampiṉ vaṇṭa layarum
tēṅkamaḻ kūntaṉ makaḷiruḷ
yāṅkā kiyatō vēntaṉiṉ ṉaruḷē" (29)
(nampi. 144, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu aṟiyāḷ pōṉṟatu.
11. "0payilpūñ cāraṉiṉ paṇpuḷaṅ kavarnta
mayilēr cāyal vāṇutal
uyirō raṉṉa vaṉpiṉa ḷemakkē" (30)
eṉpatu aṟiyak kūṟiyatu.
12. "0kaṭikamaḻ kaṇṇi neṭuvarai yāyattu
vaṭivē laṇṇaṉī maṟaippiṉ
muṭiyā tāku muṉṉiya viṉaiyē" (31)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 32, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu maṟaiyēleṉṟatu.
13. "tūymai cāṉṟa tolkuṭit tōṉṟiya
vāymai nāviṉ matitaraṉ pōla
uyartava muṉivar cārap
peyarā nilaiyatip piṟaṅkuperu malaiyē" (32)
(nampi. 147, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu tōḻi talaimakaṉ kuṟiyātatu kūṟiyatu.
14. "0koṭuñcilai valatta raṭuntoḻiṟ cīṟṟat
teṉṉaiya rivvaḻi varukuvar
ceṉmati cilampa tīṅkutara luṭaittē" (33)
eṉpatu tōḻi paṭaittu moḻintatu.
15. "0alava ṉāṭṭalu māṭṭā ḷāyamoṭu
malarpūṅ kāṉal vaṇṭalu mayarāḷ
ōviyap pāvai yottaṉaḷ
yātuko laṇṇalyāṉ collu māṟē" (34)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 32, mēṟ.)
ituvumatu.
16. "peruvarai micaiyatu neṭuveḷ ḷaruvi
mutuvāyk kōṭiyar muḻaviṟ ṟatumpic
cilampi ṉiḻitaru milaṅkumalai veṟpa
nōtak kaṉṟē kāmam yāvatum
5. 	naṉṟeṉa vuṇarār māṭṭum
ceṉṟē niṟkum perumpē taimaittē" (kuṟun. 78)
ituvumatu.
17. "0vampa varaviṉi rākali ṉuṇarīr
empatat teḷiyaḷō maṭantai
impar vāḻkkai yiyavuṇmaṉ ṉemakkē" (35)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 33, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ ariyaḷeṉṟatu.
18. "0poṉṉiyal cuṇaṅkiṉ meṉmulai yarivaikku
miṉṉiva roḷivē laṇṇal
niṉṉuṟu viḻumaṅ kūṟumati nīyē" (36)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 32, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu niṉkuṟai nīyē kūṟeṉṟatu.
19. "0uḷatā lammavōr kuṟippē yāvatu
vaḷaiyēr muṉkai maṭantaitaṉ
muḷaiyeyiṟ ṟarumpiya mukiḻnakai tāṉē" (37)
eṉpatu nakaikoṇ ṭāṟṟal.
20. "0peṟutiṟa nāṭiṉai moḻimō peyvaḷai
naṟunutaṉ mātar nakāa
tuṟutuya ralla tuyyu māṟē" (38)
eṉpatu nakāturai yeṉṟatu.
21. "0tiruntā vāḻkkaic ciṟukuṭi yemakkup
peruntakai yaṇṇa ṉiṉṉiṭaip
poruntiya kēṇmaiyum puraivatō vaṉṟē" (39)
eṉpatu yām umakku iyaiyō meṉṟatu.
22. "0puyaṟkuḻaṉ maṭantaiyar pūṅkarum pulakkaiyiṉ
nakaikkavi ṉavāya vāmeṉa
avaikkunar mātō valaṅkukatir muttē (40)
eṉpatu kaiyuṟai yeḷimai kūṟiyatu.
23. "māṇeḻi laṇṇal vāṅkutum yāmē
cēṇuyar cilampil yāṅkaṇum
kāṇala maṉṉōvik kamaḻpūn taḻaiyē" (41)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 33, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu kaiyuṟai emakkut takāteṉṟatu.
24. "māmalaic cilampa mayilēr cāyaṟ
ṟēmoḻi nilaimai terintapiṉ
pūmeṉ ṟaṇṭaḻai koḷkuvaṉ purintē" (42)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 34, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ nilaimai yaṟintu koḷkuvaṉeṉṟatu.
25. "māveṉa maṭalu mūrpa pūveṉak
kuvimuki ḻerukkaṅ kaṇṇiyuñ cūṭupa
maṟuki ṉārkkavum paṭupa
piṟitu mākupa kāmaṅkāḻk koḷiṉē" (kuṟun. 17)
eṉpatu maṭalēṟuvaṉeṉṟatu.
26. "koḷḷali ramma varuḷiṉi rātaliṟ
puḷḷiṉ peruṅkiḷai yalaṟa
vaḷḷitiṉ virinta māppaṉai maṭalē" (43)
eṉpatu maṭalēṟṟu vilakkiyatu.
27. "eḷitō vamma voḷiyiḻai maṭantai
kiḷipurai kiḷaviyu naṭaiyum
iḷameṉ cāyalu meḻutumā ṟumakkē" (44)
ituvumatu
28. "naṉṉuta lāya mevvayi ṉiyaliṉum
eṉvaḻip paṭarkuvaṇ mātō
viṉmuṉai vāḻnar meṉmaṭa makaḷē." (45)
ituvumatu.
29. "aṭuntiṟal vēḻa makaṟṟi yemvayiṟ
kaṭuntuya roḻittaṉai yātaliṉ maṭantai
kaṇṇu mēṉiyum puraiyum
taṇṇaṟuṅ kuvaḷaiyoṭu taḻaitan tīmē" (46)
eṉpatu taḻaiyetirkōṭal.
30. "aṉṉā yoruvareṉ poymmoḻi nacaii
iṉṉu mivvaḻi varukuvar
eṉṉai yāmavark kiyampuva tiṉiyē" (47)
eṉpatu melitākac collik kuṟainayappittal.
31. "kāṇāy tōḻinam mēṉaṟ ṟaṇpuṉam
pēṇā maṉṉar peyarpuṟaṅ koṭutteṉa
vaivēṟ ṟāṉai vavvaliṟ
cevvāyp pāciṉaṅ kavarntukoṇ ṭaṉavē" (48)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 35, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu nāṇoṭu nīṅkal. (pāciṉam – kiḷi.)
32. "neruna livvaḻip peṭaipuṟan tarūum
alavaṉ ṟaṉṉaiyu meṉṉaiyu nōkki
nontaṉaṉ peyarnta tuṟaivaṉ
vantilaṉ mātō varuntumeṉ maṉaṉē" (49)
eṉpatu valitākac collik kuṟainayappittatu.
33. "tāṉum yāṉumeṉ ṟiruvē ṟilamē
yāṉu maṟiyāk karappiṉaḷ
māṉuṇ kaṇṇi varaiyara makaḷē" (50)
eṉpatu tōḻi taḻīikkoṇṭatu.
34. "tantōṉ mēṉāḷ ventiṟaṟ kaḷiṟṟiṉ
uṟutuya roḻittuyt tōṉē niṟaṉaḻintu
vāṭupa kollō maṭantai
kōṭuya raṭukkatta koyaṟkarun taḻaiyē" (51)
eṉpatu kaiyuṟai ēṟpittatu.
35. "tantunī yaḷitta taṇṭaḻai kāṇṭalum
vantaṉa ḷetirnta maṭantai neñcam
maṇmicai viḷaṅkiya vaḻuttūr matitaraṉ
nuṇṇiyaṟ paṉuva ṉuḻaiporu ṇuṉitta
5. vāymoḻi yamirta maṭuttavar maṉameṉa
āṉilai peṟṟaṉṟi yāṉaṟin tilaṉē" (52)
(nampi. 149, mēṟ. kaḷaviyaṟ. mēṟ.)
eṉpatu kaiyuṟai yetirntamai kūṟiṟṟu.
36. "taḻaikeḻu ciṉaiya paṉmaran tuvaṉṟiya
maḻaitavaḻ pūmpoḻil yāvarum
viḻaitakait tammavem viyaṉpuṉa maruṅkē" (53)
eṉpatu pakaṟkuṟiyiṭam uraittatu.
37. ceṅkaḷam paṭakkoṉ ṟavuṇart tēytta
ceṅkō lampiṟ ceṅkōṭṭi yāṉaik
kaḻaṟoṭic cēey kuṉṟam
kurutip pūviṉ kulaikkān taṭṭē. (kuṟun. 1)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷaik kuṟivayiṉ niṟuttip peyarntatu.
38. "pētuṟal vāḻiniṉ kātaṟ ṟōḻi
kaipuṉai kōlameṉa meypeṟ ṟaṉṟē
aiyam yāvatu miṉṟip

1. aṟattoṭu nilai

	īṉṟō ṭaṉvayiṟ kaittā yiyampalum

	kiḷanta tamarvayi ṉaṟṟāy kiḷattalum
	iḷaiyōṟ ketirtalum veḷippaṭai yeṉmaṉār.

1. "0tōḻi vāḻi mēṉā ḷoruvaṉ

orunal liṉṉuyi rāyiṉaḷ peritē"
eṉpatu cevili talaimakaḷai nāṭkōlañ ceytatu.

naṟunuta ṉīvip paṭartan tōṉē" (96)
eṉpatu 0cevili talaimakaḷaip puṉaintu 'yārō ivaḷai eytutaṟkuriya peruntakai' eṉat tōḻi aṟattoṭu niṉṟatu.
4. "0eyyā vuḷḷamō ṭiṉaiyal vāḻi
maitapu kaḻaṅkiṟ paṭṭatu muḷatē, yatutāṉ
maiyil kāṭciyiṉ vayaṅkuyir mayaṅkiya
teyva vāṇuru vākuta ṟeḷivē" (kaḷaviyaṟ. mēṟ.) (97)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ vēṟupāṭu kaṇṭu cevili viṉavak kaṭṭuvicci colliyatu.
5. "0aṟiyā ḷaṉṉai veṟimuṟai toṭaṅkum
evaṉā kiyarō neñcē piḻaiyāk
kulañcā loḻukkamu nāṇum
nalañcāl kaṟpu mivvuḻi yemakkē" (98)
eṉpatu cevili veṟi kālkoḷḷat talaimakaḷ iraṅkiyatu.
6. "vīyā marapi ṉorutiṟa ṉāṭi
āvayi ṉuraippi ṉevaṉō tōḻi
0nāma munnīr pōlak
kāma vennōy kaiyikan taṉṟē"
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ āṟṟāmai yuṇarnta tōḻi aṟattoṭu niṟṟal takkateṉṟatu.
7. "atuvu mākuva tuṇṭō katumeṉa
vañci nuṇṇiṭai maṭantainiṉ
neñcaṅ kāṇiya poymmoḻin taṉaṉē" (100)
eṉpatu āṟṟāḷāy niṉṟa talaimakaḷait tōḻi poymmoḻin taṉaṉeṉa āṟṟuvittatu.
8. "0aṉṉaik kimmaṟai yuṇartti nilamicai
maṉṉiya vāḻkkaiya mātali ṉuṇarttā
tiṉṉuyi riṉṉē viṭukēṉ
poṉṉēr mēṉi miṉṉiḻai yāṉē" (101)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ aṟattoṭunilai maṟuttatu.
9. "0aṉpu maṉṟē kaṟpu maṉṟē
naṉṟoṭu vanta voḻukkamu maṉṟē
āṟṟutal puriyi ṉallatu
kōṟṟoṭi mātar kūṟiya tiṟamē" (102)
eṉpatu tōḻi aṟattoṭunilai nayappikkak kūṟiyatu.
10. "0āṟṟēṉ ṟōḻi yāṉē yavarmalaip
pūttāḻ poḻilakan tuḻaiiya
kāṟṟē yāṉun tīṇṭalaṅ kaṭaiyē" (103)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ aṟattoṭunilai nayantatu.
11. "0taṇṇeṉ cāyaliva ḷuṇṇōy taṇiya
eṇṇiṉai koṭutti yāyiṉ
aṇṇa lākamu muṇṇumō paliyē" (104)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. mēṟ.)
eṉpatu veṟiyāṭṭiṉkaṇ vēlaṉmēl vaittut tōḻi aṟattoṭu niṉṟatu.
12. "0muḷaiyeyiṟu nirampā viḷamaip paruvat
toḷitika ḻoruva ṉīṭṭiya
taḷaiyaviḻ kaṇṇi tāṅkiṉa ḷuvantē" (105)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 49, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu cevili viṉavat tōḻi aṟattoṭu niṉṟatu.
13. "0kayañcāṉ maṟucuḻi yaḻuntiṉa māka
muyaṅki ṉaṉeṭutta vaṇṇalai
vayaṅkiṇark kōtai maṟavalaḷ peritē" (106)
ituvum atu.
14. "0mutukkuṟain taṉaḷē mutukkuṟain taṉaḷē
malaiya ṉoḷvēṟ kaṇṇi
mulaiyum vārāṇ mutukkuṟain taṉaḷē" (107)
(tol. akat. 3, na; yā. vi. 32, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu cevili naṟṟāykku aṟattoṭu niṉṟatu.
15. "0iḷaiya ḷamma tāṉē kiḷaiyum
vaḷaipayil porutiraik kaṭalkaṇ ṭaṉaittē
puḷḷu nāḷum piṟavum
teḷḷiti ṉuṇartal periyōrkkuk kaṭaṉē" (108)
eṉpatu varaivu maṟuttatu.
16. "0iḷivaru cintaiyaṉ maṉṉē namvayiṉ
oḷitikaḻ peruntaḻai yīnta
aḷitikaḻ neñci ṉaruḷi ṉāṉē" (109)
eṉpatu varaivu maṟuttavaḻit talaimakaḷ āṟṟāḷāyiṟṟu.
17. "varuntiṉai vāḻi tōḻi nantamar
tiruntilai neṭuvē laṇṇalai
virumpiṉar mātō voḻikaniṉ melivē" (110)
eṉpatu varaivuṇarnta tōḻi talaimakaḷai āṟṟuvittatu.
18. "kalantāṅ kiḷamulaik kaṟpuṭai maṭantaiyaik
kulañcā loḻukka nōkki
nalañcā laṇṇaṟku nērvatu naṭaiyē" (111)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 49, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu naṟṟāy tamarkku aṟattoṭu niṉṟatu.
19. "0vāṉṟōy tolkuṭi marapu mavvaḻic
cāṉṟī rumatu varavu nōkki
nērntaṉa mallativ vulakam
mūṉṟuṭaṉ peṟiṉu muṭivatō vaṉṟē" (112)
eṉpatu varaivetir koṇṭatu. (22)
2. uṭaṉ celavu
23.	 kaiyaṭai makiḻcci kaṇṭōr kūṟṟē
	naivuṭaic cevili naṟṟāy moḻiyē
	celcurat tiraṅkal kaṇṭōr teḷittal
	ilvayiṟ peyarta liḷaiyōr celavē
5. 	aṟintaṉaṉ maṟaita layalōr moḻital
	peyarntaṉar varutal peruṅkiḷai makiḻvē
	tāyar kūṟṟē talaimakaṉ moḻiyō
	ṭāyiṉa piṟavu muṭaṉcela vākum.
eṉpatu uṭaṉ celavāmāṟu uṇarttutal nutaliṟṟu.
(i – ḷ.) kaiyaṭai mutalākat talaimakaṉ kūṟṟu īṟākac collappaṭṭaṉa varaivu maṟuttuḻiyākiya uṭaṉcelavām. e – ṟu.
1. "ivaḷē niṉṉala tilaḷē yāyum
kuvaḷai yuṇka ṇivaḷala tilaḷē
yāṉu māyiṭai yēṉē
māmalai nāṭa maṟavā tīmē" (113)
(tol. akat. 39, na. mēṟ.; iṟai. 23, mēṟ.; nampi, 182, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu kaiyaṭai.
2. "mella mella0niṉ ṉallaṭi yāṟṟi
varuntā tēkumati maṭavōy
iruntaṇ poḻilayāñ cellu māṟē" (114)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 51, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉ makiḻccik kiḷavi.
3. "uvantaṉai kāṇṭiyē neñcē mēṉāḷ
ivarntaṉai ceṉṟu meytāk
karuṅkuḻaṉ mātar mayiliyaṟ kaviṉē" (115)
ituvumatu.
4. "0putuppūṅ kōtai puṉainteḻi lākamum
	katuppu nīviniṉ ciṟupuṟaṅ kāṇkeṉa
	muṉcela viṭuppa nāṇip piṉpiṉ
	tāḻntaṉa ḷotuṅka yāṉun tāḻtaliṉ
	vaikiṉa mātō neñcē
	paiviri yalkuloṭu paḻuvat tāṉē" (116)
ituvumatu.
5. "villōṉ kālaṉa kaḻalē toṭiyōḷ
	mellaṭi mēlavuñ cilampē nallōr
	yārko laḷiyar tāmē yāriyar
	kayiṟāṭu paṟaiyiṟ kālporak kalaṅki
5. 	vākai veṇṇeṟ ṟolikkum
	vēypayi laḻuva muṉṉi yōrē" (kuṟun. 7)
eṉpatu curattetirppaṭṭār colliyatu.
6. "0ceṅkatirc celvaṉu mattañ cērntaṉaṉ
koṅkivar kuḻalō ṭivvaḻit
taṅkiṉai ceṉmō takaivey yōṉē" (117)
(kaḷaviyaṟ. 51, mēṟ.)
ituvumatu.
7. "0neṭuntērk kāḷaiyo ṭōrāṅ kēkik
	kaṭuñcuran tītiṉ ṟiṟantaṉa ḷavaḷeṉak
	kūṟumi ṉantaṇir toḻutaṉa ṉummē
	tēyvuṟu matiyoṭu kalaṅkum
5. 	yāyu māyamu miruntuyar keṭavē" (118)
eṉpatu etirkaṇṭārkkut talaimakaḷ colliyatu.
8. "vaṇṭār kōtai varivaḷait tōḷiyaip
paṇṭā ṭiṭaṅkaḷuṭ kāṇā tiṉṉum
nillā vuṭampoṭu keḻīi
niṉṟaṉai kollō vāḻiya neñcē" (119)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷaik kāṇātu cevili iraṅkiyatu.
9. "valiyai maṉṟa nīyē polivaḷai
kātaṟ ṟōḻi kaiyakaṉ ṟoḻiyavum
pētuṟa lilaiyā luyiroṭum puṇarntē" (120)
eṉpatu tōḻiyai nōkkic cevili colliyatu.
10. "0cūṉmutir koṇmū miṉṉupu poḻiyak
kāṉaṅ kaṭumai nīṅkuka
māṉuṇ kaṇṇi pōkiya curaṉē" (121)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ celavuṇarntu naṟṟāy iraṅkiyatu.
11. "maṟuvi ṟūvic ciṟukaruṅ kākkai
	aṉpuṭai marapi ṉiṉkiḷai vaḷarppap
	paccūṉ peyta pacuntiṉaip puṉkam
	polampuṉai kalattiṟ ṟarukuvaṉ mātō
5. 	veñciṉa viṟalvēṟ kāḷaiyo
	ṭañci lōtiyai varakkarain tīmē" (aiṅkuṟu. 391)
ituvumatu.
12. "nilantoṭṭup pukāar vāṉa mēṟār
	ilaṅkiru munnīr kāliṟ cellār
	nāṭṭi ṉāṭṭi ṉūri ṉūriṟ
	kuṭimuṟai kuṭimuṟai teriyiṟ
5. 	keṭunaru muḷarōnaṅ kāta lōrē" (kuṟun. 130)
eṉpatu naṟṟāykkuc collic cevili piṉ ceṉṟatu.
13. "0veyyōṉ cāpat teykaṇai kuḷippa
vīḻntatu maṉṟavik kaḷiṟē tāḻnta
iṭumut taṇivaṭañ cuṭarat
toṭiyō ḷotuṅkiya cūḻaṉmaṉ ṉituvē" (122)
eṉpatu curattiṭaic ceṉṟa cevili iraṅkiyatu.
14. "kāyntilai maṟavai maṉṉō vēntiḻai
aḻalkeḻu veñcurañ celavum
eḻilkeḻu pāvai yēntiya kuravē" (123)
ituvumatu.
15. "0oṇṭiṟa laṇṇalō ṭoḷiyiḻai maṭantaiyai
nuṇpuri mārpi ṉantaṇir
kaṇṭaṉir piṟavō kaṭuñcurañ celavē"
eṉpatu cevili iṭaiccurattu varuvārai viṉāyiṟṟu.
16. "0maṭavaraṉ mātarkku varuntā tīmō
kaṭalvayiṟu payanta nittilam
uṭaiyōrk kāta lulakiyal vaḻakkē" (125)
eṉpatu ceviliyai iṭaiccurattuk kaṇṭōr teruṭṭiyatu.
17. "eṉṉu muḷḷiṉaḷ kollō taṉṉai
neñcuṇat tēṟṟiya vañciṉak kāḷaiyo
ṭaḻuṅkaṉ mūtū raḻuṅkac
ceḻumpal kuṉṟa miṟantaveṉ makaḷē" (aiṅkuṟu. 372)
eṉpatu cevili maṉaivayiṟ peyarntatu.
18. "nāṇkāl vāṅkiya cilaiyar cīṟṟamo
ṭīṅkiva ṭaṉṉaiyar varukuvar
yāṅkā kuvarivar vaḷaiyuṅ kālē" (126)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ tamar piṉcellak kaṇṭār colliyatu.
19. "0ayilē rampiṉar niṉṉaiyar kayilvaḷai
payilpūñ cuṉaiyi ṉīḻal
mayilēr cāyaṉ maṟaikuvaṉ ciṟitē" (127)
eṉpatu talaimakaḷ tamarātal aṟintu talaimakaṉ maṟaintatu.
20. 	"varicilai yiṭavayi ṉēntik kaṇaiterin
	teriyumiḻ kaṇṇiṉa rivarē ñāṅkar
	āyiḻai maṭantai tamareṉa maṟainta
	cēyuyar celvaṉuñ ciṉavu māyiṭai
5. 	aṟaṉaṉ ṟituveṉa vuraiit
	tiṟaṉaṟi māntarait teruṭṭutu nāmē" (128)
eṉpatu iṭaiccurattuk kaṇṭār colliyatu.
21. "ivaḷē, nalamēm paṭṭa vaṉpiṉa ḷivaḷē
kulamēm paṭṭa cāṉṟōr makaṉē
iruvar taṉmaiyu nōkkip
paruvara leytātu koṭuttalō paṇpē" (129)
ituvumatu.
22. "perumpaṭa ruḻakkuṅ kiḷaimakiḻ tūṅkat
tirunteḻi lalku laṉpoṭu
viyaṉperun tolpatip pukukanām viraintē" (130)
eṉpatu peyarntaṉar varutal.
23. "vaḷaṅkeḻu kāṉam varukuva riṉṟeṉa
viḷampumiṉ viraintaṉir celiṉē
uḷaṅkeḻu kāta luyiraṉ ṉōrkkē" (131)
eṉpatu āyattārkkuk kūṟumiṉeṉṟatu.
24. "māṉatar mayaṅkiya malaimutaṟ ciṟuneṟi
tāṉvaru meṉpa taṭameṉ ṟōḷi
añciṉa ḷañciṉa ḷotuṅkip
pañci mellaṭi paralvaṭuk koḷavē" (132)
(tol. akat. 42, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu varavuṇarntu kiḷai makiḻntatu.
25. "0mutuvāy vēla moḻintici ṉemakkē
matuvār kūntaṉ maṭantaiyaik
katirvē laṇṇal karutiya tiṟaṉē" (133)
eṉpatu vēlaṉaic cevilittāy viṉāyatu.
26. 	"tuṟantataṟ koṇṭun tuyaraṭac cāay
	aṟampulantu paḻikku marunavai yāṭṭi
	evva neñciṟ kēma māka
	vantaṉa ḷōniṉ makaḷē
5. 	ventiṟal veṉvēl viṭalaimun tuṟavē" (aiṅkuṟu. 393.)
eṉpatu cevilittāy naṟṟāyakkuc colliyatu.
27. "aṟaṉaṉ ṟamma tāṉē niṟaiyaḻin
tāḻtuya ravala muḻakkum
kātaṟ ṟāykkuk kāṭṭiṉai vammē" (134)
eṉpatu talaimakaṟkut talaimakaṉ naṟṟāy colliyatu.
28. "nummaṉaic cilampu kaḻīi yayariṉum
	emmaṉai vatuvai naṉmaṇaṅ kaḻika
	eṉṟunāñ colli ṉevaṉō tōḻi
	maiyaṟa viḷaṅkuṅ kaḻalaṭip
5. 	poyval kāḷaiyai yīṉṟa tāykkē" (aiṅkuṟu. 396)
eṉpatu talaimakaṇaṟṟāy cevilikkuc colliyatu.
29. 	"putuvatu puṉainta vatuvaik kōlamoṭu
	mutuvāyp peṇṭir parāyiṉa rēttak
	kaṭimaṉai yemmoṭu pukuntōy mēṉāḷ
	aṟitalu maṟitiyō maṭantai
	kaṟivaḷar cāraṟ kuṟivayi ṉāṉē" (135)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉ talaimakaṭkuc colliyatu. (23)
3. cēyiṭaip pirivu
24. pirivakai yuṇarttalum pāṅki kūṟa
	uritiṉi ṉaḻuṅkalu maḻuṅkāk kālai
	aṟikuva ṉeṉṟalu maṟiyak kūṟalum
	naṟunuta liṉaitalu nayavarak kūṟalum
5.	 teriyiḻai nērtaluñ celavuṭaṉ mayaṅkalum
	vantatu poḻuteṉa varuntalun tōḻi
	nontaṉa ḷuraippavu nōvāḷ kūṟalum
	āṟṟu vittalu māyiṭai varuvōṉ
	mēṉavuñ cēyiṭaip piriveṉak koḷalē.
eṉpatu cēyiṭaip pirivāmāṟu uṇarttutal nutaliṟṟu.
(i – ḷ.) pirivuṇarttutal mutalākap peyarntōṉ kūṟṟu īṟākac collappaṭṭaṉa cēyiṭaip pirivā meṉṟavāṟu.
1. "aṟaṉu mīkaiyu maṉpuṅ kiḷaiyum
pukaḻum pōkamun tarutaliṟ puṟampeyarntu
taruvatu tuṇintaṉam peritē
viripūṅ kōtai viḷaṅkiḻai poruḷē" (136)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉ tōḻikkup pirivu uṇarttiyatu.
2. "evaṉpala moḻikō yāṉē yaṉpa
akaṉṟivaṭ pirinta vannilai
nikaḻntaṉ ṟamma nīṅkuta luyirē" (137)
eṉpatu tōḻi talaimakaḷ nilaimai kūṟic celavaḻuṅkuvittatu.
3. "yāṉoṉṟu kūṟuva laiya vāṉam
nilampaka vaṟanta pulampuṟu cēṇiṭaic
ceyviṉai nayantaṉi rāyi ṉavviṉaik
kammā varivaiyu māṟṟumō naṭaiyē" (138)
ituvumatu.
4. "āṟucel varuttañ cīṟaṭi civappavum
ciṉai nīṅku taḷiriṉ vaṇṇam vāṭavum
tāṉvara ṟuṇinta vivaḷiṉu mivaḷoṭu
vēypayi laḻuva muvakkumeṉ
pētai neñcam peruntakai yuṭaittē" (139)
(tol. akat. 41, mēṟ; nampi. 182, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu talaimakaṉ neñciṟkuc collic celavaḻuṅkuvittatu.
5. "marunteṉiṉ maruntē vaippeṉiṉ vaippē
arumpiya cuṇaṅki ṉampakaṭ ṭiḷamulaip
peruntō ṇuṇukiya nucuppiṟ
kalkeḻu kāṉavar nalkuṟu makaḷē" (kuṟun. 71)
ituvumatu.
6. "kuṟittatai yituveṉa neṟippaṭak kūṟi
avaṇilai yaṟintiyāṉ moḻintapiṉ vaḻāatu
poṉpuṉai neṭuntēr pūṇkaniṉ māvē" (140)
eṉpatu aḻuṅkātu celavu kuṟitta talaimakaṉukkut tōḻi colliyatu.
7. "aṟiyēṉ ṟōḻiyavar neñcukuṟit tatuvē
maṟipuṇar māṉpiṇai kalaiyoṭu maruvap
parivuṟu mukatta rāki
orumuṟai yaṉṟi nōkkiṉar peritē" (141)
eṉpatu tōḻi talaimakaṭkuk kuṟippiṉāṟ pirivuṇarttiyatu.
8. "varampil kēḷvip perumpeyarp paṉuval
patiṉeṇ moḻiyiṟ payaṉkoḷa virikkum
matipurai kēḷvi matiyōr nacaiip
pirivarnaṅ kātalar piṟapulam paṭarntē" (142)
eṉpatu 0kalvi viḷakkuvāṉ pirivareṉa veḷippaṭa uṇarttiyatu.
9. "māyiru ñālat tuyirtalai yaḷikkum
kāval vēṇṭiṉar kātalar
āyiḻai maṭantai yāṟṟutal kaṭaṉē" (143)
eṉpatu kāttaṟkup pirivareṉa uṇarttiyatu.
10. "mīḷā veṉṟi yiruperu vēntarai
vāḷama raḻuvam peyarkkum
āḷviṉaik kakalvarnam maṉpi ṉōrē" (144)
eṉpatu pakaitaṇippāṉ pirivareṉa uṇarttiyatu. 
11. "velperun tāṉai vēntaṉ pakaimicaic
celkuvar mātō kātalar
alliyaṅ kōtai yaḻuṅkalō voḻiyē" (145)
eṉpatu vēntaṟ kuṟṟuḻip pirivareṉavuṇarttiyatu.
12. "nāḷu nāḷu māḷviṉai yaḻuṅka
illiruntu makiḻvōrk killaiyāṟ pukaḻeṉa
oṇporuṭ kakalvarnaṅ kātalar
kaṇpaṉi tuyaroḻi tōḻi nīyē"
(ciṟṟaṭṭakam; nampi. 251, mēṟ.)
eṉpatu poruḷvayiṟ pirivareṉa uṇarttiyatu.
13. "0cēṟala reṉpa tuḷateṉi ṉuraimati
māṟiṉar valvara vuraippiṉ
ūṟiṉ ṟivvaḻi vāḻvōrk kuraiyē" (146)
eṉpatu pirivareṉpatu kēṭṭa talaimakaḷ āṟṟāḷāyt tōḻikkuc colliyatu.
14. 	"nīrvār kaṇṇai nīyiva ṇoḻiya
	yārē pirikiṟ pavarē cāraṟ
	cilampaṇi koṇṭa valañcuri marāattu
	vēṉi lañciṉai kamaḻum
5. 	tēmū coṇṇuta ṉiṉṉoṭuñ celavē" (kuṟun. 22)
eṉpatu āṟṟāḷāya talaimakaṭkut tōḻi pirivu nayappac colliyatu.
15. "nallicai niṟuttal vēṇṭit tuṇaipirintu
celvatu kātalar kaṭaṉē ceṉṟeṉa
olkiya vuḷḷamoṭu pulavā
tillirun tāṟṟutal kaṟpiṉa tiyalpē" (147)
ituvumatu.

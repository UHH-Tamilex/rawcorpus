nūṉmukam

“ellāmē ēṟkeṉavē collappaṭṭavai tām;
āṉāl yārum kaṇṭukoḷvatillai; eṉavē nām
avvappoḻutu niṉaivūṭṭa vēṇṭiyuḷḷatu”.

“Everything has been said already, but as no one listens, we must always begin again”
- antrē kaiṭu (Andre Gide)

tamiḻukku maṟakkoṇāta aruntoṇṭu āṟṟiya airōppiya - amerikka aṟiñarkaḷ palarāvar. avarkaḷil perumpālōruṭaiya arumpaṇikaḷ 1800-1980 kāla aḷavil nikaḻntavai. kuṟippiṭattakka mutaṉmai vāyntavarkaḷ 50kku mēṟpaṭṭavarkaḷaip paṟṟiyatu ip pakuti -- iṉṟum vāḻntu paṇi ceytu varupavarkaḷ cilar uṭpaṭa.

2. ovvoruvarum ovvoru vakaiyil tamiḻukkuc ceyta paṇiyil ciṟantavarkaḷ tām. eṉiṉum cilar paṇikaḷ vēṟu evarum ceytirukka iyalāta arumpaṇikaḷ; vittakarkaḷ kālṭuvel, pōp, hīrās, lakōvāri, vīramāmuṉivar, parō, emeṉō, viṉculō, kirāl, cuvalepil, hārṭ, leviṭ, hakōlā, cālek pōṉṟavarkaḷ. ivarkaḷaip paṟṟiya ceytikaḷaic caṟṟu virivākat terintu koḷvatu tamiḻiṉ, tamiḻariṉ nalaṉukku ukantatu ākaliṉ avvāṟē tarap paṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ēṉaiyōr arumpaṇikaḷum curuṅkak kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

ceyyāmaṟ ceyta utavikku vaiyakamum
vāṉakamum āṟṟal aritu

eṉṟa kuṟaḷai niṉaivu kūrka.

3. (i) “veṟum kāla muṟaippaṭiyō, allatu veṟum akara varicaippaṭiyō ivvaṟiñarkaḷai niralpaṭattuvataiviṭa enta vakaippaṇiyil īṭillāta paṅkaḷippuc ceytaṉar eṉṟa aṭippaṭaiyil ceyvatu nalameṉak karuti avvāṟē ceyyap paṭṭuḷḷatu.

(ii) mutal 16 aṟiñarkaḷ tamiḻiṉ toṉmai, muṉmai, teṉmai, ñālamutaṉ moḻikku ataṉ nerukkam ākiyavaṟṟai niṟuviyavarkaḷ; talaiciṟanta varalāṟṟu - oppiyal moḻiyiyal vallunarkaḷ; mānta iṉat toṉmai nākarikaṅkaḷ – moḻi kaḷukkum tamiḻukkum iṭaiyil uḷḷa nerukkattai niṟuviyavarkaḷ.

(iii) aṭuttu varupavarkaḷ paṇikaḷaip perumaḷavukku piṉvarumāṟu vakaippaṭuttalām:

a) tamiḻ, tamiḻ-piṟamoḻi, piṟamoḻi - tamiḻ eṉṟavāṟu ciṟanta akara mutalikaḷait tokuttamai (pepriciyas, viṉculō pōṉṟavarkaḷ)

(ā) tamiḻ ilakkiyattil tōyntu, ataṉ ciṟappai ēṉaic cemmoḻi ilakkiyaṅkaḷ, nam kālattil mutal nilai vakikkum moḻikaḷiṉ ilakkiyaṅkaḷ, ākiyavaṟṟōṭu oppiṭṭu tamiḻ ilakkiyaṅkaḷiṉ ciṟappaiyum cīrmaiyaiyum viḷakkutal. (jārj el. hārṭ, ē.kē. irāmāṉujaṉ, cuvelapil, kurō, takakaṣi)

(i) ciṟanta tamiḻnūlkaḷai (ilakkiyaṅkaḷ, ilakkaṇaṅkaḷ) āṅkilattilum piṟamoḻikaḷilum tiṟampaṭa moḻipeyarttup paṉṉāṭṭaṟiñarukku uṇarttiyamai; (vīramāmuṉivar, kirāl, pētāṉ)

u) tamiḻ nūl paṭṭiyal tokuttōr (kirikari jēms, villiyam ṭēlar.)  

4. poruḷaṭakkam 62 pirivukaḷāka amaintuḷḷatu. eṉiṉum ēṟattāḻa nūṟu aṟiñarkaḷaip paṟṟiya ceytikaḷ uḷḷaṉa. oru cilaraip poṟuttavaraiavaravarukkut taṉip pirivu taruvataṟkāṉa vāyppiṉmaiyiṉ avaravar ceytavakaip paṇiyil muṉṉaṉiyil niṟpōr paṟṟiya piriviṉ kīḻ aṉṉōr paṟṟiya takavalkaḷum curukkamākat tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

5. takka ātāra nūlkaḷ, itaḻkaḷ aṭippaṭaiyil innūl uruvākkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. antanta aṟiñaraip paṟṟi mēlum aṟintu koḷḷa virumpuvōr paṭittaṟkuriya nūlkaḷ, kaṭṭurai kaḷ uriya iṭaṅkaḷil tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivvaṟiñarkaḷ aṉaivaraip paṟṟip potuvākavō antanta nāṭṭai / camayakkuḻuvai / amaippaip paṟṟiyō aṟiya virumpuvōr pārvaikkāka piṉvarum curukkamāṉa nūṟpaṭṭiyalum tarappaṭukiṟatu.
6. “tamiḻukkut toṇṭu ceyta” eṉṟu innūliṉ talaippu curukkamāka iruppiṉum, tamiḻukku, tamiḻiyattukku tamiḻaka makkaḷukkup pala tuṟaikaḷilum toṇṭāṟṟiya palanūṟu piṟanāṭṭuc cāṉṟōrkaḷil mutaṉmai vāynta cilaraip paṟṟi maṭṭumē innūlil ōraḷavu kuṟippiṭa iyaṉṟatu. innūlai veḷiyiṭum tamiḻmaṇ patippaka iḷavaḻakaṉārukku eṉṟum naṉṟiyuṭaiyēṉ.

skāṭlāntu cārnta ivar peṟṟōr ayarlāntil cila āṇṭukaḷ vacittapoḻutu aṅku piṟanta ivar, peṟṟōr tāynāṭu tirumpiyapoḻutu tāmum skāṭlāntu pōntu kiḷāskō nakaril vacittār.

2. tamiḻiya (tirāviṭa) moḻikaḷiṉ oppilakkaṇattai 1856il eḻutit tamiḻiṉ tamiḻariṉ maṟumalarccikku vittiṭṭavar, (iraṇṭāvatu patippu 1875). iṉṟuḷḷa moḻikaḷil tamiḻum tamiḻiya moḻikkuṭumpamumē māntaṉ mutaṉmoḻikku mika neruṅkiyatāyirukkalām eṉṟa muṭivai 1856lēyē tēṟṟamākak kuṟippiṭṭavar. tamiḻaik karutum poḻutu kālṭuvelaiyum karutiyāka vēṇṭum eṉṟa perumita nilaiyil uḷḷavar.

3. tamatu irupatām vayatil laṇṭaṉ viṭaiyūḻiyar caṅkattil cērntār. kiḷāskō palkalaik kaḻakattil (accaṅkam aṉuppiya) māṇavarākac cērntu īrāṇṭu payiṉṟu pi.ē .B.A. paṭṭam peṟṟār. iyalpākavē kālṭuvel nāṭṭam koṇṭirunta moḻikaḷiṉ oppāyvu Comparative Philology eṉṉum tuṟaiyil mēlum āḻamāka avarai īrttavar avaruṭaiya kirīk pērāciriyar tāṉiyēl cāṉṭpōrṭu āvār. “nām vēṟṟumoḻikaḷ pēcum nāṭukaḷukkuc ceṉṟāl annāṭṭu moḻikaḷaiyum, oppāyvu ceytu moḻiyiyal tuṟaikku vaḷam cērkka vēṇṭum” eṉṟu appoḻutē cūḷuraittuk koṇṭatāka avar eḻutiyuḷḷār (“Caldwell Reminiscences” by J.L. Wyatt; 18987)  tamiḻiṉ, tamiḻariṉ nallūḻ, kāraṇamāka kālṭuvel tamiḻnāṭṭil paṇi ceyya aṉuppap paṭṭār. mūṉṟu āṇṭukaḷ ceṉṉaiyil taṅkiya piṉṉar tirunelvēliyil iṭaiyaṉ kuṭikku avar iṟai paṇiyāṟṟac ceṉṟār. avar ceṉṉaiyil vāḻntapoḻutu ēṟkaṉavē tamiḻppaṇiyilum tamiḻāyvilum īṭupaṭṭirunta pōp, turu, viṉslō, pavar (Bower) ākiyōroṭu paḻakit tam tamiḻaṟivaip perukkik koṇṭār. piṉṉar iṭaiyaṉ kuṭiyil maṟaippaṇi āṟṟat toṭaṅkiya piṉṉarum moḻiyiyalārāycciyil iṭaiyaṟātu kaṭumaiyāka 15 āṇṭukaḷukku mēl uḻaittut tamatu ariya oppilakkaṇa nūlai veḷiyiṭṭār.

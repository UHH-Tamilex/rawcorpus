nūṉmukam

“ellāmē ēṟkeṉavē collappaṭṭavi tām;
āṉāl yārum kaṇṭukoḷvatilli; eṉavē nām
avvappoḻutu niṉivūṭṭa vēṇṭiyuḷḷatu”.

“Everything has been sid already, but as no one listens, we must always begin agin”
- antrē kiṭu (Andre Gide)

tamiḻukku maṟakkoṇāta aruntoṇṭu āṟṟiya airōppiya - amerikka aṟiñarkaḷ palarāvar. avarkaḷil perumpālōruṭiya arumpaṇikaḷ 1800-1980 kāla aḷavil nikaḻntavi. kuṟippiṭattakka mutaṉmi vāyntavarkaḷ 50kku mēṟpaṭṭavarkaḷip paṟṟiyatu ip pakuti -- iṉṟum vāḻntu paṇi ceytu varupavarkaḷ cilar uṭpaṭa.

2. ovvoruvarum ovvoru vakiyil tamiḻukkuc ceyta paṇiyil ciṟantavarkaḷ tām. eṉiṉum cilar paṇikaḷ vēṟu evarum ceytirukka iyalāta arumpaṇikaḷ; vittakarkaḷ kālṭuvel, pōp, hīrās, lakōvāri, vīramāmuṉivar, parō, emeṉō, viṉculō, kirāl, cuvalepil, hārṭ, leviṭ, hakōlā, cālek pōṉṟavarkaḷ. ivarkaḷip paṟṟiya ceytikaḷic caṟṟu virivākat terintu koḷvatu tamiḻiṉ, tamiḻariṉ nalaṉukku ukantatu ākaliṉ avvāṟē tarap paṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ēṉiyōr arumpaṇikaḷum curuṅkak kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

ceyyāmaṟ ceyta utavikku viyakamum
vāṉakamum āṟṟal aritu

eṉṟa kuṟaḷi niṉivu kūrka.

3. (i) “veṟum kāla muṟippaṭiyō, allatu veṟum akara varicippaṭiyō ivvaṟiñarkaḷi niralpaṭattuvativiṭa enta vakippaṇiyil īṭillāta paṅkaḷippuc ceytaṉar eṉṟa aṭippaṭiyil ceyvatu nalameṉak karuti avvāṟē ceyyap paṭṭuḷḷatu.

(ii) mutal 16 aṟiñarkaḷ tamiḻiṉ toṉmi, muṉmi, teṉmi, ñālamutaṉ moḻikku ataṉ nerukkam ākiyavaṟṟi niṟuviyavarkaḷ; taliciṟanta varalāṟṟu - oppiyal moḻiyiyal vallunarkaḷ; mānta iṉat toṉmi nākarikaṅkaḷ – moḻi kaḷukkum tamiḻukkum iṭiyil uḷḷa nerukkatti niṟuviyavarkaḷ.

(iii) aṭuttu varupavarkaḷ paṇikaḷip perumaḷavukku piṉvarumāṟu vakippaṭuttalām:

a) tamiḻ, tamiḻ-piṟamoḻi, piṟamoḻi - tamiḻ eṉṟavāṟu ciṟanta akara mutalikaḷit tokuttami (pepriciyas, viṉculō pōṉṟavarkaḷ)

(ā) tamiḻ ilakkiyattil tōyntu, ataṉ ciṟappi ēṉic cemmoḻi ilakkiyaṅkaḷ, nam kālattil mutal nili vakikkum moḻikaḷiṉ ilakkiyaṅkaḷ, ākiyavaṟṟōṭu oppiṭṭu tamiḻ ilakkiyaṅkaḷiṉ ciṟappiyum cīrmiyiyum viḷakkutal. (jārj el. hārṭ, ē.kē. irāmāṉujaṉ, cuvelapil, kurō, takakaṣi)

(i) ciṟanta tamiḻnūlkaḷi (ilakkiyaṅkaḷ, ilakkaṇaṅkaḷ) āṅkilattilum piṟamoḻikaḷilum tiṟampaṭa moḻipeyarttup paṉṉāṭṭaṟiñarukku uṇarttiyami; (vīramāmuṉivar, kirāl, pētāṉ)

u) tamiḻ nūl paṭṭiyal tokuttōr (kirikari jēms, villiyam ṭēlar.)  

4. poruḷaṭakkam 62 pirivukaḷāka amintuḷḷatu. eṉiṉum ēṟattāḻa nūṟu aṟiñarkaḷip paṟṟiya ceytikaḷ uḷḷaṉa. oru cilarip poṟuttavariavaravarukkut taṉip pirivu taruvataṟkāṉa vāyppiṉmiyiṉ avaravar ceytavakip paṇiyil muṉṉaṉiyil niṟpōr paṟṟiya piriviṉ kīḻ aṉṉōr paṟṟiya takavalkaḷum curukkamākat tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

5. takka ātāra nūlkaḷ, itaḻkaḷ aṭippaṭiyil innūl uruvākkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. antanta aṟiñarip paṟṟi mēlum aṟintu koḷḷa virumpuvōr paṭittaṟkuriya nūlkaḷ, kaṭṭuri kaḷ uriya iṭaṅkaḷil tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivvaṟiñarkaḷ aṉivarip paṟṟip potuvākavō antanta nāṭṭi / camayakkuḻuvi / amippip paṟṟiyō aṟiya virumpuvōr pārvikkāka piṉvarum curukkamāṉa nūṟpaṭṭiyalum tarappaṭukiṟatu.
6. “tamiḻukkut toṇṭu ceyta” eṉṟu innūliṉ talippu curukkamāka iruppiṉum, tamiḻukku, tamiḻiyattukku tamiḻaka makkaḷukkup pala tuṟikaḷilum toṇṭāṟṟiya palanūṟu piṟanāṭṭuc cāṉṟōrkaḷil mutaṉmi vāynta cilarip paṟṟi maṭṭumē innūlil ōraḷavu kuṟippiṭa iyaṉṟatu. innūli veḷiyiṭum tamiḻmaṇ patippaka iḷavaḻakaṉārukku eṉṟum naṉṟiyuṭiyēṉ.

skāṭlāntu cārnta ivar peṟṟōr ayarlāntil cila āṇṭukaḷ vacittapoḻutu aṅku piṟanta ivar, peṟṟōr tāynāṭu tirumpiyapoḻutu tāmum skāṭlāntu pōntu kiḷāskō nakaril vacittār.

2. tamiḻiya (tirāviṭa) moḻikaḷiṉ oppilakkaṇatti 1856il eḻutit tamiḻiṉ tamiḻariṉ maṟumalarccikku vittiṭṭavar, (iraṇṭāvatu patippu 1875). iṉṟuḷḷa moḻikaḷil tamiḻum tamiḻiya moḻikkuṭumpamumē māntaṉ mutaṉmoḻikku mika neruṅkiyatāyirukkalām eṉṟa muṭivi 1856lēyē tēṟṟamākak kuṟippiṭṭavar. tamiḻik karutum poḻutu kālṭuveliyum karutiyāka vēṇṭum eṉṟa perumita niliyil uḷḷavar.

3. tamatu irupatām vayatil laṇṭaṉ viṭiyūḻiyar caṅkattil cērntār. kiḷāskō palkalik kaḻakattil (accaṅkam aṉuppiya) māṇavarākac cērntu īrāṇṭu payiṉṟu pi.ē .B.A. paṭṭam peṟṟār. iyalpākavē kālṭuvel nāṭṭam koṇṭirunta moḻikaḷiṉ oppāyvu Comparative Philology eṉṉum tuṟiyil mēlum āḻamāka avari īrttavar avaruṭiya kirīk pērāciriyar tāṉiyēl cāṉṭpōrṭu āvār. “nām vēṟṟumoḻikaḷ pēcum nāṭukaḷukkuc ceṉṟāl annāṭṭu moḻikaḷiyum, oppāyvu ceytu moḻiyiyal tuṟikku vaḷam cērkka vēṇṭum” eṉṟu appoḻutē cūḷurittuk koṇṭatāka avar eḻutiyuḷḷār (“Caldwell Reminiscences” by J.L. Wyatt; 18987)  tamiḻiṉ, tamiḻariṉ nallūḻ, kāraṇamāka kālṭuvel tamiḻnāṭṭil paṇi ceyya aṉuppap paṭṭār. mūṉṟu āṇṭukaḷ ceṉṉiyil taṅkiya piṉṉar tirunelvēliyil iṭiyaṉ kuṭikku avar iṟi paṇiyāṟṟac ceṉṟār. avar ceṉṉiyil vāḻntapoḻutu ēṟkaṉavē tamiḻppaṇiyilum tamiḻāyvilum īṭupaṭṭirunta pōp, turu, viṉslō, pavar (Bower) ākiyōroṭu paḻakit tam tamiḻaṟivip perukkik koṇṭār. piṉṉar iṭiyaṉ kuṭiyil maṟippaṇi āṟṟat toṭaṅkiya piṉṉarum moḻiyiyalārāycciyil iṭiyaṟātu kaṭumiyāka 15 āṇṭukaḷukku mēl uḻittut tamatu ariya oppilakkaṇa nūli veḷiyiṭṭār.

muṉṉuri
1979ām āṇṭu navampart tiṅkaḷil tamiḻ ilakkaṇap pērakarāti oṉṟaṉit tokuttu uruvākkum paṇiyil putuc cēriyil uḷḷa tolik kīḻip pirañcu ārāyccip paḷḷiyil amarttappaṭṭēṉ. iṭiyiṭiyē paṇikkappaṭṭa ēṉiya paṇi kaḷukku iṭiyilum akarātip paṇiyit toṭarntu 1995il ōraḷavu ataṉi niṟivu ceytēṉ. ip paṇiyil eṉakku utavi ceyya amarttappaṭṭa nārāyaṇacāmi aiyar, kumāracāmip piḷḷi, appācāmi mutaliyōr paṇiyiliruntu iṭiyiṭiyē viṭuvikkap paṭa ippaṇiyil eṉakku iṟutivari eṉ iḷaval kaṅkātaraṉē utavum nili ēṟpaṭṭatu. ippaṇikkut tolkāppiyattiṉ paḻiya urikaḷ mutal aṇmiyil veḷivanta pāvalarēṟu pālacuntaraṉāriṉ teṉmoḻi ilakkaṇam muṭiya uḷḷa pala nūlkaḷum payaṉpaṭuttappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ceṉṟa nūṟṟāṇṭiṉavāka vēṅkaṭarāculu reṭṭiyāriṉ eḻuttatikāra ārāycci, ilakkaṇak kaṭṭurikaḷ, cuppiramaṇiya cāttiriyāriṉ eḻuttatikārac col latikārac kuṟippukkaḷ, pālacuntaraṉāriṉ tolkāppiya ārāycci kāṇṭikiyuri pōṉṟa cila nūlkaḷē mēṟkōḷkaḷākak koḷḷappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ceṉṟa nūṟṟāṇṭil veḷivanta ilakkaṇam paṟṟiya kaṭṭurikaḷ pala iruppiṉum aviyellām ivvakarāti yil iṭam peṟavilli.
ivvakarāti pala tokutikaḷākap pala talippup paṟṟip pirittuk kūṟappaṭṭiruttaliṉ, ellā ilakkaṇa vakikaḷukkum potuvāṉa coṟkaḷ eṅku iṭam peṟṟuḷḷaṉa eṉpataṉi aṟitalil ciṟu cikkal ēṟpaṭum. eṭuttukkāṭṭāka ‘vaḻakku’ eṉṟa col tolkāppiyac ciṟappup pāyirattil mutaṟkaṇ varuvatāl accol pāyiram paṟṟiya pakutiyil viḷakkappaṭṭirukkum. talippuk kaḷiṉ akarāti amikkappaṭṭapiṉ attakiya coṟkaḷiṉ iruppiṭam aṟital eḷitākum.
ivvakarātip paṇiyil iṟuti meypput tiruttutal mutaliya vaṟṟil eṉ iḷaval kaṅkātaraṉē muḻumiyāka īṭupaṭṭa pōti lum, eṉ tampi tiruttuvataṟku muṉṉarē meyppukkaḷ tiruttattil īṭupaṭṭuc ceyaṟpaṭṭa cāṉṟōr aṉivariyum naṉṟiyoṭu nōkkukiṉṟēṉ. 17 tokutikaḷāka amiyum inta nūliṉi amippataṟku eṉakku eṉ tampi valakkiyāka utavuvatu pōlavē, innūlip patippikkum iḷavaḻakaṉārukku utaviya patippaka utaviyāḷarkaḷ celvaṉ ce. caravaṇaṉ, celvaṉ i. iṉiyaṉ, celvaṉ mu. kaliyaracaṉ, araṅka. kumarēcaṉ, vē. taṉacēkaraṉ, 
mu.nā. irāmacuppiramaṇiya irācā, nā. veṅkaṭēcaṉ, ila. tarmarācu ākiyōr innūl cemmiyāka veḷivarap peritum muyaṉṟuḷḷa ceyalip pōṟṟukiṟēṉ. ivarkaḷ nōynoṭi iṉṟip pallāṇṭu vāḻa vāḻttukiṟēṉ.
innūli veḷiyiṭa utaviya eṅkaḷ tolik kīḻip pirañcu ārāyccippaḷḷi niṟuvaṉattārukku naṉṟiyit teri vittukkoḷkiṟēṉ.
tokutikaḷ 17 : eḻuttu - 2, col - 4 , poruḷ - 11 (akam - 4, puṟam - 1, aṇi - 2, yāppu - 2, pāṭṭiyal, pāyiram, marapiyal - 1, meyppāṭu, nāṭakam, aḷavi, niyāyam ēṉiya - 1)

ti.vē. kōpāliyar


mukavuri
eḻuttatikāra ilakkaṇap pērakarātiyil, tolkāppiyam mutalāka iṉṟu nammiṭiyē vāḻntu varum ilakkaṇa ilakkiya moḻiyiyal pēraṟiñarām ca.pālacuntaraṉār ceṉṟa nūṟṟāṇṭiṉ piṟpakutiyil iyaṟṟiya  ‘teṉṉūl’ muṭiya, iṉṟu namakkuk kiṭṭuvaṉavāka nilavi varum ciṟanta ilakkaṇa nūl variciyil iṭampeṟum nūlkaḷum urikaḷum iṭam peṟukiṉṟaṉa.
ikkālattil tolkāppiyak kaṭal eṉṟu pōṟṟappaṭum annūl iyaṟṟappaṭṭa kālattē atu ciṟunūlākavē yākkap peṟṟatu. ataṉ eḻuttup paṭalattil uḷḷa 9 iyalkaḷilum vitik kappaṭuvaṉa ellām karuviyum ceykiyum eṉa iruvakippaṭum.  avaṟṟuḷ karuvi nūlmarapu mutaliya nāṉku ōttum, ceyki tokimarapu mutaliya eñciya aintu ōttum ām.  karuvitāṉum potuvum ciṟappum eṉa iruvakittu. mutal mūṉṟu ōttum potukkaruvi; ceyki oṉṟaṟkēyuriya puṇariyal ciṟappuk karuvi.
nūlmarapu, nūliṉatu marapu paṟṟiya peyarkaḷākiya eḻuttu - kuṟil - neṭil - uyir - mey - meyyiṉ vakikaḷ - eḻuttukkaḷiṉ māttiri - iṉṉa meykku iṉṉamey naṭpeḻuttu, paki yeḻuttu eṉpataṉik kuṟikkum meymmayakkam - meyyeḻut tukkaḷ, ekara okara uyirkaḷ, kuṟṟiyalikaram, kuṟṟiyalukaram eṉṉu mivi puḷḷipeṟutal - makarak kuṟukkam uṭpeṟu puḷḷiyum kōṭal - cuṭṭu - viṉā - aḷapeṭi - eṉpaṉa paṟṟik kuṟippiṭukiṟatu.
aṭuttu, moḻimarapu kūṟum vitikaḷ nūlmarapiṉ oḻipāka amintuḷḷaṉa. itaṉkaṇ kuṟṟiyalikaram, kuṟṟiyalukaram, āytam, aḷapeṭi, moḻiyākkam, īroṟṟuṭaṉili, makarak kuṟukkam, aikārak kuṟukkam, pōliyeḻuttukkaḷ, moḻi mutalil varum eḻuttukkaḷ, moḻiyīṟṟil varum eḻuttukkaḷ - eṉpaṉa iṭam peṟukiṉṟaṉa.
piṟappiyal, uyir - mey - cārpeḻuttukkaḷ eṉpavaṟṟiṉ piṟappiṭaṅkaḷum muyaṟciyum paṟṟi moḻikiṟatu.
puṇariyalil, ellāmoḻikaḷiṉ iṟutiyum mutalum mey uyir eṉṟa iraṇṭaṉuḷ aṭaṅkum; peyarum toḻilum eṉṟō toḻilum peyarum eṉṟō perumpāṉmiyum coṟkaḷ puṇarumiṭattu iyalpākavum tirintum puṇarum; puṇarvaṉa nilimoḻiyiṉ īṟṟeḻuttum varumoḻiyiṉ mutaleḻuttum ākiya iraṇṭumē; coṟkaḷ vēṟṟumipporuḷ paṟṟiyum al vaḻipporuḷ paṟṟiyum puṇarum; iṭiyē cāriyi varappeṟum; cila cāriyikaḷ uruttirintum puṇarum; eḻuttuccāriyikaḷ ivi, uṭampaṭumey ivi - eṉa icceytikaḷik kāṇalām.
tokimarapu eṉṉum aintām ōttiṉkaṇ, uyirīṟum puḷḷiyīṟum uyirmayaṅkiyaluḷḷum puḷḷi mayaṅkiyaluḷḷum īṟukaḷtōṟum virittu muṭippaṉavaṟṟi orōvoru cūttirat tāl tokuttu muṭipu kūṟappaṭuvaṉavum, uyartiṇippeyarp puṇarcciyum viravuppeyarp puṇarcciyum iraṇṭām mūṉṟām vēṟṟumiyurupu ēṟṟa peyarppuṇarcciyum, cila iṭiccoṟ kaḷatu muṭipum, eṇ niṟi aḷavup peyaruḷ cilavaṟṟatu puṇarcci yum kūṟappaṭṭuḷa.
urupiyal, urupēṟṟa peyar cāriyi peṟṟum peṟāmalum orōvaḻi neṭumutal kuṟukiyum varumoḻiyoṭu puṇarumāṟu kūṟukiṟatu. uyirmayaṅkiyalilum puḷḷimayaṅkiyalilum alvaḻip puṇarcci perumpālum eḻuvāyttoṭarkkē koḷḷappaṭu kiṟatu.  vēṟṟumippuṇarcci vēṟṟumiyurupukaḷ tokka vēṟ ṟumip puṇarccikkē koḷḷappaṭukiṟatu.  ippuṇarccikaḷ potuvākak kūṟappaṭṭālum, āciriyar uyartiṇippeyar - viravuppeyar - kiḷippeyar - nāṭpeyar - tiṅkaṭ peyar - eṇ niṟi aḷavup peyar - eṉpaṉavaṟṟi vitantōtiyē muṭikkum karuttiṉarātaliṉ, ippuṇarccikaḷ aḵṟiṇiyil, kiḷi mutalākac collappaṭṭa appeyarkaḷ nīṅkalāṉa ēṉiyavaṟ ṟiṟkē kōṭal āciriyar karuttām.
āciriyar īreḻuttorumoḻi eṉṟu kūṟiyami īreḻuttuk kuṟṟukarac collit tam maṉattuk koṇṭamiyālām.  kuṟṟiya lukaram puḷḷiyīṟu pōla uyirēṟa iṭaṅkoṭukkum. uyiraḷa peṭi eṉpatu neṭili aṭuttu varum otta iṉakkuṟṟeḻuttē.  puṇarcciyil tolkāppiyaṉār kuṟippiṭum eḻuttuppēṟaḷa peṭi piṟkālattē vaḻakku iṟantatu eṉṟu kōṭal takum.
tolkāppiyatti aṭuttu nāṭṭil ciṟappāka vaḻaṅki vantatākak karutappaṭum avinayam irācapavittirap pallavatariyaṉ uriyoṭu 13ām nūṟṟāṇṭuvari vaḻakkiliruntu vanta ceyti naṉṉūl mayilinātar uriyālēyē peṟappaṭukiṟatu.  annūlum ataṉuriyum vaḻakkiṟantu viṭṭaṉa.
tolkāppiyatti aṭuttu iṉṟu vaḻakkil irukkum aintilakkaṇa nūl peruntēvaṉār uriyoṭu kūṭiya vīra cōḻiyamē. pallavar kālattilē “pāratatēyattu vaḻakkilirukkum ellā moḻikaḷukkum tāy camaṟkirutamē” eṉṟa karuttu makka ḷiṭiyē uruvāka, atu 18ām nūṟṟāṇṭu muṭiya uṟutiyākak koḷḷappaṭṭu vantatu.  11 ām nūṟṟāṇṭaḷavil tōṉṟiya vīra cōḻiyam ikkaruttiyuṭkoṇṭu iyaṟṟappaṭṭa ilakkaṇamē.
vīracōḻiyattiṉkaṇ, uyireḻuttukkaḷi aṭuttu mey yeḻuttukkaḷiṉ muṉṉar neṭuṅkaṇakkil āytam iṭam peṟṟa ceyti kūṟappaṭukiṟatu.  makarak kuṟukkam mēlē peṟum puḷḷiyōṭu uḷḷēyum oru puḷḷi peṟum eṉṟa nuṭpamāṉa ceyti innūliṉ uriyiṉkaṇṇēyē kāṇappaṭukiṟatu.  vaṭamoḻip puṇarcciyil alvaḻi, vēṟṟumi eṉṟa poruḷ paṟṟiya pākupāṭu illi.  ākavē, vīracōḻiyac cantip paṭalattilum alvaḻi vēṟṟumip pākupāṭu kuṟippiṭappaṭṭilatu.  varumoḻi mutalil uyirvariṉ kuṟṟiyalukaram keṭum eṉṉum ceyti innūlilēyē mutaṟkaṇ kuṟippiṭappaṭa, acceyti nēminātam naṉṉūl mutaliya piṉ ṉūlkaḷ palavaṟṟilum iṭampeṟalāyiṟṟu.  vaṭamoḻiccoṟkaḷ tamiḻolikkēṟpat tirittu vaḻaṅkappaṭumāṟṟiṟku innūl kūṟum vitikaḷē naṉṉūl mutaliya piṉṉūlkaḷilum koḷḷappaṭalāyiṉa.  vaṭamoḻiyiluḷḷa ‘na’ eṉṟa etirmaṟi muṉoṭṭu varumoḻi yōṭu iṇiyumiṭattu ēṟpaṭum tiripukaḷi innūl iyampiṭa, ataṉi nēminātamum ēṟṟukkoḷḷa, naṉṉūl ataṉi nekiḻttu viṭṭatu, ḷakarattiṟkuk kūṟum puṇarcciviti ḻakarattiṟkum, in nūlāciriyarkku muṟpaṭṭa ilakkiyaṅkaḷil piṉpaṟṟappaṭavē, in nūlāciriyar ilakkiyam kaṇṭu ataṟku ilakkaṇam varintuḷḷa ceyti innūlil putitāka iṭam peṟukiṟatu.  icceytiyi nēminātam naṉṉūl pōṉṟa piṉṉūlkaḷ kuṟippiṭavilli.  uṭampaṭumeyyi innūlāciriyar ‘i ī ai vaḻi yavvum, ēṉi uyirvaḻi vavvum, ē muṉ ivvirumiyum, eṉṟu mutaṉmuṟiyāka variyaṟuttuk kūṟiyavarāvar. “aḷapeṭi mūṉṟu māttiri peṟum; atu neṭilum kuṟilum iṇin tolikkum ōroliyē” eṉṟa ivaratu koḷkiyē, perumpālum piṉṉūlār palarālum mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭatu. ē yā e - eṉpaṉa col mutalil viṉāvākum eṉṟa ivar karuttip piṟkālattu nūlār palarum ēṟṟukkoṇṭavarāvar.
aṭuttu vanta nēminātamum, neṭuṅkaṇakku variciyi , uyir - aṭuttu āytam - aṭuttu mey - eṉṟē kuṟippiṭukiṟatu. in nūluḷ kuṟṟiyalikaram, kuṟṟiyalukaram, aikārak kuṟukkam eṉṉum ivaṟṟōṭu oḷakārak kuṟukkamum kuṟikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. “uyiraḷa peṭi neṭiloṭu kūṭiya ōroliyām iṉak kuṟṟeḻuttu; atu mūṉṟu māttiri peṟum” eṉṟu vīracōḻiyatti oṭṭi nēminātam nuvalkiṟatu.  vaṭamoḻit tattitānta nāmaṅkaḷum etir maṟi yuṇarttum nakara muṉṉoṭṭup puṇarcciyum vīracōḻi yattip piṉpaṟṟiyē kūṟappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.  vīracōḻiyam vitikkum vaṭamoḻiyākkam nēminātattil illi.
tolkāppiyatti aṭuttu makkaḷ uḷḷattē ciṟappāka iṭampeṟuvatu pavaṇanti muṉivar iyaṟṟiya naṉṉūlē.  muta leḻuttum cārpeḻuttum eṉṟa pākupāṭu - cārpeḻuttup pattu eṉpatu - avi ovvoṉṟum paṟṟiya ceytikaḷ - moḻikku mutalil, iṭiyil, īṟṟil varum eḻuttukkaḷ - pōliyeḻut tukkaḷ - eṉpaṉa eḻuttiyalil iṭampeṟṟuḷa.  uyiraḷapeṭi neṭṭeḻuttiṉ nīṭṭamākiya mūṉṟu māttiri, kuṟil aṟikuṟi yāka varuvatē eṉṟa ceyti kuṟippiṭap paṭṭuḷḷatu.  pataviyalil, pakupatam pakuti - vikuti  mutaliya uṟuppuk kaḷākap pirittuk kāṭṭap paṭṭuḷḷatu.  pakupata uṟuppukkaḷ viḷakkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. pataviyal naṉṉūl kuṟippiṭum orumoḻip puṇarcciyākiya putuc ceytiyē. itaṉ iṟutiyil vaṭamoḻi ākkam vīracōḻiyattip piṉpaṟṟi variyappaṭṭuḷḷatu. ‘na’ eṉṟa etirmaṟi muṉṉoṭṭup paṟṟiya ceyti pēcappaṭṭilatu.
tolkāppiyattiṟku iḷampūraṇar varinta uriyiyum uṭkoṇṭu iyaṟṟappeṟṟa naṉṉūlil tolkāppiyac ceytikaḷ palavaṟṟoṭum avvuriyāciriyar kuṟippiṭṭa ceytikaḷum iṭampeṟukiṉṟaṉa. tolkāppiyam kuṟikkum puṇariyal - toki marapu - uyirmayaṅkiyal - kuṟṟiyalukarap puṇariyal - paṟṟiya ceytikaḷ palavum uyirīṟṟup puṇariyaluḷ aṭakkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.  alvaḻi yāvaṉa iviyeṉa viḷakkappaṭukiṟatu. uṭampaṭumey, kuṟṟukaram uyirvarak keṭutal - pōṉṟavi vīracōḻiyattip piṉpaṟṟiyaṉavām.  naṉṉūlil kāṇappaṭum māṟṟaṅkaḷ ‘marapu nili tiriyātu’ amintaṉa eṉpa.  tolkāppiyattiṉiṉṟu naṉṉūl caṟṟē vēṟupaṭṭuk kūṟumiṭaṅkaḷ poruḷnili tiriyāmiyāl ‘marapu nili tiriyā māṭcimi’ yuṭiyavāy mutalnūṟku malivupaṭāmal cellum iyaṟkiya ātalic civañāṉamuṉivar tamatu pāyiraviruttiyuḷ viḷaṅkak kūṟumāṟu īṇṭuk karutal takum.
ilakkaṇaviḷakkam, naṉṉūl tolkāppiyattoṭu māṟu paṭṭuk kūṟum oru cila iṭaṅkaḷic cuṭṭi viḷakkukiṟatu. taṉakku muṟpaṭṭa nūlkaḷil viḷakkappaṭum vaṭamoḻiyākkatti iv vilakkaṇanūl nekiḻttuviṭṭatu.
ilakkaṇakkottu, pirayōka vivēkam eṉpaṉavaṟṟil cila ariya puṇarccivitikaḷ kuṟippiṭappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. a - an - na - ni - ku - vi - puṇarap puṇarppatu vaṭamoḻiyil etirmaṟiyākum eṉṟa ceyti ilakkaṇakkottil viḷakkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. tamiḻc ciṟap peḻuttu aintāṉum tirintu āriyaccol vaṭacollākit tamiḻil vaḻaṅkum ceyti ivviraṇṭu nūlkaḷilum viḷakkappaṭu kiṟatu. icceyti naṉṉūlil iṭam peṟṟilatu.
toṉṉūl viḷakkam naṉṉūlip peritum piṉpaṟṟiyatu; vaṭamoḻiyākkattilum naṉṉūlip piṉpaṟṟiyuḷḷatu. muttu vīriyattil tīrkkacanti mutaliyavaṟṟiṟku vitikaḷ taṉittaṉiyē kūṟap paṭṭuḷa. kō + il = kōyil, mā + iru = māyiru - mutaliya vaṟṟiṟkut taṉiyē vitikaḷ vakukkappaṭṭuḷa.  cuvāminātattil kuṟippiṭattakum vicēṭamāka ētum iṉṟu. ataṉ āciriya virutta yāppu nayaṉuṟumāṟu illi.
tolkāppiya eḻuttatikārattu iḷampūraṇar uri - nacciṉārkkiṉiyar uri - ceṉṟa nūṟṟāṇṭu moḻiyiyal vittakarām vēṅkaṭarāculu reṭṭiyār, pi.cā. cuppiramaṇiya cāttiriyār ivarkaḷtam ārāyccik kuṟippukkaḷ - ceṉṟa nūṟṟāṇṭiṟutiyil veḷivanta pālacuntaraṉārtam tolkāppiyak kāṇṭikiyuri - civañāṉa muṉivar aracañcaṇmukaṉār varin tuḷḷa viruttiyurik kuṟippukkaḷ eṉṉumiviyum, vīracōḻiyam peruntēvaṉār uri - nēminātam vayiramēkaviruttiyuri - naṉṉūl mayilinātar, caṅkaranamaccivāyar,  irāmānucak kavirāyar, caṭakōparāmānucāccāriyār āṟumukanāvalar eṉṟiṉṉōrtam urikaḷ - eṉṉum iviyum ēṉiya mūla nūlkaḷiṉ ceyti kaḷoṭu tokukkappaṭṭu ivveḻuttatikāra ilakkaṇap pērakarāti uruvākkappaṭṭuḷḷatu.

Introduction
Tamil grammar and poetics are old and venerable disciplines interwoven into a complex system the beginnings of which are lost in legend. What is fact, however, is that we are looking back on a textual tradition representing the thought of almost two millennia: a continuous discourse on Tamil language and literature, but also a dispute with other systems of knowledge, most notably the Sanskrit grammatical and poetological traditions. To give a rough chronology, for the first millennium, we have one great treatise encompassing the whole field and developing the basic structure that is taken up, with some modifications and extensions, by the whole later tradition. This is the famous Tolka#ppiyam, consisting of three parts, two of them devoted to two different aspects of grammar, which has been ever since split into two sections, namely ElÈuttu (phonology) and Col (morphology and syntax), while the third part treats of Porul@ (poetics). It is followed by a small work specialising in a particular field of poetics, namely that of Akam (love poetry), called the IrÈaiyan_a#r Akapporul@. 
The second millennium, probably a time of socio-cultural upheaval, sets in with a voluminous commentary tradition not only for the treatises that had been written so far, but also for wide parts of the older literature. In fact our understanding of the meaning of the older texts is basically indebted to these commentaries. Nevertheless, there is a parallel development of new treatises in all sub-disciplines, mirroring the confrontation with the change of language, the arising of new literary forms and the massive impact of North-Indian, i.e. Sanskritic modes of thinking and writing in the Tamilian South. To mention just a few of the most important titles, among the inclusive texts – comprising, just as the Tolka#ppiyam, the whole range of the field – there are the heavily Sanskritised Vi#raco#lÈiyam of the 11th century, and the Tamil-conservative Ilakkan|a Vil@akkam of the 17th century. Both of them extend the original structure of three sections, dealing with ElÈuttu (phonology), Col (morphology and syntax), and Porul@ (poetics), by another two subsumed under Porul@, namely Ya#ppu (metrics) and An|i (figures of speech). Among the influential treatises devoted exclusively to grammar we may list the Nan_n_u#l (12th century), the standard book on Tamil grammar after the Tolka#ppiyam, and the Pirayo#ka Vive#kam (17th century), agin very Sanskritic. Poetics, for its part, seems to have been an even more fruitful domin, creating a number of branches with various specia-lisations. The first independent text on metrics is the Ya#pparun)-kalakka#riki (10th century); the most notable exponent of systematic Akam poetics is the Nampi Akapporul@ (12th century), while the Pur_am genre (heroic poetry) is represented by the Pur_apporul@ven|pa#-ma#li (9th century).
The encyclopedia presented here is an attempt to render accessible this wealth of materials to specialists and also to non-specialists. The vast topology and terminology of Tamil grammar and poetics are represented by key terms which are explined with reference to the corresponding su#tras in the treatises and additional explication from the various commentaries. The whole work comprises 17 volumes, structured in the traditional way into the three sections ElÈuttu (phonology), Col (morphology and syntax) and Porul@ (poetics), where 2 volumes fall on ElÈuttu, 4 on Col, and 11 on the various sub-disciplines subsumed under poetics: 2 for Ya#ppu (metrics), 2 for An|i (figures of speech), 4 for Akam (love topics), 1 for Pur_am (heroic topics), 1 for Pa#t@t@iyal (literary genres), Pa#yiram (prefaces) and Marapiyal (word usage), and finally 1 for Meyppa#t@u (physical manifestation), Na#t@akam (drama), Al@avi (valid means of knowledge), A#nantakkur|r|am (collocations to be avoided), Niya#yam (logic) and ValÈuvamiti (poetic licence). The last of these volumes contins a bibliography.
This sort of work of synthesis has long been a desideratum of research: it gives erudite references to a vast range of technical Tamil texts which are, for the most part, not well understood today. Some of the texts are hard to come by – unless in the editions of the author of this encyclopedia (on whom more below) –  most of them are not translated into any other language, general introductions into the field are few, and even fewer are written in languages more easily accessible to the general reader (like English or French). There has been more than one project comparable in range in recent years, most prominently the Encyclopedia of Tamil literature of the Institute of Asian Studies, Chenni (in English language), but sadly this opus has not yet seen more than 3 volumes, the last one already nine years old and reaching only the letter “ai”. All the more reason for scholars interested in Tamil language and literature to be grateful to the author of the present work, the venerable T.V. Gopal Iyer, with his 80 years one of the last living exponents of a great tradition of exegesis.
Space permits here no more than a brief account of the highlights of a long and in many respects exemplary career of a Tamil savant in the 20th century. As well as following a traditional path of education, the worldly marks of which are his two titles Vidvan and Panditam conferred by the University of Madras and the Maturi Tamil Cankam respectively (in 1945 and 1953), Gopal Iyer also acquired the “modern” university degrees of Bachelor of Oriental Language and Bachelor of Oriental Language with Honours at the University of Madras (1951 and 1958). From 1965-1978 he tught in Rajah’s College, Thiruviyaru, in which period he already took up his activity of editing works of Classical Tamil, especially theoretical texts. The most important publications from that phase are the Ilakkan|a Vil@akkam in 8 volumes (published in Thanjavur by the Sarasvati Mahal from 1971-1974), the Ilakkan|ak Kottu (Sarasvati Mahal 1973) and the Pirayoka Vivekam (Sarasvati Mahal 1973).
Ever since 1978, Mr. Gopal Iyer’s sphere of activity has shifted to Pondicherry, where he has been (and still is) employed as a research scholar by the École Française d’Extrême-Orient (EFEO) – i.e., the French School of Asian Studies –, a research institution financed by the French government which has 17 research centres spread across Asia, the westernmost of which is that in Pondicherry, and which has the mission of studying Asian (and notably Indian) languages, cultures and religions. In this environment, designed as a meeting place for international research, the enormous preparatory work for this encyclopedia has been accomplished. Part of the voluminous editorial output of Mr. Gopal Iyer during the last 27 years has appeared in a series co-published by the EFEO and the IFP (the French Institute of Pondicherry, another research Institution of the French government in whose premises T.V. Gopal Iyer worked for several years), such as a 3-volume edition of the Te#va#ram, his major contribution to devotional Tamil literature (1984f., 1991), and the Ma#ran_ Akapporul@ (2005). A number of further publications deserve mention, since they concern fundamental texts of the Tamil grammatical and poetological tradition upon which the encyclopedia is based. Last year he published a 14-volume edition of the complete Tolka#ppiyam with all the commentaries (through Thiru. G. Ela-vazhagan of TamizhMann Pathippagam, Chenni - 17) and this year editions of the Vi#raco#l@iyam and the Ma#r_an_ Alan)ka#ram.
The EFEO is extremely happy that it has been able to contribute its share to this publication of vital importance for the exploration of Tamil literary history, which will be a monument to a most extrordinary man, who has been teacher and adviser, nay, a living encyclopedia, to so many students of Tamil language, Indian and Western.

A final brief note of thanks to three individuals who were important in bringing this large work to the light of day. The first is Mr. T.V. Gopal Iyer’s younger brother, Mr. T.S. Gangadharan, then of the French Institute of Pondicherry and now of the Pondi-cherry Centre of the EFEO, who wrote the work out in a fair hand.  The second is Dr. Jean-Luc Chevillard, who, years later, had the encyclopeadia digitally photographed when in its yet more voluminous hand-written state and so by his timely intervention prevented the loss of some of its parts.  The third is the publisher, Mr. Ela-vazhagan, of the Thamizh Mann Pathippagam, who had the vision to see the value of this work and took on the task of setting it in type, a labour which took a year and a half and involved five sets of proofs.

‘aḷakkalākā aḷavum poruḷum tuḷakkalākā niliyum tōṟṟamum’ koṇṭa maliyaṉiya māṇpiṉ aṟiviṉarākiya ti.vē. kōpāliyar kaṟṟōrtamakku varampākiya takimiyar. taṇṭamiḻiṉ mēlāntaramāṉa ilakkiya ilakkaṇaṅkaḷiyum, avaṟṟukkup paṇṭiyōr uritta taṇṭamiḻ urikaḷiyum iḷamiyilēyē pativu ceytukoṇṭa kuṟuntaṭṭāka viḷaṅku pavar ip peruntaki. ekkāraṇattālāvatu innūlkaḷil oṉṟi iḻakka nēriṉ kavalippaṭa vēṇṭiyatilli; ivartam uḷḷap pativiliruntu ataṉi mīṇṭum uruvākkik koḷḷalām.
‘centamiḻt teṉputuvi eṉṉum tirunakarkkup’ pukaḻ cērtta pāratiyār, pāvēntar mutaliya pukaḻmaṇikaḷiṉ variciyil iṉṟu kōpāliyar viṇṇuyar tōṟṟattuṭaṉ viḷaṅkukiṟār. iḷampūraṇar, cēṉāvariyar, nacciṉārkkiṉiyar, pērāciriyar, teyvacciliyār, kallāṭar, parimēlaḻakar, civañāṉa muṉivar, kārirattiṉak kavirāyar mutaliya pulavar marapiṉōr pukaḻi yellām tam pukaḻ ākkikkoṇṭa ippulavar peruntakiyip putuvip pulavarulakam pōṟṟi ‘nūṟkaṭal’ eṉṟa ciṟappup paṭṭamaḷittup poṉpōṟ potintu koṇṭatu.
irupattāṟāṇṭukaḷukku mēlāka ivar putuvip pirañcuk kali niṟuvaṉattil (viuṭiṭana kucayanḷīyaḷina) tamiḻāyvup piriviṉ talimip poṟuppiṉi mēṟkoṇṭu ariya nūlkaḷ palavaṟṟi āyvuc cempatippukaḷāka veḷiyiṭṭu varukiṟār.
pāratiyāriṉ taliciṟanta paṭippukaḷukku vāyppāka irunta putuvimaṇ, kōpāliyariṉ pukaḻi eṉṟum niṉṟu nilavac ceyyum uyar patippukaḷ pala uruvāvataṟkum vāytta iṭamāka ilaṅkukiṟatu.
kaṭanta paṉṉīrāṇṭukaḷākap putuviyiliruntu veḷivantu koṇṭirukkum ‘teḷitamiḻ’ eṉṉum tamiḻ vaḷarccit tiṅkaḷi taḻil, ivartam paṭippiṉi ēnti vārāta itaḻē illi. atil ‘ili maṟi kaṉikaḷ’ eṉṉum talippil, tamiḻilakkaṇa ilakkiya nūlkaḷilum urikaḷilum ili maṟi kaṉikaḷāka maṟin tirukkum ariya ceytikaḷit tiraṭṭik kaṭṭurikaḷākat tantu varukiṟār.
alāṉ taṉiyēlu (haṭayaṉē ṉuyanniṭaṭira) eṉṉum mēṉāṭṭaṟiñariṉ maṇimēkali āṅkila moḻipeyarppup paṇikkut tuṇi ceytatum, cēṉāvariyattiṉ pirañcu moḻiyākkattiṟkut tuṇi niṉṟatum ivartam āṅkila aṟivukkuc cāṉṟu pakarum.
‘tolkāppiyap paḻiya urikaḷiṉ cempatippu’, ‘kal veṭṭukkaḷil nāyaṉmārkaḷ paṟṟiya aruñ ceytikaḷ’ ākiyaṉa iṉi veḷivara irukkum ivartam nūlkaḷil kuṟippiṭattakkavi.
ivartam paṇikaḷil mikamikap payaṉ viḷikkum ariya periya paṇi inta ‘tamiḻ ilakkaṇap pērakarāti’. tamiḻilakkaṇam kaṟpōrukkum, ilakkaṇa āyvāḷarkaḷukkum ki viḷakkākap payaṉpaṭakkūṭiya iv vakaravarici irupattintu āṇṭuk kāla pēruḻippāl eḻuti muṭikkappeṟṟatu. eppōtu veḷivarumō eṉṟu tamiḻaṟiñar ulakam etirpārttiruntatu.
maṟṟavar ariyateṉṟu karutum nalla patippup paṇikaḷi eḷiyateṉṟu ēṟṟu, maṭitaṟṟut tāmē muṉvantu ceyyum tamiḻmaṇ patippaka urimiyāḷar, ‘tamiḻmoḻik kāvalar’ 
kō. iḷavaḻakaṉ itaṉi aḻakuṟap patippittu vaḻaṅkukiṟār. ivvariya ceyalāl, iṉpattamiḻ irukkumaḷavum iḷavaḻakaṉ pukaḻum irukkum eṉpatu uṟuti.

‘īrattamiḻil āḻaṅkāl paṭṭavar’
paṇṭita vittuvāṉ ti.vē. kōpāliyar
ulakap potumaṟiyi aruḷiya tiruvaḷḷuvap perun takiyār periyār yār eṉpataṟku ōr ilakkaṇam vakukkiṉṟār. maṉitamēmpāṭṭukkuriyatāṉa, ceyvataṟku ariyatāṉa ceyali yār purikiṉṟārkaḷō avarkaḷē periyavar eṉkiṉṟār.
kālaṅkaḷtōṟum palvēṟu tuṟikaḷil maṉitamēmpāṭṭuk kākap palar ceyaṟkariya ceyalkaḷic ceytuḷḷārkaḷ. anta variciyil tamiḻ ilakkaṇap pērakarāti eṉṉum innūli ti.vē.kōpāliyarum, inta nūli veḷiyiṭṭa tamiḻmaṇ patippaka urimiyāḷar iḷavaḻakaṉārum aṭaṅkuvar.
tamiḻ ilakkiya ilakkaṇaṅkaḷ orucamayam illāmaṟ pōy viṭṭālum kōpāliyar oruvar iruntālē pōtum, avaṟṟi mīṭṭuk koṇṭuvantuviṭalām. anta aḷaviṟkut tamiḻ ilakkiya ilakkaṇaṅkaḷit tam niṉivil vittiruppavar.
oru tēṉkūṭṭil pala malarkaḷiṉ tēṉkaḷ kalantiruppatu pōl intap pērakarātiyil pala tamiḻilakkaṇa nūlāciriyarkaḷiṉ variyaṟikaḷum pala uriyāciriyarkaḷiṉ uri vaḷaṅkaḷum kalantuḷḷaṉa.
aṟiñar tilakam kōpāliyar eppaṭi eḷimiyāṉava rākavum, āḻamāṉa pulami uṭiyavarākavum, paḻaka iṉiyavarākavum irukkiṟārō, appaṭiyē ‘naviltoṟum nūlnayam pōlum payiltoṟum paṇpuṭiyāḷar toṭarpu’ eṉṉum tirukkuṟaḷiṉ karuttukkēṟpa innūlum nammiṭam paḻakukiṉṟatu.
inta nūl pērakarātiyāka uḷḷatāl ilakkaṇam kaṇṭu añcum māṇavarkaḷum tamiḻ ārvalarkaḷum potumakkaḷum akarātip poruḷi aṟivatu pōl eḷitākat tamiḻ ilakkaṇatti aṟintu koḷḷa muṭiyumāṟu uḷḷatu.
innūl tolkāppiyar kālattiliruntu inta nāḷvari uḷḷa tamiḻ ulakiṟkuk kiṭitta putumiyāṉa mutaṉmiyāṉa muḻumiyāṉa nūlākum.
palar muyaṉṟu ceyya vēṇṭiya paṇiyi ti.vē.kōpā liyarē ceytu muṭittuḷḷār. oru palkalikaḻakamō oru periya āyvu niṟuvaṉamō ceyya vēṇṭiya paṇiyit tamiḻmaṇ patippakam ārvattāl eḷitākac ceytuḷḷatu. tamiḻarkaḷiṉ tavappayaṉē ippaṭiyāyt tamiḻ maṇṇil mukiḻttuḷḷatu.
tamiḻakattil maṭṭumallāmal tamiḻ vaḻaṅkum aṉittu iṭaṅkaḷilum tirukkuṟaḷukku aṭuttapaṭiyāka nūlakaṅkaḷil iṭampeṟa vēṇṭiya tamiḻnūl innūl eṉpatil aiyam illi.
inta nūli veḷiyiṭṭa tamiḻmaṇ patippaka iḷavaḻaka ṉārkku namatu veṟṟi vāḻttukkaḷ. inta nūli aḷittaruḷiya aṟiñartilakam nammuṭiya ilakkaṇa māmaṇi kōpāliyarukkut taliyallāl kimmāṟilli  eṉṉum paṭiyāṉa tamiḻ vaṇakkaṅkaḷ. vāḻka tamiḻ. vaḷarka tamiḻppaṇpāṭu. velka maṉitanēyam.

uṅkaḷ kikaḷil tavaḻum it tamiḻ ilakkaṇap pērakarātit tokutikaḷ tamiḻukkuk karuvūlamāy amivatākum. tamiḻilakkaṇap perum parappi virivu ceyyum ip peṭṭakatti veḷiyiṭuvatil perumip paṭukiṟōm. tamiḻ āyvi mēṟkoḷvārkku virac curaṅkamākavum, tamiḻ ārvalarkaḷukku vaḻikāṭṭum oḷi viḷak kākavum innūl tokutikaḷ varukiṟatu.
toṉmiyum, muṉmiyum, tāymiyum, tūymiyum, meṉmiyum, mēṉmiyum, paḻamiyum, putumiyum, iḷamiyum, mutumiyum mikkamoḻi nam tamiḻmoḻi. tiraviṭamoḻikaḷukkut tāymoḻi nam tamiḻmoḻi. intiyap perumoḻikaḷukku mūlamoḻi nam tamiḻmoḻi. ulaka moḻikaḷukku mūtta moḻi nam tamiḻmoḻi. intiyap pēraracāl cemmoḻi eṉa aṟivikkap paṭṭuḷḷa in nērattil, ‘tamiḻ ilakkaṇap pērakarāti’yi veḷiyiṭuvatil peru makiḻcci aṭikiṟōm.

iruvaki nilaṉkaḷ -
iruvaki nilaṉkaḷāvaṉa cuvikkappaṭu poruḷum, ataṉic cuvikkum poṟikaḷiṉ cuviyuṇarvum ām. vēmpu eṉṉum cuvikkappaṭuporuḷum, ataṉic cuvikka vēṇṭiya nā eṉṉum poṟiyum taṉittaṉi niṉṟavaḻik kippuc cuvi piṟavātu, iraṇṭum cērntavaḻiyē kippuc cuvi piṟakkum. atupōla, accattukku mutalākiya vilaṅkum ataṉik kāṇum kaṇṇiṉ pārviyum taṉit taṉi niṉṟavaḻi accaccuvi piṟavātu, aviyiraṇṭum cērntavaḻiyē accaccuvi piṟakkum eṉpatu.
cilar aṉupavippavaṉuṭiya aṉupavam kāṇpavarkku eytutalē iruvaki nilam eṉpa.
vēmpu cuvittavaṉ aṟinta kippu uṇarviṉi nāvuṇar viṉāl piṟaṉ uṇara muṭiyātu. āyiṉ “ivaṉ vēmpu cuvittavaṉ” eṉa, avaṉmukattip pārttu ataṉkaṇ kāṇappaṭum meyppāṭukaḷālēyē aṟivāṉ. atupōla, accattukku ētuvākiya puli mutaliyaṉa kaṇṭu añci ōṭi varukiṉṟavaṉi maṟṟavaṉ kaṇṭavaḻi, “ivaṉ añci varukiṉ ṟāṉ” eṉa aṟivatallatu tāṉ puliyiṉik kaṇṭāṉ pōla accam koḷḷāṉ. ākavē, añciṉāṉik kaṇṭu nakutalum karuṇi ceytalum kaṇṭōrkkup piṟappataṉṟi accam piṟavātākaliṉ, cuvippōṉ nikaḻttiya cuviyik kaṇṭu “ivaṉ cuvittāṉ” eṉṟu aṟintavaṉ taṉ aṟivut taṉmiyiṉāl accuviyiṉip peṟāṉ ātaliṉ, cuvik kappaṭuporuḷum ataṉi nukarnta poṟiyuṇarvumē iruvaki nilam eṉappaṭum.	(to. po. 249 pērā.)

1. 	acceyal taṉakku muṉ nikaḻiṉum, ataṉkaṇ īṭupā ṭillāta maṉanili,
2.	acceyal ceyya uṭaṉpaṭṭavaṉatu kiyip piṭittuk koṇṭu acceyal ceyvati avaṉiṭam oppaṭittu makiḻtal,
3.	acceyalil tāṉ iṉi īṭupaṭṭut toṭarntu ceyaṟ paṭātiruttal.
4.	acceyal paṟṟi maṟṟavar kūṟuvaṉavaṟṟi maṉattil koḷḷātiruttal - ākiya nilikaḷākum.			(nāṭaka. 253 uri)

(to. po. 270 pērā.)

eṇṇāṉku nāṉāṉku ātal -
vīram accam iḻippu viyappu kāmam avalam uruttiram naki eṉṟa eṇvakic cuviyum, vīrak kuṟippu accakkuṟippu iḻippukkuṟippu viyappukkuṟippu kāmakkuṟippu avalakkuṟippu uruttirakkuṟippu nakikkuṟippu eṉa ivveṇvakik kuṟippum ākiya patiṉāṟum eḷḷal mutal viḷiyāṭṭu īṟākac collappaṭṭa muppattiraṇṭu poruḷiyum (to.po. 248- 255) kuṟittataṉ (kuṟittu avaṟṟiṉ) puṟattē nikaḻum.	(to. po. 245 iḷa.)

eṇvaki meyppāṭu - 
naki aḻuki iḷivaral maruṭki accam perumitam vekuḷi uvaki eṉpaṉa. ivaṟṟuḷ nakikku maṟutali aḻuki; iḷivaralukku maṟutali maruṭki; accattukku maṟutali perumitam; vekuḷikku maṟutali uvaki.
vāḻkkikku iṉṟiyamiyāta nakiyi muṉṉarum ataṟku maṟutaliyākiya aḻukiyi ataṉi aṭuttum, aḻuki yuṭaṉ iyipuṭiya iḷivarali ataṉi aṭuttum, tāṉ iḷivantu piṟitoru poruḷi viyakkumātaliṉ viyap piṉi ataṉi aṭuttum, viyappup paṟṟiyum accam piṟatta liṉ accatti ataṉi aṭuttum, accattiṟku maṟutali yākiya vīratti ataṉi aṭuttum vīrattiṉ payaṉākap piṟakkum vekuḷiyi ataṉi aṭuttum, vekuḷikku maṟutaliyākic cuvikaḷuḷ nakiyip pōlac ciṟanta uvakiyi ataṉi aṭuttu iṟutikkaṇ ṇum vittār.			(to. po. 251 pērā.)

etirpeytu parital -

‘emmeyyāyiṉum oppumi kōṭal’ -
1.	yātāṉum oru poruḷ kāṇappaṭṭālum ataṉit taṉṉōṭu oppumi koḷḷutal. talivi mālik kālatti nōkki “māliyē! nī varuntukiṉṟāyē; niṉ kaṇavaṉum eṉtalivaṉip pōlak koṭiyavaṉō?” eṉṟu viṉavutal (kuṟaḷ 1222) pōlvaṉa.			(to. po. 266 iḷa.)
2.	yātāṉum oru poruḷik kaṇṭaviṭattē ataṉit talivaṉōṭu oppiṭṭu avaṉiṭam pēcutal. (kēṭṭa poruḷ paṟṟiyum oppumi koḷvāḷ.)
	piriya niṉiyum talivaṉiṭam “nīvir cellum kāṭṭil āṇmāṉkaḷ peṇmāṉkaḷip pirintu vāḻkiṉṟa ṉavā eṉpati niṉittup pārumiṉ” (kali. 20) eṉṟāṟ pōlak kūṟal.	(270. pērā.)
3.	talivi tāṉ kāṇum poruḷ etuvum talivaṉ vaṇṇam vaṭivu paṇpu viṉikaḷuḷ oṉṟaṟku oppeṉak karutum kātaliyalpu. acōkavaṉattil cītikku irāma ṉatu vaṇṇam paṟṟiya niṉivu mikuntami pōlvatu. 			(kamparā. 5075) (meyp. 22 pārati)
itu  peruntiṇikkuriya meyppāṭu eṉpar iḷampūraṇar. ēṉiyōr kaḷavukkum kaṟpukkum uriya piriviṭi nikaḻum meyppāṭu eṉṟu karutuvar.
emmeyyāyiṉum oppumikōṭal : pirivāṟṟātu pulanta talivi neñcaḻinta niliyil tāṉ kāṇum poru ḷellām taṉṉip pōlavē tuṉpuṟuvaṉavākak karutik kōṭal. mey - poruḷ. talivaṉ piṟa poruḷit taliviyōṭu oppumi kaṇṭu kūṟuvaṉavum itaṉkaṇ aṭaṅkum.	(to. meyp. 22 ca.pāla.)

1. pukumukam purital, 2. poṟi nutal viyarttal, 3. nakunayam maṟittal, 4. citivu piṟarkku iṉmi - eṉa ivi nāṉkum mutaṟpakuti.
5. kūḻivirittal, 6. kātu oṉṟu kaḷital, 7. ūḻ aṇi tivaral, 8. uṭi peyarttu uṭuttal - eṉa ivi nāṉkum iraṇṭām pakuti.
9. alkul tivaral, 10. aṇintavi tiruttal, 11. il valiyuṟuttal, 12. irukiyum eṭuttal - eṉa ivi nāṉkum mūṉṟām pakuti.
perumiyum uraṉum uṭiya talivaṉ taṉ taḻiyum kātali maṟiyāṉāka, accamum nāṇum maṭaṉum uṭiya talivi taṉ curakkum kātalik karakku mātaliṉ, immeyp pāṭukaḷ avaḷmāṭṭē perumpālum nikaḻvaṉa.			(meyp. 13 - 15 pārati. to. po. 261 - 263 pērā.)
kaḷittōṉ avinayam -
kaṭkuṭittuk kaḷi koṇṭōṉukkuriya avinayaṅkaḷ: oḷikkat takkavaṟṟi oḷiyātu kūṟutalum, orōvaḻi uriyē illātiruttalum, orukāl kuṉintu tāḻtalum, maṟṟorukāl cōrntu vīḻtalum, taḷarntu taṭumāṟik kuḻaṟip pēcutalum, cāynta naṭiyum cuḻaṉṟu pārkkum pārviyum uṭimiyum pōṉṟavi. aṭiyārkkunallār kāṭṭuvaṉa ivi. 	(nāṭaka. 253 uri.)

camanili avinayam -
piṟarkkeṉa etiyum ceyyāta koḷkiyum, aṟam ceṟinta maṉamuma, amiti niṟinta kaṇkaḷum, mayakkamum kalakkamumāṉa piṟaḻnta aṟivu nīṅkiya tūya aṟivuniliyum, etiyum muyaṉṟaṭiyum kuṟippillāta niliyum, accamaṟṟa perumita niliyum, ulakattuyirkaḷpāl kuḷirnta uḷḷamum ceyalum paṭittiruttalum, aḷaviṭṭu urikka iyalāta arumiyum, aṉpum, kaṇṇil oḷiyuṭimiyum pōṉṟavi camanili - cāntaracam.	(nāṭaka. 246)
camanili eṉṉum meyppāṭu (1) -
(1) meyppāṭukaḷi oṉpatu eṉṟu kaṇakkiṭuvōr vīram accam mutaliya eṇvaki meyppāṭukaḷoṭu naṭuvunili yiyum cērttuk kaṇakkiṭuvar. naṭuvunili mattimam eṉavum cāntam eṉavum kūṟappaṭum. itaṉi niliyillā meyp pāṭukaḷil tolkāppiyaṉār aṭakkiyuḷḷār. 			(po. 260 pērā.)
naṭuvuniliyāvatu, kūṟappaṭṭa vīram accam mutalākiya eṇvakic cuviyoṭum tāṉ kalavātu taṉittirukkum ciṟappiṉi uṭiyatu. taṉmēl cantaṉam pūcupavarmāṭṭum taṉ uṭali vāḷkoṇṭu tuṇippavarmāṭṭum orē vita maṉaniliyoṭu kūṭiyiruttalē naṭuvuniliyākum. innili kāmam vekuḷi mayakkam mūṉṟum nīṅkiya tāpatar cāraṇar camaṇar mācaṟṟa muṉivar aṟivar mutaliyōrkaṇ nikaḻvatu. avarkaḷiṭam vīram accam mutaliya eṇvakic cuviyum nikaḻā. iṉpa tuṉpaṅkaḷil camanōkkuk koḷḷum iccuvi taṉittuk kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷatu.(245 iḷam. urimēṟ)
‘camanili ulakiyal nīṅkiṉār peṟṟi ātaliṉ ulaka vaḻakkup paṟṟiya iyaṟṟamiḻ ilakkaṇattil immeyppāṭu ēṉiyavaṟṟoṭu kūṭṭic ciṟappittu ōtappaṭṭilatu. 			(251 pērā.)
camanili eṉṉum meyppāṭu (2) -
camanili eṉṉum meyppāṭṭiṉuḷ ētēṉum vikāram uṇṭāyiṉ  atu vīram accam mutaliya ēṉiya eṇvaki meyppāṭṭiṉuḷ aṭaṅkum.	(to. po. 251 pērā.)
naṭuvunili eṉpatu oṉpatu cuviyuḷ oṉṟu eṉa nāṭaka niliyuḷ vēṇṭappaṭum camanili. aḵtāvatu ceñcan taṉattip pūciṉum uṭalic cetukkiṉum piḷappiṉum maṉam nilipēṟu kuliyātu iṉpatuṉpaṅkaḷil cellātu oru niliyilēyē irukkum nili. atu kāmam vekuḷi mayakkam nīṅkiṉārkaṇṇē nikaḻvatu. atu ciṟuvaraviṟṟāka liṉ niliyillā meyppāṭukaḷuṭaṉ cērtteṇṇap paṭṭatu. 	(to. po. 260 pērā.)
naṭuvunili aimpulaṉ aṭakkiya aṟivarpāl nikaḻvatu. ivar kāmavekuḷi mayakkattiṉiṉṟum nīṅkiyavarātalāl yāvarum kaṇṭañcum iyamaṉukkum ivar añcār; ettuṇi iḷivaral kāṇiṉum iḻittuk kūṟār; kalvi niṟintirun tum ataṉip poruḷākak koḷḷāta kāraṇattāl perumitam kāṭṭār; putumi perumi ciṟumi ākkam eṉpaṉa niliyil poruḷkaḷ eṉa aṟivarātaliṉ avaṟ ṟiṉik kaṇṭu viyappuṟār; tuṉpam vanteytāt tūya uḷḷattarākiya ivar iṉpatti viḻiyār ātaliṉ, camanili eṉappaṭum naṭuvunili porunti niṟkum nallō riṭattu, ‘nakiyē aḻuki iḷivaral maruṭki, accam perumitam vekuḷi uvaki eṉṟu, appā leṭṭē meyppā ṭeṉpa’ eṉat tollāciriyar vakuttuk kūṟiya eṇvaki meyppāṭum nikaḻāvām; orōvaḻi nikaḻu miṭattuk kāmamiṉṟi aruḷē paṟṟukkōṭāka nikaḻum eṉpatu.	(ma. cū. pak. 5, 6)
ittakiya maṉaniliyākiya naṭuvunili ulakil arukiyē kāṇappaṭutaliṉ, ulakil peruvaraviṟṟāka nikaḻum naki aḻuki mutaliya eṇvaki meyppāṭṭoṭum itaṉic cērkkātu, niliyillā meyppāṭukaḷākiya ‘uṭimi iṉpuṟal’ (to. po. 260 pērā.) eṉpavaṟṟoṭu tol kāppiyaṉār cērttuvittuḷḷār.
camam (3) -
itu camanili eṉavumpaṭum. aimpulaṉkaḷilum paṟṟaṟṟu viḷaṅkum nalla nilimi. tayiyum poṟumiyum viḷaṅkum iyalpum iccuviyē. ‘camanili’ eṉappaṭum meyppāṭu kāṇka.	(nāṭaka. 46, 47)
cāntac cuvi -
makiḻccikkuriya ceyaṟkaṇ makiḻvum, tuyarattiṟku uriya ceyaṟkaṇ avalamum koḷḷātu iṉpattiyum tuṉpat tiyum camamāka nōkkum maṉaamitiyē cāntamāvatu. taṇṭiyalaṅkāram (70) cāntam nīṅkalāka ēṉiya eṭṭuc cuvikaḷiyē kūṟiṟṟēṉum cāntamum cuviyaṇiyuḷ taḻuvappaṭum.
e-ṭu : 
‘meytti ruppatam mēveṉṟa pōtiṉum
itti ruttuṟan(tu) ēkeṉṟa pōtiṉum
citti rattiṉ alarntacey tāmari
otti rukkum mukattiṉi uṉṉuvāḷ’    (kamparā. 5088)
nāṭṭāṭciyit tantiyār nalkiyapōtum, “kāṭṭiṟkuc cel” eṉṟu tāyār āṇiyiṭṭapōtum, irāmaṉ makiḻcciyō varuttamō uṟṟilaṉāyiruntāṉ eṉṉum itaṉkaṇ cāntac cuvi amintuḷatu. itaṉi māṟaṉalaṅ kāram cāntaratam eṉṉum	(mā. a. 198 uri)
camanili, naṭuvunili eṉṟum itu peyar peṟum.
ciṅkāram -
ciruṅkāram; navaracattuḷ oṉṟākiya iṉpaccuvi. uvaki eṉpatum atu.
citivu piṟarkkiṉmi -
talivaṉi nōkkit taṉṉuḷḷam citinta taṉmiyiṉip piṟar aṟivuṟātavāṟu talivi maṟittuk kōṭal.
talivaṉik kaṇṭu accattālum nāṇattālum neṟṟi yil viyarvi tuḷikka, avaṉuṭiya kuṟippukkaḷāṉ taṉatu cirippiṉi veḷippaṭuttaṟku vāyppu nērntuḻiyum ataṉi maṟittukkoṇṭa talivi, taṉṉuḷḷam citintu niṟi aḻikiṉṟa ceyti puṟattārkkup pulaṉākāmal neñ ciṉi aṭakkivittukkoḷḷutal. avaḷ taṉ maṉatti aṭakkik koṇṭirukkum ceyti talivaṉaṟiya veḷip paṭum. talivaṉ taṉmaṉam citintatit talivipōla maṟikka muyalātu veḷippaṭiyāka uṇarttiviṭuvāṉ.			(to. po. 261 pērā.)
itu kāṭci eṉṟa mutalavattiyiṉ nāṉkām meyp pāṭu eṉpar iḷampūraṇar (to. po. 257) kaḷavu eṉṉum kikōḷil puṇarccikku muṉ nāṉāṉkāka nikaḻum mukkūṟṟup paṉṉiru meyppāṭukaḷil itu mutal kūṟṟiṉ nāṉkām meyppāṭu eṉpar piṟar.
ciṟappoṭu varum meyppāṭukaḷ -
ciṉam, pētimi, cakipput taṉmi, nalkuravu eṉṉum nāṉkum potuvāka makkaḷukku ākāta meyppāṭukaḷ eṉiṉum, oru poruḷic ciṟappittuk kūṟum kāraṇattāṉ avi variṉ amivuṭiyaṉa ākum.
e-ṭu : ‘toṭiya emakkunī yāri’ (kali. 88)
‘eṉṉarukē vantu eṉṉit tīṇṭutaṟku nīyār?’ eṉṟu talivi ciṉam paṟṟik kūṟuvatu, avaḷatu kātalic ciṟappittalāṉ amintatu.
‘cevviya tīviya colli avaṟṟoṭu
piya muyaṅkiya aññāṉ(ṟu) aviyellām
poyyātal yāṉyāṅ(ku) aṟikō?maṟṟu aiya!
akalnakar koḷḷā alartalit tantu
pakalmuṉi veñcuram uḷḷal aṟintēṉ
makaḷalli maṉṟa iṉi.’   (kali. 19)
‘kaḻikātaṉmiyāl niṉṉi uḷḷavāṟu aṟintilēṉ’ eṉṟa taliviyatu pētimi, avaḷatu kātalic ciṟappittu varutaliṉ amintatu.
‘uṟaliyām oḷivāṭa uyarntavaṉ viḻaviṉuḷ
viṟaliḻi yavarōṭu viḷiyāṭu vāṉmaṉṉō
peṟavarum poḻutoṭu piṟaṅ(ku)iṇart turutticūḻn(tu)
aṟalvārum viyieṉṟu aṟiyunar uḷarāyiṉ’ (kali. 30)
“iḷiyavarām parattiyaroṭu viyittuṟiyil viḻāk kālattē nīrāṭṭu ayarvāṉō?” eṉṟu talivaṉiṭattut talivi koḷḷum innimpiri avaroṭum viḷiyāṭu vāṉ eṉap poṟāmi kūṟiyum, “avaṉ ivviṭattu eṉpāl aṭital vēṇṭum” eṉak kātalic ciṟappittu varutaliṉ amintatu.
‘piracam kalanta veṇcuvit tīmpāl ... ... ...
ēval maṟukkum ciṟuviḷi yāṭṭi ... ... ...
koṇṭa koḻunaṉ kuṭivaṟa ṉuṟṟeṉak
koṭutta tāti koḻuñcōṟu uḷḷāḷ
oḻukunīr nuṇaṅkuaṟal pōlap
poḻutumaṟut tuṇṇum ciṟumatu kiyaḷē’    (naṟ. 110)
taṉatu kuṭumpam vaṟumiyuṟṟamiyāṉ eṉa nalkuravu kūṟiyum, ciṟuvaṉmiyuṭiyaḷāy illaṟam nikaḻttum kātal vāḻ viṉic ciṟappittu varutaliṉ amintatu.  			(to. po. 245 nac.)
ciṟumi -
itu maruṭki eṉṉum meyppāṭṭuk kuṟippu nāṉkaṉuḷ mūṉṟāvatu.
ciṟumi eṉpatu kaṭukiṉkaṇ pala tuḷi pōlum mikavum nuṇṇiyaṉa kaṇṭu viyattal. 	(to. po. 251 iḷa.)
iṟappac ciṟiyaṉa kaṇṭu viyattal.	(255 pērā.)
(eṭu) taliviyiṉ iṭiyiṉik kaṇṭa talivaṉ atu mikavum nuṇṇitāyuḷḷatu eṉṟu karutuvatu piṟaṉkaṇ tōṉṟiya ciṟumi paṟṟiya viyappu. 	(pērā.)
cītamuṟṟōṉ avinayam -
tuṉpam mikka uḷḷattoṭu taṭumāṟutal, īramākiya pōrviyi viṭuttuk kāynta pōrviyi vēṇṭal, veyililum neruppiṉ arukilum iruttali virumputal, kikaḷi uracik koḷḷutal, muṉakiyum peru mūcceṟintu koṇṭum pēcutal mutalāyiṉa. ivi aṭiyārkku nallār kāṭṭum avinayam.	(nāṭaka. 253 uri)
cuvi (1) -
eppoḻutu eccuvi tōṉṟum eṉṟa kālavariyaṟi iṉṟiyum piṟar tūṇṭutal iṉṟiyum iyaṟkiyākavē oruvaṉukkut tōṉṟum iccuviyuṇarvu maṉakkiḷarcci yāl nērntu eḻum. atu piṟar maṉattil uṭaṉē tōṉṟum uṇarcciyāl (anutāpattāl) veḷippaṭum. itaṟku viyappē uyir pōṉṟatu.
maṉattiṉkaṇ tōṉṟum kiḷarcci veḷippaṭṭut tōṉṟum vakiyil cuvi iyalum.
aḵtu uvaki, perumitam, naki, camam, vekuḷi, viyappu, iḻippu, avalam, accam eṉa oṉpatu. iḵtu āriyap pulavōr karuttu. 	(nāṭaka. 38, 39)
iṉiccuviyeṉpatu ciruṅkāram mutalākavuṭiya nāṭakac cuvi oṉpatum eṉak koḷka.	(vī. cō. 106 uri)
uvaki - kāmam mutaliya makiḻcci. perumitam - vīram; naki - cirippu; camam - camanili, cāntam; vekuḷi - kōpam; viyappu - aticayam; iḻippu - iḷivaral, avalam - aḻuki; accam - payam. ivaṟṟi muṟiyē ciruṅkāram, vīram, āciyam, cāntam, ruttiram, aṟputam, kucci, cōkam, payāṉakam eṉpar vaṭanūlār.	(nāṭaka. 39 uri)
cuvi (2) -
nāviṉāl nukarappaṭum kippu kārppu puḷippu uvarppu tuvarppu tittippu eṉpaṉa. aṉṟik kaṇṇāṉum cevi yāṉum uṇarappaṭum naki aḻuki iḷivaral maruṭki accam vīram uvaki vekuḷi eṉṉum eṇvakippaṭṭa cuvikaḷ; ivaṟṟoṭu naṭuvuniliyic cērttuc cuvi oṉpatu eṉṟalum uṇṭu. iccuvikōṭal maṉita iṉat tiṟkēyuriya taṉicciṟappu ātaliṉ ēṉiya uyirkaḷukkuriya nāccuviyiṉiṉṟum iccuvikaḷ pirittu ōtappaṭṭaṉa. ceviyāṉ perumpālum ceyyuḷkaḷ vāyilāka uṇarum iccuvikaḷi aṟiyātār ‘ceviyiṉ cuvi yuṇarā vāyuṇaviṉ mākkaḷ’ (kuṟaḷ 420) eṉa iḻikkappaṭṭaṉar.
cuviyeṉpatu kāṇappaṭu poruḷāl kāṇpōr akattil varuvatoru vikāram.	(to. po. 245. iḷa.)
cuviyāvatu nakimutaliya cuvikkuriya poruḷum cuvip pēṟuṭiya poṟiyum kūṭiyavaḻi nikaḻum maṉa uṇarcci eṉpār pērāciriyar.	(to. po. 249)
vaṭanūlār cuviyiṉi iracam eṉpa.
uvaki, aḻuki, cāntam eṉṉum mūṉṟum meṉmiyāṉa cuvikaḷ; vekuḷi, iḷivaral, vīram eṉṉum mūṉṟum vaṉmiyāṉa cuvikaḷ; naki, viyappu, accam eṉṉum mūṉṟum vaṉmikkum meṉmikkum iṭippaṭṭa cuvi eṉpatu oru cārār karuttu.
uvakiyum aḻukiyum mikumeṉmic cuvikaḷ; vekuḷi yum iḷivaralum miku vaṉmic cuvikaḷ; nakiyum naṭuvu niliyum maruṭkiyum caṟṟumeṉmiyuṭic cuvikaḷ; perumitamum accamum caṟṟu vaṉmiyuṭiya cuvikaḷ eṉpatu maṟṟum oru cārār karuttām.  (pērāciriyar - kaṇēcayyar muṉṉuri)
ivi pāṭalkaḷil varumiṭattu, avvac cuvikku ēṟpa vaṉmi meṉmi iṭippaṭṭaṉa ākiya coṟkaḷālēyē avvap pāṭalkaḷ iyaṟṟappaṭuvatu ciṟantatu.  (muṉṉuri)
naki, viyappu (-maruṭki), uvaki mūṉṟum perumpālum akattiṇiyiṉic cārum. vīram, vekuḷi, avalam, accam iḷivaral aintum perumpālum puṟattiṇiyic cārum. 
(ma. cū. pak. 6)
cuvi avinayam -
uvaki mutalāya oṉpāṉ cuvikkum uriya avinayaṅkaḷ. taṉittaṉit talippiṟ kāṇka.	(nāṭaka. 242)
cuvi iruvaki nilattiṉ iyalvatu -
cuvikkappaṭum poruḷum, cuvikkum poṟiyum oṉṟu cēriṉaṉṟi evvakic cuviyum piṟavātu; ātaliṉ, cuvipporuḷ cuvikkum poṟi eṉṉum iraṇṭum cērumpōtu tōṉṟuvatu cuvi.
cuvi, uḷḷattil eḻum kuṟippu, atu puṟattārkkup pulaṉām vaki uṇṭākum viṟal - eṉa immūṉṟum kāraṇa kāriya muṟiyil tōṉṟuvaṉa. ātaliṉ meyppāṭukaḷ ivi mūṉṟaṉiyum oruṅkē peṟum.	(nāṭaka. 231, 232)
viṟal, meyppāṭu, cuvi - oru poruḷaṉa.
cuvikku nilikkaḷaṉkaḷ -
cuvippaṭum poruḷ, cuviuṇarvu, kuṟippu, cattuvam eṉpaṉa nāṉkum cuvikkuriya nilikkaḷaṉkaḷām.
pēyiyō puliyiyō kaṇṭa oruvaṉ añciyavaḻi, mayakkamum karattalum naṭukkamum viyarppum uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. accattiṟku ētuvākiya puliyum pēyum cuvippaṭuporuḷ; avaṟṟik kaṇṭakālamtoṭṭu nīṅkātu niṉṟa accam cuviyuṇarvu; ataṉkaṇ varum mayakkamum kaṇṇi mūṭik koḷḷutalum kuṟippu; naṭukkamum viyarttalum meyppāṭu.
ivaṟṟuḷ naṭukkamum viyarttalum ākiya meyppāṭukaḷāl oruvaṉukku uṇṭāṉa accam maṟṟavarkkum pulappaṭum. 
(to. po. 245 iḷa.)
cuvikkuṟippukkaḷ oṉpatu -
cuvippaṭuporuḷum cuviyuṇarvum oṉṟu kūṭiyavaḻi maṉattiṉkaṇ tōṉṟi malarum kiḷarcci cuviyām.
ciruṅkāram mutalāvuḷḷaṉavē cuviyāvaṉa. avaṟṟi vīrakkuṟippu, payakkuṟippu, ukkirak kuṟippu, ciruṅkārak kuṟippu, kāruṇiyak kuṟippu, aṟputak kuṟippu, pukaḻk kuṟippu, nakik kuṟippu, ikaḻccik kuṟippu eṉa oṉpatākak kūṟuvar. 
(vī. cō. 106 uri)
cuvi kōṭal -
itu cuviyuṇarvu eṉappaṭum. nakiyum accamum mutalākiya uṇarvukaḷi muṟkālattu ulakiyalāṉ aṟivāṉoruvaṉ avaṟṟukku ētuvākiya poruḷ piṟa kaṇṭavaḻit tōṉṟiya poṟiyuṇarvukaḷ cuvi eṉappaṭum ātaliṉ, cuvippaṭum poruḷoṭu cuvikkum poṟi iṇintu ceyaṟpaṭutal cuvi kōṭal eṉappaṭum. vēmpu eṉṉum poruḷum nā eṉṉum poṟiyum cērātavaḻik kippuccuvi piṟavāta vāṟu pōla, apporuḷkaḷik kaṇṭavaḻiyallatu nakiyum accamum mutaliya cuvikaḷ tōṉṟā. oḻinta kāmam mutalāyiṉavum aṉṉa. itaṉāṉ ‘iruvaki nilattiṉ iyalvatu cuviyē’ eṉṟār ceyiṟṟiyaṉār.			(to. po. 249 pērā.)
cuvipporuḷi ‘vipāvam’ eṉavum. cuvinili avi nayatti ‘°tāyipāvam’ eṉavum vaṭanūlār kūṟupa. (ma. cū. pak. 116) cuvippaṭum poruḷkaḷi aimpoṟikaḷāṉ nukarntu cuvikkumāṟṟiṟku utāraṇaṅkaḷ piṉvarumāṟu.
ivaḷ mēṉi aṇi pōṉṟatu - itu paricattāl aṟintu cuvittatu.
ikkaṉi amutam pōlum - itu nāvāl uṇarntu cuvittatu.
ivaḷ mēṉi māntaḷir pōlum - itu kaṇṇāl uṇarntu cuvittatu.
ivaḷ kūntal pūviṉ maṇamuṭiyatu - itu mūkkāl uṇarntu cuvittatu.
ivaḷ moḻi yāḻici pōlum - itu ceviyāl uṇarntu cuvittatu.
cuvikōṭal paṟṟi vaṭanūlkaḷ kūṟuvaṉa piṉvarumāṟu :
oruvaṉ oruporuḷik kaṇṭaviṭattu apporuḷ kāraṇamāka avaṉ maṉattil nuṇmiyākat tōṉṟum kātal mutaliya cuviyiṉ muḷi ‘°tāyipāvam’ (niliyāṉa maṉakkaruttu) eṉappaṭum.
accuvi tōṉṟuvataṟkuk kāraṇamāṉa poruḷ ‘vipāvam’ eṉappaṭum. accuvit tōṟṟatti veḷiyē teriyappaṭuttum uḷḷa nikaḻcciyākiya kuṟippu ‘anupāvam’ eṉappaṭum.
accuvit tōṟṟattiṉ virivāy uṭampiṉ vaḻiyāka veḷippaṭum maṉavikāraṅkaḷākiya meyppāṭukaḷ ‘cāttuva pāvam’ eṉṟu peyar peṟum. maṉavikāraṅkaḷākiya meyp pāṭukaḷ vaḷarvataṟkuk kāraṇamāy avaṟṟi niṟivupaṭutti niṟpaṉavākum tuṇimeyppāṭukaḷ ‘cañcāripāvam’ eṉappaṭum.
accuvit tōṟṟam vaḷarntu avaṉāl cuvikkappaṭuta liṉāl cuvi ‘iracam’ eṉap peyar peṟum.
ātaliṉ, oruvaṉiṭattuc cuvittōṟṟamāvatu vipāvaṅka ḷāl tōṉṟi, anupāvaṅkaḷāl vaḷarntu, cañcāripāvaṅka ḷāl uṟuti peṟṟu avaṉāl cuvikkappaṭutaliṉ cuvi ākiṉṟatu.
°tāyipāvam eṉappaṭum cuvittōṟṟam oṉpatu vakip paṭum. aviyāvaṉa kātal, cirippu, tuṉpam, kōpam, eḻucci (-muyaṟci) accam, aruvaruppu, viyappu, aṭakkam eṉpaṉa.
kātalmāttiramaṉṟi uvappum uvakikkuk kāraṇamākum. ivi oruvaṉuḷḷattil tōṉṟiyapōtu niliyāyt taṭippaṭāmal vaḷarntu, pāl tayirāṉāṟ pōlac cuvi yāka māṟṟappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. cuviyum, ciruṅkāram (uvaki), hā°yam (naki), karuṇi (avalam) uruttiram (vekuḷi), vīram (perumitam), payāṉakam (accam), pīpatcam (iḷivaral), aṟputam (maruṭki), cāntam (naṭuvunili) eṉa oṉpatu vakippaṭum.
vipāvamum, ālampana vipāvam, uttīpana vipāvam eṉa iruvakittu.
ālampana vipāvamāvatu, oruvaṉ uḷḷattil tōṉṟum cuvit tōṟṟattiṟku nērē kāraṇamāyuḷḷa poruḷākum.
uttīpana vipāvamāvatu, cuvit tōṟṟattiṟkuk kāraṇamā yuḷḷa aṭippaṭip poruḷic cārntum cārāmalum porunti nuṭpamāṉa cuvittōṟṟatti vaḷarkkum poruḷ.
iḷampūraṇarum pērāciriyarum uḷḷattil nikaḻvataṉik kuṟippu eṉṟum, kuṟippukkaḷ tōṉṟiya uḷḷattiṉ nikaḻcci yāṉ uṭampil uṇṭākum vēṟupāṭukaḷic cattuvam eṉṟum kūṟuvar.
vaṭanūlāruḷ cilar, uṭampiṉ uruviṉiṉṟu tōṉṟum maṉōtarmatti anupāvam (kuṟippu) eṉṟum, uṭampiṉ vaḻiyāka veḷippaṭum maṉavikāraṅkaḷic cāttuvikam eṉṟum kūṟuvar. cāttuvikapāvam anupāvattiṉiṉṟu vēṟupaṭṭatum vēṟupaṭātatumāy iruttaliṉ, anupāvat tiṉuḷ cāttuvikapāvam aṭaṅkum eṉṟa karuttum uṇṭām. ākavē vaṭanūlār karuttuppaṭi cuvikōṭal kīḻkkaṇṭavaṟṟāl nikaḻum.
1.	°tāyipāvam - maṉattil tōṉṟum cuviyiṉ muḷi
2.	vipāvam - cuvi tōṉṟuvataṟkuk kāraṇamāṉa poruḷ
3.	anupāvam - cuviyit terivikkum uḷḷa nikaḻcci yākiya kuṟippu.
4.	cāttuvika pāvam - kuṟippip pulappaṭuttum meyppāṭu.
5.	cañcāripāvam - meyppāṭukaḷi niṟivupaṭuttum tuṇimeyppāṭu
6.	iracam - cuvit tōṟṟattic cuvikkum cuvi.

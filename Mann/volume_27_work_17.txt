muṉṉurai
1979ām āṇṭu navampart tiṅkaḷil tamiḻ ilakkaṇap pērakarāti oṉṟaṉait tokuttu uruvākkum paṇiyil putuc cēriyil uḷḷa tolaik kīḻaip pirañcu ārāyccip paḷḷiyil amarttappaṭṭēṉ. iṭaiyiṭaiyē paṇikkappaṭṭa ēṉaiya paṇi kaḷukku iṭaiyilum akarātip paṇiyait toṭarntu 1995il ōraḷavu ataṉai niṟaivu ceytēṉ. ip paṇiyil eṉakku utavi ceyya amarttappaṭṭa nārāyaṇacāmi aiyar, kumāracāmip piḷḷai, appācāmi mutaliyōr paṇiyiliruntu iṭaiyiṭaiyē viṭuvikkap paṭa ippaṇiyil eṉakku iṟutivarai eṉ iḷaval kaṅkātaraṉē utavum nilai ēṟpaṭṭatu. ippaṇikkut tolkāppiyattiṉ paḻaiya uraikaḷ mutal aṇmaiyil veḷivanta pāvalarēṟu pālacuntaraṉāriṉ teṉmoḻi ilakkaṇam muṭiya uḷḷa pala nūlkaḷum payaṉpaṭuttappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ceṉṟa nūṟṟāṇṭiṉavāka vēṅkaṭarāculu reṭṭiyāriṉ eḻuttatikāra ārāycci, ilakkaṇak kaṭṭuraikaḷ, cuppiramaṇiya cāttiriyāriṉ eḻuttatikārac col latikārac kuṟippukkaḷ, pālacuntaraṉāriṉ tolkāppiya ārāycci kāṇṭikaiyurai pōṉṟa cila nūlkaḷē mēṟkōḷkaḷākak koḷḷappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ceṉṟa nūṟṟāṇṭil veḷivanta ilakkaṇam paṟṟiya kaṭṭuraikaḷ pala iruppiṉum avaiyellām ivvakarāti yil iṭam peṟavillai.
ivvakarāti pala tokutikaḷākap pala talaippup paṟṟip pirittuk kūṟappaṭṭiruttaliṉ, ellā ilakkaṇa vakaikaḷukkum potuvāṉa coṟkaḷ eṅku iṭam peṟṟuḷḷaṉa eṉpataṉai aṟitalil ciṟu cikkal ēṟpaṭum. eṭuttukkāṭṭāka ‘vaḻakku’ eṉṟa col tolkāppiyac ciṟappup pāyirattil mutaṟkaṇ varuvatāl accol pāyiram paṟṟiya pakutiyil viḷakkappaṭṭirukkum. talaippuk kaḷiṉ akarāti amaikkappaṭṭapiṉ attakaiya coṟkaḷiṉ iruppiṭam aṟital eḷitākum.
ivvakarātip paṇiyil iṟuti meypput tiruttutal mutaliya vaṟṟil eṉ iḷaval kaṅkātaraṉē muḻumaiyāka īṭupaṭṭa pōti lum, eṉ tampi tiruttuvataṟku muṉṉarē meyppukkaḷ tiruttattil īṭupaṭṭuc ceyaṟpaṭṭa cāṉṟōr aṉaivaraiyum naṉṟiyoṭu nōkkukiṉṟēṉ. 17 tokutikaḷāka amaiyum inta nūliṉai amaippataṟku eṉakku eṉ tampi valakkaiyāka utavuvatu pōlavē, innūlaip patippikkum iḷavaḻakaṉārukku utaviya patippaka utaviyāḷarkaḷ celvaṉ ce. caravaṇaṉ, celvaṉ i. iṉiyaṉ, celvaṉ mu. kalaiyaracaṉ, araṅka. kumarēcaṉ, vē. taṉacēkaraṉ, 
mu.nā. irāmacuppiramaṇiya irācā, nā. veṅkaṭēcaṉ, ila. tarmarācu ākiyōr innūl cemmaiyāka veḷivarap peritum muyaṉṟuḷḷa ceyalaip pōṟṟukiṟēṉ. ivarkaḷ nōynoṭi iṉṟip pallāṇṭu vāḻa vāḻttukiṟēṉ.
innūlai veḷiyiṭa utaviya eṅkaḷ tolaik kīḻaip pirañcu ārāyccippaḷḷi niṟuvaṉattārukku naṉṟiyait teri vittukkoḷkiṟēṉ.
tokutikaḷ 17 : eḻuttu - 2, col - 4 , poruḷ - 11 (akam - 4, puṟam - 1, aṇi - 2, yāppu - 2, pāṭṭiyal, pāyiram, marapiyal - 1, meyppāṭu, nāṭakam, aḷavai, niyāyam ēṉaiya - 1)

ti.vē. kōpālaiyar


mukavurai
eḻuttatikāra ilakkaṇap pērakarātiyil, tolkāppiyam mutalāka iṉṟu nammiṭaiyē vāḻntu varum ilakkaṇa ilakkiya moḻiyiyal pēraṟiñarām ca.pālacuntaraṉār ceṉṟa nūṟṟāṇṭiṉ piṟpakutiyil iyaṟṟiya  ‘teṉṉūl’ muṭiya, iṉṟu namakkuk kiṭṭuvaṉavāka nilavi varum ciṟanta ilakkaṇa nūl varicaiyil iṭampeṟum nūlkaḷum uraikaḷum iṭam peṟukiṉṟaṉa.
ikkālattil tolkāppiyak kaṭal eṉṟu pōṟṟappaṭum annūl iyaṟṟappaṭṭa kālattē atu ciṟunūlākavē yākkap peṟṟatu. ataṉ eḻuttup paṭalattil uḷḷa 9 iyalkaḷilum vitik kappaṭuvaṉa ellām karuviyum ceykaiyum eṉa iruvakaippaṭum.  avaṟṟuḷ karuvi nūlmarapu mutaliya nāṉku ōttum, ceykai tokaimarapu mutaliya eñciya aintu ōttum ām.  karuvitāṉum potuvum ciṟappum eṉa iruvakaittu. mutal mūṉṟu ōttum potukkaruvi; ceykai oṉṟaṟkēyuriya puṇariyal ciṟappuk karuvi.
nūlmarapu, nūliṉatu marapu paṟṟiya peyarkaḷākiya eḻuttu - kuṟil - neṭil - uyir - mey - meyyiṉ vakaikaḷ - eḻuttukkaḷiṉ māttirai - iṉṉa meykku iṉṉamey naṭpeḻuttu, pakai yeḻuttu eṉpataṉaik kuṟikkum meymmayakkam - meyyeḻut tukkaḷ, ekara okara uyirkaḷ, kuṟṟiyalikaram, kuṟṟiyalukaram eṉṉu mivai puḷḷipeṟutal - makarak kuṟukkam uṭpeṟu puḷḷiyum kōṭal - cuṭṭu - viṉā - aḷapeṭai - eṉpaṉa paṟṟik kuṟippiṭukiṟatu.
aṭuttu, moḻimarapu kūṟum vitikaḷ nūlmarapiṉ oḻipāka amaintuḷḷaṉa. itaṉkaṇ kuṟṟiyalikaram, kuṟṟiyalukaram, āytam, aḷapeṭai, moḻiyākkam, īroṟṟuṭaṉilai, makarak kuṟukkam, aikārak kuṟukkam, pōliyeḻuttukkaḷ, moḻi mutalil varum eḻuttukkaḷ, moḻiyīṟṟil varum eḻuttukkaḷ - eṉpaṉa iṭam peṟukiṉṟaṉa.
piṟappiyal, uyir - mey - cārpeḻuttukkaḷ eṉpavaṟṟiṉ piṟappiṭaṅkaḷum muyaṟciyum paṟṟi moḻikiṟatu.
puṇariyalil, ellāmoḻikaḷiṉ iṟutiyum mutalum mey uyir eṉṟa iraṇṭaṉuḷ aṭaṅkum; peyarum toḻilum eṉṟō toḻilum peyarum eṉṟō perumpāṉmaiyum coṟkaḷ puṇarumiṭattu iyalpākavum tirintum puṇarum; puṇarvaṉa nilaimoḻiyiṉ īṟṟeḻuttum varumoḻiyiṉ mutaleḻuttum ākiya iraṇṭumē; coṟkaḷ vēṟṟumaipporuḷ paṟṟiyum al vaḻipporuḷ paṟṟiyum puṇarum; iṭaiyē cāriyai varappeṟum; cila cāriyaikaḷ uruttirintum puṇarum; eḻuttuccāriyaikaḷ ivai, uṭampaṭumey ivai - eṉa icceytikaḷaik kāṇalām.
tokaimarapu eṉṉum aintām ōttiṉkaṇ, uyirīṟum puḷḷiyīṟum uyirmayaṅkiyaluḷḷum puḷḷi mayaṅkiyaluḷḷum īṟukaḷtōṟum virittu muṭippaṉavaṟṟai orōvoru cūttirat tāl tokuttu muṭipu kūṟappaṭuvaṉavum, uyartiṇaippeyarp puṇarcciyum viravuppeyarp puṇarcciyum iraṇṭām mūṉṟām vēṟṟumaiyurupu ēṟṟa peyarppuṇarcciyum, cila iṭaiccoṟ kaḷatu muṭipum, eṇ niṟai aḷavup peyaruḷ cilavaṟṟatu puṇarcci yum kūṟappaṭṭuḷa.
urupiyal, urupēṟṟa peyar cāriyai peṟṟum peṟāmalum orōvaḻi neṭumutal kuṟukiyum varumoḻiyoṭu puṇarumāṟu kūṟukiṟatu. uyirmayaṅkiyalilum puḷḷimayaṅkiyalilum alvaḻip puṇarcci perumpālum eḻuvāyttoṭarkkē koḷḷappaṭu kiṟatu.  vēṟṟumaippuṇarcci vēṟṟumaiyurupukaḷ tokka vēṟ ṟumaip puṇarccikkē koḷḷappaṭukiṟatu.  ippuṇarccikaḷ potuvākak kūṟappaṭṭālum, āciriyar uyartiṇaippeyar - viravuppeyar - kiḷaippeyar - nāṭpeyar - tiṅkaṭ peyar - eṇ niṟai aḷavup peyar - eṉpaṉavaṟṟai vitantōtiyē muṭikkum karuttiṉarātaliṉ, ippuṇarccikaḷ aḵṟiṇaiyil, kiḷai mutalākac collappaṭṭa appeyarkaḷ nīṅkalāṉa ēṉaiyavaṟ ṟiṟkē kōṭal āciriyar karuttām.
āciriyar īreḻuttorumoḻi eṉṟu kūṟiyamai īreḻuttuk kuṟṟukarac collait tam maṉattuk koṇṭamaiyālām.  kuṟṟiya lukaram puḷḷiyīṟu pōla uyirēṟa iṭaṅkoṭukkum. uyiraḷa peṭai eṉpatu neṭilai aṭuttu varum otta iṉakkuṟṟeḻuttē.  puṇarcciyil tolkāppiyaṉār kuṟippiṭum eḻuttuppēṟaḷa peṭai piṟkālattē vaḻakku iṟantatu eṉṟu kōṭal takum.
tolkāppiyattai aṭuttu nāṭṭil ciṟappāka vaḻaṅki vantatākak karutappaṭum avinayam irācapavittirap pallavataraiyaṉ uraiyoṭu 13ām nūṟṟāṇṭuvarai vaḻakkiliruntu vanta ceyti naṉṉūl mayilainātar uraiyālēyē peṟappaṭukiṟatu.  annūlum ataṉuraiyum vaḻakkiṟantu viṭṭaṉa.
tolkāppiyattai aṭuttu iṉṟu vaḻakkil irukkum aintilakkaṇa nūl peruntēvaṉār uraiyoṭu kūṭiya vīra cōḻiyamē. pallavar kālattilē “pāratatēyattu vaḻakkilirukkum ellā moḻikaḷukkum tāy camaṟkirutamē” eṉṟa karuttu makka ḷiṭaiyē uruvāka, atu 18ām nūṟṟāṇṭu muṭiya uṟutiyākak koḷḷappaṭṭu vantatu.  11 ām nūṟṟāṇṭaḷavil tōṉṟiya vīra cōḻiyam ikkaruttaiyuṭkoṇṭu iyaṟṟappaṭṭa ilakkaṇamē.
vīracōḻiyattiṉkaṇ, uyireḻuttukkaḷai aṭuttu mey yeḻuttukkaḷiṉ muṉṉar neṭuṅkaṇakkil āytam iṭam peṟṟa ceyti kūṟappaṭukiṟatu.  makarak kuṟukkam mēlē peṟum puḷḷiyōṭu uḷḷēyum oru puḷḷi peṟum eṉṟa nuṭpamāṉa ceyti innūliṉ uraiyiṉkaṇṇēyē kāṇappaṭukiṟatu.  vaṭamoḻip puṇarcciyil alvaḻi, vēṟṟumai eṉṟa poruḷ paṟṟiya pākupāṭu illai.  ākavē, vīracōḻiyac cantip paṭalattilum alvaḻi vēṟṟumaip pākupāṭu kuṟippiṭappaṭṭilatu.  varumoḻi mutalil uyirvariṉ kuṟṟiyalukaram keṭum eṉṉum ceyti innūlilēyē mutaṟkaṇ kuṟippiṭappaṭa, acceyti nēminātam naṉṉūl mutaliya piṉ ṉūlkaḷ palavaṟṟilum iṭampeṟalāyiṟṟu.  vaṭamoḻiccoṟkaḷ tamiḻolikkēṟpat tirittu vaḻaṅkappaṭumāṟṟiṟku innūl kūṟum vitikaḷē naṉṉūl mutaliya piṉṉūlkaḷilum koḷḷappaṭalāyiṉa.  vaṭamoḻiyiluḷḷa ‘na’ eṉṟa etirmaṟai muṉoṭṭu varumoḻi yōṭu iṇaiyumiṭattu ēṟpaṭum tiripukaḷai innūl iyampiṭa, ataṉai nēminātamum ēṟṟukkoḷḷa, naṉṉūl ataṉai nekiḻttu viṭṭatu, ḷakarattiṟkuk kūṟum puṇarcciviti ḻakarattiṟkum, in nūlāciriyarkku muṟpaṭṭa ilakkiyaṅkaḷil piṉpaṟṟappaṭavē, in nūlāciriyar ilakkiyam kaṇṭu ataṟku ilakkaṇam varaintuḷḷa ceyti innūlil putitāka iṭam peṟukiṟatu.  icceytiyai nēminātam naṉṉūl pōṉṟa piṉṉūlkaḷ kuṟippiṭavillai.  uṭampaṭumeyyai innūlāciriyar ‘i ī ai vaḻi yavvum, ēṉai uyirvaḻi vavvum, ē muṉ ivvirumaiyum, eṉṟu mutaṉmuṟaiyāka varaiyaṟuttuk kūṟiyavarāvar. “aḷapeṭai mūṉṟu māttirai peṟum; atu neṭilum kuṟilum iṇain tolikkum ōroliyē” eṉṟa ivaratu koḷkaiyē, perumpālum piṉṉūlār palarālum mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭatu. ē yā e - eṉpaṉa col mutalil viṉāvākum eṉṟa ivar karuttaip piṟkālattu nūlār palarum ēṟṟukkoṇṭavarāvar.
aṭuttu vanta nēminātamum, neṭuṅkaṇakku varicaiyai , uyir - aṭuttu āytam - aṭuttu mey - eṉṟē kuṟippiṭukiṟatu. in nūluḷ kuṟṟiyalikaram, kuṟṟiyalukaram, aikārak kuṟukkam eṉṉum ivaṟṟōṭu oḷakārak kuṟukkamum kuṟikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. “uyiraḷa peṭai neṭiloṭu kūṭiya ōroliyām iṉak kuṟṟeḻuttu; atu mūṉṟu māttirai peṟum” eṉṟu vīracōḻiyattai oṭṭi nēminātam nuvalkiṟatu.  vaṭamoḻit tattitānta nāmaṅkaḷum etir maṟai yuṇarttum nakara muṉṉoṭṭup puṇarcciyum vīracōḻi yattaip piṉpaṟṟiyē kūṟappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.  vīracōḻiyam vitikkum vaṭamoḻiyākkam nēminātattil illai.
tolkāppiyattai aṭuttu makkaḷ uḷḷattē ciṟappāka iṭampeṟuvatu pavaṇanti muṉivar iyaṟṟiya naṉṉūlē.  muta leḻuttum cārpeḻuttum eṉṟa pākupāṭu - cārpeḻuttup pattu eṉpatu - avai ovvoṉṟum paṟṟiya ceytikaḷ - moḻikku mutalil, iṭaiyil, īṟṟil varum eḻuttukkaḷ - pōliyeḻut tukkaḷ - eṉpaṉa eḻuttiyalil iṭampeṟṟuḷa.  uyiraḷapeṭai neṭṭeḻuttiṉ nīṭṭamākiya mūṉṟu māttirai, kuṟil aṟikuṟi yāka varuvatē eṉṟa ceyti kuṟippiṭap paṭṭuḷḷatu.  pataviyalil, pakupatam pakuti - vikuti  mutaliya uṟuppuk kaḷākap pirittuk kāṭṭap paṭṭuḷḷatu.  pakupata uṟuppukkaḷ viḷakkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. pataviyal naṉṉūl kuṟippiṭum orumoḻip puṇarcciyākiya putuc ceytiyē. itaṉ iṟutiyil vaṭamoḻi ākkam vīracōḻiyattaip piṉpaṟṟi varaiyappaṭṭuḷḷatu. ‘na’ eṉṟa etirmaṟai muṉṉoṭṭup paṟṟiya ceyti pēcappaṭṭilatu.
tolkāppiyattiṟku iḷampūraṇar varainta uraiyaiyum uṭkoṇṭu iyaṟṟappeṟṟa naṉṉūlil tolkāppiyac ceytikaḷ palavaṟṟoṭum avvuraiyāciriyar kuṟippiṭṭa ceytikaḷum iṭampeṟukiṉṟaṉa. tolkāppiyam kuṟikkum puṇariyal - tokai marapu - uyirmayaṅkiyal - kuṟṟiyalukarap puṇariyal - paṟṟiya ceytikaḷ palavum uyirīṟṟup puṇariyaluḷ aṭakkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.  alvaḻi yāvaṉa ivaiyeṉa viḷakkappaṭukiṟatu. uṭampaṭumey, kuṟṟukaram uyirvarak keṭutal - pōṉṟavai vīracōḻiyattaip piṉpaṟṟiyaṉavām.  naṉṉūlil kāṇappaṭum māṟṟaṅkaḷ ‘marapu nilai tiriyātu’ amaintaṉa eṉpa.  tolkāppiyattiṉiṉṟu naṉṉūl caṟṟē vēṟupaṭṭuk kūṟumiṭaṅkaḷ poruḷnilai tiriyāmaiyāl ‘marapu nilai tiriyā māṭcimai’ yuṭaiyavāy mutalnūṟku malaivupaṭāmal cellum iyaṟkaiya ātalaic civañāṉamuṉivar tamatu pāyiraviruttiyuḷ viḷaṅkak kūṟumāṟu īṇṭuk karutal takum.
ilakkaṇaviḷakkam, naṉṉūl tolkāppiyattoṭu māṟu paṭṭuk kūṟum oru cila iṭaṅkaḷaic cuṭṭi viḷakkukiṟatu. taṉakku muṟpaṭṭa nūlkaḷil viḷakkappaṭum vaṭamoḻiyākkattai iv vilakkaṇanūl nekiḻttuviṭṭatu.
ilakkaṇakkottu, pirayōka vivēkam eṉpaṉavaṟṟil cila ariya puṇarccivitikaḷ kuṟippiṭappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. a - an - na - ni - ku - vi - puṇarap puṇarppatu vaṭamoḻiyil etirmaṟaiyākum eṉṟa ceyti ilakkaṇakkottil viḷakkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. tamiḻc ciṟap peḻuttu aintāṉum tirintu āriyaccol vaṭacollākit tamiḻil vaḻaṅkum ceyti ivviraṇṭu nūlkaḷilum viḷakkappaṭu kiṟatu. icceyti naṉṉūlil iṭam peṟṟilatu.
toṉṉūl viḷakkam naṉṉūlaip peritum piṉpaṟṟiyatu; vaṭamoḻiyākkattilum naṉṉūlaip piṉpaṟṟiyuḷḷatu. muttu vīriyattil tīrkkacanti mutaliyavaṟṟiṟku vitikaḷ taṉittaṉiyē kūṟap paṭṭuḷa. kō + il = kōyil, mā + iru = māyiru - mutaliya vaṟṟiṟkut taṉiyē vitikaḷ vakukkappaṭṭuḷa.  cuvāminātattil kuṟippiṭattakum vicēṭamāka ētum iṉṟu. ataṉ āciriya virutta yāppu nayaṉuṟumāṟu illai.

Introduction
Tamil grammar and poetics are old and venerable disciplines interwoven into a complex system the beginnings of which are lost in legend. What is fact, however, is that we are looking back on a textual tradition representing the thought of almost two millennia: a continuous discourse on Tamil language and literature, but also a dispute with other systems of knowledge, most notably the Sanskrit grammatical and poetological traditions. To give a rough chronology, for the first millennium, we have one great treatise encompassing the whole field and developing the basic structure that is taken up, with some modifications and extensions, by the whole later tradition. This is the famous Tolka#ppiyam, consisting of three parts, two of them devoted to two different aspects of grammar, which has been ever since split into two sections, namely ElÈuttu (phonology) and Col (morphology and syntax), while the third part treats of Porul@ (poetics). It is followed by a small work specialising in a particular field of poetics, namely that of Akam (love poetry), called the IrÈaiyan_a#r Akapporul@. 
The second millennium, probably a time of socio-cultural upheaval, sets in with a voluminous commentary tradition not only for the treatises that had been written so far, but also for wide parts of the older literature. In fact our understanding of the meaning of the older texts is basically indebted to these commentaries. Nevertheless, there is a parallel development of new treatises in all sub-disciplines, mirroring the confrontation with the change of language, the arising of new literary forms and the massive impact of North-Indian, i.e. Sanskritic modes of thinking and writing in the Tamilian South. To mention just a few of the most important titles, among the inclusive texts – comprising, just as the Tolka#ppiyam, the whole range of the field – there are the heavily Sanskritised Vi#raco#lÈiyam of the 11th century, and the Tamil-conservative Ilakkan|a Vil@akkam of the 17th century. Both of them extend the original structure of three sections, dealing with ElÈuttu (phonology), Col (morphology and syntax), and Porul@ (poetics), by another two subsumed under Porul@, namely Ya#ppu (metrics) and An|i (figures of speech). Among the influential treatises devoted exclusively to grammar we may list the Nan_n_u#l (12th century), the standard book on Tamil grammar after the Tolka#ppiyam, and the Pirayo#ka Vive#kam (17th century), again very Sanskritic. Poetics, for its part, seems to have been an even more fruitful domain, creating a number of branches with various specia-lisations. The first independent text on metrics is the Ya#pparun)-kalakka#rikai (10th century); the most notable exponent of systematic Akam poetics is the Nampi Akapporul@ (12th century), while the Pur_am genre (heroic poetry) is represented by the Pur_apporul@ven|pa#-ma#lai (9th century).
The encyclopaedia presented here is an attempt to render accessible this wealth of materials to specialists and also to non-specialists. The vast topology and terminology of Tamil grammar and poetics are represented by key terms which are explained with reference to the corresponding su#tras in the treatises and additional explication from the various commentaries. The whole work comprises 17 volumes, structured in the traditional way into the three sections ElÈuttu (phonology), Col (morphology and syntax) and Porul@ (poetics), where 2 volumes fall on ElÈuttu, 4 on Col, and 11 on the various sub-disciplines subsumed under poetics: 2 for Ya#ppu (metrics), 2 for An|i (figures of speech), 4 for Akam (love topics), 1 for Pur_am (heroic topics), 1 for Pa#t@t@iyal (literary genres), Pa#yiram (prefaces) and Marapiyal (word usage), and finally 1 for Meyppa#t@u (physical manifestation), Na#t@akam (drama), Al@avai (valid means of knowledge), A#nantakkur|r|am (collocations to be avoided), Niya#yam (logic) and ValÈuvamaiti (poetic licence). The last of these volumes contains a bibliography.
This sort of work of synthesis has long been a desideratum of research: it gives erudite references to a vast range of technical Tamil texts which are, for the most part, not well understood today. Some of the texts are hard to come by – unless in the editions of the author of this encyclopaedia (on whom more below) –  most of them are not translated into any other language, general introductions into the field are few, and even fewer are written in languages more easily accessible to the general reader (like English or French). There has been more than one project comparable in range in recent years, most prominently the Encyclopaedia of Tamil literature of the Institute of Asian Studies, Chennai (in English language), but sadly this opus has not yet seen more than 3 volumes, the last one already nine years old and reaching only the letter “ai”. All the more reason for scholars interested in Tamil language and literature to be grateful to the author of the present work, the venerable T.V. Gopal Iyer, with his 80 years one of the last living exponents of a great tradition of exegesis.
Space permits here no more than a brief account of the highlights of a long and in many respects exemplary career of a Tamil savant in the 20th century. As well as following a traditional path of education, the worldly marks of which are his two titles Vidvan and Panditam conferred by the University of Madras and the Maturai Tamil Cankam respectively (in 1945 and 1953), Gopal Iyer also acquired the “modern” university degrees of Bachelor of Oriental Language and Bachelor of Oriental Language with Honours at the University of Madras (1951 and 1958). From 1965-1978 he taught in Rajah’s College, Thiruvaiyaru, in which period he already took up his activity of editing works of Classical Tamil, especially theoretical texts. The most important publications from that phase are the Ilakkan|a Vil@akkam in 8 volumes (published in Thanjavur by the Sarasvati Mahal from 1971-1974), the Ilakkan|ak Kottu (Sarasvati Mahal 1973) and the Pirayoka Vivekam (Sarasvati Mahal 1973).
Ever since 1978, Mr. Gopal Iyer’s sphere of activity has shifted to Pondicherry, where he has been (and still is) employed as a research scholar by the École Française d’Extrême-Orient (EFEO) – i.e., the French School of Asian Studies –, a research institution financed by the French government which has 17 research centres spread across Asia, the westernmost of which is that in Pondicherry, and which has the mission of studying Asian (and notably Indian) languages, cultures and religions. In this environment, designed as a meeting place for international research, the enormous preparatory work for this encyclopedia has been accomplished. Part of the voluminous editorial output of Mr. Gopal Iyer during the last 27 years has appeared in a series co-published by the EFEO and the IFP (the French Institute of Pondicherry, another research Institution of the French government in whose premises T.V. Gopal Iyer worked for several years), such as a 3-volume edition of the Te#va#ram, his major contribution to devotional Tamil literature (1984f., 1991), and the Ma#ran_ Akapporul@ (2005). A number of further publications deserve mention, since they concern fundamental texts of the Tamil grammatical and poetological tradition upon which the encyclopaedia is based. Last year he published a 14-volume edition of the complete Tolka#ppiyam with all the commentaries (through Thiru. G. Ela-vazhagan of TamizhMann Pathippagam, Chennai - 17) and this year editions of the Vi#raco#l@iyam and the Ma#r_an_ Alan)ka#ram.
The EFEO is extremely happy that it has been able to contribute its share to this publication of vital importance for the exploration of Tamil literary history, which will be a monument to a most extraordinary man, who has been teacher and adviser, nay, a living encyclopaedia, to so many students of Tamil language, Indian and Western.

A final brief note of thanks to three individuals who were important in bringing this large work to the light of day. The first is Mr. T.V. Gopal Iyer’s younger brother, Mr. T.S. Gangadharan, then of the French Institute of Pondicherry and now of the Pondi-cherry Centre of the EFEO, who wrote the work out in a fair hand.  The second is Dr. Jean-Luc Chevillard, who, years later, had the encyclopeadia digitally photographed when in its yet more voluminous hand-written state and so by his timely intervention prevented the loss of some of its parts.  The third is the publisher, Mr. Ela-vazhagan, of the Thamizh Mann Pathippagam, who had the vision to see the value of this work and took on the task of setting it in type, a labour which took a year and a half and involved five sets of proofs.

palar muyaṉṟu ceyya vēṇṭiya paṇiyai ti.vē.kōpā laiyarē ceytu muṭittuḷḷār. oru palkalaikaḻakamō oru periya āyvu niṟuvaṉamō ceyya vēṇṭiya paṇiyait tamiḻmaṇ patippakam ārvattāl eḷitākac ceytuḷḷatu. tamiḻarkaḷiṉ tavappayaṉē ippaṭiyāyt tamiḻ maṇṇil mukiḻttuḷḷatu.

tamiḻar vāḻviyal ilakkaṇattai varaiyaṟuttuk kūṟum olkāppukaḻ tolkāppiyattiṟkup paḻantamiḻc cāṉṟōrkaḷ paṉṉūṟu āṇṭukaḷukku muṉpu eḻutiya aṉaittu uraikaḷaiyum tokuttu tolkāppiya nūl patippil ituvarai evarum kaṇṭirāta vakaiyil orēvīccil em patippakam veḷiyiṭṭatai tamiḻulakam naṉku aṟiyum.

toṉmaiyum, muṉmaiyum, tāymaiyum, tūymaiyum, meṉmaiyum, mēṉmaiyum, paḻamaiyum, putumaiyum, iḷamaiyum, mutumaiyum mikkamoḻi nam tamiḻmoḻi. tiraviṭamoḻikaḷukkut tāymoḻi nam tamiḻmoḻi. intiyap perumoḻikaḷukku mūlamoḻi nam tamiḻmoḻi. ulaka moḻikaḷukku mūtta moḻi nam tamiḻmoḻi. intiyap pēraracāl cemmoḻi eṉa aṟivikkap paṭṭuḷḷa in nērattil, ‘tamiḻ ilakkaṇap pērakarāti’yai veḷiyiṭuvatil peru makiḻcci aṭaikiṟōm.

iruvakai nilaṉkaḷ -
iruvakai nilaṉkaḷāvaṉa cuvaikkappaṭu poruḷum, ataṉaic cuvaikkum poṟikaḷiṉ cuvaiyuṇarvum ām. vēmpu eṉṉum cuvaikkappaṭuporuḷum, ataṉaic cuvaikka vēṇṭiya nā eṉṉum poṟiyum taṉittaṉi niṉṟavaḻik kaippuc cuvai piṟavātu, iraṇṭum cērntavaḻiyē kaippuc cuvai piṟakkum. atupōla, accattukku mutalākiya vilaṅkum ataṉaik kāṇum kaṇṇiṉ pārvaiyum taṉit taṉi niṉṟavaḻi accaccuvai piṟavātu, avaiyiraṇṭum cērntavaḻiyē accaccuvai piṟakkum eṉpatu.
cilar aṉupavippavaṉuṭaiya aṉupavam kāṇpavarkku eytutalē iruvakai nilam eṉpa.
vēmpu cuvaittavaṉ aṟinta kaippu uṇarviṉai nāvuṇar viṉāl piṟaṉ uṇara muṭiyātu. āyiṉ “ivaṉ vēmpu cuvaittavaṉ” eṉa, avaṉmukattaip pārttu ataṉkaṇ kāṇappaṭum meyppāṭukaḷālēyē aṟivāṉ. atupōla, accattukku ētuvākiya puli mutaliyaṉa kaṇṭu añci ōṭi varukiṉṟavaṉai maṟṟavaṉ kaṇṭavaḻi, “ivaṉ añci varukiṉ ṟāṉ” eṉa aṟivatallatu tāṉ puliyiṉaik kaṇṭāṉ pōla accam koḷḷāṉ. ākavē, añciṉāṉaik kaṇṭu nakutalum karuṇai ceytalum kaṇṭōrkkup piṟappataṉṟi accam piṟavātākaliṉ, cuvaippōṉ nikaḻttiya cuvaiyaik kaṇṭu “ivaṉ cuvaittāṉ” eṉṟu aṟintavaṉ taṉ aṟivut taṉmaiyiṉāl accuvaiyiṉaip peṟāṉ ātaliṉ, cuvaik kappaṭuporuḷum ataṉai nukarnta poṟiyuṇarvumē iruvakai nilam eṉappaṭum.	(to. po. 249 pērā.)

piṟarkkeṉa etaiyum ceyyāta koḷkaiyum, aṟam ceṟinta maṉamuma, amaiti niṟainta kaṇkaḷum, mayakkamum kalakkamumāṉa piṟaḻnta aṟivu nīṅkiya tūya aṟivunilaiyum, etaiyum muyaṉṟaṭaiyum kuṟippillāta nilaiyum, accamaṟṟa perumita nilaiyum, ulakattuyirkaḷpāl kuḷirnta uḷḷamum ceyalum paṭaittiruttalum, aḷaviṭṭu uraikka iyalāta arumaiyum, aṉpum, kaṇṇil oḷiyuṭaimaiyum pōṉṟavai camanilai - cāntaracam. camanilai eṉṉum meyppāṭu (1) -
(1) meyppāṭukaḷai oṉpatu eṉṟu kaṇakkiṭuvōr vīram accam mutaliya eṇvakai meyppāṭukaḷoṭu naṭuvunilai yaiyum cērttuk kaṇakkiṭuvar. naṭuvunilai mattimam eṉavum cāntam eṉavum kūṟappaṭum. itaṉai nilaiyillā meyp pāṭukaḷil tolkāppiyaṉār aṭakkiyuḷḷār. 			(po. 260 pērā.)
naṭuvunilaiyāvatu, kūṟappaṭṭa vīram accam mutalākiya eṇvakaic cuvaiyoṭum tāṉ kalavātu taṉittirukkum ciṟappiṉai uṭaiyatu. taṉmēl cantaṉam pūcupavarmāṭṭum taṉ uṭalai vāḷkoṇṭu tuṇippavarmāṭṭum orē vita maṉanilaiyoṭu kūṭiyiruttalē naṭuvunilaiyākum. innilai kāmam vekuḷi mayakkam mūṉṟum nīṅkiya tāpatar cāraṇar camaṇar mācaṟṟa muṉivar aṟivar mutaliyōrkaṇ nikaḻvatu. avarkaḷiṭam vīram accam mutaliya eṇvakaic cuvaiyum nikaḻā. iṉpa tuṉpaṅkaḷil camanōkkuk koḷḷum iccuvai taṉittuk kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷatu.(245 iḷam. uraimēṟ)
‘camanilai ulakiyal nīṅkiṉār peṟṟi ātaliṉ ulaka vaḻakkup paṟṟiya iyaṟṟamiḻ ilakkaṇattil immeyppāṭu ēṉaiyavaṟṟoṭu kūṭṭic ciṟappittu ōtappaṭṭilatu. 			(251 pērā.)
camanilai eṉṉum meyppāṭu (2) -
camanilai eṉṉum meyppāṭṭiṉuḷ ētēṉum vikāram uṇṭāyiṉ  atu vīram accam mutaliya ēṉaiya eṇvakai meyppāṭṭiṉuḷ aṭaṅkum.	(to. po. 251 pērā.)
naṭuvunilai eṉpatu oṉpatu cuvaiyuḷ oṉṟu eṉa nāṭaka nilaiyuḷ vēṇṭappaṭum camanilai. aḵtāvatu ceñcan taṉattaip pūciṉum uṭalaic cetukkiṉum piḷappiṉum maṉam nilaipēṟu kulaiyātu iṉpatuṉpaṅkaḷil cellātu oru nilaiyilēyē irukkum nilai. atu kāmam vekuḷi mayakkam nīṅkiṉārkaṇṇē nikaḻvatu. atu ciṟuvaraviṟṟāka liṉ nilaiyillā meyppāṭukaḷuṭaṉ cērtteṇṇap paṭṭatu. 	(to. po. 260 pērā.)
naṭuvunilai aimpulaṉ aṭakkiya aṟivarpāl nikaḻvatu. ivar kāmavekuḷi mayakkattiṉiṉṟum nīṅkiyavarātalāl yāvarum kaṇṭañcum iyamaṉukkum ivar añcār; ettuṇai iḷivaral kāṇiṉum iḻittuk kūṟār; kalvi niṟaintirun tum ataṉaip poruḷākak koḷḷāta kāraṇattāl perumitam kāṭṭār; putumai perumai ciṟumai ākkam eṉpaṉa nilaiyil poruḷkaḷ eṉa aṟivarātaliṉ avaṟ ṟiṉaik kaṇṭu viyappuṟār; tuṉpam vanteytāt tūya uḷḷattarākiya ivar iṉpattai viḻaiyār ātaliṉ, camanilai eṉappaṭum naṭuvunilai porunti niṟkum nallō riṭattu, ‘nakaiyē aḻukai iḷivaral maruṭkai, accam perumitam vekuḷi uvakai eṉṟu, appā leṭṭē meyppā ṭeṉpa’ eṉat tollāciriyar vakuttuk kūṟiya eṇvakai meyppāṭum nikaḻāvām; orōvaḻi nikaḻu miṭattuk kāmamiṉṟi aruḷē paṟṟukkōṭāka nikaḻum eṉpatu.	(ma. cū. pak. 5, 6)
ittakaiya maṉanilaiyākiya naṭuvunilai ulakil arukiyē kāṇappaṭutaliṉ, ulakil peruvaraviṟṟāka nikaḻum nakai aḻukai mutaliya eṇvakai meyppāṭṭoṭum itaṉaic cērkkātu, nilaiyillā meyppāṭukaḷākiya ‘uṭaimai iṉpuṟal’ (to. po. 260 pērā.) eṉpavaṟṟoṭu tol kāppiyaṉār cērttuvaittuḷḷār.

nāviṉāl nukarappaṭum kaippu kārppu puḷippu uvarppu tuvarppu tittippu eṉpaṉa. aṉṟik kaṇṇāṉum cevi yāṉum uṇarappaṭum nakai aḻukai iḷivaral maruṭkai accam vīram uvakai vekuḷi eṉṉum eṇvakaippaṭṭa cuvaikaḷ; ivaṟṟoṭu naṭuvunilaiyaic cērttuc cuvai oṉpatu eṉṟalum uṇṭu. iccuvaikōṭal maṉita iṉat tiṟkēyuriya taṉicciṟappu ātaliṉ ēṉaiya uyirkaḷukkuriya nāccuvaiyiṉiṉṟum iccuvaikaḷ pirittu ōtappaṭṭaṉa. ceviyāṉ perumpālum ceyyuḷkaḷ vāyilāka uṇarum iccuvaikaḷai aṟiyātār ‘ceviyiṉ cuvai yuṇarā vāyuṇaviṉ mākkaḷ’ (kuṟaḷ 420) eṉa iḻikkappaṭṭaṉar.
cuvaiyeṉpatu kāṇappaṭu poruḷāl kāṇpōr akattil varuvatoru vikāram.	(to. po. 245. iḷa.)
cuvaiyāvatu nakaimutaliya cuvaikkuriya poruḷum cuvaip pēṟuṭaiya poṟiyum kūṭiyavaḻi nikaḻum maṉa uṇarcci eṉpār pērāciriyar.	(to. po. 249)
vaṭanūlār cuvaiyiṉai iracam eṉpa.
uvakai, aḻukai, cāntam eṉṉum mūṉṟum meṉmaiyāṉa cuvaikaḷ; vekuḷi, iḷivaral, vīram eṉṉum mūṉṟum vaṉmaiyāṉa cuvaikaḷ; nakai, viyappu, accam eṉṉum mūṉṟum vaṉmaikkum meṉmaikkum iṭaippaṭṭa cuvai eṉpatu oru cārār karuttu.
uvakaiyum aḻukaiyum mikumeṉmaic cuvaikaḷ; vekuḷi yum iḷivaralum miku vaṉmaic cuvaikaḷ; nakaiyum naṭuvu nilaiyum maruṭkaiyum caṟṟumeṉmaiyuṭaic cuvaikaḷ; perumitamum accamum caṟṟu vaṉmaiyuṭaiya cuvaikaḷ eṉpatu maṟṟum oru cārār karuttām.  (pērāciriyar - kaṇēcayyar muṉṉurai)
ivai pāṭalkaḷil varumiṭattu, avvac cuvaikku ēṟpa vaṉmai meṉmai iṭaippaṭṭaṉa ākiya coṟkaḷālēyē avvap pāṭalkaḷ iyaṟṟappaṭuvatu ciṟantatu.  (muṉṉurai)
nakai, viyappu (-maruṭkai), uvakai mūṉṟum perumpālum akattiṇaiyiṉaic cārum. vīram, vekuḷi, avalam, accam iḷivaral aintum perumpālum puṟattiṇaiyaic cārum. 
(ma. cū. pak. 6)

cuvaikkappaṭum poruḷum, cuvaikkum poṟiyum oṉṟu cēriṉaṉṟi evvakaic cuvaiyum piṟavātu; ātaliṉ, cuvaipporuḷ cuvaikkum poṟi eṉṉum iraṇṭum cērumpōtu tōṉṟuvatu cuvai.
cuvai, uḷḷattil eḻum kuṟippu, atu puṟattārkkup pulaṉām vakai uṇṭākum viṟal - eṉa immūṉṟum kāraṇa kāriya muṟaiyil tōṉṟuvaṉa. ātaliṉ meyppāṭukaḷ ivai mūṉṟaṉaiyum oruṅkē peṟum.	(nāṭaka. 231, 232)
viṟal, meyppāṭu, cuvai - oru poruḷaṉa.
cuvaikku nilaikkaḷaṉkaḷ -
cuvaippaṭum poruḷ, cuvaiuṇarvu, kuṟippu, cattuvam eṉpaṉa nāṉkum cuvaikkuriya nilaikkaḷaṉkaḷām.
pēyaiyō puliyaiyō kaṇṭa oruvaṉ añciyavaḻi, mayakkamum karattalum naṭukkamum viyarppum uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. accattiṟku ētuvākiya puliyum pēyum cuvaippaṭuporuḷ; avaṟṟaik kaṇṭakālamtoṭṭu nīṅkātu niṉṟa accam cuvaiyuṇarvu; ataṉkaṇ varum mayakkamum kaṇṇai mūṭik koḷḷutalum kuṟippu; naṭukkamum viyarttalum meyppāṭu.
ivaṟṟuḷ naṭukkamum viyarttalum ākiya meyppāṭukaḷāl oruvaṉukku uṇṭāṉa accam maṟṟavarkkum pulappaṭum.

cuvaikkuṟippukkaḷ oṉpatu -
cuvaippaṭuporuḷum cuvaiyuṇarvum oṉṟu kūṭiyavaḻi maṉattiṉkaṇ tōṉṟi malarum kiḷarcci cuvaiyām.
ciruṅkāram mutalāvuḷḷaṉavē cuvaiyāvaṉa. avaṟṟai vīrakkuṟippu, payakkuṟippu, ukkirak kuṟippu, ciruṅkārak kuṟippu, kāruṇiyak kuṟippu, aṟputak kuṟippu, pukaḻk kuṟippu, nakaik kuṟippu, ikaḻccik kuṟippu eṉa oṉpatākak kūṟuvar. 
(vī. cō. 106 urai)
cuvai kōṭal -
itu cuvaiyuṇarvu eṉappaṭum. nakaiyum accamum mutalākiya uṇarvukaḷai muṟkālattu ulakiyalāṉ aṟivāṉoruvaṉ avaṟṟukku ētuvākiya poruḷ piṟa kaṇṭavaḻit tōṉṟiya poṟiyuṇarvukaḷ cuvai eṉappaṭum ātaliṉ, cuvaippaṭum poruḷoṭu cuvaikkum poṟi iṇaintu ceyaṟpaṭutal cuvai kōṭal eṉappaṭum. vēmpu eṉṉum poruḷum nā eṉṉum poṟiyum cērātavaḻik kaippuccuvai piṟavāta vāṟu pōla, apporuḷkaḷaik kaṇṭavaḻiyallatu nakaiyum accamum mutaliya cuvaikaḷ tōṉṟā. oḻinta kāmam mutalāyiṉavum aṉṉa. itaṉāṉ ‘iruvakai nilattiṉ iyalvatu cuvaiyē’ eṉṟār ceyiṟṟiyaṉār.			(to. po. 249 pērā.)
cuvaipporuḷai ‘vipāvam’ eṉavum. cuvainilai avi nayattai ‘°tāyipāvam’ eṉavum vaṭanūlār kūṟupa. (ma. cū. pak. 116) cuvaippaṭum poruḷkaḷai aimpoṟikaḷāṉ nukarntu cuvaikkumāṟṟiṟku utāraṇaṅkaḷ piṉvarumāṟu.
ivaḷ mēṉi aṇai pōṉṟatu - itu paricattāl aṟintu cuvaittatu.
ikkaṉi amutam pōlum - itu nāvāl uṇarntu cuvaittatu.
ivaḷ mēṉi māntaḷir pōlum - itu kaṇṇāl uṇarntu cuvaittatu.
ivaḷ kūntal pūviṉ maṇamuṭaiyatu - itu mūkkāl uṇarntu cuvaittatu.
ivaḷ moḻi yāḻicai pōlum - itu ceviyāl uṇarntu cuvaittatu.
cuvaikōṭal paṟṟi vaṭanūlkaḷ kūṟuvaṉa piṉvarumāṟu :
oruvaṉ oruporuḷaik kaṇṭaviṭattu apporuḷ kāraṇamāka avaṉ maṉattil nuṇmaiyākat tōṉṟum kātal mutaliya cuvaiyiṉ muḷai ‘°tāyipāvam’ (nilaiyāṉa maṉakkaruttu) eṉappaṭum.
accuvai tōṉṟuvataṟkuk kāraṇamāṉa poruḷ ‘vipāvam’ eṉappaṭum. accuvait tōṟṟattai veḷiyē teriyappaṭuttum uḷḷa nikaḻcciyākiya kuṟippu ‘anupāvam’ eṉappaṭum.
accuvait tōṟṟattiṉ virivāy uṭampiṉ vaḻiyāka veḷippaṭum maṉavikāraṅkaḷākiya meyppāṭukaḷ ‘cāttuva pāvam’ eṉṟu peyar peṟum. maṉavikāraṅkaḷākiya meyp pāṭukaḷ vaḷarvataṟkuk kāraṇamāy avaṟṟai niṟaivupaṭutti niṟpaṉavākum tuṇaimeyppāṭukaḷ ‘cañcāripāvam’ eṉappaṭum.
accuvait tōṟṟam vaḷarntu avaṉāl cuvaikkappaṭuta liṉāl cuvai ‘iracam’ eṉap peyar peṟum.
ātaliṉ, oruvaṉiṭattuc cuvaittōṟṟamāvatu vipāvaṅka ḷāl tōṉṟi, anupāvaṅkaḷāl vaḷarntu, cañcāripāvaṅka ḷāl uṟuti peṟṟu avaṉāl cuvaikkappaṭutaliṉ cuvai ākiṉṟatu.
°tāyipāvam eṉappaṭum cuvaittōṟṟam oṉpatu vakaip paṭum. avaiyāvaṉa kātal, cirippu, tuṉpam, kōpam, eḻucci (-muyaṟci) accam, aruvaruppu, viyappu, aṭakkam eṉpaṉa.
kātalmāttiramaṉṟi uvappum uvakaikkuk kāraṇamākum. ivai oruvaṉuḷḷattil tōṉṟiyapōtu nilaiyāyt taṭaippaṭāmal vaḷarntu, pāl tayirāṉāṟ pōlac cuvai yāka māṟṟappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. cuvaiyum, ciruṅkāram (uvakai), hā°yam (nakai), karuṇai (avalam) uruttiram (vekuḷi), vīram (perumitam), payāṉakam (accam), pīpatcam (iḷivaral), aṟputam (maruṭkai), cāntam (naṭuvunilai) eṉa oṉpatu vakaippaṭum.
vipāvamum, ālampana vipāvam, uttīpana vipāvam eṉa iruvakaittu.
ālampana vipāvamāvatu, oruvaṉ uḷḷattil tōṉṟum cuvait tōṟṟattiṟku nērē kāraṇamāyuḷḷa poruḷākum.
uttīpana vipāvamāvatu, cuvait tōṟṟattiṟkuk kāraṇamā yuḷḷa aṭippaṭaip poruḷaic cārntum cārāmalum porunti nuṭpamāṉa cuvaittōṟṟattai vaḷarkkum poruḷ.
iḷampūraṇarum pērāciriyarum uḷḷattil nikaḻvataṉaik kuṟippu eṉṟum, kuṟippukkaḷ tōṉṟiya uḷḷattiṉ nikaḻcci yāṉ uṭampil uṇṭākum vēṟupāṭukaḷaic cattuvam eṉṟum kūṟuvar.
vaṭanūlāruḷ cilar, uṭampiṉ uruviṉiṉṟu tōṉṟum maṉōtarmattai anupāvam (kuṟippu) eṉṟum, uṭampiṉ vaḻiyāka veḷippaṭum maṉavikāraṅkaḷaic cāttuvikam eṉṟum kūṟuvar. cāttuvikapāvam anupāvattiṉiṉṟu vēṟupaṭṭatum vēṟupaṭātatumāy iruttaliṉ, anupāvat tiṉuḷ cāttuvikapāvam aṭaṅkum eṉṟa karuttum uṇṭām. ākavē vaṭanūlār karuttuppaṭi cuvaikōṭal kīḻkkaṇṭavaṟṟāl nikaḻum.
1.	°tāyipāvam - maṉattil tōṉṟum cuvaiyiṉ muḷai
2.	vipāvam - cuvai tōṉṟuvataṟkuk kāraṇamāṉa poruḷ
3.	anupāvam - cuvaiyait terivikkum uḷḷa nikaḻcci yākiya kuṟippu.
4.	cāttuvika pāvam - kuṟippaip pulappaṭuttum meyppāṭu.
5.	cañcāripāvam - meyppāṭukaḷai niṟaivupaṭuttum tuṇaimeyppāṭu
6.	iracam - cuvait tōṟṟattaic cuvaikkum cuvai.

tam iṟai = aracaṉ; tam miṟai - tam tavaṟukaḷ eṉa iruvakaiyākap poruḷ koḷka.	(meyp. 8 pārati)
talai nōvu uṟṟōṉ avinayam -
(1) oru nilaiyil illāmal talai āṭikkoṇṭē iruttal, (2) enta iṭattilum nērāka amara muṭiyāta nilai aṭaital, (3) uṟcākam taḷarntiruttal, (4) neṟṟiyai viral kaḷukku iṭaiyil koṇṭu nerukkutal, (5) kaṇkaḷ nōvi ṉāl varuntic curuṅkutal mutaliyaṉa. ivai aṭiyārkku nallār kāṭṭuvaṉa.	(nāṭaka. 253)
talaimayir muṭinekiḻtal -
talaivaṉ talaiviyiṉ uḷḷam taṉṉiṭattu īṭupaṭṭataṉai aṟintu avaḷ meyyait tīṇṭiyavaḻi, itaṟku muṉ aṟiyāta ipputiya paricattāl taṉṉuḷḷattu nikaḻnta vēṟupāṭṭait talaivi aṭakkik koḷḷa muyalvāḷ. appoḻutu uṭampoṭu toṭarpuṭaimaiyum toṭar piṉmaiyum eṉṟa nilaiyiraṇṭu muṭaiya mayirmuṭi maṉattil ēṟpaṭṭa nekiḻcciyāṉ aviḻtalām. 	 (262 pērā.)
itaṉai vēṭkai eṉṟa iraṇṭām avattaiyiṉ mutal meyppāṭu eṉpar iḷampūraṇar. kaḷavil puṇarccikku muṟpaṭṭa nāṉkunāṉku pakuppiṉavākiya mukkūṟṟup paṉṉirumeyppāṭukaḷuḷ iḵtu iraṇṭām kūṟṟiṉ mutal meyppāṭu eṉpar ēṉaiyōr.
talaivikkut talaivaṉ kuṟippu aṟintavaḻi nikaḻvaṉa -
talaivaṉ kuṟitta puṇarcci vēṭkaiyaiyē talaiviyum uḷḷattuk koḷvāḷāyiṉ oruvarai oruvar cantitta lākiya purimukam purinta piṉṉar, (1) poṟinutal viyarttal, (2) naku nayam maṟaittal. (3) citaivu piṟarkkiṉmai, (4) kūḻai virittal, (5) kātoṉṟu kaḷaital, (6) ūḻaṇi taivaral, (7) uṭai peyart tuṭuttal, (8) alkul taivaral, (9) aṇintavai tiruttal, (10) ilvaliyuṟuttal, (11) irukaiyum eṭuttal eṉṉum patiṉoru meyppāṭukaḷum talaivikku muṟaiyē nikaḻum.	(to. po. 97 nac.)
talaivaṉatu kuṟippaṟintu ataṟku uṭaṉpāṭṭait taṉ kaṇṇiṉāl terivitta piṉṉarum, talaivi peṇmaiyāl kūṟṟu nikaḻātu. avaḷ taṉ vēṭkaiyaik kaṇṇālēyē kuṟippiṭuvāḷ.	(94 iḷa.)
taṟukaṇ -
itu perumitam eṉṉum meyppāṭṭiṟkuriya kuṟippu nāṉkaṉuḷ iraṇṭāvatu.
taṟukaṇ - añcattakkaṉa kaṇṭavaḻi añcāmai. maṭalēṟum talaivaṉ, “yāṉ pōrkkaḷattil valiya yāṉaimēl ēṟip pōr ceyya vallavaṉ” (kali. 141) eṉṟu kūṟuvatu avaṉ taṟukaṇmai paṟṟiya perumitattukku eṭuttukkāṭṭu.			(to. po. 257 pērā.)
taṉmai -
taṉmai eṉpatu cāti iyalpu.
pārppār, aracar, iṭaiyar, kuṟavar eṉṟu iṉṉōr māṭṭu oruvarai oruvar ovvāmal kiṭakkum iyalpu. atu naṭai uṭai pāvaṉaikaḷāl meykkaṇ kiṭantu veḷip paṭutaliṉ meyppāṭāyiṟṟu.
pārppāṉ vayalaikkoṭi pōṉṟu vāṭiya iṭaiyiṉai yum, taḷarnta naṭaiyiṉaiyum uṭaiyaṉ (puṟa nā. 315) eṉavum, aracaṉ pakaivar viṭutta ampiṉ taḻumpupaṭṭa paranta aḻakiya mārpiṉaṉ (puṟanā. 13) eṉavum, iṭaiyaṉ cāyam ēṟṟiya āṭaiyaiyum kāyām pūviṉālākiya muṭimālai yaiyum āṉiraikaḷai ōṭṭutaṟkuriya kōliṉaiyum uṭaiyaṉ (kali. 108) eṉavum, kuṟavaṉ tēṉum mayiṟpīliyum koṇṭu varupavaṉ eṉavum naṭaiuṭai ceyal mutaliyaṉa koṇṭu iṉṉāṉ eṉṟu uṇara utavum meyppāṭu.
(to. po. 256 iḷa.)

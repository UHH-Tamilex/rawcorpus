kālattil tāṉ atai uṇarnta nāṉ, accelvaṅkaḷait tokuttu veḷiyiṭa muyaṟci mēṟkoṇṭēṉ. eṉiṉum avariṉ maṟaivukkap piṉṉarē avaṟṟiṟku nūlvaṭivam tara emmāl iyaṉṟatu. accelvaṅ
kaḷait tamiḻulakiṟku vaḻaṅkiyataṉ mūlam, makaṉ tantaikkāṟṟum kaṭamaiyai, kaviṉ kalaiccelviyām tamiḻ aṉṉaikku āṟṟum toṇṭiṉai niṟaivēṟṟiya maṉaniṟaivum koṇṭēṉ. toṭarntu appaṇiyai em vāḻviṉ ilakkākak koṇṭu, ollum vakai
yellām ceyalāṟṟi varukiṉṟēṉ.

kaḷaiyum orē nērattil muḻut tokuppākap patippikka eṇṇiyiruntēṉ. innilaiyil, 1999-il muṉaivar iḷavaracu vaḻi, moḻikkāvalar kō.iḷavaḻakaṉ naṭpiṉaip peṟṟēṉ. tamiḻmaṇ patippakattiṉ mutal veḷiyīṭu muṭiyaracaṉ nūlkaḷē. muḻut tokaippaiyum tamiḻmaṇ veḷiyiṭum eṉa appōtu avar kūṟiṉār. ippōtu atu kaṉintatu. muṭiyaracaṉār paṭaippu

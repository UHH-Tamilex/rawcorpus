2. mika mutaṉmaiyāṉa aṭippaṭaic ceytikaḷ caṟṟē nīḷamāyiruppiṉum muḻumai yākat tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ēṉaiyavai antantap pirivukkup poruttamāṉa toṭar uraiyil āṅkāṅkē ēraṇa muṟaippaṭi antanta aṟiñar coṉṉa vāṟō, cilaviṭaṅkaḷil curukkiyō tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. tamiḻiṉ, tamiḻariṉ ciṟappaiyum taṉittaṉmaiyaiyum niṟuvum paṉṉāṭ ṭaṟiñar kūṟṟukaḷait tamiḻait tāymoḻiyākak koḷḷāta varkkum terivikka vēṇṭumāyiṉ āṅkila mūlaṅkaḷ tēvai; ākaiyāl pala iṭaṅkaḷil āṅkila mūlaṅkaḷ tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa; avaṟṟait tēṭic cellum tollaiyaik kuṟaittiṭa. nūliṉ iṟutiyil takka viḷakkappaṭaṅkaḷum nilappaṭaṅkaḷum tarap paṭṭuḷḷaṉa.
 3. it tokuppiṟku tamiḻmaṇ patippakam 2008 / 2009il veḷiyiṭṭa “tamiḻar varalāṟu - iṉṟaiya nōkkil”, “ulaka aṟiñarkaḷ pārvaiyil tamiḻ” ākiya nūlkaḷum, ulakat tamiḻārāycci niṟuvaṉam 2008il veḷiyiṭṭa “nāstirātik - ñālamutaṉmoḻi āyvukaḷukkup pāvāṇar tarum oḷi” eṉṉum nūlum payaṉ paṭṭuḷḷaṉa. pala putiya ceytikaḷum cērkkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

patippurai
māntaṉ pēciya mutaṉmoḻikku mikaneruṅkiyatum, iṉṟum pēcappaṭuvatum āṉa moḻi tamiḻ ākum eṉpatai iṉṟu paṉṉāṭṭup paltuṟai aṟiñarkaḷum ēṟṟuḷḷaṉar.
tamiḻ muṉmaiyum toṉmaiyum mikka moḻi; attakaiya moḻikkuc contakkārarkaḷ nām eṉpatu perumitam koḷḷattakkatu.
immoḻiyiṉ vaḷamai, tūymai, talaimaippaṇpu ākiyavaṟṟaip piṟanāṭṭu aṟiñarkaḷ ēṟattāḻa 150 āṇṭu kaḷākavē āyvu ceytu ataṉ ciṟappukaḷai ēṟṟu veḷippaṭutti uḷḷaṉar.
itu paṟṟiya ātārapūrvamāṉa ceytikaḷait tokuttu namakku vaḻaṅkupavar aṟiñar pi. irāmanātaṉ āvār. avar tamiḻum āṅkilamum vallavar; moḻiñāyiṟu tēvanēyap pāvaṇariṉ vaḻinilai āyvaṟiñar; varalāṟṟut tuṟaiyilum āḻaṅkāl paṭṭavar; tamiḻiya āyvuppulaṅkaḷ palavaṟṟilum toṭarntu paṅkaḷippu ceytu varupavar; ek karuttaiyum uriya ātāraṅkaḷuṭaṉ naṭuvunilaiyilaṟivulakattiṉ muṉ vaippavar.

“tirāviṭa” moḻik kuṭumpamā? “tamiḻiya” moḻikkuṭumpamā? moḻiyiyalil tamiḻiya (Tamilian) moḻikkuṭumpam eṉṟē kālṭuvel kālam varai vaḻaṅkiya peyarai avar tāṉ “tirāviṭa” moḻik kuṭumpam eṉa māṟṟi vaḻaṅkiṉār. tamiḻ eṉpataṉ koccait tiripu vaṭivamē tirāviṭam eṉpatākum. makāvamcattil “tamiḷa”, taṇṭi eḻutiyaavanticuntari kataiyil “tramiḷa” eṉṟa collum varukiṉṟaṉa. ikṣvāku kuṭimara piṉariṉ pirākirutak kalveṭṭilum ‘tamiḷa’ tāṉ varukiṟatu. piṉṉar kumārilapaṭṭariṉtantiravārttikattil “tatyatā tirāviṭāti pāṣāyam ēva” (appaṭi tirāviṭa (=tamiḻ) maṟṟum piṟamoḻikaḷil) eṉṉum iṭattil tamiḻ-tamiḷa-tramiḷa-tramiṭa-traviṭatirāviṭa eṉṟu māṟiviṭṭatu. ē.ci. parṉel 1872 intiyaṉ āṉṭikuvāri mutal tokutiyil mēṟkaṇṭa tantira vārttikap pakutiyai acciṭṭapoḻutu apattamāka, “āntra trāviṭa pāṣāyam ēva” eṉṟu acciṭṭār (mēlum paṟpala piḻaikaḷuṭaṉ). intat tavaṟāṉa vācakattai kālṭuvel (1875) sṭeṉ kōṉō (Linguistic Survey of India: 1906) pōṉṟōr piṉpaṟṟiṉar. ittavaṟai pi.ṭi. cīṉivācaaiyaṅkār intiyaṉ āṉṭikuvāri 42ām tokutiyil (1913) teḷḷat teḷivāka niṟuviyuḷḷār. Considering the fact that Tamil’s vast word-hoard could be traced to its own elementary native roots and further that some of the most ancient forms (so far as we know) have been handed down to us in their pristine nature, Tamil represents within certain limits the most conservative of Dravidian dialects. This view receives confirmation from the fact that many of the elementary roots of Tamil are found (with modifications and alterations of various degrees) in a large number of other dialects. “In this view then, if a Tamil word could be satisfactorily explained as being normally derived from a native Tamil root itself, or a combination of roots, it would be futile to construct proto-forms of words, obscuring the relationships of root and word in Tamil, unless	 indeed the affinities of Tamil words and roots with those of other dialects demand it.”
- L.V. Ramaswami Iyer

“Primitive underived Indo-Europeanisms discoverable in the
Dravidian languages (in current parlance: ‘proto-Indo European features
derived from Dravidian”):-
1. The use of n, as in Greek to prevent hiatus
2. The existence of gender in the pronouns of the third person, and in
verbs, and in particular the existence of a neuter gender.
3. The use of d or t as the sign of the neuter singular of demonstrative
pronouns of the third person.
4. The existence of a neuter plural, as in Latin, in short a.
5. The formation of the remote demonstrative from a base in a; the
proximate from the base in i.
6. The formation of most preterites, as in Persian by the addition of d.
7. The formation of some preterites by the reduplication of a portion
of the root.
8. The formation of a considerable number of verbal nouns by
lengthening the vowel of the verbal root.”

1. tolkāppiyam
tolkāppiyam māntaṉ aṟivāṟṟal evvaḷavu viyattaku uccanilai eytakkūṭum eṉpataik kāṭṭum ciṟanta cāṉṟukaḷuḷ oṉṟu One of the finest monuments of human intelligence. ilakkiyak koḷkaiyil vēṟu entap paṇṭaiya moḻi kaḷilum illāta ciṟanta karuttukkaḷait tolkāppiyam: poruḷatikāram - ceyyuḷiyal kūṟukiṟatu. kē.vi. cuvelepil tolkāppiyac ceyyuḷiyal kōṭpāṭṭuc ciṟappu: “tolkāppiyam aṭippaṭai nūlāka iruppatuilakkaṇa ilakkiya āyvukaḷukku maṭṭumaṉṟu; mānta iṉap paraval cārnta puviyiyal, kumutāya māntaviyal, paṇpāṭu, cūḻ nilaiyiyal, uḷaviyal ākiya pulaṅkaḷ cārnta ariya ceytikaḷ annūlil uḷḷaṉa; eṉavē tamiḻar nākarikam - paṇpāṭu cārnta (ēṉ potuvāṉa mānta iṉa nākarikam - paṇpāṭu cārnta) āyvukaḷukkut tolkāppiyam mika iṉṟiyamaiyātatu. eṉiṉum ilakkiyam kūṟuvatellām (akkālattil nilaviya) uṇmaik kumutāya nilaiyai nēraṭiyākak kāṭṭukiṟatu eṉṟu eṇṇutal tavaṟu; uṇmai nilaiyai viruppu veṟuppiṉṟi aṟiviyal pūrvamākak kāṭṭukiṟatu eṉṟu eṇṇutal mēlum tavaṟākum. akkāla āṇ peṇmakkaḷiṉ akam, puṟa vāḻk kaiyai uḷḷatu uḷḷapaṭi tatrupamākac caṅkap pulavarkaḷuṭaiya pāṭalkaḷ kāṭṭuvatākak kumuka varalāṟṟāciriyar cilar muṉṉar koṇṭuḷḷaṉar. āṉāl uṇmai eṉṉa? ceyyuḷ ‘uṇmai vāḻkkai’ nikaḻccikaḷai antanta nikaḻcci eppaṭi irukka vēṇṭumeṉṟa ilakkiyamarapu uḷḷatō anta marapukkuk kaṭṭuppaṭṭē cittirikkiṟatu. atāvatu marapuk kēṟṟa māṟṟaṅkaḷuṭaṉ tāṉ cittirikkiṟatu. “uṇmai vāḻkkai” vēṟu; atai ilakkiyattil kāṭṭum marapu vēṟu; eṉpataip pulavarkaḷum poruḷilakkaṇavāciriyarum uṇarn tiruntaṉar. uṇarntiruntataṉāltāṉ uṇmai vāḻkkaiyiṉ kūṟṟukkaḷ ceykaikaḷai ‘ulakiyal vaḻakku’ eṉṟum ilakkiyattil (ceyyuḷil) vāḻakkaiyaic cittarikkum marapaic ceyyuḷ vaḻakku “pulaṉeṟi vaḻakku” eṉṟum kuṟit taṉar. cila nikaḻccikaḷ, ceytikaḷai ippaṭi ippaṭittāṉ kāṭṭa vēṇṭum eṉṟu ilakkiyap paṭaippāḷarkaḷum vācakarkaḷum tamakkuḷ icaintu uruvākkum mēlvaric caṭṭamākiya marapē ilakkiya marapākum. (e.kā. ovvoru tiṇaikkum uriya mutal, karu, uripporuḷkaḷ). nikaḻcci/ ceyti vācakarukkut terintatu tāṉ; ataṉai oru putiya pārvaiyil, kōṇattil (marapai aṭippaṭaiyil mīṟāmal) kāṭṭum poḻutu vācakar anta nayattai naṉku uṇarntu pārāṭṭa iyalkiṟatu. akappāṭal etuvum orutaṭavaikūṭat talaivaṉ, talaivi peyaraic cuṭṭuvatillai. talaivaṉum talaiviyum eppoḻutum iṉṉār eṉṟu kuṟippāka aṭaiyāḷam kāṭṭa muṭiyātavarkaḷē. eṉavē akap pāṭalkaḷ cittirikkum uṇarvukaḷ (ṭapiḷpu. ec. haṭcaṉ tamatu “ilakkiyap payiṟcikkāṉa muṉṉurai” 1946 nūlpakkam 97il colvatu pōla) “potuvāka enta āṭavaṉukkum peṇ ṇukkum poruntuvatāka irukkumēyaṉṟi ivaṉukkut tāṉ/ ivaḷukkuttāṉ poruntum eṉṟu irukkātu. (uḷḷa uṇarvukaḷai viḷakkātu) veḷiyulakai virit turaikkum puṟappāṭalkaḷil cila potuvāka aṉaivarum nōkkum pārvaiyil irukkum; cila antantap pulavaruṭaiya taṉi nōkkil amaintatāka irukkum. piṉṉavaṟṟukku eṭuttuk kāṭṭu kaiyaṟunilaip pāṭalkaḷ. vaḷḷalkaḷ, naṇparkaḷ iṟantāl pulavarkaḷ tam neñciṉ āḻattiliruntu uṇarvu kaḷaik koṭṭip pāṭum tuyarap pāṭalkaḷ ovvoṉṟum taṉittaṉmai vāyntavai; potuvāṉa nāṭṭuppuṟa op pārikaḷiṉ vālāyap paṭimaṅkaḷaik koṇṭavaiyalla.”
- kē.vi. cuvelepil
2. tirukkuṟaḷ
“intiya makkaḷiṭaiyē paṇṭaik kālattiliruntē piṟa uyirkaḷ aṉaittukkum aṉpukāṭṭi utavum aṟakkoḷkai kaḷ (the idea of active love)” iruntu vantatai intiyap paṇṭaiya ilakkiyaṅkaḷilum kāṇum kataikaḷil iruntu aṟikiṟōm. attuṭaṉ kuṟippāka tirukkuṟaḷ eṉṉum nūlil kāṇum aṟavuraic ceyyuḷkaḷiliruntum aṟikiṟōm. “(ulakil piṟa uyirkaḷiṭatta aṉpu kāṭṭic ceyal paṭuvatai (life affirmation) avvaḷavāka valiyuṟuttāta maṉutarma cāttiram pōṉṟavaṟṟiliruntu māṟupaṭum) kuṟaḷil ulakaiyum vāḻkkaiyaiyum veṟuttu otukkum pōkku (ṟuṭicaṭaṉa World and life negation) paravalākak kāṇappaṭuvatu alla. eṅkō ōriru iṭattil vēṇṭumāṉāl irukkalām. “pirāmaṇīyam, puttamatam pakavatkītai pōṉṟavaṟṟaip pōl immai maṟumaip payaṉ karutiyē aṟaneṟiyil niṟṟal vēṇṭum eṉṟa vāṇikak koḷkaiyaik kuṟaḷ valiyuṟuttu vatu illai. nallatu ceyvatē takkatu eṉṟa uṇarvi ṉālēyē nallatu (aṟam) ceyya vēṇṭum eṉṟu kuṟaḷ kūṟukiṟatu (kuṟaḷkaḷ 222,211 pakavatkītaiyō ‘karumam ceytu koṇṭiruppatu pirapañca niyati’ eṉṟu vaṟaṭṭut taṉamāka valintu kūṟuki ṟatu. āṉā l , eṉṉa vi ya p pu , ku ṟa ḷō ma ṉi taṉ uḻaippatum īṭṭuvatum piṟarukkum piṟa uyirkaḷukkum avaṉ naṉmai ceyvataṟkākattāṉ eṉṟu cāṟṟukiṟatu. (kuṟaḷkaḷ 81,112) pakavat kītaippaṭi kaṭamai jātikkut takkapaṭi vēṟupaṭum. kuṟaḷō makkaḷ aṉaivarum ‘nallavai ceytoḻukak” kūṟukiṟatu. “ēṉai intiya tarmacāstirakkārar `uḻaippatil, muyaṟci ceyvatil makiḻvōṭu īṭupaṭu’ eṉṟu kūṟavillai. kuṟaḷkaḷ 619,630 pōṉṟavaiyō ulakiyalilum, vāḻkkaiyilum, ārvattuṭaṉ īṭupaṭumpaṭi aṟivuṟuttukiṉṟaṉa. “(pauttamum kītaiyum pōlap paṟṟiṉmai, veṟup piṉmai, kollāmai, akimcai ākiyavaṟṟaiyum kuṟaḷ valiyuṟuttiṉālum kūṭa atē camayattil) kuṟaḷ atikamāka 86 paṉṉāṭṭaṟiñar pārvaiyil - tamiḻum tamiḻarum valiyuṟuttuvatu aṉpum aruḷumē yām (the living ethic of love). kāṇka kuṟaḷkaḷ 72,78,79, 103,226,241.”
“aṟaneṟicār ulaka vāḻkkai ilaṭciyattait tiṟam paṭac cuṭṭukiṟatu kuṟaḷ. maṉitaṉiṉ taṉi vāḻkkai. piṟa uyirka ḷōṭum ulakattōṭum uṟaḻum avaṉ vāḻkkai ākiyavaṟṟil kaikkoḷḷa vēṇṭiya neṟikaḷaik kuṟaḷ viyattaku paṇ puṭaṉum cālpuṭaṉum naṭaimuṟaikkukanta vakaiyil vakut tuḷḷatu (characterised by nobility and good sense) . ulaka ilakkiyattil vēṟu eṅkum ivvaḷavu ciṟanta oḷi mayamāṉa aṟavurai vācakaṅkaḷai vaḻaṅkum nūlaik kāṇa iyalātu; (kuṟaḷkaḷ 92, 105, 108, 121, 159, 162, 216, 298, 319, 578, 594, 628, 757, 782, 874, 931, 973, 999, 1007, 1024, 1032”
“āka kiṟittuva ūḻit toṭakkak kālattilēyē intiya makkaḷiṭaiyē ulaka vāḻkkaiyiṭamum uyirkaḷiṭamum paṇpum aṉpum kāṭṭum oru taṉmai iyalpāka iruntu vantuḷḷataik kuṟaḷiliruntu aṟikiṟōm . pirāmaṇiya m, pauttam , pakavatkītai cār intu matam ākiyavaṟṟiṉ kōṭpāṭukaḷil illātatu atu. āṉāl kīḻcātiyiṉariṭamum, cātāraṇa makkaḷiṭamum tōṉṟi makkaḷōṭu makkaḷāka vāḻnta ciṟanta camayap pōtakarkaḷ mūlamāka attaṉmai metumetuvāka nāḷaṭaivil intu matattil iṭam peṟalāyiṟṟu.” - ālparṭ cuvaiṭcar: intiyac cintaṉaiyum ataṉ vaḷarcciyum Indian Thought and its development: 1936

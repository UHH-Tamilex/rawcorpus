“tirāviṭa” moḻik kuṭumpamā? “tamiḻiya” moḻikkuṭumpamā? moḻiyiyalil tamiḻiya (Tamilian) moḻikkuṭumpam eṉṟē kālṭuvel kālam vari vaḻaṅkiya peyari avar tāṉ “tirāviṭa” moḻik kuṭumpam eṉa māṟṟi vaḻaṅkiṉār. tamiḻ eṉpataṉ koccit tiripu vaṭivamē tirāviṭam eṉpatākum. makāvamcattil “tamiḷa”, taṇṭi eḻutiyaavanticuntari katiyil “tramiḷa” eṉṟa collum varukiṉṟaṉa. ikṣvāku kuṭimara piṉariṉ pirākirutak kalveṭṭilum ‘tamiḷa’ tāṉ varukiṟatu. piṉṉar kumārilapaṭṭariṉtantiravārttikattil “tatyatā tirāviṭāti pāṣāyam ēva” (appaṭi tirāviṭa (=tamiḻ) maṟṟum piṟamoḻikaḷil) eṉṉum iṭattil tamiḻ-tamiḷa-tramiḷa-tramiṭa-traviṭatirāviṭa eṉṟu māṟiviṭṭatu. ē.ci. parṉel 1872 intiyaṉ āṉṭikuvāri mutal tokutiyil mēṟkaṇṭa tantira vārttikap pakutiyi acciṭṭapoḻutu apattamāka, “āntra trāviṭa pāṣāyam ēva” eṉṟu acciṭṭār (mēlum paṟpala piḻikaḷuṭaṉ). intat tavaṟāṉa vācakatti kālṭuvel (1875) sṭeṉ kōṉō (Linguistic Survey of India: 1906) pōṉṟōr piṉpaṟṟiṉar. ittavaṟi pi.ṭi. cīṉivācaiyaṅkār intiyaṉ āṉṭikuvāri 42ām tokutiyil (1913) teḷḷat teḷivāka niṟuviyuḷḷār. Considering the fact that Tamil’s vast word-hoard could be traced to its own elementary native roots and further that some of the most ancient forms (so far as we know) have been handed down to us in their pristine nature, Tamil represents within certin limits the most conservative of Dravidian dialects. This view receives confirmation from the fact that many of the elementary roots of Tamil are found (with modifications and alterations of various degrees) in a large number of other dialects. “In this view then, if a Tamil word could be satisfactorily explined as being normally derived from a native Tamil root itself, or a combination of roots, it would be futile to construct proto-forms of words, obscuring the relationships of root and word in Tamil, unless	 indeed the affinities of Tamil words and roots with those of other dialects demand it.”
- L.V. Ramaswami Iyer

“Primitive underived Indo-Europeanisms discoverable in the
Dravidian languages (in current parlance: ‘proto-Indo European features
derived from Dravidian”):-
1. The use of n, as in Greek to prevent hiatus
2. The existence of gender in the pronouns of the third person, and in
verbs, and in particular the existence of a neuter gender.
3. The use of d or t as the sign of the neuter singular of demonstrative
pronouns of the third person.
4. The existence of a neuter plural, as in Latin, in short a.
5. The formation of the remote demonstrative from a base in a; the
proximate from the base in i.
6. The formation of most preterites, as in Persian by the addition of d.
7. The formation of some preterites by the reduplication of a portion
of the root.
8. The formation of a considerable number of verbal nouns by
lengthening the vowel of the verbal root.”

iṉṟu intiya nākarikam eṉṟu vaḻaṅkappaṭum nākarikak kūṟukaḷil perumpālāṉavi uṇmiyil tamiḻar, tamiḻiya nākarikak kūṟukaḷē “intiya nākarikattiṉ aṭittaḷam tirāviṭa (tamiḻ) moḻi - paṇpāṭu ākiyaviyē” eṉpar ṭilar. “All of Indian civilisation is built on an underlying base of Dravidian language and culture” - S.A. Tyler (1973) India an Anthropological perspective.
1. tol tamiḻiyac cintu nākarikam tirāviṭa moḻi pēcunar paṇṭu teṟkiliruntuvaṭakkākap paraviya poḻutu (Dravidian Ascent) kuṭiyēṟiya pakutiyil uruvākkiyatu cintuveḷi nākarikam, annākarikam 1947kku muntiya intiyā, pākistāṉaṉ, vaṅkāḷa tēcam cērnta intiyāviṉ parappaḷavil kāl pakutikku mēl parappaḷavu koṇṭa perunilappakutiyil vaṭamēṟku intiyāvil paravi iruntatu. atāvatu cumār 5 laṭcam caturamil parappaḷavil vaḻaṅkiyatu. paṇṭiya mecapotāmiya, ekiptiya nākarikaṅ kaḷiṉ parappiviṭa atikamāṉa parappil cintuveḷi nākarikam vaḻaṅkiyatu. annākarikam vaḻaṅkiya pakutiyil ki.mu. 2000 vākkil mottam aintiliruntu pattu laṭcam makkaḷ vāḻn tirukkalām eṉpar posel, parpōlā pōṉṟavarkaḷ. irpāṉ hapīp 2011 nūlil 40 laṭcam pēr vāḻntirukkalām eṉpar. (appoḻutu ulakil irunta motta makkaḷ tokiyē 9 kōṭikku kuṟivu). cintu veḷi nārikac ciṉṉaṅkaḷ 1500kkum mēṟpaṭṭa iṭaṅkaḷil uḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟil cumār 20 iṭaṅkaḷil maṭṭumē mokeñcotarō, harappā uṭpaṭa - akaḻvāyvu niṟuviyuḷḷatu. annākarikattiṉ ciṟappuṟṟa nili, ki.mu. 3200- 1800 kāla aḷavic cārntatu. mokañcatarō kara makkaḷ toki 40,000 eṉṟum harappā nakara makkaḷ toki 25000 eṉṟum matippiṭappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. māntar iṉa (AMH) paraval, moḻiyiṉ tōṟṟamum paravalum ākiyavaṟṟiṉ aṭippaṭiyil tamiḻiya moḻi pēcunar paṇṭu teṟkiliruntu vaṭakkākap paraviya poḻutu kuṭiyēṟiya pakutiyē cintuveḷi nākarikap pakuti eṉṟu Dravidian Ascent mēlē kaṇṭōm. annākarikam tirāviṭac cārpuṭiyatu eṉpataṟku uḷḷa mēlum pala valuvāṉa ātāraṅkaḷil cila varumāṟu:-
i. mokañcotarō - harappā iṭipāṭukaḷil tirāviṭak kaṭṭumāṉak kaliyik kāṇalām. vīṭukaḷil payaṉpaṭṭa poruḷkaḷ, nakikaḷ, parutti āṭikaḷ aṉittum tirāviṭac cārpuṭiyavi. caṅka nūlkaḷil kāṇum kaṭṭaṭakkali cintuveḷi nākarikak kaṭṭaṭakkaliyiṉ toṭarcciyē. eṭi aḷavukaḷ; ciṟṟilakka muṟi; 4 ce.mī (= aṅkulam)kōl; 2 3/4ō4 = 11 ākum 11,22,33 eṉṟavāṟu 11ṉ maṭaṅkāka varum aḷavukaḷ, pōṉṟavi yellām cintuveḷik kālattiliruntu toṭarntu varu paviyē (es. caṇmukam 1980; ku. vēṅkaṭācalam 1983, teyva nāyakam 2002)
(ii) ceṉṉiyil ulakat tamiḻārāycci niṟuvaṉattil 4- 2-2011l nikaḻttiya tamatu ariya uriyil ār. pālakiruṣṇaṉ I.A.S caṅka ilakkiyattil kāṇum ērāḷamāṉa ūrppeyar kaḷit tāṅkiya ūrkaḷ, nakaraṅkaḷ iṉṟu cintuppakutiyil (iṉṟu pākistāṉ - ālkāṉistāṉ) mikappala uḷḷaṉa eṉpati niṟuviyuḷḷār. avaṟṟuḷ pala iṉṟum koṟki, vañci, toṇṭi, maturi, uṟi, kūṭal, kōḷi, pōṉṟa tamiḻp peyarkaḷip pōlavē olikkap paṭukiṉṟaṉa. mali, kōṭu, vari, kuṉṟu pōṉṟa potup peyarkaḷ evvita muṉṉoṭṭum iṉṟi iṭappeyarkaḷāka aṅku uḷḷaṉa. tamiḻil vaḻaṅkum āṟṟupa peyarkaḷip pōṉṟaviyum (kāviri, poruni, kāriyāṟu mutaliyavi) aṅku uḷḷaṉa. caṅka ilakkiyak kaṭavuḷariṉ peyarkaḷ; avvilakkiyam cuṭṭum tamiḻ maṉṉar, talivarkaḷ, pulavarkaḷ ākiyōr peyarkaḷ ivaṟṟi niṉivūṭṭum ūrppeyar kaḷum cintup pakutiyil iṉṟum uḷḷaṉa. (cintup pakutiyiyum tamiḻakattiyum iṇikkum kujarāti makārāṣṭira pakuti kaḷilum ittakiya ūrppeyar oṟṟumikaḷ mikappala). mēṟkaṇṭavi maṟṟumpala ātāraṅkaḷiṉ aṭippaṭiyil avar “tamiḻccintu Tamil Indus” eṉṟa karutukōḷi nili nāṭṭivarukiṟār. tolkāppiyam māntaṉ aṟivāṟṟal evvaḷavu viyattaku uccanili eytakkūṭum eṉpatik kāṭṭum ciṟanta cāṉṟukaḷuḷ oṉṟu One of the finest monuments of human intelligence. ilakkiyak koḷkiyil vēṟu entap paṇṭiya moḻi kaḷilum illāta ciṟanta karuttukkaḷit tolkāppiyam: poruḷatikāram - ceyyuḷiyal kūṟukiṟatu. kē.vi. cuvelepil tolkāppiyac ceyyuḷiyal kōṭpāṭṭuc ciṟappu: “tolkāppiyam aṭippaṭi nūlāka iruppatuilakkaṇa ilakkiya āyvukaḷukku maṭṭumaṉṟu; mānta iṉap paraval cārnta puviyiyal, kumutāya māntaviyal, paṇpāṭu, cūḻ niliyiyal, uḷaviyal ākiya pulaṅkaḷ cārnta ariya ceytikaḷ annūlil uḷḷaṉa; eṉavē tamiḻar nākarikam - paṇpāṭu cārnta (ēṉ potuvāṉa mānta iṉa nākarikam - paṇpāṭu cārnta) āyvukaḷukkut tolkāppiyam mika iṉṟiyamiyātatu. eṉiṉum ilakkiyam kūṟuvatellām (akkālattil nilaviya) uṇmik kumutāya niliyi nēraṭiyākak kāṭṭukiṟatu eṉṟu eṇṇutal tavaṟu; uṇmi niliyi viruppu veṟuppiṉṟi aṟiviyal pūrvamākak kāṭṭukiṟatu eṉṟu eṇṇutal mēlum tavaṟākum. akkāla āṇ peṇmakkaḷiṉ akam, puṟa vāḻk kiyi uḷḷatu uḷḷapaṭi tatrupamākac caṅkap pulavarkaḷuṭiya pāṭalkaḷ kāṭṭuvatākak kumuka varalāṟṟāciriyar cilar muṉṉar koṇṭuḷḷaṉar. āṉāl uṇmi eṉṉa? ceyyuḷ ‘uṇmi vāḻkki’ nikaḻccikaḷi antanta nikaḻcci eppaṭi irukka vēṇṭumeṉṟa ilakkiyamarapu uḷḷatō anta marapukkuk kaṭṭuppaṭṭē cittirikkiṟatu. atāvatu marapuk kēṟṟa māṟṟaṅkaḷuṭaṉ tāṉ cittirikkiṟatu. “uṇmi vāḻkki” vēṟu; ati ilakkiyattil kāṭṭum marapu vēṟu; eṉpatip pulavarkaḷum poruḷilakkaṇavāciriyarum uṇarn tiruntaṉar. uṇarntiruntataṉāltāṉ uṇmi vāḻkkiyiṉ kūṟṟukkaḷ ceykikaḷi ‘ulakiyal vaḻakku’ eṉṟum ilakkiyattil (ceyyuḷil) vāḻakkiyic cittarikkum marapic ceyyuḷ vaḻakku “pulaṉeṟi vaḻakku” eṉṟum kuṟit taṉar. cila nikaḻccikaḷ, ceytikaḷi ippaṭi ippaṭittāṉ kāṭṭa vēṇṭum eṉṟu ilakkiyap paṭippāḷarkaḷum vācakarkaḷum tamakkuḷ icintu uruvākkum mēlvaric caṭṭamākiya marapē ilakkiya marapākum. (e.kā. ovvoru tiṇikkum uriya mutal, karu, uripporuḷkaḷ). nikaḻcci/ ceyti vācakarukkut terintatu tāṉ; ataṉi oru putiya pārviyil, kōṇattil (marapi aṭippaṭiyil mīṟāmal) kāṭṭum poḻutu vācakar anta nayatti naṉku uṇarntu pārāṭṭa iyalkiṟatu. akappāṭal etuvum orutaṭavikūṭat talivaṉ, talivi peyaric cuṭṭuvatilli. talivaṉum taliviyum eppoḻutum iṉṉār eṉṟu kuṟippāka aṭiyāḷam kāṭṭa muṭiyātavarkaḷē. eṉavē akap pāṭalkaḷ cittirikkum uṇarvukaḷ (ṭapiḷpu. ec. haṭcaṉ tamatu “ilakkiyap payiṟcikkāṉa muṉṉuri” 1946 nūlpakkam 97il colvatu pōla) “potuvāka enta āṭavaṉukkum peṇ ṇukkum poruntuvatāka irukkumēyaṉṟi ivaṉukkut tāṉ/ ivaḷukkuttāṉ poruntum eṉṟu irukkātu. (uḷḷa uṇarvukaḷi viḷakkātu) veḷiyulaki virit turikkum puṟappāṭalkaḷil cila potuvāka aṉivarum nōkkum pārviyil irukkum; cila antantap pulavaruṭiya taṉi nōkkil amintatāka irukkum. piṉṉavaṟṟukku eṭuttuk kāṭṭu kiyaṟunilip pāṭalkaḷ. vaḷḷalkaḷ, naṇparkaḷ iṟantāl pulavarkaḷ tam neñciṉ āḻattiliruntu uṇarvu kaḷik koṭṭip pāṭum tuyarap pāṭalkaḷ ovvoṉṟum taṉittaṉmi vāyntavi; potuvāṉa nāṭṭuppuṟa op pārikaḷiṉ vālāyap paṭimaṅkaḷik koṇṭaviyalla.”
- kē.vi. cuvelepil
2. tirukkuṟaḷ
“intiya makkaḷiṭiyē paṇṭik kālattiliruntē piṟa uyirkaḷ aṉittukkum aṉpukāṭṭi utavum aṟakkoḷki kaḷ (the idea of active love)” iruntu vantati intiyap paṇṭiya ilakkiyaṅkaḷilum kāṇum katikaḷil iruntu aṟikiṟōm. attuṭaṉ kuṟippāka tirukkuṟaḷ eṉṉum nūlil kāṇum aṟavuric ceyyuḷkaḷiliruntum aṟikiṟōm. “(ulakil piṟa uyirkaḷiṭatta aṉpu kāṭṭic ceyal paṭuvati (life affirmation) avvaḷavāka valiyuṟuttāta maṉutarma cāttiram pōṉṟavaṟṟiliruntu māṟupaṭum) kuṟaḷil ulakiyum vāḻkkiyiyum veṟuttu otukkum pōkku (ṟuṭicaṭaṉa World and life negation) paravalākak kāṇappaṭuvatu alla. eṅkō ōriru iṭattil vēṇṭumāṉāl irukkalām. “pirāmaṇīyam, puttamatam pakavatkīti pōṉṟavaṟṟip pōl immi maṟumip payaṉ karutiyē aṟaneṟiyil niṟṟal vēṇṭum eṉṟa vāṇikak koḷkiyik kuṟaḷ valiyuṟuttu vatu illi. nallatu ceyvatē takkatu eṉṟa uṇarvi ṉālēyē nallatu (aṟam) ceyya vēṇṭum eṉṟu kuṟaḷ kūṟukiṟatu (kuṟaḷkaḷ 222,211 pakavatkītiyō ‘karumam ceytu koṇṭiruppatu pirapañca niyati’ eṉṟu vaṟaṭṭut taṉamāka valintu kūṟuki ṟatu. āṉā l , eṉṉa vi ya p pu , ku ṟa ḷō ma ṉi taṉ uḻippatum īṭṭuvatum piṟarukkum piṟa uyirkaḷukkum avaṉ naṉmi ceyvataṟkākattāṉ eṉṟu cāṟṟukiṟatu. (kuṟaḷkaḷ 81,112) pakavat kītippaṭi kaṭami jātikkut takkapaṭi vēṟupaṭum. kuṟaḷō makkaḷ aṉivarum ‘nallavi ceytoḻukak” kūṟukiṟatu. “ēṉi intiya tarmacāstirakkārar `uḻippatil, muyaṟci ceyvatil makiḻvōṭu īṭupaṭu’ eṉṟu kūṟavilli. kuṟaḷkaḷ 619,630 pōṉṟaviyō ulakiyalilum, vāḻkkiyilum, ārvattuṭaṉ īṭupaṭumpaṭi aṟivuṟuttukiṉṟaṉa. “(puttamum kītiyum pōlap paṟṟiṉmi, veṟup piṉmi, kollāmi, akimci ākiyavaṟṟiyum kuṟaḷ valiyuṟuttiṉālum kūṭa atē camayattil) kuṟaḷ atikamāka 86 paṉṉāṭṭaṟiñar pārviyil - tamiḻum tamiḻarum valiyuṟuttuvatu aṉpum aruḷumē yām (the living ethic of love). kāṇka kuṟaḷkaḷ 72,78,79, 103,226,241.”
“aṟaneṟicār ulaka vāḻkki ilaṭciyattit tiṟam paṭac cuṭṭukiṟatu kuṟaḷ. maṉitaṉiṉ taṉi vāḻkki. piṟa uyirka ḷōṭum ulakattōṭum uṟaḻum avaṉ vāḻkki ākiyavaṟṟil kikkoḷḷa vēṇṭiya neṟikaḷik kuṟaḷ viyattaku paṇ puṭaṉum cālpuṭaṉum naṭimuṟikkukanta vakiyil vakut tuḷḷatu (characterised by nobility and good sense) . ulaka ilakkiyattil vēṟu eṅkum ivvaḷavu ciṟanta oḷi mayamāṉa aṟavuri vācakaṅkaḷi vaḻaṅkum nūlik kāṇa iyalātu; (kuṟaḷkaḷ 92, 105, 108, 121, 159, 162, 216, 298, 319, 578, 594, 628, 757, 782, 874, 931, 973, 999, 1007, 1024, 1032”
“āka kiṟittuva ūḻit toṭakkak kālattilēyē intiya makkaḷiṭiyē ulaka vāḻkkiyiṭamum uyirkaḷiṭamum paṇpum aṉpum kāṭṭum oru taṉmi iyalpāka iruntu vantuḷḷatik kuṟaḷiliruntu aṟikiṟōm . pirāmaṇiya m, puttam , pakavatkīti cār intu matam ākiyavaṟṟiṉ kōṭpāṭukaḷil illātatu atu. āṉāl kīḻcātiyiṉariṭamum, cātāraṇa makkaḷiṭamum tōṉṟi makkaḷōṭu makkaḷāka vāḻnta ciṟanta camayap pōtakarkaḷ mūlamāka attaṉmi metumetuvāka nāḷaṭivil intu matattil iṭam peṟalāyiṟṟu.” - ālparṭ cuviṭcar: intiyac cintaṉiyum ataṉ vaḷarcciyum Indian Thought and its development: 1936
3. eṭṭuttoki, pattuppāṭṭiṉ ciṟappukaḷ (vēṟu entac cemmoḻi ilakkiyattukkum illātavi) “eṭṭuttoki, pattuppāṭṭu nūlkaḷ (mottam 2300 pāṭalkaḷ) acciṭṭu ulakiṉ pārvikku mutalil vantatu 1880- 1910 kāla aḷavil ākum. avi veḷivanta akkaṇamē (atuvari eḷitāṉa piṟkāla intiya moḻikaḷil oṉṟākak karutappaṭṭa nili māṟi) ulakiṉ ciṟanta cemmoḻikaḷil oṉṟākat tamiḻ ākiviṭṭatu. pireñcu āciyaviyalaṟiñar piyar meyl “kirēkka uṇarccip pākkaḷil tali ciṟanta nava maṇikaḷukku iṇiyāṉavi ic caṅ ka ila kki ya p pāṭalkaḷ; intiyāvil, ēṉ ulakil, ilakkiyap pāṭalkaḷ; ilakkiyap paṭippukaḷiṉ cikaraṅkaḷil oṉṟu caṅka ilakkiyam” eṉṟu ceytuḷḷa matippīṭṭi caṅka ilakkiyap pāṭalkaḷi āḻntu payiṉṟuḷḷa evarum ēṟṟuk koḷvar.” - cuvelapil (1960)
“ulakiṉ nākarikam - paṇpāṭṭukkut tirāviṭarkaḷ, kuṟippākat tamiḻarkaḷ ceytuḷḷa ciṟanta paṅkaḷippukaḷ pallava, cōḻar kālak kōyiṟ kaṭṭaṭakkali; cōḻar kālac ceppup paṭimaṅkaḷ; (parata nāṭṭiyam eṉa aḻikkappaṭum) tamiḻar naṭaṉakkali; (karunāṭaka ici eṉa aḻikkappaṭum) tamiḻ ici mutaliya palavām; avaṟṟuḷḷum talimi cāṉṟa ciṟappu vāyntatu (caṅka kālac cemmoḻit tamiḻ) ilakkiyam, piṟamoḻi, piṟanāṭṭu aṟiñar iti uṇarntu iv vilakkiyattip payiṉṟu ulaka mānta iṉattiṉ iṉṟi yamiyāta uyariya marapuc celvamāka makiḻntu pōṟṟa vēṇṭum. uccanilikku muḻut takutiyuṭiya avvilakkiyat tiṟku ituvari annili tarappaṭavilli.”
- cuvelapil (1973)
“intiyāviṉ piṟa entamoḻi ilakkiyattilum illāta ciṟappuk kūṟukaḷik koṇṭatu eṉpatu maṭṭumaṉṟip paṇṭit tamiḻc caṅka ilakkiyam uyariya ilakkiyam nayamum vāyntatu. ulakiṉ ciṟanta cemmoḻikaḷ variciyil tamiḻuk kuriya iṭattic caṅka ilakkiyattiṉ 26350 varikaḷ koṇṭa pāṭalkaḷ peṟṟu tantuviṭṭaṉa. avvilakkiyattiṉ ciṟappi ippoḻututāṉ ulaku uṇarat toṭaṅkiyuḷḷatu.”
“aci, cīr, taḷi aṭippaṭiyilāṉa yāppiyal, akam puṟakkōṭpāṭu; uvamaviyal, ākiya aṉittilum taṉit taṉmi vāynta ciṟanta ilakkiyak koḷki koṇṭa caṅka nūlkaḷi uruvākkiya perumi tamiḻp paṇpāṭṭukku maṭṭumē uriya ciṟappākum. “intiyāvil tōṉṟi vaḷarnta cevviyal ilakkiyam - cevviyal moḻip perum paṇpāṭukaḷ iraṇṭu - oṉṟu camaskirutam; maṟṟatu tamiḻ; avi oṉṟi oṉṟu cārntavi yalla - taṟcārpāṉavi.
 “intiya moḻi ilakkiyaṅkal tamiḻilakkiyam maṭṭumē cevvilakkiyamākavum, nikaḻkāla ilakkiyamākavum orē nērattil ilaṅkum ciṟappu vāyntatu; atu camaskirutac cevviyal ilakkiyam aḷavu toṉmi vāyntatu; paṇṭi kirēkka moḻic ceyyuḷkaḷi eppaṭic cevvilakkiyam eṉa ulaku karutukiṟatō atē taṉmi vāyntatu tamiḻc caṅka ilakkiyam. uyirt tuṭippuḷḷa ilakkiyaṅkaḷ iṉṟum uruvākum moḻiyākavum tamiḻ uḷḷatu.
“tamiḻc cevvilakkiyam (caṅka ilakkiyam) makkaḷip paṟṟiya ilakkiyam; makkaḷ uruvākkiyatu - āṉāl “nāṭṭup puṟa” ilakkiyamaṉṟu”	
- cuvelapil (1973)
“vāḻkki iṉiyatu; makiḻcci taruvatu. cāvu ellō rukkum varuvatu. ivvulaka vāḻviliruntu viṭutali peṟa vēṇṭum eṉṟa muyaṟciyellām illi. vaḷḷaṇmiyum nayaṉmiyum iyalpāka amikiṉṟaṉa - āṉmīka nalaṉukkāka vō kaḻuvāyākavō alla. iṟanta piṉṉar vīrarum cāṉ ṟōrum tuṟakkattil niliyāka vāḻvar. tīyōr mīḷānirayam eytuvar eṉak karutiṉar. nila ulakil pukaḻi niṟīic ceṉṟavar tuṟakkattil iṉiyavāḻvu eṉṉum vekumati peṟuvar; kātal, puṇarcci, tirumaṇam, makkaṭpēṟu ivi mānta vāḻvil niṟivu peṟuvataṟkāṉa vaḻimuṟikaḷ eṉak karutappaṭṭaṉa. vaḷḷaṇmi, vīracceyal, piṟarkkuriyāḷaṉāka vāḻtal - ivaṟṟil oruvaṉ celaviṭṭa nāṭkaḷi niṉittup pulavarum piṟarum kūṟum pukaḻ moḻikaḷ maṭṭumē oruvaṉ cāvukkup piṉṉar niliniṟpaviyākum.” - cuvelapil (1974)
“caṅka ilakkiyaṅkaḷ ekkālattukkum poruntum aḻakiyal kūṟukaḷ, viḻumaṅ kaḷ ākiyavaṟṟi niram pak koṇṭavi. īrāyiram āṇṭukaḷukku muṉṉar yātta avvilakkiyaṅkaḷ piṟkālattil (atāvatu aṇmikkālat tiṟaṉāyvāḷar) vakuttuḷḷa ilakkiya nayakkōṭpāṭukaḷiṉ paṭiyum uyarniliyil ēṟkappaṭuvaṉavāka uḷḷaṉa.  “caṅka ilakkiyattip payilum oru kumutāyamē (taṉinapar maṭṭumalla) avvilakkiya viḻumaṅkaḷi eyta iyalum vaki yil, eyta viḻiyum vakiyil avvilakkiyam amintuḷḷatu. eṉavētāṉ ikkāla ilakkiyat tiṟaṉāyvāḷarum pōṟṟum vakiyil ulaka māntar aṉivarum matikkattakka eṉṟu muḷa (cirañcīvi)t taṉmi koṇṭuḷḷatu caṅka ilakkiyam”
- cuvelapil (1974)
“paḻantamiḻ ilakkiyamum paṇpāṭum paṭitta akam - puṟakkōṭpāṭukaḷ ulakilēyē taṉittaṉmiyum ciṟappum vāyntavi. ci la po ru ṇ mi ka ḷi l ta mi ḻi la k ki ya t tu k ku m ca ma s ki ru ta ilakkiyattukkum oppumi kāṇap paṭalām. ataṉāl eṉṉa? camaskiruta ilakkiyattiliruntu tāṉ tamiḻ ilakkiyam kaṭaṉ peṟṟirukkum eṉa ēṉ karuta vēṇṭum? (nērmāṟākavum nikaḻntirukkalām aṉṟō?)” - cuvelapil (1975)
“potuvāṉa ceytiyit teḷivāka, muḻumiyāṉa nuṇṇiya pārviyil nōkki, eḷiya āṉāl vayiram pōṉṟa coṟkaḷil, āḻnta poruḷuṭaṉ taruvatē ciṟanta pāṭal eṉa ikkālattil karutappaṭukiṟatu. veḷippaṭiyāṉa orē ceytiyit taruvativiṭa aṭukkaṭukkāṉa pala poruḷi taravallatāṉa coṟceṭṭumikka pāṭalē ciṟanta pānalat taṉmiyuṭiyatākak karutappaṭukiṟatu. tolkāppiyam nōkku, payaṉ, eccam (colleccam, kuṟippeccam) uḷḷuṟi, iṟicci eṉṟu kūṟuvatellām itutāṉ;
- pi. marutanāyakam (2007)
“āriyarukku muṟpaṭṭa teṉṉintiyāvi, ēṉ peru maḷavukku āriyarukku muntiya intiyāviyē vivarikkum āvaṇam puṟanāṉūṟu a testament of pre-Aryan South India and, to a significant extent of pre-Aryan India.” tamiḻ, ilakkiyamarapu maṭṭumtāṉ (camaskirutattiliruntu peṟappaṭāta) intiya maṇṇukkuriya ilakkiya marapu ākum. teṉṉāṭṭil camaskirutat tākkam valuppaṭuvataṟku muṉṉarē mukiḻttatu atu. camaskiruta/ piṟa intiya moḻi ilakkiyaṅ kaḷiliruntu māṟupaṭṭa taṉmiyuṭiyatu atu. tamiḻ taṉak keṉṟu taṉi ilakkiyak koḷki, ilakkaṇamarapu, murukiyal (aesthetics) uṭiyatu; ivaṟṟiṉ aṭippaṭiyil aminta tamiḻc cemmoḻi ilakkiyam vēṟu emmoḻi ilakkiyattukkum illāta taṉittaṉmi vāyntatu (unique). camaskirutattilō vēṟu intiya moḻikaḷilō uḷḷa uṇarvukaḷiliruntu māṟupaṭṭa uṇarvi (sensibility) - intiya maṇṇukkē uriya uṇarvip paḻantamiḻ ilakkiyattil kāṇalām; tamiḻppulavarkaḷ paṉṉeṭuṅkālam vaḷartteṭutta ceḻumiyāṉa uṇarvu marapunili atu. “mūṉṟāvatāka camaskirutam - ilattīṉ, cīṉam, pāracīkam, arapu ākiya moḻikaḷiṉ ilakkiyaṅkaḷil ciṟantavaṟṟukku iṇiyāṉa taram vāyntatu tamiḻc cemmoḻi ilakkiyam. avvilakkiyam nuṇmāṇuḻipulamikkatu; palvēṟu vakippāṭu poruḷ koṇṭatu (muṟkāla intiya ilakkiyaṅ kaḷil potu makkaḷip paṟṟiyum niṟiyap pāṭiyatu tamiḻilakkiyam maṭṭumē); mānta iṉattukkup potumi yāṉa viḻumiyaṅkaḷik kūṟiyatu. eṉavē piṟa cevviyal moḻikaḷiṉ marapukaḷuṭaṉum ilakkiyaṅkaḷuṭaṉum camamāka niṟkum uraṉ vāyntatu atu. ulakiṉ taliciṟanta aṟanūl kaḷil oṉṟu tirukkuṟaḷ. tamiḻc cevviyal ilakkiyattil atu pōṉṟu pala tuṟikaḷilum ciṟanta nūlkaḷ uṇṭu. av vilakkiyam pāṭit tulakkam tarāta mānta iṉap paṭṭaṟivu oṉṟum illi.
“iṟutiyāka, ikkāla intiyap paṇpāṭṭiyum marapiyum uṇarvataṟkup payilavēṇṭiya ilakkiyaṅkaḷil tamiḻilakkiyam mutaṉmi vāyntatu. camaskiruta ilakkiyaṅ kaḷil teṉṉaka maraput tākkam uḷḷatu kuṟittu nāṉ virivāka eḻutiyuḷḷēṉ. caṅkakkālam toṭaṅkit tamiḻil pāṭap paṭṭa ciṟanta intu camaya patti ilakkiya nūlkaḷ tām taṟpōtiya intu camayattukaku aṭittaḷamāka irukkiṉṟaṉa. tamiḻ pakti ilakkiyak karuttukaḷpākavata purāṇattilum piṟa (teluṅku/ kaṉṉaṭa/camaskiruta) pakti nūlkaḷilum ēṟṟappaṭṭu intiyā muḻutum paraviṉa. vētattukkuc camamākak karutappaṭum tamiḻ tivyapirapanta pakti ilakkiyap pāṭalkaḷ teṉ ṉintiyāvil periya tirumāl kōyilkaḷil (tiruppati pōṉṟavi) vētattōṭu cērttu ōtappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ikkāla intō āriya moḻikaḷukku camaskirutamē mūlamoḻi; atupōla ikkālat tamiḻukkum maliyāḷattukkum cevviyal tamiḻē mūla moḻiyākum.

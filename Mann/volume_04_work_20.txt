patippuri
ilakkiya ilakkaṇac cemmal mutumuṉivar ayyā iḷaṅkumaraṉār avarkaḷ eḻupattintu āṇṭu niṟivu eytiyatip pōṟṟum vakiyil tamiḻ iḷiyarkkeṉṟu avar eḻutiya nūlkaḷiyellām cērttu eḻupattintu nūlkaḷ kaṭanta 2005 - ām āṇṭu tiruccit tirunakaril veḷiyiṭṭu em patippup paṇikkup perumi cērttuk koṇṭōm. 
anta vakiyil 2010 - ām āṇṭu ipperun tamiḻācāṉ  81 - ām  āṇṭu niṟivik koṇṭāṭum vakiyil tamiḻmoḻi - iṉa - nāṭṭiṉ  kāppiṟkākavum, mīṭpiṟkākavum, mēṉmikkākavum ivar eḻutiya aṉittu aṟivuc celvaṅ kaḷiyum tokuttup poruḷ vaḻip pirittu 20 tokutikaḷāka veḷiyiṭṭuḷḷōm.maturit tamiḻc caṅkac centamiḻ itaḻukkum, civa cittānta nūṟpatippuk kaḻakam naṭattivarum centamiḻc celvi, kuṟaḷiyam maṟṟum piṟa itaḻkaḷukkum, malarkaḷukkum  ivar eḻutiya  aṟiviṉ ākkaṅkaḷit  tokuttut tamiḻkūṟum nallulakiṟku vaḻaṅkum  paṇiyilum īṭupaṭṭuḷḷōm.
ipperumakaṉ vāḻum kālattilēyē ivar eḻutiya eḻuttukkaḷ, pēciya pēccukkaḷ aṉittiyum tokuttu veḷiyiṭuvatu eṉpatu tamiḻnūl patippu varalāṟṟil pōṟṟi makiḻvataṟkāṉa,  aṉivarum piṉpaṟṟuvataṟkāṉa arumperum tamiḻp paṇiyākum.
ivar tannalam karutāmal tamiḻ nalam kāttu varupavar.  tammimuṉṉiṟuttātu  tamiḻi muṉṉiṟuttum peruntamiḻaṟiñar.  tamakkeṉa vāḻātu tamiḻkkeṉa vāḻpavar. orunāḷiṉ muḻup
poḻutum tamiḻākavē vāḻum tamiḻp pōrāḷi. caṅkac cāṉṟōr variciyil vittu eṇṇattakka aruntamiḻaṟiñar. tamiḻ ilakkaṇa - ilakkiya marapik kāttu varum marapu vaḻi aṟiñar. āravāram mikunta iṉṟiya cūḻniliyil paṭāṭōpam iṉṟiyum, viḷamparap pōlimi iṉṟiyum,   tamiḻ moḻiyiṉ āḻa akalaṅkaḷi akaḻntu kāṭṭum toltamiḻaṟiñar. moḻinūl katiravaṉ pāvāṇariṉ vērccol āyvil avar kāṭṭiya vaḻiyil tam tamiḻāyvit  toṭarpavar. 
ivar eḻutik kuvitta tamiḻ aṟivuc celvaṅkaḷi  avariṭamē vēṇṭip peṟṟu 20 tokutikaḷāka iḷaṅkumaraṉār tamiḻ vaḷam eṉum talippil poruḷvaḻip pirittu veḷiyiṭukiṟōm. tamiḻāyvuk kaḷattil  tam āyvuppayaṇattit toṭaṅkum tamiḻ āyvāḷarkaḷukkum, tamiḻ uṇarvāḷarkaḷukkum, māṇavac celvaṅkaḷukkum   taṅkat taṭṭil vittup poṟkuviyalākat  tantuḷḷōm. em tamiḻ nūl patippup payaṇattil,  tamiḻtteṉṟal tiru.vi.ka.viyum, taṉittamiḻiyakkat tanti maṟimaliyaṭikaḷiyum, moḻinūl katiravaṉ pāvāṇariyum, tamiḻk moḻik kāvalar ilakkuvaṉāriyum vaḻikāṭṭiyākak koṇṭu tamiḻ nūl parappiṉ elliyik kaṇṭu kāṭṭum caṅkattamiḻt cāṉṟōrāka viḷaṅkum aiyā iḷaṅkumaraṉār  vāḻum kālattilēyē avar nūlkaḷi veḷiyiṭuvati yām peṟṟa pēṟākak karutukiṟōm. tamiḻar illamtōṟum irukkattakka ivvaruntamiḻc celvaṅkaḷit tamiḻ iḷam talimuṟikku vippākak koṭuttuḷḷōm.

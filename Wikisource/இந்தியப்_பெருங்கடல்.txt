﻿patippurai





kaṭal nūl varicaiyil icciṟu nūl veḷiyiṭappaṭukiṟatu. intiyak kaṭal ārāycci eṉṉum aṉaittulakat tiṭṭam vakukkappaṭṭuc cīriya muṟaiyil ceyaṟpaṭuttappaṭṭapiṉ kiṭaitta ceytikaḷ, uṇmaikaḷ, nikaḻnta kaṇṭupiṭippukaḷ ākiyavaiyum; maṟṟum intiyak kaṭaliṉ aṭippaṭaic ceytikaḷum vakaippaṭuttiyum, tokaippaṭuttiyum itil kūṟappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. intiyap peruṅkaṭal paṟṟi mutaṉ mutalil muṟaiyāka eḻutappaṭṭa nūl ituvē. paḷḷi nūlakaṅkaḷukkum, potu nūlakaṅkaḷukkum talaivāy nūlāka itu peritum payaṉpaṭum. ceytit tāḷkaḷiṉ orumitta pārāṭṭaip peṟṟa nūl itu.

﻿1. amaippu



kaṭalkaḷ

aimperuṅ kaṭalkaḷāl cūḻappaṭṭatē nām vāḻum ulakam. avai muṟaiyē pacipik kaṭal, intiyak kaṭal, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭal, ārkṭik kaṭal, aṇṭārkṭik kaṭal ākum.

iruppiṭam

ulakil mūṉṟāvatu periya kaṭal intiyak kaṭal. ataṟku mēṟkē āppirikkāvum, vaṭakkē arēpiyā, intiyā, malēyā ākiya tīpakaṟpaṅkaḷum; kiḻakkē āstirēliyāvum uḷḷaṉa. ataṉ teṉpakuti aṇṭārkṭik kaṭalōṭu kalakkiṟatu. pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷ pōlavē, ārkṭik kaṭalōṭu atu toṭarpu koḷḷavillai.

intiyak kaṭal āciyā, āppirikkā, āstirēliyā ākiya mūṉṟu kaṇṭaṅkaḷukkiṭaiyil uḷḷatu. ataṉ vaṭapakuti intiyāvāl pirikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. atu tōṟṟattil nerukkamāy amainta kaṭal. iṅtōṉēṣiyāviliruntu āppirikkāvarai paraviyuḷḷatu. atu paruvak kāṟṟukaḷukkup piṟappiṭam.

tōṟṟam

ataṉ parappu kiṭṭattaṭṭa 3 kōṭi catura mailkaḷ; carācari āḻam 15,000 aṭi. atu ula-

1–68

kiṉ mēṟparappil 14 paṅkai aṭaittuk koṇṭirukkiṟatu. pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷ pōṉṟu avvaḷavu periyatō āḻamāṉatō alla atu. atu 20 kōṭi āṇṭukaḷukku muṉ tōṉṟiyatu.

tuṇaik kaṭalkaḷ

ataṟkuk kiḻakkilum mēṟkilum arapik kaṭalum vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvum uḷḷaṉa. ceṅ kaṭalum pāracīka nīrōṭṭamum ataṉ uḷnāṭṭuk kaṭalkaḷ. ulakiluḷḷa perum ciṟu kaṭalkaḷ atil kalakkiṉṟaṉa. imayamalaiyil urukum paṉi ellām atil kalakkiṉṟatu. cintu, pirama puttirā, kaṅkai, airāvati, kāviri mutaliya pērāṟukaḷum atil kalakkiṉṟaṉa. ataṟkup periya vaṭinilam uṇṭu.

tīvukaḷ

maṭakāskar, ilaṅkai mutaliyavai ataṉ perum kaṇṭat tīvukaḷ. ilaṭcat tīvukaḷ, mālat tīvukaḷ ataṉ mutaṉmaiyāṉa kaṭal tīvukaḷ. potuvāka, ataṉ tīvukaḷ pacipik kaṭaliṉ tīvukaḷ pōlavē, eri malaiyālum pavaḻattālum āṉavai.

malaikaḷ

intiyak kaṭalil kuṇṭuṅkuḻikaḷum, uyarnta malait toṭarkaḷum, āḻamāṉa akaḻikaḷum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

ciṟappāka, atil kāṇappaṭum pavaḻa malait toṭarkaḷ cikkalāṉa amaippuṭaiyavai. ataṉ teṉkiḻakku, teṉmēṟkup pakutikaḷait tavira, eñciyavai nilat tokutiyāl cūḻap paṭṭavai. ataṉ teṉ kōṭiyil paṉikkaṭṭikaḷum paṉippāṟaikaḷum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ

intiyak kaṭalil kāṇappaṭum nīroṭṭaṅkaḷ pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷil kāṇappaṭum nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ pōṉṟu avvaḷavu viraivum, valimaiyum koṇṭavai alla. ataṉ mēṟparappu kiḻakkākac cāyntuḷḷatu. maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷ mēṟkākac cāyntuḷḷaṉa. oru kōṭiyil maṭṭum tiṟantuḷḷa orē peruṅkaṭal ituvē.

atil kāṇappaṭum mukkiya nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷāvaṉa: nilanaṭukkōṭṭu nīrōṭṭam, mocām pikuyu nīrōṭṭam, akulās nīrōṭṭam.

vaḷam

intiyak kaṭaliṉ iyaṟkai vaḷam matippiṭaṟkariyatu. ulakak kaṭalkaḷilēyē atika aḷavukkup palavakai uyirp poruḷkaḷ uḷḷa kaṭal ituvākum. ataṉ mīṉ vaḷam niṟaivāṉa poruḷ vaḷattai aḷikkavallatu. tavira, ataṉ kaṉivaḷamum matippiṭaṟkariyatē. ataṉ vāṇipa vaḷamum varalāṟṟuc ciṟappuṭaiyatē. ﻿veṭippu

nilavulakiṉ mēṟparappu 45,000 mail tolaiviṟku veṭittuḷḷatu eṉa aṇmaiyil kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. ivveṭippu aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalaik kaṭantu āppirikkā varai celkiṟatu. intiyak kaṭalukkuḷḷum atu talaikāṭṭukiṟatu.

iṭarkaḷ

intiyak kaṭaliṉ karaippakutikaḷil perumpāṉmai vāḻvataṟku ēṟṟatalla. aṅkuppayaṅkara vilaṅkukaḷum, koṭiya nōykaḷum uḷḷaṉa. atil alaikaḷiṉ perumeḻucci vīḻccikaḷum, payaṅkara nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷum uḷḷaṉa. aṅkut toṭarntu valuvāṉa kāṟṟukaḷ aṭittavaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa.

intiyakkaṭal puyalkaḷukkum nilaikkaḷamāka uḷḷatu. atil tīṅkutarum kalmīṉ, koṭṭum mīṉ, cuṟā mutaliyavai vāḻkiṉṟaṉa. ivvāṟu atu iṭarkaḷum tīṅkukaḷum niṟaintu kāṇappaṭukiṟatu.

veppanilai

intiyak kaṭal āḻamāṉa kaṭal maṭṭumalla; veppak kaṭalumākum. ataṉ mēṟparappu veppanilai 90°F aḷavukku uyarukiṉṟatu. 12,000 aṭi āḻattil ataṉ cīrāṉa veppanilai 35° F. ﻿upputtaṉmai

intiyak kaṭalum uppu vaḷam niṟaintatē. avvaḷam nam nāṭṭiṉ poruḷ vaḷattai ōraḷavukkup perukkavallatu.

kappal ārāycci

cōviyattu aṟiviyalār allatu viññāṉikaḷ viṭyās eṉṉuṅ kappalil intiyak kaṭaliṉ nilak kāntattiṉ maṟaivai aṟiya 1959ām āṇṭu vantaṉar. taṅkaḷ āyvukku vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvil maṟaivāṉa or iṭattait tērnteṭuttaṉar. āyviṉ nōkkam intiyak kaṭaliṉ nilakkāntak kaḷattai aṟivatē.

cōviyattu aṟiviyalār intiyak kaṭaliṉ maiyap pakutiyil 20,000 mailukku mēl aḷavai ceytuḷḷaṉar. itaṉāl cila iṉṟiyamaiyāc ceytikaḷ tiraṭṭappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟaik koṇṭu intiyak kaṭaliṉ amaippu, varalāṟu mutaliyavaṟṟai nila amaippu nūl muṟaiyil aṟiya vāyppu ēṟpaṭum.

avarkaḷ taṅkaḷ āyvukaḷiliruntu terivittirukkum muṭivukaḷāvaṉa: intiyak kaṭaliṉ tarai mika ariya amaippai uṭaiyatu. kiḻakku mēṟkup pakutikaḷil ataṉ amaippu perumaḷavukku māṟupaṭukiṟatu. tarai 8.15 mīṭṭar nīḷattiṟku iṭaiyē uḷḷa nāṉku uṭpakutikaḷaik koṇṭuḷḷatu. uṭpakutikaḷil mīḷum māṟṟaṅkaḷ kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

avarkaḷ mēṟkoṇṭa nilanaṭukka uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ veḷippaṭuttuvaṉa:- intiyak kaṭalil uḷḷa taḷarcciyāṉa paṭivukaḷiṉ taṭimaṉ 100 mīṭṭariliruntu 200 mīṭṭar varai uḷḷatu.

vāṇipa vaḻi

tīvuk kūṭṭaṅkaḷ niṟaiya iruppiṉum kappal pōkku varaviṟku intiyak kaṭal mikavum payaṉpaṭukiṟatu. ciṅkappūr, pampāy, koḻumpu, ceṉṉai mutaliyavai ataṉ mutaṉmaiyāṉa tuṟaimukaṅkaḷ ākum.

1869il cūyas kālvāy tiṟakkappaṭṭapiṉ ataṉ vāṇipam periya aḷavil uḷḷatu. ataṉ ciṟanta vāṇipap pakutiyil cūyas kālvāy toṭarpu koḷkiṟatu. ikkālvāy tiṟakkappaṭṭavuṭaṉ naṉṉampikkai muṉai vaḻiyākak kappalkaḷ cellutal aṟavē niṉṟuviṭṭatu. cūyas kālvāy vaḻiyākac celvatu kuṟukku vaḻiyākum. vaḻiyiṉ tolaivu naṉṉampikkai muṉaiyiṉ vaḻiyākac celvataik kāṭṭilum 5000 mailkaḷ kuṟaiyum. itaṉāl taṟkāla vāṇipam ōṅkiyuḷḷatu.

ulakiluḷḷa mikap periya vāṇipa vaḻikaḷil oṉṟāka intiyak kaṭal uḷḷatu. paṇṭaik kālattil ikkaṭal vaḻiyāka intiyāviliruntu ekiptu, mecappaṭōmiyā, kiḻakku maiyattaraik kaṭal nāṭukaḷ ākiyavaṟṟuṭaṉ vāṇipam ciṟappāka naṭaipeṟṟatu.

tamiḻ kāṭṭiliruntu mayil tōkai, akil mutaliya poruḷkaḷ pāpilōṉiyā ceṉṟaṉa eṉṟu kiṟittuva maṟaiyāṉa paipiḷ terivikkiṟatu. tamiḻakattiliruntu palavakaip poruḷkaḷ urōmāpurikkuc ceṉṟatākat tamiḻ nūlkaḷum; urōma āciriyar piḷiṉiyiṉ nūlkaḷum kūṟukiṉṟaṉa. yavaṉarkaḷākiya kirēkkarkaḷ kāvirippūmpaṭṭiṉam mutaliya tamiḻnāṭṭut tuṟaimukaṅkaḷukku vantu vāṇipam ceytaṉar eṉṟum paintamiḻ nūlkaḷ kūṟukiṉṟaṉa. teṉṉintiyāviṟkum kaṭāram eṉṉum parmāvukkum vāṇipat toṭarpu iruntatākap paṭṭiṉappālai kūṟukiṟatu.

kiṟittuvak kālattiṉ toṭakkattil mēṉāṭṭuk kappalkaḷ intiyak kaṭal vaḻiyākac cīṉāviṟkuc ceṉṟaṉa. cīṉak kappalkaḷ arēpiyāviṟkuc ceṉṟaṉa.

ki. pi. 5ām nūṟṟāṇṭiliruntu caṟṟēṟak kuṟaiya āyiram āṇṭukaḷ cīṉāviṟkum arēpiyāvukkum vāṇipam ciṟappāka naṭaipeṟṟatu.

15ām nūṟṟāṇṭiṉ toṭakkattil cīṉak kappaṟ paṭait talaivar, ceṅ-hōs eṉpavaruṭaṉ intiyā, arēpiyā mutaliya nāṭukaḷil tam aracarukkāka navarattiṉaṅkaḷ vāṅka intiyāviṟku vantār. avarukkup piṉ pōrccukkīciyar, ṭaccukkārar, pireñcukkārar, āṅkilēyar mutaliya mēṉāṭṭār intiyāviṟku vāṇipam ceyya vantaṉar.

iṉṟu intiyak kaṭal mikapperiya kaṭal vaḻiyāka uḷḷatu. itaṉāl intiyāvilum, ataṉ arukiluḷḷa nāṭukaḷilum mikapperiya tuṟaimukaṅkaḷ ēṟpaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. intiyāviliruntu tāṉiyaṅkaḷ, malaittōṭṭap poruḷkaḷ, tātup poruḷkaḷ, kaccāp poruḷkaḷ mutaliyavai maṟṟa nāṭukaḷukku ēṟṟumati ceyyappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ellāvaṟṟiṟkum mēlāka, intiyak kaṭal intiyāviṟku iyaṟkai araṇākavum tikaḻkiṟatu.

﻿2. varalāṟu

muṟaiyāṉa ārāycci

intiyak kaṭal vāṇipa vaḻiyāka nīṇṭa kālamāka iruntu vantirukkiṟatu. āṉāl, atu muṟaiyāka iṉṉum ārāyappaṭavillai. 1873ām āṇṭiliruntu irupatiṟku mēṟpaṭṭa kappalkaḷ kaṭal ārāycciyai mēṟkoṇṭuḷḷaṉa. āṉāl, avaṟṟil oṉṟāvatu intiyak kaṭalai muṟaiyākavum virivākavum ārāyavillai.

ūkkam piṟattal

intiyak kaṭalai ārāyum ūkkam mutaṉ mutalāka 1881ām āṇṭu piṟantatu. ivvāṇṭil ec. em. ai. es. iṉvesṭikēṭṭar eṉṉuṅ kappal intiyak kaṭaṟkaraiyiṉ nīrp pakutikaḷai aḷavaiyiṭa viṭappaṭṭatu.

1885-1887il alāk eṉpār mēṟkūṟiya kappalil intiyak kaṭaṟ pakutikaḷaic cuṟṟic ceṉṟār. avaṟṟiluḷḷa uyir vakaikaḷaip paṟṟip payaṉuḷḷa ceytikaḷait tiraṭṭi 1888il ōr ariya nūl veḷiyiṭṭār. ataṉ peyar ‘intiyak kaṭaṟ pakutikaḷil ōr iyaṟkai nūl aṟiñaṉ’ eṉpatākum.

svel eṉpār atē kappalil vaṅkāḷa virikuṭā, arapik kaṭal ākiyavaṟṟil pala uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ ceytār. mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭa uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ iracāyaṉa, vāṉilait toṭarpāṉavai. avaṟṟiṉ muṭivukaḷai āciya aracar kaḻaka (Royal Asiatic Society) veḷiyīṭukkaḷil 1925-38ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭaiyē veḷiyiṭṭār.

avar mēṟkoṇṭa ārāyccikaḷ vaṅkāḷa virikuṭā, arapik kaṭal ākiyavaṟṟaip paṟṟiya aṭippaṭai aṟivait teḷivākkiṉa.

maiya araciṉ muyaṟci

intiyak kaṭaṟ pakutikaḷil mēlum ārāyccikaḷ mēṟkoḷḷappaṭa, 1947il muyaṟci toṭaṅkiṟṟu. ivvāṇṭil maiya araciṉar irāmēcuvarattiṟku arukiluḷḷa maṇṭapam eṉṉumiṭattil ōr ārāycci nilaiyattai niṟuviṉar. itu kaṭal nūl maiya mīṉ ārāycci nilaiyamākum. itaṟku intiyā muḻutum kiḷaikaḷ uḷḷaṉa. innilaiyam kaṭal mīṉkaḷaippaṟṟi ārāycci naṭattiya vaṇṇam uḷḷatu.

maṟṟa nilaiyaṅkaḷ

1952il āntirap palkalaik kaḻakam pala kaṭal payaṇaṅkaḷukku ēṟpāṭu ceytatu. itaṟku intiyak kaṭaṟpaṭai utaviṟṟu. payaṇaṅkaḷ vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvil mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭaṉa. avaṟṟāl kiṭaitta muṭivukaḷil cilavaṟṟai 1954-58il veḷiyiṭappaṭṭa iru kaṭal nūl tokutikaḷil āntirap palkalaik kaḻakam veḷiyiṭṭatu.

maṟṟoru nilaiyam kaṭal uyir ārāycci nilaiyamākum. itu paṟaṅkippēṭṭaiyil uḷḷatu. aṇṇāmalaip palkalaik kaḻakattuṭaṉ toṭarpuṭaiyatu.

intiyak kaṭal ārāyccit tiṭṭam

tavira, ceṉṉai, tiruvaṉantapuram, pampāy mutaliya palkalaik kaḻakaṅkaḷum kaṭal nūl ārāycci ceyta vaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa. maiya araciṉ cārpāka iyaṅkum viññāṉa-toḻil ārāycci maṉṟamum kaṭal ārāyccikku āvaṉa ceytu varukiṟatu. iruppiṉum, muḻu mūccāka intiyak kaṭalai ulaka aḷavil ārāyum tiṭṭam 1959ām āṇṭiltāṉ uruvāyiṟṟu. taṟpoḻutu atu ceyaṟpaṭṭa vaṇṇam uḷḷatu. ituvē intiyak kaṭal ārāyccipaṟṟiya varalāṟu ākum.

﻿3. kaṭal ārāyccit tiṭṭam

peyar

intiyak kaṭaliṉ iyaṟkai vaḷaṅkaḷai ārāyat tiṭṭam oṉṟu ulaka aḷavil vakukkappaṭṭatu. itaṟku aṉaittulaka intiyak kaṭal ārāyccit tiṭṭam eṉṟu peyar.

tōṟṟam

ittiṭṭam karu nilaiyil 1957ām āṇṭilēyē uruvāyiṟṟu. āṉal muḻu nilaiyil uruppeṟṟatu 1959ām āṇṭiltāṉ. aṉaittulaka aṟiviyal kūṭṭuk kaḻaka maṉṟam, yuṉeskō, amerikka aṟiviyal muṉṉēṟṟak kaḻakam ākiyavaṟṟiṉ naṉ muyaṟciyiṉāl, aṉaittulakak kaṭal nūl pēravai 1959il cepṭampart tiṅkaḷil niyūyārkkil kūṭiyatu: kaṭal nūlai aṟiviyalāka-viññāṉamāka-māṟṟa vēṇṭum eṉṟu muṭivu ceytatu.

avaik kūṭṭattil 45 nāṭukaḷiliruntu 1,100 aṟiviyalār kalantu koṇṭaṉar. intak kūṭṭattil aṉaittulaka intiyak kaṭal ārāyccit tiṭṭam aṟivikkappaṭṭatu. ittiṭṭattiṉ pala paṇikaḷaic ceyaṟpaṭuttum aluvalakam niyūyārkkil uḷḷatu. ittiṭṭattaip pala aṟiviyalār koṇṭa oru taṉik kuḻu vakuttatu.

kālavarai

1957il uruvāṉālum ittiṭṭattiṟkuriya kālam aintu āṇṭukaḷākum. atāvatu ittiṭṭam 1960ām āṇṭiliruntu 1964ām āṇṭu varai ceyaṟpaṭum. kālam tēvaippaṭumāṉāl, mēlum nīṭṭikkappaṭum. muḻu aḷavil 1962ām āṇṭiliruntu ittiṭṭam ceyaṟpaṭat toṭaṅkiṟṟu.

ciṟappu

ulaka aḷavil pala nāṭukaḷiṉ kūṭṭu muyaṟciyiṉāl naṭaipeṟum māperum tiṭṭamākum itu. aṉaittulaka nila iyal nūl āṇṭut tiṭṭattai muṉ mātiriyākak koṇṭu ceyaṟpaṭuttappaṭuvatu. ituvarai mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭa kaṭal ārāyccikaḷil mikap periyatu itu.

nila iyal nūl āṇṭut tiṭṭamum ulaka aḷavil 1957-58ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭaiyē ciṟappuṭaṉ iṉitu niṟaivēṟiya tiṭṭamākum. itaṉāl nila iyal nūl naṉku vaḷarvataṟku vāyppu ēṟpaṭṭatu. aṟiviyaliṉ maṟṟat tuṟaikaḷum vaḷaralāyiṉa. vāṉveḷi ārāycci karuvuṟṟu uruppeṟṟatu.

mutaṉ mutalāka intiyak kaṭal niṟaivāka aṟiviyal aṭippaṭaiyil ārāyappaṭukiṟatu. ittiṭṭattil kalantu koḷḷum ovvoru nāṭum kuṟippiṭṭa tuṟaiyai, cikkalai ārāyum; ataṟkēṟṟa muṭivukaḷaik kāṇum. ciṟappāka, vāṉilaipaṟṟiyum ārāyappaṭum.

ittiṭṭattiṟku ākum celavu 6 kōṭi rūpāy ākum. iccelavai ovvoru nāṭum taṉṉāl iyaṉṟa aḷavukkup pakirntu koḷḷum.

tiṭṭakālattil 1,88,000 mail tolaiviṟku 60 kappal payaṇaṅkaḷ mēṟkoḷḷappaṭum. 20 nāṭukaḷiliruntu 40 kappalkaḷ ippayaṇaṅkaḷai mēṟkoḷḷum; intiyak kaṭalai ārāyum. pala nāṭukaḷiluḷḷa 350 aṟiviyalār, ittiṭṭattil kalantu koṇṭu intiyak kaṭalai ārāyvārkaḷ.

itil kalantu koḷḷum nāṭukaḷil cila : āstirēliyā, piriṭṭaṉ, ilaṅkai, intiyā, pākkistāṉ, ṭeṉmārk, pārmōcā, pirāṉcu, intōṉēṣiyā, isrēl, jappāṉ, hālantu, teṉ āppirikkā, mēṟku jermaṉi, amerikkā, uruciyā.

niṟaivēṟṟum muṟaikaḷ

uṟṟu nōkkal, olittal, aḷavukaḷ eṭuttal, paṭam piṭittal, mātirip poruḷkaḷ tiraṭṭutal mutaliya pala muṟaikaḷ mēṟkoḷḷappaṭum. ivaṟṟiṟku ellām aṭippaṭaiyākak kappaṟ payaṇaṅkaḷē mēṟkoḷḷappaṭum. ciṟantaārāyccik karuvikaḷākap pala vakaik karuvikaḷaiyum uḷḷaṭakkiya kappalkaḷ payaṉpaṭum. iṟutiyākac ceyyappaṭṭa pala vakai ārāyccikaḷiṉ muṭivukaḷ tokukkappaṭum; vakaippaṭuttappaṭṭu ovvoru tuṟaiyilum payaṉpaṭuttappaṭum.

amerikkā mutaliya nāṭukaḷ kappalkaḷaiyum kaṭal nūl aṟiñarkaḷaiyum vaḻaṅkum. amerikkā, tiṭṭattil pāti celavaiyum ēṟkum. cila nāṭukaḷ ārāycci ceyvataṟkuriya vacatikaḷai aḷikkum.

intiyak kaṭalukku arukiluḷḷa nāṭukaḷ kaṭal alaikaḷiṉ eḻucci vīḻccikaḷil ēṟpaṭum māṟṟaṅkaḷaiyum, kāṟṟu mēl veḷiyil uruvākum vāṉilai māṟṟaṅkaḷaiyum uṟṟu nōkki intiyak kaṭalai ārāya utavum.

ārāyappaṭum tuṟaikaḷ

intiyak kaṭal ārāycci paranta ōr ārāycci ākum. ākavē, atil āyvataṟku eṭuttuk koḷḷappaṭum tuṟaikaḷum pala vakaippaṭṭaṉavākavē uḷḷaṉa. attuṟaikaḷil ciṟappāṉavai piṉvarumāṟu :

nila amaippu nūl

itu oru virinta tuṟaiyākum. itu pala tuṟaikaḷai mēlum taṉṉuḷ aṭakkiyatu.

nila iyal nūl

itu mika virinta tuṟaiyākum. itilum mēlum pala tuṟaikaḷ aṭaṅkukiṉṟaṉa.

kaṭal nūl

ituvum pala tuṟaikaḷait taṉṉuḷ aṭakkiyatē. iyaṟkaik kaṭal nūl, iyaipuk kaṭal nūl, uyir kaṭal nūl eṉap pala vakaippaṭum.

vāṉilai nūl

itu mēṟkūṟiya tuṟaikaḷōṭu neruṅkiya toṭarpuṭaiyatu.

nīriyal

itu kaṭal nūliṉ oru pirivu.

uyiriyal

ituvum oru virinta tuṟaiyē. ciṟappāka, payir nūlaiyum, vilaṅku nūlaiyum taṉṉuḷ aṭakkiyatu.

maṟṟum, ittiṭṭattil maḻaip poḻivu, katir vīccu, īrppu, nila naṭukkam, veppa oṭṭam, kāṟṟu (mēl) veḷi, paṭivukaḷ, kānta māṟṟam, kaṉi vaḷam mutaliyavai paṟṟiyum virivāka ārāyappaṭum.

4. ārāycci ēṉ?



intiyak kaṭalai ārāyvataṟkuc ciṟanta kāraṇaṅkaḷ pala uḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟai iṅkuk kāṇpōm.

uyirkaḷ

iṟantoḻinta paṇṭaikkāla uyirkaḷiṉ eccamiccaṅkaḷai itil tēṭik kaṇṭupiṭikkalām. avvāṟu kaṇṭupiṭippatāl, akkaṇṭupiṭippukkaḷ uyir nūl, nila amaippu nūl mutaliya tuṟaikaḷukku mikavum payaṉpaṭum. uyirkaḷiṉ paṭippaṭi vaḷarcciyaip paṟṟiya putiya uṇmaikaḷai aṟiyalām.

ulakak kaṭalkaḷilēyē atika aḷavukku uyir vakaip poruḷkaḷ itil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ciṟappāka, mīṉ vakaikaḷ atil niṟaiya uḷḷaṉa. ataṉ mīṉvaḷattai ataṟku arukilirukkum nāṭukaḷ naṉku payaṉpaṭuttavillai. avvāṟu payaṉpaṭuttumāṉal, uṇavup paṟṟākkuṟai tīrvatu maṭṭumaṉṟip poruḷ vaḷamum perukum.

mīṉ kūṭṭaṅkaḷ uḷḷa iṭam, avai mēl varum iṭam, mīṉ piṭippataṟkuriya iṭam, kālam mutaliyavaṟṟai aṟivatu mika iṉṟiyamaiyātatu. mīṉkaḷukku vēṇṭiya ūṭṭap poruḷkaḷil ēṟpaṭum māṟṟam, avaṟṟiṉ taṉmai, paraval mutaliyavai aṟiyappaṭa vēṇṭum. tavira, kaṭal uyirkaḷaippaṟṟi aṟiyavum vāyppu ēṟpaṭum. ikkaṭalil pāti aḷavukku iṉṉum uyir nūl muṟaiyil mātiri pārkkappaṭavillai.

intiyak kaṭalil mīṉkaḷ atika aḷavukku iruppatu uṇmaiyē. āṉāl, avai ṭaṉ kaṇakkil pala iṭaṅkaḷil maṭivataṟkuk kāraṇam eṉṉa eṉpatai aṟiya vēṇṭum.

1957ām āṇṭu uruciyak kappal oṉṟu intiyak kaṭal vaḻiyākac ceṉṟatu. koḻumpiṟkum ēṭaṉ kalp nīrōṭṭattiṟkum iṭaiyē milliyaṉ ṭaṉ kaṇakkil mīṉkaḷ maṭintu mitantataik kaṇṭaṟintu, uṭaṉ aṟivittatu. mīṉkaḷ mitanta parappu 650 mail nīḷamum 140 mail akalamum iruntatu. itiliruntu intiyak kaṭaliṉ ceḻumai naṉku pulappaṭukiṟatu.

kaṭaliṉ kīḻiruntu mēl, nīr varum iṭaṅkaḷil mīṉkaḷ atikamāka irukkum. innīril ūṭṭap poruḷkaḷ mikutiyāka iruppatē kāraṇam. ivviṭaṅkaḷaik kaṇṭaṟintu ataṟkēṟpa mīṉ paṇṇaikaḷai niṟuvalām. itaṉāl uṇavu vaḷam perukum.

kaṉi vaḷam

intiyak kaṭal kaṇip poruḷ kaḷañciyamāka uḷḷatu. poṭṭāciyam, makṉiciyam mutaliya aṭippaṭaik kaṇip poruḷkaḷ atilatika aḷavukku uḷḷaṉa eṉṟu potuvāka nampappaṭukiṟatu. avaṟṟai eṭuppataṟkut takunta muṟaikaḷ uruvākkappaṭa vēṇṭum. avvāṟu uruvākkappaṭumāṉal, mēlum kūṭutalāka, maṅkaṉīs, nikkal, kopālṭ, cempu mutaliya mūlakaṅkaḷ ataṉ nīriliruntu pirittu eṭukkappaṭalām.

ataṉ taraip pakutiyil cila mūlakaṅkaḷ putaintu kiṭappatākavum karutappaṭukiṟatu. intak kaṇip poruḷkaḷ ellām intiyāviṉ poruḷ vaḷattaip perukkum eṉṟu nām nampalām. itaṉāl, intiyak kaṭalukku arukiluḷḷa nāṭukaḷum payaṉaṭaiyalām.

eṇṇeyp paṭivukaḷum ataṉ karai ōraṅḷil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. avaṟṟiliruntu eṇṇeyaip piritteṭuppiṉ, ataṉāl intiyāvum maṟṟa nāṭukaḷum payaṉaṭaiyalām.

kāṟṟukaḷum nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷum

intiyak kaṭalil kāṟṟukaḷum nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷum muḻu aḷavukkut ticai māṟukiṉṟaṉa. avvāṟu māṟutal āṇṭukku iru taṭavaikaḷ naṭaipeṟukiṟatu. itu pōṉṟu vēṟu eṅkum naṭaipeṟavillai.

teṉmēṟku vaṭakiḻakkup paruvak kāṟṟukaḷāl kāṟṟōṭṭaṅkaḷ ticai tiruppappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. itaṉāl māṟupaṭum iyaipuḷḷa kāṟṟu uṇṭākiṟatu; alai ōṭṭaṅkaḷ uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. innikaḻcci vāṉilai aṟiñarkaḷukkup putirāka uḷḷatu.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷiṉ ceṟivu, iruppiṭam ākiyavaṟṟai aṟivatil periya naṉmai uṇṭu. kappalkaḷ celvataṟkuriya cikkaṉamāṉa vaḻikaḷai mēṟkoḷḷalām. itaṉāl, eriporuḷ - eṇṇeyc celavu kuṟaintu, paṇam mīṟum. immuyaṟci vaṭa aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalaip poṟuttavarai veṟṟiyaḷittuḷḷatu. nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷai aṟintu avaṟṟiṟkēṟpap payaṇa vaḻikaḷai māṟṟiyatāl, kappal payaṇaṅkaḷukku ākum eriporuḷ celavil pattuppaṅku kuṟaikka muṭintatu. ippattup paṅkiṟkuriya paṇac celavu mītiyallavā?

intiyak kaṭaliṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷil uḷḷatu pōṉṟu avvaḷavu valuvuḷḷavai alla. avai pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷiṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷiliruntu valuvilum viraivilum atika aḷavukku vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉa. itai mēlum naṉkaṟintu uṟutippaṭuttalām.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ mēṟparappu kiḻakkākac cāyntuḷḷatu. maṟṟa kaṭalkaḷ mēṟkākac cāyntuḷḷaṉa. mēṟparappuc cāyviṟkum valuvāṉa putai nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ illāmaikkum toṭarpu irukkalām. intiyak kaṭaliṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷait teṉmēṟkup paruvakkāṟṟu aṭikkum poḻutu ārāya vēṇṭum. appoḻutu tāṉ uṇmai pulappaṭum.

paruvak kāṟṟukaḷ ticaimāṟi aṭippataṉal, kaṭal nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷilum, atil vāḻum uyirkaḷilum kuṟippiṭattakka viḷaivukaḷ uṇṭākkappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

mēṟkūṟiya viḷaivukaḷukkuriya kāraṇaṅkaḷ uṟuti ceyyappaṭa vēṇṭum. itaṟku intiyak kaṭalai muḻu aḷavukku naṉku ārāya vēṇṭum. ataṉ nīr mēliruntu kīḻvarai, atiluḷḷa poruḷkaḷuṭaṉ naṉku ārāyappaṭa vēṇṭum. poruḷkaḷ eṉpatil uyir vakaip poruḷkaḷ aṭaṅkum. tavira, nīriṉ iyalpukaḷaiyum, iyaipukaḷaiyum aṟiya vēṇṭum.

ataṉ nīrukkum kāṟṟu veḷikkum iṭaiyiluḷḷa ellaiyaiyum ārāya vēṇṭum. kāṟṟu veḷiyiṉ mēl pakutikaḷaiyum ārāytal nalam. ivvāṟu pala nilaikaḷil ārāycci ceyvatāl, intiyak kaṭal veppa entiramākap payaṉpaṭuvataippaṟṟi naṉku aṟiya iyalum. atu veppa entiramāka vēlai ceyvatāl, ataṟku mēluḷḷa kāṟṟōṭṭattil kuṟippiṭattakka viḷaivu uṇṭākiṟatu. ivvāṟu ellām ceyvatāl uṇṭākakkūṭiya perum naṉmai ituvē. paruvak kāṟṟukaḷ aṭippatai muṉ kūṭṭiyē kūṟa iyalum.

kaṭal nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷaip paruvak kāṟṟukaḷ tiruppukiṉṟaṉa. itaṉāl nīrkaḷ kīḻiruntu mēl varukiṉṟaṉa. innīrkaḷil mīṉkaḷukku vēṇṭiya ūṭṭap poruḷkaḷ niṟaiya uḷḷaṉa. inta ūṭṭap poruḷkaḷiṉ taṉmaipaṟṟi mēlum ārāyalām.

pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷil kuṟippiṭṭa āḻaṅkaḷil oru vakai etir nīrōṭṭam kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. itaṟku nilanaṭukkōṭṭu etir nīrōṭṭam eṉṟu peyar. inta ōṭṭam intiyak kaṭalilum irukkalām eṉṉum aiyattiṟku iṭamirukkiṟatu. inta aiyattai ārāycciyiṉāltāṉ pōkka iyalum.

curuṅkak kūṟiṉ, paruvak kāṟṟukaḷ kaṭal nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ ākiyavaipaṟṟik kaṭal nūl toṭarpāṉa palavakaic ceytikaḷait tiraṭṭuvataṟku inta ārāycci vaḻivakai ceyyum.

tarai

potuvākak kaṭaliṉ tarai orē vakaiyāṉa amaippai uṭaiyatu alla. itaṟku intiyak kaṭalum vilakkalla. malait toṭarkaḷum, eri malait tōṟṟamuṭaiya pāṟaikaḷum ataṉ taraiyil uḷḷaṉa. taraiyil pāṟaikaḷ atikamuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟaippaṟṟi mēlum virivāka ārāyalām.

aḻakiya pavaḻamalait toṭarkaḷum atil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ivai ulakilēyē mikapperiyavai; cikkalāṉa amaippu uṭaiyavai. ivaṟṟaippaṟṟi aṟintatu mikak kuṟaivu.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ aṭippakuti 4,000 mail aḷavukku muṟaiyāka ārāyappaṭa vēṇṭum. ataṉ aṭikkum nilavulakiṉ muṭikkum iṭaiyiluḷḷa paṭiviṉ aṭukkukaḷaiyum tiṭṭappaṭutta vēṇṭum. itaṟku nilanaṭukka muṟaiyaip payaṉpaṭuttalām.

nilavulakiṉ veṭippu itil nīṇṭuḷḷatu eṉṟu kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. inta uṇmaiyaiyum inta ārāycciyāl uṟutippaṭuttalām.

vāṉilai

intiyak kaṭal ārāycciyiṉ ciṟanta nōkkam, kaṭal nūl toṭarpākat tiruttamāṉa vāṉilaic ceytikaḷ tiraṭṭuvatē ākum. itu nila iyal nūl āṇṭut tiṭṭattiṉ ciṟanta nōkkaṅkaḷil oṉṟāka iruntatu.

paruvak kāṟṟu aṭippataiyum, maḻai peyyum aḷavil ēṟpaṭum māṟṟaṅkaḷaiyum aṟiya vēṇṭum. ivai iraṇṭaiyum tiruttamāka aṟivatāl veḷḷattaik kaṭṭuppaṭuttalām. nīrppācaṉattiṟkuvēṇṭiya nīraic carivarappeṟalām. ivvārāycci nīṇṭa ellai vāṉilai muṉṉaṟivippukku mikavum iṉṟiyamaiyātatu.

ciṟanta iṭam

pala nilaikaḷilum pārkkum poḻutu, ulaka aḷavil ārāycci ceyvataṟkuc ciṟanta iṭamāka intiyakkaṭal tikaḻkiṉṟatu.

ulaka aḷavil ārāyvataṟku ēṟṟa, kaṭalāka atu uḷḷatu. aimperuṅ kaṭalkaḷilēyē mikak kuṟaivāka ārāyappaṭṭa kaṭal atu oṉṟē. payaṉpaṭum nilai, koḷkai nilai eṉa iru vakaikaḷilum ataippaṟṟi aṟiyappaṭṭa viññāṉa aṟivu mikakkuṟaivu eṉṟē colla vēṇṭum.

ataṉ parappil ovvoru 90,000 catura mailkaḷukku ōr aḷavīṭukūṭa eṭukkappaṭavillai. atil ēṟpaṭum paruvak kāṟṟu māṟṟam pōl ulakil vēṟu eṅkum ēṟpaṭavillai. ataṉ naṭattai perumaḷavukku āciyāvaiyum āppirikkāvaiyum kavarnta vaṇṇam uḷḷatu.

kaṭal nūl toṭarpāka uḷḷa pala cikkalkaḷait tiṟamaiyāka ārāyntu, avaṟṟiṟkuriya tīrvukaḷaik kāṇutaṟkuriya ciṟanta iṭam intiyak kaṭalē. avvakaiyil palavakai ārāyccikaḷ ceyya atu vāyppaḷikkiṟatu.

vāṉilaiyaiyum taṭpaveppa nilaiyaiyum uṇṭākkuvatil, maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷaip pōṉṟu itaṟkuc ciṟanta iṭam uṇṭu.

taṟkāla aṟiviyal vaḷarcciyiṉāl nuṇukkaṅkaḷum karuvikaḷum perukiyuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟai intiyak kaṭalai ārāyvataṟku naṉku payaṉpaṭuttalām. ivvāṟu pala nilaikaḷil ārāyvataṟku vāyppu iruppatāltāṉ, maṟṟa kaṭalkaḷaik kāṭṭilum itai ārāyvatil atikak kavaṉam celuttappaṭṭirukkiṟatu.



.





﻿5. payaṉkaḷ

tiṭṭamiṭṭut tiṇṇiya muṟaiyil ceyaṟpaṭuttappaṭuvatu intiyak kaṭal ārāycci. ākavē, ataṉāl uṇṭākakkūṭiya ciṟanta payaṉkaḷ palavākum. avai yāvai eṉpatai iṟutiyākak kāṇpōm.

poruḷ vaḷam

intiyāviṉ poruḷ vaḷam intiyak kaṭal ārāycciyiṉāl peruka vaḻikaḷ pala uḷḷaṉa. intiyak kaṭaliṉ uyirkaḷ yāvum muṟaiyāka ārāyappaṭumāṉal, ataṉāl iruvakaiyil naṉmaikaḷ kiṭṭum. mutalāvatākak kaṭal uyirkaḷiṉ aṟivu perukum. itaṉāl kaṭal uyir nūl vaḷarum. iraṇṭāvatāka uṇṇattakka mīṉ vakaikaḷ muḻu aḷavukkup payaṉpaṭuttappaṭumāṉal, poruḷ vaḷamum perukum. uṇavup paṟṟākkuṟaiyum nīṅkum.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ arukil vāḻum nāṭukaḷiluḷḷa makkaḷ tokai ulaka makkaḷ tokaiyil kāl paṅku ākum. ivarkaḷatu uṇavup paṟṟākkuṟai nīṅkip poruḷ vaḷam peruka, intiyak kaṭaliṉ mīṉvaḷam naṉku payaṉpaṭuttappaṭa vēṇṭum. itaṟku viññāṉa muṟaikaḷil mīṉpiṭittal naṭaipeṟutal vēṇṭum. mīṉ paṇṇaikaḷaiyum avvāṟē amaikka vēṇṭum. mīṉ toḻil viññāṉak kalaiyākavē māṟa vēṇṭum.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ kaṉi vaḷaṅkaḷait tīra ārāyntu, avaṟṟaip payaṉpaṭutta, intiyāviṉ poruḷ vaḷam perukum. itaṟku intiyak kaṭal ārāycci vaḻivakai ceyyum eṉa nām nampalām. uṇmaiyil intiyak kaṭaliṉ kaṉi vaḷaṅkaḷ aḷaviṭaṟkariyaṉavākum. cikkaṉap payaṇa vaḻikaḷai intiyak kaṭalil mēṟkoḷvatāl, atikamākum paṇac celavai ellā nāṭukaḷum ōraḷavukkuk kuṟaikkalām.

kaṭal aṟivu

intiyak kaṭalaip poṟuttavarai kaṭal nūl aṟivu pala vaḻikaḷilum perukum eṉpatil aiyamillai. intiyak kaṭalai ārāyvatāl peṟum aṟivai, pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷ mutaliya maṟṟa kaṭalkaḷai ārāyvataṟkum payaṉpaṭuttalām.

pala cikkalkaḷukkut tīrvukaḷ kāṇalām intiyak kaṭalil mīṉkaḷ atika aḷavukku maṭikiṉṟaṉa. uyir nūl viññāṉikaḷukku itu perum putirākavē uḷḷatu. avvāṟu iṟattal atikamāka mīṉkaḷ uṇṭāvatā allatu vēṟu kāraṇaṅ kuṟittā eṉṟu aṟiyalām.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷil uḷḷatu pōṉṟu. avvaḷavu valuvuḷḷavai alla. avai pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷiṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷi﻿liruntu valuvilum viraivilum atikamāka vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ mēṟparappu kiḻakkākac cāyntuḷḷatu. ataṉ mēṟparappuc cāyviṟkum atil valuvāṉa putai nīr ōṭṭaṅkaḷ illātataṟkum toṭarpu irukkalām.

mēṟkūṟiya iru uṇmaikaḷum taṟkālikamākak kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. mēlum ceyyappaṭum ārāyccikaḷāl avai uṟuti ceyyappaṭum.

1959-61il intiyak kaṭalil cōviyattu viññāṉikaḷ iru payaṇaṅkaḷai mēṟkoṇṭaṉar. avarkaḷ taṅkaḷ payaṇaṅkaḷil ceyta ārāyccikaḷiṉ muṭivukaḷait terivittuḷḷaṉar. avai piṉvarumāṟu :

intiyak kaṭalukku aṭiyiluḷḷa nilavulaka muṭiyiṉ taṭimaṉ, amaippu, taṇivu ākiyavai mutaṉ mutalāka uṟuti ceyyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

avvāṟu ataṉ taṇivai ārāynta poḻutu, ituvarai aṟiyappaṭāta malaikaḷum malait toṭarkaḷum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. atil erimalait tōṟṟaṅkoṇṭa pāṟaikaḷ atika aḷavukku uḷḷaṉa.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ aṭiyiṉ iyaipaiyum amaippaiyum ārāyntapoḻutu, ataṉ teṉ pakutiyil irumpu, māṅkaṉis tātukkaḷ atika aḷavukku iruppatu teriyavantatu. ittātukkaḷil. 5 paṅku aḷavukku nikkal, kopālṭ mutaliya ulōkaṅkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ iyaipai ārāyntatiliruntu uṟutippaṭuttappaṭṭa uṇmaiyāvatu : intiyak kaṭaliluḷḷa mīṉkaḷukkum maṟṟa uyirkaḷukkum pōtumāṉa ākcijaṉ eṉṉum uyirk kāṟṟu perumaḷavukku uḷḷatu. vilakkukaḷ arapikkaṭalum, vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvum ākum. iṅkuc cila iṭaṅkaḷil ākcijaṉ kāṇappaṭavillai. iṅku nīr pōtumāṉa aḷavukkuc ceṅkuttāka ōṭātatē ākcijaṉ illāmaikkuk kāraṇamākum. ivviṭaṅkaḷil, uyirkaḷukku ūṟutarum ayṭirajaṉ calpaiṭu eṉṉum vāyu atikamākak kāṇappaṭukiṟatu.

potuvākak kaṭal nūl tuṟaiyil vallunarkaḷ nāḷukku nāḷ kuṟaintu koṇṭē varukiṟārkaḷ. atai oruvāṟu īṭu ceyya, tiṟamaimikka māṇavarkaḷai īrkkum poruṭṭu, ittiṭṭam perumaḷavil ceyaṟpaṭuttappaṭukiṟatu. aṉṟiyum, kaṭal nūl tuṟaiyil payilvōrukku niṟainta aṟivum pōtiya payiṟciyum kiṭaikka itaṉāl ētuvākum.

inta ārāycciyiṉāl tiraṭṭappaṭum pala tuṟaic ceytikaḷ vakaippaṭuttappaṭum; kaṇṭupiṭippukkaḷ tokukkappaṭum. ivai ellām iṟutiyāka oru nilaiyāṉa nūl vāyilāka veḷiyiṭappaṭum. innūl ciṟappākak kaṭal nūlil ārāycci ceyvōrukkup peritum payaṉpaṭum.

inta ārāycciyāl kaṭal nūl vaḷarvatu maṭṭumaṉṟi, ataṉōṭu toṭarpuṭaiya nila iyal nūl, nila amaippu nūl, nila nūl, uyir nūl, vāṉilai nūl mutaliya aṟivut tuṟaikaḷum vaḷarum eṉpatu veḷḷiṭaimalai.

intiyak kaṭalai muḻumaiyāka ārāyvatāl vāṉilai aṟivum, taṭpa veppanilai aṟivum, ciṟappākap perukum eṉpatil aiyamillai. itaṉāl nīṇṭa ellai vāṉilai muṉṉaṟivippai muṉkūṭṭiyē terivikka iyalum. intiyak kaṭaṟpakutikaḷaic cūḻnta nāṭukaḷukku maṭṭumallāmal avaṟṟiṟku appālum vāṉilai muṉṉaṟivippait terivikka iyalum, ulaka vāṉilai muṉṉaṟivippait terivippatil maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷ pōṉṟu intiyak kaṭalum ciṟanta iṭattaippeṟum.

ulakiṉ puṟa veḷiyāṉa vāṉveḷiyai aṟinta aḷavukku, ataṉ aka veḷiyāṉa kaṭalai aṟiyavillai eṉpatu ellōrum kūṟum oru potukkuṟaiyākum. intak kuṟai, ulaka aḷavil tīvirak kūṭṭu muyaṟciyuṭaṉ ceyyappaṭum intiyak kaṭal ārāycciyiṉāl aṟavē nīṅka vaḻiyuṇṭu eṉṟu nām etirpārkkalām. aṉṟiyum, inta ārāycci maṟṟap peruṅkaṭalkaḷai etirkālattil ārāyvataṟkum, muṉ mātiriyākak koḷḷappaṭalām eṉṟum nām nampalām.





* * *





﻿6. kaṭal ārāycciyiṉ nilai

muḻu aḷavukku intiyak kaṭalai ārāyāviṭṭālum ōraḷavukku atai intiyā ārāyntuḷḷatu. kaṭal nūl aṟivaip perukkiyuḷḷatu.

āntirap palkalaik kaḻakam

kaṭal ārāycciyaip pala kiḷaikaḷil mēṟkoṇṭa mutal nilaiyam āntirap palkalaik kaḻakamākum. intiyak kaṭaṟpaṭai aḷitta kappalkaḷaik koṇṭum, amerikkak kaṭal nūl aṟiñar lā pāṇṭ talaimaiyilum, palkalaikkaḻaka aṟiñarkaḷum āciriyarkaḷum muṟaiyākap pala ārāyccikaḷaic ceytuḷḷaṉar.

pērāciriyar lā pāṇṭ mutalil 1952-53 il āntirap palkalaikkaḻakattil ōrāṇṭu taṅkiṉar. mīṇṭum 1955-56il pulpiraiṭ tiṭṭattiṉ ātaravil āntirap palkalaikkaḻakattiṟku varalāṉar. vantatiṉ nōkkam ituvē: vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvil muṟaiyāka ārāycci ceyya; iyaṟkaik kaṭal nūl, kaṭal nila amaippu nūl, kaṭal uyir nūl ākiyavaṟṟil muṟaiyāka ārāycci ceyya, avaruṭaiya arum paṇikaḷukkāka ṭākṭar eṉṉum viññāṉat tuṟaic ciṟappup paṭṭattai, avarukku āntirap palkalaikkaḻakam aḷittatu.

kaṭal ārāycciyil āntirap palkalaikkaḻakam koṇṭa muṭivukaḷait taṉ veḷiyīṭukaḷilumveḷiyiṭṭatu. intiyāvilum veḷināṭukaḷilum uḷḷa viññāṉa itaḻkaḷilum avai veḷiyiṭappaṭṭaṉa. mēlum pala muṭivukaḷ veḷiyiṭappaṭuvataṟku āyattamāy uḷḷaṉa.

āntirap palkalaik kaḻakattiṉ iyaṟkaik kaṭal nūl kuḻuviṉar iru karuvikaḷaiyum amaittuḷḷaṉar. avaṟṟil oṉṟu eṭaik kuṟaivāṉa tarmisṭar veppamāṉi ākum. kaṭaliṉ mēṟparappu veppa nilaiyaiyum; ataṉ kontaḷikkum aṭukkiluḷḷa nuṇṇiya veppanilai māṟṟaṅkaḷaiyum itaik koṇṭu pativu ceyyalām.

maṟrueru karuvi kalaṅkalai aṟiyum māṉi. iṉāl kaṭal nīrkaḷiṉ oḷi ūṭuruvum taṉmaiyai ārāyalām.

iyaṟkaik kaṭal nūl ārāycci eṉpatu palavakaic cikkalkaḷukkut tīrvu kāṇak kūṭiyatu. accikkalkaḷ kaṭal nīrkaḷiṉ iyakkattōṭu toṭarpu uṭaiyavai. vēṟupaṭṭa pala mātaṅkaḷil vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāviṉ upput taṉmai, veppa nilai ākiyavai paṟṟic ceytikaḷ tiraṭṭappaṭṭaṉa. kāṟṟukaḷālum nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷālum kaṭaṟkarai nīrkaḷil paruvanilaikkēṟpa uyarvu tāḻvukaḷ iruppatāka acceytikaḷ terivittaṉa. ﻿kaṭal alai ārāyccikaḷiliruntu veḷiyāṉa muṭivukaḷ, kōtāvariyil kākkināṭā virikuṭāvai amaippataṟkum, ataṟkaṭuttuḷḷa karaiyil kaṭal arippait taṭuppataṟkum uriya vaḻivakaikaḷaik kāṇuvataṟkum utavum. kaṭal nila amaippu nūl tuṟaiyilum kuṟippiṭattakka ārāyccikaḷ ceyyappaṭṭu nalla muṭivukaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. kaṭaliṉ aṭiyiliruntu eṭukkappaṭum poruḷiṉ katiriyakkam, avai irukkum iṭattiṟkēṟpa māṟupaṭukiṟatu eṉṟum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu.

takunta ārāycciyiṉ vāyilākap paṭakukaḷum kappalkaḷum kaṭal uyirkaḷāl arikkappaṭuvatum taṭukkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itaṟkut taṭaiyāṟṟal atikamuṭaiya pala vakai maraṅkaḷum, ciṟanta vaṇṇaṅkaḷum iracāyaṉap poruḷkaḷum tēvai eṉak kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭatu.

maiya ārāycci maṉṟam

maiya aracōṭu toṭarpuṭaiya maṉṟam oṉṟu uḷḷatu. itaṟku viññāṉa - toḻil ārāycci maṉṟam eṉṟu peyar. kaṭal nūl toṭarpāṉa pala tiṭṭaṅkaḷukku immaṉṟam ākkamum ūkkamum kāṭṭi varukiṟatu.

kaṭal ārāycciyil pēr pōṉatāka āntirap palkalaik kaḻakam viḷaṅkukiṟatu. iḷaiñarkaḷ palar kaṭal nūl tuṟaiyiṉ pala pirivukaḷil ṭākṭar paṭṭam peṟṟuḷḷaṉar. ulakiṉ ciṟanta kaṭal ārāycci nilaiyaṅkaḷilum ataṟkup piṉ paṇiyāṟṟi mēlum taṅkaḷatu aṟivaip perukkik koṇṭuḷḷaṉar.

maṟṟa nilaiyaṅkaḷ

āntirap palkalaik kaḻakattait tavira, maṟṟa maiyaṅkaḷ cilavum uḷḷaṉa. avaiyāvaṉa : ceṉṉaiyiluḷḷa vilaṅku nūl ārāyccic cālaikaḷ, maṇṭapattiluḷḷa kaṭal mīṉ maiya ārāycci nilaiyam, tiruvitāṅkūr palkalaik kaḻakattiṉ kaṭal uyir nūl tuṟai, paṟaṅkip pēṭṭaiyiluḷḷa kaṭal uyir nūl nilaiyam.

mitakkum uyirkaḷāṉa ṭaiyāṭṭam mutaliyavaṟṟiṉ paravalpaṟṟiyum muṟaiyāka ārāyappaṭṭuḷḷatu. ivai jaṉavari-jūṉ mātaṅkaḷil atikamākavum, jūlai-ṭicampar mātaṅkaḷil kuṟaivākavum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ivai mīṉkaḷukkuc ciṟanta uṇavākum. ivaṟṟiṉ aḷavaip poṟuttē mīṉkaḷiṉ aḷavum amaikiṟatu. ākavē, avaṟṟai ārāyvatu mika iṉṟiyamaiyātatu. paṟaṅkippēṭṭaiyiluḷḷa kaṭal uyir nūl nilaiyam kaḻimuka uyirkaḷaip paṟṟi ārāyvatil atika nāṭṭam celuttiyuḷḷatu.

maṇṭapattiluḷḷa kaṭal mīṉ ārāycci nilaiyamum ataṉ kiḷaikaḷum vāṇipa nōkkil ciṟappuṭaiya mīṉkaḷai ārāynta vaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa.

karuvikaḷ

potuvāka, intiyāvil kaṭal ārāyccikkup payaṉpaṭum karuvikaḷāvaṉa:

nīr veppanilai varaivi

itu kaṭalil pala iṭaṅkaḷil veppanilai māṟṟaṅkaḷaic ceṅkuttākap pativu ceyyum.

nēṉcaṉ cīcākkaḷ

virumpiya āḻaṅkaḷil kaṭal nīrai eṭuttu, ataṉ upput taṉmai, iyaipu ākiyavaṟṟai ārāya ivai payaṉpaṭum.

nīrōṭṭa aḷavumāṉi

kaṭalil virumpiya iṭattil nīrōṭṭa aḷavaik kaṇakkiṭa itu utavum.

mātiri eṭukkum karuvikaḷ

kaṭaliṉ aṭiyiluḷḷa paṭivukaḷai mātiri eṭuppataṟku ivai payaṉpaṭum. mātirikaḷai vēṟupaṭṭa āḻaṅkaḷil eṭukkalām.

valaikaḷ

kaṭal uyirkaḷaip piṭippataṟku ivai payaṉpaṭum.

mēṉāṭṭuk karuvikaḷ

mēṉāṭukaḷil putai nīrp pukaippaṭap peṭṭikaḷ, nilanaṭukka veṭippai ārāyum karuvikaḷ, tolaikkāṭcik karuvi amaippukkaḷ mutaliyavai kaṭal ārāyccikkup payaṉpaṭukiṉṟaṉa. mika aṇmaikkāla ārāyccik karuvikaḷāṉa ivai iṉi intiyak kaṭal ārāyccikkum payaṉpaṭum.

ulaka aḷavil intiyak kaṭalai ārāya muṉṉēṟṟa nāṭukaḷ palavum muṉaintiruppatāl, intiyak kaṭal ārāycci tiṇṇiya muṟaiyil naṭaipeṟum. ataṉāl nalla pala muṭivukaḷum kiṭaikkum. kaṭal nūl aṟivum perukum. kaṭal nūl ariya viññāṉamāka māṟum.





.





﻿7. putiya kaṇṭupiṭippukaḷ

virivāka naṭaipeṟṟa intiyakkaṭal ārāycciyiṉāl pala putiya kaṇṭupiṭippukaḷ nikaḻntuḷḷaṉa. avai piṉvarumāṟu.

paḷḷattākkukaḷ

paranta oru paḷḷattākku intiyak kaṭalil iruppatai amerikka ārāycciyāḷarkaḷ kaṇṭu piṭittuḷḷaṉar. itu 6000 mail nīḷamum 25 mail akalamum uḷḷatu. antamāṉ kaṭalil cumatrāviṟkum parmāviṟkum iṭaiyil itu kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. kaṭalil cumār 3 mail āḻattil uḷḷatu. itaṉai uyaramāṉa malaiyuccikaḷ cūḻntuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟil mika uyaramāṉatu, paḷḷattākkiṟku mēl 12000 aṭi eḻumpiyuḷḷatu.

kālvāykaḷ

pala periya kālvāykaḷum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivai cēṟṟu āṟukaḷāl araṇ ceyyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟil mikap periyatu 4 mail akalamum 300 aṭi āḻamum uḷḷatu. itu vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāviṉ mēṟparappukkuk kīḻ 2 mail tolaivil kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. itu kaṅkaiyaik kāṭṭilum 25 maṭaṅku atika nīraic cumantu celkiṟatu.

malaittoṭar

kaṭalaṭi olippukaḷiṉ mūlam intiyak kaṭaliṉ muḻuttaraiyum paṭamākkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itaṉāl,

atil kuṭaivukaḷ, periya malaittoṭarkaḷ, tāḻvāka amaintuḷḷa camaveḷikaḷ mutaliyavai iruppatu kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. tavira, ataṉ virivāṉa mutal paṭamum uruvākkappaṭṭuḷḷatu.

kāṭṭākac cilōṉukkut teṉkiḻakkākat taraiyiliruntu 14,400 aṭi uyarattiṟku erimalait toṭar oṉṟu kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. kiṟittumas tīvukaḷukkut teṟkē 219 mail tolaivil erimalaikaḷum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟuḷ oṉṟu 2500 mīṭṭar āḻattilum maṟṟeṉṟu 3700 mīṭṭar āḻattilum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

vāṉilai

vāṉilai paṟṟi nirampac ceytikaḷ kiṭaittuḷḷaṉa. teṉmēṟkup paruvakkāṟṟu, vaṭa kiḻakkup paruvakkāṟṟu, kōṭaip paruvakkāṟṟu mutaliyavai ārāyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. kōṭaip paruvakkāṟṟu mikka valimai vāyntatu. itu vaṭa araittiraḷaip pakutiyiṉ vāṉilaiyaip pātikkiṟatu. mēlum, katirvīccu aḷavīṭukaḷum eṭukkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟiṉpaṭi, arapik kaṭalilum ceṅkaṭalilum nēraṭip pakalavaṉ katir vīccil 15% mutal 23% varai nīrāviyiṉālum vāyuviṉālum uṟiñcappaṭukiṟatu eṉpatu veḷiyākiyuḷḷa uṇmaiyākum. mēkak kūṭṭaṅkaḷiliruntu nilākkaḷiṉ vāyilākap peṟṟa ceytikaḷaik koṇṭu, muḻukkaṭalilum viḻum pakalavaṉ katirvīccaik kāṇum muṟaiyiṉai vāṉilai aṟiñarkaḷ vakukka iyalum.

katiriyakka vīcciṉ aḷavu

1966-il naṭaipeṟṟa cōviyattu ārāycciyiṉ kōkkam, ceyaṟkai iyaṟkaik katiriyakka vīccukkaḷai ārāyntu, avai kaṭal nīriluḷḷa tāvaraṅkaḷaiyum, vilaṅkukaḷaiyum evvāṟu pātikkiṉṟaṉa eṉpataik kaṇṭaṟivatākum. ivvārāycciyiṉpaṭi intiyak kaṭaliṉ katiriyakka vīcciṉ aḷavu atikam eṉpatu atircci tarakkūṭiya uṇmaiyākum. iḵtu aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalil uḷḷataiviṭa 3 maṭaṅku atikamākum. pacipik kaṭalila kaṭanta aṇu āyuta āyvukaḷiṉ viḷaivāka viḻunta katiriyakkat taṉimaṅkaḷiṉ kaḻivukaḷ, inta uyarvukkuk kāraṇamākum. aṭlāṇṭik kaṭal, intiyak kaṭal ākiyavai mīṉvaḷam mikkavai. ivvaḷattai ikkatirvīccu atikam pātikkum.

kuṟainta aḷavu ākcijaṉ

arapikkaṭal nīril kuṟainta aḷavu ākcijaṉ iruppatu jermāṉiya ārāycciyāl veḷiyākiyuḷḷa uṇmaiyākum. innilai pampāyiliruntu 150 mail tolaivuvarai uḷḷatu. potuvākak kuṟainta aḷavu ākcijaṉ kāṇappaṭum pakutikaḷ intiyak kaṭalil uḷḷaṉa. itupōṉṟu ulakiṉ vēṟu entak kaṭalilum illai.

veppat tuḷaikaḷ

ceṅkaṭalil 780 mīṭṭar āḻattil iru veppat tuḷaikaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ittuḷaikaḷaic cūḻntuḷḷa pakuti, ataṟkaṭuttuḷḷa pakutiyaiviṭa 8 maṭaṅku aḷavu uppu mikuntatāka uḷḷatu. ivai ceṅkaṭaliṉ maiyattil uḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟil vaḻakkattiṟku māṟaṉa irumpup paṭivukaḷ 6000 aṭi āḻattil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. iṅku veppanilai 55.9°C. evaresṭ ucciyil veppakkāṟṟaik kāṇpatu evvaḷavu viyappāka irukkumō, avvaḷavu viyappāka ikkaṇṭupiṭippu uḷḷatu. inta āḻattil vaḻakkamāka uḷḷa veppanilai 4-5°C ākum. amerikka ārāycciyāḷarkaḷ ivvuṇmaiyiṉai veḷippaṭuttiyuḷḷaṉar.

eṇṇey vaḷam

1974-ām āṇṭu pipravarit tiṅkaḷ pampāyk kaṭaṟkaraikku appāluḷḷa kaṭaliṉ aṭiyil eṇṇey (peṭrōliyam) eṭukkppaṭṭatu. ippakutikkup pāmpē hai (Bombay High) eṉṟu peyar. 1976 mēt tiṅkaḷ 21-ām nāḷiliruntu eṇṇey uṟpatti toṭarntu naṭaipeṟṟu varukiṟatu. itaṉ aḷavu āṇṭukkāṇṭu uyarnta vaṇṇam uḷḷatu. tamiḻnāṭu, kēraḷam, kujarāt, oricā mutaliya mānilaṅkaḷiṉ kaṭaṟkaraip pakutikaḷil eṇṇey kiṭaikka vāyppuḷḷatu. ippaṇpaṭā eṇṇeyiliruntu peṭrōl mutaliya eriporuḷkaḷ kiṭaikkiṉṟaṉa.

mīḷ vaṉam

intiyakkaṭal mīṉvaḷam mikkatu. kāṭṭāka, arapik kaṭalil omaṉ, maskaṭ ākiya kaṭaṟkarai vaḻiyākac ceṉṟu oru taṭavai mīṉ piṭittatil 45 nimiṭattil 3 ṭaṉ mīṉkaḷ -kiṭaittaṉa: uṇavup pañcattai nīkka, ivvaḷattai naṉku payaṉpaṭutti intiyā vaḻivakai kāṇavēṇṭum. tavira, intiyak kaṭalil 80 putiyavakai vilaṅkukaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

kaṉi vaḷam

1872-ām āṇṭilēyē intiyak kaṭalil māṅkaṉis muṇṭukaḷ iruppatu kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭatu. 1962-64-il naṭanta intiyakkaṭal ārāycciyum itaṉai uṟutippaṭuttiyatu. ivai ciṟiya uruṇṭaikaḷiliruntu periya tiraḷkaḷ varai uḷḷaṉa. ivai intiyāviṟkum srīlaṅkāviṟkum teṟkē 4000-5000 mīṭṭar āḻattil uḷḷaṉa; kaṭaliṉ aṭiyil āṇṭutōṟum 10 milliyaṉ ṭaṉkaḷ uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. ivaṟṟil māṅkaṉis, cempu, nikkal, mālipṭiṉam mutaliya ulōkaṅkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.

mēṟkuk kaṭaṟkaraiyil ūṭṭaccattu mikunta karimappaṭivukaḷum, pāspēṭ paṭivukaḷum kaṇṭu piṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivai ciṟanta uramāka amaintu, vēḷāṇmait tuṟaikku atikam payaṉpaṭum.

intiyak kaṭal tarai ceṅkaḷimaṇṇil 220 ṭirilliyaṉ ṭaṉ alumiṉiyamum, 650 ṭirilliyaṉ ṭaṉ irumpum, 73 ṭirilliyaṉ ṭiṭṭāṉiyamum, 15 ṭirilaliyaṉ ṭiṉ, veṉāṭiyam, kōpālṭ, nikkal, cempu, kārīyamum vaṟṟātu pala āyiram āṇṭukaḷ kiṭaikkum eṉa matippiṭappaṭṭuḷḷatu.

nilattiluḷḷa kōpālṭ 40 āṇṭukaḷukku maṭṭumē varum. kaṭalukkaṭiyil uḷḷatō 200 āyiram āṇṭukaḷukku varum!

tavirac ceṅkaṭaliṉ aṭiyil kaṉipporuḷ ceṟivuḷḷa tātukkaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivai ellā niṟaṅkaḷilum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ivaṟṟil poṉ, veḷḷi, tuttanākam, cempu irumpu, māṅkaṉis mutaliya ulōkaṅkaḷ aṭaṅkiyuḷḷaṉa. ippaṭivukaḷ 7000 aṭi āḻattil kiṭaikkiṉṟaṉa.

ceṅkaṭaliluḷḷa vaṭi nilaṅkaḷil (basin) periyavai 8 mail nīḷamum, 4 mail akalamum uḷḷavai. ivaṟṟil 50 aṭi āḻattiṟku nīr uḷḷatu. innīriṉ veppanilai 133°F. ceṅkaṭal nīriṉ potuvāṉa veppanilai 68°F. iṅkuk kaṭal nīriṉ uppaḷavu maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷaik kāṭṭilum 10 maṭaṅku atikam uḷḷatu.

ceṅkaṭalil uḷḷa kaṉiporuḷ paṭukaikaḷiṉ taṭimaṉ maṭṭum 300 aṭi ākum, oru periya vaṭinilattil maṭṭum 130 milliyaṉ ṭaṉ cempu, tuttanākam, veḷḷi, taṅkam mutaliya ulōkaṅkaḷ uḷḷaṉa. mēlum, arapik kaṭaliṉ pāspēṭ aḷavu maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷaik kāṭṭiluma 5 maṭaṅku atikam uḷḷatu.

kaṇṭac carivu

intiyak kaṭaliṉ tarai paṟṟip pala putiya ciṟappiyalpukaḷ kaṇṭaṟiyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟil oṉṟu mikac ceṅkuttāṉa kaṇṭac carivu (continental slope) ākum. iḵtu ulakilēyē mika āḻamāṉatu. cilōṉ kiḻakkuk kaṭaṟkaraikku appāl uḷḷatu.

aṭlas

intiyak kaṭal ārāycciyiṉāl, intiyak kaṭal 2,80,00,000 catura mailkaḷ aṟiviyal tiṭpattuṭaṉ ārāyappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itaṉ oru pakutiyāka, akkaṭaliṉ vēti uyiriyal paṟṟi ōr aṭlas tayārikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itu kaṭaliliruntu atika uṇavupeṟap peritum utavum.

ivvāṟu, intiyak kaṭaliṉ koḻikkum pal vaḷaṅkaḷaiyum payaṉpaṭutti, intiyāviṉ poruḷ vaḷattaip perukkuvatē aṟivuṭaimai ākum.

8. paruvakkāṟṟu ārāycci

pakalavaṉ viḷaiviṉāl, paruvakkāṟṟu maḻaikaḷai uṇṭākkuvatu intiyap peruṅkaṭal ākum. paruvakkāṟṟukaḷai ārāyum muyaṟciyē paruvakkāṟṟu āyvu (Monsoon Experiment) ākum . itaṉaic curukkamāka mōṉaks (Monex) eṉalām.

varalāṟu

mōṉaks-79 eṉpatu uṇmaiyil mutal taṭavaiyāka naṭaipeṟum tiṭṭamaṉṟu. kaṭanta 15 āṇṭukaḷāka, atāvatu 1964-ām āṇṭiliruntu itu toṭarpākat toṭakka nilai āyvukaḷ ceyyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

1963-65-il naṭanta aṉaittulakak kaṭal ārāyccip payaṇam (IIOE), 1973-il muṭinta intiya cōviyattu vāṉiyal āyvu (ISMEX), intiyak kappalkaḷum, cōviyattuk kappalkaḷum kalantu koṇṭu 1977-ām āṇṭu mē 23-mutal ākasṭ 20 varai naṭaipeṟṟa ciṟu mōṉaks ākiyavai ellām taṟpoḻutu naṭaipeṟṟuk koṇṭirukkum mōṉaks-79-iṉ muṉṉōṭikaḷākuma.

intiya uṟṟunōkku ārāycci nilaiyaṅkaḷiṉ potu iyakkunar avarkaḷ mārip paruvakkāṟṟukaḷ, kōṭaip paruvakkāṟṟu ākiyavaṟṟiṉ tēciyap poruḷātārac ciṟappai uṇarntatālum, 1970-il piracalcil naṭaipeṟṟa ulakat tiṭṭa mānāṭṭiṉ (Global Planning Conference) pērāḷarāka iruntatālum, ammānāṭṭil teṟku āciyap pakutikkup paruvakkāṟṟu āyvu (MONEX) oṉṟiṉai naṭatta muṉmoḻintār. piṉ, itu jeṉivāvil kaṭanta ulaka vāṉilai ārāycci nilaiyak kūṭṭattil ēṟṟukkoḷḷappaṭṭu, iṉṟu ceyaṟpaṭukiṟatu.

nōkkaṅkaḷ

ittiṭṭattiṉ aṭippaṭai nōkkaṅkaḷ piṉvarumāṟu: -

1) nāṭṭiṉ vēṟupaṭṭa taṭpa veppanilaip pakutikaḷil paruvakkāṟṟu aṭikkat toṭaṅkum nāṭkaḷait tōrāyamāka aṟital.

2) 90 nāḷ koṇṭa paruvakkāṟṟuk kālattil peṟappaṭum maḻaiyiṉ muḻu aḷavai matippiṭal.

3) nāṭṭiṉ vēṟupaṭṭa pakutikaḷil 5-7 nāṭkaḷ koṇṭa kālattil atikamākavum, kuṟaivākavum ēṟpaṭum maḻaiyiṉai aṟital, curuṅkakkūṟiṉ paruvakkāṟṟiṉait teḷivāka aṟivatē nōkkamākum.



kāraṇikaḷ

pakalavaṉ katirvīccu, puvik katirvīccu, peruṅkaṭalkaḷiṉ iyakkam, nilam, kaṭal ākiyavaṟṟiṉ urāyvu viḷaivukaḷ, malaikaḷ mutaliyavai paruva maḻaikkuriya kāraṇikaḷākum. itil mukkiya paṅkupeṟuvatu imālayap pakutikaḷ ākum. ippakutikaḷ illai eṉil uṇmaip paruvak kāṟṟum illai. paruvakkāṟṟuc cuḻaṟciyil imālayap pakutikaḷiṉ veppa viḷaivukaḷum, iyakka viḷaivukaḷum ciṟanta iṭattaip peṟukiṉṟaṉa. ivviru uṇmaikaḷai itukāṟum ceyyappaṭṭa ārāyccikaḷ veḷippaṭuttukiṉṟaṉa.

paruvakkāṟṟu muṉṉarē uṇṭāvatu kōṭaiyil tipēttiyac camaveḷi veppamaṭaivatāl eṉpatum maṟṟum oru kāraṇiyākum.

paṅkupeṟum nāṭukaḷum niṟuvaṉaṅkaḷum

ittiṭṭattiṟkāka intiya aracu 25 kōṭi rūpāy otukkiyuḷḷatu. intiya vāṉilai ārāycci varalāṟṟil itu periya āyvu ākum. itaṉai intiyā, uruciyā, amerikkā ākiya nāṭukaḷ naṭattukiṉṟaṉa.

ittiṭṭattil kalantukoḷḷum intiya ārāycci niṟuvaṉaṅkaḷ piṉvarumāṟu. pampāy pāpā aṇu ārāycci maiyam, pampāy ṭāṭṭā aṭippaṭai ārāycci niṟuvaṉam, akamatāpāt iyaṟpiyal ārāyccik kūṭam, haitarāpāt tolaiyiṭa aṟinilaiyam.

naṭaipeṟum muṟai

cōviyattuk kappalkaḷ kaṭal uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ, vāṉilai uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ mutaliyavaṟṟai naṭattum. ivai tam paṇiyait toṭaṅki uḷḷaṉa. teṉmēṟkup paruvakkāṟṟai uruvākkuvatil āṟṟalvāynta cōmali nīrōṭṭattiṟkup paṅkuṇṭu eṉṉum oru nampikkaiyuḷḷatu. itaṉai ārāya ikkappalkaḷ āppirikkāviṉ kiḻakkuk karai neṭuka uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷai eṭutta vaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa.

innīrōṭṭattai ārāyntapiṉ, ikkappalkaḷ arapikkaṭal, vaṅkāḷa virikuṭā ākiya pakutikaḷukkuc ceṉṟu, aṅkuḷḷa intiyak kappalkaḷōṭu cērntu, teṟku allatu vaṭakkākap palkōṇat tōṟṟattai (Polygonal configuration) eṭukkum.”

amerikkā, taṟkāla aṟiviyal karuvikaḷ niṟainta vāṉa ūrtiyiṉai vaḻaṅkum. kāṭṭiṉ kuṟukkāka vaṭa-teṉ ticaikaḷilum kiḻakku mēṟkut ticaikaḷilum ivvūrti iyaṅki, vēṇṭiya vāṉoli aḷavīṭukaḷai eṭukkum.

ittiṭṭattil nāṉku intiyak kappalkaḷum paṅkukoḷḷum. ivaṟṟil kuṟippiṭattakka aḷavīṭukaḷai eṭukkap pōtiya karuvikaḷ uḷḷaṉa.

aṭukku veḷiyil uḷḷa kāṟṟukaḷ, nilavum veppanilai ākiyavaṟṟiṉ aḷavīṭukaḷai mūṉṟu nilaiyaṅkaḷiluḷḷa ēvukaṇaikaḷāl eṭukkappaṭum. avai tumpā (7° va), srīkarikōṭṭā (16° va), pālacōr (22° va) ākum. inta uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ uruciyak kappalkaḷ eṭukkum ēvukaṇai uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷōṭu oppiṭappaṭum. intiyak kappal vāṉilai mitappukaḷaip pala iṭaṅkaḷil viṭṭu, mitappu uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷai eṭukkum.

kōs-10 eṉpatu amerikka nilaiyāṉa nila nilā ākum. itu puviyiṉ cuḻaṟciyōṭu cērntu cuṟṟuvatāl pārppataṟku nilaiyāka iruppatu pōl teriyum. nila naṭukkōṭṭil itu 60°, kiḻakku nīḷ kōṭṭil valam varum. intiyap peruṅkaṭal, arapikkaṭal ākiyavaṟṟiṉ pakutikaḷai itu vaṭṭamiṭum.

teriyum pakutiyil tōrāyamāka 3 - 5 kilō mīṭṭar pakuppum, akaccivappup pakutiyil 7 kilō mīṭṭar pakuppumuḷḷa mēkappaṭaṅkaḷai itu aṉuppum. ivai mīṭiyōsṭāṭ-1 eṉṉum nilāviṉāl oru nāḷaikku nāṉku taṭavaikaḷ pampāykku añcal ceyyappaṭum. innilā airōppiya vāṉaveḷi nilaiyattiṉ nilaiyākavuḷḷa nila nilāvākum. itaṟkākat tērnteṭukkappaṭṭuḷḷa maṟṟa nilaiyaṅkaḷukku ippaṭaṅkaḷ, pampāyiliruntu mīṇṭum aṉuppappaṭum. tavira, nilaiyāṉa aṭippaṭaiyila tāmākap paṭam eṭukkum karuvikaḷ ēḻu intiyāviṭam uḷḷatu. naṉku tiṭṭamiṭappaṭṭiruppatāl, inta ārāycciyiṉāl pala naṉmaikaḷ uṇṭāka nalvāyppuḷḷatu.





inta miṉṉūlaip paṟṟi


uṅkaḷukku immiṉṉūl, iṇaiya nūlakamāṉa, vikkimūlattil iruntu kiṭaittuḷḷatu[1].

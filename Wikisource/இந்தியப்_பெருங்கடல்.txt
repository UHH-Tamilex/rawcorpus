﻿1. amippu



kaṭalkaḷ

aimperuṅ kaṭalkaḷāl cūḻappaṭṭatē nām vāḻum ulakam. avi muṟiyē pacipik kaṭal, intiyak kaṭal, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭal, ārkṭik kaṭal, aṇṭārkṭik kaṭal ākum.

iruppiṭam

ulakil mūṉṟāvatu periya kaṭal intiyak kaṭal. ataṟku mēṟkē āppirikkāvum, vaṭakkē arēpiyā, intiyā, malēyā ākiya tīpakaṟpaṅkaḷum; kiḻakkē āstirēliyāvum uḷḷaṉa. ataṉ teṉpakuti aṇṭārkṭik kaṭalōṭu kalakkiṟatu. pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷ pōlavē, ārkṭik kaṭalōṭu atu toṭarpu koḷḷavilli.

intiyak kaṭal āciyā, āppirikkā, āstirēliyā ākiya mūṉṟu kaṇṭaṅkaḷukkiṭiyil uḷḷatu. ataṉ vaṭapakuti intiyāvāl pirikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. atu tōṟṟattil nerukkamāy aminta kaṭal. iṅtōṉēṣiyāviliruntu āppirikkāvari paraviyuḷḷatu. atu paruvak kāṟṟukaḷukkup piṟappiṭam.

tōṟṟam

ataṉ parappu kiṭṭattaṭṭa 3 kōṭi catura milkaḷ; carācari āḻam 15,000 aṭi. atu ula-

1–68

kiṉ mēṟparappil 14 paṅki aṭittuk koṇṭirukkiṟatu. pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷ pōṉṟu avvaḷavu periyatō āḻamāṉatō alla atu. atu 20 kōṭi āṇṭukaḷukku muṉ tōṉṟiyatu.

tuṇik kaṭalkaḷ

ataṟkuk kiḻakkilum mēṟkilum arapik kaṭalum vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvum uḷḷaṉa. ceṅ kaṭalum pāracīka nīrōṭṭamum ataṉ uḷnāṭṭuk kaṭalkaḷ. ulakiluḷḷa perum ciṟu kaṭalkaḷ atil kalakkiṉṟaṉa. imayamaliyil urukum paṉi ellām atil kalakkiṉṟatu. cintu, pirama puttirā, kaṅki, airāvati, kāviri mutaliya pērāṟukaḷum atil kalakkiṉṟaṉa. ataṟkup periya vaṭinilam uṇṭu.

tīvukaḷ

maṭakāskar, ilaṅki mutaliyavi ataṉ perum kaṇṭat tīvukaḷ. ilaṭcat tīvukaḷ, mālat tīvukaḷ ataṉ mutaṉmiyāṉa kaṭal tīvukaḷ. potuvāka, ataṉ tīvukaḷ pacipik kaṭaliṉ tīvukaḷ pōlavē, eri maliyālum pavaḻattālum āṉavi.

malikaḷ

intiyak kaṭalil kuṇṭuṅkuḻikaḷum, uyarnta malit toṭarkaḷum, āḻamāṉa akaḻikaḷum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

ciṟappāka, atil kāṇappaṭum pavaḻa malit toṭarkaḷ cikkalāṉa amippuṭiyavi. ataṉ teṉkiḻakku, teṉmēṟkup pakutikaḷit tavira, eñciyavi nilat tokutiyāl cūḻap paṭṭavi. ataṉ teṉ kōṭiyil paṉikkaṭṭikaḷum paṉippāṟikaḷum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ

intiyak kaṭalil kāṇappaṭum nīroṭṭaṅkaḷ pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷil kāṇappaṭum nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ pōṉṟu avvaḷavu virivum, valimiyum koṇṭavi alla. ataṉ mēṟparappu kiḻakkākac cāyntuḷḷatu. maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷ mēṟkākac cāyntuḷḷaṉa. oru kōṭiyil maṭṭum tiṟantuḷḷa orē peruṅkaṭal ituvē.

atil kāṇappaṭum mukkiya nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷāvaṉa: nilanaṭukkōṭṭu nīrōṭṭam, mocām pikuyu nīrōṭṭam, akulās nīrōṭṭam.

vaḷam

intiyak kaṭaliṉ iyaṟki vaḷam matippiṭaṟkariyatu. ulakak kaṭalkaḷilēyē atika aḷavukkup palavaki uyirp poruḷkaḷ uḷḷa kaṭal ituvākum. ataṉ mīṉ vaḷam niṟivāṉa poruḷ vaḷatti aḷikkavallatu. tavira, ataṉ kaṉivaḷamum matippiṭaṟkariyatē. ataṉ vāṇipa vaḷamum varalāṟṟuc ciṟappuṭiyatē. ﻿veṭippu

nilavulakiṉ mēṟparappu 45,000 mil toliviṟku veṭittuḷḷatu eṉa aṇmiyil kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. ivveṭippu aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalik kaṭantu āppirikkā vari celkiṟatu. intiyak kaṭalukkuḷḷum atu talikāṭṭukiṟatu.

iṭarkaḷ

intiyak kaṭaliṉ karippakutikaḷil perumpāṉmi vāḻvataṟku ēṟṟatalla. aṅkuppayaṅkara vilaṅkukaḷum, koṭiya nōykaḷum uḷḷaṉa. atil alikaḷiṉ perumeḻucci vīḻccikaḷum, payaṅkara nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷum uḷḷaṉa. aṅkut toṭarntu valuvāṉa kāṟṟukaḷ aṭittavaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa.

intiyakkaṭal puyalkaḷukkum nilikkaḷamāka uḷḷatu. atil tīṅkutarum kalmīṉ, koṭṭum mīṉ, cuṟā mutaliyavi vāḻkiṉṟaṉa. ivvāṟu atu iṭarkaḷum tīṅkukaḷum niṟintu kāṇappaṭukiṟatu.

veppanili

intiyak kaṭal āḻamāṉa kaṭal maṭṭumalla; veppak kaṭalumākum. ataṉ mēṟparappu veppanili 90°F aḷavukku uyarukiṉṟatu. 12,000 aṭi āḻattil ataṉ cīrāṉa veppanili 35° F. ﻿upputtaṉmi

intiyak kaṭalum uppu vaḷam niṟintatē. avvaḷam nam nāṭṭiṉ poruḷ vaḷatti ōraḷavukkup perukkavallatu.

kappal ārāycci

cōviyattu aṟiviyalār allatu viññāṉikaḷ viṭyās eṉṉuṅ kappalil intiyak kaṭaliṉ nilak kāntattiṉ maṟivi aṟiya 1959ām āṇṭu vantaṉar. taṅkaḷ āyvukku vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvil maṟivāṉa or iṭattit tērnteṭuttaṉar. āyviṉ nōkkam intiyak kaṭaliṉ nilakkāntak kaḷatti aṟivatē.

cōviyattu aṟiviyalār intiyak kaṭaliṉ miyap pakutiyil 20,000 milukku mēl aḷavi ceytuḷḷaṉar. itaṉāl cila iṉṟiyamiyāc ceytikaḷ tiraṭṭappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟik koṇṭu intiyak kaṭaliṉ amippu, varalāṟu mutaliyavaṟṟi nila amippu nūl muṟiyil aṟiya vāyppu ēṟpaṭum.

avarkaḷ taṅkaḷ āyvukaḷiliruntu terivittirukkum muṭivukaḷāvaṉa: intiyak kaṭaliṉ tari mika ariya amippi uṭiyatu. kiḻakku mēṟkup pakutikaḷil ataṉ amippu perumaḷavukku māṟupaṭukiṟatu. tari 8.15 mīṭṭar nīḷattiṟku iṭiyē uḷḷa nāṉku uṭpakutikaḷik koṇṭuḷḷatu. uṭpakutikaḷil mīḷum māṟṟaṅkaḷ kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

avarkaḷ mēṟkoṇṭa nilanaṭukka uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ veḷippaṭuttuvaṉa:- intiyak kaṭalil uḷḷa taḷarcciyāṉa paṭivukaḷiṉ taṭimaṉ 100 mīṭṭariliruntu 200 mīṭṭar vari uḷḷatu.

vāṇipa vaḻi

tīvuk kūṭṭaṅkaḷ niṟiya iruppiṉum kappal pōkku varaviṟku intiyak kaṭal mikavum payaṉpaṭukiṟatu. ciṅkappūr, pampāy, koḻumpu, ceṉṉi mutaliyavi ataṉ mutaṉmiyāṉa tuṟimukaṅkaḷ ākum.

1869il cūyas kālvāy tiṟakkappaṭṭapiṉ ataṉ vāṇipam periya aḷavil uḷḷatu. ataṉ ciṟanta vāṇipap pakutiyil cūyas kālvāy toṭarpu koḷkiṟatu. ikkālvāy tiṟakkappaṭṭavuṭaṉ naṉṉampikki muṉi vaḻiyākak kappalkaḷ cellutal aṟavē niṉṟuviṭṭatu. cūyas kālvāy vaḻiyākac celvatu kuṟukku vaḻiyākum. vaḻiyiṉ tolivu naṉṉampikki muṉiyiṉ vaḻiyākac celvatik kāṭṭilum 5000 milkaḷ kuṟiyum. itaṉāl taṟkāla vāṇipam ōṅkiyuḷḷatu.

ulakiluḷḷa mikap periya vāṇipa vaḻikaḷil oṉṟāka intiyak kaṭal uḷḷatu. paṇṭik kālattil ikkaṭal vaḻiyāka intiyāviliruntu ekiptu, mecappaṭōmiyā, kiḻakku miyattarik kaṭal nāṭukaḷ ākiyavaṟṟuṭaṉ vāṇipam ciṟappāka naṭipeṟṟatu.

tamiḻ kāṭṭiliruntu mayil tōki, akil mutaliya poruḷkaḷ pāpilōṉiyā ceṉṟaṉa eṉṟu kiṟittuva maṟiyāṉa pipiḷ terivikkiṟatu. tamiḻakattiliruntu palavakip poruḷkaḷ urōmāpurikkuc ceṉṟatākat tamiḻ nūlkaḷum; urōma āciriyar piḷiṉiyiṉ nūlkaḷum kūṟukiṉṟaṉa. yavaṉarkaḷākiya kirēkkarkaḷ kāvirippūmpaṭṭiṉam mutaliya tamiḻnāṭṭut tuṟimukaṅkaḷukku vantu vāṇipam ceytaṉar eṉṟum pintamiḻ nūlkaḷ kūṟukiṉṟaṉa. teṉṉintiyāviṟkum kaṭāram eṉṉum parmāvukkum vāṇipat toṭarpu iruntatākap paṭṭiṉappāli kūṟukiṟatu.

kiṟittuvak kālattiṉ toṭakkattil mēṉāṭṭuk kappalkaḷ intiyak kaṭal vaḻiyākac cīṉāviṟkuc ceṉṟaṉa. cīṉak kappalkaḷ arēpiyāviṟkuc ceṉṟaṉa.

ki. pi. 5ām nūṟṟāṇṭiliruntu caṟṟēṟak kuṟiya āyiram āṇṭukaḷ cīṉāviṟkum arēpiyāvukkum vāṇipam ciṟappāka naṭipeṟṟatu.

15ām nūṟṟāṇṭiṉ toṭakkattil cīṉak kappaṟ paṭit talivar, ceṅ-hōs eṉpavaruṭaṉ intiyā, arēpiyā mutaliya nāṭukaḷil tam aracarukkāka navarattiṉaṅkaḷ vāṅka intiyāviṟku vantār. avarukkup piṉ pōrccukkīciyar, ṭaccukkārar, pireñcukkārar, āṅkilēyar mutaliya mēṉāṭṭār intiyāviṟku vāṇipam ceyya vantaṉar.

iṉṟu intiyak kaṭal mikapperiya kaṭal vaḻiyāka uḷḷatu. itaṉāl intiyāvilum, ataṉ arukiluḷḷa nāṭukaḷilum mikapperiya tuṟimukaṅkaḷ ēṟpaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. intiyāviliruntu tāṉiyaṅkaḷ, malittōṭṭap poruḷkaḷ, tātup poruḷkaḷ, kaccāp poruḷkaḷ mutaliyavi maṟṟa nāṭukaḷukku ēṟṟumati ceyyappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ellāvaṟṟiṟkum mēlāka, intiyak kaṭal intiyāviṟku iyaṟki araṇākavum tikaḻkiṟatu.

﻿2. varalāṟu

muṟiyāṉa ārāycci

intiyak kaṭal vāṇipa vaḻiyāka nīṇṭa kālamāka iruntu vantirukkiṟatu. āṉāl, atu muṟiyāka iṉṉum ārāyappaṭavilli. 1873ām āṇṭiliruntu irupatiṟku mēṟpaṭṭa kappalkaḷ kaṭal ārāycciyi mēṟkoṇṭuḷḷaṉa. āṉāl, avaṟṟil oṉṟāvatu intiyak kaṭali muṟiyākavum virivākavum ārāyavilli.

ūkkam piṟattal

intiyak kaṭali ārāyum ūkkam mutaṉ mutalāka 1881ām āṇṭu piṟantatu. ivvāṇṭil ec. em. ai. es. iṉvesṭikēṭṭar eṉṉuṅ kappal intiyak kaṭaṟkariyiṉ nīrp pakutikaḷi aḷaviyiṭa viṭappaṭṭatu.

1885-1887il alāk eṉpār mēṟkūṟiya kappalil intiyak kaṭaṟ pakutikaḷic cuṟṟic ceṉṟār. avaṟṟiluḷḷa uyir vakikaḷip paṟṟip payaṉuḷḷa ceytikaḷit tiraṭṭi 1888il ōr ariya nūl veḷiyiṭṭār. ataṉ peyar ‘intiyak kaṭaṟ pakutikaḷil ōr iyaṟki nūl aṟiñaṉ’ eṉpatākum.

svel eṉpār atē kappalil vaṅkāḷa virikuṭā, arapik kaṭal ākiyavaṟṟil pala uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ ceytār. mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭa uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ iracāyaṉa, vāṉilit toṭarpāṉavi. avaṟṟiṉ muṭivukaḷi āciya aracar kaḻaka (Royal Asiatic Society) veḷiyīṭukkaḷil 1925-38ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭiyē veḷiyiṭṭār.

avar mēṟkoṇṭa ārāyccikaḷ vaṅkāḷa virikuṭā, arapik kaṭal ākiyavaṟṟip paṟṟiya aṭippaṭi aṟivit teḷivākkiṉa.

miya araciṉ muyaṟci

intiyak kaṭaṟ pakutikaḷil mēlum ārāyccikaḷ mēṟkoḷḷappaṭa, 1947il muyaṟci toṭaṅkiṟṟu. ivvāṇṭil miya araciṉar irāmēcuvarattiṟku arukiluḷḷa maṇṭapam eṉṉumiṭattil ōr ārāycci niliyatti niṟuviṉar. itu kaṭal nūl miya mīṉ ārāycci niliyamākum. itaṟku intiyā muḻutum kiḷikaḷ uḷḷaṉa. inniliyam kaṭal mīṉkaḷippaṟṟi ārāycci naṭattiya vaṇṇam uḷḷatu.

maṟṟa niliyaṅkaḷ

1952il āntirap palkalik kaḻakam pala kaṭal payaṇaṅkaḷukku ēṟpāṭu ceytatu. itaṟku intiyak kaṭaṟpaṭi utaviṟṟu. payaṇaṅkaḷ vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvil mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭaṉa. avaṟṟāl kiṭitta muṭivukaḷil cilavaṟṟi 1954-58il veḷiyiṭappaṭṭa iru kaṭal nūl tokutikaḷil āntirap palkalik kaḻakam veḷiyiṭṭatu.

maṟṟoru niliyam kaṭal uyir ārāycci niliyamākum. itu paṟaṅkippēṭṭiyil uḷḷatu. aṇṇāmalip palkalik kaḻakattuṭaṉ toṭarpuṭiyatu.

intiyak kaṭal ārāyccit tiṭṭam

tavira, ceṉṉi, tiruvaṉantapuram, pampāy mutaliya palkalik kaḻakaṅkaḷum kaṭal nūl ārāycci ceyta vaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa. miya araciṉ cārpāka iyaṅkum viññāṉa-toḻil ārāycci maṉṟamum kaṭal ārāyccikku āvaṉa ceytu varukiṟatu. iruppiṉum, muḻu mūccāka intiyak kaṭali ulaka aḷavil ārāyum tiṭṭam 1959ām āṇṭiltāṉ uruvāyiṟṟu. taṟpoḻutu atu ceyaṟpaṭṭa vaṇṇam uḷḷatu. ituvē intiyak kaṭal ārāyccipaṟṟiya varalāṟu ākum.

﻿3. kaṭal ārāyccit tiṭṭam

peyar

intiyak kaṭaliṉ iyaṟki vaḷaṅkaḷi ārāyat tiṭṭam oṉṟu ulaka aḷavil vakukkappaṭṭatu. itaṟku aṉittulaka intiyak kaṭal ārāyccit tiṭṭam eṉṟu peyar.

tōṟṟam

ittiṭṭam karu niliyil 1957ām āṇṭilēyē uruvāyiṟṟu. āṉal muḻu niliyil uruppeṟṟatu 1959ām āṇṭiltāṉ. aṉittulaka aṟiviyal kūṭṭuk kaḻaka maṉṟam, yuṉeskō, amerikka aṟiviyal muṉṉēṟṟak kaḻakam ākiyavaṟṟiṉ naṉ muyaṟciyiṉāl, aṉittulakak kaṭal nūl pēravi 1959il cepṭampart tiṅkaḷil niyūyārkkil kūṭiyatu: kaṭal nūli aṟiviyalāka-viññāṉamāka-māṟṟa vēṇṭum eṉṟu muṭivu ceytatu.

avik kūṭṭattil 45 nāṭukaḷiliruntu 1,100 aṟiviyalār kalantu koṇṭaṉar. intak kūṭṭattil aṉittulaka intiyak kaṭal ārāyccit tiṭṭam aṟivikkappaṭṭatu. ittiṭṭattiṉ pala paṇikaḷic ceyaṟpaṭuttum aluvalakam niyūyārkkil uḷḷatu. ittiṭṭattip pala aṟiviyalār koṇṭa oru taṉik kuḻu vakuttatu.

kālavari

1957il uruvāṉālum ittiṭṭattiṟkuriya kālam aintu āṇṭukaḷākum. atāvatu ittiṭṭam 1960ām āṇṭiliruntu 1964ām āṇṭu vari ceyaṟpaṭum. kālam tēvippaṭumāṉāl, mēlum nīṭṭikkappaṭum. muḻu aḷavil 1962ām āṇṭiliruntu ittiṭṭam ceyaṟpaṭat toṭaṅkiṟṟu.

ciṟappu

ulaka aḷavil pala nāṭukaḷiṉ kūṭṭu muyaṟciyiṉāl naṭipeṟum māperum tiṭṭamākum itu. aṉittulaka nila iyal nūl āṇṭut tiṭṭatti muṉ mātiriyākak koṇṭu ceyaṟpaṭuttappaṭuvatu. ituvari mēṟkoḷḷappaṭṭa kaṭal ārāyccikaḷil mikap periyatu itu.

nila iyal nūl āṇṭut tiṭṭamum ulaka aḷavil 1957-58ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭiyē ciṟappuṭaṉ iṉitu niṟivēṟiya tiṭṭamākum. itaṉāl nila iyal nūl naṉku vaḷarvataṟku vāyppu ēṟpaṭṭatu. aṟiviyaliṉ maṟṟat tuṟikaḷum vaḷaralāyiṉa. vāṉveḷi ārāycci karuvuṟṟu uruppeṟṟatu.

mutaṉ mutalāka intiyak kaṭal niṟivāka aṟiviyal aṭippaṭiyil ārāyappaṭukiṟatu. ittiṭṭattil kalantu koḷḷum ovvoru nāṭum kuṟippiṭṭa tuṟiyi, cikkali ārāyum; ataṟkēṟṟa muṭivukaḷik kāṇum. ciṟappāka, vāṉilipaṟṟiyum ārāyappaṭum.

ittiṭṭattiṟku ākum celavu 6 kōṭi rūpāy ākum. iccelavi ovvoru nāṭum taṉṉāl iyaṉṟa aḷavukkup pakirntu koḷḷum.

tiṭṭakālattil 1,88,000 mil toliviṟku 60 kappal payaṇaṅkaḷ mēṟkoḷḷappaṭum. 20 nāṭukaḷiliruntu 40 kappalkaḷ ippayaṇaṅkaḷi mēṟkoḷḷum; intiyak kaṭali ārāyum. pala nāṭukaḷiluḷḷa 350 aṟiviyalār, ittiṭṭattil kalantu koṇṭu intiyak kaṭali ārāyvārkaḷ.

itil kalantu koḷḷum nāṭukaḷil cila : āstirēliyā, piriṭṭaṉ, ilaṅki, intiyā, pākkistāṉ, ṭeṉmārk, pārmōcā, pirāṉcu, intōṉēṣiyā, isrēl, jappāṉ, hālantu, teṉ āppirikkā, mēṟku jermaṉi, amerikkā, uruciyā.

niṟivēṟṟum muṟikaḷ

uṟṟu nōkkal, olittal, aḷavukaḷ eṭuttal, paṭam piṭittal, mātirip poruḷkaḷ tiraṭṭutal mutaliya pala muṟikaḷ mēṟkoḷḷappaṭum. ivaṟṟiṟku ellām aṭippaṭiyākak kappaṟ payaṇaṅkaḷē mēṟkoḷḷappaṭum. ciṟantārāyccik karuvikaḷākap pala vakik karuvikaḷiyum uḷḷaṭakkiya kappalkaḷ payaṉpaṭum. iṟutiyākac ceyyappaṭṭa pala vaki ārāyccikaḷiṉ muṭivukaḷ tokukkappaṭum; vakippaṭuttappaṭṭu ovvoru tuṟiyilum payaṉpaṭuttappaṭum.

amerikkā mutaliya nāṭukaḷ kappalkaḷiyum kaṭal nūl aṟiñarkaḷiyum vaḻaṅkum. amerikkā, tiṭṭattil pāti celaviyum ēṟkum. cila nāṭukaḷ ārāycci ceyvataṟkuriya vacatikaḷi aḷikkum.

intiyak kaṭalukku arukiluḷḷa nāṭukaḷ kaṭal alikaḷiṉ eḻucci vīḻccikaḷil ēṟpaṭum māṟṟaṅkaḷiyum, kāṟṟu mēl veḷiyil uruvākum vāṉili māṟṟaṅkaḷiyum uṟṟu nōkki intiyak kaṭali ārāya utavum.

ārāyappaṭum tuṟikaḷ

intiyak kaṭal ārāycci paranta ōr ārāycci ākum. ākavē, atil āyvataṟku eṭuttuk koḷḷappaṭum tuṟikaḷum pala vakippaṭṭaṉavākavē uḷḷaṉa. attuṟikaḷil ciṟappāṉavi piṉvarumāṟu :

nila amippu nūl

itu oru virinta tuṟiyākum. itu pala tuṟikaḷi mēlum taṉṉuḷ aṭakkiyatu.

nila iyal nūl

itu mika virinta tuṟiyākum. itilum mēlum pala tuṟikaḷ aṭaṅkukiṉṟaṉa.

kaṭal nūl

ituvum pala tuṟikaḷit taṉṉuḷ aṭakkiyatē. iyaṟkik kaṭal nūl, iyipuk kaṭal nūl, uyir kaṭal nūl eṉap pala vakippaṭum.

vāṉili nūl

itu mēṟkūṟiya tuṟikaḷōṭu neruṅkiya toṭarpuṭiyatu.

nīriyal

itu kaṭal nūliṉ oru pirivu.

uyiriyal

ituvum oru virinta tuṟiyē. ciṟappāka, payir nūliyum, vilaṅku nūliyum taṉṉuḷ aṭakkiyatu.

maṟṟum, ittiṭṭattil maḻip poḻivu, katir vīccu, īrppu, nila naṭukkam, veppa oṭṭam, kāṟṟu (mēl) veḷi, paṭivukaḷ, kānta māṟṟam, kaṉi vaḷam mutaliyavi paṟṟiyum virivāka ārāyappaṭum.

4. ārāycci ēṉ?



intiyak kaṭali ārāyvataṟkuc ciṟanta kāraṇaṅkaḷ pala uḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟi iṅkuk kāṇpōm.

uyirkaḷ

iṟantoḻinta paṇṭikkāla uyirkaḷiṉ eccamiccaṅkaḷi itil tēṭik kaṇṭupiṭikkalām. avvāṟu kaṇṭupiṭippatāl, akkaṇṭupiṭippukkaḷ uyir nūl, nila amippu nūl mutaliya tuṟikaḷukku mikavum payaṉpaṭum. uyirkaḷiṉ paṭippaṭi vaḷarcciyip paṟṟiya putiya uṇmikaḷi aṟiyalām.

ulakak kaṭalkaḷilēyē atika aḷavukku uyir vakip poruḷkaḷ itil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ciṟappāka, mīṉ vakikaḷ atil niṟiya uḷḷaṉa. ataṉ mīṉvaḷatti ataṟku arukilirukkum nāṭukaḷ naṉku payaṉpaṭuttavilli. avvāṟu payaṉpaṭuttumāṉal, uṇavup paṟṟākkuṟi tīrvatu maṭṭumaṉṟip poruḷ vaḷamum perukum.

mīṉ kūṭṭaṅkaḷ uḷḷa iṭam, avi mēl varum iṭam, mīṉ piṭippataṟkuriya iṭam, kālam mutaliyavaṟṟi aṟivatu mika iṉṟiyamiyātatu. mīṉkaḷukku vēṇṭiya ūṭṭap poruḷkaḷil ēṟpaṭum māṟṟam, avaṟṟiṉ taṉmi, paraval mutaliyavi aṟiyappaṭa vēṇṭum. tavira, kaṭal uyirkaḷippaṟṟi aṟiyavum vāyppu ēṟpaṭum. ikkaṭalil pāti aḷavukku iṉṉum uyir nūl muṟiyil mātiri pārkkappaṭavilli.

intiyak kaṭalil mīṉkaḷ atika aḷavukku iruppatu uṇmiyē. āṉāl, avi ṭaṉ kaṇakkil pala iṭaṅkaḷil maṭivataṟkuk kāraṇam eṉṉa eṉpati aṟiya vēṇṭum.

1957ām āṇṭu uruciyak kappal oṉṟu intiyak kaṭal vaḻiyākac ceṉṟatu. koḻumpiṟkum ēṭaṉ kalp nīrōṭṭattiṟkum iṭiyē milliyaṉ ṭaṉ kaṇakkil mīṉkaḷ maṭintu mitantatik kaṇṭaṟintu, uṭaṉ aṟivittatu. mīṉkaḷ mitanta parappu 650 mil nīḷamum 140 mil akalamum iruntatu. itiliruntu intiyak kaṭaliṉ ceḻumi naṉku pulappaṭukiṟatu.

kaṭaliṉ kīḻiruntu mēl, nīr varum iṭaṅkaḷil mīṉkaḷ atikamāka irukkum. innīril ūṭṭap poruḷkaḷ mikutiyāka iruppatē kāraṇam. ivviṭaṅkaḷik kaṇṭaṟintu ataṟkēṟpa mīṉ paṇṇikaḷi niṟuvalām. itaṉāl uṇavu vaḷam perukum.

kaṉi vaḷam

intiyak kaṭal kaṇip poruḷ kaḷañciyamāka uḷḷatu. poṭṭāciyam, makṉiciyam mutaliya aṭippaṭik kaṇip poruḷkaḷ atilatika aḷavukku uḷḷaṉa eṉṟu potuvāka nampappaṭukiṟatu. avaṟṟi eṭuppataṟkut takunta muṟikaḷ uruvākkappaṭa vēṇṭum. avvāṟu uruvākkappaṭumāṉal, mēlum kūṭutalāka, maṅkaṉīs, nikkal, kopālṭ, cempu mutaliya mūlakaṅkaḷ ataṉ nīriliruntu pirittu eṭukkappaṭalām.

ataṉ tarip pakutiyil cila mūlakaṅkaḷ putintu kiṭappatākavum karutappaṭukiṟatu. intak kaṇip poruḷkaḷ ellām intiyāviṉ poruḷ vaḷattip perukkum eṉṟu nām nampalām. itaṉāl, intiyak kaṭalukku arukiluḷḷa nāṭukaḷum payaṉaṭiyalām.

eṇṇeyp paṭivukaḷum ataṉ kari ōraṅḷil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. avaṟṟiliruntu eṇṇeyip piritteṭuppiṉ, ataṉāl intiyāvum maṟṟa nāṭukaḷum payaṉaṭiyalām.

kāṟṟukaḷum nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷum

intiyak kaṭalil kāṟṟukaḷum nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷum muḻu aḷavukkut tici māṟukiṉṟaṉa. avvāṟu māṟutal āṇṭukku iru taṭavikaḷ naṭipeṟukiṟatu. itu pōṉṟu vēṟu eṅkum naṭipeṟavilli.

teṉmēṟku vaṭakiḻakkup paruvak kāṟṟukaḷāl kāṟṟōṭṭaṅkaḷ tici tiruppappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. itaṉāl māṟupaṭum iyipuḷḷa kāṟṟu uṇṭākiṟatu; ali ōṭṭaṅkaḷ uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. innikaḻcci vāṉili aṟiñarkaḷukkup putirāka uḷḷatu.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷiṉ ceṟivu, iruppiṭam ākiyavaṟṟi aṟivatil periya naṉmi uṇṭu. kappalkaḷ celvataṟkuriya cikkaṉamāṉa vaḻikaḷi mēṟkoḷḷalām. itaṉāl, eriporuḷ - eṇṇeyc celavu kuṟintu, paṇam mīṟum. immuyaṟci vaṭa aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalip poṟuttavari veṟṟiyaḷittuḷḷatu. nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷi aṟintu avaṟṟiṟkēṟpap payaṇa vaḻikaḷi māṟṟiyatāl, kappal payaṇaṅkaḷukku ākum eriporuḷ celavil pattuppaṅku kuṟikka muṭintatu. ippattup paṅkiṟkuriya paṇac celavu mītiyallavā?

intiyak kaṭaliṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷil uḷḷatu pōṉṟu avvaḷavu valuvuḷḷavi alla. avi pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷiṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷiliruntu valuvilum virivilum atika aḷavukku vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉa. iti mēlum naṉkaṟintu uṟutippaṭuttalām.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ mēṟparappu kiḻakkākac cāyntuḷḷatu. maṟṟa kaṭalkaḷ mēṟkākac cāyntuḷḷaṉa. mēṟparappuc cāyviṟkum valuvāṉa puti nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ illāmikkum toṭarpu irukkalām. intiyak kaṭaliṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷit teṉmēṟkup paruvakkāṟṟu aṭikkum poḻutu ārāya vēṇṭum. appoḻutu tāṉ uṇmi pulappaṭum.

paruvak kāṟṟukaḷ ticimāṟi aṭippataṉal, kaṭal nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷilum, atil vāḻum uyirkaḷilum kuṟippiṭattakka viḷivukaḷ uṇṭākkappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

mēṟkūṟiya viḷivukaḷukkuriya kāraṇaṅkaḷ uṟuti ceyyappaṭa vēṇṭum. itaṟku intiyak kaṭali muḻu aḷavukku naṉku ārāya vēṇṭum. ataṉ nīr mēliruntu kīḻvari, atiluḷḷa poruḷkaḷuṭaṉ naṉku ārāyappaṭa vēṇṭum. poruḷkaḷ eṉpatil uyir vakip poruḷkaḷ aṭaṅkum. tavira, nīriṉ iyalpukaḷiyum, iyipukaḷiyum aṟiya vēṇṭum.

ataṉ nīrukkum kāṟṟu veḷikkum iṭiyiluḷḷa elliyiyum ārāya vēṇṭum. kāṟṟu veḷiyiṉ mēl pakutikaḷiyum ārāytal nalam. ivvāṟu pala nilikaḷil ārāycci ceyvatāl, intiyak kaṭal veppa entiramākap payaṉpaṭuvatippaṟṟi naṉku aṟiya iyalum. atu veppa entiramāka vēli ceyvatāl, ataṟku mēluḷḷa kāṟṟōṭṭattil kuṟippiṭattakka viḷivu uṇṭākiṟatu. ivvāṟu ellām ceyvatāl uṇṭākakkūṭiya perum naṉmi ituvē. paruvak kāṟṟukaḷ aṭippati muṉ kūṭṭiyē kūṟa iyalum.

kaṭal nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷip paruvak kāṟṟukaḷ tiruppukiṉṟaṉa. itaṉāl nīrkaḷ kīḻiruntu mēl varukiṉṟaṉa. innīrkaḷil mīṉkaḷukku vēṇṭiya ūṭṭap poruḷkaḷ niṟiya uḷḷaṉa. inta ūṭṭap poruḷkaḷiṉ taṉmipaṟṟi mēlum ārāyalām.

pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷil kuṟippiṭṭa āḻaṅkaḷil oru vaki etir nīrōṭṭam kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. itaṟku nilanaṭukkōṭṭu etir nīrōṭṭam eṉṟu peyar. inta ōṭṭam intiyak kaṭalilum irukkalām eṉṉum aiyattiṟku iṭamirukkiṟatu. inta aiyatti ārāycciyiṉāltāṉ pōkka iyalum.

curuṅkak kūṟiṉ, paruvak kāṟṟukaḷ kaṭal nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ ākiyavipaṟṟik kaṭal nūl toṭarpāṉa palavakic ceytikaḷit tiraṭṭuvataṟku inta ārāycci vaḻivaki ceyyum.

tari

potuvākak kaṭaliṉ tari orē vakiyāṉa amippi uṭiyatu alla. itaṟku intiyak kaṭalum vilakkalla. malit toṭarkaḷum, eri malit tōṟṟamuṭiya pāṟikaḷum ataṉ tariyil uḷḷaṉa. tariyil pāṟikaḷ atikamuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟippaṟṟi mēlum virivāka ārāyalām.

aḻakiya pavaḻamalit toṭarkaḷum atil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ivi ulakilēyē mikapperiyavi; cikkalāṉa amippu uṭiyavi. ivaṟṟippaṟṟi aṟintatu mikak kuṟivu.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ aṭippakuti 4,000 mil aḷavukku muṟiyāka ārāyappaṭa vēṇṭum. ataṉ aṭikkum nilavulakiṉ muṭikkum iṭiyiluḷḷa paṭiviṉ aṭukkukaḷiyum tiṭṭappaṭutta vēṇṭum. itaṟku nilanaṭukka muṟiyip payaṉpaṭuttalām.

nilavulakiṉ veṭippu itil nīṇṭuḷḷatu eṉṟu kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. inta uṇmiyiyum inta ārāycciyāl uṟutippaṭuttalām.

vāṉili

intiyak kaṭal ārāycciyiṉ ciṟanta nōkkam, kaṭal nūl toṭarpākat tiruttamāṉa vāṉilic ceytikaḷ tiraṭṭuvatē ākum. itu nila iyal nūl āṇṭut tiṭṭattiṉ ciṟanta nōkkaṅkaḷil oṉṟāka iruntatu.

paruvak kāṟṟu aṭippatiyum, maḻi peyyum aḷavil ēṟpaṭum māṟṟaṅkaḷiyum aṟiya vēṇṭum. ivi iraṇṭiyum tiruttamāka aṟivatāl veḷḷattik kaṭṭuppaṭuttalām. nīrppācaṉattiṟkuvēṇṭiya nīric carivarappeṟalām. ivvārāycci nīṇṭa elli vāṉili muṉṉaṟivippukku mikavum iṉṟiyamiyātatu.

ciṟanta iṭam

pala nilikaḷilum pārkkum poḻutu, ulaka aḷavil ārāycci ceyvataṟkuc ciṟanta iṭamāka intiyakkaṭal tikaḻkiṉṟatu.

ulaka aḷavil ārāyvataṟku ēṟṟa, kaṭalāka atu uḷḷatu. aimperuṅ kaṭalkaḷilēyē mikak kuṟivāka ārāyappaṭṭa kaṭal atu oṉṟē. payaṉpaṭum nili, koḷki nili eṉa iru vakikaḷilum atippaṟṟi aṟiyappaṭṭa viññāṉa aṟivu mikakkuṟivu eṉṟē colla vēṇṭum.

ataṉ parappil ovvoru 90,000 catura milkaḷukku ōr aḷavīṭukūṭa eṭukkappaṭavilli. atil ēṟpaṭum paruvak kāṟṟu māṟṟam pōl ulakil vēṟu eṅkum ēṟpaṭavilli. ataṉ naṭatti perumaḷavukku āciyāviyum āppirikkāviyum kavarnta vaṇṇam uḷḷatu.

kaṭal nūl toṭarpāka uḷḷa pala cikkalkaḷit tiṟamiyāka ārāyntu, avaṟṟiṟkuriya tīrvukaḷik kāṇutaṟkuriya ciṟanta iṭam intiyak kaṭalē. avvakiyil palavaki ārāyccikaḷ ceyya atu vāyppaḷikkiṟatu.

vāṉiliyiyum taṭpaveppa niliyiyum uṇṭākkuvatil, maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷip pōṉṟu itaṟkuc ciṟanta iṭam uṇṭu.

taṟkāla aṟiviyal vaḷarcciyiṉāl nuṇukkaṅkaḷum karuvikaḷum perukiyuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟi intiyak kaṭali ārāyvataṟku naṉku payaṉpaṭuttalām. ivvāṟu pala nilikaḷil ārāyvataṟku vāyppu iruppatāltāṉ, maṟṟa kaṭalkaḷik kāṭṭilum iti ārāyvatil atikak kavaṉam celuttappaṭṭirukkiṟatu.

﻿5. payaṉkaḷ

tiṭṭamiṭṭut tiṇṇiya muṟiyil ceyaṟpaṭuttappaṭuvatu intiyak kaṭal ārāycci. ākavē, ataṉāl uṇṭākakkūṭiya ciṟanta payaṉkaḷ palavākum. avi yāvi eṉpati iṟutiyākak kāṇpōm.

poruḷ vaḷam

intiyāviṉ poruḷ vaḷam intiyak kaṭal ārāycciyiṉāl peruka vaḻikaḷ pala uḷḷaṉa. intiyak kaṭaliṉ uyirkaḷ yāvum muṟiyāka ārāyappaṭumāṉal, ataṉāl iruvakiyil naṉmikaḷ kiṭṭum. mutalāvatākak kaṭal uyirkaḷiṉ aṟivu perukum. itaṉāl kaṭal uyir nūl vaḷarum. iraṇṭāvatāka uṇṇattakka mīṉ vakikaḷ muḻu aḷavukkup payaṉpaṭuttappaṭumāṉal, poruḷ vaḷamum perukum. uṇavup paṟṟākkuṟiyum nīṅkum.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ arukil vāḻum nāṭukaḷiluḷḷa makkaḷ toki ulaka makkaḷ tokiyil kāl paṅku ākum. ivarkaḷatu uṇavup paṟṟākkuṟi nīṅkip poruḷ vaḷam peruka, intiyak kaṭaliṉ mīṉvaḷam naṉku payaṉpaṭuttappaṭa vēṇṭum. itaṟku viññāṉa muṟikaḷil mīṉpiṭittal naṭipeṟutal vēṇṭum. mīṉ paṇṇikaḷiyum avvāṟē amikka vēṇṭum. mīṉ toḻil viññāṉak kaliyākavē māṟa vēṇṭum.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ kaṉi vaḷaṅkaḷit tīra ārāyntu, avaṟṟip payaṉpaṭutta, intiyāviṉ poruḷ vaḷam perukum. itaṟku intiyak kaṭal ārāycci vaḻivaki ceyyum eṉa nām nampalām. uṇmiyil intiyak kaṭaliṉ kaṉi vaḷaṅkaḷ aḷaviṭaṟkariyaṉavākum. cikkaṉap payaṇa vaḻikaḷi intiyak kaṭalil mēṟkoḷvatāl, atikamākum paṇac celavi ellā nāṭukaḷum ōraḷavukkuk kuṟikkalām.

kaṭal aṟivu

intiyak kaṭalip poṟuttavari kaṭal nūl aṟivu pala vaḻikaḷilum perukum eṉpatil aiyamilli. intiyak kaṭali ārāyvatāl peṟum aṟivi, pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷ mutaliya maṟṟa kaṭalkaḷi ārāyvataṟkum payaṉpaṭuttalām.

pala cikkalkaḷukkut tīrvukaḷ kāṇalām intiyak kaṭalil mīṉkaḷ atika aḷavukku maṭikiṉṟaṉa. uyir nūl viññāṉikaḷukku itu perum putirākavē uḷḷatu. avvāṟu iṟattal atikamāka mīṉkaḷ uṇṭāvatā allatu vēṟu kāraṇaṅ kuṟittā eṉṟu aṟiyalām.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷil uḷḷatu pōṉṟu. avvaḷavu valuvuḷḷavi alla. avi pacipik, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalkaḷiṉ nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷi﻿liruntu valuvilum virivilum atikamāka vēṟupaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ mēṟparappu kiḻakkākac cāyntuḷḷatu. ataṉ mēṟparappuc cāyviṟkum atil valuvāṉa puti nīr ōṭṭaṅkaḷ illātataṟkum toṭarpu irukkalām.

mēṟkūṟiya iru uṇmikaḷum taṟkālikamākak kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. mēlum ceyyappaṭum ārāyccikaḷāl avi uṟuti ceyyappaṭum.

1959-61il intiyak kaṭalil cōviyattu viññāṉikaḷ iru payaṇaṅkaḷi mēṟkoṇṭaṉar. avarkaḷ taṅkaḷ payaṇaṅkaḷil ceyta ārāyccikaḷiṉ muṭivukaḷit terivittuḷḷaṉar. avi piṉvarumāṟu :

intiyak kaṭalukku aṭiyiluḷḷa nilavulaka muṭiyiṉ taṭimaṉ, amippu, taṇivu ākiyavi mutaṉ mutalāka uṟuti ceyyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

avvāṟu ataṉ taṇivi ārāynta poḻutu, ituvari aṟiyappaṭāta malikaḷum malit toṭarkaḷum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. atil erimalit tōṟṟaṅkoṇṭa pāṟikaḷ atika aḷavukku uḷḷaṉa.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ aṭiyiṉ iyipiyum amippiyum ārāyntapoḻutu, ataṉ teṉ pakutiyil irumpu, māṅkaṉis tātukkaḷ atika aḷavukku iruppatu teriyavantatu. ittātukkaḷil. 5 paṅku aḷavukku nikkal, kopālṭ mutaliya ulōkaṅkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.

intiyak kaṭaliṉ iyipi ārāyntatiliruntu uṟutippaṭuttappaṭṭa uṇmiyāvatu : intiyak kaṭaliluḷḷa mīṉkaḷukkum maṟṟa uyirkaḷukkum pōtumāṉa ākcijaṉ eṉṉum uyirk kāṟṟu perumaḷavukku uḷḷatu. vilakkukaḷ arapikkaṭalum, vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvum ākum. iṅkuc cila iṭaṅkaḷil ākcijaṉ kāṇappaṭavilli. iṅku nīr pōtumāṉa aḷavukkuc ceṅkuttāka ōṭātatē ākcijaṉ illāmikkuk kāraṇamākum. ivviṭaṅkaḷil, uyirkaḷukku ūṟutarum ayṭirajaṉ calpiṭu eṉṉum vāyu atikamākak kāṇappaṭukiṟatu.

potuvākak kaṭal nūl tuṟiyil vallunarkaḷ nāḷukku nāḷ kuṟintu koṇṭē varukiṟārkaḷ. ati oruvāṟu īṭu ceyya, tiṟamimikka māṇavarkaḷi īrkkum poruṭṭu, ittiṭṭam perumaḷavil ceyaṟpaṭuttappaṭukiṟatu. aṉṟiyum, kaṭal nūl tuṟiyil payilvōrukku niṟinta aṟivum pōtiya payiṟciyum kiṭikka itaṉāl ētuvākum.

inta ārāycciyiṉāl tiraṭṭappaṭum pala tuṟic ceytikaḷ vakippaṭuttappaṭum; kaṇṭupiṭippukkaḷ tokukkappaṭum. ivi ellām iṟutiyāka oru niliyāṉa nūl vāyilāka veḷiyiṭappaṭum. innūl ciṟappākak kaṭal nūlil ārāycci ceyvōrukkup peritum payaṉpaṭum.

inta ārāycciyāl kaṭal nūl vaḷarvatu maṭṭumaṉṟi, ataṉōṭu toṭarpuṭiya nila iyal nūl, nila amippu nūl, nila nūl, uyir nūl, vāṉili nūl mutaliya aṟivut tuṟikaḷum vaḷarum eṉpatu veḷḷiṭimali.

intiyak kaṭali muḻumiyāka ārāyvatāl vāṉili aṟivum, taṭpa veppanili aṟivum, ciṟappākap perukum eṉpatil aiyamilli. itaṉāl nīṇṭa elli vāṉili muṉṉaṟivippi muṉkūṭṭiyē terivikka iyalum. intiyak kaṭaṟpakutikaḷic cūḻnta nāṭukaḷukku maṭṭumallāmal avaṟṟiṟku appālum vāṉili muṉṉaṟivippit terivikka iyalum, ulaka vāṉili muṉṉaṟivippit terivippatil maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷ pōṉṟu intiyak kaṭalum ciṟanta iṭattippeṟum.

ulakiṉ puṟa veḷiyāṉa vāṉveḷiyi aṟinta aḷavukku, ataṉ aka veḷiyāṉa kaṭali aṟiyavilli eṉpatu ellōrum kūṟum oru potukkuṟiyākum. intak kuṟi, ulaka aḷavil tīvirak kūṭṭu muyaṟciyuṭaṉ ceyyappaṭum intiyak kaṭal ārāycciyiṉāl aṟavē nīṅka vaḻiyuṇṭu eṉṟu nām etirpārkkalām. aṉṟiyum, inta ārāycci maṟṟap peruṅkaṭalkaḷi etirkālattil ārāyvataṟkum, muṉ mātiriyākak koḷḷappaṭalām eṉṟum nām nampalām.

﻿6. kaṭal ārāycciyiṉ nili

muḻu aḷavukku intiyak kaṭali ārāyāviṭṭālum ōraḷavukku ati intiyā ārāyntuḷḷatu. kaṭal nūl aṟivip perukkiyuḷḷatu.

āntirap palkalik kaḻakam

kaṭal ārāycciyip pala kiḷikaḷil mēṟkoṇṭa mutal niliyam āntirap palkalik kaḻakamākum. intiyak kaṭaṟpaṭi aḷitta kappalkaḷik koṇṭum, amerikkak kaṭal nūl aṟiñar lā pāṇṭ talimiyilum, palkalikkaḻaka aṟiñarkaḷum āciriyarkaḷum muṟiyākap pala ārāyccikaḷic ceytuḷḷaṉar.

pērāciriyar lā pāṇṭ mutalil 1952-53 il āntirap palkalikkaḻakattil ōrāṇṭu taṅkiṉar. mīṇṭum 1955-56il pulpiriṭ tiṭṭattiṉ ātaravil āntirap palkalikkaḻakattiṟku varalāṉar. vantatiṉ nōkkam ituvē: vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāvil muṟiyāka ārāycci ceyya; iyaṟkik kaṭal nūl, kaṭal nila amippu nūl, kaṭal uyir nūl ākiyavaṟṟil muṟiyāka ārāycci ceyya, avaruṭiya arum paṇikaḷukkāka ṭākṭar eṉṉum viññāṉat tuṟic ciṟappup paṭṭatti, avarukku āntirap palkalikkaḻakam aḷittatu.

kaṭal ārāycciyil āntirap palkalikkaḻakam koṇṭa muṭivukaḷit taṉ veḷiyīṭukaḷilumveḷiyiṭṭatu. intiyāvilum veḷināṭukaḷilum uḷḷa viññāṉa itaḻkaḷilum avi veḷiyiṭappaṭṭaṉa. mēlum pala muṭivukaḷ veḷiyiṭappaṭuvataṟku āyattamāy uḷḷaṉa.

āntirap palkalik kaḻakattiṉ iyaṟkik kaṭal nūl kuḻuviṉar iru karuvikaḷiyum amittuḷḷaṉar. avaṟṟil oṉṟu eṭik kuṟivāṉa tarmisṭar veppamāṉi ākum. kaṭaliṉ mēṟparappu veppa niliyiyum; ataṉ kontaḷikkum aṭukkiluḷḷa nuṇṇiya veppanili māṟṟaṅkaḷiyum itik koṇṭu pativu ceyyalām.

maṟrueru karuvi kalaṅkali aṟiyum māṉi. iṉāl kaṭal nīrkaḷiṉ oḷi ūṭuruvum taṉmiyi ārāyalām.

iyaṟkik kaṭal nūl ārāycci eṉpatu palavakic cikkalkaḷukkut tīrvu kāṇak kūṭiyatu. accikkalkaḷ kaṭal nīrkaḷiṉ iyakkattōṭu toṭarpu uṭiyavi. vēṟupaṭṭa pala mātaṅkaḷil vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāviṉ upput taṉmi, veppa nili ākiyavi paṟṟic ceytikaḷ tiraṭṭappaṭṭaṉa. kāṟṟukaḷālum nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷālum kaṭaṟkari nīrkaḷil paruvanilikkēṟpa uyarvu tāḻvukaḷ iruppatāka acceytikaḷ terivittaṉa. ﻿kaṭal ali ārāyccikaḷiliruntu veḷiyāṉa muṭivukaḷ, kōtāvariyil kākkināṭā virikuṭāvi amippataṟkum, ataṟkaṭuttuḷḷa kariyil kaṭal arippit taṭuppataṟkum uriya vaḻivakikaḷik kāṇuvataṟkum utavum. kaṭal nila amippu nūl tuṟiyilum kuṟippiṭattakka ārāyccikaḷ ceyyappaṭṭu nalla muṭivukaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. kaṭaliṉ aṭiyiliruntu eṭukkappaṭum poruḷiṉ katiriyakkam, avi irukkum iṭattiṟkēṟpa māṟupaṭukiṟatu eṉṟum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu.

takunta ārāycciyiṉ vāyilākap paṭakukaḷum kappalkaḷum kaṭal uyirkaḷāl arikkappaṭuvatum taṭukkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itaṟkut taṭiyāṟṟal atikamuṭiya pala vaki maraṅkaḷum, ciṟanta vaṇṇaṅkaḷum iracāyaṉap poruḷkaḷum tēvi eṉak kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭatu.

miya ārāycci maṉṟam

miya aracōṭu toṭarpuṭiya maṉṟam oṉṟu uḷḷatu. itaṟku viññāṉa - toḻil ārāycci maṉṟam eṉṟu peyar. kaṭal nūl toṭarpāṉa pala tiṭṭaṅkaḷukku immaṉṟam ākkamum ūkkamum kāṭṭi varukiṟatu.

kaṭal ārāycciyil pēr pōṉatāka āntirap palkalik kaḻakam viḷaṅkukiṟatu. iḷiñarkaḷ palar kaṭal nūl tuṟiyiṉ pala pirivukaḷil ṭākṭar paṭṭam peṟṟuḷḷaṉar. ulakiṉ ciṟanta kaṭal ārāycci niliyaṅkaḷilum ataṟkup piṉ paṇiyāṟṟi mēlum taṅkaḷatu aṟivip perukkik koṇṭuḷḷaṉar.

maṟṟa niliyaṅkaḷ

āntirap palkalik kaḻakattit tavira, maṟṟa miyaṅkaḷ cilavum uḷḷaṉa. aviyāvaṉa : ceṉṉiyiluḷḷa vilaṅku nūl ārāyccic cālikaḷ, maṇṭapattiluḷḷa kaṭal mīṉ miya ārāycci niliyam, tiruvitāṅkūr palkalik kaḻakattiṉ kaṭal uyir nūl tuṟi, paṟaṅkip pēṭṭiyiluḷḷa kaṭal uyir nūl niliyam.

mitakkum uyirkaḷāṉa ṭiyāṭṭam mutaliyavaṟṟiṉ paravalpaṟṟiyum muṟiyāka ārāyappaṭṭuḷḷatu. ivi jaṉavari-jūṉ mātaṅkaḷil atikamākavum, jūli-ṭicampar mātaṅkaḷil kuṟivākavum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ivi mīṉkaḷukkuc ciṟanta uṇavākum. ivaṟṟiṉ aḷavip poṟuttē mīṉkaḷiṉ aḷavum amikiṟatu. ākavē, avaṟṟi ārāyvatu mika iṉṟiyamiyātatu. paṟaṅkippēṭṭiyiluḷḷa kaṭal uyir nūl niliyam kaḻimuka uyirkaḷip paṟṟi ārāyvatil atika nāṭṭam celuttiyuḷḷatu.

maṇṭapattiluḷḷa kaṭal mīṉ ārāycci niliyamum ataṉ kiḷikaḷum vāṇipa nōkkil ciṟappuṭiya mīṉkaḷi ārāynta vaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa.

karuvikaḷ

potuvāka, intiyāvil kaṭal ārāyccikkup payaṉpaṭum karuvikaḷāvaṉa:

nīr veppanili varivi

itu kaṭalil pala iṭaṅkaḷil veppanili māṟṟaṅkaḷic ceṅkuttākap pativu ceyyum.

nēṉcaṉ cīcākkaḷ

virumpiya āḻaṅkaḷil kaṭal nīri eṭuttu, ataṉ upput taṉmi, iyipu ākiyavaṟṟi ārāya ivi payaṉpaṭum.

nīrōṭṭa aḷavumāṉi

kaṭalil virumpiya iṭattil nīrōṭṭa aḷavik kaṇakkiṭa itu utavum.

mātiri eṭukkum karuvikaḷ

kaṭaliṉ aṭiyiluḷḷa paṭivukaḷi mātiri eṭuppataṟku ivi payaṉpaṭum. mātirikaḷi vēṟupaṭṭa āḻaṅkaḷil eṭukkalām.

valikaḷ

kaṭal uyirkaḷip piṭippataṟku ivi payaṉpaṭum.

mēṉāṭṭuk karuvikaḷ

mēṉāṭukaḷil puti nīrp pukippaṭap peṭṭikaḷ, nilanaṭukka veṭippi ārāyum karuvikaḷ, tolikkāṭcik karuvi amippukkaḷ mutaliyavi kaṭal ārāyccikkup payaṉpaṭukiṉṟaṉa. mika aṇmikkāla ārāyccik karuvikaḷāṉa ivi iṉi intiyak kaṭal ārāyccikkum payaṉpaṭum.

ulaka aḷavil intiyak kaṭali ārāya muṉṉēṟṟa nāṭukaḷ palavum muṉintiruppatāl, intiyak kaṭal ārāycci tiṇṇiya muṟiyil naṭipeṟum. ataṉāl nalla pala muṭivukaḷum kiṭikkum. kaṭal nūl aṟivum perukum. kaṭal nūl ariya viññāṉamāka māṟum.

﻿7. putiya kaṇṭupiṭippukaḷ

virivāka naṭipeṟṟa intiyakkaṭal ārāycciyiṉāl pala putiya kaṇṭupiṭippukaḷ nikaḻntuḷḷaṉa. avi piṉvarumāṟu.

paḷḷattākkukaḷ

paranta oru paḷḷattākku intiyak kaṭalil iruppati amerikka ārāycciyāḷarkaḷ kaṇṭu piṭittuḷḷaṉar. itu 6000 mil nīḷamum 25 mil akalamum uḷḷatu. antamāṉ kaṭalil cumatrāviṟkum parmāviṟkum iṭiyil itu kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. kaṭalil cumār 3 mil āḻattil uḷḷatu. itaṉi uyaramāṉa maliyuccikaḷ cūḻntuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟil mika uyaramāṉatu, paḷḷattākkiṟku mēl 12000 aṭi eḻumpiyuḷḷatu.

kālvāykaḷ

pala periya kālvāykaḷum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivi cēṟṟu āṟukaḷāl araṇ ceyyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟil mikap periyatu 4 mil akalamum 300 aṭi āḻamum uḷḷatu. itu vaṅkāḷa virikuṭāviṉ mēṟparappukkuk kīḻ 2 mil tolivil kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. itu kaṅkiyik kāṭṭilum 25 maṭaṅku atika nīric cumantu celkiṟatu.

malittoṭar

kaṭalaṭi olippukaḷiṉ mūlam intiyak kaṭaliṉ muḻuttariyum paṭamākkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itaṉāl,

atil kuṭivukaḷ, periya malittoṭarkaḷ, tāḻvāka amintuḷḷa camaveḷikaḷ mutaliyavi iruppatu kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. tavira, ataṉ virivāṉa mutal paṭamum uruvākkappaṭṭuḷḷatu.

kāṭṭākac cilōṉukkut teṉkiḻakkākat tariyiliruntu 14,400 aṭi uyarattiṟku erimalit toṭar oṉṟu kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. kiṟittumas tīvukaḷukkut teṟkē 219 mil tolivil erimalikaḷum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟuḷ oṉṟu 2500 mīṭṭar āḻattilum maṟṟeṉṟu 3700 mīṭṭar āḻattilum kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

vāṉili

vāṉili paṟṟi nirampac ceytikaḷ kiṭittuḷḷaṉa. teṉmēṟkup paruvakkāṟṟu, vaṭa kiḻakkup paruvakkāṟṟu, kōṭip paruvakkāṟṟu mutaliyavi ārāyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. kōṭip paruvakkāṟṟu mikka valimi vāyntatu. itu vaṭa arittiraḷip pakutiyiṉ vāṉiliyip pātikkiṟatu. mēlum, katirvīccu aḷavīṭukaḷum eṭukkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟiṉpaṭi, arapik kaṭalilum ceṅkaṭalilum nēraṭip pakalavaṉ katir vīccil 15% mutal 23% vari nīrāviyiṉālum vāyuviṉālum uṟiñcappaṭukiṟatu eṉpatu veḷiyākiyuḷḷa uṇmiyākum. mēkak kūṭṭaṅkaḷiliruntu nilākkaḷiṉ vāyilākap peṟṟa ceytikaḷik koṇṭu, muḻukkaṭalilum viḻum pakalavaṉ katirvīccik kāṇum muṟiyiṉi vāṉili aṟiñarkaḷ vakukka iyalum.

katiriyakka vīcciṉ aḷavu

1966-il naṭipeṟṟa cōviyattu ārāycciyiṉ kōkkam, ceyaṟki iyaṟkik katiriyakka vīccukkaḷi ārāyntu, avi kaṭal nīriluḷḷa tāvaraṅkaḷiyum, vilaṅkukaḷiyum evvāṟu pātikkiṉṟaṉa eṉpatik kaṇṭaṟivatākum. ivvārāycciyiṉpaṭi intiyak kaṭaliṉ katiriyakka vīcciṉ aḷavu atikam eṉpatu atircci tarakkūṭiya uṇmiyākum. iḵtu aṭlāṇṭik kaṭalil uḷḷativiṭa 3 maṭaṅku atikamākum. pacipik kaṭalila kaṭanta aṇu āyuta āyvukaḷiṉ viḷivāka viḻunta katiriyakkat taṉimaṅkaḷiṉ kaḻivukaḷ, inta uyarvukkuk kāraṇamākum. aṭlāṇṭik kaṭal, intiyak kaṭal ākiyavi mīṉvaḷam mikkavi. ivvaḷatti ikkatirvīccu atikam pātikkum.

kuṟinta aḷavu ākcijaṉ

arapikkaṭal nīril kuṟinta aḷavu ākcijaṉ iruppatu jermāṉiya ārāycciyāl veḷiyākiyuḷḷa uṇmiyākum. innili pampāyiliruntu 150 mil tolivuvari uḷḷatu. potuvākak kuṟinta aḷavu ākcijaṉ kāṇappaṭum pakutikaḷ intiyak kaṭalil uḷḷaṉa. itupōṉṟu ulakiṉ vēṟu entak kaṭalilum illi.

veppat tuḷikaḷ

ceṅkaṭalil 780 mīṭṭar āḻattil iru veppat tuḷikaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ittuḷikaḷic cūḻntuḷḷa pakuti, ataṟkaṭuttuḷḷa pakutiyiviṭa 8 maṭaṅku aḷavu uppu mikuntatāka uḷḷatu. ivi ceṅkaṭaliṉ miyattil uḷḷaṉa. ivaṟṟil vaḻakkattiṟku māṟaṉa irumpup paṭivukaḷ 6000 aṭi āḻattil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. iṅku veppanili 55.9°C. evaresṭ ucciyil veppakkāṟṟik kāṇpatu evvaḷavu viyappāka irukkumō, avvaḷavu viyappāka ikkaṇṭupiṭippu uḷḷatu. inta āḻattil vaḻakkamāka uḷḷa veppanili 4-5°C ākum. amerikka ārāycciyāḷarkaḷ ivvuṇmiyiṉi veḷippaṭuttiyuḷḷaṉar.

eṇṇey vaḷam

1974-ām āṇṭu pipravarit tiṅkaḷ pampāyk kaṭaṟkarikku appāluḷḷa kaṭaliṉ aṭiyil eṇṇey (peṭrōliyam) eṭukkppaṭṭatu. ippakutikkup pāmpē hi (Bombay High) eṉṟu peyar. 1976 mēt tiṅkaḷ 21-ām nāḷiliruntu eṇṇey uṟpatti toṭarntu naṭipeṟṟu varukiṟatu. itaṉ aḷavu āṇṭukkāṇṭu uyarnta vaṇṇam uḷḷatu. tamiḻnāṭu, kēraḷam, kujarāt, oricā mutaliya mānilaṅkaḷiṉ kaṭaṟkarip pakutikaḷil eṇṇey kiṭikka vāyppuḷḷatu. ippaṇpaṭā eṇṇeyiliruntu peṭrōl mutaliya eriporuḷkaḷ kiṭikkiṉṟaṉa.

mīḷ vaṉam

intiyakkaṭal mīṉvaḷam mikkatu. kāṭṭāka, arapik kaṭalil omaṉ, maskaṭ ākiya kaṭaṟkari vaḻiyākac ceṉṟu oru taṭavi mīṉ piṭittatil 45 nimiṭattil 3 ṭaṉ mīṉkaḷ -kiṭittaṉa: uṇavup pañcatti nīkka, ivvaḷatti naṉku payaṉpaṭutti intiyā vaḻivaki kāṇavēṇṭum. tavira, intiyak kaṭalil 80 putiyavaki vilaṅkukaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

kaṉi vaḷam

1872-ām āṇṭilēyē intiyak kaṭalil māṅkaṉis muṇṭukaḷ iruppatu kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭatu. 1962-64-il naṭanta intiyakkaṭal ārāycciyum itaṉi uṟutippaṭuttiyatu. ivi ciṟiya uruṇṭikaḷiliruntu periya tiraḷkaḷ vari uḷḷaṉa. ivi intiyāviṟkum srīlaṅkāviṟkum teṟkē 4000-5000 mīṭṭar āḻattil uḷḷaṉa; kaṭaliṉ aṭiyil āṇṭutōṟum 10 milliyaṉ ṭaṉkaḷ uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. ivaṟṟil māṅkaṉis, cempu, nikkal, mālipṭiṉam mutaliya ulōkaṅkaḷ uḷḷaṉa.

mēṟkuk kaṭaṟkariyil ūṭṭaccattu mikunta karimappaṭivukaḷum, pāspēṭ paṭivukaḷum kaṇṭu piṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivi ciṟanta uramāka amintu, vēḷāṇmit tuṟikku atikam payaṉpaṭum.

intiyak kaṭal tari ceṅkaḷimaṇṇil 220 ṭirilliyaṉ ṭaṉ alumiṉiyamum, 650 ṭirilliyaṉ ṭaṉ irumpum, 73 ṭirilliyaṉ ṭiṭṭāṉiyamum, 15 ṭirilaliyaṉ ṭiṉ, veṉāṭiyam, kōpālṭ, nikkal, cempu, kārīyamum vaṟṟātu pala āyiram āṇṭukaḷ kiṭikkum eṉa matippiṭappaṭṭuḷḷatu.

nilattiluḷḷa kōpālṭ 40 āṇṭukaḷukku maṭṭumē varum. kaṭalukkaṭiyil uḷḷatō 200 āyiram āṇṭukaḷukku varum!

tavirac ceṅkaṭaliṉ aṭiyil kaṉipporuḷ ceṟivuḷḷa tātukkaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. ivi ellā niṟaṅkaḷilum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ivaṟṟil poṉ, veḷḷi, tuttanākam, cempu irumpu, māṅkaṉis mutaliya ulōkaṅkaḷ aṭaṅkiyuḷḷaṉa. ippaṭivukaḷ 7000 aṭi āḻattil kiṭikkiṉṟaṉa.

ceṅkaṭaliluḷḷa vaṭi nilaṅkaḷil (basin) periyavi 8 mil nīḷamum, 4 mil akalamum uḷḷavi. ivaṟṟil 50 aṭi āḻattiṟku nīr uḷḷatu. innīriṉ veppanili 133°F. ceṅkaṭal nīriṉ potuvāṉa veppanili 68°F. iṅkuk kaṭal nīriṉ uppaḷavu maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷik kāṭṭilum 10 maṭaṅku atikam uḷḷatu.

ceṅkaṭalil uḷḷa kaṉiporuḷ paṭukikaḷiṉ taṭimaṉ maṭṭum 300 aṭi ākum, oru periya vaṭinilattil maṭṭum 130 milliyaṉ ṭaṉ cempu, tuttanākam, veḷḷi, taṅkam mutaliya ulōkaṅkaḷ uḷḷaṉa. mēlum, arapik kaṭaliṉ pāspēṭ aḷavu maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷik kāṭṭiluma 5 maṭaṅku atikam uḷḷatu.

kaṇṭac carivu

intiyak kaṭaliṉ tari paṟṟip pala putiya ciṟappiyalpukaḷ kaṇṭaṟiyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. avaṟṟil oṉṟu mikac ceṅkuttāṉa kaṇṭac carivu (continental slope) ākum. iḵtu ulakilēyē mika āḻamāṉatu. cilōṉ kiḻakkuk kaṭaṟkarikku appāl uḷḷatu.

aṭlas

intiyak kaṭal ārāycciyiṉāl, intiyak kaṭal 2,80,00,000 catura milkaḷ aṟiviyal tiṭpattuṭaṉ ārāyappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itaṉ oru pakutiyāka, akkaṭaliṉ vēti uyiriyal paṟṟi ōr aṭlas tayārikkappaṭṭuḷḷatu. itu kaṭaliliruntu atika uṇavupeṟap peritum utavum.

ivvāṟu, intiyak kaṭaliṉ koḻikkum pal vaḷaṅkaḷiyum payaṉpaṭutti, intiyāviṉ poruḷ vaḷattip perukkuvatē aṟivuṭimi ākum.

8. paruvakkāṟṟu ārāycci

pakalavaṉ viḷiviṉāl, paruvakkāṟṟu maḻikaḷi uṇṭākkuvatu intiyap peruṅkaṭal ākum. paruvakkāṟṟukaḷi ārāyum muyaṟciyē paruvakkāṟṟu āyvu (Monsoon Experiment) ākum . itaṉic curukkamāka mōṉaks (Monex) eṉalām.

varalāṟu

mōṉaks-79 eṉpatu uṇmiyil mutal taṭaviyāka naṭipeṟum tiṭṭamaṉṟu. kaṭanta 15 āṇṭukaḷāka, atāvatu 1964-ām āṇṭiliruntu itu toṭarpākat toṭakka nili āyvukaḷ ceyyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

1963-65-il naṭanta aṉittulakak kaṭal ārāyccip payaṇam (IIOE), 1973-il muṭinta intiya cōviyattu vāṉiyal āyvu (ISMEX), intiyak kappalkaḷum, cōviyattuk kappalkaḷum kalantu koṇṭu 1977-ām āṇṭu mē 23-mutal ākasṭ 20 vari naṭipeṟṟa ciṟu mōṉaks ākiyavi ellām taṟpoḻutu naṭipeṟṟuk koṇṭirukkum mōṉaks-79-iṉ muṉṉōṭikaḷākuma.

intiya uṟṟunōkku ārāycci niliyaṅkaḷiṉ potu iyakkunar avarkaḷ mārip paruvakkāṟṟukaḷ, kōṭip paruvakkāṟṟu ākiyavaṟṟiṉ tēciyap poruḷātārac ciṟappi uṇarntatālum, 1970-il piracalcil naṭipeṟṟa ulakat tiṭṭa mānāṭṭiṉ (Global Planning Conference) pērāḷarāka iruntatālum, ammānāṭṭil teṟku āciyap pakutikkup paruvakkāṟṟu āyvu (MONEX) oṉṟiṉi naṭatta muṉmoḻintār. piṉ, itu jeṉivāvil kaṭanta ulaka vāṉili ārāycci niliyak kūṭṭattil ēṟṟukkoḷḷappaṭṭu, iṉṟu ceyaṟpaṭukiṟatu.

nōkkaṅkaḷ

ittiṭṭattiṉ aṭippaṭi nōkkaṅkaḷ piṉvarumāṟu: -

1) nāṭṭiṉ vēṟupaṭṭa taṭpa veppanilip pakutikaḷil paruvakkāṟṟu aṭikkat toṭaṅkum nāṭkaḷit tōrāyamāka aṟital.

2) 90 nāḷ koṇṭa paruvakkāṟṟuk kālattil peṟappaṭum maḻiyiṉ muḻu aḷavi matippiṭal.

3) nāṭṭiṉ vēṟupaṭṭa pakutikaḷil 5-7 nāṭkaḷ koṇṭa kālattil atikamākavum, kuṟivākavum ēṟpaṭum maḻiyiṉi aṟital, curuṅkakkūṟiṉ paruvakkāṟṟiṉit teḷivāka aṟivatē nōkkamākum.



kāraṇikaḷ

pakalavaṉ katirvīccu, puvik katirvīccu, peruṅkaṭalkaḷiṉ iyakkam, nilam, kaṭal ākiyavaṟṟiṉ urāyvu viḷivukaḷ, malikaḷ mutaliyavi paruva maḻikkuriya kāraṇikaḷākum. itil mukkiya paṅkupeṟuvatu imālayap pakutikaḷ ākum. ippakutikaḷ illi eṉil uṇmip paruvak kāṟṟum illi. paruvakkāṟṟuc cuḻaṟciyil imālayap pakutikaḷiṉ veppa viḷivukaḷum, iyakka viḷivukaḷum ciṟanta iṭattip peṟukiṉṟaṉa. ivviru uṇmikaḷi itukāṟum ceyyappaṭṭa ārāyccikaḷ veḷippaṭuttukiṉṟaṉa.

paruvakkāṟṟu muṉṉarē uṇṭāvatu kōṭiyil tipēttiyac camaveḷi veppamaṭivatāl eṉpatum maṟṟum oru kāraṇiyākum.

paṅkupeṟum nāṭukaḷum niṟuvaṉaṅkaḷum

ittiṭṭattiṟkāka intiya aracu 25 kōṭi rūpāy otukkiyuḷḷatu. intiya vāṉili ārāycci varalāṟṟil itu periya āyvu ākum. itaṉi intiyā, uruciyā, amerikkā ākiya nāṭukaḷ naṭattukiṉṟaṉa.

ittiṭṭattil kalantukoḷḷum intiya ārāycci niṟuvaṉaṅkaḷ piṉvarumāṟu. pampāy pāpā aṇu ārāycci miyam, pampāy ṭāṭṭā aṭippaṭi ārāycci niṟuvaṉam, akamatāpāt iyaṟpiyal ārāyccik kūṭam, hitarāpāt toliyiṭa aṟiniliyam.

naṭipeṟum muṟi

cōviyattuk kappalkaḷ kaṭal uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ, vāṉili uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ mutaliyavaṟṟi naṭattum. ivi tam paṇiyit toṭaṅki uḷḷaṉa. teṉmēṟkup paruvakkāṟṟi uruvākkuvatil āṟṟalvāynta cōmali nīrōṭṭattiṟkup paṅkuṇṭu eṉṉum oru nampikkiyuḷḷatu. itaṉi ārāya ikkappalkaḷ āppirikkāviṉ kiḻakkuk kari neṭuka uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷi eṭutta vaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa.

innīrōṭṭatti ārāyntapiṉ, ikkappalkaḷ arapikkaṭal, vaṅkāḷa virikuṭā ākiya pakutikaḷukkuc ceṉṟu, aṅkuḷḷa intiyak kappalkaḷōṭu cērntu, teṟku allatu vaṭakkākap palkōṇat tōṟṟatti (Polygonal configuration) eṭukkum.”

amerikkā, taṟkāla aṟiviyal karuvikaḷ niṟinta vāṉa ūrtiyiṉi vaḻaṅkum. kāṭṭiṉ kuṟukkāka vaṭa-teṉ ticikaḷilum kiḻakku mēṟkut ticikaḷilum ivvūrti iyaṅki, vēṇṭiya vāṉoli aḷavīṭukaḷi eṭukkum.

ittiṭṭattil nāṉku intiyak kappalkaḷum paṅkukoḷḷum. ivaṟṟil kuṟippiṭattakka aḷavīṭukaḷi eṭukkap pōtiya karuvikaḷ uḷḷaṉa.

aṭukku veḷiyil uḷḷa kāṟṟukaḷ, nilavum veppanili ākiyavaṟṟiṉ aḷavīṭukaḷi mūṉṟu niliyaṅkaḷiluḷḷa ēvukaṇikaḷāl eṭukkappaṭum. avi tumpā (7° va), srīkarikōṭṭā (16° va), pālacōr (22° va) ākum. inta uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷ uruciyak kappalkaḷ eṭukkum ēvukaṇi uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷōṭu oppiṭappaṭum. intiyak kappal vāṉili mitappukaḷip pala iṭaṅkaḷil viṭṭu, mitappu uṟṟu nōkkalkaḷi eṭukkum.

kōs-10 eṉpatu amerikka niliyāṉa nila nilā ākum. itu puviyiṉ cuḻaṟciyōṭu cērntu cuṟṟuvatāl pārppataṟku niliyāka iruppatu pōl teriyum. nila naṭukkōṭṭil itu 60°, kiḻakku nīḷ kōṭṭil valam varum. intiyap peruṅkaṭal, arapikkaṭal ākiyavaṟṟiṉ pakutikaḷi itu vaṭṭamiṭum.

teriyum pakutiyil tōrāyamāka 3 - 5 kilō mīṭṭar pakuppum, akaccivappup pakutiyil 7 kilō mīṭṭar pakuppumuḷḷa mēkappaṭaṅkaḷi itu aṉuppum. ivi mīṭiyōsṭāṭ-1 eṉṉum nilāviṉāl oru nāḷikku nāṉku taṭavikaḷ pampāykku añcal ceyyappaṭum. innilā airōppiya vāṉaveḷi niliyattiṉ niliyākavuḷḷa nila nilāvākum. itaṟkākat tērnteṭukkappaṭṭuḷḷa maṟṟa niliyaṅkaḷukku ippaṭaṅkaḷ, pampāyiliruntu mīṇṭum aṉuppappaṭum. tavira, niliyāṉa aṭippaṭiyila tāmākap paṭam eṭukkum karuvikaḷ ēḻu intiyāviṭam uḷḷatu. naṉku tiṭṭamiṭappaṭṭiruppatāl, inta ārāycciyiṉāl pala naṉmikaḷ uṇṭāka nalvāyppuḷḷatu.





inta miṉṉūlip paṟṟi

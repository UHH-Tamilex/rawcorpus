﻿patippurai

⁠taṟpōtu tamiḻnāṭu pala tuṟaikaḷil maṟumalarcci aṭaintu varukiṟatu. paṟpala tuṟaikaḷil cīrtiruttam mukiḻttu varukiṟatu. aṟivilē puraṭci, vāḻkkaiyilē puraṭci ēṟpaṭṭu varukiṟatu. innilai mēṉmēl vaḷara vēṇṭum. ittakaiya puraṭcikaḷil oṉṟutāṉ iṉṟu naṭaipeṟum tamiḻt tirumaṇam. taṟkālattē pēraṟiñar aṇṇā avarkaḷiṉ ariya muyaṟciyiṉāl ittakaiya tirumaṇaṅkaḷ caṭṭappaṭi ēṟkattakkavai eṉṟa nilai ēṟ paṭṭiruppatu tamiḻarkaḷiṉ perumitattukkuriyatu.

civamuttum tamiḻar tirumaṇamum

⁠it tirumaṇam tamiḻ nāṭṭiṟkup putuvataṉṟu. paḻan tamiḻar mēṟkoṇṭu vāḻnta maṇamuṟaiyil makkaḷ maṉanilaikku ēṟpac ciṟcila māṟutalkaḷai amaittu aṟivuṭaip perumakkaḷ palar kūṭi āyntamaitta tākum. im muṟaiyil eṇṇaṟṟa tamiḻt tirumaṇaṅkaḷaic ceytu vaittut tamiḻ uṇarvu parappi vantavar nam perum pulavar mayilai civamuttu avarkaḷ. inneṟi vaḷara avar āṟṟiya tiyākaṅkaḷ aḷavilaṭaṅkātaṉa.

ēṉ cīrtiruttam vēṇṭum?

⁠maṇam eṉpataṟkuk kūṭutal eṉpatu poruḷ. aṉpāl piṇaikkappaṭṭa iruvar uḷḷaṅkaḷ oṉṟu kūṭuvataiyē maṇam eṉṟu koṇṭōm. paṇṭaik kālattil vāḻnta tamiḻ makaṉum tamiḻ makaḷum oruvarai oruvar nēriṟkaṇṭu paḻaki maṉa oṟṟumai uṭaiyavarāṉa piṉpē avartam peṟṟōrkaḷ avarkkut tirumaṇam ﻿ceytu vaippatu vaḻakkam. attirumaṇamum mikac curuṅkiya muṟaiyil cikkaṉamākavē naṭaipeṟum. aṉpiṉāl piṇaikkappaṭṭa maṇamakkaḷ iruvarum illaṟameṉṉum nallaṟam ēṟṟu vāḻkkai toṭaṅkukiṟārkaḷ eṉpatai aṟivikkavē maṇac caṭaṅkukaḷ naṭaipeṟṟaṉa

⁠cāṉṟōrkaḷ vakutta ammuṟaikaḷaip piṉ paṟṟip pērāciriyar mayilai civamuttu avarkaḷ āyirak kaṇakkāṉa tirumaṇaṅkaḷai naṭatti nalvaḻi kāṭṭiyuḷḷār. am muṟaikaḷai aṉaivarum aṟintu piṟar utaviyiṉṟit tāṅkaḷē tirumaṇaṅkaḷai naṭattat tuṇaiyāka irukka vēṇṭum eṉṟa nōkkattōṭu innūlai veḷiyiṭa virumpiṉōm.

⁠innūlaic cemmaiyāṉa muṟaiyil acciṭap pērutaviyāka irunta pulavarkaḷ tiru. ta. irāmaliṅkam, taṇikai ulakanātaṉ ākiyōrukku maṉṟattiṉ naṉṟi eṉṟum uriyatu.

﻿pērāciriyar mayilai civamuttu avarkaḷ nikaḻttiya tamiḻt tirumaṇamuṟai



tamiḻt tirumaṇam kaṭavuṭ koḷkaiyōṭu kūṭiyatu. maṇam ceytukoḷḷuvōr piṟkāla vāḻkkaiyilē tammuṭaiya kaṭamaiyai uṇarntu nalvaḻiyilē naṭattal vēṇṭum eṉpatē ittirumaṇattiṉ mutal nōkkam. ātalāl tirumaṇattil ceyyum caṭaṅkukaḷiṉ poruḷaic ceyvippōrum ceytukoḷvōrum uṭaṉiruppōrum purintukoḷḷattakka muṟaiyilē intat tamiḻt tirumaṇam amaikkap peṟṟuḷḷatu. eṉavē it tirumaṇattai naṭatti vaippōr aṅkuk kuḻumiyuḷḷa makkaḷukkum maṇam ceytukoḷḷum maṇamakkaḷukkum avvappōtu viḷakkik kūṟutal vēṇṭum. aṅṅaṉam kūṟattakkavaikaḷuḷ kīḻ varupavai iṉṟiyamaiyātaṉavākum.

1. maṇamakaṉ vīṭṭār, maṇamakaḷ vīṭṭār aḻaip pitaḻkaḷait tirumaṇa maṉṟattil kūṭiyuḷḷa periyōrkaḷukkup paṭittuk kāṭṭutal.

2. varavēṟpavar, maṇamakkaḷiṉ peṟṟōr mutaliyavarkaḷiṉ peyarkaḷaip paṭittuk kāṭṭik kūṭumāṉāl avarkaḷaiyum aṟimukappaṭuttutal nalam.

8. maṇamakaṉ, maṇamakaḷ ākiya iruvaraiyum āṅkuk kuḻumiyuḷḷavarukku aṟimukappaṭuttutal.

4. maṇamakkaḷiṉ eṇṇam aṟintukoḷḷum vakaiyil intat tamiḻt tirumaṇattaik kuṟittum tirumaṇam ceytukoḷḷa vēṇṭiya kaṭamaiyaik kuṟittum, maṇamakkaḷukkuc curukkamāka viḷakkik kūṟutal.

tirumaṇam ceytukoḷvōrkku avaravar paḻakka vaḻakkaṅkaḷukku ēṟṟavakaiyil intat tirumaṇattai naṭattutal nalam.



﻿ tamiḻt tirumaṇam nikaḻttum muṟai

avaravar paḻakka vaḻakkaṅkaḷukku ēṟpa, maṇappantalai malar mālaikaḷālum palvēṟu viḷakkukaḷālum aḻaku paṭuttumāṟu ceytal vēṇṭum. kuttuviḷakku, kuṭaviḷakku, kiḷaiviḷakku, kaiviḷakku mutaliyavaikaḷuḷ oṉṟiṉai avaravarkaṭku iyainta vakaiyil ēṟṟivaikkalām. cilar varṇam tīṭṭiya maṭkalaṅkaḷ, mara ural mutaliyavaikaḷai maṇappantalil vaippiṉum āciriyar avaikaḷai ēṟṟuk koḷḷalām. marapukku māṟupaṭāta vakaiyil kuṟaintatu iraṇṭu kuttuviḷakkukaḷēṉum iruttal vēṇṭum.

maṇappantalil maṇamakkaḷ kiḻakku nōkkiyum maṇam ceytu vaikkum āciriyar vaṭakku nōkkiyum amarntiruttal nam nāṭṭu makkaḷiṉ vaḻakkamāka iruttaliṉ itaṉēyē aṉaivarum mēṟkoḷḷalām.

oru talaivāḻaiyilaiyai maṇamakkaḷukku muṉpuṟamāka (atāvatu kiḻakkutticaiyil) vaittu, avvāḻai ilaiyil mūṉṟu piṭi paccarici parappi vaittu ataṉ mītu vāḻaip pū vaṭivilamainta oru cempiṉai mañcaḷ kuṅkumaṅkaḷāl alaṅkarittu vaittal vēṇṭum. piṉpu accempil tūymaiyāṉa nīrvārttu māvilaik kottiṉaip porutti ataṉmītu muṟṟiya tēṅkāy oṉṟiṉai mañcaḷ pūcik kuṅkumam iṭṭu vaittal vēṇṭum. ac cempiṉukkup pakkattil araitta mañcaḷaip piṭittu vaittu ataik kuṅkumattāl alaṅkarittal vēṇṭum. cempiṉukku maṟṟāeru pakkattil orutaṭṭil mañcaḷ kalanta paccariciyaip parappi ataṉ mītu muṟṟiya tēṅkāy oṉṟiṉai vaittu ataṉaiyum mañcaḷ kuṅkumaṅkaḷāl alaṅkarittup poṟṟāviyōṭu kūṭiya mañcaḷ kayiṟṟai atil cuṟṟi vaittal vēṇṭum. maṇamakkaḷukku muṉṉar iraṇṭu mukkālikaḷ iṭṭu maṇamakaṉukkākavum maṇamakaḷukkākavum peṟṟāērkaḷ paricākat tarum puttāṭaikaḷum malar mālaikaḷum nirampiya taṭṭukaḷai vaittal vēṇṭum.



iṟai vaṇakkam muṭintapiṉṉar maṇappantalil kuḻumiyuḷḷa aṉparkaḷiṭam apputtāṭaikaḷai vāḻttit tarumāṟu kēṭṭu avarkaḷ vāḻttiṉaip peṟṟu maṇamakkaṭku apputtāṭaikaḷai aḷittal vēṇṭum.

puttāṭaiyuṭaṉ maṇamakaṉum maṇamakaḷum maṇap pantalukku vantatum avaravar marapukkut takkapaṭi iṟaivaḻi pāṭṭiṉai muṭittuk koṇṭu maṇamakkaḷ maṇamēṭaiyil vantu amara vēṇṭum.

maṇamakaṉ maṇamakaḷukku maṅkala nāṇ aṇiyum varaiyil maṇamakaḷ maṇamakaṉukku valappakkattil iruttal marapu eṉpataṉai oṭṭi avvāṟu irukkac ceyyalām. im maṅkala nikaḻcci muṭintatum maṇamakaḷai maṇamakaṉukku iṭap pakkattil amarac ceyyalām.

mañcaḷāl piṭittu vaikkappaṭṭuḷḷatai aruva meṉṟum uruva meṉṟum colla iyalāta kantaḻik kaṭavuḷ vaṭivameṉṟum, nīr nirampiya cempiṉmēl tēṅkāyōṭu viḷaṅkum vaṭivattiṉai iṟaivaṉ ammai appaṉāka irukkum vaṭivameṉṟum, kuṭaviḷakku, kiḷaiviḷakku mutaliya vaikaḷ iṟaivaṉ oḷivaṭivamāka viḷaṅkum nilaiyiṉaik kuṟippaṉa eṉṟum maṟaimalai aṭikaḷār terivittuḷḷa karuttiṉpaṭi maṇamakkaḷ im mūṉṟu vakaiyālum iṟaivaṉē vaṇaṅkuvārkaḷ.

camittukaḷ koṇṭu ceyyum eriyōmputalum iv vaḻipāṭukaḷuḷ talaiciṟanta oṉṟāka viḷaṅkutal vēṇṭum.

puttāṭai aṇintu vanta maṇamakkaḷ mutalil peṟṟāēr vaḻipāṭu ceytu avartam nalvāḻttiṉaip peṟṟu eriyōmpum ceyalāl oḷivaṭivil kaṭavuḷē vaṇaṅkuvar. centīyōmputalāl kaṭavuḷai vaḻipaṭumpōtu tēvāra, tiruvācakaṅkaḷ, tamiḻ maṟaiyākiya tirukkuṟaḷ ivaikaḷuḷ avaravar viruppattiṟku ēṟṟa vaṇṇam takutivāynta pāṭalkaḷait tirumaṇam nikaḻttum āciriyar uḷaṅkaṉintu pāṭutal vēṇṭum. (aṅṅaṉam pāṭutaṟkuriya pāṭalkaḷ innūlil āṅkāṅkē tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. vaiṇava camayattavar tirumaṇattiṟkupporuttamāṉa pāṭalkaḷ piṟcērkkaiyākat tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.) ivvaḻipāṭu muṭintatum kuḻumiyuḷḷa periyōrkaḷāl vāḻttit tarappeṟṟa maṅkalanāṇai maṇamakaṉ maṇamakaḷukku aṇital vēṇṭum. appoḻutu iṉṉicai muḻakkamum "maṉamakkaḷ vāḻka! maṇamakkaḷ vāḻka!” eṉa aṉparkaḷiṉ vāḻttalum, mañcaḷ kalanta malarum tūvutalum nikaḻum.

piṉṉar maṇamakkaḷ mālaimāṟṟik koḷḷavēṇṭum. oruvarukkoruvar tam mālaikaḷai mūṉṟu muṟai māṟṟik koḷvatāl maṇamakkaḷiṉ maṉa orumaippāṭṭiṉai makkaḷ aṟintu koḷkiṉṟaṉar. mālai māṟṟutal muṭintapiṉṉar maṇamakaṉ taṉ vāḻkkait tuṇaiviyiṉ kaiyaippiṭittuk koṇṭu maṇappantalaic cuṟṟivarutal vēṇṭum. mālai māṟṟutalum maṇamakaḷaik kaippiṭittu valam varutalum tamiḻttirumaṇattiṟku iṉṟiyamaiyātaṉavāka uḷḷaṉa. aṉṟiyum maṇamakkaḷiṉ uṭaṉ pāṭṭiṉai aṅkuk kuḻumiyuḷḷōr aṟintu koḷvataṟkum ciṟanta cāṉṟāka viḷaṅkukiṉṟaṉa. ic ceyalkaḷōṭu tirumaṇam muṭivu peṟukiṉṟatu. piṉṉart tirumaṇam ceytu vaitta āciriyarum āṉṟōrkaḷum cāṉṟōrkaḷum maṇamakkaḷukku ilvāḻkkaiyiṉ ciṟappiṉai eṭuttuk kūṟi vāḻttuvar.

kuṟippu

kāppukkaṭṭutalum kaḷaitalum

tamiḻt tirumaṇam mikavum cikkaṉa muṟaiyil orē vēḷaiyil nikaḻumāṟu amaittiruppatāl, kāppuk kaṭṭal kāppukkaḷaital, aracāṇikkāl naṭukal mutaliyaṉa tēvai illai. ivaikaḷaiyum maṇa nikaḻcciyil cērttuc ceyya vēṇṭumeṉṟu kēṭṭuk koḷpavarkaḷukkup piṉvarumāṟu ceytal vēṇṭum.





kāppuk kaṭṭutal: oru taṭṭilē mañcaḷ kalanta ariciyaip parappivaittu ataṉ mītu mañcaḷ kuṅku maṅkaḷāl alaṅkarikkappaṭṭa tēṅkāyai vaittu antat taṭṭiṉai maṇamakaṉukku muṉṉāl vaikkappaṭṭirukkum mukkāliyiṉ mēl vaittal vēṇṭum. maṇamakaṉ valakkaiyiṉai antat tēṅkāyiṉmītu vaittapaṭi irukka muḻumañcaḷ kaṭṭappeṟṟa nūlkayiṟṟiṉai maṇamakaṉ kaiyil kaṭṭutal vēṇṭum. ataṉ piṉṉar maṇamakaḷ taṉ iṭakkaiyai antat tēṅkāyiṉmēl vaikka maṇamakaṉ mañcaḷ kaṭṭiya nūliṉai maṇamakaḷ kaiyil kaṭṭutal vēṇṭum. atēcamayattil avarkaḷukku etirppuṟattil naṭappeṟṟuḷḷa araca marakkikaḷaikkum mañcaḷ nūlkayiṟu oṉṟu kaṭṭutal vēṇṭum.

kāppukkaḷaital

tirumaṇam muṭintapiṉṉark kāppukkaṭṭiya atē muṟaiyil maṇamakaṉ mañcaḷ pūciya tēṅkāyiṉ mēl kaiyaivaikkac ceytu muṉ pukaṭṭiya mañcaḷ nūl kayiṟṟiṉai aviḻttiṭac ceytal vēṇṭum anta nērattilēyē aracaṅkiḷaiyil kaṭṭiya mañcaḷ kayiṟṟiṉaiyum aviḻttu viṭutal vēṇṭum. innikaḻccikaḷiṉpōtu tummal, irumal mutaliyaṉa kēḷāmal iruttaṟ poruṭṭu mēḷam koṭṭutal vaḻakkam.

pantaṟkāl naṭutal

tamiḻt tirumaṇam ceytukoḷpavarkaḷ intac caṭaṅkaic ceyvataṟku āciriyar utaviyai nāṭavēṇṭiyatillai maṅkala makaḷir aivar oṉṟu kūṭip pantaṟkālkaḷuḷ vaṭa kiḻakku mūlaiyil naṭavēṇṭiya oṉṟukku mañcaḷ pūci, kuṅkumam iṭṭu malar cūṭi mēḷatāḷa muḻakkattuṭaṉ taṅkaḷ kaikaḷāl antak kampattaik kuḻiyil naṭalām. āciriyarum uṭaṉiruntu nikaḻtta vēṇṭumeṉṟu virumpiṉāl āciriyaraiyum vaittuk koḷḷalām. āciriyar iṟaivaḻipāṭu ceytu mañcaḷ kalanta ariciyaip parappiya talai vāḻaiyilaiyiṉmītu kalacam niṟutti, antak kalacattiṟku vaḻipāṭāṟṟik kalacattiluḷḷa nīriṉaik koṇṭu pantaṟ kāliṉaik kaḻuvumpaṭi ceyvar. inta nikaḻcciyai avaravar paḻakka vaḻakkaṅkaḷukku ēṟpap periyōr karuttaṟintu ceytal nalam.

uṟuti ceytal

itu tirumaṇam nikaḻvataṟku muṉṉar maṇamakaḷ vīṭṭil nikaḻum nikaḻcciyākum. maṇamakaṉukku uriyōrum maṇamakaḷukku uriyōrum oru nalla nāḷil uṟṟār uṟaviṉar muṉṉilaiyil oruvarukkoruvar uṟuti kūṟik koḷvatu marapu. peṇvīṭṭār tam peṇṇaik kuṟitta oru maṇamakaṉukkut taruvatākavum avvāṟē ammaṇamakaṉ vīṭṭār maṇamakaṉukku vāḻkkait tuṇaiviyākak kuṟitta oru peṇṇai ēṟṟuk koḷvatākavum oruvarkkoruvar eḻuttu mūlamākavum vāymoḻi mūlamākavum āciriyar muṉṉilaiyil cuṟṟattārkaḷ naṇparkaḷ cāṉṟāka viḷaṅka uṟuti ceytu koḷḷavēṇṭum.

tamiḻt tirumaṇa nikaḻccikaḷum poruttamāṉa mantirap pāṭalkaḷum

1. iṟaivaḻipāṭu

mañcaḷ piḷḷaiyār piṭittuvaittu viḷakkēṟṟi vaittal. āciriyar vaṭakku nōkki amarntu maṇamakaṉai aḻaittuk kiḻakku nōkki amarac ceytal. āciriyar piṉvarum tiruppāṭalaip pāṭikkoṇṭu mañcaḷ kalanta arici, utirimalar ākiyavaṟṟai maṇamakaṉkaiyil koṭuttup piḷḷai yārai vaḻipaṭac ceytal.

piṭiyataṉ uruvumai koḷa miku kariyatu

vaṭikoṭu taṉataṭi vaḻipaṭu mavariṭar

kaṭikaṇa pativara aruḷiṉaṉ mikukoṭai

vaṭiviṉar payilvali valamuṟai iṟaiyē.





2 kālnaṭutal

aracaṅkiḷai allatu ālaṅkiḷai oṉṟiṉai maṅkala aṇi aṇinta makaḷir mūvaruṭaṉum maṇamakkaḷuṭaṉum naṭutal. āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalai ōtikkoṇṭu kālkaḷukku. mañcaḷ, kuṅkumam iṭṭut tuṇi kaṭṭac ceytal.

māvēlai ālamatai aṭakkit taṉṉuḷ

maṇṇulakam aṇṭamelām vaḷarntutāṉōr

kāvēyiṉ muṉṉutitta araciṟ ṟōṉṟik

kaṭampupuṉai kuruntiṉukkut tuṇaiya tākit

tūvētan talaikāṇṭaṟku ariya tākit

tuṉpamuṟu piṟaviyeṉun tukaḷcēr veyyil

āvēṉait taṉṉaṭiyām niḻaliṟ cērtta

attitaṉaip patti ceytu mutti cērvām.





3. kumpa pūcai

vāḻaiyilaiyil ariciyiṭṭu iraṇṭu kumpaṅkaḷil taṇṇīr niṟaittup periya kumpattai valappakkattilum ciṟiya kumpattai iṭappakkattilum vaittuk kumpaṅkaḷai iṟaivaṉ iṟaiviyāka niṉaittut tūpa tīpaṅkāṭṭi āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalkaḷai ōtikkoṇṭu maṇamakkaḷai vaḻipāṭu āṟṟumpaṭi ceytal :

iṟai vaḻipāṭu

valappakkak kumpavaḻipāṭu

aṟuvakaic camayat tōrkkum avvavar poruḷāṅ vēṟām

kuṟiyatu uṭaittāy vētā kamaṅkaḷiṉ kuṟiyiṟantaṅku

aṟiviṉil aruḷāl maṉṉi ammaiyōṭu appaṉākic

ceṟivoḻi yātu niṉṟa civaṉaṭi ceṉṉi vaippām.



ulake lām uṇarn tōtaṟ kariyavaṉ

nilavu lāviya nīrmali vēṇiyaṉ

alakil cōtiyaṉ ampalat tāṭuvāṉ

malarci lampaṭi vāḻtti vaṇaṅkuvām.





iṟaivi vaḻipāṭu

iṭappakkak kumpavaḻipāṭu

taiyalnal lāḷait tavattiṉ talaiviyai

maiyalai nōkkum maṉōṉmaṇi maṅkaiyaip

paiyaniṉ ṟēttip paṇimiṉ paṇintapiṉ

veyya pavamiṉi mēva kilāvē.





4.maṇamakkaḷukku āṭai vaḻaṅkutal

maṇamakaṉukku āṭai aḷittal

oru taṭṭil puttāṭai, pū, veṟṟilaipākku ivaikaḷai vaittu āciriyar piṉvarum tiruppāṭalai ōtikkoṇṭu maṇamakaṉukkut tarutal.



﻿ pōṉṉu meypporu, ḷuntaru vāṉaip

⁠pōka mumtiru vumpuṇarp pāṉaip,

piṉṉai eṉpiḻai yaippoṟup pāṉaip,

⁠piḻaiye lāmtavi rappaṇip pāṉai,

iṉṉa taṉmaiyaṉ eṉṟaṟi voṇṇā

⁠emmā ṉaieḷi vanta pirāṉai,

aṉṉam vaikum vayalpaḻa ṉattaṇi

⁠ārū rāṉai maṟakkalu māmē.





maṇamakaḷukku āṭai vaḻaṅkutal

āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalai ōtikkoṇṭu maṇamakaḷukku āṭaiyai aḷittal.

tammai yēpukaḻn ticcai pēciṉum

⁠cārpiṉum toṇṭar tarukilā

poymmai yāḷaraip pāṭātē entai

⁠pukalūr pāṭumiṉ pulavīrkāḷ,

immai yētarum cōṟum kūṟaiyum

⁠ētta lām iṭar keṭalumām,

ammai yēciva lōka māḷvataṟku

⁠yātum aiyuṟa villaiyē.





5. kāppu aṇital

orutaṭṭil paccarici, veṟṟilai pākku, mañcaḷ pūciya tēṅkāy, paḻam, mañcaḷ kōkkappaṭṭa iraṇṭu mañcaḷ nāṇ ākiyavaṟṟai vaittal.

tirumaṇam taṭaiyiṉṟi iṉitu naṭaipeṟum poruṭṭut tiruvaruṭkāppāka in nāṇaik kaiyil kaṭṭukiṉṟēṉ eṉṟu āciriyar collik koṇṭu piṉvarum pāṭalai ōti maṇamakaṉ valakkaiyil kaṭṭutal.



﻿

maṇamakaṉ valakkaiyil kaṭṭal

ōrvaru kaṇka ḷiṇaikkayalē umaiyavaḷ kaṇkaḷ iṇaikkayalē

ērmaru vuṅkaḻa ṉākamatē eḻilkoḷu tācaṉa ṉākamatē

nīrvaru kontaḷa kaṅkaiyatē neṭuñcaṭai mēviya kaṅkaiyatē

cērvaru yōkati yampakaṉē cirapura mēyati yampakaṉē.





āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalai ōta maṇamakaḷukku iṭak kaiyil maṇamakaṉ kāppukkaṭṭutal.

aintu karattaṉai yāṉai mukattaṉai

intiṉ iḷampiṟai pōlum eyiṟṟaṉai

tanti makaṉṟaṉē ñāṉak koḻuntiṉaip

puntiyil vaittaṭi pōṟṟu kiṉṟēṉē.





6. peṟṟāēr vaḻipāṭu



maṇamakaṉ taṉ peṟṟāēriṉ tiruvaṭiyait tūya nīrāl viḷakkip poṭṭiṭṭu malar tūvip piṉvarum tiruppāṭṭai ōtik koṇṭu vaḻipaṭavēṇṭum.

appaṉnī ammainī aiya ṉumnī

aṉpuṭaiya māmaṉum māmi yumnī

oppuṭaiya mātarum oṇporu ḷumnī

orukulamum cuṟṟamum ōrū rumnī

tuyppaṉavum uyppaṉavum tōṟṟu vāynī

tuṇaiyāyeṉ neñcam turappip pāynī

ippoṉnī immaṇinī immut tumnī

iṟaivaṉ nī ēṟūrnta celvaṉ nīyē.





maṇa makaḷ taṉ peṟṟōriṉ tiruvaṭiyai nīrāl viḷakki malar tūvip piṉvarum tiruppāṭalai ōtikkoṇṭu vaḻipaṭutal.



ammaiyē appā oppilā maṇiyē

aṉpiṉil viḷainta āramutē,

poymmaiyē perukkip poḻutiṉaic curukkum

puḻuttalaip pulaiyaṉēṉ taṉakkuc

cemmaiyē āya civapatam aḷitta

celvamē! civaperu māṉē!

immaiyē uṉṉaic cikkeṉap piṭittēṉ

eṅkeḻun taruḷuvatu iṉiyē.

﻿ 9. maṅkala nāṇ vaḻipāṭu

poṉ tāliyil mañcaḷ pūcik kuṅkumam iṭṭu irumuḻa nīḷamuḷḷa mañcaḷ pūciya nāṇil ap poṉ tāliyaik kōttu iru puṟamum muṭiyiṭṭut taṭṭil vaittu, pūveṟṟilai pākku tēṅkāy paḻam mañcaḷ ivaṟṟaiyum vaittuk kuḻumi yuḷḷa cāṉṟōr vāḻttukkūṟik kaikaḷāl toṭum vaṇṇam kāṇpittu varutal vēṇṭum.

maṇamakaṉ maṇamakaḷukku vaṭakkup pakkattil amartal. maṇamakaḷ valappakkamum maṇamakaṉ iṭap pakkamum amartal vēṇṭum. appoḻutu āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalai ōta. maṅkala nāṇ vaḻipāṭu naṭaipeṟum.

maṉṟilmaṇi viḷakku eṉalām maruvumuka nakaipōṟṟi

oṉṟiyamaṅ kalanāṇiṉ oḷipōṟṟi ulakumpar

ceṉṟutoḻa aruḷ curakkum civakāma cuntaritaṉ

niṉṟatiru nilai pōṟṟi nilavutiru vaṭipōṟṟi.





10.maṇamakkaḷ uṟutimoḻi

maṇamakaṉ uṟutimoḻi kūṟutal

perumatippiṟkuriya periyōrkaḷē ! pēraṉpukeḻumiya uṟaviṉarkaḷē ! uṅkaḷ aṉaivarkkum aṉpu kaṉinta vaṇakkam. vāḻvilōr tirunāḷākiya innaṉṉāḷil uṅkaḷ muṉṉilaiyil ippeṇṇiṉ nallāḷai eṉ vāḻkkait tuṇaiviyāka ēṟṟukkoḷḷa maṭṭaṟṟa makiḻcciyuṭaṉ icaikiṉṟēṉ. aṉpil malarkiṉṟa ilvāḻkkaiyil iṉpattilum tuṉpattilum iṇaipiriyātu vāṉpukaḻ vaḷḷuvar vakutta teḷḷu tamiḻ neṟiyil niṉṟu vāḻvēṉ eṉṟu uṟuti kūṟukiṉṟēṉ. eṉ tuṇaivikku illaṟa vāḻvil aḷikka vēṇṭiya urimaikaḷai aḷittu aṉpuṭaṉ vāḻvēṉ. ‘kātalar iruvar karuttu orumittu ātaravu paṭṭatē iṉpam’ eṉṟa āṉṟōr moḻiyai niṉaiviṟ koṇṭu eṉ vāḻkkait tuṇaiviyuṭaṉ oṟṟumai﻿yōṭu vāḻvēṉ eṉpatu uṟuti. eṅkaḷ illaṟa vāḻvu peṟṟāērum periyōrum pukaḻum vakaiyil iṉitu naṭaipeṟa vēṇṭumeṉṟu eṅkum niṟainta iṟaivaṉai iṟaiñcukiṉṟēṉ.

maṇamakaḷ uṟutimoḻi kūṟutal

iṅkuk kuḻumiyuḷḷa periyōrkaḷ aṉaivarkkum vaṇakkam. uṅkaḷ muṉṉilaiyil in nampiyai eṉ vāḻkkait tuṇaivarāka ēṟṟukkoḷḷap perumakiḻcciyuṭaṉ icaikiṉṟēṉ. eṉ kaṇavar karuttaṟintu oḻuki ilvāḻkkaikku uṟṟa tuṇaiyākat tikaḻvēṉ. iṉpattilum iṉṉalilum iṇaipiriyātu eṉ kaṇavarai matittu avar iṉpamē eṉ iṉpam; avar nalamē eṉ nalam eṉakkaruti vāḻvēṉ eṉṟu uṟuti kūṟukiṉṟēṉ. tamiḻ maṟaiyām tirukkuṟaḷ kūṟum illaṟa neṟiyil eṉ kaṇavaruṭaṉ aṉpuṭaṉ iṇaintu vāḻvēṉ eṉpatu uṟuti. eṅkaḷ maṉaiyaṟa vāḻkkai makiḻcci poṅkac ciṟappuṭaṉ viḷaṅka aruḷpuriyumāṟu ellām valla iṟaivaṉai vēṇṭukiṉṟēṉ.





11. maṅkala nāṇ aṇital

1. ⁠maṇamakaṉiṉ uṭaṉ piṟantāḷ oru taṭṭil tiruviḷakkēṟṟi ataṉai maṇamakaḷiṉ piṉpuṟam ēnti niṟṟal vēṇṭum.

2. ⁠āciriyar maṅkala nāṇai maṇamakaṉ kaiyil koṭuttal.

3.⁠ maṇamakaṉ taṉ valakkai maṇamakaḷiṉ kaḻuttiṟkup piṉpuṟamāka avaṉatu valappakkam varumpaṭivaḷattu, maṟṟāerukai iṭappuṟattil irukka, maṅkala nāṇiṉ iru puṟaṅkaḷaiyum paṟṟit tāliyiṉ mukappiṉ muṉpuṟam mārpiṉ naṭuvē viḷaṅka, kaḻuttil kaṭṭi mūṉṟu muṭiyiṭutal vēṇṭum. piṉ kaiyeṭāmal tirunīṟu, kuṅkumam cantaṉam, malar ivaṟṟai muṭiyil vaittal vēṇṭum.

maṇamakaṉ maṇamakaḷukku maṅkala nāṇ aṇiyum poḻutu āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalkaḷai ōtutal vēṇṭum.

1.⁠maṇṇiṉalla vaṇṇam vāḻalām vaikalum

eṇṇiṉ nallakatikku yātumōr kuṟaivilē

kaṇṇiṉ nallaḵtuṟum kaḻumala vaḷanakarp

peṇṇiṉ nallāḷoṭum peruntakai yiruntatē.



2.⁠naṉṟuṭai yāṉai tīyatil lāṉai naraiveḷḷēṟu

oṉṟuṭai yāṉai umaiyoru pākam uṭaiyāṉai

ceṉṟaṭaiyāta tiruvuṭai yāṉai cirāp paḷḷik

kuṉṟuṭai yāṉaik kūṟaveṉ uḷḷam kuḷirummē.



3.⁠muṉṉam avaṉuṭaiya nāmam kēṭṭāḷ

mūrtti avaṉ irukkum vaṇṇam kēṭṭāḷ

piṉṉai avaṉuṭaiya ārūr kēṭṭāḷ

peyarttum avaṉukkē picci āṉāḷ

aṉṉaiyaiyum attaṉaiyum aṉṟē nīttāḷ

akaṉṟāḷ akaliṭattār ācā rattai

taṉṉai maṟantāḷ taṉṉāmam keṭṭāḷ

talaippaṭṭāḷ naṅkai talaivaṉ tāḷē.





12. poṭṭu iṭutal

maṇamakaḷukku maṇamakaṉ poṭṭu iṭutal. appōtu, āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalai ōtuvār.

kalavañcēr kaḻikkāṉal katir muttam kalantu eṅkum

kurumpaimulai malarkkuḻali koṇṭatavaṅ kaṇṭu

maṇamakaḷ maṇamakaṉiṉ iṭappakkattil māṟi amartal vēṇṭum. maṇamakkaḷiṉ ammāṉmārkaḷ maṇamakkaḷiṉ neṟṟiyil paṭṭam kaṭṭutal. māmippaṭṭam, nāttippaṭṭam, kaṭṭutalum uṇṭu. appoḻutu āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalaip pāṭuvār.

ivvāṟu mūṉṟu muṟai valam vantu poriyiṭutal.appoḻutu piṉvarum pāṭalkaḷai ōtutal vēṇṭum.

akaṉamarnta aṉpiṉarāy aṟupakaiceṟṟu

vaḷḷuvar kaṇṭa kātalar





1.⁠ aṇaṅkukol āymayil kollō kaṉaṅkuḻai

mātarkol mālumeṉ neñcu.





⁠ivaḷ ic cōlaiyil vāḻum teyvamakaḷō, ciṟanta mayilō, allatu makkaḷ iṉattaic cērnta peṇtāṉō; ivaḷ iṉṉa vakaiyaic cārntavaḷ eṉat tuṇintu colla muṭiyāmal eṉ maṉam mayaṅkukiṉṟatu.

2.⁠ irunōk kivaḷuṇkaṇ uḷḷa torunōkku

nōynōkkoṉ ṟannōy maruntu.





⁠ivaḷuṭaiya maitīṭṭiya kaṇkaḷukku iraṇṭu vakaiyāṉa pārvai irukkiṟatu. avaṟṟuḷ oru pārvai nōyai uṇṭākkuvatu, maṟṟāeru pārvai annōykku maruntu.

3.⁠ ētilār pōlap potunōkku nōkkutal

kātalār kaṇṇē uḷa.





⁠puṟattē, ayalārpōl aṉpillāta potu nōkkāl nōkkutal akattē kātal koṇṭavariṭam uḷḷa ōr iyalpu ākum.

4.⁠kaṇṇoṭu kaṇiṇai nōkkokkiṉ vāyc

eṉṉa payaṉum ila.





⁠kātaloṭu kūṭiya iruvar kaṇkaḷuḷ oruvar kaṇkaḷ maṟṟāeruvaratu kaṇkaḷai nōkkumēyāṉal avarkaḷ collak kūṭiya vāyc coṟkaḷāl oru ciṟitum payaṉ illai.

5.⁠kaṇṭukēṭ ṭuṇṭuyirt tuṟṟaṟiyum aimpulaṉum

oṇtoṭi kaṇṇē uḷa.





⁠kaṇṇāl kaṇṭum, kātāl kēṭṭum, nāvāl cuvaittum mūkkāl mōntum, uṭampāl toṭṭum aṉupavikkappaṭum aimpula iṉpaṅkaḷum oḷi poruntiya vaḷaiyalaṇinta ivaḷiṭattē uḷḷaṉa.



6.⁠ tammil iruntu tamatupāttu uṇṭaṟṟāl

ammā arivai muyakku.





⁠māniṟamuṭaiya ivvaḻakiya maṅkaiyiṉatu cērkkai tam vīṭṭiliruntu tammuṭaiya muyaṟciyāl vanta poruḷait tamakku uriyavar yāvarukkum pakirntukoṭuttut tāmum tamakkuriya pākattai aṉupavippataip pōṉṟa iṉpattait tarukiṟatu.

7.⁠ aṟitōṟum aṟiyāmai kaṇṭaṟṟāl kāmam

ceṟitōṟum cēyiḻai māṭṭu.





⁠nūlkaḷai ūṉṟip paṭikkuntōṟum muṉpu irunta aṟiyāmaiyai uṇarntukoḷvatu pōṉṟu ciṟanta āparaṇaṅkaḷai aṇinta ivaḷiṭam cēruntōṟum ivaḷ pāl uḷḷa kātal putumaiyāy mēṉmēlum mikuntu iṉpat tait tarukiṟatu.

8.⁠ naṉṉīrai vāḻi aṉiccamē niṉṉiṉum

meṉnīraḷ yāmvīḻ pavaḷ.





⁠aṉiccappūvē, nī ellā malarkaḷiṉum meṉmaiyāṉa taṉmaiyaip peṟṟirukkiṉṟāy. ātaliṉ nī vāḻvāyāka. āṉāl eṉṉāl virumpappaṭukiṉṟavaḷ uṉṉaikkāṭṭilum meṉmaiyāṉa taṉmaiyuṭaiyavaḷ.

9.⁠ muṟimēṉi muttam muṟuval veṟināṟṟam

vēluṇkaṇ vēyttō ḷavaṭku.





⁠mūṅkilpōlum tōḷiṉaiyuṭaiya ivaḷukku uṭal taḷir pōṉṟa taṉmai vāyntatu. pal muttuppōṉṟatu, maṇam iyaṟkaiyāka amainta nalla maṇam. mai tīṭṭap peṟṟa avaḷ kaṇkaḷ vēlpōṉṟu irukkum.

10.⁠ aṉiccamum aṉṉattiṉ tūviyum mātar

aṭikku neruñcip paḻam.





⁠aṉiccamalarum aṉṉap paṟavaiyiṉ iṟakum ākiya ivaikaḷ ivaḷ pātattiṟku (muḷḷōṭu kūṭiya) neruñcip paḻam pōṉṟavaikaḷ.

11.⁠ pālōṭu tēṉkalaṅ taṟṟē paṉimoḻi

vāleyiṟu ūṟiya nīr.





⁠meṉmaiyāṉa moḻikaḷaip pēcukiṉṟa ivaḷuṭaiya veṇmaiyāṉa paṟkaḷil ūṟiya nīr pālōṭu tēṉ kalantāṟ pōṉṟa cuvaiyai yuṭaiyatāyirukkiṉṟatu.



12.⁠ uṭampoṭu uyiriṭai eṉṉamaṟ ṟaṉṉa

maṭantaiyoṭu emmiṭai naṭpu.





im maṅkaiyōṭu emakkuḷḷa toṭarpu uṭampiṟkum, uyirkkum uḷḷa toṭarpukaḷ eppaṭiyō appaṭiyākum.

uṭampum uyirum vēṟṟumaiyillāmal kalantiruttal pōlavum, iṉpa tuṉpaṅkaḷaiccērntu aṉupavittal pōlavum nāṅkaḷ iruvarum vēṟṟumaiyillāmal iṉpa tuṉpaṅkaḷaic cērntu aṉupavikkakkūṭiya avvaḷavu neruṅkiya toṭarpu uṭaiyavarāṉōm eṉpatām.

13.⁠ karumaṇiyil pāvāynī pōtāyām vīḻum

tirunutaṟku illai iṭam.





eṉ kaṇṇiṉ karumaṇiyiluḷḷa pāvaiyē eṉṉāl virumpappaṭukiṉṟa aḻakiya neṟṟiyai yuṭaiya ivaḷ taṅki iruppataṟku vēṟu iṭam illai. ākaiyāl nī taṅki yirukkum avviṭattaiviṭṭu veḷiyē varuvāyāka.

14.⁠ eḻutuṅkāl kōlkāṇāk kaṇṇēpōl koṇkaṉ

paḻikāṇēṉ kaṇṭa viṭattu.





mai tīṭṭappaṭumpōtu mai tīṭṭum kōlaik kaṇ uṇarātatu pōlak kaṇavaṉaik kaṇṭapōtu avaraik kāṇātapōtu muṉaittirunta kuṟṟaṅkaḷuḷ oṉṟaiyum nāṉ kāṇamāṭṭēṉ

15.⁠ kāṇuṅkāl kāṇēṉ tavaṟāya kāṇākkāl

kāṇēṉ tavaṟal lavai.





eṉ kaṇavarai yāṉ kāṇumpōtu kuṟṟamāṉavai oṉṟaiyum nāṉ aṟiyēṉ ; avaraik kāṇātapōtu kuṟṟamallātavaṟṟai aṟiyavē māṭṭēṉ (avar kuṟṟattaiyē kāṇpēṉ.)

16.⁠ malariṉum mellitu kāmam cilarataṉ

cevvi talaippaṭu vār.





kāma iṉpam malaraik kāṭṭilum meṉmaiyuṭaiyatāka irukkum. aṅṅaṉam atu meṉmai yuṭaiyatāy iruttalai aṟintu ataṉ nalla payaṉaip peṟakkūṭiyavarkaḷ ulakattiṟ cilarē āvar.



47.⁠uppamain taṟṟāl pulavi atuciṟitu

mikkaṟṟāl nīḷa viṭal.





⁠ūṭal uṇavuvakaikaḷukkuc cuvai tarum uppup pōṉṟatu. avvūṭalai nām ciṟitu atika nēram nīṭṭittōmāṉāl uppu atikappaṭṭu uṇavu cuvai keṭṭuppōvatu pōl ilvāḻvu payaṉaṟṟatākiviṭum.

18.⁠ immaip piṟappil piriyalam eṉṟēṉāk

kaṇṇiṟai nīrkoṇ ṭaṉaḷ.





⁠intap piṟaviyil nāṉ uṉṉai viṭṭup piriyavē māṭṭēṉ eṉṟēṉ.appaṭi yāṉāl maṟu piṟaviyil piriyak karutukiṉṟīrō eṉṟu piṇaṅkit taṉ kaṇkaḷ niṟaiya nīraip perukkiṉāḷ.

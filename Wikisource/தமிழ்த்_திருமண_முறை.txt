﻿patippuri

⁠taṟpōtu tamiḻnāṭu pala tuṟikaḷil maṟumalarcci aṭintu varukiṟatu. paṟpala tuṟikaḷil cīrtiruttam mukiḻttu varukiṟatu. aṟivilē puraṭci, vāḻkkiyilē puraṭci ēṟpaṭṭu varukiṟatu. innili mēṉmēl vaḷara vēṇṭum. ittakiya puraṭcikaḷil oṉṟutāṉ iṉṟu naṭipeṟum tamiḻt tirumaṇam. taṟkālattē pēraṟiñar aṇṇā avarkaḷiṉ ariya muyaṟciyiṉāl ittakiya tirumaṇaṅkaḷ caṭṭappaṭi ēṟkattakkavi eṉṟa nili ēṟ paṭṭiruppatu tamiḻarkaḷiṉ perumitattukkuriyatu.

civamuttum tamiḻar tirumaṇamum

⁠it tirumaṇam tamiḻ nāṭṭiṟkup putuvataṉṟu. paḻan tamiḻar mēṟkoṇṭu vāḻnta maṇamuṟiyil makkaḷ maṉanilikku ēṟpac ciṟcila māṟutalkaḷi amittu aṟivuṭip perumakkaḷ palar kūṭi āyntamitta tākum. im muṟiyil eṇṇaṟṟa tamiḻt tirumaṇaṅkaḷic ceytu vittut tamiḻ uṇarvu parappi vantavar nam perum pulavar mayili civamuttu avarkaḷ. inneṟi vaḷara avar āṟṟiya tiyākaṅkaḷ aḷavilaṭaṅkātaṉa.

ēṉ cīrtiruttam vēṇṭum?

⁠maṇam eṉpataṟkuk kūṭutal eṉpatu poruḷ. aṉpāl piṇikkappaṭṭa iruvar uḷḷaṅkaḷ oṉṟu kūṭuvatiyē maṇam eṉṟu koṇṭōm. paṇṭik kālattil vāḻnta tamiḻ makaṉum tamiḻ makaḷum oruvari oruvar nēriṟkaṇṭu paḻaki maṉa oṟṟumi uṭiyavarāṉa piṉpē avartam peṟṟōrkaḷ avarkkut tirumaṇam ﻿ceytu vippatu vaḻakkam. attirumaṇamum mikac curuṅkiya muṟiyil cikkaṉamākavē naṭipeṟum. aṉpiṉāl piṇikkappaṭṭa maṇamakkaḷ iruvarum illaṟameṉṉum nallaṟam ēṟṟu vāḻkki toṭaṅkukiṟārkaḷ eṉpati aṟivikkavē maṇac caṭaṅkukaḷ naṭipeṟṟaṉa

⁠cāṉṟōrkaḷ vakutta ammuṟikaḷip piṉ paṟṟip pērāciriyar mayili civamuttu avarkaḷ āyirak kaṇakkāṉa tirumaṇaṅkaḷi naṭatti nalvaḻi kāṭṭiyuḷḷār. am muṟikaḷi aṉivarum aṟintu piṟar utaviyiṉṟit tāṅkaḷē tirumaṇaṅkaḷi naṭattat tuṇiyāka irukka vēṇṭum eṉṟa nōkkattōṭu innūli veḷiyiṭa virumpiṉōm.

⁠innūlic cemmiyāṉa muṟiyil acciṭap pērutaviyāka irunta pulavarkaḷ tiru. ta. irāmaliṅkam, taṇiki ulakanātaṉ ākiyōrukku maṉṟattiṉ naṉṟi eṉṟum uriyatu.

﻿pērāciriyar mayili civamuttu avarkaḷ nikaḻttiya tamiḻt tirumaṇamuṟi



tamiḻt tirumaṇam kaṭavuṭ koḷkiyōṭu kūṭiyatu. maṇam ceytukoḷḷuvōr piṟkāla vāḻkkiyilē tammuṭiya kaṭamiyi uṇarntu nalvaḻiyilē naṭattal vēṇṭum eṉpatē ittirumaṇattiṉ mutal nōkkam. ātalāl tirumaṇattil ceyyum caṭaṅkukaḷiṉ poruḷic ceyvippōrum ceytukoḷvōrum uṭaṉiruppōrum purintukoḷḷattakka muṟiyilē intat tamiḻt tirumaṇam amikkap peṟṟuḷḷatu. eṉavē it tirumaṇatti naṭatti vippōr aṅkuk kuḻumiyuḷḷa makkaḷukkum maṇam ceytukoḷḷum maṇamakkaḷukkum avvappōtu viḷakkik kūṟutal vēṇṭum. aṅṅaṉam kūṟattakkavikaḷuḷ kīḻ varupavi iṉṟiyamiyātaṉavākum.

1. maṇamakaṉ vīṭṭār, maṇamakaḷ vīṭṭār aḻip pitaḻkaḷit tirumaṇa maṉṟattil kūṭiyuḷḷa periyōrkaḷukkup paṭittuk kāṭṭutal.

2. varavēṟpavar, maṇamakkaḷiṉ peṟṟōr mutaliyavarkaḷiṉ peyarkaḷip paṭittuk kāṭṭik kūṭumāṉāl avarkaḷiyum aṟimukappaṭuttutal nalam.

8. maṇamakaṉ, maṇamakaḷ ākiya iruvariyum āṅkuk kuḻumiyuḷḷavarukku aṟimukappaṭuttutal.

4. maṇamakkaḷiṉ eṇṇam aṟintukoḷḷum vakiyil intat tamiḻt tirumaṇattik kuṟittum tirumaṇam ceytukoḷḷa vēṇṭiya kaṭamiyik kuṟittum, maṇamakkaḷukkuc curukkamāka viḷakkik kūṟutal.

tirumaṇam ceytukoḷvōrkku avaravar paḻakka vaḻakkaṅkaḷukku ēṟṟavakiyil intat tirumaṇatti naṭattutal nalam.



﻿ tamiḻt tirumaṇam nikaḻttum muṟi

avaravar paḻakka vaḻakkaṅkaḷukku ēṟpa, maṇappantali malar mālikaḷālum palvēṟu viḷakkukaḷālum aḻaku paṭuttumāṟu ceytal vēṇṭum. kuttuviḷakku, kuṭaviḷakku, kiḷiviḷakku, kiviḷakku mutaliyavikaḷuḷ oṉṟiṉi avaravarkaṭku iyinta vakiyil ēṟṟivikkalām. cilar varṇam tīṭṭiya maṭkalaṅkaḷ, mara ural mutaliyavikaḷi maṇappantalil vippiṉum āciriyar avikaḷi ēṟṟuk koḷḷalām. marapukku māṟupaṭāta vakiyil kuṟintatu iraṇṭu kuttuviḷakkukaḷēṉum iruttal vēṇṭum.

maṇappantalil maṇamakkaḷ kiḻakku nōkkiyum maṇam ceytu vikkum āciriyar vaṭakku nōkkiyum amarntiruttal nam nāṭṭu makkaḷiṉ vaḻakkamāka iruttaliṉ itaṉēyē aṉivarum mēṟkoḷḷalām.

oru talivāḻiyiliyi maṇamakkaḷukku muṉpuṟamāka (atāvatu kiḻakkutticiyil) vittu, avvāḻi iliyil mūṉṟu piṭi paccarici parappi vittu ataṉ mītu vāḻip pū vaṭivilaminta oru cempiṉi mañcaḷ kuṅkumaṅkaḷāl alaṅkarittu vittal vēṇṭum. piṉpu accempil tūymiyāṉa nīrvārttu māvilik kottiṉip porutti ataṉmītu muṟṟiya tēṅkāy oṉṟiṉi mañcaḷ pūcik kuṅkumam iṭṭu vittal vēṇṭum. ac cempiṉukkup pakkattil aritta mañcaḷip piṭittu vittu atik kuṅkumattāl alaṅkarittal vēṇṭum. cempiṉukku maṟṟāeru pakkattil orutaṭṭil mañcaḷ kalanta paccariciyip parappi ataṉ mītu muṟṟiya tēṅkāy oṉṟiṉi vittu ataṉiyum mañcaḷ kuṅkumaṅkaḷāl alaṅkarittup poṟṟāviyōṭu kūṭiya mañcaḷ kayiṟṟi atil cuṟṟi vittal vēṇṭum. maṇamakkaḷukku muṉṉar iraṇṭu mukkālikaḷ iṭṭu maṇamakaṉukkākavum maṇamakaḷukkākavum peṟṟāērkaḷ paricākat tarum puttāṭikaḷum malar mālikaḷum nirampiya taṭṭukaḷi vittal vēṇṭum.



iṟi vaṇakkam muṭintapiṉṉar maṇappantalil kuḻumiyuḷḷa aṉparkaḷiṭam apputtāṭikaḷi vāḻttit tarumāṟu kēṭṭu avarkaḷ vāḻttiṉip peṟṟu maṇamakkaṭku apputtāṭikaḷi aḷittal vēṇṭum.

puttāṭiyuṭaṉ maṇamakaṉum maṇamakaḷum maṇap pantalukku vantatum avaravar marapukkut takkapaṭi iṟivaḻi pāṭṭiṉi muṭittuk koṇṭu maṇamakkaḷ maṇamēṭiyil vantu amara vēṇṭum.

maṇamakaṉ maṇamakaḷukku maṅkala nāṇ aṇiyum variyil maṇamakaḷ maṇamakaṉukku valappakkattil iruttal marapu eṉpataṉi oṭṭi avvāṟu irukkac ceyyalām. im maṅkala nikaḻcci muṭintatum maṇamakaḷi maṇamakaṉukku iṭap pakkattil amarac ceyyalām.

mañcaḷāl piṭittu vikkappaṭṭuḷḷati aruva meṉṟum uruva meṉṟum colla iyalāta kantaḻik kaṭavuḷ vaṭivameṉṟum, nīr nirampiya cempiṉmēl tēṅkāyōṭu viḷaṅkum vaṭivattiṉi iṟivaṉ ammi appaṉāka irukkum vaṭivameṉṟum, kuṭaviḷakku, kiḷiviḷakku mutaliya vikaḷ iṟivaṉ oḷivaṭivamāka viḷaṅkum niliyiṉik kuṟippaṉa eṉṟum maṟimali aṭikaḷār terivittuḷḷa karuttiṉpaṭi maṇamakkaḷ im mūṉṟu vakiyālum iṟivaṉē vaṇaṅkuvārkaḷ.

camittukaḷ koṇṭu ceyyum eriyōmputalum iv vaḻipāṭukaḷuḷ taliciṟanta oṉṟāka viḷaṅkutal vēṇṭum.

puttāṭi aṇintu vanta maṇamakkaḷ mutalil peṟṟāēr vaḻipāṭu ceytu avartam nalvāḻttiṉip peṟṟu eriyōmpum ceyalāl oḷivaṭivil kaṭavuḷē vaṇaṅkuvar. centīyōmputalāl kaṭavuḷi vaḻipaṭumpōtu tēvāra, tiruvācakaṅkaḷ, tamiḻ maṟiyākiya tirukkuṟaḷ ivikaḷuḷ avaravar viruppattiṟku ēṟṟa vaṇṇam takutivāynta pāṭalkaḷit tirumaṇam nikaḻttum āciriyar uḷaṅkaṉintu pāṭutal vēṇṭum. (aṅṅaṉam pāṭutaṟkuriya pāṭalkaḷ innūlil āṅkāṅkē tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa. viṇava camayattavar tirumaṇattiṟkupporuttamāṉa pāṭalkaḷ piṟcērkkiyākat tarappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.) ivvaḻipāṭu muṭintatum kuḻumiyuḷḷa periyōrkaḷāl vāḻttit tarappeṟṟa maṅkalanāṇi maṇamakaṉ maṇamakaḷukku aṇital vēṇṭum. appoḻutu iṉṉici muḻakkamum "maṉamakkaḷ vāḻka! maṇamakkaḷ vāḻka!” eṉa aṉparkaḷiṉ vāḻttalum, mañcaḷ kalanta malarum tūvutalum nikaḻum.

piṉṉar maṇamakkaḷ mālimāṟṟik koḷḷavēṇṭum. oruvarukkoruvar tam mālikaḷi mūṉṟu muṟi māṟṟik koḷvatāl maṇamakkaḷiṉ maṉa orumippāṭṭiṉi makkaḷ aṟintu koḷkiṉṟaṉar. māli māṟṟutal muṭintapiṉṉar maṇamakaṉ taṉ vāḻkkit tuṇiviyiṉ kiyippiṭittuk koṇṭu maṇappantalic cuṟṟivarutal vēṇṭum. māli māṟṟutalum maṇamakaḷik kippiṭittu valam varutalum tamiḻttirumaṇattiṟku iṉṟiyamiyātaṉavāka uḷḷaṉa. aṉṟiyum maṇamakkaḷiṉ uṭaṉ pāṭṭiṉi aṅkuk kuḻumiyuḷḷōr aṟintu koḷvataṟkum ciṟanta cāṉṟāka viḷaṅkukiṉṟaṉa. ic ceyalkaḷōṭu tirumaṇam muṭivu peṟukiṉṟatu. piṉṉart tirumaṇam ceytu vitta āciriyarum āṉṟōrkaḷum cāṉṟōrkaḷum maṇamakkaḷukku ilvāḻkkiyiṉ ciṟappiṉi eṭuttuk kūṟi vāḻttuvar.

kuṟippu

kāppukkaṭṭutalum kaḷitalum

tamiḻt tirumaṇam mikavum cikkaṉa muṟiyil orē vēḷiyil nikaḻumāṟu amittiruppatāl, kāppuk kaṭṭal kāppukkaḷital, aracāṇikkāl naṭukal mutaliyaṉa tēvi illi. ivikaḷiyum maṇa nikaḻcciyil cērttuc ceyya vēṇṭumeṉṟu kēṭṭuk koḷpavarkaḷukkup piṉvarumāṟu ceytal vēṇṭum.





kāppuk kaṭṭutal: oru taṭṭilē mañcaḷ kalanta ariciyip parappivittu ataṉ mītu mañcaḷ kuṅku maṅkaḷāl alaṅkarikkappaṭṭa tēṅkāyi vittu antat taṭṭiṉi maṇamakaṉukku muṉṉāl vikkappaṭṭirukkum mukkāliyiṉ mēl vittal vēṇṭum. maṇamakaṉ valakkiyiṉi antat tēṅkāyiṉmītu vittapaṭi irukka muḻumañcaḷ kaṭṭappeṟṟa nūlkayiṟṟiṉi maṇamakaṉ kiyil kaṭṭutal vēṇṭum. ataṉ piṉṉar maṇamakaḷ taṉ iṭakkiyi antat tēṅkāyiṉmēl vikka maṇamakaṉ mañcaḷ kaṭṭiya nūliṉi maṇamakaḷ kiyil kaṭṭutal vēṇṭum. atēcamayattil avarkaḷukku etirppuṟattil naṭappeṟṟuḷḷa araca marakkikaḷikkum mañcaḷ nūlkayiṟu oṉṟu kaṭṭutal vēṇṭum.

kāppukkaḷital

tirumaṇam muṭintapiṉṉark kāppukkaṭṭiya atē muṟiyil maṇamakaṉ mañcaḷ pūciya tēṅkāyiṉ mēl kiyivikkac ceytu muṉ pukaṭṭiya mañcaḷ nūl kayiṟṟiṉi aviḻttiṭac ceytal vēṇṭum anta nērattilēyē aracaṅkiḷiyil kaṭṭiya mañcaḷ kayiṟṟiṉiyum aviḻttu viṭutal vēṇṭum. innikaḻccikaḷiṉpōtu tummal, irumal mutaliyaṉa kēḷāmal iruttaṟ poruṭṭu mēḷam koṭṭutal vaḻakkam.

pantaṟkāl naṭutal

tamiḻt tirumaṇam ceytukoḷpavarkaḷ intac caṭaṅkic ceyvataṟku āciriyar utaviyi nāṭavēṇṭiyatilli maṅkala makaḷir aivar oṉṟu kūṭip pantaṟkālkaḷuḷ vaṭa kiḻakku mūliyil naṭavēṇṭiya oṉṟukku mañcaḷ pūci, kuṅkumam iṭṭu malar cūṭi mēḷatāḷa muḻakkattuṭaṉ taṅkaḷ kikaḷāl antak kampattik kuḻiyil naṭalām. āciriyarum uṭaṉiruntu nikaḻtta vēṇṭumeṉṟu virumpiṉāl āciriyariyum vittuk koḷḷalām. āciriyar iṟivaḻipāṭu ceytu mañcaḷ kalanta ariciyip parappiya tali vāḻiyiliyiṉmītu kalacam niṟutti, antak kalacattiṟku vaḻipāṭāṟṟik kalacattiluḷḷa nīriṉik koṇṭu pantaṟ kāliṉik kaḻuvumpaṭi ceyvar. inta nikaḻcciyi avaravar paḻakka vaḻakkaṅkaḷukku ēṟpap periyōr karuttaṟintu ceytal nalam.

uṟuti ceytal

itu tirumaṇam nikaḻvataṟku muṉṉar maṇamakaḷ vīṭṭil nikaḻum nikaḻcciyākum. maṇamakaṉukku uriyōrum maṇamakaḷukku uriyōrum oru nalla nāḷil uṟṟār uṟaviṉar muṉṉiliyil oruvarukkoruvar uṟuti kūṟik koḷvatu marapu. peṇvīṭṭār tam peṇṇik kuṟitta oru maṇamakaṉukkut taruvatākavum avvāṟē ammaṇamakaṉ vīṭṭār maṇamakaṉukku vāḻkkit tuṇiviyākak kuṟitta oru peṇṇi ēṟṟuk koḷvatākavum oruvarkkoruvar eḻuttu mūlamākavum vāymoḻi mūlamākavum āciriyar muṉṉiliyil cuṟṟattārkaḷ naṇparkaḷ cāṉṟāka viḷaṅka uṟuti ceytu koḷḷavēṇṭum.

curukkamākat tamiḻt tirumaṇam ceyya virumpupavarkaḷ * ikkuṟiyiṭṭuḷḷa nikaḻccikaḷimaṭṭum piṉpaṟṟalām.

tamiḻt tirumaṇa nikaḻccikaḷum poruttamāṉa mantirap pāṭalkaḷum

1. iṟivaḻipāṭu

mañcaḷ piḷḷiyār piṭittuvittu viḷakkēṟṟi vittal. āciriyar vaṭakku nōkki amarntu maṇamakaṉi aḻittuk kiḻakku nōkki amarac ceytal. āciriyar piṉvarum tiruppāṭalip pāṭikkoṇṭu mañcaḷ kalanta arici, utirimalar ākiyavaṟṟi maṇamakaṉkiyil koṭuttup piḷḷi yāri vaḻipaṭac ceytal.

piṭiyataṉ uruvumi koḷa miku kariyatu

vaṭikoṭu taṉataṭi vaḻipaṭu mavariṭar

kaṭikaṇa pativara aruḷiṉaṉ mikukoṭi

vaṭiviṉar payilvali valamuṟi iṟiyē.





2 kālnaṭutal

aracaṅkiḷi allatu ālaṅkiḷi oṉṟiṉi maṅkala aṇi aṇinta makaḷir mūvaruṭaṉum maṇamakkaḷuṭaṉum naṭutal. āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭali ōtikkoṇṭu kālkaḷukku. mañcaḷ, kuṅkumam iṭṭut tuṇi kaṭṭac ceytal.

māvēli ālamati aṭakkit taṉṉuḷ

maṇṇulakam aṇṭamelām vaḷarntutāṉōr

kāvēyiṉ muṉṉutitta araciṟ ṟōṉṟik

kaṭampupuṉi kuruntiṉukkut tuṇiya tākit

tūvētan talikāṇṭaṟku ariya tākit

tuṉpamuṟu piṟaviyeṉun tukaḷcēr veyyil

āvēṉit taṉṉaṭiyām niḻaliṟ cērtta

attitaṉip patti ceytu mutti cērvām.





3. kumpa pūci

vāḻiyiliyil ariciyiṭṭu iraṇṭu kumpaṅkaḷil taṇṇīr niṟittup periya kumpatti valappakkattilum ciṟiya kumpatti iṭappakkattilum vittuk kumpaṅkaḷi iṟivaṉ iṟiviyāka niṉittut tūpa tīpaṅkāṭṭi āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalkaḷi ōtikkoṇṭu maṇamakkaḷi vaḻipāṭu āṟṟumpaṭi ceytal :

iṟi vaḻipāṭu

valappakkak kumpavaḻipāṭu

aṟuvakic camayat tōrkkum avvavar poruḷāṅ vēṟām

kuṟiyatu uṭittāy vētā kamaṅkaḷiṉ kuṟiyiṟantaṅku

aṟiviṉil aruḷāl maṉṉi ammiyōṭu appaṉākic

ceṟivoḻi yātu niṉṟa civaṉaṭi ceṉṉi vippām.



ulake lām uṇarn tōtaṟ kariyavaṉ

nilavu lāviya nīrmali vēṇiyaṉ

alakil cōtiyaṉ ampalat tāṭuvāṉ

malarci lampaṭi vāḻtti vaṇaṅkuvām.





iṟivi vaḻipāṭu

iṭappakkak kumpavaḻipāṭu

tiyalnal lāḷit tavattiṉ taliviyi

miyali nōkkum maṉōṉmaṇi maṅkiyip

piyaniṉ ṟēttip paṇimiṉ paṇintapiṉ

veyya pavamiṉi mēva kilāvē.





4.maṇamakkaḷukku āṭi vaḻaṅkutal

maṇamakaṉukku āṭi aḷittal

oru taṭṭil puttāṭi, pū, veṟṟilipākku ivikaḷi vittu āciriyar piṉvarum tiruppāṭali ōtikkoṇṭu maṇamakaṉukkut tarutal.



﻿ pōṉṉu meypporu, ḷuntaru vāṉip

⁠pōka mumtiru vumpuṇarp pāṉip,

piṉṉi eṉpiḻi yippoṟup pāṉip,

⁠piḻiye lāmtavi rappaṇip pāṉi,

iṉṉa taṉmiyaṉ eṉṟaṟi voṇṇā

⁠emmā ṉieḷi vanta pirāṉi,

aṉṉam vikum vayalpaḻa ṉattaṇi

⁠ārū rāṉi maṟakkalu māmē.





maṇamakaḷukku āṭi vaḻaṅkutal

āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭali ōtikkoṇṭu maṇamakaḷukku āṭiyi aḷittal.

tammi yēpukaḻn ticci pēciṉum

⁠cārpiṉum toṇṭar tarukilā

poymmi yāḷarip pāṭātē enti

⁠pukalūr pāṭumiṉ pulavīrkāḷ,

immi yētarum cōṟum kūṟiyum

⁠ētta lām iṭar keṭalumām,

ammi yēciva lōka māḷvataṟku

⁠yātum aiyuṟa villiyē.





5. kāppu aṇital

orutaṭṭil paccarici, veṟṟili pākku, mañcaḷ pūciya tēṅkāy, paḻam, mañcaḷ kōkkappaṭṭa iraṇṭu mañcaḷ nāṇ ākiyavaṟṟi vittal.

tirumaṇam taṭiyiṉṟi iṉitu naṭipeṟum poruṭṭut tiruvaruṭkāppāka in nāṇik kiyil kaṭṭukiṉṟēṉ eṉṟu āciriyar collik koṇṭu piṉvarum pāṭali ōti maṇamakaṉ valakkiyil kaṭṭutal.



﻿

maṇamakaṉ valakkiyil kaṭṭal

ōrvaru kaṇka ḷiṇikkayalē umiyavaḷ kaṇkaḷ iṇikkayalē

ērmaru vuṅkaḻa ṉākamatē eḻilkoḷu tācaṉa ṉākamatē

nīrvaru kontaḷa kaṅkiyatē neṭuñcaṭi mēviya kaṅkiyatē

cērvaru yōkati yampakaṉē cirapura mēyati yampakaṉē.





āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭali ōta maṇamakaḷukku iṭak kiyil maṇamakaṉ kāppukkaṭṭutal.

aintu karattaṉi yāṉi mukattaṉi

intiṉ iḷampiṟi pōlum eyiṟṟaṉi

tanti makaṉṟaṉē ñāṉak koḻuntiṉip

puntiyil vittaṭi pōṟṟu kiṉṟēṉē.





6. peṟṟāēr vaḻipāṭu



maṇamakaṉ taṉ peṟṟāēriṉ tiruvaṭiyit tūya nīrāl viḷakkip poṭṭiṭṭu malar tūvip piṉvarum tiruppāṭṭi ōtik koṇṭu vaḻipaṭavēṇṭum.

appaṉnī amminī aiya ṉumnī

aṉpuṭiya māmaṉum māmi yumnī

oppuṭiya mātarum oṇporu ḷumnī

orukulamum cuṟṟamum ōrū rumnī

tuyppaṉavum uyppaṉavum tōṟṟu vāynī

tuṇiyāyeṉ neñcam turappip pāynī

ippoṉnī immaṇinī immut tumnī

iṟivaṉ nī ēṟūrnta celvaṉ nīyē.





maṇa makaḷ taṉ peṟṟōriṉ tiruvaṭiyi nīrāl viḷakki malar tūvip piṉvarum tiruppāṭali ōtikkoṇṭu vaḻipaṭutal.



ammiyē appā oppilā maṇiyē

aṉpiṉil viḷinta āramutē,

poymmiyē perukkip poḻutiṉic curukkum

puḻuttalip puliyaṉēṉ taṉakkuc

cemmiyē āya civapatam aḷitta

celvamē! civaperu māṉē!

immiyē uṉṉic cikkeṉap piṭittēṉ

eṅkeḻun taruḷuvatu iṉiyē.





7.makaṭ koṭi nērtal

maṇamakaḷiṉ valakkiyi maṇamakaṉiṉ valakkiyiṉ mēl vikkac ceytu, maṇamakaṉiṉ peṟṟāērum, maṇamakaḷiṉ peṟṟāērum tattam kikaḷiyum maṇamakkaḷiṉ kikaḷōṭu mēlum kīḻumāka vittukkoḷḷumpaṭi ceytal. maṇamakaḷiṉ peṟṟōr kiyil veṟṟili, pākku, paḻam ivikaḷuṭaṉ nīr vārttuk koṭuttal. appoḻutu piṉvarumāṟu kūṟutal vēṇṭum.

maṇamakaṉ peṟṟāēr

.......irukkum ...... ākiya yāṉ eṉ arumi makaṉ tiruvaḷarcelvaṉ ............ kku ...... ... irukkum ........... makaḷ tiruvaḷarcelvi ... naṅkiyi uṟaviṉar āṉṟōr mutaliyavarkaḷ cāṉṟākat tirumaṇam ceytu koḷḷa icikiṉṟēṉ,' eṉṟu kūṟutal.



8.centīvēṭṭal

⁠in nikaḻccikkuc camittu, ney, pori, maṇal ākiyavi vēṇṭum.

⁠maṇal parappic camittiṉi iṭṭut tī vaḷarttal. maṇamakaṉik koṇṭu centīyil cuḷḷiyum neyyum iṭac ceytal. āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalkaḷi ōtutal.

namaccivāya vāḻka!nātaṉtāḷ vāḻka!

imippoḻutum eṉneñcil nīṅkātāṉ tāḷvāḻka!

kōkaḻi yāṇṭa kurumaṇitaṉ tāḷ vāḻka!

ākamam ākiniṉ ṟaṇṇippāṉ tāḷvāḻka!

ēkaṉ anēkaṉ iṟivaṉ aṭivāḻka!





āṭaka maturi aracē pōṟṟi!

kūṭal ilaṅku kurumaṇi pōṟṟi!

teṉtilli maṉṟiṉuḷ āṭi pōṟṟi!

iṉṟeṉak kāramutu āṉāy pōṟṟi!

mūvā nāṉmaṟi mutalvā pōṟṟi!

cēvār velkoṭic civaṉē pōṟṟi!

miṉṉār uruva vikirtā pōṟṟi !

kalnār uritta kaṉiyē pōṟṟi !

kāvāy kaṉakak kuṉṟē pōṟṟi!

āvā eṉṟaṉakku aruḷāy pōṟṟi !

māṉēr nōkki maṇāḷā pōṟṟi!

vāṉakat tamarar tāyē pōṟṟi!

pāriṭi aintāyp parantāy pōṟṟi !

nīriṭi nāṉkāy nikaḻntāy pōṟṟi !

tīyiṭi mūṉṟāyt tikaḻntāy pōṟṟi!

vaḷiyiṭi iraṇṭāy makiḻntāy pōṟṟi!





veḷiyiṭi oṉṟāy viḷintāy pōṟṟi!

teṉṉāṭuṭiya civaṉē pōṟṟi!

ennāṭṭa varkkum iṟivā pōṟṟi!

pōṟṟi pōṟṟi puyaṅkap perumāṉ!

pōṟṟi pōṟṟi purāṇa kāraṇa!

pōṟṟi pōṟṟi cayacaya pōṟṟi!



centī vaḷarttu muṭintapiṉ ōtutal

iru nilaṉāyt tīyāki nīru māki

iyamāṉa ṉāyeṟiyum kāṟṟu māki

aruniliya tiṅkaḷāy ñāyi ṟāki

ākācamāy aṭṭa mūrtti yākip

perunalamum kuṟṟamum peṇṇum āṇum

piṟaruruvum tammuruvum tāmē yāki

nerunaliyāy iṉṟāki nāḷē yāki

nimir puṉcaṭi aṭikaḷ niṉṟa vāṟē.



cōtiyē cuṭarē cūḻoḷi viḷakkē

curikuḻal paṇimuli maṭanti pātiyē paraṉē pālkoḷ veṇṇīṟṟāy

paṅkayat tayaṉumāl aṟiyā

nītiyē celvat tirupperun tuṟiyil

niṟimalark kuruntam mēviyacīr

ātiyē yaṭiyēṉ ātarit taḻittāl

atentuvē eṉṟa ruḷāyē.



oḷivaḷar viḷakkē ulappilā voṉṟē

uṇarvu cūḻ kaṭantatōr uṇarvē

teḷivaḷar paḷiṅkiṉ tiraṇ maṇik kuṉṟē

cittattuḷ tittikkum tēṉē

aḷivaḷar uḷḷattu āṉantak kaṉiyē

ampalam āṭu araṅkāka

veḷivaḷar teyvak kūttu ukantāyit

toṇṭaṉēṉ viḷampumā viḷampē.



﻿ 9. maṅkala nāṇ vaḻipāṭu

poṉ tāliyil mañcaḷ pūcik kuṅkumam iṭṭu irumuḻa nīḷamuḷḷa mañcaḷ pūciya nāṇil ap poṉ tāliyik kōttu iru puṟamum muṭiyiṭṭut taṭṭil vittu, pūveṟṟili pākku tēṅkāy paḻam mañcaḷ ivaṟṟiyum vittuk kuḻumi yuḷḷa cāṉṟōr vāḻttukkūṟik kikaḷāl toṭum vaṇṇam kāṇpittu varutal vēṇṭum.

maṇamakaṉ maṇamakaḷukku vaṭakkup pakkattil amartal. maṇamakaḷ valappakkamum maṇamakaṉ iṭap pakkamum amartal vēṇṭum. appoḻutu āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭali ōta. maṅkala nāṇ vaḻipāṭu naṭipeṟum.

maṉṟilmaṇi viḷakku eṉalām maruvumuka nakipōṟṟi

oṉṟiyamaṅ kalanāṇiṉ oḷipōṟṟi ulakumpar

ceṉṟutoḻa aruḷ curakkum civakāma cuntaritaṉ

niṉṟatiru nili pōṟṟi nilavutiru vaṭipōṟṟi.





10.maṇamakkaḷ uṟutimoḻi

maṇamakaṉ uṟutimoḻi kūṟutal

perumatippiṟkuriya periyōrkaḷē ! pēraṉpukeḻumiya uṟaviṉarkaḷē ! uṅkaḷ aṉivarkkum aṉpu kaṉinta vaṇakkam. vāḻvilōr tirunāḷākiya innaṉṉāḷil uṅkaḷ muṉṉiliyil ippeṇṇiṉ nallāḷi eṉ vāḻkkit tuṇiviyāka ēṟṟukkoḷḷa maṭṭaṟṟa makiḻcciyuṭaṉ icikiṉṟēṉ. aṉpil malarkiṉṟa ilvāḻkkiyil iṉpattilum tuṉpattilum iṇipiriyātu vāṉpukaḻ vaḷḷuvar vakutta teḷḷu tamiḻ neṟiyil niṉṟu vāḻvēṉ eṉṟu uṟuti kūṟukiṉṟēṉ. eṉ tuṇivikku illaṟa vāḻvil aḷikka vēṇṭiya urimikaḷi aḷittu aṉpuṭaṉ vāḻvēṉ. ‘kātalar iruvar karuttu orumittu ātaravu paṭṭatē iṉpam’ eṉṟa āṉṟōr moḻiyi niṉiviṟ koṇṭu eṉ vāḻkkit tuṇiviyuṭaṉ oṟṟumi﻿yōṭu vāḻvēṉ eṉpatu uṟuti. eṅkaḷ illaṟa vāḻvu peṟṟāērum periyōrum pukaḻum vakiyil iṉitu naṭipeṟa vēṇṭumeṉṟu eṅkum niṟinta iṟivaṉi iṟiñcukiṉṟēṉ.

maṇamakaḷ uṟutimoḻi kūṟutal

iṅkuk kuḻumiyuḷḷa periyōrkaḷ aṉivarkkum vaṇakkam. uṅkaḷ muṉṉiliyil in nampiyi eṉ vāḻkkit tuṇivarāka ēṟṟukkoḷḷap perumakiḻcciyuṭaṉ icikiṉṟēṉ. eṉ kaṇavar karuttaṟintu oḻuki ilvāḻkkikku uṟṟa tuṇiyākat tikaḻvēṉ. iṉpattilum iṉṉalilum iṇipiriyātu eṉ kaṇavari matittu avar iṉpamē eṉ iṉpam; avar nalamē eṉ nalam eṉakkaruti vāḻvēṉ eṉṟu uṟuti kūṟukiṉṟēṉ. tamiḻ maṟiyām tirukkuṟaḷ kūṟum illaṟa neṟiyil eṉ kaṇavaruṭaṉ aṉpuṭaṉ iṇintu vāḻvēṉ eṉpatu uṟuti. eṅkaḷ maṉiyaṟa vāḻkki makiḻcci poṅkac ciṟappuṭaṉ viḷaṅka aruḷpuriyumāṟu ellām valla iṟivaṉi vēṇṭukiṉṟēṉ.





11. maṅkala nāṇ aṇital

1. ⁠maṇamakaṉiṉ uṭaṉ piṟantāḷ oru taṭṭil tiruviḷakkēṟṟi ataṉi maṇamakaḷiṉ piṉpuṟam ēnti niṟṟal vēṇṭum.

2. ⁠āciriyar maṅkala nāṇi maṇamakaṉ kiyil koṭuttal.

3.⁠ maṇamakaṉ taṉ valakki maṇamakaḷiṉ kaḻuttiṟkup piṉpuṟamāka avaṉatu valappakkam varumpaṭivaḷattu, maṟṟāeruki iṭappuṟattil irukka, maṅkala nāṇiṉ iru puṟaṅkaḷiyum paṟṟit tāliyiṉ mukappiṉ muṉpuṟam mārpiṉ naṭuvē viḷaṅka, kaḻuttil kaṭṭi mūṉṟu muṭiyiṭutal vēṇṭum. piṉ kiyeṭāmal tirunīṟu, kuṅkumam cantaṉam, malar ivaṟṟi muṭiyil vittal vēṇṭum.

maṇamakaṉ maṇamakaḷukku maṅkala nāṇ aṇiyum poḻutu āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭalkaḷi ōtutal vēṇṭum.

1.⁠maṇṇiṉalla vaṇṇam vāḻalām vikalum

eṇṇiṉ nallakatikku yātumōr kuṟivilē

kaṇṇiṉ nallaḵtuṟum kaḻumala vaḷanakarp

peṇṇiṉ nallāḷoṭum peruntaki yiruntatē.



2.⁠naṉṟuṭi yāṉi tīyatil lāṉi nariveḷḷēṟu

oṉṟuṭi yāṉi umiyoru pākam uṭiyāṉi

ceṉṟaṭiyāta tiruvuṭi yāṉi cirāp paḷḷik

kuṉṟuṭi yāṉik kūṟaveṉ uḷḷam kuḷirummē.



3.⁠muṉṉam avaṉuṭiya nāmam kēṭṭāḷ

mūrtti avaṉ irukkum vaṇṇam kēṭṭāḷ

piṉṉi avaṉuṭiya ārūr kēṭṭāḷ

peyarttum avaṉukkē picci āṉāḷ

aṉṉiyiyum attaṉiyum aṉṟē nīttāḷ

akaṉṟāḷ akaliṭattār ācā ratti

taṉṉi maṟantāḷ taṉṉāmam keṭṭāḷ

talippaṭṭāḷ naṅki talivaṉ tāḷē.





12. poṭṭu iṭutal

maṇamakaḷukku maṇamakaṉ poṭṭu iṭutal. appōtu, āciriyar piṉvarum pāṭali ōtuvār.

kalavañcēr kaḻikkāṉal katir muttam kalantu eṅkum

alavañ cē raṇi vārik koṇarn teṟiyum akaṉṟuṟivāy

nilavañcēr nuṇṇiṭiya nēriḻiyā ḷavaḷōṭum

tilakamcēr neṟṟiyiṉar tiruvēṭṭak kuṭiyārē.

﻿1. amippu



iruppiṭam

ulakiṉ teṉ kōṭiyit teṉ muṉi eṉṟu kūṟukiṟōm. immuṉiyic cuṟṟi amintuḷḷa kaṭalē aṇṭārkṭik kaṭal ākum. aimperuṅ kaṭalkaḷil itu mikak kuṟinta āḻamuḷḷatu.

itaṟkut teṉ kaṭal eṉṉum peyarum uṇṭu. pacipik kaṭal, aṭlāṇṭik kaṭal, intiyak kaṭal ākiya mūṉṟiṉ pakutiyāka amintuḷḷa kaṭal itu. itit taṉik kaṭal eṉṟu kūṟuvataṟkilli. itiliruntu kiḷampum periya nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷāka mēṟkūṟiya kaṭalkaḷik karutalām. itaṟkuk karikaḷ illi.

kaṇṭam mutaliyavi

itaṉ miyattiluḷḷa kaṇṭam aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭam. itil tīvukaḷum malit toṭarkaḷum uḷḷaṉa. itaṉ mukkiya tuṇik kaṭalkaḷ veṭal kaṭalum rās kaṭalum ākum. maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷik kāṭṭilum kuṟivāka ārāyappaṭṭa kaṭal itu. itaṉ parappu 80 ilaṭcam catura mil. ﻿

paṭivukaḷ

⁠itaṉ aṭiyil paṭivukaḷ paṭintuḷḷaṉa. avi mutaṉmiyākac cēṟu ākum. iccēṟu nilappakutiyiliruntu paṉi āṟukaḷāl koṇṭu varappaṭukiṟatu.

veppa nili

⁠ārkṭik kaṭaliviṭa itu mikak kuḷirnta kaṭal; āḻamāṉa kaṭal. itiliruntu periya paṉip pāṟikaḷ veppat tuṇik kaṭalkaḷuk kuc celkiṉṟaṉa.

⁠itaṉ mēṟparappu veppa nilikaḷ āṇṭu tōṟum 40° F eṉṉum aḷavil uḷḷaṉa. avi aṭikkaṭi 28° F aḷavukkum varum. aṭip pakutikaḷiṉ veppa nilikaḷ 31° F eṉṉum aḷa vil irukkum.

paṉikkaṭṭi

⁠itu perumpālum aṭarnta paṉikkaṭṭiyāl mūṭappaṭṭuḷḷatu. mārik kālattil muḻuk kaṭa lum uṟintu orē paṉi veḷiyākak kāṭci aḷikkum. pārppataṟku eṅkum paṉikkaṭṭi tāṉ teṉpaṭum. kōṭiyil paṉikkaṭṭi urukum; nīr mīṇṭum talikāṭṭum.

ikkaṭal aṉuppum paṉippāṟikaḷ ārkṭik kaṭaliṉ paṉippāṟikaḷik kāṭṭilum mikap periyavi. ākavē, avi mika metuvāka urukum.

uppu

⁠maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷik kāṭṭilum itaṟku up put taṉmi kuṟivu eṉṟē colla vēṇṭum. nīr āviyātal kuṟinta aḷavukku naṭipeṟuva tālum, kōṭiyil paṉip pāṟikaḷ urukuva tāl varum nīriṉālum itaṉ upput taṉmi atāvatu karikkum taṉmi kuṟivākavē uḷḷatu.

vāyukkaḷ



⁠itil karintuḷḷa vaḷikaḷ allatu vāyukkaḷ niṭrajaṉ eṉṉum uppu vāyuvum; ākcijaṉ eṉṉum uyirk kāṟṟum ākum. ivvaḷikaḷ karintirukkum aḷavu atikamāka uḷḷatu.

aṭartti

⁠ikkaṭal nīril kuṟinta veppa nilikaḷ nilavukiṉṟaṉa. ākavē, nīriṉ aṭartti atika mākum.

ōṭṭaṅkaḷum ali eḻuccikaḷum

itil ceṟivāṉa kuḷirnta nīrōṭṭam oṭukiṉṟatu. itaṟku aṇṭārkṭik mitappu nīrōṭ ṭam eṉṟu peyar. itu naṉṉampikki muṉi yil akulās nīrōṭṭam eṉṉum peyarip peṟukiṟatu.

⁠itiliruntu ali eḻuccikaḷ toṭaṅki, maṟṟap peruṅkaṭalkaḷil talikāṭṭukiṉṟaṉa.

uyirkaḷ

⁠ikkaṭal uṟinta pōtilum, atil uyir kaḷ vāḻkiṉṟaṉa. mēṟparappil mutaṉmiyāka ṭiyāṭṭam eṉṉum ōraṇu uyirkaḷ uḷ ḷaṉa. maṟṟum, kīḻnilit tāvaraṅkaḷum kāṇap paṭukiṉṟaṉa.

⁠itaṉ āḻamaṟṟa pakutiyil kaṭaṟ pañcu pōṉṟa mutuku elumpu illāta uyirkaḷ vāḻ kiṉṟaṉa. kaṭaṟ pūṇṭukaḷ atikam. ka ṭa l nāykaḷ, nīr yāṉi mutaliya vilaṅkukaḷum uṇṭu.

⁠ikkaṭalil mīṉkaḷ atikam. avi poruḷ vaḷatti aḷikka vallavi.

⁠palavakit timiṅkilaṅkaḷum kāṇappaṭu kiṉṟaṉa. avaṟṟil nīlat timiṅkilam eṉpatu 100 aṭi nīḷamum 100 ṭaṉ eṭiyum irukkum. itu aṇṭārkṭik vilaṅkukaḷilēyē mikap periyatu. ﻿



vāṇipa vaḻikaḷ

⁠maṟṟak kaṭalkaḷ pōṉṟu itil vāṇipa vaḻikaḷ illi. iṅku mukkiyamākat timiṅkila vēṭṭik kappalkaḷum ārāyccik kappal kaḷumē varum. vimāṉaṅkaḷum iṟaṅkum. etir kālattil vāṇipa vaḻikaḷ amiyalām.





﻿2. aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭam



iruppiṭam

ulakiluḷḷa nilap pakutikaḷ palavaṟṟil makkaḷ vāḻkiṟārkaḷ. appakutikaḷiliruntu ilaṭcakkaṇakkāṉa āṇṭukaḷukku muṉpē paṉi maṟintu viṭṭatu. āṉāl, ulakiṉ teṉkōṭiyil iṉṉum orē oru nilappakuti uḷḷatu. atiliruntu paṉi iṉṉum nīṅkavilli. atutāṉ aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭam ākum. potuvāka, aṇṭārkṭik eṉṉum col nīr eṉṉum niliyil aṇṭārkṭik kaṭaliyum, nilam eṉṉum niliyil aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattiyum kuṟippatākum.

ikkaṇṭam nilavulakiṉ teṉ muṉiyic cuṟṟi amintuḷḷatu. itaṉ parappu 55 ilaṭcam catura milkaḷ. orē paṉiyum paṉik kaṭṭiyum niṟinta kaṇṭam. makkaḷ naṭamāṭṭam illātatu. paṉip paṭalam teṟkēyum vaṭakkēyum 1,000 mil aḷavukkup paraviyuḷḷatu. itu uyaramāṉa kaṇṭam; kaṭal maṭṭattiṟkumēl 1 mil uyaramuḷḷatu.

itic cūḻntuḷḷa aṇṭārkṭik kaṭalil maṭṭum paṉikkaṭṭi 2 ilaṭcam catura milkaḷ paraviyuḷḷatu. kaṇṭattiṉ carācari uyaram 6,500 aṭi. teṉ muṉiyiluḷḷa mēṭṭuc camaveḷi maṭṭum 11,000 aṭi uyaram uḷḷatu. ﻿iccamaveḷi, amerikkāviyum airōppāviyum cērttāl evvaḷavu periyatāka irukkumō, avvaḷavu periyatāka irukkum. ikkaṇṭam vaṭṭa vaṭivamāṉatu. āstirēliyāviviṭa iru maṭaṅku periyatu.

mali

aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭam ulakil mikak kuḷirnta kaṇṭam. itil pala mēṭṭuc camaveḷikaḷum, uyarnta paṉi paṭarnta malikaḷum uḷḷaṉa. 15,000 aṭi uyaramuḷḷa malikaḷum iṅkuk kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa.

itiluḷḷa rās paṉit tiṭṭu maṭṭumē mitakkumpaṉikkaṭṭiyālāṉatu. paṉikkaṭṭiyiṉ taṭimaṉ 600-1,000 aṭi. ittiṭṭiṉ parappu pirāṉciṉ parappiviṭap periyatu. ittiṭṭu kaṭali nōkki oru nāḷikku aintu aṭi vītam kakarṅtu koṇṭirukkiṟatu.

ik kaṇṭattil iru erimalikaḷ uḷḷaṉa. oṉṟu mavuṇṭ eripas. itaṉ uyaram 13,350 aṭi. niliyāṉa ceyalākkam uḷḷatu. iti aṭiya muyaṟcikaḷ naṭipeṟṟavaṇṇam uḷḷaṉa; maṟṟoṉṟu mavuṇṭ ṭor. itaṉ uyaram 900 aṭi. taṟpoḻutu itu oyntu oḻintuḷḷatu.

itiluḷḷa paṉippāṟikaḷiṉ aḷavu, mitakkum paṉikkaṭṭiyiṉ maṭṭa uyarvu ākiyavaṟṟi liruntu iṅku nila kaṭukkaṅkaḷ nilavalām eṉṟu nampappaṭukiṟatu.

kaṭaṟkariyic cuṟṟilum ularnta paḷḷat tākkukaḷ uḷḷaṉa. iṅkup paṉiyāṟukaḷ piṉ vāṅkat toṭaṅkiyuḷḷaṉa. iruppiṉum, avaṟṟil cila nākku pō l paḷḷattākkukaḷil toṅkikkoṇṭirukkiṉṟaṉa.

paḷḷattākkukaḷiṉ kariya tari muḻu aḷa vukku ularntatu alla. ittari katiravaṉ veppattip peṟṟup paṉiyāṟukaḷi uruka vikkiṉṟaṉa. peyyum paṉi tariyi aṭi vataṟku muṉpē āviyākum. cila paḷḷattākku kaḷil paṉiyā ṟu ka ḷ vaḷarnta vaṇṇamum tēynta vaṇṇamum uḷḷaṉa.

puyalkaḷ

iṅkup puyalkaḷum uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. ataṉāl, iti virivāka ārāya muṭiyavilli. iṅku aṭikkum payaṅkarak kāṟṟukaḷiṉ virivu maṭ ṭum 1 maṇikku 200 mil. iṅkuk kāntap puyal kaḷum aṭikkiṉṟaṉa.

oḷikaḷ

kaṇṇiyum karuttiyum kavarum pala vaṇṇa oḷikaḷāṉa teṉmuṉi oḷikaḷ iṅku uṇṭākiṉṟaṉa. vāṉveḷik kappalkaḷil celvōr ikkaṇkoḷḷāk kāṭciyik kaṇṭukaḷikkalām. ﻿



taṭpaveppa nili

aṅkup paruva kālaṅkaḷum uṇṭu. avi kōṭik kālamum mārikkālamum ākum. kōṭi kuḷirntatu; kuṟukiyatu. māri mikak kuḷirntatu; nīṇṭatu.

mārik kālattil--70° F veppaki2la pati vāki uḷḷatu. ik kālattil eṅkum oru nāḷ muḻutum katiravaṉē talikāṭṭātu. āṇṭiṉ enta mātamum 32° F veppa niliyil iruppa tilli. 32° F eṉpatu nīr uṟiyum veppa niliyākum. potuvāka, ārkṭik kaṇṭattiṉ veppa nilikaḷiviṭa ikkaṇṭattiṉ veppa nilikaḷ 5' kuṟivu ivveppa nilikaḷ payir kaḷ vaḷarvataṟku ēṟṟatalla.

iṅkup paṇi peytalum maḻi poḻitalum matippiṭappaṭṭirukkiṟatu. ataṉpaṭi, avaṟṟiṉ aḷavu, or āṇṭil 30 aṅkulam maḻi peyvataṟkuca camamāṉatu.

nila iyalnūl āṇṭiṉ poḻutu aṇ ṭārkṭikkil ārāycci naṭipeṟṟatu. anta ārāycciyiṉ muṭivukaḷāvaṉa: ulakam veppam aṭintukoṇṭu varukiṟatu. ik kaṇṭatti luḷḷa paṉikkaṭṭi urukuvatē, veppa uyar vukkuk kāraṇamāka irukkalām. ﻿



teṉ muṉi ulakilēyē atika veppatti peṟum iṭamākum; mikak kuḷirnta iṭamum ākum.

nila iyalnūl āṇṭiṉ poḻutu cōvi yattu ārāycciyāḷarkaḷ aṇṭārkṭikkil vāstōk eṉṉum niliyattil veppa nili yip pativu ceytaṉar. avvāṟu pativu cey tatu 1958 ām āṇṭu jūli mātattil naṭi peṟṟatu. ivveppa niliyē ulakil mikak kuṟinta veppa niliyākum. ataṉ aḷavu –87.4°C (–126°F).

ivveppa niliyil eḵkuk kuḻāykaḷic cammaṭṭiyāl aṭittuk kaṇṇāṭiyi uṭip patu pōl uṭikkalām. iṅkut tirava uṇavic camikkavē aintu maṇi āyiṟṟu!

teṉ muṉi atika veppatti ṭicampar mātattil peṟṟa pōtilum, avvāṟṟalil 89 paṅku aḷavu, paṉi uṟiyiṉāl piratipalikkap paṭukiṟatu. itaṉāl, veppa iḻappu uṇṭākiṟatu.

paṉikkaṭṭiyiṉ mēl aṭukkil nērtti yāṉa maṇaltuḷikaḷ paṭintuḷḷaṉa. itaṉāl, veppam uṟiñcappaṭutal metuvāka naṭipeṟu kiṟatu. inta aṭukkuk kaṭiṉamāka iruppatāl, ataṉ mītu aṭiccuvaṭukaḷ paṭiyāmalē kaṭakka lām. appaṭi aṭiccuvaṭukaḷ paṭintāl, avi maṟivataṟkup pala vāraṅkaḷ ākum. icceyal āviyātal kuṟivāka naṭipeṟuvatik kāṭṭu kiṟatu!

uyirkaḷ

mikak kuḷirnta kaṇṭamākiyāl, u ya r vakip payiriṉaṅkaḷ iṅku vaḷarvatilli. tāḻnta payir vakikaḷāṉa pul, pāci, likaṉ mutaliyavi iṅku vaḷarkiṉṟaṉa.

vilaṅkukaḷil peṉkuyiṉ eṉṉum paṟavi kaḷ iṅkuḷḷaṉa. pūccikaḷum uḷḷaṉa. mīṉ, kaṭal nāy, timiṅkilam mutaliyavi kaṭal vilaṅ kukaḷ, karip pakutikaḷ timiṅkila vēṭṭikkuc ciṟantavi. ulakiṉ timiṅkila viḷi poruḷ kaḷil 90 paṅku aḷavu iṅkiruntu kiṭikkiṟatu. itaṉāl aṇṭārkṭik kaṭalil timiṅkilaṅkaḷ atikam eṉpatu terikiṟatu.

itu makkaḷ vāḻum iṭamāka iṉṉum āka villi. viññāṉikaḷ maṭṭumē ta ḷa ṅ ka ḷ amittu ārāycci ceytu varukiṉṟaṉar.

rās tiṭṭiṉ mītu uṟinta mīṉkaḷ 1,100 āṇṭukālamāka ataṉ mītē kiṭakkiṉṟaṉa. nūṟṟāṇṭukaḷukku muṉ paṉik kaṭṭikkuk kīḻ akappaṭṭup piṉ mēl vantavi avi.

kaṉivaḷam

paṉikkaṭṭikkuk kīḻ nilakkarip paṭivukaḷum, tātukkaḷum iruppatākak karutappaṭukiṟatu. avi iṉitāṉ ārāycci ceytu, tōṇṭi eṭukkappaṭa vēṇṭum.

nāṭukaḷ

iṅku niliyāṉa kuṭiyēṟṟaṅkaḷ illi. iruppiṉum, nilappakutiyil pala pirivukaḷ uṇṭu. piriṭṭaṉ, nārvē, amerikkā, uru ciyā mutaliyavi ikkaṇṭattil akkaṟi kāṭ ṭum nāṭukaḷākum.

ārāycci

iti ārāyccikkuc ciṟanta iṭamāka viññāṉikaḷ karutukiṉṟaṉar. paṉikkaṭṭi iyakkam, vāṉili nilimikaḷ, nila amippu nūl varalāṟu mutaliyavaṟṟip paṟṟi iṅku ārāyalām.

aṇṭārkṭik paṉikkaṭṭiyiliruntu aṟiya vēṇṭiyavi atikam. atika āḻaṅkaḷiliruntu ataṉ uṭpakutikaḷ veḷik koṇṭuvarappaṭu kiṉṟaṉa. inta uṭpakutikaḷil ellā vakit tukaḷkaḷum, iṟukiya kāṟṟuk koppuḷaṅkaḷum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa. ivi iraṇṭum nūṟṟāṇṭuk kaṇakkākap peyyum paṉiyāl piṭipaṭṭu uḷḷē ceṉṟavi.

3. aṇṭārkṭik ārāycci

uṇmi

18ām nūṟṟāṇṭu vari aṇṭārkṭik oru periya kaṇṭam eṉṟu makkaḷ eṇṇi vantaṉar. āṉāl, ārkṭṭik kaṇṭampōl allāmal, itu atika aḷavukku nilappakuti yik koṇṭirukkiṟatu. inta uṇmi kēpṭaṉ kukkiṉ payaṇattiṟku muṉpē uṟuti ceyyappaṭṭatu.

aṇṭārkṭik ārāycciyiṉ varalāṟu kēpṭaṉ kukkiṉ kaṭaṟpayaṇaṅkaḷōṭu toṭaṅkukiṟatu. 1772-75 ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭiyē ivar recalyūcaṉ, aṭveṉccar eṉṉuṅ kappalkaḷil tam payaṇaṅkaḷi mēṟkoṇṭār. ivar 1773-ām āṇṭil jaṉavari 17-il aṇṭārkṭik vaṭṭattik kaṭantār; āḷ naṭa māṭṭamillāta veṟum tīvukaḷ palavaṟṟik kaṇṭār.

nērs

ivar talimiyil cēlaṉjar payaṇam 1874-il toṭaṅkiṟṟu. cēlaṉjar eṉṉuṅ kappal aṇṭārkṭik vaṭṭatti mutaṉ mutalā kak kaṭantatu, nērs eṉpavar tiraṭṭiya aṇṭārkṭik kaṭal payir vakikaḷum, eṭutta olippu aḷavukaḷum car jāṉ murē eṉpārukku mikavum tuṇi ceytaṉa. car jāṉ murē eṉpār avaṟṟik koṇṭu, aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭam eṉṉum oru kaṇṭam uḷḷatu eṉṟu muṭivu kaṭṭiṉar.

kiṟisṭaṉcaṉ

ivar tam kuḻuviṉaruṭaṉ mutaṉ mutalil aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattil kālaṭi eṭuttu vittār. teṟkē aṇṭārkṭik kaṭalil kappal celluvataṟku iyalum eṉṟu ivar aṟintār.

ṣēkkiḷṭaṉ

ivar 1908-1909-ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭiyē ciṟanta kaṇṭupiṭippukkaḷik kaṇṭupiṭittu, vīrar paṭṭam peṟṟār. kaṇṭupiṭippukkaḷ viññāṉam, nila nūl mutaliya tuṟikaḷ toṭarpāṉavi.

amuṇṭcaṉ

ivar pukaḻ peṟṟa vaṭamuṉi ārāycciyāḷar. ivar nārvē nāṭṭic cārntavar ; mutaṉ mutalil teṉmuṉiyi aṭintavar. 1911-ām āṇṭu ṭicampar 14-il ivar teṉ muṉikku vantār. aṅku ivar 10,000 aṭi uyaramuḷḷa akalamāṉa paṉiccamaveḷi iruppati aṟintār.

skāṭ

1933-1934ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭiyē ivar tam kuḻuviṉaruṭaṉ aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattil mārik kālattik kaḻittār. paṉikkuk kīḻ āḻattil valuvāka amikkappaṭṭa vīṭukaḷil ivar mārik kālattik kaḻittār. teṉ muṉiyiliruntu 200 mil tolivukku mukkiya nilakkarip paṭivukaḷ iruppatāka ivar kūṟiṉar.

vilkiṉs, els vort mutaliyōr aṇṭārkṭikkiṟkup payaṇaṅkaḷi mēṟkoṇṭaṉar.

1935-1937 ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭiyil ṭiskavari-2 eṉṉum āṅkilak kappal aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattic cuṟṟi vantatu. atu kaṭanta tolivu 50,000 mil. aṇṭārkṭik pakutiyil timiṅkilaṅkaḷ e v vā ṟu paraviyuḷḷaṉa eṉpatik kappalil ceṉṟu ārāyntaṉar. tavira, rās kaṭalippaṟṟi, uyir nūl toṭar pākavum, nīr nūl toṭarpākavum ceytikaḷ tiraṭṭiṉar.

palanāṭukaḷ

1939 ām āṇṭilum, 1946-1947 ām āṇṭukaḷukkiṭiyilum pa ya r ṭu eṉpavar talimiyil pala payaṇaṅkaḷukku amerikkā ēṟpāṭu ceytatu. payaṇaṅkaḷiṉ nōkkam aṇṭārkṭikkiṉ kaṉi vaḷaṅkaḷi ārāyvatāka iruntatu. uruciyā, nārvē, piāāṉcu, jermaṉi mutaliya pala nā ṭu kaḷu m aṇṭārkṭikkil payaṇaṅkaḷi mēṟkoṇṭu ārāycci naṭattiyuḷḷaṉa. iṉṟum amerikkāvum uruciyāvum aṇṭārkṭikkil ārāyccikaḷ na ṭa t ti ya vaṇṇamē uḷḷaṉa.

﻿4. aṇṭārkṭik payaṇam

peyar

kāmaṉvelt cārpil aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattik kaṭappataṟkāka oru payaṇam ēṟpāṭu ceyyappaṭṭu iṉitu niṟivēṟiyatu. itaṟkuk kāmaṉvelt aṇṭārkṭik kaṭappup payaṇam eṉṟu peyar.

kālam

nila iyal nūl āṇṭut tiṭṭattōṭu ottu amintatu; āṉāl, taṉiyāka amikkap paṭṭatu; niṟivēṟṟappaṭṭatu ippayaṇam. ākavē, itaṉ kālam 1957-58 āṇṭukaḷukku iṭiyiluḷḷa kālam ākum.

ciṟappu

ippayaṇam kūṭṭu muyaṟciyāl naṭipeṟṟa oru tiṭṭamākum. itaṟku āṅkila nāṭṭu araciyār puravalarāka iruntār. taṉiyār muyaṟciyālum, pala aracukaḷiṉ kūṭṭu muyaṟciyiṉālum kaṭipeṟṟa payaṇam itu. itaṟku āstirēliyā, teṉ āppirikkā, niyūcilāntu ākiya nāṭukaḷ paṇa utavi ceytu ātarittaṉa. muḻup payaṇattiṟkum 500,000 pavuṉkaḷ celavaḻiṅan tatu. taṉiyār toḻil niṟuvaṉaṅkaḷum itaṟku utavi ceytaṉa.

nōkkam

aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭam naṉku ārāyap paṭāta kaṇṭam. āstirēliyāvi viṭa itu iraṇṭu maṭaṅku periyatu. iruppiṉum, ataṉ tariyi yārum kaṭakkavilli. ataṉ tariyik kaṭantu celvatum; cellum poḻutē pala vaki ārāyccikaḷ naṭattuvatum payaṇattiṉ mukkiya nōkkamākum.

vāṉili nūl, paṉi nūl, nila amippu nūl mutaliya tuṟikaḷil ārāyccikaḷ naṭantaṉa. ivi aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattiṉ aṟivi mēlum perukka utavi ceytaṉa.

varalāṟu

1775 ām āṇṭiliruntē aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattil āṅkilēyar ārvam kāṭṭivara lāyiṉar. inta āṇṭil kēpṭaṉ kuk eṉpār mutaṉ mutalāka aṇṭārkṭik vaṭṭattik kaṭantār. aṅkup paṉikkaṭṭikaḷukkiṭiyē pācaṟi amittut taṅkiṉar.

19 ām nūṟṟaṇṭiṉ muṟpakutiyil āṅkilak kappalōṭṭikaḷ mutaṉ mutalil aṇṭārkṭikkil nilappakutiyik kaṇṭupiṭittaṉar. ataṉ kaṭaṟkariyiṉ ellikaḷiyum aṟintaṉar.

20 ām nūṟṟāṇṭiṉ toṭakkattil pala nāṭukaḷiṉ viññāṉikaḷāl toṭarntu pala payaṇaṅkaḷ aṇṭārkṭikkiṟku mēṟkoḷḷaṭippaṭṭaṉa.

1908 il car ērṉasṭ ṣēkkiḷṭaṉ eṉpār teṉmuṉiyil 90 mil aḷavukkuc ceṉṟār. iru taṭavikaḷ ikkaṇṭattik kaṭakkavum muyaṟci ceytār. āṉal, muyaṟci kiviṭap paṭṭatu. iraṇṭāvatu muyaṟciyiṉ poḻutu (1922 il) ivarē iṟakka nēriṭṭatu. ivar ciṟanta āṅkilēya turuva ārāycciyāḷar.

1911 il nārvē nāṭṭic cārnta amuṇṭcaṉ eṉpār teṉ muṉiyi aṭintār. itaṟkup piṉ yārum akkaṇṭattiṉ tariyik kaṭakkavilli. ivarukku muṉ ceṉṟa skāṭ eṉpār iṟakka nēriṭṭatu.

1952 il vēṭikkiḷṭaṉ, skāṭ ākiya iruvaruṭaṉum paṇiyāṟṟiya cilar aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattik kaṭakka vēṇṭum eṉṟu muṭivu ceytaṉar. ataṉ viḷivākak kāmaṉ velt aṇṭārkṭik kaṭappuppayaṇam mēṟkoḷḷap paṭṭatu. itaṟkut talivarāka ṭārkṭar viviyaṉ paks e ṉ pār tērnteṭukkappaṭṭār ivarukkut tuṇiyāka evaresṭ pukaḻ hillāri iruntār. ivar niyūcilāntu nāṭṭic cērn tavar. avvāṟu kūṭṭu muyaṟciyuṭaṉ tiṭṭa miṭṭa payaṇam 1957-58 il iṉitu niṟivēṟiyatu. 1952-1956 vari ataṟkuriya muyaṟci kaḷum muṉṉēṟpāṭukaḷum naṭipeṟṟaṉa.

payaṇam

payaṇa vaḻiyiṉ tolivu 2,000 mil. payaṇavaḻi kāmaṉvelt nilappakutiyilēyē amintatu. atāvatu vaḻi veṭal kaṭaliluḷḷa vēcal virikuṭāviliruntu rāskaṭaliluḷḷa makmarṭō cavuṇṭuvari amintiruntatu.

payaṇattiṉ muṉcellum kuḻu ilaṇṭaṉi viṭṭu 1955 ām āṇṭu navampar mātam kappalil puṟappaṭṭatu. veṭal kaṭaliṉ paṉikkaṭṭiyiṉ vaḻiyākak kappal kaṭiṉap payaṇatti mē ṟ koṇṭu vēcal virikuṭāviṉ karikaḷi 1956 ām āṇṭu jaṉavari mātam aṭintatu.

iṅku mukkiya taḷam amikkappaṭṭu, ataṟku ṣēkkiḷṭaṉ eṉap peyariṭappaṭṭatu. eṭṭup pēr aṭaṅkiya kuḻuviṉari iṅku viṭṭu viṭṭu, mēṟkoṇṭu ākavēṇṭiya vēlikaḷik kavaṉikka ṭākṭar paks iṅkilāntiṟku vantār. tirumpavum 1957 ām āṇṭu jaṉavari mātam vēṭikkiḷṭaṉ taḷattiṟku vantār.

car eṭmaṇṭ hillāri tam kuḻuviṉaruṭaṉ 1956 ām āṇṭu ṭicampar mātam niyūcilānti viṭṭup puṟappaṭṭār. 1957 ām āṇṭu jaṉavari mātattil makmarṭō cavuṇṭi aṭintār.

veṭal kaṭalil iruntu paks tam kuḻuviṉa ruṭaṉ kiḷampiṉār. rās kaṭalil iruntu hil lāri tam kuḻuviṉaruṭaṉ kiḷampiṉar. ivviru kuḻuviṉariyum maṟṟoru amerikkak kuḻuviṉar vimāṉattil paṟantu vantu teṉ muṉiyil cantittaṉar. ivarkaḷ aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattiṉ tariyik kaṭantu teṉ muṉiyi aṭintatu nila iyalnūl āṇṭiṉ pakuti yākavē amintatu.

payaṇam tāḻnta veppa nilikaḷ, uyarnta malikaḷiṉ kuṟukkīṭu mu ta li ya iṉṉalkaḷuk kiṭiyē naṭipeṟṟatu. 30 milkaḷukku oru taṭavi nilanaṭukka olippu aḷavīṭukaḷ eṭukkap paṭṭaṉa. paṉikkaṭṭiyiṉ aṭarttiyik kaṇṭu piṭikka inta aḷavīṭukaḷ eṭukkappaṭṭaṉa.

ituvari kaṇṭaṟiyappaṭāta iru malit toṭarkaḷ iruppatākavum; ulakiṉ periya paṉi yāṟukaḷil oṉṟu iruppatākavum ārāycci kaḷiṉ mūlam teriya vantatu.

paṉikkaṭṭi aṭarttiyik kaṇakkiṭṭatil iruntum; maṟṟum nila iyalnūl āṇṭut toṭarpākac ceyyappaṭṭa ārāyccikaḷilirun tum, aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭam oru taṉi nilat tokuti alla, tīvukaḷum malikaḷum aṭaṅkiya tokuti eṉpatu teriya varukiṟatu.





﻿5. aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattil ōr ārāycciyāḷar

paḷḷic cāraṇar

1928 ām āṇṭu pāl cippiḷ amerikkap paḷḷic cāraṇarāka iruntār. appoḻutu avarukku vayatu 9. iruppiṉum añcā neñcattuṭaṉ avar paḷḷic cāraṇar cārpil payarṭu eṉpār kuḻuviṉaruṭaṉ teṉ muṉikkucceṉṟu tirumpiṉār. payarṭu teṉ muṉikkuc ceṉṟa āṇṭu 1928 ākum.

in nikaḻccikkup piṉ ulakiluḷḷa iḷiñarkaḷ ellām cippiḷip pōṟṟat toṭaṅkiṉārkaḷ. ēṉ porumi kūṭap paṭṭārkaḷ. āṉal, innikaḻcci cippiḷēp periya ārāycci yāḷarāka māṟṟiṟṟu.

aṇṭārkṭik payaṇam

ṭākṭar cippiḷ aṇṭārkṭikkiṟku aintu taṭavikaḷ ceṉṟirukkīṟār. aṅku avar nāṉku mārikkālaṅkaḷik kaḻittirukkiṟār. mutaṉ mutalil avar niyūyārkkiliruntu aṇṭārkṭikkiṟkuc ceṉṟār. avar tam kuḻu viṉaruṭaṉ cella 100 nāṭkaḷ āyiṟṟu. kappal payaṇam muṭinta piṉ, kuḻuviṉar naṭantu ceṉṟaṉar. muṉṉē nāykaḷ vaḻikāṭṭic ceṉṟaṉa.

kaṭiciyāka, avar vimāṉa mūlam ceṉṟār, avvāṟu celvataṟku 40 maṇi nēram āyiṟṟu. oru taṉi vimāṉattil nērākat teṉ muṉiyi aṭintār.

avaratu aṇmip payaṇaṅkaḷ teṉmuṉiyil ārāycci ceyya, avarukkut tuṇi ceytaṉa. nila nūlum, taṭpa veppa nili nūlum maṉitaṉōṭu neruṅkiya toṭarpu uṭiyavi. ākavē, attuṟikaḷil avar ārāyccikaḷ naṭattiṉar.

avar kūṟuvatāvatu: “ -70° C veppa nili kaḷukkuk kīḻ nāṉ mātakkaṇakkil (teṉ muṉi yil) vāḻntirukkiṟēṉ. inta nilimikaḷil mūccu, nīrāviyiṉ olipōl veḷivarum'.

avar teṉmuṉi allatu aṇṭārkṭik vāḻkki paṟṟiyum kūṟiṉār. 'aṅku makkaḷ eṉpōr ārāycci ceyyum viññāṉikaḷē. avarkaḷit tavira vēṟu yāriyum nām aṅkup pārkka iyalātu.”

nila amippu nūl ārāycci, paṉikkaṭṭi yil nila naṭukka olippu aḷavīṭukaḷ eṭuttal, vāṉili nilimikaḷi uṟṟu nōkkal mutaliya ārāyccip paṇikaḷi viññāṉikaḷ ceyvārkaḷ

kuḷirnta kāṟṟu, nīr irunta pōtilum, payirkaḷum vilaṅkukaḷum veppa maṇṭalaṅkaḷik kāṭṭilum turuvap pakutiyil mika virivākap perukukiṉṟaṉa.

paṟavikaḷ kōṭiyil aṅku varukiṉṟaṉa. peṉkuyiṉ paṟavi vakikaḷ patiṉeṭṭiṟku mēl uḷḷaṉa. avi kūṭṭamāka vāḻkiṉṟaṉa. paṉikkaṭṭiyil muṭṭiyiṭṭa pōtilum, avi virivākap perukukiṉṟaṉa.

450 āṇṭukaḷukku muṉpu teṉmuṉi, veppac cakārāvil kaṇṭa nakarvu viḷiviṉāl (continental drift) kāṇappaṭṭatu eṉṉum oru koḷki nilavukiṟatu.

mēlum nila naṭukkōṭṭiṟkut teṟkēyuḷḷa kaṇṭaṅkaḷ mikapperiya kaṇṭamāṉa ṭāṇṭ vāṉālāntiṉ pakutiyāka oru kālattil iruntaṉa eṉṉum koḷkikkum aṇmik kālak kaṇṭupiṭippukaḷ aravaṇippāka uḷḷaṉa. aṇṭārkṭik kaṭal, ataṉ tarikaḷ, tariyiṉ kāntappulam ākiyavaṟṟi ārāyntatiṉ vāyilāka oru putukkoḷki uruvākiyuḷḷatu. kaṭaltari paravukiṟatu eṉpatē akkoḷkiyākum.

taṭpa veppanili

payaṅkarak kāṟṟukaḷ, kaṭuṅkuḷir, paṉippuyal mutaliya iyaṟkik koṭumikaḷukku etirāka naṭipeṟum pō rā ṭ ṭa mē aṇṭārkṭik vāḻkkiyākum. ivvāḻkki ārāycciyāḷar kaḷukku maṭṭumē uriyatu.

cūṟāvaḷik kāṟṟiṉ virivu viṉāṭikku 40 mīṭṭar. paṉip puyalkaḷiṉ virivu oru viṉāṭikku 50 mīṭṭar. cila camayaṅkaḷil atu 60 mīṭṭar viriviyum aṭiyum. kāṟṟiṉ virivu oru viṉāṭikku 15 mīṭṭar. kāṟṟaḻuttam 560 milli mīṭṭar, uṟi paṉi veppanili -56°C.

ikkaṇṭattiṉ mēṟkē āṇṭuc carācari veppanili 0°F. uḷḷē 400 mil ceṉṟāl -30°F. 900 mil ceṉṟāl-60°F. jaṉavarit tiṅkaḷil kōṭi mika uccamāka irukkum. aṇ ṭārkṭikkiṉ motta maḻiyaḷavu āṇṭukku 4 mutal 6 aṅkulamākum.

tāvaraṅkaḷ

mūṉṟu pūkkā tāvaraṅkaḷē uḷḷaṉa. ālkākkaḷum, likkaṉkaḷum kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

vilaṅkukaḷ

cīlkaḷ, emparar, aṭilis peṉkuyiṉ paṟavikaḷ, timiṅkilaṅkaḷ, aṇṭaraṇṭap paṟavi, kaḻuku, puṟākkaḷ mutaliyavi nīril vāḻpaviyē. nilattil vāḻum mutukelumpu vilaṅkukaḷ illi. ciṟakillāta curuḷ vāl pūccikaḷ, maṟṟum ciṟu pūccikaḷ pāṟikaḷukkuk kīḻ vāḻkiṉṟaṉa. ṣirimp eṉṉum purataccattu mikunta vilaṅkukaḷ veṭal kaṭalil niṟiya uḷḷaṉa.

paṭivuc cāṉṟu

2 aṅkula nīḷamuḷḷa tavaḷi iṉa vilaṅkiṉ tāṭi elumpu oṉṟu paṭivāka aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattil kaṇṭu piṭikkappaṭaṭuḷḷatu. 200 milliyaṉ āṇṭukaḷukku muṉpu teṉmuṉikku arukiluḷḷa tuṇi veppa maṇṭalakkāṭukaḷil vāḻnta vilaṅkākum itu. ippakutiyil oru kālattil mutukelumpuḷḷa vilaṅkukaḷ vāḻntaṉa eṉpataṟku itu cāṉṟākum. tāvara, vilaṅkut tōṟṟamuḷḷa paṭivukaḷum teṉkaṭaliṉ aṭip pakutiyil kāṇappaṭukiṉṟaṉa.

maliyum malit toṭarkaḷum

aṇṭārkṭikāvil nīril mūḻkiyuḷḷa malikaḷ, malittoṭarkaḷ, vaṭinilaṅkaḷ ākiyavaṟṟiṉ ellikaḷ ārāyappaṭṭuḷḷaṉa iru eri malikaḷik koṇṭa 30-130 mil akalamuḷḷa mali mukaṭu āppirikkāviṉ teṟkē kaṇṭupiṭik kappaṭṭuḷḷatu. immukaṭu 5000 mīṭṭar āḻaṅ kaḷukku mēl uḷḷa ikōlṉi virikuṭāviṉ (Igolny Bay) arukēyuḷḷa vaṭi nilattiṉ miyattil uḷaḷatu.

aṇṭārkṭik peruntokuti (horst) malit toṭarkaḷālāṉatu. ivi ataṉik kiḻakkuk kaṇṭam, mēṟkuk kaṇṭam eṉap pirikkiṉṟaṉa.

kiḻakkuk kaṇṭam kaṭal maṭṭattiṟkumēl 500-1000 mīṭṭar uyaram toṭarcciyāka uḷḷatu; paṉikkaṭṭiyāl mūṭappaṭṭuḷḷatu. paṉikkaṭṭi 2-2.5 ki. mī. taṭimaṉuḷḷatu. ataṉ miyattil kāmparṭcav malittoṭar (Gamburtzev Muntin Range) kāṇappaṭukiṟatu. ataṉ kiḻakkē teṉ muṉiyiliruntu kaṭaṟkari vari paḷḷam oṉṟu uḷḷatu. maṟṟoru tuṇip paṇiyāṟṟuk kuḻivu (Subglacial bowl) kamparṭcav malikku mēṟkē vari paraviyuḷḷatu kuyiṉmā.ḵṭlāntiṟkum (Queen Maud Land) teṉ muṉikkum iṭiyē maṟṟoru kuḻivu (bowl) iruppatāka ārāycciyāḷarkaḷ eṇṇukiṉṟaṉar. ik kaṇṭam oru kālattil maṟṟat teṉkaṇṭaṅkaḷōṭu cērntu iruntatu.

mēṟku aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭamum paṉikkaṭṭiyāl mūṭappaṭṭuk kaṭal maṭṭattiṟkuk kīḻ 1500 mīṭṭar āḻattil uḷḷatu. maliyuccikaḷil cila paṉikkaṭṭiyit tāṇṭi, ataṟku mēlum varukiṉṟaṉa. paṉikkaṭṭi uṟi urukumāṉāl, nīriṉ mēṟparappil oru periya tīvut tokuti (archipe1lago) tōṉṟum. immēṟkuk kaṇṭam tōṟṟattil erimalic cārpuṭiyatu. 10 milliyaṉ āṇṭu kaḷukku muṉ tōṉṟiyatu.

aṇṭārkṭik kaṇṭattil paṉikkaṭṭi uṟivu uḷḷa pakuti 12 milliyaṉ catura kilō mīṭṭar eṉṟu ārāycciyāḷarkaḷ uṟuti ceytuḷḷaṉar. carācari ataṉ taṭimaṉ 2,200 mīṭṭar. mēri pērḵṭlāntil (Mary Birdland) atu 4 ki.mī.

nīr vaḷam

aṇṭārkṭik paṉikkaṭṭi aḷa vilā nīr vaḷam uṭiyatu. ōrāṇṭiṟku 1,00,000 ōlkā āṟukaḷ nīri atu tāṅkavallatu. ulakiṉ taṇṇirp pañcatti atu nīkkum.

kaṉi vaḷam

1973-il rās kaṭalil īttēṉ mīttēṉ iruppataṟkuriya aṟikuṟikaḷ kaṇṭupiṭikkap paṭṭaṉa. iyaṟki vāyuvum, eṇṇeyp paṭivu kaḷum iruppataṟku iviyē aṟikuṟikaḷ. aṇ ṭārkṭik kaṇṭat tiṭṭukaḷil 45 milliyaṉ pīp pāykaḷ eṇṇeyum, 115 ṭirilliyaṉ iyaṟki vāyum uḷḷaṉa eṉṟu amerikka nila amippu aḷavit tuṟiyiṉar uṟuti ceytuḷḷaṉar. kaṭaṟpaṭukiyiṉ periya pakutikaḷil māṅkaṉis muṇṭukaḷ kōlikkuṇṭu aḷaviliruntu kāṟpantu aḷavuvari uḷḷaṉa.

paṉikkaṭṭi illāta malikaḷi nila amippu nūl aṟiñarkaḷ ārāyntuḷḷaṉar. aṇṭārkṭikkil ittakiya malikaḷiṉ parappu 70,000 catura kilō mīṭṭar. taṅkam, periliyam, vayiraṅkaḷ, irumputtātu, cempu, nikkal tātu, katiriyakkak kaṉip poruḷkaḷ, mikkā, pāṟip paṭikam, kirāpiṭ mutaliyavi iṅkuk kaṇṭupiṭikkappaṭṭa poruḷkaḷākum. veṭal kaṭal, rās kaṭal ākiyavaṟṟiṟkiṭiyē uḷḷa maṇaṟkal pakutikaḷil eṇṇey kiṭikka vāyppuḷḷatu. mālipṭiṉam, kārīyam, tuttanākam mutaliyaṉavum kiṭikka nal vāyppuḷḷatu.

mutal aṭlas

aṇṭārkṭikkiṉ mutal aṭlacic cōviyattu oṉṟiyam uruvākkiyuḷḷatu. ulaka aṟiviyalār tiraṭṭiya ceytikaḷiliruntu ippaṭam variyappaṭṭuḷḷatu. tīvukaḷ, mēṭṭuccamaveḷikaḷ, malit toṭarkaḷ, ērikaḷ, paṉiyāṟukaḷ, puvi muṉikaḷ, nīrōṭṭaṅkaḷ, vaṉaccōlikaḷ, tīvakkuṟi ākiyavi paṭaṅkaḷil kuṟikkappaṭ ṭuḷḷaṉa. aṭlaciluḷḷa paṭaṅkaḷ paṉik kaṇṭatti maṭṭumallāmal, teṉkaṭalic cūḻntuḷḷa pakutikaḷiyum kāṭṭukiṉṟaṉa.

aṇṭākṭik cōlikaḷ

uyiraṟṟa veṇmiyē aṇṭārkṭikki aṇi ceyvatu. vāṉa ūrtiyil cellukiṉṟa poḻutu inta veṇmikkiṭiyē paṉi mūṭiya kuṉṟukaḷ teriyum. kaṟutta māniṟa allatu turuppiṭitta kaṟuppu niṟamuḷḷa pāṟi oṭṭukaḷ teriyum. ivaṟṟiṟkiṭiyē pacumiyāṉa kuṭṭikaḷum, ērikaḷum kaṇṇukkuppaṭum iviyē aṇṭārkṭik cōlikaḷ. tiṅkaḷil kaṭalkaḷ ' iruppatu pōṉṟu ivi aṇṭārkṭikkil uḷḷaṉa.
